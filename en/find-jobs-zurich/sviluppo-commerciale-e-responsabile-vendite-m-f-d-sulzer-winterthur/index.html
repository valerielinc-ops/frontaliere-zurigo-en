About the role

As our new Business Development & Sales Manager Rental, you play a central role in expanding our rental business. You are responsible for identifying and developing new business opportunities, acquiring projects and building and maintaining long-term customer relationships. As an important interface between customers, sales, application engineering and operations, you ensure that customer-oriented rental solutions are successfully developed and implemented.

This position is located in the Home Office throughout Germany and includes regular business trips to customer locations (cars are placed).

The position combines business development, technical sales and project management in a dynamic industrial environment. You will help to expand the market presence of S.U.L., to develop new market segments and to achieve sustainable growth in the rental business. You will become part of an international and growth-oriented team and benefit from development opportunities resulting from the further expansion of our rental business.

Your main tasks and responsibilities

  • Development and implementation of strategies for identifying, acquiring and implementing new business opportunities and projects in the rental business.
  • Maintenance, support and expansion of existing customer relationships as well as building long-term strategic partnerships with industrial, infrastructure and key account customers.
  • Consulting customers for suitable rental solutions taking into account their technical and commercial requirements as well as coordination of the supply process in cooperation with application engineers and technical teams.
  • Conduct market and competition analyses, identify new market potentials and business segments, and represent S & D at customer events, trade fairs and industry events.

What you should bring with you for this position

  • Professional experience in technical sales, business development or project-oriented sales, ideally in an industrial, technical or rental environment.
  • Successfully completed studies or comparable qualifications in the fields of industrial engineering, mechanical engineering, process engineering, business management or a similar field of expertise.
  • Experience in the development of new business opportunities, project acquisition as well as in the negotiation of contracts and commercial agreements.
  • Excellent communication and negotiation skills and secure presence towards customers and internal stakeholders.
  • Self-employed, structured and result-oriented working methods with a high customer orientation and an entrepreneurial mindset.
  • Fluent German and English skills in word and writing.
  • A willingness to travel of about 50-60% within the supervised region.

What we offer you

  • Close cooperation with experienced technical and commercial managers.
  • Company cars for private and business use.
  • The opportunity to actively contribute to the growth of a strategically important business area.
  • A varied activity with a combination of business development, technical sales and customer management.
  • High ownership and the possibility to actively shape new markets, customer relationships and business opportunities.
