You can look forward to that.

As AI Portfolio & Process Manager: in marketing, you actively shape the future of marketing at SBB Immobilien. You identify and control AI and automation potential along our marketing processes – from customer and market knowledge to the marketing of real estate and train stations to content, campaigns and digital customer interfaces. You combine the needs of the marketing areas with the possibilities of modern technologies and data. Together with internal specialist departments, you develop concrete AI cases from ideas and accompany them from prioritization through implementation to sustainable anchoring in everyday marketing. You can expect a dynamic working environment in which innovation and future orientation are the focus. You can move that.

  • You develop and control the AI and automation portfolio and align it consistently with the marketing strategy and goals of SBB Immobilien.
  • Along with customer knowledge, real estate marketing, campaigns, content marketing and digital customer experience, you will identify targeted AI and automation potential.
  • By optimizing cross-divisional marketing processes, you create more freedom for creative, strategic and customer-oriented tasks.
  • You develop specific use cases based on the needs of the marketing teams and control their piloting, implementation, scaling and transfer to operations.
  • With suitable goals and key figures, you make the contribution of AI cases to marketing impact, process quality, speed and cost-effectiveness visible.
  • Together with the relevant specialist bodies, you promote the responsible use of AI and ensure data protection, compliance, transparency and quality.

You bring that with you.

  • Several years of experience in marketing as well as in process, project, product or portfolio management characterize your profile
  • Knowledge of the real estate industry is an advantage.
  • In-depth knowledge of the application possibilities of artificial intelligence, marketing automation, CRM, analytics and digital platforms enable you to develop concrete marketing solutions.
  • You have a good understanding of customer journeys and the effective marketing of properties, locations, services and digital offerings.
  • Analytical and networked thinking, consistent customer orientation and the ability to evaluate potential and set clear priorities are among your strengths.
  • You communicate appropriately and have very good knowledge of German
  • Knowledge of another national language is an advantage.
  • A completed university or university of applied sciences degree in marketing, business administration, business informatics, process management or a comparable discipline forms your professional basis.
