Full description

Real Estate Financing Solutions is the Zurich-based competence center for mortgages and real estate financing and is centrally responsible for the entire Private Clients Unit. The mortgage business is an integral part of the bank’s holistic advisory approach to wealthy private clients and complements the existing investment and wealth management offering.

As Head of Real Estate Financing Solutions, you have great influence in the development and development of Financing Advisory & Structuring. In addition, you support customer advisors in advising wealthy private customers in all questions regarding mortgages and real estate financing. They develop financing solutions, advise on mortgage strategies and pricing topics, structure complex financing and ensure that high-quality and creditworthy applications and dossiers are handed over to credit management. They also ensure that their team works efficiently and effectively with the customer advisors and makes an adequate prioritization.

They make a significant contribution to the advisory quality, customer satisfaction, process efficiency and sustainable credit quality of the bank’s mortgage portfolio.

What is the content of the role?

Advice and structuring

  • Support of customer consultants in advising wealthy private customers from Germany and abroad as well as real estate companies.
  • Accompaniment of the customer consultants during demanding customer discussions on complex financing inquiries.
  • Analysis of customer needs, asset situations and financing goals.
  • Development and structuring of mortgage and real estate financing solutions for home, holiday, luxury and return properties.
  • Advice on mortgage strategies, product choice, maturity models and pricing.
  • Structuring complex financing in cooperation with customer advisors and credit management.

Gatekeeper function

  • Ensure that applications comply with the bank’s mortgage strategy and the defined risk appetite.
  • optimization or rejection of incomplete or non-compliant requests to the customer advisors.
  • Technical pre-examination of financing applications including assessment of feasibility and assurance of dossier quality when submitted to credit management.
  • Contribute to the sustainable securing of credit quality and to the further development of the bank’s risk culture.

Analysis and quality assurance

  • Independent implementation, interpretation and plausibility checking of hedonic property valuations.
  • Assessment of sustainability, eligibility, creditworthiness and collateral.
  • Analysis of risks and development of suitable solutions.
  • Preparation and quality assurance of mortgage dossiers.
  • Ensure compliance with internal policies and regulatory requirements.
  • Responsibility for preparing and submitting clear, complete and decision-ready funding applications to credit management.

Professional management and further development

  • Development and development of the Real Estate Financing Solutions function.
  • Sparring partners for client advisors and internal stakeholders (e.g. senior management, financial planners, pension advisors).
  • Responsible for training and knowledge building within the sales organization in coordination with the 2nd Line.
  • Participation in the further development of processes, standards and guidelines.
  • Participation in the development and development of Financing Real Estate Solutions.
  • management of a team.
