Full description

Your main task is to carry out painting work indoors and outdoors. The company attaches great importance to quality, safety and a pleasant working environment. You benefit from modern tools and a motivated team. For our client we are looking for an activity

  • Interior and exterior painting of buildings
  • Preparation of substrates (filling, grinding, priming)
  • Wallpapering
  • Painting of doors, windows and other components
  • Execution of decorative painting techniques
  • Protection and covering work
  • Compliance with safety and quality regulations
