Full description

Are you sociable and want to share your enthusiasm for health and fitness? Do you like working in a team and understand how to respond to customer needs and motivate customers for their goals? Then you are right with us!

Mansioni

  • You support and accompany our customers throughout the training process
  • You create training plans and monitor the trainings and their achievement
  • Ensure cleanliness and hygiene within the studio
  • You conduct introductory trainings and sell subscriptions
  • You take on various administrative tasks, create contracts and actively acquire future customers

Requirements

  • Desired: Experience as a fitness instructor
  • Completed basic or further training: Training as a fitness instructor with valid BLS/AED certificate
  • You have a sales flair as well as a high level of service and consulting expertise
  • You are a motivated and responsible personality who inspires our customers
  • You are flexible and like to work in the evening as well as on weekends and public holidays
  • You enjoy working in a team
  • You have a valid work permit
  • German (good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Mobile subscription: National flat subscription from CHF 10.– / month (Swisscom /digitec connect)
  • Migros Bank: expense-free private and investment account with preferential interest
  • Employee offers: Various discounts in the area of holidays and insurance (Hertz up to 10%, Generali 25%, Axa insurance up to 15%, etc.)
  • Du-culture: We stand for a respectful coexistence at eye level
