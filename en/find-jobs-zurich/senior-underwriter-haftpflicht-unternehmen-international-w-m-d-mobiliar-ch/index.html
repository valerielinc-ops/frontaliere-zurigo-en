Full description

This is what awaits you

As a senior underwriter, you are responsible for underwriting in the area of corporate liability insurance (operating and professional liability). In this function, you will accompany the entire sales and consulting process from risk assessment to the processing of the appropriate insurance solution.

This is what you do with us

Your main activities as an underwriter include the independent analysis, assessment, classification and subscription of liability risks of internationally active corporate customers as well as the negotiation and determination of terms and conditions.

Among other things, you structure and lead international programs and develop creative solutions for various risks. In addition, you take over an existing portfolio and develop it further with the aim of high customer loyalty and expansion through new business.

The organization and participation in events and periodic customer meetings as well as the participation in projects round off your interesting and demanding field of activity.

This is what you bring with you

  • Basic commercial education in the insurance sector with higher education (specialised certificate/diploma of insurance) or academic education (law or economics)
  • Professional experience in national and international liability business as well as knowledge of the corresponding market, foreign legal systems and local conditions
  • Negotiable personality with competent appearance
  • Pleasure to work in interdisciplinary teams, good skill in networking and pleasure in active listening
  • Fluent knowledge of German and English. Knowledge of French is a plus

Working with us

There are varied activities waiting for you as well as a motivated and customer-oriented team. Together we live a team-oriented, solution-oriented and communicative working atmosphere.

The Insurance Supervision Act requires for the exercise of this function the conclusion of insurance intermediary /-in VBV, profile non-life. If this is not available, we are happy to support with tailor-made internal training.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
