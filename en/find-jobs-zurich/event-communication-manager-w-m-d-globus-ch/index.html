Full description

For our HQ at Zurich Bahnhofstrasse we are looking for an Event & Communication Manager who will make our high-calibre events unforgettable with creative flair and organisational talent. Whether local highlights for our branches in German-speaking Switzerland and Romandie or GLOBUS-wide events: You plan and run events independently, create creative briefings for agencies and ensure smooth processes and a first-class atmosphere. With your sense of the wishes of the customers and your network, you inspire guests and strengthen the GLOBUS brand. Together with the marketing team, you will implement event strategies and coordinate invitation management, budgets and third-party partners. Your eye for detail makes you the ideal contact person for internal and external stakeholders. In addition, you are responsible for planning and implementing internal and external communication measures and ensure target-group-friendly and brand-compliant communication via various channels. Here you make the difference

  • You implement the existing event strategy at national and local level and manage high-quality events from planning to follow-up. With an eye for detail, you make every occasion an unforgettable experience and make the GLOBUS brand tangible. You ensure close and successful coordination with those responsible in Zurich and Geneva.
  • You are responsible for the development, implementation and reporting of national, regional customer events and customer activations, marketing campaigns and promotions as well as marketing measures in the individual divisions in the sense of 360° communication for the Instore area.
  • You will maintain and coordinate cooperation with third-party partners, branches and brand partners in order to implement joint activities in a brand-compliant and smooth manner.
  • Together with the CRM team, you take care of the invitation management and ensure the optimal care of our guests.
  • You create creative briefs for the creative team and external agencies so that everyone involved works in line with our brand identity.
  • You always have the budget in mind.
  • You use the GLOBUS event tool to coordinate the processes efficiently. With the maintenance of the event database and a close eye on trends and technologies, you bring fresh impulses and ensure the continuous optimization of our processes.
  • You contribute with your ideas to the further development of our event strategy – here your creativity is needed!
  • You are part of a small team, work with agencies and are the contact for our internal and external partners.
  • You are responsible for internal and external communication.

That draws you out

  • You have successfully completed your studies (business administration, marketing or communication) or an education with a focus on marketing or have at least 3 years of proven professional experience in the areas of event management and communication.
  • You have in-depth project management knowledge and are characterized by a high resilience as well as a solution-oriented, independent and reliable working method. With organizational talent and assertiveness, you master challenging projects and always keep an overview.
  • You plan major events from A to Z and have a strong network in the industry.
  • You appear charming and well-groomed - you meet your target group at eye level.
  • In addition to high affinity, you already have experience in the transmission of messages and have a strong feeling for the language of our target group as well as for Ästhetik.
  • You convince with stylish writing and can prepare content for different communication channels.
  • You are sure to communicate in German, English, ideally French.
  • A versatile field of work that combines creative and analytical skills is exactly your thing.
