Full description

As a video producer, you and your team develop customized moving image content for social media platforms (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram). You are responsible for the conception and implementation and ensure that content, tonality and playout correspond to our communicative goals.Please supplement your application documents with work samples or link your portfolio.

Mansioni

  • You design and produce moving image formats for the Galaxus social media platforms and website, including preparation, shooting, setting and post-production
  • You are an important source of inspiration, develop new formats and bring creative ideas to editorial planning
  • Together with the team, you are responsible for the management of the internal studio and equipment and take on tasks in other projects, such as lighting, camera assistance, sound assistance or editing.
  • As a team player in the internal creation team, you promote an open feedback culture and work closely with other teams and departments such as editorial, digital marketing, corporate communications or brand communications management.
  • Optionally, you act as a host on camera and act as a face in your own formats.

Requirements

  • In-depth experience: in social media production, film production or a related industry
  • Initial or continuing education completed: Creation/production
  • You have extensive experience in creating short- and long-form content for various platforms (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram) and in-depth knowledge in video editing (Adobe Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve or Final Cut Pro)
  • You have the ability to design stories for different target groups and implement them independently
  • Current trends on different platforms and in different media formats such as social media, advertising, movies and series are very familiar to you and in the digital world you feel at home
  • The willingness to question “as it has always been done” and propose more efficient, automated or effective solutions
  • Have the courage to try things without planning them down to the smallest detail
  • in German (negotiation confident)
  • English (negotiable)
