Role overview

Near the Schnebelhorn in the Zurich Oberland we operate an outdoor location for people with increased structural needs in a secluded and natural environment on the Beamegg.

For this we are looking for you as a social pä-ediologist / social pedagogue for the residence Beamegg in the Tösstal (50 – 80 %) by immediate or by appointment.

You have many years of experience in the care of people with cognitive impairment, especially with people with challenging behavior and increased structural requirements.
