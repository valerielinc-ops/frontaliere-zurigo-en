Full description

Put to the Point

Does your heart beat for logistical issues in a unique environment? The logistics base of the Swiss Army offers you the opportunity to ensure a smooth process in your specialist area for the acceptance, storage, dispatch, delivery and handover of army equipment. Use your expertise as a link between the Army Logistics Center and the military and civilian service providers of army supplies, supplies, food and personal equipment to ensure a smooth flow of materials throughout Switzerland. An exciting job with a lot of responsibility is waiting for you!

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20638
  • Location: Lufingerstrasse 19, 8302 Kloten

You can make this contribution

  • Vehicles as well as operational and training materials stored, removed and relocated professionally, inventory and provide according to the applicable specifications
  • Assist in the takeover, take-over (WEME) of vehicles and operational and training materials
  • Manage stockpiles and ensure proper storage of materials, vehicles and equipment
  • Incoming and outgoing goods are checked, recorded and the necessary accompanying documents in German are prepared and processed
  • Record material movements, inventory changes and bookings in the ERP system as well as ensure data quality and inventory accuracy
  • Ensure order, safety and cleanliness in the warehouses and logistics areas as well as ensure compliance with applicable processes and instructions

This makes you unique

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as logistician/in EFZ
  • Pronounced service orientation in contact with the troops as well as in their professional advice
  • Independent, efficient and reliable working methods with a strong organisational talent
  • Driving licence of categories B, BE and C (mandatory), CE and F advantageous
  • Good knowledge of SAP (S4/HANA) and secure user knowledge of MS-Office products

We offer this

  • We are committed to the success model Switzerland and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom The logistics base of the Swiss Army (LBA) is there for the force. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss army and manages the materials, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss army with its approximately 3'500 employees and 12,000 militia members. Whether in the workshop or in the office, inside or outside: The LBA offers a wide range of workplaces throughout Switzerland. We have something for everyone.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.

Additional information

Employing the Swiss Army makes sense and brings many advantages. Convince yourself of Army.ch/advantages. Offers from recruiters are not taken into account.

Women are still underrepresented in our administrative unit. Your applications are therefore particularly welcome.
