Tasks

  • Very good knowledge of German and English (C2) in word and writing.
  • You like to work in the team and get actively involved in working with different stakeholders to drive projects together.
  • You are a competent and winning player, have a solid understanding of the excellent universitary environment and work precisely and structuredly.
