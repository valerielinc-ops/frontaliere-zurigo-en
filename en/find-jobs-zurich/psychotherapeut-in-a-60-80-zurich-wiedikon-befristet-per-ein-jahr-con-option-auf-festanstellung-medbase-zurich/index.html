Welcome to Medbase Zürich Wiedikon

Our versatile, lively Medical Center (no walk-in practice) is located in the urban district 3, a few minutes walk from Wiedikon station with a view of the Üetliberg. Here we care for patients interdisciplinary and quality oriented. The team, consisting of about 40 employees, consists of specialists as well as therapists from various special fields. The main focus of the center lies on the interaction of family doctor and complementary medicine.

At Medbase Zürich Wiedikon we appreciate the respectful and helpful handling of the team. We also pay attention to a balanced work-life balance. Working in the part-time shop is possible in all functions, and the working hours can be flexibly designed.
