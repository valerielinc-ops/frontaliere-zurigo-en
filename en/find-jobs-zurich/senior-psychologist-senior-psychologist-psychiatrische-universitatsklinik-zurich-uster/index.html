Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility.

For our ambulatory in Uster in child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement one or more

Senior psychologist / Senior psychologist

80%

Their tasks

  • Professional and organizational co-management of the outpatient team and ensuring high-quality patient care
  • Design and further development of the clinical treatment offer
  • Promoting interdisciplinary cooperation and fostering exchange with internal and external stakeholders
  • Networking and cross-location cooperation within child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy
  • Completed study of psychology (master or doctorate) and recognized further education in psychotherapy
  • Several years and broad therapeutic professional experience as well as ideally leadership experience or corresponding leadership training
  • Team, cooperative and social skills
  • Analytical, innovative and solution-oriented way of thinking with design pleasure
  • Resilient, flexible and responsible personality with assertiveness
  • Together for people: Interdisciplinary cooperation for holistic psychiatric care
  • Strong in the team: appreciative cooperation and mutual support
  • Knowledge that advances: Extensive initial, further and further training offers
  • Working flexibly: working time models that fit your life
  • Future provision with added value: 60% employer contribution to the pension fund
  • Sustainable travel: participation in the ZVV-BonusPass or general subscription
