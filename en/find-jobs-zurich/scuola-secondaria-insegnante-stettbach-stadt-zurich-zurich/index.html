Full description

Immediately we are looking for a teacher at secondary level, 32% (9 lessons, municipally employed).

Tasks

  • Collaboration in the Learning and Promotion Center, which covers various needs
  • Individualized lessons with students from all grades in a small group
  • Lessons on Monday afternoon and in agreement one half day each divided between Thursday and Friday
  • Preferably coverage of subjects: German, English, RZG, MI, career choice

Profile

  • Teaching diploma of secondary education
  • broad knowledge of the learning content of secondary education
  • Willingness to work closely with the LFZ team
  • Flexibility
  • Load capacity
  • reliable and structured working method
  • good access to young people with a multicultural background

We offer

Secondary school with an open and tolerant team spirit, competent co-management, stable team.

About us

The school district Schwamendingen comprises the entire district 12 of the city of Zurich. Schwamendingen is a lively, multicultural residential quarter. With public transport we are easily accessible via Stettbach station and Oerlikon with various tram and bus lines.

The Stettbach Day School is a QUIMS secondary school in Zurich-Schwamendingen. It manages two separate departments A and B and works with both the integrated promotion and the schoolhouse-internal learning and promotion center. The school Stettbach currently comprises sixteen classes, one of which is for the DaZ initial lessons and the lunchtime care.
