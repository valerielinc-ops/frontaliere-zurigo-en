Full description

Become part of our SUPPLY & GRIDS team. We lead, develop, design and plan national infrastructure and energy projects. We accompany these projects from planning to implementation. By including environmental issues, we implement our projects in an environmentally friendly and sustainable way.

Take on responsibility with us as

PROJECT AND CONSTRUCTION MANAGER FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF HIGH-VOLTAGE LINES - UTILITIES & GRIDS 80-100% - ZURICH (high-voltage, overhead and cable lines)

YOUR TASKS

  • Responsible for the planning and supervision of demanding projects with a focus on high-voltage overhead line and cable construction from study to implementation
  • Independent execution of technical project tasks, including coordination of model creation, participation in technical and coordination meetings, preparation of calculations and reports
  • Preparation of deadlines and cost plans
  • Preparation of tender documents for our customers' construction projects
  • Specialist construction management for high-voltage and extra-high-voltage lines with a focus on overhead line and cable construction
  • Coordination and control of work on the construction site up to commissioning
  • National travel activities (approx. 10-20%)

YOUR OPPORTUNITIES

  • Mutual support, knowledge transfer and cooperation are important cornerstones of our way of working
  • Varied and interdisciplinary projects as well as with employees from different departments
  • Diverse team with different professional backgrounds
  • Individual support and encouragement: whether through targeted training or opportunities for professional development
  • Flexible working models with the possibility of working from home as well as a modern infrastructure in a central location

YOUR PROFILE

  • Electrical engineer (BSc / MSc) or comparable education
  • University graduates with motivation and willingness to learn are welcome
  • Professional experience in electrical engineering and especially in overhead line and cable construction is an advantage
  • Good MS Office skills, knowledge of AutoCad, REVIT, Civil 3D or comparable CAD systems is an advantage
  • Experience in dealing with BIM (Building Information Modeling) methodology is an advantage
  • Customer-oriented thinking and enjoyment of working together in a team
  • Very good written and spoken German skills (at least C1)
  • Foreign language skills (French, Italian, English) are an advantage
  • Reliable, independent and precise way of working

Become part of the AFRY success story! We offer creative freedom and personal responsibility, a collegial and international working atmosphere, modern employment conditions, flexible working models, above-average social benefits, attractive development opportunities and generous participation in the public transport subscription as well as a Half-Fare travelcard so that you can easily reach our modern office buildings in central locations. We look forward to receiving your online application!
