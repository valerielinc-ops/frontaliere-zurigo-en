Full description

You work in the various sports and bathing facilities. You maintain green and ice areas and clean hall floors. You are skilled and athletic. You will finish the swimming brevet.

Tasks

  • You clean installations indoors and on building parts.
  • You maintain and control the furniture and building technology installations.
  • You monitor the water quality in the swimming pool.
  • You prepare ice sheets and dismantle them according to ecological specifications.
  • You help with the care of green areas.

Profile

  • Compulsory schooling, upper or middle secondary education
  • Good health and physical fitness
  • Enjoyment of practical activities and technical understanding
  • High awareness of safety and responsibility as well as reliability
  • Ability to work independently and in a team and to deal well with different people

We offer

You can expect a well-founded and varied education. Your apprentices support you on your way to a successful teaching degree. You benefit from attractive employment conditions.

About us

The Sports Department is a service department of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich. It is the competence centre for sports promotion and provides sports facilities, indoor and outdoor swimming pools to the active population and clubs. The organisation of compulsory swimming lessons and voluntary school sport is also one of the core tasks of the Sports Office. With targeted information, the Sports Office motivates more movement and advises and supports the sports organizations.
