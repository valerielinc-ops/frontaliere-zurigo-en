Full description

In mid-2027, UBS will open a new culinary flagship in the heart of Zurich. At Paradeplatz, an exclusive meeting point for customers and the top management of UBS is created. The combination of exclusive private dining, high-quality bistro services and individual event formats creates a place for special encounters, business events and first-class hospitality in the heart of Zurich. Are you our new team member who leads us: in gastronomy at 1. May 2027 or by arrangement from Monday to Friday with working hours between 07:00 and 22:00? The new UBS gastronomy at the Zurich Paradeplatz is an exclusive concept for customers and the management of UBS. This is where sophisticated cuisine, discreet service and international hospitality standards meet. In gastronomy, you assume overall responsibility for the new gastronomy and shape its quality, culture and success right from the start. You combine entrepreneurial thinking with a distinct host personality and create an experience together with your team that meets the highest demands. Your task menu

  • You assume overall responsibility for the operational management and economic development of the company and ensure the highest standards in cuisine, service and hospitality
  • You accompany the new opening from preparation to test operation to go-live, establish clear processes and standards and ensure that the company and team are optimally prepared for the start.
  • You shape an exclusive service and host culture and are present as a visible host personality. Understand the needs of discerning international guests and provide a discreet, professional and personalized guest experience
  • You are the first contact person for UBS on site and are in close contact with internal and external interfaces
  • You develop and take responsibility for the gastronomic offer with a focus on fine dining, private dining, business lunches and exclusive events and recognize new opportunities to inspire guests and clients
  • You lead, coach and develop your team, gain new employees and establish a culture in which commitment, professionalism and the joy of hospitality are taken for granted.
  • You control the operation on the basis of budget and key figures and derive suitable measures for sustainable economic development from this
  • You ensure compliance with and further development of quality, hygiene and occupational safety standards and promote responsible use of food and resources
  • You continuously optimize processes and processes and promote cross-divisional cooperation in the sense of a shared hospitality experience
  • A completed training EFZ in the catering or hotel industry; a hotel school or comparable further education is an advantage
  • 10 to 15 years of professional experience in the upscale catering and/or hotel industry and at least 6 years of management experience in a comparable function
  • Several years of experience in fine dining at the highest quality level, ideally in a company with at least 16 GaultMillau points and / or Michelin Stern award
  • Several years of experience in the event, banquet or catering business as well as in the care of internationally demanding guests, VIPs and clients:
  • Experience of new openings, pre-opening projects and/or in the development of demanding catering concepts
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, business know-how and an experienced handling of budgeting, cost control and key figures
  • Strong leadership skills and the ability to build, promote and inspire a high-performing team
  • Communication strength, a secure presence and the ability to bring together different stakeholders and build trusting relationships
  • A structured, quality-conscious and solution-oriented working method as well as flexibility, resilience and enjoyment of continuous development
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing as well as sound IT skills.
  • Experience in corporate business is an advantage
