Full description

Spitex Stäfa accompanies people to live in their familiar home for as long as possible. We are currently in an exciting phase of further development, which also changes our understanding of leadership. As a team coach, you provide orientation in close cooperation with the management and create a reliable framework, at the same time you strengthen personal responsibility and promote the development of your team. Team coach Care & Housekeeping 80 – 100% Your main tasks

  • You take over the professional, organizational and personnel management of your team and together with the employees ensure a high-quality and needs-based service provision.
  • As a team coach, you strengthen partial autonomy, promote personal responsibility and support your team in actively performing the assigned functions and jointly developing good solutions.
  • You ensure clear processes and reliable cooperation in the team, ensure the exchange of information and accompany employees in their professional and personal development.
  • Together with your team, you ensure high quality, keep an eye on relevant key figures and continuously develop the work processes. To ensure quality, you are directly used by the clients.
  • You work closely with the management, the team coach and external specialist positions and actively contribute your perspective to the further development of Spitex.

You have the following qualifications

  • Nursing specialist FH/HF with proven experience in nursing as well as acquiring in-depth knowledge for billing care services according to KVG
  • Leadership training and several years of leadership experience in health or social services
  • Experience in coaching is beneficial
  • Networked thinking, communication strength, delegation ability, moderation ability, appreciation and willingness to change

You can look forward to that. We offer you advanced employment conditions, five weeks of vacation and even six weeks from the age of 50.
