Role overview

As a Sourcing & Science Intern, you will support a focused 6-month project to build a more complete and actionable view of the global BECCS and biochar project landscape.

Working closely with the Sourcing and Science teams, you will help map the market, improve supplier data quality, conduct project assessments, and identify high-potential suppliers for future sourcing opportunities and customer RFPs.

Through your work, you will strengthen Climeworks’ market intelligence and contribute to informed decision-making across biomass-based carbon removal pathways.
