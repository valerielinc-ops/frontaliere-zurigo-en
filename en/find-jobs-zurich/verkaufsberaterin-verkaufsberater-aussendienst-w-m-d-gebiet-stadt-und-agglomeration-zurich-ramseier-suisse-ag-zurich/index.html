“Together quenching thirst” – that is our mission. Join us and become part of one of Switzerland’s largest beverage producers. In a down-to-earth environment with flat hierarchies, where great importance is placed on personal responsibility and collaboration is actively practiced, we are looking to strengthen our team.

Stephan Fluri HR Business Partner +41 58 434 41 42

These tasks will inspire you

  • Cultivating and expanding relationships with partners in the sales territory
  • Independently acquiring new customers in the hospitality and retail sectors
  • Achieving sales targets through systematic and active management of the sales territory as well as carrying out sales promotion activities
  • Planning, expanding and reporting on sales activities in the territory via CRM tool
  • Preparing customer analyses, including regular reporting to the regional sales manager
  • Conducting negotiations with the assigned regional key accounts

This is the profile we are looking for

  • Outgoing and creative sales professional with negotiating skills, powers of persuasion and passion
  • Experience in field sales (alternatively also in retail) and knowledge of the hospitality industry
  • Excellent written and spoken German
  • Good self-organization, perseverance and a healthy level of ambition
  • Independent, reliable and proactive way of working
  • Well-groomed appearance and pleasant manners
  • Good MS Office skills
  • Residence in the sales territory

Discover your benefits

  • Training and professional development: We invest in the development of our employees. A broad range of internal training opportunities is open to everyone, and we support external training.
  • Attractive vacation policy: Our vacation policy guarantees all employees at least 5 weeks of vacation. As of 50. Year of age, it is 6 weeks, and from the 60. Year of age, even 7 weeks. In addition, it is possible to purchase additional vacation days.
  • Family time: Mothers receive 100 percent of their insured salary during the 18 weeks of maternity leave. The other parent enjoys 3 weeks of family time at full pay.
  • Insurance benefits: We provide our employees with above-average social security and pension benefits and offer special rates on supplementary insurance with various health insurers for their entire family.
  • Perks and discounts: Among other things, our employees benefit from discounts when shopping and refueling, credits for REKA-Geld, and discounted mobile plans for themselves and their families.
