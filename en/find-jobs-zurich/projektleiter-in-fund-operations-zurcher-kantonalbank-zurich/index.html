Swisscanto is the successful fund manager of the Zürcher Kantonalbank. In Switzerland, we are the No. 2 market for investment funds. We offer our customers comprehensive and innovative services and products. Our customers include banks, pension funds, insurance companies, fund management and investment foundations. For our Team Fund Projects in the area of Administration & Operations, we are looking for an experienced and committed personality who supports us in our exciting projects.

Your tasks

  • In projects in cooperation with the Zürcher Kantonalbank and/or external customers, you develop feasible solutions and take over the coordination within the area during the implementation.
  • You skilfully guide new customers through onboarding and until the launch of their product
  • For your own projects, you are responsible for planning, implementing and communicating to stakeholders
  • You represent the interests of your area towards various stakeholders and are perceived as a competent contact person
  • Risks in projects you recognize early and address them adequately
  • You have completed a basic economic education and further training (Fachhochschule, Diplom HF)
  • Strong conceptual and analytical skills as well as a structured and well organized way of working are indispensable
  • Thanks to your winning personality and strong communication skills, you will be able to convince in project work with customers, internal departments (customer bank, legal, administration, IT, etc.) and senior management, taking a determined and solution-oriented approach.
  • Even in hectic situations, you keep a calm head
  • You are very familiar with the fund industry and you have experience in fund management, asset management or asset servicing.
  • You have fluent German and negotiation-proof English skills
  • We offer you opportunities to develop personally and professionally, and an environment that gives you the chance to take on responsibility
  • An employer who takes care of and invests in her employees
  • A centrally located, easily accessible and attractive workplace in the heart of Zurich with the possibility to organize your work flexibly (hybrid)
  • An attractive overall package that offers many benefits and perks beyond wages – from A such as retirement savings to Z such as ZVV Bonuspass
