Description

Our bank stands for innovation, stability and first-class service – and exactly that is also reflected in our IT. As part of our IT strategy, we are looking for you to increase transparency, control and professionalism in our IT processes. Together, you will drive forward the further development of IT Asset and Configuration Management, align the lifecycle management of our IT assets specifically with business objectives, regulatory requirements and risk guidelines, and lay the foundation for efficient IT Service Management. Become part of a diverse team that passionately advances the governance of IT with extensive experience and expertise.

Your responsibilities

  • Take responsibility for the technical design, implementation and further development of IT asset and configuration management processes in accordance with best practices (ISO/ITIL)
  • Further develop and optimize the ITAM and Configuration Management strategy to meet business, regulatory and security-related requirements
  • Establish an end-to-end lifecycle for IT assets (Plan, Acquire, Deploy, Operate, Retire), as well as being responsible for license management and supporting the budgeting process
  • Technical responsibility for Service Configuration Management, including CM processes, policies and CMDB (data model, CI classes, relationships, data flows)
  • Ensure the data quality of IT asset and configuration data through defined data quality checks and establish controls (IKS)
  • Promote collaboration with IT Operations, Security, Risk Management and other stakeholders
  • Increase acceptance of ITAM and CM processes through targeted training and clear communication

Your profile

  • Completed degree in computer science, business informatics or a related field
  • Several years of demonstrable experience in defining, implementing and managing IT Asset Management and/or Configuration Management processes in a medium-sized to large organization, ideally in a regulated environment (e.g. banks, insurance companies)
  • In-depth knowledge of ITIL processes, particularly in the area of IT Asset and Configuration Management
  • Experience with CMDB tools and IT asset management systems, as well as their integration into existing IT landscapes
  • Ability to analyze complex requirements, translate them into clear, implementable process and data models, and implement them pragmatically within the bank
  • Strong communication and facilitation skills to convince various stakeholders, as well as experience working with cross-functional teams
  • Very good command of German and English, both spoken and written (min. C1)

Our offer

  • We encourage employees to contribute their ideas and play an active role in shaping the topics
  • An employer that offers you opportunities to develop personally and professionally while supporting you with many benefits
  • A modern workplace in vibrant District 5 with very good public transport connections is waiting for you
  • Various events and internal networks offer you the opportunity to connect quickly
  • Access to internal and external networks and mentoring programs (e.g. our Talent Community, Queers & Peers, Fondsfrauen membership, "Mensch" network and others)
  • Your colleagues and stakeholders are located in the same building, working from home or in the building across the street – with no time difference
  • An attractive overall package with interesting additional benefits and employee discounts (sports, leisure, childcare, health insurance)
