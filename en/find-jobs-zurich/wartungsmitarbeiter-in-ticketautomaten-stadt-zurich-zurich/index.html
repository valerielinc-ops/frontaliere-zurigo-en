Full description

We are the pulse of Zurich and keep the city in motion every 7.5 minutes. In order for this to work reliably and smoothly, a lot moves behind the scenes of the VBZ. Our colleagues work in over 50 professions: in technology, IT, administration, traffic development, offer planning, infrastructure and many other exciting areas of responsibility. Where we drive, Zurich lives. Come on!

Tasks

  • You are responsible for the exterior and interior cleaning as well as the maintenance and inspection of ticket machines in the ZVV network and carry out the work according to specifications and checklists
  • You carry out simple repairs and maintenance work as part of the maintenance work and pass on non-remediable faults to the responsible service technician
  • You support in the installation and disassembly of BA at stops by means of crane cars
  • You report orders and working hours independently and reliably via SAP

Profile

  • You have a completed apprenticeship, ideally in the technical or craft sector
  • You bring a good technical understanding as well as craftsmanship and can independently analyze and correct simple mistakes.
  • Ideally, you have experience in the field and appreciate being on the road independently and independently
  • You are physically resilient and willing to work regularly in a hunched or kneeling position and lift loads of up to 25 kg
  • You also feel comfortable in changing and sometimes demanding working conditions – for example in noise, different lighting conditions, traffic, weather conditions and cramped space conditions.
  • You work reliably, independently and responsibly and are flexible and resilient even when the workload is high
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Ticket of the Cat. B; C1 of great advantage

We offer

A lot of appreciation for what you do: The people of Zurich like their VBZ. Working with us is proud. We want you to feel good: We are “Friendly Workspace” certified and do a lot for your health. Discover the whole of Switzerland with the GA – and the family also drives really well thanks to generous discounts.

About us

With our vehicles we travel twice a day around the world and transport over 860,000 passengers. This means that every day, 365 days a year and almost around the clock we make an important contribution to the quality of life in one of the most beautiful cities in the world.
