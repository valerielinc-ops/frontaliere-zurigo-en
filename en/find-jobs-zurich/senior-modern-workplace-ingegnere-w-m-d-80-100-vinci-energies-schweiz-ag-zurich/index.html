Main responsibilities

  • In the position of Senior Modern Workplace Engineer (m/f/d), you will oversee our existing IT infrastructure with a focus on Modern Workplace Management for Switzerland.
  • You will be responsible for the further development and operation of our workplace platforms and work closely with our IT support teams as well as internal stakeholders. Your tasks will include:

  • You will manage both our existing SCCM environment and modern cloud management solutions based on Microsoft Intune and Windows Autopilot and accompany the transformation to a modern, cloud-based workplace.
  • Processes and standards: You understand the Group's requirements and implement them in your region.

  • Project management: You will contribute to the further development of our solutions by getting involved in national and international project groups. Requirements
