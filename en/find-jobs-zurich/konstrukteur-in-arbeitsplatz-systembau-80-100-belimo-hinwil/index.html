Full description

Introduction

You design what moves our everyday production: from assembly workstations to material feeders to shelving systems and material wagons. With CAD know-how and initiative, you develop well-thought-out workplace systems and take on exciting small projects.

Main responsibilities

  • Construction and further development of workstation systems and operating facilities using CAD according to information/order of department, line or project managers
  • Preparation of design drawings and risk assessments and participation in technical documentation in cooperation with the technical documentation team
  • Planning and implementation of small projects as well as procurement of new workplace systems
  • Calculation of weight loads and installation of materials and types in SAP
  • Independent production and mechanical processing of workstation systems
  • Selection of suitable machine and standard elements and coordination with suppliers
  • Removal, removal and restart assistance
  • Warehouse management and procurement of BOSCH components as well as compliance with occupational safety

Required expertise

  • Basic technical training, e.g. as a constructor:in, polymechanic:in, automatic technician:in or metalworker:in
  • Ideally, further education at RF level in mechanical engineering, mechatronics or automation
  • Mind. 3-year professional experience in profile system construction
  • Mind. 3-year professional experienceCAD experience, ideally with Siemens NX
  • Further education as a lean production expert and in project management is desired
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Own initiative, sense of responsibility and assertiveness
  • Communication strength, helpfulness and cross-cutting teamwork

People at Belimo

Intelligent buildings are created by committed people. We bring talented people together and create an environment where they can succeed. At Belimo, respect, trust and mutual support across departmental boundaries help turn ideas quickly into practical solutions. Together we achieve more than we could ever achieve alone.

About Belimo

Belimo is the world market leader in the development, manufacture and sale of field devices for energy-efficient control of heating, ventilation and air conditioning systems. Flap drives, control valves, sensors and meters form our core business. Founded in 1975, the company has been listed on the Swiss Stock Exchange (SIX) since 1995.
