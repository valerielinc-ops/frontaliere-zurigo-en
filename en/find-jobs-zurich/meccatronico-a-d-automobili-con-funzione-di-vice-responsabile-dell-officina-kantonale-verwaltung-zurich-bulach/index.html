Full description

The Cantonal Police of Zurich and its approximately 4,000 employees assume responsibility for security in the canton. Our vehicle workshop in Bülach is responsible for maintenance/repair work, 24-hour piquetting and police-specific equipment of the service vehicles as well as motorcycles and trailers. For this demanding and responsible function we are looking for a competent, independent and dedicated automotive mechatronics technician with Stv. function workshop management 100% tasks

  • Professional, personnel and organizational management of the vehicle workshop Bülach in the absence of the group manager
  • Performing diagnostic, repair and maintenance work on emergency vehicles of the Cantonal Police of Zurich
  • Assistance in special missions (driving courses, etc.)
  • Piquet service providers
  • Assistance and organization of the Dübendorf driving course, as well as in the training of the learning profile
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as an automotive mechatronics engineer/in EFZ
  • Additional training as an automotive diagnostician with a federal certificate of advantage
  • Several years of professional experience in the field
  • Brand knowledge BMW, Mercedes-Benz and/or VW is an advantage
  • Good IT user knowledge (Word, Excel)
  • Reliable, accurate operation and high load capacity
  • Flexibility, dedication, teamwork and confidentiality
  • Cat B driving licence (A and C required)
  • Correct reputation and offer
