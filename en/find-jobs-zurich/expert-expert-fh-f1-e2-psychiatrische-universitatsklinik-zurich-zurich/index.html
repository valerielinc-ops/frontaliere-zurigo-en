Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 80-100% At the Center for Acute Psychiatric Diseases, we treat people who show the most severe psychiatric conditions. Often accompanied by acute self- or external danger. We are the psychiatric emergency room, intensive care and acute care. We clarify the incidence situations, treat acute crises and stabilize them, so that patients can move to an outpatient follow-up setting, to a therapy station or to a social psychiatric institution or return. The focus is on individual, recovery-oriented, holistic treatment and care. For our care team on our acute care stations we search immediately or by agreement Their tasks

  • Ensuring and further development of nursing quality in close cooperation with the station management and the competence center specialist development
  • Implementation of a recovery-oriented care as well as co-design of treatment and group offerings, such as psychoeducation and mindfulness groups
  • Fulfillment of the supply mandate according to best practice standards
  • Conception, evaluation and establishment of evidence-based therapeutic group offerings in the care concept of the station
  • Management and implementation of specialist projects for the further development of nursing practice
  • Exemplary function in the care process with reference work as well as mentoring in assessment, treatment planning, implementation and evaluation of the care process

Your profile

  • Completed, recognised vocational training in the field of care FH/BSC (certificate of recognition of the Swiss Red Cross for foreign diplomas)
  • High level of technical, social and self-competence as well as high resilience and flexibility
  • High motivation to engage in a demanding working environment and take on responsibility
  • Readiness for night and weekend services
  • Strong communication strength and innovative and solution-oriented working methods
