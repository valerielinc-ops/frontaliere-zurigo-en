Together with our customers, we connect the real and the digital world. As one of the leading technology companies, Siemens employs around 6,000 people in Switzerland. Shape the digital future with us and shape our IT young talents! You have a flair for the personal development of young people and burn for modern information technologies? You can inspire learners and want to share your IT expertise in an innovative environment? Then read on. For our locations in Zug (60-70%) and Zurich (30-40%), we are looking for a dynamic, inspiring personality with a heart for young people, who will accompany our computer science learners on their way to a successful vocational degree. Your new tasks

  • You will develop and promote the technical, methodological and social and self-competence of 15-20 learners in the basic apprenticeship year Computer Science in the training centers Zug and Zurich
  • You accompany, develop, lead and qualify young people during their basic education and recognize their strengths and fields of development
  • You will design and conduct inter-company courses as well as specific Siemens training modules and combine expertise with practical IT and project tasks
  • Support in cross-vocational training processes: Bspw. Responsibility for the training feedback process (all stakeholders)
  • You will follow current developments in ICT, analyze new trends and technologies and adapt learning content accordingly or develop new training formats
  • You design the training of learners according to agile principles (e.g. Scrum), structure learning content in sprints and accompany learning teams as coach through planning, reviews and retrospectives
  • You accompany real, simple computer science projects in the form of junior services together with learners, promote their personal responsibility and teamwork and supervise internal applications for vocational training
  • You work as a practical trainer: in a learner in the 2nd year of computer science application development and accompany him: she in his: her professional and personal development in everyday work
  • You work in regional committees of computer science and actively represent your talents and experiences in the vocational training and IT landscape.
  • Completed computer scientist apprenticeship with higher specialist training (HF/FH) in computer science or a comparable field, or the willingness to acquire it
  • In-depth professional experience in computer science with good to very good knowledge in application and/or platform development
  • Experience in the training of learners, ideally with a SVEB certificate or equivalent qualification, or the willingness to undertake appropriate training
  • Team players with pleasure in dealing with young people and a feeling for their individual promotion
  • High motivation, willingness to shape and willingness to take on responsibility
  • You think agile and open, go new ways and bring fresh ideas into your education
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing as well as good knowledge of English Period From 1. September 2026 or by arrangement Your benefits
