Full description

For Clinical Area B (Surgery) we are looking for a motivated, committed and proactive personality within the framework of a maternity representation for 6 months in order to support in the management area. This offers the opportunity to gain insight into a center hospital and to actively participate in exciting projects and to take on supporting tasks for departmental and clinic managers as well as clinic managers. Tasks:

  • You support clinic managers in Clinical Area B in various evaluations, analyses and creation of various decision bases
  • You will help set up an outpatient operations center and support the project team in the development and definition of processes, structures and get involved proactively
  • You support with budget creation, personnel requirements planning and procurement projects Profile:
  • You have a completed commercial education, an education as a Med. Practical assistant or a comparable qualification
  • If necessary, you are currently studying in the field of health management / health economics / public health
  • You bring very good MS-Office knowledge
  • An independent, proactive and accurate way of working distinguishes you
  • Thanks to your open personality, you are motivated to learn new things
  • You have a great interest in Swiss health care and health economic issues Benefits: KSW offers employees conditions that enable them to reconcile their professional goals and personal needs. Flexible, flexible working hours, home office as well as a generous maternity leave and employment levels adapted to different situations – all this is possible at KSW, as long as the company allows it. Every year, the KSW offers 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses. We also support you temporally and/or financially with external training.
  • In extraordinary situations, e.g. B. prolonged illness, severe personal stress or difficult experiences in private life as well as in everyday work, various offers of conversation are available to you.
  • The appreciative and friendly cooperation within the teams and across the departments characterizes the working atmosphere at KSW. As a sign of a strong team spirit, the “you” was introduced as a standard at KSW. In addition, the KSW supports annual team events financially and organizes a large staffing event for the whole house every four years. KSW employees benefit from very good social benefits. Thus, the pension fund solution with BVK is far above the legal minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, KSW employees are very well covered.
  • With discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training opportunities and course offers, perks in pharmacies as well as at the most popular health insurance companies. You can benefit from a comprehensive offer for your health.
  • Our daycare center La Luna with spacious opening hours is located next to the KSW. In addition, you can use the income-dependent, financial support from the KSW for the crib costs.
  • In cooperation with cultural partners in and around Winterthur, we regularly offer discounts for concerts and theater performances. The borrowing of media in the in-house library is also free of charge for employees.
