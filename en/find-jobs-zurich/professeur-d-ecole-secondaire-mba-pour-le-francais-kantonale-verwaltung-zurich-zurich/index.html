Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School High Promenade Secondary School Teacher mbA for Physics 75% The cantonal school Hohe Promenade is a long-school school with an old- and modern-language profile (from 2029 onwards additionally with specializations in social sciences and natural sciences), an immersion course German/English and a strong STEM offer. Around 800 students learn at our school, teach and accompany 150 teachers and employees in administration and operations. In attitude and practice, we combine inspired tradition with future-oriented innovation. Committed students, the open-minded college and the latest infrastructure ensure a stimulating environment. More information about our school in the heart of Zurich in the immediate vicinity of the universities can be found at www.kshp.ch . At the beginning of the school year 2027 / 28 or by agreement, 75 job percentages are to be filled at our school in the subject of physics as a secondary school teacher mbA (with special tasks). Their tasks

  • They teach physics in the basic and supplementary subject in the lower and upper high school as well as the interdisciplinary subject "Experimentation and discovery"; Further interdisciplinary formats are possible.
  • You will contribute to convincingly and attractively shaping the future area of engineering, consisting of an interplay between chemistry, physics and computer science (and then teaching).
  • They shape our dynamic, analogue and digital teaching formats.
  • They are involved in the student board and perform special tasks within the school (class teacher office, participation in commissions and school development projects, etc.). Your profile
  • They have completed university studies at master level as well as the teaching diploma for maturity schools (completed or imminent). Ideally, you can teach physics immersively (English).
  • They bring several years of teaching experience at the gymnasium, ideally at a long gymnasium, and stand for demanding and contemporary teaching.
  • They are an open, team-capable and committed personality who cares about both professional training and personal development.
  • They want to engage in a diverse and lively school culture. Our offer
  • In our school you welcome committed students, a competent physics department and an open-minded college.
  • We are a centrally located and modernly equipped school.
  • The employment conditions depend on cantonal specifications. Application Have we aroused your interest? Come and talk to us. We look forward to your application. Please submit your documents no later than 25. October 2026 exclusively via the online tool. For information please contact Martin Schaub, Rector, Tel. 044 224 64 64, e-mail: martin.schaub@kshp.ch.

Pensum: 75%
