Full description

In this key role, you will lead and develop the Switzerland-wide HR Business Partner & Recruiting team of BKW Building Solutions. With your team, you ensure professional HR support for our companies and actively contribute to the further development of our HR organization.

Your tasks

  • Leadership, coaching and further development of the Swiss-wide teams of 8 HR business partners as well as 4 recruiting partners and sourcers
  • Ensuring effective HR Business Partnering and Recruitment for BKW Building Solutions companies as well as continuous development of structures, processes and services
  • Co-design and implementation of the HR strategy as a member of the HR management team of BKW Building Solutions
  • Sparring partner for management and management in strategic HR topics, organizational development and the further development of our leadership culture
  • Management and support of cross-divisional transformation projects, organizational changes as well as mergers and acquisitions
  • Responsibility for the further development of key HR topics such as talent management, succession planning and talent acquisition in close cooperation with the business

You bring that with you

  • Higher education with a focus on human resources, business administration or comparable discipline
  • Several years of leadership experience in HR Business Partnering and/or Recruiting
  • Proven experience in accompanying change processes and organizational developments
  • In-depth knowledge of Swiss labour law and security in complex HR issues
  • Entrepreneurial, pragmatic and solution-oriented personality with high consulting competence
  • Enjoying a certain travel activity within Switzerland
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of French
