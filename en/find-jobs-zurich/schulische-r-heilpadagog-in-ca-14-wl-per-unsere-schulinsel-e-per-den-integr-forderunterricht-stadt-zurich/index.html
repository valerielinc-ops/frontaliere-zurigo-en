Full description

The Kartaus-Münchhalde Day School is located in Seefeld and belongs to the Zurichberg School District. In total, we host 20 classes from kindergarten to sixth grade. Around 450 children are distributed among our two adjacent schoolhouses. As a day school, we are on the road with an extensive range of care. We are a dedicated team in which school and care are well connected.

To build up our new team, we are looking for reinforcement as of 1 August 2026.

Tasks

Your tasks in the School Island

  • Cooperation on the established school island (system-strengthening offer of integrative curative education)
  • Support and accompaniment of students with conspicuous behavior integrative in classroom teaching as well as short-term interventions on the school island
  • Preventive support for all classes and advisory support for teachers

Your tasks as SHP in ISR setting

  • Case management in ISR setting and support for teachers in children with special educational needs
  • Design of the promotional teaching lively, competence-oriented and age-appropriate
  • In the future, team teaching hours and assignments can also be included in cycles 1 and 2, so that a clear contact person is regularly on site in each class.

Working days are Tuesday to Friday.

Profile

  • Teaching diploma (EDK recognized) for the elementary school with corresponding additional training as SHP or willingness to complete this part-time in the next 3 years
  • Resilience, flexibility and an inclusive attitude
  • Enjoy working with children and teaching
  • Good resource management
  • Openness for digital change in schools

We offer

An open-minded, versatile and helpful team in which the teaching staff works closely with each other and with the support. We attach importance to a solution- and goal-oriented cooperation as well as the development and development opportunities of the individual employees. Our school is very well connected to the public transport network and is geographically ideally located (near nature, next to the botanical garden and close to Lake Zurich).

About us

The Zurichberg School District comprises districts 1, 7 and 8 with the districts Altstadt, Fluntern, Hottingen, Hirslanden, Riesbach and Witikon. The district school authority manages 17 school houses. These include 44 kindergartens and over 40 care facilities of different sizes. Of the 13 organizational units, 9 are day schools.
