Full description

Do you want to lead an experienced team, ensure the quality of the revision work and actively shape its further development? The municipal audit independently and on the basis of the legal requirements checks the approximately 160 municipal tax offices in the canton of Zurich. The focus is on the various areas of state taxes as well as the interfaces to the cantonal tax office. In this way, we support the cantonal tax office in its supervisory responsibility and make an important contribution to the quality assurance and further development of tax processes in the canton of Zurich. Your tasks – you lead, shape and move something:

  • You lead and develop your team: You accompany a team of 3-5 experienced auditors, strengthen their individual abilities and create an environment in which cooperation, personal responsibility and further development are actively lived.
  • You are responsible for risk analyses and audit planning: You develop, maintain and implement the risk analyses as well as the audit and audit plan.
  • You ensure quality: You ensure that the examinations in the areas of reference, register, finance and investment are carried out carefully, uniformly and correctly.
  • You coordinate the audit work: You ensure that quality, deadlines and quantities are met, keep track of the audit progress and ensure reliable controlling and reporting.
  • You stay close to practice: You carry out selected on-site audits at the municipal tax offices yourself and bring your expertise and experience directly to bear.
  • You provide advice to the municipal tax offices: You answer questions, discuss findings and explain professional contexts. Your profile – this is what you bring with you:
  • You bring experience: You have in-depth professional experience in the tax, accounting or audit environment – for example as a mandate manager in auditing, as a trustee or tax consultant.
  • You want to progress professionally: You have an in-depth further education in one of the mentioned areas or a business degree and are ready to continuously develop your knowledge.
  • You support and promote your team: You are happy to share your knowledge, have an open ear for technical questions and support your employees in developing themselves and achieving the goals set together.
  • You perform confidently and represent your opinion: you work precisely, think critically and stick to complex questions. In demanding situations, you communicate clearly, argue objectively and do not let yourself get out of peace so quickly.
  • You communicate to the point: You express yourself verbally and in writing very well in German and can also convey complex issues in an understandable way.
  • You like to travel: You bring a high degree of flexibility and like to work directly at the local municipal tax offices in the canton of Zurich on a daily basis.
