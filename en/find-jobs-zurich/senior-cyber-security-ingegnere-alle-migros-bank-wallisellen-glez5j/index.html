Main responsibilities

  • Put our bank in front of cyber threats by analyzing security incidents and developing effective countermeasures.
  • Identify vulnerabilities in our systems, conduct vulnerability analyses and follow-up of appropriate measures.
  • Work together with internal and external partners in security projects to continuously and sustainably improve our cyber resilience.
  • Actively shape the future of our security standards and processes to ensure highest compliance.

Key requirements

  • 5 years: Cyber Security, Computer Science, Business Informatics
  • Completed studies (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): Information/Cyber Security, Computer Science, Business Informatics or similar with appropriate further education
  • Well-founded knowledge in Security Operations & Cyber Incident Reponse, Security Monitoring & Detection (also EDR/XDR/ Endpoint), Network & Firewall, as well as Threat & Vulnerability Management.
  • You are well-versed with the Microsoft Security Stack (Azure Cloud, Defender, Sentinel) & in Linux Security and you know SIEM solutions (Splunk, Sentinel).
  • High level of ownership, resilience and the ability to find pragmatic, risk-oriented solutions in a dynamic environment and to manage and accompany projects across disciplines.
