Full description

PROFILE

  • Completed training as a polymechanic or machine mechanic/EFZ or equivalent
  • Several years of professional and industrial experience in the maintenance of production plants and machines
  • In-depth knowledge of mechanical processing and troubleshooting
  • readiness to become familiar with the maintenance and testing of welding equipment
  • Appropriate prior knowledge
  • Secure handling of IT systems for order and test documentation
  • Strong personal responsibility, initiative and problem-solving competence
  • Communicative, capable of teamwork, strong implementation, flexible and resilient
  • Advantage: Experience in the maintenance of machining machine tools (portal milling machines)

TASKS

In order to succeed a long-standing specialist, we are looking for an experienced specialist for the maintenance of our production plants and machines. The training takes place over a longer handover phase by the previous vacant holder.

  • Implementation of the planned maintenance according to the maintenance plan and troubleshooting during ongoing operation
  • Maintenance, maintenance and repair of our production plants and machines: from welding and robotic plants to surface treatment plants to machine tools
  • Troubleshooting and problem solving on mechanical equipment as well as ensuring availability and operational readiness
  • Participation in general maintenance of equipment and building infrastructure
  • Deputy and cooperation as required in the workshop hand welding machines (maintenance and recurring inspections)
  • Ordering spare parts and participating in meaningful spare parts management
  • In-house production of spare parts that can no longer be procured or only with long delivery periods
  • Order and feedback recording as well as documentation of maintenance and test work in our IT systems
  • compliance with quality, safety and environment requirements
  • Decommissioning of dangerous installations and corrective action
