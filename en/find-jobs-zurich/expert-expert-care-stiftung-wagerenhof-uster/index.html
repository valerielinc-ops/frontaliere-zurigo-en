Full description

To complement our team, we are looking for 1. November 2026 or by arrangement as Specialist care (80 - 100%) At the heart of what we do is people with disabilities. Through differentiated perception, you recognize the needs of our residents and are ready to respect them as independent personalities with their own will. As a responsible, reliable and resilient personality, you support a large professional nursing service team. The Wagerenhof Foundation (affectionately called “Wagi”) in Uster has been a permanent home for around 250 people with multiple cognitive impairments for more than 100 years and offers over 50 employees who need support accompanied jobs. We are a recognized, non-profit institution with a social mission. With a demand-oriented offer of living and care places as well as various day and leisure offers, we promote the quality of life, participation and self-determination of our residents. The special features of the Wagerenhof include size and Dörfli character, which allows space for encounters. For employees with different careers and professionalism, the Wagerenhof Foundation is an attractive employer with a wide range of development opportunities. For learners, Wagerenhof is a competent training partner.

For this we give you the responsibility

  • Professional development of care
  • Development and implementation of nursing-medical concepts and specialist strategies
  • professional support and cooperation with external specialists (specialists, therapists and other specialist bodies)
  • professional cooperation with the nursing home owners
  • Management of care projects
  • Conduct of thematic training

You fit in with us and with this position, if you also put our residents (and our supervised employees) at the center of your activities and you bring the following:

  • Care expert / care expert
  • Training in rehabilitation, dementia and palliative care
  • Professional experience of several years
  • Good professional and social skills
  • High willingness for interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation

This makes us the place for your work

  • The appreciative, human togetherness
  • the well thought-out overall concept
  • the village character and the park-like structure of the Wagerenhof
  • Good accessibility with public transport
