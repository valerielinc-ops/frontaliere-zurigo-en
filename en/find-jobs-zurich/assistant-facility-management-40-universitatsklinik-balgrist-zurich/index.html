Original description

The Balgrist University Hospital is one of the most renowned orthopedic clinics in Switzerland with an affiliated center for paraplegia and international recognition. For the operations area, we are looking for a task(s) to supplement the existing team as of August 1st or by appointment:

  • Collaboration in the management of keys, parking cards and badges, including checking authorizations as well as entering and managing data in the IT software
  • Support in the planning and coordination of internal moves and castling, coordination of room requests as well as ongoing adjustment of signage and labeling in the clinic's buildings
  • Data maintenance (e.g. recording floor plans, filing contracts) in Waveware
  • Vacation and hourly control in the PEP as well as preparation of expense and allowance reports
  • General, varied administrative and organizational activities
  • Support of management and management in the operations area as per assignment Requirements:
  • Completed training in the commercial sector or facility management as well as initial professional experience in a comparable position
  • Structured, reliable and conscientious way of working, high service orientation and a strong organizational talent
  • A team player, flexible and constructive personality who is not out of touch even in the event of unforeseen events Calm down and act pragmatically and solution-oriented
  • Communication skills in order to communicate at different levels appropriate to the level
  • Good IT user knowledge, especially the Microsoft Office range
  • Excellent knowledge of German is required, knowledge of English is an advantage for dealing with the various target groups We offer:
  • Opportunity to work in a respected orthopedic university clinic
  • Collaboration with motivated and committed employees
  • Travel discount of 50% on the ZVV bonus pass or an annual contribution to a general subscription
  • Free day off for your birthday
  • Enjoy a healthy and balanced lunch at reduced prices in our company employee restaurant with free coffee/hot drinks
  • 20% discount in the Balgrist pharmacies You can find further attractive offers below, just a click away: Benefits for our employees
