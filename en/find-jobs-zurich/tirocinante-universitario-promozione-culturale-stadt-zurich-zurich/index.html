Full description

The cultural promotion of the City of Zurich contributes to the vitality and diversity of cultural life in Zurich. The Department of Culture of the Presidential Department promotes cultural creation on three levels: It supports institutions from all fields of art with subsidies. It promotes the cultural creation of freelance artists in the various fields with unique contributions, scholarships and studios. And she herself organizes cultural events and runs cultural institutions.

Tasks

  • They support both teams "Projects Culture" and "Project Promotion Culture" in the implementation of projects and projects.
  • They participate in the development of presentations, concepts, strategy papers and legal bases.
  • They help with the planning and implementation of events or workshops.
  • They take on administrative tasks such as organising meetings or drawing up minutes.
  • They complement the project funding team in supervising the award of grants.

Profile

  • Recent degree (bachelor or master), e.g. in humanities, cultural or social sciences as well as ideally first practical experience in the field of culture and/or project management.
  • They have a quick understanding, work independently and have high social and linguistic skills.
  • They enjoy working in a team and are interested in working at the interface of administration and politics.

We offer

If you are interested in culture, its promotion and would like to gain an insight into the administration and processes of a funding agency, then you are exactly right with us: We are looking for you from 15 January 2027 or by agreement for twelve months. You can expect a varied activity, an insight into the cultural promotion and project management of a municipal administration, a pleasant corporate culture and the opportunity for home office.

About us

The “Culture Project Funding” team comprises the departments: fine arts, film, jazz/rock/pop, classical/new music, literature as well as dance and theatre. The team «Pro-Jekte Kultur» is responsible for projects of the service department Culture. These have a diverse character and range from the implementation of new measures from the cultural mission statement or from the administration, through the development of strategies to the development of new funding instruments.
