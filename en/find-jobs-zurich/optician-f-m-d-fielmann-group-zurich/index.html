The Fielmann Group is the third largest company in the eye-optical industry worldwide. The listed family company based in Hamburg provides its customers with glasses, contact lenses and hearing systems via an omnichannel business model. More than 45 specialist shops are located in Switzerland and with around 180 apprentices, we are one of the largest trainers in the industry.
