Full description

movemi AG is the leading company in the Swiss fitness industry. For our Fitnesspark, we are looking for active support in the area of supervision/cleaning. Become part of a holistic and successful training philosophy: fitness combined with offers for relaxation and nutrition.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for hygiene and cleanliness in the Fitnesspark during opening hours
  • You are responsible for monitoring and maintaining our high hygiene standards
  • You advise our guests on the use of the facility
  • You assist in the laundry room
  • You provide first aid in emergencies

Requirements

  • 1–3 years of experience: In a comparable role
  • Completed basic or further training: No specific education required
  • You have a flair for order and cleanliness
  • You possess an independent way of working and can assert yourself in everyday work
  • You possess a valid BLS/AED certificate
  • You have a high degree of flexibility regarding shift planning (7-day shift operation, weekend shifts every 2 weeks)
  • You are willing to take on short-term substitutions in case of illness or vacation
  • German (negotiation level)

  • Central workplace: Our studios are located in well-connected central locations
  • Mobile subscription: National flat-rate subscription from CHF 10.– / month (Swisscom / digitec connect)
  • Migros Bank: Fee-free private and investment account with preferential interest rates
  • Employee offers: Various discounts in the areas of holidays and insurance (Hertz up to 10%, Generali 25%, Axa insurance up to 15%, etc.)
  • "Du" culture: We stand for respectful interaction on an equal footing
