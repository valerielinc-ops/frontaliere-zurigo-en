Full description

USZ Campus

By agreement

Bring your expertise where it makes the biggest difference: In ortho-optics at the University Hospital Zurich, you diagnose and treat visual and eye movement disorders in an interdisciplinary manner and at the highest medical level – with a focus on early detection, individual therapy and sustainable quality of life.

Their main tasks

  • Prevention, diagnosis and therapy of amblyopias, skull diseases, eye movement disorders and neuro-central vision disorders
  • Accompanying the students Orthoptik HF
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation and regular internal training
  • Possibility to complete further training as a practical trainer: in (SVEB certificate)

Your profile

  • Diploma in Orthoptik as well as in-depth orthoptic, strabological and neuroophthalmological knowledge in theory and practice
  • At least 2 years of professional experience
  • In-depth expertise in the field of orthoptics and neuroophthalmology
  • They are communicative, accustomed to independent and service-oriented work as well as resilient, conscientious and flexible
  • You enjoy working in a team
  • You are engaged in professional development

Our offer

You work in an innovative team of several orthoptists and vocational trainers as well as ophthalmologists at the University Hospital Zurich, Department of Pediatricophthalmology, Strabology and Rare Diseases. We offer you a varied and challenging task in an exciting environment in a university setting. For this post, dossiers from recruiters cannot be considered. Thank you for your understanding.
