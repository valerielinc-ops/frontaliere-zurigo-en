Full description

Competence and precision in the city of today, for the Zurich of tomorrow.

On February 1, 2027 or by agreement, we are looking for you as a committed geomatician.

Tasks

  • You advise our customers on the construction permit procedure from the perspective of geomatics + surveying.
  • You support our team in drawing up ordered cadastral plans.
  • You integrate project data from the planners and architects and create further special plans.
  • You accompany orders from advice to invoicing.
  • You accompany our learners during their assignment in the data transfer.

Profile

  • You have completed training as a geomatician.
  • You have good knowledge of GIS systems (preferably geomedia) and CAD systems as well as basic knowledge of official surveying.
  • You are a structured and reliable personality with strong competences in personal responsibility, independence and communication, complemented by technical expertise.

We offer

An attractive activity at the interface between politics, administration and the population awaits you. As a service department of the City of Zurich, we offer advanced employment conditions with excellent social benefits and flexible working hours. We support you in keeping your knowledge up-to-date and offer you a comprehensive training offer for your continuous development. We are a motivated and generationally diverse team and, in addition to a healthy performance orientation, attach importance to equality and a good balance between work and family / private life. Your workplace is located at Weberstrasse 5 in Zurich.

About us

Geomatics + Surveying (GeoZ) is a service department of the City of Zurich with around 85 employees (including 11 apprentices in four professions). We measure the city in high quality, make the geoinformation available to interested parties and strive for innovative solutions as a forward-looking and reliable service provider.
