Digital Business Zurich | Pensum: 80-100 %

Working on the pulse of digital transformation. Our Digital Business department makes it possible. Together with us, shape the future of real estate management, develop innovative products and processes and move Livit forward together with us. In digital business, you can expect a great, interdisciplinary team, an inspiring working atmosphere and exciting projects in which you can actively participate. Are you excited about innovations, the latest systems, an agile setting and new challenges? Then you are exactly right with Livit. We look forward to your personal contribution.

Your tasks

  • You will lead the project and quality management, architecture & engineering, platform & operations and data & analytics teams professionally and in personnel
  • You are responsible for the stability, security, scalability and cost effectiveness of the platforms and services
  • You will continuously develop an ITIL-4 compatible service model with clear KPIs and establish a high quality of service
  • Drive end-to-end automation and reduce technical debt and dependencies on individual technologies and vendors
  • You anchor security-by-design, architecture principles and quality standards across all areas
  • You are responsible for resource, budget and supplier management and ensure efficient end-to-end supply chains
  • Strengthen the company’s data and analytics capabilities and promote the use of modern platform and cloud services
  • You work closely with Digital & Business Services and position your area as a reliable platform and enabling partner

Your profile

  • You have a university degree in business, business informatics, engineering or a comparable education
  • You bring several years of management and practical experience in the areas of IT infrastructure, platforms, engineering or operations
  • You have already successfully led several teams or leaders and develop organizations sustainably
  • You lead empoweringly, promote personal responsibility and create an environment where teams can reach their full potential
  • You combine strategic thinking with a high implementation strength and convince through pragmatism, decision-making and reliability
  • You have an in-depth understanding of modern IT platforms, cloud services, architecture and operational models and security topics
  • You convince through customer orientation, high social and self-competence as well as a professional and cooperative approach at all levels
  • You communicate stylishly in German and English, get excited about new technologies and actively share your knowledge with others

For this vacancy, only direct applications are considered and we do not accept dossiers from recruitment agencies.
