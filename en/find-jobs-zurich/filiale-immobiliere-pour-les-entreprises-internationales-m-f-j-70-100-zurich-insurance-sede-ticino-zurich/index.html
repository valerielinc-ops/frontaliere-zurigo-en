Original description

Do you have experience in the non-life business and want to deepen your expertise further? Are you fascinated by the complex and dynamic tension between growth and profitability, while at the same time you want to find the right insurance solution for every customer?

As a property underwriter for international corporate customers, you ensure a market-oriented and risk-oriented drawing. With excellence, integrity and process efficiency, you are responsible for the operational risk assessment in exchange with our sales partners and ensure that our customers receive a first-class customer experience at all times.

Does working with our customers and brokers motivate you to find the best possible insurance solution? Then this place is just right for you!

Find out here why Zurich Switzerland is one of the "Top 3" employers in Switzerland. Become part of it and apply as a property underwriter for international corporate clients.

What you do

  • You are responsible for identifying, evaluating and assessing risks for your assigned corporate customers
  • You draw risks independently and profit-oriented within the framework of the competence regulations and the internal guidelines
  • You ensure market-oriented risk selection including portfolio quality of the company products and conduct risk consulting for our sales partners
  • You advise and negotiate with direct customers and act as a professional contact for our sales partners as well as within Zurich
  • You help shape the strategic orientation of operational underwriting and contribute to the continuous optimization of processes and services

What you bring

  • Commercial basic training in the insurance industry, including subject-specific further education (e.g. Federal Law). Insurance certificate or HFV
  • Relevant professional experience in international corporate business and operational underwriting or product management
  • Proven experience in successful relationship management with sales partners and the associated customer-, solution- and performance-oriented workflow
  • Good analytical skills and independent work, combined with a strong sense of responsibility
  • Confident knowledge of German and English, other national languages beneficial
