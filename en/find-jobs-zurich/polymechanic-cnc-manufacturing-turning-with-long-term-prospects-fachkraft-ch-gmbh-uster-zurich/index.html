Full description

Mechanical components are precisely manufactured on modern CNC machines and programs are processed independently. In a collegial team, you get long-term prospects and attractive leisure arrangements. For our client we are looking for an activity

  • Precisely manufacture mechanical parts on modern CNC machines
  • Create NC programs independently and develop program documents
  • Create and optimize CAM programs with TopSolid 7
  • Inspect manufactured parts independently and carry out general mechanic work
  • Requirements
  • Completed training as a polymechanic
  • Experience in independent programming of CNC machines
  • Knowledge of CAM programming, ideally TopSolid 7
  • Good German skills as well as reliable and independent work
  • Five to six weeks of holidays plus six pre-cooked Fridays
  • Long-term perspective in a safe working environment
  • Collegial team and free parking
