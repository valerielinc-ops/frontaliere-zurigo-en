Marketing Manager, Paramit CDMO Solutions

Morgan Hill, CA 95037 Full-Time | Exempt | Remote Travel: Approximately 10–20% as needed Salary Range: $100,000–$125,000

Make an Impact at Tecan

Are you a strategic, hands-on B2B marketer who understands how to position complex technical solutions and turn market insights into commercial opportunities? Join Tecan as a Marketing Manager, Paramit CDMO Solutions, where you'll play a key role in building awareness, generating qualified demand, strengthening customer engagement, and supporting the growth of Tecan's CDMO business.

In this role, you'll serve as a strategic marketing partner to Paramit Commercial Teams, Sales, Business Development, Product Management, Brand, Internal Communications, and Global Regional Teams. You'll develop and execute integrated marketing strategies, campaigns, digital programs, thought leadership, events, and sales enablement initiatives that support commercial objectives and strengthen Paramit's position in the market.

The ideal candidate brings experience marketing complex B2B technical solutions or services, particularly within EMS (Electronic Manufacturing Services), CDMO, contract manufacturing, electronics manufacturing, OEM, Life Sciences, Medical Technology, or related industries. You'll need to be comfortable translating technical capabilities and customer needs into clear, compelling value propositions and marketing programs.

This is a highly collaborative and hands-on role for a marketer who can move comfortably between strategy and execution, work independently in a remote environment, and partner effectively with commercial teams across a global organization.

How Will You Make an Impact?

  • Develop and execute strategic marketing plans for Paramit, Tecan's CDMO offering, aligned with business and commercial objectives.
  • Develop messaging frameworks, value propositions, and market positioning that clearly communicate Paramit's capabilities, differentiation, and customer value.
  • Translate complex technical, manufacturing, and business concepts into compelling marketing messages and customer-facing content.
  • Design and implement integrated marketing campaigns, including Account-Based Marketing (ABM) initiatives.
  • Create and manage multi-channel marketing programs that drive brand awareness, engagement, qualified demand, and commercial growth.
  • Develop and execute digital marketing initiatives across LinkedIn, email, website, and other relevant media channels.
  • Manage website and LinkedIn content and lead-generation activities in partnership with Paramit Commercial Teams.
  • Develop thought leadership content and executive communications that strengthen market positioning and industry visibility.
  • Gather and translate customer, market, competitive, and industry insights into actionable marketing strategies, campaigns, and content.
  • Partner with Sales and Business Development to understand target customers, buying processes, market opportunities, and customer needs.
  • Develop sales enablement materials, including presentations, brochures, case studies, campaign toolkits, and digital assets.
  • Support commercial teams with marketing programs designed to strengthen customer engagement and advance opportunities through the sales funnel.
  • Manage the assigned marketing budget and marketing resources to maximize effectiveness and return on investment.
  • Monitor campaign performance, lead quality, conversion metrics, and marketing ROI, using data-driven insights to optimize marketing activities.
  • Lead, execute, and support local and global events, including industry conferences, customer events, trade shows, and webinars.
  • Maintain and update the Partnering Business presentation library and coordinate with content owners to ensure materials are current and aligned.
  • Collaborate with Brand and Internal Communications to ensure consistent messaging and alignment with Tecan's corporate brand.
  • Collaborate with Global Regional Teams to support effective marketing execution across markets.
  • Support the development of executive-level presentations and communications for internal and external meetings.
  • Identify opportunities to improve marketing processes, customer engagement, lead generation, and overall marketing effectiveness.
