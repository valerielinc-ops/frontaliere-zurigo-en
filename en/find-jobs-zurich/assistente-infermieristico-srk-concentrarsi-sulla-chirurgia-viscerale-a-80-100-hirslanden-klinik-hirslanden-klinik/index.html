Do you like to get involved and feel comfortable in a lively hospital environment?

On the ward with a focus on visceral surgery, you can expect a versatile day-to-day work in which you actively support our team and ensure that it runs smoothly. With your reliable and team-oriented manner, you make an important contribution to the care of our patients.

YOUR TASKS

  • Carriage of nursing services in consultation with nursing staff
  • Support of nurses in day-to-day work in the station as well as smooth department operation
  • Implementation of patient and material transport
  • Support for documentation of care measures and monitoring of patients’ vital signs
  • Communication with relatives and other specialist areas in the context of patient care and coordination of appointments
  • Early and late service missions

Your profile

  • Completed training as a nursing assistant / nursing assistant SRK or equivalent qualification
  • First experience required in acute hospital
  • High physical fitness, resilience and pleasure in varied tasks
  • Responsibility, reliability and teamwork
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing, other languages are advantageous

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. You work with a modern medical clinic information system and can expect extensive training and development opportunities from us. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family responsibilities. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. Employees who have to move completely for their work receive four additional paid days off with a 100% workload. With a reduced workload, the days off reduce accordingly. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from an equivalent financial compensation. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.
