Full description

For our IT department we are looking for an System Engineer -/in (2nd level support) As a system engineer, together with our ICT team, you are responsible for the maintenance, development and renewal of our diverse systems such as virtualization (VMware) networks, Apple ecosystem (JAMF), security awareness, Azure and Office 365 and other applications. In this role, you are also responsible for the analysis and resolution of IT disruptions in 2nd level support and contribute to the stable operation of our IT infrastructure. In project teams, you plan, develop and integrate new services and technologies. You also support the 1st level support for demanding technical requests. Good technical understanding, an independent working method and the motivation to familiarize ourselves with new topics are important to us. We offer you a modern working environment, varied tasks and the opportunity to actively contribute and develop your experience. What matters is you. Every single person is at the center with their individual quality of life. We do everything we can to ensure that our approximately 250 residents, 246 daycareers and 52 employees at protected workplaces in the Wagerenhof can enjoy the highest possible quality of life. For employees of different backgrounds and professionalism, Wagerenhof is an attractive employer and a competent training partner for learners of all levels. For this we give you the responsibility:

  • Together with our ICT Operations team, you will supervise and maintain our IT systems
  • You are jointly responsible for the constant guarantee of security.
  • Maintenance of IT documentation and instructions
  • Administration of own areas of responsibility (update/release management, migration, roadmap, knowledge transfer)
  • Analysis and resolution of incidents You suit us and this place if you bring the following:
  • Typically, you bring a completed ICT training or are a dedicated “techie” (cross-entry technician)
  • Several years of experience in 1st Level or 2nd Level Support
  • You bring a great sense of responsibility, self-initiative and an independent working method
  • Readiness for further education and development of your skills
  • High customer orientation and team skills. This makes us the place for your work:
  • The appreciative, human togetherness
  • The well thought-out overall concept and the technical support available at all times
  • The village character and the park-like structure of the Wagerenhof
  • Good accessibility with public transport. We currently only consider direct applications for this position For questions please contact Susanne Scherrer, Human Resources, 044 905 13 91.
