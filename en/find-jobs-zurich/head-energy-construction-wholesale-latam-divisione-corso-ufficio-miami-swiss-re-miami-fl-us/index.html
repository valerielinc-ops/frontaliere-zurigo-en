Head E&C Wholesale LatAm Location: Miami Office - Miami, Florida Shape the future of risk in the engineering in Latin America with Swiss Re Corporate Solutions Are you a seasoned Lead Underwriter ready to drive innovation in risk management for complex construction and engineering projects and renewable energy deals? Join us in this pivotal leadership role where your technical expertise and strategic mindset will influence how major projects are managed across Latin America. About the Role As the Head E&C Wholesale LatAm, you'll lead our vision and strategy to drive profitable growth across the Latin America. Working collaboratively with the Market Unit Head for IAMEA (Ibero-America, Middle-East and Africa), you'll shape our underwriting approach in this region while ensuring alignment with broader business objectives. Key Responsibilities

  • Strategic Leadership - Define the vision and strategy for the E&C LatAm Wholesale Portfolio, driving profitable growth and performance
  • Portfolio Management - Take accountability for the Profit & Loss of the E&C LatAm Wholesale portfolio, applying risk selection as global risk appetite and ensuring profitable growth
  • Technical Excellence - Serve as point of reference on technical issues and stay ahead of emerging technologies and market trends in your sub-region for E&C purposes
  • Relationship Building - Cultivate strategic relationships with key clients and brokers, representing Swiss Re Corporate Solutions at the highest levels for respective Market Units
  • Risk Governance - Ensure full compliance with applicable underwriting standards and risk management frameworks
  • Innovation - Drive the development of new solutions to address evolving client needs in the engineering and construction sectors

About the Team You'll be part of an international team with diverse technical and scientific backgrounds across the global and shall lead a team of 3 underwriters located in Mexico City. You'll work closely with colleagues across the global Engineering & Construction and Renewable Energy community. Our team combines excellent technical expertise with strong team spirit, always focused on delivering the best solutions for our clients. The collaborative environment encourages innovation and knowledge sharing across borders.

About You

You're an experienced engineering professional who has successfully transitioned into insurance leadership, combining deep technical knowledge with commercial acumen. You thrive in collaborative environments while being decisive when needed. Your strategic vision is matched by your ability to execute and deliver results, and you're passionate about developing talent and fostering an inclusive team culture.

We are looking for candidates who bring

  • University degree in an engineering discipline (civil, mechanical, chemical or process engineering) or comparable education with strong affinity to the engineering & construction industry
  • Minimum of 5 years of experience as a professional engineer, including managerial roles and site experience with construction/erection supervision
  • Minimum of 10 years engineering underwriting experience with a primary insurance or reinsurance company
  • Proven track record in leading and managing teams with diverse backgrounds

I t would be an advantage if you had

  • Broad network with corporate clients and brokers in Latin America
  • Experience in change management with a visionary mindset
  • Excellent negotiation and communication skills across all organizational levels
  • Demonstrated commitment to fostering diversity and talent development
