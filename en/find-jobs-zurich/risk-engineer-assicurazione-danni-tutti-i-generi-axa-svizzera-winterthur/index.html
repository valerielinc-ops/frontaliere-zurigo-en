Shape your future – together with us!

Risk engineering in property insurance means analyzing and evaluating risks using engineering technology and controlling them with targeted measures in order to prevent or limit major losses. In this way, you create transparency about our customers' risk profile, support risk-appropriate underwriting and strengthen the resilience of companies. AXA and its customers benefit from well-founded decision-making principles, effective loss prevention and partnership-based cooperation on a win-win basis.

If you enjoy advising and dealing with customers, have good communication and analytical skills, and regular travel within Switzerland doesn't put you off, then read on - our team of experts looks forward to hearing from you!

This is what awaits you

Operational business

  • You carry out risk assessments for major customers domiciled in Switzerland.
  • You analyze companies regarding fire, explosion, natural hazards and business interruption.
  • You assess structural fire protection, property protection, extinguishing systems and the organization of fire prevention.
  • You create risk reports with clear prevention measures for all stakeholders (underwriting, brokers, co-insurers, customers).
  • You will accompany the implementation of these measures and support customers in improving their risk quality.
  • You evaluate risk reports from other companies and calculate the maximum subscription share of AXA in collaboration with underwriting.

Training

  • You pass on your knowledge to underwriters and risk consultants in training courses and presentations.
  • You maintain professional exchanges with risk engineers from other insurers and bring in best practices.

Further development of risk engineering

  • You will help to continually update our guidelines and optimize the tools used.
  • You analyze how other hazards besides fire can be integrated into risk engineering practice.

This is what you bring with you

  • You have a (technical) university degree in engineering or equivalent training (e.g. environmental or natural sciences, process engineering, chemistry, mechanical engineering).
  • You have completed further training in the areas of fire protection, explosion protection or business interruption (BCM).
  • You have several years of experience in risk engineering at an insurer or broker or have worked in security consulting at an engineering company.
  • You have already worked in industry and are familiar with production processes.
  • Ideally, you have insurance and business expertise.
  • You advise customers and internal stakeholders confidently, communicate clearly and appear convincing.
  • You are willing to travel regularly within Switzerland and enjoy visiting customer companies on site.
  • You express yourself fluently in spoken and written German and have a good knowledge of Italian
  • Knowledge of English and French is an advantage.
