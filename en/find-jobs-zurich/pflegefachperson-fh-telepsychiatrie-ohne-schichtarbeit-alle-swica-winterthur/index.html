Full description

Become a part of santé24 and bring your expertise to our innovative online therapy offer! santé24 is the online practice of SWICA and offers patients modern, low-threshold and high-quality healthcare. In order to further develop our psychological-psychiatric offers, we are looking for a committed nurse FH or a committed nurse FH with a focus on psychiatry who or who enjoys actively shaping mental health in the digital space. This is what we offer you

  • A versatile and meaningful activity at the interface of psychiatry, care, telemedicine and digital care
  • The opportunity to actively shape a new role within santé24 and further develop innovative care offerings
  • An interprofessional, committed and appreciative working environment
  • Modern forms of work with digital advice via video, telephone and other secure communication channels
  • Place of work in Winterthur or Basel
  • Temporary employment for 30 months This is how you shape health
  • You accompany patients with mental illnesses in a nursing and therapeutic support. You work by phone and video based in online setting
  • You carry out structured assessments of the mental state as well as risk and need assessments by phone, especially in the event of crises, suicidality or psychosocial stress
  • You triage patients after assignment by doctors, health consultants or other specialists of santé24 into suitable internal or external offers
  • You support patients in dealing with symptoms, dealing with everyday life, self-management and activating personal resources
  • You guide patients in online self-help trainings, accompany their course and promote motivation and adherence
  • You work closely with psychologists, psychotherapists, psychiatrists and other internal and external partners
  • You document professionally in the electronic system and contribute to the quality assurance and further development of digital supply offers This sets you apart
  • Completed degree program Nursing FH (BSc) or an equivalent recognised education with a focus on psychiatry
  • At least five years of professional experience in psychiatric or psychosomatic care, ideally with experience in crisis and risk management
  • Competence in nursing dialogue, crisis intervention, motivational interviewing or comparable approaches
  • Enjoyment of working in digital setting as well as secure communication in video and telephone consulting as well as very good user knowledge in electronic documentation
  • Structured, independent and responsible working methods as well as high communication skills and empathy
  • Resilience, security of action in demanding situations and the ability to prioritize and escalate appropriately
  • Interested in developing and actively shaping a new role within santé24
  • Very good knowledge of German and French
  • English and Italian skills are beneficial Why SWICA
  • We build on humanity. That is why we focus on openness, respect and trust in our everyday work. We take on responsibility and think in terms of solutions – together, for our customers. This is also why we have been number 1 in customer satisfaction for many years.
  • We trust you and give you space. Annual working hours, at least 5 weeks of holidays, part-time models and generous home office within the scope of the possibilities of the job – you make your everyday life largely flexible.
  • We value and reward your work with a fair market wage.
  • Because health is everything. With our company health management and region-specific offers, we take care of your well-being – so that you are healthy and energetically involved.
  • We think long term. With three savings plans, pensum-dependent coordination deduction and benefits well above the legal minimum, you ensure good for your future. Andri Kömeter, HR Recruiting, is happy to provide you with information.
