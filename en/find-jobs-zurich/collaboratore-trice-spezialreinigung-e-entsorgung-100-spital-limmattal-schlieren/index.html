100% by agreement The special cleaning and disposal team takes on numerous important tasks in the Limmattal Hospital and is in daily contact with many different departments. As a result, you can expect a versatile and varied field of work with exciting interfaces throughout the hospital operation. We are looking for a dedicated and competent professional who sees their strengths in this versatile area of responsibility. Direct applications are preferred, external recruitment offices are not treated as a priority. Here they engage

  • Maintenance, intermediate and basic cleaning work
  • Cleaning/maintenance of work equipment and machines incl. Defect reporting
  • Disposal and special disposal operations
  • Assistance with internal relocations or setting up meeting rooms
  • Communication with interfaces

Size

  • As far as possible, training as a building cleaner EFZ, housekeeping EFZ or business maintenance EFZ
  • Advantageous Health Care Cleaning Experience
  • Exact and organized working method with an eye for the whole and for detail
  • Flexible and responsible alignment of cleaning to customer needs
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German and a service-oriented attitude
  • Working hours vary from Monday to Sunday, between 06:00 and 21:00

Your new place of work Our top location (Limmattalbahn, 4 S-Bahn, 1 bus line in the immediate vicinity) is complemented by the LIMMI mobility concept:

  • Reduced employee parking
  • Motorcycle parking, lockable bicycle parking
  • With the ZVV Bonus Pass / Z-Bonus Pass we offer an attractive public transport subscription for employees

s'LIMMI: Your partner for life As an important health partner with a comprehensive training mandate, the Hospital Association Limmattal takes over the extended basic medical care of more than 100,000 patients annually, in all phases of life. The LIMMI is broadly specialized and offers high-quality medical care as well as in-depth expert knowledge in numerous surgical, medical and nursing specialist and specialist disciplines. The hospital association has 188 beds in the acute care sector, 126 beds in long-term care, a hospital-bound emergency service as well as nine operating rooms and eight intensive care facilities. More than 1,700 employees from 58 nations provide excellent and diverse services every day.
