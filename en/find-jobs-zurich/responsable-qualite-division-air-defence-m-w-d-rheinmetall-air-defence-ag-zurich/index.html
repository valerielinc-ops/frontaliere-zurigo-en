Full description

For the Air Defence Division, we are looking for a Head of Quality Division Air Defence (m/f/d) at the Zurich location at the earliest possible time. In this newly created function, you are responsible for the strategic and functional management of the quality function at the locations in Zurich, Rome and Neuss. You lead the local Heads of Quality professionally and develop a uniform framework for quality strategy, processes, standards and key figures. They report to the division’s COO and work closely with division management, site management and relevant corporate functions. You will assume the following specific tasks:

  • Development and implementation of the quality strategy for the Air Defence Division
  • Functional management and technical management of the Heads of Quality at the locations in Zurich, Rome and Neuss
  • Establishment and further development of a uniform quality governance across the division
  • Harmonization of quality processes, methods and standards across sites
  • Definition and implementation of a uniform quality KPI and reporting system - analysis and benchmarking of quality performance as well as derivation and implementation of improvement measures
  • Coordination of cross-site CAPA, 8D and root cause activities as well as escalation of key quality risks
  • Ensure implementation of relevant quality and certification requirements and coordinate divisional audit activities
  • Further development of supplier and product quality management with cross-site relevance
  • Promoting cross-location knowledge and best practice exchange as well as a common quality culture WHAT YOU SHOULD BUILD
  • Completed degree in engineering, quality management, industrial engineering or comparable qualification
  • Several years of experience in an operational quality management function in a regulated industrial environment
  • Experience in cross-site management or harmonization of quality organizations and systems
  • In-depth knowledge of ISO 9001, AS/EN 9100 or comparable quality standards
  • Good knowledge of Lean / Six Sigma as well as common quality methods, in particular 8D, CAPA and Root Cause Analysis
  • Experience in matrix leadership without direct disciplinary leadership responsibility
  • Strong analytical and strategic skills as well as a structured and solution-oriented way of working
  • High communication and assertiveness as well as experience in stakeholder management at different hierarchical levels
  • International and intercultural experience
