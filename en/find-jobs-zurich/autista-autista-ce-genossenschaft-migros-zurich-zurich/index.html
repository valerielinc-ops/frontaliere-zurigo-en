Full description

We are moving! The transport department of the cooperative Migros Zurich is responsible for the distribution of goods within its own economic area. Every day you supply 150 supermarkets and restaurants and ensure the transport of returns.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Zurich, operations headquarters Herdern, Pfingstweidstrasse 101, 8005 Zurich

Important information

Start of work from 03:30 We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Delivery of our points of sale according to the daily orders of the disposition
  • Assistance in loading and unloading vehicles

Requirements

  • 1-2 years of professional experience: in the required area
  • No training required: road transport specialist*woman
  • In-depth knowledge of various transport equipment such as solo vehicles, semi-trailers, semi-trailers and trailers
  • Readiness for Saturday work and irregular working hours
  • Flexibility and independence in the various assignments
  • Residence in the Zurich area is advantageous
  • Detailed knowledge of Migros locations
  • German (good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Central location: Working in Zurich West
  • Handy subscription: You can look forward to special conditions for the Handy subscription
  • Occupational pension provision: Excellent pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Social benefits: Fair social benefits
  • Food: Discount in the staff restaurant
  • Cumulus Points: You get additional Cumulus Points when shopping
  • Migros Bank: Preferred interest on private and investment account, free account management, discounted mortgages and more
  • Club School Migros: Participation in the course costs in the club school
  • Leisure and cultural offers: You can benefit from discounted tickets at selected cultural events
  • Sports and fitness offers: You can get a total free time bonus of max once a year. CHF 1000.– e.g. redeem for an annual subscription to the M-Fitnesscenter

Contatto

Application and contact Monika Allenbach

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision to advance or withdraw from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Test work: A trial day or half day gives the opportunity to get to know the future working environment and the team members better. And conversely, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week

Grado di occupazione: 80-100 %
