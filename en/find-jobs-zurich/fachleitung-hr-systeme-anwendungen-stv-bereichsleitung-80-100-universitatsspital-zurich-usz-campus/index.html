Full description

You are actively involved in shaping the digital HR landscape at USZ and are responsible for the professional implementation and further development of HR systems, with a special focus on the new HR Suite. You translate technical requirements into efficient system solutions and digital workflows, ensure sustainable operation and accompany releases, tests and system adjustments. At the same time, you observe new technologies such as automation and AI and examine their benefits for HR. A versatile key role with a great deal of room for manoeuvre and a clear development perspective.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • Analysis, design, harmonization and continuous optimization of HR processes and workflows • Development and further development of user-oriented end-to-end processes • Adopting and structuring professional requirements from HR and other stakeholders • Professional support for the implementation of requirements in the HR Suite and other relevant HR applications • Implementation and moderation of process and requirements workshops • Preparation and maintenance of process descriptions, work instructions and professional documentation • Planning and coordination of technical tests, key user tests and acceptances • Development and implementation of training and competence measures • Establishment, coordination and further development of the HR key user network Requirements • Completed education or training with a focus on human resources, business administration or comparable discipline • Several years of professional experience in the HR environment as well as a sound understanding of HR processes and their organizational contexts • Several years of leadership experience • Experience in the analysis, design and optimization of end-to-end processes and workflows • Experience in the structured translation of technical requirements into a system engineering environment • Experience with HR systems and ideally with the introduction or further development of integrated HR solutions • Ability to prioritize several parallel topics and to keep an overview even in demanding situations Independent, competence-oriented decision-making including careful consideration of opportunities and risks • Structured, independent and solution-oriented working method • High level of communication and consulting competence as well as addressee-friendly behaviour We offer: • Demanding specialist function with high strategic relevance • Design latitude in a central control area • Active participation in the digital HR landscape • Modern working conditions and development opportunities • Collaboration in a dedicated and specialized team with short decision paths and a high professional impact
