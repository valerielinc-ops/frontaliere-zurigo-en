Help shape the digital future of Migros Bank! We are looking for a Software Solution Engineer who will drive the technical development of our e-banking and website with passion and know-how. In collaboration with external partners, you will help shape the digital world of Migros Bank on our secure platform and accompany the digital transformation of Migros Bank! Mansioni

  • Further development of e-banking as a web application with microservices architecture on the secure DevOps platform in collaboration with internal teams and external partners in an agile setup.
  • Collaborate with other Software Solution Engineers on architectural topics for new and existing features to develop innovative and sustainable solutions.
  • Ensuring the smooth operation of e-banking and supporting the analysis and resolution of incidents.
  • Assumption of responsibility as a system engineer and/or application engineer for the assigned applications
  • Optimization and further development of the platform with a focus on stability, resilience and scalability in operation.
  • Active participation in optimizing development processes and tools to increase efficiency and quality.
