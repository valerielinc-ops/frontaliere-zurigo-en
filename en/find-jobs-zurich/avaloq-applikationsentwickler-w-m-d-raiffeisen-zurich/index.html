Role overview

In Team Tax & Clients we are responsible for central regulatory and customer-related services of our core banking application. Together, we are driving forward the implementation, digitalization and automation of regulatory processes. In close cooperation with the departments, we develop sustainable solutions that ensure high service quality, regulatory compliance and efficient processes.

For our locations at Zurich Airport and in St. Gallen, we are looking for a committed and reliable personality as a Avaloq application developer (f/m/d).

Tasks

  • The further development of our regulatory services in Avaloq and the implementation of requirements in the areas of AML, Tax and Crossborder.
  • The digitalization and automation of regulatory processes with a focus on efficiency, quality and sustainability.
  • The analysis of professional requirements as well as the conception and implementation of future-proof technical solutions together with business and IT.
  • Cooperation in the analysis and resolution of productive incidents to ensure stable service operations.
  • A versatile environment with the opportunity to actively shape and develop regulatory solutions.

Requirements

  • Several years of experience in Avaloq development, successfully completed Avaloq certification and ideally experience in client lifecycle management (CLM).
  • Very good knowledge of PL/SQL and Oracle SQL.
  • Experience in dealing with data models, data migrations and the implementation of interface solutions.
  • Knowledge of the regulatory environment of a bank, in particular in the topics AML, Tax, Crossborder and/or Compliance.
