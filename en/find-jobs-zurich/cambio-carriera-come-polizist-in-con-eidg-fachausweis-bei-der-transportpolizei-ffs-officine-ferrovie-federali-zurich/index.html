Cross-entry as a police officer: in with federal professional ID at the transport police 100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Cross-entry as a police officer: in with federal technical card at the transport police Zurich, 01.10.2027, 100% Apply now

You can look forward to that.

An exciting and challenging 24-month, paid second training for the policeman: in the transport police awaits you. You will complete the first year of this training at the Police School Amriswil (TG). In the second year, you gain valuable practical experience at the front in the exciting everyday life of the railway environment. With citizen-oriented police work, you ensure that people feel safe in public transport. Instead of administrative routine, the focus is on the mission close to the action – across cantonal borders and embedded in a strong, appreciative team. More information on profession, training and the transport police can be found here. You can move that.

  • As a police officer: in the Transport Police SBB, together with more than 200 colleagues, you ensure security, peace and order in train stations and public transport from various transport companies throughout Switzerland.
  • Your focus is on uniformed patrol services in means of transport, stations and on the areas of transport companies.
  • In your role, you make a significant contribution to the safety of travellers and public transport staff.
  • In the case of police interventions, you carry responsibility in the team and in cooperation with partner organisations – even on major occasions.
  • You work situation-oriented and are always on the pulse of what is happening in public transport.
  • You earn the trust and respect of customers by acting in an appreciative and responsible manner.

You bring that with you.

  • You act openly, respectfully and professionally and meet customers with a strong understanding of services.
  • For the challenging and varied work as a policeman: you have mental stability, physical robustness, flexibility and a quick comprehension.
  • You are a Swiss citizen, at least 21 years old and have a category B driving licence.
  • For your application, you need a certificate of participation from the Police Requirements Exam in Hitzkirch (www.fukunftpolizei.ch).
  • You have completed vocational training or an equivalent degree such as the Matura and have very good oral and written knowledge of German (level C2). Knowledge of a second national language is an advantage.
  • During the application process, we will ask you for a current penalty and enforcement record (not older than 3 months). You can order the documents at the invitation to get to know each other personally.

Your rewards and benefits.

Classification during secondary education shall be based on function and age. More about the concrete reward for this role can be found under Quereinstieg.
