Neumünster Park Residence Dipl. Nursing Specialist - Neumünster Park Residence — Spital Zollikerberg, Zollikerberg (ZH).

Area: Nursing professions.

Welcome to your new place of work.

The Spitex of the Neumünster Park Residence in the Zollikerberg Health World offers you an exciting position with a workload of 80-100% as

Dipl. Nurse - Residenz Neumünster Park

We continue to develop and are very happy to have reinforcements in the team.

If you want to make a difference in people's lives, these are your tasks:

  • Care, support and advice for our tenants at home such as our deaconesses in the Quelle
  • Carrying out needs assessments and case management
  • Ensuring the maintenance process with appropriate documentation
  • Correct recording of performance data
  • Cooperation with relatives, doctors, hospitals, homes and other specialist agencies

This is how you convince us

  • Completed training as a graduate nurse DN II/HF/FH
  • High social, methodological and professional competence
  • Very good written and spoken German skills
  • Up-to-date IT skills (MS Office, Carecoach)
  • Independent and responsible way of working

The following features are particularly convincing and unique at your new place of work:

  • Short distances within walking distance (without a car) and interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation, as everything is under one roof: Spitex, living area of the deaconesses, hospital, family doctor's practice
  • Palliative care: holistic recording of tenants and deaconesses
  • Various vessels for exchange such as specialist groups or (interprofessional) case discussions
  • Regular short training courses for all employees on topics such as palliative care, aroma care, care documentation and dementia
  • Close cooperation and support from the nursing experts in complex nursing and care situations
  • Beautiful park with pond to take a deep breath

Get a first impression of your new place of work in our 360° tour.

To make you feel at home with us, many advantages await you. To name just a few:

  • Own pension fund with non-mandatory participation of the employer
  • Eco-bonus of up to CHF 650 per year
  • Generous working time bonuses
  • Free use of the fitness room on the premises
  • Easily accessible by public transport
  • Modern and fair employment conditions
  • Support for further training (financial, working hours)

About Residenz Neumünster Park #LI-PZ1

The Neumünster Park Residence is a company of the Diakoniewerk Neumünster Foundation – Swiss School of Nursing / Zollikerberg Health World, which covers the care and support of tenants in over 80 apartments on the site, facility management and gardening. She is also responsible for the care of the deaconesses in the Neumünster Deaconess Sisterhood.
