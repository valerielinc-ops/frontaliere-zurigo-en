Role overview

You will become part of an interdisciplinary team in the area of data and information protection as well as in the area of compliance.

  • You will become part of an interdisciplinary team in the area of data and information protection as well as in the area of compliance.

Company and context

  • The team supports the organization in addressing complex regulatory requirements, particularly those related to data protection and company-wide compliance.
  • In a dynamic and international environment, you will work on a wide range of questions and actively contribute to the further development of processes, guidelines and solutions.
  • Advising internal stakeholders and coordination as well as independent processing of assigned requests, contract negotiations and internal projects.
  • Supervision of central group mailboxes and coordination within the teams.
  • Analysis of legal issues related to data processing, data flows and data transfers, including the development of practical solutions.
  • coordination and support of various processes within the Technology Risk and Risk Management Team.
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with specialists from other fields such as IT, Human Resources and Cyber Security.
  • Cooperation in the development and further development of policies, guidelines, regulations and processes as well as their communication - especially in the area of data protection.
  • Advice and support in other, evolving legal fields in the field of data protection in close cooperation with experienced professionals.
  • Complementary cooperation and answering legal questions in the area of people and firm compliance.

Additional details

  • Organization, coordination and support of various processes within the Technology Risk and Risk Management Team.
