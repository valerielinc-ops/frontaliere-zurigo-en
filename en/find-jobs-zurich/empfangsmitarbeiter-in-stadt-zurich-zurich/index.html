Full description

Due to a pending retirement, we are looking for a working colleague who will support the reception team administratively on Tuesday and Wednesday afternoons, Friday full day and another afternoon. Job as of 1 March 2027 resp. 1 April 2027.

Tasks

  • Reception of our customers as well as our external and in-city partners
  • Advising prospective tenants in dealing with our electronic tenant portal
  • Provision of telephone information
  • Preparation and distribution of mail
  • Various administrative tasks such as handling incoming invoices

Profile

  • Professional and service-oriented reception of customers
  • Strong friendliness in serving an international clientele
  • Several years of experience as a receptionist*
  • Experience in intercultural interaction is advantageous
  • Independence, resilience, reliability
  • Good application knowledge of MS-Office programs

We offer

a diverse and collegial working environment in a growing organization. Our employees benefit from attractive employment conditions such as family-friendly working time models and good social benefits. The centrally located workplace at Hallwylplatz and a wide range of training and further education are further qualities.

About us

Properties for a livable city of Zurich

Real Estate City of Zurich (LSZ), with its portfolio of 5.8 billion francs in insurance value, is one of the major real estate organizations in Switzerland. In addition to the management of around 10,300 apartments and 1,600 commercial premises, we are responsible for the comprehensive ownership representation, the long-term value preservation and the further development of this versatile portfolio. In addition, we manage the urban land reserves and building rights and are responsible for urban real estate transactions.
