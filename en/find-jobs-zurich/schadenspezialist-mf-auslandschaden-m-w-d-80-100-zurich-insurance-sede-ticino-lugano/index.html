136423, 80 - 100%, Oerlikon, Zurich, Hybrid

Does the idea of supporting our customers in an emergency motivate you to use both your experience in the motor vehicle damage area and your foreign languages?

The team MF-Auslandsschäden der Zurich - Swiss Inter Austrias - handles claims caused by insured persons with foreign victims in Switzerland and abroad. We also represent the National Guarantee Fund and the National Insurance Office and deal with claims from unknown or uninsured motor vehicles, as well as damage events of foreign motor vehicles in Switzerland.

Are you a team of players and constantly striving to deliver targeted and customer-oriented results? Then apply today, this could be your new challenge.

That's what awaits you

  • You carry out cover, liability and receivables checks
  • You clarify the facts of damage to motor vehicle liability
  • You have regular contact with insured persons, claimants, legal representatives, insurance companies, experts and authorities in Germany and abroad
  • You answer customer enquiries as part of the activity as a service of the National Insurance Office and the National Guarantee Fund
  • You bestimmt possible re-gresses and initiate the collection process

That's what makes you feel

  • Successful commercial training
  • At least 3 years of professional experience in engine damage management
  • High customer and service orientation
  • Distinct negotiating skills
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German and English
