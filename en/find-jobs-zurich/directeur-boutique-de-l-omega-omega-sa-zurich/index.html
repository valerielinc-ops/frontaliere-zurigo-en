Full description

Introduction of the société

OMEGA is a prestigious global brand that has been striving for the highest quality, precision and innovation for more than 170 years. Our watches are among the best in the world, they are the result of technologies that are leaders in the watch industry and are characterized by a timeless design.

Over the course of our long history, we have set precision records, played a decisive role in sports timekeeping and continue to play a pioneering role in the art of watchmaking.

As an international brand, we are always interested in meeting future colleagues who are ready to support our teams on virtually every continent through their talents, ambitions and inspiration.

Description of post

  • You actively support the Boutique Director in the operational and strategic leadership of the Boutique
  • Develop and maintain a strong local network and position the boutique as an indispensable reference in the region
  • You build long-term relationships with our local and international customers and ensure continuous high customer loyalty
  • They create a first-class customer experience, characterized by personal advice, service excellence and in-depth knowledge about our brand as well as the technical characteristics of our timepieces
  • They promote a strong clienteling culture: Proactive customer engagement, customer portfolio maintenance, targeted outreach activities and organization of VIP experiences
  • They motivate, accompany and develop the sales team to ensure a powerful and inspiring team culture
  • As an ambassador of our brand, you are always exemplary and contribute to the positive image of the boutique

Profile

  • Several years of professional experience in the luxury segment, ideally in the watch or jewelry industry.
  • Extraverted, open and communication-strong personality with a strong talent for networking and relationship building, especially in the local environment.
  • High flexibility regarding working hours and days (including Saturdays).

Competence requirements

  • Excellent sales skills and an intuitive sense of the needs of demanding customers.
  • Maintained, representative appearance as well as a high quality and service awareness.

Langues

  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Every other language (especially French) is a plus.
