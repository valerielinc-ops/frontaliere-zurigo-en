Full description

Shaping the future together. accompanying people. Promote skills. Are you enthusiastic about training future care professionals and would like to pass on your expertise and experience? Then become part of our dedicated team and actively shape the development of our learners and students. Their tasks

  • Accompaniment, promotion and assessment of learners FaGe as well as students care HF/FH in clinical everyday life
  • Planning, coordination and ensuring high-quality practical training
  • Implementation of learning accompaniments, location interviews and certificates of competence
  • Promoting the transfer of theory and practice and supporting the achievement of training objectives
  • Close cooperation with educational partners and internal specialists
  • Participation in the further development of the training concept and a modern learning culture
  • Active participation in the direct care and care of our patients
  • Promoting high quality care and safe and patient-oriented care
  • Training as a Dipl. Nursing specialist HF/FH and at least 2 years of professional experience in an acute hospital
  • Training as a vocational trainer (SVEB 1+2 / vocational trainer course; at least 40 hours) or willingness to complete it
  • Joy of accompanying and promoting learners and students
  • High level of technical, social and communication skills
  • Enthusiasm, patience and joy in passing on knowledge
  • An independent, competent person with a willingness to take on responsibility, to cooperate in the team and to support it professionally
  • A varied activity in cooperation of many disciplines
  • A family, personal working atmosphere
  • Development opportunities within the clinic and the Swiss Medical Network
  • Support for individual training wishes
  • Possibility to actively shape the training quality
  • Numerous discounts (see here), including a rich lunch buffet for only CHF 5.-
  • Discounts through Benifits for Work (e.g. at Coop, Zalando, Ikea, etc.)
  • Full coverage of the sickness allowance insurance
  • Various employee actions such as summer party, birthday and Christmas present, etc.
  • Free breakfast, coffee, tea & mineral

We look forward to your application! Create the future of care with us and accompany the next generation of care professionals on their way to practice.

Treatment at the highest level, high-class medical care and care, competent care in a more familiar, private atmosphere with hotel comfort, dedication, conscientiousness and absolute discretion – this is what the private clinic Bethanien stands for. Around 340 medical doctors from different disciplines provide high-quality, integrated medical care. Among the most important specialties are orthopaedics, visceral and thoracic surgery, urology, gynecology and birth, gastroenterology, cardiology, pneumology and internal medicine incl. Oncology. In the birth department, around 500 children are born each year. Founded in 1912, the private clinic Bethanien is part of the Swiss Medical Network.
