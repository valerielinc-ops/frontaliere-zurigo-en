Full description

We at CSS help people lead a healthy life – whatever life has in store for them. Maybe our mission will soon be yours too? We would be delighted. We are a diverse group of ambitious people who want to make a difference. Some of us have done the KV, others come from the retail trade, the hospitality industry or from another professional corner. Everyone brings their experience. What unites us is our passion to be there for our customers. We help each other, stick together and laugh a lot. And we prefer to celebrate success together in a team. Put yourself in pole position for future vacancies!

This is what your everyday life looks like

  • At the heart of it: You convince people of CSS and turn them into customers.
  • You can advise them personally, on the phone, by e-mail or even by video call – in very good German, also in other languages.
  • Our customer portfolio grows thanks to you through cross- and upselling.
  • You work closely with your colleagues and you support each other in achieving the common goals.
  • Every now and then you look outside the box and take on additional tasks or project work.

You bring that with you

  • First things first: You realize that you like to be among people – you approach them openly and your positive nature is contagious.
  • Completed vocational training in the commercial sector, in the retail trade or in another exciting profession – ideally with customer contact.
  • You already bring experience from health insurance and have the VBV certification already in your pocket.
  • You can respond well to your counterpart, listen and love to convince others in conversation with strong arguments.
  • Digitalization is not a foreign word for you – you are a professional in online consulting.
  • You have a large relationship network and preferably live in the region.
  • Work Flexible: Adapt your work to your lifestyle annually with part-time options and other flexible models like home office and more.
  • Enjoy your wallet: Benefits such as bonus, Reka, discounted health insurance premium and others that quickly make up a few thousands a year.
  • Relax during the holidays: You work 41 hours a week and enjoy 6 weeks of vacation.
  • Improve yourself further: We like to see it if you educate yourself internally or externally and support generously with time and money.
  • Celebrate with us: Focused work and celebrate. We love this combination and maintain it regularly.
  • Stay fit: Ergonomic workplaces, promotion of mental well-being and many other offers around your health.
