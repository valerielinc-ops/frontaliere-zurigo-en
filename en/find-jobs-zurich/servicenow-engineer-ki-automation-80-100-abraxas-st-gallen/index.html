Original description

At Abraxas, we want to consistently anchor AI in ServiceNow and further develop our platform as a key lever for innovation, automation and efficiency. You shape the next generation of our ServiceNow platform – with generative AI, intelligent applications, autonomous agents and automation that significantly relieve employees and customers. You will identify relevant AI use cases, develop prototypes and convert them together with the team into scalable, data-driven solutions for our customers and internal processes. For St. Gallen or Zurich Airport, we are looking for a committed personality who actively brings together ServiceNow, AI and automation and takes our platform to the next level. Your role

  • Identify, evaluate and prioritize AI use cases within the ServiceNow platform
  • Design, implement and further develop AI-enabled solutions such as Virtual Agent, Predictive/Task Intelligence and GenAI Features (NOW Assist)
  • Accept requirements from specialist areas and customers and translate them into effective, practical solutions
  • Further develop the ServiceNow architecture with a focus on automation, scalability and AI
  • Build best practices, standards and guidelines for using AI in ServiceNow
  • Promote structured, high-quality data as a basis for AI
  • Implement PoCs and pilot projects and gradually transfer successful approaches into operation
  • Transfer knowledge to the team and actively shape our AI strategy in the ServiceNow environment

Your profile

  • Demonstrable practical experience with AI functionalities in ServiceNow, such as Predictive/Task Intelligence (Machine Learning) or NOW Assist (GenAI)
  • Several years of experience as ServiceNow Engineer, Developer or Consultant and corresponding certification
  • In-depth knowledge of central ServiceNow modules, for example ITSM, CSM or ITOM
  • Experience in process automation with workflows, Flow Designer and IntegrationHub
  • Analytical thinking and the ability to identify potential for efficiency enhancement through AI and automation
  • Enjoy innovation, experimentation and iterative approach with a concrete implementation focus
  • Good communication skills and maker mentality – you move safely between business and IT
  • German at native language level and negotiable English

Your benefits

  • The active participation of our AI initiative with high strategic relevance
  • A lot of design freedom and the opportunity to use new technologies early and meaningfully
  • A motivated, collaborative team with high quality standards and innovation
  • Exciting customers, challenging issues and complex ServiceNow environments
  • Up to 60% home office possible with a 100% workload
  • Holiday purchase (2 weeks per year)
  • 41h week with flexible working hours on a full-time workload
  • Mind. 25 days of holidays per year
  • Support for 100% financial or temporal training
  • Free SBB half-ax subscription for all employees

In order to support our customers in future-oriented IT strategies, we rely on people who think, take responsibility and question the status quo. With us, you can actively develop ServiceNow, contribute new ideas and work with a lot of decision-making freedom in a modern, agile environment. Become part of our #TeamAbraxas. You can expect a future-oriented working environment with modern workplace design, very good transport connections, generous home office regulation and excellent training opportunities. We maintain an open you culture – so please send us your application directly in the you form.
