E-Shop Manager

Coop Cooperative — Winterthur, Zurich, Svizzera

Tasks

  • As a product owner, you are responsible for the further development and implementation of our e-commerce environment.

  • You prioritize requirements from business, marketing, CRM, retail and IT and ensure the balance between customer benefit, business value and technical feasibility.
