Full description

Are you ready to move up a gear? Our sales team is growing twice! As of January 1, 2027, we are looking for two additional account managers – one for Central Switzerland and one for Northeast Switzerland. Please only apply for the job that best suits your region and network. Your tasks:

  • Sales & Territorial Responsibility: You run your own sales area, support existing customers and tap into new potential.
  • Growth: You gain new customers and develop existing partnerships. With your consulting expertise at eye level, you create trust, recognize opportunities and support car dealers and garage operators in successfully developing their business.
  • Guarantee of success: You present our new digital products and independently implement targeted marketing and customer loyalty measures.
  • Data-Driven Sales: You use market analysis, downtime data and user statistics from our platform to strategically advise your customers and show them the measurable success of their listings. ## Qualificates This is what you bring with you:
  • Industry knowledge: Several years of sales experience in the Swiss automotive industry as well as a strong, existing network in the regional car trade.
  • Local anchoring: You are well connected in your focus region – Central Switzerland – understand the needs of car dealers and garage operators and know the dynamics of the regional automotive market.
  • Education & experience: You have completed vocational training, further training in sales and profound experience in the field service. Ideally, you will also have knowledge of digital marketing and a good understanding of digital business models.
  • Personality: You perform professionally, communicate confidently and convincingly and tackle challenges with a positive, solution-oriented attitude. Even in demanding customer situations, you remain sovereign and work consistently towards joint solutions.
  • Languages & Mobility: You have fluent knowledge of German
  • Any other national language is beneficial. In addition, you have a category B driving license and bring a high willingness to travel within your region. ## Informazioni aggiuntive Why you should come to us: You can expect a committed and diverse team with a strong sales mentality and a lot of mutual support. We work flexibly in a hybrid model. While you spend most of your time with customers in your region (Central Switzerland), our office in Zurich Oerlikon and your home office are at your disposal as working places. The SMG also offers you:
  • 6 weeks holidays (with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days)

  • 18 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave. It also applies to adoption.
  • Flexible working models (work-life balance is very important to us)
  • Access to mental health, well-being and counselling platforms
  • Half-tax subscription for public transport and 1st class travel by train between SMG locations in Switzerland Diversity at SMG At SMG, we value different perspectives and believe in the potential of each individual.
