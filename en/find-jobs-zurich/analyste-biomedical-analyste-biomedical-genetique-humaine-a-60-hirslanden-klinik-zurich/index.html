Occupation by appointment

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 72275

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.As an important part of precision medicine, the Hirslanden Group’s laboratory for genetic medicine was set up in Zollikon.

Despite the reach of the Hirslanden Group, there is a family atmosphere at the Hirslanden Precise site and an extremely close exchange with colleagues on site as well as on a group-wide level. To strengthen our team of the Competence Center in Genetic Medicine, we are looking for a BMA or a laboratory assistant / laboratory assistant for molecular diagnostics.

YOUR TASKS

  • Molecular genetic pre- and postnatal analyses (sample acquisition, NGS, Sanger sequencing, MLPA analyses, ctDNA analyses, etc.) according to ISO-accredited quality guidelines
  • Work according to SOP specifications
  • Documentation of quality-relevant processes
  • Support and operation of analysis equipment and automation
  • Implementation and evaluation of process-relevant quality control steps, input into quality management
  • Evaluation and technical validation of the analysis results
  • Support in the administrative area of the team

Your profile

  • Completed training as Dipl. biomed. Analyst/in HF or as Dipl. Biology laboratory (or equivalent training)
  • Several years of professional experience in the field of medical genetics are beneficial
  • High sense of responsibility and quality as well as very good communication skills
  • Good knowledge in dealing with universal computer programs such as Excel, Word, genetic software such as Coffalyser.net, SeqPilot and laboratory information systems
  • Strong team spirit and a high degree of concentration at work
  • Very good knowledge of German

You can look forward to this

  • Work-life balance: regulated working hours without weekend or night services
  • Varied tasks: An exciting and responsible job in a dynamic and innovative environment – at Switzerland’s largest private hospital group
  • Strong team spirit: You can expect a committed, expert team that welcomes you warmly and supports you with a structured training.
  • Development opportunities: We promote your professional and personal development with targeted training and further education
