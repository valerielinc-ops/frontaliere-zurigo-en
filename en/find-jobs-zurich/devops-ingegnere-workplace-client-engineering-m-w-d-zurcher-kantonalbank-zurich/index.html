80% - 100% | IT / Business Engineering | Zurich | Experienced professionals Is client engineering, Microsoft Windows and the world of the digital workplace your passion and do you want to be part of an agile team where you take responsibility? With us, you work in a wide range of tasks with flat organizational structures, attractive development opportunities and a lot of design freedom. In an agile team, you design the digital workplace for our employees and ensure that cooperation, communication and security work together optimally. You combine technical know-how with a strong understanding of service and contribute to the fact that we as a close bank are reliable for our customers every day. Your tasks

  • Further develop the hardware and software solutions of our digital workplace
  • Development and implementation of new, sustainable solutions and concepts in the areas of client engineering (hardware, operating system, software distribution, active directory, virtual clients, security solutions)
  • Ensuring high safety and operational requirements in the workplace sector
  • Planning and implementing challenging enterprise projects in close cooperation with architecture, network engineering and IT security
  • Design and support the journey to the cloud, in particular connecting and managing our clients to various cloud services (e.g. zero trust clients, client monitoring, device control)
  • Identify and implement automation potential along client and workplace processes

Your profile

  • Completed university degree (FH, university) in computer science or equivalent education
  • Several years of professional experience in a complex client engineering environment as well as practical experience in programming or Scripting
  • In-depth knowledge in at least two of the following areas: Device Management, Windows Defender, System Center Configuration Manager, Citrix or remote access solutions
  • Experience in designing and developing new solution architectures in the workplace or client environment
  • You convince with your team-oriented, communicative and learning-ready way and are open to new things
  • You communicate verbally and in writing very safely in German
