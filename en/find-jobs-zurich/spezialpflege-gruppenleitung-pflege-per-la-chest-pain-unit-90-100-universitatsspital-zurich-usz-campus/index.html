Full description

As a specialist care provider at the University Hospital Zurich, we stand for our commitment to show our professional diversity in the top medical environment and to always be on the pulse of time. We achieve successes not alone, but together! Each individual counts and together we bring ourselves to the highest level of nursing.

Our Chest Pain Unit is in an exciting development phase. With the expansion to 24/7 operations, new opportunities are created to shape structures, promote people and actively shape the future of our unit. You work flexibly and in a demand-oriented manner in all shifts and make an important contribution to reliable patient care around the clock. The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees are committed to the welfare of our patients every day.

Tasks: You are a specialist in nursing / graduate in nursing HF/FH and ideally bring experience in acute care as well as knowledge in the cardiological environment. You have also completed or are ready to complete an NDK monitoring care.

You'll find

  • A lot of space to bring in ideas and to initiate change
  • A key role in the further development of our Chest Pain Unit
  • An experienced team with high expertise and strong cohesion
  • Cooperation at eye level with different occupational groups
  • A working environment in which responsibility is taken over and co-design is lived
