Full description

For one of the largest non-profit, outpatient care organizations in the canton of Zurich with around 170 employees, we are looking for an experienced and charismatic leader in the course of a succession regulation. In this absolute key role, you are part of the four-member management and actively shape the strategic and operational development of the future-oriented company. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for you!

This is your job

  • Divisional management: Personnel and professional management of Finance & Administration, HR and IT with currently nine directly subordinate employees.
  • Financial responsibility: overall responsibility for finance, controlling and reporting as well as operational accounting including the preparation of quarterly and annual accounts.
  • Strategy & Sparring: Active participation at the strategic level within the management as well as the development of sound decision-making basis for the co-CEOs.
  • Projects & processes: management of cross-divisional projects, coordination of internal and external reporting and the continuous digital development of processes and structures.

This is what you bring with you

  • Education: Completed (specialised) university studies, preferably in business administration or a comparable subject area.
  • Experience: Several years of in-depth management experience with comprehensive financial responsibility and proven project management practice
  • Ideally, you already know the non-profit or health-related environment.
  • Competence: in-depth knowledge of finance, accounting, controlling and reporting; routine handling of common financial and ERP systems (Abacus knowledge is a clear advantage).
  • Personality: entrepreneurial thinking, strategic foresight coupled with implementation power, communication strength and a process- and solution-oriented working method.
  • Languages: Very good knowledge of German in word and writing at native language level.

This is what awaits you from the customer

  • Strategic influence: A challenging top position in which you as a GL member can leave real traces and directly shape the strategic orientation of the company.
  • Diversity: A varied and responsible environment at the interface of finance, HR and IT.
  • Culture: A meaningful activity in a dynamic, crisis-proof and well-established company with short decision-making paths.
