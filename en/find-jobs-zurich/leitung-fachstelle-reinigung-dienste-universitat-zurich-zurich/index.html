Operational service Irchel

Job opening by agreement, indefinitely

The Irchel operating service of the University of Zurich creates the infrastructural conditions for efficient teaching, research and service operations at the Irchel campus. The specialist office is responsible for maintenance, basic and special cleaning as well as waste management and disposal. In addition, it operates the sports facility, the customer service desk, the parking management and offers services for events. With 5 team leaders and around 75 employees, it ensures quality, hygiene, value preservation and an economical and sustainable operation at a high level. We are looking for a dedicated, entrepreneurial and professional leader who understands FM services and shapes them together with their team.

Tasks

They shall bear the technical, human and financial overall responsibility for the specialist centre.

  • Leadership and development of 5 team leaders and around 75 employees as well as promotion of performance-oriented, binding and appreciative cooperation
  • Ensuring high-quality, customer-oriented and economical cleaning as well as continuous development of strategies, concepts and processes
  • Budget, cost, procurement and resource responsibility
  • Planning and implementation of tenders, procurement and management of external service providers
  • Promotion and targeted use of modern cleaning systems and digitalization to optimize processes and services
  • Ensuring environmental and sustainable waste management and disposal
  • Responsible for implementing the requirements in the areas of occupational safety and fire protection
  • Responsibility for the operation of the sports facility, customer service (parking management, event service, information desk)
  • Strategic management of the specialist office for a modern, sustainable and economic facility service
  • Supporting internal and external stakeholders as well as developing constructive and long-term viable solutions together with relevant partners

Profile

They have in-depth training in facility management and proven experience in professional cleaning.

  • Higher education in facility management, e.g. FH or comparable qualification
  • Several years of management and professional experience in professional cleaning with in-depth knowledge in cleaning organization, quality assurance, cleaning technology and value preservation
  • Ideally, experience in facility or building management as well as in service, process and quality management
  • Practice in tendering, procurement and management of external service providers
  • Strong business understanding and the competence to formulate strategies and business plans
  • Experience with budget and cost responsibility as well as the creation of forecasts
  • Interest in digitalization, sustainability and modern FM concepts
  • Appreciative, clear and binding leader with high social competence
  • Strong communication, solution-oriented and entrepreneurial
  • Good IT user knowledge and digital affinity
  • English skills are beneficial
