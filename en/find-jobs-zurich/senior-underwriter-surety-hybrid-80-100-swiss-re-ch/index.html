Original description

Are you ready to bring your risk and financial expertise as well as market experience to a dynamic, global environment? Join Swiss Re Corporate Solutions in Madrid and play a key role in supporting our Spanish, Portuguese and broader European clients with their surety and guarantee needs. This is your opportunity to combine analytical excellence with meaningful client relationships in a truly entrepreneurial setting to develop our growth ambition in Spain, Portugal and across Europe. About the Role As Senior Underwriter Surety based in Madrid, you will be an integral part of our growing European surety team, representing Swiss Re Surety in the local market. You will work closely with the Head Surety Europe to set the local market strategy and tactical guidance, lead the distribution with key clients and brokers, and execute business in line with our strategic ambitions and defined portfolio strategy. As Senior Underwriter on the ground, you will be part of the regional Surety leadership team as well as the first point of contact to the Head Iberia.

Key responsibilities

  • Delivering towards our business growth ambition for Surety Iberia by defining and executing on priorities of key ongoing and future business initiatives
  • Underwriting for new and existing clients in a senior portfolio in accordance with internal guidelines while maintaining highest standards of client satisfaction
  • Independently acquire and handle client relationships, originate and process business opportunities with particular focus on client's needs and quality of service
  • Analyse new business opportunities by accurately assessing credit and transactional risks, legal due diligence, following Swiss Re's processes and guidelines
  • Develop account strategies to adapt position on existing clients to expand market footprint and steer own client portfolio
  • Regularly meet and interact with key clients and brokers gather relevant information on market trends and opportunities and represent Swiss Re at market conferences and associations.
  • Mentoring and advising younger professionals as the need arises

About the Team Backed by more than 150 years of experience, significant capacity and the financial strength of the Swiss Re Group, Swiss Re Corporate Solutions solves the risk management and financing needs of corporations around the globe. We pursue the goal of results-oriented underwriting. The positive relationship with our customers and brokers across Europe, combined with our strong brand, is the foundation of our success. If you share the passion for surety and financial products, we encourage you to apply for this position. As part of a global team offering tailor-made solutions across a broad range of surety products, you will have the opportunity to join our highly motivated team that shares the same passion for this business.

About You

  • Significant relevant experience in surety, commercial banking and/or credit-risk related financial services with visible market knowledge and reputation
  • Experience in dealing with multinational corporations and providing credit, guarantee & surety solutions to large and mid-sized corporate customers
  • End-to-end understanding and thinking across the Surety value chain
  • Excellent understanding of financial markets including debt financing, capital market instruments, and industry knowledge
  • Mature and confident personality with strong communication and negotiation skills
  • Entrepreneurial mentality, with strong focus on meeting objectives and achieving long-term goals
  • Curiosity, agility and ability to develop and present tailored solutions
  • A university degree or equivalent qualification in finance, economics, law or a related discipline
  • Spanish native speaker with proficiency in English (written and verbal) is essential, additional language skills are a plus.
  • Willingness to travel throughout Europe
