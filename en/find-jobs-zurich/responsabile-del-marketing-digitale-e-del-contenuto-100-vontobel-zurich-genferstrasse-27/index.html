Original description

Are you an experienced and forward-thinking digital marketing and content leader who combines strategic vision with strong execution capabilities?

Do you want to shape and execute a client-centric digital and content strategy that supports business growth, strengthens thought leadership, and enhances client engagement across all channels?

Are you excited by the opportunity to leverage data, technology, and Artificial Intelligence to create impactful client experiences and drive measurable business outcomes?

If so, we invite you to explore this opportunity to join Vontobel Asset Management as Head of Digital Marketing & Content.

What would your role be?

  • Define and execute Vontobel Asset Management’s global digital marketing and content strategy, aligned with commercial and growth objectives
  • Build client-centric digital journeys and content ecosystems that drive acquisition, engagement, retention, and growth
  • Strengthen Vontobel’s thought leadership by translating complex investment topics into compelling client experiences and measurable business outcomes
  • Leverage data, AI, and emerging technologies to enhance personalization, scalability, client engagement, and marketing effectiveness
  • Lead the continued evolution of Vontobel’s digital capabilities and drive transformative change across the organization

What are we looking for?

  • Proven experience in digital marketing, content strategy, and delivering measurable business impact in a global environment
  • A strong data-driven mindset, including experience defining and tracking KPIs and performance metrics
  • Passion for AI, automation, and innovation, with a track record of applying technology to improve outcomes
  • Strong leadership and people development skills, with the ability to build high-performing and collaborative teams
  • Excellent stakeholder management and influencing skills across investment, product, sales, marketing, technology, and compliance functions
  • A change-oriented mindset with experience leading transformation and driving adoption of new ways of working
  • The strategic perspective and collaborative approach needed to contribute effectively as a key member of the Client Content Center leadership team and help shape the evolution of Vontobel’s digital capabilities

Candidate Profile

  • Strategic, commercially minded leader with a strong focus on transformation, innovation, and measurable business outcomes
  • Exceptional stakeholder management, communication, and influencing skills, with the ability to simplify complex investment topics into compelling client narratives
  • Collaborative and adaptable people leader, passionate about leveraging AI and emerging technologies to drive growth and continuous improvement
  • University degree in Marketing, Communications, Business, Finance, Digital Media, or a related discipline
  • 10+ years of experience in digital marketing, content strategy, communications, or related fields
  • Leadership experience managing teams and complex cross-functional initiatives
  • Strong understanding of asset management, investment products, and financial markets
  • Proven track record of developing and executing successful digital and content strategies in a B2B environment
  • Experience leading digital transformation and leveraging technology to improve client engagement and business outcomes
  • Experience working effectively within a regulated environment
