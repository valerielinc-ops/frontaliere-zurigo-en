Occupation by: by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 74716

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

YOUR TASKS

  • Interface function for the transfer of information between service providers (employment physicians, clinic institutes), coding centre and payers.
  • Ensuring complete and qualitatively correct documentation
  • Advice and support of the medical profession and nursing staff in the form and content of the medical documentation
  • Supporting case-accompanying coding in close cooperation with the medical profession

Your profile

  • Degree as a Dipl. nurse or medical training with hospital or Hospital experience
  • Swiss Federal Professional Card of Medical Coding or equivalent from German-speaking countries with several years of coding experience
  • Style-confident knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Addressable communication skills to all stakeholders
  • Advanced knowledge of Office programs (Excel etc.) and classification systems (Swiss DRG, CHOP and ICD)
  • Holistic thinking about processes as well as a structured and independent working method

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. You work with a modern medical clinic information system and can expect extensive training and development opportunities from us. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family responsibilities. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from equivalent financial compensation. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.
