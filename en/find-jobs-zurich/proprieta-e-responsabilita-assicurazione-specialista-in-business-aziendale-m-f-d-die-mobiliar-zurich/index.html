Full description

This is what awaits you

In the claims department of the brokerage management, you are responsible for the claims cases of the institutional customers of insurance brokers.

This is what you do with us

In this role, you assess coverage and liability issues in the property and liability insurance sectors and independently regulate the incoming claims. In addition, you conduct interesting negotiations with policyholders: inside, brokers, injured parties, experts and other insurance companies. If necessary, visits and investigations will also take place on site.

You maintain personal relationships with your assigned insurance brokers, are in close contact with our institutional clients and play an important, representative role.

With modern IT infrastructure and in a pleasant working environment, you bring your ideas, develop yourself and your claims team further and further and are in friendly exchange with other teams and departments.

This is what you bring with you

• Basic training in the insurance industry, Federal insurance certificate of advantage • Several years of professional experience in handling material and liability cases • Responsible, empathetic and upright personality • High degree of independence and personal responsibility • Fluent German skills, every other language is a plus

Working with us

A motivated damage team is waiting for you. Together you live a collegial, solution-oriented and communicative working atmosphere.

Flexible working time models and home office are a matter of course for us. To your mind. 25 holiday days, we allow you to buy up to four additional holiday weeks. You will receive a free subscription for your smartphone and a half-tax and up to 25% discount on our insurance and pension products. In addition, benefit from a large internal training offer and benefits at various events.

Your workload is negotiable from 80%. More about our commitment as an employer can be found below.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programmes abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
