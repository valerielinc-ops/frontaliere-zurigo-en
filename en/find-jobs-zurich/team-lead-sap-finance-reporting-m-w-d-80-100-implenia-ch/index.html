Full description

As a leading Swiss construction and real estate service provider, Implenia develops, realizes and manages living spaces, working environments and infrastructure for future generations in Switzerland and Germany. Implenia also offers tunnelling and related infrastructure projects in other markets. The company combines the know-how of highly qualified consulting, development, planning and execution units under one roof into an integrated, multinationally leading construction and real estate service provider. Headquartered in Opfikon near Zurich, Implenia employs more than 8,000 people (FTE) across Europe and achieved a turnover of CHF 3.5 billion in 2025. As Team Lead SAP Finance & Reporting, you will play a central role in the further development of our Finance and Reporting IT landscape. You are an experienced leader with many years of expertise in the SAP finance and analytics environment and lead a dedicated team in the area of Finance & Reporting. Your focus is on the standardization and optimization of end-to-end finance processes in close cooperation with the specialist areas and the successful implementation of transformation and migration projects. The place of work is our headquarters at Ambassador House in Opfikon, combined with the opportunity to work flexibly in the home office.
