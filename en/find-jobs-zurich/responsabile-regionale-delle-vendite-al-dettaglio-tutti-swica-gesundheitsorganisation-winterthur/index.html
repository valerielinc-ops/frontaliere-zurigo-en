Full description

Short paths, quick decisions: You are right in the middle of it, working on a wide range of projects and actively shaping solutions. You bring your ideas directly and see how they work. That makes the difference. In this demanding role, you carry the overall responsibility for the private customer business of the Eastern Switzerland region with approx. 110 employees spread over fifteen locations (general agencies / agencies). You will report directly to the Regional Director. Your place of work is both in St. Gallen and in Winterthur. This is how you shape health

  • Ensuring the necessary measures to achieve the portfolio and production goals in the private customer business in the region of Eastern Switzerland
  • Planning and implementing the necessary measures to achieve customer satisfaction goals
  • Ensuring lead and campaign management between customer support and retail sales as well as implementation of recruitment campaigns
  • First professional contact person in the region for all sales-relevant customer processes in private customer business
  • Preparation and implementation of a regional marketing plan in private customer business incl. Mark analysis
  • Implementation of regional retail marketing actions in coordination with the Regional Director
  • Support of the Directorate-General in projects and responsibility for sub-projects as well as participation in the optimization of processes
  • Management, training and promotion of the organisational unit retail sales

This sets you apart

  • Completed basic commercial training or equivalent, supplemented by business and/or sales-specific training, minimum level HF
  • Several years of professional experience in a similar position, preferably in the health/social insurance sector or private insurance
  • Insurance intermediary/in VBV, profile Allbranche
  • Leadership and coaching experience in sales
  • Acquisition and sales flair
  • Comprehensive network of relationships in the sales area and strong market knowledge
  • Very good communication skills oral/written as well as representative personality with impeccable and situational performance skills

Why SWICA

  • We build on humanity. That is why we focus on openness, respect and trust in our everyday work. We tackle this, take responsibility and think in terms of solutions – together, for our customers. This is also why we have been number 1 in customer satisfaction for many years.
  • We trust you and give you space. With annual working hours, at least 5 weeks holidays and on request also holiday purchase. With part-time models and generous home office within the scope of the possibilities of the job, you make your everyday life largely flexible.
  • We value and reward your work – with a market-based wage, up to 1,500 francs annual fee for personal wishes and a generous participation in the basic and additional insurance for you and your family.
  • We offer you prospects. With a generous training regulations, we support your professional and personal development – for your role today and for your goals of tomorrow.
  • Because health is everything. With our company health management and region-specific offers, we take care of your well-being. And if it comes otherwise: 100% net wage continued payment.
  • We think long term. With three savings plans, pensum-dependent coordination deduction and benefits well above the legal minimum, you ensure good for your future.
