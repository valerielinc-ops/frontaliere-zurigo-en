Full description

Are you looking for an activity in which psychological expertise, human closeness and interdisciplinary cooperation meet? In psycho-oncology, you accompany patients and relatives through challenging phases of illness and life and make an important contribution to their quality of life. You can expect a versatile task with creative leeway, professional exchange and attractive development and further education opportunities. >The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.

Tasks

  • Psychological care of patients: women with oncological diagnosis as well as their relatives – both ambulatory and inpatient
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation with the participating clinics of the USZ
  • Flexible cooperation in various areas of application: rapid change between office hours, consultancy and station work
  • Independent and reliable organization of your activities as well as targeted design and completion of therapy sequences under regular, structured medical supervision

Requirements

  • Completed study of psychology (master, license or diploma)
  • Ideally already started training in psychotherapy
  • Advantage: Experience in psychotherapeutic accompaniment of somatically ill patients: inside and their relatives
  • Resilient, communicative personality with pleasure in interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation as well as psychosocial issues
  • High flexibility and willingness to work with inpatients: Openness for changing locations within the hospital as well as safe prioritization in the event of varying workloads

We offer

We offer an attractive workplace in a motivated team with diverse activities in the university environment. In addition, there is an extensive range of internal and external training and supervision. If we are interested in research, we offer support.
