As the largest university in Switzerland, the University of Zurich offers a variety of attractive positions in various departments and professional fields. With around 10,000 employees and currently 12 professional profiles for learners, the university offers an inspiring working environment in cutting-edge research and education. Use your talent and skills with us. Find out more about UZH as an employer!

  • Responsibilities: You look after dogs and cats in general internal medicine as well as in the specialist areas of gastroenterology, endocrinology, nephrology/urology and infectiology. The various departments are each led by specialized senior physicians (Dipl. ACVIM or Dipl. ECVIM-CA). The daily clinical work takes place in close cooperation and under expert supervision in a dedicated internal team. In addition, you will be actively involved in the clinical training of students and support the care of inpatients on weekend shifts.
  • Requirements: We are looking for a motivated personality with a great interest in internal issues in small animal medicine, a high level of commitment and flexibility, team spirit and enjoyment of interdisciplinary collaboration and very good theoretical knowledge of internal medicine in small animals. Practical experience in the small animal sector is an advantage.
  • What we offer Working in our internal medicine team offers an excellent opportunity to gain clinical experience at a high professional level and can provide a valuable basis for a later residency (ECVIM-CA or ACVIM). In addition to broad clinical training, you benefit from state-of-the-art diagnostic and therapeutic equipment, intensive supervision and individual mentoring, regular journal clubs, rounds and internal training, scientific work as part of a research project, and a dynamic university environment with close interdisciplinary collaboration. The position also offers attractive conditions outside of the professional environment: Zurich is regularly one of the cities with the highest quality of life internationally. Despite the comparatively high cost of living in Switzerland, the pay is attractive compared to other countries and provides very good living conditions overall.
  • We offer: Our employees benefit from a wide range of attractive offers. Find out more at: https://www.uzh.ch/de/explore/work.html.
