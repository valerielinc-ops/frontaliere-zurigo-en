Full description

Strengthen our Cybersecurity & Privacy team and work in an interdisciplinary environment with consultants, risk experts and technology specialists. Together, we help organizations effectively manage cyber risks, strengthen their digital resilience and meet regulatory requirements sustainably. The role combines cyber strategy, governance, risk management, technology and transformation with cybersecurity audits and assessments in the regulated environment.

  • Leading and implementing multidisciplinary projects to help clients assess and ensure the applicability and impact of cybersecurity laws and regulations.
  • Implement legal and regulatory obligations into resilient cyber (risk) strategies, governance and risk management models, policies, control frameworks, target operational models and risk-based implementation plans.
  • Development and review of end-to-end cybersecurity compliance programs, including regulatory requirements inventories, commitment and control allocation, responsibility models, demonstration, control review, problem and remediation management, metrics, reporting and monitoring regulatory framework changes.
  • Conduct regulatory readiness assessments, regulatory compliance and maturity assessments. Support customers in implementation, testing and independent review. All this to make our customers more resilient and stronger.
  • Advising and reviewing clients on the skills required by applicable regulatory frameworks, including cybersecurity risk management, incident response and regulatory reporting, third-party security and supply chain security, vulnerability management, secure product development, business continuity and management oversight.
  • Moderation of workshops and information events with board members, management and stakeholders from the areas of law, compliance, risk, IT, security, product development and technology in order to translate complex regulatory and technical issues into clear decisions and measures.
  • Act as a trusted consultant and central point of contact for clients, managing projects from start to finish, including project scope, project teams, budget, quality and stakeholder expectations.
  • Preparation of high-quality results such as reports, playbooks, risk registers, control matrices and presentation-ready documents.
  • Build long-term customer relationships and contribute to the growth of our business by working on proposals, methodological results, developing point-of-view papers (e.g. white papers) on current topics and working together across the entire PwC network.
  • Team members coach and promote and contribute to a cooperative, inclusive and quality-oriented work environment.
  • 6–10 years of relevant professional experience in cybersecurity, IT or technology risk, preferably in a consulting, big-4 or regulated environment.
  • Certifications such as CISSP, CISM, CISA, CRISC or ISO 27001 Lead Implementer are beneficial.
  • Understanding relevant cybersecurity standards, frameworks and best practices, in particular ISO/IEC 27001/27002, NIST CSF, CRI Profile, CIS Controls, CIS Benchmarks, MITRE ATT&CK and similar reference works are beneficial.
  • Experience with or strong interest in regulatory requirements and resilience-related issues related to technology (such as FINMA 2023/01, DORA, NIS2).
  • Familiarity with central topics such as cyber governance, IT and cyber risk management, security controls, cloud security, identity & access management, data security and security operations as well as their business-relevant classification.
  • Structured, analytical and quality-oriented working methods as well as the ability to prepare complex issues appropriate to the addressee.
  • Strong communication, presentation and stakeholder management skills in exchange with specialist areas, risk and control functions as well as top management.
  • Leadership experience of lines and/or project teams.
  • A commercial mindset and experience in the areas of offering creation, market development and building long-term customer relationships.
  • High degree of initiative, self-organization and sense of responsibility in a dynamic, team-oriented environment.
  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in computer science, business informatics, business administration, engineering or an equivalent professional qualification.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Knowledge of French is beneficial.
