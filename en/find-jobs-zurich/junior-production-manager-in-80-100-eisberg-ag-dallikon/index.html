Full description

With us, fresh production meets fresh ideas. As a junior production manager, you are in the middle of it instead of just there: you continue to think about processes, advance projects, work with different teams and take on more responsibility step by step. Your ideas count. Your drive takes us further. Your development is in your hands.

  • Contract type: Perpetual
  • Pensum: 80% - 100%
  • Job: from now on
  • Language: German
  • Location: Eisberg AG, Dänikerstrasse 31 8108 Dällikon

Tasks

  • You support production management in planning, organizing and controlling production processes as well as in production planning.
  • You ensure smooth running production operations and coordinate cooperation with internal partners.
  • You ensure a systematic way of working, transparent documentation and structured processes.
  • You lead and accompany projects for the optimization and further development of production processes.
  • You create and analyze production key figures, derive targeted measures from them and implement them together with the team.
  • As a key user for production and goods acceptance in the CSB ERP system, you play a central role in the further development of the corresponding processes.
  • You develop the ERP and production processes in close cooperation with the production management.
  • You perform tests on new processes and system updates and ensure their practicality.
  • You create training materials and train employees on site.
  • In the future, you will assume your own areas of responsibility as well as leadership responsibility for employees.

Requirements

  • Completed education or studies in production, food technology, business administration, technology or equivalent qualification.
  • First professional experience in production, production planning or process optimization.
  • Interest in the further development of production processes and pleasure in project-related work.
  • Strong understanding of processes, key figures and systematic working methods.
  • Affinity to ERP systems and digital processes
  • Experience with CSB is beneficial.
  • Analytical, structured and solution-oriented working method.
  • Enjoy working with different interfaces and the $ ability to attract employees to new processes.
  • Good communication and organizational skills.
  • Willingness to take responsibility and develop into a leadership role in the future.

This is what we offer

  • Food: Cuisine is very important to us: Freshly prepared salads, soups, tea and fruit are available to our employees free of charge. In addition, you can take home products from the personal shelf every day.
  • Mobility: Benefit from convenient connections to public transport, attractive discounts for public transport and sufficient parking – sustainable and worry-free on the road!
  • Professional development opportunities: Expand your knowledge through extensive further education offers, high-quality training in the Coop Education Center and various personal development opportunities such as mentoring and coaching.
  • Premiums, social benefits, holidays: You benefit from 5 weeks of holidays, from the age of 50 even from 6 weeks. The pension fund contributions are borne 2/3 by the company and 1/3 by the employees. If the employee recommendation is successful, you will receive a premium of CHF 1,000. –
