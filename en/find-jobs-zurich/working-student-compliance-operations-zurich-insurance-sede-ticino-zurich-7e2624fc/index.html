Full description

Interested in exploring the world of insurance Compliance? Curious about working in a stimulating and challenging environment of a multinational company? We are looking for a talent with a positive can-do attitude who is able to think out of the box.

Join us as a working student and become part of our new generation of talent at Zurich.

Now is the time to move forward and make a difference. At Group Compliance, we want you to share your unique perspectives, experiences, and ideas so we can grow and drive sustainable change together. You will be introduced to a broad spectrum of topics while working on several priorities in close collaboration with many team members in a truly international environment. The role provides a great opportunity to collaborate closely with many experts, while being empowered to contribute and present your own ideas.

Start date: January 2027

What you will do

  • Support the Compliance Operations and Assurance team to execute core Compliance processes such as the Compliance Risk Assessment and the annual Compliance activities planning and to drive continuous process improvement
  • Support the team in the development, operation and continuous enhancement of the Compliance GRC tool, existing dashboard applications in Power BI and visualization tools
  • Drive data analysis and preparation to identify global trends and insights
  • Support the team in managing our Group, regional and local stakeholders
  • Support and drive existing and future innovation and automation projects.

What you bring

  • Actively pursuing higher education, ideally studying Data Analytics, Business Administration or equivalent
  • Prior work experience is required, financial services/consulting is an advantage
  • Growth mindset, can-do attitude, team player, solution oriented, curiosity, learning agility
  • Tech skills: data analytics skills, Power Bi and AI/Copilot knowledge required. Python and Databricks nice to have
  • Strong oral and written communication skills. Business English fluency is essential
  • For the length of the contract, enrolled at a University/ Institute of higher education and be located in Switzerland.

Additional Information
