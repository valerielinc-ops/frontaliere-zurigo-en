The leading private hospital group sets standards with its 16 private clinics, more than 100 integrated competence centers and more than 50 specialized institutes. First-class medical services and the well-being of people are our focus.

At the CheckupCenter of Hirslanden Clinic, we use detailed medical programs to clarify the health status of our customers and advise them with competent experts regarding their optimal lifestyle and a healthy, balanced diet. We are convinced that early detection and prevention are the driving forces of future medicine.

In this interesting and versatile position, you work in a team with other doctors and prevention consultants and take over the professional care of our national and international patients who come to us for one- or multi-day check-ups.

ITS TASKS

  • Medical history survey, clinical examination and performance of additional examinations
  • Conducting consultations with individual recommendations in German and English
  • Close cooperation with prevention consultancy
  • Exchange in a network with specialist and clinicians
  • Active participation in the further development of checkup products and services

YOUR PROFILE

  • Specialist title FMH for General or Internal Medicine
  • Work experience in preventive medicine
  • Joyful exchange with personalities from home and abroad
  • Interest in sports and exercise medicine

Do your strengths include social skills, commitment and enthusiasm? Do you appreciate the detailed discussion with your clients, where you can take enough time? Do you prefer a personal, motivating and mutually supportive atmosphere in a small team? Do you like working in a beautiful and stylish environment?Then we look forward to your application!
