Full description

The public Spitex Wald Fischenthal is responsible for the ambulatory health care of the inhabitants of Wald and Fischenthal. Spitex employees care for their clients competently and empathetically in their own home, look after them psychiatrically or support them in everyday life. Spitex Wald Fischenthal is part of the non-profit foundation Drei Tannen, which together with Spitex runs the newly built Rosenthal Care Centre and offers old-age apartments with needs-based service. Dipl. Nurse HF/FH Somatic

Your tasks

  • Individual care & support: You accompany our clients with heart and expertise in their everyday life.
  • Case Management & Responsibility: You control the care and care process and take over the case management.
  • Medical technology measures: You use your expertise specifically to ensure the best possible care.
  • Flexibility in use: Evening and weekend assignments are part of your varied workday.
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: You work closely with colleagues within the Foundation Drei Tannen, external partners and relatives to ensure holistic support.
  • Innovation: You see change as part of life. You meet new requirements openly, think innovatively and actively shape further developments.

What you bring

  • Diploma as nurse HF/FH
  • Experience with RAI HC and digital work tools
  • A communicative, team-capable personality with a good knowledge of German
  • Category B driving licence
  • Self-initiative, self-responsibility and flexibility – you tackle and find solutions
  • And of course: a good dose of humor – because our job is fulfilling, but also challenging!
