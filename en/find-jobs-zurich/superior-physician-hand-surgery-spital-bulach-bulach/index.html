Full description

<b>Bülach Hospital grows - grow with it.</b>

Tasks

  • Professional responsibility in the entire spectrum of hand and peripheral nervous surgery as well as carrying out operations under your own responsibility
  • Independent consultation hours in close interdisciplinary cooperation with the occupational therapists
  • Active participation in further education and training and support of our assistant doctors
  • Cooperation and assumption of responsibility in clinical projects and the further development of hand surgery
  • A versatile area of responsibility with high professional and personal development potential as well as the opportunity to contribute your own ideas and strengths

Requirements

  • Swiss specialist title hand surgery or shortly before graduation
  • Around 3–4 years of professional experience as well as in-depth knowledge in the entire spectrum of hand and peripheral nerve surgery
  • Strong patient orientation, high service orientation and enjoyment of professional interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Enjoying the training and promotion of young colleagues as well as strong knowledge transfer skills
  • A value- and strength-oriented attitude that makes confidence, professionalism, agility, efficiency and service orientation visible in daily actions
