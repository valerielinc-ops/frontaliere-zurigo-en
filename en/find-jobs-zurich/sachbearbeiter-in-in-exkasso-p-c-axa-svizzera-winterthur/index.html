Come to AXA, Switzerland's leading insurance company - would you like to actively shape the future with us? And develop innovative solutions together? If yes - then you have come to the right place!

  • Independent processing from the invoice to the debt collection up to the loss certificate in your own area of responsibility (property and personal insurance)

  • Completed commercial apprenticeship or Equivalent basic training
  • Excellent knowledge of German and very good knowledge of French (knowledge of Italian is an advantage)
  • 2 to 3 years of experience in debt collection and/or knowledge of the SchKG (an advantage) or 2 to 3 years of experience in the insurance industry in internal sales or customer service
  • Reliable, resilient and open-minded personality
  • Strong customer and service orientation
  • Openness to change, enjoys customer contact and good communication skills
  • Dedicated team player with a good dose of humor
