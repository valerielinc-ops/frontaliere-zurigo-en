Full description

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a controller immediately or by agreement 60-80%

Your responsibilities

  • Working in a small dedicated controlling team
  • Support for a wide range of business tasks
  • Support for the further development of the management information system and monthly reporting
  • Preparation of the cost, contribution and performance accounts and ad hoc evaluations for ambulatory and stationary performance areas
  • Support for the production of annual statistics for external bodies
  • Ensuring data quality in the controlling process

Your profile

  • Completed higher business education (FH or university)
  • Professional experience in controlling, experience in healthcare is beneficial
  • Very good knowledge of Microsoft Excel
  • Responsible, reliable and solution-oriented personality
  • Knowledge of BI Software HCe is an advantage
  • Systematic, careful and independent working method
  • Strong analytical skills, fast comprehension and high IT affinity

What we offer you

  • Working independently in a motivated and collegial team that supports each other.
  • A place of work with the best connection to public transport as well as cost-effective staff parking spaces.
  • Modern infrastructure and a working environment in which you can feel comfortable – lake views included.
  • Diverse benefits – from great employee discounts to further education offers.
  • A pleasant working atmosphere in an attractive location near Zurich, which has a lot to offer you both professionally and privately. Physical applications cannot be considered or returned.
