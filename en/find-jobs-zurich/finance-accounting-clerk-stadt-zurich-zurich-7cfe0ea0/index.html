Full description

Due to a retirement in our "Finance" team, which is located in the "Central Services" area, we are looking for a resilient and flexible personality with quick comprehension as of 1.2.2027 or by agreement.

Tasks

  • Independent support and advice to the various management teams in accounting matters
  • Accounts payable and payable
  • Management and administration of intra-city billing and reconciliation of accounts with counterparty
  • Implementation of debtor warning runs and monitoring of all dunning controls
  • Cooperation on incidental cost accounts
  • Payment processing (DTA, LSV, DD)
  • Independent monitoring of Sachkonti

Profile

  • Basic Commercial Training and Completed Further Training as an Accounting Officer*
  • Several years of professional experience in a comparable area of responsibility
  • High customer and service orientation
  • Good communication skills in dealing with different stakeholders and high social skills
  • Very good MS Office and SAP knowledge (FICO, RE-FX, etc.)
  • Independent, reliable and exact working method
  • Rapid comprehension and networked thinking

About us

Properties for a livable city of Zurich

Real Estate City of Zurich (LSZ), with its portfolio of 5.8 billion francs in insurance value, is one of the major real estate organizations in Switzerland. In addition to the management of around 10,300 apartments and 1,600 commercial premises, we are responsible for the comprehensive ownership representation, the long-term value preservation and the further development of this versatile portfolio. In addition, we manage the car parks, the municipal land reserves and building rights and are responsible for municipal real estate transactions.
