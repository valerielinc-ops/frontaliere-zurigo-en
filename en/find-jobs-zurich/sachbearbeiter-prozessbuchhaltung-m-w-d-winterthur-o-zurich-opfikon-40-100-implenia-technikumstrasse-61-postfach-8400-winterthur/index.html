Full description

As the largest real estate management service provider of demanding residential, office and commercial space, we offer our customers a comprehensive all-round service package.

The Finance & Corporate Center (FCC) division provides a wide range of services for our property owners and internal customers with the aim of providing a complete service of high quality and efficiency.

To support an existing team, we are looking for a committed, experienced and independent personality as

Case Manager Process Accounting (m/f/d)

Winterthur or Zurich (Opfikon), 40 - 100%

This is what moves you

  • You manage your own portfolio in the side book and accompany digital accounting processes
  • Creditor and debtor invoices and credits are booked on time
  • You maintain, vote and correct creditor and debtor accounts and keep all bookings clean
  • Creditor invoices and reminders in digital inbox (D/F/I) you process efficiently and solution-oriented
  • You take over the charge of individual bills to tenants
  • As a contact person for internal and external stakeholders, you will answer accounting questions competently
  • You support and advise real estate accounting, management and center management in financial processes
  • Your reporting ensures transparency: You keep a pendant list of open/unprocessed cases

This is why you succeed

  • You have completed a basic commercial education or are in training – ideally with experience in real estate management or accounting
  • With MS Office (Word/Excel/PowerPoint) you know very well, and ideally you already have experience with specialist applications (e.g. Immo-ERP)
  • An exact, structured and forward working method with a high service orientation distinguishes you
  • You communicate stylishly in German, French and/or Italian are beneficial

Here are some of our benefits

  • Working hours: We live mobile work with individual (year) working hours and/or part-time work. Up to 40% home office is possible. Work-life balance is a top priority for us, because balanced and relaxed employees are important to us. The purchase of additional holidays is possible.
  • Mobility and food: You will receive a contribution for the mobile phone. For your food, we also make a contribution.
  • Education and training: Promoting employees is an important part of our culture. Benefit from generous support in internal and external education and training. We offer career opportunities in management, specialist and project functions
  • Salär: With us, you can expect a market-based remuneration with absolute equal pay for all genders. The pension benefits are attractive and you can choose the right one for you from different models. Accident protection insurance covers the private hospital department.

More information about our benefits can be found on our career page.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
