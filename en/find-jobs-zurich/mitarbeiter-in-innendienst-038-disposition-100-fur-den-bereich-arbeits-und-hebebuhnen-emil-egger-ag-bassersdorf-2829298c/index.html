Full description

You like to organize, coordinate with foresight and ensure that everything comes together? Then you are right with us. Emil Egger AG stands for competence, quality and reliable solutions. For our field of work and lifting platforms, we are looking for a dedicated personality who holds the strings in the office and in the disposition. You are the central hub between our chauffeurs, project managers and customers and ensure that orders are efficiently planned, coordinated and reliably processed. Your tasks

  • Scheduling and operational planning: You plan and coordinate the operations of our chauffeurs and work and lifting platforms.
  • Order processing: You process customer inquiries, record orders and accompany them from the request to the successful execution.
  • Turntable function: You coordinate the day-to-day operations (chauffeurs, project managers and customers) and keep an eye on deadlines, capacities and priorities
  • Customer contact: You are the contact person for our customers, advise them on organizational questions and find suitable solutions.
  • Administrative support: You keep the overview and support the project managers in the administrative offer and order processing

You bring that with you

  • A commercial or technical education or a comparable qualification.
  • Experience in in-house service, scheduling, order coordination or transport logistics is an advantage.
  • Organizational talent and pleasure in reconciling different tasks and contacts.
  • An independent, structured and forward-looking way of working.
  • A communicative personality with customer orientation and healthy assertiveness.
  • Resilience, flexibility and enjoyment of a dynamic working day.
  • Good knowledge of German and secure user knowledge in the current IT programs.

This is what awaits you

  • A varied combination of in-house service, customer contact and disposition.
  • A central function with responsibility and short decision paths.
  • A versatile area of responsibility in which organizational talent and initiative are required.
  • A collaborative team and a practical working environment.
  • Contemporary employment conditions at an established Swiss company.

You enjoy coordinating people, orders and processes and keeping track of them? Become part of our team. We look forward to seeing you!
