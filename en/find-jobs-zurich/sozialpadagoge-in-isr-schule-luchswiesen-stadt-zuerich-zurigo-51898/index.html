Full description

As of 1 January 27 or by agreement we are looking for a social pedagogue*in ISR about 20%.

Tasks

  • You work from the 1. January 2027 or by appointment on two to three days per week as a social pedagogue*in ISR in our classroom and accompanied two children in the 3. Great.
  • You're implementing support measures in class.
  • These are created by a school-based curative teacher in cooperation with you.
  • Cooperation and arrangements in the class team with class teachers and school-based Heilpädagogin.

Profile

  • Experience in working with children
  • Openness and willingness to engage students with special needs
  • Joy of working in school
  • Team spirit and interest in interdisciplinary cooperation
  • A solution-oriented and reflected approach
  • Flexibility, initiative and resilience
  • As a sociologist*in ISR you have a diploma in: Sozialpädagogik FH/HF, Social Work FH/HF, Child Education HF or equivalent recognised training (for foreign diploma degrees, the application documents must be accompanied by Swiss recognition)

We offer

  • A dedicated, supportive team and an appreciative school management
  • Space for your ideas and participation
  • An open, collegial collaboration in the school team
  • Conditions of employment and social benefits according to the guidelines of the City of Zurich

About us

The Schwamendingen School District covers the entire urban district of 12 of the city of Zurich. Schwamendingen is a lively, multicultural residential district. By public transport we are easily accessible via Stettbach and Oerlikon station with various tram and bus lines.

The school Lynchswiesen is a quiMS school in Zurich-Schwamendingen. It currently comprises five kindergartens, sixteen primary classes and the care area. The school Lynchswiesen has a long tradition as a school club, which offers the students an integrated full-day care.
