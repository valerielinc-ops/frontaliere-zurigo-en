Full description

You lead challenging infrastructure projects from planning to implementation and thus actively participate in the further development of our plants and buildings. You coordinate interdisciplinary project teams, external partners and operations, keep an eye on costs, deadlines and quality and ensure that complex projects are successfully implemented taking into account operational and public framework conditions.

Ready for a job with meaning?

Tasks

  • You lead infrastructure projects such as renewals of process engineering plants in the area of wastewater treatment plant (ARA), the construction of a new logistics base or renovations / conversions of the building infrastructure.
  • You coordinate interdisciplinary project teams, external planning offices, executing construction companies and the operating team.
  • You control projects in a forward-looking manner with an additional view of regulatory requirements, risks, contracts and operational framework conditions.
  • You are responsible for the creation of concepts, decision bases and specifications as well as tendering and awarding of planning and construction services according to the guidelines of public procurement.
  • You ensure compliance with legal regulations, quality requirements and common safety standards.

Profile

  • Completed degree in mechanical engineering, civil engineering, industrial engineering, process engineering, architecture or similar discipline
  • At least 5 years of experience managing large or infrastructure projects, ideally with points of contact with the public sector
  • In-depth knowledge in project management and ideally in construction and procurement law
  • Experience in cooperation with planning offices, general planners and suppliers
  • Negotiation skills, assertiveness and diplomatic sensitivity in dealing with different stakeholders

About us

With around 1,000 employees, ERZ is one of the largest service departments in the city of Zurich and operates in an exciting environment at the pulse of time. We are modern, down-to-earth, open-minded, all per you and want to make a difference. Together we are committed to a clean, innovative and sustainable Zurich.

ERZ stands for diversity and mixed teams.
