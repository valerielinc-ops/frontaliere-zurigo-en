Full description

dormakaba is one of the top 3 companies in the market for access and security solutions worldwide. We bring more than 100 years of experience and presence in over 60 countries. We offer innovative, comprehensive access control solutions for buildings, facilities and locations as well as solutions for collecting employee and company data.

As Project Coordinator PMO (m/f/d), you are the central hub between project management, controlling, technology, purchasing and logistics. You ensure that our ambitious projects in the area of access control systems and time management are optimally supported administratively, organizationally and operationally. In this function, Ensure that materials, information and resources are available at the right place at the right time. Thanks to your understanding of technical connections and local market requirements (construction industry, SIA work contract, installation and commissioning processes of system solutions), you will recognize dependencies at an early stage and actively contribute to the success of the project.

A job that counts: Your tasks

  • Close support to project leaders throughout the project process
  • Monitoring of project-related material orders and delivery dates
  • Support in controlling the finances of complex projects
  • Coordination between project management, controlling, logistics & purchasing
  • Maintaining and ensuring data quality in SAP and other project tools
  • Support for technical and organisational clarifications
  • Participation in the continuous improvement of our PMO and project processes

Experience that matters: Your skills

  • Commercial or basic technical training
  • Further education in the field of business administration, project management or supply chain is advantageous
  • Very good SAP knowledge (modules MM and SD of advantage) as well as safe handling of Microsoft 365
  • Familiar with local market conditions: SIA work contracts as well as installation and commissioning processes of technical system solutions in the construction industry
  • Strong coordination skills and enjoyment of working with different stakeholders
  • Excellent knowledge of German in words and writing. English is beneficial.

A workplace that counts: Our offer

  • Best opportunities in a global group with the culture of a family business
  • Attractive salary package
  • Good further development opportunities
  • A first-class staff restaurant
  • Support for militia work/non-profit projects also during working hours
  • Modern employment conditions with flexible working models (work time, place of work, home office)
