Full description

At the Institute for Therapies and Rehabilitation about 100 people work. The clinical staff consists of different therapeutic professional groups and is divided into five different specialist teams. We offer specialized individual and group therapies for inpatients and outpatients. The institute also offers training places for up to ten students.

Tasks

  • Independent treatment of patients with pelvic floor complaints in the ambulatory and inpatient area
  • Implementation of current evidence-based standards and further development of the field of basin flooring
  • Interprofessional cooperation and cooperation with the various departments of the Basin Floor Center at the KSW (urology, gynecology, gastroenterology and more)

Profile

  • You have completed a Bachelor of Science in Physical Therapy
  • You are motivated to specialize in the field of pelvic flooring and are ready to acquire new knowledge
  • Interest in the treatment of dysfunctions of pelvic floor complaints
  • You appreciate the interdisciplinary cooperation with doctors and colleagues from different therapeutic fields

Benefits: Work and private life should fit together. Therefore, the KSW offers you – where the company makes it possible – flexible and flexible working hours, home office, generous maternity leave, paternity leave and employment levels that can be adapted to different phases of life. Develop yourself further professionally and personally: Every year, around 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses are open to you at KSW. We also support external training – temporally and/or financially.

  • Sometimes it needs support. In the event of personal stress, prolonged illness or difficult experiences in your professional or private life, various confidential discussions are available at KSW.
  • At KSW we work together and for each other. Appreciation, open and respectful communication and cooperation across professional groups and departments shape our everyday work. You should be able to contribute with your ideas and strengths and develop. Your future is also ensured: You benefit from very good social benefits and a pension fund solution with BVK, which is significantly above the legal minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, you are very well covered as a KSW employee.
  • Your health is important to us. Benefit from discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training and courses as well as discounts in pharmacies and selected health insurance companies.
  • Family and work can be well connected with us. The daycare center La Luna with spacious opening hours is right next to the KSW. In addition, the KSW – depending on income – financially supports you with the crib costs.
