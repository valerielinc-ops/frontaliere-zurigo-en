Full description

With us you can actively participate. We are looking for you: A personality with integrity who, together with all colleagues, ensures that internal and external requirements are met and that our bank, as well as yourself, can continue to develop splendidly.

Mansioni

  • You will assist in the preparation of management and board meetings and prepare presentations and decision bases for the CRO, the management and the board of directors
  • You support the Risk & Compliance divisional development and are jointly responsible for the development and delivery of the deliverables by the governance and reporting team
  • Managing the mailbox of Governance & Reporting is also part of your area of responsibility
  • You will support the organisation of townhalls, roadshows and FINMA risk monitor meetings and coordinate smaller (renovation) projects in the area of risk and compliance
  • You are responsible for various administrative tasks (e.g. default management, monitoring of deadlines and pendency management, maintenance and acuteization of Confluence and intranet site)
  • You are responsible for planning, coordinating and monitoring e-learning as well as creating risk reporting

Requirements

  • 1 - 2 years: in banking (mandatory) or risk management (advantageous)
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): Banking & Finance or Risk
  • You have initial experience in the banking or risk management environment and have knowledge of the processes and processes in the banking and / or regulatory environment
  • You enjoy administrative activities and work efficiently and accurately even under high workload
  • You have an experienced use of PC programs and good MS Office knowledge (especially PowerPoint, Excel and Word) as well as experience with Confluence or similar tools
  • You are a friendly and solution-oriented personality, have communication strength and teamwork skills
  • You have a strong organizational flair and make sure that deadlines are always met
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • French (advantageous)
  • Italian (advantageous)
  • English (good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Migros Bank: accounts, mortgages and financial services with preferential terms
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Child care: up to CHF 300 per month per child to external child care
  • Employee offers: Annual generous well-being credit
