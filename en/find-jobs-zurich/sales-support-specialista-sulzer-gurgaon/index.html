SUMMARY: The Sales Support Specialist will be a pivotal member of our team in Gurgaon, providing comprehensive administrative and operational support to our global front-sales and technical sales teams. ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS

QUALIFICATIONS

  • Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Commerce, or a related field is preferred.
  • 2-5 years of experience in a sales support, administrative, or office management role, preferably within a global organization.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English are essential.
