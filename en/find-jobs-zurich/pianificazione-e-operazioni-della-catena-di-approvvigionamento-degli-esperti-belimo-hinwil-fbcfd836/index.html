Full description

Introduction

Do you want to actively shape global master data processes and contribute your SAP and MDM expertise effectively? In this role, you take responsibility for our Master Data Governance and develop sustainable solutions with various specialist areas

Main responsibilities

  • You design and take responsibility for the global master data governance of the BELIMO Group in accordance with the supply chain strategy
  • You will supervise and develop the globally used MDM tools and SAP processes and use your expertise in SAP Customizing.
  • You ensure global standards and sustainably high master data quality while keeping an eye on relationships and effects along processes.
  • You coordinate and are responsible for periodic calculation runs as well as the calculation for the annual financial statements
  • As a competent contact person, you advise and accompany different specialist areas, mediate between different requirements and jointly develop viable solutions
  • With strong stakeholder management and persuasiveness, you will gain understanding for sustainable data structures and win different stakeholder groups for joint solutions.
  • You lead cross-divisional MDM projects, think in a networked way and ensure that sustainable solutions are successfully anchored in the organization

Required expertise

  • You have a bachelor’s degree, preferably with a focus on data, analytics, business administration or a comparable discipline.
  • You will bring at least 5 years of relevant professional experience in production planning and master data management and know the requirements of a complex production environment.
  • You have in-depth SAP expertise including customizing as well as very good knowledge of the modules MM, PP, SD and BI and the variant configuration.
  • You have experience in project management and bring know-how in data analysis and big data.
  • With your logical, analytical and networked thinking, you quickly capture complex relationships, keep an overview and work precisely and solution-oriented.
  • You are a strong and convincing personality who competently advises different stakeholders, brings different interests together and also brings persistence in demanding situations.
  • An efficient and decision-making way of working, team orientation and very good German and English skills round off your profile.

About Belimo

Belimo is the world market leader in the development, manufacture and sale of field devices for energy-efficient control of heating, ventilation and air conditioning systems. Flap drives, control valves, sensors and meters form our core business. Founded in 1975, the company has been listed on the Swiss Stock Exchange (SIX) since 1995.
