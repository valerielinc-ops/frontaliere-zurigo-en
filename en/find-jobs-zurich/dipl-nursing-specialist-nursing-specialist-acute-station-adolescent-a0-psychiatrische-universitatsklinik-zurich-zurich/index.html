Full description

  • The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich treats and cares for mentally ill people of all ages. The more than 2,500 employees from 55 different professional profiles work in interdisciplinary teams to contribute to the holistic care of patients, from assessment to therapy to aftercare. Our work is based on mutual trust and personal responsibility and together we promote an innovative corporate culture. As a university clinic, we are successfully involved in national and international research projects. At three youth wards in child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy (KJPP), young people between the ages of 14 and 17 with mental disorders are treated and cared for in an interdisciplinary team in psychiatric-psychotherapeutic and milieu therapeutic settings. We are looking for employees who would like to do primarily night shifts and employees who would like to do day shifts as a qualified nursing specialist (acute ward for young people A0) 70% - 90% Your tasks

  • Designing a resource-enhancing milieu-therapeutic treatment setting
  • Implementing the nursing process in the reference person system

  • Active participation in an intensive interprofessional collaboration
  • You see our children and young people as the focus of your work
  • Completed vocational training in the specialist area (proof of recognition for foreign qualifications)
  • You have experience in the field of child and adolescent psychiatry or are willing to acquire the relevant skills
  • Willingness to work various shifts on all days of the week (either early and late shifts or predominantly night shifts)
  • Exciting, varied and responsible areas of responsibility in a versatile, dynamic and university-based environment
  • Case and intervisions
