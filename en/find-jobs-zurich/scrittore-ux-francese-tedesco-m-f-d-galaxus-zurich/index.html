Full description

Do you love language, think user-centred and want to create digital experiences with words? As a UX Writer in our newly founded team, you write texts that intuitively guide customers through our shop. You work in four languages and closely with UX Design, Research and Product Development – so that digital experiences work linguistically and sound like Digitec Galaxus.

Mansioni

  • You write clear, concise and user-centred UX texts in French and German for a wide range of online shop touchpoints, including navigation elements, buttons, notifications, customer communications, etc.
  • You consistently apply UX writing standards, tone-of-voice policies, terminology, and microtypography principles to ensure correct, consistent, and branded language across all digital channels
  • You work closely with UX Design, UX Research, Product Development and other language and writing experts to create coherent, seamless user experiences
  • You make sure that texts are written primarily for people without losing sight of the requirements of search engines and digital platforms
  • You will exchange ideas with other language and writing experts and actively contribute to a collaborative and learning team culture.

Requirements

  • 0-3 years: Multilingual UX Writing (FR/DE) in digital retail/e-commerce
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): communication, linguistics, UX, psychology or design
  • French is your mother tongue and you have very good knowledge of German (C2 level) for working with multilingual content
  • Sound training in communication, linguistics, UX, psychology or design – or a comparable qualification with a focus on digital writing
  • Experience in UX Writing in the digital environment
  • Strong sense of language as well as the ability to formulate complex content in a simple, understandable and target group-oriented manner
  • French (negotiable)
  • German (flowing)
