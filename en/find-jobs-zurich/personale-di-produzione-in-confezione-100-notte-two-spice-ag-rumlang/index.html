Production staff: in 100% rice kitchen

Two Spice AG — Dietlikon, Dietlikon, Svizzera

Yooji’s Manufaktur, which is part of Two Spice AG, offers a versatile and exquisite range of sushi, sashimi and much more. The production site of the Two Spice Manufaktur is located in Rümlang and employs more than 90 people. Various delicacies are pre-made every day and delivered to Yooji's restaurants and stores several times a day. Specially developed products for our Japanese retail brand ZENBU are also produced in the factory. ZENBU products such as Sushi, Poké, Onigiri and vegan delicacies are produced for Coop and are available directly from Coop throughout Switzerland. Do you want to be part of our team? Now is your chance!
