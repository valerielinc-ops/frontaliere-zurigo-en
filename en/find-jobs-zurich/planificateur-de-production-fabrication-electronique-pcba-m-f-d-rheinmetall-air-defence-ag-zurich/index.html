Full description

As a production planner (m/f/d) for electronics production, you are responsible for planning our manufacturing processes in the area of SMD and THT assembly of electronic assemblies. Specifically, you take on the following tasks:

  • Independent planning of production orders in the respective production area
  • Scheduling and capacity planning taking into account material availability and capacities at the respective workplaces
  • Ensuring on-time production of production orders
  • Participation in regular matching and exchange dates with the fine control in production as well as the upstream and downstream areas in production as well as material management and project management operations
  • Master data maintenance WHAT YOU SHOULD BUILD
  • Completed technical apprenticeship, e.g. in the field of electronics or mechatronics as well as further training as a technician HF (m/f/d) or comparable
  • Experience in electronics manufacturing (SMD and/or THT equipment)
  • Knowledge of methodological competence and lean management
  • Good application knowledge in SAP, WayRTS/MES and MS Office
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing WHAT WE ARE TO YOU
  • A dynamic working environment with exciting projects
  • Flexible working hours (active and trusted working hours)
  • Very good social benefits and a super-compulsory pension fund
  • Employee benefits of a large corporation, employee share purchase program
  • Promotion of needs-based training
  • Staff restaurant and external catering and shopping facilities nearby
  • Individual internal and external personnel development measures in cooperation with our Rheinmetall Academy
  • Workplace a few minutes walk from the Zurich-Oerlikon train station KONTAKTDETAILS Responsible contact: Melanie Huellen For questions about your application, please use the contact form. Please note that dossiers of recruiters are not considered.
