Original description

In a versatile function as Experienced Professional Procurement - Sensors, you assume responsibility for the strategic and operational management of defined product groups in the international procurement environment. They actively shape the further development of our supplier and product group strategies, drive cost optimization and make a decisive contribution to securing our global supply. You work closely with internal departments, international teams and external partners and contribute your expertise in a dynamic, innovation-driven environment.

What will you do • Ensuring the management and supply of the allocated product groups, taking into account all internal needs and procurement organisations • Active coordination with purchasing regions as well as involvement and information of management level and team • Co-development and implementation of product group and supplier strategies in line with the global procurement strategy • Maintenance, development and regular review of the product group portfolio in cooperation with innovation and annual presentation to management • Responsibility for performance, operational controlling and risk management of the assigned product groups and suppliers • Identification and implementation of cost optimizations along the entire inbound logistics taking into account TCO • Observation of procurement markets and trends for the further development of the product portfolio in compliance with relevant standards • Maintenance of relevant master data in ERP as well as participation in cost reduction and development projects and deputy in the team

Who you are

  • Completed basic technical or commercial training
  • Knowledge of manufacturing processes of products, assemblies and individual parts is advantageous
  • Several years of experience in the procurement environment and in interdisciplinary and global project management
  • Good understanding of industrial supply chains and production processes
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in words and writing (minimum B2–C1) for international cooperation
  • Very good SAP knowledge (module MM) and secure handling of MS Office
  • Analytical thinking skills as well as structured, flexible and independent working methods
  • Ability to quickly grasp complex relationships and a high level of personal responsibility
  • Readiness for moderate travel (about 10%)
