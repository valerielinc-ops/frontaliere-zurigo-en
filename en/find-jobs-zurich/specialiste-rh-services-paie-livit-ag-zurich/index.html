Full description

You are open-minded, ambitious and value-added in a future-oriented way of working on the pulse of the real estate industry? With your commitment, we are jointly facing the challenges of today and tomorrow. You want to participate and make a difference? Then you are exactly right at Livit. We look forward to your personal contribution.

Tasks

  • You make the complete master data acquisition of the employees upon entry
  • You record, check and process wage-relevant mutations
  • You support the pre- and post-checking of monthly payroll processing
  • You coordinate the cooperation with the external Payroll provider
  • You process withholding tax cases including registrations, exits and mutations
  • You lose family allowances and EO/compensation cases administratively
  • You collect and mutate data on occupational pension / pension fund
  • You are in correspondence with authorities, insurance companies and external bodies
  • You provide information to employees and managers on administrative and payroll-related topics
  • You actively work in the team and take over mutual representation

Requirements

  • You have basic commercial training or equivalent training, ideally with further training in payroll, human resources or social security
  • You have professional experience in Payroll, HR Administration or Social Security Administration (D.A.)
  • You have stylish German, good English skills and French on a basic communication level.
  • You communicate service-oriented and addressee-oriented
  • You are acting safely and discreetly, especially when dealing with sensitive personnel data
  • You provide reliable information to employees and managers
  • You like to work in a team and work together constructively
  • You have experience with Workday or a comparable HR/Payroll system is beneficial, as well as very good knowledge of Microsoft 365, especially Excel mandatory
  • You work system-affected, precise and process-oriented

We offer

  • Internal offer of our Livit Academy for specialist and management courses
  • Financial and/or temporary support for external specialist training and access to electronic learning platforms for online language courses
  • Individual career planning and development perspectives
  • Home / Mobile Office
  • Bonus depending on the company's success and personal performance
  • Minimum 5 weeks holidays and purchase of up to 10 additional days holidays (holiday flex)
  • Above average benefits and premiums for day-of-sickness and accident insurance are 100% taken over by Livit
  • 14 days paternity leave

We love real estate and are enthusiastic about maintaining the value of our customers' properties.

Because not only your skills and experience, but also you as a human being are important to us. In our working environment you feel comfortable and can develop. This is due on the one hand to our versatile job profiles and the development opportunities within our company. On the other hand, also to the distinctive corporate culture that is unparalleled in the real estate world.
