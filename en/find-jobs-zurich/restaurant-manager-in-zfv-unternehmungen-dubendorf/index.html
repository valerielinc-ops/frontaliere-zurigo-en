Sorell Hotels is the largest Swiss hotel group owned by 15 individual city and holiday hotels in the 3- and 4-star area in Zurich, Dübendorf, Winterthur, Bern, Rapperswil, St. Gallen, Schaffhausen and Bad Ragaz. The 3-star Sorell Hotel Sonnental with its 100 rooms and its 3 flexibly combinable banquet and seminar rooms for up to 120 people is a popular business and city hotel. The restaurant and wellness facility are a popular meeting place not only for hotel guests, but also for guests from the area. Due to the convenient location with good motorway and public transport connections, the hotel is very easily accessible.
