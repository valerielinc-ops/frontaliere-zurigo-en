Canton of Zurich Finance Directorate Office of Informatics Application Manager SAP Logistics 80 - 100% The Office of Informatics (AFI) is the IT competence center of the Canton of Zurich. It provides comprehensive services in the area of ​​central IT infrastructure and canton-wide applications for directorates, authorities, the judiciary and independent institutions. Would you like to further develop the SAP logistics landscape in the canton of Zurich and provide technical guidance? In this senior specialist role, you combine SAP know-how with strategic vision and a good feel for different stakeholder groups. You coordinate the SAP Logistics specialist group, develop viable solutions together with those involved and help shape the future direction of our services. Your tasks:

  • Technical leadership of the SAP Logistics specialist group and active design of standards, methods and the future direction of the SAP logistics landscape in the Canton of Zurich
  • Responsible for the further development of SAP logistics services and maintaining the service, product and technology roadmap
  • Conception and support of integrated SAP solutions from requirements gathering to successful introduction
  • Control and prioritization of changes, releases, projects and plans as well as shared responsibility for stable, secure and economical operations
  • Advice to the specialist departments, coordination of external partners and participation in projects and specialist committees Your profile:
  • Higher education in computer science, business informatics or business administration or a comparable qualification
  • Several years of SAP experience as well as in-depth knowledge of at least one SAP logistics area, for example SD, MM, PM/CS or Ariba, and a good understanding of integrated business processes
  • Sound knowledge of application management as well as experience with integrated SAP solutions, interfaces, releases and service processes
  • Ability to take responsibility and lead specialists, project teams or external partners clearly and appreciatively
  • Networked, solution-oriented working methods and convincing communication Our offer:
  • A versatile SAP specialist role with impact throughout the canton of Zurich
  • Freedom of design in the further development of our SAP logistics landscape
  • Exciting transformation projects in the environment of SAP S/4HANA and modern SAP technologies
  • Flexible working models, attractive employment conditions and individual development opportunities Interested? We look forward to receiving your application. Please use our online tool for this. Jürg Kasper (Head of Business Solutions) will be happy to answer any questions you may have about the position on phone number: 41 43 259 33 48. Your new environment: www.afi.zh.ch
