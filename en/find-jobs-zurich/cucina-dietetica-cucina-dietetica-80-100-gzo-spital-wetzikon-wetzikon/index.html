Role overview

Tasks

  • As the person responsible for the day, you run the elective food office for patient catering.

In this function, you are the contact person for the nurses and the room service.

In addition, during the absence of the chef on weekends and public holidays, you are responsible for the smooth running of the kitchen.

  • You take over the guidance and guidance of the cooking and diet cooking learners and the kitchen employees assigned to you.
  • You are responsible for the preparations, preparations, tasting and preparation of meals in the small and large production of cold and hot food.

The preparation of food on the food distribution belt is just as much part of this varied activity as work assignments in the patisserie, cold kitchen and equipment kitchen.

  • In this function, you are responsible for hygiene (compliance with and implementation of the GZO hygiene concept), cleanliness and order in the workplaces, in the refrigeration and freezing rooms as well as the day camps and keep track of the cleaning control lists.

Requirements

  • You have completed your further training as a dietitian / dietitian cook with a Swiss certificate or have the previous degree as a dietitian / dietitian EFZ.
  • You have a high degree of duty and quality awareness and are interested in nutrition and dietetics.
