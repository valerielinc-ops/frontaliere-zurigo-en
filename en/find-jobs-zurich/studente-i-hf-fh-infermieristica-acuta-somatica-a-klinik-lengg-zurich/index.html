Full description

You would like to complete your studies for the Dipl. Nursing specialist/man HF at the Lengg Clinic, a hospital with a high reputation – and in the most beautiful location in the city of Zurich? You will care for people with epilepsy and neurological diseases, as well as their complexity throughout the body. They provide holistic care according to the individual needs of our patients. You are involved in a team that accompanies, promotes and challenges you. In the three-year course, you will complete two practical semesters at the Lengg Clinic and one exchange semester with our cooperation partners. A well-trained and highly motivated educational team will accompany you throughout your studies. Possibility of a shortened course of study with an existing apprenticeship as a specialist in health (fage)
