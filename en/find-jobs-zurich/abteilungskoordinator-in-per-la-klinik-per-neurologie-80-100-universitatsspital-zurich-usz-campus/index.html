Full description

Zurich University Hospital is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.

Tasks

  • Health specialist / Health specialist (f/m/d) and already have experience in the acute field or want to change there
  • Comes from the age and long-term care or the rehabilitation area and wants to take the step into acute setting
  • Starting fresh from training or having many years of professional experience, we are open to both
  • Are motivated to learn new things and engage in a dynamic, neurospecialized environment

Requirements

  • In tandem with a dipl. You are a nurse at one of our two neurological bed stations
  • Your main task is the care of patients: women with stroke, Parkinson's, multiple sclerosis and neuromuscular diseases
  • We also take on patients: insides from our vertigo center as well as from the Stroke Center
  • You work closely with an experienced interdisciplinary team (doctor: inside, therapist: inside as well as specialist: inside different disciplines and focus areas)

We offer

With us you will experience the entire, exciting breadth of neurology. Much of it will seem familiar to you and we will be happy to show you how many touch points you already bring. In the areas that are still new to you, you learn quickly and practically with us.

Our two neurological bed stations at USZ work closely together and support each other. Interdisciplinary cooperation is also central: Our experts, from the medical service to the therapist, are working with you to ensure the best possible care for our patients.

Since we regularly coordinate with the two IMCs of the Stroke Center (Stroke Unit and Post Stroke Unit), you also benefit from station and cross-professional training, which is prepared both by nursing and doctors.
