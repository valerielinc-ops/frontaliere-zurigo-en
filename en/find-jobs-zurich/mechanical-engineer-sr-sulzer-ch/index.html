Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

Job Summary The (Mechanical) Engineering Expert is responsible for leading the design, development, and optimization of mechanical systems and components. This role provides expert-level technical leadership in mechanical engineering, ensuring that products meet performance, reliability, and cost targets. The expert collaborates across departments to drive innovation and resolve complex engineering challenges. This job profile is intended for recognized subject matter experts in a job area. Required to manage large projects or processes with limited oversight from manager. Coaches, reviews and delegates work to lower level professionals. Position Summary: Lead design, development, and testing and the implementation of innovative products and technologies. The roles is responsible for driving mechanical engineering solutions from product feasibility through implementation into production by collaborating with cross-functional teams. The position combines strong technical expertise, creativity, and project leadership skills to solve complex engineering challenges and deliver high quality products.

Principle Duties and Key Responsibilities

  • Lead mechanical design and development of new products and technologies.
  • Lead mechanical design of product improvements for cost out or consolidation of similar technologies.
  • Generate and evaluate engineering concepts, prototypes, and design alternatives.
  • Performa engineering calculations, simulations, and tolerance analysis.
  • Develop detailed #D CAD models, engineering drawings, and technical documentation.
  • Conduct design verification, validation testing, and root cause analysis.
  • Manage product development projects, schedules, budgets, and milestones.
  • Review and check engineering documentation, specifications and change requests.
  • Stay current with industry trends, emerging technologies, materials, and manufacturing processes.
  • Perform other duties and special projects as assigned.

Education

Required: BSME Bachelor of Science Mechanical Engineering or related discipline
