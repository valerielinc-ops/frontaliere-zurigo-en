Role overview

Canton Zurich Tax Office Application Operation Manager /-in ICT 90% Welcome to the fascinating world of taxes! The cantonal tax office together with the municipal tax offices collects taxes for the federal government, the canton and the municipalities. We support private individuals and companies throughout the tax collection process. Around 800 qualified employees are involved in this at the site in Zurich Altstetten. We have a complex IT environment with central technical applications and systems around the tax collection process.

You bring in your technical know-how, take responsibility and are part of a team that together ensures stable and secure IT operations.

Your tasks

  • Operation and LifeCycle Management of specialist applications, IT systems, tools and services in the cantonal tax office
  • Analysis of faults and error states as well as introduction, implementation and monitoring of suitable measures
  • Ensure availability, security and stability of applications, data and IT infrastructure
  • Technical support of Service Owner in 2nd level IT operations and professional support of external employees
  • Further development of IT infrastructure and preparation and maintenance of technical documentation
  • Active participation in various

Projects and interdisciplinary teams Your profile

  • Completed higher vocational training in the field of computer science or comparable qualifications
  • Multi-year experience in the operation of technical applications, IT systems or complex IT infrastructures
  • Advanced practical knowledge with Unix/Linux systems and good understanding for IT operation, availability and security
  • Additional certifications such as ITIL, HERMES, TOGAF, SAFe or Scrum are advantageous
  • Structured, independent and solution-oriented working methods
  • Service-oriented personality with team spirit and

good communication capability in German and technical English

  • openness to change and interest in digitalization, agility and continuous development
  • willingness to use outside office hours, for example in maintenance, installation or malfunctions Our offer
