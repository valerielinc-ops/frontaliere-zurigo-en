Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School Wiedikon Rector: in 80 - 100% The Cantonal School Aussersihl (KAS) emerges from the Hohlstrasse branch of the Cantonal School Wiedikon since the school year 2024/25. For the school year 2027/28 it is founded as an independent middle school. This exciting development phase offers the opportunity to actively shape the cultural, organizational and educational identity of a new cantonal school in Zurich. For the management of the Cantonal School Aussersihl, we advertise the position as Rector:in on 1 March 2027. You take over the pedagogical, personnel and organizational management of the Cantonal School Aussersihl and decisively shape its development in the development phase.

  • Strategic, pedagogical and operational management of the school
  • Leadership and promotion of school management, teachers and staff
  • Further development of teaching quality and school culture
  • Responsibility for human, financial and resource planning
  • Cooperation with cantonal bodies and other stakeholders
  • You have a university degree, a teaching diploma for maturity schools, many years of teaching experience at high school level and strong leadership experience in the field of education. They think strategically, communicate winningly and shape change with joy and conviction. We offer the unique opportunity to help shape a new middle school in the heart of Zurich. You can expect a versatile and challenging management task with great creative leeway, a well-established team and employment conditions according to cantonal specifications. If you are interested, please submit your application exclusively via our online tool. For information please contact Karin Grob (043 256 99 41 or karin.grob@kwi.ch).

Pensum: 80-100 %
