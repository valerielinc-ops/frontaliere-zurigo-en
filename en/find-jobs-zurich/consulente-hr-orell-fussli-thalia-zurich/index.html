Full description

Would you like to open a new chapter in your professional career? With your passion you are exactly right with us. At Orell Füssli Thalia AG, we create spaces for diverse stories and knowledge that inspire and enrich our society. At the same time, we are a place where employees feel comfortable, enjoy working together and achieve more together. For our headquarters in Zurich Wiedikon, we are looking for the imminent retirement of a long-term colleague on 1 January. March 2027 or by arrangement a committed personality as HR Consultant 100% (a)

Their main tasks

  • Advising and supporting managers and employees along the entire Employee Life Cycle in the assigned region
  • Responsibility for operational HR support in recruitment, personnel planning, development and performance as well as in labour law issues
  • Sparring partner for managers in challenging management, organizational and change topics as well as in discussion and conflict situations
  • Implementation of the HR strategy as well as active participation in HR projects and in the further development of our HR processes
  • Analysis and interpretation of HR indicators and reports as well as conducting training and workshops on HR topics

Your profile

  • In-depth further training in the field of human resources, e.g. HR specialist with federal certificate, DAS/MAS HRM, as well as several years of experience in a comparable HR function
  • Strong guidance and service orientation as well as enjoyment of working with different people and stakeholders
  • Good knowledge of modern HR systems, digital processes and contemporary recruiting channels
  • Networked thinking and a good feeling for operational contexts and different needs
  • Independent and solution-oriented personality with initiative, team spirit and pleasure in continuously developing

Our offer

  • Collaborative and appreciative corporate culture in a well-established HR team
  • Versatile HR function with design scope and proximity to business
  • Individual development and training opportunities
  • Generous holiday arrangements as well as attractive staff discounts and other benefits
  • Central workplace in Zurich Wiedikon, just a five-minute walk from the train station

Be there in our world of stories and knowledge. We look forward to your complete online application via our recruiting tool. A competent and established HR team looks forward to meeting you.
