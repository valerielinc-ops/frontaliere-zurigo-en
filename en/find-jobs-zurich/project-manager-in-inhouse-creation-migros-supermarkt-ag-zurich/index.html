Role overview

We are looking for a project manager for the in-house creation of Migros.

You are the central interface to internal clients, coordinate communication projects, sharpen briefings and ensure smooth processes.

Together with the team management, you will continue to develop processes and standards and work closely with art, text, concept and video on various marketing measures.
