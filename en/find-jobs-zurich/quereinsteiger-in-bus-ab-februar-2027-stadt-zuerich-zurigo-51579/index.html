Full description

Welcome to the front of the bus! As a lateral entrant* on a bus, you play a decisive role in determining the rhythm of our city. You bring ours, no, yours! Customers safely, punctually and pleasantly from A to B almost around the clock in 7.5 minutes.

Tasks

  • Safe and reliable transport of our passengers with modern vehicle fleet 365 days a year, almost around the clock
  • Provision of information to passengers, information in the event of diversions and disruptions
  • Constantly expand your knowledge of the line network

Profile

  • Trustworthy and reliable personality with pleasure in customer contact
  • Communicative skill: friendly and courteous treatment of our passengers
  • Flexibility and willingness to work irregular hours
  • Category B driving licence (for at least three years or category C driving licence)
  • Complete knowledge of German (at least level B2)

We offer

  • The people of Zurich are öv fans, they appreciate what you do
  • Discover the whole of Switzerland with the free GA – and the family also drives really well thanks to generous discounts
  • We help you stay physically and mentally fit with our internal courses and our own fitness room

About us

We are the pulse of Zurich and keep the city in motion every 7.5 minutes. In order for this to work reliably and smoothly, a lot moves behind the scenes of the VBZ. Our colleagues work in over 50 professions: in technology, IT, administration, traffic development, offer planning, infrastructure and many other exciting areas of responsibility. Where we drive, Zurich lives. Come on!
