Key responsibilities Are you looking for a flexible part-time job in permanent employment at UBS with a degree of employment of 40% to 60% and thus a first entry into the financial world? Digital technologies – not a foreign word for you? You are a communicative personality who convinces on the phone and in video consulting. With your sensitive and open-minded nature, you quickly recognize the signals in the customer conversation. Your talent is in demand! For our location in Zurich we are looking for reinforcement.

You will

  • You can deepen your interest in provision, investments, and financing and use it profitably.
  • optimally apply your knowledge in pension planning and pension planning as well as in the mortgage sector.
  • On behalf of UBS, you contact our customers as part of bank- and product-specific campaigns.
  • Apply your sales skills on the phone and support our specialists by making appointments
  • over time in your professional development closely accompanied by the management team.

In "Remote Sales & Advice - Outbound" you lay the foundation for your career in the financial industry. The ideal opportunity to develop personally and professionally in a dynamic team. You get the opportunity to expand your banking knowledge and further develop your communication skills.

The team You work in the outbound team of the Digital Remote Sales unit in Zurich. We are a young, motivated team with many part-time employees in particular. Students. Surrounded by a young and dynamic team, you will contact our existing and new bank customers. Thanks to our “Remote Sales & Advice” unit, they benefit from professional and location-independent advice by telephone or in video consulting. Diversity helps us grow together. That is why we are committed to promoting and nurturing diversity, equal opportunities and inclusion. This strengthens our business and adds value to our customers.

Your skills and experience

  • A completed basic education with a deepening in the area of Banking & Finance (e.g. commercial apprenticeship Bank, Matura with a focus on Economics/Finance, studies or further education in the financial sector)
  • Strong interest in banking topics as well as passion for sales and the talent to inspire and convince people
  • Safe and competent appearance on the phone, with which you address customers professionally and effectively
  • Routine in the efficient use of IT applications and digital technologies, especially in the banking environment
  • Knowledge of our financial products, such as bank packages, credit cards and digital products
  • Advantage: Experience in the distribution of investment and pension solutions and, if necessary, mortgage financing
  • Very good knowledge of German and a good understanding of spoken Swiss dialects; further language skills (English, French, Italian) are beneficial
