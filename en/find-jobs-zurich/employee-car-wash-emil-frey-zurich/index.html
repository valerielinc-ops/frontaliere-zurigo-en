Full description

What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family company with 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessories as well as the provision of financial services. At the headquarters in Zurich-Altstetten is the central administration of the Emil Frey Group, which controls the overarching activities. In Switzerland, the Treasury Department is responsible for the financing and processing of banking activities of Group companies, including foreign currency and interest rate risk hedges. To strengthen the team, we are looking for a Transaction Manager - Structured Finance

  • Creation, analysis and further development of data models, statistics, data tapes and management and ad hoc reports
  • Support in structuring new financing solutions and capital market transactions
  • Preparation of investor presentations, marketing documents and decision-making basis for management
  • Identification and solution of operational and transaction-related issues (troubleshooting)
