Full description

Canton of Zurich Financial Directorate Office for Computer Science ICT Professionals 80 - 100% The Office for Computer Science (AFI) is the computer science competence center of the Canton of Zurich. It provides comprehensive services in the area of central computer infrastructure and canton-wide applications for directorates, authorities, the administration of justice and independent institutions. In charge of implementing the new Information and Communication Technology (ICT) strategy, the Office accompanies the canton into the digital world. With us, young ICT talents not only learn theory, but also work in the middle of a versatile computer science environment. In order for them to grow professionally, find their place and be well prepared for the professional world, they need people who combine education with heart, structure and understanding of ICT. This is where you come in. You take on a central role in our ICT basic education, currently accompany 11 learners through their training and develop a modern, practical learning environment together with the practical trainers. Your tasks:

  • Coordination and further development of ICT basic education in the AFI, with a focus on well-coordinated and practical training
  • Planning of training processes, stages and practical assignments in coordination with education plan, learning objectives and operational opportunities
  • Professional, methodological and personal support of the learners during the entire apprenticeship period, including promotion, educational reports, probationary and assessment interviews
  • Close cooperation with the AFI practitioners, the staff office, vocational schools and other internal and external bodies
  • Further development of the basic education concept, training tools, modern learning formats and the AFI learning organisation
  • Establishment and development of the learning laboratory, including infrastructure, budget, training and preparation for practical examinations
  • Participation in recruitment, selection and trial apprenticeships in cooperation with the personnel office as well as in the onboarding and introduction programme of new learners
  • Ensuring training quality as well as cantonal, legal and internal requirements
  • Reliable contact person for questions, uncertainties or conflicts in everyday training Your profile:
  • Completed ICT vocational training EFZ, ideally complemented by higher education (FA, HF or equivalent)
  • Several years of professional experience in the IT environment, preferably related to systems technology
  • Experience in accompanying and promoting learners as well as pedagogical-didactic skills in teaching ICT topics
  • Enjoyment in working with young people and a good feeling for different personalities, stages of development and situations
  • High social and communication skills combined with clarity, commitment and sensitivity
  • Structured, reliable and organizationally strong working method, even if several topics run in parallel
  • Interest in modern, practical vocational training and in the further development of learning formats and training tools
  • Willingness to continuously train you in the field of vocational training and ICT What we offer:
  • Design leeway in a versatile role around ICT basic education in the AFI
  • Opportunity to help shape the training of young ICT specialists in the context of the digital transformation of the Canton of Zurich in terms of expertise and methodology
  • Working environment with openness to new ideas, modern forms of learning and practical approaches
  • Reliable employer with flexible working hours, home office opportunities, individual training opportunities and place of work in the city of Zurich
  • classification of the post in wage class 17; the individual wage level depends on the professional experience and the qualifications you have brought Do you feel addressed?
