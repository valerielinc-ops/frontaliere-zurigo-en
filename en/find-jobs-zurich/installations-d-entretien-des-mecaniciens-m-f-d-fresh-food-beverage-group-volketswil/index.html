Full description

We produce favorite products that inspire – in Switzerland and for Switzerland. As a maintenance mechanic, you are an indispensable part of the great team that brings our buildings to life. Do you want to work in the Migros industry and get involved in the latest production facilities? Then go, apply to us!

Mansioni

  • Ensuring the operational readiness of our production facilities
  • Independent troubleshooting of production facilities
  • Performing periodic maintenance work
  • Responsible for preventive maintenance at allocated facilities
  • Evaluating and implementing plant optimization
  • Collaboration in shift operation with alternating 3-shifts incl. Piket
  • Ensuring compliance with applicable guidelines and legal requirements
  • Project cooperation in installations and commissioning operations

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: beneficial in maintenance
  • No training required: mechanic, polymechanic or automatic technician
  • Strength in analytical thinking and systematic troubleshooting approach
  • Experience in dealing with SAP and MS-Office
  • Sympathetic team player
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Employee offers: Employee Points Cumulus, Discount Mobile Subscriptions, Higher Interest Rates Migros Bank, Club School/Fitness contributions and more.
  • Occupational pension provision: Comprehensively secured in the Migros pension fund – with first-class and attractive services.
  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks paid maternity leave (instead of 14 weeks), 4 weeks paternity leave (instead of 2 weeks).
  • Holidays: 5 weeks holiday for all, 6 weeks holiday from 50 years of age, 7 weeks holiday from 60 years of age, additional purchase of additional holiday days possible.
  • Food: Delicious daily menus at reasonable prices.
