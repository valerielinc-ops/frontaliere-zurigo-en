As a renowned personnel services provider focused on specialists and executives in industrial environments, we are currently looking for a Strategic Buyer (m/f) for one of our clients. We are seeking a committed individual who brings a passion for industry and feels comfortable in a dynamic, technology-driven environment.

Our clients are among Switzerland's leading industrial companies. They stand out through their innovative strength, sustainable processes, and stable, future-oriented structures. Become part of a dynamic team that is actively helping shape the future of industry!

  • Cost and value analysis (cost breakdowns, savings programs, TCO considerations, benchmarking)
  • Participation in cross-functional projects (e.g. product development, supply chain optimization, sustainability, digitization)
