Full description

Become part of our HR team and shape the future of Emil Frey Mobility! The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family company with 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessories as well as the provision of financial services. At Emil Frey Mobility, we rely on a trusting and appreciative cooperation. As an HR generalist (100%), you play an important role in making our HR processes efficient and supporting our team with your expertise. Do you value a dynamic working environment and enjoy working with a positive and proactive attitude? Then you are exactly right with us! You can look forward to this:

  • You are the first point of contact for all administrative HR issues and support the team in all processes.
  • You will help with the coordination and implementation of recruitment activities, from tendering to interview preparation with supervisors to contract drafting.
  • You contribute your affinity to numbers and support in the preparation and processing of the monthly wage run.
  • From creating employment reports to other personnel-related documents - you always keep track. This is what you bring with you:
  • You have successfully completed a basic commercial training and already have experience in HR administration. Further training as an HR assistant is desired.
  • Your German is fluent, you speak English very well and have a good knowledge of French so that you can also look after and recruit our staff in Romandie.
  • When dealing with numbers, you feel safe.
  • As an open-minded personality, you enjoy working in a team and with a positive attitude you contribute to our success as a team.
  • You go the extra mile, feel driven by change, and with your excellent administration and multitasking skills, you can dance at different weddings without losing track. Why Emil Frey Mobility AG?
