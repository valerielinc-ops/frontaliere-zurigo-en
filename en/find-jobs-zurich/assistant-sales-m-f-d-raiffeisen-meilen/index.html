Become part of our team!

We are a modern and successful universal bank with a strong regional anchorage on the right lake of Zurich. For our newly created position with a lot of creative leeway, we are looking for a motivated personality with a pronounced affinity for numbers, organizational talent and a sense of connections as an assistant sales assistant (m/f/d).

What awaits you?

What awaits you?

  • Support of sales management in strategic, operational and organizational topics
  • Creation, analysis and interpretation of key figures, reports, forecasts and management information
  • Preparation of decision-making bases, reports and the coordination of sales-relevant topics
  • Support in planning, implementing and measuring the success of sales and marketing campaigns as well as carrying out target group and potential analyses
  • Assistance in the design and implementation of training courses as well as ensuring addressee-oriented sales communication
  • Implementation of key controls and support for compliance, risk and quality assurance issues in the sales environment
  • Administrative and organisational support of the sales unit and ensuring efficient cooperation with internal and external stakeholders

What do you bring?

What do you bring?

  • You have a banking education
  • Several years of professional experience in an analytical, organizational or sales-related function, preferably in the financial services environment
  • Strong affinity for numbers as well as pleasure in the analysis and preparation of data
  • Very good knowledge of Excel
  • Teamwork, strong communication skills and enjoyment of working with different stakeholders
  • Networked thinking and an independent, structured and solution-oriented working style
  • High initiative, flexibility and quality awareness
