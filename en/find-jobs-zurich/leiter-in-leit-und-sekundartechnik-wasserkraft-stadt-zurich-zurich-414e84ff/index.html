Full description

In this key position, as part of the management team, you strategically develop the control and secondary technology and create the basis for a highly available, secure and sustainable “Digital Power Plant”.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for the holistic development and standardization of control and secondary technology across all our power plant groups (Mittelbünden, Bergell and Limmat).
  • Together with the local teams, which are led directly by the local operations managers, you will ensure a highly available, economical and cyber-secure (OT-Security) control infrastructure according to current standards for critical infrastructures.
  • As the highest technical authority for control and secondary technology issues within ewz Hydro, you act as a screenwriter and provide technological guidelines.
  • You are part of the Hydro management team and actively participate as SPOC Hydropower in the further development of technical products, platforms and requirements within ewz.
  • You control complex, conceptual projects in the field of control and secondary technology independently and accompany the regional teams in the technical implementation on site.

Profile

  • You have in-depth technical training (HF/FH/ETH) in systems engineering, process control technology, electrical engineering, computer science or a related discipline as well as several years of experience in project management of complex control and secondary technology projects in the field of critical infrastructures.
  • With your eye for the overall system, you develop complex technological roadmaps and translate them into pragmatic, operational maintenance and renewal concepts.
  • You have a high degree of assertiveness coupled with diplomatic skill. You can easily build bridges between classic power plant technology on site and the modern IT world.
  • Your main place of work is our technical heart in Sils im Domleschg. For coordination with our partner areas and the operating groups, you bring a regular willingness to travel (especially to Zurich-Oerlikon and to the operating groups).

We offer

  • More energy for your life: With mobile work, flexible working models and part-time opportunities, you can actively shape your working time and divide it into the best fit for your life. For example, you can buy up to 2 weeks of extra holidays and work part-time at all levels. Job sharing/splitting and unpaid holidays are also possible with us.
  • Sustainable and cheaper travel: With the mobility contribution of up to 600 francs a year, you travel cheaply and sustainably – whether you want to cycle through Zurich or explore Switzerland by train.
  • An investment in your future: We not only value paying fair and transparent wages, but also ensure that you have more of your wages left over. We take over 60% of the savings contributions and additionally offer a super-compulsory interest on the capital accumulated.
  • An energy boost for your free time: We pay you 100 francs a month on your lunch-check card. In addition, you benefit annually from further Fringe Benefits worth 150 francs and attractive mobile subscriptions.
  • Development and training: With our talent management, we promote your development in a targeted manner – through a wide range of internal and external training offers as well as exciting development opportunities.
  • Meaningful work: Sustainability and meaningful work are not just words for us: shape Zurich and the future for us. Together with us, release energy for a good morning.
