Full description

As a result of a maternity in the HR Service Center, we are looking for our Corporate Service Center in Dübendorf as of 1. January 2027 or by agreement a dynamic, communicative and dutiful personality as

Personnel/in HR Administration 80 - 100% Temporary employment until 31. August 2027 with option of extension

Their tasks

  • Independent handling of personnel administration (entry, mutations, exits) for approximately 450 – 500 employees
  • Preparation of employment contracts, contract changes and processing of withdrawals
  • Taking over various payroll tasks (holiday reduction, payment of holidays and overtime, registration of family allowances, etc.)
  • Contact person for the line and employees around the personnel administration
  • Check and process time and absence management (polypoint)
  • Deputy teammates
  • Contact with social insurance concerning family allowances, EO/MSE and BVG

Your profile

  • Basic commercial training EFZ
  • Ideally, specific HR training (e.g. HR assistant or HR certificate)
  • Professional experience in personnel administration
  • Good HR system user knowledge (SuccessFactors (SAP), Polypoint and UKA advantageous)
  • Independent and exact working method, as well as discreet personality
  • A heartfelt, humorous and service-oriented basic attitude
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing, French is advantageous
