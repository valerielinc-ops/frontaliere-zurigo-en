Policy Dialogue Manager AI & Digital Governance (m/f/d)

80%-100%, Zurich, temporary

Printed Printing

The rapid development of artificial intelligence and digital technologies opens up new opportunities for business and society, but at the same time raises important questions about security, regulation, democracy, the labour market, education and innovation.

As Policy Dialogue Manager AI & Digitalization, you will shape the exchange between leading researchers at ETH Zurich and decision-makers from politics, administration, civil society and business. They identify relevant policy issues, support researchers in addressing these issues, establish and maintain networks and develop formats that contribute scientific findings to policy-making processes.

Project background

The Albert Einstein School of Public Policy at ETH Zurich was founded in autumn 2025. It is an interdisciplinary centre that combines public policy with science and technology to develop solutions to the great societal challenges of our time.

Through research, teaching and dialogue, we strengthen evidence-based policymaking and promote exchange between science, politics, administration, civil society and business.

The Einstein School comprises around 80 professors and group leaders from ETH Zurich and the ETH Domain.

Thematically, the Einstein School focuses on the following topics:

  • AI and digitalisation
  • Environmental, energy and food systems
  • Planning and Built Environment
  • Öpublic health
  • Economy and innovation
  • Peace, conflict and security

Job description

You will identify relevant developments and policy issues in the field of artificial intelligence and digital transformation and governance at an early stage, and follow developments in research on AI and technology governance. They observe national and international policy developments in the field of AI and digitalization and identify fields of action for the Einstein School.

  • They independently design and organize dialogue formats such as roundtables, workshops, conferences and background discussions with the relevant members of the Einstein School.
  • They build up a network of actors in the federal administration, parliament, cantons, international organizations, civil society and business and actively maintain it.
  • You maintain a network with ETH-internal actors, for example other centers in your field.
  • They advise and support researchers at ETH Zurich in activities at the interface of science and politics.
  • They design new teaching formats and support members of the Einstein School in applied research projects.
  • They write briefings, background analyses and communication materials for different target groups.
  • They take overarching activities in the area of policy dialogue was. In particular, they define and implement best practices in the area of policy dialogue, in close cooperation with other members of the Policy Dialogue team.
  • They represent Einstein School vis-à-vis external stakeholders and are responsible for the strategic positioning of the school in the field of AI and digitalization.
  • They support the managing director of the Einstein School in the strategy development and implementation of the Einstein School, as well as in individual management tasks.

This position is limited to three years. An extension is sought.
