Full description

Do you like to work with people? You would like to work with the Alter Käferberg Health Centre as of November 1, 2026 or by arrangement with our nursing team?

Tasks

  • They lead the department of acute care and transitional care in a targeted manner and encourage employees to follow professional, social and economic principles.
  • You take responsibility for the holistic care and care of our patients
  • They are responsible for quality development and ensure compliance with hygiene, care and training requirements.
  • They actively participate in the care and support the team.

Profile

  • A strong ability to think holistically and connectedly
  • Management training or willingness to take such a course, as well as multi-year practical and management experience, preferably in acute, long-term care and geriatrics
  • A training for nursing specialist (HF/FH/Danii) with SRK recognition for diplomas from abroad
  • Loadability, flexibility, a pinch of humour and a high level of social competence
  • Good communication skills, written and oral

We offer

  • Look forward to an exciting and varied area of responsibility in a professional and team-oriented working environment with a Du culture throughout the company.
  • The human being is at the center of our work. That is why we support the reconciliation of private and professional life.
  • As part of the city of Zurich and due to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, advanced employment conditions and excellent social benefits.
  • The wages of the nursing and therapeutic professions were raised to strengthen these occupational groups. Depending on experience, the annual salary for this function ranges from Fr. 93,838.– and Fr. 125,386.– (100%).

About us

The health centres for the age of the city of Zurich are one of the largest Geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive range from a single source.

The health centre for Alter Käferberg in Wipkingen in district 10 combines various care and care services under one roof and is characterized by many years of experience in the field of old age medicine. Thanks to the unique location on Käferberg, the residents, patients and guests enjoy a wonderful view of Lake Zurich, the Alps and the rooftops of the city. Here you work together with 400 colleagues.
