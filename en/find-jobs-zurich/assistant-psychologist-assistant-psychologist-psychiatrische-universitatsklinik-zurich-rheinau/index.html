Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility.

For our Center for Inpatient Forensic Therapy in Rheinau, we are looking for a

Assistant psychologist / Assistant psychologist

80-100%

Their tasks

  • Case-leading responsible for the implementation of court-ordered, stationary measures
  • Professional tort prevention, forensic treatments in individual and group setting
  • Legal prognostic assessments (risk assessment) in cooperation with the pluridisciplinary team
  • Psychodiagnostic examinations
  • Technical and timely reporting, documentation and correspondence
  • Conducting group therapies
  • Completed psychology degree with focus on psychopathology of adulthood (master or equivalent)
  • At least 1 year of professional experience from master’s degree, clinical-forensic experience beneficial
  • Ongoing supplementary psychotherapeutic training
  • Team-oriented, interprofessional action and good communication skills
  • Analytical thinking and empathy and assertiveness
  • Interest in forensic issues and modern forensic treatment principles
  • Development with perspective: Diverse career paths in one of the largest psychiatric clinics in Switzerland
  • Clinic, research and teaching united: working in a university environment with international networking
  • Together for people: Interdisciplinary cooperation for holistic psychiatric care
  • Strong in the team: appreciative cooperation and mutual support
  • More time for relaxation: at least 25 days of holiday and possibility for unpaid leave
  • Future provision with added value: 60% employer contribution to the pension fund
