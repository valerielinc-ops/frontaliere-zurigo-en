Full description

The Department of Process Engineering and Plant Projects is responsible for the planning, project planning, execution and commissioning of water supply facilities. In order to strengthen our engineering project planning team in plant engineering, we are looking for agreement reinforcement. Are you our newcomer?

Tasks

Planning and construction

  • You take over the CAD-based planning and construction of plants and pipelines and develop concepts to meet new or changed operational needs.
  • You technically and organizationally secure the interfaces and the functional planning of your constructions in the existing infrastructure and update the documentation of changed plants.
  • You support the project managers with your technical know-how in constructive tasks, create drawings, R&I flow pictures and develop our CAD standards further.

Call for tender and implementation

  • You work in cross-departmental projects and realize larger plant construction projects (e.g. new plants / fountains / shafts incl.) Building and civil engineering, electrical, HVAC.
  • You create cost estimates, specifications and tenders, get offers, compare and check them and hold meetings with providers. In addition, you accompany the executing companies, ensure the execution quality, handle the ordering and control project invoices.

Profile

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as constructor/in EFZ with further training e.g. as technician*in HF with a focus on construction technology
  • Several years of professional experience in the engineering of process engineering plant and pipeline construction with CAD and BIM; ideally from the areas of water supply, wastewater, etc.
  • In-depth experience in project management with excellent communication skills
  • Initiative, structured and problem- and service-oriented working method
  • Driving licence Cat. B

We offer

An exciting and varied area of responsibility awaits you in a collegial, agile working environment. About us

The Zurich water supply supplies the city and numerous municipalities with fresh, clean drinking water around the clock. We fulfill this task in the service of the public with around 300 dedicated employees.

Züriwasser. Anytime. For another 150 years. That is our vision.
