Full description

For the festival edition 2028, we are looking for an artistically profiled, leadership-strong and internationally networked personality who shapes the festival and develops it together with an experienced team. The Zurich Theater Spectacle is an internationally renowned festival of contemporary performing arts and a special meeting place in Zurich. Since 1980 it takes place in August on the Landiwiese on Lake Zurich. The festival management bears the artistic and strategic overall responsibility for the festival with an annual turnover of around CHF 6 million and shapes its next phase of development and transformation. Further information can be found on the website "Future for the Zurich Theater Spectacle" of the city of Zurich.

Tasks

  • They carry the overall artistic and strategic responsibility, develop an independent vision and position the festival in the local, national and international context.
  • They are responsible for the programme and artistic orientation, lead the programme group, conduct research at home and abroad and maintain a broad network of artists and partner institutions.
  • They develop the festival and the Landiwiese as a place for innovative, accessible and surprising artistic formats and encounters and shape the transformation process within the legal, financial and administrative framework of the city of Zurich.
  • Together with the administrative and technical management, you form the management. As festival manager, you will lead the administrative and technical management as well as the festival organization with around eight full-time positions throughout the year and around 160 temporary employees during the festival.
  • They combine artistic ambition with organisational and economic sustainability and represent the theatre spectacle towards artists, the public, partner institutions, politics and administration.

Profile

  • Several years of curatorial work and management experience with a proven track record in a festival or theatre (including university degree at master level)
  • In-depth knowledge of contemporary performing arts, a broad international and intercontinental network as well as experience with challenging programs, formats and collaborations with a focus on encounter and participation
  • Strong understanding of the Zurich and Swiss cultural scene, its actors and cultural policy framework as well as interest in local and national creativity
  • An open, initiative and visionary personality with an independent artistic attitude, leadership and sense of what is possible
  • Experience in strategic leadership of complex cultural organizations and transformation processes
  • Ability to bring diverse disciplines, teams and interests together and make sustainable decisions
  • Good understanding of financial, organisational and political contexts as well as sensitivity to social developments, participation, diversity and sustainability
  • Good knowledge of German and English
  • Other languages are beneficial. International travel and time flexibility, especially during the festival, are part of the function.

We offer

A versatile management task with artistic creative leeway and the opportunity to decisively shape an internationally renowned festival in its next development phase. the post is limited to four years; an extension for a further four years is possible.

About us

The Zurich Theater Spectacle has been bringing contemporary performing arts from different regions of the world to Zurich for decades. The Landiwiese is much more than a festival ground: it combines international art with an open meeting place for a diverse audience. Artistic quality, international perspectives, encounter and the special atmosphere of the place characterize the festival and form the basis for its further development. The Zurich Theater Spectacle is part of the cultural promotion of the city of Zurich. Equality, diversity and inclusion are important to us.
