Physio Schwerzenbach GmbH is located directly at the station Schwerzenbach (ZH), (6min from Uster and 11min from Zurich). Despite our central location, it is pleasantly quiet in practice. Among other things, we have a well-equipped training room, spacious treatment rooms and a quiet office for breaks and administration time. Our clientele is very mixed and usually decent and friendly. Our team consists of staffed physiotherapists and independent therapists who are available for rent in the premises of Physio Schwerzenbach GmbH in the sense of a group practice. All infrastructure of the practice is included in the very fair rent for self-employment. We are looking for someone who appreciates the exchange, but also the independent work. Someone who can be his own boss but is still happy about the benefits of a team (team meetings and events, internal training, holiday representation). Your employer
