Full description

As an HR team, we look after around 500 employees in all personnel matters and are looking for from 1. December 2026 a motivated and committed HR specialist with a focus on time recording and controlling. You keep track of our time recording system, make sure that all data is correctly and efficiently included in the wage run - and get involved in exciting HR topics. The 50% allowance corresponds to 2.5 working days per week. The working days can be flexibly chosen and divided between Monday and Thursday.

Tasks

  • You are the central contact person for the time recording system E3, from data quality to absence management to the monthly and annual accounts.
  • You coordinate adjustments and create reports and analyses in E3, SAP HCM and Excel.
  • You are the interface to our external partner, perform release tests and participate in experience groups.
  • You are responsible for controlling insurance allowances, permits and medical certificates.
  • You ensure the correct handling and payment of expenses.

Profile

  • Commercial education with further training in human resources
  • Several years of experience in HR, ideally with a focus on time management and wages
  • Good knowledge of SAP HCM, M365, E3 beneficial
  • Independent and solution-oriented working method with a high sense of responsibility, commitment and flexibility

We offer

  • A diverse and collegial working environment in an agile organization
  • Flexible working time model and above-average social benefits
  • Possibility for home office
  • Targeted education and training opportunities
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave and 4 weeks for co-parents
  • Various perks (mobility contribution, lunch check, fitness, telephony, etc.)
  • Annual health contribution to promote your well-being
  • Accessible jobs

About us

As a competence center for digitalization and IT in the city of Zurich, the Organization and Computer Science (OIZ) provides high-quality services for its customers.
