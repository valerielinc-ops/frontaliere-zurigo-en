Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With over 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric services offered by Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in outpatient, day clinic and inpatient settings. The group employs around 1800 people and generated sales of CHF 240 million in 2025.

Have you completed your training in somatics and wanted to get to know a new field? Or did you work in nursing in the past and would like to return to this valuable profession? Then you will find exactly the right offer with us and we look forward to getting to know you.

Your tasks The work in psychiatry is versatile and varied and differs depending on the ward and treatment focus. What you can certainly expect, however, is

  • the individual care and support of the patients and the contact with the relatives within the framework of the caregiver work and in consultation with the core team
  • Leading one-on-one meetings, group offers and reports
  • Consultation and planning of treatment with the responsible doctors and therapists

We will be happy to provide you with information about the different areas, with their wards and treatment focuses. With 17 stations at our location in Oetwil am See, there is sure to be the right station for you.
