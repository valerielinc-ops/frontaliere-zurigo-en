Full description

Founded in 1836, the Swiss family business is today one of the most renowned confiseries in Europe with its exquisite range. Sprüngli products stand for the best quality, unique freshness and naturalness. The completed creations bring customers from all over the world to swarm daily.

Apprenticeship: retail specialist EFZ, Zurich, Basel and Wallisellen for 2027

100%,

Working at Sprüngli

Retail professionals know about the products they sell. They are sociable people and respond with interest and patience to the wishes of the customer and keep calm, even if it gets hectic. Basic training in the retail trade focuses on activity in the sales business. Aspiring retail professionals learn to advise customers professionally. This means that they know the range, provide information and can advise customers. A special variety offers individual customer orders, such as a special birthday cake or a rich aperitif. The training lasts 3 years. The additional training for the DHF for retail assistants lasts 2 years. Next possible start of teaching: August 2026.

What you do with us

Retail professionals have a creative flair and pack gift items skillfully and imaginatively. You collect the amount and serve the cashier. As sales professionals, they offer the customer a solution to a complaint and present the freshly produced products tastefully and invitingly, so that the customer is encouraged to buy.
