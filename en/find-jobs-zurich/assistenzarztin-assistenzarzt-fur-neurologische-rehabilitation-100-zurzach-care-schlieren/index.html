Full description

Our rehabilitation clinic Limmattal offers specialized musculoskeletal and neurological rehabilitation including surveillance rehabilitation and accident rehabilitation at a high medical level. Through targeted diagnostics and individually tailored therapy programs, our interdisciplinary team ensures that our patients are optimally looked after on their way back to better health and quality of life. Due to the close cooperation with the Hospital Limmattal and the direct connection to the medical emergency unit, prompt acute somatic treatment is always ensured even in the event of emergency complications.

Your tasks

  • Care: Caring for our inpatients in cooperation with the other responsible doctors
  • Rehabilitation: Planning of the rehabilitation steps in an interdisciplinary team with nurses and therapists
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: Through close cooperation with various disciplines, you contribute significantly to holistic treatment and rehabilitation
  • Contact person: Responsible contact person for patients and their relatives with medical questions during their inpatient stay

Your profile

  • Qualifications: Federal or MEBEKO Recognized State Exam
  • P sociability: You bring professional and social competence for a good interdisciplinary cooperation and work structured and independently

  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, car dealers, pharmacies and our membership at Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other benefits at the FORTYSEVEN spa.
