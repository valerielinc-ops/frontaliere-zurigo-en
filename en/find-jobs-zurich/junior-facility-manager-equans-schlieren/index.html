Full description

We are looking for an independent and service-oriented personality in the Zurich region by agreement, who will become part of our Dynamic programme.

You can move that with us.

You manage several objects on site and are the contact person: in for our customers. Your responsibilities include the following activities:

  • Project support for mandate management and own project responsibility
  • Professional management of employees
  • Optimization of processes and development of processes
  • Coordination of various services
  • Direct customer contact with various clients (management, suppliers, etc.)
  • Cooperation in technical and commercial facility management
  • Assistance tasks and administrative activities

That's what makes you.

You describe yourself as a service provider: in the innermost conviction. Positive feedback on your performance motivates you and spurs you on to further top performances. You also have the following requirements:

  • Bachelor of Science in Facility Management
  • Experience in Facility Management is an advantage
  • General interest and affinity for real estate
  • Enjoyment of working in a team
  • Engaged, communicative and flexible personality
  • Strong customer orientation and responsibility
  • Number flair

That's what we offer you.

During the Dynamic Program, you will be accompanied by a mentor, receive on-the-job training and take part in internal and external training to expand your professional know-how. You will also become part of the Junior Facility Manager community to exchange ideas with other Junior Facility Managers during various events and get to know other Equans units. Equans Switzerland Facility Management AG. Shaping the future together.

We specialize in holistic facilities management services for every phase of the business real estate and area lifecycle. Buildings and infrastructures are complex systems. Whether technical operation, strategic planning or infrastructural services – we ensure maximum availability, increase operational reliability and improve comfort in buildings. In this way, we minimize costs and create optimal conditions so that our customers can concentrate on their core business. As a company of Equans Switzerland, the leading full-service provider for holistic building and energy solutions in Switzerland, we are also driving the transformation and digitalization of our customers and working together on the energy transition.
