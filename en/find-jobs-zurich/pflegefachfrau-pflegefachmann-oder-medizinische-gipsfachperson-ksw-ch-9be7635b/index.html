Full description

For the plaster room in the emergency center, we are looking for a motivated nurse / medical plaster specialist to support the team. In the plaster room of the emergency center, adult patients and children and adolescents of traumatological, orthopaedic and hand-surgical disciplines are treated with plaster applications.

Tasks

  • Implementation of medical gypsum applications of the upper and lower extremities and extension therapies in inpatient and outpatient patients according to medical prescription
  • Advice and education of patients and their relatives regarding plaster care, handling of plaster and possible complications
  • Organization and coordination of the applicable orders in the plaster room and operating room in cooperation with doctors, doctors, secretariats and the nurses of the departments in the day program
  • Supporting the care team of the emergency centre in carrying out gypsum applications
  • Participation in the supervision of trainees in the plasterroom
  • Documentation of ongoing therapy and billing of the services provided in the system

Profile

  • You have completed vocational training as an expert / expert emergency care NDS HF or as a nurse HF/FH and already have experience in the field of medical plaster applications
  • You bring several years of experience in the acute hospital
  • Good working environment and cooperation in a small, established team of medical plaster specialists is important to you
  • If desired, there is the possibility of additional employment in the part-time work as an expert / expert emergency care on the emergency department for adults
