Specialist: in real estate transactions investment properties 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Specialist: in real estate transactions for investment properties Zurich Altstetten / Work Smart, October 1st, 2026 or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. The Real Estate Investment Properties department assumes comprehensive responsibility for all of SBB Immobilien's properties. With us you will work in a well-rehearsed team and actively contribute to the further development of SBB's attractive real estate portfolio. In a professional environment you will find a lot of creative freedom as well as modern work equipment and development opportunities. In your daily collaboration, you will network closely with internal departments and external partners. You will further expand your expertise at the interface between property and project valuation, property law and the conclusion of real estate transactions and benefit from the professional exchange within the team. You can move that.

  • In this demanding role, you will evaluate properties and development projects for your own investments and building rights and provide a viable decision-making basis for investments, negotiations and committees.
  • You structure and support property law transactions with a focus on purchase contracts, building rights contracts and easement contracts and conduct the relevant negotiations independently until the contract is concluded.
  • In development projects, you advise project teams on property law issues and coordinate collaboration with internal departments and external partners.
  • You prepare tenders for building rights and land sales, carry them out and evaluate the incoming offers.
  • You maintain all business cases in the designated IT systems and ensure data quality, traceability and adherence to deadlines. This is what you bring with you.
  • You enjoy working in a team and communicating appropriately with internal and external contacts.
  • Your structured and analytical way of working helps you to process complex issues in a solution-oriented manner.
  • Several years of experience in evaluating properties and projects and in dealing with building rights as well as training or further education in real estate, construction, law or economics. Knowledge of value added tax is an advantage.
  • Very good knowledge of property law transactions including purchase contracts, building law contracts and easement contracts.
  • Experience in conducting and evaluating tenders and in working with external service providers.
  • Confident in dealing with IT applications and maintaining business transactions in the corresponding systems. Very good knowledge of German at least C1 and ideally French or Italian at least A2.
