Full description

With your expertise, secure the legally compliant and safe handling of hazardous substances at ewz and actively shape the further development of hazardous material management.

Tasks

  • You are actively developing the hazardous material management and the safety culture of ewz.
  • You are a consultant in the areas of occupational safety, health protection and occupational safety.
  • You carry out risk analyses, risk investigations and accident investigations and perform improvements.
  • You are constantly developing the emergency organisation and taking over the technical management of the ewz emergency team.
  • Training and audits are part of your task as well as the further development of the LMS (Learning Management System), the training processes and the related documentation.

Profile

  • You have a technical or scientific degree on a university degree, university degree or university degree.
  • You are a specialist in ASGS, safety engineer, ASA specialist in industrial hygiene or you are also bringing a DAS/MAS Work+Health with you and want to develop further in the field of ASGS.
  • You have experience in ASGS, occupational hygiene, hazardous material management or technical safety.
  • You analyze legal requirements and regulatory requirements safely and implement them in operational processes in a practical manner.
  • A high self-initiative as well as an independent, structured and reliable working method round off your profile.
