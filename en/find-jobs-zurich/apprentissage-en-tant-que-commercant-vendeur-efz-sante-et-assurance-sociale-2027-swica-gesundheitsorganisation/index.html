Full description

Goodbye school, hello professional world. Or even better: Hello SWICA! We like to train young people like you for life. That is why we really do our best to make it easy for you to start your career. From the introductory week to the final exam: we pack it together! What you learn and do with us

  • You take care of all customer requests (at the counter, by phone or by mail)
  • You know which services are covered by the insurance
  • You can see the connections between social security
  • You will learn about the different laws and regulations
  • You manage customer dossiers and maintain the data

What you bring

  • Good performance in all major subjects (D/F/E/M) at the highest elementary school level or at the 10. School year (grades 4.5 or higher)
  • In the Multicheck Economy and Administration you reach at least 50 points
  • You are interested in insurance and health care
  • You are reliable, friendly and communicative and enjoy contact with people
  • You have a safe and well-groomed appearance and pleasant manners
  • You have talent in dealing with numbers and have a quick understanding

Why SWICA

  • Start your professional life optimally: In the introductory week, get to know the whole SWICA and your fellow learners.
  • A well thought-out training program with lots of variety and insights into different departments and areas.
  • Vocational trainers who accompany you competently and also support you with advice and deeds.
  • Internal industry customer, so that you can make the step from theory to practice early on and "work properly".
  • Interesting opportunities for the time after your successful teaching degree – foreign language stay included.
  • A strong team spirit among the approximately 60 learners (KV and IT) and throughout SWICA – you are with everyone from day one.

Danielle Grimm, Responsible Department of Vocational Education and Training, is happy to provide you with information.
