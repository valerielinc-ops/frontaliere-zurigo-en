Full description

Are you a strategic thinker with exceptional content production skills? Join our Marketing Services & Operations EMEA team on a 1-year temporary contract and become the force behind sales enablement that drives business success! In this role, you'll blend sales enablement expertise with operational excellence to produce high-quality assets that enhance client engagement. About the Role As our Sales Enablement Content Producer & Manager, you'll be the vital link between enablement strategy and execution. You'll transform complex information into compelling, brand-aligned content that equips our sales teams with the tools they need to succeed in the competitive re/insurance landscape. Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and design market-specific sales enablement strategies aligned with marketing objectives to drive targeted client engagement in coordination with marketing managers and business stakeholders.
  • Produce high-quality content across various formats (slides, PDFs, visuals, infographics) based on structured briefs
  • Format and finalize content using brand-approved templates and layout standards
  • Maintain and evolve branded templates ensuring proper structure, tagging, and accessibility
  • Track production progress and ensure timely delivery of assets meeting quality specifications
  • Coordinate with internal teams and external vendors to manage production timelines
  • Support intake workflows and contribute to improving content delivery processes
  • Maintain understanding of Business Unit services and marketing priorities for contextually aligned content
  • Identify opportunities for content reuse and support quality control across assets
  • Manage structure and version control of marketing and sales enablement asset library
  • Monitor adoption and effectiveness of enablement tools, gathering feedback to continuously refine practices

About the Team You'll be joining the EMEA Marketing Services & Operations department within the Branding and Marketing Division. Our team is responsible for delivering marketing strategies and plans that support business growth, with a focus on events, digital marketing and impactful content that enables Swiss Re to communicate our value proposition to clients.

About You

You're a detail-oriented marketing professional who thrives in collaborative environments. You have a keen eye for copywriting, design and formatting while understanding the strategic importance of sales enablement in driving business results. You're comfortable managing multiple requests simultaneously and delivering high-quality assets under pressure.

We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements

  • Bachelor's degree (or equivalent) in Marketing, Communications, or related field
  • 3+ years of experience in B2B marketing content production or sales enablement, preferably within re/insurance/finance industry
  • Strong knowledge of content marketing principles and asset lifecycle management
  • Experience in sales enablement function
  • Proficiency in formatting, visual layout, and working with templates and brand guidelines
  • Experience working with internal marketing teams and external content vendors
  • Excellent written and oral English

These are additional nice to haves

  • Familiarity with content asset libraries and sales enablement platforms such as Seismic
  • Understanding of best practices for tagging, storage, and taxonomy
  • High attention to detail and consistency in formatting and file organization
  • Collaborative, delivery-focused team player who thrives in a matrixed environment
  • Additional European languages such as German, French, Spanish, Italian or other
  • Global experience in marketing or content production
