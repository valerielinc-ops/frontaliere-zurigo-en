Full description

To strengthen our team at the Sihlstrasse site in Zurich, we are looking for a qualified and efficient team by immediately or by agreement

With its 35 locations and approximately 500 employees, Vista Eye Practices & Clinics are the largest provider of eye medicine in Switzerland.

  • We offer first-class medical services for our patients and set standards in the fields of ophthalmology, ophthalmology and eye laser medicine.

Zurich / ZH location

Tasks

  • You welcome and receive our patients
  • You take care of our patients before, during and after the medical consultation
  • Support for Kleinchi's surgical procedures
  • You carry out independent preliminary examinations such as autorefractometers, facial fields, biometry, corneal topography, endothelial cell measurement, slit lamp photography and subjective refractions for un-s medical team
  • You organize the practice together with your colleagues

Requirements

  • A complete training as an eye optician with several years of professional experience and training in subjective refraction is required
  • flexibility, organizational talent, resilience and team ability
  • The willingness to perform cross-site missions (Region Zurich and Pfäffikon SZ)
  • A structured working method and a well-versed use of the MS-Office programmes
  • Very good German knowledge
  • other national and foreign languages are of benefit

We offer

  • A very good infrastructure and a modern working environment in the city center
  • Individual and structured introduction
  • A corporate culture in which ideas and suggestions are welcome
  • The possibility to participate regularly in training courses
  • A place of work with very good public transport connection
