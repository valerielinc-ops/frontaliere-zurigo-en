Full description

Personal – competent – close Welcome to the Hospital Uster, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As the central pillar of health care in the Oberland region of Zurich and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you experience the highest level of expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and promoted in your professional development. Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of a “regional and digital health hub in Züri East”! Our wound treatment centre focuses on the care of people with acute and chronic wounds. Our focus is on treating severely healing, infected and complex wounds as well as the associated pain, symptoms and complications. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed personality as of 01.01.2027 or by agreement

Exciting challenges await you

  • professional and interprofessional wound treatment of our ambulatory and inpatient patients with acute and chronic wounds
  • Treatment of inpatient stomapatients
  • independent organisation and design of the work area
  • Support for the further development of standards, processes and quality processes
  • Professional advice and support of employees, patients and relatives
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with doctors, nurses, Spitex, family doctors and other disciplines

What sets you apart for the job

  • Dipl. Nursing professional HF/FH
  • Completed further training as a certified wound expert / expert SAfW or comparable training
  • Experience in the care of complex and chronic wounds
  • Knowledge of stoma counselling is advantageous or the willingness to complete a corresponding further training
  • independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Strong communication and team skills

7 Reasons to Work with Us

  • interesting and varied activity in a committed and motivated team
  • Structured training in the care and treatment of our patients
  • regulated working hours Monday-Friday
  • Family atmosphere
  • Excellent staff restaurant and numerous discounts
  • Central location near Uster station and motorway connection
  • ZVV BonusPass or cheap parking
