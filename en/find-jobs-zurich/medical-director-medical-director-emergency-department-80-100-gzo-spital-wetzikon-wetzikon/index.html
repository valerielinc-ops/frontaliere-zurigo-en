Company and context

  • They manage the interdisciplinary emergency unit professionally, organizationally and administratively and develop it as an independent functional unit.
  • They triage and treat outpatient and inpatient emergency patients and involve internal and external specialists.
  • They lead the cadre doctors of the Department of Medicine and Surgery who work in the emergency department.
  • They integrate the various disciplines into emergency operations and continuously develop the interdisciplinary GZO emergency standards.
  • They are shaping the continuing education and training programme for medical and non-medical staff and are further expanding the emergency department as a recognised training centre SGNOR.
  • You are responsible for quality assurance and process optimization at the emergency station.
  • You are the contact person for the emergency practice at the GZO operated by family doctors and take up a seat on their joint works commission.
  • They represent the emergency medicine of the GZO to emergency services, assignees and partner hospitals.
  • They bear the cost centre and budget responsibility for the emergency department and, as a member of the upper cadre, participate in the further development of the GZO.
  • The participation in the medical services, analogous to the entire cadre of the Department of Medicine is expected.
