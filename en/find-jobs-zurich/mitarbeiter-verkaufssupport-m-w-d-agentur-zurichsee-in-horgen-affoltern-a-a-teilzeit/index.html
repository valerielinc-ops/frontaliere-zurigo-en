Full description

Customer Advice – Sales Pensum: 50 - 100% Place of work: Level of experience: With professional experience FlexOffice: on site Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, this means more than simply working through tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high demands and have the courage to go new ways. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand and your ideas, initiative and contribution count. So you have the opportunity to really make a difference for our customers, your team and your personal development.

What you do

  • You actively support the sales force throughout the sales process and thus contribute to joint success.
  • As a central contact person, you will answer professional and technical questions in the areas of non-life private customers, non-life corporate customers, individual lives, accident, sickness and BVG.
  • You support our private and corporate customers by telephone, in writing and personally with great competence and commitment.
  • With a sense of individual needs, you develop customized concepts and solutions.
  • Together with the team, you actively participate in market development and sales promotion.
  • You accompany our learners in everyday work and promote their professional and personal development.

What you bring

  • Commercial insurance training and/or several years of experience with an all-industry insurer.
  • You already bring the VBV certificate (Allbranchen) or have the willingness to achieve it.
  • Enjoying direct customer contact and a pronounced service orientation.
  • Sales-oriented thinking and enthusiasm for the development of individual customer solutions.
  • Efficient, solution-oriented cooperation with the field service and relevant specialist departments.
  • Willingness to work in two places (Horgen and Affoltern a.A.).
