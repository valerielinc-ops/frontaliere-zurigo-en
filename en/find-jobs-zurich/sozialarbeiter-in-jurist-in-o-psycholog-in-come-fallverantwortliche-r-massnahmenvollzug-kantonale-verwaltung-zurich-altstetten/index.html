Are you looking for a varied and meaningful activity in which no day is like the other? You like to face new challenges and keep a cool head even in demanding situations? Then you are exactly right with us! For the department Implementation of Measures 1 - with a focus on outpatient and inpatient therapeutic measures for delinquent persons with mental disorders - we are looking for 1. February 2027 a socially competent, assertive, resilient personality. You do that with us.

  • With the aim of minimizing recidivism, you are responsible for the organization and implementation of measures and manage the cases independently, using risk-oriented sanctions (ROS).
  • You advise and empower your clients and also refer them to suitable clinics, law enforcement institutions or therapists.
  • In close cooperation with specialists, you set individual treatment goals and continuously check the course of therapy and the achievement of goals.
  • You issue orders, monitor instructions, make decisions about easing of executions and conditional dismissals and coordinate the dismissal preparations in cooperation with specialist partners.
  • You regularly report and submit applications to courts and other relevant working partners and represent applications in subsequent proceedings in court.
  • In addition, you control activity, contact and rayon bans and triage clients in need of support to suitable specialist positions.
  • In your role, you actively contribute to the further development and design of the department by participating in committees and projects. You bring that with you
  • You have a completed degree in social work, law or psychology as well as several years of professional experience. Knowledge of administrative law, forensics and/or case management is an advantage.
  • You master the German language in word and writing at a high level and are distinguished by excellent consulting and discussion skills.
  • Your interest is in forensic psychiatry and criminology as well as working with mentally ill offenders. Interdisciplinary cooperation and coordination with different partners brings you pleasure. You always act efficiently, precisely and structured.
