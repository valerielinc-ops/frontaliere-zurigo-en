Full description

Technology is your world. You like to make components from steel, chromium steel or plastics and assemble them into devices, apparatus and machines.

Tasks

  • You manufacture parts for devices, instruments and machines.
  • You use different machines, wait and repair them.
  • You master techniques such as drilling, milling or grinding.
  • You monitor the production process and check the manufactured parts.
  • You work according to production documents and technical drawings.

Profile

  • Compulsory schooling completed
  • Practical skill and technical understanding
  • Spatial imagination
  • Precise operation
  • Joy at work by hand and with machines

We offer

You can expect a well-founded and varied education. Your apprentices support you on your way to a successful teaching degree. You benefit from attractive employment conditions.

About us

In your 3-year apprenticeship at VBZ, you will get to know the production of workpieces, the various production processes, the construction of devices, apparatuses and machines. You will manufacture and process components made of metal and plastic. You will also work on our vehicles in cooperation with other professionals, solving orders and then executing them. If you are committed and responsible and are interested in public transport, you are in the right place with us. So look in and who knows, maybe public transport will soon bring you professionally to your destination. It is worth getting in!
