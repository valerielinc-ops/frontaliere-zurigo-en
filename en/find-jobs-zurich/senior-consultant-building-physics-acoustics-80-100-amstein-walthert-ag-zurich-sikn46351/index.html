Full description

Amstein + Walthert is an engineering company with around 1100 employees:innen. As engineers, planners and consultants, we work on all aspects of the technology of civil engineering and construction over the entire life cycle. Our solutions are innovative, elegant and sustainable. These are your tasks

  • Consulting, project management and project processing as well as independent acquisition
  • Advising integral project teams on the implementation of building physics and/or noise and acoustic topics
  • Independent implementation of structural physics and/or structural and spatial acoustic as well as noise measurements using the current standards
  • Consulting and support of the building owner and architect: in competitions in the fields of building physics and energy as well as noise and acoustics
  • Completed technical/university studies in building physics, architecture, civil engineering, environmental and natural sciences or related to the construction industry
  • Experience on construction (craftsmen) is advantageous
  • Several years of experience (professional and project management)
  • Experience in construction processes and their processes
  • Good network in the Swiss construction industry
  • Sound project experience in the implementation of large and medium-sized projects
  • Experience in solution-oriented and conceptual work – independently and within teams
  • Experience in dealing with common software tools
  • Empathy and a good feeling for people
  • Recognizing the demands and needs of customers: inside
  • Creativity, commitment, adaptability, teamwork and good manners
  • Mastery of the German language in word and writing (minor level C1/C2)
  • Sound English and French skills are beneficial
