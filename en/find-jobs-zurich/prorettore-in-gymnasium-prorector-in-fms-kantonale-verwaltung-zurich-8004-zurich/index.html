Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School Wiedikon Prorector: in Gymnasium, Prorector: in FMS 80 - 100% The Cantonal School Aussersihl (KAS) emerges from the Hohlstrasse branch of the Cantonal School Wiedikon since the school year 2024/25. For the school year 2027/28 it is founded as an independent middle school. In this exciting development phase, there is the opportunity to actively shape the cultural, organizational and pedagogical identity of a new cantonal school in Zurich.For the management of the cantonal school Aussersihl, we are bidding two vice-rector:innen positions as of 1 September 2027. As a member of the school management, you assume the pedagogical, personnel and organizational responsibility for the high school or the specialised secondary school (FMS) as well as for other operational areas of the school.

  • Participation in the strategic and operational management of the school.
  • Responsibility for the implementation and further development of educational offerings in high school or FMS
  • Leadership, support and promotion of teachers
  • Ensuring and further developing the quality of teaching and a positive school culture
  • You have a university degree, a teaching diploma for maturity schools, many years of teaching experience at high school level and ideally leadership experience in education. They convince through pedagogical competence, communication strength and goal-oriented work. We offer the opportunity to help shape the further development of the Cantonal School Aussersihl. You can expect a versatile and challenging job with room for manoeuvre, a committed team and contemporary employment conditions according to cantonal specifications. If you are interested, please submit your application exclusively via our online tool. For information please contact Karin Grob (043 256 99 41 or karin.grob@kwi.ch).

Pensum: 80-100 %
