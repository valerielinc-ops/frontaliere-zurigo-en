Welcome to the Medbase Zentrale Winterthur

Our modern office building is located just three minutes from Winterthur Train Station, in an urban setting that offers ideal conditions for focused work and creative collaboration. At our headquarters, around 150 specialists from the fields of IT, HR, marketing and finance work hand in hand to jointly develop innovative solutions and achieve our corporate goals. With flexible workplaces and modern meeting pods, we promote an open and efficient working culture. At Medbase, we attach great importance to teamwork and the exchange between departments in order to be successful together. Here, colleagues from our sister companies such as Zur Rose, WePractice or the dental center meet, which further enriches the interdisciplinary exchange.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed, independent personality as an administrative employee 100% . The position is broad and covers a wide range of tasks with a focus on administrative support for the business area Medbase Pharmacies in the area of advertising implementation, logistics & assortment pricing.
