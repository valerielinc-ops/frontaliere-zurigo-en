Team Leader in Software Development Cards, ATM and TWINT (80-100%)

Zurich | Home office up to 40% possible | Reference 8280

Together we shape the future of payment in Switzerland. Our platforms process a wide variety of payment methods and create the basis for innovative services, secure transactions and digital payment experiences.

IT takes responsibility for the entire development cycle. You lead an agile team of Java software developers, test experts and requirements engineers to success. As a product owner, you are responsible for requirements management and delivery in the form of software releases. Through the DevOps approach, we ensure the high stability of our services.

Openness, cooperation and a positive culture of error are particularly important to us. Do you want to take responsibility in the PO and Leadership team?

These are your tasks

  • Professional and personnel responsibility for a Scrum team in the role of product owner
  • Proactive co-creation of the roadmap and cooperation in an interdisciplinary agile setup
  • Ensure that implementation targets are met in terms of timetable, cost and quality
  • Establishment and expansion of methodological competence in various areas such as software development, architecture and quality assurance

You bring that with you

  • Completed computer science training (university, higher education or higher education)
  • Strong DevOps competencies and good communication skills
  • Due to your previous activities, you are very familiar with the role of the product owner. Leadership experience is an advantage.
  • Team player who contributes ideas and likes to present work results
