Full description

More than a job

As part of our operational team, you are largely responsible for ensuring that everything arrives at the right place at the right time and that our customers feel that they are in good hands. With your commitment, you keep supply chains running and find the right solution for every challenge. Whether medicines, toys or important machine parts – thanks to you, products reach people all over the world. This not only brings goods to their destination, but also enables the small and big moments in life. Your contribution to Kühne+Nagel has more impact than you can imagine.

‎
In this role, you are the central contact person for our customers and ensure the smooth processing of their sea freight shipments. With your forwarding expertise and your strong service philosophy, you ensure a high level of customer satisfaction and actively contribute to building long-term customer relationships.

How you get involved

  • Holistic support for customers in the area of sea freight and promotion of long-term, cooperative customer relationships
  • Processing customer inquiries and creating offers
  • Control of order processing from order acceptance through document checking to completion of the shipment
  • Integration and maintenance of customer data as well as creation of reports to analyze and optimize our service
  • Close cooperation with the operational departments, sales and Carriers
  • Expanding the portfolio of our existing customers, developing new business and winning back customers

What sets you apart

  • Completed training in freight forwarding/logistics or comparable qualification
  • First professional experience in the field of sea freight
  • Confident use of MS Office programs
  • Independent, team-oriented and service-oriented working style
  • Confident demeanor, strong communication skills as well as high service and customer orientation
  • Good German and English skills, spoken and written, French are an advantage

What's in it for you

  • Performance-related compensation package including 13. Monthly salary
  • Extensive training and development opportunities
  • Exclusive discounts at numerous partner companies
  • Employee recruitment program with attractive bonuses
  • Regular team and company events

Who we are are

Logistics touches every aspect of our lives - from the goods we consume every day to the healthcare we rely on. With us, your work goes beyond logistics: it enables the small and big moments of life for people all over the world.

As a leading global logistics company with the vision to further develop our world, we offer you a safe and stable environment. This is where your work makes a real difference. Whether it's delivering food, developing sustainable transportation solutions or engaging in community engagement, your work has more impact than you can imagine.
