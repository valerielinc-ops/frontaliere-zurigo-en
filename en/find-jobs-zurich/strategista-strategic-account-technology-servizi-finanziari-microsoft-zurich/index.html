Overview As a Strategic Account Technology Strategist, you will lead the technology strategy for one of Microsoft’s largest global insurance customers. Partnering with top-level business and technology executives, you will align business priorities with Microsoft’s AI, Copilot, Security, Cloud, and Data solutions to drive innovation, growth, and digital transformation.As a core member of the Account Team Unit, you will work across Microsoft, partners, and customer stakeholders to shape strategy, influence opportunities, and deliver business outcomes. This role combines technology leadership, executive engagement, and industry expertise to help customers realize their strategic ambitions. At Microsoft, our mission to empower every person and every organization on the planet to achieve more guides how we partner with customers to deliver trusted, impactful solutions. With a growth ‑ mindset culture, we innovate responsibly and measure success by shared progress, people, teams, and customers. Join us to do meaningful work that changes the world and helps shape what’s next for everyone.

Responsibilities

  • Partner with top-level executives and C-level stakeholders to define and execute technology strategies that drive business outcomes.
  • Align customer priorities with Microsoft’s AI, Security, Cloud, Data, and Copilot solutions.
  • Identify opportunities to accelerate innovation, transformation, efficiency, and growth.
  • Lead strategic account planning, forecasting, and execution across cross-functional teams.
  • Act as a trusted advisor, providing industry and technology thought leadership to customer and Microsoft stakeholders.

Qualifications Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, Business, or a related field AND several years of technical consulting, consultative selling, business consulting, or related technology experience
  • OR equivalent experience?
  • Understanding of the insurance industry, including business models, market dynamics, and transformation challenges.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Proven track record driving digital transformation and measurable business outcomes.
  • Experience within the insurance or broader financial services industry.
  • German language skills are preferred to support effective collaboration with local customers and stakeholders.
