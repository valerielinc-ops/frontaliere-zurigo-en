The Clinic for Infectious Diseases and Hospital Hygiene at the University Hospital Zurich offers the entire spectrum of modern clinical infectiology from outpatient care to highly specialized care of complex infections. As a university centre, we combine clinical excellence with research, innovation and the education and training of the next generation.

Their main tasks

  • Active participation in the education and training of assistant doctors and students
  • Self-responsible care of outpatient and inpatient patients with a wide range of infectiological clinical pictures
  • Independent management of an infectiological consultation
  • Senior medical service in the outpatient consultation, in the consultancy service as well as on the bed station
  • Active organization and participation in interdisciplinary and inter-hospital boards

Your profile

  • Specialist title in Infectiology
  • Specialist title in general internal medicine of advantage
  • MEBEKO proof required for foreign diplomas
  • Sound clinical, internistic-infectiological training and experience
  • Enthusiasm for knowledge transfer and the promotion of young medical researchers
  • Enthusiasm for interdisciplinary and versatile clinical activity
  • Interested in actively developing the department and providing new impetus
  • Independent, team-oriented and committed working method

This is what awaits you

  • Independent management of the infectiological consultation hours
  • Demanding interdisciplinary consultancy in a university centre
  • Excellent opportunities to develop your own interests and priorities
  • Market remuneration
