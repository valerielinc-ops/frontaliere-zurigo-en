The sales outlet in Lufingen ZH is looking for a responsible and motivated person to start immediately or by agreement as:

You can look forward to these tasks

  • Always fresh and friendly advice and support for our customers
  • You manage your store staff and are responsible for work schedules
  • Optimal inventory management and ordering of sales and promotional goods
  • Ensuring an attractive presentation of merchandise in line with the concept
  • Looking after, training and developing apprentices
  • Various administrative tasks
  • Managing the postal agency in the branch
  • Responsibility for the budget and key figures

We look forward to candidates with this profile

  • You have completed a retail apprenticeship with a Federal Certificate of Competence (preferably in the food and beverages sector)
  • Experience in a similar role
  • Leadership experience (e.g. department management)
  • Enjoyment of actively engaging with customers
  • Social and professional skills

Up to 7 weeks of vacation

About us Volg is the specialist for village shops and small-format stores in German-speaking and Western Switzerland. We embody closeness to our customers – as a nearby shopping option and through personal contact. Employees are the heart of the store, and we place great value on individuality and personality. That is why our motto is "fresh and friendly".

  • Up to 7 weeks of vacation: Our vacation policy guarantees all employees at least 25 days of vacation. From the 50. Year of age, you receive 30 vacation days and from the 60.   • Flexible work models: From flexible working hours and transparent electronic time tracking to working from home – depending on your role at Volg, you benefit from various options for adapting your work to your needs.
