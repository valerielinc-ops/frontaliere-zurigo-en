Original description

In this role, you will apply deep synthetic chemistry expertise to design, synthesize and evaluate next-generation sorbent materials for Direct Air Capture. You will take scientific ownership of early-stage material innovation, connect synthesis decisions with performance data and help identify the most promising sorbent concepts for further development. You will play a central role in coordinating across stakeholders to ensure alignment of research priorities, technical development and broader organization goals. The role is ideal for a hands-on chemist who enjoys driving technical work independently while collaborating closely with experts across characterization, engineering, scale-up, legal, and external innovation.

This role is a strong fit if you

  • You are a hands-on synthetic chemist, ideally with experience in material synthesis or functional material development.
  • You enjoy working as an individual contributor and technical expert, while effectively coordinating with cross-functional stakeholders.
  • You want to stay close to lab-based experimentation, synthesis, characterization, and scientific problem-solving.
  • You are motivated by early-stage R&D and material innovation, with a passion for dynamic, discovery-driven environments.

What you will do

  • Design and synthesize new sorbent materials: You develop and improve sorbent chemistries for Direct Air Capture, with a focus on performance, durability, cost and scalability.
  • Evaluate material performance: You plan experiments, analyze characterization and adsorption data, and connect synthesis decisions with structure-property relationships.
  • Identify promising concepts: You screen internal and external sorbent concepts against clear technical, commercial, regulatory, scalability, and IP-related criteria.
  • Drive hands-on laboratory work: You run safe, well-documented synthesis and testing campaigns, maintain structured material data, and translate results into recommendations.
  • Collaborate and coordinate across disciplines: You work closely with experts in characterization, engineering, scale-up, legal, patents and external innovation to move the most promising sorbent concepts forward.

Requirements

  • Synthetic chemistry expertise: You have strong hands-on experience in synthetic organic or inorganic chemistry. You have experience in porous, functionalized, polymeric or amine-based. Experience with other sorbent-relevant materials is a plus.
  • Relevant experience: You bring at least 5 years of experience in synthetic chemistry, material synthesis or material development, including applied R&D, start-up, industrial or technology development experience.
  • Chemistry background: You hold an advanced degree in organic, inorganic, synthetic, materials chemistry or a related field. A PhD is a plus.
  • Scientific ownership: You are comfortable leading your own workstreams, interpreting data critically and translating findings into clear recommendations.
  • Clear communication: You communicate clearly with technical and non-technical stakeholders and are proficient in English.
