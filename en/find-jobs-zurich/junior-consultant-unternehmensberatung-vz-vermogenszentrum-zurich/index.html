Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We create transparency in the banking and insurance market and advise our customers comprehensively and neutrally. As we are growing strongly, we are looking for reinforcements for our pension foundations at our headquarters at Paradeplatz in Zurich.

  • You work closely with experienced consultants and develop challenging concepts for companies in which you consider pensions, investments and taxes.
  • You accompany our consultants in customer discussions and are responsible for the professional preparation and follow-up.
  • You are the contact point for the advisors of the VZ VermögensZentrum for questions regarding mandatory and super-compulsory provision.
  • You expand your knowledge continuously and develop step by step into an independent consultant.
  • Your place of work is in Zurich and includes travel activities in the Mittelland.
  • Ongoing studies (shortly before graduation) or already completed studies and first professional experience in customer consulting
  • Enthusiasm for occupational provision and pleasure in customer contact
  • Optimally completed VBV certification
  • Rapid comprehension, exact working methods and analytical thinking
  • Perfect Swiss German and ideally very good English skills
  • You work in an uncomplicated and open environment.
  • After a careful training, you will independently conduct customer meetings.
  • You are also at the right place for your further education: We support you individually and support you with an external further education within the framework of the job profile.
  • Most of the positions are also open for a part-time stay, so that there is time for further education, family, sports and projects that are important to you.
  • Our surveys on satisfaction regularly confirm that our employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
