Welcome to Medbase Zurich Oerlikon

Our modern medical center directly at Zurich Oerlikon train station offers our patients high-quality all-round care. The range of services includes general internal medicine, gynecology, dermatology and cardiology as well as physiotherapy, nutritional advice and psychotherapy. The Zurich City Hospital also operates a dialysis center on our premises. We are a well-organized, motivated team and maintain an open and collegial exchange with each other. Become part of it and benefit from our benefits and internal training opportunities. What is important to us: an appreciative working atmosphere, active professional exchange and targeted further development. Flexible working models also support a healthy work-life balance. The Medbase Group is one of the leading companies in outpatient healthcare. This means services from health promotion to medication supply and acute treatment to rehabilitation.
