The internal audit of the Zürcher Kantonalbank supports the bank in achieving its objectives by assessing the effectiveness of risk management, controls and management and monitoring processes with a systematic and targeted approach. As an auditor: in the team Audit Sales Corporate customers you are part of the area Audit Sales. The team offers assurance in all point-of-sale aspects of corporate banking. This includes in particular the financing business, compliance topics as well as start-up, investment, pension and succession consulting. We also cover the interbank business as well as trade and export finance. In order to successfully fulfill this demanding task tomorrow, we are looking for you as a reinforcement for our team.

Your tasks

  • Planning, managing and conducting internal audits within an audit team
  • Advising the departments on banking and audit-specific issues
  • Analyse portfolio data in close collaboration with the data analytics team
  • Perceiving a professional expert: Inside role in selected topics in sales Corporate customers
  • Provision of annual planning risk assessments
  • Assessing compliance with regulatory and internal requirements in sales of corporate customers
  • Completed university studies (uni, FH) in the field of business or finance or basic banking training with a corresponding further business or finance qualification as well as audit-specific training (e.g. auditor or CIA)
  • Several years of experience as auditor: in internal or external audit
  • In-depth knowledge of corporate and real estate finance as well as experience in VSB/GwG, crossborder and sanctions
  • Knowledge of the use of data analytics in audit execution (data extraction and visualization) is advantageous
  • Secure, convincing appearance as well as step-by-step communication in word and writing
  • Strong conceptual and analytical skills combined with a high sense of responsibility and high willingness to perform
  • Fluent German skills in word and writing as well as good to very good English skills
  • A wide range of tasks with broad insight into the corporate banking business of one of the largest Swiss banks
  • We offer you space for your ideas and thus design the bank of tomorrow
  • Motivated and dedicated team that looks forward to working with you

  • You can flexibly organize your work in consultation with your team
  • Various opportunities to develop personally and professionally
  • Culture of appreciation combined with an open and honest communication style
