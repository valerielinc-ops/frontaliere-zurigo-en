As a heating installer: you provide comfort. You deal with the installation, maintenance as well as the maintenance and repair of heating and cooling installations. Expertly, you direct the heat evenly to where it is needed.

Your field of activity

  • Assembly of various components of heating systems
  • Installation of modern thermal systems (e.g. heat pumps, solid firing systems, solar systems)
  • Use in companies of different sizes
  • Work on construction sites and in company workshops
  • Ensuring warmth and comfort in living, working and leisure areas
  • Ensure uniform heat distribution (e.g. over floors or radiators)
  • Contribution to high living quality and satisfied residents
  • Focus on innovative and energy-efficient solutions
  • Important role in the implementation of the energy strategy

You bring that with you

  • Artisanal skills
  • Practical and technical understanding
  • spatial imagination
  • Physical mobility and robust health
  • Teamwork
