Full description

The Chiropractic Medicine degree program at the University of Zurich, implementation site of the University Hospital Balgrist, is the training center for chiropractic in Switzerland. Our students benefit from the proximity to research and clinic as well as from the good interprofessional cooperation within the University Hospital Balgrist.

Tasks

  • Support to the main lecturer in the preparation and conduct of the introductory course on chiropractic palpations and examinations as well as the conduct of the examinations in the 1. Year of study (about 14x 2 hours per semester)

Requirements

  • You are a qualified chiropractor
  • You take on responsibility in a small team to help shape and advance the teaching and learning of chiropractic students
  • Awareness of quality and responsibility as well as flexibility
  • Interest in teaching
  • You are safe in the German language (C2) and can communicate in English (B1)
