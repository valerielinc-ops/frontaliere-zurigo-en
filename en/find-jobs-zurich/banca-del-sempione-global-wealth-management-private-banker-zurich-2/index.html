Full description

The role

As part of our Global Wealth Management team at our Zurich representative office, we are looking for a Private Banker with an international vision, able to manage customers with global needs and offer customized financial solutions. The person we seek will take care of acquiring and fidelifying new customers, through their knowledge of the market, investments and financial planning.

Liability

  • develop a portfolio of Private Customers;
  • daily support investment and asset management processes, providing investment recommendations and constantly dialogue with customers to quickly grasp their needs and identify customized solutions;
  • ensure the satisfaction of the needs of customers, acting as an interface with the internal functions of the Bank and ensuring an excellent quality of the service.

The profile

  • at least 10 years of experience in the role at Banks in Switzerland (Canton Zurich), proven by a portfolio of loyal customers;
  • excellent knowledge of Swiss and international banking, services and financial products, as well as the regulatory system;
  • entrepreneurial mindset and ability to create strong relationships with customers;
  • advanced use of the office package;
  • necessary knowledge of English, Italian and German.
  • excellent communication skills, teamwork and team spirit.
