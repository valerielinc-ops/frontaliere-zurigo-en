Full description

For the network maintenance team, as the operating partner of PubliBike AG’s «Züri Velo» bike-sharing system, we are looking for temporary support for one to two years starting February 1, 2027, or by agreement.

Tasks

  • You independently plan routes for battery and rebalancing logistics and complete them efficiently.
  • You transport batteries by cargo bike, Kyburz (Cat. M) or van (Cat. B, automatic/electric).
  • You clean stations as needed and carry out minor repairs on Züri Velo bikes and e-bikes.
  • You are responsible for compliance with standards for quality, infrastructure and materials, as well as occupational safety.
  • You work during the week, in the evenings and on weekends. Working hours during the week are three shifts: 8-17 hrs / 13-22 hrs and Saturdays 8-17 hrs.

Profile

  • EBA in a skilled trade or comparable training
  • Flexibility in terms of working hours (including evening shifts and Saturday-/Sunday work)
  • Good physical condition
  • Willingness to work outdoors in all weather
  • Driving licence Cat. B required (automatic/electric)

We offer

  • Fixed-term employment for 1 year with the prospect of an extension of up to 24 months
  • A varied and challenging position
  • Attractive employment conditions
