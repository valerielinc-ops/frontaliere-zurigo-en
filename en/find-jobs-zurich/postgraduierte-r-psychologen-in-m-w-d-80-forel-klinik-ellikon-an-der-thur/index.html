Full description

Forel Klinik is one of the leading addiction clinics in Switzerland and specializes in the treatment of people with alcohol or drug dependence. At two locations, more than 180 employees provide support for those affected on their personal path to a better quality of life and self-determination. We offer individually tailored therapy concepts in the inpatient, daily clinical and outpatient setting. Our addiction treatment combines evidence-based methods with a value-oriented attitude – with the aim of enabling sustainable and effective change. As an addiction specialist clinic, it is anchored in our DNA to open up prospects and contribute to the development of people. This principle also shapes our dealings with employees. Create with us paths that work. With us, you work in an environment that combines therapeutic diversity, professional reputation and the space for careful relationship design. As part of an interdisciplinary team, you contribute to ensuring that our treatment concepts have a lasting effect and are constantly evolving – for patients, their environment and society. For our location in Ellikon we are looking for you to strengthen the team as of 1. November 2026 or by arrangement.

Their tasks

The field of activity includes the treatment of alcohol- and drug-dependent patients in individual and group setting and the support in solving their social problems. Supervisions and clinic-internal training events provide an opportunity to deepen daily experience and knowledge. They take over the co-management and later the independent management of group therapies, are used for various representation situations in individual and group setting and will take over case management under supervision. They are introduced to documentation and reporting in order to be able to do this work independently later. The area of activity also includes the diagnostic investigation and the support of the scientific evaluation of the therapy offers.

Your profile

For this position, we expect a completed master’s degree with a specialization in clinical psychology. We would like to see a specialist with an interest in interdisciplinary cooperation in a team of doctors, psychologists, design, sports and occupational therapists with the professional goal of the Swiss-approved psychotherapist.

Our offer

With this 15-month position, we offer you responsible tasks in a motivated, modern and well-connected environment. Your professional and personal development is a concern for us, which we support generously and specifically with structured further education and training offers. In addition, you receive weekly supervision, which can be credited depending on the training institute and status.

The working conditions are contemporary and appealing

  • Reliable service planning in a stable environment
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
  • 60 % employer contribution to the pension fund
  • 100 % employer contribution to KTG insurance

Your place of work Our inpatient clinic in Ellikon an der Thur is idyllic in the countryside and at the same time offers good accessibility from the Zurich, Winterthur and Eastern Switzerland region and the German border area. Further advantages of the place of work are the in-house gastronomy and sufficient free parking. Our offer is aimed at people of all genders and identities.
