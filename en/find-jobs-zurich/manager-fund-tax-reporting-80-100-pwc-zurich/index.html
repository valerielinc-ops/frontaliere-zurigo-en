Full description

At PwC, we support leading asset managers, fund initiators, and institutional investors throughout the entire fund lifecycle – from classic UCITS structures to complex alternative investment vehicles. Our goal is not only to implement regulatory and tax requirements but to create sustainable, future-proof solutions. To strengthen our specialized Fund Tax Reporting team, we are looking for an experienced managerial personality who combines deep specialist knowledge with leadership, ownership, and a genuine team orientation.

  • In your role as Manager, you actively shape demanding mandates and work in an excellent, collegial team at the interface of taxes, regulation, and investment structures:
  • Responsibility for the tax analysis of Swiss and foreign investment funds, including UCITS funds and alternative investment structures (e.g., private markets, fund-of-funds, complex holding and target fund structures)
  • End-to-end management of the annual Swiss tax reporting for foreign funds – in close collaboration with demanding customers and our global PwC network
  • Supervision of the calculation of taxable income for Swiss tax purposes using modern PwC tax reporting technologies and ensuring publication on the course list of the Federal Tax Administration
  • Advising our customers on the tax accounting of Swiss investment funds, particularly on income from UCITS and alternative target funds
  • Assuming project and quality responsibility (timelines, governance, risks) with a clear focus on efficiency, scalability, and regulatory security
  • Active contribution to the automation and digitalization of Fund Tax Reporting processes as an interface between tax experts, IT, and customers
  • Mentoring and professional development of team members as well as active contribution to an open, high-performance, and appreciative team culture
  • Building and maintaining long-term, trust-based customer relationships on an equal footing
  • University degree (Bachelor / Master) in business, finance, taxes, or higher professional training in the financial sector.
  • At least five years of relevant professional experience in Fund Tax Reporting, Fund Accounting, or a comparable function
  • Experience in dealing with UCITS structures and alternative investment vehicles, ideally in an international context
  • Sovereign handling of complex fund structures
  • Strong sense of responsibility, entrepreneurial thinking, and enjoyment of driving topics forward
  • Very good English and German skills
  • Affinity for technology-driven and data-based solutions
  • Team-oriented personality with high social competence and clear communication
