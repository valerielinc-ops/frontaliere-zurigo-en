Head of Controlling (80-100%) m/f/d - NSN medical AG NSN medical AG is a company of the NSN medical group and provides commercial services for the entire group of companies. The NSN medical group successfully operates various innovative healthcare companies in Switzerland, including the Limmat Clinic in Zurich, the Center for Integrative Oncology at the locations in Zurich, Winterthur, Uster, Glarus and Richterswil, as well as narkose.ch, which operates throughout Switzerland in the field of anesthesia. Controlling with impact – shape the economic future of an innovative healthcare group. Our goal is to sustainably combine medical quality, efficiency and innovation. To further develop our group, we are looking for an entrepreneurial personality as head of controlling who will support our organization with well-founded analyses, transparency and an operational and strategic perspective. In this role, you will actively shape the financial management of our companies and locations and make a significant contribution to the further development of our organization.
