Assistant Professor of Italian Literature and Culture (m/f/d)

10%, Zurich, temporary

Printed Printing

You speak fluent Italian, English and German? Are you in contact with people and can you become enthusiastic about literature and culture? If you enjoy communication and organization and you are also enrolled students (not doctoral candidates) of a Swiss university, ideally the ETH Zurich, then apply as an auxiliary assistant (10%) of the visiting professorship for Italian Literature and Culture of the ETH Zurich, as of 1 September 2026.

The visiting professorship for Italian Literature and Culture at the Department of Humanities, Social and Political Sciences (D-GESS) at ETH Zurich organizes lectures, events and cultural projects in cooperation with partners from the literary and cultural sectors. The assistant supports the care of the guests as well as the communication around lectures and events and contributes to the implementation of the activities of the guest professorship.

Job description

  • Support of those in charge of administration in the care of guests before and during lectures and events
  • Communication with students and other interested parties (online and offline)
  • Communication of events and information through various channels (website, email, newsletter, Instagram and Facebook)
  • Design of posters and text

Profile

  • Fluent Italian, English and German skills
  • Joy of contact with people
  • Interest in Literature and Culture
  • Joy of communication and organization
  • Enrolled students (no doctoral candidates) at a Swiss university, ideally at ETH Zurich
  • Time flexibility for lectures and evening events
