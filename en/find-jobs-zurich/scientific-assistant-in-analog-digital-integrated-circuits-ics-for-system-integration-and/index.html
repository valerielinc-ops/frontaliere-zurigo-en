Scientific Assistant in Analog/Digital Integrated Circuits (ICs) for System Integration and Testing

80%-100%, Zurich, fixed-term

The Integrated Devices, Electronics, and Systems (IDEAS) Group develops cutting-edge system-level integrated circuits (ICs) for next-generation communication and sensing technologies, with applications in 6G, satellite communication (SATCOM), and AI data-center connectivity. Our research is funded by multiple EU, ESA, and Swiss programs and projects, and contributes directly to European digital sovereignty.

These system-level IC chips are built from highly sophisticated transistor-level circuits and combine extensive analog, digital, and mixed-signal components that need to operate together to deliver full system functionality. Testing these complex ICs also requires automated test codes and operating high-end electronic measurement facilities.

We are seeking a research assistant to join our IDEAS team and contribute to analog/digital circuit design, system integration, and testing of complex IC chips. The anticipated start date is Monday, 3 August 2026.

Project background

Next-generation communication and sensing systems rely on system-level ICs with increasing complexity and integration. These highly integrated ICs typically include sophisticated support circuits, such as programmable analog biasing circuits with temperature compensation, high-speed digital interfaces with on-chip memory, and mixed-signal circuits such as low-power, compact analog-to-digital converters (ADCs) for on-chip performance monitoring and Built-In-Self-Testing (BIST).

The IDEAS Group has already developed and silicon-verified a range of these supporting circuits, providing a strong basis for the design of new system-level ICs. Designing these next-generation ICs is the core of our research and technology development, along with the testing required to bring them to life and make real-world impacts.

This position is intended for a candidate who can design, implement, and refine analog, digital, and mixed-signal support circuits for integration into future system-level ICs. The work will support real-world applications and help advance next-generation communication, computation, and sensing systems. The successful candidate will also develop test PCBs, set up measurement environments, and perform automated testing of the developed ICs.

Job description

  • Design, implement, and refine analog, digital, and mixed-signal support circuits for system-level ICs. Example circuits include but are not limited to programmable biasing circuits, temperature-compensation loops, digital programming and interface circuits, on-chip memory, and low-power compact ADCs for performance monitoring
  • Support top-level assembly of system-level ICs, including LVS, DRC, and density checks
  • Develop test PCBs, set up measurement environments, and support automated testing of complex IC chips
  • Lead the execution and analysis of system-level IC test campaigns to validate performance under various operating conditions
  • Document and investigate test anomalies
  • Prepare formal test reports for internal reviews and project deliverables
  • Develop and maintain automated test frameworks to improve repeatability, traceability, and testing efficiency

Profile

  • Prior experience in analog, digital, and mixed-signal IC circuit design, with familiarity with IC design tools and tapeout flows, including digital circuit synthesis
  • Prior experience in printed-circuit board (PCB) design
  • Tapeout experience is a plus
  • IC testing experience using Keysight and R&S equipment is a plus
  • Experience working in fast-paced research environments
  • Highly organized, dependable, and able to manage multiple tasks efficiently
  • Motivated to contribute to a collaborative and interdisciplinary research environment, with a strong commitment to technical excellence
  • High professional integrity, maturity, and a constructive working style
  • Excellent communication skills in English, the IDEAS team's working language

We offer

  • A stimulating, collaborative environment in a world-leading multidisciplinary electronics research team at ETH Zurich
  • The opportunity to support ongoing research projects in next-generation communication and sensing technologies for the digital revolution
  • Hands-on experience in complex system-level IC design, development, integration, and testing
  • Membership in a motivated, supportive, and collaborative team
  • The opportunity to learn from experienced researchers and technical experts while contributing to high-impact research projects and European digital sovereignty
