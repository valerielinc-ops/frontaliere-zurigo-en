Full description

The canton police, with their approximately 4000 employees, take responsibility for security in the canton of Zurich. As a stationed police station, you work at the pulse of the event. You perform post duty, intervene as first person at the event location, initiate the necessary measures and are responsible for the handling of krim's physical, traffic and security police tasks. In the Bülach district, we are looking for a station for police stations in Bülach, Embrach and Dietlikon 80

  • 100% tasks
  • ensuring basic police care
  • taking on search and prevention tasks
  • applying and implementing legal measures
  • taking up criminal offences and conducting investigations
  • networking with partner organisations (network care)
  • promoting proximity to citizens in the sense of community policy
  • providing weekend, night, exit and piket services
  • planning, coordinating and managing police services Actions
  • Requirements
  • Swiss Civil Rights and a completed training as a policeman (e.g. FA/Certificate)
  • several years of professional experience as a police officer and a well-founded and broad-based knowledge of basic police care, preferably complemented by experience in the field of investigation
  • committed personality, willingness to work in a team and to continuously develop the professional and personal skills
  • independent working methods, reliability, resilience and flexibility
