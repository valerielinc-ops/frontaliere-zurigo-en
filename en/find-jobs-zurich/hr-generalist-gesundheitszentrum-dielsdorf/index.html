Original description

Dielsdorf Health Center is a modern geriatric competence center with around 500 employees from 35 nations. Our offer ranges from counselling for older people and their relatives to general and specialized long-term care and care at home.

Tasks: • Independent processing of administrative personnel processes over the entire employee lifecycle (entry, mutation, exit, excl. recruitment / wage) • Preparation of employment contracts, contract changes and other personnel-related documents • Maintenance and management of digital and physical personnel files • Management of absences (holidays, illness, accident, etc.) • Support for authorisations, notification procedures and other administrative processes • Contact person: for employees and managers, social insurance and offices in administrative HR matters • Participation in HR projects as well as the optimization and digitization of HR processes • Responsibility for Polypoint PEP as system manager: r, including system support, user administration, configuration and programming Requirements • Basic commercial training and further training in Human Resources (HR assistant: in or equivalent qualification) • Several years of professional experience in personnel administration, ideally in health care • In-depth knowledge of Polypoint PEP, user administration as well as experience in support and further development of the system • Style-confident knowledge of German in words and writing • Very good user knowledge in MS Office (especially Word and Excel); Knowledge of LOBOS, swiss+ and Umantis is an advantage • Experience in the cooperation and implementation of HR projects, for example in the area of process optimization or digitalization • Proactive and solution-oriented personality with an independent and careful working method We offer: We offer you a responsible and interesting job in a varied, future-oriented and interprofessional environment. We are in a rural situation with good public transport links. You benefit from our attractive employment conditions, including with mind. 5 weeks holidays, good social benefits, discounted Reka checks, individual training opportunities, cheap prices in our restaurant and free parking spaces.
