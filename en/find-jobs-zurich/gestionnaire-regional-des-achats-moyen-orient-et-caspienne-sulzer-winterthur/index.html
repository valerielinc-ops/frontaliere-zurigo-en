Full description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

Develop and lead the regional procurement strategy, objectives, governance and processes for Services across the Middle East & CIS region, covering all product lines including pump services, turbomachinery services, electromechanical services, spare parts, retrofits and field/site services. The role aligns regional procurement priorities with division, global SCM and business strategies, while supporting the needs of service centres, legal entities and project execution teams. It is accountable for improving supplier performance, reducing Cost of Poor Quality (COPQ), improving On-Time In-Full (OTIF) delivery, shortening lead times, strengthening contract coverage and reducing Total Cost of Ownership (TCO). Main tasks:

  • Develop regional procurement strategy, objectives and processes for the Services division across Middle East & CIS, in accordance with Sulzer procurement guidelines.
  • Align regional priorities with Global Services SCM, division procurement and local business requirements.
  • Develop and maintain a high-performance procurement culture that improves KPI delivery and customer service to internal and external stakeholders.
  • Work across the division matrix organization, balancing global category strategies, regional needs, local legal entity requirements and service centre execution priorities.
  • Deliver regional procurement benefits, savings, cost avoidance and supplier performance improvements across all service product lines.
  • Provide specialist advice, guidance and service on leading practice procurement to the region.
  • Manage long-term partnerships with key strategic suppliers, in alignment with division procurement, based on quality, cost, OTIF, responsiveness, compliance and regional service requirements.
  • Assess, qualify and select regional suppliers in close cooperation with product line, service centre, project execution and legal entity teams.
  • Drafting of regional framework agreements and execution of contract management
  • Coordinate procurement development activities, e.g.: governance, new tools digital developments, e-procurement, supplier development, supplier risk, ESG.
  • Standardization and monitoring the procurement processes
  • Build and maintain a strong regional network with legal entity procurement managers and operational stakeholders across Middle East & CIS.
