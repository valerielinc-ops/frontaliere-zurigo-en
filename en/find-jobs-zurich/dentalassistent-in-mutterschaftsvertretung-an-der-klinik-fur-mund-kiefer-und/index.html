Original description

Would you like to use your expertise in an exciting clinical environment and become part of a well-established team? For the duration of a maternity representation, we are looking for a committed personality who accompanies our patients with competence and empathy and actively shapes the varied everyday practice. >The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.

Tasks

  • Assistance at the chair at general consultation hours of the clinic as well as in dental treatments and oral surgery
  • X-ray production
  • Planning, coordination and preparation of consultation hours
  • Documentation of findings and preparation of estimates
  • Reception and care of our patients: inside
  • Recording and securing appointment agreements and recalls
  • Performance measurement of medicines and materials
  • Responsibility for compliance with and implementation of legal hygiene guidelines
  • Blood collection in patients: inside

Requirements

  • Completed training as a dental assistant: in or Med. Practice assistant: in EFZ
  • Several years of professional experience, ideally already in oral surgery and / or oral, jaw and facial surgery
  • Self-employed work and friendly manners
  • High social competence and willingness to participate in a large team
  • Willingness to work in shift model (once a month weekend service in the morning)
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing (every other language is an advantage)
  • Good user knowledge in clinic or dental practice information systems (e.g. KISIM, ZaWin) is an advantage

We offer

In addition to advanced working conditions in a university environment, we offer you a versatile, demanding and varied activity. You have the opportunity to design your work processes in a dynamic environment in a goal-oriented and independent manner. The place of work is mainly at USZ Circle (airport).
