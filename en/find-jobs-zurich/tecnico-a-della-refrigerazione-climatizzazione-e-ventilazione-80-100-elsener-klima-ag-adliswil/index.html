Full description

Elsener Klima AG is a Swiss company with competences in ventilation and air conditioning technology. With headquarters in Adliswil and around 30 employees, we work in Zurich and the surrounding area. We belong to the Burkhalter Group, the umbrella organization of traditional companies in the fields of building technology and building envelope in Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein.

Our core business is ventilation. We are expanding the air conditioning sector in a targeted manner: You install air conditioners, put them into operation and maintain them. In the quieter season you are in use in the ventilation service and so busy throughout the year. For our team we are looking for one:

Your tasks

  • Assembly and commissioning of air conditioning systems (especially split devices) for our customers
  • Soldering, tapping, evacuating, filling refrigerants and checking leakage
  • Record keeping: commissioning, leakage control and refrigerant accounting
  • Service and maintenance of air conditioning and ventilation systems and installation of ventilation systems
  • Record faults, search for and correct errors, mechanically and electrically
  • Electrical work on the equipment as part of your training
  • Instruction of customers and friendly appearance on site

Your profile

  • Training as a refrigeration system installer or equivalent experience in cold and climate
  • Advantage: Training as an electrical installer or assembly electrician
  • Professional approval Refrigerant (FBK) or the willingness to catch up with us
  • Interested in ventilation technology, we will work you in
  • Independent, clean work and reliable documentation
  • Category B driving licence and good knowledge of German

Our offer

  • At 100%: 4.5 day week, 40 hours from Monday to Friday noon, Friday afternoon free
  • No piquet service, service vehicle and business phone
  • Employment according to GAV Building Technology with contemporary social benefits
  • Introduction to ventilation technology and further training, also for the professional approval refrigerant
  • Experienced team with experienced cold specialists and good disposition
