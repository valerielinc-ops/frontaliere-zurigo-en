Full description

Quite diverse and never boring At EPI WohnWerk, no day is like the other. The people we accompany each one bring their own life story. This makes our work sometimes challenging, but never boring. In the interprofessional team, we find the best solutions together. The residential area is led by two co-lines. You take responsibility for the residential building on the Rüti (WHR) and shape the future of the area hand in hand with your co-management. Do you want to be part of our team?

The Multifaceted Day

  • Together with your co-management, you are responsible for the area of living and develop it further conceptually.
  • They control human resources, occupancy, quality and finances, carry budget responsibility and accompany change processes
  • They lead the management team, consisting of the team leaders Living, professionally and personnel
  • They shape a leadership and team culture based on trust, appreciation and commitment and actively shape ongoing organizational development
  • They work closely with the care service provider and maintain cooperation with internal and external partners, relatives, supporters and legal representatives.

Their diverse talents

  • They are dipl. Nursing specialist HF or FH and have completed further training in the field of leadership
  • You have several years of leadership experience in social or health care, ideally five to six years in a comparable function
  • You have profound experience in long-term care or in the nursing home area
  • Positive leadership is an attitude for you: You lead in a strength-oriented, appreciative and trusting manner and at the same time clearly and bindingly. You know how strongly leadership shapes work climate, motivation, health and retention, and live health-promoting leadership in everyday life
  • They lead over clarity, relationship and responsibility instead of hierarchy. They like to share responsibility, allow strong personalities beside themselves and see different opinions and critical feedback as enrichment
  • They communicate clearly, transparently and bindingly and meet employees, relatives, supporters and other stakeholders openly, honestly and with integrity.
  • You have business understanding, show presence and decision-making pleasure and bring the necessary strategic altitude to further develop the WHR
  • You work securely with client information systems, ERP software (Abacus beneficial) and personnel planning tools (PEP beneficial) and have good MS Office knowledge
  • A great management team and a lively environment with a lot of creative freedom, in which your ideas are in demand.
  • 5-6 weeks holidays
  • Own pension fund with above average employer share of 60%
  • Extensive internal training offer and support for external training
  • Staff apartments, parking spaces, bicycle parking spaces with electricity connection as well as discounts in the EPI Park Restaurant, in the pharmacy and half-tax or general subscription
