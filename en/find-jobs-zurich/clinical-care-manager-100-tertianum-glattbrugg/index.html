Full description

The Tertianum Bubenholz residential and care center in Glattbrugg offers 59 barrier-free senior apartments with à la carte services and 43 single care rooms. Bubenholz is located just 200 meters from the city center of Opfikon/Glattbrugg.

We are looking for, by agreement,

Clinical Nursing Specialist 100%

Job-sharing also possible

Your tasks

  • Independently manage complex care situations (e.g., palliative care, validation, etc.) as a nursing contact person, create the care process, develop and implement it together with FaGe employees
  • Assess clinical situations and derive appropriate measures
  • Training and coaching of professionals and SRK employees
  • Training courses and lectures for internal employees
  • Support LPB in bed allocation and admission process
  • Check MQIs together with LPB and derive measures for the care team
  • Implement current care concepts in practice together with the team (pain, dementia, malnutrition, wound care, etc.)

Your profile

  • Completed nursing training HF or FH or equivalent diploma recognized by SRK
  • 3 years of professional experience, preferably in long-term care
  • Enjoyment in training and accompanying, as well as pronounced coaching skills
  • RAI and Care Coach knowledge advantageous
  • Structured, self-reliant way of working
