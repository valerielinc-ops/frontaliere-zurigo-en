Full description

Here we are

We accompany people with tumor and blood diseases holistically – medically, therapeutically and humanly. Our approach combines modern conventional medicine with complementary therapies and consistently focuses on people. At our locations in Zurich, Winterthur, Uster, Oerlikon, Richterswil and Glarus, doctors, nurses, therapists and administrative staff work closely together – interdisciplinary, respectful and on an equal footing. We create an environment where quality, time and humanity are not just words, but are lived daily. Do you want a task in which you can contribute your organizational talent and at the same time accompany people in a demanding life situation? Then you are exactly right with us. To strengthen our versatile team in the ZIO Zurich lake in Richterswil, we are looking for you as a medical assistant immediately or by appointment.

Medical assistant (80% - 100%)

Your everyday work

  • You organize, accompany and support our office hours in a well-structured way - with a lot of heart
  • You ensured a warm and smooth patient reception and are the first point of contact for our patients, who are always in good hands with you
  • You will independently carry out laboratory diagnostics and help to gain important insights for medical care (Fuji DriChem, Sysmex, Afias)
  • You implement the medically ordered diagnostic and therapeutic measures professionally and thus contribute to a holistic healing
  • You take over the telephone management and coordinate appointments professionally
  • You perform administrative tasks and ensure clean documentation (reporting from dictates)

This is what you bring with you

  • You have completed training as a medical assistant – and you feel at home in this profession
  • You are confident in dealing with MS Office (Axenita practice software knowledge is a plus)
  • You have very good knowledge of German in words and writing. Knowledge of foreign languages is a welcome bonus
  • You show high flexibility and resilience and are always service-oriented
  • You are communicative, open to patients and always professional
  • You enjoy dealing with people and have a high level of empathy

Your personality

  • Friendly, empathetic and service-oriented
  • Reliable and well organized
  • Team-ready and flexible
  • Maintained appearance with positive radiation

This is what we offer you

  • An exciting and responsible task in a dynamic, holistic medical environment
  • A unique working environment in which conventional medicine is combined with anthroposophically extended healing methods – for a holistic and sustainable patient care
  • The opportunity to actively contribute and shape everyday practice
  • Flexibility in working hours so you can balance work and private life
  • A modern, friendly and inspiring working environment where collaboration and mutual appreciation are a priority
