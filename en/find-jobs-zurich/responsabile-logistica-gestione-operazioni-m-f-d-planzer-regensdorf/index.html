Full description

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, participate and grow with us. Become part of our family and shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us.

Tasks

  • You lead our logistics branch with heart, mind and live the role model function
  • For you, leadership means more than plans and conversations: you recognize potential, promote talent and create an environment in which around 14 employees can work together successfully.
  • You keep track of warehouses, processes, deadlines and priorities – even if things get hectic
  • You are responsible for process optimization (space, processes, personnel, CIP)
  • You maintain contact with our customers and ensure that service is not just a word, but a daily life.
  • You have key figures, costs and evaluations under control and make decisions independently and independently within the defined area of responsibility
  • Administrative tasks are just as reliable as operational challenges
  • You keep an eye on technical facilities and infrastructure maintenance and recognize the need for action at an early stage
  • Work safety, order and cleanliness in the warehouse you ensure at all times

Profile

  • A further training as a logistics specialist or a comparable qualification is part of your backpack and there is a lot more practical know-how. Experience in technical maintenance rounds off your profile
  • You have already gained several years of leadership experience. Motivating, promoting and celebrating success together gives you at least as much pleasure as a well-organized stock.
  • Numbers are a helpful tool for you. You not only read key figures, costs and evaluations, you also understand what lies behind them
  • You will certainly master German in words and writing and will always find the right tone with both employees and customers.
  • You keep track even if several topics demand attention at the same time

Benefits

  • With us you start with 5 weeks vacation and benefit from the 5th year of service even from 6 weeks!
  • With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • You will spend your first working days in the central introduction, where you will be taught the most important basics
  • Get ready for parking - with us you can park for free

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
