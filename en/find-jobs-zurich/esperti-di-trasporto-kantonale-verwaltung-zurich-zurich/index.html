Canton of Zurich Security Directorate Road Traffic Agency Transport Expert 80 - 100% Around 460 employees work at the Canton of Zurich’s Road Transport Office at nine locations. Our task is road safety in the Canton of Zurich. We are responsible for the approval and inspection of vehicles and ships, for driver tests, for administrative measures and for the collection of traffic taxes. For the Zurich-Albisgütli, Bülach, Regensdorf and Bassersdorf road transport offices, we are looking for transport experts. In the working area of vehicle and driver tests, you can expect versatile, structured, technical tasks and intensive contacts with our customers. The responsible work of the transport expert also requires a high level of understanding of the legal framework of the Road Traffic Act. You bring that with you

  • Degree as an automotive mechatronics engineer or automotive specialist (if possible specialisation in commercial vehicles), agricultural machinery or construction machinery mechanic
  • Joy in working with different people
  • A professional, safe and respectful appearance
  • Quick comprehension and the motivation to keep your knowledge always à jour
  • Minimum age 24 years and no driving license withdrawal in the last 5 years We offer
  • Attractive employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines
  • Comprehensive theoretical and practical training
  • Self-employment and the opportunity to continuously develop professionally
  • Generous support for further training
  • Collegiality, which is noticeable in everyday life If you want to work together with us for road safety, we look forward to your complete application. For further information, Andrea Haas, Head of HR (043 257 83 16), is at your disposal.
