Full description

We design Zurich from the ground up: liveable and climate-neutral. Bring your expertise and work with us on a future-oriented city. For the Department of Legal Service we are looking for reinforcement from 1 January 2027 or by agreement.

Tasks

  • They draft and examine loan and award permits for city-wide coordinated projects of the civil engineering office to the competent authorities (e.g. department head, city council, municipal council, voters).
  • They accompany the answering of political advances (motions) with financial law content.
  • They develop challenging problem solutions and accompany consultations.
  • They transmit complex content in connection with credit and procurement law to partners of different interests and points of view.
  • They work in cross-departmental cooperation with project managers and lawyers as well as with the Departmental Secretariat of the Department of Civil Engineering and Waste Management.

Profile

  • Law degree (lic. iur. or MLaw)
  • Administrative, judicial or equivalent professional experience
  • Preferably knowledge of administrative law and in particular of financial and public procurement law
  • Excellent linguistic, analytical and conceptual skills as well as a flair for quickly reducing new complex content to relevant legal issues
  • Precise, independent, prudent and solution-oriented working methods as well as resilience and skill for interdisciplinary cooperation

We offer

  • The opportunity to shape the future of the largest Swiss city.
  • A culture that is shaped by respectful, reliable, ambitious and inclusive values and an employer who lives equality, diversity and inclusion.
  • Passionate and committed colleagues whose heart beats for Zurich.
  • A workplace located within walking distance to the HB Zurich.
  • Attractive employment conditions, such as contribution to catering costs, a solid pension fund with an above-average employer share and the opportunity to reconcile work and private life with flexible working hours and home office.

About us

For a high quality of life: The approximately 350 employees of the civil engineering office shape the mobility and public space in the largest city in Switzerland every day.
