University Library

Job as of now or by agreement, indefinitely

The University Library of Zurich (UB) together with the Central Library of Zurich ensures the library coverage of the University of Zurich. Their offer is primarily aimed at the researchers, lecturers and students of UZH. It is also open to the public. The UB Natural Sciences provides the Faculty of Mathematics and Natural Sciences, the Vetsuisse Faculty and the Institute of Computer Science as well as interested specialists with scientific information and comprises four library locations. It offers a diverse and innovative range of courses, courses and consulting services as well as attractive learning and working spaces on the Irchel campus, in the Botanical Garden of the University of Zurich and at the Tierspital.

Tasks

In a team together with other Liaison Librarians (LL) for natural sciences:

  • Selection of information resources according to university needs, participation in the licensing of electronic media
  • Conception and implementation of courses and participation in courses on topics such as dealing with scientific information, research with artificial intelligence, publishing or research data management
  • Advising students, researchers and lecturers on subject-specific inquiries and research
  • Networking with researchers and lecturers in the department as well as with the Liaison Librarians from the other areas of the UB and ZB
  • Participation in the further development of the LL service offering at UB and ZB together with other positions within UB and UZH

Profile

  • Completed university degree in natural sciences, doctorate of advantage
  • Further education (e.g. CAS, MAS) and/or experience in academic library work is advantageous
  • Ability to think interdisciplinary
  • Didactic know-how, experience in teaching
  • Joy of meeting people in a university environment
  • Efficient and responsible working style
  • Willingness to perform, high customer orientation, team and communication skills
  • Openness to flexible forms of work and to innovations in scientific libraries
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
