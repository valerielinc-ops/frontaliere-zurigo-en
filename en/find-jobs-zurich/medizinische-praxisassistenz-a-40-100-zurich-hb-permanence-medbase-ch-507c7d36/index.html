Welcome to Medbase Zurich HB Permanence

In our Medical Center in the middle of Zurich, directly in the main railway station, we look after patients without prior notification 365 days a year. Our team, consisting of almost 60 employees, consists of specialists in general internal medicine, laboratory employees and MPAs and covers the clinical, diagnostic and therapeutic spectrum of general and internal medicine. At Medbase Permanence Hauptbahnhof Zurich, we ensure a balanced work-life balance. Working part-time is possible in all functions, and working hours can be made flexible.
