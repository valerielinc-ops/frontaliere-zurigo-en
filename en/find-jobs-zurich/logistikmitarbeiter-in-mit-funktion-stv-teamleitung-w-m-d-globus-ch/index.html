Full description

Welcome to Globus, the heart of the Swiss premium and luxury world! Our stores are represented in all major Swiss cities as well as in the Glattzentrum. With a unique range, high-quality products and first-class service, we create special shopping experiences for our customers. Behind our stores, our logistics ensure every day that our high-quality products are in the right place at the right time. For our logistics center in Otelfingen, we are looking for a deputy team leader in logistics with a workload of 100% as of November 1, 2026 or by arrangement. In this role, you support the team management in day-to-day operational business and take responsibility for the processes in the Home & Living area in their absence. HERE YOU MAKE THE DIFFERENCE

  • You unpack, identify and check incoming goods.
  • You mark and display merchandise.
  • You attach fuses according to specifications.
  • You store and move merchandise.
  • You pick the goods based on store orders.
  • You take on the role of deputy in the absence of the team leader.
  • You support the team management in training new employees.

THIS IS WHAT MAKES YOU STAND OUT

  • You have completed an apprenticeship, preferably in logistics, as well as professional experience in the logistics sector.
  • Ideally, you have experience in a deputy or managerial role.
  • You have good IT user knowledge.
  • You are characterized by a high degree of reliability and a sense of responsibility as well as service-oriented thinking and actions.
  • You have a structured, independent and efficient way of working.
  • You have strong communication skills and are team-oriented.
  • You are physically and mentally resilient as well as flexible and willing to take on responsibility.

BENEFITS FOR YOU

You can also look forward to

  • An exciting working environment, flat hierarchies and cool people.
  • A short transfer if you come to work by S-Bahn - our logistics center is located right next to the S-Bahn station.
  • A free parking space, also right next to the building.
  • Attractive social benefits and at least 5 weeks of vacation.
  • Up to 50% discount on products from Globus Welt for you and your family.
