Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With more than 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric offer of Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in ambulatory, day-clinical and inpatient settings.

We are looking for an agreement on January 1, 2027 or

Their tasks

  • Support of the hotel management team in the daily management and organization of station service and cleaning
  • Deputy team leader in their absence
  • Active cooperation in the areas of station service and cleaning
  • Ensuring service quality and compliance with quality and hygiene standards
  • Support and guidance of employees in everyday work
  • Control of operations and ensure proper operation
  • Flexible use in different shifts and readiness for weekend services
  • Close cooperation with care and other professional groups

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training in the field of hotel industry - housekeeping (mandatory)
  • Several years of professional experience, ideally in the hospital or clinic sector
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • First leadership experience or motivation to take on leadership responsibility
  • Organizational skills, sense of responsibility and independent working methods
  • High service orientation and pleasure in dealing with patients
  • Flexibility and willingness to work different hours and weekend services
  • Empathy, high resilience, teamwork and good communication skills
  • Knowledge of hygiene and quality standards
