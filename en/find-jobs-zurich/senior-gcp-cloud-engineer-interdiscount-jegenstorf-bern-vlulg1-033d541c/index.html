Senior Cloud Engineer

Interdiscount — Zurich, Zurich, Svizzera

At Interdiscount, we offer our customers the best shopping experience in Switzerland. Engage in a dynamic and entrepreneurial working environment. Take responsibility, be proactive with us and develop further.

Tasks

  • As part of our systems team, you are a central hub in our agile release train

  • You have knowledge of agile frameworks such as SAFe and Scrum
  • Ideally, know You are in Microservice Development with Java Spring Boot
