Commissioner/Commissioner Winterthur 80-100%

Transgourmet Schweiz AG — Winterthur, Winterthur, Sizzera

We are committed to our customers. We are committed to our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive private brands, professional specialist advice and innovative services.Transgourmet Schweiz AG belongs to the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in Swiss supply and pick-up wholesale and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retail).

Tasks

  • You commission customer orders on time, error-free and top quality – even in cold rooms (3-5 °C).
  • You keep set-up sequences and load the transport containers correctly.
  • You order orders for the right tour and place them at the appropriate delivery site.
  • You trigger replenishment when items are running out.
  • You support in ventures and dispose of waste professionally.
  • You work system-led and comply with all picking, QS and ASIB guidelines.

Requirements

  • You have experience in incoming goods and picking.
  • You have a valid SUVA-recognized forklift card.
  • You work with a goal-oriented, structured and with a high initiative.
  • You are physically resilient and made for cold storage rooms.
  • You know how to handle storage systems.
  • You speak good German and are reliable. Operating times
