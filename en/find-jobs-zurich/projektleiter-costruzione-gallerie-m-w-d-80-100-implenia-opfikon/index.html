Full description

As a leading Swiss construction and real estate service provider, Implenia develops, realizes and manages living spaces, working environments and infrastructure for future generations in Switzerland and Germany. Implenia also offers tunnelling and related infrastructure projects in other markets. The company combines the know-how of highly qualified consulting, development, planning and execution units under one roof into an integrated, multinationally leading construction and real estate service provider. Headquartered in Opfikon near Zurich, Implenia employs more than 8,000 people (FTE) across Europe and achieved a turnover of CHF 3.5 billion in 2025. More information at implenia.com

For the Tunneling Switzerland department, we are looking for you as a project manager to strengthen our team for Western Switzerland (and the whole of Switzerland as required).

You move that

  • Independent management of small and larger complex tunnelling projects
  • Cooperation in the acquisition
  • Responsibility for Profit + Lost of own projects
  • Resource and operational planning
  • Personnel management of construction site managers and the construction site cadre
  • Competent contact for builders, planners and business partners
  • Cooperation with local partners, subcontractors, suppliers and authorities
  • Support for projects at AVOR
  • Procurement of subcontracting services and materials in cooperation with Procurement incl. Postponement of contracts
  • Supplementary management, independent preparation and enforcement of supplementary offers
  • Financial and technical project controlling

This is why you succeed

  • Graduation and qualification: You have a degree as a civil engineer or a comparable training and already have experience in underground construction.
  • Computer skills: You are a routine, skilled user of MS Office. Experience in scheduling with MS Project or TILOS is an advantage.
  • Working method: You are used to working in a team, think analytically and networked and work independently and efficiently on the tasks assigned to you. In addition, you are characterized by a high sense of responsibility, good organizational skills and a high degree of flexibility.
  • Language skills: Good French and German skills are required. If you have additional knowledge in English and/or Italian, this is very welcome.

This is what we offer you

  • Innovative and sustainable working environment
  • Use of modern technologies
  • Support for employee development (promotion of further education by the Implenia Academy)
  • Implenia-Benefits, including (to name a few):
  • a company-owned pension fund with three contribution levels: Implenia Provision
  • 5 weeks of vacation plus 9 bridge days
  • Travel insurance for you and everyone living in your household
  • Benefits for Implenia employees on products and services

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
