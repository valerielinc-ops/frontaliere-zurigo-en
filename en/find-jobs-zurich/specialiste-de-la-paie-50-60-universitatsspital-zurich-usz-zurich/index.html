Full description

In this versatile function, you take on a central role in wage processing and support employees and managers in all wage-relevant topics. You work in a dynamic environment and actively contribute to ensuring that payroll and HR processes are implemented efficiently and correctly. You have the opportunity to contribute your expertise and develop it further.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • You are responsible for maintaining personnel and wage data, processing wage mutations and expenses and ensuring the correct handling of social insurance and withholding taxes. • For wage-relevant questions, you are the competent contact person for employees, managers and external offices such as social insurance institutions, pension funds and tax offices. • You handle the administrative processes in attendance management (AWM) and ensure the correct processing and booking of social security allowances. • You carry out the monthly reconciliations of the salary-relevant non-cash accounts and support in financial statements and revisions. • With your expertise, you are actively involved in the optimization and further development of our payroll and HRM processes. • Ideally, you will have an HR professional card and/or social security professional card and bring several years of experience in Swiss contract processing and in the HR environment. • Numbers, accuracy and a structured working method are yours – even in complex situations, you keep an overview. • With MS Office you work safely; Experience with SAP HCM is an advantage. • You work independently and reliably and at the same time appreciate the professional exchange and cooperation in the team.
