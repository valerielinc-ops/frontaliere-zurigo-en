Full description

You can get involved with us and help shape the customer advice of the future and the digitalization of banking. We are looking for you: a personality that thinks ahead. Someone with whom we as a team shape the future with fresh ideas, innovations and passion. Come on.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Bank, Wallisellen, Industriestrasse 17-19, 8304 Wallisellen

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You contact existing and potential customers to arrange consulting appointments for our customer advisors*
  • You may conduct simple consultations and transactions and inform our customers about our services and products
  • You work according to predefined campaigns as part of a consulting group and work efficiently on sales opportunities based on qualified potential analysis
  • You are responsible for the high-quality and comprehensive documentation of CRM information as well as generation of demand-oriented manual sales opportunities

Requirements

  • Mind. 1 - 2 years: in telephone or personal customer contact
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): Commercial education and financial training beneficial
  • Enjoy the active expansion of customer relationships, the recognition of customer wishes and daily telephone customer contact
  • Experience in active telephone customer acquisition and development as well as a final, communicative and professional appearance
  • Healthy level of self-confidence and a winning personality
  • Connected thinking, flexibility and teamwork are among your strengths
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • French (advantageous)
  • Italian (advantageous)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Flexible place of work / Home Office: Individual possibilities for home office
  • Child care: up to CHF 300 per month per child to external child care
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Migros Bank: accounts, mortgages and financial services with preferential terms
  • Employee offers: Annual generous well-being credit

Contatto

Application and contact Fabienne Klauser HR Business Partner

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Delayed video interview: For a large number of suitable applications, a delayed video interview can optionally be conducted. As part of this video interview, the answers to previously recorded questions are recorded with the web or mobile camera. This gives us the opportunity to get to know more candidates within a short time in order to invite suitable people for the next round. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week

Note for personnel service providers For this position, we only consider direct applications.
