Full description

You will become part of the CURE (Cloud and Undercloud Reliability Engineering) circle, a team of around 10 people that collaborates collaboratively and agilely. The team language is mainly English. Together they develop and operate the research cloud of tomorrow for the Swiss university and research landscape. Their focus is on stable, secure and scalable cloud services, as well as an engineering culture where collaboration, knowledge sharing and continuous improvement are a matter of course. These are your main responsibilities in this role:

  • You will continue to run and develop the OpenStack-based Compute platform of our SwitchCloud. You will design technical solutions, accompanying upgrades and support capacity planning and long-term technical development. You implement security standards and coordinate technical changes within the framework of reliable and transparent change management
  • You will analyze and solve complex technical problems across compute, storage and networking and work in 2nd/3rd level support. You will continue to develop monitoring and alerting, identify trends and risks at an early stage and continuously improve the reliability of our services based on operational experience and direct customer feedback
  • You develop and maintain automations for deployment, configuration management and ongoing operations. You design processes efficiently, reproducibly and maintainably, document technical changes and operational processes in a comprehensible manner and support other engineers through active knowledge transfer You should bring this
  • A university degree in computer science (uni, FH, HF) or comparable qualifications with corresponding professional experience
  • At least five years of practical experience in the engineering and productive operation of OpenStack as well as experience in securing the platform and technical troubleshooting. You work independently, take responsibility and develop sustainable solutions together with the team
  • Strong technical expertise in OpenStack, solid Linux expertise and good programming skills in Python. You bring experience with infrastructure automation, especially Ansible, as well as Git/GitLab and Docker. Basic knowledge of Kubernetes and an understanding of Monitoring, Reliability Engineering and Service Level Objectives (SLOs) complement your profile
  • Negotiable knowledge of English as well as knowledge of German or the willingness to acquire it independently Not absolutely necessary, but we are happy if you also bring the following:
  • Experience with site reliability engineering and provisioning workflows, and willingness to participate in the development of OpenStack in the community This is how we work at Switch
