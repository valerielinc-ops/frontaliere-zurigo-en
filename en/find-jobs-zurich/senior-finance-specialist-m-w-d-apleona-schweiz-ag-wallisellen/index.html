Full description

Your tasks SG&A Controlling & Planning

  • Responsibility for SG&A controlling including budget, forecast and month-end
  • Analysis of cost development, identification of deviations and derivation or Follow-up of measures together with the responsible managers
  • Responsibility for SG&A delimitations and ensuring the correct presentation in the monthly, quarterly and annual accounts

Cash and Working Capital

  • Management and analysis of the short-term cash forecast and cash development
  • Support in the management of working capital, especially in receivables management

Closing and Legal Entity Controlling

  • Participation in monthly, quarterly and annual accounts
  • Coordination and review of the financial statements of two subsidiaries
  • Responsibility for VAT accounts

Governance, Audit & Finance Projects

  • Coordination of internal and external audits and central contact point for audit bodies
  • Responsibility for monitoring, documentation and further development of the ICS as well as corresponding corporate reporting
  • Collaboration in finance projects and implementation of demanding ad hoc analyses

Your requirements

  • Completed commercial or business education, ideally with further training in finance and accounting, controlling or comparable qualifications
  • Several years of professional experience in finance, accounting and controlling
  • Experience in cost controlling and budget planning
  • Good knowledge of liquidity planning and cash forecasting
  • Very good MS Office / Excel knowledge and a high affinity for IT systems and ERP solutions
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing and good knowledge of English
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Strong analytical skills and a good understanding of business relationships
  • Communication and team skills as well as a professional approach to internal and external contacts
