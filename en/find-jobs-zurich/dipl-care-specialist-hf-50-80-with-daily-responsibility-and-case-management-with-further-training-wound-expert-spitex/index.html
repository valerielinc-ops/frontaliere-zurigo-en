Original description

Spitex Regio ZO is a progressive (non-profit) organization and provides professional and comprehensive needs-oriented care and support on behalf of the municipalities of Fehraltorf, Russikon and Weisslingen. Our goal is to enable sick, impaired or reconvicted people of all ages to live a self-determined life at home.

Dipl. Care specialist HF 50-80 % with daily responsibility and case management with further training wound expert Do you want to experience meaningful and exciting moments in your learned profession? Then we are the perfect partner for you.

These tasks await you

  • Execution of comprehensive care measures in complex client situations
  • Ensuring a good quality of care taking into account the quality requirements
  • Responsibility for meaningful and appropriate care documentation
  • Responsibility for the entire care process including needs assessments with interRAI
  • Ensuring a meaningful flow of information to supervisors, employees and other services
  • Talks with family doctors, relatives and hospitals
  • Responsible for correct electronic power measurement
  • Assuming daily responsibility
  • Responsible for the competence-based use as well as the guidance and monitoring of employees
  • Participation in internal company measures for occupational safety and health protection
  • Control of wounds
  • Support, advice and training of employees as WEX

We want you to

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Care specialist HF/FH, DN2, AKP (further training desirable e.g. wound expert)
  • Practical professional experience, preferably in a Spitex organization
  • High client and service orientation
  • High communication, self- and social skills
  • Resilient, innovative and flexible personality
  • Good computer science user knowledge
  • Experience with interRAI
  • Category B driving licence
  • Further training as a wound expert or willingness to complete it
