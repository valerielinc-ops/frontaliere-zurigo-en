Full description

With Clienia Ambulant, the Clienia Group bundles its psychiatric-psychotherapeutic services for the best possible outpatient and day-to-day clinical care for mentally ill patients of all ages – at 20 locations from Amriswil to Zurich. Interdisciplinary teams of psychotherapists, psychotherapists, nursing specialists and specialists in psychiatry and psychotherapy guarantee comprehensive outpatient treatment methods.

For our day clinic in Frauenfeld we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement one or more

For this job you like to work in a multi-professional treatment team. Multi-year institutional clinical experience is beneficial. We are psychotherapeutically oriented and treat adults with diseases from the entire psychiatric diagnosis spectrum mainly in groups and in a milieutherapeutic setting. In the sense of integrated treatment, we work in a networked manner with the External Psychiatric Services of Clienia Littenheid AG at the locations Frauenfeld and Sirnach as well as the Clienia Private Clinic Littenheid.

Their tasks To your

  • Tasks include independent management of ergotherapeutic groups, ergotherapeutic assessment and management of further group therapies.

They promote the everyday competences of patients, document the therapy processes and courses.

Your profile They bring a completed vocational training as a Dipl. occupational therapist (certificate of recognition in foreign diplomas), a high degree of independence and decision-making competence as well as the ability to actively shape interdisciplinary work in a multi-professional team.

Our offer We offer you an exciting, versatile and responsible work in a collegial team that attaches great importance to a supportive and appreciative working atmosphere. We are constantly developing our offers and supporting our employees through internal and external training opportunities as well as regular supervision. The place of work is modern, centrally located and very easy to reach from the regions of Zurich/Winterthur and Kreuzlingen/Constance.

For further information, see Colette Guillaumier, Therapeutic Director of the Day Clinic Center for External Psychiatric Services, tel. +41 71 929 67 67.
