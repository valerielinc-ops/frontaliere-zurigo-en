Full description

Do you like working at night and appreciate the calm, focused atmosphere in the hospital? In nephrology / angiology at USZ you take on responsibility in late and night service and make a significant contribution to offering our patients safety and continuity.

We offer you flexible workload options – but a minimum of 80% workload is required during the probationary period.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • Care of patients with complex clinical pictures with a focus on nephrology/angiology • Counselling and support of patients and their relatives during hospitalization • Participation in learning support in cooperation with vocational training • Participation in the construction of the new bed station for nephrological patients Requirements • You have an education as a nurse / nurse specialist HF/FH • You bring good communication skills and a quick understanding • You feel comfortable in quickly changing situations and can set priorities • You work independently, like to take responsibility for the patients: inside and are committed to the implementation of patient-oriented and professional care • You are looking forward to a newly assembled team and are actively involved in cooperation and team development • Re-entry:inside and new entrants:inside are just as welcome as experienced nurses • You work mainly on late and or night duty We offer: The extension of the department offers insight into other departments outside of nephrology and the opportunity to actively participate in a growing permanent team and to shape a common team culture. Our department specializes in peritoneal dialysis, hemodialysis takes place in another department. Two experienced heads of department and an experienced shared governance team from education and management are looking forward to working with you and supporting you in everyday work. We promote an appreciative and self-determined working environment We know that you have other priorities besides work. That is why it is important to us that we can tailor our work plans to your wishes in the best possible way. In order to ensure a well-founded familiarization, you should be able to set up a minimum of 80% workload for the first 3 months.
