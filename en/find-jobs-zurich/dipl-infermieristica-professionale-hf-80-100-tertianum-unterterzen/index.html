Full description

The charming Tertianum Blumenaupark residential and care center in Unterterzen am Walensee with 60 care beds offers people in their 4th years a comfortable home. 48 nursing guests find a comfortable home in long-term care and 12 nursing guests in the protected living area.

Does your heart beat for older people? Is the nursing profession your calling that not only improves the quality of life of others, but also creates satisfaction and meaning in your own life? Do you see opportunities to use your skills and experience to have a positive impact on the lives of our customers? Then you've come to the right place!

We are looking for someone for the Tertianum Blumenaupark in Unterterzen am Walensee immediately or by appointment

Dipl. Nursing Specialist HF 80-100%

Your tasks

  • Holistic care and support for guests in accordance with our nursing principles and the nursing process
  • Electronic care documentation using CareCoach
  • Taking on tasks as a caregiver
  • Assumption of day-to-day responsibility and coordination of the day-to-day team
  • Taking on tasks as a topic carrier for an assigned specialist topic with technical authority to give instructions

Your profile

  • Completed training to become a qualified nursing specialist HF
  • Completed vocational training course and SVEB 1 or willingness to complete these
  • Professional experience in long-term care desired
  • RAI knowledge desired
  • Enjoy a variety of challenges
  • Good knowledge of spoken and written German
