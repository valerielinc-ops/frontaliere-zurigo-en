Full description

What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family company with over 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessories as well as the provision of financial services. Emil Frey Mobility AG is a competence center for integrated mobility services for all aspects of the car. This includes vehicle insurance, but also our own financing companies. You can look forward to this: You have an eye for detail, think risk-consciously and appreciate responsibility? Then shape the future of mobility financing with us! An exciting position awaits you: you do not simply check applications, you ensure that our leasing transactions are carried out safely, efficiently and customer-oriented.

  • Examination of lease applications for completeness, plausibility and quality
  • Conducting legally compliant credit and risk analyses (GWG, KKG)
  • Processing of contract changes such as rewrites and renewals
  • Telephone & written advice for dealers, partners and end customers
  • Support of the sales force in the implementation of our sales strategy You bring:
  • You have a completed commercial education. Ideally, with experience in finance or mobility.
  • You bring professional experience in the application examination or in leasing / credit.
  • Legal requirements such as the GWG and KKG are familiar to you and you can assess credit ratings.
  • Dealing with contract documents as well as digital CRM and financing systems is easy for you.
  • You work structured, think analytically and keep track even under time pressure.
  • You communicate clearly and professionally. In German stylish, in French and/or Italian is a plus.
  • You are customer-oriented, capable of teamwork and bring the willingness to continuously develop professionally. In addition to the opportunity to shape the future of mobility and to realize auto dreams, we offer further benefits:
  • As a family-run company, we value long-term relationships and sustainable growth.
  • The opportunity to actively shape the expansion and orientation of Emil Frey Mobility AG with your ideas.
  • With us, you can expect a positive working environment based on team spirit and respect.
  • Numerous discounts (new and used cars, service work and spare parts, vehicle and health insurance, fuel cards, hotels and much more)
  • An own, attractive pension fund as an independent pension institution
  • We invest in your further education and offer you numerous development opportunities.
