Full description

80% - 100% | IT / Business Engineering | Zurich | Experienced professionals With more than 1,000 colleagues, Zürcher Kantonalbank’s IT develops its own software for the most part. In the Yoda DevOps team, you’ll work on business-critical payments applications and help ensure millions of payments and bookings are reliably processed every day. You move in an environment with a modern tech stack, high automation and plenty of room for your ideas. For us, the focus is clearly on Engineering/DevOps (about 80%), the Scrum Master role is additionally located in the team (about 20%). Your tasks

  • Further develop and operate our applications in payment transactions and customer reporting in the DevOps team
  • Development of optimal solutions for our customers in close cooperation with IT and specialist areas
  • Contribute your ideas and actively shape a motivated DevOps team
  • Taking on the Scrum Master/Agile Coach role in Team Yoda
  • Promote self-organization, continuous improvement and an open feedback culture in the team
  • Support the further automation of tests and processes to increase quality and efficiency

Your profile

  • Completed education or study in computer science or equivalent qualification
  • several years of experience in software development, ideally with Java or Kotlin
  • Knowledge of Angular is a plus
  • Experience as a Scrum Master or in a similar role in agile teams
  • Enjoy being involved in both code and team development and collaboration
  • Empathetic and communicative personality with the ability to identify challenges early and address them constructively
  • Interest in modern DevOps practices, test automation and monitoring
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • English skills are a plus

Our offer

  • Great team with open, positioned people who are looking forward to you
  • Technically diverse tasks in payment transactions and customer reporting with insight into central bank processes
  • Space for your ideas to help shape the bank of tomorrow
  • Continuous development, personal, professional and technical
  • Central and modern workplace with the possibility to organize your work flexibly
  • A du culture as well as a supportive and appreciative atmosphere in the team
  • Comprehensive package with attractive basic salary, variable compensation and versatile benefits
