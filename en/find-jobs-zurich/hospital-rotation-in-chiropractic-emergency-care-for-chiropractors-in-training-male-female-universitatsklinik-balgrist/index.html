Full description

The University Hospital offers comprehensive care for patients with musculoskeletal complaints. The Department of Chiropractic Medicine offers conservative treatment options for the musculoskeletal system with a focus on back disorders. In the orthopaedic specialist emergency, there is the opportunity to get a broad insight into the entire emergency orthopaedic and traumatological initial diagnosis and treatment. The goal is to get to know both conservative and surgical treatment options.

In cooperation between the Department of Chiropractic Medicine and the orthopaedic emergency, we offer hospital rotation for chiropractors in further education.

Possible time windows are each: 1 March-30. June, 1 July-31 October, 1 November-28 February Tasks: • On 2 days a week you are jointly responsible for the chiropractic patient care in our polyclinic and look after your personal patients with musculoskeletal complaints • On 2 to 3 days a week, together with the emergency team, you cover the care of emergency patients in close cooperation with the highly specialized orthopaedic teams. Requirements • Federal State Examination in Chiropractic • You want to continue your education in various orthopaedic disciplines • They care about interdisciplinary work and use it for the needs and concerns of people with musculoskeletal complaints. • Awareness of quality and responsibility, flexibility • German language skills, at least level B2 We offer: • You will complete the 4-month hospital rotation required for further training in chiropractic to obtain the title of specialist chiropractor/in • The training program includes case discussions, presentations, and feedback from mentors. In addition, a morning orthopaedic curriculum training will take place on the days of emergency rotation. • They are supported both by the team of the Department of Chiropractic Medicine and by the emergency team in close cooperation with orthopaedics to ensure high-quality healthcare for patients. • You will have the opportunity to work in a diverse, interprofessional setting at a university clinic with an excellent reputation. • 20% discount in Balgrist Apotheke AG • Mind. 26 holidays per year • Gifted free day for birthday • 50 % discount on the ZVV bonus pass or an annual contribution to a general subscription • Enjoy a healthy and balanced lunch at discounted prices at our company-owned staff restaurant • Free coffee/hot drinks
