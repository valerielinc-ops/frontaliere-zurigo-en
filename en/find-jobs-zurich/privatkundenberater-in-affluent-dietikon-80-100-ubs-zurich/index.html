Key responsibilities Are you an initiative, sociable personality and are you at your best when you advise customers with the help of the latest technologies?

Please contact us. We are looking for a colleague who has a flair for the concerns of the customer, feels comfortable in the digital world and takes on the following tasks:

  • You manage and process your own customer portfolio with investment, pension and mortgage customers.
  • They capture the versatile needs of our customers precisely and advise them competently.
  • They have a strong sense of optimal solutions. This ensures long-term customer relationships and scores with new customers.
  • You actively approach your customers and create a customer proximity that allows you to exploit further potential.
  • In your consulting, you also rely on innovative tools and apps. This is how you take customers on our journey into the future: you encourage them to use our digital products and support them in doing so.

The team They work in the Affluent team in Dietikon, Bahnhofplatz 13, 8953 Dietikon. We are a success-oriented and committed team with the motivation to walk the path of success together.

Our team is characterized by a strong cohesion: We tackle challenges together, support each other and celebrate successes together. In an environment where trust, openness and appreciation are lived, everyone can contribute their strengths and develop personally and professionally. In this way, we create a positive working atmosphere in which high professionalism, pleasure in consulting and sustainable success go hand in hand.

Your skills and experience

  • Commercial education, ideally with further training in banking or finance
  • Very good knowledge of the local market and the ability to build or expand a customer portfolio
  • Sound understanding in the area of investments and financing is required
  • Joy in dealing with people as well as a safe and professional appearance
  • Strong sales passion and high service orientation
  • Dedicated commitment to the success of the entire team
  • fluent German and very good knowledge of English
  • Italian and French are beneficial.
