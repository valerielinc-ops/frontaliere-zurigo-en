About us

We are a large school community in Zurich-Nord with 18 schools. We derive our pedagogical attitude from the concept of New Authority and thus meet the daily challenges in everyday school life together and effectively.

The school Hürstholz is a modern, innovative school, which is idyllic on the edge of the forest and at the same time centrally connected to the important transport lines of the city of Zurich. In addition to 12 primary classes and 4 kindergartens, the care area for all ages is also part of the school. This includes a large care facility on the school grounds as well as two external care centers. Up to 170 children are currently cared for at the school Hürstholz every day. The entire care team currently consists of 25 employees in the functions of trustee, specialist care, school assistants and 3 learners (FaBe).
