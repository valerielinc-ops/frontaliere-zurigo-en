Full description

You would like to start training as a nurse HF and are looking for a training company to complete your 2-3-day aptitude internship? Then you are exactly right with us!

Also for lateral beginners this internship and the study is ideally suited. Start your new journey with us. We accompany and support you.

Tasks

  • You actively shape the everyday life of our residents and ensure a pleasant daily structure.
  • You support the holistic care and care and learn how to deal with challenging situations.
  • You show your communication and team skills in contact with residents and in exchange with colleagues.
  • You bring motivation for the care and take the opportunity to learn more about profession and training.
  • You are willing to learn, implement instructions reliably and acquire new knowledge.

Profile

  • Interest in HF studies in nursing (full-time, part-time or shortened)
  • Open, empathetic, responsible personality
  • Minimum age 17 years and residence in Switzerland
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing (level C1 or higher)
  • Basic education at the level of EFZ, intermediate school or graduate school

We offer

After your aptitude internship, if suitable, you will have the best chances for an apprenticeship for the entire study. You decide whether to complete your studies full-time or part-time. With an EDC as a health specialist, shortened training is possible.

As a student of the Hochschule Pflege (HF) you will become part of the team and get to know the care in all its facets – from long-term care, via dementia, geriatrics, gerontopsychiatry to palliative care and rehabilitative transition care.

We offer you a professional training environment with committed vocational trainers, an open du-culture, team spirit and passion. At the City of Zurich, you benefit from attractive working conditions and comprehensive social benefits. Our houses create a special flair and the ideal environment to develop your talents.

About us

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.
