Full description

As a specialist for general internal medicine in Wolfhausen in the canton of Zurich, you take over the independent family care of your patients. They work in the entire spectrum of family medicine with their own laboratory, ultrasound, ECG and spirometry and are supported in everyday practice by medical practice assistants. The permanent position is to be filled by agreement in a charge of 80 to 100% and offers a guaranteed fixed salary and regulated working hours. What awaits you in this task

  • Independent family doctor with history, diagnosis, therapy and prevention
  • Long-term medical care for patients in outpatient setting
  • Conduct and assess diagnostic tests using laboratory, ultrasound, ECG and spirometry
  • Performing small-surgical procedures and treating chronic wounds
  • Electronic medical history and performance recording with the practice software SMR
  • Participation in internal and external quality circles and in the regional medical emergency service

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist General Internal Medicine / Specialist General Internal Medicine or an equivalent qualification recognised in Switzerland
  • Professional experience in family or outpatient medical care
  • Interest in evidence-based medicine and long-term patient care
  • Ability to work independently and independently
  • Very good knowledge of German for consultation, documentation and cooperation in the practice team

That sets them apart.

  • You make medical decisions independently and comprehensibly
  • They accompany patients in the long term from prevention to the treatment of acute and chronic diseases
  • They communicate in an understandable and binding manner with patients and relatives
  • They work in a structured manner with medical practice assistants and other specialists
  • They actively participate in professional exchange and quality assurance

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Permanent employment of 80 to 100%
  • Guaranteed fixed content
  • Regulated working hours
  • Support in everyday practice by medical practice assistants
  • Own diagnostic options with laboratory, ultrasound, ECG and spirometry
  • Costs of continuing education and training
  • Family-friendly employment conditions and social security benefits
  • Opportunity to work part-time
  • Practical location in Wolfhausen, about half an hour from Zurich

Additionally for applicants from the EU

  • Support for questions concerning MEBEKO recognition
  • Assistance in organizing travel to Switzerland
  • Assistance in finding accommodation
