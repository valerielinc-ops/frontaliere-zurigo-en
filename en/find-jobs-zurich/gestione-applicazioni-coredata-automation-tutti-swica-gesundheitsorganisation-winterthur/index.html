Full description

Do you want to actively shape the future of input management and data platforms? With us, you not only run central applications, but also continuously develop them together with business, IT and external partners. In this versatile role, you are responsible for solutions in the field of input management as well as our central data platform. You combine technical requirements with technological possibilities and actively promote digitization, automation and innovation. This is how you shape health

  • You are responsible for the application management of our input management applications as well as the central data platform
  • You control the entire lifecycle, from operations, costs and capacities to changes, releases, tests and implementation
  • You are the central contact person for specialist departments and IT, control external partners and represent SWICA’s interests vis-à-vis suppliers and providers.
  • You analyze and prioritize requirements and drive forward together with the specialist areas of digitalization and automation
  • You keep an eye on dependencies, interfaces and data flows and design cross-system solutions
  • Ensure compliance with information security, privacy, IAM, governance and business continuity requirements

This sets you apart

  • Higher specialist training in computer science or business informatics (HF/FH/University) or a comparable qualification
  • Several years of experience in application management, service management, technical product management or a comparable function
  • Good understanding of modern input management solutions, data platforms and complex system landscapes, ideally in the Microsoft Azure environment
  • Experience in controlling external IT service providers, software suppliers and service providers
  • You capture technical connections quickly and confidently mediate between business and technology
  • Experience in input management, intelligent document processing or insurance environment is an advantage
  • Very good German and English skills
