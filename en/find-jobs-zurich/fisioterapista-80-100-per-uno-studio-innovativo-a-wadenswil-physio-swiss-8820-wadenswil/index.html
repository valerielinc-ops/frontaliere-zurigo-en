As a physiotherapist: in our modern practice, you treat mainly orthopaedic complaint pictures with a lot of room for high-quality therapy. You benefit from the latest technology and an advanced infrastructure. Our interdisciplinary team culture also offers you the opportunity to develop professionally and let new impulses flow into your daily work. We are looking for a motivated, flexible and initiative personality as of 01.03.2027 or by agreement Physiotherapist (80-100%) to strengthen our young and dynamic team.

This is what awaits you with us

  • Modern equipment: Our therapy and training facility has innovative devices such as AlterG, SensoPro, isometric force measuring devices and indoor careers.
  • Interdisciplinary work: You work in a dedicated team of physiotherapy, medical massage and TCM/acupuncture and benefit from regular patient boards that enable the professional exchange and joint discussion of complex cases.
  • Time for quality: Generous administration times (1 hour/day) allow you to have detailed documentation and professional exchange.
  • Time for development: Every Friday afternoon, with our treatment plans LAB, we deliberately create space for professional development and participation. Together we continue to develop our treatment standards, discuss new ideas and actively involve the team in the further development of medimpuls.
  • Further development: We plan long-term with you and support you financially and organizationally in further education.
  • Top location & accessibility: Thanks to our central location directly at the Wädenswil train station, you can start your working day stress-free and enjoy the relaxed atmosphere on Lake Zurich during your lunch break.
  • Good work-life balance: A fair wage and sufficient recovery time are a matter of course for us.
  • Team culture: A positive working atmosphere with annual team events lets you guess that we are refreshingly uncomplicated.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training as a physiotherapist:in with SRK recognition must be available
  • Further training in sports physiotherapy or great interest in completing such or related discipline
  • As a team player, you are open and involved in the team
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation is important to you
  • A detailed (digital) documentation is a matter of course for you
