Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With more than 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric offer of Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in ambulatory, day-clinical and inpatient settings. The Group employs around 1800 people and achieved a turnover of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For our psychotherapy station A0 with treatment focus depression we are looking for 1. November 2026 or by agreement one or more

The station specializes in the treatment of people with depressive symptoms. Therapeutic work is based on a multidimensional understanding of depression. Psychotherapeutic and pharmacological treatment is based on current knowledge and focuses on the modular integration of CBASP and ACT. In addition, complementary medical offers are used. The usually open-guided maisonette station has 21 treatment places.

Their tasks

  • Accompaniment of patients on their recovery path in the context of one-on-one discussions and in daily work in the station
  • Bringing the perspective of mental illness experience and recovery into treatment-relevant areas
  • Collaboration with the core team
  • Conducting a peer-led group offer to support the personal recovery process of those affected
  • Support and networking for aftercare
  • Offered, e.g. self-help group participation in Intervisions as well as in the Peer & Recovery group

Your profile

  • You bring a high level of professional and personal competence as an expert from experience and were ideally already working as a peer in stationary setting
  • You have completed peer training (EX-IN or PMS)
  • You have an expanded understanding of your recovery history and can talk about it in a differentiated way
  • You can be a translator and facilitator for patients (e.g. answering questions about clinical pictures)
  • You can approach other people empathetically and unbiased
  • You have a high level of teamwork ability
  • You have the ability to work independently and self-organized

Our offer In addition to an exciting work area, we offer you a good introduction on the station, a versatile training offer and attractive employment conditions in a modern clinic.
