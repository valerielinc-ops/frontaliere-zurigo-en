Full description

When production stops, you ensure that everything remains clean and hygienic! You take over the leadership of our cleaning team during the night shift and ensure that our facilities meet the highest hygiene standards. With your commitment, you lay the foundation for a safe and smooth production start the next day.

  • Contract type: Perpetual
  • Pensum: 100%
  • Working time model: night shift
  • Job: from now on
  • Language: German
  • Location: Eisberg AG, Dänikerstrasse 31 8108 Dällikon

Tasks

  • You lead and coordinate 29 cleaning employees at two production sites
  • Checking and monitoring the hygiene status of production facilities and ensuring hygiene standards
  • You plan, coordinate and control the daily and periodic cleaning work
  • Ensuring compliance with safety regulations, hygiene plans and operating regulations
  • Optimization of the use of cleaning chemistry, water and personnel
  • You accompany the commissioning of new plants and ensure compliance with cleaning requirements
  • Carry out microbiological and chemical controls and take appropriate measures in case of deviations
  • You are responsible for the cleaning budget and ensure an economical use of the available resources

Requirements

  • At least 5 years of professional experience in cleaning, ideally in the food industry or a comparable industry
  • At least 2 years of leadership experience and the ability to motivate and lead employees in a goal-oriented manner
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Safe handling of MS Office
  • Teamwork and enjoyment of working together in a multicultural environment
  • An own vehicle, as this is absolutely necessary for the exercise of the function
  • Readiness for permanent night work and regular day and weekend assignments
  • You convince through motivation, initiative and reliability.
  • An efficient, careful and accurate working method as well as a pronounced hygiene and quality awareness round off your profile.

This is what we offer

  • Food: Cuisine is very important to us: Freshly prepared salads, soups, tea and fruit are available to our employees free of charge. In addition, you can take home products from the personal shelf every day.
  • Mobility: Benefit from convenient connections to public transport, attractive discounts for public transport and sufficient parking – sustainable and worry-free on the road!
  • Professional development opportunities: Expand your knowledge through extensive further education offers, high-quality training in the Coop Education Center and various personal development opportunities such as mentoring and coaching.
  • Discounts & Discounts: You benefit from the Coop Personal Discount Card with various discounts in the retail trade, in specialty shops and supermarkets as well as from other attractive services, for example in the area of insurance.
  • Premiums, social benefits, holidays: You benefit from 5 weeks of holidays, from the age of 50 even from 6 weeks. The pension fund contributions are borne 2/3 by the company and 1/3 by the employees. If the employee recommendation is successful, you will receive a premium of CHF 1,000. –
