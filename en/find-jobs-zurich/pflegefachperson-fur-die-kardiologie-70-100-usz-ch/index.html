Full description

The professional care and care of our patients: at our department of cardiology is a matter of the heart. An exciting patient clientele awaits you, which is primarily involved in the treatment of grafical diagnostics and therapy. Among other things, patients are treated: in front of and after transcatheter aortic valve implants, coronarangiographies or razological interventions. In addition, we care for patients: innate heart defects. The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland.

Our over 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees are committed to the well-being of our patients every day.

Tasks

  • Care and care for cardiological patients, including patients from other areas of expertise requiring telemetry monitoring
  • Responsibility for the quality of care, as well as for the control and monitoring of the care process, in cooperation with a health specialist in tandem
  • Participation in the practical training of our foster offspring
  • Co-design of the department and co-responsibility for the achievement of the departmental objectives

Requirements

  • You are a nurse or nurse RF / FH or you are completing your studies this year
  • You are interested in the care and care of cardiological patients: female at a universitary level
  • You feel comfortable in fast changing situations
  • Good and close teamwork is for you, of course, in everyday work
  • You want to develop yourself professionally and personally

We offer

  • A young, dynamic and highly motivated care team that is looking forward to welcoming you to the team soon
  • A trusting and appreciative, interdisciplinary cooperation at eye level
  • An exciting patient clientele and a department where you can learn new things every day
  • A four-person management team that attaches great importance to participation and individual employee development
