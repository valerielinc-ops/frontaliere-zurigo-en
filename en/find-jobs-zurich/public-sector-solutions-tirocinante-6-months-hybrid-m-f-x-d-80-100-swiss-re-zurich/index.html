Full description

Are you passionate about the intersection of public policy, risk management, and innovative financial solutions? Do you want to gain real-world experience working with governments, municipalities, and international organisations — while contributing to meaningful outcomes that strengthen financial resilience across the public sector? If so, this internship could be the perfect next step in your career journey. About the Role As an Intern in the Public Sector Solutions (PSS) team at Swiss Re, you'll be right in the action. Over six months, you'll take ownership of specific workstreams, engage with public sector clients and stakeholders, and contribute to the development of innovative insurance solutions. From parametric insurance projects to public tender strategies and client events, this is a hands-on experience that will give you a genuine edge in the world of risk, finance, and public sector engagement. Key Responsibilities

  • Market Research & Business Development: Conduct in-depth market research including stakeholder mapping, analysis of financial and loss histories, and identification of relevant risks, trends and topics to support targeted client outreach
  • Public Tenders: Gather and analyse information on public insurance tenders, identify relevant opportunities, and support the development of Corporate Solutions' public tender strategy in select European markets
  • Client Engagement: Prepare and support meetings with public sector clients and stakeholders — including cities/municipalities, governments, and international organisations — by developing briefings, pitch materials, coordinating logistics, documenting discussions and following up on key actions
  • Innovative Insurance Solutions: Take ownership of at least one innovative insurance project, such as the development of a parametric solution for a municipality, coordinating across internal teams including PSS Technical Sales, Reinsurance, Underwriting and Legal
  • Public Sector Event Organisation: Support the end-to-end planning and delivery of a Swiss Re event for selected Swiss public sector clients in H1 2027, including concept development, speaker and participant identification, content preparation and internal coordination
  • Team Contribution: Support ongoing PSS business development activities, pipeline management, and preparation of internal and external materials as needed

The internship is scheduled for 6 months starting ideally in January 2027. About the Team The Public Sector Solutions (PSS) team is a dedicated, specialist unit within Swiss Re's Corporate Solutions function, focused on serving public sector clients and brokers across Switzerland and internationally. The team addresses the unique and complex challenges faced by governments, municipalities, federal authorities, and international organisations — combining trusted risk insights, cutting-edge data analytics, and tailored risk transfer solutions to deliver real impact. By acting as a bridge between public sector clients and the full capabilities of the Swiss Re Group, the PSS team transforms client needs into tangible outcomes, helping public institutions build stronger financial resilience in the face of natural catastrophes and evolving risk landscapes. The team operates in a collaborative, entrepreneurial environment where curiosity, ownership, and cross-functional engagement are genuinely valued.
