Full description

Are you interested in IT topics in the financial industry, but do not want to work with outdated technologies, IT infrastructures and methods? Perhaps you believe that working in the financial industry is characterized by rigid hierarchies, little freedom and a lack of innovation?

We are here to convince you otherwise!

As an Avaloq parameterization specialist, you support the expansion, operation and maintenance of our Avaloq core banking system at Vontobel. They actively participate in ongoing projects and act as an interface to the business. In addition, you take responsibility for parts of the application, master operational problems and analyze the requirements together with the specialist departments. A team of long-term employees is at your side with advice and deeds in both German and English.

What role would you play?

  • Avaloq parameterization in the Front Basis team with a focus on investment performance, portfolio management and business analysis
  • Solution design, programming, parameterization and testing.
  • Operational tasks – handling production incidents and requests.

What do we offer?

  • Exciting projects with the opportunity to take responsibility.
  • Direct communication channels instead of hierarchical levels simplify solution and decision-making.
  • Development opportunities and insights into various areas of banking.
  • Top location in Zurich close to Paradeplatz with collaboration spaces and roof terraces.
  • Free lunch, fruit and coffee.
  • Teamwork is important to us: We always exchange valuable knowledge and skills with each other - "One Vontobel".
  • Possibility for internal and external training.
  • Market-friendly remuneration (including bonus) and excellent social benefits.
  • Possibility of home office and flexible working hours.
  • On request, an additional ten days of unpaid leave per year can be obtained.

What are we looking for?

  • Avaloq parameterization experience, preferably in the investment performance and portfolio management field, and willingness to acquire new knowledge.
  • Focus on the essentials and enthusiasm for new technologies.
  • Ability to take responsibility and maintain an independent working method.
  • Good knowledge of German and English is a prerequisite.

At Vontobel, you are building a better future for our customers and society – and for yourself. By 2030, we want to be known as one of the leading and most trusted global investment firms. We employ more than 2,000 people at 28 locations worldwide and have our headquarters in Zurich.

Our diverse team values different perspectives, and we are looking for people who want to make a difference in a dynamic and changing world. Even if you do not meet all criteria, we look forward to your application if you are enthusiastic about the job. Do you want to become Vontobel?
