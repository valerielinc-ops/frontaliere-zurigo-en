Full description

Start a varied and meaningful training with us! Your future is important to us. Our experienced vocational trainers will accompany and support you throughout your entire training.

We are looking for someone for the Tertianum Blumenaupark in Unterterzen as of August 1, 2027

Apprentice / Apprentice Cook / Chef EFZ

You'll learn that with us

  • Work in various areas of the kitchen as per curriculum
  • Set-up work and storage: You ensure that all ingredients are fresh and in stock
  • Care and cleanliness: Hygiene is particularly important; with us you will learn about the HACCP regulation and how it is implemented in the kitchen

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed school education (secondary school A or B) with good academic performance
  • Great willingness to learn, sense of responsibility as well as hygiene awareness and sense of order
  • Interest in cooking and enjoyment of handling food
  • Helpful, outgoing and team-oriented, with a strong sense of care and conscientiousness
  • Willingness to work irregular hours
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoy diverse challenges and practical work
  • Good knowledge of spoken and written German

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
