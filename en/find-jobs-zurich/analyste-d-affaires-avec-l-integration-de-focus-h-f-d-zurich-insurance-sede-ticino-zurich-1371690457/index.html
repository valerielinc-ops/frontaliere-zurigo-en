Full description

136702 | 80 - 100% | Oerlikon, Zurich | Hybrid

Is the idea of having an influence on the design of the Zurich strategy and the promotion of corresponding initiatives inspired you? Do you enjoy penetrating complex system landscapes and enabling smooth end-to-end processes? Then you are exactly right with us!

In your role as Business Analyst at Zurich Switzerland, you will strengthen the Information & Technology division in the Solution Domain P&C and make a decisive contribution to joint success with your work.

You will become part of one of Zurich Switzerland’s most important strategic P&C initiatives, in which we design and implement future products, processes and technologies. As a business analyst with a focus on integration, you play a central role at the interface between the professional and IT worlds: you analyze processes and system interfaces, create well-founded integration specifications and work together with development teams and stakeholders to ensure that our systems work seamlessly together along the value chain.

This is what awaits you

  • You will analyze and document process and interface requirements between Guidewire and adjacent Zurich systems along the P&C value chain
  • Create precise integration specifications, such as API definitions, data flows and mappings, as the basis for implementation by development teams
  • You’ll work closely with product owners, solution architects, development teams and subject matter experts to ensure smooth end-to-end processes
  • Translate complex integration requirements into actionable user stories and support the team during implementation, integration tests and go-live
  • You coordinate requirements across different systems, teams and departments and are the central contact for integration issues

This sets you apart

  • You have completed studies, for example in business informatics, computer science or business administration, or a comparable qualification as well as several years of experience as a business analyst or requirements engineer in an agile environment.
  • You will bring deep experience in the analysis and specification of system integrations, such as REST/SOAP APIs, event-based architectures, message queues, data flows and mappings
  • You have proven experience in the insurance environment, ideally with Guidewire systems such as PolicyCenter, BillingCenter, Jutro Digital Platform or Integration Gateway and their integration into complex IT architectures
  • You convince with strong analytical and conceptual skills, a structured way of working and the ability to convey technical issues appropriately to the addressee
  • You have very good knowledge of German and/or English at level C1/C2
