Institute for Molecular Cancer Research

Job opening 01. January 2027 or by arrangement, temporary

The Institute for Molecular Cancer Research (IMCR) is a dual institute of the Medical and Natural Science Faculty of the University of Zurich with approximately 55 employees. The institute is located on the Irchel campus and offers a cutting-edge research infrastructure. In addition, IMCR employees have access to the central facilities of the University of Zurich and ETH Zurich. Scientists from all over the world work at the institute, which combines a common passion for research into DNA repair mechanisms and basic cancer research. For the research group Sartori we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by appointment a dedicated, independent and motivated personality to strengthen our team. The post covers a workload of 80-100% and is limited to one year initially, with the possibility of renewal by agreement. Further information about the institute and the Sartori research group can be found on the IMCR website.

Tasks

  • Cell culture and cell line development: cultivation and maintenance of mammalian cell cultures in the sterile laboratory and establishment of stable, monoclonal cell lines.
  • Experimental research: planning, conducting and evaluating experiments in ongoing research projects using various molecular and cell biology methods, including Western blotting, colony formation assays and fluorescence microscopy.
  • Data analysis and presentation: Evaluation and interpretation of experimental results as well as creation of scientific graphics and presentations to present the research results.
  • Laboratory management and organisation: ordering laboratory materials, carrying out sterility and quality controls, maintenance and maintenance of laboratory equipment, as well as ensuring smooth laboratory operation and taking over general institute tasks.

Profile

We are looking for a dedicated, independent and well-organized personality with a master’s degree in biology, biomedicine or a related natural science discipline.

  • Practical laboratory experience, in particular in the cultivation of mammalian cells and the application of molecular biological methods, is advantageous.
  • They have strong organizational, communicative and social skills.
  • We attach great importance to a proactive, reliable and flexible way of working, a high level of problem-solving skills and the ability to efficiently coordinate multiple tasks at the same time.
  • They work both independently and like in a team and feel comfortable in an international research environment.
  • Very good German and English skills in word and writing are required.
  • Initial experience in laboratory management, in particular in the coordination of everyday laboratory life, material management and the ordering of laboratory requirements, is advantageous.
