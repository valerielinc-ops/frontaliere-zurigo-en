Full description

Repairs and maintenance of sun and weather protection systems are among your main tasks. The company supports you with modern tools and a well-equipped service bus. You will help customers competently on site and ensure that the systems function perfectly. We are looking for a job for our client:

  • You will independently carry out repair, service and maintenance work on sun and weather protection systems such as blinds, roller shutters, awnings and slat systems
  • You will analyse faults and efficiently rectify mechanical and electrical defects on site at the customer's site
  • You will professionally replace wearing parts such as motors, controls, belts or articulated arms
  • You will ensure the proper functioning and adjustment of the systems, including final inspection
  • You will advise customers competently on repair solutions, spare parts and possible modernisations
  • You will document your operations cleanly and report on the work carried out
  • Requirements
  • Training as a blind fitter EFZ or several years of professional experience
  • Driving licence Cat. B
  • Independent work as well as customer advice on site
  • Fluent Swiss German or very good High German
  • Well-groomed appearance Offer
  • Young, motivated team with a flat hierarchy
  • Quality over quantity
  • Own, fully equipped service bus
  • High-quality tools
  • Further training opportunities
  • Very good working atmosphere
  • Very good pension fund benefits
  • and much more
