About the role

Job Description

Serve as a senior technical expert within the Workplace Services team, providing advanced (L2 + L3) support and operational excellence across Microsoft 365 technologies - including Exchange (Hybrid/Online), Intune, Teams & Enterprise Voice, Microsoft SharePoint and Entra ID. This role ensures secure, reliable, and efficient digital workplace experiences for a global enterprise, driving collaboration, communication, and endpoint management.

Main accountabilities and tasks

  • Provide Level 2 and Level 3 support for Exchange, Intune, Teams/Enterprise Voice, Microsoft SharePoint and related M365 services, ensuring timely and effective resolution of incidents and service requests.
  • Collaborate with IT, security, and business teams to deliver seamless digital workplace experiences.
  • Develop and maintain technical documentation (HLDs, LLDs, SOPs) for Workplace Services technologies.
  • Automate routine administrative tasks using PowerShell and other tools to improve efficiency.
  • Enforce security, compliance, and data protection policies across all supported platforms.
  • Participate in change management, patching, and upgrade projects for M365 services.
  • Mentor and collaborate with local IT teams, contribute to knowledge base articles, and lead training sessions to ensure consistent standards and best practices across the organization.
  • Continuously review and improve processes, driving automation, standardization, and operational excellence.
  • Support and troubleshoot Microsoft Teams Meeting Room (MTR) devices, including certified hardware such as Yealink and other Teams‑certified vendors.
  • Handle escalations related to audio, video, call quality, and meeting room reliability.

Support Teams resource accounts and room mailboxes in coordination with Exchange Online.

  • Work with Network and Local IT teams to ensure stable connectivity and operational readiness of meeting room environments.
