Full description

Troubleshooting and maintenance of complex facilities are the focus of this function. The company attaches importance to technical improvements and cooperative cooperation. For our client we are looking for an activity

  • Removal of faults at mill plants, high-bay warehouses and packaging plants in production
  • Carrying out maintenance work on production, intralogistics and building technology systems
  • Support in technical projects and commissioning of new or refurbished plants
  • Identification of potential for improvement and implementation of corresponding measures in operation
  • Coordinating and monitoring cooperation with external craft partners:
  • Requirements
  • Completed basic technical training as an automatic technician:in, electrical installer:in or equivalent degree
  • First practical experience in a comparable position of advantage
  • Commitment to actively participate in technical developments in production
  • Willingness to participate in the Pikettdienst
  • Team-oriented personality with careful, conscientious work and strong service awareness Offer
  • Modern working environment with versatile technical facilities
