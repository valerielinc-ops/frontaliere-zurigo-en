Would you like to develop viable digital solutions together with the specialist areas and keep an eye on the existing applications?   • Coordinating technical solution development and integration with the specialist and ICT centres involved
  • Help shape the ICT reference architecture and implement architecture, governance and security requirements Your profile
  • Tertiary education (FH/uni) in business informatics or computer science
