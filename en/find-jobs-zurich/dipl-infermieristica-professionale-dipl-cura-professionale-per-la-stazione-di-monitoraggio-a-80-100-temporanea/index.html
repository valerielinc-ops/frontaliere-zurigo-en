Full description

Location: Hirslanden Clinic in the Park | Zurich

Occupation by: 01 October 2026 to 28. February 2027 or by agreement

Type of employment: Temporary

Reference number: 73608

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

When every second counts, your knowledge makes the difference.

At our monitoring station in Hirslanden Klinik In the park you look after patients in a sensitive phase after surgery. With attention, precision and a high sense of responsibility, you work in a demanding environment in which modern medicine, interdisciplinary cooperation and highly specialized care are intertwined on a daily basis. Through your professional expertise and your prudent actions, you make a decisive contribution to conveying safety, stability and trust to our patients.

YOUR TASKS

  • Monitoring, care and care of patients in the monitoring station in all medical specialties
  • Responsibility for postoperative monitoring of vital signs as well as operation-specific care measures
  • Independent performance of the diagnostic, therapeutic and prophylactic measures prescribed by the doctor
  • Ensure proper performance recording and maintenance documentation
  • Identification of emergency situations and initiation of adequate measures until the arrival of the doctor

Your profile

  • Basic training dipl. Nursing professional (FH/HF) with further training in intensive care, anesthesia or IMC or willingness to complete the IMC course
  • Empathy in dealing with patients and their relatives
  • Customer and service orientation as well as resilience, teamwork and flexibility (shift work 24/7)
  • Willingness to develop your expertise and make an important contribution to the success of our clinic

You can expect a motivated, committed and multi-professional team in an advanced working environment. This is characterized by high quality of care, independence, flexibility and a sense of responsibility. We also offer our employees generous social benefits, sports offers, special insurance conditions and further education opportunities as part of our program.
