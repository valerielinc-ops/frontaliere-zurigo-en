Full description

Key responsibilities

  • lead the architecture, engineering and continuous optimization of our diverse HSM landscape, ensuring full adherence to security and compliance standards
  • automate the HSM environment and optimize configurations and related processes to meet demanding SLAs
  • monitor current trends and technologies in the HSM space and evaluate their potential and applicability for our organization
  • drive the further development of the HSM environment and design solutions in close collaboration with application projects
  • analyze issues, resolve incidents and optimize performance in close cooperation with infrastructure and application teams
  • participate in an on-call rotation for technical incidents and access to key material as part of a four-person HSM support team
  • take on selected network-related tasks to support colleagues in the areas of firewall, load balancing and switching/routing, with the opportunity to further develop your skills within a team of network specialists

The team To strengthen our IT Platform Services department, we are looking for an experienced, service-oriented HSM Engineer (f/m/d) with project management experience. We offer a dynamic and exciting environment where you can actively contribute, as well as a highly motivated team and a collaborative working atmosphere.

Your skills and experience

  • university degree or equivalent education in computer science or a related field
  • several years of experience in designing and managing complex projects
  • solid technical experience with the following products: Thales Luna, Payshield 10k, KeySecure/CTM, ProtectServer 2/3, Azure Key Vault
  • willingness to continue learning and to take on challenges in a demanding IT environment
  • experience with network technologies such as firewalls (Fortigate), load balancing (F5) and switching/routing (Cisco/Arista) is an advantage
  • experience as a Product Owner or in requirements management, ideally with knowledge of agile methods (e.g. Scrum, SAFe), is a plus
  • strong analytical skills, a collaborative mindset and excellent communication abilities
  • business-fluent German and English skills

About us UBS Card Center AG with its headquarters in Glattbrugg and an additional office in Manno TI is part of UBS Switzerland AG and employs over 750 people. As the leading credit card processor in Switzerland, we process over 3 million Visa and Mastercard credit cards. In doing so, we stand close by our customers' side - with experience, commitment and vision.

Join us At UBS Card Center we embrace flexible working arrangements and offer different working models such as part-time, job-sharing and hybrid working. Our purpose-driven culture and global infrastructure help us connect and collaborate in agile ways to meet all our business needs. A working environment driven by innovation, digitalization, new mobile payment methods and customer experience awaits you.
