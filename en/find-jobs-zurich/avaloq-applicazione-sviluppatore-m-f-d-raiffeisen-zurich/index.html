12.08.2026 Zurich St. Gallen

Become part of our team!

As a competent and innovative solution provider, the Custody & Securities team supports the business in the implementation of projects and projects. It is responsible for the development and implementation of software solutions and operational change management. For our locations at Zurich Airport and in St. Gallen, we are looking for a committed and reliable personality as an Avaloq application developer (m/f/d).

What awaits you?

  • Design and implementation of innovative solutions within the Raiffeisen Avaloq banking platform
  • Development of solution designs together with specialists from business and IT
  • 3rd level support for our customers in the assigned subject areas
  • Expansion and maintenance of Corporate Actions (CA), Valors and Settlement

What do you bring?

  • Several years of experience in Avaloq development, ideally experience in Corporate Actions (CA), Valors and/or Settlement
  • Successfully completed Avaloq certification
  • Very good knowledge of PL/SQL and Oracle SQL
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Experience in dealing with data models, data migrations and the implementation of interface solutions
  • Rapid comprehension of complex relationships and dependencies as well as strong analytical skills
