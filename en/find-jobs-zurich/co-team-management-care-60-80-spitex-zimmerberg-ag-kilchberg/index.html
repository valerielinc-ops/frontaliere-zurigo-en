Full description

Do you like to take responsibility, inspire your team and actively shape the care? Then bring your passion for care and team leadership to us. We are looking for you at the beginning of September 2026 or by appointment as co-team leader care at the Kilchberg/Rüschlikon location. Co-team management Care 60%-80 %

Your tasks

  • In co-management with another team management, you take on the personnel, professional and organizational responsibility for a care team with around 20 employees
  • Together with your team, you ensure professional, customer-oriented care
  • You ensure good networking and cooperation with other specialist areas
  • You take over the day-to-day business punctually, e.g. customer receptions in close cooperation with the route planning and the day-to-day responsibility
  • In addition to your management task, you can also jump into customer assignments yourself if necessary and bring your specialist and social skills into direct contact with our customers:
  • You actively participate in the further development of high-quality care and are committed to a good practical training of learners and students

Requirement profile

  • Completed training (HF/FH)
  • Management training or willingness to undertake such training
  • Enjoyment of a varied working environment as well as independent and self-responsible work
  • Networked thinking, high social skills and strong communication skills
  • Resilient, independent leader with the courage to make clear decisions
  • Responsibility for the entire care process of your customers
  • Safe handling of digital work equipment (Swing)
  • Driving licence Cat. B

This is what we offer you

  • Holidays : 27 days holidays (from 50 years 32 days)
  • BVG : Flexible choice between the “Mini” and “Maxi” (AXA) plans
  • Diversity: A responsible, varied and independent activity
  • Working environment: A dynamic, growing environment with a motivated and collaborative team
  • Further development: Support for further education and other attractive advantages
  • Occasions : Large employee party twice a year
