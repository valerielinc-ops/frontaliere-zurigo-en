Canton of Zurich Financial Directorate Office for Computer Science Platform Engineer KI 80 - 100% The Office for Computer Science (AFI) is the computer science competence center of the Canton of Zurich. It provides comprehensive services in the area of central computer infrastructure and canton-wide applications for directorates, authorities, the administration of justice and independent institutions. Artificial intelligence (AI) offers great potential to effectively support administration and provide its services to the population and businesses efficiently, securely and appropriately. At the same time, clear framework conditions, reliability and responsible handling of data, security and ethical issues are required. In the Canton of Zurich, we therefore promote the safe, responsible and value-adding use of AI. A central component is the AI service platform, on which, among other things, the basic AI services are operated. Are you ready to harness the potential of AI in the Canton of Zurich and thus drive the digital transformation of the Canton of Zurich? For this purpose, we are looking for AI specialists who will help shape the development and development of the AI service platform and the associated AI solutions. Your tasks

  • Development, integration and operation of productive AI applications and models on the cantonal AI service platform
  • Support of the responsible departments in the technical implementation and integration of AI use cases and participation in proofs of concept
  • Ensuring stable, scalable, secure and economically operated operating processes for AI solutions
  • Establishment and operation of standardized MLOps and deployment processes
  • Development and documentation of technical standards and reusable components
  • Advising the specialist areas on the technical implementation and integration of AI use cases Your profile
  • University degree in computer science, comparable education or equivalent practical experience
  • In-depth knowledge of MLOps, deployment and productive use of complex technical platforms
  • Safety in dealing with architectural issues, technical operation and interface work.
  • Clear, solution-oriented and addressable communication in German and English.
  • Strong willingness to continuously familiarize yourself with new AI and cloud technologies. What we offer you
  • A technically challenging role with a direct contribution to the cantonal AI implementation
  • Collaboration on a new platform with high relevance for the entire administration
  • Close cooperation with specialists from different disciplines
  • Space for technical standards, quality and further development
  • An open development culture based on the principle of "Public Money, Public Code" and Swiss practice according to EMBAG: We provide our own components as open source wherever possible and make contributions to the open source software used.
  • A solid, fair and secure employer with modern workplaces near Zurich Central Station.
  • In addition to lunch check, home office opportunities and flexible working hours, we also offer internal training and further education opportunities and actively support the further development of our employees. Ready to take on your new challenge? If we have already sparked your enthusiasm, we look forward to your application. Please note that we only accept and process applications that are received via the online tool. Questions about the job are answered by Kevin Mauchle (Recruiting Partner). Tel. +41 43 257 60 43.
