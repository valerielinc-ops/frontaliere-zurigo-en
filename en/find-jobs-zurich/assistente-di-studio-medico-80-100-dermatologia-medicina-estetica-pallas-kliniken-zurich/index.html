Full description

The Pallas Clinics are specialized in ophthalmology and aesthetics. With over 350 employees and 17 locations, we are one of the leading providers in Switzerland.

Our medical staff plays an indispensable role in the customer care process. They act as direct contacts and provide professional and individual support. The focus is always on the well-being of our customers. Through close cooperation with our specialists, we guarantee a holistic supply. Our team is characterized by high commitment, regular training and the pleasure of the interdisciplinary cooperation with our customers.

In order to strengthen our team in Zurich, we are looking for a motivated/- n

Medical assistant in 80-100% (dermatology, aesthetic medicine)

Responsibility

  • Professional customer and patient support
  • Ensuring a smooth daily routine and practical operation
  • Assistance in medical treatments in the fields of aesthetic medicine and dermatology (e.g. assistance in all treatments of soft aesthetics)
  • Independent implementation of medical prescriptions (e.g. blood sampling, change of association, etc.)
  • Self-responsible implementation of aesthetic services (e.g. cryolipolysis etc.)
  • Advice and active sales of care and cosmetic products
  • Support for doctors in administrative tasks

Requirements

  • Completed training as medical/practice assistant/EFZ or similar
  • Several years of professional experience as a medical assistant, mandatory in the field of aesthetic medicine and dermatology
  • High customer orientation and pleasure in providing high-quality services
  • Good performance and proper manners
  • Enjoyment of working in a team
  • Initiative, thinking personality who likes to take responsibility
  • Readiness for monthly Saturday services
  • Very good PC skills, fluent German skills and good English skills, all other languages are advantageous

Benefits

  • Varied and challenging activity with a lot of personal responsibility and independence
  • Special conditions within Pallas Kliniken AG
  • Family culture
  • Various trainings and workshops
  • Assistance with various events
  • Workplace at the new Pallas Klinik Aesthetics in the heart of Zurich
