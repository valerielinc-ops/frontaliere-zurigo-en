Full description

SRG German-speaking Switzerland (SRG. D) is one of the four regional companies of the SRG SSR association, which is the largest media company in Switzerland. With around 17,000 members, SRG. D anchors the company in society and advocates for the concerns and needs of the population. It accompanies the journalistic work of Swiss Radio and Television (SRF), supports the transformation of SRG SSR and promotes public discourse on the public media service.

The Audience Council plays an important role in this context. At the heart of its work is the Resonanzraum – a physical and virtual meeting place where people from different walks of life and opinions come together in dialogue on programme-related topics, including with SRF programmers. In addition, the Audience Council is the electoral body of the Ombudsman's Office.

The Regional Executive Board of SRG. D has realigned the Audience Council as of October 2026: more focused on its mandate, with increased management responsibility in the Executive Committee and professional support from the office. In addition to the Executive Committee, we are looking for another committed member of the management team of the SRG.D Audience Council as of 1 January 2027.

This is your contribution

As a full member of the management team, you will shape the substantive work of the Audience Council together with the Presidium and the other members. Your main tasks are:

  • Active participation in the management team and co-creation of the annual planning, selection of topics and working methods of the Audience Council
  • Preparation and implementation of dialogue and participation formats within the framework of the Resonance Room
  • Collaboration on programme-related issues, in exchange with SRF programme creators and in the election of the ombudsman's office
  • Regional networking and representation of the Public Council in German-speaking Switzerland vis-à-vis member societies and other stakeholder groups

This is how you inspire us

  • Independent and honest personality with no current or terminated employment or mandates at SRF or SRG SSR in the last two years and no relevant conflicts of interest.
  • Interest in the Swiss media system, the public service mandate and public discourse, as well as the ability to assess facts independently.
  • Willingness to represent different population groups, regions and living environments and to bring their concerns into the work of the Audience Council
  • Experience in militia, association, association or civil society structures is an advantage.
  • Time availability of around 300 hours per year (approx. 6 hours per week), including evening appointments and sessions
  • German at least level C2.

This is how we inspire you

  • Active participation in shaping a renewed body with a clear social mandate
  • Insight into the work of Swiss Radio and Television and direct dialogue with programme creators
  • Networking with committed personalities from all over German-speaking Switzerland
  • Compensation in accordance with SRG regulations. D

Our Promise

What does it take to reach people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expertise, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can get involved and develop. In order for the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: Welcome to the world of SRG!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, promote creativity and offer a lot of creative freedom for new and high-quality things.
  • Shaping digitalization: For us, quality journalism is also digital. That's why we explore new possibilities and experiment with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-media. We design at the cutting edge and on the nerve of the audience.
  • Being human: We treat each other in a collegial manner, work in a self-determined manner and help shape our working atmosphere in a very individual way. Our success is based on the personal strengths as well as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programmes. In doing so, we act respectfully and independently; in this way, we promote opinion-forming and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.
