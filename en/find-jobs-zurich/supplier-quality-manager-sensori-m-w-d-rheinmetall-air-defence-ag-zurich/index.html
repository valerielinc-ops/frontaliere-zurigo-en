Full description

Tasks

  • Organization, planning, implementation and documentation of quality workshops with suppliers and supplier audits
  • Tracking and ensuring the measures derived from quality workshops and supplier audits
  • Organizing, planning, supporting and documenting initial sample tests at suppliers including upstream quality planning (APQP, PFMEA, PPAP)
  • Review of statements (4D report) and 8D reports from suppliers
  • Comprehensive support and documentation of the respective supplier development
  • Support in the operational processing of supplier complaints in SAP

Requirements

  • Mandatory advanced knowledge in the areas of sensors and electronics
  • Successfully completed technical apprenticeship
  • Relevant professional experience in quality management/supplier management, ideally in industry
  • Strong negotiation skills and proven ability to build and maintain supplier relationships
  • Experience in purchasing and with SAP is an advantage
  • Strong communication skills, intercultural competence and enjoyment of teamwork
  • Very good knowledge of spoken and written German as well as good knowledge of English

We offer

  • A dynamic working environment with exciting projects
  • Flexible working hours (flexitime and trust working hours)
  • Very good social benefits and a non-mandatory pension fund
  • Employee benefits of a large corporation, employee share purchase program
  • Promoting needs-based further training
  • Staff restaurant as well as external catering and shopping options nearby
  • Individual internal and external personnel development measures in collaboration with our Rheinmetall Academy
  • Workplace a few minutes' walk from Zurich-Oerlikon train station
