Full description

Do you want to shape challenging construction projects in interior design and take on responsibility? Then you are exactly right with us. In this role, you coordinate complex developments and ensure that quality, deadlines and costs go hand in hand.

You can move that with us.

  • Independent management and coordination of challenging development projects (especially office buildings, retail and catering facade and roof renovations)
  • Responsibility for cost, deadline and quality management
  • Monitoring of projects from execution planning to handover
  • Leadership of entrepreneurs, specialist planners and external partners
  • Ensure smooth communication between all project participants
  • Preparation of building applications and clarifications with authorities

That's what makes you.

  • Training as a construction manager:in, high-end designator:in or equivalent technical training
  • At least 3 years of professional experience in Switzerland
  • Strong architectural understanding and high structural demands
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing, French is an advantage
  • CAD knowledge is advantageous
  • Knowledge of Messerli Baua and MS Project is an advantage
  • Messerli Baua
  • MS Project

That's what we offer you.

  • Exciting projects with a great creative and organizational influence
  • Attractive employment conditions and development opportunities
