Senior Procurement Specialist — ANYbotics

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

ANYbotics is a fast-growing tech company dedicated to shaping the future of mobile robotics across multiple industries. Join our highly talented and motivated team of more than 200 people and work on cutting-edge robot technology.

The Opportunity

At ANYbotics, we build autonomous, end-to-end robotic solutions that automate routine industrial inspections in complex and hazardous environments. As a Senior Procurement Specialist , you will play a pivotal, high-ownership role in shaping the supply chain foundation for our next-generation robotics platform.

Operating at the intersection of strategic sourcing and industrialization, you will drive critical procurement activities from early prototype phases straight through to full production ramp-up. You won't just be managing transactional purchase orders—you will build scalable procurement processes from scratch, secure high-performing global supplier networks, and directly influence the cost, quality, and delivery of cutting-edge robotic hardware.

The Market & Our Technology

Industrial facilities present some of the world's most severe operating conditions. Our autonomous quadrupedal platforms navigate multi-story plants, stairs, and hazardous areas—delivering continuous operational insight without human risk. Bringing a next-generation, high-complexity mechatronic product like ANYmal from concept to serial production demands a resilient, agile, and cost-effective supply chain. Sourcing high-precision mechanical parts, advanced electronics, and specialized electromechanical systems requires a strategic procurement leader who thrives in fast-paced hardware environments.

Your Impact • Establish and execute the end-to-end procurement strategy for the new ANYmal project across both prototype and production phases • Secure critical suppliers and manage complex global sourcing networks across Europe and Asia • Lead high-stakes RFQs, contract negotiations, and cost-optimization initiatives to meet ambitious target costs and timelines • Identify, assess, and proactively mitigate single-source and supply chain risks for high-criticality components • Drive supplier qualification, capacity planning, and onboarding in close alignment with industrialization roadmaps • Partner cross-functionally with Engineering, Operations, Quality, and Supply Chain teams to ensure seamless NPI transition

Your Profile • 10+ years of hands-on experience in procurement, strategic sourcing, and supplier management within a complex industrial or hardware environment (robotics, automotive, aerospace, electronics, or industrial automation) • Proven track record of managing procurement activities throughout the entire product lifecycle—from early-stage NPI prototypes to serial production ramp-up • Deep expertise in sourcing custom mechanical, electrical, and complex electromechanical components • Strong mastery of strategic sourcing, contract negotiations, supplier agreements, and long-term commercial terms • Demonstrated ability to build supplier networks and procurement processes from the ground up in fast-growing or dynamic hardware organizations • Experience working effectively with international supplier bases, particularly across Europe and Asia • Collaborative approach with strong experience influencing and partnering with cross-functional Engineering, Quality, and Operations teams • Business fluency in English

Bonus Points • Deep domain knowledge in sourcing specialized robotic hardware such as electronics, PCBAs, high-density batteries, motors, or drives • Fluency in German for local supplier communication • Background in low-to-medium volume, high-complexity hardware manufacturing environments

We offer you a very exciting and dynamic work environment, the opportunity to become part of a fast-growing company and an ambitious team that is on a mission to change the industrial inspection market, a chance to leverage your experience and bring in your own ideas, a fair market salary, an attractive employee stock ownership plan, and a job in the city of Zurich.
