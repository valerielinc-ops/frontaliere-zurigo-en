Full description

For the acute care unit for people with dementia in Winterthur-Wülflingen we are looking for you Dipl. Nursing professional HF or FH 70% Winterthur Apply now In the acute care unit for people with dementia, we offer 16 treatment places for people with a manifested dementia diagnosis who are in temporary mental crisis and need a protected framework in the acute psychiatric setting. The interprofessional team treats patients with the close involvement of relatives, assignees and follow-up caregivers. Through medical, psychiatric, nursing, social service and therapeutic measures, an individual treatment plan for each patient is created and targeted exit management is pursued. Tasks

  • Comprehensive care and care of elderly psychiatric patients with a dementia diagnosis in the acute psychiatric setting
  • Design and implementation of the age psychiatric care process
  • Acting in psychiatric and somatic emergency situations
  • Close cooperation with intraprofessional professional colleagues within the framework of the skill-and-grade mix
  • Co-design and implementation of change processes Profile
  • Completed HF studies with professional experience in old-age psychiatry
  • Foreign diplomas require SRC recognition
  • High social/personal competence and structured working methods
  • Willingness to contribute to change processes, to engage in new things and to actively shape changes
  • Flexibility in service scheduling
  • Very good knowledge of German and PC
  • CAS in Gerontology or willingness to complete such an offer
  • Interesting, versatile and demanding workplace in an interprofessional treatment team
  • Participation in the further development of the offer
  • Regular supervision
  • Good social benefits and training opportunities
  • Costs of the CAS in Gerontology are covered by the ipw
