Role overview

As a Data Engineer in the Medical Data Products team, you ensure that clinical data can be used reliably, safely and efficiently.

  • As a Data Engineer in the Medical Data Products team, you ensure that clinical data can be used reliably, safely and efficiently.
  • Operation and continuous development of our clinical data platform and data products
  • Development and maintenance of interfaces, data pipelines and integration solutions

Main responsibilities

  • Operation and continuous development of our clinical data platform and data products
  • Development and maintenance of interfaces, data pipelines and integration solutions
  • Developing, integrating and harmonizing new data sources
  • Analysis of data requirements as well as development and implementation of suitable data solutions with specialist areas and stakeholders
  • Ensuring data quality, traceability and reliable operating processes
  • Creating and maintaining technical documentation, architecture and operational concepts Requirements
  • University or technical college degree in computer science, medical informatics or a comparable specialisation
  • Several years of experience in data engineering as well as in setting up and operating data-oriented solutions
  • In-depth knowledge of SQL, C# with .NET, data modeling and the processing of large amounts of data with SSIS, Spark, dbt and Airflow as well as the implementation of Power BI Reports
  • Experience with data integration and streaming technologies and building robust data pipelines

Application process

  • You develop and operate modern data solutions, integrate new data sources and ensure the sustainable provision of data for different application areas.
  • This way you create the technical basis for data-based processes and decisions in the healthcare sector.
  • The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland.
  • Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.

Company and context

  • We continuously develop our product organization and working methods towards agile methods and offer you a modern working environment with flexible working models and home office possibilities.
  • You have the opportunity to continuously develop your professional and technical skills.

Additional details

  • Creating and maintaining technical documentation, architecture and operational concepts
  • Structured and self-responsible working methods as well as a high awareness of data quality, data protection and sustainable solution architectures
