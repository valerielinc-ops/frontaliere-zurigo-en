# Datacenter Specialized Sales

Il tuo futuro inizia qui

In this role, you drive our systems integration business for data center and cloud and are responsible for order intake and profitability from a sales perspective. You identify opportunities with customers, accompany them until the start of the project and position our Swisscom-specific Huawei portfolio in the market. You monitor current developments on an ongoing basis and share your findings with product management and account management. Together we work with modern methods and the latest technologies and give you space for personal responsibility and development.

Highlights of the place

  • Responsible for incoming orders and profitability in the area of system integration
  • Identify and qualify leads and lead them until the project starts
  • Positions the Swisscom-specific Huawei portfolio in the market and supports sales
  • monitor the market on an ongoing basis and share your insights with product management and account management
  • Benefit from flexible working conditions – Work smart, work anywhere

Your skills

  • Degree (ETH/Uni/FH) in Business Administration or Engineering
  • Several years of experience in complex ICT sales
  • Very good knowledge of data center, especially with Huawei
  • Good knowledge of Dell EMC and Cisco UCS
  • Very good negotiating and consulting skills
  • Communicative, proactive and reliable
  • Enjoy working with customers and internal teams
  • Very good German and English in word and writing
  • French advantage

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Benefits.
