Full description

You take our picking to the next level: With your expertise, you ensure a smooth process, optimize processes and ensure the highest quality standards. You take responsibility in day-to-day business, support your team with your know-how and actively contribute to the further development of our logistics processes.

Mansioni

  • You pick customer orders on time and ensure the correct quantity, quality and compliance with the durability requirements
  • You set up the picking plants, lead the line and ensure a smooth flow in daily business
  • You work with SAP, scanner and logistics systems, control orders and check batches and labels
  • You carry out inventory checks and inventories, document deviations and report disruptions to the competent authorities
  • You support process optimization, train employees and actively participate in the implementation of new processes and systems

Requirements

  • Several years of experience: picking
  • Completed initial or continuing education: logistics or similar specialisation
  • You have experience in logistics or picking, ideally in a production environment
  • You work safely with scanners, digital systems and ideally have SAP knowledge
  • You have a strong quality and hygiene awareness as well as a good understanding of processes
  • You work reliably, independently and accurately and take responsibility for your tasks
  • You are flexible, resilient and appreciate working together in a dedicated team
  • in German (negotiation confident)

Cosa of friamo

  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks paid maternity leave / 8 weeks paternity leave (4 weeks paid and 4 weeks unpaid)
  • Mobile subscription: Top prices on mobile, TV and Internet subscriptions at Galaxus, Sunrise or Wingo
  • Occupational pension: Comprehensively secured in the Migros pension fund – with first-class and attractive services
  • Social benefits: 730 days of sick leave with full net wages
  • Cumulus points: 2 times Cumulus points on food and 5 times on non-food items
  • Migros Bank: Migros grants higher interest rates on the private and investment account for its employees
  • Holidays: 5 to 7 weeks holidays per year depending on age and service
  • Club School Migros: refund of 70% of the course costs up to a maximum of CHF 1,000 per calendar year
  • Leisure and cultural offers: You can benefit from discounted tickets at selected cultural events
  • Du-Culture: At Migros you smell – because it’s simply more relaxed
