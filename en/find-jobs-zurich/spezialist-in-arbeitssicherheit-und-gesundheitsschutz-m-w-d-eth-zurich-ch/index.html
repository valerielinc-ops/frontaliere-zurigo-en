Specialist: in Occupational Safety and Health Protection (m/f/d)

80%-100%, Zurich, permanent

The Department of Safety, Health and Environment (SGU) advises, supports and trains all organizational units and members of ETH Zurich on questions relating to safety and health at work. It is the point of contact for all centrally organized measures in dealing with hazards and risks for the protection of people, animals, property values, infrastructure and the environment as well as for questions regarding the fulfilment of the corresponding legal requirements. In the event of an incident, it shall maintain an appropriate emergency organisation. Both in everyday life and in the event of an incident, it works closely with the authorities and external forces.

SGU ensures the systematic inclusion of ASA specialists in accordance with EKAS guideline 6508 for the ETH. In order to be able to perform this task professionally and efficiently, we are looking for an experienced and committed personality as a specialist: in occupational safety and health protection (m/f/d).

Job description

Ready for the next career step in occupational safety?

You have a good sense of security, love to make complex topics understandable and really want to make a difference with your know-how? Then you are exactly right with us.

  • As part of the “ASA team” of the Occupational Safety, Health and Environment Section, you are the central contact person for all questions relating to occupational safety and health protection at ETH Zurich.
  • You advise our organizational units with expertise, empathy and foresight – and not only from the desk
  • Whether laboratory, workshop or field work: You are right in the middle of combining security with innovation
  • You work closely with an interdisciplinary team and use your expertise in laboratory safety
  • In doing so, you develop security concepts, carry out visits, train employees and find pragmatic solutions – always with the aim of protecting people, the environment and infrastructure in the best possible way.
  • Your work is characterized by personal responsibility, dialogue and the aspiration to become better together
  • Your workplace is in Zurich, but at times you also work at other ETH locations

Profile

  • Completed scientific training, e.g. chemistry, biology
  • ASA specialist: in with Swiss certificate
  • Several years of experience in the laboratory environment with very good knowledge in the field of laboratory safety and chemical safety
  • Interested in further training in other specialist topics (e.g. radiation protection, biosecurity)
  • Enjoyment of continuous professional development
  • Very good knowledge of German, good knowledge of English (level C1) and strong communication skills
