Original description

Internship Accounting & Reporting 60-80%

(6 months)

We are looking for an Intern Accounting & Reporting at our Headquarters in Pfäffikon/SZ.

During this 6-month internship you will support the Accounting & Reporting team in recurring reporting, monthly closing, half-year and annual reporting, as well as project-specific tasks in IFRS, consolidation, financial analysis and process digitalization.

Your Responsibilities

  • Support the preparation of finance sections for the annual and half-year reports
  • Assist with monthly closing, recurring reporting tasks and financial data plausibility checks
  • Prepare internal presentations, reports, analyses and overviews for the Accounting & Reporting team
  • Research IFRS/accounting topics and support related assessments
  • Support day-to-day activities of the Accounting & Reporting team and contribute to ad hoc finance projects
  • Analyze AI/Copilot/bot use cases and support automation or digitalization initiatives
  • Support the team with administrative and organizational tasks where required

Your Profile

  • Ongoing or completed Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Business Administration, Accounting, Finance or a similar field
  • Initial practical experience in accounting, finance or reporting is an advantage
  • Interest in IFRS and international financial reporting
  • Interest in digitalization, automation and AI/Copilot solutions
  • Quick comprehension and structured working style
  • Attention to detail and ability to perform plausibility checks on financial data
  • Reliable, proactive team player with an analytical mindset
  • Very good English skills
  • German is an advantage

We offer

  • A broad insight into the processes and work routines within the groupwide Accounting & Reporting department
  • A very welcoming, supportive and collaborative team spirit and an open company culture
  • An exciting and challenging opportunity to work for a global technology company
  • Attractive compensation and working conditions incl. Lunch Checks
  • Modern office facilities within 10 min walk to the train station Pfäffikon/SZ with direct connections to Zurich; the motorway Chur/Zurich within 5 min drive

Why Oerlikon

Team Spirit is one of our core values. Collaborating across borders, languages and cultures is key to us delivering results. Supporting our people’s growth and development is a top priority. We believe in the creative power of a team that draws strength from welcoming a broad expanse of knowledge, experience and perspectives.

About Oerlikon

Oerlikon is a global leader in surface technologies and advanced materials. With a unique portfolio spanning surface engineering, high-performance materials, coating equipment and components, we make products better by enhancing performance, efficiency and sustainability. Oerlikon serves a wide range of industries, including aerospace, automotive, energy, medical, luxury, semiconductors and tooling. Head-quartered in Pfäffikon, Switzerland, Oerlikon operates in 38 countries with around 10,000 employees, achieving sales of CHF 1.6 billion in 2025.
