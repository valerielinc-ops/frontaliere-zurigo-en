Full description

We are closer: to the research, the therapies of tomorrow, alive – with a family atmosphere in the middle of Zurich. The Centre for Paraplegia at Balgrist University Hospital guarantees comprehensive care for paraplegic people.

We are looking for Paraplegia bed stations by arrangement Tasks: • They care for patients with para- and tetraplegic or neurological diseases in acute situations and in the rehabilitation process • As a reference nurse, you are actively involved in the patient’s rehabilitation process and regularly exchange information about the development steps and objectives in the interdisciplinary team. • They support patients and relatives in their difficult situation, accompany the processing process and provide support for their reintegration into everyday life. • They act preventively against complications, perform patient education and also accompany and instruct relatives Requirements • You have a degree as dipl. Nursing professional HF/FH, ideally you bring professional experience • You are committed to a professional, long-term relationship with patients and their relatives in the combination of acute and rehabilitation care • They enjoy working in a large care team, bring a high level of customer orientation and appreciate the close cooperation in the interdisciplinary team. • They show great interest in participating in innovations and like to try out new approaches • They value the targeted promotion of patients’ resources in everyday care • You have a good knowledge of German and a high communication ability We offer: • The opportunity to participate in a prestigious university clinic with an excellent reputation • An interesting, versatile task in an innovative company with an appreciative working atmosphere and an open leadership culture • Close interprofessional cooperation with various highly specialized teams • Above-average support for further education and training • Care and care standards according to the latest findings of science and practice as well as the opportunity to take care of fully and partially ventilated patients • Openness to individual working models • 50 % discount on the ZVV bonus pass or annual contribution to a general subscription • 5 additional free days a year and an attractive allowance for the care of our Paraplegic patients • Gifted free day for birthday • 20% discount in Balgrist Apotheke AG • Place of work in an attractive location in Zurich with very good connections to public transport Further advantages are just one click away: Attractive offers for our employees
