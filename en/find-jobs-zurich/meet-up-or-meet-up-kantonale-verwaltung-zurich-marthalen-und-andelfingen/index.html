Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Vocational Advice Center Breitenstein Meeting Director 10% Our youth meetings are looking for new meeting leaders!

As one of a total of 15 child and youth welfare centres in the Canton of Zurich, the Breitenstein Centre ensures a uniform, regionally and municipally sound provision of services in the areas of child and adult protection, personal assistance, advice and prevention for the population of the Andelfingen district.
