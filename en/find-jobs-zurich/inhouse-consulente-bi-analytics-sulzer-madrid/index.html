About the role

Are you passionate about transforming data into business value? Do you enjoy working closely with senior stakeholders and influencing business decisions through analytics, insights, and emerging technologies? At Sulzer, we're embarking on an exciting journey from traditional Business Intelligence towards Artificial Intelligence-powered solutions. We're looking for an experienced Inhouse Consultant BI & Analytics to join our Global IT BI & Analytics team in Madrid and help drive our data-driven transformation. This is not a pure IT development role. We are looking for someone who can combine strong technical expertise with business consulting skills, building trusted relationships with senior leaders across the business while delivering innovative analytics solutions. As part of our global BI & Analytics team, you will work closely with business stakeholders across Sulzer to understand challenges, identify opportunities, and deliver solutions that drive better decision-making.

Key responsibilities include

  • Partnering with business leaders to understand requirements and translate them into scalable analytics solutions
  • Designing, developing and enhancing enterprise BI applications using Qlik Sense
  • Delivering meaningful insights and dashboards that support strategic and operational decision-making
  • Working with Snowflake and modern data platforms to create trusted, scalable data solutions
  • Supporting Sulzer's "One Sulzer" harmonisation strategy through standardised reporting and analytics
  • Driving self-service analytics and data literacy across the organisation
  • Exploring and implementing emerging technologies including AI, Generative AI, Copilots and Agentic AI solutions
  • Building intelligent applications and data products that connect to Sulzer's modern data platform
  • Collaborating with Data Engineering teams to develop robust data models and scalable solutions
  • Acting as a trusted advisor to both business and IT stakeholders globally

  • Strong hands-on experience with Qlik Sense
  • Advanced SQL skills

  • Strong business consulting and stakeholder management skills
  • Experience gathering business requirements and translating them into technical solutions
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills

  • Experience with Snowflake

  • Experience in AI, Generative AI, Copilots or emerging analytics technologies
  • Experience working within an international or global organisation

The Opportunity

This is a unique opportunity for someone who enjoys operating at the intersection of business, data, analytics and AI. You'll play a key role in helping Sulzer evolve from traditional reporting and dashboards towards intelligent, AI-enabled business solutions that create measurable value across the organisation. If you're excited by modern analytics, emerging AI technologies, and partnering with senior business leaders to drive transformation, we'd love to hear from you.
