Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Vocational Advice Scientific staff member in the Financial Assistance – Processes & Projects 80% Good for life – this is our motto. As part of the Education Directorate of the Canton of Zurich, the Office for Youth and Vocational Counselling (AJB) is responsible for the offers in the field of child and youth welfare as well as in vocational counselling. Our work focuses on children, young people and families. We work for them every day with a lot of heart and commitment. The Financial Assistance section of the AJB examines requests for financial support for people and organisations working on behalf of people – from scholarships and loans to alimony aid to grants for charitable projects and offers. As of 1 October 2026 or by agreement, we are looking for a forward-looking, self-responsible and conceptually strong personality for the Financial Aid Unit. Their tasks

  • Further development and optimization of the Coordination Office Subsidies with focus on process improvement, portfolio development and strategy implementation
  • Financial examination of grant applications in close exchange with experts from other service areas and management staffs
  • Support for financial assistance in other projects to optimise processes in the Alimentenhilfe and Fellowship departments
  • Preparation of statistics and evaluations of your profile
  • Master's degree in Business Economics, Finance and Accounting, Business Law or Public Management
  • Professional experience of several years, preferably in the public sector
  • Know-how in the fields of trust, auditing or financial controlling
  • Experience in managing projects with appropriate training
  • Independent, structured work and strong conceptual skills
  • Excellent expressiveness and precise communication
  • Joy in the design of change processes and perseverance in the pursuit of goals
  • Humor and high social skills We offer them
  • A challenging task in a meaningful environment
  • Good infrastructure as well as social benefits, flexible working models (flexwork) and cantonal employment conditions
  • Work location at central location just a few minutes walk from Zurich HB Have we aroused your interest? Then we look forward to your complete online application. Applications from job placements as well as applications via e-mail are not considered for this vacancy. Jeanine Zimmermann, Head of Financial Assistance, is available to answer questions (email: jeanine.zimmermann@ajb.zh.ch ).

Pensum: 80 %
