Full description

Spital Männedorf – Your very special place of work. Whether a small “thank you” or a morning “grüezi” – we focus on real appreciation and humanity. The proximity in our house makes everyday life uncomplicated and allows patient-centered work at the highest level. In an environment where interdisciplinary cooperation at eye level is a matter of course, we offer individual treatments from primary medical care to rehabilitation.

The nature of your activity

  • Friendly and helpful reception and care of our patients
  • Organization of appointments and administrative work on the computer and telephone

  • Conduct of laboratory work (e.g. blood or urine tests)
  • Assistance with X-ray examinations
  • Share responsibility for a smooth everyday practice

The basis of your performance

  • Secondary School A (or Good Sek B)
  • Enjoying science as well as precise work and organization
  • Empathetic and respectful treatment of people
  • Interested in working with people from different cultures
  • Reliability, psychological and physical resilience
  • Team player or team player and pleasure in assuming responsibility Submitting are:
  • Letter of application / letter of motivation (minimum 1⁄2 page, max. 2 pages) or a video explaining the choice of profession

  • 2 reference persons (teachers/employers) with telephone number and e-mail address
  • Current Multicheck “Health and Social Affairs” for the profession MPA
  • All trial reports and/or traineeship evidence, if available

The reasons for your decision

We offer modern working conditions, promote internal training and focus on professional exchange across all disciplines. The quiet location directly on the lake and the proximity to the vibrant city of Zurich round off your workplace. In addition, you can look forward to very special advantages.
