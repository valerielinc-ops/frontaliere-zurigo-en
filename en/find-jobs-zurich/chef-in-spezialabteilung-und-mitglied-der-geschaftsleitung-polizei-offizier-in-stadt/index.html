Full description

The special department brings together four special security police units and the office for events. In order to further increase the proportion of women in our leadership, we are also looking forward to numerous applications from women to lead this front department in the police environment. We are looking for due to an internal change of function of the current job holder as of 1. February 2027 or by agreement a chief special department and member of the management / police officer

Tasks

  • Technical and personnel management in the areas of water protection, traffic, administrative police, detention centre and police assistance service with around 350 employees
  • Performing command and leading large-scale police events as General Operations Commander, member of the City Crisis Management Organization (SFO)
  • Representation of the city police to the outside and representation of the department-specific topics in delegations, commissions, steering and steering committees
  • In the role of GL member strategic, personnel, tactical and operational leadership of the Zurich City Police

Profile

  • Master level training (FH or university) and/or police career (police officer) with several years of management experience or corresponding leadership career in larger security organizations
  • Good knowledge of police, criminal and criminal procedural law as well as relation to administrative law
  • Experience in personnel and organizational development
  • Readiness for training and further education in the areas of Brandtour training and CAS FIP (training as police officer)
  • High performance orientation and social competence as well as a strong will to shape politics (city or canton of Zurich) and administration

We offer

  • Versatile position in an exciting and lively working environment
  • The challenge as a member of the management in the leadership of the city police to participate strategically, personnel and operationally
  • A modern workplace in the immediate vicinity of Zurich Central Station
  • Attractive employment conditions such as mobile work, targeted education and training, assumption of 60% of the savings contributions, mobility contribution of up to CHF 600 per year

About us

The city police is a service department of the security department of the city of Zurich and with around 2250 employees the third largest police corps in Switzerland
