Full description

Are you ready for a new adventure at the Migros? As an electrician*in building technology at our Herdern headquarters, you have the chance to shine in a technically demanding environment. Become part of our dedicated team in Zurich and promote your career. Your next chapter is waiting!

Mansioni

  • General maintenance and maintenance work on infrastructure systems (including refrigeration, waste heat and ventilation systems)
  • Ensure trouble-free operation (interruption analysis, troubleshooting, care, maintenance and repair of plants) in cooperation with internal and external bodies
  • Ensure compliance with applicable directives and legal requirements
  • Support for new installations and commissioning of the maintenance team

Requisiti

  • Multiannual professional experience: In building technology or maintenance
  • Complete professional teaching (EFZ): electrical installerin, assembly electriciansin or equivalent training
  • Ready-to-use electrical approval according to NIV Art. 13 to acquire
  • knowledge of domestic engineering and control technology and understanding of troubleshooting
  • Reliable and responsible operation
  • Team skills and self-employment
  • willingness and interest to develop in the field
  • Ready for Pikett Service
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa offriamo

  • Occupational pension: Excellent pension funds with high employer participation
  • Social benefits: Fair social benefits

  • Cumulus points: You get additional cumulus points when shopping
  • Migros Bank: preferential interest in private and investment account, free account management, discounted mortgages and more
  • Employee offers: Up to 1,000 CHF per year (depending on your pensum) for your recovery and further development – usable for fitness subscriptions, courses, health offers or a grant for the ÖV subscription.
  • Leisure and cultural activities: You benefit from discounted tickets at selected cultural events
