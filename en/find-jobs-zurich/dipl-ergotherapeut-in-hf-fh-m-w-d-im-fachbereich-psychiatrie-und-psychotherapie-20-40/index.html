Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With more than 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric offer of Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in ambulatory, day-clinical and inpatient settings. The Group employs around 1800 people and achieved a turnover of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For the area of specialist therapies we are looking for a /n as of March 2027 temporarily until November 2027

Occupational therapy, together with music, body, and painting and design therapy, forms the area of specialist therapies at Clienia Littenheid AG and is available to all clinic areas.

Their tasks

  • Conduct group services of competence-, expression-centred, and interactional occupational therapy
  • The focus of therapy is on strengthening everyday skills, resource activation, as well as concentration and memory performance.
  • Promoting patient self-awareness and creativity
  • Small share of hand therapy treatments
  • Recording and history documentation
  • Occupational therapy treatment of our adult patients of acute and psychotherapy stations

Your profile

  • Graduate degree in occupational therapy HF/FH (certificate of recognition in foreign diplomas, at least SRK pre-check))
  • Interest and pleasure in working with people in mental crisis situations
  • Experience in the field of psychiatry is an advantage
  • Ideally experience in hand therapy/basic knowledge
  • Resilient, reflective and team-interested personality
  • Special degree of responsibility, reliability, flexibility and readiness
  • Very independent and resource-oriented working method
  • They bring skills in the creative, technical and design fields

Our offer

  • Challenging and multifaceted work
  • Regular case supervision
  • Internal training opportunities
  • Part of a competent and motivated team
  • Beautiful parking area, quiet working environment

For further information, please contact Germaine Frey Koss, Head of Department Specialised Therapies, +41 71 929 64 24.
