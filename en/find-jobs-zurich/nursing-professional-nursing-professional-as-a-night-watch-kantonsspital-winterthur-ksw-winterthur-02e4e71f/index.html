Full description

Do you like to travel at night or do you plan to re-enter after a "family break"? The Social Paediatric Center (SPZ) is an organizational unit at the Center for Pediatric and Adolescent Medicine of the Cantonal Hospital Winterthur. For the field of inpatient psychosomatics, the therapy unit for children and adolescents was established. The therapy unit offers a total of 12-14 patients who are treated due to psychosomatic diseases (e.g. eating disorders, depression, pain disorders). During the day, the children and adolescents are supervised and supported by an interdisciplinary team consisting of social educators and nurses in a milieutherapeutically oriented setting. During the night, patient care is guaranteed by a nurse.

Tasks

  • You ensure the smooth flow in the day-night-day rhythm
  • You are responsible for the implementation of professional care, care and social support taking into account modern care and treatment concepts as well as care standards
  • You carry out therapeutic and medical prescriptions
  • Keeping the maintenance documentation, planning and control tasks as well as administrative work are also among your tasks

Profile

  • Training as a Dipl. Nurse HF/FH, with at least 2 years of current professional experience in a somatic acute care hospital
  • High decision-making competence, a secure appearance and good communication skills

  • Goal- and solution-oriented work
  • Organizational talent, good IT user knowledge and good oral and written German language skills
