Full description

NICE TO MEET YOU!

IN SHORT Are you passionate about data, optimization, and minimizing food waste? As an Intern in Data Analytics & Operations (starting in August or September, 100%, fixed term for 6 months) in Zurich Wiedikon, you will play a crucial role in allocating the perfect amount of food and coffee to our FELFEL fridges and Gavetti machines. Working closely with our sales team, you will evaluate client requests and constantly strive for high customer satisfaction.

YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES

Daily Tasks

  • Run our daily stocking algorithm and analyze key performance statistics to optimize the inventory in our FELFEL fridges and Gavetti coffee machines.
  • Make proactive, data-driven decisions to initiate changes, reduce food waste, and maximize customer satisfaction.
  • Manage and coordinate special client requests, such as holiday adjustments or additional orders.

Projects

  • Contribute actively to ongoing projects by identifying pain points, proposing creative improvements, and tracking the impact of new initiatives.
  • Maintain the accuracy and integrity of our internal tools and information databases.

WHAT TO EXPECT

YOUR PROFILE

  • You just completed your Bachelor and are currently in between studies, or looking for a hands-on opportunity to gain practical experience.
  • Proficiency in MS Office, particularly feeling comfortable with Excel and PowerPoint.
  • Entrepreneurial mindset – curious about how things work and always looking for ways to help the business grow.
  • Exceptional attention to detail combined with the ability to simultaneously balance multiple tasks.
  • Fluency in English; additional knowledge of German and/or French is a plus.
  • Non-smoker - we don't want you to smell like cigarettes, neither at work nor at customer meetings. After all, FELFEL is about good food.
  • Valid work permit or Swiss passport.

YOU WILL BE MEASURED BY

OUR BENEFITS

  • Delicious Meals at Your Fingertips: Enjoy fresh, high-quality meals just steps away from your workspace – every day.
  • Barista-Style Coffee & Premium Tea: Fuel your day with unlimited barista-style coffee and premium tea, thanks to our Gavetti machine.
  • Halbtax Travel Pass Coverage: We cover the cost of your Swiss Halbtax (half-fare) travel pass to support sustainable commuting.
  • A Supportive, Dynamic Team Culture: Work in a collaborative environment that values initiative and gives you the space to contribute your ideas.

If this sounds like the perfect role for you, apply now with your documents in German or English. We look forward to hearing from you!

ABOUT US FELFEL is all about good food and coffee in the workplace: we help Swiss employers become masters of hospitality. With our smart fridge ‘FELFEL’ and delicious barista coffee ‘Gavetti’, we enable companies to provide their employees with first-class, ultra-fresh food and drinks throughout the day. Whether hearty and traditional, modern and vegan or high in protein - our weekly changing menu ensures that all employees always have something to be excited about ;). FELFEL is a family business that was founded in 2013.
