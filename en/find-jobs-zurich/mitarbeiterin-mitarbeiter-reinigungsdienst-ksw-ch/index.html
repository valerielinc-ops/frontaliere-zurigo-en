Full description

In the field of housekeeping for cleaning, the company is looking for a flexible personality for a temporary use of 12 months.

Tasks

  • General cleaning work on bed stations
  • Execution of discharge cleanings
  • Internal material order for the allocated area
  • Working hours from 6 a.m. to 4 p.m. 365 days a year according to the roster

Profile

  • Several years of professional experience in cleaning, preferably in health care and/or in the hotel industry
  • Good oral German language skills
  • High quality and service awareness and good physical performance
  • High flexibility regarding changing work assignments as well as readiness to work on weekends is mandatory
  • Motivated, honest, team capable and friendly personality with a sense of responsibility

You should be able to contribute with your ideas and strengths and develop.
