Full description

Do you distinguish yourself through food expertise and leadership skills? We are looking for a dedicated specialist lead for our MM Stäfa branch. You will be responsible for product presentation, lead your team, and ensure the highest level of customer satisfaction. Apply now and become part of our success!

Duties

  • You act as a competent contact person for our customers together with your team and take responsibility for your area xy
  • You can effectively use your organizational skills in staff scheduling and lead your team with dedication
  • You enjoy passing on your expertise and knowledge to new employees and introducing them to your area
  • As a deputy to the branch manager/supermarket manager, you ensure smooth business operations within the branch
  • You are informed about the requirements within your area by conducting inventories and take responsibility for product orders
  • You carry out sales-promoting measures (such as promotions) on the sales floor in a targeted manner and use promotions skillfully
  • You keep an overview through the ongoing analysis and management of business key figures
  • You are very familiar with internal and external quality standards and implement them skillfully

Requirements

  • 2-3 years of professional experience: in the food sector
  • Completed basic or further training: in sales/retail
  • You appear customer-oriented and friendly
  • You work independently, efficiently, and precisely
  • You bring experience in leading and developing teams in retail and are able to inspire and motivate employees
  • You have completed the specialist lead training or are motivated to complete it internally
  • Through your networked thinking, goal-oriented and solution-oriented action comes easily to you
  • You are stress-tolerant and can assert yourself
  • You have strong communication skills and are an organizational talent
  • German (very good knowledge)

What we offer

  • Working in health: Health-promoting offers such as massages and health checks
  • Mobility: With the one-time annual leisure bonus of up to CHF 500.–, we contribute to your public transport subscription
  • Mobile subscription: You can look forward to special conditions for your mobile subscription
  • Occupational pension: Excellent pension fund benefits with high employer contributions
  • Social benefits: Fair social benefits
  • Cumulus points: You receive additional Cumulus points when shopping
  • Migros Bank: Preferential interest rates on private and investment accounts, free account management, discounted mortgages, and more
  • Employee offers: Up to 1,000 CHF per year (depending on your workload) for your recreation and development – usable for fitness subscriptions, courses, health offers, or a subsidy for a public transport subscription.
  • Sports and fitness offers: You can redeem a one-time annual leisure bonus of up to CHF 1000.–, e.g., for an annual subscription to the M-Fitness center
  • Education and training: Support for professional education and training
