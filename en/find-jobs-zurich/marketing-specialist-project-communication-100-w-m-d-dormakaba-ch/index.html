Full description

Summary

Location: Rümlang (Headquarters)

Do you want to actively shape marketing activities and at the same time promote the communication of a strategically important company project? In this versatile function, you play a central role in the implementation of marketing campaigns and communication measures for the Swiss market and contribute to success with targeted project communication.

As a Marketing Specialist & Project Communication, you implement marketing campaigns for defined product portfolios and verticals, organize trade fairs and events and thus support sales and distribution in Switzerland. In addition, you control and coordinate the communication of a transformation project, ensure a transparent flow of information within the project team and throughout the dormakaba organization and thus make an important contribution to the successful implementation of the project goals.

Look forward to a varied position where you combine marketing, communication and project work and achieve lasting impact together with dedicated teams.

Your tasks

Marketing Specialist

  • Planning, control and implementation of local marketing campaigns for defined product portfolios and verticals based on global marketing and communication concepts.
  • Adaptation, localization and ongoing maintenance of marketing and communication tools for the Swiss market, including brochures, factsheets, presentations, websites and social media content.
  • Design and implementation of local advertising materials in cooperation with external service providers and agencies.
  • Planning, organization and implementation of trade fairs, customer events and other marketing events.
  • Close cooperation with internal stakeholders and support in the implementation of cross-divisional marketing and communication measures.

Project Communication

  • Development, planning and control of communication activities within the framework of the transformation program.
  • Creation and coordination of program communication, including all-hands events, townhall meetings, newsletters, program updates and other internal communication formats.
  • Ensure a consistent, transparent and target group-oriented information flow for the program team and the entire dormakaba organization.
  • Management and further development of the communication channels of the transformation program, the editorial plans and the central program announcements.
  • Close coordination with programme management, project teams and other relevant stakeholders to support successful programme implementation.

Your profile

  • At least 5 years of professional experience in marketing communication, marketing or a related field.
  • Completed studies in marketing, communication, business administration or a comparable discipline.
  • An additional qualification, such as a federal certificate in marketing communication or project management, is an advantage.
  • Proven experience in the implementation of marketing campaigns, content localization and coordination of marketing projects.
  • Strong organizational and project management skills, as well as the ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills with a collaborative and pragmatic way of working.
  • Experience in using digital marketing tools, social media platforms and email marketing systems is beneficial.
  • Negotiable knowledge of German and English in words and writing.
  • Knowledge of Italian is a big plus.
  • High level of detail, proactive working methods and the ability to work successfully both independently and in a team.
