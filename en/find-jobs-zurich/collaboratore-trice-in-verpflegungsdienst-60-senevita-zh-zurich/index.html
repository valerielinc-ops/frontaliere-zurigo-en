Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,000 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. The Senevita Vivimus is located in the city of Zurich and comprises a residence hall with 18 apartments. You can expect a family, personal and varied working environment.

We are looking for an immediately or by agreement Employee / catering service 60% Your tasks

  • Service of all 3 meals in the care department
  • Responsibility for a pleasant atmosphere in the food rooms
  • You coordinate the tasks with the care service and the kitchen
  • Room service in apartments
  • You are responsible for the general cleaning of the office and compliance with hygiene regulations

You bring that with you

  • Training as a restaurant professional, service employee or equivalent
  • Experience in serving food and drinks
  • Good manners and a high understanding for elderly and sick people
  • Good German (minimum B2) and ideally Swiss German skills
  • Willingness to work on weekends, Sundays and public holidays

We offer you

  • Pure day operation – your evenings remain free
  • Two whole free weekends per month
  • Working hours from 8:00 to 13:30 and from 15:00 to 18:00, with a break between the two assignments and free evenings from 18:00
  • An independent activity with a lot of personal responsibility, in which the person takes over the service on site alone
  • An activity with a lot of personal contact and daily exchange with the residents in a family and warm atmosphere.
  • Cheap food in the in-house gastronomy
  • Various advantages
  • Good social benefits, weekend and holiday allowances and min. 5 weeks holiday (from 50 years 6 or from 60 years 7 weeks)
