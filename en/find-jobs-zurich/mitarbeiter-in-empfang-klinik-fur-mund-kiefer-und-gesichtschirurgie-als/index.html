Role overview

Do you have organizational talent, enjoy working with people and feel comfortable in a modern dental practice? Then become part of our team as a maternity representative and take on a central role in reception and coordination of our patients until the end of June 2027.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.

Tasks

  • Direct reception of patients in the outpatient clinic and the dental practice with a representative function
  • Fulfilling the role of host, ensuring professional care for patients in the waiting area
  • Receive and process telephone calls and patient inquiries at the front desk
  • Supporting the clinic's own consultation hours by arranging follow-up appointments as well as confident handling and understanding of the clinic's internal appointment system and understanding of the corresponding processes
  • Case opening and case processing, recording of payers and guarantors, control of patient master data
  • Ordering materials and ordering support services

Requirements

• Training as a dental assistant or professional experience in a dental environment

  • Communicative and personable personality with high social skills and good manners
  • High organizational skills and a pronounced service orientation
  • Experience in organizing and scheduling patient appointments an advantage
