Full description

Do you like working with people? You want to start at 1. November 2026 or by agreement with our team of the Technical Service for the Health Center for the Old Mathysweg?

Tasks

  • They test, maintain and control buildings, plants and turnarounds based on the object-specific working catalogue and carry out versatile maintenance and audit work on plants and equipment.
  • They care for the environment from an ecological and economic point of view.
  • They support and comply with the measures ordered by the security officers and also work with the care of the learners.
  • They provide support for operational events and ensure the technical facilities for events.
  • They maintain good contact with residents and respond to their wishes and needs within the operational framework conditions.

Profile

  • A responsible, reliable and exact way of working as well as teamwork, openness and good physical strength
  • Completed training as a specialist in operation maintenance EFZ and several years of professional experience
  • Knowledge of relevant safety regulations (ETS, EKAS, etc.) and driving licence category B mandatory
  • Readiness for cross-site operations in the facilities of the Letzi health quarter
  • Joy at work, a heart for our residents as well as very good PC and German skills

We offer

Look forward to an exciting area of work in a professional and team-oriented working environment with a du-culture throughout the company. The human being is at our center. That is why we support the reconciliation of private and professional life, different working models make it possible.

As part of the city of Zurich and due to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, progressive employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

Depending on the experience, the annual salary for this function is between Fr. 64’070.– and 85’610.– (100%).

About us

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

The Health Center for the Old Mathysweg in the idyllic district of Albisrieden in district 9 offers residents a homely home with all amenities. The modern new building impresses with spacious, modern and comfortable living spaces that leave nothing to be desired. Here you work together with 80 colleagues.
