Summary

Shape the future of building technology products at Belimo. Bring your expertise in mechatronic product development to advance our damper actuator portfolio. We are looking for an open-minded, technically savvy team player to join our group of product engineering experts.

What You'll Do

  • Partner with the Product Category Owner in creating innovative products and delivering customer value.
  • Define, manage, and refine product requirements and specifications
  • Conceptualize products, including variants and feature sets and lead design changes
  • Lead cross-functional teams to ensure feasible, optimized solutions
  • Own key workstreams within product development projects
  • Collaborate with software engineers to manage software requirements and validate implementation
  • Manage product cost calculations and support cost optimization
  • Drive risk management through design FMEA and product assessments and resolution of global quality issues related to product design.
  • Ensure smooth transfer to production and work closely with testing, product management and compliance to ensure verification, validation, and certification.

Who You Are

  • Degree in Electrical, Mechanical Engineering, or related technical field
  • Several years of experience in mechatronic product development for industrial manufacturing
  • Involvement with software requirements management and strong understanding of mechanics and electronics
  • Deep expertise in requirements engineering, certification is a plus
  • Structured, analytical mindset with strong problem-solving skills
  • Excellent communication skills in English
  • German is a plus
  • Collaborative, proactive team player with a positive attitude
