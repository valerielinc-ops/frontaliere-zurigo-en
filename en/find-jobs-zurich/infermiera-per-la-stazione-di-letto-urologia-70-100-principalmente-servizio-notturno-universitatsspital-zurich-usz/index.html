Original description

With us at URO not only the catheter runs, but also the interdisciplinary cooperation! We are a multicultural team – from young to old. For our team, we are looking for a nurse who would like to work primarily on night work and who will independently and responsibly look after our patients. Once URO, always URO! >The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.

Tasks

  • You take over the comprehensive pre- and postoperative care of patients with complex urological procedures as well as the care of people with predominantly urological clinical pictures.
  • As a reference nurse: r you control the care process specifically to ensure professional and interdisciplinary care.
  • You work closely with the specialists in leadership, education and specialist development and are actively involved in the further education of the nursing profession.
  • You work mostly on night service and support other services if necessary.

Requirements

  • Completed training as a nurse HF/FH – or you are about to graduate.
  • You appreciate the independent work and the responsibility in the night service.
  • Interested in familiarizing yourself with a new field and developing your knowledge and skills.
  • A respectful and dignified accompaniment of people in difficult situations is just as important to you as it is to us.
  • Do you come from long-term care or have no experience in urology? No problem! We look forward to open-minded and inquisitive new team members.

We offer

  • A complex and versatile field where you can gain valuable experience.
  • A structured introduction and support by our care expert in challenging situations.
  • Targeted promotion of your personal and professional development.
  • An international, diverse team that stands for tolerance, responsibility and humor.
  • A supervisor who meets their employees at eye level and maintains a transparent, open communication.

Even if you are unsure: Just upload your CV – we will see if we fit together.
