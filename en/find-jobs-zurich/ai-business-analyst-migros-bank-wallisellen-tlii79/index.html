Full description

With us, you actively shape the future in the field of artificial intelligence! You will be instrumental in the analysis, conception, support and adoption of our AI solutions and will drive them forward together with dedicated internal and external teams. We are looking for a personality who never loses sight of the essentials when immersed in concepts and code.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Bank, Wallisellen, Industriestrasse 17-19, 8304 Wallisellen

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You analyze business processes, identify specific GenAI/AI use cases on-prem / cloud and drive them forward with a pronounced implementation orientation.
  • You translate business needs into clearly understandable requirements for IT, Data & AI teams.
  • You accompany the introduction of AI solutions from the idea to the pilot to the bank-wide rollout.
  • You drive AI adoption through training, guidelines and best practice formats.
  • You act as an internal contact person around GenAI/AI, advise departments and moderate stakeholders.
  • You measure the benefits and acceptance of AI solutions and derive concrete improvement measures from them.

Requirements

  • At least 5 years: AI Engineering / Business Analysis
  • Master's degree (university, university of applied sciences): Computer Science / Informtics / Business Informatics
  • Several years of experience in business analysis or requirements engineering, ideally in AI, IT or digitalization projects.
  • Several years of experience with GenAI/AI solutions (e.g. LLMs, chatbots, engineering) and typical use cases.
  • Experience in the preparation of management presentations and the addressable presentation of AI topics at all management levels.
  • Very good knowledge of IT and AI landscapes (on-prem & cloud) as well as basic understanding of data protection / compliance.
  • Experience working with different stakeholders and experience in change management / enablement/adoption initiatives for new digital AI solutions.
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • English (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central place of work (GABS): Optimally accessible by train (10 minutes from Zurich HB) or car
  • Modern infrastructure: future-oriented and ergonomic workplace
  • Flexible place of work / Home Office: Individual possibilities for home office
  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks for mothers and 4 weeks for fathers
  • Child care: up to CHF 300 per month per child to external child care
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Employee offers: Annual generous well-being credit

Contatto

Application and contact Dario D’Agostino HR Business Partner

Recruitment process

  • Personal video or telephone conversation: For a large number of applicants, we optionally conduct a personal telephone or video conversation. This gives us the opportunity to get to know more candidates within a short time in order to invite suitable people for the next round. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Personality/performance test: Under certain conditions, we use a personality test (BIP, Scales test, etc.) as an additional step to support selection. The results of the test may be discussed personally and form the basis for further selection. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Discussion with case presentation: Specific expertise is examined on the basis of a case or other assessment instruments. After processing, the result of the leader is presented and then discussed together. Usually, the team is also represented at the presentation. As a rule, the conversations are conducted by the superior, without a representative from the HR. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition: After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
