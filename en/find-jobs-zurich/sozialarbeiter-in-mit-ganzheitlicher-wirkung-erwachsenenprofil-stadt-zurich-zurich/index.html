Full description

If nothing is taken for granted anymore, make the difference.

People come to us when the ground falls under our feet - financially, professionally, personally. As a social worker at the Social Centre Selnau, you accompany adult clients on their way and work with them to find viable solutions for sustainable professional and social integration. Their work works - not abstractly, but concretely, in the lives of real people.

For our dedicated team at the Social Centre Selnau we are looking for reinforcement from January 2027 or by agreement.

Tasks

  • They advise and accompany adults in economic welfare and adult protection.
  • They advise and accompany adult clients in financial and social difficulties – at eye level and with a clear view for their resources.
  • For us, securing livelihoods is not a paperwork, but the foundation on which everything else is built: They provide economic social assistance and carry out civil mandates in the protection of adults. Two worlds, one goal – and does not guarantee a routine.
  • Yes, this also includes weighing up support and setting limits. It is precisely this field of tension that makes the work technically demanding – and honest.
  • You keep your work traceable in the case management system (FFS).

Profile

  • University of Applied Sciences degree in Social Work
  • Experience in economic welfare and/or adult protection
  • Commercial organizational flair and security in financial topics
  • The ability to quickly grasp complex situations and take on different roles in counselling and support
  • Sovereignty, clarity and a professional approach to different concerns and expectations

We offer

A well-established team with a high level of expertise that lives together. The possibility to work partially in the home office. An exciting internal and external training offer as well as internal specialist positions that keep your back in day-to-day business. And the certainty that your work really counts for the city of Zurich – and for the people in it.

About us

With around 2,400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a social Zurich. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.

With their work in the five social centres, the social services ensure that people in the city of Zurich can count on effective basic social care.
