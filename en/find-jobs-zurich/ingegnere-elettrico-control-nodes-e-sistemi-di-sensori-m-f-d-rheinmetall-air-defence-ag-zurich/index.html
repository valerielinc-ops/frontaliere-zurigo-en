Full description

Tasks

  • New and further development of the electronic and electrical system components of our guidance systems and sensor systems

  • Implementation and support in the qualification (environmental, EMC, shock & vibration) of overall and subsystems

  • Flexibility and willingness to travel for assignments at home and abroad
  • Strong communication skills, intercultural competence and enjoyment of teamwork
  • Driving license category B

  • Staff restaurant as well as external catering and shopping options nearby
  • Individual internal and external personnel development measures in collaboration with our Rheinmetall Academy
  • Workplace a few minutes' walk from Zurich-Oerlikon train station
