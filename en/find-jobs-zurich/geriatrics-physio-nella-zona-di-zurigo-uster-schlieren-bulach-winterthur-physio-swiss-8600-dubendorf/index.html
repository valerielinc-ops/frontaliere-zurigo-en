Your tasks

You bring the following

  • Completed training as a physiotherapist/in FH/HF or with SRK recognition and min. 1-3 years of professional experience.
  • Interested in interdisciplinary cooperation with doctors, nurses and physical colleagues.
  • You are resilient, independent and enjoy shaping your own workflows!
  • You value good, digital documentation.

What we can offer you

  • Business car to visit the sites and for private use.
  • Sympathetic, experienced and helpful team, in which the person is in the foreground.
  • Extraordinarily high salary.
  • Flexible working hours (you decide).
  • Mind. 5 weeks of vacation and 5 days of further education.
  • Enough training and administration time. We are always there for you!
