Full description

Become part of the innovative development for the quality of life of our customers. In order to strengthen our Spitex team, we are looking for an Co-Head of the Housekeeping Team

What moves you with us

  • Together with your colleague, you lead the housekeeping team
  • You create the service and holiday planning of the employees
  • They advise our clients and their relatives competently and sensitively
  • They carry out needs assessments and plan the corresponding operations
  • They participate operationally if necessary
  • They are in regular exchange with doctors, social workers and advisers
  • They contribute to the assurance and further development of quality in the field of housekeeping
  • Active participation in shaping a positive, solution-oriented culture at Sophie Guyer Alterszentrum as a member of the cadre

What you bring

  • Degree in professional health or housekeeping EFZ
  • Quality awareness, organizational skills and a winning appearance
  • An appreciative, empathetic basic attitude in dealing with clients and employees
  • Self-employed, responsible and reliable working methods
  • Good PC user knowledge
  • Knowledge of RAI-HC is an advantage
  • Good knowledge of German at level B2
  • Driving licence

What you get with us

  • Possibility to shape your function and the Spitex
  • With us you stay on the ball and are informed via ‘Beekeeper’, our employee app
  • In addition to 5, i.e. 6 weeks holiday two days of relaxation
  • A collaborative, motivated team with openness, humour and passion for the common cause
  • Diverse training and development opportunities
  • An attractive overall package with health promotion, top pension fund and cheap food
