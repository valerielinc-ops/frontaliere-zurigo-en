Full description

We design Zurich from the ground up: liveable and climate-neutral. Bring your expertise and work with us on a future-oriented city. For the value preservation business unit, we are looking for reinforcement from 1 January 2027 or by agreement.

Tasks

  • They ensure that the legal and internal requirements for occupational safety at the workshops are complied with.
  • They develop the security system further and ensure its management.
  • They guide the BeSiBe professionally on the workshops and support them with questions about occupational safety.
  • They coordinate and represent the safety-relevant topics in the field of execution, in particular absence management, occupational health management and emergency organisation.
  • They support emergency organisation and operational pandemic planning throughout the civil engineering office.

Profile

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship or comparable qualification in technical or craft direction as well as further training to / to the specialist ASGS with Federal Government. Professional card or equivalent training
  • Category B driving licence mandatory
  • Professional experience in the construction or craft sector
  • Good knowledge of German in word and writing as well as good user knowledge in MS Office and ERP systems (SAP of advantage)
  • Pronounced cost awareness and high willingness to provide services

We offer

  • The opportunity to shape the future of the largest Swiss city.
  • A culture that is shaped by respectful, reliable, ambitious and inclusive values and an employer who lives equality, diversity and inclusion.
  • Passionate and committed colleagues whose heart beats for Zurich.
  • Attractive employment conditions, such as contribution to catering costs, a solid pension fund with an above-average employer share and targeted training and further education opportunities.

About us

For a high quality of life: The approximately 350 employees of the civil engineering office shape the mobility and public space in the largest city in Switzerland every day.
