Full description

Introduction

Shape scalable manufacturing processes and bring products successfully into series production. As Lead Make, you connect product industrialization, equipment engineering and operations in global projects.

Key Responsibilities

  • Be the focal point of process flow and line layout design across global production setups
  • Lead end-to-end design and optimization of global manufacturing and assembly processes in industrialization projects
  • Act as Lead Make in product creation projects, representing manufacturing processes in the core team
  • Drive process industrialization and transparently align product, process and equipment requirements
  • Collaborate closely with Supply Chain, Equipment Engineering, Quality, IT and Operations worldwide
  • Own implementation, validation and ramp-up of manufacturing processes including pilot runs
  • Perform technical risk analyses (PFMEA) and ensure implementation of mitigation measures
  • Ensure process capability and monitor performance and stability
  • Support continuous improvement initiatives such as automation, cost optimization and global transfers

Required Expertise

  • University or technical college degree in engineering (e.g. mechanical, industrial, production) or similar
  • At least 7 years of experience in manufacturing engineering, process engineering or industrialization in a global environment
  • Strong technical background in device manufacturing, manufacturing equipment and process validation methods (PFMEA, control plans)
  • Ability to influence and collaborate across functions, cultures and hierarchy levels
  • Structured, analytical and entrepreneurial personality with high ownership and accountability
  • Very good English skills (C2); German proficiency (B2-C1) is necessary.

About Belimo

Belimo is the global market leader in the development, production, and sales of field devices for the energy-efficient control of heating, ventilation and air-conditioning systems. The focus of our core business is on damper actuators, control valves, sensors and meters. Founded in 1975, the company has been listed on the Swiss Exchange (SIX) since 1995.
