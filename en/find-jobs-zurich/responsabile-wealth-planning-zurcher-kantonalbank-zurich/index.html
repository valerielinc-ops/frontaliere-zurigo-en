80% - 100% | Wealth Planning | Zurich | Experienced, experienced professionals with budget responsibility, experienced professionals with personnel responsibility, experienced professionals with project responsibility Does your heart beat for clients with challenging retirement and financial planning topics and do you want to contribute and further develop your strategic expertise, expertise and passion in a successful universal banking environment? Then you may soon be our new face for private provision and help to make the Zürcher Kantonalbank even more visible on this topic. As a leading bank in the Zurich economic area, we accompany our customers in all important life events and rely on comprehensive advice that explains complex issues professionally, comprehensibly and life-like. As a member of the private banking management teams, you will shape the further development of our successful wealth planning and position ZKB in the market with private customers and entrepreneurs as a reference for holistic pension and financial planning solutions. Your tasks

  • Strategic management and further development of the Wealth Planning department with around 100 employees in the areas of financial planning and provision, inheritances, taxes and staff/service center
  • Translating complex prevention and planning topics into a clear, understandable customer language and ensuring uniform, integral and customer-oriented advice in the market
  • Driving and shaping the bank’s pension strategy in close cooperation with product management as well as strengthening ZKB’s position in the area of pension and wealth planning
  • Promote proactive, sales-oriented and cross-sectoral collaboration
  • Lead, inspire and develop your leaders and specialists across multiple levels of leadership and promote a committed and learning-oriented culture
  • Positioning the Zürcher Kantonalbank as a competent contact in all segments
  • Advancing the digitalization of the field of wealth planning and ensuring interdisciplinary networking in the field of prevention within private banking and beyond
  • To help shape the strategic development of private banking as a member of the executive committee and contribute your networked, entrepreneurial thinking to the future of the business unit and the bank

Your profile

  • Completed university degree in economics, law or a related field as well as further education (e.g. MAS, MBA, federal dipl.). Financial planning expert: in or equivalent qualification
  • Many years of professional experience in private banking, ideally with a focus on wealth planning, financial planning and pension issues as well as experience in a sales-related management function
  • In-depth knowledge in the areas of pensions, asset and estate planning as well as taxes and the ability to translate this knowledge into an understandable, addressable customer language
  • Strategic, analytical and networked thinking as well as security in dealing with economic control parameters in order to further develop and strengthen private banking in a targeted manner
  • Proven leadership competence with experience in leading larger units across multiple levels of leadership, combined with empathy, clarity and joy in developing people
  • High affinity for market development, sales and external presence as well as the willingness to represent ZKB as a strong voice in the prevention and wealth planning market
  • Openness to change and the ability to further develop existing initiatives in the field of digitization, processes and consulting concepts
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing, good knowledge of English – every other language is a plus
  • Excellent communication, presentation and representation skills to perform convincingly both internally and externally

  • A high degree of strategic freedom and responsibility in a dynamic and future-oriented environment
  • An appreciative corporate culture that fosters diversity, respect and collaboration, as well as a motivated and dedicated team
  • Interesting employment conditions and benefits, home office opportunities and support in reconciling work and private life
  • A modern and centrally located workplace in the heart of Zurich that offers an inspiring working environment
