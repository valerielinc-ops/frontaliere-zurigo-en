Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 100% The Infrastructure and Technology department is responsible for construction projects, the maintenance and maintenance of operational facilities and buildings as well as the nursery with outdoor facilities. The object management / technology organizational unit ensures safe operation for 365 days for 24 hours. On the basis of a holistic maintenance strategy, we ensure safe building maintenance and maintenance of the building infrastructure. A helpline offers support with the Pikettdienst around the clock. For this organizational unit, which supervises our 25 locations, with work location at Lenggstrasse in Zurich, we are looking for 1. May 2027 or by agreement one or more Their tasks

  • Establishment, management and further development of the field of object management / technology (about 40 employees in total) including professional and personnel management of directly subordinate employees
  • Definition, introduction and monitoring of standards, instructions and specifications in the area of object management / technology
  • Management of state analyses and ongoing analyses of the operating characteristics and derivation and initiation of optimisation measures
  • Ensuring optimal operation of facilities and infrastructure and initiating corrective measures in the event of technical failures
  • Ensuring the implementation of safety standards and the operation of safety systems
  • Ensuring the need-based availability of the supply (energy and media) and thus ensuring a smooth 24/7 operation of the PUK infrastructures at all locations

Your profile

  • Higher education as an engineer, Dipl. Technician in HF in the field of building technology or higher specialist examination HFP with several years of management experience
  • Sound knowledge and practical experience in project management
  • Independent and responsible acting personality with self-confidence and decision-making ability
  • High communication and cooperation capacity and negotiating skills
  • Strong networked, strategic, analytical and conceptual thinking skills
  • Leadership skills and great social skills

Our offer

  • Together for people: Interdisciplinary cooperation for holistic psychiatric care
  • Space for ideas: High personal responsibility and design possibilities
  • Strong in the team: appreciative cooperation and mutual support
  • Combining family and work: access to the company’s own crèche
  • Focus on health: Various health-promoting offers, green oases and an in-house care team
  • Sustainable travel: participation in the ZVV-BonusPass or general subscription
