Full description

Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Public Prosecutor's Office Psychologists (on call) 10% The Public Prosecutor's Office I of the Canton of Zurich conducts criminal investigations against adults in the area of ​​serious violent crime across the canton. We are looking for psychologists (on call) immediately or by appointment to work on an hourly basis in interviewing child and adolescent victims of sexual and physical assault crimes. These are your tasks

  • Observation of the child from the next room during the questioning by specially trained criminal police officers
  • Ensuring child-friendly questioning
  • Write a short report on the interview (without assessing the child's statements or credibility). This is what you bring with you
  • Qualified training/degree in psychology or education
  • Practical, professional experience working with children and/or young people
  • In-depth knowledge of verbal and non-verbal communication is desired
  • Impeccable reputation, solid personality
  • Flexibility in terms of time (telephone inquiry from the Zurich cantonal and city police) This is what we offer
  • You take on responsible and socially significant work in the area of child and victim protection
  • The location is in the city of Zurich (premises of the Zurich Cantonal Police in the PJZ or the Zurich City Police Criminal Investigation Department on Mühleweg)
  • Thematic and task-specific training in the area of operation
  • Remuneration is based on an hourly wage on a contract basis. Application If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact the public prosecutors Sabine Tobler (Tel. 043 257 38 22) and Sabrina Bienz (Tel. 043 257 38 46). We look forward to receiving your application via our job portal by September 20, 2026. Information about the public prosecutor's office in the canton of Zurich can be found at www.zh.ch/sta.

Workload: 10%
