Full description

As an internal bank, the Treasury department is part of the financial administration. Among other things, she is responsible for the maintenance of business partners in SAP MDG and handles payment transactions. The Group Accounting Department is responsible for the consolidated financial statements of the City of Zurich and ensures compliance with the legal requirements of accounting. To support both departments, we are looking for reinforcement immediately or by agreement.

Tasks

  • They ensure the application management of SAP solutions FI/CO, ICMR, AFC, In House Banking, BCM, MBC and MDG-Finance and act as an interface between financial administration and computer science in Zurich.
  • They coordinate the technical processes in payment transactions and support the creation of change requests, testing and implementation in production.
  • You moderate the monthly change advisory board for handling the change requests of the listed SAP solutions.
  • They are responsible for documenting the operational and training concepts.
  • They supervise the authorization and master data concepts of the financial administration.

Profile

  • Training in business administration or business informatics with a degree such as Bachelor, HF or equivalent training with corresponding further education
  • Several years of experience in a similar function as a financial specialist SAP or as an SAP consultant
  • Stylistic German
  • In-depth knowledge in payment transactions and in the listed SAP solutions
  • Independent working method with high communication competence

We offer

  • the opportunity to work at home and at work in the center of Zurich (near Stauffacher)
  • generous training opportunities
  • A diverse field of work
  • An appreciative working atmosphere
  • very good employment conditions – social benefits, public holidays, health management

About us

Does financial management sound boring? - It's not. The variety of tasks of the city administration is unique and extends into all aspects of life (school, health, culture, transport and much more). In addition to advising and supporting the city’s areas of responsibility, we prepare the financial planning, the overall city budget and the consolidated accounts. We are also responsible for payment transactions, procurement and risk and insurance management.
