Role overview

Introduction

Together with a highly motivated team, you will develop mixed signal ASICs that form the heart of every Belimo drive and sensor and are produced millions of times.

You take over the technical responsibility for the analogue part, which is developed externally by our ASIC supplier.

Main responsibilities

  • Interest in digital circuit design (Verilog or VHDL)
  • Enthusiasm for new technologies and innovative ideas, a high level of initiative, a high awareness of quality and an independent working method
