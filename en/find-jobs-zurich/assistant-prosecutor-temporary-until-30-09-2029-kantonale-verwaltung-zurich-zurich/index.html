Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Assistant Prosecutor / Assistant Prosecutor (temporary until 30.09.2029) 80 - 100% The Public Prosecutor's Office II of the Canton of Zurich, Department B, conducts complex criminal investigations against adults in the field of narcotics and organized crime, as well as human trafficking and the promotion of prostitution. We are looking for our dedicated team at the Zurich Police and Justice Centre as of 1 January. October 2026 or by agreement a motivated personality as legal / legal assistant prosecutor / assistant prosecutor (80 - 100% – limited to 30.09.2029). Prosecuting organized crime poses increasingly complex and technically demanding challenges for law enforcement agencies. Processes are becoming more complex, international and data-intensive, requiring new solutions and specialized support. For this purpose, the public prosecutors of the Canton of Zurich have created a new job profile in the legal middle structure, which is aimed at people who are not looking for training as a prosecutor, but want to participate directly in demanding procedures in a support function. In this exciting new role, you can support our prosecutors from day one in close teamwork in their case management and directly participate in complex cases of qualified narcotics and organized crime as well as human trafficking (Department B). Their tasks include, inter alia:

  • Preparation of requests and orders for coercive measures (including secret surveillance measures)
  • Preparation of indictment and other settlements and pleas
  • Gathering of evidence (in cooperation with the police) for examinations or Preparation of hearings
  • Writing of legal memos (e.g. for complaint procedures)
  • Writing of requests for legal assistance and correspondence with foreign services This is a new job profile and therefore no candidacy with a view to obtaining the elective certificate for prosecutors is planned. The posts are temporary under a pilot project. As you convince us, prerequisites for this activity are a completed legal degree and relevant professional experience with good qualifications, preferably with law enforcement or judicial authorities or in advocacy. The attorney’s patent or similar additional professional qualifications are advantageous. Impeccable knowledge of criminal and criminal procedure law, stylish German in word and writing as well as good command of the English language are fundamental skills in our field. They also have a high level of self-motivation and work efficiently and purposefully, but with the necessary care. We also expect integrity, discretion, resilience and strong teamwork. What we offer you to work in our team is an investment in your future and a unique experience: You are right from the start in the middle of it instead of just there, you benefit from the experience and expertise of our prosecutors. Her workplace is located in the police and justice centre in Zurich. The terms of employment correspond to the cantonal guidelines. Classification is given in wage class 18. For further information, department head MLaw D. Fantoni (dominik.fantoni@ji.zh.ch) is at your disposal. We look forward to your application by September 7, 2026 at the latest via our job portal. Information about the Public Prosecutor’s Office of the Canton of Zurich can be found at www.zh.ch/sta.

Pensum: 80-100 %
