Operational service Irchel

Job opening by agreement, indefinitely

The Irchel operating service of the University of Zurich ensures the technical framework for an orderly and efficient teaching, research and service operation at the Irchel campus. The Department of Building Technology is responsible for the operation, maintenance, repair and optimization of heating, ventilation, air conditioning, refrigeration and sanitary facilities.

Tasks

  • Ensuring ventilation and air conditioning functionality
  • Maintenance and repair work
  • Initialization of operational and energy optimizations
  • Accompanying conversion and maintenance projects
  • Coordination of the update of revision plans and operating documents
  • Piketting services

Profile

  • Completed technical training as a ventilation system manufacturer/EFZ or several years of professional experience in installation and/or service in the field of ventilation/air conditioning
  • Great personal responsibility
  • PC user knowledge
  • Good oral and written expression in German. English skills are beneficial
  • Service-oriented appearance, good communication skills and well-kept manners
