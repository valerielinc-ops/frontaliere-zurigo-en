Institute of Anatomy

Job opening immediately or by agreement

At the Anatomical Institute of the University of Zurich, innovative research, committed teaching and an open, collaborative working culture meet. Our research groups deal with current biomedical questions about health, disease, regeneration and tissue plasticity. Together we create an environment in which scientific exchange, reliable cooperation and good administrative support work closely together. For our central administration, we are looking for a committed personality with pleasure in finance and a good sense of administrative processes. In this versatile function, you will assume responsibility for the financial administration of several research groups and third-party funded projects and thus directly support the scientific work of our institute. In addition, you support the institute management in the organization and coordination of central administrative tasks of the institute. They are a point of contact for internal and external concerns and work closely with researchers, professors and central offices of UZH.

Tasks

  • Independent financial administration of several research groups and third-party funded projects
  • Budget management and monitoring, forecasting and controlling, and financial reporting support
  • Processing of accounts payable and accounts payable, payment orders, rebookings, expense and credit card statements
  • Support of the institute management in general administrative tasks, in particular during events, internal and external inquiries, access rights as well as the maintenance of the institute website and online agenda

Profile

They have a commercial education and several years of professional experience in administration, ideally with a focus on finance. You like to work with numbers, keep track of several parallel tasks and appreciate an environment in which you can assume responsibility independently. Experience in a university or university environment is beneficial.

In addition, you bring the following

  • good user knowledge in MS Office; SAP knowledge is an advantage
  • stylish German and good English skills in word and writing
  • a careful, reliable and service-oriented way of working
  • Enjoy working with different contacts
