Full description

To complement our team in the care service night we are looking for 1. November 2026 or by arrangement as Certified nurse for night service (80 – 100%) With your differentiated, mindful perception, you recognize the needs of our residents with mental and multiple impairments. And as an experienced, responsible and reliable personality, you manage to take this perception into account when caring for and caring for these people. Between 8 p.m. and 7 p.m., you and our team make a significant contribution to the health and well-being of the people entrusted to us. The Wagerenhof Foundation (affectionately called “Wagi”) in Uster has been a permanent home for around 250 people with multiple cognitive impairments for more than 100 years and offers over 50 employees who need support accompanied jobs. We are a recognized, non-profit institution with a social mission. With a demand-oriented offer of living and care places as well as various day and leisure offers, we promote the quality of life, participation and self-determination of our residents. The special features of the Wagerenhof include size and Dörfli character, which allows space for encounters. For employees with different careers and professionalism, the Wagerenhof Foundation is an attractive employer with a wide range of development opportunities. For learners, Wagerenhof is a competent training partner.

These important tasks are waiting for you

  • Care and care of people with mental and multiple impairments in the team
  • Taking medical and nursing responsibility during the night
  • Adequate action in acute situations
  • Participation in interdisciplinary meetings

You fit in with us and with this position, if you also put our residents (and our supervised employees) at the center of your activities and you bring the following:

  • Training as a certified nurse at the level of HF, DN II or AKP
  • Experience in acute care
  • Experience working with people with mental and multiple impairments
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German
  • Physical and psychological resilience
  • Joy at work at night
  • Enjoyment of working in a team

This makes us the place for your work

  • The appreciative, human togetherness
  • The well thought-out overall concept
  • The village character and the park-like structure of the Wagerenhof
  • Good accessibility with public transport
  • Free parking during night service
