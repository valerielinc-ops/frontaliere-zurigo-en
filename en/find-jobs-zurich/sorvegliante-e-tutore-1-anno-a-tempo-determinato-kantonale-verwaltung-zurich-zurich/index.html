Full description

As part of the Zurich Detention Center (UGZ), the Zurich West Prison (GZW) is located in the Zurich Police and Justice Center and has 241 detention places. It specialises in temporary arrest and pre-trial detention and plays a central role in the reception and first-aid of detained persons. We are looking for committed, motivated and capable personalities with a high sense of responsibility who want to actively participate in the prison in Zurich with their expertise and positive attitude. The post shall be limited to one year. You do that with us.

  • You take responsibility for the care, advice and guidance of our inmates, always in accordance with our duty of care
  • If it gets dicey, you'll be there. You solve crisis situations with a cool head and ensure a harmonious prison everyday life
  • Your support in promoting personal development is irreplaceable and an important step on the path of the detainees to reintegration.
  • Safety is our top priority. As part of passive safety, you monitor the headquarters with all safety facilities and coordinate calmly and purposefully in emergencies.
  • Administrative activities are also part of this: You write reports, document events and thus contribute to the smooth running of our business.

  • You express yourself in German in writing and verbally skillfully and can communicate well in at least one other language
  • MS Office is not an enemy, but a friend
  • Flexibility comes naturally to you. Whether shift, weekend, night or special services – you are ready for action
  • An impeccable reputation is required (no entries in the criminal record and the enforcement record)

Zurich Detention Centers (UGZ) are one of eight main departments of JuWe. They comprise seven companies with about 735 prison places and about 500 employees. They are responsible for provisional arrest, pre-trial detention and security detention, as well as the early execution of penalties and measures until transfer to a prison facility. In this way, they fulfil a central function within the Zurich justice system right from the start. As employees of the Zurich Detention Centers, we provide structured and human support for detained persons with expertise and commitment and help ensure that criminal proceedings are carried out in an orderly manner before a decision is made on guilt or innocence. In the middle of life: The Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs of the Canton of Zurich covers a wide range of tasks.
