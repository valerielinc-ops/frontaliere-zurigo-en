Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With over 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric services offered by Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in outpatient, day clinic and inpatient settings. The group employs around 1800 people and generated sales of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For the area of adult psychiatry, the Park C ward, with the treatment focus on acute crisis, we are looking for a

The Park C ward offers support and help in mental crises to adults between the ages of 18 and 60. We work according to current clinical guidelines and findings of evidence-based medicine. The recovery-oriented therapeutic concept of the ward combines disorder-specific therapy elements with established crisis intervention techniques (Safewards concept) and milieutherapeutic effective factors.

Your tasks Your core tasks include the individual, professional care and support of patients, as a caregiver. You will use nursing diagnostics as a working tool, actively shape the therapeutic milieu of the ward and orient yourself in your way of working to the ward concept. The coordinated and multidisciplinary cooperation offers our patients the best possible treatment success. Group management is one of the

  • tasks, as well as the interdisciplinary coordination of treatment. They work closely with the social service and strive for suitable aftercare at an early stage. They offer professional crisis management and enjoy supporting patients in their recovery process.

Your profile You have completed training as a qualified nurse HF/FH and have an interest in the psychiatric field. Enjoy meeting our patients at eye level and understand adults from the age of 18 in their specific problem management. You work with the caregiver system, know the different methods of nursing treatment and support. You are oriented towards recovery and are interested in the further development of the concept and the services offered by the ward. A high-quality offer is very important to you. If you are also a team-oriented, communicative and prudent personality, we will be happy to accept your application.

Our offer You can expect a pleasant, collegial, open, supportive and interdisciplinary working atmosphere.

  • We offer you an interesting and challenging task in a dynamic, modern environment with progressive employment conditions. It is important to us that new employees are carefully trained. In order to support you in your professional development, you will be supported by your direct supervisor as well as by specific further education and training. You will also benefit from various discounts.

For further information, please contact Elisabeth Burtscher, Head of Nursing, Center for Acute Adult Psychiatry, +41 71 929 64 05.

We look forward to receiving your online application!
