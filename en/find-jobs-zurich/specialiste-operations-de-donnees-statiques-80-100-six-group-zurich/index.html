Specialist Static Data Operations (80-100%)

Zurich | 40% Home Office possible | Reference 8057

As a Specialist Static Data Operations, you work in exciting areas at the heart of stock market operations. You ensure the quality of the master and trading data of the trading platform. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a reliable personality who would like to contribute with pleasure in our dynamic and international environment.

These are your tasks

  • Ensure quality and capture of market and trade reference data
  • Processing of simple to very complex customer inquiries and cooperation in the further or new development of customer solutions
  • Close cooperation with Product Management, Change Management and SIX Exchange Regulation on various projects
  • Conducting Business Acceptance Tests in Projects and Exchange Releases

You bring that with you

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship and/or university degree preferably in business or business informatics
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in the operational field and/or in supporting clients in the financial sector
  • Good knowledge of the stock market environment, in particular knowledge of financial products over the entire lifetime
  • Understanding technical relationships and data modeling as well as strong analytical thinking
  • Engaged, independent and solution-oriented personality with exact and precise working methods
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German and English
