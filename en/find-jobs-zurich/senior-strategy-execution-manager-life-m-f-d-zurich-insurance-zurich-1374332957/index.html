Description

Does the thought of leading strategically important transformation programmes and successfully and sustainably guiding complex initiatives in the Life business of Zurich Switzerland motivate you?

In this role, you will assume overall responsibility for planning and implementing such strategic major projects. You will steer these programmes across various areas and workstreams, create transparency regarding progress and risks, and, together with experienced internal and external stakeholders, ensure successful implementation.

What you can expect

  • You lead strategic projects in the life insurance business of Zurich Switzerland and bear overall responsibility for scope, schedule, project governance, budget, quality and successful implementation.
  • You develop the implementation roadmap, structure the required workstreams and manage dependencies, milestones and resources.
  • You lead interdisciplinary project teams, coordinate internal and external partners and ensure focused collaboration across various areas.
  • You identify operational, financial and organisational risks at an early stage, develop appropriate measures and create transparency for the relevant decision-making and steering bodies.
  • You prepare steering committees and management decisions professionally, ensure communication tailored to the target audience and ensure the sustainable embedding of the results.

What sets you apart

  • Several years of experience leading complex, strategic major projects or transformation programmes, ideally in the insurance or financial services sector
  • Proven experience managing multiple workstreams and leading interdisciplinary teams and external partners
  • Very strong stakeholder management skills and confidence working with senior and executive stakeholders
  • Strong risk awareness, negotiating skills and the ability to structure complex situations and facilitate sound decisions
  • High degree of initiative, strong execution skills and clear communication, as well as very good German and English language skills

Good to know

  • Part-time or full-time: 80 - 100%
  • Location: Oerlikon, Zurich
  • Start date: immediately or by agreement
  • Travel: none
  • Hybrid working: up to 40%
