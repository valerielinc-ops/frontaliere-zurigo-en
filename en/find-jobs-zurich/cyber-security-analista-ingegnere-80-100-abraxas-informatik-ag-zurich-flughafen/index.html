Full description

Are you a team player and not only looking for a job, but also want to actively shape cyber security? Then you are exactly right in our cyber security team. In our SOC, you play a key role in protecting critical infrastructures in the Swiss authorities’ environment. With us, you combine the dynamics of a highly specialized security team with the stability of an established IT service provider – including a lot of personal responsibility, influence and room for manoeuvre. Your role

  • Responsibility for analysis, triage and incident response of complex security incidents within the framework of an established emergency response team
  • Management and coordination of security incident investigations (end-to-end)
  • Active participation and further development of our cyber security services and SOC processes
  • Technical and conceptual management of projects
  • Ensuring stable and highly available operation of our security platforms and services
  • Consulting and sparring partners for customers (including implementation of security briefings at senior level)
  • Participation in tenders – with the possibility to contribute your expertise directly
  • Participation in piquetting and extraordinary assignments outside normal working hours

Your profile

  • At least 2 years of experience in cyber security analysis and incident response (ideally SOC/CSIRT)
  • Deep practical expertise in several of the following areas: SIEM, SOAR, EDR, NDR, Threat Intelligence, Vulnerability Management
  • Very good technical understanding of networks, operating systems (Linux/Windows/macOS), firewalls and log analysis
  • Experience managing incidents or projects
  • Computer science degree or equivalent qualification
  • Recognized cyber security certifications (e.g. GIAC, CISSP, CISM, etc.)
  • Very good knowledge of German and English (German C1)
  • Structured, self-responsible and communication-strong personality with high trustworthiness

Your benefits

  • Up to 60% home office possible with a 100% workload
  • Holiday purchase (2 weeks per year)
  • 41h week with flexible working hours on a full-time workload
  • Mind. 25 days of holidays per year
  • Support for 100% financial or temporal training
  • Free SBB half-ax subscription for all employees

We maintain an open you culture – so feel free to send us your application directly in the you form.
