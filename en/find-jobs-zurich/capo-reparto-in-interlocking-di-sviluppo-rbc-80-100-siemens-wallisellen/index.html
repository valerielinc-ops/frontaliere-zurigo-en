Create a better #TomorrowWithUs Want to help shape the future of the railway world together with us? At Siemens Mobility, we are the leading provider of mobility and are constantly expanding our innovative portfolio, which includes rail vehicles, rail automation, electrification solutions, complete rail systems and accompanying services. With around 800 specialist employees in Switzerland and 39 600 employees worldwide, we shape mobility with passion and are one step ahead of our time. Shape the future of control and safety technology. You combine strategic thinking with technical excellence and understand how to inspire people for common goals. With your innovative spirit, your decision-making power and your ability to successfully lead interdisciplinary teams, you are actively driving the transformation of classical signalling technologies towards scalable, digital platform solutions. Your tasks

  • You lead and develop a department with around 20 employees in terms of technical, organizational and personnel.
  • You are responsible for the strategic planning and control of development projects in the field of signal boxes and RBC with a focus on platform architectures, modern development environments, automation and scalability.
  • You ensure the successful implementation of complex projects in terms of quality, costs and deadlines.
  • You will actively develop the technological roadmap and provide impulses for innovative and forward-looking solutions.
  • You promote the continuous development of development processes and drive efficiency improvements and standardization.
  • You will work closely with development, product management and other specialist areas to exploit synergies and integrate new market requirements into product development at an early stage.
  • You ensure compliance with the high demands of a safety-critical development environment. Your profile
  • Completed university technical studies (master) in electronics, software engineering, mechanical engineering or a comparable field of study.
  • Further training in business administration or a sound understanding of business relations.
  • Several years of leadership experience in the management of development or project teams, ideally in the environment of complex technical systems or safety-critical applications and at department manager level
  • Experience in the development of platforms, system architectures or integrated technical solutions.
  • Strong understanding of modern development processes, automation and scalable software and system landscapes.
  • Very good German and English skills in word and writing You benefit from
  • an open and flexible working culture at one of the leading companies in the field of mobility in Switzerland
  • a 40-hour week on a 100% fee
  • at least 5 weeks of holidays

  • Various employee benefits and training opportunities
