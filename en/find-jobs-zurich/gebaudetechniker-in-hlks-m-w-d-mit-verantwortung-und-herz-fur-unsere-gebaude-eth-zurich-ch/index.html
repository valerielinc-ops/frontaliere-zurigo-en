Building technician: in HLKS (m/f/d) – with responsibility and heart for our buildings

100%, Zurich, perpetual

In the Facility Services department of ETH Zurich, we create the optimal conditions for teaching, research and technology transfer. We maintain the buildings and ensure the energy and media supply as well as the technical and infrastructural management of all rooms used by ETH Zurich.

Are you enthusiastic about technical challenges and would like to work where sophisticated building technology enables the operation of teaching and research? Even in a dynamic environment, do you keep an overview and ensure smooth operation with your commitment? Then we look forward to meeting you.

For the Hönggerberg location, we are looking for a:n building technician:in HLKS (m/f/d) in the building area HI.

Job description

In this versatile and responsible position, you and your team ensure that all HVAC systems are operated safely, trouble-free and energy-efficiently.

You plan, prioritize and coordinate maintenance and repair measures to ensure that all technical systems function reliably at all times. You coordinate external service providers, accompany their operations on site, check the quality of the work carried out and ensure that agreed maintenance contracts are adhered to.

The planning, procurement and management of replacement and reserve materials is also your responsibility. With your technical expertise, you actively contribute to the continuous optimization of operations. You will analyze processes as well as control and regulation systems, identify potential for improvement and implement appropriate measures independently.

When commissioning and accepting new and existing plants, you contribute your HVAC expertise and ensure safe, reliable and efficient operation. During construction projects, you represent the interests of building technology, coordinate interfaces, work closely with project teams and participate in construction meetings.

In quality management, you ensure compliance with internal standards, maintain plant data in GMIS/Planon and actively advance optimizations. In addition, you take care of piketting services as well as tasks in shift operations, such as opening and closing buildings.

You also play an important role in the area of security: You provide personal and operational security, supervise security areas, respond to alarms, provide first aid in an emergency and support the BMA and EVAK equipment.

In short, you are a pillar for smooth, safe and modern building operations. With your expertise, your sense of responsibility and your focus on the whole, you make a decisive contribution to the success of the company.

Profile

  • At least 5 years of experience in the operation, maintenance and troubleshooting of complex technical installations.
  • In-depth expertise, analytical thinking and the ability to assess and specifically improve processes, control strategies and system behavior.
  • Organizational skill, flexibility and experience in dealing with different contact groups – from specialists to external partners.
  • Careful, structured working methods and willingness to systematically maintain and develop operational and plant data.
  • Responsibility, reliability and the ability to act calmly and professionally in security-related situations.
  • High level of teamwork, clear and step-oriented communication, solution-oriented working methods and enjoyment of an academically demanding environment.
  • Good MS Office and CAFM skills, as well as very good German and English skills.
