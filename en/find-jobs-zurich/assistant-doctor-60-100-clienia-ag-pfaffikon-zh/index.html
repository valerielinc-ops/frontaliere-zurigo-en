Full description

With Clienia Ambulant, the Clienia Group bundles its psychiatric-psychotherapeutic services for the best possible outpatient and day-to-day clinical care for mentally ill patients of all ages – at 20 locations from Amriswil to Zurich. Interdisciplinary teams of psychotherapists, psychotherapists, nursing specialists and specialists in psychiatry and psychotherapy guarantee comprehensive outpatient treatment methods.

For the Männedorf location, we are looking for a

The Clienia Psychiatry Center Zurich Lake (PZZ) is an outpatient institution of Clienia Schlössli AG (Medical Director Prof. Dr. med. Wolfram Kawohl). As part of the Wetzikon Psychiatric Center (PZW), it provides psychiatric care in the Zurich Oberland/Zurichsee region at the Männedorf location. The psychiatric centers run polyclinics with a wide range of special consultation hours, two day clinics, a consultancy and liaison psychiatric service and are recognized as ambulatory training centers of category A (3 years).

Their tasks The position includes a broad policlinical activity as an assistant doctor with the conduct of clarification interviews as well as the psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment of all psychiatric disorders in the entire area of adult and age psychiatry. They participate in the emergency, consular and liaison services of the supply region.

Your profile We expect you to have several years of inpatient and outpatient psychiatric experience and advanced or completed psychotherapeutic training. They work independently within the framework of the degree of training as well as interest in psychiatric-psychotherapeutic activity and enjoyment of the best possible, responsible and networked patient care.

Our offer We offer you interesting and varied work in an interdisciplinary team, an extensive range of in-house and external training and supervision, regular working hours (Monday to Friday without night and weekend service) and progressive employment conditions. Recognition as a training centre for the specialist psychiatry and psychotherapy category A, for the focus on consultative and liaison psychiatry category A, for the focus on old-age psychiatry and psychotherapy category B as well as for the focus on psychiatry and psychotherapy of addiction diseases category B. On request, there is the possibility of expert work.

For further information, Mr. David Rohrbach, Chief Physician, Tel. +41 43 843 32 00 available. (Please direct your online application to Mr. David Rohrbach, Senior Doctor).
