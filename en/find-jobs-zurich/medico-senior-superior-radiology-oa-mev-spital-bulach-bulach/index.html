Tasks

  • Depending on qualification and suitability, direct employment as OA meV or a development to OA meV is also possible
  • Independent radiological diagnostics, including reports as part of the service mandate for outpatient and inpatient patients
  • Maintenance of contact with assigning doctors
  • Cooperation in internal departmental training or further development of the radiological department

Requirements

  • Completed specialist training in radiology
  • Several years of experience in the diagnosis of CT, MRI, ultrasound, mammography and digital X-ray examinations
  • Responsible action
  • High social skills, flexibility and enjoyment of teamwork

For employment as MEV/later development to meV additionally

  • Several years of senior medical experience
  • Subspecialization present, preferably in musculoskeletal imaging
  • Designated professional and organisational competencies
  • Willingness to assume additional professional and organisational responsibility
