Tasks

Because addiction disorders practically never occur in isolation and the integrative treatment of comorbidities is essential for our concept, you are responsible for the psychotherapeutic treatment of patients with disorders of the entire psychiatric spectrum. They also carry out outpatient assessments and assessments.

Requirements

Prerequisite is a completed psychology degree with an advanced or completed psychotherapy education. Experience in the treatment of addiction diseases is advantageous.
