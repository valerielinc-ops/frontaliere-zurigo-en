Head:in service of use (m/f/d)

Printed Printing

The Archiv für Zeitgeschichte AfZ collects private estates and institutional archives on the history of Switzerland in the 20th and 21st centuries. These are of supraregional importance and document the history of Switzerland in a transnationally networked world. It complements the formation of state traditions and offers research and private individuals access to multi-layered archival stocks. The focus of the collection activities is on politics, economics and Jewish contemporary history.

The job starts on 1 October or by agreement.

Job description

  • In this position, you are responsible for the lending and use of the Archive for Contemporary History at ETH Zurich.
  • Personnel management and training and guidance of the user service team and of trainees:
  • Authorisation and processing of requests for access
  • Collection of user service key figures and reporting
  • Collaboration in the networking and mediation activities of the AfZ in archival and historical studies
  • Cooperation in the field of development, management of small development projects

Profile

  • MA degree in contemporary history, ideally professional knowledge in the three main focus areas of the AfZ collection
  • At least three years of professional experience in an archive
  • Good knowledge of various media (AV media and written material)
  • Good knowledge of the norms and standards of the archival development and preparation of collection material on different media
  • Safe handling of computer tools (administrative software and archival information systems)
  • Joy of contact with customers: inside
  • Very good knowledge of German and English and editorial skills
  • Very good French skills desired
  • Driving licence
  • You are accustomed to precise and structured work
