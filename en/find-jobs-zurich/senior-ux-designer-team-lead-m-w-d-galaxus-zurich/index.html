Full description

As a Senior UX Designer & Teamlead, you design our online shops for over 4 million customers in Switzerland and the EU. You take responsibility for your product area and the personnel development of two UX designers. In a collegial environment, you get the freedom to contribute your expertise and actively develop the user experience of our online shops.

Mansioni

  • Responsibility for the user experience of your product area as part of a cross-functional product team of developers, product owners, analysts and UX researchers
  • Active participation in the further development of our online shops and ensuring a high design quality
  • Responsibility for the visual design process in the team within the design system as well as ensuring compliance with our design guidelines
  • Development and validation of solutions in an iterative human-centered design process based on qualitative and quantitative data, mockups and prototypes
  • Comprehensive communication of complex topics through convincing presentations and active sharing of your expertise in the team
  • Personnel management and development of two UX designers as Senior UX Designer with Teamlead responsibility (20% of your working time)

Requirements

  • 5 years: User Experience Design
  • Completed degree (uni/ETH, FH, HF): Interaction Design, Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), UX Design, Product Design, Communication/Visual Design
  • At least 5 years of professional experience as a UX designer
  • In-depth experience in user interface design
  • Experience with design systems is beneficial
  • Strong ability to design robust solutions for complex, branched processes with numerous special cases and dependencies
  • Practical experience in agile product development and interdisciplinary teams, ideally in the e-commerce environment
  • Initial leadership experience as well as strength in the development and motivation of UX designers combined with a solution-oriented way of working
  • The willingness to question “as it has always been done” and propose more efficient, automated or effective solutions
  • Emotional intelligence to promote human-centred approaches and solutions
  • Using creativity and initiative to solve problems and achieve goals
  • German (flowing)
  • English (flowing)
