Student Assistant: in Human Medicine for Planetary Health (m/f/d)

10%-20%, Zurich, temporary

Printed Printing

ETH Zurich has been conducting the bachelor’s degree program in human medicine since the autumn semester 2017. This is developed in close cooperation with the university partners for the further master education (University of Basel, University of Zurich and Università della Svizzera italiana). We are looking for a student assistant (10-20%) for a temporary project in the field of Planetary Health at the earliest possible time. The employment is limited until the end of 2026 and should be able to start at the beginning of September at the latest.

Project background

The project PHUSE (Planetary Health for Universities’ Sustainability Engagement), funded by swissuniversities, is a national initiative to integrate the concept of Planetary Health into health-related courses at universities throughout Switzerland. As part of the project, a survey will be carried out at universities with health-related degree programmes. The aim is to record and evaluate the current state of implementation of planetary health topics.

Job description

  • Preparation of a survey on the implementation of Planetary Health at Swiss universities in the field of health sciences
  • Send the survey to different universities and communicate with contacts
  • Evaluation of the results and writing of a scientific report (DE/EN)
  • Flexible working hours largely possible in the home office with regular meetings

Profile

  • Enrolled ETH student or student
  • Initial experience in creating and evaluating surveys
  • Interested in Planetary Health
  • An independent, exact and pragmatic way of working
  • Stylistic German and negotiation-safe knowledge of English
