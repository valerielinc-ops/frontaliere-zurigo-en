Full description

For our surgery care area at the Waid site, we are looking for a competent personality on 1 September or by appointment.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for carrying out care interventions with a patient group or on delegation. In addition, you take on administrative tasks.
  • You are responsible for the professional documentation, performance recording and medical technology activities.
  • You competently ensure an efficient and targeted exchange of information.
  • You actively contribute to constructive interdisciplinary cooperation and work with high service quality.

Profile

  • Training as a health professional / health professional EFZ
  • Professional experience in an acute hospital is absolutely necessary
  • Independent and self-responsible working methods as well as a strong quality and service awareness
  • Willingness to take on preferably shortened mid- and late services (4 hours)
  • Friendly and cultivated manners

We offer

We offer you more than just a job! Our city hospital world is characterized by an open dual culture, an uncomplicated, personal togetherness, respect and appreciation. As a team of over 120 professional groups, we work every day in very different ways for the well-being of our patients. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions and excellent social benefits from the city of Zurich.

About us

With over 36'000 inpatients and around 455,000 outpatients per year, the Stadtspital Zürich ranks among the top 10 Swiss hospitals. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,500 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
