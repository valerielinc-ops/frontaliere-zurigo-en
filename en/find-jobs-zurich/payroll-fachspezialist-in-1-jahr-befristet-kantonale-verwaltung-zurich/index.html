Canton of Zurich Financial Directorate Payroll Specialist (1 year limited) 80 - 100% The Staff Office of the Canton of Zurich is the competence centre for all personnel, labour and social security issues of the more than 35,000 employees of the Canton of Zurich as well as many public institutions and municipalities. The Payroll team in the Financial Directorate coordinates and monitors the salary-relevant data management in SAP and ensures a legally correct billing of social insurance and withholding tax as well as family allowances. The Payroll department is also the contact point for directorates, offices and employees for questions about wage processing and wage calculation as well as for account reconciliations in this area. Due to a major project and the resulting resource shortage, we are looking for committed, service-oriented and numerical support in the area of account reconciliation. The position is limited to one year for the time being. Your tasks

  • You reconcile the payroll accounts assigned to you monthly and ensure a correct booking.
  • You check and edit the content of the SAP interfaces and support with questions about bookings and wage types.
  • You analyze differences in coordination, clarify these with the relevant specialist areas and ensure a sustainable solution.
  • You support in the creation of control reports and evaluations and in the year-end account reconciliations.
  • You will compile the necessary audit documents and coordinate cooperation with the audit and the team.
  • You bring in ideas to optimize the voting processes and implement them if possible.
  • With free capacities, you support the Payroll team in day-to-day business and projects. Your profile
  • You have a basic commercial education (EFZ) and several years of professional experience, ideally in wage accounting and account reconciliation.
  • Further training in the payroll or financial sector is advantageous.
  • You are well-versed in dealing with EXCEL and ideally bring SAP skills.
  • You communicate verbally and in writing stylishly in German and have a networked mindset.
  • Teamwork, secrecy, independence, number flair and resilience distinguish you. We offer
  • The opportunity to actively engage and make a difference.
  • Enough challenges, it will not be boring.
  • A solid, fair and reliable employer.
  • Attractive employment conditions such as flexible working hours, a modern workplace, lunch checks, etc.
  • A central place of work in the middle of Zurich, just a few minutes walk from the main train station. Do you feel addressed? But you come to us not only because of the benefits, but above all because you want to make a difference, like to work in a team and achieve goals together with us.
