Full description

From 1 January 2027 to 30 January June 2027 we are looking for a senior doctor internal medicine - temporary 80-100%

Your area of responsibility

  • In a team of 7 senior doctors, together with the chief physician and the 12 leading doctors, they ensure the medical care of our patients.
  • Its field of application includes the bed stations, the interdisciplinary emergency unit in the shift system, intensive care unit and our day clinic
  • They supervise and supervise our assistant doctors and actively participate in their further education
  • You participate in the internal emergency service as well as in our internal consultancy service

Your profile

  • You have a specialist title of Internal Medicine or are about to complete it
  • First experience as a senior doctor is desired, but not condition
  • You have experience in an internist emergency and intensive care unit
  • They are characterised by high commitment, commitment and social competence
  • The joy of interdisciplinary cooperation and the teaching of assistant doctors round off your profile

What we offer you

  • Working independently in a motivated and collegial team that supports each other.
  • Broad and exciting internal medicine
  • Further development of your internistic skills (including ultrasound)
  • A place of work with the best connection to public transport, as well as cost-effective staff parking spaces.
  • Modern infrastructure and a working environment in which you can feel at home – lake views included.
  • Various benefits – from great employee discounts to further education offers.
  • A pleasant working atmosphere in an attractive location near Zurich, which has a lot to offer you both professionally and privately.
