Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Career Advice Social Worker in Youth and Family Advice, kjz Winterthur, 60 - 90% The Office for Youth and Vocational Counselling of the Education Directorate provides, appoints, controls and coordinates offers of child and youth welfare and vocational counselling. The kjz Winterthur (Children and Youth Assistance Centre) is a polyvalent counselling centre for families, parents, adolescents, children and young children and works on the basis of the Children and Youth Assistance Act of the Canton of Zurich. The youth and family counselling (JFB) of kjz Winterthur is looking for a social worker 60 - 90% as a relief on January 2027. The post is limited to one year with an option of renewal. As an assistant, you carry out child protection measures under civil law, carry out child welfare assessments on behalf of KESB and advise families, children and young people on their assignments. The consultations cover a wide range of topics on family organisation, upbringing, separation and divorce, visitation rights issues and child protection issues. Good and close cooperation with authorities, schools, institutions and specialist bodies is very important. They bring with them:

  • Training at a Swiss University of Applied Sciences for Social Work
  • Experience in youth and family assistance and statutory social work
  • Optional additional training in systemic, solution-oriented consulting
  • Empathy for people in special life situations
  • Negotiable performance skills
  • Resilient personality
  • Good written knowledge We offer you:
  • Good introduction and coaching by the head of department
  • Collegial exchange as well as intervisions and supervision
  • Demanding, meaningful and self-employed work
  • Living work that takes place in the middle of the life of a wide variety of population groups
  • Supporting interdisciplinary team
  • Fair employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines The kjz Director Regula Kupper (tel. 043 259 94 30) is at your disposal for further questions. We look forward to your online application. www.ajb.zh.ch

Pensum: 60-90 %
