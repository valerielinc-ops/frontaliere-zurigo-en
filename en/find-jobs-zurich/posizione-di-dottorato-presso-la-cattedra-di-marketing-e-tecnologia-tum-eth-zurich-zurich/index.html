Doctoral Position at the Professorship of Marketing & Technology (TUM)

70%-80%, Zurich, fixed-term print Drucken

We are recruiting a research associate / Ph.D. candidate for a cross-border research project (funded by SNSF’s & DFG’s WEAVE scheme) on Virtual Reality, jointly hosted at ETH Zurich (Chair of Technology Marketing) and the TUM Campus Munich (Professorship of Marketing & Technology).

  • The SNF & DFG funded project investigates how people perceive time and space in immersive virtual environments, approaches related questions in an interdisciplinary manner (i.e., at the intersection of psychology, technology, and human–computer interaction).
  • We welcome candidates with a strong interest in fundamental research and, ideally, prior experience in VR research.
  • The project duration is set for three years. Job description Your role in the team
  • Work directly with the Principal Investigators at ETH Zurich and TUM to develop the research agenda, refine study designs, and drive the project toward top-tier publications.
  • Position B — TUM Campus Munich (behavioral & psychology focus)
  • Primary emphasis on experimental design, consumer psychology/neuroscience, and data analysis using VR as a research tool and/or to study the peculiarities of VR.
