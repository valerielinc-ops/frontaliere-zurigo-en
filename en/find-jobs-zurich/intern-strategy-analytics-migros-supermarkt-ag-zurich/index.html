Full description

Migros is changing. As an intern in Strategy & Analytics, you will work on central strategic issues and support the further development of the sales strategy. In close cooperation with the ten regional cooperatives and diverse stakeholders, you actively shape the future of Migros.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Supermarket AG, Limmatplatz Zurich, Limmatstrasse 152, 8005 Zurich

Important information

Temporary for 6 months

Mansioni

  • Participation in the implementation of strategic initiatives of Migros Supermarket AG
  • Implementation of analyses and development of decision-making bases for strategic issues
  • Support for the further development of the management and governance landscape of Migros Supermarket AG
  • Participation in concrete strategy and transformation projects from conception to implementation
  • Cooperation with the ten regional cooperatives and other stakeholders along the entire value chain
  • Coordination and coordination with interdisciplinary project teams at different organizational levels

Requirements

  • Professional experience: Previous internships or professional experience in the field of retail and/or strategy consulting are beneficial
  • Master's degree (university, university of applied sciences): in economics, data science, business administration or similar
  • Analytical thinking skills as well as interest in strategic issues and data-based decisions
  • Quick comprehension and the ability to capture complex issues in a structured way
  • Independent, reliable and goal-oriented working method
  • Strong communication and organizational skills as well as enjoy working with a wide range of stakeholders
  • Affinity for data analysis as well as very good knowledge of MS Office applications, in particular Excel and PowerPoint
  • Interest in retail, strategy development and transformation projects
  • German (flowing)
  • French (good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central place of work: Central place of work in the city of Zurich (Limmatplatz or near Hardbrücke)
  • Modern infrastructure: Diverse work zones and modern co-working spaces with advanced equipment
  • Fair wages: functional and market-oriented remuneration - "We pay fair" certified
  • Leisure and cultural activities: As the main sponsor of runs and events, Migros offers employees limited seats at half price.
  • Targeted promotion: Promotion of our employees with targeted personnel development measures
  • Self-responsibility & freedom: Varied tasks with a high level of self-responsibility
  • Design and decision-making leeway: Possibility to independently shape and further develop the field of responsibility
