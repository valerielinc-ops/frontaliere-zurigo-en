Full description

We design Zurich from the ground up: liveable and climate-neutral. Bring your expertise and work with us on a future-oriented city. As of 1 March 2027 or by appointment, we are looking for you for our 20-month trainee program.

Tasks

  • You work on challenging projects in various business areas: Transport + Urban Space / Planning + Project Planning / Realization / Preservation of Value.
  • You lead road construction and plant management projects under guidance.
  • You work in interdisciplinary project teams.
  • You use tools of project management and controlling.

Profile

  • Higher education in civil engineering, transport engineering, environmental engineering or infrastructure systems (Bachelor or Master)
  • Stylistic German in Word and Writing
  • Self-responsible and service-oriented action
  • Flexibility and willingness to learn
  • Interest in project implementation in the sense of digital planning, construction and operation

We offer

  • The opportunity to shape the future of the largest Swiss city.
  • A workplace located within walking distance to the HB Zurich.
  • The opportunity to build up your own network within and outside the administration.
  • Individual coaching in order to fully exploit your potential and to grow both professionally and personally – with versatile training and further education opportunities, be it professional, personal or especially in the field of digitalization in the construction industry.
  • The opportunity to combine work and private life with flexible working hours, home office and a holiday model.
  • Very good remuneration, contribution to food costs, health and mobility contributions and a solid pension fund with an above-average share of the employer.
  • A culture that is shaped by respectful, reliable, ambitious and inclusive values and an employer who lives equality, diversity and inclusion.
  • Good chances for a permanent position as overall project manager.

About us

For a high quality of life: The approximately 350 employees of the civil engineering office shape the mobility and public space in the largest city in Switzerland every day.
