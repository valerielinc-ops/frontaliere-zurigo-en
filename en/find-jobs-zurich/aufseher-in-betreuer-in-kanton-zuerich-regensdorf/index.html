Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Correctional Services and Reintegration Pöschwies Correctional Facility Supervisor / Supervisor 80 - 100% With around 1,400 employees, Corrections and Reintegration (JuWe) is the largest office within the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs and is responsible for the execution of sentences and measures as well as for the implementation of various types of detention. Our employees from around 40 professions support and promote the reintegration of people who have committed crimes and work effectively and progressively to ensure a safe and fair society. The Pöschwies Correctional Facility (JVA) is one of eight main departments of JuWe and the largest closed facility for men in Switzerland. With around 400 prison places, it contributes to the protection of the general public in accordance with its legal mandate and at the same time creates the conditions for the rehabilitation of the imprisoned people.
