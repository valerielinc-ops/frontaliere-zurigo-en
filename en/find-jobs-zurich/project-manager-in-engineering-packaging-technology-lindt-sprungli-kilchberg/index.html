About us

We enchant the world with chocolate! Lindt & Sprüngli (Schweiz) AG is a subsidiary of the global Lindt & Sprüngli Group with headquarters and production facilities in Kilchberg on Lake Zurich. We operate our own logistics center in Altendorf, while the Lindt Cocoa Center in Olten produces cocoa mass for our own use and for other European production facilities. We proudly operate 15 Lindt Shops across Switzerland, including iconic locations such as the Jungfraujoch and the Swiss Museum of Transport in Lucerne. Over 1,000 employees from more than 50 nations contribute to our success.

What you do with us

  • Independent planning and implementation of technical investment projects (including adapting or converting existing packaging systems or components). Format parts, sensors, gluing devices, vision systems (data matrix)
  • Management of projects to increase the efficiency of packaging systems
  • Evaluation of new packaging systems or components
  • Estimating necessary adjustments to packaging systems and budgeting costs
  • Technical feasibility test or evaluation for industrialization for new packaging concepts on existing packaging systems
  • Support or carry out test runs when changing suppliers or to optimize existing packaging concepts
  • Finding technical solutions for the use of new materials on existing systems (Sustainable Packaging Initiative)
  • Supporting the installation and commissioning of purchased machines or modifications to packaging machines in the factory
  • Support with operator and maintenance training in the packaging area
  • Know-how transfer engineering/maintenance

When you're right for us

  • Completed studies at HF/FH or university/ETH level in packaging technology, process engineering, mechanical engineering or a comparable field
  • At least 3 years of professional experience in a comparable position
  • Experience in managing projects in the food industry
  • Knowledge of lean manufacturing and TPM (Total Productive Management), especially early management
  • Very good knowledge of German (mother tongue or business fluent)
  • Good knowledge of spoken and written English
  • Confident use of drawing programs (e.g. AutoCAD)
