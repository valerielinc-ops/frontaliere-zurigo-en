The Institute of Anesthesiology and Perioperative Medicine provides more than 30,000 services annually in outpatient and inpatient anesthesiology, in internal and extraclinical emergency medicine as well as in acute and chronic pain medicine. As part of the highly specialized medicine at the University Hospital Zurich, the Institute of Anesthesiology and Perioperative Medicine also has performance contracts for the transplantation surgery of all solid organs and the care of all cardiac and vascular surgical procedures as well as for the treatment of severe burn injuries and multiple injuries.

Their main tasks

  • Anesthesiological care of patients of all disciplines
  • Operational activities in all areas of operation
  • Participation in the operation
  • Supervision and further training of assistant doctors and specialists as well as experts in anesthesia care
  • Engagement in the clinical and academic development of the institute

Your profile

  • They are highly motivated, resilient, solution-oriented and creative
  • A completed promotion is required
  • You have completed specialist training in anesthesiology with experience in regional anesthesia
  • Ideally, you already have leadership experience
  • An own research activity is an advantage
  • Promote your empathetic and inclusive personality It sustains interprofessional and interdisciplinary cooperation, team spirit and patient trust
