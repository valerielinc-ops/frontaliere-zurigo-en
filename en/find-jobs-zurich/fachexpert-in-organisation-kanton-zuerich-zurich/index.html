Canton of Zurich State Chancellery Digital Administration Expert Expert Organisation 80 - 100% The canton of Zurich's administration is undergoing a change: digital solutions are being developed, new offers for the population and the economy are being built up – and this is also linked to changes in roles, structures and cooperation. The task is to design structure and process organisation in such a way that they meet the requirements of a networked and digital administration. An important focus is the organisational approach to artificial intelligence.

Within the Digital Administration division, we are responsible for the organisational development and design the necessary framework conditions for this, so that comprehensive topics and projects can be implemented and anchored in a coordinated manner. Are you ready to actively shape the future of the Canton of Zurich? Together with you, we implement the Digital Administration Strategy 2025+ and thus shape the digital transformation of the Canton of Zurich. As of January 2027 or by agreement, we are looking for an expert organisation, 80 - 100%.
