Full description

BMS Building Materials Suisse is a wholesaler of structural and interior construction materials. Our group of companies includes the renowned specialists Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced experts advise, support and inspire customers from the construction industry as well as private customers at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. Close to everyday construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Quick, easy and competent.

Temporary period from December 2026 to July 2027

You are at home in the HR administration, like to work in a structured way and appreciate the contact with employees and managers? Then you are exactly right with us! For our HR team in Rümlang, we are looking for a committed and service-oriented personality from December 2026, who will support us in a temporary assignment until July 2027 and will contribute her experience in HR administration and exciting HR projects.

Task area

  • Independent handling of all administrative HR processes along the entire employee lifecycle – from entry via mutations to exit
  • Preparation of employment contracts, agreements, confirmations, employment certificates and other HR correspondence
  • Processing of personnel mutations as well as maintaining and updating personnel master data in our HR systems
  • Administrative support for absentee management and handling of social insurance issues
  • Competent contact person for employees and managers in administrative HR issues
  • Close cooperation with HR Business Partners, Payroll Team and other internal contacts
  • Active participation in HR projects as well as support in the optimization and further development of existing HR processes

Profile

If your heart beats faster in the task area described above and you bring the following skills, you are our desired candidate:

  • Basic commercial training, ideally supplemented by further training in human resources
  • Professional experience in operational HR administration as well as good knowledge of Swiss HR processes
  • Very good knowledge of German and French in word and writing
  • High digital affinity including AI and safe handling of MS Office
  • Independent, structured and exact working methods as well as a high degree of discretion and sense of responsibility
  • Service-oriented, flexible and team-capable personality who quickly familiarizes himself with new tasks

We offer

  • a challenging, versatile and interesting job in a good working environment in which active cooperation and self-employment are emphasised
  • Intensive training is a program
  • 5 weeks of holiday (6 weeks from 50). Year of age), additional benefits such as an excellently financed independent pension fund and benefits on our complete product range as well as external premium benefits
