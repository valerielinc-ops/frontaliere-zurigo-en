ERP Developer (NAV) — Brack.Alltron AG

Full description

Tasks

  • Further development of our comprehensive and highly individualized Microsoft Dynamics NAV 2009 solution in the logistics environment
  • Develop scalable solutions for business-critical warehouse and e-commerce processes
  • Active participation in architectural, quality and product decisions within the team
  • Gradual modernization and improvement of a code base that has grown over many years
  • Analysis of existing legacy structures and informed decisions about what to develop, isolate or replace
  • Development and further development of integrations between NAV and surrounding systems
  • Close cooperation with product management, team lead and other software engineers
  • Active participation in refinements and in the joint design of requirements
  • Exchange of knowledge through pair programming, code reviews and joint technical discussions
  • Working with Scrum or Kanban – suitable for the needs of the team

Requirements

  • Several years of practical development experience with Microsoft Dynamics NAV or Business Central
  • Sound experience with C/AL in an individualized NAV application
  • Secure handling of grown codebases and a good understanding of maintainable, clean code
  • Experience with integrations between NAV and third-party systems, for example via SOAP Web Services
  • Ability to analyze complex legacy code and develop it pragmatically
  • Enjoy designing technical solutions together with product management and other engineers
  • Product-oriented thinking and interest in truly understanding the technical issues behind requirements
  • Openness to short iterations, feedback and continuous learning
  • Experience with logistics, warehouse or WMS systems is a plus
  • Experience with ERP migrations is also beneficial

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Supercompulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business meetings.
