Full description

Do you have a heart for the elderly? Do you want to express your creativity and passion for high-end gastronomy and at the same time delight our customers? Do you see opportunities to bring your skills and experience to us, to have a positive impact on the lives of our guests? Then you are in the right place!

Tertianum, as a market leader in the field of life, living, and care for the elderly, significantly contributes to improving the quality of life of older people. Tertianum is present with residences and care centers in around 100 locations throughout Switzerland.

The exclusive Tertianum Residence Horgen, with 57 modern apartments and 21 spacious care single rooms, as well as an acute geriatrics unit with 20 beds, offers guests a suitable home in the upscale segment. Guests are offered lived hospitality to maintain an individual lifestyle. In the public restaurant „elegante“, fresh market and balanced dishes are also served to external guests.
