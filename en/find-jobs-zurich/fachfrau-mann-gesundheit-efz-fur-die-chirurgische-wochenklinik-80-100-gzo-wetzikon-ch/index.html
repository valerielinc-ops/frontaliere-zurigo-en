Full description

You want to maintain as a FaGe versatile and still have every weekend free? On our mixed bed department, we look after women who have recently given birth and their newborns as well as patients at the weekly surgical clinic. Your focus is on the care of surgical patients. Because the weekly clinic is open from Monday to Friday, you work without weekend and night services. You can expect a motivated team that sticks together, learns from each other and supports each other. With your expertise, your empathy and your commitment, you ensure that our patients feel professionally competent and well looked after. Become part of our team as of 1 November 2026 or by agreement

Tasks

  • You care for and care for surgical patients before and after surgery as well as during their inpatient stay.
  • You take on tasks of basic and treatment care, for example vital sign checks, wound care and the direction and administration of medication according to medical prescription.
  • You observe the health status of your patients, recognize changes at an early stage and inform the responsible nurse.
  • You also accompany people in sensitive situations sensitively and professionally.
  • You contribute to entry and exit and document your work carefully.
  • You work closely with nurses, doctors and other specialist areas. Requirements
  • You have completed an education as a specialist health EFZ or an equivalent education recognised in Switzerland.
  • You enjoy learning new things and developing your expertise.
  • You meet patients with empathy, respect and professionalism.
  • You enjoy working in a team, communicating openly and contributing your ideas.
  • You take responsibility and appreciate a varied work day. You can look forward to an introduction that deserves this name You will be structured and carefully introduced to your new area of responsibility.
