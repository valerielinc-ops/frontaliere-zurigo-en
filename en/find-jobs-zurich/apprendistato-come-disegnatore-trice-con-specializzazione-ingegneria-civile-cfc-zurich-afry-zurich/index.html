Full description

With a draughtsman apprenticeship at AFRY Schweiz AG, you will set the course for your professional future. We offer you a varied and challenging apprenticeship in the exciting world of an engineering company. Over a period of four years, you will gain interesting insights into various specialist areas and conclude your apprenticeship with a federal certificate of competence at the end. As of August 2027, we have two vacant apprenticeship positions at our headquarters in Zurich-Altstetten: DRAUGHTSMAN WITH A SPECIALIZATION IN CIVIL ENGINEERING - ZURICH During the apprenticeship, you will learn the following activities, among others:

  • Creation of digital models and plans in civil engineering (including public buildings, residential buildings, bridges, roads, sewerage systems and utility lines)
  • Creation of digital models and plans in tunnel construction (e.g. road and rail tunnels, tunnels)
  • Collaborate with civil engineers and assist with construction inspections
  • Field assignments in cooperation with surveying
  • Construction site internship of at least 2 weeks, at the earliest in the 4th semester

For the apprenticeship, you preferably have the following profile:

  • Completed elementary school (Sek A or good Sek B)
  • Interests / strengths in geometry, mathematics and physics
  • Enjoyment of technical drawing and precise work
  • Good spatial awareness
  • Basic computer skills and interest in technology

We offer an exciting and varied apprenticeship, in a very easily accessible location, with modern employment conditions in a collegial and helpful team. We look forward to receiving your online application!
