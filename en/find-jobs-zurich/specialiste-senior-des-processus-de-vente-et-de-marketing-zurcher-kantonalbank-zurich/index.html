Full description

80% - 100% | Product and channel management | Zurich | Experienced professionals In the sales and marketing processes team, you actively shape the sales platform and processes of the Zürcher Kantonalbank. You are responsible for change projects and processes for sales, sales and marketing – from attracting and qualifying prospects to converting into customer relationships as well as supporting cross-/upselling and churn activities for existing customers. In this way, you make a direct contribution to ensuring that we accompany our customers even more specifically along the entire customer journey and can further develop the interaction with our customers in a demand-oriented manner. Your tasks

  • Managing change projects in the field of sales, sales and marketing processes
  • Promoting and further developing bank capabilities in the functional area of sales and marketing processes
  • Design and optimize end-to-end processes in sales, sales and marketing
  • Translating professional requirements into framework concepts, solution variants and implementable specifications
  • Support departments and sales in the further development and process optimization of the CRM BSI
  • Leading and coordinating the departments involved in the implementation of IT systems or SaaS solutions

Your profile

  • Completed university degree (uni/FH) in the field of economics, marketing, data/business analytics, computer science or an equivalent education
  • At least 8-10 years of professional experience in digital sales as well as in project and change management in the banking environment
  • Experience in modeling, designing and optimizing sales and marketing processes across the entire end-to-end process chain in store and digital sales
  • Very good knowledge in the application of sales and marketing automation solutions, proven expertise in BSI is preferred, as well as experience with analytics and AI-supported use cases in sales and marketing
  • Very good analytical and conceptual skills
  • Strong communication and performance skills as well as a high level of resilience in a dynamic environment
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

Our offer

  • An exciting and responsible position in an innovative and future-oriented environment
  • The opportunity to actively shape the sales of the future and to directly influence the further development of our sales and marketing tools and processes
  • A highly qualified, motivated and professional team that supports you in your tasks
  • A modern workplace in the heart of Switzerland’s largest economic area
  • Comprehensive package, which in addition to the attractive basic salary and a variable remuneration offers many benefits and perks – from A such as retirement provision to Z such as ZVV bonus pass
