yellowshark® has been a reliable and sustainable partner in recruitment services and career consulting since 2009. Both the candidate and the client are always at the center of attention. This focus on companies and employees results in a dynamic service company that brings interesting candidates together with exciting companies. Our motivated team has in-depth knowledge of market requirements in the specialist areas of commercial administration, IT, technology and the construction industry. Thanks to innovative technologies, a solid network throughout Switzerland and its strategically important location in Zurich's economic center, yellowshark® operates dynamically, close to the market and sustainably.

To strengthen our Construction Industry specialist department, we are looking for a motivated and ambitious individual with a flair for sales, starting immediately.
