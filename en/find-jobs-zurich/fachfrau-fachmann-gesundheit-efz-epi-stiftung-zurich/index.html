The Multifaceted Day

  • Together with their colleagues, they accompany and care for adults with cognitive and psychological impairments as well as multiple disabilities with sometimes challenging behaviors and epilepsy in their everyday lives.
  • They perceive the wishes and resources of the residents and shape their daily and leisure structure together with them. They use their medical expertise specifically
  • You take responsibility for planning and implementing the agogic, nursing and medical care process
  • You are responsible for the smooth departmental organization and have an overview of administrative and domestic work
  • They support learning professionals care and health in developing their skills

Their diverse talents

  • You are a specialist in health EFZ and have experience working with people with disabilities or are interested in familiarizing yourself with this field of activity
  • They are motivated to engage with their counterpart
  • You have a solution-oriented work organization and a high level of personal responsibility
  • With their creative and transparent communicative way of shaping everyday life proactively
  • They are enthusiastic about creating together with the residents a beautiful home that meets their needs
  • Targeted further education on agogic and nursing topics and a very good internal education offer and promotion of external further education
  • Thematic support by internal experts
  • 5 weeks holiday, from the 50th. Age 6 weeks
  • Varied career perspectives and support in career planning by those responsible for vocational training
  • Own pension fund with an above-average employer share of 60%
  • Staff apartments, and perks in the EPI Park restaurant, pharmacy and half-tax or general subscription
