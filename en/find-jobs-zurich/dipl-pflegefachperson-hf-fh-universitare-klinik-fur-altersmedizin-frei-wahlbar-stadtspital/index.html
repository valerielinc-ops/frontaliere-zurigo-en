Full description

Strengthen our team at the Centre for Old Age Medicine in Zurich! For our Waid location, we are looking for a committed personality who enjoys acute medicine and working with older people and who wants to enrich our interdisciplinary team.

Tasks

  • With your commitment, you contribute to an activating, accompanying and therapeutically oriented care of our acute care patients.
  • Based on the care process, you ensured professional care.
  • In our interdisciplinary environment you act safely and take responsibility in the coordination and support of various concerns.
  • As a real team player, you accompany, promote and support our learners and students on their training path.

Profile

  • Completed training as a nurse HF/FH or an equivalent diploma recognised by the SRC
  • Interest and willingness to develop further in the field of geriatrics and acute medicine
  • Engaged and team-oriented personality with high social competence
  • Solution- and goal-oriented working method, combined with organizational skills and flexibility

We offer

We offer you more than just a job! Our city hospital world is characterized by an open dual culture, an uncomplicated, personal togetherness, respect and appreciation. As a team of over 120 professional groups, we work every day in very different ways for the well-being of our patients. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions and excellent social benefits from the city of Zurich.

About us

With over 34,000 inpatients and around 436,000 outpatients per year, the Stadtspital Zürich ranks among the top 10 Swiss hospitals. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,400 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
