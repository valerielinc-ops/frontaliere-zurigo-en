Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Interior Office for Statistics and Data Specialists/public statistics 80 - 100% In the Canton of Zurich, public statistics are organized decentrally. The Office for Statistics and Data promotes professional exchange and coordinates cooperation within the cantonal administration as well as with the Confederation and the municipalities. In addition, we support and advise other administrative units in the production, publication and mediation of statistical information as a basis for good decisions and to strengthen data competence. Design the public statistics in the Canton of Zurich with us October 2026 or by agreement we are looking for 80 – 100% of a specialist responsible / public statistics Your tasks

  • You shape the exchange with our various stakeholders and target groups and know their requirements and needs.
  • You will support and advise the specialists in the office and in the cantonal administration in ensuring that statistical information is comprehensibly prepared, communicated in a comprehensible manner and published in a coordinated manner.
  • You participate in the establishment of the Statistical Committee and take over its coordination (see RRB No. 331/2026 ).
  • You bear the substantive responsibility for the statistics portal « Zurich municipalities in figures ».
  • You work in projects to build new statistical products. Then this job is for you
  • You are service-oriented and enjoy working together and consulting.
  • You are an organizational talent, you work in a structured way and you have experience in managing committees and processes.
  • You have in-depth knowledge of public statistics, data management and interest in technical products such as dashboards.
  • You have a university degree and several years of relevant professional experience.
  • You have excellent knowledge of German in words and writing and proven experience in communication. We offer you
  • A versatile activity with many design possibilities and visible effect.
  • A meaningful work at the interface of society, administration and politics.
  • A central work location in the immediate vicinity of Zurich Central Station as well as the possibility to choose your work location based on activity with up to 50% home office.
  • Conditions of employment according to cantonal guidelines. We look forward to your online application including a letter of motivation.
