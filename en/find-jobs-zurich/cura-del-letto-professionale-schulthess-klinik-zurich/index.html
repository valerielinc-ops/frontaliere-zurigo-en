Full description

Tasks

  • Care and care of patients before and after orthopaedic surgery
  • Ensuring seamless and professional documentation
  • Open, respectful communication with patients, relatives and the treatment team
  • Active co-creation of a motivational team spirit
  • Cooperation in organisational and administrative tasks

Requirements

  • Recognised nursing diploma (HF/FH)
  • Team spirit, empathy, resilience and joy of responsibility
  • Professional experience in orthopaedics is beneficial
  • Good IT knowledge (MS Office, KIS)
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

We offer

  • Flexibility period in early, late and night service
  • Attractive social benefits and numerous benefits
  • Paid or compensable changing time (15 min./day / 6.5 Fridays/year)
  • 40 hour week / 12.5 additional scheduled Fridays per year
  • Night shift allowance CHF 10.-/h plus 20% time credit
  • Weekend, public holiday and late service allowance CHF 8.-/h
  • ZVV BonusPass / ZVV BonusPass Flex

The Schulthess Clinic in Zurich is one of the leading orthopaedic clinics in Europe with over 1,100 employees. Our central concern is to free people from their pain and restore their mobility. As a specialist clinic, we focus on surgical orthopedics, neurology, rheumatology and sports medicine. Schulthess Clinic is Swiss Olympic Medical Center.
