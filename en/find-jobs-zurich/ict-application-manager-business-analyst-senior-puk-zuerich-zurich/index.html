Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich treats and cares for mentally ill people of all ages. The more than 2,500 employees of the 55 different professions make their contribution to the holistic care of patients in interdisciplinary teams, from clarification and therapy to follow-up care. Our work is based on mutual trust and personal responsibility and together we promote an innovative corporate culture. As a university clinic, we are successfully involved in national and international research projects. 100% The information and communication technology sector ensures patient-oriented high-quality and economic ICT services for the entire PUK. The ICT Business Unit Service team is particularly responsible for the provision, maintenance and further development of the specialist applications and their integration. For our ICT department in Zurich Lenggstrasse we are looking for 1. September 2026 or by agreement one or more Their tasks

  • Ensuring the monitoring of the ongoing application operation incl. Incident corrections
  • Advising and supporting users in the areas of application and process handling
