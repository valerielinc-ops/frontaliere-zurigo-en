Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Center for Education in Health Care Vocational school teacher vocational basic education, (11-21 lessons per week) 40 - 80% The ZAG Center for Education in the Health Care System offers education and training at various educational levels (vocational basic education, higher vocational training and vocational training). With around 3,600 learners and students and 250 employees, it is one of the largest training centers in Switzerland for professions in the health care sector. At the beginning of the school year 2027/2028 (23 August 2027) we are looking for new colleagues for vocational teaching in basic vocational education. Their tasks
