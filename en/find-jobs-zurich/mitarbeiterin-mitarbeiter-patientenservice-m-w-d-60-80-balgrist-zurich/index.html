Full description

Balgrist University Hospital is one of the most renowned orthopaedic clinics in Switzerland with an affiliated centre for paraplegia and international recognition.

The patient service takes care of our patients safely and professionally.
For our motivated team search immediately or by agreement Tasks: • Recording of the wishes of patients and guests by electronic menu selection system • All tasks involved in preparation and follow-up for the service • Completion of customer-oriented beverage and meal service as well as laundry logistics in the patient rooms • Weekly ordering of food and non-food for the stations • Organization of rooming-in of guests and flower care • Daily contribution to the well-being of our patients Requirements • Some years of experience in the service sector, ideally in the hotel industry or in a hospital environment • Good PC user knowledge • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing (understanding of Swiss dialect), knowledge of foreign languages is desirable • Careful appearance and courteous and respectful treatment of patients • Teamwork, professional conversation skills We offer: • Opportunity to participate in an orthopaedic clinic with an excellent reputation • 4-day week (100%) • Work assignments without room hours • Collaboration with motivated and very committed employees • 50 % discount on the ZVV bonus pass or an annual contribution to a general subscription • Enjoy a healthy and balanced lunch at discounted prices at our company-owned staff restaurant • Gifted free day for birthday Further advantages are just one click away: Attractive offers for our employees
