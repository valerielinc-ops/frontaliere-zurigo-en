Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Rapperswil-Jona, a successful, innovative and locally oriented bank with headquarters in Rapperswil-Jona SG and offices in Rüti ZH, Hombrechtikon ZH and Egg ZH, we are looking for a committed, service-oriented and independent personality as a private client consultant and Stv for our modern advisory bank in Hombrechtikon ZH. Head of Office Hombrechtikon ZH (m/f/d).

What awaits you?

  • Development and management of the own retail portfolio
  • Development potential for deputising for the Head of the Hombrechtikon Office
  • Ensure competent advice on financing issues (housing) as well as standardised advice on investing and provisioning
  • Development of needs-based solutions taking into account the individual needs, wishes, goals and life situations of the customers as well as within the segment-specific product range
  • Expansion of the existing customer base through targeted acquisition of new customers
  • Cooperation in target group-specific market development measures such as contact campaigns, events and representative tasks

What do you bring?

  • Banking and/or financial education completed
  • Experience in the financial services sector in particular in mortgage consulting
  • Strong customer and sales-oriented thinking
  • Independent and goal-oriented working method as well as enjoying intensive customer contact
  • High level of self-organisation, structured working methods and strong organisational talent
  • Teamwork and flexibility
