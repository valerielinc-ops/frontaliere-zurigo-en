About us

The Fielmann Group is the world's third-largest company in the optical industry. The listed family-owned company based in Hamburg serves its customers with glasses, contact lenses and hearing systems through an omnichannel business model. More than 40 specialist stores are located in Switzerland, and with around 200 apprentices, we are one of the largest training providers in the industry.
