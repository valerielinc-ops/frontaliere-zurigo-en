Full description

You want to teach at a Quims school with strong learning relationships?

We are looking for a SHP ISR for primary school, about 25%, starting November 1, 2026.

Tasks

  • Integration of 2 children with a special school status in a 3rd grade
  • Case management and funding planning
  • Cooperation in the Class Team

Profile

  • They enjoy dealing with children and parents of different cultures.
  • As an open and resilient personality you appreciate the cooperation in the team.
  • They have a master's degree in the medical school and/or an EDK recognised teaching diploma for primary education.

We offer

  • A lively school and a school management awaits you, which will support you in your work together with the consulting team and the school island.
  • Good connection to the public transport.

About us

The Schwamendingen School District covers the entire urban district of 12 of the city of Zurich. Schwamendingen is a lively, multicultural residential district. By public transport we are easily accessible via Stettbach and Oerlikon station with various tram and bus lines.

The school Lynchswiesen is a quiMS school with voluntary full-day care. In the school club we offer the children with a course offer and homework hours a varied care offer. In 16 primary classes and 5 kindergartens about 400 children attend our school. The cooperation within the teaching team as well as the exchange beyond are important pillars of our school.
