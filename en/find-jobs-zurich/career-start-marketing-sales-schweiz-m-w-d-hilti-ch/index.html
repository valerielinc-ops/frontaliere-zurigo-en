Full description

The best start to your career at Hilti

You have successfully completed your master’s degree and do not just want to start a job, but lay the foundation for a long-term career? In the two-year Career Start program, which is carried out throughout Switzerland, you take responsibility for your own sales area at an early stage. You will get to know our business directly at Kund:innen and develop the skills for future leadership roles at Hilti. We are convinced that those who understand customers, markets and business models create the basis for sustainable success. That’s why you start your career where Hilti makes the biggest difference: in direct customer contact. You do not need any industry experience – much more important is the enthusiasm for the Hilti brand and your interest in the construction industry. In our onboarding you will be made fit for your career start.

Your profile

  • Successful Bachelor's or Master's degree (University, ETH/EPFL or University of Applied Sciences)
  • Practical professional experience through internships or work study activities, ideally complemented by challenging side projects or volunteer work
  • You are enthusiastic about direct sales and our business model and identify yourself with the fact that we act as an owner-managed company for the long term.
  • Flexibility in terms of location within Switzerland and willingness to move
  • Motivation to take on long-term responsibility and develop into a leadership role
  • Flowing knowledge of German and English, understanding of Swiss German and driving licence category B

  • We undertake to have all applications reviewed by one person. Even if no one is infallible, we stand by our person-centered approach to everything we do.

  • Applications that do not make it to the interview with a hiring manager do not receive personalized feedback.
  • You can expect to hear from us within 2–3 weeks (on average) regardless of the result. The entire recruitment process can take between 5 and 6 weeks.
  • We wish you every success in your application process. Tips for applying and interviewing can be found in our frequently asked career questions.

If technical problems occur during the application, please contact mycareer-techsupport@hilti.com.
