Full description

80% - 100% | IT / Business Engineering | Zurich | Experienced professionals Create a lean, robust and future-proof IT architecture in the context of the Nordstern Master Data Initiative of the Zürcher Kantonalbank. As a highly experienced and committed IT-lead architect, you will play a key role in this multi-year strategic project and assume overall responsibility for the system architecture and take over the professional leadership of the Solution architects. Your tasks

  • Develop a future-oriented architectural strategy for sales master data management and select and evaluate suitable technologies and solutions
  • Creating target architecture and architectural concepts, including integration and migration strategies taking into account quality, scalability, security and performance
  • Support strategic product decisions, including make-or-buy analyses, preparation and evaluation of tenders and selection of appropriate solutions
  • Working closely with other architects, disciplines and stakeholders to ensure a consistent architecture
  • Ensuring compliance with architectural standards, regulatory requirements and best practices

Your profile

  • Completed studies, ideally with a Master in Computer Science
  • Several years of experience as an IT architect in complex IT landscapes, preferably in the financial industry
  • Experience with modern architecture frameworks such as arc42, deep knowledge in domain-driven design (DDD) and agile environments (SAFe). Knowledge of cloud technologies, containerization and Kafka as well as experience with AI are beneficial
  • Experience in the selection and implementation of complex standard software as well as knowledge of customer lifecycle management and handling of customer data are advantageous
  • An enthusiastic and assertive personality who pursues strategic goals with conviction, remains steadfast in challenging situations, feels comfortable in a complex and very demanding environment and proactively drives solutions
  • Very good knowledge of German, good knowledge of English are advantageous

Our offer

  • The unique opportunity in the context of a highly complex modernization initiative to actively shape and implement the future of a central bank ability
  • Plenty of freedom and opportunities for participation, as well as a trusting and respectful cooperation
  • An employer who offers you opportunities to develop personally and professionally and supports you with many offers
  • Attractive additional services such as e.g. B. Low-cost contributions to public transport, special conditions on banking transactions, supplementary insurance of the health insurance fund and pension benefits well above the legal minimum
