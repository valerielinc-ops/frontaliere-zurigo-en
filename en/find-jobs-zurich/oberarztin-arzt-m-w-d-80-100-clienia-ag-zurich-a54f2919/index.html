Full description

To strengthen our team, we are looking for someone for the External Psychiatric Services Thurgau of Clienia Littenheid AG in Sirnach immediately or by appointment

Your tasks As part of your work, you will be involved in the management and training of our assistant doctors and psychologists, as well as in providing professional support to the entire team. On the other hand, you will also be responsible for carrying out assessments and treatments for adults with illnesses from the entire psychiatric diagnostic spectrum. They also have the opportunity to help shape group offerings and participate in the further development of our outpatient services. You will work in a networked manner in a multidisciplinary, dynamic and constantly evolving environment that maintains successful collaboration with the referring physicians and other involved departments.

Your profile You have a recognized specialist title in psychiatry and psychotherapy, have in-depth clinical experience and a good knowledge of the Swiss healthcare system. On the one hand, you value working independently and with personal responsibility, but on the other hand, you also value being an interdisciplinary and networked team player. You enjoy working in a patient- and value-oriented manner in a social psychiatric environment.

Our offer We offer you an interesting and varied job with the assumption of leadership responsibility. In our multidisciplinary team you can expect a collegial, value- and process-oriented working atmosphere in which you will be individually supported and encouraged through good coaching and excellent training opportunities. You work on weekdays in modern premises close to the train station and without any significant workload.

For further information please contact Dr. med. Jörg Bitter, Chief Physician, External Psychiatric Service Thurgau, 41 71 929 67 67, is available.

We look forward to receiving your application via the application tool.
