Full description

In the job placement, we offer the opportunity to complete a preliminary internship for the field of social work. We are looking for 1 March 2027 and 1. May 2027 for 8 to 12 months reinforcement.

Tasks

  • You support clients in the job search within the framework of the offer writing service.
  • After a thorough induction, you will lead your own cases in the application process.
  • You support the coaches and take on organizational and administrative tasks.
  • You participate in project groups, team meetings and case meetings.
  • You support the young people and young adults of the JOB PLUS offer in the search for apprentices, tests and in their personal development.
  • You regularly exchange ideas with other practitioners.

Profile

  • Educational degree at secondary level II (FMS, Matura, EFZ – ideally KV) or university degree and planned entry into working life.
  • Minimum age 24 years
  • Interest in work integration and pleasure in accompanying clients in the field of job search and developing application dossiers.
  • Willingness to actively participate and take responsibility.
  • Self-employment and safe appearance, high flexibility.

We offer

You can expect an exciting insight into the versatile professional field of work integration – with varied, challenging tasks and a lot of space to gain your own experience. You have the opportunity to get to know other exciting companies of work integration and get insights into different working worlds. You benefit from an experienced team, a strong network and the comprehensive know-how of a large organization.

About us

With around 2,400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a social Zurich. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion and contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.

The work integration division of the Social Institutions and Enterprises Service Department (SEB) comprises a range of work integration services for adults and young people. The aim of the programmes is the professional and social integration of the participants.
