Full description

Do you want to share your nursing knowledge instead of just applying it? As a specialist coach – we call our specialist experts internally, you accompany employees in the North region in their professional development, strengthen the quality of care and actively shape the outpatient care. You benefit from attractive working conditions: no shift work, no night work and no regular weekend assignments. Together with a dedicated team of specialist coaches, you support our employees in demanding care situations and bring your expertise where it makes a real difference.

(a)lle are addressed. Gender, origin, age? We are interested in your qualification and enthusiasm for work.

YOUR TASKS

  • Further development and implementation of guidelines in the field of memory care (dementia) and support.
  • Introduction and support of new employees with a focus on the role of case management.
  • Coaching, training and case discussions for the professional development of employees.
  • Consulting in complex customer situations as well as punctual assignment of assignments.
  • Promoting resource and goal-oriented care.
  • Close cooperation with employees, specialist positions, doctors and in the coach trio with team and HR coaches.
  • Active participation in the further development of care and organization.

You find us at least.

  • A key role with a direct impact on the quality of care and the further development of our organization.
  • No shift and night work and no regular weekend assignments.
  • Flexible working models with annual working hours and high personal responsibility.
  • An experienced coach trio and an appreciative cooperation at eye level.
  • Diverse training and further education opportunities for your professional and personal development.
  • With FreizeitPlus the possibility to buy 1 or 2 additional holiday weeks every year.
  • A learning and forward-looking organization that breaks new ground in outpatient care.
  • Our other benefits can be found here: https://jobs.spitex-zuerich.ch/benefits/

You bring with you

  • Enjoy the tasks described as well as the co-creation and further development of our team and our organization.
  • Bachelor of Science in Nursing or an equivalent academic qualification.
  • At least 2 years of professional experience in the ambulatory or inpatient sector.
  • The pleasure of coaching, accompanying and developing employees.
  • Strong skills to professionally empower and promote employees of all levels of competence.
  • Interest in business connections.
  • Openness to further develop your own role in the context of organizational development.
  • Coaching and/or leadership experience are beneficial.
