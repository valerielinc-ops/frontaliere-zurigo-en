Full description

Vocational trainer at the interdisciplinary private station 1 — Spital Zollikerberg, Zollikerberg (ZH).

Area: Nursing professions.

Welcome to your new place of work.

Ms. Melanie Bernsteiner, station manager, offers you an attractive job with a fee of 70-100% as an

Vocational trainer at the interdisciplinary private station 1

XX %

If you too want to make a difference in people’s lives, these are your tasks:

  • Professional support as well as targeted promotion and development of trainees, practice-oriented, concept-based and individually adapted to the learning process
  • Create targeted learning opportunities in day-to-day care and carry out practical learning days.
  • Together with the trainees, ensure competent and holistic care and care for our patients (mainly in early service)
  • Active participation in the station’s management team for the further development of training, including the introduction of educational topics
  • General further development of training together with the vocational training team and strengthening of the nursing team in accompanying the apprentices
  • Further development of one’s own professional pedagogical competence through professional exchange, networking and active participation in the network training

Become part of the Department of Emergency and Acute Medicine at the Zollikerberg Hospital. In the Clinic for Internal Medicine with continuing education status A, we look after more than 2,500 inpatients, more than 6,000 emergencies and more than 450 patients in the interdisciplinary intensive care unit every year. On our interdisciplinary stations, you can expect a versatile field of activity with a focus on palliative care, internal medicine and surgery. Our stations are guided according to the reference care system.

In our 360° tour, get a first impression of your new place of work.

To make you feel comfortable with us, many benefits are waiting for you. Just to name a few:

  • Flexibility in the organisation of working time: participation in service planning (in cooperation with the planning manager)
  • Focus on further education: We support you generously financially and time-wise in your further education – so that you are always up-to-date
  • Fit and active: Use our fitness room on the Zollikerberg site for free and stay healthy and motivated
  • With us there is an eco-bonus of up to CHF 650.– per year for environmentally conscious action
  • Own pension fund with super-compulsory participation of the employer
  • Exclusive discounts on telephony, Internet and TV at Salt and Sunrise

This convinces us

  • Completed training as FaGe EFZ or HF/FH care
  • Further training in the field of vocational training (SVEB) or willingness to complete it
  • Professional experience of at least one year, preferably in an acute hospital
  • Joy in accompanying, inspiring and specifically promoting learners in their professional development
  • Commitment, resilience and the willingness to continuously develop and actively shape change
  • Enjoying an appreciative interprofessional cooperation

About the Zollikerberg Hospital We stand for a comprehensive range of services and strong cooperation partners. With a public service mandate and 173 beds, the Zollikerberg Hospital contributes to the optimal medical care of the greater Zurich area. Every year, more than 11,000 inpatients and 75,000 outpatients in all insurance classes can feel comfortable and in good hands. Our broadly based primary care includes internal medicine, gynecology and obstetrics with neonatology, general surgery, an interdisciplinary emergency center and orthopedics with specialized spinal surgery.
