Full description

Create a better #TomorrowWithUs Want to help shape the future of the railway world together with us? At Siemens Mobility, we are the leading provider of mobility, constantly expanding our innovative portfolio, which includes rail vehicles, rail automation, electrification solutions, complete rail systems and accompanying services. With around 750 specialist employees in Switzerland and 39 600 employees worldwide, we shape mobility with passion and are one step ahead of our time. You are the driving force for quality and continuous improvement in all project phases within our area of project execution for Rail Infrastructure. At the same time, you appreciate the diversity of the project business and, if necessary, also support adjacent tasks in project coordination. With your hands-on mentality, you get involved where the project team needs support and continuously develop into new topics. Your new role

  • Preparation and further development of project-specific quality plans, definition of quality goals and monitoring of goal achievement based on relevant KPIs
  • Ensuring compliance with Siemens quality standards, industry regulations and contractual requirements throughout the project lifecycle
  • Accompaniment of milestone processes, moderation of quality gates as well as implementation of root cause analyses and implementation of corrective and preventive measures (CAPA)
  • Identification of quality-related risks and development of appropriate risk mitigation measures
  • Planning and implementation of project audits, assessments and reviews as well as preparation and communication of the results to project management, management and customers
  • Close cooperation with project management, engineering, operations and supplier quality teams to ensure the required level of quality
  • Assuming further project-related coordination and support tasks in the PMO, document management, scheduling or project management environment as well as actively participating in projects beyond the classic QMiP role
  • Higher education in engineering, quality management or technical vocational teaching combined with relevant training in quality
  • Sound experience in project planning, execution and/or project management is absolutely necessary
  • Knowledge of relevant standards (e.g. ISO 10005/10006) and QM methods
  • Very good organizational skills, self-employment, assertiveness and the ability to proactively drive improvement
  • Interest in overarching project management topics as well as the willingness to take on additional tasks in the project environment if necessary
  • openness to becoming familiar with new areas of expertise and methods
  • Experience in PMO, scheduling or document management tasks is an advantage, but not a requirement
  • Fluent knowledge of German and English, French of advantage You benefit from
  • an open and flexible working culture at one of the leading companies in the field of mobility in Switzerland
  • a 40-hour week on a 100% fee
  • at least 5 weeks of holidays
  • a workplace at the pulse of railway technology
  • Various employee benefits and training opportunities
