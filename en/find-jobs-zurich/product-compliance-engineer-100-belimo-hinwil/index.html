Full description

Introduction

Shape product compliance from requirements through approval and lifecycle. As a Product Compliance Engineer, you will independently lead compliance activities across assigned products and regulations, serving as the go-to expert for projects and critical issues. You’ll work across engineering teams and with external authorities to turn regulatory requirements into practical solutions.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead product compliance activities and independently manage approvals, audits, and inspections.
  • Ensure products, technical documentation, and processes comply with applicable regulations, directives, and standards.
  • Define, challenge, and approve regulatory requirements and product compliance information.
  • Serve as the subject-matter expert for compliance topics in development projects and customer-critical escalations.
  • Lead regional registrations and approvals, collaborating with authorities and notified bodies.
  • Monitor regulatory and standards developments and translate changes into actionable product and process requirements.
  • Coordinate cross-functional initiatives to embed regulatory requirements into product design, development, and lifecycle processes.
  • Contribute to relevant standardization committees where appropriate.

Required Expertise

  • Bachelor’s degree in electronics engineering, engineering, or another relevant technology-related field, or an equivalent higher technical qualification.
  • Several years of experience in compliance engineering or a comparable role, with demonstrated ability to handle complex compliance topics independently.
  • Deep knowledge of product regulations, directives, and technical standards, particularly EMC and electrical safety.
  • Ability to translate regulatory requirements into concrete technical and organizational actions.
  • Strong technical writing and documentation skills.
  • Very good English and strong communication skills for collaboration with technical teams and external stakeholders.
  • Reliable, self-organized, and proactive, with a strong willingness to continuously learn.
  • Advanced or further professional training is an advantage.

People at Belimo

Smart buildings start with dedicated people. We bring talented people together and foster an environment to succeed. At Belimo, respect, trust, and support across departments help ideas move quickly into real-world solutions, because we achieve more together than we ever could alone.

About Belimo

Belimo is the global market leader in the development, production, and sales of field devices for the energy-efficient control of heating, ventilation and air-conditioning systems. The focus of our core business is on damper actuators, control valves, sensors and meters. Founded in 1975, the company has been listed on the Swiss Exchange (SIX) since 1995.
