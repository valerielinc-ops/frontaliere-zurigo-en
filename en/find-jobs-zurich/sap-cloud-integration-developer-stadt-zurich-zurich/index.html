Full description

Connect SAP backend and cloud, develop smart integrations and ensure that systems work seamlessly together.

Tasks

  • You develop end-to-end integrations – from the ABAP backend to the iFlow on SAP BTP.
  • You develop interfaces and APIs in our SAP systems and make them available for integrations.
  • With SAP Cloud Integration, you ensure reliable data exchange between SAP, cloud and third-party systems.
  • You transfer existing PI/PO interfaces step by step into our modern integration landscape.
  • Together with the team, you bring new technologies, patterns and standards into practice.

Profile

  • You have experience in software development and interest in SAP, cloud and integration. ABAP knowledge is a plus – you learn the rest with us.
  • You enjoy development and when different systems end up speaking the same language.
  • APIs, REST, OData, events and messaging are not completely foreign to you.
  • Clean code, automated tests and git are part of modern software development for you.
  • You know that 200 OK is nice, but it doesn’t mean that everything is OK.

For example, you can buy up to 2 weeks of extra holidays and work part-time at all levels. Job sharing/splitting and unpaid holidays are also possible with us.
  • Sustainable and cheaper travel: With the mobility contribution of up to 600 francs a year, you travel cheaply and sustainably – whether you want to cycle through Zurich or explore Switzerland by train.
