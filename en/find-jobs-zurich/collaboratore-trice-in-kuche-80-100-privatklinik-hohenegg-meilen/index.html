Full description

Hohenegg Private Clinic is a specialist clinic with 95 beds for psychiatry, psychotherapy and psychosomatics. It is open and accessible to private, semi-private and general insured persons from Switzerland as well as self-payers. It is housed in a listed magnificent complex, in the middle of nature and high above Lake Zurich. In addition to inpatient services, we operate a mental health centre (ZPG) for outpatient and hospital-associated psychiatry at two locations. The sponsorship is a charitable foundation. With us, you can contribute yourself and your proven professional skills to a coherent, meaningful working environment. We are looking for well-qualified and socially competent employees who are passionate about their profession. We are looking for an active and motivated personality immediately or by agreement as Their tasks

  • Washing of kitchen utensils and dining room utensils
  • Equipment work
  • Helping to prepare meals
  • Food transport with small van
  • Help with the production of hot and cold kitchen
  • Responsibility of the cold and warm buffet on the station
  • Finishing and preparing dessert
  • Support for evening service
