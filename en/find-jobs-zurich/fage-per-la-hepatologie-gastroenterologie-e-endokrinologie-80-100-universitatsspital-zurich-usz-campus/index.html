Full description

We are a department with a focus on hepatology, gastroenterology and endocrinology. We care for patients with inflammatory bowel diseases, metabolic diseases and patients with liver cirrhosis, who are waiting for a transplant. The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and about 700 trainees are committed to the welfare of our patients:inside.

Tasks

  • Care and care of patients: women with hepatic, gastroenterological and endocrinological diseases in tandem and in close consultation with a qualified nursing specialist in early and late duty
  • Dealing with complex, fast-changing situations
  • Coordination of patient care in the multiprof-essional team
  • Continuous development of your knowledge and sharing of new insights in the team

Requirements

  • You are a qualified health professional or you will soon complete your training. You have had first experience in acute medicine
  • You are looking for medically complex challenges and appreciates the dynamics of fast changing situations
  • You are responsible and appreciate a working environment that is oriented towards clear guidelines
  • Close cooperation in the team, mutual willingness to help and the exchange of knowledge are a concern to you

We offer

  • A dynamic, exciting and demanding working environment in which you can continuously develop your expertise at a university level
  • Our team is not only helpful, but also full of marquis and mixed in colours. We attach great importance to our team spirit and support each other
  • A positive culture of cooperation with mutual support and knowledge transfer is central to us
  • We recognize and promote the potential of all employees and offer versatile development opportunities in different directions
  • In the planning of services, we take into account individual preferences and thus promote the compatibility of professional and private life
