Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility.

The Center for Social Psychiatry (ZSP) is embedded in the clinical care and university research structures of adult psychiatry and psychotherapy. The Supported Employment department of the Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich supports people with mental illnesses related to work and mental health. We coach them in an existing workplace, in the search for a new job or during integration measures. We work in a resource-oriented, interdisciplinary network, according to recognized methods and according to the current state of research.
