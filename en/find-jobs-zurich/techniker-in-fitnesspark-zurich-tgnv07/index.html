Full description

We offer our customers impeccable fitness and wellness facilities and ensure that the operational safety and availability of the building services, structural facilities, and water treatment systems for our Fitnessparks are implemented economically.

Tasks

  • Maintenance and repair of building services, structural facilities, and water treatment systems for the Fitnessparks Zurich Stockerhof, Puls 5, and Stadelhofen
  • Ensuring the operational readiness of the Fitnessparks
  • Maintenance and support of the entire fitness equipment park
  • Inventory management and material ordering
  • With your strong service orientation, you contribute to customer loyalty

Requirements

  • Min. 3 years of professional experience: in a comparable position
  • Completed vocational training (EFZ): in a technical field, preferably as an electrical fitter / installation electrician / electromechanic
  • High service orientation and good communication skills
  • Team-oriented thinking and acting, sense of responsibility, and independence
  • Flexibility regarding availability and workplace during the regular work week from Monday to Friday (evening & weekend assignments only in exceptional cases and on-call duty)
  • Holidays can be taken throughout the year outside of revision periods (July/August), in consultation with supervisors
  • German (very good knowledge)

What we offer

  • Central workplace: Our studios are located in well-connected central locations
  • Mobile subscription: National flat-rate subscription from CHF 10.– / month (Swisscom / digitec connect)
  • Migros Bank: Fee-free private and investment account with preferential interest rates
  • Employee offers: Various discounts in the areas of holidays and insurance (Hertz up to 10%, Generali 25%, Axa insurance up to 15%, etc.)
  • "Du" culture: We stand for respectful interaction on an equal footing
  • Working climate: Dynamic, motivated company
