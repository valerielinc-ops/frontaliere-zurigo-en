Location: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Composition by: 01. January 2027 or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 71418

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

You will accompany families with heart and expertise through one of the most beautiful experiences of their lives. Would you like to bring your passion for obstetrics to us?Then please contact us.

YOUR TASKS

  • Counselling and care of expectant mothers during inpatient stay in pregnancy and in postpartum
  • Holistic, independent accompaniment of births in cooperation with the attending physicians
  • Use of alternative medicine that rounds off maternity care adapted to the woman
  • Care of mother and child after birth in the birth hall, taking into account individual and family-oriented accompaniment
  • Identification and action in emergency situations according to the latest recommendations and guidelines in obstetrics and neonatology

Your profile

  • Completed training as a diplomat midwife
  • Professional experience in the birth room desirable
  • Good knowledge of German (verbal and written), knowledge of English (verbal)
  • High customer orientation and discretion is a matter of course for you
  • Interest in birth preparation and courses around obstetrics

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. You work with a modern medical clinic information system and can expect extensive training and development opportunities from us. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family responsibilities. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. Employees who have to move completely for their work receive four additional paid days off with a 100% workload. With a reduced workload, the days off reduce accordingly. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from equivalent financial compensation. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.
