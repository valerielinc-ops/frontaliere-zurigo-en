Full description

The Swiss family-owned company, founded in 1836, is now one of Europe’s most renowned confectioneries with its exquisite range. The products from Sprüngli stand for the finest quality, unique freshness and naturalness. The perfected creations delight customers from all over the world every day.

Service staff member (80-100%) Paradeplatz Zurich

80% - 100%, Zurich

Working at Sprüngli

In 2018, Confiserie Sprüngli was awarded 1st place for the best café in the nationwide Swiss ranking by the Choco Guide, based on the criteria of ambience, service, quality, selection, freshness and innovation. Would you also like to be among the best and make your valuable contribution every day? We pamper our guests at Paradeplatz in the traditional café-restaurant opened in 1859 on the first floor, in the modern café-bar on the ground floor, and in the Café & Lounge at Zurich Airport.

For our food service operations at Paradeplatz, we are looking for a service staff member to start immediately or by agreement.

What you will do with us

Do you love the vibrant atmosphere at Paradeplatz Zurich? Do you enjoy treating your guests to the finest products, enchanting them with your smile and impressing them with your well-groomed appearance? Are friendliness, efficiency and compliance with service standards a matter of course for you? Then it is time for us to get to know you.

Your responsibilities include

  • Serving and advising our national and international customers (including cash handling in some cases)
  • Preparation and service of our various coffee & chocolate specialties
  • Compliance with hygiene regulations as well as general tidying and cleaning work

What we are looking for

  • Completed training as a restaurant specialist EFZ
  • High level of service orientation and enjoyment of working in a lively establishment with an international clientele
  • Well-groomed appearance and good manners
  • A team-oriented, efficient and conscientious approach to work
  • You speak fluent German and English; additional foreign languages are an advantage
  • High flexibility with regard to working hours (shifts between 6.00 and 19.30 in a 7-day operation)

Here’s what you can look forward to

  • We offer you a varied role at a vibrant location in a well-maintained environment, an employee restaurant and shopping discounts.
