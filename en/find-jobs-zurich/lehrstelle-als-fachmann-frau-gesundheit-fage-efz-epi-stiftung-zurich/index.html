Full description

More than 200 adults with disabilities live in the EPI WohnWerk – and you can accompany them in their everyday life. Here no day is like the other: exciting, sometimes challenging, but always varied.

Tasks

  • You work permanently in a residential group and support the residents in everything they need – from personal care to free time.
  • You learn medical tasks: directing medications, controlling vital signs, administering tube food, taking blood.
  • You also get involved in organization & logistics – e.g. when ordering food or materials.
  • You plan and design leisure activities.
  • Your trainee accompanies you step by step.

Requirements

  • A degree Sek A, Sek B or 10. School year (at least 16 years old at start).
  • The pleasure of working with people with disabilities - you have sniffed the best.
  • Team spirit, responsibility, empathy and desire to work practically.
  • Openness to irregular working hours.

We offer

3-year training as a specialist / health specialist EFZ (possible with professional qualification). Learning workshops with other learners. Internships in various areas of the Epilepsy Foundation. 5 weeks of vacation. Discounts in the EPI Park Restaurant, the pharmacy and the public transport subscription.

Sometimes quite challenging, but never boring: At EPI WohnWerk, no day is like the other. Because the people we accompany each and everyone have their unique life story. Thanks to the cooperation in the interprofessional team, we are well positioned for our working day. Do you want to be part of this team?
