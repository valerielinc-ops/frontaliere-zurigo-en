The product management investment fund ensures an attractive and future-oriented product offer for our investors. For the maintenance and further development of our Swisscanto fund range, we are looking for you! A committed personality with curiosity, team spirit and a careful way of working. Look forward to exciting insights into different asset classes and fund types as well as cross-divisional collaboration. Become part of our team and actively shape the future of our fund offering!

Your tasks

  • Collaboration and support of the funds throughout the product life cycle
  • Creating performance, price, competitor and market analysis
  • Preparation of product data, graphics and reporting
  • Leading and active participation in projects on technically assigned topics
  • Close cooperation with various specialists (legal service, fund management, asset management, sales, custodian bank); Performing a control and coordination function
  • Coordinated communication on fund updates for distribution
  • University degree in Banking & Finance, Business Administration or VWL (university or college of applied sciences)
  • Initial experience in the investment business and / or product management of a financial service provider, preferably bank
  • Ideally, further education such as AZEK or CFA (or willingness to undertake a corresponding further education)
  • Strong conceptual and analytical skills
  • Experience with project work
  • Curious, team-oriented and down-to-earth personality with maker qualities and enjoyment of new topics
  • Willingness to participate in a wide range of topics – from conceptual issues to administrative tasks – and to continuously develop specialist and methodological knowledge
  • Strong communication skills
  • Versatile, interesting and demanding tasks in a dynamic business area
  • An agile and open environment in one of the most successful Swiss banks
  • An employer who takes care of her employees and invests in them
  • A destination team with a healthy mix of experienced specialists: younger talents in which you can learn and develop a lot
  • A centrally located workplace in the lively district 5 with very good transport connections and the possibility to organize your work flexibly
