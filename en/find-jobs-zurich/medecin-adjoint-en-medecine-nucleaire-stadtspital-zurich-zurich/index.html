Full description

The Institute of Radiology and Nuclear Medicine is organised across locations and operates nuclear medicine with training category A at the Triemli site, both at the Triemli site and at the Waid site. The institute offers two modern SPECT/CT cameras, a high-end PET/CT device of the latest generation, a thyroid camera, a DEXA measuring station, an ultrasound device, an ergometry room and a modern therapy station. All diagnostic as well as all common outpatient and stationary nuclear medicine therapy methods are offered. In addition, we operate a thyroid consultation session at both locations.

To complement our team, we are looking for a communicative, team-capable and service-oriented personality.

Tasks

  • In this versatile function, you support the diagnostic and therapeutic processes in the entire spectrum of nuclear medicine – from imaging to patient care at our therapy station.
  • You look after outpatient and inpatient patients together with the team and actively participate in tumor boards, reports and interdisciplinary case discussions.
  • You are in close, collegial exchange with other clinics, departments and the assigning medical profession.
  • With personal commitment and professional interest, you gain valuable clinical experience and develop continuously on your way to the title of specialist.
  • Depending on your interest and level of training, we also offer you insights into radiological modalities and are happy to support a scientific commitment.

Profile

  • Federal medical diploma or a foreign diploma in human medicine recognised by MEBEKO
  • Interest in specialist training in nuclear medicine
  • Initial clinical experience in radiology or nuclear medicine is beneficial but not a requirement
  • Team-oriented, communication-strong personality with high social competence and enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Good knowledge of German (ideally C1)

About us

With over 34,000 inpatients and around 436,000 outpatients per year, the Stadtspital Zürich ranks among the top 10 Swiss hospitals. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,400 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
