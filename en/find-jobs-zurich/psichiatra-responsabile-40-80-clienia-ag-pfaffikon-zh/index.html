Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With more than 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric offer of Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in ambulatory, day-clinical and inpatient settings. The Group employs around 1800 people and achieved a turnover of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For the Clienia Privatklinik Schlössli we are looking for an

Their tasks Psychiatric-superior medical care of the stations of acute and old-age psychiatry in representation as well as participation in the medical background service of the clinic and the possibility of cooperation in the offers of interventional psychiatry.

Your profile We expect from you several years of inpatient psychiatric experience, the specialist title or the specialist maturity for psychiatry and psychotherapy with advanced psychiatric and psychotherapeutic training as well as interest and pleasure in the best possible patient care.

Our offer We offer attractive working conditions at an innovative private clinic, which attaches great importance to the entire treatment chain (close cooperation with institutional outpatients and external bodies). 42-hour working week. Extensive internal training curriculum. Option for outpatient treatment of selected patients.

Further information can be provided by Dr. med. Franziskos Xepapadakos, Chief Physician and Stv. Medical Director, +41 44 929 83 19.

We look forward to your application via application tool.
