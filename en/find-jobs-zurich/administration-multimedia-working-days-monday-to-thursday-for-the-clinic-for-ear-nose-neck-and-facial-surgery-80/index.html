Full description

Zurich University Hospital is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.

Tasks

Their tasks are mainly administrative in nature

  • Editing the clinic’s internet and intranet site for ORL with internal CMS tools
  • Support of the administration management Education and events in the organization and implementation of lectures, clinical courses, training and congresses

Take care of about one day a week They deal with the following issues:

  • Design of print products according to CD specifications
  • Editing the texts
  • Data preparation, printing release and communication with the printing works

Requirements

  • Completed basic commercial training and practical experience in the design and release of print products
  • Alternatively: completed training Polygraf:in / Mediamatiker:in and the willingness to carry out administrative activities.
  • Very good InDesign, Photoshop and illustrator skills, respectively. Affinity Publisher, Affinity Photo and Affinity Designer
  • Very good MS Office knowledge (especially PowerPoint, Excel and Word)
  • Stylish-confident German and good English skills in word and writing
  • Resilient personality with a structured and precise working method and a very good comprehension
  • High willingness to provide services and customer orientation

We offer

We offer you a challenging and versatile task in a lively and exciting environment. A small, dedicated team is happy to meet you. The working days are Monday to Thursday on site at the USZ in the center of Zurich.
