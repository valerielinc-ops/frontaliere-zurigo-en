Tasks: At the interface between the alumni association OEC ALUMNI UZH, the faculty, the alumni-châtel and the umbrella organization UZH ALUMNI, you are responsible for the administrative supervision of the OEC ALUMNI UZH office, the event organization and the member communication. The task portfolio includes the following points:

  • Primary contact person for the board of directors, members and other stakeholders of the association (rotary function)
  • Administration of the membership, database management and invoicing (incl. Mahnwesen)
  • Support for the voluntary executive board (meeting and project (Danish))
  • Communication with members via website, newsletter and LinkedIn
  • Planning and implementation of Alumni-Events (incl. Support on site) in cooperation with the board of association and the faculty
  • Network maintenance, stakeholder management as well as planning and implementation of activities for the member acquisition
  • Strategic planning and further development in cooperation with the board of association and the faculty Requirements: In order to meet the diverse tasks and requirements, you bring a high level of service readiness, an open, contact-friendly personality and a reliable working method. You are a strong, proactive and resilient operator and do not lose track of the situation even in busy times. We require a training at tertiary level (HF, FH or university). Several years of professional experience in a similar function are of advantage. Besides the business-economic know-how, you also bring a flair for communication and marketing. In addition, you are trained in dealing with CRM and content management systems and ready for regular evening operations (about 12-15 times per year). We look forward to a responsible and flexible personality with team spirit. We offer: Our employees benefit from a wide range of attractive offers.
