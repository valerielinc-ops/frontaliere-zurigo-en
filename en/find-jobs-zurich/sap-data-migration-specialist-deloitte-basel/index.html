Role overview

Location: Basel, Zurich Work Pattern Percentage: 80-100% Help our clients successfully manage global challenges Our Deloitte SAP team can help transform an organization and take it to new levels of performance with a suite of solutions centred on the leading-edge SAP S/4 HANA platform.

We work with our clients to plan and implement a full suite of solutions in areas such as enterprise transformation, finance, and supply chain, incorporating advanced technology applications such as artificial intelligence, blockchain, and IoT.

The SAP team delivers business focussed transformation programmes supported by process design, people solutions and technology.

We partner with the most renowned technology providers globally and combine their tools with our proven expertise to offer the best solutions to our clients.

How you can make an impact

  • You will support exciting large-scale projects with a focus on S/4 HANA or SAP BTP in Switzerland and abroad
  • You will accompany clients from the conceptual design of initial ideas to successful implementation in operations.
  • You will be working on the assumption of sub-project manager tasks within the scope of new implementations
  • You will design and extend SAP standard software solutions
  • You will be working on the development of customer applications and the integration of various software solutions Who we are looking for
  • You can act as the Technical Lead Architect for end-to-end design, build and deploy of data migration project covering individual project implementations and template and rollout implementations
  • You drive design and development of full migration solution for all migration objects relevant for a comprehensive ERP implementation and can provide expertise and guidance for technical design of all data objects in SAP Advanced Data Migration by Syniti (SAP preferred data migration tool)
  • You are responsible for the technical coordination between onsite and offshore team and the quality review of implemented objects
  • You can design and implement Project Management Dashboards and Data Validation KPI reports in SAP Advanced Data Migration Your team Become part of a diverse, multinational and highly motivated team in our Zurich office.
