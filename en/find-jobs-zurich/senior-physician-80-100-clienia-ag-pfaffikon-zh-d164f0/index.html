Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With more than 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric offer of Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in ambulatory, day-clinical and inpatient settings. The Group employs around 1800 people and achieved a turnover of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For the Clienia Private Clinic Schlössli we are looking for 1. November 2026 or by agreement one or more

Their tasks Senior medical management and medical-technical responsibility as well as – in cooperation with the nursing department management – administrative management as well as joint further development of the specialised therapy offer for the acute care unit T2 with a focus on affective diseases.

Your profile We expect from you several years of inpatient psychiatric experience, the specialist title or the specialist maturity for psychiatry and psychotherapy, interest and pleasure in the best possible patient care and team-oriented leadership as well as ideally a proven clinical expertise in the field of acute psychiatry.

Our offer We offer attractive working conditions at an innovative private clinic, which attaches great importance to the entire treatment chain (close cooperation with institutional outpatients and external bodies). 42-hour working week. Extensive internal training curriculum. Option for outpatient treatment of selected patients.

Further information is available to you Dr. med. Franziskos Xepapadakos, MBA, Chief Physician and Stv. Medical Director, tel. +41 44 929 83 19.

We look forward to your application via application tool.
