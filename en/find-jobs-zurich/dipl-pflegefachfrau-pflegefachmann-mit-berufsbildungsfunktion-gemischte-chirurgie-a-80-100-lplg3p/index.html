Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Occupation by: Immediately or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 62785

You want to contribute your nursing expertise and at the same time accompany learners on their way? Then you are exactly right with us – in a modern environment with exciting surgical challenges.

YOUR SCOPE

  • Need- and customer-oriented care and care of patients according to the care process in cooperation with our partner doctors and an interprofessional team.

YOUR TASKS

  • Competent and individual care as well as care of our patients taking into account our care mission statement
  • Independent implementation of the diagnostic as well as therapeutic measures prescribed by the doctor in cooperation with our specialist health staff
  • Recognize and control emergency situations and initiate adequate measures until the arrival of the doctor
  • Assuming responsibility for the correct patient documentation as well as information sharing in the assigned task area

As part of your additional function as a vocational trainer, you can expect the following tasks:

  • You will accompany and support our RF student nursing and the learner health during their practical training – both technically and educationally.
  • You ensure that the educational assignment is professionally implemented in the department according to the training concept.

REQUIRED TRAINING

  • Completed training for/for dipl. Nursing professional or equivalent SRC recognition for foreign diplomas
  • Vocational training: Vocational trainers* in SVEB 1 or the willingness to complete them

Previous experience

  • At least two years of professional experience in acute care

OTHER SKILLS AND KNOWLEDGE

  • Great flexibility, resilience and high organisational talent
  • Good knowledge of German in word and writing (certificate B2)
  • Good IT user knowledge
  • High social skills and strong empathy
  • Teamwork and ability to guide employees

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. You work with a modern medical clinic information system and can expect extensive training and development opportunities from us. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family responsibilities. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. Employees who have to move completely for their work receive four additional paid days off with a 100% workload. With a reduced workload, the days off reduce accordingly. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from an equivalent financial compensation. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.
