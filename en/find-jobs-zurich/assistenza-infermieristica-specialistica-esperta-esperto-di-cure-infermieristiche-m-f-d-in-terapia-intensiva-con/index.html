As a specialist care provider at the University Hospital in Zurich, we are committed to showing commitment with our professional diversity in the top medical environment and always keeping up with the times. We do not achieve success alone, but together! Each one counts and together we bring ourselves to the highest level of care.

Want to be part of our team? With us you can make a difference!

You actively shape evidence-based nursing practice: You identify, evaluate and implement current scientific findings and empower nursing teams to consistently align their actions with evidence. You empower employees in their professional development: Through coaching, consulting and knowledge transfer, you strengthen the skills of nurses and promote a culture of continuous learning. You think visionarily and develop the care of tomorrow: You recognize future challenges at an early stage and initiate innovative concepts that combine nursing practice, research and teaching. You promote interprofessional cooperation on an equal footing: Together with clinical, scientific and academic partners, you develop sustainable solutions for high-quality patient care. You act flexibly in a dynamic university environment: you accompany change processes agile and solution-oriented and ensure that new

  • requirements and knowledge are effectively integrated into practice.

You have a postgraduate degree in intensive care, a completed master's degree in nursing sciences, or the willingness to complete it.
