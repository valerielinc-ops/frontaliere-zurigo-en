Full description

The nationally managed Communication & Public Affairs division conveys the added value that SRG provides for the people of Switzerland with its public service. It protects the reputation of SRG, is in dialogue with employees, the media, politics and the public and provides transparent information about the entire company.

This is your contribution.

  • They support SRG media management in the processing of media inquiries and in day-to-day business – either with a focus on SRF in Zurich or with a focus on corporate topics at SRG in Bern
  • They support the creation, coordination and publication of media releases and other means of communication
  • They create monitoring and prepare relevant findings for internal stakeholders
  • They manage communication platforms, newsletters and databases
  • You take on your own projects during the internship and participate independently in communication measures

They inspire us.

  • You are studying or have a university or university of applied sciences degree, preferably in communication, media or social sciences
  • Ideally, you have already gained initial experience in media work, communication or journalism
  • You have stylish knowledge of German. Knowledge of a second national language is an advantage
  • They work carefully, independently and structured, are flexible, reliable and communicative, think in a networked way and keep a cool head even in hectic situations.

So we inspire you.

  • You will gain insights into the work of the Corporate Newsroom and experience how topics, media work and communication are coordinated across language regions
  • You work on current topics and issues of a diverse media organization
  • You will work on interesting topics, diverse content and exciting challenges in a dynamic environment
  • You have creative freedom and self-responsibility and can thus make full use of your skills
  • You work in a multilingual and motivated team with experienced communication and media professionals

The internship is limited to 6 months, from January 2027 or by arrangement.

Please submit your application documents (motivation letter and CV) via the “Apply now” button. The application period ends on 9 November 2026.

Our promise

What does it take to reach the people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expertise, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can contribute and develop. For the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: Welcome to the world of SRG!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, promote creativity and offer a lot of design space for new and high-quality products.
  • Shaping digitalization: Quality journalism is also digital for us. That’s why we explore new possibilities and experiment with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-medial. We design on the pulse of time and on the nerve of the audience.
  • Being human: We meet each other collegially, work independently and shape our working atmosphere individually. Our success is based on the personal strengths as well as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programs. In doing so, we act respectfully and independently; in this way we promote opinion formation and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.
