  • As a polisher: you carry out maintenance or renewal work as well as superstructure and civil engineering work in the road area and manage them.
  • You ensure the technical management of workplaces and ensure the optimal handling on site; either for the entire workplace or limited to one track section (for large construction sites).
  • At several workplaces you lead the work and take over the specialist management of work managers and employees.
  • You provide support in work preparation and reporting.
  • As a security manager: you take security measures and check their compliance in accordance with the security arrangement.
  • You manage the security of the piket service and in case of special incidents you intervene on site.

You bring that with you.

  • For this role, you have a vocational apprenticeship in the construction industry (e.g. Verkehrswegbauer:in or Maurer:in) and experience in the area of roads.
  • You could complete a completed training as a polisher:in (POS) or as a polisher:in SBV and completed training as a foremen:in SBV (VOS).
  • As a leader, you distinguish yourself through your good communication and your teamwork ability.
  • You bring the willingness to irregular working hours and work outdoors.
  • You have the driving license Cat. B., advantageously valid driving license category E (trailer).
