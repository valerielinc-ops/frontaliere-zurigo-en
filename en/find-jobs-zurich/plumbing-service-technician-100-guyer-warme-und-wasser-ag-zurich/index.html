Full description

Do you know us? We are a Swiss company with competences in the fields of sanitary heating and refrigeration technology as well as their planning. With our location in Zurich and around 100 employees, we operate throughout the Canton of Zurich. We belong to Burkhalter Group AG, the umbrella organization of traditional companies in building technology and the building envelope in Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein. Are you ready for the next career step? We are looking for one:
