Full description

The Human Resources area, managed at the national level, is responsible for and manages the staff affairs of the SSR as a whole. Human Resources supports SSR collaborators in all personnel matters, offers a modern offer of initial and continuing training, and responds to the overall implementation of modern key processes.

Your Mission

  • Ensure the hierarchical and functional management of the Swiss French sales team (3 employees) dedicated to radio and TV sponsorship
  • Take responsibility for revenue and budget at regional and national level, in line with the objectives set in the field of radio and TV sponsorship
  • Develop and implement the sales and market development strategy, including the planning of trade measures and structured prospecting activities at regional and national levels
  • Develop revenue and maintain relationships with customers and agencies, ensure systematic acquisition of new customers and design customized offers to meet their needs
  • Work closely with internal interfaces and external partners
  • Establish sales forecasts, reporting, offers and presentations tailored to customers

Your profile

  • Strong business skills over the entire sales cycle (sals funnel), with an excellent understanding of customer needs and development opportunities
  • Several years of experience in a leading role in media sales
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills to facilitate customer interviews, negotiations, events and market actions
  • Very good organisational and coordination capacities, particularly in the planning and implementation of commercial actions, customer events and market communication
  • Professional network developed and excellent ability to build and maintain trust relationships
  • Good to very good knowledge of at least two national languages
  • English proficiency is an asset

What we offer you

  • A position in a company of high societal value committed to the audiovisual public service
  • A national working environment with the management of clients from French-speaking Switzerland for all SSR channels and antennas
  • A direct and significant contribution to public service financing
  • An activity in an interdisciplinary environment characterized by a strong spirit of innovation
  • Attractive working conditions, a modern management culture and an inspiring work environment
  • Possibility of teleworking

Our promise

Reaching the entire Swiss population with high quality formats requires expertise, enthusiasm and a stimulating and creative environment, in which everyone can invest and evolve. For the best to give the best of themselves, they need to have a source of inspiration: Welcome to the universe of SSR!

  • Create Content: Quality journalism is the heart of our activity and passion. We enjoy complete freedom of writing, encourage creativity and grant autonomy for innovation and quality.
  • Living digital: In our society, quality journalism can also take a digital form. This is why we are exploring new opportunities and experimenting with new formats and practices: digital, interactive and cross-media. Our content fits into the air of time and responds to public concerns.
  • Human being: We maintain informal relations, work independently and contribute to a good working atmosphere. Our success is based on the strengths of everyone and on the technical skills of all our employees.
  • Make sense: With our program, we reach all of Switzerland. We act independently and respectfully to encourage the formation of opinion and strengthen cultural diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.

Requirements

  • Solides compétences commerciales sur l'ensemble du cycle de vente (sales funnel), avec une excellente compréhension des besoins clients et des opportunités de développement

  • Plusieurs années d'expérience dans une fonction dirigeante au sein des ventes dans le secteur des médias

  • Excellentes aptitudes en communication et en présentation permettant de conduire avec aisance les entretiens clients, les négociations, les événements et les actions de marché

  • Très bonnes capacités d'organisation et de coordination, notamment dans la planification et la mise en œuvre d'actions commerciales, d'événements clients et de communication marché

  • Réseau professionnel développé et excellente aptitude à construire et entretenir des relations de confiance

  • Bonnes à très bonnes connaissances d'au moins deux langues nationales ; la maîtrise de l'anglais constitue un atout
