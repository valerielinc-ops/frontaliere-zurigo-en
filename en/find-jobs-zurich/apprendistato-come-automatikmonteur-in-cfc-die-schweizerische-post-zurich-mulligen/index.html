Full description

Are you a technical talent, skilled craftsman and enthusiastic about machines? Then an education in automation is just right for you! During the challenging three-year basic education, you will acquire the tools to successfully enter the professional world.

Your education

  • In your training, you will learn a lot about complex systems, electronics and automation.
  • You are the specialist for the maintenance and functionality of the sorting and conveying systems and help to ensure trouble-free operation. In addition, you carry out orders or simple projects independently or with support.
  • In the first year of apprenticeship, you will learn the basics in the creation, commissioning and maintenance of automated systems and discover further exciting topics at the partner company login Vocational Training in Zurich-Altstetten.
  • During the last two years of apprenticeship, you can practically apply your acquired knowledge in a sorting center.

You bring that with you

We are looking for young people with enthusiasm, joy and motivation who want to make a difference. You should fulfill the following points:

  • You have good knowledge of mathematics and physics and can think logically.
  • You have completed compulsory schooling.
  • Technical connections and electrical engineering interest you.
  • You enjoy working in a team, are reliable and responsible and have a technical and craftsmanship.
