Full description

In the Zurich notary offices, around 650 employees are committed to the concerns of customers and attach great importance to quality, discretion and customer orientation. All 44 notaries in the canton of Zurich are also land register and bankruptcy offices and belong to the Zurich administration of justice organization. By 01.12.2026 or by agreement a notarial secretary / notarial assistant / in 80 - 100% You work in a motivated and helpful team in a collegial working atmosphere. The preparation and review of contracts in the area of property law (such as purchase, easement and deposit contracts) is just as much a part of your daily work as the advice of customers and the independent certification of contracts. What you bring

  • Completed training businessman/woman EFZ Notariate Switzerland
  • Authorisation for the canton of Zurich
  • Experience in the processing of land register transactions
  • Precise, reliable, independent and forward working methods
  • Excellent knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Professional appearance and confidentiality
  • Service-oriented, friendly and courteous nature We offer you an exciting, versatile and varied activity in a collegial team of around 15 people. You can count on careful training and the support of our motivated team. Our employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines can also be seen. This position is also suitable for re-entrants. Internal further education also offers you exciting career opportunities. Further information about the respective office, the Zurich notarial system and our exciting tasks can be found on our website. If you have any questions, notary Daniel Felix is at your disposal at 044 859 25 52. Please send your complete application documents to daniel.felix@notariate-zh.ch . We look forward to your contact! notariate-zh.ch/waedenswil Apply now
