At our company, you can live your passion for financial planning every day. You contribute to helping our customers make better decisions for their retirement. We are looking for you: a personality who puts individual solutions for our customers at the center.

  • Responsibilities

  • Close collaboration with customer advisors
  • Professional support and guidance for customer advisors in the financial planning process
  • Conducting technical presentations at customer events
  • Active development of the financial planning business in the region
  • Requirements
  • Two to five years of experience in financial consulting/planning
  • Federal diploma in financial planning or equivalent qualification
  • Passion for financial planning and holistic consulting
  • Experience in customer consulting at a bank or financial services provider
  • Convincing presence and strong communication skills
  • Enjoy supporting, coaching, and developing customer advisors professionally
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and strong closing skills
  • Independent, structured, and solution-oriented working style
