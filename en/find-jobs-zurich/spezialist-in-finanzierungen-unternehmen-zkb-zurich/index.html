Full description

80% - 100% (job sharing possible) | Banking Operations | Zurich | Experienced professionals In the area of financing companies of the financing center you accompany our corporate customers in the implementation of their financing needs. In an open-minded and imaginative team of nine employees, you contribute to the efficient and high-quality processing of corporate financing. Together with around 30 colleagues in the field, you make a responsible contribution to professional financing solutions for the Zurich economy. Your tasks

  • Creation, processing and control of financing processes such as applications, contracts, final processing and other small contracts in the area of financing companies
  • Ensuring efficient and high-quality credit processing over the entire life cycle
  • Maintain close cooperation with internal interfaces such as risk management, legal, product management and other specialist departments
  • Support of projects and technical tasks in the field of corporate financing
  • Participate in the optimization of processes, systems and templates in financing
  • Contributing your expertise as a sparring partner: for colleagues in the area as well as for internal stakeholders

Your profile

  • Banking education or equivalent
  • Higher education in banking or finance or willingness to do so (e.g. professional card, high school, bachelor’s degree or equivalent)
  • Several years of experience in credit processing
  • Very good knowledge in the financing business with focus on real estate and corporate financing as well as in-depth know-how in adjacent specialist areas
  • You convince through a careful, structured working method and a high willingness to take responsibility
  • You score with team orientation and clear, address-oriented communication
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing, every other language is a plus

Our offer

  • Committed and collaborative team with a lot of experience that lives openness and cooperation
  • Exciting and diverse range of tasks with many network possibilities
  • We offer you space for your ideas and thus design the bank of tomorrow
  • Various opportunities to develop personally and professionally
  • Your career is important to us as an employer and therefore we support and accompany you on your way
  • Centrally located, easily accessible and modern workplace at Hardbrücke station with the possibility to do part of your work in the home office
