About us

The Dielsdorf Health Center is the geriatric competence center of the region as a special purpose association of the municipalities of the Dielsdorf district. With around 500 employees from 35 nations, we offer a comprehensive range of services ranging from advising older people and their relatives to general and specialized long-term care and care at home. For the strategic development of our health center, we are looking for an entrepreneurial, analytically strong and committed personality. In this versatile staff role, you work closely with the CEO and the management and actively shape the future of the GZD.

Tasks

  • They support the CEO and the management in the strategic development and positioning of the GZD and develop well-founded decision-making principles.
  • They coordinate the strategy process, accompany the short-, medium- and long-term business planning and regularly review the implementation of the strategic goals.
  • You will design and moderate strategy and development workshops.
  • They analyse the company key figures and produce meaningful reports and reporting for the management and the board of directors.
  • They observe market and industry trends in stationary and ambulatory long-term care and develop concepts and recommendations for action.
  • They are responsible for the further development of professional project management, lead strategic projects and accompany cross-divisional change processes.
  • They analyse the needs of important stakeholders and support the maintenance and further development of relationships, especially with our association communities.
  • They lead, promote and develop the employees under their control.

Profile

  • University degree in business administration, economics or a comparable discipline.
  • Several years of professional experience in corporate development, strategy work or a comparable function.
  • Further training in project management as well as several years of experience in the management and implementation of demanding projects, preferably in the health care or public law environment.
  • Strong strategic and entrepreneurial thinking, analytical skills and a good understanding of complex organizational and political contexts.
  • High social and communication skills, negotiation skills and the ability to attract different stakeholders for common goals.
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working methods as well as the ability to process complex issues in an understandable and addressable manner.
  • Secure appearance, initiative and joy in triggering changes and actively shaping the further development of our organization.

Offer

It offers you a versatile key position at the interface between corporate management and operational operations. We offer you flexible working time models, the opportunity for home office and support in your professional and personal further education. In addition, you benefit from at least five weeks of holidays, a discounted staff restaurant, free parking, attractive employee discounts and other benefits.
