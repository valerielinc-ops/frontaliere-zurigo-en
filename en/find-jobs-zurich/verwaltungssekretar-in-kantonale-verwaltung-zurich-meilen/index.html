Full description

The district court of Meilen decides first instance disputes in civil and criminal matters. More than 4000 procedures are carried out annually. In order to strengthen our commercial team, we are looking for an ADMINISTRATIVE SECRETAR/IN as of 1 January 2027 or by agreement (80-100%) Your responsibilities

  • Administrative supervision of civil and criminal proceedings.
  • Capture of new incoming procedures.
  • Keeping the schedule and summoning to court hearings.
  • copying and sending decisions.
  • Statistical execution and archiving of procedures.
  • Telephone contacts with courts, authorities, parties and lawyers.
  • General secretarial work and postal service. Your profile
  • They have completed a commercial education and have good computer skills.
  • They work independently, reliably, results-oriented and accurately.
  • They are characterized by a flexible working method, stylish German and a quick understanding.
  • Hectic situations do not bring you out of peace and do not let you lose track.
  • Ideally, you have already gained initial experience in a court or law firm. Our offer
  • Thorough familiarisation with the individual tasks. Interesting, varied and independent work in a collegial, well-established team.
  • Progressive social benefits and a wide range of training opportunities.
  • Place of work in miles with immediate proximity to the lake. For further information, the Chief Court Clerk MLaw E. Szczogiel (tel. 058 111 21 21) is at your disposal. We look forward to your online application.
