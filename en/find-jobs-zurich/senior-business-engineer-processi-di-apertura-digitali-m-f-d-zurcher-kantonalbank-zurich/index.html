  • Senior Business Engineer - Digital Opening Processes (m/f/d)

80% - 100% | IT / Business Engineering, Product and Channel Management | Zurich | Experienced Professionals

Apply online now

The Client Channels division designs the digital and physical channels of the Zürcher Kantonalbank and thus ensures an efficient and trusting customer experience. In this role, you take on a central role in the further development of our digital onboarding and product opening processes. Together with an interdisciplinary team of experts, you will develop sustainable solutions and drive forward the strategic conception and design of these processes. Thanks to your proven experience, you combine regulatory requirements and ease of use in the best possible way to create sustainable and trustworthy customer experiences.

Your tasks

  • Strategic conception of digital onboarding and product opening processes with focus on user-friendliness, efficiency and sustainability
  • Analyze existing processes, identify potential for improvement and develop clear, future-oriented process images
  • Translating process vision into clear target images and structured artifacts (e.g. E2E process documentation, BPMN diagrams) as a basis for later feature development and implementation
  • Collaborating with UX experts, developing convincing storylines and participating in user tests
  • Proactive coordination and collaboration with internal and external stakeholders, such as product management, sales, IT, legal & compliance
  • Ensuring compliance with legal, regulatory and safety-relevant requirements in process design
  • University degree (Uni/FH) in business informatics, computer science, business administration or comparable discipline
  • At least 6 years of professional experience in the field of digital channels or digital banking, a significant part of which focuses on digital client lifecycle and opening processes
  • Several years of experience as a business analyst: in/engineer in a comparable environment as well as the ability to translate strategic goals into concrete measures and innovative solutions are mandatory
  • Sound technical understanding of modern IT architectures as well as the ability to communicate with architects and developers at eye level and to actively shape the interaction of central components
  • Good understanding of user-centred design
  • Strong ability to work effectively with different stakeholders (e.g. product management, sales, IT, legal & compliance) and communicate complex issues in an understandable way
  • Open-minded, empathetic personality with team player qualities, high independence, strong self-drive and a solution-oriented working method
  • Strong communication skills in the German language
  • Open culture of cooperation in which mutual support and exchange are a matter of course
  • We support you in reconciling work and private life - whether through part-time, hybrid work, unpaid leave or flexible working hours
  • Your career is important to us as an employer and therefore we support and accompany you on your way and support you with many offers
  • Our modern office in the vibrant Kreis 5 in Zurich offers you the ideal environment to work creatively and productively
  • We offer you a fair and transparent compensation based on clearly defined pay bands
  • Comprehensive package, which in addition to the attractive basic salary and a variable remuneration offers many benefits and perks – from A such as retirement provision to Z such as ZVV bonus pass
