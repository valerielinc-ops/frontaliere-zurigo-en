Full description

You support our team in Mittelbünden in the construction, operation and maintenance of medium, low and high voltage systems and ensure that our network remains safe and stable with fast, reliable deployments.

Tasks

  • You are active in the construction, operation and maintenance of grid systems in the medium, low and high voltage grids as well as on overhead lines.
  • The management network is maintained continuously by you and your teammates.
  • Interruption on the net? You and your teammates will fix it immediately.
  • You're taking the pike-tick service for granted.
  • Your time from your place of residence to your place of work is 60 minutes maximum.

Profile

  • You have a completed vocational apprenticeship as a network electricianin EFZ or electricianin EFZ.
  • Ideally, you're already bringing your first experience in network construction.
  • You work independently, carefully and accurately and understand yourself as a reliable team of player personality. You are also robust, free of vertigo and physically resilient.
  • You have the category B driver's card.
  • You can speak German both in oral and in writing.

We offer

  • More energy for your life: mobile work, flexible work models and part-time opportunities enable you to actively shape your working time and divide it into the way it suits your life best. For example, you can shop for up to 2 weeks extra holidays and work part-time at all levels. Job I/O/-spli-t and unpaid holiday are also possible with us.
  • Sustainable and discounted travel: With the mobility contribution of up to 600 francs per year, you travel cheaply and sustainably – whether you want to cycle through Zurich by bike or explore Switzerland by train.
  • An investment in your future: We not only value paying fair and transparent wages, but also ensure that you have more of your wages. We take over 60% of the savings contributions and offer an over-compulsory interest on the capital saved.
  • You also profit annually from additional Fringes of $150 and attractive mobile phone subscriptions.
  • Development and further education: With our talent management, we specifically promote your development – through a wide range of internal and external training opportunities as well as exciting development opportunities.
  • Meaningful work: Sustainability and sun-sustainable work are not just words: Join us in shaping Zurich and the future. Let energy be released for a good morning together with us

ewz has been on the road for over 130 years, dynamic and visionary every day. Whether it's sustainable energy production, smart energy solutions or innovative networking technology, develop with us today what is important tomorrow.
