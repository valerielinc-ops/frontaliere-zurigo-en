Purpose of the Job

主要任务与职责:

  • 负责高速永磁电机和潜水异步电机的本地化开发与优化，包括结构强度设计、参数计算与性能分析。
  • 参与本地化电机相关技术方案的指定与评审，确保设计方案的可行性和先进性
  • 负责电机样机的试制、测试及问题分析，提出改进措施并优化产品性能
  • 协助解决电机生产过程中出现的技术问题，提供技术支持和改进指导
  • 跟踪行业技术发展趋势，参与新技术、新工艺的研究与应用
  • 编写相关技术文档，包括设计说明书、测试报告及专利申请

Main tasks and responsibilities

  • Responsible for the localization development and optimization of high-speed permanent magnet motors and submersible asynchronous motors, including structural strength design, parameter calculation, and performance analysis.
  • Participate in the specification and review of technical solutions related to localized motors, ensuring the feasibility and advancement of design plans.
  • Responsible for prototyping, testing, and problem analysis of motors, proposing improvements, and optimizing product performance.
  • Assist in resolving technical issues during motor production, providing technical support and guidance for improvement.
  • Track industry technology development trends and participate in the research and application of new technologies and processes.
  • Prepare related technical documents, including design specifications, test reports, and patent applications.
