Full description

This workplace includes a canteen and the 24/7-«Nellis»-concept. The meeting place in Winterthur creates an inviting gathering place for trainees and employees. From Monday to Friday, our team delights an average of 200 guests here with a varied offering – from daily changing menus through sandwiches and snacks to internal catering events. Are you our new team member who will support us as an office employee with immediate effect or by arrangement? You work in an approximately 50% position from Monday to Friday from 11:30 to 15:30. During school holidays, there are no shifts at the operation; temporary assignments at other operations are possible if needed. Your task menu:

  • Washing dishes, cutlery and all kitchen utensils
  • Operating the dishwasher
  • Ensuring cleanliness and order in the office area
  • Waste disposal and sorting
  • Maintenance and care of the machines in the dishwashing area
  • Carrying out various cleaning tasks in the kitchen as well as throughout the entire operation
  • Implementation of the ZFV quality and service standards
  • Compliance with and implementation of the hygiene and occupational safety guidelines
  • Assistance with serving food during the lunch service Your recipe for success:
  • Initial experience in gastronomy / the hotel industry
  • Experience in a dishwashing area
  • Good spoken German skills
  • Ability to work in a team
  • Willing to learn and resilient
  • Hygiene-conscious
  • Structured and independent What we offer you: We create teams that support each other and in which you are encouraged. We work together and focus on everything that feels good. Among other things, we place great emphasis on salary transparency and equal pay. For this position, at a 100% workload, depending on your experience and qualifications, we offer an annual salary of around CHF 52'000 (incl. 13. Monthly salary). If you would like to share your passion for gastronomy with us, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
