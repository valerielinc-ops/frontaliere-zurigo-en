as of 1.1.2027

Advances in radio-oncology result from the close link between clinical excellence and research. The Clinic for Radio-Oncology at USZ aims to continuously develop and improve treatment options for patients. With a strong commitment to clinical trials as well as to physical-technical and radiobiological research, we make an important contribution to the future of cancer treatment and create optimal conditions for innovative therapeutic concepts.

Your main tasks

  • Support of the Department of Clinical Studies in a wide range of administrative and organisational tasks
  • Collaboration in the collection, processing and quality assurance of data in clinical research projects
  • Maintenance, evaluation and clear preparation of Excel databases as well as descriptive statistical analyses

Your profile

  • Students: human medicine with an interest in scientific work and clinical research
  • Strong interest in organizational processes and working with figures and data
  • Good knowledge of Excel as well as interest in statistical questions and data analysis
  • High accuracy, responsibility and reliability

What you can look forward to

We offer you an interesting activity with the opportunity to take on exciting challenges. With your commitment, you contribute to the sustainable success of clinical research in radio-oncology.
