Business Engineer Digital Teaching Operations & Workflow Automation (m/f/d)

80%-100%, Zurich, temporary

At ETH Zurich, more than 20,000 students study at Bachelor's and Master's level or are enrolled as listeners. The teaching operation behind it is handled over a broad application landscape. The Digital Teaching Operations team of the Academic Services department is responsible for the support, maintenance, conception, configuration and further development of the digital teaching business landscape.

For the strategic project "Digital Campus", we are looking for a committed and solution-oriented personality as a business engineer in temporary employment until the project ends in September 2029. In this role, you actively shape the digital transformation of teaching operations and make a decisive contribution to the successful introduction of new processes, systems and working methods.

Project background

In the "Digital Campus" project, the existing teaching business landscape is fundamentally renewed. Numerous historically grown applications and processes are replaced by an integrated, sustainable solution landscape.

Successful implementation requires not only technological changes, but also the continuous development of processes, roles and forms of collaboration. As a business engineer, you will form the bridge between departments, IT and project organization and ensure that professional requirements are effectively converted into functioning solutions.

Job description

  • Analysis, design and optimization of business processes along the entire solution lifecycle
  • Collection, structuring and prioritization of professional requirements in close cooperation with departments, external software provider and internal IT
  • Development of viable and sustainable solution concepts at the interface between business and technology
  • Participation in the introduction, further development and continuous improvement of the digital campus solution landscape
  • Design, implement and further develop workflow automation as well as support for the introduction of AI-based working methods and tools
  • Definition of professional quality and acceptance criteria and ensuring the professional quality of solutions
  • Planning, implementation and support of testing, professional acceptance, releases and quality assurance
  • Ensuring sustainable documentation and establishing and maintaining process, product and project knowledge
  • Shared responsibility for the operation, further development and support of existing applications
  • Close cooperation with specialist areas, project management, developers: inside and other stakeholders for the successful implementation of change projects

Profile

  • Completed university degree in business informatics, computer science or a comparable field of study
  • 3-5 years of experience as a business engineer, business analyst:in, requirements engineer or in a comparable role
  • Experience in digitalization, transformation or IT projects
  • Practical experience with workflow automation, ideally based on N8N, and interest in AI-enabled tools and ways of working
  • Good knowledge of SQL and data analytics
  • Strong analytical skills and a structured and solution-oriented way of working
  • Ability to prepare complex technical and technical contexts appropriate to the addressee
  • High quality awareness as well as experience in testing, professional acceptance or quality assurance
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills as well as enjoy working with different stakeholder groups
  • Independent, responsible and proactive personality with a high level of initiative
  • Safe handling of Jira, Confluence and Microsoft 365
  • Enthusiasm for actively shaping digital transformation and developing sustainable solutions at the interface of business and IT
  • Flowing German oral and quirky
