From visit to visit: customers, relatives and me. Compatibility of family, hobby and profession: a lot is possible at our base in Gossau! We are a colorful, motivated team with a supportive leadership culture. Re-entrants are also welcome! Nursing specialist HF in late service for the Zentrum Gossau ZH You are right with us if you enjoy it ...

  • travel independently and at the same time be part of a strong treatment team.
  • To provide reliable support to customers as a caregiver or in case management – even in demanding situations.
  • to exchange views with external specialists and partner organisations such as family doctors, palliative care teams, nutritional consultations, hospitals and homes.
  • to work solution-oriented and based on the maintenance process.
  • to record the care documentation electronically directly with the customers.
  • Interprofessional cooperation with different professional groups.
  • actively participate in case discussions and reflect on joint work.
  • to use digital communication possibilities for an uncomplicated exchange in a team.
  • to assume various roles in everyday work – for example as a reference care person, day-to-day manager, role model or competent Spitex specialist.
  • to be a reliable contact person for our customers and their relatives.
  • to work in a structured manner according to the operational plan, to set priorities and to assume responsibility.
  • develop professionally and personally, share knowledge and support colleagues.
  • Be mindful of your own resources and regularly reflect on your personal boundaries.
  • be mobile with your own vehicle or a business vehicle.

So that you can focus on your customers, we offer you ...

  • modern technical equipment such as smartphone and tablet.
  • an electronic maintenance documentation with Perigon, which you can use directly on site.
  • Beekeeper as an internal platform for a fast and uncomplicated exchange.
  • regular opportunities for professional and personal exchange – for example in case meetings, team meetings and at team events.
  • an open error and feedback culture in which challenges are discussed together and solutions are developed.
  • a living learning culture with targeted knowledge exchange and diverse development opportunities.
  • a wide range of services with which we can comprehensively accompany customers and relatives.
  • a wide range of possibilities to actively shape Spitex Bachtel – for example in specialist groups, the personnel committee, networking meetings or projects.
  • professional administrative areas that effectively relieve our employees and treatment teams in everyday life.

What you bring

  • completed training as a qualified nurse HF/FH or equivalent training recognised in Switzerland
  • ideally work experience in outpatient care – Spitex experience is beneficial but not a requirement
  • Enjoy independent and responsible work
  • a high level of technical and social competence as well as an appreciative approach to customers inside, relatives and teammate inside
  • Good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Safety in dealing with digital work equipment or the willingness to familiarize yourself with it
  • a category B driving licence and willingness to travel with your own vehicle or business vehicle
  • Flexibility for assignments as part of our service planning
