Full description

Doing the right thing. In Switzerland and in around 20 countries on four continents. Engage with us for the vision of a solidarity society, as Caritas Switzerland has been running asylum centres for families, individuals and unaccompanied minors (MNA) at five locations in the Canton of Zurich since March 2024 on behalf of the Cantonal Social Office (KSA). The central administration is responsible for the collection of client master data and dossier mutations. In addition, all client-related invoices are checked, internal cost account languages are obtained, entered and forwarded to the center management for payment release. Furthermore, the central administration ensures the insurance obligation of all clients in the Zurich mandates. For this cross-location activity, we are looking for reinforcements in the mandate office at Bleicherweg. Their tasks

  • Collection of master data and transfer of dossiers to centres for processing
  • Processing of status changes and changes in household size
  • Examination, processing and accounting of client-related invoices and credits in tutorials
  • Ensuring compliance and further development of the requirements of Caritas Switzerland in the Department of Administration and Finance
  • Cooperation in the development of the organisation manual, work instructions and checklists
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation (health, social work and case management)
  • Ensuring the insurance obligation of all clients in the Zurich mandates
  • Processing of all health-related invoices incl. Premium and benefit statements and recovery from the health insurance fund
  • Assumption of responsibility (e.g. dental services) including creation/revision of process flows, maintenance of knowledge management and training of the team in the area of responsibility, contact person for internal and external positions in the area of responsibility

Our requirements

  • Completed training in the commercial sector (minor e-profile)
  • At least three years of professional experience in the administrative sector, preferably in public welfare and/or public administration in the Canton of Zurich
  • Knowledge and experience in asylum matters
  • At least one year of accounting experience, especially invoice processing incl. Control and accounting
  • Very good computer user knowledge (especially MS-Office incl.) Teams, SharePoint and Internet ideally with case guidance software Tutoris
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German
  • Strong field guidance and communication skills
  • Ability and resilience to work in a dynamic environment
  • Rapid comprehension, networked thinking and action

Place of work: City of Zurich, directly at Enge Station Start of work: as of 01.01.2027 or by agreement Caritas offers attractive employment conditions and an open working environment at the pulse of social issues. For the transformation towards role-based work, we count on employees with a digital and agile mindset who like to participate in change processes and take responsibility.
