Original description

Location: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Composition by: 01. December 2026 or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 72994

Are you ready for the next career step in a highly specialized field?

Then become part of our dedicated team at Klinik Hirslanden Zurich!

In the exciting discipline of electrophysiology, you work closely with experienced specialists and take on a central role in the care of our patients – before, during and after the interventions.

If you are a qualified nurse with experience and passion for complex medical procedures, we look forward to meeting you!

YOUR SCOPE

  • Safe and high-quality treatment in cooperation with a specialist in Rhythmology / Electrophysiology

YOUR TASKS

  • Care of the patient before, during and after the intervention
  • Sterile and technical assistance in interventions and implantations in the field
  • Professional and appropriate provision of consumables and equipment
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with patients, doctors and employees

TRAINING REQUIRED

  • Training as a qualified nurse HF/FH
  • Further development in the field of intensive care or anesthesia care is advantageous

Previous experience

  • At least two years of professional experience in internal medicine, intensive care unit, recovery room or anesthesia
  • Professional experience in a similar capacity

OTHER SKILLS AND KNOWLEDGE

  • Great flexibility, resilience and high organizational talent
  • Good knowledge of German in word and writing (certificate B2)
  • Good IT user knowledge
  • High social skills and strong empathy
  • Teamwork and ability to guide employees

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. You work with a modern medical clinic information system and can expect extensive training and development opportunities from us. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family responsibilities. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. Employees who have to move completely for their work receive four additional paid days off with a 100% workload. With a reduced workload, the days off reduce accordingly. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from an equivalent financial compensation. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.
