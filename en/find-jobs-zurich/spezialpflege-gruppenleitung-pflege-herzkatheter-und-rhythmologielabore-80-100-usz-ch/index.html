Full description

As a specialist care provider at the University Hospital in Zurich, we are committed to showing commitment with our professional diversity in the top medical environment and always keeping up with the times. We do not achieve success alone, but together! Each individual counts and together we bring ourselves to the highest level of professional care.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • Together with your team, you provide professional, safe and patient-oriented care in our highly specialized heart catheter and rhythmological laboratory environment. • You assume the professional and organizational responsibility for your unit and ensure an efficient process and optimal use of human resources. • You are also a competent contact person for complex interventional procedures and strengthen the professional development of your team in cardiology and rhythmology. • You promote your employees individually, recognize their strengths and development opportunities and create space for personal and professional growth. • Together with your team and our interprofessional partners, you will continue to develop existing processes and actively shape the future orientation of cardiac catheterology and rhythmology laboratories.
