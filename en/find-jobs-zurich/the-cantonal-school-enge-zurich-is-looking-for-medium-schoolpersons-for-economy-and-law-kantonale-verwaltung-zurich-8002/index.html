Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School Enge The cantonal school Enge Zurich is looking for: MEDIUM SCHOOL PERSONS FOR ECONOMY AND LAW 50 - 100% The Cantonal School Enge Zurich is a short-term high school with the maturity profiles "Economy and Law", "New Languages" and "Philosophy, Pedagogy, Psychology" as well as a commercial school (HMS). In the profile Economy and Law, we also offer a bilingual degree course in German/English. We also lead accent classes in the field of sustainability and cooperation. With 170 teachers, 30 employees in administration and operations and over 1,100 pupils, the Enge Cantonal School is one of the largest secondary schools in the canton.

  • Their tasks At the beginning of the school year 2027/2028 we are looking for committed secondary school teachers for economics and law (180-200%). The employment takes place in accordance with the secondary and vocational school teacher ordinance with special
  • Tasks (mbA) and thus requires a commitment to the school beyond teaching.
  • Prerequisite for employment is a university degree at master level as well as the teaching diploma for mature schools for business and law. In addition, you have an additional vocational education qualification. In addition to several years of teaching experience in the HMS (FRW, WR, PWR and education professional practice), at the KV and/or in the secondary school (business and law), you also have at least six months of professional experience outside of the teaching activity (full-time equivalent). Our offer
  • We offer you an interesting, diverse and challenging job at a school with a comprehensive range of secondary schools and a contemporary remuneration according to the specifications of the Canton of Zurich. More information about our school can be found at www.ken.ch. If you have any questions, please contact the Secretariat of the Enge Cantonal School (telephone 044 286 76 11); E-mail sekretariat@ken.ch. Please submit your application with all documents from maturity or comparable degree by 30 October 2026 via the online tool. We look forward to your application!

Pensum: 50-100 %
