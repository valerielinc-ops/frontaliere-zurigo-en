Full description

Would you like to support the medical monitoring station at the Triemli site? As of now, we are looking for a team-oriented personality, temporarily until the end of January 2027 with an option of extension.

Tasks

  • You monitor and look after cardiological patients on the monitor.
  • You act competently and safely in emergency and resuscitation situations.
  • You take over the professional operation of the available equipment and materials.
  • You promote a cooperative, constructive, interdisciplinary cooperation.

Profile

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nursing professional / Dipl. Nursing professional with NDK (formerly IMC)
  • Experience in surveillance care / IMC
  • High level of service and service competence as well as high quality awareness
  • Resilience and flexibility, organisational skills and independent, responsible working methods

We offer

We offer you more than just a job! Our city hospital world is characterized by an open du-culture, an uncomplicated, personal togetherness and the necessary touch of humor in everyday life. As a team of over 120 professional groups, we work every day in very different ways for the well-being of our patients. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions and excellent social benefits from the city of Zurich.

About us

With over 36'000 inpatients and around 455,000 outpatients per year, the Stadtspital Zürich ranks among the top 10 Swiss hospitals. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,500 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
