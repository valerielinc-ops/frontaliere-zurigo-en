Full description

We are looking for a chauffeur/all-rounder that gets our stages, trucks and machines rolling – while being just as flexible as our missions. The Emil Egger Group is the leading provider of heavy goods logistics in Switzerland. In the areas of crane work, special transports, industrial removals and rental lifts, more than 230 employees are employed. With the comprehensive special know-how, individual and innovative customer solutions are developed every day.

Tasks

For us, everything revolves around work platforms, trucks and construction machines – and we need you for that! As a chauffeur/all-rounder, you ensure that our machines reach their destination safely and reliably and that our customers are served at all times.

  • Delivery and pick-up service of our work platforms and trucks with the semi-tractor (Volvo FH 540 with 3-axis semi-trailer)
  • Transport of construction machinery, ballast, crane parts and other heavy goods of all kinds
  • Provision of work platforms and forklifts for transport
  • Performing sporadic minor maintenance work
  • Assignments as jumpers on various vehicles

We expect

  • In-depth knowledge in the field of lifting platforms and construction machinery (including operation for loading and unloading)
  • Ideally, IPAF training (Operator and Introductory Lift Course)
  • Resilient, flexible and a real team player
  • Customer-oriented with friendly appearance
  • Readiness for occasional nights

We offer

  • Interesting, future-oriented activities in a dynamic company
  • Varied tasks in an exciting environment
  • Progressive employment conditions and attractive social benefits
  • Modern, well-equipped fleet
  • Free parking directly on site
  • Family corporate culture with lived du-culture
  • Entry by arrangement
