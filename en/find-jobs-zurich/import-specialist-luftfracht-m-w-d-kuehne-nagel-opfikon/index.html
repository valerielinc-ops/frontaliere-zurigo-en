Full description

More than a job

As part of our operational teams, you play a key role in ensuring that everything arrives at the right place at the right time and that our customers feel well looked after. Through your commitment, you keep supply chains running and find the right solution for every challenge. Whether medicines, toys or important machine parts – thanks to you, products reach people all over the world. In doing so, you not only get goods to their destination, but also make the small and big moments of life possible. Your contribution at Kühne+Nagel makes more of a difference than you can imagine.

How you contribute

  • End-to-end customer support throughout the entire air freight import chain
  • Management and coordination of import shipments while taking service, quality and deadline requirements into account
  • Planning and scheduling of transports and ensuring smooth operations
  • Proactive shipment monitoring and transparent communication with customers and partners
  • Preparation and review of relevant transport and customs documents.
  • Close collaboration with airlines, transport companies and internal stakeholders
  • Continuous monitoring of KPIs and implementation of optimization measures

What sets you apart

  • Completed training in freight forwarding and logistics or a comparable qualification
  • Professional experience in air freight
  • Proficiency in MS Office
  • Strong customer and service orientation as well as excellent communication skills
  • Organizational skills, team spirit and flexibility
  • Fluent German and good written and spoken English

What we offer

  • Performance-based compensation package including a 13th month's salary
  • Extensive training and development opportunities
  • Exclusive discounts at numerous partner companies
  • Employee referral program with an attractive bonus
  • Regular team and company events
  • Modern office premises with ideal public transport connections

Who we are

Logistics touches every aspect of our lives – from the goods we consume every day to the healthcare we rely on. With us, your work goes beyond logistics: It makes the small and big moments of life possible for people around the world.

As a globally leading logistics company with a vision of advancing our world, we offer you a safe and stable environment. Here, your work makes a real difference. Whether delivering food, developing sustainable transport solutions or engaging with society – your work makes more of a difference than you can imagine.
