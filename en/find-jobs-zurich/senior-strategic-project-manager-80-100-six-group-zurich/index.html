Senior Strategic Project Manager (80 -100%)

Zurich | up to 40% home office possible | Reference 8201

Do you want to actively shape strategic projects in payment transactions? Are you excited about complex issues at the interface of banking, technology, regulation and market needs? Then become part of our strategy and future business team at SIX Banking Services. We develop and manage initiatives that further develop Swiss payments and support banks in the implementation of new requirements and business opportunities.

These are your tasks

  • Lead strategic initiatives in the payment and banking environment, from problem structuring to analysis and implementation.
  • develop target images, business cases, value propositions, operating models and roadmaps for new or further developed services.
  • analyse market, customer, technology and regulatory developments and derive concrete options for action for SIX Banking Services.
  • lead cross-divisional projects with internal departments, banks, technology partners and external service providers and create alignment across different interests.
  • Prepared content for senior management, steering committees and other stakeholders is concise, analytically strong and decision-oriented.

You bring that with you

  • at least 6 years of experience in management consulting, corporate strategy or strategic transformation, ideally in the financial services or payments environment.
  • demonstrable experience in the independent management of complex strategic projects with multiple stakeholder groups and management exposure.
  • Strong skills in analysis, structuring and synthesis as well as experience with executive recommendations, business cases and management-ready presentations.
  • Strong communication and moderation ability to lead workshops, prepare decisions and mobilize stakeholders without formal line responsibility.
  • very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • Knowledge of payments, digital banking, banking operations, platform models or regulatory topics is an advantage.
