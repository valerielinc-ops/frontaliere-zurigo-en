Location: Zurich Work Pattern Percentage: 80% - 100% Help our clients to be compliant and on top of their tax needs As a Manager in our growing corporate tax team, you will be providing Swiss and international tax advisory services to our clients. You will be working on (global) engagements and together with your team provide international organisations with leading services in tax advisory encompassing the full bandwith of expertise in Swiss tax, international tax (such as key US tax principles, EU directives, etc) as well as Pillar 2. – ultimately enabling our clients to successfully navigate the ever changing Swiss and international tax landscape. Further, this role may provide for the opportunity to benefit from a secondment to Deloitte’s Tax Desk Program in New York City. How you can make an impact

  • Take responsibility and coordinate local as well as international tax advisory projects and act as an advisor for our international clients from various sectors on complex tax issues
  • Supervise Swiss tax compliance services to large multinational enterprises and assist them administering their global tax compliance
  • Work in a cross-divisional as well as international team and collaborate with other teams around the world
  • Be responsible for your own career and explore our exciting career opportunities as well as a broad internal management training program
  • Lead and inspire juniors and help them grow within their career Who We Are Looking For
  • You hold a Bachelor or Master in relevant field and have completed the Swiss tax expert diploma (Steuerexperte) or similar education (e.g. LLM Swiss and International Taxation Kalaidos FH)
  • Minimum 4 years of experience in providing tax advisory services to international clients
  • You are keen to learn and develop yourself and a team of junior staff, like to challenge the status quo, are interested in the broader context of what you do and work independently, efficiently and solution oriented
  • You have a good network in the Swiss market and are able to build customer relationships
  • You communicate fluently in German and English. Any further languages would be an advantage Your Team Become part of a fun, highly motivated team of 30+ people at our Zurich office.
