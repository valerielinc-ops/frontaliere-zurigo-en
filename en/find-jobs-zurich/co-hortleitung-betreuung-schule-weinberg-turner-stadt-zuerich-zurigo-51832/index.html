Full description

We are looking for an experienced and open-minded reinforcement as of 1 January 2027 or by agreement.

Tasks

She is interested in working with and living with children. The further development of our day school and the support team will give you pleasure. You are happy to be part of a team and lead it pedagogically, organizationally and deputy for the management of support in a cooperative cooperation. You are in close contact with your own team and management support. Interdisciplinary working and exchange groups see you as an enrichment and regularly participate in them and contribute with your socio-pedagogical expertise in a targeted and proactive manner and find pragmatic, pedagogically sustainable solutions within the scope of possible. You share our basic attitudes within the framework of the “New Authority” and actively carry your presence into everyday life with the team and the children.

Profile

You are a committed, established specialist and have a completed study of social work, a comparable educational study or a teaching diploma. Working in a team and leading a team cooperatively and at eye level gives you pleasure. Collaboration with different professional groups in the school team will enrich you. Experience or interest in the BBV function would also be welcome. They are basically used to multi-layered

  • Managing tasks, being authentic and communicative and are open to new things. They find challenges exciting, recognize the need and actively use the room for manoeuvre with a clear, appropriate and inclusive approach. If you feel addressed, you are in the right place with us!

For applications with a foreign diploma, a Swiss diploma recognition must be attached.

We offer

A varied, versatile, independent and responsible work in an open, motivated and helpful team, which supports our school motto "zämä läbe - zämä lehre" and a supportive management who look forward to working together. An attractive working environment, with clear structures and room for manoeuvre and a salary according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich, 4-6 weeks of holidays depending on age and 6 days of compensation. Part of the extra time can be compensated during school holidays (= additional weeks off work). Working hours = four working days: Mo, Di, Do, Fr

About us

Our school is a lively learning and living space. The day school Weinberg-Turner is located in Zurich Unterstrass and comprises 12 classes, 4 kindergartens and 4 support locations from August 2025. The approximately 370 children entrusted to us are the focus, we are guided by their competences. Treating each other with respect and appreciation is one of our strengths. From a good school atmosphere, we can promote the children according to their abilities.
