Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Occupation by: immediately or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 65711

Create the radiooncology of tomorrow. We combine sustainable innovation, medical quality and access to cutting-edge technology. As one of the first clinics in the world, we work with the new Accela linear accelerator from Varian – and offer you the opportunity to be part of this technological development.

Are flexibility and a quick apprehension among your strengths? Do you want to be a member of a strong and successful team? Then you fit us perfectly. As Klinik Hirslanden, we are only so successful because around 2,000 employees, 510 partner and employed doctors, 130 students and 70 trainees work together hand in hand – and can rely on each other at any time. At the heart of high-quality medicine is always a strong team. To expand our radiotherapy team, we are looking for you as active support.

YOUR TASKS

  • Care of radio-oncological patients
  • Coordination and implementation of percutaneous radiation therapy on the linear accelerator (Varian TrueBeam)
  • Implementation of planning CT and production of individual storage aids
  • Daily quality controls on the linear accelerator (compliance with radiation protection)
  • Possibility to take on the role of vocational trainer if interested

Your profile

  • Completed training for/for dipl. Radiology specialist (MTRA) and professional experience in the field of radiotherapy
  • Experience in dosimetry and irradiation planning is advantageous
  • Very good knowledge of German (other national languages and English are a plus)
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation with patients, employees and the medical profession

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. You work with a modern medical clinic information system and can expect extensive training and development opportunities from us. We offer you a modern, pleasant working atmosphere as well as a thorough introduction to the world of radiotherapy. Thanks to the working time model «Leisure Plus» you have the opportunity to enjoy 7 instead of 5 weeks of holidays with a percentage wage reduction. As a radiology specialist you wear a uniform workwear with us. Your changing time will be compensated with a 100% fee in the form of four additional paid days off. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. If you travel by public transport, we will contribute to your travel costs. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.
