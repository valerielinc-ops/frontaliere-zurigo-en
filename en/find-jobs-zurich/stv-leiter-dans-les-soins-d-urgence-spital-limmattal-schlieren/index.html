365 days a year, 24 hours a day, the emergency center is responsible for the medical emergency care of more than 35,000 emergency patients every year. We are proud of our efficient, effective and coordinated, comprehensive and comprehensive care for our patients. We look forward to seeing you as a new member of our team.

Our emergency department comprises 14 monitoring stations and a shock room with 2 stations as well as an integrated emergency practice with 4 treatment rooms. Direct applications are preferred, external recruitment offices are not treated as a priority.

  • Active participation in care in all 3 shifts
