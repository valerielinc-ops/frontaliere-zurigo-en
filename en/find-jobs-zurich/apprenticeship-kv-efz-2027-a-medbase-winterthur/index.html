Welcome to the Medbase Zentrale Winterthur

Our modern office building is located just three minutes from Winterthur Train Station, in an urban setting that offers ideal conditions for focused work and creative collaboration. At our headquarters, around 150 specialists from the fields of IT, HR, marketing and finance work hand in hand to jointly develop innovative solutions and achieve our corporate goals. With flexible workplaces and modern meeting pods, we promote an open and efficient working culture. At Medbase, we attach great importance to teamwork and the exchange between departments in order to be successful together. Here, colleagues from our sister companies such as Zur Rose, WePractice or the dental center meet, which further enriches the interdisciplinary exchange.

Do you want to complete a versatile and exciting education? At Medbase, you can expect a varied apprenticeship period in which you get to know different departments and actively participate in everyday business. In a support team, you will learn step by step the diverse tasks of a merchant EFZ and lay the foundation for your professional future.

The Medbase Group is one of the leading companies in ambulatory healthcare. This means services ranging from health promotion to medication and acute treatment to rehabilitation. Health professionals from different disciplines work hand in hand to provide people across Switzerland with integrated and world-class medical care. With logistics services and digital solutions, we support doctors so that they too can fully concentrate on patients.

The Medbase Group includes the brands Medbase Pharmacies, Zur Rose, zahnarztzentrum.ch, WePractice and Bluecare.
