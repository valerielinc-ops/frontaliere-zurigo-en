Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School High promenade ICT specialist / ICT specialist EFZ 60% The cantonal school Hohe Promenade is a long-school school with an old- and modern-language profile, an immersion course German / English and a strong STEM offer. Around 800 students learn at our school, teach and accompany 150 teachers and employees in administration and operations. Committed students, the open-minded college and the latest infrastructure ensure a stimulating environment. More information about our school in the heart of Zurich can be found at www.kshp.ch. We are looking for an employee for our ICT team immediately or by agreement. For good accessibility, we want to have a presence on at least four days per week, distributed in a reduced way (e.g. two whole and two half days), the exact distribution we set flexibly together. Their tasks

  • 1st-level support for the school’s own computers (Mac), iPads, printers, beamers and visualizers as well as for the BYOD devices (Mac/iOS and Windows) of students, teachers and employees
  • Support and management of the school’s own iPads and Macs via our MDM (Jamf), including enrollment, app distribution and security measures (e.g. LostMode)
  • Supporting teachers with the technology in the schoolrooms (beamers, visualizers, presentation devices) and ensuring smooth teaching operations
  • Processing of incoming ICT concerns of all school members via telephone, ticket system or at the counter
  • Participation in ICT projects in the context of digital transformation
  • Maintaining the internal IT infrastructure (clients, peripherals) and helping to ensure IT security Your profile
  • Broad expertise in Apple hardware and software as well as Microsoft Windows
  • Office 365
  • Familiarity with Jira and Confluence as well as affinity to use AI tools specifically for problem analysis and solution
  • Completed training as a specialist EFZ, a computer science degree or comparable experience
  • Good general technical knowledge, independent, reliable and responsible working methods
  • Strong service and service orientation as well as enjoyment of direct contact with students and teachers
  • Open, team capable and committed personality Our offer
  • Working in an experienced computer team
  • Living environment in a medium-sized high school
  • Workplace in a centrally located and modernly equipped school
  • Conditions of employment according to cantonal requirements Application Have we aroused your interest? Come and talk to us. We look forward to your application. Please submit your documents exclusively via the online tool. For information please contact Cédric Decoster, Head of ICT, Tel. 044 224 64 88, e-mail cedric.decoster@kshp.ch . Applications from recruitment offices shall not be considered.

Pensum: 60%
