Full description

  • The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich treats and cares for mentally ill people of all ages. The more than 2,500 employees of the 55 different professions make their contribution to the holistic care of patients in interdisciplinary teams, from clarification and therapy to follow-up care. Our work is based on mutual trust and personal responsibility and together we promote an innovative corporate culture. As a university clinic, we are successfully involved in national and international research projects. Forensic Psychiatry and Psychotherapy (Director: Prof. Elmar Habermeyer) is a treatment and assessment centre with university connection. In the area of crime prevention, the specialist centres "Do not become a culprit / Pedosexuality prevention centre" and the "Consultation for mental and sexual health" offer low-threshold and free consultations and treatments for different target groups. While the “Don’t Become a Perpetrator” office is aimed at people with a paedophile inclination, the “Consultation for Mental and Sexual Health” is open to employees of the Catholic Church who seek support or advice in dealing with psychological stresses or sexual issues.
