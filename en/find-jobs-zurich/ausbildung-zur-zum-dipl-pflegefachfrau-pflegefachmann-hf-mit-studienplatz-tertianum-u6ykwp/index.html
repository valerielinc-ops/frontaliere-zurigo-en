Full description

Your heart beats for older people? Is the nursing profession your calling? Do you see opportunities to contribute your skills and experience to us in a meaningful way in order to have a positive impact on the lives of our customers? Then you fit perfectly with us!

For the house Rosenau in Bazenheid we are looking for you in 2027 for the

Training for/for the Dipl. Nursing specialist / nursing specialist HF with study place

You will learn this with us

  • Care, care and accompaniment of guests in everyday and demanding situations
  • You will gain competencies in the main and work processes (maintenance process, communication process, knowledge management, organizational process) according to the framework curriculum Dipl. Nurse HF / Nurse HF
  • Professional and managerial responsibility in cooperation with the various professional groups
  • Independent implementation of the nursing process
  • Electronic maintenance documentation

You bring that with you

  • Completed three-year vocational apprenticeship with a federal certificate of ability, a DMS/FMS degree or a high school diploma. (For foreign completed vocational apprenticeship, appropriate recognition by the SBFI or the SRC).
  • Great willingness to learn as well as willingness to reflect and sense of responsibility
  • Willingness to help, sociability and team orientation
  • Willingness to work irregularly
  • A heartfelt attitude
  • Enjoying versatile challenges and practical work
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
