Full description

80% - 100% | IT / Business Engineering | Zurich | Experienced professionals with personnel responsibility You will lead and transform our organizational unit IT Operations Management (ITOM). Together with your team, you will be responsible for Major Incident Management, 7x24h Operational Monitoring and Control of the Bank’s business-critical processing networks. In a complex enterprise IT landscape in the highly regulated banking environment, you will develop the team with technical foresight and pragmatism from a traditional process organization to a modern, engineering-oriented IT operations hub. You drive automation and observability, establish modern post-mortem and learning cultures and make operational operations fit for the future. Your tasks

  • Operational overall responsibility & stability: Ensuring 7x24h operation (OCC), major incident management, error-free execution of mission-critical job networks and supporting hypercare phases.
  • Team leadership & responsibility: You take the existing team of around 15 employees on the journey – you promote targeted technical understanding, strengthen personal responsibility and gradually transform manual working methods into automated, data-supported processes.
  • Engineering Mindset & Toil Reduction: Identify recurring manual activities and work with your team to advance standardization and runbook automation to noticeably minimize operational friction losses.
  • Blameless Post-Mortems & Resilience: Establishing a modern culture of error and learning in problem management. Causes are not only documented, but also systemically understood and sustainably solved in cooperation with the engineering teams.
  • Collaboration at eye level: As the most important operational partner and “power user”, you formulate clear requirements for software development as well as for platform, cloud and observability teams and speak their technical language.
  • Process evolution: Continuous development and optimization of ITSM and operational processes to increase stability, efficiency and service quality.

Your profile

  • In-depth technical background: training or study in computer science/business informatics (or comparable practice) with a solid understanding of modern IT architectures, platforms, software development and automation principles. We expect proven experience in the operation of complex enterprise IT landscapes (mission-critical systems, high availability, ITSM processes).
  • Empathic Leadership & Change Competence: Experience in leading and, above all, developing IT operations teams in the order of >10 employees. You enjoy introducing employees with different experience backgrounds to new methods and technologies.
  • Crisis Resistance & Communication Strength: Confident appearance at major incidents with addressable communication – from engineers to top management.
  • Modern Operations & SRE pragmatism: Practical proven knowledge in incident and problem management, combined with the understanding of SRE principles (toil reduction, further development towards observability, Blameless post-mortems).
  • Industry understanding: Experience in the regulated environment (preferably financial services) and understanding of business-critical core banking processes.
  • Language skills: Very good knowledge of German in words and writing, English skills are an advantage.

Our offer

  • Culture of continuous development – technical, professional and personal
  • Exciting and diverse range of tasks with many network possibilities
  • Your career is important to us as an employer and therefore we support and accompany you on your way
  • You can flexibly organize your work in consultation with your team (e.g. mobile work, flexible working hours, etc.)
  • Centrally located, modern and attractive workplace in the Zurich economic area
  • Comprehensive package with attractive basic salary, variable compensation and versatile benefits (e.g. sports activities, leisure activities, childcare, participation in health insurance premiums)
