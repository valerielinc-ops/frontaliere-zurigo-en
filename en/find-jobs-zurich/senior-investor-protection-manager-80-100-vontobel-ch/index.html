Senior Investor Protection Manager (80-100%) — Vontobel

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

We are looking for an analytically strong, entrepreneurial and design-oriented personality who transforms regulatory requirements into sustainable solutions and actively drives change. The focus is on investor protection. At the same time, the role offers the opportunity to shape further strategic and regulatory topics within the department and to achieve sustainable impact in an international environment.

In this role, you will shape the further development of the investor protection management of an international investment company and play a key role in regulatory initiatives, digital transformation and cooperation between business, front and control functions.

Your tasks

  • Responsibility for the further development of the Investor Protection Framework as well as the design and implementation of regulatory initiatives. This includes in particular methods, processes, governance structures, reporting and operating models with targeted use of modern analysis, automation and AI solutions.
  • Leadership and cooperation in cross-divisional projects for the further development of regulatory, governance-related and strategic topics within the department
  • Central contact and competent sparring partner for investor protection issues towards front units, relationship managers, senior management, compliance and IT
  • Advising and supporting front units and relationship managers on investor protection issues as well as the conception and implementation of target group-oriented training and awareness measures
  • Active participation in the digital transformation and continuous improvement of processes, controls and reporting
  • Content support and support in internal audits and regulatory audits

Your profile

  • Completed university or technical college studies in economics, law, finance or a comparable field
  • Several years of professional experience in the field of investor protection in the financial sector, consulting and front-line experience of advantage
  • Very good knowledge of the front-to-end core processes of a financial institution as well as relevant national and international regulatory requirements (e.g. MiFID II, PRIIPs, FinSA/FIDLEG)
  • Broad interest in regulatory, governance-related and strategic issues as well as the willingness to participate flexibly in different topics
  • Sound knowledge of project and stakeholder management combined with excellent communication skills in German and English
  • Good knowledge of risk & control frameworks as well as experience with control tests, monitoring and governance processes are advantageous
  • Strong analytical and conceptual skills as well as the ability to prepare complex issues appropriately and solution-oriented
  • Proactive, team-oriented and collaborative personality with pragmatic working methods, high learning agility and affinity for digital and AI-supported working methods

What we offer

  • A technically challenging and future-oriented working environment with complex issues, modern technologies and a strong focus on innovation
  • A dynamic, international environment with a great deal of room for manoeuvre and direct cooperation with diverse stakeholders
  • The opportunity to actively shape investor protection and other regulatory and strategic issues
  • High personal responsibility, visibility towards senior management as well as diverse attractive development and career opportunities
  • An enterprise culture that promotes collaboration, innovation and continuous learning

We are looking for talented people and not just a CV that meets all the criteria. Even if you don’t bring everything we ask of you, you should apply.
