Full description

Are you a critical thinker who thrives on unraveling complex technology risks? Do you get excited by the idea of working with a diverse, forward-thinking team that uses cutting-edge tools and innovative techniques? If so, we want to hear from you! Join Group Internal Audit at Swiss Re and play a pivotal role in advancing organizational resilience in an ever-evolving risk landscape.

About the Role As a Principle Senior IT Auditor within Group Internal Audit (GIA), you'll bring your expertise in Information Technology and Cyber Security to life across a wide range of impactful audit activities. This is your chance to explore Swiss Re's diverse IT domains, deepen your understanding of processes and controls, and engage with topics that shape the organization's risk culture. Whether you're scoping an audit, uncovering insights, or presenting findings to senior stakeholders - your work will genuinely matter.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead and participate in a variety of audits end-to-end - from scoping, planning and developing the testing plan, to executing the testing plan and documenting the results, to presenting the findings to the relevant stakeholders and finally writing and issuing the audit report.
  • Collaboration in the audit team - plan and deliver your assigned task and work in close collaboration with the audit team, challenge and coach the audit team to excel by acting as a role model, positively influence the overall audit outcome.
  • Drive technical excellence - apply and leverage your technology expertise to deliver advanced assurance over complex technology topics, support the development of innovative audit techniques by applying automation, AI and data analytics.
  • Workpaper review - review the work of peers and more junior audit team members and provide feedback and coaching.
  • Build and maintain strong relationships with IT stakeholders - interact with IT management as part of audits and regular meetings and engage with various stakeholders across the business.
  • Contribute to the development of the audit plan - apply your expertise and use the information gained from stakeholders to provide inputs for future audit topics.

About the Team Group Internal Audit (GIA) is a dynamic, international team of auditors united by a shared vision: 'Advancing Swiss Re's Resilience'. We are curious problem solvers who lead from every seat - challenging the status quo, understanding risk deeply, and responding to it with agility and purpose. We don't audit the old-fashioned way. We apply innovative, risk-focused techniques to ensure our audit efforts are always proportionate to the risks that matter most. We're continuously expanding our use of data analytics and automation, pushing the boundaries of what modern internal audit looks like. If you're someone who wants to be part of a team that's shaping the future of audit - this is the place for you.

About You

You're intellectually curious, delivery-oriented, and a natural collaborator. You bring a critical mindset to everything you do and thrive in environments where no two days look the same. You communicate with confidence and clarity in English, and you're comfortable navigating ambiguity with composure. Above all, you're genuinely passionate about technology, innovation, and transformation - and you want your work to have real impact. Essentials

  • At least 7 years of work experience in IT auditing and/or practical experience in the field of IT and Cyber security
  • Advanced understanding of IT risk and controls and the underlying technologies, concepts and processes
  • Demonstrated ability to adapt quickly to changing demands and rapidly develop in-depth knowledge of new audit areas
  • Proficiency in English, written and spoken

Nice to Haves

  • Qualified for professional certifications such as CISA, CISM, CISSP, CSX-P, CIA, or actively working towards one
  • Experience with cloud services and related security principles
  • Experience with auditing AI systems or familiarity with AI governance and risk frameworks Our company has a hybrid work model where the expectation is that you will be in the office at least three days per week
