Full description

Do you enjoy working with people? Would you like to join our nursing team at Gesundheitszentrum für das Alter Sydefädeli from January 1, 2027, or by agreement?

Tasks

  • As the primary contact person for the residents assigned to you, with a focus on psychiatric illnesses, you are responsible for planning, implementing, monitoring and adapting the care process, as well as delegating tasks to employees at other competency levels.
  • You perform nursing and medical-technical procedures, predominantly in complex, unstable situations in the long-term psychiatric care sector.
  • You advise and support residents and relatives during illness and crises.
  • You assume responsibility for the ward during the day.
  • You contribute to the development of ward goals to ensure quality and collaboration.

Profile

  • A strong sense of responsibility, the ability to think in an interconnected way and high resilience, as well as a willingness to embrace new things
  • Excellent organizational skills and a precise working style
  • Enthusiasm for working independently, combined with team spirit and commitment
  • Completed training as a nursing professional (HF/FH/DNII) with SRK recognition for diplomas from abroad, as well as professional experience in long-term psychiatric care or equivalent psychiatric nursing training
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude with a strong focus on people

We offer

Look forward to an exciting range of responsibilities in a professional and team-oriented working environment with a first-name culture throughout the company. People are at the center of what we do. That is why we support work-life balance; different working models make this possible.

As part of the City of Zurich and thanks to the size of our organization, we offer attractive development opportunities, progressive employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

The annual salary for this position ranges from Fr. 85’228.– to Fr. 113’882.– (100%), depending on experience.

About us

The City of Zurich’s geriatric centers are among the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in elderly care and nursing - with expertise, heart and a comprehensive range of services from a single source.

The Sydefädeli geriatric center is located in the lively and diverse Wipkingen neighborhood in District 10 and offers residents a comfortable home with all amenities. We are well integrated into the neighborhood and are committed to lively exchange with the neighborhood and between generations. Here, you will work alongside 60 colleagues.
