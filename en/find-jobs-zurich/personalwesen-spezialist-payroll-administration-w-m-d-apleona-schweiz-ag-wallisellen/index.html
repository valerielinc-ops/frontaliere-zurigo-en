Your tasks Do you want to use your payroll and HR administration expertise in a dynamic environment with a lot of creative leeway?   • Very good knowledge of MS Excel (incl. pivot) and ideally experience with Power BI
  • German at mother tongue level, very good knowledge of French
  • English is beneficial
  • High service orientation, structured and reliable working methods and teamwork
