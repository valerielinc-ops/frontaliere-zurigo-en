Position Overview

Who we are Novelis is driven by its purpose of Shaping a Sustainable World Together. As a global leader in innovative products and services and the world's largest recycler of aluminum, we partner with customers in the automotive, beverage can, aerospace and specialties industries to deliver solutions that improve the benefits of balanced lightweight aluminum. We have a coordinated network of technically sophisticated rolling and recycling plants as well as foundries across 29 facilities in 9 countries throughout North America, South America, Europe, and Asia with 12,750 employees worldwide. The company is headquartered in Atlanta, USA.

Our customers include some of the largest and best-known brands in the world, including Coca-Cola, BMW, Jaguar Land Rover, Airbus, Boeing, Nespresso, Samsung, and others. In addition, Novelis is leading the industry with its sustainability and recycling efforts. Responsibilities & Qualifications

What awaits you

The Senior Global Event Specialist is responsible for leading the planning, coordination, and execution of multiple high-impact events across Novelis’ regions and time zones. In this role , you will turn big ideas into memorable event experiences , bringing together commercial priorities, strong storytelling, smart budgets, and seamless execution. You will work with stakeholders across Commercial, Sales, Leadership, Legal, and other business functions to create events that feel polished, purposeful , and distinctly Novelis. From shaping the guest experience and coordinating creative partners to managing exhibits , on-site details, and post- event learnings, you will keep every moving part on track and help make each event better than the last.

  • Bring trade shows and customer events to life from first idea to final applaus - shaping concept s , booth experie nce , messaging , and the creative and logistic partners, who help make it happen
  • Coordinate with internal stakeholders to gather inputs, manage deliverables, and coordinate reviews and approvals
  • Own the practical magic behind great events : smart budgets, strong vendor partnerships, solid contracts, travel plans, venues, shipping, accommodations, and every on-site detail
  • Partner with Corporate External Communications and internal teams (Commercial, Strategy, Sustainability) to create event communications that are on-brand, well-timed, and easy for everyone to act on
  • Be the calm, capable lead on-site : coordinating teams, solving issues quickly, capturing what worked, and using insights and ROI to make the next event even stronger

How you convince us

  • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience in event management , marketing communications , or related field
  • Minimum of 5 years’ experience in trade show and/or event management
  • Strong background in event planning or project managem ent , marketing and budgeting
  • Experience sourcing, negotiating with, and managing event suppliers
  • Excellent time management and multitasking ability
  • Outstanding communication and stakeholder management skills
  • Ability to travel to events within Europe with possible international travel
  • Language excellent written and spoken English, German is a plus
