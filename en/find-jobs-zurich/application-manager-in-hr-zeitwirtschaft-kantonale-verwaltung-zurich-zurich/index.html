Role overview

Canton of Zurich Finance Directorate Office of Informatics Application Manager 100% The Office of Informatics (AFI) is the IT competence center of the Canton of Zurich. It provides comprehensive services in the area of ​​central IT infrastructure and canton-wide applications for directorates, authorities, the judiciary and independent institutions. Tasked with implementing the new information and communication technology (ICT) strategy, the Office of Information Technology accompanies and supports the Canton of Zurich in the digital world. Our IT solutions are just as diverse as the canton itself – from police and courts to hospitals, schools and universities. The focus is on our applications, which make the everyday lives of countless employees and residents easier. In the Applications Operations Services team, we take responsibility for the stable operation, further development and integration of these applications in our on-prem environment. Now we are looking for you – a committed personality who wants to actively shape digital change with know-how, curiosity and team spirit. Your tasks

  • Monitoring ongoing application operations and integration of cantonal on-prem applications (Windows, Linux and Kubernetes)
  • Carrying out 2nd and 3rd level support including monitoring
  • Provide workarounds as part of problem management
  • Assisting in the implementation of releases including change, deployment and test management incl. Document creation
  • Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders
  • Support lifecycle and interface management as well as further development
  • Active participation in the development of the new service area Your Profile
