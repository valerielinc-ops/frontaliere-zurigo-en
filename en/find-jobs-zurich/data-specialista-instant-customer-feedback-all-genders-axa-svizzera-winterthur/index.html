40-60%, permanent Winterthur / Smart Working

Shape your future – together with us!

Do you want to overcome technical challenges while helping to improve customer experiences? Do you enjoy making things work, diving into system landscapes, hunting bugs, evaluating data and actively shaping a tool with real impact? Then you are exactly right with us!

For our Customer Insights team, we are looking for a dedicated personality who technically supports and develops our customer feedback program. You work closely with Lead Instant Customer Feedback and data specialists in the departments, ensure smooth operations and carry out data analysis.

In the area of Customer Experience & Strategy, we are committed to ensuring that our customers are just as enthusiastic about AXA as we are. To achieve this goal, we are constantly developing our products and strategies. We develop innovative, inspiring solutions – and present them simply and comprehensibly to our customers, our employees and our partners as well as media professionals.

This is what awaits you

  • Technical support and optimization of our customer feedback tool in cooperation with Medallia, the leading provider of customer feedback systems
  • Create, customize and test new surveys, functions and processes as part of the customer feedback program
  • Identification of potential for improvement at technical, content and process level
  • Identification and fixing of bugs in the customer feedback tool or at AXA internal stakeholders
  • Support for the further development of the tool
  • Analysis of data from the customer feedback program and preparation of results for various stakeholders
  • Support in the “run the business”, e.g. processing requests from stakeholders
  • Support for the further development of the customer feedback program

So you help us to carry the voice of the customer into the organization and become even more customer-oriented.

You bring that with you

  • In the last year or completion of a technical or university degree, preferably in the fields of data science, business engineering or a related subject
  • Pronounced technical affinity and ability to move safely in complex system landscapes and keep an overview even in demanding situations
  • First experience on the Google Cloud Platform (GCP), SAP knowledge is an advantage
  • Basic knowledge of SQL & Python
  • Sound knowledge in quantitative analyses
  • Very good knowledge of dealing with Excel
  • Familiarity with BI tools such as Power-BI, SAP Analytics Cloud, Data Studio desirable
  • Interest in mastering technical challenges
  • Structured, exact and solution-oriented working method
  • Independent personality
  • Excellent knowledge of German and fluent English (word and writing)

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
