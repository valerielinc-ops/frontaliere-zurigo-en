Full description

<b>Bülach Hospital grows - grow with it.</b>

Tasks

  • Professional preparation, storage and support of patients in the operating room
  • Provision and operation of medical devices
  • Independent preparation of all required storage materials for the respective OP
  • Support for pre- and post-operative activities
  • Shared responsibility for a smooth interdisciplinary operation

Requirements

  • Operational storage course completed
  • Professional experience as a storage nurse*
  • Good knowledge of German (min. level B2)
  • PC knowledge is an advantage
  • Readiness for shift work
  • Teamwork, initiative and high commitment
