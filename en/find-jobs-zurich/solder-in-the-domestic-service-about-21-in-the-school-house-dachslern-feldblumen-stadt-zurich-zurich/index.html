Full description

For the school Dachslern Feldblumen we are looking for 26. October 2026 an experienced detacher (about 21%).

Tasks

During the school weeks, the gymnasium is handed over cleanly to the external clubs every two weeks. This includes cleaning the hall, wardrobes, showers, toilet and corridor, before and after the changing clubs. Once in the allocated week, the hall must be cleaned with a scrubber. The working hours are from Monday to Friday from 17:15 to about 23:00 (depending on the occupancy of the hall and season). During sports, minor cleaning work must be done in adjacent school buildings. Holiday payments are only possible during school holidays. Possible weekend assignments can be added in the future.

Profile

  • Good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Experience in cleaning with scrubbers
  • Physical robustness, mobility and good health
  • Independent working methods and reliability.

We offer

We offer you a varied, versatile and independent activity and a salary according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich.

About us

The Zurich-Letzi school district comprises Stadtkreis 9 with the districts Albisrieden, Altstetten and Grünau. In the 16 schools of our rapidly growing school circle, more than 5,000 primary school students are taught and supervised. More than 1,200 employees work for them in our schools in the areas of teaching, care, home service and technology as well as administration. They all work daily for the interests of the students and a smooth school day.
