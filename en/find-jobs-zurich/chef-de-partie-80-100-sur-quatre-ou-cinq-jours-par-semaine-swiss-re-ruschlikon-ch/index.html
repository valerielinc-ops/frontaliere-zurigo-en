Full description

To strengthen the Swiss Re Gastronomy kitchen team at the Swiss Re Centre for Global Dialogue in Rüschlikon, we are looking for a committed and passionate personality. as Chef de Partie. Entry as of 1 August 2026 or by arrangement. Their main tasks

  • Independent preparation, organization and leadership in various posts to ensure a professional, creative and sustainable banquet service
  • Implementation of our sustainable gastronomy strategy in our own area of responsibility in close cooperation with the team
  • Active participation in the further development of our entire culinary offering
  • Transfer of expertise to learners and take on a role model in their development
  • The working days are at an 80% expense Monday to Thursday, with a 100% expense additional Friday
  • Flexible working hours between 07:00 and 22:00 in shift operation (through service possible, room hour optional)
  • Sporadic assignments at headquarters in Zurich and occasional weekend services

Swiss Re Gastronomy – Your working environment The Swiss Re Centre for Global Dialogue is a modern conference centre with hotel operations and stands for lived, uncompromising hospitality. In a kitchen team of around 25 employees and learners, you will create a versatile culinary offer for internal and external events – from aperos to lunches and dinners to large events and buffets. Our guests benefit from a wide range of creative concepts in different locations. What you bring

  • Completed training as a cook and at least two years of professional experience
  • Structured, independent and quality-oriented working method with high operational readiness
  • Team-oriented personality with appreciative communication style and strong social skills
  • Interest and commitment to the topic of sustainability and its implementation in everyday working life
  • Motivation to develop and take responsibility
  • Good oral German skills

What we offer

  • Swiss Re, in every respect a modern and renowned employer
  • Above-average pension fund, pension and insurance benefits
  • Highly discounted public transport and leisure subscriptions
  • Internal and external training opportunities
  • Kids House – Child Care
  • And much more.
