Do you want to actively shape the digital transformation of our bank? In the four-year apprenticeship as a developer: in digital business, you work on projects around digital solutions and benefit from a varied and future-oriented education.

We offer you a future-oriented, exciting and varied vocational training – your ideal start in the world of digital business. The Zürcher Kantonalbank is one of the largest training companies in the canton of Zurich and supports more than 420 apprentices every year. Would you like to be part of our Young Talents? We look forward to your application!

Learn more – Your tasks

  • Packing your competence backpack with all important basic skills during your basic apprenticeship year with our external provider
  • Get to know different business processes and data of our bank and work with advanced technologies
  • Analyse processes, develop profitable solutions and coordinate implementation – independently or together with specialists
  • Jointly driving and simplifying processes – ensuring efficiency, automation and a digital future
  • Forming an important link between business and technology
  • Participation in training and internal events to further develop your personal, social and methodological skills
  • Graduation from secondary school A with a grade average of 4,625 (mathematics including geometry, German, English)*
  • Good Multicheck Economy and Administration – Evaluation Developer of digital business
  • You are excited about topics such as computer science, technology, sales and marketing
  • You like to communicate, are open-minded and have fun working with others
  • You find networked thinking and concentrated work easy
  • Required application documents: application/motivation letter, curriculum vitae, all four upper-level certificates, multicheck economics and administration (evaluation developer:in digital business), location determination of the secondary level (interface test, check S2 or similar)

*The grade averages given refer to the grades in level 1. For compartments in level 2, the grade must be correspondingly higher.
