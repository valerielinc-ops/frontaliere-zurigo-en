We are innovative hosts: in more than 200 companies in the gastronomy, hotel industry, bakery, child care and catering. The residential and business properties are maintained and the hotels are constantly developed. You want to not only accompany construction projects, but as a builder: actively participate? At ZFV, you take responsibility for medium-sized construction projects of our hotels and investment properties, from project planning to successful implementation. You combine your know-how in construction management with an understanding of real estate, profitability and our products. Are you our new team member who will join us from 1. December 2026 or supported by agreement? Your task menu
