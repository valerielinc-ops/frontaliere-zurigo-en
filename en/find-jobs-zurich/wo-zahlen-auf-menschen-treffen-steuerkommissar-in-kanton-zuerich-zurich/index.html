Canton of Zurich Financial Directorate Tax Office Where figures meet people - tax commissioner 80 - 100% Approximately 750 employees work in the tax office of the Canton of Zurich and ensure at the Zurich Altstetten location that taxes are assessed and received correctly and uniformly. A task that is more versatile than you think. Because behind every tax return hides more than just numbers. It is about people, their stories and connections that need to be understood. This is exactly what requires a clear head – maybe exactly yours? And this is exactly where you come into play – as a tax commissioner in the private sector. You will deepen your knowledge in tax law or re-enter, analyze issues and bring structure to complex topics. Step by step you develop a sense of taxation. You are not alone. Your team accompanies you from day one, supports you in everyday life and shares its knowledge with you. Curiosity is the best prerequisite for the start, which is done by agreement and coordinated with our internal training start. Your area of responsibility:

  • Audit and assessment of tax returns of self-employed and self-employed persons
  • Answer tax questions in writing, by telephone or in personal conversation
  • Professional contact person for municipal tax offices
  • Examination of objections and further clarification of open issues
  • Participation in review proceedings
  • Accompaniment of appeal proceedings and representation of the state in corresponding proceedings Your profile:
  • A completed bachelor's degree, ideally in business administration or law, or a higher specialist training in finance and accounting, trust or as a business economist / in HF
  • Interest in tax issues and initial insights into the financial or tax area of advantage
  • Interested in understanding complex issues and making decisions independently
  • A structured, independent working method and enjoyment of taking on responsibility
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing (C2)
  • CH citizenship, since it is a public function This is what we offer:
  • Training “on the job” and at full wage, as well as versatile development opportunities such as further specialization in tax law, participation in process and project work and assumption of management responsibility
  • An experienced, committed and collaborative team
  • Attractive employment conditions such as flexible working hours, the opportunity to work in the home office, lunch check and a modern workplace in the immediate vicinity of Zurich Altstetten station
  • Internal and external training opportunities in the new field of activity Are you interested? We look forward to your application. Please use the online tool so that we can process your documents. Your new environment: www.zh.ch/steueramt

Pensum: 80-100 %
