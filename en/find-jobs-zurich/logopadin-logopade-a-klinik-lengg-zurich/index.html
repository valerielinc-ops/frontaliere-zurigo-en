Full description

Tasks

  • Introduction and progression diagnostics as well as treatment of neurologically caused aphasia, dysarthrophonia, facial paralysis, cognitive communication disorder, dysphagia and speechapraxia especially in ambulatory setting
  • Possibility for active participation in in-house logopaedic and interdisciplinary training
  • Competent advice for relatives and patients
  • Cooperation with external institutions

Requirements

  • Completed training for Speech Pedophile/Logopaedist (MSc, BSc or BA)
  • First professional experience in neurorehabilitation
  • Advantageous training(s) in the treatment of neurological speech, speech and swallowing disorders
  • Self-employed work
  • Interest in interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Enjoying the use of evidence-based methods

Klinik Lengg AG in Zurich is the centre of excellence for epileptology and neurological rehabilitation.

It is our aim to provide patients with neurological diseases with the widest possible rehabilitation and to ensure an accurate diagnosis and sustainable treatment of patients with epilepsy. Around 300 employees are committed to humanely, competently, innovatively and reliably ensuring that our patients regain the greatest possible independence - and thus their quality of life. Become part of one of our interdisciplinary and interprofessional teams.
