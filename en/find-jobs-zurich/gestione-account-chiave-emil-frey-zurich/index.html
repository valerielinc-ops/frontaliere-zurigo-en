What you should know about us

The Emil Frey Gruppe is not only a Swiss family-owned company with over 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the company's supporting pillars. The focus of its activities is on importing automotive brands, operating garages, logistics for spare and accessory parts, and providing financial services. PCDOL & Logep is the association of the official importers of the Peugeot, Citroën, DS Automobiles, Opel and Leapmotor brands. It also includes Logep AG, which manages the brands' entire after-sales business.

Your responsibilities

To strengthen our Fleet team in Zurich Oerlikon for the brands Peugeot, Citroën, DS Automobiles, Opel and Leapmotor, we are looking for, with immediate effect or by agreement, a Key Account Manager for Romandy, based in French-speaking Switzerland.

  • Building and maintaining customer relationships
  • Acquiring new customers
  • Needs analysis and consulting
  • Identifying optimization potential and cost-cutting measures in fleet management
  • Independently handling all sales-related processes
  • Coordination between internal departments: Working with Sales, Marketing, After-Sales and Logistics to initiate and implement customer-oriented solutions
  • Sales and revenue responsibility, as well as achievement of sales targets and KPIs
  • Reporting and analysis
  • Market monitoring: Actively monitoring trends, competitors' activities and market conditions in order to identify opportunities and risks for the company at an early stage
  • Participation in/planning of marketing activities and campaigns to retain and acquire customers in the area of fleet management

Your profile

  • Completed training in sales, business administration or comparable qualifications
  • Several years of experience in the fleet business – in-depth knowledge of light commercial vehicles is an advantage
  • B2B sales experience: Experience in direct sales to companies, particularly in the areas of long-term business relationships, contract negotiations and fleet management
  • Analytical skills, negotiating ability and strong communication skills
  • Customer- and solution-oriented personality with a strong ability to identify customer needs and offer tailored solutions that meet the specific requirements of fleet customers
  • Initiative and self-organization, as well as the ability to work in a team
  • Flexibility and willingness to travel
  • Language skills: German and French, spoken and written
  • English and/or Italian is an advantage
