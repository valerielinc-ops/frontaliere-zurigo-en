Full description

Become part of our HVAC team. We develop, design and plan construction and industrial projects of all kinds, from the initial idea to implementation.

Take on responsibility with us as

PROJECT MANAGER HVAC-Building Technology 80-100% - ZURICH

YOUR TASKS

  • Project management for planning contracts in the field of HVAC (construction and industry)
  • Project management in compliance with cost, quality and deadline targets
  • Leadership of the project team
  • Consulting and support of customers in all project phases, from strategic planning to commissioning and final optimization
  • Development of conceptual designs and technical feasibility
  • Implementation of innovative solutions, advising customers on the economic efficiency of the property over the entire life cycle

YOUR PROFILE

  • Higher education in the field of HVAC activities
  • Technical diploma SSST / technician HF or HS/FH in the field of RCVS is an advantage
  • Several years of experience as a project manager in the planning of building technology and HVAC systems in Switzerland
  • Willingness to work in an interdisciplinary manner with the branches in Bellinzona and Fribourg
  • Good knowledge of Swiss plant construction laws and regulations
  • Good organizational and communication skills in the team and with customers
  • Precise, independent and team-oriented personality with entrepreneurial thinking
  • Very good knowledge of German (oral and written); other languages (English, French, Italian) are an advantage
  • BIM knowledge and experience with digital planning methods are an advantage
