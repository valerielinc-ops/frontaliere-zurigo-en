Full description

Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for a specialist surgical technique for our specialist team "Musculoskeletal".

Tasks

  • In the specialist team of the musculoskeletal group you work mainly in the areas of ortho, trauma, neuro, hand/plastic surgery, jaw surgery and ENT
  • Various intervention methods with state-of-the-art technology such as navigation, mobile CT or personalized 3D models guarantee a varied and exciting everyday work

Profile

  • You are a competent, flexible and motivated dipl. Specialist surgical technology with great professional commitment
  • You are distinguished by resilience and empathy
  • You have professional experience in the fields of musculoskeletal group
  • Initiative, creativity, organizational talent and team-oriented action are your strengths
  • Due to your communicative nature, it is easy for you to maintain a good cooperation with the interdisciplinary interfaces in the hospital
