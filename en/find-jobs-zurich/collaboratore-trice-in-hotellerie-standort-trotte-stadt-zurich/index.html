Full description

Do you like working with people? From January 1, 2027, would you like to work with our team in the hotel industry for the Health Center for the Old Trotte?

Tasks

  • With your work in the areas of cleaning and service, you make an important contribution to the quality of life of our residents.
  • They support the professional cleaning of resident apartments and public spaces.
  • They provide a professional and warm service in the dining room and restaurant, which is oriented towards the needs and habits of our residents and guests.
  • They provide support for service events and, if necessary, help flexibly in all areas of the hotel industry, including the laundry.
  • They consistently comply with the applicable safety, hygiene and cleaning regulations.

Profile

  • Several years of professional experience in the fields of cleaning and service, ideally in an old age institution or a comparable environment
  • Enjoyment of contact with older people as well as a pronounced service and quality orientation
  • Teamwork, care, reliability as well as good knowledge of German and basic IT user knowledge (MS Office and working time recording)
  • Willingness to work irregular hours and weekend services

We offer

Enjoy an exciting field of work in a professional and team-oriented working environment with a du-culture throughout the company. The human being is at our center. That is why we support the reconciliation of private and professional life, different working models make it possible.

As part of the city of Zurich and due to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, progressive employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

Depending on the experience, the annual salary for this function is between Fr. 57’490.– and Fr. 71’764.– (100%).

About us

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

The health center for the Alter Trotte is located between Hönggerberg and Limmat in Wipkingen in district 10 and offers residents a home with all amenities. The modern new building of the health centre for the Alter Trotte is located in a unique location - with views over Zurich, the lake and the mountains - and offers a high-quality living and working experience. Here you work together with 70 colleagues.
