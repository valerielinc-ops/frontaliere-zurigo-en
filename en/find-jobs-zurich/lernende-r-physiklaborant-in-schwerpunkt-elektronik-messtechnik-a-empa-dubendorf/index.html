Full description

Materials science and technology are our passion. With our cutting-edge research, Empa’s approximately 1,100 employees make decisive contributions to the benefit of society for a future worth living in. Empa is a research institution of the ETH Domain. Apprentice/physics laboratory (focus on electronics/measurement) (a) For August 2027, in the Transport at Nanoscale Interfaces department and the Centre for Electronics and Reliability, we offer two challenging training places in a fascinating research environment. What to expect Working at an ETH research institute brings a lot of variety into everyday work. If you are interested in physics, electronics, technology and materials, we guarantee you exciting tasks in an international research environment. You also love working and tinkering. What you bring Secondary school A will soon be history and you will finish it with very good performances, especially in the natural science, mathematical but also linguistic subjects. Your theoretical and practical interest in physics, electronics and computer science and in technical systems and in the field of materials and chemistry supports you in your apprenticeship. You have a good ability to observe and comprehend, are persistent, experimental and enjoy working in a team. Your technical understanding and manual skill are just as much a part of this as your ability for abstract and analytical thinking. We also appreciate care and reliability. The joy of dealing with computers and electronics is a must. You are also convinced to start your future with a vocational apprenticeship. What we offer

  • Versatile basic training in electronics, metrology, mechanics, design, programming, microscopy and materials science
  • Development, manufacture and commissioning of measurement systems and test setups
  • Preparation, implementation and documentation of experiments
  • Physical investigation of the material properties of electronic assemblies and components
  • Collaboration in research projects at various Empa departments
  • Dedicated and qualified specialist trainers
  • Very well equipped laboratory workstations with modern equipment
  • You attend the vocational school in Zurich. The visit of the BMS is supported By July 2026, the incoming applications will be reviewed. If you have progressed, a first meeting will take place for you in July / August. Then we decide the closest selection for the following selection day. Further information on the teaching of Empa can be found in our factsheet. We offer an exciting training with the latest technology. Empa with its extensive campus is a lively and innovative research environment with an international flair and excellent employment conditions in the agglomeration of Zurich. “a” stands for “all” in our job advertisements. We live a culture of inclusion and respect. We welcome all people who have the desire for innovative, sustainable and meaningful activities - only that counts. We look forward to your complete online application incl. Letter of motivation, CV, certificates, diplomas and contact details of two reference persons. Please submit them exclusively via our application portal. Applications by e-mail or by post will not be considered.
