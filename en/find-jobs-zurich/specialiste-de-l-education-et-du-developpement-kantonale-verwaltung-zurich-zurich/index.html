Canton of Zurich Economic Directorate General Secretariat Specialist/in Education & Development 50% The General Secretariat is committed to high location quality, attractive jobs and prosperity for the population in the Canton of Zurich. It is a hub, planning centre and competence centre for the business of the Economic Directorate, advises and supports the Directorate and the organizational units and offers services in the areas of human resources, finance, communication, law and digital transformation. The Education & Development team in the Human Resources & Development department is the competence center for human resources development and, among other things, is responsible for an effective educational offer that is continuously developed in cooperation with the areas. Our goal is to empower employees and leaders for their tasks and to support them in the development of competences for tomorrow. As of November 1, 2026 or by agreement, our team is looking for reinforcement, for the time being limited to two years. Do you want to develop educational offers with an impact, think conceptually and like to work project-oriented? Then shape the future of education and development with us. Your tasks:

  • Demand assessment as well as conception, planning and implementation of practical educational offers (webinars, courses, change of sides, etc.) for internal employees
  • Management of projects for the development and introduction of new training offers in cooperation with the departments
  • Participation in cross-divisional projects and active participation in the learning and development culture
  • Revision, further development and quality assurance of existing offers
  • Assuming further projects and tasks, tailored to your experiences and strengths Your profile:
  • Continuing education in the field of adult education, education or personnel development (e.g. SVEB 1 certificate or federal law). Professional ID Instructor
  • Demonstrated experience in the development and implementation of educational offers as well as in the management and cooperation of projects
  • Methodological-didactic skills, conceptual strength and skill in using digital tools (e.g. M365, collaboration tools) as well as digital learning methods
  • Experience in dealing with LMS/LXP is an advantage
  • Strong social and advisory skills as well as gaining communication in dealing with different stakeholders
  • Independent, structured and service-oriented working method with hands-on mentality, team spirit and a touch of humor We offer: The Canton of Zurich as an employer has a lot to offer: Flexible working hours, contributions to the food costs and a workplace directly in the heart of Zurich, a few minutes walk from Zurich Central Station. And best of all: a varied task with creative scope awaits you! Find out for yourself what still distinguishes us. Bettina Mikschi, Head of Education & Development, 043 259 26 25 or bettina.mikschi@vd.zh.ch, will be happy to provide you with further information. We look forward to your application! Find out more about us at www.zh.ch/vd.

Pensum: 50%
