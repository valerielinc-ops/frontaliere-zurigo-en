Location: Wangen b. Dübendorf When: 01. January 2027 or upon availability

Your mission: Shape the digital workplace at Skyguide! You will design and evolve secure, intuitive workplace services that enhance the digital employee experience across Skyguide. You will own the workplace lifecycle from architecture and deployment to operations and continuous improvement. Through automation and collaboration, you will deliver reliable, compliant and future-ready workplace services that enable people to perform at their best. Your tasks:

  • Design, implement and continuously improve modern workplace services, ensuring security, stability and compliance
  • Drive workplace lifecycle management, including Windows 11, device management and workplace technologies
  • Automate operating system, software and configuration deployments
  • Manage workplace operating systems and application portfolios throughout their lifecycle
  • Maintain standards, procedures and documentation to support efficient and compliant operations
  • Lead workplace initiatives and projects, driving innovation and service improvements
  • Provide expert 3rd level support and collaborate with internal stakeholders and external providers What we are looking for:
  • Higher education in Information Technology or equivalent professional experience
  • Proven experience in workplace engineering in enterprise environments
  • Strong expertise in Windows, Entra ID, Active Directory, Intune, SCCM and Microsoft 365
  • Experience delivering technical solutions and projects in a structured and collaborative way
  • Problem solving: You analyse complex challenges and deliver pragmatic, sustainable solutions that create lasting value
  • Teamwork: You foster strong partnerships, share knowledge openly and collaborate effectively across diverse stakeholders
  • Fluent in English and German
  • French is an advantage

What we offer: We offer flexible working options, depending on the role, and a competitive salary with attractive working conditions and numerous fringe benefits. Our employees have access to a wide range of training opportunities to support their growth and development. As an equal-opportunity employer, we are committed to building a diverse and inclusive team, bringing together people of different identities and backgrounds.
