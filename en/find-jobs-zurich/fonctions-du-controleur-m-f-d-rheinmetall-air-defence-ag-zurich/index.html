Full description

WHERE FOR WE SEE YOU Want to make a significant contribution to corporate management with sound analyses and business know-how? In this versatile business partner role, you create transparency about the financial development of your areas of responsibility and support the management with well-founded decision-making principles. Your tasks in detail:

  • Responsibility for budget, forecast and medium-term planning of the assigned divisions
  • Business Partner for Finance, Human Resources, Information Technology and Real Estate
  • Implementation of the monthly, quarterly and annual accounts including analysis and commentary on the development of costs and earnings
  • Analysis of personnel, IT, infrastructure and administrative costs as well as derivation of recommendations for optimization and action
  • Preparation of target-actual, forecast and previous-year comparisons to ensure transparent financial management
  • Support for cost centres, internal orders and investments as well as support for profitability calculations and business cases
  • Creation of management reports, KPI evaluations and well-founded decision-making basis for management
  • Ensuring a high data quality in SAP as well as continuous development of the controlling processes and reporting WHAT YOU SHOULD INVOLVE
  • Completed studies (FH/uni) in business administration, finance, controlling or a comparable education
  • Alternatively, basic commercial training with corresponding further training in controlling or accounting
  • Several years of professional experience in controlling, ideally in an industrial environment
  • In-depth knowledge in budgeting, forecasting, monthly financial statements and cost center controlling
  • Very good user knowledge in Excel and experience with SAP FI/CO
  • Experience with Power BI or comparable business intelligence tools
  • Strong business understanding as well as high numerical affinity and analytical thinking
  • Structured, independent and solution-oriented working method
  • High service and business partner orientation as well as enjoyment of working with different specialist areas
  • Team-oriented personality with hands-on mentality and the ability to set priorities under time pressure
  • Negotiable knowledge of German as well as good knowledge of English in word and writing WHAT WE ARE TO YOU
  • A dynamic working environment with exciting projects
  • Flexible working hours (active and trusted working hours)
  • Very good social benefits and a super-compulsory pension fund
  • Employee benefits of a large corporation, employee share purchase program
  • Promotion of needs-based training
  • Staff restaurant and external catering and shopping facilities nearby
  • Individual internal and external personnel development measures in cooperation with our Rheinmetall Academy
  • Workplace a few minutes walk from the Zurich-Oerlikon train station KONTAKTDETAILS Responsible contact: Christoph Badertscher For questions about your application, please use the contact form. Please note that dossiers of recruiters are not considered.
