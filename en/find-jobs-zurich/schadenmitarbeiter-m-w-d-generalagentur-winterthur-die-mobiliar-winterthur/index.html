Full description

This is what awaits you

The human being counts with us - even in the event of damage. You fit into our motivated claims team if you work with heart, appreciate real closeness and enjoy helping people when they need us most. We believe that every claim is an opportunity to build trust and take responsibility. This is exactly what our team in Winterthur is all about. If you communicate openly, like to tackle and keep a laugh in hectic moments, you are right with us. Our claims department is the central service area to deliver on our performance promise to our customers. We are therefore looking for an employee who is ready to go the extra mile for our loyal customers immediately or by agreement to strengthen our claims teams.

This is what you do with us

The focus of your job is the uncomplicated support of our customers in the event of damage – from notification to completion – on request also including on-site damage inspections in order to help our customers quickly and easily.

You use your attention and service orientation in the support of the entire claims process for the benefit of our customers.

With your personal nature, commitment and ideas, you contribute to successful team collaboration in various topic areas and are in contact with internal and external contact persons, companies and craftsmen. You maintain a close exchange with your team and all parties involved in the event of damage.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training in the insurance sector
  • Completed training «Insurance intermediary/in VBV» or willingness to complete it
  • Professional experience in claims processing material/liability beneficial
  • Enjoying contact and accompanying our customers in the event of damage
  • Proactive and independent working methods
  • Responsible, committed and open personality
  • Fluent German skills in words and writing, other languages are a plus

Working with us

We offer you a versatile, varied and responsible job in a motivated environment and a modern workplace in a central location in the city of Winterthur. In addition to attractive employment conditions, you get the chance for continuous further training.

In order to perform this function, the Insurance Supervision Act requires the conclusion of an insurance intermediary/in VBV, Profil Allbranche. If this is not available, we are happy to support with tailor-made internal training.

The job starts immediately or by agreement.

This is what we offer

Participation in success

If we are successful, our employees also benefit. Each year, the distribution of a successful share and its amount is decided.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses. We participate in job-relevant training.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer up to 25% discount on bonuses, discounts on mobile phones and free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
