Full description

At Julius Baer, we celebrate and value the individual qualities you bring, enabling you to be impactful, to be entrepreneurial, to be empowered, and to create value beyond wealth. Let’s shape the future of wealth management together.

In this role, you will be part of the Strategic Pricing team and support the development of the bank's pricing tool, a key platform used to manage and govern pricing across multiple booking centres. You will work at the intersection of business, technology, and data, helping to translate stakeholder needs into innovative and business-driven solutions.

This internship offers a unique opportunity to gain insight into the private banking industry, build a strong network across the organisation, and develop practical product management skills in a dynamic and highly collaborative environment.

Start Date: January 2027 or later. Flexible start dates can be arranged in Q1

  • January or February is preferred.

YOUR CHALLENGE Join a leading private bank in Switzerland and accelerate your career in the Pricing and Revenue Development department, where you will contribute to strategic pricing initiatives across the organisation.

What to Work on?

Pricing Tool Strategy and Development

  • Support the planning, prioritisation, and tracking of the pricing tool product roadmap
  • Perform business and data analyses to support product decisions, roadmap prioritisation, and strategic initiatives
  • Gather business requirements from cross-functional stakeholders across the organisation
  • Translate business needs into clear and actionable functional requirements that guide the development of impactful solutions

Stakeholder Management and Communication

  • Prepare presentations, decision papers, and communication materials for management and key stakeholders
  • Collaborate with stakeholders across Pricing, Operations, Finance, and IT
  • Coordinate stakeholder feedback and support alignment on product priorities and strategic objectives

Process Improvement and Automation

  • Identify opportunities to improve processes, reporting, and documentation
  • Contribute to automation initiatives that increase efficiency, scalability, and improve data quality

What to Learn?

  • Swiss and international wealth management market dynamics and client expectations
  • The business model, strategic priorities, and performance drivers in private banking
  • The core product suite and service offerings of a global wealth manager
  • Product management principles and best practices, including roadmap planning, requirements management, and feature delivery
  • How to translate business needs into functional requirements and collaborate with software development teams to develop effective solutions
  • Pricing strategies and revenue management concepts within a leading private banking organisation
  • Stakeholder management and cross-functional collaboration across Pricing, Operations, Finance, and IT
  • Business analysis techniques and data-driven decision-making in a strategic business environment

YOUR PROFILE

  • Educational Background: University degree (or equivalent) in Banking and Finance, Business Administration, Economics, Business Information Systems, Data Science, or another analytical discipline
  • Analytical Skills: Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Experience: First professional experience in private banking, financial services, product management, or strategy consulting
  • Project Management: First project management experience, with the ability to complete work packages independently and collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams
  • Communication and Analytical Skills: Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to prepare professional presentations and perform structured analyses. Advanced proficiency in Microsoft PowerPoint and Excel is required
  • Technical Affinity: Strong interest in technology and digital products
  • Attention to Detail: Structured and detail-oriented working style with a strong focus on quality and accuracy
  • Proactive and Results-Oriented: Self-starter with a proactive mindset and a strong ability to identify opportunities for improvement and drive solutions
  • Language Skills: Fluency in German and English is required
  • Eligibility to work in Switzerland
  • Experience with tools such as Jira, Confluence, Tableau, SQL, Python, or similar technologies is considered a plus
