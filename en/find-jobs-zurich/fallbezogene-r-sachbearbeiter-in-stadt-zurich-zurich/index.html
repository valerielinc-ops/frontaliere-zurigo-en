Full description

For the social center Selnau we are looking for 1. November 2026 or by arrangement, limited to 1 year, a new team member.

Tasks

  • They work hand in hand with the case-leading social workers in the profile teams.
  • You take over the administrative processing of client dossiers. This includes, inter alia, settlements and resettlements for persons with social assistance and/or assistance, income and wealth management, scholarship applications and family allowances.
  • You are an important contact person for clients, clarify the formal responsibility and provide information.
  • You have regular contact with branch offices, such as tax authorities, health insurance funds, banks and insurance companies.
  • They shall perform general secretarial tasks.

Profile

  • Completed Swiss Federal Commercial Education
  • Good knowledge of social security or work experience in a social service
  • High service orientation and pleasure in working with people
  • Ability to deal with numbers
  • Correct oral and written expression in German

We offer

An interesting and varied activity in a meaningful area. A careful familiarization and a collegial working environment. In our organization, we maintain open and transparent communication.

About us

With around 2,400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a social Zurich. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.

In the social services more than 1000 employees shape the social Zurich. We accompany and support people in different situations, promote participation and strengthen social cohesion. Our services include social welfare, child and adult protection, school social work, child and youth welfare and socioculture. We do our work interdisciplinary and throughout the city: in school houses, counselling centres, the five social centres as well as in facilities such as the Youth and Culture House Dynamo, the Debate House Karl der Grosse and the district centres Schütze and Bakeranlage.
