Full description

Over two billion people rely on Schindler every day. Our many years of experience and innovative strength make us number one in the Swiss elevator and escalator business. As part of the Schindler family, you design places for everyone more accessible, inclusive and sustainable. We combine innovation, security and inclusion and diversity within the company. Become part of our 150-year success story and shape the future of mobility.

Together one floor up as

Sales Support Modernization of Elevator Systems (m/f/d), 80-100 %

Your day – your plan! Design your 40-hour week according to your needs. We attach great importance to your further development and offer you exactly what you need. Learn more about us in this video

Your new challenges

If an elevator has to be modernized or replaced because, for example, a commercial building becomes a residential building or the elevator no longer corresponds to the state of the art, then the team «Sale Modernization and Replacement» is at the place. We show architects, owners and tenants the possible solutions, advise them comprehensively and sell them the best solution. You will see the latest products and digital solutions, have many touch points for service, project implementation and, of course, for sale. In this important turntable function, you get to know the entire elevator business from scratch.

These are your varied and versatile tasks

  • You go alone or with the service technician in the “field” to the facilities on site to take project shots / mass shots. So you are also "outside" with the customer and get exciting insights behind the scenes of many properties that you otherwise only know as a guest
  • Together with the responsible sales representative, our engineering and the processing you create the offer for our customers
  • For this, you also obtain external supplier offers or calculate the subcontractor costs in our own tools
  • From time to time you also support sales in administrative matters

Your ideal conditions

  • Completed (technical) training and/or affinity for technology
  • High willingness to learn new things and always look for new solutions
  • Pragmatic, committed, self-employed, you enjoy working alone as well as in and with the team
  • Secure handling of MS-Office and not afraid of something SAP and other IT tools

Our benefits

  • Take your next career step and get started. We offer you perspective and platform and you shape your future
  • Become part of Team Züri and experience People Development! Maybe even in one of our talent programs?
