Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With more than 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric offer of Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in ambulatory, day-clinical and inpatient settings. The Group employs around 1800 people and achieved a turnover of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For our patient admission and triage site we are looking for from February 2027 or by agreement

Their tasks

  • Responsible for answering and handling all allocations and requests
  • Lively contact with referees, patients, relatives and authorities
  • Responsible for a good triage and thus for optimal bed management
  • Maintaining waiting lists, appointments and advice for patients as well as relatives or referrals

Your profile

  • Completed training for the Dipl. Care professional HF / FH
  • Professional experience in the psychiatric environment as well as a secure feeling in dealing with people in challenging life situations
  • Good IT user knowledge
  • Willingness to work shifts (Monday – Friday, 7 a.m. – 7 p.m.)
  • Teamwork, flexibility, independence and a sense of responsibility distinguish you

Our offer

  • No night, weekend or holiday services
  • A varied and responsible activity in a motivated team
  • Meaningful working environment with close interdisciplinary exchange
  • Modern infrastructure
  • Current employment conditions
  • Miscellaneous benefits

If you have any questions, Bianca Dürr, Head of Patient Admission, is at your disposal on +41 (0)71 929 60 04.

We look forward to your online application!
