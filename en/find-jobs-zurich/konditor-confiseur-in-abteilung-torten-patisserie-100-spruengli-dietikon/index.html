Full description

The Swiss family business, founded in 1836, is now one of the most renowned confectioneries in Europe with its exquisite range. The products from Sprüngli stand for the best quality, unique freshness and naturalness. The perfect creations delight customers from all over the world every day.

Pastry chef/confectioner in the cake/patisserie department (100%)

100%, Dietikon

Working at Sprüngli

In our factory in Dietikon we are looking for an experienced, motivated and committed person for the cakes/patisserie department immediately or by appointment

What you do with us

You will work in our cake/patisserie department in our factory in Dietikon and work as a team to produce our sophisticated cream cakes and cream patisserie of the highest quality. You can start work early (5 a.m. or 6 a.m.) and work regular weekend shifts on a night shift.

Your tasks

  • Handcrafted production of our cake, cream and patisserie range
  • Production of standard and seasonal products as well as special orders
  • Covering, glazing and coating different products
  • Compliance with safety, hygiene and quality requirements

What we wish for

  • Apprenticeship qualification as a confectioner EFZ or EBA
  • Enjoy the job you have learned and enjoy working in a team
  • Exact and quality-conscious working methods as well as resilience even in hectic situations
  • Mobility and flexibility, especially in season
  • An open, communicative personality with technical skills and good physical condition
  • Good knowledge of German
  • PC knowledge

You can look forward to that

With us you can expect a diverse and challenging range of tasks in a lively, modern environment. You work in a motivated team with an open “you” culture and real cohesion.
