Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Occupation by appointment

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 19303

Hirslanden Clinic is the largest private acute care hospital in Switzerland. It has a wide range of cantonal service contracts (hospital list) as well as service contracts for highly specialized medicine (IVHSM).

ITS TASKS

  • Care of patients of internal medicine and those of invasive disciplines with relevant comorbidities and complex clinical pictures on the bed stations
  • Professional and personnel management and further training of assistant doctors
  • Collegial and close cooperation with other disciplines, centres and assignments
  • Participation in the institute’s medical service
  • No night services need be provided, only background services

YOUR PROFILE

  • Specialist title General Internal Medicine (or internationally comparable title)
  • Broad knowledge of inpatient internist care and work experience in intensive care and/or emergency medicine
  • Knowledge of sonography is an advantage
  • High social skills and initiative
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary cooperation and teaching assistant doctors

You can expect a versatile and demanding job in an advanced working environment. We offer you an exciting and versatile environment with modern employment conditions and are also recognized as a category A institution for general internal medicine.

You benefit from regular, high-quality internal further education and training events, and the Institute for General Internal Medicine offers comprehensive support for external further education – both professionally and financially.

We offer you an exciting and versatile environment with modern employment conditions, a varied work in a dynamic team and an interesting internal medicine. Furthermore, we promote the further development of internistic skills (e.g. training and the possibility of obtaining the qualification certificate POCUS Basic Emergency Nonography through tutor qualification of CAe) and training / further education of your leadership skills.

In addition to participation in the consultancy service and pre-operative investigations, there is the possibility of participation in specialised visits with other disciplines (e.g. neurology/cardiovascular visit), as well as the possibility of participation in scientific research projects.

For additional information, Dr. med Gregor Herfs, Chief Physician, is available at gregor.herfs@hirslanden.ch.

We look forward to your application

PD Dr.med. Martina Kleber, Chief of General Internal Medicine
