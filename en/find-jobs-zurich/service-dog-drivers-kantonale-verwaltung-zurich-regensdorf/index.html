Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Prison and Reintegration Prison Pöschwies Service Dog Guide 80 - 100% With around 1,400 employees, Prison and Reintegration (JuWe) is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as for the execution of various types of detention. Our employees from around 40 professions support and promote the reintegration of delinquents and work effectively and progressively for a safe and fair society. The Pöschwies Prison (JVA) is one of eight main departments of JuWe. With around 400 detention places, it is the largest closed prison in Switzerland for men who have committed crimes. It fulfils a central state task and contributes to the protection of the general public in accordance with the legal mandate and at the same time creates the conditions for the rehabilitation of detained persons. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed, responsible and resilient personality who, together with her service dog, makes an important contribution to safety within the JVA Pöschwies.
