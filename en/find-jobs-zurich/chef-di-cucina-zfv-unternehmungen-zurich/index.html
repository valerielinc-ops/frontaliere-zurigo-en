In mid-2027, UBS will open a new culinary flagship in the heart of Zurich. At Paradeplatz, an exclusive meeting point for customers and the top management of UBS is created. The combination of exclusive private dining, high-quality bistro services and individual event formats creates a place for special encounters, business events and first-class hospitality in the heart of Zurich. Are you our new team member who chefs us: in per 1. March 2027 or by arrangement from Monday to Friday with working hours between 07:00 and 22:00? The new UBS gastronomy at the Zurich Paradeplatz is an exclusive concept for customers and the management of UBS. This is where sophisticated cuisine, discreet service and international hospitality standards meet. As a chef, you take responsibility for the à-la-carte and private dining offer and design with your culinary vision a modern fine dining cuisine that combines regionality, seasonality and highest quality in an unmistakable way.
