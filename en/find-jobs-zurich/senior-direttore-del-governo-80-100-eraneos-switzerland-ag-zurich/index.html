Full description

Eraneos is an international consulting group for strategy, transformation and technology that helps companies succeed in a dynamic digital world. With top-class experts from business and technology, we empower our customers to set new standards for sustainable transformations – from strategy to implementation.

Whether it is in the design of sustainable organisations, the development of new potentials through data and AI or the securing of companies through holistic cybersecurity: We deliver results pragmatically and purposefully. Our team of around 1,200 dedicated employees is represented in offices in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, Denmark, Sweden, Austria, Great Britain, Singapore and the USA and combines global perspectives with strong local roots. Around 450 employees work at the Swiss locations Zurich, Bern and Lausanne.
