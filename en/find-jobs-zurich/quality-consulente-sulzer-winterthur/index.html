Full description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

Quality Advisor role driving audits, compliance, and continuous improvement across operations. Partner with leaders, strengthen IMS, and influence a strong quality culture at Sulzer. At Sulzer, we don’t just adapt to change, we drive it. With nearly 200 years of innovation and a global footprint spanning 180 facilities, we deliver world-class engineering solutions that keep critical industries moving.

At Sulzer Australia, our teams support essential rotating equipment across power generation, petrochemicals, mining, and heavy manufacturing. Quality isn’t a function here, it’s fundamental to how we operate.

We’re looking for a Quality Advisor who thrives on improving systems, influencing culture, and partnering with operational leaders to lift performance across the business. This role can be based at our Maitland, Cardiff or Brisbane locations.

If you enjoy being in the detail, driving continuous improvement, and making a tangible impact, then this role is for you.

 The Role This is a hands-on quality role with real influence. You’ll partner closely with operational teams to strengthen compliance, enhance processes, and embed a culture of accountability and continuous improvement. From leading audits to coaching leaders and investigating non-conformances, you’ll play a critical role in ensuring our Integrated Management System (IMS) remains effective and aligned with certification requirements.

⚙️ What You’ll Do

  • Lead internal audit programs and support external certification audits
  • Drive continuous improvement initiatives that enhance operational performance
  • Investigate non-conformances, perform root cause analysis, and implement corrective actions
  • Support the ongoing implementation and governance of IMS
  • Partner with supervisors and leaders to strengthen quality culture
  • Develop and coordinate quality compliance plans and training initiatives
  • Ensure documentation and processes align with ISO standards
  • Provide practical, value-adding advice to operational teams

✅ About You You’re pragmatic, detail-oriented, and confident influencing stakeholders at all levels. You don’t just identify problems, you solve them.

You bring

  • Experience in manufacturing, mechanical, or electrical environments
  • Strong working knowledge of quality systems and certification standards
  • The confidence to engage leaders and challenge the status quo
  • Excellent time management and communication skills
  • A proactive mindset with a passion for continuous improvement

 Qualifications & Experience

  • Lead Auditor qualification (ISO 9001 highly regarded)
  • Trade or engineering qualification strongly preferred (electrical is advantageous)
  • Experience implementing quality or continuous improvement systems
  • Highly desirable: experience in Ex certification (IECEx 60079.19 and AS/NZS 3800) and strong understanding of AC/DC electric motors.
