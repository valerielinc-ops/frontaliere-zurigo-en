Full description

Location: Clinic Beau-Site

Occupation by: Immediately or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 70840

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

Reinforce our team as Dipl. Care specialist HF/FH for the KardUnit (weekly clinic) and design the cardiological care of tomorrow with us. We look forward to your application!

You work from Monday to Saturday. You also have the option of fixed working days.

Your advantages with us

  • Attractive weekly station with a maximum of one Saturday assignment per month in day service
  • Sundays and public holidays are guaranteed free
  • further development in the ECG area without IMC training
  • After the probationary period visit of an ECG basic course
  • Family team with appreciative cooperation
  • Little night watches thanks to existing permanent night watch

We offer you very good employment conditions, five weeks holidays, internal and external training opportunities and other benefits such as:

  • Staff restaurant with good conditions
  • Possibility to obtain up to two additional weeks of holiday with the leisure plus model
  • Very good social benefits in the pension fund as well as accident and sickness allowance insurance
  • As compensation for the changing time, you will receive four additional paid free days for a 100% fee (for a smaller fee, the free days are reduced accordingly)

All further information on employment conditions and benefits can be found under the following link: Team diagnosis - What we lack is you!

YOUR TASKS

  • Ensuring the quality of care for inpatient and outpatient cardiology and angiology patients in a dynamic and collegial team
  • Preparation and follow-up of patients before and after invasive procedures such as dilatations, stent implantations, pacemaker implantations or electrophysiological examinations in the cardiac catheter laboratory and in the angiosuite, including monitoring as needed
  • Close cooperation with the nursing team of the cardiac catheter laboratory and the Angiosuite as well as with the medical doctors
  • 3 shift operations with focus on day and late service

Your profile

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Medical professional HF/FH/DNII or AKP
  • Professional experience in acute care, ideally in cardiology, or interest and motivation to develop in this exciting field
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary cooperation and actively shaping a positive team culture

For further information, Ulf Schreiber, Head of Intervention Rooms Heart & Vascular Medicine, under T or Cindy Auf der Maur, HR recruitment partner, is at your disposal at cindy.aufdermaur@hirslanden.ch. We look forward to your contact.

Please note that we cannot consider paper dossiers (not returned) or applications by e-mail. Therefore, please apply exclusively online via this tool.
