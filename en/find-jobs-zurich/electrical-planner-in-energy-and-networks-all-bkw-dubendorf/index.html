Full description

Join our inspired team at our innovative engineering company and become part of the future of living spaces. Benefit from our experience, skills and many employee benefits.

Your tasks

  • Contribute to our success by designing principle schemes and asset overviews
  • Passionately you manage electrical planning mandates for exciting construction projects
  • You are responsible for creating the execution documents, installation plans, detailed concepts and cost estimates
  • Your task also includes the preparation and implementation of submissions and tenders
  • Your colleagues and you benefit from the partnership support of the team in all tasks

Your strengths

  • You have successfully completed training as an electrical planner / in EFZ
  • Your previous professional experience as an electrical planner enables you to already have sound knowledge in this area
  • High language competence in German, both oral and written
  • Of particular note is your curiosity and enthusiasm for working on projects using modern industry-related IT programs as well as BIM technology.
  • The confident use of CAD systems is not only a skill for you, but a source of joy that you are happy to bring to your work.
