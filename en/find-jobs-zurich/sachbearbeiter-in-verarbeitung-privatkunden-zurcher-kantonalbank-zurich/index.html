We are a well-established team of 14 dedicated specialists who work with passion and high professional competence on the concerns of our private customers. Your contribution goes beyond efficient order processing, because with every contact we create a first-class customer experience – internally and externally. You like to drive forward topics such as efficiency and progress and want to actively contribute to further developing our processes and stay on the pulse of time.

Your tasks

  • Responsibility for customer, professional and timely processing of business cases in connection with openings, balances and cards of natural persons
  • Processing of fraud and damage cases in connection with debit cards and further development of experts in this field
  • Ensuring exception handling, including whitelist management, partner matching and name matching
  • Acting as a specialist: in the core business and supporting the private customer consultants of the entire bank
  • Identification of optimization potential in our processes and active promotion of corresponding improvements
  • Completed banking (EFZ) or equivalent training in the financial sector
  • Several years of experience in the banking or financial sector, ideally focusing on customer support and processing activities
  • Flair in dealing with different IT applications as well as good MS Office user knowledge, in particular Excel
  • You convince with strong oral and written communication skills in contact with our customers as well as with colleagues
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Every other language is a plus.
  • Mutual support and team spirit are important to us
  • Various opportunities to develop personally and professionally
  • We offer you space for your ideas and thus design the bank of tomorrow
  • Modern workplace in district 5 with very good transport connections (hard bridge station)
  • Comprehensive package, which in addition to the attractive basic salary and a variable remuneration offers many benefits and perks – from A such as retirement provision to Z such as ZVV bonus pass
