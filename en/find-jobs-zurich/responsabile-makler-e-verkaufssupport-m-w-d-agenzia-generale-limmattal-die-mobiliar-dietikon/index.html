Full description

This is what awaits you

You like to take on responsibility, lead with commitment and enjoy developing people? Then we look forward to meeting you. For our general agency Limmattal, we are looking for an entrepreneurial, initiative and goal-oriented personality as head of brokerage and sales support.

This is what you do with us

You lead, promote and develop the brokerage and sales support team, ensure efficient operational processes and are responsible for the corresponding controlling. In addition, you maintain an exchange with brokers, negotiate with them on an equal footing and build sustainable business relationships.

As a member of the management team, you actively participate in the strategic and operational orientation of the general agency and contribute your ideas.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training in the insurance sector and degree as an insurance intermediary/in VBV, profile Allbranche (according to the Insurance Supervision Act prerequisite for this function)
  • Eidg. Professional ID as an insurance specialist, dipl. Insurer HF or equivalent training
  • Good business knowledge
  • Competent appearance and winning personality
  • Enjoyment of cooperation and leadership of employees
  • Initiative, resilience and high reliability
  • Strong communication, negotiation and enforcement

Working with us

You can expect a good working atmosphere in a young and motivated team, a responsible and varied management task and the opportunity to actively shape the development of our general agency. We also offer you attractive employment conditions.

Job starts on 1 January 2027 or by agreement.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
