More about this search for cross-border applicants

Gardener jobs in Ticino and Italian-speaking Switzerland include maintenance of private gardens, public parks, residential developments, nurseries, hotels and landscaping projects. The current search groups 325 listings, ranging from hands-on garden maintenance to more specialized work in planting, pruning, irrigation and landscape construction. Employers may seek qualified gardeners, experienced grounds workers, seasonal staff or candidates able to operate basic machinery. Opportunities are concentrated around urban and lakeside areas such as Lugano, Bellinzona, Locarno and Mendrisio, while rural districts also offer nursery and estate work. Italian is usually important in Ticino; practical experience, reliability and a driving licence can improve your prospects.
