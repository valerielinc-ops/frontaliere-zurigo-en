Full description

For the start of training in August 2027, the GZO Spital Wetzikon offers 6 apprenticeships for the profession of specialist health. In your work as a professional health, the focus is on the person. You like to work in a team, are communicative and flexible, because in their profession no day is like the other. You look after, care for and accompany people of all ages and take on nursing, domestic and administrative activities. Requirements Prerequisite for this education are a minimum age of 15 years, the completion of secondary school (level A / B), mental and physical health, sociability, a good comprehension, team and communication skills, practical understanding and manual skill. Application The following application documents must be submitted:

  • Tabular CV with photo
  • Manual justification of career choice and motivation (1 A4 page)
  • School certificates from 1st secondary school
  • Evaluations of trial internships
  • Multicheck Health and Social Services (www.gateway.one) The selection procedure shall take place in September 2026. We will contact you from September 1, 2026. Further information is provided by Carolina Steiner, Education Manager, by telephone 044 934 23 59. We look forward to seeing you!
