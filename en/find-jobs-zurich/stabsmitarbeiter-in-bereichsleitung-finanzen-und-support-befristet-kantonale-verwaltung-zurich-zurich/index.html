Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Internal Affairs Department of Integration Staff member:in Head of Finance and Support (temporary) 70% The Department of Integration is responsible for coordinating and strategic development of integration promotion in the canton of Zurich. We are the cantonal competence center for questions on integration and social diversity. To strengthen our department, we are looking for a service-oriented and committed person for a newly created position as staff member/in departmental manager Finance and support (temporary, 70%) as soon as possible or by agreement, with a two-year extension option. Your challenge will be to take on a key role in supporting the departmental management.
