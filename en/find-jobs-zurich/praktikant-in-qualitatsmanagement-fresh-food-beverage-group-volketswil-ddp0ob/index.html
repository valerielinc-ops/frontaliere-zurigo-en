Full description

Are you interested in gaining an exciting and varied insight into the quality assurance of a large food company? If this is possible for ideally 12 months, don’t hesitate to apply!

Mansioni

  • Processing of QM topics in various areas such as raw materials, food law, food safety, sustainability and occupational safety
  • Data maintenance in SAP and daily complaint statistics
  • Contact with various suppliers and internal specialist bodies
  • Coordinate internal requirements with existing external standards and standards
  • Participation in QM projects

Requirements

  • Student (Uni/FH/HF): Completed Matura or teaching and planning a degree in the field of food or degree as Bsc / Food Science ETH/FH
  • Great interest in issues of quality assurance and quality management
  • High reliability, independence and exact working methods
  • Good MS-Office knowledge
  • Professional experience
  • German (flowing)
