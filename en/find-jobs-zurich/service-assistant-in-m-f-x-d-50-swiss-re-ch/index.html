Full description

To support our service team, we are looking for an experienced service assistant who likes to be active in an international environment and enjoys taking on the following tasks in the areas of employee restaurant, take away and in the break zones. Your place of work is Zurich Mythenquai and the entrance would be at 1. July 2026 or by agreement

Their main tasks

  • Carry out preparatory work to ensure professional food preparation and filling during service hours.
  • Ensure a smooth and professional process at the lunch service dispensing stations and provide important information regarding products, content and allergens.
  • Independently carry out cleaning work after the lunch service and prepare the stations for the next day.
  • When used in the Take away area, ensure an attractive presentation of the offer and operate the Gastronovi checkout system including final cleaning work.
  • Support the break zone team in preparing, filling and maintaining the break zones in the various buildings in the event of absences.

Requirements for all areas of use

  • They know how to look after our discerning guests in German and English with a high degree of quality awareness.
  • They enjoy working in a team, are eager to learn, open to new tasks and are characterized by dedication and team spirit.
  • Your workload is 50% and will be completed from Monday to Friday between 10.00-15.30.
  • Occasional assignments at evening events.

Swiss Re Gastronomy – Your working environment Our gastronomic locations with the two employee restaurants and the other gastronomic outlets are located in Zurich and Adliswil and cover a wide range of services. Our hotel-operated conference centre, Swiss Re Centre for Global Dialogue, is located in Rüschlikon and enjoys international popularity with its 50 hotel rooms and 3 suites. They are active in Zurich and support the buffet team in the clubhouse, which is located in Zurich on the Mythenquai.

What do you bring with you

  • Experience serving guests at self-chosen buffet and banquets
  • Knowledge in dealing with POS systems is an advantage
  • High quality standards and strong service orientation
  • Teamplayer with pleasure in guest contact
  • Flexible in terms of operating times
  • A well-groomed appearance and good manners
  • Good oral knowledge of German (level B2); Advantage in writing
  • Good oral English skills required (level B1)
