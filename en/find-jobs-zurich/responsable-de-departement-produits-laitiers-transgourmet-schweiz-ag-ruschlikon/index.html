Full description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. We are there for our customers with a lot of commitment. In our 31 cash-and-carry stores, via field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a diverse range, attractive own brands, professional advice and innovative services. Transgourmet Schweiz AG is part of the international Transgourmet group. The company is a leader in Swiss delivery and collection wholesale and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retailers). Tasks

  • You manage the department operationally according to business principles and implement the defined corporate and market performance processes
  • You promote and develop employees, including the implementation of employee and holiday planning as well as horizontal promotion (TOJ)
  • You ensure compliance with internal and statutory QS and ASIB guidelines as well as the implementation of internal regulations and instructions
  • You maintain the flow of information and communication within the department, the market and MCM
  • You build a network of relationships with our customers
  • You are responsible for the daily sales readiness and presentation of goods, the implementation of sales activities in accordance with annual sales planning and ensuring the availability of goods.
  • Requirements
  • You already have management experience and have several years of experience in a comparable function
  • You enjoy active customer contact
  • You are well versed in the MS Office range
  • You are enthusiastic about new challenges
  • You speak perfect German
  • It is an advantage if you already have forklift and/or drawbar equipment training (SUVA standard) Your contact person Nicola Schaub Human Resources
