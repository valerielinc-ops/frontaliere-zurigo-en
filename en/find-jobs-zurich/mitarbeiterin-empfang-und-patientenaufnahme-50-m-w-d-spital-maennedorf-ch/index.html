Full description

Spital Männedorf – Your very special place of work. Whether a small “thank you” or a morning “grüezi” – we focus on real appreciation and humanity. The proximity in our house makes everyday life uncomplicated and enables interprofessional cooperation at the highest level. In an environment where support goes without saying, your commitment is appreciated and encouraged.

The nature of your activity

  • Professional reception and information hub for patients, relatives, hospital visitors, doctors and staff
  • Administrative admission of outpatients and inpatients
  • Accompaniment of inpatients to the bed station
  • Clarification of insurance covers and obtaining the cost guarantees with the insurance companies (including any claim for deposit payments)
  • Maintenance of administrative data of patients
  • Application of social security bases (KVG, UVG, IVG, MVG, etc.)
  • Administrative tasks in the front and back office such as providing telephone and personal information, processing internal and external incoming and outgoing mail, etc.
  • Cross-divisional cooperation with various internal employees
  • Care of the reception on weekends and public holidays

The basis of your performance

  • Basic commercial training
  • At least three years of professional experience in the healthcare sector, combined with good knowledge of the statutory social security bases
  • Precise and responsible working methods as well as stylish German in word and writing
  • Initiative, resilient personality with rapid comprehension and cross-cutting thinking and action
  • Pronounced service orientation with very good manners and a well-kept appearance
  • Teamwork, good communication skills and enjoyment of work

The reasons for your decision

We offer modern working conditions, promote internal further education and focus on professional exchange across all disciplines. The quiet location directly on the lake and the proximity to the vibrant city of Zurich round off your workplace. In addition, you can look forward to very special advantages.
