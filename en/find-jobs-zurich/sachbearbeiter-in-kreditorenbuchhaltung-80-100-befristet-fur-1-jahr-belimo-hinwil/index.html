Original description

As a clerk accounting payable During a temporary employment until the end of August 2027, you can ensure the professional and timely handling of our creditor processes. You manage the entire digital invoice workflow, ensure high data quality and act as an important contact for internal departments and external suppliers. With your structured working method, your quality awareness and your service orientation You make a significant contribution to a smoothly functioning financial area.

What will you do

  • Independent support of the entire digital creditor process from invoicing to auditing and accounting to booking.
  • Ensuring the timely and correct processing of supplier invoices, credit notes and expense reports.
  • Implementation and monitoring of payment flows in compliance with internal clearance and control processes.
  • Reconciliation of accounts payable, processing of outstanding items and settlement of differences with internal and external contacts.
  • Maintenance of supplier master data as well as close cooperation with purchasing, finance, HR and other specialist areas.

Who you are

  • Basic commercial training, ideally supplemented by further training in the field of accounting or accounting.
  • Several years of experience in financial accounting, preferably with a focus on accounts payable in an international environment.
  • Good SAP knowledge and secure handling of MS Office, especially Excel.
  • Structured, precise and independent working methods combined with a high sense of quality and responsibility.
  • Team-oriented personality with a strong service orientation as well as good knowledge of German and English.
