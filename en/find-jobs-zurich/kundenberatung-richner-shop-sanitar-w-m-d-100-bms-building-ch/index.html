Full description

BMS Building Materials Suisse is a wholesaler of building materials for shell and interior construction. Our group of companies includes the renowned specialists Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced experts advise, support and inspire customers from the construction industry as well as private customers at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. Close to everyday construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Quick, easy and competent.

Ready for a sparkling career in the sanitary sector?

Become part of our team in our Richner shop in Zurich at Staffelstrasse 12! This is all about water, design and comfort. As part of our enthusiastic team, you will help our customers find the perfect sanitary products for their needs. With your enthusiasm for innovative solutions and your technical understanding, you are the key to satisfied customers by agreement.

Become part of an inspiring environment where teamwork and customer focus come first.

Apply now and bring your passion for sanitary products to our team.

Task area

  • Technical and sales advice to customers
  • Preparation and post-processing of offers, collection, delivery and returns notes
  • Entry and exit handling and control of delivery notes
  • Picking and provision of our products
  • Direct contact person for our pick-up customers
