Full description

For Strategic Controlling in the Department of Finance, we are looking for a committed personality from now on or by agreement.

Tasks

  • In this exciting function within Strategic Controlling, you are a member of the planning and control team.
  • The core task of this department is the financial management of the business units as well as ensuring cost and earnings transparency and the development of measures to improve the financial situation.
  • You support the operational managers in the medical areas as a sparring partner. Furthermore, you advise the clinics in strategic development. In addition, you work closely with medical controlling and company accounting.
  • You create meaningful analyses and economic calculations and work on the annual budgeting.
  • You take an active role in the further development of the controlling instruments and reporting tools.
  • You work with and/or lead projects on business and interdisciplinary topics (introduction center billing, ambulatory tariffs, etc.).

Profile

  • Graduate degree in business administration or equivalent education
  • Several years of professional experience in controlling
  • Analytical and conceptual thinking
  • Ability to understand and communicate complex relationships
  • Target and result orientation
  • Initiative Personality with assertiveness and high personal responsibility
  • You are used to step-by-step communication and see yourself as a team player
  • High IT affinity and experience in dealing with SAP and BI systems round off your profile
  • Healthcare experience and industry-specific knowledge of REKOLE data and value streams are beneficial

We offer

We offer you more than just a job! Our city hospital world is characterized by an open du-culture, an uncomplicated, personal togetherness and the necessary touch of humor in everyday life. As a team of over 120 professional groups, we work every day in very different ways for the well-being of our patients. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions, flexible working time models including home office and excellent social benefits in the city of Zurich.

About us

With over 36'000 inpatients and around 455,000 outpatients per year, the Stadtspital Zürich ranks among the top 10 Swiss hospitals. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,500 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
