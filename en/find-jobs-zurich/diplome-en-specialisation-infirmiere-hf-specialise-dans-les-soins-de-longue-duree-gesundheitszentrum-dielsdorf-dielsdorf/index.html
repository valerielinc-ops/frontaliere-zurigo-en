Every year, many people take the leap into professional life with us as chefs, kitchen employees EBA, health specialists (FaGe), health and social assistants (AGS), hotel housekeeping specialists EFZ and EBA, as well as business people. Or complete a nursing degree. The Dielsdorf Health Center is a modern geriatric competence center with around 500 employees from 35 nations. Our offering ranges from advice for older people and their relatives to general and specialized long-term care through to care and support at home. With over 200 beds, the inpatient offering of general long-term care at the Dielsdorf location is supplemented by specialized treatment paths for rehabilitative acute and transitional care, palliative care, psychiatric geriatrics and a focus on dementia.
