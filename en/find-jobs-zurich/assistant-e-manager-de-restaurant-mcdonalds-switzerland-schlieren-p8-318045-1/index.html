Job Description

As an Assistant Restaurant Manager, you support the Manager in managing the team and ensuring the smooth running of operations. You take on additional responsibilities to ensure quality, food safety, service and hygiene, and you contribute to the success of the restaurant. You coach and motivate team members, coordinate priorities and promote a positive and respectful atmosphere.

Main responsibilities

  • Lead the team with empathy, respect and patience
  • Communicate openly and manage conflicts constructively
  • Define strategic priorities and coordinate daily operations
  • Coach and support Shift Leaders and Crews for optimal performance
  • Guarantee standards of food safety, quality, service, cleanliness and hygiene
  • Use management tools for planning, cash register, orders and inventory
  • Analyze KPIs and support restaurant development initiatives
  • Participate in recruitment, onboarding, training and staff engagement
  • Manage complaints and create ‘feel-good’ moments for guests
  • Maintain equipment and resolve problems

Required Qualifications

  • Experience in team management or catering preferred
  • Excellent communication and conflict management skills
  • Analytical mind, organized and comfortable with technology
  • Proactive, solution-oriented and eager to learn
  • Reliable, trustworthy and able to work under pressure
  • Good command of the local language (oral and written)

Benefits

  • Multicultural and supportive team
  • Training and development opportunities – many Restaurant Managers started as Assistants!
  • Remuneration and conditions of employment in accordance with the provisions of the CCNT
  • 50% on our products in restaurants in Switzerland
  • Discounted Sunrise subscriptions
  • Access to the Corporate Benefits platform
  • Additional holidays offered to over 50s
