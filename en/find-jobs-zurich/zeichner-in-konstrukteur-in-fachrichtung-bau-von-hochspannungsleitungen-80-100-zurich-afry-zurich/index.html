Full description

Become part of our Supply & Grids team. We lead, develop, design and plan national infrastructure and energy projects. By incorporating environmental issues, we implement our projects in an environmentally friendly and sustainable way.

Take on responsibility with us as

DRAUGHTSMAN / DESIGNER SPECIALIZING IN CONSTRUCTION OF HIGH-VOLTAGE LINES 80-100% - ZURICH (high-voltage, overhead and cable lines)

YOUR TASKS

  • Creation of 2D plans for overhead line and cable construction with AutoCAD
  • Drawing foundation, formwork and reinforcement plans for concrete and steel pylons
  • Creation of 3D models for overhead line and cable construction with REVIT and Civil 3D possible if interested and suitable
  • Technical and administrative support for the project management in the planning of high-voltage lines (overhead lines and cables)
  • Close cooperation with project management and interdisciplinary teams
  • If you are interested, it is possible to work independently on small to medium-sized projects, including construction management tasks

YOUR OPPORTUNITIES

  • Mutual support, knowledge transfer and cooperation are important cornerstones of our way of working
  • Diverse team with different professional backgrounds from different disciplines
  • Individual support and encouragement: whether through targeted training or opportunities for professional development
  • Flexible working models with the possibility of working from home as well as a modern infrastructure in a central location
  • Participation in major infrastructure projects for a secure energy supply

YOUR PROFILE

  • Completed training as a draughtsman or design engineer specializing in civil engineering
  • A few years of professional experience in a comparable environment is an advantage
  • Apprenticeship graduates with motivation and willingness to learn are encouraged to apply
  • Very good knowledge of AutoCAD, knowledge of REVIT and/or Civil 3D is an advantage
  • Experience in dealing with BIM (Building Information Modelling) is an advantage
  • Solid written and spoken German language skills (min. C1)
  • Foreign language skills (French, Italian and English) are an advantage
  • High quality and responsibility awareness
  • Independent, team-oriented and communicative personality
  • Enjoyment of working and working together in a collegial team

Become part of the AFRY success story! We offer creative freedom and personal responsibility, a collegial and international working atmosphere, modern employment conditions, flexible working models, above-average social benefits, attractive development opportunities and generous participation in the public transport subscription as well as a Half-Fare travelcard so that you can easily reach our modern office buildings in central locations. We look forward to receiving your online application!
