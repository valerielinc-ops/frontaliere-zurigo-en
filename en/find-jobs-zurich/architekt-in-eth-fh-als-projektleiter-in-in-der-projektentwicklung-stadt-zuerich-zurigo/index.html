Full description

For project development, we are looking for a new team member immediately or by agreement.

Tasks

  • They see building for the city of Zurich as an opportunity to promote public building culture and develop it in a sustainable way.
  • By means of strategic planning and feasibility studies, you develop viable and promising foundations for future construction tasks.
  • They organise fair and transparent planning and competition procedures in order to find the best possible projects and partners.
  • They are responsible for the client-side project management of smaller public construction tasks in the planning and execution phase.

Profile

  • Experience and competence in project development
  • Secure architectural judgment and competence in constructive solutions to complex issues
  • High service orientation and entrepreneurial thinking
  • Goal-oriented and independent working method, experience with BIM is advantageous
  • Power of persuasion, initiative, teamwork and communication

We offer

It offers you a challenging and responsible task in a professional, future-oriented environment and a motivated team. We offer attractive employment conditions as well as development and further education opportunities and a job in the center of Zurich. Flexible working hours and a generous home office regulation enable a good work-life balance.

About us

Public buildings give people space for encounter and exchange, for development and future. They shape the cityscape and are a valuable cultural asset. In the role of the client representative, the Office for Building Constructions develops and realizes high-quality buildings for a liveable and urban Zurich on the way to net zero.
