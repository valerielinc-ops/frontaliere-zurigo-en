Original description

The Center Management Glatt takes care of the most successful shopping center in Switzerland with great passion and on behalf of the owner. With a small, powerful team, we ensure all services in the areas of center management and marketing as well as technical, infrastructural and commercial building management around this property. Our customers are the focus with the goal of raising more than 9 million euros. to offer annual visitors a relaxed, varied and unique shopping experience.

Tasks

  • You ensure the professional operation of the house control center in shift service and act as central contact for authorities as well as internal and external stakeholders.
  • You support the management of the building control centre (HLZ) in operational security tasks and take over their deputy in case of absences
  • You process buses, advertisements and complaints and ensure correct data maintenance in the relevant systems
  • You are responsible for access and closing management, including the configuration of access media and the maintenance of hardware, logbooks and master data
  • You coordinate operational crime management in the event of thefts, vehicle incidents and police requests as well as securing CCTV data
  • You are actively involved in projects and develop concepts, processes and operational basics
  • You create, maintain and optimize operating manuals, specifications and other security-relevant documentation

Requirements

  • You have a completed vocational apprenticeship as well as a well-founded education or further education in the field of safety and security
  • You bring experience or a high affinity in project management and work structured and solution-oriented
  • You communicate stylishly in German and convince through your safe appearance and pleasant manners
  • You have a high affinity for security, data protection and compliance and have an impeccable reputation
  • You are a resilient, flexible and responsible personality with strong conflict management skills
  • You work independently, accurately and customer-oriented and keep an overview even in demanding situations
  • You bring a pronounced technical affinity, good MS-Office knowledge and the willingness to carry out irregular work assignments in shift operations

We offer

  • Modern workplace in the Glatt shopping centre, with very good connections to public transport
  • Free parking
  • Access to corporate benefits platforms with various discounts and perks of well-known providers in the areas of travel, leisure, fashion, living and more
  • Financial and/or time support for external specialist training
  • Bonus depending on company success and personal performance
  • Premiums for medical day allowance and accident insurance are 100% covered by Livit
  • Obtaining an SBB voucher or benefit on the ZVV BonusPass

We love real estate and are enthusiastic about preserving the value of our clients’ properties.

When you work at Livit, you belong to the Livit Family. You are appreciated for what you are and what you do. Because not only your skills and your experience, but also you as a person are important to us. In our working environment you feel comfortable and can develop. On the one hand, this is due to our multifaceted job profiles and the development opportunities within our company. On the other hand, there is also the distinctive corporate culture that is unparalleled in the real estate world.
