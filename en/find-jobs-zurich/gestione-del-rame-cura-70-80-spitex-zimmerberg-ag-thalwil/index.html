Do you like to take responsibility, inspire your team and actively shape the care? Then bring your passion for care and team leadership to us. We are looking for you, immediately or by agreement as co-team management care at the Thalwil location. Co-team management Care 70%-80 %

  • In addition to your management task, you also jump into customer assignments yourself if necessary and bring your specialist and social skills into direct contact with our customers:
