Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Occupation by: Immediately or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 73043

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

YOUR TASKS

  • Serve in all operations including open interventions in hybrid surgery mainly cardiovascular interventions (coronary angiographies, TAVI, MitraClip, PM, ICD, EVAR, stents) and further invasive radiological and neuroradiological interventions
  • Preparation of the various interventions and the necessary instruments
  • Ensuring all technical conditions of equipment and machinery
  • Patient monitoring before, during and after the intervention
  • Registration of hemodynamic patient data and logging of hybrid interventions

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a Dipl. Nursing professional /-mann HF / Dipl. Exp. intensive care NDS or Dipl. Specialist /-man operation technology HF
  • SRC recognition in foreign diplomas
  • You have already been able to gain some years of professional experience in the acute hospital, ideally in the cardiac catheter laboratory or hybrid surgery (no requirement)
  • You have an affinity for medical technology
  • With a high physical and psychological resilience you can handle well
  • You are distinguished by your communicative nature and your openness to new things

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, a cool team awaits you with open arms, so that you quickly feel "at home" and like to show you a lot. We offer you individual and tailor-made training as well as flexible working time models that enable you to combine work and family. Re-entrants are also welcome.
