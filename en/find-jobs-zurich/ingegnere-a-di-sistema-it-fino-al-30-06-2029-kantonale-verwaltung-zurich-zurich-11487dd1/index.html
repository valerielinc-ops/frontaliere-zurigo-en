Full description

The Cantonal Police of Zurich and its approximately 4,000 employees assume responsibility for security in the canton. In order to strengthen our IT department in Zurich, we are looking for a committed and competent personality in the area of data centers and operational control systems as: IT System Engineer/in (temporary until 30.06.2029) 80 - 100% Your responsibilities:

  • Operation and further development of highly available ICT systems such as data center basic systems, mission control system, image system or even voice system in the mission-critical area
  • Identify, consolidate and translate user requirements into feasible technical concepts
  • Implementation of projects (planning, engineering, implementation)
  • Steering and coordinating cooperation with our partners and service providers, both in day-to-day operations and in projects
  • Maintenance and further development of technical documentation Your profile:
  • Completed degree in computer science (FH) or equivalent education
  • Good knowledge of client/server systems, secure handling of associated administration technologies (e.g. OS, virtualization software, monitoring, backup, IAM, etc.)
  • Experience in the cloud environment
  • Skills in Infrastructure as Code (Terraform/Ansible)
  • Containers and Kubernetes are not foreign words for you
  • Stylistic German and good English skills
  • Independence, teamwork and confidentiality
  • Readiness for Pikett service Our offer:
  • We offer you a versatile and meaningful activity in a dynamic environment
  • With employment at the Canton of Zurich, you enjoy attractive employment conditions at a secure employer
  • A centrally located, state-of-the-art workstation in the Zurich Police and Justice Centre including staff restaurant and access to the in-house fitness centre
