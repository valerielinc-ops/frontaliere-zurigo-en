Full description

Are you interested in security? Would you like to support PostBus in carrying out ticket checks of various public transport companies, mainly in the Canton of Zurich (Zurich Transport Association)? Then apply now, because with you every passenger travels with a valid ticket.

You can do that.

  • Together with your teammate, you are responsible for the professional implementation of ticket checks and the maintenance of security on many transport companies in the Zurich Transport Association.
  • In the event of disruptive actions, disputes or acts of violence during ticket checks, you will de-escalate and intervene if necessary.
  • You carry out a wide range of security tasks as part of ticket checks and provide security and order with your presence.
  • Your competent and friendly advice to our passengers in public transport vehicles will be asked.
  • You contribute to a significant contribution to the income security and security of public transport.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed a vocational apprenticeship and have the driver’s license Cat. B or are ready to graduate.
  • You act independently, have assertiveness and are psychologically and physically resilient.
  • Your experience in security makes your daily work easier.
  • The advantages of irregular working hours incl. You appreciate Sundays and public holidays as well as at night and you are flexible.
  • As a good communication personality, you can argue skillfully in conversation. You also have a strong team orientation.
  • Your very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of French and/or Italian will help you to carry out this activity optimally. Any other foreign language is beneficial.
