Nursing specialist HF/FH with focus on wound expert:in (60–100%) | Spitex As a qualified nurse with a focus on wound management, you combine professional outpatient care with in-depth expertise in wound care. You look after clients with acute, chronic and complex wounds, advise participants professionally and support your colleagues in demanding wound situations. You work independently and at the same time closely with our nursing team as well as doctors, hospitals and other specialists.

Your main tasks include

  • Professional assessment, treatment and support of clients: insides with acute, chronic and complex wounds
  • Planning, implementation and evaluation of individual and evidence-based wound care
  • Advice and guidance of clients: inside and their relatives in handling wounds and supportive measures
  • Professional advice and support for our nurses and other employees in complex wound situations
  • Close interprofessional cooperation with family doctors: inside, hospitals, wound ambulatories and other specialists involved in the treatment
  • Ensuring professional and comprehensible wound and care documentation
  • Participation in the further development of standards, working instruments and processes in the area of wound management
  • Promoting knowledge transfer within the organisation as well as disseminating current professional knowledge to the nursing team
  • Active participation in quality assurance and development
  • Assumption of tasks relating to material management
  • Taking over tasks as dipl. Nursing professional HF/FH as part of ambulatory care

  • Enjoy passing on your expertise and supporting colleagues:
