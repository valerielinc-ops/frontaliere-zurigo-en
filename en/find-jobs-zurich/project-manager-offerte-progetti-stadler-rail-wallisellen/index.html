Full description

Project Manager Bids & Projects at Stadler Rail in Wallisellen. Stadler is a globally operating Swiss manufacturer of rail vehicles with its headquarters in Bussnang (Canton Thurgau). Founded in 1942, the company develops and produces intercity, regional and S-Bahn trains, trams, locomotives, cogwheel trains and employs thousands of staff in Switzerland. Stadler offers a modern working environment, attractive employment conditions and diverse development opportunities in an innovative Swiss industrial company. Stadler Rail is a leading company in the rail vehicle sector, with a global presence and a strong presence in Switzerland. The company is committed to developing and producing high-quality trains that meet customer needs and contribute to sustainable mobility. If you are a motivated and results-oriented professional with experience in the rail vehicle sector and good knowledge of design and development technologies, you are the ideal candidate for this role.
