The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.

Tasks

  • Ensure a smooth administrative process for our patients, as well as for all internal and external interfaces
  • Written and telephone contact with patients: inside, relatives, doctors, insurance companies and hospitals
  • Sending medical reports and monitoring the reporting latencies of ambulatory and inpatient patients

  • Deputy Complaint Management and Reciprocal Holiday Representation in the Team

  • General secretarial and back office tasks incl. Reporting sender

Requirements

  • Commercial training with several years of experience in health care or training as medical secretary:in / Medical:r Practice assistant:in with professional experience in administration
  • In-depth knowledge of PC users (Microsoft Office, electronic medical history KISIM)
  • Back office experience or experience in patient disposition/reception
  • stylish knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Good English skills as well as other languages are beneficial
  • Strong quality and service awareness
  • Reliable, service-oriented and socially competent personality with structured and independent work organization

An exciting and versatile area of responsibility awaits you, in which you can contribute and expand your knowledge and skills. We offer you the opportunity to participate together with a motivated and committed team in the design and further development of the outpatient administration.
