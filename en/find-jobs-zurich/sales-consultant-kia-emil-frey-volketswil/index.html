Full description

What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family company with 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessories as well as the provision of financial services. Emil Frey AG Volketswil is the official sales and service partner of the Mercedes Benz, Kia, Opel, Mazda and Suzuki brands and also has a car body and spare parts warehouse. Their tasks

  • Success-oriented sales: You take responsibility for the sale of new cars and occupations of the innovative KIA brand
  • Customer advice: They offer customer-oriented, comprehensive advice and ensure consistent, professional support for existing and new customers
  • All-round service: You create customized offers, exchange estimates, leasing and insurance offers
  • Relationship management: You actively maintain our existing customer base and gain new customers with enthusiasm
  • Presence at the point of sale: You ensure our accessibility and make Saturday assignments in the showroom
  • Events: You actively work with your profile at exhibitions and exciting customer events
  • Sales experience: You must have at least two to three years of successful sales experience, ideally in the automotive or service sector
  • Automobile Passion: You have a great enthusiasm for the automotive industry and burn for car sales
  • Winning personality: Customer contact is your absolute strength. They convince with a sovereign, well-kept appearance and a pronounced sales flair
  • Service mentality: A strong customer orientation, a high level of service and negotiating skills characterize you
  • Flexibility: You are resilient, willing to learn and willing to work on Saturday
  • Language competence: You communicate stylishly in German and have a good knowledge of English. Other foreign languages such as French or Italian are a big plus
  • Will to succeed: You have a real winner mindset with focus on goal achievement and a high level of initiative
  • Mobility : You are in possession of a valid driving licence of category B What we offer you You will find a new challenge with us with exciting tasks and much more:
