Full description

Variety is guaranteed with us. You will be carefully trained and introduced step by step into new tasks. Become part of our team, grow professionally and personally and bring your strengths where they really count. >The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.

Tasks

  • Routine? There are hardly any of us. Instead, you can expect exciting care situations in the eye clinic, plastic surgery and hand surgery.
  • You accompany patients after surgery, burn injuries and in all phases of life, from young to elderly.
  • Patient-oriented care is not a buzzword for us, but everyday life. For this to work, we share our knowledge, support each other and pull together as a team.

Requirements

  • Are you a qualified nurse/certified nurse or are about to graduate? Very well, we like fresh wind as much as experience.
  • You have a good eye for pre- and post-operative monitoring, enjoy dressings and keep an overview even in lively moments.
  • You work independently, structured, with responsibility and ideally bring a dose of humor and team spirit.

We offer

What makes us special? A team that holds together, strengthens each other and laughs together. We are open, learn from each other and do not take ourselves too seriously, all the more so our work. When it comes to scheduling, we are flexible and like to discuss individual working time models with a workload of 50%.
