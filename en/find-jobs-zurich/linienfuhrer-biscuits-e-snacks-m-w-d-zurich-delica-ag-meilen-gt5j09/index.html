Full description

When technology becomes enjoyment, are you in your element? Then become part of our biscuits and snacks production and help ensure that our popular biscuits and snack products are produced in the highest quality every day. Look forward to a strong team and the chance to make a difference together that we are proud of. We are one of the best employers in Switzerland!

Luogo di lavoro: Delica AG, Delica AG miles, Bruechstrasse 70, 8706 miles

Important information

Letter of motivation not necessary! Complete employment certificates and diplomas are part of this with us. We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You set up, operate and monitor one or more production facilities.
  • You fix disruptions and perform minor repairs.
  • You control the quality of the manufactured products.
  • You are responsible for the training, guidance and control of operations personnel.
  • You optimize the processes and plants as part of our Continuous Improvement Process (CIP).

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: beneficial in the food sector or industrial environment
  • Completed basic or further training: as a food technologist, milk technologist, baker, cook, etc. or a technical training (e.g. plant operator, plastics technologist, etc.)
  • German (very good knowledge): B1 language level is mandatory
  • Technical understanding and pleasure in the operation, maintenance and optimization of modern plants
  • Readiness for shift work (3-4 shifts) and corresponding mobility
  • Open-mindedness, perseverance and motivation

Cosa of Friamo

  • Mobile subscriptions: Top prices on mobile subscriptions at Galaxus Mobile, Sunrise or Wingo – starting from CHF 10.- / month
  • Professional provision: Compared to the BVG, depending on age, 7% - 18% more is paid by Delica
  • Meals: Balanced and fine lunch menu/buffet in the staff restaurant for only CHF 8.-
  • Employee offers: Discount on various services at Medbase
  • Sports and fitness offers: Free use of the fitness studios integrated at the site in Buchs, Birsfelden and Meilen as well as CHF 200.- Refund on fitness annual subscriptions
  • Migros Bank: Migros grants higher interest rates on the private and investment account for its employees

Contatto

Application and contact Esther Reipen Recruitment Business Partners

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Personal video or telephone conversation: For a large number of applicants, we optionally conduct a personal telephone or video conversation. This gives us the opportunity to get to know more candidates within a short time in order to invite suitable people for the next round. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Test work: A trial day or half day gives the opportunity to get to know the future working environment and the team members better. And vice versa, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Final discussion before issuing the contract: Before the contract is issued, we hold a final discussion in order to clarify open questions of both sides and to regulate contractual details. If all points are clarified, the written offer follows. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition: After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
