Full description

Do you like working with people? You would like to work on January 1, 2027 or by agreement with our care team for the Health Center for the age of Gehrenholz?

Tasks

  • They ensure high-quality care in accordance with current care and treatment standards and, as a day-to-day responsible person, take on the efficient delegation of tasks to employees of different levels of competence.
  • In day-to-day nursing care, they assume responsibility for complex care situations and, as caregivers, are the central contact person for relatives and third parties.
  • They support, accompany and promote learners and students from different educational fields in health and social services on their way to developing their skills.
  • You contribute your own ideas and take on joint responsibility for the implementation of the departmental goals.
  • They make an important contribution to ensuring a high quality of care and actively promote interdisciplinary cooperation.

Profile

  • Empathetic personality with a strong human orientation
  • Enthusiasm for self-responsible work, combined with team spirit and commitment
  • Completed training as a nurse (HF/FH/DNII) with SRC recognition for diplomas from abroad
  • Excellent knowledge of German in words and writing as well as excellent communication skills
  • Strong sense of responsibility, the ability to think in a network and high resilience

We offer

Look forward to an exciting area of work in a professional and team-oriented working environment with a du-culture throughout the company. The human being is at our center. That is why we support the reconciliation of private and professional life, different working models make it possible.

As part of the city of Zurich and due to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, progressive employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

Wages of care and therapeutic professions have been raised to strengthen these occupational groups. Depending on the experience, the annual salary for this function is between Fr. 85’228.– and Fr. 113’882.– (100%).

About us

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

The health center for the age of Gehrenholz in Wiedikon in district 3 combines various care and care offers under one roof and is characterized by many years of experience in age medicine. The exceptional architecture and the quiet, green location are further advantages. Here you work together with 250 colleagues.
