Full description

Become part of a passionate team producing Swiss culinary culture. With us, you design processes, promote your team and celebrate joint successes. We give you space for development – professionally and personally. Appreciation, pride in our products and growth in the Migros universe are waiting for you.

Luogo di lavoro: Delica AG, Delica AG miles, Bruechstrasse 70, 8706 miles

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You lead, coach and develop your team (20-25 employees) to top performance.
  • You control the production and delivery processes and ensure timely deliveries.
  • You continuously optimize processes (lean, kaizen) to increase efficiency and quality.
  • You ensure compliance with quality, hygiene and safety standards.
  • You coordinate interfaces to purchasing, technology and quality management.

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: in production or supply chain in the food industry / FMCG, of which experience in management roles.
  • Completed apprenticeship (EFZ): technical or commercial training (e.g. food technology, production technology, HF/FH or equivalent).
  • In-depth knowledge in production control, lean methods and basic knowledge in QM and GMP/HACCP requirements.
  • Strong leadership, communication and team support; pronounced process thinking and solution-oriented action.
  • Readiness for 3 shifts and 4 shift work if needed
  • in German (negotiation certain)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Cumulus points: 2 times Cumulus points on food and 5 times on non-food items
  • Migros Bank: Migros grants higher interest rates on the private and investment account for its employees
  • Employee offers: Discount on various services at Medbase
  • Club School Migros: Up to 70% refund per course
  • Leisure and cultural activities: Over 50% discount on subscriptions in the Schinznach thermal bath, Sole Uno or Alpamare adventure park
  • Sports and fitness offers: Free use of the fitness studios integrated at the site in Buchs, Birsfelden and Meilen as well as CHF 200.- Refund on fitness annual subscriptions
  • Professional provision: Compared to the BVG, depending on age, 7% - 18% more is paid by Delica

Contatto

Application and contact Patrick Mawete Senior Recruitment Business Partner

Recruitment process

  • Submit application documents: For a complete application, we need your CV, certificates and diplomas. A photo in the CV and a letter of motivation are not necessary. We live equal opportunities for all applicants. We have a standardized recruitment process. If this does not work for you, mention it in your application dossier.
  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Personal video or telephone conversation: For a large number of applicants, we optionally conduct a personal telephone or video conversation. This gives us the opportunity to get to know more candidates within a short time in order to invite suitable people for the next round. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Test work: A trial day or half day gives the opportunity to get to know the future working environment and the team members better. And vice versa, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
