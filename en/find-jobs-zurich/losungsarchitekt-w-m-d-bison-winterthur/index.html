Full description

As a partner in agriculture, BISON ensures that Swiss food reaches consumers. It is important that IT can be optimally adapted to the needs of the specialist areas. This is where you come in as a solution architect! Thanks to your daily work in Winterthur, Sursee or Bern, everything is coordinated with the business and optimally aligned for the future.You have the "can do" mentality and like to work in a team? You are open and communicate comprehensible and give feedback?We look forward to your application and get to know you personally in a timely manner.

You do that.

  • Design, evaluation and coordination of future-proof ICT infrastructure solutions along the interfaces PLAN, BUILD and RUN
  • Analysis of requirements, dependencies, risks, efficiency and feasibility
  • Consulting services for specialist areas, management and corporate architecture
  • Accompaniment of projects from the idea to the project application and implementation until acceptance and operational handover
  • Observation of technological developments and development of sound decision-making basis
  • Documentation of architecture and infrastructure overviews
  • Support for sustainable solutions in problem and demand management

You bring that to me.

  • Completed vocational training, higher vocational training in computer science or studies in computer science/business informatics
  • Experience in solution architecture as well as in requirements management with a focus on IT infrastructure and already worked in different companies or industries
  • In-depth knowledge of network, back-end and client infrastructure in both on-prem and cloud environments
  • Customer orientation and personal responsibility in business analysis and requirements management
  • Good analytical and communication skills and willingness to further develop the role of the solution architect in the field of IT infrastructure
  • You have stylish German and good English skills, French skills are advantageous
