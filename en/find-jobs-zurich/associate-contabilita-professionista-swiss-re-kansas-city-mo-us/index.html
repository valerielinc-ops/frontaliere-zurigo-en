Associate Accounting Professional — Swiss Re

Full description

Are you a detail-oriented finance professional who thrives in a dynamic regulatory environment? Join our team where your analytical skills will directly impact our compliance success across North America. This role offers an exceptional opportunity to develop specialized expertise in insurance financial compliance while working with a collaborative team of industry professionals. About the Role As an Associate Accounting Professional, you'll be at the forefront of ensuring our financial compliance across multiple state jurisdictions. You'll manage critical reporting requirements, develop specialized knowledge in Property and Casualty insurance compliance, and build valuable relationships with regulatory agencies. Key Responsibilities

  • Data Management : Mine system data for required state reporting, ensuring accuracy and completeness
  • Regulatory Filings : Prepare and file premium tax returns, data calls, surcharge reports, and guaranty fund payments
  • Financial Tracking : Monitor guaranty fund credits, premium tax refunds, and credits with precision
  • Reconciliation : Prepare related account reconciliations to maintain financial accuracy
  • Stakeholder Communication : Engage with State Insurance Departments and related state agencies regarding required filings
  • Cross-functional Collaboration : Maintain open communication with the financial compliance team and other Swiss Re departments

About the Team You'll be joining a specialized Financial Compliance team that works directly with state insurance departments and related regulatory agencies. Based in Kansas City, the team consists of three professionals reporting to the Hiring Manager. As part of Corporate Solutions Finance – Financial Compliance, you'll collaborate with colleagues who are dedicated to ensuring regulatory excellence across our operations.

About You

You're a methodical professional who pays close attention to detail while maintaining a big-picture understanding of compliance requirements. You take ownership of your work, meet deadlines consistently, and communicate effectively with stakeholders at all levels. Your analytical mindset allows you to identify potential issues before they become problems.

We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements

  • College graduate with BA or BS in Accounting with at least 2 years of relevant working experience
  • Knowledge of finance and accounting principles with demonstrated ability to apply them in practical settings
  • Strong analytical skills with the ability to interpret complex financial data accurately
  • Proven attention to detail and ability to review returns for reasonableness and identify issues
  • Experience with data mining and financial reporting

These are additional nice to haves

  • Experience in insurance industry, particularly in property and casualty
  • Knowledge of state insurance regulatory requirements
  • Proficiency with financial compliance software and data management tools
  • Experience communicating with regulatory agencies
  • Process improvement mindset with ability to identify efficiency opportunities
