Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 100% As of March 22, 2027, we offer a one-year internship in activation therapy at the Minervastrasse site in Zurich. Their tasks

  • Co-direction and management of activation therapy group therapies
  • Activation therapeutic individual therapies / individual accompaniment within the group therapies
  • Documentation of therapy courses in the clinic information system
  • Participation in team meetings and interprofessional reports as well as in interprofessional practice teaching

Your profile

  • Existing apprenticeship place at the ZAG - Center for Education in the Health Care System
  • Understanding and empathy in dealing with psychiatric patients in the field of gerontopsychiatry
  • Teamwork, reliability and independent working methods
  • Organizational skill and good user knowledge in MS Office

Our offer

  • Development with perspective: Diverse career paths in one of the largest psychiatric clinics in Switzerland
  • Strong in the team: appreciative cooperation and mutual support
  • Space for ideas: High personal responsibility and design possibilities
  • Focus on health: Various health-promoting offers, green oases and an in-house care team
  • Well supplied in everyday work: Discounted catering offers
