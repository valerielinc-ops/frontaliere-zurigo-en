Role overview

The Digital Marketing Manager - Campaign Specialist will be instrumental in shaping and delivering key components in EFG’s transformation of its digital ecosystem and presence across strategic digital marketing channels.

In your role you will work closely with members of the Digital and broader global marketing team to combine strategy, execution, and data analysis to ensure campaigns are client-centric, measurable, and compliant, focused on measurable demand generation and attributable to NNA growth for the broader business.

The successful candidate will bring deep channel expertise and be comfortable translating business priorities into strategic digital marketing activity that drives awareness, engagement, and measurable commercial outcomes. As EFG develops it’s digital ecosystem you will support the team to help implement connected and scalable solutions that deliver ROI across the business and support growth.

  • Lead the end-to-end strategy, planning, execution, and optimisation of global digital demand generation campaigns across web, email, paid media, and social channels.
  • Collaborate with business experts across global Marketing, Sales, Product, and Investment teams to define target audiences across multiple regions and develop campaign strategies tailored to different markets and segments.
  • Build test-and-learn frameworks (A/B testing, multivariate testing) to continuously improve performance.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Business, Communications or related field.
  • Professional marketing qualifications (e.g., CIM, Google, Analytics certifications) desirable.
  • 5+ years’ digital marketing experience, ideally within Private Banking, investment management, financial services. Alternatively a background in targeting HNWI with a luxury services.

Main responsibilities

  • Lead the end-to-end strategy, planning, execution, and optimisation of global digital demand generation campaigns across web, email, paid media, and social channels.

  • Work with skilled content creators and subject matter experts across the business to adapt investment insights and marketing collateral into engaging digital formats.
