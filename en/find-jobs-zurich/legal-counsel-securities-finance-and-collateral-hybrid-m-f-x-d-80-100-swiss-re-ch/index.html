Legal Counsel Securities Finance and Collateral (Hybrid; m/f/x/d; 80-100%) — Swiss Re

Full description

Are you a sharp, commercially minded legal professional with deep expertise in financial markets and collateral? This is your opportunity to take on a high-impact role at the heart of Swiss Re's Capital Markets Legal function — advising on complex, multi-jurisdictional matters and shaping the legal framework for one of the world's leading reinsurance groups. About the Role As Legal Counsel for Securities Finance and Collateral, you will be a trusted legal advisor embedded in a dynamic and internationally connected team. You will provide expert legal counsel to Swiss Re Group and its various Business Units, translating complex business requirements into clear legal processes and actionable guidance. This role offers the opportunity to work at the intersection of law, finance, and regulation — with real visibility at senior levels of the organisation. Key Responsibilities

  • Manage day-to-day legal matters related to collateral trading and management, including the drafting and negotiation of master agreements for Securities Financing Transactions (SFTs), triparty collateral agreements, trade confirmations, security agreements, and bespoke collateral arrangements
  • Advise on financial markets regulations , with a particular focus on the Swiss regulatory landscape, ensuring Swiss Re remains compliant with evolving legal and regulatory requirements
  • Conduct legal and regulatory risk gap assessments and provide timely, well-reasoned legal opinions on operational and legal risks
  • Draft and review legal policies and guidelines , ensuring they reflect current regulatory requirements and best practices
  • Collaborate closely with Compliance, Credit Risk, Finance, and Asset Management stakeholders to deliver integrated, commercially sound legal solutions
  • Engage with market associations such as ICMA and ISLA, representing Swiss Re's interests and staying ahead of industry developments
  • Provide periodic updates to Senior Management, Internal Audit, and relevant Committees on legal developments and risk matters
  • Coordinate with the governance team for regulatory filings and reporting obligations
  • Escalate material issues appropriately to the Head of Global Collateral and the Head of Capital Markets, Finance – Legal
  • Lead and support group-wide internal projects and contribute to broader Legal & Compliance initiatives
  • Liaise and collaborate with peers across other branches of the legal department, fostering knowledge sharing and consistency

About the Team Based in Zurich, you will report to the Head of Capital Markets Finance Legal and join a team of seven talented legal professionals. The team is truly international in character, with members based across Zurich and London, bringing a rich diversity of qualifications, backgrounds, and legal expertise. The team operates in a collaborative and intellectually stimulating environment, advising on some of the most complex and high-profile matters within Swiss Re's legal function. You will have regular interactions with the General Counsel Corporate Center & Group Functions, the Group Treasurer, the Head of Risk & Short-Term Funding, and Swiss Re Senior Management.

About You

You are a confident, intellectually curious legal professional who thrives in a fast-paced, international environment. You bring sound legal judgement that goes beyond the letter of the law — you understand the business context, communicate complex concepts with clarity, and build trusted relationships across all levels of an organisation. You are open, collaborative, and willing to share your knowledge and perspective, while remaining receptive to challenge and committed to continuous learning.

We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements

  • Law degree with honours and admission to practice , with a Swiss legal qualification required ; an additional US or UK qualification is strongly preferred
  • Minimum 8 years of significant international legal experience in securities lending, repo, and ideally derivatives businesses, gained at a renowned financial services institution and/or a top-tier law firm
  • Strong experience in complex, multi-jurisdictional legal matters , with demonstrated ability to manage competing priorities and deliver high-quality legal advice under pressure
  • Fluency in English and German (written and spoken), enabling effective communication with internal and external stakeholders across jurisdictions
  • Proven expertise in drafting and negotiating financial market agreements, including GMRA, GMSLA, CSA, and related documentation

These are additional nice to haves

  • Experience interacting with or representing organisations at market associations such as ICMA or ISLA
  • Familiarity with Swiss Re's business model or the broader reinsurance/insurance industry
  • Experience advising on regulatory filings and liaising with financial regulators
  • Demonstrated ability to lead internal legal projects and drive cross-functional initiatives
  • Strong influencing and stakeholder management skills, with experience presenting to senior leadership and governance committees
  • A proactive, solutions-oriented mindset with a measured appetite for risk and a track record of delivering workable outcomes
  • Professional references available upon request
