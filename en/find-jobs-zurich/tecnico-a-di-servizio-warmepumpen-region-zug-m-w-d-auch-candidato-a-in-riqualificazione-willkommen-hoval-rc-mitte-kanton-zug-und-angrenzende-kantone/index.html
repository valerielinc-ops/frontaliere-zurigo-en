Full description

To strengthen our service team in the Zug region, we are looking for YOU!

As a leading technology company, Hoval has been inspiring for decades with groundbreaking system solutions for heating and air conditioning technology. In order to offer our extensive services to our customers, we need motivated personalities who use their knowledge in a targeted manner. You bring technical understanding, like working independently and want to develop professionally? Then you are exactly right with us. Also motivated lateral entrants from related technical professions are welcome.

Activity

  • Commissioning of new plants
  • Corrective action on Hoval heat pumps
  • Maintenance and maintenance services
  • Customer advice on the sale of service contracts and spare parts
  • Pikettdienst, classification in consultation with the team
  • The assignments are each from the place of residence to the region

We offer you

  • Responsible and varied activities
  • Sales premiums and attractive social benefits
  • Collegial and helpful team
  • Targeted training in our Hoval products as well as ongoing training
  • Possibility of further training as a professional
  • Business vehicle as well as laptop and smartphone etc.

Your profile

  • Completed technical training, for example as a refrigerator, heating installer, electrical installer or car mechanic
  • Completed training as a specialist for thermal systems is advantageous
  • Experience in control technology and hydraulic knowledge desired
  • Independent, reliable and resilient personality
  • Good communication and sales skills
  • Enjoying a busy travel activity within the region

Whether experienced professional or motivated lateral entrant with a technical background: At Hoval, exciting challenges, a strong team and attractive development opportunities await you. We look forward to getting to know you and receiving your full online application.
