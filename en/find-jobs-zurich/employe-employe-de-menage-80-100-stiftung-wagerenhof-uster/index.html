Full description

For our Hauswirtschaft Agogik team, we are looking for you as Employee/employee housekeeping (80-100%) Our residents, our external daycare providers and our people in need of support are at the heart of our activities and work. Housekeeping has a large field of meaningful activities, both in laundry and cleaning. In both areas, we work closely with the specialist teams and can thus find suitable work for all the people entrusted to us and live the inclusion and participation daily. We look forward to you integrating an open and upstanding personality who succeeds well with an appreciative and empathetic way of guiding, integrating and promoting 43 people who are currently being cared for. The Wagerenhof Foundation (affectionately called “Wagi”) in Uster has been a permanent home for around 250 people with multiple cognitive impairments for more than 100 years and offers over 50 employees in assisted jobs. We are a recognized, non-profit institution with a social mission. With a demand-oriented offer of living and care places as well as various day and leisure offers, we promote the quality of life, participation and self-determination of our residents. The special features of the Wagerenhof include size and Dörfli character, which allows space for encounters. For employees with different careers and professionalism, the Wagerenhof Foundation is an attractive employer with a wide range of development opportunities. For learners, Wagerenhof is a competent training partner.

For this we give you the responsibility

  • Support, support and promotion of employees with disabilities
  • Need-based and resource-based support for work support
  • Maintaining resident documentation, such as history records
  • Administrative activities with knowledge of the common Office programs
  • We would like to see a trained worker/agogue. If you do not bring this education, but a lot of work experience, we are also happy
  • Basic training in housekeeping - cleaning, laundry or building maintenance
  • Delight in guiding and accompanying employees who need support in their daily work in housekeeping
  • Constructive cooperation with the specialist teams and a strong interdisciplinary understanding
  • Strong social skills and a good physical and psychological constitution
  • Open communication style and the ability to self-reflection

This makes us the place for your work

  • The appreciative and human togetherness
  • The clearly strategic and professional organization
  • Professional support through an interdisciplinary and professionally acting environment
  • Good accessibility with public transport
