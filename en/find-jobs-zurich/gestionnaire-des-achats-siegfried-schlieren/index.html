Founded in 1873 in Zofingen, Switzerland, Siegfried has grown into a global network of 13 sites across three continents. As a fully integrated CDMO, Siegfried is one of the few organizations today that can carry out the development and manufacturing of APIs and finished dosage forms under one roof. This unique combination of know-how and experience makes us the most trusted partner in the pharmaceutical industry.
