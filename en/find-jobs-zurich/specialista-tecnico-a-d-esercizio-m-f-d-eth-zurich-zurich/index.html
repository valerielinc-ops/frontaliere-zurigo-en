The Institute of Molecular Health Sciences researches the molecular basis of health and disease with the aim of translating these findings into innovative therapeutic approaches. On 1 June 2027 (or by agreement) the position of:r Technical Operations Specialist:in is to be filled.

Project background

As a result of the retirement of a long-term employee, we are looking for a:n technical:n company specialist for the support of the institute management and the research operation.

Job description

  • Ensuring the smooth operation of the institute infrastructure (e.g. cleaning and autoclaving, gas supply, alarm system)
  • Organisation, operation and maintenance of the Institute’s general equipment
  • Contact person/interface between institute (IMHS), ETH buildings (construction projects) and ETH operations (HPL buildings)
  • Support of professorships in the maintenance and repair of research and laboratory equipment
  • Support of laboratory management in technical matters incl. Biosecurity and storage of chemicals
  • Support for budget planning and management

Profile

  • For this position, you ideally bring a bachelor's degree in mechanical engineering, a technical vocational training with targeted further education or an equivalent qualification. Relevant professional experience, preferably in the higher education sector, is beneficial
  • Very good German and English skills in words and writing, a proactive and flexible working method and the ability for successful interdisciplinary cooperation are required
  • Secure in this central role at the heart of our institute You ensure smooth operation and create the basis for excellent research and teaching
