Full description

Personal – competent – close Welcome to the Hospital Uster, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As the central pillar of health care in the Oberland region of Zurich and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you experience the highest level of expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and promoted in your professional development. Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of a “regional and digital health hub in Züri East”! As a student / student of NDS HF anesthesia care, you are an important link in the entire perioperative treatment chain. With the help of your pronounced professional knowledge, your technical skills and your high level of social competence, you independently carry out anesthesia under the delegation of a specialist and are responsible for the continuous perioperative monitoring of patients. The practical training takes place at the Hospital Uster, the theoretical lessons are attended at the higher technical school Z-INA. The professional exchange with our cooperation hospitals (Kinderspital Zürich, GZO, Spital Männedorf, Spital Zollikerberg and Spital Bülach) is an integral part of the training and completes our offer. Exciting challenges await you

  • Acquisition of in-depth anesthesiological, pharmacological and medical expertise
  • independent and responsible preparation and implementation of general anesthesias in delegation and cooperation with a specialist anesthesiology within the framework of your competences
  • Assistance and professional supervision in regional anaesthesia
  • interdisciplinary emergency medical patient care and resuscitation in the shock room and throughout the hospital
  • Complete anesthesia documentation and performance monitoring
  • Close cooperation in the intra- and interprofessional team

What sets you apart for the job

  • Degree as a Dipl. Nurse HF/FH or a foreign diploma recognised by the SRC
  • at least 12 months of professional experience as a Dipl. Nurse at the acute hospital
  • Assessment of language competence level C1 (see European Framework of Reference for Languages)
  • sound nursing and medical basic knowledge
  • technical flair
  • Decision and responsiveness in rapidly changing medical situations
  • Resilience, flexibility and a high degree of responsibility
  • high social competence in caring for people in extreme situations
  • high motivation to learn new things and quick comprehension
