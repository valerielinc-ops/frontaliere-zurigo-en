Full description

The Office for Youth and Vocational Guidance (AJB) is responsible for child and youth assistance as well as career guidance. Our work focuses on children, young people and families. We work for them daily with a lot of passion and commitment. The kjz (Kinder- und Jugendhilfezentrum) is a poly-valent consulting centre for families, parents, young people, children and young children and works on the basis of the Kinder- und Jugendhilfegesetz of the canton of Zurich. Do you want to be involved in the welfare of infants and young children? Then you will be as an assistant mother and father counseling in Küsnacht by immediately or by appointment part of our team. Tasks

  • Preparation of counselling spaces
  • Reception of parents and children
  • Weighing and measuring of infants
  • Occupation with the larger children
  • Promotion of contact among waiting parents
  • Monitoring of the entire procedure
  • Clearing of premises after completion of the consultation half day
