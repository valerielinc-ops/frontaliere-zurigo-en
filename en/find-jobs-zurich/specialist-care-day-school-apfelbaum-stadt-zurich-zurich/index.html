Full description

In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a specialist in the care of Apfelbaum by immediately or by agreement.

Tasks

  • They support the care teams with their pedagogical expertise in everyday life and keep a cool head even in challenging situations.
  • They do the 60% workload four days a week. The assignments are in a care facility outside the school facility of the day school Apfelbaum
  • They work mainly with kindergarten and individual primary school children (up to 6th grade) and accompany them in regular operation, in holiday care and in care on school-free days

Profile

  • Degree as an EFZ specialist Supervision (for applications with a foreign diploma / certificate, a Swiss diploma recognition must be attached)
  • Motivated, open, resilient personality with a positive human image
  • Flexibility as well as very good team and communication skills
  • Joy in dealing with people of different cultures and willingness for interdisciplinary cooperation in the school team
  • Professional experience in supplementary family care is beneficial

We offer

  • A varied, versatile and independent activity
  • A multicultural, exciting environment
  • Competent support from the management
  • Progressive salary and social benefits according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich (Horte)

About us

We are a large school community in Zurich-Nord with 18 schools. We derive our pedagogical attitude from the concept of New Authority and thus meet the daily challenges in everyday school life together and effectively.

The Apfelbaum school includes morning care and four lunch/evening care. A total of around 470 pupils from kindergarten to middle school age are fed and looked after in these care centres. Lunch and evening attendance hours are from Monday to Friday from 11.55 a.m. to 6 p.m.
