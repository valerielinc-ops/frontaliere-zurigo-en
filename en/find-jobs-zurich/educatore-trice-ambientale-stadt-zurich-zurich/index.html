Full description

Are you ready for a job where you can make a meaningful difference for people and nature? From April 2027, or if possible earlier, you will have the chance to do so as a nature educator at the forest school Adlisberg.

Tasks

  • They take over the preparation and implementation of nature school days with school classes, including administration, as well as procurement and production of teaching materials
  • They take over the preparation and implementation of courses and events for adults in the field of nature education
  • Develop new offers for school classes and teachers
  • You work on projects and carry out administrative work for nature school operations

Profile

  • Scientific and/or pedagogical studies or comparable training with in-depth knowledge of nature and nature conservation or forestry as well as craft skills
  • Experience and joy in the design and management of mostly full-day nature pedagogical offers outdoors, especially with school classes of cycle 1 and 2
  • A natural authority in dealing with students with a view to the whole
  • High resilience due to the handling of changing school classes, year-round working outdoors, long lessons: time availability in school hours on three to four days per week, annual working hours with seasonal fluctuations: multi-time compensation possible during the Zurich school holidays
  • Class time on Tuesdays and Thursdays
  • Independent and responsible working methods as well as constructive cooperation with different groups
  • Communicatively strong and secure use of digital tools

We offer

  • Advantages for public transport
  • Internal training offer
  • Support for external education and training
  • 16 weeks maternity leave and 4 weeks paternity leave
  • Above-average social benefits

About us

Zurich greener - that is our vision. Because Grün City of Zurich maintains, designs and manages the public green areas. These make up about 40 percent of the city area. With their work, our 580 employees from over 20 nations contribute to the high quality of life in the city of Zurich.
