Full description

In the area of project management, we are looking for a new colleague immediately or by agreement due to early retirement.

Tasks

  • They lead and coordinate project teams from the preliminary project to the completion of the construction.
  • They are responsible for the client-side quality management and place high demands on the comprehensive fulfilment of the project goals.
  • You bring about decisions, represent your projects in a communicative and addressable manner and contribute the necessary administrative knowledge.
  • They understand how to combine cost and deadline compliance with high-quality construction and architecture as well as ecological, economic and social aspects.

Profile

  • Proven project management experience in the planning and execution of complex construction projects, comprehensive project management skills in the areas of organization, costs, deadlines and quality, experience with BIM beneficial
  • High architectural judgment and good knowledge of Swiss building laws, standards and standards
  • Personal responsibility, commitment, communication strength, teamwork, persuasion and strong social skills
  • High service orientation, entrepreneurial thinking, creative solutions for challenging issues
  • Very good knowledge of German

We offer

It offers you a challenging and responsible task in a professional, future-oriented environment and a motivated team. We offer attractive employment conditions as well as development and further education opportunities and a job in the center of Zurich. Flexible working hours and a generous home office regulation enable a good work-life balance.

About us

Public buildings give people space for encounter and exchange, for development and future. They shape the cityscape and are a valuable cultural asset. In the role of the client representative, the Office for Building Constructions develops and realizes high-quality buildings for a liveable and urban Zurich on the way to net zero.
