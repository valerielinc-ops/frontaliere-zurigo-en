Personnel: in disposition and customer service

Fust | Swiss Household Services AG — Otelfingen, Zurich, Svizzera

Switzerland buys at Fust! We are proud to be a leader in electrical household appliances, multimedia/entertainment electronics and kitchen and bathroom remodeling, and with our comprehensive range of services and unique services we create real added value! Become part of our team and help us continue to create customer experiences! A job at Fust, and it’s running!

Tasks

  • Support in day-to-day operations as well as in strategic tasks and projects
  • Coordination of Swiss-wide scheduling and operational planning of service technicians according to regional responsibilities
  • Communication and correspondence with internal and external contacts, including interface management (shop builder, electrical planner, project management)
  • Independent allocation of tasks within the assistance and scheduling pool as well as processing of incoming customer orders
  • Taking over general administrative activities such as logging and office equipment management

Requirements

  • Commercial education with several years of professional experience in a comparable function
  • Sound MS-Office knowledge, especially in Excel; SAP knowledge is an advantage
  • Experience in disposition – ideally in the field of service technicians – or readiness for familiarization
  • Strong customer and service orientation as well as enjoyment of direct customer contact
  • Negotiating knowledge of German
  • French skills desired at the conversation level

What we offer

  • Conditions of employment: Flexible working hours, attractive fixed salary, flat-rate expense allowance, company vehicle for private use
  • Discounts: Discounts in various co-op formats, 5 times the number of co-op super points, Up to 20% discount on various insurance services, Cheap mobile and prepaid subscriptions at Coop Mobile, Reka Card: Discounts on subscription, staff discount also for partners: in (living in the same household), discount on the travel offer ITS Coop Travel, CHF 650.- Voucher for the purchase of an SBB GA's
  • Training: internal training, support for external training, development opportunities
  • Communication & culture: DU culture throughout the company, orientation towards common values, internal app for communication with employees
  • Insurance: Pension Fund: 2/3 is paid by Coop (base), various savings plans at the pension fund, generous and super-compulsory insurance benefits in the event of accident and illness
  • Holidays: At least 25 days of holiday or according to valid GAV, if possible support for unpaid holidays
  • Health: Update Fitness: CHF 200.- Discount on annual subscription, free and anonymous company social consulting 24/7
