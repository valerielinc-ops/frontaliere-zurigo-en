Full description

Are you rethinking medical coding? Do you bring energy, ideas and enthusiasm? Then we look for you. For our modern and dynamic field, we are looking for a positioned, flexible and motivated personality who not only leads, but moves. A person who actively shapes change, breaks new ground and wants to develop and rethink medical coding together with the team.

Tasks

  • Professional and personnel management of medical coding
  • Further development and optimization of existing processes
  • Building a modern, efficient and future-oriented coding system
  • Promoting a motivating and positive team spirit
  • Ensuring high quality and correct coding
  • Contact person for doctors, nursing and other specialist areas
  • Active participation in change and innovation
  • Coaching and support of the team in everyday life

Profile

  • Medical coding imperative
  • Experience in medical coding and ideally first leadership experience
  • An engaging, positioned personality with a high IT affinity
  • High flexibility and pleasure in change and development
  • Teamwork, communication strength and a positive appearance
  • Independent, solution-oriented and responsible working method
  • Enthusiasm for taking people and making a difference together

Flexible, flexible working hours, home office as well as a generous maternity leave and employment levels adapted to different situations – all this is possible at KSW, as long as the company allows it. Every year, the KSW offers 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses. We also support you temporally and/or financially for external training.
