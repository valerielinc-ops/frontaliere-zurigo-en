Their tasks

  • They accompany and support our assistant doctors with entrance examinations, geriatric assessments, visits and administrative tasks.
  • On request and if suitable, you can supervise inpatient patients on your own.
  • As a student, you will be closely accompanied and supported by the assistant doctor and the cadre doctor. You can thus gain first practical experience in the care of geriatric, multimorbid or seriously ill patients in the palliative department.
  • Insight into the outpatient dementia assessment, consultancy service and cooperation in the care of residents in long-term care is possible
  • You study human medicine at a university
  • They can integrate into operational processes, show interest and friendly treatment of patients, employees and supervisors.
