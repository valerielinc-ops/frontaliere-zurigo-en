Full description

The money market department is responsible for the bank’s operational cash management, the procurement of regulatoryly valuable liquidity on the international money market and the expansion of its own asset business. There is also close cooperation with Treasury on planning, regulatory issues and related issues such as SNB facilities.

In order to strengthen our team with workplace Zurich Airport, we are looking for an experienced, resilient and efficient personality as a cash and collateral trader (m/f/d). In addition to day-to-day business with fixed deposits and the scheduling of the contingents, the focus of the role is on strengthening operational resilience and commitment to projects and initiatives. Tasks: • Operational liquidity management (loro/nostro) through unsecured fixed deposits, repos and FX swaps • Procurement of regulatoryly valuable liquidity on the international money market • Execution of trading activities with the primary objective of generating income for the bank • Participation in and/or management of strategic and operational projects (e.g. regulatory issues, IT platform, operational risks) • Coordination of cooperation with various stakeholders (middle and back office, risk, accounting, etc.) Requirements • Completed banking studies with subsequent academic further education or completed university studies in a relevant subject area • Several years of experience in treasury, trading or sales and broad knowledge of payments, liquidity management and front-to-back process management; a track record in the project business is an advantage • Entrepreneurial thinking and action and a clear focus on customer satisfaction • Limited willingness to travel internationally and regular participation in evening events or conferences in Switzerland • In-depth interest in global financial markets, Swiss and international monetary policy and regulatory issues We offer: The work-life balance of our employees is important to us. That’s why we create the conditions for flexible working hours – with offers for mobile work, home office and part-time work.
