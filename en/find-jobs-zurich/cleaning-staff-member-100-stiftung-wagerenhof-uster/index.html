Full description

From 1.1.2027 or by agreement we are looking for you as Employee / in cleaning 100% Do you like to get involved, work carefully and enjoy dealing with people? For the cleaning and care of our premises, we are looking for a reliable, independent and responsible personality. At Wagerenhof, people with disabilities live and work and a respectful and appreciative treatment of people with disabilities is therefore particularly important. In this versatile function, you support our team in cleaning and at the same time accompany residents and employees who need support in everyday work. You can expect a varied activity with meaning, responsibility and an appreciative working environment. The Wagerenhof Foundation (affectionately called “Wagi”) in Uster has been a permanent home for around 250 people with multiple cognitive impairments for more than 100 years and offers over 50 employees who need support accompanied jobs. We are a recognized, non-profit institution with a social mission. With a demand-oriented offer of living and care places as well as various day and leisure offers, we promote the quality of life, participation and self-determination of our residents. The special features of the Wagerenhof include size and Dörfli character, which allows space for encounters. For employees with different careers and professionalism, the Wagerenhof Foundation is an attractive employer with a wide range of development opportunities. For learners, Wagerenhof is a competent training partner. In these activities you support us

  • Implementation of daily maintenance and basic cleaning
  • Cleaning and maintenance of bathrooms, sanitary facilities, work and common areas
  • Compliance with hygiene, safety and quality standards
  • if necessary: guidance and support of employees in need of support

You suit us and this place, if you also put the people entrusted to us at the center of your activities and bring the following:

  • you bring many years of experience in maintenance and basic cleaning
  • You can handle cleaning products and equipment professionally
  • You can independently organize your assigned cleaning tasks
  • You are physically resilient
  • You are responsible, reliable and on time
  • You have high hygiene awareness
  • You have good oral knowledge of German, as well as a good understanding of reading (at least B1)
  • You are discreet and have a well-groomed appearance
  • You like to fit into a team

This makes us the place for your work

  • The appreciative, human togetherness
  • the well thought-out overall concept and the technical support available at all times;
  • the village character and the park-like structure of the Wagerenhof
  • Good accessibility with public transport
