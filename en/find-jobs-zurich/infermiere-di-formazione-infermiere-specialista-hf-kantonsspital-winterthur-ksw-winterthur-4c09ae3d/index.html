Full description

The threads run together with you: As a nurse HF or nurse HF at the Cantonal Hospital Winterthur, you are the central contact person for patients. You have the care process under control and act as a hub between doctors, patients, their relatives and the care team. With your professional competence and your empathy, you provide security and ensure that patients feel well looked after by us. Neither day is the same. At the Cantonal Hospital Winterthur, you are a member of a motivated interdisciplinary team, gain insight into a wide range of areas and have many opportunities for further education.

Profile

  • Sek II degree (Federal Certificate of Competence, High School Matura, FMS)
  • No fear of contact in dealing with people in difficult circumstances
  • Mental and physical resilience
  • Good observation skills, networked thinking
  • Team and communication skills
  • Quality awareness
  • Independence, reliability
  • Passed Multicheck® Competence Analysis Health HF

Benefits: KSW offers employees conditions that enable them to reconcile their professional goals and personal needs. Flexible, flexible working hours, home office as well as a generous maternity leave and employment levels adapted to different situations – all this is possible at KSW, as long as the company allows it. Every year, the KSW offers 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses. We also support you temporally and/or financially for external training.
