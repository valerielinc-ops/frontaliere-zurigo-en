With more than 170 own and franchise studios worldwide, we pursue one goal: to make people efficient, healthy and self-determined – at any age through targeted strengthening of the musculoskeletal system. Kieser physiotherapy Kieser Physiotherapy is an independent and integral part of our brand. Our physiotherapy practice is based on the Kieser method and combines active rehabilitation with medical strength training.

Your mission

As part of our physio team, you combine evidence-based physiotherapy with the transfer of patients into active training directly into a Kieser studio. You think therapy further, strengthen self-efficacy and help people take responsibility for their health. What you do

  • Accompanying instead of just treating: From structured findings to active therapy tailored to the cause to independent training – step by step, at the right pace.
  • Understandably explain & motivate: You create knowledge, trust and movement - through clear communication.
  • Treatment as needed: You work independently and purposefully in the sense of the individual needs of our patients and find effective solutions for the treatment of symptoms.
  • Co-create & think: You bring ideas and develop processes, processes and offers in the field of physiotherapy with us.
  • Make progress visible: You set realistic goals and reflect success – together with the team and the patients.
  • Connect Therapy & Training: You work closely with the studio team and contribute to the transition to independent training in the studio.

What makes you

  • You really want to make a difference with your work – for your patients and for your professional environment. You believe in the potential of health-oriented training, education and self-responsible health promotion. You not only bring professional knowledge, but also:
  • A recognized training as a physiotherapist (m/f/d) with SRK recognition.
  • A structured, self-responsible working method combined with real pleasure in teamwork.
  • The motivation to actively accompany people, strengthen them and empower them in the long term.
  • Interest in evidence-based physiotherapy and the link with targeted strength training
  • Very good knowledge of German (minimum C1) – other languages such as English or French are advantageous
  • An open, empathetic way and the ability to communicate clearly and solution-oriented.
  • Responsibility & expertise: You act reliably, contribute your experience in a targeted manner, use evidence-based methods and approach tasks in a committed and solution-oriented manner.
  • Collaboration & attitude: In our team, commitment, mutual support and respectful cooperation counts
  • Enthusiasm for work, courage for clear positions and openness to new things are important to us.
  • What to expect from us
  • Targeted further development: We actively support your professional development with further training so that you can continuously grow and discover new perspectives.
  • Provision: Above-average participation in occupational pension schemes
  • Health as a priority: Use our free Kieser training sabo in all Kieser studios for health strength and a good balance.
  • Exclusive benefits: Access to Corporate Benefits (www.corporate-benefits.ch) and other employee benefits that enrich and appreciate your everyday work
  • Team spirit & environment: A cooperative, supportive environment in which mutual appreciation and lived attitude count. Experience an open du-culture, flat hierarchies, regular team events and a centrally located workplace with best transport connections.
