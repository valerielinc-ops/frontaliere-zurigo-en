Full description

Would you like to contribute and further develop your technical expertise in the performance test of life insurance in an international reinsurance environment? Then we look forward to welcoming you to our Life & Health team. Together we further develop the performance strategy in the German market, promote innovations and build sustainable customer relationships. In this position, you will become part of an agile performance review and risk review team. You actively contribute your experience, work together across disciplines and contribute to providing high-quality and modern services for our customers in the reinsurance context. The location is Munich

  • Her focus is on supporting the German market. In addition, you can also support the team with inquiries from other European markets, such as Central and Eastern Europe or Scandinavia.

Main responsibilities

  • Technical examination and independent handling of benefits cases in disability, basic and life insurance and serious diseases, including complex cases, under the delegated powers of attorney
  • Establishment and maintenance of customer relationships as a competent contact for the performance auditors of our customers
  • Close cooperation with client management, risk assessment and actuary for the continuous optimization of portfolios and processes
  • Participation in the further development of performance testing strategies, product concepts and new business areas
  • Participation in customer audits and support in the development of cost-efficient claims approaches
  • Participation in strategic projects, including further development of performance testing systems
  • Active participation in topics related to digitalization, automation and the use of AI in performance testing
  • Analysis of market trends and customer needs as a basis for professional and strategic decisions

About the team As a Life & Health Hub in Munich, we are part of a networked continental European Life & Health team as well as a global Life & Health community. The team combines expertise from client management, actuary, performance and risk testing and medicine. We work in a dynamic, demanding and collaborative environment, which is characterized by professional exchange, analytical thinking, joint responsibility and a good portion of the joy of cooperation. They report to the Head of Claims and Underwriting for Germany, Nordics/Baltics, Austria and Central and Eastern Europe.

About you

They combine in-depth expertise in performance testing with an open, cooperative way of working and gladly assume responsibility in decision-making processes. Different levels of experience are welcome – we actively support your professional and personal development. Our expectations

  • Education or professional background in life insurance, financial services, legal or medical specialty
  • At least five years of experience in the German life insurance and/or reinsurance market, in particular in performance testing of disability insurance
  • Enjoy building and maintaining long-term customer relationships
  • Basic skills in data analysis and structured problem solving
  • Interest in digitalisation, automation and the use of modern technologies (e.g. AI-based analyses) in performance testing
  • High care in detail while at the same time looking for higher-level relationships
  • Fluent and secure communication in German and English

Even if you do not fully meet all requirements, we welcome your application. Decisive is your professional motivation, your interest in modern performance testing and cooperation with customers. Remuneration In Germany, the basic salary for this position is between 95,000 EUR and 158.000 EUR (full-time employment).
