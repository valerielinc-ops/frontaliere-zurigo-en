Marketing & Communications Coordinator

Join our global Marketing & Communications team in our Headquarters in Pfäffikon SZ (Switzerland) or Garching (Germany) and help drive impactful communications, campaigns, publications and events worldwide. As MarCom Coordinator, you will play a key role in supporting the day-to-day operations of the function while ensuring the successful coordination of marketing and communication initiatives across the organisation.

Your Responsibilities

  • Support the planning, coordination and execution of global Marketing and Communications activities, projects and continuous improvement initiatives
  • Prepare and distribute communications materials in German and English, including research and background information on current topics and projects
  • Coordinate content collection, publications, translations, reviews, approvals and distribution
  • Support the production of corporate publications, including financial reports
  • Support the organisation of corporate events, trade shows and internal events, coordinating logistics, suppliers, registrations and exhibition materials
  • Provide organisational and administrative support to the Head of Marketing and Communications, including meetings, travel arrangements and departmental activities
  • Maintain departmental mailing lists, contact databases and distribution lists
  • Support budget tracking, procurement and vendor administration

Your Profile

  • Commercial education or a comparable qualification, combined with several years of experience in an assistant, coordination, communications, marketing or administrative role
  • Experience working in an international environment; exposure to events, trade shows or communications activities is an advantage
  • Excellent organisational, planning and coordination skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a dynamic environment
  • Structured, pragmatic and service-oriented approach, with strong attention to detail and a commitment to high-quality results
  • Strong interpersonal and intercultural communication skills, paired with a collaborative and professional mindset
  • High level of discretion, reliability and integrity
  • Fluent written and spoken English and German
  • French is an advantage

We offer

  • A very welcoming, supportive and collaborative team spirit and an open company culture
  • An exciting and challenging opportunity to work in an international global technology company
  • Attractive compensation and working conditions

About Oerlikon

Oerlikon (SIX: OERL) is a global leader in surface technologies and advanced materials. With a unique portfolio spanning surface engineering, high-performance materials, coating equipment and components, we make products better by enhancing performance, efficiency and sustainability. Oerlikon serves a wide range of industries, including aerospace, automotive, energy, medical, luxury, semiconductors and tooling. Headquartered in Pfaeffikon, Switzerland, Oerlikon operates in 38 countries with a global workforce of around 10,000 people. In 2025, Oerlikon achieved sales of CHF 1.6 billion.
