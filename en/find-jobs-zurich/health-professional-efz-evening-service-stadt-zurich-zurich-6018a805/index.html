Full description

As of 01. November 2026 or by appointment we are looking for a committed personality as a specialist in the evening & weekend service, 40-60 %

Tasks

  • They take over the professional and needs-based care and support of our customers in all services.
  • They support our customers in the activities of daily life and provide nursing and domestic services according to care planning.
  • They advise and instruct relatives and caregivers and work actively and constructively together in the interprofessional team.
  • They maintain a careful and comprehensible care documentation and contribute to the securing and further development of care quality.
  • They take on assignments in day and late service as well as on weekends and public holidays and contribute to an appreciative care of our customers.

Profile

  • Completed training as a specialist in health EFZ or equivalent qualification
  • Independent, responsible and team-oriented working methods
  • Resilience, flexibility and strong customer orientation
  • Readiness for assignments in all services as well as on weekends/holidays
  • Good IT user knowledge and driving license Cat. B, car of advantage

We offer

Freedom in the design of your work as well as a motivated care team with a fixed customer base. Our employees also benefit from attractive employment conditions in accordance with the personnel law of the City of Zurich as well as targeted training and further education. In addition to at least four weeks holidays, you are entitled to 6 company holidays.

About us

The Foundation for Old-Age Apartments of the City of Zurich (SAW) operates 40 settlements in urban areas with more than 2000 affordable apartments for people aged 60 and over. It supports its tenants with everyday, social and nursing services and thus enables them a high quality of life into old age. Its diverse socio-cultural offer supports the development of neighbourly contacts and an active settlement life.
