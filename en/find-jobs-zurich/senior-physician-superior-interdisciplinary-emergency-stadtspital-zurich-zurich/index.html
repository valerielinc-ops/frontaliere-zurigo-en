Role overview

The Institute of Radiology and Nuclear Medicine is organised across locations and operates nuclear medicine at both Triemli and Waid sites.

The institute has two modern SPECT/CT cameras, a high-end PET/CT device of the latest generation, a thyroid camera, two DEXA measuring stations, an ultrasound device, an ergometry room and a modern therapy station with currently 5 beds.

All diagnostic as well as all common outpatient and stationary nuclear medicine therapy methods are offered.
