Full description

Pack the digital future with us and, together with our specialist areas and IT, bring a breath of fresh air into our systems and processes in order to create innovative and sustainable solutions.

Tasks

  • You actively participate in digitalization and transformation projects in the field of smart metering, energy data management and workforce management and drive the automation of business processes.
  • As a business engineer, you will analyze requirements, advise on best practices and design efficient end-to-end processes across multiple systems.
  • You design, realize and accompany solutions for data, process and system landscapes up to successful introduction, with a focus on master, meter and measurement data processes.
  • Together with specialist departments and IT, you will continue to develop our business applications and accompany extensions, optimizations and new solutions.
  • As a key user, you ensure stable operation, high data quality and reliable 2nd-level support, empower power users and coordinate incidents and changes.

Profile

  • You have a higher education (HF, FH, ETH or university) in computer science, business informatics, business administration or a comparable field of expertise.
  • In business or requirements engineering, in the further development of business applications and in the analysis and optimization of end-to-end processes, you have already gained experience.
  • Strong analytical skills set you apart and help you identify connections between data and processes
  • Knowledge of SQL, Data Warehouse, BI or Analytics is an advantage.
  • Ideally, you are familiar with SAP (e.g. IS-U, BW, MM, WM) or comparable enterprise applications and have already gained experience in smart metering, energy data management or the energy industry.
  • You work in a structured, self-initiative and solution-oriented manner and develop sustainable solutions for technical requirements together with the specialist areas.

We offer

  • More energy for your life: With mobile work, flexible working models and part-time opportunities, you can actively shape your working time and divide it into the best fit for your life. For example, you can buy up to 2 weeks of extra holidays and work part-time at all levels. Job sharing/splitting and unpaid holidays are also possible with us.
  • Sustainable and cheaper travel: With the mobility contribution of up to 600 francs a year, you travel cheaply and sustainably – whether you want to cycle through Zurich or explore Switzerland by train.
  • An investment in your future: We not only value paying fair and transparent wages, but also ensure that you have more of your wages left over. We take over 60% of the savings contributions and additionally offer a super-compulsory interest on the capital accumulated.
  • An energy boost for your free time: We pay you 100 francs a month on your lunch-check card. In addition, you benefit annually from further Fringe Benefits worth 150 francs and attractive mobile subscriptions.
  • Development and training: With our talent management, we promote your development in a targeted manner – through a wide range of internal and external training offers as well as exciting development opportunities.
  • Meaningful work: Sustainability and meaningful work are not just words for us: shape Zurich and the future for us. Together with us, release energy for a good morning.
