Full description

Faculty of Law / Chair Prof. Dr. iur. Walter Boente Job as of 1 January or 1 February 2027. However, a later job can be agreed flexibly. Chair of Prof. Dr. iur. Walter Boente is dedicated to research and teaching in Swiss private law. The focus of the activity is on legal theory, comparative law and civil law, in particular national and international law on persons and inheritance, as well as related questions of civil procedure law. The chair is involved in various European research networks and works closely with international organisations. In addition, the chair includes various legal technology and digital humanities projects in the field of Swiss, foreign and international law as well as legal history.

Tasks

Your work at the chair depends on the status of your education and your profile. It includes participation in research and teaching, in particular the preparation of courses (materials, exercises) and publications (research, corrections) as well as support and cooperation in research projects of the chair. In addition, depending on the qualification and level of employment, the provision of courses and the supervision of students can be added. It is also possible to participate in the preparation and correction of examinations and in the supervision of bachelor and master theses. Own projects within the framework of the research focuses of the chair can be carried out independently by agreement.

Profile

You study law in the Bachelor or Master degree program. An above-average track record as well as the willingness to familiarize oneself in the teaching and research areas of the chair and to work independently is required. Due to the close cooperation of the chair with international research networks and universities abroad, foreign language skills are very welcome in addition to very good knowledge of German.
