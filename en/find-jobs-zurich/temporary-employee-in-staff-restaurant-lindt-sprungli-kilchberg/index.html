About us

We enchant the world with chocolate! Lindt & Sprüngli (Switzerland) AG is a subsidiary of the global Lindt & Sprüngli Group with headquarters and production facilities in Kilchberg on Lake Zurich. In Altendorf we operate our own logistics centre, while the Lindt Cocoa Center in Olten produces cocoa mass for our own use as well as for other European production facilities. Throughout Switzerland, we proudly operate 15 Lindt shops, including iconic locations such as the Jungfraujoch and the Swiss Transport House in Lucerne. More than 1000 employees from more than 50 nations contribute to our success.

For our staff restaurant in Kilchberg, we are immediately looking for reliable and committed support in the hourly wage (pensity on demand usually about 60%).

What you do with us

  • In our staff restaurant (self-service) you advise and serve the guests with pleasure
  • During lunch you are responsible for efficient food delivery
  • As part of the service team, you are responsible for preparing and implementing deployments, aperitifs and events.
  • You maintain the sales showcases and are responsible for a nice product presentation
  • You support the team in all work
  • Working hours are from Monday to Friday between 07:00 and 16.00
  • Events or aperitifs take place irregularly in the evening and also require a corresponding work assignment

When you fit in with us

  • Professional experience in service, specifically in system catering
  • Very good oral German language skills
  • Guest-oriented thinking and acting
  • Responsible and service-oriented personality with a well-groomed appearance

Have you become curious? Then we look forward to your application including CV, motivation letter and relevant employment certificates.
