Full description

Is it also your motto that there is no second chance for a first good impression? And do you understand “customer care” not only as a pure advice to our customers, but also as a unique opportunity to expand and maintain the customer relationship?

The Direct, Digital & Customer Care division is an important sales channel in Zurich Switzerland and ensures that our customers receive the best protection in every situation. As a Special Service Advisor in Special Services, you will process requests from international customers, partners and existing customers on the phone, answer written correspondence reliably and give the customers the feeling that they are in good hands with you.

Our ambition is to lead the customer- and technology-driven transformation in the Swiss insurance market. Want to be part of this journey? Then this could be your next career step.

Find out here why Zurich Switzerland is one of the "Top 3" employers in Switzerland. Become part of it and apply as Customer Advisor Special Services.

What you do

  • You process incoming customer inquiries and concerns (in writing and by phone)
  • You are responsible for high-quality processing of dismissals and annulments

You work in complaint management and support your colleagues professionally

  • You provide competent information for customer inquiries and ensure high-quality processing of written customer correspondence
  • You take on offline activities such as administrative and organizational activities

What you bring

  • Successfully completed commercial training, ideally in the insurance or financial sector
  • Interest and pleasure in consulting as well as telephone and written customer contact
  • Rapid understanding for the secure handling of IT applications and processes
  • Excellent communication skills and strong customer orientation
  • Stylistic German as well as French and Italian in word and writing, every other language and English is advantageous
