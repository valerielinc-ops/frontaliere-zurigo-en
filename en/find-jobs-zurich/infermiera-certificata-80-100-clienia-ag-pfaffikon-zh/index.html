Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With over 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric services offered by Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in outpatient, day clinic and inpatient settings. The group employs around 1800 people and generated sales of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For our Privé Station D2 with a treatment focus on psychotherapy, we are looking for a

The range of therapies on this private ward is aimed at patients with depression, burnout and stress crises, anxiety and obsessive-compulsive disorders, sleep disorders, as well as psychosomatic complaints. In addition to a high therapeutic treatment quality, with a focus on psychotherapeutic offers, patients are offered a courteous and cultivated atmosphere. In addition to occupational and physiotherapy, the range of therapies also includes complementary medicine and psychosocial tasks. Our patients are cared for in close cooperation with the case therapist in the caregiver system. The atmosphere on the ward is characterized by a respectful attitude and the careful cultivation of interpersonal relationships.

Your tasks

  • the individual care and support of the patients and the contact with the relatives within the framework of the caregiver work and in consultation with the core team
  • Leading one-on-one meetings, group offers and reports
  • Consultation and planning of treatment in an interdisciplinary and interprofessional team
  • Supporting patients in crisis situations

Your profile For this exciting job, you have a nursing degree at HF level and, in the best case, you have already gained professional experience in the field of psychiatric nursing. You would like to work in a special area where customer orientation is lived. Variety and changing situations are a challenge for you and do you enjoy it?

Are you an open, communicative and present personality? Then D2 is the right place for you. We look forward to applicants with joy, humor, their own ideas, a great interest in interprofessional work and cultivated manners.

Do you feel addressed now? Then feel free to apply to us, we look forward to getting to know you.
