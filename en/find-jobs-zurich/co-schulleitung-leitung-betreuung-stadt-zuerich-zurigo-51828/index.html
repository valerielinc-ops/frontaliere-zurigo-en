Full description

Tensions in the heart of Züri - these are me! Every day on the pulse of education, where our future is growing and learning. Become part of our team! We are looking for a person as of February 1, 2027, combined as:

Co-management (52%) + management supervision (48%)

Tasks

Your main tasks as a school management (52%) include

  • Joint operational management of the school field and representation of the school against the outside, towards parents, school authority and administration
  • Personnel management of the department of home service and technology, the school management assistant and the staff in the area of teaching in consultation with the job partner
  • Co-responsible for school development, quality development and assurance, operational organisation, administration and finance
  • Cooperation with the school authority

As Head of Care (48%)

  • Personnel management of the employees* in the care
  • Administrative work related to personnel management, budget management and student allocation (using various tools such as peps.NET, PSL, etc.)
  • Contact person for external specialist positions / institutions
  • Organisation and securing of catering loans and their settlement

Profile

  • Professional experience as school management and/or management supervision
  • School management training completed
  • Flexibility in everyday school life
  • Great motivation and a lot of joy in the further development of a “good school”
  • Enjoying a close and open cooperation with the SL job partner and the management team (SAR, LHT)
  • Readiness for professional reflection in the management team
  • Leader with high social skills, assertiveness, communicative and organizational skills
  • High motivation and interest in school development issues and educational policy issues

We offer

  • Responsible, versatile management tasks
  • Engaged and supportive job partner, school management assistant and management of home service and technology
  • A dedicated and well-mixed school team, school social work and speech therapy
  • Great students and parents
  • An exciting, challenging field of impact in the school sector
  • Central location with good public transport connection
  • A well-functioning school management conference and a supporting authority
  • Contemporary employment according to the guidelines of the canton

The school district Zurich-Limmattal (city districts 4, 5 and part district 3) is with its 14 schools managed a diverse school circle in the city of Zurich.
