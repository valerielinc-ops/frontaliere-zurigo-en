Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 75085

Hirslanden stands for first-class, responsible and individual health care that puts people first. From birth to old age as well as from prevention to healing – at any time, physically and digitally – the Hirslanden Group is concentrating on the further development of the “Continuum of Care” together with private and public partners.

Our focus is not only on the well-being of our patients, but also on that of our employees.

YOUR TASKS

  • You code the inpatient cases in different medical specialties
  • Together with our team, you will continue to develop our coding processes
  • Complaints of the cost carriers you check and deal with with your professional insight
  • In exchange with treating doctors, you provide complete, accurate and comprehensible documentation
  • You ensure that our data is complete, correct and valid at all times
  • You are happy to pass on your knowledge – be it during the training of new colleagues or during internal training courses

Your profile

  • Completed basic medical or nursing training (HF/FH) with clinical experience in the hospital – a post-diploma in emergency, anesthesia or intensive care is an advantage
  • Further training in medical coding, ideally with federal law. Certificate of Competence
  • Experience in coding in the acute field and in-depth knowledge of the Swiss DRG system
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss health insurance system
  • Structured, independent and analytical working method with high self-motivation
  • Experience in dealing with clinic information systems and coding software
  • Stylistic communication in German, knowledge of French is a plus

Are you ready to take off in an innovative environment?

With us you can fully develop your strengths, your know-how and your skills. On your first working day, we warmly welcome you and make sure that you feel comfortable with us from the beginning.

Already in the first week you can benefit from many advantages:

  • Varied menus in our staff restaurant at an advantage price
  • Fitness 24/7: Free access to our fitness room (including lunch classes)
  • Free employee advice: For your well-being – whether in professional questions, private challenges or simply to gain new perspectives
  • More than 5 weeks of vacation as part of our Leisure Plus program – for the perfect work-life balance

And many more exciting benefits can be found here!

For additional information, Kristina Pettofrezza (Team Leader Coding) is available at Kristina.Pettofrezza@hirslanden.ch. Celina Timmer is looking forward to your full online application.
