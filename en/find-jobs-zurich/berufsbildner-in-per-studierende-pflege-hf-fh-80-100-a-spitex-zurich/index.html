Full description

Your passion is to pass on knowledge and accompany future care professionals in their development? Our vocational trainers are organised in their own team at Spitex Zurich and can thus fully concentrate on accompanying students and learners. This means for you: The close cooperation in the team allows you a good exchange and best results. You will be actively supported by those responsible for education and training. You work situationally across locations, have a strong team behind you, work independently and enjoy many freedoms and opportunities in practical vocational training. In order to strengthen our vocational trainer teams, we are looking for a personality as

YOUR TASKS

  • You accompany, promote and empower students in practical and theoretical training and thus ensure a high-quality training of students care HF/FH. If necessary, you also participate in the training of Learner FaGe.
  • During practical learning days, we work according to the concept of the learning and working community and promote a committed and practice-oriented education, in which you can actively contribute to the further development of the apprentices.
  • You work across locations and closely with other vocational trainer teams, the training and education managers, the coach trio and the care teams.
  • You conduct regular training and qualification discussions with your trainees.
  • You are actively involved in public relations (quarter events, events at higher technical schools or technical colleges) at Spitex Zurich
  • You teach students training transfer days and use your methodological-didactic skills in a goal-oriented manner.
  • So that you stay on the pulse of practice, you supplement your activity with individual care assignments.

You find us at least.

  • A meaningful activity with great influence on the development of future care professionals
  • A specialized vocational trainer team with regular professional exchange
  • A lot of personal responsibility as well as scope for action and design
  • An appreciative leadership culture with short decision paths
  • Attractive employment conditions and other benefits: our other advantages can be found
  • Our other benefits can be found here: https://jobs.spitex-zuerich.ch/benefits/

You bring with you

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nurse or nurse HF
  • SVEB certificate practical trainer/in (level 1) available or ready to complete
  • At least two years of professional experience as a Dipl. Nursing professional
  • Flair for digital tools and very good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Joy in the education of young people and adults and interest in the design of individual learning settings
  • High sense of responsibility, organisational capacity and independence
  • You like to travel by e-bike and/or have a driver’s license Kat. B
  • Working hours: part-time / full-time
