Design Engineering Manager Morrisville, NC 27560 Full-Time | Exempt Are you a hands-on engineering leader who thrives at the intersection of technical innovation, team leadership, and customer collaboration? Join our team as a Design Engineering Manager, where you will lead a team of design engineers responsible for developing custom hardware solutions that meet customer needs while ensuring quality, compliance, and operational excellence. In this role, you will oversee engineering design activities from concept through testing and documentation while managing engineering resources, collaborating with cross-functional teams, and supporting the successful delivery of customized products. You'll serve as a key technical leader working closely with Sales, Project Management, Operations, Quality, Purchasing, and Manufacturing to bring innovative solutions to life. If you're an experienced mechanical engineering leader with a passion for developing people, solving complex technical challenges, and delivering customer-focused solutions, we invite you to bring your expertise to our team. How Will You Make an Impact?

  • Lead, coach, and develop a team of Design Engineers responsible for custom hardware development
  • Review and approve engineering specifications, drawings, test plans, and technical documentation
  • Manage engineering schedules, project forecasts, and custom hardware deliverables
  • Translate customer requirements into practical, cost-effective engineering solutions
  • Prepare cost estimates and technical specifications for customized hardware components
  • Partner with Sales to define and validate customer design requirements
  • Develop complete engineering design packages, including drawings, specifications, test plans, and supporting documentation
  • Provide technical guidance throughout the design, testing, and product development process
  • Coordinate with vendors and manufacturers to resolve technical questions and support product development
  • Oversee hardware testing to ensure designs meet performance and quality requirements
  • Develop and maintain engineering SOPs, templates, and documentation supporting Quality and Regulatory standards
  • Ensure compliance with applicable engineering standards, regulatory requirements, and company procedures
  • Collaborate with Project Management, Purchasing, Operations, Quality, Service, and Warehouse teams to support successful project execution
  • Monitor project priorities while balancing multiple engineering initiatives in a fast-paced environment
  • Promote a culture of safety by ensuring compliance with OSHA regulations and company safety policies
  • Support continuous improvement initiatives across engineering processes and documentation What You Bring
