Canton of Zurich Health Department Veterinary Office Temporary Animal Care 50% The Canton of Zurich Veterinary Office is committed to animal protection and animal health. It helps to ensure the welfare of animals and the quality of animal foods. It helps ward off animal diseases by monitoring animal populations with laboratory tests and combating animal disease outbreaks. It also creates framework conditions for the public to safely interact with dogs. We are looking for a temporary assistant (50%) for 6 months for the animal welfare area in the animal care team immediately or by arrangement. Tasks You will work in an animal shelter in Zurich North. This primarily cares for dogs, cats, rabbits, small mammals, birds and reptiles that are temporarily in the care of the veterinary office. You ensure the well-being of these animals through correct feeding and care and ensure that they are adequately occupied. In addition to direct animal care, there are other tasks related to animal care: administrative work on the computer, cleaning and disinfection of the accommodation, storage management and environmental work at the location. You will also work at the cantonal foundling registration office, where you will check reports from the Swiss Animal Registration Center (STMZ) and initiate the necessary measures. Profile You are a trained animal keeper EFZ or holder of the “FBA Animal Care” diploma and preferably have already gained practical experience. You are flexible and used to working in a structured and independent manner, but also in a team, and processing data using computers. You enjoy taking on responsibility and working in a small, well-coordinated team suits you. You are willing to work on weekends and holidays. Offer

  • Varied and demanding tasks
  • Regular further training
  • Social benefits and employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines
  • Work in a motivated team Application Do you feel addressed? Then we look forward to receiving your online application by September 20, 2026. Do you have any questions? The head of the pet facility, Natascha Wenger, will be happy to provide you with information about the task on 41 43 259 41 41. Information about our office can be found at www.zh.ch/veta

Workload: 50%
