Full description

Do you like working with people? Would you like to work immediately with our education team for the nursing professions?

Tasks

  • They create learning situations for the trainees, guide them in the departments, document the learning process, provide feedback and assess them verbally and in writing.
  • They plan and coordinate the learning accompaniments independently and work with the trainees also in shift work or on weekends.
  • They are involved in further developing the training of nursing professions in the company and are jointly responsible for the implementation of the training concept.
  • They communicate openly and transparently in our education team and care teams and play an important role in the leadership triangle.
  • They deal with developments in care, incorporate innovations in practical activity and actively participate in change processes.

Profile

  • Joy of working with learners and students
  • Willingness to develop and shape change processes
  • Experience in group guidance
  • Completed training as a nurse HF with SRK recognition for diplomas from abroad, some years of professional experience and good communication skills (written and oral)
  • A recognised further education in the pedagogical field (at least SVEB 1)

We offer

Enjoy an exciting field of work in a professional and team-oriented working environment with a du-culture throughout the company. The human being is at our center. That is why we support the reconciliation of private and professional life, different working models make it possible.

As part of the city of Zurich and due to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, progressive employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

Depending on the experience, the annual salary for this function is between CHF 93'838.- and CHF 125'386.- (100%).

About us

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

The health centers Witikon and Riesbach are run jointly and are located in the middle of Zurich-Witikon and Zurich-Riesbach in districts 7 and 8. The Health Center for Old Witikon brings together various care and care services under one roof. The Riesbach Old Age Health Center is fully focused on the treatment, care and care of people with dementia. The runaway house offers peace, security and security. Both health centers are characterized by many years of experience in age medicine. Here you work together with 400 colleagues.
