We are looking for a construction manager: in GU/TU (new building) for the Zurich region with place of employment in Opfikon. Exciting projects await you - from business and residential construction to high-rise buildings to industrial and data center buildings, we offer you a modern working environment. In addition, modern working methods such as LEAN-Construction and BIM are not only theory for us, but are also implemented comprehensively and consistently in our projects.
