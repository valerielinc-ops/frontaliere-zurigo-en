Full description

For the Tunnel Construction Switzerland department, we are looking for you as a cost estimator to strengthen our acquisition team at our headquarters in Opfikon.

What moves you • Responsible for estimating complex projects in the field of underground construction as part of a tender team led by an experienced project manager • Developing the cost calculation basis, including execution variants and project optimizations • Construction sequence and scheduling • Supplier and subcontractor inquiries • Preparing cost estimates and cost projections • Supporting the supervisor in contract award negotiations • Price calculations for change-order quotations for ongoing construction sites • Preparing tenders internally in collaboration with civil engineering and special foundation engineering

This is how you will succeed • Degree and qualifications: You have a degree in civil engineering from a university of applied sciences or equivalent training and already have knowledge of underground construction. • Interest in cost estimating: Even if you have not yet worked full-time as a cost estimator, you are very welcome. • IT skills: You are an experienced, proficient user of MS Office. Experience with BAUBIT or a comparable estimating program is an advantage. The same applies to scheduling with TILOS. • Working style: You are used to working in a team, think analytically and holistically, and handle the tasks assigned to you independently and efficiently. • Language skills: Good German language skills are required. Additional knowledge of French and/or Italian is very welcome.

What we offer you • Innovative and sustainable working environment • Use of modern technologies • Support for employee development (support for continuing education through the Implenia Akademie) •

Implenia-Benefits, including (to name just a few)

• an in-house pension fund with three contribution levels: Implenia Vorsorge • 5 weeks of vacation plus 9 bridge days • Travel insurance for you and everyone living in your household • Discounts for Implenia employees on products and services
