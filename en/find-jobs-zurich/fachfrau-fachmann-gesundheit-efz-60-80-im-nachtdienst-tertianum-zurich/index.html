Full description

Tertianum Etzelgut is located in a quiet side street in the middle of Zurich-Wollishofen. This modern home has spacious single rooms in the nursing department and a specialized residential group for people with dementia. The restaurant is also open to external guests 365 days a year.

Your heart beats for older people? Is the nursing profession your vocation which for you not only improves the quality of life of others, but also creates a satisfaction and meaningfulness in your own life? Do you see opportunities to use your skills and experience with us to make a positive impact on our customers’ lives? Then you are right with us!

For the Tertianum Etzelgut in the region of Zurich - Wollishofen we are looking for an / an

Professional Health EFZ 60- 80% on night duty

Their tasks

  • Holistic care and care of guests in the sense of our care mission statement and the care process
  • Assumption of tasks as a caregiver
  • Participation in the care process and in the care needs classification with RAI LTCF among the assigned guests
  • Electronic care documentation using CareCoach (mobile possible with CareCoach smartphone)
  • Planning and support of medical visits
  • Assuming daily responsibility
  • Assuming tasks as a topic carrier for a subject and thus participating in the group-wide network on care quality
  • Independent assumption of responsibility for the entire station during the night shift

Your profile

  • Completed training for/specialist health EFZ
  • Professional experience in long-term care is beneficial
  • RAI LTCF - Knowledge desired or willingness to attend these courses (paid)
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude
  • Enjoying multiple challenges
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
