Original description

In Short

Are you the hands-on finance enthusiast we are seeking? We are looking for a process-oriented and numbers-driven team player to take ownership of our group-wide CAPEX. You will act as the financial architect of our global expansion, working at the intersection of Controlling, Retail, and Office Expansion teams. With your proven experience in accounting and a high affinity for modern cloud-based tools, you will take our CAPEX accounting to the next level.

Your Mission

  • Financial Integrity PPE: Act as the subject matter expert for the lifecycle of all fixed assets. This includes determining initial recognition, identifying subsequent expenditures that should be capitalized vs. expensed, and managing complex depreciation models and impairment testing.
  • Financial Integrity IFRS 16: Lead the accounting for the global lease portfolio. This involves ensuring the accurate calculation of Right-of-Use assets and lease liabilities, managing lease modifications (extensions, terminations, or rent concessions), and ensuring all disclosures meet the latest IFRS standards.
  • Operational Excellence: Oversee daily operations, including additions, disposals, depreciation, and re-measurements.
  • Global Connectivity: Act as the primary contact for all CAPEX-related requests, bridging the gap between global standards and local needs.
  • External Partner Management & Mentorship: Act as the primary point of oversight and quality control for our outsourced accounting provider. You will be responsible for reviewing their deliverables, and mentoring their team members to ensure alignment with our global standards and quality requirements.
  • Reporting & Compliance: Ensure accurate disclosure in our consolidated IFRS Financial Statements and oversee internal SOX controls.
  • Process Innovation: Actively challenge and automate global processes using our digital toolkit.
  • Strategic Documentation: Create and maintain robust process documentation and user guidance to support a scaling organization.

Your Story

  • Education: Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Accounting and Finance.
  • Experience: 4+ years of practical experience within a large-scale or international corporate environment with a focus on IFRS and preferably CAPEX.
  • Knowledge: Solid understanding of IFRS, with specific, hands-on experience in IAS 16 and IFRS 16. You should be comfortable navigating complex lease contracts and determining the correct accounting treatment for diverse global asset classes in autonomy.
  • Effective Communication: Ability to navigate a matrix organization, effectively communicating complex financial concepts to stakeholders at all levels
  • Tech Savvy: You are a power user of MS Excel and Google Workspace. Experience with Dynamics 365 is a plus.
  • Modern Toolkit: Familiarity with tools that drive automation and leverage AI to optimize financial workflows.
  • Mindset: A hands-on mentality with the ability to adapt to a constantly changing environment.
  • Soft Skills: Exceptional analytical skills and business proficiency in English.

Meet The Team

Join an international finance team that acts as both the backbone and the advanced navigator of our speedboat. We constantly challenge the status quo to handle rapid growth. We shape tomorrow’s best practices by thinking differently, acting cross-functionally, and leveraging a cutting-edge tech stack.

What We Offer

A dynamic environment ranked as a "fastest-growing sports company" where your work directly impacts our global footprint. A culture that fosters excellence and potential. This role offers deep exposure to strategic planning and technical knowledge growth. The chance to build something incredible with a collaborative, high-energy global community where you can truly thrive.

If you are driven to bring On to the next level, curious about the opportunity and want to be part of our growing team send us your application, and join the ONiverse!
