Full description

As a leading Swiss construction and real estate service provider, Implenia develops, realizes and manages living spaces, working environments and infrastructure for future generations in Switzerland and Germany. Implenia also offers tunnelling and related infrastructure projects in other markets. The company combines the know-how of highly qualified consulting, development, planning and execution units under one roof into an integrated, multinationally leading construction and real estate service provider and employs around 9,000 people throughout Europe. More information at implenia.com.

For our bridge construction in the Special Competence Team we are looking for a dedicated project manager teaching scaffolding and bridge construction (m/f/d). Your place of employment is our headquarters in Opfikon.

You move that

  • Independent offer processing of teaching scaffolding offers and acquisition of teaching scaffolding contracts
  • Project management in work preparation and execution of teaching scaffolding or auxiliary bridges
  • Interface planning and coordination of various trades, subcontractors and project participants
  • Enforcement of occupational safety and quality management
  • Responsibility for Profit + Lost of own projects
  • Resource and deployment planning with securing deadlines
  • Personnel management (construction manager, polisher & GWP) on projects
  • Cooperation with local partners, subcontractors, suppliers, planners and authorities
  • Competent contact for builders and business partners and employees
  • Supplementary management, independent preparation and enforcement of supplementary offers
  • Supervision of contract management
  • Financial and technical project controlling

This is why you succeed

  • Completed structural training (civil engineer, master builder, construction manager)
  • Experience in planning and execution in teaching scaffolding
  • Basic understanding of statics and steel construction
  • Comprehensive knowledge in the construction industry as project manager / construction manager
  • Major project experience and references in infrastructure construction
  • Leadership experience and strong social skills
  • Negotiation skills with a commercial focus
  • Team spirit
  • Customer proximity and customer-oriented thinking with strong practical relevance
  • Analytical and communicative skills, number flair and networked thinking and acting
  • High sense of responsibility, independent working methods and resilience
  • Very good knowledge in MS Office, MS Project, Thilos of advantage, industry software such as Ababau / Baubit / SORBA / SAP

This is what we offer you

  • Modern, innovative, sustainable and young working environment
  • Use of modern technologies
  • Support for employee development, promotion of training
  • Flexible working hours
  • Implenia-Benefits, including (to name a few):
  • Implenia Provision: A company-owned pension fund with very good coverage and three selectable contribution levels
  • 5 weeks of holidays plus pre-made bridge days
  • numerous benefits and discounts on well-known suppliers of products and services
