Original description

Canton of Zurich Prison and Reintegration Staff Members: in Zurich Prisons of Detention (UGZ) 80 - 100% Prison and Reintegration (JuWe), as part of the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs of the Canton of Zurich, is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as the execution of the various types of detention. For our 1,400 employees, the successful social reintegration of people who have committed crimes and the prevention of recidivism are of particular importance. The Zurich Detention Centers (UGZ) are one of JuWe’s eight main departments, comprising seven companies with around 700 prison places and around 500 employees. They are responsible for provisional arrest, pre-trial detention and security detention, as well as the premature execution of penalties and measures until transfer to a prison institution. In this way, they fulfil a central function within the Zurich justice system.
