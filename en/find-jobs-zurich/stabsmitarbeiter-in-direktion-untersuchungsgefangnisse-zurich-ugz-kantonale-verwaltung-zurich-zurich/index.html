Role overview

Canton of Zurich Prison and Reintegration Staff Members: in Zurich Prisons of Detention (UGZ) 80

100% Prison and Reintegration (JuWe) is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as the implementation of the various types of detention as part of the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs of the Canton of Zurich.

For our 1,400 employees, the successful social reintegration of people who have committed crimes and the prevention of recidivism are of particular importance.
