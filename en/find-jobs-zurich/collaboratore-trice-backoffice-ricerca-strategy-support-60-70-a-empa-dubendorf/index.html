Full description

Materials science and technology are our passion. With our cutting-edge research, Empa’s approximately 1,100 employees make decisive contributions to the benefit of society for a future worth living in. Empa is a research institution of the ETH Domain. Employee Research & Strategy Support 60-70% (a) Research & Strategy Support supports our management in central issues relating to Empa’s research activities and works closely with the research departments. Our group’s diverse range of tasks includes research funding, science reporting, supporting research projects and helping to shape strategically relevant initiatives. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed and initiative personality with a focus on the following areas of responsibility. Their tasks

  • Recording and processing of project applications of Empa researchers as well as ensuring high data and document quality in close cooperation with applicants
  • Organisation and administrative support of internal grant invitations – from receipt of applications to preparation for evaluation to communication of grant decisions
  • Preparation, implementation and follow-up of meetings and workshops
  • Support of the central mailboxes and the Empa portal as well as ensuring professional and service-oriented support
  • Participation in the further development and automation of administrative processes to increase efficiency
  • Support for the implementation and application of AI-powered solutions and automation tools (e.g. Claude, Microsoft Copilot, Power Automate) Your profile
  • Commercial basic training, ideally with a vocational degree or graduate degree, at most FH/HF degree in economics, business administration or similar
  • Interest in research and science management
  • Practical knowledge in the use of AI applications and digital automation solutions or great motivation to develop further in this area
  • Experience in a demanding administrative function and in the research, university or innovation environment is of great advantage
  • Pronounced service orientation and pleasure in contact with different stakeholders
  • Independent, structured and precise working method
  • High level of teamwork, communication strength and organisational skills
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing Our offer You can expect a varied and responsible activity at the interface of research, strategy and innovation. You will work in a dynamic, international research environment and gain exciting insights into Empa’s diverse research activities. We offer you the opportunity to actively participate in the further development of modern work processes and to help shape the use of AI-supported solutions. You benefit from a collegial working environment, short decision-making paths and a culture that promotes initiative, creativity and personal development. The job is initially limited to one year, with potential for extension or permanent employment. “a” stands for “all” in our job advertisements. We live a culture of inclusion and respect. We welcome all people who have the desire for innovative, sustainable and meaningful activities - only that counts. We look forward to your complete online application incl. Letter of motivation, CV, certificates, diplomas and contact details of two reference persons. Please submit them exclusively via our application portal. Applications by e-mail or by post will not be considered. - Please no applications from agencies.
