Full description

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a dedicated auditor (all). You play a key role in ensuring our IT security and compliance. You check our systems and processes and ensure that they comply with the given standards and regulatory requirements.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Bank, Wallisellen, Industriestrasse 17-19, 8304 Wallisellen

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Planning, control and implementation of IT-related auditing activities in accordance with the annual audit plan in compliance with the relevant guidelines.
  • Responsible for the assessment of the risks inherent in the audit objects and for their management and control.
  • Central contact in the responsible department as well as continuous maintenance and deepening of relevant stakeholder relationships.
  • Responsible for reporting on the audit activities performed (reporting) and for discussing the results with the audited unit (final meeting).
  • Assignment planning, coaching and practical training of the respective test team auditors "on the job" as well as ongoing quality assurance (review) of the work carried out by the test team.
  • Your IT affinity keeps you on the pulse of the times and keeps an eye on current technological developments.

Requirements

  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): CISA, CISM, CRISC, CIA, auditors
  • Broad banking and audit expertise
  • Very good communication skills and high social skills
  • General management skills
  • Leadership experience
  • At least 8 years of professional and/or functional professional experience (including 3 to 5 years in a similar position)
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • French (advantageous)
  • Italian (advantageous)
  • English (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central place of work (GABS): Optimally accessible by train (10 minutes from Zurich HB) or car
  • Child care: up to CHF 300 per month per child to external child care
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Migros Bank: accounts, mortgages and financial services with preferential terms
  • Advantages of cadre: One week holidays per year more for cadre members
  • Du-Culture: Lived team spirit with transparent communication

Contatto

Application and contact Moritz Forrer Team Leader HR Business Partner

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Personality/performance test: Under certain conditions, we use a personality test (BIP, Scales test, etc.) as an additional step to support selection. The results of the test may be discussed personally and form the basis for further selection. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week

Note for personnel service providers For this position, we only consider direct applications.
