Original description

Come and be part of one of the most dynamic areas of the company, with interaction with different areas, people, processes and activities. Here in Operations you will be challenged to innovate and work as a team to create solutions and support improvements to provide a good experience to our customers. About the Role . The Business Services Analyst is responsible for supporting daily business operations, managing administrative and operational processes, ensuring data quality and compliance with internal controls, and identifying opportunities for process optimization and efficiency improvements. Key Responsibilities

  • Support business teams with day-to-day operational and administrative activities;
  • Review, analyze, and process documentation in accordance with internal procedures and quality standards;
  • Act as a key point of contact for internal stakeholders and external partners, ensuring timely and high-quality service;
  • Monitor operational requests and follow up on pending actions to ensure service level commitments are met;
  • Maintain and strengthen operational processes, controls, and governance practices;
  • Analyze workflows and identify opportunities to improve efficiency, productivity, and service quality;
  • Prepare reports, data analysis, and operational metrics to support business decision-making;
  • Participate in projects and continuous improvement initiatives, contributing to process redesign and implementation of best practices;
  • Support system enhancements by gathering business requirements, participating in testing activities, and validating new functionalities;
  • Ensure compliance with company policies, procedures, and regulatory requirements.
