Production staff: 80-100%

2 Spice AG — Dietlikon, Dietlikon, Sizzera

The Yooji's Manufaktur, which belongs to the two spice AG, offers a versatile and exquisite range of Soshi, Sashimi and much more. The production site of the two spice factory is located in Rümlang and employs over 120 employees. Every day various delicacies are prepared and delivered several times a day to Yooji's restaurants and stores. Also specially developed products for our Japanese retail brand ZENBU, are produced in the factory.

ZENBU products such as Sushi, Poké, Onigiri and vegan delicacies are produced for Coop and are available directly from Coop throughout Switzerland. Do you want to become part of our team? Then now is your chance!

Tasks

  • Cutting, filling, packaging and awarding of products according to quality specifications
  • Setting up and preparing the workplace (AVOR)
  • Picking the products after ordering.
  • Performing daily cleaning and cleaning work according to cleaning specifications.
  • Compliance with hygiene quality and safety specifications
  • Maintenance and maintenance of the infrastructure

Requirements

  • Mobility must be available (or live in Rümlang)
  • Standby for night shift: Start of service is either at 11.30, 03.00 or 04.00
  • Work experience in a comparable function in the food industry
  • Reliability
  • Good German skills
  • Clean, accurate and efficient working methods
  • Stress-resistant, resilient and physically fit
