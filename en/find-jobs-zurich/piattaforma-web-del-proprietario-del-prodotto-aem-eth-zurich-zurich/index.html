Product Owner Web Platform (AEM)

60%-80%, Zurich, permanent

The ETH Zurich website is the most important digital communication platform of our university and reaches millions of people worldwide every year. Behind this range is an extensive, multilingual and decentrally managed web and CMS landscape. The Department of University Communication operates and develops these on the basis of Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) together with the internal IT department and external service providers.

To strengthen our web team, we are looking for a tech-savvy personality as a product owner for the further development of our web platform.

Job description

They help shape the roadmap as well as the technical and functional development of our web platform. They prioritize product backlogs based on strategic goals, user needs, and insights from analytics. Translate requirements into epics, user stories and acceptance criteria and coordinate their implementation in the Scrum process.

You work closely with internal and external experts from development, design, communication, analytics, UX and digital accessibility. They test and document CMS components, maintain the web manual and ensure compliance with our quality standards. In addition, you coordinate the release management, communicate with the addressee and advise web managers within the entire ETH Zurich on technical and conceptual issues.

Profile

  • University degree in computer science, business informatics, media informatics, digital product management, in a related discipline – or a comparable qualification
  • At least 3–5 years of experience as a Product Owner (or equivalent) for complex web platforms, ideally with practical knowledge in Adobe Experience Manager (AEM)
  • Security in Scrum and in dealing with Jira, including backlog maintenance, user stories and acceptance criteria
  • Experience in functional testing and technical acceptance of CMS components in different system environments as well as in the coordination of releases
  • Good technical understanding of web architectures, interfaces and deployment processes as well as basic knowledge of HTML and CSS
  • Understanding user-oriented, web-oriented content and editorial processes as well as central quality requirements such as digital accessibility, data protection, information security and web performance
  • Communication strength in exchange with different stakeholders and external service providers as well as very good knowledge of German and English
  • Structured, exact, team- and solution-oriented working method with pleasure in consulting and cooperation
  • Safe and responsible use of generative AI

We offer

  • A responsible and varied activity with room for manoeuvre at the interface of communication and IT
  • A dedicated, collaborative and helpful team in the exciting context of research and teaching
  • An attractive workplace in the center of Zurich
  • Family-friendly working conditions
  • Attractive offers and benefits as well as opportunities for further development
