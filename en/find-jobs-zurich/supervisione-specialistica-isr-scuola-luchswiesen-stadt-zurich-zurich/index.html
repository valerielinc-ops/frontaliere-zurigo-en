Full description

As of 1 January 27 or by appointment we are looking for a specialist care provider Focus ISR for the care of ISR children (in the regular school integrated special pupils) in the care Kronwiesen and Luchswiesen 1.

The 80% workload is spread over four or five working days per week.

Tasks

You accompany and support students with special needs during the school-supervisory care. As a team you are responsible for the promotion and integration of the children. Your tasks include:

  • Care and guidance of children in school care
  • Building a relationship with ISR children and accompanying them in social and professional learning
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with the school staff involved in the funding
  • Parent contacts in consultation with the teachers
  • School route guidance
  • Active participation in the school team
  • Organisational and administrative tasks

Profile

  • Openness and willingness to engage students with special needs
  • Ideally, they already have experience in caring for children with special needs (preferably in the area of learning / behavior / supportive communication)
  • High load capacity in a dynamic field
  • High flexibility in thinking and acting, initiative and teamwork
  • Professional care for EFZ, specialist in childcare or an equivalent recognised training (small children's teacher, social scientist), in the case of foreign diploma degrees, the application documents must be accompanied by Swiss recognition.

We offer

  • A team and a management that are looking forward to seeing you and supporting you
  • A motivated team in which you can contribute your ideas and change wishes
  • A cooperative, appreciative and intensive cooperation throughout the school team
  • A Salary according to the guidelines of the City of Zurich

About us

The Schwamendingen School District covers the entire urban district of 12 of the city of Zurich. Schwamendingen is a lively, multicultural residential district. By public transport we are easily accessible via Stettbach and Oerlikon station with various tram and bus lines.

The school Lynchswiesen is a quiMS school in Zurich-Schwamendingen. It currently comprises five kindergartens, sixteen primary classes and the care area. The school Lynchswiesen has a long tradition as a school club, which offers the students an integrated full-day care.

Interested?

For information on the situation, please contact the management of the Luchswiesen school: Esti Schönberger, esti.schoen hoteler@schulen.zuerich.ch

The applications will only be processed from 19 October.
