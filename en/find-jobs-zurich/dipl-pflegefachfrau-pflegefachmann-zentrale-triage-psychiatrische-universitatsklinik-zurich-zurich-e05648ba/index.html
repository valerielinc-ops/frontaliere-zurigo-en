Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility.

To support the team of the Central Triage at our location Lenggstrasse and the large ambulatorium Im Quadro in Oerlikon, we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by arrangement a dedicated personality as

Dipl. Nursing Professional, Central Triage

80-100%

Their tasks

  • Triagulation of patients to the adapted treatment offer ambulatory, semi-stationary and inpatient
  • Conducting advisory and crisis intervention talks on the telephone and on site
  • Processing requests from internal groups of persons and external assignees
  • Maintaining contacts with internal service providers
  • Close cooperation with triage senior physicians
  • Participation and design for process optimization
  • Completed, recognised vocational training in the field of care HF/FH (certificate of recognition of the Swiss Red Cross for foreign diplomas)
  • Several years of practical experience in psychiatric institutions in Switzerland, preferably in the Canton of Zurich
  • High professional, social and self-competence, high resilience as well as flexibility and analytical thinking
  • Enjoy working with different professional groups
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing, further language skills are advantageous
  • Readiness for night and weekend services
  • Knowledge that advances: Extensive initial, further and further training offers
  • Strong in the team: appreciative cooperation and mutual support
  • Focus on health: Various sports offerings, green oases and an internal care team
  • Sustainable travel: Participation in the ZVV-BonusPass or general subscription
  • Well taken care of in everyday work: discounted food offers or lunch checks
  • Future provision with added value: 60% employer contribution to the pension fund
