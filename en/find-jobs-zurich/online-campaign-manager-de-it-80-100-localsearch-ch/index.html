Full description

localsearch is the leading marketing and advertising partner for Swiss SMEs. We make customers successful in the digital world.

The Online Campaign Management Team is looking for a new team member. Our team is the operational centerpiece in the field of Advertising Products and we ensure that our Advertising products (especially advertiseONE) are efficiently produced, activated and maintained on an ongoing basis. We work closely with sales, product and external partners and drive quality, speed and customer satisfaction in campaign implementation.

We offer you the following exciting tasks

  • Operational Campaign Production & Activation
  • Creation, setup and activation of advertising campaigns on Google, Meta, Youtube, Display etc. via internal and external systems
  • Control of the production process

Processing of production, onboarding and approval cases based on customer feedback and ensuring timely campaign activations

  • Customer interaction along the process

Conducting customer calls (e.g. consultation calls, approval calls or performance calls) to ensure campaign quality and customer satisfaction in German & Italian

  • Cooperation with Sales & Internal Stakeholders

Close coordination with customer consultants: inside and interface to product, technology and external partners to clarify requirements and solve operational issues

  • Quality assurance & troubleshooting

Ensuring correct campaign setups as well as identification and escalation of errors (e.g. data, tracking or system topics)

  • Backlog and case management

Prioritization and structured processing of open productions, releases and blocked cases

  • Administrative and system-side tasks

Maintenance of campaign data and status in CRM systems as well as support for reporting and process requirements

Skills and requirements

You are

  • Structured, operationally strong and you keep the overview even at high volume
  • Solution-oriented and pragmatic in dealing with daily challenges
  • Communicative and secure in exchange with internal stakeholders and customers
  • Responsible and reliable in implementation

You bring with you

  • Interest in digital marketing and ideally a certification for “Google search engines”
  • Experience in operational processes, back office, campaign management or customer service
  • Basic technical understanding and ability to quickly familiarize yourself with new tools (e.g. CRM systems, campaign platforms)
  • Structured and efficient operation, even under time pressure
  • stylish German and good knowledge of Italian
  • Advantageous French skills, basic English skills
  • Commercial education, study or equivalent qualification
