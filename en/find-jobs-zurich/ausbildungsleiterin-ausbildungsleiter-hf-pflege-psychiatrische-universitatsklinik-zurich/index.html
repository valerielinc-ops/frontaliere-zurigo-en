Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich treats and cares for mentally ill people of all ages. The more than 2,500 employees of the 55 different professions make their contribution to the holistic care of patients in interdisciplinary teams, from clarification and therapy to follow-up care. Our work is based on mutual trust and personal responsibility and together we promote an innovative corporate culture. As a university clinic, we are successfully involved in national and international research projects.

Here you not only design education – They are actively developing education. Would you like to contribute new ideas, co-develop innovative learning formats and shape nursing training together with a committed team in a future-oriented manner? Then we look forward to meeting you. For an exciting challenge we are looking for a committed, strategically thinking and creative personality as

Training Director HF Care with responsibility for violence prevention training for trainees and students

80% - 100%

  • Overall responsibility for the training course HF Care (planning, organisation, implementation and quality)
  • Developing innovative educational offerings and learning formats and ensuring practical learning support

  • Responsibility for the internal training programme (LTT) incl. Quality assurance and participation in the further development of the Competence Center Education

  • Cooperation with internal and external educational institutions and representation of the PUK in expert committees
  • Design, implementation and further development of training courses for violence prevention and de-escalation for learners and students in close coordination with APN Violence Prevention
  • degree as a nurse HF/FH
