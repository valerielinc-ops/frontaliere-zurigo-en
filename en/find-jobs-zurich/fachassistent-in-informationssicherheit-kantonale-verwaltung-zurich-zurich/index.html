Full description

Canton of Zurich Financial Directorate Office for Information Security 100% 100% The Office for Computer Science (AFI) of the Canton of Zurich is the central IT competence center of the cantonal administration. We provide the central IT infrastructure as well as canton-wide applications and carry responsibility for information security across several directorates and the State Chancellery. To strengthen our Security Operations Center (SOC), we are looking for 1. 2026 or, by appointment, a committed individual in the field of IT security who is enthusiastic about current developments in cybersecurity and likes to actively participate in modern security solutions. Your tasks

  • Analysis, processing and triage of security incidents as well as support of the CSIRT for security-relevant events
  • Identification, evaluation and correction of technical vulnerabilities incl. Optimisation of monitoring systems
  • Development and further development of use cases for the detection of security incidents in the SIEM
  • Working with the baseline management of security configurations in cloud environments
  • Operation and further development of security tools as well as consulting in cyber defense and prevention
  • Automation and continuous optimization of processes and technical security measures (SOAR)
  • Preparation of technical documentation and training for internal support teams
  • Management and implementation of procurement projects according to HERMES You bring with you
  • Completed degree (FH/HF) in computer science or comparable technical training with relevant professional experience
  • IT security training (e.g. CAS, MAS, CISSP or equivalent) completed or planned
  • Experience in the security environment, especially in SOC operation, is an advantage
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft environments (Windows Server/Client), virtualization and cloud technologies
  • Sound practical experience in network and system security
  • Analytical, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Enjoying the exchange with different stakeholders as well as a pronounced customer orientation
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing, good knowledge of English
  • Readiness for Picetting We offer
  • You can expect a motivated team in an exciting and dynamic environment where you can take on responsibility and actively contribute to the further development of AFI as a modern IT service provider.
  • We offer contemporary employment conditions, attractive training opportunities and a modern workplace in a central location in the middle of the city of Zurich. Do you feel addressed? We look forward to your application. Please note that we only consider online applications. Markus Regensburger, Head of SOC, will be happy to answer your specific questions on tel +41 43 259 42 39.

Pensum: 100%
