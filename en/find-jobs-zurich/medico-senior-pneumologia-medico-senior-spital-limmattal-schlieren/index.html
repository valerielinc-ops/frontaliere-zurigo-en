The Medical Clinic deals with the diagnosis and therapy of a very broad spectrum of diseases. Our team of internists and specialists covers practically the entire spectrum of internal medicine. In view of growing diagnostic and therapeutic advances, we focus on the holistic view of the patient. It is our aim to treat and care for every patient with the greatest possible medical competence and care. In addition: you are exactly right in the LIMMI if a family atmosphere and a modern workplace are important to you.

Direct applications are preferred, external recruitment offices are not treated as a priority.

  • with personal responsibility and the room for manoeuvre in a highly motivated and well-coordinated team in a pleasant working atmosphere
  • in an uncomplicated interdisciplinary cooperation with colleagues in cardiology, radiology, intensive care unit, oncology and other specialists
  • during regular internal training and interdisciplinary patient consultations
