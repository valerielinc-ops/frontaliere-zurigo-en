Full description

As Business Engineer Jira Service Management & Integration, you are the central interface between Category Management, Integration Management and Product Development. You will develop clever, market-driven tools and processes to optimize our data import and export interfaces, and you will also be an Application Manager for Jira Service Management.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Headquarters, Pfingstweidstrasse 60b, 8005 Zurich

Mansioni

  • You are responsible for the technical connection of new suppliers and dealers and, together with Leader Integration Management, accompany the integration process from data analysis to go-live.
  • Web-EDI (partner portal) and EDI data interfaces analyze and optimize for a stable, automated data flow
  • For technical issues and faults in Category Management, you are the contact point and work closely with Integration Management and Product Development on troubleshooting
  • Technical documentation on import processes and interfaces is created and maintained for internal and external stakeholders
  • Optimization potential in existing import processes you identify and drive automation and scale
  • Together with product development and partners, you define technical requirements and ensure implementation along the roadmap
  • As an Application Manager, you are responsible for the configuration, administration and further development of Jira Service Management
  • Workflows, automations, forms and SLAs in the JSM are designed to meet the needs of category management and adjacent teams
  • Collect and prioritize requirements from specialist departments and business engineering teams and implement them independently or with product development
  • New users: you will train, create instructions and ensure high tool adoption and efficient ticket processes
  • Relevant key figures (e.g. throughput times, ticket volume, ticket allocation) you monitor and derive improvement measures
  • You ensure the interface between technical topics and technical requirements of category management – always with a view to the partner perspective
  • Training / workshops you organize and conduct and provide structured documentation
  • You promote knowledge exchange among business engineers beyond the Partner Development Platform

  • 3-5 years: Technical Partner Integration / Interface Management
  • Completed studies (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): Business Informatics, Computer Science, Business Engineering
  • A completed higher education (Bachelor / Master), ideally with a technical or business-related background, you bring with you
  • Several years of professional experience in technical partner integration, interface management or IT business analysis
  • In-depth experience with Jira Service Management (ideally as an administrator) as well as knowledge of workflow configuration and permission management of the Atlassian world set you apart
  • Technical data formats and interfaces (CSV, XML, JSON, APIs) SQL knowledge is an advantage
  • Solution-oriented, independent and self-responsible you work with a high level of organizational and networking capability
  • It is easy for you to communicate technical issues clearly for different target groups

  • Analytically strong and well-organized, you are inspired by enthusiasm and curiosity for new technologies
  • Working independently in a young, energetic team makes you fun
  • Dealing with and coordinating with different contacts within and outside your team corresponds to your nature
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • The willingness to question “as it has always been done” and propose more efficient, automated or effective solutions
  • Identify potential for improvement and implement changes to increase productivity and effectiveness
