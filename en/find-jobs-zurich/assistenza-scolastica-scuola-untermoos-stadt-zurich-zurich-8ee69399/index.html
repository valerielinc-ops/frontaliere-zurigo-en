Full description

To complement our team at the Untermoos Day School, we are looking for a dedicated, flexible and competent school assistant ISR who can handle challenging situations.

Are you an empathetic, open and humorous personality who enjoys working with children, meets challenges with commitment and wants to actively shape everyday school life? Then we look forward to meeting you!

Working hours are currently still planned and are expected to include: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday in the morning (about 8.20 - 12.00) and Monday afternoon (about 13.30 - 15.05) in the ISR setting, possibly Monday noon in the care.

Tasks

  • They support the students in close consultation with the teachers in everyday school life.
  • They actively contribute to a positive, appreciative as well as socially and learning-promoting relationship with and between the students.
  • They accompany and support classes, groups and individual pupils in class and in everyday school life.
  • You work in an ISR setting and accompany students with special support needs.
  • They participate in the supplementary school care.
  • They promote the attention, independence and reliability of the students.
  • They provide maintenance, clean-up and the provision of teaching, play and learning materials.

Profile

  • Enjoy working with children and ideally experience in a school or educational environment.
  • A flexible, committed, organized and team-oriented way of working.
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing.
  • Reliability, responsibility and resilience.
  • An appreciative, open and empathetic attitude in dealing with children.

We offer

We offer you

  • A dedicated, collegial and supportive team.
  • A varied and meaningful activity in a lively school day.
  • Conditions of employment and remuneration in accordance with the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich.

About us

The Zurich-Letzi school district comprises Stadtkreis 9 with the districts Albisrieden, Altstetten and Grünau. In the 16 schools of our rapidly growing school circle, more than 5,000 primary school students are taught and supervised. More than 1,200 employees work for them in our schools in the areas of teaching, care, home service and technology as well as administration. They all work daily for the interests of the students and a smooth school day.
