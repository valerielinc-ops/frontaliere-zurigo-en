Physiotherapist: in Therapy Internal Medicine 80-100 % Do you want to treat patients effectively and develop yourself in a university environment? Zurich, Zurich / Schaffhausen Cooperation Permanent employment 80–100% Job: By appointment General physiotherapy Orthopaedics / Traumatology Internal Medicine / Pneumology / Cardiology / Oncology Contact details Employers University Hospital Zurich Directorate of Human Resources Management (HRM) Rämistrasse 100 8091 Zurich Office description Job title Do you want to treat patients effectively and develop yourself in a university environment? In our committed interprofessional team you treat people with complex clinical pictures and actively shape the further development of physiotherapy. Your main tasks

  • Professional physiotherapeutic care of inpatients: in the fields of cardiology, pneumology, oncology/hematology, infectiology, nephrology and gastroenterology and hepatology
  • Implementation of physiotherapeutic treatments in outpatient setting, especially in patients: in the fields of cardiology, pneumology, oncology/hematology and with metabolic diseases
  • Responsibility for structured electronic documentation as well as the preparation of history and final reports including performance recording
  • Participation in the training and support of students

Your profile

  • You have an education as a physiotherapist: in FH or a comparable foreign degree including SRK recognition
  • You are familiar with the treatment of complex patients: inside and appreciate this work
  • Your expertise, ideally supplemented by further training in dysphagia therapy, respiratory or rehabilitative physiotherapy and lymphology, enriches our interprofessional team
  • You actively tackle challenges, bring flexibility, high social skills and communication strength and shape the knowledge transfer and innovative projects with pleasure

Become part of our team! With us you will meet 29 motivated physiotherapists who work enthusiastically in specialized fields. Here you can contribute your knowledge, develop professionally and create exciting projects together with an open, collaborative team. Want a working environment full of dynamism, joy and team spirit? We look forward to your reinforcement.
