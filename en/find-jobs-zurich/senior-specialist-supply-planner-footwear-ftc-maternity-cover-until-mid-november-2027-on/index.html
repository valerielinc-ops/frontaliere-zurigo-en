Full description

In short As a Senior Specialist - Supply Planner, Footwear, you will be responsible for creating and executing global supply plans to match demand for our footwear. Reporting to the Senior Lead - Supply Planning Footwear, this role is crucial for optimizing inventory and ensuring product availability worldwide.

Your mission

  • Develop and execute supply plans to meet demand for footwear.
  • Manage purchase orders and ensure on-time delivery from suppliers.
  • Monitor inventory levels, identify potential risks, and implement mitigation strategies.
  • Analyze supply chain performance and identify opportunities for continuous improvement.
  • Support cross-functional projects centered around planning, product launch, and operations improvements.

Your story

  • Bachelor's degree in Supply Chain Management, Engineering or a related field.
  • 5+ years of experience in supply planning or supply chain management, preferably within footwear or consumer goods.
  • Proficiency in supply chain planning software (Anaplan is a plus) and advanced Excel skills.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, dynamic environment.
  • Ability to collaborate with multiple stakeholders including supply planning, demand planning, production planning, logistics, etc.
  • Business proficiency in English is required. Other languages are a plus.

Meet the team The Integrated End-to-End Planning team drives scalable growth at On by integrating demand, supply, IBP, and cross-functional data across the entire product lifecycle to power data-driven decision-making. Within this organization, the Supply Planning sub-team converts demand signals into optimized production plans to achieve high on-time delivery (OTIF) and balanced inventory levels at optimal cost. By acting as a collaborative bridge across diverse stakeholders, the team aligns critical planning paths to ensure accuracy, accountability, and seamless execution across all product categories.
