Full description

Region: Fehraltorf

You can sell. Lead too. And you want more?

Then shape the future with us – in a leadership role in which you not only make your team strong, but also actively control your own success.

This is what you bring with you to be successful with us

  • Responsible business development in your sales area. You will set the direction, develop sales strategies, shape success and also sell customized digital marketing solutions yourself.
  • Coaching and leadership: Take your team to the next level, inspire with your expertise and make sure your team gets to work with commitment and enthusiasm and sees challenges as an opportunity.
  • Responsible action: Your appearance is always professional, and you attach great importance to an impeccable reputation. As a leader, you are not only a role model for your team, but also the trusted partner for our customers.
  • Communication strength and role model: You not only speak fluent German, but also master the common means of communication and use them specifically to structure your work and lead your team.
  • Charisma and Leadership: With humor and a strong personality, you ensure that your team enjoys working under your leadership and feels motivated to achieve their goals. You understand how to use your charisma for a positive working atmosphere and strong results.

We offer this for your success

  • Team leadership with room for development: You take over an existing team, develop it further – and contribute to every successful completion of your employees.
  • Attractive remuneration: You will receive a fixed salary with transparent participation in success, a monthly team fee and a communication grant. Good leadership and strong sales performance pay off immediately.
  • Exciting product range: You have a diverse portfolio of digital marketing solutions at your disposal – modern, on-demand and effective.
  • Own sales area: You are responsible for a clearly defined region with loyal existing customers and great potential for new customers.
  • In-depth training and further education: You start with a training course lasting several weeks and are continuously supported by training courses and sales coaching – both professionally and personally.
  • Modern working methods: We promote personal responsibility, flexibility and exchange – with digital tools, regular meetings and short decision-making paths.
  • Flat-rate: With your own vehicle, you are on the road – we compensate you for this timely and uncomplicated.
