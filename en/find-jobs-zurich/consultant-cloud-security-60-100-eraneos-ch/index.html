Full description

Eraneos is an international strategy, transformation and technology advisory group that helps companies thrive in a dynamic digital world. With first-class experts from business and technology, we empower our customers to set new standards for sustainable transformations – from strategy to implementation. Whether in the design of sustainable organizations, the development of new potential through data and AI or the protection of companies through holistic cybersecurity: We deliver results pragmatically and purposefully. Our team of around 1,200 dedicated employees is represented in offices in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, Denmark, Sweden, Austria, the UK, Singapore and the USA and combines global perspectives with strong local roots. Around 450 employees work at the Swiss locations in Zurich, Bern and Lausanne. This is the job - your main tasks

  • Together with your colleagues from the Cyber Security & Privacy Team and the Cloud Consulting Team, you will advise our customers in the field of cloud security and the digital workplace.
  • In challenging consulting projects, you take on responsibility and take care of security analyses, security strategies and concepts. Together with our customers, you design, evaluate or design the most suitable target solution for them.
  • In addition, you will accompany the secure introduction and optimization of cloud services as an expert and advise customers competently on various information security issues for cloud applications.
  • In addition to the project business, you can support us in the further development of our cloud security services or coaching your teammates. This job is for you if
  • You bring a combination of cloud and cyber security skills, as well as the strong will for further education in this environment.
  • You already have conceptual or operational knowledge of a common cloud provider, including application of the security portfolio.
  • You burn for a secure design of digital Switzerland, especially in the cloud environment and your joy in the topic is contagious.
  • You can explain complex solutions to our customers in German or English.
  • You can have a university degree in computer science, business informatics, electrical engineering or physics and a high IT affinity. As an i-top you bring with you
  • Security or cloud certifications (e.g. CISSP, CCSP, CCSS, common AWS / Azure / GCP certifications, etc.).
  • Practical experience in the secure handling of cloud solutions in a large company. Your next step towards a successful application
  • Jeffrey as your contact person is looking forward to receiving your direct application via our website (CV, diplomas and employment certificates in PDF).
