Full description

Become part of our CIVIL team. We manage, develop, design and plan infrastructure projects of all kinds in railway and tunnel construction. We accompany these projects from planning to implementation.

Take on responsibility with us as

PROJECT ENGINEER RAILWAY CONSTRUCTION 80-100% - ZURICH

YOUR TASKS

  • Work as a project engineer for versatile and demanding projects of different sizes in the field of railway construction.
  • Railway civil engineering and track planning in close cooperation with the Supporting Structure Department and interface coordination with the specialist services of railway technology.
  • Support in the construction phase planning for the implementation of complex renovation and new construction projects under operation.
  • Support for BIM projects and creation of BIM models (AutoCAD Civil 3D, Revit)
  • Local and specialist construction management of demanding projects in railway construction
  • Participation in negotiations with the client as well as authorities and construction companies.

YOUR OPPORTUNITIES

  • When you start your career, you will benefit from a mixed, helpful team that will support you professionally and methodically.
  • In our interdisciplinary projects, you will work with numerous departments on a daily basis and quickly find competent contact persons.
  • Targeted personal career and development opportunities (project management, technical expert, construction management and further training opportunities in digital construction.
  • Flexible working hours and the possibility of working from home / mobile working support a healthy work-life balance.
  • Exciting, challenging and complex projects with a wide range of tasks and varied tasks

YOUR PROFILE

  • Completed studies in civil engineering (ETH, EPFL or FH) or graduate technician HF construction planning civil engineering and you may have gained initial experience in an internship.
  • German mother tongue or at least C1 – stylistically confident orally and in writing.
  • Passion for the railway world and enjoyment of the implementation of demanding infrastructure projects
  • Enjoy open communication and teamwork – you actively contribute to a positive, constructive working environment.
  • High motivation to learn new things: Many subject areas (e.g. BIM) are new to career starters. We support you with our expertise and broad experience.
