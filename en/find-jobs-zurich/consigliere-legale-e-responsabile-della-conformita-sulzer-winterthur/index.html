The Legal counsel and Compliance Officer based in AU is responsible for delivering legal services that support business operations and strategic initiatives and ensuring the local operation is compliant with the applicable laws as well as Sulzer internal policies. This role ensures compliance with applicable laws, mitigates legal risks, and provides expert legal counsel across a range of corporate matters. Key Responsibilities
