Welcome to the Medbase headquarters in Winterthur

Our modern office building is located just three minutes from Winterthur train station, in an urban environment that offers ideal conditions for concentrated work and creative collaboration. At our headquarters, around 150 specialists from the areas of IT, HR, marketing and finance work hand in hand to develop innovative solutions together and achieve our corporate goals. With flexible workstations and modern meeting pods, we promote an open and efficient work culture. At Medbase we attach great importance to teamwork and exchange between departments in order to be successful together. Colleagues from our sister companies such as Zur Rose, WePractice or the Dental Center meet here, which further enriches the interdisciplinary exchange.

You can move that

As project manager for strategic business development, you will actively shape the further development of Medbase and ensure that strategic initiatives are implemented effectively. You will work closely with management and the departments and take responsibility for demanding, cross-departmental projects.

  • You lead strategic and cross-departmental projects from conception to sustainable implementation.
  • You develop well-founded analyses, business cases and decision-making principles for management.
  • You coordinate interdisciplinary project teams and successfully bring different stakeholders together.
  • You create transparency about progress, risks and dependencies and ensure goal achievement.
  • You drive forward the further development of structures, processes and methods and promote cross-departmental collaboration.

What you bring with you

  • A completed university degree in business administration, health sciences or a comparable field.
  • Several years of professional experience (at least 3-5 years) in corporate development, strategy or management consulting - ideally in the healthcare sector.
  • Experience in leading and implementing strategic projects as well as managing various stakeholders.
  • Experience in designing and implementing strategic transformation and omnichannel initiatives as well as optimizing patient journeys is an advantage.
  • Strong analytical skills and the ability to present complex issues in an understandable and appropriate manner.
  • A high level of initiative, entrepreneurial thinking and a pragmatic, solution-oriented way of working.
  • Persuasiveness, strong communication skills and a confident demeanor towards managers and decision-makers.
  • Enjoy cross-departmental collaboration and actively shaping changes.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English, both spoken and written. Knowledge of French is a plus.

The Medbase Group is one of the leading companies in outpatient healthcare. This means services from health promotion to medication supply and acute treatment to rehabilitation. The Medbase Group includes the brands Medbase Pharmacies, Zur Rose, zahnarztzentrum.ch, WePractice and Bluecare.
