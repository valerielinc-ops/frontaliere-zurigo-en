The Campus Services department supports teaching, research and administration in the areas of logistics, mobility and events. It also ensures comprehensive information management and orientation at ETH Zurich and is responsible for the retail and catering services.

Do you like to get started, keep an overview even in hectic situations and ensure that everything is available at the right time in the right place?

Then this versatile task could be just right for you. In this position, you are an important hub for transport, furniture services, relocations and warehouse management. With your craftsmanship, your service orientation and your sense of responsibility, you contribute every day to ensuring that office, teaching and laboratory rooms are professionally furnished and operational.

Job description

They independently take over deliveries, transports and the assembly and dismantling of furniture and facilities. You ensure that workplaces, offices and laboratory rooms are professionally furnished and moves and conversions run smoothly. In addition, you take care of the return, reprocessing and storage of furniture and support the sustainable reuse of facilities.

You also play an important role in the warehousing sector: You check incoming goods, receive deliveries, store materials and prepare picked furniture for delivery. In addition, you carry out triage and recycling work, assist with inventories and ensure order and functionality in the warehouse.

Your tasks also include the care, cleaning and maintenance of the vehicles, machines, equipment and tools entrusted to you. You work closely with colleagues as well as internal and external partners and make an important contribution to the success of the team with your solution-oriented and reliable working method.

  • Professional experience in transport, assembly, warehouse management or operational service
  • Good craftsmanship skills and technical understanding
  • Experience in the installation and dismantling of furniture and equipment
  • Understanding floor plans and furnishing plans
  • Driving licence category B and ideally category C
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • High level of independence and organisational capacity
  • Reliable, responsible and careful work
  • Good physical resilience and pleasure in practical work
  • Strong teamwork and collegial behaviour
  • Friendly and professional approach to customers and partners
  • High service and solution orientation

Successful together – with the ETH values

In addition to your professional qualifications, we attach great importance to ETH social and leadership skills. You act responsibly, carry out your tasks reliably, safely and on schedule and handle resources, furniture and work equipment carefully.
