01

Future employer

Our client, a leading company in the field of building services and security systems in the Zurich region, is looking for a committed and experienced Security Project Manager who is passionate about implementing modern security solutions for demanding construction projects.

Do you want to do more than just install systems, and instead plan, manage and successfully implement comprehensive security concepts for buildings? Then you have come to the right place!

02

Your responsibilities

  • Overall project management of security systems in new-build and renovation projects (access control, video surveillance, intrusion detection systems, fire alarm systems, perimeter security, etc.)
  • Creation and coordination of security concepts in close collaboration with clients, architects and specialist planners
  • Technical and commercial responsibility for the projects (schedules, costs, quality)
  • Coordination of internal and external teams as well as subcontractors
  • Technical advice and support for customers throughout the entire project phase
  • Acceptance, commissioning and handover of the systems

04

Your profile

  • Completed training as an HF technician, BSc in building electrical engineering, electrical planner, systems technician or comparable qualification
  • Several years of professional experience as a project manager in the field of security systems / building technology
  • In-depth knowledge of security technology (e.g. VdS, EN standards, SIA standards)
  • Strong organizational skills and enjoyment of managing complex projects
  • Good German language skills (English is an advantage)
  • Driving licence category B (mandatory)

  • Flexible working-time models and a high proportion of home office possible
  • Varied major projects, from modern office buildings and industrial facilities to critical infrastructures
  • Very good professional development opportunities (e.g. VdS certifications, project management)
  • Modern work equipment, company car (also for private use) and advanced infrastructure
  • Collegial team with flat hierarchies and genuine appreciation
  • Very good social benefits and attractive fringe benefits
