Full description

For the cultural percentage of the cooperative Migros Zurich we are looking for an initiative personality with in-depth knowledge of the local music landscape as well as experience in event management. You are responsible for the requests of the music sector and support partnerships. You implement projects according to strategic objectives and take responsibility for the internship.

Mansioni

  • Processing of submitted applications and responsibility for awarding funding in the field of music (all styles)
  • Management and implementation of the annually recurring Summer Night Ball at the HB in Zurich as well as the support, support and development of further events and formats
  • Building, maintaining and developing partnerships
  • Planning and implementation of communication measures
  • Management and participation in various cultural percent projects
  • Responsibility and supervision of the internship

Requirements

  • At least 2-3 years of professional experience: in the field of cultural management, promotion or event management
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): in the field of cultural management, music/arts and cultural education or equivalent
  • Pronounced interest and openness towards different musical styles as well as knowledge of the funding landscape
  • In-depth experience in the field of event organisation, project management and communication
  • In-depth understanding of business processes and relationships
  • Readiness for occasional visits to events and networking in the evening and on weekends
  • Creative, initiative and networked personality with a high sense of quality and responsibility
  • Joy of dealing with different groups of people
  • in German (negotiation certain)
