Full description

PROFILES • Degree in electrical engineering or mechanical engineering (TU/FH) or comparable specialist training • Several years of practical work in the industrial sector, preferably in rail transport • Experience in commissioning, testing and troubleshooting • Experience in preparing, testing and maintaining technical documentation • Experience in requirements management (IBM DNG, PLM or similar) • Knowledge of railway signalling systems (CBTC and/or mainline interlock) is a great advantage • Team player, solution-oriented and flexible for night work and travel • Very good German language skills are absolutely necessary, in addition English and French are of great advantage

TASKS

• Develop, maintain and update T&C procedures, test specifications and workflows to ensure consistent execution • Coordinating, executing and logging system deployments and field tests based on approved on-site test documentation • Management of hardware and software configurations on site, ensuring proper documentation and version control • Recording and analysis of incidents and tracking of mitigation and corrective actions • Troubleshooting and resolving technical issues in close cooperation with engineering, project manager and customer • Planning electrical and mechanical changes and procuring components in coordination with engineering and project manager • Performing or supporting other field activities (tests, limited electrical and mechanical changes and more), including work on tracks • Close cooperation with the customer and the commissioning teams
