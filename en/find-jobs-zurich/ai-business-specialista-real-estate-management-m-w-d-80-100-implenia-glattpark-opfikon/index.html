Full description

Are you looking for a new challenge in the IT environment, are you enthusiastic about the possibilities of AI and would like to use it where it creates real added value? You are an innovative personality, think entrepreneurially, recognize potential for optimization and implement it pragmatically together with the business? Together with the departments, you will drive the digital transformation of the Group and develop innovative solutions with measurable benefits. Then we are looking for you for our newly created position as AI Business Specialist (Real Estate Management) (m/f/d) 80-100%.

Your place of work is our headquarters in the Ambassador House in Opfikon, with the possibility to work partly in the home office.

Tasks

What tasks await you as an AI Business Specialist (Real Estate Management) (m/f/d), Opfikon, 80-100%? • In this role, you develop AI-based solutions for real estate management together with the specialist areas and identify concrete applications with added business value. • You will analyze professional requirements and existing business processes and translate them into technical AI and data solutions. • You design and realize AI use cases independently, from requirements analysis to technical conception and development to implementation in everyday work. • You work hands-on with modern AI technologies and pro-code approaches and use Python and SQL as well as suitable AI models, APIs and platforms. • You work with different data sources, analyze and structure data and develop scalable and practical solutions for real estate management. • As a sparring partner of the departments, you advise internal stakeholders and support them in translating business requirements into suitable technological solutions. • You empower teams through training, workshops and coaching to understand the developed solutions and to integrate them into their daily work. • You bring new ideas, technologies and working methods to the company and promote their meaningful and practical use within the organization. • As a connecting interface between real estate business and technology, you actively contribute to advancing innovative AI solutions in the company and establishing them sustainably.

Requirements

What requirements do you bring with you as an AI Business Specialist (Real Estate Management) (m/f/d), Opfikon, 80-100%? • You have a suitable professional and technical background, for example through relevant professional experience, an education, further education or a study in computer science, business informatics, data science, digital business or a comparable field. • You have profound experience in the development and implementation of AI and data solutions and understand how to translate technical requirements into concrete technical solutions. • You have practical experience with modern AI technologies and pro-code approaches, as well as good programming skills, especially in Python and SQL. • Experience in real estate management or In the real estate industry is an advantage. • You are interested in AI, data and digital innovations and follow current developments with curiosity, openness and enthusiasm. • You think connected and entrepreneurial, recognize opportunities and understand how to translate technological possibilities into concrete solutions with a comprehensible business added value. • You act in an advisory and convincing manner, communicate safely at different levels and can inspire different stakeholders for new technologies and solutions. • You work pragmatically, independently and solution-oriented and enjoy working together with the specialist areas to achieve visible and measurable results. • You have very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing.

About Implenia

As a leading Swiss construction and real estate service provider, Implenia develops, realizes and manages living spaces, working environments and infrastructure for future generations in Switzerland and Germany. Implenia also offers tunnelling and related infrastructure projects in other markets. The company combines the know-how of highly qualified consulting, development, planning and execution units under one roof into an integrated, multinationally leading construction and real estate service provider. Headquartered in Opfikon near Zurich, Implenia employs more than 8,000 people (FTE) across Europe and achieved a turnover of CHF 3.5 billion in 2025. More information at implenia.com.
