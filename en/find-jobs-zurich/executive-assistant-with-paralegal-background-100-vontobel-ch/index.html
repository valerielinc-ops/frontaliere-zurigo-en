Full description

Are you an experienced Executive Assistant who enjoys creating structure, staying ahead of evolving priorities and supporting senior leaders in a demanding environment? We are looking for a proactive, resilient and highly organised professional who acts with confidence, sound judgement and discretion. This is a fully office-based role supporting primarily legal matters, with opportunities to grow into broader business management responsibilities.

What would your role be?

  • Manage complex calendars, meetings and business travel with foresight and discretion.
  • Coordinate management meetings, agendas, documentation and follow-up activities.
  • Anticipate requirements or potential issues and follow through until matters are resolved.
  • Maintain structure and momentum across competing and frequently changing priorities.
  • Handle correspondence, legal documents and confidential information with due care.
  • Act as a trusted point of contact for internal and external stakeholders.
  • Take on broader business management responsibilities as the role develops.

What are we looking for?

  • Experienced Executive Assistant with a strong track record supporting senior management.
  • Legal Assistant or Paralegal background and familiarity with legal topics preferred.
  • Excellent organizational skills and the ability to manage competing priorities.
  • Proactive mindset with sound judgement, discretion and attention to detail.
  • Strong communication skills in German and English, both written and spoken.
  • Willingness to work from our Zurich office five days per week.
  • Affinity for Artificial Intelligence (AI) and a willingness to embrace AI-enabled tools and ways of working.

What do we offer?

  • Top Zurich location with collaboration spaces and free lunch.
  • Friendly, diverse team in an open-office space.
  • All the latest technology to help you excel and advance.
  • Agile environment in how we think, the way we work and our trust for each other.
  • Flat hierarchy with collaboration at all levels.
  • Campus feeling and a collaborative spirit.
  • Varied role with close exposure to senior management and legal business priorities.
  • Opportunity to grow and incorporate further business management responsibilities.

We are looking for talented people, not just a CV that ticks all the boxes. Even if you do not have absolutely everything we have asked for, if this role sounds like a good fit for you, we encourage you to apply.
