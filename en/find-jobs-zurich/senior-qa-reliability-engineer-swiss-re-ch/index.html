Full description

Are you looking for an exciting opportunity in Cloud Infrastructure Engineering ? Are you passionate about everything Azure Cloud? Then join us as a Senior QA & Reliability Engineer About the Role As a Senior QA & Reliability Engineer, you will be at the heart of Swiss Re's cloud transformationjourney — embedding quality and reliability into every layer of our Azure cloud platform. This isnot a siloed QA role. You will think holistically, connecting testing, monitoring, and observabilityas interconnected disciplines that drive real engineering excellence. You will work in a fast-paced,cross-functional environment, partnering with platform, product, and delivery teams to ensure ourcloud solutions are secure, compliant, and built to last.

Your main responsibilities

This role requires curious, driven, and collaborative engineer who takes pride in building systems that are not only functional but truly dependable. You bring a holistic mindset to reliability - thinking about quality, monitoring, and testing as interconnected disciplines rather than silos. You communicate clearly, thrive in cross-functional environments, and are energized by solving complex technical challenges with real business impact.

  • Define and execute test strategies (integration, performance, reliability testing)
  • Build and maintain automated test frameworks and CI/CD quality
  • Ensure end-to-end functional and non-functional validation (NFRs)
  • Drive test coverage, defect analysis, and root cause resolution
  • Establish quality metrics and reporting frameworks
  • Proven experience with Microsoft Azure - including Azure Monitor, Application Insights, Log Analytics, and related observability and reliability services
  • Strong background in reliability or quality engineering - with hands-on experience designing and executing testing strategies (integration, performance, chaos/resilience testing) in cloud-native environments
  • Solid expertise in monitoring and observability - including the design of logging frameworks, metrics pipelines, distributed tracing, and alerting systems at scale
  • Embed quality and reliability practices into SDLC and DevOps pipelines
  • Mentor engineers and uplift QA/SRE practices across teams
  • Partner with product, platform, and delivery teams to align quality with business goals

About You

The following proven technical skills will be required

  • Deep hands‑on knowledge of Azure
  • Hands-on expertise in designing app workload using Cloud Platform Services (SaaS, and PaaS).
