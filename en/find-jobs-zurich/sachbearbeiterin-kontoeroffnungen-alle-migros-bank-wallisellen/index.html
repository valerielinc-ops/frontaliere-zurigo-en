Full description

With us you can take responsibility for the clean and forward handling of all account openings. We are looking for you: a personality that always keeps an overview. Come on – at the bank, which together with around 1,800 employees has been putting people first since 1958.

Mansioni

  • You are responsible for the processing of account and custodian openings on the correspondence and digital way incl. Special products such as Migros-Personalkonti, 3rd pillar-conti and rental deposit-conti responsible
  • You are responsible for the control of customer documents and for carrying out the necessary corrections of faulty basic documents
  • You answer 2nd level inquiries from our customers and have written and telephone contact with our customer advisors and other internal offices

Requirements

  • min. 2 years: in material processing
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): Commercial education or training
  • French (very good knowledge) Italian (advantageous): This position necessarily requires good to very good oral French skills
  • You have an exact, forward-looking and independent working method
  • You enjoy administrative activities and work efficiently and accurately even under high workload
  • You have an experienced handling of PC programs and have a technical flair

Cosa of friamo

  • Central place of work (GABS): Optimally accessible by train (10 minutes from Zurich HB) or car
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Child care: up to CHF 300 per month per child to external child care
  • Migros Bank: accounts, mortgages and financial services with preferential terms
  • Employee offers: Annual generous well-being credit
