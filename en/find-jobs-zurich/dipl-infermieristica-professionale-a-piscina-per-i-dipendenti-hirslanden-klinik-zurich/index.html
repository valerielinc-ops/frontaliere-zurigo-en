Full description

Location: Klinik Hirslanden and Klinik im Park

Occupation by: Immediately or by agreement

Reference number: 15521

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

Would you like to take on an independent, demanding task in a modern and dynamic clinic? We address initiative and responsible personalities who enjoy holistic care and first-class individual care. You determine your service assignments and your monthly workload according to your needs. Your employment is done by agreement in the hourly wage.

YOUR TASKS

Klinik Hirslanden strengthens its nursing know-how in highly specialized medicine. For the Employee Pool in Zurich, we are looking for motivated and committed employees in the fields of heart medicine, neurosurgery, neurology including stroke, visceral surgery, urology, orthopaedics and oncology.

Are you looking for an independent and demanding task with the opportunity to adapt your roster to your needs? In the employee pool, you determine your service assignments and your monthly workload according to your needs, tailored to the needs of the department. The employment takes place after agreement in the hourly wage. Assignments are possible in the clinic Hirslanden and in the clinic Im Park.

  • Both competent and individual care and care of our patients taking into account our nursing mission statement
  • Independent implementation of the diagnostic as well as therapeutic measures prescribed by the doctor in cooperation with our specialist health staff
  • Recognize and control emergency situations and initiate adequate measures until the arrival of the doctor
  • Assuming responsibility for the correct patient documentation as well as information sharing in the assigned task area
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration in the team with our observing physicians and other clinical areas

Your profile

  • Completed training for the Dipl. Nursing professional
  • Professional experience and/or training in acute care
  • Very good knowledge of German (verbal and written)
  • Enjoy working in different teams
  • High social competence and customer orientation

In addition to an attractive workplace in the Klinik Hirslanden and Klinik In the park, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. You can expect extensive training and development opportunities from us. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family responsibilities. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. As an employee who has to move completely for their work, you receive four additional paid days off (except specialists and cadre doctors) for a 100% workload. With a reduced workload, the days off reduce accordingly. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from an equivalent financial compensation. These are just a few reasons why the Hirslanden Private Hospital Group is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.

For additional information, Eveline Haussener and Tanja Brüggemann, Heads of Employees-Pool Platz Zurich, under T or Janine Moschetto, Team Leader HR Recruitment Partner Platz Zurich, under janine.moschetto@hirslanden.ch are at your disposal. We look forward to your contact.
