Welcome to the Medbase headquarters in Winterthur

Our modern office building is located just three minutes from Winterthur train station, in an urban environment that offers ideal conditions for concentrated work and creative collaboration. At our headquarters, around 150 specialists from the areas of IT, HR, marketing and finance work hand in hand to develop innovative solutions together and achieve our corporate goals. With flexible workstations and modern meeting pods, we promote an open and efficient work culture. At Medbase we attach great importance to teamwork and exchange between departments in order to be successful together. Colleagues from our sister companies such as Zur Rose, WePractice or the Dental Center meet here, which further enriches the interdisciplinary exchange.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed, independent personality as a paralegal. The position is broad and includes a diverse range of tasks with a focus on data protection and compliance with the opportunity to further develop legally.

Your tasks

Data protection

  • Preparation and negotiation of order data processing agreements
  • Conduct data protection impact assessments
  • Processing requests for information and complaints
  • Further development and maintenance of the data protection organization

Compliance

  • Implementation of group-wide compliance requirements
  • Support with business partner audits
  • Ensuring and further developing the compliance framework
  • Creation of reports as well as training and working documents
  • Maintaining the Legal & Compliance intranet area

Contract management

  • Classification and structured storage of contracts in the tool
  • Training and support for internal users

Corporate housekeeping

  • Support in corporate law issues
  • Preparation of corporate legal documents

Your profile

  • Several years of experience (ideally more than 4 years) in a law firm or in-house legal department
  • Paralegal training an advantage
  • Experience in data protection and/or compliance is an advantage
  • Stylish German, knowledge of French is an advantage

What is important to us

  • Structured, reliable and independent way of working
  • Taking responsibility and proactively driving forward issues
  • Hands-on, pragmatic and solution-oriented way of working

What awaits you

  • Lots of personal responsibility and scope for creativity
  • Opportunity for professional development and greater legal input
  • Broad and varied range of topics
  • Flat hierarchies in a small, efficient team
  • Working in an attractive, growing healthcare industry with a leading provider
