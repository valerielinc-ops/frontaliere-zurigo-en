Doctoral Position in Lower-Field Cardiac Magnetic Resonance Imaging

100%, Zurich, fixed-term print Drucken

The CMR group at the Institute for Biomedical Engineering develops Magnetic Resonance (MR) technology and methods to assess the cardiovascular system.
