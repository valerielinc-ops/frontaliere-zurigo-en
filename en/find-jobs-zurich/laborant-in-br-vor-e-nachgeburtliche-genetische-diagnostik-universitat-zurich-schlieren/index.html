Institute of Medical Genetics

Job opening by agreement

The Institute of Medical Genetics of the University of Zurich is involved in research and teaching as well as in the service for patients with congenital diseases and comprises about 80 employees. To support the team of the accredited diagnostic laboratory, we are looking for a:n laboratory employee:in.

Tasks

  • Extraction of DNA or RNA from blood or other materials
  • Various DNA-based specialised studies including high-throughput sequencing for non-invasive prenatal testing and preimplantation diagnostics
  • Various cell culture-based genetic tests with microscopic evaluation
  • Quality control and evaluation of analysis results
  • Documentation, research, preparation of findings
  • General laboratory activities

Profile

  • Training as a laboratory assistant:in or BMA
  • Team capable, conscientious and resilient
  • Experience with molecular genetic or cytogenetic techniques would be beneficial
  • Motivation to learn new techniques is an important requirement
  • Good knowledge of German and English is an advantage
