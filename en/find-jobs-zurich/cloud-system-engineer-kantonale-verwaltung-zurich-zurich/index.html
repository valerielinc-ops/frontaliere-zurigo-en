Full description

Our cloud platform is growing rapidly: With increasing Azure, Power Platform and automation projects, we are looking for you as a cloud system engineer to further develop, automate and operate our Microsoft Azure environment. Your tasks:

  • Development and operation of our Microsoft Azure Cloud Platform (IaaS/PaaS) – sustainable and stable
  • Accompaniment of cloud onboardings and migrations to the Azure environment with focus on the Power Platform
  • Design of Azure solution architectures and active support for their implementation in the ICT landscape
  • Automation and optimization of installation, transition and operational processes (e.g. through scripting)
  • Processing of 2nd and 3rd level incidents as well as participation in incident, problem and change management (ITSM)
  • Ensure stable operation incl. Implementation of recovery and restart procedures for Azure services
  • Testing and integration of cloud data and application solutions in compliance with security and platform specifications
  • Support for DR/BCM measures as well as for provider reviews
  • Close cooperation with engineers, architects and specialists from different departments and directorates Your profile:
  • Higher degree at tertiary level in computer science or equivalent education
  • Several years of professional experience in cloud engineering and operation (ideally experience with the power platform)
  • Very good knowledge of Microsoft Azure (IaaS/PaaS); Basic knowledge of AWS or Google Cloud is an advantage
  • Experience in operations (monitoring, stability, security) as well as in architecture design and ITSM processes
  • Secure development with DevOps methods and scripting/automation (e.g. Bicep)
  • Advantages: Microsoft certifications in the Azure area
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Readiness for Pikett assignments Your contact For technical questions, Patrick is at your disposal. If you have any questions about the recruitment process, please contact Julia.
