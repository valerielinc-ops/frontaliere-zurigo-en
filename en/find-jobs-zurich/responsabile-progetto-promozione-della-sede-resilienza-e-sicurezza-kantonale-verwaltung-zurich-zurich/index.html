Full description

In the Office for Economic Affairs (AWI), we are committed to an attractive, leading and future-oriented canton of Zurich. In the interest of companies and the population, we identify economic action needs and make effective contributions to the sustainable development of Zurich as a business location. Our core services are aimed at creating optimal economic conditions and at the same time implementing applicable law in the interests of the legislator. The area of location promotion strengthens Zurich as a business, research and innovation location. We network stakeholders from the economy, science and politics, support business relocations and provide important impetus for future-oriented location development. In the context of increasing hybrid threats, you build up the competence area of „Resilience and Security within the area of location promotion and strengthen the innovation and research capacity of the canton of Zurich. With a large scope of design, you develop offerings and measures in the security policy area for business and society.
