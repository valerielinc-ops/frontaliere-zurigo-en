Full description

You are an organizational talent. Order is important to you and that you have the overview of the stockpiles at all times. You like to go and make sure that goods are in the right place at the right time.

Tasks

  • You take goods, control and store them.
  • You organize delivery papers and execute orders.
  • You pack and load all goods professionally.
  • You use the lift truck and other transport vehicles.
  • You assign storage spaces, print orders and show transport routes.

Profile

  • You have successfully completed compulsory schooling.
  • Your way of working is characterized by independence and a customer-oriented way of working.
  • Your craftsmanship is characterized by precision and creativity.
  • With your concentration and reliability, you master every challenge.
  • Thanks to your robust health, you are ready to handle physically demanding tasks.

We offer

You can expect a well-founded and varied education. Your apprentices support you on your way to a successful teaching degree. You benefit from attractive employment conditions.

  • Community with 40 learners in 12 professions
  • 5 weeks holidays and 6 business holidays
  • Contribution to your public transport ABO, the school teaching materials and the food costs
  • Individual support (both school and company) and preparation courses for the final examinations
  • Possibility of a professional experience year after successful completion of the apprenticeship

About us

ewz has been dynamic and visionary on the road every day for more than 130 years. Whether sustainable energy production, smart energy solution or innovative networking technology: Develop with us today what is important tomorrow.
