Set new standards and shape the future together – that means more for us than simply to work out tasks.   • Workplace: Dietlikon Requirements:

  • You have ideally already gained professional experience in the insurance industry, ideally in sales – your knowledge and practice make you a strong partner for our team.
  • Experience in training, training or coaching is beneficial – so you can actively contribute your skills and commitment to the further development of our partners.
  • You convince people with your personality and your skills in communication, presentation and moderation.
  • You are very enthusiastic about people and you are able to communicate with them.

  • Very good German language skills in word and writing are among your strengths – you communicate clearly, precisely and confidently.
