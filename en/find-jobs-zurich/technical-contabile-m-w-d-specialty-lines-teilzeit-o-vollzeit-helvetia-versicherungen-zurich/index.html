Full description

Finance / Controlling / Audit Pensum: 50% - 100% Place of work: Level of experience: With professional experience FlexOffice: on site Understand the numbers. Designing processes. International insurance contracts. Do you want to use your accounting know-how where international insurance contracts, complex payment flows and modern financial processes come together? In this multifaceted professional role, you will assume responsibility for the technical accounting of Helvetia Specialty Lines CH & International. You ensure reliable financial processing, create transparency for informed decisions and develop our processes and systems together with underwriting, claims and finance. In this way, you make a direct contribution to the quality and sustainable success of our international insurance business.

What you do

  • You ensure that insurance premiums, duties and contract data are fully, correctly and on time in the SAP FS-RI subbook.
  • You control the receivables management in SAP FS-CD, assign payment receipts, balance open items and accompany the payment processing.
  • You check invoices and Bordereaux for accuracy and conformity with the contract and clarify inconsistencies directly with our internal and external partners.
  • You vote on side and general ledgers, check the data flows from the pre-systems and support quarterly and annual accounts as well as audits.
  • You will analyse open positions, cash flows, contract data and underwriting results and provide meaningful information for reporting and management decisions.
  • You are responsible for the correct reporting and payment of relevant insurance taxes in different countries and ensure compliance with applicable sanctions.
  • You contribute your expertise to system, process and automation projects, accompany tests and migrations and consistently implement identified improvement options.
  • Place of work: Basel or Stettbach possible in combination with Flex-Office.

What you bring

  • You have completed a degree in business administration, accounting or a comparable qualification.
  • You have several years of professional experience in technical accounting, insurance accounting or in a comparable financial function.
  • You are familiar with IFRS 17 and/or local accounting rules.
  • Knowledge of SAP FS-RI and SAP FS-CD are a plus. Much more important is your sense of numbers and your motivation to become deeply involved in our systems and processes.
  • You think analytically, work carefully and find pragmatic solutions – even if it becomes dynamic, you set the right priorities.
  • You like to tackle challenges, represent your professional point of view convincingly and work constructively and team-oriented at the same time.
  • You have very good Excel knowledge and experience in data analysis, ideally you have already participated in testing, migrations, automation or process improvements.
  • You communicate in German and English safely in word and writing and also bring complex topics clearly and understandably to the point. Other languages are a plus.
