About the role Sulzer is a global leader in fluid engineering with headquarters in Winterthur, Switzerland. Since 1834, we have been developing innovative solutions for pumping, mixing and separating liquids. With around 13,800 employees and over 180 locations worldwide, we stand for quality, customer proximity and technical excellence. Our services At more than 100 service locations, Sulzer offers comprehensive maintenance, repair and spare parts solutions for pumps, turbines, compressors and other rotating machines – independent of the manufacturer.
