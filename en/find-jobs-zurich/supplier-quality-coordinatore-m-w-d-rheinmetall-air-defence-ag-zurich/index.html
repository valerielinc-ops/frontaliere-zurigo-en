WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

  • Execution of communication of supplier error messages to the supplier
  • Prioritize the processing of supplier error messages according to urgency
  • Creation, distribution and processing of error reporting measures
  • Coordination with suppliers, internal error coordinators, buyers and other internal stakeholders on supplier error messages and the resulting measures
  • Support in tracking measures from supplier workshops and audits

WHAT TO BRING

  • Successfully completed technical apprenticeship
  • First professional experience in material management/supplier management, ideally in industry
  • Good negotiation skills
  • SAP knowledge is an advantage
  • Strong organizational skills
  • Strong communication skills, intercultural competence and enjoyment of teamwork
  • Very good written and spoken German skills as well as good English skills

WHAT WE OFFER YOU

  • A dynamic working environment with exciting projects
  • Flexible working hours (flexitime and trust-based working hours)
  • Very good social benefits and an extra-mandatory pension fund
  • Employee benefits of a large corporation, employee share purchase program
  • Promotion of needs-based further training
  • Staff restaurant as well as external catering and shopping facilities nearby
  • Individual internal and external personnel development measures in cooperation with our Rheinmetall Academy
  • Workplace within walking distance of Zurich-Oerlikon train station

CONTACT DETAILS Responsible contact: Jascha Köster If you have any questions about your application, please use the contact form . We would like to point out that dossiers of recruiters are not taken into account.
