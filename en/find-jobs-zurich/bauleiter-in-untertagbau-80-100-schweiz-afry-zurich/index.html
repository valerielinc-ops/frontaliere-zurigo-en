Full description

Become part of our team CIVIL. We manage, develop, design and plan infrastructure projects of all kinds in civil engineering and tunnelling. We accompany these projects from planning to implementation.

Take responsibility with us as

BAULEITER: IN SUBTAGBAU 80-100% - SWITZERLAND

YOUR TASKS – Bringing TECHNIK into PRAXIS

  • Part of the local construction management of demanding underground mining projects and support of the implementation directly on site
  • Coordination of work on the construction site and control of its professional execution
  • Compliance with deadlines and contractual requirements in view and responsibility for acceptance and guarantee work
  • Care of several construction sites simultaneously
  • Bringing experience from execution to project planning and project management
  • Use of digital working methods such as “BIM2field” on the construction site and connection of planning and execution

YOUR HANCES – MEMBERS AND RECOMMENDS

  • Challenging projects: Active participation in large underground mining projects in Switzerland and experience how planning becomes infrastructure
  • Professional exchange: part of an interdisciplinary construction management team and linking different perspectives to practical solutions
  • Digital construction practice: training in “BIM2field” and application of knowledge in your projects
  • Concrete perspective: Possibility to develop towards chief construction management
  • Planning and construction site: Depending on the application and interest, activities in construction management and project planning can be combined

YOUR PROFIL – TECHNIQUED, PRACTICAL ORientated

  • Training as a technician:in TS, civil engineer:in FH/ETH or equivalent qualification
  • Several years of experience in the construction management of tunnel projects in Switzerland as well as knowledge of project planning
  • Interest in technical connections and their practical implementation on the construction site
  • Independent and reliable working methods, customer-oriented thinking and assumption of responsibility
  • Clear communication and enjoyment of working together in a team
  • Secure application of MS-Office programs
  • Knowledge of "Messerli BAUAD" is an advantage
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing (min. C1), knowledge of English, French or Italian are welcome
