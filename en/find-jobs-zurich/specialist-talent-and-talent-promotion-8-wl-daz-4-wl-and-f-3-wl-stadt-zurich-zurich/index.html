Full description

Around three hundred and forty pupils attend the school Hirschengraben in the old city centre of Zurich. We have a kindergarten, four primary classes and nine secondary classes. The classes of the primary school are taught in age-mixed groups. The new concept of talent and talent promotion (https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/en/bildung/volksschule/support-foerderung/begabungsfoerderung.html) will be introduced in stages at all elementary schools in the city of Zurich from school year 2023/24.

Our school is looking for our age-mixed primary classes by 1. August 2026 Reinforcement.

Tasks

  • They teach in a strengthening-oriented manner in the existing gifted and gifted promotion of the school and support the teachers in the implementation of individualizing teaching.
  • A cooperative cooperation with the primary team, external specialists, parents and the authorities is a matter of course for you. They advise school team and parents dealing with gifts.
  • They participate in school conferences, in-school training and team events and actively participate in school development in order to advance the BBF area.
  • They design the internal school pull-out program (POP) in the BBF team and teach children with high skills and great interests in various areas.
  • They supervise appropriate learning materials and BBF resources.

Profile

  • Teaching diploma (EDK recognized) for elementary school
  • Preferably a CAS in integrated talent and talent promotion (CAS BBF) or the willingness to complete this training immediately
  • Knowledge of the forms of educational, gift-specific diagnostics
  • Experience in teaching and school development with involvement of the school team and parents
  • Of course in dealing with heterogeneity and integration
  • Enjoyment of working with children and individual, strengthening-oriented talent promotion and talented promotion as well as the willingness to further develop one’s own teaching

We offer

A challenging task in a versatile and helpful team, in which the teaching staff work closely together with each other and with the support. We attach importance to a solution- and goal-oriented cooperation as well as the development and development opportunities of the individual employees. We achieve this through a committed school management, a well-organized school secretariat and an established cooperation with the school authority. Our school is well connected to the public transport network and is very centrally located.

About us

The Zurichberg School District comprises districts 1, 7 and 8 with the districts Altstadt, Fluntern, Hottingen, Hirslanden, Riesbach and Witikon. The district school authority manages 17 school houses. These include 44 kindergartens and over 40 care facilities of different sizes. Of the 13 organizational units, 9 are day schools.
