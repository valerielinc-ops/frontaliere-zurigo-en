Full description

The Swiss family business, founded in 1836, is now one of the most renowned confectioneries in Europe with its exquisite range. The products from Sprüngli stand for the best quality, unique freshness and naturalness. The perfect creations delight customers from all over the world every day.

Confectioner-Confectioner*in Deputy Head of Department / Decor Department (100%)

100%, Dietikon

Working at Sprüngli

In our factory in Dietikon we are looking for an experienced, motivated and committed person for the Decor department as of December 1, 2026 or by appointment

What you do with us

You work in the Decor department in our factory in Dietikon and produce our sophisticated range as a team. Your regular working hours usually extend from Monday to Friday from 5:30 a.m. to around 2:45 p.m. (workload).

Your tasks

  • Production of special decor according to customer requirements
  • Representation of department performance
  • Manufacture of various special items
  • Inserting special cakes
  • Making and garnishing seasonal items
  • Writing from Schildli
  • Modeling various marzipan figures

What we wish for

  • Apprenticeship qualification as a confectioner EFZ or EBA
  • Ideally, first experience or recognizable potential to take on the role of deputy head of department
  • Enjoy the job you have learned and enjoy working in a team
  • Decor talent and a strong flair for colors and shapes
  • Consistent compliance with our hygiene regulations
  • An exact and quality-conscious way of working as well as resilience even in hectic situations
  • Mobility and flexibility, especially in season
  • Good knowledge of German

You can look forward to that

With us you can expect a diverse and challenging range of tasks in a lively, modern environment. You work in a motivated team with an open “you” culture and real cohesion.

  • 5 weeks vacation
  • Free parking space

  • Catering options on site
