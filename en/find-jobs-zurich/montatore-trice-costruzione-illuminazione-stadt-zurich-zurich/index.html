Full description

In the Building Lighting team, you will realize modern public lighting systems and use your electrical know-how to ensure safe, reliable and sustainable solutions in public spaces.

Tasks

  • You help ensure that streets, parks, pedestrian zones and underpasses are always lit.
  • With connection work in cable and network construction as well as installations in buildings and underpasses, you are familiar with.
  • For new and renovations you ensure the smooth lighting.
  • You are safe in the assembly of e.g. candelabras, masts, cable overvoltages and risers.
  • The occasional provision of special and night assignments is a matter of course for you.

Profile

  • You have successfully completed an electrotechnical training (network electrician, electric assembler or assembly electrician).
  • In the best case, you bring several years of professional experience and city knowledge and are familiar with the current office programs.
  • A category B driver’s license and fluent knowledge of German round off your profile.
  • Working in a team, outdoors and at night is fun for you. Also on ladders and lifts in airy height you feel comfortable.
  • You are committed, work independently and can be counted on.

We offer

  • More energy for your life: With mobile work, flexible work models and part-time opportunities, you can actively shape your working time and divide it into the best fit for your life. For example, you can buy up to 2 weeks of extra holidays and work part-time at all levels. Job sharing/splitting and unpaid holidays are also possible with us.
  • Sustainable and cheaper travel: With the mobility contribution of up to 600 francs a year, you travel cheaply and sustainably – regardless of whether you want to cycle through Zurich by bike or explore Switzerland by train.
  • An investment in your future: We not only attach importance to paying fair and transparent wages, but also ensure that you have more of your wages left over. We take over 60% of the savings contributions and additionally offer a super-compulsory interest on the capital accumulated.
  • An energy boost for your free time: We pay you 100 francs a month on your lunch-check card. In addition, you benefit annually from further Fringe Benefits worth 150 francs and attractive mobile subscriptions.
  • Development and training: With our talent management, we promote your development in a targeted manner – through a wide range of internal and external training offers as well as exciting development opportunities.
  • Meaningful work: Sustainability and meaningful work are not just words for us: shape Zurich and the future for us. Together with us, release energy for a good morning.
