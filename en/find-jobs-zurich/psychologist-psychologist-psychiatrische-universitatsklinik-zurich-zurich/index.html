Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility.

Working together where children and young people live!

For our consultancy and liaison service of child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement one or more

Psychologist/psychologist

60% - 80%

Their tasks

  • Work in a future-oriented, newly developing consultancy and liaison program under medical direction with psychological activity directly in the institutions and homes of the Kinder- und Jugendhilfe
  • Carrying out, evaluating and interpreting psychodiagnostic test procedures with a focus on psychological diagnosis in children and adolescents with different disorders, taking into account differential diagnostic and comorbid aspects
  • Planning and implementation of treatments according to current guidelines
  • Cooperation with other departments and special consultation hours of child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy incl. Involved external experts and institutions
  • Participation in the planning and implementation of further education and lectures
  • Psychology degree with psychotherapeutic training or advanced psychotherapeutic training
  • Comprehensive knowledge of psychodiagnostics and psychotherapy with children and adolescents
  • Knowledge of developmental psychology and psychopathology
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary cooperation with a high level of initiative and resilience
  • Good work organisation and experience in independent work
  • Experience/flexibility in dealing with different nationalities and cultural backgrounds
  • Together for people: Interdisciplinary cooperation for holistic psychiatric care
  • Strong in the team: appreciative cooperation and mutual support
  • Knowledge that advances: Extensive initial, further and further training offers
  • Flexible working: working time models that fit your life
  • Future provision with added value: 60% employer contribution to the pension fund
  • Sustainable travel: participation in the ZVV-BonusPass or general subscription
