Original description

Are you open-minded, ambitious and value a future-oriented way of working on the pulse of the real estate industry? With your commitment, we face the challenges of today and tomorrow together. Do you want to participate and make a difference? Then you are exactly right with Livit. We look forward to your personal contribution.

Tasks

  • You are fully responsible for managing a portfolio of majority residential properties
  • You develop and control the annual budget incl. Forecasts and controls and contracts creditors
  • You lead and train your subordinate employees (including target agreement and target achievement control)
  • You look after and advise tenants, owners and control the facility managers
  • You are responsible for securing, executing and accompaniment of maintenance as well as for periodic condition assessments including reporting
  • You make lease contract changes, rental interest changes, and indexed rental interest adjustments
  • You monitor the current rental policy and advise homeowners when determining rental rates
  • You issue repair and renovation orders and monitor their execution

Requirements

  • You have an oath. Property management or an Eidg. Diploma Immobilientreuhand
  • You bring many years of professional experience in the real estate industry, have special knowledge in rental law and building knowledge
  • Your mother tongue is German and you can ideally communicate verbally and in writing in other languages
  • You have a high level of social and self-competence with the ability to self-control
  • You live the values of customer orientation, communicate professionally and enjoy dealing with people
  • You bring an affinity for technology, can apply new technologies and are open to new things
  • You have self-learning skills and it gives you pleasure to pass on your knowledge to others.

We offer

  • Internal offer of our Livit Academy for specialist and leadership courses
  • Financial and/or time support for external specialist training and access to electronic learning platforms for online language courses
  • individual career planning and development prospects
  • Home / Mobile Office
  • Bonus depending on company success and personal performance
  • At least 5 weeks holiday and purchase of up to 10 additional days holiday (Ferienflex)
  • Above-average pension benefits and premiums for medical day allowance and accident insurance are 100% covered by Livit
  • 14 days paternity leave

We love real estate and are enthusiastic about preserving the value of our clients’ properties.

When you work at Livit, you belong to the Livit Family. You are appreciated for what you are and what you do. Because not only your skills and your experience, but also you as a person are important to us. In our working environment you feel comfortable and can develop. On the one hand, this is due to our versatile job profiles and the development opportunities within our company. On the other hand, there is also the distinctive corporate culture that is unparalleled in the real estate world.
