Full description

In the spinal lock, the nurseries of the GMZ, you focus on children and their individual needs. In 3 mixed-age groups with children from 3 months to kindergarten entry, you accompany up to 10 children per group with heart, expertise and commitment – together with 1 professional and 1 learner. Look forward to closeness, security and individual accompaniment.

Luogo di lavoro: cooperative Migros Zurich, crèche vertebral shot, Pfingstweidstrasse 101, 8005 Zurich

Important information

Further information about our crèche vertebral lock can be found at www. Wirbelschloss.ch We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Accompaniment and promotion of children according to their individual needs
  • Co-creation of a varied and age-appropriate crib everyday life
  • Observation and documentation of children’s developmental steps
  • Building trusting cooperation with parents and guardians
  • Share responsibility for a safe, appreciative and stimulating environment
  • Active participation in team meetings and participation in pedagogical development
  • Assumption of organizational and domestic tasks in group everyday life
  • Accompaniment and competent guidance of our learners in their everyday training

Requirements

  • First professional experience: in the field of family-supplementary childcare
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): as a FaBe child or an equivalent educational training
  • Experience in pedagogical and interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Warm, empathetic and patient treatment of children
  • Enjoying the appreciative treatment of children, families and caregivers from different cultures
  • High social skills and strong team skills
  • Self-employed, responsible and reliable working methods
  • Creativity and joy in accompanying children in their development
  • in German (negotiation certain)

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Test work: A trial day or half day gives the opportunity to get to know the future working environment and the team members better. And vice versa, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
