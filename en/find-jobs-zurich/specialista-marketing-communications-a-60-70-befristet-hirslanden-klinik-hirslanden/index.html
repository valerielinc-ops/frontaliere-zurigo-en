Location: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

For our marketing and communications team in Zurich, we are looking for a committed personality as Specialist Marketing & Communications (60–70%) as of December 2026. The post is limited in time in the framework of a maternity representation until the end of July 2027.

In this versatile function, you independently take on marketing and communication projects for various medical focus areas and work closely with internal specialists, doctors and other partners.

YOUR TASKS

  • You implement the marketing strategy for the focus areas Hirslanden Baby, Neurology, Radiology and Complementary Medicine target-oriented and tailored to the company goals.
  • You plan and realize marketing measures and campaigns for the various focal areas – from social media activities to thematic actions to digital and printed marketing materials.
  • You plan and organize the monthly information evenings for expectant parents and participate in the conception and implementation of further events and topic days in the medical focus areas.
  • You will design and implement internal and external communication measures for the focus areas under supervision and accompany relevant topics, projects and events in a communicative manner.

Your profile

  • You have a completed education or study in marketing, communication, business administration or a comparable qualification.
  • You will bring professional experience in marketing and/or communication, ideally in a project management role.
  • You have experience in the conception and implementation of marketing and communication measures (including events).
  • You have good editorial skills and a feeling for target group and channel-oriented communication.
  • You are a communicative and team-oriented personality and enjoy working with different specialist areas and stakeholders.

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. You work with a modern medical clinic information system and can expect extensive training and development opportunities from us. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family responsibilities. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.
