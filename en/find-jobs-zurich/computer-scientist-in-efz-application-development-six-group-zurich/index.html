Computer Scientist/in EFZ Application Development

Zurich | 100% | Reference 8073

Are you interested in software development and are you looking for a varied apprenticeship for 2027? Then the teaching as a computer scientist / in application development at SIX is exactly the right thing for you.You develop applications, work in projects and get to know modern technologies. You gain practical experience and contribute your ideas. By regularly changing departments, you get insights into various IT areas and are accompanied by vocational trainers.

These are your tasks

  • You develop applications and digital solutions for the everyday work of our employees
  • You work in projects and get to know modern technologies
  • You bring your own ideas and implement innovative solutions
  • Every 6-12 months you move to a new department and get insights into different IT areas
  • In the first year of apprenticeship, you will complete a multi-month base year with an external partner
  • You are part of a group of many learners. You will meet during work and events
  • Your workplace is in Zurich

This you bring with you

  • Very good grades in secondary school A or an equivalent education
  • Interest in software development, technologies and digital solutions
  • Enjoy analysing problems and finding solutions
  • Flair for programming and possibly already experience
  • Initiative, teamwork and motivation to learn new things
