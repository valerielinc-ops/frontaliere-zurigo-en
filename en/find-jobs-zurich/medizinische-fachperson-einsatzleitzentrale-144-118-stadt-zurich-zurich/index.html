Full description

With your voice you save life - day after day! To expand our team, we are looking for competent and motivated reinforcement as of 1 February 2027 or by agreement.

Tasks

  • As a medical specialist ELZ, you are responsible for the initial assessment and prioritization of emergency calls 144 and 118. Your medical expertise as well as a high customer and solution orientation are very important.
  • As an initial part of the rescue chain, you lead and empower people in distress on the phone until the rescue workers arrive on site – from the instructions for a resuscitation, to instructions for evacuation and the accompaniment of a birth.
  • You organize relocation transports, switch hazard alarm systems, provide medical emergency services, first responders, the emergency chaplain and much more in the 24/7 shift service.

Profile

  • Completion as a dipl. Rescue paramedic HF, transport paramedic with Federal FA, dipl. Nursing specialist HF or medical assistant EFZ with experience in emergency medicine
  • Experience in initial assessment and care of emergency patients
  • Rapid comprehension, situational decision-making, resilience and willingness to learn
  • Flawless written and oral expression in German and Swiss dialect as well as knowledge of mind. second national language and/or English
  • Computer science affinity and geography knowledge in the planning area of the operational control centre (canton Schaffhausen, Schwyz, Zug and Zurich)

We offer

  • Much variety and challenge in the dynamic environment of blue light organizations in Switzerland’s largest operational control centre
  • A roster where you decide when you want to work
  • Further development possibility to ar poly-dispatcherin ELZ, at the end as a dispatcher*in emergency call center with federal law. Professional card and free language courses to expand your foreign language skills
  • A state-of-the-art workplace with the latest technologies and systems in a central location at Zurich Airport with the best transport connections, parking facilities and infrastructure
  • Free meals during service, good social benefits and other attractive benefits from which you benefit as an employee of SRZ

About us

Protection & Rescue Zurich (SRZ), as a service department of the Department of Security, unites the professional and militia fire brigades, the medical department and the operational control center (ELZ) for the emergency calls 144 and 118. In addition, civil protection, fire police and a training center with an affiliated Higher School for Rescue Occupations (HFRB) belong to the largest civil rescue organization in Switzerland. Intervention and prevention units protect and save people, animals, property and the environment around the clock in the city of Zurich, at Zurich Airport and in the agglomeration.
