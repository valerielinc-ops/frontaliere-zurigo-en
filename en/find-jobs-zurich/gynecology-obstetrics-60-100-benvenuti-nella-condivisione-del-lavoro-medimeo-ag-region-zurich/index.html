Would you like to return to gynecology after a family phase or are you looking for a flexible working model with an existing patient base and long-term perspective in Zurich? For an established outpatient gynecologist practice within an interdisciplinary medical centre in Zurich, we are looking for a specialist in gynecology and obstetrics. The workload is flexible between 60 and 100%. Job sharing is welcome. The job is suitable both for experienced gynecologists working on an outpatient basis and for doctors who want to return to work after a family phase or a professional break.

Their tasks

  • Independent care of gynecological patients in the outpatient setting
  • Carrying out precautionary examinations and gynecological investigations
  • Pregnancy and follow-up care
  • Advice on contraception, desire to have children, menopause and general women’s health
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with family doctor colleagues
  • Co-creation and further development of the gynecological offer

Your Competences

  • Specialist title Gynecology and Obstetrics or corresponding recognition in Switzerland
  • At least three years of professional experience in a Swiss training institution or gynecological practice
  • Enjoying a long-term outpatient activity
  • Self-employed and responsible working methods
  • High social competence and appreciative treatment of patients
  • Certification for pregnancy ultrasound beneficial, but not a requirement

Their advantages

  • Flexible workload between 60 and 100%
  • Job sharing explicitly possible
  • Ideal for re-entry after a family or professional break
  • Existing patient base and continuous new patients
  • Attractive remuneration model with fixum from CHF 200,000 plus revenue share
  • Alternatively, an exclusively sales-based model is also possible
  • Five weeks of holidays and the possibility of an additional unpaid holiday week
  • Multicultural patient clientele
  • No mandatory requirement of a certificate for pregnancy ultrasound
  • Long-term perspective with scope for design
