Full description

USZ Campus

by February 2027 or by agreement

Design the diabetes therapy of tomorrow – at the University Hospital Zurich you work with evidence-based and interprofessional endocrinologists, nutrition consultants, nurses and many other professional groups for a better quality of life for our patients. Become part of a highly specialized, committed team that implements, co-designs and further develops outpatient and inpatient diabetes therapy to the highest standard.

Your main tasks

  • Development and implementation of new care models in diabetes treatment
  • Nursing case management in complex situations in stationary setting
  • Development of interprofessional diabetes treatment, development of concepts and implementation of evidence-based guidelines
  • Participation in interprofessional research projects

Your profile

  • Diplomated nurse with several years of professional experience and a completed Master of Science in Nursing
  • Additional training CAS Diabetes Counseling or HöFa 1 Diabetes Counselling or the willingness to complete this in the course.
  • High interest in merging research & practice in everyday professional life
  • Commitment and joy in the further development of the topic of diabetes treatment in the USZ
  • You have a high level of personal responsibility, are agile in the interprofessional setting and are enthusiastic about empowering both patients and employees.

Our offer

An exciting role awaits you, which you can develop and build yourself. Together with a dedicated interprofessional team, you will actively develop diabetes care at USZ and contribute your clinical expertise to the development of new concepts and treatment pathways. You take on responsibility at an early stage, drive innovation and shape cooperation with internal and external partners. Our university environment offers you both exciting and challenging perspectives and we look forward to accompanying you on your personal development path.
