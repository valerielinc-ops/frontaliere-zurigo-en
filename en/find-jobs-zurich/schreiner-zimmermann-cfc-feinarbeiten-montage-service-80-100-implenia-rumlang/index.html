Full description

At Implenia Holzbau, a lot is currently in motion – driven by the growing importance of sustainable construction and our clear focus on modern timber construction. With our many years of experience, high technical competence and a strong interdisciplinary team, we realize demanding timber construction services across all phases – from planning to production to execution.

In order to further develop our organization and shape wood construction in a future-oriented way, we are looking for committed personalities who want to take responsibility and grow together with us – as carpenters / carpenters EFZ (m/f/d).

Do you have what it takes to turn your communication skills and entrepreneurial thinking into attractive offers? Are analytical, timely and independent work just as important to you as your excellent skills as a team player? Then we are very much looking forward to your commitment for our team in Rümlang!

You move that

  • Service assignments for private and business customers: repairs, adjustments, rework
  • Installation services of doors, furniture and other wooden components
  • Fine assembly and completion of high-quality woodwork and carpentry work
  • Flexible use in workshops and on construction sites

This is why you succeed

  • Completed training as carpenter EFZ or carpenter EFZ (m/f/d)
  • Experience in service work, assembly and carpentry
  • Self-employed and structured working methods
  • Driving licence Cat. B
  • Good knowledge of reading plans and technical drawings
  • Readiness for mobile work on various construction sites

Great advantage

  • Knowledge of assembly of facade elements, doors and furniture
  • Interest in interdisciplinary cooperation with planning and workshop
  • Readiness for further training in the field of service and customer communication

This is what we offer you

  • A multicultural working environment with the chance to shape a future worth living
  • State-of-the-art technologies and a contemporary workplace at a sustainable company
  • A company that promotes talent and the training of its employees
  • The security of a financially healthy, broad-based group with strong roots in Switzerland
  • Flexible working models and modern tools
  • An exciting selection of benefits:
  • at least 5 weeks holidays and 9 paid bridge days
  • an in-house pension fund with very good coverage and 2 contribution levels
  • numerous discounts on well-known suppliers of products and services
