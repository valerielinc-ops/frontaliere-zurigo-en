Information specialist: in inventory management (m/f/d)

80%, Zurich, perpetual

The ETH Library promotes knowledge, supports research and teaching and, as a trustworthy institution, makes the world of yesterday, today and tomorrow more comprehensible. For the Group Integrated Media Processing, we are looking for an information specialist in the field of inventory management as of January 1, 2027 or by agreement.

Job description

  • You will contribute to the development and development of professional inventory management and actively contribute your expertise to the design of efficient processes.
  • You plan and coordinate stock shifts, prepare media for transport and ensure smooth implementation
  • You identify, check and process stocks for cleaning and thus contribute to the sustainable maintenance and optimization of stocks
  • You work closely with the Liaison Services as well as other internal and external stakeholders and ensure a coordinated implementation of common goals

Profile

  • You have a degree as a specialist information and documentation EFZ or a BSc as an information specialist:
  • You have in-depth knowledge of the regulations RDA and GND and enjoy continuously expanding your expertise in formal development and GND editorial.
  • You analyze complex issues in a structured, networked way and develop pragmatic solutions for technical and organizational issues
  • You have very good knowledge of German and English
  • You convince through strong communication and teamwork skills and organize your tasks forward-looking
  • You approach changes openly and actively shape them

We offer

  • A varied activity and a dynamic working environment in the exciting context of research and teaching
  • Working in a committed and collegial team
  • An attractive workplace at ETH Zurich in the center of Zurich
  • Cultural diversity and attractive offers and benefits
  • Family-friendly working conditions and active promotion of education and training
  • We live lifelong learning
