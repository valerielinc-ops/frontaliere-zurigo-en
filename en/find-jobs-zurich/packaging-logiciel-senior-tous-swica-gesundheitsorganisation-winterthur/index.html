Senior Software Packager (alle) — SWICA Gesundheitsorganisation

Full description

Thousands of employees rely on a modern and reliable digital workplace every day. For this to work, you need specialists who provide applications, manage devices and actively develop modern workplace solutions. That's what we're looking for. For our Modern Workplace team, we are looking for operational reinforcement with a focus on software packaging and modern software distribution. We are looking for a real change agent: someone who not only runs existing processes, but also has the drive to actively automate and develop our packaging, deployment and patch processes. If Microsoft Intune, PowerShell and modern Windows client management solutions are part of your day-to-day work and you like to move something directly, you are at the right place. This is how you shape health

  • Software Packaging: Responsibility for packaging, repackaging and lifecycle management of applications for Windows 11 with a focus on modern deployment technologies in Microsoft Intune (Win32)
  • Automation & modernization: Ongoing optimization and automation of software packages, software deployments and patch processes using PowerShell, PSAppDeployToolkit (PSADT), Intune and other modern tools
  • Software distribution: Operation, monitoring and continuous development of Intune-based software distribution in an enterprise environment with over 1,000 clients
  • Operational backbone: Analysis and resolution of packaging, installation and deployment issues in 2nd and 3rd level support as well as close cooperation with service desk, client engineering and application managers
  • Holistic system view: You understand the Modern Workplace as a coherent ecosystem and assess the impact of software rollouts, packages and configurations on the entire device platform
  • Documentation & Compliance: Creation, maintenance and ownership of technical documentation, operating manuals (BHB) as well as support for ISDS-relevant concepts and compliance requirements
  • Teamwork: Active knowledge transfer within the team as well as securing deputies and sustainable knowledge assurance

This sets you apart

  • Your mindset: We are not looking for the person with the most certificates, but someone with a pronounced hands-on and makers mentality. You question existing processes, think in a networked way and actively drive improvements forward.
  • Completed IT training (EFZ, HF, FH or comparable) or equivalent, many years of practical experience in the field of endpoint management and software packaging
  • Several years of experience in software packaging and endpoint management in enterprise environments with over 1,000 clients
  • Demonstrable, in-depth practical experience with Microsoft Intune and the distribution of Win32 applications
  • Very good knowledge of MSI, MSIX and repackaging technologies including package analysis, adjustments and troubleshooting
  • Very good PowerShell knowledge and practical experience in the automation of operational and deployment processes
  • Practical experience with PSAppDeployToolkit (PSADT) and ideally knowledge of Patch My PC
  • Very good knowledge of Windows 11 and modern understanding of endpoint management platforms
  • Strong documentation, communication and organisational capabilities
  • You are a team player, take responsibility in day-to-day business and enjoy actively modernizing and automating existing processes
  • Very good German and good English skills in word and writing
