Place of deployment: Opfikon and surroundings Start of deployment: Now or by agreement Opfikon is located in the Limmat Valley and offers excellent transport connections by the S-Bahn and Zurich Airport in the immediate vicinity. The region impresses with its central location between Zurich and the Glatttal, which allows short working distances. As a place of work, Opfikon is attractive because of the good infrastructure, numerous companies and the proximity to shopping and leisure facilities. For a modern practice with a holistic care concept in the room Opfikon we are looking for an empathetic and responsible specialist in gynecology and obstetrics (m/f/d). The position offers you an exciting and self-responsible field of activity in the outpatient sector with a wide range of patients – from prevention to pregnancy care to menopause. Your tasks – Medical diversity with humanity

  • Comprehensive gynecological care for patients of all age groups
  • Preventive examinations, swabs, cytologies, ultrasound
  • Pregnancy counselling and guidance incl. First trimester diagnostics
  • Diagnosis and treatment of gynecological diseases
  • Advice on contraception, family planning, hormone therapy and menopause
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation with midwives, family doctors, hospitals and other specialists

Your profile – expertise and personal commitment

  • Specialist title Gynecology and Obstetrics (FMH) or equivalent MEBEKO recognition
  • Entry in the Medical Professional Register (MedReg) required
  • Several years of professional experience in Switzerland or in the ambulatory gynecological area of advantage
  • Empathetic personality with pleasure in personal and long-term patient care
  • Self-employed, structured and responsible working practices
  • Very good knowledge of German, other national languages are advantageous

Our offer – your new job with perspective

  • Attractive vacancies in gynecology in Opfikon
  • Modernly equipped practice with digital patient management
  • High design scope and self-responsible work
  • Flexible working time models (also possible part-time)
  • Support for training and specialisations
  • Pleasant, collegial team and appreciative practice structure
