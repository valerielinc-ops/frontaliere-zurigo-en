Full description

As a result of retirement, we are looking for 1. March 2027 or by agreement a committed and responsible person who strengthens our IT and digitalization department.

The IT and digitalization department supports the City Chancellery around its digital processes, supervises the city council’s specialist application and ensures the digital workplace of its employees. A versatile, open and dynamic working environment awaits you at the digital pulse of the city of Zurich.

Tasks

  • They support the employees of the City Chancellery in IT application issues, in particular in the use of the business administration system CMI.
  • They receive fault messages, process them and/or coordinate incident tickets in the ITSM system.
  • They support release management and accompany hard- and software rollouts.
  • They coordinate new entries and resignations of employees of the City Chancellery by IT.
  • You are jointly responsible for granting and managing access rights (authorization and identity management).
  • They work in digitalization projects and support testing.
  • You lead the computer science inventories and support the commissioning and maintenance of special hardware.

Profile

  • Completed vocational training as a computer scientist, commercial apprenticeship with administrative and computer science or equivalent training (EFZ level)
  • Some years of professional experience in a similar position and/or in administration beneficial
  • Pragmatic and far-sighted approach to complex tasks, solution-oriented approach
  • Independent, structured and customer-oriented working method even under time pressure
  • Patient and emphatic communication skills
  • Organizational and networking capabilities

We offer

A collegial and supportive working environment in a central organization as well as a dedicated team that looks forward to working with you. Your workplace is located in the historic townhouse, with the opportunity to work partly in the home office.

They benefit from attractive working conditions, comprehensive training opportunities and an outstanding pension fund. The city of Zurich participates in your meals with lunch checks and supports your mobility by contributing to public transport or the bike. The employment takes place in the annual working time model.

About us

The City Registry, which is run by the town clerk and employs around 75 people, is a municipal council office that also provides a wide range of services to the administration and the public. It is divided into the areas of voting and elections, law firm services, communications, organisation and logistics, staff, strategy and law and central services.

The Department of Law Firm Services draws up the resolutions of the weekly meetings of the City Council, carries out the business and deadline control, prepares the archiving and handover of completed transactions to the City Archives, manages the Official Collection, coordinates the annual report with the departments and supervises the specialist informatics in the City Chancellery.
