Do you want to actively shape the future of modern obstetrics? Are you looking for a management task that offers you long-term prospects, creative leeway and a collegial environment? Our client is a modern, cross-location women’s clinic in German-speaking Switzerland, which is continuously developing and jointly designs the obstetric care of the region. With around 1,000 births per year, a contemporary infrastructure and close interdisciplinary cooperation, it offers a versatile medical spectrum and an environment in which quality, teamwork and innovation are lived equally. The strategic development of obstetrics gives you the opportunity to contribute your own ideas and provide sustainable impulses – embedded in an appreciative corporate culture with a long-term perspective.

Their tasks

  • You take on a responsible leadership role within obstetrics and actively contribute your experience to the further development of the department
  • Together with the squad team, you shape the professional and organizational future of the Frauenklinik
  • They look after outpatient and inpatient patients in the entire obstetric spectrum – from the consultation hour to the birth hall to surgical procedures
  • They are committed to the further development of fetomaternal medicine and promote high-quality care for patients
  • They accompany assistant doctors in their further education and take on supervision tasks
  • They maintain close cooperation with internal departments as well as regional assignments and further develop regional partnerships

Your Competences

  • You have the specialist title Gynecology and Obstetrics
  • Ideally, you will bring the focus on Fetomaternal Medicine or would like to actively develop this area
  • You have several years of experience in a cadre function and take on leadership responsibility with pleasure
  • They are characterized by a high level of social competence, decision-making strength and a team-oriented working method
  • The promotion and further education of assistant doctors is important to you

Their advantages

  • Responsible leadership function with plenty of room for manoeuvre
  • Active participation in the further development of obstetrics and fetomaternal medicine
  • Broad obstetric spectrum with around 1,000 births per year at two closely networked locations
  • Modern infrastructure and excellent interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Collegials, appreciative team with short decision paths
  • Long-term perspective in a future-oriented women’s clinic
  • Working in a region with a high quality of life and diverse leisure opportunities
