Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,300 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland. For the Senevita Limmatfeld in Dietikon we are looking for a dipl on October 1, 2026 or by appointment. Care professional HF/FH (m/f/d) 50-100 % Your tasks

  • You lead the team during the service and help our residents enjoy professional and individual care and support every day
  • You ensure an optimal operational sequence and the care process in everyday life
  • The electronic care documentation in the Care Coach you lead independently and seamlessly
  • You are involved in the daily care of our residents and, if necessary, support in the education and training of learners and interns
  • You are the contact person for residents, relatives and other interest groups such as doctors, pharmacists, etc.

You bring that with you

  • Training as a Dipl. Nursing professional HF/HF or similar
  • You are motivated and like to bring your strong professional and social skills into everyday life
  • You bring knowledge in RAI/RUG and careCoach
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German (B2)
  • with your cheerful manner you actively contribute to a company culture in which residents and employees feel comfortable
