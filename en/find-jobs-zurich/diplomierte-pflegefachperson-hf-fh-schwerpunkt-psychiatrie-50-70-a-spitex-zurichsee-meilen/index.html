Full description

Your job, your rhythm – independently on the road and yet part of a strong team Spitex Zurichsee is a public nonprofit organization located on the beautiful Lake Zurich. On behalf of the municipalities of Erlenbach, Herrliberg, Meilen, Uetikon am See and Männedorf, we provide professional care, care and support at home. Our services are in demand like never before. This is why we are looking for YOU to strengthen our committed psychiatry team as

Graduate nurse HF/FH – focus on psychiatry 50–70% (a) Your tasks

  • Independent implementation of the entire care process and implementation of care at the customer
  • Management of needs assessment using the interRai-CMH instrument
  • Accompanying clients with psychiatric diseases at home and practicing everyday practical skills
  • Building a trusting and sustainable relationship with customers
  • Promotion and empowerment of self-employment, autonomy, stability and quality of life

What you bring

  • Diploma Nursing HF/FH or equivalent training
  • Years of experience in psychiatry
  • Driving licence Cat. B
  • Independent, reflected and resilient personality
  • Joy of relationship work and networked thinking
  • Empathy, team spirit and desire to take responsibility

What you can look forward to

  • Strong team cohesion and a corporate culture with a focus on people and togetherness
  • Working at office hours, without weekend
  • Exciting and varied activity in the ambulatory setting
  • Opportunity for professional development
  • Progressive employment conditions, fair social benefits and regular employee events.
  • Modern equipment: tablet, smartphone, car.
