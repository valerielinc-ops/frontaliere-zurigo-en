Full description

Responsibility for the correct processing of customer orders is part of daily business. The company offers a wide range of activities in warehouse logistics, including transport coordination and packaging. You will find an open-minded team and contemporary employment conditions. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Loading and unloading trucks and checking goods
  • Order orders from customers, provide them and store them properly
  • Coordinate transports and outsource customer material
  • Carry out packaging work internally and at customers and record ERP processes

Requirements

  • Completed training as logistician/EFZ
  • Professional experience in warehouse logistics and valid truck identification
  • Good knowledge of German and safe handling of PC and ERP systems
  • Self-employed, careful and responsible work

Offer

  • Various tasks in warehouse logistics, transport and packaging
  • Modern workplace with contemporary employment conditions
  • Support for professional and personal training
  • Collegial team with short decision paths
