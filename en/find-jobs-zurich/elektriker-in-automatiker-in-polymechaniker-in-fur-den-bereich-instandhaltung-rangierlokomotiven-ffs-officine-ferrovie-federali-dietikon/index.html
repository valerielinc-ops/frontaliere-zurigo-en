Electrician, automation engineer, polymechanic for the maintenance of shunting locomotives 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Electrician, automation engineer, polymechanic for the maintenance of shunting locomotives Dietikon, January 4th, 2027 or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. For our specialized maintenance workshop in Dietikon, we are looking for a technically experienced person to maintain our shunting locomotives. In this role, you combine practical workshop work, repairs and diagnostics on the vehicles (approx. 80%) with the technical support of our operational engineering (approx. 20%). We also support your professional development through targeted further training offers. We are an attractive employer and offer many benefits such as extra child allowances, free 2nd class GA and discounted travel abroad - also for the family. You can find all information on our website under Benefits. You can move that. Workshop, support & maintenance shunting locomotives (approx. 80%)

  • You carry out preventive maintenance on diesel and hybrid locomotives and check vehicle-specific systems such as radio remote controls, train radio systems, compressed air/brake systems and train protection. - Functional checks are carried out by you and components are replaced if necessary
  • Conversions and modifications to prototypes are carried out in coordination with engineering. - Using technical know-how, you will systematically search for errors and use computer-aided diagnostics to resolve faults. - You replace and repair mechanical, hydraulic and electronic assemblies such as axle gears, compressors, bogie components, pumps or power converters. In addition, you carry out checks and approvals of the vehicles in accordance with the applicable maintenance and safety guidelines. Operational engineering & technical support (approx. 20%)
  • As a technical interface, you coordinate concerns between the workshop and operational engineering.
  • In the event of serial problems, you create error analyzes (root cause), findings reports and the associated technical documentation.
  • You will be involved in optimizing maintenance processes, test plans and work instructions.
  • You actively support special technical clarifications, fleet modifications and retrofitting projects and ensure the quality of traceability and documentation. This is what you bring with you.
  • You are characterized by a high level of safety and quality awareness, an independent way of working and enjoyment of practical workshop work and teamwork.
  • You have in-depth knowledge of diesel engines, hydraulics, pneumatics and vehicle electrics as well as an interest in systematic diagnostics. Further training to become a diagnostician (e.g. agricultural machinery, commercial vehicle or automotive diagnostician) or similar additional training is an advantage.
  • You have completed an apprenticeship as an electromechanic, electrician, automation engineer, automation fitter, polymechanic or mechanic/mechatronics engineer (automotive, commercial vehicles, construction machinery, agricultural machinery).
  • You can use your in-depth knowledge of MS Office and diagnostic software on a daily basis.
