Full description

The Klubschule Migros is the largest provider of adult education in Switzerland. The Genossenschaft Migros Ostschweiz operates 7 Klubschulen, where our customers are offered over 600 different courses and training programs in a pleasant learning atmosphere.

Tasks

  • Your joy in teaching, your commitment, and your winning personality inspire the participants
  • Preparation, execution, and follow-up of Oriental dance lessons
  • Practice-oriented and health-promoting lesson design, taking into account the different prerequisites of the course participants
  • Ensuring course quality and implementation of Klubschule standards

Requirements

  • Professional experience: in teaching adults
  • Completed basic or further training: in Oriental dance
  • Independent, flexible, and reliable personality who conveys their knowledge and joy for dance courses with commitment
  • Winning personality and good communication skills
  • German (good knowledge)

What we offer

  • Mobile subscription: Highly discounted conditions for private mobile subscriptions, also for family and friends
  • Cumulus points: Multiple Cumulus points on food and non-food items
  • Klubschule Migros: Generous financial contribution to self-selected courses
