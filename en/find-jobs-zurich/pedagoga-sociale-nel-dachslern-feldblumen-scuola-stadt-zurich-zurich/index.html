About us

The Zürich-Letzi school district comprises district 9 with the neighborhoods of Albisrieden, Altstetten, and Grünau. In the 16 schools of our rapidly growing school district, over 5,000 compulsory school students are taught and cared for. Over 1,200 employees work in our schools in the professional groups of teaching, care, building services and technology, and administration. All of them are committed daily to the well-being of these children aged between 4 and 13 years and to ensuring a smooth school day.
