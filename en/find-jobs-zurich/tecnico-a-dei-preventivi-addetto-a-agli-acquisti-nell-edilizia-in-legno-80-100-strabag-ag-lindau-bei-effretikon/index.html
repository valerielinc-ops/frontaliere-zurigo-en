Calculator:in / Purchaser:in Holzbau 80-100 % Your contribution with us

  • You work out offers, determine quantities based on plans and calculate prices for timber construction projects
  • You create work calculations and accompany projects from the offer phase to execution
  • You are responsible for project purchase, obtain offers from suppliers and subcontractors and compare them technically and priced
  • You negotiate contracts with subcontractors and material suppliers and conclude them in coordination with the project management
  • You maintain the calculations and keep master data and guidelines up-to-date
  • You coordinate with construction management and plant planning so that material and scheduling fit together
  • You evaluate opportunities and risks in tenders and bring them into the bid strategy
  • You check invoices from suppliers and subcontractors for their compliance with the agreed conditions

What matters to us

  • Completed apprenticeship as carpenter or basic education in the construction industry
  • Further training for polishing:in, timber construction technician:in HF or construction manager:in, or equivalent advantageous
  • Practical experience in calculation, AVOR or purchasing wood and wood-based materials
  • Secure handling of industry software and MS-Office
  • Good feeling for quantities, materials and prices in timber construction
  • Negotiation skills as well as an independent and responsible working method round off your profile

Our added value for you
