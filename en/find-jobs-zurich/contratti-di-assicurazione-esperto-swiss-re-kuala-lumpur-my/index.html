Full description

About the Team P&C Contracts is part of Underwriting Excellence in Property & Casualty Reinsurance and is responsible for ensuring Swiss Re's (re)insurance agreements comply with underwriting and contract standards. You will join a highly engaged global team of approximately 40 legal, claims, underwriting, and contract specialists supporting Property, Casualty, and Specialty business lines worldwide. The team is responsible for:

  • Drafting and reviewing (re)insurance wordings.
  • Advising on contract-related matters.
  • Negotiating and finalising reinsurance contract terms with internal and external stakeholders.
  • Supporting the development of contract management tools, wording analytics, and contract governance standards.
  • Collaborating across regions, including APAC, EMEA, and the Americas, on global initiatives and projects.

About the Role As Reinsurance Contracts Expert / Counsel, you will support Swiss Re's Property & Casualty Reinsurance business across Asia Pacific, partnering closely with underwriting, client management, and other stakeholders to manage contract risks and ensure contract certainty.

Key responsibilities include

  • Independently drafting, reviewing, and negotiating reinsurance contracts, binders, slips, endorsements, and related documentation.
  • Ensuring contract wordings align with business intent, corporate standards, regulatory requirements, and applicable laws.
  • Conducting portfolio analysis and providing insights on contract-related risks and trends.
  • Supporting the Head of P&C Reinsurance Contracts in managing contract risks across the reinsurance portfolio.
  • Collaborating with Client Managers, Underwriters, brokers, and external clients during contract negotiations.
  • Acting as a technical advisor on contract interpretation, wording implications, and market best practices.
  • Contributing to the development of corporate policies related to contracts.
  • Supporting the enhancement of contract management and analytical systems.
  • Developing and delivering training on contract-related topics and thought leadership initiatives.
  • Maintaining strong governance, risk, and control standards for contract wordings and processes.
  • Participating in global projects and working closely with colleagues across APAC, EMEA, and the US.
  • Travelling for business approximately two weeks per year.

Location: Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia

About You

We're looking for an experienced reinsurance professional with strong technical, analytical, and stakeholder management capabilities who can work independently while building collaborative relationships across a global organisation.
