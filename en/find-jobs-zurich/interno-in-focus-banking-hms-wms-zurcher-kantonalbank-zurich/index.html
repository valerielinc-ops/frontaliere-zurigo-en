Do you want to immerse yourself in the exciting world of banking? During our one-year HMS internship, you benefit from a solid and eventful basic education and discover the diversity of a modern bank.

We offer you a future-oriented, exciting and varied vocational training – your ideal start in the world of banking. The Zürcher Kantonalbank is one of the largest training companies in the canton of Zurich and supports more than 420 apprentices every year. Would you like to be part of our Young Talents? We look forward to your application!

Your tasks

During one year you will enter one of the following subject areas:

  • In the customer halls, you support the advice of private customers on site. The topics of figures, saving, investing and provision are in focus. You help to handle everyday banking transactions, identify potential and accompany customers into the digital world. At the same time, you get exciting insights into the holistic advice of private customers and actively contribute to a high customer satisfaction.
  • In the direct bank private customers you support the advice and support of private customers who contact us online or by phone. You will help process customer requests, find solutions and ensure smooth service. You will gain exciting insights into digital customer support and learn how we can ensure high customer satisfaction even without personal contact on site.
  • In the direct bank Corporate Clients you support the processing of customer orders and the telephone support of our corporate clients. You will help to process requests efficiently, find solutions and ensure professional service. In addition, you are involved in consulting about company foundations and the onboarding of new corporate customers. You will gain exciting insights into the needs of business customers and learn how we accompany them competently and service-oriented in the digital environment.
  • In the financing center, you support the processing of financing applications and ensure that they are processed in a timely manner and in the best quality. You will help to monitor the business of customers and contribute to the further development of financing processes, basic products and training and development measures.

In parallel, you will attend inter-company courses at the IGKG, including course days on banking topics at the CYP, a competence center for modern banking expertise.

  • Learning new things and moving things give you joy
  • Administrative activities give you pleasure and you bring impulses and creative ideas
  • You like to communicate, are open-minded and have fun working with others
  • Required application documents: application/motivation letter, CV, school certificates, optional further documents such as diplomas, etc.
  • In-depth insight into an exciting specialist area of our successful bank

  • Open team that looks forward to working with you
  • Our young talents are important to us and therefore we actively promote you
  • Have fun and great colleagues – because without this an education is only half as beautiful
  • Benefit from other benefits such as the ZVV Bonus Pass, support for competitive sports, social events and much more
