Description

Our Sustainability & Strategic Regulatory team supports clients in transforming sustainability ambitions into practical business solutions. We work at the intersection of sustainability, regulation and supply chain transformation, helping organisations navigate an evolving regulatory landscape while creating long-term value. We support clients in integrating sustainability requirements into their business models, operations and supply chains. As part of our international and multidisciplinary team, you will gain exposure to diverse projects, develop your expertise and build meaningful client relationships.

  • Support clients in assessing and implementing sustainability strategies across their supply chains and business operations.
  • Analyse the implications of national and international sustainability regulations and translate regulatory requirements into practical business solutions.
