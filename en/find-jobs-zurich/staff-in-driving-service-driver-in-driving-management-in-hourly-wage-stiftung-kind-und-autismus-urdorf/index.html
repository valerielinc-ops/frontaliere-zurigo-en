Full description

The Foundation Child and Autism is a center of competence for children and young people with autism in Urdorf (ZH). The offer includes the special school for 56 children and adolescents, the occupant living with 16 seats, the driving service, the consultancy and the early area, the course and the auxiliary shop. Around 120 employees work in the child and autism foundation. From August 2026 we are looking for an employee/in driving service (driver and driving guide) in the hourly wage. --- Mansioni In this function, according to the application plan (approximately 10 hours per week) you take over the safe and reliable transport of our students as well as the driving instructions. You will ensure a pleasant and secure day-to-day transport and work closely with the transport team, parents and pedagogical staff.

  • Requisiti
  • A personality with a strong sense of responsibility
  • High reliability, punctuality, independence and social competence
  • Educational skills and the ability to react appropriately in stress situations
  • A high interest in autism and no fear of touch
  • A skill certificate for commercial passenger transport according to the CZV Chauffeur Registration Regulation
  • A category D guide card
  • Cosa offriamo
  • A meaningful activity with direct influence on the lives of children and young people and their families
  • Regular working hours from Monday to Friday and school holidays
  • Conditions of employment
  • A well-established and committed team
  • Internal and external vocational training --- Contatto If you have any questions, Sandra Meier, Head of Service and Driving Service, is happy to be available (tel. 044 736 50 70).
