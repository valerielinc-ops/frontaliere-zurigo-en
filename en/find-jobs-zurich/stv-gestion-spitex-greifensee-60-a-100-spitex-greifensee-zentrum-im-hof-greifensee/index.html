Original description

The center "Im Hof" in Greifensee is located near the lake and was created in 2008. The foundation fulfills the service mandate for the municipality of Greifensee with 28 beds for inpatient long-term care, twelve age-appropriate apartments and the public Spitex. After many years in our foundation, our director Spitex Greifensee will be retired in the middle of next year. In the sense of a succession regulation, we are looking for a successor by 1 January or by agreement. Stv. management Spitex Greifensee 60% to 100%

  • Their tasks

In this function, you are subordinate to the Spitex line. In agreement with the management, you will assume personnel, professional and organizational responsibility for a team of around 12 employees. Together with the management and the team, you ensure professional, customer-oriented care. Regular practical assignments are part of your task, you create needs assessments (RAI-HC) and thus keep the pulse at the base. You ensure process-oriented processes, use your creative leeway and actively shape the Spitex Greifensee. Special concerns are ensuring a smooth daily routine, as well as a continuous development of care and care quality. Together with the new Spitex management, you will create conditions for good interdisciplinary cooperation and high customer and employee satisfaction.

Your profile You are an experienced nurse HF/FH with leadership experience in a Spitex. As Stv. management, you are a strong team player, communicate transparently and appreciate shared responsibility and open feedback. Networked thinking, high social skills, a secure appearance and good IT knowledge (Perigon) round off

  • your profile. It would be nice but not a must if you owned the vocational trainer.

Our offer A motivated, collegial and supportive working environment awaits you in beautiful premises near the Greifensee. This new function offers space for flexibility, work-life balance and mutual representation. The task also allows space for your own ideas and stimuli for developments. Furthermore, we can offer you progressive working conditions, fair pay and employee events together with the nursing home.
