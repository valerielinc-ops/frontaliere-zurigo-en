Full description

Create a better #TomorrowWithUs Want to help shape the future of the railway world together with us? At Siemens Mobility, we are the leading provider of mobility and are constantly expanding our innovative portfolio, which includes rail vehicles, rail automation, electrification solutions, complete rail systems and accompanying services. With around 750 specialist employees in Switzerland and 39 600 employees worldwide, we shape mobility with passion and are one step ahead of our time. Do you not only want to accompany digitalization, but actively shape it? In this key role, you will be responsible for the strategic development of processes, data management and digitalization in the area of Project Execution & Engineering (PE). Together with a technical lead, you create the conditions for efficient, integrated and sustainable working methods. You understand how to attract people to change, reduce complexity and bring different interests together into a common vision. With your sense for processes, data and digitalization, you create sustainable added value for the business. You can look forward to that.

  • Design and implementation of the digitalization strategy in the area of PE
  • Leadership and further development of an interdisciplinary team of process, data and digitalization experts:
  • Great design freedom with direct influence on efficiency, quality and transparency
  • Close cooperation with specialist areas, IT and management
  • Responsibility for a diverse portfolio of transformation and digitalization initiatives
  • You lead the area Process-/Datamanagement & Digitalization professionally, personnel and strategically.
  • You develop the target image for processes, data and digitalization and implement it together with the relevant stakeholders.
  • You standardize and harmonize end-to-end processes along the value chain of Project Execution.
  • You will establish governance structures and standards in process and data management.
  • You identify and prioritize digitalization and automation potentials with clear business benefits.
  • You work closely with the technical lead and ensure that business requirements and technological solutions work together optimally.
  • You control resources, budget and portfolio and are responsible for the successful implementation of the initiatives. You bring that with you
  • University degree in business informatics, business administration, engineering or a comparable discipline
  • Several years of experience in the areas of digitalization, process management, data management or transformation
  • Leadership experience and joy in developing teams and driving change
  • Strong strength in stakeholder management and cooperation across organizational boundaries
  • Strategic thinking combined with a pragmatic implementation strength
