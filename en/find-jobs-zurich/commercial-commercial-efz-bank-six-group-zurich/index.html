Commercial woman / merchant EFZ Bank

Zurich, 100%, Reference 8077

Do you want to contribute your enthusiasm for organization and communication to the innovative and dynamic financial world? You have a talent for numbers and a flair in dealing with people and are looking for a varied apprenticeship for 2027? Then a commercial apprenticeship – banking industry is just the right thing for you!

As a modern and future-oriented company, SIX offers you numerous opportunities to develop your potential in the exciting world of technology and finance. Start your three-year KV apprenticeship in the banking industry at SIX – the perfect kick-start for your career! With us you can also complete the KV apprenticeship with professional character or in the BM focus profile.

Tasks

  • You support teams in administrative and organisational tasks
  • You help coordinate processes and ensure good cooperation
  • You work in projects and bring your own ideas
  • You get to know our four Business Units and build up sound financial knowledge
  • Every 6–12 months you switch to a new department and get new insights
  • You are part of a group of many learners. You meet during work and at events

Requirements

  • Very good performance in secondary school A or equivalent education
  • joy in dealing with people, technologies and data
  • an open and communicative personality
  • interest in the financial world and joy in planning and organizing
  • initiative, team spirit and motivation to learn new things

We offer

  • A varied apprenticeship in the banking industry
  • The opportunity to deepen your skills in different departments
  • A dynamic and innovative working environment
  • Support in preparing for the professional nature or the BM focus profile
