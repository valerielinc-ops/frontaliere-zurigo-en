Full description

With Clienia Ambulant, the Clienia Group bundles its psychiatric-psychotherapeutic services for the best possible outpatient and day-to-day clinical care for mentally ill patients of all ages – at 20 locations from Amriswil to Zurich. Interdisciplinary teams of psychotherapists, psychotherapists, nursing specialists and specialists in psychiatry and psychotherapy guarantee comprehensive outpatient treatment methods.

For the Clienia Psychiatry Center Wetzikon we are looking for an

Focus for the consular and liaison service incl. Work in the polyclinic.

Their tasks

  • Participation in the consultancy and liaison service in the hospitals and homes of the region
  • Working hours
  • Leadership and professional support of assigned medical assistants and psychologists
  • Conceptual development of the area including the implementation of specific group offers

Your profile

  • Specialist title or specialist maturity for psychiatry and psychotherapy or several years of inpatient and outpatient psychiatric experience
  • Interest in the focus on consultancy and liaison psychiatry
  • Ideally, you have a driver’s license
  • Interest and pleasure in the best possible patient care with special consideration of the upstream and downstream body in the sense of networked care

Our offer

  • An interesting, versatile and challenging task in a motivated interprofessional team within an innovative institution
  • By agreement, we can offer locations in Wetzikon, Uster and Zurich, among others.
  • Attractive working conditions
  • Extensive training opportunities
  • Support for obtaining the priority title

For further information, Volker Böckmann, Chief Physician, +41 44 931 39 39 is at your disposal.
