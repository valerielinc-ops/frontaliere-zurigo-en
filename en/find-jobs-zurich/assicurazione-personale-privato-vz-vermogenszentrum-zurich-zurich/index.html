Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We create transparency in the banking and insurance market and advise our customers comprehensively and neutrally. As we are growing strongly, we are looking for reinforcements for our insurance team at our headquarters at Paradeplatz in Zurich.

  • You have the exciting opportunity to be in close contact with insurers in the household, vehicles, travel and legal protection industries. In new transactions and changes, you play an important role.
  • You’ll become more and more an insurance expert by analysing insurance, clearing coverage and showing price and performance differences. Your work provides valuable information that helps our clients make important decisions.
  • In addition, you actively support our consultants in the preparation of offers and ensure a smooth process.
  • You take on a wide range of tasks in the back office and front office and are happy to contact our customers by phone and in writing.
  • KV training with an insurance or experience in private insurance
  • VBV degree (insurance intermediary) or willingness to obtain it
  • Enjoyment of customer care
  • Quick understanding and solution-oriented work
  • Secure communication in German
  • Other languages are a plus
  • We are proud of our culture, which is based on openness and informal relationships. You have the opportunity to participate in the development of a unique company.
  • You are also at the right place in terms of further education: We support you individually and support you with an external further education within the framework of the job profile.
  • A fixed annual wage without being dependent on commissions.
  • Most of the positions are also open for a part-time stay, so that there is time for further education, family, sports and projects that are important to you.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
