Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Imprisonment and Reintegration Trainee in Project & Quality Management 80 - Get to know 100% Imprisonment and help shape enforcement practice? Imprisonment and Reintegration (JuWe), as part of the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs of the Canton of Zurich, is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as the implementation of the various types of detention. For our 1,400 employees from around 40 professions, the successful social reintegration of people who have committed crimes, the prevention of recidivism and the detention and preparation of the departure of persons without a valid residence permit are of particular importance. The Zurich Prisons Department (VEZ) – one of JuWe’s eight main departments – comprises four institutions with different types of detention. In total, more than 300 detainees are cared for. With its specialist services, the management supports the institutions in fulfilling their legal mandate. For the project and quality management in the staff of the VEZ, we are looking for a reliable, empathetic and team-capable person on 15.02.2027 or by appointment for an internship, who is willing to contribute creatively to ongoing projects and the further development of everyday life processes.
