Full description

Personal – competent – close Spital Uster is the networked and efficient focus hospital of the Zurich Oberland and the Upper Glattal. Every year, around 75,000 patients rely on our expertise, broad range of services and advanced infrastructure. We are the largest employer of Uster and offer our approximately 1,200 employees an exciting working environment. Nevertheless, we are familiar enough that you know each other personally and feel well looked after. You organize our internal and external events with a lot of overview and responsibility and ensure that everything runs smoothly from planning to implementation. At the same time, you are an important organizational hub and actively supports us in the versatile day-to-day business. For our Marketing & Communications department, we are looking for an immediately or by agreement *n

Exciting challenges await you

  • Project management of internal and external (specialist) events – from planning and coordination to implementation on site
  • Organization and coordination of various administrative tasks in day-to-day business
  • Support in support of our communication channels such as website, employee app and social media
  • Data maintenance and organization of actions for employees
  • Coordination and management of promotional products and other means of communication

What sets you apart for the job

  • stylish German and very good written and oral communication skills

  • Very good knowledge of Microsoft Office
