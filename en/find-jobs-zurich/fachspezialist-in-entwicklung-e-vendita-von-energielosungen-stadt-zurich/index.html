Full description

In the team Consulting and sales, you design future-oriented energy supply solutions in the field of renewable heat and cooling supply and open up new business opportunities throughout Switzerland.

Tasks

  • In the team Consulting and sales of ewz energy solutions you are responsible for the development and acquisition of innovative renewable energy supply solutions for infrastructure and individual projects.
  • You identify, develop and win economically viable and ecologically sustainable projects for an innovative heating and cooling supply.
  • The entire development process – from the initial idea to the ready-to-use solution – is managed in close coordination with customers, authorities, municipalities and other stakeholders, keeping in touch with existing customers and identifying new target groups.
  • You recognize cross-selling potentials and initiate additional business opportunities for the ewz performance portfolio.
  • Ensuring the economy, creating, presenting and negotiating offers and contracts complete your diverse field of responsibility.

Profile

  • Basic technical training or further training as an engineer FH or technician HF in the Hink environment, environmental technology or mechanical engineering forms your professional basis.
  • You bring with you sound sales experience with demonstrable successes, ideally in the heat market.
  • The outstanding negotiating strength, resilience as well as a clear project and customer focus characterize your professional appearance.
  • You convince by an independent, structured working method, high self-initiative and joy in active customer contact.
  • Stylish German in word and writing and a ticket Cat. B round off your profile.

We offer

  • More energy for your life: mobile work, flexible work models and part-time opportunities enable you to actively shape your working time and divide it into the way it suits your life best. For example, you can shop for up to 2 weeks extra holidays and work part-time at all levels. Job I/O/-spli-t and unpaid holiday are also possible with us.
  • Sustainable and discounted travel: With the mobility fee of up to 600 francs per year, you travel cheaply and sustainably – whether you want to cycle through Zurich by bike or explore Switzerland by train.
  • An investment in your future: We not only value paying fair and transparent wages, but also ensure that you have more of your wages. We take over 60% of the savings contributions and offer an over-compulsory interest on the capital saved.
  • A boost to your leisure time: We pay you 100 francs a month on your lunch check card. You also profit from additional Fringe Bernards worth 150 francs a year and attractive mobile subscriptions.
  • Development and further education: With our talent management, we specifically promote your development – through a wide range of internal and external training opportunities as well as exciting development opportunities.
  • Meaningful work: Sustainability and sun-sustainable work are not just words for us: Join us in shaping Zurich and the future. Let energy be released for a good morning together with us.

About us

ewz has been on the road for over 130 years, dynamic and visionary every day. Whether it's sustainable energy production, smart energy solutions or innovative networking technology, develop with us today what is important tomorrow.
