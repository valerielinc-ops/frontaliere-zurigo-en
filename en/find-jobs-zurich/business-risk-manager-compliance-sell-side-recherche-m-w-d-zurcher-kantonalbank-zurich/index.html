You have drive, appreciate challenges and want to make a difference. Then you support our research team in the implementation and implementation of contemporary compliance processes as well as their further development in sell-side equity and bond research. The multi-award-winning research department of the Zürcher Kantonalbank is a lively area with constantly new ideas and innovations. We cover around 190 public companies, real estate funds and investment foundations as well as around 180 bond issuers throughout Switzerland – more than any other institution in Switzerland. Our analysts thus form the most important research team in the country. As a Business Risk Manager, you ensure that all compliance requirements are adhered to at all times, take the lead in monitoring and documenting them and continuously develop the existing processes. For the analysts, the departmental management and the control room of the bank, you are the central contact person in day-to-day business for all compliance issues.

  • Acting as a sparring partner: for analysts: for compliance issues
  • Taking over the deputy head of Business Development

  • Very good understanding of financial analysis, especially compliance-relevant aspects

  • Very good German skills in word and writing, English skills are an advantage
  • Exciting and diverse range of tasks in the area of risk and compliance topics with broad insight into the bank
  • Motivated, dynamic research team with committed colleagues who are looking forward to working with you
  • We count on your ideas and offer you freedom for your impulses in the further development of our compliance processes
  • Various opportunities to develop personally and professionally
