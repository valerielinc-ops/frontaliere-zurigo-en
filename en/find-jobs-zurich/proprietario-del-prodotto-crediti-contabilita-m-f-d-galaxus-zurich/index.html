Full description

For Hermes, one of our three Finance Developer teams in the Order Management Area, we are looking for you as Product Owner (m/f/d). The goal of our Finance teams is to provide optimal financial processes throughout the company to make working life easier for our departments and to develop efficient banking connections, payment processes and exciting financing features.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Headquarters, Pfingstweidstrasse 60b, 8005 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Vision, roadmap and prioritization of Hermes products are your responsibility – Success is measured by outcomes, not output
  • You know our accounting, debtor and banking processes in detail and translate the needs of the finance department and adjacent teams into clear initiatives
  • Together with your team, you develop scalable solutions while keeping an eye on the overall system instead of individual requirements
  • Projects you cut small, release early and learn from real data

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: in agile product development as well as in the development of ERP systems with a focus on finance / accounting
  • Completed studies (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): In a relevant area
  • Extensive experience in the successful management of projects from conception to rollout
  • A strong affinity for financial issues and technical details
  • Both the development team and your stakeholders are deeply rooted in technical and professional processes
  • Knowledge of agile project management and accounting topics is essential
  • The willingness to question “as it has always been done” and propose more efficient, automated or effective solutions
  • Have the courage to try things without planning them down to the smallest detail
  • German (flowing)
  • English (flowing)

  • Personal responsibility & freedom: Our working environment is characterized by our values, an open feedback and a positive culture of error
  • Design and decision-making leeway: Via our innovation board, everyone has the opportunity to contribute their ideas directly to the management
  • Values instead of blabla: cooperative, innovative, pirate, self-responsible, ambitious – these five values we all demand and live.
