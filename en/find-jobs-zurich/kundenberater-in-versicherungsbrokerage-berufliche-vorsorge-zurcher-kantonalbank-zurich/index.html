As an unbound insurance broker, we advise and support small and medium-sized companies on the topics of occupational pension provision (BVG, KTG, UVG, UVGZ).

We are a motivated team. Together, we want to accompany our customers responsibly and independently and provide them with permanent care. We have a collegial and open-minded team and an appreciative corporate culture at the leading bank in the Zurich economic area. From the very beginning, you will get the opportunity to take on responsibility, contribute your experience and continue to develop.

Your tasks

  • Independent and holistic advice and professional support of entrepreneurs: in and around the topics of occupational pension provision (BVG, KTG, UVG, UVGZ)
  • Development of customized solutions according to the needs of customers in the field of occupational pension and personal insurance
  • Recognizing and exploiting customer potential, deepening and expanding the customer relationship across departments
  • Build, maintain and actively develop a comprehensive relationship network, internally and externally
  • Appearances as a speaker:in at internal bank events
  • Positioning and representation of the bank in external events
  • Completed basic commercial training (bank or insurance)
  • Completion of a further education, ideally in the preventive care area (technical college, higher vocational training, Federal FA or similar)
  • Completed intermediary examination (VBV) or dipl. Financial planner with federal certificate
  • Very good knowledge of the pension (BVG) and personal insurance sector (KTG/UVG) as well as in-depth market knowledge in the collective foundation sector are advantageous
  • Independent, responsible and quality-conscious working methods
  • Enjoying customer contact and high skills in negotiating and presenting
  • Flexible and team-capable personality
  • Very good knowledge of German, reliable knowledge of English and/or French
  • We work in a vibrant we-culture, courageous, networked and at eye level, together solve difficult tasks and can rely on the support of our team
  • The opportunity to take the next step in this task together with us personally and professionally
  • An employer who takes care of her employees and invests in them
  • A centrally located, easily accessible and attractive workplace in the heart of Zurich with the possibility to organize your work flexibly (hybrid)
  • An attractive salary package with extensive ancillary benefits and employee benefits – from A such as retirement savings to Z such as zoo tickets
