Full description

Are you ready to shape the future of core banking technology?

At Vontobel, we work on the central platforms of our investment house. Our Avaloq core banking system and its core platforms form the foundation of many business-critical processes. For our Core System Management team, we are looking for a motivated and technology-enthusiastic personality who takes responsibility, drives modern automation and ensures the stable operation of our platforms.

You are not simply part of an operating team – you actively shape the further development of our core banking landscape.

Your tasks with us

  • You plan, coordinate and distribute releases in the Avaloq Core Banking System and on the container platform (ACPR).
  • You ensure the successful introduction of changes across all environments – from development to production.
  • You take responsibility for end-of-period processing in Avaloq.
  • You will operate, monitor and optimize Avaloq and ACPR platforms together with internal and external partners.
  • You will continuously develop and automate deployment, build and release processes.
  • You work with modern CI/CD methods and advance the standardization of technical processes.
  • You analyze technical incidents and identify sustainable improvement measures.
  • You will work closely with Avaloq specialists, software engineers, test engineers, service managers and infrastructure teams.
  • You bring your own ideas and actively shape the future of our core banking platform.

What are we looking for?

  • You have experience in the operation, engineering or release management of business-critical applications.
  • You are interested in modern platforms, automation and complex technical contexts.
  • Ideally, you know Avaloq or want to familiarize yourself with this environment.
  • You bring experience with Git as well as CI/CD pipelines: ideally you know GitHub Actions, Jenkins, Bamboo or similar tools.
  • Safe handling of Unix/Linux systems as well as good knowledge of scripting or automation are advantageous.
  • You work structured, self-responsible and solution-oriented.
  • You communicate appropriately and enjoy working across teams.
  • Very good German and English skills round off your profile.
  • Readiness for planned weekend assignments and pikett.
  • You use modern automation and AI-supported tools to continuously develop operational and engineering processes.

What do we offer?

  • Top location in Düsseldorf or Zurich with attractive premises.
  • Training opportunities and mentoring to promote your development.
  • The latest technology for your success and your career.
  • Agile working environment, which is reflected in our thinking, working methods and mutual trust.
  • Flat hierarchies and cooperation at all levels.
  • Shared team spirit.
  • Exciting projects with the opportunity to take responsibility.
  • Market-oriented remuneration (including bonus) as well as excellent social benefits.
  • If desired, an additional ten days of unpaid leave per year can be obtained.

We are looking for talented people and not just a CV that meets all the criteria. Even if you don’t bring everything we ask of you, you should apply.

Place of work The position is currently based in Zurich. In 2030, the workplace will be moved to Baar in order to further strengthen cooperation with key stakeholders in the business.
