Senior Privacy & AI Governance Specialist (temporary position)

Zurich | Working from home up to 40% possible | Reference 8160

At SIX, we drive the transformation of financial markets. What sets us apart drives us ahead: we are a unique blend of tradition and future, foundation and growth, local roots and global relevance. Join us and contribute to shaping the future of data protection and responsible AI.

We are looking for a motivated and experienced privacy professional to support our team on a temporary basis for approximately 8 - 10 months.

What You Will Do

  • act as a trusted advisor to business, IT and corporate functions on data protection topics (GDPR and Swiss DSG)
  • support and execute core Data Protection Officer (DPO) activities, including data breach management, data subject requests (DSRs) and regulatory assessments
  • conduct and review Privacy Impact Assessments (PIA), Transfer Impact Assessments (TIA) and other risk assessments related to personal data processing
  • provide pragmatic compliance guidance in projects, including digital initiatives and AI use cases
  • contribute to the development and implementation of AI governance frameworks and responsible AI practices
  • support awareness and training activities across the organization
  • collaborate closely with an international team of privacy and compliance experts

``

What You Bring

  • several years of hands-on experience in data protection, privacy or compliance (ideally in a complex or regulated environment)
  • strong knowledge of GDPR and Swiss data protection regulations
  • experience with DPO-related activities and a pragmatic, business-oriented approach
  • solid understanding or strong interest in AI governance and emerging regulatory requirements
  • ability to work independently with a high level of ownership and accountability
  • strong stakeholder management and communication skills across different functions
  • fluency in English and German (both required)
