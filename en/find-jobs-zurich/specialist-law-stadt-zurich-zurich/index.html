Full description

As a centre of excellence, we make an important contribution to the supply of drinking water to more than one million people. We operate in a dynamic environment that encompasses both public and private law issues. For legal support of the management and the main departments as well as the medium-term establishment of our own legal service, we are looking for a proven specialist. If you enjoy a diverse task with a high degree of personal responsibility and freedom of design, we look forward to your complete application.

Tasks

  • You are the central contact person of the water supply for legal issues and advise the management and the main departments in legal matters.
  • In close coordination with the Legal Service of the Department of Industrial Enterprises You ensure that legal requirements are always met.
  • You create, check and negotiate contracts of all kinds, accompany the preparation and follow-up of notarizations and easements and support the responsible internal bodies in tenders and submissions.
  • Your tasks also include the analysis of consultations and political initiatives at the urban, cantonal and federal level, on which you write well-founded statements in the interest of water supply.
  • You bring Your legal expertise is specifically integrated into the further development of the internal regulations and also has the opportunity to participate strategically in city-wide projects or to provide valuable impulses in committees if necessary.

Profile

  • You have a law degree with several years of professional experience in public administration, preferably with a lawyer's patent
  • In addition to in-depth knowledge, you also bring practical experience in both public law and private law
  • You are an open and initiative personality who thinks in a networked and structured way, likes to work independently and act in a solution-oriented way.
  • Your integrity, convincing appearance is rounded off by clear and appreciative communication (stylish German in word and writing)

We offer

  • Plenty of design freedom in a collegial and agile working environment
  • The attractive employment package of the city of Zurich
  • Flexible forms of work and extensive training opportunities
  • A modern infrastructure with good public transport connections

About us

Züriwasser. Anytime. For another 150 years. That is our vision. The long-standing successful history of water supply in Zurich is also our ambition for the future.
