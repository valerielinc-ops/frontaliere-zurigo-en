Full description

Are you a transformation-driven professional with a passion for reimagining how organisations operate at scale? Do you thrive at the intersection of strategy, innovation, and execution? If so, this is your opportunity to make a lasting impact at a global level. About the Role As an Operational Excellence Lead , you will be a key driver of business transformation within a high-impact, globally connected team. At the core of this role is the opportunity to design and shape new operating models for e.g. fast follow and facility schemes — reimagining how these critical commercial insurance structures function end-to-end, and setting the standard for how they scale globally. This role places you at the heart of evolving underwriting operating models, enabling the organisation to operate smarter, faster, and more effectively. You will lead strategic change initiatives, challenge the status quo, and work collaboratively across Lines of Business, Integrated Platforms, and Global Functions to design fit-for-purpose operating models that balance local market needs with global consistency. You will also champion digital innovation — including GenAI — to unlock new levels of productivity and deliver measurable, sustainable business impact. This is not a back-office role — it is a high-visibility, high-influence position for someone who is energised by complexity, motivated by outcomes, and committed to shaping the future of how a global organisation operates. Key Responsibilities

  • Champion digital innovation and adoption , including the integration of GenAI and emerging technologies to enhance productivity and deliver measurable business impact.
  • Lead end-to-end transformation initiatives , designing and implementing operating models that balance global consistency with local market needs.
  • Drive operational rigor and efficiency by identifying process gaps, designing solutions, and embedding best practices across the organisation.
  • Manage and execute complex projects from inception through to delivery, ensuring milestones, timelines, and quality standards are consistently met.
  • Engage and align senior stakeholders across Lines of Business, Integrated Platforms, and Global Functions to build consensus and drive change.
  • Analyse and optimise underwriting operating models , identifying opportunities to enhance service differentiation, compliance, and user experience.
  • Monitor and report on transformation outcomes , using data-driven insights to track progress, demonstrate value, and inform strategic decisions.
  • Collaborate across geographies and functions to ensure initiatives are inclusive of diverse market needs and deliver consistent global impact.

About the Team The Operational Model Excellence (OMEX) team is a global transformation function dedicated to evolving end-to-end operational models across Lines of Business (LoBs), Integrated Platforms (IP), and Global Functions. With a mission rooted in driving operational rigor, user relief, and capability building, the team works at the intersection of people, processes, systems, and data — all within a unified framework. OMEX leads business transformation and strategic change initiatives that enhance efficiency, compliance, and service differentiation. The team designs and implements end-to-end operating models that balance local market needs with global consistency, while championing innovation and digital adoption — including GenAI — to improve productivity and deliver measurable business impact. Joining OMEX means becoming part of a forward-thinking, collaborative, and globally connected team that is actively shaping the future of how the organisation operates. Here, your ideas matter, your expertise is valued, and your work creates real, visible impact.

You are a strategic thinker and a pragmatic doer — someone who can navigate complexity with confidence, inspire teams and stakeholders, and translate ambitious transformation goals into tangible results. You bring deep domain expertise, a collaborative mindset, and an unwavering commitment to excellence. You demonstrate st rong communication and storytelling skills, with the ability to present complex ideas clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences.   • Strong expertise in digital innovation and transformation , including familiarity with emerging technologies and the application of tools such as GenAI to drive productivity and business value.
  • Demonstrated ability to motivate, engage, and align diverse stakeholders , building trust and consensus across functions, geographies, and seniority levels.
