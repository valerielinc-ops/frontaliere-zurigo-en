Full description

With us you can get involved and help shape the customer advice of the future and the digitalization of banking. We are looking for you: a personality that thinks ahead. Someone with whom we as a team shape the future with fresh ideas, innovations and passion. Come on.

Mansioni

  • Our customers, whom you inspire with a smile and your positive charisma as soon as they enter our office.
  • With a lot of humanity, you approach our customers with a holistic basic consultation.
  • Your team, because you recognize the existing customer potential together with them and develop customer relationships further.
  • Existing customers, whom you can also advise and inspire by telephone for Migros Bank with your winning and positioned nature.
  • Our experienced customer advisors by actively supporting them and performing administrative tasks.

Requirements

  • 1 to 2 years: private client consultancy
  • Completed Vocational Apprenticeship (EFZ): Banking & Finance
  • You have initial practical experience in the sales-oriented advice of bank customers in the retail environment.
  • You appreciate the personal and telephone customer contact and have a flair for recognizing customer needs and their potential.
  • You are a communicative, structured as well as ambitious and warm personality.
  • You have a hands-on mentality combined with the will to take ownership and responsibility.
  • in German (negotiation certain)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Employee offers: Annual generous well-being credit
  • Child care: up to CHF 300 per month per child to external child care
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Migros Bank: accounts, mortgages and financial services with preferential terms
  • Modern infrastructure: future-oriented and ergonomic workplace
  • Mobile subscription: discounted subscriptions and registration also for family and friends
  • Mobility: ZVV BonusPass for discounted public transport
