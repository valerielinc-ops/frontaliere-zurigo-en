Full description

For the Ambulatorium der Medizinn Oncologie-Hämatologie we are looking for a qualified nurse HF/FH as of 1 January 2027 or by agreement. We offer you regulated working hours as well as a varied job in a team with a high level of expertise. By conducting clinical studies, you will have insight into the latest developments in oncology and hematology.

Tasks

  • Your work includes the outpatient support and care of patients suffering from an oncological and/or hematological disease.
  • You administer various tumor therapies and transfusions
  • You take supportive measures and organize the various office hours with

Profile

  • You have several years of professional experience in the somatic acute hospital
  • You enjoy the demanding care of oncological and hematological patients and their caregivers
  • If you bring an NDS in oncology, this is beneficial but not a condition
  • Your strengths include flexibility, resilience and team-oriented action
  • In addition, you bring a high level of social skills and are a humorous, reliable and innovative personality
