Working at Sprüngli

Do you want to actively shape the future of our supply chain and make a direct contribution to delivery capability with data-based decisions? We look forward to your application. In Paradeplatz we are looking for a committed Demand Planner & Process Automation Specialist (m/f/d) by appointment. In this key role, you will be responsible for operational demand planning, work closely with sales, production and logistics and actively shape the further development of our supply chain and the development of future Sales & Operations Planning (S&OP).

What you do with us

Process development

  • Further development of our disposition processes
  • Participation in digitalization projects
  • Building the future S&OP process
  • Participation in SAP S/4HANA optimization projects

Operational needs planning

  • Responsibility for short- to medium-term sales and demand planning (0–28 days)
  • Maintenance and control of LSF requirements in SAP S/4HANA
  • Ongoing optimisation of delivery capability
  • Analysis of sales trends and short-term fluctuations in demand

Inventory and flow management

  • Monitoring of stocks, transit stocks and closed and quality stocks
  • Analysis and correction of shortfalls
  • Ensuring high data quality in all sales channels

Cooperation with branches

  • Assisting branches with disposition issues
  • Support for the centralisation of goods planning
  • Training and support of employees regarding new processes

Interface management Close cooperation with

  • Production (PPS)
  • Logistics
  • Purchase
  • IT
  • Marketing
  • Controlling

Training

  • Further training in project management or experience in managing sub-projects of advantage

Professional experience

  • Supply Chain Management

You bring that with you

  • Analytical thinking

  • Enjoyment of process optimization
