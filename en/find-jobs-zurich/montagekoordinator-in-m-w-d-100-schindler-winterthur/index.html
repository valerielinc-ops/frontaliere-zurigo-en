Your new challenges

You are an organizational talent and keep an overview even in hectic situations? Do you like to coordinate different stakeholders and manage to align people successfully to common goals even without direct authority? Then we look for you.

  • You plan and coordinate assembly orders as well as the assignments of installers and subcontractors
  • You ensure on-time and quality order processing as well as optimal utilization of resources
  • You are a central contact person for customers, suppliers and internal departments and communicate proactively and solution-oriented.
  • You lead installers and partners to joint success and ensure a high level of customer satisfaction

Your ideal conditions

  • You have basic technical training as well as experience in scheduling, assignment planning or order processing
  • Commercial or business education is an advantage
  • You have a pronounced service and customer orientation, a high sense of responsibility and work solution-oriented
  • A high resilience, flexibility and sovereignty even in demanding and stressful situations characterize you additionally
  • You show leadership, have a convincing appearance and the ability to successfully coordinate different stakeholders and drive joint solutions
