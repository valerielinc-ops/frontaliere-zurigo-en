Centre for Dentistry

Job start 1 November 2026 or by arrangement, temporary

The Center for Dental Medicine of the University of Zurich (ZZM) is an internationally recognized clinic, training and research center. In six specialized clinics and one institute, dentists, dental assistants and scientists work at the forefront of knowledge and in interdisciplinary teams in order to achieve the highest quality in patient treatment, teaching and research. The Clinic for Reconstructive Dentistry (RZM) ensures the care of patients with a need for reconstructions with a complex starting position and aesthetically high demands. In order to restore the chewing function and the aesthetics after tooth loss, dental implants are anchored in the bone and used as artificial tooth roots, depending on the initial situation.

Tasks

  • Reception of patients: Inside and external guests of the RZM
  • Oral and written appointments for patients: inside
  • Planning of appointment series for patients:inside and coordination between patients:inside, dentist:inside and dental technicians:inside
  • Providing dental information and initial contact for ongoing treatments
  • Administrative management of the clinical agendas of dentists:
  • Coordination and organisation of patient referrals
  • Handling of incoming and outgoing mail and taking on additional administrative activities

Profile

You have completed training as a dental assistant as well as additional commercial qualifications. Your personal strengths include loyalty, secrecy, teamwork, resilience and initiative. In addition, they work very independently, perform well-groomed and have very good manners. It is particularly important to us to have an open, helpful and cordial personality that keeps calm and overview even in hectic situations and works reliably at all times. Perfect German skills as well as very good English skills in word and writing round off your profile. Further language skills are advantageous. They enjoy working in a dynamic team and are characterized by a high willingness to provide services.

We offer

We offer you employment on cantonal terms at a centrally located workplace in the heart of Zurich with good connections to public transport. The position is initially limited to one year, with the possibility of a subsequent permanent employment. She expects a varied and interesting activity on the pulse of dental teaching and research as well as the cooperation in a well-established team that looks forward to you. We look forward to your complete application documents and getting to know you.
