As an employee: in medical technology, you ensure that medical devices remain reliable, safe and ready for use. With your technical know-how, you make a direct contribution to modern patient care.

Your main tasks

  • You are responsible for the maintenance, repair and further development of a modern medical infrastructure with different devices and systems
  • Preventive maintenance, repairs, functional tests and safety controls are carried out according to manufacturer and quality specifications
  • When integrating medical systems into existing IT and network environments, you work with
  • You document all service and maintenance work and ensure compliance with regulatory and qualitative requirements
  • In medical engineering projects you are actively involved

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a medical technician: in HF or a comparable technical training in electronics, electrical engineering, mechatronics or system engineering
  • Ideally, you will have experience in the technical service of medical devices, laboratory or hospital technology
  • In the area of IT infrastructure, networks and Windows you have good knowledge
  • You like to work in a team and think and act interdisciplinary
  • Social competence and good manners distinguish you
  • You are an independent, reliable, innovative and dedicated personality
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing round off your profile

Our offer

In a modern working environment, we offer you a varied task in medical technology that promotes you. You will get in-depth insights into interesting development projects at USZ or our partners. Together with you, we want to meet the challenges of changing medical technology and ensure an ambitious and agile cooperation in the projects of the overall renewal of the USZ.
