Full description

Do you enjoy traditional crafts and are enthusiastic about high-quality baked goods? 🥐🍞 Do you like to advise and communicate safely with our customers? 😊 Then you are exactly right with us! ✅ Let us be «tämä success». 🤝🧡

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Eastern Switzerland, Hausbäckerei Neuwiesen, Strickerstrasse 3, 8400 Winterthur

Mansioni

  • You are a master baker 🥖✨. With your skills, you can professionally produce various bread products and pastry products.
  • Whether you are working on dough, table or oven – you carry out your tasks with precision and care, including cleaning tasks 🧼.
  • You are always in the middle of it. In day-to-day business, you actively support and ensure that everything runs smoothly.
  • You have responsibility ✅. With your commitment, you ensure that our customers are supplied with fresh products at all times.
  • You pay attention to the highest standards ♦. Hygiene and safety regulations are carefully implemented.

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: as a bakerin-confectionerin
  • Completed basic or further education: as a bakerin-confectionerin
  • You are a professional in your field 🥖. With sound expertise in the bakery and pastry sector, you shine in your craft.
  • You are a team player 🤝😊. Your open, motivated and team-oriented personality makes collaboration pleasant and successful.
  • You are resilient 💪⏰. Good physical fitness and the readiness for day and night shifts distinguish you.
  • You work independently and efficiently ⚡✅. Your high quality awareness ensures first-class results.
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Mobile subscription: Highly discounted conditions for private mobile subscription also for family and friends
  • Occupational pension provision: Attractive pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation in self-selected courses
