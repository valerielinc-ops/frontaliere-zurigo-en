Full description

Key responsibilities Do you like working in dynamic environment with focus on global markets products? Do you seek an opportunity to work cross-product sales teams and business stakeholders in driving the sales strategy forward? We’re looking for a self-driven, analytical, forward-thinking individual who can to high degree work independently to join our Zurich UGM COO distribution team to support the UGM EMEA Distribution.

Key responsibilities include (but are not limited to)

  • drive day to day COO BAU activities: business analytics / performance summaries / MI, controls, problem solving, management support,
  • drive preparation of highly qualitative outputs such as presentations and various MI documentation for Senior Management
  • support orchestration of AI agenda within COO and platform transformation in sales processes though automation / AI
  • maintain robust operating platform front-to-back incl. ownership of key controls and front to back product flows / process mapping & review
  • support implementations of the sales and client strategies with product business area/sales team
  • serve as liaison between lines of business and various support partners, helping to drive accountability across all internal functions
  • work with Technology to support implementation of front to back systems / improvements and MI, incl. One UBS Revenue reporting, CID management across divisions

Your Career Comeback We are open to applications from career returners. Find out more about our program on ubs.com/careercomeback .

The team The UGM COO distribution team is a high-performing, strategic and cross-functional team that is responsible for the formulation, implementation and support of the key WM- aligned Distribution focused initiatives, and processes that impact our client’s experience. This is a diverse role spanning across Execution Sales as well as parts of Digital Platforms, covering elements of strategy, analytics, budgeting, performance monitoring, regulatory impact, F2B controls, tech implementation, AI enabled solutions and comprehensive understanding of the sales and client business. Members of the team will have broad exposure across the business often collaborating with sales teams, management, support and control functions to execute a wide array of initiatives.

Your skills and experience

  • You’re curious to explore how AI can improve how we build, deliver, and optimize workflows. You do this with sound judgment – validating outputs and aligning with policies, risk standards, and ethical use
  • min. 4 years relevant industry experience (Business Mgmt, COO, business transformation and/or consulting experience)
  • You have first practical experience in designing / implementing AI driven solutions to enable business and optimize processes.
  • min. Bachelor degree in Finance or Business Administration
  • ability to work independently on given deliverables / book of work, drive the work / implementation forward with limited supervision and deliver preliminary outcomes / analysis for further assessment
  • skilled in synthesizing qualitative and quantitative data effectively into presentable format for delivery to senior management
  • partnership approach with a high degree of collaboration across COO team globally and control functions
  • self-starter, takes initiative, ability to work autonomously and juggle multiple priorities, and ensure completion of deliverables in timely fashion
  • very good Ms Office skills, PowerPoint, and Excel in particular
  • analytical and comfortable with exposure / presenting to senior management
  • ability to identify, articulate and escalate issues / risks to your line manager timely
  • technology and AI affinity and interest in developing the skillset, first working experience is an asset
  • understanding of bank/financial services operational processes and product knowledge as big asset
