Full description

Installations in business and industry shape everyday work. The company offers versatile projects with development opportunities. For our client we are looking for an activity

  • carry out installations in commercial, industrial and residential buildings
  • perform maintenance work and faults independently
  • implement wiring exactly according to plan
  • implement orders cleanly and on time
  • Requirements
  • Training degree as an assembly electrician
  • Several years of practice as an auxiliary electric assembler or auxiliary electric assembler alternatively possible
  • Knowledge in high- or low-current systems
  • Self-responsible working in everyday life
  • Driver's license category B would be desirable offer
  • Choice between determinations and temporary use
  • Assignments in varied projects
  • Space for professional further education
