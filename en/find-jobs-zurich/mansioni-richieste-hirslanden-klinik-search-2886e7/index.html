Place of work: Hirslanden Clinic Hirslanden | Zurich Occupancy: Immediately or by appointment Type of employment: Permanent Reference number: 34915 Are you looking for a challenge and would like to demonstrate your leadership skills as an intensive care team leader in a dynamic environment? The Hirslanden Clinic has a total of 22 beds available. The main focuses of our interdisciplinary intensive care department include cardiac, visceral and neurosurgery.

  • We offer our patients a high-quality range of care in new premises equipped with the most modern infrastructure. YOUR TASKS
  • Organizational and technical support of the intensive care unit's nursing team, as well as active participation in nursing in shift operations
  • Independent coordination of the 22 beds in the intensive care unit and the beds in the monitoring ward in collaboration with the intensive care doctor in the early and late shifts
  • Checking and monitoring the specified quality standards and characteristics of the clinic, in collaboration with the management team
  • Instructing, supporting and advising the intensive care unit employees in nursing, professional and administrative matters organizational matters
  • Responsible and empathetic leadership of a team of around 20 employees
  • Conducting employee appraisals and qualifications as well as recognizing employees' resources and their targeted support and motivation
  • Conducting team meetings, regular exchanges with the management team and the professional trainers as well as taking on additional responsibilities such as sub-projects in various fields of work (further training, equipment, etc.) YOUR PROFILE
  • You are trained to become a Dipl. Intensive care expert NDS HF or have completed an equivalent specialist training in intensive care and have at least five years of professional experience in an intensive care unit of a Swiss acute hospital
  • You may have already gained initial leadership experience and you enjoy leading, supporting and developing a team
  • You would like to combine different profiles in your job
  • with us you can coordinate, lead and work at the bedside in a balanced relationship
  • Solution-oriented and entrepreneurial thinking and acting awaits you A well-rehearsed team with a strong hands-on mentality that offers you support at all times and gives you a lot of joy at work. For the perfect start, we offer you individual training tailored to your needs as well as flexible working time models that enable you to combine work and family. In doing so, we are laying the foundation for sustainable, long-term cooperation. At the same time, we support you at all times in your development and provide further training opportunities to shape your career path. You can also expect other attractive benefits such as four additional days off with a 100% workload as compensation for your changing time. Our staff restaurant is open to you every day with a wide range of offerings and fair prices. We always meet each other in a friendly, helpful and respectful manner in the hallways. At Hirslanden as an employer you can expect quality at the highest level in every respect.
