Children's Hospital Zurich Eleonorenstiftung | online since : 21.08.2026

Gastroenterology

Your tasks

  • You take over the medical and administrative management of the department of gastroenterology and nutrition and lead a team of cadre doctors, fellows, assistant doctors and nutrition consultants.
  • Together with the team, you cover the entire specialized and highly specialized gastroenterological diagnosis and therapy in accordance with the service mandate of the Health Directorate.
  • With other pediatric subspecialties, visceral surgery, anesthesia and intensive care unit, you maintain a close cooperation.
  • You represent gastroenterology professionally and organizationally inwards and outwards.
  • You are responsible for university teaching and research in the field of gastroenterology and nutrition as well as for the further education of the Fellows.

Your profile

  • Diploma of Physician and Specialist in Paediatric and Adolescent Medicine as well as major title in Paediatric Gastroenterology or equivalent
  • Research card with its own gastroenterological focus and habilitation
  • Proven clinical competence and leadership experience in the entire field of gastroenterology and hepatology as well as proven expertise in nutrition
  • Interest in education, further education and student teaching
