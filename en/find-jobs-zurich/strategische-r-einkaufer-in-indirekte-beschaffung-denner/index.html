Full description

Behind the scenes in indirect procurement ensure that our store runs? Welcome to the winning team. You negotiate smart, optimize costs and make services transparent. Want to make a difference with us? Denner. This is our store.

Mansioni

  • As a business partner, we advise and accompany the needers in all matters of procurement and negotiation competence.
  • Identify and realize optimization potential within the framework of independently managed purchasing initiatives
  • Design and further develop strategies for the product categories under management
  • Ensure professional supplier management and continuously optimize the supplier portfolio
  • Manage and monitor contracts
  • Actively manage stakeholders and work in cross-functional teams with different committees, departments and specialists

Requirements

  • At least 5 years of professional experience: in a related capacity
  • Completed basic or further education: in business administration, supplemented by a purchasing specialization
  • In-depth purchasing experience in various categories of goods, with a focus on procurement in the field of logistics and shop construction (e.g. facilities and technical services)
  • In-depth understanding of strategic procurement
  • Strong negotiating skills
  • Sovereign and winning appearance among very different stakeholders
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • English (advantageous)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Part of the Migros Group: Strong brand anchored in the retail trade under the Migros Group umbrella
  • Migros Bank: Better off with interest and fees on your private, investment or mortgage accounts.
  • Holidays: 6 weeks holidays for all and even more days for service anniversaries.
  • Mobile subscription: Spoiled for choice: With Galaxus Mobile, Wingo or Sunrise already from CHF 10.- one or the other really connected.
  • Flexible place of work / home office: One to two days a week working at home. Except in IT - we are even more flexible.
