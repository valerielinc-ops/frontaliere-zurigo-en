Full description

Bräcker AG, the world’s leading supplier of rings and ring travelers for ring spinning machines, is a subsidiary of the Rieter Group. Headquartered in Pfäffikon, Switzerland, Bräcker offers added value to its customers with highly developed industry knowledge, innovative solutions, excellent after-sales services and a global presence. The company’s main products – rings and ring travelers for ring spinning machines – are manufactured in Pfäffikon and Wintzenheim (France). The company’s product range also includes grinding machines for the maintenance of roll covers.
