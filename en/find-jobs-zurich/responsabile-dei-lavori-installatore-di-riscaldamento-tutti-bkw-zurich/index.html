We make habitats worth living.

Become part of a motivated, professional and diverse team in a dynamic company. Together we strengthen our position as a first-class company in building technology!

Your tasks

  • Leadership and further development of a small heating team with clear, user-friendly communication and high social skills
  • Independent handling of heating projects in compliance with deadlines and with a pronounced awareness of costs and benefits
  • Participation in construction and coordination meetings to ensure the smooth running of the project
  • Responsibility for the needs-based procurement of materials and equipment
  • Support of the project manager in the preparation of overdraft and final accounts
  • Taking over piketting services to ensure reliable customer service and high asset availability

You bring that with you

  • You have a successfully completed apprenticeship as a heating installer: in EFZ or building technology planner: in EFZ field of heating
  • Preferably, you already have work experience in a similar position
  • Your independent and reliable working method and your high level of personal responsibility distinguish you
  • You have a skilful and secure appearance in dealing with customers and have good communication skills
  • Even hectic times do not bring you out of peace
