Do you want to make a difference and make a social contribution to the promotion of culture in the Canton of Zurich? Then you are right with us. The Department of Culture defines the strategic orientation of cultural promotion and implements the cultural policy of the Government Council. It promotes contemporary cultural creation in various fields, supports projects and institutions, awards prizes and awards and supports communities in their cultural programmes. With these activities, the Department of Culture contributes to a diverse cultural life in the Canton of Zurich.
