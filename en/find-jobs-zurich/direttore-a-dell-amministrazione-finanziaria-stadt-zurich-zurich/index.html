Full description

The Financial Department of the City of Zurich operates as a cross-sectional organization, the finance, human resources management, IT, real estate, taxes and housing promotion departments. It thus plays a key role in shaping the future of the city of Zurich as an attractive location for living, working and business. For the Financial Administration department, we are looking for a successor as a result of the health-related resignation of the director as of spring 2027.

Tasks

  • Management of the service department and its professional, business and digital development.
  • Overall responsibility for the central financial and steering tasks of the city (financial and task planning, budget, annual accounts, financial controlling).
  • Ensure sustainable and forward-looking financial planning.
  • Development of strategic decision-making bases for city council, municipal council and other political bodies as well as participation in shaping urban financial policy.
  • Management of the management and directly subordinated employees as well as responsibility as an employee for around 50 employees.

Profile

  • Completed university studies (UNI/ETH) in business or economics, supplemented by further training in leadership and management, public finances and digitalization
  • Comprehensive financial and strategic competence and strong analytical and conceptual skills
  • Several years of proven leadership experience and the ability to develop an organization strategically and future-oriented
  • Sound understanding of political processes, experience working with political authorities
  • Convincing communication, negotiation and representation skills as well as a secure appearance

We offer

  • challenging and exciting leadership task in a dynamic and diverse environment
  • An appreciative working atmosphere
  • Diverse training opportunities
  • very good employment conditions (social benefits)
  • Workplace in the center of Zurich (near Stauffacher) with the opportunity to work in the home office

About us

The variety of tasks of the city administration is unique and extends into all aspects of life (education, health, environment, housing, culture, transport and much more). In addition to advising and supporting all areas of responsibility of the city on financial topics, we create the financial planning, the overall city budget and the group accounting. We are also responsible for payment transactions, participation management, procurement and risk and insurance management.
