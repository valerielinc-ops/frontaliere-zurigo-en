Full description

In this versatile key role, you will lead a team of several people, advise our customers competently and actively and proactively promote sales deals. By building long-term customer relationships, recognizing customer needs and identifying sales opportunities, you contribute significantly to the success of the center and to achieving the company’s goals.

Luogo di lavoro: Club School Migros, Club School Migros Zurich Oerlikon, Hofwiesenstrasse 350, 8050 Oerlikon

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Leadership, coaching and targeted further development of a dedicated team with a focus on performance, cooperation and customer satisfaction
  • Ensuring professional customer advice and active sales of the entire educational offer at the reception and by phone
  • Responsibility for the planning, scheduling and optimal utilization of the course offer as well as assignment planning of the teachers
  • Active co-creation of an attractive and professional center environment through sales promotion measures, the organization of events, the coordination of infrastructural concerns and the submission of optimization proposals
  • Management and monitoring of business indicators such as turnover, class utilization and budget compliance
  • Responsibility for administrative processes such as invoice control, student refunds and correct wage runs

Requirements

  • Several years of professional and first management experience: in a comparable function, ideally in the educational or service environment
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): with business focus, preferably in the commercial environment, retail trade, tourism or in the hotel industry
  • English (flowing): Every other foreign language is of great advantage
  • Strong sales strength as well as enjoyment of customer advice, customer loyalty and the recognition of customer needs
  • Winning, professional appearance and strong communication and organizational skills
  • Flexibility and readiness for irregular missions (evening and Saturday services)
  • Performance-oriented, entrepreneurial personality with a pragmatic and solution-oriented way of working, high affinity for numbers and openness to change and new ideas

Cosa of friamo

  • Central location: Central locations with excellent public transport connections
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Diverse training offer: Generous offer of free, internal training, free participation in selected courses (national languages and health courses) and financial participation in the total course offer.
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Wide range of discounts (car rental, hotels, events, etc.)
  • Self-responsibility & freedom: Varied tasks with high self-responsibility
  • Public Transport Participation: Public Transport Cost Sharing and Mobility CarSharing

Recruitment process

  • Submit application documents: For a complete application, we need your CV, certificates and diplomas. A photo in the CV and a letter of motivation are not necessary. We live equal opportunities for all applicants. We have a standardized recruitment process. If this does not work for you, mention it in your application dossier.
  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision to advance or withdraw from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Delayed video interview: For a large number of suitable applications, a delayed video interview can optionally be conducted. As part of this video interview, the answers to previously recorded questions are recorded with the web or mobile camera. This gives us the opportunity to get to know more candidates within a short time in order to invite suitable people for the next round. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Expert Discussion: In a specialist discussion, specific knowledge relevant for the execution of the job is requested by the manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, the team. If possible, there is time to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
