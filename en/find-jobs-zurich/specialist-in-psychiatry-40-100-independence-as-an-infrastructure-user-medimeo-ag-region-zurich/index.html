Here you actively shape a new practice – with a complete infrastructure, an existing patient base and no entrepreneurial risk. They start without complicated acquisition and can fall back on an existing, well-stocked patient base right from the start. At the same time, you enjoy a high degree of entrepreneurial freedom without having to bear the administrative burdens of your own practice: As an infrastructure user, you benefit from the existing practice space, well-established processes and the support of the practice staff. 40% of your revenue will remain directly with you, while the remaining share covers the use of the infrastructure and the comprehensive support services of the practice. In this way, you combine the advantages of independent work with the security and relief of an established practice structure. The practice is aimed at people with complex mental illnesses and focuses on continuity, stability and recovery-oriented care. From the outset, you work in an interprofessional setting: an Advanced Practice Nurse (Psychiatric Mental Health) takes care of clinical coordination and follow-up, while an experienced senior physician is available in the background for technical advice.

Their tasks

  • Outpatient psychiatric diagnosis and progress assessment
  • Drug treatment and medical prescriptions
  • Participation in crisis intervention
  • Close cooperation with nursing, psychology and Spitex
  • Professional exchange in the interprofessional team
  • Co-design of practice structures and treatment concepts

Your Competences

  • Specialist title Psychiatry and Psychotherapy (FMH or equivalent)
  • At least three years of professional experience in Switzerland

(mandatory for fulfilling the official requirements for this activity)

  • Joy of Outpatient Psychiatric Work
  • Interest in integrated care models
  • Structured, reflected and responsible working methods
  • Affinity to complex clinical pictures (e.g. trauma) is beneficial

Their advantages

  • Outpatient work without night and weekend services
  • Flexible workload (40-100%)
  • No economic risk, no administrative overload
  • Support through Advanced Practice Nurse and Spitex
  • Senior physician as a professional resource in the background
  • Existing patient base from start
  • Co-creation opportunities in a newly emerging practice
  • Modern understanding of work: clear roles, short paths, trust
