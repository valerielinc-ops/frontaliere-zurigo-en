Full description

Students' Department, Admissions Office Job as of 1 January 2027 or by arrangement, limited in time to 30. September 2027. The Students Department is responsible for all centrally organised student administration processes at the University of Zurich such as admission, change of course and study program, collection of semester fees, leave and exmatriculation. The Admissions Office of the Students department evaluates the applications for a bachelor's degree program of persons with foreign prior education as well as all applications for a master's, doctoral and teaching diploma study program. The entire authorisation procedure shall be carried out electronically. In order to strengthen our team of admissions offices, we are looking for a dedicated specialist or specialist admission. The post is limited in the framework of a maternity representation initially until 30 September 2027. There is the possibility of subsequent further employment in a permanent function.

Tasks

  • They check the digital application documents for completeness and authenticity and carry out corresponding verifications.
  • They assess diplomas and other documents taking into account the legal framework with regard to their formal recognition by the University of Zurich. If necessary, research foreign educational qualifications and education systems.
  • In close cooperation with institutes and faculties, you create admission and rejection orders.
  • They inform prospective students about admission requirements and the application process by e-mail and telephone and support them with technical questions about the application portal.

Profile

You are a committed, service-oriented personality with a high willingness to learn and an interest in complex issues. Administrative and organizational tasks are performed independently, structured and with great care. Even in times of high workload, you keep an overview and set priorities. The contact with prospective students from all over the world brings you pleasure and you appreciate the cooperation in a collegial team.

They shall have at their disposal

  • Interest in international education systems and pleasure in dealing with educational qualifications from all over the world
  • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent education
  • ideally professional experience in higher education, public administration or in a comparable job
  • stylish German and very good English skills in word and writing
  • Further language skills are advantageous
  • analytical thinking skills as well as the ability to handle complex questions independently, structured and solution-oriented
  • a high digital affinity and willingness to quickly familiarize yourself with new IT applications and legal framework conditions

It offers you a varied and responsible activity in an international, dynamic and socially relevant environment. They deal daily with educational qualifications and education systems from all over the world and make an important contribution to the consistent and legally compliant implementation of the application, admission and matriculation process at the University of Zurich. After training, it is possible to work partially in the home office.
