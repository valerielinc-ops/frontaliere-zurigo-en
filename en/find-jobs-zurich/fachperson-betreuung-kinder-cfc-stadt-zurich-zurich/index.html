Full description

The Kita Selnau is looking for 1. January 2027 motivated and committed professionals.

Tasks

  • You design a differentiated pedagogical everyday life based on pedagogical concepts, such as the situational approach and the BULG educational concept, which focuses on the strengths of the children and the needs and interests of the children.
  • You work in the evaluation and reflection of everyday pedagogical life as part of the quality development.
  • You work closely with parents and other professionals.
  • You take on independently delegated tasks, such as habituation, targeted observations and organizing parental evenings.

Profile

  • Completed training as a specialist Care EFZ, specialty children
  • Interest in education-oriented nursery education (BULG, Situational Approach, Pikler)
  • Personal maturity, resilience and good organizational ability
  • Administrative and organizational skills as well as good PC knowledge

We offer

A challenging, varied challenge in a dynamic and pedagogically oriented environment in which you can focus on working with the children. As part of the Zurich city administration, you benefit from the attractive employment conditions of the city of Zurich.

About us

With around 2,200 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a social Zurich. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods. To accompany and support young children in their development in the important first years and thus give them a good foundation for their future life: we are committed to this with great commitment. In the city of Zurich, the childcare division of the social institutions and companies operates 11 daycare centres, a crisis intervention group and a residential group for infants, a family meeting place and at two locations "accompanied visiting meeting places" for families in conflictive separation situations.

The nursery Selnau offers 31 nursery care places, including two places for children with special care. It is divided into 3 groups (2 mixed-age, 1 infant group), which are located next to each other and operationally connected.
