Full description

Take responsibility for your team and actively shape payment transactions. We are looking for you: a leader with an overview, strategic thinking and fresh ideas. Come on – at the bank, which has been putting people first with 1,800 employees since 1958.

Mansioni

  • You take over the professional and personnel management of 10 to 12 employees and create a motivating working environment.
  • Ensuring that all orders are completed on time and in perfect quality
  • You can independently make informed decisions in complex research and support your team in finding solutions.
  • Professional error management for the detection, analysis and correction of affected transactions.
  • You maintain and develop close cooperation with internal stakeholders (e.g. sales, compliance) as well as external financial institutions.
  • Systematic optimization of our internal processes for the benefit of our customers as well as increasing the efficiency and effectiveness of the team through solution-oriented approaches.

Requirements

  • At least 4 years: Professional and/or functional professional experience with strong leadership and methodological skills.
  • Higher School: Banking
  • You have profound knowledge of the rules in payment transactions.
  • High personal responsibility, flexibility and reliability distinguish you.
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • French (good knowledge)
  • Italian (advantageous)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central place of work (GABS): Optimally accessible by train (10 minutes from Zurich HB) or car
  • Modern infrastructure: future-oriented and ergonomic workplace
  • Child care: up to CHF 300 per month per child to external child care
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Coaching: Personal coach for executives
  • Migros Bank: accounts, mortgages and financial services with preferential terms
  • Advantages of cadre: One week holidays per year more for cadre members
