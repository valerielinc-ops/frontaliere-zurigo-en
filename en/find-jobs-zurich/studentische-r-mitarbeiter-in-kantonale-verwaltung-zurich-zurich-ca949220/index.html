Canton of Zurich State Archives of the Canton of Zurich Student staff / 40% The State Archives of the Canton of Zurich (www.staatsarchiv.zh.ch) is the central archive of the Canton of Zurich and its historical legal predecessors. It transmits original documents from the Cantonal Council, the Government, the cantonal central and district administration as well as the cantonal courts, notaries and independent public institutions and makes them available to the public. The Development Department is looking for 1. December 2026 or by agreement a student employee 40 %. Their tasks

  • Written and verbally eloquent, quick and reliable
  • Independent and efficient working methods, but also enjoyment of working in a team Our offer We offer a three-year part-time job, a pleasant working atmosphere in a medium-sized team, a job opportunity closely related to your studies and the opportunity to gain professional experience in archiving.
