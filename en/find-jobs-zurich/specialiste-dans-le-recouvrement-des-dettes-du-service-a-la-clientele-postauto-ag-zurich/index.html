Full description

Is a positive customer experience also a top priority for you? Do you want to make a difference and participate in the success story of PostBus? Then you are exactly right with us in customer service debt collection! There are no chances with you.

PostAuto AG operates a collection agency for various transport companies for driving without a valid ticket, with corresponding customer service.

You can do that.

  • In this exciting function, you will handle demanding written and telephone requests from passengers without a ticket.
  • You correspond with internal and external customers and with law enforcement agencies.
  • You arrange installment payments, payment period extensions and independently handle the invoice, reminder and collection shipping.
  • You book current payments independently in SAP and monitor the daily data transfer with other collection agencies.
  • You take on special tasks such as initiating criminal cases or helping with the development of legal debt collection.
  • You serve our debt collection software Pink, eSchKG and the digital inbox.

You bring that with you

  • You bring a basic vocational training in the commercial sector or as a specialist or expert customer dialogue.
  • Ideally, you could already gain your first professional experience in public transport.
  • In addition, you have very good interpersonal and communicative skills.Even in challenging telephone conversations, you will not let yourself get out of peace and maintain your friendliness and patience.
  • You bring good MS-Office and SAP knowledge.
  • You communicate verbally and in writing stylishly in German and Italian. Any other foreign language is beneficial.
