Full description

Eraneos is an international strategy, transformation and technology advisory group that helps companies thrive in a dynamic digital world. With first-class experts from business and technology, we empower our customers to set new standards for sustainable transformations – from strategy to implementation. Whether in the design of sustainable organizations, the development of new potential through data and AI or the protection of companies through holistic cybersecurity: We deliver results pragmatically and purposefully. Our team of around 1,200 dedicated employees is represented in offices in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, Denmark, Sweden, Austria, the UK, Singapore and the USA and combines global perspectives with strong local roots. Around 450 employees work at the Swiss locations in Zurich, Bern and Lausanne. This is the job - your main tasks

  • Together with our team, you will advise our customers on strategy, architecture and implementation of concepts as well as solutions in the field of digital platforms.
  • Thanks to your creativity, analytical skills and expertise, you will work closely with our clients to develop the best technical solutions for complex business issues and challenges.
  • You will evaluate business requirements and develop innovative solutions in the form of products, applications or services. You actively bring the technical vision of our customers to success.
  • You develop system/software architectures and solution concepts on public cloud platforms such as Microsoft Azure and AWS.
  • You formulate well-founded recommendations and create roadmaps for proposed solutions – including product/service evaluation or make-or-buy decisions.
  • You run proof-of-concept or MVP (Minimal Viable Product) projects to validate proposed solutions.
  • You are the coordinator between business and IT and master communication at all levels. This job is for you if
  • You have a university degree in computer science or a related field.
  • You have at least three years of relevant experience in financial services (banking or insurance) and three years as a solution architect.
  • You have knowledge and experience in solution design and/or software development on public cloud platforms such as Azure or AWS.
  • You have already dealt with the conception and use of CI/CD tools/toolchains as well as IaC (infrastructure-as-code) frameworks.
  • You communicate and present safely and in line with the target group up to the management level.
  • You are a team player, can control teams and promote a positive team climate.
  • You can present and explain complex solutions in German and English. As an i-top you bring with you
  • Relevant certifications (e.g. CPSA, Open Group, Azure, AWS, Scrum or SAFe) Your next step towards a successful application
