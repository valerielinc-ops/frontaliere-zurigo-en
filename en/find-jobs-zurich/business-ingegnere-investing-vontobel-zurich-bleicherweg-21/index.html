Business Engineer Investing — Vontobel

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

At Vontobel, we are committed to shaping a better future and providing investment opportunities that enable our clients to thrive. Our Private Clients platform combines Vontobel’s investment expertise with modern technology and a personal touch, delivering tailored solutions for the diverse needs of our clients.

What would your role be?

  • Join our Private Clients Business Engineering team and take end-to-end business ownership of our Digital Meeting and Holistic Advice applications – the tools our client advisors use to prepare, run and follow up client meetings and to advise clients across their entire wealth.
  • Cover a broad range of responsibilities, from acting as deputy product owner and managing the backlog to running technical analyses and user acceptance testing.
  • Translate client and advisor needs into clear, testable user stories with well-defined acceptance criteria, and work closely with developers to ensure a shared understanding of the requirements throughout implementation.
  • Collaborate with neighboring teams – UX, Investment Platform and CRM/CLM – to deliver seamless end-to-end solutions.
  • Train and support our client advisors, drive adoption of the applications, and keep technical and user documentation up to date.

What are we looking for?

  • Several years of experience as a Business Engineer, Business Analyst or Product Owner, ideally in banking or wealth management.
  • Experience with client-facing applications and agile software development (e.g. Scrum), including close collaboration with UX.
  • A basic understanding of software architecture and APIs, and confidence working with Jira, Confluence, and GitHub; ideally first hands-on experience with AI-supported features.
  • A track record of delivering complex digital solutions from concept to production, on time and within budget, combined with a passion for great user experience and high-quality standards.
  • The ability to grasp complex topics quickly and explain them to both business and IT audiences, and a collaborative mindset that moves things forward together with the Private Clients platform teams.
  • Fluent in German and English (must-have).
  • Affinity for Artificial Intelligence (AI) and a willingness to embrace AI-enabled tools and ways of working.

What do we offer?

  • Top Zurich location with collaboration spaces and free lunch.
  • Friendly, diverse team in an open-office space.
  • All the latest technology to help you excel and advance.
  • Agile environment in how we think, the way we work and our trust for each other.
  • Flat hierarchy with collaboration at all levels.
  • Campus feeling and a collaborative spirit.

We are looking for talented people, not just a CV that ticks all the boxes. Even if you do not have absolutely everything we have asked for, if this role sounds like a good fit for you, we encourage you to apply.

Why Vontobel?

At Vontobel, you are building better futures for our clients and society, as well as for yourself. By 2030, we want to be known as one of the leading and most trusted global investment firms. We have 2,300+ engaged professionals, based in over 25 locations globally and headquartered in Zurich.

We are both majority family-owned and publicly listed. Our capacity for long-term focus, on topics such as sustainability, is underpinned by the security of the family’s 100-year commitment. Driven by an ownership mindset, you can unlock your potential and translate insights into opportunities. We are hiring talented individuals who are determined to deliver in a changing world and who come from a wide variety of backgrounds. Do you want to be Vontobel?
