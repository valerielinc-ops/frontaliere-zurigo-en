Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With more than 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric offer of Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in ambulatory, day-clinical and inpatient settings. The Group employs around 1800 people and achieved a turnover of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For patient administration we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement

Their tasks

  • Case and cost language management
  • Invoicing of cases and benefits
  • Support and processing of telephone and written customer inquiries including bill rejections
  • Collaboration in projects and process adaptations with a focus on digitalization

Your profile

  • You have a basic commercial and/or medical education with experience in the hospital environment
  • You have experience in billing for ambulatory and stationary services and know the tariff models
  • You work solution-oriented and appreciate the working method according to the lean principle
  • You are familiar with MS Office
  • Ideally, you have experience with the Ines and Opale systems
  • They value the personal treatment of internal and external customers

Our offer

  • Interesting and varied work in a collegial and motivated team
  • Opportunity for further training and individual support
  • Flexibility in terms of working time and work organisation
  • Current employment conditions
  • Discounted food in the restaurant

For further information and questions, please contact Eveline Leuenberger, Head of Patient Administration, tel. +41 71 929 62 17.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
