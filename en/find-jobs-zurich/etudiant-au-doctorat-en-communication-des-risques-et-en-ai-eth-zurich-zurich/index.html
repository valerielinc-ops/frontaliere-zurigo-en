Doctoral student: in risk communication and AI 100%, Zurich, temporary Printed Printing How can people make informed decisions in the event of an incident? How do you inform the population, authorities and emergency agencies about the likelihood of further shocks after an earthquake? Become part of the risk communication research group at the Swiss Seismological Service (SED) at ETH Zurich and help us answer these questions. Specifically, you will investigate how earthquake probabilities can be comprehensibly prepared for different user groups. You will also explore how helpful, trustworthy and reliable AI-supported systems are when it comes to conveying event information. The SED is the Federal Office for Earthquakes. You will work with researchers from various disciplines as well as with practitioners, working together with the TdLab and the Professorship for Ethics, Technology and Society at ETH Zurich for the transdisciplinary aspects. This gives you the opportunity to combine theory and practice and to incorporate your research directly into real applications. Project background After each major earthquake, the probability of a similarly large or even a larger quake increases in the short term. Although the probability increases significantly, the absolute values often remain small. This makes comprehensible and addressable communication challenging. In the earthquake probability project, you will investigate how to comprehend such information in a comprehensible, helpful and appealing way for various applications. In addition, you are involved in the European project EARTH-AID, where we develop agent-based AI to support the assessment of geological hazards.
