Location: Zurich/Basel Work Pattern Percentage: 80% - 100% Join Our Innovative IT Team as an Azure Cloud Engineer! Are you passionate about cloud technology and eager to make a real impact? We’re seeking a dynamic and proactive Azure Cloud Engineer with expertise in Infrastructure as Code and a structured problem-solving mindset. In this role, you’ll quickly master and manage our unique dual-cloud environment, including the exclusive "Sovereign Cloud Switzerland" platform—operated entirely within Switzerland—and Deloitte’s regional cloud workloads. You will play a key role throughout the entire lifecycle of our cloud platforms, driving continuous improvement with a strong focus on automation and operational excellence, l everaging emerging AI technologies where appropriate. Collaborate closely with our business teams to seamlessly integrate new applications, enhancing efficiency and productivity. Plus, you’ll have the advantage of tapping into the extensive knowledge and resources of a leading g lobal organisation. If you’re ready to innovate, automate, and elevate cloud operations in a supportive and forward-thinking environment, we want to hear from you! How you can make an impact

  • Develop and manage the technical capabilities of our cloud operations team in Switzerland.
  • Build and maintain the cloud platform while also supporting multiple Azure solutions to meet business needs.
  • Collect and exchange best-practice knowledge among our international cloud teams.
  • Develop and maintain automation solutions to deploy platform and application workloads.
  • Monitor and manage technical escalations for cloud operations and identify new tools and practices to improve upon our cloud platform. Who we are looking for
  • You consistently demonstrate excellent communication skills. You proactively reach out when needed and step in with your expertise to add significant value, ensuring that projects run smoothly and efficiently.
  • Not only are you curious and comfortable to adapt to new technologies, but you’re also a great learner with the ability to share your knowledge with your team members.
  • You are a certificated Azure Admin Associate (AZ-104) and Azure DevOps Expert.
  • (AZ-400), any additional certifications (cloud architecture, system administration, or development) are a great asset.
  • Over the course of your career, you have gained experience of automation and provisioning approaches, using tools such as Ansible, Terraform, Bicep, Bash, PowerShell, Python, and C#.
  • In-depth knowledge of configuring and managing Windows and Linux environments, experience with infrastructure (databases, Active Directory, DNS, firewalls, networking) in Azure and on-premises data centres would be an advantage.
  • Knowledge of agile software development tools and methodologies.
