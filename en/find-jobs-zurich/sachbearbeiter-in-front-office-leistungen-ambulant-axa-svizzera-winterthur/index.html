80-100%, Winterthur / Smart Working

Shape your future – together with us!

Do you enjoy direct customer contact, think in a solution-oriented manner and would you like to help shape one of the most dynamic business areas of AXA Switzerland? Then our health care team is the right place for you.

Since 2017, AXA health care has been one of the fastest growing areas of AXA Switzerland - today we support over 200,000 customers with supplementary health insurance and innovative services such as our exchange and billing service. We take over the administrative work so that our customers save time and money.

When it comes to health care, you will work in the prevention department at AXA Switzerland. With our diverse and progressive products, we create freedom and security for our customers so that they can live their lives carefree.

AXA is the number 1 pension partner in Switzerland. With our diverse and progressive products in the areas of health and private and professional pensions, we create freedom and security for our customers so that they can live their lives without any worries.

This is what awaits you

  • You are the first point of contact for insured persons, service providers and other stakeholders - by telephone, in writing and via digital channels such as the customer portal and myAXA app
  • You ensure competent, solution-oriented support and a first-class customer experience
  • You use modern, AI-supported tools to efficiently process customer concerns and actively contribute to the further development of our digital services
  • You coordinate concerns independently and forward them to the relevant departments in a targeted manner
  • You work closely with the back office and other interfaces and promote smooth, cross-departmental collaboration
  • If you are suitable, you will be involved in cross-departmental projects

This is what you bring with you

  • Commercial training with experience in the health insurance environment and/or basic medical training (e.g. pharmaceutical assistant, druggist, MPA, FaGe, nursing specialist)
  • Enjoy working with digital tools and be open to new, AI-supported ways of working
  • Strong communication skills and enjoyment of challenging customer discussions
  • Very good knowledge of spoken and written German; a second national language (French or Italian) is mandatory, knowledge of English is an advantage

This is what we offer you We are convinced that satisfied employees deliver the best performance. That's why we invest in benefits that offer noticeable advantages - beyond the usual.

Flexibility and freedom: Smart working (flexible working model) annual working time, 5 weeks of vacation (from 50 years 6 weeks), holiday gift of 2 weeks every 5 years, holiday purchase up to 10 additional days/per year

Job and family: Family-friendly meeting times, 22 weeks of maternity leave, 8 weeks of leave for the other parent, daycare subsidies, company family allowances, days off to look after relatives and in challenging personal situations

Pension provision and insurance: Attractive pension fund with extensive extra-mandatory benefits including optional savings options, up to 30% discount on insurance products

Flexible benefits: AXA share plan (employee participation), CHF 500 per year on the Swibeco benefits platform, discounted lunch or lunch card, Smart Working flat rate CHF 200 per year

Mobility: Refund for half-fare travelcards, Reka-Pay up to CHF 1,000 per year (20% discount), fleet discount, free e-charging station, AXA rental bikes

Healthy balance in everyday working life: Free check-ups, annual health weeks, a variety of exercise offers (online/offline), massage offers, relaxation rooms, employee care, online health portal, various seminars/events related to health prevention and mental health

Development: 3 days of individual learning time, free coaching, a wide range of learning and development opportunities, financial participation in further training, promotion of internal mobility, talent development offers, seminars for employees 55+ (AXA Horizon), involvement in communities on various topics, participation in innovation projects (Innoday, AXA Hackathon, UnConference), 2 days for volunteer work (environmental & social)
