We are looking for a dynamic and motivated Logistics Apprenticeship to join our team. This position offers a unique opportunity to gain practical experience and deepen your knowledge of logistics operations in a leading industrial company.

Responsibilities

  • Attend receipt, control and storage of goods.
  • Participate in the preparation of orders and packaging of products for shipment.
  • To assist in the management of stocks and the conduct of regular inventories.
  • Contribute to the optimization of logistics flows and continuous process improvement.
  • Collaborate with production and supply teams to ensure smooth operations.
  • Ensure compliance with existing safety rules and quality procedures.
  • Enter logistical data into the information system.

Qualifications

  • Preparation of a BAC pro, BTS or DUT degree in Logistics, Transport or Supply Chain.
  • Mastery of basic computer tools (Pack Office).
  • A good level of technical English is a plus.
  • Rigor, organization and sense of detail.
  • Ability to work as a team and communicate effectively.
  • Motivation, proactivity and strong learning capacity.
  • Permit B desired.
