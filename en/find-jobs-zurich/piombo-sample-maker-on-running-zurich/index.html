Role overview

In Short

We are looking for a motivated and detail-oriented Sample Maker with a passion for athletic and ready to wear apparel.

In this role, you will be responsible for creating high-quality prototypes, muslins and mockups in partnership with pattern engineers, product development and designers to bring product concepts to life.

  • In Short
  • We are looking for a motivated and detail-oriented Sample Maker with a passion for athletic and ready to wear apparel.

Additional details

  • This person should be self driven and eager to continuously grow their skills in apparel development.
  • Stay current on both athletic and ready to wear industry trends and advanced sewing techniques, specific to appearance and function.
  • Understanding of garment construction and pattern interpretation.
  • Ability to read and follow patterns, spec sheets, and technical sketches. Experience working with all fabrications
  • Strong attention to detail and a commitment to quality craftsmanship.
  • Basic knowledge of garment dyeing, washing, or textile printing techniques (DTF, DTG).
  • As an integrated function in the design and development organisation; we are detail-minded problem solvers; guiding and executing apparel technical development within tight timelines and the highest possible product quality, both on the shelf and in use.
