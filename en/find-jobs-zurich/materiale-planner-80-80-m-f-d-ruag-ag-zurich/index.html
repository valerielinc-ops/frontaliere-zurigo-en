Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organizations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Creation and maintenance of demand planning as well as order data and billing in SAP
  • Ongoing monitoring of delivery dates, quantities and availability
  • Early detection of material bottlenecks and deadline risks
  • Initiation of countermeasures in coordination with purchasing, project management and logistics
  • Coordination of priorities for scarce materials or delivery delays
  • Support of the programme manager in all administrative and organisational matters
  • Supporting project purchase
  • Organization of meetings and workshops including the necessary preparations (invitation and agenda list, etc.)

You bring that with you

  • Commercial or technical training of advantage with experience in project management or job preparation and SCM
  • Very good MS-Office and SAP user knowledge (S/4 Hana)
  • Enjoy working in an interdisciplinary team
  • Very flexible, resilient, organizational talent – joy to work in a dynamic environment
  • Stylistic German as well as very good English and French skills
  • Friendly appearance, motivated, dynamic

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.

Over the area

We provide secure and interoperable communications solutions for the Swiss army and national and international security agencies and are responsible for the integration, operation and maintenance of security-relevant information, communication and command systems. This position is part of the Swiss Army’s existing Integrated Military Telecommunications System (IMFS) project.

Your well-being is important to us. That is why you have many advantages with us. These include, for example:

  • Leisure activities: Our employees are close to our hearts. That is why we give them a day off for their birthday, offer a diverse sporting offer and the opportunity to receive additional days off against pay deduction in addition to the annual holiday credit.
