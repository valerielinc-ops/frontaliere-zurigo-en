Full description

Two Spice – that’s us! A creative and innovative gastronomy and lifestyle company from Zurich. Since 1990 we bring fresh ideas to the table and develop unique concepts and brands that inspire. Our versatile companies are at home at the central hotspots of various cities and stand for quality, design and pure pleasure moments.

Welcome to Rice Up!, inspired by the vibrant ambience of the street food markets in Asia. The bowls are prepared fresh and as desired in the wok before the eyes of the guests. Immerse yourself in the world of Rice Up! and become part of our team that is passionately and creatively redefining Asian cuisine. Do you want to be part of our team? Now is your chance!

Tasks

  • Active cooperation as a host personality in the service
  • Assumption of various administrative tasks such as daily billing, correspondence, ordering and time management
  • Responsibility for all operational processes in the absence of management
  • Support in the leadership, training and operational planning of the team as well as promoting a motivating working environment
  • Planning and implementing sales promotion measures
  • Professional management of reservations and guest feedback
  • Compliance with and control of operational hygiene and quality standards

Requirements

  • Several years of experience in the gastronomy or hotel industry, ideally in a management position
  • Strong leadership and organisational skills
  • Sovereign, service-oriented appearance and pleasure in guest contact
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method, even in hectic situations
  • Knowledge of service planning, billing systems and hygiene standards is advantageous
  • Knowledge of German at least. B2 level in word and writing, other languages are a plus
