Full description

For our exclusive private bank at Bahnhofstrasse 1 in Zurich, we are looking for a professional and dedicated service force to strengthen our team. In this responsible position, you make an important contribution to providing our guests and customers with a first-class, attentive and discreet service at all times. With your professional appearance and your pronounced service orientation, you create a pleasant atmosphere and contribute significantly to an outstanding customer experience.

Tasks

Guest support and customer service

Professional and warm support of customers, guests and employees at meetings, events, and business lunches

  • Ensuring a first-class service experience and high guest satisfaction;

o Friendly reception and competent care of guests on site

Preparation / follow-up of meetings and events

o Preparation and attractive installation of conference and meeting rooms, including setting and decorating of tables/rooms; ensuring a well-maintained, clean and representative environment; follow-up of meetings and events, including clearing, cleaning and restoration of premises

Service & Catering

Professional service of food and drinks at meetings, business lunches and events; o Close cooperation with our internal cook in the planning and implementation of events; ensuring the availability of beverages, fruits, coffee capsules and other consumables

Organization and coordination in the F&B team

o Responsibility for the control and management of stockpiles and the execution of orders for drinks and materials; support in planning and coordinating daily service processes; o Coordination and instruction of external service professionals at larger events

  • Active participation in the continuous improvement of our service quality and guest satisfaction

Reception and administrative support

o Welcome and care of guests at the reception; receiving and forwarding incoming telephone calls; support for general administrative tasks

Profile

  • Completed training at a hotel management school or comparable training in the field of hospitality, hospitality or hospitality;
  • Several years of experience in the service sector, ideally in the upscale gastronomy, hotel industry or banking environment;
  • Strong experience in direct customer contact and a high understanding of first-class customer service;
  • First experience in the reception area is advantageous;
  • organizational talent as well as a structured and careful workforce with particular attention to details;
  • High service and customer orientation and initiative;
  • Flexibility, resilience and professionalism even in demanding and dynamic situations;
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Knowledge of French or other languages is advantageous;
  • well-groomed, professional appearance and excellent communication and manners
  • The Client Services – Food & Beverage (F&B) position is a 100% permanent position based in Zurich.
