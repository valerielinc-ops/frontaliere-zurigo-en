Paediatric radiology

Your tasks

  • In this role, you will be responsible for the assessment of radiological examinations in the entire spectrum of pediatric imaging diagnostics.
  • You perform sonographies and screenings independently and evaluate the results professionally.
  • In interventional paediatric radiology, you actively participate and develop your practical skills.
  • Participation in interdisciplinary boards and case discussions is part of your everyday clinical life.
  • You participate in the radiological service and take over night and weekend services.
  • With increasing experience, you take on responsibility in the supervision of assistant doctors.
  • You are involved in clinical and scientific projects and contribute your own ideas.
  • You prepare cases and specialist topics and present them in internal and external training.

Your profile

  • You have a specialist title in radiology or are about to graduate.
  • You have a special interest in pediatric radiology and interdisciplinary cooperation.
  • Your way of working is committed, structured and characterized by high professional curiosity.
  • You enjoy developing yourself professionally and actively participate in teaching and further education.
  • Ideally, you are interested in scientific activity and want to develop academically.
