About the Role Are you looking for an exciting opportunity in Cloud AI and Data platform capabilities ? Are you passionate about everything Azure Cloud? Then join us as a Senior Product Owner This role requires to drive the evolution of Swiss Re’s Azure AI & Data Platform into a scalable, governed, and business-aligned product, enabling:

  • Enterprise-wide adoption of AI/GenAI and data capabilities
  • Faster realization of data-driven business outcomes
  • Strong alignment with risk, compliance, and cloud governance standards

Your main responsibilities

  • Own and drive product vision, roadmap, and OKRs for Azure AI & Data services
  • Translate enterprise priorities into measurable product outcomes and value realization
  • Ensure alignment with GTS strategy, cloud transformation, and business unit needs
  • Own the product backlog, prioritization, and delivery sequencing
  • Define clear product features, acceptance criteria, and KPIs
  • Make independent scoping and prioritization decisions balancing value, risk, and cost
  • Ensure delivery predictability through Agile execution and governance rhythms
  • Accountable for platform capabilities across:
  • Azure OpenAI / AI Services and responsible AI controls,
  • Databricks / Synapse / Fabric for analytics and data engineering
  • Data governance, lineage, catalog, and platform standards
  • MLOps / LLMOps lifecycle management
  • Act as the primary interface between business, engineering, and platform teams
  • Influence senior stakeholders and drive cross-functional alignment
  • Communicate roadmap, risks, and outcomes to leadership forums
  • Embed security, regulatory, and audit controls into product design
  • Ensure adherence to cloud governance frameworks and policies
  • Drive standardization and guardrails for safe AI/data adoption
  • Monitor product health via adoption, cost efficiency, and reliability metrics

About you

The following proven technical skills will be required

  • 12+ years in product management / cloud platform / data engineering roles
  • Deep hands‑on knowledge of Microsoft Azure.
  • Strong experience with Azure Databricks as an enterprise data platform capability.
  • Proven delivery in enterprise-scale cloud or data platform environments
  • Hands-on expertise in Infrastructure as Code (IaC) such as Bicep and CI/CD Azure DevOps pipeline integration for automated quality and reliability checks
  • Strong grounding in Platform engineering concepts: golden paths, paved road tooling, self‑service automation, RBAC, and internal developer platforms (IDPs).
  • Experience in Azure landing zone design , platform automation design, DevSecOps tooling, network topology & connectivity, access management & privileged identity design, platform monitoring, security architecture, high availability architecture design.
  • Experience in DevOps methodology (preferably DevSecOps), both technically and organisationally, including continuous deployment, delivery pipelines and test environments.
  • Good stakeholder communication. Ability to work with Delivery Directors, Security Leads, Architects and engineering teams.
  • At ease working in a transformational and complex environment at a fast-pace and getting things done.
  • Proficiency in English is required.
