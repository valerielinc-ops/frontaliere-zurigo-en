Full description

Do you like to use your neurological expertise for well-founded insurance medical assessments? Then you are right with us. What you do

  • You assess neurological questions on the basis of files or as part of medical examinations.
  • You will prepare actuarial assessments of causality, working ability, reasonableness and damage to integrity.
  • You document your assessments clearly, comprehensibly and addressable in written reports.
  • You work closely with professionals from different disciplines and bring your neurological expertise to case discussions.
  • You actively participate in the further development of processes and professional standards. What you bring
  • You have the specialist title Neurology and at least 3 years of clinical professional experience.
  • You work reliably and independently, take responsibility and make informed decisions.
  • You are well-versed with digital work tools and medical information systems.
  • You appreciate the interdisciplinary cooperation and communicate safely with different stakeholders.
