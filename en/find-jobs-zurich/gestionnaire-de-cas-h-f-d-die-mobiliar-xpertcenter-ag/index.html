Full description

This is what awaits you

In this position, you are part of XpertCenter AG, a subsidiary of Mobiliar. Together with your team, you will accompany insured persons in the event of illness and accidents.

This is what you do with us

The core of your job is the independent handling of complex cases in connection with day-care insurance, accident insurance, liability insurance and prevention.

You take on a coordinating role for the professional reintegration of sick and injured persons. For this purpose, you develop integration plans in cooperation with all parties involved (e.g. doctors, insurance companies, employers) and accompany and document the course of the case according to the process steps of case management.

The cases are to be expected especially in Zurich and the Eastern Switzerland region. At the same time, you can be used flexibly throughout German-speaking Switzerland. The position is associated with a high travel activity.

This is what you bring with you

  • Graduate degree at university level with social or health background and further education in case management
  • Several years of professional experience in integration, case management or personal case management
  • Experience in providing case management services to third-party customers is beneficial
  • Sound knowledge of social security law
  • Proven consulting experience, as well as strong negotiating skills and assertiveness
  • High customer orientation and entrepreneurial thinking
  • Fluent knowledge of German, very good knowledge of another language, especially Italian and English are a plus

Working with us

A dedicated team is waiting for you in a dynamic and modern environment with exciting interfaces.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

Our family allowances are based on the highest cantonal contributions of the cantons of Bern, Vaud and Zurich.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
