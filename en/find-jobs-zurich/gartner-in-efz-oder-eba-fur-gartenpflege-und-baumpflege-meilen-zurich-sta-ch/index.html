Full description

The work includes the care of lawns and the cutting of beds. The company attaches great importance to environmentally conscious approach and safe working methods. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Lawns regularly mow, fertilize, verticute and resow
  • Preservation of beds by pruning, moulding and thinning
  • Remove dead branches on trees and perform tree care
  • Remove weeds on pathways, beds and lawns mechanically or manually
  • Perform seasonal gardening such as pruning, fertilization and winter protection

Requirements

  • Gardener EFZ or EBA
  • Experience in lawn and plant care including cutting techniques
  • Knowledge of tree maintenance and security controls
  • Ability to seasonally plan and perform gardening
  • Competence in dealing with various gardening machinery and equipment
  • Environmentally conscious approach to pest control and fertilisation

Offer

  • Responsible activity in varied garden projects in the Uster region and the surrounding area
  • Working in an experienced team with diverse tasks in the field of garden care
  • Modern fleet and professional equipment for efficient work assignments
