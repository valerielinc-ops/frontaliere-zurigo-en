Original description

We offer you varied and exciting training! During your three-year training, you will work in various departments such as central secretariat, personnel development, personnel administration as well as marketing & communication and finance.

Get your EFZ through training in administration. With us you will start a varied and meaningful apprenticeship with a future, because as a businesswoman you can expect a supportive working environment from day one. During your training you will be accompanied and supported by our experienced vocational trainers.

We are looking for you for our Corporate Service Center in Dübendorf from August 1, 2027

Apprenticeship as a businesswoman/businessman EFZ

You'll learn that with us

  • Administrative tasks, such as writing letters, protocols and contracts
  • Competent advice and support for our internal and external customers
  • Organizing meetings and different types of events
  • Getting to know and daily use of various systems and tools, such as MS Teams

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed school education (secondary school A or B) with good academic performance
  • Good computer skills (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) desired
  • Mastery of the 10-finger system is required
  • Quick comprehension and good manners
  • Reliable and precise work
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoy a variety of challenges
  • Good knowledge of spoken and written German

What benefits do we offer you?

You are just as important to us as your training is to you! That's why we offer you the following advantages:

  • You get the day off on your birthday
  • CHF 500 towards your compulsory school materials
  • Discounted cell phone subscriptions with Sunrise
  • Free SBB half-fare subscription
  • Every lunch for CHF 5
  • Discounts and offers relating to fitness, leisure and your wellbeing
  • Up to 10% discount on your supplementary health insurance
  • and much more

You start straight away with an exciting introductory day and halfway through your training we invite you to our summer camp for a whole week. Of course, your completion of your training must be properly celebrated, so we organize an unforgettable graduation party for all graduates. And maybe this is just the beginning of your career with us! We offer numerous opportunities to work within our company immediately after completing your apprenticeship.

You can find more benefits and insights on our website.
