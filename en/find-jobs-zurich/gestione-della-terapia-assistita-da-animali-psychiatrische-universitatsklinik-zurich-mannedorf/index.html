Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 60-90% For our innovative and close-to-nature offer "Sandgrueb Animal and Nature-Supported Therapy" in child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy at the Egg site, we are looking for a committed and organizationally strong Their tasks

  • Professional and personnel management of the dedicated team "Sandgrueb Animal and Nature-Supported Therapy"
  • Coordination of the offers on site, such as coordination of the therapeutic uses of horses, donkeys and dogs in close exchange with the veterinary management
  • Continuous daily availability on site as a central, coordinating specialist for all stakeholders
  • Active participation in the day-to-day operational tasks on site, including animal-assisted interventions, experiential education and the implementation of individual and group offers
  • Organisation of patient transfers to child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy centres

Your profile

  • Completed studies or higher specialist training in the field of healing/special education, social work, psychology, medicine, care or a comparable qualification in health or education with focus on children and adolescents
  • Demonstrable experience in a management position with sound personnel management, ideally in the environment of child and adolescent psychiatry
  • In-depth knowledge of pedagogy as well as in-depth and proven experience in handling animals (especially in-depth horse competence required)
  • Further training or experience in the field of animal-assisted therapy is expressly desired
  • Strong skills in interdisciplinary cooperation as well as a basic understanding of business processes (budgeting, controlling)
  • Strong communication, empathetic and resilient personality with high mediation competence at the interface between clinical requirements and practical nature and therapy operations

Our offer

  • Contributing to innovation: participation in forward-looking research and development projects
  • Together for people: Interdisciplinary cooperation for holistic psychiatric care
  • Strong in the team: appreciative cooperation and mutual support
  • Future provision with added value: 60% employer contribution to the pension fund
  • Sustainable travel: Participation in the ZVV-BonusPass or general subscription
