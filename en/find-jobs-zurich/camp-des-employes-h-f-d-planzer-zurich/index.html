Full description

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, get involved and grow with us. Become part of our family and shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us.

What you move

  • When loading and unloading trucks, you are the absolute professional and control the goods and pallets
  • You ensure that the goods land safely and quickly in the right storage space. Chaos? Not with you!
  • You record all material movements in the system and take care of the processing of delivery notes and loading papers
  • You always keep the camp in check and are open for additional tasks to ensure a smooth process.

Why do you succeed?

  • You are ready to work shifts – whether in the afternoon (14:00 to 23:30) or in the night shift (23:30 to 08:00)
  • First experiences in the camp and in dealing with the palettrolli you already bring with you
  • You are the proud owner of a forklift card
  • You are fit and like to get involved – even when storing goods, you always remain resilient
  • Oral and written you can certainly express yourself in German
  • You work reliably, accurately and quickly adapt to new challenges
  • Even if it gets stressful, you stay cool and keep track.

Your Benefits

  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Further education and development opportunities: With us you can develop yourself - we promote and challenge talents
  • Central introductory training: You will spend your first working days in the central introduction, where you will be taught the most important basics
  • Good transport connections: Top transport connections - faster to work, more free time

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
