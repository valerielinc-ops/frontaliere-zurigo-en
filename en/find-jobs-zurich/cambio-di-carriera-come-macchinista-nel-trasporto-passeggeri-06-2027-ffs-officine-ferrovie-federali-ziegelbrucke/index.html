Cross-entry train driver: in passenger transport 06/2027 100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Cross-entry train driver:in passenger transport 06/2027 Brick Bridge, 01.06.2027, 100% Apply now

You can look forward to that.

As a train driver, you make a big deal: at SBB Passenger Transport, you move around 508 million travelers every year. After completing the paid 15-month second training, the job takes you along the most beautiful railway lines in Switzerland. You bring our passengers to their destination on time and thus ensure a positive travel experience with the SBB. You see irregular working hours and shift work as an advantage because you can enjoy your free time while others work. Night and weekend work is also paid for, for example, an hourly wage supplement of 16 francs for Sunday work. For night assignments, you get time surcharges of 10 to 30%, which allows you more free time. In addition, you can design your workload individually from 60 percent after the training to:to the train driver:in your consultation, with different part-time models available. Find out more about the job profile at the next information event. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • As a train driver, you not only take over the top of the train, but also responsibility for safety and punctuality on the busy rail network.
  • During your journey, you are mindful and respond quickly and correctly to signals, speed controls and unpredictable events.
  • You act independently and are always in contact with operations centres and control centres.
  • In addition to driving and maneuvering the trains, checking the technical condition before and after taking over the vehicle is one of your tasks.
  • Thanks to the precisely clocked roster, you can be on time and without further tasks or mails in the well-deserved evening.

You bring that with you.

  • Challenges you approach calmly, make thoughtful decisions even in stressful situations and bring readiness for shift work.
  • For the intensive and demanding training period, you bring a lot of personal responsibility and motivation and are ready to engage in different learning environments – from face-to-face teaching on site to digital self-study.
  • You have completed a three-year apprenticeship or the Matura. Your motivation is more important to us than the industry you come from.
  • You are confident in German (level C2) and bring beginner knowledge of a second national language (level A1).
  • For safety reasons, you have a perfect color sense (no red-green weakness). In addition, you have no entries in the penal and enforcement register.
