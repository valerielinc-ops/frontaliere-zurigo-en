Institute of Neuroinformatics at the University of Zurich

Job as of now

The University of Zurich maintains various zoo-like wild animals within the framework of research projects. In the quality of animal husbandry, animal care and animal welfare, the University of Zurich plays a leading international role in the field of research. For the next development phase of our wildlife farms, we are looking for an engaged and experienced personality as: Head: in animal care and wildlife training 80-100% In this role, you take over the professional and organizational management of a team of animal care providers:innen . At the same time, you are mainly responsible for the training of animals, work practically in animal care and shape the further development of our husbandry, care and enrichment programs. The position offers the opportunity to further develop existing processes, to clearly structure responsibilities and, together with an interdisciplinary team of animal care professionals, researchers and students, to implement high standards in animal welfare, animal care and research practice.

Tasks

  • They lead and coordinate a team of 3-5 animal keepers: professional and organizational.
  • They train animals in the context of animal care and behavioral research, especially with positive reinforcement.
  • They plan work processes, priorities and assignments in the animal care team.
  • They develop clear routines, standards and documentation processes for animal care, training and enrichment.
  • They are responsible for the conception, implementation and ongoing improvement of a species-appropriate and varied enrichment program.
  • They observe, assess and document animal behaviour, health and welfare.
  • They work closely with researchers, veterinarians, animal welfare officers and other internal bodies.
  • They provide practical assistance in animal care, including feeding, cleaning and care of facilities.

Occasional assignments outside the usual working hours and on weekends are expected and are part of the regular working hours.

Profile

What you bring

  • A training in animal care or a comparable qualification.
  • Several years of experience in the care and training of animals, ideally wild animals.
  • Solid experience with training based on positive reinforcement, for example clicker training.
  • High sense of responsibility, reliability and careful working methods.
  • Initiative, creativity and joy in continuously developing animal husbandry and care.
  • The ability to structure processes, set priorities and promote clear communication within the team.
  • You have experience in the guidance, coordination or leadership of employees.
  • They attach importance to an open exchange and strong team spirit.
  • You lead solution-oriented and can resolve conflicts objectively and constructively
  • You can respond to the needs of employees, researchers and external bodies
  • Good oral English skills
  • Written English skills are helpful for reading documents.
