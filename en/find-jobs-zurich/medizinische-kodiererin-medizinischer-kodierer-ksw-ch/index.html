Full description

For our clinic of urology we are looking for an additional PhysCian Associate by immediately or by appointment.

Tasks

  • As a PhysCian Associate, you are involved in the medical team of urology and support the medical care and diagnostics of patients outpatients and also in hospital
  • You are ready to take responsibility, appreciate an independent activity and support the further development of the team and the clinic
  • Arrange diagnostic and therapeutic measures
  • Perform visits and consultation hours as well as documentation and coordination work

Profile

  • You have at least 2 years of professional experience in the somatic acute hospital, knowledge in the field of urology is desired

  • You are motivated to apply your skills in a new field of responsibility and independent work is no problem for you
