Specialist Nursing - Focus on Internal Medicine and Acute Care - Spital Zollikerberg, Zollikerberg (ZH).

Area: Nursing professions.

Welcome to your new place of work.

Urs Wildbolz, Leiter Pflege Bettenstation offers you a job with a selectable amount between 60 -100% as

Specialist nursing - Focus on internal medicine and acute geriatrics

If you too want to make a difference in people’s lives, these are your tasks:

  • You are responsible for the professional care of patients with a focus on internal medicine and acute geriatrics
  • They ensure professional support and quality assurance on the station in close cooperation with the care expert
  • You are actively integrating specialist topics into everyday station work
  • You share your expertise and pass on your enthusiasm for the profession

Become part of the Department of Emergency and Acute Medicine at the Zollikerberg Hospital. In the Clinic for Internal Medicine with continuing education status A, we look after more than 2,500 inpatients, more than 6,000 emergencies and more than 450 patients in the interdisciplinary intensive care unit every year. On our interdisciplinary stations, you can expect a versatile field of activity with a focus on palliative care, internal medicine and surgery. Our stations are guided according to the reference care system.

In our 360° tour, get a first impression of your new place of work.

To make you feel comfortable with us, many benefits are waiting for you. Just to name a few:

  • Ecobonus of up to CHF 650 per year
  • Free use of the fitness room on the site
  • Generous financial and time support for further training
  • Transverse shifts to other areas, the development into a team leader or a specialization - even across several companies

All amenities for employees can be found here . If you also want to be the best version of yourself, you will find the possibilities with us.

This convinces us

  • Nurse with Bachelor’s degree (BSc) and professional experience in acute care
  • Rapid comprehension and the ability to set clear priorities in dynamic environments
  • Commitment to a successful cooperation with the station management and the trainers in the station squad
  • Excellent communication skills and didactic skills

More information is available to Urs Wildbolz, Head of Bed Stations at 044 397 22 94

About the Zollikerberg Hospital We stand for a comprehensive range of services and strong cooperation partners. With a public service mandate and 173 beds, the Zollikerberg Hospital contributes to the optimal medical care of the greater Zurich area. Every year, more than 11,000 inpatients and 75,000 outpatients in all insurance classes can feel comfortable and in good hands. Our broadly based primary care includes internal medicine, gynecology and obstetrics with neonatology, general surgery, an interdisciplinary emergency center and orthopedics with specialized spinal surgery.
