Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Vocational School for Retail Trade and Pharmacy Zurich Accounting Secretary/in 60% The Vocational School for Retail Trade and Pharmacy Zurich (BSDPZ) is a cantonal vocational college with around 1,900 learners and students as well as around 100 teachers and lecturers in basic vocational training and tertiary education. We consistently develop our school – with the aim of strengthening, empowering and effectively preparing learners and students for a complex world of work and living. Due to retirement, we are looking for a personality as accounting secretary as of 1 February 2027 or by arrangement (60%) Their tasks

  • In this versatile function, you are responsible for the administrative support of the accounting system and support the school management with reliable financial foundations. In particular, its tasks include:
  • Processing of creditors including formal and mathematical checking as well as correct accounting in cost and performance accounting
  • Accounting of debtors including invoicing, dunning, cancellation and credits
  • Invoicing of extraordinary services based on declarations and contracts
  • Settlement of cantonal contributions and school fees
  • Implementation of cash and financial statements
  • Cooperation in budgeting, budget control and annual financial planning
  • Preparation of the monthly financial reporting for the rector with target-actual comparisons, deviation analyses and relevant financial indicators
  • Contact person for accounting issues vis-à-vis internal and cantonal authorities
  • Implementation and documentation of ICS processes as well as reporting to the competent authorities of the canton
  • Inventory management
  • Administrative responsibility for spatial permits including billing
  • Maintaining and monitoring the list of contracts as well as initiating timely contract reviews and renegotiations Your profile
  • Completed basic commercial education (Kauffrau/Kaufmann EFZ) or equivalent training
  • Further education in finance and accounting, for example as a clerk/in accounting edupool.ch or a specialist / expert in finance and accounting with eidg. Specialist card
  • professional experience in finance and accounting
  • Experience in a school administration or in the cantonal environment is advantageous
  • Strong numerical understanding and analytical skills
  • Precise, structured and timely working methods as well as high quality awareness
  • Self-employed and self-responsible working methods
  • Sound IT user knowledge, especially in SAP and Excel
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Forward-looking and networked thinking
  • Strong service orientation Our offer
  • A competent and collaborative team
  • An interesting and varied field of activity
  • Modern infrastructure
  • Central place of work, from new school year 2027/2028 the new place of work is for 3 years near station Enge
  • Remuneration according to cantonal guidelines Application Have we aroused your interest? We look forward to your application. Please submit your documents exclusively via the online tool. For information stands Ms. Fleischmann Christa, Head of Services, Tel. +41 44 368 27 05, email christa.fleischmann@edu.zh.ch, please contact us.
