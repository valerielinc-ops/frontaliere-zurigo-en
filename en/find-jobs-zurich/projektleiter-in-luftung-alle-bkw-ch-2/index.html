Full description

Join our inspired team at our innovative engineering company and become part of the future of living spaces. Benefit from our experience, skills and many employee benefits.

Your tasks

  • Support of the project team in exciting construction projects in the field of ventilation and air conditioning systems.
  • Independent implementation of planning services from concept development through planning to implementation.
  • Competent contact person for customers and the planning team in technical and economic matters.
  • As a link to the various trades in construction, you feel safe in dealing with and communicating with the project participants.

Your expertise

  • You have basic technical training and further education as a technician HF or engineer FH Building Technology (HLKK).
  • You can boast several years of professional experience in the design and realization of demanding ventilation and air conditioning systems.
  • You have experience working with 3D-based CAD systems.
  • Even in the dynamic building environment, you show that you are a resilient team player.

We do not want requests from personnel service providers.
