Full description

Join the team at the heart of vendor governance and risk management. In this role, you'll oversee third-party assessments, challenge risks, and help strengthen Vontobel’s control environment across the entire vendor lifecycle.

What would your role be? As a Third Party Risk Management Specialist, you will own the execution and ongoing development of Vontobel's Third Party Risk Management (TPRM) processes. You will work closely with Business Owners, Procurement, Legal, Compliance and Risk functions and be accountable for ensuring that third-party relationships are assessed, documented and monitored in line with internal requirements and regulatory expectations.

Your responsibilities include

  • Owning vendor onboarding, due diligence and periodic re-assessment activities from initiation through completion.
  • Conducting vendor risk assessments, documenting results and ensuring identified risks and actions are appropriately addressed.
  • Maintaining the vendor inventory and ensuring the completeness, accuracy and quality of third-party data.
  • Planning, coordinating and completing annual review and continuous monitoring activities.
  • Producing reports, presentations and management information on third-party risks, trends and required actions.
  • Managing TPRM-related governance, audit and regulatory reporting activities.
  • Driving the continuous improvement of TPRM processes, templates and tools.
  • Engaging Business Owners and control functions to ensure assessments, reviews and remediation actions are completed accurately and on time.
  • Managing audit, regulatory and control-related requests concerning third-party risk management.
  • Overseeing TPRM activities throughout the full vendor lifecycle, including onboarding, monitoring, re-assessment and offboarding.

What are we looking for?

  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent qualification in Business Administration, Finance, Law, Risk Management or a related field.
  • First professional experience in Procurement, Risk Management, Compliance, Vendor Management or a similar area.
  • Interest in third-party risk management and regulatory topics.
  • Structured and detail-oriented working style.
  • Strong organisational and coordination skills.
  • Ability to collaborate with stakeholders across different functions.
  • Good analytical skills and willingness to learn.
  • Excellent command of English
  • German is an advantage.
  • Affinity for Artificial Intelligence (AI) and a willingness to embrace AI-enabled tools and ways of working.

What do we offer?

  • Agile environment in how we think, the way we work and our trust for each other.

  • Campus feeling and a collaborative spirit.
