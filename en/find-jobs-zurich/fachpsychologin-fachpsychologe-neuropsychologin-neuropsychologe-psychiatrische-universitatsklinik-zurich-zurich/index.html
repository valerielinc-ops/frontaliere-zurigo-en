Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility.

For our ambulatory ADHD in child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy we are looking for 1. October 2026 or by arrangement one or more

Specialist psychologist / Specialist neuropsychologist / Neuropsychologist

60% - 80%

Their tasks

  • Working in an interdisciplinary team under psychological-medical co-direction
  • Carrying out, evaluating and interpreting psychodiagnostic testing procedures with a focus on psychological diagnosis in children and adolescents suspected of ADHD taking into account differential diagnostic and comorbid aspects
  • Implementation and/or planning of treatments according to current guidelines
  • Cooperation with other departments/special consultation hours of KJPP Zurich
  • Cooperation with involved external experts and institutions
  • Participation in the planning and implementation of further education and lectures as well as current and future research projects
  • Completed study of clinical psychology
  • As far as possible completed or advanced psychotherapeutic training
  • Comprehensive knowledge of psychodiagnostics and psychotherapy with children and adolescents
  • Knowledge of developmental psychology and psychopathology
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation with high initiative and resilience
  • Good work organization and experience in independent work
  • Experience/flexibility in dealing with different nationalities and cultural backgrounds
  • Together for people: Interdisciplinary cooperation for holistic psychiatric care
  • Strong in the team: appreciative cooperation and mutual support
  • Knowledge that advances: Extensive initial, further and further training offers
  • Working flexibly: working time models that fit your life
  • Future provision with added value: 60% employer contribution to the pension fund
  • Sustainable travel: Participation in the ZVV-BonusPass or general subscription
