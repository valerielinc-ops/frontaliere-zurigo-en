Full description

Are you looking for a very independent job in a lively, technically demanding working environment and in a well-established team? Then our maintenance and maintenance team for commercial refrigeration systems is just right for you!

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Zurich, operations headquarters Herdern, Pfingstweidstrasse 101, 8005 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Monitoring and operation of commercial refrigeration facilities in our supermarkets
  • Defect analysis, regulation and optimization at all refrigeration systems from ready-made refrigerators to central cooling.
  • Disposition of orders and support during order processing with our external refrigeration companies
  • Support in the area of food quality in connection with our data collection and evaluation M-Qool

Requirements

  • 4 - 5 years: as a refrigeration system technician / refrigeration installer
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EBA): as a refrigeration system technician / refrigeration installer or a similar field
  • You are a solution-oriented and artisanal personality
  • You have a Cat driver's license. B
  • Knowledge of natural refrigerants (CO2, propane)
  • You are cold resistant and physically resilient
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Healthy work: Health-promoting offers such as massages and health checks
  • Occupational pension provision: Excellent pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Social benefits: Fair social benefits
  • Cumulus Points: You get additional Cumulus Points when shopping
  • Migros Bank: Preferred interest on private and investment account, free account management, discounted mortgages and more
  • Employee offers: Up to 1,000 CHF per year (depending on your workload) for your recovery and further development – usable for fitness subscriptions, courses, health offers or a subsidy for public transport.
  • Sports and fitness offers: You can get a total free time bonus of max once a year. CHF 1000.– e.g. redeem for an annual subscription to the M-Fitnesscenter

Contatto

Application and contact Tony Jedamski Stv. Head of HR Marketing & Recruitment

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision to advance or withdraw from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Test work: A trial day or half day gives the opportunity to get to know the future working environment and the team members better. And conversely, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition: After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks

Grado di occupazione: 80-100 %
