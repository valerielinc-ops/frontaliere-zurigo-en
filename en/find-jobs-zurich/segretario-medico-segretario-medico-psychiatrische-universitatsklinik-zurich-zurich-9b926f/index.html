Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility.

For the medical administration of adult psychiatry and psychotherapy we are looking for

  1. November 2026 or by arrangement one or more

Medical Secretary / Medical Secretary

80%

Their tasks

  • Administrative management of assigned stations in the stationary area - Administrative care and responsibility of an outpatient area
  • Planning and coordination of outpatient appointments in cooperation with doctors
  • Regular protocols at the weekly meetings of the ambulatory area
  • Formal revision of withdrawal reports and other medical reports
  • Administrative and organizational support of involved professional groups, such as processing and sending file requirements, obtaining and clarifying the cost language
  • Control and maintenance of digital patient documentation, including scanning
  • Turntable function between doctors, psychologists and other internal and external bodies
  • deputy duties within the secretarial teams; in the ambulatory, as well as in the stationary area
  • Completed medical/commercial training or many years of experience in administration
  • Further training as medical/hospital secretary or several years of professional experience in this field
  • Knowledge of medical terminology and in-depth IT user knowledge (KISIM, ARTS, MS-Office)
  • Impeccable orthography and stylish German in word and writing
  • Reliable, motivated and resilient personality
  • Self-employed, self-responsible, service-oriented and structured working methods
  • Together for people: Interdisciplinary cooperation for holistic psychiatric care
  • Strong in the team: appreciative cooperation and mutual support
  • Shaping the future actively: Participation in change and development processes
  • Future provision with added value: 60% employer contribution to the pension fund
  • Sustainable travel: participation in the ZVV-BonusPass or general subscription
  • Well taken care of in everyday work: discounted food offers or lunch checks
