About the job Google's software engineers develop the next-generation technologies that change how billions of users connect, explore, and interact with information and one another. Our products need to handle information at massive scale, and extend well beyond web search. We're looking for engineers who bring fresh ideas from all areas, including information retrieval, distributed computing, large-scale system design, networking and data storage, security, artificial intelligence, natural language processing, UI design and mobile; the list goes on and is growing every day. As a software engineer, you will work on a specific project critical to Google’s needs with opportunities to switch teams and projects as you and our fast-paced business grow and evolve. We need our engineers to be versatile, display leadership qualities and be enthusiastic to take on new problems across the full-stack as we continue to push technology forward.

In this role, you will design, build, and launch end-to-end generative AI and agentic capabilities that help viewers research, compare, and buy products recommended by creators they trust. Working across the YouTube Shopping and Search stack, you will develop LLM prompts, modular skills, tool integrations (such as live product catalog and affiliate offer retrieval), and interactive shopping UI components (such as product comparison tables, shoppable video indicators, and seamless Search-to-Watch handoffs). You will partner closely with engineers, TLs, PMs, and UX designers across YouTube Shopping and core YouTube Search/Ask teams to take features from rapid prototyping and offline evaluation (Autoraters) to A/B experimentation and global production launch. At YouTube, we believe that everyone deserves to have a voice, and that the world is a better place when we listen, share, and build community through our stories. We work together to give everyone the power to share their story, explore what they love, and connect with one another in the process. Working at the intersection of cutting-edge technology and boundless creativity, we move at the speed of culture with a shared goal to show people the world. We explore new ideas, solve real problems, and have fun — and we do it all together.

Responsibilities

  • Build agentic workflows, modular LLM skills, prompts, and backend tool integrations for conversational responses in live, verified YouTube Shopping product metadata and creator perspectives.
  • Improve the retrieval, ranking, and personalization of shopping results—ensuring shoppable videos and product offers are tailored to user preferences across both Ask YouTube and traditional YouTube Search.
  • Build evaluation pipelines (LLM Autoraters, golden datasets) and run live A/B experiments to optimize answer quality, factuality, latency, and core shopping metrics (such as Shopping DAU and Good Offer Clicks).
  • Collaborate with partner teams across YouTube Search, Ask YouTube, and Shopping Infrastructure, and cross-functional peers in PM and UX, to shape technical designs and deliver outcomes.
  • Design and launch AI shopping experiences on YouTube search and Ask YouTube (together with TLs, PMs, UXDs), including product comparisons, shoppable video treatments, and seamless Search-to-Watch handoffs.
