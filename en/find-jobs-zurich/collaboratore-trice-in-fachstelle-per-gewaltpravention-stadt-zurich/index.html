Full description

To strengthen our team in the Schools department, we are looking for 1. January 2027 (or by arrangement) a dedicated person for a fixed-term employment for one year with a prospect of permanent employment.

Tasks

  • They support teachers in difficult class situations, carry out targeted class interventions to maintain school operations and accompany students and specialists in acute moments of crisis.
  • You act as a competent driving force for schools and offer sound advice and information in the field of conflict management.
  • They initiate, accompany and implement innovative projects for violence prevention and control operational processes within the school system

Profile

  • They have a university degree in psychology, pedagogy, social work or socio-cultural animation. Further training in psychosocial counselling is an essential advantage.
  • they bring with them in-depth experience in dealing with the target group
  • Practical experience in class intervention is desirable.
  • They have strong methodological, didactic and communication skills. In addition, you are experienced in project management and have secure MS-Office knowledge.
  • They enjoy developing new methods and using them in a targeted manner. They have the ability to develop innovative approaches and implement them in a targeted manner.

We offer

  • A challenging challenge in a dynamic, constantly evolving environment where you make a direct contribution to society.
  • We offer first-class social benefits and a central workplace in the city of Zurich (near Enge train station).

About us

The Centre for Violence Prevention of the School and Sports Department is the central point of contact for crisis situations in connection with incidents of violence at the schools of the city of Zurich. We work in the areas of intervention, prevention, cooperation and empowerment to professionally support school leaders, teachers and students in challenging situations.
