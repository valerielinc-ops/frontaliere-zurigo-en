Full description

At KSW, we offer places for the FMS internship in the health profile. The internship lasts 24 to 52 weeks, includes a specialist maturity thesis and is geared towards the desired degree programmes of the university of applied sciences.

Tasks

  • You stand out for your open, helpful and friendly nature as well as your reliability
  • You have fun working in a team and you have good knowledge of German
  • You will visit the FMS profile Health at one of the following cantonal schools: Zurich Nord, Frauenfeld, Schaffhausen or Romanshorn
  • Mind. 24-week internship in the acute hospital
  • The start of the internship is in September
  • Your place of residence is in the greater Winterthur area, so you can be daily (even on weekends) at 6.45 am in the KSW

Profile

  • Every 6-8 weeks a learning accompaniment by vocational training
  • Reference person/contact person in practice
  • FMA support by a specialist in practice
  • In the course of the internship and good performance: introduction to personal care, vital sign control and other activities
  • Internships in the fields of medicine / surgery / surgery center / children's clinic

Benefits: KSW offers employees conditions that enable them to reconcile their professional goals and personal needs. Flexible, flexible working hours, home office as well as a generous maternity leave and employment levels adapted to different situations – all this is possible at KSW, as long as the company allows it. Every year, the KSW offers 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses. We also support you temporally and/or financially for external training.

  • In extraordinary situations, e.g. B. prolonged illness, severe personal stress or difficult experiences in private life as well as in everyday work, various offers of conversation are available to you.
  • The appreciative and friendly cooperation within the teams and across the departments characterizes the working atmosphere at KSW. As a sign of a strong team spirit, the “you” was introduced as a standard at KSW. In addition, the KSW supports annual team events financially and organizes a large staffing event for the whole house every four years. KSW employees benefit from very good social benefits. Thus, the pension fund solution with BVK is far above the legal minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, KSW employees are very well covered.
  • With discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training opportunities and course offers, perks in pharmacies as well as at the most popular health insurance companies. You can benefit from a comprehensive offer for your health.
  • Our daycare center La Luna with spacious opening hours is located directly next to the KSW. In addition, you can use the income-dependent, financial support from the KSW for the crib costs.
  • In cooperation with cultural partners in and around Winterthur, we regularly offer discounts for concerts and theatre performances. The borrowing of media in the in-house library is also free of charge for employees.
