Full description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional specialist advice and innovative services.

Transgourmet Schweiz AG is part of the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss supply and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retailers).

Tasks

  • You create an environment in which our customers find their way around well and like to shop and communicate with experts at eye level
  • You ensure compliance with the internal and legal QS and ASIB guidelines
  • In addition to ensuring the freshness and presentation of goods, you are responsible for a correct labeling
  • You ensure the constant availability of our products and regularly carry out inventories
  • You take customer concerns professionally and forward them accordingly
  • At the check-out you scan the goods

Requirements

  • You have completed training in the food industry (chef, retailer, butcher)
  • You enjoy offering our customers a great shopping experience
  • You distinguish your highly reliable and efficient work method
  • You are a team capable personality who is enthusiastic about new challenges
  • You communicate perfectly in German
  • You have already completed the truck and/or drawbar training (SUVA standard)
