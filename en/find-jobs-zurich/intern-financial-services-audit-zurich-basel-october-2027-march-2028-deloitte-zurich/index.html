As the world changes, businesses must be ready to respond to the challenges and opportunities that lie ahead. Your active contribution is pivotal, as you engage in shaping the future of our offerings and nurture your own professional growth. How you can make an impact

  • Gain insight into how financial services organizations operate and the economic and industry challenges they are facing
  • Drive effective and reliable financial and non-financial reporting as a forward-looking auditor with in-depth business process and internal controls understanding
  • Develop expertise in financial services regulations (e.g., AMLO-FINMA) and support organizations in meeting evolving regulatory requirements. Conduct regulatory audits that identify compliance gaps, assess control effectiveness, and drive remediation strategies to ensure adherence to supervisory expectations and industry standards.
  • Develop people early on in your career and build a broad professional network

  • You come with a bachelor’s or master’s degree in Business Administration or Economics (ideally with a specialization in Accounting, Banking, Finance, or Controlling)
  • You have excellent analytical competencies and are interested in understanding the world of banking and finance
  • You enjoy solving complex challenges in a team and are interested in the financial market, as well as innovation, and transformation (including but not limited to data analytics)
  • You are fluent in German and English, French or Italian are a plus
  • An attitude that matches our core values. Be curious and courageous, respectful and connected Your team Become part of a diverse, multinational and highly motivated team at our Zurich office. Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
