Service/Cash staff:on call

2 Spice AG — Dietlikon, Dietlikon, Sizzera

Two Spice – that's us! A creative and innovative food and lifestyle company from Zurich. Since 1990 we have been bringing fresh ideas to the table and developing unique concepts and brands that inspire. Our versatile businesses are at home at the central hotspots of different cities and stand for quality, design and pure moments of enjoyment. Will come to Nooba, which is inspired by the pulsing streets of Asia and serves a diverse Pan Asian cuisine.

Our dishes are a fusion of traditional flavors of Thailand, Japan and India, coupled with urban flair. Dive into the world of Nooba and become part of a team that redefines the boundaries of Asian cuisine with passion and creativity. Do you want to become part of our team? Then now is your chance!

Tasks

  • Accommodating and competent support as well as advice of guests at the cash desk and at the service station
  • Delivery of food and beverages including collection
  • Active sale with additional sales and recommendation of our specials
  • Implementation of cash statements and daily invoices
  • Advice of guests on our offer and information about Allergenen
  • Compliance and implementation of all hygiene regulations (HA 152)

Requirements

  • Several years of experience in service or in the gastronomy
  • Friendly, well-kept and service-oriented appearance
  • Anessness for system catering and joy in structured processes
  • resilience and calm even with large numbers of guests
  • readiness to shift, weekend and holiday services
  • German knowledge at B1 level, other languages of advantage
