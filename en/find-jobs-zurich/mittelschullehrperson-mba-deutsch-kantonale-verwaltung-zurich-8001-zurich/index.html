Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School Stadelhofen MIDDLE SCHOOL TEACHERS mbA German 50 - 80% The Stadelhofen Cantonal School is a short-term high school with a profile in ancient and modern languages, music and mathematics and natural sciences. Our school is medium-sized, is centrally located in the middle of the city of Zurich and has a high proportion of women among the students. At the beginning of the fall semester 2027/28, a mbA teaching position for German 50 - 80% is available at our high school. The employment takes place in accordance with the middle and vocational school teacher regulations as a teacher with special tasks (mbA). Your profile - You have completed a university degree in teaching as well as a teaching diploma for secondary schools. - Employment is also possible if the teaching diploma is not yet available but will be acquired in the foreseeable future. - You are motivated to take on additional tasks in addition to lessons as part of school development and to actively participate in our diverse school community. Application We look forward to your application. Further information about our school can be found at www.kstadelhofen.ch. Prorector, Joanna Anders, will be happy to provide you with further information, Tel. 044 268 36 60. Please submit your application by September 7, 2026, exclusively via the following link. Workload 50-80%
