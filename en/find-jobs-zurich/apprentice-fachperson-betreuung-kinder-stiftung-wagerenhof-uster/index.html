Full description

For the beginning of the lesson on 1. August 2027 we are looking for you as Learning professional child care / Learning professional child care EFZ As a learning specialist, you accompany and support children with and without impairment in our crèche Beluga. For example, you support them in body care, eating or playing. You take into account the abilities and possibilities of the children. You structure the daily routine and accompany the coexistence in the group. Your relationships with the children are professional. The independence and self-determination of the children are in the center. What matters is you. Every single person is at the center with their individual quality of life. We do everything we can to ensure that our 250 residents, 246 daycareers and 52 employees at protected workplaces in the Wagerenhof can experience the highest possible quality of life. Wagerenhof is an attractive employer for employees of different backgrounds and professionalism and a competent training partner for learners of all levels.

  • Accompanying and caring for children with and without disabilities in various everyday situations, e.g. when putting on and taking off, washing, brushing teeth, eating and drinking
  • Leisure activities such as weekly forest and farm days
  • Accompanying together in the group
  • Building relationships with the children cared for professionally and lovingly
  • performing various household chores such as dishwashing or light cleaning
  • Joy in dealing with children and people with and without impairment
  • Team and communication skills, good manners
  • empathy and helpfulness
  • Strong, reliable and motivated personality
  • Careful and precise working method
  • Sense of cleanliness and order
  • Higher secondary school A or B

This makes us the place for your education

  • Appreciative, human togetherness
  • qualified and committed vocational trainers
  • Diverse and meaningful tasks
  • Good accessibility with public transport

We look forward to your complete application documents: CV, letter of motivation, school certificates, multicheck and trial evaluations.
