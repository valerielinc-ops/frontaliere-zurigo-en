Senior Test Manager - Platform Migrations (80-100%)

Zurich | Home office up to 40% possible | Reference 8370

Are you looking for an exciting challenge in a dynamic working environment? Do you want to take responsibility for the test activities in a challenging migration project and successfully promote it together with internal and external partners? Then you are exactly right with us! Our payment infrastructure team at the Swiss financial centre is looking forward to hearing from you.

Our team supports strategically relevant transformation programs in the area of Swiss payment infrastructure and ensures the quality of mission-critical platforms and processes. As part of our team, you will work closely with stakeholders from business, IT and management as well as with external partners to successfully and continuously develop the system landscape.

These are your tasks

  • You are responsible for the overarching test strategy for complex transformation and migration programs and ensure their implementation
  • Ensure the quality of end-to-end processes, interfaces and data migrations across application and supplier boundaries
  • You coordinate distributed test teams, create the essential prerequisites for a successful test implementation and ensure a coordinated test process across all parties involved
  • You control internal and external partners based on agreed results, quality criteria and milestones
  • You identify and manage risks, dependencies and escalations and create transparent decision-making bases for stakeholders at management level

You bring that with you

  • You have at least five years of experience in test management of complex IT and transformation programs. Ideally, you have accompanied platform or infrastructure migrations in the financial environment, especially in the areas of payments or card processing
  • You have in-depth knowledge in test and quality management as well as in end-to-end, integration, migration and acceptance tests
  • You have experience in the professional management of distributed teams as well as in stakeholder and supplier management
  • You convince with strong ownership and move safely in a multi-vendor environment
  • You have a university degree in computer science, business informatics or business administration or have a comparable qualification
  • You communicate verbally and in writing very well in German and English
