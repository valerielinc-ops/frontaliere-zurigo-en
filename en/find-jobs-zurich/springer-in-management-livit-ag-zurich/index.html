Full description

You are open-minded, ambitious and value-added in a future-oriented way of working on the pulse of the real estate industry? At Livit, you are committed to the concerns of owners with an exemplary customer orientation: inside and inside tenants, thus contributing to a trusting, long-lasting customer relationship and a positive company's image. Are you ready to tackle the challenges of today and tomorrow and to make a difference in the real estate world? Then you are exactly right at Livit. We look forward to your personal contribution.

Tasks

  • You support various management teams in staffing and technical bottlenecks in a department in Zurich
  • You are responsible for the acceptance and transfer of rental properties for first rentals/changes of rental
  • You are in direct contact with owners, tenants, property managers, craftsmen and authorities
  • You ensure the property maintenance according to the management contract
  • You conduct hartic real estate visits and write state assessments with corresponding reports
  • You make rental & rental interest changes and indexed rental interest adjustments
  • You monitor the current rental interest policy and advise homeowners on setting the rental interest rate

Requirements

  • You have an eidg. specialist pass as a real estate manager:in or have an adequate training in the real estate sector
  • You have several years of experience in the real estate industry, have special knowledge of rental law and construction skills
  • Your native language is German and you have very good correspondence skills
  • You are digital affin and have the ability to actively contribute to changes
  • You have an independent and reliable working method and bring flexibility with you
  • You are customer-oriented and have a partnership
  • You think and act entrepreneurially and have pleasant manners

We offer

  • Internal offer of our Livit Academy for specialist and management courses
  • Financial and/or temporary support for external specialist training and access to electronic learning platforms for online language courses
  • Individual career planning and development perspectives
  • Home / Mobile Office
  • Bonus depending on the company's success and personal performance
  • Minimum 5 weeks holidays and purchase of up to 10 additional days holidays (holiday flex)
  • Above average benefits and premiums for day-of-sickness and accident insurance are 100% taken over by Livit
  • 14 days paternity leave
