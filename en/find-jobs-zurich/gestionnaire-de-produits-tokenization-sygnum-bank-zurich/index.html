Full description

As a Senior Product Manager in the Tokenization team, you will play a key role in developing and scaling Sygnum’s tokenization platform and on-chain investment solutions. You will work closely with engineering, legal and business teams to deliver innovative on-chain applications for our global client base. Key Responsibilities:

  • Own and drive Sygnum’s tokenization platform and on-chain investments initiatives, ensuring successful delivery to Sygnum’s global clients
  • Collaborate with clients, partners, engineering teams, and internal stakeholders to gather requirements and translate business needs into product specifications, user stories, and workflows
  • Lead the development and launch of new features to scale Sygnum’s tokenization platform, working closely with engineering teams throughout the product lifecycle
  • Support the definition and evolution of the tokenization platform’s vision and strategy, contributing insights from client needs, market developments, and technical capabilities
  • Conduct analysis and identify opportunities to expand Sygnum’s on-chain solution and tokenization offerings We are seeking someone who is passionate about the future of blockchain, tokenization, and decentralized finance, and who thrives in a fast-moving, entrepreneurial environment. Requirements:
  • Several years of relevant experience, ideally in a top-tier consulting firm, or as a Product Manager in a dynamic technology or fintech environment
  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Finance, or a related field
  • Strong interest in digital assets, tokenization, blockchain, and DeFi
  • Ability to deep dive into technical topics while maintaining strategic perspective
  • Excellent analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills
  • Strong communication skills and ability to collaborate with cross-functional teams
  • Fluent in English Nice to Have:
  • Technical background with hands-on coding experience
  • Understanding of blockchain infrastructure
  • Experience building blockchain-based financial products Joining Sygnum means being part of a dynamic, global team that is building a trusted gateway between the traditional and digital asset economies. Working at Sygnum, you will experience our fast-paced, exciting work environment that embraces meritocracy and collaboration and open communication. Alongside our ambitious long-term mission, we also come together for reaching important milestones and annual crypto-industry anniversaries like Bitcoin Pizza Day, and regularly celebrate together at themed company events as part of our journey to shape Future Finance. Sygnum offers a comprehensive package of benefits for all team members. They include:
  • Attractive combination of market salaries and entrepreneurial incentive scheme
  • Flexible/Work at home policies
  • Professional development via Mentoring and Buddy programs
  • One-month fully paid sabbatical after five years of continuous employment If you are passionate about the potential of blockchain to shape Future Finance and your profile is a good fit for this position, please send us your CV today!
