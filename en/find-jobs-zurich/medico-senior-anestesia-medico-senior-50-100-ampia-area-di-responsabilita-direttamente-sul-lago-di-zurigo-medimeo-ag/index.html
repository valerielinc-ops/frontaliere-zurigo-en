Do you want to work broadly anesthesiologically, but not go down in an anonymous large company? Expected in this acute hospital directly on Lake Zurich It is a versatile anesthesia with surgery, shock room, birth room, intensive care service and intensive care unit – in a personal environment where you know each other, work together uncomplicatedly and appreciation is noticeable in everyday life. After termination of service, compensation begins right outside the door: a jogging round on the lake, a coffee in Zurich, shopping, culture, museums or events. Here you combine technically broad medicine with an everyday life that also offers a lot of quality of life privately. The hospital offers a wide range of medical services from primary care to rehabilitation and is manageable enough so that direct communication and uncomplicated cooperation can really be lived in everyday life. The Institute of Anesthesia and Intensive Care Medicine is closely involved in perioperative care and looks after patients from different surgical specialist areas. In addition to the operation, missions in the shock room, in the bear room, in the acoustic service, in the anesthesia consultation as well as the co-care of the intensive care unit are part of everyday life. The position is particularly suitable for doctors who want to work professionally and at the same time attach importance to a personal hospital environment, good compatibility and a high quality of life.
