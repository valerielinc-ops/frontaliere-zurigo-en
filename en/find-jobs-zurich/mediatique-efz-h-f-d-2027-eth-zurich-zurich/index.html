Media specialist: in EFZ (m/f/d) 2027

Zurich, teaching position

Do you want to do a sustainable apprenticeship? Are you curious, cosmopolitan, want to make a difference and grow personally at the same time? Want to be on the pulse of time? Then you are exactly right at ETH Zurich, because this is where the future arises.

Job description

The diverse teaching for makers: women who understand and implement the media.

As a media specialist: in EFZ, your focus is on designing multimedia content and implementing it creatively. You work with modern ICT tools and support projects in visual communication and internal media production. Among other things, you will learn:

  • Design various media products, for example posters, flyers, websites, animations or presentations
  • Take and edit photos
  • Create and edit videos, for example for social media or internal platforms
  • Participating in projects – from planning to presentation of results

The training lasts four years and is divided into several phases. In the first year of apprenticeship, you are in basic training and are trained by specialists in the areas of design, photography, video and web design. Then you change the locations internally and externally in the rotation principle and get to know the profession from different angles.

Profile

New technologies, media and forms of communication inspire you. You are sociable, communicative and actively involved in the team. With your quick comprehension, your ability to concentrate and your creativity, you are always able to learn new things and think “out of the box”.

For this vocational apprenticeship you need a compulsory school degree and good performance in the following school subjects:

  • Mathematics
  • German
  • English

We offer

  • Introductory weeks at the beginning of teaching with all new learners at ETH Zurich
  • Six weeks of holidays per year
  • Modern infrastructure and professional support throughout the apprenticeship period
  • Regular events and workshops for personal development and networking with other learners
  • Free, wide range of sports in the ASVZ

Your portfolio

To get a better impression of you, we look forward to a portfolio with the following content:

  • Show your creativity – such as photographs, illustrations, graphics, videos, animations or websites
  • Choose a project that is particularly important to you and briefly describe your approach
  • No QR codes – please use only active links (YouTube, Vimeo or your own website)
