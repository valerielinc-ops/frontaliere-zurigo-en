This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

For the outpatient and inpatient patients of the Clinic for Hand and Plastic Surgery, we are looking for a Physician Associate as of 1 October 2026 or by agreement. The position is part of a Physician Associate team, which has been successfully established for many years and is firmly integrated into the clinical processes and works closely with the medical service. Due to the continuous growth and further development of our clinic, we continue to expand this well-established team and are looking for a committed personality to reinforce it.

Tasks

  • As a member of our experienced Physician-Associate-Team, you will take over the medical care of our patients in hand and plastic surgery together with the medical staff.
  • In addition, in cooperation with the documentary doctors, you also support patients of oral surgery
  • You are part of the medical team and work closely with nurses and diagnostic and therapeutic services
  • Supported by clearly defined treatment pathways and medical supervision, you take over medical care in stable patient situations and support in complex and unpredictable treatment processes.
  • On behalf of and in agreement with the attending physicians, you will take on the following tasks: admission and discharge of patients, prescription of diagnostic and therapeutic measures, carrying out visits, documentation and coordination work, carrying out your own consultation hours, care of tumor patients

Profile

  • You have an education as a Dipl. Nurse HF/FH and bring at least two years of professional experience in the somatic acute care hospital
  • Ideally, you have completed a bachelor's or master's degree program, completed a further education in intensive care or have a corresponding CAS
  • You have strong medical expertise and are motivated to continuously expand it and use it responsibly in practice
  • A high level of technical, social and methodological competence distinguishes you
  • You appreciate the work in a well-established, collegial team and actively participate in its further development

Benefits: Work and private life should fit together. Therefore, the KSW offers you – where the company makes it possible – flexible and smooth working hours, home office, generous maternity leave, paternity leave and employment levels that can be adapted to different phases of life. Develop yourself professionally and personally: Every year, around 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses are open to you at KSW. We also support external training – temporally and/or financially.

  • Sometimes it needs support. In the event of personal stress, prolonged illness or difficult experiences in your professional or private life, various confidential discussions are available at KSW.
  • At KSW we work together and for each other. Appreciation, open and respectful communication and cooperation across professional groups and departments shape our everyday work. You should be able to contribute with your ideas and strengths and develop.

Your future is also ensured: You benefit from very good social benefits and a pension fund solution with BVK, which is significantly above the legal minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, you are very well covered as a KSW employee.

  • Your health is important to us. Benefit from discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training and courses as well as discounts in pharmacies and selected health insurance companies.
  • Family and work can be well connected with us. The daycare center La Luna with spacious opening hours is directly next to the KSW. In addition, the KSW supports you – depending on income – financially with the crib costs.

There is also something to discover outside of work: Thanks to our cooperation with cultural partners in Winterthur and the surrounding area, you regularly benefit from discounts for concerts and theatre performances.
