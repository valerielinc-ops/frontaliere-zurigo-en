Full description

For the communication office we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement for 12 months an ambitious university intern.

Tasks

  • They support the team of two in communicative measures and tasks.
  • They help to write and revise specialist presentations, interdisciplinary texts, speeches and media releases.
  • They write contributions for social media and manage the channels (Facebook, LinkedIn).
  • They obtain information on the processing of media and citizen inquiries and coordinate the response.
  • They support and accompany the production of means of communication.
  • They help with the organization of guided tours or events, e.g. media conferences or information and participation events in the quarter.
  • They create, maintain and update content on the intranet and on the city website.

Profile

  • Successfully completed bachelor/master degree in communication, journalism, humanities or architecture (max. 12 months since graduation)
  • Engaged, communicative and independently working personality with a flair for language and a connection to the city of Zurich
  • Initial experience in organizational communication is a plus
  • Rapid comprehension and ability to work independently into new topics
  • Flexibility, resilience and service orientation

We offer

You can expect an instructive internship in a dynamic organization, versatile activities in administrative communication and exciting insights into the city planning and politics of Zurich. In addition, you will become part of the community for university interns in the city of Zurich, benefiting from attractive employment conditions, good social benefits, targeted internal education and training, flexible forms of work, and a centrally located workplace.

About us

The Office for Urban Development develops the foundations for Zurich’s future in terms of construction, develops urban development strategies and projects for spatial development and coordinates plans for a liveable, urban city. We are creating the conditions for Zurich to change and grow - but remain the whole of Zurich. For this we are in constant exchange with the media and various stakeholders.
