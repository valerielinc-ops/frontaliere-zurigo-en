Who you are

When Köttbullar meets Tilsammans – then you are at IKEA.

You love our legendary food, even if you saw it every day? Then the job at IKEA Food could be interesting for you. People work better when they are well. At IKEA, we believe that challenges bring joy when you have fun at work and achieve goals together. We want you to feel comfortable with us and to be able to develop personally and professionally.

How do you see it? See if this suits you:

  • You already have experience in a customer-oriented environment with a high volume of goods, ideally in the hospitality industry or in sales.
  • You like direct contact with customers: inside, like to be among people and can offer an excellent service.
  • You are able to perform standing work for longer periods of time.
  • You enjoy working efficiently in a team, but you also take responsibility for your own work.
  • You want to keep evolving.
  • You feel comfortable in a highly dynamic environment and can also deal with challenging situations.
  • You speak fluent German and understand English. Knowledge of another national language is an advantage.
  • You are flexible and can work every Saturday in shifts of up to 8 hours.
  • You ideally have computer skills, especially in MS Office.

You are responsible for

These tasks await you

Welcome customers in an inviting and courteous way based on IKEA values.

  • Ensure that the food, drinks and products available are of the highest quality and freshness and are presented in an appealing manner.
  • Preparation and distribution of food in the IKEA bistro or restaurant.
  • Maintain your area of responsibility so that it is always organized and welcoming and the prices are written.
  • receiving payments and processing cash transactions at the cash desk.
  • View the food sector from the point of view of our customers:inners.
  • Optimize the existing processes with your own initiative.
  • Compliance with hygiene guidelines.
