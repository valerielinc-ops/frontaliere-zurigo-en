Original description

Are you a technology-driven problem-solver with a passion for automation and a sharp analytical mind? Do you thrive at the intersection of business logic and engineering excellence? If so, this could be the perfect opportunity for you to make a real impact on a globally deployed platform that's shaping the future of automated underwriting. About the Role As an Automated Solutions Rule Developer within the Underwriting Solutions Centre at Swiss Re, you will be at the heart of designing, building and continuously improving automated underwriting rules within Magnum— Swiss Re's industry-leading automated underwriting platform deployed across more than 60 clients worldwide. This is a role where your work directly influences the quality, scalability and innovation of underwriting automation solutions delivered to clients across the globe. This role spans the full automation lifecycle — from business rule analysis and solution design, through development, testing, deployment and production support — making it a dynamic and varied position with real ownership and impact.

Key Responsibilities

Rule Engineering

  • Analyse business and underwriting requirements to design optimal automated underwriting rules that meet client and market needs
  • Develop, configure and maintain Magnum rules using visual rule editors and supported scripting capabilities
  • Produce clear functional specifications and maintain high-quality technical documentation throughout the rule lifecycle
  • Ensure version control, traceability and governance are applied consistently across all rule development activities

Automation Development Apply software engineering principles to deliver scalable, maintainable and reusable rule solutions Leverage automation technologies, UiPath concepts and AI-enabled productivity tools to improve engineering efficiency and delivery quality Identify opportunities to simplify processes through reusable components and automation best practices Quality Engineering

  • Design and execute unit, integration, regression, scenario and user acceptance testing to validate underwriting outcomes
  • Validate underwriting outcomes against business requirements and agreed acceptance criteria
  • Perform defect analysis and support release readiness and production validation activities

Subject Matter Expertise & Continuous Improvement

  • Act as the Subject Matter Expert (SME) for Magnum rule development, providing technical guidance and supporting global implementation teams
  • Analyze production outcomes and underwriting data to drive rule performance improvements and enhance platform quality
  • Support production incidents, perform root-cause analysis and implement sustainable corrective actions
  • Contribute to testing standards, governance frameworks and reusable assets to support continuous improvement initiatives

Stakeholder Collaboration Collaborate with regional implementation teams, market underwriters, architects, product owners and engineering teams to deliver high-quality solutions About the Team The Underwriting Solutions Centre is at the forefront of an exciting period of growth and innovation. With significant new client activity and the ongoing delivery of the next generation of Magnum — Swiss Re's market leading automated underwriting platform — the team is building the future of underwriting automation. We are a collaborative, globally connected group of business analysts and technical specialists, passionate about delivering an ever-improving solution for our clients' automated underwriting needs. If you're looking to join a team where your contributions have real, visible impact, this is the place to be.

About You

You are a curious, detail-oriented and collaborative professional who combines strong technical skills with a genuine passion for automation and continuous improvement. You bring a solutions-focused mindset, thrive in a global team environment and are energized by the challenge of turning complex business requirements into elegant, scalable automated solutions. You take ownership of your work, communicate with clarity and are always looking for smarter ways to deliver.

We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements

  • Hands-on experience in business rule development and testing, ideally within automated underwriting systems or similar rule-based platforms
  • Proficiency with UiPath or other RPA/automation platforms, with a solid understanding of automation engineering principles
  • Working knowledge of Java or JavaScript, with SQL knowledge considered a strong advantage
  • Solid understanding of SDLC and Agile delivery methodologies, with experience delivering in iterative, fast paced environments

These are additional nice to haves

  • Experience with Magnum or similar automated underwriting platforms
  • Practical awareness and hands-on use of AI and Generative AI tools to improve software development, testing, documentation and engineering productivity within enterprise governance frameworks
  • Proficiency with JIRA and Microsoft Office tools for project tracking and collaboration
  • Experience working across business analysis, rule development, testing and production support in a combined capacity
  • A track record of challenging existing processes constructively and driving continuous improvement through innovation
  • Comfort working independently and collaboratively across time zones and cultures in a global organization
