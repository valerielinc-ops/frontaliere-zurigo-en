Full description

For our Technology and Construction department with around 47 employees, we are looking for a committed personality as a space and usage manager for our Relocation and Space Management team. We offer you a responsible and varied activity in which you can use your experience and expertise in a targeted manner and further develop it. You can expect a modern workplace in a lively environment, a collegial and motivated team and contemporary employment conditions with regular working hours. As part of our interdisciplinary technology and construction team, you take responsibility and actively participate.

Tasks

  • Space requirements planning (short- / medium-term), in cooperation with corporate development, controlling and user representatives of the areas
  • Development and visualization of an optimal area occupancy, in compliance with the strategic specifications, with inclusion of the company development and user representatives: inside the areas
  • coordinating and communicating space allocations and repurposing
  • Control of implementation by the corresponding project managers and relocation management
  • Area analysis and controlling as well as maintenance of space and occupancy data in the CAFM/IWM system "Planon" ; Providing the basis for strategic land management
  • Formulation of requirements for the CAFM/IWM system "Planon", regarding work, floor space and real estate management
  • Project management in corresponding digitalization projects
  • Maintaining and updating architecture and CAFM plans, as well as managing electronic and physical plan data
  • Preparation of all relevant real estate data and reports, evaluations, overviews of various stakeholders

Profile

  • Commercial or basic technical training with business training level HF
  • Several years of professional experience in CREM or working environment and space management of a company with a comparable, very heterogeneous real estate portfolio
  • You work safely in interdisciplinary teams and have appropriate communication, coordination and negotiation skills as well as assertiveness towards different user groups
  • High IT affinity and experience in dealing with databases and/or CAFM/IWM systems, as well as basic user knowledge CAD, is an advantage
  • You communicate safely in German in word and writing
  • Professional experience in hospital services

Benefits: Work and private life should fit together. Therefore, the KSW offers you – where the company makes it possible – flexible and flexible working hours, home office, generous maternity leave, paternity leave and employment levels that can be adapted to different phases of life. Develop yourself further professionally and personally: Every year, around 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses are open to you at KSW. We also support external training – temporally and/or financially.

You should be able to contribute with your ideas and strengths and develop. Your future is also ensured: You benefit from very good social benefits and a pension fund solution with BVK, which is significantly above the legal minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, you are very well covered as a KSW employee.
  • Your health is important to us. Benefit from discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training and courses as well as discounts in pharmacies and selected health insurance companies.
  • Family and work can be well connected with us. The daycare center La Luna with spacious opening hours is right next to the KSW. In addition, the KSW – depending on income – financially supports you with the crib costs.
