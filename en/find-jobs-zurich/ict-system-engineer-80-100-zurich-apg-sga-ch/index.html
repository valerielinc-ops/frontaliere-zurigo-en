ICT System Engineer Linux

  • 80-100%
  • Permanent employment

Your tasks

  • You ensure the reliable operation of Linux systems throughout the entire lifecycle, from installation and configuration to patches and upgrades to replacement
  • You will analyze, develop and implement challenging and important IT operational projects in the area of Linux, infrastructures and applications – sometimes as part of the team and sometimes independently
  • As a 2nd level support, you monitor the Linux servers, network components (virtual and physical) and applications you use, carry out error analyses, maintain and document the system landscape (in a meaningful framework).
  • You automate recurring operational and administration tasks, especially with Ansible and shell scripting
  • You operate and optimize Linux infrastructures including file systems, storage connections and system resources
  • As part of the company, you ensure IT security through hardening, patch management and vulnerability resolution
  • You monitor and control several key suppliers for Linux and infrastructure services and selected applications
  • In our SAFe (Scaled Agile Framework) processes and ceremonies you coordinate content with the other company colleagues and become an important part of our Agile Release Train

You bring with you

  • Completed training as computer scientist/platform development (EFZ) or equivalent qualification
  • At least 2 years of experience with Windows and Linux servers, data networks and IT infrastructure
  • Experience in application management of various applications
  • Good knowledge of network technologies such as TCP/IP, VLAN and DHCP are a matter of course for you. Practice with DNS, VPN, ports and network drives
  • Ideally, experience in collaboration with agile development and basic knowledge of DevOps methodologies
  • First experiences in the use of AI and openness to use AI to make your everyday life much more efficient
  • Experience in Powershell and Azure-Cli
  • Experience with monitoring, logging, alerting and performance analysis of Linux systems
  • Knowledge of storage, file systems, LVM as well as backup and restore

This is what awaits you

  • Modern jobs and 1/2 of working hours in the home office
  • Flexible working hours and at least five weeks of holidays as well as the possibility of two additional weeks of holiday purchase
  • Very good social benefits, it is provided for you
  • Free tickets to various events
  • Top location in Zurich
