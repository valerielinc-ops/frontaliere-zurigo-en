Full description

IN BREAKDOWN

The mix of software and technology is exactly your thing and you love to be on the road independently throughout Switzerland? As a service technician (from now on or by agreement, 80-100%) in Zurich Wiedikon you bring a breath of fresh air to the offices of Switzerland and become part of our dedicated hardware team.

Your responsibilities

  • Help your team complete all hardware tasks during the week – without picket or weekend assignments.
  • Install our FELFEL refrigerators and Gavetti coffee machines directly at the customer’s site.
  • Coordinate your assignments closely with the Implementation Team to ensure smooth installations.
  • Perform regular maintenance and maintenance work on our systems throughout Switzerland.
  • Take over repair work at the customer and make your daily routine flexible.
  • Perform versatile assembly work in our workshop in Zurich.
  • Work flexibly in a team of two, in the workshop or independently "on the road".

The assembly and maintenance of FELFEL and Gavetti is an exciting mix of electronics, mechanics, carpentry, software and technology. Accordingly, the role is very varied and the team is interdisciplinary, with the opportunity to develop further in different areas.

What you bring

The hardware team is very interdisciplinary and there is a lot of internal training. The following backgrounds are advantageous:

  • Mechanic, carpenter or in a comparable technical profession.
  • Versatile talent at the interface of electronics, mechanics, carpentry and software.
  • High flexibility, independence and enjoyment of travelling within Switzerland.
  • Great passion both for practical, hands-on work at the workbench and for the precise writing of technical standards.
  • Fluent knowledge of German and very good English.
  • Mandatory: Valid Swiss driving licence (category B).
  • Non-smoker – we don’t want you to smell like cigarettes, neither at work nor at customer meetings. After all, FELFEL is all about good food.
  • Valid work permit for Switzerland or Swiss passport.

What we offer

  • Lots of delicious food, just a few steps from your desk - we fulfill all your culinary desires...
  • Best barista coffee and tea for the whole team thanks to our innovative Gavetti coffee machine
  • 5 weeks vacation per year, and the possibility to purchase an extra week
  • We think about your future and offer a super-compulsory pension fund solution
  • Freshly baked parents receive 16 or more from us. 3 weeks of fully paid parental leave. We also support the return to work by making a generous financial contribution to childcare until kindergarten age.
  • We finance your half-tax subscription and thus support our employees in the use of public transport
  • Great team events, such as our well-known FELFEL Summer Outings
  • Stay Moving and Healthy with Our Weekly Sports Courses
