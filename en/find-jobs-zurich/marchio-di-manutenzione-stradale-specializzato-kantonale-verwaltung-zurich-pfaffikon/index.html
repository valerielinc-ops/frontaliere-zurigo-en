Would you like to be part of our marking team? Proper road marking makes an important contribution to traffic management, thereby increasing traffic safety and traffic flow. The Maintenance District 10 in Pfäffikon, with its 23 employees, is responsible for the maintenance of around 130 km of state roads and 300 km of hiking trails in the Canton of Zurich. For our 5-member marking group, which carries out maintenance markings on more than 1300 km of state roads in the canton, we are looking for reinforcements by agreement. Your tasks

  • Operation of machines is easy for you
