Original description

The Eye Clinic of the University Hospital Zurich is one of the leading centers for ophthalmology in Switzerland. With top university medicine, 24/7 emergency care, innovative research and a strong commitment to teaching and further education, we offer a varied and demanding working environment. >The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.

Tasks

  • Good PC knowledge is an advantage
  • Very good knowledge of German
