Full description

For the medical / rescue service department, we are looking for a vocational trainer by agreement.

Tasks

  • Learning support / coaching of students and contact person for all questions
  • Ensuring a high quality standard of practical training
  • Active discussion of the technical content of the courses
  • Developing learning situations
  • Monitoring, documentation and assessment of the level of training
  • Exchange of experience with other vocational trainers
  • Assistance in the introduction of new employees

Profile

  • Professional experience as a dipl. HF paramedics as well as in-depth and up-to-date expertise
  • Very good knowledge of SRZ structures and processes
  • Excellent organizational skills
  • High empathy and good communication skills
  • Educational skills and possibly. Training experience
  • Joy of participating in exercises / student learning days

We offer

  • Challenging, responsible training in an exciting and dynamic environment
  • Good development and further training opportunities in a 24-hour operation
  • Modern equipment and service clothing
  • Attractive employment conditions of the city of Zurich with extensive non-wage benefits, a healthy pension fund and various benefits

About us

Protection & Rescue Zurich (SRZ), as a service department of the Department of Security, unites the professional and militia fire brigades, the medical department and the operational control center (ELZ) for the emergency calls 144 and 118. In addition, civil protection, fire police and a training center with an affiliated Higher School for Rescue Occupations (HFRB) belong to the largest civil rescue organization in Switzerland. Intervention and prevention units protect and save people, animals, property and the environment around the clock in the city of Zurich, at Zurich Airport and in the agglomeration.
