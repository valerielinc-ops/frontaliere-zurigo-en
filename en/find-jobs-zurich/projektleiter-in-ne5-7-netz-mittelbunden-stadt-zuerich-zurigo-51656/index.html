Full description

As a project manager, you help shape the energy supply from our grid in Graubünden and take responsibility for the project planning and implementation of our grid infrastructure.

Tasks

  • You project the medium- and low-voltage network on network levels 5-7 including transformer stations and street lighting and accompany your projects from planning to successful completion.
  • With effective project and quality controlling, you ensure compliance with costs, deadlines and legal and technical requirements.
  • You coordinate sub-projects, work closely with internal and external bodies and ensure a proactive exchange of information for smooth project processes.
  • Your tasks include competent processing of internal and external inquiries as well as the preparation of cost estimates and offers for customer orders.
  • You always keep the network documentation up-to-date and complete and perform calculations in the low-voltage network, especially for zeroing and network design.

Profile

  • With a basic electrotechnical training and a further education as a network specialist with a federal technical certificate or at the level of HF, you bring the necessary equipment.
  • Project management is in your blood: You control complex projects from A to Z and ideally bring experience in network planning and network construction.
  • You use your knowledge in the electrical energy supply in a targeted manner, communicate clearly and objectively and act safely towards internal and external stakeholders.
  • With your conceptual and solution-oriented approach, you promote projects independently, customer-oriented and in a team.
  • You are committed and committed to a safe, reliable and sustainable energy supply.

We offer

  • More energy for your life: With mobile work, flexible working models and part-time opportunities, you can actively shape your working time and divide it into the best fit for your life. For example, you can buy up to 2 weeks of extra holidays and work part-time at all levels. Job sharing/splitting and unpaid holidays are also possible with us.
  • Sustainable and cheaper travel: With the mobility fee of up to 600 francs a year, you travel cheaply and sustainably – whether you want to cycle or explore Switzerland by train.
  • An investment in your future: We not only attach importance to paying fair and transparent wages, but also ensure that you have more of your wages left over. We take over 60% of the savings contributions and additionally offer a super-compulsory interest on the capital accumulated.
  • An energy boost for your free time: We pay you 100 francs a month as a food boost. In addition, you benefit annually from further Fringe Benefits worth 150 francs and attractive mobile subscriptions.
  • Development and training: With our talent management, we promote your development in a targeted manner – through a wide range of internal and external training offers as well as exciting development opportunities.
  • Meaningful work: Sustainability and meaningful work are not just words for us: shape Zurich and the future for us. Together with us, release energy for a good morning.
