Full description

Life is too short for bad software – we are looking for smart minds! Become part of our 70-strong IT team full of energy, creativity and team spirit.Our team consists of innovative business analysts, crazy programmers, helpful supporters and many other work-loving employees. Are you ready to make a difference together? Then apply now!

Tasks

  • You will develop our business solutions for our new Warehouse Management System (WMS) and bring your understanding of logistics processes, material flow, data and structures to the table.
  • You transform operational logistics needs into specific IT requirements (user or tech stories), create feasibility studies and define application and test scenarios together with the team
  • Critically question existing processes, identify potential for optimization and actively shape the digitalization of our warehouse processes
  • Together with the development and tester team, you will implement suitable product increments and added value for the business within the sprints
  • Requirements documentation as well as the technical support of tests are also part of your task area
  • For workshops, rollouts and technical questions, you are an important interface between our logistics locations and IT.

Profile

  • You have completed training in logistics or computer science (EFZ, HF, FH, dipl.) Technician/in HF company processes or similar) and have already gained initial professional experience
  • Requirements engineering, business analysis or the structured recording and documentation of requirements are familiar to you from practice - tools such as Azure DevOps or Jira are no stranger to you
  • You have experience in warehouse logistics and have already worked in IT, digitization or process optimization projects
  • You are a team player with a sense of responsibility, initiative and convincing communication skills at all levels
  • You approach challenges analytically, solution-oriented and pragmatically – an independent, process-oriented way of working distinguishes you
  • You communicate safely in German (min. C1) and English (min. B2) in word and writing

Benefits

  • With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • With us you start with 5 weeks vacation and benefit from the 5th year of service even from 6 weeks!
  • We go with the times - enjoy with us the advantage of Homeoffice

For us as a company, the focus is on supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
