Senior Specialist - Operational Excellence Strategy & Transformation — On Running

Full description

In short You will play an important role in helping On evolve how products are developed and brought to market. In this role, you will contribute to strategic initiatives and cross-functional projects that support On's long-term business priorities. Working closely with teams across the product organization, you will help improve processes, capabilities and ways of working that enable greater speed, effectiveness and quality across Footwear, Apparel and Accessories.

The Senior Specialist role will report to the Head of Strategy & Transformation - Operational Excellence.

Your mission

  • Apply a strategic and analytical lens to problem-solving: Support the structuring of complex and often ambiguous business challenges. Break down problems into key questions, conduct analyses, synthesize findings and help identify practical recommendations that support On's strategic objectives.
  • Drive strategic initiatives and project work streams: Own and drive specific workstreams within cross-functional projects that support the evolution of the product organization’s processes, capabilities, technologies and ways of working. Help ensure projects are delivered with a high level of quality, rigor and stakeholder alignment.
  • Support cross-functional collaboration: Partner closely with stakeholders across innovation, product strategy, design, product development, sourcing, planning, commercial, and other functions. Help facilitate discussions, align perspectives and contribute to solutions that improve organizational effectiveness.
  • Contribute to key LRP priorities: Support the delivery of strategic initiatives that are critical to On's Long Range Plan (LRP), working across Footwear, Apparel and Accessories to help drive scalable and sustainable business impact.
  • Develop insights and recommendations: Translate data, analyses and stakeholder inputs into clear insights and recommendations. Create materials and narratives that support decision-making and help move initiatives forward.
  • Drive project governance and execution: Help establish transparency across projects through structured planning, milestone tracking, risk management and stakeholder communication, ensuring initiatives remain on track and aligned with business objectives.

Your story

  • 5+ years of experience in product strategy, operational excellence, business transformation, consulting or related fields.
  • Master's degree in Business Administration, Engineering, Innovation Management, or a related field.
  • Footwear and/or apparel industry experience and/or experience in a strategy or consulting environment is a must.
  • Experience contributing to cross-functional projects involving multiple stakeholders and business functions.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to structure complex topics and derive actionable insights.
  • Ability to balance strategic thinking with hands-on execution.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to build productive working relationships across teams.
  • Comfortable working across a variety of topics, including process improvement, operating models, product creation and organizational effectiveness.
  • Strong project management and organizational skills, with attention to detail and follow-through.
  • Passion for understanding and supporting On's vision and long-term business objectives.
  • Fluent in English, both spoken and written.

Meet the team The Operational Excellence team is part of the product organization: an entrepreneurial, dynamic and talented group focused on evolving the product organization (innovation, product strategy, design, development, sourcing) to new levels of effectiveness and performance.
