As a specialist care provider at the University Hospital in Zurich, we are committed to showing commitment with our professional diversity in the top medical environment and always keeping up with the times. We do not achieve success alone, but together! Each one counts and together we bring ourselves to the highest level of care.

Want to be part of our team? With us you can make a difference!

  • You accompany patients in complex anesthesia procedures and interventions in cardiac, vascular, thoracic, transplantation, visceral and robotic surgery.
  • Even in demanding and dynamic emergency situations, you act safely, proactively and professionally.
  • As a professional contact person for colleagues, you support the knowledge transfer in the team.
  • You are actively involved in the further development of standards, processes and evidence-based care.
  • When introducing new employees as well as professional learning and development topics, you will contribute.
  • By working closely with nurses, doctors and other professional groups, you strengthen interprofessional cooperation.
