Full description

The OR-X is a surgical research and training infrastructure that is unique and highly technologically equipped in Switzerland directly at the University Hospital Balgrist. The OR-X consists of a simulation operating room and a training laboratory, where ex-vivo operations, courses and research and development are carried out in a realistic environment.
In order to strengthen our administrative team, we are looking for temporary support until 31. December 2029. Tasks: • Reception and support of participants as well as coordination of appointments and course processes (including preparation and follow-up) • Assuming a wide range of administrative and organizational tasks as well as support for events and IT workplaces • Professional telephone and written correspondence as well as coordination of internal processes and interfaces • Responsibility for the creditor workflow (invoicing reporting and clearance) as well as order organization, dosimetry and catering • Support in personnel administration and time recording as well as in varied daily business • Working days: Tuesday, Thursday and Wednesday morning Requirements • Open, communicative and service-oriented personality with organizational talent • Team-capable, resilient and sovereign in a dynamic environment • Structured, independent working method and quick comprehension • Mastery of Microsoft Office programs (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) • Very good oral and written knowledge of German and English We offer: • A positive, exciting and innovative working environment • A versatile and responsible activity with development potential • A dynamic team in a company with an excellent reputation • 20% discount in Balgrist Apotheke AG • Mind. 26 holidays per year • Gifted free day for birthday • 50 % discount on the ZVV bonus pass or an annual contribution to a general subscription • Enjoy a healthy and balanced lunch at discounted prices at our company-owned staff restaurant • Free coffee/hot drinks • Miscellaneous shopping incentives Further advantages are just one click away: Attractive offers for our employees
