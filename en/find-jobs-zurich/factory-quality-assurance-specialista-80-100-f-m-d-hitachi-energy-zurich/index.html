About the position

For our location in Zurich Altstetten we are looking for a Factory Quality Assurance Specialist. You not only want to fix errors, but also understand their causes and permanently eliminate them? Then you are exactly right with us.

In this key role, you analyze quality deviations, perform root cause analyses and initiate sustainable improvements. Together with production, process engineering, design engineering and quality, you reduce scrap, rework and error costs and actively contribute to the reliability of our products and processes.

You work on exciting technical challenges instead of routine tasks, take responsibility for visible improvements and have a direct impact on quality and customer satisfaction. In an environment that promotes initiative, curiosity and continuous improvement, you develop solutions that remain.

You can do that.

  • Analysis of quality deviations, scrap and rework as well as identification of the actual causes
  • Applying structured problem-solving methods such as 5-Why, Ishikawa, FMEA and 8D
  • Development and implementation of sustainable corrective and preventive measures together with the specialist areas
  • Supporting production in complex quality and process issues
  • Perform root cause analyses in internal and external quality reports
  • Analysis of quality indicators and identification of trends and risks
  • Active participation in a culture of continuous improvement and error prevention

Your background

  • Completed technical studies (e.g. electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, production engineering or industrial engineering) or comparable technical training
  • Several years of professional experience in production, manufacturing engineering, engineering or quality management
  • Strong analytical skills and passion for root cause analysis and the sustainable resolution of quality issues
  • Knowledge of quality methods and tools such as 8D, FMEA, SPC, 5-Why and Ishikawa
  • Communication strength and successful collaboration across disciplines
  • Initiative, responsibility and a pronounced continuous improvement mindset
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing

The opportunity

For our Zurich Altstetten site, we are looking for a Factory Quality Assurance Specialist who is driven to go beyond fixing problems and passionate about understanding their true causes. If you enjoy uncovering root causes, solving complex challenges, and driving lasting improvements, this is your opportunity to make a real impact.

In this key role, you will lead root cause investigations, analyze quality deviations, and drive sustainable corrective actions. Working closely with Production, Process Engineering, Design Engineering, and Quality teams, you will help reduce scrap, rework, and quality costs while improving the reliability of our products and processes.

This is more than a quality role. It is an opportunity to tackle meaningful technical challenges, influence how things are done, and shape the future of our manufacturing excellence. Your ideas, initiative, and determination will directly contribute to customer satisfaction, operational performance, and continuous improvement across the factory.

How you'll make an impact

  • Analyze quality deviations, scrap, and rework, and identify the true root causes
  • Apply structured problem-solving methodologies such as 5 Why, Ishikawa, FMEA, and 8D
  • Develop and implement sustainable corrective and preventive actions in collaboration with cross-functional teams
  • Support production in resolving complex quality and process-related issues
  • Conduct root cause analyses for internal and external quality notifications
  • Analyze quality metrics and identify trends and risks
  • Actively contribute to a culture of continuous improvement and error prevention
  • Degree in a technical field (such as Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, or Industrial Engineering) or an equivalent technical qualification
  • Several years of professional experience in Production, Manufacturing Engineering, Engineering, or Quality Management
  • Strong analytical skills and a passion for root cause analysis and the sustainable resolution of quality issues
  • Knowledge of quality methods and tools such as 8D, FMEA, SPC, 5 Why, and Ishikawa
  • Strong communication skills and a proven ability to collaborate effectively across functions
  • Proactive mindset, sense of ownership, and a strong commitment to continuous improvement
  • Fluent German and English skills, both written and spoken
