Full description

The water protection laboratory carries out analyses on water bodies and in the vicinity of the wastewater treatment plant (ARA). On the basis of studies of wastewater and sewage sludge, material inputs, pollution and risks for the environment can be detected at an early stage and the effectiveness of measures can be assessed. The expertise in the team also supports the assessment of water pollution and enables sound reporting. In this versatile function, you are the central interface between sampling in the field, precise laboratory analysis and technical advice for plant operators in the Canton of Zurich. Their tasks

  • Monitoring of private and municipal sewage treatment plants in the Canton of Zurich, including routine on-site sampling and subsequent chemical-physical laboratory analyses
  • Plausibilization of measurement results by comparison with plant data as well as quality assurance reporting by means of LIMS
  • Conduct collaborative trials for ARA and actively participate in external collaborative trials to ensure the highest analytical standards
  • Technical advice and support to operators in dealing with operational disruptions and incidents
  • Professional sample preparation and treatment of sewage sludges
  • Treatment of water pollution through targeted laboratory analyses and the preparation of the corresponding test reports
  • You have a passion for environmental analysis and appreciate the change between laboratory work and outdoor operations.
  • Completed training as a chemical laboratory technician, environmental technician; ideally training as a sewage treatment specialist
  • In-depth knowledge in the physicochemical analysis of water and sewage sludges
  • Experience in dealing with LIMS (laboratory information and management systems) and MS Office applications
  • Ideally, knowledge of water regulations and limit values (especially in the Canton of Zurich)
  • Possession of a valid category B driving licence (mandatory for sampling tours)
  • Good written and oral German skills This is what awaits you
  • Exciting tasks with real environmental relevance - taking on A lot of personal responsibility
  • A dedicated team that sticks together and in which you quickly become part of the whole
