Full description

Get your EFZ through an education in administration. With us you start a varied and meaningful apprenticeship with a future, because as a merchant / merchant expects you from day one a supportive working environment. During your training, you will be accompanied and supported by our experienced vocational trainers.

For the house Tertianum Neuhegi in Winterthur we are looking for you at 1. August 2027 for the

Apprenticeship as a merchant / merchant EFZ

This is what you learn with us

  • Daily Guest Administration and Guest Counselling
  • Assuming the role of contact person for internal guests and external visitors
  • Operation of the telephone exchange and support from reception/reception
  • General correspondence and administrative work
  • Management and ordering of office supplies and printed matter
  • Processing of the guest address tool
  • Conduct of house inspections
  • Cooperation in invoicing and cash/monthly accounts

You bring that with you

  • Completed school education (Secondary School A or B) with good academic achievements
  • Good computer science skills (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
  • Mastery of the 10-finger system
  • Reliable, responsible and accurate work
  • Quick understanding and good manners
  • Empathy and patience as well as interest in dealing with our guests
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude
  • Enjoying multiple challenges
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

What benefits do we offer you?

You are just as important to us as your education! Therefore, we offer you the following advantages:

  • On your birthday you get free
  • CHF 500 to your compulsory school supplies
  • Free SBB half-ax subscription
  • Each lunch for CHF 5
  • Reduced mobile subscriptions at Sunrise
  • Perks and offers around fitness, leisure and your wellbeing
  • Up to 10% discount on your health insurance supplementary insurance
  • Regular team events and events for apprentices
  • Learning café and apprenticeship forum
  • Exciting rotation concept
  • Internal short-term training at the Wochenknowhow
  • And much more...

You start directly with an exciting introductory day and in half of your training we invite you to our summer camp for a whole week. Of course, your training degree must be properly celebrated, for this we organize an unforgettable graduation ceremony for all graduates. And maybe this is just the beginning of your career with us! We offer numerous opportunities to work within our company directly after graduation.

Further benefits and insights can be found on our website.
