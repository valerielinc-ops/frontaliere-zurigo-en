Full description

Are you looking to work in a young team and help us develop the Eye Clinic at KSW into a beacon of Swiss ophthalmology? We are known for our appreciative and responsible team culture and, as a central clinic, we help our patients achieve the best possible vision through interdisciplinary care. In doing so, we are reshaping patient care and further education in ophthalmology. As part of this exciting vacancy, we offer a responsible leadership position in an innovative clinic as well as the opportunity for continuous professional and personal development in a supportive and appreciative environment. Furthermore, participation in clinical studies and scientific projects in the field of vitreoretinal surgery is encouraged. As part of the leadership team, you can actively help shape and further develop our eye clinic.

Tasks

  • Independent and versatile work in vitreoretinal surgery for outpatient and inpatient care
  • Management and further development of the vitreoretinal service
  • Active participation in education and training with the aim of achieving the highest level of professional competence and quality of care
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with internal and external partners to optimize treatment quality

Profile

  • Completed specialist training as an FMH specialist in ophthalmology with a specialization in vitreoretinal surgery
  • Several years of experience in vitreoretinal surgery and high professional competence in this field
  • High social competence, team spirit, and leadership qualities
  • You are a committed, service-oriented, and reliable personality
  • You love working with people and communicate openly and transparently
  • You work with passion in a team and enjoy sharing your knowledge so that we can constantly improve as a team

We offer

  • KSW offers employees conditions that allow them to reconcile their professional goals with their personal needs. Flexible, sliding working hours, home office, as well as generous maternity leave and employment levels adapted to different life situations – all this is possible at KSW, provided the operation allows it.
  • Every year, KSW offers 40 interdisciplinary and 50 specialist training courses. We also support you with time and/or financial assistance for external training.
  • In extraordinary situations, such as prolonged illness, severe personal stress, or difficult experiences in private or professional life, various counseling services are available to you.
  • The appreciative and friendly cooperation within the teams and across departments characterizes the working climate at KSW. As a sign of a strong team spirit, the "Du" (informal address) has been introduced as the standard at KSW. In addition, KSW financially supports annual team events and organizes a large staff party for the entire house every four years.
  • KSW employees benefit from very good social benefits. The pension fund solution with BVK is well above the legal minimum. KSW employees are also very well protected in the event of illness or accident.
  • With discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training opportunities and course offers, discounts in pharmacies, and with the most popular health insurance companies, you can benefit from a comprehensive offer for your health.
  • Our daycare center La Luna with generous opening hours is located right next to KSW. In addition, you can use the income-dependent financial support from KSW for daycare costs.
  • In cooperation with cultural partners in and around Winterthur, we regularly offer discounts for concerts and theater performances. Borrowing media from the in-house library is also free for employees.
