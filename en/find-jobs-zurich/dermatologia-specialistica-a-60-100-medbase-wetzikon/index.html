Welcome to Medbase Wetzikon

Our lively and familiar medical center with its own ultrasound and x-rays is located in the idyllic Zurich Oberland and is well connected to public transport. 12 employees from the disciplines of general internal medicine and manual medicine care for patients of all ages in a committed and quality-oriented manner. After consultation, we also carry out home visits. At Medbase Wetzikon we value the flat hierarchies and honest, open communication. Our flexible working models and substitute solutions enable a good work-life balance.

You can move that

As a specialist, you offer comprehensive care and ensure high quality patient care. At Medbase you are part of an interdisciplinary team that supports you in your professional development and sets high standards.

  • Independent medical practice as a specialist in (m/f/d) dermatology
  • Basic care for our dermatological patients
  • Collaboration in a committed, interdisciplinary and professional team to provide the best possible patient care
  • Highly motivated and very well trained MPAs in the dermatological team
  • Regular training and participation in the quality circle

What you bring with you

  • You have completed a medical degree and have a specialist title in dermatology
  • You are familiar with the Swiss healthcare system
  • Several years of broad experience in dermatology
  • You independently conduct your specialist consultation in the field of dermatology and ideally in other specifications
  • It is important to you to provide your patients with comprehensive, interdisciplinary and quality-oriented care
  • You are a team player and work in a solution-oriented manner
  • Good knowledge of German (at least B2)

The Medbase Group is one of the leading companies in outpatient healthcare. This means services from health promotion to medication supply and acute treatment to rehabilitation.
