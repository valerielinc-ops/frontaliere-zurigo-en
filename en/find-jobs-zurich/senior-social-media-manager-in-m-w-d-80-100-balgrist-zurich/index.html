Full description

Support as responsible for supporting the digital Balgrist channels with a focus on social media They actively pursue the strategic development of digital branding measures and position the brand in social media communities. The focus of your activity is on the conception and implementation of platform-oriented, innovative content formats that are not only reaching, but also inspire our target groups through informative and inspiring content, create trust and strengthen our brand. With a 360° understanding of communication, strategic decision-making, a creative mindset and high design quality standards, you ensure that digital content convinces both content and visually and achieves measurable success. Become part of a team that makes medical excellence visible and tells stories that move.

Tasks

  • Strategic advice to the Head of Branding in the area of social media management to strengthen Balgrist’s brand presence
  • Planning, implementation and further development of the content strategy
  • Development and implementation of digital campaigns
  • Creation and control of high-quality multimedia content (text, image, moving image) incl. Editing and video production
  • Planning and controlling the publication of content on digital channels
  • Monitoring, analysis and strategic derivation of measures based on performance data and trends in the digital environment
  • Project management of various initiatives with internal and external network partners

Requirements

  • Completed studies in marketing, communication, media, journalism or comparable qualification/technical training
  • Strong understanding of brand management in the digital space as well as cross-channel communication strategies
  • Several years of professional experience in social media management: In-depth knowledge of common social media as well as relevant publishing and monitoring tools (including design software)
  • Strong sense for storytelling, brand-effective content and visual preparation (UI design expertise is beneficial)
  • Virtuosic written expression skills in German as well as very good knowledge of English
  • Willingness to take on regular weekend piquetting services
  • Initiative, structured and team-oriented personality with a high sense of responsibility
  • Enjoy communicating in teams with different specialists at eye level and finding the most effective solutions
