Original description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

As our new Rental Sales Support, you play a central role in supporting the rental business. You are responsible for managing rental orders, maintaining pricing data and ensuring a professional and customer-centric service. Support as an important interface between sales, operations, engineering and customers They coordinate rental facilities throughout their entire life cycle – from the preparation of offers and order processing to delivery, take-back and invoicing.

The position combines commercial support, administration and data management in a dynamic technical environment. In doing so, you will help maximize the utilization of rental assets and ensure an excellent customer experience.

You will become part of a team of nine colleagues in a growing business area and benefit from development opportunities that arise as the team expands. Your main tasks and responsibilities

  • Management of the administrative handling of rental orders from order entry to invoice preparation, including order processing, documentation, customer coordination and status tracking.
  • Maintain and update rental price structures and commercial data while ensuring accuracy, consistency and compliance with company policies.
  • Management of rental master data, including asset information, technical specifications, availability, rental prices and data quality across different systems.
  • Support of rental business and sales through reporting, KPI tracking, sales documentation, process improvements and the coordination of cross-divisional stakeholders.

What you should bring for this position

  • Professional experience in rental, sales, customer service or sales support, ideally in a technical, engineering, industrial or rental environment.
  • In-depth knowledge in dealing with ERP systems and enterprise software as well as experience in maintaining customer, order, price and master data.
  • Fluent German language skills in words and writing as well as the ability to communicate effectively with customers and internal stakeholders.
  • Strong administrative and organizational skills with high accuracy and a strong focus on detail.
  • Secure handling of Microsoft Office applications, especially Excel, for reporting, data analysis and documentation.
  • Ability to coordinate multiple priorities at the same time, meet deadlines and work effectively with the sales, operations, engineering and finance teams.

What we offer you

  • Close cooperation with experienced leaders in the commercial sector
  • Insights into international business topics, reporting and sales key figures and evaluations
  • Varied activities with a combination of administrative support, business support and process coordination
  • Active participation in increasing efficiency within the sales organization
