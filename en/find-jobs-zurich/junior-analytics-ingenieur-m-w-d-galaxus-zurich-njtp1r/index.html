Full description

As the top Swiss e-commerce platform, with a crew of over 4’000 across Switzerland, Germany, and Serbia, Galaxus is poised to move into new markets.The data team Kassandra is looking for a motivated Analytics Engineer to simplify decision-making at Galaxus through data, automation, and analytics expertise. Help us deliver the best customer ordering experience in a financially sound way!

Mansioni

  • As part of our team, you will be responsible for maintaining and further developing and shaping our various products (e.g., provider reporting and profitability)
  • Model and transform data using SQL to build business-aligned data models that form a reliable foundation for reporting, analytics, and data science
  • Build and maintain dashboards and automated reports that present data clearly and enable stakeholders to access insights independently
  • Conduct deep-dive analyses using SQL and Python to answer concrete business questions, create meaningful insights, and support data-driven decision-making
  • Work in a cross-functional Scrum team (data architect, analytics engineer, data scientist, product owner, team lead) to tackle complex initiatives

Requisiti

  • 1-3 years: Analytics Engineer
  • Relevant experience in data modeling and analytics, gained through professional experience and/or a degree in a relevant field, including hands-on analysis, building dashboards, and supporting business decisions
  • Comfortable working with SQL with exposure to ELT processes and a motivation to further develop technical skills. Experience with Python or R would be advantageous
  • Experience with data transformation tools such as dataform or dbt is a plus
  • Experience with data visualization tools (e.g. Tableau, Power BI) with the ability to derive actionable insights
  • Fluent in English, with good working proficiency in German or a strong willingness to learn
  • You have strong problem-solving skills, a good technical understanding and an above-average ability to understand complex relationships, and can work independently or as part of a team
  • Identifying opportunities for improvement and implementing changes to enhance productivity and effectiveness
  • Maintaining resilience and agility while adjusting strategies as needed
  • Completed degree (university / university of applied science / higher technical college)
  • English (Advantageous)

  • Courageous thinking: We encourage everyone to think on their feet, and love it when they come up with bold or edgy ideas.
  • Uncomplicated approach: Simplicity is at our core. We avoid the unnecessary and focus on what really matters – from overcoming challenges to celebrating our shared successes.
  • Flexibility and freedom (Office Jobs): We offer working-from-home options, up to 8 weeks of workation, and every position is possible with an 80% workload.
  • Rapid growth: With us, growth is a continuous journey. We also support you with EDU Points (CHF 2,000 for employees in Switzerland, EUR 1,600 for employees in Germany, and EUR 700 for our Serbian employees) for your professional development, so there’s no risk of you getting stuck in a rut.
