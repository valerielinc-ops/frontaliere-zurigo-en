Specialist: in (m/f/d) Data & Information Management Real Estate

80%-100%, Zurich, permanent

The real estate management of ETH Zurich is responsible for the real estate portfolio of the university. For the annual investments of approximately CHF 200 million, we are guided by the highest standards of real estate management and provide ETH Zurich with forward-looking space and modern working environments in good time for teaching, research and knowledge and technology transfer.

We set ourselves up for the future and align the organization with our future challenges. We rely on partnership-based cooperation with the beneficiaries and other relevant stakeholders. This is reflected in our vision and mission “Partnership for Space – Creating space for development together”.

Immediately or by agreement we are looking for a:n specialist:in (m/f/d) data & information management real estate.

Job description

Data is the basis for informed decisions, efficient processes and sustainable real estate management. In this exciting specialist role, as part of our data management team, you take on a central function at the interface of construction projects, real estate operations, facility management and digitalization. You ensure that information is available where it is needed and make a significant contribution to the further development of our digital ways of working.

Together with internal and external stakeholders, you ensure the quality, consistency and availability of real estate, project and inventory data. You are responsible for the examination, acquisition and archiving of construction and existing documentation as well as master data. You develop data and information structures further, ensure compliance with standards and optimize data stocks in document management, CAFM and archive systems.

A special focus is on the implementation and further development of BIM-to-FM processes. You check and ensure the quality of BIM, CAD and IFC data, accompany project transfers into operation and create the basis for consistent digital real estate management. In addition, you participate in digitalization, integration and transformation projects, support data migrations and system integrations, and drive the automation of processes.

As a competent contact person, you advise project managers and specialist areas on questions of data, documentation and information management. You promote cross-divisional cooperation, actively accompany changes and support the organization on its way to the digital future. In doing so, you live the ETH values of a trusting cooperation, take on responsibility, think in a networked way and create sustainable added value for the organization.

Profile

Several years of experience in data, document or information management, ideally in the real estate, construction, infrastructure or facility management environment

  • Training in property management, facility management, construction, geomatics, computer science or information management
  • Experience with BIM methods, digital building data and digitization, integration or migration projects (e.g. introduction of a common data environment (CDE))
  • Very good knowledge of document management, CAFM and archive systems (e.g. Planon)
  • Good knowledge of databases, data interfaces, data migrations, data analysis and reporting
  • In-depth knowledge of BIM, CAD, IFC, Data Governance and Data Quality Management
  • Experience in process management and process optimization
  • High affinity for digitalization, data management and modern information systems
  • Analytical, structured and quality-oriented working method
  • Strong communication and consulting skills as well as enjoyment of working with different stakeholders
  • High level of initiative, sense of responsibility, implementation and enforcement strength
  • Ability to actively shape change, attract people to new ways of working and constructively accompany change processes
  • Team-oriented personality with networked thinking and high service orientation
  • Very good German and English skills
