Apply online now

Do you want to immerse yourself in the exciting world of banking? During our three-year apprenticeship, you will benefit from a solid and eventful basic education and discover the diversity of a modern bank.

We offer you a future-oriented, exciting and varied vocational training – your ideal start in the world of banking. The Zürcher Kantonalbank is one of the largest training companies in the canton of Zurich and supports more than 420 apprentices every year. Would you like to be part of our Young Talents? We look forward to your application!

Learn more – Your tasks

  • Collaboration in various teams during assignments in the branches of your residential area as well as in the Hard business building
  • Get to know current and relevant banking topics – with a focus on basic, investment and financing business
  • Applying the learned knowledge in direct customer contact as well as in process management
  • Participation in training and internal events to further develop your personal, social and methodological skills
  • Visit the CYP (Challenge Your Potential) courses in order to acquire in-depth banking expertise
  • Completion of secondary school with a grade average of 4,625 (Second A) or 5.25 (Second B) in major subjects (Mathematics without geometry, German, English, French)*
  • Good Multicheck Economics and Administration – Evaluation Businesswoman or businessman
  • You are enthusiastic about the financial world and you like working with office programs
  • Learning new things and moving things give you joy
  • You like to communicate, are open-minded and have fun working with others
  • Required application documents: application/motivation letter, curriculum vitae, all four upper-level certificates, multi-check economics and administration, location determination of the secondary level (intermediate test, check S2 or similar)

*The grade averages given refer to the grades in level 1. For compartments in level 2, the grade must be correspondingly higher.

  • Versatile insights into the different areas of our successful bank
  • Engaged and motivated mentors accompany and support you comprehensively during your entire apprenticeship period
  • Experienced practical trainers introduce you specifically to the world of banking
  • Interesting and open teams looking forward to working with you
  • Our young talents are important to us and therefore we actively promote you
  • Plenty of fun and variety as well as good exchange opportunities with around 300 learners
  • Possibility of completing your apprenticeship with the professional diploma
  • Numerous exciting and varied events such as the learning camp, impact days and social events
  • Benefit from other benefits such as the ZVV Bonus Pass, support for competitive sports, language stays and much more
