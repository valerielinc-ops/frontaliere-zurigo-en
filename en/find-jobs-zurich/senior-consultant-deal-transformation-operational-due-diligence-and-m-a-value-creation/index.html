Main responsibilities

  • Your role and responsibilities
  • Drive the day-to-day delivery of defined analyses and workstreams within M&A value creation and pre-deal due diligence engagements, working under the guidance of senior team members;
  • Conduct operational due diligence, operational analyses and financial baselining, translating findings into high-quality client deliverables;
  • Support corporate and private equity clients in understanding the operational capabilities of potential acquisition targets and existing portfolio companies;
  • Assess potential merger synergies, the impact and complexities of carve-outs on seller and target organisations, and the standalone performance improvement potential of buyouts and corporate divestments;
  • Prepare for and participate in client interviews and workshops to identify operational complexities and value creation opportunities, including interactions with senior stakeholders across the organisation;
  • Rapidly synthesise qualitative and quantitative information from diverse sources into clear, evidence-based value creation hypotheses and a compelling narrative that clients can readily understand;
