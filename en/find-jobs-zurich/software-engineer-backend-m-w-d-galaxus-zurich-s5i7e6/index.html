Full description

Become part of one of our dedicated product teams and, as a software engineer, develop innovative software solutions that have a real impact – for our internal processes and for our more than 3 million customers in Switzerland and the EU. With your curiosity, your team spirit and your willingness to learn, you grow together with us and actively shape our platform.

Mansioni

  • As a software engineer in product development, you are responsible for the implementation and continuous development of functions that improve the shopping experience.
  • You develop new features together in a team, implement technical innovations and ensure that our software remains user-friendly, performant and scalable
  • You take care of the technical infrastructure of your area of responsibility, monitor the systems and ensure stable and reliable operation
  • You work closely with other engineers as well as colleagues from your team to realize customer-oriented solutions
  • You feel comfortable in an agile, experimental environment and like to bring your own ideas to constantly improve our platform

Requirements

  • At least 2 years of experience: In object-oriented software development
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): Computer science
  • You are already familiar with one or more technologies from our tech stack: .NET (C#), relational & non-relational databases (SQL, MongoDB) and cloud technologies (Azure)
  • First touch points with topics such as domain driven design, distributed systems or messaging are a plus, but not a must
  • Readiness to learn, curiosity and the ability to familiarize yourself with new topics and technologies
  • You recognize potential for improvement and can implement changes to increase productivity and effectiveness with support
  • The ability to adapt to continuous changes in the working environment
  • Secure communication in German or English
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • English (negotiable)
