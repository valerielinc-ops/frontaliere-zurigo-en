Full description

Are you ready to motivate and lead your team to deliver outstanding results and a unique shopping experience? 💪 Then you could be the branch management we are looking for! 🙋 What we offer you

You will get the chance to join a retail company that wants to be the first choice of candidates as an employer. As a JYSK Ambassador you get the following advantages:

  • Development opportunities through excellent training and mentoring
  • Internal competitions between branches with attractive prizes such as money or vouchers
  • Above-average sales bonus for the entire team
  • 20% discount at JYSK and other Lars Larsen Group companies
  • Top pension funds services
  • 5 weeks of vacation, which increases every 5 years of service
  • Further advantages such as an above-average accident insurance, various activities (anniversary, team events) and great anniversary gifts

Why It Will Go In Your Next Job

  • Together with the deputy branch management, you are responsible for your JYSK branch.
  • You will lead and coach your team in order to optimally prepare the store for sales and customers, according to our JYSK Leadership values
  • You analyze key figures, set goals and motivate your team accordingly
  • You implement store concepts and routines
  • You recruit, introduce new colleagues and develop the existing team

Meet one of our branch managers and learn more about the job here What you should bring with you

  • You bring experience in leading and developing a team, ideally in retail or similar industries
  • You are an inspiring and communicative personality that creates a positive working atmosphere
  • You feel in a dynamic working environment in constant change
  • You like physical work, have a hands-on mentality and lead by example
  • You are ready to take responsibility for your branch and its results
  • You have the ability to stay calm and purposeful even in stressful situations
  • You are always striving to improve your results and recover quickly after a setback
  • You are flexible and ready to work on Saturdays too
