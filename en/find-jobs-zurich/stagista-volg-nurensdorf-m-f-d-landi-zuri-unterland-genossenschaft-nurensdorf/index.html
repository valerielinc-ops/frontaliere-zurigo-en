The LANDI cooperatives support Swiss farmers in the economic development of their farms. Together with the brands Volg, TopShop and AGROLA, the LANDI cooperatives also provide the population in rural Switzerland with food, everyday goods and energy. LANDI Züri Unterland is successfully active in the Zurich Unterland region. For the Volg Laden Nürensdorf we are looking for an intern/intern immediately or by appointment.

Personnel department +41 44 217 70 30

These tasks inspire you

  • You are there for our customers and provide your advice for a great shopping experience
  • You present our products attractively and actively shape the store
  • You pack when filling the shelves and keep an overview of our range
  • You learn how to handle the checkout safely and make payments independently
  • You receive goods, control deliveries and support warehousing
  • You work in a motivated team and take on more responsibility step by step

We look forward to this profile

  • Joy of dealing with people and customer contact
  • Friendly, well-groomed and helpful
  • Communication strength and team spirit
  • Interest in retail trade and food
  • Organizational talent as well as a careful and reliable working method
  • Motivation to learn new things and take responsibility

Discover your benefits

  • Education and training: We invest in the development of our employees. A wide range of internal education is open to all, and we support external training.
  • Attractive holiday scheme: Our holiday scheme guarantees all employees at least 5 weeks of holiday. For learners and employees under 20 years of age, it is 6 weeks. In addition, the purchase of unpaid leave is possible.
  • Family time: Mothers receive 100% of their insured wages during 14 to 16 weeks of maternity leave. The other parent enjoys 3 weeks of family time at full wage.
  • Insurance benefits: We provide our employees with above-average social and pension benefits and offer special conditions on supplementary insurance with various health insurance funds for their entire family.
  • Discounts and discounts: Our employees benefit, among other things, from discounts for the purchase of furniture and new cars, special conditions on private insurance as well as discounted mobile subscriptions for them and their families.
  • Every five years we thank our employees for their loyalty and great commitment. Be it with a financial premium or with additional holidays.
