Full description

You work at the Limmatfeld Physio Centre in Dietikon, an external location of the Canton Hospital Baden. The modern ambulatorium is centrally located by the station and is easily accessible – both by public transport and by car. You can expect a committed, professionally broad team that supports each other and develops together. With us, you do not plan your everyday work alone, but in a team: working hours and holidays are coordinated together and offer you a lot of flexibility in everyday life. At the same time, you can continue to develop professionally and actively shape your priorities. The position is available immediately or by agreement.

Their tasks

  • You independently look after patients in various specialist areas.
  • You work with subscribers and patients in the training area.
  • You take over the supervision in the training room.
  • You work interdisciplinary with other specialist areas.
  • You are involved in the further development of the offer and set your own technical priorities.

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a physiotherapist (SRC recognition or in clarification).
  • You are interested in active physiotherapy, training and care.
  • You want to develop professionally and bring your own initiative.
  • You work independently and at the same time like to be part of a team.
  • You appreciate the interdisciplinary cooperation.
