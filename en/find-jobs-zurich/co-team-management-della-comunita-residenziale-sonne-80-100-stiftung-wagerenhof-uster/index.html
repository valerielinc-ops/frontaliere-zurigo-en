We have lived here for many years, many of us almost all our lives. Side by side, in a pavilion in the middle of the Wagerenhof campus, we ten residents rely on our employees to accompany us in everyday life. You and I are looking for you! As Team management of the residential community Sonne (80 – 100%) You support our team leadership and all employees. Our housing unit is divided, on the one hand we have the focus on dementia and on the other hand, the focus on "special care needs". But we all have one thing in common – we need a lot of patience! On the residential unit, you take over the personnel, organizational and financial management together with your team management. The management of employees is fun for you and you have already shown that you can do it well. You are used to working in a team. You work in the accompaniment and care of us residents. You have already gained experience in the care of people with a physical and cognitive impairment and have an education as a social educator / social educator or as a specialist care or a similar education in the social field. You also enjoy administrative activities and enjoy working together in an interdisciplinary manner. In the Wagerenhof we live together with around 250 people with multiple mental and physical impairments. He is the loving, lasting Dihei for us. Around 650 employees do everything we can to enjoy our lives here. Each individual is at the centre of his own needs. "What counts is you." - they say in the Wagerenhof. What matters is you. Every single person is at the center with their individual quality of life. We do everything we can to ensure that our 250 residents, 246 daycareers and 52 employees at protected workplaces in the Wagerenhof can experience the highest possible quality of life.
