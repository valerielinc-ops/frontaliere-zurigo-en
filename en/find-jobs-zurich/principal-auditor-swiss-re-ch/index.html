Original description

About the Role As a Principal Auditor of the Regulatory Audit China team in Group Internal Audit (GIA), you will operate within a dynamic, agile working environment — self-organizing with talented peers to deliver high-quality work, including not only the audit activities related to Swiss Re's business and operations in China, but also local regulatory deliverables, relationship management, risk assessment, initiatives and projects in wider GIA as appropriate. You will contribute to the independent assurance for the Board of Directors of Swiss Re Corporate Solutions China and other Legal Entities in China, with exposure to senior stakeholders. Together with the audit team, you ensure strong execution and delivery of audits, contribute strong business insights, and uphold internal standards and quality. For you, this role offers an opportunity to deal with themes that have organizational impact at the most senior level of the organization driving the risk culture of Swiss Re. You will be constantly challenged to learn new areas of Swiss Re's business and operations, to assess the effectiveness of the relevant processes, risk and controls and to devise workable solutions to address complex problems. Relevant work experience together with the ability to analyze, structure and assess difficult circumstances, to work collaboratively as well as speaking and writing in a concise manner, are key success capabilities required for this position. Your engagement combined with your talent to negotiate effective solutions will make a difference! Key Responsibilities

  • Deliver local regulatory audits end-to-end, ensuring high-quality, timely and impactful outcome
  • Identify control weaknesses and process gaps, and develop pragmatic, commercially sound recommendations to address them
  • Demonstrate strong knowledge of GIA Methodology and apply it consistently across audits
  • Support the local regulatory deliverables, ensuring compliance with Chinese insurance regulatory requirements and internal standards
  • Build and maintain trusted relationships with stakeholders, communicating complex findings with clarity and confidence, and at the same time maintain independence to make objective assessments
  • Provide input to risk assessments to identify and evaluate key risks across Swiss Re's China operations, in forming audit scope and priorities
  • Apply data-driven auditing techniques, including data analytics, to assess risks, scope audits and test the effectiveness of controls, and continuously explore effective AI use cases throughout the auditing life cycle
  • Contribute to wider GIA initiatives and projects, bringing fresh perspectives and innovative thinking to continuously improve audit practices
  • Collaborate across teams and functions, sharing knowledge and influencing stakeholders to foster a strong risk and control culture

About the Team Our vision as Group Internal Audit is to advance Swiss Re's resilience. Our purpose and mandate are to strengthen Swiss Re's ability to create, protect, and sustain value by providing objective assurance, advice, insight, and foresight. The Regulatory Audit China team plays a pivotal role in this mission — working closely with local leadership and regulators to ensure Swiss Re's China operations are well-governed, compliant, and continuously improving.

About You

You are an intellectually curious, results-driven professional who combines strong technical audit expertise with excellent interpersonal skills. You are comfortable navigating ambiguity, working collaboratively in agile environments, and communicating difficult messages with tact and professionalism. You bring a strategic mindset — always seeing the bigger picture — while remaining meticulous in your analysis and delivery.

We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements

  • 5+ years of experience in external and/or internal audit or other assurance functions in financial industry
  • Solid knowledge of (re)insurance business, including its governance frameworks, risk landscape, and control environments
  • Good understanding of local insurance regulations in China, with the ability to interpret and apply regulatory requirements in an audit context
  • Highly organized, natural self-starter, with the ability to take accountability and work under pressure and tight timelines
  • Result oriented, able to plan well, challenge oneself amid complexity and ambiguity, delivering results on time and with high quality
  • Collaborative and motivated team player who can listen actively, build consensus, and influence stakeholders at all levels
  • Exceptional communication skills — both written and spoken — in English and Chinese (Mandarin), particularly in a business context
  • Strong problem-solving ability with a commitment to quality, accuracy, and on-time delivery
  • Critical and innovative thinker who can generate creative solutions in complex, fast-moving situations
  • Excellent knowledge of Microsoft Office (Excel, PowerPoint, Word), plus data analytics and AI working experience, with the ability to leverage tools and techniques to enhance audit efficiency and insight
  • Professional certification, such as CPA, CA, or CIA, is preferred
  • University degree in a relevant field (e.g., Finance, Accounting, Business, Law)
  • IT audit experience is a plus, bringing an additional dimension to risk assessment and audit testing

About Swiss Re Corporate Solutions Swiss Re is one of the world’s leading providers of reinsurance, insurance and other forms of insurance-based risk transfer. We anticipate and manage risks, from natural catastrophes and climate change to cybercrime.

Swiss Re Corporate Solutions is the commercial insurance arm of the Swiss Re Group. We offer innovative insurance solutions to large and midsized multinational corporations from our approximately 50 locations worldwide. We help clients mitigate their risk exposure, whilst our industry-leading claims service provides them with additional peace of mind.
