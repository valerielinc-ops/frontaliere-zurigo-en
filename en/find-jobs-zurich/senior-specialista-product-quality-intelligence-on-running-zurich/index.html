Role overview

In short

As the Senior Product Quality Intelligence Specialist, you will be responsible for transforming post-market product data into actionable business intelligence that drives product improvements, reduces quality risks, and protects consumer trust.

Working across Warranty, Returns, Consumer Feedback, Reviews, Customer Support, and Product Quality data, you will build a unified view of product performance and convert complex data into clear recommendations for Product Creation, Sourcing, Manufacturing, Customer Experience, and Commercial teams.
