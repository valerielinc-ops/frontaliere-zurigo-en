Talent Pool - Operations Internship (m/f/d)

We are looking for dedicated interns who want to put their passion for food science and engineering into practice. At Givaudan, you have the opportunity to participate in projects that challenge your skills and foster your creativity. You will work in an environment where you help develop novel solutions that meet our customers’ needs.

During your internship, you will gain valuable insights into different departments within Givaudan’s Operations functions (Production, Supply Chain, Logistics & Shipping, Plant Maintenance, Engineering, Quality and Process Technology) at our sites in Zurich. We offer you the chance to bring in your ideas and work together with experienced professionals on solutions that make a difference.

Requirements

  • Students preferably in the 3rd year of a Bachelor's program or recently graduated in food science, food technology, chemical engineering, biotechnology or a comparable discipline at ETH.
  • Availability for a minimum internship duration of 6 months
  • Fluent English, additional languages are an advantage
  • Enthusiastic about food science and engineering
  • Team player, but also able to work independently
  • Professional in communication at different levels
