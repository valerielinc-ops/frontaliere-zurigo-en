Full description

Union Bancaire Privée (UBP) is a leading Private Bank specialising in Wealth Management (WM), serving a sophisticated international client base. Within the WM Client Lifecycle AI & Reporting Team, we are building practical AI solutions that improve how Wealth Management teams work across the client lifecycle - from onboarding and periodic review to KYC, transaction alert handling, to client intelligence. We are looking for an WM AI Data Scientist / Engineer who enjoys turning difficult business problems into working technology. This is not a traditional reporting role, and it is not a pure research position. We are looking for someone who can take an idea, challenge it, shape the solution and then build it. You will work across AI, software engineering, data and business processes, developing tools that combine Large Language Models, APIs, structured and unstructured data, backend services and user-facing applications. Additionally, the role offers opportunities to contribute to client onboarding, periodic review, KYC/KYT, transaction monitoring, document understanding and banker productivity. The right candidate will be comfortable moving from a conversation with a business stakeholder to a technical architecture, from a rough prototype to a usable application, and from experimentation to something that can operate reliably in a regulated banking environment. You will work closely with the Head of WM Client Lifecycle AI & Reporting in Zurich, with occasional travel to Geneva.

Main responsibilities

  • ■ Turn ambiguous business problems into clear technical solutions;

  • ■ Build backend services and APIs that connect AI applications with internal systems, data sources and business processes;
  • ■ Develop or contribute to user-facing web applications that make AI capabilities simple and intuitive to use;
  • ■ Work with structured and unstructured information, including documents, free text, client data and workflow data;
  • ■ Build data-processing pipelines that prepare and organise information for AI applications;
  • ■ Test solutions with real users, challenge assumptions and improve applications based on feedback;
  • ■ Build solutions that are traceable, maintainable and appropriate for a regulated environment;

  • ■ Explain technical choices, limitations and risks clearly to non-technical stakeholders

  • ■ Hands-on experience integrating LLMs or Generative AI into applications;
