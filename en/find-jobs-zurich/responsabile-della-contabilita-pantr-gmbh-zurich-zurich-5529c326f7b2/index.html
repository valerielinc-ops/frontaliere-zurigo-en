Full description

Projekt Interim is a market-leading specialist for intermediate uses in Germany, Austria and Switzerland. We transform vacant properties into lively, meaningfully used spaces. Our projects actively contribute to the circular economy of real estate and promote affordable living and working space. Do you have a hands-on mentality and desire to help shape creative and meaningful intermediate uses? Then you are exactly right with us – we look forward to your application

Your tasks

  • You manage the complete financial and secondary accounting (3 employees) of the project Interim Group with 9 companies
  • You lead the creditor and debtor management
  • You support the HR manager in payroll accounting
  • You are responsible for the preparation and submission of VAT declarations
  • You create the monthly and annual accounts

Your profile

  • You are flexible, uncomplicated and like to take part
  • You have several years of professional experience in accounting or as a trustee
  • You are familiar with ERP Abacus
  • You are sociable and maintain good manners with your fellow humans
  • You speak fluent German and are sure to negotiate in English (verbal & written)

This is what we offer

  • An exciting and varied range of tasks
  • A collaborative, young and motivated team with a very good working atmosphere
  • Organization with flat hierarchical structure and short decision paths
  • Individual support through 100% paid further education
  • A modern, unique workplace in Seefeld
  • 1 day of home office per week
  • Possibility to buy up to 10 holiday days per year
