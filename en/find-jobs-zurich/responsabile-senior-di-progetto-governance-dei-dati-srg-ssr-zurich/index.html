Full description

How does SRG ensure that its data governance projects are implemented on time – and that progress, data quality and compliance are transparent at all times? As part of the transformation, we are building company-wide data governance that does just that. In Data Governance, you are the methodical anchor for planning, controlling and tracking our governance initiatives and ensure that decisions are made on a solid database with sound business analysis.

This is your contribution.

  • You take over the project and program control of governance projects and actively participate in their implementation
  • They collect, document and prepare requirements for data governance initiatives and projects
  • Develop and maintain KPIs, dashboards and reporting to monitor data quality, policy compliance and governance progress
  • You support governance controls and participate in the implementation of audits
  • They coordinate data quality rules and their measurement across domains and ensure audit readiness
  • You prepare analyses and decision-making basis for the leaders: in Data Governance and the Data Governance Council

They inspire us.

  • University degree or equivalent education as well as in-depth experience in business analysis methods and project/programme management (classical and/or agile)
  • Analytical skills and a structured, solution-oriented approach
  • Secure handling of data, KPIs and reporting/BI tools
  • Strong communication and moderation skills in interdisciplinary and cross-functional settings
  • A basic understanding of data management, strategy and governance. Knowledge of the DAMA-DMBOK is an advantage, as is a CDMP certification
  • Very good language skills at level C1 in a national language as well as German and French at least at level B1, English at level B1

So we inspire you.

  • Meaningful operational key role in building enterprise-wide data governance with a direct impact on the transparency and governance capability of governance initiatives and SRG’s audit readiness
  • Close cooperation with editorial, business, technology and governance in an interdisciplinary environment
  • Active participation in data governance, data quality and data-driven working methods within the SRG
  • Space for personal responsibility, professional impact and continuous learning in the area of data & organization
  • Collaboration with an interdisciplinary team across all language regions in Switzerland
  • Hybrid working model with flexible forms of work and home office possibilities

Please submit your application documents (motivation letter and CV) via the “Apply now” button. The application period ends on 6 November 2026.
