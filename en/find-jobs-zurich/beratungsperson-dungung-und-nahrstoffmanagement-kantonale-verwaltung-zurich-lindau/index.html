Full description

To strengthen our soil and fertilization department, we are looking for a committed personality at the Lindau site. Their tasks

  • They advise farms on the planned use and measurement of farm manure (manure, manure, compost) as part of a farm nutrient management
  • They supervise the nitrate projects in the Canton of Zurich and act as an important interface between agriculture and groundwater protection
  • They plan, accompany and evaluate practical tests on fertilisation use and nutrient efficiency in cooperation with farms
  • They actively network theory and practice through the organisation of specialist events, punctual participation in teaching and the support of student work This is what they bring with them.
  • Training as Master Farmer, HF Agrotechnik or BSc/Master in Agronomy or a related field of study
  • sound knowledge and practical experience in agriculture
  • Experience in the field and in the environmental sector of advantage or motivation to develop specifically in these topics
  • Strong social, methodological and self-competence as well as enjoyment of exchange with farms
  • Enthusiasm for the education and training of farmers If suitable and interested, it is possible to teach in basic and/or higher vocational training.
