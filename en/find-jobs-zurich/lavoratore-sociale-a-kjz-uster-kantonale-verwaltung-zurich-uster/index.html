Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Vocational Advice Social Worker in kjz Uster, 60 - 100% Good for life - that is our motto. The Office for Youth and Vocational Counselling (AJB) is responsible for child and youth welfare as well as professional counselling. Our work focuses on children, young people and families. We work for them every day with a lot of heart and commitment. The kjz (Child and Youth Assistance Centre) is a polyvalent counselling centre for families, parents, adolescents, children and toddlers and works on the basis of the Children and Youth Assistance Act of the Canton of Zurich. For the department of social work and mandates in kjz Uster we are looking for 1. December 2026 or by arrangement a social worker (60-100%). They advise and accompany children, adolescents, mothers and fathers in the context of child protection measures and on a voluntary basis. In addition, you carry out clarifications in the case of child welfare on behalf of KESB. In fulfilling your tasks, you work closely with authorities, schools, other institutions and specialist bodies. They bring with them:

  • Completed training in social work at a technical college
  • Experience in youth and family assistance and statutory social work
  • a negotiable, goal-oriented and assertive personality
  • Empathy for people in special life situations
  • systemic thinking and enjoyment of administrative activities We offer you:
  • an interesting, demanding job in a varied and lively working environment
  • a professional, collegial and supportive team consisting of 12 social workers
  • Regular interception and supervision
  • A well-accompanied familiarization
  • a meaningful work for the Canton of Zurich including cantonal employment conditions and social benefits
  • a centrally located workplace with modern infrastructure Can we answer further questions? For further information, please contact Nico Zuberbühler, Department of Social Work and Mandates, at tel. 043 257 57 39 is available. We look forward to your complete application via online tool to Ms. Stefanie Porcelli, Human Resources, Hinwil, Meilen, Pfäffikon and Uster district office in Wetzikon. Only applications submitted via our online platform can be processed.
