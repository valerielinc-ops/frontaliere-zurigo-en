Full description

To strengthen our team in the field of toolmaking / assembly WZB, we are looking for an experienced and dedicated toolmaker with a focus on injection moulding tools.

Your tasks

  • Timely production and assembly of new tools
  • Carrying out modifications, repairs and servicing of injection moulding tools
  • Execution of maintenance and maintenance work on injection moulding tools
  • Coordination and processing of assigned orders (new tools, changes, repairs, service) according to priorities
  • Ensuring a complete and qualitatively correct order conclusion in cooperation with Equipment & Tool Design (ETD) and Molding
  • Analysis of defects and quality problems as well as preparation and proposal of appropriate measures
  • Technical cooperation in optimization and innovation projects

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training as a toolmaker or polymechanic, specialisation in mold making
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in toolmaking
  • Experience in the use of injection molding tools
  • Safe handling of technical documents and work orders
  • Very precise and structured working method
  • High reliability and independence
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

Our offer

As one of the world’s leading audio-acoustics providers with headquarters in Switzerland, we are committed to an inclusive corporate culture. We want to create an environment where you can balance a successful career with your commitments and interests outside of work.
