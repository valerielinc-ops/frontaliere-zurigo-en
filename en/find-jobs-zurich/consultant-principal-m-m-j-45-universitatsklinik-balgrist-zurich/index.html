Full description

"We offer space for innovation" Balgrist Tec AG is a leader in the development and production of individual orthopaedic aids. With our solutions, we improve the mobility and quality of life of our patients every day.

For the operational management and further development of our medical and retail areas, we are looking for an experienced, customer-oriented and implementation-oriented personality with pleasure in leadership, consulting and active customer contact. Tasks: • Responsibility for medical and incontinence care as well as its professional and economic development • Active participation in consulting and sales with regular customer and patient contact • Operational management and organizational coordination of three specialized sales outlets for medical, orthopaedic shoes and medical compression at the Zurich Lengg site • Personnel management, operational planning and further development of retail employees • Ensure efficient processes in sales, performance recording, ERP and invoicing • Responsibility for medical and incontinence purchasing, goods management and warehouse management • Monitoring of relevant indicators and implementation of improvement measures • Shared responsibility for the evaluation and development of digital sales and service offerings • Close cooperation with the specialist areas orthopaedic shoe technology, orthopaedic technology / bandagistics and rehabilitation technology • Co-design of new sales areas, shop concepts and service offerings Requirements • Health, medical, nursing, pharmacy/drug or retail training • Professional experience in the health retail, medical specialist trade or comparable health-related specialist trade • Good medical knowledge; Experience in incontinence care is beneficial • Experience in advising patients and in direct customer contact • Experience in employee management and operational retail management • Digital affinity and experience with online or e-commerce processes • Independent, structured, solution-oriented and entrepreneurial working method • High customer and service orientation as well as pleasure in working in day-to-day business • Very good knowledge of German; Other languages are beneficial Applications from professionals with experience in: • Medical homes • Homecare organizations • Incontinence, stoma or wound care • Specialised rehabilitation operations • Pharmacies/drugseeds • Trade in medical devices or aids We offer: • Multifaceted management function with operational proximity and scope for design • Modern working environment in the health cluster Zurich Lengg • Interdisciplinary collaboration in a specialized environment • Possibility to actively shape new retail and supply concepts • Attractive employment conditions and development opportunities • Appreciative and experienced team • 50 % contribution to the ZVV bonus pass or contribution to the GA • Gifted free day for birthday • Lunch at discounted prices in the staff restaurant • Purchasing and free coffee and hot drinks Further advantages are just one click away: Attractive offers for our employees
