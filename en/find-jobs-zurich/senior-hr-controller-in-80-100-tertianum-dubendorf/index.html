Full description

Senior HR Controller/-in (80-100%)

For you, numbers are more than reporting? They identify connections, create transparency and provide the basis for informed decisions.

In order to strengthen our team in our Corporate Service Center in Dübendorf, we are looking for an analytically strong personality who will further develop HR Controlling as a central management and sparring partner function between HR, finance and operations.

Their tasks

  • Responsibility for HR reporting as well as the analysis of FTE, headcount, personnel costs, social insurance and other relevant HR indicators.
  • Collaboration in monthly financial statements, forecasts, budgeting processes and management and board reporting.
  • Analysis and commentary of deviations as well as identification of risks and need for action.
  • Creation of meaningful basis for decision-making for management, HR and finance.
  • Further development of HR indicators, reporting standards and data models.
  • Ensure high data quality and a consistent database across HR, wage and financial systems.
  • Conduct productivity, personnel and resource analyses and derive recommendations for action.
  • Responsibility for transparency and control around job planning, resource planning and personnel costs.
  • Active sparring partner role for CFO, CHRO, FP&A, Business Controlling, regional management, management and operational units in HR and personnel cost topics.

Your profile

  • University degree in business administration, finance, controlling, HR management or comparable qualification.
  • Many years of professional experience in controlling, ideally in HR controlling.
  • Very good understanding of personnel cost structures, budgeting and forecasting processes.
  • Strong analytical skills and a high number affinity.
  • Experience with HR systems, ERP solutions and BI and reporting tools.
  • Secure handling of Excel and large amounts of data.
  • Ability to understand complex issues and to communicate them appropriately.
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method as well as a high level of initiative.
