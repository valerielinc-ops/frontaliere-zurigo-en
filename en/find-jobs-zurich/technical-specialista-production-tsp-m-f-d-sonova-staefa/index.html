Full description

In this role, you are a central part of our support team in electronics manufacturing as a Technical Production Specialist TSP (m/f/d).

You ensure equipment availability through preventive maintenance, minimize downtime through sustainable maintenance strategies, and resolve malfunctions, including root cause analysis. In doing so, you drive continuous improvements in Maintenance and continuously develop your technical expertise (e.g., in TPS).

As a technical interface between production, engineering, and development, you support the specialist departments, advise on technical issues, and contribute to optimizing our systems.

More about the role

  • Ensuring equipment availability through preventive maintenance of equipment, operating resources and testing equipment
  • Minimizing downtime through sustainable maintenance strategies and supporting machine operators with basic maintenance tasks
  • Analysis and resolution of disruptions in production, including root cause analysis
  • Continuous Improvement (Continuous Improvement) in the Maintenance area
  • Technical interface between Production, Engineering, Development, and other specialist departments (R&D, NPI, B&I)
  • Technical consulting and support for the production departments, as well as involvement in the optimization of equipment
  • Coordination, documentation and monitoring of maintenance activities (including LEXA) as well as compliance with quality and safety regulations
  • Management and procurement of spare parts, as well as support in the further development of automated systems and on-call service as needed

More about you

  • Completed technical vocational training (EFZ) in the electronic field (e.g. automation technician, polymechanic, electronics technician)
  • Experience in electronics production, ideally with knowledge of lean management, supply chain and SMD/flip-chip assembly
  • Good overview of the entire production chain and, ideally, experience in automation or semiconductor manufacturing
  • Strong technical understanding, quick comprehension and cross-departmental thinking
  • High quality awareness as well as structured and responsible way of working
  • Experience in conducting training courses as well as in adhering to processes and quality standards
  • Very good knowledge of German as well as good written and spoken English skills
  • Good PC user skills (MS Office 365); SAP knowledge is an advantage

More about our offer

As one of the world's leading hearing solutions companies headquartered in Switzerland, we attach great importance to an inclusive and appreciative corporate culture.

We offer you

  • Structured induction as well as targeted internal and external training
  • Individual development and growth opportunities
  • A modern production environment with short decision-making processes
  • The opportunity to take responsibility and actively shape processes
