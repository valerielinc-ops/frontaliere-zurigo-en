In dialysis at USZ, a versatile and highly specialized working environment awaits you. You care for patients with acute and chronic kidney diseases and accompany them with expertise and empathy through their dialysis treatment. Look forward to a varied job in a committed, interprofessional team as well as a variety of opportunities to develop professionally and personally.

Your main tasks

  • You carry out dialysis (HD and CAPD) professionally and independently
  • You move safely in a complex and rapidly changing work environment
  • You take on responsibility in your area of responsibility and work closely together in the interdisciplinary team

Your profile

  • You enjoy a dynamic, demanding field of expertise
  • Ideally, you will bring 1–2 years of professional experience in care – even without dialysis experience, you are welcome with us
  • You will be carefully and individually introduced to the field and get to know the latest treatment methods under professional guidance
  • You are motivated to develop professionally and personally and appreciate a flexible work plan and strong team spirit

Our offer

We offer you a structured familiarization, a modern university environment, flexible working hours, a committed team and a wide range of development opportunities. For this post, dossiers from recruiters cannot be considered. Thank you for your understanding. Our Benefits

Kita on site

In our in-house daycare centers, your children will be cared for lovingly. Long opening hours without company holidays and subsidized care places relieve you in everyday life.

Health

We support your health. In addition to personal consultations and courses of our health management that promote your well-being, we offer you access to over 120 sports with a subsidized annual subscription to the ASVZ.

Flexibility

We promote a balanced work-life balance: Almost half of our employees work part-time, and about 50% of the tasks can be done in the home office – if your working day allows it.

Family friendliness

We help you to reconcile family and work. Mothers are entitled to at least 6 months of maternity leave (of which 16 weeks are paid in full), fathers to at least 2 months of paternity leave (of which 2 weeks are paid in full).

Further education and development

We accompany you on your professional path and support your development with individual career and further education opportunities.

Insurance

We cover 60% of your pension fund contributions and offer attractive special conditions for supplementary insurance.

Mobility

You benefit from a personal ZVV BonusPass at a special price or receive a fixed amount of money for a general subscription (GA) or half-tax PLUS.

Telephony

You and your family members benefit from special conditions for private mobile subscriptions. Other advantages: Details
