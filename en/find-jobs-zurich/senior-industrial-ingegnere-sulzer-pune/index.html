Job Title: Senior Industrial Engineer

Location Sulzer Production Facility (Global/Regional Site)

Reporting To Global Head Operations Excellence Purpose of the Job The Senior Industrial Engineer is responsible for driving operational excellence, productivity improvements, factory optimization, and manufacturing system design across Sulzer operations. The role combines industrial engineering expertise, lean manufacturing principles, digital manufacturing tools, and factory design methodologies to improve safety, quality, delivery, cost, and sustainability performance.

The position supports both local manufacturing operations and global operations initiatives through value stream optimization, standard work development, capacity planning, line balancing, factory layout design, and deployment of the Sulzer Excellence System (SES). The engineer will lead data-driven improvement projects and support the development of future-state manufacturing systems utilizing digital twins, simulation, and advanced manufacturing technologies.

Main Tasks and Responsibilities

Industrial Engineering & Operational Excellence

  • Lead productivity improvement and cost reduction initiatives across manufacturing operations.
  • Conduct time studies, motion studies, and video-based manufacturing method analysis using tools such as AVIX.
  • Develop and maintain standard work instructions and work combination sheets.
  • Perform line balancing and workload optimization to improve flow and labor efficiency.
  • Calculate labour standards and manufacturing performance metrics.
  • Analyse bottlenecks and implement sustainable corrective actions.
  • Support capacity planning and resource optimization activities.
  • Conduct overall equipment effectiveness (OEE) analysis and improvement projects.
  • Utilize industrial engineering methodologies to improve throughput, quality, and operational performance.

Lean Manufacturing & Sulzer Excellence System

  • Drive implementation of Sulzer Excellence System (SES) principles.
  • Facilitate Value Stream Mapping (VSM) and future-state design workshops.
  • Lead Kaizen, continuous improvement, and waste elimination initiatives.
  • Implement pull systems, supermarket concepts, and material flow improvements.
  • Support visual management, workplace organization, and 5S deployment.
  • Develop and monitor KPI dashboards for operational performance.
  • Coach production teams on Lean methodologies and problem-solving techniques.

Factory Design & Digital Manufacturing

  • Create and maintain factory layouts and manufacturing line designs.
  • Design factory and production flows using Visual Components and AutoCAD.
  • Generate digital twins of manufacturing areas and production systems.
  • Model equipment, fixtures, tools, lifting devices, and visual management solutions.
  • Define material flow concepts and ergonomic workstation designs.
  • Support simulation and validation of manufacturing concepts through digital modelling.
  • Arrange production areas considering safety, infrastructure, and operational constraints.
