Full description

As a data analyst, you are part of our Analytics & Data Science team in the online shop area. Together with our product teams, you will analyze the behavior of millions of users and gain relevant insights, KPIs and decision-making principles. This way you contribute to the data-based further development of our shop.

Mansioni

  • Analysis of user behavior and derivation of hypotheses as well as concrete recommendations for product development
  • Development of relevant KPIs together with product owners, engineers and UX experts as well as provision of comprehensible and user-friendly reports
  • Conception and statistical evaluation of online experiments as well as derivation of concrete recommendations for action from the results
  • Translation of business information needs into tracking requirements and joint implementation with our developers
  • Collaboration on cross-cutting initiatives to further develop our analytics and BI landscape together with data engineers and data scientists

Requirements

  • 3–5 years: Data Analytics / Business Intelligence / Product Analytics
  • Completed studies (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): Data Science / Statistics / Computer Science / Business Informatics / Mathematics
  • University degree in a quantitative, technical or data-oriented field (ETH, university or university of applied sciences) or comparable practical experience
  • Experience analyzing complex data sets, ideally in an e-commerce environment
  • Very good knowledge of SQL and Python for extracting and processing data as well as experience in dealing with large amounts of data and database systems such as BigQuery
  • In-depth knowledge in the field of online experiments and statistics, from the conception and implementation to the evaluation of A/B tests, supplemented by other methods of causal analysis
  • Experience with modern visualization and reporting solutions as well as safe use of AI-based tools to make analytics more efficient and promote self-service access to insights
  • Strong communication skills and a proactive and responsible way of working with product teams and other interdisciplinary stakeholders
  • Safe knowledge of German and English for everyday work
  • Taking calculated risks to create value and drive growth
  • The willingness to question “as it has always been done” and propose more efficient, automated or effective solutions
  • German (flowing)
  • English (flowing)
