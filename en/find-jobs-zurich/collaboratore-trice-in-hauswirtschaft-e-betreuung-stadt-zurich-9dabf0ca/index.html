Full description

Spitex SAW supports older people with everyday domestic, care and social services. To strengthen our team, we are immediately looking for a committed, reliable and sensitive personality as an employee* in housekeeping and support.

Tasks

  • They perform domestic and supervising tasks in accordance with assistance and care planning as well as the standards and guidelines of Spitex SAW. If possible, the customers actively involve you in the activities.
  • They provide needs-based care and support services and thus contribute to a high quality of life for customers.
  • They observe changes in our customers, document them professionally and inform the responsible case management housekeeping and support about important observations.
  • They actively participate in meetings, reports and case discussions, independently collect important information and ensure its sharing.
  • They communicate reliably, professionally and appropriately – both in personal contact and via email.

Profile

  • Experience in private household management and independent, reliable and responsible working methods
  • High social competence and pronounced customer and service orientation
  • Teamwork, physical and psychological resilience, flexibility and openness to change
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German as well as secure and professional communication
  • Possession of a valid driving licence of category B and readiness to carry out operations independently in the entire area of operation by car

We offer

Freedom in the design of your work as well as a motivated nursing team with a fixed customer base. Our employees also benefit from attractive employment conditions in accordance with the personnel law of the City of Zurich as well as targeted training and further education. In addition to at least four weeks holidays, you are entitled to 6 company holidays.

About us

The Foundation for Retirement Apartments of the City of Zurich SAW operates around 40 settlements with more than 2000 affordable apartments for people aged 60 and over. It supports its tenants with everyday, social and nursing services and thus enables them a high quality of life into old age. More about the SAW at www.wohnenab60.ch.
