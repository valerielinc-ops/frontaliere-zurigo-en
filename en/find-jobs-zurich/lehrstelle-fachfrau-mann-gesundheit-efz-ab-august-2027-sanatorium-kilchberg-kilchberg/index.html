Nursing education

The Kilchberg Sanatorium offers you a fulfilling working environment with exciting development opportunities in a dynamic and appreciative team. A team that holds together, thinks and often laughs together: innovative, communicative and future-oriented, we develop the care of tomorrow together.

Your tasks

  • Ensure that our patients feel well looked after
  • Helping to diet and shape everyday life for patients
  • Get in touch with patients and have conversations
  • Medical tasks such as drug administration, blood sampling or vital sign measurement
  • Taking over various administrative and logistical tasks

Your profile

  • A good school report from Sek A or B
  • Approach people openly and interested and enjoy getting involved and supporting them
  • Like to work in a team, but also be independent and active
  • Be physically and psychologically resilient so that nothing throws you off track so quickly
  • Like to learn new things
  • Being able to express yourself well in conversations Your additional benefit: 37-hour week for nurses in shift work - also for apprentices In addition, we support you during your apprenticeship with up to 3,295 CHF - from multicheck to teaching materials, laptop and school costs up to allowances for public transport and food.

The Kilchberg Sanatorium is one of the most traditional psychiatric private clinics in Switzerland and looks back on almost 160 years of history. Only 15 minutes from the city centre of Zurich with beautiful views of Lake Zurich and the Alps. As a Listespital, we offer comprehensive psychiatric care for all insured persons and have inpatient and outpatient facilities in Kilchberg and Zurich. Our approximately 650 employees from over 25 professional groups are at the heart of the clinic and are committed to the best possible treatment of our patients. Become part of our team and shape the future of psychiatric care.
