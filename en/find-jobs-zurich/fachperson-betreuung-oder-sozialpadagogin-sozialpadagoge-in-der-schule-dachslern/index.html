Full description

For our day school we are looking for 1. November 2026 or by appointment a dedicated and motivated specialist care (FaBe) or social pedagogue / social pedagogue to support our kindergarten team.

Pensum: 40-60 %

40%: Two working days per week 60%: Three working days per week

The employment is temporary until summer 2027.

Tasks

  • Constructive cooperation in the care team as well as in the interdisciplinary cooperation with other specialists of the school team
  • Provision of recreational activities and offers during the absence period
  • Parent contacts and parent talks
  • Organizational, planning and administrative tasks

Profile

  • Degree with EFZ as a professional supervisor or
  • Completed training as a social pedagogue/social pedagogue FH/HF, childhood pedagogue/childhood pedagogue HF or equivalent qualification; for foreign diplomas, Swiss diploma recognition is required
  • Professional experience working with children at kindergarten level
  • Good team and communication skills
  • Own initiative, resilience and flexibility
  • Joy of dealing with people of different cultures

We offer

  • A dedicated and supportive team as well as close cooperation with the responsible teachers and specialists and a present school management
  • A varied, interesting activity in a versatile school environment
  • The opportunity to contribute your own ideas and strengths to the accompaniment and promotion of the children
  • A varied working day in a lively school in the city of Zurich
  • A centrally located workplace with easy access to public transport
  • Salary and social benefits according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich

About us

The Zurich-Letzi school district comprises Stadtkreis 9 with the districts Albisrieden, Altstetten and Grünau. In the 16 schools of our rapidly growing school circle, more than 5,000 primary school students are taught and supervised. More than 1,200 employees work for them in our schools in the areas of teaching, care, home service and technology as well as administration. They all work daily for the interests of the students and a smooth school day.
