Full description

Do you like working with people? You want to start from the 1. December 2026 or by arrangement with our care team for the care housing groups?

Tasks

  • They are responsible for the training processes of learners and students from recruitment to vocational training completion in close cooperation with the vocational trainers on the residential groups.
  • They help to continuously review and further develop the quality of vocational training.
  • They conduct internal training for interns, learners and students and participate in public relations work.
  • They actively support the continuous review of the quality of vocational training and work on the further development of company training plans and concepts.
  • Together with care managers, vocational trainers and the existing staff, they participate in securing learning-promoting training structures in the departments and living areas and coach the vocational trainers in their area.

Profile

  • Several years of experience in vocational training and in the development and implementation of training strategies
  • Completed training as a qualified nurse HF/FH with several years of professional experience and SRC recognition for diplomas from abroad
  • Vocational pedagogical training such as, for example, specialist card eidg. Trainer or equivalent knowledge
  • Well-founded, evidence-based nursing expertise and the motivation to further develop it in the context of nursing housing groups from a vocational perspective
  • In-depth knowledge in the field of Swiss education in health care

We offer

Enjoy an exciting field of work in a professional and team-oriented working environment with a du-culture throughout the company. The human being is at our center. That is why we support the reconciliation of private and professional life, different working models make it possible.

As part of the city of Zurich and due to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, progressive employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

Depending on the experience, the annual salary for this function is between CHF 102’263.– and CHF 136’644.– (100%).

About us

The health centers for the age of the city of Zurich are one of the largest geriatric institutions of Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

Our nine nursing housing groups (PWG) at currently six locations offer people with Dementia or a psychiatric disease a manageable and homely environment. The small units with eight to eleven places are in settlements of the respective quarters embedded and enable comprehensive care and care in as far as possible Everyday environment. Cooking, eating and shaping everyday life together Living in the foster housing groups - as close as possible to the familiar life at home and adapted to the individual needs.
