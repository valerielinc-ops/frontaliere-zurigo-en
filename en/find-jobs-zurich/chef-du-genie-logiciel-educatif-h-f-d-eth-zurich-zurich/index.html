Head of Educational Software Engineering (m/f/d)

100%, Zurich, perpetual

To support research and teaching, the IT services of ETH Zurich operate as a service organization in this large and complex IT environment. For our part, we offer the members of ETH Zurich high-quality, flexible and scalable computer science services and thus support them in competition with the best universities in the world.

Do you want to develop software that is used by our students every day? Does it appeal to you to further develop open source solutions with international visibility and to actively shape the digital future of teaching?

Do you want to use your technical skills in an environment that combines research, education and innovation? Are you looking for a task with social relevance, high personal responsibility and the opportunity to achieve sustainable impact with a committed team? Then you are in the right place with us as Group Leader Educational Software Engineering at ETH.

Project background

The Educational Software Engineering group develops and operates centralized digital solutions for teaching and study, including the Open-Source Safe Exam Browser Ecosystem (SEB), the EduApp, and Moodle extensions for digital exams and the Digital Campus. Within the framework of a succession regulation, we are looking for an experienced leader with a well-founded technical background who successfully connects people, processes and technologies.

Job description

  • Leadership and development of a team of eight employees and external specialists with regular support from student staff
  • Coordination of software projects and portfolio management
  • Responsibility for the further development of various teaching support applications (SEB, EduApp, Moodle plugins, etc.)
  • You are responsible for critical security components in the area of SEB and SEB servers and continuously develop them further
  • You show great initiative and personal responsibility in the exercise of your tasks, contribute your seniority and enjoy a lot of creative freedom with us

Profile

  • University of applied sciences or university degree in computer science or in another STEM subject area
  • Leadership experience in a software engineering environment
  • Practical experience in C and C++ programming (system, kernel driver programming) in the Windows area
  • Very good knowledge in the IT security environment, in particular application security and hardening of applications as well as code signature and certificate chains
  • Experience in reverse engineering with the associated tools
  • Practical experience in using AI-enabled development tools: working with agents, project-specific context and automated comprehensive review processes
  • Ability to generate documentation with AI support and keep it up-to-date. Experience in generating test code and evaluations in an automated manner and checking their meaningfulness
  • Knowledge of test automation
  • Good knowledge of web technologies JavaScript, Vue.js, Progressive Web Apps, HTML
  • Basic knowledge of learning management systems
  • Practical experience in open source projects
  • Practice in agile software development and an agile mindset
  • Rapid comprehension to cope with technically complex problems and develop solutions up to prototypes
  • Secure communication in German and English and is adept in dealing with stakeholders at all levels of the university, including senior management functions such as professor:innen, department managers and staff management
