Become part of our team!

The Raiffeisenbank region on the left of Lake Zurich is one of the leading banks in the region. For our modern advisory bank in Horgen, we are looking for an experienced and entrepreneurial personality in holistic private client consulting. In this role, you take care of an existing customer portfolio and take over an attractive customer base. At the same time, you use your network and consulting expertise to build sustainable customer relationships and further strengthen our bank’s market position in the region. If you value personal customer proximity, entrepreneurial leeway and a strong regional anchorage, we look forward to meeting you.

What do you expect?

  • You look after and develop your own customer portfolio with a focus on long-term and trusting customer relationships.
  • They advise our customers holistically in the areas of investing, provisioning, paying, saving and financing.
  • They recognize customer needs at an early stage and tap into potential through active and forward-looking consulting.
  • You maintain existing relationships and gain new customers thanks to your personality and your network.
  • They work closely with our specialists from the fields of investing, financing and pension planning.
  • They represent our bank in the region and actively contribute to the further development of our customer base.

What do you bring?

  • Several years of successful experience in holistic private customer consulting, ideally with a focus on investing.
  • Proven expertise and pleasure in accompanying customers in the long term.
  • Completed banking training and further banking training.
  • A strong regional anchorage or a close connection to the Horgen region.
  • SAQ certification or the willingness to complete it.
  • A sovereign appearance, negotiating skills and strong communication.

We are looking for an experienced personality with passion for advice. Someone who creates trust, develops customer relationships in the long term and contributes with their regional networking to continue writing our success story. Marcel Kormann Head of Private Client Services Why Raiffeisen?
