Working Student - Primary Market Origination 40-60%

Zurich | 40-60% | Reference 8385

The Primary Market Origination team at SIX Swiss Exchange is responsible for developing the IPO pipeline, engaging with companies considering a public listing, and promoting Switzerland as an attractive capital market destination. As part of our team, you will gain hands-on exposure to Equity Capital Markets (ECM), IPOs and the Swiss capital markets, while contributing to business development initiatives and supporting our engagement with companies, banks, investors and other market participants. This is a 12-month position, with the option of extension.

What You Will Do

  • Support the Primary Market Origination team in various business development and client-related activities
  • Prepare high-quality presentations and materials for client meetings and internal discussions
  • Conduct market analyses and research on IPOs, Equity Capital Markets, listed companies and relevant industry developments
  • Support the preparation and execution of events, including IPO Workshops and the SIX IPO Academy
  • Assist with the preparation of market intelligence, client materials and strategic analyses
  • Contribute to ongoing initiatives aimed at strengthening SIX's position as an attractive listing venue

What You Bring

  • Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's degree, preferably in Business Administration, Banking & Finance or a related field
  • Strong interest in Equity Capital Markets, IPOs and financial markets
  • Ideally, prior experience in banking / equity capital markets
  • Strong MS Office skills, particularly in Excel and PowerPoint. Experience working with Bloomberg, FactSet or Dealogic is a plus
  • Strong communication skills and a collaborative, team-oriented mindset & solid analytical, planning and organisational skills, with a high level of attention to detail
  • Proactive mindset with a with a strong sense of ownership and responsibility and the ability to quickly learn and adapt to new topics.
  • Native-level German and fluency in English are essential
