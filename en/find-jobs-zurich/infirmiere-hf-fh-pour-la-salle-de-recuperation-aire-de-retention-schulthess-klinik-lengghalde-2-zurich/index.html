Original description

Tasks

  • Competent and self-responsible care of patients before and after surgery in the IMC and recovery room area
  • Ensuring professional and patient-oriented admission and admission organization
  • Implementation and monitoring of differentiated pain therapy, ensuring neurological monitoring and carrying out specific therapeutic measures
  • Early detection of clinical deterioration and acute life-threatening situations and initiation of necessary measures
  • Assuming additional responsibilities in the day-to-day work of the station, for example as shift management, as well as cooperation in internal tasks and projects
  • Interprofessional cooperation with doctors and other participating professional groups to ensure high-quality and patient-centered care

Requirements

  • Dipl. Nurse HF/FH with completed training in supervision care or with the motivation and willingness to complete it
  • Expertise and enjoyment of demanding patient situations
  • Clear, appreciative and targeted communication
  • Willingness to take responsibility and continuously develop professionally
  • Team spirit, initiative and a solution-oriented working method
  • Readiness to work in 2-shift operation with start of work partly from 06:00 am. You benefit from a scheduled working time model without night services, with only about one Saturday service per month and usually free holidays

We offer

  • Little weekend use
  • Attractive social benefits and numerous benefits
  • Paid or compensable changing time (15 min./day / 6.5 Fridays/year)
  • 15 minutes paid break per day
  • 40 hour week / 12.5 additional scheduled Fridays per year
  • ZVV BonusPass / ZVV BonusPass Flex

The Schulthess Clinic in Zurich is one of the leading orthopaedic clinics in Europe with over 1,100 employees. Our central concern is to free people from their pain and restore their mobility. As a specialist clinic, we focus on surgical orthopedics, neurology, rheumatology and sports medicine. Schulthess Clinic is Swiss Olympic Medical Center.
