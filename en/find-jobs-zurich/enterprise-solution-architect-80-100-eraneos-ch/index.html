Full description

Eraneos is an international strategy, transformation and technology advisory group that helps companies thrive in a dynamic digital world. With first-class experts from business and technology, we empower our customers to set new standards for sustainable transformations – from strategy to implementation. Whether in the design of future-proof organizations, the development of new potential through data and AI or the protection of companies through holistic cybersecurity: We deliver results pragmatically and purposefully. Our team of around 1,200 dedicated employees is represented in offices in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, Denmark, Sweden, Austria, the UK, Singapore and the USA and combines global perspectives with strong local roots. Around 450 employees work at the Swiss locations in Zurich, Bern and Lausanne.

This is the job - your main tasks

In this role, you design architectures that are not only technologically sustainable, but also make a clear contribution to the transformation of our customers. You move between enterprise architecture, solution design and integration issues and bring structure to complex projects along the industrial value chain.

  • Development of strategic targets and architecture roadmaps along the entire manufacturing value chain
  • Evaluation of relevant technology trends such as Industry 4.0, digital twins or modern platform approaches with regard to their architectural relevance
  • Conception of end-to-end solution designs for central business solutions in areas such as CRM, ERP, PLM or MES
  • Translation of professional requirements into robust and feasible technical solutions in close cooperation with business, IT and management
  • Development of modern integration architectures with a focus on middleware, API management, event-driven architecture, shopfloor integration and real-time data exchange
  • Implementation of architectural assessments and support for make-or-buy decisions in the context of selection and procurement processes
  • Definition of architectural principles, standards and governance guidelines as well as co-creation of suitable operating models in the architectural context

This job is for you if

  • several years of experience in IT consulting or in architectural work within demanding industrial environments
  • complex relationships can be quickly captured, structured and translated into clear architectural targets
  • the ability exists to comprehend technically demanding topics in an understandable manner and to convey them at different levels in a manner appropriate to the addressees
  • a reliable understanding of central processes in manufacturing – for example in R&D, production, supply chain and sales & service – is available
  • in-depth architectural knowledge can be used in established business systems such as ERP and CRM/CX solutions, such as SAP, Microsoft Dynamics or Salesforce
  • Experience in setting up or in integrating PLM, CAD or MES solutions in a shop floor environment
  • modern integration concepts, API management and software architectures such as microservices and cloud-native approaches are securely mastered
  • Knowledge of Azure or AWS cloud ecosystems and a good understanding of modern infrastructure concepts
  • Architecture frameworks such as TOGAF and notations such as UML or ArchiMate are routinely applied

As an i-top you bring with you

  • Certifications in the architecture or cloud environment, such as TOGAF, CPSA/iSAQB or Azure/AWS Architect
  • Practical knowledge in Industry 4.0 concepts and relevant standards as well as their application in concrete transformation projects
  • Experience in the design of industrial data platforms or in the integration of AI use cases
  • Know-how in the field of security architecture, such as Zero Trust, Identity Management, IEC 62443 or ISO 27001
  • Routine in agile working methods such as Scrum or SAFe as well as the ability to use them effectively in the customer environment
