Main responsibilities

  • Own, prioritize, and drive the tracking of complex, financially motivated threat actors (e.g., ransomware, extortion) and associated research projects.
  • Lead complex technical analyses, modeling threat activity, tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs), and indicators of compromise (IOCs) across internal platforms.
  • Create and deploy detection signatures to maintain visibility over threat actors and assist in closing security gaps.
  • Produce polished, high-quality technical intelligence documentation and actor profiles to deliver actionable insights to internal and external stakeholders.
  • Influence technical direction within your scope, mentor junior developers, and collaborate with cross-functional Google teams to support threat disruption efforts.

Key requirements

  • 5 years of experience in threat intelligence, intrusion analysis, incident response, or a similar security role.
  • Experience with threat intelligence platforms and tools (e.g., VirusTotal, SIEMs).
  • Experience in analyzing and correlating data from various sources to track threat activity.
