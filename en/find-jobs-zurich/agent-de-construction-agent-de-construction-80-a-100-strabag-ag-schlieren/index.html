Full description

At STRABAG, around 89,000 people worldwide drive progress at more than 2,400 locations. Uniqueness and individual strengths define not only our projects but also every single one of us. Whether in building and civil engineering, road and underground construction, bridge and tunnel construction, building materials production, project development or facility management - we are rethinking construction in order to become Europe's most innovative and sustainable construction technology group. Equal opportunities, diversity and inclusion are integral parts of what defines us as a company and how we work.

Together, we successfully implement projects as partners and grow through new challenges. Together, we create great things. Let's progress!

Construction commercial clerk 80 to 100%

Your contribution to our company

  • You independently handle the operational commercial tasks of your construction projects in a goal-oriented manner
  • You are responsible for internal accounting and the monthly site billing (accounts payable and accounts receivable)
  • You help shape the operational objectives, strategy and planning of your projects
  • You collect the key financial data of the construction sites and projects, including a planned-versus-actual comparison
  • Together with the construction management, you are responsible for project controlling
  • You contribute to acquisition and project start-up (AVOR)
  • You take care of the personnel administration of the operational construction-site staff
  • You also supervise the commercial apprentices

What matters to us

  • You have completed basic commercial training or a technical apprenticeship with further training in the commercial field
  • You have already gained professional experience in a similar position, preferably in the construction industry
  • You have a strong affinity for numbers and impress with your commitment, reliability and ability to grasp things quickly
  • You are team-oriented and keep an overview even in hectic times
  • A self-directed, proactive and responsible approach to work rounds off your profile
  • Your workload is 80 to 100%, and the place of employment is Schlieren
  • Starting in 2027

What we offer you

  • A responsible role with a direct impact on the success of our projects
  • A well-coordinated team that sticks together, with team gatherings and company events
  • Individual development and further training opportunities
  • Modern work tools and the opportunity to work from home
  • Fair employment conditions at an internationally operating construction group
  • Free drinks, parking, good transport connections and employee discounts
