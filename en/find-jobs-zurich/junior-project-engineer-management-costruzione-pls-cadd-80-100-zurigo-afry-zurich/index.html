Full description

Become part of our Transmission & Distribution team. We have been active in the field of planning systems for the transmission and distribution of electrical energy for decades. The treatment of the associated environmentally relevant aspects rounds off our services. Take on responsibility with us as: JUNIOR PROJECT ENGINEER PIPELINE CONSTRUCTION PLS-CADD 80-100% - ZURICH

YOUR TASKS

  • Collaboration in the technical processing of pipeline construction projects, e.g. support with plans, simple calculations, documentation and technical documents.
  • Support in the planning and supervision of pipeline construction projects – with the opportunity to familiarize yourself step by step with the application of PLS-CADD.
  • Preparation and maintenance of DCS-CADD models (e.g. preparing data, creating first model parts, supporting model testing).
  • Accompaniment of coordination with internal contact persons as well as participation in meetings with customers or authorities
  • Collaboration in compiling decision-making bases, e.g. research, comparative overviews, technical assistance.

YOUR OPPORTUNITIES

  • Exciting and challenging projects with a broad and varied range of tasks
  • You will work in a growing organization for which sustainability and future-oriented technologies are important
  • Targeted personal development opportunities
  • Maximum flexibility in working with the option of mobile working / home office
  • Modern office space with the latest infrastructure in a convenient location near Zurich-Altstetten railway station

Qualifications

YOUR PROFILE

  • Degree in electrical engineering, electrical power engineering, civil engineering or a comparable technical field
  • First practical insights (e.g. through internships, working student activities or project work) in the field of pipeline construction or energy technology
  • Very good written and spoken German, as well as knowledge of French or Italian is an advantage
  • Basic understanding of technical relationships in the field of overhead lines / energy technology
  • Interest in familiarizing yourself with PLS-CADD (previous knowledge is a plus, but not mandatory).
  • Careful, structured way of working and willingness to take on responsibility step by step
  • Team-oriented, communicative way of working and enjoyment of working together in a team

Company Description AFRY provides engineering, project management and consulting services that support the energy transition and industrial transformation and strengthen societal resilience. With 18,000 experts worldwide, we combine global reach with local expertise and extensive industry knowledge to make the future of future generations sustainable.

We offer creative freedom and personal responsibility, a collegial and international working atmosphere, modern employment conditions, flexible working models, above-average social benefits, attractive development opportunities and modern office buildings in a central location.
