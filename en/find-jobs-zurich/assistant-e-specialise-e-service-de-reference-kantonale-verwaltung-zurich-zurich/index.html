Canton of Zurich Financial Directorate Tax Office Specialist Supporter/in reference service 100% The reference service department acts as the tax reference legal service. To strengthen our department, we are looking for a dedicated specialist supporter/reference service as of 1 December 2026 or by agreement. Job description

  • Handling demanding legal and administrative activities together with an existing employee.
  • Processing of requests for a waiver and amendment of regulations.
  • Preparation of liability and tax refund orders including the corresponding calculations.
  • List and statistics management of the Reference Service section.
  • Assist legal staff and the head of the reference service in administrative tasks.
  • Active participation in projects.
  • Training and guidance of trainees. Requirement profile
  • Completed commercial training and several years of professional experience.
  • Ideally, professional experience in the legal environment (court, law firm) or in a public administration.
  • Excellent knowledge of German in words and writing, a pronounced IT affinity and pleasure in dealing with numbers.
  • Independent, committed and team-capable personality.
  • Professional appearance and stylish, precise written and oral expressive ability.
  • Resilience, high service orientation and the ability to think goal-oriented and networked. We offer
  • Exciting and versatile activity with a high degree of independence and responsibility.
  • Careful introduction to the various areas of activity by a well-established and experienced team.
  • Collegial, trusting and appreciative working environment.
  • Development opportunities in the specialist and project area.
  • Flexible working hours, home office and a modern workplace in the immediate vicinity of Zurich Altstetten station. Do you feel addressed? We look forward to your application. Please use the online tool so that we can process your documents. Further information can be provided by Roger Zugr (head of reference service) on tel. 043 259 45 95 or Dominic Ryser (Stv. Head of Reference Service) on tel. 043 259 32 97.

Pensum: 100%
