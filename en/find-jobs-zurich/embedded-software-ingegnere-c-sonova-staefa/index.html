Full description

Are you passionate about solving complex technical challenges where every byte of memory matters?

As an Embedded Software Engineer C++, you will join an experienced team focused on developing deeply embedded firmware for our next generation of hearing devices (medical devices).

This is an opportunity to apply your deep C++ expertise in embedded development. You will work close to the hardware, navigate complex, state-of-the-art development and test environments, and solve challenging problems across the boundaries of software, tooling and hardware.

More about the role

  • Develop object-oriented embedded software in modern C++ for resource-constrained wireless hearing devices.
  • Contribute to the design and architecture of new functionality, working closely with software architects and interdisciplinary platform teams.
  • Develop, document, test, integrate and maintain software throughout the development lifecycle, while ensuring appropriate test coverage and reliable system behavior.
  • Debug complex issues directly on target hardware and in simulators.
  • Work within state-of-the-art development, build and test environments and contribute to their continuous improvement.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams across mobile and desktop software, firmware, verification and requirements engineering to bring new hearing solutions from concept to product.

More about you

  • You have excellent embedded software C++ development know-how, including operating systems, concurrency, communication, distributed systems, and object-oriented design, combined with 5+ years of professional experience.
  • You have developed software for highly resource-constrained systems, with limited memory and battery power.
  • You are skilled in debugging and comfortable investigating complex issues on actual hardware, including situations where the root cause may lie in software, hardware or the development environment.
  • You are familiar with modern embedded software engineering practices and development environments and continuous integration. Knowledge of Python, C#, test frameworks or related tooling is an advantage.
  • You hold a university degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, the natural sciences, or a comparable technical discipline and communicate confidently in English; knowledge of German is a plus.

More about what we offer

As one of the world’s leading hearing care providers headquartered in Switzerland, we’re committed to building an inclusive culture. We want to create an environment where you can balance a successful career with your commitments and interests outside of work, through our flexible hybrid working model. We offer a wide range of training opportunities for both your professional and personal development, and there are exceptional growth opportunities with individual development plans.
