Full description

Do you want not only to operate an integration and data landscape, but also help shape it from the ground up? At Tertianum, you will have the opportunity to build a new company-wide core competency and thereby create the foundation for «One Data», analytics, automation, and future AI applications.

For our IT department at the Dübendorf location, we are looking for, as soon as possible or by agreement, a

Lead Integration & Data Architect 80-100%

Your responsibilities

  • You will build our central integration and data platform and develop it further over the long term
  • You will create the foundation for «One Data» and connect SAP, HR, care, reporting, and other business systems
  • You will be independently responsible for standards and governance for data flows, interfaces, and APIs
  • Across the entire system landscape, you will ensure transparency, monitoring, stability, and traceable documentation of the integrations
  • You will support strategic SAP, cloud, and digitalization initiatives
  • As a point of contact, you will advise the business units on projects and manage external integration partners

Your profile

  • Several years of experience in integration, data architecture, or solution architecture, ideally combined with a software engineering background
  • Solid practical experience with modern integration platforms, APIs, and data flows; knowledge of WSO2, SAP Cloud Integration (CPI), or comparable enterprise integration platforms, as well as experience in the SAP or cloud environment, are an advantage
  • Good understanding of data models, governance, and data quality
  • You think entrepreneurially, translate complex requirements into viable solutions, and bring architecture and implementation together
  • Analytical and conceptual strength combined with a pragmatic hands-on mentality
  • Enjoy building up new areas and shaping them over the long term
