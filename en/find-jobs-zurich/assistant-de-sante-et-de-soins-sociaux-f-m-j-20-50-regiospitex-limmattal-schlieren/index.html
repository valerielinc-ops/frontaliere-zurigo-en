Full description

RegioSpitex Limmattal is an important partner in healthcare RegioSpitex Limmattal is a modern, future-oriented non-profit organization with around 130 employees, which ensures the hospital external care and care for the inhabitants of Dietikon Schlieren and Urdorf. To complement our team, we are looking for a Health and social care assistant (f/m/d) (20-50%) Your multifaceted tasks

  • You care for our customers in their own four walls
  • You promote self-employment and self-responsibility within the scope of the individual possibilities of customers
  • You work with different professional groups
  • You perform administrative tasks
  • The assignments are usually half-day (morning or evening) as well as on weekends.

Your skills

  • Completed training as a nurse or assistant in health and social care
  • You have already gained Spitex experience (forced)
  • You are an open-minded and flexible personality who likes to approach people
  • You bring good communication skills as well as safe handling of mobile care documentation (smartphone)
  • You think networked, work independently and responsibly
  • You have very good knowledge of German (at least B2 level)
  • Driving license category B or the willingness to travel with the e-bike

We offer

  • A versatile and challenging workspace where you can use and develop your expertise and experience
  • A working environment characterized by respect and appreciation
  • A careful introduction
  • Attractive employment conditions and modern infrastructure
  • Freely selectable workload as well as day service and evening service, no night services
  • Generous training and education opportunities
  • All employee benefits
  • Du-culture at all levels
