Full description

Your role Interested in learning more about the dynamics of corporate finance and capital markets?

We’re looking for ambitious students to join our 2027 Internship within our Investment Bank, Global Banking business.

You’ll

  • learn all about the business: mergers and acquisitions (including corporate and sponsor-backed transactions, leveraged buyouts or carve-outs), equity capital markets and debt capital markets transactions
  • be involved in providing advice and execution of merger and acquisitions, spin-offs, refinancings, and other advisory services with a focus on Swiss mid-sized corporations, entrepreneurs, and private equity funds across all sectors
  • analyze complex situations and develop the best strategy to maximize our clients’ diverse objectives
  • support the team on financial analyses and valuation exercises, conducting research activities on markets and companies
  • work closely with our Swiss corporate banking, private banking and our international investment banking network of industry experts, providing an optimal platform for collaboration and your development

Your team You’ll be working in Zürich, Switzerland in our Global Banking business (part of the Investment Bank), within our Swiss Mid Market Investment Banking team. We advise Swiss mid-sized corporations, entrepreneurs and private equity funds on their strategic development and financing activities, including M&A, debt and capital raisings as well as leveraged buyouts.

Your expertise

  • is currently enrolled in studies with a graduation date in 2027 or 2028
  • is motivated to work in a fast-paced business with high demands and tight deadlines
  • is a strategic thinker with sharp analytical and quantitative skills
  • solid corporate finance knowledge of accounting and valuation techniques
  • has an excellent academic track-record and with strong communication skills in English and German, French is a plus
  • previous experience or familiarity with Investment Banking, Transaction Services, Consulting or related financial services is an advantage
  • can evidence how experimental and responsible use of AI can improve outcomes and efficiency

Start date: Between July to December 2027

Your program During your 6 month internship, you’ll work with our experts and learn about the industry, our clients and our firm. You’ll be right at the heart of our business, learning from your colleagues, taking part in day-to-day operations. You’ll have plenty of opportunities to develop new skills and make contacts along the way. If you like challenges, enjoy learning and want to work where no two days are the same, this program is for you. If you have a successful internship, you may get an offer to return for our Graduate Talent Program after you've completed your studies.
