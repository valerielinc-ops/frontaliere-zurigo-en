Full description

Do you want to actively shape the introduction of Building Information Modelling (BIM) method in the Zurich Building Directorate? As part of the BIM@BD program, you support in defining processes, roles and standards in several offices with the aim of making planning, construction and operation processes more efficient and transparent. In your function as a BIM specialist at the Tiefbaumt, you use your knowledge in the construction industry to analyze existing processes and shape concrete applications.

Your tasks

  • Analysis of existing planning, construction and operation processes as well as development of future digital processes (e.g. by means of BIM).
  • Development and description of BIM application cases and processes, as well as concrete information and data requirements for buildings and infrastructures.
  • Participation in the development and further development of BIM standards, guidelines and information requirements of the construction directorate.
  • Documentation and further development of roles and organizational foundations for the application of the BIM method.
  • Support of projects in the introduction and application of the defined BIM work methods. Your profile
  • Multi-year professional experience in the field of digital planning and construction as well as sound knowledge of the BIM method and open BIM standards.
  • Very good understanding of planning, construction and project processes in Swiss construction.
  • Graduate in civil engineering, architecture, construction informatics or a similar subject.
  • Experience in the development of BIM application cases, information requirements or data structures as well as in the analysis and modelling of processes (e.g.BPMN).
  • Team-oriented personality with joy in interdisciplinary cooperation and cross-disciplinary coordination. This awaits you
  • An exciting task at the interface of construction, digitalization and organizational development.
  • The possibility to actively shape the introduction of the BIM method in the construction direction of the canton of Zurich.
  • Cooperation with specialist offices from several offices as well as with external partners from planning, construction and operation.
