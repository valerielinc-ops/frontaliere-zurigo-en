02.10.2026 Thalwil

Become part of our team!

Raiffeisenbank Thalwil is a well-positioned and regionally oriented bank with headquarters in Thalwil and a branch office in Adliswil. As a young, independent cooperative banking institution of the Raiffeisen Group, it operates successfully on the market. As of 30.06.2026, the bank has a balance sheet total of around 917 million francs with a focus on investment and mortgage business. Around 42 employees provide a lot of enthusiasm and passion for satisfied customers and cooperatives. In order to continue to develop our bank in the coming years, we are looking for a creative and entrepreneurial leader as Chairman of the Bank Management.

What do you expect?

  • Together with the management, you lead the operational banking business and implement the strategic guidelines of the Board of Directors together with your employees.
  • As an entrepreneur, you will recognize potential in a challenging market and develop ideas on how to exploit it to ensure healthy growth of the bank.
  • They position the bank as a sympathetic, attractive financial service provider throughout the region and exploit existing potential.
  • The profile and emotional customer experience is at the center – You motivate your employees to provide exemplary services, unique customer rituals and top performance.
  • They network in the market and represent the bank in public.

What do you bring?

  • You are an entrepreneurial, emphatic personality with proven leadership experience - you create framework conditions in which employees can develop and create motivation.
  • Your broad banking expertise, which is based on a banking or business education, complements you with many years of sales experience and an appropriate risk awareness.
  • You have already demonstrated your skills in different functions and leadership roles in different banking divisions.
  • You identify with the unique Raiffeisen culture and also live our values in your leadership work.
  • You are characterized by a professional appearance, are open and the appropriate and effective communication with a wide range of stakeholders is one of your strengths.
  • Local anchoring is advantageous and desirable.

As a locally anchored bank, we are firmly rooted in our region: we know the people, companies and their concerns and act with responsibility for the sustainable development of our community. Together. For each other. Are you ready to contribute with passion and responsibility and to further develop our modern bank in this sense?
