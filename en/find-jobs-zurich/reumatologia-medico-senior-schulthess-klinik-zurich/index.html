Tasks

  • Care of consultation hours with the entire spectrum of rheumatological diseases including osteoporosis
  • Internal hospital consultancy services
  • Opportunity to participate in the sonography consultation
  • Teaching and supervision of assistant doctors

Requirements

  • Specialist training in rheumatology
  • Enjoying the interdisciplinary cooperation and the education and training of young colleagues
  • Willingness to shape the department, assuming leadership responsibility and organizational skills

The Schulthess Clinic in Zurich is one of the leading orthopaedic clinics in Europe with over 1,100 employees. Our central concern is to free people from their pain and restore their mobility. As a specialist clinic, we focus on surgical orthopedics, neurology, rheumatology and sports medicine. Schulthess Clinic is Swiss Olympic Medical Center.
