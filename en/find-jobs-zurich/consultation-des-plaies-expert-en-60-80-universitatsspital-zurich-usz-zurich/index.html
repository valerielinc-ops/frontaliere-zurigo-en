Full description

USZ Campus by December or by agreement: The USZ wound counselling team is looking for support!

Who are we?

As a wound consulting team, we carry out outpatient and inpatient wound consiles and accompany patients with complex wound situations. We work closely with various specialist areas and interprofessional professional groups. We advise, coach and support nurses and treatment teams in professional wound care. At the same time, we advise and empower patients in dealing with their wound and thus promote as independent care as possible. With regular further education and training, we also contribute to passing on current knowledge and professional competences in wound management. With a lot of commitment, we also devote ourselves to specialist and practical development. In addition to our clinical activities, we take on conceptual tasks and actively shape the further development of wound management at the USZ – for example in the development of action-oriented recommendations, the further development of standards or as part of certifications.

If you bring the following, you are in the right place with us

  • Completed training as a dipl. Nurse FH with at least two years of professional experience
  • A degree in wound management, for example as a dipl. wound expert: in SAfW, or the willingness to complete a corresponding further education in a timely manner
  • Practical experience in wound care and treatment
  • Enjoy sharing your expertise and advising and empowering nurses and patients
  • An independent, structured and solution-oriented working method as well as strong organizational and communication skills
  • Enjoying interprofessional cooperation and actively shaping developments

What do we offer?

With us, you can expect a versatile and responsible work in a university environment characterized by professional development, innovation and digitalization. By working with different clinics and specialist areas, you will encounter diverse and complex wound situations and can continuously expand your professional repertoire. You will have the opportunity to contribute your expertise and to actively shape the further development of wound management at USZ. At the same time, we support you in your personal and professional development and offer you a wide range of internal further education and training opportunities. In your function, you organize and shape your work largely independently. At the same time, you are part of an experienced team in which professional exchange, mutual support and joint learning are a matter of course. Did we get your interest?
