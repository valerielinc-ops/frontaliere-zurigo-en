Full description

Hohenegg Private Clinic is a specialist clinic with 95 beds for psychiatry, psychotherapy and psychosomatics. It is open and accessible to private, semi-private and general insured persons from Switzerland as well as self-payers. It is housed in a listed magnificent complex, in the middle of nature and high above Lake Zurich. In addition to inpatient services, we operate a mental health centre (ZPG) for outpatient and hospital-associated psychiatry at two locations. The sponsorship is a charitable foundation. With us, you can bring yourself and your proven professional skills into a coherent, meaningful working environment. We are looking for well-qualified and socially competent employees who are passionate about their profession. Are you looking for a new and exciting professional challenge? Is the psychological well-being of people as important to you as the physical one? And do you appreciate the teamwork at an exceptionally beautiful place of work high above Lake Zurich? Then you are exactly right with us. We are looking for a motivated personality immediately or by agreement that complements our team as Your tasks

  • Working in a competent and committed team
  • Implementation of the care process as part of our clinic model
  • Caregiver for patients with a diagnostic focus on depression, burnout, anxiety disorders or psychosomatics

Your profile

  • HF Diploma, DN II Diploma or equivalent education
  • Professional experience in psychiatry is beneficial
  • Willingness to work in shift work (no night work)
  • High level of expertise and teamwork
  • Independent work and high quality of care

Your advantages

  • Appreciative and varied work environment
  • Dynamic team with interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation
  • Free parking and sports facilities
  • Various team and company events
  • Working on a privileged location high above Lake Zurich

Interested? For further information, please contact Sadik Bahadir, Co-Head of Care Directorate, telephone: +41 44 925 18 33.
