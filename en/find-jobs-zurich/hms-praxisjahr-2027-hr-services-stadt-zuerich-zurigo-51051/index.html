Full description

Are you planning your career and attending the Handelsmittelschule Büelrain, Enge or Hottingen? Do you want to work in Zurich? You already know that you are interested in working in the Human Resources department. Then you are at the right address at ewz.

Tasks

  • You take over the triage of the physical mail and manage the general email mailbox.
  • In the personnel administration you support with entry and exit, service anniversaries, baby gifts, work permits and accident reports.
  • You will take care of document management, including uploading images for new entries, creating badges and archiving relevant documents.
  • In addition, you create and send work and intermediate certificates as well as welcome emails.
  • Your responsibilities also include checking social security numbers and residency permits, ordering office supplies, and managing side jobs and civil status changes.

Profile

  • You are enthusiastic about human resources, are eager to know and convince with your willingness to learn.
  • Dealing with internal and external customers gives you pleasure and you bring a strong sense of service.
  • Your good knowledge of German distinguishes you and you work independently and organized.
  • You are safe in dealing with digital systems or Tools and work trustworthy with sensitive data.
  • As a team player, you fit well into our diverse HR team with your friendly and helpful manner.

We offer

You can expect a well-founded and varied education. Your vocational trainees support you on your way to a successful practical year. You benefit from attractive employment conditions.

  • Community with 40 learners and interns
  • 5 weeks holidays and 6 business holidays
  • Contribution to your public transport subscription and the food costs
  • Preparation for statutory audits
  • Possibility of a year of professional experience after successful completion of the internship.

About us

ewz has been dynamic and visionary on the road every day for more than 130 years. Whether sustainable energy production, smart energy solution or innovative networking technology: Develop with us today what is important tomorrow.
