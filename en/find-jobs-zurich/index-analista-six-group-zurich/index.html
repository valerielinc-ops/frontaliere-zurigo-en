Account Manager

Do you want to work in a highly dynamic environment? Are you passionate about market data? Then our Account Management team wants to hear from you! In this position you will work closely with Administration & Intelligence, Legal & Compliance and Operations.

What You Will Do

  • Responsible for the holistic and service-oriented management of existing and new clients within the framework of proactive account management.
  • Acting as the primary point of contact for clients, coordinating cross-functional teams (Product, Legal, Operations) to ensure seamless service delivery, while monitoring account performance and aligning solutions with client needs and business objectives.
  • Building and maintaining sustainable client relationships.
  • Driving commercial growth through upselling and cross-selling of market data services and value-added analytics offerings.
  • Initiate and support client audits and assisting the external auditor in the preparation and execution of audit activities.

What You Bring

  • Proven experience in the financial services industry (3-8 years).
  • Solid background in the market data domain (e.g. data vendor, exchange or bank).
  • Experience in account management, sales, or client relationship management.
  • Strong sense of ownership and accountability.
  • Fluent in German and English (both verbal & writing), Spanish or French is a plus.
