The Multifaceted Day

  • They are the technical contact person for pedagogical issues in the area of housing and support teams and managers in a practical way in day-to-day care and team life.
  • They accompany case discussions, pedagogical processes and professional reflections and support the teams in complex situations.
  • They help develop agogic specialist principles, standards and instruments and support their implementation and further development in everyday living.
  • They design practical specialist inputs and further training for employees and participate in educational offers for clients.
  • They promote professional exchange and knowledge transfer and work closely with Agogik experts: women from the fields of work, supported communication and prevention as well as other specialist areas.

Their diverse talents

  • they have completed training at tertiary level in the field of social, medical or special education or in a related field with comparable qualifications;
  • They bring in-depth agogic expertise and experience in working with people in need of support as well as in specialist advice or practical support.
  • They are an empathetic, communication-strong personality with professional serenity, which also conveys peace and orientation in complex situations.
  • They meet teams and clients at eye level, think in a networked way and look for pragmatic, solution-oriented ways.
  • Enjoyment in specialist development, knowledge transfer and cooperation with different professional groups rounds off your profile.

A varied field of activity between specialist advice, practice support, education and specialist development A versatile technical function with creative leeway and direct influence on the agogic quality in the EPI WohnWerk A motivated, interdisciplinary team and a lively environment that you can help shape with your own ideas Extensive internal training offer and support for external training.
