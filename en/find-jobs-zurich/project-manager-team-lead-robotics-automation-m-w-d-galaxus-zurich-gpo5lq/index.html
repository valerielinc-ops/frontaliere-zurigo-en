Full description

In the role of Project Manager & Team Lead - Robotics & Automation in the area of operations, you support us in expanding our storage capacities and automating our processes. You take responsibility for projects in the logistics environment and actively shape the future of Digitec Galaxus.

Mansioni

  • Together with the team, overall responsibility for robotics and plant optimization projects in the logistics environment (“Brown Field”) will be assumed
  • As a team leader, you lead a team of project managers (coaching & governance), but you also work as a project manager (60–80%)
  • As a project manager, you lead project teams of four to five project leaders.
  • Responsibility for the robotics roadmap, determination of optimization potential at existing plants together with the operation and development of suitable solution concepts
  • Implementation and support of research, analyses and calculations in the concept phases as well as presentation of findings to management
  • High understanding of operational needs and dependencies and ensuring the smoothest possible implementation of plant extensions
  • Active exchange with operational stakeholders (safety, technology, control room, etc.)

Requirements

  • 5+ years (senior/lead profile, with first management experience): Robotics & Automation Project Management in logistics (AGV/AMR, intralogistics)
  • Completed studies (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): Project Management / Engineering
  • Completed technical studies (ETH/UNI/FH) with a focus on mechanical engineering, mechatronics, electrical engineering, automation engineering, industrial engineering, etc.
  • At least 5 years of experience as a project manager in complex environments – ideally in industrial automation, intralogistics or production environments
  • Ideally, already experience in leading a team of specialists
  • Practical experience with robotics, AGV/AMR or material flow engineering projects is a clear advantage
  • Secure, open and target-group-oriented communication skills, with the ability to lead discussions to consensus
  • Style-confident knowledge of German and English in words and writing
  • Correct reputation and willingness to provide relevant reference persons who can assess work experience
  • The willingness to question “as it has always been done” and propose more efficient, automated or effective solutions
  • Using creativity and initiative to solve problems and achieve goals
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • English (very good knowledge)
