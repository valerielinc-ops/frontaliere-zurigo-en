Location: Hirslanden Clinic in the Park | Zurich

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 71928

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

YOUR TASKS

  • You lead, promote and develop your dialysis team professionally and organizationally and create a motivating working environment.
  • You ensure high-quality, safe and patient-oriented care as well as compliance with our quality standards.
  • You plan and coordinate the personnel deployment and ensure that the dialysis station runs smoothly.
  • You are actively involved in day-to-day care and perform complex dialysis-specific treatments and shunt punctures professionally.
  • You promote close interdisciplinary cooperation and are committed to the continuous development of processes and quality standards.
  • You take responsibility for the organization of the Picett Service and participate in the weekend Picett Service if necessary.

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a qualified nurse HF/FH or an equivalent education recognised in Switzerland.
  • You bring several years of professional experience in dialysis as well as in-depth expertise.
  • You already have leadership experience or want to take the next step into a leadership function and bring the corresponding potential.
  • You convince through organizational skill, sense of responsibility, decision-making and a structured way of working.
  • You communicate appreciatively, enjoy working in an interdisciplinary manner and understand how to motivate and develop employees.
  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing, further language skills are an advantage.

A motivated, committed and multi-professional team awaits you in an advanced working environment. This is characterized by high quality of care, independence, flexibility and a sense of responsibility. We also offer our employees generous social benefits, sports offers, special insurance conditions and further education opportunities within the framework of our program.
