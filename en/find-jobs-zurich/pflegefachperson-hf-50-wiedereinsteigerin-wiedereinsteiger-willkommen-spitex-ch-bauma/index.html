Full description

As a non-profit organisation, Spitex Bauma looks after sick, elderly and convalescent people at home. An offer for podology and foot care rounds off our services. We are looking for an immediately or by agreement Nurse HF 50% (re-entry/re-entry welcome)

Their tasks shall include

  • holistic care and care of sick and elderly people including case management
  • Advice and guidance for relatives and carers
  • Collaboration in a team
  • Cooperation in quality development

We wish to see

  • a dedicated person with pleasure in hospitalexternal care
  • Flexibility and willingness to take responsibility
  • Teamwork
  • Openness to new things
  • Client and service-oriented thinking and acting
  • Knowledge of interRAI HC or willingness to attend a course
  • Driving licence Cat. B

We offer them

  • A motivated and collaborative team
  • An appreciative corporate culture
  • Live carers
  • A careful, comprehensive introduction
  • a varied activity with the opportunity to contribute your personality
  • Employee-friendly service planning
  • a flat hierarchy with a lot of scope for action
  • Employment according to cantonal regulations with at least five weeks holidays
  • generous billing of the pension fund (employer / employee 60%/40%)

Are you interested? We look forward to meeting you personally. For further information we are at your disposal, telephone 052 386 25 25

Please send your written or electronic application to
