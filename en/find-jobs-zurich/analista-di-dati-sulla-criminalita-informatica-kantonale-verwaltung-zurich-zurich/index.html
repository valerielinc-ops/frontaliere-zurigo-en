Full description

The Cantonal Police of Zurich and its approximately 4,000 employees assume responsibility for security in the canton. The Cybercrime department provides support in operational investigation procedures. The tasks of the Investigation Support Service include the analysis of information and data as well as the training, advice and support of internal and external partners. For these tasks within the crime analysis group we are looking for a cybercrime data analyst in 80 - 100% As a specialist in operational crime analysis, you are responsible for the following tasks:

  • Preparation, evaluation and analysis of information and data in the context of police investigation procedures
  • Focus on financial analysis, text analysis and database management
  • Evaluation and implementation of AI solutions
  • Detection of crime patterns and visualization of evaluated data
  • Development of hypotheses and recommendations for action
  • Preparation of reports for procedural management
  • Active cooperation with various partner organisations
  • Speaker work and participation in internal and external education and training Profile

  • Good communication skills and strong team skills
  • Team-oriented, innovative and resilient personality with a high sense of responsibility

  • Stylistic German and good English skills (B2)
