Full description

You will be working with one of the most exciting insurance product of the industry. Your challenge would be developing the expansion of our business by building strong inside and outside networking to enhance growth and our firm's reputation in the Casualty Insurance Market. You show leadership through your proactiveness. Also, you're empowered to manage your business routine and where you work.

About the Role Support the Casualty underwriting team in delivering sustainable portfolio growth through sound underwriting practices, data-driven insights, process optimization, and the adoption of digital solutions.

  • Underwrite and manage Casualty accounts within delegated authority, ensuring adherence to underwriting guidelines, risk appetite, and profitability targets.
  • Analyze risk information, exposures, loss experience, and coverage requirements to support underwriting decisions and portfolio management.
  • Contribute to portfolio monitoring through the development and maintenance of management reports, dashboards, and performance analyses using tools such as Power BI, Excel, and other analytical platforms.
  • Collaborate with brokers, clients, and internal stakeholders to structure insurance solutions and ensure high service standards throughout the underwriting process.
  • Identify opportunities to improve underwriting controls, operational processes, and data quality, contributing to greater efficiency and governance.
  • Support the automation of underwriting workflows, reporting routines, and data management processes to enhance productivity and decision-making.
  • Explore and leverage artificial intelligence, advanced analytics, and digital tools to optimize underwriting activities, portfolio analysis, and business insights.
  • Participate in testing, implementation, and continuous enhancement of underwriting systems, tools, and data quality initiatives related to the Casualty portfolio.
  • Work closely with Product, Risk Engineering, Claims, Operations, and Data teams to support portfolio development and strategic initiatives.
  • Contribute to a culture of continuous improvement, innovation, and knowledge sharing across the Casualty underwriting team.

About the Team

The Casualty Underwriting team is responsible for underwriting and managing General Liability and specialized Casualty risks for corporate clients across a diverse range of industries. We focus on delivering sustainable and profitable growth through disciplined underwriting, technical excellence, and a strong understanding of clients' risk exposures. Working closely with brokers, clients, Risk Engineering, Claims, and other internal stakeholders, the team develops tailored insurance solutions that address complex liability risks while maintaining portfolio profitability and underwriting rigor. Data, analytics, and digital innovation are at the core of our approach. By leveraging advanced tools, portfolio insights, and emerging technologies, including automation and artificial intelligence, we continuously enhance decision-making, operational efficiency, and customer experience. As part of Swiss Re Corporate Solutions, the team fosters a collaborative, forward-looking environment that encourages continuous learning, innovation, and professional development.

  • 4 to 7 years of experience in the insurance industry, preferably within Commercial Insurance, Casualty / Liability.

  • Interest in data-driven decision making, portfolio analytics, and continuous process improvement.

  • Strong communication, negotiation, and relationship management skills.
