With us, you can alternatively start your application via WhatsApp and contact us directly and easily. Your new tasks

  • You take on responsibility for maintenance and retrofit projects of systems in the field of protection and control technology in high and medium voltage
  • You will create holistic concept descriptions, specifications and investment documentation and coordinate the implementation with relevant specialists
  • In addition, you will be responsible for hardware and software engineering, carry out integration tests and implement commissioning both in the test laboratory and on site at the customer in Switzerland.
  • In this context, you will advise customers and internal positions competently on technical solutions and are available as a contact person for complex questions Your talents and experiences
  • You have completed an apprenticeship as an engineer: in FH or as a technician: in HF with a specialisation in electrical engineering, automation or computer science
  • You have profound experience in commissioning protection and control systems
  • Ideally, you have knowledge of project management and network technology
  • You are familiar with the creation of concept descriptions, specifications and technical investment documentation
  • You convince through your structured, solution-oriented working method and your secure presence with the customer
  • You have reliable knowledge of German as well as good knowledge of English, French or Italian are advantageous
