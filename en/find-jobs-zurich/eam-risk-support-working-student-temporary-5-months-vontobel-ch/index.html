Full description

Support our EAM (External Asset Managers) Business Management team in risk & compliance tasks. This role offers hands-on experience at the intersection of business management, risk, and regulatory oversight within a Private Banking front organization.

What would your role be?

  • Support client reviews , prepare and coordinate the resolution of outstanding review items with front-office teams.
  • Gather, analyze , and consolidate data from multiple sources to support risk and compliance activities.
  • Perform data quality checks, analyses, and plausibility reviews to ensure accurate and reliable information.
  • Assist with internal audit follow-up activities, including controls, reporting, and tracking of remediation measures.

What are we looking for?

  • We are looking for a motivated young professional who enjoys a hands-on role and is keen to learn and grow on the job.
  • Currently pursuing or recently completed a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Law , Business Administration, Banking & Finance, Risk Management, Compliance, or related field.
  • Willingness to take ownership of both operational and administrative tasks in a proactive and reliable manner. Strong interest in regulatory, risk, and compliance topics within Private Banking.
  • Analytical mindset with a structured and detail-oriented approach to work.
  • Excellent communication skills in German and English.

What do we offer ?

  • Top Zurich location with collaboration spaces and free lunch .
  • Friendly, diverse team in an international environment .
  • Agile environment in how we think, the way we work and our trust for each other .
  • Flat hierarchy with collaboration on all levels .
  • Collaborative spirit with opportunities to learn and thrive .
