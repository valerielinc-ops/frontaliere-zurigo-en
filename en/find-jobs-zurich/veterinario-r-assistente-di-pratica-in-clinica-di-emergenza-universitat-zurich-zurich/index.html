University Animal Hospital Zurich

Job opening by agreement, indefinitely

The University Animal Hospital Zurich is a leader in the field of veterinary medicine. Every day, it makes a comprehensive patient-oriented contribution to the care of sick and injured animals from all over Switzerland and nearby abroad. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for a committed and active personality as a veterinary assistant: in emergency clinic.

Tasks

You are interested in working in a motivated and productive team. They enjoy learning new things and appreciate the professional care of emergency patients. This important position in a modern environment includes the following tasks:

  • Care, supervision and care of the entering emergency patients in shift operation incl. Weekend, late and night service on an appropriate scale
  • Assistance in treatments, interventions and patient clarifications
  • Close cooperation with the entire medical team of the emergency center, as well as all doctors from the various fields

Profile

To strengthen our teams, we want a motivated person who meets the following requirements:

  • Completed training as a veterinary practice assistant with broad professional experience is an advantage
  • Completed training as a veterinary practitioner with broad professional experience is an advantage
  • Social skills, resilience and motivation
  • Thoughtful working methods even in hectic situations
  • Very good observation and empathy
  • Fun with active participation in the team
  • Enjoyment of a good quality of work
  • High sense of responsibility
  • Interest in your own further development
  • English skills are beneficial

We offer a varied, challenging activity in an unusual modern environment. Training opportunities for internal and external events. The salary is based on the cantonal guidelines.
