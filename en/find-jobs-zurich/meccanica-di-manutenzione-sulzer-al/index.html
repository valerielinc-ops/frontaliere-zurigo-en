About the role

Job Summary Responsible for maintaining and repairing industrial machinery and mechanical systems. This role performs complex diagnostics, preventive maintenance, and repairs, ensuring equipment reliability and operational efficiency.

Key Responsibilities

  • Inspect, troubleshoot, and repair mechanical equipment.
  • Perform preventive maintenance and document findings.
  • Replace or fabricate parts as needed.
  • Maintain tools and ensure safe working conditions.

  • Train and support junior mechanics.
  • Follow safety and operational procedures.

Qualifications / Requirements

  • Strong mechanical aptitude and diagnostic skills.
  • Experience with industrial machinery and tools.
  • Ability to read technical manuals and schematics.
  • Knowledge of safety regulations and maintenance procedures.
  • Good communication and teamwork skills.
