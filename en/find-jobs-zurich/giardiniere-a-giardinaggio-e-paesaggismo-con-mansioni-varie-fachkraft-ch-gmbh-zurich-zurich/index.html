Full description

Maintaining private gardens and public green spaces is one of the core tasks. The company relies on independent work directly with the customer and an appreciative cooperation. For our client we are looking for an activity

  • care and maintenance of private gardens as well as public green areas
  • implementation of lawn, hedge and planting work
  • execution of smaller renovation and redesign projects
  • Self-responsible work directly at the customer on site
  • Requirements
  • Completed training as a gardener with a focus on gardening and landscaping
  • Experience in the maintenance of gardens and public green spaces
  • Self-employed, reliable and team-oriented working methods
  • Friendly behaviour in customer contact
  • Category B driving licence is an advantageous offer
  • Diverse and varied
  • Tasks in a dedicated team - Use of modern tools and vehicles - Opportunities for further education and long-term development
