Full description

Movemi AG operates the largest fitness network in Switzerland with 126 ACTIV FITNESS and 16 fitness parks. For the professional management of our extensive rental portfolio, we are looking for an experienced personality who combines legal, economic and operational topics.

Luogo di lavoro: movemi AG, Verwaltungs movemi, Thurgauerstrasse 32, 8050 Oerlikon

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned. An application letter or video is desired for this position.

Mansioni

  • Support and management of the commercial rental conditions of our fitness locations
  • Preparation and management of addenda, extensions, options and further contractual adjustments
  • First contact person for landlords and subtenants for contractual and management-related issues
  • Monitoring of deadlines, terminations and lease obligations
  • Support for contractual issues and clarifications in connection with conversions, renovations, land adjustments, dismantling obligations and landlord approvals
  • Maintenance and updating of lease contract, cost and master data
  • Support in matters of rental law as well as coordination of external investigations
  • Verification of heating and ancillary cost accounts
  • Preparation of contract- and location-related information for expansion, construction and management
  • Close cooperation with expansion, construction, facility management, operations and finance

Requirements

  • Several years of experience: in the management of commercial real estate or in commercial rental contract management
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): further education in the field of property management or a corresponding certificate of advantage
  • Independent and structured handling of contracts, deadlines and complex issues
  • Strong communication skills in dealing with landlords, subtenants and internal contacts
  • Safe handling of MS Office, especially Excel; SAP knowledge is an advantage
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Part of the Migros Group: Attractive special conditions at Migros Bank, premium discounts at Migros Insurance and discounts at Migros Holidays
  • Central location: offices and studios in a well-developed, central location
  • Healthy work: Modernly furnished workstations with height-adjustable tables
  • Mobile subscription: National flat subscription from CHF 10.– / month (Swisscom /digitec connect)
  • Migros Bank: expense-free private and investment account with preferential interest
  • Employee offers: fleet discount on numerous car brands and special conditions on car sharing offers and car rentals
  • Work climate: Helpful team members and uncomplicated handling across all hierarchical levels
  • Du-culture: We stand for a respectful coexistence at eye level

Contatto

Application and contact Susanne Hug HR Business Partner

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Expert Discussion: In a specialist discussion, specific knowledge relevant for the execution of the job is requested by the manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, the team. If possible, there is time afterwards to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition: After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
