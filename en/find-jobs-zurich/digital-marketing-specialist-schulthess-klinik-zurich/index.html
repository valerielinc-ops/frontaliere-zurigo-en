Digital Marketing Specialist — Schulthess Klinik

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Tasks

  • You are responsible for the strategic and operational development of our digital communication channels and ensure their target group-oriented, brand-compliant and effective orientation.
  • You will continuously develop our website, plan and control online campaigns as well as SEO/SEA measures and analyze their success based on relevant KPIs.
  • You plan, create and send newsletters, maintain the shipping lists and supervise our in-house screens. You will also develop channel-oriented content for our digital platforms as well as internal communication.
  • As a sparring partner, you support the strategic development of our social media channels, observe trends and developments and provide impulses for contemporary digital communication.
  • You advise internal stakeholders on communication issues, work on cross-divisional projects and ensure consistent compliance with our brand presence.

Requirements

  • You have a (specialist) university degree and several years of professional experience in communication, digital marketing or content management.
  • You bring in-depth experience in managing digital channels and are familiar with CMS, SEO/SEA, web analytics and newsletter marketing.
  • You have experience in project management and ideally have already successfully planned and implemented digital projects – for example a website relaunch.
  • You enjoy developing target-group-oriented content and preparing complex topics in an understandable and creative way.
  • You work independently, structured and analytically, convince with a high level of initiative and keep an overview even in challenging projects.
  • You communicate stylishly in German and have good knowledge of English. Team spirit, service orientation and a sense of digital trends and brand communication round off your profile.

We offer

  • 40 hourly week
  • Possibility for home office
  • Work-Life Balance / Family Friendliness
  • Team spirit
  • Family management
  • Cheap food in the staff restaurant

The Schulthess Clinic in Zurich is one of the leading orthopaedic clinics in Europe with over 1,100 employees. Our central concern is to free people from their pain and restore their mobility. As a specialist clinic, we focus on surgical orthopedics, neurology, rheumatology and sports medicine. Schulthess Clinic is Swiss Olympic Medical Center.
