Full description

Children's Hospital Zurich Eleonorenstiftung | online since : 14.07.2026

temporary for 1 year in the outpatient follow-up care for patients and their families after an intensive stay with sepsis and in combination with further clinical activity

Your tasks

  • You are responsible for the assessment of patients and their families after an intensive stay with sepsis, triage and clarify interprofessional follow-up care.
  • You will advise those affected and their families in the symptom management of PICS-p/f.
  • You work closely with the interprofessional treatment team.

Your profile

  • Diploma Care HF or FH
  • Several years of experience in the care of children in the intensive care unit (NDS IPS)
  • In-depth expertise on the subject of PICS or the willingness to acquire them
  • Completed Master of Science FH in Nursing (APN) or ongoing/future equivalent studies
