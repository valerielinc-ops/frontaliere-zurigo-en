Full description

Your mission

  • In coordination with the management, you coordinate all logistics-related activities in the store and take care of the correct implementation of the processes
  • You are responsible for the restocking of the shelves, register receipts and check them for completeness and integrity
  • You control all deliveries of packages and pallets for damage and completeness
  • You ensure the agreed availability of the own use material
  • In cooperation with the back office, you monitor inventory deviations and misdeliveries
  • You are responsible for the control and handling of shipping (returns, transfers, customer shipments)
  • You are responsible for SUVA-compliant storage of goods and keeping emergency exits clear
  • You are responsible for the management of all disposals and recycling and their transport
  • You are responsible for goods expenses to customers, suppliers and service providers
  • You help to make and optimize our logistics processes even more efficiently

Your match with us

  • You have completed vocational training, ideally as a logistics specialist EFZ or as a retail specialist EFZ
  • You bring several years of relevant professional experience in sales or logistics
  • You have a strong customer-oriented thinking and action
  • You distinguish yourself through your IT affinity, your own initiative and your planning and organizational skills
  • You are a team player and you enjoy achieving something together with others
  • You have a very good oral and written expression in German
