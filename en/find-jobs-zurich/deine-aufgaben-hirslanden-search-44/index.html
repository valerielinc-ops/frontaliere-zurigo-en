Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Occupation by appointment

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 19410

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

YOUR TASKS

  • Instrumentation and service during operations as well as carrying out all preparatory and follow-up measures of the operations in a specialist group selected by you: specialist group 1: cardiac / thoracic / vascular surgery, hybrid specialist group 2: visceral surgery, plastic surgery, urology, gynecology, Da Vinci, Dexter specialist group 3: orthopedics, traumatology, sports / hand / foot surgery specialist group 4: neurosurgery, spine, ORL, eyes
  • Technical and professional preparation and operation of the necessary equipment, instruments and sterile goods
  • Monitoring and care of patients in local anesthesia during procedures
  • Documentation of all nursing activities including performance recording
  • Assistance in ensuring a smooth daily routine in the operating room
  • Instrumentation at HSM Surgery

Your profile

  • Training for the specialist surgical technique HF or Dipl. FA OP nurse (corresponding SRC recognition for foreign diplomas)
  • Knowledge in the subject areas is an advantage, but not a condition. We are happy to accompany and support you in your development.
  • Whether generalist or specialist, with us you will find what suits you.
  • You are a team player and open and respectful communication is just as important to you as it is to us.
  • Your own initiative and commitment are a matter of course for you.
  • High motivation and dedication as well as willingness to perform weekend/picquet as well as night service

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, a cool team awaits you with open arms, so that you quickly feel "at home" and like to show you a lot. We offer you individual and tailor-made training as well as flexible working time models that enable you to combine work and family. Re-entrants are also welcome.

Our benefits for employees

For additional information, Mihaela Filipovic, Team Leader OP, is available at mihaela.filipovic@hirslanden.ch or Janine Moschetto, Team Leader HR Recruitment Partner Platz Zürich, at janine.moschetto@hirslanden.ch. We look forward to your contact.
