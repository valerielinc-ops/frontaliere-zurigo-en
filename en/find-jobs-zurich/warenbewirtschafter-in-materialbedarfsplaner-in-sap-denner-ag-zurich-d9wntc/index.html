Full description

Do you want to help in the Operation Center, a dynamic department, to provide our goods in the right quantity at the right place at the right time and are passionate about it? Then you are right with us! Denner. This is our store.

Mansioni

  • Process the procurement of the assigned goods group in the SAP S/4 HANA ERP system.
  • You provide an impeccable and forward-looking disposition incl. Action orders at the suppliers safely.
  • You ensure optimal product availability in the distribution centers, monitor the daily reports and analyze the key figures.
  • You work on the continuous improvement and consistent further development of the disposition processes.
  • You maintain the master data in the scheduling area, which requires a regular exchange with purchasing and logistics.
  • You support strategic purchasing for listings and exclusions of items by means of inventory calculations and their reach.

Requirements

  • 4-5 years of professional experience: Relevant experience in this or related field
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): professional training in purchasing or procurement logistics
  • User knowledge in SAP is mandatory, as is an experienced handling of MS-Office, especially Excel.
  • A winning personality with healthy assertiveness who acts entrepreneurially, self-initiatively and responsibly. In addition, your analytical skills ideally round off your profile.
  • Interest in goods flow processes and understanding business relationships. You are happy to be involved in finding solutions and implementing them.
  • in German (negotiation confident)
  • English (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Central place of work: In the casual Binz district in Zurich district 3 (2 minutes by foot from Zurich station Binz).
  • Part of the Migros Group: Strong brand anchored in the retail trade under the Migros Group umbrella
  • Flexible place of work / home office: One to two days a week working at home. Except in IT - we are even more flexible.
  • Mobile subscription: Spoiled for choice: With Galaxus Mobile, Wingo or Sunrise already from CHF 10.- one or the other really connected.
  • Migros Bank: Better ride on interest and fees of your private, investment or mortgage accounts.
  • Holidays: 6 weeks holidays for all and even more days for service anniversaries.
  • Individual training: Internal training and financial support for meaningful external training and further education.
  • Personal responsibility & freedom: plenty of room for your ideas and to make a difference together with us.
