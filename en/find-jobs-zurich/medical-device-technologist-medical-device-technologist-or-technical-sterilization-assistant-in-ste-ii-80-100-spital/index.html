In our eight operating rooms, 7,600 procedures are performed annually in the following areas of expertise: visceral surgery, bariatrics, orthopaedics, traumatology, vascular surgery, gynecology, urology incl. Da Vinci, plastic reconstructive surgery, hand surgery and ENT. Our Central Sterile Goods Supply Department (ZSVA) and Central Equipment Processing Department (ZGA) is a modern service provider for all hospital departments. The task of the ZSVA includes the cleaning, disinfection, sterilization and provision of medical products. All instruments are checked and maintained for their functionality. Direct applications are preferred, external recruitment offices are not treated as a priority.

Here they engage

  • in the routine control of all devices / batch release in general
  • in the technical and professional preparation of the instruments and endoscopes to be sterilized
  • Work in the ZSVA, possibly ZGA (early, middle, late and weekend/holiday)
  • have completed training as medical device technologist/EFZ or completed specialist course as Technical sterilization assistant/II, advantageously Endo-Ste completed or ready to complete
  • have good PC knowledge and in-depth knowledge of MS Office applications and a batch documentation system (T-Doc, Euro SDS or Instacount)
  • Have a good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Reduced employee parking
  • Motorcycle parking, lockable bicycle parking
  • With the ZVV Bonus Pass / Z-Bonus Pass we offer an attractive public transport subscription for employees
