Full description

To strengthen our team at the Waid site, we are looking for a committed and motivated professional as of 1 January 2027 or by agreement.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for providing high-quality physiotherapy care to inpatients in the fields of internal medicine, neurology and intensive care medicine.
  • You are responsible for ensuring and continuously developing the quality of treatment in your specialist area.
  • You will provide practical training to ZHAW physiotherapy students with expertise and commitment.
  • You establish up-to-date, evidence-based specialist knowledge across sites within the team.
  • You represent physiotherapy professionally both within the organisation and externally.
  • You actively promote professional exchange and collaboration within the therapy services and interprofessionally.

Profile

  • Completed training as a qualified physiotherapist
  • In-depth specialist knowledge and several years of professional experience in internal medicine and intensive care medicine, ideally in an acute-care hospital
  • Relevant further training, particularly in pulmonology, cardiology, neurology and intensive care medicine
  • Willingness and ability to share knowledge and continuously develop your own expertise
  • Strong service orientation, high quality and cost awareness, as well as organizational skills
  • Willingness to work weekend and public holiday shifts, as well as assignments across locations

We offer

We offer you more than just a job! Our Stadtspital world is characterized by an open first-name culture, straightforward and personal interaction, and the necessary pinch of humor in everyday life. As a team comprising over 120 occupational groups, we work every day in many different ways for the well-being of our patients. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions and excellent social benefits provided by the City of Zurich.

About us

With over 36'000 inpatients and around 455'000 outpatients per year, Stadtspital Zürich ranks among the top 10 hospitals in Switzerland. As a modern central hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the full range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency care and intensive care medicine. Every day, our 4'500 employees dedicate themselves with great commitment and passion to providing excellent patient care.
