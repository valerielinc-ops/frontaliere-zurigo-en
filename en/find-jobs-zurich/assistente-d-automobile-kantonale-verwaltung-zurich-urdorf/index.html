Full description

The Cantonal Police and its approximately 4,000 employees assume responsibility for security in the canton. Our vehicle workshop in Urdorf is responsible for maintenance/repair work, 24-hour piquetting service and police-specific equipment of the service vehicles as well as trailers. For this demanding and responsible function, we are looking for a professionally competent, independent and committed automotive assistant 100% tasks

  • Carrying out cleaning and service work on the official vehicles of the Cantonal Police of Zurich (located in Zurich, Urdorf and Wädenswil/Neubüel)
  • Cleaning of machinery, tools and workshops
  • Transfer of official vehicles
  • Piquet service providers
  • Assistance in special missions (driving courses, etc.)
  • Participation in training and mentoring of the learner profile
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as an automotive assistant/in EBA
  • Several years of professional experience desired
  • Brand knowledge BMW, Mercedes-Benz and/or VW is an advantage
  • Good IT user knowledge (Word, Excel)
  • Reliable, accurate operation and high load capacity
  • Flexibility, independence, teamwork and confidentiality
  • Cat B (BE or C desired) driving licence
  • Correct reputation and offer
