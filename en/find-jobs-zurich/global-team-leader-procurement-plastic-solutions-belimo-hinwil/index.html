Full description

Introduction

Designed here You are not just shopping – You are actively shaping our global supply chain in the field of Plastic Solutions. With your team, you develop strategies, strengthen partnerships and bring ideas into real life.

Main responsibilities

  • You lead and develop the Plastic Solutions team and assume the technical and disciplinary responsibility for our operational and strategic buyers.
  • You will develop and implement a competitive, innovative and sustainable procurement strategy for plastic parts, assemblies and modules, ensuring cost, quality and delivery performance across all locations.
  • You are responsible for strategic supplier management – from negotiations and contract conclusions to evaluation and the continuous development of our international suppliers.
  • You lead and prioritize sourcing and cross-divisional assortment projects and bring them to a successful conclusion.
  • You take over the escalation management of critical supplier or quality issues and develop pragmatic and sustainable solutions.
  • You are responsible for controlling and reporting and work closely with the areas of innovation and quality to transform new ideas into efficient and sustainable supply chains.

Required expertise

  • You have a basic technical or commercial training and have ideally trained in mechanical engineering, business administration or purchasing (BSc, MAS supply chain, operations or similar).
  • You bring several years of experience in technical purchasing – ideally in an international environment.
  • You have in-depth know-how in plastic technologies, especially in injection molding and automation solutions.
  • You think strategically and entrepreneurially, act solution-oriented and keep an overview even in a dynamic environment
  • You convince through leadership, high social skills, negotiating skills and an open, communicative way.
  • You move You are safe in international and intercultural contexts and have very good knowledge of German and English
  • You work routinely with SAP S/4HANA, especially in the module MM, and bring a travel readiness of around 10%.

About Belimo

Belimo is the world market leader in the development, manufacture and sale of field devices for energy-efficient control of heating, ventilation and air conditioning systems. Flap drives, control valves, sensors and meters form our core business. Founded in 1975, the company has been listed on the Swiss Stock Exchange (SIX) since 1995.
