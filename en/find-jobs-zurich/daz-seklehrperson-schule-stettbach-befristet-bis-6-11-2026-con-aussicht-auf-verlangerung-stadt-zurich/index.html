Full description

We are looking for a DaZ teacher at secondary level immediately or by agreement (temporary until 06.11.2026 with the prospect of extension).

70 percent (19 weekly lessons, including 8 lessons in team teaching and 11 lessons in DaZ-building lessons in small groups)

Tasks

They teach German lessons and DaZ post-graduate lessons with students from different classes and work together with an experienced DaZ teacher.

Profile

  • Interested in working with young people from different cultures
  • Joy of working together in team teaching with different teachers
  • Flexibility, commitment
  • Flair for Languages
  • Teaching diploma for the Sek I
  • CAS Deutsch für Zweitsprache or willingness to complete the CAS DaZ in the next three years

We offer

Secondary school with an open and tolerant team spirit, competent co-management, stable team.

About us

The school district Schwamendingen comprises the entire district 12 of the city of Zurich. Schwamendingen is a lively, multicultural residential quarter. With public transport we are easily accessible via Stettbach station and Oerlikon with various tram and bus lines.

The Stettbach Day School is a QUIMS secondary school in Zurich-Schwamendingen. It manages two separate departments A and B and works with both the integrated promotion and the schoolhouse-internal learning and promotion center. The school Stettbach currently comprises sixteen classes, one of which is for the DaZ initial lessons and the lunch care.
