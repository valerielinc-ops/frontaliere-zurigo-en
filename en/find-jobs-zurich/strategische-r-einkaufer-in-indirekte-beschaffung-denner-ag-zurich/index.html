Full description

Behind the scenes in indirect procurement, ensuring our store runs smoothly? Welcome to our winning team. You'll negotiate smart, optimize costs, and make performance transparent. Ready to move with us? Denner. That's our store.

Responsibilities

  • As a business partner to the requisitioners in all procurement and negotiation matters, advise and accompany
  • Identify and realize optimization opportunities within self-led procurement initiatives
  • Develop and further develop strategies for the managed categories
  • Ensure professional supplier management and continuously optimize the supplier portfolio
  • Manage and monitor contracts
  • Manage stakeholders and work in cross-functional teams with various committees, departments, and specialists

Requirements

  • At least 5 years of work experience: in a related function
  • Completed degree or diploma: in business administration, supplemented by a procurement specialization
  • In-depth procurement experience in various categories, with a focus on procurements in the logistics and store fit-out sector (e.g., facilities and technical services)
  • In-depth understanding of strategic procurement
  • Developed negotiation skills
  • Confident and convincing presence in front of various groups
  • German (negotiation secure)
  • English (of advantage)

What we offer

  • Part of the Migros Group: Strong, detail-oriented brand under the Migros Group umbrella
  • Migros Bank: Guide your bank accounts with better interest rates and fees
  • Holidays: 6 weeks of holiday for all, and even more days off on service anniversaries
  • Mobile phone subscription: The choice is yours: with Galaxus Mobile, Wingo, or Sunrise from CHF 10.-, or even more comprehensive
  • Flexible work location / Home Office: One or two days a week working from home. Outside of IT, we're even more flexible.
