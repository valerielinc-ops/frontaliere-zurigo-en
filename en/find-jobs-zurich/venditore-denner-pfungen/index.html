Fresh products that deserve their name. Shelves filled. Area clean. Cashier is right. Customers satisfied. This is our store. And soon yours too. Now switch to the fresh winning team.

Mansioni

  • Depository and accounting
  • Accept fresh produce and of course all other goods, check and fill them on the shelves in your store

Requirements

  • Ideally first professional experience: in the retail trade
  • Completed basic or further training: in the retail trade is an advantage
  • Ready to work beyond “nine to five” – and enjoy free time when others work
  • Physically fit, some colis are up to 10 pounds heavy
