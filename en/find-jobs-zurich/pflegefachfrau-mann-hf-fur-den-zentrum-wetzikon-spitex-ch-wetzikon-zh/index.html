From visit to visit: customers, relatives and me. "Good work needs a good environment" We create a field of work in which our employees can motivate, self-determined and largely self-organized relationships. We look forward to sharing our knowledge and humor with you soon. We search immediately or by agreement. Nursing specialist HF for the Center Wetzikon They suit us if you...

  • have completed training as a qualified nurse HF/FH or equivalent training recognised in Switzerland
  • like to travel independently and at the same time be part of a strong treatment team
  • Competently accompany customers as caregivers or in case management even in demanding situations
  • work solution-oriented on the basis of the care process and take responsibility
  • network with family doctors, palliative care teams, hospitals, homes and other specialists
  • appreciate interprofessional cooperation and professional exchange
  • actively participate in case discussions and reflect joint work
  • electronically record the maintenance documentation directly with the customers
  • want to assume various roles – for example as a caregiver, day-to-day manager, role model or Spitex specialist
  • for customers and their relatives a reliable contact person
  • Work in a structured way, set priorities and organize your assignments responsibly
  • develop professionally and personally, share your knowledge and support colleagues
  • Be mindful of your own resources and reflect your boundaries
  • have a category B driving licence and are mobile with their own vehicle or business vehicle

What we offer you

  • modern work equipment such as smartphone and tablet
  • Electronic maintenance documentation with Perigon and uncomplicated exchange via Beekeeper
  • regular case meetings, professional exchange and joint team events
  • An open culture of error, feedback and learning
  • Targeted knowledge exchange and diverse development opportunities
  • a wide range of services with varied and demanding tasks
  • Opportunities for active participation, for example in specialist groups, projects, networking meetings or the personnel committee
  • a professional administration that relieves you and the treatment team in everyday work
