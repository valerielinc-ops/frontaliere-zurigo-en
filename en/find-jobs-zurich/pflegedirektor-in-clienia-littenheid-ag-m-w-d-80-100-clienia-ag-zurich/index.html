Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With more than 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric offer of Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in ambulatory, day-clinical and inpatient settings. The Group employs around 1800 people and achieved a turnover of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For our private clinic in Littenheid TG, near Wil SG, we are looking for an / an

Join us to shape the future of psychiatric care for our patients. As a member of the Executive Board, together with the Clinic Director and the Medical Director, you have a significant influence on the strategic and operational development of Privatklinik Clienia Littenheid AG.

Their tasks

  • They lead the care directorate consistently and bindingly, provide orientation and create a motivating and performance-oriented work culture.
  • You work in the clinical field in tandem with the Medical Director.
  • They promote competences, personal responsibility and interdisciplinary cooperation in which employees can engage and develop further.
  • They develop care qualitatively, economically and innovatively – with a high standard of treatment quality.
  • They manage human and financial resources effectively and ensure an efficient and sustainable organisation.
  • They are actively involved in the management, drive strategic projects and contribute to the long-term future of the clinic.

Your profile

  • Nursing vocational training and several years of leadership experience in a senior position, ideally in inpatient psychiatry and psychotherapy
  • Business management understanding, a corresponding further education such as MAS/MBA is a plus
  • Clear, consistent and appreciative leader with strong social skills
  • High quality standards, determination and strong implementation
  • Strategic thinking, innovation and entrepreneurship
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss healthcare system

Our offer

  • Design leeway: A responsible key position with the opportunity to actively shape the future of our clinic.
  • Strong management team: A committed and performance-oriented management and divisional management with short decision paths and an open, uncomplicated cooperation.
  • Exciting prospects: A dynamic, future-oriented hospital environment with challenging development projects.
  • Meaningfulness: They create something valuable every day – for our patients and our employees.
  • Attractive place of work: A clinic in beautiful, green surroundings.

Would you like to promote high-quality patient care, further develop the care directorate and make a difference together with us?

For further information, Dr. Fabienne Niederer, Clinic Director, +41 71 929 62 10, is at your disposal.
