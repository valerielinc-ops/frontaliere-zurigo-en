Full description

The Capability Unit Central Services is responsible for providing IT services and delivers a multitude of central services for the various strategic business fields of the Migros Group. We are looking for a committed personality to help shape the SAP Security area of the CU Central Services and the agile Release Train «Core Technologies».

Tasks

  • Conception, implementation, and operation of authorization concepts for S/4HANA incl. Fiori (collaboration/sub-project management)
  • Implementation and consulting for SAP security topics (SAP system security, Security Audit Log, authentication, compliance)
  • Contribution to compliance with agreed quality standards, co-shaping the SAP security architecture
  • Execution or support of security and audit reviews, remediation, and documentation
  • Close coordination with SAP Basis, module teams, IT security, IAM team, internal audit, and external partners

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience as an SAP Security Consultant in demanding IT environments and projects
  • Completed degree (University/ETH, FH, HF) in Computer Science, Business Informatics, Industrial Engineering with further training in the SAP and security environment
  • Experience with BTP Security, SAP IAS/IPS, SAP security architecture topics
  • Comprehensive SAP security knowledge within SAP system environments (ERP, S/4 Hana, Fiori)
  • Practical experience in Governance, Risk, and Compliance management as well as audit processes (e.g., ITGC)
  • High communication and presentation skills, as well as consulting qualities
  • Knowledge of agile working methods (preferably SAFe framework)
  • German (negotiation level), English (very good knowledge)

What we offer

  • Creative and decision-making freedom: Opportunity to independently shape and further develop the area of responsibility
  • Targeted support: Promotion of our employees with targeted personnel development measures
  • Leisure and cultural offers: As the main sponsor of races and events, Migros offers employees limited places at half price.
  • Occupational pension: Above-average benefits and three different savings plans suitable for individual life situations
  • Catering: Excellent catering at low prices in the staff restaurant
  • Modern infrastructure: Diverse work zones and modern co-working spaces with advanced equipment
