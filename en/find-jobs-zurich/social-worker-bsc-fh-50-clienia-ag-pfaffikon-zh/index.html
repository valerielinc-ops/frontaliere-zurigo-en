Full description

Social workers record, define and point out social problems and work closely with the respective treatment teams. The individually tailored therapy offer on the wards is specifically geared towards the reintegration of patients into the old or new circumstances. The focus is on networking with institutions in the age range, relatives and specialist departments as well as the search for suitable follow-up solutions.

  • For this demanding activity, we are looking for a specialist with a Bachelor in Social Work who can move professionally in the interdisciplinary team. They bring with them a high level of professional and personal competence in social-psychiatric work, resilience, flexibility, humor and a special interest in interprofessional teamwork. Experiences in working with older adults and corresponding networks are beneficial. In addition to an exciting and versatile work area, we offer you a good introduction on the stations and in the social service, as well as attractive employment conditions in a modern clinic and an experienced social service team. We support you generously in further education. Further information can be provided by Monika Filliger, Head of Social Service, Tel. +41 44 929 82 28 or Gabriela Thiébaud, Head of Care, Occupational Therapy and Social Service, tel. +41 44 929 82 50. We look forward to your application via application tool. The Clienia Group is the largest private hospital group in the field of psychiatry and psychotherapy in German-speaking Switzerland. At various locations, Clienia offers a diverse spectrum of psychiatric, psychotherapeutic and psychosomatic treatment methods for all age groups in the ambulatory, semi-stationary and inpatient setting up to psychiatric long-term care.

Clienia AG – the largest private hospital group for psychiatry and psychotherapy in German-speaking Switzerland. Locations: Privatklinik Bellevue (Pfäffikon ZH), Klinik Schlössli (Oetwil am See ZH), Tagesklinik Zürich, Tagesklinik Wetzikon.
