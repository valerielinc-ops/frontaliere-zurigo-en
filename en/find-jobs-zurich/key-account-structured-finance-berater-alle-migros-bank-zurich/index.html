Full description

With us you can live your passion for corporate customer business every day and become a trusted partner for all financial topics for your customers. We are looking for you: A personality that is best known in the company's customer business and also brings a lot of enthusiasm for the acquisition.

Mansioni

  • You bear the holistic responsibility for consulting, care and further development of medium-sized companies in the Zurich area
  • You advise medium-sized companies on commercial & complex real estate financing, analyze business figures and conduct the examination and approval of loan applications
  • In cooperation with our specialists in Leasing & Structured Finances, you can fully support our customers in financing decisions
  • With your communicative, customer-oriented personality, you establish our position as a corporate customer bank in Zurich and convince by your professional performance
  • Your opinion counts, both for your young, dynamic teammates and across all departments, to the management

Requisiti

  • Mind. 4-6 years: Corporate customer consulting
  • Completed degree (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): Business Administration
  • Your very good communication skills, your proven acquisition strength and your negotiation skills can be used with pleasure and ease in everyday life
  • Our corporate customers benefit from your deeper expertise on all financing needs and your multi-year consulting experience
  • You recognize complex relationships quickly and have the gift of directly networking them and presenting exciting solutions.
  • Your proactivity and implementation strength in collaboration with various internal and external stakeholders characterize you
  • German (sales-proof)

Cosa offriamo

  • Central workplace: optimally accessible, centrally located workplace
  • Flexible working time: annual working time for all and confidence working time for many
  • Migros Bank: Accounts, mortgages and financial services with preferential conditions
  • Social benefits: Optimum coverage of illness and accident «private»
  • Flat hierarchies: Permissible hierarchies and cross-level exchange
  • Flexible Workplace / Home Office: Individual Opportunities for Home Office
  • Fair wages: Strong total package of wages, social security and pensions
