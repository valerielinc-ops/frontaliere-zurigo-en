Full description

Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Correctional Services and Reintegration Pöschwies Correctional Facility Foremen/in Korberei 80 - 100% With around 1,400 employees, Corrections and Reintegration (JuWe) is the largest office within the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs and is responsible for the execution of sentences and measures as well as for the implementation of various types of detention. Our employees from around 40 professions support and promote the reintegration of people who have committed crimes and work effectively and progressively to ensure a safe and fair society. The Pöschwies Correctional Facility (JVA) is one of eight main departments of JuWe and the largest closed facility for men in Switzerland. With around 400 prison places and around 300 employees, it contributes to the protection of the general public in accordance with its legal mandate and at the same time creates the conditions for the rehabilitation of imprisoned people. We are looking for a resilient personality with very high social skills as of December 1, 2026 or by agreement. Together with your experienced professional colleagues, you will look after and support the imprisoned people in the basket and weaving workshop.

Your tasks

  • Professional guidance, support and support of a group of incarcerated people in their everyday employment
  • Responsibility for order and safety as well as helping to shape a structured everyday work routine
  • Planning and coordinating individual employment interventions, taking into account the abilities and resources of the incarcerated people
  • Writing reports and assessments on the development and behavior of incarcerated people
  • Commitment as an active member of the company fire department Your profile
  • Basic craft training (EFZ), for example as a carpenter, wood craftsman, saddler or upholsterer
  • Professional experience in a craft environment and interest in working with people
  • Team-oriented, flexible and resilient personality with a positive and appreciative attitude
  • Further training as a correctional specialist
  • Additional training in work psychology is an advantage
  • Good knowledge of spoken and written German as well as solid MS Office skills
  • Further language skills are a plus
  • Minimum age of 30 years and an impeccable reputation (no entries in the criminal or debt collection register) Our offer
  • A meaningful and varied job in a special and exciting working environment
  • Diverse internal and external training and further education opportunities
  • Meals available on site in the Pöschwies JVA restaurant
  • Regulated working hours in uniformed service operations
  • Attractive employment conditions in accordance with cantonal guidelines Your application Are you looking for a new challenge? Then become part of our team and apply directly via our online application tool. We look forward to seeing you.
