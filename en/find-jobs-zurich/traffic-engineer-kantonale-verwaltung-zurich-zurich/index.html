Full description

The Cantonal Police, with its approximately 4000 employees, is responsible for security in the canton. For our Traffic Police Special Division, Traffic Orders Unit, in Zurich, we are looking for a traffic engineer (80 - 100%). Your responsibilities:

  • Identifying hazardous locations in road traffic (BSM, RSI), conducting traffic engineering studies in accordance with VSS standards, and developing measures and concepts for the sustainable improvement of road safety
  • Reviewing traffic orders, traffic concepts and road construction projects with regard to legal compliance, road safety and transport planning requirements
  • Drafting well-founded statements, decisions, expert reports and concepts on traffic-related issues as well as political and legislative proposals
  • Representing the Traffic Police on expert committees and coordinating cooperation with cities, municipalities, federal and cantonal authorities, engineering firms and other partner organizations
  • Conducting traffic analyses, traffic counts and accident analyses, producing complex analyses in ASTRANA/VUGIS, and preparing and publishing geospatial information
  • Contributing to the further development of road safety, supporting road safety campaigns, participating in the induction and further training of employees, and taking on additional specialist tasks within the Traffic Police Main Division. Your profile:
  • Swiss bachelor's degree in traffic engineering, transportation engineering or a comparable field; alternatively, with a foreign bachelor's degree: several years of professional experience in Switzerland in the field of transportation engineering
  • Further training as a CAS road safety expert or willingness to complete it
  • Training and/or experience with ASTRANA, VUGIS or the Confederation's infrastructure safety instruments (ISSI) are an advantage
  • Very good German skills, both spoken and written, as well as good English skills
  • Proficiency in Microsoft 365; knowledge of ArcGIS and database applications is an advantage
  • Category B driving licence
  • Impeccable reputation You can look forward to:
  • We offer you a varied and meaningful role in a dynamic environment
  • Attractive employment conditions with a secure employer
  • The opportunity to make an impact in an exciting new challenge and shape the future together
  • An attractive workplace in a central location with good parking facilities Start date: 1.
