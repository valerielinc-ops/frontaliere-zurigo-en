Are you looking for an exciting role with a wide range of development opportunities and a lot of creative scope? As an employee in the Business & Management Support Team, you and your colleagues are responsible for all staff tasks in the area. You work together with internal and external bodies across departments and organizations and actively shape the strategic and operational orientation of the department. Your tasks

  • Support the divisional management in organization, planning, administration, controlling and further development of the subordinate teams
  • Coordinating and accompanying team meetings and workshops incl. Preparation and follow-up of decision bases and results
  • Development of concepts, proposals and statements for business initiatives as well as accompanying implementation

  • Carrying out risk-oriented controls and ensuring compliance with instructions and regulations
  • Create transparency about material risks and ensure that transactions are in line with the bank’s risk appetite
  • Maintain an active network with internal stakeholders (project teams, central sales teams, controlling, auditing, etc.) and mediate between different stakeholder groups
  • Represent your teammates in absence in all aspects of staff work

  • Excellent handling of Excel and PowerPoint, very good user knowledge in Outlook, also beneficial in SharePoint, Confluence and OneNote
