Full description

Create the data-driven future of Denner with us: For our DWH & BI team, we are looking for an experienced Microsoft Fabric Data Engineer who will actively drive the development of our new data warehouse. In close cooperation with the departments, you will develop modern data solutions and become part of a dynamic, motivated team with a lot of room for manoeuvre.

Mansioni

  • Build a new retail data warehouse based on a modern technology stack (Azure, Microsoft Fabric)
  • Actively shape and further develop the data architecture
  • Build the central data warehouse and work closely with cross-functional teams on solutions for data integration, reporting and analytics
  • Promote a data-driven culture and continuously develop processes, best practices and team skills
  • Develop analytical capabilities and gain new insights through a reliable, high-quality data base
  • Integrate forward-looking technologies and set the strategic direction to continuously improve and develop the data warehouse platform

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: in the area of DWH and BI based on Microsoft products
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): in the field of computer science or equivalent training
  • Experience in Data Warehouse (DWH) and Business Intelligence (BI), ideally with a strong focus on the retail industry
  • Experience in the design and design of data warehouse (DWH) and business intelligence (BI) solutions
  • Experience with DevOps best practices, including end-to-end responsibility for development and operations
  • Experience in process automation with a focus on scalability, reliability and operational efficiency in cloud environments
  • German (good knowledge)
  • English (flowing)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central place of work: In the casual Binz district in Zurich district 3 (2 minutes by foot from Zurich station Binz).
  • Flexible place of work / home office: One to two days a week working at home. Except in IT - we are even more flexible.
  • Fair wages: 13 monthly wages and fixed wage increases for all instead of bonuses for a few.
  • Holidays: 6 weeks holidays for all and even more days for service anniversaries.
  • Individual further education: internal training and financial support for meaningful external education and training.
  • Personal responsibility & freedom: plenty of room for your ideas and to make a difference together with us.
  • Design and decision-making leeway: Possibility to help shape Denner’s success story and implement your own ideas
