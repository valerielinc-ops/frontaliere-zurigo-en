Full description

For a renowned trust company that has been operating successfully on the market for eight decades and benefits from massive synergies in know-how transfer and digitalization embedded in a strong Swiss group of companies, we are looking for a motivated reinforcement as a junior clerk (80–100%) at the location in Zurich Enge. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for you!

This is your job

  • Finance: Independent management of customer accounting and active assistance in the preparation of interim and annual accounts.
  • Taxes & Payroll: Independent preparation of tax returns for natural and legal persons as well as the support of smaller wage mandates.
  • Support: Support of the mandate managers in professional customer support and cooperation in audits.

This is what you bring with you

  • Training: Completed basic commercial training, ideally already completed directly in the trust area.
  • Experience: First professional experience in trust or in finance and accounting.
  • Mindset: A strong service and customer orientation, a high quality standard and the willingness to continuously develop in the escrow sector.
  • Soft skills: You are open, flexible, resilient, think positively and act absolutely team-oriented.

This is what awaits you from the customer

  • Culture: An open corporate culture in a family environment with short lines of communication and experienced, qualified team members.
  • Flexibility: Modern workplaces with modern IT infrastructure as well as flexible models through home office and part-time opportunities.
  • Development: Plenty of room for manoeuvre in the daily business as well as active support in your external training and further education.
