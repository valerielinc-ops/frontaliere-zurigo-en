Growth Marketing Specialist, temporary, 60% — UBS

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Key responsibilities The Digital Growth & Intelligence team at UBS Switzerland is looking for talented professionals to help shape sustainable growth. This role is part of the Marketing, Design & Digital Sales Center of Excellence, which is responsible for all marketing & digital sales activities for UBS Switzerland and it’s a fixed-term position until 30 April 2027.

Your responsibilities

  • As a Growth Marketing Specialist, you will be part of the Growth & Content Orchestration team within Marketing & Digital Sales and support growth initiatives across different client segments, products and channels.
  • You will assess incoming growth opportunities, structure business requirements, gather relevant data and translate your findings into actionable concepts and recommendations.
  • You will help analyze and optimize end-to-end client journeys, communication touchpoints and digital conversion paths, identifying client pain points, process gaps and opportunities to improve the overall experience.
  • You will develop data-driven measures to support client acquisition, activation, engagement and retention, including lead generation, onboarding, cross-selling and reactivation approaches.
  • You will contribute to the development and optimization of digital and CRM communication, including automated journeys, landing pages, forms, email and other digital touchpoints, with a strong focus on relevance, personalization and conversion.
  • You will take ownership of selected growth initiatives from initial assessment and stakeholder alignment through concept development, implementation preparation and delivery, including end-to-end project management where required, while coordinating dependencies across business, product, sales and marketing teams.
  • You will monitor campaign and journey performance, consolidate insights from different data sources and communicate recommendations that support client experience, sales impact and operational efficiency.
  • You will actively share insights, methods and best practices, contribute to cross-functional working groups and help foster a collaborative, client-centric and experimental growth mindset across the organization.

Your Career Comeback We are open to applications from career returners. Find out more about our program on ubs.com/careercomeback .

The team You’ll be working in our Digital Growth & Intelligence team within Marketing, Design & Digital Sales in Zurich. We shape UBS’s client engagement by creating data-driven campaigns, optimizing digital channels and refining our online presence to deliver innovative, client-focused experiences. You’ll work with talented professionals as we help manage, preserve and grow the wealth of our clients. Digital Growth & Intelligence brings together disciplines such as Growth Marketing and E2E Orchestration, Content Marketing, Organic Social Media and On-site Steering. You will work at the intersection of business, SEO/GEO and CRO and gain insights into the operational setup of a large marketing organization.

Your skills and experience

  • 5+ years of experience in digital marketing, preferably in financial services or e-commerce
  • strong experience in client journey and conversion optimization, data and analytics, lead generation and digital sales
  • a good understanding of behavioral economics and its influence on client behavior and sales journeys
  • strong stakeholder and project management skills, with attention to detail and the ability to maintain an overview of wider dependencies and objectives
  • experience in applying user research, client feedback and data insights to optimize marketing activities and client journeys
  • comfortable working in interdisciplinary and cross-functional teams
  • a communicative, structured and proactive personality
  • ability to thrive in a highly dynamic environment and manage multiple initiatives and stakeholders
  • experience working in agile structures is an advantage
  • a native German speaker who is comfortable working in an international environment; additional languages are a plus
