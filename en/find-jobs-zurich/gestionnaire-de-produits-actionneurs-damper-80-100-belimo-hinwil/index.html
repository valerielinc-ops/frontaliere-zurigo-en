Full description

Introduction

As Global Product Manager Damper Actuators, you own the global upstream product lifecycle of a strategically important actuator portfolio. Working closely with regional product managers, innovation teams, sales organizations, and technical experts, you define portfolio strategy, shape product roadmaps, and ensure long-term business success. You combine market insight, customer understanding, and technical expertise to drive sustainable growth, competitiveness, and customer value across international markets.

Key Responsibilities

  • Define and continuously develop the global product and portfolio strategy in alignment with business objectives and market opportunities.
  • Translate customer, market, competitive, regulatory, and technology insights into product roadmaps and future growth initiatives.
  • Collaborate closely with regional product managers to align priorities and ensure global portfolio consistency.
  • Lead product management activities throughout product development and industrialization, acting as a key interface between business and innovation teams.
  • Validate product concepts and solutions through customer engagement, market feedback, and field testing activities.
  • Develop global value propositions, positioning strategies, and pricing approaches to maximize customer and business value.
  • Support successful product launches, lifecycle management activities, portfolio optimization, and phase-in/phase-out planning.
  • Monitor portfolio performance, market trends, and key business indicators to identify opportunities and drive continuous improvement.

Required Expertise

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in HVAC, Engineering, Mechatronics, Building Systems, Industrial Engineering, or a comparable technical discipline.
  • Several years of product management experience within HVAC, building automation, electromechanical products, or related industries.
  • Strong understanding of product lifecycle management, portfolio strategy, and new product development processes.
  • Ability to translate complex market and customer requirements into clear business cases and product requirements.
  • Data-driven and analytical mindset with a structured approach to problem-solving and decision-making.
  • Self-driven and adaptable personality with a strong sense of ownership, initiative, and accountability for achieving results.
  • Strong collaboration, stakeholder management, and influencing skills in international and cross-functional environments.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English and the ability to simplify complex topics for diverse audiences.
  • Passion for technology, willingness to travel internationally (approximately 10%), and strong alignment with Belimo’s collaborative culture.

About Belimo

Belimo is the global market leader in the development, production, and sales of field devices for the energy-efficient control of heating, ventilation and air-conditioning systems. The focus of our core business is on damper actuators, control valves, sensors and meters. Founded in 1975, the company has been listed on the Swiss Exchange (SIX) since 1995.
