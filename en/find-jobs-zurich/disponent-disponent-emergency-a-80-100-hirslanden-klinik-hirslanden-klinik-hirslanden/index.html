Location: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Occupation by 1 February 2027 or by arrangement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 74715

At the Hirslanden Clinic Zurich Emergency Center, you are an important point of contact for our patients. With your friendly, empathetic and service-oriented nature, you contribute to a positive patient experience. In close exchange with the shift management and the triage specialist, you make an important contribution to a smooth process at the reception.

YOUR TASKS

  • You welcome patients in a friendly and professional manner and ensure a smooth administrative reception.
  • You are a central point of contact for patients, relatives and internal and external contact persons.
  • You provide with your attentive and appreciative communication for a pleasant reception atmosphere.
  • You take on organizational and administrative tasks around the reception.
  • You are in regular exchange with shift management and triage specialists and support a smooth flow of information.

Your profile

  • You have completed training as Medical Practice Assistant (MPA) or as Medical and Hospital Secretary.
  • Professional experience as a consulting assistant in an acute hospital and/or in an emergency department is beneficial.
  • You know the basic differences between illness and accident cases as well as the corresponding insurance processes.
  • You communicate very well in German, both verbally and in writing, and have good oral English skills. Other foreign languages are beneficial.
  • Even in demanding situations, you perform professionally and calmly.
  • You have a good feeling for the needs of patients and contribute with your commitment to a high quality of service.

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. You work with a modern medical clinic information system and can expect extensive training and development opportunities from us. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family responsibilities. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. Employees who have to move completely for their work receive four additional paid days off with a 100% workload. With a reduced workload, the days off reduce accordingly. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from equivalent financial compensation. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.
