Full description

Tasks

  • Ensure the electrical operation of the entire hospital – from energy supply to lighting
  • Maintenance, repair, and troubleshooting of electrotechnical systems, devices, and installations
  • Support for new installations and electrical modifications in hospital operations
  • Collaboration on smaller projects, parades, and technical adjustments
  • Coordination and supervision of external electrical companies and specialized firms
  • Recording and maintenance of technical data and systems in the CAFM system Campos
  • Willingness to participate in on-call services in the rotating team
  • Requirements
  • Completed vocational training as an electrician EFZ or equivalent qualification
  • Several years of professional experience in maintenance or building technology, ideally in a hospital or industrial environment
  • Solid knowledge of applicable electrical regulations (e.g., NIN, NIV)
  • Good IT skills (MS Office); experience with CAFM systems like Campos is an advantage
  • Independent, solution-oriented, and reliable working style
  • High sense of responsibility, team spirit, and a service-oriented demeanor
  • Driving license category B
  • We offer
  • a diverse and responsible role in a meaningful environment
  • a collegial, motivated team with open communication
  • a modern infrastructure, regulated working hours, and attractive employment conditions
