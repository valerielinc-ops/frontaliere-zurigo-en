Full description

Personal – competent – close Welcome to the Hospital Uster, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As the central pillar of health care in the Oberland region of Zurich and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you experience the highest level of expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and promoted in your professional development. Spital Uster is a teaching hospital of the Medical Faculty of the University of Zurich. Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of a “regional and digital health hub in Züri East”! For our hospital-owned nursing staff pool we are looking for flexible and initiative immediately or by agreement

Exciting challenges await you

  • You care for and care for patients in sometimes complex situations and in an interdisciplinary treatment team
  • you are responsible for and control the care process of your patients entrusted to you and transfer tasks to FaGe, nursing aids and interns
  • you work closely with other professional groups involved in the treatment process and shape the appreciative team culture

What sets you apart for the job

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Care professional HF/FH
  • open-minded, capable of learning and responsible person with a professional way of working
  • Reliable/reliable and set up/standing jumper
  • Thinking and motivating you relieve the task force team
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • adept at dealing with computer / software (patient documentation KISIM)
  • Incorporation ideally up to 70%

8 reasons to work with us

  • fixed workload (40-70%) in monthly wages
  • Assignments we plan early according to your availability – varied everyday life is guaranteed
  • in addition to family or other earnings regular income and/or insight as well as continuity of your professional experience in acute care
  • at least five weeks holidays and joker days for changing time
  • Excellent staff restaurant and numerous discounts
  • Central location near Uster station and motorway connection
  • ZVV BonusPass or cheap parking, max. CHF 4.50 per day
  • free parking time during the night shift (until 7 a.m.)
