Would you like to develop further into a qualified nurse HF? With us you will find a living environment that attaches great importance to individual promotion and further development. The start of training is possible in autumn and spring.

(a)lle are addressed. Gender, origin, age? We are interested in your qualification and enthusiasm for work.

Why it is worthwhile for you to complete your education with us

  • Well-structured training with comprehensive introduction
  • Our motivated and well-qualified vocational trainees accompany you on the way to self-responsible and independent work
  • We bring people joy into their homes
  • Teamwork, communication and fun at work are for us the A and O
  • Attractive working hours
  • 5 weeks holiday
  • You can also develop after studying with us (fixed position, further study...)

You will learn this with us

  • Resource- and goal-oriented implementation of the varied and high-quality care and support of our customers at home
  • To accompany and advise relatives
  • Steer the care process by planning, implementing and evaluating it
  • Instruct, coach and advise employees of other levels of competence
  • Maintenance documentation to be conducted carefully and seamlessly
  • Competent medical, logistical and administrative services
  • Cooperation with employees, specialists and doctors

You bring with you

  • You have a lot of joy and fun working with people of all ages and different cultures
  • You have a high sense of responsibility, are independent and flexible
  • You are healthy and physically and psychologically resilient
  • You have a teaching degree level EFZ or a maturity degree
  • Your German skills in word and writing are good (minor level B2)
  • You can cycle and like to ride the e-bike in the fresh air
