Are you curious, motivated and looking for a new challenge? As a student expert / student expert NDS, you can expect an exciting environment in which you can continuously grow and develop. You will become part of a team of experts who will accompany you with comprehensive knowledge and support. Your field of activity covers a wide range of medical disciplines, including cardiac, neuro, thoracic, vascular, visceral and plastic surgery, orthopaedics, traumatology, obstetrics, gynecology, oto-rhino-laryngology, urology and ophthalmology. Our state-of-the-art hybrid operating room combines the tradition of conventional surgery with the latest techniques in interventional radiology.

Take the chance to expand your skills and gain new experience in a varied and challenging environment.

YOUR TASKS

  • Under professional, competent guidance and guidance, you will acquire and apply the necessary skills for anesthesia care
  • You are in constant interdisciplinary exchange with medical and nursing, medical specialists
  • Documentation of procedures and support of the team in carrying out anesthesia procedures
  • Operation and control of medical devices and monitoring systems

Your profile

  • You have a degree at tertiary level as a Dipl. Nursing specialist HF or Bachelor of Science in Nursing FH with at least six months of professional experience in acute care
  • Do you have a foreign diploma? Please provide us with the copy of the SRK order for diploma recognition (recognition procedure)
  • German is your daily communication language for words and writing (for foreign-language applicants: German certificate, TELC or GOETHE, minimum level C1, minimum grade 4.5)
  • You are distinguished by your empathetic nature and your great empathy, as well as your high social skills.
  • You are motivated to learn a high degree of technical expertise and thus make an important contribution to the success of our anesthesia team
