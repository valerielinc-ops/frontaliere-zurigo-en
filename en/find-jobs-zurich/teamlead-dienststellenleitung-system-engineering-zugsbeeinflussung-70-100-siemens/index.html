Full description

Create a better #TomorrowWithUs Want to help shape the future of the railway world together with us? At Siemens Mobility, we are the leading provider of mobility and are constantly expanding our innovative portfolio, which includes rail vehicles, rail automation, electrification solutions, complete rail systems and accompanying services. With around 800 specialist employees in Switzerland and 39 600 employees worldwide, we shape mobility with passion and are one step ahead of our time. In this key role, you skillfully connect technology, strategy and business development, driving innovation with real market impact. Your team provides tools and processes for efficient and economical project implementation. Become part of our management team and develop business, products and organization sustainably. With a 70% allowance, the working time is divided into four working days per week. Your new tasks

  • You take over the overall responsibility for the team (service) – technical, personnel and strategic – and develop it consistently
  • As part of the management team at department level, you will shape the future of the Train Influencing business unit and provide impetus for the strategic direction
  • You lead an interdisciplinary team of technology experts and software developers and develop it further
  • You recognize market trends, identify future customer needs and derive new solutions and offers from them
  • You actively influence product developments (e.g. ETCS, ZSI127, ZSL90, ATO) from a technical and economic perspective and work closely with product management
  • Together with your team, you initiate innovations such as new tools or simplified customer solutions in the engineering context
  • You are responsible for the further development of processes, methods and tools (including automation) for an efficient project implementation
  • Strong analytical and conceptual thinking skills
  • Entrepreneurial mindset as well as initiative and innovation
  • Strong, reflective leader who questions constructively and provides impulses
  • Completed technical studies (FH/ETH) or comparable, ideally supplemented by business education
  • Solid understanding of train control (e.g. ETCS/ERTMS, CSI systems)
  • Experience in managing interdisciplinary teams as well as project work in a technical environment
  • Affinity to Business Development and Economic Issues
  • Very good knowledge of German and English You benefit from
  • an open and flexible working culture at one of the leading companies in the field of mobility in Switzerland
  • a 40-hour week on a 100% fee
  • at least 5 weeks of holidays
  • a workplace at the pulse of railway technology
  • Various employee benefits and training opportunities
  • a hybrid working model: balanced work-life balance and more autonomy Our team and location You want information about our culture, our values, diversity and other important topics?
