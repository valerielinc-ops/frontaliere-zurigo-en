Students/e HF Operational Engineering

100%

Are you looking for a new challenge, have manual skills and enjoy working in a team? Then you are exactly right with us. We are looking for a student for the HF Operational Engineering program for the start in autumn 2025. Do you feel engaged and interested? Then we look forward to your application after the admission procedure at the Careum Education Center in Zurich.

  • Service and instrumentation according to the framework curriculum

  • Completed 3-year vocational apprenticeship or secondary school diploma and passed admission

  • Manual skill
  • Flexibility
  • Constituted personality

The Cantonal Hospital Frauenfeld introduces itself to you

The cantonal hospitals Frauenfeld & Münsterlingen are part of Spital Thurgau AG, a subsidiary of the thurmed Group. Our acute care hospitals serve around 32,000 inpatients every year and offer a comprehensive range of services in a high-quality infrastructure. Spital Thurgau is one of the largest and most successful hospital groups in Switzerland and has around 4,100 employees at various company locations. We offer attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!
