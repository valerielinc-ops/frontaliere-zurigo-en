Canton of Zurich Judicial execution and reintegration Head of the Department of Probation and Enforcement Services 80 - % With around 1400 employees, JuWe is the largest office within the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs and responsible for the execution of criminal and measures as well as for the execution of various types of detention.Our employees from around 40 professions support and promote the reintegration of people who have become criminally liable and are effective and progressive in promoting a secure and just society.

The probation and enforcement services (BVD) are one of eight departments of JuWe. They are responsible for regulating and controlling the execution of imprisonment and judicially ordered measures as well as for probation assistance and the implementation of learning programs. With around 180 employees, they make an important contribution to the prevention of offences and social integration. As part of the BVD, the area of enforcement 2 is responsible for the implementation and monitoring of outpatient and inpatient measures as well as replacement measures and instructional checks.

The focus is on individual support and ensuring compliance with requirements in different measures such as the "sixties".
