Full description

The National Driving Human Resources sector is responsible for the personnel issues of the entire SSR. Human Resources assist SSR collaborators and collaborators in all HR issues, provide them with a continuous training offer at the pace with times and are responsible for the complete implementation of the most modern key processes in HR.

Shipments

We look for, with immediate effect or to agree, a HR Partner 80-100%

Within the HR consulting team, in Operations, you will take care of the following topics:

  • It acts as a strategic interface between management and employees, taking on the overall responsibility of all HR processes in its field of competence and accompanying the Operations division in the current phase of transformation.
  • It supports and advises online managers in all matters relating to staff, in particular regarding recruitment, wage definition, conducting interviews with employees, professional development and career management, ensuring compliance with internal directives, legal provisions and company standards.
  • It provides qualified counselling on labour law, organizational and operational issues, assuming the leading role of reference for all issues related to working relationships.
  • It manages delicate cases (for example conflicts, absences or disciplinary situations) with great sensitivity and professionalism.
  • In close collaboration with managers, it contributes to the implementation of staff policy and the guarantee of HR standards.

Requirements

  • Empathic and solutions-oriented personality, able to actively accompany collaborators and managers in the processes of transformation.
  • Solid multi-year experience in HR consulting matured in complex and dynamic business contexts of comparable dimensions.
  • Wide and thorough knowledge of human resources, including future-oriented methodologies and developments.
  • Excellent communication and relational skills, with the ability to interact clearly, adapted to the different interlocutors and with security at all levels of the organization.
  • Availability to work regularly at the offices of Bern and Zurich.
  • Excellent knowledge of German and French, with minimum level B2 in French.

What we offer

  • A stimulating, dynamic and lively environment, with ample opportunities to actively contribute to the development of the organization.
  • An interesting, varied and challenging activity.
  • Available and collaborative colleagues, ready to share their professional experience.
  • A modern working environment with flexible hours and possibility to work partially in the home office.
