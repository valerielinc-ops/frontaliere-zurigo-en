Full description

In a nutshell

Are you fascinated by technology and would you like to expand your many years of technical know-how with us? As part of the team, you will help ensure that our technical systems for the Swiss Army are functional and ready for deployment.

  • Start date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: permanent
  • Reference no.: JRQ$540-19533
  • Work location: Ueberlandstrasse 17, 8340 Hinwil

This is what you can contribute

  • Carry out maintenance and servicing work on equipment and systems in the technical workshop area (power generation units, lighting systems, air-conditioning systems, system containers, rescue equipment, catering systems)
  • Handle specialist orders in SAP independently and on time
  • Provide customer-oriented, specialist advice and support to the troops when taking over and returning equipment, as well as during service
  • Ensure the operation of the metal-washing facility
  • As the person responsible, ensure the return of kitchen and catering materials
  • Assume responsibility for the maintenance work on the catering systems as well as the operational readiness of the metal-washing facility

What makes you unique

  • Completed vocational training as a motor equipment mechanic, automotive mechanic or agricultural machinery mechanic with EFZ, or in a related profession
  • Several years of technical professional experience
  • Independent, efficient working style with good organizational skills
  • Good grasp of technical contexts and analytical skills
  • Customer-oriented service mindset
  • Flexible personality with a willingness to work at various locations

What we offer

  • Working for Switzerland We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Diversity in action Thanks to equal opportunities, we can develop our skills and contribute different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees on physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom The Logistics Base of the Swiss Armed Forces (LBA) serves the troops. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss Armed Forces and, with its approximately 3'500 employees and 12'000 militia personnel, manages the material, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss Armed Forces. Whether in the workshop or in the office, indoors or outdoors: the LBA offers a wide range of jobs throughout Switzerland. There is something for everyone here.

The Swiss Armed Forces employ over 9'000 people in four military and around 200 civilian professions—and at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Armed Forces train apprentices in over 30 professions.

Additional information

A position with the Swiss Armed Forces makes sense and offers many advantages. See for yourself at armee.ch/vorteile. Offers from recruitment agencies will not be considered.

Women are still underrepresented in our administrative unit. Their applications are therefore particularly welcome.
