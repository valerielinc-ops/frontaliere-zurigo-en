Canton of Zurich Financial Directorate Tax Office Service Operation Manager/-in Integration & Data Services 100% Welcome to the fascinating world of taxes! Together with the municipal tax offices, the cantonal tax office collects taxes for the Confederation, the canton and the municipalities. We support individuals and companies throughout the tax collection process. Around 800 qualified employees are committed to this at the Altstetten location in Zurich. With your team, you ensure that our data services work smoothly and are continuously developed. Together we create the technological basis for a modern tax system. Your tasks

  • You build interfaces and services, develop them further and ensure their stable operation and monitoring.
  • You evaluate requirements for feasibility and complexity and coordinate them with specialist bodies, suppliers and partners.
  • You ensure compliance with standards and guidelines and promote their application.
  • You prepare management reports appropriately and precisely.
  • You lead sub-projects and are responsible for budget and delivery. Your profile
  • You have a university degree in business informatics or a comparable profile.
  • You bring several years of experience in operational service management and have established, operated, monitored and further developed IT interface services.
  • You have experience with data services as well as knowledge of ITIL and HERMES.
  • You communicate safely in German and bring good technical English skills.
  • With your networked thinking and solution-oriented action, you contribute to the further development of our services.
  • Readiness for occasional weekend assignments (e.g. release weekends) is a matter of course for you. Our offer
  • You have the opportunity to actively shape the future of digital administration.
  • With the Canton of Zurich as an employer, you benefit from attractive employment conditions such as flexible working hours, home office opportunities and lunch checks.
  • You can benefit from internal and external training opportunities.
  • Your modern workplace is located in the immediate vicinity of Altstetten station. Interested? Then apply now – we are happy to meet you. Please note that we only process applications that are received via our online tool. For further information, Daniel Boettcher (Head of Data Services) is available at the telephone number: +41 43 258 49 84. Your new environment: Working at the tax office For this position we do not consider applications about personnel service companies.

Pensum: 100%
