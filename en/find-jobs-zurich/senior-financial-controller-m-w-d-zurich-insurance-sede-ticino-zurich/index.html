Senior Financial Controller (m/w/d) — Zurich Insurance

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

137751 | 80 - 100% | Oerlikon, Zurich | Hybrid

Do you want to actively shape the financial management of our insurance business with sound financial analyses and innovative controlling solutions? Are you excited about understanding complex cost drivers, creating meaningful decision-making bases and continuously developing financial processes through digitalization and automation?

As a Senior Financial Controller, you will strengthen our Finance Team at Zurich Switzerland and take on a central role in the cost controlling of our insurance and pension solutions. You will analyze the cost development, be responsible for planning and forecasting processes and support our reporting teams and product managers as a competent Finance Business Partner. With your analytical view and your own initiative, you create transparency, identify optimization potential and make an important contribution to the efficient financial management of our business.

This is what awaits you

  • You will analyse and comment on the monthly and quarterly cost development of our insurance and pension products and prepare the results according to the addressee.
  • You are responsible for budgeting, medium-term financial planning and regular forecasting of segment and product costs, analyze deviations and derive concrete recommendations for action.
  • You act as a Finance Business Partner for reporting teams and product managers, questioning cost drivers and supporting sound financial decisions.
  • You will develop meaningful reports, dashboards and data-based analyses and drive the standardization and automation of our controlling processes.
  • You will actively participate in cross-sectoral projects and initiatives and bring innovative ideas to improve our financial systems, data quality and cost management.

This sets you apart

  • university or university degree in business administration, finance, controlling or a similar discipline
  • Further qualifications are beneficial
  • Several years of professional experience in financial controlling, financial planning & analysis or cost management, ideally in the insurance or financial services environment
  • Strong analytical skills, an understanding of complex financial contexts and the ability to translate numbers into understandable, decision-oriented recommendations
  • Excel knowledge, ideally experience with SAP, Power BI, Anaplan or comparable reporting and planning solutions as well as interest in automation and digital technologies
  • Initiative, structured and solution-oriented working methods and strong communication in German and English
