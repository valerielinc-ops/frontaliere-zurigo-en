Team leader Individual customer Zurich (m/f) 80-100 %

We are very much on Za(c)k. As a young subsidiary of the Basler Kantonalbank, we launched the first Swiss smartphone bank, Zak. This makes us a tick faster, smarter and perhaps even more cheeky than other banks. We also deal openly with the topic of money, in general we address topics that are important to us directly. More women power, for example. That is why we are particularly pleased about applications from women. Men? Of course, they are still welcome. Want to know more about who we are and how we tick? It is with pleasure.

The team of individual customers in Zurich is looking for reinforcement. In other words, you!

Your new job

  • Overall responsibility for the team of individual customers with four employees
  • Maintaining your own customer book (advice in the areas of numbers and savings, investing, financing and provision)
  • Organization and assurance of highly customer-oriented and holistic advice
  • Customers’ enthusiasm for our excellent products and services with a proactive market development
  • Establishment and maintenance of networks and active representation of our bank outside

We benefit from this

  • At least three years of professional experience as a customer consultant with her own customer book
  • Very good knowledge of mortgages and investments
  • Strong social skills and the ability to inspire and motivate people
  • Solution-oriented working method and pleasure in selling
  • Curiosity and openness towards new technologies and digitalisation measures

This is how you benefit

  • An open approach to the topic of money, also with regard to equal pay – that really exists with us
  • A positive team spirit
  • We do not confuse success orientation with elbows
  • A workplace in the middle of the city of Zurich
  • Do you expect an all-round good employment package from us? You are right, here are the details and many other advantages

Still questions?

It is a pleasure! When it comes to technical matters, Patrick Geyer, Head of Private Customers Switzerland, is looking forward to your call at 061 286 26 35. For all other questions about working for perhaps the coolest bank in Switzerland, Marijan Davidovic, HR Business Partner is at 044 218 63 01.

P.S. Because we are looking for a bank professional and not a copywriter, you can save yourself the motivation letter. Instead, we are happy if you answer three short questions in the application tool. Thank you.
