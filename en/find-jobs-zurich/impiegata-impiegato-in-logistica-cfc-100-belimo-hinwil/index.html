Full description

We stack up and build up. We are looking for reinforcements for three of our logistics teams. You keep an overview even at high speed and ensure with your experience that flows of goods from receipt to shipping run smoothly? If you like to tackle, take responsibility and make a difference in an international environment, you are at the right place with us.

In these shift models you keep the logistics running with us

Storage

  • 2 shifts between 06:00 and 23:00
  • 06:00 - 15:15
  • 14:00 - 23:00

Intralogistics

  • 3 shifts between 06:00 and 23:00
  • 06:00 - 15:30
  • 07:00 - 16:30

Dispatch

  • 06:30 - 15:40
  • 12:00 - 21:10

What will you do

  • Physical movement and transport of goods incl. Loading/unloading, storage and relocation
  • Safe use of industrial trucks
  • Checks on quantity and quality of goods
  • Order picking, set-up and provision of materials and customer orders
  • Correct detection of all material movements in the SAP-EWM system
  • Cooperation with adjacent logistics areas and support for inventories
  • Compliance with all safety and quality requirements as well as participation in process improvements

Who you are

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as a logistician:in EFZ
  • At least 4 years of professional experience in an international industrial, commercial or logistics company
  • Forklift driving licence
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Pronounced:r Teamplayer:in with an independent and exact working method
  • Good physical condition (good fitness)
  • Readiness for changing working hours between 6:00 and 23:00, see above
  • Knowledge of MS Office and SAP (MM/EWM)
  • Economic and solution-oriented thinking

Who we are

At Belimo, we are proud to make our company a rewarding place to work. Our leaders are value-driven and promote new ideas in their teams around the world. Through our passion, our employees not only create amazing products, but also revolutionize the HVAC industry. Investing in our employees is at the heart of Belimo when it comes to engaging diverse creative talents to build an industry-leading company. The success of our corporate brand is based on the competence and commitment of each individual.
