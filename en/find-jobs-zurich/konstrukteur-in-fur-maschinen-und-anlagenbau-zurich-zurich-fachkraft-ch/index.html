Full description

The focus is on the development of 3D designs and 2D drawings in a modern working environment. Collaboration with internal departments and suppliers enables a wide range of technical solutions. For our client we are looking for an activity

  • create 3D designs and 2D drawings for machines and plants
  • develop R&I schemes and dimension components and assemblies
  • plan and implement smaller development projects independently
  • Coordinate technical solutions with suppliers and internal specialist departments
  • Requirements
  • Completed training as a constructor or comparable
  • Several years of experience in machine or plant engineering
  • Very good 3D CAD knowledge, ideally with Autodesk Inventor
  • Good English language skills as well as exact and independent working methods Offer
  • Modern CAD, PDM and ERP systems
  • Varied projects with high personal responsibility
  • International environment and free parking spaces
