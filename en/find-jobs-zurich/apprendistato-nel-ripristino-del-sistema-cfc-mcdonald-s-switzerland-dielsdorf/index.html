Apprenticeship in CFC System Restoration

Our goal: to form the succession!

For our restaurant we are looking for an apprentice manager in the restoration of CFC system.

SWC learning is a 3-year training course for motivated people to acquire a global vision of the profession, to gradually develop responsibilities and to evolve in the long term in system restoration.

Profile

This training is for you if

  • You've finished your compulsory education.
  • You have a good command of French (oral and written)
  • You value teamwork and customer contact
  • You show reliability, organization and autonomy
  • You can keep an overview, even in rhythmic situations
  • You have a fast learning ability and a good analytical mind
  • You show an interest in processes, quality and performance
  • Order, cleanliness and respect for rules are important to you

Your future job

As an apprentice in the restoration of the CFC system, you will benefit from a complete and varied training, combining practice in the field and in-depth theoretical learning.

You will learn to

  • Prepare products and serve customers according to high quality standards
  • Organize the work and collaborate effectively within the team
  • Understand and apply restaurant work processes
  • Ensure quality assurance, hygiene and safety
  • Gradually assuming more responsibility for different positions

With the advancement of your training, you will also be initiated to:

  • Work planning and team organisation
  • Cost control and resource management
  • The basics of marketing and customer satisfaction
  • Principles of personnel management

Your training

SWC training lasts 3 years and includes

  • Practical training directly in a restaurant
  • Professional courses to deepen your theoretical knowledge
  • Business-to-business courses to link theory to practice

Your Federal Certificate of Capacity (FCC) is a solid foundation for your professional future at McDonalds or in the catering sector.

Your outlook for change

After your CFC, there are many possibilities for you

  • Direct entry into the labour market
  • Change to positions of responsibility (manager, management)
  • Internal or external continuing training
  • Development opportunities at restaurants or Head Office in Crissier

We offer

  • Recognized, demanding and rewarding training
  • A dynamic, structured and caring working environment
  • Professional support and personalized follow-up throughout your learning
  • Experienced and engaged trainers
  • Real long-term outlook
