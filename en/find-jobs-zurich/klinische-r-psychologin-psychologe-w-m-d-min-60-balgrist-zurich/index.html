Full description

Balgrist University Hospital is one of the most renowned orthopaedic clinics in Switzerland with international recognition. A center for paraplegia is attached.


Are you our new team member? Tasks: • Psychological-psychotherapeutic support of paraplegic patients and their relatives in the adaptation process • Crisis interventions and conducting couple and family talks • Psychological-psychiatric diagnosis and test psychology • Organization and mediation of psychological-psychiatric follow-up treatments • Supporting the interdisciplinary team in psychological-psychiatric issues • Documentation of psychotherapeutic disease course • Psychological advice for employees Requirements • Completed psychology studies, preferably with completed psychotherapy training in behavioral therapy • Professional experience of several years • Knowledge of foreign languages is an advantage, especially English, French and/or Italian • Structured working methods and networked, interdisciplinary thinking • High service orientation, social skills and assertiveness We offer: • Working in a prestigious university clinic with an excellent reputation • Diverse, demanding tasks in a highly specialized, interdisciplinary team with an appreciative and respectful working culture • Place of work in an attractive location in Zurich with very good connections to public transport • 20% discount in Balgrist pharmacies • Mind. 26 holiday days per year and an additional free day for your birthday • 50% discount on the ZVV bonus pass or an annual contribution to a general subscription • Enjoy a healthy and balanced lunch at discounted prices at our company-owned staff restaurant • Free coffee/hot drinks • Miscellaneous shopping incentives Further advantages are just one click away: Attractive offers for our employees
