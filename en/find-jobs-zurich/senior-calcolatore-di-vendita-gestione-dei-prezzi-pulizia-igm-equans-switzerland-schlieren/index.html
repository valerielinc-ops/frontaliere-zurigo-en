Full description

Create the future of professional cleaning with us As a (senior) calculator / pricing manager cleaning, you will continue to develop the Facility & Property Management division of Equans Switzerland on a national level. You are the central contact person for tenders in the field of cleaning and develop economic, innovative and customer-oriented solutions for national customers.

You can move that with us.

  • You control complex bid and tender processes from request to contract signing.
  • You analyze tender documents and coordinate the entire tender process with sales, operations and specialists: inside.
  • You will develop supply and pricing strategies for maintenance, specialty and infrastructural cleaning services.
  • You carry out total and partial calculations in the area of infrastructural building management (IGM).
  • You develop space, personnel and performance calculations for demanding customer mandates.
  • You create professional offer and customer presentations and accompany customer appointments.
  • You ensure the quality and timely submission of all offer documents.
  • You optimize processes, templates and digital tools and use modern AI solutions to increase efficiency.

That's what makes you.

  • Completed training or further education in facility management, building cleaning, business administration or a similar discipline.
  • Several years of experience in the calculation of cleaning and facility services.
  • In-depth knowledge of infrastructural building management (IGM), especially in the area of maintenance and special cleaning.
  • Experience with specifications, tenders and area calculations. Strong numerical understanding as well as a structured and analytical working method.
  • Very good MS Office skills, especially in Excel and PowerPoint.
  • Experience with CRM, CAFM and AI tools is beneficial.
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English. Knowledge of French is an advantage.

That's what we offer you.

  • A responsible and versatile function with a great deal of creative freedom.
  • Participation in national tenders and major projects.
  • Flexible working models and modern working conditions.
  • Diverse development and further education opportunities.
  • A dedicated and professional team.
  • Market-oriented remuneration and attractive ancillary services.

At Equans Switzerland, exciting challenges, short decision-making paths and the opportunity to actively shape the future of Facility & Property Management await you.
