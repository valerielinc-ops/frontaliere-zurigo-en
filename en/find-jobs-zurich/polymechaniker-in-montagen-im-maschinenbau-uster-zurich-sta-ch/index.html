Full description

In this task, you assemble customer-specific machines precisely and carefully. The company attaches great importance to quality and safety at the workplace. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Pre-assembly and final assembly of assemblies in mechanical engineering
  • Completely build customized flat and profile grinding machines
  • Precisely fit guides and machine parts
  • Connect hydraulic, pneumatic and valve systems
  • Checking machine geometry and recording measurement data
