Meier Maschinen, part of Serco Retail AG, is a leading supplier of agricultural and construction machinery as well as forestry and municipal technology in Eastern Switzerland. As a CLAAS Harvest Center, Meier Maschinen plays a central role as a CLAAS Service Partner in Eastern Switzerland. At the Marthalen site, we are looking for an agricultural machinery mechanic to strengthen our team with immediate effect or by appointment.

Corin Rebmann Head of HR Serco Suisse +41 58 434 07 06

These tasks inspire you

  • Carry out diagnostic, maintenance and repair work on agricultural machinery of various brands.
  • Carry out service and repair calls in the workshop and, if necessary, directly at the customer's site.
  • Preparing new machines and used machines for delivery.
  • Analyze and rectify mechanical, hydraulic and electrical faults.
  • Ensure high-quality work in compliance with safety regulations and deadlines.
  • Providing our customers with competent advice and offering first-class service.

We are looking forward to this profile

  • Completed training as an agricultural machinery mechanic EFZ, construction machinery mechanic EFZ or a comparable qualification.
  • Several years of professional experience in the maintenance and repair of agricultural machinery.
  • Independent, reliable and solution-oriented way of working.
  • Team spirit and enjoyment of customer contact.
  • Category B driving licence (category BE is an advantage).

Discover your advantages

  • Training and development: We invest in the development of our employees. A wide range of internal training courses is open to everyone, and we support external training.
  • Attractive vacation policy: Our vacation policy guarantees all employees at least 5 weeks of vacation. From 50. Year of age, it is 6 weeks and from the 60. Age even 7 weeks. In addition, the purchase of additional vacation days is possible.
