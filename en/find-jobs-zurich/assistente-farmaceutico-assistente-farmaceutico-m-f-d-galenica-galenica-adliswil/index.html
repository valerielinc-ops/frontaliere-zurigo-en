About us

The largest pharmacy network Amavita is a company in the Galenica network and, with the largest pharmacy network in Switzerland, forms an essential pillar of health care close to home. We stand for a wide range, ranging from prescription and over-the-counter medicines to care products to natural remedies and natural cosmetics. We are also experts in dermatological skin care. Amavita pharmacies are the most represented in Western and Northern Switzerland. They are usually located in attractive and busy locations.
