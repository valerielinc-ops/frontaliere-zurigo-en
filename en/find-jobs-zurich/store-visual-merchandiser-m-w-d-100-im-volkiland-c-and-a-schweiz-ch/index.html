Description

Since its founding by brothers Clemens and August in 1841, C&A has been a pioneer in fashion. From introducing ready-to-wear clothing, when made-to-measure garments were still the norm, to popularizing miniskirts in the 1960s, introducing the Com-Bi-Kini in the 1970s, organic cotton in the early 2000s, and the first Cradle-to-Cradle Gold-certified garment in 2017—we have always moved with the times and made stylish, high-quality clothing possible for everyone. And while C&A and the world around us have changed over the decades, one thing has remained the same: our commitment to offering sustainable, high-quality fashion for the whole family.

We envision a fashion industry for the future that is more innovative and sustainable; one that is moving toward a circular future and promotes a cycle of clothing use, reuse and rebirth.

In Switzerland, C&A has more than 90 stores and employs over 1'300 people.
