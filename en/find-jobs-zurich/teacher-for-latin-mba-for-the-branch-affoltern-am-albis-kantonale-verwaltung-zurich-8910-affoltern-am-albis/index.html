Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School Limmattal Teacher of Latin mbA for the branch Affoltern am Albis 50% The cantonal school Limmattal is a school of maturity of the Knonauer Amt and Limmattal regions and is located in Urdorf. It comprises a two-year secondary school (7th/8th school year) and the subsequent short high school (9th-12th school year). In the coming years, our school will be expanded by a branch in Affoltern am Albis. In the first year of operation 2027/28, some first classes of the gymnasial secondary school are taught at the Urdorf site; they move to Affoltern a. A. as soon as the new school building is ready for use (probably for the school year 2028/29). From school year 2029/30 onwards, new classes of high school are formed.

  • Tasks At the beginning of the school year 2027/28 we are looking for a teacher mbA for Latin, who teaches 2-3 classes, together with the construction team drives the development of a new school learning environment and participates in the development of the Fachschaft Latin. After completion of the project phase, the teaching burden can be expanded. You have completed university studies in the subject of teaching as well as the teaching diploma for maturity schools or an equivalent degree and bring teaching experience at a Swiss middle school. They are motivated to engage in the framework of the development project, class leadership and school development with pioneering spirit and innovative ideas. In addition, you are ready to get involved in the school community as well as in the area of managing special weeks. It offers you the unique opportunity to shape a new school from the ground up: a varied activity in an innovative environment! The employment takes place in accordance with the secondary and vocational school teacher ordinance of the canton of Zurich as a teacher with special
  • Tasks (mbA). For information, the project manager, Alexandre Nava, is at your disposal, reachable via e-mail at alexandre.nava@edu.zh.ch . Application Please submit your application with the usual documents no later than 15. October 2026 via bewerbungen.ksl@edu.zh.ch.

Pensum: 50%
