This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Do you love Glacé like we do? Do you have technical experience and would like to take over the production of our fine glacé (Crème d'Or or FUN Cornet)? Then join us in the enjoyment world of the Migros industry (Chocolat Frey, Café Royal, Crème d'Or).

Mansioni

  • You operate our Ice Cream systems from bottling to packaging.
  • You carry out regular quality controls of our products.
  • You take care of minor disturbances and involve the mechanics if necessary.
  • You are responsible for cleaning the facilities.
  • You help with packaging work and support the entire team on the line.

Requirements

  • 2-3 years of professional experience: in a production environment
  • No training required: as a food technologist, dairy technologist, baker, cook, etc. or a technical training (e.g. plant operator, plastics technologist, etc.)
  • German (good knowledge): B1 language level is mandatory
  • Readiness for shift work (3-4 shifts) and corresponding mobility
  • technical flair and a lot of self-motivation

Cosa of Friamo

  • Mobile subscriptions: Top prices on mobile subscriptions at Galaxus Mobile, Sunrise or Wingo – starting from CHF 10.- / month
  • Professional provision: Compared to the BVG, depending on age, 7% - 18% more is paid by Delica
  • Cumulus points: 2 times Cumulus points on food and 5 times on non-food items
  • Sports and fitness offers: Free use of the fitness studios integrated at the site in Buchs, Birsfelden and Meilen as well as CHF 200.- Refund on fitness annual subscriptions
  • Employee offers: Discount on various services at Medbase
