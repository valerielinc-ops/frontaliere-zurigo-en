Welcome to Medbase Zurich Wiedikon

Our diverse, lively medical center (not a walk-in practice) is located in urban district 3, a few minutes' walk from Wiedikon train station with a view of the Üetliberg. Here we care for patients in an interdisciplinary and quality-oriented manner. The team, consisting of around 40 employees, is made up of specialists and therapists from various specialist areas. The center's main focus is on the interaction between family medicine and complementary medicine. At Medbase Zurich Wiedikon we value the respectful and helpful interaction within the team. We also pay attention to a good work-life balance. Part-time work is possible in all functions and working hours can be arranged flexibly.

Tasks

As head of physiotherapy and complementary medicine, you are at the head of a department that forms the foundation of our company. With your commitment and your eye for quality, you will shape the further development of physiotherapy offerings and lead a team of committed therapists. Your work not only shapes everyday life, but also drives the interdisciplinary collaboration that characterizes Medbase.

  • Independent treatment and care of patients from musculoskeletal physiotherapy
  • Guiding patients in rehabilitation and prevention
  • Active exchange with other departments in an interdisciplinary team
  • Independent documentation and performance recording
  • Professional and personnel management of the physiotherapy team and complementary medicine
  • Support in quality development and compliance with standards

Requirements

  • Completed training as a physiotherapist HF/FH with SRK recognition
  • Structured and independent way of working
  • Experience or interest in a management position
  • Commitment to quality development and team development
  • Leadership skills and ability to work in a team
  • Good knowledge of German (at least B2 level)

We offer

  • A diverse and lively medical center in urban District 3
  • Interdisciplinary care of patients
  • Respectful and helpful interaction within the team
  • Balanced work-life balance
  • Flexible working hours on a part-time basis
