Put to the Point

With the revision of water protection legislation, non-relevant metabolites of plant protection products should also comply with the limit value of 0.1 μg/L in groundwater in the future. In this project on behalf of the Federal Office for the Environment, possible variants of an implementation of the legal regulations on non-relevant metabolites in the inflow regions of groundwater traps are to be investigated. This should result in practical specifications for the evaluation and control of PSM applications.

Good food, healthy environment

Agroscope is the federal competence centre for research in the agricultural and food industry. The researchers carry out their activities at various locations in Switzerland. The headquarters is Bern-Liebefeld (from 2026: Posieux FR). Agroscope is located at Eidg. Department of Economy, Education and Research WBF.

Additional information

The research group Plant Protection Products – Effect and Evaluation combines comprehensive knowledge of plant protection products with expertise on their behaviour and effects in the environment and their evaluation. In her projects, she combines experimental research with model approaches and data analyses and thus contributes to the interdisciplinary consideration of plant protection products in the context of agricultural use.
