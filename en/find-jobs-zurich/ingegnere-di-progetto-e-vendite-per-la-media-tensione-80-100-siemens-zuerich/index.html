Full description

Together with our customers, we connect the real and the digital world. The energy market depends on companies that can offer marketable solutions with comprehensive services and innovative ideas. We are professionals in this. Energy generation, transmission and distribution as well as management systems, automation and control technology are the main pillars of our business area. Protection systems for high and medium voltage systems secure our energy supply. Do you also want to actively participate in the pulse of time and make the lives of millions of people in Switzerland better? Then you are exactly right with us! Apply today as a project and sales engineer: in medium tension and start immediately or after your availability – your team is looking forward to you! Your new tasks

  • Responsibility for medium-voltage systems (concept definitions, project planning, elaboration of offers, prices and presentations of offers)
  • Project management with full cost, deadline and quality responsibility incl. Assembly and commissioning coordination with subsequent transfer to the clientele
  • Support and training of customers on site for our products and plants in the medium-voltage sector
  • Responsibility for customer acceptance of our plants and systems at the various Siemens plants
  • Active participation in customer events and trade fairs
  • Maintaining contact with internal and external bodies, especially with the customers of the energy supply companies (EVU), our parent company and our engineering offices Your talents and experience
  • Training as an electrical engineer: in FH/HF, ideally field of energy engineering or a comparable training
  • Experience in project management and/or sales in energy technology
  • Knowledge of distribution networks and/or power plants
  • Customer knowledge (EVUs) in the Swiss energy market is an advantage
  • Communicative and customer-oriented personality who wants to contribute and develop the technical know-how
  • Willingness to travel about 20%
  • Self-employed and organised working practices
  • Negotiating knowledge of German
  • English and French or Italian skills benefit your advantages
  • Varied places of work: In the office in Zurich, home office, with customers: insides throughout Switzerland or travel to our manufacturing plants
  • Discount public transport subscription and free parking
  • Diverse training opportunities
  • Access to employee stock plans
