Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School Büelrain IT-Supporter/in 60% The Cantonal School Büelrain Winterthur (www.kbw.ch) is a short high school with the profiles "Economy and Law" as well as "Philosophy, Pedagogy and Psychology". In addition to the secondary school, we run a commercial and computer science school. Do you want to work in a lively school environment with around 850 young people and are interested in computer science and digitalization in the education sector? Then you are exactly right at the Cantonal School Büelrain Winterthur. In order to strengthen our IT team, we are looking for an IT Supporter 60% as of January 1, 2027 or by agreement. Their tasks

  • Contact point for pupils, teachers and administration for technical issues and problems
  • 1st-level support for Windows, BYOD and Microsoft 365
  • Support for onboarding and offboarding processes
  • Creating and updating instructions and documentation
  • Management of badges for the electronic locking system
  • Support for printers, copiers and IT and AV infrastructure
  • Preparation and set-up of devices Your profile You have completed training as an ICT specialist EFZ, computer scientist or media specialist or have comparable practical experience. We require professional experience in IT support as well as in-depth knowledge of Windows and Microsoft 365. They enjoy working with people, explaining technical topics in an understandable way and addressing problems independently and in a solution-oriented manner. When dealing with students, teachers and administration, you are characterized by a pronounced service idea, patience and a friendly attitude. In addition, you bring very good German skills in word and writing. Our offer
  • A varied activity at the interface of IT and school operations
  • A small, collegial IT team with short distances
  • An employment according to the provisions of the Canton of Zurich with progressive social benefits
  • A modern, centrally located workplace, easily accessible by public transport
  • A longer-term career perspective Application Have we aroused your interest? For questions about the job content, please contact Dominic Kleiner (dominic.kleiner@kbw.ch, tel. 052 260 03 50) and Mélanie Frommherz (melanie.frommherz@kbw.ch, tel. 052 260 03 03) for questions about the employment conditions and the status of recruitment. We look forward to your complete application documents, which you can request by 8. Submit November 2026 via the button "Apply now".
