Location: Hirslanden Clinic in the Park | Zurich

Composition by: 01. December 2026 or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 73192

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

As Head of Finance, you take on a central role in the financial management and further development of the clinic in the park and, as a member of the management team, actively shape its strategic and operational orientation. With your business know-how, you ensure transparency, sound decision-making and sustainable financial development. Together with internal and external stakeholders, you actively shape processes and make an important contribution to the success of our clinic.

YOUR TASKS

  • Implementation and safeguarding of the financial and operational Group requirements within the clinic
  • Planning, control and continuous development of the financial and controlling processes incl. Budget, forecast, medium-term and investment planning and cash flow and balance sheet management
  • Responsibility for the timely and high-quality preparation of the monthly and annual accounts as well as meaningful financial and annual reports
  • Analysis of economic development, implementation of economic calculations and derivation of concrete measures to optimize sales, costs and results
  • Development and assessment of business plans for the establishment and expansion of doctors, specialist areas, centers and the entire clinic as well as participation in strategic and overarching projects
  • Ensuring the quality, completeness and timely provision of relevant cantonal and national data and reporting

Your profile

  • Completed higher education in accounting or business administration (at least professional card or FH)
  • Multi-year, versatile experience in finance and accounting, ideally with knowledge in investment accounting
  • Experience in the operational accounting of a hospital as well as in-depth knowledge of cost accounting according to REKOLE
  • Very good SAP and MS Office knowledge, especially Excel, as well as a strong affinity for information and communication systems
  • Independent, exact and reliable working methods with a high level of initiative, a strong sense of duty as well as very good communication and team skills

Are you ready to take responsibility and actively shape the future of the clinic in the park? With us you can contribute your expertise and entrepreneurial thinking in a modern and dynamic environment.

You can expect a motivated, committed and multi-professional team in an advanced working environment. This is characterized by high quality of care, independence, flexibility and a sense of responsibility. We also offer our employees generous social benefits, sports offers, special insurance conditions and further education opportunities as part of our program.
