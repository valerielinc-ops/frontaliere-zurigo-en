BMS Building Materials Suisse is a wholesaler of building materials for shell construction and interior construction. Our group of companies includes the renowned specialists Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced experts advise, support and inspire customers from the construction industry as well as private customers at around 110 locations throughout Switzerland. Close to everyday construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Quick, easy and competent. Looking for an offer professional! You juggle requests with ease, always keep track and have a sense of first-class customer service? Then you are exactly right in the Customer Care Team of the Richner brand! For our location in Buchs (AG), we are looking for a committed personality for the Central Region immediately or by agreement. With your competence and your organizational talent, you ensure that our customers in the sanitary industry are well advised and looked after. Sounds exciting? Then become part of our dynamic team and shape the future of the industry with us! Apply now and get started! Your main tasks include the creation and processing of offers and submissions in the area of bathroom renovation up to overbuildings, hospitals and hotel facilities. You are responsible for all telephone requests of our customers (mainly commercial customers from the sanitary industry) From this, you provide information, make clarifications and work closely with field service, key account, exhibition consultants and order processing. Further administrative tasks such as receiving customer inquiries, obtaining offers and tailor-made sketches from suppliers round off the field of activity. If your heart beats faster in the task area described above and you bring the following competencies, you are our desired candidate: You bring a completed training in the field of sanitary / building technology / interior design or a commercial training with a technical background resp. Great affinity towards interior design We require good IT knowledge and an experienced handling of the common MS-Office programs Networked thinking, customer orientation and high quality awareness enhance your profile Your advantages are detailed and team-oriented working methods Very good knowledge of German in word and writing is required a challenging, versatile and interesting job in a good working environment in which active cooperation and self-employment are emphasised Intensive training is a program, whereby our new colleagues can benefit from product and system training within the company Support our employees in their professional and personal development Home office possible according to regulations and agreement 5 weeks holiday (6 weeks from 50 years of age) and holiday shopping up to max. 10 days, additional benefits such as an excellently financed independent pension fund and discounts on our complete product range as well as external premium benefits We look forward to your complete application documents in electronic form.
