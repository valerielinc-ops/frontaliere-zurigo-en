Full description

Your heart beats for older people? Is the nursing profession your calling? Do you see opportunities to contribute your skills and experience to us in a meaningful way in order to have a positive impact on the lives of our customers? Then you fit perfectly with us!

For the Tertianum Zur Heimat in the Zurich region we are looking for an

Dipl. Nursing specialist HF/FH/DNII as RAI LCTF responsible 40-50 %

Their tasks

  • Among other things, they are responsible for ensuring the correct implementation of the RAI system in accordance with legal and contractual principles.
  • They monitor the RAI-LTCF-supported care process in our institution and check it on a random basis
  • They coach and guide all persons involved in the care process with the RAI-LTCF as appropriate to the addressees
  • They introduce new employees to the RAI system and accompany them in the practical application in everyday care
  • They carry out in-house RAI-relevant training
  • They check the correctness and traceability of the MDS codes relevant to the care burden group on the basis of the care documentation
  • They participate decisively in quality management in the interpretation of RAI quality indicators

Your profile

  • Completed training for/for dipl. Nurse HF/FH/DNII or equivalent training recognised by the SRC
  • Several years of professional experience in long-term care
  • Completion of the RAI LTCF Expert Course of Advantage (min. supervisor training)
  • Understanding care financing and benefit billing
  • Enthusiasm for coaching, guiding and accompanying employees
  • Efficient, solution- and process-oriented workflow
  • An open, appreciative attitude and enjoyment of working in an interdisciplinary team
