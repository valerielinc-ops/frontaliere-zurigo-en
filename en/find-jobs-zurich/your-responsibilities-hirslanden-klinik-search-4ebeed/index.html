Quality, freshness and team spirit are the focus of our kitchen at Hirslanden Clinic. We are looking for a committed personality with passion for cooking, who takes responsibility, who likes to pass on their knowledge and who wants to create creative dishes with high-quality products.

Become part of our team and bring your ideas with you!

YOUR TASKS

  • Self-employed management and organisation of a post
  • Compliance and implementation of our hygiene guidelines and quality standards
  • Leadership of employees and support in the training of our learners
  • Help in the design and development of new dishes and recipes
  • Ordering fresh goods
  • Irregular working hours as well as evening and weekend services are part of this activity.

YOUR PROFILE

  • Completed vocational training as a cook (EFZ or equivalent)
  • Experience as a chef in the high-class gastronomy
  • Interest and motivation in the training of learners
  • Good German skills

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multiprof-unemployed team awaits you. You work with a modern medical information system and can expect extensive further education and development opportunities from us. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family tasks. We reward regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits.

Employees who have to move completely for their work receive four additional days off at a 100% work-pensum. In the case of a reduced work-pensum, the free days are reduced accordingly. Employees employed on hourly wages benefit from an equivalent financial compensation. These and many more are reasons why Hirslanden Clinic is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss health care sector.
