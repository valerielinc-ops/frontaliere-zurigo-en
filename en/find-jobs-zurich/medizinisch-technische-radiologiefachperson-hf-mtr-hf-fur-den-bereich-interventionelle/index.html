Full description

In the clinic for radiology and nuclear medicine, more than 105,000 examinations and interventions are carried out annually. The Clinic of Radiology and Nuclear Medicine ensures the highest level of expertise and quality of care in all its areas: X-ray and fluoroscopy diagnostics, breast imaging, ultrasound, nuclear medicine, magnetic resonance imaging, computed tomography, pediatric radiology, neuroradiology and interventional radiology. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a dedicated and qualified medical-technical radiology specialist HF (MTR HF) for interventional radiology.

Tasks

  • Assistance in diagnostic and therapeutic interventional radiological procedures in compliance with the radiation protection guidelines
  • Preparation, care and monitoring of patients before, during and after the interventions
  • Operation and quality control of modern imaging equipment (angiography equipment, CT)
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation with doctors, nurses and other specialist areas
  • Participation in the further development of work processes and quality assurance

Profile

  • Completed training as MTR HF or equivalent training recognised in Switzerland
  • Ideally experience in interventional radiology or a high willingness to familiarize
  • Responsible, independent and precise working methods
  • High social skills, teamwork and resilience
  • Flexibility for piketting or standby services
