Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Vocational Advice Professional, Study and Career Consultant/in 70 - 90% The Office for Youth and Vocational Counselling (AJB) is part of the Education Directorate and is responsible for child and youth welfare, career counselling and scholarship granting. It provides the various consulting and support services centrally and decentrally at various locations in the Canton of Zurich. The biz Winterthur is one of 7 biz in the canton of Zurich and responsible for the city of Winterthur and the districts of Winterthur and Andelfingen. Through a wide range of offers, the biz Winterthur supports both young people and adults in their entire education, career and career. This takes place in the form of individual consultations, diagnostic clarifications, information events, seminars and the use of the information center. To complement our team, we are looking for a sociable, humorous and service-oriented personality who is committed to our customers and our company. As of October 1, 2026, we are looking for a professional, study and career consultant with a time limit of 70 – 90% until the end of 2027 (with the option of extension). Main tasks:

  • Advice for young people and their parents in connection with the first career choice
  • Cooperation with schools (class and parent orientations, school house consultations and other school-related activities)
  • Advice for adults
  • Infothek service
  • Organisation and conduct of events Requirements:
  • College degree in psychology or related field of study
  • Completed or ongoing training in vocational, study and career guidance
  • Work experience in working with adolescents and adults Are you a reflected, flexible and open personality? Do you like to take on responsibility and are you used to working independently? You can expect very versatile and interesting tasks in an active team and lively environment. We offer a careful introduction and employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines. The place of work is just a few minutes' walk from Winterthur Train Station. For further questions, Erika Deiss, head of department biz Winterthur, tel. 043 259 82 45 is at your disposal. The interviews are expected to take place between 1 and 3 September 2026. Information about the biz Winterthur can be found at www.bizwinterthur.zh.ch. We look forward to your online application.

Pensum: 70-90 %
