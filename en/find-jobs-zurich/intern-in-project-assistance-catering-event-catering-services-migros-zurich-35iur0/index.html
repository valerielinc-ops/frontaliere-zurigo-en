Full description

The catering services of the Migros Zurich create exclusive events – from galas in the circus tent to unforgettable anniversary celebrations. We offer dedicated students of hotel specialist schools the opportunity to complete their internship in a dynamic environment and to actively experience and shape the exciting world of event and catering management.

Mansioni

  • Insight into event and catering project planning: You support the planning and organization of catering events and help with logistics coordination (menu planning, equipment, transport).
  • You are involved in the construction, care and dismantling of catering events and support team coordination on site.
  • You can help you plan your offer and schedule, personal planning and visit event locations. You will also create checklists and schedules.
  • You organize the warehouse management like dishes, cooling cells and equipment and pay attention to compliance with hygiene and quality standards.
  • Pre- and post-calculation: You support cost planning and analyze the cost-effectiveness of projects.

Requisiti

  • You want to complete your final internship: in the field of events
  • Higher Fachschule: Student*in a recognized hotel-school
  • Self-employed, flexible and forward-looking
  • Open, enthusiastic and dynamic personality with a high level of service
  • Interest in the collection of experiences in the field of the exciting event industry as well as leadership and operational work
  • Joy in working together a colorfully mixed, motivated team
  • Good MS Office knowledge
  • German (flowing)
  • English (good knowledge)

Cosa offriamo

  • Occupational pension: Excellent pension funds with high employer participation
  • Social benefits: Fair social benefits
  • Cumulus points: You get additional cumulus points when shopping
  • Migros Bank: preferential interest in private and investment account, free account management, discounted mortgages and more
  • Leisure and cultural activities: You benefit from discounted tickets at selected cultural events
  • Sports and fitness offers: You can enjoy a leisure bonus of max. CHF 1000.– e.g. for an annual subscription for the M-Fitnesscenter
  • Targeted promotion: GMZ Young Talents Community: You participate in exciting projects of our community and participate in events that will take you on
  • Social and personal advice: Internal social counselling
