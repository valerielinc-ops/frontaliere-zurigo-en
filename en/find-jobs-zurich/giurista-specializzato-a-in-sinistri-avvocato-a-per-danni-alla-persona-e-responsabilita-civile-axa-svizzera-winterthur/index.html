As a claims lawyer: at AXA you will be part of a highly motivated and ambitious team. In our department in the Zurich team (with work location Winterthur) you deal with complex liability claims from personal injury from a large part of Switzerland. Your focus is on the legally demanding case handling, but also training and project work as well as the processing of damage cases with an international reference are part of the exciting and versatile task area of this cadre position.

In the area of customer operations, we design the individual experiences of our customers in the event of damage and performance. They are our focus and together with them we find the right solution. In this way, our customer-first mentality ensures maximum customer satisfaction.

This is what awaits you

  • You will handle complex and demanding claims cases in all liability sectors (e.g. motor vehicle, operational, professional and private liability) with a clear focus on personal injury.
  • Among other things, you take responsibility for cases in the area of motor vehicle and construction accidents, medical and hospital liability cases, state liability disputes and complex contract chain cases and bring them to a solution.
  • You clarify well-founded and solution-oriented legal questions of various kinds (e.g. coverage and liability issues, determination of the damage quantitative).
  • You conduct negotiations with victims, their lawyers and with private and social insurers involved.
  • You instruct external lawyers in litigation cases and accompany them professionally as a competent contact person.
  • You are open to using new technologies and AI-based applications to support and improve claims management processes.
  • You participate in projects and conduct internal training to share your knowledge in the team.

You bring that with you

  • A law degree at a Swiss university is required, ideally supplemented by a law patent.
  • You bring several years of professional experience in the legal profession, in court or in the private sector, preferably in civil liability and social security law.
  • You have a passion for handling complex personal injury cases – the more demanding, the better.
  • You are familiar with liability, social security and coordination law as well as personal injury calculation (including using appropriate tools such as ‘Leonardo’).
  • Negotiation skills, assertiveness and clear, convincing and solution-oriented communication distinguish you.
  • You bring a high degree of empathy and common sense to find the right solutions for everyone involved.
  • Stylistic German in word and writing is a matter of course, knowledge of another national language is a big plus.
