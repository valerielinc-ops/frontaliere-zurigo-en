Full description

The company is based in the canton of Glarus. Founded in 1962, the family-run company employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries, and is now increasingly active in the group. The company's headquarters are located in the canton of Glarus. In front of the picturesque alpine scenery, we produce our fresh chocolates and develop new, innovative ideas in the teams of the company. The "Retail" division comprises over 250 chocolateries in over 30 countries.

Here we offer our chocolate specialities and invite customers to experience fresh chocolate again and again. Do you want to participate in this? Then come to us.

That's what awaits you

  • You advise our customer-oriented and sell our diverse range of goods, for example in the selection of gifts
  • You actively engage our customers, recognize their needs and offer suitable solutions
  • You take care of the cashing operations and ensure a precise and correct settlement
  • You present the exquisite confiserie and chocolate assortment in a tasteful and inviting way
  • You also take on manufacturing activities and the filling and maintenance of the sales space
  • You implement our high quality standards with dedication and care
  • You consistently pay attention to compliance with the hygiene and freshness concept
  • You work hand in hand with your team and contribute to a positive working environment

You bring this with you

  • You bring professional experience in retail and are enthusiastic about sales and direct customer contact
  • You quickly recognize customer needs and advise friendly, individual and convincing
  • You have very good knowledge of German and English
  • French and other languages are of advantage
  • You work responsibly, reliably and quality-oriented and consistently adhere to hygiene and cleanliness standards
  • You are a team player, are happy to take responsibility and want to develop in sales and service
  • You are flexible and ready for weekend and public holidays (fixed days: Tuesday, Friday, Saturday)

We'll give you that

  • An exciting working environment with motivated colleagues
  • A position with a lot of personal responsibility
  • The possibility of bringing fresh ideas
  • An open and respectful feedback culture
  • Generous discounts on our products
  • Chocolate at the workplace
  • Good accessibility of the sites

Sounds interesting? Apply with us. Jasmin Anderegg, branch manager, +41 44 830 55 24 is at your disposal if you have any questions.
