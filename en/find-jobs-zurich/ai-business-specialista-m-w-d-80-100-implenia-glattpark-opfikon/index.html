Full description

Are you looking for a new challenge in the IT environment, are you enthusiastic about the possibilities of AI and would like to use it where it creates real added value? You are an innovative personality, think entrepreneurially, recognize potential for optimization and implement it pragmatically together with the business? Together with the departments, you will drive the digital transformation of the Group and develop innovative solutions with measurable benefits. Then we are looking for you for our newly created position as AI Business Specialist (m/f/d) 80-100%.

Your place of work is our headquarters in the Ambassador House in Opfikon, with the possibility to work partly in the home office.

Tasks

What tasks await you as AI Business Specialist (m/f/d), Opfikon, 80-100%? • In this role, you will analyze existing business processes together with the departments and identify concrete optimization and efficiency potentials. • Based on this, you will examine how modern technologies such as AI and automation can meaningfully improve existing processes and evaluate possible solutions according to their business benefits. • You design and design optimized business processes, implement suitable solutions independently in short projects and ensure that this results in a measurable added value and return on investment (ROI) for the business. • You work hands-on with common AI and automation solutions such as Copilot, Claude, Power Automate or comparable platforms. • As a sparring partner of the departments, you advise internal stakeholders on the analysis and further development of their processes and support them in the targeted use of suitable technological solutions. • You empower teams through training, workshops and coaching to integrate the developed solutions sustainably and independently into their everyday work. • You bring new ideas, technologies and ways of working into the company and promote their meaningful and practical use within the organization. • As a connecting interface between business and technology, you actively contribute to further developing business processes and driving digital transformation in the company.

Requirements

What requirements do you bring as AI Business Specialist (m/f/d), Opfikon, 80-100%? • You have a suitable professional background, for example by training or studying in business informatics, business administration, digital business, computer science or a comparable field, supplemented by relevant professional experience. • You bring in-depth experience in the analysis, optimization and redesign of business processes and understand how to capture complex processes in a structured manner and identify potential for improvement. • Ideally, you have experience in process optimization, business analysis, management consulting or a comparable function and are used to working closely with different specialist areas. • You are interested in AI, automation and digital innovations and follow current developments with curiosity, openness and enthusiasm. • You will bring practical experience in dealing with common AI and automation solutions such as Copilot, Claude, Power Automate or similar tools. • You think networked and entrepreneurial, recognize opportunities and understand how to translate potential for improvement into concrete solutions with a comprehensible business added value. • You act in an advisory and convincing manner, communicate safely at different levels and can inspire different stakeholders for new processes and technologies. • You work pragmatically, independently and solution-oriented and enjoy working together with the specialist areas to achieve visible and measurable results. • You have very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
