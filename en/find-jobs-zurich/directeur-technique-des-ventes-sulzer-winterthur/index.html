About the role

Purpose of the job The role is responsible for driving sustainable Order Intake growth for Sulzer Chemtech’s Mass Transfer Components (MTC) business through: · Active hunting of brownfield revamp opportunities from existing operating assets · Early involvement and development of greenfield / grassroot CAPEX projects, from feasibility to FID · Providing front-end technical sales leadership, translating customer process challenges into differentiated, value-based MTC solutions Main tasks and responsibilities Revamp & Brownfield

  • Proactively identify revamp, debottlenecking, capacity expansion, separation efficiency improvement, and reliability upgrade opportunities in existing operating plants
  • Ability to conduct asset‑level analysis (age of internals, operating issues, performance gaps, fouling, corrosion, capacity creep, energy efficiency requirements)
  • Drive revamp‑led sales strategies, including Tray replacement, Packing retrofits (random ↔ structured), Mist eliminator and separator upgrades, Hydraulic rating changes and column internals optimization
  • Position Sulzer as a lifecycle partner, not just an equipment supplier Greenfield/Grassroot Project Development
  • Actively pursue new CAPEX grassroot projects
  • Engage customers, licensors, consultants & EPCs early (feasibility ,pre-FEED,FEED)
  • Coordinate with application & technology, global applications teams in accordance to Sulzer project governance to lock in Sulzer MTC solutions early.

Technical & Application Leadership Translate customer challenges into value-based technical proposals that differentiate MTC from competitors with Application & Technology team.

  • Order Intake & Sales Achievement Deliver annual Order Intake targets, ensuring both revenue and expected margin objectives are met.
  • Strategic Planning & Execution Implement long-term and short-term business strategies and ensure alignment with regional and global MTCS initiatives.
  • Proposal Follow-up & CRM Management Proactively follow up on outstanding proposals and maintain accurate, up-to-date information in the Sulzer Customer Relationship Management system.
  • Cross-Functional Coordination Collaborate closely with Head Office, Regional Offices, and local intermediaries to support project acquisition, proposal development, and market initiatives.
  • Market Intelligence & Reporting Provide regular updates on market trends, competitive landscape, project pipeline, and sales forecasts to support strategic decision making. Lead country-level market mapping, demand analysis, competitive intelligence, and customer segmentation
  • Compliance & Governance Ensure all commercial activities comply with local legal requirements, industry regulations, and company policies and procedures.
  • Other Duties Perform any additional business related tasks assigned by the superior to support business objectives.
