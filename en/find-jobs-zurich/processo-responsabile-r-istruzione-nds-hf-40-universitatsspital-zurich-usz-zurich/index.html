Full description

USZ Campus

1 January 2027 or by agreement

Specialised nursing education think ahead – and help shape their next development phase. At USZ, we train experts in anesthesia care, intensive care adults, intensive care pediatrics and emergency care. The individual programmes are led by experienced educational managers. Its task is to take the overarching perspective, advance common issues and help shape developments for the USZ. Particularly exciting: With the transfer of today’s NDS HF to higher specialist examinations, specialized nursing education is facing a fundamental change. They work on the foundations for the new educational landscape and actively shape its implementation at the USZ.

Your role with us

They connect the four specialised programmes and create the conditions for joint developments to progress.

  • They lead the specialist group NDS HF and shape the exchange of those responsible for education.
  • They address higher-level pedagogical and conceptual issues and develop solutions together with the participants.
  • They use findings from evaluations and quality development to set new impulses.
  • They develop common structures and interfaces of specialized nursing education.
  • They bring the perspective of the USZ into the cooperation with educational institutions, specialist committees and other partners.
  • They are actively working on the foundations for the transfer of NDS HF to the higher technical examinations and shape the resulting structures and processes at the USZ.

What you bring

  • MSc Vocational Training or comparable qualification
  • Completed NDS-HF degree programme
  • Sound knowledge of specialized nursing education and the Swiss education system
  • Experience in the development and quality assurance of educational offers
  • Conceptual strength and the ability to bring different perspectives together
  • Own initiative and joy in promoting change together with others

What to expect

An independent function with experienced educational managers, different specialist perspectives and a broad internal and external network. They enter at a time when the proven has to be continued and at the same time new things have to be developed. This gives you the opportunity to shape the future framework conditions of specialized nursing education at the USZ. For this post, dossiers from recruiters cannot be considered.
