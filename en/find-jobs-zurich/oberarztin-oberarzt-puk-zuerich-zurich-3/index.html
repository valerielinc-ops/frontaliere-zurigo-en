Role overview

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich provides psychiatric and psychotherapeutic care for residents in the city and in the greater Zurich area.

The range of services includes the treatment of mental illnesses over the entire life span.

As a university hospital, it is also engaged in teaching and research and is very well connected internationally.
