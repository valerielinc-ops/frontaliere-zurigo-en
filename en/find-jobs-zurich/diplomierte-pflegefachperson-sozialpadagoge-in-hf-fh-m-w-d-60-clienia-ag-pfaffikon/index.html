Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With more than 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric offer of Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in ambulatory, day-clinical and inpatient settings. The Group employs around 1800 people and achieved a turnover of CHF 240 million in 2025.

Are you looking for a competent, appreciative and humorous team that works together at eye level and enthusiastically accompanies the young people in their individual topics and questions through the therapy stay? Are you interested in the DBT and DBT PTSD concept? Are you open-minded, resilient and responsible? Then you are exactly right with us!

For our team we are looking for 1. November 2026 or by arrangement one or

In our center for children and adolescents, we offer treatment offers on 7 small groups according to the latest child and adolescent psychiatric principles. The psychotherapy group Linde D is an open, specialized and certified DBT-A station and with the complementary offer of DBT PTSD treatment. It offers inpatient treatment facilities with a focus on emotional regulation disorders and trauma follow-up disorders for adolescents aged 14 to 18.

Their tasks

  • Professional and individual support of young people in the interdisciplinary team
  • Reference work including residence and care planning
  • Systemic work with relatives and cooperation with authorities
  • Conducting DBT-A group therapies, actively designing the therapeutic environment and leisure
  • Participation in the implementation of the DBT-A and the DBT-PTBS concept on station

Your profile

  • Completed training as a dipl. Nurse or social worker
  • Enjoying comprehensive and intensive work with young people
  • Interest in interdisciplinary cooperation according to the principles of a clear treatment concept as well as the conceptual further development
  • Engaged, relationship-oriented and structured personality

Our offer

  • Varied and exciting everyday work
  • Experienced, flexible, innovative and appreciative team
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation at eye level
  • Monthly Team Meeting and Supervision (Team and DBT Case Supervision)
  • Co-creation opportunities throughout the Centre for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Psychotherapy
  • Internal and external subject-specific training
  • Health and sports services
  • Regular employee events
