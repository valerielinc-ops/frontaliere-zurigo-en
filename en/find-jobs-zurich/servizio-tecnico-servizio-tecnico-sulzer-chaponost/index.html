Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and innovative development solutions that enable a successful and more sustainable society.

About the role

Job Summary The Lead (Field) Service Technician is responsible for installing, configuring, calibrating, and repairing technical products and systems at customer field locations. This role performs a variety of maintenance and technical support tasks and may provide onsite training to customer support. This job profile is intended for experienced support-level staff that generally works automatically and only require close supervision in non-routine situations. Summary of the post

We are looking for a passionate and experienced Service Technician to join our team at Sulzer Ensival Moret France SASU. The incumbent will be responsible for the installation, maintenance, diagnosis and repair of our equipment on site and in workshop.

Post responsibilities

  • Carry out the installation, commissioning, preventive and corrective maintenance of Sulzer pumps, agitators and rotary equipment in customers.
  • Perform accurate technical diagnostics and identify the causes of breakdowns or malfunctions.
  • Write detailed intervention reports, including actions, replaced parts and recommendations for the client.
  • Provide technical support to customers, advise them on the use and maintenance of their equipment.
  • Participate in customer training on basic product use and maintenance.
  • Respect scrupulously existing safety procedures and quality standards.
  • Manage spare parts stocks required for interventions.
  • Work closely with commercial and engineering teams to improve customer satisfaction.
  • Participation in fixed interventions

Qualifications required

  • Minimum 3 years of experience as a Service Technician or in a similar role, ideally in the field of pumps, rotary equipment or heavy industry.
  • Technical degree (BTS, DUT) in industrial, mechanical, electromechanical or equivalent maintenance.
  • Strong skills in mechanics, hydraulics, electricity and industrial automation.
  • Ability to read and interpret technical and electrical diagrams.
  • Mastery of diagnostic and measurement tools.
  • Valid driver's licence (routine travel).
  • Good relational skills, customer service and autonomy.
  • Ability to work independently and as a team.
  • Master's degree in French (written and oral). Technical English would be a plus.
  • Up-to-date electrical empowerment (B1V, BR, H0V) would be an advantage.
  • Knowledge of Sulzer products would be appreciated.
