Do you want to shape under-paper quality, governance, 6.7 and learning initiatives and thus make a central contribution to the future of our P&C business?

As Head of Technical Under Paper & Academy Switzerland, you will take over the key role in P&C Under Paper and Commercial Insurance Switzerland in the Under Paper Quality, asserting and governance. You will lead a specialized team, function as a central point of contact for around 300 Under-Berber and senior stakeholders and will continue to develop our First Line Risk & Internal Control Framework as well as central governance and regulatory projects. With the Under Paper Academy, you will strengthen technical excellence, 1.8-wide learning and the international Under Paper community.

Does this sound like your next challenge? Then we look forward to your application!

That's what awaits you

  • You lead, develop and coach the TechnicalUnder Papers & Academy team and are responsible for implementing Under Paper Quality, Assurance and Governance concepts for all P&C Lines of Business and customer segments with around 300 Under-Irrespondences
  • You control and develop the First Line Risk & Internal Control Framework and ensure meaningful reporting to Management, Risk Management, Compliance and the Group
  • As a strategic sparring partner for senior stakeholders (management, risk, compliance, legal, group under-paper) and you coordinate cross-sector initiatives with high business and regulatory relevance
  • You are responsible for the Under-Path Academy as a competence platform for cross-locular training, promoting technical excellence and developing learning formats such as onboarding programs and initiatives to strengthen the international sub-saw community
  • You will be the central expert for quality, filial and governance issues for the multinational program business

That's what makes you feel

  • University degree or sound insurance training with relevant further education - ideally several years of experience in the Under-Path, Governance, Assurance, Risk & Controls or Commercial Insurance
  • Extensive experience in stakeholder management at C level and proven management of governance, regulatory or cross-sectoral transformation projects; leadership experience is beneficial
  • Very good understanding of under-saw governance, first line risk & internal control frameworks, compliance requirements
  • Strong conceptual, analytical and communicative skills; complex topics are quickly captured, prepared in line with management and translated into clear, feasible measures
  • Enjoying technical excellence, learning and community-building and developing the Under-Bath Academy as an effective competence platform; knowledge of German and English in a stylish manner
