Would you like to join a global leader and build your career in a well-known company with strong reputation? About the Role As part of the L&H Sales Operations team you will be acting as a point of contact and partner for Client Managers (client facing employees).

  • Ad hoc preparing presentations for client visits, client events and conferences

About You

You have the ability to be a great teammate with constructive and open minded thinking who is reliable, dedicated and has strong owership, attention to detail and is able to prioritize.

Essentials

  • Previous working experience in relevant field, however position is also suitable for a graduate
  • Language skills: fluent English and German
  • MS office package (Excel, Word, PowerPoint)
  • Data literacy- ability to read and interpret data is an advantage
  • Nice to have Experience in Business Administration or Insurance/Reinsurance
