NICE TO MEET YOU!

IN SHORT Do you speak multiple languages, have a knack for logistics, and always keep track of everything? Join FELFEL’s dispatcher team as an Operations Assistant (hourly contract, 50-60%) in either Zurich Wiedikon or Lausanne Flon and help us elevate our operational excellence!

YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Ensure high-quality service and a smooth operational experience by consistently monitoring our service standards to keep customer satisfaction as a top priority.
  • Communicate effectively with our third-party logistics partners and multilingual drivers across various channels, acting quickly while maintaining a clear overview.
  • Act as a key point of contact for operational supply chain processes, driving efficient organization, oversight, and proactive issue resolution.
  • Coordinate closely with our customer service and hardware teams to resolve any challenges quickly and efficiently.

WHAT TO EXPECT

YOUR PROFILE

  • Practical experience in an operational, logistics, or coordination role is a strong advantage.
  • Excellent communication and coordination skills to engage effectively with a wide variety of internal and external stakeholders as a dedicated team player.
  • Exceptional eye for detail combined with a natural talent for multitasking and seamless organization.
  • Solution-oriented mindset and high motivation, maintaining focus and drive even when navigating complex challenges.
  • Fluency in English is required. For the Zurich office, fluency in German is essential, while for the Lausanne office, fluency in French is necessary (speaking all three is a fantastic bonus!).
  • Non-smoker - we don't want you to smell like cigarettes, neither at work nor at customer meetings. After all, FELFEL is about good food.
  • Valid work permit or Swiss passport.

YOU WILL BE MEASURED BY

OUR BENEFITS

  • Hourly rate of CHF 25.00 with regular working shifts.
  • Enjoy fresh, high-quality meals just steps away from your workspace, every day.
  • Fuel your day with barista-style coffee and premium tea, thanks to our Gavetti machine.
  • Work in a collaborative environment that values initiative and gives you the space to contribute your ideas.

ABOUT US FELFEL is all about good food and coffee in the workplace: we help Swiss employers become masters of hospitality.

With our smart fridge ‘FELFEL’ and delicious barista coffee ‘Gavetti’, we enable companies to provide their employees with first-class, ultra-fresh food and drinks throughout the day. Whether hearty and traditional, modern and vegan or high in protein - our weekly changing menu ensures that all employees always have something to be excited about ;).

FELFEL is a family business that was founded in 2013. Today, we take care of around 1000 locations in Switzerland, and have 150 employees across our offices in Lausanne, Zurich and New York.
