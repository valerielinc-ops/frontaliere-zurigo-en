Full description

Location: Lifestage Solutions AG, Industriestrasse 19, 8112 Otelfingen

You want to combine your pleasure in customer contact with your communication talent and your language skills? In this versatile front office function, you are the first point of contact for our customers. With your professional, reliable and solution-oriented nature, you ensure competent processing of requests. As of now or by agreement, we are looking for a committed personality, who together with various teams contributes to smooth processes and high customer satisfaction.

You can do that.

  • You will advise our customers professionally and reliably via telephone, live chat and e-mail – in German and French.
  • You process orders, billings and returns registrations efficiently and ensure a smooth processing.
  • You tackle out-of-stock situations and customer complaints in a solution-oriented manner and find suitable alternatives.
  • You support our customers with product inquiries and help with the use of our app.
  • You are actively involved in the further development of our customer service and work interdisciplinary with teams such as logistics, back office and purchasing.

You bring that with you

  • You have a commercial education with experience in customer support – ideally in the health sector.
  • Or: You have a basic medical education (e.g. as an MPA, nurse HF or FaGe) and would like to develop professionally from the patient bed.
  • You are IT-savvy and have a strong technical understanding. You are familiar with laptop, MS Office, Google Workspace and digital tools.
  • You have a quick apprehension and quickly work your way into new tasks. You think in a networked way and recognize cross-sectoral connections – so you bring in efficient solutions.
  • You like to communicate professionally – especially on the phone. Customer orientation is a matter of course for you.
  • You have very good knowledge of German and fluent French, Italian is a welcome plus.

We fill this position without external personnel service providers.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday
  • Pension fund with advantageous premium distribution - approx. 2/3 are taken over by us
  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and other top brands
  • Extend competences through funded and supported training and internal development opportunities
  • Family friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of wages) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of wages), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Registered shares at preferential prices - not only engage professionally in Galenica, but also invest
  • Mobile and flexible work in consultation with the team

Benefits may vary slightly depending on area/place

About us

Lifestage Solutions AG is a strategic partner in the health sector, including ambulatory and inpatient care. With our products and services, we ensure that our customers can concentrate on their core tasks – care – and save significantly in their cost structures by simplifying their everyday processes with digitalization and the latest technologies. We are not a classic consumables supplier, even if we can offer products from all major manufacturers. Our heart beats for strategic solutions that we develop together with our customers with the help of modern technologies.
