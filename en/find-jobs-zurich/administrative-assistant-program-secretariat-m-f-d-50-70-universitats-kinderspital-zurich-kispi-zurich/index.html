Temporary for 2 years

Your tasks

  • You support program coordination and program management administratively and organizationally in day-to-day operations.
  • The coordination of internal and external appointments as well as the organization of program, working group and other meetings are part of your area of responsibility.
  • You maintain pendences and appointment lists carefully and keep them up-to-date.
  • When organizing workshops, meetings and other events, you will be responsible for logistics planning, including spatial planning, technology, catering and participant management.
  • You take care of the structured filing and maintenance of program documents, contracts, correspondence and other administrative documents.
  • In the financial sector, you support administrative processes, in particular invoice processing, expense reporting, compilation of documents and preparation of payment processes in cooperation with the competent specialist departments.
  • Administrative support in contracting involves preparing documents, obtaining signatures and filing and tracking deadlines.
  • You keep contact, distribution and participant lists up-to-date and maintain them carefully.
  • You coordinate administrative communication with internal and external partners as well as specialist departments and take care of the sending of information, documents and reminders.
  • In addition, you bring your ideas to the further development and optimization of administrative processes, templates and filing structures.

Your profile

  • Completed training in a field relevant to the function, such as commercial training, administration or health care

Even newcomers: women with a suitable background are welcome

  • Initial professional experience in an administrative or organizational function is beneficial, ideally in the healthcare, university, research or scientific environment
  • Enjoy organizational tasks as well as interest in familiarizing oneself with different administrative processes and topics
  • First experience in organizing and coordinating appointments, meetings or events is beneficial
  • Basic understanding of administrative financial processes or willingness to familiarize yourself with topics such as invoice processing, expense reports and payment processes
  • Independent, structured and careful working methods as well as good organisational talent
  • Ability to keep an eye on different tasks, deadlines and deadlines and set priorities
  • Enjoying a versatile interface function and working with different internal and external partners
  • Good communication skills, service orientation and reliability
  • Secure handling of common MS Office applications
  • Excel knowledge is an advantage
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English in word and writing
