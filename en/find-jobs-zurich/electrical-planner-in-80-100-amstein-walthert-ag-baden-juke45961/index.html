Full description

Amstein + Walthert is an engineering company with around 1,100 employees. As engineers, planners and consultants, we work on all aspects of the technology of civil engineering and civil engineering over the entire life cycle. Our solutions are innovative, elegant and sustainable. These are your tasks

  • Support of the project managers:women in various interesting projects
  • Drawing of plans
  • Creation of schemes
  • Processing of the entire electrical technology (high and low current) You bring that with you
  • Degree as an electrical planner: in EFZ
  • Initial professional experience
  • Knowledge of Swiss installation standards and legal regulations (e.g. NIN, NIV, High Power Ordinance) desirable
  • BIM and Revit knowledge are beneficial
  • Nova CAD knowledge
  • Dynamic personality with affinity for digital planning / building
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Messerli NPK knowledge is beneficial
  • Enthusiasm and enjoy working in a team
  • Enjoyment of demanding work (home office possible)
