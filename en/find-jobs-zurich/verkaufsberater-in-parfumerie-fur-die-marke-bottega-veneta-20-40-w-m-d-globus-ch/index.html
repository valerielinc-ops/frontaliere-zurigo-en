Full description

With great attention to detail, our employees make GLOBUS the leading premium and luxury address in Switzerland. It is people like you who make the difference and bring the “place we love to be” to life together with us.

For our counter of the luxury brand Bottega Veneta in the globe Zurich Bahnhofstrasse we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement a reliable and motivated sales person in the amount of 20-40% . Here you make the difference

  • As a perfumery expert:in and host:in you ensure that our customers feel welcome, well advised and taken care of as guests.
  • Thanks to your excellent service, your fascination with our fragrance brands and your individual advice, you can ensure unique shopping experiences.
  • With your positive charisma and your passion for fragrances & beauty, you contribute decisively to the company’s success.

That draws you out

  • You have professional training in the retail trade and/or bring several years of sales experience in the premium sector, preferably in the field of perfumery or beauty.
  • You are a service-oriented personality with high passion for selling high-quality fragrances and care products.
  • You are young (remained), trendy and flexible and bring a great passion for perfumery.
  • You love and live the role as a host:in and have a pronounced customer orientation.
  • You have a good relationship and a good appearance.
  • You are very reliable, like to work on Saturdays and are also flexible on the other days of the week (Monday to Saturday).
  • You have excellent German and good English skills. Other languages are not a must, but of great advantage for our international guests.

BENEFITS FOR YOU Together with around 1,700 other lovers of detail, you are an important part of our fascinating premium and luxury world. You experience real appreciation, work in a great team and we trust you so that you can use space and make a difference with your skills. If you understand your craft and bring a large portion of passion, then many career doors are open to you. We are happy to support you with internal but also external training and further education.

Also, look forward to

  • an exceptional working environment with flat hierarchies in the heart of Zurich.
  • Benefit-based premiums and attractive social benefits.
  • Flexible working time models and at least 5 weeks holidays (PS: You get more free time thanks to the option "unpaid holiday" - from 7 to 365 days).
  • up to 50% discount on products from the globe world for you and your family.
  • Much more...
