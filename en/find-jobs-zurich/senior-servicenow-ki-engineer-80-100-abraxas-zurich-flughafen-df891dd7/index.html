Full description

Artificial intelligence is changing the way services are delivered, knowledge is used and processes are automated. ServiceNow is one of the leading platforms worldwide. At Abraxas, we have the clear goal of consistently integrating AI, GenAI and intelligent automation into our ServiceNow platform and creating real added value for customers, employees and processes. For this strategically important role, we are looking for an experienced personality who brings together ServiceNow and AI, recognizes new possibilities and develops productive solutions from them. You are not working on theoretical concepts, but are actively shaping the next generation of our ServiceNow platform. You will strengthen our ServiceNow team and drive the next evolutionary stage of our platform: the targeted use of AI-assisted features, automation and data-driven services. As part of a strategic initiative, you will identify relevant AI use cases, develop prototypes and convert them into scalable solutions for our customers and internal processes together with the team. Your role

  • Development and implementation of the ServiceNow AI and automation roadmap
  • Identification, evaluation and prioritization of AI use cases
  • Conception and implementation of solutions based on Now Assist and other AI features
  • Building intelligent assistants, AI agents and automated service processes
  • Implementation of proof of concepts and pilot projects
  • Transfer of successful solutions into productive operations
  • Advising customers and internal stakeholders on the meaningful use of AI
  • Further development of architecture, standards and governance for the use of AI within ServiceNow
  • Promoting high data quality as the basis for successful AI solutions
  • Active knowledge transfer within the ServiceNow team

Your profile

  • Several years of practical experience as ServiceNow Engineer, Developer, Architect or Senior Consultant
  • In-depth knowledge of ServiceNow ITSM and ideally other modules such as CSM or ITOM
  • Experience with Flow Designer, IntegrationHub and automation solutions
  • Demonstrate practical experience with Now Assist, Predictive Intelligence, Task Intelligence, Virtual Agent, AI Search
  • Experience in the design and implementation of productive AI solutions
  • ServiceNow certifications
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English

Nice-to-have

  • Experience with GenAI, LLM or AI agent approaches
  • Architecture experience in the enterprise environment
  • Experience in public administrations or regulated environments
  • Knowledge of Data Governance and AI Governance

Your Benefits

  • You will have the opportunity to actively shape and strategically shape the use of AI within our ServiceNow platform.
  • New ideas, pilot projects and innovative solutions are expressly desired. You can accompany topics from the first idea to the productive implementation.
  • Now Assist, AI Agents, Generative AI and intelligent automation are not future topics for us, but concrete fields of action.
  • The solutions you develop support numerous customers, employees and processes and create measurable added value.
  • A motivated, collaborative team with high quality standards and innovation
  • Exciting customers, challenging issues and complex ServiceNow environments
  • Up to 60% home office possible with a 100% workload
  • Holiday purchase (2 weeks per year)
  • 41h week with flexible working hours on a full-time workload
  • Mind. 25 days of holidays per year
  • Support for 100% financial or temporal training
  • Free SBB half-ax subscription for all employees

Abraxas is one of the leading Swiss IT service providers for the public sector. We combine innovation with social relevance and use modern technologies where they make a real difference. If you not only operate ServiceNow, but also want to actively shape the future of AI, automation and service management, we look forward to your application. Become part of our #TeamAbraxas and shape the next generation of ServiceNow with us.
