Your

  • Tasks You supervise and manage a permanent group of employees (alphabetical allocation) independently from entry to exit in hourly and monthly wages. We carry out the monthly wage runs for the entire 3,000 employees together as a team – you ensure the error-free preparation of your circle. You juggle with allowances, irregular shift models and the correct handling of withholding taxes. You maintain the personnel and wage master data in our system Hospis and ensure a high data quality. After familiarizing yourself with day-to-day business, you will actively participate in HR projects, question existing processes and optimize our payroll processes. You support the team on a professional level and act as a competent contact for line managers in complex payroll questions. You bring several years of profound experience in the payroll of a large company (ideally 1,500+ employees). Industry experience in healthcare is a plus, but not a must. You are very familiar with Swiss social security law, withholding tax as well as the UVG and KTG areas. An HR training (e.g. HR specialist card or payroll specialist) underpins your knowledge. Ideally, you have already worked with Hospis or find your way quickly into new HR systems. You are well versed in using MS Excel for the structured and forward processing of data. You think along, like to tackle, bring a proactive mindset and appreciate a dynamic, complex environment.
