Full description

The social paediatric centre (SPZ) is an organizational unit at the center of the cantonal hospital Winterthur. In the stationary area, the SPZ leads a therapy station for 12 patients (alternatively mixed). Children and adolescents receive a multimodal stationary treatment due to psychosomatic and psychiatric illnesses (key points: eating disorders, depression, anxiety, pain and forced disorders). They are accompanied by milieu-therapeutic and visit the clinic school daily. Mansioni
