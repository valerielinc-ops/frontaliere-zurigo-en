About the role

Project Buyer with Supply Chain mindset to Nordic Water Products AB

You are a proactive and self-driven purchasing professional with experience from project-driven manufacturing or industrial environments. You combine strong analytical skills with commercial awareness and technical understanding, enabling you to manage complex sourcing activities and support successful project execution.

You have experience in project purchasing, supplier negotiations, and supply chain management, and are comfortable working closely with project managers, engineering, production, and suppliers. You take ownership, prioritize effectively, and thrive in a role with a high degree of independence.

As a person, you are a collaborative team player who builds strong relationships, drives continuous improvement, and delivers results in a dynamic environment.

To succeed in this role, you have

  • Experience in project purchasing within manufacturing or industrial businesses
  • Strong negotiation and supplier management skills
  • An analytical and structured approach to problem-solving
  • Technical understanding and commercial acumen
  • Knowledge of industrial manufacturing processes, sheet metal fabrication and stainless-steel products is meritorious
  • A proactive mindset, ability to work independently, and strong teamwork skills
  • Fluent in Swedish and English, both spoken and written

This is an excellent opportunity for someone who enjoys combining commercial responsibility, technical challenges, and project-driven work in a dynamic international environment.

Place of employment: Helsingborg
