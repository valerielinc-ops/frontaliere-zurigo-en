This is what awaits you: As a market development unit of Mobiliar, the management of brokerage business in Zurich-Oerlikon is responsible for the partnership with professional brokers:innen. Through high service quality and willingness towards the brokers: inside you make a direct contribution to the company’s success. This is what you bring with you:
