Think along. co-creating. Creating pleasure.

The Dielsdorf Health Center is about MORE than care. It’s about people, quality of life and making the difference at the right moment. Around 500 employees from 35 nations are pulling in the same direction – interprofessional, committed and with an attitude.

Their tasks

Ensuring the proper preparation and distribution of food (cook and chill) Drawing the food on the belt and partly their acceptance and control Use on all items of the kitchen as well as internal or external banquets and aperitifs Compliance with hygiene guidelines Close cooperation with the chef and the care Participation in daily briefings Assistance in learning

Your profile

  • Completed training as a cook EFZ
  • Experience in the kitchen in the home or hospital sector as well as in classical gastronomy
  • Good knowledge of German (minor level B1)
  • IT user knowledge
  • Joy of cooking with creativity as well as guest-oriented action and appearance
  • High quality awareness and willingness to perform
  • Flexibility (different working hours, use on about two weekends per month on 7 days per week, excluding room hours)
  • Service orientation, communication and reliable working methods

Our offer

A responsible task with room for manoeuvre in a varied, future-oriented and interprofessional environment. You benefit from attractive employment conditions: at least 5 weeks holidays, good social benefits, discounts at SBB and Reka checks, individual training opportunities, discounted food in our restaurants and free parking spaces.
