Full description

For the parliamentary business & support department, we are looking for a service-oriented leader immediately or by agreement.

Tasks

  • You manage the parliamentary business & support department in terms of personnel and expertise.
  • They advise, support, coordinate and perform quality assurance tasks in the handling of parliamentary affairs and (political) initiatives of the Office for Urban Development.
  • They advise, support and perform quality assurance tasks in procurement (e.g. submission procedure and contract management) of the Office for Urban Development.
  • They lead interdisciplinary projects and actively participate in them.
  • They are responsible for infrastructural issues at the Office for Urban Development (e.g. spatial and material responsibility).

Profile

  • University degree preferably in law, business administration or political science
  • In-depth knowledge of political processes and parliamentary processes on the one hand and public procurement law on the other
  • Experience in public administration
  • Leadership experience, ideally with leadership training
  • Experience in procurement, ideally with additional training
  • Excellent written and oral expression and ability to present complex issues accurately and comprehensibly and to communicate them appropriately
  • Experience in project management and strong process understanding
  • Structured and precise working method
  • Interdisciplinary, networked and future-oriented thinking and acting
  • Resilience and teamwork
  • High motivation and willingness to work for the concerns of the city in the field of tension between politics and administration

We offer

It awaits you a demanding and versatile activity. We value good cooperation: trust and the recognition of good performance are important to us. We create an environment for new ideas and responsible work. Attractive employment and training conditions as well as a centrally located workplace near the central station are further advantages. With the title "Family AND Occupation", we promote the compatibility of family and work as well as equal opportunities for women and men.

About us

Zurich’s future, that is what we are committed to.

At the Office for Urban Development, today we are planning the Zurich of tomorrow. We weave the history of the city with future visions for livable quarters. We are creating the conditions for Zurich to change and grow. Visions of the future also shape the self-image of our office. We see ourselves as a learning organization that attaches importance to further developing structures, processes and competencies in order to optimally meet today’s requirements and be prepared for future requirements.
