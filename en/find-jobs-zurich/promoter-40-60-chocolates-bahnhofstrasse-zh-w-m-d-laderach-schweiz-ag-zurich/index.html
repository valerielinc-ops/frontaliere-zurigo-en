Full description

High-quality, fresh chocolate – that's what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family-owned company today employs over 2500 people from more than 80 countries, with a growing trend. The company's headquarters are located in the canton of Glarus. Against a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new, innovative ideas in agile teams. The «Retail» business unit includes over 250 chocolateries in over 30 countries. Here, we offer our chocolate specialties and invite customers to experience fresh chocolate again and again. This is what you can expect

  • You actively approach our customers and excite them about our walk-in atelier
  • You advise our customers with friendliness and competence – and help them find the right gift from our high-quality assortment
  • You actively bring our fine products closer: with targeted sampling directly on site, in accordance with Läderach's specifications and quality standards
  • You ensure that our Christmas stand is ready for the day – by helping in the morning with setting up and in the evening with cleaning up
  • You reliably take over cash transactions and ensure correct billing
  • You observe our hygiene and freshness requirements and contribute to a harmonious and well-groomed shopping experience with your commitment
  • You work in shifts according to the deployment plan This is what you bring with you
  • Valuable work experience in retail or as a promoter and a genuine passion for sales
  • You inspire our customers with your enthusiastic and infectious style
  • You love making a special joy for our customers and offering them a unique shopping experience
  • You always put the highest quality and customer orientation first, even in turbulent times
  • You willingly accept responsibility, are a true team player and work hand in hand with your colleagues
  • You appreciate open and respectful communication and speak German and English fluently; knowledge of French and other languages is an additional advantage
  • You have no fear of flexible working hours and are also on board on weekends, Saturdays, Sundays or public holidays This is what we offer you
  • A stimulating work environment with motivated colleagues
  • A position with a lot of personal responsibility
  • The opportunity to bring forward fresh ideas
  • An open and respectful feedback culture
  • Internal and external training
  • Individual career opportunities
  • Generous discounts on our products
  • Chocolate at the workplace
  • Good accessibility of the locations Sounds interesting? Then apply with us. Jessica Carvalho, branch manager, +41 442 101 11 60, is available to answer your questions. We kindly request that you understand that we do not consider external personnel agencies for the filling of this position.
