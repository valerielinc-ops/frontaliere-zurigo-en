Finance Transformation & Compliance Specialist (m/f/d)

90%-100%, Zurich, permanent

Printed Printing

In the area of Vice President Finance and Controlling (VPFC), the newly created Financial Management & Compliance department plays a central role in the management and monitoring of regulatory standards.

Are you enthusiastic about finance transformation, compliance and sustainable process improvements? You not only want to manage existing structures, but actively build and shape something? Then, at ETH Zurich, you can expect a versatile key role as Finance Transformation & Compliance Specialist (m/f/d) with a great deal of room for manoeuvre and a direct influence on the further development of finance-related governance and compliance topics.

Job description

In this newly created position, you combine challenging project work with long-term responsibility for financial compliance. Initially, the focus will be on the introduction of an organization-wide expense and travel tool – then you will play a central role in the further development of ETH Zurich’s financial compliance.

In the first 12 to 18 months, you will assume overall technical responsibility for the introduction of an organization-wide expense and travel tool. You will analyse existing processes, develop standardised and sustainable target processes, define control and approval mechanisms and control cooperation with internal stakeholders from finance, IT, HR and departments. You contribute your expertise in the selection and implementation of the system solution, coordinate the implementation and support rollout, training and documentation.

After the successful introduction of the tool, your focus increasingly shifts to financial compliance. They actively develop compliance issues in the financial sector and contribute significantly to the operation and expansion of financial compliance. They create and update financial guidelines and regulations, develop training and awareness-raising measures and advise organizational units on financial compliance-relevant issues. In addition, you will conduct risk analysis and internal audits, ensuring that regulatory requirements are translated into pragmatic and sustainable solutions.

In this role, you work independently, networked and with high impact across organizational boundaries. In doing so, you live the ETH values of respectful cooperation, promote open dialogue with different stakeholders and create trust and orientation through your expertise.

Profile

  • University degree (FH/university) in business administration, finance, accounting or a comparable discipline
  • Several years of professional experience in Finance, Audit, Compliance, Risk Management, Finance Transformation or Internal Control Systems (ICS)
  • Demonstrable experience in the design and optimization of financial processes, especially in the areas of expenses, procurement and accounting
  • Ideally experience in introducing or transforming financial and compliance systems
  • Experience in process and project management
  • High IT and system affinity
  • Excellent knowledge of German and very good knowledge of English
  • Ability to implement complex regulatory requirements in an understandable and practical manner
  • Strong analytical, conceptual and structured working method
  • Convincing communication skills and a secure appearance towards different stakeholders
  • High level of independence, initiative and a sense of responsibility
  • Diplomatic skill and enjoyment of cross-cutting cooperation
  • Experience
