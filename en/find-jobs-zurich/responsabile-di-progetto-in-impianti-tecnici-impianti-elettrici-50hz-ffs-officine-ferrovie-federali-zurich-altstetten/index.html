Project manager: in technical systems/electrical systems 50Hz 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Project manager: technical systems/electrical systems 50Hz Zurich Altstetten / Work Smart, January 1st, 2027 or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. In this versatile role, you represent the interests of the client in demanding infrastructure projects in the area of ​​technical and electrical systems (50 Hz). Your field of activity includes platforms, tunnels, railway technical rooms and buildings along the railway infrastructure. You take on the role of builder in multidisciplinary project teams and manage external planning and execution partners throughout the entire project life cycle. In doing so, you ensure that requirements regarding safety, quality, cost-effectiveness and sustainability are met. A dedicated team of generalists with a broad technical background awaits you, who will support you as you get started and offer you a varied and responsible working environment. With “Work Smart” you can organize your everyday working life flexibly – in the office, in the home office or on the go. You can move that.

  • You represent the interests of the client in all project phases
  • from strategic planning through tendering and awarding to implementation and commissioning.
  • You are responsible for achieving the project goals in terms of costs, deadlines, quality, safety and functionality.
  • You control and coordinate external planning offices, entrepreneurs and specialists and ensure efficient collaboration between all those involved.
  • You ensure compliance with standards, guidelines, legal requirements and client requirements.
  • You conduct transparent contract, supplementary and claims management and identify opportunities and risks at an early stage in order to initiate suitable measures for successful project management.
  • You act as a central interface between the client, project teams, authorities, operating organizations and other stakeholders. This is what you bring with you.
  • For you, teamwork is the basis of successful projects and you enjoy working in an interdisciplinary manner.
  • You have strong analytical, conceptual and organizational skills as well as entrepreneurial thinking. You act independently, structured and solution-oriented and impress with your strong communication skills and assertiveness.
  • You have several years of experience in project management, project control or client representation of electrical, energy or building technology systems.
  • Knowledge of public procurement as well as contract and supplementary management is an advantage. You are familiar with SIA's service and contract models as well as the relevant standards and guidelines in the area of ​​electrical and infrastructure systems.
  • You have a university degree (FH/ETH/University) or higher vocational training (HF, federal specialist certificate or federal diploma) in the field of electrical engineering, energy supply or building technology.
  • You communicate stylishly in German
  • Knowledge of another national language is an advantage.
