Full description

For the further expansion of the Ambulatorium of Angiology at the Triemli site, we are looking for committed and motivated personalities immediately after agreement.

Tasks

  • You receive, supervise and competently advise our (outpatient and inpatient) patients and are our first business card against the outside.
  • The angiology consultation hours you organize and plan ahead.
  • In addition, you independently perform non-invasive angiological examinations such as oscillographies, Doppler pressure measurements and treadmill examinations.
  • Another exciting task is assistance in vein interventions.
  • General administrative work complements your versatile range of tasks.

Profile

  • Completed training and experience as medical practice assistant
  • High sense of responsibility, independence and reliability
  • Open-minded personality with high social competence
  • Quick comprehension and flexibility
  • Experienced PC user knowledge

We offer

We offer you more than just a job! We live an open du-culture, which is characterized by an uncomplicated and personal cooperation at eye level, respect and appreciation. As part of our diverse team of over 120 professional groups, you actively shape a meaningful environment. You can expect a systematic introduction to the field as well as an interesting, varied area of responsibility in a lively ambulatorium. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions and excellent social benefits from the city of Zurich.

About us

With over 34,000 inpatients and around 436,000 outpatients per year, the Stadtspital Zürich ranks among the top 10 Swiss hospitals. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,400 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
