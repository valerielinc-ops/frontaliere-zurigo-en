Full description

Tension in the handle. Technology in the blood. You are the one who intervenes when it technically matters. As a company electrician, you ensure that our systems run reliably, faults are repaired quickly and our production works at all times.

  • Contract type: Perpetual
  • Pensum: 100%
  • Job: from now on
  • Language: German
  • Place of work: Geiser AG, Rietbachstrasse 11 8952 Schlieren

Tasks

  • Responsibility for all electrical installations and installations in the company
  • Maintenance and repair of electrical equipment
  • Electrical installations and technical adaptations
  • Ensuring compliance with electrical and safety standards
  • Testing of equipment and electrical tools
  • Initial checks and logging of the work carried out
  • Collaboration in technical projects and interdisciplinary working groups
  • Support for the further development of the electrical safety concept
  • Further tasks according to the instructions of the supervisor

Requirements

  • Completed training as an automatic engineer EFZ, electro-installer EFZ, electrical installer EFZ or similar technical training
  • At least two years of professional experience in the electrical environment
  • Technical understanding and independent working methods
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Basic knowledge of SAP, Windows and MS Office is an advantage
  • Responsible, reliable and resilient personality
  • Flexible operational readiness as well as high safety and hygiene awareness
  • NIV13 approval of benefit

This is what we offer

  • Food: Cuisine is very important to us: freshly prepared food in our canteen, free soups and fruits as well as high-quality products at greatly reduced prices await you in our outlet.
  • Mobility: Benefit from convenient connections to public transport, attractive subsidies for tickets and sufficient parking - sustainable and worry-free on the road!
  • Professional development opportunities: Expand your knowledge through extensive further education offers, high-quality training in the Coop Education Center and a wide range of personal development opportunities such as mentoring and coaching.
  • Health: Your well-being is important to us: Benefit from our health management, rewards for reliability, discounted Reka checks, exclusive employee discounts and promoted fitness subscriptions – for health and leisure!
