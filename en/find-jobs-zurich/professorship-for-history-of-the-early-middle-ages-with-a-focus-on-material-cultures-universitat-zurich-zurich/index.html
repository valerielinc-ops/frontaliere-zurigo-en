Full description

Historical Seminar

Job opening 1 August 2028

At the University of Zurich is on the 1. August 2028 to fill a professorship for the history of the early Middle Ages with a focus on Material Cultures (Open Rank). The future job holder should have done excellent research in the history of the former Middle Ages and have teaching experience. It is expected that the research focus will be on material cultures, which also includes writing culture, as well as concrete projects to set innovative accents in this area, attract highly competitive third-party funding and build international partnerships. In addition to internationally visible, innovative research impulses for the subject, cooperation in research and teaching with other professorships within the Historical Seminar and in the medical focus areas of other subjects and the Center of Zurich Medieval Science at the Faculty of Philosophy is expected. A doctorate in history completed at the time of application and further excellent scientific publications are required. Depending on the qualification of the person to be appointed, the professorship is filled as an ordinariat, extraordinariat or as an assistant professorship with tenure track. Teaching takes place in German and / or in English. The Historical Seminar, the University of Zurich and the university location of Zurich offer a stimulating research and teaching environment with a wide range of opportunities for professional exchange and cooperation. The University of Zurich strives to increase diversity in research and teaching and therefore explicitly asks qualified women and people from other underrepresented groups to apply. Applications are up to 4. Submit online November 2026. Access and information on online applications can be found on the website of the Faculty of Philosophy. Further information is provided by Prof. Dr. Claudia Zey.
