  • As a customer companion: in Frequency Acquisition you are in direct contact with our customers and accompany them on their journey. You ask our passengers for their travel route and tickets to determine their travel behaviour.
  • You collect the collected data in an electronic device.
  • You inform the customer about their journey, ensure travel comfort and direct the customer flows.
  • In a very varied working day, you move with daily reassigned teamwork: Switzerland.
  • Thanks to the precisely clocked roster, you can be on time and without further tasks or e-mails in the well-deserved evening.

You bring that with you.

  • You are communicative, open-minded and your friendly dealings with people and your strong customer orientation distinguish you. With your very good communication and conflict skills, you make a decisive contribution to a positive customer experience.
  • We focus on cooperation and team spirit in order to cope with the sometimes physically demanding tasks.
  • You have a flair for numbers, a quick comprehension and a strong sense of responsibility. You work efficiently and accurately, master digital tools and are ready to work shifts.
  • You have completed a vocational apprenticeship (at least two years old) or graduate degree. Your motivation is more important to us than the industry you come from.
  • You have very good knowledge of German (level C1). You will also be prepared to obtain a language diploma (level A2) within two years: French, Italian or English.
  • In the application process, we will ask you for a current criminal record and enforcement record (not older than 3 months). The document can be ordered at the invitation for personal acquaintance.
