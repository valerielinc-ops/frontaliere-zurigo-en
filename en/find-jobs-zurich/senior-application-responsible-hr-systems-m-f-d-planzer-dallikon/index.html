Full description

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, participate and grow with us. Become part of our family and shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us.

What you move

  • You take on the professional responsibility for part of our HR system landscape and develop it continuously together with the team. You work with existing systems such as rexx and also accompany the introduction and further development of future HR solutions.
  • You will analyze and prioritize requirements, question existing processes and actively shape our HR system landscape.
  • As an interface between HR, IT, our decentralised HR managers and external partners, you bundle the requirements and provide suitable solutions.
  • Major developments and new introductions accompany you from the idea to the successful implementation and also take over the management of HR system projects.
  • Testing, acceptance and quality assurance are just as much your tasks as working in the system administration and in 2nd/3rd level support.

Why do you succeed?

  • You have several years of experience in the support and further development of applications, ideally in the HR system environment.
  • In requirements and project management, you are experienced and understand how to analyze needs, prioritize them and derive suitable solutions from them.
  • You think networked, have a good understanding of processes and feel comfortable at the interface between the department and IT.
  • You combine conceptual thinking with a pronounced hands-on mentality and like to take part in the implementation yourself.
  • You have an education or study in business informatics or a comparable qualification and have a good understanding of HR and IT
  • Personal responsibility, team spirit and openness to new things characterize you.

Your Benefits

  • Further education and development opportunities: With us you can develop yourself - we promote and challenge talents
  • Family culture: You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Opportunity for home office: We go with the times - enjoy with us the advantage of home office

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
