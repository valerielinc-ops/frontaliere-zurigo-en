Welcome to Medbase Zürich Löwenstrasse

In the heart of Zurich, we provide interdisciplinary and quality-oriented care for approximately 700 m2 patients in our modern medical center. Around 40 employees from the disciplines of General Internal Medicine, Sports Medicine, Orthopaedic Surgery and Cardiology, Physiotherapy, Performance Diagnostics, Nutritional Consulting and Osteopathy work together interprofessionally. Our Medical Center is certified in the field of Sports Medicine by Swiss Olympic. Our flexible working models and representative solutions enable a good work-life balance.

You can move this as a specialist, you offer comprehensive care and ensure a high quality of patient care. At Medbase, you are part of an interdisciplinary team that supports you in your professional development and sets high standards.

  • Internal and external training to improve quality

What you bring
