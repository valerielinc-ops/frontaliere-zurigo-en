Full description

Tasks

Her field of activity includes psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment of alcohol- and drug-dependent patients in individual and group setting. This includes the implementation of diagnostics, reporting, careful management of medical history, the development of therapy goals and plans as well as the implementation and evaluation of psychotherapeutic and psychiatric interventions in individual and rarely in couple or family discussion. The management of open and manualized groups is also part of the task area. Site discussions with employers and, if necessary, with other third parties or institutions involved round off the spectrum.

Requirements

We want a specialist with an interest in interdisciplinary cooperation in a team of doctors, psychologists, nurses, social workers, movement and occupational therapists. Ideally, you already have psychiatric and psychotherapeutic work experience and preferably a completed foreign year, an advanced psychotherapy education is beneficial. You have previous experience in psychiatric, team-oriented and interprofessional action as well as a CH doctor’s diploma or entry in the MEBEKO registry. Very good knowledge of German in word and writing we presuppose.

We offer

With this position, we offer you responsible tasks in a motivated, modern and well-connected environment. Your professional and personal development is a concern for us, which we support generously and specifically with structured further education and training offers. The working conditions are contemporary and appealing:

  • Reliable service planning in a stable environment
  • 42 hours + 4 hours working week
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
  • 60 % employer contribution to the pension fund
  • 100% employer contribution to KTG insurance Your place of work Our day clinic in Zurich is centrally located on Sihlquai and is very easily accessible by public transport. The urban location creates a rich working environment. Our offer is aimed at people of all genders and identities.
