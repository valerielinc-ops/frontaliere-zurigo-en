Scientist (m/f/x) – Cell Biology and Gene Editing Temporary position, 1 year | Schlieren, Switzerland

We are looking for a hands-on Scientist to join our Mechanistic Pharmacology team within Translational Research in Schlieren. This temporary one-year position will focus on developing and applying human in vitro models, functional assays, and gene editing approaches to support cardiovascular and renal research programs.

About the Role

  • Develop and apply human in vitro models and functional assay workflows to support translational research programs.
  • Generate high-quality experimental data to characterize the mode of action of novel therapeutic candidates in cardiovascular and renal indications.
  • Independently perform human cell culture, transfection, gene editing, and assay workflows with strong attention to scientific rigor, reproducibility, and data quality.
  • Contribute to the design, optimization, and continuous improvement of experimental approaches and assay performance.
  • Analyze, interpret, and clearly present experimental results to project teams and stakeholders.
  • Maintain accurate documentation of experimental work and support implementation of laboratory best practices and quality standards.
  • Ensure compliance with laboratory safety procedures and contribute to a safe, well-organized, and collaborative working environment.
  • Work closely with scientists across projects and functions, contributing to a highly interactive and team-oriented research setting.

Your Profile

  • Master’s degree in Cell Biology, Life Sciences, or a related scientific discipline, or equivalent relevant laboratory experience; industry experience is an advantage.
  • At least 3 years of practical laboratory experience in cell biology, including strong expertise in human cell culture, such as HUVECs, and transfection methods.
  • Hands-on experience with CRISPR-based gene editing approaches.
  • Proficiency in molecular biology techniques, including RT-qPCR, immunoassays and Western blotting.
  • Experience with imaging-based techniques is an advantage.
  • Experience in troubleshooting experiments, handling laboratory equipment, and optimizing workflows to improve assay robustness and performance.
  • Ability to work independently and take ownership of experiments from study design through execution, analysis, and presentation of results.
  • Strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and commitment to high-quality experimental documentation.
  • Proficiency in written and spoken English.
  • Collaborative mindset, flexibility, and ability to work effectively across teams and with different project stakeholders.
