Full description

You are the voice of our customers at Migros Supermarket AG. Instead of optimizing individual advertising materials, you get to the bottom of the crucial consumer insights – the insights that really make the difference. You translate company goals into measurable communication strategies and make our brand tangible from the app to the POS.

Mansioni

  • You are responsible for the further development and implementation of overarching projects in close exchange with internal departments
  • You act as a sparring partner for agencies and convey complex issues to target groups in cross-divisional committees
  • You analyze consumer behavior and derive sound insights from complex data. Based on this, you will develop holistic customer journeys that consistently orchestrate our customer relationships in the digital & physical ecosystem.
  • You will develop future-oriented concepts on how we can further develop the customer relationship through technological innovations (e.g. AI-supported personalization) and new interaction models in a customer-centric manner.
  • You define relevant KPIs, keep an eye on campaign success and optimize strategic measures based on data

  • Ability to act both strategically and operationally and lead complex projects in an agile environment to successful completion
