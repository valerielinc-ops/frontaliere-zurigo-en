Full description

In this responsible role, you will take on a wide range of tasks related to grid operations and make an important contribution to the secure supply of electricity to the City of Zürich.

Tasks

  • As part of a team of six specialists, you work with grid control and calculation systems and thus contribute to a secure electricity supply.
  • You plan, review and approve switching orders in the medium- and high-voltage grid and issue switching instructions.
  • You write documents and instructions in polished German for employees in the City of Zürich’s distribution grid area.
  • You independently manage smaller projects and participate in larger projects as a representative of grid operations.
  • After comprehensive onboarding, you provide on-call duty (Pikett) approximately eight times a year and, in the event of a supply outage, lead the coordination of incident management.

Profile

  • Building on your basic training in electrical engineering with an EFZ (e.g. electrical installer or automation technician), you have completed a degree (FH or HF) in electrical or energy engineering.
  • Ideally, you have already gained professional experience in medium or high voltage during or after your studies.
  • Experience in grid calculation and operational management will be an added advantage.
  • You keep a cool head even in stressful situations and, when on call (Pikett), can reach Zürich Oerlikon within 30 minutes.
  • Your reliable and resilient nature is just as convincing as your service-oriented and forward-looking way of working.

What we offer

  • More energy for your life: Through mobile working, flexible work models and part-time options, you can actively shape your working hours and organize them in the way that best suits your life. For example, you can purchase up to 2 additional weeks of vacation and work part-time at all levels. Job sharing/-splitting and unpaid leave are of course also possible with us.
  • Travel sustainably and at a reduced cost: With the mobility contribution of up to 600 francs per year, you can travel affordably and sustainably – whether you want to cycle through Zürich or explore Switzerland by train.
  • An investment in your future: We not only place importance on paying fair and transparent salaries, but also ensure that more of your salary remains in your pocket. We cover 60% of the savings contributions and also offer an extra-mandatory interest rate on the capital you have saved.
  • A boost of energy for your leisure time: We pay 100 francs into your Lunch-Check card every month. In addition, you benefit each year from further Fringe Benefits worth 150 francs and attractive mobile phone subscriptions.
  • Development and further education: With our talent management, we specifically promote your development – through a wide range of internal and external further education opportunities as well as exciting development opportunities.
  • Meaningful work: Sustainability and meaningful work are not just words to us: Help shape Zürich and the future with us. Together with us, unleash energy for a good tomorrow.
