Full description

For the VOI management team, we are looking for an ambitious and goal-oriented leader. In franchise partner management, you accompany our partners from evaluation to successful operational cooperation and actively contribute to the further development of the Migros supermarket as part of the Supermarket Directorate.

Mansioni

  • Holistic support and empowerment of franchise partners with focus on opening phase and early years of operation
  • Ensuring system and quality standards
  • Strategic management of the network
  • Product and loss responsibility as well as investment planning
  • Expansion and conversions
  • Communication and interface work
  • Strategic development of the VOI concept within the given guidelines

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience in the required area: as well as several years of management experience
  • Completed degree (uni/ETH, FH, HF): in economics or equivalent
  • In-depth experience in supporting partners in the franchise system or branched retail businesses
  • Very good market knowledge of the national retail industry
  • Strong communication skills coupled with a high level of performance skills as well as negotiating skills and assertiveness
  • Strong entrepreneurial and networked thinking and acting
  • Very good analytical and conceptual skills as well as experience in project management
  • Good self-organization and structured working methods
  • Willingness to travel throughout the economic area of the Migros Zurich Cooperative
  • in German (negotiation certain)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Flexible working hours: reconciling private and professional requirements
  • Childcare: In the in-house crèche "Wirbelschloss"
  • Mobility: With the one-off leisure bonus per year of max. CHF 500.– we participate in your public transport subscription
  • Occupational pension provision: Excellent pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Social benefits: Fair social benefits
  • Handy subscription: You can look forward to special conditions for the Handy subscription
  • Food: Discount in the staff restaurant
  • Migros Bank: Preferred interest on private and investment account, free account management, discounted mortgages and more
  • Cumulus Points: You get additional Cumulus Points when shopping
  • Employee offers: Up to 1,000 CHF per year (depending on your workload) for your recovery and further development – usable for fitness subscriptions, courses, health offers or a subsidy for public transport.
  • Holidays: You benefit from 5 weeks of holidays per year
  • Leisure and cultural offers: You can benefit from discounted tickets at selected cultural events
  • Sports and fitness offers: You can get a total free time bonus of max once a year. CHF 1000.– e.g. redeem for an annual subscription to the M-Fitnesscenter
  • Design and decision-making room: Realize your innovative ideas
  • Social and personnel consultancy: Internal social consultancy
