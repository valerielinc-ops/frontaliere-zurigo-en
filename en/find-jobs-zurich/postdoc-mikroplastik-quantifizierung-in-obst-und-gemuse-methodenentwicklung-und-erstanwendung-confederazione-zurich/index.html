Full description

  • Development of appropriate sample preparation workflows for isolation of microplastics from different fruit and vegetable matrixes, based on established internal protocols for extraction and detection in complex environmental samples using FPA-μ-FTIR or QCL-based IR imaging

  • Reporting to authorities, stakeholders and science at national and international meetings and conferences through presentations, reports and publications
  • PhD in Chemistry, Environmental Sciences, Materials Sciences or a Related Field
  • In-depth knowledge in polymer chemistry and analytical environmental chemistry, knowledge in fruit and vegetable cultivation and food safety
  • Practical experience in sampling, sample preparation and application of state-of-the-art analytical equipment to quantify microplastics in environmental compartments
  • Experience in programming and data analysis
  • Scientific curiosity and pronounced observational ability in experiments
  • Knowledge of two official languages and English
