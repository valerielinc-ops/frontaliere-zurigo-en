Would you like to work as a pediatrician in Switzerland and combine your professional development with a high quality of life? Medimeo accompanies you personally in this step. We support you in the recognition process, advise you on the Swiss labour market and the specific work situation and show you which possibilities fit your personal and professional situation. You work in an established and growing group practice for pediatrics and adolescent medicine with several locations in German-speaking Switzerland. You combine the independence of an outpatient practice with the advantages of a larger network. In everyday life, you benefit from the exchange with medical colleagues, internal medical meetings and further training. Experienced assistance teams and a professional practice administration relieve you of the burden organizationally and create more space for the medical care of your patients. Depending on your personal situation, you will have several places of work in Eastern Switzerland and the Mittelland as well as different commutes.
