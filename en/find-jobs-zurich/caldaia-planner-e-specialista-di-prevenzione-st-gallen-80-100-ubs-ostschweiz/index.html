Key responsibilities Are you a proactive, independent and entrepreneurial person? Does an activity in the field of prevention attract you with a high level of personal responsibility and entrepreneurial freedom? Would you like to build this area together with us in the Basel region? Then you are right with us!

The main responsibilities of this position include

  • As a specialist, you create the provision planning for our customers and support our customer consultants
  • As a person responsible for personal banking in the Eastern Switzerland region, you develop holistic solutions for our customers, identify additional potential and make recommendations for action for our customer advisors.
  • Close cooperation with customer consultants, as well as managers and market area managers in the Eastern Switzerland region
  • Planning, organization and participation in customer events, including acting as a speaker, as well as a lively exchange with all relevant stakeholders
  • Coaching of employees in their further development around the topic of prevention

Our goal is also to be the first choice for our private customers in Switzerland. Become part of our team and discover your opportunities at one of the world’s leading financial institutions with strong Swiss roots.

Your skills and experience

  • Several years of professional experience in a bank or insurance company as a customer consultant with in-depth knowledge in the field of pension provision
  • Completed training as a financial planner
  • Willingness to travel within our Eastern Switzerland region to our customers and our offices
  • analytical working methods and strong interest in prevention and financial products
  • results-oriented, committed and focused on the essentials
  • Passionate networker and fun in customer consulting
  • Enjoyment of continuous further education and a self-assured appearance
  • fluent German as well as other national languages and/or English of advantage
