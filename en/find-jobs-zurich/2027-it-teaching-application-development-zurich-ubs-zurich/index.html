Full description

The role Are you interested in technology, apps or digital solutions? Do you want to understand how programs are created that are used by many people every day?

Then our IT teaching “Application Development” is just right for you.

We offer you

  • Personal support by experienced practical trainers
  • A practical education where you learn step by step
  • Exciting projects where you work from the beginning

In the field of application development, you develop software solutions and ensure that programs and apps work reliably and securely. You fix errors and improve existing applications on an ongoing basis. You will learn programming languages such as Java, Python and C#, test applications and work closely with other developers in the team.

Your education What is teaching at UBS? We offer the perfect mix of practice and theory. In the UBS Training Center, you learn everything you need to become a real pro in the technology field during your first year. This includes not only practical experience, but also visiting the vocational school, where you deepen your knowledge one or two days a week. In the following three years, you will work in international teams on exciting IT projects for UBS and thus for our customers. In addition to a great team spirit, you will experience up close how your projects are put into reality and deliver real results.

At the end of your teaching you will receive your Federal Certificate of Ability (EFZ).

After that, there are many ways open to you

  • Entry as an IT specialist
  • Deepening your knowledge in our two-year young talent programme

Your team During your teaching, you are part of a group of learners with whom you regularly exchange ideas and work together on new topics. You support each other, learn from each other and grow together as a team. This often creates friendships that persist beyond teaching.

In your team, you actively shape the future. Together, we integrate artificial intelligence (AI) into our digital solutions and processes to drive contemporary innovation, work more efficiently and make our everyday life easier. You will learn how AI makes our digital products smarter and how you can contribute to further development yourself. Be part of our team and be the change you want to see.

Your knowledge and experience

  • Completion of the highest elementary school level
  • Good basics in mathematics, German and English
  • You burn for new trends and technologies
  • Joy of learning and working in a team

Want to get it? Then simply apply online here. On this website you will also find further details about teaching and the application process.
