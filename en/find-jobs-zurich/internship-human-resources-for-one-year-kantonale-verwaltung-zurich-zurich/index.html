Full description

Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Public Prosecutor's Office Internship in Human Resources (for one year) 80 - 100% The Zurich public prosecutor's office is responsible for prosecuting crimes committed by adults in the Canton of Zurich. It employs around 500 people in nine offices and the public prosecutor's office. The “Human Resources and Training” department in the Attorney General’s Office is responsible for the entire HR life cycle and professional support for human resources work in the official offices. As of October 1, 2026 or by arrangement, we are offering a Human Resources internship (80-100%, for one year). These are your tasks:

  • You work on projects, e.g. on the topics of further training, employee surveys or employer marketing
  • You will be responsible for planning the internal training program as well as the organizational support of trainers and participants
  • You will be involved in overarching HR tasks, e.g. organizing events and workshops or maintaining the HR intranet. You fit in with us with the following profile:
  • At least a bachelor's degree, ideally in the areas of business administration, human resources or psychology
  • Initial professional experience in the HR environment and interest in a wide range of tasks from administrative and organizational tasks to project work
  • Drive and joy in learning as well as quick comprehension and high solution orientation
  • Open, flexible and uncomplicated personality with a strong team spirit. This is what we offer:
  • An ideal introduction to the HR world with a diverse range of tasks and an independent way of working
  • A pleasant working environment in which teamwork is valued
  • An attractive workplace in the police and justice center in Zurich, just a few minutes from Hardbrücke train station
  • If necessary, we are open to accompanying a master's thesis in the HR area. Application Marion Scharpf, Head of Human Resources and Training, ( marion.scharpf@ji.zh.ch ), will be happy to provide you with further information. Please send your complete documents electronically via the job portal. We look forward to your application!

Workload: 80-100%
