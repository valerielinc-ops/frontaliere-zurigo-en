To the job overview Apply for Secretarial employee Radiology reception (usually 3 days per week (60%), during approx. 6 weeks of the year 100% due to holiday absences) 70% Spital Affoltern AG - Affoltern am Albis Employees by appointment Your tasks As the central contact person for our radiology team, you will ensure a smooth organizational process and contribute significantly to optimal patient care at.

  • Coordination and scheduling of all radiological examinations (MRI, CT and X-rays)
  • Preparation of examination registrations for the following day including recording in the RIS, appointment confirmations and reminder telephone calls
  • Processing of incoming registrations and appointments with referring physicians
  • Arrangement and support of patients for various examinations
  • Friendly reception and administrative reception of our patients
  • Processing of inquiries from insurance companies, patients and external institutions
  • Professional telephone and e-mail correspondence
  • Sending of Finding reports and medical documents
  • Responsibility for material control and ordering
  • Processing daily mail and general administrative tasks Your profile
  • Completed basic commercial training with further training as a medical secretary or training as a medical practice assistant (MPA)
  • Professional experience in a radiological environment is an advantage
  • Confident use of common MS Office applications
  • Knowledge of the Medavis and/or Medidata systems is a plus
  • Fluent written and spoken German skills
  • Knowledge of English is an advantage
  • Self-employed, structured and efficient way of working
  • High service orientation and a strong sense of responsibility
  • Team player mentality combined with flexibility and initiative
  • Willingness to increase the workload depending on the situation during holiday absences Our culture – your benefits “Bi öis kännt mer no”: For us it is family and personal. As part of the Affoltern Hospital team, you also benefit from attractive employment conditions and numerous Fringe benefits: Joker day, shopping benefits and two subscriptions for free entry to the FORTYSEVEN wellness spa are just a few examples of our Fringe benefits.
