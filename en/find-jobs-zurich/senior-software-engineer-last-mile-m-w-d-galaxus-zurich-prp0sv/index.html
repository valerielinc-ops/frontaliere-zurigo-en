Full description

For the last mile, we are strengthening our product development team. The last mile is the interface between our warehouses and our customers: it starts in the checkout and extends to ramp and yard management, dangerous goods and customs processes, to tracking of shipments and tracking of losses and transport damage. If something goes wrong there, customers notice it immediately.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Headquarters, Pfingstweidstrasse 60b, 8005 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You build the last mile services and you run them too. From carrier connection to shipment tracking to shipment loss detection, what your team builds is also responsible for operations
  • Grown structures you modernize in the ongoing shipping. In small steps, secured by tests and measured values without the shipping chain standing still
  • Connecting external systems is part of your everyday life. Shipping service providers and authorities rarely provide clean interfaces, so you build them fault-tolerant, idempotent and observable
  • You shape the engineering culture in the team. We continuously develop reviews, API guidelines, monitoring and documentation, you bring your experience and set standards instead of just adopting specifications
  • Together with product owner, team and architecture you cut the work. You make technical considerations and their consequences transparent, also towards logistics, operations and external partners

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: As a software engineer – part of which is responsible for solutions that run productively and under load
  • Completed studies (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): In computer science (ETH/Uni/FH) or equivalent practical work experience
  • In-depth knowledge of C# and .NET, SQL and experience with distributed, high-throughput event-based systems
  • Experience with the connection of external partner systems and their interfaces, ideally in the shipping or logistics environment
  • Secure handling of grown code and the sense to modernize it incrementally without disturbing the current shipping
  • The operation is considered by you from the beginning, from monitoring and alerting to behavior in peak times
  • AI as an integral part of analysis, conception, implementation and testing – no buzzword
  • Clear, situational communication, also towards logistics, operations and external partner companies
  • Taking calculated risks to create value and drive growth
  • The willingness to question “as it has always been done” and propose more efficient, automated or effective solutions
  • in German (negotiation confident)
  • English (flowing)
