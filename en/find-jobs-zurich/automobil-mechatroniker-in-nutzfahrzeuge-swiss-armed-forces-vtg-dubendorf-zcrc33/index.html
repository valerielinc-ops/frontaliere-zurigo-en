Full description

Put to the Point

Are you a technology freak, enjoy military and special vehicles and also want to benefit from a pronounced work-life balance in your job? Then strengthen our team, because together we contribute to making our special and wheeled vehicles of the Swiss Army and Air Force ready for use and functional.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-18390
  • Place of work: Military airfield, 8600 Dübendorf

You can make this contribution

  • Repair, diagnosis and adjustment of engines, drive assemblies, brakes, steering, chassis, sub-assemblies and safety and comfort electronics
  • Check and repair defects and repairs on special and wheeled vehicles
  • Carry out maintenance and deadline work on special and wheeled vehicles
  • Provide technical support for material transfers and material returns with regard to driving safety, accident prevention, maintenance, cleaning and vehicle inventory in our own area
  • Capture defect messages and spare parts in the SAP system
  • Army personnel (Ada)

This makes you unique

  • Completed vocational training as an automotive mechatronics specialist, specialisation in commercial vehicles or related professions
  • Driving licence of the Kat C, Kat CE and SUVA recognized forklift
  • Self-employed, forward working methods, with good organizational skills
  • Good comprehension of technical relationships and analytical ability
  • Pronounced service concept in contact with and in advising customers, for example the troop

We offer this

  • We are committed to the success model Switzerland and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom The logistics base of the Swiss Army (LBA) is there for the force. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss army and manages the materials, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss army with its approximately 3'500 employees and 12,000 militia members. Whether in the workshop or in the office, inside or outside: The LBA offers a wide range of workplaces throughout Switzerland. We have something for everyone.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.

Additional information

Employing the Swiss Army makes sense and brings many advantages. Convince yourself of Army.ch/advantages. Offers from recruiters are not taken into account.

Women are still underrepresented in our administrative unit. Your applications are therefore particularly welcome.
