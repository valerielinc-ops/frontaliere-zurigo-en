Full description

26.10.2026 Affoltern on the Albis

Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Kelleramt-Albis, a successful and locally oriented bank with headquarters in Oberlunkhofen and headquarters in Affoltern am Albis, we are looking for a personality as head of the Affoltern am Albis office.

What do you expect?

  • They represent our bank in Affoltern on the Albis and the region. You actively maintain your network and are involved in customer events, market development measures and campaigns.
  • You manage the office Affoltern am Albis and lead a team of individual customer consultants.
  • You develop your employees further and promote a performance-oriented and appreciative leadership culture.
  • You look after your own customer portfolio and accompany your customers in the topics of investing, provisioning and financing. Through targeted acquisition of new customers, you expand your customer base.

What do you bring?

  • They are at home in our region and ideally rooted in the Mauliamt.
  • They enjoy proactive customer contact and are willing to represent our bank to the outside world.
  • They are authentic, have good communication skills and a high level of social competence.
  • They have completed banking education as well as higher bank-specific training.
  • You have several years of experience in customer consulting in the areas of investment, pensions and financing.
  • You have proven experience in managing employees.

You enjoy contact with people and like to take responsibility? I look forward to your application documents. Manuel Huber Member of the bank management Why Raiffeisen?

We maintain a family environment

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

We give space for personal initiative and participation

We invite our employees to actively participate in their work and to take on responsibility. This creates varied tasks that create real added value – for everyone who wants to make a difference.

We promote personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.

We offer attractive conditions and social benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages in banking products, various purchase discounts at third parties and an above-average retirement provision.
