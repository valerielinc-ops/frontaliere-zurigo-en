Full description

The canteen of Institut Minerva Zürich AG offers 70 seats. The offer ranges from break food to sandwiches, snacks and daily changing menus at the self-service buffet. True to the ZFV philosophy, menus with regionally and ecologically produced and fairly traded food are offered. Are you our new team member who supports us as a cafeteria employee: in immediately or by arrangement? You usually work Tuesdays to Thursdays from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. in a 20% hourly allowance. During school holidays, the company remains closed. Your task menu:

  • Welcome and advice of guests at the coffee bar
  • Sale of hot and cold snacks and baked goods
  • Ensuring an attractive presentation of goods
  • Creating the Mise en place
  • Collaboration at caterings and special events
  • Completion of general clean-up and cleaning work
  • Implementation of ZFV quality and service standards
  • Compliance and implementation of the hygiene and occupational safety guidelines Your recipe for success:
  • First practical experience in gastronomy
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Warm, winning and empathetic
  • Enjoyment of pleasure
  • This is what we offer you: We create teams that support each other and in which you are promoted. We work together and focus on everything that does good. Among other things, we attach great importance to wage transparency and equal pay. For this position, we offer an annual wage of between CHF 52'000 and CHF 60'000 (including 13th monthly wage), depending on your experience and qualifications. If you want to share your passion for gastronomy with us, we look forward to your complete online application.
