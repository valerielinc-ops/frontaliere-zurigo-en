Full description

The Management Fire Protection Skills Centre MES - Migros Engineering Solutions is the focal point for all fire protection issues within the Migros community. He is actively involved in the development of the subject, both within and outside the Migros community.

Luogo di lavoro: The Migros Federation of Cooperatives, Limmatplatz Zürich, Limmatstrasse 152, 8005 Zürich

Important remarks

Responsibility for many projects in Romandie and member of a Zurich-based team

Mansioni

  • Advice and support to Migros Group companies on fire protection for the implementation of efficient and cost-effective fire protection measures
  • Fire protection services in projects (design, planning, optimization, control, quality assurance)
  • Guidance and support of specialist planners and specialists involved in the planning and construction process in the field of fire protection
  • Internal training of Migros Group staff in the application of internal fire protection standards
  • Representation of Migros' interests as contractor/operator with authorities and project partners

Requirements

  • Work experience of several years: in the field of fire protection in accompanying construction projects
  • Completed studies (UK/EFP, HES, ES): In the field of engineering or architecture with additional training as fire protection expert VKF
  • Wide project experience in the role of AEAI expert or fire protection speclist AEAI
  • Provision to use your expertise in construction projects, as well as in building life cycle management for the entire portfolio
  • Directorate of negotiations with fire protection authorities and project stakeholders
  • Diplomatic behaviour and economic thinking for the development and implementation of optimal solutions
  • Sociability and communication capacity based on facts and analysis
  • French (Very good mastery)
  • German Switzerland (Good Master's)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Modern infrastructure: Various work areas and modern co-working spaces with state-of-the-art equipment
  • Individual Responsibility and Attitude: Various areas of competence with great autonomy
  • Club schools/Recreation centres: 70% discount on Migros Club School offers, 30% discount on Fitness-/Wellness-/Aquaparks
  • Cumulus Points: Additional Cumulus Points in all Migros stores and various subsidiaries
  • Insurance: Up to 25% discount on various insurances - car, private and household civil liability, legal protection, etc.
  • Mobile and flexible work model: Hybrid work - temporal and spatial flexibility in carrying out tasks
