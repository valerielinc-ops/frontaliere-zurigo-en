Full description

Region: Agglomeration Zug – Lucerne – Zurich, incl. Knonaueramt’

localsearch is the leading marketing and advertising partner for Swiss SMEs. We make customers successful in the digital world.

Is selling your passion? Is your heart beating for digital marketing? Do you like to travel and immerse yourself in the SME world? Do you want to influence your own earnings? Then you are exactly right here!

We are looking for you as a sales talent in the field service

You will visit both existing SME customers and potential new customers and present them with customized digital marketing solutions. Your goal is to create sustainable added value and thus make SMEs digitally successful.

Skills and requirements

This is what you bring with you to be successful with us

  • The pleasure of selling and consulting: You burn for personal and telephone customer contact and attach great importance to really understanding our customers.
  • Customer orientation: You build trusting relationships with your customers and successfully explain the benefits of our online marketing solutions.
  • Success orientation: Your self-motivation and ambition are your success factors. Even in turbulent times, you remain focused and purposeful.
  • Communicative : You speak fluent German and master the common means of communication.
  • Radiance: You have humor and like to show your personality.
  • Mobility: You have a valid driver’s license (cat. B) and your own car, so you can visit our customers reliably on site.
  • Responsible action: Your appearance is professional and your reputation is impeccable (reputable)
  • Flexibility: Part-time work is possible in principle, but only from the second month of employment. In the first month, participation in the basic training at our headquarters in Zurich is required.

This is what we offer you

This is why you want to become part of localsearch

  • Teamwork: You are integrated into a lively team that supports each other and meets for regular meetings.
  • Own sales area: You will be assigned an area with existing SME customers and potential new customers.
  • Marketing solutions: You have a varied range of products from digital marketing at your disposal.
  • Working independently: You take responsibility for your tasks, plan your customer appointments independently and act with a high degree of initiative.
  • Comprehensive training and further development: You start with several weeks of training and receive regular support and training in the area of sales and products.
  • An attractive provisioning model: Your success is immediately rewarded – every good advice that leads to an assignment determines your income.
  • Flat-rate expenses: Your own vehicle will bring you safely to the customer appointment and we will compensate you for this in a timely manner.

Contact and Application

For professional questions you can contact our Sales Manager Dursun Birinci directly: +41 79 851 40 43.

We look forward to meeting you!
