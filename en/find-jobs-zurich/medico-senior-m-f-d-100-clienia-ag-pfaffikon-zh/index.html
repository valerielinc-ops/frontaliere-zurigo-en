For our private station Panorama D in the Centre for Old Age Psychiatry and Privé we are looking for 1. March 2027 or by arrangement one or

Their tasks

  • Independent medical director of the private ward, where patients of a wide range of age and diagnosis are treated with a focus on "50 Plus and Privé"
  • Interprofessional tandem tour of the station together with the station management
  • Responsibility for the diagnosis, therapy, treatment and advice of primarily additional insured patients

Your profile

  • You are a specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy and have experience as a manager
  • Enjoy working with people of a wide age range and in management tasks
  • Sound psychiatric and psychotherapeutic knowledge
  • Openness to business issues
  • Interested in a longer-term perspective as a cadre physician in our clinic

  • The agglomerations St. Gallen, Winterthur, Zurich and Constance are very easy to reach
