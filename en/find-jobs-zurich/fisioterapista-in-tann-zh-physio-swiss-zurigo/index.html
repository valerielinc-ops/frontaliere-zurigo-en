Our practice has been successfully established at its Uster location for several years. Due to our continuous growth, we opened a second location in Tann (Florastrasse 16, 8632 Tann ZH) in October 2026. To build up this new practice location, we are looking for committed and motivated employees who would like to actively help shape this exciting development and build something new together with us. Do you love the varied work with patients of all ages and a wide range of diagnoses, as well as interacting with friendly colleagues? Are you motivated, positive, professionally competent, responsible and a team player? Would you like to play an active role in the company's growth and development phase? Then you are exactly who we are looking for!

  • We offer state-of-the-art working conditions, flexible working hours and newly renovated practice premises near Rüti railway station, as well as a collegial working atmosphere in a positive team.
  • Can you also identify with PSI (professional, personable and individualized treatment of patients) and are you looking for a new challenge? Then apply to us!
  • P.S. Your CV is all we need; you can skip the cover letter. As you can see, we are completely straightforward.
