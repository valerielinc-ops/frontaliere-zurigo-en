Full description

As a subject matter specialist, you ensure that the network information systems are operated efficiently and meet users' requirements. You are the link between the business and IT.

Tasks

  • You play a key role in the further development of our network information systems and assume partial responsibility in the process.
  • When drawing up concepts and guidelines, you think across disciplines and with foresight.
  • With your quality awareness, you shape the data capture processes and thus ensure high data quality.
  • In your role, you represent the users of our systems to IT.
  • You issue assignments to IT, monitor implementation and are responsible for acceptance, including testing.

Profile

  • You have completed an apprenticeship as a geomatics technician or drafter.
  • You have successfully completed further training at FA/HF/FH level (for example, an HF in computer science or a geomatics technician FA qualification).
  • You have several years of professional experience in the field of GIS and CAD systems—not merely as a user.
  • You can grasp topics holistically, and your way of working is clearly structured.
  • Collaboration and communication across various interfaces come easily to you and are something you enjoy.

What we offer

  • More energy for your life: Through mobile working, flexible work models and part-time options, you can actively shape your working hours and arrange them in the way that best suits your life. For example, you can purchase up to 2 additional weeks of holiday and work part-time at all levels. Job sharing/-splitting and unpaid leave are of course also possible with us.
  • Sustainable and affordable travel: With the mobility allowance of up to 600 francs per year, you can travel affordably and sustainably—whether you want to cycle through Zurich or explore Switzerland by train.
  • An investment in your future: We not only place value on paying fair and transparent salaries, but also ensure that more of your salary remains for you. We cover 60% of the savings contributions and also offer an interest rate above the statutory minimum on the capital saved.
  • An energy boost for your free time: We pay 100 francs per month into your Lunch-Check card. In addition, every year you benefit from further fringe benefits worth 150 francs and attractive mobile phone plans.
  • Development and continuing education: With our talent management, we specifically promote your development—through a wide range of internal and external continuing-education opportunities as well as exciting development opportunities.
  • Meaningful work: Sustainability and purpose-driven work are more than just words to us: Help shape Zurich and the future with us. Together with us, unleash energy for a better tomorrow.
