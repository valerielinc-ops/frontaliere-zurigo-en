As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

With us you have Saturday and Sunday guaranteed free – as well as all holidays. In addition, we offer company holidays in the summer as well as over Christmas and New Year. This leaves you more room for rest, family and personal plans. Become part of our team and enjoy a balanced work-life balance!

YOUR TASKS

  • Instrumentation and service in operations of various disciplines
  • Implementation of all preparatory and follow-up operations
  • Professional preparation and operation of the necessary equipment, instruments and sterile goods
  • Careful documentation of all nursing activities including performance recording
  • Assistance in ensuring a smooth daily routine in the operating room

Your profile

  • Training as a specialist surgical technique HF or Dipl. Medical professional FA OP
  • Strong service orientation and high social competence as well as customer orientation
  • Enjoying the interdisciplinary cooperation with patients, doctors and employees

You can expect a motivated, committed and multi-professional team in an advanced working environment. This is characterized by high quality of care, independence, flexibility and a sense of responsibility. We also offer our employees generous social benefits, sports offers, special insurance conditions and further education opportunities as part of our program.
