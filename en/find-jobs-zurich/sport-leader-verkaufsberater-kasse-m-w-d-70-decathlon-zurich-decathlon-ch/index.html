Full description

Mission Your future playing field: Decathlon Zurich You will become part of our well-established team at the Decathlon Store in Selnau, Zurich. Here you will meet a highly motivated team of passionate athletes who equip locals and tourists with the perfect equipment every day. Become part of a Swiss success story Decathlon Switzerland is growing rapidly: Since the start of 2017 in Neuchâtel, we have expanded our network to around 53 sales outlets. And we continue to expand – especially in German-speaking Switzerland. For you, this means a crisis-proof workplace with real, nationwide career opportunities. Why this job is perfect for you You are not looking for a classic 08/15 job, but want to share your passion for sports from morning to evening? You love the action on the sales floor and want to see your clientele break up with a smile? Your 3 biggest advantages with us:

  • In the middle of the action: Your everyday life takes place 100% on the sales area in Zurich, Selnau. You are the face of Decathlon, advising beginners as well as professionals and building a real local community.
  • Trading rethought: You don’t just “sell.” You drive sustainable circular models – from material rental to the buy-back of used goods (Second Life) to smart digital orders. Your daily action: The tasks
  • Enthusiastic advice: You listen, analyze the needs and find the perfect equipment – directly in the store or via our digital tools.
  • A store to feel good: You provide a complete presentation of goods ("facing") and make your department dynamically and seasonally attractive.
  • Efficient goods management: From incoming goods to placement on the shelf, you can actively participate and prepare online orders (Click & Collect) quickly.
  • Circular economy: You inspire customers for our rental and second-life offers. This job is NOTHING for you if...
  • You prefer to sit quietly behind a screen all day.
  • It is difficult for you to approach people openly and actively.
  • You think unpacking pallets and racking up shelves is “not your job.”
  • You would rather have free Saturdays.
  • You are looking for rigid, unchangeable working hours (we plan your bets very fair 6 weeks in advance, but work in the flexible rhythm of sports retail). Profile
  • Athlete DNA: You are active in sports and are happy to share your passion with our customers.
  • Maker mentality: You are motivated, curious and eager to learn new things every day and develop yourself.
  • S ervice-Herz & Organisational Talent: You enjoy direct customer contact and keep an overview even on lively days – be it at the checkouts/self-checkouts, at Click & Collect, at returns or at the Infodesk (experience in the checkout or customer service area is a clear plus).
  • Language competence: You communicate perfectly in German (understanding Swiss German is a big plus). If you can additionally communicate in French or English, this is a great bonus. This is what we offer you (at 100% Pensum)
  • Attractive wage & bonus: A fixed gross salary between CHF 4'100 and CHF 5'000 (disbursed in 13 monthly wages) plus a monthly team bonus (up to 10% of the salary) and an annual profit share.
  • Your sports budget: We give you CHF 225 per year for your sports or fitness subscription and you get 25% off all Decathlon products (even an additional 25% off sustainable mobility for your commute).
  • Recovery: 5 weeks of paid vacation per year.
  • Success Sharing: The opportunity to invest once a year in high-performing Decathlon employee stocks.
  • Real career: internal development is lived with us – many of our current managers have started on the sales floor themselves! The application process: Fast & uncomplicated
  • Application: No long letter of motivation necessary! Just send us your CV and answer a few short questions.
  • Personality Check: You will receive a link to a 30-minute meeting questionnaire (AssessFirst). Don’t worry: we don’t filter out AI – we read every application personally! You get your results immediately as cool feedback for yourself.
  • Conversation in the Store: We meet directly in the Store to talk about your future playing field and see if the chemistry is right. Practical information:
  • Job: By immediate or by agreement
  • Availability: early/late shift, weekend
