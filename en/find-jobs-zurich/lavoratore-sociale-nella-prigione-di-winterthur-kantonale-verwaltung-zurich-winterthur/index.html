Full description

Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Interior Prisons and Reintegration Zurich Social worker in Winterthur Prison 60 - 80% Prison and Reintegration (JuWe) is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as the implementation of the various types of detention as part of the Directorate of Justice and Interior of the Canton of Zurich. For our 1,400 employees from around 40 professions, the successful social reintegration of people who have committed crimes and the prevention of recidivism are of particular importance. The Zurich Detention Center (UGZ) – one of eight JuWe detention centers – currently operates seven prisons for provisional arrest, pre-trial and security detention. As part of the Zurich Prisons of Detention (UGZ), Winterthur Prison (GFW) assumes central tasks in the area of detention without judgment. The focus is on pre-trial detention and security detention as well as on the reception of persons in provisional detention. A total of 101 detention places are available for this purpose. For the Department of Social Service in Winterthur Prison, we are looking for a responsible, dynamic and capable person as of 1 October 2026. Your tasks

  • Individual and self-responsible case work
  • Resource-oriented advice and care of detainees in relevant areas of life such as housing, finances and social security
  • Planning and coordination of residency, reintegration and transition management, (e.g. connection to economic social assistance, placement to employment partners and external bodies, transfer to the penal and measures execution)
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with internal bodies such as medical service, supervisory and care staff, psychiatric psychology service, as well as with external bodies such as criminal investigation authorities, probation and enforcement services, social services, social insurance, KESB etc.
  • Further development of the social service sector in custody
  • Transfer of the pilot project "Model test in custody Canton of Zurich / Canton of Bern" into regular operation Your profile
  • Bachelor’s degree in social work or comparable studies
  • Ideally, professional experience in law enforcement or dealing with delinquent persons
  • Self-employed, reliable, responsible and careful work
  • High social skills and psychological resilience as well as empathy and assertiveness
  • Flexibility and enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Stylistic German in word and writing, knowledge of other languages of advantage, good IT user knowledge
  • Flawless reputation and our offer
  • An exciting, versatile activity in an exceptional environment
  • A motivated and committed team
  • Independent work in the assigned area and cross-sectoral co-responsibility
  • Regular working hours
