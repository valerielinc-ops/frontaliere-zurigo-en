Full description

Become part of our team! In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a social pedagogue for the school year 2026/27 for the 1:1 accompaniment of a primary school child in the first grade in the supplementary school care in the ISR setting (autism spectrum) on a temporary basis. .The assignment covers about 67% and takes place during schooling five days a week in the nurseries. Several missions are also planned during school holidays.

Tasks

  • 1:1 accompaniment of a student in the context of supplementary school care
  • individual support of the child in day-care as well as in social, emotional and everyday practical situations
  • Supporting integration into the group and promoting social participation, taking into account the individual needs of the child
  • Accompanying meals, free play, leisure and exercise activities
  • Support for the development of self-employment and social skills
  • Appropriate parental work
  • Close cooperation with the support team, the Hortleitung and the specialist departments involved

Profile

  • Experience working with primary school-age children and ideally already knowledge of the autism spectrum is beneficial
  • high level of social competence and an appreciative and sensitive approach to children
  • Patience, resilience and the ability to act calmly and structured even in demanding situations
  • Independent and self-organized work
  • Motivated, team-capable, open, resilient personality with a positive human image
  • High flexibility and solution-oriented basic attitude in cooperation
  • degree as social pedagogue FH or HFS; Social worker FH or HFS; director; kindergarten teacher
  • Primary school teacher (CH); university degree with major in social education, pedagogy or educational sciences
  • Child educator or childhood educator HF
  • For applications with a foreign diploma / certificate, a Swiss diploma recognition must be attached

We offer

  • the possibility of individually accompanying and promoting a child with special support needs
  • a supportive, appreciative and interdisciplinary working environment
  • an experienced, committed and humorous care team
  • well-organised and proven cross-hort cooperation
  • varied, interesting and independent activity in a versatile, changing field of activity of the school
  • Possibility to develop and contribute your own ideas within the framework of the day school
  • Competent support from the management
