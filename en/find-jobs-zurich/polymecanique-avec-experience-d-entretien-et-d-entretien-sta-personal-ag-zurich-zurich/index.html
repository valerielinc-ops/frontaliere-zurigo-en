Full description

Activities

  • You organize and carry out maintenance and repair work on machines, plants and technical facilities at the Zurich and St. Gallen locations.
  • You repair problems and carry out unplanned repairs independently in the current production operation.
  • You support machine revisions at our international production sites in Austria and Hungary (about 3 assignments of 2 weeks per year).
  • You work conventionally on turning, milling, drilling and grinding machines and take on various welding and manual work.
  • You plan, coordinate and carry out preventive maintenance measures.
  • You procure spare parts, partly manufacture them yourself and manage the spare parts warehouse.
  • You actively contribute your technical know-how to projects and support them in their implementation.

Requirements

  • Completed training as a machine or polymechanic.
  • In-depth knowledge in assembly technology, pneumatics and conventional production.
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in an industrial environment.
  • Confident knowledge of German in words and writing.
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method.
  • High load capacity, flexibility and enjoyment of varied tasks.
  • Readiness for occasional missions abroad.

  • Flexible working hours thanks to the flextime model.
  • Centrally located workplace in Zurich with excellent public transport connections.
  • Free parking directly on site.
  • Numerous food options in the immediate vicinity.
  • A collaborative team that values cooperation and mutual support.
