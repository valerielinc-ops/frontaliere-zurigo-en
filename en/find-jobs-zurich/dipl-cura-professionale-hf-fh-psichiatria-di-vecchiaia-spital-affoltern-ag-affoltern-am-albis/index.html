Main responsibilities

  • Your responsibilities
  • Reference personnel work and nursing case management: You plan, control and evaluate the care process of the patients assigned to you.
  • Shift responsibility: You coordinate the shift professionally, set priorities and ensure a clear distribution of tasks and delegation within the care team.
  • Psychiatric care: You assess the mental state, recognize changes at an early stage and act professionally and appropriately in crises and challenging situations.
  • Old-age psychiatric care: They accompany people with different clinical conditions such as dementia, delirium, depression, dependency diseases or psychosis as well as psychiatric and somatic multimorbidity.
  • Family work: They advise and accompany relatives and involve them appropriately in the treatment process.
  • Interprofessional collaboration: You bring your nursing expertise to visits and case discussions and actively participate in setting and reviewing treatment goals.
  • Exit and transition management: Together with the treatment team, you design viable follow-up solutions, working with Spitex, homes, referees and other external partners.
  • You create a trusting and appreciative atmosphere in which our patients feel safe, taken seriously and professionally accompanied.
