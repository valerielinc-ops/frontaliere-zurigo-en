Full description

For the production AEMP at the Triemli site, we are looking for a committed and team-oriented personality immediately or by agreement.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for the professional and appropriate preparation of medical products for our internal and external customers.
  • You organize the day-to-day business, are responsible for the technical guidance and training of the employees and ensure the quality of the medical device preparation taking into account the hygiene guidelines and regulations.
  • You bring experience in lending and master data management and actively support their maintenance and organization.
  • You actively support quality management and participate in the continuous optimization of processes.
  • You take on delegated tasks from the team management and represent them in their absence.

Profile

  • Completed vocational training as medical device technologist / medical device technologist EFZ, specialist training Technical sterilization assistant II, dental assistant EFZ or Med. Practical assistant* in EFZ
  • Several years of professional experience in a large ZSVA/AEMP
  • Very good MS-Office user knowledge as well as reliable negotiation knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Target-oriented and innovative personality with enforcement and organizational talent
  • Readiness for weekend and shift work as well as interest and pleasure in cross-location assignments

We offer

We offer you more than just a job! Our city hospital world is characterized by an open du-culture, an uncomplicated, personal togetherness and the necessary touch of humor in everyday life. As a team of over 120 professional groups, we work every day in very different ways for the well-being of our patients. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions and excellent social benefits from the city of Zurich.

About us

With over 36'000 inpatients and around 455,000 outpatients per year, the Stadtspital Zürich ranks among the top 10 Swiss hospitals. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,500 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
