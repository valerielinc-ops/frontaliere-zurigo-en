Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Career Advice Employees/in Administration kjz Horgen 70% Good for life – that is our motto. As part of the Education Directorate of the Canton of Zurich, the Office for Youth and Vocational Advice is responsible for the offers in the field of child and youth welfare and vocational guidance. Our work focuses on children, young people and families. We work for them every day with a lot of heart and commitment. For the Kinder- und Jugendhilfezentrum (kjz) Horgen we are looking for an employee / administration immediately or by agreement. Their tasks

  • Social work and maternal/father counselling services
  • Redacting demanding reports and applications as well as submitting applications to foundations
  • Cooperation in the organisation of events
  • Telephone and personal contact with clients and other internal and external offices
  • Correspondence and general secretarial work Your profile
  • Completed commercial basic education EFZ (profile E or M mandatory) and at least two years of professional experience
  • Style-confident knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Service-oriented, structured and efficient working methods
  • Resilient and sensitive in dealing with people in difficult situations
  • Humorous and team-oriented
  • Monday and Thursday are mandatory working days We offer them
  • An interesting and versatile activity in a social and lively working environment
  • A dedicated, collaborative team
  • A workplace on Lake Zurich at Horgen Station
  • Attractive cantonal employment conditions For further information, Nadine Fritschi, Head of Administration, is available at nadine.fritschi@ajb.zh.ch. On www.ajb.zh.ch you will find additional information. We look forward to your complete online application with motivation letter. Applications from recruitment agencies and applications via e-mail are not considered for this vacancy.

Pensum: 70%
