Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Occupation by appointment

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 70391

At Hirslanden Clinic in Zurich, you actively shape the future of cutting-edge medicine and work in an environment that combines innovation, quality and teamwork.

Do you want to take on a central leadership role in a highly specialized and innovative operating environment?

The powerful, efficient and patient-oriented operation is of great importance to us. The management of surgical care is responsible for the management and further development of the surgical care teams and reports to the management of surgical & intervention rooms.

As part of the re-occupation, we are looking for an experienced and leadership-strong personality as

Head / Head of OP care

YOUR TASKS

  • Operational management, support and further development of surgical maintenance, storage maintenance, instrument management and surgical support
  • Ensuring a stable, efficient and patient-safe day-to-day operation in the operating room including management of bottlenecks and emergency situations
  • Close control of interfaces with anesthesia, surgery, surgical planning and attending physicians
  • Analysis, control and further development of relevant OP key figures
  • Shared responsibility for the economic use of resources, personnel planning, budget compliance and sustainable personnel structure

Your profile

  • Dipl. Specialist surgical technology HF or equivalent training in the nursing sector with specialist training OP
  • Several years of professional and managerial experience, ideally in a comparable function in the surgical area of an acute hospital
  • Experience in managing complex operational processes as well as in interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Experience in the analysis and optimization of processes and key figures in the operating environment is an advantage

What makes this place special

This position is a central management function with a great deal of room for manoeuvre, interdisciplinary connection and a professional working environment. You have the opportunity to shape and develop structures sustainably. In addition to a state-of-the-art and innovative working environment with the latest technologies, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you.

For the optimal work-life balance, we offer extensive training and development opportunities as well as flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family tasks. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and wage benefits. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.
