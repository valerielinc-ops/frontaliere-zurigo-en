Aufgaben

  • You will coordinate and manage all aspects of your own clinical research project and support others.
  • As part of this role, you will recruit study participants in accordance with the study protocols.
  • Research data will be collected, managed, and entered into study databases by you with accuracy and attention to detail.
  • You will analyse research data and contribute to the interpretation of study findings.
  • Your research findings will be presented at international scientific conferences.
  • You will publish research results in peer-reviewed scientific journals.
  • Throughout their thesis projects, you will support and supervise Master's students.

Anforderungen

  • Master in Medicine, Biology or Psychology
  • Knowledge of the Good Clinical Practice (GCP) Guidelines E6(R3) and Swiss regulatory requirements would be an advantage.
  • Excellent oral and written knowdlege in German and English
  • Interest in working in a clinical study team, with patients – especially children and their parents
  • Attention to detail
  • Structured process thinking
  • Ability to cope with frustration
  • Ability to work independently but also as a part of the team
