Job description

The optimal teaching for tech-savvy minds and creative solution seekers: inside!

In this exciting training, you will carry out the following activities:

  • Planning, development and programming of electronic hardware and software
  • Manufacture of electronic equipment and whole equipment
  • Commissioning of equipment and equipment and ensuring their maintenance
  • Working with various measurement and test methods and documenting your work

The training lasts 4 years, including 2 years of basic training in our training workshop on the Hönggerberg campus of the ETH.

Profile

You are interested in technical and physical connections and tackle problems with analytical thinking. You enjoy solving complex problems. At the same time, you like to experiment and like to do crafts.

For this vocational apprenticeship you need a mandatory school degree and good performance in the following school subjects:

  • Mathematics
  • Physics
