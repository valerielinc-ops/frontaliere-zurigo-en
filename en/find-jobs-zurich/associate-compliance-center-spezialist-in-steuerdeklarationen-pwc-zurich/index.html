Your impact

  • Create and review profit tax returns and tax assessments for legal entities.
  • Accompany the final planning of companies and calculate the necessary tax provisions.
  • Process tax inquiries.
  • Support corporate structuring, transactions and tax planning projects.
  • Acquire comprehensive technical know-how and soft skills through on-and-off-the-job training.
  • Work in an inspiring team and sharpen your personal and professional profile through regular feedback from Kolleg: inside and supervisor.

Your skill set

  • Have an education in the field of taxation or a completed commercial basic education with experience or are looking for a return to taxation for legal persons.
  • Be a highly motivated, dynamic and purposeful personality who values analytical work and is not afraid of complex questions.
  • Show a high willingness to learn and have a strong flair for numbers.
  • Take responsibility right from the start and move in an environment with an active coaching and feedback culture.
  • Provides excellent IT skills in Word, Excel and PowerPoint.
  • Be stylish in German and have very good knowledge of English.
