Put to the Point

Old potato and grape varieties are obtained in the test tube. They are responsible for their care and preservation of these valuable genetic resources for research and future generations. They work in the fields of in vitro culture and molecular biology, ensuring that each strain remains healthy, identified and available to partners as needed.

Good food, healthy environment

Agroscope is the federal competence centre for research in the agricultural and food industry. The researchers carry out their activities at various locations in Switzerland. The headquarters is Bern-Liebefeld (from 2026: Posieux FR). Agroscope is located at Eidg. Department of Economy, Education and Research WBF.

We offer this

We are committed to the success model Switzerland and work for the benefit of the population. Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring different perspectives. We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Additional information

The plant genome dynamics research group is investigating how plants can adapt rapidly to climate change at the genetic and epigenetic levels. This basic knowledge supports the development of new breeding methods and breeding approaches, which should then help to make our cultures “climate-ready” faster. The research group uses genomic and epigenomic bioinformatics tools.
