Full description

IN BRIEF

You are welcome to join us, work in a structured manner and want to work on a clear goal in a well-established team? For the next 3 months (100%, starting immediately or by arrangement) we are looking for a reinforcement for our hardware team in Zurich Wiedikon. Your task: Together with the team you build up a security inventory of ready-to-use FELFEL refrigerators so that we can supply customers without waiting time in the future.

YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Build a safety inventory of ready-to-use FELFEL refrigerators together with the hardware team.
  • Mount and enable refrigerators according to our internal quality standards in our workshop in Zurich Wiedikon.
  • Carefully check each device for functionality before it goes into storage.
  • Document, label and organize the finished devices for smooth delivery later.
  • Conform yourself closely with the 177 team to ensure that the stock meets the forecast needs of new customers.
  • Support the team in further assembly and preparation work in the workshop.
  • Keep workshop and material storage clear and ready for use. The assembly of FELFEL refrigerators is an exciting mixture of electronics, mechanics, joinery and precision work. You work in our workshop in Zurich Wiedikon, closely integrated into our interdisciplinary hardware team.

WHAT YOU MITTING

  • You are about to complete your training as a mechanic, carpenter, electrician or in a comparable technical profession, have already completed it – or have practical experience.
  • Craft skills and pleasure in concentrated, structured work on the workbench.
  • Reliable, efficient working methods – you adhere to quality standards and schedules.
  • Team skills, as well as the joy of working in the team of two as well as independently.
  • Good German skills, English is an advantage.
  • Guide Card category B is an advantage – so you can also support installation on site if necessary.
  • Non-smokers – we don't want you to smell like cigarettes. FELFEL is all about good food.
  • Valid work permit for Switzerland or Swiss passport.

WHAT WE ARE

  • Best barista coffee and tea for the whole team thanks to our gavetti coffee maker.
