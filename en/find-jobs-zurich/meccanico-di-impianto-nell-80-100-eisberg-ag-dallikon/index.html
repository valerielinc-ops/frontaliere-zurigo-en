Full description

As an operational mechanic, you ensure the smooth operation of our production facilities and infrastructure.

  • Contract type: Perpetual
  • Pensum: 80% - 100%
  • Working time model: 3-shift operation
  • Job: from now on
  • Language: German
  • Location: Eisberg AG, Dänikerstrasse 31 8108 Dällikon

Tasks

  • Maintenance, repair and maintenance of production lines, packaging machines, sorting plants, conveyor systems and more.
  • Effective troubleshooting and carrying out repairs in the production plant.
  • Regular inspections and preventive maintenance to ensure plant availability.
  • Documentation of all maintenance and repair work in our maintenance software.
  • Support in the optimization and improvement of our facilities.
  • If necessary, manufacture of special parts or small series.
  • Compliance with all safety regulations and order in the workplace.

Requirements

  • Completed training as a polymechanic/EFZ specialisation in maintenance, production mechanic or similar technical training.
  • Ideally, professional experience in the maintenance of production facilities (experience in the food industry is beneficial).
  • In-depth knowledge of mechanics, pneumatics and electrical engineering (machining technology is a plus).
  • Independent, reliable and team-capable working method.
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing.
  • Willingness to work shifts (including weekend and night work).
  • We offer an activity in the 3-shift model, which enables a flexible working time arrangement. Shifts include early, late and night shifts and occasional weekend services.

This is what we offer

  • Food: Cuisine is very important to us: Freshly prepared salads, soups, tea and fruit are available to our employees free of charge. In addition, you can take home products from the personal shelf every day.
  • Professional development opportunities: Expand your knowledge through extensive further education offers, high-quality training in the Coop Education Center and various personal development opportunities such as mentoring and coaching.
