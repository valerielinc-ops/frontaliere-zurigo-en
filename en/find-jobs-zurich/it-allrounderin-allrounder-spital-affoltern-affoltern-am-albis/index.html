Full description

You are tech-savvy, like to take part and appreciate a versatile area of responsibility? Then you are exactly right with us! In order to strengthen our IT team, we are looking for a committed and independent personality who will support us in our IT operations and actively participate in the digital development of the Affoltern hospital. Their tasks

  • Together with our IT team and external partners, you ensure a stable, secure and reliable operation of our IT infrastructure
  • You take over the 2nd-level support for technical issues relating to our IT systems and applications and support the 1st-level support if necessary
  • They analyze technical disruptions, develop sustainable solutions and implement improvements independently
  • You actively participate in IT projects and take responsibility for subprojects
  • You support us in the further development, standardization and optimization of our IT landscape Your profile
  • They are trained in computer science or have a comparable qualification
  • You have in-depth practical experience in IT support (1st and 2nd level) as well as in dealing with IT infrastructures
  • They are well-versed in Windows environments and ideally have experience with Microsoft 365 and Azure
  • You work independently, structured and solution-oriented and are happy to take on responsibility
  • They are service-oriented, communicative and have a strong hands-on mentality.
  • You enjoy learning about new technologies and applications
  • Experience in the cooperation or management of IT projects is an advantage
  • They communicate verbally and in writing very well in German Our culture – Your benefits «Bi öis can mer no»: With us it is family and personal. As part of the Affoltern hospital team, you will also benefit from attractive employment conditions and numerous Fringe Benefits: Jokertag, shopping benefits and two free admission subscriptions to the FORTYSEVEN Wellness Spa are just a few examples of our Fringe Benefits. The Hospital Affoltern is the largest employer and training company in the Knonau Office. Around 550 employees work for the well-being of patients every day. Find out more about us in our portrait.
