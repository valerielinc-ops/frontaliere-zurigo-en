Full description

Our rehabilitation clinic Limmattal offers specialized neurological rehabilitation including surveillance rehabilitation as well as accident rehabilitation at a high medical level. Through targeted diagnostics and individually tailored therapy programs, our interdisciplinary team ensures that your patients are optimally looked after on their way back to better health and quality of life. Due to the close cooperation with the Hospital Limmattal and the direct connection to the medical emergency unit, prompt acute somatic treatment is always ensured even in the event of emergency complications.

Your tasks

  • Care: Medical care of patients in the area of neurological rehabilitation including surveillance rehabilitation and accident rehabilitation
  • Rehabilitation: Planning of the rehabilitation steps in an interdisciplinary team with nurses and therapists
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: Through close cooperation with various disciplines, you contribute significantly to holistic treatment and rehabilitation
  • Contact person: Responsible contact person for patients and their relatives with medical questions during their inpatient stay
  • Supervision: Accompany our assistant and specialist doctors in specialist topics

Your profile

  • Qualifications: Swiss Specialist in Neurology, Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation or Internal Medicine or equivalent with MEBEKO recognition
  • Personality: You enjoy interdisciplinary cooperation, convince through a sense of responsibility and high-quality work and have good communication skills
  • Approval: You are in possession of a professional practice permit (BAB) or ready to apply for it

What we offer

  • Co-creation: Work in a modern working environment and actively participate
  • Inspiring and collegial working atmosphere: Working in a supportive and appreciative team
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Mobility: Financial contribution for travelling with the Braunwaldbahn
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, car dealers, pharmacies and our membership at Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other benefits in the thermal baths FORTYSEVEN and the thermal bath Bad Zurzach

Contact person Jeton Beka Chief physician Rehaklinik Limmattal, member of the GL Email: jeton.beka@zurzachcare.ch

Start: January 1, 2027, or by arrangement.
