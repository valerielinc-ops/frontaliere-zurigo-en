Full description

Become part of our team ELEKTROTECHNIK in Zurich. We develop, design, plan and support construction and industrial projects in the field of electrical building installations. We accompany these projects from planning to implementation.

Take responsibility with us as

PROJECTIVE: IN ELECTROTECHNIK 80-100% - AUSTRIA

ITS TASKS

You accompany your versatile projects from concept development through tendering to realization and work closely with clients, architects, specialist planners and executing companies.

  • Independent management and processing of electrical planning projects in various SIA phases incl. Ensuring quality, cost and timetable objectives
  • Development of electrotechnical concepts, dimensions and technical solutions and the associated plans, schemes and calculations
  • Active participation in 3D modeling and planning with Autodesk Revit as well as technical examination of the created models
  • Preparation of tenders and specifications and comparison of tenders
  • Accompanying implementation with participation in construction meetings and carrying out site inspections and acceptances
  • Technical support of junior project managers:inside and electrical planners:inside

Your opportunities

  • Various projects in the areas of buildings, energy and infrastructure with the use of modern project planning and calculation software as well as the BIM method
  • Self-responsible project work with creative leeway and the opportunity to assume responsibility
  • Personal working environment in a team in Zurich with short coordination paths
  • Professional exchange and cooperation with experienced colleagues at various AFRY locations
  • Interdisciplinary working environment with direct cooperation between the different disciplines
  • Opportunities for professional and personal development within an international company
  • Flexible working methods and modern infrastructure at a central location in Zurich Altstetten

YOUR PROFILE

  • Completed training in the field of electrical engineering, ideally with further training as electrical project manager, dipl. Technician HF, engineer FH or a comparable qualification
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in electrical design
  • Good knowledge of Swiss standards and regulations as well as the SIA planning phases
  • Practical experience with Autodesk Revit in electrotechnical 3D modeling and planning as well as good knowledge of BIM methodology
  • In-depth experience in the creation of tenders and specifications with Messerli or a comparable tender software
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Good communication skills and enjoy working with customers and project teams
  • Good knowledge of German, at least level B2, in word and writing
  • Further language skills are advantageous
