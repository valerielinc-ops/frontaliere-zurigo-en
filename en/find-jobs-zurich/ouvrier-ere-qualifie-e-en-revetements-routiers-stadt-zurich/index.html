Full description

We are shaping Zurich from the ground up: making it a great place to live and climate-neutral. Bring your expertise and work with us to create a city fit for the future. For the Asset Maintenance department, we are looking for additional support as of 1 October 2026 or by agreement.

Tasks

  • Performing various construction work such as maintenance, repairs, relocation work and manual pavement repairs.
  • Providing winter on-call service and emergency summer on-call duty once or twice a year.

Profile

  • Vocational training in construction (road builder, paver, civil engineering mason) or several years of relevant professional experience
  • Category B driving licence; category BE an advantage
  • Good knowledge of the city
  • Good spoken German

We offer

  • The opportunity to help shape the future of Switzerland's largest city.
  • Regular working hours year-round from Monday to Friday, no weekend work (except winter service and summer on-call duty), and rare night work.
  • Flexible holiday rental, possible during the summer months up to 3 weeks and 2 days.
  • A modern, environmentally friendly vehicle and machinery park.
  • A culture that is respectful, reliable, ambitious and inclusive and that lives out of values of equality, diversity and inclusion.
  • Attractive employment conditions, such as catering, public transport, health contribution and loyalty premium, a solid pension fund with an above average employer share, and targeted training and training opportunities.
