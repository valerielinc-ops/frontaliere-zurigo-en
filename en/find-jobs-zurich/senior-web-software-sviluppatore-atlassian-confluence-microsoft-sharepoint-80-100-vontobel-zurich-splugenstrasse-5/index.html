Full description

Ready to move beyond “keep-the-lights-on” and shape the future of a global bank’s digital workplace? At Vontobel, we’re transforming our core collaboration platforms from on-prem Atlassian to Confluence Cloud, while evolving SharePoint into a powerful, modern intranet experience used by every employee, every day. As a Senior Microsoft SharePoint Engineer / Developer, you won’t just support systems; you’ll redefine them. Take ownership, drive innovation, and build solutions that truly matter across the organization. If you’re hands-on, forward-thinking, and excited to make a visible impact, this is your chance to lead from the front.

What would your role be?

  • Own and deliver the technical implementation of intranet and collaboration use cases across Microsoft SharePoint and Atlassian Confluence, from requirements to production operations.
  • Play a leading engineering role in the migration from on‑prem Atlassian Data Center intranet content to SharePoint platform evolution.
  • Design, build, and maintain modern SharePoint Online solutions (sites, information architecture, permissions, content models, search, and governance) with a strong focus on developer-grade extensibility.
  • Build and evolve intranet features and integrations (e.g., navigation, publishing patterns, content rollups, automation, and platform-wide components) that scale to a firm-wide audience.
  • Develop and maintain integrations and migration tooling between Confluence and SharePoint (content move, metadata mapping, link integrity, redirects, and quality checks) to enable a controlled, low-risk transition.
  • Act as a senior hands-on engineer: lead code reviews, establish engineering standards, improve CI/CD and operational readiness, and mentor colleagues working on the intranet and platform ecosystem.

What are we looking for? Bachelor’s degree in computer science, a related field, or a completed software engineering apprenticeship.

  • At least 5 years of hands-on professional experience as senior Microsoft SharePoint Engineer / Developer with strong delivery track record in enterprise platforms and high-visibility web systems such as intranets.
  • Deep, current expertise in Microsoft SharePoint (development and administration) including modern SharePoint patterns and operating models in a regulated environment.
  • Strong experience building web solutions (TypeScript/JavaScript, modern frontend, APIs) and implementing secure, maintainable backend services where needed.
  • Solid understanding of identity, permissions, and governance concepts, comfortable designing and operating access models for bank-wide information platforms.
  • Experience with migrations (platform or content), including planning, tooling, validation, stakeholder communication, and incremental rollout strategies.
  • A pragmatic, communicative team player who can own decisions, handle ambiguity, and collaborate closely with business stakeholders, security, and IT operations.
  • Fluent in English, with basic German (A2 level) to read documentation and navigate our bilingual intranet application and content.

What do we offer?

  • Top Zurich location with collaboration spaces and free lunch
  • All the latest technology to help you excel and advance
  • Agile environment in how we think, the way we work and our trust for each other
  • Lots of special perks, including home office possibility up to 20%
  • Flat hierarchy with collaboration at all levels
  • Campus feeling and a collaborative spirit
