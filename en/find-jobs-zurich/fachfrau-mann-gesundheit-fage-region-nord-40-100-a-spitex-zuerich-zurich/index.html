Full description

You don’t have to be at home here to feel at home here. Maybe you already know the North region like the back of your hand? Maybe you will discover them with us. At one of our locations you will become part of a well-established team and experience the region very closely. Whether lively streets in Oerlikon, green corners in Affoltern, family neighbourhoods in Köschenrüti or the diverse neighbourhoods in Schwamendingen: here each quarter has its own character and each person its own history. As a FaGe at Spitex Zurich, you are right in the middle of it. You meet different people, accompany them in their familiar environment and create a day care that is as versatile as the region itself. Discover the North region and maybe soon your new favorite place to work!

(a)lle are addressed. Gender, origin, age? We are interested in your qualification and enthusiasm for work.

WHAT YOU ARE RIGHT WITH US

  • Your area of expertise: Whether memory care (dementia) or somatics: together we find the area that fits your strengths.
  • Flexible pens between 40 and 100% - we adapt to your life, not vice versa.
  • Majority assignments in the day service, usually between 07:00 and 16:00, with 1-2 weekend and evening services per month.
  • Your quarters, your place of work: You are travelling in a region that you may have called home for a long time.
  • More than just care: You accompany customers in their familiar environment and support them in living as independently as possible.
  • Real encounters: You build trust and experience how valuable personal relationships are in everyday care.
  • Variety instead of routine: Every person, every apartment and every day brings new stories.
  • Attractive employment conditions: family-friendly conditions, good social benefits and the possibility to buy additional holiday weeks. Further benefits can be found here: https://jobs.spitex-zuerich.ch/benefits/

You have completed an apprenticeship as a specialist health EFZ and feel comfortable in an independent working environment.
