28.08.2026 Zurich

Become part of our team!

Raiffeisen Switzerland supports the Raiffeisen banks with a team of specialists in providing holistic advice to our customers – from financial planning to pensions and inheritance issues. We are expanding the area of financial planning for entrepreneurs. In doing so, we support our banks in advising corporate clients, in particular on the topics of pension provision, taxes, asset structure and succession planning. In order to strengthen our team based at Zurich Airport (The Circle) and the opportunity to work partly in the home office, we are looking for a flexible and independent personality as a senior financial planner for entrepreneurs (m/f/d).

What awaits you?

  • Holistic advice for business clients (precautionary care, taxes, assets, succession)
  • Preparation of comprehensive financial planning taking into account the private and entrepreneurial situation
  • Challenging customer conversations
  • Development of individual solutions incl. Planned balance sheets and income statements
  • Optimizations resp. Structure of pension schemes, in particular pension funds
  • Development of concepts for restructuring and succession in cooperation with tax experts
  • Active participation in the development of the financial planning service for entrepreneurs
  • Visibility through presentations and customer events

What do you bring?

  • Several years of experience in financial planning related to management consulting
  • Degree as a financial planning expert or SME financial expert, supplemented by further training in the field of succession or trust
  • Knowledge of Logismata Allfina and MS Office
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Excellent communication, presentation and negotiation skills

"If financial planning and entrepreneurship are your passion and you want to actively shape the development of the Raiffeisen Group in the area of pensions and the preparation of entrepreneurs for their succession, then you are in the right place with us."
