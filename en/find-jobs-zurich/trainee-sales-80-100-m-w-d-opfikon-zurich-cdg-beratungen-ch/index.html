Full description

You want to gain a foothold in sales, enjoy direct customer contact and want to learn how to win customers and support them in the long term? With cdg Consulting you practically get into B2B sales and take responsibility step by step. We are an owner-managed consulting company with a focus on sales, marketing and personnel services for SMEs throughout Switzerland. To strengthen our sales team, we are looking for a motivated and learning personality as Trainee Sales (80-100%).

We are waiting for you

  • Entry into the planning and implementation of Telesales projects for our customers
  • Direct contact of potential new customers on the phone
  • Preparation and conduct of customer meetings with decision-makers
  • Researching potential customers and maintaining contacts and conversation results in the CRM
  • Support for the preparation of offers and preliminary studies
  • Gradual assumption of own tasks in customer support and project management

Your profile

  • Interest in B2B and B2C distribution and pleasure in actively reaching out to people
  • First experience in sales or customer contact is advantageous, but no requirement
  • You communicate safely and convincingly in Swiss vernacular
  • Willingness to learn, initiative and the motivation to develop professionally and personally
  • Endurance and the willingness to reach for the phone again even after a rejection
  • Structured working methods and safe handling of digital tools
  • Interest in long-term cooperation with a development perspective
  • Structured, entrepreneurial mindset and high self-motivation
  • Safe use of digital tools and CRM systems
  • Your goal: a long-term role with a development perspective

What we offer you

  • Practical training in sales, customer support and project work
  • Personal support and regular feedback from experienced colleagues
  • Increasing responsibility, coordinated with your development
  • Flat hierarchies and direct cooperation with the partner team and the management
  • A dedicated team with personal cohesion
  • Flexible working hours and modern tools
  • Prospects for further development towards project management and later also leadership
