Full description

The Communication Directorate is responsible for internal and external corporate communication, media relations, and the publication of corporate documents.

Responsibilities

  • Receive and coordinate media inquiries
  • Plan media events and support their implementation
  • Draft press releases
  • Support the media team with administrative and organizational tasks
  • Monitoring, media clippings, and dashboard analysis

Requirements

  • Initial work experience: advantageous
  • Student (Uni/FH/HF): Communication, Media, or Journalism
  • Interest in corporate communication and media work
  • Independent, structured working style
  • German (fluent)
  • French (good knowledge)

What we offer

  • Modern infrastructure: diverse work zones and modern co-working spaces with advanced equipment
  • Flexible work model: temporal and spatial flexibility in fulfilling the role (approximately 40% home office)
  • Leisure and cultural offers: as a main sponsor of runs and events, Migros offers employees limited spots at half price
  • Responsibility and autonomy: diverse field of activity with high personal responsibility
  • Room for creativity and decision-making: opportunity to independently shape and develop the field of activity
  • Sports and fitness offers: cost contribution to subscriptions for over 50 Migros fitness/wellness/aquaparks
