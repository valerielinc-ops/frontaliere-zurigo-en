Full description

This position clearly focuses on your software and IT skills: you work with our lawyer software on a daily basis, support the team technically and actively promote the further development of our digital work processes.

In addition, you take on administrative tasks in accounting and coordination between different departments. They bring a commercial education, are digitally affine and enjoy interface work.

For our location in Zurich, we are looking for a technology-enthusiastic, structured, service-oriented and team-oriented personality who will perform the following main tasks:

  • Participation in accounts payable and payable
  • Various clarifications in day-to-day business and processing of incoming payments
  • Support for projects for the further development of our lawyer software
  • Participation in the optimization of processes, workflows and systems
  • 1st level support in case of difficulties with our lawyer software
  • Preparation of training materials and training for end-users
  • Daily use of digital tools and participation in IT and digitalization projects
  • Completed commercial apprenticeship with professional experience in accounting as well as interest in legal services and working with our specialized lawyer software ELLE
  • Pronounced IT affinity and pleasure in digitalization, modern software tools and technical issues
  • First experience in IT projects or great motivation to actively participate in digital projects
  • Good knowledge of MS365, very good Excel skills and experience or interest in Dynamics BC and Power BI
  • Structured, precise and solution-oriented working method as well as a high degree of independence
  • Very good organizational skills as well as an integrative, team-oriented and reliable personality
  • Stylistic in communication in German and English
  • Working in a professional, collegial environment with direct exchange with financial management, HR management and operational management.
  • Individual development opportunities within the Finance team, depending on interest, talent and personal development.
  • Attractive employment conditions and flexible working time models (including home office)
  • A central place of work directly at Stadelhofen station or Bellevue

Job opening: immediately or by agreement. Place of work: at our location in Zurich (Bellevue).
