Spital Männedorf – Your very special place of work. Whether a small “thank you” or a morning “grüezi” – we focus on real appreciation and humanity.   • Solution-oriented thinking, diplomatic skills, assertiveness and strong organisational talent
  • IT affinity as well as high service and customer orientation and analytical working methods
