Role overview

NVIDIA is the world leader in accelerated computing, powering the AI revolution across the world's largest data centers, clouds, and supercomputers.

Within NVIDIA, the Networking Business Unit (NBU) builds the high-speed interconnect—including Ethernet, InfiniBand, NVLink, and BlueField DPUs—that connects thousands of GPUs into a single AI supercomputer, moving data at the scale and speed required by the world's most demanding AI workloads.
