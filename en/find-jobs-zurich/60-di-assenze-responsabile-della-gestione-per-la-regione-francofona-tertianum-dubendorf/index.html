As part of a job renewal within the HR Service Center, we are looking for a dynamic, communicative and conscientious person for our Corporate Service Center in Dübendorf or Berne, immediately or to be agreed, to occupy the position of 60% Absence Management Officer for the Romandie region Your missions

  • Control of short and long-term absences, including export from the UKA system, as well as continuous monitoring
  • Exchanges with companies insurance and case analyzes
