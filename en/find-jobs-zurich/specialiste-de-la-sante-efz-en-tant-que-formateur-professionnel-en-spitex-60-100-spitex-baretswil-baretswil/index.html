Health professional EFZ (FaGe) 50–90% | Spitex As a health specialist, you take on a versatile and responsible task in the ambulatory sector. You accompany our clients in their usual environment and make an important contribution to their quality of life and independence with your work.

Your main tasks include

  • Care and support of our clients in their home environment
  • Support in basic and treatment care according to nursing planning
  • Monitoring and communicating relevant changes to the case-leading care professionals
  • Electronic documentation of the services provided
  • Co-creation of professional, appreciative and client-oriented care
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with relatives, doctors and internal and external specialists

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training as FaGe EFZ or FaBe EFZ with treatment course
  • Ideally, you already have experience in the Spitex
  • Self-employed, responsible and reliable working methods
  • Enjoyment of contact with people and strong social skills
  • Flexibility, team spirit and resilience
  • Safe handling of digital work equipment
  • Driver’s license Cat. B or willingness to take part with the e-bike
