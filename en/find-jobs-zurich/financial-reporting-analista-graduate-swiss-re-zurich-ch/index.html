Main responsibilities

  • Key Responsibilities
  • Own and coordinate quarterly financial reporting processes, partnering with Group Finance and regional finance teams across multiple geographies
  • Analyse and interpret L&H financial performance data, providing clear and insightful explanations of variances against internal and external targets
  • Consolidate financial inputs from a diverse range of stakeholders, ensuring accuracy, consistency, and timeliness of reporting outputs
  • Contribute to external L&H disclosures and internal management reporting, supporting the delivery of high-quality analysis used for strategic steering purposes
  • Identify opportunities for process improvement within reporting workflows, driving efficiency and quality enhancements
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to support ad hoc financial analyses and special projects as required by senior management About the Team
  • We are a vibrant, multinational team based in Zürich, working closely with specialists around the world.
  • Our team holds ownership of the consolidated L&H results, with a sharp focus on both external disclosures and internal management reporting.
  • We pride ourselves on delivering high-quality, actionable analysis to senior management that directly supports strategic decision-making.
