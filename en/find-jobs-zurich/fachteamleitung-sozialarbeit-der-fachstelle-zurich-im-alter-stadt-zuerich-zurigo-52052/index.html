Full description

As a result of retirement, we are looking for our Zurich department at age 1. March 2026 or by arrangement a dedicated person for the management of the professional team social work.

Tasks

  • You lead the team of social workers - at three locations - both professionally and in terms of personnel.
  • They guarantee a high-quality and needs-based service in social consulting.
  • They ensure the continuous development of target group and customer-oriented information and consulting services (point of contact for all age issues).
  • They play a decisive role in shaping the strategic orientation of the specialist centre and support projects in the Age Strategy 2035.
  • They actively network with partner organisations in the age sector and represent the centre in specialist groups, projects and public relations.

Profile

  • University of applied sciences degree (bachelor or master) in social work or comparable level of training
  • In-depth knowledge in the age and health sector as well as experience in community work
  • Several years of leadership experience
  • Understanding political frameworks and urban structures
  • Winning and communication-strong personality with a secure appearance and a high degree of independence and initiative

We offer

A responsible and exciting task in a lively working environment and committed team. A structured introduction to the task area and an attractive workplace with modern infrastructure. As an administrative unit of the City of Zurich, we stand for attractive development opportunities, very advanced employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

About us

The Municipal Health Services (SGD) is a service department of the Department of Health and the Environment with around 250 employees, which offers a variety of health services for the city of Zurich. They include the business units Stadtärztlicher Dienst (SAD), Zürich im Alter (ZiA), Public Health (PH), Spitex, Management & Support.

The city of Zurich offers older residents a wide range of options in the different walks of life. In Oerlikon/Seebach, Wipkingen and Klus Park, the specialist office Zurich in old age operates contact points for all questions relating to old age. Information and advice, preventive health advice with home visit, registration for urban retirement housing offers, support in the search for housing and an online platform with services for old age are among the offers of the specialist centre.
