Specialist therapy

The Kilchberg Sanatorium offers you a fulfilling working environment with exciting development opportunities in a dynamic and appreciative team. Bring your strengths and shape the future of the clinic.

Their tasks

  • Planning and implementation of individual therapies
  • Planning and implementation of group therapies (e.g. Nordic walking, strength training, progressive relaxation, play & sports, QiGong / yoga)
  • Continuous documentation of the therapy process
  • Participation in reports and case discussions
  • Interest in additional work assignments beneficial

Your profile

  • Completed training in movement and/or sports therapy
  • Experience in psychiatry as well as in group and individual therapy
  • Responsible and adaptable personality
  • Good resilience and high social skills
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Good knowledge of German and English

The Kilchberg Sanatorium is one of the most traditional psychiatric private clinics in Switzerland and looks back on almost 160 years of history. Only 15 minutes from the city centre of Zurich with beautiful views of Lake Zurich and the Alps. As a Listespital, we offer comprehensive psychiatric care for all insured persons and have inpatient and outpatient facilities in Kilchberg and Zurich. Our approximately 650 employees from over 25 professional groups are at the heart of the clinic and are committed to the best possible treatment of our patients. Become part of our team and shape the future of psychiatric care.
