Full description

With great attention to detail, our employees make GLOBUS the leading premium and luxury address in Switzerland. It's people like you who make the difference and, together with us, bring the "place we love to be" to life.

HERE'S WHERE YOU MAKE THE DIFFERENCE

  • You advise our customers personally, attentively and with a sense of beauty, charisma and individuality.
  • You are familiar with fragrances, care lines and international brands and will find the right product for every personality.
  • You will actively participate in visual merchandising and help shape the appearance of the counter in style.
  • You will cultivate customer relationships, accompany the customer card program and advise with aplomb, calmness and genuine enjoyment of dialogue.

WHAT SETS YOU APART

  • You have completed an apprenticeship in the retail trade or perfumery sector as well as several years of experience in premium sales, which require intensive consultation.
  • You move confidently in the beauty world – with a feel for trends, quality and personality.
  • You have a fine eye for detail and a real talent for advising people according to type and style.
  • You work in a structured, responsible manner and contribute to success on the sales floor.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English.
  • Your fixed working days are Friday & Saturday

BENEFITS FOR YOU

You can also look forward to

  • an exceptional working environment in Switzerland's highest-turnover and most popular shopping centre.
  • Excellent public transport connections – tram and bus stop right in front of the Glatt centre.
  • direct motorway connection (A1) if you come to work by car.
  • Free parking for employees as well as petrol stations and e-charging stations directly in front of the building.
  • a roof terrace for employees to have lunch in the fresh air or simply soak up the sun.
  • Top catering options thanks to Glatt gastronomy: 14 restaurants and bars offer everything from delicacies, Mediterranean delicacies, Asian delicacies to takeaways.
  • 100 shops, including pharmacies, a post office and much more - the Glatt, the small shopping town! PS: Thanks to the Glatt card, you get various discounts.
  • attractive social benefits.
  • at least 5 weeks of vacation.
  • the outfit pass with around 50% on 30 items of clothing per year for you.
  • up to 30% discount on products from the globe world for you and your family.
  • a fairytale atmosphere during the extraordinary Glatt Christmas season.

YOUR CONTACT If you want to develop your consulting skills, your knowledge of human nature and your feeling for brands, fragrances and care products in a high-quality environment that values exactly such skills, then we look forward to receiving your online application.
