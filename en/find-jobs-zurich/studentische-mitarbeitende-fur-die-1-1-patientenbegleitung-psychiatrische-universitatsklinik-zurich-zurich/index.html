Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. (in hourly wage) You are studying medicine, psychology, nursing, occupational or physiotherapy and would like to gain valuable practical experience in addition to your studies? With us you will get exciting insights into the psychiatric hospital everyday life and learn to accompany people in challenging crisis situations professionally. You will strengthen your observation and communication skills, take responsibility and work closely with an experienced care team. Whether as a part-time job during the semester or with a higher workload in the lecture-free period: With us, you can put your knowledge from your studies into practice and further develop both professionally and personally. For the 1:1 accompaniment of people in a crisis situation in the stationary care we are looking for immediately or by agreement Their tasks

  • 1:1 accompaniment of patients in psychiatric crisis situations in inpatient everyday life
  • Close supervision and observation of patients with increased need for care and safety, pronounced behavioural problems and physical limitations
  • Attentive observation and assessment of situations as well as early recognition of self-harming actions
  • Close cooperation with the care team and implementation of the agreed care and safety measures under the guidance of the responsible care specialists.

Your profile

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in medicine, psychology, nursing, occupational or physical therapy
  • Self-employed, attentive and responsible work
  • Good observation and ability to assess situations prudently
  • Strong communication skills and respectful treatment of people
  • Willingness to engage with people in challenging life situations and keep calm even in demanding moments

Our offer

  • Practice that takes you further: You gain valuable experience in the psychiatric hospital everyday life
  • Flexible studying and working: You design your workload between 10 and 100% and combine study and work according to your possibilities
  • Focus on health: Various sports offerings, green oases and an internal care team
  • Future provision with added value: 60% employer contribution to the pension fund
  • Well supplied in everyday work: Discounted catering offers

Contact Rodolpho Allison Head of Care at the Centre for Acute Mental Illness +41 58 384 35 75 will be happy to answer questions. We look forward to your application! Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich Lenggstrasse 31, PO Box 363 8032 Zurich www.pukzh.ch
