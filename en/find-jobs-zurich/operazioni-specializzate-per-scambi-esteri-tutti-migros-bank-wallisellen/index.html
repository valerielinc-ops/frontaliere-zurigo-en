Full description

At Migros Bank, you are the central expert in the money market and foreign exchange back office. We are looking for a personality who takes responsibility, analyzes complex issues, thinks in a network, works precisely and shapes high quality standards. Do you want to contribute your know-how to a dynamic team and develop it further? We look forward to seeing you!

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Bank, Wallisellen, Industriestrasse 17-19, 8304 Wallisellen

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You record and control money market and foreign exchange orders in the settlement systems and ensure compliance with internal policies.
  • You manage interest rates and master data of the settlement systems (accounts, counterparties, products) and update them on an ongoing basis.
  • Settlement of foreign exchange options including triggering of maturities and bank transfers while ensuring correct value.
  • Oversee payments and interest rate swaps and resolve inconsistencies with internal and external agencies to minimise risk.

Requirements

  • Mind. 3 years: foreign exchange, money market and/or securities business
  • You have very good knowledge of the processes and processes in your area of responsibility, especially in foreign exchange, money market and securities transactions.
  • You know the internal and external regulatory foundations for your work and can safely apply them in practice.
  • You have experience in dealing with the Finnova core banking system
  • Networked thinking and the ability to keep track even in hectic situations
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ)
  • in German (negotiation confident)
  • French (good knowledge)
  • Italian (advantageous)

Cosa of friamo

  • Central place of work (GABS): Optimally accessible by train (10 minutes from Zurich HB) or car
  • Child care: up to CHF 300 per month per child to external child care
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Migros Bank: accounts, mortgages and financial services with preferential terms
  • Employee offers: Annual generous well-being credit

The decision to advance or withdraw from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks

Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
