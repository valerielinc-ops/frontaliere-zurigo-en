Full description

It's all about keeping it safe in the digital world these days – and you can be a part of how that plays out in a multi-national company like Swiss Re! As the Cyber Security Analyst, you will support and resolve identity and access-related issues, bring your contributions to interesting projects to streamline our daily conduct of business, and more. We are a modern company, and your time is valuable to us! That is why we are offering you the option to work from home as well as the office premises in a perfectly balanced way. About the team The Security Team is the focal point for all security activities across Swiss Re. We are responsible for cybersecurity engineering and operations, corporate security, governance, operational resilience, risk and compliance. We define and advance the company's security strategy. You will work directly in Cyber SecOps – a team of security experts focus on providing the best possible Identity & Access Management services to our employees and customers. Got a knack for IT and cybersecurity? Are you not a seasoned expert but still eager to grow and learn? We are looking for a new colleague who will help to support our team in timely issue resolutions, bring in innovative ideas to improve our processes, and be a valuable addition to a cheerful, professional team of experts! In your role, you will…

  • Support and resolve all identity and access-related issues
  • Conduct regular access rights reviews
  • Maintain a positive communication level towards customers
  • Be a part of interesting projects to streamline the established services and processes
  • Deal with tasks and tickets in a timely and high-quality-meeting manner
  • Bless our team with enthusiasm and passion
  • Foster a learning and development-centred culture and support team-based collaboration

Your qualifications Nobody is perfect and meets 100% of our requirements. If you, however, meet some of the criteria below and are curious about the world of great customer service inside IT and cybersecurity, we will be more than happy to meet you! What we need from you

  • Basic experience working with Identity & Access Management and end-user support
  • Ability to explain IT stuff to non-technical crowds
  • Basic understanding - Active Directory / Azure AD (now Entra ID), SSO and MFA
  • IAM knowledge - Authentication, authorization
  • A people-loving personality and fondness of collaborating with colleagues, partners, and customers
  • Good written and verbal communication skills in English
  • Experience dealing with customers and clients
  • Tech-savviness

Nice-to-have

  • VBA or SQL knowledge

The minimum gross base salary for this role is 10 EUR/ per hour. Our final offer to you will be set up fairly, considering the skills and experience that you bring to the Swiss Re Group. We provide feedback to all candidates. If you have not heard from us, please, check your spam folder. About Swiss Re Swiss Re is one of the world’s leading providers of reinsurance, insurance and other forms of insurance-based risk transfer, working to make the world more resilient. We anticipate and manage a wide variety of risks, from natural catastrophes and climate change to cybercrime. Combining experience with creative thinking and cutting-edge expertise, we create new opportunities and solutions for our clients. This is possible thanks to the collaboration of more than 15,000 employees across the world.
