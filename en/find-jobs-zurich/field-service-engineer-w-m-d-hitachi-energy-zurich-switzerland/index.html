Full description

The possibility

Hitachi Energy High Voltage Products is the world-renowned specialist in energy transmission and distribution. The service unit is responsible for the worldwide after sales support of generator circuit breaker systems (GCB) and gas-insulated switchgear (GIS) including training, installation and commissioning, scheduled maintenance, additional services, troubleshooting, extensions, conversions and replacements.

Service is the key to success. As a colleague in our global service team, you appreciate the independent, varied and solution-oriented work. After a versatile, practical training in Switzerland and abroad, you will lead GIS installations and maintenance around the world. It offers you a technically challenging position in a multicultural environment.

They can do that.

  • Management of multicultural construction sites worldwide for gas-insulated switchgear for the voltage level of 110KV – 550KV
  • Responsible for the successful implementation of on-site installation, maintenance and repairs in compliance with contractual deadlines and taking into account local legal requirements
  • Responsible for the quality of the work carried out on site by means of quality control and assessment of the skills of local assistants
  • Performing product training on site on the product and during installation
  • Responsible for evaluating risks and minimizing them.
  • Coordination and direction of workers
  • Cost control of the resources used and regular reporting to the project management
  • Actively participating in process improvements and proactively taking measures to increase the efficiency of on-site activities

Your background

  • Completed apprenticeship in the metal construction, electrical or automotive sector
  • Basic electrotechnical knowledge is a must, electrotechnical further education is advantageous
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in the field of assembly and commissioning
  • Very good German and English skills, at least B2
  • Readiness for national and especially international worldwide missions with up to 80% travel activity
  • Flexible, resilient and communicative team player
  • Flair for pragmatic and unconventional solution search with high quality and safety standards

More about us

  • Exciting and varied tasks with a lot of creative scope
  • Attractive opportunities for further education and personal development
  • Interesting and diverse activities/projects – also with assignments abroad
  • Experienced and dynamic team
  • Open corporate culture
