Full description

<b>Bülach Hospital grows - grow with it.</b>

Tasks

  • Processing of absence reports in the event of illness and accident as well as ensuring correct and timely processing.
  • Checking of incapacity for work certificates for completeness and ensuring correct and timely registration in the PEP and UKA Solution.
  • Support and advice of executives and employees on all topics related to absentee management in cooperation with the HR Business Partner (HRBP).
  • Support of the HRM team in various tasks along the entire HR life cycle.

Requirements

  • Completed commercial vocational training, ideally with further training in the field of wages and/or HR (e.g. HR assistant)
  • Proactive and solution-oriented case handling
  • Structured, exact and independent working method
  • Strong teamwork, a pinch of humor and a high service orientation
