Full description

As an electrical installer: in the Energy Management team, you are responsible for the professional installation, commissioning and testing of measurement and data transmission systems. You make an important contribution to the recording, monitoring and optimization of our customers’ energy consumption.

You can move that with us.

  • Installation and commissioning of private energy meters
  • Installation and connection of energy consumption measurement systems
  • Assembly, installation and testing of the data transmission infrastructure
  • Establishment and commissioning of communication and transmission equipment
  • Performance of measurements, functional checks and error analysis
  • Preparation and maintenance of measurement, testing and commissioning protocols
  • Documentation of the work carried out and updating of the technical documentation
  • Support for troubleshooting and troubleshooting measurement and communication systems
  • Independent implementation of Swiss-wide projects
  • Readiness for multi-day assignments in Romandie and Ticino

That's what makes you.

  • Completed training as an electrical installer:in EFZ
  • Professional experience in electrical installations, ideally in the field of energy, metrology or building technology
  • Good knowledge of high and low current installations
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Knowledge of French and/or Italian is an advantage
  • Category B driving licence
  • Interest in energy management, metrology and digital communication systems
  • Independent, reliable and careful working method
  • Strong quality and safety awareness
  • Good organizational skills and enjoyment of varied projects
  • Readiness for Switzerland-wide assignments and multi-day stays in Romandie and Ticino
  • Safe handling of digital work equipment and technical documentation

That's what we offer you.

  • Exciting and varied work in a committed team
  • Modern work equipment and professional training
  • Development opportunities within the company
  • Attractive employment conditions and benefits
