Full description

The club school Migros Eastern Switzerland supports refugees on their integration path by offering work integration coaching. As a coach, you accompany the participants in the individual phases on their way to the job market or an apprenticeship.

Luogo di lavoro: Club School Migros, Club Schools Winterthur and Wetzikon, Rudolfstrasse 11, 8400 Winterthur

Mansioni

  • Carrying out coaching (usually individual support, partly group setting).
  • Preparation of the work integration plan
  • Support of participants during individual funding measures
  • Application support
  • Follow-up during the first months of the follow-up solution
  • Clarification and exchange with case management bodies and third parties
  • Participation in specialist exchange and internal training courses
  • The comprehensive preparation and follow-up of the coachings including administrative activities (reports etc.) is also one of your tasks.

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: in coaching of job seekers
  • Experience in teaching location and application courses is beneficial for job seekers
  • Experience in migration / labour integration
  • Networking with employers from the region
  • You are a flexible, reliable and team-oriented personality.
  • They have a high level of social competence and a solution-oriented basic attitude.
  • They are characterized by self-reflection and meet the participants with empathy.
  • You design your work independently and responsibly.
  • They communicate clearly, effectively and respectfully.
  • They value meaningful work and attach importance to a stimulating learning and working environment.
  • Initial or continuing education completed
  • German (very good knowledge)
