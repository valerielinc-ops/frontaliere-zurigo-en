Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

We are looking for a Credit Manager to join our Group Treasury team in Madrid. In this role, you will play a key part in managing and strengthening Sulzer's global credit risk framework while supporting strategic treasury initiatives across the organization. This is an exciting opportunity for a finance professional who enjoys working in an international environment, leading cross-functional projects, and influencing decision-making at a global level. You will collaborate with senior stakeholders across the business to ensure effective credit risk management, support factoring activities, and contribute to treasury transformation initiatives. What you will do:

  • Support the establishment and development of the HUB credit risk management team.
  • Drive the creation of standardized and harmonized credit risk management processes across the organization.
  • Manage global service contracts related to credit risk management, including relationships with external credit rating agencies and tender processes.
  • Lead the assessment and design of credit risk management technologies supporting multiple ERP platforms.
  • Collaborate with IT and business stakeholders to define and implement standard SAP credit risk management templates.
  • Evaluate high-value credit limit requests and provide recommendations to Division CFOs and the Group CFO.
  • Deliver training and guidance to internal stakeholders on credit risk assessment and banking policies.
  • Support and further develop the company's global factoring processes.
  • Manage the global bank account structure and maintain operational banking relationships.
  • Coordinate access management for centrally controlled bank accounts.
  • Participate in treasury projects and continuous improvement initiatives across the organization.

What you need to succeed

Required

  • University degree in Finance, Accounting, Business Administration, or a related field.
  • Minimum 5 years of experience in a similar role within an international organization, ideally in a regional or group treasury environment.
  • Strong experience in credit risk management and third-party risk assessment.
  • Proven success in managing complex cross-functional and international projects.
  • Excellent organizational and planning skills.
  • Ability to work effectively under pressure and manage priorities in a fast-paced environment.
  • Strong stakeholder management skills with the ability to build relationships across all levels of the organization.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
  • Strong Microsoft Office skills, particularly Excel and PowerPoint.
  • Professional proficiency in English.
  • Ability to work independently and take ownership of responsibilities.

Preferred

  • Experience with integrated ERP systems.
  • Exposure to SAP credit risk management solutions.
  • Experience in global treasury or shared services environments.

What we offer you

  • A competitive salary as well as participation in the variable remuneration system based on personal and financial objectives.
  • Meal vouchers.
  • Access to a voluntary flexible compensation scheme allowing employees to optimize their salary package, such as health insurance, transport, or childcare.
  • Be part of an experienced and international team.
  • A good vibe working environment.
  • The opportunity to grow your career within a solid and well-established international company.
  • Be part of a dynamic organization undergoing continuous transformation and improvement.
  • Office location: Nuevos Ministerios, Madrid, Spain.

Additional benefits may apply depending on the position and location.
