Full description

Your mission

  • Sales is your stage: With competence, passion and charm you win customers for us
  • With your enthusiasm for our products, offers and services, you make shopping for our customers an experience where they feel like real VIPs
  • You understand what our customers really need and proactively find solutions that fit them perfectly
  • With your friendly manner, you serve the cash desk confidently and accompany customers with repairs, complaint handling and everything about online pickups.

Your match with us

  • You enjoy dealing with people and meet customers with heart, service awareness and a strong host mentality
  • Vocational training or experience in sales is ideal, but not a must. It is important that you are curious, open to new things and motivated. We look forward to professional experience as well as lateral entrants
  • Reliability, friendliness and team spirit are just as important to you as they are to us
  • You enjoy achieving goals, developing yourself and earning attractive bonuses through good performance
  • You have good knowledge of German

We are

At MediaMarkt, you are part of a team that achieves more together. We exchange tips, pack together and laugh about small everyday moments – this energy makes our everyday work special. Many colleagues especially appreciate the feeling of moving big things together and having fun. Technology excites us, but the team behind it makes the difference. Want to know more about working at MediaMarkt and what we stand for? Along here!

We believe that you are best when you can be yourself. We value diversity and equal opportunities and therefore welcome all applications!

Your extras

On top to your monthly salary from CHF 4’200 (gross/full-time); Adaptation according to experience & qualification) we still have a lot of benefits for you:

  • Bonus program: Your bet pays off – with up to several 100 francs extra per month
  • Employee discounts: Our products are available for you at particularly attractive employee prices
  • Cool deals: e.g. personnel discount on repairs and display protection, product promotions, reduced additional insurance with the health insurance, discounts on corporate benefits (all around fashion, leisure, car, travel, sports, tickets, household, etc.)
  • Employees recruit employees: Get new colleagues on board & secure premium
  • Learning, growing, taking off: from entry, to specialists and market management – our internal and external training opportunities make your career boost possible
  • Let’s RISE: Our mentoring program actively supports you on your professional success path
  • We celebrate you: with small surprises, vouchers at special moments (e.g. birthday or birth of a child), and always great employee parties
  • Rental equipment: Our technology for testing, testing and experiencing our employees – to get to know new equipment, whether professional or not
  • Platform for mental well-being: Confidential 1:1 consultations with external experts practical exercises for everyday life, workshops, etc. Confidential & anonymous – for professional or private topics
