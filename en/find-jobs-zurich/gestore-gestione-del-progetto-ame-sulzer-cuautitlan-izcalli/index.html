About the role

Job Summary The Project Management Director is responsible for managing multiple project teams and supporting the development and execution of functional strategy. This includes overseeing complex projects, ensuring quality standards, and influencing executive leadership. This job profile is intended for senior leaders that support the mid to long-term (3–5 years) strategy development for their functional area, and have ownership of the short to mid-term (1–3 years) execution of functional strategy/operational direction. Requires ability to communicate with and influence executive leadership. Typically manage multiple teams. Position Summary The Head of Project Management, Americas is responsible for leading and standardizing project execution across MTCS Americas under the One Americas operating model. The role provides strategic and functional leadership for Project Management activities supporting engineered products, capital equipment, manufacturing projects, revamps, retrofits, and selected service projects throughout the region. Reporting to the Head of Product Operations Americas and General Manager Mexico, the position serves as the regional Project Management Office (PMO) leader and is accountable for driving project execution excellence, customer satisfaction, profitability, cash collection performance, risk mitigation, resource utilization, and project governance across the Americas. The role is responsible for establishing a consistent project execution framework across the region while ensuring projects are delivered safely, on time, within budget, in accordance with contractual requirements, and aligned with MTCS growth and profitability objectives. Key Responsibilities Project Management Leadership

  • Lead the Americas Project Management organization under a One Americas operating model.
  • Establish and maintain project management standards, governance processes, methodologies, tools, and reporting systems across the region.
  • Develop a scalable PMO organization capable of supporting future business growth and increasing project complexity.
  • Promote project execution consistency across the United States, Mexico, Canada, and Latin America.
  • Establish departmental objectives, KPIs, development plans, and succession planning activities.
  • Recruit, develop, coach, and retain project management talent throughout the region.
  • Foster a culture of accountability, collaboration, customer focus, and execution excellence.

Project Acceptance & Execution Governance

  • Establish project acceptance and order review processes across the Americas.
  • Ensure customer Purchase Orders align with approved quotations, scope of supply, pricing assumptions, and contractual terms.
  • Oversee project transfer from Sales to Operations and ensure execution readiness prior to project launch.
  • Lead project kick-off governance processes and establish execution strategies based on project complexity.
  • Ensure project scope, schedule, resource requirements, risks, and financial objectives are clearly defined at project initiation.
  • Establish governance requirements for strategic, high-risk, and mega-project opportunities.

Project Portfolio Management

  • Maintain visibility and oversight of the regional project portfolio.
  • Lead project portfolio review meetings and regional project governance forums.
  • Drive prioritization and allocation of project management resources across the Americas.
  • Identify project risks, execution bottlenecks, capacity constraints, and resource conflicts.
  • Support proactive mitigation strategies and escalation of critical project issues.
  • Monitor project backlog health, execution capacity, and future resource requirements.
  • Provide executive reporting on project portfolio
