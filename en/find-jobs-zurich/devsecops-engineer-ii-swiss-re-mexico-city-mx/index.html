Full description

About the Role You are a DevSecOps engineer with strong expertise in Terraform, Microsoft Azure, and Kubernetes. You are passionate about building secure, scalable, and automated cloud platforms that enable engineering teams to deliver reliably and efficiently. You combine infrastructure engineering, cloud automation, platform security, and operational excellence with an evolving focus on AI-assisted engineering and future system specification-driven delivery models. You think in systems, automate wherever possible, and help define secure engineering guardrails across the full software lifecycle. About the Team The CorSo Accident and Health team is building innovative, cloud-native insurance technology platforms designed for the future. We operate with a startup mentality, move quickly, challenge conventional approaches, and leverage modern engineering and AI-assisted capabilities to deliver differentiated solutions in a competitive marketplace.

Responsibilities

Cloud Platform Engineering

  • Design, build, and maintain Azure-based cloud infrastructure
  • Develop and manage Infrastructure as Code using Terraform
  • Build and operate Kubernetes platforms and container orchestration environments
  • Design reusable infrastructure modules and deployment patterns

Security & Compliance

  • Implement cloud security best practices and secure-by-default infrastructure patterns
  • Design and manage identity, authentication, and authorization controls
  • Implement secrets management, policy enforcement, and runtime security controls
  • Support vulnerability management, auditability, and compliance initiatives

Automation & Operations

  • Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
  • Implement monitoring, logging, alerting, and operational observability solutions
  • Support incident response, diagnostics, and platform reliability initiatives
  • Improve developer experience through automation and platform tooling

AI-Assisted Engineering (Emerging)

  • Use AI-assisted tools to accelerate infrastructure engineering and operational workflows
  • Validate AI-generated infrastructure configurations and automation artifacts
  • Contribute to machine-readable infrastructure specifications and deterministic deployment patterns
  • Help define engineering guardrails and validation-first operational models
  • Support the evolution toward agentic engineering and autonomous operational capabilities

Technical Collaboration

  • Collaborate closely with software engineers, architects, and security teams
  • Participate in platform architecture and technical design discussions
  • Contribute to engineering standards, operational excellence, and continuous improvement
  • Mentor teams on cloud-native and secure engineering practices

Required Experience & Capabilities

  • 5–10+ years of infrastructure, cloud, or DevSecOps engineering experience
  • Strong Terraform experience Deep Azure cloud platform knowledge Strong Kubernetes and container orchestration expertise
  • Experience with CI/CD tooling and deployment automation
  • Experience implementing cloud security and identity solutions
  • Strong Linux and networking fundamentals
  • Experience with monitoring, logging, and observability tooling
  • Strong troubleshooting and operational support capabilities

Growth & Leadership Signals

  • Ability to think in end-to-end platforms and systems
  • Strong automation-first mindset
  • Experience validating complex infrastructure implementations
  • Interest in AI-assisted infrastructure engineering and emerging operational models
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills
  • Continuous learner with awareness of cloud and security trends
