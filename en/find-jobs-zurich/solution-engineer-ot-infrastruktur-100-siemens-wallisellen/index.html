Full description

Create a better #TomorrowWithUs Want to help shape the future of the railway world together with us? At Siemens Mobility, we are the leading provider of mobility and are constantly expanding our innovative portfolio, which includes rail vehicles, rail automation, electrification solutions, complete rail systems and accompanying services. With around 800 specialist employees in Switzerland and 39 600 employees worldwide, we shape mobility with passion and are one step ahead of our time. You move at the interface between classical IT and industrial control systems and ensure that the systems and infrastructure landscapes approved by us are efficient, secure and future-oriented. You also take responsibility for our extensive test laboratory. If you have a real passion for hardware, like working independently and taking on responsibility, then you are at the right place with us! Your new tasks

  • Evaluation and approval of COTS hardware: You are our expert when it comes to evaluating, testing and releasing commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) hardware for our OT infrastructures. Your decisions secure the basis of our systems!
  • Design, development and operation of OT infrastructures (laboratory): This includes server systems, virtualization environments and industrial IoT components, which you optimize with your hardware knowledge.
  • Installation and patch management of operating systems: You take care of the installation, configuration and maintenance of operating systems such as Windows and Linux on our OT hardware to ensure smooth operation.
  • Planning and implementing OT architectures taking into account security, availability and compliance requirements
  • Close collaboration with engineering and IT teams to integrate systems and applications
  • Analysis of existing OT environments and identification of optimization potential
  • Implementation and further development of cybersecurity measures in the OT environment (e.g. network segmentation, zero-trust approaches)
  • Management of external partners and suppliers as well as participation in projects across the entire lifecycle
  • Creating technical documentation and support in incident and problem management Your profile
  • Completed studies in computer science, electrical engineering, automation technology or completed vocational training in the relevant environment coupled with solid professional experience
  • Sound experience in the field of OT, industrial IT, automation or production infrastructures
  • In-depth knowledge of hardware evaluation and integration as well as operating systems such as Windows and Linux.
  • Experience in the implementation of cybersecurity concepts in the OT environment is advantageous
  • Analytical and structured working method paired with a high quality standard
  • Communicative personality with the ability to convey complex issues appropriately
  • Very good German and English skills You benefit from
  • an open and flexible working culture at one of the leading companies in the field of mobility in Switzerland
  • a 40-hour week on a 100% fee
  • at least 5 weeks of holidays
  • a workplace at the pulse of railway technology
  • Various employee benefits and training opportunities
  • a hybrid working model: balanced work-life balance and more autonomy Our team and location You want information about our culture, our values, diversity and other important topics?
