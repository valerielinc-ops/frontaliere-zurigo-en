Main responsibilities

  • Your main responsibilities:
  • Provide day-to-day support to the Credit & Lending team, including loan administration, documentation handling, and client interaction.
  • Assist in the structuring, financial analysis, and execution of complex structured loan transactions.
  • Support the preparation of credit proposals, internal reports, and presentations for both internal stakeholders and external partners.
  • Contribute to the syndication process of structured loans by managing data rooms, coordinating investor communications, and supporting transaction execution.
  • Conduct market research on crypto-related lending products (e.g., crypto-backed, uncollateralized, ASIC-backed loans) and perform benchmarking to inform product development and strategic decisions.
  • Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams such as Legal, Risk, and Client Services to ensure smooth execution of lending activities.
  • Demonstrated interest in credit markets, structured finance, and digital assets.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a keen eye for detail.
  • Proficient in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel, PowerPoint, and Word; experience in financial modelling is a strong advantage.
