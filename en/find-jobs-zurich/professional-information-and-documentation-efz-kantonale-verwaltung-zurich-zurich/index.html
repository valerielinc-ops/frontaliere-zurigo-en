Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Career Advice Professional Information and Documentation EFZ 50% Good for life – that is our motto.

As part of the Education Directorate of the Canton of Zurich, the Office for Youth and Vocational Counselling (AJB) is responsible for the offers in the areas of child and youth welfare and vocational counselling.

The Information Agency (INA) of the AJB designs and implements all communication and information tools of the Office.
