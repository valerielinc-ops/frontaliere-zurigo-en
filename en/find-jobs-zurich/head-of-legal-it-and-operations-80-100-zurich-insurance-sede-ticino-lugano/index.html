Full description

In this role as Head of Legal IT and Operations within the Group Legal Department of Zurich you will lead the Legal Team that supports internal stakeholders of Zurich on all issues related to strategic sourcing and Information Technology (IT). You will lead the team that provides legal advice in connection with outsourcing and procurement contracts, with the deployment of new technologies and digitalization and potentially a variety of other topics. You are responsible for the provision of legal advice and guidance to the business related to sourcing and procurement process, contract drafting and negotiations.

What you will do

  • General legal advice in the area of strategic sourcing and IT procurement including e-commerce and digitalization, e.g. online contract formation, distribution of digital customer offerings, incl. Apps etc.
  • Legal advice on contracting in particular, especially by way of drafting and negotiating various sourcing contracts and in particular large international outsourcing contracts.
  • Tailoring of the advice per the above within the context and confines of the various insurance business activities of Zurich.
  • Maintaining the terms and conditions for intra-group agreements and reviewing intra-group agreements from a Group perspective.
  • Provide support to Zurich Capability Centers around the globe.
  • Provide Board Secretariat support to certain Group entities.
  • Lead and develop a team of two specialized lawyers.

What you bring

  • Law degree and Bar exam, preferably from Switzerland
  • Post graduate degree in foreign jurisdiction and/or foreign working experience is an advantage
  • At least ten years of relevant experience in the areas of contract drafting and negotiations, preferably in the area of IT/new technologies and the (digital) distribution of offerings/services incl. the associated legal topics (agency law, unfair competition etc.), especially in the context of insurance/financial services
  • Good knowledge of the captive reinsurance business and experience in drafting, reviewing and negotiating captive reinsurance contracts.
  • Interest in (new) technology and related legal environment and eagerness to deepen specialist know-how in mentioned areas
  • Experience in working on global or regional projects and in coordinating lawyers in other jurisdictions
  • Sound negotiating skills as well as strong communication skills
  • Fluent written and spoken English and German skills, any other language is a plus
