Senior Operations & NPI Engineer – New Product Introduction

Shape the Future of Mechanical Circulatory Support

Join Abbott's Heart Failure business and play a key role in bringing the next generation of Circulatory Support Systems from development into global manufacturing.

As a Senior Operations & NPI Engineer, you will lead the industrialization, validation, and manufacturing scale-up of complex electromechanical medical devices that directly support critically ill cardiac patients. This is a highly visible role with the opportunity to influence product design, manufacturing strategy, supplier readiness, and production ramp-up from early development through commercial launch.

A unique aspect of this position is the close collaboration between our teams in Zurich, Switzerland and Pleasanton, California. Working as part of a highly integrated global organization, you will partner with experts across R&D, Quality, Regulatory, Operations, and Supplier Engineering to bring innovative technologies to market.

If you enjoy solving complex technical challenges, working hands-on with manufacturing processes, and turning innovative concepts into scalable products that improve patients' lives, we'd love to hear from you.

What You'll Do

  • Lead the transfer of new products from R&D into manufacturing, ensuring a smooth transition from prototype and low-volume production to scalable serial production.
  • Develop and implement manufacturing processes, production workflows, work instructions, and manufacturing documentation for new products.
  • Define manufacturing requirements and provide Design for Manufacturing (DfM), Design for Assembly (DfA), and Design for Test (DfT) feedback to R&D throughout the product development lifecycle.
  • Design, develop, qualify, and release production test systems, measurement equipment, fixtures, and tools required for manufacturing.
  • Establish manufacturing routings, Bills of Materials (BOMs), production versions, and associated master data within SAP.
  • Coordinate product industrialization activities with R&D, Project/Program Management, Supply Chain, Quality, Manufacturing, Procurement, and external suppliers.
  • Define process validation and product qualification strategies, including IQ/OQ/PQ activities where applicable.
  • Analyze manufacturing data using statistical methods to assess process capability, yield, throughput, and product quality.
  • Identify manufacturing risks and drive corrective and preventive actions to ensure successful product launches.
  • Support pilot builds, engineering builds, process validation activities, and ramp-up to volume production.
  • Ensure manufacturing documentation packages are complete, controlled, and released before production launch.
  • Drive continuous improvement initiatives focused on quality, cost, delivery, and manufacturing efficiency.

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Mechanical, Electrical, Manufacturing, Industrial, Mechatronics, or Software Engineering.
  • Minimum 3 years of experience in New Product Introduction, Manufacturing Engineering, Process Engineering, Industrialization, or Operations Engineering.
  • Experience working with cross-functional product development teams.
  • Basic knowledge of manufacturing processes, process validation, and production scaling.
  • Experience with ERP systems; SAP experience preferred.
  • Familiarity with statistical analysis and continuous improvement methodologies.
  • Fluent in English
  • German is an advantage.

Why Join Abbott?

  • Make a direct impact on technologies that help save and improve patients' lives.
  • Lead critical industrialization activities for a next-generation circulatory support platform.
  • Collaborate within a truly global team spanning Zurich, Switzerland and Pleasanton, California.
  • Influence both product design and manufacturing strategy early in the development process.
  • Work with advanced electromechanical medical technology in a highly innovative environment.
  • Grow your career within one of the world's leading healthcare companies.

The Best of Both Worlds

Our Zurich Heart Failure site is home to approximately 160 employees , offering the visibility, ownership, agility, and family-like culture often associated with a smaller organization. Here, you can see the direct impact of your work, build strong cross-functional relationships, and influence key decisions.

At the same time, you will be part of Abbott's global organization , collaborating closely with teams in Pleasanton, California and around the world while benefiting from the resources, stability, technology leadership, and career opportunities of one of the world's leading healthcare companies.
