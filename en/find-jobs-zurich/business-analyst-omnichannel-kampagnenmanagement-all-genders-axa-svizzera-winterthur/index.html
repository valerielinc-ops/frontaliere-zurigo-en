80-100% Winterthur / Smart Working

Every success story begins with faith in yourself.

As AXA, we see ourselves as a partner who stands by your side and believes in you. That is why we challenge and promote you and proactively show you possibilities for your future. We encourage you and give you freedom so that you can connect the things that are important to you. Together with your commitment and your individual contribution, this will become a forward-looking partnership. For you, for us and for our customers. And not just for the moment. But in the long term.

Together with campaign managers, we in the Omnichannel Campaign Solutions (OCS) team are developing innovative solutions for new campaigns and customer interactions along the customer journey. We design and technically implement customer contact channels (email, leads, letters, SMS, push notifications, agentic agents, ...) as well as the provision and management of data from all core processes and interactions used for campaign target groups and personalized content.

We are currently in an exciting transformation phase in which we are migrating our system from SAP marketing to the BSI Customer Suite. To support this transition and to further develop our campaign management data and system landscape, we are looking for a motivated person in business analysis to strengthen our team – during and after the migration.

In the area of Customer Experience & Strategy, we are committed to ensuring that our customers are just as enthusiastic about AXA as we are. To achieve this goal, we are constantly developing our products and strategies. We develop innovative, inspiring solutions – and present them simply and comprehensibly to our customers, our employees and our partners as well as media professionals.

This is what awaits you Embedded in our agile DevOps team, you contribute the following:

  • You actively support the development project for our new, future-oriented BSI platform and accompany the conversion of our previous system
  • As part of the agile products team, you take responsibility for the marketing platform, analyze business processes and requirements, and design outstanding, innovative, stable and high-performance campaign management solutions.
  • You bring that with you
  • You have at least 3-4 years of professional experience in Business Analysis, Requirements Engineering or Campaign Management (Marketing or Sales)
  • You have experience with campaign management systems & data selections and the number-based management of CRM campaigns.
  • You are open to new technologies, especially artificial intelligence. Ideally, you have initial practical experience with it and are ready to actively use its potential.
  • You are communicative, committed and enjoy working in an agile and self-organized team.
  • You have a goal- and solution-oriented implementation mentality, are enthusiastic, curious and proactive.
  • You have very good knowledge of German.
  • Experience in the BSI platform – especially with CDP or CX modules – is of great advantage and a strong differentiator.
