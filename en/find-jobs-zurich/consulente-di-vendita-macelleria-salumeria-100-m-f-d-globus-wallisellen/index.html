Full description

With great attention to detail, our employees make GLOBUS the leading premium and luxury address in Switzerland. It is people like you who make the difference and bring the “place we love to be” to life together with us. Here you make the difference

  • As a host, you make sure that our customers at the butcher and charcuterie counter feel welcome and well looked after as guests.
  • Thanks to your excellent service and individual advice, you can ensure unique shopping experiences.
  • You are jointly responsible for the freshness and quality of our high-quality products.
  • You ensure strict compliance with legal and operational hygiene guidelines and regulations.
  • With your positive charisma and your passion for our *delicatessa you contribute decisively to the company’s success.

That draws you out

  • You are a service-oriented personality with a high passion for high-quality food.
  • You have successfully completed an apprenticeship as a meat specialist EFZ, cook EFZ or retail specialist in the food sector and bring in-depth professional experience in the field of butchers or charcuterie.
  • You have a good relationship and a good appearance.
  • You are very reliable and act on your own initiative.
  • You have excellent knowledge of German
  • Other languages are not a must, but of great advantage for our international guests.

BENEFITS FOR YOU Together with around 1,700 other lovers of detail, you are an important part of our fascinating premium and luxury world. You experience real appreciation, work in a great team and we trust you so that you can use space and make a difference with your skills. If you understand your craft and bring a large portion of passion, then many career doors are open to you. We are happy to support you with internal but also external training and further education.

Also, look forward to

  • an exceptional working environment in the highest-grossing and most popular shopping centre in Switzerland.
  • an excellent public transport connection – tram and bus stop directly in front of the Glattzentrum.
  • Direct motorway connection (A1) if you come to work by car.
  • Free parking for employees as well as petrol stations and e-gas stations directly in front of the building.
  • A great roof terrace for employees to eat in the fresh air of Zmittag or simply to recharge with the sun.
  • Top food options thanks to smooth gastronomy: 14 restaurants and bars offer everything from delicacies, Mediterranean treats, Asian delicacies to takeaways.
  • 100 shops, including pharmacies, a post office and much more – the Glatt, the small shopping town! PS: Thanks to the smooth card you get various perks.
  • attractive social benefits.

  • the outfit pass with around 50% on 30 clothes per year for you.

  • a fairytale atmosphere during the extraordinary Glatt Christmas period.
