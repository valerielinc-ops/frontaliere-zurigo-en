Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate – that is what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family business today employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries across the Group, and the trend is rising. The company’s headquarters are located in the Canton of Glarus. In front of a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new, creative ideas in agile teams. The “Retail” division comprises more than 230 chocolateries in more than 25 countries. Here we offer our chocolate specialties and invite customers to experience fresh chocolate again and again. You will get the chance to complete a multi-year EFZ training for the retail trade in one of our Swiss chocolateries. There you will learn how to competently advise customers, decorate shop windows and shelves and organize different work processes. We familiarize you with hygiene regulations and food laws and introduce you to ordering. Do you want to enter the professional world and learn new things? Then come to us.

This is what awaits you

  • You will be supported and accompanied by us during the step between school and professional world
  • You will be looked after individually and personally by our branch managers
  • You will receive a diverse, practice-oriented education with very good prospects

You bring that with you

  • You love Schoggi
  • You have a secondary school degree (A) or a good secondary school degree (B)
  • You have a quick understanding and prefer a dynamic environment
  • You like contact with people and are communicative
  • You appreciate a careful working method and reliability

Own it. Grow it. Make it fresh. Sounds interesting? Then apply to us. Michael Notter, telephone +41 44 210 11 59, is at your disposal for any questions.
