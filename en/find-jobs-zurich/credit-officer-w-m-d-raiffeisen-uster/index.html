Full description

11.10.2026 Uster

Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Zürcher Oberland, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Uster, Gossau, Pfäffikon, Wetzikon and Hinwil, we are looking for a personality as a credit officer (m/f/d) with a place of work in Uster.

What awaits you?

  • Analysis, assessment and approval of credit transactions in the private and corporate customer segment within the jurisdictional order
  • Sparring partners and professional support for consultants in complex financing, risk and approval issues
  • Identification, assessment and active management of credit risks incl. Monitoring of existing commitments
  • Ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements and co-responsibility for data quality and reporting to bank management and board of directors
  • Internal professional responsibility with active transfer of know-how as well as participation in professional topics and projects

What do you bring?

  • Banking education with higher professional training in finance or banking
  • Several years of profound experience in the credit business (private and corporate customers)
  • Strong competences in the analysis, assessment and decision-making of credit risks
  • Experience in estimation and ideally knowledge of tools such as Wüest Dimensions
  • Independent, careful and structured working methods
  • High reliability, sense of responsibility and teamwork
