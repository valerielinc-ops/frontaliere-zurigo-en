Full description

137456 | 80 - 100% | Oerlikon, Zurich | Hybrid

Do you not only want to accompany transformation, but also actively shape it? Does it appeal to you to coordinate strategic programs, prepare decisions and work closely with executives and various specialist areas?

In this role, you are part of the Life business unit in the area of Strategy & Transformation at Zurich Switzerland. You support the Life Transformation Team in planning, managing and implementing strategic initiatives. You will work closely with transformation management and other stakeholders and help create transparency and ensure the successful implementation of central transformation projects.

This is what awaits you

  • You coordinate central activities within transformation programs, plan and track work packages, milestones, risks and dependencies, and ensure transparent reporting.
  • You prepare decisions, create management presentations, decision bases and status reports and actively engage in dialogue with decision makers.
  • You support senior management in the pragmatic, structured and efficient implementation of strategic initiatives and work closely with different specialist areas and stakeholders.
  • You will analyze measures and action plans in connection with operational events, accompany follow-up steps and contribute to the creation of regulatory documents and communication.
  • You identify potential for optimization in processes, workflows and controls and implement simplifications and automation – for example with AI tools and digital solutions – as well as organize workshops and program meetings, including professional preparation and follow-up.

This sets you apart

  • Higher education or comparable training in business administration, finance, business informatics or a related field
  • Several years of professional experience in the project, program or transformation environment, ideally in the insurance or financial services industry
  • Secure use of AI tools and digital solutions to simplify workflows and increase quality
  • Very good skills in the addressable preparation of presentations, reporting and decision documents as well as stylish German and English skills
  • Pronounced initiative, high quality standards, willingness to take responsibility, structured and reliable working methods as well as enjoyment of working with different stakeholders
