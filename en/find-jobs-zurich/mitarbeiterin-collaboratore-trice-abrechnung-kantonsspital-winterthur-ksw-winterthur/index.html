Full description

For our team billing we are looking for 1. October 2026 or by appointment a motivated, flexible and committed personality. Tasks:

  • Ensure correct, complete and timely billing of outpatient and inpatient cases
  • Collaboration in debt management
  • Recording of services and plausibility checking of the services covered
  • Checking tariff bases and correctly applied case handling
  • Handling of daily rejections and complaints
  • Advising all internal and external offices on all billing topics Profile:
  • Commercial and/or basic medical training and several years of professional experience in an acute hospital and/or professional experience in the provision of health insurance
  • In-depth technical and tariff expertise is beneficial (DRG, Tarmed and new since 1 January 2026 TARDOC and ambulatory packages)
  • In-depth knowledge of social insurance (KVG, IVG, UVG, VVG)
  • Appreciative and stylish communication
  • Strong attitude towards both internal and external services
  • Networked thinking and quick comprehension
  • Very good PC user knowledge Benefits: KSW offers employees conditions that enable them to reconcile their professional goals and personal needs. Flexible, flexible working hours, home office as well as a generous maternity leave and employment levels adapted to different situations – all this is possible at KSW, as long as the company allows it. Every year, the KSW offers 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses. We also support you temporally and/or financially with external training.
  • In extraordinary situations, e.g. B. prolonged illness, severe personal stress or difficult experiences in private life as well as in everyday work, various offers of conversation are available to you.
  • The appreciative and friendly cooperation within the teams and across the departments characterizes the working atmosphere at KSW. As a sign of a strong team spirit, the “you” was introduced as a standard at KSW. In addition, the KSW supports annual team events financially and organizes a large staffing event for the whole house every four years. KSW employees benefit from very good social benefits. Thus, the pension fund solution with BVK is far above the legal minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, KSW employees are very well covered.
  • With discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training opportunities and course offers, perks in pharmacies as well as at the most popular health insurance companies. You can benefit from a comprehensive offer for your health.
  • Our daycare center La Luna with spacious opening hours is located next to the KSW. In addition, you can use the income-dependent, financial support from the KSW for the crib costs.
  • In cooperation with cultural partners in and around Winterthur, we regularly offer discounts for concerts and theater performances. The borrowing of media in the in-house library is also free of charge for employees.
