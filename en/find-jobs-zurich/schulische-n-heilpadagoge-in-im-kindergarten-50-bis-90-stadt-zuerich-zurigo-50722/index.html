Full description

At our location in the school building Staudenbühl in Zurich Seebach we are looking for a school curative teacher in kindergarten 50% to 90% from August 2026.

Tasks

  • Class leadership of the kindergarten class together with the social pedagogue
  • Preparation of funding planning
  • Promotion of children according to the goals defined in the SSG and the curriculum 21
  • Preparation of certificates and learning reports
  • Cooperation with parents or legal guardians, external specialist bodies and the regular school
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation

Profile

  • EDK-approved curative education or the willingness to complete the training part-time
  • Practical experience in the special education sector or the willingness to become familiar with this task.
  • Joy in the promotion and accompaniment of children with cognitive impairment
  • Teamwork, motivation, resilience, humour, flexibility and creativity
  • Willingness and interest in interdisciplinary cooperation

We offer

  • Self-employed and varied work in a professional team
  • Exciting interdisciplinary tasks in the special education sector
  • Possibility of active participation in our school
  • Conditions of employment in accordance with the requirements of the City of Zurich (VLT)
  • Regular working hours
  • Motivated and competent team of teaching assistants

About us

The Heil Pädagogschule der Stadt Zürich is a special day school for 4- to 15-year-old children with a cognitive and/or multiple impairment. We offer the pupils who are taught in separate classes in different school buildings in the city of Zurich a supportive environment and place the focus of the interdisciplinary promotion in their strengths.

Our teams at the various locations in the city of Zurich consist of curative school teachers, social teachers, specialists, caregivers, learners and assistants.
