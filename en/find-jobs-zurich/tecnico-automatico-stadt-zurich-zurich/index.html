Full description

One thing we definitely don’t have to offer: boredom. With us, pure variety awaits you. You come into play for maintenance, inspections and repairs of technical systems and systems such as passenger displays, safety systems and gate controls.

Tasks

  • With us, pure variety awaits you. You come into play for maintenance, inspections and repairs of technical systems and systems such as passenger displays, safety systems and gate controls.
  • As a professional, this is just as easy for you as the commissioning of technically demanding new systems – such as a point control.
  • You provide technical support for the depot and garage employees and also support the “Engineering” department if necessary.
  • In addition, you create practical instructions, fill out work reports and fit them into the SAP.
  • An all-round exciting job that actually only has a “small but”: Working in difficult space, noise and light conditions should not matter to you. And also construction site assignments and pikettdienst belong from time to time. Want to get in? We look forward to seeing you.

Profile

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as an electronics engineer or automatic engineer or installer with knowledge of electrical engineering.
  • Ideally, you can have several years of professional experience in the field of metrology, industrial electronics or technical maintenance.
  • Good MS Office knowledge and SAP basic knowledge is beneficial.
  • Category B driving licence.
  • You are performance-oriented and are used to working independently and independently.
  • You like to work hand in hand with others – You are happy to accept the help of your colleagues and you are happy to support them as much as you can.
  • Your analytical and networked thinking rounds off your profile.
