Full description

Due to retirement, we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by arrangement a committed leader as co-team leader for the meeting point City/Frauentreff.

Tasks

  • Together with your co-management, you will take over the professional, personnel and organizational leadership as well as the further development of the City/Frauentreff meeting point.
  • They guide, accompany and promote a team of 18 employees and create the framework for a professional, committed and appreciative team culture.
  • They control human and operational resources, plan personnel deployment and participate in the further development of quality, structures and offers.
  • They work on projects and processes and maintain cooperation with internal and external stakeholders.
  • They actively participate in day-to-day operations, take over shift services (even weekends) and carry out short and individual consultations.
  • You support the department management in strategic and operational business and keep an eye on the financial framework conditions.

Profile

  • Completed studies in social work FH or comparable training
  • Leadership experience beneficial and willingness to complete leadership training
  • Proven experience in low-threshold social work, ideally in the area of addiction, health and social integration
  • Communicative and networked personality with structured, exact working methods and business understanding
  • They are characterised by openness, humour and flexibility and promote appreciative and collegial cooperation.

We offer

  • A meaningful leadership task with a high degree of creative freedom and the possibility of setting new impulses together with a co-direction
  • A dedicated, versatile team with an acceptance-oriented attitude
  • Varied everyday work at two different locations
  • Attractive employment conditions with very good social benefits
  • Diverse training and further education offer of the city of Zurich
  • Various discounts (mobile phone, lunch checks, mobility contribution, etc.)

About us

With around 2,400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich is committed to a social Zurich every day. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.

Do you want to further develop a low-threshold offer with social significance and take on responsibility together with a committed team? Then you are right with us.

The City meeting point and the Frauentreff are low-threshold locations with offers in the areas of protection and prevention. They are aimed at adults who are in challenging living situations and are affected, for example, by poverty, homelessness, mental difficulties and/or addictive addiction.
