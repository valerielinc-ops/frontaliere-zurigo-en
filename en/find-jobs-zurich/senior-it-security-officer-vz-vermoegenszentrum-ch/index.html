Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We advise individuals and companies on all matters relating to money, manage assets and offer attractive financial services. As we are growing strongly, we are looking for reinforcements for our IT team at our headquarters at Paradeplatz in Zurich.

  • You help ensure the confidentiality, integrity and availability of our IT services (infrastructure, applications, data) both on-premise and in the cloud (M365, Azure).
  • You work with different areas (e.g. infrastructure, software development, architecture, security operation center and risk) and ensure the implementation and further development of security measures.
  • You are actively involved in projects and projects and support in all IT security issues.
  • You develop technical security concepts and architectures and then ensure their implementation and monitoring.
  • You will help evaluate new services and assess the security measures of partners and suppliers.
  • You accompany audits and coordinate security initiatives (e.g. penetration tests).
  • Training in computer science and at least 5 years of professional experience as a security engineer, security analyst or system engineer
  • Certifications such as CISSP or OSCP are beneficial
  • Strong ability to capture complex issues and work on them in line with target groups as well as a pragmatic and solution-oriented approach
  • Knowledge of Vulnerability Management, Data Loss Prevention, SIEM and/or Security Incident Response
  • Experience and technical knowledge with current IT technologies (e.g. container services) as well as Microsoft services (e.g. Defender XDR, Sentinel, Purview or M365)
  • Every year you have three days and CHF 1'000 free for further education. We support larger external training as part of your job profile.
  • You work at a modern and air-conditioned workplace in the center of Zurich and have the possibility of two days of home office per week. For this we provide notebooks, which allows flexible working.
  • In a collegial working environment with regular social events, an exciting field of activity awaits you as well as the opportunity to take on responsibility and work independently.
  • The job is also open for part-time work, so that there is more time for further education, family, sports and projects that are important to you.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of the VZ family and experience it for yourself!
