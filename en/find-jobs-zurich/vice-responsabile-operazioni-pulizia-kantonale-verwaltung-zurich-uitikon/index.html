Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Uitikon Stv. Operations Management Cleaning 80 - 100% Prison and Reintegration (JuWe) is part of the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs of the Canton of Zurich and employs around 1,400 women and men with a wide range of professional profiles. JuWe is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as the implementation of the various types of detention. For the employees of JuWe, the successful social reintegration of people who have committed crimes and the prevention of recidivism is of particular importance. The Uitikon Action Center (MZU) is the JuWe’s socio-pedagogical-therapeutic competence center for the execution of penalties and measures for juvenile and young adult offenders.
