Full description

Tasks

  • As a quality inspector for outgoing goods (m/f/d), you ensure compliance with customer-specific specifications for shipping material.
  • You carry out visual and measurement checks for damage and dimensional accuracy.
  • You also create COCs, completion reports and error messages according to the process.
  • Labeling and prepackaging spare parts and supplies according to product and customer requirements is also part of your area of ​​responsibility.
  • Identification of spare and supplier parts according to specifications
  • Releasing and blocking products in outgoing goods
  • Participation and support in customer acceptances
  • Checking the Q certificates for completeness and accuracy
  • Put together parts ranges according to the order
  • Preparation of the items including the associated quality documents for external acceptance
  • Participation in incoming goods inspection

Requirements

  • Completed mechanical apprenticeship with ideally additional training in quality assurance (QA)
  • Further training in measurement technology, at least AUKOM 1 and VT1&2 is required
  • Several years of professional experience in the field of quality assurance in the field of length measurement technology
  • Good knowledge of the methods and procedures of quality assurance and measurement technology
  • Good knowledge of relevant IT applications and reporting tools (MS Office, SAP Q module, PPS systems)
  • Good knowledge of ISO 9001:2015, EN9100 would be an advantage
  • Good knowledge of reading technical drawings and interpreting them
  • Very good knowledge of spoken and written German
  • Assertiveness, decision-making skills, motivated and a team player

We offer

  • A dynamic working environment with exciting projects
  • Flexible working hours (flexitime and trust working hours)
  • Very good social benefits and a non-mandatory pension fund
  • Employee benefits of a large corporation, employee share purchase program
  • Promoting needs-based further training
  • Staff restaurant as well as external catering and shopping options nearby
  • Individual internal and external personnel development measures in collaboration with our Rheinmetall Academy
  • Workplace a few minutes' walk from Zurich-Oerlikon train station
