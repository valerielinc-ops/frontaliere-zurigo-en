Full description

For the medical clinic, which runs several stations with a total of 68 beds, an ambulatory and special consultation hours, we are looking for a dynamic and experienced personality as a supplement to our medical secretariat team.

Tasks

  • Working as medical secretary / medical secretary in the field of dermatology / cardiology / Med. Ambulatorium
  • Receiving outpatients
  • Scheduling of angiological consultation hours, examinations and appointment coordination
  • Editing and sending medical and insurance reports
  • General secretarial services such as postal processing, telephone service and scanning
  • telephone and written contact with internal and external contacts
  • Performance billing and controlling
  • Mutual support as well as holiday and sickness representation within the team

Requirements

  • Completed commercial training with additional training as medical secretary / medical secretary or MPA / FAGE with additional training medical secretary
  • at least two years of professional experience in a hospital or medical practice
  • Experience in disposition and preparation of medical reports
  • stylish German in word and writing as well as strong communication skills
  • Resilient and reliable personality with quick comprehension and a high level of initiative
  • pleasant manners and pleasure in patient contact
  • Good MS Office skills, KISIM and TARDOC skills are beneficial

We offer

  • Working in a committed team
  • Responsible and versatile work in a dynamic environment
  • Regulated working hours without evening and weekend services
  • Above-average social benefits
  • Excellent staff restaurant and numerous discounts
  • Central location near Uster station and motorway connection
  • ZVV BonusPass or cheap parking - with Parkingpay, max. CHF 4.50 per day
