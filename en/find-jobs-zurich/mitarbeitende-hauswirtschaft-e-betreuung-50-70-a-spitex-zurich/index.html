Full description

Are you looking for a meaningful activity in which you enable people to live longer self-determined in their home?

In the area of Home & Care, you are close to people: you support the household, accompany in everyday life and help our customers continue to live in their usual environment.

(a)lle are addressed. Gender, origin, age? We are interested in your qualification and enthusiasm for work.

YOUR TASKS

  • You ensure that our customers feel at home and can live in their familiar environment for a long time. The main part of your work is household management (maintenance cleaning/weekend return). This includes cleaning living rooms and bathrooms, laundry care, creating order, and occasionally doing simple tasks such as pouring plants or caring for pets.
  • In addition, you sometimes accompany our customers in everyday life, for example through personal conversations or by supporting them in various small activities.
  • You work in different households and deal respectfully and openly with the different living and living situations of our customers.
  • You document your services and observations electronically in our Perigon system – simple and understandable.

You bring with you

  • Professional experience in the service sector (e.g. housekeeping, gastronomy) or a completed training in this field
  • Knowledge of German (at least level B2) and the ability to understand Swiss German
  • Completed course of housekeeping and supervision or basic course as a home helper
  • Service orientation as well as reliability, independence and flexibility
  • Basic knowledge of electronic devices and willingness to further develop them

You find us at least.

  • Family-friendly employment conditions with working hours from Monday to Friday between 08:00 – 17:00
  • You also benefit from excellent social benefits
  • Our other benefits can be found here: https://jobs.spitex-zuerich.ch/benefits/
