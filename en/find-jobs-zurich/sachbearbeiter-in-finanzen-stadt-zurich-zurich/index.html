Full description

For support in the finance department of the technical school Viventa we are looking for 01. January 2027, one clerk in finance, 60%.

Tasks

  • They process all creditors and debtors incl. Payment and debt collection.
  • They independently run the cash register incl. Balance control.
  • They record the receipts of payments.
  • You are responsible for the reconciliation of accounts.
  • They carry out various administrative tasks.

Profile

  • Basic commercial training
  • Professional experience in accounting
  • Very good user experience in Office 365, IT affinity
  • Precise, independent and reliable working methods
  • Enjoyment in dealing with different contact persons

We offer

We offer you a versatile and challenging task in a dynamic and innovative environment. They benefit from attractive employment conditions according to urban specifications and contemporary infrastructure at a central workplace.

About us

The technical school Viventa is an educational offer of the city of Zurich. We are a competence center for vocational preparation, integration, adult and parent education and run the special school Viventa15plus.
