Full description

Do you want to improve processes, use data and achieve visible results? At Delica, you actively shape the operational excellence of our production. Together with the teams, you will analyze processes, implement improvements and help to increase efficiency, quality and stability sustainably.

Luogo di lavoro: Delica AG, Delica AG miles, Bruechstrasse 70, 8706 miles

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You will analyze production and support processes, identify potential for improvement and support their successful implementation.
  • You are actively involved in the introduction and application of lean methods such as 5S, KVP, shop floor management or problem solving.
  • You support the construction and further development of KPI systems, dashboards and visualizations for data-based control of operations.
  • You work in cross-departmental improvement projects and, after the familiarization phase, assume responsibility for complete projects, sub-projects and concrete implementation measures.
  • You support managers and employees in sustainably anchoring continuous improvements in everyday work.

Requirements

  • 1-3 years: Professional experience in the production, process management, lean or continuous improvement environment.
  • Completed degree (uni/ETH, FH, HF): in supply chain management, engineering, business administration or comparable, ideally with a focus on process management or operational excellence, and further education in lean management (e.g. Lean Six Sigma Green Belt or comparable)
  • You convince through your communication strength, enjoy working with different stakeholders and implement changes pragmatically and sustainably.
  • You work analytically, structured and solution-oriented and enjoy continuously improving processes and processes.
  • You bring first knowledge in Lean Management, OperationalExcellence or CVP. Experience with Power BI, SAP S/4HANA or comparable digital tools is an advantage.
  • in German (negotiation certain)
