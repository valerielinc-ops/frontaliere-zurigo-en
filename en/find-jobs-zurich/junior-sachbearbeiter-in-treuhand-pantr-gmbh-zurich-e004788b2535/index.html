Full description

For our customer – a young, established trust company with bright and modern offices in a central location in Zurich and Lenzburg – we are looking for a motivated reinforcement. You can expect an extremely versatile and exciting area of work with great development opportunities, in which you can participate in both the specialist area and organizational development. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for you!

This is your job

  • Trust & Finance: Assistance with accounting mandates (creditors, debtors, wages, finances, financial statements) as well as assistance with tax returns, VAT declarations, financial planning and consulting mandates.
  • Financial planning: Support in financial planning and monitoring of companies.
  • Administration: Creating reports, presentations and correspondence as well as making phone calls, orders and reservations.
  • Growth & HR: Support for organizational development, third-party evaluation and recruitment.

This is what you bring with you

  • Training: basic commercial training, ideally in escrow, or first relevant work experience in escrow.
  • Continuing education: Ongoing or planned further education (professional: in Treuhand, ed. Professional ID Treuhand or expert / accounting and controlling.
  • IT & language skills: Knowledge of the ERP system Abacus, perfect German in word and writing as well as good English skills as a big plus.
  • Working method: A pronounced flair for numbers, good organizational talent, a quick comprehension and high concentration ability coupled with enthusiasm for trusting.

This is what awaits you from the customer

  • Diversity: A varied area of responsibility in which you can actively participate in the further growth of the company.
  • Flexibility: annual working hours with flexible working hours and the possibility to work regularly in the home office.
  • Environment: A young, dynamic team and modern, centrally located workplaces in Zurich and Lenzburg.
