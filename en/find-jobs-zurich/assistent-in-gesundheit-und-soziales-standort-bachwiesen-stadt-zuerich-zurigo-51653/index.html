Full description

Do you enjoy working with people? Would you like to join our care team at the Gesundheitszentrum für das Alter Bachwiesen from December 1, 2026, or by agreement?

Tasks

  • You share responsibility for the careful support and care of our residents.
  • You support the team in engaging residents and organizing their daily routines, as well as in conducting resident activities.
  • You maintain a professional and respectful relationship with relatives.
  • You are familiar with handling electronic care documentation and complete it correctly within your area of competence.
  • You make independent decisions within your area of responsibility.

Profile

  • Several years of professional experience in the field of support and care
  • An independent and responsible approach to work
  • Completed training as an Assistant in Health and Social Care EBA
  • Good spoken and written German skills, including Swiss German dialect
  • A big heart and a great deal of understanding for our residents

We offer

Look forward to an exciting range of responsibilities in a professional, team-oriented working environment with a first-name culture throughout the company. People are at the heart of what we do. That is why we support compatibility between private and professional life; different working models make this possible. In this position, you can actively contribute to choosing your workload and working hours.

As part of the City of Zurich and thanks to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, progressive employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

The annual salary for this position ranges from Fr. 58'532.– to Fr. 78'210.– (100%), depending on experience.

About us

The City of Zurich's geriatric centers are one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in elderly care and nursing - with expertise, heart and a comprehensive range of services from a single source.

The Bachwiesen geriatric center in Albisrieden (District 9) offers specialized care and support for people with dementia-related conditions. Benefit from our many years of expertise in geriatric medicine. Together with 120 colleagues, we would like to work for the well-being of 64 residents.
