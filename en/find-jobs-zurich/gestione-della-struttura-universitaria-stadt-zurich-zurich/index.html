Full description

Facility Management operates all properties and rental properties. It ensures that users find optimal conditions in the workplace in order to carry out their activities. The planning and construction-related facility management process ensures that the VBZ properties can be operated sustainably over the entire life cycle.

We are looking for you, with fresh ideas and a clear, analytical mindset. Together we not only want to master the daily challenges in facility management, but also actively enrich the team with your perspective and go new ways.

Tasks

  • Participation in technical property management of VBZ properties
  • Planning, organisation and implementation of removals
  • Verification of services and quality assurance
  • Recording and mutation of technical building data
  • Carrying out plannable maintenance
  • Access and security management support
  • Developing office concepts for worksmart@vbz
  • Participation in construction-related facility management

Profile

  • Bachelor degree at a university of applied sciences in the field of facility management
  • In addition, you have good user knowledge in M365
  • Interest in holistic facility management
  • Connected and analytical thinking
  • Interested in challenging tasks in plant and building management

We offer

  • A lot of appreciation for what you do: The people of Zurich love their VBZ. Working here is proud.
  • We want you to feel good: We are certified “Friendly Workspace” and are strongly committed to your health.
  • Discover the whole of Switzerland with the free GA – and thanks to generous discounts, the family also drives really well.
