From August 2027 we are looking for two motivated, curious and willing personalities.

YOUR TASKS

During your apprenticeship, you will be deployed in a new department every six months. So you get to know different exciting areas – for example:

  • Receipt
  • Personnel department
  • Finance Department
  • Marketing
  • Invoicing
  • Procurement and Logistics

These departments are partly in the corporate office and partly in the Hirslanden clinic at the Balgrist – so a lot of variety and exciting insights into the health care system!

You bring that to me.

  • You complete the Sek-A degree with an average of at least 4.5 to 5 in the majors.
  • You can express yourself well orally and in writing in German, are friendly, helpful and perform safely.
  • You find the healthcare system exciting, motivated and constantly want to learn new things.
  • You already have experience with the 10-finger system – maybe even already taken a course?

Already in the first week you can benefit from many advantages:

  • Varied menus in our staff restaurant at an advantage price
  • Fitness 24/7: Free access to our fitness room (including lunch classes)
  • Diverse training thanks to assignments in different departments
  • Personal and professional support during the entire apprenticeship
  • A lively workplace with motivated teams
  • Assumption of costs for teaching materials and participation in the costs of your personal work equipment (BYOD)

Have you become curious?

Then apply online with the following documents

  • Letter of motivation
  • CV with photo
  • Certificates of upper school & possibly. Testimonials/internship evidence
  • Result of your multicheck
  • Confirmations of trial apprentices or internships

For additional information, please contact Noémie Sassano (Apprenticeship Manager Corporate Office) at noemie.sassano@hirslanden.ch. Celina Timmer is looking forward to your full online application.

Please note that we cannot consider paper dossiers (not returned) or applications by e-mail. Therefore, please apply exclusively online via this tool.
