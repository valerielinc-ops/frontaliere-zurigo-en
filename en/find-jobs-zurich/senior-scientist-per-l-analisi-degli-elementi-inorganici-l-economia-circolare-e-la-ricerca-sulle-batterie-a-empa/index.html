Full description

Empa combines materials science and technology with application-oriented research for a sustainable future. As a research institution of the ETH Domain, we work closely with partners from science, industry and the public sector.

Senior Scientist for Inorganic Elemental Analysis, Circular Economy and Battery Research (a)

For our Analytical Center in Dübendorf, we are looking for a committed personality who will shape a leading competence center for elemental and material analysis. We want to support research at Empa and expand our activities in the areas of circular economy, recycling, sustainable resource use and battery technologies. We focus on efficient analytics and close cooperation with science, industry and authorities.

In this key position, you combine challenging analytical tasks with the development of new research fields and the acquisition of projects. They shape the scientific and strategic development of the Analytical Center and strengthen its national and international visibility.

Their tasks

  • Conducting sophisticated inorganic elemental analyses and developing and validating new methods with ICP-MS, ICP-OES, AAS, WD-XRF and LA-ICP-MS
  • Planning and management of research, development and service projects for Empa-internal research groups, industry and authorities
  • Expansion of our activities in the areas of circular economy, recycling, urban mining and battery technologies
  • Acquisition of new projects and development and maintenance of cooperation with universities, industry and public institutions
  • Professional and personnel management as well as further development of scientific and technical employees
  • Co-design of the Analytical Center as a competence center for elemental and material analysis

Your profile

  • University degree in chemistry or a related natural science discipline, preferably with a doctorate
  • Several years of experience in inorganic element analysis, in particular with ICP-MS and ICP-OES
  • In-depth knowledge in method development, quality assurance and laboratory operation
  • Experience in the acquisition and management of research or industrial projects
  • Management experience or experience in the professional management of scientific and technical staff
  • Knowledge of circular economy, recycling, materials science or battery research
  • Independent, initiative and team-oriented personality with strong communication skills and pleasure in building new collaborations
  • Very good knowledge of German and English

Our offer

We offer you a versatile task at the interface of analytical service and application-oriented research. You work with modern analytical infrastructure in an interdisciplinary, internationally networked environment. In doing so, you will have room for manoeuvre to implement your own ideas, open up new research fields and further develop the Analytical Center.

The term of office shall initially be two years. There is the possibility of subsequent permanent employment.
