More about this search for cross-border applicants

Basel is a key hub for research jobs in Switzerland, particularly in life sciences, pharmaceuticals, and chemical industries. The city hosts numerous international companies and research institutions, offering diverse opportunities for professionals in scientific, technical, and academic roles. Cross-border workers from northern Italy often seek positions here due to Basel’s proximity and competitive salaries. While commuting is convenient, it is important for frontaliers to be aware of Swiss work permits, taxation rules, and social security agreements that apply to cross-border employment. The local labor market continues to demand skilled researchers, making Basel an attractive destination for career growth in research fields.
