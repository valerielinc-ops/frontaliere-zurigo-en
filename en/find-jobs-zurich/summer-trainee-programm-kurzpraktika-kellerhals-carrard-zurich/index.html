##

  • Their main tasks

Do you want to gain insight into the working world of a commercial law firm and gain practical experience?

At the Zurich location, we offer students of law at a Swiss university with a completed bachelor’s degree the opportunity to gain practical experience in our law firm.

What you bring

As a summer trainee you support our partners and their teams for 5 weeks. They participate in the processing of current mandates and will work on suitable solutions in the team. You will also participate in workshops, lectures and specially tailored events. Our lawyers will answer all questions that have always interested you, so that you can get an accurate idea of how business law firms are doing.

What we offer you

Free places from summer 2027 . Applications will be received between the beginning of August 2026 and the end of October 2026. The selection will generally take place in November 2026.

If you are interested in an internship with us, we look forward to your application.

About us

We are a leading Swiss law firm with offices in Basel, Bern, Geneva, Lausanne/Sion, Lugano and Zurich as well as internationally in Shanghai and Tokyo. We advise discerning national and international customers – companies, organisations and private individuals – in a highly competent manner in all legal matters.
