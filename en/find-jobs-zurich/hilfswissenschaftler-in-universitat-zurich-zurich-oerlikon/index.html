Jacobs Center for Productive Youth Development

Job opening 1.11.2026 or by agreement, temporary

As part of the research project "Mapping the margins of digital harm: Intersectional pathways in Swiss adolescents' experiences of online sexual harassment and abuse", a collaboration between the Jacobs Center for Productive Youth Development, the Institute of Epidemiology, Biostatistics and Prevention and the Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich, a student assistant is sought for support in the qualitative part of the project for 6 months. This project is about how young people experience sexual harassment and violence in the digital space. The qualitative part examines which support offers young people know and use and how they perceive and assess different situations with the help of situation vignettes. Data is collected at schools in the Canton of Zurich at the beginning of 2027 through interviews and focus groups. The project is funded by the Population Research Center. More information about the project: here

  • Assistance in communication and coordination with schools and teachers
  • Preparation and support in the implementation of qualitative data collection with young people
  • Literature research
