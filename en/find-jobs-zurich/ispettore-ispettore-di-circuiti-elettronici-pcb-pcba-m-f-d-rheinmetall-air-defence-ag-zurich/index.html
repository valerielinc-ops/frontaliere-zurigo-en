Full description

Where we are looking for them

  • Incoming goods and quality control of electronic components, assemblies, unloaded (PCB) and loaded printed circuit boards (PCBA)
  • Optical inspection and processing testing based on technical drawings, specifications and test instructions
  • Detailed testing for damage, solder quality, correct assembly, markings and completeness
  • Conducting dimension, visual and documentation tests strictly in accordance with the test plan and quality requirements
  • Testing of CoC (Certificate of Conformity), test protocols, measurement reports, material certificates, proof of conformity and technical manufacturer and supplier documentation
  • Ensuring the completeness and accuracy of all accompanying documents in accordance with customer specifications and specifications
  • Creation, processing and tracking of error messages in the event of material, document or supplier deviations
  • Physical blocking and documentation of faulty materials as well as active support for root cause analyses and supplier complaints
  • Close, cross-departmental cooperation with the areas of purchasing, development, production, logistics and quality assurance
  • Participation in test plans, test instructions, test strategies, as well as support for initial samples, re-qualifications and technical acceptances WHAT YOU SHOULD TEACH
  • Technical training as electronics technician, electronics installer EFZ or equivalent
  • Practice in incoming goods inspection, quality assurance or electronics manufacturing is advantageous
  • Knowledge of PCB/PCBA exam and ideally IPC standards (e.g. IPC-A-610, IPC-J-STD-001)
  • Safe to read technical drawings, parts lists, specifications and quality documentation
  • Experience in the SAP environment (preferably error messages) as well as good MS Office knowledge.
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • English skills are beneficial
  • High quality awareness, pronounced detail as well as structured, careful and independent action
  • Strong technical understanding of electronics coupled with analytical thinking and solution-oriented approach
  • Capacity for quality, strong teamwork and enjoyment of cross-sectoral collaboration
  • Ideally, knowledge of IPC standards (e.g. IPC600, IPC-A-610, IPC-J-STD-001)
  • A dynamic working environment with exciting projects
  • Flexible working hours (active and trusted working hours)
  • Very good social benefits and a super-compulsory pension fund
  • Employee benefits of a large corporation, employee share purchase program
  • Promotion of needs-based training
  • Staff restaurant and external catering and shopping facilities nearby
  • Individual internal and external personnel development measures in cooperation with our Rheinmetall Academy
  • Workplace a few minutes walk from the Zurich-Oerlikon train station KONTAKTDETAILS Responsible contact: Michael Zmoos For questions about your application, please use the contact form. Please note that dossiers of recruiters are not considered.
