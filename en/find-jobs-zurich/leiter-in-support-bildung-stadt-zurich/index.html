Full description

We are looking for a committed personality as of 1 January 2027 or by agreement.

Tasks

  • Management of the departments Vocational Education, Management, Finance and Springer*innen-Pool for the division Child Care and other departments by agreement.
  • Recruitment, coaching, target assessment and promotion of directly assigned employees in compliance with personnel policy standards.
  • Advising and supporting the operational and day-care management in all relevant departmental and development topics.
  • Responsibility for achieving the quantitative and qualitative objectives in the allocated offers and portfolios.
  • Management and participation in cross-sectoral projects.
  • Representation of the ministries to all stakeholders, public relations in consultation with the Communications Department.

Profile

  • Master’s/University level education (educational sciences, social work, psychology), supplemented by CAS or MAS training in leadership
  • Sound management experience as well as business thinking and a good understanding of administrative processes
  • High planning and organizational competence as well as experience in project management
  • Strong social and self-skills: open communication style, conflict capability, solution orientation, flexibility and networked thinking
  • Strong cooperation ability and enjoyment of working with different stakeholders, teams and leaders

We offer

You can expect a meaningful and exciting task in an organizational unit in which cooperative cooperation is a priority and an employer who offers good working conditions and employees a wide range of opportunities for further development.

About us

With around 2,400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a social Zurich. We support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.

To accompany and support young children in their development in the important first years and thus give them a good foundation for their future life: we are committed to this with great commitment.

The childcare division of the social institutions and companies operates 11 daycare centers in the city of Zurich, a crisis intervention group and a residential group for infants, a family meeting place and at two locations "accompanied visiting meeting places" for families in conflictive separation situations.

We offer a flat hierarchy - embedded in the administration -, agile structures, self-responsibility, a learning culture and an appreciative approach in our organization.
