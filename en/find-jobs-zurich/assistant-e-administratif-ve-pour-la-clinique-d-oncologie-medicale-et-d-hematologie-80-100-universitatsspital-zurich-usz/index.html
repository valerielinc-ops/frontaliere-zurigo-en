Work in an exciting and versatile environment: The Clinic for Medical Oncology and Hematology combines highly specialized patient care, modern diagnostics, teaching and international research under one roof. Together with our interdisciplinary teams, we make an important contribution to patients and the medical future every day.

Their main tasks

  • Clinical performance management and control
  • Processing of complaints and rejections by insurers
  • Preparation of internal clinic evaluations and statistics
  • Calculation and monitoring of patient depots
  • Settlement of accounts payable and payable
  • Support of the clinic manager in internal and hospital-wide projects
  • Participation in process optimization in the assigned areas of responsibility
  • Deputy within the team

Your profile

  • Commercial or business training completed
  • Knowledge and experience in healthcare is beneficial
  • Experienced handling of MS-Office in particular. Excel (knowledge in hospital information systems, SAP is an advantage)
  • Affinity to Numbers and Analytical Thinking
  • Responsible, resilient personality with structured, goal-oriented and independent working methods
  • High willingness to learn and the ability to quickly familiarize yourself with new tasks
  • Flexibility and motivation to participate proactively in a dynamic environment
