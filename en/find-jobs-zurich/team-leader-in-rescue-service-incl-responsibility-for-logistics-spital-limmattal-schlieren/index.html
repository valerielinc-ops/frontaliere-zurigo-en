Description

Our hospital-based rescue service performs over 7,100 missions annually, at our locations in Schlieren and Regersdorf. Since March 2020, our emergency service has been an emergency training facility.

The area of operation includes ten Limmattal municipalities as well as the seven municipalities of the Zurich Furttal planning group and includes active neighbourhood assistance in the neighbouring communities as well as the city of Zurich. Through close cooperation with partner organisations such as police, fire brigade and other rescue services, we ensure fast, competent and complete care.

As a training company, we train several students each year to become a medical assistant. The flexible service planning in the rescue service enables an optimal work-life balance.

External employment agencies are not included.

Here you get involved

  • Professional, lls-adapted, medical and rescue care and care for patients in primary and secondary operations
  • day responsibility in shift operations
  • operational management at the site of operation in emergency vehicles
  • taking over of various administrative activities
  • support of the management of emergency service in organisational and professional matters
  • organization and management of the department logistics

Requirements

  • have completed Swiss training as a rescue paramedic or recognition of training by the SRK
  • have professional experience in Swiss rescue services
  • have a precise working method
  • are an organisational talent with good and solution-oriented problem solving skills
  • have flair and skills in the administrative field
  • have a high social and self-competence
