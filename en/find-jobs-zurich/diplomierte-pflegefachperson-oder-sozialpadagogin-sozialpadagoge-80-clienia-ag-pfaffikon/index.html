Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With over 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric services offered by Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in outpatient, day clinic and inpatient settings. The group employs around 1800 people and generated sales of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For our area of child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy, we are looking for a

The acute care unit Z1 specialises in the care of children and adolescents in acute mental crisis situations. We offer immediate psychiatric emergency care with precise diagnostics, targeted treatment and rapid clarification of the social situation. Our interprofessional team provides comprehensive psychiatric-psychotherapeutic and milieutherapeutic treatment. This includes an individual range of therapies as well as a close exchange with the social environment of the young patients. The overarching goal of our work is the psychological stabilisation of those affected and the promotion of individual coping strategies. There are 10 patient places available on the ward. If you are interested in actively participating in the further development of our ward and making a positive contribution to our work, we look forward to receiving your application!

Your tasks Active and present support for children and adolescents, this is exactly what our work in child and adolescent psychiatry entails.

This includes

  • the individual and resource-oriented support of our patients within a milieu-therapeutic treatment setting
  • the work with caregivers and co-creative interprofessional cooperation
  • Needs- and resource-oriented planning of stay and care
  • conducting individual and family discussions and leading therapeutic group offers
  • the design of the daily structure and leisure activities (actively leading and accompanying)
  • Active contact with the system of origin

Your profile You are flexible, innovative and enjoy variety. You bring expertise, enthusiasm for the children's and youth sector and a lot of humor paired with empathy. With these qualities, we achieve the best treatment results for our patients and we are pleased that you will become part of the team.

In addition, we would like to

  • Experience in the field of child and adolescent psychiatry
  • An open, communicative and present personality with the willingness to continuously educate oneself and pass on knowledge to the nursing team
