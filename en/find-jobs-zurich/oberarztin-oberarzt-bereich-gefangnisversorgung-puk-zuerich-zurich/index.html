Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich is a leading institution for the treatment and care of mentally ill people of all ages. With more than 2,500 employees in 55 different occupational profiles, we work in interdisciplinary teams on the holistic care of our patients, from clarification and therapy to follow-up care. Our work is based on mutual trust and personal responsibility and together we promote an innovative corporate culture. Prison care is part of PUK’s Forensic Psychiatry and Psychotherapy (Director: Prof. E. Habermeyer). It is responsible for the psychiatric care of persons detained in prisons of the Prison Office.
