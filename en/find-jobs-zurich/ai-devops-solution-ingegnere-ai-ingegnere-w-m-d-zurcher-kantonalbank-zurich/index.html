80% - 100% | IT / Business Engineering | Zurich | Experienced professionals In our IT department, we develop the technology of tomorrow with over 1,000 colleagues – right in the heart of Zurich. Together with you, we want to celebrate successes and live our values: act courageously, bring in ideas, deliver with an eye on the whole and live your enthusiasm. Shape the future of our digital, AI-native solutions. Become part of a team with the vision to combine modern cloud and AI technologies – especially LLMs, agent workflows and AI-enabled developer tools – into a powerful, scalable platform. Do you feel addressed? Then you are perfect for our interdisciplinary team, because we are looking for you as an AI Solution Engineer / AI Engineer with a strong, agile mindset. We look forward to your application. Your tasks

  • Take on end-to-end responsibility from conception to solution architecture to implementation of AI-native solutions for software development
  • Analyze the requirements of various IT stakeholders and translate into viable, AI-enabled technical solutions
  • Design and build modern, cloud-based architectures (Azure) with a focus on AI workloads
  • Implementation and further development of AI capabilities and services including connection to AI/LLM services (e.g. Azure OpenAI)
  • Build and / or integrate AI-native components (e.g. LLM-based services, code assistants, agent workflows, coding harness / open code) into existing systems and dev processes
  • Working on the design of prompting strategies, orchestrating AI skills and integrating coding guidelines into AI-enabled development tools
  • Ensure security, compliance and governance requirements when using AI in collaboration with architecture and security
  • Collaborating in the automation of build, test and deployment processes in our CI/CD pipelines, including AI-supported DevOps workflows and working closely with product owners, business engineers and architects to develop optimal, AI-driven solutions

Your profile

  • Completed degree in computer science or comparable professional experience
  • Several years of experience as a solution engineer / software engineer with end-to-end responsibility (concept, architecture, implementation)
  • Sound experience in software development (programming languages verifiable, technology stack flexible)
  • Experience with Azure Cloud (e.g. App Services, Functions, Kubernetes, Storage, Identity), ideally in the context of AI/ML workloads
  • First practical experience or strong interest in AI-native applications (e.g. LLMs, Prompting, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Agentic Systems)
  • Interest or experience with AI-enabled development tools and DevOps platforms (e.g. GitLab Duo, Azure DevOps, GitHub Copilot, Claude Code, etc.)
  • Experience with modern architectural patterns (e.g. microservices, event-driven, API-first) is beneficial, ideally combined with AI integrations
  • Analytical, structured and at the same time pragmatic way of working as well as communicative and team-oriented
  • Very good German and English skills

Our offer

  • Motivated, self-organized and established team that looks forward to you and your AI expertise
  • End-to-end responsibility from idea to productive operation in an innovative, AI-driven environment
  • The ability to help shape modern cloud and AI technologies (especially LLMs, code assistants, coding harness/opencode) hands-on
  • Collegial and appreciative cooperation at eye level
  • Culture of continuous (further) development – personal, technical and professional, with a focus on AI skills
  • Centrally located, easily accessible and attractive workplace with the possibility of flexible, hybrid work
  • Your career in the AI environment is close to our hearts – we support you and support you with a wide range of training opportunities in the field of AI, cloud and modern software engineering practices.
