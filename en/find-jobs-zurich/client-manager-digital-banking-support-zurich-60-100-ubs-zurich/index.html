Full description

Key responsibilities Are you looking for an attractive entry opportunity into the digital world of private customer business or would you like to deepen your existing expertise in digital banking? Do you appreciate the customer contact on the phone and in the written consultation and would like to work in a young, dynamic team? Then you are right with us.

We are looking for client managers for telephone and written advice on our digital banking products. In this role:

  • support our customers with technical and application-specific questions about digital banking
  • provide efficient advice on bank inquiries, in particular on the account range and card blocks
  • use your language skills in a targeted manner and support customers from all over Switzerland
  • With your solution-oriented and friendly way, you help our customers to handle their daily banking transactions digitally, securely and easily.

The team In the Digital Banking Support team, we support our customers in handling their daily banking transactions via digital channels – quickly, competently and solution-oriented. True to our motto "Every contact is with our most important client", we are completely aligned with the customer's concerns. You work in the Digital Banking Support team at Client Solutions Switzerland (CSS). With around 1,000 employees at several locations, CSS provides a wide range of services and consulting services in the telephone, administrative and digital sectors. This includes the 7x24h Digital Banking Support for all customer segments of the bank.

Your skills and experience

  • a completed banking apprenticeship or equivalent education
  • Enjoy working in a dynamic, digital environment
  • technical flair and understanding as well as readiness for constant and independent further education in digital topics
  • Ability to present complex issues verbally simply and comprehensibly
  • Empathy, commitment and resilience, also in shift operations
  • fluent command of three of the following languages: German, French, English or Italian

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
