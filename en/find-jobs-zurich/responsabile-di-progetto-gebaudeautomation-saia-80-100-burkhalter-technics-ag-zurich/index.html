Full description

Burkhalter Technics AG is the competent and reliable partner for comprehensive services in the field of electrical engineering in the building, communication technology and building informatics. We develop and implement energy-efficient solutions for projects of all sizes from a single source.

From planning to implementation to management: We offer the full spectrum of electrical installation from a single source.

To strengthen our GA team, we are specifically looking for technicians with in-depth SAIA knowledge.

What moves you

  • Independent management and implementation of building automation projects from the concept phase to the handover
  • Programming and parameterization of SAIA PCD controllers and visualization systems
  • Creation and further development of software solutions for the automation and management level
  • Commissioning, optimisation and acceptance of building automation systems
  • Integration and networking of HVAC systems as well as connection to higher-level management systems
  • Coordination of internal specialists, customers and external partners
  • Ensuring timely and economical project implementation
  • Preparation of documentation, functional descriptions and customer training

What you bring

  • Basic training in electrical engineering, automation, building informatics or building technology (HLKS)
  • Training at HF, FH or equivalent level
  • Several years of experience in building automation
  • In-depth knowledge in the programming of SAIA PCD systems
  • Experience with SAIA PG5 and web-based visualization solutions
  • Experience with Promos NT is beneficial
  • Experience in managing projects or sub-projects
  • Experience with communication protocols such as BACnet, Modbus, MP-Bus, KNX or M-Bus
  • Good knowledge of HVAC controls and energy management
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • High customer orientation and pleasure in dealing with different contacts
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

What we offer you

  • Exciting and varied building automation projects
  • Modern work equipment and flexible working time models
  • Collegial and motivated team with short decision paths
  • Individual training and development opportunities
  • Progressive conditions of employment
  • Opportunity to actively shape the future of smart and energy-efficient buildings
  • Advanced 40 hours week
  • Opportunity to work part-time
  • Mind. 27 days holidays and 9 public holidays
  • Additional holidays as a service anniversary gift
  • Possibility to buy up to 4 weeks holidays
  • Employee discounts for hotels, leisure events and much more
  • Financial security by intra-corporate pension funds
