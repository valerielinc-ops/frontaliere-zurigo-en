Full description

Eraneos is an international strategy, transformation and technology advisory group that helps companies thrive in a dynamic digital world. With first-class experts from business and technology, we empower our customers to set new standards for sustainable transformations – from strategy to implementation. Whether in the design of sustainable organizations, the development of new potential through data and AI or the protection of companies through holistic cybersecurity: We deliver results pragmatically and purposefully. Our team of around 1,200 dedicated employees is represented in offices in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, Denmark, Sweden, Austria, the UK, Singapore and the USA and combines global perspectives with strong local roots. Around 450 employees work at the Swiss locations in Zurich, Bern and Lausanne.

This is the job - your main tasks

  • Advising clients, project developers and operators on the strategic planning, conception and realization of highly available data centers and their support in demanding decision-making processes along the entire project life cycle.
  • Assumption of the client representation and support of realization projects – from analysis and procurement to tendering and construction phase to final acceptance.
  • Implementation of technical due diligence and assessments to evaluate existing properties with regard to their data center and TGA infrastructure, potential, risks and sustainability.
  • Development of sustainable concepts for critical trades (electricity, climate, safety, network) and quality assurance according to relevant standards (e.g. EN 50600, TIA-942).
  • Leadership of challenging consulting mandates and moderation of workshops with interdisciplinary teams from architecture, technical building equipment (TGA) planning and IT.
  • Development of decision-making bases for management and project committees and presentation of complex technical issues appropriate to the addressee at different hierarchical levels.

This job is for you if

  • You have several years of experience in the planning, construction, operation or consulting of data centers and are happy to contribute your expertise in the context of construction and real estate projects (especially in the area of TGA).
  • You have a sound technical understanding of the central trades of a data center and their interaction – underpinned by a study (engineering, (business) informatics) or, correspondingly, many years of professional experience.
  • D u has a strong analytical and conceptual ability to solve complex challenges in a structured way.
  • Thanks to your confident appearance and your communication strength, you can understand complex technical issues for different target groups.
  • D u show a high degree of initiative and sense of responsibility and likes to think and act entrepreneurially.
  • You communicate negotiably in German and English.

As an i-top you bring with you

  • E in basic understanding of OT/SCADA systems, especially in building automation (GLT/BMS), or with IoT platforms in the real estate context.
  • First touch points with OT security or IT security for critical infrastructures.
  • Certifications in the data center environment (e.g. CDCP®, CDCS®, TÜV TSI), in project management (e.g. HERMES, IPMA) or in the OT security area (e.g. IEC 62443) are a welcome plus.

This is why Eraneos is your next career step

  • Groundbreaking projects: You design the digital DNA of future-oriented real estate and critical infrastructures and work at the exciting interface between real estate, TGA and digitalization.
  • An environment full of experts: You become part of an interdisciplinary team of first-class specialists. We promote the active exchange of knowledge and support you in continuously expanding your expertise.
  • Entrepreneurial freedom: We trust in your abilities. With us, you get the necessary freedom to take responsibility, contribute your own ideas and lead projects pragmatically to success.
  • A culture based on trust and collegiality: Flat hierarchies, short decision paths and an appreciative cooperation create a working environment in which you can develop and grow.
