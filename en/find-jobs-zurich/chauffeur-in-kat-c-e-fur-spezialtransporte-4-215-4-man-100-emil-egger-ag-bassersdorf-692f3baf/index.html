Full description

The EMIL EGGER Group is the leading provider of heavy goods logistics in Switzerland. More than 230 employees realize challenging projects every day in the areas of crane work, special transports, industrial removals and rental lifts. With expertise, state-of-the-art technology and a lot of passion, we develop individual solutions for our customers. You like to travel, take responsibility and are looking for a task that goes far beyond classical transport? Then you are exactly right with us. For our team in Bassersdorf we are looking for an experienced chauffeur / an experienced chauffeuse Kat. C/E for special transports with our 4×4 MAN semi-trailer and semi-trailer.

Among other things, you transport

  • Crane parts
  • Ballast weights
  • Plate elements
  • Special goods

You can expect varied assignments throughout Switzerland and partly in border countries – including occasional overnight stays. Your tasks

  • Operation of special transports
  • Safe driving of a 4×4 MAN semitrailer tractor
  • Loading and unloading checks and load securing
  • Cooperation with dispatchers, crane operators and project managers
  • Responsibility for vehicle care and operational safety
  • Professional Appearance to Customers and Partners

You bring that with you

  • Cat C/E driving licence
  • Several years of experience as a chauffeur in the transport industry
  • Experience in special transport is an advantage
  • Technical understanding and responsibility
  • Self-employed, reliable and flexible working methods
  • Readiness for overnight stays outside the house
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

This is what we offer you

  • Permanent employment in a successful family business
  • Exciting and varied special transport instead of routine journeys
  • Modern, own and excellently equipped vehicle
  • Du-College Culture and Family Working Environment
  • Comprehensive training by experienced specialists
  • Regular education and training
  • Attractive employment conditions and benefits
  • Long-term development opportunities within the EMIL EGGER Group

Ready for special transports? If you enjoy challenging transport tasks and want to become part of a strong team, we look forward to your application.
