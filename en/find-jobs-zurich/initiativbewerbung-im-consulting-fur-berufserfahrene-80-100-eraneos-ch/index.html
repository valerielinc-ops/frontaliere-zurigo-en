Full description

Eraneos is an international strategy, transformation and technology advisory group that helps companies thrive in a dynamic digital world. With first-class experts from business and technology, we empower our customers to set new standards for sustainable transformations – from strategy to implementation. Whether in the design of sustainable organizations, the development of new potential through data and AI or the protection of companies through holistic cybersecurity: We deliver results pragmatically and purposefully. Our team of around 1,300 dedicated employees is represented in offices in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, Portugal, Denmark, Sweden, Austria, the UK, Singapore and the USA and combines global perspectives with strong local roots. Around 450 employees work at the Swiss locations in Zurich, Bern and Lausanne.

This is the job Where exactly your entry is depends on your profile and our needs. Typical for everyday consulting with us:

  • Accompany and actively shape customer projects in public administration, financial service providers, industry or in the security environment
  • Accept requirements, analyze processes and use them to develop viable solutions, both technically and technologically
  • Assuming responsibility in projects and sub-projects and working closely with specialist departments and decision-makers
  • Advancing topics such as Data & AI, Cyber Security, Enterprise Architecture or Digital Transformation
  • Sharing knowledge in the team and growing professionally and personally with each project

This job is for you if

  • a completed degree (university, ETH, FH) or an equivalent education with relevant further education forms your basis
  • at least three years of professional experience in consulting, IT, project or transformation work behind you or in a specialist function that is relevant to our customers
  • Project methods and tools, as they are used today, you are familiar from your own, current practice
  • English flows over your lips and your German sits at level C1

As an i-top you bring with you

  • Experience from consulting or projects on the client side
  • Certifications such as SAFe, PMP, IPMA, TOGAF or cloud certificates
  • Industry knowledge in Public Sector, Financial Services, Industry or Public Security

Do you still lack something at the icing on the cake, or does your CV have a break for family, further education or a reorientation? Apply anyway. We look at the big picture.

What is important to us With Eraneos, what counts is what you want to move. Even if you do not fully meet every requirement, we look forward to your application. We are interested in your mindset, your ideas and the impact you want to create. Diversity is a source of growth and inspiration for us. We look forward to every application.

The recruiting team is looking forward to receiving your direct application via our website (CV, diplomas and employment certificates as PDF).

Applications by e-mail or through personnel service providers are not considered.
