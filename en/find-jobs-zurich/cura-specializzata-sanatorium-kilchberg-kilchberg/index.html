Psychotherapeutic Private Unit E

Additional benefit for carers in shift work: 37-hour week The Kilchberg Sanatorium offers you a fulfilling working environment with exciting development opportunities in a dynamic and appreciative team. Bring your strengths and shape the future of the clinic. Humor, team spirit and open communication characterize Station E. We live appreciation and promote a positive working atmosphere.

Their tasks

  • Monitoring and further development of care processes and concepts on the station
  • Advice to nurses in complex psychiatric and psychotherapeutic situations
  • Implementation of reference care
  • Active participation in the implementation of new stationary projects and station processes
  • Representation of the station management in their absence
  • Management of inpatient nursing therapy groups
  • Promoting the therapeutic recovery relationship

Your profile

  • Higher technical training in nursing (FH, CAS, MAS)
  • High level of expertise in psychiatric care
  • Experience working with inpatients on a psychiatric-psychotherapeutic station
  • Flexibility, resilience, independence and teamwork
  • Ability for constructive and solution-oriented cooperation
  • High customer orientation with well-kept manners
  • Stylistic German in word and writing, English language skills desired

The Kilchberg Sanatorium is one of the most traditional psychiatric private clinics in Switzerland and looks back on almost 160 years of history. Only 15 minutes from the city centre of Zurich with beautiful views of Lake Zurich and the Alps. As a Listespital, we offer comprehensive psychiatric care for all insured persons and have inpatient and outpatient facilities in Kilchberg and Zurich. Our approximately 650 employees from over 25 professional groups are at the heart of the clinic and are committed to the best possible treatment of our patients. Become part of our team and shape the future of psychiatric care.
