What we offer you.

The "Expansion and Renewal Projects" department deals with all the construction projects of the FFS Infrastructure division, concerning the extension, modernization and renewal of the railway infrastructure of the FFS. About 1800 collaborators and collaborators work every day to implement the requirements required in over 2000 construction projects. In the business unit "Engineering", the key competences in project management, procurement, contract management and stakeholders are combined with the specialist skills for rail, traction current, cables, access to the railway and technical facilities (design and execution), as well as for geomatics, sustainability and the environment and data management (a centre of expertise). The specialist "Geomatica" at "Engineering" is responsible for the key themes set up of the track and reliefs. It is on the one hand the design complies with the rules of all the systems of tracks and exchanges in view of the financial requirements and on the other the acquisition, assignment and accompaniment of the performance of measurement and monitoring within the framework of the projects. As part of Building Information Modelling (BIM), "geomatics" plays a central role in recording existing data. The specialist field is also responsible for updating and managing the system of ordering in "Databack of fixed systems (DfA)" (road network, rail network, fixed points) and the quality of the rail insurance network. Develop your talent in a motivated and ambitious management team of Engineering and directly contribute to the development of Switzerland's transport infrastructure, with particular attention to the use of innovative technologies and the benefits for customers.   • Within the "Engineering" business unit, the "Geomatic" specialised area is established. (about 50 employees). Together with your management team you will further develop the specialized scope in a future-oriented way.

  • Ensure the performance of the specialized field in terms of resources and skills and you are responsible for the safe design and realization, in accordance with the costs, deadlines and quality of all the projects of expansion and renewal in the specialized "Geomatica".
  • With digitization, harmonization and standardization of collaboration with both internal and external partners, you can actively contribute to the efficient implementation of projects.
  • You are responsible for the specialized management "Geomatica", representing the interests of the SBB and the specialized field and you are the interlocutor for the internal and external stakeholders.
  • As a member of the management team of "Engineering" you provide advice and support in terms of content and processes on strategic and conceptual issues, as well as for the sustainable, cultural and digital development of the entire business unit.
