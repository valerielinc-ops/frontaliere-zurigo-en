100% | Banking Operations | Zurich | Experienced professionals In the area of market operations, together with around 70 dedicated personalities, you ensure smooth trading for the entire bank. You will help ensure that our foreign exchange, precious metals and money market transactions are booked efficiently and reliably. Together we bring new ideas, keep an eye on the whole thing and live our enthusiasm for trade. Your tasks

  • Co-shaping and further developing cross-divisional projects and processes

Your profile

  • Careful and reliable completion of routine tasks is a matter of course for you
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English in word and writing
