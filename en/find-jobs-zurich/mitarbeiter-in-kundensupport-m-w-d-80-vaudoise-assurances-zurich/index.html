Full description

Vaudoise is one of the leading Swiss insurance companies and an attractive and future-oriented employer. In keeping with its cooperative identity and values, Vaudoise creates a healthy, inclusive and inspiring working environment in which you can develop further. In our general agency in Zurich, we live daily the values of closeness, proactivity, humanity and trustworthiness. We see ourselves not only as an insurance provider, but rather as a partner of our customers on their way to financial security and well-being. In order to meet this demand, we are looking for an employee in customer support who lives up to these values and supports our customers with commitment and warmth. The following exciting tasks are waiting for you:

  • Support of our employees in customer support
  • Preparation of offers in the non-life sectors in private customer business
  • Treatment of claims
  • Completion of the resulting correspondence
  • Operation of the insurance partner and his customers on the phone This distinguishes you:
  • Completed training in the insurance industry with experience in the commercial sector
  • Good knowledge of the insurance agency channel is beneficial
  • Stylistic German in Word and Writing
  • Sales and customer oriented
  • Working independently and flexibility
  • Communicative and open personality If you are also a dynamic personality who keeps calm even in hectic times, is resilient and is not afraid to make decisions independently, then you fit perfectly into our team! We are looking for someone who not only thinks, but also acts proactively and can adapt flexibly to changing requirements. In return, we offer you:
  • A pleasant working environment in a cooperative company that has been voted the best employer several times
  • Numerous social benefits, including flexibility in work organisation and various employee discounts
  • A dynamic team
  • Employee well-being as a first priority We look forward to receiving your complete application documents electronically (with CV, letter of motivation, copy of employment certificates and diplomas).
