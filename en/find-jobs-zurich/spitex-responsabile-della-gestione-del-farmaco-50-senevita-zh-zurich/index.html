Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,000 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland.

For the Senevita Residenz Nordlicht in Oerlikon we are looking for a mindful and team-oriented person as Spitex - responsible drug management 50% Your tasks

  • You are responsible for medication management and make sure that our residents & pensioners always receive the right medication, punctual assignments in the morning possible.
  • You take over the planning, organization and control of the drug delivery.
  • In everyday life, residents and pensioners are looked after individually according to their needs and according to our Spitex care guidelines.
  • You actively participate in preventive, diagnostic and therapeutic measures and carry out the care documentation seamlessly and independently.

You bring that with you

  • You have a degree as a specialist health EFZ and ideally bring a vocational trainer card.
  • You are proud of your important, meaningful and versatile profession and want to contribute your expertise in an environment that values quality and humanity.
  • Your joy in being part of a dedicated team and working “closer to people” sets you apart.
  • With your curiosity and openness, you are looking forward to getting to know a new working environment and actively shaping it.

We offer you

  • We offer you the opportunity to actively participate in an innovative environment and to shape the change process.
  • Attractive training offer of the Senevita Academy, which inspires ongoing development.
  • An environment where vocational training is written GROSS and brings a breath of fresh air into the house
  • Free drinks and reduced food - heartily prepared by our gastro team
  • Discounts (SBB half-ax, fueling, supplementary insurance, fitness, pharmacy, etc.)
  • Good social benefits and at least. 5 weeks holiday (from 50 years 6 weeks, from 60 years 7 weeks)
  • Exciting activities / events that move employees and residents
  • Very good accessibility (bus stop outside the door, close to Oerlikon station)
