Full description

Your tasks Management talent sought for our centers! Commercial know-how meets technical understanding: Support in the area of retail and center management. Together with the Center Manager, you control our locations for success.

  • Commercial and technical management of assigned shopping centers in cooperation with the Center Manager
  • Active support for new and existing tenants: as well as comprehensive lease contract management
  • Cooperation in the negotiation and drafting of business and supplementary lease contracts
  • Effective vacancy management including subsequent rental
  • Creditor and creditor management
  • Implementation of property acceptance and transfer as well as support for tenant development projects
  • Prediction, budgeting and planning with earnings responsibility
  • Preparation and verification of detailed heating and operating costs accounts
  • Preparation of solution-oriented applications to contracting entities
  • Preparation of meaningful reports
  • Leadership and coordination of assigned FM service providers
  • Observation of market and legal developments
  • Complementary full support of a small housing portfolio

Your requirements

  • Successful graduation as a real estate manager: in with federal professional certificate
  • Several years of experience in the management of commercial properties as well as in the negotiation and drafting of retail and business lease contracts, annexes/attachments and supplements
  • Stylistic German in Word and Writing
  • Good IT application knowledge (MS Office programs, GREM)
  • Valid driving licence and own PW are required
