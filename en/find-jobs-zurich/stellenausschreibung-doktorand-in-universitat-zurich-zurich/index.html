Institute for Animal Nutrition and Dietetics

Job opening immediately or by arrangement, temporary

The Institute of Animal Nutrition and Dietetics deals in research, teaching and service with the feeding of agricultural livestock and the nutrition of pet and zoo animals. In particular, the influence of diet on the welfare of animals and on the quality of foodstuffs derived from livestock is in the foreground. A special aspect of veterinary animal nutrition is dietetics, that is, feeding as part of disease prophylaxis and therapy. All these areas are taught together with feed science.

Tasks

During your employment at the Institute of Animal Nutrition and Dietetics, you will develop a doctoral thesis on the topic: "Development and validation of in vitro methods for simulating digestive processes in pigs and small ruminants".

Profile

Veterinarians interested: women who are willing to get involved in various animal experimental and laboratory techniques. Good knowledge of English and German is just as beneficial as a solid basic knowledge of biochemistry and physiology.
