Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

Sulzer is a global industrial leader with a worldwide network of over 100 service centres offering state-of-the-art spare parts, maintenance and service solutions for pumps, turbines, compressors, motors and generators. In our Global Parts Pricing & Projects Team we are building pricing as a strategic commercial capability — and we are looking for exceptional analytical talent to accelerate that journey.

The Role This is not a typical pricing or analytics position. You will sit at the intersection of data science, commercial strategy and digital transformation — shaping pricing decisions across a ~ CHF 1.4B+ Services portfolio spanning 45 countries and 100+ service centres worldwide. You will work directly with the Global Pricing Head and regional leadership to close pricing gaps, build visibility infrastructure and drive measurable commercial impact at scale. If you have a premier engineering foundation, a consulting mindset and the ambition to build something that matters globally — this role is designed for you.
