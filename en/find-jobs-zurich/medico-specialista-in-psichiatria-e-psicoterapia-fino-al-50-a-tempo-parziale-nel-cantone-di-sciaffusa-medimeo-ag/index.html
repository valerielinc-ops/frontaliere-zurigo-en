Your experience remains valuable – even if you want to shorten your career. You want to continue to work psychiatrically and psychotherapeutically, but no longer work full time? In a natural psychosomatic private clinic in the canton of Schaffhausen, you can expect a part-time assignment in an interdisciplinary team. Even after regular retirement. Here you will meet a psychosomatic private clinic in the rural Schaffhauser Klettgau, near the German border. In a quiet, natural environment, she accompanies people with mental and psychosomatic diseases. The multi-professional team of psychology, nursing and other therapeutic specialists works with an already developed, holistically oriented treatment concept. This combines psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment with resource-oriented approaches as well as elements of mindfulness, closeness to nature and self-reflection. We are looking for an experienced specialist who supports this approach, contributes her clinical experience and enjoys accompanying people in demanding life situations.

Their tasks

  • Psychiatric and psychotherapeutic care of patients with mental and psychosomatic diseases
  • Implementation of psychiatric examinations, diagnosis and individual treatment planning
  • Implementation of individual and group therapies within the framework of the existing treatment concept
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with psychology, nursing and other therapeutic specialists
  • Participation in case meetings and professional exchange within the treatment team

Your Competences

  • Specialist title Psychiatry and Psychotherapy FMH or federally recognized equivalent training title
  • Fulfilment of the prerequisites for the professional qualification (BAB) and admission to the compulsory health insurance (OKP) in the Canton of Schaffhausen, in particular at least three years of professional activity at a recognised Swiss training institution
  • Many years of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic professional experience as well as an independent, responsible working method
  • Openness for a holistic, nature-connected and mindfulness-oriented therapy approach as well as enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Empathetic, appreciative communication and willingness to work within an existing therapeutic concept

Their advantages

  • Part-time workload up to a maximum of 50% : A suitable way to remain professionally active in a later professional phase
  • Attractive remuneration: Up to CHF 150,000 annual salary on a 100% basis, corresponding to up to CHF 75,000 for 50% employment
  • Manageable workload: 42-hour week on a full-time basis, equivalent to 21 hours per week at 50%
  • 24 days of holiday on a full-time basis: holiday entitlement according to the agreed amount and working day distribution
  • Semi-natural place of work: Psychosomatic clinic setting in a rural environment on a farm
  • Professional exchange: cooperation with a multi-professional treatment team and structured familiarisation
  • Proven treatment concept: You contribute your medical experience without having to take on the task of building a new treatment concept
