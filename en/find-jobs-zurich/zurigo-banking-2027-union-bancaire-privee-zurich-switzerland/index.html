Full description

You are motivated, committed and want to discover the exciting world of banking? Then you are exactly right with us! For the apprenticeship start in 2027 we offer a versatile and practical KV-lehrstelle in the banking industry (M-profile). Union Bancaire Privée (UBP) is one of the leading private banks in Switzerland. With a bank apprenticeship at UBP you get a first-class training in private banking and legat the foundation for a successful career in the financial world.

During the three-year banking apprenticeship with the professional matura you run through different departments and experience valuable experiences in the core areas of banking business. UBP offers you during the three-year training, in the city of Zurich the opportunity to work actively in different departments and to support and support our customers and employees.

What awaits you

  • Individual support and personal support throughout the entire apprenticeship period
  • Insights into key areas such as customer consulting, back office, payment transactions, compliance, operations, etc.
  • A family and international working environment
  • A high-quality, practice-oriented bank training: you are a profit of CYP, the vocational school and our practice experts
  • Graduate as a merchant / merchant EFZ with professional matura. (Industry: bank)

What you're saying

  • Highest elementary school level with very good grades, especially in German, mathematics, English and French
  • Interest in the financial world and in current world events
  • Motivation, a positive attitude and openness to new things
  • Careful, reliable and team-oriented working methods
  • Ideally in preparation for the BM test

Application: We look forward to receiving your complete application:

  • school certificates from recent years
  • Multicheck
