As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

YOUR TASKS

  • You love variety! With us, you treat patients: in the areas of neurology (focus on Stroke) and neurosurgery, in addition to patients: in the cardio-pulmonary area and, if necessary, in the intensive care unit
  • You are responsible for the professional and independent care of inpatients and outpatients: taking into account individual needs
  • If you like to pass on your neuro know-how to the next physiogeneration, then you can enjoy realizing yourself in the additional function Vocational Training

YOUR PROFILE: EXPERIENCE AND FLEXIBILITY

  • Training must be: Completed training for/for dipl. Physiotherapist/in with SRK recognition
  • A big plus is if you have already worked in an acute hospital
  • Quality assurance is also an important part of our work, so we do findings and progress documentation, write reports when necessary and perform performance monitoring.
  • The wheel of medicine turns quickly: current knowledge in the fields of neurology and neurosurgery makes your work easier
  • Good knowledge of German, knowledge of foreign languages advantageous
  • As part of our very helpful and interprofessional team, you support us with regular weekend assignments
  • If you like to laugh, are set up and can tackle, you are exactly right in our team – social moments are also important to us

WHAT WE ARE: TRANSFERENCE TIME AND SUPER TEAM

In addition to an exciting working environment with interdisciplinary exchange, we offer a well-founded introduction, regular professional training and interesting development opportunities. Our work models tailored to you not only allow you to combine family and work, but also to pursue your hobbies.

And now comes the best: Employees who have to move completely for their work receive four additional, paid days off with a 100% workload. Absolutely top are the services of our pension fund and insurance companies as well as at least 5 weeks holidays, which can even be extended with the "Leisure Plus model".
