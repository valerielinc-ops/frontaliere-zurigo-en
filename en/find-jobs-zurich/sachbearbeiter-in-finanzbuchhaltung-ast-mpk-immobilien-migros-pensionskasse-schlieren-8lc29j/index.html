Full description

In order to strengthen our financial accounting of the Migros Pension Fund Investment Foundation, we are looking for a committed, reliable and service-oriented personality as a financial accounting officer for the real estate investment group.

Mansioni

  • Responsibility for the timely booking of all bank receipts and exits as well as ensuring a correct account closure
  • Recording of financial entries (changes, cancellations, etc.)
  • Implementation of payment cycles and manual payments
  • Review, processing and follow-up of creditor warnings
  • Cooperation and representation in electronic creditor processing
  • Booking of the monthly wage runs of the housekeeper
  • Cooperation in financial statements

Requirements

  • First experience: in financial accounting
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): merchant/cooperative with figures flair
  • Networked thinking
  • Negotiation skills
  • Precise operation
  • Good manners
  • IT affinity and experienced handling of Microsoft 365
  • GREM application knowledge as well as knowledge in creditor processing with Basware
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • French (advantageous)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central location: Work 3 minutes walk from Schlieren station
  • Modern infrastructure: spacious and bright offices
  • Flexible working hours: Liberal working time regulations with a lot of flexibility for employees
  • Social benefits: Excellent pension fund and generous sickness allowance and accident insurance
  • Migros Bank: Preferred interest at Migros Bank on investment and personnel accounts
  • Holidays: Large Trains Holiday Regulation
  • Education and training: financial and/or temporary support for education and training
  • Du-culture: Uncomplicated and open approach
  • Flat hierarchies: Friendly and family environment
