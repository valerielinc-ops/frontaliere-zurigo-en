Full description

The Pallas clinics are specialized in ophthalmology and aesthetics. With over 350 employees and 17 locations, we are one of the leading providers in Switzerland.

Our medical staff plays an indispensable role in the customer care process. They act as direct contacts and provide professional and individual support. The focus is always on the well-being of our customers. Through close cooperation with our specialists, we guarantee a holistic supply. Our team is characterized by high commitment, regular training and the pleasure of the interdisciplinary cooperation with our customers.

In order to strengthen our team in Winterthur, we are looking for a committed personality as

Senior Medical Assistant in 80–100% – Aesthetic Medicine

Responsibility

  • Organisation and coordination of day-to-day operations and ensuring smooth operation in practice
  • Professional leadership and support of the MPA team
  • Planning and coordination of employee assignments
  • Cooperation in day-to-day business and care of our patients
  • Assistance in aesthetic medicine treatments
  • Organisation and coordination of office hours and management of office hours calendars
  • Support in the development and implementation of structures, process standards and internal processes
  • Ensuring a high quality of service and treatment
  • Taking over various administrative and organisational tasks
  • Performance billing and collection and mutation of patient data
  • Cooperation in the training and introduction of new employees
  • Close cooperation with the medical profession and other internal interfaces

Requirements

  • Completed training as medical/practice assistant/in EFZ
  • Several years of professional experience as an MPA, mandatory in aesthetic medicine, dermatology or a related field of expertise
  • First leadership experience of advantage or the motivation to take responsibility for a team
  • Enjoy both taking on organizational and coordinative tasks and continuing to actively participate in everyday practice
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • High level of commitment, initiative and responsibility
  • Strong service and patient orientation
  • Safe and professional appearance as well as very good communication skills
  • Good computer science user knowledge
  • Very good knowledge of German, English skills are advantageous

Benefits

  • A varied and responsible activity with scope for design
  • A versatile area of responsibility between leadership, organization and direct patient contact
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration in a dynamic and motivated team
  • A modern workplace in an exciting and growing specialist area
  • Regulated working hours, five weeks of holidays and flexible holiday planning
  • Attractive employee benefits for internal treatments
  • Regular training and training opportunities
  • Collaboration and participation in various events

You want to take responsibility, support your team in everyday practice and at the same time continue to work close to our patients? Then we look forward to meeting you personally!
