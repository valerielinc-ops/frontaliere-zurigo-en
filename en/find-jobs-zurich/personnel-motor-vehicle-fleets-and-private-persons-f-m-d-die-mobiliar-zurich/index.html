Full description

This is what awaits you: As a clerk for motor vehicle fleets and private individuals, you will be part of the brokerage management in Zurich-Oerlikon. Together with the Mobility Market Development team, you will ensure professional, cooperative collaboration with our brokers - with a focus on motor vehicle fleets and private customers. This is what you do with us: You are responsible for concluding and supporting demanding motor vehicle fleet and private customer transactions. You will also process inquiries from the brokerage business independently, create offers, renovation proposals and policies and ensure that contracts are managed correctly throughout the entire life cycle. With your professional, solution-oriented communication, you will build sustainable business relationships and, with your high level of service, you will ensure that our partners feel well accompanied and supported at all times. This is what you bring with you:

  • Completed insurance apprenticeship and several years of professional experience in motor vehicle and household insurance
  • Strong customer and service orientation as well as a high level of personal responsibility
  • Enjoy working with brokers, strong communication skills and a confident demeanor
  • Negotiation skills, assertiveness and a structured, efficient way of working
  • Fluent in spoken and written German Working with us: In the Market Processing Mobility team, you can expect a collegial, solution-oriented and communicative working atmosphere, in cooperation and mutual support are lived. Your place of work is Zurich-Oerlikon and the workload is negotiable from 80%. This is what we offer: Compatibility We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund. Further training and development We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible foreign programs. We contribute generously to job-relevant, external further training.
