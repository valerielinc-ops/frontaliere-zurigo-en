Original description

Tasks

  • As a specialist in health at Lengg Clinic, you care for people with neurological diseases and epilepsy
  • In addition, you take on tasks in the areas of administration and organization, working closely with doctors and therapists and are involved in a team of nurses who accompany, promote and challenge you during your training.

Requirements

  • Graduation Sek. A., B. or 10. School year with good grades (German grade at least). 4.5, Mathematics. 4.0
  • Readiness for irregular working hours
  • Good accessibility of the clinic and vocational school
  • Very good social skills such as empathy, good manners, reliability and responsibility
  • Multicheck

Klinik Lengg AG in Zurich is the centre of excellence for epileptology and neurological rehabilitation.

It is our aim to provide patients with neurological diseases with the widest possible rehabilitation and to ensure an accurate diagnosis and sustainable treatment of patients with epilepsy. Around 300 employees are committed to humanely, competently, innovatively and reliably ensuring that our patients regain the greatest possible independence - and thus their quality of life. Become part of one of our interdisciplinary and interprofessional teams.
