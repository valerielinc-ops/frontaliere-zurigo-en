Full description

Your heart burns for the broad internal medicine and you love to provide patients with general internal and comprehensive care? Then you are exactly right with us! The KSW is the medical contact point for around 200,000 inhabitants for inpatient emergency and basic care and for around 500,000 inhabitants for specialized and highly specialized medicine. We are not divided into organ clinics. This offers you the special opportunity to encounter a varied and exciting potpourri of internistic medical conditions every day and to provide patients with corresponding versatile and comprehensive care. We are happy to support you! We not only maintain a very appreciative and collegial exchange within our team, but also work closely with colleagues from Palliative Care, Acute Care and Stroke-Unit. In addition, we are in active interdisciplinary exchange with the other specialists, so that we can look after our patients in the best possible way and with care. You also rotate with us on our lively and no less exciting internistic emergency station. We are recognized for the Emergency Center as a training center category IV (outpatient, 1 year) and for General Internal Medicine category A (inpatient, 3 years). Welcome to the team! Tasks:

  • You treat and look after patients as well as patients on the bed stations of the Clinic for Internal Medicine
  • You will take care of the triage, assessment and initial care of patients of all medical specialties in the emergency center
  • You work as a consulate in the surgical clinics of the KSW
  • You pass on your experience in education and training Profile:
  • You are a specialist in general internal medicine and have professional experience in Switzerland
  • You are inspired by complex internal issues and comprehensive patient treatments
  • You are committed and enjoy working in a team and convince with your motivation and social skills
  • The training of our young assistant doctors is a big concern for you and you are happy to pass on your knowledge Benefits: KSW offers employees conditions that enable them to reconcile their professional goals and personal needs. Flexible, flexible working hours, home office as well as a generous maternity leave and employment levels adapted to different situations – all this is possible at KSW, as long as the company allows it. Every year, the KSW offers 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses. We also support you temporally and/or financially with external training.
  • In extraordinary situations, e.g. B. prolonged illness, severe personal stress or difficult experiences in private life as well as in everyday work, various offers of conversation are available to you.
  • The appreciative and friendly cooperation within the teams and across the departments characterizes the working atmosphere at KSW. As a sign of a strong team spirit, the “you” was introduced as a standard at KSW. In addition, the KSW supports annual team events financially and organizes a large staffing event for the whole house every four years. KSW employees benefit from very good social benefits. Thus, the pension fund solution with BVK is far above the legal minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, KSW employees are very well covered.
  • With discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training opportunities and course offers, perks in pharmacies as well as at the most popular health insurance companies. You can benefit from a comprehensive offer for your health.
  • Our daycare center La Luna with spacious opening hours is located next to the KSW. In addition, you can use the income-dependent, financial support from the KSW for the crib costs.
  • In cooperation with cultural partners in and around Winterthur, we regularly offer discounts for concerts and theater performances. The borrowing of media in the in-house library is also free of charge for employees.
