Full description

Near the Schnebelhorn in the Zurich Oberland we operate an outdoor location for people with increased structural needs in a secluded and natural environment on the Beamegg. For this we are looking for you as a social pä-ediologist / social pedagogue for the residence Beamegg in the Tösstal (50 – 80 %) by immediate or by appointment. You have many years of experience in the care of people with cognitive impairment, especially with people with challenging behavior and increased structural requirements.

With your dedicated, natural and human-oriented way, you make a significant contribution to ensuring that we can offer our residents a home that is precisely tailored to their needs. What counts is you. Every individual person is at the centre of his or her individual quality of life. We do everything we can to ensure that our 250 inhabitants, 246 day care centers and 52 employees in protected workplaces in the Wagerhof can enjoy the highest possible quality of life.

For employees of different backgrounds and professionality, the Wagerhof is an attractive employer and a competent training partner for learners of all levels.

Tasks

  • Agogic and nursing care and care for people with cognitive impairments and behavioural abnormalities
  • Active design and support of the daily structure offers, among others, on the corresponding farm Bärloch
  • Interdisciplinary and interprof-essional cooperation and administrative tasks

Requirements

  • Social Pädagogin / Social pedagogue HF / FH
  • Several years of experience in the supervision of people with cognitive impairments
  • willingness to jointly design the "life on the beamegg"
  • Preferably own vehicle available

We offer

  • cooperation in the highly motivated team of the residence on the Beamegg
  • A modern workplace in idyllic surroundings, far from hectic
  • many possibilities for personal and professional development.
