Full description

Are you sociable and have an open ear for the needs of customers? Do you like working in a dynamic and promising environment? Do you feel comfortable in the digital world? At BANK-now you will find the ideal conditions for this. As a subsidiary of UBS Switzerland AG, we are a Swiss bank specialized in credit and vehicle financing. Take advantage of the promotion opportunities in a young and dynamic team and come to BANK-now.

As a business-side product owner, you play a key role in the success of our products in our bank. They define the product vision, prioritize requirements and ensure that the product creates maximum value for customers and the company. You work closely with various stakeholders on the specialist and IT side.

And these are your core tasks

  • Assuming professional responsibility for the provision, quality and operation of our products (credit financing, leasing and supplementary insurance solutions) as well as the associated applications and services.
  • Definition of the specialist requirements, creation and maintenance of the product backlog as well as planning and prioritizing improvements and further developments.
  • Develop and implement a clear vision for long-term development and optimization.
  • Professional management of implementation projects to improve and expand our product, application and service platform.
  • Effective stakeholder management through close cooperation and coordination with internal and external stakeholders to meet the requirements and expectations of all stakeholders.
  • Thematic management of interdisciplinary teams as well as close cooperation with external partners and providers to develop innovative and efficient solutions
