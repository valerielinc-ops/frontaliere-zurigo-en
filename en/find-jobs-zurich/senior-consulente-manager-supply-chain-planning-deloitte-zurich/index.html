Full description

Location: Zurich, Basel Work Pattern Percentage: 80-100% Are you passionate about shaping the future of supply chains? Ready to dive into cutting-edge technology and make a real difference for global clients? At Deloitte, we’re looking for dynamic, curious, and driven Supply Chain Planning consultants who want to grow their expertise while working on exciting, real-world challenges. How you can make an impact

  • Develop deep functional and conceptual knowledge of supply chain planning — from Sales & Operations Planning to Demand Forecasting and beyond.
  • Drive innovative, tech-enabled solutions that transform how our clients plan, produce, and deliver.
  • Support clients in building robust processes, capabilities, and governance — or guide them through Advanced Planning Systems (APS) assessments, selections, and implementations.
  • Advise organisations of all sizes on capacity planning, product launch strategies, materials management, and the latest enabling technologies.
  • Prepare reports, proposals, and documentation to support client engagements and business growth.
  • Own your projects with autonomy, collaborating closely in small, agile teams.
  • Pick your passion: dive into a topic that excites you and develop it through research, a point of view (PoV), or even a client engagement.
  • Collaborate regularly with Deloitte’s Supply Chain Planning team to brainstorm solutions and develop ideas for ongoing projects and business development.
  • Experience a vibrant mix of working alongside colleagues in our offices and spending time on-site with clients — from corporate HQs to factories — to truly understand their challenges. Who we are looking for
  • A seasoned management consulting professional with strong experience in supply chain, operations, planning, or digital transformation.
  • At least 2 years of experience in a consulting company is highly preferred.
  • Strong knowledge of Supply Chain Planning disciplines, including Integrated Business Planning, Inventory Optimisation, Network Design, and Production Scheduling.
  • Previous exposure to APS implementation is strongly preferred, either as an implementation consultant, product specialist, solution expert, or advanced end-user.
  • Exposure to Materials Management, Product Launch Planning, and cut-over management is a plus.
  • Knowledge of AI applications in supply chain, planning, operations, or consulting is a strong plus, especially for someone who has worked with AI-enabled tools or use cases and can bring creative ideas to client projects.
  • Able to take ownership of workstreams, manage stakeholders, and act as a trusted advisor to clients and internal teams.
  • Independent, proactive, and confident operating in projects where you can contribute as a key senior resource.
  • A collaborative team player with intellectual curiosity, a continuous improvement mindset, and the ability to coach more junior colleagues.
  • Comfortable working in an international environment, including travel abroad when required.
  • Fluent in English. Additional languages such as French or German are a strong bonus.
  • Certifications such as APICS or other Supply Chain/Operations credentials are a plus. Your Team
  • A vibrant, supportive team that’s well connected across EMEA and globally.
  • Exposure to end-to-end supply chain transformation projects — from strategy and design to hands-on implementation.
  • Opportunities to work with multinational clients across diverse industries including Consumer Business, Life Sciences, Pharmaceuticals, Chemicals, and Industrial Manufacturing.
  • Access to extensive internal and external training programmes to boost your skills and career.
  • The freedom to explore and develop your own ideas through research, PoVs, or client projects — your growth is in your hands.
  • Career progression opportunities aligned with your ambitions and interests.
  • A supportive environment where your input is heard, your ideas are taken seriously, and your growth is encouraged. Careers at Deloitte. Choose your impact.
