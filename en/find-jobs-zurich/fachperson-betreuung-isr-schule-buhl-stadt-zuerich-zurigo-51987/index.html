Full description

For our care location Horteria at the school Bühl we are looking for reinforcements by immediately or by appointment until 31 July 2027.

Tasks

As a specialist care provider, you are responsible for the supervision of a child at primary school age (3rd grade) on four days per week (Mo, Tue, Do and Fr) at the Meiliweg care location. Detailed information about the working hours can be discussed during a personal interview with the management. Information and imprint on the locations can be found on the website of Schule Bühl.

Your tasks include

  • The organisation of school care and the organisation of day-to-day operations
  • Support and support for children in social and school matters
  • Cooperation with parents from different cultural backgrounds
  • Cooperation in the holiday shelter and on school-free days

Profile

  • As a specialist care provider you have a professional degree as a specialist care provider or toddler educator
  • Swiss Diploma Recognition is mandatory for foreign diplomas
  • Work experience in caring for children (hoor, nursery, home)
  • You have very good oral and written German knowledge
  • Dedicated and enthusiastic personality, who enjoys the further development of the care services for our children
  • Personality for which cooperative cooperation in the team and diversity are a matter of course, and feedback and reflection are not foreign words
  • You are a co-designer on your way to the day school Bühl from SJ 27/28

We offer

...a varied and sinnförderende activity in a lively environment. Our employees also benefit from the attractive employment conditions of the city of Zurich. A motivated and open team is looking forward to your application!

About us

The school district Uto is one of seven school districts in the city of Zurich and comprises the city districts 2 and 3 with the districts Leimbach, WollisInsingen, Enge, Friesenberg and Alt-Wiedikon.

The Bühl School is a public school in the city of Zurich. It is located in the heart of the Wiedikon district at the foot of the Üetliberg. In the school year 2023/24, 485 children from kindergarten to 6th grade attend the school Bühl, which currently leads 18 primary classes, of which 9 are under and 6 are under and medium grade.

In addition to the school house and the gymnastics hall building, there are also 4 kindergarten locations in the quarters and 8 care facilities.
