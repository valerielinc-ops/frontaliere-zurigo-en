Job purpose The Global Head of Commercial Operations is responsible for leading the commercial management of Sulzer’s global claims portfolio, driving strong commercial and customer outcomes across complex, high-value projects within the oil & gas, industrial, and energy sectors. This is a senior, strategic and operational role leading a team of commercial contract managers, ensuring robust contract interpretation, commercial positioning, and delivery of business objectives, whilst operating at the interface between customers, EPC partners, and internal stakeholders. Working closely with the Global Head of Legal Excellence, the role combines strategic customer relationship management with hands-on engagement in complex commercial matters, requiring strong international experience, sound judgement, and a collaborative leadership approach within dynamic, fast-paced environments.

Main accountabilities and tasks

This role carries direct accountability for delivering measurable Contribution and EBITDA improvement through disciplined, data-driven claims management.

Global Claims Portfolio Management

  • Lead and manage the full global claims portfolio, ensuring effective handling of complex, high-value and cross-border claims, delivering strong financial outcomes and reduction in claim exposure. Direct line management responsiblity for the global aftersales warranty team.

Customer & EPC Relationship Management

Cross-Functional Alignment & Integration

Continuous Improvement & Operational Excellence
