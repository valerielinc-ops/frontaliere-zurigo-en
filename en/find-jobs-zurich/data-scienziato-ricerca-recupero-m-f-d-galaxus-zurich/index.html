Role overview

Retrieval is the foundation of every search result.

As a Data Scientist on our Search team, you'll own the algorithms (keyword, vector, and beyond) that surface the right products for every customer query.

Your work has a direct, measurable impact on how customers discover products and experience our shop.

Main responsibilities

  • Design, build, and improve retrieval passes for our product search, including keyword-based (e.g., BM25) and vector-based (embedding) search
  • Develop hybrid retrieval strategies that blend lexical and semantic signals, including candidate merging and score normalization
  • Continuously improve retrieval systems by evaluating performance and leveraging interaction data from millions of users
  • Analyze search logs and user behavior to identify recall gaps, underperforming query segments, and long-tail query issues
  • Bring models from prototype to production together with engineers, ensuring low latency and high availability at e-commerce scale

Key requirements

  • mindestens 3 Jahre relevante Berufserfahrung: Search / Information Retrieval / Machine Learning
  • Completed degree (university / university of applied science / higher technical college): Data Science / Computer Science / Machine Learning
  • University degree in data science or a related field (MSc or PhD), as well as at least 3 years of relevant professional experience, ideally with practical experience in search or information retrieval
  • Excellent Python knowledge including experience with machine learning libraries such as PyTorch, TensorFlow and scikit-learn
  • Experience designing experiments and interpreting metrics for large-scale online systems (A/B testing, offline evaluation)
  • Experience in handling large amounts of data, including advanced SQL knowledge and experience with database systems (e.g. BigQuery) for analyzing user interactions
  • Experience in the field of MLOps, especially in the deployment and operation of machine learning models in production, e.g. with orchestration tools like Airflow or Dagster
  • Solid command of English, both written and spoken; German is a plus
  • Pragmatic, product-oriented mindset and good communication skills for working across disciplines
