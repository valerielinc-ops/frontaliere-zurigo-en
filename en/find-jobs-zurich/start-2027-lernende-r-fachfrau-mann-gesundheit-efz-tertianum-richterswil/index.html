Full description

Are you ready for a FaGe apprenticeship? Get your EFZ through nursing training. With us you will start a varied and meaningful apprenticeship with a future, because as a health specialist you can expect a supportive working environment from day one in which you can help people and are accompanied and supported by our experienced vocational trainers.

We are looking for YOU for the Tertianum House “Etzelblick” in Richterswil on August 1, 2027

Apprenticeship as a health specialist EFZ

You'll learn that with us

  • Care, support and support of guests in everyday and demanding situations
  • Support with personal hygiene, getting up, dressing and undressing, eating and drinking
  • Medical technical tasks such as measuring blood pressure, pulse and temperature, taking blood, changing bandages and much more
  • Accompanying the guests on a daily basis with games, crafts, singing, etc.
  • Housekeeping, administrative and logistical tasks
  • Compliance and implementation of hygiene and safety regulations
  • Electronic nursing documentation

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed school education (secondary school A or B) with good academic performance
  • Great willingness to learn and sense of responsibility
  • Empathy and patience as well as interest in dealing with our guests
  • Helpful, outgoing and team-oriented, with a strong sense of care and conscientiousness
  • Willingness to work irregular hours
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoy diverse challenges and practical work
  • Good knowledge of spoken and written German

What benefits do we offer you? You are just as important to us as your training is to you! That's why we offer you the following advantages:

  • You get the day off on your birthday
  • CHF 500 towards your compulsory school materials
  • Free SBB half-fare subscription
  • Every lunch for CHF 5
  • Discounted cell phone subscriptions with Sunrise
  • Discounts and offers relating to fitness, leisure and your wellbeing
  • Up to 10% discount on your supplementary health insurance
  • And much more…

You start straight away with an exciting introductory day and halfway through your training we invite you to our summer camp for a whole week. Of course, your completion of your training must be properly celebrated, so we organize an unforgettable graduation party for all graduates. And maybe this is just the beginning of your career with us! We offer numerous opportunities to work within our company immediately after completing your apprenticeship.

You can find more benefits and insights on our website.

Well, interested? As soon as we have your complete application documents, they will be checked by our team. If your dossier impresses us, we look forward to getting to know you personally during an initial conversation. Afterwards you can immerse yourself in the Tertianum world on one or more trial days and get an idea of ​​everyday life with us.
