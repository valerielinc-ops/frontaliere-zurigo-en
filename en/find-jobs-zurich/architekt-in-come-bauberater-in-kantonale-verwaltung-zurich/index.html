Full description

Would you like to use your many years of experience as an architect where your professional judgment counts? As a construction consultant, you will assess construction projects for the canton of Zurich that are eligible for state and fund contributions and advise the commissioning specialist departments and offices, municipalities and private developers on demanding construction and construction economic issues. You combine your experience in planning and implementation with a sure eye for costs, quality and practical solutions. Your tasks

  • You assess a variety of construction projects by public and private developers - for example for special schools or children's and youth homes - from a structural and economic perspective.
  • Based on the applicable regulations, you examine the requirements for state contributions as well as contributions from the lottery and culture fund in a multi-stage process and thus create a well-founded basis for decision-making in accordance with the phases.
  • You determine the spatial suitability of private schools, provide municipalities, private bodies and specialist departments with information on construction projects eligible for subsidies and promote the implementation of obstacle-free construction, including internally within the building construction authority.
  • You bring in your experience from planning and implementation, constructively question solutions and suggest viable alternatives.
  • You record your assessments in well-founded statements and reports in a level-appropriate and comprehensible manner. Your profile
  • You have completed a degree in architecture (ETH/FH) and have many years of professional experience in the planning and implementation of demanding, preferably public, construction projects.
  • Thanks to your broad experience across all project phases, you assess structural solutions holistically and with a clear eye for quality, practicality and feasibility.
  • You have in-depth knowledge of construction cost planning and assessment and understand the essential real estate economics.
  • You advise different stakeholders competently, communicate in an appropriate manner and represent your professional assessment convincingly and comprehensibly.
  • You have a confident expression in German and get to the heart of complex issues precisely, even in writing. You choose us because you use your many years of experience in a versatile expert role and thus directly influence the quality of subsidized construction projects. As part of a small team of specialists on the staff of the building construction authority, you will enjoy a high degree of professional freedom and gain insight into a wide range of construction projects in the canton of Zurich. At the same time, you benefit from a broad network and professional exchange with specialists within the building construction authority. About us The Building Authority is responsible for the development, realization and repair of cantonal buildings, from the Grossmünster to the university building. It ensures professional planning and implementation of new construction, repairs and art and construction projects for public buildings.

It is committed to sustainability and building culture, ensures careful use of resources and high operational suitability. In addition, the building construction authority is also responsible for the construction assessment of state contributions as well as contributions from lottery and cultural funds. Over 130 construction projects are examined annually by the assessment department on behalf of the specialist directorates and offices and subsidized by the Canton of Zurich.
