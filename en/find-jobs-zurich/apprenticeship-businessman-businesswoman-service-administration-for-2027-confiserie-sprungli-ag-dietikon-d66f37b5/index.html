Full description

Founded in 1836, the Swiss family business is now one of the most renowned confectioneries in Europe with its exquisite range. The products from Sprüngli stand for the best quality, unique freshness and naturalness. The perfect creations make customers from all over the world go into raptures every day.

Apprenticeship: Commercial clerk (service & administration) for 2027

100%, Dietikon

Working at Sprüngli

As a prospective clerk at Confiserie Sprüngli, you will be guided through various exciting departments during your 2.5-year apprenticeship at our production site in Dietikon. These include finance and controlling, accounts receivable and accounts payable, cash management, purchasing, human resources and payroll accounting. You will also gain an insight into the demanding tasks of the Corporate Clients and Marketing and Communications departments. You will get to know them during a six-month phase at the headquarters in Zurich. Hardly any other profession offers such a wide range of activities and areas of responsibility as that of a businessman.

Start of teaching: Monday, 09 August 2027

What you can do with us

  • Collaboration in various commercial departments and getting to know diverse areas of the company
  • Support with HR topics such as applications, admissions and personnel administration (3rd year of apprenticeship)
  • Organization and coordination of appointments, meetings and internal events
  • Collaboration on exciting projects and step-by-step assumption of own responsibility
  • Creation of presentations, evaluations and other important documents

What we want

  • Good secondary school leaving certificate
  • Good grades in mathematics and a flair for numbers
  • Completed Multicheck for Merchants (www.multicheck.ch)
  • Enjoyment of contact with people as well as administration and PC activities
  • Well-groomed appearance
  • Ability to work in a team and good manners
  • Reliability and a sense of responsibility
  • Willingness to learn something new again and again

What you can look forward to

  • Apprenticeship at one of the most renowned confectioneries in Switzerland
  • Motivated trainers with excellent specialist knowledge
  • Interesting and varied basic education
  • 2 interesting introductory days with all new apprentices
  • Inter-company courses: Industry knowledge on the subject of services and administration
  • 6 weeks vacation
  • Assumption of the costs of school materials
  • Free courses at the vocational school
  • Annual apprentice excursion

Are you interested? Then we look forward to receiving your electronic application.* We are currently not accepting applications from recruitment agencies for this position.
