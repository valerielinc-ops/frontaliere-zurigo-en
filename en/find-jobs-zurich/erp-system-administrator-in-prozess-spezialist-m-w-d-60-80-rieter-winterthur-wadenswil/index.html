Full description

Rieter is the world’s leading system supplier for the processing of natural and man-made fibres into short and long staple yarns and for the production of filament yarns, man-made fibres and nonwovens. With its comprehensive technology portfolio, Rieter covers the entire textile value chain – from fiber to yarn as well as from melt to textured filament, synthetic staple fiber and nonwoven fabric. Headquartered in Winterthur (Switzerland), the company develops and manufactures complex spinning plants, machines, systems and components for efficient and economical production. Rieter’s leading technologies enable a minimal use of resources and thus make a significant contribution to sustainability along the global textile value chain. Rieter has existed for more than 230 years, has 22 production sites in nine countries and employs around 6,400 people worldwide, of which around 9% are in Switzerland. Rieter is listed on the SIX Swiss Exchange under the ticker symbol RIEN.
