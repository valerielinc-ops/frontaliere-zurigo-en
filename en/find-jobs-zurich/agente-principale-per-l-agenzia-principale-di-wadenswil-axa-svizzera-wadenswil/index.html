Shape your future – together with us!

As of 1.7.2027, we are looking for an experienced and committed leader with a strong local connection. You are an entrepreneur, your passion is to lead people and have a passion for creative ideas and a connection to the Wädenswil region. As a main agent: at AXA, you are responsible for your team and agency success and have a wide range of tasks as an entrepreneur every day, which fill you with joy and content.

Start your career in an agency at AXA, the No. 1! We stand by our customers as a competent partner – in every situation of life. You too?

This is what awaits you

  • Professional and human leadership, coaching and development of employees
  • Responsibility for achieving the objectives of the main agency
  • Planning and implementation of sales promotion measures
  • Implementation and assurance of AXA’s sales philosophy and principles
  • Responsibility for solution-oriented customer advice, the sale of non-life products and mediation of life products
  • Participation in regional events and organisation of marketing actions

You bring that with you

  • Joy and ability to lead and motivate a team
  • Good anchorage in the region and place of residence in the immediate vicinity
  • Proof of success in the financial services sector or in the non-life insurance sector
  • Passed intermediary examination (VBV) is required
  • Good German and English skills in words and writing, other foreign languages are advantageous
  • Independent and goal-oriented working method as well as high commitment and initiative
  • enforcement, communication and negotiation skills
  • Strong entrepreneurial thinking and action

This is what we offer you

We create framework conditions that give you space to develop and fully exploit your potential. As a leading Swiss insurance company, we offer ambitious people attractive career opportunities and entrepreneurial leeway.

  • Discount of up to 30% on AXA products
  • 5 weeks holiday
  • Fleet discount/refund of half-tax subscription/GA (proportional)
  • Access to the Swibeco platform
  • Leeway for self-responsible action
  • Innovative structures, processes and modern tools
  • Sound, professional training and further education opportunities
