Full description

The Swiss family business, founded in 1836, is now one of the most renowned confectioneries in Europe with its exquisite range. The products from Sprüngli stand for the best quality, unique freshness and naturalness. The perfect creations delight customers from all over the world every day.

Production employee*in machine operation 100% / Luxemburgerli department

100%, Dietikon

Working at Sprüngli

In our factory in Dietikon we are looking for an experienced, motivated and committed person for the Luxemburgerli department immediately or by appointment

What you do with us

You will work in our Luxemburgerli department in our factory in Dietikon and support the team in producing our outstanding and popular Luxemburgerli. Your working hours are between 4 a.m. and 4 p.m. and take place between Monday and Friday. Due to the season, operations must be possible on Saturday.

Your tasks

  • Production of our leading product Luxemburgerli as a team
  • Production of various fillings, jellies, creams and egg white masses
  • Setup, preparation and operation of production machines
  • Grinding and grating the various nuts
  • Compliance and control of safety, hygiene and quality requirements

What we wish for

  • Worked for several years in a food processing position
  • A good understanding of machine controls, ideally initial experience with PLCs, as well as knowledge of operating systems
  • A precise, quality-conscious and independent way of working as well as resilience even in intensive situations
  • Mobility and flexibility
  • with manual skills and a good physical condition
  • PC knowledge (MS Office) is an advantage
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Willingness for shift work
