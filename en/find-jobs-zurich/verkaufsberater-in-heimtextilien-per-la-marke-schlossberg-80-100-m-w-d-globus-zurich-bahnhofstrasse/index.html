Full description

Write the next chapter of Schlossberg's history with us!

As a Swiss family-owned company with a history spanning almost 200 years and a deep passion for textile craftsmanship, we stand for that one moment when an everyday moment becomes a unique moment of well-being.

Our values, personal, passionate, honest, meticulous and stylish, are deeply rooted in our corporate culture and are both tangible and visible in everything we do each day. You matter to us—your personality, your knowledge and your talent.

Become part of us and our long-standing history!

Continue the Schlossberg story with us… and become the perfect addition to our point of sale at Globus in Zurich, effective immediately or by agreement.

HERE YOU MAKE THE DIFFERENCE

  • You advise our customers with your open, competent and charming manner and ensure that they feel welcome and in good hands.
  • As a Schlossberg brand ambassador, you offer our customers a sustainably inspiring customer experience.
  • Thanks to your charisma and passion for our products, you make a decisive contribution to the company's success.
  • You will design the visual merchandising at the POS, which inspires our customers to buy our exclusive products.

WHAT SETS YOU APART

  • You have successfully completed an apprenticeship in the retail trade and several years of experience in the upscale specialist trade, preferably in home textiles.
  • You have a sales talent with a pronounced customer and service-oriented nature as well as a passion for high-quality products.
  • You have flexibility in terms of working hours and keep track of things even in hectic moments.
  • You have excellent communication skills and a very good command of German and English, other national languages and/or Russian are an advantage.

Your previous experiences fit your future area of activity? How wonderful!

You can look forward to a varied job with customer contact and an attractive workplace in the heart of Zurich, to a modern company with an uncomplicated corporate culture and to the sale of unique and high-quality products.

BENEFITS FOR YOU

  • One month more. The year-end bonus, the 13th month's salary, is contractually guaranteed by us.
  • Your sales talent will be rewarded! If you reach the POS sales targets, you are entitled to a monthly and annual bonus.
  • Reason to celebrate! We appreciate your loyalty and loyalty, which is why you will receive a seniority gift from us after just five years of employment.
  • In the distant future. We offer you the option of taking over higher pension fund contributions through our welfare fund.
  • Stay relaxed. With the possibility of part-time work and flexible assignments, we combine family, leisure time and work.
  • Learn more. You never stop learning in life, which is why we offer you the opportunity for individual training through targeted seminars, both internally and externally.
  • Your feel-good moment. Experience what we promise our customers – benefit from employee discounts on our own products as well as on the products of our partners.
  • Take a break. If you work full-time, you are entitled to at least 5 weeks of vacation and 6 weeks from the age of 50.
