Hospital School of Psychosomatic Psychiatric Therapy Station

Your tasks

  • You teach and accompany hospitalized children and adolescents during their approximately three-month stay in the Psychosomatic Psychiatric Therapy Station according to the mission statement of the hospital school as well as the individual needs of the students.
  • You are a reference teacher and work closely with the relatives of children and adolescents, the schools of origin and the interprofessional treatment team (medicine, psychology, care, therapies).
  • As part of a small school team, you support and represent your staff professionally and in absences and actively contribute to the further development of the hospital school.

Your profile

  • EDK recognised teaching diploma (all levels)
  • Relevant teaching experience at secondary level and/or in cross-level setting
  • Knowledge of the special education area is advantageous, ideally paired with a degree in curative education
  • Professional and personal flexibility in the design of the work or School life
