Full description

Key responsibilities Are you passionate about bank loans? Are you self-reliant, reliable and have a high professional conscience? Do you enjoy working in a dynamic and stimulating environment where goals motivate you? If you answered yes to these questions, then join our Credit Services team and be part of a company where your talent will be recognized and valued.

As a credit specialist, you will be responsible for

  • processing nationally approved loan applications, playing a key role in the delivery of our clients' projects
  • prepare credit agreements with appropriate security documents, thus ensuring the necessary documentation for our loans
  • capture and manage credits and warranties (e.g. mortgages, policies and sureties)
  • issue promises of payment and their execution
  • ensure correspondence and dialogue with land registers, notaries, banks and insurance companies, establishing trust relations with our partners
  • maintain professional contacts with client advisors, Credit Officers and legal services

The team Joining our Credit Services team is integrating an environment where innovation and collaboration are at the heart of our success. You will work with experienced experts and innovative collaborators, benefiting from flexible schedules.

Homework is enhanced by our commitment to digitizing our processes. Teamwork and commitment are essential to us, and we look forward to seeing how you can contribute to our collective success.

Your skills and experience

  • full commercial training
  • experience in mortgage processing, banking training or knowledge of notarial law and land registry are assets
  • a committed and resilient personality, always ready to meet challenges
  • rapid understanding and autonomy
  • an independent and rigorous working method
  • knowledge in German is a plus
