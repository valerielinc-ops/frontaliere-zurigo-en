Full description

Real professionals are in demand here – not only on the road, but also in logistics and our own workshops. Our state-of-the-art fleet of around 1,800 vehicles deserves regular maintenance and precise repairs. Are you joining us?

What you move

  • You accompany and promote learners in their professional and personal development
  • Providing practical expertise in the diagnosis, maintenance and repair of commercial vehicles is one of your tasks
  • You plan and coordinate the training content and ensure high-quality training
  • When preparing for the qualification process, you support the learners and promote their independence
  • As a professional and personal role model, you actively participate in the workshop

Why do you succeed?

  • Completed vocational training as an automotive mechatronics specialist EFZ specialising in commercial vehicles, truck, agricultural or construction machinery mechanic forms your basis
  • Ideally, you already have experience as a vocational trainer or are ready to complete the corresponding further education
  • You enjoy training and promoting young professionals
  • Empathy, responsibility and solution orientation distinguish you
  • An independent and structured working method is a matter of course for you – quality and safety are your top priority

Your Benefits

  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Family culture: You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • Modern work equipment: We keep up with the times – modern and safe work equipment is available to you
  • Free parking: Get ready for parking - with us you can park for free

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
