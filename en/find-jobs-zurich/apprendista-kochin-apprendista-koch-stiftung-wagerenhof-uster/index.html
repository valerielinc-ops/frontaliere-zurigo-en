August 2027 we are looking for you as Learning cook / Learning cook EFZ As a learning cook, together with the kitchen team you are responsible for the food of our residents and employees. From day one, you help ensure that the inhabitants have a loving home. What matters is you. Every single person is at the center with their individual quality of life. For employees of different backgrounds and professionalism, Wagerenhof is an attractive employer and a competent training partner for learners of all levels.
