Full description

Location: Hirslanden Clinic in the Park | Zurich

Occupation by appointment

Type of employment: Temporary

Reference number: 16228

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

YOUR TASKS

  • Responsible for the care and care of patients from hospital entry to exit
  • Control of the entire care process
  • The pivot for patients, relatives and the interdisciplinary team
  • Taking on helping, consulting, coordinative and analytical functions
  • Completion of the practical part at Klinik Im Park, the theoretical part at the educational provider Careum Zürich or at the ZAG in Winterthur

Your profile

  • Completion of a three-year vocational training course with a federal certificate of competence, a DMS/FMS degree or a high school diploma
  • Empathy and a high level of social competence in dealing with patients and their relatives as well as in working with employees and doctors
  • Customer and service orientation, a strong sense of responsibility and self-employment
  • Willingness to develop your expertise and make an important contribution to the success of our clinic

FOLLOWING DOCUMENTS HAVE TO BE INCLUDED

  • Motivation letter (justification, why you have the profession Dipl.) Nursing professional want to learn HF) as well as a tabular CV with current photo
  • Results Multicheck
  • Teaching certificate of completed training EFZ incl. Vocational education certificates
  • Provide two reference persons with telephone number and e-mail address
  • Copies of employment certificates and trial days and/or trial weeks reports (if any)

Accompanied by your vocational trainer and your training managers, you will gain insights into various areas of highly specialized medicine during your practical assignments. You have the opportunity to be employed directly by Klinik Im Park for the duration of your training and to benefit from our employment conditions and our employees. This also includes a constant wage payment during the entire training period at attractive conditions. If you are interested in direct employment, we offer you an aptitude internship after the interview. This is part of the aptitude procedure for training HF care. Duration of training: 3 years (a shortened RF study of 2 years is possible with a teaching degree as a health specialist).

As employees who have to move completely for their work, you will receive four additional paid free days (excluding specialists and cadre doctors) with a 100% allowance. With a reduced workload, the number of days off is reduced accordingly. Employees in the hourly wage receive an equivalent financial compensation.

For additional information, Andreja Sokler Per, responsible for training, is available at andreja.soklerper@hirslanden.ch. We look forward to your contact.

Please note that for data protection reasons we cannot consider paper dossiers (will not be returned) nor applications by email. Please apply exclusively online via this tool.
