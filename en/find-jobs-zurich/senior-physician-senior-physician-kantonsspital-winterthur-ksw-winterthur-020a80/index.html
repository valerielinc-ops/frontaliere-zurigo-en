Full description

For geriatrics we are looking for 1. October 2026 or by agreement a committed and motivated personality as a senior physician / senior physician. This senior medical activity can be credited as further training to the focus in geriatrics (recognition category A). We cover a wide range of geriatrics (acute geriatrics, MemoryClinic, age traumatology, long term care). We offer the opportunity to use a wide range of training opportunities within the KSW due to the close cooperation with the Center for General Internal Medicine.

Tasks

  • Treatment and care of patients as well as patients at the acute care bed station and in the old-age trauma center of the Cantonal Hospital Winterthur
  • Treatment and care of the people in the Oberi and Adlergarten age centres of the city of Winterthur within our team of home doctors
  • Teaching and supervision of assistant doctors
  • Participation in in-house counselling
  • Design of treatment concepts of multimorbid patients and patients taking into account ethical and palliative medicine principles, including the social environment and the various therapeutic services
  • Participation in the Pikettdienst of the age centres Adlergarten and Oberi

Profile

  • You prefer to have completed further training as a specialist / general internal medicine specialist or are about to graduate and are interested in obtaining the main title of geriatrics
  • You are interested in geriatrics and the problems of older people and want to develop your expertise in this field
  • You are a committed, resilient and extremely team-capable personality with a high level of social competence and very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • You are flexible, appreciate a collegial approach and would like to work in an innovative, leading healthcare company, where you can use your skills and knowledge comprehensively.

Benefits: Work and private life should fit together. Therefore, the KSW offers you – where the company makes it possible – flexible and smooth working hours, home office, generous maternity leave, paternity leave and employment levels that can be adapted to different phases of life. Develop yourself professionally and personally: Every year, around 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses are open to you at KSW. We also support external training – temporally and/or financially.

  • Sometimes it needs support. In the event of personal stress, prolonged illness or difficult experiences in your professional or private life, various confidential discussions are available at KSW.
  • At KSW we work together and for each other. Appreciation, open and respectful communication and cooperation across professional groups and departments shape our everyday work. You should be able to contribute with your ideas and strengths and develop.

Your future is also ensured: You benefit from very good social benefits and a pension fund solution with BVK, which is significantly above the legal minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, you are very well covered as a KSW employee.

  • Your health is important to us. Benefit from discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training and courses as well as discounts in pharmacies and selected health insurance companies.
  • Family and work can be well connected with us. The daycare center La Luna with spacious opening hours is directly next to the KSW. In addition, the KSW supports you – depending on income – financially with the crib costs.

There is also something to discover outside of work: Thanks to our cooperation with cultural partners in Winterthur and the surrounding area, you regularly benefit from discounts for concerts and theatre performances.
