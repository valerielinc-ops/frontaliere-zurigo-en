Your tasks and perspectives

  • The internship lasts 6 months and you get an in-depth insight into the daily care of an acute hospital
  • You support the professionals in the care of patients
  • Also the completion of logistical and administrative tasks, such as ordering bed linen is one of your tasks.
  • You work with a wide range of patients, specialists and professional colleagues
  • The aim of the internship is to gain experience in the professional field of care and the examination of suitability for a profession in the health care sector.

What you bring for this job

  • You are at least 15 years old at the start of your internship
  • You are planning a bridging year after completing the compulsory schooling period and before starting your apprenticeship
  • You are looking for an internship, as your desired course of study at the university of applied sciences requires an internship
  • You work responsibly and very carefully because you are aware of your responsibility towards patients
  • Physically and psychologically you are resilient
  • You are able to organize and plan your work independently

Our offer to you

  • During your internship, you can inform our trainees about their apprenticeships and also make important contacts
  • You can combine the internship with a preliminary apprenticeship
  • For your further professional career you can make contacts
  • The internship will take place in a family environment.
