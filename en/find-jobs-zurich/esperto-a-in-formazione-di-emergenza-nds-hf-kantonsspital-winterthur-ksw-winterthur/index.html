Full description

You are looking for the challenge in dealing with quickly changing situations and complex questions. You enjoy the independent and interdisciplinary care of people from all age groups, different socio-cultural origins and with different degrees of illness and injury.

Tasks

  • Professional and comprehensive care in the emergency department requires a high level of professional knowledge and social competence. You ensure physical and psychological well-being and patient safety
  • Dealing with rapidly changing situations and understanding people in exceptional moments is part of the everyday work of an expert Emergency Care Postgraduate (NDS) HF
  • In emergency situations, you keep a cool head and can act competently and thoughtfully. Social skills help you to feel comfortable in an interdisciplinary team and to cope with changing requirements

Profile

  • Admitted to NDS HF are individuals who have a degree as a dipl. Nurse HF / dipl. Nursing specialist HF or Bachelor of Science in Nursing FH or a foreign diploma in nursing recognised as equivalent by the competent authority under federal law
  • You have at least 6 months of professional experience in acute care in a hospital or clinic
  • Start of studies in April and October, starting 2 to 4 months before
  • The duration of studies depends on the workload and varies as follows: workload of 100%
  • study duration of two years, workload of 90%
  • study duration of two years and 10 weeks, workload of 80%
  • study duration of two years and 20 weeks
