Full description

Join our Group Corporate Center Legal team and help enable Zurich’s success in digital innovation through strategic legal support. You will advise on complex, cutting-edge matters, collaborate with Zurich’s industry-leading teams and shape the strategic direction of Legal Data & Digital. This is an opportunity to innovate, grow and make an impact with a passionate team.

What you will do

  • Provide legal advice on technology, data, privacy and digital business models, translating complex legal requirements into practical solutions.
  • Build strong relationships across Zurich’s businesses and functions globally, using those relationships to shape key decisions, global projects and business initiatives.
  • Own legal preparedness for and the end-to-end response to significant data incidents, coordinating stakeholders and advising on legal and regulatory obligations.
  • Draft and review intra-group agreements, contracts and other legal documents relating to technology, data and privacy matters.
  • Advance a legal operating model that uses technology to deliver efficient, scalable and commercially focused legal services.
  • Develop and deliver guidelines and training on technology, data and privacy legal matters, and lead relevant legal knowledge-sharing initiatives.
  • Set and execute the strategic direction for Legal Data & Digital, aligned with Zurich’s business priorities, risk appetite and legal strategy.
  • Lead, develop and inspire a high-performing team, setting clear priorities and fostering collaboration, accountability and professional growth.
  • Constantly identify and implement opportunities to make governance processes and related frameworks effective, proportionate and supportive of responsible business execution.
  • Represent the legal perspective in engagements with control functions, providing input on interpretations and proposed requirements to support proportionate, risk-informed and practicable outcomes.
  • Support M&A transactions and public affairs efforts.
  • Engage with external legal counsel, regulators and other relevant authorities on technology, data, privacy and digital matters.

What you bring

  • A strong interest in and passion for technology, together with a sound understanding of its implications for the insurance industry and the applicable legal and regulatory frameworks.
  • A proactive, hands-on, curious and solution-oriented approach, with the ability to think creatively and navigate complex issues.
  • Extensive expertise and a strong track record in technology, data, privacy and digital legal matters, preferably gained at an international insurance company, leading law firm or other global organization.
  • Proven people leadership experience, with the ability to set direction, build capability and create an inclusive, high-performing team culture.
  • Strong strategic judgment and commercial acumen, with the ability to evaluate complex legal and regulatory issues and translate them into practical legal advice for business stakeholders.
  • Credibility and confidence to advise, challenge and influence senior executives and stakeholders across businesses, functions and geographies.
  • Law degree and bar exam, preferably from Switzerland.
  • Postgraduate studies, such as an LL.M., and relevant privacy qualifications are advantageous.
  • Fluency in English is required; proficiency in additional languages is desirable.
