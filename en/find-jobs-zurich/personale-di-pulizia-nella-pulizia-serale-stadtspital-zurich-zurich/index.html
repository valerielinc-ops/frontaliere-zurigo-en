Full description

For the Facility Management department at the Waid site, we are looking for 1. November 2026 or by arrangement temporary for six to a maximum of 18 months dedicated and competent personalities for the evening cleaning.

Tasks

  • Responsible for maintenance and intermediate cleaning in all hospital premises such as patient stations, special areas, medical treatment tracts, offices, public areas and exit service.
  • Implementation of the cleaning work according to the work plan (X-Clean).
  • Carry out all insulation cleaning operations in accordance with the applicable hygienic principles.
  • Responsibility for compliance with safety regulations and guidelines when handling equipment, machinery and materials.
  • Participation in the smooth running of cleaning processes in the interdisciplinary team.

Profile

  • Working hours between 16.00 and 22.00 hrs, applicable from Monday to Friday
  • Several years of professional experience, ideally in hospital cleaning
  • Training as a housekeeping practitioner EBA or building cleaner EBA beneficial
  • Good knowledge of German (at least level B1) as well as basic IT user knowledge
  • High flexibility, resilience, willingness to provide services as well as strong hygiene and quality awareness
