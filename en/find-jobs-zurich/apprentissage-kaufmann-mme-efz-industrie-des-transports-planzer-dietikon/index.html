Full description

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family-run company, we value people who think ahead, pitch in and want to grow with us. Become part of our family and help shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us.

What you will do

  • You will learn the world of the transport and logistics industry from the ground up
  • You will look after customers, process inquiries and organize orders as part of the team
  • You will work confidently with numbers, support accounting and maintain data
  • You will use foreign languages purposefully, organize meetings and record information in writing

Why you will succeed

  • You enjoy interacting with people, like working in a team and can adapt well to others
  • You have an interest in business contexts, organization and modern IT
  • You are articulate in writing and speech and communicate clearly
  • You are confident with numbers and think ahead
  • Independence, reliability and willingness to learn set you apart – you remain flexible even when things change

Your benefits

  • Family-oriented corporate culture: Respect, trust and fair treatment matter to us – we use first names and support you personally on your journey.
  • Holidays: In 1. Apprenticeship year you have 8, in the 2. 7 and 6 weeks of vacation in the following years.
  • Events: During your apprenticeship with us, you will take part in various events such as WelcomeDay, advanced seminar, apprentice camp, apprenticeship graduation ceremony, etc.
  • Financial support: With us, it’s not just the salary that’s right: You get a fair apprentice salary plus a 13th-month salary. If you give your all at school, in üK and at work, you can even earn a performance bonus. We cover the cost of your school materials, and we provide a financial contribution toward your laptop. On top of that, you get a Halbtax-Abo for your entire apprenticeship.
  • Language stay: Fancy a language stay? With us, you can polish your language skills and experience a great deal along the way!
  • Talent development: We offer you good opportunities for advancement and development.

Who we are For us as a company, supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere are at the heart of everything. That is why we particularly value qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also place great importance on punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
