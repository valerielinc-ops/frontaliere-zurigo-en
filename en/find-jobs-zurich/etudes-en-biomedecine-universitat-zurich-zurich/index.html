Department of Biology Administration

Job opening 01.02.2027

The study coordination biology takes care of the study programmes of the Department of Biology of the Faculty of Mathematics and Natural Sciences. We advise and support lecturers and students, administer the study programmes and services and maintain contacts and interfaces with other administrative units. We are a team of six people.

Tasks

As a study advisor/in biomedicine, you are the first point of contact and responsible for advising the students and prospective students of the bachelor and master programme biomedicine. They offer specialist advice, carry out information events and update the homepage and guidance. They advise the students on the transition to the master’s degree program and the creation of the individual learning agreement. They maintain contact with the relevant departments of UZH, in particular with the study coordination of the Department of Biology and the Program Directorate Biomedicine. In addition, you are responsible for organizing the assignments of doctoral candidates as examination supervisors in the Department of Biology.

Profile

We expect a completed scientific degree (Master of Science) or an equivalent previous education, the degree of which is several years ago. Good knowledge of German and English is required. They work very independently, accurately, responsibly and efficiently and like to deal with young people.
