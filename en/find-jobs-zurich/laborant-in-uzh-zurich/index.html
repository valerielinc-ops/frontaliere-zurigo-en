Main responsibilities

  • The group's research is based, among other things, on induced pluripotential stem cell (iPSC) models of genetic diseases in the ciliopathy group.
  • These are inherited diseases which lead to developmental disorders, malformations and progressive kidney or retinal disease in humans.
  • To understand the pathogenesis of these diseases, we differentiate iPSCs into 2D and 3D neural models and kidney organoids.
  • The activity includes support for researchers in cell culture and the general organisation of the molecular biology laboratory and cell culture room.
  • This also includes ordering reagents and preparing stock solutions, cloning, etc.
  • For the time being, it is a temporary project-related employment (annual contracts). Requirements
  • We want a responsible, enthusiastic, flexible, socially and technically competent professional with completed training as a laboratory assistant: in or a master’s degree in biology or a related discipline.
  • They have experience in cell culture (ideally stem cell culture) and molecular biology.
  • They perform molecular biology techniques efficiently and accurately and enjoy developing, introducing and learning new methods.
  • They are ready for interdisciplinary cooperation with different research groups.
