Full description

As a leading Swiss construction and real estate service provider, Implenia develops, realizes and manages living spaces, working environments and infrastructure for future generations in Switzerland and Germany. Implenia also offers tunnelling and related infrastructure projects in other markets. The company combines the know-how of highly qualified consulting, development, planning and execution units under one roof into an integrated, multinationally leading construction and real estate service provider and employs around 9,000 people throughout Europe. More information at implenia.com

For our Civil Engineering department we are looking for you as our new security guard / security chief (m/f/d) 80-100%. Your future employment location will be in Opfikon. Your location is between Zurich and Winterthur.

You move that.

  • You ensure that employees are protected from the risks of railway operations and railway power plants at all times.
  • As a security guard, you make sure that all people on the construction site are warned in time of passing trains.
  • You set up the security device, carry it out and speak directly with the relevant authorities – on the phone or by radio.
  • You report special or unusual events in the work area to the responsible persons on the construction site.

This is why you succeed

  • You have completed training as a security guard / security manager and have already gained experience in the field of occupational safety.
  • You have good knowledge of German in words and writing.
  • You are in possession of a category B ticket (car with manual control) and have good eyesight and hearing.
  • For this responsible activity, you bring the willingness to work independently, reliably and team-oriented – as well as the openness to assignments in shift operations (early, day, night and weekend services).
  • You have a high physical and mental resilience and act at all times with a strong security awareness.
  • The use of digital tools such as iPad and iPhone is easy for you, and you are motivated to continuously develop and learn new applications.

This is what we offer you

  • A multicultural working environment with the chance to shape a future worth living in
  • State-of-the-art technologies and a contemporary workplace at a sustainable company
  • A company that promotes talent and the training of its employees
  • The security of a financially healthy, broad-based group with strong roots in Switzerland
  • An exciting selection of benefits:
  • 5 weeks holidays and 9 paid bridge days
  • an in-house pension fund with very good coverage and 3 contribution levels
  • numerous discounts on well-known suppliers of products and services
