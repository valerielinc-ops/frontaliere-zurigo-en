University didactic training

Job as per 1. March 2027 or by arrangement, indefinitely

The University Didactic Training at the University of Zurich supports the more than 4,800 teachers in further developing their teaching skills. It offers a comprehensive high-school educational offer, which includes individual courses as well as longer programs such as a CAS in Higher Education. Information about the team “Highly Guilty Education” can be found on our website. In order for our German and English-language programmes to run smoothly and for participants and course managers to be accompanied reliably, we are looking for a committed, service-oriented and digitally affine personality for programme coordination.

Tasks

Together with the programme managers, they ensure a professional flow of our further education offers. Their main tasks include:

  • Coordination and administration of the Teaching Skills and CAS in Higher Education programmes in German and English
  • Support for participants
  • Implementation of the registration procedures and maintenance of the participant database
  • Preparation and management of contracts, performance certificates and other programme documents
  • Organizational support of programme and course leaders in the preparation and follow-up of the modules
  • Provision of course materials, coordination of performance evidence and conduct of course evaluations
  • Compilation of final documents and participant statistics
  • Preparation of annual and accounting reports
  • Further development and digitalization of administrative processes

Profile

  • Commercial or equivalent training and professional experience in a comparable administrative or coordinating capacity
  • Very good work organization, independence, structured thinking and a careful, efficient way of working
  • High service orientation and a professional, addressee-oriented approach to different stakeholders
  • Enjoy working in a small team
  • Stylistic German and very good knowledge of English in word and writing
  • Very good application knowledge in Microsoft 365
  • Experience with serial letters and forms as well as advanced Excel knowledge (e.g. creation of pivot tables)
  • Experience with learning management systems and databases is an advantage
  • Digital affinity and interest in using AI to support administrative processes
  • Willingness to distribute the 50% allowance on at least four working days per week
