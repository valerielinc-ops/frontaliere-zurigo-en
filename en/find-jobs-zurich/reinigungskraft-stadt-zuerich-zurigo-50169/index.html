Full description

ZüriWC operates and maintains both operated and unoperated public toilets at more than 100 locations in the city and ensures a hygienic and customer-oriented offer. The operated ZüriWC facilities are open for 365 days. Our team is looking for reinforcements as of 1 November 2026 or by agreement.

Tasks

  • They are responsible for cleaning the four supervised ZüriWC facilities at Paradeplatz, Bellevue, Bürkliplatz and ShopVille.
  • They work shifts and weekends (early shift: 06:20 to 2:45 pm, late shift: 13:50 to 22:15 pm).
  • They look after our customers on site, answer questions and ensure a friendly and service-oriented reception.

Profile

  • You bring the willingness to clean toilet facilities and convince with your physical strength.
  • First experience in cleaning is beneficial.
  • High reliability, flexibility and a friendly and service-oriented appearance.
  • Good German skills in word and writing are required.

We offer

An independent meaningful activity in a lively environment and a diverse team. As an employee of the City of Zurich, you benefit from attractive employment conditions.
