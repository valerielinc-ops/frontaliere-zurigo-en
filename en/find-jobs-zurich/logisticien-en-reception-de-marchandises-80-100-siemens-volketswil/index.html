  • Warehouse & Resource Management: You optimize the warehouse design through clear systematics and ensure the constant availability of packaging and consumable materials.
  • Product assembly: You produce end products based on specific customer needs, working closely with specialist departments, product management and sales.
  • New product evaluation: You accompany the introduction of new products and evaluate them in close cooperation with specialist bodies and product managers. Your talents and experiences
  • Successfully completed apprenticeship as electrical installer EFZ, electrical installer EFZ, polymechanic EFZ, logistician EFZ, warehouse operator EBA or comparable training
