Full description

Vontobel has a strong market position in asset management for pension funds and has also successfully developed the business for individualised pension solutions (1e, cadre provision, freedom of movement and pillar 3a) in recent years. For the further growth of our pension business, we are looking for a strong and highly qualified specialist to strengthen our team in Zurich.

What are your tasks

  • Addressing and acquiring customers in the target segment of pension solutions, n amentlich executives, self-employed and entrepreneurs, through the distribution of the bank’s 1e-cade pension solutions
  • Acquisition of new customers in the area of other cadre provision, freedom of movement and pillar 3a
  • Cross-selling in various other areas of the bank, in particular in wealth management
  • Systematic processing of the broker market and development of a network of intermediaries, consultants and intermediaries in your region
  • Advising demanding customers in the area of all pension solutions

What do we expect?

  • Excellent acquisition, consulting and negotiation skills
  • Strong network of external business partners
  • Sound consulting expertise in all topics of financial and pension consulting incl. Pension fund topics, pension planning, financial planning, taxes, etc.
  • Many years of experience in the field of occupational pensions, e.g. at an insurer or at a bank
  • In-depth knowledge of asset management and/or investment advice
  • Academic training with degree and/or high level training in financial planning or occupational pension schemes
  • Very good knowledge of German and English (in words and writing), knowledge of French is advantageous
  • Entrepreneurial, motivated, team capable and winning personality with sovereign appearance, empathy and the ability to speak the language of the customer
  • Readiness for customer-related travel throughout German-speaking Switzerland, especially in the greater Zurich area

What we offer

  • A highly interesting working environment with complex issues, modern technology and a strong focus on innovation.
  • A dynamic and international environment with many ideas that requires flexibility and continuous calibration of priorities without losing sight of the long-term goal.
  • The opportunity to gain end-to-end perspectives on client management and to design processes across departmental boundaries.
  • High personal responsibility and numerous opportunities to learn new things and develop yourself further.

We are looking for talented people and not just a CV that meets all the criteria. Even if you don’t bring everything we ask of you, you should apply.

Our ability to focus on issues such as sustainability in the long term is underpinned by the safety of the family’s 100-year commitment. We are looking for diverse talents who want to make a difference in a changing world and who come from a wide range of fields.
