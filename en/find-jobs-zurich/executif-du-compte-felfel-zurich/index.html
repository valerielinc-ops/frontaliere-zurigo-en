Full description

IN BRIEF

As our Account Executive (80-100%) in Zurich, you are at the center of our customer journey. You take full responsibility for a diverse portfolio and promote customer satisfaction, growth and customer loyalty.

YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Self-responsible management and support of a large customer portfolio, from the phase after the test period to the contract extension.
  • Establish and maintain strong long-term relationships with key contact persons to meet expectations, general satisfaction and customer loyalty to the company.
  • Continuous analysis of customer needs to optimize their experience and identify cross-selling or up-selling opportunities.
  • Actively promote organic growth in your segment to maximize the account potential and business value.
  • Proactive exchange with customers who are aware of health checks, as well as monitoring key indicators to prevent migration (Churn) proactively.
  • Contract negotiations, account-returns and processes for renewing or cancelling contracts.
  • Interfunctional collaboration with our acquisition, product, operations and support teams to ensure a unified customer experience.
  • Sharing valuable feedback based on customer insights to internal teams to continuously improve our offerings and processes.

WHAT YOU MITTING

  • 3–5 years of experience in account management, client sic or a similar position with customer contact.
  • Proven successes in efficient management and the expansion of a large, diverse customer portfolio.
  • Safe handling of CRM tools (HubSpot) to organize your work and to provide your customers with the best possible care.
  • A proactive, solution-oriented way of thinking that combines a sense of distribution with genuine hospitality – you recognize growth opportunities in your accounts and at the same time take care of the people sincerely.
  • Excellent communication skills and the ease to get in touch with everyone – from office manager to CEO.
  • Energy for a dynamic, fast-paced environment, coupled with the patience to build long-term relationships.
  • German and very good English skills.
  • Non-smokers – we don't want you to smell like cigarettes, neither at work nor at customer meetings. At FELFEL everything is about good food.
  • Valid work permit or Swiss passport.

WHAT WE ARE

  • A lot of delicious food, just a few steps from your desk - we fulfill all your culinary wishes...
  • Best barista coffee and tea for the whole team thanks to our innovative gavetti coffee maker
  • 5 weeks of holiday per year, and the possibility to purchase an extra week
  • Every year we grant two whole weeks of "work from the world" and thus provide a variety of work day
  • We think of your future and offer an over-compulsory pension fund solution
  • Freshly baked parents receive 16 or 3 weeks of fully paid parental leave from us. We also support the return to work by making a generous financial contribution to childcare until the kindergarten age.
  • We finance your half-tax subscription and thus support our employees in the use of the public transport
  • Great team-Events, such as our well-known FELFEL Summer-Outings
  • Stay in motion and healthy with our weekly sports courses
