Original description

Canton of Zurich Security Directorate General Secretariat Appeals lawyer (temporary) 80 - 100% In the appeals department of the Security Directorate, we handle all appeals against orders from our offices, especially those from the Migration Office and the Road Traffic Office. We are looking for a lawyer immediately or by appointment. The position is limited to two years. Your tasks

  • You independently handle complex appeals proceedings within the area of responsibility of the Security Directorate.
  • You grasp complex issues quickly, recognize the essentials and work on legal issues in a structured, analytical and precise manner.
  • You process a large number of sometimes urgent transactions quickly, efficiently and carefully.
  • You actively participate in the professional exchange and work constructively in a team. Your profile
  • You have a completed legal degree (Mlaw / lic.iur.) and several years of professional experience, ideally with one to two years of court or administrative practice.
  • You have a stylish, clear and legally precise way of expressing yourself.
  • You work independently, enjoy taking on responsibility and are characterized by an efficient and conscientious way of working.
  • You are resilient, team-oriented and maintain an overview even in demanding situations. What we offer You can expect a challenging and exciting job in which you can use and further develop your legal expertise in a variety of legal areas. We also offer good employment conditions and a workplace in the immediate vicinity of Zurich main station. Interested? Then we look forward to receiving your complete application exclusively via the online tool. Applications from job agencies and applications via email will not be considered for this vacancy. Ramona Lagler, head of the appeals department (043 259 23 45), will be happy to provide you with further information.

Workload: 80-100%
