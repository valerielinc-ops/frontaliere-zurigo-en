Full description

For stationary residential integration, we are looking for a committed and motivated personality (1 year on a temporary basis) as of 01.01.2027 or by agreement.

Stationary residential integration, an offer by the Social Department of the City of Zurich, is a supervised housing offer for socially and health-impaired persons who are unable to fit into a regular home and live abstinently.

Tasks

  • You make a significant contribution to the quality of life for our addicted and mentally impaired clients through your cooperation in the areas of cleaning, laundry and catering.
  • Together with the clients, you are responsible for order in the rooms and personal laundry – in which you guide, accompany and take on tasks where necessary.
  • They regenerate and prepare daily meals and ensure kitchen hygiene.
  • They perceive the sensitivities of the residents and discuss with the caregivers.
  • If necessary and capacity, you accompany the clients during shopping, other appointments or leisure activities.

Profile

  • Completed training in the hospitality industry (EFZ) or comparable training or Interest in the department
  • Experienced professionals and returnees
  • Joy of contact with addicted and mentally impaired clients, passion for the profession, high quality standards and service orientation
  • Teamwork, care and commitment as well as good German skills
  • Readiness for working hours from 08.00 to 16.45 or 11.00 to 20.00 as well as weekend services

We offer

A public employment relationship, fair remuneration with the 13th month’s salary, very good social benefits (pension fund of the city), various training opportunities, various benefits (lunch checks, mobility contribution). You can expect an extremely attractive team environment and a versatile workplace.

About us

With around 2,400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a social Zurich. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.

The Social Institutions and Companies (SEB) of the Social Department contribute to the professional and social integration of people with a wide range of services. The approximately sixty offers include housing and work integration, survival and drug assistance, and childcare.
