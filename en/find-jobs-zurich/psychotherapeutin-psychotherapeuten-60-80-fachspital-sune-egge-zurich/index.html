Full description

Our specialist hospital for social medicine and dependency diseases Sune-Egge offers stationary and outpatient treatment of patients with combined somatic and psychiatric and diseases. For them we have 22 acute and 20 long-term care beds in Zurich and work closely with public hospitals, doctors, local departments and addiction institutions. They take psychotherapeutic and psychosocial treatments in stationary and outpatient settings. Therapeutic work often starts with us not in the therapy room, but in the everyday life of the station, on visits or in informal encounters. The aim is to build confidence through low-swelling contacts and to enable progressively further therapeutic processes. Your tasks include:

  • Psychotherapeutic and psychosocial interventions in people with severe substance dependence, dual diagnosis and social marginalization
  • Diagnostic assessments according to ICD / DSM
  • Individual discussions, crisis interventions and group offers
  • Working with treatment and exit planning as well as interprofessional case meetings
  • Working with medicine (assistance), care, social services, pastoral care, art therapy, physiotherapy and external institutions
  • Combining progress report You can professionally deal with ambivalence, relapses and chronological pathologies and maintain therapeutic contact. We wish:
  • Certified titles of psychotherapy
  • Cantonal professional training permit for independent professional practice
  • At least three years of clinical experience
  • Experience in addiction medicine and/or acute psychiatry of advantage
  • Interest in people with complex psychosocial burdens
  • High relationship competence, empathy and resilience
  • humorous, pragmatic and team-oriented personality
  • Joy in creative and patient-centric solutions
  • A sensuous, professionally demanding activity at the interface of somatics, psychiatry, addiction medicine and social medicine. They work in a dedicated interprofessional team and get space to actively shape low-swelling and patient-centered psychotherapeutic approaches. For information please contact Mr Karim Tissira, Medical Co-Head, 044 537 57 68 or Mr Kuvinda Kossina, Medical Co-Head, 044 537 57 66. You can also find information at www.swsieer.ch. We offer an open working environment with dedicated employees who are employed daily for people from the addiction and homeless environment.
