Full description

We care with heart and professionalism for people with disabilities. We support, support, care and care. For this purpose, we offer 147 living spaces in various forms of living as well as 180 workplaces. In addition, 90 seats are available in the day care center. Our 11 residential groups with individual focuses are located in Bülach and Kloten. For our team at the Kloten location (grass angle) we are looking for an Dipl. Care professional or SozPäd with leadership experience 80-100 % At Pigna, we create living spaces where people with impairments live with joy, dignity and self-determination. In our residential group with direct access to the park, you will accompany twelve adult residents and lead a dedicated, interdisciplinary team of twelve professionals. Your activities Your daily work is versatile: You lead your team professionally and personally, provide individual care and support according to agogic principles and design nursing and support plans together with your team that see people in their entirety. You plan and coordinate everyday life, talk to relatives, doctors, doctors and authorities, organize services and lead team meetings in which exchange and development are promoted. You actively participate in the team management committee and in higher-level processes and thus contribute to the further development of the living area. Around a third of your time you are actively in contact with the residents – because you know that closeness to the practice and real encounter are the basis for quality and team culture. You bring an education as a social pedagogue HF/FH or nurse HF/FH and have several years of experience in accompanying people with disabilities or in long-term care. But above all, this work makes sense to you and fills you with joy. Leadership experience is a prerequisite, more important to us is your attitude: empathy, humor, openness and the passion to take responsibility and motivate and empower others. You are resilient, self-employed, flexible and enjoy working in a team. You live a convincing, sustainable team culture and have clear procedures to optimize and maintain processes and working methods. You are convinced that good cooperation combines and values professional and human competences and you succeed in sparking this conviction in your team. A workplace with trust, appreciation and freedom of design awaits you. We live an open du-culture, promote initiative and offer attractive conditions: paid regeneration leave, additional Fridays and a wide range of further education opportunities. Do you feel addressed? If you want to design living spaces with us in which people with disabilities can develop their possibilities – then we look forward to your application! If you have any questions or need more information, Holger Nithack, Division Management Living, at 044 800 75 97, is at your disposal.
