Full description

At Implenia Holzbau, a lot is currently in motion – driven by the growing importance of sustainable construction and our clear focus on modern timber construction. With our many years of experience, high technical competence and a strong interdisciplinary team, we realize demanding timber construction services across all phases – from planning to production to execution.

In order to further develop our organization and shape timber construction in a future-oriented way, we are looking for committed personalities who want to take responsibility and grow together with us – as Ingenieur Holzbau (m/f/d).

Do you have what it takes to turn your communication skills and entrepreneurial thinking into attractive offers? Are analytical, timely and independent work just as important to you as your excellent skills as a team player? Then we are very much looking forward to your commitment for our team in Rümlang!

Tasks

  • Management of structural designs, fire protection topics and timber constructions and detailed development in the field of engineering
  • Support partners for implementation and acquisition in the areas of structural design, fire protection and detailed constructions
  • Coordination of engineering services and ensuring a smooth process internally and externally
  • Joint further development and expansion of engineering competencies in Implenia Holzbau
  • Assumption of project management functions in development and planning projects
  • Preparation of various expertises

What you bring

  • Successfully completed study in the field of timber construction engineering or equivalent training
  • User knowledge of the MS-Office range and CAD software
  • Good knowledge of German in writing and language
  • Knowledge of current Swiss standards
  • A fascination with wood construction and the ability to find creative and innovative solutions

Of great advantage you also bring the following

  • Professional experience as a wooden engineer
  • Knowledge of Frilo, Dlubal and Revit software
  • Good knowledge of bran protection in timber construction

What we offer

  • Innovative, sustainable and international working environment
  • Use of modern technologies
  • Support for employee development (promotion of further education by the Implenia Academy)
  • Flexible working hours included. Home office option
  • Implenia Benefits, including (to name just a few):
  • a company-owned, excellent pension fund with 3 contribution levels: Implenia Provision
  • at least 5 weeks holidays and 9 paid bridge days
  • Travel insurance for the whole family (everyone in your household is insured)

In addition to challenging and varied tasks, you can expect attractive benefits that Implenia offers as an innovative and sustainable company. We look forward to meeting you!
