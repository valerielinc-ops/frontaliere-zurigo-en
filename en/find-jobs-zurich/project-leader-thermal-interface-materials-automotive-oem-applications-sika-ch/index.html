Full description

About the role As a Project Leader, you will play a central role in the rapid and targeted development of innovative thermal interface materials (TIM) for battery and automotive applications. In particular, the focus is on thermally conductive adhesives (thermal conductive adhesives) and gap fillers. They translate demanding customer requirements into concrete material concepts and prototypes in the shortest possible time and accompany their development until successful sampling at the customer. You coordinate interdisciplinary development activities and ensure that customer requirements quickly lead to marketable solutions.

  • Leading and conducting feasibility studies for TIM materials from requirements analysis to successful customer evaluation and transfer to a development project.
  • Documentation of development results, risks, decisions and next steps in a comprehensible and management-friendly form.
  • Translation of customer requirements in techn. Specification, test concepts, DoE approaches and clear basis for decision-making
  • Evaluation and selection of suitable polymer chemistry, fillers, filler combination and process parameters
  • Direct technical communication with customers, internal interfaces, suppliers and production areas
  • Initiating and driving patenting of new technologies and processes
  • Customer communication is binding, fact-based and solution-oriented, without exaggerating technical risks or uncertainties.
  • Trial plans are efficient, hypothesis-based and allow quick technical decisions even in the case of incomplete data.
  • Represents Sika and promotes positive perception in the technology sector (investors, congresses, seminars, etc.)
  • Maintenance and maintenance of laboratory and equipment as well as ensuring order, cleanliness
  • Implementation of technology projects and promotion of innovative ideas with a clear focus on technology development and active generation of technology impulses

Your skills and experience

  • Completed training as a chemist:in or materials scientist:in, midestens Master
  • Mind. 3 years of successful development experience with TIM materials, TC fillers, adhesives or sealants
  • Professional experience with polymer chemistry - polyurethanes, STP/silane, epoxy, acrylate, silicone
  • Experience with Automotive OEM customer projects including direct customer communication
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in words and writing, other languages of advantage (Chinese, French, Italian)
  • Strong communication and customer orientation
  • High implementation and solution orientation
  • Quick understanding and pragmatic decision-making
  • Networked thinking and creative solution development
  • Enforcement and secure prioritization

RELATED

  • Experience in carrying out rapid prototype development for battery applications
  • Ability to set up and interpret fast DoE and screening approaches / flair for data science
  • Experience in the field of e-mobility / manufacturer of batteries or manufacturers of electric vehicles, including serial transfer or industrialization
  • Knowledge of Automotive Requirements (IATF, IMDS, REACH)
  • Experience with simulations or modeling of TIM behavior
