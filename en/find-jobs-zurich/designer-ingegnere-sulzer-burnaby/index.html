Full description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

Position: Designer I

Currently, we have an immediate opening for a Designer I at our Burnaby facility.

At our Burnaby facility we provide pump related services and products including field engineering support, hydraulic/mechanical design and analysis, pump repairs, performance testing, retrofitted/upgraded pumps, duplicate/replacement pumps, pump packages and spare parts. On site we have 5,600 m^2 covered manufacturing space including a machine shop, repair/assembly area and two pump test sites with power capabilities of up to 15,000 HP with 50/60 hertz speed.

To learn more about Sulzer, visit our website at www.sulzer.com. Position: Designer I As a member of our Engineering Department you will assist, at a junior level, in the design and order processing of centrifugal pumps, components and equipment; prepare design layouts; interpret design requirements; perform calculations; create and modify drawings; create Bills of Materials (BOM) and create other engineering related documentation in accordance with customer specifications and company policies.

How You Will Contribute

  • Prepare engineering drawings using CAD programs (Inventor, AutoCAD, Siemens NX & Medusa) to enable the complete manufacturing of pump products.
  • Provide engineering drawing control using product lifecycle management software (Teamcenter)
  • Prepare Bills of Materials within the ERP system (SAP)
  • May contact suppliers and other departments with respect to the coordination of design work and clarification of requirements.
  • May work from more detailed design requirements laid down by others in the form of notes, layouts and verbal instruction.
  • May perform stress analysis.
  • May assign and check the work of others.
  • May prepare design reports or notes.
  • Handle special engineering tasks as assigned by the supervisor.
  • Support, cooperate with, and participate in company-wide EHS Management System which includes following all safety, health and environmental policies and procedures.
  • Perform other duties as necessary.

Qualifications

Our ideal candidate will have

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Mechanical Engineering or Diploma in Mechanical Design Technology, including such courses as mechanical design, hydraulics, computer-aided drafting (2D/3D), etc.
  • One to two years of experience in a design and manufacturing environment using techniques applicable to product design and order processing.
  • SAP and Teamcenter experience is an asset.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications.
