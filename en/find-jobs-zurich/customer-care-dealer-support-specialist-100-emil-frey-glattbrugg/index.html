What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family company with over 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessories as well as the provision of financial services. Emil Frey Mobility AG is a competence center for integrated mobility services around the car. This includes vehicle insurance, but also our own financing companies. This is what awaits you:

  • You make the difference in the customer experience You accompany our customers along their entire journey and provide positive experiences for every telephone and written request.
  • Whether leasing, insurance or contract issues: You advise competently, solution-oriented and with a lot of heart and soul.
  • You hold the strings together You are responsible for the timeliness of our customer data as well as the contract termination process and ensure that our customers experience a positive experience at the end of their contract period.
  • You support our garage partners As an important interface between customers, garages and internal departments, you will be at your side for questions about products and processes.
  • Together with internal teams, you actively design new processes, contribute your ideas and help to continuously improve our services and processes. This is what you bring with you:
  • Commercial education or comparable experience in customer contact

  • Communication strength, empathy and a sovereign appearance
