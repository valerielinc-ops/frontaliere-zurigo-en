Full description

The Zweckverband Pflege und Betreuung Mittleres Tösstal provides the population of the four association communities Turbenthal, Wila, Wildberg and Zell with inpatient and ambulatory care, care and residential services according to the service mandate. Spitex Mittleres Tösstal provides professional outpatient care and care for the inhabitants of the mentioned communities. We are a progressive and dynamic organization with over 90 employees and integrate interdisciplinary specialist teams, such as acute and long-term care, psychosocial care, palliative care, housekeeping and care as well as Spitex Plus services. To strengthen our dedicated team, we are looking for an Nurse FaGe or HF for evening work with a workload of approximately 30-50 %

We offer

  • A dedicated, far-sighted and interdisciplinary team
  • Open, warm and human team culture
  • A responsible, varied and independent work
  • Careful training
  • Digital working instruments
  • Individual, vocational and subject-specific training opportunities
  • Monthly team meetings
  • Good pay and above-average social benefits and at least. 5 weeks holiday

Your profile

  • Completed training as a nurse Health or HF - or study
  • In the evening service (period about 16.00 - 23.00)
  • Self-employed, responsible and team-oriented working methods
  • High level of social and professional competence
  • Flexibility and resilience
  • Computer skills
  • Driving licence Cat B, own car

This position is also suitable for re-entry into care and for family managers and students. Do you feel addressed? We look forward to your application and to meeting you personally. If you have any questions, Mrs. Valentina Schalcher (Tuesday, Wednesday morning and Thursday) is at your disposal on telephone 052 385 23 30.

Please send your electronic or written application to

Spitex Mittleres Tösstal, Ms Renate Stucki, Divisional Management, Tösstalstrasse 111b, 8488 Turbenthal Email: bereichleitung.smt@hin.ch
