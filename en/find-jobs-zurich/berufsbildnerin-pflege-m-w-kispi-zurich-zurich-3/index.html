Full description

Children's Hospital Zurich Eleonorenstiftung | online since : 06.10.2026

In combination with employment focusing on oncology, stem cell transplantation and haematology

Your tasks

  • You take responsibility for the training of learners and students in the Ambulatory Centre for Oncology, SZT and Hematology.
  • You plan and design individual learning opportunities for the students Care HF and FH as well as Learning FAGE.
  • You conduct training interviews, control the learning history documentation and create the qualifications of learners and students.

Your profile

  • Completed Diploma Care HF / FH
  • Professional experience in pediatric oncology
  • Further education SVEB 1 (or readiness to graduate)
  • Experience as a day companion of learners / students
  • Professional experience preferred in paediatrics as well as oncology or SZT care
  • In-depth knowledge in evidence-based care
