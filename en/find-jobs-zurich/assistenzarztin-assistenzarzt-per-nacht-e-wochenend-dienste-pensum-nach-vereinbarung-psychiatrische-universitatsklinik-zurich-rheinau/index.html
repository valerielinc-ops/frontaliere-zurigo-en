Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility.

For our Center for Inpatient Forensic Therapy we are looking for an immediate or by appointment, temporarily for one year.

Assistant doctor for night and weekend services (pensus by arrangement)

30% - 100%

Their tasks

  • ensuring medical care (psychiatric and somatic) during the night and weekends in cooperation with a psychiatric and an internist senior physician (Picket) at the Centre for Inpatient Forensic Therapy and the Centre for Integrative Psychiatry (General Psychiatry)
  • Professional documentation of investigations, treatments and relevant events carried out in clinical information systems
  • Ensuring structured and addressable communication (including institutions and authorities and external practitioners)
  • Transfers to the interprofessional treatment teams
  • Interest in forensic-psychiatric issues
  • Experience in clinical psychiatry
  • They comply with the legal system
  • Federal State Examination in Medicine or an equivalent education (MEBEKO recognition available for foreign diplomas)
  • Structured and interdisciplinary working methods
  • Good knowledge of German (at least C1)
  • Development with perspective: Diverse career paths in one of the largest psychiatric clinics in Switzerland
  • Knowledge that advances: Extensive initial, further and further training offers
  • Clinic, research and teaching united: working in a university environment with international networking
  • Focus on health: Various sports offerings, green oases and an internal care team
  • Future provision with added value: 60% employer contribution to the pension fund
