Full description

The Balgrist Campus is a modern research and development centre dedicated entirely to the human musculoskeletal system. As a research centre of national importance, we operate three open research platforms, which are available as open access platforms primarily to academic research groups, but also to industry. Our offer includes support for scientific research projects as well as clinical services in the field of movement analysis.

The goal of the Swiss Center for Movement Analysis, SCMA, is to quantify the functionality of the musculoskeletal system by means of highly precise, multimodal measurement techniques, prevent sports injuries and falls and optimize motor rehabilitation after orthopaedic or neurological injuries.
