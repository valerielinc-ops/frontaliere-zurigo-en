Career / Vacancies Graduate nurse HF as director Night Watch Pensum: 80-100% admission: by appointment Apply PDF advertisement People with dementia need people who are happy to be there for them. Sonnweid is considered one of the leading institutions for people with dementia. In our competence center, our 330 employees from various departments look after and care for 174 residents. Family doctors and old-age psychiatrists ensure medical care. The differentiated living and care concept is adapted to the stage of the disease. Our semi-cut ward offers a temporary home for people with particularly challenging behavior, while the day/night ward relieves carers with day and holiday stays. Our mission statement, which focuses on the needs of people suffering from dementia, forms the basis for our daily actions. With joy and conviction, we do everything we can to make the inhabitants feel at home and at home in Sonnweid.
