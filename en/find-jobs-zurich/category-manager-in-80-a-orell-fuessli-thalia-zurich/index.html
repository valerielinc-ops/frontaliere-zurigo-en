Full description

Do you like stories like us? With your passion you are exactly right with us. At Orell Füssli Thalia AG, we create spaces for diverse stories and knowledge that inspire and enrich our society. In addition, Orell Füssli is a place where employees feel comfortable, where they enjoy working well together and achieve more together. For our headquarters in Zurich Wiedikon we are looking for a category manager 80% (a) as of 1 February 2027

Their main tasks

  • Supervision, design and further development of the assortment gifts (non-books) and food in close cooperation with assortment professionals from other areas (branch representatives, marketing, etc.)
  • Develop, create and implement range concepts with relevant stakeholders
  • Procurement of goods at home and abroad
  • Coordination and support of the assigned purchasing assistance in professional matters
  • Analysis and controlling
  • Optimisation of sales and gross profit
  • WC negotiations with suppliers

Your profile

  • Completed training as a retailer, buyer or similar
  • Further development into am Category Managerin an advantage
  • Professional experience of at least 3 years
  • High affinity for gifts and related products
  • Ability and passion to jointly develop ranges in an agile and decentralized environment

Our offer

  • Collaborative and appreciative corporate culture
  • Central location with good public transport connections
  • Working time models adapted to the current living situation
  • Generous holiday scheme and varied discount offer
  • Individual training opportunities; branch and cross-divisional development options
