Full description

We are looking for an independent and service-oriented personality for the Mandat Prime Tower.

You can move that with us.

You manage a complex object on site and are the contact person: in for our customers. Your responsibilities include the following activities:

  • Contact person: in our customer for the support of all house technical systems (HLKKSE)
  • Coordination of various services
  • Advising on ownership in all technical trades - even in complex issues
  • Supervision of maintenance planning including the required evidence
  • Organising and implementing building technology projects
  • Monitoring of service and maintenance of heating, ventilation, air conditioning and sanitary facilities
  • Optimization of building operating costs with innovative ideas
  • Ensuring customer satisfaction
  • Repair services of household equipment
  • Pictet service

That's what makes you.

You describe yourself as a service provider: in the innermost conviction. Positive feedback on your performance motivates you and spurs you on to further top performances. You also have the following requirements:

  • Basic technical training in HLKSE
  • Ideally, further training as a maintenance specialist
  • Several years of experience in technical building maintenance are advantageous
  • Organizational and communication talent
  • Good computer user knowledge
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Cat B driving licence mandatory

That's what we offer you.
