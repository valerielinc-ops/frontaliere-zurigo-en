Do you want to be part of our team and make a difference? For our endoscopy center, we are looking for a dedicated nurse who will actively support our team, especially in the recovery room and in monitoring care. A versatile and varied area of responsibility awaits you, in which you competently and sensitively look after our patients before, during and after endoscopic procedures. You benefit from regular working hours from Monday to Friday between 10:00 and 18:00.

Your main tasks

  • You look after and accompany patients throughout the endoscopic process – from preparation to examination to monitoring and follow-up in the recovery room
  • You assist in diagnostic procedures in the university environment
  • You ensure a high quality of care and contribute to a smooth process in the endoscopy center
  • You work interdisciplinary and maintain a close cooperation with different specialist areas with a clear focus on the care of our patients:
  • You take on administrative tasks and contribute to an efficient organization of everyday work

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a qualified nurse HF/FH or an equivalent qualification recognised in Switzerland
  • Ideally, you will bring experience in endoscopy assistance or monitoring care. Also motivated career starters: women are welcome
  • You meet patients: inside also during and after sedation empathetic, attentive and sensitive
  • You work independently, responsibly and structured
  • You are capable of teamwork and appreciate collaboration in a dynamic, university environment
  • You are open to new things, bring your own initiative and want to actively shape the further development of our endoscopy center

Our offer

  • A varied and responsible activity in a highly specialized university environment
  • A careful introduction with structured onboarding and targeted support for your professional development
  • Diverse internal and external training offers
  • An appreciative leadership culture at eye level and a committed, collaborative team
  • Attractive employment conditions and a contemporary remuneration
  • At least 25 days of holiday per year and additional holidays depending on age
  • Financial support for further education and attractive development opportunities within the USZ
  • Attractive employee benefits, for example in catering, selected partners and public transport
  • A central workplace in Zurich with very good connections to public transport
