Full description

Our customers retire well prepared, invest their money successfully, are optimally secured and save taxes, premiums and fees. This makes us the number one independent financial advisory service. If you would like to contribute to our success story, you will find us in Bülach in a central location.

  • We optimize the entire asset situation of our customers. This makes your work more versatile and varied than other financial services providers.
  • You are involved in consultations from day one and take on the lead more and more.
  • With experienced consultants, you will work out how best to provide for your customers, invest money, finance real estate, regulate discounts and save taxes.
  • The trainee program is a direct entry: In 18 to 24 months, you will acquire everything you need for your work as a consultant.

  • Interest in money topics and pleasure in customer contact

  • Because we grow strongly, many doors are open to you. Make your way with us.

  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
