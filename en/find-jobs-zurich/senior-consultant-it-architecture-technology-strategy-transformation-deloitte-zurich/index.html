  • At Deloitte, you will move beyond hands-on delivery and join our Technology Strategy & Transformation team, working with experienced architects and strategy consultants to help C-suite executives of global organisations on some of the most complex IT projects to modernize application landscapes, define target architectures, assess platform choices and shape transformation roadmaps.
