The organizational unit Financial Institutions is responsible for, controls and coordinates the international business relationships of the Zürcher Kantonalbank with other financial institutions in close cooperation with trade, trade and export finance, asset management, operations and payments. You move in an international environment with a strong connection to the Zurich financial centre and work together with involved stakeholders throughout the company. You will make an important contribution to the further development of the interbank and trade finance business of Zürcher Kantonalbank.

Your tasks

  • Supervise banks and other financial institutions (especially brokers) in the countries assigned to you as the primary point of contact
  • Develop a proposal for business policy in your markets and implement it in close coordination with the specialist areas involved
  • Visits to customers on site, maintenance of an intensive dialogue and further development of the interbank and trade finance business in a high degree of independence
  • Define and assess the risks and return potential in your countries and with your counterparties
  • Acting as a first line of defence, creating risk analyses and loan applications as well as final assessment of KYC profiles
  • Ensure compliance with the business policy with Financial Institutions clients at all levels
  • Completed higher education (HF, FH or university), additional further education is beneficial
  • Several years of professional experience in support of countries and financial institutions including risk analysis and KYC
  • In-depth knowledge of financial products in the interbank market and in the trade finance environment
  • Strong interest in international political, economic and social contexts
  • You convince with your own initiative, high willingness to perform and a strong risk awareness
  • Very good knowledge of German and English, every other language is a plus
  • Motivated, self-organized and established team that looks forward to you
  • Exciting and diverse range of tasks with many network possibilities
  • We offer you space for your ideas and thus design the bank of tomorrow
  • Your career is important to us as an employer, so we support and accompany you on your way
  • Central and modern workplace with the possibility to organize your work flexibly (hybrid)
  • Culture of appreciation and continuous (further) development – personal, technical and professional
