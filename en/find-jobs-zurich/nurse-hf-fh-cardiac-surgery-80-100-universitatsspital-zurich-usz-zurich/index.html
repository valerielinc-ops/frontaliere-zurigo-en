Become part of our dedicated team in heart surgery! With us, you support patients with complex clinical pictures in an innovative university environment. You can expect a varied job with high personal responsibility, a well-founded familiarization and attractive opportunities for professional development.

Your main tasks

  • You care for and care for cardiac surgical and cardiological patients (including heart transplant patients and assistant device patients)
  • You are responsible for the quality of care and for controlling and monitoring the care process
  • You carry out your training task and are also jointly responsible for the implementation
  • You are committed to achieving the departmental goals

Your profile

  • Completed training as a qualified nurse / qualified nurse HF/FH
  • Acute professional experience
  • Flexible and resilient in a dynamic environment
  • Self-employed and reliable working methods
  • Enjoyment of the nursing profession and high willingness to provide services

Our offer

You can expect a versatile task in an advanced working environment, which is characterized by high quality of care, independence, flexibility and a sense of responsibility. The systematic introduction to the field is important to us. A motivated and committed team supports you. For this post, dossiers from recruiters cannot be considered.
