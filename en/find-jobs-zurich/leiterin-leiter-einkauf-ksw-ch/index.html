Full description

In this responsible management role, you lead the purchasing department of the Cantonal Hospital Winterthur and together with your team ensure an economical, legally secure and reliable procurement for a modern center hospital. You are responsible for the operational and strategic development of purchasing, lead your team professionally and in terms of personnel and ensure professional procurement processes, a high quality of service towards the specialist areas as well as effective cooperation with internal and external partners. A special focus is on the further development of purchasing processes, the safe operation and optimization of the ERP and P2P systems used as well as the correct implementation of the requirements of public procurement law.

Tasks

  • You will lead KSW’s purchasing professionally, personnel and organizationally and develop team, processes and structures in a targeted manner.
  • You are responsible for efficient, transparent and legally compliant procurement processes – from demand clarification to ordering and contracting to supplier management
  • Ensure that public procurement is properly prepared, executed and documented
  • You develop operational and strategic purchasing in close cooperation with specialist areas, logistics, finance and other interfaces
  • You take on a central role in the further development and implementation of ERP, P2P and other system solutions as well as in the optimization of master data, invoicing processes and reporting
  • You recognize potential for standardization, digitalization and process improvement and combine economic efficiency, security of supply, compliance and user orientation in a sustainable way.

Profile

  • You have completed higher education and/or further education in business administration, purchasing, supply chain management, business law or a comparable area
  • You bring several years of leadership experience in purchasing, ideally in a complex health or public law environment
  • You have in-depth knowledge of modern purchasing processes, supplier, contract and goods group management
  • Experience with public procurement is a clear advantage
  • You have very good ERP knowledge and experience with P2P solutions
  • Knowledge of Microsoft Dynamics Navision and Jaggaer is an advantage
  • You understand the requirements for service-oriented purchasing and support specialist areas competently, pragmatically and solution-oriented
  • You are a leadership-strong, communicative and implementation-oriented personality who actively shapes changes and convincingly takes employees and stakeholders along with you.

Benefits: Work and private life should fit together. Therefore, the KSW offers you – where the company makes it possible – flexible and flexible working hours, home office, generous maternity leave, paternity leave and employment levels that can be adapted to different phases of life. Develop yourself further professionally and personally: Every year, around 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses are open to you at KSW. We also support external training – temporally and/or financially.

  • Sometimes it needs support. In the event of personal stress, prolonged illness or difficult experiences in your professional or private life, various confidential discussions are available at KSW.
  • At KSW we work together and for each other. Appreciation, open and respectful communication and cooperation across professional groups and departments shape our everyday work. You should be able to contribute with your ideas and strengths and develop.

Your future is also ensured: You benefit from very good social benefits and a pension fund solution with BVK, which is significantly above the legal minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, you are very well covered as a KSW employee.

  • Your health is important to us. Benefit from discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training and courses as well as discounts in pharmacies and selected health insurance companies.
  • Family and work can be well connected with us. The daycare center La Luna with spacious opening hours is right next to the KSW. In addition, the KSW supports you – depending on income – financially with the crib costs.

There is also something to discover outside of work: Thanks to our cooperation with cultural partners in Winterthur and the surrounding area, you regularly benefit from discounts for concerts and theater performances.
