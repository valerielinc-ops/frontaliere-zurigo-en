Full description

At our "Wohnen" location in Winterthur, tenants not only feel at home, but are also looked after optimally and efficiently by our new division of tasks in the team. This not only has advantages for tenants and ownership, but also for you. Through more specialization in the team, we work more closely together, and everyone can focus on their strengths.

If your heart beats for real estate, you are motivated, goal-oriented and also like to work in a team, then we look forward to your application as

Property Manager (m/f/d)

Winterthur, 80-100 %

This is what moves you

  • You are responsible for supporting the team in all administrative activities within the Living site to ensure a smooth operation
  • As the first point of contact on site, you take over the customer support and are responsible for the first contact with customers and tenants
  • Communicating with the tenant, home maintenance, craft businesses and offices is one of your tasks to ensure an effective exchange of information.
  • You are responsible for carrying out final statements and custodial settlements as well as for obtaining and assessing offers, awarding contracts to craft businesses and monitoring the work
  • The processing and forwarding of creditor orders and invoices as well as the debtor control and processing of the dunning system also fall within your area of responsibility.

This is why you succeed

  • You have completed basic commercial training and preferably the property management clerk or the willingness to complete this further training
  • First professional experience as a clerk: in (advantage in the real estate industry or similar) you could already collect
  • You are team capable, resilient and distinguished by your customer- and service-oriented personality
  • An independent and goal-oriented working method as well as good communication skills qualify you in dealing with different groups of people
  • You communicate verbally and in writing fluently in German, English of advantage

Here are some of our benefits

  • Working hours: We live mobile work with individual (year) working hours and/or part-time work. Up to 40% home office is possible. Work-life balance is a top priority for us, because balanced and relaxed employees are important to us. The purchase of additional holidays is possible.
  • Mobility and food: You will receive a contribution for the mobile phone. For your food, we also make a contribution.
  • Salär: With us, you can expect a market-based remuneration with absolute equal pay for all genders. The pension benefits are attractive and you can choose the right one for you from different models. Accident protection insurance covers the private hospital department.

More information about our benefits can be found on our career page.
