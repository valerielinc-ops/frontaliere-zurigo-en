Full description

Want to do more than just drive a truck? Then you are right with us. At EMIL EGGER AG, you do not simply move freight from A to B. You transport heavy machinery, bring industrial plants to the customer and ensure that even demanding transports are carried out safely and professionally. For this, you are on the road with a modern Volvo FH 500 6×4 with a 6-ton lift all over Switzerland. No day is like the other. No order is 08/15. If you like to work independently, have something technical and rather tackle than watch, we look forward to you.

This is what awaits you

  • You guide and serve our truck for Switzerland-wide transports.
  • You transport, move and bring processing machines and technical equipment to the customer.
  • You independently carry out machine removals and introduction work of small machines.
  • You support in dismantling, assembly and installation work.
  • You will find solutions if things do not go according to plan on the construction site.
  • You are the direct contact person at the customer’s site and represent Emil Egger AG professionally.
  • You ensure that your vehicle is operational, clean and technically maintained.
  • You travel throughout Switzerland – occasional overnight stays are part of this.

This is what you bring with you

  • Cat C/E driving licence
  • Experience as a chauffeur and pleasure in driving
  • Technical understanding and skill
  • Independent and reliable working method
  • Enjoy being involved and finding practical solutions
  • Safe and professional approach to the customer
  • Physical resilience and team spirit
  • Readiness for Switzerland-wide missions and occasional overnight stays

Experience with machine moves? Perfect! You have already moved and brought machines, industrial plants or other heavy goods? Then you bring an ideal condition. Still no experience with machine moves, but an experienced C/E chauffeur and tech-savvy? Even then we want to get to know you. For us, it is crucial that you take responsibility, think and like to tackle. We can provide you with the specific know-how for machine moves.

This is what we offer

Your workplace on wheels A Volvo FH 500 (6×4) with 6-ton lifting platform – modernly equipped for demanding machine moves and special transports. Variety instead of routine Transport a machine today, bring a plant to the customer tomorrow. You work in different places with different challenges. Working independently You get responsibility and can contribute your experience. With us, not every handle is prescribed – we expect you to think and find solutions. Professional environment Modern vehicles, professional work equipment and an experienced team that knows what matters when it comes to demanding industrial moves. Long-term perspective Emil Egger AG is an established Swiss logistics company with around 800 employees at 11 locations. Ready for something other than standard transport? If you are looking for a varied job, like working with large machines and want to take on responsibility, then we should get to know each other. We look forward to seeing you!
