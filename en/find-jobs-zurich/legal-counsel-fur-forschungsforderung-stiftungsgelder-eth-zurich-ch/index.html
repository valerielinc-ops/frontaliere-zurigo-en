Legal Counsel for Research Funding & Foundation Funds 50%-60%, Zurich, unlimited descrizione ETH Zurich is one of the world's leading scientific and technical universities. Within the Partnerships for Innovation area, the Research Contract Group supports researchers to place their projects with national and international partners on a secure legal basis. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a solution-oriented personality as a Legal Counsel. Description Your role at the interface between research and law In this position you are the central point of contact for contracts financed from private funding, foundations and non-profit organizations. They work together with the Grants Office and other bodies at ETH Zurich. In addition to consulting the applicants, their work includes the self-responsible preparation and legal examination of contracts in the field of research with partners, mainly from the field of private funding (including foundations and non-profit organisations, Swiss and foreign) . Tasks

  • Coordination with other positions within and outside the ETH is a central task. They work closely with the Grants Office and other positions at ETH Zurich.
