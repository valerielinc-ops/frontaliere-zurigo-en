Mechanic: in conveyor technology & maintenance (logistics)

Coop Cooperative — Dietikon, Zurich, Svizzera

Tasks

  • Carrying out maintenance, maintenance and inspection work on conveyors and logistics systems in cooperation with the supplier

  • Replacement and repair of mechanical components such as rollers, bearings, chains, motors and conveyor belts
