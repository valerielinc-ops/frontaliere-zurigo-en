Full description

Our team is growing - that's why we are looking for reinforcement! As a maintenance technician, you ensure a reliable heating and cooling supply and make an important contribution to sustainability and efficiency with your commitment.

Tasks

  • You perform inspection, maintenance and disruption work on heating and cooling systems reliably and competently.
  • Together with your team, you ensure a reliable heating and cooling supply for our contracting customers in the city of Zurich.
  • You successfully complete work and make sure that everything is documented precisely.
  • When troubleshooting, you act quickly and take the necessary measures.
  • Technical optimization potentials and possibilities for energy saving are identified proactively.

Profile

  • You bring several years of experience in building technology and ideally further training in the area of maintenance.
  • Your strength lies in a solution- and goal-oriented way of working, coupled with a high level of customer and team orientation.
  • Dealing with MSRL systems and good PC skills are part of your repertoire.
  • You are ready to take responsibility for our facilities in the Pikettdienst and have the category B driving licence in your pocket.
  • Commitment and sustainability are part of your DNA. You are a dedicated doer.

We offer

  • More energy for your life: With mobile work, flexible working models and part-time opportunities, you can actively shape your working time and divide it into the best fit for your life. For example, you can buy up to 2 weeks of extra holidays and work part-time at all levels. Job sharing/splitting and unpaid holidays are also possible with us.
  • Sustainable and cheaper travel: With the mobility contribution of up to 600 francs a year, you travel cheaply and sustainably – whether you want to cycle through Zurich or explore Switzerland by train.
  • An investment in your future: We not only value paying fair and transparent wages, but also ensure that you have more of your wages left over. We take over 60% of the savings contributions and additionally offer a super-compulsory interest on the capital accumulated.
  • An energy boost for your free time: We pay you 100 francs a month on your lunch-check card. In addition, you benefit annually from further Fringe Benefits worth 150 francs and attractive mobile subscriptions.
  • Development and training: With our talent management, we actively tackle your development. You benefit from versatile internal and external training opportunities and exciting opportunities to develop further.
  • Meaningful work: Sustainability and meaningful work are not just words for us: shape Zurich and the future for us. Together with us, release energy for a good morning.

About us

ewz has been dynamic and visionary on the road every day for more than 130 years. Whether sustainable energy production, smart energy solution or innovative networking technology: Develop with us today what is important tomorrow.
