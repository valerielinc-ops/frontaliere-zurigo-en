About the role

Talent Acquisition Manager | 8-12 Years Experience

  • Lead end-to-end Talent Acquisition strategy and delivery for Shared Services organization of an Engineering Organization
  • Manage and mentor a team of recruiters, driving recruitment performance, capability building, accountability, and delivery against hiring targets
  • Establish recruitment governance, dashboard, SLAs, KPIs, and operating rhythms to ensure consistent and high-quality hiring outcomes
  • Partner with business leaders and global stakeholders to develop workforce plans aligned to business growth, transformation, and expansion objectives
  • Own headcount planning and hiring execution against approved HC budgets, ensuring workforce requirements are delivered within financial targets
  • Demonstrate proven experience in rapid ramp-ups, Center expansion, transitions, and large-scale hiring programs across multiple business functions
  • Develop proactive sourcing strategies and talent pipelines to support both immediate and future hiring requirements
  • Drive campus recruitment programs including university partnerships, employer branding initiatives, internship programs, graduate hiring, management trainee programs, and pre-placement offers
  • Build and maintain strong relationships with colleges, universities, and educational institutions to create a sustainable early talent pipeline
  • Manage end-to-end campus hiring processes including demand planning, assessments, selection, offer management, and onboarding
  • Own key recruitment metrics including time-to-fill, cost-per-hire, quality of hire, offer-to-join ratio, recruiter productivity, diversity hiring, and hiring manager satisfaction
  • Monitor and control recruitment spend, agency utilization, vendor costs, and sourcing channel effectiveness to drive optimal cost-per-hire and recruitment ROI
  • Lead recruitment process improvement initiatives, leveraging technology and data analytics to enhance hiring efficiency and candidate experience
  • Manage relationships with external recruitment partners, search firms, RPOs, and staffing vendors to support business hiring demands
  • Deliver recruitment dashboards and analytics to leadership, providing insights on hiring progress, budget adherence, ramp-up status, and market trends
  • Ensure a seamless candidate experience from sourcing through onboarding, with a strong focus on offer acceptance, joining conversion, and new hire integration
  • Drive employer branding, employee referral, social recruiting, and talent attraction initiatives to strengthen the organization's employer value proposition

Required Experience & Skills

  • 8-12 years of Talent Acquisition experience within GBS, GCC, Shared Services, Captive Centers, or multinational organizations
  • Minimum 2-4 years of experience leading and managing recruitment teams
  • Proven track record of delivering high-volume hiring, rapid ramp-ups, and campus hiring programs
  • Strong experience in headcount budgeting, workforce planning, and recruitment analytics
  • Demonstrated success in optimizing cost-per-hire and driving recruitment operational excellence
  • Experience managing global stakeholders and influencing senior business leaders across multiple geographies
  • Strong leadership, stakeholder management, vendor management, and project management capabilities
  • Proficiency with ATS and Workday
  • MBA/PGDM in Human Resources
