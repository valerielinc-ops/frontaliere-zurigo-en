Full description

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a specialist supervisor (FaBe) for 1:1 accompaniment of a middle school student in the ISR setting.

The assignment includes 16-17 weekly lessons (about 38%) and is spread over 3-4 days of the week during school hours (certainly Tuesday, Thursday and Friday, possibly still Monday). No use in school holidays. A possible increase in the workload depends on further clarifications (e.g. additional care needs in the nursery).

Tasks

  • Support of the student in everyday school life in consultation with the school curative teacher and the class teachers
  • If necessary, separate promotion for individual lessons in 1:1 (even outside the classroom)
  • Promoting integration into the class community, taking into account individual needs

Profile

  • Completed training as a specialist
  • Experience working with middle school children is beneficial
  • High social skills and an appreciative and sensitive approach to children
  • Patience, resilience and the ability to act calmly and structured even in demanding situations
  • Teamwork and enjoyment of constructive and interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Flexibility (the child does not need constant intensive 1:1 support)
  • Very good oral knowledge of German (at least level C2)

We offer

  • The opportunity to individually accompany and promote a child with special support needs
  • A motivated and experienced teaching team
  • Guidance and support from an experienced school curative teacher
  • A centrally located workplace in a sympathetic school
  • permanent employment in accordance with attractive employment conditions of the City of Zurich, classification in wage level 6
  • Have we aroused your interest? We look forward to your application.

About us

The Zurich-Letzi school district comprises Stadtkreis 9 with the districts Albisrieden, Altstetten and Grünau. In the 16 schools of our rapidly growing school circle, more than 5,000 primary school students are taught and supervised. More than 1,200 employees work for them in our schools in the areas of teaching, support, home service and technology as well as administration. They all work daily for the interests of the students and a smooth school day.
