Institute of Art History

Job opening 01.03.2027, permanent

The Kunsthistorisches Institut of the University of Zurich is one of the largest in Switzerland with six chairs and two lecturers who represent the subject broadly and offers its employees a diverse and stimulating environment.

Tasks

Within the secretarial team, you support the management. In the secretarial area, you take on the following tasks:

  • Clarifications, research and editorial work for the management and for chair holders
  • Reporting Assistance (Academic Report)
  • Organisation of events of the Institute
  • Correspondence
  • Material order
  • Furniture order
  • Room Ordering and Management (Planon)
  • Establishment of workstations (telephony, key order)
  • Equipment order
  • Postal administration
  • Management and editing of the institute newsletter
  • Student administration with SAP Campus
  • Supervision of the institute website (Magnolia)
  • Social media

Profile

Businessman EFZ or comparable qualification Stylistic German in word and writing, good knowledge of English and French are an advantage. Experience with the Microsoft 365 product range (especially Word and Excel) as well as good Mac and SAP skills.
