Full description

We make habitats worth living.

Technology inspires you and you like to take responsibility? Then work with us to design challenging ventilation and climate projects from the idea to the successful implementation. Look forward to a lot of personal responsibility, a professional team and sustainable solutions for modern buildings.

Your field of activity

  • You lead and develop the field of building technology with an entrepreneurial eye and actively shape its future.
  • You control challenging building technology projects from the first idea to the successful implementation and keep an eye on quality, deadlines and profitability.
  • Together with our customers, you will develop sustainable and economically convincing solutions in the areas of HVAC.
  • You build and expand strong customer and partner relationships and maintain your network with builders, planners, architects, general contractors and other market partners.
  • You take on entrepreneurial responsibility for budget, results and the cost, deadline and quality goals of your area.
  • You develop your team professionally and personally, promote personal responsibility and create an environment in which people like to be successful together.

Your expertise

  • You will bring basic technical training in building technology, preferably in heating or sanitary facilities, and have received further training at the level of HF, FH or comparable.
  • You have profound experience in project or division management and have already successfully assumed leadership responsibility.
  • You think entrepreneurially, negotiate convincingly and meet customers and business partners confidently and at eye level.
  • You like to take on responsibility, think beyond your own area and find pragmatic and sustainable solutions together with your team.
