Full description

It's more than a job

When you work in Freight Logistics and Operations at Kuehne+Nagel, you play a key role in optimising processes and ensuring that inventory and shipments move efficiently. But that is not all. Your work also helps ensure that vital medicines reach patients on time and supports other essential deliveries, from wind turbines to semiconductors. At Kuehne+Nagel, our contribution counts in more ways than we imagine.

‎
Join our Sea Logistics team as graduate or working student !

How you create impact

  • Support the coordination and handling of sea freight import and export shipments throughout the transport process.
  • Assist in arranging pre-carriage, on-carriage, and port-related activities in cooperation with internal teams and service providers.
  • Prepare, check, and maintain shipping and transport documentation, ensuring accuracy and completeness.
  • Communicate with shipping lines, carriers, customers, and business partners regarding bookings, shipment status, and operational requirements.
  • Monitor shipments, update relevant systems, and support the timely resolution of operational discrepancies.
  • Contribute to process improvements, data quality initiatives, and KPI monitoring to support operational excellence.

What we would like you to bring

  • Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's program, or recently graduated in Logistics, Supply Chain Management, International Business, or a related field.
  • Strong interest in international transportation, sea freight, and global logistics operations.
  • Good knowledge of MS Office applications, especially Excel; willingness to learn new systems and technologies.
  • Strong organizational skills with a structured and detail-oriented approach to work.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork skills, with a proactive and solution-oriented mindset.
  • Good command of English, both written and spoken; additional language skills in French, Italian or German are an advantage.

What's in it for you

  • Attractive compensation package
  • Modern office with excellent public transport connections and discounted parking
  • Diverse training and development opportunities within a global market leader
  • Numerous employee discounts in fashion, leisure, electronics, home & more
  • A supportive team environment with regular team and company events

Who we are

Logistics shapes everyday life - from the goods we consume to the healthcare we rely on. At Kuehne+Nagel, your work goes beyond logistics; it enables both ordinary and special moments in the lives of people around the world.

As a global leader with a strong heritage and a vision to move the world forward, we offer a safe, stable environment where your career can make a real difference. Whether we help deliver life-saving medicines, develop sustainable transportation solutions or support our local communities, your career will contribute to more than you can imagine.
