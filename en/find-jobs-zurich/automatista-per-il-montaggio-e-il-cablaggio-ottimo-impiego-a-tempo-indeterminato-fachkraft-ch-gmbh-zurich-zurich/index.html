Full description

You are responsible for the construction and wiring of demanding serial devices. The company attaches great importance to an accurate and fast working method in the team. For our client we are looking for one or more activities

  • Construction and wiring of our three main series equipment according to technical drawings
  • Adaptation and assembly of special and individual products
  • Independent testing of assemblies and equipment for completeness
  • Safe working according to internal guidelines
  • Requirements
  • Apprenticeship as an automatic technician EFZ completed
  • Continuing education in the field of quality assurance / process management is advantageous
  • In-depth industry experience
  • Exact and forwarded working methods
  • Customer-friendly appearance
  • Joy and interest in the profession Offering a permanent position with a renowned client with interesting prospects. You can also expect an attractive salary and contemporary social benefits.
