Full description

USZ Stettbach

By agreement

You are the technical heart of our IAM solution and take on the role of lead engineer and architect for our central identity governance platform. Together with Product Owner and Team Coach, you will shape the technical future of our IAM landscape and ensure a sustainable, scalable and secure architecture. You look outside the box (Privileged Access Management, Partner IAM, Customer IAM) and ensure that all components merge into a secure and efficient overall system, while consistently focusing on standardization.

Your tasks

  • Lead for the OneIdentity architecture and engineering: You are responsible for the technical architecture within the OneIdentity suite (designer, manager, web designer and object browser). You implement complex business logics and approval workflows, but always prefer standard functionalities over complex customizing
  • IAM overall understanding and coordination: You understand the identity flows across all systems. You will advise product owners and specialist areas on the technical impact of requirements and ensure that architecture principles and IAM guidelines are taken into account in the solutions
  • Architecture functions (Tech-Lead): You coach the engineers on technical issues, promote knowledge transfer within the team, carry out architecture and code reviews (QS) and evaluate solution variants on their sustainability. In addition, you are responsible for the architectural documentation and plan to reduce the technical legacy loads
  • Technical contact for application integrations: You act as a technical counterpart to the IAM onboarding team and advise them on the connection of complex applications to the IAM system. You define the technical integration paths and ensure that new systems are connected according to the architectural specifications
  • Roadmap and further development: You define the roadmap for the further development of the OneIdentity platform towards a modern cloud identity architecture (Entra ID). You support the Product Owner in the technical evaluation and architecture planning of the further development of the OneIdentity platform and derive technical requirements and architecture decisions from this
  • Support for AD and Entra ID: You have in-depth knowledge in the hybrid Microsoft environment and support the team in the AD/Entra ID area in engineering and advise on the technical connection to the IAM
  • 3rd level Support and DevOps: You solve deep-seated problems (e.g. SQL errors, synchronization conflicts) and optimize processes sustainably to continuously increase the efficiency of IAM operations

Your profile

  • Training: Completed degree in computer science, training as a computer scientist or a comparable technical qualification
  • IAM expertise: Several years of professional experience in the administration and engineering of complex IAM systems and hybrid architectures (AD, Entra ID)
  • OneIdentity Deep Dive: In-depth knowledge in the OneIdentity Manager (or a comparable IGA/IAM tool). You understand the data model and can technically map business logic
  • Technical overall understanding: In-depth knowledge in the Microsoft environment (Active Directory, Entra ID and M365) as well as very good SQL and PowerShell knowledge. You understand technical connections across system boundaries, support Microsoft specialists at eye level and are able to read, analyze and adapt scripts and business logics in the OneIdentity environment (e.g. VB.NET/C#) if necessary.
  • Methodological leadership: Experience in the methodological leadership of (external) partners or engineers in complex IAM implementations without direct line responsibility
  • Plus Infrastructure as Code (IaC): Ideally, you will have experience with IaC concepts (e.g. git pipelines) to automate and professionalize the deployment and configuration of the IAM system landscape.
  • Architecture Mindset: In-depth knowledge of role and rights management and access governance. You can technically justify technological decisions and represent them to various stakeholders
  • Languages: Very good knowledge of German and English

Our offer

With us, you work in an agile SAFe environment and take on a central technical role in our IAM team. Together with Product Owner and Team Coach, you shape the future of our identity platform. You get a lot of freedom for technical decisions, bring your own ideas and shape the architecture of a strategically important platform for the USZ. Flexible working hours and the opportunity to work in the home office help you to combine work and private life well. For this post, dossiers from recruiters cannot be considered.
