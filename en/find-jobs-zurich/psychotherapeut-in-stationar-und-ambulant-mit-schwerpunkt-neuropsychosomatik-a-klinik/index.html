Full description

Tasks

  • Assessment and psychological care of inpatients, mostly with epilepsy or dissociative seizures
  • Clinical psychological assessments and outpatient psychotherapeutic treatments with a focus on neuropsychosomatics with adult patients
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation within the clinic
  • Case documentation, reporting and case-related exchange with allocators, insurance companies and other specialist bodies
  • Psychotherapeutic treatment of patients with cognitive impairment or supervision for their environment

Requirements

  • Completed psychology studies and completed or advanced psychotherapy training
  • Interest in neuropsychosomatic/somatic patients
  • Ability to work independently, high social skills, resilience and teamwork

Klinik Lengg AG in Zurich is the centre of excellence for epileptology and neurological rehabilitation.

It is our aim to provide patients with neurological diseases with the widest possible rehabilitation and to ensure an accurate diagnosis and sustainable treatment of patients with epilepsy. Around 300 employees are committed to humanely, competently, innovatively and reliably ensuring that our patients regain the greatest possible independence - and thus their quality of life. Become part of one of our interdisciplinary and interprofessional teams.
