Full description

We deliver! Migros Logistics brings together Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (MVB) and Migros Logistique Romandie SA (MLR) to supply Switzerland every day. With modern technology and highly automated processes, we shape the logistics of tomorrow. Start with us into the future of logistics as Head of Disposition, Order Management and Network Planning MTM at 80 - 100%.

Mansioni

  • You are responsible for the centralised, Switzerland-wide distribution of exchange equipment (cargo carriers such as pallets and containers) for the entire Migros Group and its suppliers.
  • Your area of responsibility includes complete network and process responsibility with over 1,000 partner companies in rail and road transport.
  • You are responsible for the main responsibility of external warehouses and other service companies.
  • You are responsible for the defined KPI’s incl. Cost responsibility.

Requirements

  • Professional experience of several years: Logistics
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): in the commercial or technical sector with business education
  • Drive for optimizations in Migros logistics (info: www.tauschgeraete.ch).
  • Flair for customer/partner orientation and enjoyment of working with a large number of companies (stakeholder management)
  • Driving licence of category B incl. Readiness for sporadic travel in Switzerland
  • German (flowing)
  • French (good knowledge)
  • English (advantageous)
