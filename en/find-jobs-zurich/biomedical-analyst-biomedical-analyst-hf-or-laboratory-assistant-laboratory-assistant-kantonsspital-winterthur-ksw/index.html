For our modernly established, accredited histology laboratory (focus on conventional histology and immunohistochemistry) at the Institute of Pathology we are looking for 1. September 2026 or by agreement a flexible and resilient personality as biomedical analyst / biomedical analyst HF or laboratory assistant / laboratory assistant.

Tasks

  • Working according to ISO standard 15189

Profile
