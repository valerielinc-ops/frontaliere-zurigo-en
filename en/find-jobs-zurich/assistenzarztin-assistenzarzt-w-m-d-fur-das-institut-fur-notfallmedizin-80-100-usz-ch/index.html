About the USZ

Tasks

At our Institute for Emergency Medicine, you provide and support all emergency patients with the most diverse problems in close cooperation with the corresponding USZ specialists. The workspaces include the emergency core zone, the fast track and the shock room. During the 24-hour shift operation, supervision is continuously guaranteed by our cadre doctors from the Institute of Emergency Medicine.

Requirements

  • You have at least 2-3 years of clinical experience, preferably at least. six months of experience in a surgical discipline
  • They are team-oriented and motivated to work successfully in a lively and demanding environment and to contribute to the further development of the institute.
  • If you are not a native speaker, a C1 language certificate is required according to the Common European Framework of Reference.

We offer

We offer a 6-18 month employment at our recognized Category 1 clinical emergency medicine facility SGNOR. The Institute of Emergency Medicine also has the recognition for General Internal Medicine category IV 12 months. We also offer a diverse training programme and training offer internally.

We provide you with a versatile, demanding and varied activity in the exciting environment of emergency medicine in a university-innovative environment. At USZ, you can consolidate and expand your skills in interdisciplinary care including emergency-relevant ultrasound methods.

Part-time employment is possible and fits perfectly into our system of modern working models.

A motivated and committed team will actively support you in your function and all matters.
