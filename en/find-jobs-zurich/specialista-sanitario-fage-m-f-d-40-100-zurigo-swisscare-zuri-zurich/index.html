Role overview

SwissCare Züri is a Spitex organization in the city of Zurich and a member of the Spitex Association of the Canton of Zurich. We work according to the principle of reference care. You manage a fixed circle of clients instead of having new names on the tour plan every day. For our team we are looking for a professional health FaGe (m/f/d), Pensum 40-100%. We are around 15 people, mostly planning early services and the travel time between assignments counts as working time. Health specialist FaGe (m/f/d), 40–100%, Zurich

What we offer

  • Work-life balance is important to us. We usually plan early service and keep weekend assignments as low as possible. What suits your situation, we look at together.
  • Reference care instead of daily changing tours. You know your clients and they know you.
  • We are open to improvement so that your ideas arrive. If you notice that something could go better or you have suggestions for improvement, say it and discuss it together in a timely manner and implement it, without committees, without waiting half a year. So we are constantly improving and growing as a team, together!
  • The travel time from client to client counts as working time and is paid
  • Expenses according to expense regulations, official vehicles available
  • We financially support further education and post-qualifications
  • Digital documentation and time recording via app, no paper economy
  • An assignment planning that works in everyday life
  • Plannable service planning min. 1 month in advance so you can plan your private life without further ado
