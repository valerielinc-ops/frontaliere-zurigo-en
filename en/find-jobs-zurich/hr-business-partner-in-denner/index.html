Full description

Place of work: Denner AG, headquarters, Grubenstrasse 10, 8045 Zurich

Holistic HR care is in your blood? With managers, do you like to be in active dialogue around operational HR topics? In short, employees are close to your heart? Then, as an HR Business Partner in the Denner administration, you are in the right place.

Luogo di lavoro: Denner AG, headquarters, Grubenstrasse 10, 8045 Zurich

Important information

In this function, you mainly manage the area of IT.

Mansioni

  • Supervisors in the assigned area advise and supervise on an equal footing in all personnel and management issues
  • Act as a contact person for employees for all HR topics
  • Implementing the Denner HR strategy and coordinating recurring processes independently
  • All HR activities along the entire Employee Life Cycle with the support of a personal assistant
  • Working in HR projects and promoting cross-sectoral exchange

Requirements

  • At least 3 years of professional experience: in a demanding HR support function
  • Completed basic or further education: further education as HR specialist*man
  • Sound track record in HR support as well as very good employment law knowledge
  • You are familiar with the challenges of a large company
  • Solution-oriented, holistic and cross-process thinking and action
  • Thanks to your high social skills, you convince our employees and managers
  • in German (negotiation certain)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central place of work: In the casual Binz district in Zurich district 3 (2 minutes by foot from Zurich station Binz).
  • Flexible place of work / home office: One to two days a week working at home. Except in IT - we are even more flexible.
  • Fair wages: 13 monthly wages and fixed wage increases for all instead of bonuses for a few.
  • Food: No problem. Many restaurants and takeaways around the headquarters attract with preferential prices.
  • Holidays: 6 weeks holidays for all and even more days for service anniversaries.
  • Personal responsibility & freedom: plenty of room for your ideas and to make a difference together with us.
  • Design and decision-making leeway: opportunity to help shape Denner’s success story and implement your own ideas
  • Individual training: internal training and financial support for meaningful external training and further education.

Contatto

Application and contact Selina Haak Head of Talent Acquisition

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Personality/performance test: Under certain conditions, we use a personality test (BIP, Scales test, etc.) as an additional step to support selection. The results of the test may be discussed personally and form the basis for further selection. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Final discussion before issuing the contract: Before the contract is issued, we hold a final discussion in order to clarify open questions of both sides and to regulate contractual details. If all points are clarified, the written offer follows. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition: After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
