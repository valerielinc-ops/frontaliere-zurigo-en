Full description

We focus on women's health. In a dynamic and interprofessional environment, we ensure comprehensive gynecological care. In the ambulatory setting we work during the day between Monday and Friday.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • You receive and accompany patients during the office appointment and assist in various examinations. You carry out blood tests, urine analyses and injections and support patient education. In the disposition, you coordinate appointments with patients and order findings from other clinics. • As a nurse, you have already gained experience in an acute hospital. Professional experience in gynecology is desirable, but not mandatory. • You have a stylish German and good English skills. Further knowledge of foreign languages is an advantage. • We attach great importance to a careful introduction to the field. In addition, you have a broad, internal training offer at your disposal. • We are a young, motivated 20-strong team and accompany four MPA learners into the future. Even in difficult situations, we support each other. Only together will we make progress. We look forward to your application.
