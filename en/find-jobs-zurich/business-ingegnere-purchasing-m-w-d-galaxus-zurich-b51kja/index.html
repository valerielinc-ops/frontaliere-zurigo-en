Full description

As a Purchasing Business Engineer, you act as the central interface between Business, Product Development (IT) and the warehouse. Together with the relevant stakeholders, you will develop clever tools, systems and processes to further optimize automated or strategic purchasing.

Mansioni

  • You take on business requirements for optimization, innovation and development around strategic purchasing, question them, structure and cluster them into individual projects
  • You will carry out evaluations, researches and concept analyses regarding purchasing and provide recommendations for further developments and the use of existing tools.
  • You drive the purchasing roadmap and innovation projects from the business side and prioritize them in coordination with the Head of Platform and the Product Owner
  • Together with Data Scientists, you will develop reporting to facilitate easy monitoring and controlling of the entire purchasing process
  • By optimizing the working methods and tools available in purchasing, you will help reduce financial storage risk, use storage capacities efficiently and increase the overall profitability of the value chain.
  • You ensure the interface between engineering teams and category management in the subject area you are responsible for
  • You will train our Category Business Managers, provide support with questions and provide complete know-how documentation
  • You ensure stakeholder management and the involvement of eligible stakeholders and convince through clear communication and expertise in your field
  • You promote the exchange of knowledge among business engineers beyond your own areas of responsibility

Requirements

  • More than 5 years of professional experience: as a business engineer, product owner, e-commerce category manager or in similar positions
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): in business informatics, business administration or similar discipline
  • You have a fast comprehension, very strong analytical skills and work intensively with tools such as Excel, AI or Tableau. SQL experiences are of great advantage
  • You have profound experience in the successful management of agile projects, from conception to implementation
  • It is easy for you to abstract complexity, bring different stakeholders and opinions together, win for a common goal and focus on implementing it.
  • You have the willingness to question “as it has always been done” and propose more efficient, automated or effective solutions
  • You are accessible and have an efficient and clear form of communication
  • In changing times, you are resilient and tackle change positively
  • You use your creativity and initiative to solve problems and achieve goals
  • You are inspired by your enthusiasm, curiosity and cooperative nature
  • Self-responsible, structured and reliable work in an ambitious, energetic team is fun for you, because we are not lone fighters
  • German (flowing)
  • English (flowing)
