Full description

For our region of Zurich, the unit "New Construction - Special Projects" within the division "Buildings", we are looking for reinforcement in the project management. Your place of work will be our headquarters «Connect» in Opfikon.

In our new construction unit special projects we realize challenging industrial and large-scale construction projects in the Zurich region. The projects operate in a complex environment with high technical requirements, demanding approval procedures and diverse interfaces to authorities, planners and building owners.

Do you have experience in managing new construction projects and are enthusiastic about demanding industrial and special construction projects? We look forward to your application.

Tasks

Which tasks await you as Project Manager GU/TU Special Projects (m/f/d)?

  • Responsibility for the economic achievement of new construction projects in the Zurich region – from the start of implementation to the turnkey handover – in compliance with quality, deadlines and costs
  • Responsible operational project management taking into account all safety, timing and qualitative requirements
  • Active management of subcontractors, suppliers and specialist planners
  • Leadership of the execution team, composed of construction managers and other specialists
  • Main contact for builders, business partners and authorities incl. Ensuring transparent communication and seamless documentation

Requirements

What requirements do you bring as project manager GU/TU Special Projects (m/f/d)?

  • Completed basic technical training with further training in construction or project management (e.g. technician:in HF, construction manager:in, project manager:in) or completed studies in architecture, civil engineering or a similar specialisation (FH/ETH/University)
  • In-depth practical experience in construction management as well as 2 to 5 years of experience in the management and control of demanding new construction projects in the TU/GU or construction management environment
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, assertiveness and strong social skills
  • Experience as a general contractor, preferably in an international environment
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing

About Implenia

As a leading Swiss construction and real estate service provider, Implenia develops, realizes and manages living spaces, working environments and infrastructure for future generations in Switzerland and Germany. Implenia also offers tunnelling and related infrastructure projects in other markets. The company combines the know-how of highly qualified consulting, development, planning and execution units under one roof into an integrated, multinationally leading construction and real estate service provider. Headquartered in Opfikon near Zurich, Implenia employs more than 8,000 people (FTE) across Europe and achieved a turnover of CHF 3.5 billion in 2025. More information at implenia.com.
