Full description

With your expertise, you put our utility line projects on paper and thereby create an important foundation for their successful implementation.

Tasks

  • In preparing the project and execution documents for our utility line projects, you play an important role and support both internal and external customers.
  • You create standards-compliant and consistent utility line documentation for all network levels on time – including route and profile plans with third-party utility lines.
  • You carefully prepare overview plans, network schematics (as-is/to-be), ESTI submissions and cable lists and keep them up to date.
  • You assign new object and feeder numbers across all network levels and create material requirement lists as a basis for cost estimation.
  • In project planning, you contribute your specialist knowledge and actively support the development of suitable technical solutions.

Profile

  • You have an EFZ qualification as a drafter specializing in civil engineering, an electrical drafter or a grid electrician. Experience in utility network construction is a plus.
  • Experience at an electricity utility is an advantage and will help you quickly find your way in your new area of responsibility.
  • You work independently, accurately and reliably and impress with good professional, methodological and social skills.
  • You are familiar with SAP and MS Office and are confident working in digital work environments.
  • Very good spoken and written German skills round out your profile and enable you to communicate clearly and professionally.
