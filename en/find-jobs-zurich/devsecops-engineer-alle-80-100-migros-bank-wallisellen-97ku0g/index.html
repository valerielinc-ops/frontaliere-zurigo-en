Full description

With us, you can be an IT trendsetter and build innovative banking solutions of the future. We are looking for you: A personality who never loses sight of the essentials when immersed in codes and concepts for applications and processes that serve our customers and ourselves. Come on.

Mansioni

  • You will actively work in an agile multidisciplinary team and continuously develop our digital services and platforms. You always keep the customer’s view in focus.
  • With your DevSecOps mindset, you identify and evaluate the automation potential at the integration level. You will continuously inform yourself about the current developments in the market.
  • You design and create declarative source code for infrastructure, configuration and monitoring, and integrate our security mechanisms into the CI/CD pipelines.
  • You manage the entire life cycle of the applications, design the architecture and define the requirements for the implementation of the projects in cooperation with our development partners.
  • You are the link between the DevSecOps and the software engineering community and you are involved in architecture and governance issues. You advise the departments on technical topics.
  • You support the developers with your focus on "Secure by Design" in the design and maintenance of the applications.

Requirements

  • 3-5 years of relevant professional experience: Full-Stack Software Engineering
  • Knowledge and practical experience in dealing with Gitlab and container technology / OpenShift, as well as CI/CD pipeline, and / or artifact store and / or secret vault technologies
  • Knowledge and coaching of stakeholders in the field of cloud native development and principles of automated software quality assurance
  • Knowledge of IT security (vulnerability management, network policies, shift left security).
  • You have excellent analytical skills, recognize connections and can also tackle complex topics in a structured manner.
  • You understand technical connections and can discuss them at eye level with our software development partners.
  • Bachelor's degree (university, university of applied sciences)
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • English (negotiable)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Flexible working hours: annual working hours for all and trusted working hours for many
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Cumulus points: Up to 5 additional points per franc purchase value
  • Social benefits: Optimal coverage for sickness and accident "Private"
  • Individual training: Attractive contributions for external training
