Full description

As a Global Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) Intern you will join our Global Retail team in Kilchberg. Global Retail passionately translates the LINDT brand equity into a distinctive omni-channel experience, bringing chocolate smiles to our customers. As part of our team you will help improve customer experience and shape marketing related activities for our >500 Lindt Shops and 21 Online Shops worldwide.

YOUR MAIN RESPONSIBILITIES WILL INCLUDE

  • Identify best practice key activities to build traffic, drive conversion & repeat purchase for our Lindt Shops globally.
  • Work closely together with our subsidiaries to improve customer experience in both our Lindt physical stores and online shops.
  • Enable the local implementation of our global portfolio strategy for D2C.
  • Gain experience in data analysis, reporting and creating impactful presentations.
  • Responsible for the set‑up and distribution of various recurring reports.
  • Support on the organization of global business gatherings.
  • Take responsibility for your own small projects.

YOUR PROFILE

  • Completed or advanced Bachelor’s or Master's degree in business administration / related subjects.
  • First working experience in the FMCG / retail / e-commerce sector is an advantage.
  • Entrepreneurial mindset with a hands‑on, proactive, and communicative approach.
  • Independent, precise and conscientious way of working with self-initiative.
  • Very strong analytical and structured thinking skills.
  • Very good MS Office skills (PowerPoint and Excel).
  • Fluent in English
  • German at least B1
  • French and other languages are a plus.
