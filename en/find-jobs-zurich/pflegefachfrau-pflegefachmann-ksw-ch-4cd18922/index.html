Full description

In addition to special consultation hours (hepatology, chronic inflammatory intestinal diseases, functional disorders), around 7000 diagnostic and therapeutic endoscopies as well as over 4000 ultrasound examinations including punctures are carried out annually. Our department is responsible for the gastroenterological examinations of outpatient and inpatient patients, whereby in addition to the usual diagnostic examinations, ERCP and endoscopic ultrasound examinations, including endosonographic punctures and advanced interventions, are maintained. To complement our team, we are looking for a Dipl immediately or by agreement. Endoscopy nurse.

Tasks

  • Your task is to prepare and carry out the examination assistance, the sedation during the procedure, the follow-up of the workplace and the assistance in the preparation of the endoscopes
  • You monitor the outpatients before, during and after the examinations
  • You like to work at regular working hours, but you are also ready for regular late-term and piketting services.
