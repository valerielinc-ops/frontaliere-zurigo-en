The Institute of Anesthesiology and Perioperative Medicine (IFA) of the University Hospital Zurich (USZ) provides more than 30,000 services annually in outpatient and inpatient anesthesiology, intraclinical and extraclinical emergency medicine as well as in acute and chronic pain medicine.

In the context of highly specialized medicine, the USZ has service contracts for all transplantations as well as for the treatment of severely burnt and multiple injured patients.
