Full description

In the endoscopy center, we perform both diagnostic and complex interventional procedures for ambulatory and inpatient patients. For this dynamic working environment, we are building a new supply model. That is why we are looking for a committed person as a specialist / expert MPA who will be involved in developing and actively shaping this new area of responsibility right from the start.
If you enjoy rethinking processes, taking responsibility and actively driving change, we look forward to getting to know you.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • Coordination of patient information for diagnostic and interventional interventions in close cooperation with the medical service • Organization and provision of all relevant information for patients: before examinations and interventions • Coordination of internal appointments and procedures with different interfaces, for example for interventions involving anesthesia • Central contact person for internal professional groups, patients and assignees for organizational questions about planned interventions • Cooperation in the development, implementation and evaluation of processes, standards and operational documents • Active participation in the new care model and assuming other tasks that will be jointly developed – also in the area of clinical patient care Requirements • Completed training as a medical practice assistant / medical practice assistant with professional experience in an acute hospital or a larger practice • Completed further training as medical practice coordinator / medical practice coordinator (MPK, clinical direction) or willingness to complete this or a comparable further training • Independent, structured and reliable working methods and strong organisational capacity • Engaged, initiative and enthusiastic personality with pleasure in developing new things and actively shaping changes • Enjoying interprofessional cooperation and a patient-oriented way of working We offer: You can expect a varied and responsible activity in a highly specialized university environment that promotes innovation, knowledge and further development. You benefit from attractive employment conditions with regular working hours from Monday to Friday, at least 25 holiday days and additional free days depending on your age. We support your professional and personal development with a wide range of internal and external training opportunities. At the same time, you get the chance to actively shape a new care model and contribute your own ideas. And thanks to our air-conditioned workspaces in the endoscopy center, you will also work in a pleasant atmosphere on warm days. Did we get your interest?
