About the role

Job Summary Acts as primary factory contact for Sales and Customer Service on all matters related to new Spare part & Accessory orders. Responsible for determining and communicating lead-times for new orders with the goal of ensuring they will be on time. Overall Spare part & Accessory Backlog management will be another vital task in ensuring orders continuously go out on time with competitive lead times while minimizing overdues. Essential Functions

  • Responsible for following Sulzer's program.
  • Provides accurate lead times for new Spare Part & Accessory orders.
  • Create and maintain a Master Backlog Schedule for the Easley Factory with the intent of ensuring orders go out on time and overdues are minimized.
  • Maintains a 6-week rolling plan with a clear display of how each order is tracking within that timeframe.
  • Able to collaborate in a cross functional setting to obtain information in a timely and organized manner.
  • Organized and able to display daily/weekly clear updates to the Planning Manager.
  • Proactively communicates with Sales in matters related to upcoming orders.
  • Acts as backup to coworkers in the Planning Department.
