Career Starter Technique et Gestion Rail 80-100% - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Career Starter Technical and Rail Management Zurich, Bern, Renens, 01.04.2027, 80-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

SBBs are the backbone of the Swiss transport network. Within the Infrastructure Division, the Availability and Maintenance sector ensures the safe, reliable and sustainable operation of railway facilities. As a Career Starter, depending on your training and interests, you assume a technical or strategic and conceptual role as a project leader. The management of resources, monitoring and maintenance of rail infrastructure and its continued development are at the heart of your business. You participate in technical projects, develop decision-making bases for decision-making bodies and use your technical and/or management skills in a targeted manner. The 18-month program begins on April 1, 2027. Thanks to an indefinite contract, you can find many avenues at the end of the program, whether it is a path of expertise, project or management. A completed degree is required to allow you to start fully from the start. Career Starter Program: Rail Engineering and Management What you can move.

  • From the outset, you contribute to projects close to the ground and you are actively involved in areas such as monitoring, maintenance or renewal of railway infrastructure.
  • Depending on your interests, you focus either on technical topics or on strategic and conceptual missions.
  • You analyze infrastructure projects, identify potential improvements and, if necessary, also get direct lighting on the construction sites.
  • In collaboration with different fields and stakeholders, you develop a valuable network within the SBB.
  • You are attached to the Availability and Maintenance area and perform three six-month assignments that you define in collaboration with the program team.
  • Your development is at the forefront. You benefit from individual development and continuing training measures aligned with your objectives.

What you bring.

  • A good technical or managerial understanding and interest in complex, technical and economic issues.
  • Interest in the railway system, especially in infrastructure, railway technology and control.
  • Strong analytical capabilities to understand linkages and identify avenues for improvement.
  • A high degree of autonomy, conceptual thinking and motivation to advance projects under your own responsibility.
  • A degree completed from an ES, HES or university, for example in engineering, systems engineering, electrical engineering or related fields.
  • An excellent command of German, and ideally a good knowledge of the other language.

Your salary and benefits.
