  • Process Engineering & R&D Support: Design, develop, optimize, and document nanofabrication processes.
  • Support cutting-edge academic and industrial R&D projects through process evaluation, troubleshooting, and statistical process control.
  • Equipment Ownership & Maintenance: Take technical ownership of selected semiconductor cleanroom systems including deposition (e.g. atomic layer deposition), characterization, and wet-processing.
  • Coordinate preventive maintenance, repairs, upgrades, vendor interactions, and the evaluation, commissioning, and installation of new equipment.
  • Safety Ownership: Act as a key point of contact for safety within the BRNC and contribute to the development and continuous improvement of its safety protocols.
  • Assume hands-on and administrative responsibilities related to chemicals, gases, safety equipment, and cleanroom operations.
  • User Training & Knowledge Exchange: Train and support researchers, advising on tool usage, process development and optimization.
