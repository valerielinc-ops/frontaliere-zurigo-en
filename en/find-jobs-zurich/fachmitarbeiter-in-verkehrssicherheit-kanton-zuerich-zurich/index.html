You are burning for traffic safety and like to work at the interface between technology, data and practice?

  • We offer you an exciting and responsible position in the department of traffic safety of the civil engineering office. We are working with project managers, road regions and the cantonal police to design secure canton roads. Your
  • tasks With us you can participate in the implementation of the infrastructure safety instruments (ISSI) on the cantonal roads. You carry out road safety audits (RSA) and road safety inspection of the measures and contribute to the monitoring of the effects of the measures. In the Veloexpress programme you accompany rapid safety measures in the event of bicycle-cluster accidents from analysis to implementation. You advise project leaders and road regions, answer questions from the administration and the public and support administrative ISSI training. Your profile It is important to us that you find yourself on the ground with information and fact-based and communicate with different stakeholders at eye level. You are delighted to be able to improve traffic safety with the application of the infrastructure safety instruments. You have a training as a civil engineer, traffic planner or geomatist (ETH, university or FH), are familiar with the use of the giro and always open to new methods. Ideally, you are already trained as an auditor for road safety audits (RSA) or ready to get trained accordingly. This is what you expect you to do because we offer you a sinister activity with a high degree of design flexibility. We are a well-established team of experts and you are helping to ensure that fewer people are injured on the canton roads.
