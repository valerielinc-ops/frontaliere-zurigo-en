Do you want to work in a governance role that actively promotes topics, shapes themes and works closely with the specialist areas?

In this role, you are part of the Life Business Unit in the area of governance at Zurich Switzerland. Together as a team, you ensure that our life insurance products and processes meet regulatory requirements – while remaining customer-oriented and efficient. In close exchange with colleagues from individual lives, you keep an overview of current developments. With your precise way of working, you create transparency and develop pragmatic processes that really work in everyday life.

This is what awaits you

  • You take the lead in cross-functional regulatory adjustments and in the further development of guidelines in individual life – from the interpretation of the guidelines, design of the adjustments to the effectively implemented control
  • You coordinate audits and audits across different teams: from preparation and support to providing evidence to tracking action
  • Identify risks at an early stage, assess and quantify them and ensure the definition, implementation and effectiveness testing of appropriate measures
  • You will be responsible for the overall overview of product governance in individual life, including the associated conditions and will continue to develop the governance framework.
  • You work closely with pricing, product management and the relevant second-line functions and create a clear, constructive and solution-oriented cooperation

This sets you apart

  • Completion of a university or university of applied sciences – or equivalent insurance-specific training
  • Experience in an actuarial function, in risk and control management or in auditing
  • Strong analytical and conceptual thinking as well as the ability to explain complex issues easily and appropriately
  • Experience in managing, coordinating and successfully implementing topics across all areas
  • Very good German and good English skills in word and writing
