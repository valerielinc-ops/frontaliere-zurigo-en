Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Secondary School and Vocational Education Office HR Business Partner /in 60 - 80% EMPATHISCH AND SERVICE ORIGINATED The Secondary School and Vocational Education Office is the competence and service center for vocational training, high schools, higher vocational training and further education in the Canton of Zurich. We support and supervise the secondary schools, the vocational colleges, the providers of vocational preparation years and the teaching companies. For our team, we are looking for an experienced and committed personality who convinces as a maternity representative with consulting competence, pronounced closeness to the company and pleasure in the operational HR work. They support the implementation of the personnel development strategy to ensure that employees can optimally develop and develop their potential.
