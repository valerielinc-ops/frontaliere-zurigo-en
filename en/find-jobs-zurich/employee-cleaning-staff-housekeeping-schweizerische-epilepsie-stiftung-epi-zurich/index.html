Full description

The Swiss Epilepsy Foundation (EPI) is a multi-sector company. It includes the EPI WohnWerk, the Lengg High School, the donation Dapples, a seminar center with restaurant and the EPI Central Services. In addition, the Swiss Epilepsy Foundation together with another foundation forms the sponsorship of Klinik Lengg AG, each with a clinic for epileptology and neurorehabilitation. Around 1000 employees are committed to these social tasks. The area is located on the outskirts of Zurich, with a fantastic view of the nearby Lake Zurich and the Alps.

Tasks

  • They are responsible for the cleaning of the rooms and buildings entrusted to them from Monday to Sunday in various services between 6:15 a.m. and 5:54 p.m., according to internal guidelines and guidelines, and thus contribute significantly to a good image of the company.
  • You maintain a correct handling of our cleaning machines and equipment and use the existing cleaning and aids correctly
  • You independently forward repair messages to the superior
  • Assistance in the introduction of new employees rounds off the task area

Requirements

  • You have several years of experience in professional cleaning, preferably in health care
  • They are physically fit and resilient, work independently and can inspire with their reliability, flexibility and service orientation
  • Due to their good knowledge of German, they can easily communicate with various contacts

We offer

  • Annual holiday planning, fixed working hours and a bi-monthly service planning with regulated free weekends so that you can also reconcile family and work well
  • Good accessibility by public transport (bus stop outside the door)
  • A lively and appreciative environment in which you can contribute your own ideas
  • 5 weeks holiday, from the 50th. Age 6 weeks
  • Own pension fund with above average employer share of 60%
  • Extensive internal training offer and support for external training
  • Staff apartments, parking spaces and perks in the EPI Park restaurant, pharmacy and half-tax or general subscription
  • In the Swiss Epilepsy Foundation, you will meet different professional and stakeholders who enrich your work every day.

Cleaning The 35-strong intercultural team, which belongs to the hotel industry and infrastructure, ensures order and cleanliness in various buildings throughout the EPI area from the basement to the roof. With their competent and, in the truest sense of the word, "clean" work, the team makes a significant contribution to making both residents, patients, customers and our guests and employees feel at home.
