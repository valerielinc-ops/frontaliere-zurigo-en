Full description

At the Wolfsblick Day School, a new place of learning and living has emerged where teaching, care and community are connected. Supervision of the secondary department is part of the day school Wolfsblick in Zurich Affoltern. Over noon, up to 280 young people are fed and cared for every day. The care facility is open to secondary school students from Monday to Friday (except Wednesday) from 11.50 to 1.30 pm.

We are looking for an expert care immediately or by agreement.

Tasks

  • You work in the 5 team of the department of secondary education. You support your work with the attitude of the New Authority.
  • They do the work on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday during about 8.30 a.m. to 2.30 p.m. in annual working hours.
  • During the school holidays you have free time (holiday and overtime compensation).
  • They are concerned about the concerns of care for the support of the students within the framework of the reference person system in the living space school.
  • They engage with projects or specialist inputs to support the students’ interdisciplinary skills.

Profile

  • Specialist supervision EFZ (for applications with a foreign diploma / certificate, a Swiss diploma recognition must be attached).
  • Engaged, reflected and resilient personality with a positive human image.
  • An agile way of working and very good team and communication skills.
  • Professional experience in youth work distinguishes their career.

We offer

  • Professional, reflective and self-effective activity
  • Competent support from the management Support and team vessels
  • Progressive salary and social benefits according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich (Horte)

About us

The Glattal School District in Zurich-Nord comprises 19 schools. We derive our pedagogical attitude from the concept of New Authority and thus meet the daily challenges in everyday school life together and effectively.

At our school you can expect an open team that supports each other. We have clear binding rules that are supported by everyone. High binding standards regarding respect and consideration for people, materials and the environment are part of our everyday life.
