HR Consulting Team Leader 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Make Switzerland move with us. HR Consulting Team Leader Zürich Altstetten / Work Smart, 01.11.2026 or to be agreed, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you. A varied and stimulating field of activity awaits you within a team recognized for its sense of service and its strong commitment. We advise managers, in person, digitally and by telephone, on all questions relating to the conditions of employment (CCT and CO) as well as on the application of the guidelines. As a member of our HR Consulting & Personnel Policy management team, you make a decisive contribution to the development and evolution of our field of activity. You are flexible and work regularly at our locations in Zurich and Bellinzona, as well as within our “Work Smart” flexible working model. To find out about social benefits, such as free GA and discounted travel abroad (also for the family), go to our website under Social benefits. What you can make move. - As HR Consulting team leader, you manage a team of 10 HR advisors at the Zurich Altstetten and Bellinzona sites. You are responsible for high-quality, professional advice for executives of the group's divisions and units, primarily in the regions of Zurich, Eastern Switzerland and Ticino. The emphasis is placed on questions of labor law and personnel management as well as support for reorganizations. - Together with your team, you ensure efficient work processes as well as the maintenance and continuous development of specialist knowledge. You ensure the quality of consulting services, optimal use of resources and continuously develop HR consulting and its processes.

  • You are close to executives and maintain regular exchanges with the line. In doing so, you quickly identify needs and developments and position HR as a competent and solution-oriented partner.
  • You manage HR projects or sub-projects and ensure the implementation, from an HR point of view, of reorganizations and organizational changes.
  • As a member of the HR Consulting & Personnel Policy management team, you actively contribute to the development of HR consulting. You contribute to guaranteeing a consistent and high-quality advisory service throughout Switzerland as well as to the development of the service portfolio. What you bring.
  • You have excellent consulting, communication and interpersonal skills and are able to convince orally and in writing.
  • You hold a university degree in business management or psychology, supplemented by continuing education in human resources (e.g. MAS, EMBA, HR manager or equivalent).
  • You have several years of experience in leading teams as well as solid professional experience in HR consulting, ideally within a large company.
  • You have excellent knowledge of labor law as well as experience in change management and project management.
  • German is your mother tongue. You also have a fluent command of Italian (level C1). Good knowledge of French is an asset. Active throughout Switzerland, you appreciate collaboration in a multilingual environment and know how to adapt your communication to your interlocutors with varied profiles.

Your salary and benefits.

We are actively committed to equal pay. This commitment occurs in accordance with the Code of Obligations, taking into account a comparison with the team, the market and the sector.
