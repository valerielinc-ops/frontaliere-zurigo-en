Full description

At our location in the Vogtsrain School House in Zurich Höngg we are looking for immediately or by arrangement for 6 - 12 months an intern 80 - 100%

Tasks

  • Practical and school accompaniment as well as support in the hygienic and social area of the students
  • Cooperation in extracurricular and holiday care and support for leisure activities and school events
  • Administrative support for teachers as well as preparation and follow-up of teaching
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation in the school and care team
  • Participation in meetings, school meetings and in-school training

Profile

  • Willingness to engage with students with special needs
  • Motivated, open, resilient personality with a positive human image
  • Independent working methods, great flexibility as well as very good team and communication skills
  • Students or lateral entrants are welcome
  • very good knowledge of German orally and in writing (min. C1), at least 20 years old

We offer

  • Self-employed and varied work in a professional team
  • Exciting interdisciplinary tasks in the special education sector
  • Possibility of active participation in our school
  • Conditions of employment in accordance with the requirements of the City of Zurich
  • Regular working hours

About us

The Heil Pädagogschule der Stadt Zürich is a special day school for 4 to 15-year-old children with a cognitive and/or multiple impairment. As part of the special education department, pupils who are taught in separate classes in different school buildings in the city of Zurich provide a supportive environment and place the focus of interdisciplinary promotion in their strengths.

Our teams at the various locations in the city of Zurich consist of curative school teachers, teachers, social pedagogues, specialists care, learners, assistants as well as speech pedagogues and psychomotor therapists.
