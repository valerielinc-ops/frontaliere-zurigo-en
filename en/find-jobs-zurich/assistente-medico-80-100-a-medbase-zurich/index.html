Welcome to Medbase Zurich Wiedikon

Our versatile, lively medical center (no walk-in practice) is located in urban district 3, a few minutes' walk from Wiedikon train station with a view of the Üetliberg. Here we care for patients in an interdisciplinary and quality-oriented manner. The team, consisting of around 40 employees, is made up of specialists and therapists from various specialist areas. The main focus of the center is on the interaction between family medicine and complementary medicine. At Medbase Zurich Wiedikon we value the respectful and helpful interaction within the team. We also pay attention to a good work-life balance. Part-time work is possible in all functions and working hours can be arranged flexibly.

As an MPA, you are the first point of contact for our patients and ensure a pleasant practice experience. Medbase offers you a dynamic team that values ​​quality and collegiality.

  • Reception and care of patients, including agenda planning and appointment coordination
  • Performing laboratory tests, EKGs and x-rays
  • Assistance with minor surgical procedures
  • Management and electronic billing of patient records
  • Ensuring smooth practice processes
  • Cooperate with the team on administrative tasks

What you bring with you

  • Training as a medical practice assistant EFZ
  • Willingness to continue training as a vocational trainer
  • Mandatory: X-ray authorization
  • Organizational skills and enjoyment of patient care
  • Empathy and flexibility
  • Teamwork and humor in challenging situations
  • Good knowledge of German (at least B2 level)

The Medbase Group is one of the leading companies in outpatient healthcare. This means services from health promotion to medication supply and acute treatment to rehabilitation.
