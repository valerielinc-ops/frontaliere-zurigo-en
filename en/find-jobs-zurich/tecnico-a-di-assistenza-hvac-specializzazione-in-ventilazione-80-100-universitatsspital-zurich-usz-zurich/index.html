You would like to contribute your technical skills to the ventilation department and are looking for a new challenge? As a service installer: at the University Hospital Zurich, you can expect a wide range of tasks around our ventilation systems. We look forward to meeting you.

Your main tasks

  • You ensure the security of supply and the trouble-free operation of the RLT systems
  • In your area of responsibility, you plan and organize all maintenance measures by priority and coordinate the deployments of external companies.
  • In day-to-day business, you take over the maintenance work and repair disturbances at the RLT plants
  • For renovation and renewal projects you bring your expertise and accompany the projects until commissioning and acceptance
  • You ensure compliance with applicable standards, legal regulations and the USZ internal guidelines, instructions and processes
  • You create the plant documentation and keep it up-to-date
  • After a thorough training, you also take on assignments in our pikett organization (workshop readiness and deployment service around the clock, seven days a week)

Your profile

  • You have completed vocational training in the field of HVAC. A further development, for example as a maintenance specialist, is advantageous
  • In plant maintenance, maintenance, energy optimization and building automation (MSRL) you will bring in-depth professional experience, ideally from the hospital environment or a comparable company with 24/7 operation.
  • You have good IT knowledge (MS Office, maintenance software) as well as good communication and organizational skills
  • Your way of working is proactive, self-responsible, goal-oriented and customer-oriented
  • You are resilient, enjoy working in a team and convince with high social skills as well as impeccable knowledge of German in word and writing
