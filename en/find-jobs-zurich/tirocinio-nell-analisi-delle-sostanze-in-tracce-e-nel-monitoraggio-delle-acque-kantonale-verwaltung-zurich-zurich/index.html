Full description

The Zurich waters and soils are habitat for a large number of plants and animals. They form the basis for a reliable supply of quality drinking water and serve as local recreational areas. The Office for Waste, Water, Energy and Air (AWEL) examines water and soil samples for pollutants and assesses their status. In the context of national and cantonal surface water and groundwater monitoring, water bodies are examined for organic trace substances such as drug residues and pesticides. Your

  • Tasks The focus is on sample preparation and instrumental analysis, such as the measurement and evaluation of water samples using state-of-the-art GC-APCI-MS/MS methods. In addition, the internship offers the opportunity to get an insight into other areas of AWEL. You have a completed Bachelor or Master in Chemistry, Environmental Sciences or similar and would like to get to know the field of environmental analysis better. A high level of motivation and interest in working in the laboratory, as well as an exact and careful way of working are a matter of course for you.
