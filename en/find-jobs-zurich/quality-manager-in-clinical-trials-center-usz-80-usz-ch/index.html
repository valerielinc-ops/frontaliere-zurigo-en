Role overview

The Clinical Trials Center (CTC) at the University Hospital Zurich is the central center of excellence for clinical research at the USZ.

We accompany and support study projects from academic and industrial research with the aim of ensuring the highest quality and safety standards - for participants, researchers and partners.

As part of a university centre of excellence, we work in an interdisciplinary, regulatoryly up-to-date manner and with a clear focus on scientific integrity. >The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland.
