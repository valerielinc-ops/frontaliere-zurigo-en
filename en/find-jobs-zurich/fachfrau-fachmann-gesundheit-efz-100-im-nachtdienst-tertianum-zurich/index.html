Full description

Your heart beats for older people? Is the nursing profession your calling? Do you see opportunities to use your skills and experience in a meaningful way in order to have a positive impact on the lives of our customers? Then you fit perfectly with us!

For the Tertianum Residenz Zürich Enge in Zurich we are looking for an

Professional / Health EFZ 100% on night duty

Their tasks

  • Holistic care and care of guests in the sense of our care mission statement and the care process
  • Assumption of tasks as a caregiver
  • Participation in the care process and in the care needs classification with RAI/BESA for the assigned guests
  • Electronic care documentation using CareCoach (mobile possible with CareCoach smartphone)
  • Planning and support of medical visits
  • Independent assumption of responsibility for the entire station during the night shift

Your profile

  • Completed training for/specialist health EFZ
  • Professional experience in long-term care is beneficial
  • RAI knowledge desired or willingness to attend these courses (paid)
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude
  • Enjoying multiple challenges
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
