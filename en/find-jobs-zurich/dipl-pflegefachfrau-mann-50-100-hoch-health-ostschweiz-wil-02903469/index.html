Dipl. Nursing Specialist 50-100%

Interdisciplinary oncology outpatient clinic

Place of work Wil

Job advertisement

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Independent care and support of outpatient oncology patients undergoing drug tumor therapy of varying complexity
  • Professional application of all antitumor therapies in accordance with applicable quality standards and guidelines
  • Accompanying medical consultations as well as advising and educating patients
  • Recording and complete documentation of the care services provided
  • Care, monitoring and support of patients and their relatives with pneumological, rheumatological and internal diseases

What you bring to this position

  • Completed training as a qualified nurse HF, preferably with experience in medical oncology
  • Higher specialized training in oncology or comparable qualification or willingness to complete HFP oncology nursing
  • High social skills and commitment to constructive interdisciplinary and interprofessional collaboration
  • Flexible, team-oriented and independent personality with high resilience
  • Responsible, service- and patient-oriented way of working

Our offer to you

  • Regulated working hours from Monday to Friday
  • Attractive working hours in daily operations between 7 a.m. and 5 p.m
  • Dedicated interdisciplinary team with appreciative collaboration
  • Family collaboration in a highly specialized, interdisciplinary environment
  • Wide range of professional and personal training and further education opportunities
