Full description

For the management of our K-Bar and Get & Give Take away team in Zurich, we are looking for a responsible service teamlead who is happy to be active in an international environment and will gladly take on the following tasks. Her place of work is Zurich Mythenquai and admission by appointment.

Their main tasks

  • Leadership and development of the service team (K‐Bar / Get&Give)
  • Implementation of the performance management process
  • Assignment and service planning including time recording and holiday planning
  • Recruitment, introduction and training of new employees
  • Model role in service and ensuring a first-class guest experience
  • Active management of sales and sales activities, bringing in new ideas
  • Ensuring a smooth daily routine throughout the service area
  • Active participation in day-to-day business and support of the team
  • Responsibility for quality, hygiene and safety standards
  • Correct cash management and settlement
  • Planning Organisation and implementation of events
  • Supply development together with production managers based on trends and guest needs
  • Co-responsibility for the budget and the creation of analyses and sales statistics for the Unit Head
  • Cooperation in the further development of the Unit Staff Gastronomy
  • They work from Monday to Friday between 7.00 and 21.00

Swiss Re Gastronomy – Your working environment Corporate Real Estate & Services Zurich (CRES Zurich) is a division of Swiss Re Management Ltd, which is responsible for managing the internal catering and hotel industry. In addition to the joint catering companies in Adliswil and Zurich, CRES Zurich runs the guest and management restaurant K2, the casual restaurant and the K-Bar in Zurich and the conference centre with hotel operation Centre for Global Dialogue (CGD) in Rüschlikon. The K-Bar with around 80 seats is the meeting point within the clubhouse and a place to relax and enjoy. In addition to the individual guest in the K-Bar I also like to have team events. Quick, uncomplicated and flexible employees can choose from various freshly prepared cold and hot dishes and drinks in our Take Away Outlet "Get & Give". Appropriate, healthy nutrition is provided in the Get & Give for "the fast break".

What you bring

  • Gastronomy or equivalent education completed
  • Sound and several years of professional experience in the service or in the well-kept bar area
  • Passion and enthusiasm for high service quality
  • Barista knowledge is beneficial
  • Broad expertise in wines and spirits
  • Very good oral English and German skills
  • A well-groomed appearance and good manners
  • Very good PC knowledge regarding Microsoft applications
  • Knowledge of the Gastronovi POS system is an advantage
