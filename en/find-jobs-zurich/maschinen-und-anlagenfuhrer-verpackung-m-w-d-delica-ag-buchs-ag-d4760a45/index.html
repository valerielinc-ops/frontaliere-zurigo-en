Full description

You make sure that the lines run, the quality is right and your team is proud. At Delica AG (former Chocolat Frey), you take responsibility for chocolate processing and bottling lines, develop further and celebrate success in the team. Appreciation, product pride and real development opportunities included.

Luogo di lavoro: Delica AG, Delica AG Buchs, Bresteneggstrasse 4, 5033 Buchs AG

Important information

Application mandatory with all employment certificates and diplomas (without letter of motivation)! We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You set up production and filling plants yourself, operate them and ensure process stability.
  • You carry out planned production orders independently and control format changes efficiently.
  • You control and document quality and process parameters seamlessly.
  • You carry out cleaning cycles according to specifications and contribute to hygiene safety.
  • Recognize maintenance needs, fix disruptions (1st level) and actively bring ideas to the KVP/Shopfloor.

Requirements

  • 3 - 5 years of professional experience: in food production, ideally chocolate/cocoa, baked goods, snacks, drinks or dairy
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): As a plant manager, food technologist or comparable qualification (e.g. baker, brewer, miller)
  • German (very good knowledge): Min. B1
  • Readiness for 2- to 4-shift work (weekend if necessary) and corresponding mobility
  • High quality, safety and hygiene awareness
  • You work structured and clean.
  • Technical understanding, problem-solving skills in disruption and team and communication strength

Cosa of Friamo

  • Meals: Balanced and fine lunch menu/buffet in the staff restaurant for only CHF 8.-
  • Professional provision: Compared to the BVG, depending on age, 7% - 18% more is paid by Delica
  • Mobile subscriptions: Top prices on mobile subscriptions at Galaxus Mobile, Sunrise or Wingo – starting from CHF 10.- / month
  • Cumulus points: 2 times Cumulus points on food and 5 times on non-food items
  • Migros Bank: Migros grants higher interest rates on the private and investment account for its employees
  • Sports and fitness offers: Free use of the fitness studios integrated at the site in Buchs, Birsfelden and Meilen as well as CHF 200.- Refund on fitness annual subscriptions
  • Club School Migros: Up to 70% refund per course
  • Health insurance: Up to 25% discount at various insurance partner companies (CSS, Sanitas, AXA, Generali and many more)

Contatto

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Reference information: When obtaining references, we ask for impressions, strengths and weaknesses again and have them verified by former supervisors or employees. References can be obtained after the first or second round and are only made with consent. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Test work: A trial day or half day gives the opportunity to get to know the future working environment and the team members better. And vice versa, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Final discussion before issuing the contract: Before the contract is issued, we hold a final discussion in order to clarify open questions of both sides and to regulate contractual details. If all points are clarified, the written offer follows. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
