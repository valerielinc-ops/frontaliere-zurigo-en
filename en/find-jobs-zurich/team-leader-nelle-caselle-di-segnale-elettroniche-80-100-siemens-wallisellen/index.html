Create a better #TomorrowWithUs Want to help shape the future of the railway world together with us? At Siemens Mobility, we are the leading provider of mobility and are constantly expanding our innovative portfolio, which includes rail vehicles, rail automation, electrification solutions, complete rail systems and accompanying services. With around 800 specialist employees in Switzerland and 39 600 employees worldwide, we shape mobility with passion and are one step ahead of our time. Do you want to inspire an engineering team, take responsibility in challenging railway projects and help shape the safe mobility of tomorrow? Then you are exactly right with us. As a team leader: in the field of electronic signal boxes Simis IS, you take over the technical and human leadership of a committed engineering team. With your positive nature, you create orientation, strengthen cohesion and motivate your team to achieve ambitious goals together. At the same time, you bring your technical know-how to exciting railway projects and work with interdisciplinary teams to ensure the successful implementation of our signalling solutions. Your new role

  • You will lead and develop the engineering team in the area of security systems Simis IS and promote an open, trusting and powerful team culture.
  • You provide orientation, motivate your team in challenging situations and support the professional and personal development of your employees.
  • You are responsible for engineering processes, drive their continuous improvement and strengthen quality, efficiency and profitability.
  • As a sub-project manager, you assume responsibility for costs, deadlines and technical objectives.
  • You work closely with international engineering teams as well as with product management, sales and other specialist areas and support in the creation of technical offers. You bring that with you
  • Technical training at HF/FH level or equivalent, ideally in electrical engineering, transport systems or a related field.
  • Initial experience in the management, development or professional management of employees and the ability to inspire, take and promote people.
  • Experience in technical project environments as well as a structured, solution-oriented working method.
  • Networked thinking, high personal responsibility and enjoyment of working with different stakeholders.
  • Flowing knowledge of German and English
  • Experience in railway safety technology or in the signal box environment is a plus. You benefit from
  • an open and flexible working culture at one of the leading companies in the field of mobility in Switzerland
  • a 40-hour week on a 100% fee
  • at least 5 weeks of holidays
  • a workplace at the pulse of railway technology
  • Various employee benefits and training opportunities
