Full description

  • Caritas Zurich is an independent Catholic aid organization. We work for families and disadvantaged people affected by poverty, regardless of nationality or worldview. In this way, we make a significant contribution to their social integration in the Canton of Zurich.

Social worker in social counselling for families (80%)

The counselling department supports low-income families in psychosocial and financial matters and offers short telephone counselling, debt counseling, social counseling for families and mobile social counseling.

We are looking for 15. November 2026 or by arrangement as a result of retirement to complement our team of social counselling for families a social worker.

Their tasks

As part of the social counselling for families, you make a valuable contribution to supporting families affected by poverty in the Canton of Zurich. Their tasks are versatile and practical:

  • Advice for families at the poverty line living in the Canton of Zurich
  • Support and support for families in securing their livelihoods, especially in the areas of finance, housing, education and everyday life management, as well as promoting sustainable prospects within the framework of independent dossier management
  • Telephone and personal short consultations of help seekers
  • Taking on coordinative tasks in the social consulting team
  • Participation in the media and public relations work of Caritas Zurich, e.g. B. through technical contributions, case studies or involvement in events

They like to advise people in challenging life situations. They also recognize the prerequisites for sustainable change in small steps. Complex connections do not bring you out of peace, on the contrary: you meet them with serenity and full of curiosity. The resources, strengths and independence of those seeking advice are the focus for you. They treat them with appreciation and respect.

You also bring the following

  • Completed study of social work with several years of consulting experience
  • Competence and pleasure in consulting in an intercultural context
  • Knowledge of the Swiss social system
  • Experience in providing financial support in consulting settings is beneficial
  • Speditive, independent and pragmatic working method with a pinch of humor
  • Enjoy working in a small team
