Full description

Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Prisons and Reintegration Detention Centers Zurich Supervisors with duty in administration and headquarters 80 - 100% With around 1400 employees, JuWe is the largest office within the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs and is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as for the execution of various types of detention. Our employees from around 40 professions support and promote the reintegration of delinquents and work effectively and progressively for a safe and just society. The Zurich Detention Centers (UGZ) are one of eight main departments of JuWe. They comprise seven companies with about 735 prison places and about 500 employees. They are responsible for provisional arrest, pre-trial detention and security detention, as well as the early execution of penalties and measures until transfer to a prison facility. In this way, they fulfil a central function within the Zurich justice system right from the start. As part of the UGZ, Horgen Prison specializes in pre-trial detention for adult men and has 51 detention places. To complement our team in Horgen Prison, we are looking for a committed, cosmopolitan and humorous personality by immediate or by agreement. Your tasks

  • You will accompany, assist and employ detained persons during their pre-trial detention from entry to exit, taking on tasks in care, security and organisation
  • You will meet imprisoned people and visitors professionally, respectfully and in a situationally appropriate manner and act clearly and de-escalatingly even in demanding situations.
  • You ensure an orderly daily routine, enforce safety and service regulations and manage crisis situations carefully
  • Test at headquarters You ensure the safety of employees, detainees and third parties by operating security, surveillance and communications facilities as well as by triggering the security facility in emergencies
  • You coordinate and supervise the visitation, receive gifts and ensure the necessary controls, including X-ray controls
  • You take on administrative tasks such as reports and documentation and support the administration within the framework of detachment agencies, in particular dossier management, correspondence and the management of detainee accounts Your profile

  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Knowledge of foreign languages is advantageous

  • A demanding, versatile job in a dynamic work environment with a supportive team

  • A centrally located place of work with good connections to public transport
