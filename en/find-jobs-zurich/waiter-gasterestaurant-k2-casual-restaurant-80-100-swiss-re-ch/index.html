Full description

In order to support our team in the guest restaurant K2 as well as in the casual restaurant, we are looking for a restaurant specialist who is happy to be active in an international environment and will gladly take on the following main tasks as of 1 August, or by arrangement.

Their main tasks

  • They welcome, look after and say goodbye to their guests in a professional, courteous way and create a pleasant atmosphere in which our guests feel comfortable through their very good manners and sound expertise.
  • They communicate with our international guests in both German and English (company language).
  • In addition to the lunch service in our à la carte guest restaurant She independently organizes and carries out breakfasts, aperos, cocktails and evening events.
  • Active participation in apprenticeship training is a matter of course for you.
  • Mise en place – working, cleaning and material care.

Working days, hours

  • They generally work from Monday to Friday between 8 a.m. and 11 p.m., if possible without a room hour.
  • Deviations and bets on weekends are possible, but very rare.
  • Your location is primarily Zurich, can also be sporadically at the Rüschlikon location for support.
