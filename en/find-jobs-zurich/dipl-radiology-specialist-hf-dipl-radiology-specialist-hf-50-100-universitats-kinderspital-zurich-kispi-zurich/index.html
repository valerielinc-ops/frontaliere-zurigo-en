Children's Hospital Zurich Eleonorenstiftung | online since : 28.09.2026

With heart and high-tech – radiology for children in the new Kispi

Your tasks

  • Independent performance of radiological examinations for children and adolescents using state-of-the-art equipment (CT, MR, screening, digital X-ray, EOS).
  • Empathic accompaniment of patients: inside and their relatives. You give security and provide a trusting atmosphere.
  • Assuming special tasks in areas such as PACS, RIS, CT or MR and actively participating in the further development of our high quality standards.
  • Responsibility for smooth processes and reliable cooperation in our diverse service assignments.

Your profile

  • Completed education as a radiology specialist HF with in-depth professional experience.
  • Joy, patience and empathy in dealing with children and their relatives. You are their anchor in challenging moments.
  • Organizational talent, resilience and a cool head even in hectic situations.
  • Strong social skills and enthusiasm for interdisciplinary cooperation in a well-established team.
  • Flexibility and openness to new things in a modern, growing environment.
