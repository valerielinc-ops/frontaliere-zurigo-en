BAULEITER:IN HIGH BUILDING / COLLECTION 80–100% — AFRY, Zurich. Job description About us We develop, plan and implement challenging infrastructure projects of various kinds – from residential and commercial buildings to public buildings and industry and technical facilities as new buildings to complex conversions and repairs. Together with architects, specialist planners and clients, we accompany our projects from planning to successful realisation. Their tasks

  • Independent construction management of demanding infrastructure projects for the departments of overall planning and structural engineering
  • Responsibility for coordination and monitoring of construction work in terms of quality, costs and deadlines
  • Leadership and coordination of entrepreneurs, specialist planners, authorities and other project participants
  • Ensuring professional implementation in accordance with planning documents, SIA standards and technical specifications
  • Preparation of tenders, forecasts, work contracts and appointment and construction programmes
  • Management of construction meetings including logging and site reporting
  • Examination of invoices, supplements and dimensions
  • Support of project and overall management during all implementation phases
  • Cooperation in the further development of modern construction processes and digital working methods (BIM/BIM2Field) Your profile
  • Completed training in construction (construction manager: in HFP/HF, technician: in HF, architect: in FH/ETH or equivalent)
  • Experience in the construction management of building construction projects in Switzerland
  • Good knowledge of Swiss standards as well as construction and contracting (SIA)
  • Experience in tendering and devising with NPK/CRB
  • Safe handling of MS Office and digital construction administration tools
  • Structured, independent and solution-oriented working method
  • Communication-strong, assertive and team-oriented personality
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing Your opportunities
  • Cooperation in architecturally and technically demanding infrastructure projects
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation with our teams from overall planning and structural planning
  • High personal responsibility and direct influence on project success
  • Modern working methods and digital planning tools
  • Collegial corporate culture with short decision-making paths and flat hierarchies
  • Flexible working models as well as attractive development and further education opportunities
  • Modern workplace in Zurich Altstetten, just a few minutes walk from the train station We offer you a modern and professional working environment with exciting projects, interdisciplinary cooperation and high technical competence. You take responsibility in challenging construction projects and actively shape the interface between planning and execution. Enjoy attractive employment conditions, modern workplaces in Zurich, flexible working hours and a motivated team with a high level of expertise. We look forward to your online application!
