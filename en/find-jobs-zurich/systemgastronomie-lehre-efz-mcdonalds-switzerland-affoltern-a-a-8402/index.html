Requirement profile

  • You have a good secondary degree or comparable
  • You have very good knowledge of German (word and writing)
  • You enjoy the lively gastronomic environment
  • You are a friendly team player
  • You are resilient and like to participate
  • You enjoy taking on responsibility and keep track even in hectic situations
  • You have a quick understanding and recognize connections without problems
  • You bring sense of numbers
  • Cleanliness and order are a matter of course for you

If you can answer all questions with yes, then you are the right one!

Task area

As an apprentice system gastronomy specialist EFZ, you can expect a well-founded and wide-ranging education in our restaurants: During the three-year apprenticeship, you not only learn how products are prepared and guests are served, but also get to the bottom of business contexts. In addition to food science, quality assurance, product manufacturing and sales, marketing, human resources, cost control and the organization of work processes are on your curriculum. With a clear plan: Enter restaurant management after your education. By the way, you will complete your vocational training in parallel at the vocational school and in one of our restaurants.Your apprenticeship and thus the EFZ is the first milestone in your development at McDonald’s. You can train yourself internally and externally. In the restaurant it goes up for you to the managing director. And in the field of gastronomy, you have a variety of development opportunities. An exciting and diverse activity is waiting for you!

What we offer

As an apprentice, you are in good hands with us: In each restaurant, you have a trained job profile and support during your entire apprenticeship. In addition, you benefit from 6 weeks of holidays per year, internal further training (parallel to school and company training) and a specially tailored annual plan with exciting activities. Then take the first step and send us your application! Would you like to find out more about the training? You can find a lot of information about training at http://jobs.mcdonalds.ch/en/lehre
