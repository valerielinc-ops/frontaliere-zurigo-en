Full description

Forel Klinik is one of the leading addiction clinics in Switzerland and specializes in the treatment of people with alcohol or drug dependence. At two locations, more than 180 employees provide support for those affected on their personal path to a better quality of life and self-determination. As an addiction specialist clinic, it is anchored in our DNA to open up prospects and contribute to the development of people. This principle also shapes our dealings with employees. Create with us paths that work. They create a well-kept, reliable environment for patients and employees. Her work in housekeeping is central to the well-being of everyone – day after day. In our team you will experience appreciation, structure and space for further development. We are looking for you immediately or by agreement to strengthen our team.

  • irregular working days of 8:24 hours per day and max. 2 days on weekends a month, services between 06:00 and 17:00

Your profile

  • Compliance and implementation of our high hygiene standard
  • German skills in word and writing
  • Independent and exact work

Our offer

  • Reliable service planning
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
  • 60 % employer contribution to the pension fund
  • 100 % AG contribution to KTG insurance
  • long-term employer

Our stationary clinic in Ellikon an der Thur is idyllic in the countryside and at the same time offers good accessibility from the Zurich, Winterthur and Eastern Switzerland region and the German border area. Further advantages of the place of work are the in-house gastronomy and sufficient free parking. Look forward to a new challenge in a great team, an innovative addiction clinic with a pleasant working atmosphere. Our offer is aimed at people of all genders and identities.
