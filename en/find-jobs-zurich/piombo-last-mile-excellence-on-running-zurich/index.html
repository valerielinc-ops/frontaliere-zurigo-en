Full description

In short The Lead - Last Mile Excellence role will report to the Head of Outbound Transportation & Last Mile Delivery on the Last Mile Excellence team supporting our omnichannel last mile operations. You will drive complex, end-to-end projects to ensure On customers receive the best possible delivery and returns experience across ecommerce, retail, and wholesale. As an agile thinker in a brand-new team, you will be responsible for landing some of our largest initiatives, directly improving customer experience in a fast-growing retail environment.

Your mission

  • Lead the global rollout and strategic scaling of our multi-carrier strategy across all regions. Currently, On single source carriers in the majority of our global regions and our team is responsible for evolving our last mile into a multi-carrier model.
  • Develop the methodology for us to measure and improve our expected delivery date performance, working collaboratively across Tech, Operations, Regional Supply Chain and our carrier partners to drive this number as high as possible.
  • Help develop, align, and optimize foundational ways of working across our ecommerce, retail, and wholesale channels.
  • Make critical operational and project decisions autonomously while effectively gathering input from cross-functional stakeholders.
  • Balance innovation with efficiency, continuously seeking new ideas or technologies to improve processes without reinventing the wheel.

Your story

  • 7+ years of hands-on experience navigating complex supply chain environments, with a deep operational understanding of retail, delivery carriers, or 3PL networks.
  • Proven experience managing carrier relations or delivery solutions, matching the expertise of a carrier manager or delivery solutions manager.
  • An agile thinker who is highly comfortable navigating changing priorities and ambiguity in a fast-paced, high-growth environment.
  • A proactive problem-solver with a track record of working autonomously to address complex, multi-layered logistical challenges.
  • Excellent communication skills, with a natural ability to build a diverse internal network and collaborate closely with functional peers.
  • Proficient English language skills. Other languages are a plus.
  • A results-oriented individual with a deep curiosity, open to learning, and determined to make a direct impact on the customer journey.
  • Experience managing a carrier or retailer delivery P&L and operating a Carrier Management System is advantageous.

Meet the team Welcome to the brand-new Last Mile Excellence team! We exist to ensure On customers receive the best possible delivery and returns experience across our three core channels: ecommerce, retail, and wholesale. We sit under the larger Global Logistics and Distribution team, whose mission it is to introduce global standards and practices and roll them out to our colleagues in Regional Supply Chain. We are a fast-growing, agile group dedicated to making a direct, tangible impact on the post-purchase journey. If you love solving complex supply chain problems, navigating changing priorities, and collaborating with a diverse network to improve global ways of working, you will fit right in.

What we offer

On is a place that is centered around growth and progress. We offer an environment designed to give people the tools to develop holistically – to stay active, to learn, explore and innovate. Our distinctive approach combines a supportive, team-oriented atmosphere, with access to personal self-care for both physical and mental well-being, so each person is led by purpose.
