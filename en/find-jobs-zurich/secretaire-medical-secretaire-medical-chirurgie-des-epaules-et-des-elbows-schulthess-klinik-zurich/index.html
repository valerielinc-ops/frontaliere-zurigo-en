Full description

Tasks

  • Organisation, coordination and preparation of consultation hours
  • Welcome our patients to the reception
  • Order of missing X-ray images and files of the patients to be asked
  • Writing consultation, exit, operation and insurance reports from Diktaphon and recording the data in the system
  • Completion of incoming mail and telephone requests
  • Close cooperation with the medical team and contact person for their patients
  • Representation of holidays and illnesses in the secretarial team

Requirements

  • Training as MPA and/or additional training as hospital and medical secretary with professional experience in a comparable position
  • Joy of writing and safe application of medical terminology
  • Service-oriented and enjoyment of direct patient contact
  • Trustworthy, independent, resilient and communicative personality
  • Flexible and team-oriented workflow
  • Very good and stylish knowledge of German, knowledge in English, Italian or French is advantageous

The Schulthess Clinic in Zurich is one of the leading orthopaedic clinics in Europe with over 1,100 employees. Our central concern is to free people from their pain and restore their mobility. As a specialist clinic, we focus on surgical orthopedics, neurology, rheumatology and sports medicine. Schulthess Clinic is Swiss Olympic Medical Center.
