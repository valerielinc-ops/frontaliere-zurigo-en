Full description

For Tax I we are looking for reinforcement immediately or by agreement.

Tasks

  • You take over the personnel and professional management of a team of nine employees and are responsible for ensuring the qualitative and quantitative work done with regard to tax emoluments, assessments and tax return procedures, including deregistrations abroad.
  • They plan the assignments of the employees and, if necessary, organize deputies for the customer zone together with the team management of the Services team.
  • They independently conduct team meetings and actively participate in team leader meetings.
  • They support the head of department in his management tasks.
  • You actively contribute to the strategic development of the area, are involved in cross-departmental projects and take responsibility for the promotion and development of your directly subordinate employees.

Profile

  • We address people with a university degree or equivalent education and / or work experience as well as in-depth knowledge in Zurich taxation. You have preferably appropriated these at a municipal tax office.
  • Leadership and project experience are of great advantage, but not a condition.
  • They convince through communication skills, performance skills and the ability to act calmly, solution-oriented and determined even in demanding situations.
  • They have team and integration skills, sensitivity to people and situations, and an open communication style.
  • They strive to develop themselves and their team personally and professionally and to promote the development of the tax specialists.
  • In addition to your flair for numbers, you can express yourself in German both verbally and in writing.
  • Your strengths include commitment, perseverance, independence and the ability to think beyond your own area of responsibility. A conscious time and self-management is a matter of course for you. In addition, you are characterized by a high willingness and openness to change in order to react flexibly and proactively to challenges.

We offer

We actively promote your further development and offer you, if suitable and interested, opportunities to participate outside of day-to-day business and in a larger environment. We offer you a modern working environment as well as the possibility of home office. Your workplace is located in the customer zone of the tax office. You and the employees have an office on the 4th floor as a retreat. The employment conditions comply with the city guidelines and contain very good social benefits and fringe benefits.

Our workplaces are accessible, centrally located and easily accessible by public transport.

About us

The city of Zurich offers high living, working and quality of life. The wide range of cultural offerings and educational institutions characterizes the character as a diverse, cosmopolitan and fun-loving city. The Tax Office of the City of Zurich is the largest municipal tax office in Switzerland. It implements the cantonal and federal tax legislation at municipal level and supervises the tax procedures for around 300 000 natural and legal persons. Do you like working with numbers and love professional challenges? The city of Zurich offers you both as an attractive and dynamic center.
