Specialist study administration (m/f/d)

80%, Zurich, temporary

Printed Printing

At ETH Zurich, more than 20,000 students study at Bachelor's and Master's level or are enrolled as listeners. The Department of Academic Services creates the basic administrative requirements for teaching and is responsible for central processes of teaching operations for the entire ETH Zurich.

The Department of Study Administration is part of the Academic Services and accompanies students in administrative matters before and during their studies.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for 1. December 2026 or by agreement a committed and reliable personality as a specialist:in study administration. The term of office shall be two years.

Job description

  • They are part of the team that handles requests for exemptions. They clarify matters carefully, process the applications and prepare the corresponding basis for decision-making.

  • They maintain the relevant data in the corresponding systems and ensure that deadlines are met reliably.
  • They work together with the relevant advisory centres, the study administrations of the study programmes and other contact persons within the ETH Zurich.

When organizing meetings and events, you take over the planning, coordination and follow-up – from scheduling and protocol management to the implementation of agreed measures.
  • They actively participate in the further development of processes and processes, contribute their own ideas and contribute to the continuous optimization of application procedures.

Profile

  • They work very carefully, structured and independently.
