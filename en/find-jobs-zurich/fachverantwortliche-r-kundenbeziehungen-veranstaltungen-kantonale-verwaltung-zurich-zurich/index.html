Full description

Canton of Zurich Economic Directorate Office for Labour Specialist responsible for customer relations & events 80% work changes lives - and that is exactly what drives us. Every day, the Office of Labour (AFA) of the Canton of Zurich works to ensure that people regain their jobs, that companies find the right talents and that the labour market in the Canton of Zurich remains strong. With around 900 employees at various locations, we bundle labour market skills and are close to the people, the companies and the realities of working life. You don’t work anywhere with us. You work where your commitment really counts. How does a Labour Office communicate so that it is heard? How do we explain complex issues in an understandable way, for job seekers, companies, the public and politics? These questions are our craft. In the AFA Team Communication & Networking, we develop strategies, produce content and accompany exponents in all relevant appearances, both internally and externally. Networking is just as important to us: with events for our stakeholders and with new, attractive channels, we cultivate and strengthen relationships – with companies, specialist partners and within the AFA. As of January 1, 2027, we are looking for you as a committed, networked personality for our customer relations & events area of responsibility. Your tasks:

  • Overall responsibility and project management for the AFA events: program, topics, speaker management and event design (logistics is implemented internally by another team)
  • Customer communication around our events – from invitation to post-processing
  • Establishment and design of a new AFA newsletter, including address management, editing and shipping
  • Conception, implementation and evaluation of customer relationships and information to job seekers, companies and other stakeholders
  • Playing the social media channels around our events
  • Close cooperation with internal departments and external partners Your profile:
  • University degree in communication, event management, marketing or comparable
  • Experience in organizing events and project management, ideally in a public or non-profit environment
  • Strong sense of customer relationships and stakeholder management
  • Experience in building or editing newsletters of advantage
  • Organizational talent, communication strength and initiative
  • Interest in labour market policy issues
  • We offer style-confident German: You can expect a versatile function with a great deal of room for manoeuvre: You are actively involved in establishing the area of customer relationships and events and make a significant contribution to an effective networking of the AFA with its stakeholders. In addition, flexible working hours and catering allowances are standard with us. Your workplace? Centrally located in Zurich, only a 5-minute walk from the main train station. Does this task appeal to you and are you an editorial, structured personality? Monika Gut, Head of Communication, is happy to provide you with further information: monika.gut@vd.zh.ch. We look forward to your application. You can find out more about us and our employees at www.zh.ch/afa .

Pensum: 80 %
