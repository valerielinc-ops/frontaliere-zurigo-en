What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family company with over 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessories as well as the provision of financial services. Emil Frey Digital AG is part of the Emil Frey Group and is a full-service communications service provider for the automotive industry and responsible for digitalization within the Emil Frey Group. To strengthen our consulting team, we are looking for a customer consultant at the Zurich-Oerlikon location immediately or by agreement, a key account with paid media reference Your tasks

  • You independently and independently lead challenging digital, analogue & cross-media projects as well as campaigns for Emil Frey Group brands from briefing to go live
  • You offer your customers comprehensive advice about our digital and analog services
  • You are responsible for quality, budget, timing, resources and the success of your projects. You are responsible for the internal & external coordination of all stakeholders as well as for the management of external partners & agencies
  • You will strategically advise on the optimal use of digital advertising formats, support the development of cross-channel media plans and work closely with the paid media team
  • You support the paid media team with administrative tasks such as billing, update media planning, etc.
  • You inspire customers with convincing presentations and show how additional services can further increase their success Your profile
  • Completed studies or professional experience in the field of digital marketing, communication or media business, supplemented by experience in the field of paid media
  • At least 3-5 years of professional experience in marketing with focus on project management and customer consulting
  • Several years of experience in project management in an agile environment and in complex tasks, preferably in an agency
  • Experience in strategy and concept development
  • High degree of initiative, flexibility and teamwork as well as communicative and analytical skills
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • Enthusiasm for the automotive industry and interest in media trends What we offer you You will find a new challenge with exciting tasks and much more:
  • Numerous discounts (new cars, service work and spare parts, vehicle and health insurance, fuel cards, hotels and much more)
  • An own, attractive pension fund as an independent pension institution
  • A long-term business perspective
  • Individual training and development opportunities
  • At least 25 days of vacation How to contact us We look forward to the full application (e.g. CV, employment certificates, etc.) via the link "Apply now!".
