Full description

What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family company with over 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessories as well as the provision of financial services. Emil Frey Digital AG is part of the Emil Frey Group and is a full-service communications service provider for the automotive industry and responsible for digitalization within the Emil Frey Group. To strengthen our Paid Media Team in Zurich Oerlikon, we are looking for a committed, technically-savvy and leadership-strong personality as Media Operations Lead Your tasks

  • Ensuring the technically correct, high-quality and timely implementation of paid media campaigns
  • Professional and disciplinary leadership as well as further development of the Media Operations Team
  • Planning, prioritization and control of resources, tasks and operational processes
  • Development, documentation and continuous optimization of processes, quality standards and technical specifications
  • Responsibility for complex tracking, measurement and verification set-ups as well as their quality assurance
  • Analysis and correction of technical errors and validation of measurement results and data quality
  • Coordination of interfaces between campaign operations, ad operations, media planning, strategy & planning and external partners
  • Several years of professional experience in campaign operations, ad operations or a comparable paid media environment
  • Experience in the management and development of employees as well as enjoyment of coaching and knowledge transfer
  • Sound hands-on knowledge of Campaign Manager 360, Display & Video 360 and relevant tracking and verification tools
  • Good understanding of cross-channel paid media campaigns, quality control and operational control
  • Strong analytical skills as well as the competence to independently detect technical errors and solve them in a solution-oriented manner
  • Structured, reliable and quality-conscious working method with a high level of personal responsibility
  • Stylistic German as well as very good English skills in word and writing What we offer you You will find a new challenge with us with exciting tasks and much more:
  • Numerous discounts (new and used cars, service work and spare parts, vehicle and health insurance, fuel cards, hotels and much more)
  • An own, attractive pension fund as an independent pension institution
  • A long-term business perspective
  • Individual training and development opportunities
  • At least 25 days of vacation How to contact us We look forward to the full application (e.g. CV, employment certificates, etc.) via the link "Apply now!".
