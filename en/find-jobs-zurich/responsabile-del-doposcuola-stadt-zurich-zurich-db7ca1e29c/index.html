Full description

Become part of our team! We are looking for a manager on 4.1.27 or by arrangement at a fee of 65%. Working days: Mo, Di, Do, Fr

Tasks

  • Care of young people in secondary education, as well as children in middle and lower education, together with a care team
  • Management of own offers as well as cross-supervision tasks in the department of supervision
  • Promotion and support of students in cooperation with teachers and specialists of the school team
  • Participation in school events and in school working groups
  • Administrative and operational tasks
  • Implementation of the educational objectives of the school
  • Participation in the holiday camp, in age-mixed groups for 3 weeks per school year

Profile

  • degree as a social pedagogue; social worker; the manager; kindergarten teacher or teacher
  • Motivated, open, resilient personality with a positive human image
  • Flexibility as well as very good team and communication skills
  • Joy in dealing with people of different cultures
  • Readiness for interdisciplinary cooperation in the school team
  • Advantageous professional experience in supplementary family care
  • ADB knowledge is desirable, otherwise interested in dealing with new computer programs
  • Certificate PA escort or willingness to make the certificate to accompany a social educator in education

We offer

  • An experienced and committed support team
  • varied, versatile and independent work
  • A multicultural, exciting environment
  • Competent support from the management
  • 10 weeks of school holidays
  • a contemporary employment according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich
  • a centrally located place of work with good connections to public transport
