Full description

In deals, we offer a wide range of services including due diligence, valuation, transaction structuring, financial modeling and integration planning. We support our customers throughout the entire transaction cycle, from strategic preparation and target identification to negotiations and successful implementation. As trusted advisors for transactions, mergers and acquisitions, we combine in-depth industry knowledge with a collaborative approach and PwC's global reach to create sustainable value in a dynamic market environment.

  • Supporting companies and private equity funds in national and international M&A transactions across all phases of the deal lifecycle
  • Analysis and classification of valuation-relevant tax aspects with a focus on purchase price mechanics, including within the framework of tax due diligence, EV-to-equity bridge, net debt, management incentive plans and SPA negotiations
  • Conception of sustainable, cash flow-effective financing and acquisition structures as well as tax support for the integration of Target companies in existing group structures
  • Overall responsibility for M&A projects including project management, quality assurance and maintenance of long-term customer relationships
  • Professional and personal management of employees as well as active promotion of their further development in project and day-to-day business
  • Establishment and further development of your own customer portfolio by helping to design innovative deal value propositions in the PwC ONE Deals team and their positioning in the market
  • Master's degree in banking & finance, business administration or economics as well as qualification as a Swiss tax expert
  • Several years of professional experience in the national and international corporate tax advice, ideally with a strong focus on M&A
  • Confident understanding of the interface between taxes, valuation, financing and legal framework conditions
  • Structured, analytical way of working as well as high resilience in complex and time-critical transaction situations
  • Very good communication skills with the ability to convey complex issues clearly, in an appropriate and commercial manner
  • Very good written and spoken German and English skills; other languages ​​are an advantage
  • Entrepreneurial mindset, team and customer orientation as well as a long-term development and leadership perspective
