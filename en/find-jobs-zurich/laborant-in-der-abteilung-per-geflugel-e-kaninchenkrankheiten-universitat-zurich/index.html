Institute for Food Safety and Hygiene

Job 15 January 2027 or by arrangement, temporary

As a result of the retirement of a long-term employee, we advertise the position. The position is initially limited to one year with the option of permanent employment. Employer is the Department of Poultry and Rabbit Diseases, Institute for Food Safety and Hygiene of the University of Zurich. We are a veterinary laboratory accredited for animal diseases, dissect animal bodies to determine the cause of the disease and carry out examinations of various sample materials (bacteriology, stains, serology and molecular biology). They work in a team of 10 employees, consisting of veterinarians, laboratory assistants and secretaries.

Tasks

  • Independent cooperation (after thorough training) in the routine diagnostics of animal diseases in the fields of bacteriology, serology, molecular diagnostics and section.
  • Their tasks include, inter alia, the reception, processing and testing of various samples (blood, chicken eggs, faeces, tissues, carcases) by PCR, ELISA, HAH, culture and MALDI-TOF-MS in a veterinary laboratory accredited for the testing of animal diseases.
  • They work in the section hall to prepare the carcasses of poultry, birds and rabbits for veterinary examination and use various methods to examine samples in a demanding routine operation.
  • Possibility to attend training events.

Profile

  • Completed vocational training as a laboratory assistant: in or an adequate non-academic education and / or several years of professional experience.
  • Flexibility, precise, organized and forward-looking working methods (including protocols in the accredited area).
  • Enjoy learning new methods and laboratory techniques.
  • Responsible, service-oriented and team-capable personality, resilient physical constitution (in the section hall and in the laboratory mainly standing work, possible odor emissions by animal carcasses).
  • Good knowledge and experience in the application of real-time PCR in the diagnostic routine.
  • Knowledge of ELISA, HAH, bacterial cultures and MALDI-TOF-MS can be acquired during training.
  • Interest in the examination of carcasses and animal samples focusing on poultry.
  • Languages: Good knowledge of German (verbal and written at least level C1) available. Good understanding of Swiss German (customer contact) is available. Basic English skills are desirable.
