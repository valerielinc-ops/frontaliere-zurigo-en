Store: Manor Winterthur, ZH, Switzerland. Job ID: R3713.

Happiness can be found in the smallest of things. It is our passion to turn everyday routines into more meaningful moments and connect with customers and colleagues. At Rituals, we blend a thriving culture with top talent. Join our fast growing wellbeing brand and nurture an impactful career with us. •

Forming successful teams

Hire talent, coach and inspire your team to drive success and strong sales. Recognize individual strengths and promote the growth of each person through new tasks. Manage collaboration with the department store by confidently representing the interests of Rituals and maintaining a strong partnership. •

Operations Management & Counter-Optimization

Manage the day-to-day business from cash accounting to inventory management, always in compliance with our retail standards. Be responsible for improving performance as well as for our sales and customer experience goals incl. KPI management and sales promotion. Recognize opportunities, develop concepts to increase sales and ensure that our customers are satisfied and the team remains motivated. •

Creating unforgettable shopping experiences

Build lasting relationships with customers by remembering faces and favorites and feeling what first-time visitors will love. Through your well-kept and professional appearance, be a real brand ambassador where every detail counts. Actively address department store customers, familiarize them with rituals and turn curious people with confidence into loyal customers. •

Experience & background

You have at least 3 years of leadership experience as well as at least 3 years of experience in the retail, hospitality or cosmetics industry. •

Flexibility & Availability

You bring flexibility – also for late shifts and weekends – and have a valid work permit or work permit. Residence permit (C, B, L or G permit) or Swiss citizenship. •

Personal strength

You combine human knowledge with entrepreneurial thinking. •

Readiness

You are flexible – both when coaching your team and when working on the sales floor.
