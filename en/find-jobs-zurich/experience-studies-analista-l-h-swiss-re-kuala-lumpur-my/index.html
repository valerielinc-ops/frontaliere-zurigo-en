Original description

About the Team We are the L&H Experience Studies Centre (ES Centre), part of L&H Pricing & Portfolio Performance (PPP) within the Chief Underwriting Office (CUO). PPP brings together pricing expertise and portfolio oversight to support effective risk management, sustainable profitability , and strong performance across both new business and in-force portfolios.

The ES Centre act s as an in-house Experience Studies global solutions provider, delivering the platforms, tools, methodologies, and expertise that support the production of Experience Studies for traditional L&H business globally. Through the development and continuous enhancement of the Experience Studies Platform (ESP), we enable teams worldwide to perform efficient, consistent, and high-quality E xperience S tudies.

About the Role

This is a junior role for someone who is curious, proactive, and keen to learn. With guidance and on-the-job training, you will help develop and improve analytical tools, platforms, and methodologies that support Experience Studies across L&H. You will gain hands-on experience in actuarial analysis, R programming, platform testing, reporting, and user support while working with actuarial and technology colleagues globally.

Key Responsibilities

Actuarial Analytics and Tool Development

  • Apply actuarial concepts and programming skills to build reusable analytical solutions, primarily in R.
  • Test new platform features and contribute ideas that improve the user experience and solution quality.
  • Explore opportunities to automate processes and introduce emerging analytical techniques and best practices.

Experience Studies Operational Analytics

  • Deliver meaningful operational insights that help leaders understand Experience Studies activity, demand patterns, resource utilisation, and service performance.
  • Build and enhance interactive dashboards and visualisations that enable stakeholders to monitor performance and make informed decisions.
  • Investigate data anomalies, reporting challenges, and workflow bottlenecks, driving improvements in data quality and KPI reliability.

Global Collaboration

  • Work alongside actuaries, technology specialists, and stakeholders across different markets to shape the future of the Experience Studies Platform.
  • Gain exposure to how actuarial analysis is delivered at scale across a global organisation.

Learning and Knowledge Sharing

  • Create engaging learning materials on experience studies, actuarial methodologies, and the common R calculation engine.
  • Share knowledge with actuaries and experience studies practitioners around the world.
  • Support platform users, solve practical problems, and turn their feedback into meaningful improvements.

About You

Essential

  • Degree in Actuarial Science, Mathematics, Statistics, Data Science, Computer Science, or a related quantitative discipline.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to investigate issues and work with data.
  • Interest or experience in actuarial analysis, data, technology, and programming.
  • Proactive, curious, and willing to learn new concepts and skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to delivering high-quality work.
  • Good communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work effectively in a global team environment.
  • Takes ownership of assigned tasks and follows through on commitments .

Desirable

  • Exposure to R or another programming language.
  • Knowledge of data visualisation, dashboards, or analytical tools.
  • Understanding of Life & Health insurance products, actuarial concepts, or experience studies.
  • Experience with automation, data quality analysis, or reporting.
  • Interest in developing training materials, sharing knowledge, and supporting users.
