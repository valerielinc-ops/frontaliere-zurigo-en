The ten employees of the Department of Environmental Protection and Accident Prevention of the Office for Waste, Water, Energy and Air (AWEL) develop technical foundations and implement the regulations of water protection, waste management and incident prevention in industrial and commercial enterprises. During your internship you will get a versatile insight into the official enforcement and actively participate in current specialist and enforcement projects. In your half- or one-year internship you will get an insight into the varied implementation activities of a cantonal environmental authority and have the opportunity to establish different contacts.Your
