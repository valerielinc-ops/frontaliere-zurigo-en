Full description

Sometimes quite challenging, but never boring: At EPI WohnWerk, no day is like the other. Because the people we accompany each and everyone have their unique life story. Thanks to good cooperation, we are well positioned for our working day. Do you want to be a part of this team?

The Multifaceted Day

  • You work on a residential group
  • You accompany adults with disabilities professionally in their everyday life
  • You enable them to lead their lives as independently as possible
  • You support them in their personal care as well as in eating and drinking and create their free time with them.
  • In your daily work, you will be supported by a vocational trainer

Their diverse talents

  • You have a good school degree Sek A, Sek B or 10. School year
  • You are at least 16 years old at the beginning of your training and can express yourself orally and in writing in German
  • You are interested in working with people with disabilities and ideally have already completed a trial apprenticeship in this environment
  • You are responsible, capable of teamwork and sociable, have empathy and a good observation ability
  • You work carefully, have practical skills, patience and are healthy
  • You are open to irregular working hours
  • Integrated professional education (flex) possible
  • Learning workshops and workshops with other trainees
  • Internships in various areas of the EPI WohnWerk
  • 5 weeks holiday
  • Discounts in the restaurant, the pharmacy and at Half Tax or GA
