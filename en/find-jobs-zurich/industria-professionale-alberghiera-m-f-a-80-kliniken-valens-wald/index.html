Full description

A broad perspective on rehabilitation and my career – that is the experience of life and work at the Valens Clinic Group. Exchange, progress, interdisciplinary teamwork and good prospects are highly valued here.

To complement our team, we are looking for, by arrangement, a

Responsibilities

  • In this varied role, you will look after our patients with homemade culinary delights
  • You advise patients, take orders and actively participate in service
  • You welcome our patients, accompany and support them during admissions and discharges, and are available to them as a point of contact
  • Your working hours start at 07.00 at the earliest, depending on the shift, and end at 20.00 at the latest

Requirements

  • It is an advantage if you have completed vocational training as a hotel specialist, restaurant specialist or home economics specialist
  • You are a committed, cheerful personality with strong hospitality skills
  • You are a team player, flexible and act in a customer- and service-oriented manner, and are also willing to work weekend and public holiday shifts
  • You have very good German language skills and have experience in guest care and service

We offer

  • A promising and easily accessible workplace – in a collegial environment with clear structures, competent managers and appreciative communication
  • Real benefits instead of empty buzzwords – with flexible shift scheduling * , paid changing time, a bonus for covering shifts, 28 days of vacation, as well as numerous internal and external discounts and offers. See for yourself
  • Training that moves you forward – internal and external courses, including through our Rehab Academy, the group-wide training and continuing education center
  • Diversity as a strength – We rely on thoughtful, self-confident and critical employees with different experiences, qualifications and perspectives, because we are convinced that this benefits us all

The Valens Clinic Group is Switzerland’s largest rehabilitation provider, with 13 locations and nine specialist areas. As the holder of the "Friendly Work Space" and "Beste Arbeitgeber Schweiz" labels, we support our 2300 employees in achieving work-life balance and foster a culture of togetherness.
