Full description

To strengthen our Real Estate Development Team, we are looking for a committed personality as Project Support Real Estate Development (80-100%). Your place of work is our headquarters Connect in Opfikon.

Does that sound like your next challenge?

If you want to contribute your analytical skills, your interest in real estate and your enthusiasm for project development in an exciting environment, we look forward to your application.

Tasks

What tasks await you as Project Support Real Estate Development (80-100%)?

  • You support project development and transaction teams in the identification, analysis and implementation of real estate developments and acquisitions
  • You will develop market, location and competition analyses as well as decision-making basis for strategic initiatives and project developments
  • You coordinate feasibility studies and actively participate in purchase, sales and development processes
  • You create and maintain presentations, factsheets, contact lists, reports and evaluations for internal and external stakeholders
  • You will monitor relevant key figures of the project pipeline and support the team in administrative, organisational and operational tasks

Requirements

What requirements do you bring with you as Project Support Real Estate Development (80-100%)?

  • Bachelor degree in real estate management, architecture, spatial planning, business administration, civil engineering or a comparable field of study
  • Strong analytical skills as well as a high affinity for data, figures and business contexts
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss real estate market, ideally supplemented by basic knowledge in spatial planning, construction or real estate law
  • Very good MS Office knowledge, especially in PowerPoint and Excel, as well as a structured and independent working method
  • Communicative, team-oriented personality with entrepreneurial thinking, high level of initiative and stylish German
  • English skills are beneficial

  • A multicultural working environment with the chance to shape a future worth living

  • A company that promotes talent and the training of its employees

About Implenia

As a leading Swiss construction and real estate service provider, Implenia develops, realizes and manages living spaces, working environments and infrastructure for future generations in Switzerland and Germany. Implenia also offers tunnelling and related infrastructure projects in other markets. The company combines the know-how of highly qualified consulting, development, planning and execution units under one roof into an integrated, multinationally leading construction and real estate service provider. Headquartered in Opfikon near Zurich, Implenia employs more than 8,000 people (FTE) across Europe and achieved a turnover of CHF 3.5 billion in 2025. More information at implenia .com.
