# Technician security systems

Il tuo futuro inizia

Swisscom Secure Vision combines the strengths of Swisscom Broadcast and audio-video g+m s.a. We combine many years of experience, regional proximity and innovative strength to take on the leading role in the Swiss security market. You will help shape intelligent, connected spaces in which technology makes everyday life safe in public areas, critical infrastructures and in industry and commerce. We focus on data protection, cyber resilience and Swiss hosting, work with short paths and offer flexible solutions.

  • Installation, commissioning, maintenance and modernisation of video surveillance systems and networks
  • Close cooperation with project management and specialist teams, customer support on site and remotely
  • Modern work equipment, Swiss hosting and focus on data protection and cyber resilience
  • Region Zurich, Zug, Thurgau, Aargau

Your skills

  • Degree (EFZ) in computer science, telematics, electrical engineering or multimedia electronics
  • Several years of experience in video surveillance, security or ICT systems are advantageous
  • Secure in IP networks (TCP/IP, VLAN, routing, switching)
  • Experience with video surveillance systems and video management systems, Windows server knowledge is advantageous
  • You work independently, reliably and service-oriented
  • You recognize causes quickly and correct disorders targeted
  • Readiness for Picetting and Service Operations
  • Category B driving licence
  • Very good German in word and writing, other national languages are advantageous

Stephan Frey is at your disposal at 079 437 78 19. With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As an employee of Swisscom Secure Vision AG, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience.
