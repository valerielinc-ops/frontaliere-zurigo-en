Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich treats and cares for mentally ill people of all ages. The more than 2,500 employees of the 55 different professions make their contribution to the holistic care of patients in interdisciplinary teams, from clarification and therapy to follow-up care. Our work is based on mutual trust and personal responsibility and together we promote an innovative corporate culture. As a university clinic, we are successfully involved in national and international research projects. 80% - 100% As a leading clinic for adult psychiatry and psychotherapy in Switzerland, we not only take over the care of the Canton of Zurich, but also act as a university institution with a research and teaching mandate from the University of Zurich. At eight locations, including five clinical facilities and a research center, more than 1,000 dedicated employees are shaping a versatile and dynamic work environment. Here you have the opportunity to actively participate in an important institution. The Center for Dependency Diseases (Chief physician and Center Head PD Dr. med.) Marcus Herdener) offers an extensive range of inpatient, outpatient and day-to-day clinical treatment and comprehensive diagnostic investigation options for people with dual diagnoses, i.e. for patients with an addiction disease and other mental illnesses. It also conducts translational addiction research with the aim of improving the lives and health of affected people by developing new prevention and treatment approaches. With immediate effect or by agreement, we are looking for the multi-professional treatment team of the ambulatorium at the Selnaustrasse site in Zurich. Their tasks

  • Senior medical director of two outpatient treatment teams together with the co-leading psychologist or the co-leading senior physician
  • Design and further development of the outpatient treatment offer
  • Joint supervision of medical, psychological and nursing staff
  • Disorder-specific and integrated psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment of patients with dual diagnoses in the outpatient setting
  • Participation in research projects, teaching, training and education

Your profile

  • Approval as a doctor (Prerequisite: Swiss professional recognition MEBEKO & language entry German)
  • Completed or advanced training as a specialist FMH psychiatry and psychotherapy
  • Psychotherapeutic experience
  • Previous experience in the field of dependency diseases is advantageous
  • Strong team and communication skills
  • Patient orientation and openness to innovative treatment concepts
  • Interest in scientific activity

Our offer

  • Exciting field of activity with responsible and versatile tasks
  • Hospitation before a potential employment
  • Very good and specific introduction to the tasks
  • Regular supervision and individual supervision
  • Working in a motivated and collegial interprofessional team
  • Institution with excellent infrastructure
  • Comprehensive training in a stimulating university environment with good networking
  • Regular external supervision and training offers
  • Conditions of Employment under the Cantonal Personnel Act
  • Attractive social benefits (acquisition of 60% of pension contributions)
  • Discounted shopping opportunities as well as sports offers, participation in the ZVV BonusPass/General subscription, lunch checks, company-owned crèche and much more.
