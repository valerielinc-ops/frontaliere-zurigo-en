Are you looking for a family atmosphere in a large, multinational company? Do you prefer a manageable and practice-oriented working environment, but do not want to do without profiting from a broad field of activity and a large company? Is it important to you to really know your colleagues, to work hand in hand with them and not to get lost in the anonymity of a big team?

Then you are exactly the right person to join our great team! Show us what you have on it – because you can expect some from us!

For our modern radiology location in the clinic In the park we are looking for a real team player with many talents, with love for technology, precise working methods and enjoyment of contact and collaboration with people. We work according to the latest scientific standards on modern devices and offer a varied and comprehensive range of radiological examinations and interventions

YOUR TASKS

  • You work closely with your colleagues at all our radiological workstations (conventional X-ray, radiography/angio, mammography, CT, MRI)
  • You prepare the premises and the patients for their examinations, carry out the examinations according to applicable standards and guidelines and document all necessary data
  • You take care of our patients with heart and commitment: prepare them, answer their questions and provide them with comprehensive information
  • You support the radiologist in sterile and unsterile procedures and take care of a smooth process
  • You prepare the planned medications and administer them in consultation with the doctors
  • You are committed to working together in the team and participating You are actively involved in the further development of our department
  • You participate Actively participate in departmental services

Your profile

  • You are a qualified radiology specialist or have an equivalent foreign degree and already have experience in conventional and sectional image diagnostics
  • You are a real team player and love being with people, laughing and working together
  • You are drawing Excel yourself through a professional way of working and attach importance to a varied and demanding job profile
  • You speak fluent German and ideally bring one or more foreign languages to communicate with our multicultural patients
  • You are an independent organizational talent and practical
  • It is important for you to contribute your ideas and make a difference in your workplace

We offer you an attractive workplace in beautiful surroundings on the left shore of Lake Zurich. You can expect a multicultural and professional team. We maintain a flat hierarchy and work together among all professional groups at eye level and in partnership. We focus on family and helpful cooperation and give all team members the opportunity to actively participate in the design of their workplace.

We pay regionally in line with the market, offer attractive social and non-wage benefits and a large number of other employee benefits.

Granted as compensation for daily moving to work Hirslanden 4 extra free days with a 100% fee. You also benefit from an attractive and generous compensation regulation as well as flexible working hours.
