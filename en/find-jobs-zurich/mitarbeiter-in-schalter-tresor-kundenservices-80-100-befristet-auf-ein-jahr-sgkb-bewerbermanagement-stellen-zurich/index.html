Full description

Do you enjoy looking after our customers personally and providing a positive customer experience? In our Zurich office, you will find a dedicated private banking team that attaches great importance to competence, reliability and personal proximity. With us, you take on a wide range of tasks in the counter and vault business as well as in the services team and are an important contact person for our internal and external customers.

This is what you will do.

  • You support and advise our customers at the counter friendly and competent.
  • The independent handling of counter transactions in the point of sale and in cash transactions is one of your daily tasks.
  • You take over the implementation and support of safe and closing shops.
  • You recognize customer needs at an early stage and pass on relevant concerns to the relevant specialists and customer consultants.
  • With your pronounced service orientation also for various in-house tasks, you contribute to a high quality of service and a positive customer experience.
  • Compliance with regulatory and safety-relevant requirements is a matter of course for you.

You bring that with you

  • You have experience in the bank counter business or have recently completed your basic banking education.
  • Direct contact with customers at the counter and in-house gives you pleasure and a pronounced service orientation distinguishes you.
  • With your friendly, safe and professional appearance, you convince in personal customer contact.
  • An independent, reliable and exact working method as well as a trusting and discreet handling of customer data and values are a matter of course for you.
  • You have excellent knowledge of German in words and writing. Further language skills are advantageous.

Due to maternity representation, the position is limited until the end of 2027, covers a workload of 80 – 100% and is ideally to be filled immediately. A later permanent employment is possible if appropriate suitability and operational requirements.

Information Petra Heussi, Head of Infrastructure, Security & Switch, +41 44 214 34 40, is happy to answer your questions.

Application www.sgkb.ch/karriere, Martin Knechtle, HR Business Partner For this position, no applications from external personnel service providers are considered.
