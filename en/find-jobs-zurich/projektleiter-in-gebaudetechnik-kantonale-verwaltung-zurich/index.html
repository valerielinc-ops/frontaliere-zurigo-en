Full description

The Zurich Cantonal Police, with around 4,000 employees, takes responsibility for security in the canton. The IT infrastructure service (IT-IN) is responsible for the procurement and operation of all technical infrastructure of the Zurich Cantonal Police (Polycom, networks, data centers, building technology, etc.). For the technology/radio group we are looking for a team-oriented project manager in building technology with 100% tasks

  • Planning and implementation of systems in the area of building technology and other technical systems
  • Leading projects
  • Assisting in the execution of procurements
  • Assistance and/or coordination with the maintenance and lifecycle of systems in the area of building technology and other technical systems
  • On-site operations at our traffic bases and police posts profile
  • Completed training as a police officer (BP) with several years of professional experience
  • Completed basic technical training, ideally in electrical engineering or computer science
  • Sound knowledge of police processes and knowledge of the needs of police forces
  • Stylish appearance, level-appropriate communication and good assertiveness
  • Committed personality who enjoys working in a team
  • Reliable, independent and flexible way of working
  • Interest in new technologies
  • Willingness to undertake training and further education in the field of ICT
  • Impeccable reputation offer
  • We offer you a varied and meaningful job in a dynamic environment
  • A centrally located, ultra-modern workplace in the Zurich police and justice center including a staff restaurant and access to the in-house fitness center
  • Attractive employment conditions with a secure employer
  • The opportunity to make an impact in an exciting new challenge and shape the future together. Start of the position: on January 1st, 2027 or by appointment.
