Are you ready for an exciting challenge in the dynamic environment of an emergency department?

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

YOUR TASKS

  • Initial estimate and triagulation according to the Emergency Severity Index (ESI)
  • Care and care for emergency patients: inside in a dynamic environment
  • Care for a variety of illnesses and injuries – from entry to transfer or dismissal
  • Close cooperation with internal and external specialists for optimal patient care
  • Completion of the training modules of NDS HF Emergency Care at Z-INA Zurich

Your profile

  • Completed nursing diploma (with Swiss recognition or SRC registration)
  • At least 6 months of experience in acute care
  • High social competence and enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Independent and responsible working in a fast, varied environment
  • Very good observation ability, strength and flexibility
  • Proper knowledge of German is absolutely necessary

What You Expect in Us

You can expect a motivated, committed and multi-professional team in an advanced working environment. This is characterized by high quality of care, independence, flexibility and a sense of responsibility. We also offer our employees generous social benefits, sports offers, special insurance conditions and further education opportunities as part of our program.
