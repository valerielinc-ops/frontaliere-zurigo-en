Hirslanden stands for first-class, responsible and individual health care that puts people first. From birth to old age as well as from prevention to healing – at any time, physically and digitally – the Hirslanden Group is concentrating on the further development of the “Continuum of Care” together with private and public partners.

Our focus is not only on the well-being of our patients, but also on that of our employees.

YOUR TASKS

  • You will accompany the integration of new business units and actively participate in the development and development of their financial and controlling structures.
  • You analyze and optimize financial processes – from invoicing to receivables management to reporting.
  • You lead challenging financial and controlling projects and represent the interests of the CFO area in group-wide projects, for example when introducing new tariff systems.
  • You will develop sound decision-making bases for management, develop concrete recommendations for action and accompany their implementation.
  • You work closely with various specialist areas such as finance, controlling, revenue management, strategy and IT as well as with the clinics.

Your profile

  • You have a specialist or university degree in business administration or an equivalent education, ideally with a focus on health economics, finance or controlling.
  • You will bring professional experience in finance and controlling, preferably in healthcare. You understand the financial relationships and processes in hospitals and corporate structures.
  • You work independently and independently, are flexible and appreciate the variety of assignments at different locations in Switzerland as well as the regular exchange in the team.
  • You have strong analytical and conceptual skills, critically question existing processes, develop practical solutions and convincingly coordinate different interests.
  • You have a good understanding of financial systems and digital processes. Knowledge of SAP FI/CO is an advantage.
  • You communicate safely in German and English. Knowledge of French is an added plus.
