Very diverse & never boring Sometimes quite challenging, but never boring: In the EPI WohnWerk is no day like the other. For the people we accompany each and every one have their unique life history. Thanks to the cooperation in the interprofessional team, we are well positioned for our working day. Do you want to be part of our team? Tasks:

  • They develop, implement and optimize contemporary, holistic concepts and standards to ensure high-quality support, support and support for residents
  • They develop and implement innovative offers and structures for the continuous improvement of living area
  • They control and monitor management-relevant processes, including change management, take over budget responsibility and are responsible for planning, implementation and control
  • They lead the management team, consisting of team leaders living, professional and personnel
  • They promote a positive team culture and a close interdisciplinary cooperation taking into account the current organisational development process
  • They cooperate closely with internal and external contact persons, legal representations, assistance personnel as well as relatives and actively build networks Requirements:
  • They have completed an agogic or nursing training at tertiary level (HF/FH/BSc or MSc) as well as completed guidance training (e.g. CAS)
  • You have a well-founded knowledge of the accompaniment and support in the area of living for people with special needs and convince us with your strong leadership experiences
  • They possess an economic understanding and a safe handling of economic aspects, through their proactive action and holistic thinking they quickly recognize complex relationships
  • They are a winning personality with a safe appearance that can inspire our employees
  • Safe handling of client information systems, ERP software (abacus of advantage) and personnel deployment planning tools (PEP of advantage) as well as good MS office skills
  • Your knowledge of the UNBRK and the self-determination law of the canton of ZH complete your profile We offer:
  • A living environment that can be shaped with your own ideas
  • 5 weeks vacation, from 50. Age 6 weeks
  • Own pension fund with an above-average employer share of 60%
  • Extensive internal training and support for external training
  • Personal apartments, parking spaces and discounts at EPI Park Restaurant, pharmacy and half-tax or general subscription In the EPI WohnWerk, more than 200 adults with impairment, mostly in combination with epilepsy, find a home and a daily structure, the best possible care, care and medical care to the high age. The EPI WohnWerk is an operation of the Swiss Epilepsy Foundation. epi-wohnwerk.ch
