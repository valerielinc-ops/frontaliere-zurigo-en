Full description

Spital Männedorf – Your very special place of work. Whether a small “thank you” or a morning “grüezi” – we focus on real appreciation and humanity. The proximity in our house makes everyday life uncomplicated and allows patient-centered work at the highest level. In an environment where interdisciplinary cooperation at eye level is a matter of course, we offer individual treatments from primary medical care to rehabilitation.

The nature of your activity

  • Share responsibility – together with the nursing management – for ensuring and further developing the quality of care in the intensive care unit
  • Active participation, implementation and evaluation of nursing concepts for the continuous professionalization of nursing practice
  • Collaboration in interprofessional project groups together with experts, APN and other specialists for the further development of evidence-based care
  • Ensuring competent, patient-oriented care as well as regular assignments in direct care in all shifts
  • Accompaniment, promotion and support of students and employees in their professional development
  • Technical advice to the nursing team and planning and implementation of training and further education
  • Analysis, support and co-creation of highly complex care situations as well as advice for patients, relatives and employees
  • Work in an interdisciplinary intensive care unit with modern infrastructure and contemporary workplaces

The basis of your performance

  • Completed training as an expert intensive care NDS HF or equivalent qualification in intensive care; ideally supplemented by a bachelor's and master's degree in nursing or the willingness to complete a corresponding degree course
  • Pronounced interest in the further development of care processes and a good feeling for the needs of patients
  • High resilience and joy in challenging situations, combined with the ability to keep an overview even in demanding situations
  • Openness to innovations and motivation to actively shape changes
  • Empathetic, integrative personality with a high service orientation and strong communication skills as well as a great commitment to the nursing profession

The advantages of your position

  • In close cooperation with specialists and care experts, design APN You are actively developing the quality of care – especially in the intensive care unit
  • They work with a high degree of personal responsibility and independence and benefit from a wide range of opportunities for interdisciplinary networking.
  • They use a state-of-the-art clinic information system that digitally maps the entire treatment process and provides an up-to-date overview of all patient data at all times.

The reasons for your decision

We offer modern working conditions, promote internal further education and focus on professional exchange across all disciplines. The quiet location directly on the lake and the proximity to the vibrant city of Zurich round off your workplace. In addition, you can look forward to very special advantages.
