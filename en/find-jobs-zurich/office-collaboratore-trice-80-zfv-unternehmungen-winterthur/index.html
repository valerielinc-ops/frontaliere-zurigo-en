Role overview

The Restaurant Pioneer at the AXA Superblock in Winterthur consists of a public self-service restaurant with 334 indoor and 114 outdoor seats and a cafeteria with 266 seats. 700 guests are served here every day.

In addition, four daycare centers and an external operation in the region are supplied, various catering activities are carried out and the catering facilities are made available for special events.

It is cooked fresh on site with regional, ecologically well-produced and fair trade food.
