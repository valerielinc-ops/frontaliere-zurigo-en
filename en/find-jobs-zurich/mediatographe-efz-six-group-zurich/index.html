Mediatographer/EFZ

Zurich | 100% | Reference 8078

You burn thanks to your creative way for the design of vivid designs and videos? Are you interested in image editing programs, social media and marketing? Are you looking for a varied apprenticeship for 2027? Then an apprenticeship as a mediamatist with us is exactly the right thing for you!

During your teaching, you will get to know different teams and tasks. By regularly changing departments, you will gain versatile insights into the world of communication, marketing and project management. You will be accompanied and supported by experienced vocational trainers. As part of our learning group, you can also exchange ideas with other learners, learn from each other and gain unforgettable experiences together.

This is what awaits you

  • You design designs for brochures, presentations and other means of communication.
  • You develop creative ideas and support marketing and communication projects.
  • You will get exciting insights into the areas of communication, social media, marketing and project management.
  • Every 6 to 12 months you move to a new department and get to know different tasks and topics.
  • In the first year of apprenticeship, you spend the first few months in an instructive base year with an external partner.
  • You are part of a large learner community and meet your fellow learners both in everyday work and at joint events.

You bring that with you

  • Very good performance in secondary school A or an equivalent education.
  • Joy of language, communication and foreign languages.
  • Creativity as well as the motivation to contribute your own ideas.
  • Interconnected, logical and abstract thinking.
  • Team spirit and joy in working with others.
