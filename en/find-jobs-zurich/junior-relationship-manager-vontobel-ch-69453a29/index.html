Full description

Are you passionate about the financial world? For our Private Clients Employee Desk team, we are looking for a highly motivated and engaged Junior Relationship Manager.

What would your role be?

  • Take ownership and support the management of a client portfolio, putting the client’s needs in first place.
  • Act as a trusted advisor to clients and prospects by providing recommendations that encompass multiple products and services.
  • Develop in-depth understanding of client’s needs, preferences and overall wealth and be responsive to the changes in the business environment or clients’ needs.
  • Effectively manage a book of clients and act as a relationship lead accountable for the clients’ experience.
  • Leverage specialists, service officers and other expert resources to ensure the integrated delivery of investment fiduciary, credit, and banking solutions.

What are we looking for?

  • Previous experience in a similar position within the banking industry.
  • A client-focused individual with exceptional interpersonal skills.
  • A team player who can work under pressure.
  • Highly motivated and with an entrepreneurial spirit.
  • Fluency in English and German and effective communication skills.

What do we offer?

  • Top Zurich location with collaboration spaces (including rooftop terraces) and free lunch.
  • Friendly, diverse team in an open-office space with dancing walls.
  • All the latest technology to help you excel and advance.
  • Agile environment in how we think, the way we work and our trust for each other.
  • Flat hierarchy with collaboration on all levels.
  • Campus feeling and collaborative spirit.

We are looking for talented people, not just a CV that ticks all the boxes. Even if you do not have absolutely everything we have asked for, if this role sounds like a good fit for you, we encourage you to apply.

Why Vontobel? At Vontobel, you are building better futures for our clients and society, as well as for yourself. As a family-owned and publicly listed company with over 2,000 professionals based in 28 locations globally and headquartered in Zurich, we’re committed to long-term thinking and sustainability. Our diverse team values unique perspectives, and we hire individuals determined to deliver in a dynamic and changing world. Even if you don’t meet all the criteria, we encourage you to apply if you’re enthusiastic about the role. Do you want to be Vontobel?
