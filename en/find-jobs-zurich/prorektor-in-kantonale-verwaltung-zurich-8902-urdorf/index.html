Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School Limmattal Prorector/in 100% The cantonal school Limmattal is a school of maturity of the Knonauer Amt and Limmattal regions and is located in Urdorf. It comprises a two-year secondary school (7th/8th school year) and the subsequent short high school (9th-12th school year). All maturity profiles and a bilingual matur are offered. In the coming years, our school will be expanded by a branch in Affoltern am Albis. Therefore, we are looking for reinforcement as of January 1, 2027. The planned opening of the site is planned for the school year 2028/29.

  • Tasks As Vice Rector, you are a member of the school management and take on responsible planning, organization and management tasks. As project manager/project manager, you are responsible for the pedagogical development of the branch. They lead various specialist bodies and bear both the educational and the personnel responsibility. In this activity you work closely with the school management of the Cantonal School Limmattal. We are addressing applicants with a completed university degree (licensure / master), a teaching diploma for maturity schools or an equivalent education as well as extensive teaching experience at all levels of the high school curriculum. As a Prorector, you teach up to ten lessons per week. Experience in project management and organizational development is an advantage. It offers you an interesting and varied task in the dynamic environment of a middle school. With your dedicated commitment, you make an important contribution to a motivating school operation and contribute to the culture of our school. The Conditions of Employment are governed by the Staff Regulations of the Canton of Zurich. Please submit your application with the usual documents by the 25th at the latest. August 2026 electronically to bewerbungen.ksl@edu.zh.ch . There are internal applications. Information about the school can be found on our homepage www.kslzh.ch.

Pensum: 100%
