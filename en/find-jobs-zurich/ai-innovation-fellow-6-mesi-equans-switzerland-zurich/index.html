Equans Switzerland. Shaping the future together. Do you want to actively shape the digital future in Switzerland? As an AI Innovation Fellow, you work directly with various business functions and develop AI solutions that solve real challenges and create measurable added value for Equans Switzerland. From the identification of use cases to the implementation of productive solutions You will gain a unique insight into the practical application of Artificial Intelligence in the corporate environment.

You can move that with us.

  • Analysis of processes and identification of AI use cases with high business impact
  • Development, testing and implementation of AI solutions, workflows, automations and AI agents
  • Collaboration with specialist areas such as Finance, HR, Quality, Operational Excellence and other business teams
  • Measurement and evaluation of the impact achieved and continuous optimisation of existing solutions
  • Promoting AI application in the enterprise through training, documentation and knowledge building
  • Help build scalable standards and best practices for AI deployment
  • Accompanying pilot projects from idea to productive use

That's what makes you.

  • Ongoing or completed studies in business informatics, computer science, data science, business administration or a comparable degree programme
  • Enthusiasm for Artificial Intelligence, Automation and Digital Innovation
  • First experience with AI tools, automation, coding or low-code/no-code solutions
  • Demonstrable own projects in which processes have been improved, automated or new solutions have been developed
  • Analytical thinking and structured and independent working methods
  • High initiative, willingness to learn and implementation strength
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in words and writing
  • French is a plus

That's what we offer you.

  • Direct collaboration on real AI solutions with visible business impact
  • Collaboration with experienced executives and specialists from various areas of the company
  • High personal responsibility and quick assumption of responsibility for own projects
  • An innovative and dynamic environment with the latest AI technologies
  • Valuable practical experience, steep learning curve and attractive development opportunities at Equans Switzerland
