Sanatorium Kilchberg AG | online since 17.08.2026

We are looking for from 1. December 2026 or by agreement

Psychotherapy Unit Focus on Depression (German/English)

Additional benefit for carers in shift work: 37-hour week The Kilchberg Sanatorium offers you a fulfilling working environment with exciting development opportunities in a dynamic and appreciative team. Bring your strengths and shape the future of the clinic. On Station B2, you will find a benevolent, age-mixed team that supports each other and contributes its many strengths. Our motivating working atmosphere must be experienced.

Their tasks

  • Working in an open department with a focus on depression treatment and burnout in English
  • Individual treatment of mentally ill people with consultation and planning in an interdisciplinary team
  • Implementation of reference care and maintenance process
  • Management of inpatient nursing therapy group services
  • Work in shift service early service/late service/weekend
  • Support for the further development of the inpatient treatment setting and active participation in the implementation of new projects

Your profile

  • Degree as a Dipl. Nurse HF/FH/DNII (certificate of recognition for foreign diplomas)
  • High level of technical and social competence
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary team culture
  • Interest in developing further, participation in internal training
  • Flexibility, resilience and teamwork and humor
  • Solution-oriented working methods and networked thinking
  • Stylistic German in word and writing, good English language skills desired
  • High customer orientation with good manners

The Kilchberg Sanatorium is one of the most traditional psychiatric private clinics in Switzerland and looks back on almost 160 years of history. Only 15 minutes from the city centre of Zurich with beautiful views of Lake Zurich and the Alps. As a Listespital, we offer comprehensive psychiatric care for all insured persons and have inpatient and outpatient facilities in Kilchberg and Zurich. Our approximately 650 employees from over 25 professional groups are at the heart of the clinic and are committed to the best possible treatment of our patients. Become part of our team and shape the future of psychiatric care.
