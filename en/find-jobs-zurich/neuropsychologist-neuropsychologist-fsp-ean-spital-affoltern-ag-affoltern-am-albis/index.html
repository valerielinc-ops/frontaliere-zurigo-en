Full description

At Memory Clinic, we are a dynamic team of doctors and neuropsychologists. As a recognised training centre for neuropsychology, we value the interprofessional exchange and work together with various specialists. We are a member of the Swiss Memory Clinics (SMC). Their tasks

  • Neuropsychological examination focusing on cognitive diseases
  • Presentation of neuropsychological findings in the interprofessional team
  • Joint, interprofessional development of a medical diagnosis
  • Professional reporting of your profile
  • University degree in psychology and completed postgraduate training as a specialist psychologist for neuropsychology FSP / EAN
  • Professional experience in neuropsychological diagnosis in the field of geriatrics
  • They are accustomed to independent, structured work, but also appreciate the interprofessional exchange and cooperation in the team.
  • You are happy to participate in the training/supervision of aspiring neuropsychologists
  • You enjoy dealing with elderly patients
  • You are a friendly and flexible personality We offer
  • A pleasant working environment with flat hierarchical structures in a set up, motivated team in our Memory Clinic
  • A collegial working environment in an interdisciplinary team with regular professional exchange
  • Support in your personal development goals
  • Progressive Conditions of Employment Our Culture – Your benefits «Bi öis can mer no»: With us it is family and personal. As part of the Affoltern hospital team, you will also benefit from attractive employment conditions and numerous Fringe Benefits: Jokertag, shopping benefits and two subscriptions for free admissions to the FORTYSEVEN Wellness Spa are just a few examples of our Fringe Benefits. The Hospital Affoltern is the largest employer and training company in the Knonau Office. Around 550 employees work for the well-being of patients every day. Find out more about us in our portrait.
