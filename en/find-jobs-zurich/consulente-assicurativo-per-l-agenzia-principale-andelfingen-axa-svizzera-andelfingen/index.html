Shape your future – together with us!

To strengthen our successful team for the Andelfingen area, we are looking for a customer-oriented and negotiating personality with a lot of commitment. For lateral entrants and professionals with a local connection, we offer the perfect introduction with first-class training and development opportunities.

Whether an insurance professional or a career changer – start your career in an agency at AXA, the No. 1! We stand by our customers as a competent partner – in every situation of life. You too?

This is what awaits you

  • Passionate commitment to the customers of AXA
  • Solution-oriented and personal all-round advice (physical or digital) from insurance customers: inside
  • Maintaining and expanding existing customer relationships
  • Mediation of other products (financial and preventive care, health services) of AXA
  • Acquisition of new customers (using modern and digital tools and existing internal AXA processes)

You bring that with you

  • Completed apprenticeship and professional experience in the service sector or ideally in the insurance sector
  • Professionals and lateral entrants with a corresponding certificate of performance are welcome
  • Anchorage in the region
  • Convincing appearance and good articulation ability
  • Winning and resilient personality combined with a high customer and service orientation
  • High level of independence and flexibility
  • Style-confident knowledge of German in words and writing, other languages advantageous
  • Enjoying modern and contemporary digital tools

This is what we offer you

We create framework conditions that give you space to develop and fully exploit your potential. As a leading Swiss insurance company, we offer ambitious people attractive career opportunities and entrepreneurial leeway.

  • Discount of up to 30% on AXA products
  • 5 weeks holiday
  • Fleet discount/refund of half-tax subscription/GA (proportional)
  • Access to the Swibeco platform
  • Leeway for self-responsibility
  • Innovative structures, processes and modern tools
  • Sound, professional training and further education opportunities
