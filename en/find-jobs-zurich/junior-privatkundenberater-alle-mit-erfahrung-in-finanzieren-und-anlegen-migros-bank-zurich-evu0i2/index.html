Full description

With us, you can contribute your sales strength, actively shape customer relationships and help shape the customer advice of the future. We are looking for a sales-oriented personality with a passion for sales who recognizes opportunities, shows initiative and convinces with fresh ideas. Do you inspire people and enjoy achieving success together? Then we look for you.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Bank, Oerlikon Branch, Nansenstrasse 21, 8050 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You inspire our customers with competent, needs-oriented advice.
  • You expand existing customer relationships and gain new customers.
  • You recognize sales opportunities and successfully convert them into deals.
  • You achieve our ambitious goals with your own initiative, sales pleasure and team spirit.
  • You think entrepreneurially, act proactively and enjoy making a difference together in the team.

Requirements

  • min. 2 to 3 years: Customer Consulting / Retail Banking
  • Experience in private client consulting and good expertise in the areas of investing, financing and pension provision
  • Flair to actively expand customer relationships and maintain them in the long term
  • Experience in the successful acquisition of new customers and an open, positioned personality
  • Flair and joy in recognizing customer needs and inspiring them for the right financial solutions
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ)
  • German (flowing)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central workplace: Optimally accessible, centrally located workplace
  • Child care: up to CHF 300 per month per child to external child care
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Migros Bank: accounts, mortgages and financial services with preferential terms
  • Employee offers: Annual generous well-being credit

Contatto

Application and contact Manuela Meier-Pacozzi HR Business Partner LinkedIn

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition: After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
