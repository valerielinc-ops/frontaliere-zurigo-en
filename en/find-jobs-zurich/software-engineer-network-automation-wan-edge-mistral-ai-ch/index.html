The Role As a Software Engineer, Network Automation - WAN & Edge, you will design, automate, and operate resilient connectivity between data centers, cloud environments, external networks, and the internet. Your work will span routing, firewalls, traffic engineering, and production troubleshooting. This is a hands-on role for an experienced network engineer who can build, test, debug, and review production-grade automation. Networking depth and strong software-engineering judgment matter more than familiarity with any particular language, platform, or tool.

What You Will Do

  • Design and evolve resilient inter-site, cloud, and internet connectivity for large-scale AI infrastructure.
  • Own external BGP policy and traffic engineering at the edge, including route filtering, failover, and capacity planning.
  • Build API-driven automation for edge routing, firewalls, VPNs, carrier connectivity, and network segmentation.
  • Coordinate with carriers, cloud connectivity providers, colocation partners, and vendors on design, provisioning, incident escalation, and service improvement.
  • Design safe change workflows with automated validation and resilience testing, staged rollouts, observability, rollback, and recovery.
  • Participate in on-call rotation, troubleshoot active WAN and edge incidents, restore service, and turn root causes into lasting improvements.
  • Review designs and code, explain networking trade-offs, and help raise network engineering quality across the organization.

What We’re Looking For

  • Deep, hands-on WAN and edge networking experience across internet connectivity, data-center interconnect, private WAN, or cloud networking.
  • A track record of taking external BGP designs from whiteboard to production and debugging them during production incidents.
  • Experience integrating and troubleshooting stateful firewalls, NAT, asymmetric routing, and redundant edge designs on at least one major routing or firewall platform.
  • Experience building or operating production network automation using network-management APIs and data models. You understand desired state and idempotency; relevant technologies might include REST, NETCONF, gNMI/OpenConfig, Ansible, or Terraform.
  • Strong software-engineering fundamentals in a general-purpose language. You can design, test, debug, and review production systems and are willing to become productive in Go.
  • Operational troubleshooting experience using routing and flow state, packet captures, link health, telemetry, and logs to diagnose production incidents.
  • Clear, pragmatic communication and effective stakeholder management. You can translate technical needs into connectivity designs and provider requirements, define acceptance criteria and service-level expectations, and provide technical leadership.

Nice to Have

  • Experience with FortiGate, FortiManager, or comparable edge and firewall platforms.
  • Deeper specialization in external connectivity, such as public-cloud or hybrid-cloud interconnect, or internet transit and peering.
  • Exposure to edge security and scale techniques such as RPKI and route-origin validation, DDoS mitigation, or global traffic engineering.
  • Familiarity with streaming telemetry, network source-of-truth systems, and configuration-compliance tooling.
  • Experience reading, reviewing, or contributing to Go code.
  • Familiarity with Kubernetes controllers, operators, or other declarative, reconciliation-based systems.
