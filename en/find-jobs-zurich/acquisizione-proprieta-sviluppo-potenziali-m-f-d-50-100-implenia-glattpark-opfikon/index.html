Full description

As a leading Swiss construction and real estate service provider, Implenia develops, realizes and manages living spaces, working environments and infrastructure for future generations in Switzerland and Germany. Implenia also offers tunnelling and related infrastructure projects in other markets. The company combines the know-how of highly qualified consulting, development, planning and execution units under one roof into an integrated, multinationally leading construction and real estate service provider. Headquartered in Opfikon near Zurich, Implenia employs more than 8,000 people (FTE) across Europe and achieved a turnover of CHF 3.5 billion in 2025. More information at implenia.com

To strengthen her Real Estate Transaction team in our “Buildings” division, Jelena Radovic is looking for a proactive and entrepreneurial personality as an acquirer of real estate and development potential (m/f/d), 50-100%.

With your sense of opportunity, your network and your entrepreneurial thinking, you identify attractive development areas and plots for Implenia’s future projects. Your future place of work is Opfikon, your market area focuses on the Aargau, northwestern Switzerland and the Zurich region.

You move that

  • You actively identify and acquire land, areas and development potential with a focus on the Canton of Aargau as well as in the northwestern Switzerland and the Zurich region
  • Proactively approach landowners, farmers and communities and build long-term relationships with relevant market participants
  • You maintain and expand your personal network to municipal authorities, brokers, architecture offices, notaries, trustees and other decision-makers
  • You evaluate potential acquisition properties in an initial assessment and identify opportunities for real estate and site developments at an early stage
  • You hand over qualified opportunities to the transaction team and, if necessary, support the processing of acquisition cases together with internal and external specialists

This is why you succeed

  • You have a commercial education or come from the planning sector and bring at least three years of experience in the real estate industry
  • You think in a networked way, recognize development potential at an early stage and have a strong understanding of economic relationships in the real estate market
  • With your communicative and winning nature, you can build sustainable relationships with landowners, communities and other market participants.
  • Ideally, you will have additional knowledge in the areas of property valuation, real estate transactions, real estate management, spatial planning or permitting.
  • You communicate stylishly in German, have good knowledge of English and ideally already bring a network in the Aargau

This is what we offer you

  • A multicultural working environment with the chance to shape a future worth living
  • State-of-the-art technologies and a contemporary workplace at a sustainable company
  • A company that promotes talent and the training of its employees
  • The security of a financially healthy, broad-based group with strong roots in Switzerland
  • Flexible working models and modern work equipment with home office possibility
  • An exciting selection of benefits:
  • 5 weeks holidays and 9 paid bridge days
  • an in-house pension fund with very good coverage and 3 contribution levels
  • numerous discounts on well-known suppliers of products and services
