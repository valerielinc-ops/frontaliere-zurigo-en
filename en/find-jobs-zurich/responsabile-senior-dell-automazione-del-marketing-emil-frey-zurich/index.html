What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a family-owned Swiss company with over 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare parts and accessories as well as the provision of financial services.

Emil Frey Digital AG is part of the Emil Frey Group and is a full service communications service provider for the automotive industry and responsible for digitisation within the Emil Frey Group. To strengthen our team in Zurich Oerlikon we are looking for a Senior Marketing Automation Manager with immediate or by agreement your role As a Senior Marketing Automation Manager you play a central role at the interface between marketing, data and technology.

As an in-house agency of the Emil Frey Group, we support importers and dealer networks in the design and implementation of their data-driven customer communication. You are responsible for expanding our marketing automation landscape with a clear Big-Picture approach: you think in consistent customer journeys and ecosystems instead of in ad hoc actions. We are looking for a personality that inspires stakeholders for new ways, speaks to developers and data specialists at eye level and does not shy away from entering the technology itself in the case of data flows and system logics.
