For our PhysioA.R.T. At the centre in Regensdorf, we are looking for a strong-leading personality who will initially take over the site management as part of an interim mandate – with responsibility, room for manoeuvre and the prospect of further development together with us. You lead and develop our team, ensure quality and professional practices and actively shape the further development of our competence center. We do not see the interim as a mere deputy, but as an opportunity: for you to take over leadership and design with us – and for us to find out together what long-term perspective can result from it. Tasks & Responsibilities

  • · Leadership, motivation and further development of the physiotherapy team as main task
  • · Creating an appreciative, committed and performance-oriented team culture
  • · Ensuring high treatment quality and professional practices
  • · Overview of team, resources, utilization and organization
  • · Co-design of structures and treatment offers
  • · Sovereign representation of the centre towards doctors and partners
  • · Continued clinical activity and thus close to patients and team
  • Requirements

  • · Vocational training permit (BAB) Canton of Zurich must be available
  • · Several years of professional experience in Switzerland
  • · Proven leadership experience and joy in developing people
  • · Very good communication skills and stylish, binding appearance
  • · High value on loyalty and responsibility
  • Personal responsibility, decision-making strength and organizational talent
  • · High professional standards and evidence-based work
  • Nice-to-haves

  • · Specialization in MSK, sports physiotherapy, lymphology or pelvic floor therapy

In our innovative and modern practice, experts for physiotherapy work in an evidence-oriented manner and offer our patients therapy, training offers and prevention for health. The practice is embedded in the health centre of the Regensdorf Centre (shopping centre) with excellent access to public transport. There is also an interprofessional exchange with the family doctors (same floor), an orthodontist and complementary medical TCM offer. A beautiful working environment with state-of-the-art infrastructure (e.g. digitally supported training infrastructure, Sensopro, InBody, AI tracked exergame etc.) and an excellent working environment should offer you the prerequisite that you can develop professionally and personally.
