Full description

The ParaWG residential training in Schenkon, Nottwil and Zurich offers paraplegic people as well as people with cross-sectional symptoms specific learning fields with the aim of living independently and self-determined. ParaWG employees support clients in temporary residential training in promoting their self-competence and self-management.

Tasks: • Responsibility for the resource- and solution-oriented everyday support of people with paraplegia or cross-sectional syndromes during occupational and social integration • Promoting the independence of clients by including the system of origin and helpers and integration into society • Responsibility for planning, developing, implementing and reflecting on measures as well as training units that enable clients to achieve their goals during residential training • Reference work for several clients during residential training at the ParaWG and cooperation with their relatives • Preparation of documentation for the cost carriers • Work in shift work and partly also on weekends as well as takeover of pikett services Requirements • Training as a social pedagogue/in HF/FH or specialist care EFZ with several years of professional experience and preferably experience in dealing with people with paraplegia • Ideally completed IVR1 course or the willingness to complete it • Open, flexible and team capable personality who appreciates interdisciplinary collaboration and a dynamic environment • Flexibility in terms of working hours (e.g. shift work, weekend) • Resilience in dealing with strokes of fate We offer: You not only expect attractive employment conditions, but also numerous other advantages. Look forward to meaningful work in a modern working environment, flexible working hours and a wide range of further education opportunities. Come and experience our unique spirit. More information on parplegie.ch/benefits. The Swiss Paraplegic Centre in Nottwil is a private, nationally and internationally recognized specialist clinic for cross-sectional, back and respiratory medicine. As an acute and rehabilitation clinic, the centre offers its patients comprehensive care and supports them throughout their lives. The clinic is part of the Swiss Paraplegic Group, which provides a globally unique service network for the holistic rehabilitation of people with paraplegia.
