Original description

We are committed to health – because health is everything. We are committed to this, also as an employer. We are convinced: Only in a healthy working world do the best talents feel comfortable and move forward: professionally and personally. For that we give everything. This is how you shape health

  • Self-responsible management of a team consisting of 8 to 10 employees
  • Personal and professional development of the team
  • Responsibility for reviewing and handling the performance cases with the aim of achieving the best customer satisfaction
  • Networking and coordination with other departments, departments and social and private insurance
  • Responsibility for quality assurance and efficient functioning

This sets you apart

  • Commercial or equivalent basic training completed
  • Several years of professional experience in a similar position, preferably in the health/social insurance sector
  • First leadership experience is beneficial
  • Dedication and your entrepreneurial and customer-oriented thinking and action round off your profile
  • Impeccable oral and written expression in German, good knowledge of English, additional language skills are an advantage

Why SWICA

  • We build on humanity. That is why we focus on openness, respect and trust in our everyday work. We take on responsibility and think in terms of solutions – together, for our customers. This is also why we have been number 1 in customer satisfaction for many years.
  • We trust you and give you space. With annual working hours, at least 5 weeks holidays and on request also holiday purchase. With part-time models and generous home office within the scope of the possibilities of the job, you make your everyday life largely flexible.
  • We value and reward your work – with a market-based wage, up to 1,500 francs annual fee for personal wishes and a generous participation in the basic and additional insurance for you and your family.
  • We offer you prospects. With a generous training regulations, we support your professional and personal development – for your role today and for your goals of tomorrow.
  • Because health is everything. With our company health management and region-specific offers, we take care of your well-being. And if it comes otherwise: 100% net wage continued payment.
  • We think long term. With three savings plans, pensum-dependent coordination deduction and benefits well above the legal minimum, you ensure good for your future.

Vanessa Landi, HR Recruiting, is happy to provide you with information.
