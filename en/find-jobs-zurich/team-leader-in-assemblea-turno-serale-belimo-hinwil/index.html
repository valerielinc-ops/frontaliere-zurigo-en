Summary

You can not only deal with tools, but also with people?

Then you are golden with us! For our assembly department, we are looking for a group leader (m/f/d) for the evening shift (14.00 - 23.00) who coordinates several small teams with an overview, heart and humor – and who can help us when it matters.

What you will do

  • Personnel and professional management in several assembly groups
  • Productive work in the lines, equipment, logistics
  • Responsibility for performance, quality and motivation of the subordinate employees feedback of production orders
  • Capacity and personnel deployment planning in consultation with day group leaders
  • Ensure availability and functionality of equipment and equipment
  • Monitor the quality, deadlines and services provided as well as the workflows Change and optimization work
  • Enforcement of occupational safety regulations and compliance with order and cleanliness

Who you are

  • Technical apprenticeship, ideally as a polymechanic, electromechanic or similar technical training
  • Some years of management experience as well as experience in assembly
  • High problem-solving skills and strong social skills, goal- and performance-oriented working methods
  • Very good knowledge of German (minor level Cl)
  • Good PC knowledge (MS Office, SAP)
  • Medical training or willingness to undertake such training
  • Enjoy teamwork with day and night group leaders
  • High self-motivation and empathy, coupled with high quality awareness
  • Enjoyment of active participation in assembly and logistics tasks

Who we are

At Belimo, we are proud to make our company a rewarding place to work. Our leaders are value-driven and promote new ideas in their teams around the world. Through our passion, our employees not only create amazing products, but also revolutionize the HVAC industry. Investing in our employees is at the heart of Belimo when it comes to engaging diverse creative talents to build an industry-leading company. The success of our corporate brand is based on the competence and commitment of each individual
