Full description

As a raw material manager for nuts, dried fruits and rice, you are the central specialist for sustainability in these raw materials during a maternity representation. Together with your stakeholders, you ensure ongoing activities and pragmatically advance the implementation of our sustainability goals.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Industrie AG, Migros Industrie AG – Zurich, Limmatstrasse 152, 8005 Zurich

Mansioni

  • You are the single point of contact for sustainability around nuts, dried fruits and rice and ensure professional continuity during the representation period.
  • Together with internal stakeholders, you implement the sustainability goals for your raw materials and coordinate the associated activities.
  • You supervise ongoing sustainability projects and continue to lead prioritized work packages independently.
  • You will advise on raw material-specific requirements and procurement principles and contribute your specialist perspective to their further development.
  • You work closely with purchasing, sustainability functions and the relevant companies of the Migros Group.
  • You represent the Migros industry in raw material-specific committees and prepare the basis for decision-making and status updates.

Requirements

  • At least 3 years of professional experience: in the agricultural or food sector, ideally related to nuts, dried fruits, rice or comparable global supply chains
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): in agricultural sciences, sustainability, environmental sciences or a comparable discipline
  • In-depth knowledge and practical experience in the field of sustainability, in particular in the implementation of goals, standards or supply chain projects.
  • Independent, structured working methods as well as strong skills in project management, coordination and stakeholder management.
  • You have a pragmatic, committed and communication-strong personality and can quickly familiarize yourself with existing topics, prioritize them and take on responsibility.
  • Experience with plant raw materials and understanding of sustainability risks and requirements in international supply chains.
  • Experience in purchasing or procurement and in cooperation with commercial functions.
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • English (good knowledge)
  • French (advantageous)
