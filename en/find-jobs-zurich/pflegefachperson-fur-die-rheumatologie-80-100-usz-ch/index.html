On the bedside ward of rheumatology, patients with various diseases are treated. The spectrum ranges from highly acute inflammatory autoimmune diseases, which require rapid inpatient care, to chronic pain patients who are admitted for an intensive exercise and rehabilitation program. In addition, eye patients and patients with dermatological and other internistic diseases are cared for.

Your main tasks

  • Patient-oriented care based on the definition of professional care, the care process and individual care
  • Co-creation of the patient process in an interdisciplinary and interprofessional environment
  • Engaged participation in the further development of the nursing profession
  • Cooperation with persons involved in leadership, education and professional development

Your profile

  • You have a degree as a nurse / nurse specialist HF / FH and already gained professional experience in the acute hospital
  • You enjoy working together as well as shaping and maintaining relationships with highly inflammatory autoimmune patients as well as chronically ill people, but also assume responsibility independently
  • You feel comfortable in an innovative and development-oriented environment and are ready to actively shape it
  • You develop your personal potential through self-reflection
