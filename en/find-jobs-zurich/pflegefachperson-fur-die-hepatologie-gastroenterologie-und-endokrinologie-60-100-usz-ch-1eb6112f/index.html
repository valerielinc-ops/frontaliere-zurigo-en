We are a department with a focus on Hepatology, Gastroenterology and Endocrinology. We look after patients with inflammatory bowel diseases, metabolic diseases and patients with cirrhosis of the liver who are waiting for a transplant.

More than gastro. More than routine.

You think in gastroenterology only bowel movements? We think of high-level medicine, dynamics and teamwork. On our ward we look after patients with complex gastroenterological, hepatological and endocrinological diseases. This requires clinical thinking, technical know-how and rapid response – comparable to a surgical department.

Your work day? Everything but 08/15

  • Care of patients: women with IBD, rare metabolic diseases and cirrhosis of the liver until transplantation listing
  • Rapidly changing situations in which your power of observation counts
  • Close interprofessional cooperation in acute and complex care situations
  • Care with responsibility, ambition and development opportunities

Our team We are a humorous, open and supportive team – mixed in age, experience and skill levels. We laugh a lot, help each other and pull together in challenging situations.
