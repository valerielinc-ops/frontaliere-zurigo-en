# ICT System Manager

Il tuo futuro inizia

As a system manager, you are the central hub in the operational operation (24/7) of business-critical ICT services: You work closely with operations, engineering, suppliers and internal and external customers. You combine methodical event, incident and problem management with an eye for the whole – including continuous optimization of processes and tools.

  • Proactive and continuous monitoring of ICT services, networks and applications via central observability dashboards
  • Event analysis, qualification, triagulation and correlation of system messages and alarms to avoid escalations
  • Anomaly detection, early identification of deviations in normal operation to prevent failures
  • Ensure stakeholder communication (internal / external) incl. Status updates, decision-making basis and follow-up
  • Analyse disturbances and together with engineering/operation derive sustainable measures (problem/root-cause approaches)
  • Lead in critical situations calmly, clearly and bindingly
  • Working in 24/7 operation, high reliability and unrestricted readiness for shift work (night, weekend, public holidays)

Your skills

  • Several years of experience in ICT service operations or IT service management, ideally in incident/major incident management
  • Completed training/further training in the ICT environment (e.g. computer science, cloud, systems engineering/network) or corresponding long-term professional experience in IT
  • Experience working with modern monitoring and observability platforms (e.g. Grafana, Splunk, or Prometheus)
  • Good knowledge of ITIL processes, especially in event, incident, change and problem management
  • Strong analytical skills, networked thinking and a structured approach to complex disruptions
  • Precise, addressable language and communication both technically towards engineers and service-oriented towards management
  • Very good German as well as good knowledge of English and French
  • Hands-on mentality: you tackle, prioritize pragmatically and ensure consistent implementation
  • Experience with continuous improvement (processes, tooling, automation) and high quality standards
  • Readiness for regular shift work (24/7)
  • Ticket

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. Benefits.
