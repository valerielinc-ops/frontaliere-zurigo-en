Your team

You will become part of a dedicated trust team at PwC Switzerland that accompanies national and international customers in a dynamic and digital environment. Together, she oversees demanding mandates for SMEs in different sectors and legal forms, working closely with specialists from other PwC departments. In the team, knowledge is actively shared, responsibility is assumed and cooperation is given priority. You can expect a professional environment with short decision-making paths, high quality orientation and a strong coaching and feedback culture. Your impact

  • Take on the independent support of a versatile customer portfolio with SMEs from a wide range of industries and legal forms as well as branches of international customers: inside and develop towards self-responsible mandate management.
  • Advise and support customers comprehensively in the areas of financial, debtor and creditor accounting as well as tax issues – in close cooperation with our tax experts.
  • Take on external assignments in finance and accounting at national and international companies.
  • Actively contribute to the knowledge transfer in the team, especially support younger colleagues in technical issues and promote a collaborative learning culture.
  • Participate in the acquisition of new customers and the creation of offers and drive the continuous optimization and digitalization of processes through modern technologies and tools.

Your skill set

  • Completed further education with professional certificate or degree in business administration HF/FH with a focus on accounting & controlling; current or completed training as a dipl. Trust Expert:in is an advantage.
  • at least three years of professional experience in a comparable environment
  • First experience in the self-responsible management of demanding mandates is an advantage.
  • German as a mother tongue and very good knowledge of English.
  • Analytical, exact and results-oriented working methods and behaviour that is open, team-oriented, responsible and flexible.
  • Motivation to lead a small team in the future and take on coaching tasks.
  • High IT enthusiasm, a strong affinity for modern technologies and a professional, service-oriented appearance.
