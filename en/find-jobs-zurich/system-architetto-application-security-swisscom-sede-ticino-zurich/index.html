System Architect Application Security

Your future starts here You are part of our B2B Application Security Team, which works according to agile methods and is passionate about the challenges in the field of IT security.

  • Responsibility for the architecture governance of our Agile Release Train (ART) as well as leadership of our Architecture Circle
  • Technical leadership as well as coaching and mentoring of engineers and architects
  • Design, development, and continuous enhancement of highly available and scalable system architectures in line with company and customer requirements
  • Close collaboration with engineering, operations, and architecture teams to implement holistic end-to-end architecture solutions
  • Leading migration initiatives and ensuring a smooth transformation of existing system landscapes
  • Definition, documentation, and establishment of architecture standards, best practices, and governance models within our ART

Your skills

  • Passion for architecture, high self-motivation, and a pronounced hands-on mentality combined with a strong systems engineering background
  • Fluency in German and English
  • Experience implementing security controls to ensure holistic system security and reliability, for example in the areas of Security & Infrastructure as Code, Identity & Access Management (IAM), Key Management Systems (KMS), Security Monitoring and Auditing
  • Experience transforming architectures and leading complex migrations across all layers of the technology stack
  • Experience developing platforms and automating through software development and the use of Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) and CI/CD principles
  • Demonstrable experience as an IT architect with the ability to design products from the ground up, structure their development, and take responsibility for end-to-end implementation

At our company, you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or from home. In doing so, you will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a wide range of attractive benefits that will enhance your work experience. These include a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Benefits.
