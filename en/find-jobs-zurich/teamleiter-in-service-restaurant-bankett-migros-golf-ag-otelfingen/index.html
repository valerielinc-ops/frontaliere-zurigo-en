Full description

Do you burn for excellent service and warm hospitality? Then this position is perfect for you! Inspire and look after our guests in our golf park with your commitment.

Mansioni

  • Operational responsibility for day-to-day business in the area of restaurant and banquet service including active participation in service
  • Planning, management and operational management of the restaurant and banquet service team taking into account load, quality and cost-effectiveness
  • Ensuring a professional guest experience, especially for banquets, events and company events, in close cooperation with the Head of Gastronomy & Events and the interface partners
  • Planning and securing of cash processes as well as administrative processes and systems
  • Management of procurement and storage management of drinks and snacks
  • Planning and further development of the Beverage competence and the offer
  • Planning and ensuring quality, hygiene and safety in service in compliance with legal requirements and standards

Requirements

Professional experience: 2 to 3 years

  • Completed basic or further education: in the catering or hotel industry
  • Communication and team skills
  • Customer-oriented, open and friendly personality
  • Readiness for regular evening and weekend assignments in the main season from April to October; closed over Christmas and New Year
  • Driving licence Cat. B

German (very good knowledge)
