Team leader: in HR consulting 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Team leader: in HR consulting Zurich Altstetten / Bellinzona / Work Smart, November 1st, 2026 or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. A diverse and varied area of ​​responsibility awaits you in a team that is characterized by a high level of service orientation and great commitment. We advise managers personally, digitally and by telephone on all questions relating to employment conditions (GAV and OR) and the application of guidelines. As a member of our HR Consulting & Personnel Policy management team, you will make a significant contribution to the further development and design of our department. You are flexible and work regularly in Zurich and Bellinzona as well as within the framework of Work Smart. You can find benefits such as free GA and discounted travel abroad

  • on our website under Benefits. You can move that.
  • As an HR consulting team leader, you will lead a team of 10 HR consultants at the Zurich Altstetten and Bellinzona locations. You are responsible for professional, high-quality advice for managers in the divisions and corporate areas, mostly in the Zurich, Eastern Switzerland and Ticino regions. The focus is on labor and personnel law issues as well as supporting reorganizations.
  • Together with your team, you ensure efficient work processes as well as the maintenance and continuous expansion of specialist knowledge. You ensure high quality advice, optimal use of resources and continually develop HR consulting and its processes.
  • You are close to the managers and seek regular communication with the line. You will identify needs and developments at an early stage and position HR as a competent and solution-oriented partner.
  • You lead HR projects or sub-projects and ensure the HR implementation of reorganizations and organizational changes.
  • As a member of the HR consulting & personnel policy management team, you will actively shape the further development of HR consulting. You contribute to consistent, high-quality advice throughout Switzerland and to the further development of the product portfolio. This is what you bring with you.
  • You have strong consulting, communication and social skills and are convincing both verbally and in writing.
  • Your backpack includes a completed university degree in business administration or psychology as well as further training in human resources (e.g. MAS, EMBA, HR manager or comparable).
  • You have several years of management experience as well as solid professional experience in HR consulting, ideally in a large company.
  • You have very good knowledge of labor law as well as experience in change and project management.
  • Your native language is German. You also have fluent and business fluent Italian skills (level C1). Good knowledge of French is an advantage. Since we operate throughout Switzerland, you value collaboration in a multilingual environment and communicate appropriately with different stakeholders.
