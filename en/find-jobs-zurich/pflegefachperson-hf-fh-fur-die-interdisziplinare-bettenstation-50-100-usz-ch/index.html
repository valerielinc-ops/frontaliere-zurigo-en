Full description

Experience diversity. Design care.
You want to work in an environment that challenges, encourages and inspires you? You appreciate the diversity of different medical fields, are particularly interested in internal medicine, like to work interprofessionally and want to develop professionally and personally?
Then you are exactly right with us.
You do not want to commit yourself in the long term? We also offer temporary, flexible employment – without changing locations. You work on our bed station, get to know the team and the processes and at the same time benefit from the flexibility of a temporary employment. >The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • No day is like the other: On our interdisciplinary bed ward with a focus on internal medicine, you look after patients from different medical fields. You take on challenging nursing tasks and continuously develop your skills in dealing with a wide range of diseases and modern medical technology. • You work in the nursing tandem with health professionals and together with students and interns. You are part of a dedicated team that shares knowledge, learns from each other and supports each other. • Your ideas, experience and expertise are welcome – you can actively shape the further development of our care. Requirements • You are a qualified nurse HF or FH and meet people with expertise, empathy and commitment. • You keep an overview even in challenging situations, enjoy working independently and at the same time appreciate the cooperation in the team. • You are curious, open to change and want to continuously develop professionally. • You are happy to share your knowledge and enjoy accompanying students and trainees on their learning path. We offer: We are convinced that excellent care is created where people learn from each other, take responsibility and contribute their different strengths. We are waiting for you: • Structured and individual training • the possibility of temporary, flexible employment on our station – without changing stations • A motivated, appreciative and collaborative team • diverse development and career opportunities in nursing, education or management • the opportunity to bring findings from nursing science and university medicine into practice • a culture in which co-thinking, co-shaping and co-decision are expressly desired
