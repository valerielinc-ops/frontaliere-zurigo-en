Full description

To strengthen the team, we are looking for a social pedagogue in the role of hoarding management in the supervision of our day school as of 1 November 2026 or by appointment, among other things, you take over the responsibility and organization of the internal school BeFrei offers (further tasks in delegation of the LB). We wish you an experienced, committed, carrier- and team-capable specialist, who has pleasure in resource-oriented and interdisciplinary work in a day school. In the regular operation you work in principle 4 days/week as well as during the school holidays 3 weeks.

About us

The school district of Letzi includes the 9th district with the districts of the city of Albisrieden, Altstetten and Grünau, with a total of 16 schools. In our day school up to 180 children aged 4-13 are cared for daily. The school unit consists of 4 kindergarten classes and 11 primary school classes. We are an inclusive quiMS school with children from all over the world.
