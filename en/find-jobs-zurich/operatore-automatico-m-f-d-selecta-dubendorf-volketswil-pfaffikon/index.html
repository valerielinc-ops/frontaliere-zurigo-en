Automatic service provider (m/f/d) 100% The Selecta Group, headquartered in Switzerland since 1957, is Europe’s leading provider of coffee and innovative food tech solutions in the workplace and in public spaces. Every day, we serve more than 10 million people in 16 European countries with premium coffee and drinks, snacks and fresh meals. Our success with an annual turnover of 1.4 billion euros is due to our approx. 6,000 highly qualified, committed and passionate Selecta employees: women who create millions of moments of joy for our customers every day: women and their consumers: women. Sustainability is an integral part of our business and focuses on the key areas where we can make a positive contribution. Place of work Region Dübendorf / Volketswil / Pfäffikon Start of work immediately or by agreement Main tasks

  • You will manage our catering areas and vending solutions at our local customers.
  • You make sure that our break zones are clean, well-kept, welcoming and optimally filled.
  • You fill vending machines and food stations as needed and pay attention to order, freshness and quality.
  • You recognize the needs of our customers and guests and act service-oriented.
  • You approach customers and guests openly, friendly and communicatively.
  • You work independently on your tour and provide a reliable cleaning and filling service in the application area. Training
  • An education or experience in the hospitality industry, retail trade or in the food industry is advantageous.
  • Also motivated lateral entrants with pleasure in customer contact and practical activities are welcome. Profile
  • You have the driver's license Kat. B with manual gear and find your way quickly in new areas of application.
  • You work independently, reliably and structured and organize your tours efficiently.
  • You are flexible, resilient and keep calm and overview even with high workloads or short-term changes.
  • You enjoy practical work, actively participate and ensure clean, well-maintained vending machines and break zones.
  • You have good knowledge of German in words and writing.
  • Friendly manners, a well-groomed appearance and a positive attitude are a matter of course for you.
