Full description

This workplace includes the "Münzhof" customer restaurant, twelve VIP lunch rooms, services for the management floor and the counter hall. The meeting place in Zurich creates an inviting meeting place for UBS clients, employees, executives and event guests. Our team delights an average of 12 guests every day with a wide range of products – from fresh regional gourmet cuisine to high-quality, discreet service and events for up to 200 people. Are you our new team member who will support us as a sous chef as of 1 January 2026 or by appointment? You will work from Monday to Friday from 06:00 a.m. to 03:00 p.m. For events that can be planned, you will occasionally be on duty in the evening. Your task menu:

  • Organization and management of the entire kitchen area hand in hand with the kitchen management
  • Ensuring high-quality food at a constant 16-point GaultMillau level
  • Development of an innovative culinary concept for our restaurant (Breakfast & Lunch à la carte) as well as for exclusive events (Flying & Seated Dinner)
  • Participation in the scheduling, training and further development of our kitchen team with and support of our apprentices
  • Participation in team and management meetings
  • Collaboration in all positions – from the perfect mise en place to the perfect presentation
  • Analyze guest needs and current gastronomic trends
  • Support with calculations and formulations
  • Responsibility for the receipt of goods, professional warehouse management and administrative tasks
  • We exemplify our ZFV leadership model "We make others successful" and promote a team culture based on trust, courage, transparency and joy
  • Organization and strict implementation of all quality standards and ZFV guidelines regarding hygiene (HACCP) and occupational safety
  • Understand, represent and actively drive vision and strategy as well as pursue, prioritize and measure their implementation
  • Participation in the implementation of the ZFV sustainability and environmental strategy Your recipe for success:
  • Completed training as a cook EFZ or basic gastronomic training EFZ with corresponding additional training
  • At least 8 years of in-depth professional experience in Michelin-starred gastronomy or the leading luxury hotel industry, including at least 2 years in a management role as a sous chef
  • Experience in training and supporting apprentices
  • Fluent in written and spoken German as well as an absolutely impeccable reputation
  • Agile and innovative mindset
  • Very high quality orientation as well as in-depth product knowledge of high-end ingredients, sensory properties, textures and modern techniques
  • Consistent focus on the perfect guest experience paired with economic thinking and goal-oriented action
  • Strong ability to work in a team, strong leadership skills and a high willingness to change and learn What we offer you: We create teams that support each other and in which you are encouraged. We work together and focus on everything that is good for you. Among other things, we attach great importance to pay transparency and equal pay. For this position, we offer an annual salary of between CHF 60,000 and CHF 78,000 (incl. 13. Monthly salary). If you would like to share your passion for gastronomy with us, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
