For our specialized maintenance workshop in Dietikon, we are looking for a technically skilled person for the maintenance of our shunting locomotives. In this function, you combine practical workshop work, repairs and diagnosis on the vehicles (about 80%) with the technical support of our operational engineering (about 20%). We offer you a varied turntable function with practical work on shunting locomotives and insight into operational engineering. In addition, we support your professional development through targeted further education offers. We are an attractive employer and offer many benefits such as extra child allowances, free 2nd class GA and discounted travel abroad - also for the family. We look forward to seeing you from 22 to 24. 2026 in our SBB Berufswelten Café at Zurich Central Station. All information can be found at sbb.ch/berufsweltencafe. You can move that. Workshop, support and maintenance of shunting locomotives (about 80%)

  • In the preventive maintenance of diesel and hybrid locomotives, you test vehicle-specific systems such as radio remote controls, train radio systems, compressed air and brake systems as well as train protection systems and their components.
  • You perform functional checks and replace components as needed
  • Conversions and modifications of prototypes are carried out in coordination with engineering.
  • With your technical know-how, you systematically search for faults and use computer-aided diagnostic systems to correct malfunctions on diesel engines, exhaust aftertreatment systems, hydraulics and electrical control technology.
  • You replace and repair mechanical, hydraulic and electronic assemblies (e.g. axle transmission, compressors, bogie components, pumps, power converters) and carry out checks and approvals of the vehicles in accordance with the applicable maintenance and safety guidelines.

Operational engineering and technical support (about 20%)

  • As a technical interface between workshop and operational engineering, you ensure the flow of information and clarify technical issues.
  • You create error analyses (root-cause), write findings reports and take over the technical documentation for serial problems.
  • In our locomotive workshop, you are actively involved in the optimization of maintenance processes, test plans and work instructions and support in special technical checks, fleet modifications and retrofit projects.

You bring that with you.

  • High safety and quality orientation, independent working methods as well as enjoyment of teamwork and practical workshop work round off your profile.
  • A further development as dipl. HF technician (e.g. electrical engineering, mechatronics, system engineering or mechanical engineering) or as dipl. Master (e.g. maintenance manager: in, production manager: in industry, electrical installation and safety expert: in) rounds off your profile.
  • A good understanding of diesel engines, hydraulics, pneumatics and vehicle electrics as well as an interest in systematic diagnostics and troubleshooting distinguish you. As well as a secure handling of common MS Office applications and diagnostic software is required for documentation and reporting work.
  • With your independent, reliable and structured way of working, you take responsibility and consistently pay attention to safety and quality.
  • You have a successfully completed apprenticeship as an electromechanic:in, electrician:in, automatic engineer:in or automatic installer:in.
