Full description

What awaits you

You have been there for 200 years – would you also like to work for Switzerland’s most personal insurance company? As an employee in the sales support of our general agency, you support the insurance consultants and look after our customers on the phone and at the counter of the general agency.

This is what they do with us

The focus of your job is the active support and support of the insurance and pension consultants (including appointments and processing) and the advice of customers on the phone and at the counter. In addition, you take care of your own customer base.

You create offers, process applications and take on further administrative work. Furthermore, you help with the training of our learners and support in sales promotion.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed insurance studies and/or experience in insurance
  • Insurance intermediary/in VBV benefit conclusion
  • Enjoying direct customer contact and close cooperation in the team
  • Communicative personality with sales flair
  • Independent and reliable operation and load capacity
  • They like to take responsibility
  • Flowing knowledge of German

Working with us

We want to be successful with you. For this purpose, we offer you a pleasant working atmosphere in a young, motivated team as well as a demanding and varied workplace in our modern offices.

In order to perform this function, the Insurance Supervision Act requires the conclusion of an insurance intermediary/in VBV, profile non-life and life. If this is not available, we are happy to support with tailor-made internal training.

Your place of work is Wetzikon and the workload is 50% - 100%. The appointment is made by agreement.

This is what we offer

Participation in success

If we are successful, our employees also benefit. Each year, the distribution of a successful share and its amount is decided.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses. We participate in job-relevant training.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer up to 25% discount on bonuses, discounts on mobile phones and free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
