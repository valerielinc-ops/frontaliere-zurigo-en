Full description

  • The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich treats and cares for mentally ill people of all ages. The more than 2,500 employees from 55 different professional profiles work in interdisciplinary teams to contribute to the holistic care of patients, from assessment to therapy to aftercare. Our work is based on mutual trust and personal responsibility and together we promote an innovative corporate culture. As a university clinic, we are successfully involved in national and international research projects. The geriatric psychiatric acute wards G0 and G1, with a focus on the treatment and support of people with dementia, delirium and psychotic disorders, fulfill their mandate as part of basic psychiatric care with an average of 14 beds. The therapeutic offer includes a differentiated assessment and treatment, support and therapy tailored to the respective patient, as well as planning discharge management under psychological, biological and social aspects. The nursing offering with a holistic approach supports the living system of caregivers.
