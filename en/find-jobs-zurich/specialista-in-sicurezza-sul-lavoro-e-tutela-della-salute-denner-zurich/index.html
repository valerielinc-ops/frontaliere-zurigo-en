Place of work: Denner AG, headquarters, Grubenstrasse 10, 8045 Zurich

Behind the scenes ensure that colleagues can safely carry out their jobs in the shops and distribution centres? Then come with your talent, because that is in demand in our winning team. We have quick decisions and appreciation. Denner. This is our shop!

Luogo di lavoro: Denner AG, headquarters, Grubenstrasse 10, 8045 Zurich

Important information

Very high willingness to travel is mandatory, you will look after the regions of Zurich, East / Central Switzerland. We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Design, control and anchoring of occupational safety & health protection at Denner
  • Regular visits and security tours in the branches and distribution centres
  • Maintenance of AS/GS documentation
  • Support for coordination and implementation of training and instruction
  • Planning and controlling of prevention measures
  • Advise and support the company management and line in questions about AS/GS

Requirements

  • 2-3 years of professional experience: in a comparable function
  • Higher technical school: Specialist for occupational safety and health protection (ASGS) with federal law. Specialist card
  • An independent, organized and responsible personality
  • A pragmatic way of thinking that takes our occupational safety and health protection to the next level
  • An attitude that promotes willingness to change and cohesion in the team
  • Good user experience MS Office
  • German (flowing)

Cosa of friamo

  • Part of the Migros Group: Strong brand anchored in the retail trade under the Migros Group umbrella
  • Flexible place of work / home office: One to two days a week working at home. Except in IT - we are even more flexible.
  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 5 weeks longer baby break (19 weeks) for mothers at 100% wage. Fathers enjoy the offspring 4 paid weeks.
  • Fair wages: 13 monthly wages and fixed wage increases for all instead of bonuses for a few.
  • Mobile subscription: Spoiled for choice: With Galaxus Mobile, Wingo or Sunrise already from CHF 10.- one or the other really connected.
  • Employee offers: 10 percent on the next major purchase (twice a year).
  • Holidays: 6 weeks holidays for all and even more days for service anniversaries.
  • Personal responsibility & freedom: plenty of room for your ideas and to make a difference together with us.

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision to advance or withdraw from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Expert Discussion: In a specialist discussion, specific knowledge relevant for the execution of the job is requested by the manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, the team. If possible, there is time to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition: After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
