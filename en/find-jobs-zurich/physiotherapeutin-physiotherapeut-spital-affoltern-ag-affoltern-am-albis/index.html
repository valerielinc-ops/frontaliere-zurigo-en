Full description

Physical therapy means more to you than exercise training? You want to accompany people on their way to more stability, physically and mentally? Then you fit in with us! In order to strengthen our physiotherapy team, we are looking for a dedicated personality for therapeutic work in our centers of psychiatry and psychotherapy with 56 beds as well as age medicine and palliative care with 66 beds. In addition to the treatment of inpatients, they also work on an outpatient basis. Another part of your work is the professional support and support of students of physiotherapy during their practical assignments. Their tasks

  • Physiotherapeutic findings, treatment and support of patients
  • Planning and implementation of individual and group therapies
  • Individual target definition, therapy planning, evaluation and progress documentation
  • Professional support, guidance and assessment of students of physiotherapy during their practical assignments (no previous experience required - but the willingness to carry out a practical manager training)
  • Close interprofessional cooperation with nursing, medical profession and other therapeutic and advisory professional groups
  • Active participation in the further development of our physiotherapeutic offers and treatment standards Your profile
  • Bachelor of Science in Physiotherapy (BSc) or equivalent, SRK-approved training
  • Joy of working with people with psychiatric and geriatric complex health issues
  • Interest and pleasure in accompanying and promoting students
  • Independent, structured and responsible working methods
  • Strong social and communication skills and enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation
  • Interest in evidence-based physiotherapy and in the professional development of our own work area Our culture – Your benefits «Bi öis can mer no»: With us it is family and personal. As part of the Affoltern hospital team, you will also benefit from attractive employment conditions and numerous Fringe Benefits: Jokertag, shopping benefits and two free admission subscriptions to the FORTYSEVEN Wellness Bathrooms are just a few examples of our Fringe Benefits. The Hospital Affoltern is the largest employer and training company in the Knonau Office. Around 550 employees work for the well-being of patients every day. Find out more about us in our portrait.
