Full description

Use as a detacher and cleaner in the school district of Limmattal with place of work at the school Im Gut.

Tasks

  • Compliance with urban safety, hygiene and quality guidelines
  • Cleaning work
  • Further tasks according to the order LHT
  • Cooperation with other domestic service staff of the school building and other staff of the school
  • Representation of the competent contact person for the evening clubs

Profile

  • Experience in cleaning
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Social skills, good manners, friendly, skill in dealing with users
  • Self-employment, reliability and responsibility
  • Physically fit and resilient
  • Enjoyment at work in a lively company with children, young people, care and teaching staff and parents

We offer

  • Self-employed, responsible and varied activity
  • Involvement in an active school team
  • Contemporary employment according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich
