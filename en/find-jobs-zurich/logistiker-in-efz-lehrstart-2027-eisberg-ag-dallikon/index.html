Full description

With an apprenticeship as a logistics specialist at Eisberg AG, you take the first steps into your professional life. During your training, you will get to know different areas of our logistics and get exciting insights into the world of the food industry. From the very beginning, you take on practical tasks in warehouse and goods management, gain valuable practical experience and develop step by step into a logistics professional. You are not alone: Your vocational trainers and the practical trainers in the departments accompany and support you throughout your apprenticeship. Look forward to a versatile education, a great team and a strong start to your future!

  • Contract type: apprenticeship
  • Pensum: 100%
  • Working time model: Flexible time
  • Job opening: 01.08.2027
  • Language: German
  • Location: Eisberg AG, Hüttenwiesenstrasse 4 8108 Dällikon

Tasks

  • In the acceptance of goods you will unload trucks and check delivery notes 🚛
  • Throughout the logistics you will store goods and control stocks 📦
  • In order picking, you will put together orders for our customers 📋
  • In the administration area you will do simple office work 🖥️
  • In the transport department you will organize the delivery of goods 🚚

Requirements

  • You attend secondary school at level B or A or have an equivalent degree 🎓
  • Joy of practical work and willingness to physically tackle 🧤
  • Interest in logistics, flow of goods and the food industry 🥬
  • Reliability, responsibility and a careful working method ✅
  • Teamwork and joy in dealing with people of different cultures 🌍
  • Basic computer knowledge of modern storage and scanning systems 🖥️
  • Curiosity and motivation to learn new things and develop yourself 🔧
  • Enthusiasm for fresh produce and salad 🥗

This is what we offer

  • Food: Cuisine is very important to us: Freshly prepared salads, soups, tea and fruit are available to our employees free of charge. In addition, you can take home products from the personal shelf every day.
  • Mobility: Benefit from convenient connections to public transport, attractive discounts for public transport and sufficient parking – sustainable and worry-free on the road!
  • Discounts & Discounts: You benefit from the Coop Personal Discount Card with various discounts in the retail trade, in specialty shops and supermarkets as well as other attractive services, for example in the area of insurance.
