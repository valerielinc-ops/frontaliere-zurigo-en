Original description

Do you not only want to assemble, but also create jobs that make everyday production easier, more efficient and more ergonomic? To strengthen our team, we create an additional position and look forward to a personality who tackles, thinks and improves.

What will you do • Construction, manufacture and construction of workstation systems (assembly workstations, material wagons, shelving systems, lighting, etc.) according to LEAN and 5S principles • Close cooperation with design, test workshop, test technology and internal and external partners • Mechanical machining of various materials (cutting, drilling, mounting) • Coordination of orders, appointments and coordination with internal customers and suppliers • Preparation of operating instructions, safety documentation and calculation of weight loads • Analysis and optimization of workstations, material flows and storage processes in the sense of LEAN and 5S • Support for internal relocations as well as for the commissioning and dismantling of workstation systems • Participation in projects for the planning, procurement and management of workplace systems and BOSCH components in compliance with the occupational safety and health protection guidelines

Who you are

  • Completed basic technical training (e.g. polymechanic, constructor, automatist, metalworker or plant/device builder), ideally with further training at the level of HF in mechanical engineering, mechatronics or automation
  • Several years of professional experience in the technical environment
  • In-depth experience in aluminum profile system construction
  • Good CAD knowledge
  • Further education in lean production and project management
  • Strong communication, organisation and coordination skills
  • Cooperative, helpful and open personality with assertiveness
  • Team-oriented, responsible, initiative and innovative working methods
