Full description

We are looking for a committed, competent and resilient personality for our monitoring station, IPS / IMC / recovery room / day clinic, which contributes with heart and professionalism to the optimal care of our patients. In a varied and demanding working environment, you take on responsibility, work interdisciplinary and actively contribute your skills in various areas.

  • Professional care and care of our patients from different disciplines
  • Ensuring and further developing the entire care process as well as a high quality of care
  • Continuous monitoring and assessment of patient status and early detection of changes
  • Cross-divisional work in the recovery room, at the IMC, in the day clinic and in emergency rooms
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation with evidence doctors and other professional groups
  • Collaboration in the resuscitation team of the clinic
  • Management of Duty (organisation of emergencies, patient room management)
  • Professional implementation of medical technology measures and care interventions
  • Guidance and support for new employees and learners/students
  • Complete and correct maintenance documentation
  • NDS intensive care training
  • At least 2 years of professional experience
  • An independent, competent person with a willingness to take on responsibility, to cooperate in the team and to support it professionally
  • Ability to apply specialised, social and self-competence to situations
  • A sense of quality and responsibility as well as a quick understanding
  • Flexibility and physical and psychological resilience
  • Reliability, commitment and a high level of service
  • A varied activity in cooperation of many disciplines
  • Interdisciplinary teamwork from surgery to birth department
  • A family, personal working atmosphere
  • Development opportunities within the clinic and the Swiss Medical Network
  • Support for individual training wishes
  • Numerous discounts (see here), including a rich lunch buffet for only CHF 5.-
  • Discounts via Swibeco (e.g. at Coop, Zalando, Ikea, etc.)
  • Full coverage of the sickness allowance insurance
  • Various employee actions such as summer party, birthday and Christmas present, etc.
  • Free coffee/tea

Treatment at the highest level, high-class medical care and care, competent care in a more familiar, private atmosphere with hotel comfort, dedication, conscientiousness and absolute discretion – this is what the private clinic Bethanien stands for. Around 340 medical doctors from different disciplines provide high-quality, integrated medical care. Among the most important specialties are orthopaedics, visceral and thoracic surgery, urology, gynecology and birth, gastroenterology, cardiology, pneumology and internal medicine incl. Oncology. In the birth department, around 500 children are born each year. Founded in 1912, the private clinic Bethanien is part of the Swiss Medical Network.
