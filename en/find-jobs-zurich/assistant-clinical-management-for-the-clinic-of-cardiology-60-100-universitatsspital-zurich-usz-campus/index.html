Role overview

The Clinic of Cardiology stands for highly specialized heart medicine, interprofessional cooperation, research and innovation.

In this dynamic environment, you will have the opportunity to take on responsibility, develop professionally and actively shape the processes of the clinic.

You expect a versatile function with insight into the control and further development of a university clinic.

  • The Clinic of Cardiology stands for highly specialized heart medicine, interprofessional cooperation, research and innovation.
  • In this dynamic environment, you will have the opportunity to take on responsibility, develop professionally and actively shape the processes of the clinic.

Company and context

  • You work at a central interface between clinic management, the medical profession and other internal areas and can actively contribute your organizational and business skills.
  • Your workplace is located at the USZ campus in the heart of Zurich, close to the University of Zurich and ETH Zurich.
  • The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland.
  • Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.
  • You support the clinic management in business, organizational and administrative issues and assume self-responsible defined tasks
  • You are responsible for managing and monitoring the third-party business as well as the third-party funded projects of the Clinic of Cardiology at USZ and UZH
  • You take over the personnel management of the third-party-funded employees of the Clinic of Cardiology
  • You are responsible for forwarding and invoicing in third-party fund management
  • You actively work on projects for the further development and optimization of structures, processes and processes within the clinic and independently take over subprojects
  • You contribute to financial and HR analyses, evaluations, reporting and controlling tasks and prepare relevant decision bases for clinic management

Additional details

  • You support clinic management in day-to-day operations, especially in the case of holiday absences and other absences
  • You have very good German and English skills as well as very good MS Office skills, especially in Excel and PowerPoint Healthcare experience is beneficial
