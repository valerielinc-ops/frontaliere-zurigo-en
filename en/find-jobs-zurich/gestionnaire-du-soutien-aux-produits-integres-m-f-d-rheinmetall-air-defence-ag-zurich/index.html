Full description

What we're looking for

Are you looking for an exciting challenge with comprehensive design opportunities and excellent development prospects? Then you've come to the right place! As an IPS Manager (m/w/d), you are responsible for the planning, management and monitoring of the Integrated Product Support project, as well as the optimization of all support measures (maintenance, spare parts, documentation) for complex military products. Your tasks include in particular:

  • Advisory and support for sales in the field of Integrated Product Support and project management in Customer Service, including on-site customer support
  • Development and creation of strategic and operational product support concepts
  • Planning and management of the IPS sub-project within the overall project framework, as well as control and monitoring of contract-compliant implementation
  • Ensuring and monitoring contract-compliant delivery of IPS deliverables
  • Management and supervision of subcontractor deployment
  • Technical leadership and supervision of IPS engineers within the matrix organization

What you should bring

  • Completed apprenticeship in the technical field with further training as a business engineer (FH) or another relevant qualification
  • Professional experience as an IPS Manager with proven project success
  • Knowledge of sales is desirable
  • Knowledge of standards in the IPS environment, such as ASD S Series
  • High personal responsibility, initiative and willingness to perform, as well as a structured, independent working style with pronounced networked thinking skills
  • Fluent German and English
  • Willingness for occasional business travel
  • Strong social and multicultural competence
  • Experience in the military environment is desirable

What we offer

  • A dynamic working environment with exciting projects
  • Flexible working hours (flexible time and flexible working time)
  • Excellent social benefits and a supra-legal pension fund
  • Employee benefits of a large group, employee stock purchase program
  • Support for needs-based further training
  • Company restaurant as well as external dining and shopping options nearby
  • Individual internal and external personnel development measures in collaboration with our Rheinmetall-Academy
  • Workplace just a few minutes' walk from Zurich-Oerlikon train station
