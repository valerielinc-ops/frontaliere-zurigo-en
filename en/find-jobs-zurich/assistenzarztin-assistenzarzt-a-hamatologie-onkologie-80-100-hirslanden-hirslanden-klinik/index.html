As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

The Clinic for Hematology and Oncology is the competence center for oncology at the Klinik Hirslanden in the Zurich area and an essential part of the DKG-certified tumor center. As part of the Hirslanden Cancer Institute (HCI), it offers all modern methods of cancer therapy incl. Immune and cellular therapies. The care includes both outpatient and inpatient care and is provided by other specialist departments incl. Complementary medicine and radiotherapy. The clinic is a SIWF-approved training centre A for the field of oncology and can also offer a foreign year of hematology.

YOUR TASKS

  • Care of ambulatory and inpatient patients with oncological and haematological diseases under specialist supervision
  • Professional training in all types of cancer and blood diseases based on scientific diagnosis, therapy and care
  • This also includes indications and treatment of patients under autologous stem cell transplantation. CAR T Cell therapy
  • Participation in the regular internal and external training courses is part of the curriculum and is actively promoted
  • Independent working with inpatient haemato-oncological patients and prescribing or Monitoring chemo and immunotherapies

Your profile

  • Swiss state examination in human medicine or an equivalent education recognised in Switzerland (MEBEKO recognition)
  • Your training goal is specialist medical oncology (or hematology)
  • Prior knowledge of internal medicine is desired, at least. 2 years of professional experience
  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Interested in working in an interdisciplinary team

The employment period is usually initially 12 months.

We attach great importance to your further education so that you are optimally prepared for the future challenges of the medical profession.

In addition, we offer work in a highly qualified, dynamic team, advanced employment conditions, regular working hours from Monday to Friday, no weekend services, internal and external training and good social benefits.

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. You work with a modern medical clinic information system and can expect extensive training and development opportunities from us. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family responsibilities. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from equivalent financial compensation. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.
