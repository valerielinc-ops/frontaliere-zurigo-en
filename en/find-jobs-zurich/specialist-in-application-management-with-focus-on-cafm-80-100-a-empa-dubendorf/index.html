Full description

Materials science and technology are our passion. With our cutting-edge research, Empa’s approximately 1,100 employees make decisive contributions to the benefit of society for a future worth living in. Empa is a research institution of the ETH Domain.

Specialist in Application Management with focus on CAFM (80-100%) (a)

The Real Estate Department is responsible for the operation, maintenance and management of Empa’s infrastructure. It is also the joint client representative body of three research institutions in the ETH Domain. As part of a small team within the Business Development department, you will be responsible for the operation and further development of our real estate-specific specialist applications – with a special focus on our new CAFM system.

Their tasks

They are responsible for the operation and further development (application lifecycle management) of our approximately a dozen real estate-specific specialist applications, including partly business-critical processes, with a particular focus on the support and further development of our new CAFM system – from configuration to rollout support to second-level support. They ensure data quality and consistency in the maintained systems and maintain master data, authorizations and configurations. In close cooperation with the ICT department, you will clarify questions about application operations, interfaces and IT security and participate in the further development of our system landscape and application strategy. You will process support requests from our employees, assist with tests, data migrations and release changes – for example as part of the CAFM rollout – and advise and train employees in dealing with existing and new applications.
