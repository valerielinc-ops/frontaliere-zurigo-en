Institute of Parasitology

Job opening immediately or by agreement

The parasites are everywhere... To strengthen our team, we are looking for a:n BMA /Laborant:in without fear of parasites. The Diagnostic Center Parasitology is a department at the Institute of Parasitology at the University of Zurich and examines stool, blood and tissue samples from humans and animals for parasites. We use microscopic, serological and molecular methods.

We offer

  • Exciting tasks in the diverse fields of parasitological diagnostics
  • A broad spectrum of less automated examination procedures and always new diagnostic questions.
  • Regular working hours, excluding late and weekend work
  • A well-founded familiarization in two areas: safe in microscopy (chair), as well as in serology or PCR diagnostics depending on current needs
  • A small, collegial team with short communication paths

The job is paid on an hourly basis and offers freedom for personal flexibility. We are happy to explain more about this alternative employment model. The wage depends on the wage system of the canton ZH. The university offers various perks in the areas of food, sports or telephony.

Tasks

  • Independent performance of diagnostic examinations
  • Receiving and assessing test material and data collection
  • Recording of results and preparation of findings
  • Care of students and academic guests

Profile

  • Completed training as BMA HF or laboratory assistant:in EFZ
  • Good knowledge of German
  • They are capable of teamwork, but also independent and reliable
  • You have no fear of touch when dealing with stool samples

It is important to us to find someone who not only fits us humanly and characterally, but also wants to become part of our team in the long term.
