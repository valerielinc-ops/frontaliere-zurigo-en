Cook for our S-Sushi-Manufactory 100%

2 Spice AG — Dietlikon, Dietlikon, Sizzera

The Yooji's Manufaktur, which belongs to the two spice AG, offers a versatile and exquisite range of Sushi, Sashimi and much more. The production site of the two spice factory is located in Rümlang and employs over 90 employees. Every day various delicacies are prepared and delivered to Yooji's restaurants and stores several times a day. Also specially developed products for our Japanese retail brand ZENBU, are produced in the manufaktur.

ZENBU products such as Sushi, Poké, Onigiri and vegan delicacies are produced for Coop and are available directly from Coop throughout Switzerland. Do you want to become part of our team? Then now is your chance!

Tasks

  • Production of kitchen products according to daily requirements
  • Compliance with quality, hygiene and cleaning standards
  • Careful work preparation
  • Help with cleaning, cleaning and maintenance work
  • Detecting defects and introducing suitable measures
  • Active participation in process improvements

Requirements

  • You have an education or practical experience in the food industry with – for example as a cook EFZ, food technologist, baker confectioner, meat specialist or in a comparable function.
  • Experience in food production of advantage
  • High quality and hygiene awareness
  • Self-employed, reliable and accurate working methods
  • Team-capable, resilient and motivated personality
  • Good physical condition
  • German language skills in word and writing at B1 level
