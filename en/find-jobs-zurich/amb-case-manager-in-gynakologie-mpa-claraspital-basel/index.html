About the Claraspital

The Claraspital, the largest private hospital in Basel, is a university teaching and research hospital with a full service mandate for highly specialized abdominal surgery and a supra-regional broadcast of its centers “Bäu” and “Tumor” as well as its special fields. In the Claraspital, employees find jobs where they can develop both professionally and humanly. At Claraspital, we attach great importance to consciously nurturing and further developing our corporate culture and actively living the cultural criteria developed together “together – competently – responsibly”. «To pass on knowledge and values, to accompany a young person into working life and to open up opportunities and perspectives for him – in our case, for example, a later training as a specialist in surgical technology, biomedical analyst or radiology specialist – is very satisfying.» Andrea Kiefer, Head of AEMP
