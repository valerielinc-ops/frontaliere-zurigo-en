Full description

You put new plants into operation and integrate them into the existing heat distribution network of the city of Zurich.

Tasks

  • After comprehensive training, you will be responsible for fault location and troubleshooting in the heat distribution networks of the city of Zurich.
  • You evaluate the measurement results of the installed leakage computers on the network and evaluate them.
  • You manage and archive the relevant technical documents, reports and protocols, ensuring that all documents are always up-to-date.
  • In the district heating chambers you carry out electrical installations, changes and repairs independently. In addition, you will carry out factually limited electrical installation work in the low-voltage area.

Profile

  • You have completed basic training as an electrical installer at the EFZ level and have the qualification according to NIV Art. 13 or are ready to acquire them.
  • You bring several years of professional experience in the company or maintenance.
  • You are independent and responsible, describe yourself as a team player and inform proactively.
  • Good knowledge of German and a safe use of the computer distinguish you.
  • Category B driver’s license is in your pocket and you are ready to do picket service.
