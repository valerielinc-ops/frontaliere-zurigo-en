Full description

Share your passion for books

Do you love reading? Do you enjoy immersing yourself in incredible book worlds? And do you love sharing your passion for books with other enthusiastic readers? Then apply now for an apprenticeship as a bookseller at Orell Füssli. Because we love books just as much as you do. What's more, Orell Füssli is a place where employees feel comfortable, where people enjoy working well together and achieve more together. During your training, you will get to know the book trade from the ground up.

We look forward to welcoming you to one of our branches in the Winterthur region (Volketswil, Winterthur) / Eastern Switzerland (St.Gallen, Wil, Schaffhausen) on 1 August 2027.

Your main responsibilities

  • Providing expert advice to customers
  • Recommending books and new releases
  • Receiving and processing orders
  • Various computer-based tasks and work in our inventory management system
  • Presentation of books and non-books

What you bring

  • Good secondary school-leaving certificate
  • A strong service orientation, an outgoing nature, attentiveness and curiosity
  • Excellent German language skills and an enthusiasm for foreign languages
  • A passion for books and reading
  • A passion for media and current affairs

What to expect

  • Varied training in various departments
  • Collaborative and appreciative company culture
  • Generous vacation policy
  • Wide range of discounts
  • Central workplace with good public transport connections

We are very much looking forward to your online application. Please send us your application including a cover letter, CV, complete school certificates and a short text about your favorite book. See you soon.
