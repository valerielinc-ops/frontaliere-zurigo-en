Full description

  • Carry out maintenance, testing, maintenance, modifications and fault analysis on electrical and electronic aircraft systems (Line Maintenance +) using English language technical documentation
  • Carry out control and service work on the aircraft before, between and after the missions (e.g. towing, refuelling, loading and unloading of cargo and luggage, as well as transferring and taking over the aircraft to and from the crew)
  • Support missions at home and abroad as an accompanying mechanic, from demonstrations with the PC-7 team to SWISSTOPO and LTDB flights (Federal Air Transport Service) to training missions
  • Assume active missions in the Flight Accident Pikett and systematically develop the targeted further development into a function as Flight Accident Pikett Operations Manager
  • As a technical instructor, ensure knowledge transfer in technical courses and take over administrative tasks in the operational competence position
  • Carry out, document and release inspection and repair work in the company’s own component workshop
  • Electronic engineer, automatic engineer or similar 4-year basic technical education
  • EASA Part-66 Aircraft Maintenance Licence (Category B2) requested
  • Precise, responsible, flexible and team-oriented way of working with pleasure and motivation on a wide range of tasks related to aviation
  • Category B (C/E beneficial), good IT skills (MS Office/SAP)
  • Very good knowledge of one or more official languages, knowledge of English (A2)
  • Military basic training beneficial
