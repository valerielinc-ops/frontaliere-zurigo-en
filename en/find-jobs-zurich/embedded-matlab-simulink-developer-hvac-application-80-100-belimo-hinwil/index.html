Summary

In this role, you will own designated library topics, collaborate with architects and requirements engineers, and develop robust, maintainable solutions in a Matlab/Simulink environment. Your expertise will contribute to the quality, reliability, and scalability of a platform used across multiple products.

What You'll Do

  • Develop and maintain assigned HVAC library components, ensuring high quality, reliability, and scalability.
  • Collaborate with architects and requirements engineers to understand, refine, and clarify technical requirements.
  • Design structured and maintainable solutions in Matlab/Simulink aligned with system and architectural requirements.
  • Provide technical guidance and hands-on support to developers during implementation.
  • Participate in and contribute to code reviews, ensuring compliance with quality standards and design specifications.
  • Ensure adherence to architectural principles, coding guidelines, and software development best practices.
  • Investigate, troubleshoot, and resolve technical issues while continuously improving performance and maintainability.
  • Document designs, technical decisions, and lessons learned to support knowledge sharing and long-term sustainability.

Who You Are

  • BSc or MSc in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Control Engineering, or a related technical discipline.
  • Several years of experience in embedded and model-based software development using Matlab/Simulink.
  • Experience in control systems, device logic, or similar engineering software applications.
  • Strong understanding of software design principles, code reviews, and maintainable software architectures.
  • Experience working in cross-functional engineering teams and collaborating with multiple stakeholders.
  • Ability to translate requirements into practical, scalable technical solutions.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a quality-focused mindset.
  • Fluent English language skills
  • German is considered an advantage.
