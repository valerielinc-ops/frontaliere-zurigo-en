Full description

The school Riedenhalden is looking for reinforcements from 4 January 2027 (or by agreement).

Tasks

Position key data

  • Pensum 3 weekly lessons French, about 10%
  • 6. Category
  • Working days: Wednesday morning and Thursday afternoon (other teaching hours may be negotiable)

Main tasks

  • Planning, preparation and implementation of teaching based on the curriculum
  • Follow-up and evaluation of lessons
  • Advising pupils on individual learning and performance difficulties as well as showing funding opportunities
  • Collaboration in the college, with school management, external specialist bodies and guardians

Profile

  • Teaching diploma for the primary level (recognized in Switzerland) with teaching qualification for the subject French
  • Enjoy teaching students from different cultures
  • Strong skills in classroom management
  • Willingness and joy at work in the integrated setting, together with school curative teachers
  • Identification with our core values and willingness to live them in everyday school life
  • Positive basic attitude, teamwork, resilience, high social skills

We offer

  • a well-organized school that has been professionally run for many years
  • An open-minded, committed team: Work in a collegial, supportive environment that is open to new ideas and approaches
  • Value-oriented education: Our school culture is based on the pillars of appreciation, responsibility, relationship readiness, mindfulness and self-efficacy
  • Innovation in teaching: As a QUIMS school and new Mint partner school, we attach great importance to the promotion of mathematics, computer sciences and technology
  • Classroom support: Benefit from the support of class assistance that helps you create an optimal learning environment
  • Collaboration with professionals: They are supported by competent and trained curative teachers in their work with students

About us

We are a large school community in Zurich-Nord with 19 schools. We derive our pedagogical attitude from the concept of the New Authority and thus meet the daily challenges in everyday school life together and effectively. Additional support in challenging situations with the students is available at each school through the human and spatial resources of STIS (Stay in School).

The school Riedenhalden (10 secondary classes, 10 primary classes, 3 kindergartens and 5 hoards) is located in the center of the Affoltern district and only a few minutes walk to the S-Bahn stop. Since this school year we are a day school. As a QUIMS school for many years, we have great experience for our multicultural environment.
