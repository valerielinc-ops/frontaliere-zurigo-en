Full description

IKMZ - Department of Communication and Media Research

Start of employment approximately in 01.02.2027 or upon agreement

We are looking for an excellent scholar in communication science or a closely related discipline who combines a strong empirical social-science profile with expertise or a clear research interest in science communication and/or climate change communication. Research at the Chair for Science Communication examines how science- and technology-related issues are publicly communicated and perceived, including topics such as climate change, artificial intelligence, health, and emerging technologies, as well as the role of digital platforms, political actors, scientists, and other stakeholders. About the position

  • 3-year postdoctoral position (80%, paid according to the cantonal salary scheme)
  • Can potentially be extended for an additional 3 years
  • Workplace is Zurich

Your responsibilities

  • Conduct excellent research in science communication and/or climate change communication
  • Contribute to team research projects and develop your own research agenda
  • Contribute to the mentoring of junior colleagues and the collaborative development of the team
  • Contribute to ongoing and future empirical projects, including survey-based research
  • Publish in leading academic journals and present research at international conferences
  • Teach one class per semester in IKMZ's BA/MA programs and supervise BA/MA theses
  • Take on a limited amount of organizational and administrative responsibilities

Your profile

  • Doctoral degree in communication science or a closely related discipline
  • Excellent track record of conceptually guided, rigorous empirical research
  • A research profile in science communication, climate change communication, or a closely related area
  • Strong methodological expertise; experience with survey research would be particularly welcome
  • Ability to work both independently and collaboratively in an ambitious research environment
  • Experience in mentoring, project coordination, or other forms of academic leadership would be an asset
  • Proficiency in English; knowledge of German would be helpful
