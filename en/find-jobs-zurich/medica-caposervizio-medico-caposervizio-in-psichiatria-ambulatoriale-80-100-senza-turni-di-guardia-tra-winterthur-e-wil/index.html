Leadership responsibility and free weekends – who says that this does not go together? An outpatient psychiatric center in southern Thurgau proves the opposite. As a senior physician, you treat adults from the entire diagnostic spectrum and train the offspring, on working days and without significant service burden. People come here when everyday life becomes too much: adults of all ages, even seniors, in an acute crisis, with depression or burnout or after a hospital stay, if the treatment should continue. They are registered by family doctors: inside, therapist: inside and relatives, but often also directly by the affected persons themselves. The center is one of the external psychiatric services of the canton, which operate a locally located service in each district. The medical-psychological core team is manageable: a chief physician, a senior physician, two senior physicians and two senior psychologists, plus assistant physicians and psychologists in further education. In addition to individual treatments, there are skills and emotion regulation groups, an interactional group and social competence training. In the same house also works an ambulatory for children and young people. The sponsor is a large private psychiatry group with several clinics in German-speaking Switzerland. The rooms are modern and are close to the train station. The municipality is located on the railway line Winterthur-St. Gallen. Trains run every half hour towards Zurich until Wil is a station, and via the A1 you are quickly on the road by car. Zurich Airport can be reached in about 40 minutes.

Their tasks

  • They clarify adults from the entire psychiatric diagnosis spectrum independently and treat them
  • You lead and train assistant doctor: inside and psychologist: inside
  • They support the entire team professionally
  • They help shape group offers and further develop the ambulatory offer
  • They work in a networked manner with assignors and other participating bodies

Your Competences

  • Recognized specialist title psychiatry and psychotherapy (in Germany: specialist for psychiatry and psychotherapy)
  • Sound clinical experience and good knowledge of the Swiss healthcare system
  • Independent work and at the same time enjoy interdisciplinary teamwork in a socio-psychiatric environment
  • Knowledge of German at a very good level
  • At MEBEKO recognition, we are happy to advise you and support you financially.

Their advantages

  • Hours that can be planned: work on working days, without significant workload, flexible working time models
  • 42-hour week: significantly less than usual in many Swiss clinics
  • More days off: at least 25 days of holiday (from 50 years of age 27, from 60 years of age 32), working time credit before public holidays, additional Fridays from the fifth year of service
  • Leadership with support: leadership responsibility, good coaching and generous participation in further education and training
  • Salary: normal market salary at 13. Monthly wage and voluntary participation in success
  • Protection: worldwide private accident insurance, daily sickness allowance insurance and reduced collective agreements with health insurance funds
  • Easy to reach: modern rooms close to the train station, S-Bahn every half hour towards Winterthur and Zurich
