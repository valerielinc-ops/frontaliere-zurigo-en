Full description

Do you want to take responsibility for central issues in water protection? Take over in this versatile function You play a key role in the canton for the protection of groundwater, ensuring the supply of drinking water and regulating the uses and anthropogenic influence of the underground. Look forward to exciting and varied challenges as well as the opportunity to make a significant contribution to ensuring that groundwater remains protected and the supply of drinking water in perfect quality for future generations. Your tasks

  • Section management and group management (water supply and geothermal use)
  • Implementation of groundwater legislation and enforcement in the areas of groundwater protection and use
  • Ensuring an environmentally sound, public interest and legally compliant groundwater management
  • Initiate, coordinate and shape forward-looking drinking water supply planning
  • Implementation and enforcement in connection with the Underground Use Act (GNU) Your profile
  • Master College in Science, Engineering or Geology
  • Extensive professional experience in the field. Public management and/or enforcement experience is beneficial
  • In-depth management experience possibly in public administration
  • Enjoy networking and information and knowledge exchange at cantonal and national level
  • Constructive approach to resolving conflicts of interest
  • Political sense Our offer We work collegially, uncomplicated and with pleasure in the team. You take on responsibility in a broad and varied field of activity with a lot of creative scope. The reconciliation of professional and private life is a central part of our culture. Flexible working hours, mobile work and a wide range of opportunities for professional and personal development are a matter of course for us – and at a workplace just a few minutes from Zurich Central Station.
