Full description

You are as individual as the people you support. That is why we create an environment of equality that offers you therapeutic freedom and a flexible working model – so that you can shape your professional and private life according to your needs. WePractice is a leading outpatient practice group with over 140 psychotherapists at 17 locations and is part of the Medbase Group. Tasks

  • From May 2026, we are opening a practice for children and adolescents at Stadelhofen station. A dedicated team of psychotherapists and psychotherapists in training is setting new standards.
  • Work in a warm practice and benefit from the individual support of an experienced site manager – regular case discussions and supervisions are a matter of course.
  • Accompany your patients in outpatient psychotherapy – flexible in terms of time and location.
  • Decide for yourself who you work with. We will help you build a patient base that suits your specialization.
  • Develop individual therapy plans, take medical histories, and make diagnoses – our practice software, developed especially for you, gives you more time for the essentials.
  • Requirements
  • Initial professional experience: Child & adolescent psychotherapy
  • Completed basic or advanced training: Federally recognized psychotherapist
  • Psychologist with completed advanced training as a federally recognized child and adolescent psychotherapist.
  • Comprehensive knowledge of psychotherapy with children and adolescents
  • You fulfill all requirements to be able to bill via the basic insurance in Switzerland
  • Initiative, team-oriented and resilient personality with a joy for independent work
  • Very good written and spoken German skills
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • What we offer
  • Central workplace: Our practice rooms are warm and modern – a place where you and your patients feel comfortable.
  • Individual further education: We support you with supervisions, case discussions, development meetings, and further education time/budget.
  • Flat hierarchies: We are a young team that works together with fresh ideas and a lot of drive.
  • Scope for design and decision-making: Choose your patients freely and design the therapeutic approach so that it is pragmatic and meaningful for the care.
