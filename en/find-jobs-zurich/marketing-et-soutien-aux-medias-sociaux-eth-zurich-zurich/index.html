Marketing and Social Media Support

20%-40%, Zurich, fixed-term

print Drucken

ETH Zurich is one of the world’s leading universities specializing in science and technology. It is renowned for its excellent education, cutting-edge research, and its efforts to put new knowledge and innovations directly into practice. ETH Zurich Space boosts space activities to establish ETH Zurich as the leading university in space research, education, Earth observation and entrepreneurship within Europe and beyond.

Project background

To support the growing visibility of ETH Zurich Space and to ensure clear, engaging, and up-to-date communication across our channels, we are looking for a marketing and social media support. Start date: Beginning of September or immediately.

Job description

  • Collecting content for newsletters, preparing and formatting it for publication
  • Creating engaging space-related content for ETH Zurich Space’s social media channels
  • Regularly maintaining and updating the ETH Zurich Space website to ensure accuracy and relevance
  • Developing a solid understanding of the ETH Zurich Space ecosystem and staying informed about current activities, news, and opportunities
  • Preparing space-related content from various sources (researchers, partners, events, missions) for use across different media formats
  • Support with other tasks as needed (e.g. PPT presentations, creating brochures and print material, sourcing merchandising, creating videos and podcasts and the planning of events)
  • The role will be a scope of 20 - 40% and is flexible in terms of working hours

Profile

  • Longer term employment horizon (2 plus years)
  • In-depth interest/understanding in Space and relevant educational background
  • Be familiar with the ETH Zurich (Space) environment / willing to explore it
  • Fluent in written and spoken English, German is a plus
  • Ability to transform complex topics into clear, engaging, and accessible content
  • Strong organizational skills and attention to detail
  • Good understanding and knowledge of digital communication channels

Workplace

We offer

  • A dynamic, international, and interdisciplinary environment at one of the leading technical universities in the world
  • A motivated team of people sharing a passion for space and the goal to strengthen and enhance space activities at ETH Zurich
  • A stimulating environment at the intersection of science, technology, and communication
