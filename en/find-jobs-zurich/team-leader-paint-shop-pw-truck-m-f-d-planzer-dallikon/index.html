Full description

Real professionals are in demand here – not only on the road, but also in logistics and our own workshops. Our state-of-the-art fleet of around 1,800 vehicles deserves regular maintenance and precise repairs. Are you joining us?

Tasks

  • You lead the paint shop team including learners, promote their development and provide a motivating working environment
  • You plan, control and optimize day-to-day business with an overview and a good sense of efficient processes
  • Quality, adherence to deadlines and a smooth process are your top priority – you keep everything under control
  • As a hub, you coordinate the collaboration between workshop, customer service and your team
  • Material management as well as order and safety are also your responsibility and are consistently ensured by you.

Profile

  • You have completed an apprenticeship as a carrosserie painter: in EFZ and ideally have further education
  • With your several years of professional experience – and in the best case first management experience – you bring the necessary know-how
  • You tackle, think and take responsibility – you can rely on
  • With clear communication and safe appearance, you convince in everyday life and enjoy working in a team
  • Very good knowledge of German as well as the driver’s license of category B round off your profile

Benefits

  • With us you start with 5 weeks vacation and benefit from the 5th year of service even from 6 weeks!
  • You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • We keep up with the times - you have modern and safe work equipment at your disposal
  • Get ready for parking - with us you can park for free

For us as a company, the focus is on supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
