Full description

Design information security with effect: As part of the CSO/CISO team, you ensure that IAM governance works in everyday life. You coordinate the central IAM governance body, bring together medicine, care, administration and IT, and create solutions that combine security and clinical agility.

As an Information Security Officer, you also work on a wide range of topics in cyber and information security – from system assessments and project consulting to assessments and inquiry processing.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • IAM Governance: You are responsible for developing and updating the IAM guidelines and guidelines, ensuring that they are practical, comprehensible and in line with the CSO/CISO strategy. • Establish and lead IAM Governance Panel: You bring medicine, care, administration and IT together, moderate the dialogue and ensure that decisions are made and different interests between operational requirements and security are combined in a solution-oriented manner. • Managing governance processes: You define scope, frequency and quality requirements for central IAM governance processes such as role recertification and SoD testing and ensure their consistent implementation and tracking. • Exceptions and risks : You evaluate exemption applications, weigh security risks and operational requirements and ensure a comprehensible and audit-proof documentation of decisions. • Ensure IAM Compliance: Ensure legal, regulatory and internal compliance and assist with audits, reviews and security incidents in the IAM environment. • Further develop IAM strategy: Together with the specialist departments and IT, you will further shape the IAM strategy and translate it into understandable and binding guidelines. Requirements • Education: Completed studies in (economic) computer sciences, law or economics or a comparable qualification. Certifications in the governance and audit environment (e.g. CISA, CISM, CRISC, ISO 27001 Lead Auditor) are a plus. • IAM & Governance: Several years of experience in IAM governance processes and/or IAM audits as well as in-depth understanding of relevant IAM concepts such as identity lifecycle, role and entitlement models, PAM, MFA and SSO. • Professional experience: Experience in a management or control function, for example as a governance manager, IT auditor or risk manager. • Working method: Analytical, structured and solution-oriented. You can understand complex issues and translate them into clear, comprehensible decisions. • Stakeholder management: Safe in dealing with different stakeholders at a professional and technical level and enjoying bringing different perspectives together. • Language skills: Very good knowledge of German and English. We offer: We are a small, experienced team with high motivation and a clear mission: protecting the data, systems and employees of the USZ. In the field of information security (ISO), we work closely with a wide range of stakeholders to transform requirements and concepts of information security into effective and practical solutions. This involves close cooperation with the Security Operations Center (SOC) team. The team is characterized by an open and trusting cooperation, short decision-making paths and a high degree of personal responsibility. New ideas and impulses are welcome and contribute to the continuous development of information security at USZ. Together we create sustainable solutions and make an important contribution to the protection of the organization.
