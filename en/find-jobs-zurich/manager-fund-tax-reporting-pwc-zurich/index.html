Your team

Our Fund Tax Reporting team accompanies leading asset managers, fund initiators and institutional investors throughout the fund’s lifecycle – from UCITS structures to alternative investments. Professional excellence, team spirit, trust and high quality standards characterise our cooperation. At PwC, you have the opportunity to achieve visible impact and develop professionally and personally. Your impact

  • Responsibility for the tax analysis of Swiss and foreign investment funds, including UCITS funds and alternative investment structures (e.g. private markets, fund-of-funds, complex holding and target fund structures)
  • End-to-end management of annual Swiss tax reporting for foreign funds – in close cooperation with demanding clients and our global PwC network
  • Managing the calculation of taxable income for Swiss tax purposes using modern PwC tax reporting technologies as well as ensuring publication on the course list of the Swiss Federal Tax Administration
  • Advising our clients on the tax accounting of Swiss investment funds, in particular on income from UCITS and alternative target funds
  • Assuming project and quality responsibility (timelines, governance, risks) with a clear focus on efficiency, scalability and regulatory security
  • Active participation in the automation and digitalization of fund tax reporting processes as an interface between tax experts: inside, IT and customers
  • Mentoring and professional development of team members as well as active contribution to an open, powerful and appreciative team culture
  • Building and maintaining long-term, trusting customer relationships at eye level

Your skill set

  • University degree (Bachelor / Master) in Economics, Finance, Taxation or a higher specialist training in the financial sector.
  • Very good knowledge of English and German
  • At least five years of relevant professional experience in Fund Tax Reporting, Fund Accounting or equivalent function
  • Experience in dealing with UCITS structures and alternative investment vehicles, ideally in an international environment
  • Sovereign handling of complex fund structures
  • Strong sense of responsibility, entrepreneurial thinking and joy in advancing topics
  • Affinity to technology-driven and data-based solutions
  • Team-oriented personality with high social competence and clear communication
