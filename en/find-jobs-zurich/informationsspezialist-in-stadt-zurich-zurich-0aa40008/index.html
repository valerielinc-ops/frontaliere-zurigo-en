Full description

To strengthen our team in the Information Management department, we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by arrangement, limited in time to 31. December 2027 a new team member.

Tasks

  • They support the information specialists in consolidating existing information stocks and documentation as well as processing open implementation orders independently and reliably.
  • They research, structure and update information and digital content and ensure their quality and findability in repositories and content management systems.
  • They prepare professional content in accordance with the target group in text, image and digital formats and participate in the optimization of templates, tools and digital tools.
  • They undertake defined research, coordination and documentation work for knowledge security as well as for the preparation of migration, digitalisation and development projects.

Profile

  • EFZ Information and Documentation, Mediamatics or comparable training or ongoing Bachelor's degree in Information Science or related field.
  • Practical experience in the structured processing of information and data as well as in the editorial and visual processing of content
  • High IT affinity and secure handling of Microsoft 365
  • Experience with content management systems and common editorial, analysis or design tools such as WordPress, InDesign etc.
  • Rapid comprehension and an exact, systematic and careful working method
  • Flexibility, reliability, personal responsibility and strong service orientation
