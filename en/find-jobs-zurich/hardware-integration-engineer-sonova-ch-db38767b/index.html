Purpose of the Job

Hardware development of hearing instrument systems

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • Develop product specific hearing system solutions
  • Hardware integration of all sub-systems
  • Define electroacoustics transducers for hearing systems
  • Develop software tools for measurement systems
  • Build hearing system prototypes
  • Determine and define limits, test criteria and quality characteristics of hearing system hardware
  • Know and follow Sonova’s standard operating procedures according to the training requirements
  • Contribute to the R&D development process: planning, review, refinement of product increments
  • Contribute to interdisciplinary agile development teams and participate in the team rituals
  • Support the hiring process
  • Support onboarding for new hires, act as primary contact person for new hires if needed
  • Share and learn R&D knowledge in communities
  • Create intellectual property and support IP process

Requirements

  • BSc or MSc in Electrical Engineering or similar
  • Wireless technologies, PCB design engineering
  • Measurement experience, electronic hardware design, Design of wireless systems
  • Self-motivated, pro-active
  • Willing to learn and share knowledge
  • Reliable, responsible, able to take accountability
  • Positive thinking
  • System thinking
  • Flexible, creative, solution-oriented
  • Quality awareness
  • Open and transparent also about own work
  • Credible
  • Team-player, inclusive mindset
  • Good communication skills
  • Able to work in a distributed, diverse collaboration environment
  • Good self-leadership and awareness of responsibilities
  • Very good knowledge in:
  • Electrical Engineering
  • Acoustics
  • Measurement Technologies
  • RF Engineering
  • Soldering, gluing, handling small parts to build prototypes.
  • Good English and German skills in both oral and written communication
