Il tuo futuro inizia

You give Swisscom a face and inspire our customers with your personality and expertise. Are you ready...

  • to inspire with your fascination for the products and trends of the ICT world
  • with your comprehensive product knowledge to win and retain customers
  • Advise precisely tailored to customer needs and search for the optimal solution
  • Safely work with the various databases and tools
  • in addition to your activities on the customer front, also tackle exciting tasks behind the scenes – such as purchasing, goods presentation, storage or sales promotion

At these shop locations we currently offer apprenticeships in this training:

  • Shop Wetzikon
  • Shop Adliswil

What you bring

  • Your place of residence should not be more than an hour from the location of the shop
  • You have completed the compulsory school period at the middle or upper elementary school level with consistently good to very good performance
  • German, mathematics and at least one foreign language (French or English) are among your strengths
  • You are fascinated by the products and trends of the ICT world
  • You convince with your good verbal and written expressive and communication skills – be it in German or a foreign language
  • You attach great importance to good manners and can easily empathize with your counterpart
  • In hectic situations, you keep calm and are physically and psychologically resilient
  • Your curiosity drives you to constantly want to discover and learn new things
  • You know the slightly different training model of Swisscom and want to be part of it
  • At the age of majority, as part of the integrity check, a criminal record is requested before the beginning of the teaching
