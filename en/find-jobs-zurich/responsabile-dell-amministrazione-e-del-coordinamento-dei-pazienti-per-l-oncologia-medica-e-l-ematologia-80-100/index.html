The Clinic for Medical Oncology and Hematology (MOH) looks after patients with cancer and blood diseases. In order for them to focus on their treatment, an administration that can be relied upon is needed in the background. As head of patient administration and coordination, you lead a team of around 15 employees in the scheduling (deadline and treatment planning) and in the administrative secretariat. They are directly subordinated to the medical service of the MOH and ensure that appointments, processes and information between the medical profession, nursing and administration reliably mesh.

Their main tasks

  • Professional and personnel management of the approximately 15-member team of Disposition and Administrative Secretariat
  • Responsibility for efficient scheduling and resource planning that reliably considers medical priorities
  • Design and standardization of administrative processes and further development of medical-administrative interfaces
  • Central contact person for the medical service and care in all administrative matters of disposition and the administrative secretariat
  • Management of digitalisation and process optimisation projects
  • Promotion and development of employees with a view to high service quality

Your profile

  • Completed training as Medical Practice Coordinator / Medical Practice Coordinator (MPK), qualified nurse HF/FH or comparable medical training, ideally supplemented by business or leadership-specific training
  • Several years of management experience in a larger medical practice, an ambulatory or a comparable medical organizational unit
  • Good knowledge of medical procedures, especially in patient administration, scheduling and coordination of complex treatment processes
  • Enjoy leading, developing and improving a team together
  • Structured, pragmatic and solution-oriented working method with a sense for efficient processes
  • Communication strength, assertiveness and enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation

Our offer

You will assume a versatile leadership function at the interface of medicine and administration in a large, specialized university hospital. They actively shape the further development of our administrative processes and digitization and make a significant contribution to ensuring that cooperation between the various professional groups works well.

You can also look forward to the following points

  • Targeted promotion of your leadership and expertise
  • Five weeks of holidays and attractive social benefits
  • Discounts in staff restaurant, public transport and other offers
  • Workplace in the center of Zurich, easily accessible by public transport
