Full description

The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and about 700 trainees are committed to the well-being of our patients every day.

Tasks

  • Versatile and challenging tasks in all areas of neuroradiology
  • Execution and evaluation of the entire spectrum of cerebral imaging (including structural MRI, BOLD-MRI, fMRI, perfusion imaging)
  • Patient and patient care
  • Participation in the emergency service of the clinic
  • Participation in the further education of the Neuroradiology Clinic and the Clinical Neurocenter

Requirements

  • You are a specialist in radiology / neuroradiology
  • You are interested in research and teaching
  • You have good German language skills in word and writing

We offer Our clinic is clinically and scientifically internationally recognized. It covers the entire diagnostic and interventional neuroradiology fully. Apparatus equipment and infrastructure are up-to-date with its own MR center, biplanning neuroangiography units, computer tomography, conventional X-ray and sonography. By connecting to the neurointensive station, Stroke unit, bed stations and the clinical neurocenter, optimal conditions exist.

The Department of Neuroradiology is a training centre in category A (2 years, full qualification) for the two main topics Diagnostic and Invasive Neuroradiology. These are based on the specialist training radiology. Qualified candidates receive a content and time optimized, individually tailored further training at the highest level in the academic environment.
