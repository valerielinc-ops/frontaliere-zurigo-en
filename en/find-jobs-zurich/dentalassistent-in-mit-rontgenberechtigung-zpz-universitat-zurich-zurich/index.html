Centre for Dentistry

Job start 1 December 2026 or by arrangement, temporary

The Center for Dental Medicine of the University of Zurich (ZZM) is an internationally recognized clinic, training and research center. In six specialized clinics and one institute, dentists work at the forefront of knowledge and in interdisciplinary teams to achieve the highest quality in patient treatment, teaching and research. The Clinic for Dental Preservation and Preventive Dentistry (ZPZ) specializes in the maintenance of healthy and the rehabilitation of damaged teeth.

Tasks

  • Assistance at the treatment chair (experience with surgery, endodontology and cerec desired)
  • Intraoral X-ray
  • Preparation and cleaning of the workplace
  • Patient administration such as: telephone service, short correspondence, electric. Agenda

Profile

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as a dental assistant:in with an X-ray certificate (at least 1 year of professional experience)
  • Enjoyment in the treatment, care and advice of patients
  • Flexible, reliable and dedicated team player with a high quality awareness and friendly, well-kept appearance
  • Empathy and joy in caring for patients: inside

A job on cantonal terms in a modern workplace in the heart of Zurich. The position is for the time being limited to one year, with the possibility of a subsequent permanent employment.
