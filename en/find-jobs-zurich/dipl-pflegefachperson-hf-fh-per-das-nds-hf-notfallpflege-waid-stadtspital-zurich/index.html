Full description

Do you want to develop professionally? Then start the two-year postgraduate program Emergency Care HF at the Waid site!

Tasks

  • You care for, care for and accompany patients in exceptional life situations.
  • You act professionally, independently and independently within your competence framework.
  • You ensured patient acceptance, acceptance and current situation analysis.
  • You are involved in diagnostic and therapeutic measures and the continuous monitoring of vital signs.
  • You are jointly responsible for good interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation to ensure an optimal therapy process.

Profile

  • Completed training as a nurse / nurse specialist HF/FH
  • At least 6 months of professional experience in the acute hospital in Switzerland
  • Motivation and perseverance for the demanding postgraduate studies
  • Team-capable, committed and sympathetic personality with a strong social competence and a high sense of responsibility
  • Readiness for shift work
  • Responsible and self-employed work

We offer

We offer you more than just a job! Our city hospital world is characterized by an open dual culture, an uncomplicated, personal togetherness, respect and appreciation.
