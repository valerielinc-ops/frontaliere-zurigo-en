Full description

PROFILE

  • Completed technical training at ETH, TU, FH level or equivalent qualification
  • Experience in functional safety
  • Knowledge of relevant railway standards (e.g. EN 50126, EN 50716, EN 50129, TS 50701, ISO 27001, ISO 9001) and/or train protection technologies
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Open, communicative, resilient and goal-oriented personality with a high willingness to learn as well as openness to new topics and challenges
  • High motivation to become familiar with new technologies, development processes and toolsStrong analytical and organizational skills as well as a structured working method

TASKS

As a member of the management team, the function includes the management of the Stadler Signalling RAMS department with 14 employees.

The area of responsibility shall include

  • RAMS management
  • Validation
  • Verification
  • Information and Cyber Security
  • Safety demonstration
  • Quality management
  • General admission topics

The professional and personal development of the team as well as the expansion of the existing competences within the department are ensured.

The organization of the department, the planning and prioritization of work, the assignment of tasks and the monitoring of work progress are the responsibility of the function.

Representation of the department in management meetings and in order reviews is also one of the key tasks.

As a member of expert groups, we contribute to strategic decisions and the further development of Stadler Signalling.

In addition, the function represents the RAMS department and Stadler Signalling to experts, regulatory authorities, customers and within the entire Stadler Group.
