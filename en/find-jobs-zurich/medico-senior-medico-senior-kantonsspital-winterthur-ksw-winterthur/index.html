Role overview

The Social Paediatric Center (SPZ) at the Cantonal Hospital Winterthur diagnoses and treats more than 4,500 children and adolescents each year in an ambulatory with the following specializations: child/adolescent psychiatry and psychosomatics, developmental paediatrics, paediatric neurology, special education, school medical service.

In addition, the SPZ runs a therapy station in which children and adolescents with a wide range of psychosomatic and mental disorders are treated in hospital.
