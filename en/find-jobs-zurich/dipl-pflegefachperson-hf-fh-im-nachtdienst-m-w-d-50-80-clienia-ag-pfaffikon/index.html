Full description

For the area of adult psychiatry, Station Park A, with the treatment focus on depression, anxiety disorder and crisis intervention, we are looking for a

Station Park A offers adults between the ages of 18 and 60, support and assistance in crisis situations, difficult phases of life. We work according to current clinical guidelines and findings of evidence-based medicine. The recovery-oriented therapeutic concept of the station combines disorder-specific therapeutic elements with general crisis intervention techniques and milieutherapeutic action factors. Park A focuses on acceptance and commitment therapy

Their tasks

  • Individual, professional care and care of patients during the night
  • Active participation in the therapeutic environment of the station
  • Creating a safe and calm atmosphere
  • Orientation to the station concept in its mode of operation
  • With commitment and openness, they think and act for the benefit of patients
  • Show understanding towards patients and their individual psychological and somatic problems

Your profile

  • You have completed training for the Dipl. Care professional HF
  • You are interested in the psychiatric area
  • They like to work independently and can make targeted decisions
  • You have experience with crisis management and de-escalation
  • They are open-minded and enjoy working with patients who have to cope with crises and are looking for ways to shape a life with the disease in which they feel comfortable.
  • They treat them with empathy and openness.
  • They are a team-capable, communicative and prudent personality and can make independent decisions for the benefit of patients.

Our offer

  • Collegial, open and interdisciplinary working environment
  • Interesting and challenging task in a dynamic, modern environment with advanced employment conditions
  • Careful training of new employees
  • Self-employment
  • Support through specific training
  • Various advantages

For further information, please contact Elisabeth Burtscher, Head of Care for Adult Psychiatry, direct dial +41 71 929 64 05.
