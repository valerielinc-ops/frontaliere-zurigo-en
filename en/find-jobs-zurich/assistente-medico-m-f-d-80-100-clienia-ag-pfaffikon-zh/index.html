Full description

Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for a partner for the External Psychiatric Services Thurgau of Clienia Littenheid AG in Frauenfeld.

The External Psychiatric Services Thurgau in Frauenfeld is an outpatient training institution of category A (3 years) for the specialist title "Psychiatry and Psychotherapy". They have a cantonal care mandate and belong to the Center for External Psychiatric Services of Clienia Littenheid AG.

Their tasks As an assistant physician, they carry out self-responsible assessments and treatments of adults with diseases from the entire psychiatric diagnosis spectrum under the guidance of our staff members. Crisis interventions, cooperation with our interdisciplinary team, networking with external services and authorities and billing according to TARDOC are also part of your responsibilities.

Your profile For this diverse and interesting task, you already have previous knowledge of integrated psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment and interest in social psychiatry. As a committed and relationship-oriented personality, you can flexibly adjust to different treatment situations in your encounter with patients and enjoy working independently.

Our offer For this activity, we offer you a modern and appealing environment with your own office as well as a collegial and process-oriented working environment, in which you are supported by regular coaching and excellent further training offers in your professional and personal development. As attractive working conditions we offer a 42 hour week without weekend or night services.

For further information, Mr. Torsten Held, Chief Physician of the External Psychiatric Services Thurgau of Clienia Littenheid AG in Frauenfeld, tel. +41 71 929 67, available.

We look forward to your application via application tool.
