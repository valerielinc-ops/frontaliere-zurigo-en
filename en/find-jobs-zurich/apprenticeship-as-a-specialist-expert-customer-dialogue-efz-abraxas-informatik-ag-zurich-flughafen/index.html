Full description

You want to know how professional customer communication works and how you can competently support people? As a specialist customer dialogue at Abraxas, you are the first point of contact for our customers and ensure a smooth exchange. In your teaching with us, discover how exciting the connection between communication, service and IT is – and how you can take on responsibility early on. Your role As a specialist in customer dialogue EFZ, you are a specialist in professional customer communication and service. You advise and support our customers on various products and services and are their first point of contact for concerns of all kinds. You accept orders, inquiries and complaints and ensure a solution-oriented processing. You work in the areas of advice, support and communication and have conversations via various channels such as telephone, e-mail or chat. You recognize customer needs, analyze concerns and find suitable solutions. In addition, you ensure that our customers are serviced competently, friendly and efficient at all times. Your profile

  • Completed secondary school with good performance in German and French
  • Contactful and friendly appearance – also on the phone
  • Patient, established and proactive personality who would like to work with customers
  • Joy and interest in IT
  • Language skills in German, French B1 level (advanced:r beginner:in)

Please also note the school requirements required for this teaching and read the documents of the BIS. Our offer Do you need a distance from the digital? With us, pens, paper and describable walls are always available for creative ideas. To switch off, you can turn around the circle or relax in our rest room. You can also look forward to benefits such as discounted fitness training, the opportunity to buy a holiday and an annual excursion for all learners. With us in teaching, you can expect flexible working hours, 6 weeks holidays, financial support for school funds and many other goodies. During your apprenticeship, you will be accompanied by dedicated trainers and take on responsibility early on – because our learners are an important part of the team. Sounds exciting? Then apply for the apprenticeship as a specialist customer dialogue EFZ starting in August 2027.
