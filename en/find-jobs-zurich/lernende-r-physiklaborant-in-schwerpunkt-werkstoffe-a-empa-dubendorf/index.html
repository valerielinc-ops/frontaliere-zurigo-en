Materials science and technology are our passion. With our cutting-edge research, Empa’s approximately 1,100 employees make decisive contributions to the benefit of society for a future worth living in. Empa is a research institution of the ETH Domain. Apprentice physics laboratory (focus on materials) (a) For August 2027, we offer a challenging training place in a fascinating research environment. What awaits you Working at an ETH research institute brings a lot of variety into everyday work. The versatile and exciting tasks in an international environment guarantee young people with an interest in physics, materials, chemistry, technology and electronics an exciting and versatile teaching. Your core tasks include:

  • You will investigate the physical and chemical integrity of metallic materials and their components, as they are applied e.g. in buildings, bridges, tunnels, production plants, mechanical engineering, energy, railway and medical technology
  • You determine material characteristics using state-of-the-art analytical methods and carry out physical measurements and test series with materials and systems
  • You work on experimental facilities, prepare experiments, carry them out and document them
  • You visit the Technical Vocational School in Zurich What do you bring with you
  • Secondary School A with very good performance in the natural sciences, mathematics and linguistic subjects
  • The visit of the BMS is supported
  • Theoretical and practical interest in physics, chemistry, materials, electronics, computer science and technical systems
  • Abstract and analytical thinking, technical understanding and manual skill
  • Good observation and comprehension, care, independent and reliable work
  • Enjoy working in a highly technical and complex work environment
  • Good manners in contact with customers and employees
  • You are convinced to start your future with a professional apprenticeship What we offer Up to approx. Incoming applications will be reviewed in mid-July 2026.
