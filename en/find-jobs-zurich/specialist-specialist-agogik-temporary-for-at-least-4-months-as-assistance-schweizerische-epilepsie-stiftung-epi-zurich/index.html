Full description

Sometimes quite challenging, but never boring: At EPI WohnWerk, no day is like the other. Because the people we accompany each and everyone have their unique life story. Thanks to the cooperation in the interprofessional team, we are well positioned for our working day. Do you also want to be part of this team?

Tasks

  • In the highly structured studios of the day care centers, they accompany and support adults with physical and cognitive impairments as well as highly challenging behaviors in meaningful and comprehensible activities.
  • They carry out various individual and group activities and thus ensure a varied and meaningful everyday routine.

Requirements

  • They have completed training in the social or special education sector and/or have many years of professional experience accompanying people with severe mental and physical impairments as well as challenging behaviours
  • You enjoy working independently, are patient and resilient and can quickly fit into an existing system
  • They have a strong social competence, have a pinch of humor and enjoy working in a team

We offer

  • A living environment that you can help shape with your own ideas
  • Working hours from Monday to Friday, 07.55 to 16.55
  • Own pension fund with above average employer share of 60%
  • Parking and discounts at EPI Park Restaurant and Pharmacy
