Over two billion people rely on Schindler every day. Our many years of experience and innovative strength make us number one in the Swiss elevator and escalator business. Become part of our 150-year success story and shape the future of mobility. Together one floor up as Service technician in the area of repairs (m/f/d) 80-100 % Your day – your plan! Design your 40-hour week according to your needs. With your (e-)business car, which you can also use privately, you are flexible on the road. Learn more in this video Your new challenges:

  • Carrying out repairs and restarts for the purpose of preserving value or converting lifts
  • Replacement repair of defective and/or worn electrical, hydraulic or mechanical components
  • Focus on electrical/electronics or mechanics/hydraulics Your ideal conditions:
  • Completed apprenticeship in the mechanical, electrical or hydraulic sector (e.g. polymechanic, car mechanic, plant and apparatus manufacturer or electrical installer)
  • Some years of professional experience, ideally with customer contact
  • Impeccable manners and a high willingness to learn
  • Good knowledge of German, other languages beneficial
  • Valid driver’s license – you can’t do without it Our benefits:
  • 2-month training at full wage in our lift camp for your optimal preparation for your new job.
  • Own company vehicle, which you can also use privately.
  • Compensation for your lunchtime meals, fair on-call allowance for pikett and the cleaning of your work clothes, which of course will be provided free of charge by us.
