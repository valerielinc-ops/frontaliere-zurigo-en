Role overview

As a university competence centre, we are one of the leading centres for stem and immune cell therapies in Switzerland.

On our highly specialized station, we accompany patients with complex haematological diseases and continuously develop care based on current scientific findings and innovative therapy concepts.

As a specialist, you actively participate in clinical care and support the treatment team in complex issues.

  • As a university competence centre, we are one of the leading centres for stem and immune cell therapies in Switzerland.
  • On our highly specialized station, we accompany patients with complex haematological diseases and continuously develop care based on current scientific findings and innovative therapy concepts.
  • Direct clinical practice with exemplary function in the care of complex haematological-oncological patients as well as support of nurses in demanding situations
  • Counselling and support of patients and their relatives before, during and after cell therapies and chemotherapy with a focus on promoting self-management
