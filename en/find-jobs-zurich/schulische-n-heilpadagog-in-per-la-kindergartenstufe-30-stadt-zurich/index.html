Full description

At our location Schulhaus Gubel in Zurich Oerlikon (school district Glattal) we are looking for 4. January 2027 a school curative teacher for kindergarten level 30%

Tasks

  • Class leadership in close cooperation with the class team
  • Promotion of children according to the goals defined in the SSG, the curriculum 21 and the empowerment-oriented support planning
  • Preparation of funding planning, learning reports and certificates
  • Cooperation with parents or guardians, speech therapists and psychomotor therapists, external specialists and specialists from the regular school
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation within the HPS Gubel team and, if necessary, topic-specific cooperation in working groups for the entire institution

Profile

  • EDK-approved curative education with preference in the area of cognitive impairment or willingness to complete the training part-time
  • Practical experience in the special education area "Cognitive Impairment" (assisted communication, TEACCH, ASS, etc.) or the willingness to familiarize yourself with this task
  • Enjoyment in the promotion and accompaniment of children with cognitive and communicative impairment as well as difficulties in impulse control and control
  • Teamwork, motivation, resilience, humour, flexibility and creativity
  • Professional management of your own class team, as well as readiness and interest in interdisciplinary cooperation

We offer

  • Self-employed and varied work in a professional team
  • Exciting interdisciplinary tasks in the special education sector in cooperation with the regular school
  • Conditions of employment according to the requirements of the City of Zurich (VLT) in monthly wages
  • Regular working hours
  • Training opportunities

About us

The Heil Pädagogschule der Stadt Zürich is a special day school for 4- to 15-year-old children with a cognitive and/or multiple impairment. We provide a supportive environment for pupils who are taught in separate classes in different school buildings in the city of Zurich and focus on the interdisciplinary promotion of their strengths. Our teams at the various locations in the city of Zurich consist of curative school teachers, social teachers, specialists and assistants.
