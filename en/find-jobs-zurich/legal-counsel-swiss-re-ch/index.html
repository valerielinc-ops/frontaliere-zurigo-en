Help Shape the Future of Insurance—From a Legal and Regulatory Frontline We’re looking for a strategic, forward-thinking Legal Counsel to join our North America team and play a key role in supporting a complex, fast-moving (re)insurance business. This is a high-impact role at the intersection of regulation, governance, and business strategy—ideal for a lawyer who thrives in a dynamic environment and is energized by working closely with senior leaders, regulators, and cross-functional teams. You will help drive proactive regulatory reporting, strong governance practices, and business-enabling legal solutions across a diversified insurance platform. If you’re looking to combine technical excellence, regulatory insight, and real business impact, we’d love to hear from you. What You’ll Do Lead Regulatory & Governance Efforts

  • Take a hands-on, proactive role in regulatory reporting and disclosures across U.S. insurance entities
  • Partner with Compliance, Risk, Finance, and business leaders to deliver clear, accurate, and timely regulatory submissions
  • Anticipate regulatory developments and translate them into practical guidance and action plans
  • Support interactions with regulators, including preparing materials for examinations, inquiries, and meetings
  • Help strengthen and streamline governance frameworks, reporting processes, and internal controls

Be a Trusted Business Partner

  • Serve as a go-to legal advisor for underwriting, claims, and operations teams, in particular for Swiss Re's Corporate Solutions Property & Specialty book across the United States
  • Provide practical, solution-oriented guidance on insurance regulatory matters, in particular in relation to Property & Specialty
  • Support innovative and complex insurance transactions, including bespoke and structured deals
  • Collaborate on strategic initiatives, including in relation to Swiss Re's international programs and new business opportunities

Work Across a Global, Collaborative Network

  • Partner with colleagues across Legal, Compliance, Risk, Finance, and Actuarial
  • Contribute to cross-functional projects that shape how we operate and grow
  • Engage with global teams on matters with broader business or regulatory impact

About the Team You will join the Corporate Solutions Legal North America team, a group of 10 experienced lawyers/legal professionals supporting a broad commercial insurance platform across the U.S. and Canada. The team works closely with the business as trusted advisors; balancing growth ambitions with regulatory discipline and reputational protection. We operate in a fast-paced, matrix environment and collaborate extensively with colleagues across regions and functions.

About You

What You Bring

Experience

  • 6–10+ years of legal experience in a top law firm, insurance company, or financial services institution
  • Strong background in U.S. insurance regulation and governance frameworks
  • Experience with regulatory reporting, filings , or supervisory engagement preferred
  • Familiarity with insurance transactions, policy wordings, or structured deals is a plus

Skills & Mindset

  • Commercially-minded and proactive—you anticipate issues before they arise
  • Able to simplify complex legal and regulatory topics into clear, actionable advice
  • Strong communicator with experience preparing senior-level or executive-facing materials
  • Comfortable operating in a fast-paced, matrixed environment
  • Collaborative, adaptable, and driven by delivering solutions, not just identifying risks

Why Join Us

  • Regulatory influence: Play a meaningful role in shaping how we engage with regulators across the U.S.
  • Business exposure: Partner closely with underwriting and deal teams on complex transactions
  • Collaborative culture: Join a high-performing, supportive legal team that values innovation and growth

Additional Information

  • Bar admission in at least one U.S. state required (IL, NY, or MO preferred)
  • Travel: Limited (<10%)
  • Other languages are a plus
