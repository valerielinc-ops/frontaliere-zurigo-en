Full description

Central computer science

Job opening immediately or by agreement, indefinitely

Our team is responsible for the design, setup and operation of cloud services, managed Linux services and container services within the central computer science department of the university. For the implementation of our cloud strategy, we are looking for a motivated, committed and initiative personality.

Tasks

  • You will participate in the further development of our Azure Landing Zone and the operation of our cloud services including customer support
  • Active design and participation in building a multi-cloud environment
  • In the public cloud (Azure, GCP or AWS) you feel at home and have already migrated and implemented projects there
  • With your passion, you inspire our institutes and support them in a variety of ways and advise them competently on technical issues as well as technical problems and implement projects from PoC to migration.
  • With your help, build processes, deployments, testing and monitoring are realized using cloud technologies
  • You use AI consciously and purposefully to support you in development tasks
  • You are happy to take responsibility for the availability, stability, security and performance of the infrastructure
  • Your tasks also include implementing and maintaining interfaces for connecting different systems and architectures.
  • You continuously strive to improve and refine automation processes to achieve even greater efficiency and effectiveness

Profile

  • Higher technical training in computer science or comparable education/training
  • At least 3 years of experience in a DevOps/Systems Engineer role
  • Familiarity with Versioncontrol (Git) and CI (GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Jenkins, etc.)
  • Experience with an IaC tool such as OpenTofu / Terraform or Pulumi
  • Knowledge of Software Engineering
  • In-depth knowledge of at least one public cloud (Azure, GCP or AWS)
  • Openness to agile working methods
  • Kubernet knowledge desirable
  • Team-capable, cooperative and committed personality
  • High level of personal responsibility and independence
  • Good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
