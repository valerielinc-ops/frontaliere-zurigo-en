Welcome to the Medbase Zentrale Winterthur

Our modern office building is located just three minutes from Winterthur Train Station, in an urban setting that offers ideal conditions for focused work and creative collaboration. At our headquarters, around 150 specialists from the fields of IT, HR, marketing and finance work hand in hand to jointly develop innovative solutions and achieve our corporate goals. With flexible workplaces and modern meeting pods, we promote an open and efficient working culture. At Medbase, we attach great importance to teamwork and the exchange between departments in order to be successful together. Here, colleagues from our sister companies such as Zur Rose, WePractice or the dental center meet, which further enriches the interdisciplinary exchange.

Many locations. A common goal. Your leadership.

You know the Swiss pharmacy market, think entrepreneurially and like to design and move? As regional manager, you will continue to develop our pharmacies in the Winterthur/Eastern Switzerland region economically, qualitatively and in terms of personnel. You shape the future of the locations together with the management of your region, you combine economic responsibility with pharmaceutical-medical and customer-oriented quality.

You can do that with us.

  • Effective management : You will continue to develop the management of your region, strengthen its leadership skills and make important contributions to efficient teams.
  • Entrepreneurial control : You combine economic thinking with a good sense of the situation on the ground. Relevant key figures and identified leverage help you identify potential, set the right priorities and achieve sustainable results together with the locations.
  • Further develop pharmacy and customer experience : With a clear focus on the needs of our customers, you strengthen consulting and service, further develop the pharmaceutical-medical offer and ensure attractive, efficient locations. Together with the central departments and locations, you will successfully implement strategic initiatives.
  • Change and growth : You observe the local market environment, recognize opportunities and developments at an early stage and actively shape changes. You accompany the successful integration of new locations and ensure that new impulses have a lasting impact at the locations and in your region.

What you bring

  • Education and training: You bring a higher education or training in business administration, retail trade, healthcare, pharmacy or a similar direction.
  • Market knowledge: You know the Swiss pharmacy market very well from practice and understand its regulatory framework as well as the relevant economic levers.
  • Leadership skills: Several years of experience in the management of decentralized or branch-based organizations distinguish you. You develop leaders clearly, bindingly and appreciatively.
  • Retail and customer understanding: You combine sound pharmacy knowledge with a strong sense of customer needs, sales, location quality, product presentation and efficient personnel use
  • Entrepreneurial thinking: Key figures and business relationships as well as affinity for marketing and sales are important basis for your decision-making. From this you derive priorities and concrete measures.
  • Implement effectively: With decision-making power, communication skills and creative will, you advance topics and teams and ensure sustainable implementation.
  • Digital and close to home : You have a very good knowledge of German, move safely in the digital world of work and are happy to stay at the stand regularly
