Canton of Zurich Education director Vocational school Winterthur Vocational school teacher for vocational education Specialist care (FaBe) with specialization in children (KIN), per autumn semester 2026/27/ 30

  • 40% The Vocational School Winterthur (BFS) The Vocational School Winterthur (BFS) is a competence center for social professions and retail trade. Around 4000 students are taught here by about 200 teachers. The school is organized in two departments: the Social Professions Department, in which the professional women and assistants are trained (FaBe) and the Assistants/Assistenten Gesundheit & Soziales (AGS), as well as the General Department of Retail Trade Professions (EFZ and EBA) and Pre-Lecture. Tasks
  • Teaching of the skills of action in the field of vocational education KIN in the Department of Social Professions on two days of the week (30-400)
  • Working in the department, working groups and projects
  • Creating computer-based, practice- and action-oriented teaching units
  • Working in the further development of the field of vocational education FaBe
  • Participation in internal school events and in-service training in internal school teaching concepts
  • Requirements
  • Professional education at tertiary level (highest professional level) e.g., Social Work FH (specialized profile of social education), degree in (special) pedagogy / psychology (MSc), social pädagogik HF, child education HF
  • Preferably you have an education and several years of experience as a vocational school teacher
  • practical experience in the extra-family care area (hort, crib, social pedagogical institutions etc.)
  • willingness to teach professional subjects with a high theoretical practice transfer
  • team skills, resilience and joy to accompany young people during their vocational training
  • joy in innovation
  • IT skills and willingness to teach computer-based teaching
  • We offer
  • employment with a workload of 8 weekly lessons
  • a competent, collegial team
  • supporting school management and departments
  • an innovative school in which teaching development topics are addressed in an internal training programme
  • comprehensive mentoring programme for new teachers
