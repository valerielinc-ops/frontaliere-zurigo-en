Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Secondary School and Vocational Education Office Lawyer/in 80% KOMPETENT UND VERNETZT DENKEND The Secondary School and Vocational Education Office is the competence and service center for vocational education, high schools, higher vocational education and training in the Canton of Zurich. We support and accompany the secondary schools, the vocational colleges, the providers of vocational preparation years and the teaching companies. The area of law is located in the staff of the MBA and ensures the legal specialist and consulting competence within the office. We are looking for a lawyer (80%) by 1 February 2027 or by agreement. The term of office is limited to 31.01.2028. In this versatile and responsible function, you are the central legal contact for the administration and the departments of the MBA. They provide competent advice in exciting legal fields such as secondary school, vocational training, submission, IT and data protection law and thus make an important contribution to the further development of education. In addition, you actively participate in legislative projects, write injunctions and opinions in appeal proceedings and prepare the basis for decision-making for the Government Council, the Education Council and the Education Director. The examination and drafting of contracts is also part of your varied field of activity.

  • You are a committed and experienced lawyer (MLaw or lic. iur.) with proven knowledge of general administrative law. Ideally, you will bring additional know-how in IT and data protection law and are interested in legal issues related to digitalization in the education sector. Thanks to your quick comprehension, you can also quickly penetrate complex issues and identify the key legal issues. You work in a structured, independent and meticulous way – and keep an overview even in demanding situations as a solution-oriented personality convinces you with linguistic precision, a confident appearance and pronounced negotiating skills. You communicate clearly, addressee-friendly and contribute your expertise effectively. Are you interested in working in the service of education and youth?
  • We offer you a challenging, versatile and independent job in a motivated and collegial team, contemporary employment conditions (including home office) and a workplace close to Zurich Central Station. Are you interested? Are you motivated to engage in a growing and changing organization? Then we look forward to your contact and application. Marija Jenny, Head of Law (+41 43 259 78 31) is happy to answer your questions. Applications from job placements as well as applications via e-mail are not considered for this vacancy. Additional information can be found on the Internet at www.zh.ch/mba.

Pensum: 80 %
