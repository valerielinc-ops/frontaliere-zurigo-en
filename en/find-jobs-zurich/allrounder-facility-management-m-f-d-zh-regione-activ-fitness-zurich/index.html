Full description

Do you appreciate variety and a versatile area of responsibility? In an exciting environment you ensure the availability of the fitness and wellness facilities of Activ Fitness. You manage some studios in your area and are the first point of contact for defects. Become part of our dynamic team now and apply today.

Luogo di lavoro: ACTIV FITNESS, Studios in the Zurich region, Badenerstrasse 120, 8004 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned. An application letter or video is desired for this position.

Mansioni

  • You maintain the building technical facilities independently and in the team
  • With your high level of service, you ensure the operational readiness of the studios
  • You will perform general repair work in the assigned region and be the first contact person for defects
  • Through regular maintenance and support of all fitness equipment, home technology and sanitary facilities, you make an important contribution to the satisfaction of our customers
  • You plan your assignments independently and ensure an efficient day organization
  • You support in the organization and coordination of external companies

Requirements

  • First professional experience: in a similar field
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): in the technical field, preferably as a maintenance specialist or electric engineer
  • Self-employed and responsible work and willingness to provide services
  • Basic technical training and experience in the maintenance of building technology and sanitary facilities
  • Category B driving licence
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central location: Our studios are located in a well-developed central location
  • Mobile subscription: National flat subscription from CHF 10.– / month (Swisscom /digitec connect)
  • Migros Bank: expense-free private and investment account with preferential interest
  • Employee offers: Various discounts in the area of holidays and insurance (Hertz up to 10%, Generali 25%, Axa insurance up to 15%, etc.)
  • Du-culture: We stand for a respectful coexistence at eye level

Contatto

Application and contact Céline Lässer

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Expert Discussion: In a specialist discussion, specific knowledge relevant for the execution of the job is requested by the manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, the team. If possible, there is time to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Test work: A trial day or half day gives the opportunity to get to know the future working environment and the team members better. And vice versa, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition: After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
