Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With over 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric services offered by Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in outpatient, day clinic and inpatient settings. The group employs around 1800 people and generated sales of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For the hotel industry, we are looking for a

Your tasks

  • Maintenance cleaning of ward and patient rooms, offices, toilet facilities, corridors, stairs, kitchens, seminar rooms, etc. according to specified systems and processes in compliance with hygienic guidelines
  • Professional operation of machines and cleaning equipment
  • Professional disposal
  • Participate in team meetings
  • Weekend and holiday assignments are no problem for you
  • Independent ordering of materials
  • Deputy function of the hotel manager on the ward in her absence

Your profile You have several years of professional experience in cleaning or housekeeping and have very good written and spoken German. You are service-oriented, willing to learn, responsible, a team player, flexible, resilient, balanced and have respectful, polite manners. Computer skills and car skills are an advantage.

Our offer You can expect an unusual environment with interesting tasks in a motivated hotel team.

  • 5 weeks of vacation, from the 50. 27 days of age and from 60. Age 32 days
  • Mobility concept with the option of a ZVV bonus pass or discounted parking spaces
  • Excellent catering options at very fair prices and much more...
