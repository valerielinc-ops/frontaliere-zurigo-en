Full description

Education Directorate Vocational School Uster Vocational School teacher mbA for the subject German (16 weekly lessons) 69% From spring semester 2027 we are looking for a committed and initiative vocational school teacher mbA. You accompany and promote our learners in basic vocational education (apprenticeship lessons) and our students in vocational education 2. With us, you have the chance to develop new training concepts in an innovative and multifunctional learning and working environment and to implement your ideas creatively. Take a look at our website and get an impression. Your tasks

  • You impart knowledge in the subject of German and/or the corresponding area of competence (Lehre Kauffrau/Kaufmann EFZ) and accompany our learners on their way in basic vocational training or in a training course of Berufsmaturität 2.
  • In our modern learning environment, you develop and implement creative and future-oriented training concepts.
  • You promote the social development of our learners and support them in their professional and personal development.
  • With your enthusiasm for the subject, you inspire the learners and motivate them to realize their full potential. Your profile
  • You have completed a degree in German studies (major subject) and the corresponding teaching diploma.
  • It gives you pleasure to inspire young people and inspire them with new ideas.
  • You’ll be creative and enjoy developing and applying innovative teaching methods and have a high technological affinity to effectively integrate modern tools and digital media into the classroom.
  • Your social competence helps you to promote the personal development of the learners and to accompany them individually.
  • Your own initiative, team spirit and sense of responsibility characterize you. Our offer
  • A state-of-the-art infrastructure with diverse learning and work opportunities.
  • A motivated, collegial and creative team working together to further develop the educational landscape.
  • The opportunity to contribute your ideas in a dynamic environment and to actively shape the future of vocational training. Become part of our team and shape the educational world of tomorrow. We look forward to meeting you. If you have any questions, Debora Matthey (debora.matthey@bzu.ch ), Head of Human Resources, is happy to apply for you.
