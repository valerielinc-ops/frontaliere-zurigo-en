About us

We enchant the world with chocolate! Lindt & Sprüngli (Switzerland) AG is a subsidiary of the global Lindt & Sprüngli Group with headquarters and production facilities in Kilchberg on Lake Zurich. In Altendorf we operate our own logistics centre, while the Lindt Cocoa Center in Olten produces cocoa mass for our own use as well as for other European production facilities. Throughout Switzerland, we proudly operate 15 Lindt shops, including iconic locations such as the Jungfraujoch and the Swiss Transport House in Lucerne. More than 1000 employees from more than 50 nations contribute to our success.

What you do with us

Support the technology team in the implementation of its activities. These include:

  • Optimization of existing products and manufacturing processes
  • Technical project management in the product development process: product development from production feasibility to market launch in cooperation with the product development, marketing, production and quality assurance departments
  • System maintenance and administration tasks
  • Support for technological testing in the assessment of planned production facilities or technical changes and support for their commissioning
  • Troubleshooting at the production plant

When you fit in with us

  • Bachelor's degree in food sciences (employment as part of the dual master's degree at the ZHAW)
  • Ideally already professional experience in the food industry
  • Flair for food production
  • Solution-oriented thinking and initiative
  • Technical understanding, high quality awareness
  • assertiveness and good communication ability
  • Enjoyment of working together in a team
  • High sense of responsibility and reliability
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
