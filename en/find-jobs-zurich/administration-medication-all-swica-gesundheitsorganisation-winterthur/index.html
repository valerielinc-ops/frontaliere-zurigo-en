Full description

Are you consciously looking for a temporary part-time position that can be flexibly reconciled with your studies, further education or your current life situation? Then this task could suit you exactly. For our service processing division, we fill two temporary positions with a workload of 40–60% until the end of April 2027. You mainly work in the home office and come to Winterthur for a team day about every two weeks. During your assignment, you will gain valuable insights into the Swiss healthcare system, be carefully incorporated and accompanied by experienced colleagues. This is how you shape health

  • You make recovery orders to drug manufacturers
  • You keep and maintain overview lists in Excel
  • You control and process payment runs
  • You create invoices and collect relevant data in our systems
  • You support the team in various administrative tasks

This sets you apart

  • Ongoing studies or completed training in the commercial sector, pharmacy or health care
  • You are familiar with Excel and have already used it regularly
  • Drug knowledge is beneficial, but not a must
  • Enjoy administrative tasks and handling figures and data
  • Your way of working is careful, reliable and structured
  • Stylistic German in Word and Writing

Why SWICA

  • We build on humanity. That is why we focus on openness, respect and trust in our everyday work. We tackle this, take responsibility and think in terms of solutions – together, for our customers. This is also why we have been number 1 in customer satisfaction for many years.
  • We trust you and give you space. Annual working hours, at least 5 weeks of holidays, part-time models and generous home office within the scope of the possibilities of the job – you make your everyday life largely flexible.
  • We value and reward your work with a fair market wage.
  • Because health is everything. With our company health management and region-specific offers, we take care of your well-being – so that you are healthy and energized.
  • We think long term. With three savings plans, pensum-dependent coordination deduction and benefits well above the legal minimum, you ensure good for your future.
