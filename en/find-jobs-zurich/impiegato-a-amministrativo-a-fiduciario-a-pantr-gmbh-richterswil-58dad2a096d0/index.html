Full description

For an established, owner-managed trust office in Richterswil on Lake Zurich, we are looking for a committed, independent and trustworthy reinforcement. In this versatile function, you benefit from short decision-making processes away from rigid corporate structures and manage a varied mandate portfolio. As of September 2026 or by appointment, we are looking for you!

This is your job

  • Payroll & Personnel: Self-employed processing of wage runs for monthly and hourly wage mandates as well as the entire personnel administration (social insurance, withholding taxes, year-end declarations).
  • Finance: Independent management of accounts payable, including payments via e-banking and digital platforms, as well as cooperation in the preparation of interim and annual accounts.
  • Taxes & operations: Assistance in the preparation of company accounts, preparation of VAT bills and preparation of tax returns for natural and legal persons.
  • Support: Direct customer contact and support for general administrative inquiries.

This is what you bring with you

  • training: Completed initial commercial training (e.g. KV apprenticeship, commercial school); Further training in escrow or finance and accounting is a welcome advantage.
  • Experience: Ideally, several years of solid practical experience in the field of trust or in a qualified accounting function.
  • IT & Language: Good MS Office knowledge and familiarity with common accounting software (preferably Topal, Bexio or Abacus) as well as stylish German in word and writing.
  • Personality: A careful, accurate and structured way of working characterized by reliability, responsibility and integrity.

This is what awaits you from the customer

  • Precautions & Benefits: Excellent social benefits with a 70% takeover of the pension fund contributions by the employer and the complete takeover (100%) of all health and accident contributions.
  • Flexibility & mobility: Flexible working hours within block hours, home office options, 5 weeks holidays and a free half-tax subscription (cheap outdoor parking is also available if required).
  • Workplace: A modern, digital workplace in an appreciative work environment with an excellent working atmosphere.
