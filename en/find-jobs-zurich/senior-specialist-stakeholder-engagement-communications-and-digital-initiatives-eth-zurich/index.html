Senior Specialist - Stakeholder Engagement, Communications and Digital Initiatives

100%, Zurich, permanent

This Senior Specialist supports the Vice President in advancing ETH Zürich's priorities in artificial intelligence, digital transformation, innovation, and emerging technologies, with particular responsibility for stakeholder engagement, project coordination and communications. This role includes monitoring developments in these areas, coordinating the stakeholders involved in related projects and initiatives, and ensuring internal and external partners. It also ensures that these activities and their outcomes are communicated effectively to both internal and external audiences.

Focusing on specific projects, the Senior Specialist maintains relationships with key stakeholders and coordinates their involvement across initiatives, managing engagement and follow-up, to keep projects on track. Communications, including narrative development and content for the VP's external engagements, is an integral part of the role.

Job description

  • Drive selected initiatives in AI, digital transformation, and emerging technologies, from design through delivery
  • Coordinate cross-functional projects, keeping timelines, deliverables, and stakeholders aligned
  • Manage relationships with key stakeholders across academia, industry, government, philanthropy, and international partners
  • Coordinate engagement activities and follow-up
  • Prepare analyses and briefing materials to support decision-making on key initiatives
  • Draft speeches, talking points, briefing notes, and articles, and support the VP's public appearances and thought leadership
  • Develop presentations, storylines, and background materials for leadership meetings, conferences, and public events
  • Oversee and contribute to the visibility and positioning of the VP's and VPWW's activities, partnerships, and initiatives

Key Interfaces

  • VP and Chief of Staff
  • Communications and marketing teams
  • Academic units, research groups, and technology transfer teams
  • Industry, government, philanthropic, and international partners
  • External relations, events, and protocol teams

Profile

  • 10+ years of experience in stakeholder relations and partnerships, connecting business, government and community stakeholders
  • Proven track record in managing a portfolio of key accounts
  • Comfortable operating in cross-border, multicultural, and multilingual environments
  • Experience from an academic environment is a plus
  • Excellent writing skills; able to translate complex topics into clear speeches, talking points, and articles
  • Genuine interest in AI, digital transformation, innovation, and emerging technologies
  • Independent, structured, and discreet, with sound judgement in a fast-moving environment
  • Proficient English and German
