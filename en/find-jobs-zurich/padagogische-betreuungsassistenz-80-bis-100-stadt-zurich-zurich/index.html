Full description

At our location in the school building Staudenbühl in Zurich Seebach, we are looking for an educational assistance 80% to 100% by appointment.

Tasks

  • Pedagogical accompaniment and promotion of pupils in the practical life as well as in the school sector
  • Support for teachers in administration and in the preparation and follow-up of teaching
  • Assistance and guidance in both the hygienic and social areas of children’s lives
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation in the school and care team
  • Participation in the organisation and organisation of the care period as well as accompaniment in individual and joint leisure activities and school events as well as participation in meetings, school interviews, in-school training, etc.

Profile

  • Willingness to engage with students with special needs
  • As much experience as possible in working with children in the field of healing education
  • Motivated, open, resilient personality with a positive human image
  • Great flexibility and very good team and communication skills
  • good knowledge of German orally and in writing (min. B2)

We offer

  • Self-employed and varied work in a professional team
  • Exciting interdisciplinary tasks in the special education sector
  • Possibility of active participation in our school
  • Conditions of employment in accordance with the requirements of the City of Zurich
  • Regular working hours

About us

The Heilpäschule der Stadt Zürich is a special day school for 4- to 15-year-old children with cognitive and/or multiple impairments. We offer the pupils who are taught in separate classes in different school buildings in the city of Zurich a supportive environment and place the focus of the interdisciplinary promotion in their strengths.

Our teams at the various locations in the city of Zurich consist of school curative educators, social educators, specialists, students and assistants.
