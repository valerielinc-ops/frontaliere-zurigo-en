As the first real estate service provider, we offer owners and tenants a comprehensive package of services in the area of commercial real estate: from center and mixed-use site management to commercial management, CREM, special properties and commercial management to the attractiveness, marketing and strategic development of commercially used properties. For our area, we are looking for an entrepreneurially thinking, leadership-strong and highly networked personality as Regional Manager Commercial Real Estate Region East (m/f/d) Zurich (Opfikon), 80 - 100% This is what moves you:

  • You lead and develop the subordinate Real Estate Area Directors and promote a powerful, customer-oriented organization

  • You maintain sustainable relationships with owners, tenants and other stakeholders and represent Wincasa professionally on the market

  • You work together with the specialist departments across departments and ensure compliance with our quality standards
  • You recognize market potential, acquire new mandates and actively drive the further development of our business area.
  • You have a university degree or a comparable education with corresponding further education as well as several years of management experience in a comparable function

  • You are very well connected in the real estate industry and know the needs of the owner side
  • You convince with your entrepreneurial thinking and your strong customer and service orientation

  • You communicate stylishly, appear confident and have a strong negotiating and assertiveness.
