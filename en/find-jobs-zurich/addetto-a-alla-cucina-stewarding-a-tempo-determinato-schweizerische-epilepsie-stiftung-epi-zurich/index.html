Full description

The Swiss Epilepsy Foundation (EPI) is a multi-saving company. It includes the EPI WohnWerk, the EPI Spitalschule, the Lengg High School, the donation Dapples, a seminar center with restaurant and the EPI Central Services. In addition, the Swiss Epilepsie Foundation, together with another foundation, forms the body of the Lengg AG Clinic, a clinic for epilepsy and neurorehabilitation. Around 1000 employees work for these social tasks. The area is located on the outskirts of Zurich, with a fantastic view of Lake Zurich and the Alps. Tasks:

  • Cleaning work according to HACCP regulations and standards in the kitchen, as well as inserts on different dishwasher lines
  • Working with the food chain service, production in the cold and warm kitchen
  • Compliance with HACCP regulations
  • Occasionally used on large occasions such as banquets and buffets
  • Working in shiftwork with working hours between 6:00 and 19:45 Requirements:
  • Completed training as a kitchen worker / kitchen employee EBA
  • Very good German knowledge (min. level B2)
  • Multiannual professional experience in a similar position, ideally in a large kitchen
  • Professional experience in the preparation of cold and hot dishes
  • Clean, precise and speditive operation We offer:
  • Monthly schedules with regulated free weekends and flexible Fridays
  • 5 weeks vacation, from 50. Age 6 weeks
  • Own pension fund with an above-average employer share
  • Participation in semi-tax
  • Benefits in our pharmacy
  • Employee discount in our public restaurant Kitchen Around 40 employees prepare around 1200 meals a day fresh and in different forms. We supply the EPI Park restaurant and the home and hospital area. The preparation of events is also part of our culinary offer.
