Original description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility.

For our team of capacity management of adult psychiatry and psychotherapy we are looking for 1. October 2026 or by agreement

Medical practice assistant (MPA) / Medical practice assistant or specialist Health / Health specialist (FaGe)

80-100%

Their tasks

  • Professional receipt and processing of patient registrations and assignments from the entire care network
  • Planning, coordination and scheduling of office hours as well as stationary admissions – including the associated administrative processes
  • Written and oral communication with patients and internal and external colleagues
  • Close cooperation with medical colleagues and nurses of capacity management
  • Active participation in the further development and optimization of our processes
  • Completed training as an MPA or FaGe (for foreign diplomas: proof of recognition of the Swiss Red Cross)
  • Experience in a comparable function in healthcare is beneficial
  • High technical, social and self-competence, resilience, flexibility and analytical thinking skills
  • Personal initiative and pleasure in taking on responsibility in a dynamic environment
  • Appreciative attitude towards people with mental illnesses and in dealing with different professional groups
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Together for people: Interdisciplinary cooperation for holistic psychiatric care
  • Strong in the team: appreciative cooperation and mutual support
  • Shaping the future actively: Participation in change and development processes
  • Future provision with added value: 60% employer contribution to the pension fund
  • Sustainable travel: participation in the ZVV-BonusPass or general subscription
  • Well taken care of in everyday work: discounted food offers or lunch checks
