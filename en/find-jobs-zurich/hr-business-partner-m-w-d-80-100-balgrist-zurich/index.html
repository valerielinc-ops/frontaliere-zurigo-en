Full description

Our team is moving. We work for our employees on a positive and professional working environment. To this end, we are continuously developing our employer brand and our working methods in HR.

Tasks

  • Sparring partners for managers and employees of various business areas in all personnel-relevant issues
  • Predictive support of personnel and organizational changes thanks to good HR process and business understanding
  • Engagement in HR projects for the continuous development of working methods and services
  • Recruitment of new employees in the assigned areas
  • Ensuring efficient, service-oriented personnel work in cooperation with HR administration and payroll accounting

Requirements

  • Further education or university degree (FH/University) ideally with a business or comparable background, or completed further education in the field of human resources combined with in-depth knowledge of Swiss labour law
  • Mind. 5 years of proven professional experience in a comparable, advisory role
  • Experience in the healthcare industry is beneficial
  • Confident knowledge of German as well as good knowledge of English
  • Pleasure of advice, interest in business and a good look for the essentials
  • Analytical and conceptual skills paired with solution-oriented and pragmatic hands-on mentality
