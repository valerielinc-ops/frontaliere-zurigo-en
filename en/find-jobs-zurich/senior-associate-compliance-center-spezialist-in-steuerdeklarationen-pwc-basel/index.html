Your team

You will become part of our Corporate Tax Compliance Team in Basel within Tax, Legal and Workforce. Our team combines in-depth tax know-how with a pragmatic approach and works closely with other PwC specialists in areas such as deals, legal and accounting. You can expect a collegial environment with short decision-making paths, high professional quality and a pronounced coaching and feedback culture, in which even experienced profiles can contribute their expertise in a targeted manner and further develop it. Your impact

  • Responsibility for the preparation and review of tax returns, as well as the assessment of tax assessments of legal persons.
  • Active role in tax planning, including calculation and assessment of tax provisions.
  • Independent processing of complex issues in the area of corporate tax and tax compliance.
  • Professional cooperation in corporate structuring, reorganization, transactions and tax planning projects.
  • Ensuring high-quality, timely and compliant tax compliance taking into account current legislation.
  • Close cooperation with internal and external stakeholders as well as specific technical support for less experienced team members.

Your skill set

  • Completed training in the tax sector (e.g. trust, university of applied sciences, university) or basic commercial education with sound further education.
  • Approximately 5 years of practical experience in tax declaration and tax compliance of legal entities, preferably in a consulting, trust or industrial environment.
  • Very good knowledge of German and very good knowledge of English in word and writing.
  • Very good understanding of tax contexts and ability to analyze complex issues in a structured and pragmatic way.
  • High personal responsibility, quality awareness and a secure attitude towards customers: inside and tax authorities.
  • Very good MS Office knowledge
  • Experience with tax software and digital compliance processes is an advantage.
