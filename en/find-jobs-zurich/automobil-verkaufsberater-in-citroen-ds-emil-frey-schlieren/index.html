What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family company with over 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessories as well as the provision of financial services. Emil Frey AG Schlieren is the official sales and service partner of the Citroën, DS, Opel, Toyota and Lexus brands and has a car body and spare parts warehouse. Their tasks
