Full description

About the position As an automated (electronic) test field, you test and document our products before delivery to customers worldwide. You perform Factory Acceptance Tests (FAT) at our production site in Zurich, analyze errors and ensure that our generator circuit breakers meet the highest quality and safety requirements.

You can do that.

  • Preparation and construction of test objects for conducting tests
  • Performing routine and factory acceptance tests (FAT) with international customers
  • Assessment and documentation of test results
  • Detection and correction of mechanical and electrical errors

You bring that with you

  • Basic electrotechnical training completed (e.g. automatic engineer EFZ, electrical installer EFZ)
  • Experience in reading and understanding electrical schemes
  • Good knowledge of German and English
  • Enjoying practical troubleshooting and technical challenges

More about us As the world’s only production site for generator circuit breakers at Hitachi Energy, our business continues to grow. That is why we are investing specifically in our infrastructure, testing technology and employees in the coming years. Exciting technical challenges, international customer contacts and the opportunity to actively shape a growing and future-proof environment await you.
