Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We advise individuals and companies about money, manage assets and offer attractive financial services. As we are growing strongly, we are looking for reinforcements at our headquarters at Paradeplatz in Zurich.

  • You relieve our consultants administratively in the data collection and master data maintenance.
  • You collect information about the personal and financial situation of our customers in the customer system and thus create an important basis for our success.
  • You archive customer records and ensure that all relevant information from the documents has been transferred to the customer system.
  • With your work, you guarantee a high data quality in the electronic customer systems.
  • You are constantly expanding your financial knowledge so that you can use it effectively in your work.
  • Commercial training completed
  • Ideally, first professional experience in banking or Precautionary area
  • Terms such as freedom of movement, obligations and pillar 3b policy are not foreign words
  • Reliable, independent and precise working methods, as well as experience with MS-Office
  • Interest in pension and financial issues
  • Very good knowledge of German, good knowledge of French and/or Italian are advantageous
  • Flexible working hours and working days, so that there is more time for further education, family, sports and projects that are important to you.
  • A careful introduction including detailed work instructions.
  • An uncomplicated, dynamic team of students and re-entrants.
  • A growing company that you can help shape with your own initiative and commitment.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
