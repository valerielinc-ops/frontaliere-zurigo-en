Role overview

You work in our Retirement Day Clinic, affiliated with our outpatient retirement psychiatry and psychotherapy in our outpatient psychiatric center in Frauenfeld.

Under medical supervision, they independently and on a case-by-case basis conduct psychotherapeutic discussions in individual settings, conduct psychotherapeutic group sessions and coordinate the treatment in the interdisciplinary team.

They treat and accompany patients in the second half of life from a broad spectrum of diagnosis with a focus on affective diseases, excluding cognitive disorders.
