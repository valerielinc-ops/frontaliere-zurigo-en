Full description

This awaits you

  • On our highly specialized Stroke Unit you make a decisive contribution to the best possible care of our patients with acute strokes. You accompany those affected in a complex and dynamic environment where every moment counts.
  • In addition, we look after and monitor surgical and medical patients who require continuous monitored monitoring.
  • You are part of an interdisciplinary team working together on a fast and sustainable recovery.
  • You monitor the vital signs, perform neurological assessments and coordinate therapeutic measures. You use your in-depth expertise to professionally care for and accompany patients as well as their relatives. You bring that with you
  • Completed training as a Dipl. Care professional HF/FH
  • Certificate course adult supervision care (ÜWP) or willingness to complete it
  • Enjoying complex care situations and acting quickly
  • High level of technical and social competence and team spirit.
  • Resilience and flexibility in challenging situations Professional advantages Our department-specific offer
  • Innovative environment: Work on a Stroke Unit that is both clinically and scientifically excellent. We attach great importance to applying the latest research findings in order to continuously improve the care of our patients.
  • Specialised team: You are part of a highly motivated and collegial team that works hand in hand and supports each other
  • Career Opportunities: Benefit from extensive training and development opportunities to deepen your expertise.
  • Work-Life-Balance: We offer flexible working hours and a supportive environment that leaves room for your personal needs (write the roster yourself, no service limitations, annual working hours, intermediate services, etc.)
  • Attractive remuneration: You receive a fair and competitive remuneration as well as extensive social benefits (pension fund, daycare, etc.). Attractive working time model
  • Up to 24 additional free days per year, with the same wage
  • The weekly working time* is reduced to 37.8 hours with a workload of 100%, the basic wage remains unchanged
  • An innovative model with a scientifically proven effect: The accompanying study of the University of Bern shows higher employee satisfaction, fewer redundancies and a noticeably better time welfare *This working time model applies to all nurses, health care specialists and nursing aids at the GZO who regularly work in the three-shift system (proportionately for part-time work).
