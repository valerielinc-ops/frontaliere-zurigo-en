Aufgaben

We are looking for a highly motivated, curious and technically skilled Technical Officer to strengthen the CLARIN-CH Coordination Office and help bridge computational and data infrastructures with research in linguistics, digital humanities and related disciplines.

Key responsibilities

Researcher support

  • Provide technical advice and practical support on language data, research data management, data workflows and Data Management Plans (DMPs).
  • Support researchers in selecting and using appropriate standards and infrastructure services.
  • Contribute to the CLARIN-CH FAIRification pipeline , identifying relevant datasets and tools and supporting their integration into CLARIN and CLARIN-CH infrastructures.

Infrastructure and FAIR data

  • Contribute to developing, maintaining and improving technical solutions supporting FAIR and Open Research Data (ORD) practices in collaboration with the technical partners forming the CLARIN-CH ecosystem
  • Support the integration and interoperability of repositories, metadata, data workflows and linguistic resources within the Swiss and European CLARIN ecosystem.
  • Document and improve technical workflows supporting the accessibility, discoverability and reuse of language resources.

Anforderungen

  • MA/MSc or current PhD enrolment in computational/corpus linguistics, digital humanities, language technology, computer science or a related field.
  • Demonstrated technical skills in language technology, data processing and/or research software development, including Python, APIs, JSON/XML, metadata schemas and GitHub/GitLab .
  • Previous experience with research data management, DMP tools, data stewardship and/or research data infrastructures .
  • Good knowledge of FAIR and ORD practices , preferably for language data.
  • Familiarity with CLARIN infrastructures is an advantage.
  • Strong technical curiosity and ability to learn new technologies quickly.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively in an interdisciplinary environment.
  • Good organisational and task-management skills.

Languages

  • Excellent command of English required.
  • Working capacity in German (native or min. B2) required.
  • French and/or Italian is an advantage.
