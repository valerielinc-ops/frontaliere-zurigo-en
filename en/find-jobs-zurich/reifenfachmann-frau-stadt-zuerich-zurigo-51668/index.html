Full description

We are the pulse of Zurich and keep the city in motion every 7.5 minutes. In order for this to work reliably and smoothly, a lot moves behind the scenes of the VBZ. Our colleagues work in over 50 professions: in technology, IT, administration, traffic development, offer planning, infrastructure and many other exciting areas of responsibility. Where we drive, Zurich lives. Get on.

Tasks

  • In the Hagenholz garage, everything revolves around the safety and operational readiness of our bus fleet - you change wheels on our buses, check the condition and tread depth of the tires and replace them when necessary.
  • You also mount and remove tires on rims and make sure that the right tyres are available at all times
  • you organize the storage and removal of around 1000 tires, manage the tire warehouse and initiate orders - you open, process and complete the corresponding orders independently
  • In addition, you take buses, exchange vehicles on the lines and support your colleagues in the workshop if necessary
  • a modern workplace with modern infrastructure and high-quality work equipment
  • Your safety and health are our top priority - that's why we provide you with the right protective equipment and ensure a working environment in which you can contribute your strengths
  • the regular working hours from Monday to Friday are supplemented by individual early services, which start about four times a year at 3.30 or 4.00.
  • with your work you make your important contribution to ensuring that the buses of the VBZ are safe, reliable and on time every day
  • The team looks forward to your support and motivation

Profile

  • you have the Federal Professional Examination for Tyre Professionals or an adequate degree
  • You have a category C driving licence
  • You have profound experience in the automotive industry.
  • you are physically resilient and like to work in an environment where your approach is in demand and you sometimes get black hands
  • You appreciate varied activities and tackle where support is needed

We offer

  • A lot of appreciation for what you do: The people of Zurich love their VBZ. Working here is proud.
  • We want you to feel good: We are certified “Friendly Workspace” and are strongly committed to your health.
  • Discover the whole of Switzerland with the GA – and thanks to generous discounts, the family also drives really well.
