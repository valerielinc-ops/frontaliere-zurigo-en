Role overview

In the endoscopy center at USZ Airport “The Circle” we perform gastro-, recto- and colonoscopies and are a center for interventional functional diagnostics.

As part of the preventive colonoscopy, we remove polyps, take tissue samples and perform minor procedures.

In our interdisciplinary ambulatory, we look after patients from the fields of gynecology, gastroenterology and radiooncology as well as the Prevention Center.
