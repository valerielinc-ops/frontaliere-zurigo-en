Population Research Center

Job start 1 February 2027 or by arrangement, temporary

The Institute of Epidemiology, Biostatistics and Prevention and the Population Research Center are leading institutions of the University of Zurich in the fields of public health and population-related research and are looking for 1. 2027 or by agreement a specialist in communication and networking for the SNSF research project "Mapping Mental Health in Municipalities" in municipalities of the Canton of Zurich. The position is for the time being limited to two years. The project examines the mental health and well-being of the population in the Canton of Zurich and will be one of the largest population-based studies in Switzerland with around 80,000 invited persons. Eleven selected communities, including four with a full survey of all adults, produce results that can directly benefit communities, specialist bodies and policymakers. Our goal is to reach population groups that have so far been underrepresented in surveys. For this purpose, we combine classic random samples with practical and participatory approaches via local organizations and networks. This requires trust, sustainable relationships and communication that truly reaches people in communities. That is what we are looking for. You work in an interdisciplinary team of epidemiology, psychology and political science that combines science, community work and project management.

Tasks

They play a central role in networking with communities and local stakeholders as well as in recruitment and communication, in close cooperation with the project team. Its tasks shall include in particular:

  • Building networks: The aim is to create a local network through which as many residents as possible learn about the survey and are motivated to participate. They identify relevant partners from society, administration and politics and win them over to the project. They build sustainable relationships with these stakeholders and maintain them over the duration of the project.
  • Involvement of different population groups: They identify population groups that are often underrepresented in surveys (e.g. people with a migrant background, elderly or socially isolated people, people in difficult situations). Together with the project team, they develop suitable, participatory and community-oriented approaches via local organisations and key persons and recruit these groups to participate in the survey.
  • Communication, knowledge transfer and media work: You develop communication measures for communities and implement them. They are in exchange with participants and partners, support media work and prepare project results in an understandable way for communities, specialist bodies and the public.

Profile

  • Several years of experience in networking, project coordination and communication, as well as in establishing and maintaining relationships with different partners.
  • Completed studies (higher education or university) in a relevant field, such as public health, communications, social sciences, psychology, social work or related fields; or a comparable qualification with relevant professional experience.
  • Experience in cooperation with municipalities, authorities, organisations or population groups as well as familiarity with the structures and processes in the Swiss health and community.
  • Experience in the conception and implementation of communication measures and in media work.
  • Strong communication and relationship skills, as well as negotiating skills. initiative and an independent, structured and team-oriented working method.
  • Very good knowledge of German (verbal and written) and the ability to move safely in the Swiss-German environment
  • Further language skills are advantageous. Interest in research and experience with surveys or participatory projects are desirable.
