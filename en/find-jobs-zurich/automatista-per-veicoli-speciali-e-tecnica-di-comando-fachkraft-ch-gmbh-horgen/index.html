Full description

The task includes the electrical equipment of special vehicles including wiring and connection of controls and sensors. The company offers you freedom to contribute your ideas to the vehicle development. For our client we are looking for an activity

  • electrically equipping and wiring special vehicles
  • connecting controls, sensors and cable harnesses
  • commissioning vehicles and testing functions
  • systematically analysing and correcting electrical faults
  • Participating in the technical development of vehicles
  • Requirements
  • Degree as a vehicle electrician, automatic technician, electrician, electronics engineer or equivalent qualification
  • Practice in vehicle electrics or control technology would be desirable
  • Electrical diagrams and technical documents safely interpreted
  • Structured, accurate and solution-oriented working method
  • Enthusiasm for electrics, technology and special vehicles Offer
  • Work on unique special and lift spectacle vehicles
  • Freedom for own ideas in vehicle development
  • Opportunities for professional development
