Look forward to working together in a diverse, open and helpful team that is characterized by strong cohesion and comprehensive expertise. Together we plan, commission and accompany exciting and varied projects in the field of railway infrastructure. You independently take over the commissioning of projects in the area of roadways and accompany them from the commissioning of the study, the resulting project to the successful completion. You make an important contribution to the further development and maintenance of the railway infrastructure. You actively shape a sustainable mobility of the future.   • For you and the plant responsible roadway (Life Cycle Decision Maker), the threads in the roadway area of the East region come together on the customer side.
  • In this function, you independently supervise the road projects of the SBB Infrastructure in the East region and have an overview of the upcoming and ongoing projects.
  • Taking into account railway operational and structural aspects, you will bundle construction measures of the road into meaningful projects. You accompany the construction and economic implementation of the construction work and pay attention to quality, cost control and customer satisfaction.
  • In addition, you coordinate the integral commissioning of the construction projects and thus make an important contribution to the planning and control of the entire construction portfolio plan of the SBB infrastructure.
  • In your role as an internal client at SBB Infrastruktur, you accompany the projects over their entire duration from the study assignment to the end of the project. You actively contribute to ensuring that projects are financially and on time, representing the interests of the plant managers (Life Cycle Decision Maker).
  • You take over the requirements and change management, coordinate the communication between the regional partners in the East region in the area of roadway and control the projects over their entire duration.

You bring that with you.

  • You are capable of teamwork, network quickly, like to work self-organized and win others for your ideas with your empathic appearance.
  • You are an inclusive personality with a strong ability to stick to topics and make decisions. It is important to you to work solution-oriented.
  • A target group-oriented communication with different partners: as well as conducting negotiations are fun for you.
  • In order to successfully carry out this interesting and versatile task, you will bring professional experience in the area of railway infrastructure - an advantage of the road - e.g. as a project manager: in (multidisciplinary) or as a customer: in strategic plant management. You understand how railways are operated or you are looking forward to learning this knowledge.
  • In German you communicate skillfully and stylishly in word and writing (min. C1). Knowledge of French is beneficial, but not absolutely necessary.
