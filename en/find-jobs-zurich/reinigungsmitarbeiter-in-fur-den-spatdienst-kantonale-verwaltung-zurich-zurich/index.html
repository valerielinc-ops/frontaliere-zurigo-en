  • Office, boardroom and common room cleaning services
  • Cleaning of sports halls, wardrobes and sanitary facilities
  • Maintenance and cleaning of public areas and restaurants
  • Compliance with cleaning, hygiene and safety standards
  • Replenishment of consumables
  • Mutual support in the team and deputies
  • Application of cleaning application (soobr) and robotics
  • Independent organization of daily cleaning work Your profile
