Role overview

Correctional Services and Reintegration (JuWe), as part of the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs in the Canton of Zurich, is responsible for the execution of sentences and measures as well as the implementation of various types of detention.

For our 1,400 employees from around 40 professions, the successful social reintegration of people who have committed crimes, the prevention of relapses and the detention and preparation for the departure of people without a valid residence permit are of particular importance.

The Bachtel Correctional Center (VZB) is one of the correctional facilities of the Canton of Zurich (VEZ) one of seven main departments of JuWe
