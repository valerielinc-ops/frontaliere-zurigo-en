Full description

For strategic real estate development, which belongs to the Corporate Development Department, we are looking for a dynamic personality who wants to shape the future of the hospital. You are responsible for real estate development projects and ensure that all construction projects are aligned with the hospital’s strategy and the needs of the users. Tasks:

  • Responsibility for the strategic development of the real estate portfolio, preparation and regular review of the real estate strategy
  • Design of site strategies and development of master planning. Assist and support the management in strategic real estate planning and development concepts
  • Overall project management use-oriented real estate projects such as strategic castle projects of several units up to and including the feasibility phase. Representation of the interests of the owner/builder vis-à-vis authorities and other stakeholders
  • Development and implementation of a holistic surface strategy including processual conception of a strat. Land management
  • Development of usage concepts and spatial programmes for new buildings and existing properties
  • Conduct feasibility studies, test planning and competitions Profile:
  • Master’s degree (uni/FH) in business administration, architecture, engineering or in a similar direction
  • Several years of professional experience in complex real estate and construction projects – ideally in hospital construction or in construction activities in the public sector
  • In-depth knowledge in the healthcare sector as well as in hospital-specific operational processes
  • Strong conceptual capability for analysis, systematization, visualization and addressable preparation of complex issues. Development of decision-making basis for the decision-makers (management / hospital council)
  • Strong understanding of interdisciplinary collaboration across all levels, in complex matrix organizations
  • Strategic thinking, independent work as well as solution-oriented action, high resilience, conflict resolution ability and negotiating skills round off your profile Benefits: KSW offers employees conditions that enable them to reconcile their professional goals and personal needs. Flexible, flexible working hours, home office as well as a generous maternity leave and employment levels adapted to different situations – all this is possible at KSW, as long as the company allows it. Every year, the KSW offers 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses. We also support you temporally and/or financially with external training.
  • In extraordinary situations, e.g. B. prolonged illness, severe personal stress or difficult experiences in private life as well as in everyday work, various offers of conversation are available to you.
  • The appreciative and friendly cooperation within the teams and across the departments characterizes the working atmosphere at KSW. As a sign of a strong team spirit, the “you” was introduced as a standard at KSW. In addition, the KSW supports annual team events financially and organizes a large staffing event for the whole house every four years. KSW employees benefit from very good social benefits. Thus, the pension fund solution with BVK is far above the legal minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, KSW employees are very well covered.
  • With discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training opportunities and course offers, perks in pharmacies as well as at the most popular health insurance companies. You can benefit from a comprehensive offer for your health.
  • Our daycare center La Luna with spacious opening hours is located next to the KSW. In addition, you can use the income-dependent, financial support from the KSW for the crib costs.
  • In cooperation with cultural partners in and around Winterthur, we regularly offer discounts for concerts and theater performances. The borrowing of media in the in-house library is also free of charge for employees.
