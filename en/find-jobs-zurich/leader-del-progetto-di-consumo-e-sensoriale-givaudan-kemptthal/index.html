Consumer and Sensory Project Leader

Overall Mission

The Consumer & Sensory Project Leader performs research activities and documents results to develop improved and new products and solutions within flavour and food ingredients categories, according to our goals and corporate strategy We are looking for a junior profile with good knowledge in sensory and consumer science and statistics You will be part of a global R&D team of Consumer and Sensory Scientists, and support a culture of innovation aligned with the needs while also supporting a research portfolio. You will be based in Kemptthal (Switzerland) and report to the S&T Consumer & Sensory Director.

Main Responsibilities

Perform Consumer & Sensory research activities and document results to develop improved and new products and solutions within the consumer and sensory area, according toour goals and corporate strategy.

Technical knowledge

  • Develop or contribute to the development of new consumer and sensory methodologies, and associated tools (statistics, programming)
  • Independently organise work internally and externally (with other departments and with contractors)
  • Suggest future research activities
  • Use digital tools and systems to document and manage experimental results
  • Follow scientific advancements in the consumer and sensory science field and suggest future research activities

Project Management

  • Provide technical advice and collaborate on projects among functions and regions
  • Accomplish tactical plans to achieve functional strategy
  • Based on project objective and testing needs, recommend approaches and collaborate with project team and Consumer & Sensory Science project manager(s) and panel leader(s) to deliver appropriate results

Collaboration & Communication

  • Prepare detailed reports of experiments, observations, conclusions and recommendations
  • Share and debate ideas with peers, build on scientific feedback
  • Contribute to our culture of information sharing, collaboration and relationships within the department, and with all relevant groups within and outside of S&T

You?

Are you someone who wants to shape your own world? Who excels being in a culture where you can express yourself in a creative team environment? Who wants to collaborate and work in a team as passionate as you are? Then join us – and improve your world.

Required skills and background

  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent in Consumer & Sensory Science or related fields + 3 years of relevant experience or Master's degree in Consumer & Sensory Science or related fields + 1 year of experience or PhD in Consumer & Sensory Science or related fields
  • Is reliable and rigorous in consumer and sensory science
  • Have a good technical skills in consumer and sensory science, including basic statistics
  • Manage independently plans and complete technical projects
  • Possess the ability to interpret experimental analytical results and create the next round of experiments with some guidance
  • Can respond within required timelines
  • Applies creative, critical and innovative thinking skills to research problems and projects
  • Applies technical skills to solve problems
  • Expresses business understanding
  • Proficient in relevant digital tools, including sensory, consumer, programming, and statistics software
  • Ability to taste all food and beverage products, and work with smells and taste
  • Fluency in English in written and verbal form
