Full description

The Information department produces numerous offers for SRF audio, video and digital channels. With versatile news and background formats as well as classifying discussion and advisory programmes on current events in politics, business and society, SRF makes it easier for the audience to form opinions – informative, competent and independent.

What are your tasks?

The editorial staff «Arena» offers an internship from 8 February to 7 July 2027. We will gladly accept your application until 15 November 2026.

During your internship, you will be involved in the following areas:

  • As an intern at the “Arena”, you are closely involved in shaping a discussion on current topics from politics, business and society.
  • They write dossiers on current topics and draft press texts.
  • They research facts and support the producer, the editors and the moderator in the production of the show.
  • On Friday you will be present at the rehearsals and during the show live.

What do you bring?

  • They have a great interest in journalism and preferably have initial journalistic experience.
  • They bring a solid political background - especially to Swiss politics - and want to deepen their research.
  • You can think quickly and networked.
  • They are flexible and communicative and keep a cool head even in hectic situations.

What can you look forward to?

  • As an intern at the “Arena”, you immerse yourself in a political dossier every week and are very close to political events.
  • An exciting environment and insights into all relevant processes from planning to the realization of a discussion programme.
  • Careful support during the internship and a very collegial team who will be happy to share their professional experience with you.

What we offer

What does it take to reach the people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expert knowledge, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can contribute and develop. For the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: Welcome to the world of SRF!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, promote creativity and offer a lot of design space for new and high-quality products.
  • Shaping digitalization: Quality journalism is also digital for us. That’s why we explore new possibilities and experiment with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-medial. We design on the pulse of time and on the nerve of the audience.
  • Being human: We meet each other collegially, work independently and shape our working atmosphere individually. Our success is based on the personal strengths as well as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programs. In doing so, we act respectfully and independently; in this way we promote opinion-forming and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.

Requirements

  • Sie verfügen über ein grosses journalistisches Interesse und haben vorzugsweise erste journalistische Erfahrungen.

  • Sie bringen einen soliden politischen Backgrund - insbesondere zur Schweizer Politik - mit und wollen sich in die Recherche vertiefen.

  • Sie können schnell und vernetzt denken.

  • Sie sind flexibel und kommunikativ und bewahren auch in hektischen Situationen einen kühlen Kopf.
