Gesundheitsnetz Küsnacht AG (GNK), which includes the Tägerhalde and Wangensbach elderly care and health centers with around 130 residents, as well as a day-care center, Spitex, senior housing and counseling services with a total of around 250 employees, is a non-profit joint-stock company wholly owned by the municipality of Küsnacht.

We are looking for a humorous, upbeat and flexible payroll accountant to complement the HR team.

Your new responsibilities

  • Responsibility for and execution of monthly payroll runs (approx. 250 employees)
  • Processing and posting of payroll runs including year-end closing and reconciling payroll accounting with financial accounting
  • Advising employees on payroll and insurance matters
  • Controlling a sub-area of personnel deployment planning
  • Processing family allowances and withholding tax and reconciling social insurance
  • Supporting and driving digitalization, co-developing and managing all payroll-related processes, and contributing to projects
  • Commercial basic training with specialized further education as a payroll specialist and preferably with additional further education as a social insurance specialist
  • Several years of professional experience in payroll accounting including year-end work
  • Very good knowledge of MS Office
  • Integrative personality, socially competent and team-oriented
  • Quick comprehension and interconnected thinking
  • Reliable and independent way of working
  • Interest in professional and personal development
