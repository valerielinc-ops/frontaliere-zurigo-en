Full description

Personal – competent – close Welcome to the Hospital Uster, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As the central pillar of health care in the Oberland region of Zurich and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you experience the highest level of expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and promoted in your professional development. Spital Uster is a teaching hospital of the Medical Faculty of the University of Zurich.Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of a "regional and digital health hub in Züri East"! Shape the future of PET/CT diagnostics at Spital Uster. Radiology offers the entire spectrum of modern imaging diagnostics and works closely with the clinical disciplines as well as the Medical Diagnostics Center. Exciting challenges await you

  • Assuming medical, technical and organizational responsibility for the PET/CT area and actively shaping its further development
  • Ensuring high-quality, modern and patient-centered PET/CT diagnostics including specialist diagnosis and clinical support
  • Further development of the diagnostic spectrum with focus on oncological and molecular imaging
  • Establishment and further development of standardized PET/CT protocols and quality standards
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation with oncology, hematology, internal medicine, surgery and other clinical disciplines as well as with radiology specialists, medical physics, nuclear medicine and other participating professional groups
  • Expanding cooperation with the referring doctors in the region and establishing a strong regional PET/CT network
  • Responsibility for quality, radiation protection and efficient processes
  • Participation in the strategic and economic development of the location as well as in innovative diagnostic and care concepts
  • Participation in training and interdisciplinary case discussions

What sets you apart for the job

  • Specialist title FMH for Radiology and Nuclear Medicine
  • In-depth and several years of experience in PET/CT diagnostics as well as very good knowledge of oncological PET/CT imaging
  • High competence in CT and morphological imaging
  • Experience with modern nuclear medicine or molecular tracers is advantageous
  • Leadership experience or strong interest in a medical leadership role
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary cooperation and direct exchange with clinical partners
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and interest in the further development of a medical location
  • Strong communication skills and high social skills
  • Very good knowledge of German

9 reasons to work with us

  • A modern and interdisciplinary working environment
