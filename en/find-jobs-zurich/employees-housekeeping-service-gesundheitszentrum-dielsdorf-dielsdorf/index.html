Original description

Dielsdorf Health Center is a modern geriatric competence center with around 500 employees from 35 nations. Our offer ranges from counselling for older people and their relatives to general and specialized long-term care and care at home. With more than 200 beds, the inpatient range of general long-term care at the Dielsdorf site is complemented by specialized treatment pathways for rehabilitative acute and transitional care, palliative care, psychiatric geriatrics and the focus on dementia.

Tasks: • Serving all meals and care of residents in the Palliative Care Department • Inspection of residential rooms at entrance and exit • Filling of consumables, flat linen and dwelling clothes in dwelling rooms • Preparation and control of entrances and exits as well as inscription and inventory of resident clothes • Professional implementation according to operational cleaning plans and methods • Compliance with internal instructions and HCCP guidelines • Working hours from 07.00 to 16.00 / 09.30 to 18.30 Requirements • Completed training in the hospitality, catering or household sector (EBA or EGZ) or experience in this field • Good knowledge of German in words and writing (at least level B1) • High level of social, methodological and professional competence • Contemporary IT skills • Self-employed and responsible work We offer: We offer you a responsible and interesting job in a varied, future-oriented and interprofessional environment. You benefit from our attractive employment conditions, including with mind. 5 weeks holidays, good social benefits, discounts at SBB and Reka checks, individual training opportunities, low prices in our restaurants and free parking spaces.
