University Animal Hospital Zurich

Job opening immediately or by agreement

Zurich University Veterinary Hospital is a highly specialized veterinary clinic offering a wide range of services for animals, including 24-hour emergency service, diagnostics, treatment and rehabilitation of diseases and injuries.

Tasks

We are looking for a dedicated TPA (m/f/d) that supports our medical department with passion and professionalism – and also assumes responsibility as a vocational trainer.

  • Care of our inpatient and outpatient patients in the fields of medicine, dermatology, cardiology and oncology
  • Implementation of nursing activities incl. Medication, infusions and catheters
  • Assistance in investigations, endoscopies, imaging and emergencies
  • Independent placement of venous catheters and blood sampling as directed
  • Preparation and implementation of sedations/narcoses incl. Monitoring
  • Organization and prioritization of daily processes and shift transfers
  • Maintenance and maintenance of rooms, equipment and materials (including sterilization)
  • Communication and collaboration with doctors, nursing assistants and students
  • Responsibility for the supervision and training of apprentices as vocational trainers
  • Weekend and night services in the rotation system

Profile

  • Completed TPA training, ideally with clinical or emergency experience
  • Self-employed, structured and responsible working practices
  • Teamwork and good communication skills
  • Readiness for shift, weekend and night services
  • Additional training (e.g. anesthesia, Fear Free) is beneficial
  • Vocational trainer certificate or willingness to acquire it
