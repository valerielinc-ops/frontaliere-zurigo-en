Personal – competent – close Welcome to the Hospital Uster, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As the central pillar of health care in the Oberland region of Zurich and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you experience the highest level of expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and promoted in your professional development. Spital Uster is a teaching hospital of the Medical Faculty of the University of Zurich.Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of a "regional and digital health hub in Züri East"! The Department of Operational Disciplines includes the Surgical Clinic (hand surgery, orthopaedics, otorhinolaryngology, traumatology, urology, visceral surgery) and the Women's Clinic (Gynecology, Obstetrics). In the department we carry out almost 7000 interventions every year. In the field of urology, about 850 stationary recordings are carried out annually.
