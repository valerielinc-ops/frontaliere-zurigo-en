Full description

You work independently on modern CNC machining centers and ensure smooth processes. The company offers you a family environment with short decision paths and varied tasks. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Self-responsible setup and monitoring of CNC processing machines
  • Programming and optimizing CNC processes based on plans and production data
  • Manufacture of kitchens, furniture and individual interior fittings
  • Processing of solid wood, board materials and other carpentry raw materials
  • Implementation of quality controls and ensuring a smooth production process

Requirements

  • Completed carpentry apprenticeship (EFZ) with experience at CNC machining centres
  • Knowledge in programming CNC machines is an advantage
  • Secure handling of plans, parts lists and production documents
  • Practical experience in furniture production and demanding carpentry work in the banking room
  • Independent, precise working method combined with technical understanding and high reliability

Offer

  • Varied position with focus on CNC and classic carpentry tasks
  • Modern machine park including two powerful CNC machining centers
  • Working independently in a family team with short decision-making paths and long-term perspective
