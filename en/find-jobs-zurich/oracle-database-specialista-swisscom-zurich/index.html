# Oracle Database Specialist

Il tuo futuro inizia

You develop and operate Oracle-based solutions for our AVANTI software, which supports blue light organizations such as police, firefighters and emergency services in everyday life. You combine professional requirements with a stable technical implementation and accompanying solutions from conception to productive operation. In an experienced team of database specialists, you work independently with great creative leeway, actively contribute your expertise and are in close contact with development, project management and customers. Your contribution ensures that our systems remain reliable, performant and highly available. Highlights of the place

  • Installation, configuration and operation of Oracle databases on Windows
  • Monitoring, performance optimization and backup and recovery processes
  • Planning and implementation of release changes and migrations during ongoing operations
  • Cooperation in the conception, development and implementation of software solutions in the authorities and rescue environment
  • Advising on development and project management on Oracle and database topics as well as ensuring stable operation around the clock including regular pikett service, including analysis and correction of complex faults and continuous optimization of the system landscape

Your skills

  • Several years of experience as an Oracle database specialist in the corporate environment
  • Very good knowledge of operation, administration, troubleshooting and tuning of Oracle databases
  • Experience with release changes, migrations and high availability solutions including software change management
  • degree (uni/FH/HF) in computer science or equivalent; experience in data modeling and software development
  • Knowledge in other relational databases is advantageous
  • Personal responsibility, structured and reliable in cooperation
  • Communicative, team capable and with high quality awareness
  • Curious and committed, you approach topics pragmatically and constantly optimize solutions
  • Very good German and good English
  • French advantage

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Benefits.
