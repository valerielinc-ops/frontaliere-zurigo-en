Welcome to the Medbase Pharmacy Zurich District 12

Directly on the Schwammendingerplatz we advise an urban, multicultural clientele as well as long-term regular customers. In addition to a wide range of products from conventional medicine, naturopathy and cosmetics, we offer services such as vaccinations, health checks or consultations. In addition, we look after numerous homes and are familiar with the listing of medicines. Our pharmacy is also involved in the training of learners and pharmacists in the assistant year. We at Medbase Apotheke Zürich Kreis 12 are a young, competent team that speaks ten different languages. We care for each other and love the lively exchange with customers.

The focus of your work at Medbase is the holistic support and the pharmaceutically competent advice of our customers in all health issues. As part of a dedicated team, you play an active role in pharmacy everyday life and contribute significantly to the comprehensive care of our patients.

  • Competent and individual advice to our customers on health concerns
  • Active participation in day-to-day business – from recipe testing to quality assurance
  • Implementation of health services such as vaccinations or checks to promote health literacy
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with doctors, therapists and other specialists for networked care
  • Engagement in the training of learners and support for pharmacists: women in the assistant year

What you bring

  • A degree as a federal dipl. Pharmacist: or a recognised equivalent education (MEBEKO)
  • The desire to further develop yourself as part of the further education to / to the specialist pharmacist: in FPH in official pharmacy
  • A customer-oriented, responsible and independent working method
  • Joy of teamwork and an open, committed personality
  • Good IT knowledge (e.g. ProPharmaXX) as well as knowledge of German at least at level B2
