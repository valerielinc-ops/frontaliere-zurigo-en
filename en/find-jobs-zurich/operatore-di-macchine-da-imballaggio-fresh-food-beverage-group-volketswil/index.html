Full description

You are responsible for the operation, setup and control of the packaging systems and work occasionally in a 2-shift operation from Monday to Friday and sometimes also on Saturday.

Mansioni

  • You operate, control and set up the packaging systems
  • You recognize the problems and ensure the troubleshooting
  • You are responsible for the quality and compliance with the guidelines
  • You have fun and bring the willingness to work in 2-shift operation and partly on Saturday

Requirements

  • Experience: with packaging plants is an advantage
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): Food sector (EBA also possible)
  • Very good technical understanding
  • High quality, hygiene and duty awareness
  • Good physical condition
  • Reliable and likeable team player
  • Experience in managing larger plants
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Employee offers: Employee Points Cumulus, Discount Mobile Subscriptions, Higher Interest Rates Migros Bank, Club School/Fitness contributions and more.
  • Food: Delicious daily menus at reasonable prices.
  • Holidays: 5 weeks holiday for all, 6 weeks holiday from 50 years of age, 7 weeks holiday from 60 years of age, additional purchase of additional holiday days possible.
  • Occupational pension provision: Comprehensively secured in the Migros pension fund – with first-class and attractive services.
  • Social benefits: 730 days of sick leave with full net wages.
  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks paid maternity leave (instead of 14 weeks), 4 weeks paternity leave (instead of 2 weeks).
  • Education and training: We actively train employees as part of internal programs and, if necessary, support external training with time and money.
