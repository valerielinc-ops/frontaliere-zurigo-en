Full description

137478 | 80 - 100% | Oerlikon, Zurich | Hybrid

Are you motivated by the idea of turning redundancies into opportunities and convincing customers with suitable pension solutions? Do you enjoy making a difference for customer loyalty in a dynamic environment?

In this role, you will strengthen the Life division in the area of Retention & Reinvestments at Zurich Switzerland and make an important contribution to our joint success.

As a retention specialist, you and your team are responsible for contacting customers, advising them and offering them solutions in order not to lose them as Zurich customers. In this role, you act as a retention expert in the field of private pensions, work with various interfaces such as sales, customer service, product management, pricing and underwriting and proactively contribute to customer excellence projects.

This is what awaits you

  • You actively approach customers in the termination process, understand their motivations and open up new perspectives with Zurich
  • You create trust through personal telephone advice and show solutions that fit the individual life situation of our customers
  • You work closely with sales and help strengthen long-term and stable customer relationships
  • You act as a central contact person for internal interfaces such as sales, customer service, product management, pricing and underwriting
  • You use your experience in customer contact to further develop retention processes and advance projects for customer excellence

This sets you apart

  • Completion of a higher technical school (HF) or university of applied sciences (FH), ideally in business administration, insurance, finance or a related discipline
  • VBV intermediary certificate (all industry or life) or the clear willingness to acquire it
  • At least three years of professional experience with intensive customer contact, preferably in the private pension or financial services environment
  • Excellent knowledge of German, other national languages beneficial
  • Strong consulting and communication skills as well as a motivated, proactive team player personality
