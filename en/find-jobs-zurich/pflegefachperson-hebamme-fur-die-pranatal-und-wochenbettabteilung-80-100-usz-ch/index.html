Full description

With heart and expertise for the start of family life! Become part of our team and accompany newborn women empathetically in the first days with their newborn.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • You care for mothers, children and their families according to the concept of "family-centered care" • You take care of simple to highly complex care situations in the postpartum as well as in pregnant women • You promote breastfeeding and prepare the women who have recently given birth at home • Close cooperation with our interdisciplinary team is just as important to you as it is to us • You take on partial tasks in the accompaniment of student nurses and midwives Requirements • You are a midwife or nurse / nurse specialist KJFF or will soon complete your training – Job entrants:women are welcome with us! • Professional experience in postpartum care is beneficial • Interest in the accompaniment of women and families in the sensitive phase of the postpartum we presuppose • Confident knowledge of German and other language skills is an advantage • Secure PC user knowledge We offer: • A responsible task in a university environment • A targeted and individual introduction to the field • A dedicated team that meets at eye level and cultivates an appreciative approach • Per service planning, 4 to 6 service or free requests can be granted • An open ear for your ideas and opinions and the power to implement them • Professional and personal development
