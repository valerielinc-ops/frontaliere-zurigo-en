Full description

The GZO Spital Wetzikon is the acute care hospital for the Zurich Oberland with around 900 employees. Our Clinic for Medical Oncology and Hematology is recognised as a category B training centre and offers modern, patient-centred care. Supported by two assistant doctors, our team works closely with the participating disciplines within and outside the GZO. Flat hierarchies, short decision paths and a collegial culture create an environment in which you can take on responsibility and develop professionally. The GZO is currently in a financial restructuring phase as part of a deferred estate. Hospital operations continue to be stable and unrestricted. Together we shape the future of our hospital – with high medical quality and a clear focus on our patients. In order to strengthen our medical team, we are looking for an empathetic, committed, competent personality as of September 2026 or by agreement.

  • Professional and empathetic care of our patients according to current evidence-based findings
  • Close cooperation with three medical colleagues and the interprofessional treatment team

  • Participation in clinical studies as well as commitment to the education and training of assistant doctors They are happy
  • A versatile and responsible activity with a high level of personal responsibility
  • A collegial team with flat hierarchies and short decision paths
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation within and outside the GZO
  • Design possibilities in a future-oriented clinic
  • Professional competence and experience as a specialist FMH Medical Oncology (or shortly before graduation)
  • Advantageous interest in gynecological oncology
  • Enjoying patient-oriented, evidence-based medicine
  • High social skills, empathy and team spirit
  • Would you like to contribute your experience in a broad-based oncology clinic and actively shape its future? Then we look forward to meeting you.
