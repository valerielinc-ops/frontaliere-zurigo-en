  • Permanent employment

Office Wage band 75'000 - 95'000 CHF 01 Future employer For our customer, we are looking for a technically experienced and organizationally strong personality for the operational coordination of a service center. Our customer is an established company in the technical trading and service environment. In the service center, electronic devices and technical products are diagnosed, repaired and repaired. In addition, customer-specific cables and electrical components are manufactured and repaired. For this versatile function, we are looking for a personality who likes to work in practice, at the same time keeps an overview of daily processes and enjoys actively developing structures and processes. 02 Your task You take over the operational coordination of the service area and ensure that orders are processed efficiently, on time and in high quality. You combine technical expertise with organizational talent and continue to work actively in the workshop.

Its field of activity includes, inter alia

– Carrying out repairs, error diagnosis and technical work – Production and processing of cable assemblies and electrical components – Organization and coordination of daily workshop operations – Planning of capacities, work assignments and priorities Ensuring efficient order processing and consistent service quality – Coordination of the required workshop capacities for recurring production and repair orders – Technical advice in repair, warranty and service cases Processing of customer concerns and complaints – Development of sustainable solutions for technical or organizational problems – Management and planning of spare parts and other required resources – Monitoring material availability – Analysis of existing workflows and continuous optimization of processes – Participation in establishing clear structures and transparent processes in the service area Important: In this position, the classic personnel management is not in the foreground. They focus on the technical, technical and organizational coordination of the service center. 03

04 Your profile You are a reliable and solution-oriented personality who combines technical understanding with organizational skill.

Ideally, you have the following requirements

– Technical education or training in electronics, electrical engineering, vehicle electrical engineering or equivalent specialisation Practical experience in repair, diagnosis or technical service – Experience in the after-sales or customer service environment is an advantage – Enjoying practical workshop work and at the same time organizational tasks – Strong sense of order and a structured working method – Ability to set priorities and keep an overview even with higher workloads – Independent, reliable and resilient personality – Customer-oriented and communicative nature – Good knowledge of German and English in word and writing – Knowledge of French is advantageous – Good user knowledge in MS Office, especially Excel and Outlook 05 Worth knowing She expects a varied position with an exciting combination of technology, organisation and practical cooperation.
