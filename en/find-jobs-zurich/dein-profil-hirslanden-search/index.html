Start your education in the largest medical network in Switzerland – Hirslanden Clinic is looking for learners from August 2027

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set the highest standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. We focus on first-class medical quality and the well-being of the people.

Are you looking for a varied and professional education?

You like contact with people, enjoy working in a team and want to make an important contribution to the well-being of our patients? Then you are exactly right with us!

This is what awaits you: During your three-year training, you will get to know many different areas of our clinic. You support our care teams, provide clean and well-kept rooms and patient rooms, serve meals and help with the reception and economy. Thanks to the diverse assignments in different places, you will never get bored.

Your profile

  • Completed school education (secondary school A or B)
  • High motivation and commitment to three-year training
  • Customer and service orientation
  • Empathy in dealing with patients and their relatives
  • Interested in developing yourself personally and professionally

FOLLOWING DOCUMENTS HAVE TO BE INCLUDED

  • Motivation letter with current CV incl. Photo
  • Copies of your school certificates from year 6
  • If available: Multicheck result
  • Reports of trial days/weeks (vocational internships)
  • Provide two reference persons (teachers) with telephone number and e-mail address
