Full description

As a central point of contact for our patients, you play a key role at the interface between those affected, specialist areas and quality management. With empathy, expertise and a strong sense of legal issues, you make an important contribution to a patient-oriented culture.

Tasks

  • One-stop-shop for patient feedback - receiving, recording and initial assessment of concerns
  • Coordination with the departments for a timely and solution-oriented processing
  • Support and support of the departments in the content processing of complaints
  • Active feedback to patients at their request
  • Processing of inquiries up to minor liability cases, including clarification of the legal basis
  • Competent information on the rights and obligations of patients
  • Further development, implementation and evaluation of patient satisfaction measurements for the entire operation
  • Derivation of insights from the satisfaction data and feeding them into quality management for continuous improvement
  • Preparation of evaluations and reports for quality management and management

Requirements

  • basic commercial or equivalent training with an administrative focus
  • Several years of professional experience in the commercial sector
  • Experience in healthcare or in clinical processes is advantageous
  • Further training or practical experience in the area of complaint, quality or case management is a plus
  • Affinity for legal issues, ideally first experience in dealing with liability cases or patient rights
  • excellent expressive ability in German, both oral and written
  • Empathic and sovereign handling of partly burdensome situations
  • Structured, careful and independent working methods
  • safe handling of MS Office
  • Experience with acquisition and evaluation tools is advantageous

We offer

  • Responsible part-time function related to our patients
  • Insight into the quality development of the entire operation
  • fixed working days each Tuesday and Thursday with reduced daily target
  • Family atmosphere and very good social benefits
  • at least five weeks’ leave
  • Excellent staff restaurant and numerous discounts
  • Central location near Uster station and motorway connection
  • ZVV BonusPass or cheap parking, max. CHF 4.50 per day
