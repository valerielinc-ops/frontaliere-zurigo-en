Welcome to the Medbase Pharmacy Kriens Mattenhof

The modern pharmacy is located in the up-and-coming Mattenhof district, directly at Kriens Mattenhof train station. In addition to a wide range of products from conventional medicine, naturopathy and cosmetics, the offer also includes services such as vaccinations, health checks and consultations. A modern Rowa storage system ensures efficient processes. The close interdisciplinary collaboration with the Medbase Medical Center, which is located in the same building, is particularly valued. A particular commitment is to the training of apprentices and pharmacists during their assistant year. At the Medbase Pharmacy Kriens Mattenhof, an appreciative atmosphere is cultivated - lively exchanges with customers are an integral part of everyday life. A dynamic, collegial working environment is offered with a wide range of opportunities for professional and personal development. The team looks forward to receiving applications from committed individuals who would like to contribute to holistic healthcare.

You can move that As a coordinator: in the pharmacy, you work with the management to ensure that pharmacy operations run smoothly. As a pharmacy professional, you support the team with your organizational talent, advance processes and design structures that benefit customers and employees.

  • Optimization of operational procedures, processes and interfaces in close collaboration with our specialists
  • Coordination of orders, warehouse, product range maintenance and health insurance billing
  • Implementation of marketing activities and coordination of the product range in consultation with the responsible teams
  • Participation in new services and innovative concepts

#WeAreMedbase

The Medbase Group is one of the leading companies in outpatient healthcare. This means services from health promotion to medication supply and acute treatment to rehabilitation. Health professionals from various disciplines work hand in hand to offer people throughout Switzerland integrated, first-class all-round medical care. We support doctors with logistics services and digital solutions so that they can fully concentrate on patients.

The Medbase Group includes the brands Medbase Pharmacies, Zur Rose, zahnarztzentrum.ch, WePractice and Bluecare.
