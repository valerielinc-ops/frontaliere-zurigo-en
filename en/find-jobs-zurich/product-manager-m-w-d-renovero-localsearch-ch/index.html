Full description

Zurich (100% - 4 days on site, 1 day home office) With renovero, the leading platform for craft services in Switzerland, we connect thousands of people and companies with the right craftsmen every year. Together we develop innovative solutions to make the platform even more user-friendly and successful. As a digital and entrepreneurial person, you shape the next stage of renovero’s development. In this key position, you are the driving force behind the product and take responsibility for its success, from strategic vision to implementation with our development and AI partners. renovero is evolving from the established craftsman tendering platform to an AI-supported ecosystem around the Craftsmen Agent. The focus of your role is on continuously deepening the added value for craftsmen and publishers, expanding platform use and introducing AI into the product. You are the link between business strategy, technical development and AI delivery. You act as a sparring partner for the Head of Verticals, adopt a "mini-CEO" mentality for your product and remain hands-on in the backlog at the same time. In a focused, powerful team, you set priorities clearly and push results forward. Strategic product management and financial responsibility

  • Product vision and roadmap: Together with a colleague, you develop a clear, market-oriented product vision and a data-based roadmap that consistently develops renovero in the direction of its target architecture. You make sure the roadmap pays in on business goals, especially recurring revenue and user adoption.
  • Financial management: You are responsible for the financial health of your product to the Head of Verticals. You will provide data bases for business cases, monitor critical SaaS metrics (recurring revenue, active contracts, renewable rate, customer loyalty) and make decisions that ensure profitability and sustainable growth.
  • Market and competition analysis: You will continuously analyze the market and competition, identify growth opportunities and expand Renovero’s position as a leading Swiss platform for craftsman tenders. AI and Agentic Product Development (focus)
  • AI Initiative: You drive renovero’s core AI initiative, from intelligent offering tools to conversational agents with voice and chat to data-driven comparison and recommendation capabilities. You translate the vision into deliverable releases.
  • LLM and Agentic Competence: You are very familiar with the common LLMs and Agentic setups (prompt design, knowledge bases and retrieval, voice and chat flows, tool and function calls) and systematically evaluate AI output quality.
  • Data and data protection: You work with knowledge bases and clean data flows and think trust, safety and data protection (nDSG) consistently into the product.
  • AI quality assurance: You define clear acceptance criteria for AI features and ensure their quality together with our partners. Operational Product Ownership
  • Backlog Management: You write user stories with clear acceptance criteria, prioritize the product backlog and lead refinements with the development team.
  • Sprint control: You act as a product owner in the agile ceremonies (sprint planning, refinement, review, retrospective) and ensure a reliable delivery rhythm.
  • Acceptance: You coordinate and perform user acceptance testing and grant formal acceptance on behalf of renovero.
  • Tooling: You work safely with Jira and Confluence and adhere to the governance of the project structure. Interface and Partner Management
  • Bridge to IT: You are the main contact for the external development team. Your colleague and you translate the business needs of marketing, sales, customer service and management into clear, prioritized and actionable requirements.
  • AI Delivery: You control the AI Delivery along clearly defined Acceptance Criteria and actively lead the cooperation with our partners.
  • Active management: You act proactively, drive issues forward and ensure clear, coordinated decision-making paths between product, IT and management.
  • Internal stakeholders: You coordinate closely with marketing, sales, customer care and legal and optimize the cooperation between business and IT. User funnel, platform KPIs and growth
  • Conversion rate optimization: While marketing is responsible for traffic, you are responsible for conversion throughout the tender funnel, from launch to successful publication.
  • Retention and Renewal: You expand customer loyalty and increase the Renewal rate on the craftsman side through new, AI-supported value-added and premium offers.
  • User activity: You monitor and increase activity and satisfaction of both user groups, publishers and craftsmen.
  • Platform development: You accompany the expansion of the native apps and the technical development of the platform.
  • New segments: You enter new customer segments and revenue models (B2B partnerships, API integrations, transactional models). Digital depth and AI flux: You are very familiar with the common LLMs and Agentic setups and bring in-depth digital product understanding.
  • Experience (mandatory): 3 to 5 years in the development of web and app products in the SaaS and/or ecommerce environment.
  • Technical understanding: You discuss at eye level with the IT team, understand technical decision templates and ask the right questions. You don’t have to be a developer, but you understand the implications of technical decisions.
  • Entrepreneurial action: You think and act like an entrepreneur, take full responsibility and make bold decisions.
  • Communication strength and assertiveness: You communicate clearly, directly and convincingly, simply present complex issues and do not shy away from difficult conversations.
  • Analytical skills: You’ll make data-driven decisions, be adept at analytics tools and interpreting SaaS metrics.
  • Structured working method: You derive concrete plans and requirements from overarching goals (OKRs) and keep track of their implementation.
  • Languages (mandatory): German and English fluent. French and/or Italian are a welcome extra. That's why we visit you. You expect a high level of personal responsibility, short decision paths and visible impact on a product that consistently puts AI at the center. If you want to decisively shape and shape a product, you will find here a rare key position. Contact & Application More information about localsearch as an employer can be found here.
