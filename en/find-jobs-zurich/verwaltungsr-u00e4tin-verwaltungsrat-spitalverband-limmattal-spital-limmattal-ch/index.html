Full description

The Spitalverband Limmattal ensures the healthcare of a rapidly growing region through its hospital, its own rescue service, and the Limmattal care center. The Administrative Council seeks to enrich its council with an entrepreneurial personality with proven leadership and transformation experience. Preferably, a still active entrepreneur or entrepreneur is sought with strong roots in Limmattal and a good understanding of the economic, social, and political conditions of the region. Tasks

  • Strategic development of the Spitalverband Limmattal
  • Long-term positioning of the company in a changing healthcare market
  • Promotion of digitalization, innovation, and technological development with a particular focus on integrated healthcare
  • Monitoring of economic, financial, and organizational development
  • Ensuring sustainable company management and governance
  • Support the management as a strategic partner
  • Requirements - Leadership experience of at least 5 years as CEO or member of the management of a medium to large company and a strong entrepreneurial mindset - Experience in the IT, technology, or digital sector
  • Digital Transformation, Innovation, New Technologies, and Platform and Ecosystem Strategies are at the core of your competencies
  • Experience in accompanying complex change processes
  • Good understanding of Governance, Risk Management, and Strategic Leadership
  • Strong communication and teamwork skills that align with the values of the Spitalverband Limmattal
  • Strong regional roots; preferably entrepreneurs with roots in Limmattal or with a tangible connection to the region and its economic environment
  • Due to the current VR composition and the statutes of the Spitalverband Limmattal, candidates from the administrative communities of Schlieren and Dietikon are excluded from the selection process
  • Person in active professional life Offer

  • Possibility to influence the future management of a significant regional healthcare provider
