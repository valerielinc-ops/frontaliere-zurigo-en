Full description

<b>Bülach Hospital grows - grow with it.</b>

Tasks

  • They coordinate the patient flow and bed capacities and keep an overview even in dynamic situations.
  • They plan and coordinate operational interventions and, together with the participating areas, ensure a smooth process.
  • They are an important hub between the medical profession, nursing, surgery, anesthesia and other specialist areas.
  • They organize patient missions and accompany the planning from registration to hospital entry.
  • Contact person for internal and external assignments and cooperation partners
  • They bring their own ideas and actively shape the further development of our processes and processes.

Requirements

  • You have a medical background or have professional experience in the healthcare sector.
  • They enjoy planning, coordination and organization and appreciate working in a varied and dynamic environment.
  • Even with several tasks occurring at the same time, you keep an overview, set clear priorities and make situational decisions.
  • They communicate safely, respectfully and appreciatively with patients and employees from different professional groups.
  • They are characterized by reliability, flexibility and resilience and like to work constructively in a team.
  • They are well versed in using digital work equipment and can quickly find their way around new IT systems and digital processes.
  • You like to think along, bring your own ideas and take responsibility for your tasks and their further development
