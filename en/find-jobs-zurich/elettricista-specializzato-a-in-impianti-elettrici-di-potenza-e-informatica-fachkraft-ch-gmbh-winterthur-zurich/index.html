Full description

The execution of customer orders precisely and on time is one of the core tasks. You benefit from a modern working environment that attaches great importance to quality. For our client we are looking for an activity

  • to carry out customer orders professionally and on time
  • to install strong and low-current systems for new and conversions
  • to take over maintenance work on telephone, computers and high-voltage systems
  • to organize orders independently and to close them cleanly
