Canton of Zurich Economic Directorate Office for Economics Team leader/in Control Centre Labour Market 80 - 100% The Economic Directorate of the Canton of Zurich ( www.vd.Hh.ch ) is responsible for the Office for Labour, the Office for Economy, the Office for Mobility and the administrative department of the Zurich Transport Association (ZVV). In the interest of employees and companies, the Office for Economic Affairs (AWI) helps to strengthen the Zurich site. With its approximately 100 employees, the AWI bundles the economic competences and is based on the areas of location promotion, work permits, industrial relations, labour inspectorate and economics as well as the staff. The area of industrial relations is active in the implementation of the activities of the christening. Wage and undeclared work controls are carried out, compliance with the reporting obligation is checked, the Posting of Workers Act is implemented, the Federal Act on Measures to Combat Undeclared Work is implemented and the labour market is monitored in respect of compliance with local, professional and industry wages. The offices of the tripartite commission of the canton of Zurich (TPK) and the unification office are also in the area. The Team Control Centre Labour Market is responsible for controlling the field of the free movement of persons in sectors without generally binding general employment contracts, the obligation to report on jobs and the fight against undeclared work in all sectors.
