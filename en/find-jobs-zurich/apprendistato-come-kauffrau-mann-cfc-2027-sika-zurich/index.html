About the role Would you like to start after school, gain exciting insights into the professional world and become part of an international company? Then you are exactly right with us! Whether high-rise buildings, tunnels, wind farms or modern industrial plants: Sika products all over the world ensure that buildings and vehicles become more durable, safer and more sustainable. Behind these projects are not only technical professionals, but also dedicated merchants who hold the strings together in the background. For the apprenticeship start in August 2027 we are looking for motivated and curious talents for training as a merchant / merchant EFZ . During your apprenticeship, you will get to know different departments and gain exciting insights into the diverse world of an international company. You work actively from the beginning, take responsibility and are an important part of our teams.

With us you will

  • Managing administrative and organizational tasks independently
  • Get to know different areas of the company
  • Taking responsibility for own tasks and projects
  • Collaborate with colleagues from different fields
  • Gaining insights into business processes
  • Develop your communication and team skills
  • Working in a global environment and experiencing international contacts

Of course, you will be accompanied by experienced vocational trainers who support and promote you.

Your skills and experience You bring that with you

You fit in with us when you

  • Are curious and want to learn new things
  • glad to take responsibility
  • Open, reliable and motivated
  • Enjoy teamwork and communication
  • Bringing interest in economic issues and figures
  • attend or have completed secondary school level A with good performances
  • Have fun with foreign languages, especially English
  • want to find out where your strengths lie and how you can develop them further

Why you should come to us You can look forward to it. At Sika, we rely on trust, personal responsibility and development opportunities. During your teaching, you are not just there, but in the middle of it.

Look forward to

  • varied assignments in various departments
  • Exciting internal training and events
  • Exchange with other learners
  • An open and appreciative corporate culture
  • Dedicated professional and practical trainers
  • The unique Sika spirit
  • Attractive prospects after successful teaching

Ready for your next step?

Sika is a great employer for young people who want to make a difference. Find out more about our apprenticeships at www.sika.ch/lehrstellen. Nihaila Iaquinta, HR generalist (tel. +41 58 436 54 23), is happy to answer your questions. We look forward to your online application!

We offer competitive remuneration based on local market standards and the specific responsibilities of each job. The remuneration shall be based on the skills, responsibility, education and/or training relevant to the position. We are committed to fair and equitable remuneration schemes in accordance with the applicable laws, the Chemical Tariff Agreement and regulations.

About Sika Sika is a specialty chemical company, global leader in the development and production of systems and products for gluing, sealing, steaming, reinforcing and protecting in construction and industry. Sika has a global presence with subsidiaries in 103 countries, produces in over 400 factories, develops innovative technologies for customers around the world and thus contributes significantly to the sustainable transformation in construction and transport. The more than 33,000 employees generated a turnover of CHF 11.20 billion in 2025.
