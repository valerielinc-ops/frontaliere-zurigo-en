What to expect from us and how to make an "Impact that matters"

  • From the first day of direct customer contact – Right from the start, you make a relevant and important contribution to auditing consolidated and annual financial statements in accordance with Swiss and international accounting standards, directly at our customers’ premises, offices or home offices.
  • Diverse tasks with growing personal responsibility – New challenges await you every day. Depending on the mandate, you will support with process understanding, the analysis of balance sheet performance statement positions, control audits, detailed tests and inventories. With increasing experience, you conduct surveys with the customer independently, apply analytical auditing procedures and coordinate tasks with the extended team.
  • Deloitte Way of Working - You use our audit tool OMNIA, which integrates AI-powered technologies to perform risk-based and data-driven audits. You will develop skills in data analysis and digital audit methods, which are essential in modern auditing. You bring that with you
  • You will ideally bring a bachelor's or master's degree (uni or FH) in economics with a focus on auditing, accounting, finance or controlling or have started such a degree.
  • You have excellent analytical skills, a quick comprehension, and an interest in solving complex tasks quickly and accurately.
  • You come with a positive and result-oriented attitude as well as a willingness to take responsibility and find individual solutions for our customers.
  • Excellent German and good English skills. You can score extra points.
  • Excel knowledge, data analysis & data management - secure handling of data, affinity for modern analysis tools and technologies.
  • Organizational talent and structured thinking – you keep track of multiple tasks at the same time and communicate progress and challenges transparently and proactively.
