Full description

We are looking for the Specialist Staff Social Integration with its topics housing, work and education as well as personal help on 01. January 2027 or by arrangement a new team member.

Tasks

  • You actively shape and develop the technical foundations in the field of living and contribute your expertise in a targeted manner.
  • They prepare complex topics appropriate to the addressee and create decision-making bases, presentations, concepts, reports and statements.
  • With your expertise, you support and advise social workers on housing issues and promote successful cooperation with internal and external partners.
  • They manage projects on their own responsibility and deal with political projects and inquiries from the municipal council.
  • You develop needs-based training concepts for employees and impart your knowledge in presence and online formats.

Profile

  • University of applied sciences degree in social work or a university degree (master) in social sciences
  • Several years of professional experience in a comparable function or field of activity
  • In-depth knowledge of the subject-specific discourses around the topic of housing and the “residential integration landscape” of the city of Zurich
  • Conceptual and organizational tasks give you pleasure. At the same time, you convince with a high level of self-employment and strong social skills
  • Experience in managing projects. Further training in project management is an advantage

We offer

You work in a small team in the heart of the city of Zurich with a high degree of independence in dealing with your topics and tasks. At the same time, you are embedded in a large organization and thus benefit from attractive employment conditions in the city of Zurich.

About us

With around 2,400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a social Zurich. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.

Working with people. Around 900 employees work daily in the social services to ensure that people in the city of Zurich can count on effective basic social care and are committed to a good quality of life in all urban quarters. Through our work in the areas of social urban life, child and youth welfare, economic assistance, adult protection and social inclusion, we make an important contribution to social peace and social cohesion. The services are provided in five social centres, the performance development in five specialist departments.
