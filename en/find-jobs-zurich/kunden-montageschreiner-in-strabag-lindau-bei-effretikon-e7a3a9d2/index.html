Customer / assembly carpenter:in Your contribution with us

  • You produce roof window lining to measure and mount it professionally on the construction site
  • You manufacture lid, pass and connection strips and ensure a clean and precise assembly
  • For our special formwork construction you produce special wooden components and work them with the moulding machine, among other things
  • You create stairs according to plan specifications and take over their installation on the construction site
  • You independently carry out customer work, adjustments and individual special solutions
  • You take over finishing and completion work on our construction sites and ensure that our work is completed neatly down to the last detail
  • If necessary, you also take part in general carpentry and timber construction work
  • You work closely with our carpenters, builders and other specialist areas and find a practical and clean solution even for special requirements

What matters to us

  • You have completed training as a carpenter EFZ or a comparable craftsmanship training
  • You are not a pure workshop carpenter, but like to work practically and versatile - even on the construction site
  • Carpenter and timber construction work is not a foreign word for you and you are ready to take part outside of the classic carpentry craft
  • You can read plans and implement guidelines independently
  • You have a good craftsmanship and also find suitable solutions for individual tasks and special products
  • You work independently, accurately and reliably and have a high quality standard
  • A professional attitude towards our customers and an uncomplicated cooperation in the team are a matter of course for you
  • A category B driving licence is a must. (Fully equipped carpenter bus available)
  • A driving licence of category BE is an advantage
  • Good German skills in word and writing round off your profile

Our added value for you With us, you can expect an exceptionally versatile activity between carpentry, wooden construction and special productions. Instead of carrying out the same tasks on a daily basis, you take on a wide range of tasks - from precise detail work to special components and stairs to assembly, customer and finish work on our construction sites. You work independently, can contribute your craftsmanship and get the necessary space to find good and practical solutions for special tasks. We offer you contemporary employment conditions, modern work equipment, varied projects and a collegial team in which carpenters and carpenters work closely together. If you like to work versatile, wood is your material and you do not want to limit yourself to the classic carpentry crafts, you are exactly right with us. We look forward to meeting you. :::
