Full description

To strengthen our R&D team at the Volketswil site, we are looking for a motivated and resilient personality: Color metrics in the company laboratory, Volketswil, 100% (Job as of 1 September 2026 - Replacement due to planned retirement) Their tasks

  • Creation and correction of colour recipes for production
  • Preparation of colorimetric calibration series
  • Incoming control of raw materials
  • Quality inspection of our products
  • Support for R&D and manufacturing in scale-up trial production
  • Collection and monitoring of samples for the establishment of bacterial and fungal cultures in the context of farm hygiene processes
  • Documentation and thorough evaluation of measurement data Your profile
  • Completed training as lacquer or chemical laboratory technician
  • Interpretability and understanding of laboratory measurement data and standards
  • In-depth knowledge in the field of colorimetrics and in dealing with corresponding measuring instruments
  • Affinity to modern software solutions for analytical measuring instruments in general
  • Delight in practical laboratory craftsmanship with pasty, water-based building materials
  • High comprehension and team-oriented thinking
  • Willingness to be flexible when changing priorities
  • Very good knowledge of German / English, French is advantageous Their perspectives
  • Versatile area of responsibility in an international and yet family-run company
  • A corporate culture with space for personal responsibility and entrepreneurial ideas
  • An environment in which openness and appreciation are a matter of course
