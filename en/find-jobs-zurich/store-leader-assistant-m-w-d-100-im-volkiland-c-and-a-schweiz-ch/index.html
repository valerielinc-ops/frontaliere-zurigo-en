Full description

The future looks like you

Since its founding by the brothers Clemens and August in 1841, C&A has been a pioneer in fashion. From the introduction of made-to-measure clothing, when customization was still the norm, to the popularization of miniskirts in the 60s, the introduction of the com-bi-kini in the 70s, organic cotton in early 2000, to the first cradle-to-cradle gold-certified garment in 2017 - we have always gone with the times and made stylish and high-quality clothing possible for everyone. And while C&A and the world around us have changed over the decades, one thing has remained the same: our commitment to providing sustainable and quality fashion for the whole family. We want for the future a fashion industry that is more innovative and sustainable

  • One that moves toward a circular future and promotes a cycle of use, reuse and rebirth of clothing.

In Switzerland, C&A has more than 90 stores and employs more than 1,300 people.
