Full description

You are part of a team that not only checks numbers, but recognizes connections. At PwC in the field of audit - financial services (banking), you analyze companies: How successful are they? What are the opportunities and risks? How can they become more sustainable?

Here you work with curious and ambitious people to shape the economy of tomorrow – digitally, sustainably and globally. Each project is unique and offers you the chance to grow and make a difference.

Diversity is our strength. Your perspective counts, and we support you in achieving your goals.

You don’t just want a job, you want to make a difference? Welcome to PwC! Here you can:

  • Understand and transform companies: Review consolidated and annual financial statements and ensure that they meet regulatory requirements – on site, in the office or flexibly from the home office.
  • Shaping the future digitally: Use cutting-edge tools, AI and data analysis to take auditing to a new level. Your ideas count!
  • Promote sustainability: Support the review of ESG reports and accompany companies on their way to greater environmental awareness and social responsibility.
  • Build networks: Collaborate with leadership teams and CFOs and build connections that will shape your career.
  • Take responsibility: Develop your leadership skills in an international team that promotes and inspires you. Especially in the intensive Busy Season from January to Easter, you make a decisive contribution to the success of our customers.

If you are still unsure what everyday life looks like in auditing, then try our Virtual Case Experience. In 3-4 hours you can solve real business problems and gain an insight into the practice.

With us count your personality, your skills and your will to make the world a bit better. What else you should bring:

  • An upcoming or completed bachelor's or master's degree in economics, (business) informatics, STEM or another field of study.
  • Enthusiasm for numbers, digital trends and sustainability.
  • The desire to work in an international environment and to work with people from different cultures.
  • Strong communication in German and English.
  • Team spirit and the ability to solve challenges with fresh ideas.

Your perspective – More than just a job We know that starting your career can be exciting, but also challenging. That’s why we accompany you on your way – with a clear plan and strong support:

  • Temporal and financial support: We support you comprehensively in your further education to/from the dipl. Auditors: You not only get financial support, but also the flexibility to make your learning time optimal.
  • Flexibility that suits you: Our working time models give you the freedom to balance work, learning and leisure time.
  • Mentoring and development: You will be accompanied by experienced colleagues who promote and inspire you.
  • A team that empowers you: With us, you’ll find a community that values diversity, celebrates ideas, and encourages you to grow beyond yourself.

Why PwC? Because we know what’s important to you. Meaningful work that makes a difference. A team that inspires you. And the opportunity to actively shape the future.

Apply now and become part of our Community of Solvers. Together we shape the future of auditing – digitally, sustainably and globally.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
