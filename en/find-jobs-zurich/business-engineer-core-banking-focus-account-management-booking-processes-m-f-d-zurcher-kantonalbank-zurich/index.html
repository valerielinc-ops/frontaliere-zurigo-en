Description

Together, we are shaping the account management processes of the future!

Our "Account, Card and Pension Foundation Administration" division is responsible, among other things, for the functionally correct operation of a central system network within the bank's Core Banking platform. We ensure that the further development of booking processes takes place within the framework of our business requirements. We also serve as the link between the core business processes and the bank's ERP system landscape. In a complex business and technical environment, we analyze requirements, support implementation and are responsible for the functionally correct introduction and business support.

Your responsibilities

  • Assuming the co-lead role as subject-area owner for the "Account Management and Booking Processes" segment on our Core Banking platform
  • Assuming the co-lead role as process design owner for our account management and booking processes
  • Further developing the operational resilience of our Core Banking platform
  • Contributing to the business conception and specification of epics, features and stories for IT development
  • Precisely analyzing matters and concretely elaborating business requirements for account management and booking processes
  • Developing and representing business requirements for our system network and acting as the link between diverse stakeholders such as Product Management, subject-matter experts, Risk, Accounting, Business and IT Architecture, ensuring cross-functional coordination and communication
  • Supporting the renewal of our Core Banking solution with a focus on the deposit business
  • Representing interests in day-to-day business as well as in strategic initiatives of bank-wide significance

Your profile

  • Completed university degree (university or university of applied sciences) in Business Administration / Banking & Finance / Business Information Systems / Business Engineering or similar
  • Several years of professional experience in Business Engineering with a focus on processes in Core Banking platforms
  • Knowledge of agile project implementation (SAFe, SCRUM, Kanban) is an advantage
  • Strategic foresight with a willingness to go into detail
  • A high level of personal responsibility, flexibility, as well as entrepreneurial thinking and action
  • Strong service orientation, a can-do attitude, willingness to learn and ability to work in a team
  • An engaging and confident presence and eloquent communication in German

Our offer

  • A challenging and varied role at an innovative and future-oriented company
  • A motivated, performance-oriented team and working environment that looks forward to your contribution
  • A wide range of opportunities for your personal and professional development
  • We give you room for your ideas and thus shape the bank of tomorrow
  • A comprehensive package that, in addition to an attractive base salary and variable compensation, offers many benefits and discounts – from A for retirement provision to Z for ZVV-Bonuspass
  • A centrally located workplace in the vibrant Kreis 5 district with very good public transport connections and the opportunity to organize your work flexibly
