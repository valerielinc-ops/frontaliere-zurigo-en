Full description

Welcome to the Medbase Pharmacy Dübendorf

Our well-frequented pharmacy is located in the middle of Dübendorf. In addition to a wide range of products from conventional medicine, naturopathy and cosmetics, we offer services such as vaccinations, health checks and consultations. We also look after numerous homes and are familiar with the packaging of medication in blisters. We provide competent advice to our large regular customers and passers-by. The Medbase Pharmacy Dübendorf is also committed to training apprentices and pharmacists during their assistant year. We treat each other with respect and love lively exchanges with customers.

You can move that To strengthen our team, depending on the workload, we are looking for 1-2 committed and motivated people who enjoy working in a busy pharmacy.

You are the backbone of our pharmacy and your commitment ensures high customer satisfaction. At Medbase you can fully develop your consulting skills and be part of a committed team that will support you on your professional path.

  • Pharmaceutical advice and support for customers
  • Recipe execution and inventory management
  • Maintaining the range
  • Perform administrative tasks and inventory management

What you bring with you

  • Training as an EFZ pharmacy specialist
  • Service orientation and sales talent
  • Good IT knowledge (ProPharmaX)
  • Good knowledge of German (at least B2 level)

#WeAreMedbase

The Medbase Group is one of the leading companies in outpatient healthcare. This means services from health promotion to medication supply and acute treatment to rehabilitation. Health professionals from various disciplines work hand in hand to offer people throughout Switzerland integrated, first-class all-round medical care. We support doctors with logistics services and digital solutions so that they can fully concentrate on patients.

The Medbase Group includes the brands Medbase Pharmacies, Zur Rose, zahnarztzentrum.ch, WePractice and Bluecare.
