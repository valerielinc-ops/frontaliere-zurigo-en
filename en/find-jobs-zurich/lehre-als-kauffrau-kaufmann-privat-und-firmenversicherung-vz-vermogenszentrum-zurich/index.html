Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We advise individuals and companies about money, manage assets and offer attractive financial services. We are very successful and growing strongly.

  • With us, you work for both an insurer and a broker. So you get to know the whole world of private insurance.
  • You are in contact with customers and other companies, compare policies, prepare offers and contracts and process claims reports
  • You attend vocational school once or twice a week and take intercompany courses with learners from other insurers. There you will learn everything important about the industry.
  • You put your theoretical knowledge directly into practice and learn to move too safely in a networked and digital working world.
  • Throughout your apprenticeship, you will be personally accompanied by experienced trainers.
  • You can do the apprenticeship with BMS, but you don’t have to.
  • You are interested in insurance.
  • You visit the Sek A in the canton of Zurich or an equivalent school in another canton, have good grades and fun with numbers.
  • You work carefully and reliably, express yourself well and approach people openly.
  • You are a team player, want to learn as much as possible and make a difference.
  • Regular development discussions and individual support
  • Costs for school supplies and a contribution of CHF1'300 to your school laptop
  • Generous participation in language stays and language diplomas (English and French)
  • Six weeks of holidays
  • Many events such as the summer and X-Mas party or learning event
  • Free cold drinks, coffee, tea and fresh fruits
  • Even glacé in summer
  • Very good takeover opportunities after graduation
  • Anonymous surveys on satisfaction regularly show that our employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!

You can apply with just a few clicks: Just upload your application documents (motivation letter, CV, certificates, multicheck and trial reviews).
