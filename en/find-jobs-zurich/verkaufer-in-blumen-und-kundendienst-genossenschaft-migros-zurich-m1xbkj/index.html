Full description

Are flowers your passion? Come to our branch M Rigiplatz. There you provide a beautiful ambience and advise our customers on the selection of flowers. Our customers also appreciate your good service at the PickMup counter when they pick up their online orders from you. Interested? Apply now!

Mansioni

  • You support us with your creativity in the field of flowers and plants and combine appealing and tasteful bouquets according to customer requirements with great attention to detail
  • You are responsible for various services (including Textile Fresh, Photo Machine, PickMup)
  • You provide first-class customer service and support our customers with various concerns, such as returns and complaints
  • You ensure the high quality of our products
  • If necessary, you can also support in other store areas

Requirements

  • 1-3 years of professional experience: in the field of flowers/plants
  • Completed apprenticeship (EFZ): as a florist or in the retail trade
  • You are customer-oriented and friendly
  • You like to work independently and reliably
  • You deal with customer complaints confidently and communicate gladly and effectively
  • You are creative and realize the flower wishes of our customers
  • German (very good knowledge)

  • Occupational pension provision: Excellent pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Social benefits: Fair social benefits
  • Cumulus Points: You get additional Cumulus Points when shopping
  • Migros Bank: Preferred interest on private and investment account, free account management, discounted mortgages and more
  • Employee offers: Up to CHF 1,000 per year (depending on your workload) for your recovery and further development – usable for fitness subscriptions, courses, health offers or a subsidy for public transport.
  • Sports and fitness offers: You can get a total free time bonus of max once a year. CHF 1000.– e.g. redeem for an annual subscription to the M-Fitnesscenter
  • education and training: support for professional education and training,
