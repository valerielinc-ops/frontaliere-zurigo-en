Full description

My name is Senevita and I am one of the leading providers in the field of long-term care and support. I have many exciting apprenticeships to offer; in nursing, in the hotel industry, in the commercial sector and in business maintenance. I currently have around 210 apprentices training with me. Imagine: over 200 young people have chosen me as their training company! It's cool, isn't it? I'm really proud of that!

Would you like to become part of our team at Senevita Regensberg in Zurich-Oerlikon? As of August 2027, we have the following vacant apprenticeship position: Trainee Health Specialist EFZ This is what awaits you With us you will start a varied and important career with a future! No two days are the same: you take on responsibility, work with people and learn something new every day. You can discover your strengths, develop yourself and make a real difference in the lives of our residents. During your training, you will support our residents in their everyday activities and help them with tasks that they can no longer carry out independently. You accompany them individually and play an active role in shaping their daily structure. You will also take on tasks in the field of medical technology. This includes, for example, carefully measuring blood pressure, taking blood samples or professionally caring for and dressing wounds. Administrative and organizational tasks are also part of your varied everyday working life. You work closely with various specialists, exchange ideas within the team and discuss together the best possible care and support for our residents. With us you can expect an exciting and varied training in which you will take on responsibility, develop your skills and make a valuable contribution to the well-being of our residents. This is what you bring with you

  • You have completed secondary school or achieved good results in secondary school and have good written and spoken German skills.
  • You are interested in the exciting and diverse field of nursing and enjoy working with people.
  • You approach our residents sensitively, respectfully and openly.
  • You are reliable, service-oriented and can quickly understand and implement new things.
  • You work carefully and responsibly and are ready to take on responsibility.
  • You enjoy working in a great team and are happy to contribute your strengths and ideas.
  • You bring team spirit, humor and good manners.
  • You are physically and mentally resilient and keep a cool head even in challenging situations.

This is what we offer you Here at Senevita Regensberg we will get you fit for your future! You can expect professional and varied training, accompanied by motivated and highly qualified vocational trainers. We support you individually and give you a varied insight into the different areas of care. You can discover your strengths, learn new things and take on more and more responsibility step by step. We welcome your creativity and ideas - we look forward to your fresh input and to designing new things together with you. Exciting activities and events regularly provide shared experiences that move and connect employees and residents. And the most important thing: you as a person are important to us!
