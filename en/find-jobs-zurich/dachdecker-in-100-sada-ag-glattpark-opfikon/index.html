Full description

Do you know us? We are a Swiss company with competences in sanitary, heating/cooling and ventilation/air conditioning technology as well as roofing and spenglerei. With headquarters in Glattpark and around 160 employees, we operate throughout the Canton of Zurich. We belong to the Burkhalter Group, the leading provider of cross-trade building technology in Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein.

Are you ready for the next career step?

Their tasks

  • Carry out assembly work on flat and steep roofs
  • Do service work and repairs
  • Planning, organizing and coordinating daily and weekly work
  • Administrative order processing incl. Create weekly/regulation reports
  • Accompanying the practical training of learners

Your profile

  • Completed training as a roofer with several years of professional experience
  • Safe knowledge of German in words and writing and good manners
  • Independent and dutiful working methods with a high level of teamwork
  • Experience working with learners is desirable

We offer

  • 40-hour week
  • At least 5 weeks of holiday, with age-staged increase
  • Reduced membership in the gym – Benefit from discounts in the in-house studio
  • Appreciative company culture and regular employee events
  • Good accessibility with public transport
