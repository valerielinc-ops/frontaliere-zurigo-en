Full description

Work where others go on holiday! Employees/in housekeeping and support 40% - 60 % Can you imagine working in a small team without a direct leader? We have converted our organizational form to semi-autonomous teams, i.e. we are guided by the famous Buurtzorg model, but have adapted it to our needs. In an interdisciplinary team, with case managers, FaGes and housekeeping and support staff at your side, it should be a new form of work that is contemporary and allows for the necessary flexibility, but also structure and continuity.

You have the following qualifications

  • A broad life experience, social skills in dealing with people as well as pronounced household experience are required
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German
  • Self-employed and responsible working practices
  • You are flexible and resilient
  • Valid ticket category B and own car

Your tasks: You are used by the beaten, impaired, sick and families in various life situations. You independently carry out clearly defined and defined activities in the household with the involvement of the clients (weekend, laundry care, shopping). In addition to the budgetary assignments observed You assess the physical, mental, mental and social state of the clients and inform the management about changes.

We offer you

  • a varied, responsible and independent task in an innovative organization
  • Flexible employment conditions
  • 5 weeks holiday (from 50 years 6 weeks)
  • very good social benefits (e.g. pension fund contribution 60:40)
  • A motivating and appreciative working environment

Job opening: by immediate or agreement Pensum: 40% - 60% or by agreement Do you have any questions? Call us. Do you feel appealed to? We look forward to your application!
