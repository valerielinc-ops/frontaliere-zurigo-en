immediately or by agreement

In this role, you report directly to the management of Controlling FP&A and take over the technical and personnel management of a team of six employees. You are responsible for the financial management of several central divisions and are an important sparring partner for management.

You move that

  • Leadership, coaching and further development of the Controlling Management Services team
  • Financial management of HRM, Real Estate & Operations, Digital & IT and Corporate Center Functions
  • Advising directorates as sparring partners on financial, business and strategic issues
  • Responsibility for budgeting, rolling forecasts and multiannual planning
  • Creating meaningful management reports and deriving recommendations for action
  • Responsibility for CAPEX/OPEX controlling, investments and profitability analyses
  • Development of controlling instruments, key performance indicator systems, dashboards and reporting solutions
  • Optimization of controlling processes, methods, data quality and cross-divisional cooperation

This is how you succeed.

  • Higher business education (FH/university) with a focus on finance, controlling or business administration or a comparable qualification (e.g. expert in accounting and controlling)
  • Several years of professional and managerial experience in controlling, ideally in complex organizations
  • In-depth knowledge in financial, performance and cost controlling
  • Proven experience in budgeting, forecasting, management reporting and financial management of organizational units
  • Experience in OPEX, CAPEX and investment controlling, ideally also in project/construction controlling
  • Strong analytical, conceptual and solution-oriented skills
  • High communication, integration and leadership competence as well as a strong management understanding
  • IT affinity and experience with modern controlling, reporting and BI solutions
  • Knowledge of the Swiss healthcare system/REKOLE® is an advantage

This is what sets you apart.

  • Sound controlling expertise combined with a strong management understanding
  • High service and customer orientation
  • Ability to bring together different stakeholders
  • Developing viable and practice-oriented solutions
  • Strategic thinking combined with operational implementation power
  • Secure appearance as a sparring partner for executives

You can look forward to that.

  • Responsible leadership with direct influence on financial management
  • Leadership of an experienced team of six Finance Business Partners
  • Broad scope of activity from HRM to Digital & IT to real estate and construction projects
  • Exciting strategic and operational issues
  • Dynamic and demanding environment
  • Possibility to sustainably develop controlling
  • Design and further development of Finance Business Partnering
  • Great scope for design and visible influence on corporate management
