Full description

The Commissariat Investigations of the City Police of Zurich ensures the criminal police basic care for offences and crimes in the entire city area around the clock. In addition to its own investigation orders, the associated investigation service also handles orders from the investigation authorities as well as detention cases handed over by the uniform police.

Cases shall be dealt with from the first investigation to the conclusion of the procedure, unless a specialised group is responsible. They accompany the events from the crime scene work to the investigations, examinations, coercive measures to the final reporting - close to the case, close to the practice.

Tasks

  • Moving to crime scenes for the purpose of detecting crimes and coordinating on site in relevant criminal police events
  • Ensure criminal prosecution of offences and crimes and take measures to prevent them
  • Implementation of demanding investigation procedures, also in cooperation with other specialised groups, investigating authorities and other specialised services
  • Participation in extra services and active participation in own actions
  • Provides night, weekend and piquet crime services (about 12 night services per year)

Profile

  • Completed police training (professional ID police officer) and several years of criminal police experience or interest in developing in this regard
  • In-depth police expertise, knowledge of the relevant secondary legislation as well as a structured approach to complex fact-finding
  • Writing skill with solid knowledge in the field of reporting
  • Interest in comprehensive criminal investigations incl. Crime scene work and delegated hearings as well as high motivation and pleasure in self-responsible and exact working methods
  • Teamwork and openness as well as a professional appearance in cooperation with different stakeholders

We offer

A challenging, versatile and wide-ranging job in the criminal police area with reporting and return service, crime scene work and in-depth investigation work. You will be carefully and comprehensively introduced to your daily work. Adequate and adapted training and further education opportunities in the criminal police sector and work in an urban environment with a modern workplace in Zurich-West await you. Attractive employment conditions such as taking over 60% of the PK savings contributions, mobility contribution of up to CHF 600.00 per year and monthly up to CHF 100.00 food compensation round off our offer.

About us

The city police is a service department of the security department of the city of Zurich and, with around 2,150 employees, the third largest police corps in Switzerland.
