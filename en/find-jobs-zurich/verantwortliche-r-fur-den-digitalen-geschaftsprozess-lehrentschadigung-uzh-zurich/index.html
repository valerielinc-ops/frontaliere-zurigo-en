University of Zurich / Faculty of Medicine / Student Candidate

Job as per 1. December 2026 or by arrangement, indefinitely

The «University Medicine Zurich» (UMZH) unites the most renowned universities and university hospitals in the network: University of Zurich, ETH Zurich, University Hospital of Zurich, University Children's Hospital of Zurich, University Hospital of Balgrist and Psychiatric University Hospital of Zurich. By networking research with medical treatment, current and future challenges in research, teaching and healthcare can be overcome. The Medical Faculty (MeF) counts around 4000 students. Every year, approximately 430 graduates complete their studies in human, dental or chiropractic medicine. Over 2,000 academic lecturers are available for training. Students are offered a modern and high-quality education. The responsible Dean of Studies is responsible, among other things, for the planning of the courses as well as for the settlement of the teaching compensation and for the Stakaholder teachers. The billing process is currently being digitized and automated. Do you want to help shape and take responsibility for the business process teaching compensation?

Tasks

They are tech-savvy, have a flair for numbers and enjoy actively shaping digital processes. You are happy to take responsibility for:

  • Accounting and statistics: You are responsible for the accounting of the teaching allowances for the partner and teaching hospitals and produce the annual statistics on the teaching services provided by all participating institutions.
  • Stakeholder and human resources management: They supervise the teachers and coordinate their registration and deregistration in cooperation with the HR department of UZH. In addition, you will develop and maintain a practice-oriented manual for these processes.
  • Planning and organization: You coordinate and organize twice a year the elective area of human and dental medicine using planning software.
  • Process and data management: You coordinate and optimize business processes, implement them efficiently and ensure careful data maintenance.
  • Digital processes and projects: You contribute your ideas for the further development of digital processes, quickly integrate into new software tools and actively support projects.

Profile

  • Completed training in administration (KV) or business or similar/equivalent direction and professional experience in higher education
  • You are a motivated, technically affine personality with a strong flair for numbers and data.
  • They have SAP knowledge and can quickly find their way into new software applications.
  • They enjoy working independently, digital processes and creating meaningful statistics.
  • They think analytically, work in a structured way and keep an overview even in complex processes.
  • They are team-oriented, like to communicate with different stakeholders and appreciate cooperation in a dynamic environment.
