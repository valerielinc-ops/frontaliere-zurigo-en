The Biosecurity Section is responsible for the protection of humans, animals, the environment and biodiversity from hazards caused by pathogenic, genetically modified or locally absent organisms. Our enforcement tasks include activities in the areas of prevention, control, damage mitigation and coordination. Among other things, you are jointly responsible for the development and implementation of the Neobiota Action Plan in the Canton of Zurich. This presupposes close cooperation with other cantonal specialist bodies and federal authorities, as well as the participation in or management of specialist working groups. You work in a dedicated collegial team and supervise an intern. You have completed university studies in the field of biology (preferably organismic biology, ecology) or environmental sciences as well as several years of professional experience.
