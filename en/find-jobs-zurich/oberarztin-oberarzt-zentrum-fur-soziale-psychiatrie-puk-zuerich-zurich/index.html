Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 80-100% For our Center for Social Psychiatry of Adult Psychiatry at the military road site, we are looking for an adult psychiatry from 1 February 2027 or by agreement Their tasks

  • Senior medical responsibility in an interprofessional team in the ambulatory or semi-stationary area
  • Integrated psychiatric-psychotherapeutic diagnosis and treatment of adults
  • Supervision and further training of medical, psychological and nursing staff
  • Co-design of our ambulatory and semi-stationary offerings as well as networking with internal and external partners
  • Participation in teaching and further education as well as – if interested – in scientific projects

Your profile

  • Presence of a Swiss medical diploma (MEBEKO registered) or MEBEKO recognition of the foreign medical diploma (not registration) plus MEBEKO language entry German, to be submitted with the application
  • Completed or advanced training as a specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy
  • Psychotherapeutic experience
  • Leadership experience or supervision experience is advantageous
  • Team and communication skills, personal responsibility and patient orientation
  • Openness for innovative treatment concepts and interprofessional cooperation

Our offer

  • Clinic, research and teaching united: working in a university environment with international networking
  • Development with perspective: Diverse career paths in one of the largest psychiatric clinics in Switzerland
  • Shaping the future actively: Participation in change and development processes
  • Sustainable travel: participation in the ZVV-BonusPass or general subscription
  • Future provision with added value: 60% employer contribution to the pension fund
  • Well taken care of in everyday work: discounted food offers or lunch checks
