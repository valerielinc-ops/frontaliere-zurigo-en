Full description

Amstein + Walthert is an engineering company with around 1,100 employees. As engineers, planners and consultants, we work on all aspects of the technology of civil engineering and civil engineering over the entire life cycle. Our solutions are innovative, elegant and sustainable. designs. Lead. Development. Do you want to promote people, advance challenging projects and actively shape the future of building technology? Then you take responsibility for an HLKS team and shape innovative solutions for our customers together with your colleagues. These are your tasks

  • Professional and human leadership as well as further development of a motivated HLKS team
  • Promoting open, performance-oriented and cross-team collaboration Responsibility for the successful planning and implementation of challenging HVAC projects
  • Active maintenance and further development of customer relationships as well as support in the acquisition of new projects
  • Participation in market activities, customer events, offers and presentations
  • Promoting professional know-how within the team and actively shaping our training and development culture
  • Ensuring quality, deadlines, costs and resources in your area of responsibility
  • Close cooperation with interdisciplinary teams from building technology, electrical engineering, building automation, building physics and other specialist areas
  • Representation of our company towards clients, planners and partners You bring that with you
  • Completed studies as an HLKS engineer: in FH/RF or a comparable education with corresponding professional experience
  • Several years of experience in the planning and realization of demanding building technology projects
  • First leadership experience or motivation to take the next career step towards team leadership
  • Entrepreneurial thinking as well as a strong customer and service orientation
  • Enjoy promoting employees and achieving success together
  • Secure appearance and stylish communication to customers and project partners
  • Interest in innovations, sustainable solutions and the further development of building technology
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Good knowledge of common planning and BIM tools such as Revit and MS Office
