Full description

VZ is Switzerland's leading independent financial services provider. We advise private individuals and companies on all matters relating to money, manage assets and offer attractive financial services. As we are growing rapidly, we are looking for additional support for our software engineering team at our headquarters near Paradeplatz in Zurich.

Responsibilities

  • You develop and maintain business applications in the corporate client, pension and insurance sectors.
  • You have end-to-end responsibility for development, deployment and operations in accordance with well-established DevSecOps principles.
  • You work directly, as partners and on an equal footing with the relevant departments.
  • You will become part of a well-coordinated, agile team in a close-knit environment that continuously works to improve what already exists.

Requirements

  • A higher education in information technology and at least 3 years of experience in a comparable role
  • Experience developing modern web applications – from frontend frameworks through REST APIs to databases, ideally with Angular, C#/.NET and MS SQL Server
  • Knowledge of the design and use of distributed systems with Kubernetes and Microsoft Azure
  • Enjoyment of working in a team and with our departments to develop joint solutions
  • Expertise in corporate clients, pensions and insurance is a plus

We offer

  • Flexible working hours, several work-from-home days per week and a modern workplace in a central location in Zurich.
  • A collegial environment with regular social events, varied tasks and plenty of scope for independent work.
  • The opportunity to actively shape a growing company, try out new ideas and continuously develop yourself.
  • Every year, you have three days and CHF 1'000 available for your professional development. We support more extensive training within the scope of your job profile.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees rate VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully thrive. Become part of it!
