When precision joins system understanding

For our Customer Master Data team, we are looking for a reliable and analytical personality with a strong sense of quality and organisation. In this function, you ensure that our customer data is structured in a correct, complete and sustainable manner. You are evolving at the interface of different areas of activity, analyzing complex customer structures and finding technical solutions to meet specific requirements. This position is particularly suitable for people who like to work in a concentrated way, understand the links between the different elements and continually develop their expertise in a central business process. Thanks to your contribution, you create the foundation for our teams to collaborate effectively and efficiently with our customers every day.

Your assets for this position

  • Trade, technical or equivalent training
  • Experience in using ERP systems and good MS Office knowledge; SAP knowledge (in particular SD and/or FI module) is an asset
  • Interest in working with complex data, systems and structures
  • High precision, patience and perseverance as well as an analytical and structured approach
  • Excellent knowledge of French and German; knowledge in English is an asset
