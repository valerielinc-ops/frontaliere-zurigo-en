In this responsible function, they ensure efficient appointment and process coordination and thus enable a smooth process in therapeutic everyday life. We offer: We offer you a varied activity with our own area of responsibility and development opportunities in a competent and committed team. If you are interested in a stimulating working day in the professional environment of Physiotherapy Occupational Therapy USZ and would like to actively participate, we look forward to your application.
