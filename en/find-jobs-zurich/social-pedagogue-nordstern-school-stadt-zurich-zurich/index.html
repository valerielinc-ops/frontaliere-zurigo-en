Full description

In order to strengthen our committed team, we are looking for a motivated specialist with a socio-educational background. We are looking for you for the co-responsibility and further development of our alternative learning location Starship as well as for networking and regular exchange with offices in the quarter. Their work makes a decisive contribution to ensuring that children with special needs are stabilised and can return to classrooms successfully.

Tasks

  • They share the responsibility for the educational work at the alternative learning location "Starship".
  • They support children with challenging behavior in their development.
  • They plan and carry out learning and support offers for emotional-social learning in individual and small groups.
  • They work closely with teachers, parents, school specialists and other quarters to support the children sustainably.
  • They work on school days from Monday to Friday and make assignments after school closing and during school holidays in the quarters as required.

  • Open, resilient and capable of teamwork with a clearly positive human image that enables trust and development.
  • Enjoying intercultural exchange and profound willingness for interdisciplinary cooperation in the school and district environment.
  • Independent, organized and ready for action – even beyond the regular school day, including readiness for evening services.

We offer

  • Responsible work with great creative leeway that appreciates your pedagogical ideas and competences.
  • A multicultural, dynamic and professional team where you come into contact with different perspectives on a daily basis.
  • They are competently accompanied by the school management and the management supervision.
  • Contemporary salary and social benefits according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich.

About us

The school Nordstern belongs to the school district Glattal in Zurich with 18 schools. Our pedagogical attitude is based on the concept of New Authority – we meet challenges together, clearly and effectively.

Our pedagogical mission statement focuses on strengthening children’s curiosity and willingness to learn. We accompany the children in discovering their individual strengths and developing a healthy self-confidence in their abilities. Because only psychologically empowered children who feel safe and competent remain willing to learn and motivated in the long run. This is why we not only promote school content, but also emotional well-being as an indispensable basis for sustainable learning.

The alternative learning location "Starship" is a central component of our concept. It offers children in need of rest a protected retreat and children with challenging behavior the necessary space for stabilization and regulation. The goal is always that the children can return to the regular class lessons strengthened and reflected.

Networking with the neighbourhood is important from our point of view, as living together in the settlement has a great influence on the children’s school situation. Close cooperation with parents, specialist bodies and other local actors creates the necessary bridges to support the children holistically and sustainably.
