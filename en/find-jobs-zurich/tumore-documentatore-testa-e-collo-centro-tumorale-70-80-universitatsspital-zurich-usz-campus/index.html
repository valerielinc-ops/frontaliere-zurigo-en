Full description

The tumor documentation is part of the Clinical Program (CP) of the Comprehensive Cancer Center Zurich (CCCZ) at USZ. It creates a reliable data basis for quality assurance, certification and the further development of our oncological care. For the head and neck tumor center, we are looking for a dedicated personality who documents medical data in a structured, plausibility checked and evaluated manner and supports the center in quality-relevant questions. >The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.

Tasks

  • You document clinical data of patients with head and neck tumors in OnkoStar and ensure their completeness and plausibility.
  • You collect relevant metrics and data for the head and neck tumor center and prepare them for quality and certification purposes.
  • You support the head and neck tumor center in the annual certification according to the requirements of the German Cancer Society (DKG).
  • You work closely with the interdisciplinary treatment team, center coordination and the CCCZ Clinical Program.
  • You maintain quality-relevant documents in the management system and support the further development of standardized documentation processes.

Requirements

  • You have a medical or scientific education or a comparable qualification with good anatomical and biological foundations.
  • You bring a basic medical or oncological understanding as well as a secure knowledge of medical terminology.
  • You work very carefully, structured and reliable and enjoy dealing with medical data.
  • You move safely in different IT systems, have very good knowledge of Excel and PowerPoint and quickly get involved in new applications

Advantage

  • Experience in tumor documentation, quality management or oncology
  • Knowledge of OnkoStar as well as experience with clinical information systems and medical databases
  • Experience with DKG certifications and/or ISO-based quality management systems

We offer

  • You can expect a versatile and responsible task in an interdisciplinary oncological environment.
  • You will receive a structured training and work closely with an experienced team in the CCCZ and the head and neck tumor center.
  • We offer you flexible working time models and the opportunity for home office within the scope of operational possibilities.
