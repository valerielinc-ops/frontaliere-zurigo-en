Full description

As one of the largest Swiss pension funds, we have properties with around 14,000 apartments and about 315,000 m2 of commercial space, which we manage on our own.

Mansioni

  • Proceeding the interests of the pension fund as builder
  • Control and management of external planners, total contractors, general planners and specialists
  • Ensuring quality, costs and deadlines across all project phases
  • Coordination of all specialists and project managers in larger new construction and renovation projects
  • Independent management of small to medium-sized construction, conversion and renovation projects
  • Support for real estate management in construction issues and damage cases
  • Independent management of the project administration
  • Ensure the flow of information between all parties involved

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: as client representative or construction project manager, preferably in a GU/TU environment or in a comparable function
  • Higher technical school: architect/in FH or building engineering technician/in HF ideally supplemented by business education
  • Strong negotiating skills and confident appearance
  • Communication strength and the necessary diplomacy in dealing with different stakeholders
  • Enforcement capacity
  • good user knowledge in common IT tools, in particular construction administration, MS Office and project management software
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central location: Work 3 minutes walk from Schlieren station
  • Modern infrastructure: spacious and bright offices
  • Flexible working hours: Liberal working time regulations with a lot of flexibility for employees
  • Du-culture: Uncomplicated and open approach
  • Flat hierarchies: Friendly and family environment
  • Social benefits: Excellent pension fund and generous sickness allowance and accident insurance
  • Migros Bank: Preferred interest at Migros Bank on investment and personnel accounts
  • Holidays: Large Trains Holiday Regulation
  • Education and training: financial and/or temporary support for education and training
