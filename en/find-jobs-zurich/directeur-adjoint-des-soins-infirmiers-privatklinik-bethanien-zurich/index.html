Around 340 affiliated doctors from various disciplines ensure high-quality, integrated medical care. The most important specialist areas include orthopedics, visceral and thoracic surgery, urology, gynecology and childbirth, gastroenterology, cardiology, pulmonology and internal medicine including oncology. Around 500 children are born in the maternity ward every year. The Bethanien private clinic, founded in 1912, is part of the Swiss private clinic group Swiss Medical Network. Job description To strengthen our nursing department, we are looking for a committed, technically experienced and strong managerial personality as deputy nursing director. In this responsible role, you will support the Nursing Directorate in the operational management of the nursing area and actively contribute to the further development of our high-quality patient care.   • Active participation in projects, quality and digitalization initiatives
  • Promoting appreciative, interprofessional collaboration
  • Participation in budget planning, controlling and resource management Qualifications
