Role overview

Welcome to Balgrist Tec AG – the leading competence center for orthopaedic aids and medical retailers in Zurich.

As a subsidiary of the Balgrist University Hospital, we combine precision craftsmanship with the latest medicine.

We are looking for an entrepreneurial personality who can further develop our business systems, drive digitalization and implement selected strategic corporate projects.

  • You will continue to develop our ERP governance, establish clear roles, test and acceptance processes and, together with the specialist areas, build an effective key user organization with which you can ensure documentation, knowledge transfer and training around our ERP.
