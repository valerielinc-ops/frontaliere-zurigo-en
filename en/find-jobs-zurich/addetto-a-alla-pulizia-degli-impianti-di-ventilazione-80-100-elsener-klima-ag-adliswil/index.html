Full description

Elsener Klima AG is a Swiss company with competences in ventilation and air conditioning technology. With headquarters in Adliswil and around 30 employees, we work in Zurich and the surrounding area. We belong to the Burkhalter Group, the umbrella organization of traditional companies in the fields of building technology and building envelope in Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein.

Clean ventilation systems are our everyday life: we clean ventilation ducts, ventilation devices and kitchen exhaust systems for our customers. For this we are looking for someone to tackle. You do not need an education, we will teach you. For our team we are looking for one:

Your tasks

  • Cleaning of ventilation ducts, ventilation devices and kitchen exhaust systems for our customers
  • Change filters, clean fans and heat exchangers, disinfect equipment
  • Open and close audit openings, cover and protect rooms at the customer
  • Documentation of work with photos before and after and short rapport
  • Provide materials and equipment, keep vehicle and tools in order
  • After training: Support of service technicians for maintenance

Your profile

  • No training needed, lateral entry welcome. Experience in cleaning or crafting helps
  • Craftsmanship and physical fitness (ladders, narrow spaces, equipment)
  • Reliability and punctuality, you are the face of Elsener Klima
  • Clean, careful work and friendly treatment of people
  • Good knowledge of German and driving licence category B

Our offer

  • At 100%: 4.5 day week, 40 hours from Monday to Friday noon, Friday afternoon free
  • No cooking, regular working hours
  • Thorough training by experienced service technicians
  • Regulated employment conditions with contemporary social benefits
  • Perspective: Anyone who wants to can develop into a ventilation service technician with us
  • Experienced team with good disposition and clear processes

Your contact

Does that sound exciting to you? Then apply with your wage expectations and the earliest possible entry date to Corine Kaderli, staff: corine.kaderli@sada.ch. Eldin Dracic, managing director: +41 44 711 80 40, eldin.dracic@elsener-klima.ch.
