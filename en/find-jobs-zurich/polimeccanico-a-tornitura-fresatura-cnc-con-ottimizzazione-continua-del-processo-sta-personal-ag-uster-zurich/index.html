Full description

The activity includes the independent setup and programming of CNC machines. The company attaches importance to precise production and the collection of production documentation. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Automatically set up, operate and program CNC lathes and/or milling machines
  • Precisely manufacture workpieces according to technical drawings
  • Create production documentation and monitor production processes
  • Continuous optimization of production processes
  • Maintenance, maintenance and assembly of machines mechanically and pneumatically

Requirements

  • Completed training as a polymechanic
  • At least two years of professional experience in CNC
  • Sound knowledge of the Heidenhain programming language
  • Experience with common CAM systems such as Autodesk Inventor, Fusion360 or Hypermill is an advantage
  • German skills in word and writing

Offer

  • Workplace in a dynamic, constantly evolving production environment in Wetzikon
  • 42-hour week with regular overtime pay, five weeks of holidays and additional days off, including company holidays
  • Various development opportunities, regular company events and attractive additional services such as free parking and coffee
