Full description

For our practice, integrated in the hospital Männedorf, we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement *n Senior Medical Assistant 100%

Tasks

  • Leading the MPA team and operational planning
  • Main responsibility for the planning, organization and preparation of office hours incl. Patient reception and care
  • Preparation of urological examinations and assistance in examinations and minor procedures (cystoscopy, prostate biopsy, vasectomy, etc.)
  • Independent performance of blood sampling, urine analysis and injections
  • Optimisation of processes
  • Reception work such as appointments, reception of patients, operation of telephone, etc.
  • Performance controlling
  • General office work, termination

Conditions

  • Completed medical/practice assistant training EFZ and several years of professional experience
  • Leadership experience, further education as an MPK beneficial
  • TARDOC knowledge
  • Good MS-Office user knowledge
  • Friendly and patient personality as well as high resilience and teamwork ability
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and action
  • Experience with the Axenita operating system is advantageous (not mandatory)

  • Attractive, contemporary social benefits
  • Mooring time is paid in time or financially (as desired)
  • Private, worldwide accident insurance
  • Short decision paths, flat hierarchs, possibility to contribute your own ideas
