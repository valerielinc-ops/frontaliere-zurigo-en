Put to the Point

Do you want to take responsibility and shape the future of Command Cyber? In this versatile function, you will work on the technical innovations and you will have the unique opportunity to actively shape the future of Command Cyber and make an important contribution to Switzerland’s defence capability together with a committed team. Direct contact with the troop is also very welcome in your work, as you can prove your practical relevance.

You can make this contribution

  • Support the Chief Projects, Experiments and Corporate Development (C PVUE) in his planning and information tasks
  • On behalf of C PVUE, lead projects and trials in accordance with a uniform process and work out the basis for decision-making
  • Collaborate with and support forces in specific areas related to projects and trials
  • In the field of innovations and technical innovations for the Cyber Command
  • Participate in expert committees and working groups and represent the predefined standards
  • Support in the area of reporting and project documentation

This makes you unique

  • Higher vocational training in the electrotechnical or computer science field resp. Farming
  • Several years of professional experience in the project Management Office (PMO) in the administrative environment, ideally in ICT projects
  • Strong analytical and conceptual skills as well as experience in the preparation of reports and decision bases
  • Convincing communication, presentation and consulting skills as well as experience in cooperation with different stakeholders
  • A military career as an officer (from Kp Kdt) or higher non-commissioned officer (from level Trp Kö) of the Swiss army or a prior use within the group defense is advantageous

Your commitment to security and freedom The Command Operations (Kdo Op) is responsible for all military operations and operations of the Swiss Army. The Kdo Op includes the Luftwaffe, the Army, the four territorial divisions, the SWISSINT Competence Center, the Special Forces Command and the Military Police. Around 40% of the approximately 2,500 employees are civil employees – from engineers to aircraft mechanics to analysts.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
