For our team in the Actives Production department at the location in Volketswil we are looking for a professional production(m/f/d) as of February/March 2027. In this function, you are responsible for the timely production of products in the required quality and quantity. You work with a wide range of raw materials and thus gain an in-depth insight into the world of cosmetics. Your new team is already curious to meet you.

In this exciting position, you will

  • Process implementation and monitoring and cleaning of reactors and peripherals in compliance with ISO9001, ISO14001 and EffCI guidelines (cosmetics GMP) for the following:
  • Processes/product categories: polymers, biotechnological production of cosmetic active ingredients and mixtures for the cosmetics industry (componding)
  • Serve very different production facilities: Mulitpurpose reactors, various vacuum pumps, filter plants, mixers and extraction plants
  • Productions including logging, packaging and cleaning process independently
  • Quality controls: ensuring compliance with quality standards, performing in-process controls (pH, density, refractive index, viscosity and olefin determinations)
  • Managing complex processes in small teams and supporting other employees as needed

Your qualifications and professional experience include

  • Completed teaching, preferably in the field of chemistry, food industry (e.g. baker/confectioner) or mechanics
  • Experience in food, pharmaceutical or chemical production desired
  • Safe handling of chemicals
  • High awareness of compliance with safety, quality and hygiene regulations
  • No (acute) allergies
  • Enjoying a varied activity and the resulting challenges
  • Accurate, forward working methods, high social skills and teamwork skills
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Readiness to work in 2-shift operation
