Full description

You strengthen the physical, social and psychological well-being of our residents and promote their independence in everyday life. You learn to care for and care for people. You help them eat and move. You work closely with medical and therapeutic professionals.

Tasks

  • You take on nursing and medical tasks such as setting infusions or checking vital signs.
  • You support people in the activities of their daily life.
  • You plan, organize and document your work.
  • You manage care materials and medicines.
  • You work closely with qualified nursing staff HF and other professional groups.

Profile

  • Completed compulsory schooling, Sek A or good performance in Sek B
  • Joy in dealing with people and empathy
  • Confidentiality and ability to work in a team
  • Good knowledge of German (minor language level B2) and communication skills
  • Willingness to work irregular hours (taking into account the protection of minors)

We offer

You can expect a well-founded and varied education. Your vocational trainees support you on your way to a successful teaching degree as FaGe. You benefit from attractive employment conditions.

About us

At one of our 34 locations throughout the city of Zurich, you will lay the foundation for your career: Become part of our team and learn from the best. From the beginning you are in the middle of it and not only there. With more than 3,300 employees from more than 100 nations, we are one of the largest healthcare training companies in Switzerland. We currently train 800 apprentices, students and interns in a wide variety of professional groups. We look after and care for people of older age with competence and heart, contribute to their well-being and offer them a comfortable home. With us, the human being is at the center.
