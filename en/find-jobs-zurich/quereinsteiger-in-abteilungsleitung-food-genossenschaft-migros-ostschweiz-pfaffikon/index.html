Full description

In the approximately 100 supermarket branches of Genossenschaft Migros Ostschweiz, we provide everything needed for the daily needs of our customers. Our employees work with great enthusiasm and spirit to guarantee our traditional values such as freshness and quality at a fair price.

Responsibilities

  • Technical management of the Food department
  • Revenue, cost, inventory, and stock management
  • Ordering, goods receipt control, product presentation, and disposition
  • Sales promotion
  • Customer service and advice

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: in retail
  • Completed basic or advanced training: in retail
  • Very good expertise in the Food sector
  • Reliability, flexibility, and high commitment
  • Independent working style and friendly interaction with customers
  • Willingness for continuous training
  • German (very good knowledge)

What we offer

  • L-GAV: Attractive Migros national collective labor agreement
  • Insurance: Preferential conditions with various insurance companies
  • Occupational pension: Attractive pension benefits with high employer contributions
  • Social benefits: Excellent social benefits in the areas of child allowances, daily sickness benefits, and accident insurance
  • Cumulus points: Multiple Cumulus points on Food and Non-Food items
