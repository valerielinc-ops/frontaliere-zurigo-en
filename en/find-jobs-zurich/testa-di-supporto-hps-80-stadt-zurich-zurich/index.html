Full description

The Riedtli site in the Waidberg School District was opened in the 2025-26 school year. We have three classes from kindergarten to intermediate school with a total of 21 students*. We are looking for an experienced team to complement our management team from 01 January 2027. Management Support HPS, 80%.

Tasks

  • Management of special education in the fields of personnel, operation and pedagogical
  • Staff management of staff in the area of support with a management margin of about 20 employees
  • Ensuring that needs and needs-oriented care is provided in teaching and care
  • Support of the school management at the site in the planning and implementation of school development processes
  • Representation of the school management in the day-organization at the site

Profile

  • Graduate in the field of social pädagogik at tertiary level (university, higher technical school)
  • Completed or willing to take a lead and practical leadership experience
  • Experience in the field of special education
  • Extensive social and self-competences, as well as a clearly inclusive attitude
  • Very good team and communication skills

We offer

  • Challenging, varied and responsible work with a lot of design space
  • Exciting interdisciplinary tasks in the special school area in close cooperation with the regular school
  • Possibility of active participation in our school
  • Advanced employment conditions according to the requirements of the City of Zurich

About us

The Heilpädagogische Schule of the City of Zurich is a day special school for 4 to 15 year old children with a cognitive and/or multiple impairment. As part of the service department Sonderpädagogik, students who are trained in separate classes in different school houses in the City of Zurich offer a supportive environment and set the focus of interdisciplinary support in their strengths.

Our teams at the various locations in the city of Zurich consist of school healing teachers, social educators, specialist care staff, learners and assistants.
