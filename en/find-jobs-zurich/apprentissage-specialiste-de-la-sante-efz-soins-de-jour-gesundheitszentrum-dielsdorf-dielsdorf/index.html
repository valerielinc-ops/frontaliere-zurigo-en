Das Gesundheitszentrum Dielsdorf is a modern geriatric competence centre with around 500 employees from 35 nations. Our services range from counseling for older people and their relatives, through general and specialised long‑term care, to home care and support. With more than 200 beds, the inpatient offering of general long‑term care at the Dielsdorf site is complemented by specialised treatment pathways for rehabilitative acute and transitional care, Palliative Care, psychiatric geriatrics and a focus on dementia. At the Regensdorf site we provide 54 residents with a family‑like environment and professional nursing care.
