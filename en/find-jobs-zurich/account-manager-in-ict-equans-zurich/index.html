You don’t want to just go to work in the morning, but make a difference? Inspire customers, win exciting ICT projects and celebrate success together with a strong team? Welcome to EQUANS ICT Services. We are not looking for classic sales profiles, but makers with technical understanding, vision and enthusiasm for people and customer solutions. With us, you get the freedom to take responsibility, contribute your ideas and grow together with a top team. For our sales area Zurich, Lucerne and Eastern Switzerland, we are looking for an ambitious personality who wants to grow together with us, win new customers and take existing customer relationships to the next level.

You can move that with us.

  • Win new customers and build long-term customer relationships
  • Advising customers on modern ICT solutions for cloud, workplace, security, network and managed services
  • Identify sales opportunities and successfully complete them
  • Develop offers independently, calculate and present them
  • Acting on an equal footing with IT leaders, CIOs, managing directors and decision-makers
  • Develop customer strategies and sustainably expand accounts
  • Represent EQUANS at events, trade fairs and customer meetings

That's what makes you.

  • Basic technical training, e.g. in computer science, telematics or ICT
  • Several years of experience as an engineer or system engineer
  • Experience in sales, account management or solution sales. Experience in public procurement is an advantage.
  • Good knowledge of Cloud, Microsoft 365 & Azure, Security, Network or Modern Workplace
  • Secure and professional appearance at all hierarchical levels
  • Negotiation skills, initiative and final strength
  • Category B driving licence and very good knowledge of German

That's what we offer you.

  • Exciting customer projects with the latest technologies
  • Lots of personal responsibility, freedom of design and short decision paths
  • A motivated team with real DU culture and strong cohesion
  • Individual training and development opportunities
  • Flexible working models and modern forms of work
  • Attractive compensation with performance-oriented components
  • The strength and security of an international group combined with the agility of a strong Swiss ICT team
