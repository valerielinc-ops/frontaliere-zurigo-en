Do you know our multiple award-winning and award-winning wines? During your training as a wine expert specializing in winemaking at Strickhof, you will learn more about this and become a professional yourself. What you learn with us You will carry out the varied, year-round work of a winery. This includes work in the vineyard, pressing and bottling the wines as well as marketing them from the winery or at various events. You will learn a lot about wine through training courses or in connection with grape variety experiments. The goals are: exchange of experiences between fellow winemakers in favor of Eastern Swiss wine quality and working close to nature in the vineyard. What sets you apart For this training, you bring with you technical skills and a technical understanding of machines and equipment. Robust health for working in wind and weather as well as enjoyment of nature are basic requirements. We support you if you want to complete your apprenticeship with a vocational qualification.
