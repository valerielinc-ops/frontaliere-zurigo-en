Full description

For the operation Handwerk & Logistik we are looking for 1. November 2026 or by arrangement half a year reinforcement in the team.

Tasks

  • You will accompany work teachers in the preparation for work as well as in the order-related technical instructions.
  • You support in the planning of the participants.
  • You provide practical cooperation and relieve the workforce in everyday work.
  • You participate in team meetings.
  • You do administrative work.

Profile

  • Swiss Federal Certificate of Competence in a professional field of craftsmanship and logistics (shyman, seamstress, mechanic, logistician or related professions)
  • Scheduled training course in Labour Education
  • Openness and understanding towards people with disabilities
  • Ability to self-reflection
  • Flexibility, teamwork, resilience and strong communication skills

We offer

A meaningful activity with intensive co-creation opportunity. Through your active participation, you will gain an insight and gain initial theoretical and practical experience in the field of employment. Important: The job with us is not an apprenticeship, but a preliminary internship for the training course Arbeitsagogik.

About us

With around 2,400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a social Zurich. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.

The work integration division of the Social Institutions and Enterprises Service Department (SEB) comprises a range of work integration services for adults and young people. The aim of the programmes is the professional and social integration of the participants. The company Handwerk & Logistik consists of the teams Sewing, Woodworking, Velowerkstatt, Netzpflege Züri Velo and Werkatelier.
