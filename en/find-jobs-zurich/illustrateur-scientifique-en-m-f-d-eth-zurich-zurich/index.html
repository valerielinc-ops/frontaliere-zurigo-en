Project background

The SED is significantly involved in the development of an earthquake early warning app that is already in use in several countries. With a view to the introduction in Switzerland and in other European countries, we are developing communication materials that convey the functioning and benefits of earthquake early warning in an understandable way.

You develop illustrations and animations in an iterative, user-centred process and contribute to making the visual language understandable and effective across cultures.
