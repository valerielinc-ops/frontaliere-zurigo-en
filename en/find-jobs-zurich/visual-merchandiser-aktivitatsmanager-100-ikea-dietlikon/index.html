Full description

Who you are

You have a degree in Visual Merchandising, Industrial Design, or a related field, combined with proven experience in creating inspiring and commercially successful retail environments.

  • You are a strong and inclusive leader who can inspire, motivate, coach, and develop people, enabling your team to achieve business goals, drive growth, and contribute to long-term profitability.
  • You have a solid understanding of the IKEA concept, brand identity, home furnishing solutions, and customer experience, and you are passionate about bringing these elements to life in the store.
  • You are proactive and solution-oriented, always looking for opportunities to improve ways of working, optimize performance, and create better results for customers and the business.
  • You enjoy challenging both yourself and your team to continuously develop, strengthen the IKEA brand, and exceed customer expectations.
  • You build strong relationships and collaborate effectively with stakeholders across the store, creating alignment and ensuring a consistent and inspiring customer experience.
  • You thrive in a fast-paced environment, balancing creativity, commerciality, and operational excellence to deliver outstanding results.

Your responsibilities

As a Visual Merchandising Team Leader, you will play a key role in strengthening the IKEA brand and creating an inspiring shopping experience that makes IKEA the first choice for home furnishing in the local market.

You will

-Lead, recruit, coach, and develop a high-performing Visual Merchandising team, creating an environment where people can grow and perform at their best.

-Create and implement inspiring home furnishing solutions that showcase the IKEA range and help customers improve their everyday life at home.

-Use the store layout and visual merchandising tools to support commercial priorities, drive sales, enhance customer flow, and create a strong visual impact throughout the store.

  • Collaborate closely with key stakeholders across the store to align commercial priorities, ensure seamless execution, and achieve common business goals.
  • Secure the implementation and continuous development of IKEA visual merchandising standards, concepts, and ways of working.
  • Drive commercial growth and profitability by combining creativity, customer insight, and business understanding.
  • Challenge and inspire your team to continuously improve, strengthen the IKEA brand, and exceed customer expectations.
  • Contribute to delivering an outstanding customer experience by creating inspiring, relevant, and affordable home furnishing solutions for the many people.

This is an exciting opportunity for a leader who is passionate about people, home furnishing, and creating commercial solutions that inspire customers every day.
