Full description

Does your heart beat for older people? Is the nursing profession your calling? Do you see opportunities to meaningfully contribute your skills and experience to us in order to have a positive impact on the lives of our customers? Then you are a perfect fit for us!

We are looking for someone for the Restelberg in the Zurich region immediately or by appointment

Deputy Head of Care and Support / Team Leader 100%

Your tasks

  • Personal and professional management of the care team for approx. 26 guests
  • Shared responsibility for recruiting new employees
  • Planning and leading team meetings and participating in interdisciplinary reports
  • Responsible for organizing ward processes, implementing work processes and duty scheduling with Polypoint PEP
  • Leadership and control as well as participation as a team leader in nursing reports and nursing discussions.
  • Shared responsibility for the implementation of internal concepts
  • Active participation in the further development of the care offerings in the company
  • Holistic care and support for guests in accordance with our nursing principles and the nursing process
  • Taking on tasks as day-to-day responsibility and caregiver
  • Taking on tasks as a subject in a specialist topic and thus participating in the group-wide network on care quality

Your profile

  • Completed training to become a qualified nursing specialist FH/HF/DNII
  • Possession of BAB is an advantage
  • Professional and managerial experience in long-term care an advantage
  • Professional experience working with people with dementia desired
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoy a variety of challenges
  • Good knowledge of spoken and written German
