Full description

The Office for Additional Benefits to the AHV/IV (AZL) is a service department of the Social Department of the City of Zurich. It ensures the securing of an adequate material existence of low-income citizens. It provides additional benefits to AHV and IV pensioners, bridging benefits for older unemployed people, contributions to relief offers and acute and transitional care, as well as the energy cost allowance. In addition, the AZL is responsible for the alignment of public care contributions to inpatient long-term care and the municipal solidarity contribution.

We are looking for a reliable, flexible and friendly clerk in the area of additional services as of November 2026 or by agreement.

Tasks

  • Comprehensive handling of cases for the provision of additional benefits to the AHV and IV
  • Thorough analysis and documentation of the economic and – where relevant – personal circumstances of pensioners
  • Advice for beneficiaries and their relatives – empathetic and competent
  • Independent handling of all tasks from correspondence to dossier management to pending management
  • Work according to clear legal bases and internal guidelines
  • Various assignments and holiday representatives for clerks with their own cases

Profile

  • Completed commercial vocational training or higher qualifications (e.g. certificate of social insurance)
  • Several years of professional experience in an independent work area
  • analytical, structured and responsible way of working
  • Enjoyment of administrative tasks
  • Understanding and empathy for elderly and disabled people
  • Interest in social and social issues
