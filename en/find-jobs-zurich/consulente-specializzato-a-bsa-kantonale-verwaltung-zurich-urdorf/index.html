Full description

For our team of specialist and project support we are looking for you as a specialist consultant for operational and safety equipment. You have completed an electrotechnical apprenticeship and further training as a technician in HF and would like to use your knowledge in a variety of ways? With us, you represent the operational interests of the Territorial Unit VII with the Urdorf Operations Control Centre in expansion and maintenance projects of the Federal Office for Roads ASTRA. Together with internal and external specialists, you ensure that technical plants can be operated reliably, safely and economically in the long term. Diversity strengthens our teams. That is why we welcome applications from people with different experiences and perspectives. Your tasks

  • Representing operational interests: Representing GE VII in ASTRA conservation projects and introducing operational and maintenance requirements for the Department of Operational and Safety Equipment (BSA)
  • Providing technical advice: Advising planners and entrepreneurs on GE VII BSA conditions, in particular energy supply, lighting, ventilation, signalling, monitoring, communication and network technology
  • Processing project documents and specifications: Examination of BSA project documents, preparation of technical opinions and development of operational requirements and technical standards
  • Accompanying inspections and acceptances: Participation in factory inspections, system tests, commissioning and acceptance of BSA to ensure reliable and maintainable facilities
  • Coordinate cooperation and knowledge transfer: coordination between projects and the internal BSA specialist teams as well as management of technical meetings and organisation of training Your profile
  • Technical understanding: In-depth knowledge in the electrical field and understanding of the interaction of operational and safety equipment
  • Networked thinking: identifying technical relationships and assessing the impact of project decisions on operation and maintenance
  • Communication and teamwork: conveying technical concerns in an understandable way and working constructively with planners, entrepreneurs and internal teams
  • Self-responsibility and organization: Process tasks independently, forward-lookingly and reliably and keep an eye on deadlines and priorities
  • Quality awareness and solution orientation: carefully examine documents and plants and develop practical, reliable and business-friendly solutions
  • Category B driving licence and the willingness to take on occasionally scheduled night missions This is what awaits you
  • You move in a broad technical world from energy supply and ventilation to communication and network technology and benefit from a varied working day between conceptual work, professional exchange and assignments at the plants
  • You accompany projects from planning to commissioning and experience the results of your work directly on site
  • You work independently and at the same time can count on the knowledge and support of our technical teams
  • You make an important contribution to the reliable and safe operation of national roads
