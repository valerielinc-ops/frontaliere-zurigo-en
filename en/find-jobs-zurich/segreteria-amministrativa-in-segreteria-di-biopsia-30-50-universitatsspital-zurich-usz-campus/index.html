Full description

As administrative secretary: in the biopsy secretariat, you are an important point of contact for our administrative processes and ensure that everything runs smoothly in the background. With your precise and reliable way of working, you make a valuable contribution to ensuring that reports, patient data and orders are processed efficiently and that our diagnostics is optimally supported.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • Exact addressing and input of clearing data of internal and external orders • Recording of gynecological cases • Telephone and written information and clarifications • Preparation and settlement of tumor boards • Writing histological and cytological reports from dictates or via the SynReport Tool • Completion of all secretarial tasks (archiving, mail and material dispatch nationally and internationally) • Enjoyment of routine work Requirements • Commercial or basic medical training • Completed education or further education as medical / hospital secretary: in desirable • Style-confident knowledge of German in words and writing • Reliability, independence and responsibility • Team-oriented and solution-oriented thinking • Openness to new things and willingness to continuously improve workflows • Joy of writing and safe application of medical terminology We offer: We offer you a versatile task in an advanced working environment, which is characterized by high quality, independence, flexibility and a sense of responsibility. A motivated and committed team supports you in all respects. Do you feel addressed by this task and this interesting field of expertise? We look forward to meeting you!
