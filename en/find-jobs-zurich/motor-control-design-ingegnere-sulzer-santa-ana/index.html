About the role

Position Summary

This position operates within the Order Related Engineering (ORE) department and is responsible for designing Sales Order specific motor control electrical schematics and entering the associated bill of material (BOM) into the ERP system. This position closely works with Project Engineer to support the submittal package and provide electrical expertise to the customers. Upon approval of the submittal package, this position is accountable for generating part numbers in the ERP system and releasing the finalized package to production. Furthermore, this position provides electrical engineering expertise to Tendering and Operations by reviewing project specifications and offering recommendations Principal Duties and Responsibilities:

  • Review project scope and specifications. Collaborate with Mechanical Engineer to organize hand-off meetings as necessary to ensure alignment and clarity.
  • Design motor control electrical schematics to support production sales orders by reading and understanding specifications, quotes, purchase orders, or company scope worksheets.
  • Enter parts and create assemblies of the motor control designs in the ERP system for procurement and production electrical assembly.
  • Support Project Engineer with motor control design documentation required for sales order submittal packages.
  • Check sales order specific electrical schematics (Drawings and BOM) created by other team members in ORE.
  • Provides electrical review of specifications for Tendering, as assigned by ORE Manager.
  • Provides electrical review and support to other departments, as assigned by ORE Manager
  • Provide electrical support to field service technicians during installation and acceptance of products.
  • Perform additional duties and participate in special projects as assigned.

Decision Authority

Responsibility: This position is responsible for making decisions related to electrical design, drawing creation, BOM verification and co-ordination between different departments. Also responsible for collaborating with Project Engineer during submittal approval process and support Operations.

Delegation: May delegate tasks such as Drawing checks and BOM verification to other team members within the ORE department, under the guidance of ORE Manager.

Approval Process: This position is responsible for approving electrical components and drawings before release to production. Collaborate with Project Engineer in the customer approval process for Submittal packages.

Consensus: Required to participate in consensus decision-making during cross-functional meetings such as Tendering and Operations to ensure alignment.

Automated Decisions: Utilizes Visual system and design tools such as AutoCAD Electrical and Vault to support decision-making related to component selection, BOM creation, and workflow updates.
