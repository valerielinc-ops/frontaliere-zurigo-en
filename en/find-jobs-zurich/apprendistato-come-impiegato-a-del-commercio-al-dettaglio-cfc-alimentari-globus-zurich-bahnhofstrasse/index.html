Full description

GLOBUS is the leading premium and luxury department store in Switzerland and stands for inspiration, international brands and excellent customer service. Start your 3-year apprenticeship as a retail specialist EFZ with a focus on designing shopping experiences and go through a versatile sales apprenticeship. You will not only gain comprehensive knowledge of the sale of high-quality products, but also get to know the processes in the back office and in the logistics centre. This will give you an overview of the entire retail chain and help you understand what makes a luxury department store tick. Your place of work is in the heart of Zurich on the famous Bahnhofstrasse. Be part of a team that appreciates first-class customer service. The apprenticeship will start on 1 August 2027.

  • You advise and serve our demanding clientele professionally and individually.
  • You will acquire extensive specialist knowledge of our industry-specific products.
  • You design the sales area through an attractive presentation of goods.
  • You will use various digital tools to optimize the sales process.
  • You will support our team in warehouse management and ensure efficient inventory management.
  • You operate the checkouts confidently and contribute to a smooth shopping experience.

WHAT YOU BRING TO THE TABLE

  • You have a good secondary school leaving certificate by the start of your apprenticeship.
  • You enjoy selling and are interested in high-quality and exclusive products.
  • You are motivated and want to learn all the time.
  • Communication and teamwork skills are your strengths.
  • You are reliable and like to take on responsibility.
  • You bring vitality and physical resilience with you.
  • A well-groomed appearance, impeccable manners and a positive charisma are a matter of course for you.
  • You have a very good knowledge of German; other foreign language skills are an advantage.

YOUR ADVANTAGES AS A SALES APPRENTICE WITH US

  • Top career start: After your training, you will have great opportunities to connect and a cool job reference in your pocket.
  • Varied tasks: Work in an exciting environment, surrounded by a motivated team and qualified practical trainers who will be happy to train you.
  • Modern working environment: Our workplace is located in the heart of the city, right on the pulse of the hustle and bustle of our department store.
  • Work-life balance: As an apprentice, enjoy 6 weeks of vacation and flexible working hours.
  • Financial support: Each year of your apprenticeship you will receive a training benefit that you can use for a laptop, language stays or diplomas.
  • Cost coverage : We cover the cost of your compulsory textbooks.
  • Employee discounts: Benefit from up to 50% discount on products from the GLOBUS world for you and your family.
  • 13th month's salary: Last but not least: GLOBUS apprentices receive a 13th month's salary at the end of the year.

YOUR CONTACT Have we aroused your interest? Then apply now and become part of the GLOBUS community! We look forward to receiving your application and expect it with a letter of application, CV and all school reports including personal and social skills.
