Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 100% Working time is more than just recording hours: Different working time models, absences and legal requirements make time management a versatile and demanding field. We are looking for a personality for our time management team immediately or by agreement who enjoys working precisely and likes to deal with such questions, in the role of: Their tasks

  • Checking, recording and correcting absences and working hours in our time recording system Polypoint PEP
  • Processing of medical certificates as well as other mutations in the area of time management and testing / management of holiday, flextime and absence balances
  • Support for entry, exit and internal mutations in the area of time management
  • Support for the end of the month and the processing of data for contract processing
  • Answering questions from employees and managers about working hours, absences and time management
  • Participation in ensuring correct and legally compliant time management as well as in other administrative tasks and projects

Your profile

  • Commercial education or a comparable education and very good knowledge of German
  • Professional experience in administration, HR or in a comparable, number- and process-oriented environment
  • High interest in working time issues as well as the relevant provisions of the Labour Code and pleasure in familiarizing oneself with complex regulations and processes
  • Very accurate, reliable and responsible working methods, a quick understanding and a strong ability to analytically capture issues
  • Safe handling of MS Office
  • Experience with Polypoint PEP or a comparable time recording system is advantageous
  • Service-oriented, team-capable and independent personality

Our offer

  • Space for ideas: High personal responsibility and design possibilities
  • Strong in the team: appreciative cooperation and mutual support
  • Shaping the future actively: Participation in change and development processes
  • Focus on health: Various health-promoting offers, green oases and an in-house care team
  • Future provision with added value: 60% employer contribution to the pension fund
  • Sustainable travel: participation in the ZVV-BonusPass or general subscription

Good to know: The position is initially until 30. April 2027, with the possibility of extension until 30. September 2027. After successful training, it is also possible to reduce the workload from the fourth month.
