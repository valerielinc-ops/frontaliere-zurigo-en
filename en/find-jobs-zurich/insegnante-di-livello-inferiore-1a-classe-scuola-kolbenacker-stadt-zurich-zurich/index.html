Full description

For a temporary assignment from 19 October 2026 (or by arrangement) until the end of the school year 2026/27, we are looking for reinforcements!

Tasks

Basic data

  • Pensum: about 95%, 26 WL
  • Class: Lessons at three 1. Primary classes
  • Task: Support to stabilize a demanding situation at the entire level of 1st primary classes. More information is available from the school management.

Main tasks

  • Planning, preparation and implementation of teaching based on the curriculum
  • Follow-up and evaluation of lessons
  • Advising pupils on individual learning and performance difficulties as well as showing funding opportunities
  • Care of parental contact as well as planning and implementation of special events
  • Collaboration in the college, with the school management, external specialist departments and the guardians (with parental discussions)

Profile

  • Teaching diploma for the primary level, recognized in Switzerland
  • Enjoy teaching students from different cultures
  • Ability to successfully lead and organize the class
  • Willingness and joy at work in the integrated setting, together with school curative teachers and assistants
  • Positive basic attitude, teamwork, resilience, high social skills

We offer

  • a well-organized school that has been professionally run for many years
  • An open-minded, committed team
  • established, supportive cooperation in the pedagogical team
  • supplementary offers for teaching (learning island, assistantships)
  • Educational KITS support and school management secretariat on site
  • free use of public transport and cultural offers with class (in the city of Zurich)

About us

We are a large school community in Zurich-Nord with 19 schools. We derive our pedagogical attitude from the concept of New Authority and thus meet the daily challenges in everyday school life together and effectively.

The primary school Kolbenacker is a modern, innovative QUIMS school in the district of Seebach with 20 primary classes, 8 kindergartens and 6 hoards. It is easily accessible by public transport.
