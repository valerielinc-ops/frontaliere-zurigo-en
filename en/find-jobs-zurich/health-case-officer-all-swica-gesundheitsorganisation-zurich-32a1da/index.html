Full description

We are committed to health – because health is everything. We are committed to this, also as an employer. We are convinced: Only in a healthy working world do the best talents feel comfortable and move forward: professionally and personally. For that we give everything. For this important role, we are looking for a detail-oriented and reliable personality who appreciates the exchange with our insured. In the "Healthy Being" department, we are committed to the concerns of our insured every day and attach great importance to personal and competent care. We offer you a modern and safe working environment in a warm and committed team. This is how you shape health

  • Ensuring customer-centred support for policyholders
  • Checking and processing of invoices, requests for cost-related language and requests for services
  • Taking on an important role along the customer journey
  • Handling of benefits cases in the areas of prevention, aids, maternity, dentistry, complementary medicine and nutritional advice
  • Close cooperation with process and specialist support
  • Cooperation with customer service
  • Correspondence with policyholders and service providers
  • Telephone contact with social, private insurance institutions and authorities and internal interfaces

This sets you apart

  • Commercial or medical professional training
  • Experience in the field of services as well as a health or social security card of advantage
  • Flexible, responsible and resilient personality
  • Precise and independent work
  • Proper oral and written expression in German, good English skills, additional language skills are an advantage
  • Open personality with communication flair and enjoyment of cross-line collaboration

Why SWICA

  • We build on humanity. That is why we focus on openness, respect and trust in our everyday work. We tackle this, take responsibility and think in terms of solutions – together, for our customers. This is also why we have been number 1 in customer satisfaction for many years.
  • We trust you and give you space. With annual working hours, at least 5 weeks holidays and on request also holiday purchase. With part-time models and generous home office within the scope of the possibilities of the job, you make your everyday life largely flexible.
  • We value and reward your work – with a market-based wage, up to 1,500 francs annual fee for personal wishes and a generous participation in the basic and additional insurance for you and your family.
  • We offer you prospects. With a generous training regulations, we support your professional and personal development – for your role today and for your goals of tomorrow.
  • Because health is everything. With our company health management and region-specific offers, we take care of your well-being. And if it comes otherwise: 100% net wage continued payment.
  • We think long term. With three savings plans, pensum-dependent coordination deduction and benefits well above the legal minimum, you ensure good for your future.

Vanessa Landi, HR Recruiting, is happy to provide you with information.
