Application process

  • Develop and conduct an independent postdoctoral research project in Japanese Art History leading to further academic qualification (including publications, conference presentations and competitive grant applications).
  • Actively contribute to the Chair's research, teaching, and academic administration, with particular responsibility for Japanese Art History and Digital Art History.
  • Teach 2-4 semester hours per week (SWS; one SWS equals 45 minutes), normally corresponding to one or two courses per semester.
  • Teaching may include theoretical, methodological and object-based formats developed in collaboration with museums and collections.
  • Contribute to the integration of digital methods into art-historical research and teaching, including the development of suitable learning formats and research resources.
  • Practical experience in the application of digital methods relevant to humanities or art-historical research.
  • This should be demonstrated through completed research projects (incl. datasets, code), publications, teaching or other scholarly activities.
  • Relevant approaches may include digital image analysis and computer vision, GIS and spatial analysis, network analysis, semantic modelling and linked data; corpus construction from digital repositories.
