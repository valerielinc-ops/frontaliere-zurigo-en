Senior Test Engineer (80-100%)

Zurich, Up to 40% HomeOffice possible, Reference 8324

You like working in a dynamic team and a agile environment

and test automation is your strength? Then become Senior Test Engineer at Terravis and actively shape how we develop smart, scalable solutions.

As a leading digital platform, Terravis sets standards in the domain-wide digital processing of property business and connects the financial industry, notarial and land accounting for secure, efficient and legally compliant property processes and property transactions. As part of our Business Engineering & Testing team, you work closely with internal and external stakeholders to further develop and optimize the range of services around Terravis. We look forward to receiving your application documents.

These are your tasks

  • You work closely with development and DevOps and pass on your expertise in the team in the field of development.
  • Conduct manual tests based on internal requirements / customer requirements, documentation and analysis of test results.
  • Creation of meaningful Jira tickets as the basis for bugfixing during development and at the same time as a decision-making aid for release management.
  • Execution and documentation of automated tests.
  • Support for re-factoring of existing test machines and the development of a scalable test automation solution.

You bring this with you

  • Test architecture – You know how to build up repairable and configurable test automation frameworks from practice. You can process and process test data independently via interfaces such as SQL servers or Excel and thus create the basis for reproducible manual and automated testing.
  • Error Analysis and Reporting – Ability to identify and fix bugs in the test run (including logs, screenshots, and reports). Create meaningful tickets for bugs that are found with the functional tests in the test subject.
  • Safe handling of test automations – Safe handling of selenium webdriver and cucumber. You write time-proven test scenarios and implement the corresponding step definitions across browsers (Chrome, Edge) against our web application. We require a well-founded experience in Java and Maven. JavaScript knowledge is an advantage.
  • Frameworks and Tools – Experience with Jetbrains IntelliJ IDE, JUnit, Selenium, Cucbridge, JDBC, jabsimport, smart bear SOAP-UI, GitLab and JIRA.
  • Languages - Very good German and English language skills.
