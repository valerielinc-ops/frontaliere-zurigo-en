To expand our team in the Zurich region, we are looking for someone immediately or by appointment

Spitex specialist responsibility for interRAI-HC clarifications 90% -100%

Your tasks

  • Responsible for the timely and correct planning and implementation of initial assessments and reassessments (needs clarifications) as well as reports of needs
  • Planning of operations (operations plan) for the Spitex teams of the assigned companies
  • Collaboration with health insurance companies -> process rejections/feedbacks
  • Professional and warm advice and support for people in need of care
  • Observation and analysis of nursing and care situations including initiation of necessary corrective measures/measures
  • Checking Spitex services for completeness and correctness for all guests (monthly closing)
  • Coaching of employees in the company to correctly and completely record Spitex services

Your profile

  • Completed nursing training HF/FH/DNII or equivalent training recognized by the SRK
  • At least 2-3 years of professional experience in nursing
  • 1-2 years of professional experience at Spitex with requirements clarification
  • Ability to analyze rejections
  • Knowledge of the correct planning and recording of Spitex services (including billing)
  • Willingness to travel within the canton of Zurich
  • Impeccable knowledge of German, differentiated oral and written expression skills
