Full description

The territorial unit VII is responsible for the operation and maintenance of the national roads. This also includes the higher-level control system, which comprises all safety-relevant installations on around 220 kilometres of motorway and 22 tunnels. 1600 video cameras, a fiber-optic network, a cross-canton IT/OT network, around 450 Windows/Linux servers and much more are maintained and monitored by the IT team. Now we are looking for reinforcement for our IT & communication team. Would you like to be part of our team and work with us to ensure that everything works reliably behind the scenes? Your tasks

  • Monitoring IT/OT systems, capturing and prioritizing incident tickets
  • 1st and 2nd level support, troubleshooting, problem solving and service requests
  • Monitoring, prioritizing and coordinating open tickets in compliance with defined SLAs
  • Configuration adjustments to systems according to checklists and specifications
  • Support in the implementation of the IT/OT security concept Your profile
  • Solid knowledge of network fundamentals, operating systems (Windows/Linux), hardware, peripherals and basic security
  • The willingness to continuously deal with new products, technologies and concepts
  • Team-oriented, reliable and independent working method
