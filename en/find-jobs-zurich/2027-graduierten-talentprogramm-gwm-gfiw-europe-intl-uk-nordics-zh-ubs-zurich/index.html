Global Wealth Management Graduate Talent Program

Interested in working in finance, specifically as a wealth management professional serving clients, but not sure where to start? Do you want to work for a firm with a truly global footprint that consistently ranks among the world's top financial institutions? We're looking for ambitious students to join our 2027 Global Wealth Management Graduate Talent Program.

Responsibilities

  • manage daily business in GFIW client servicing and deliver exceptional client service and experience
  • be the point of entry for processing client orders, e.g. stock orders, foreign exchange, loans and deposits
  • be the direct contact for daily banking activities and administrative work for our clients
  • manage daily client transactions and carry out inquiries and troubleshooting
  • perform account maintenance tasks and client-related processes; e.g. account opening and closing, suitability reviews, initial and recurring KYC and due diligence
  • collaborate with different teams on strategic projects with a goal of improving our client experience and driving engagement
  • identify patterns in client behavior and support the Client Advisors in expanding depth and breadth of the relationship (cross-selling opportunities); think strategically, propose options and support the development of the client book
  • support client advisors in administrative, risk and legal and compliance related tasks and duties
  • build an understanding of our industry, culture, and strategy, while learning about how we operate to effectively meet the needs of our clients
  • develop professionally by establishing a network of mentors and business contacts we believe are some of the best in the business
  • attend educational events led by senior leaders that provide extensive exposure and insight into the business
