Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Interior Prison Imprisonment and Reintegration Prison Pöschwies Springerin / Springer, temporary 80 - 100% Prison Imprisonment and Reintegration (JuWe) is responsible as part of the Directorate of Justice and Interior of the Canton of Zurich for the execution of penalties and measures as well as the execution of the various types of detention. For our 1,400 employees from around 40 professions, the successful social reintegration of people who have committed crimes and the prevention of recidivism are of particular importance. The Pöschwies Prison (JVA) Pöschwies is one of eight main departments of JuWe and, with 399 seats, the largest closed JVA in Switzerland for delinquent men. It fulfils a central state task and, in accordance with the legal mandate, takes into account the rehabilitation of detained persons and the protection of the general public. In the field of economics and work, we are looking for a flexible, resilient and eager-to-learn personality with pronounced social competence and interest in the prison system. The term of office shall be two years. Your tasks

  • Support of our work masters in versatile and varied activities in the work facilities
  • Assuming responsible safety, control and oversight tasks and ensuring the smooth operation of operations
  • Assistance and guidance of detained persons in various workplaces with the aim of promoting their personal and professional skills
  • Provision of work materials and quality controls to ensure careful and timely execution of work
  • Creating a safe and structured environment for detainees, staff and external partners
  • Active teamwork and co-responsibility for safety within the context of emergency management Your profile
