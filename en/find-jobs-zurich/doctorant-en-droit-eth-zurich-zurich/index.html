Doctoral student: in law (m/f/d)

100%, Zurich, temporary

As part of the SNSF-funded project “Responsible AI for the Swiss Judiciary”, we are investigating together with Swiss courts how artificial intelligence can be responsibly integrated into the judiciary.

Project background

The doctoral position is focused on the empirical research of AI-based decision-making aids in justice. The doctoral candidate will examine the impact of such systems on judicial practice by designing and conducting field experiments in cooperation with Swiss courts as well as their legal and institutional evaluation.

Job description

  • Investigation of judicial decision-making processes and legal argumentation in interaction with AI tools
  • Participation in the development of experimental designs and evaluation framework for AI-based decision-making
  • Collaborating with the technical team to explore the interaction between AI systems and legal users
  • Publication of research results in leading journals and presentation at international conferences
  • The position is designed as a cumulative dissertation at the interface of law, computer science and empirical social science.
  • Our offices are located in downtown Zurich
  • The working languages are German and English

Profile

  • Bring an outstanding bachelor's and master's degree in law from a German-speaking university (Germany, Austria or Switzerland)
  • Have a keen interest in the impact of AI on law and society
  • Bring interest in field experiments and experimental research designs
  • Show willingness to learn and apply quantitative and empirical research methods
  • have very good knowledge of English
  • Knowledge of German and French is an advantage
