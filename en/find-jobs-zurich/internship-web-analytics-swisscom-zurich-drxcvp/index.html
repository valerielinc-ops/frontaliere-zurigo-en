Internship Web Analytics

Your future starts here During the university internship in Web Analytics, you will help shape the digital future with us. Passionately and enthusiastically, you will further develop our analytics concepts and ensure the tracking implementation. You will be responsible for establishing and further developing Web Analytics in the business customer area. With your passion for data and insights, you will ensure simple and inspiring customer experiences. As the Experience Architecture domain, we are responsible as a team for the digital experiences of our business customers, partners and employees. To this end, we have bundled all the necessary competencies within our team, from research through experience design and testing to analytics. You can expect a highly motivated and collegial team that learns together and brings inspiring business experiences to customers.

  • Using well-thought-out tracking strategies, you translate complex data and structures into understandable information that adds value for your stakeholders
  • You analyze business needs, translate them into performance indicators and convert these into tracking concepts
  • In close collaboration with development, you implement the tracking concepts and ensure their quality in our system
  • To ensure a high level of tracking quality, you transfer the necessary knowledge to the development teams

Your skills

  • Bachelor's or master's degree (university/UAS/ETH) in computer science, data science or a related field
  • Strong technical understanding, basic knowledge of tag management, HTML and Javascript
  • Knowledge of tracking concept development, implementation and quality assurance is an advantage
  • Knowledge of agile methodologies and structures is an advantage
  • Holistic thinking and action, solution-oriented, committed and assertive
  • Enjoyment of delving into highly complex topics
  • Very good German and good English, other national languages an advantage

With us, you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or from your home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can count on a range of attractive benefits that will enrich your work experience, such as a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting professional development opportunities.
