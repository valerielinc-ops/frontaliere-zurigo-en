Full description

Spital Männedorf – Your very special place of work. Whether a small “thank you” or a morning “Grüezi” – we focus on real appreciation and humanity. The proximity in our house makes everyday life uncomplicated and enables interprofessional cooperation at the highest level. In an environment where support goes without saying, your commitment is appreciated and encouraged.

The nature of your activity

  • Responsibility for the life cycle of selected clinical and administrative applications from introduction to replacement
  • Ensuring stable, safe and efficient application operations
  • Support and further development of interfaces and data flows between the various systems
  • Participation in challenging digitization and IT projects, especially in the introduction and further development of our new clinic information system (KIS), as well as assuming partial or project responsibility
  • Advising and supporting the departments in optimizing processes and digital solutions
  • Planning and coordination of releases, updates and changes
  • Cooperation with internal departments, manufacturers and external partners
  • Implementation of training and monitoring of changes
  • Participation in a regulated, rotating Pikett service within the IT team

The basis of your performance

  • Training in computer science, business informatics or a comparable specialisation
  • Experience in application management
  • Healthcare knowledge is beneficial
  • Knowledge of clinical information systems (CIS), ERP solutions or comparable specialist applications
  • Experience with interfaces and data flows (e.g. HL7, FHIR, DICOM or APIs)
  • Ideally, knowledge of ITIL processes
  • Enjoy working with users from a wide range of disciplines
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Strong communication skills as well as high customer and service orientation

The advantages of your position

  • A versatile and responsible activity with a large creative scope
  • Participation in important digitalization and innovation projects
  • Flexible working hours and modern forms of work

The reasons for your decision

We offer modern working conditions, promote internal training and focus on professional exchange across all disciplines. The quiet location directly on the lake and the proximity to the vibrant city of Zurich round off your workplace. In addition, you can look forward to very special advantages.
