Full description

IMMO plans today for the Zurich of tomorrow: We combine user needs with economic efficiency and environmental compatibility in a sustainable building and portfolio management. In the portfolio administrative and social buildings as well as in the portfolio school buildings we are looking for the two departments as of 1. March 2027 each one*

Tasks

  • You assume responsibility for the assigned objects in terms of usability, functional safety, value preservation, costs and operation as well as the safety of buildings and facilities.
  • The schedule-, life-cycle-, needs- and cost-based monitoring and maintenance you ensure, develop appropriate strategies and record maintenance topics in the walking tours of school, administrative and social buildings in the city of Zurich.
  • During maintenance, repair and building operations, you will gain insights and support smaller projects and tasks.
  • With SAP, CAFM and other internal systems you work in everyday life and contribute to the improvement of our master data.
  • In exchange with the technical service, the management of home service and technology as well as suppliers and service providers, you support the coordination of offers, appointments, reports, orders and orders.

Profile

  • Strategic, conceptual and networked thinking
  • Currently studying in Facility Management
  • Interest in Facility Management, Real Estate and Building Maintenance
  • Open, reliable, communicative and motivated working method
  • Interest in technical, organizational and economic contexts

We offer

You can expect a collegial environment, a motivated team and attractive employment conditions with good social benefits. A structured familiarization is a matter of course for us. The place of work is centrally located, just a few minutes walk from Zurich Central Station.

We are open to applications from people with physical impairments.

About us

On behalf of the City Council, we represent and manage around 1900 properties. In the areas of space and infrastructure, we offer numerous services to the city administration and advise the departments and the city council on strategic issues.

Facts and figures

  • IMMO manages 1900 properties.

  • IMMO manages a floor area of 2.6 million square meters.
  • The insurance value of the IMMO portfolio is around 8 billion francs.
  • IMMO purchases construction services of CHF 300 million and energy for CHF 25 million each year.
  • It equips around 7000 office workplaces, 3000 school rooms and more than 3000 additional rooms.
