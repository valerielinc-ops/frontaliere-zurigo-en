Full description

KAPEL’S is Europe’s leading provider of a unique selection of vintage fashion jewelry from the world’s most luxurious brands. We specialize in exceptional pieces from the 1940s to 2000s and offer exclusive models directly from the catwalk. For our stand in the Globus department store on the magnificent Bahnhofstrasse we are looking for a reliable, motivated and fashion-conscious new team member in the amount of 30-60%, at best from September 2026.

Here you make the difference

  • Welcome discerning customers from home and abroad and ensure that they enjoy a special shopping experience.
  • They present our outstanding range of jewellery and advise our customers on style, color matching and fashion trends.
  • They prepare sales and close them.
  • They build close relationships with our customers and maintain them in the long term.
  • They ensure that the stand is always in the best condition.

That draws you out

  • Strong customer orientation.
  • They are very reliable and trustworthy.
  • They have an excellent eye for detail.
  • They have a great passion for luxury fashion, jewelry and art.
  • They follow the seasonal haute couture collections and trends.
  • They have an excellent feel for Ästhetics and design (colors and textures).
  • They are able to advise customers when putting together their outfits with the appropriate selection of jewelry.
  • They have perfect manners and a well-groomed appearance.
  • They have excellent communication skills in German and English
  • Other languages are of great advantage.
  • Experience in retail and sales is beneficial.

BENEFITS FOR YOU Up to 30% discount in all Globus department stores. All legally required insurance is covered. Participation in specially designed Globus training courses. Your contact If you are interested at this point, we look forward to receiving your application by email to info@kapels.com
