Vetsuisse Faculty / ICT Services

Job opening 01.03.2027 or by arrangement

Would you like to help shape a modern IT infrastructure in an exciting environment of research, teaching and clinical practice? As a Senior ICT System Administrator, you will play a key role in the reliable operation and further development of the Vetsuisse Faculty’s IT services. Together with a dedicated team, you ensure that researchers, lecturers, students and employees can count on efficient and future-oriented IT solutions every day.

Tasks

  • You ensure a stable, secure and sustainable operation of our IT infrastructure and continuously develop it further
  • You support and optimize our server, cloud and workplace environments as well as central infrastructure services such as Active Directory, DNS, DHCP and Group Policies
  • You take over the administration, maintenance and further development of our Windows server landscape including lifecycle, patch and authorization management
  • You work in exciting infrastructure and digitalization projects, contribute your own ideas and evaluate new technologies
  • They advise our institutes and departments competently on IT issues and support them in 1st and 2nd level support also in the area of lecture theatre service.

Profile

You have in-depth training in the computer science sector and have several years of experience in Windows Server and System Administration or in the operation of modern ICT infrastructures. You are familiar with current Microsoft technologies and you are also safe when it comes to topics such as cloud, virtualization or automation. You have experience in supporting Windows and Apple devices as well as IT peripherals. You have very good knowledge and experience in Windows Server Administration, Active Directory, (DNS, DHCP, GPO) and VMware. In addition to your professional expertise, you convince with team spirit, independence, curiosity, initiative and a solution-oriented working method. They enjoy working with different people and understand how to translate technical requirements into practical solutions. German is our colloquial language. In addition, you can also communicate safely in English.
