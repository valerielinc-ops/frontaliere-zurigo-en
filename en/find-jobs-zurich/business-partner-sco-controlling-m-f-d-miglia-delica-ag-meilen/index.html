Full description

Are you a numbers professional and would like to actively shape the financial management of a modern supply chain? With us, you move more than numbers – you drive improvements, support the management and ensure the success of our production plant. We celebrate success together, appreciate your commitment and offer you the freedom to contribute your ideas. Become part of our team!

Luogo di lavoro: Delica AG, Delica AG miles, Bruechstrasse 70, 8706 miles

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You monitor and design the local Supply Chain & Operations (SCO) value flows in SAP.
  • You analyze and are responsible for the correct factory-specific standard manufacturing costs (COGS) and their deviations.
  • Proactively drive COGS improvements at the production plant, e.g. by lean management and scrap optimization.
  • You ensure the planning and forecasting process in the SCO area and actively accompany it, including risk and opportunity management.
  • You create profitability calculations and accompany the investment process at the location.

Requirements

  • Several years of experience: in a comparable function in the manufacturing industry, as well as in-depth knowledge in CO processes (completion, planning, calculation).
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): in business administration/controlling
  • Very good knowledge of the CO processes and concepts (DBR, COGS, etc.) as well as an integrated understanding of the financial value flows in the SCO environment.
  • In-depth SAP knowledge and experience with Microsoft Excel and SAP Analysis for Office (Power BI is beneficial).
  • Analytical skills, a structured and solution-oriented way of working, and strong team and communication skills.
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • English (advantageous)
