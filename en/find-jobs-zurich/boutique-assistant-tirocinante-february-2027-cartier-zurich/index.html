Our 10,000+ colleagues of more than 105 nationalities are united by a shared independent spirit and commitment to excellence, striving to continuously enrich our Maison’s heritage by pushing the boundaries of creativity and innovation.

HAVE YOU EVER WONDERED WHAT IS LIKE TO BE PART OF THE CARTIER FAMILY?

A UNIQUE RETAIL EXPERIENCE IS WAITING FOR YOU IN OUR SWISS BOUTIQUES!

WE ARE LOOKING FOR OUR NEXT SUPER INTERNS!

WE WELCOME

Passionate and enthusiastic students curious about seeing & participation in the day-to-day retail operations and projects of high-end luxury brands in the jewelry, timepiece & accessories industries for 6 months internship starting in February 2027.

YOU WILL BE

Immersed in our organization and Swiss Culture, as well as, involved in a full range of activity at Cartier.

You will join the Zurich Boutique as part of the Operations team and will be responsible for :

  • Assisting the boutique team at large to ensure a seamless and smooth running of the boutique operations
  • Welcoming client in line with Cartier hosting standards to ensure a high quality of client experience
  • Managing client quick services requests (i.e bracelet adjustments, client creations cleaning, etc)
  • Assisting our Sales Advisor in their daily routing including sales ceremonies support, gift wrapping, etc.
  • Contributing maintaining a qualitative boutique organization (i.e daily set up of workstations, management of non sellable goods, delivery dismantling, etc)
  • Contributing to the implementation of compliance processes such as boutique inventories

ABOUT YOU

  • You are currently enrolled in a Bachelor or master’s degree in business, Luxury and able to provide an internship agreement (compulsory)
  • You are available to start in February 2027 (compulsory)
  • You are fluent in English; other Swiss languages would be really appreciated. German a strong plus.
  • You are a team player who is excited to shine, as well as empower and assist others to achieve success
  • You have an excellent presentation and excellent communication skills
  • You are pro-active, autonomous and eager to assist on various tasks all day long
  • You have a strong attention to details
  • You are excited to work in an international environment

WE OFFER

All our interns have a key role in our day-to-day business success. Moreover, the close collaboration with an engaged and dedicated team is there to support you to succeed in this challenging and exciting role. During your time with us, you will get familiar with our know-how and gain practical insights into different business areas. Best of all, you will grow in a stimulating environment with excellent working conditions and attractive benefits!
