Full description

We are looking for an immediate product owner: 80-100%. At ZFV, we are consistently driving forward the digitalization of our core processes. With modern technologies and a clear focus on customer value, we create the basis for more efficient processes, sustainable growth and innovation. Become part of this transformation and thus provide measurable added value for our companies, employees and guests. We look forward to your application! Your area of activity:

  • You take on the professional product responsibility for digital products and processes in the value-added area “Food”, in particular in the areas of planning, purchasing, goods management, production, operation, quality and reporting
  • Together with the specialist departments, you develop a clear product vision and ensure that further developments create a measurable benefit for companies, specialist departments and guests.
  • You work closely with specialist departments, project management, IT, application specialist: internal and external partners and ensure a clear coordination between business, technology and operations
  • You understand processes, data objects and system contexts along the value chain and translate professional needs into clear, implementable requirements
  • You create, maintain and prioritize the product backlog with regard to business impact, feasibility, dependencies and risks
  • You formulate user stories and acceptance criteria comprehensible and verifiable
  • You accompany testing, acceptances and roll-outs from a professional point of view and ensure that new functions are implemented in a practical, user-friendly and high-quality manner.
  • You integrate feedback, bugs and suggestions for improvement from the company into the further development and thus drive the continuous optimization
  • You monitor relevant KPIs and derive measures from them to improve efficiency and quality
  • You make sure that data protection, security, compliance and architecture are taken into account at an early stage and coordinated with the competent authorities.
  • Study of business informatics or a related field
  • Experience in the conception, prioritization and implementation of digital products, process optimizations or transformation projects
  • Experience as a product owner, business analyst, process manager: in or in a comparable interface role between business and IT
  • Experience in working with departments, IT, external partners and various stakeholder groups
  • Experience in requirements engineering, backlog management, testing or roll-out of digital solutions
  • Understanding business processes, data flows, interfaces and system contexts
  • Advantage: Experience in the field of gastronomy, hospitality, food, goods management, supply chain
  • Agile working methods and methods in the product or project environment
  • Requirements engineering, user stories and acceptance criteria
  • Basic understanding of ERP systems, data models, interfaces and digital process chains
  • German very good in word and writing, French is advantageous
  • Strategic and entrepreneurial thinking
  • Strong process understanding with high customer orientation
  • Structured, analytical and solution-oriented working method
  • Strong communication and moderation ability
  • Decision-making and enforcement capacity in cooperation with stakeholders as well as prioritization competence
  • Quality awareness and openness for continuous improvement Our company: The cooperative ZFV-Unternehmenen (ZFV) creates together with partners inspiring meeting places, inspires with hospitality and authentic experiences. Founded in 1894 by visionary Zürcher Bürgersfrauen, the ZFV continues to attach particular importance to innovation and sustainability. In more than 200 companies throughout Switzerland, the employees of the ZFV take care of the needs of customers and guests.
