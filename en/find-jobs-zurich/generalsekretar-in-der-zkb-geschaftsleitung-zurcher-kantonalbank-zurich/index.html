Full description

80% - 100% | Management Support / Staff | Zurich | Experienced professionals As a secretary: in the General Directorate, you take on a central role in the CEO office. You ensure that the meetings of the Directorate-General are efficiently prepared, carried out and post-processed, thus contributing directly to the management of the Bank. With your banking expertise, your structured way of working and your eye for the whole, you ensure that decisions can be made on a solid basis. In a staff team, you work closely with the Chairman of the Directorate General, the Chief of Staff and other internal stakeholders. You move daily in the direct environment of the management of our bank. Your tasks

  • Planning and organising the meetings of the Directorate-General, including annual and rolling planning, calendar of meetings, deadlines and milestones
  • Taxation of the agenda and dossier management for the Directorate-General
  • Review of submitted templates and ensure compliance with standards and organisational regulations
  • Recording, documentation and follow-up of the meetings of the Directorate-General
  • Coordinating the Bank Board-relevant business in cooperation with the General Secretariat Bank Bureau/Bankrat
  • Participation in the further development of committee work in relation to processes, templates, digital tools as well as guidelines, instructions and organizational regulations
  • Support for the Chief of Staff in selected tasks

Your profile

  • Completed commercial training, supplemented by paralegal training or further training in business administration, management assistance or project management
  • Several years of professional experience in a comparable function in the General Secretariat, DG/VR Secretariat or in a staff function
  • In-depth banking expertise and a very good understanding of processes, decision-making processes and governance structures of a bank or regulated company
  • Very good MS Office knowledge (especially PowerPoint, Word, Excel) as well as experience with collaboration tools and document management systems
  • Strong organisational and planning competence as well as a structured, forward-looking and reliable working method
  • High level of care and accuracy, discretion and integrity as well as a service-oriented and pragmatic attitude
  • Stylistic German in word and writing, good English skills are an advantage

Our offer

  • A motivated CEO staff team looking forward to working with you
  • Responsible and varied work in the direct vicinity of the Directorate General with many insights into the management of the bank
  • The opportunity to contribute your ideas and actively shape the further development of committee work
  • Various opportunities to develop personally and professionally
  • Central and modern workplace with good transport connections
  • Comprehensive package with attractive basic salary, variable compensation and versatile benefits
