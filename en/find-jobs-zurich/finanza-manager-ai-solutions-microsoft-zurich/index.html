Role overview

Overview Empower every person and organization on the planet to achieve more. That's what inspires us, drives our work, and pushes us to challenge the status quo every day.

We are looking for a Finance Manager to help accelerate growth across Microsoft's AI solutions across Switzerland Enterprise business, with a particular focus on Copilot, Cowork, and Azure AI workloads.

This is a commercial role operating at the intersection of Finance, Sales, and Operations. You will translate strategic priorities into execution by identifying growth opportunities, understanding customer adoption and business performance, and partnering closely with business leaders to drive results. Success in this role comes from connecting customer needs, sales execution, and business performance. As a trusted advisor, you will turn insights into actions that help teams prioritize opportunities, accelerate customer value realization, and drive revenue growth.

The ideal candidate is curious, proactive, and collaborative. They enjoy understanding how customers adopt and expand technology solutions, uncovering opportunities through data and business insight, and working across teams to drive impact.

  • Partner with Sales, Operations, Finance, and Regional teams to accelerate growth across Microsoft's AI Solutions portfolio, with a focus on Copilot and AI-powered solutions.
  • Develop insights into customer adoption, deployment, expansion, and revenue performance, owning the end-to-end analysis and recommendations that drive business outcomes.
  • Communicate complex insights through clear storytelling that helps leaders prioritize actions, align stakeholders, and mobilize teams.
  • Act as a trusted advisor by combining analytical rigor, technical/product knowledge, commercial acumen, and sound business judgment.
  • Leverage data, AI, and emerging technologies to simplify processes, improve decision-making, and increase impact.

Curious, proactive, and eager to learn. A self-starter who identifies opportunities and drives action. Skilled at connecting data, identifying patterns, and communicating insights through clear storytelling. A team player who enjoys bringing people together, sharing ideas, and helping others succeed. Effective at building trusted partnerships and influencing without direct authority. Passionate about technology, AI, and helping customers achieve business outcomes
