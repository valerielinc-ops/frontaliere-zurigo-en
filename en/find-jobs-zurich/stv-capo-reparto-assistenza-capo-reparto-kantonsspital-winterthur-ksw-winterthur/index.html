Full description

For the Clinic of Nephrology, which employs 43 employees, we are looking for 1. February 2027 or by agreement a competent, reliable and committed personality as deputy head of department care. Nephrology includes a nephrological ambulatorium with about 4500 consultations per year, an infusion room with 400-450 treatments / year, the dialysis station with about 11,000 hemodialysis per year, renal replacement procedures at the Center for Intensive Care Medicine, plasma exchange treatments and the care of patients of peritoneal dialysis and home hemodialysis. We offer you an exciting, varied and demanding job in a modern department. In addition, you lead a motivated nursing team and are part of the interdisciplinary leadership team. We look forward to seeing you.

Tasks

  • Together with the head of department, you take over the professional, personnel and organizational leadership of the nursing team of the Clinic for Nephrology and are jointly responsible for the treatment quality and further development of professional and patient-oriented care.
  • You are jointly responsible for compliance with the establishment plan and the recruitment of new employees
  • Interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation and leadership in organizing and structuring relevant processes are an exciting challenge for you.
  • As deputy head of the department, you are particularly responsible for the further development of the growing nephrological ambulatory and the infusion room.

Profile

  • You are a qualified HF nurse who ideally already has professional experience in a larger ambulatory or in the field of nephrology and dialysis
  • You have already successfully demonstrated your leadership skills in a comparable function or are ready to undertake an appropriate training
  • You are enthusiastically involved in the further development of the Clinic for Nephrology
  • You practice your profession with great motivation and joy, enjoy working in a team, think and act networked and service-oriented
  • In interdisciplinary questions and their implementation, you take your leadership role constructively

Benefits: Work and private life should fit together. Therefore, the KSW offers you – where the company makes it possible – flexible and smooth working hours, home office, generous maternity leave, paternity leave and employment levels that can be adapted to different phases of life. Develop yourself professionally and personally: Every year, around 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses are open to you at KSW. We also support external training – temporally and/or financially.

  • Sometimes it needs support. In the event of personal stress, prolonged illness or difficult experiences in your professional or private life, various confidential discussions are available at KSW.
  • At KSW we work together and for each other. Appreciation, open and respectful communication and cooperation across professional groups and departments shape our everyday work. You should be able to contribute with your ideas and strengths and develop.

Your future is also ensured: You benefit from very good social benefits and a pension fund solution with BVK, which is significantly above the legal minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, you are very well covered as a KSW employee.

  • Your health is important to us. Benefit from discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training and courses as well as discounts in pharmacies and selected health insurance companies.
  • Family and work can be well connected with us. The daycare center La Luna with spacious opening hours is directly next to the KSW. In addition, the KSW supports you – depending on income – financially with the crib costs.

There is also something to discover outside of work: Thanks to our cooperation with cultural partners in Winterthur and the surrounding area, you regularly benefit from discounts for concerts and theatre performances.

  • Pensum: 80-100 %
