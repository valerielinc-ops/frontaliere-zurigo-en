Full description

Are you ready to shape the future of Enterprise Service Management at a global scale? Do you thrive at the intersection of technology strategy, product leadership, and meaningful business impact? If so, this could be the role you've been waiting for.

At Swiss Re, we're on a mission to make the world more resilient - and our digital platforms are at the heart of how we do it. We're looking for a driven and experienced Senior Product Lead to take ownership of one of our most strategic digital platforms and lead it to new heights.

About the Role

As Senior Product Lead for Enterprise Service Management, you will define and drive the strategic direction of the Swiss Re Digital Business Management (DBM) Platform - a critical ServiceNow-based platform that powers service management, workflow automation, and digital experiences across the organisation.

You'll be the connective tissue between business stakeholders, engineering teams, architects, and product leaders - translating vision into value, and strategy into scalable, secure, and business-focused solutions. This is a role for someone who combines sharp strategic thinking with hands-on product leadership and a genuine passion for technology innovation.

Key Responsibilities

Product Strategy and Vision

  • Define and communicate the long-term product vision, roadmap, and strategic objectives for the DBM Platform
  • Align platform priorities with business objectives and stakeholder needs to ensure maximum relevance and impact
  • Drive product decisions grounded in business value, measurable outcomes, and continuous customer feedback
  • Champion knowledge sharing and cross-team collaboration to foster a culture of transparency and innovation

Platform Governance and Architecture

  • Ensure alignment with enterprise architecture principles, platform standards, and engineering guardrails
  • Collaborate with architecture and engineering teams to guide solution evolution and responsible technology adoption
  • Support sustainable platform growth while upholding compliance, scalability, and operational excellence

Product Delivery and Value Realization

  • Own and prioritise the product backlog to consistently maximise business value and customer outcomes
  • Partner with stakeholders to define compelling business cases, clear requirements, and meaningful success measures
  • Manage dependencies, priorities, and competing demands across multiple concurrent initiatives
  • Ensure successful delivery of features, enhancements, and platform capabilities on time and to quality

Operational Excellence

  • Oversee platform health, service quality, and operational performance across the board
  • Support incident management and service restoration activities when required
  • Ensure releases meet quality standards and organisational governance requirements
  • Drive continuous improvement through performance monitoring, retrospectives, and customer feedback loops

Financial Management

  • Contribute to annual planning and quarterly review processes with clear financial insight
  • Manage product investments and budgets in close collaboration with stakeholders and leadership
  • Ensure transparency of costs, value delivery, and prioritisation decisions
  • Drive effective resource allocation to maximise business outcomes and return on investment

People and Leadership

  • Foster a collaborative, inclusive, and high-performing team culture where everyone can do their best work
  • Support talent acquisition, onboarding, and capability development to build a strong, future-ready team
  • Encourage continuous learning, innovation, and professional growth at every level
  • Collaborate with Engineering Leads and Platform Leadership to build sustainable organisational capabilities

About the Team

The ESM team is a dynamic, globally connected group that designs, develops, and manages cutting-edge service management, workflow automation, and digital experience solutions across Swiss Re.

Our mission is to enable efficient, scalable, and integrated services through a modern Enterprise Service Management platform. We strike the right balance between innovation and governance - ensuring sustainable growth while maintaining platform consistency, quality, and compliance.

Operating in a scaled agile environment, we collaborate closely across engineering, architecture, operations, and business teams to continuously enhance the value we deliver to users and stakeholders worldwide. We're a team that values curiosity, inclusion, and a shared commitment to excellence.

About You

You are a strategic thinker and a natural collaborator - someone who can inspire confidence in stakeholders, energise a team, and navigate complexity with clarity and purpose. You bring a balance of technical understanding and business acumen, and you know how to turn ambiguity into action. You communicate with impact, lead with empathy, and are committed to building inclusive, high-performing environments where great work thrives.

We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements

  • Extensive experience in ServiceNow delivery, product ownership, or product management within complex enterprise environments
  • Strong understanding of Enterprise Service Management principles and platform governance, with the ability to apply them pragmatically
  • Proven ability to define product strategies and translate them into clear, executable roadmaps that deliver measurable business outcomes
  • Demonstrated experience managing complex stakeholder landscapes, balancing competing priorities with confidence and diplomacy
  • Experience working in Agile or Scaled Agile environments, with a track record of delivering value iteratively and sustainably

These are additional nice to haves

  • ServiceNow certifications and advanced platform expertise across multiple modules
  • Experience leading platform products within a large, global organisation with distributed teams
  • Background in enterprise architecture, platform engineering, or digital transformation initiatives at scale
  • Experience managing platform financials, business cases, and investment planning processes
  • Familiarity with automation, workflow optimisation, and AI-enabled service management capabilities and emerging trends
