Application process

  • In the legal auditorate, you will get insight into the sanctions execution from day one and the opportunity to apply your knowledge acquired during your studies quickly and independently in practice.
  • After training, you will conduct your own cases in the area of fines and fines in GA and examine applications for penalty in the special enforcement form.
  • For this purpose, you carry out aptitude assessments with convicted persons in personal conversations, then decide You about admission to community service
  • You independently make decisions relevant to enforcement, such as on the Gutheissung, conditions or the termination of the alternative penal system and independently create appropriate written documents and orders
  • You coordinate, plan, control and support the implementation of community service, arrange suitable GA operations and conduct site and reminder discussions
  • You work in the execution of penalties in the execution form EM and participate, for example, in conversations and installations of the EM devices
  • You work with internal and external partners in an interdisciplinary environment
  • You conduct research and legal analyses regarding sanctions in various legal fields and data protection issues
