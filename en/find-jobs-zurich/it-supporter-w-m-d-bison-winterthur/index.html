Full description

As a partner in agriculture, BISON ensures that Swiss food reaches consumers. It is important that IT can be optimally adapted to the needs of the specialist areas.This is exactly where you come into play as an ICT supporter! Thanks to your daily work in Winterthur, everything is coordinated with the business and optimally aligned for the future. As an ICT supporter, together with our team, you ensure that 2nd-level support runs smoothly in the Microsoft environment.You have the "can do" mentality and enjoy working in a team? You are open and communicate comprehensible and give feedback?We look forward to your application and get to know you personally in a timely manner.

You do that.

  • Installation and maintenance of IT workstation systems (e.g. PCs, laptops), peripherals, network components and communications
  • Analysis and resolution of HW and SW problems – remotely, on the phone or directly on site
  • Support 3rd-level support and external partners in solving tricky cases, especially in client and printer areas
  • Collaboration in exciting projects around clients, printers and networks as well as independent implementation of smaller technical projects
  • Support for a wide range of client applications – both remotely and on-site
  • In addition to the main tasks, you will be involved in the training of our learners as a practical trainer.

You bring that to me.

  • Completed vocational training as computer scientist, ICT specialist or equivalent professional experience
  • Experience with Windows domains (AD, DNS, DHCP, file and print servers)
  • In-depth knowledge of Windows 11, M365 and networking
  • Knowledge of printer systems
  • Experience in training IT learners is beneficial
  • High quality and responsibility, reliability, flexibility, fast comprehension and strong communication skills
  • Helpful team player with high customer and service orientation as well as a structured, independent and solution-oriented working method
  • Negotiation-proof knowledge of German, good knowledge of English for IT specialist literature
  • Category B driving licence

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
