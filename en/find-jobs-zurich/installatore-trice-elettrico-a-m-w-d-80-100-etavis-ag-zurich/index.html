Description

In the Technopark, our Business Units are specialized in serving demanding customer segments, including universities, banks, hospitals, and other public infrastructures. We provide all services related to consulting, project planning, execution, support, and maintenance for electrical and building technology as well as building automation. In addition, we implement solutions in the field of renewable energies, particularly solar and photovoltaic technology or E-Mobility. We do this with a focus on employees and customers, using the latest technical developments.

This is who we are: ETAVIS

ETAVIS is the optimal partner for innovative electrical engineering, communication, and automation solutions in Switzerland and Liechtenstein. We offer tailored, high-quality solutions for business customers of all industries and sizes, as well as individually customized services for private customers. As part of the VINCI Group, we benefit from global innovative strength and are simultaneously locally anchored with our approximately 60 branches. As the largest small company, we operate in a more human and familiar way – here, every single person counts. With over 2,400 employees and several hundred apprentices, we work every day towards a more sustainable, better-connected world. Become part of the ETAVIS team and make a big impact – in the top league of electrical engineering!
