Full description

Renovations and conversions to electrical systems are among your main tasks. The company offers you varied customer orders in a modern working environment. You benefit from further education opportunities in a committed team. For our client we are looking for an activity

  • implement refurbishments on low- and high-voltage systems
  • carry out conversions of electrical systems professionally
  • realize new installations in low- and high-voltage systems
  • competently accompany private customers and SMEs
  • take over maintenance work on telephone, computer and high-voltage systems
  • Requirements
  • Training degree as an electrical installer EFZ

  • Experience in the service area would be desirable
  • Category B driving licence is available
  • Good user knowledge of the PC offer
