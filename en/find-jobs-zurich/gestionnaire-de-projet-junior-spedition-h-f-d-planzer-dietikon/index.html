Full description

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, get involved and grow with us. Become part of our family and shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us.

What you move

  • You participate in forwarding projects and take over the deputy of the project managers if necessary
  • Presentations, solution concepts and project documentation are created independently
  • You optimize, implement and control processes in international transport and customs
  • As a contact person for forwarding processes with a focus on land transport, you also monitor and coordinate the requirements and project planning tool
  • You work closely with internal and external stakeholders and act as an interface between the operational area, software supplier and internal IT.
  • You ensure compliance with professional, technical and legal requirements

Why do you succeed?

  • You have successfully completed a commercial education and have experience in international forwarding as well as import and export
  • Good IT knowledge and interest in forwarding and customs systems distinguish you
  • You are also well versed in dealing with MS Office
  • Very good knowledge of German and French you bring
  • Italian and other languages are beneficial
  • As an initiative personality, you convince with a high willingness to perform, self-initiative and a strong quality awareness
  • Methodical, solution-oriented and pragmatic thinking as well as an independent, careful and goal-oriented working method are among your strengths
  • Teamwork, responsibility, flexibility and resilience round off your profile. You are also ready to travel within the Planzer Group

Your benefits

  • Further education and development opportunities: With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • Family culture: You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Own staff restaurant: Enjoy delicious menus at fair prices

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
