80 - 100% Winterthur / Smart Working

Shape your future – together with us!

As a customer operations manager, you and your teams provide excellent service to our customers and distribution partners in all language regions of Switzerland. In doing so, you cover the span from the new addition to the contract management to the performance service. Come on board and actively shape our successful and rapidly growing business area Individual Life!

In the area of customer operations, we design the individual experiences of our customers in the event of damage and performance. They are our focus and together with them we find the right solution. In this way, our customer-first mentality ensures maximum customer satisfaction.

This is what awaits you

  • You lead the operational department Customer Operations with 50 employees in 4 teams for all language regions in Switzerland.
  • You ensure a high-quality service to customers and distribution partners.
  • You make sure that the regulatory and legal provisions are adhered to in day-to-day business.
  • You ensure the application of the Chief Underwriting Officer’s Underwriting Guideline.
  • You ensure the professional standards in the area of performance testing, check them regularly and are responsible for the performance savings individual lives.
  • You ensure efficient and resilient operation and actively use all OPEX instruments and involve the corresponding transversal teams.
  • As a business owner, you set requirements for process developments and ensure the realization of efficiency and effectiveness goals.
  • You drive the target image of the department further forward in synchronization with the higher-level target images and the corporate strategy
  • You act as a leader and change manager and continue to lead the department with the leadership team through transformation on a factual and cultural level; you enable the further development of employees into an increasingly digital and dynamic future
  • With your holistic thinking and your sovereignty in dealing with various internal and external stakeholders, you maintain a sustainable network and interface management.

You bring that with you

  • Seniority & Leadership: Several years of leadership experience, ideally in the leadership of executives & team leaders
  • Maker/in & implementer: hands-on, pragmatic, decision-making, solution-oriented and consistent in the implementation
  • Perseverance: High resilience and persistence; remains on the ball even with complex topics and resistances
  • Industry competence: In-depth know-how in individual life and understanding of the relevant regulatory framework
  • Operations Excellence: Several years of experience in controlling operational units as well as in quality, performance, cost, KPIs and resource management
  • Transformation: Experience in process optimization, lean, digitalization and change management
  • High IT affinity
  • Leadership & Culture: Clear, binding and appreciative; promotes personal responsibility, performance and further development
  • Stakeholder Management: Sovereign at interfaces, especially in the departments of provision and distribution, and able to combine different interests and enforce decisions.
  • Languages: German and French very good to negotiable, English good to very good.
