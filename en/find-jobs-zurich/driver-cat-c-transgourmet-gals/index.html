Full description

Our heart beats for the Swiss gastronomic sector. We are constantly committed to our clients. In our 31 self-service markets, through external service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a varied assortment, attractive own brands, specialized and professional advice and innovative services. Transgourmet Suisse SA is part of the international group Transgourmet. The company is a leader in the delivery and self-service business in Switzerland and has around 2'100 employees, as well as 100'000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals, as well as retail).

Mission

  • Establish daily tour using the tour schedule
  • Treat the same day tours and tour closures using the freight preparation system for transport
  • Load the vehicle in accordance with temperature guidelines, including loading securing
  • Verify the accuracy and completeness of customer orders using delivery slips
  • Unload delivery to customer correctly
  • Resumption of recorded packaging and possible returns of goods
  • Recognizing, seizing and conveying the needs of clients to the transportation agent

Qualifications and qualifications

  • Driver's licence cat. C
  • Several years of experience as a heater/heater
  • Good geography
  • Good physical constitution
  • Good oral and written expression in French
  • Personal initiative, reliability and strong commitment
  • Way of working structured and oriented towards objectives and client
