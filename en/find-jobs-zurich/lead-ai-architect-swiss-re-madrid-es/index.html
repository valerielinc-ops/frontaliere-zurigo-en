About Us At Swiss Re, we build innovative, cloud‑native solutions that help Life & Health insurance companies around the world operate smarter, faster, and more securely. Our internal software house delivers modern SaaS and PaaS products used globally — from mature enterprise platforms like Magnum to new and rapidly scaling solutions. About the Role We are looking for a AI Architect to lead architecture across our Data Analytics and AI/GenAI offerings. You will design and shape a cloud-native, enterprise-grade SaaS environment built on Azure, using technologies such as Azure Data Factory, Databricks, Synapse, modern data lakehouse architecture , and supporting AI components powered by LLMs, RAG, OCR, NLP , and more. This is a hands-on leadership role where you will combine high-level architecture with real implementation, experimentation, and guidance for engineering teams. You will be part of our technical leadership group, influencing product direction, platform evolution, and long-term technology strategy. What You Will Do Architecture & Design

Technical Leadership

  • Guide engineering teams through best practices, design decisions, and innovation

  • Mentor engineers and drive strong engineering standards

Hands-on Engineering

Strategic Impact

  • Shape the long-term architecture vision for Data & AI platforms

About You

We don’t expect perfection — but we’re looking for someone who brings strong architectural thinking, a hands-on mindset, and the curiosity to push technical boundaries.

You have strong experience with

  • Cloud Architecture: Enterprise-grade Azure solutions (AKS, networking, CDN, WAF, etc.)
  • SaaS Platforms: Multi-tenant, high-availability systems
  • System Design: Java/Spring MVC and distributed systems
  • Security: Zero Trust principles, authentication & network security
  • Integrations: REST, SOAP, event-driven patterns
  • DevSecOps: CI/CD, IaC, cloud automation, resilient infrastructure
  • Architecture Modelling: Technical specs, design documentation
  • Team Leadership: Coaching multicultural engineering teams

Bonus points for
