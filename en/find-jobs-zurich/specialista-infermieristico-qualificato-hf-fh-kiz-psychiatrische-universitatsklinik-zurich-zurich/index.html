Full description

  • The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich treats and cares for mentally ill people of all ages. The more than 2,500 employees from 55 different professional profiles work in interdisciplinary teams to contribute to the holistic care of patients, from assessment to therapy to aftercare. Our work is based on mutual trust and personal responsibility and together we promote an innovative corporate culture. As a university clinic, we are successfully involved in national and international research projects. As a leading clinic for adult psychiatry and psychotherapy in Switzerland, we not only take on the care mandate of the Canton of Zurich, but also act as a university institution with a research and teaching mandate from the University of Zurich. At 8 locations, including five clinical facilities and a research center, over 1,000 committed employees create a diverse and dynamic working environment. Here you have the opportunity to get actively involved in an important institution. The Center for Social Psychiatry (chief physician PD. Dr. med. Stefan Vetter) offers extensive outpatient and part-time inpatient as well as short-term inpatient services (crisis intervention). Innovative therapy concepts are an integral part of our clinical offering. For inpatient crisis intervention (KIZ), we are looking for a qualified nurse HF/FH, KIZ 80%, immediately or by appointment. Your tasks
  • Crisis intervention in the inpatient setting (admission, follow-up and exit interviews)
  • Short consultations, crisis intervention and triage on the telephone
  • Independent management of group activities (mindfulness, stress management and sleep hygiene)
  • Administrative tasks (management of patient documentation), operational tasks (hotel business, catering, orders)
  • Instruction of support staff
  • Case management under medical supervision
  • Responsibility for one's own treatment path
  • Completed, recognized professional training in the field of nursing HF/FH (proof of recognition from the Swiss Red Cross for foreign diplomas)
  • Several years of professional experience in a psychiatric facility is a prerequisite
  • Additional training in conversation management and/or relaxation techniques is an advantage
  • Enjoyment of acting independently and largely on one's own responsibility as well as in interdisciplinary work in a team
  • An exciting one Field of activity with responsible and varied tasks
  • Room for maneuver to contribute innovative ideas and actively shape processes
  • Motivated, competent and collegial team
  • Personal and professional development opportunities
  • Conditions of employment in accordance with the cantonal personnel law
  • Attractive social benefits (payment of 60% of pension fund contributions)
  • Discounted shopping opportunities and sports activities, participation in the ZVV BonusPass/general subscription, staff restaurant with subsidized prices or lunch Checks (depending on location), company-owned crèche and much more.
