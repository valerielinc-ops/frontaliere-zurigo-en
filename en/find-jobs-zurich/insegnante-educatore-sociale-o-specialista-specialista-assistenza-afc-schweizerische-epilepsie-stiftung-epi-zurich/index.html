The Multifaceted Day

  • In the highly structured studios of the day care centers, you accompany and support adults with physical and cognitive impairments as well as highly challenging behaviors in pursuing meaningful and comprehensible activities and enable self-acting actions as well as the further development of skills and abilities.
  • They carry out various individual and group activities and thus ensure a varied and meaningful everyday life design

  • Organizational talent, a structured way of working and humor round off your talents
