Full description

At PostFinance, our customers are the focus. As a customer consultant, you recognize their needs and accompany them with competent advice at eye level – whether digitally, on the phone or on site. Together with your team, you develop solutions that are simple and relevant and thus strengthen the customer relationship in a sustainable manner. With your commitment, you contribute to shaping the future of consulting at PostFinance personally and effectively.

You can do that.

  • You take responsibility for your own customer portfolio and conduct holistic consultations on creation, provision and financing
  • You plan your day independently, actively arrange customer appointments and shape your everyday life with high personal responsibility
  • You contribute your ideas and achieve ambitious goals together with the team
  • You will develop your knowledge in a targeted manner, especially in the areas of investing, provisioning and financing, and actively share your know-how in the team
  • You optionally take over the deputy of the branch management

You bring that with you

  • Completed basic training (EFZ) in the commercial sector or in sales as well as further training in banking and/or in pension schemes
  • Experience in investment and pension consulting as well as enthusiasm for financial topics
  • Open and empathetic approach with focus on building trust and customer centering
  • Strong skills in customer acquisition and lead processing
  • Collegial and helpful personality with pleasure in cooperation and joint success.
  • Very good German and English skills as well as additional language skills as a plus
