Who we are

Novelis is driven by its purpose of Shaping a Sustainable World Together.

As a global leader in innovative products and services and the world's largest recycler of aluminum, we partner with customers in the automotive, beverage can, aerospace and specialties industries to deliver solutions that improve the benefits of balanced lightweight aluminum.

We have a coordinated network of technically sophisticated rolling and recycling plants as well as foundries across 29 facilities in 9 countries throughout North America, South America, Europe, and Asia with 12,750 employees worldwide. The company is headquartered in Atlanta, USA.

Our customers include some of the largest and best-known brands in the world, including Coca-Cola, BMW, Jaguar Land Rover, Airbus, Boeing, Nespresso, Samsung, and others. In addition, Novelis is leading the industry with its sustainability and recycling efforts.

Become part of our mission to create a sustainable world and start in our plants in Göttingen, Nachterstedt, Ohle, Koblenz or in our European Headquarter in Küsnacht , Switzerland as:

IT Solutions Lead (m/f/d)

Verantwortlichkeiten & Qualifikationen

What awaits you

Demand Execution & Solution Delivery

  • Serve as the regional or capability-level point of contact for incoming business demand, capturing requests and performing initial triage before escalating to the Solutions Partner for prioritization decisions
  • Manage demand intake, requirements gathering, solution design, estimation, and delivery execution using standardized processes and tools, ensuring alignment with business priorities, architecture standards, and cost objectives
  • Oversee implementation through support transition, while monitoring solution performance, user experience, and service levels to ensure sustained business value and continuous improvement

Local/Regional Application Stewardship

  • Act as the business-side application steward for assigned small/regional applications, ensuring business continuity, stakeholder satisfaction, and alignment with global strategy
  • Develop and maintain an application roadmap and identify and recommend application rationalization opportunities

Strategy Support & Governance Contribution

  • Contribute to technology roadmaps for assigned capability/functional areas by partnering with business capability leaders
  • Monitor emerging technology trends and recommend innovations to the Solutions Partner to improve business outcomes and operational efficiency

Cross-Functional Collaboration & Communication

  • Foster strong partnerships with internal stakeholders, cross-functional teams, and regional IT colleagues to ensure seamless service delivery and operational alignment
  • Support change management and communication efforts to drive adoption of new solutions

How you convince us

  • Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Business Administration, or related field
  • 7+ years of experience in IT business partnering, project management, or enterprise solutions delivery
  • Knowledge of IT governance, demand management, and service delivery frameworks (e.g., ITIL, Agile) and tools (e.g., Accolade, ServiceNow)
  • Excellent communication, relationship-building, and problem-solving skills
  • Proficiency in German and English
  • Proven ability to translate business needs into technology solutions, including requirements gathering, feasibility analysis, and business case development
  • Demonstrated ability to deliver scalable, secure solutions that achieve business objectives and measurable outcomes
  • Experience facilitating workshops and leading solution design sessions with business and technical stakeholders
  • Proven ability to manage application health, performance, and user experience to deliver measurable business value
