Full description

The public Spitex Wald Fischenthal is responsible for the ambulatory health care of the inhabitants of Wald and Fischenthal. Spitex employees care for their clients competently and empathetically in their own home, look after them psychiatrically or support them in everyday life. Spitex Wald Fischenthal is part of the charitable foundation Drei Tannen, which together with Spitex runs the newly built Rosenthal Care Centre and offers old-age apartments with needs-based service. Specialists* Health EFZ Somatic

Your tasks

  • Individual care & support: You accompany our clients with heart and expertise in their everyday life.
  • Primary care and care: You take responsibility and contribute to a high quality of care.
  • Medical technology measures: You use your expertise specifically to ensure the best possible care.
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: You work closely with colleagues within the Foundation Drei Tannen to ensure holistic support.
