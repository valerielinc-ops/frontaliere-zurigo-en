PMO-Lead / Head of Project Management Office (m/f/d) 80%-100%, Zurich, temporary Printed Printing ETH Zurich is launching the Living Lab HIL, a pioneering research project directly related to building practice. The HIL building on Campus Hönggerberg becomes a real laboratory. The building in need of renovation will be rebuilt and expanded. In the coming years, professorships in the Department of Architecture (D-ARCH) and Construction, Environment and Geomatics (D-BAUG) will research and plan the renovation of the HIL building in the sense of circular construction. The aim is to further develop sustainable design and construction in the existing area and to apply it as an example to a real planning and construction project. For the establishment, structuring and operational management of our Living Lab HIL Project Management Office (PMO), we are looking for an organizationally strong and assertive personality as a PMO lead who works closely with the management. Together with a management assistant to be recruited as well as administrative support in the areas of accounting and human resources, you form the administrative and organizational heart of the overall project. The position offers space for different focus areas in operations management and resource management, as well as stakeholder management in a demanding setting. Job description Taking on this project-related role They focus on the following tasks and responsibilities:

  • Construction & Operations Management: Responsibility for the operational management of the Living Lab HIL project office and the establishment of efficient, transparent PMO processes.
  • Planning: Overarching schedule, milestone and resource planning for the interdisciplinary overall project in close coordination with the planning team, internal ETH participants and external project partners.
  • Stakeholder Management: Central hub for the flow of information and communication with internal and external stakeholders (professorships, ETH Real Estate, external implementation partners e.g. Gesamtleister, specialist planner).
  • Resource Allocation & Controlling: Strategic allocation of human and financial resources as well as ongoing budget controlling in cooperation with the managing director and accounting.
  • Contract & Document Management: Structuring, auditing and reliable management of contracts, agreements and central project documents.
  • Publications and reporting: coordination and cooperation in the preparation of reports and presentations together with the internal communications office.
  • Session management: organisation, preparation and follow-up of meetings, workshops and committees, including protocol and pendences in German and English.
  • Team leadership: leadership of the PMO team in coordination with management.

  • Education: Completed university studies (ETH/FH or university) in architecture, civil engineering, project management, business administration or a comparable discipline.
  • Professional experience: Several years of experience in operational management, PMO management or the higher-level management of complex large-scale projects in planning and construction or in the academic environment.
  • Methodological competence: Strong skills in structuring management processes, organization, resource planning and project controlling.
  • Administration & Law: Secure presence in contracting, deep understanding of administrative and operational processes.
