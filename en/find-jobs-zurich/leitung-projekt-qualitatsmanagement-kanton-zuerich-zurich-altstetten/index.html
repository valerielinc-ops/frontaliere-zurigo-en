Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Implementation of justice and reintegration Medical Service Leadership support of the head of department 60 - 80% With around 1400 employees, JuWe is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as for carrying out various types of detention. Our employees from around 40 professions support and promote the reintegration of delinquents and work effectively and progressively for a safe and just society. The Medical Service is one of eight main departments of JuWe and as a central organization responsible for the medical-somatic basic care of all detained persons in the institutions of the Canton of Zurich. With around 90 employees, he ensures medical care from initial examination and treatment to emergency care and accompanies the detained people in everyday life with health issues.
