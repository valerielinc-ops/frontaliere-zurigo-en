Original description

You lead and coach your teams through a strong growth phase, optimize processes and keep our logistics world running – from goods receipt to high-bay robots. If you feel like taking on leadership, mastering processes and driving innovation, you are at the right place with us.

What will you do

  • Lead, motivate, develop: You will strengthen and develop your team leaders and employees (total about 80 employees) in a targeted manner, promote leadership skills and accompany the organization through a dynamic growth phase.
  • Mastering personnel and assignment planning: Optimal shift planning (3-shift operation), emergency management and resource management are in your hands.
  • Performance, quality and costs under control: You are responsible for goods receipt, storage, picking, disposal and shipping – including budgets, inventory and inventory management.
  • Technology & plant operation: You ensure the availability of all plants (including highly automated high-bay and small parts warehouse and external external warehouse).
  • Optimize logistics processes: Quality and efficiency – You develop processes further, procure capital goods and ensure their sustainable maintenance and continuous improvement in the sense of the CIP.
  • HSSE & compliance living: occupational safety, health, environment, legal requirements, ISO standards and internal guidelines You keep everything and develop it further.
  • Reporting & Control: Statistics, KPI and BSC reports serve as a basis for you to continuously monitor and improve logistics performance.
  • Quality & Projects: Support for quality cases, process communications and active participation in relevant projects.

Who you are

  • Completed technical or logistical vocational training (e.g. logistician/EFZ) plus higher specialist training in logistics
  • Many years of management experience with responsibility for large teams (at least 80 people) as well as the ability to accompany and develop teams and managers in a dynamic growth field
  • Mind. 10 years of professional experience in international industrial, commercial or logistics companies in the field of operations with focus on logistics
  • In-depth experience in the successful implementation of operational excellence initiatives, process optimization, change management and cross-divisional transformation projects
  • Very good IT and SAP skills (SD, MM, PP, EWM) and secure communication in German (minimum C1) and English (minimum B2)
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, decision-making pleasure and high independence
  • Team player with assertiveness: strong communication skills, conflict competence, reliability and structured working methods
  • Practical prerequisites: electrode stacker testing and readiness for shift work

Who we are

At Belimo, we are proud to make our company a rewarding place to work. Our leaders are value-driven and promote new ideas in their teams around the world. Through our passion, our employees not only create amazing products, but also revolutionize the HVAC industry. Investing in our employees is at the heart of Belimo when it comes to engaging diverse creative talents to build an industry-leading company. The success of our corporate brand is based on the competence and commitment of each individual.
