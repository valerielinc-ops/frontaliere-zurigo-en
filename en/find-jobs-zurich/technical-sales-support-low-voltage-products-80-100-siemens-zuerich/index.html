Together with our customers, we connect the real and the digital world. As one of the leading technology companies, Siemens employs around 6,000 people in Switzerland. With our groundbreaking solutions in the areas of building technology, energy, mobility, health and industry, we are shaping the future. As Technical Sales Support, you are the technical interface between our sales team and our customers. You support the entire sales process with your technical expertise and ensure the optimal integration of our low-voltage products for industrial and infrastructure applications. Do you also want to actively participate in the pulse of time and make the lives of millions of people in Switzerland better? Then you are exactly right with us! Apply today as a low-voltage technical sales support product and start immediately or after your availability – your team is looking forward to you! Your new tasks

  • Professional advice and technical support for customers and internal stakeholders for the entire range of our low-voltage products
  • Technical support of the offer process in all phases as an integral part
  • Analysis of technical customer configuration for optimal product and solution integration
  • Verification of technical drawings and installation schemes
  • Close cooperation with product management and sales team Your talents and experiences
  • Completed technical training (electrician, automatician) and/or technical HF/FH studies (electrical engineering, energy engineering, mechanical engineering)
  • Good knowledge of low-voltage and switching technology
  • Experience in the technical sales environment or in project planning is an advantage
  • Strong consulting competence and enjoyment of direct customer contact
  • Ability to technical problem analysis and solution development
  • Very good knowledge of German, good knowledge of English, French or Italian
