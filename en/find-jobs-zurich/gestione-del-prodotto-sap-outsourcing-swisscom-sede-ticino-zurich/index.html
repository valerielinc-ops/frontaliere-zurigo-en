Application process

  • In our Platform & Application Operation team, you take responsibility for the strategic orientation and further development of our SAP Basis Services.
  • You build the bridge between technical depth and strategic product management and transform complex platform capabilities into marketable, secure managed services for our B2B customers.
  • This role is ideal for an experienced product manager with a solid technical background in the SAP base and managed services environment – someone who designs product vision and roadmap as confidently as they translate customer requirements into technical solutions.
  • You will be responsible for the strategy, positioning and further development of our managed services in the area of SAP Basis over the entire product lifecycle – from vision to operation.
  • You define and maintain product vision, roadmap, and features, and control the prioritization of strategic initiatives along business value, customer needs, and security requirements.
  • You support sales and solution architecture in tenders, check the portfolio fit and ensure the technical feasibility of complex customer requirements.
  • You support sales with your technical expertise directly in customer discussions and accompany pre-sales phases for challenging projects.
  • Working closely with DevOps teams, architecture, product owners and other stakeholders, you translate high-level market needs into concrete technical capabilities and user stories.
