Personal – competent – close Welcome to the Hospital Uster, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As the central pillar of health care in the Oberland region of Zurich and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you experience the highest level of expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and promoted in your professional development. The Hospital Uster is a teaching hospital of the Medical Faculty of the University of Zurich. Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of a “regional and digital health hub in Züri East”! To complement our endoscopy team, we are looking for 1. July 2026 or by agreement

Exciting challenges await you

  • Care of patients before, during and after endoscopic examinations as well as assistance of gastroenterologists and pneumologists in diagnostic and therapeutic interventions
  • Sedating and monitoring patients together with the doctor
  • Cleaning and maintenance of instruments in accordance with the hygiene directive and participation in the provision of endoscopy material
  • Assistance in organizing the day program
  • Participation in the endoscopic emergency picture service

What sets you apart for the job

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Care professional HF/FH
  • Previous experience in endoscopy is desirable, but in any case the willingness and joy of learning new things
  • Enjoying teamwork, technically demanding examinations and a varied daily program
  • reliable, careful and professional way of working
  • friendly, patient-oriented manners and good knowledge of German
