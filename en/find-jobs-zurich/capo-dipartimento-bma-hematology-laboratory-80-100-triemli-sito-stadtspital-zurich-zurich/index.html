Full description

Shape the future of laboratory medicine with us and make an important contribution to high-quality patient care.

At the Institute of Laboratory Medicine of the City Hospital Zurich, you can expect a responsible leadership role in a modern and accredited laboratory environment. Together with the department head FAMH Hematology You ensure the reliable operation of the hematology laboratory as well as the transfusion capability around the clock. You take on a central role in the professional, personnel and organizational development of the department.

As a result of ordinary retirement of the long-term job holder, we are looking for a job on 1 May 2027 or by agreement

Tasks

  • Together with the department management FAMH führst You surgically work in the hematology laboratory including transfusion and develop the department further.
  • You and your team ensure smooth 24/7 operation in the areas of morphology (including blood counts, bone marrow, cytology, analyzers), hemostasis, immunohematology, serology and immunophenotyping.
  • You take over the professional and personnel management of the employees as well as the assignment, service and holiday planning.
  • You are responsible for compliance with and further development of quality management according to ISO 15189 as well as applicable guidelines and legal regulations.
  • Together with FAMH, you will evaluate, validate and implement new analysis methods, devices and processes and actively drive forward optimizations.
  • You will analyze performance and quality indicators, derive improvement measures and support change and digitalization projects.
  • You promote interdisciplinary cooperation and are the contact person for internal and external partners in technical issues.
  • You are involved in the education and training of students and employees and accompanying internships as well as project and bachelor theses.

Profile

  • Diploma as biomedical analyst HF or FH, ideally with a higher specialist examination or the willingness to complete it.
  • Several years of professional and managerial experience in a medium-sized or larger medical laboratory.
  • In-depth knowledge and practical experience in diagnostic hematology and transfusion.
  • Strong leadership, organizational and communication skills as well as enjoyment of the further development of employees.
  • High quality orientation and experience with accredited laboratory processes, preferably according to ISO 15189.
  • Responsible, resilient and solution-oriented personality with an independent and structured working method.
  • Willingness to make night, weekend or piquette assignments if necessary.

We offer

Look forward to a varied leadership task in which you can take on responsibility and contribute your own ideas. Together with a motivated and competent team You are actively involved in the further development of a central department in the Institute of Laboratory Medicine. You can expect a modern working environment, exciting projects, diverse development opportunities and attractive employment conditions in the city of Zurich. At the same time, you benefit from collaborative cooperation and a working environment that focuses on collaboration, quality and innovation.

About us

With over 36'000 inpatients and around 455,000 outpatients per year, the Stadtspital Zürich ranks among the top 10 Swiss hospitals. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,500 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
