PROFILE

  • Support engineering management in day-to-day operations and in the coordination of projects and resources
  • Participation in department controlling, creation of KPIs, forecasts and evaluations for capacity and resource planning
  • Preparation of offers, examination of orders and follow-up of milestones and deadlines
  • Management, maintenance and archiving of technical documentation as well as support of specialist areas in engineering processes
  • Assumption of classic assistance tasks such as appointment and travel management, session organization, presentation creation and administrative HR tasks

TASKS

  • Technical training completed with further training in business administration, industrial engineering or equivalent qualifications
  • Very good MS Office knowledge (especially Excel, PowerPoint and Word) and ideally experience with ERP, PLM or document management systems
  • Strong organizational skills and high resilience even in demanding project phases
  • Independent, structured and proactive personality with fast comprehension and high reliability
  • Very good German and English skills in word and writing, other languages are advantageous
