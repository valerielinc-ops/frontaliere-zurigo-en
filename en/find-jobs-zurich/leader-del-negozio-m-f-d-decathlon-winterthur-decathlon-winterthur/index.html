Take the lead in Winterthur! Become a store leader and shape the next chapter! Are you ready to take your leadership skills to the next level and take on a key role in one of our established branches? Your opportunity: Lead an established branch to new successes Our branch in Winterthur is an important part of Decathlon in Switzerland. Here you have the opportunity to build on a solid foundation, lead a large team and strategically develop the branch. Winterthur is a dynamic location with a sports-loving audience, and we are looking for a leader who will further strengthen our position as the first port of call for athletes. Your mission: Become an inspiring leader with strategic vision

  • Form your dream team: You lead a large and diverse team, promote collaboration, develop talent and create a positive working atmosphere.
  • Strategic Planning: You will develop and implement local strategies to further develop the branch, increase customer satisfaction and achieve business goals.

  • Operational Efficiency: You will optimize store processes, inventory management and resource utilization to maximize efficiency and profitability.
  • Omnichannel strategy: You develop and implement a seamless omnichannel strategy to offer our customers an optimal shopping experience across all channels.
  • Local networking: You will build strong relationships with sports clubs, local communities and other stakeholders in the region. What to expect from us With us, you will take on responsibility from day one and promote a strong team spirit. With us you will find opportunities for professional development, both nationally and internationally, and enjoy a wide range of benefits, profit sharing, 30 days vacation to ensure a healthy work-life balance and much more. For us, every day brings new challenges and exciting opportunities for personal and professional development.
