Full description

Are you a curious and analytical thinker looking to apply your skills in a global business environment? Join us as a Portfolio Monitoring & Insights Intern and gain hands-on experience in data-driven portfolio management while working alongside regional and global teams across the Life & Health (L&H) business. About the Role As part of the Portfolio Monitoring & Insights (PM&I) team, you’ll contribute to shaping strategic decisions through data analysis, collaboration, and performance insights.

Key Responsibilities

  • Support the monitoring strategy for the Continental Europe PM&I team.
  • Take ownership for the accurate ingestion of client data into the PM&I monitoring tool.
  • Help to identify gaps in the current data and suggest remediation actions to be discussed with our clients.
  • Proactively reach out to colleagues across the globe to gather product and data knowledge for the treaties you will look after.
  • Support with deep dive investigations to understand the drivers of performance that will lead to execution of relevant actions.
  • Use data analytics and visualisations to communicate portfolio performance, highlighting areas of concern and insights that steer business decisions.

The internship is scheduled for 3-5 months starting in September 2026. About the Team The Portfolio Monitoring & Insights EMEA (PM&I EMEA) team works in close collaboration with various functions in Life & Health (L&H) and in partnership with Market Units in Continental Europe, UK & Ireland and Middle East & Africa. Our purpose is to generate insights and connect our business to proactively steer the business in delivering sustainable profits. We achieve this by strengthening feedback loops across L&H and by delivering portfolio monitoring capabilities. Now you can become a part of this international and dynamic team! We are an engaged and agile group of individuals with a distinct financial foresight and a flair for creativity and problem solving. By effectively applying our skills and collaborating across functions, we make a difference to our partners and generate value in our portfolio both for our clients and partners.

About You

We are looking for a brilliant critical thinker who thrives in a dynamic environment and isn’t afraid to constructively challenge the status quo.

We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements

  • Actuarial or Mathematics student, ideally on master's level (at least 6 semesters of studies completed).
  • Understanding of various insurance lines of business.
  • Naturally curious mindset.
  • Ability to take ownership of tasks and work independently.
  • Strong analytical skills with a passion for leveraging data and technology to steer business decisions.
  • Experience with coding and data analytics tools (e.g. R, Power BI) is an advantage.
  • Previous experience in the insurance/(re)insurance industry is a plus.
  • Excellent oral and written English
  • Fluency in an additional European language is beneficial.

We look forward to receiving your application!
