Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School Limmattal Project staff with focus administration 60 - 100% The cantonal school Limmattal is a school of maturity of the Knonauer Amt and Limmattal regions and is located in Urdorf. It comprises a two-year secondary school (7th/8th school year) and the subsequent short high school (9th-12th school year). All maturity profiles and a bilingual matur are offered. Presumably in 2028 our school will be expanded by a branch in Affoltern am Albis. That is why we are looking for additional support as a project employee with a focus on administration as of 1 January 2027 or by agreement. After completion of the project, it is possible to take on a further position in the secretariat at the school in Affoltern am Albis.

  • Tasks The project is in an ongoing development process and the task area changes as the project progresses. As part of the development work, you are responsible for the following activities:
  • Establishment and cooperation in project planning
  • Creation and maintenance of project communication, for example newsletter and/or website
  • Taking over subprojects, e.g. documentation of administrative processes for the establishment
  • Planning and organization of events within the project
  • Support of the project team in various areas
  • Support and cooperation in internal processes and processes (central entrance examination, accounting, etc.) Profile
  • Completed commercial training, ideally with further education and experience in project management
  • Qualified professional experience (preferably in education)
  • Stylistic German, foreign languages of advantage
  • Very good knowledge of the MS Office applications
  • Sociable and resilient personality, secure appearance even in hectic times
  • Independent work with high quality awareness Offer It awaits you an interesting and varied task in the dynamic environment of a middle school. With your dedicated commitment, you make an important contribution to the establishment of a new branch and contribute to the culture of our school. The training takes place in an existing team.
