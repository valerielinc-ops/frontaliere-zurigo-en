Full description

Location: Amavita Erlibacher-Märt, Bahnhofstrasse 40, 8703 Erlenbach ZH

Do you want to accompany people with your pharmaceutical expertise and actively promote their health? At Amavita Apotheke Erlibacher-Märt, you will find a well-established team in which you can take on responsibility and contribute your expertise every day. With your advice, you ensure a positive customer experience and help to strengthen the pharmacy as a trusted point of contact on site.

You can do that.

  • You support the management and offer it hand in the daily work
  • You give our customers a welcome feeling: Advice on health issues is important to you
  • You train the learners and accompany the students and pharmacists in the FPH training
  • Together with the team you provide the services of the pharmacy and expand them as needed
  • Nothing escapes your watchful eyes: You make sure that the quality is right at all times

You bring that with you

  • Completed pharmacy studies with independent professional license (BAB) and vaccination competence
  • Strong service and customer orientation
  • Interest in pharmaceutical services
  • Independent, structured working methods and a strong sense of responsibility
  • Team spirit, initiative and motivation
  • Working together in a team is important to you and you are committed to a good working environment
  • Enjoy further developing the pharmacy with your ideas
  • German at least level B2, good foreign language skills (English, other languages of advantage)

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday
  • Pension fund with advantageous premium distribution - approx. 2/3 are taken over by us
  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and other top brands
  • Extend competences through funded and supported training and internal development opportunities
  • Family friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of wages) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of wages), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Registered shares at preferential prices - not only engage professionally in Galenica, but also invest

Benefits may vary slightly depending on area/place

About us

The largest pharmacy network Amavita is a company in the Galenica network and, with the largest pharmacy network in Switzerland, forms an essential pillar of health care close to home. We stand for a wide range, ranging from prescription and over-the-counter medicines to care products to natural remedies and natural cosmetics. We are also experts in dermatological skin care. Amavita pharmacies are the strongest in Western and Northern Switzerland. They are usually located in attractive and busy locations.
