The LANDI Weinland Cooperative is a trading and service company. We convince in the areas of agriculture, retail trade and energy, live close to agriculture and are anchored in the Zurich wine country. Every year we train several learners in the professions of retail specialists EFZ, retail assistants EBA and merchants EFZ. In addition to internal events, personal support and six weeks of holidays, you benefit from a varied education with exciting insights into many different areas. We would be happy to welcome you from August 2027 in our Volg shop in Seuzach.
