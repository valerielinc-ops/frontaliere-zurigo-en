More about this search for cross-border applicants

Mendrisio, nestled at the heart of Ticino, is a hub for research and innovation where Italian-speaking professionals from northern Italy can find opportunities that align with their expertise. With a strong presence in biotechnology, pharmaceuticals, and advanced materials research, the region attracts cross-border workers—often referred to as *frontalieri*—who commute daily between Italy and Switzerland. The local job market offers specialized roles in academia, R&D, and tech-driven industries, catering to those with advanced qualifications in fields like life sciences, engineering, and environmental research. Whether you’re seeking a position in a Swiss research institute or a multinational company, Mendrisio provides a dynamic environment for professionals who value both career growth and proximity to home. The city’s bilingual infrastructure and well-connected transport links further simplify the transition for those accustomed to cross-border work.
