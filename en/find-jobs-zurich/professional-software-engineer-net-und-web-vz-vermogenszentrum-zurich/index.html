Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We advise individuals and companies on all matters relating to money, manage assets and develop attractive financial services. As we continue to grow strongly, we are strengthening our software engineering team at the headquarters at Paradeplatz in Zurich.

  • You develop and operate business-critical applications in the banking environment – with the Microsoft stack and modern web technologies.
  • You actively shape the further development and modernization of our systems and bring current technologies into practice.
  • Together with the team, you will design and realize our applications with Angular, .NET and SQL Server on Microsoft Azure.
  • In a self-organized engineering team, you take on end-to-end responsibility for the development and operation of our services and applications according to the DevOps principle.
  • You work in a focused team with short decision paths and a pragmatic, agile development process.
  • A higher computer science education and at least five years of experience in a comparable role
  • Experience in developing modern web applications – from front-end frameworks to REST APIs to databases, ideally with Angular, C#/.NET and SQL Server
  • A strong hands-on mentality and enthusiasm for good software engineering and new technologies
  • Enjoy working in the team and our departments to develop joint solutions
  • Flexible working hours, several home office days per week and a modern workplace in a central location in Zurich.
  • A collegial environment with regular social events, versatile tasks and plenty of space for independent work.
  • The opportunity to actively shape a growing company, try out new ideas and continuously develop.
  • Every year you have three days and CHF 1'000 at your disposal for your further education. We support larger training within the framework of your job profile.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
