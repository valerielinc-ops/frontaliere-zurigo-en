Full description

Designing psychiatry. People lead. Improve the effect. The Spitex right Limmattal with its urban and rural surroundings offers a unique environment for exciting encounters and varied missions. We are a modern non-profit company with over 90 employees and live a reliable, appreciative and collegial team culture. We are trusted by the municipalities of Oberengstringen, Unterengstringen, Weiningen, Geroldswil, Oetwil a.d.L. and Fahrweid with a total of over 22,500 inhabitants. People are at the heart of our actions – both our clients and our employees. So that we can continue to ensure our professional and individual care quality and successfully develop our psychiatric area, we are looking for a personality who accompanies people, supports employees and actively shapes the future of our psychiatric area together with us. After many successful years, our long-term team leader psychiatry is retired. This is why we are looking for 1. June 2027 or by appointment a dedicated leader with psychiatric expertise, foresight and joy in taking on responsibility. Professional and human team management Psychiatry (70–90%) | Spitex This function combines leadership, expertise and operational cooperation in an ideal way. You lead your team with appreciation and clarity, support complex psychiatric situations and at the same time stay close to our clients. You take responsibility for people, processes and quality – and actively contribute your ideas to the further development of our psychiatry area. Your main tasks include:

  • Professional and personnel management as well as further development of a small, well-established psychiatry team
  • Ensuring professional, client-oriented and high-quality psychiatric care
  • Regular assignments with our clients in the home environment as well as support in complex psychiatric care situations
  • Promotion and support of your employees in their professional and personal development
  • Active participation and further development of the Department of Psychiatry as well as participation in projects and change processes
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with relatives, doctors and internal and external specialists This is what you bring with you:
  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nursing specialist HF/FH psychiatry as well as approval for needs assessment according to Bepsy
  • Leadership experience beneficial or the motivation to take the next step into a leadership function
  • Ideally experience in outpatient psychiatry or in the Spitex environment
  • High level of technical and social competence and pleasure in accompanying and promoting people
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method with a high degree of personal responsibility
  • Openness to change and joy in actively shaping developments
  • Safe handling of digital work equipment and documentation systems
  • Cat B driving licence (no own vehicle required) Our successful cooperation:
