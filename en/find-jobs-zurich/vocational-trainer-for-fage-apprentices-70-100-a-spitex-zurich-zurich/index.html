Full description

Do you want to accompany the next generation of health professionals on their way to vocational qualification, inspire them and help them lay the foundation for their future career? Our vocational trainers are organized in their own team at Spitex Zurich and can thus fully concentrate on accompanying the trainees. The close cooperation in the team enables a valuable professional exchange and creates the best conditions for high-quality training. You will be actively supported by our education and training managers. You work situationally across locations, have a strong team behind you and at the same time enjoy a lot of personal responsibility, freedom of design and scope for action in practical vocational training.

In order to strengthen our vocational trainer team, we are looking for an established personality as

YOUR TASKS

  • You accompany, promote and empower trainees in practical and theoretical training and thus ensure a high-quality education.
  • You regularly conduct training and qualification interviews and support individual learning processes.
  • You work across locations with vocational trainees, training and education managers, team coaches and nursing teams.
  • You are involved in trade fairs, information events at schools, future days and other events.
  • So that you stay on the pulse of practice, you supplement your activity with individual care assignments.

You find us.

  • A meaningful task in which you actively accompany and shape the next generation of health professionals
  • A specialized team with strong cohesion, valuable exchange and active support
  • A lot of personal responsibility and scope for design in practical vocational training
  • Various benefits such as attractive further education and training opportunities, excellent social benefits or the opportunity to buy holiday weeks.

You bring with you

  • Completed regular 3-year training as a health professional EFZ
  • Vocational trainer/in certificate available or willing to complete it
  • SVEB certificate practical trainer (level 1) and experience as a vocational trainer
  • At least two years of professional experience as a health EFZ
  • Flair for digital tools and very good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Joy in the education of young people and interest in the design of individual learning settings
  • High sense of responsibility, organisational capacity and independence
  • You like to travel with the e-bike
