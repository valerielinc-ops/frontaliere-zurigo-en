Full description

Create the working world of tomorrow with us! You are passionate about change, want to actively promote future topics and support people in breaking new ground? Then this could be your next field of action. With us you are not only looking for ideas and concepts, but also bring them into the implementation and ensure that they have a lasting effect. You design innovative development initiatives, strengthen the future viability of the organization and accompany change and transformation projects. This is how you shape health

  • You develop and realize initiatives for the working world of tomorrow (e.g. AI Enablement, Future Skills, etc.)
  • You translate future topics and strategic requirements into concrete development measures with a measurable impact
  • You will design innovative learning journeys and moderate workshops and learning units
  • You will develop new formats for collaborative learning, knowledge exchange and competence building
  • You will be responsible for initiatives from the first idea to piloting to successful introduction and anchoring, coordinating different stakeholders

This sets you apart

  • Higher / university degree or corresponding further education in the field of personnel development
  • Several years of professional experience (ideally 8+ years) in personnel development
  • Experience in the development and implementation of enablement and change initiatives
  • Enthusiasm for modern learning approaches and new forms of skills development
  • Enjoy not only developing concepts, but implementing them yourself and achieving impact
  • Strong ability to attract people to change and bring different stakeholders with them
  • Strong communication, moderation and presentation skills
  • Implementation strength and a pragmatic and independent working method
  • Curiosity, creativity and openness to new ideas and perspectives
  • Negotiable in German, English and French is an advantage

Why SWICA

  • We build on humanity. That is why we focus on openness, respect and trust in our everyday work. We tackle this, take responsibility and think in terms of solutions – together, for our customers. This is also why we have been number 1 in customer satisfaction for many years.
  • We trust you and give you space. With annual working hours, at least 5 weeks holidays and on request also holiday purchase. With part-time models and generous home office within the scope of the possibilities of the job, you make your everyday life largely flexible.
  • We value and reward your work – with a market-based wage, up to 1,500 francs annual fee for personal wishes and a generous participation in the basic and additional insurance for you and your family.
  • We offer you prospects. With a generous training regulations, we support your professional and personal development – for your role today and for your goals of tomorrow.
  • Because health is everything. With our company health management and region-specific offers, we take care of your well-being. And if it comes otherwise: 100% net wage continued payment.
  • We think long term. With three savings plans, pensum-dependent coordination deduction and benefits well above the legal minimum, you ensure good for your future.
