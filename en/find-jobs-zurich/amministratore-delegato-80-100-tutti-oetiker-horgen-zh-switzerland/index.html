Full description

As a central organizational hub, you ensure smooth processes, reliable governance processes and a professional flow of information. You support the CEO, the management and the Board of Directors at the highest professional level. You work forward-looking, independently and with a high level of quality awareness in an internationally successful Swiss family business.

  • Coordination of the CEO’s agenda, mailbox and priorities and organization of international business travel
  • Preparation of presentations and preparation of meeting documents for the CEO
  • Organization of meetings, off-sites and workshops for the CEO and Board of Directors
  • Professional support of Board meetings and committees including annual planning, meeting documents, minutes
  • Organising and supervising the General Assembly and the shareholders
  • Coordination of business register mutations, signatures, certifications and other corporate law issues
  • Preparation, editing and quality assurance of reports, presentations and decision documents
  • Central interface for board of directors, shareholders, management and internal and external partners
  • Ensuring an efficient and organized office day
  • Completed commercial or business education (as well as further training as assistant director)
  • Professional experience as Executive Assistant CEO, Executive Board or Board of Directors, ideally in an international environment
  • High integrity, absolute discretion and a sovereign, binding approach in dealing with different stakeholders
  • Strong organizational skills, networked thinking and the ability to control complex processes and changing priorities
  • Independent, structured and forward-looking working method with high quality standards and a strong hands-on mentality
  • High service orientation and flexibility
  • Stylistic German and very good English skills in word and writing
  • Very experienced handling of Microsoft 365 and AI technology
  • Readiness for rice (about 1-2 business trips per year)
  • Readiness for physical presence at headquarters in Horgen

What to expect

  • A versatile area of responsibility with a high degree of personal responsibility and a lot of room for manoeuvre
  • An international Swiss family business with a culture of trust, respect and cooperation
  • Attractive framework conditions as well as space for personal and professional development
