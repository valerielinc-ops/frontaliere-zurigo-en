Our team ensures the pharmaceutical care of the hospital Limmattal and the care center. We pay particular attention to the various interfaces in the medication process: from the selection of drugs, the distribution in-house, the directional and application process on the stations, to the patient’s exit. Our main goal is to ensure effective, safe and economical medication.

  • A versatile activity at the interface between logistics, production and clinical consulting
  • Structured familiarization and targeted promotion on the way to the FPH title
  • A dynamic, collegial team in a modern hospital
  • in the field of pharmaceutical services & logistics and in the day-to-day business of central cytostatics production
  • in drug care and specialist advice for outpatient and outgoing hospital patients
  • in the processing of pharmaceutical inquiries and consultancies within the framework of interprofessional cooperation with Ärzt:inside and nurses
  • in the active participation of a safe, effective and economical medication process
  • have completed studies in pharmacy
  • have great interest in hospital pharmacy and the motivation for the 3-year FPH training
  • have an independent, structured and exact working method
  • have very good IT skills as well as a high level of communication and team skills
  • Reduced employee parking
  • Motorcycle parking, lockable bicycle parking
