Full description

The new school Borrweg has been a day school since this school year. We are looking for support for Cluster 3B (1st class) as of 01.11.26 or by agreement reinforcement.

Tasks

As a FaBe or social pedagogue you work 4 days a week (certainly Monday and Friday). During the school holidays, you are also deployed for 5 weeks per school year in the holiday camp. Together with two FaBe colleagues, you shape everyday life for the children in Cluster 3B. Within the support team, they ensure day-to-day operations throughout the schoolhouse and in the holiday camp.

Their tasks include

  • Together with your colleagues, you are responsible for caring for a group of children in kindergarten age.
  • They ensure day-to-day operations
  • They talk to parents.
  • You work with teachers and other teachers

Profile

  • Degree as a FaBe or social pedagogue, a primary school teaching diploma or an equivalent education (for a foreign diploma, a Swiss diploma recognition is mandatory)
  • Professional experience in caring for children (hoard, daycare)
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • You enjoy working in a vibrant, multicultural care facility in a new schoolhouse
  • They like to participate and participate.
  • They are flexible, resilient and keep an overview and joy even in lively groups of children.
  • You enjoy working in a team and appreciate cooperation with other professional groups

We offer

A varied and meaningful activity in a living environment. Our employees also benefit from attractive employment conditions. A motivated and open-minded team is looking forward to your application!

About us

The school district Uto is one of 7 school districts in the city of Zurich and includes districts 2 and 3 with the districts Leimbach, Wollishofen, Enge, Friesenberg and Alt-Wiedikon. The newly built Borrweg School is located in the Friesenberg Quarter, at the foot of the Uetliberg. It consists of 3 kindergartens, 12 primary and 2 admission classes.
