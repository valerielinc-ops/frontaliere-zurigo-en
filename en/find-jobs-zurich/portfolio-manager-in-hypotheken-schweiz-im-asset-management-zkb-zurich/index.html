Full description

80% - 100% | Asset Management / Portfolio Management | Zurich | Experienced professionals Zürcher Kantonalbank’s asset management is the second largest provider in Switzerland with over CHF 353 billion in asset management (as at 31 July 2026) and around 280+ investment experts. We offer all institutional asset management services for financial services providers, companies, foundations and pension funds. In particular, our sustainability and prevention competence is internationally excellent. In the area of mortgages, we are responsible for direct Swiss mortgage portfolios for institutional clients and are continuously developing our offer. You take on a central role in portfolio management and actively shape the further development of our mortgage business. Your tasks

  • Portfolio Management in Mortgages Switzerland
  • Development, implementation and further development of the investment strategy
  • Management of the mortgage portfolio (cash flows, procurement policy, capital drawdowns, assessment of mortgage loan applications and mutations, etc.)
  • Further development of processes and systems for further professionalization of the area
  • Close cooperation with internal interfaces (e.g. sales, risk, legal, fund management, IT)
  • Maintaining and developing relationships with intermediaries, servicing partners and other market participants
  • Winning communication with investors and preparation and implementation of presentations
  • Creation of decision-making basis, reporting and analysis for internal and external stakeholders

Your profile

  • University degree at a university or university of applied sciences in economics, finance, mathematics, computer science, engineering or comparable subject area Advanced training is an advantage (e.g. CFA, AZEK)
  • Several years of experience in portfolio management, ideally in the mortgage or real estate environment
  • Passion for the mortgage business and the real estate market
  • Affinity/interest in controlling and compliance topics (possibly also deepened by targeted further education)
  • Very strong analytical and conceptual thinking skills as well as systematic, structured working methods
  • Strong data and analytics skills
  • Programming skills are a plus
  • High level of social competence and team-oriented personality who takes on responsibility and drives issues independently
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Other national languages are beneficial

Our offer

  • Responsibility in a strategically important business area with direct links to institutional clients
  • Broad range of tasks from analysis and portfolio management to investor discussions and cross-divisional projects
  • Active participation in the further development of our offer in the mortgage business
  • An experienced, professionally strong team with short decision-making paths and an open feedback culture
  • Support from the employer with attractive further education offers and the opportunity to organize your work flexibly
  • An attractive overall package with interesting additional services and employee benefits (sports, leisure, child care, health insurance)
  • An employer who takes care of and invests in her employees. In addition, you have access to various networks and mentoring programs in and outside the bank (e.g. bank-internal talent promotion program, fund women’s membership, employee networks, etc.)
