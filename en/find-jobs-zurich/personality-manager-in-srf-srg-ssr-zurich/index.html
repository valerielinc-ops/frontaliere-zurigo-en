Full description

The distribution department ensures that SRF’s offerings are published on the linear radio and TV programs as well as on the digital channels and platforms on which our target groups operate – with the right mix of topics, in the right form, at the right time, on the right scale, with the right brand – supported by digitally oriented marketing.

What are your tasks?

In the Brand Management & Design team, you shape the future of the SRF brand. Personalities are the human carriers of the SRF brand and thus sustainably strengthen audience loyalty and brand impact. As a Personality Manager, you strategically develop and manage the SRF Personality portfolio and support the sustainable development of personalities as formative ambassadors of the SRF brand.

In the Brand Management & Design team, you are committed to the following tasks:

  • They develop, manage and analyze the SRF personality portfolio across all genres and platforms and derive data-based recommendations for long-term portfolio development. You initiate and lead steering measures and larger initiatives to achieve the strategic goals of the Personality portfolio
  • Together with the personnel responsible in the departments, they advise on recruitment, recruitment, succession and development decisions and contribute the perspectives of the brand, audience and portfolio.
  • Together with the personality managers in the departments, they identify and promote future personality talent and support the strategic positioning and further development of existing personalities.
  • You lead the Personality Management department, develop standards and control instruments and promote networking, visibility and appreciation of SRF personalities

What do you bring?

  • You have several years of professional experience in media, brand management, talent management, program strategy, audience development or a comparable strategic environment
  • You have experience in the development and management of talent, person or brand portfolios as well as in advising executives and decision-making bodies
  • You have a sound understanding of brand management, personal brands, audience loyalty and developments in the Swiss media and platform environment
  • They convince with their strong social and consulting skills, strategic thinking and the ability to bring different interests together at eye level

What can you look forward to?

  • On a newly sharpened key strategic function with great creative leeway at the interface of brand, audience, offer and personnel development
  • The opportunity to actively shape the future development of the SRF personality portfolio and create a sustainable impact for the brand and audience
  • On the cooperation with committed specialist areas, executives and experts: women from program, HR, training and brand management
  • To a socially relevant environment in which you actively shape the future of public media service in Switzerland

What we offer

What does it take to reach the people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expert knowledge, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can contribute and develop. For the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: Welcome to the world of SRF!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, promote creativity and offer a lot of design space for new and high-quality products.
  • Shaping digitalization: Quality journalism is also digital for us. That’s why we explore new possibilities and experiment with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-medial. We design on the pulse of time and on the nerve of the audience.
  • Being human: We meet each other collegially, work independently and shape our working atmosphere individually. Our success is based on the personal strengths as well as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programs. In doing so, we act respectfully and independently; in this way we promote opinion formation and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.

Requirements

  • Sie verfügen über mehrjährige Berufserfahrung in Medien, Markenführung, Talent Management, Programmstrategie, Audience Development oder einem vergleichbaren strategischen Umfeld

  • Sie bringen Erfahrung in der Entwicklung und Steuerung von Talent-, Personen- oder Markenportfolios sowie in der Beratung von Führungskräften und Entscheidungsgremien mit

  • Sie verfügen über ein fundiertes Verständnis von Markenführung, Personal Brands, Publikumsbindung und den Entwicklungen im Schweizer Medien- und Plattformumfeld

  • Sie überzeugen durch ausgeprägte Sozial- und Beratungskompetenz, strategisches Denken sowie die Fähigkeit, unterschiedliche Interessen auf Augenhöhe zusammenzuführen
