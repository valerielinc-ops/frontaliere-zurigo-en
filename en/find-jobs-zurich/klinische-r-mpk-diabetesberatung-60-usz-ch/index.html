By agreement

Design the diabetes therapy of tomorrow – at the University Hospital Zurich you work interprofessionally with highly specialized endocrinologists, nutrition consultants, nurses and many other professional groups based on evidence for a better quality of life for our patients. Become part of a highly specialized, dedicated team that implements, shapes and redevelops outpatient and inpatient diabetes therapy to the highest standard.

Your main tasks

  • Standardized training of blood sugar measurements and simple insulin injections
  • Provision of blood sugar data and maintenance of data platforms
  • Support for quality, material and document management
  • Patient coordination

Your profile

  • Completed training as medical practice coordinator:in (clinical direction), or in training as medical:r practice coordinator:in
  • You are a flexible, committed and self-employed personality
  • Thanks to your organizational talent, you can organize your day-to-day work independently even under high workload and prioritize upcoming work accordingly.
  • You are capable of teamwork and enjoy working directly with patients: inside and on interprofessional cooperation

Our offer

Become part of a specialized diabetes counselling team and work closely with experienced colleagues in a highly specialized, interprofessional environment. You can expect exciting and varied tasks with direct patient contact as well as demanding clinical questions. In our university environment, you can contribute your skills in a targeted manner, further develop them and actively shape diabetes care.
