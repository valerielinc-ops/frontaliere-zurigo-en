Original description

Create, build and manage high-quality website experiences across the region and, for US (CoE), lead the creation and management of global templates, workflows, and processes to ensure consistency, scalability, and performance.

Key objectives include

  • Review briefs and provided content to build and publish user-friendly web pages using approved templates, ensuring clarity, structure, and performance
  • Analyse and execute website requests, managing deployment timelines and coordinating across teams to deliver on time and to spec
  • Apply SEO, metadata, tagging, UX best practices, and personalisation to improve reach, engagement, and conversion
  • Work with stakeholders across Brand and Marketing (content) and Group Comms (platform) to align content structure, timing, and publishing plans
  • Use dashboards and A/B testing tools to monitor website performance and recommend optimisation actions
  • Use data and insights, UX principles, automation, and AI tools to improve website management and enhance customer engagement across journeys
  • Understand customer segmentation on the website to align page structure, content, and targeting with campaign goals
  • Ensure content complies with publishing standards, brand guidelines, accessibility, and legal/compliance requirements
  • Maintain documentation and contribute to team knowledge sharing on CMS capabilities, publishing workflows, and issue resolution
  • (CoE) Develop and maintain global templates, components, and publishing standards for layout, navigation, and accessibility
  • (CoE) Support website managers in other hubs on complex builds and CMS capabilities, and lead knowledge sharing and onboarding
  • (CoE) Align with Group Comms, GDTO, and Marketing Strategy on shared workflows and platform roadmap decisions
