Full description

"We offer space for innovation" Balgrist Tec AG as a leading manufacturer and supplier of orthopaedic aids has the knowledge and infrastructure to develop high-quality solutions and thus increase the mobility of its patients. In our workshops we produce individual orthopaedic shoes, shoe inlays, prostheses and orthoses.

From August 2027 we offer an exciting 4-year vocational training. Tasks: • Manufacture of orthopaedic footwear and orthopaedic footwear • Carrying out post-corrections and repairs • Customer services Requirements • Secondary level A or B with very good grades • Craftsmanship and a precise and careful working method • Good feeling for materials - sense of shapes and colors • Interest in medical issues • Good manners We offer: • You have the opportunity to participate in a leading company in the industry with an excellent reputation and connection to the University Hospital Balgrist • Benefit from the extensive expertise and experience of our trainers • 50% discount on the ZVV bonus pass or an annual contribution to an existing general subscription • Enjoy a healthy and balanced lunch at discounted prices in our company’s staff restaurant with free coffee/hot drinks • Miscellaneous shopping incentives Further advantages are just one click away: Attractive offers for our employees
