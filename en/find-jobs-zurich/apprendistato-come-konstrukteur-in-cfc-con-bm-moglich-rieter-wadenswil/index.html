Full description

SSM Textilmaschinen, headquartered in Wädenswil, Switzerland, is a subsidiary of the Rieter Group. SSM is the world's leading supplier of precision winding machines in the fields of dyeing, weaving and sewing yarn preparation and also successfully in individual segments of filament yarn production. In addition to SSM Schärer Schweiter Mettler AG in Wädenswil, the company also includes subsidiaries in Italy and China. SSM is represented in all major markets worldwide.

You have power, are curious and like to join? You like working in a team, trying new things and looking for creative solutions when things get trickier? Then you are exactly right with us! We look forward to you and to accompany you on your way into professional life.

Are you open, motivated and like to participate independently? You enjoy varied tasks, enjoy working in a team and keep an overview even when things get hectic? We look forward to meeting you!

Your tasks

  • With us, you can expect a varied and exciting 4-year apprenticeship in which you get to know the different areas of construction in an industrial company.
  • In the first year of training, you will visit the RAU Regional Training Centre in Au/Wädenswil. There you will learn the basics of the construction as well as the professional handling of the CAD.
  • From 2 to 4. You deepen your knowledge directly with us in the teaching company and can further develop your skills step by step.
  • You will be accompanied and supported by experienced trainers and our team of constructors.

You bring that with you

  • You attend secondary school A. Good grades in mathematics and geometry as well as in technical drawing – on average at least 4.5.
  • You can imagine things well in space and are interested in how technical systems and machines work.
  • You are motivated, like to learn new things and want to develop further.
  • Rely on you and you work focused, carefully and accurately.
  • You enjoy working on the computer and enjoy developing technical solutions.
  • For the BM profile you meet the recording conditions according to cantonal specifications.

Your advantages

  • A varied and versatile 4-year apprenticeship in a manageable company.
  • A structured education with professional supervision and personal support.
  • A team where you can contribute your creativity and analytical thinking.
  • A pleasant working atmosphere, modern employment conditions and good opportunities to develop with us after your apprenticeship.
  • And above all: the opportunity to think, shape and gain practical experience right from the start.

Are you interested in starting your career with us? Then we look forward to your application documents (application letter, CV with photo, all high school certificates and possibly multicheck) by e-mail to jobs@ssm.ch.
