Role overview

SSM Textilmaschinen, headquartered in Wädenswil, Switzerland, is a subsidiary of the Rieter Group.

SSM is the world's leading supplier of precision winding machines in the fields of dyeing, weaving and sewing yarn preparation and also successfully in individual segments of filament yarn production.

In addition to SSM Schärer Schweiter Mettler AG in Wädenswil, the company also includes subsidiaries in Italy and China.
