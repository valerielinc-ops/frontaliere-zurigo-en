Full description

The production of sophisticated workpieces requires precise setup of modern CNC milling machines. In an innovative environment, additive technology produces precision parts with demanding quality. For our client we are looking for one/n activities

  • manufacturing demanding workpieces on CNC milling machines
  • setting up and operating 5 axes milling center independently
  • machining additively manufactured blanks into precision parts
  • ensuring quality and helping with general workshop work
  • Requirements
  • Completed training as a polymechanic / in EFZ
  • Several years of experience in CNC milling
  • High quality awareness and enjoyment of complex components
  • Self-employed, committed and team-oriented working methods Offer
  • Modern machine park with 5 axes Technology and robotics
  • Innovative environment in the field of 3D metal printing
  • Bright workplace and family team culture
  • Plenty of personal responsibility, flat hierarchies and free parking spaces
