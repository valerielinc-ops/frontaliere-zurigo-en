Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Law Enforcement and Reintegration Human Resources Specialist: in HR processes & system implementation (temporary until the end of 2027) 80 - 100% With around 1,400 employees, JuWe is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as for carrying out various types of detention.
