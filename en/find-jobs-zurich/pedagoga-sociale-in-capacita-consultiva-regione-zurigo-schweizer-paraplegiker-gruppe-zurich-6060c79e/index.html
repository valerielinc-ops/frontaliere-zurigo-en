Full description

Tasks

  • As a Consultant Supported Communication (UK), you advise our clients and their environment (family, caregivers, teachers and therapists), clarify the existing needs and set goals together to ensure tailor-made and high-quality care in the area of communication.
  • You support our customers on site and train them in the use of the aids in order to achieve optimal use of the aid solution in everyday life. You carry out your customer projects independently and efficiently.
  • You will carry out administrative activities (e.g. reporting to the IV offices).
  • You will participate in customer-oriented events and trade fairs and actively engage in knowledge transfer in the industry.

Requirements

  • We are looking for an independent maker personality with a strong sense of community, who wants to make ordinary things possible for extraordinary people.
  • You have an education in the social sector (e.g. social educator or similar) with experience in the field of supported communication.
  • We want a communicative personality with an uncomplicated service mentality.
  • You like to work customer-oriented, think entrepreneurially and like to act independently.
  • You meet people with and without impairment always appreciative, open and turned and are eager to know.
  • You also convince with organizational skills, technical flair and an efficient way of working. Administrative work you do routinely.
  • In addition to the German mother tongue, you ideally have good knowledge of English.
  • If you also like driving, we have found our new team member in you.

We offer

You not only expect attractive employment conditions, but also numerous other advantages. Look forward to meaningful work in a modern working environment, flexible working hours and a wide range of further education opportunities. Come and experience our unique spirit. More information on parplegie.ch/benefits.

Active Communication is a subsidiary of the Swiss Paraplegic Foundation. As a leading provider of digital assistive technologies, she ensures greater self-determination and participation and thus contributes to the inclusion of people with disabilities. It offers tailor-made solutions in the areas of communication aids, computer and workplace adjustments, as well as environmental controls.

We make it possible: Communicate even when it seems almost impossible. Learning with limited perception and usability. Working despite restrictions and barrier-free living without great dependency.
