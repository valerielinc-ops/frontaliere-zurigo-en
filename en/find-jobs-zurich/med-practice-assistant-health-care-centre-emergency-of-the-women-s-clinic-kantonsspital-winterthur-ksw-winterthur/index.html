Full description

To complement our team recovery room, ultrasound and ambulatorium of gastroenterology and hepatology, we are looking for a specialist health or medical assistant / practice assistant immediately or by agreement. Together with an experienced colleague, you will take over the waking-up eel and the consultation and ultrasound assistance of the doctors. In the gastroenterology and hepatology of the Cantonal Hospital Winterthur, there is also a lively consultation with about 3,500 outpatient and stationary ultrasound examinations per year.

Tasks

  • Preparatory and follow-up care of patients and patients before an endoscopy including monitoring
  • Performing blood sampling, laying venous catheters, dressing changes and other medical assistance activities
  • Support of doctors for smaller outpatient procedures
  • Organisation of ultrasound and special consultation hours (hepatology, chronic intestinal inflammation)
  • Coordination tasks with internal and external bodies
  • Performance recording as well as the assumption of organizational tasks

Profile

  • Specialist training for health or Med. Practical assistant with professional experience in acute care
  • Exact, independent, efficient and structured work
  • You have an open, communicative and courteous attitude
  • You are motivated and bring a high level of social skills
  • Flexibility and team-oriented action is one of your strengths
  • Practical handling of MS-Office products
