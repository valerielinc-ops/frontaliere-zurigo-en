Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Vocational Advice Auditor in Child Protection, Bülach Office 80 - 100% The Office for Youth and Vocational Advice (AJB) informs, advises and supports the population of the Canton of Zurich on the topics of family, career and career. It provides the various services decentrally in four supply regions in the Canton of Zurich, which include various child and youth welfare centers (kjz), job information centers (biz) and a branch office.

  • We offer a legal auditorium from now on or by agreement, for a limited period of one year at the Bülach office.
  • In this role, you write legal documents in judicial proceedings and submit applications to the child and adult protection authorities. After a training period, there is the possibility, if suitable, to take over the independent representation of child interests in court within the framework of child protection mandates in civil and criminal proceedings. In addition, you will advise the various internal departments in various areas of law. At most, you also participate in the advice of parents regarding the maintenance and custody regulations for their children.
  • For this versatile and demanding activity, you need a completed legal education (Master of a Swiss University) and a special interest in civil and procedural law. They are capable of teamwork, work accurately, efficiently and independently. One of your strengths is to quickly familiarize yourself with new topics and to recognize the essentials in a short time. They are also distinguished by their ability to work with contact persons from a wide range of disciplines and bring interest and empathy for the concerns of children, adolescents and their families.
