Canton of Zurich Security Directorate Migration Office Team Principal/in Approval Area 80 - 100% The Migration Office of the Canton of Zurich operates daily in the exciting political environment of all migration issues and the resulting tasks. It is responsible for the implementation of the Swiss law on aliens and asylum, the agreement on the free movement of persons between Switzerland and the EU/EFTA countries as well as the Schengen and Dublin agreements. For the Entry/FZA area we are looking for a team principal 80 - 100% by 1 November 2026 or by agreement. In this interesting management position, you are responsible for the technical and personnel management of a team of seven employees. You handle legally demanding individual cases and together with your team you provide services in the “Entry and Free Movement of Persons” approval process of the cantonal migration authority. This includes all questions concerning the regulation of the right of residence of foreign nationals. For this challenging task, you have a law degree, management experience or comparable competencies and preferably administrative practice. We appeal to an independent, positive and resilient personality with a high level of social competence. Challenging situations are familiar to you and you meet new things openly. You live a constructive and collegial cooperation, communicate authentically and formulate stylishly. In addition to a pleasant, professional appearance You have a precise and efficient way of working. Flexibility, commitment, reliability and a pinch of humor round off your profile. You also have Swiss citizenship. You expect a meaningful, responsible and varied activity with a high level of personal responsibility. We guarantee you interesting contacts with the federal, cantonal and municipal authorities as well as with private stakeholders. In addition, you benefit from modern forms of work, flexible working hours, discounts (such as lunch check or mobile subscription), good pension benefits and extensive home office. We also promote and support your education and training. Do you feel engaged in this exciting task? Then we look forward to your complete electronic application with photo no later than 30. September 2026. For questions, the head of department Dieter Hirt (phone 043 259 85 00) is at your disposal. You can find out more about the Migration Office on our homepage zh.ch/ma.

Pensum: 80-100 %
