Tasks

You love order, like to get involved and keep an overview even in turbulent moments? As technical object manager / technical housekeeper (m/f/d), you ensure that our property runs technically, visually and organizationally. You are the face on site and shape our everyday life with your craftsmanship, your service mentality and your pronounced quality awareness. The position shall be filled immediately.

  • Ensure technical building maintenance and smooth plant operation. Installation troubleshooting
  • Ensuring proper maintenance of building infrastructure and outdoor facilities
  • Coordination, monitoring and documentation of internal and external services and technical operations

Requirements

  • Technical training and professional experience Completed technical or craft vocational training (e.g. professional maintenance EFZ, electrical installer, sanitary or heating installer)
  • Understanding of technical installations, their operation and maintenance. Ability to detect and properly assess technical failures
  • Good knowledge of HVAC, electrical engineering and building control technology
  • Independent and structured working method Ability to set priorities and act solution-oriented
  • Friendly and professional approach towards customers, tenants and building users.
  • High responsibility and awareness of security
  • Teamwork, flexibility and resilience
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Safe handling of digital work equipment and documentation systems
