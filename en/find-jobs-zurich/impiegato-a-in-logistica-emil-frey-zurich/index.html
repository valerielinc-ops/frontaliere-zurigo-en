Full description

What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family company with over 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessories as well as the provision of financial services. The Zurich-Altstetten dealership – with its location at the headquarters of the Emil Frey Group – presents the model palettes of seven global brands as well as a large selection of occasions. The company also includes the brand workshops, a carrosserie department and a spare parts warehouse. In order to strengthen our spare parts storage team, we are looking for a logistics specialist (pensum 50%) with experience as a retail specialist in automotive parts logistics (pensum 50%). Their tasks

  • Reception, inspection and storage of spare parts, accessories and wheels and tyres
  • Order picking and provision of parts for workshop, sales and external customers
  • Active participation in storage management and relocation operations
  • Processing of customer inquiries as well as the sale of spare parts and accessories at the counter and by telephone
  • Advising our internal and external customers on products, availability and delivery dates
  • Accounting and offsetting of spare parts orders and control of supplier invoices
  • Ensuring order, quality and efficiency throughout the spare parts warehouse Your profile
