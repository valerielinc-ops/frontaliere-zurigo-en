For the Tunneling Switzerland department, we are looking for you as a calculator to strengthen our acquisition team at our headquarters in Opfikon.

This moves you

• Responsible for calculating complex projects in the field of underground construction in an offer team under the direction of an experienced project manager • Development of calculation bases incl. Implementation variants and project optimizations • Construction and scheduling • Supplier and subcontractor requests • Preparation of cost estimates and cost estimates • Supervisor assistance in procurement negotiations • Price calculations for supplementary quotes of construction sites in execution • Development of offers internally with engineering and special foundation construction

This is why you succeed

• Graduation and qualification: You have a degree as a civil engineer or a comparable training and already have knowledge in the field of underground construction. • Interest in the calculation: Even if you have not yet worked as a full-time calculator, you are welcome. • Computer skills: You are a routine, skilled user of MS Office. The same applies to scheduling with TILOS. • Working method: You are used to working in a team, think analytically and networked and work independently and efficiently on the tasks assigned to you. • Language skills: Good German skills are required. If you have additional knowledge in French and/or Italian, this is very welcome.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
