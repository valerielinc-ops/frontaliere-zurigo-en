Full description

For a traditional and independent Swiss family business from the industrial and consumer goods sector in the Zurich (Wallisellen) area with around 120 employees, we are looking for a thinking and organized personality as a clerk: in finance. In an innovative environment with flat hierarchies, you take on responsibility in the ongoing financial process, manage the ledger and actively participate as an ERP super-user in exciting digitalization projects. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for you!

This is your job

  • General ledger & taxes: Maintenance of the general ledger including monthly balance sheet account votes and processing of VAT bills for Switzerland and Germany.
  • Accounting & secondary books: Self-employed management and management of accounts payable, accounts receivable and assets.
  • Reporting & Financial Statements: Active participation in the preparation of monthly reporting as well as in the monthly and annual financial statements.
  • Projects & Digitalization: Participating in the further development and digitalization of the financial processes as well as the perception of the role as Finance Super User: in the ERP system.

This is what you bring with you

  • Training: basic commercial training EFZ, ideally supplemented by further training in accounting (e.g. in accounting).
  • Experience: Sound professional experience in accounting.
  • Languages: stylish knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Oral French skills are beneficial.
  • Personality: Independent organization of the working environment, structured and solution-oriented working methods as well as charming and constructive assertiveness in compliance with specifications.

This is what awaits you from the customer

  • Safety & Tradition: A versatile function in an established Swiss family business with over 100 years of success.
  • Design leeway: The possibility to actively digitally shape processes and make a tangible difference as an ERP super-user.
  • Culture & Team: A highly motivated, collegial team in a convenient location in the Zurich area with a passionate culture.
