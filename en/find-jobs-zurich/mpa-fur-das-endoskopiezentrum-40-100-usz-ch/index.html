Full description

In the endoscopy center, we carry out diagnostic examinations as well as complex interventional procedures in ambulatory and inpatient patients. For this versatile and dynamic environment, we are currently developing a new supply model. For this purpose, we are looking for committed MPAs who enjoy coordinating organizational processes, further developing existing processes and actively shaping new structures.

You want to take responsibility and build a new field of activity together with us? We look forward to seeing you.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • You coordinate patient education for diagnostic and interventional examinations and interventions in close cooperation with the medical service • You make sure that patients receive the necessary information before planned examinations and procedures • You coordinate appointments and processes with various internal interfaces, for example in interventions involving anesthesia • You are the central contact person for internal professional groups, patients and referees for organizational questions about planned examinations and interventions • You are actively involved in the development, implementation and evaluation of new processes • You contribute your ideas and experiences and support us in continuously developing the new care model Requirements • You have completed training as a Medical Practice Assistant (MPA) and bring professional experience in an acute hospital or a larger practice • You work independently, structured and reliable and keep track of several parallel tasks • You have a strong organizational talent and like to coordinate different requirements and interfaces • You are committed, proactive and open to change • You enjoy developing new structures and processes and taking responsibility for their implementation • You communicate safely and appreciatively with patients: inside, referents and different professional groups • Teamwork is important to you and you appreciate the interprofessional exchange We offer: • A versatile and responsible activity in a highly specialized university environment • The opportunity to actively develop a new care model and contribute your own ideas • Regulated working hours from Monday to Friday • Flexible working models and job-sharing opportunities • At least 25 days of holiday and additional free days depending on age • Diverse internal and external training opportunities for professional and personal development • A modern working environment with interprofessional cooperation and short paths • Pleasant working conditions in air-conditioned premises We look forward to your application and to further developing the new care model together with you.
