Full description

Canton of Zurich Financial Directorate Human Resources Recruiting Partner/-in 100% The Staff Office of the Canton of Zurich is the competence center for all personnel-relevant topics of the cantonal administration with more than 35,000 employees. We accompany managers and employees in a wide variety of HR topics and shape modern human resources work in a dynamic environment. There is a lot going on at the same time: retirements, new requirements, transformation projects and recruitment in very different areas. This is precisely why we are looking for an experienced recruiter personality who quickly takes on responsibility, is pragmatic and feels like participating in an environment with a lot of movement. We are a small recruiting team that moves a lot, likes to laugh and still works very accurately. We like people who think along, communicate openly and do not lose humor even when three processes are urgent at the same time. Your tasks

  • You are responsible for recruitment in various offices of the Finance Directorate with around 1,200 employees and control the process from the first briefing to the commitment.
  • As a sparring partner for our line, you clarify profiles, mirror the market and also say if desire and reality do not fit together.
  • You view dossiers, conduct interviews, conduct online assessments and use the results specifically for your interview.
  • You formulate advertisements so that they fit the target group and do not sound like copy-paste.
  • You actively use LinkedIn recruiters and other channels if suitable applications do not simply come by themselves.
  • You keep applicants clean in the process, communicate bindingly and pay attention to a professional candidate experience.
  • You work with different offices within the financial directorate and quickly realize: Recruiting does not work the same everywhere.
  • With recruiting topics in addition to day-to-day business, you get involved when it is burning or something needs to be brought forward. Your profile
  • Recruiting is not new territory for you. You bring in-depth experience in in-house recruiting and have an education at least at the level of specialist certificate.
  • In active sourcing, you are fit and know how to find and address suitable talents.
  • You advise managers clearly, bindingly and at eye level. Even if the desired profile, market and time pressure do not quite match.
  • Multiple parallel recruitments, changing priorities and different stakeholder groups do not get you out of concept so quickly.
  • You work quickly, structured and pragmatic, without losing sight of the quality and the candidate experience.
  • Different offices, working methods and cultures make the task exciting for you. You find yourself quickly and adapt situationally.
  • You bring assertiveness, but stay professional, solution-oriented and close to the people.
  • Digital recruiting tools, SAP SuccessFactors, LinkedIn recruiters or similar systems can be used safely or quickly.
  • In German you communicate clearly, safely and in line with the target group. Our offer
  • A task where your experience counts from day one.
  • Different offices, departments and functions that make your recruiting day versatile.
  • A small team that tackles, thinks and can laugh even when it is a bit turbulent.
  • Flexible working, modern working conditions and places of work that are easily accessible with public transport. Interested? Then we should get to know each other. We look forward to your application! Please note that we only consider applications received via our online tool. We do not need a classic letter of motivation. We are more interested in how you think, work and approach recruiting topics. Therefore, applicants answer short questions in the online form. Keep your answers short, clear and meaningful. For further information, Vincenza Scopelliti (Recruiting Partner) is at your disposal at the telephone number +41 43 257 66 62. Your new environment: Personnel Office Canton of Zurich For this position we do not consider applications from recruiters.

Pensum: 100%
