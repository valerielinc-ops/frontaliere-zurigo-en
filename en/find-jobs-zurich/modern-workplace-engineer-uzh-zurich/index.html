Central computer science

Job opening immediately, indefinitely

Central Computer Science is the computer science competence center of the University of Zurich. The Managed Device Services team is responsible for the managed workstation for Windows, macOS and mobile devices, with Microsoft Intune as the basis. We design and automate this platform ourselves instead of implementing customer projects. We offer flexible working hours, home office and the employment conditions of the Canton of Zurich.

Tasks

  • Collaboration in the conception, planning and implementation of Intune-based modern workplace topics for Windows and macOS in the team Managed Device Services
  • Significant contribution to the further development of the cloud-based endpoint management platform for Windows, macOS and Windows 365 workstations
  • Management of mobile devices (iPadOS and Android) via Intune, including enrollment, app deployment and compliance
  • Further development and optimization of intune configurations, policies, deployments and security specifications for different workplace scenarios, including based on the Open Intune Baseline and the CIS benchmarks
  • Operation and further development of the existing zero-touch deployment with Windows Autopilot and Apple Automated Device Enrollment including the Enrollment, SSO and Compliance scenarios
  • Application deployment and lifecycle for Windows and macOS, mostly automated via existing repositories and patch management tools, with proprietary packaging where an application requires it
  • Automation of client management processes via the Microsoft Graph API and n8n
  • Creation of technical concepts, documentation and decision-making basis as well as support for knowledge transfer towards support

Profile

  • Completed higher computer science education (HF, FH), a comparable qualification or corresponding practical experience in endpoint management
  • Several years of proven, in-depth experience in endpoint management with Microsoft Intune for Windows, macOS, including Windows Autopilot, Apple Automated Device Enrollment and MDM and MAM concepts
  • In-depth knowledge of software packaging, software distribution and application lifecycle management for Windows and macOS
  • Experience with open intune baselines (CIS), macOS configuration profiles (platform SSO, FileVault, declarative device management) and privileged access management on the client
  • Very good knowledge of PowerShell and the Microsoft Graph API for automating client management processes, plus good knowledge of Microsoft 365 and Entra ID
  • Advantages include knowledge of Patch My PC, RoboPack, Winget, Munki or Installomator, workflow automation such as n8n as well as experience with Entra Hybrid Join and co-management
  • Structured, independent and quality-oriented working methods and strong communication skills
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English
