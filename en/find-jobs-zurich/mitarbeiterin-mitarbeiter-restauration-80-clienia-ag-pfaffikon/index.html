Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With more than 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric offer of Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in ambulatory, day-clinical and inpatient settings. The Group employs around 1800 people and achieved a turnover of CHF 240 million in 2025.

Together with our team, you ensure the well-being of our residents, relatives, employees and external guests. For the self-service restaurant of our institution we are looking for 1. December 2026 or by agreement a dedicated, warm and service-oriented personality as

Our experienced team convinces with dynamism, cohesion and joy at work. We are looking for an open and flexible personality who keeps an overview even in hectic situations and actively shapes our special working environment.

Our self-service restaurant offers 66 indoor seats as well as a summer terrace with 70 seats and a beautiful view of Lake Zurich and the Alps. We are there every day from 08:00 to 17:00 for our guests – also on weekends and public holidays.

Their tasks

  • Care and advice of our residents, guests and employees
  • Preparation, attractive presentation and sale of our products
  • Ensuring cleanliness and order in the work area
  • Operation of the cash register and implementation of the cash statement
  • Support for banquets and seminars

Your profile

  • Service experience
  • Joy of contact with people and a pronounced service orientation
  • Flexibility and readiness for early and late services (07:30 – 17:30) and weekend services

Our offer

  • Early and reliable service planning for optimal planning reliability
  • A warm team with strong cohesion and appreciative cooperation
  • A varied and meaningful working environment
  • Opportunities for personal and professional development
  • Various perks, e.g. Reka
  • Regular team events and shared experiences

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
