Original description

In this versatile position within our Research and Development department, you will translate the product needs into algorithmic specifications for various audiological functionalities within Sonova’s executable audiological model, “EXAM.” These functionalities enable hearing aids to be optimally fitted to customers’ individual needs.

More about the role

  • Develop audiological algorithms, models and software prototypes in our executable audiological model (EXAM)
  • Based on study outcomes propose algorithmic changes of audiological functionalities
  • Conduct measurements and evaluate the results
  • Analyze big data
  • Verify implemented functionalities
  • Support Marketing with product launches and the preparation of publications

More about you

  • University degree in a scientific or technical discipline, such as Physics, Electrical Engineering or similar, PhD is an advantage
  • Experience in modelling complex relationships and applying statistical methods, as well as knowledge of Python, MATLAB and object-oriented software development
  • Experience with and interest in AI-assisted software development
  • Knowledge of psychoacoustics, electroacoustics or signal processing is an advantage
  • Proactive, goal- and solution-oriented personality with strong systems-thinking skills
  • Enthusiasm for working in an interdisciplinary team
  • Fluent English skills are mandatory
  • German language skills are highly desirable

More about what we offer

As one of the world’s leading hearing care providers headquartered in Switzerland, we’re committed to building an inclusive culture. We want to create an environment where you can balance a successful career with your commitments and interests outside of work, through our flexible hybrid working model. We offer a wide range of training opportunities for both your professional and personal development, and there are exceptional growth opportunities with individual development plans.
