ETH Zurich is the study, research and workplace of around 30,000 people from over 120 countries. The Department of Physics (D-PHYS) pursues a diverse research agenda and a broad educational programme, from research into fundamental principles of nature to innovative technologies for society. A large part of the approximately 1,500 Bachelor, Master and PhD students, postdocs and professors, learners as well as scientific and service employees are involved in experimental research in one way or another.

The technical companies have as their main goal the optimal support of the research and teaching tasks of the department. The associated D-PHYS-Shop provides the researchers with around 5,000 technical articles. This service on Campus Hönggerberg facilitates everyday research and thus makes a significant contribution to efficient processes. The competent advice of customers in the selection and application of these articles is a central pillar of the service.

Job description

For the team in the shop of the Department of Physics, we are looking for a customer-oriented personality who enjoys a versatile and broad field of activity.

  • Responsibility for the product groups Machine elements, tools and installation materials. This includes ensuring availability, purchasing from suppliers, checking appointments and assortment maintenance.
  • Technical advice to researchers and technical staff
  • Provision and output of articles (online orders and on-site purchase)
  • Incoming goods, price and invoice inspection including the annual inventory
  • Maintenance and support of the Internet catalogue and the database. Support for the continuous optimization of master data and processes as part of a system migration
  • As part of the planned move to the new research building HPQ, further tasks in the area of logistics and organization can be co-designed

Profile

  • Completed technical apprenticeship with experience in purchasing or logistics
  • Secure handling of MS Office (Word, Excel) and user knowledge in ERP systems (e.g. SAP)
  • Technical knowledge in the areas of: machine elements, tools, installation materials, laboratory, electrical and electronic articles is also advantageous.
  • Very good German and English skills
  • Customer-oriented, team-ready and friendly
  • They value a creative leeway and bring a high degree of personal responsibility
