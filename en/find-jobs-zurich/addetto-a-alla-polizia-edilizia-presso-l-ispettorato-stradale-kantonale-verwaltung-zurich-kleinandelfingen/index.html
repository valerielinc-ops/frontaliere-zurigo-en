Full description

The Canton of Zurich is constantly developing. New construction projects along state roads bring with them different interests and challenges. At the Civil Engineering Office (TBA), we assess construction projects, provide clarity in the development and ensure that the development of our canton can be reconciled with the requirements for a safe and functioning state road infrastructure. You independently examine and assess building applications with regard to road law requirements as well as development, installation and environmental plans. You are involved in design and quarter plans as well as other spatial planning procedures. In addition, you will advise clients, planners and authorities, develop expert opinions and support the Head of the Road Region in special questions and clarifications. You have completed basic technical training, preferably as a draftsman or in a comparable field of expertise, and you have knowledge of construction and planning. Legal and normative requirements interest you and you can safely read and judge technical plans. You work independently, responsibly and carefully, have analytical thinking skills and convince through your communication skills as well as a good written and oral expression. Our offer

  • Varied and responsible activity at the interface between construction, law and road safety
  • High personal responsibility and independent work
  • Versatile exchange with builders, planners, municipalities and other specialist bodies
  • Close technical cooperation with the legal service and the building police officers of the other road regions
