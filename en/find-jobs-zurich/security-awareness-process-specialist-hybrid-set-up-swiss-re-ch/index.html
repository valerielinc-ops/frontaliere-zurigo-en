Security Awareness Process Specialist (Hybrid set up) — Swiss Re

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Join a team of security and technology professionals and help Swiss Re to fulfil its mission in making the world more resilient. As the Security Awareness Process Manager, you'll support the development of Swiss Re's information security & risk awareness programme, establish a communication plan to get across security awareness topics, and coordinate proprietary trainings to educate employees on the importance of staying safe in the digital world. What's more, you'll be working in a hybrid setup, perfectly balancing work from home and the office premises.

About the team

Group Security & Technology Platforms (GSTP) brings together security, technology platforms and operational resilience to provide secure, reliable and scalable foundations for Swiss Re's business. The function ensures that security, resilience and engineering quality are embedded by design into how technology is built, operated and evolved across the organisation.

The Strategy & Operations team – which you will be a direct part of – drives GSTP's overall strategy and operations, acting as a hub for its continuous improvement with a strong focus on culture, ambition and mandate.

We're looking for a resourceful individual skilled in communications and reporting, preferably with an information security background – one who will embody the value of security culture and awareness as a whole.

In your role, you will…

  • Support the development of Swiss Re's security & risk awareness programme
  • Coordinate, prepare, and roll out campaigns, trainings and awareness-related activities in close coordination with other departments to increase security awareness for all Swiss Re employees
  • Establish annual change and communication plan for security awareness topics and support the rollout of communication posts and promotions
  • Support the execution of the Phishing Awareness programme, evaluate the results and publish articles on the results and/or awareness measures of the simulated phishing attack simulations
  • Assist in periodic updating of all Security trainings and regularly review all targeted security trainings in coordination with subject-matter experts
  • Support the rollouts and communications surrounding targeted security trainings for specific staff and further develop the security training programme with business authorities
  • Manage and ensure regular monitoring and metrics reporting on awareness topics
  • Support your team with ad-hoc operational tasks
  • Be someone who believes in continuous innovation, is curious and steadfast in finding a better way every day

Your qualifications

Nobody is perfect and meets 100% of our requirements. If you, however, meet some of the criteria below and are curious about the world of teaching employees about cyber security's importance and raising their awareness, we'll be more than happy to meet you!

  • What we need from you
  • 2-4 years of experience in cyber security or related fields
  • Passion, drive, and a belief in the value of security culture as an enabler of business performance
  • Understanding and basic knowledge of cyber security concepts and practices
  • Experience with Microsoft Office and the ability to build spreadsheets and reports
  • Experience in Marketing/Communication or related fields – preferably with a strong focus on IT security or equivalent background
  • Must have experience with different methods of communications, such as social media, blogging, videos, online messaging, printed materials, hosted & virtual events, etc.
  • Keeping the oversight and monitoring of the different cyber security related projects, campaigns and initiatives
  • Project management experience and the ability to plan and manage an organisation-wide security & risk awareness programme
  • Ability to take the initiative, get in touch, and coordinate with stakeholders across different departments in the company – collaboration and partnering with others are a key factor to success!
  • Nice-to-have
  • Experience in developing and handling eLearning courses via LMS (learning management system) and handling phishing simulation platforms
