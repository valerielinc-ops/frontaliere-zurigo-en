Full description

We are looking for an ambitious university practitioner for the Faculty of Building Consultancy Monument Preservation as of 1 January 2027 or by agreement for 12 months.

Tasks

  • They do research in archives and libraries and write about buildings in Zurich.
  • They provide documentation for advice on heritage conservation.
  • They take on administrative tasks, organize meetings and create minutes.
  • They support the preparation of content related to public relations and the organisation of the European Days of the Monument.
  • You create final reports about completed conversions for our webpage.
  • They accompany the building consultant on construction site dates.

Profile

  • You are about to complete a degree in architecture or art history or have successfully completed your studies in the last 12 months.
  • Basic knowledge of architectural history and architectural vocabulary
  • Good written expression in German
  • Experienced use of the common computer programs (MS 365)
  • Interest in the building culture of Zurich and understanding of overarching urban contexts
  • Motivation to engage within the administration for the concerns of the city
  • Independent, precise and collegial working methods

We offer

An instructive internship, diverse activities and exciting insights into the largest city administration in Switzerland await you. In addition, you will become part of the community for university interns in the city of Zurich, benefiting from attractive employment conditions, good social benefits, targeted internal education and training, flexible forms of work, and a centrally located workplace.

About us

What would Zurich be without its architectural heritage? As an important part of strategic urban planning, we conservators actively shape the future of Zurich. We are committed to ensuring that the important monuments and townscapes in our growing city remain as intact as possible or can develop further in quality with regard to the valuable building substance.
