With more than 150 employees, the Clinic of Cardiology is one of the largest clinics within the University Hospital. To strengthen our team, we are looking for you as a team member in the field of outpatient disposition. In this position, you are an important team member in outpatient disposition. You are responsible for the entire patient process – from assignment to consultation hours planning to the sending of reports. You love working independently and are used to acting in a very lively environment and being proactive. You also feel comfortable communicating with many different people. If you also bring a large portion of service orientation and team spirit, then we look forward to your application documents.

Are you our new team member?

  • You are proud to hold a commercial EFZ degree and/or have basic medical education with professional experience in healthcare, preferably acute medicine
  • Ideally, you can have further training as a doctor/hospital secretary or knowledge of the Swiss health care system? Great, then you fit perfectly into our team!
  • You are already familiar with tasks such as the scheduling of various special consultation hours and the entire patient process
  • You love to communicate and like to take responsibility for the main phone line of our heart center – our poster child
  • You assist in sending out doctor reports
  • You are open for further secretarial tasks, where you can use your knowledge specifically

We offer you an interesting, versatile and responsible task in an innovative working environment characterized by high professionalism and appreciation. A motivated and committed team awaits you.
