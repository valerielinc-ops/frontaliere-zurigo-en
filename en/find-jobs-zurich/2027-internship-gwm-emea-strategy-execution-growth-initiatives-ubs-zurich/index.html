Full description

Your role Interested in working in finance, specifically as a wealth management professional serving clients, but not sure where to start? Do you want to work for a firm with a truly global footprint that consistently ranks among the world's top financial institutions? Are you a natural problem solver who enjoys connecting the dots and finding creative ways to improve how things are done?

We’re looking for an ambitious student to join our 2027 Off-cycle Internship Program within our Global Wealth Management business, in the GWM EMEA Strategy Execution & Growth Initiatives team.

You’ll get to

  • build an understanding of our industry, culture and strategy, while learning about how we operate to effectively meet the needs of our clients
  • develop professionally by establishing a network of mentors and business contacts we believe are some of the best in the business
  • collaborate with different teams on strategic projects and key initiatives, improving our client experience, driving engagement and supporting GWM EMEA’s priorities and growth ambitions
  • engage with various professionals from around the bank, from our front units to functions like finance, sales, marketing and business risk
  • gain invaluable insights into EMEA’s wealthiest families and individuals, from the source of their wealth to their investments and financing needs
  • attend educational events led by senior leaders that provide extensive exposure and insight into the business

Your team You’ll be working in our Global Wealth Management team in Zürich. We provide advice-based solutions to deliver products and services designed to address the needs of ultra-high net worth and high-net worth individuals and families. We’ve successfully built the number one wealth management franchise in the world. You’ll work with the best talent in the industry as we help manage, preserve and grow the wealth of our clients.

You will join the GWM EMEA Strategy Execution & Growth Initiatives team within Chief of Staff GWM EMEA in Zürich. The team acts as a partner to senior business leadership, supporting the development of business priorities, strategic decision-making and the execution of key growth initiatives across GWM EMEA. You’ll also work closely with business representatives in booking center Switzerland and in domestic locations across GWM EMEA, giving you first-hand exposure to how strategic initiatives are shaped and implemented in practice.

Your expertise

We’re looking for a candidate who

  • has completed at least 4 semesters of a bachelor’s degree, and graduated no longer than twelve months ago (bachelor’s or master’s degree)
  • has a competitive minimum GPA of 4.5 according to the Swiss grading system
  • has excellent English (written and oral), with any fluency in other languages being a plus
  • has strong analytical and conceptual skills, and enjoys solving problems and connecting the dots
  • has values that align with ours: hard-working, trustworthy, dedicated and collaborative, and is a strong team player
  • communicates clearly and is comfortable sharing ideas in a simple and structured way
  • is motivated to work in a business with high demands and tight deadlines, with a well-structured and result-driven way of working
  • is comfortable using Excel and PowerPoint to analyze information and create clear presentations, and is open to using new tools (including AI) in a responsible way to work more efficiently

Start Date: February 2027

We’re looking for someone who’s curious and wants to thrive in a business that never stops moving. We welcome all majors, but it’s important to have an interest in the financial industry. We want to see what makes you unique and discover what you can bring to our team. Perhaps you’ve organized a fundraising event or taken part in team sports? Or stepped out of your comfort zone to learn about a subject you found challenging? Let us know how your achievements match the skills we’re after.

Your program This paid internship will last 3-6 months, depending on your availability. You’ll work with our experts and learn about the industry, our clients and our firm. You’ll be right at the heart of our business, learning from your colleagues, taking part in day-to-day operations. You'll be given mentors along the way and will have the chance to get to know some of the most senior members of our teams.

You’ll have plenty of opportunities to develop new skills and make contacts along the way. If you like challenges, enjoy learning and want to work where no two days are the same, this program is for you.

If you have a successful internship, you may get an offer to return for our Graduate Talent Program after you've completed your studies.
