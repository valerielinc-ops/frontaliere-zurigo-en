Full description

What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family company with over 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessories as well as the provision of financial services. Emil Frey Digital AG is part of the Emil Frey Group and is a full-service communications service provider for the automotive industry and responsible for digitalization within the Emil Frey Group. To strengthen our Paid Media Team in Zurich Oerlikon, we are looking for an analytically strong and structured personality as Paid Media Performance Manager.

  • You convert customer-approved media plans completely and up-to-date into annual plans for all paid media channels including offline media
  • You maintain and version annual plans with planned, booked and actually used budgets
  • You will perform plan-actual reconciliations, analyze budget and spend deviations and develop well-founded correction proposals
  • You create forecasts for budgets, spending, performance metrics, goal achievement and commitments
  • Ensure reporting governance via digital and offline channels and ensure consistent definitions, data quality and timing
  • You check cross-channel reports as well as suppliers of internal teams and external partners for completeness, plausibility and quality
  • You create initial performance analyses and optimization approaches as a basis for decision-making for strategy & planning and the management of your profile
  • You have at least five years of professional experience in Paid Media, Media Planning, Performance Management, Campaign Operations or Reporting Governance
  • You have experience in a media agency or a complex in-house media organization with cross-channel media budgets and multiple brands or customers
  • You have in-depth practice in annual planning, plan-actual matching, budget and spend control, forecasting and commitment monitoring
  • You know relevant paid media platforms such as Campaign Manager 360, Display & Video 360, Google Ads and Meta and can critically assess campaign, tracking and performance set-ups
  • You work safely with Excel and can efficiently apply complex data, formulas, pivot evaluations and forecast models
  • You think analytically, work very accurately and readily complex issues understandable, structured and ready for decision
  • You communicate stylishly in German and very well in English and enjoy working with internal teams and external partners What we offer you You will find a new challenge with exciting tasks and much more:
  • Numerous discounts (new and used cars, service work and spare parts, vehicle and health insurance, fuel cards, hotels and much more)
  • An own, attractive pension fund as an independent pension institution
  • A long-term business perspective
  • Individual training and development opportunities
  • At least 25 days of vacation How to contact us We look forward to the full application (e.g. CV, employment certificates, etc.) via the link "Apply now!".
