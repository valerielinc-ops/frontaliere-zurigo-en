Shape your future – together with us!

Would you like to actively shape the future with us? Developing innovative solutions together? If yes - then you are right with us! We offer a working environment in which thinking is required, teamwork is a high priority and flexibility is encouraged. Support us on the way to a successful future - committed, courageous and open to new things. We look forward to you and your participation and are convinced: “Together we make the difference.”

In the area of customer operations, we design the individual experiences of our customers in the event of damage and performance. They are our focus and together with them we find the right solution. In this way, our customer-first mentality ensures maximum customer satisfaction.

This is what awaits you

  • In this position, you will assess damage to motor vehicles both on the screen and on site and prepare comprehensive technical damage assessments.
  • You will negotiate with customers and repair technicians to achieve optimal solutions
  • You will analyze cover issues from a technical perspective and make informed decisions
  • In addition, you determine the time values of passenger cars, trucks, motorcycles, agricultural vehicles and equipment as well as young and vintage cars
  • By checking repaired vehicles and checking invoices, you ensure the quality and performance of our services
  • You actively expand your internal and external network and maintain it through regular exchange and cooperation
  • With your proactive attitude you contribute to the continuous improvement and further development of our business processes

You bring that with you

  • You have an education as an automotive mechatronics specialist / in or carrosseriespengler / in EFZ as well as a further education as an automotive diagnostician / in or workshop coordinator / in
  • You bring several years of practical professional experience in customer service, as a workshop manager or plant manager
  • You master the German language in word and writing, further language skills are an advantage
  • Your strengths lie in a strong sense of service and a high level of communication skills
  • You have in-depth knowledge in dealing with common IT tools and use them efficiently
  • You are characterized by flexibility, independence and a good organizational ability
  • You are a committed and motivated personality with a strong entrepreneurial mindset
  • You are based in the main area of activity
