Full description

Do you know us? We are a Zurich-based company with competences in sanitary, heating and air conditioning technology. With our location in Dübendorf and our approximately 12 employees, we are active throughout the region. We belong to the Burkhalter Group, the leading provider of cross-trade building technology in Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein.

Are you ready for the next career step? Then we should get to know each other! We are looking for a service technician in heating, 100%.

Their tasks

  • Installation services of heating equipment for new construction and conversion
  • Complete handling and coordination of small conversions
  • Execution of service work and repairs
  • Readiness for piquetting services
  • Heating maintenance services

Your profile

  • Completed training as a heating installer and some years of professional experience in the heating sector
  • Enjoyment of customer contact with sales flair and independent working methods
  • Careful and professional appearance and good manners
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Team-capable, punctual and reliable personality
  • Ticket Cat. B

We offer them

  • Varied activities in a collegial team
  • Modern workplace in good traffic conditions
  • Modern service wagons
  • Regional activity
  • Advanced working conditions and training opportunities

Your contact
