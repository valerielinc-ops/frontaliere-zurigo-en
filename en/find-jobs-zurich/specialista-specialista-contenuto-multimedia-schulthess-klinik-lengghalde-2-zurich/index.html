Full description

Tasks

  • You identify relevant topics, plan them strategically and develop compelling stories for our various communication channels.
  • You design and realize cross-media storytelling – from the idea to implementation on digital channels as well as in print media such as brochures, flyers or other publications
  • You independently produce high-quality content in text, image and video, organize photo and video shoots and continuously develop our image and video library
  • As a sparring partner, you support the strategic development of our social media channels, observe trends and bring new ideas and formats into our communication.
  • You advise internal stakeholders on communication issues and work closely with external partners such as photographer, videographer and media
  • You plan, coordinate and develop our sponsoring activities – from cooperation with partners to implementation and success monitoring.

Requirements

  • You have an education or training in communication, journalism, multimedia production or a related field and have several years of experience in corporate communication or content creation
  • You are a passionate storyteller and develop topics creatively and in line with the target group – from the first idea to implementation via various channels
  • You independently produce high-quality content in text, image and video. Dealing with the Adobe Creative Cloud, Canva and the production of high-quality video recordings – also with the iPhone – are part of your everyday work routine
  • You coordinate communication projects confidently, work in a structured and secure manner in cooperation with internal stakeholders and external partners
  • You enjoy trying out new technologies and bring your first experience in using AI tools for content creation and creative processes
  • You communicate stylishly in German, have good knowledge of English and are enthusiastic about new formats, digital trends and the further development of our communication

We offer

  • Flexible working hours/models
  • 40 hourly week
  • Possibility for home office
  • Attractive social benefits and numerous benefits
  • ZVV BonusPass / ZVV BonusPass Flex

The Schulthess Clinic in Zurich is one of the leading orthopaedic clinics in Europe with over 1,100 employees. Our central concern is to free people from their pain and restore their mobility. As a specialist clinic, we focus on surgical orthopedics, neurology, rheumatology and sports medicine. Schulthess Clinic is Swiss Olympic Medical Center.
