Shape your future – together with us!

Every success story begins with faith in oneself.

As AXA, we see ourselves as a partner who stands by your side and believes in you. That is why we challenge and promote you and proactively show you possibilities for your future. Write your own success story with us now. Know you can.

For us, everything revolves around what is most important to us: the fulfillment of living dreams. For over a hundred years, we have been helping people to realize their small and big dreams. We are looking for a committed and empathetic communication talent who is independently responsible for a mortgage portfolio and maintains a clear and transparent communication with our customers. We offer you flexible working hours and a modern working environment.

In the area of finance, we have the responsible task of controlling the finances and risks of AXA Switzerland and managing them reliably. This creates the basis for strategic business decisions and the future of the company.

Our asset management focuses on our external and internal customers. It focuses on their needs and accelerates and simplifies processes to their advantage.

This is what awaits you

  • You independently manage an exciting mortgage portfolio and are the competent, personal contact for our customers, the sales organization and broker
  • You will advise our customers comprehensively in all matters relating to mortgages and financing – especially in the case of extensions, adjustments and new financing
  • You actively maintain and develop existing customer relationships, recognize new financing needs and develop individual, customer-oriented solutions
  • You assess loan applications carefully and critically, review mortgage applications under internal guidelines, and ensure compliance with legal and regulatory requirements, as well as the necessary customer and compliance clearances
  • You actively support your manager in the development and acquisition of institutional customers

You bring that with you

  • It gives you pleasure to gain new customers and to offer them real added value through your advice
  • You have successfully completed commercial apprenticeship as well as several years of experience in mortgage consulting and processing
  • You have extensive knowledge in the mortgage business, in the valuation of real estate as well as the Swiss mortgage market, mortgage products, strategies and pricing methods
  • You work independently, structured and responsible and distinguish yourself through a pronounced customer and service orientation
  • Fluent language skills in German are a must
  • Knowledge of at least one second national language (e.g. French or Italian) is a big plus
