Full description

Our rehabilitation clinic Limmattal offers specialized musculoskeletal and neurological rehabilitation including surveillance rehabilitation and accident rehabilitation at a high medical level. Through targeted diagnostics and individually tailored therapy programs, our interdisciplinary team ensures that our patients are optimally looked after on their way back to better health and quality of life. Due to the close cooperation with the Hospital Limmattal and the direct connection to the medical emergency unit, prompt acute somatic treatment is always ensured even in the event of emergency complications.

For the expansion of surveillance rehabilitation we are looking for you!

This makes the job special

  • Patient-oriented care: care and care of our patients in rehabilitation who need continuous and professional supervision
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: Through close cooperation with various disciplines, you contribute significantly to holistic treatment and rehabilitation
  • Responsibility: You take on joint responsibility for the quality of care and the further development of processes. Together with the team, you ensure a high quality of care both on the monitoring unit and on the 12 stepdown patients.
  • Regular training: We offer you regular external training so that you can keep your expertise up to date

Your profile

  • Training: You have completed training as a qualified nurse / qualified nurse with additional training IMC or intensive care, alternatively an equivalent training with at least one positive SRK PreCheck.
  • Experience: You already have experience in an IMC or intensive care unit in the Swiss healthcare system
  • Personality: You are an enthusiastic and communicative personality with a sense of humor. You convince through a high sense of responsibility, high-quality work and can set priorities effectively.

What we offer

  • Co-creation: In our modern and professional working environment, you have the opportunity to actively contribute your nursing expertise and to help shape the further development of care.
  • Inspiring and collegial working atmosphere: Working in a supportive and appreciative team
  • Above-average social security benefits: We offer you attractive, above-average social security benefits
  • Training and further education: Your professional and personal development is important to us – we therefore participate generously in training and further education
  • Attractive additional services: Use discounts and exclusive discounts at various partners, such as wholesalers, car dealers and pharmacies
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other benefits at the FORTYSEVEN spa.
