System Engineer

Zurich | working from home up to 40% possible | Reference 8098

Are you passionate about securing digital ecosystems at scale? Do you want to design and operate missioncritical cryptographic services in a regulated financial environment?

Join our Cryptographic Services team and help build secure, scalable platforms that protect SIX’s core infrastructure. You will work closely with application, infrastructure, and security teams to deliver trusted cryptographic solutions across hybrid environments.

What You Will Do

  • Design, implement, and operate enterprise cryptographic services (PKI, HSM, certificates)
  • Automate certificate lifecycle and integrate cryptography into applications and CI/CD pipelines
  • Contribute to the enterprise cryptographic inventory and visibility across assets
  • Support vulnerability remediation and incident resolution for cryptographic components
  • Ensure compliance with security standards and regulatory requirements (e.g., PCI DSS, FINMA, DORA)

What You Bring

  • Strong expertise in PKI, TLS, HSM, or key management technologies
  • Experience with automation, APIs, or infrastructure-as-code
  • Understanding of cloud and modern application environments (e.g., Azure, containers)
  • Ability to translate security requirements into practical engineering solutions
  • Fluent in English (written and spoken)
