Full description

Do you like working with people? Do you want to work with our team in the kitchen for the health centers for Old Witikon and Riesbach from January 1, 2027?

Tasks

  • They take over the professional support and training of one to 6 apprentice chef EFZ and kitchen assistants EBA, as well as for our pre-interns in the kitchen.
  • They independently plan and coordinate the learning support days, learning situations, document the learning process, provide feedback and assess them verbally and in writing.
  • They support the kitchen team in their daily work in the kitchen and during events and banquets.
  • They independently prepare fresh and high-quality food for our residents, employees and external guests and comply with the quality and hygiene guidelines as well as occupational safety.
  • They are ready for irregular working hours and weekend services.

Profile

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as a cook and vocational trainer
  • Joy to motivate and accompany learners in their development
  • Passion for the profession, high quality standards, willingness to provide services and flexibility
  • Teamwork, openness, commitment and very good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Good computer skills, especially in MS Office applications and the learning platform WIGL

We offer

Enjoy an exciting field of work in a professional and team-oriented working environment with a du-culture throughout the company. The human being is at our center. That is why we support the reconciliation of private and professional life, different working models make it possible.

As part of the city of Zurich and due to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, progressive employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

Depending on the experience, the annual salary for this function is between CHF 70’300 and CHF 93’936 (100%).

About us

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

The health centers Witikon and Riesbach are run jointly and are located in the middle of Zurich-Witikon and Zurich-Riesbach in districts 7 and 8. The Health Center for Old Witikon brings together various care and care services under one roof. The Riesbach Old Age Health Center is fully focused on the treatment, care and care of people with dementia. The runaway house offers peace, security and security. Both health centers are characterized by many years of experience in age medicine. Here you work together with 400 colleagues.
