Full description

We are the pulse of Zurich and keep the city in motion every 7.5 minutes. In order for this to work reliably and smoothly, a lot moves behind the scenes of the VBZ. Our colleagues work in over 50 professions: in technology, IT, administration, traffic development, offer planning, infrastructure and many other exciting areas of responsibility.

Tasks

  • As a superstructure manager on site on the construction site, you manage the creation of electrical systems for very complex bus and railway technology projects (costs, deadlines and quality) with a wide variety of internal and external trades. These include, for example, traction power supply, switch and control technology, catenary line (VBZ), switchgear systems, electrical installations of railway technology, video systems, return and stray current protection measures, locking systems and door systems.
  • Your activities in SIA phases 4 and 5 (tendering and realization) are conducted according to private sector principles taking into account the urban framework conditions, usually in the project team of the Civil Engineering Office of the City of Zurich (TAZ) and on behalf of the project manager VBZ.
  • In addition, you will carry out conceptual activities according to defined goals and develop coherent problem solutions (e.g. construction phase planning for large projects). You also transmit complex and delicate content.
  • The processing, assessment and awarding of contracts and services to third parties as well as cost management (order and invoice control, post-calculation) are also part of your area of responsibility as well as the responsibility for project and product documentation (also change documentation).
  • The quality acceptance according to internal VBZ guidelines of the SIA, the creation of QS documentation as well as the basic creation for construction and material procurement and the organization of all components required for construction make you happy.
  • You lead catenary projects on our VBZ network for trolleybus and tram independently.
  • You provide valuable strategic leadership support for the team leader and head of realization in all aspects of the realization department.

Which also includes

conducting difficult internal and external negotiations. The readiness for special missions in the event of widespread disruptions or special events and individual missions at night and on weekends.

Profile

  • You have completed an apprenticeship in the electrical industry.
  • You have completed your further education with a Bachelor in Electrical Engineering FH.
  • You can also have a master’s degree in construction, electrical or mechanical engineering.
  • Ideally, you will have a higher technical examination (diploma) as a construction manager or a diploma in HF electrical engineering/control engineering.
  • We require knowledge of Swiss law (construction industry) and in-depth expertise in the field of railway technology.
  • In the best case, you also bring in-depth knowledge in the management and realization of projects for electrical systems in the industrial environment, ideally railway systems.
  • We expect experience in project team management and construction management from you.
  • High personal responsibility and performance orientation, healthy resilience and flexibility are required.
