Sales Internship (Trade Repository)

Madrid | Working from home up to 40% | Reference 8124

We are looking for a motivated Sales Intern to support our sales team in business development and client relationship activities for our Trade Repository area. This internship offers hands-on exposure to sales processes, financial markets, regulatory reporting, and client engagement while providing valuable learning and development opportunities.

Don't hesitate and apply now. We are looking forward to hear from you!

What You Will Do

  • Support the sales team in daily commercial activities with prospects and clients.
  • Assist in prospecting and lead generation, including research and outreach.
  • Prepare client presentations, proposals, and marketing materials.
  • Maintain and update CRM systems with accurate client and pipeline data.
  • Participate in client meetings and calls, taking notes and delivering agreed follow-ups.
  • Coordinate internally with product, marketing, and operations teams.
  • Support the preparation of sales reports, performance tracking and KPI’s.

What You Bring

  • You are studying a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Business, Finance, Economics, or a related field.
  • Strong interest in financial services, capital markets, and regulatory reporting.
  • Excellent communication skills in English and in Spanish.
  • Analytical mindset, attention to detail, and proficiency in Excel and PowerPoint.
  • Previous exposure to sales, banking, financial markets, or CRM tools (e.g., SAP) is a plus.
  • Proactive, client-oriented team player with strong organizational skills and a willingness to learn in a fast-paced environment.
