Full description

To strengthen our support team, we are looking for 1. December 2026 a qualified specialist 50% for a middle school regular school. They work for 3 days (Mo, Di, Do) during school operations, as well as for 3 to 5 hoarding weeks.

Tasks

  • Care of children during lunchtime and leisure activities
  • Co-responsibility of day-to-day operations in the hoard (pedagogy, household, general organisation, administration)
  • Promoting children's strengths and independence
  • Constructive cooperation interdisciplinary and with parents
  • Participation in school development

Profile

  • Diploma Social Pedagogue or EFZ FaBe
  • Professional experience in caring for children in hoarding is an advantage
  • Positive human image
  • Strong sense of responsibility as well as a high level of self-reflection and networked thinking
  • Flexibility and independence as well as a high level of team and communication skills
  • Solution-oriented attitude and humor in daily work
  • Readiness for interdisciplinary cooperation

We offer

  • A versatile activity in an exciting environment
  • A motivated, committed and professional team
  • The cooperation in a well-established care team
  • Support from the management
  • Salary and social benefits according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich

About us

The Zurich-Letzi school district comprises Stadtkreis 9 with the districts Albisrieden, Altstetten and Grünau. In the 16 schools of our rapidly growing school circle, more than 5,000 primary school students are taught and supervised. More than 1,200 employees work for them in our schools in the areas of teaching, care, home service and technology as well as administration. They all work daily for the interests of the students and a smooth school day.
