Executive Education of the Faculty of Economics

Job opening immediately or by agreement

The Executive Education of the Faculty of Economics of the University of Zurich offers a wide range of further education for national and international specialists and executives. Our portfolio includes certificate programs, customized company programs and executive MBA programs. We stand for scientifically sound, practical and reflective training, which is conveyed with high quality and great commitment. In the Executive Education of the Faculty of Economics and Business Administration, you can expect an entrepreneurial environment with a lot of room for manoeuvre and personal responsibility. Our offer includes programs in Business Administration, Finance, Real Estate and Computer Science. You can expect a varied and responsible activity in an exciting and socially relevant environment. Modern and flexible working conditions, a wide range of further development opportunities and participation in innovative projects offer you the opportunity to contribute your ideas and actively make a difference. Become part of a dynamic and innovative team and shape the future of further education with us!

Tasks

  • Conceptual development: Take responsibility for the study programs in the field of computer science. Develop existing curricula in terms of content and didactics and design new modules and programs together with Professor.
  • Acquisition and support: Actively promote your programs and gain new training participants. During the implementation, you accompany and look after the participants professionally.
  • Budget and Resource Planning: Prepare the program budget and account and represent the programs to various bodies.

Profile

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree, preferably in the field of computer science or business informatics.
  • You are intellectually curious, critical of the status quo, working in a structured and networked way.
  • You enjoy working on new topics and developing creative solutions that you implement independently and in a team.
  • You are flexible, show your own initiative and take responsibility.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English (C2) in word and writing.
  • You enjoy working in a team and actively engage in collaboration with various stakeholders in order to advance projects together.
  • You perform competently and gainfully, have a solid understanding of the excellent university environment and work precisely and structured.
