Full description

Your area of responsibility: Do you feel at home in HR and would like to focus on vocational training? Then you are exactly right with us! To strengthen our HR team, we are looking for a committed, flexible and service-oriented personality who actively supports our Head of Education and at the same time takes on a wide range of tasks throughout HR.

  • Support of the Head of Education in all administrative and organizational matters
  • Coordination and support of learners throughout the training cycle
  • Planning and coordination of training dates and training courses
  • Maintenance and management of training documentation
  • Contact person for trainees, vocational trainers and internal positions in administrative HR issues incl. Telephone service
  • Support of the HR team in day-to-day operations if required
  • Preparation of various personnel-relevant documents and participation in projects Your profile:
  • Completed basic commercial training, initial HR experience or further training as an HR assistant* is advantageous
  • Interest in vocational training and enjoyment of dealing with young people
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Organizational talent as well as a high service orientation
  • Flexibility and willingness to take on different HR tasks if necessary
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Enriched by your reliable, flexible and helpful nature You are our team
  • Team-capable, communicative and helpful personality with high IT affinity What we offer you:
  • Working independently in a motivated and collegial team that supports each other.
  • A place of work with the best connection to public transport, as well as cost-effective staff parking spaces.
  • Diverse benefits with great employee discounts.
  • A pleasant working atmosphere in an attractive location near Zurich, which has a lot to offer you both professionally and privately. Physical applications cannot be considered or returned.
