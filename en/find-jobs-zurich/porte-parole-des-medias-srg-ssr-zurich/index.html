Full description

The Media Management Department of SRG is looking for a new Media Spokesperson with a focus on corporate and program topics from German-speaking Switzerland as of December 2026 or by agreement as a result of an internal change of the current job holder.

This is your contribution.

In this role, you contribute to the understanding and performance of SRG’s public service mission and assume the following tasks:

  • They develop and maintain close relationships with media professionals, are the first point of contact for media inquiries and organize media conferences
  • They are responsible for active media work in various projects, coordinate interviews and support and accompany SRG executives and SRF moderators.
  • They ensure reputation monitoring in the media of German-speaking Switzerland. You observe the reporting, analyze current developments and share your assessments within the company
  • They develop and are responsible for the communication measures for external target groups in various SRG projects, including sports-related topics, and are responsible for controlling and publishing media-relevant communication on the SRF programmes.
  • They work in the Corporate Newsroom and take on various tasks according to a set cycle, in particular those as boss:in vom Dienst (CvD). In this role, you coordinate and take responsibility for the daily communication measures across all channels

They inspire us.

  • You have a university degree or equivalent education and experience in communication and media work, as well as managing cross-sectoral projects and teams
  • They have in-depth knowledge of the media landscape and corporate communication, audiovisual production and SRG
  • Mother tongue German, very good oral and written knowledge of a second national language
  • You have a very good general education, an interest in sport is beneficial
  • You can prepare complex topics quickly and appropriately for target groups
  • They have a pronounced quality and service orientation, are resilient and flexible

So we inspire you.

  • Varied activity in the heart of a media service public company
  • Diverse and responsible tasks in a newly assembled, multilingual team
  • A dynamic and exciting working environment

Please submit your application documents (motivation letter, CV and portfolio) via the “Apply now” button. The application period ends on 4 November 2026.

Our promise

What does it take to reach the people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expertise, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can contribute and develop. For the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: Welcome to the world of SRG!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, promote creativity and offer a lot of design space for new and high-quality products.
  • Shaping digitalization: Quality journalism is also digital for us. That’s why we explore new possibilities and experiment with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-medial. We design on the pulse of time and on the nerve of the audience.
  • Being human: We meet each other collegially, work independently and shape our working atmosphere individually. Our success is based on the personal strengths as well as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programs. In doing so, we act respectfully and independently; in this way we promote opinion-forming and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.
