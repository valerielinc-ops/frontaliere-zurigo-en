Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,000 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland.

For the Senevita Alterzentrum Gehren in Erlenbach we are looking for a specialist health EFZ 50-100 % Your tasks

  • You like to care for older people professionally and individually according to their needs.
  • You are jointly responsible for the quality of care, the support during the assessment and the RAI documentation.
  • You care about the self-determined and fulfilled life of the inhabitants and you promote them.
  • You support our training manager in the training of learners.

You bring that with you

  • A training as a health specialist EFZ.
  • You enjoy taking care of older people.
  • You are dynamically on the road and like to develop our company further.
  • You have professional experience in inpatient long-term care and with people with dementia development.
  • RAI or careCoach are familiar to you.
  • You work goal-oriented and can thus support the qualified nurse in the assessment and in the development of the measures.
  • You enjoy improving and securing the quality of care and can also inspire your team.
  • You work independently, reliably and accurately and appreciate innovation.
