Full description

The ETH Domain is facing significant organisational development. As part of FIT for the Future, the administrative services of Empa, Eawag, WSL and PSI (4 Research Institutes) are strategically aligned for the future. The aim is to create comprehensive, integrated and digitally oriented service organizations. Senior Program Manager (100%) For the management of the program office and as a central key function for the program management, we are looking for an experienced person with a proven ability to lead complex transformations in the context of scientific excellence and professional, scalable operating models to success. Their role

  • They significantly steer the transformation at the interface of strategy and implementation and ensure that this ambitious structural reform is effectively implemented.
  • Together with the research institutes, they design and implement the target operating models and integration architectures for finance, HR and IT.
  • Translate strategic decisions into actionable programmes with realistic decision-making and governance structures
  • They shape the implementation of the transformation and ensure prudent and effective change and stakeholder management
  • They are sparring partners of the programme management and also promote topics where interests diverge and decisions are politically sensitive. Your profile
  • Master's degree in business administration, business informatics or similar discipline
  • Several years of experience in the design and implementation of transformations in complex organizations (public context, science or large organizations of advantage)
  • Demonstrate success in cross-organisational integration projects such as the establishment of shared services, harmonisation projects or the development of new operating models
  • Strong ability to develop viable and feasible solutions in politically shaped and stakeholder-intensive contexts
  • Ability to safely switch between strategic concept, governance issues and operational implementation
  • Proven leadership experience in demanding environments with highly qualified specialists
  • High personal sovereignty, decision-making strength and assertiveness
  • Very good knowledge of German and English Our offer An exceptional key task awaits you with high visibility, great design possibilities and direct influence on the strategic realignment of the service organizations of the four research institutes of the ETH Domain.
