Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Statutory Office District of Zurich Auditorate ( criminal law) 100% The Zurich Statutory Office is responsible for numerous tasks in the district of Zurich as a decentralized cantonal administrative unit. The focus is on the prosecution of violations. We are looking for a legal trainee or a legal intern (100%) for the cooperation in our criminal law department as of 1 September 2026. Your duties

  • You are assigned to the Head of the Criminal Law Department and are responsible for criminal investigations in the field of infringements of the StGB and secondary criminal law
  • Evaluation of advertisements, drafting of criminal decisions and recruitment orders, participation in procedural proceedings, etc.
  • Preparation and execution of investigations and preparation of forced action Your profile
  • Completed legal studies and preferably with judicial experience
  • Interest in criminal and criminal law in particular.
  • independent, efficient and precise working methods
  • agility in oral and written expression
  • friendly and safe appearance and good manners
  • absolute discretion, reliability and teamwork What we offer
  • well-founded introduction and support in the work
  • place of work in the centre of Zurich
  • employment according to the auditor's regulation
  • partial credit for the practice year for the examination of the lawyer Your application For further information, please contact the head of criminal law, Davide Dalla Bona, (043 258 58 32). Further information can be found here. We look forward to receiving your online application.
