Full description

Canton of Zurich State Chancellery for Business Informatics, 80 - 100% Are you interested in public administration in the digital transformation? Are you collaborative and self-employed in your professional environment? Do you want to actively shape the digitalization of the cantonal administration in your area of activity? Then we look for you! The State Chancellery advises and supports the Government Council and the Presidium in the performance of government tasks. It acts as an interface between the Directorates and as a staff office provides services that ensure the smooth functioning of the Government Council as a collegiate authority. The staff of the State Chancellery unites important internal services such as human resources, finance or computer science. To strengthen our ICT team, we are looking for a personality as business informatics specialist, 80-100%. Your tasks

  • Analysis and management of existing business processes, modeling of new or optimized processes and collecting the relevant requirements.
  • Cooperation in further developments in the ICT environment in cooperation with the specialist departments.
  • Development of concepts and implementation within the framework of projects in cooperation with internal bodies or external service providers.
  • Management and implementation of projects in the renewal of existing services and specialist applications.
  • Responsibility for technical operation and updating of specialist applications.
  • Support and assumption of tasks in providing and ensuring internal support services.
  • Assumption of deputies and tasks in cantonal expert committees and projects. Your profile
  • Higher degree at tertiary level in business informatics or a related field as well as several years of experience in project management.
  • Analytical skills and pleasure in an environment in which a high degree of professionalism and independence is required.
  • Ability to understand technical problems and their effects holistically.
  • Implementation-strong, structured and communicative personality with an IT flair.
  • Professionally experienced and flexible personality with a wide range of interests and applications as well as enjoyment of a wide range of tasks and topics.
  • Experience in business analysis or process management.
  • Further training in the areas of business analysis, process management and/or project management, preferably according to the Hermes method, is desired.
  • A few years of experience in the public sector are beneficial.
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German. We offer you an interesting and responsible task in a dynamic environment in which the digital transformation can be experienced in all dimensions. We offer contemporary working conditions and a workplace in the immediate vicinity of Zurich Central Station. Are you motivated to get involved in our organization? Then we look forward to your online application.

Pensum: 80-100 %
