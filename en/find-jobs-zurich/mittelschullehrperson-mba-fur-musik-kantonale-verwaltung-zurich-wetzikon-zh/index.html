Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School Zürcher Oberland Secondary School Teacher mbA for Music 70 - 80% The Cantonal School Zürcher Oberland (KZO) in Wetzikon is a lively school that values an open culture, shared commitment and an inspiring learning environment. She currently leads a high school secondary level (9th/10th school year) in 60 classes and all Zurich maturity profiles. With around 1300 students, 200 teachers and 40 employees in administration and operations, the KZO is one of the major high schools in the canton. A rich musical life takes place at the KZO, which we would like to continue to cultivate. Now we are looking for a secondary school teacher mbA for music (70-80%) at the beginning of the school year 2027/28. According to the Zurich secondary and vocational school teachers ordinance, it is a permanent employment with special tasks (mbA). What we expect:

  • You have a Master’s degree in School Music II (Master of Arts in Music Pedagogy School Music II) or an equivalent diploma for teaching at high school schools according to MAR.
  • You are a versatile committed musician, have experience in project work (choir conduction, orchestra conduction, bandleader etc.) or are ready to embark on an appropriate training. In addition, you bring competences in different music styles as well as broad professional experience.
  • They are motivated to teach both students in the basic subject and the main subject and are ready to be present at the KZO for at least 4 days per week.
  • They bring solid practical teaching skills, especially in piano and singing.
  • You have teaching experience at the high school level and want to get involved in our school (supervision of mature work, participation in commissions, conceptual development of the subject of music). For further information please refer to Mr. Thomas Stecher, Adjunkt, telephone number 044 933 08 16. We look forward to your online application until October 22, 2026. Please submit your documents exclusively via the online tool.

Pensum: 70-80 %
