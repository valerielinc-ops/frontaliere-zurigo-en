Tasks

Personal administration

  • Independent handling of all entry and exit processes
  • Organisation and support of recruitment and employment processes
  • Supervision of temporary employment and coordination of contract extensions
  • Processing of wage mutations and administrative personnel processes
  • Maintenance and control of personnel data and evaluations (e.g. wage data, holiday overviews, timesheets, membership lists)
  • Management of access authorisations and key orders

Cooperation and consultation

  • Advising and supporting professors and third-party funders in personnel-relevant issues
  • Close cooperation with institute controlling, the central personnel department of the University of Zurich and external authorities (e.g. Migration Office, Office for Economic Affairs and Labour)
  • Ensuring correct and timely personnel administration in compliance with university and legal requirements

Requirements

Your qualifications and skills

  • Completed training or further education in Human Resources (e.g. HR certificate or equivalent qualification) is a prerequisite
  • Several years of professional experience in human resources, ideally in a public administration, university or comparable educational or research institution
  • Knowledge of public or cantonal personnel law is an advantage
  • Enjoying an independent, exact and responsible way of working
  • Pronounced service orientation and stylish communication skills
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing

We offer

  • A varied and responsible job in a renowned university research institute
  • A collegial, appreciative and international working environment
  • Modern working conditions at Campus Irchel
  • Contemporary employment conditions in accordance with the guidelines of the University of Zurich
  • The opportunity to actively shape processes and take on responsibility
