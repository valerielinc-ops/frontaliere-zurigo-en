Full description

For the Accounting Group, we are looking for a new colleague immediately or by agreement.

Tasks

  • They process the daily receipts in the electronic creditor workflow independently (quality assurance / booking) and maintain master data in SAP (including creditors).
  • You are responsible for the processing of reminders (incl. coordination in case of bankruptcies), management of the group mailbox, booking of incoming payments (incl. monitoring / warning of open items), as well as the creation of payment proposals in the team (job rotation).
  • You are responsible for the processing (including regular coordination with other service departments) of city-internal receipts / payment run in the team (job rotation) as well as create evaluations from SAP (including recovery and monitoring of double-entry invoices, SKWF dashboard).
  • You advise the employees in the group and internal / external customers / project managers in accounting business cases, as well as in the development of solutions to the questions.
  • Collaboration in annual accounts, in accounting projects, as well as in SKWF training courses and walk-ins.

Profile

  • Basic commercial training with further training as a clerk/accounting (Diplom edupool) or comparable qualification and several years of experience in clerical accounting, ideally in public administration
  • Very good knowledge in the application of SAP and MS-Office tools
  • Good organizational skills as well as an analytical, goal-oriented and result-oriented working style, structured and efficient working method with high quality awareness and precise time management
  • Independent, motivated personality with a high commitment, a sense of responsibility and a good understanding
  • Strong team and communication skills, as well as a high level of service orientation, flexibility and commitment, especially in the event of a heavy workload

We offer

It offers you a challenging and responsible task in a professional, future-oriented environment and a motivated team. We offer attractive employment conditions as well as development and further education opportunities and a job in the center of Zurich. Flexible working hours and a generous home office regulation enable a good work-life balance.

About us

Public buildings give people space for encounter and exchange, for development and future. They shape the cityscape and are a valuable cultural asset. In the role of the client representative, the Office for Building Constructions develops and realizes high-quality buildings for a liveable and urban Zurich on the way to net zero.
