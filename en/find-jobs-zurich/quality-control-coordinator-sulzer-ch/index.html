Full description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

We are looking for a Quality Control Coordinator to join our team in Burgos, Spain. In this role, you will play a key part in ensuring that incoming materials, manufacturing processes, and finished products meet internal, customer, and regulatory quality requirements. As a quality professional, you will coordinate quality control activities throughout the production cycle, working closely with suppliers, production teams, engineering, procurement, and project stakeholders. Your contribution will help maintain product conformity, strengthen quality performance, and support operational excellence. This is an excellent opportunity for an autonomous and detail-oriented professional who enjoys working in a manufacturing environment and influencing quality standards across the organization. What you will do:

  • Review and validate supplier quality documentation, material certificates, and inspection records.
  • Coordinate incoming quality control activities and support supplier quality issue resolution.
  • Define and maintain quality control standard operating procedures across production activities.
  • Lead critical quality inspections and support in-process inspections.
  • Monitor manufacturing quality performance and identify deviations from requirements.
  • Ensure compliance with internal procedures, customer specifications, and applicable standards.
  • Document inspection results and maintain accurate quality records.
  • Collaborate with production, engineering, procurement, and project teams to ensure product conformity.
  • Coordinate interactions with external inspectors when required.

What you need to succeed

  • Experience in manufacturing quality control environments.
  • Strong knowledge of quality control practices and quality documentation processes.
  • Experience reviewing material certificates and supplier quality documentation.
  • Experience using ERP systems and quality management systems.
  • Ability to interpret technical specifications, test certificates, and quality standards.
  • Experience conducting incoming, in-process, and final inspections.
  • Familiarity with welding, painting, dimensional inspections, and manufacturing processes.
  • Strong organizational and documentation skills.
  • Ability to work independently and manage priorities effectively.
  • Fluent Spanish and professional English.

What we offer you

  • A competitive salary as well as participation in the variable remuneration system based on personal and financial objectives.
  • Meal vouchers.
  • Access to a voluntary flexible compensation scheme allowing employees to optimize their salary package, such as health insurance, transport, or childcare.
  • Be part of an experienced and international team.
  • A good vibe working environment.
  • The opportunity to grow your career within a solid and well-established international company.
  • Be part of a dynamic organization undergoing continuous transformation and improvement.
  • Office location: Burgos, Spain.
  • Additional benefits may apply depending on the position and location.
