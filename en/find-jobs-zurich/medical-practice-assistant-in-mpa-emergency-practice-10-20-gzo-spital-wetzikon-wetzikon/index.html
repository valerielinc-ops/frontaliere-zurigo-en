Full description

The GZO Spital Wetzikon is an innovative acute care hospital in the Zurich Oberland with 150 beds and around 900 employees. As a company with high customer orientation and appreciative communication, we are committed to our patients on a daily basis. The GZO is in a transformation phase. The estate deferral provides the necessary time for the development of a comprehensive restructuring plan. Operation continues normally. Do you want to support us in this extraordinary situation and actively contribute? To complement our team, we are looking for a dedicated MPA.

These are your tasks

  • Independent organisation of emergency practice and ensuring smooth operation
  • Triagulation of all incoming calls via the emergency number
  • Comprehensive care of patients in emergency practice
  • Preparing and helping with wound care and minor septic and aseptic local interventions
  • Preparation, cooperation and follow-up of special diagnostic and therapeutic measures such as various laboratory tests, monitoring, ECG writing etc.
  • Independent performance of laboratory analyses
  • Creation of all customary orthoses and dressings
  • Ensuring the sending of patient reports
  • Responsibility for correct performance documentation
  • Completion of general administrative work
  • Economical and correct handling of equipment and materials, medicines, material order The family doctor emergency practice is open at the following times: Monday to Friday, 18.00 – 22.00 hrs (MPA 16.30 – 22.30 hrs) Sa/So/holiday, 09.30 – 22.00 hrs (in shift operation) You bring that with you
  • You have the diploma as a medical assistant and bring several years of professional experience
  • Coordination of consultation hours with patients You have a lot of joy
  • You understand how to react agilely to new situations
  • You perform the assigned tasks well and ensure the high quality
  • You like to work on the patient and keep an overview even in hectic situations
  • You are an independent, reliable and open personality, have good MS Office user knowledge
  • You can use the German language stylishly
  • Very high flexibility in operational planning and short-term requests for service coverage We offer a very interesting and versatile field of activity in an advanced, dynamic and multi-professional working environment, which is characterized by high quality, respect and appreciation. With us you can develop further and benefit from internal and external training opportunities. Various discounts and internal offers are just as much a benefit as an excellent staff restaurant, a connected KiTa and various events for our employees. Further benefits can be found here.
