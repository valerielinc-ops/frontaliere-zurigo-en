Fire protection specialist (m/f/d)

80%-100%, Zurich, permanent

The Department of Safety, Health and Environment (SGU) advises, supports and trains all organizational units and members of ETH Zurich on questions relating to safety and health at work. It is the point of contact for all centrally organized measures in dealing with hazards and risks for the protection of people, animals, property values, infrastructure and the environment as well as for questions regarding the fulfilment of the corresponding legal requirements. In the event of an incident, it shall maintain an appropriate emergency organisation. Both in everyday life and in the event of an incident, it works closely with the authorities and external forces.

In the field of fire and explosion protection (BEx), we are responsible for the ETH-wide organization and coordination of structural, technical and organizational measures: this includes, for example, the development and updating of internal concepts, structural and technical specifications and leaflets, leadership or participation in integral safety concepts as well as control of their implementation. We also provide information and training for ETH members and contribute our expertise to construction projects. The digital documentation of the quality assurances carried out is also one of our tasks.

Do you like to tackle, think and bring the necessary expertise? In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed and knowledgeable personality as a fire protection specialist (m/f/d).

Job description

  • Consulting & support: Accompanying construction projects at ETH Zurich as well as specialist advice from the Academy, project managers and specialist planners in all fire protection issues
  • Solution development: Development of practical fire protection solutions coordinated with ETH Zurich in coordination with authorities and stakeholders
  • Concept Review & Optimization: Review of existing and new fire protection concepts and proactive submission of improvement proposals
  • Coordination & Interface: Central contact person for fire protection topics and coordination of cooperation within and outside the department for construction and project enquiries
  • Document management: Review, creation and ongoing updating of fire documentation
  • Quality assurance & controls: organization and implementation of internal and external fire police controls as well as acceptance of events at ETH Zurich
  • Construction acceptances: Accompanying construction acceptances from the point of view of the building authority and ownership to ensure quality management
  • Explosion & Hazardous Substances Protection: Advising academy and operation on explosion protection, safe handling and proper storage of hazardous materials (especially combustible liquids and gases)
  • Rulebook development: Development and review of concepts and internal guidelines for increasing security at ETH Zurich
