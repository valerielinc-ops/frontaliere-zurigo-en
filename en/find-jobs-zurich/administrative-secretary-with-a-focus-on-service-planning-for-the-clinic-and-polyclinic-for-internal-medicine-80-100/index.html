In the Clinic and Polyclinic for Internal Medicine, we combine the inpatient with the outpatient assessment, treatment and care of patients with internistic diseases. With us, you work in a dynamic team of experienced colleagues who are happy to work for the well-being of our patients.

Their main tasks

  • Preparation of service plans of our senior and assistant doctors: inside
  • Coordination of absences and holidays of our assistant doctors
  • Failure management
  • Entrance and rotation management of the assistant doctors: inside
  • Analysis and optimization of the operational planning processes in the PEP
  • Active role in the upcoming introduction of Swisio
  • General administrative tasks e.g. billing, reporting, etc.

Your profile

  • Completed basic commercial training or comparable training
  • Experience in healthcare
  • Further training HR assistant: in desirable
  • PEP knowledge imperative, Swisio knowledge beneficial
  • Interest in labour law issues
  • Handling MS Office Programs
  • Engaged, flexible and resilient personality with very fast comprehension
  • High social skills, hands-on mentality and assertiveness
  • German mind. at level C1
  • Readiness to start work at 7 a.m.
