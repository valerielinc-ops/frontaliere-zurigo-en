Vocational trainer/vocational trainer Professional/health specialist at the interdisciplinary private station 1 — Spital Zollikerberg, Zollikerberg (ZH).

Area: Nursing professions.

Welcome to your new place of work.

Ms. Larissa Huber Stv. Station Manager, offers you an attractive job with a fee of 80-100% as an

Vocational trainer / Vocational trainer Professional / Health specialist at the interdisciplinary private station 1

If you too want to make a difference in people’s lives, these are your tasks:

  • Professional support as well as targeted promotion and development of FaGe learners, practice-oriented, concept-based and individually adapted to the learning process
  • Together with the learners, ensure competent and holistic care and care for our patients and create diverse learning opportunities in everyday work, mainly in early service.
  • Actively participate in the management team of the station in the further development of training and bring in educational topics
  • Together with the vocational training team, further develop the training on the station and strengthen the care team in the accompaniment of FaGe learners
  • Continuously develop your own vocational pedagogical competence through professional exchange, networking and active participation in network training

Become part of the Department of Emergency and Acute Medicine at the Zollikerberg Hospital. Under one roof, we combine the areas of internal medicine, surgery, nephrology with dialysis center, the emergency department, the eye center, the medical ambulatorium, the day clinic and other specialized areas. Every year, the Clinic for Internal Medicine looks after more than 2,500 inpatients, while the Clinic for Surgery looks after more than 1,500 inpatients. With more than 6,000 emergency consultations and more than 450 patients in the interdisciplinary intensive care unit, we offer a versatile and demanding medical working environment.

On our private station you can expect an exciting and challenging field of activity in the disciplines of general surgery, orthopaedics, gynecology, urology and plastic surgery. We work according to the reference care system and create an environment in which personal and individual care of our patients is the focus.

In our 360° tour, get a first impression of your new place of work.

To make you feel comfortable with us, many benefits are waiting for you. Just to name a few:

  • Flexibility in the organisation of working time: participation in the scheduling of the service in order to contribute personal needs (in cooperation with the planning manager)
  • Focus on further education: We generously support you financially and time-wise in your further education – so that you are always up-to-date
  • Fit and active: Use our fitness room on the Zollikerberg site for free and stay healthy and motivated.
  • Green bonus

We offer an eco-bonus of up to CHF 650 per year for environmentally conscious action

  • Own pension fund with super-compulsory participation of the employer
  • Exclusive discounts on telephony, Internet and TV at Salt and Sunrise

  • Completed training as FaGe EFZ as well as further training in the field of vocational training (SVEB) or the willingness to complete it
  • Professional experience of at least one year, preferably in an acute hospital
  • Joy in accompanying, inspiring and specifically promoting learners in their professional development
  • Commitment, resilience and the willingness to continuously develop and actively shape change
  • Enjoying an appreciative interprofessional cooperation
