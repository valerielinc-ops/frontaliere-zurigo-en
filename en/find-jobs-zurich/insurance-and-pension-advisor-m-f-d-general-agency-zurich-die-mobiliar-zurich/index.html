What awaits you

In this position, as a sales person, you are where our customers are – and there when it matters.

This is what they do with us

You are responsible for supporting and expanding a private and company portfolio.

As the first point of contact for customers, you advise them personally and offer individually tailored solutions from the entire insurance and pension sector.

You ensure your success through active networking and representation in the Zurich-Stadt agency area.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training preferably in the commercial sector
  • Well-founded several years of professional experience of advantage in insurance and pension consulting
  • Enjoyment in attracting new customers through active networking
  • Authentic and winning personality
  • Self-employed, responsible and customer-oriented working methods
  • Diligent knowledge of German in words and writing, further language knowledge is a plus

Working with us

We offer you a challenging, varied and independent activity in an exciting environment.

In order to perform this function, the Insurance Supervision Act requires the conclusion of an insurance intermediary/in VBV, Profil Allbranche. If this is not available, we are happy to support with tailor-made internal training.

This is what we offer

Participation in success

If we are successful, our employees also benefit. Each year, the distribution of a successful share and its amount is decided.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as Cicero certifications for a high quality of consulting and consulting competence.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer up to 25% discount on bonuses, discounts on mobile phones and free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
