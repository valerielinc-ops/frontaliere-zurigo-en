A future-oriented and modern non-profit organization, which with around 80 employees on behalf of ten municipalities ensures the care and care for customers of all ages at home for around 32,000 inhabitants. A satisfying task that is fun. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a competent, humorous and service-oriented Businesswoman EFZ focus HR, Pensum 60-80 %

To make you feel comfortable with us, we offer

  • A responsible, varied and independent work
