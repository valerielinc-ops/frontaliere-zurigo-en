What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family company with over 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessories as well as the provision of financial services. At the headquarters in Zurich-Altstetten is the central administration of the Emil Frey Group, which controls the overarching activities. The insurance department is the central point of contact for all questions regarding the Group’s insurance and is also responsible for all Automotive Insurance products of Emil Frey AG Switzerland.
