Full description

Between psychiatry and somatics: They connect both worlds where it counts the most. In Winterthur, we are looking for a senior physician or a senior physician immediately or by appointment. a senior psychologist in the field of consiliar and liaison psychiatry. She expects a versatile job with a high level of independence in an inspiring, interdisciplinary team. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • They lead and supervise assistant doctors and psychologists in the work with patients who suffer mainly from disorders of categories F3 to F6 according to ICD-10
  • They perform treatments in the ambulatory and consiliar psychiatric area
  • You will lead a special consultation session for mental illnesses in the context of pregnancy and childbirth (gynacosychiatry) in cooperation with a cantonal hospital

This is what they bring with them.

  • Recognition as a specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy or as a specialist psychologist
  • Previous experience in the field of consiliar and liaison psychiatry or psychosomatics is advantageous
  • Interest in interdisciplinary collaboration in multidisciplinary teams

That sets them apart.

  • Resilient, independent and congruent personality
  • High team and communication skills
  • Joy of versatile, independent work at the interface of psychiatry and somatics

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Interesting, versatile work with high independence
  • Inspiring working atmosphere in a competent and stimulating team
  • Promotion of professional development by financing in-depth training

Additionally for applicants from the EU

  • Support for MEBEKO recognition of your diploma
  • Assistance in organizing your journey to Switzerland
  • Assistance in finding accommodation through our internal rental department
