Full description

Whether on the road, sea or in the air – our highly trained employees transport and store valuable goods and valors with the highest safety and precision. This ensures that valuables arrive safely, on time and as quickly as possible. Become part of our team and shape the future of logistics!

What you move

  • Based on the scheduling of the schedule, you deliver the shipments in the Valoren area reliably and on time within Switzerland and Europe
  • Loading and unloading? No problem for you - you get involved and make sure that everything is safely stored
  • Together with you, your vehicle is the most important and always visible business card of our company – so you take care of its care and maintenance
  • You stay up-to-date through regular training and develop constantly

Why do you succeed?

  • You have the driver's license Kat. C or CE in the bag and already have professional experience in transport
  • Technology and vehicles excite you, and you love being on the road in the truck and keeping logistics running
  • Security experience is beneficial, but not a must
  • An impeccable reputation (ADMAS, no entries in the enforcement and criminal register) is essential for this responsible task
  • Your geographical knowledge within Switzerland and Europe is top – you will always find the best way
  • You communicate safely in German in word and writing, and other national languages or foreign languages are a plus
  • You are reliable, discreet and, through your resilience, ready to go full speed even in stressful moments

Your Benefits

  • Family culture: You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • Central introductory training: You will spend your first working days in the central introduction, where you will be taught the most important basics
  • Generous expense regulations: With our expense regulations you are well taken care of

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
