Full description

Your heart beats for older people and your passion belongs to gastronomy? You love good food, like to take on responsibility and want to make a difference with your culinary art? As a team leader / in kitchen & production, you bring your experience, your passion and your leadership talent – develop new ideas, set culinary accents and bring enjoyment to your plate together with your team.

For the Tertianum Letzipark in the Zurich region we are looking for from 01.12.2026 or by appointment a

Team Leader / Team Leader Kitchen & Production 100%

In this key operational role, you take over the leadership of the kitchen team and, together with the kitchen cluster responsibility, ensure high-quality production for the Tertianum Letzipark and the associated sites. In your function, you are under the management of the hotel industry.

Their tasks

  • Operational responsibility for the kitchen sector
  • Leadership, operational planning, development and training of employees in the entire kitchen area
  • Organization, coordination and assurance of timely production and delivery of the connected sites including quantity, quality and interface control
  • Control of production volumes, control of goods costs and active reduction of overproduction and food waste
  • Cooperation in menu planning as well as implementation and further development of recipes, production specifications and portion sizes
  • Active participation in the kitchen as well as continuous development of operational processes
  • Implementation, monitoring and compliance with quality standards, HACCP concept, occupational safety guidelines, audit requirements
  • Active management of the CIP process using Kaizen tool
  • Co-responsibility for purchasing goods, budget and controlling management, inventory and quality management
  • Technical support and training of kitchen learners
  • Close technical cooperation with the cluster responsibility kitchen and those responsible for the supplied locations
  • Ensuring smooth cooperation between F&B, front office, care and management of the hotel industry
  • Close cooperation and direct reporting line to the management of the hotel industry
  • Deputy in the absence of the management of the hotel industry (service and kitchen)

Your profile

  • Several years of leadership experience in a comparable kitchen function, ideally in the community gastronomy or a health institution
  • Further vocational training completed
  • High service orientation and visible presence in the operational kitchen area
  • Good MS Office knowledge and a high affinity for digital kitchen, ordering and planning systems
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, creativity and high quality and service standards
  • Resilience, assertiveness and at the same time appreciative leadership style
  • A positive, solution-oriented and humorous basic attitude
  • Category B ticket for vehicles with manual transmission is a requirement
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
