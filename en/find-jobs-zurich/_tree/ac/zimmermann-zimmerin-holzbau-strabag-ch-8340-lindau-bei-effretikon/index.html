Full description

You don’t just want to work on wood, but make a difference with it? With us you build on quality, teamwork and real prospects. Pack in, bring your know-how and become part of a team that creates great things together – in timber construction, for the future. What matters to us

  • Successfully completed vocational apprenticeship as a carpenter:room with good specialist knowledge
  • Reliable, flexible, quality-conscious
  • Precise and clean work
  • On time and good manners
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Cat B driving licence advantageous

Your contribution to us

  • Self-employed work on construction sites in new construction or conversion
  • Establishment of elements
  • General woodwork
  • All incurred Zimmermann's work

Our added value for you With us, a young, dynamic team awaits you with real cohesion, joint barbecues and relaxed evening chat. We are actively investing in the future: Our new, large wooden construction hall is currently being built in Lindau, where you work as a carpenter with state-of-the-art equipment. You can expect exciting, challenging timber construction projects from erection to the finished building as well as varied missions on the construction site and in the hall. We rely on fair employment conditions as well as further training that will help you advance in your craft. Become part of a company that lives the carpentry trade and builds the future.
