Behind the scenes in indirect procurement ensure that our store runs? Welcome to the winning team. You negotiate smart, optimize costs and make services transparent. Want to make a difference with us? Denner. This is our store.

Luogo di lavoro: Denner AG, headquarters, Grubenstrasse 10, 8045 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • As a business partner to advise and accompany the needers in all matters of procurement and negotiation competence
  • Identify and realize optimization potential within the framework of independently managed purchasing initiatives
  • Design and further develop strategies for the product categories under management
  • Ensure professional supplier management and continuously optimize the supplier portfolio
  • Manage and monitor contracts
  • Actively manage stakeholders and work in cross-functional teams with different committees, departments and specialists

Requirements

  • At least 5 years of professional experience: in a related capacity
  • Completed basic or further education: in business administration, supplemented by a purchasing specialization
  • In-depth purchasing experience in various categories of goods, with a focus on procurement in the field of logistics and shop construction (e.g. facilities and technical services)
  • In-depth understanding of strategic procurement
  • Strong negotiating skills
  • Sovereign and winning appearance among very different stakeholders
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • English (advantageous)

  • Migros Bank: Better off with interest and fees on your private, investment or mortgage accounts.
  • Holidays: 6 weeks holidays for all and even more days for service anniversaries.
  • Mobile subscription: Spoiled for choice: With Galaxus Mobile, Wingo or Sunrise already from CHF 10.- one or the other really connected.
  • Flexible place of work / home office: One to two days a week working at home. Except in IT - we are even more flexible.

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Discussion with case presentation: Specific expertise is examined on the basis of a case or other assessment instruments. After processing, the result of the leader is presented and then discussed together. Usually, the team is also represented at the presentation. As a rule, the conversations are conducted by the superior, without a representative from the HR. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition: After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
