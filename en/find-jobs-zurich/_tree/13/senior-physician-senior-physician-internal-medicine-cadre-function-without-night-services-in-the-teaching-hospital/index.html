Become a senior physician or senior internal medicine in Switzerland, without night services? In the greater Zurich area, you will lead assistant doctors, supervise complex internistic cases and obtain the POCUS certificate of competence, in a teaching hospital with research options and only with background services. Her future workplace will be a large acute care hospital in the greater Zurich area, integrated into a hospital group operating nationwide. More than 300 beds, more than 1800 employees, service orders from the cantonal hospital list and highly specialized medicine as well as an own emergency department provide a range of cases that is technically demanding. Patients of all insurance classes are treated. As a teaching hospital of a medical faculty, teaching is part of everyday life here. General internal medicine is the internal hub of the house. She clarifies unclear clinical pictures, supervises polymorbid patients, performs preoperative investigations before complex interventions and, together with intensive care medicine, forms the medical emergency team that is ready for use around the clock. Anyone who thinks broadly as an internist or internist and wants to make a career will find a stage here. In addition, the city: Zurich is the largest city in Switzerland, is located on the lake overlooking the nearby Alps and has direct train connections to Munich and Stuttgart.

Their tasks

  • They are responsible for the internistic care on the bed stations, complex cases of internal medicine and interventional patients with relevant concomitant diseases
  • They guide assistant doctors professionally and personnel and actively participate in their further training at a category A training centre.
  • They work closely with special disciplines, centres and assignees, for example in consultancy, preoperative assessments and in interdisciplinary focus visits (e.g. neurology, cardiovascular).
  • They are part of the medical service. You do not provide night services, only background services
  • On request, you can participate in scientific research projects

Your Competences

  • Specialist title General Internal Medicine
  • In-depth experience in inpatient internal medicine and professional experience in intensive care and/or emergency medicine
  • Joy of leadership, teaching and interdisciplinary collaboration
  • High social skills and initiative
  • Do you have a foreign diploma? We advise you on the recognition via MEBEKO and also support you financially

Their advantages

  • Cadre role without night services: They are responsible, but only provide background services
  • Leadership career with system: Leadership development, promotion of leadership potential and support of leaders are part of the offer
  • POCUS until graduation: training up to the qualification certificate POCUS basic emergency onography, with tutor qualification in-house
  • Further education with support: There are regular internal training courses. External training courses are technically, temporarily and financially supported
  • Raise your profile: You teach at a teaching hospital, participate in research projects and make interdisciplinary focus visits. These are advantages for your next career step
  • Salary and retirement provision: You receive a 13th month salary. The pension fund (company pension provision) provides more than the legal minimum and you have private accident coverage
  • Holidays: 25 days of vacation per year, additional weeks of vacation can be purchased, and with increasing seniority, additional paid days are added
