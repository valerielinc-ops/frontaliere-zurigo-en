Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School High Promenade secondary school teacher mbA for French 50 - 60% The cantonal school Hohe Promenade is a long-school school with an old- and modern-language profile, an immersion course German / English and a strong STEM offer. Around 800 students learn at our school, teach and accompany 150 teachers and employees in administration and operations. Our humanist attitude and practice combine inspired tradition and forward-looking innovation. Committed students, the open-minded college and the latest infrastructure ensure a stimulating environment. More information about our school in the heart of Zurich in the immediate vicinity of the universities can be found at www.kshp.ch . At the beginning of the autumn semester 2027/28 or by appointment, our school must fill a course in French as a secondary school teacher mbA (with special tasks). Their tasks

  • They teach French and interdisciplinary formats at our long school and participate in project weeks.
  • They are involved in the student body and are committed to the subject in all its facets.
  • They perform special tasks within the school (class teacher office, participation in commissions and school development projects, etc.). Your profile
  • They have completed university studies at master level as well as the teaching diploma for maturity schools.
  • They bring several years of teaching experience at the high school, ideally at a long school, and stand for a demanding, contemporary and dynamic teaching.
  • They are an open, team-capable and committed personality who cares about both professional education and interdisciplinary skills.
  • They want to engage in a lively school culture. Our offer
  • In our school you will welcome a committed French student body and an open-minded college.
  • We are happy about a diverse language culture at our school.
  • We are a centrally located and modernly equipped school.
  • The employment conditions depend on cantonal specifications. Application Have we aroused your interest? Come and talk to us. We look forward to your application. Please submit your documents no later than 11. September 2026 exclusively via the online tool. For information please contact Gabriella Valisa, Vice Rector, tel. 044 224 64 64, e-mail: gabriella.valisa@kshp.ch.
