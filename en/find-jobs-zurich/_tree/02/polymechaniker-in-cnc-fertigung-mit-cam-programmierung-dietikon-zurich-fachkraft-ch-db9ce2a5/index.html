Full description

The activity includes setting up and operating complex milling and lathes. Several automated systems are monitored during work and the processes are constantly improved. For our client, we are looking for one or more activities

  • 3 and 4-axis milling machines independently set up and operate

  • Practice in setting up and operating CNC lathes and milling machines independently
  • Knowledge of CAM software such as TopSolid is desirable
  • Existing basic knowledge in ISO programming
  • Experience with Fanuc controls counts as a plus
  • Technically demanding offer
  • Tasks in a versatile environment - Flexible working hours for flexible operational planning - Attractive framework conditions
