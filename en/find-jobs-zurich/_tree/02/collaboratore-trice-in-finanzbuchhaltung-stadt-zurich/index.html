Full description

For the financial accounting team we are looking for 1. April 2027 or by arrangement an independent and responsible person.

Tasks

  • They independently process and record the creditor invoices, ensure quality and maintain creditor master data in SAP.
  • They check and record the monthly statements of funds and carry out checks on funds.
  • You are responsible for the processing of incoming payments and, if necessary, the execution of reminder runs for a service department.
  • They answer accounting inquiries from employees within the team as well as internal and external customers.

Profile

  • Basic commercial training with further training as an accounting officer or comparable qualifications as well as several years of experience in finance and accounting
  • Very good knowledge in the application of SAP and MS-Office tools
  • Strong team and communication skills with a high understanding of services and a sense of responsibility
  • Efficient and structured working methods, a good understanding and flexibility

We offer

A varied, challenging and versatile job in an exciting environment as well as a committed and motivated team. We offer good employment conditions as well as development and further training opportunities. In addition to an attractive workplace in the heart of the city of Zurich, there is the opportunity to work partly in the home office.

About us

With around 2,400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a social Zurich. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.

The Finance department is responsible for finance and accounting as well as client accounting in the areas of economic assistance and civil law measures. With 13 employees, the Financial Accounting team is responsible for accounting and preparing the accounts of the five service departments of the Social Department.
