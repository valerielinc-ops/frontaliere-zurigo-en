Welcome to the Medbase Pharmacy Glattbrugg

Our pharmacy is located in the middle of Glattbrugg and is well connected to public transport. In addition to a wide range of products from conventional medicine, naturopathy and cosmetics, we offer services such as vaccinations, health checks or consultations. We advise our large regular customers and passers-by competently and motivated. Medbase Apotheke Glattbrugg is also involved in the training of apprentices and pharmacists in the assistant year. We are a well-established team with long-standing employees and maintain an appreciative relationship with each other.

The focus of your work at Medbase is professional advice and warm contact with our customers. Embedded in a dynamic team, you focus on quality and commitment, supported by a modern infrastructure and training opportunities.

  • Competent and individual advice to our customers on health concerns
  • Active participation in day-to-day business – from recipe testing to quality assurance
  • Implementation of health services such as vaccinations or checks to promote health literacy
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with doctors, therapists and other specialists for networked care
  • Engagement in the training of learners and support for pharmacists: women in the assistant year

What you bring

  • A degree as a federal dipl. Pharmacist: or a recognised equivalent education (MEBEKO)
  • The desire to further develop yourself as part of the further education to / to the specialist pharmacist: in FPH in official pharmacy
  • A customer-oriented, responsible and independent working method
  • Joy of teamwork and an open, committed personality
  • Good IT knowledge (e.g. ProPharmaXX) as well as knowledge of German at least at level B2

The Medbase Group is one of the leading companies in ambulatory healthcare. This means services ranging from health promotion to medication and acute treatment to rehabilitation.
