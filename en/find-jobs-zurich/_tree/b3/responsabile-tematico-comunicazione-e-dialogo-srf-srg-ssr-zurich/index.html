Full description

Within the SRG, the regional unit SRF is responsible for the content-related regional orientation of the offer in German-speaking Switzerland and shapes the journalistic identity in this language region. Communication & Dialogue SRF is responsible for internal and external communication activities for the regional unit SRF. Thematic management plays a central role within the area in the coordination of SRF's program communication. Communication & Dialogue also maintains a close exchange with the supra-regional area of Communication & Public Affairs at SRG.

What are your tasks?

Their main tasks are

  • As a topic manager: in Communication & Dialogue, you are responsible for the medium- and long-term conception and support of strategic communication topics, for projects as well as for central development and transformation projects of the Information Department of SRF. The department includes all SRF information and news services on TV, radio and online.
  • They act as a link between the Communication & Dialogue department and the Information department and advise the department management, their managers and moderators on strategic communication issues. This also includes the communicative positioning of the new editorial team.
  • They identify the communicative needs, opportunities and risks and prioritize them in accordance with the company goals.
  • They create integrated communication strategies and concepts, Q&A and core messages. For strategic communication projects, you take over the project management and control the cooperation of different specialist areas and stakeholders. In addition, you implement communicative measures in consultation with the other teams and, in cooperation with SRG Media Management, actively participate in answering reputation-relevant media inquiries.

What do you bring?

  • They have a university degree or equivalent education and have several years of experience in all disciplines of corporate communication such as media work, social media and internal communication. Experience in crisis communication and in the communication of change processes is an advantage.
  • Strategic and networked thinking, personal initiative, creativity and a good feeling for communicative developments distinguish you. In addition, your mother tongue is German and you have a good knowledge of a second national language.
  • They can quickly analyse complex topics, prepare them for target groups and convey them in an understandable manner. You convince with conceptual strength and textual competence as well as a high quality and service orientation.
  • They are resilient, flexible and team-oriented. In addition, you have strong social skills, persuasion and experience in dealing with different stakeholders.

What can you look forward to?

  • An exciting, responsible and varied task with a great deal of creative leeway in the communication team of a large media company in transition.
  • On an active participation and further development of our communication activities as well as an experienced, competent and helpful team.
  • To an environment with high social relevance, in which you help shape the future of public media service in Switzerland. They accompany the further development of the information department of SRF and shape their communication in an important transformation phase.
  • On committed colleagues: women, short distances and a culture characterized by cooperation, trust and mutual respect.

What we offer

What does it take to reach the people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expert knowledge, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can contribute and develop. For the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: Welcome to the world of SRF!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, promote creativity and offer a lot of design space for new and high-quality products.
  • Shaping digitalization: Quality journalism is also digital for us. That’s why we explore new possibilities and experiment with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-medial. We design on the pulse of time and on the nerve of the audience.
  • Being human: We meet each other collegially, work independently and shape our working atmosphere individually. Our success is based on the personal strengths as well as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programs. In doing so, we act respectfully and independently; in this way we promote opinion formation and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.

Requirements

  • Sie verfügen über einen Hochschulabschluss oder eine gleichwertige Ausbildung und haben mehrjährige Erfahrung in allen Disziplinen der Unternehmenskommunikation wie etwa Medienarbeit, Social Media und interne Kommunikation. Erfahrung in der Krisenkommunikation und in der Kommunikation von Veränderungsprozessen ist von Vorteil.

  • Strategisches und vernetztes Denken, Eigeninitiative, Kreativität sowie ein gutes Gespür für kommunikative Entwicklungen zeichnen Sie aus. Zudem ist Ihre Muttersprache Deutsch und Sie verfügen über gute Kenntnisse einer zweiten Landessprache.

  • Sie können komplexe Themen rasch analysieren, zielgruppengerecht aufbereiten und verständlich vermitteln. Dabei überzeugen Sie durch Konzeptionsstärke und Textkompetenz sowie eine hohe Qualitäts- und Dienstleistungsorientierung.

  • Sie sind belastbar, flexibel und teamorientiert. Zudem verfügen Sie über ausgeprägte Sozialkompetenz, Überzeugungskraft und Erfahrung im Umgang mit unterschiedlichen Anspruchsgruppen.
