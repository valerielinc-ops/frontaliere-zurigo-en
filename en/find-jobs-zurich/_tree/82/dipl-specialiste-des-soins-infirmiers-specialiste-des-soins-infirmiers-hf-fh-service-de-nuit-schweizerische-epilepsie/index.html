Full description

The Multifaceted Day

  • During the night they bear the nursing and professional responsibility for our residents
  • They recognize and assess epileptic seizures, initiate the necessary measures and administer emergency medication according to medical prescription
  • They assess health situations and decide independently to provide medical support
  • They carry out medical-technical tasks and basic care
  • They ensure professional care documentation and hand it over to the day team
  • You guide employees on night service and support them

Their diverse talents

  • Completed training as a dipl. Nursing professional / dipl. Nursing professional HF or FH (or diploma recognised by the SRC)
  • Professional experience in care or care, ideally with people with disabilities or epilepsy
  • Safe occurrence and clear decision-making ability, even in emergency situations
  • Self-employed, responsible and calm work
  • Readiness for regular night assignments, also on weekends and public holidays
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

A responsible and meaningful job in a committed team Careful familiarization and internal training, e.g. on epilepsy and emergency management Attractive night and weekend allowances
