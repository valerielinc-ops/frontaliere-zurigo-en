This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

This workplace includes a cafeteria. The meeting place in Zurich creates an inviting gathering place for students, employees and guests. Here, our team delights an average of 350 guests with a diverse range – from freshly prepared lunch menus and regional and fair-trade foods to catering services. Are you our new team member who will support us as a buffet employee (50%) immediately or by agreement? You will work in a 50% position from Monday to Friday. Working hours are between 09:15 and 16:15. In addition, assignments at evening events are possible once or twice a year.

Your task menu

  • Welcoming, serving and advising guests
  • Ensuring a high level of guest satisfaction
  • Operating the cash register
  • Plating salads and snacks and preparing soups
  • Preparing coffee specialties
  • Closing the cash register at the end of the day
  • Preparing the mise en place
  • Ensuring an appealing presentation of products
  • Assisting with catering and special events
  • Ensuring cleanliness and order in the work environment
  • Carrying out general tidying and cleaning duties
  • Implementing and ensuring compliance with the quality standards and ZFV guidelines (hygiene and occupational safety, service, etc.)

Your recipe for success

  • Practical experience in gastronomy / hospitality
  • Progresso is an advantage
  • Good spoken German skills
  • Customer orientation
  • Teamwork
  • Willingness to learn
  • Warm, engaging and empathetic
  • Enjoyment of good food

What we offer you

We create teams that support one another and in which you are encouraged to develop. We work together and focus on everything that does us good. Among other things, we place great value on salary transparency and pay equality. For this position, at a 100% workload, depending on your experience and qualifications, we offer an annual salary between CHF 52'000 and CHF 55'000 (including a 13th monthly salary). If you would like to share your passion for gastronomy with us, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
