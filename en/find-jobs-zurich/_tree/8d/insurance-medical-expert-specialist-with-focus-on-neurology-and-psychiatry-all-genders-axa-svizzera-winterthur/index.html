Shape your future – together with us!

Would you like to contribute your many years of clinical experience and actively shape insurance medicine?

With us, you combine specialist medical expertise with professional leadership responsibility and quality development. In a collegial, experienced and interdisciplinary team, you will assess complex medical issues and further develop our medical network. You shape our insurance standards and the cooperation with our service areas. We support you specifically in your insurance and professional training.

Are you ready for the next step?

We are looking for an experienced, customer-oriented and communication-strong personality with specialist medical and insurance training.

Support us on the way to a successful future - committed, courageous and open to new things.

We look forward to you and your participation. Together we are stronger.

This is what awaits you

  • You take over the professional management and support of our Swiss-wide medical network and are responsible for quality management, insurance support and the recruitment and introduction of new colleagues.
  • You represent the medical perspective vis-à-vis our service areas and other internal partners and shape our insurance medical processes together with them
  • Together with our specialist areas, you will develop relevant medical quality and control indicators and use them for the further development of our medical service.
  • Ensure that underwriting expertise is available in the Medical Network, CoC Medical Service and other departments
  • You write technically sound and linguistically precise actuarial statements based on current literature and evidence-based knowledge
  • You actively maintain a broad professional network and expand it specifically

You bring that with you

  • You have a medical title Neurology and/or Psychiatry or equivalent foreign title
  • Sound insurance expertise (certificate SIM-appraiser)
  • Ideally, a broad, underwriting practical experience (work at Suva or MV, IV, KK, private care)
  • Many years of practical, clinical work in the Swiss healthcare system
  • With your curious, open-minded and hands-on nature, you actively shape your working environment and develop it further.
  • You are strong in communication and communicate with different stakeholders at eye level, you are happy to instruct and train
  • German stylish confident in word and writing, desirable additionally good French skills

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
