Full description

Together with our customers, we connect the real and the digital world. As one of the leading technology companies, Siemens employs around 6,000 people in Switzerland. With our pioneering solutions in the areas of building technology, energy, mobility, health and industry, we are shaping the future. Do you also want to actively participate in the pulse of time and make the lives of millions of people in Switzerland better? Then you are exactly right with us! Apply today as Product Portfolio Manager HVAC / field devices and start immediately or after your availability – your team is looking forward to you! Your new tasks

  • You assume strategic and operational responsibility for your product portfolio in the field of HVAC field devices with a focus on valves, actuators and consumption data acquisition and control it over the entire product life cycle.
  • You develop the portfolio and go-to-market strategy for the Swiss market and derive concrete growth and optimization measures from market, customer and competition analyses.
  • You translate requirements from the Swiss market into specific product requirements and represent them to our international headquarters and technology units.
  • You plan and control product launches, further developments and phase-outs and together with sales, marketing and technology ensure a successful market positioning.
  • You’ll develop business cases, pricing and positioning strategies, and evaluate market potential and the economic development of your portfolio based on feedback from your stakeholders.
  • You have a technical education or study in the field of building technology, HVAC, automation, engineering or a comparable qualification.
  • You bring experience in product management in building technology.
  • You have a good knowledge of HVAC systems. Knowledge of valve technology, actuators, hydraulic control or heat/cold measurement is advantageous.
  • You combine technical understanding with entrepreneurial thinking and can assess market potential, customer needs and economic contexts.
  • You work independently and structured, take responsibility for your portfolio and can bring together different internal and external stakeholders.
  • You communicate negotiably in German and English
  • Knowledge of French is an advantage. Your advantages
  • 2–3 days/week mobile work is standard
  • Discount public transport subscription and free parking
  • Diverse training opportunities
  • Access to employee stock plans
