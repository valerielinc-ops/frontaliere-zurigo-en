Full description

Build your career in Property Facultative underwriting in one of the world’s leading reinsurance platforms. We are looking for an underwriter with strong analytical skills, commercial curiosity and the ambition to grow. You will work on complex UK&I property risks, engage with brokers and clients, and develop your underwriting expertise alongside a highly experienced team.

About the Role

As a Property Facultative Underwriter focused on the UK & Ireland market, you will evaluate and underwrite single risks and facilities while progressively taking greater responsibility for your portfolio and market relationships.

Based in Zurich and part of our Global Facultative Market Unit, you will combine technical risk assessment with commercial thinking, supported by experienced colleagues, advanced modelling and analytics. The role offers significant opportunity to develop your underwriting authority, market exposure and expertise.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assess, price and structure risks in line with our underwriting standards, risk appetite and return targets
  • Manage and develop an assigned portfolio, with increasing ownership as your experience grows
  • Build relationships with brokers and clients and contribute to new business opportunities
  • Translate risk, portfolio and market insights into sound underwriting decisions
  • Work closely with experienced colleagues and leverage expertise across the wider organisation

About the Team

You will combine the strength, data and global reach of a leading reinsurer with the pace, ownership and direct market access of a specialist facultative team.

You will join the Global Fac Market Unit, a highly experienced, internationally connected department with a well-established UK market franchise. We are recognised for our expertise in single-risk and facility underwriting, strong broker and insurer relationships, and high service quality in a fast-paced market.

The role sits within a dedicated team of five more underwriters covering UK&I, reporting to the Head of Facultative Property UK&I & MEA. You’ll join a collaborative, intellectually curious team that values technical rigour and commercial creativity. We invest in developing our underwriters through hands-on coaching, exposure to experienced colleagues and progressively broader underwriting responsibility.

About You

You bring a foundation in property underwriting and the ambition to grow in Facultative reinsurance. Analytical, commercially curious and collaborative, you enjoy taking ownership and working in a dynamic, collaborative and transaction-focused environment.

We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements

  • Relevant property underwriting experience, ideally with exposure to commercial or industrial risks
  • Risk assessment and pricing skills, with experience and interest in catastrophe modelling and analytics
  • Sound commercial judgement and curiosity about clients, markets and portfolios
  • Confidence engaging with brokers, clients and internal stakeholders
  • A collaborative mindset and willingness to learn from and contribute to the wider team
  • Excellent written and verbal English

These are additional nice to haves

  • Previous exposure to facultative reinsurance or the UK property market
  • Experience in primary insurance
  • Experience with facilities or portfolio underwriting
  • Curiosity, resilience and adaptability, with a continuous learning mindset
  • Relevant degree or equivalent professional experience

If you bring a strong underwriting foundation but do not meet every requirement, we would still encourage you to apply.
