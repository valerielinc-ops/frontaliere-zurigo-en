Administrative Assistant at the Laboratory for Inorganic Chemistry (m/f/d)

30%-50%, Zurich, temporary

Build, organize and help shape: The Laboratory for Inorganic Chemistry (LAC) at ETH Zurich is a dynamic research environment with eight professorships and around 130 employees. For the newly founded research group of Prof. Elzbieta Gradauskaite we are ideally looking for a committed administrative assistance as of January 2027 or by agreement. The assistant function will also support Prof. Günther’s existing research group.

In this versatile hub function, you accompany the development of a growing research group right from the start and actively shape the administrative processes.

The employment is for the time being limited for a period of 5 years, with an option of termination, which is in the course of the retirement of Prof. Detlef Günther as well as the development of the research group is jointly examined.

Job description

  • HR & Guest Support: They accompany the entire administrative personnel process (entry to departure) of our employees and organize stays for guest researchers and interns from A to Z.
  • Finance & third-party funding: You plan and monitor project budgets (EU, SNSF, Innosuisse, industry), create financial reports and handle ongoing financial administration (bills, credit cards).
  • Research Proposals & Reporting: You support with budgeting and submission of research proposals and coordinate project documentation and reporting.
  • Group & laboratory organization: They ensure smooth processes in everyday life, coordinate procurement and equipment inventories and organize business trips, visits and events.
  • Digital & Communication: You maintain our digital workflows (Microsoft 365, Teams, SharePoint) as well as our website and social media channels (LinkedIn, ResearchGate).
  • Exchange with other ADM. Assistance in the department, internal and external interfaces as well as cooperation with HR partners:inside / HR specialist:inside

Profile

For this responsible and versatile position, you will ideally bring the following qualifications, experience and competencies:

  • Completed commercial or equivalent administrative training and experience in a comparable administrative function, ideally in a higher education environment
  • A highly independent, structured and reliable way of working, as well as the ability to keep track of multiple parallel tasks and set priorities effectively
  • Initiative, forward-looking work and strong organisational skills
  • They are service and team oriented and discreet
  • You can quickly adapt to different and changing requirements and familiarize yourself with ETH-specific management systems
  • Very good knowledge of Microsoft Office and familiarity with digital applications for collaboration and organization (e.g. Microsoft Teams, SharePoint and Copilot)
  • Interest in learning about new applications and improving workflows is particularly welcome
  • Stylistic German and very good English language skills in word and writing, to handle administrative matters independently in German and to communicate securely in English in an international research environment

We offer

  • Become part of ETH Zurich, which not only supports your professional development, but also actively contributes to positive changes in society
  • A varied and responsible work in an open, appreciative and collegial working environment
  • You have the opportunity to contribute to the establishment of a newly founded research group and to help shape its administrative processes
  • Your workplace on the ETH Hönggerberg campus offers modern infrastructure and excellent access to public transport.
  • Numerous benefits, such as public transport subscriptions and car sharing, a broad sports offer of the ASVZ, child care and attractive social and pension benefits
