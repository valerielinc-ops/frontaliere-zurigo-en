Full description

Do you want to help shape the digital transformation in the clinical field? As an Application Manager Manager with a focus on KISIM, you ensure the operational operation of our clinic information system and other responsible applications. You will also develop concepts and solutions for their further development. The job is initially limited to one year. An extension and a transfer to a permanent employment relationship will be examined.

Tasks

  • You ensure the stable operation of the clinic information system KISIM and other responsible applications. To do this, you monitor their availability, performance, recoverability and resource consumption
  • You will analyze application disorders, correct them or coordinate their processing and provide suitable bypass solutions if necessary.
  • You develop technical solutions and specifications for the CIS, submit them to the supplier, accompany the implementation and carry out functional tests until the productive introduction
  • You plan, test and accompany releases as well as rollouts and ensure a smooth introduction of new functions
  • You create and maintain operational, user and system documentation. In addition, you advise and support users in the use of the applications and the associated processes
  • You work in cross-divisional projects and actively contribute your expertise to the further development of our clinical application landscape

Profile

  • Completed studies in computer science, business informatics or medical informatics or a comparable qualification as well as experience in application support
  • In-depth clinical experience and a good understanding of hospital processes, needs and challenges; practical experience with KISIM or another KIS system is particularly welcome
  • Good knowledge of application servers, databases and interfaces
  • Knowledge of interface standards in healthcare, especially HL7, complement your profile
  • Broad ICT know-how for the exchange at eye level with infrastructure teams from the fields of network, server, client engineering and IAM as well as the ability to formulate requirements in a technically clear and implementable manner
  • Open, committed personality with a strong customer orientation and a high level of consulting and communication skills
  • Analytical, independent and solution-oriented working methods as well as the ability to recognize relationships holistically
  • High initiative, reliability and pleasure in working together in a team
  • Stylistic German in Word and Writing
