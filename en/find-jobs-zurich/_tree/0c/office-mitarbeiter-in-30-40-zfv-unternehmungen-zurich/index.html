Full description

This workplace includes a public self-service restaurant with a free-flow area, indoor and outdoor seating, and a cafeteria. The meeting place in Zurich Altstetten creates an inviting meeting place for employees, travelers and external guests. Our team inspires an average of 610 guests here every day with a wide range of activities - from varied lunch dishes and regular promotional weeks to banquets, aperitifs and events. Are you our new team member who will support us as an office employee (30-40%) as of February 1, 2027 or by appointment? You usually work from Monday to Thursday from 11:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. or from 12:00 p.m. to 3:30 p.m. In exceptional cases and by arrangement, assignments on Fridays are possible. Your task menu:

  • Washing dishes, cutlery and all kitchen utensils
  • Operation of the dishwasher
  • Ensuring cleanliness and order in the office area
  • Disposal and waste separation
  • Maintenance and care of the machines in the washing-up room
  • Completion of various cleaning expenses in the kitchen as well as in the entire company
  • Implementation of ZFV quality and service standards
  • Compliance with and implementation of hygiene and occupational safety guidelines Your recipe for success:
  • Basic knowledge of gastronomy / hotel industry
  • Experience in the washing-up room is an advantage
  • Oral German language skills
  • Ability to work in a team
  • Willing to learn and resilient
  • Hygiene-conscious
  • Structured and independent This is what we offer you: We create teams that support each other and in which you are supported. We work together and focus on everything that is good. Among other things, we attach great importance to wage transparency and equal pay. For this position, we offer an annual salary of between CHF 52,000 (incl. 13. Monthly salary).
