As a Clinical Specialist / Clinical Specialist Back, you design the “patient journey” through projects around the care content as well as organizational. You develop new paths and work in the interprofessional team. We would like to complement our motivated team in the field of back, pelvic floor, mental health with a committed, innovative personality with a lot of pleasure in the profession. We look forward to seeing you! Your tasks

  • You treat patients with complaints of the spine/back in the ambulatory and stationary setting
  • You will be guided by the latest science and keep an eye on innovations and developments in your field. You pass on your knowledge in the team and thus contribute to a high quality of treatment
  • Together with the medical teams of rheumatology, neurosurgery and pain medicine, you will continue to develop therapeutic pathways and shape new paths in care
  • In the aftercare of patients with back complaints, you develop defined tasks that have previously been performed by doctors (Advanced Practitioner Physiotherapy).

Your profile

  • Promote contact and cooperation with doctors and nurses Your joy
