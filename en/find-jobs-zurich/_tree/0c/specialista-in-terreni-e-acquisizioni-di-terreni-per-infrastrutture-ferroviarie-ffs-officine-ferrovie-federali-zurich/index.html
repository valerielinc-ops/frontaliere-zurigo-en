Specialist: in land acquisition railway infrastructure 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Specialist: in property and land acquisition railway infrastructure Zurich Altstetten / Work Smart, 01.01.2027 or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

The property management division bears overall responsibility for the properties of SBB and its wholly owned subsidiaries. Through transactions, the area enables new railway infrastructures, attractive railway stations and modern operating facilities. The department of railway operations ensures that the necessary land or the necessary rights for railway projects including energy supply are available on time.In Zurich, you can expect a motivated, committed team that does not lose its sense of humor even in a dynamic project environment. Teamwork is very important to us. At the same time, you get a lot of personal responsibility and the opportunity to lead your dossiers independently and actively participate. With courteous and professional services, we help to inspire our customers. You benefit from a wide range of development opportunities in direct exchange with specialists from law, technology and project management. You can move that.

  • As a specialist, you are responsible for the timely acquisition of land and rights for railway projects and thus contribute to the timely implementation of the projects.
  • Together with the project managers of the infrastructure, you develop the appropriate acquisition strategy and implement it independently.
  • Negotiations with landowners: you lead sovereignly, develop viable solutions and represent the interests of SBB professionally.
  • You create and execute the contracts and accompanying real estate transactions until the conclusion ensure a complete documentation.
  • You represent the SBB in front of notaries, the Swiss Federal Appraisal Commission and municipal and cantonal authorities.
  • Assess plans and project documents from a real estate perspective, introduce relevant legal and ownership-related issues into the projects at an early stage and negotiate legal grants on railway-operated properties with third parties.

You bring that with you.

  • Communication and negotiation skills, with which you combine different interests and clearly represent the concerns of SBB.
  • Networked and solution-oriented thinking as well as an independent working method with which you advance dossiers from structuring to completion.
  • Enjoy working in interdisciplinary projects with project leaders, technical specialists, authorities and landowners: inside.
  • Ability to read plans and derive relevant information about property and rights or a willingness to quickly acquire this competence.
  • education or training in law, notary, real estate or real estate. Alternatively, you bring a technical or commercial background with a very great interest in legal, technical and real estate-related issues. Knowledge of Swiss property, contract or administrative law is desired.
  • Very good knowledge of German at least at level C1 and ideally French or Italian at level B1.
