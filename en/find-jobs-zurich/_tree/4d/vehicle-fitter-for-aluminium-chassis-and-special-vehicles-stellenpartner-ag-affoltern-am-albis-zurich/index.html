Full description

The production of lightweight cases and special vehicle bodies requires precise craftsmanship. The company attaches great importance to high-quality processing and promotes an independent working method in the team. You benefit from a modern workplace and regular company events. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Production of aluminium chassis, lightweight cases and bridges according to parts lists and CAD specifications
  • Manufacture of special purpose vehicles, trailers and semi-trailers
  • Use of gluing, riveting, screwing and welding techniques in production
