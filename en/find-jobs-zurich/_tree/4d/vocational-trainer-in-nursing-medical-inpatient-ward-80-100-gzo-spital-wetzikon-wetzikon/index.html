Full description

(By agreement) You not only want to cultivate, but also pass on your knowledge with enthusiasm? You accompany students with passion, promote their development and enjoy working closely with your team? Then you are exactly right on our medical bed station.

Tasks

  • You accompany, promote and support students and learners during their practical training.
  • You plan and design individual learning processes and carry out structured learning support.
  • You are the contact person for practical trainers and the team in training questions.
  • You work closely with the education manager and the management nursing and develop the training continuously.
  • You participate in the recruitment of learners as well as in the implementation and further development of modern training concepts.
  • You take on responsibility in direct care and are a professional and professional role model.
  • You actively contribute to evidence-based, patient-oriented care and an appreciative team culture.
  • A versatile function with development potential awaits you Perspectively, there is the opportunity to expand the area of responsibility by the postpartum and to accompany students in this exciting field of expertise. Requirements
  • Diploma as a nurse HF/FH or an equivalent education recognised in Switzerland
  • At least two years of professional experience in the acute hospital
  • Ideally, further training in the field of vocational training (e.g. SVEB) or the willingness to complete it
  • Enjoyment of accompanying and promoting students
  • Educational skill as well as a structured and independent working method
  • Strong communication and team skills.
  • Enthusiasm for evidence-based care and continuous development
  • You take responsibility and appreciate a varied everyday work life You can look forward to an introduction that deserves this name You will be structured and carefully introduced to your new area of responsibility. Development that continues You benefit from internal and external training opportunities. A hospital where you know each other Short distances, direct contact persons and the cooperation in a multi-professional team that is characterized by respect and appreciation.
