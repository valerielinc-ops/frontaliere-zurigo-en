Full description

What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family company with over 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessories as well as the provision of financial services. At the headquarters in Zurich-Altstetten is the central administration of the Emil Frey Group, which controls the overarching activities. For our Swiss legal department we are looking for a Legal Operations & Paralegal Specialist. Their tasks

  • Self-responsible support and further development of central legal operations processes of the Swiss Legal Department
  • Administration, development and quality assurance of the contract management system including filing structures, deadlines, workflows and reporting
  • Coordination of requests and structured collection, allocation and follow-up
  • Operational support for the data protection organisation in Switzerland, in particular for information requests, the editing directory, data protection impact assessments, training and documentation
  • Coordination and administrative support of the internal data protection coordinators as well as cooperation with relevant specialist bodies
  • Creating, maintaining and standardizing templates, guidelines, checklists, knowledge content and other tools of the legal department
  • Legal research and preparation and processing of standardizable contracts, legal documents and recurring legal issues
  • Support of the team in projects, due diligence and internal clarifications as well as coordination of cross-sectoral projects in the areas of law, data protection, governance, and legal tech
  • Evaluation and practical use of appropriate legal tech, automation and AI solutions to improve quality, transparency and efficiency
  • Completed studies in business law or law (bachelor or master, university or university of applied sciences) or a qualified paralegal education
  • At least three years of relevant professional experience, ideally acquired in a legal department of a larger company, a commercial law firm or in a corporate governance, compliance or data protection function
  • Experience in legal operations management, contract management or corporate housekeeping and pleasure in permanently taking over and further developing processes
  • Good knowledge of data protection law (DSG and GDPR) as well as practical experience with data protection processes, data protection organizations or compliance structures are of great advantage
  • Safe use of digital work equipment
  • Experience with contract management systems, legal tech, workflow automation or AI applications is desired
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method with a high level of quality awareness and consistent tracking of tasks and deadlines
  • Ability to quickly capture legal and organizational issues, prioritize them meaningfully and implement them pragmatically
  • Service-oriented, committed personality with pleasure in working in a small, versatile team and with different internal stakeholders
  • Interested in building a long-term leading role at the interface of law, data protection, governance, processes and technology
  • Confident knowledge of German as well as very good knowledge of English in words and writing
  • What we offer you You will find with us a versatile key role at the interface of law, data protection, governance and technology with wide scope and much more:
  • A long-term company perspective in an established, family-run and diverse Swiss group of companies
  • A newly created function with the possibility to actively shape and sustainably anchor legal operations and data protection processes
  • A high degree of independence and responsibility as well as close cooperation with the General Counsel Switzerland and the legal team
  • Individual development opportunities towards senior legal operations, paralegal or data protection
  • Numerous discounts (new cars, service work and spare parts, vehicle and health insurance, fuel cards, hotels and much more)
  • An own, attractive pension fund as an independent pension institution
  • At least 25 days of holiday As you can contact us We look forward to your full application (letter of motivation, CV, employment certificates and diplomas) via the link "Apply now!".
