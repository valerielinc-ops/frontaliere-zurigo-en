(Senior) Product Manager Digital Workplace (m/f/d) — SRG SSR

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

The Operations Department is responsible for the technical production of all SSR content and its technical processing and archiving (TV, radio and multimedia). Within the SSR, the PM Digital Workplace (or Head of Digital Workplace) provides employees with a modern, secure and reliable digital working environment. It includes Windows and Apple (HW) devices, Microsoft 365 services, collaboration tools and intranet as well as automation of processes and solutions based on artificial intelligence.

Your Mission

Within the IT portfolio, you assume, as an open and curious personality, the responsibility for strategic development and the successful implementation of all projects related to the Digital Workplace product:

  • Strategy and responsibility for implementation: You define the product vision as well as the long-term roadmap and ensure the consistent and targeted implementation of complex initiatives in the area of Digital Workplace, from the definition of architecture and solutions to the successful deployment throughout the company.
  • End-to-end life cycle: You manage the entire life cycle of the product in an active and data-based way: from the demanding evaluation and technical realization of new solutions to stable and efficient exploitation, to large-scale replacements and migrations (Intune transition included).
  • Technical conduct and orchestration: You ensure the functional conduct of Product Owners, support implementation teams in their empowerment and coordinate close collaboration with Operations Personnel Management, Business Partners and central stakeholders across all business units.
  • Financial pilotage and suppliers: You are responsible for the budget of the product, manage external suppliers, partners and services in a binding manner on the quality of the results and ensure the achievement of economic objectives.
  • Organizational Development: You make a decisive move towards a modern, agile and product-oriented organization and anchor sustainable working methods in implementation.

Your profile

  • Experience confirmed: You have proven practical experience of several years (at least 5 years) in managing senior products or in the overall responsibility for complex IT and Digital Workplace implementations in large multisite organisations. A successful migration to Intune is a real plus.
  • Strong implementation capacity and methodology: You have strong experience in agile product and portfolio management (e.g., FASe, Scrum) and have already successfully proven your ability to translate strategic roadmaps into concrete and sustainable solutions in a binding manner.
  • Comprehensive technical and architectural understanding: You have a broad and solid technical understanding of the entire Digital Workplace. This includes the Modern Endpoint Management (Windows/Apple, MDM/MAM, packaging) and the Microsoft 365 ecosystem (collaboration, intranet, provisioning, automation and self-service solutions as well as IA integrations).
  • Innovation force and UX orientation: You combine a coherent orientation towards a fluid and needs-driven user experience (UX) with a great deal of proposal power in the face of technological trends and digital innovations.
  • Pilotage and financial skills: You have proven experience in budget management, managing costs and product quality using relevant KPI as well as in developing solid business boxes and cost-benefit analyses.
  • Leadership and communication ease: You convince yourself by your persuasion, your ease of management and your strong animation skills, in order to successfully balance the different interests of the stakeholders.
  • Languages: Very good knowledge of a national language, supplemented by good knowledge of German or French (minimum B1 level) and English (minimum B1/B2).

What we offer you

  • Framework function: A framework function with related decisions.
  • Key role of national importance with real end-to-end responsibility and wide flexibility in implementing a central product at the company level.
  • Modern work culture: Agile and product-oriented method of work in a dynamic media and technological environment.
  • Stimulating environment: Collaboration with experienced, interdisciplinary and highly motivated teams.

Our promise

Reaching the entire Swiss population with high quality formats requires expertise, enthusiasm and a stimulating and creative environment, in which everyone can invest and evolve. For the best to give the best of themselves, they need to have a source of inspiration: Welcome to the world of SSR!

  • Create Content: Quality journalism is the core of our activity and passion. We enjoy complete freedom of writing, encourage creativity and grant autonomy for innovation and quality.
  • Living digital: In our country, quality journalism can also take a digital form. This is why we are exploring new opportunities and experimenting with new formats and practices: digital, interactive and cross-media. Our content is part of the air of time and responds to public concerns.
  • Human being: We maintain informal relations, work independently and contribute to a good working atmosphere. Our success is based on the strengths of everyone and on the technical skills of all our employees.
  • Make sense: With our program, we reach all of Switzerland. We act independently and respectfully to encourage the formation of opinion and strengthen cultural diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.
