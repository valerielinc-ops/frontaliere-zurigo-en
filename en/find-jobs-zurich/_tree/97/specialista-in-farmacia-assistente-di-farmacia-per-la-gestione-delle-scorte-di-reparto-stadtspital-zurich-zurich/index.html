Full description

In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a motivated and reliable personality immediately or by agreement and for a temporary period of 2 years with an option of renewal.

  • As part of the team of media assistants, you are responsible for drug management on the station
  • You supervise the warehouse of the station pharmacy, also in areas upstream of the operating room, carry out orders, check incoming goods and ensure the proper storage of deliveries.
  • You ensure compliance with the requirements of the Medicines Act and carry out, among other things, expiration date and narcotics controls
  • You act as an important interface between hospital pharmacy and nursing staff
  • You actively support the optimization of the drug process
  • You are committed to the expansion of pharmaceutical services on the station
  • Training as a specialist Pharmacy / Pharmaceutical assistant, drugist or pharmaceutical-technical assistant
  • Professional experience in the hospital is advantageous
  • Willingness to actively participate in an interprofessional environment
  • High degree of independence, sense of responsibility, flexibility and teamwork
  • Physical load capacity, in particular for lifting and carrying goods up to 10 kg
  • Quality-conscious, structured and efficient action
  • Open and secure communication capability

We offer you more than just a job! We live an open du-culture, which is characterized by an uncomplicated and personal cooperation at eye level, respect and appreciation. As part of our diverse team of over 120 professional groups, you actively shape a meaningful environment. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions, flexible working time models and excellent social benefits from the city of Zurich.

With over 36'000 inpatients and around 455,000 outpatients per year, the Stadtspital Zürich ranks among the top 10 Swiss hospitals. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,500 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
