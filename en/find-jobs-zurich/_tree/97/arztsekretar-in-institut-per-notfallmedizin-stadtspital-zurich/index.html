Full description

For the secretariat of the Institute of Emergency Medicine we are looking for 1. February 2027 or by appointment a team-oriented personality.

Tasks

  • You ensure that all administrative and organisational secretarial tasks at the Waid and Triemli locations are carried out efficiently, on time and in good quality.
  • You review, correct, send and archive outpatient reports and provide complete and careful documentation in the clinic information system.
  • You handle demanding internal and external correspondence and are responsible for telephone and written communication with family doctors, insurance companies, internal contact persons, etc.
  • You support the induction of new colleagues and promote a cooperative interdisciplinary cooperation with internal and external bodies.

Profile

  • Completed commercial training and further training as medical and hospital secretary
  • Professional experience in healthcare
  • Strong service and service orientation and strong communication skills
  • High organisational skills with good prioritisation
  • Enjoyment in dealing with people as well as exact, efficient and responsible working methods

About us

The City Hospital Zurich is one of the top 10 Swiss hospitals with over 36'000 inpatient and around 455,000 outpatient patients per year. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,500 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
