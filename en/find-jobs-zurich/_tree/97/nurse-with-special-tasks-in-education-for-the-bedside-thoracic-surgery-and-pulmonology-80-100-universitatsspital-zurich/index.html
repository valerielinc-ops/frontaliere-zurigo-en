How do you support patients in breathing deeply and becoming mobile after a lung operation? How do you know that breathing worsens in a lung disease? On our bed station, two specialist areas meet that shape everyday care from different perspectives. You accompany people after thoracic surgery and care for patients with acute or chronic lung diseases. This is where your job in vocational training starts. You show FaGe learners and nursing students HF and FH at the patient bed, what they need to pay attention to, how they classify their observations and why they choose a nursing measure.

Your main tasks

  • Together with the FaGe vocational trainers: inside and the vocational trainers: inside care on the station you take responsibility for the training of our learners and students
  • In learning and working communities (LAG), you work with the trainees at the patient bed and accompany them to apply their knowledge in practice.
  • You are the contact person on the station for questions about vocational training and support the nursing team in carrying out its training assignment
  • In this role, you have the opportunity to become a member of the department’s shared governance leadership team.

You bring that with you

  • You are a qualified nurse HF or FH and have at least one year of professional experience in the acute hospital
  • You have the SVEB-1 degree or are ready to acquire it with us
  • You will find good access to young people, motivate learners and students and awaken their curiosity for thoracic surgery and pulmonology

This is what awaits you with us

You deepen your expertise in two areas that complement each other in everyday care. When mobilizing after an operation, breath observation or dealing with shortness of breath, you can make learning moments from concrete care situations. In the vocational trainer team, you develop pedagogically and shape how we train on the station. If you are involved in the shared governance leadership team, you can also contribute your perspective from vocational training to the further development of the department. You want to show how much you can learn at the patient bed? We look forward to your application. For this post, dossiers from recruiters cannot be considered. Thank you for your understanding.
