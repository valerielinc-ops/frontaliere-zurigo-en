Full description

The Chiropractic Medicine degree program at the University of Zurich, implementation site of the University Hospital Balgrist, is the training center for chiropractic in Switzerland. Our students benefit from the proximity to research and clinic as well as from the good interprofessional cooperation within the University Hospital Balgrist. We are looking for temporary from 1 September 2027 to 31. May 2028 one/n:

Tasks

  • Support of the main lecturer in the preparation and implementation of the lessons in orthopaedic examination as well as in the introduction to mobilization and manipulation in the 3rd year of study (about 14 sessions of 2 hours per semester)

Requirements

  • You are a qualified chiropractor
  • You want to take on responsibility in a small team to help shape and advance the teaching and learning of chiropractic students
  • Awareness of quality and responsibility as well as flexibility
  • Interest in teaching
  • You are safe in the German language in word and writing (C2) and can communicate in English (B1)

We offer

  • 20% discount in Balgrist Apotheke AG
  • Mind. 26 holidays per year
  • Enjoy a healthy and balanced lunch at discounted prices at our company-owned staff restaurant
  • Free coffee/hot drinks
  • Miscellaneous shopping incentives
  • You have the opportunity to participate in a prestigious orthopaedic university clinic with an excellent reputation
