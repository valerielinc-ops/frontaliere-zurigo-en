Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organizations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Design, Implementation and Documentation of Communication Networks for Army, Confederation and Blue Light Organization Facilities
  • Creation and further development of network concepts with focus on stability, security and long-term operation
  • Configuration, commissioning and testing of network components in projects and operating environments
  • Support and targeted support of customer networks in the context of projects and service assignments
  • Technical support for project managers in pre-sales, for example in solution concepts, architecture issues and offers
  • Analysis and resolution of complex network and security issues in the 3rd-level environment

You bring that with you

  • Completed tertiary level technical training (HF, BSc, MSc or MAS) in engineering or equivalent qualification
  • In-depth experience with TCP/IP, routing and communications security technologies
  • Practice with network solutions from manufacturers such as Cisco, Juniper or Checkpoint
  • Further training in the field of IP communications is advantageous, for example Cisco certifications
  • Experience in directional radio or interest in getting involved in this field
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing, good knowledge of English in word and writing (level B2)
  • Motivated to learn new

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.
