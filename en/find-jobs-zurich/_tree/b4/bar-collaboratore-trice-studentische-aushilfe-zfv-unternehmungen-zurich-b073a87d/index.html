The gastronomy in the Toni area belongs to the Zurich University of Applied Sciences and the Zurich University of Arts in the Toni area campus. The gastronomy consists of the Mensa Molki, the Bistro Chez Toni, a cafeteria and the cascade gastronomy. Furthermore, numerous internal and external caterings are carried out. About 800 lunches are prepared every day. True to the ZFV philosophy, freshly cooked locally with regional and ecologically well-produced and fair trade food.
