Full description

To strengthen our team at the Triemli site, we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by arrangement a dedicated personality.

Tasks

  • You will work alternately at our modern workplaces in digital conventional radiology (Samsung GCA 85), mammography (GE senography Pristina), interventional radiology (Siemens ARTIS pheno, Siemens ARTIS zee MP), computed tomography (Revolution CT, GE) and magnetic resonance imaging (1.5T GE Signa Artist and 3.0T GE Signa Architect).
  • You also have the option to support the nuclear medicine team on our advanced devices (PETCT GE Discovery NM)
  • You are involved in the introduction of new employees and help to support our students.
  • You promote good cooperation in the team and interdisciplinary.
  • You will also have the opportunity to work across locations if you are interested.

Profile

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Radiology specialist / Dipl. Radiology specialist HF
  • Teamwork, high resilience and a strong sense of service
  • High sense of responsibility and quality as well as reliability
  • Readiness for shift and weekend work
