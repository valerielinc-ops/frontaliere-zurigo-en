Are you passionate about the kitchen and would like to become part of a dynamic team at the University Hospital Zurich? We are looking for specialists who provide with creativity and care for the culinary well-being of our patients, employees and guests. Look forward to a modern working environment and become part of our team!

Their main tasks

  • Preparation of the food offer for patients: inside and staff: inside and catering
  • Monitoring of meal preparation
  • Compliance with quality standards, hygiene and occupational safety guidelines
  • Active support in ordering and goods management
  • Contact for our learners
  • Varying working hours from 06.30-18.30

Your profile

  • Completed training as a cook EFZ
  • Operational readiness, high load capacity and flexibility
  • Interest in healthy eating
  • In addition to their cost awareness, they also have good IT knowledge (Word/Excel)
  • Passion for the daily professional challenge and professional pride
  • German in Word and Writing
  • Interest in kitchen ordering and in the associated administrative tasks is advantageous
  • Dietary knowledge or the willingness to develop in this area are desired
  • Motivated, resilient and team capable personality, coupled with high service orientation and a dose of humor

Our offer

We offer you a varied area of responsibility in a modern working environment. Their further development is important to us. For this we use our in-house offer of training and further education. With good suitability, assignments in our diet kitchen are possible. For this post, dossiers from recruiters cannot be considered. Thank you for your understanding. Our Benefits

Kita on site

In our in-house daycare centers, your children will be cared for lovingly. Long opening hours without company holidays and subsidized care places relieve you in everyday life.

Health

We support your health. In addition to personal consultations and courses of our health management that promote your well-being, we offer you access to over 120 sports with a subsidized annual subscription to the ASVZ.

Flexibility

We promote a balanced work-life balance: Almost half of our employees work part-time, and about 50% of the tasks can be done in the home office – if your working day allows it.

Family friendliness

We help you to reconcile family and work. Mothers are entitled to at least 6 months of maternity leave (of which 16 weeks are paid in full), fathers to at least 2 months of paternity leave (of which 2 weeks are paid in full).

Further education and development

Insurance
