If you too want to make a difference in people’s lives, these are your tasks:

  • Completion of the practical education part of the NDS at our intensive care department and at our contract hospital in Triemli City Hospital
  • Visits to the modular theoretical part of the NDS at the Higher School of Intensive Care, Emergency Care and Anesthesia (Z-INA)
  • Treatment of patients with special need for care, supervision and care

Become part of the Department of Medical Services at the Zollikerberg Hospital. In our interdisciplinary intensive care unit, we look after around 600 patients per year. The station complies with the current recommendations and standards of the Swiss Society for Intensive Care Medicine (SGI) and is a recognized training institution for the post-graduate degree program. Expert or Dipl. Expert intensive care NDS. As part of the Service Center Operating Rooms and Intensive Care Medicine (DLZ OP/IS), we work closely with the various departments of the hospital.
