Full description

For Helix and Hydra, two of our shop framework teams, we are looking for you as a product owner. As a bridge between our feature teams, Helix and Hydra are responsible for the technical cores of our online shop. You build the core framework and design system of the online shop, as well as the API gateway and networking on which other teams build their features.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Headquarters, Pfingstweidstrasse 60b, 8005 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • As a product owner at Hydra and Helix, you will shape the online shop frameworks of Digitec Galaxus by actively designing corresponding strategies, solutions and implementations.
  • You recognize potentials and evaluate their relevance. In addition, you help the team with prioritization to develop the right features and can represent them to stakeholders
  • Together with your cross-functional team, you will evaluate, develop and implement high-quality, scalable framework solutions that serve as the foundation for the entire online shop platform.
  • Your team develops our framework, which from the developer’s point of view always remains robust, performant and sustainable
  • Working closely with the other online shop product teams, you provide solutions and standards to continuously increase performance, stability and end-user experience.
  • As a product owner, you think entrepreneurially and always keep an eye on the technical architecture, the internal users (development teams) and the development of the Internet and its standards holistically.

Requirements

  • In-depth professional experience: in the field of data-driven, innovative product development
  • No training/further education required: Relevant professional experience is in the foreground - suitable training is an advantage
  • You think holistically and keep the needs of developers and customers always in focus
  • Technology inspires you, and you move safely in the triad of technology, outstanding user experience and business requirements
  • Platform and framework development is your topic: you have gained initial experience with scalable systems and you know the potential and challenges of modern web frameworks
  • You combine your analytical skills and your quick comprehension with a high quality standard, a pragmatic way of working and close cooperation with the interdisciplinary team.
  • Communication is your strength, enthusiasm and humor: You quickly make contact with people of all kinds and win them for your ideas
  • Taking calculated risks to create value and drive growth
  • Have the courage to try things without planning them down to the smallest detail
  • Be resilient in changing times and tackle change positively
  • German (flowing)
  • English (flowing)
