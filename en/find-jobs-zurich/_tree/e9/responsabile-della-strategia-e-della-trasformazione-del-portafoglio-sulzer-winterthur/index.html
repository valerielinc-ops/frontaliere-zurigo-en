Full description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

We are looking for a Portfolio Strategy & Transformation Lead (100%) to join our Global Portfolio Management team within the Flow division in Switzerland. In this high-impact role, you will help transform portfolio management into a strategic business capability across the Water & Industrial Business Unit (WIBU), enabling data-driven decision-making, profitable growth, and long-term portfolio optimization.

You will establish portfolio governance, performance transparency, product hierarchy ownership, lifecycle management practices, and strategic portfolio steering processes. Working across functions and senior stakeholder groups, you will play a key role in shaping the future direction of our portfolio and driving business transformation.

Your main tasks and responsibilities

  • Own and evolve the Product Hierarchy framework by driving the WIBU Product Hierarchy program, ensuring clear ownership, consistent product classification, improved master data quality, and successful milestone delivery.
  • Establish portfolio performance management capabilities , including KPI frameworks, dashboards, reporting standards, and review mechanisms that provide reliable product-level business insights and transparency.
  • Drive strategic portfolio steering by developing and facilitating portfolio review processes that support investment decisions, lifecycle management, portfolio optimization, resource allocation, and growth initiatives.
  • Generate market, competitive, and portfolio intelligence by consolidating insights on market trends, customer needs, competitive developments, portfolio gaps, and emerging business opportunities in collaboration with segment, product management, and development teams.
  • Lead portfolio transformation initiatives related to portfolio governance, lifecycle excellence, digitalization, product master data, performance and pricing management, and process harmonization across the organization.
  • Act as a trusted partner to senior leaders, enabling fact-based portfolio decisions and fostering a culture of transparency, accountability, and continuous improvement.

To succeed in this role, you will need

  • Master's degree in Business Administration, Engineering, Economics, or a related field; an MBA is a strong plus.
  • 5-8 years of experience in portfolio management, strategy, business transformation, product management, or a related function within an industrial or manufacturing environment.
  • Strong strategic and analytical capabilities, with the ability to translate complex market, portfolio, and business data into actionable recommendations.
  • Experience driving cross-functional transformation initiatives involving product management, commercial functions, operations, R&D, and senior leadership.
  • Strong change management and project management skills with a track record of driving business improvement and transformation.
  • Digital mindset with experience using analytics and reporting tools to drive performance management and business insights.
  • Fluent in English; additional languages are an advantage.
