Full description

Key responsibilities Do you have strong hands-on Swiss civil litigation experience, enjoy taking ownership of cases and thrive when facing demanding challenges in a dynamic and global environment? Are you excited by the opportunity to help build a pioneering in-house litigation function? We are recruiting Litigation Lawyers for our hubs in Zürich and Geneva.

You will

  • take end-to-end responsibility for civil litigation matters designated for full in-house handling, from fact-finding and legal assessment through resolution, including developing case strategy, drafting pleadings and other court submissions, and representing the bank before courts and conciliation authorities
  • lead and manage significant domestic and cross-border civil litigation matters in collaboration with outside counsel, setting the overall strategy and translating legal, financial, regulatory and reputational considerations into clear recommendations and decisions; where relevant, you may also be involved in criminal and regulatory matters in a supervisory and strategic capacity alongside outside counsel
  • coordinate relevant business, legal and senior stakeholders across functions, including Finance and Media Relations, and ensure effective management of outside counsel in significant matters, with appropriate quality and cost control
  • help shape a modern, collaborative and AI-enabled litigation practice across Switzerland, working with state-of-the-art legal technology to set new benchmarks for how litigation is handled in a global financial institution, and contribute your litigation expertise to the continuous development of practical, high-quality workflows

The team Global Litigation Switzerland is a specialist unit within UBS Group Legal, with hubs in Zürich and Geneva. We are responsible for civil litigation, criminal and regulatory matters across all divisions in booking center Switzerland, often in a cross-border context. You will work autonomously as part of a multilingual team and collaborate closely with colleagues across UBS on significant litigation matters.

Bring your litigation experience in-house without giving up the work you value. You will continue to investigate facts, shape legal strategies, draft court submissions and advocate in proceedings—while taking genuine end-to-end ownership of matters and their outcomes. Your role will extend beyond providing legal advice and conducting proceedings: you will help drive the bank’s overall response, align business, financial, regulatory and reputational considerations, coordinate key stakeholders and have a direct voice in the decisions that shape the outcome. Working alongside experienced colleagues and senior decision-makers, you will gain a broader perspective and assume responsibility that reaches well beyond the purely legal dimension. You will also have the rare opportunity to help build the next generation of our Swiss litigation practice, influence how we work and advance practical AI-enabled workflows.

Your skills and experience

  • admission to the Swiss bar
  • at least 4 years of post-bar Swiss litigation experience, gained in private practice, with hands-on court experience
  • track-record of leading civil litigations and delivering persuasive written and oral advocacy
  • strong knowledge of Swiss civil procedure and sound judgment in assessing litigation risks
  • native or near-native proficiency in the language of the relevant hub German or French combined with fluent English
  • native or near-native proficiency in Italian is a strong asset
  • clear communication, commercial awareness and a collaborative approach
  • enthusiasm for working with AI-enabled tools and an interest in helping develop practical, high-quality litigation workflows
