Full description

With your expertise, you ensure that hazardous waste is safely accepted, handled professionally and disposed of in an environmentally sound manner. You are the contact person for our customers, advise them in dealing with special waste. You can expect a versatile job that combines chemical expertise with practical work. We are looking for reinforcements at the Hagenholz site from January 2027 or by appointment.

Ready for a job with meaning?

  • You take over the professional acceptance, triage, storage and disposal of special waste and ensure compliance with legal regulations.
  • You will advise our customers competently on the safe handling of hazardous waste, its transport and disposal as well as on the cantonal hazardous waste concept.
  • You triage chemicals and medicines, serve industrial trucks and actively participate in the mobile municipal collection for hazardous waste.
  • You ensure the professional packaging of the special waste and create the necessary documents according to specifications (ADR) for further transport for disposal.
  • You participate in the administrative handling of hazardous waste management, record key figures and process reports in the corresponding systems (e.g. VeVA-online).
  • Vocational training EFZ in the field of chemistry (e.g. laboratory technician, chemical technologist, galvanizer, etc.).
  • Further training as a dangerous goods and radiation protection officer*r as well as the lift truck examination or the willingness to acquire it.
  • Very good knowledge of German (level C2) and a friendly attitude to advise our customers as well as a careful, responsible working method
  • Good manners, enjoyment of customer contact as well as teamwork and reliability are important for our small team.
  • Willingness to work on about 3 Saturdays per year.
  • Generous mobility contribution
  • A modern, down-to-earth and future-oriented working environment
  • Careful training in your work
  • Equal pay
  • Above-average social benefits
  • Food allowance
  • Maternity leave: 16 weeks paid
  • Paternity leave: 20 paid days
  • Parents can care for their sick child for three days without a holiday deduction
  • Further development opportunities through the broad educational offer of the City of Zurich
  • ERZ Sportclub

With around 1,000 employees, ERZ is one of the largest service departments in the city of Zurich and operates in an exciting environment at the pulse of time. We are modern, down-to-earth, open-minded, all per you and want to make a difference. Together we are committed to a clean, innovative and sustainable Zurich.

ERZ stands for diversity and mixed teams.
