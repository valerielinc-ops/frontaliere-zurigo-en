Full description

At our location in the school house Sihlfeld, school district Limmattal we are looking for by immediate or by agreement for the middle school class (separation) a school curative teacher, about 45% (Monday, Tuesday).

  • Daily responsibility for the middle school class together with the class teacher in job sharing and the social educators
  • Promotion of children according to the goals defined in the SSG and the curriculum 21
  • Participation in funding planning, certificates and learning reports
  • Cooperation with parents or legal guardians, external specialist bodies and the regular school
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation
  • EDK-approved curative education or teaching qualification as a teacher
  • Practical experience in the special education sector or the willingness to become familiar with this task.
  • Joy in the promotion and accompaniment of children with cognitive impairment
  • Teamwork, motivation, resilience, humour, flexibility and creativity
  • Willingness and interest in interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Self-employed and varied work in a professional team
  • Exciting interdisciplinary tasks in the special education sector
  • Possibility of active participation in our school
  • Conditions of employment in accordance with the requirements of the City of Zurich (VLT)
  • Regular working hours

The curative school of the city of Zurich is a special day school for 4 to 15-year-old children with a cognitive and/or multiple impairment. We offer the pupils who are taught in separate classes in different school buildings in the city of Zurich a supportive environment and place the focus of the interdisciplinary promotion in their strengths.

Our teams at the various locations in the city of Zurich consist of school curative pedagogues, social pedagogues, specialists care, learners, assistants as well as speech pedagogues, ergo-, physio- and psychomotor therapists.
