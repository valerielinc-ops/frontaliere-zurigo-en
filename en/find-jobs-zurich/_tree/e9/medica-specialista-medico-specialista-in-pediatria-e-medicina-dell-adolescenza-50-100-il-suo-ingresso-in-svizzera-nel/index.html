You want to move to Switzerland, but not as a lone fighter:in start? As part of an established group of paediatric practices, you benefit from regular professional exchange, joint training and well-established assistance teams. At the location, you actively build up the team, with a lot of support in the network. This established and growing group of pediatric practices is represented with several locations in German-speaking Switzerland. It combines the independence of outpatient practice with the advantages of a large network of paediatricians with different focuses. The professional exchange has a system here: In regular medical consultancies and internal training courses, the doctors of the various locations meet and discuss current topics and cases. Especially when entering a new healthcare system, this is a great advantage. Experienced assistant teams and a professional practice administration also keep your back. The practice rooms are generous and equipped with the latest technology. Your place of work is located in the Agglomeration Zurich and, like all locations of the group, is very easily accessible by public transport. So the place can be connected just as well with a place of residence in the city as in the green.

Their tasks

  • Independent guidance of your outpatient consultation hours for children and adolescents from newborn to teenage years
  • Preventive examinations, vaccinations as well as clarification and treatment of acute diseases and paediatric clinical pictures of primary care
  • Care for paediatric emergencies and participation in the regional emergency service according to the respective specifications
  • Advice and guidance for parents and families
  • Active participation of the growing team at the location and contribution of own priorities

Your Competences

  • Specialist title in paediatric medicine or a comparable foreign qualification
  • Clinical experience in primary pediatric care
  • Joy of independent work and exchange in the network
  • Empathic interaction with children, adolescents and their families as well as fluent knowledge of German (level C1)
  • Not yet working in Switzerland? No problem: With the MEBEKO recognition and the professional training permit, we accompany you with advice and financial support

Their advantages

  • A large pediatrician network with regular professional exchange and joint training
  • Pensum between 50 and 100% with flexible agenda planning
  • Significant relief through well-established assistance teams and a professional administration
  • Spacious, child-friendly and modernly equipped practice rooms
  • Design room in a growing team and space for your own priorities
  • Attractive employment conditions
  • Very good accessibility with public transport
