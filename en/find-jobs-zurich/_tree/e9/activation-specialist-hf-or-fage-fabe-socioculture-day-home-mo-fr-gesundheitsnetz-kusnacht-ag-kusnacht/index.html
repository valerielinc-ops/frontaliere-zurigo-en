Gesundheitsnetz Küsnacht AG (GNK), with the Tägerhalde and Wangensbach senior and health centres accommodating around 130 residents, as well as a day care centre, Spitex, senior living and counselling services, and around 250 employees in total, is a non-profit joint-stock company wholly owned by the municipality of Küsnacht.

The day care centre offers older people requiring support due to dementia-related conditions and cognitive impairments a supervised daily routine. We are looking for the right puzzle piece to complete our fun-loving team.
