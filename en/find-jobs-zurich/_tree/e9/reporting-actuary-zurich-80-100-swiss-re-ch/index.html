Full description

We are looking for a highly motivated and experienced actuary to join our Life and Health (L&H) valuation team, supporting on our key portfolios across EMEA business. This role offers the opportunity to manage in-force portfolios, assumption setting, support quarterly reporting and provide actionable insights across diverse markets. You will play a key role in delivering robust actuarial results, driving operational efficiency, and collaborating with global teams.

Key responsibilities include

  • Lead and coordinate quarterly valuation processes and assumption reviews for key lines of business ensuring timely and accurate reserve and income statement submissions under IFRS 17 and Swiss Statutory reporting bases.
  • Review and provide insightful analysis and commentary on reserve movements, P&L results, experience trends, and assumption changes for senior stakeholders.
  • Support strategic decision-making by identifying emerging risks, themes, and areas requiring management attention.
  • Act as a key liaison between Valuation, Finance, Products, Client Markets, and Global Valuation teams, fostering effective collaboration and ensuring consistent delivery across stakeholders.
  • Provide oversight of team workflows, helping to prioritise deliverables, enhance processes, and ensure high-quality execution across the team.
  • Mentor, coach, and develop junior team members, fostering technical excellence, knowledge sharing, and strong team engagement.

About You

  • Qualified or nearly qualified actuary with 8+ years of L&H valuation or pricing experience in reinsurance, insurance, or consulting.
  • Experience in Health and/or Longevity business would be beneficial but is not essential.
  • Strong knowledge of IFRS17; familiarity with EV, MCEV, or Solvency II is an advantage.
  • Proficient in MS Excel; experience with Power BI or database tools preferred.
  • Excellent analytical, communication, and stakeholder management skills.
  • Proven ability to influence across functions and deliver under tight timelines.
  • Teamwork - able to build working relationships across technical disciplines and work effectively across the L&H value chain (including non-actuaries).

About the team The EMEA Actuarial Valuation team spans multiple locations (Zurich, London, Bangalore) and is responsible for quarterly financial reporting, assumption setting, and portfolio management across Life, Health and Longevity business.
