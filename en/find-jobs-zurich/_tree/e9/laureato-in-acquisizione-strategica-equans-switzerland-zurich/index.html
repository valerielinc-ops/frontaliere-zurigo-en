Full description

Your entry into the world of procurement, digitalization & AI. You have completed your studies and do not just want to manage processes, but actively make a difference? At Equans Switzerland, you get the opportunity to purchase a company with around CHF 1 billion. Get to know sales and help shape its further development. From strategic purchasing projects to supplier negotiations to automation and artificial intelligence: You can expect a versatile role in which you take on responsibility early on, contribute your own ideas and develop both professionally and personally.

You can move that with us.

  • Automation & AI: You question existing purchasing processes, identify automation potential and develop innovative digital solutions together with the team.
  • Strategic Purchasing: You participate in tenders, market analyses, price comparisons and the development of supplier strategies.
  • Digital Procurement: You work with modern purchasing systems such as SAP and E-Procurement and support their further development and optimization.
  • Supplier Management & Negotiations: You work with suppliers, analyze offers and support in price and contract negotiations.
  • Business Collaboration: You work closely with project leaders and various specialist areas and support with specific procurement projects.
  • Own Your Projects: You gradually take responsibility for your own topics, submit suggestions for improvement and implement them together with the team.

That's what makes you.

  • You have completed or are about to complete a degree in business administration, business engineering, business informatics or a comparable field.
  • You are interested in purchasing, digitalization, automation and AI and want to actively connect these topics.
  • You think analytically, question existing processes and enjoy developing new solutions.
  • You work independently, are curious and like to take responsibility.
  • You communicate openly and enjoy working with different people and specialist areas.
  • You have good knowledge of German. Other language skills are a plus.
  • First practical experiences are welcome, but not a must. Much more important to us are your motivation, your ideas and your willingness to learn new things.

That's what we offer you.

  • Career start with perspective: You get a versatile introduction to purchasing and gradually develop towards strategic purchasing.
  • Real Business Impact: You work on concrete projects whose results are visible in the company.
  • Innovation instead of routine: you combine classic procurement with digitalization, automation and new AI applications.
  • Responsibility from the beginning: You get space to contribute your own ideas, advance topics and independently develop solutions.
  • Insights into a large company: You learn different business areas, projects and purchasing processes of a company with around CHF 1 billion. Know the turnover.
  • Learning on the Job: You will continuously expand your know-how in operational and strategic purchasing and learn from experienced colleagues.
  • A strong team: You can expect a collegial environment with an open you culture, short communication paths and mutual support.
