Full description

movemi AG operates the largest fitness network in Switzerland with 126 ACTIV FITNESS and 16 fitness parks. Do you want to create the digital basis for a strong customer experience? As Head of Platform & Data, you build the Platform & Data team and are responsible for the system landscape and data foundation on which the website, app, CRM and customer feedback will run in the future.

Luogo di lavoro: movemi AG, Verwaltungs movemi AG, Thurgauerstrasse 32, 8050 Oerlikon

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You build the team Platform & Data, manage it and are responsible for planning the project and license budgets.
  • You develop the target architecture of our system landscape, bring it to decision-making in the management and implement it step by step.
  • You determine how our systems are connected and ensure that new applications are connected according to uniform standards.
  • You build an integration layer, including for registration, member identity and payment processing.
  • You are responsible for the data architecture and data platform. This creates a uniform view of our members and a basis for control, early warning and personalization.
  • You ensure high data quality, information security and data protection and make data usable for reporting, customer feedback and CRM.
  • You technically check software and system procurements and control providers and implementation partners.
  • You are centrally responsible for systems and data at movemi and coordinate with Group IT.

Requirements

  • At least ten years of experience: in digital, architecture or IT in the end customer business, of which at least five years in a management position with budget responsibility.
  • Master's degree (university, university of applied sciences): in computer science or business informatics
  • Certifications in enterprise architecture, such as TOGAF or iSAQB, and cloud are desired
  • Experience in replacing core systems, setting up data platforms and managing IT and implementation partners.
  • In-depth knowledge of architecture, integration, cloud and data protection.
  • The ability to communicate technical connections in an understandable way, to bring about decisions, to act in a negotiated manner and to implement them pragmatically.
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • English (good knowledge)
  • French (advantageous)

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Expert Discussion: In a specialist discussion, specific knowledge relevant for the execution of the job is requested by the manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, the team. If possible, there is time to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Discussion with case presentation: Specific expertise is examined on the basis of a case or other assessment instruments. After processing, the result of the leader is presented and then discussed together. Usually, the team is also represented at the presentation. As a rule, the conversations are conducted by the superior, without a representative from the HR. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition: After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks

Grado di occupazione: 100 %
