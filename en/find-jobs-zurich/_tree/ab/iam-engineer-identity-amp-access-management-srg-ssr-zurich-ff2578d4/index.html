IAM Engineer Identity & Access Management — SRG SSR

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

The Operations sector defines and directs SSR's strategic directions in technology and production. Secure and reliable management of identities and access guarantees staff and other authorized persons access rights to applications and systems.

With your team, you ensure the operation and evolution of our cloud-based IAM solution based on Omada Identity. You integrate new target systems, automate processes and evolve role and empowerment models. You contribute to the safe, efficient and traceable management of digital identities and access rights.

Your Mission

Within the Operations sector, you are involved in the following aspects of identity management and access.

  • You administer and supervise the Omada Identity cloud solution. To ensure its proper functioning, you diagnose incidents, analyze their causes and provide lasting solutions.
  • You design and implement connections to new target systems using connectors and APIs. You ensure the reliability of data exchanges.
  • You develop and automate processes that cover the entire life cycle of identities, especially when entering service, changing function and leaving.
  • In consultation with business leaders, you are changing workflows, role and empowerment models, and rights approval and re-certification processes.
  • You plan and implement changes, tests and production. You keep the technical documentation up to date in accordance with security, quality and governance requirements.
  • You work closely with business units, IT and security teams, external partners and suppliers. You participate in the design of technical solutions and share your knowledge within the team.

Your profile

  • You have completed vocational training or graduate studies in computer science, or have acquired equivalent qualifications in practice.
  • You have at least two years of professional experience in Identity and Access Management (IAM) or Identity Governance and Administration (IGA).

A practical experience with Omada Identity is an asset.

  • You have already integrated target systems with connectors and interfaces (e.g. REST API, SCIM or LDAP) and you know directory services such as Active Directory and Microsoft Entra ID.
  • You master scripting languages, such as PowerShell or Python, and you have already automated processes and workflows. Knowledge of Infrastructure as Code (IaC) is an asset.
  • You address problems with method and analysis, especially when diagnosing incidents. You know the role models, the principle of every privilege, and the processes of secure allocation of rights. You appreciate teamwork and adapt your communication to your audience.
  • You are fluent in French, German or Italian at level C1. You also have a good level of English to interact with partners and suppliers and work with technical documentation.

What we offer you

  • A responsibility position that allows you to contribute directly to the security of information and the reliable provision of digital work tools to the SSR.
  • A real flexibility to evolve an IAM solution in the cloud and automate key identity and empowerment management processes.
  • A varied technological environment and close collaboration with business units, IT specialists and external partners.
