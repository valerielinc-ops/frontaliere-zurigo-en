by January / February 2027 or by agreement

⭐ Do you want to care for and accompany people in demanding life situations? At the Competence Center Palliative Care at the University Hospital Zurich, you will bring your expertise and empathy to bear. Together with our team, you are committed to the quality of life of our patients.

🌇 What to expect from us

  • A dedicated, warm and professionally strong team on a specialized palliative care station with 8 beds
  • A demanding, versatile care with time and space for individual needs
  • Close interprofessional cooperation with medicine, pastoral care, psycho-oncology and other specialist areas
  • An open team culture where we learn from each other and support each other
  • Opportunities for professional and personal development

🌇 What you bring

  • Diploma as nurse HF/FH
  • Enjoyment of specialized palliative care and the accompaniment of patients and relatives
  • Ideally, a further education at level B2 or a CAS in Palliative Care – or the motivation to acquire this qualification
  • Empathy, independence, ability to reflect and enjoy interprofessional teamwork
  • Willingness to work in all shifts

🌇 What makes us special

With us, your professional skills count as well as your personality, your ideas and your attitude. An environment with a high level of nursing expertise and lived interprofessional cooperation awaits you. Here you can take on responsibility, actively shape specialized palliative care and develop professionally and personally. Together, we are committed to individual, dignified and holistic care for our patients and their relatives. For this post, dossiers from recruiters cannot be considered. Family friendliness

We help you to reconcile family and work. Mothers are entitled to at least 6 months of maternity leave (of which 16 weeks are paid in full), fathers to at least 2 months of paternity leave (of which 2 weeks are paid in full).

Insurance

Mobility
