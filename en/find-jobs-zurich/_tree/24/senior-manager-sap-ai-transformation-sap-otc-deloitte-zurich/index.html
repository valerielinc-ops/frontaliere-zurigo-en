Business Line: Technology & Transformation Location: Zurich Work Pattern Percentage: 80%-100% Start date: Ongoing Experience Level: Experienced hire About the Role Join Deloitte’s SAP practice and build a career at the forefront of business transformation, where innovation, industry insight, and leading technology come together to solve complex challenges. You’ll work with dynamic teams and leading organizations to modernize core processes, enable digital transformation, and help clients unlock lasting value through SAP-enabled solutions. In this SAP AI Transformation role, you’ll contribute to Agentic ERP transformations that help clients advance toward the autonomous enterprise. Combining SAP expertise with AI-enabled delivery approaches, cross-functional collaboration, and industry insight, you’ll help address complex business challenges and create measurable client impact. How you can make an impact As a (Senior) Manager in Deloitte’s SAP practice, with a focus on SAP Order to Cash, you will work side by side with functional and technical client team members to rapidly prototype and deliver high-impact solutions that support Agentic ERP transformations. With Deloitte Ascend™, you’ll leverage AI accelerators and SAP capabilities to help clients modernize business processes and advance toward the autonomous enterprise. This role requires a highly motivated professional who moves with speed and precision, builds practical solutions, and engages confidently with senior stakeholders and delivery teams to help generate measurable business impact.

  • Support SAP Order to Cash workstreams by identifying opportunities, tracking work-plan activities, identifying dependencies, risks, and issues, and preparing deliverables.
  • Participate in and facilitate opportunity-identification, fit-to-standard, and autonomous solution-design workshops.
  • Translate commercial and Order to Cash improvement opportunities into SAP solutions and support configuration across the end-to-end Order to Cash process.
  • Support coordination of commercial integration requirements with supply chain, finance and accounting, tax, and reporting teams.
  • Contribute to data migration, testing, defect resolution, cutover, deployment, hypercare, and stabilization activities.
  • Apply an AI-native approach to SAP delivery by using AI tools to accelerate analysis, solution design, prototyping, testing, and documentation, while maintaining human oversight to review, validate, and curate outputs.
  • Communicate project progress, decisions, risks, and action items with client and Deloitte team members; support training, change adoption, post-go-live activities, proposal development, and reusable SAP practice assets. What you bring to succeed
  • 9+ years of experience in SAP consulting, enterprise resource planning (ERP) implementation, or SAP/ERP technology transformation in a consulting or industry environment.
  • Experience supporting at least one full-lifecycle SAP implementation from project initiation through go-live and hypercare and experience supporting an SAP S/4HANA implementation.
  • Hands-on functional configuration experience in at least one of the following: SAP Sales and Distribution (SD), or SAP Billing and Revenue Innovation Management (BRIM).
  • Experience working directly with client or business stakeholders to support requirements gathering, testing, or deployment activities on at least one SAP implementation, transformation, or support project.
  • Fluency in written and spoken English and German is essential, proficiency in additional languages is an advantage.
  • Ability to travel up to 50%, on average, based on the work you do and the clients and industries/sectors you serve. What sets you apart
  • Experience applying artificial intelligence (AI) tools - including generative AI, intelligent automation, machine learning, conversational AI, or AI agents - to support enterprise transformation initiatives, including solution design, implementation, testing, or human-in-the-loop review and validation.
  • Experience supporting or facilitating SAP design workshops, demonstrations, or testing activities.
  • Experience supporting SAP cloud programs or SAP transformation programs deployed across multiple countries.
  • Experience applying SAP Activate, Agile, or Waterfall methodologies on SAP implementation programs.
  • Experience contributing to proposals, estimates, solution designs, or client presentations.
  • One or more SAP certifications Your team Deloitte’s SAP practice helps clients design, implement, and sustain SAP-enabled transformations to enable an Autonomous Enterprise. Our teams combine industry knowledge, functional experience, and technology capabilities to support first-time implementations, business-led enhancements, and large-scale SAP S/4HANA programs. Professionals in the practice work with leading organizations across industries to modernize operations, improve process performance, and unlock value through cloud, automation, analytics, artificial intelligence, and machine learning.
