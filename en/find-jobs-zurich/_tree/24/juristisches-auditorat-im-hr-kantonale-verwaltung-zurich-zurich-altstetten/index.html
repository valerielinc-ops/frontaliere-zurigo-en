‘ “An employee gives a person admitted a high five, is this critical in terms of personnel law?” Exactly with such questions you deal with us in everyday life. Does that make you curious? Then read on. Maybe this place is right for you. The Personnel Law team advises and supports executives in all personnel law issues. Immediately or by agreement, the team personnel law is looking for a new team member. You can expect a 12-month auditorate with exciting tasks and versatile insights into legal practice in the public service. You do that with us.

  • You clarify legal issues in a versatile and not everyday environment and advise in personnel, social security and general administrative law

  • You write directives, legal documents, agreements and leaflets, check employment certificates and support HR projects
  • You maintain and update the internal knowledge base
  • You work with the conception and implementation of internal training events in the field of personnel law
  • Completed legal studies
  • Interest and curiosity in legal topics around employees
  • You are open to new things, ask questions and like to communicate with different people
  • Working in a team is important to you
