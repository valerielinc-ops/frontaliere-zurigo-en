Full description

The Human Resources department is part of the administration. The approximately 25 employees design and are responsible for all tasks of human resources management – from recruitment, consulting and development of JuWe employees to personnel law and administrative services, HR controlling and strategic personnel management. As a recruitment coordinator (temporary for 6 months), you ensure that our recruiting in the background works professionally, efficiently and reliably. You are a central administrative hub in the recruitment process and support our team Recruitment & Assessment with organizational talent, accuracy and overview. You do that with us.

  • You create and publish job advertisements, manage applications in the applicant management system and provide complete documentation
  • You prepare employment documents and ensure timely processing
  • You support the Recruitment & Assessment management in administrative tasks, statistical evaluations and selected tasks in the area of talent acquisition
  • You work on the further development of administrative processes, templates and tools
  • You provide professional, friendly and service-oriented candidate communication that you bring with you
  • You have a commercial education and the certificate HR-Assistant or HR-Assistant
  • You work very structured, accurate and reliable and keep track of many parallel tasks
  • You enjoy administrative activities and clear processes
  • You are IT-affine and safe with MS Office and ideally with applicant management systems
  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing
