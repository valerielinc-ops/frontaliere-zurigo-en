Administrative Assistance for the Medical Data Science Group (m/f/d)

20%-40%, Zurich, permanent

The Medical Data Science Group is part of the Institute for Machine Learning and belongs to the Department of Computer Science at ETH Zurich. It offers an international, lively and demanding working environment.

In this varied position, you proactively support the group management and their team in administrative and organizational tasks and take on a coordinating role with a versatile field of activity. It awaits you an exciting task with a lot of personal responsibility. You are also involved in the administration team of the institute. Job entry is possible immediately or by agreement.

Job description

In this versatile function, you support the group management as the central contact person in organizational and administrative matters.

Administrative tasks

  • They support the research group and the group management in a wide range of processes, such as coordinating internal processes, maintaining documentation and handling administrative inquiries.

Personnel administration

  • They record entries and exits as well as other personal data mutations in the ETH information system
  • You are the first contact person for employees of the group for personnel administrative questions, such as the recording of absences, etc.
  • They also look after academic guests

Financial management

  • You support in the management of funds and budgets, coordinate expenses and payments and take over financial project controlling including reporting.

Event organization and travel

  • Planning and implementation of conferences, including events
  • Planning business trips and care of international guests

Teamwork at the Institute

  • Active participation in the administration team for joint planning and implementation of events and for effective communication within the institute
  • Deputy of Teamkolleg: inside for holidays and absences from illness

Profile

  • Commercial education or equivalent qualification completed
  • Several years of experience in a demanding administrative function, ideally in the university environment
  • Strong organisational and coordination skills as well as a forward-looking, exact and structured working method
  • High level of independence, initiative and reliability
  • Professional and service-oriented approach towards internal and external contacts:
  • Enjoying an international, dynamic and versatile working environment
  • Very good user knowledge of common MS Office applications and affinity for digital tools
  • Discreet and trustworthy handling of sensitive information
  • Very good communication skills in German and English

We offer

  • Become part of ETH Zurich, which not only supports your professional development, but also actively contributes to positive changes in society
  • They are integrated into the working group (professor, postdoctoral, doctoral, current group size: 14 members) as well as into the admin team of the institute and the department. There is a regular exchange between each other and there is a supportive and open atmosphere.
  • After consultation with your supervisor, you can divide your working time between office and home office
  • Attractive working conditions and numerous opportunities for personal and professional development
  • Discounts such as public transport subscriptions and car sharing, wide sports offer of the ASVZ, child care etc.
  • Attractive pension services through the PUBLICA pension scheme
