Full description

As a Creative Automation Specialist, you will develop the next generation of creative workflows with us and help us to build the appropriate processes and structures with which we can create brand-compliant communication measures more efficiently and optimize them for different channels and target groups.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Headquarters, Pfingstweidstrasse 60b, 8005 Zurich

Mansioni

  • Empower our creative teams using AI and Creative Automation, establish quality and governance standards and promote systemic thinking for modular applications and generative design principles
  • You will continuously evaluate suitable tools and frameworks, validate them based on concrete use cases and ensure seamless integration into our existing structures
  • Based on the existing infrastructure, you will design a platform for the integration of models and the development of skills and agentic workflows in creation
  • Support the creation of generative and automated content and maintain the interface with the media team and ad platforms
  • In addition, you work closely with other departments in the role of business engineer and translate business requirements from marketing into precise technical specifications for product development.

Requirements

  • 4-7 years: Creative Automation, AI-powered content production and workflow automation
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): Marketing / MarTech
  • You have several years of professional experience at the interface between technology, marketing and creative production, ideally in the field of creative automation or business engineering
  • You are familiar with cloud infrastructures, APIs and agentic workflows and have relevant experience in integrating AI models and developing your own skills and agents
  • You bring a design background or have a strong sense of design principles, brand guidelines and creation processes
  • You are familiar with tools and workflows for the automated creation, adaptation and display of digital advertising and media content
  • You can see where automation creates real value for brand, user and company and assess results critically and unbiased
  • You are a team player and promote an open feedback culture
  • You have a strong hands-on mentality, work independently and solve complex problems pragmatically
  • You speak fluent German and confident English
  • Please supplement your application documents with a letter of motivation and work samples or case studies so that we get a first insight into your work
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • English (negotiable)
