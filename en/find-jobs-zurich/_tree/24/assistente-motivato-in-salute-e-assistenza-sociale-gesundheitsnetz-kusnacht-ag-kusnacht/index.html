Description

The Gesundheitsnetz Küsnacht AG (GNK), which comprises the Tägerhalde and Wangensbach senior and healthcare centres with around 130 residents, as well as a day centre, Spitex, housing for older people and counselling, and employs around 240 people in total, is a non-profit joint-stock company wholly owned by the municipality of Küsnacht.

For the Tägerhalde site, we are looking for a refreshing personality to strengthen our team.

Your new responsibilities

  • Shared responsibility for the conscientious support and care of residents with varying care needs
  • Conducting activity sessions with residents
  • Participating in implementing the primary nursing concept and documenting services provided
  • Taking on housekeeping duties
  • Assisting with the onboarding of new employees

Requirements

  • Certificate as an Assistant in Health and Social Care (EBA) or SRK Nursing Aide
  • Professional experience in the field of care and support / long-term care
  • A warm and appreciative attitude
  • Enjoyment of and the ability to support and care for older people with individual habits and needs
  • Good German language skills, both spoken and written, as well as IT user skills
