Full description

Data protection officer Legal / intern 100% "Our central task is to protect the personal data and thus the privacy of the residents of the canton of Zurich." This is what the Data Protection Officer stands for as an independent supervisory authority. It supervises the data processing of the cantonal administration, the municipalities and the other authorities and public institutions in the canton of Zurich. Together with her team, she advises public bodies, carries out controls and informs the population about data protection issues and privacy issues. For our legal team, we are looking for a legal trainee on 1 February 2027 or by agreement, for a limited period of one year. Your

  • You advise public bodies and private individuals, assess decrees, projects and reports relevant to data protection, create publications (web articles) and participate in topic-specific projects. You independently conduct legal investigations in various areas of law and are the contact person within the supervisory authority for a specific thematic area (e.g. video surveillance). You have a completed university degree in law and are interested in data protection, computer science and information security. In public law you feel comfortable and digitalization topics fascinate you. You enjoy working in an interdisciplinary environment, being communicative, service-oriented and responsible.
  • We offer this
  • We offer you an interesting and versatile activity in a socially highly topical environment. You get to know the data protection law of the Canton of Zurich and can immediately apply new knowledge to varied cases. You will also get an in-depth insight into administrative law and information security issues. You can expect a committed and motivated team and you can count on sound training. Our employment conditions are attractive and in accordance with cantonal guidelines. Your place of work is in the city of Zurich in the immediate vicinity of the main railway station. Did we get your interest? We look forward to your application! Please submit your documents exclusively via our online tool. If you have any questions, please contact Sushen Pandya, Team Leader Legal Department, Tel. 044 360 99 99.
