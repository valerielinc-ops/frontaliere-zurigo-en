Full description

Shape the future. Yours and ours.

Would you like to help people to lead a self-determined life? And do you want to use your creative scope to reconcile your professional and personal ambitions? Then we can offer you an exciting prospect: at Swiss Life you will be able to deploy your individual talents and expertise in a stimulating and flexible work environment. What’s more, you will be given a good measure of responsibility and can thus contribute to shaping the future of Swiss Life.

Your area of responsibility

  • Examine current scientific questions about the Swiss pension system, carry out statistical analyses and derive well-founded recommendations from them
  • As co-author or co-author together with the head of Research Prevention, we write our studies, participate in project planning and write accompanying texts for the website, factsheets and other formats
  • Prepare study results in an understandable and visually appealing way and make them accessible to a broad public through presentations, social media and media relations
  • Together with the content marketing team, responsible for editorial planning and creation of social media contributions to our research topics
  • Bring your own ideas and further develop Swiss Life’s prevention research together with the head of research prevention

Your strengths

  • Master’s degree or doctorate in economics as well as initial experience in a scientific context or in research, for example from a professional activity or a doctorate with a related focus
  • Great interest in the Swiss pension system and its political contexts. In-depth expertise is a plus
  • In-depth knowledge of R or Python as well as an experienced handling of Excel
  • Analytical and creative thinking, an independent and precise working method as well as initiative, commitment and pleasure in working together in a team
  • A sense of communicating complex relationships in a comprehensible, concise and appealing way, both in writing and orally. Very good knowledge of German and English, other national languages are an advantage

Your benefits We help our employees to shape their professional development and their private life to their best advantage and throughout all phases of their life. Our attractive benefits, such as options for working from home, 25 to 30 days of leave, 3rd pillar premium contributions and a broad range of continued training options contribution to this goal.
