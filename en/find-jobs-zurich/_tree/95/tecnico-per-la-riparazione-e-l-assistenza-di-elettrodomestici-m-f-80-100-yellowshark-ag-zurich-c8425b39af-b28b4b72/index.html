01 Future employer

We are recruiting for this position on behalf of an established and successful company in the household appliance service sector in the Lucerne region.

Our client is a competent, customer-oriented service provider distinguished by high service quality and many years of experience in repair and maintenance. The company places great emphasis on satisfied customers, high-quality work and an appreciative corporate culture.

02 Your responsibilities

  • You repair and service household appliances from all common brands directly at customers' premises, independently and with a high level of technical expertise
  • You quickly identify faults, determine which spare parts are needed and realistically estimate the work involved
  • You provide our customers with expert advice when purchasing new appliances, answer technical questions and provide practical tips on use and care
  • You document all work and customer appointments carefully and digitally

  • You are used to working independently, but also work very well in a team
  • You present yourself in a friendly and well-groomed manner
  • You have a category B driving licence
