You can expect a varied and responsible field of activity in one of the most renowned university clinics in Europe. Ready to make a difference together with us?

A diverse range of tasks is waiting for you

  • Interface for personnel administrative tasks, in particular in the recruitment process, entry and exit, maintenance of personnel master data and introduction programmes
  • Communication with internal and external contacts on behalf of the clinic directorate, as well as performance recording if necessary
  • Optimization and further development of internal processes and workflows, e.g. administrative support and coordination of the internal training commission
  • Preparation and preparation of presentations, protocols, reports and other documents for the clinic management
  • Monitoring deadlines and deadlines and ensuring careful, confidential and timely handling of administrative tasks
  • Deputy Directorate Assistance (scheduling of appointments, calendar coordination, correspondence and organisation of meetings)
  • Deputy in the organisation and coordination of internal and external events, meetings, training and clinical events as well as in the updating of social media channels
  • Other administrative and organisational support to the Clinic Directorate and ensuring smooth running of the Directorate Secretariat

What you bring

  • As a prerequisite, you will have a commercial education or a comparable qualification, ideally supplemented by 2-5 years of professional experience in the health care sector as well as an assistant function.
  • Very good German and English skills
  • An independent, structured and reliable way of working
  • A cool head even in hectic situations as well as strong communication and discretion
  • Motivation to go the extra mile
