Full description

Location: Zurich Work Pattern Percentage: 80-100% Help transform our clients’ organisations to support their business strategy Deloitte's Human Capital Consulting practice in Switzerland is at the forefront of transforming how organizations attract, develop, and retain their most valuable asset—their people. We partner with leading Swiss and international companies to reimagine their HR operating models, implement cutting-edge HR technology solutions, and build future-ready organizations. Our team combines deep HR expertise with advanced technology knowledge to deliver transformational outcomes across strategy, technology, and organizational change. We work across diverse industries and geographies, helping clients navigate the evolving world of work, embrace digital innovation, and unlock the full potential of their workforce. We are the largest Human Capital practice in the Swiss market and are widely recognized by our clients for our ability to walk in their shoes as a true partner, bring leading insight, expertise and consistently deliver high-quality work How you can make an impact We are looking for a person who enjoys addressing business human capital questions in an analytical manner, who has the patience to structure decision making, and who strives to develop pragmatic recommendations, tempered by experience. Our aspiration is to help clients to gain or maintain competitive advantage against an ever-changing market context. Our clients value us for our ability to see the vision through to reality, producing a plan and approach to successfully bring our client's human capital strategy to life. Your job responsibilities and how you can make an impact that matters. You will: Work directly with Swiss and international clients throughout all phases of the organizational change management lifecycle, from initial assessment and planning of the organizational change management strategy to execution, hypercare and adoption review with the right metrics.

  • Introduce, embed and drive best practice change management frameworks, governance, processes, and tools to support successful organizational transitions and stakeholder engagement at all stages.
  • Act as a key team member liaising with client leaders and internal stakeholders to align change initiatives with business objectives and ensure effective communication and collaboration.
  • Contribute to Deloitte’s business development efforts and proposal creation by partnering with Human Capital, other Deloitte service offerings, and account teams to design compelling change management solutions.
  • Support the growth and enhancement of Deloitte’s change management capabilities and service offerings within the Human Capital practice. Who we are looking for
  • Strong academic background, ideally a Master’s degree or MBA from a reputable institution, or equivalent professional experience.
  • With a masters degree/MBA minimum 3 years of relevant experience in Consulting or a similar role within the Human Capital domain, preferably with exposure to international projects.
  • Solid experience in people strategy and change management, particularly in contexts such as large technology implementation programs and/or organizational business transformations.
  • Experience contributing to client projects related to Learning & Development, Training & Re-skilling, with familiarity in ERP systems (e.g., SAP, Oracle, Microsoft) and other system implementations (e.g., Salesforce, Workday, SuccessFactors).
  • Demonstrated experience in AI-driven transformation initiatives is required.
  • Strong affinity with technology and a deep understanding of its impact on the workforce, including how digital transformation shapes employee experience, skills requirements, and organizational change.
  • Willingness and ability to support business development and offering enhancement initiatives.
  • Demonstrated capability in managing stakeholder relationships strategically.
  • Strong interpersonal and communication skills, with the ability to engage effectively with clients and internal teams.
  • Positive, proactive attitude with a focus on delivering high-quality client service and contributing to team success.
  • Fluency in written and spoken English and German or French is essential; proficiency in additional languages is an advantage.
  • Proven experience in Artificial Intelligence (AI) applications in Human Capital or Change Management is a plus, including how AI can enhance employee engagement, learning, communication, and decision-making processes.
  • Experience in Project Management and proven ability to manage multiple stakeholders or coordinating cross-functional teams in complex environments is a strong plus.
  • Willingness to travel within Switzerland and internationally as required. Your team Become part of a diverse, multinational and highly motivated team.
