To strengthen our eye center, we are looking for a committed personality as soon as possible or by agreement.

Optometrist or MPA with experience in ophthalmology (50 – 60 %)

For us, the focus is on people. If you want to work with heart, precision and team spirit, these are your tasks:

  • Conducting visual diagnostics using state-of-the-art devices in close cooperation with our doctors.
  • Assistance in the planning and organization of consultation hours as well as in team and quality management. They contribute to a smooth and well-coordinated everyday practice.
  • Actively contribute ideas for a modern and patient-oriented practice design.

Become part of the Zollikerberg Eye Centre, which in cooperation with the Eye Clinic of the Waid and Triemli City Hospitals offers a wide range of services in the fields of ophthalmology.

About the Zollikerberg Hospital We stand for a comprehensive range of services and strong cooperation partners. With a public service mandate and 173 beds, the Zollikerberg Hospital contributes to the optimal medical care of the greater Zurich area. Every year, more than 11,000 inpatients and 75,000 outpatients in all insurance classes can feel comfortable and in good hands. Our broadly based primary care includes internal medicine, gynecology and obstetrics with neonatology, general surgery, an interdisciplinary emergency center and orthopedics with specialized spinal surgery.
