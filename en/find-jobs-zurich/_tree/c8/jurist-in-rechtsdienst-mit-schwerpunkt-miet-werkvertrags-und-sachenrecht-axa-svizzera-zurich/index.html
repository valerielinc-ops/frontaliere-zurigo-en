AXA-ARAG, as one of the leading legal protection insurance companies in Switzerland, is growing and developing rapidly. Our ultimate goal is to continually improve the customer experience at a high level by breaking new ground, thinking creatively and transforming through digital change.

Do you value interdisciplinary collaboration and open, humorous exchanges within the team? Are you ready to go the extra mile for our customers? Do you not get worried lines when changes happen, but do you see the possibilities?

If yes - then you have come to the right place! We offer a working environment in which thinking is required, teamwork is highly valued and flexibility is encouraged.

At AXA-ARAG, we protect the interests of our customers in private and business life, in traffic and on the Internet. As an independent subsidiary of AXA Switzerland, AXA-ARAG is the leading legal protection insurance company in Switzerland with around 200 employees.

This is what awaits you

  • You advise our private and corporate customers by telephone and in writing in all areas of real estate law (including tenancy law, contract law, property law and insurance law)
  • You analyze a wide range of issues and solve problems in an uncomplicated and pragmatic manner for the benefit of our customers
  • You conduct negotiations independently with policyholders, counterparties and authorities
  • You are IT-savvy and actively help with the implementation of new systems

This is what you bring with you

  • You will find the best solutions for our customers efficiently and practically
  • You are pragmatic, flexible and have strong communication skills and excellent negotiation skills
  • You have professional experience in the area of ​​real estate law (tenancy law, contract law, etc.)
  • You have a Swiss university degree in law
  • Your German is very stylish, any other foreign language is an additional plus
  • Your resilience allows you to remain calm even in hectic times
