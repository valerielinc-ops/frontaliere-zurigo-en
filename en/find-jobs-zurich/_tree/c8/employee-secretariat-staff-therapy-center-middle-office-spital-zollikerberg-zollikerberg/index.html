Employee Secretariat (therapy center) Middle Office / — Spital Zollikerberg, Zollikerberg (ZH).

Domain: Administration.

Welcome to your new place of work.

Sandra Staubli, Head of Secretariat/Disposition, as well as the team offer you at 1. January 2027 or by appointment a job with a workload of 80-100% as

Employee / staff secretariat (therapy center)

If you too want to make a difference in people’s lives, these are your tasks:

  • Responsible for the desk service at the reception with personal patient contact as well as internal and external telephone calls
  • Appointment coordination of therapy appointments (individual therapies, series, group therapies, prevention offers, special programs, nutritional advice, occupational therapy, physiotherapy and speech therapy as well as self-payer offers) etc.
  • Assumption of administrative tasks in the front and back office such as scheduling patient and therapist appointments, accounting, postal mail, rejections etc.
  • Management of all fitness center customers incl. Subscription Creation
  • Participation in future-oriented projects

Become part of Spital Zollikerberg Therapy Center, which combines various therapeutic services and a wide range of services at a high level of quality under one roof. As a competent partner, we play an important role in the health care of the Zollikerberg hospital region.

In our 360° tour, get a first impression of your new place of work.

To make you feel comfortable with us, many benefits are waiting for you. Just to name a few:

  • Own pension fund with super-compulsory participation of the employer
  • Worldwide private insurance in case of accident
  • Free use of the fitness room on the Zollikerberg site
  • Generously subsidized crèche solution and a family atmosphere
  • Financial support to public transport subscription or bicycle

All amenities for employees can be found here . If you also want to be the best version of yourself, you will find the possibilities with us.

This convinces us

  • Completed training with professional experience in a similar position
  • Experience in medical terminology as well as good English skills of great advantage
  • Good MS-Office knowledge and affinity for IT programs
  • Resilient and service-oriented personality with a networked thinking and enjoyment of teamwork
  • Our and your values: appreciation, partnership, commitment, transparency and seeing the whole

About the Zollikerberg Hospital We stand for a comprehensive range of services and strong cooperation partners. With a public service mandate and 173 beds, the Zollikerberg Hospital contributes to the optimal medical care of the greater Zurich area. Every year, more than 11,000 inpatients and 75,000 outpatients in all insurance classes can feel comfortable and in good hands. Our broadly based primary care includes internal medicine, gynecology and obstetrics with neonatology, general surgery, an interdisciplinary emergency center and orthopedics with specialized spinal surgery.
