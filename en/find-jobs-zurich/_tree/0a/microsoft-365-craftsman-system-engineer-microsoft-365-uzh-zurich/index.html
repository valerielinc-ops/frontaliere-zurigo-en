Central computer science

Job opening immediately or by agreement, indefinitely

Central Computer Science provides IT services for students, researchers, institutes, faculties and central services of the University of Zurich (UZH). Our Microsoft Services team is responsible for the operation, monitoring and further development of the entire Microsoft backend infrastructure as well as the Microsoft 365 services for all members of UZH.

Tasks

We are looking for a dedicated personality as a Microsoft 365 Craftsman (System Engineer Microsoft 365) with a focus on Microsoft 365, SharePoint Online and SPFx. As part of our agile systems engineering team, you will ensure that our SharePoint Online platform and our new intranet UZHub function reliably, performantly and securely and continuously evolve. As a Craftsman, you tackle yourself: You run services, develop solutions, haggle on existing functions and bring new ideas together with our partners to fly.

Catalogue of tasks

  • Ensuring the stable Microsoft 365 and SharePoint Online operation of the University of Zurich
  • Technical responsibility for the operation and further development of our new intranet UZHub in close cooperation with the Product Owner
  • Planning and implementing new features and integrations with Microsoft cloud services and systems
  • Develop modern SharePoint solutions, web parts and extensions with SPFx and Microsoft Graph
  • Analyze and fix faults (second and third-level support) as well as continuous monitoring and quality assurance
  • Automate recurring processes and workflows with PowerShell and Power Automate
  • Participation in projects and management of smaller projects
  • Collaboration with internal and external partners as well as stakeholders from across the university

Profile

  • In-depth knowledge and experience with Microsoft Cloud services (Microsoft 365, SharePoint Online, Power Automate, Power Platform)
  • Experience with SharePoint Framework (SPFx), modern web technologies and integration of Microsoft 365 services via Microsoft Graph
  • Continuous training in the field and in Agile methods (Scrum, DevOps etc.)
  • Experience and know-how in scripting and good PowerShell knowledge
  • Good conceptual skills, initiative, commitment, independent and exact working methods, resilience, teamwork, openness and skill in dealing with customers, colleagues and manufacturers
  • Higher technical training in computer science or comparable education/training
  • Stylistic expressive ability orally and in writing in German and English
