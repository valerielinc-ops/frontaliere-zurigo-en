Full description

Central IT

Start date immediately or by agreement, permanent Central IT provides IT services for students, researchers, institutes, faculties and central services of the University of Zurich (UZH). Our “Microsoft Services” team is responsible for operating, monitoring and developing the entire Microsoft backend infrastructure, as well as Microsoft 365 services for all members of UZH.

Tasks

We are looking for a committed individual as Microsoft 365 Craftsman (System Engineer Microsoft 365), with a focus on Microsoft 365, SharePoint Online and SPFx. As part of our agile system engineering team, you will ensure that our SharePoint Online platform and our new intranet UZHub operate reliably, perform well and remain secure, while continuing to evolve. As a Craftsman, you get hands-on: you operate services, develop solutions, refine existing features and bring new ideas to life together with our partners.

List of duties

  • Ensure the stable operation of Microsoft 365 and SharePoint Online at the University of Zurich
  • Take technical responsibility for operating and developing our new intranet UZHub in close collaboration with the Product Owner
  • Plan and implement new features and integrations with Microsoft cloud services and surrounding systems
  • Develop modern SharePoint solutions, web parts and extensions with SPFx and Microsoft Graph
  • Analyze and resolve incidents (second- and third-level support), as well as carry out continuous monitoring and quality assurance
  • Automate recurring processes and workflows with PowerShell and Power Automate
  • Contribute to projects and lead smaller projects
  • Collaborate with internal and external partners and stakeholders from across the university

Profile

  • In-depth knowledge and experience with Microsoft cloud services (Microsoft 365, SharePoint Online, Power Automate, Power Platform)
  • Experience with SharePoint Framework (SPFx), modern web technologies and integrating Microsoft 365 services via Microsoft Graph
  • Ongoing professional development in the field and in agile methods (Scrum, DevOps, etc.)
  • Experience and know-how in scripting and good PowerShell skills are an advantage
  • Strong conceptual skills, initiative, commitment, an independent and precise working style, resilience, ability to work in a team, openness and skill in dealing with customers, colleagues and manufacturers
  • Higher technical education in computer science or equivalent education and training
  • Excellent written and spoken communication skills in German and English
