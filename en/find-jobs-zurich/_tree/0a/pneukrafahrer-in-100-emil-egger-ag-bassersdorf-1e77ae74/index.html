Full description

The EMIL EGGER Group is the leading provider of heavy goods logistics in Switzerland. In the areas of crane work, special transports, industrial removals and rental lifts, more than 230 employees are employed. With the comprehensive special know-how, individual and innovative customer solutions are developed every day. At our location in Bassersdorf, we are looking for team reinforcement in the heavy goods logistics department. Become part of our crane team! You love big machines, exciting missions and want to go high? Then you are exactly right with us! Your tasks

  • you operate our pneumatic cranes from 80 to 250 tons – accurate and with an overview
  • You coordinate the crane work on our construction sites
  • You help to upgrade our pneu and crawler cranes
  • You work closely with internal teams and external partners

You bring that with you

  • Experience as a pneucrane driver ideally in the assembly and dismantling of construction cranes
  • Crane ID Cat. A / Driver ID Cat. C
  • Self-employed, precise and responsible work
  • Team spirit, reliability and joy in tackling

We offer

  • Varied missions with modern infrastructure
  • An established, motivated team and a family together
  • Progressive employment conditions and attractive social benefits
  • A lived du-culture – with us, people count
