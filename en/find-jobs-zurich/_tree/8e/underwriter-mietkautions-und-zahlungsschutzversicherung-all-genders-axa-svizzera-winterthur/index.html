Full description

80-100%, Winterthur / Smart Working

Shape your future – together with us!

Become part of our underwriting team in the area of rental deposit and payment protection insurance. We are looking for a motivated team member who is willing to quickly familiarize himself with the products and processes, ensure operational processes and continuously ensure our service quality.

You can expect a dynamic, transformation-oriented environment in which you and your team can further expand the efficiency and quality of our underwriting – and at the same time drive your own professional and personal development. Become part of a team that actively lives AXA's values and works together to achieve ambitious goals.

In the Property & Casualty segment, we create freedom for our private and corporate customers with innovative products, convincing services and simple digital processes. We offer a wide range of standardised and individual insurance, legal protection and financial security in the area of credit and surety.

What to expect

  • Processing the rental deposit and payment protection insurance business
  • Carrying out technical risk assessments and credit checks of policyholders
  • Passing of acceptance decisions
  • Professional support and support of the sales force, the online channel and our customers and partners
  • Actively contributing ideas for optimizing and further developing processes in your area

What you bring to the table

  • Basic commercial training with several years of professional experience
  • Analytical thinking skills to understand insurance products holistically
  • Very good knowledge of German, Italian and French, English is an advantage
  • Experience in the areas of risk/credit assessment, rental deposit or payment protection insurance is an advantage
  • Good IT skills and enjoyment of working with digital tools
  • Independent, precise, customer- and team-oriented way of working
  • High motivation, initiative, reliability and integrity as well as strong social skills
  • Motivation to learn new things and actively participate in the optimization of our processes

What we offer you We are convinced that satisfied employees perform at their best. That's why we invest in benefits that offer tangible benefits – beyond the usual.

Flexibility and freedom: Smart Working (flexible working model) annual working hours, 5 weeks of vacation (6 weeks from the age of 50), vacation gift 2 weeks every 5 years, vacation purchase up to 10 additional days/per year

Job and family: Family-friendly meeting times, 22 weeks of maternity leave, 8 weeks of leave for the other parent, crèche subsidies, company family allowances, days off for caring for relatives and in challenging, personal situations

Pension and insurance: Attractive pension fund with extensive extra-mandatory benefits including optional savings options, up to 30% discount on insurance products

Flexible benefits: AXA share plan (employee participation), CHF 500 per year on the Swibeco benefits platform, discounted lunch or lunch card, Smart Working flat rate CHF 200 per year

Mobility: Half-Fare travelcard refund, Reka Pay up to CHF 1,000 per year (20% discount), fleet discount, free e-charging station, AXA rental bikes

Healthy balance in everyday working life: Free check-ups, annual health weeks, a wide range of exercise offers (online/offline), massage offers, relaxation rooms, employee care, online health portal, various seminars/events on health prevention and mental health

Development: 3 days of individual learning time, free coaching, wide range of learning and development opportunities, financial participation in further training, promotion of internal mobility, talent development offers, seminars for employees 55+ (AXA Horizon), involvement in communities on various topics, participation in innovation projects (Innoday, AXA Hackathon, UnConference), 2 days for volunteer assignments (Environment & Social Issues)
