Full description

University of Zurich, Psychological Institute

Job opening 01.01.2027 or by agreement, temporary

The research group Experimental psychopathology and psychotherapy under the direction of Prof. Dr. Birgit Kleim at the University of Zurich is looking for a committed personality with an interest in clinical and scientific work in the field of trauma, stress and resilience. The office combines clinical activity in the stress, trauma, anxiety and resilience (STAR) outpatient (40-50%) with research in the working group Experimental Psychopathology and Psychotherapy (40-50%). It offers the special opportunity to combine modern diagnostics and evidence-based trauma therapy with innovative, translational research and psychotherapy research and to help shape new treatment approaches from experimental investigation to clinical application. The post shall be limited to one year with the possibility of renewal.

Tasks

Clinical activity in the STAR outpatient (40-50%, by agreement)

  • Conducting evidence-based, trauma-focused psychotherapy in patients with trauma-related disorders as well as stress- and anxiety-related mental illnesses
  • Diagnosis, treatment planning and psychotherapeutic treatment of patients: insides with trauma follow-up disorders
  • Application and further development of modern, scientifically sound treatment approaches, including the integration of innovative therapeutic methods into clinical care
  • Participation in regular clinical supervision, intervision and case meetings

Scientific activity (40-50%, by agreement)

  • Development, initiation and implementation of own research projects in the field of trauma, stress, resilience, as well as prevention and intervention approaches
  • Research and evaluation of innovative psychotherapeutic interventions and
  • Use of modern research methods, such as Ecological Momentary Assessment (EMA), passive sensing, experimental paradigms and longitudinal investigation designs
  • Connection of experimental, neuroscientific and clinical research approaches in the sense of translational psychotherapy research
  • augmentation strategies, including digital and virtual reality-based approaches
  • Investigation of therapy courses, mechanisms of change and individual differences in therapy response
  • Publication of scientific results and presentation at international conferences
  • Participation in the acquisition of third-party funding and in the establishment of new research collaborations

Profile

  • Completed university degree in psychology
  • Started or advanced training in psychotherapy, interested in evidence-based and trauma-focused procedures
  • Doctorate in psychology, clinical psychology, neuroscience, psychotherapy research or a related field
  • Enjoying the link between clinical work and empirical research
  • Initiative, scientific curiosity and interest in the development of own research projects and innovative treatment concepts
  • Good knowledge of German for clinical activity, very good knowledge of English for scientific work
  • Teamwork, commitment and enjoyment of interdisciplinary collaboration

We also expressly welcome applications from candidates who are still in psychotherapeutic training. Clinical experience in trauma therapy is beneficial, but not a mandatory requirement. Crucial are a high motivation to develop in this area and a strong interest in clinically relevant research. We offer them A direct link between clinical practice and research They work in a specialized university ambulatory with a wide range of trauma, stress and anxiety-related disorders and are at the same time part of an active, internationally networked research group. The close connection between care and research makes it possible to immediately address clinically relevant questions, to investigate them scientifically and to introduce new findings into treatment. High-quality clinical monitoring and training In the STAR-Ambulatorium you will be accompanied by two experienced clinical supervisors. In addition, external supervision is available. We support your clinical development and provide a stimulating environment for deepening your psychotherapeutic skills, especially in the field of trauma therapy. Space for your own research ideas They are given the opportunity and are supported in developing and implementing their own research projects. Thematic focal points can include mechanisms of psychopathology, especially PTSD, mechanisms of change in the context of psychotherapy, therapeutic augmentation, digital interventions, virtual reality, everyday survey methods and neuroscientific foundations of psychopathology and psychotherapeutic changes. An excellent translational research environment Our research group combines experimental psychopathology, clinical psychology, neuroscience and psychotherapy research. They benefit from active international networking and a wide range of cooperation opportunities within the University of Zurich, including with the Center for Neurosciences Zurich, the Healthy Longevity Centre, University Medicine and the Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich. In addition, there are collaborations with a network of established psychotherapeutic practices, which open up additional opportunities for practical research projects, clinical studies and the investigation of treatment courses. We offer a collegial, committed and scientifically inspiring environment with space for initiative, professional development and the design of an independent clinical-scientific career.
