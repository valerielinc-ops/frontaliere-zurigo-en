Put to the Point

The position includes the development of new functionalities in data management (e.g. data observability, metadate management). The spectrum of this challenging position ranges from topics from science to operational operations to the agile development of new data platforms with E2E pipelines. The stable 24x7 availability of weather and climate data is the basis for sustainable services for the welfare and protection of the Swiss population.

  • Entry Date: Immediately
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: #LI-MCH1012
  • Location: Operation Center 1, Frachtstrasse, 8058 Zurich

You can make this contribution

  • Build a solution for monitoring and controlling data platforms with a focus on data observability and data catalogs
  • Develop, operate and optimize data platforms using AI-based solutions
  • Develop and manage typical components such as databases, ETL tools, query engines, API gateways and backend applications on premises and in the cloud
  • Responsible for requirements analysis, specification, modeling, system design, development, testing, integration of workflows, interfaces and services on Linux/Unix/Windows in the backend area
  • Develop solutions for meteorology and climatology for the integration, transformation and provision of data according to international standards (e.g. air traffic intelligence and warning systems)
  • Develop APIs and Gateways

This makes them unique.

  • Several years of experience with data management systems in the cloud (AWS) and in our own RZ (Unix/Linux)
  • Secure use of OpenTelemetry and Data Pipelines and ETL products such as Pentaho, Databases, Object Store, DWH, Lakehouse
  • Scripting or programming skills with AI support desirable (Spec-Driven-Coding)
  • Experience with technologies such as streaming (Kafka), airflow, no-SQL, query engines, data catalogs, data observability, AI tools (Claude, N8N) etc.
  • Structured work according to ITIL processes with agile methods in operational operations
  • Very good knowledge of German and knowledge of a second official language
  • English skills are beneficial
