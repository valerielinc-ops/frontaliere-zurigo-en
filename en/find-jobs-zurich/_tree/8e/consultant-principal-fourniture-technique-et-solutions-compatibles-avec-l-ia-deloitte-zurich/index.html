Location: Zurich Work Pattern Percentage: 80-100% Start date: As soon as possible About the Role If you love building things that work and asking how they could be built better, this role gives you the chance to do both. You’ll lead the delivery of enterprise solutions, get hands-on with the challenges that matter, and explore where AI can genuinely make your team’s work smarter. You’ll have the freedom to try ideas, the responsibility to turn the good ones into practice, and the opportunity to help others grow along the way. Finding the talent that makes Deloitte unique What happens when AI becomes part of the delivery team—not just a feature in the finished product? We are looking for a hands-on technical Senior Consultant who wants to help answer that question. You will design and deliver enterprise solutions while exploring how AI can reshape the way teams analyse requirements, build software, test quality and solve problems. This is a role for someone who enjoys making new ways of working real: pairing technical judgment with curiosity, experimentation and disciplined execution. How you can make an impact

  • Shape AI-enabled delivery: identify where AI can make technical work more effective, test approaches in practice and help teams adopt what works.
  • Turn complexity into working solutions: assess options, design architectures and guide implementation across applications, data, APIs and integrations.
  • Lead from the front: take ownership of technical decisions and delivery challenges, staying close enough to the work to know what will—and will not—work.
  • Redesign how teams collaborate: bring business stakeholders, engineers and delivery teams together around clear requirements, sensible trade-offs and shared outcomes.
  • Build quality in from the start: apply engineering discipline, security considerations and human review as AI becomes part of the delivery process.
  • Grow others: coach junior colleagues and share practical lessons as delivery methods evolve. What you bring to succeed
  • You have at least five years of experience in technology consulting or software delivery, with hands-on experience designing and implementing enterprise solutions.
  • You can lead a technical workstream, make sound design decisions and guide a team through the realities of implementation.
  • You understand application architecture, APIs, integrations, data and the foundations of reliable software delivery.
  • You are energised by AI’s potential to change how solutions are built and willing to experiment thoughtfully. Prior experience delivering AI solutions is valuable, but not essential.
  • You explain complex technical choices clearly and work well with both engineers and business stakeholders.
  • You are fluent in English
  • German or French fluency is a plus (not required).
  • Salesforce experience is a plus, not a prerequisite. Your team Our Sales & Service team helps clients make every customer interaction count. We bring together human insight and technology to build experiences that feel personal, useful and easy to navigate, from the first marketing touchpoint through sales and ongoing service. It’s work that connects creative thinking with practical delivery, helping clients strengthen customer relationships and find new opportunities for growth.
