About the Role

  • Design, build and run AI solutions such as LLM-based assistants, agents and retrieval (RAG) applications that work on Sika's enterprise data
  • Take use cases from first prototype to stable production service, including deployment, monitoring and ongoing improvement
  • Work with business teams to find where AI adds real value, shape the use cases and set clear success criteria
  • Connect AI applications to our data platform (Databricks) and existing internal applications, with attention to data quality, access rights and cost
  • Follow developments in AI tooling and models, and help the team decide what is worth adopting

Your Skills and Experience

  • Educational background in computer science, maths, physics, computer programming or similar
  • Experience as a software engineer, building APIs and full-stack applications with automated tests and CI/CD
  • A track record of taking AI applications from idea to production: building quick prototypes and turning them into reliable production services
  • Hands-on experience building agents and chatbots that work with company data and take actions in production business systems such as SAP
  • Experience setting up evaluations that compare models and prompts on accuracy, cost and speed, and monitoring AI applications once they are live
  • A solid grasp of security and data access concepts, including AI-specific risks such as prompt injection and data leakage
  • Confidence working directly with business teams, turning a vague request into a clear goal with an agreed measure of success
  • Good judgement on when an LLM or an agent is the right tool, and when a script, a set of rules or classic machine learning does the job better
  • Fluency in English

A strong plus

  • Experience with Azure and Databricks, with knowledge of the Databricks AI stack (Agent Bricks, Model Serving, AI Search, AI Gateway, MLflow, Databricks Apps)
