Full description

You want to not only manage quality, but also change it sustainably? In this key role, you shape the evolution of quality in the Migros industry, drive our Q roadmap and empower people and organizations to make quality better every day.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Industrie AG, Migros Industrie AG – Zurich, Limmatstrasse 152, 8005 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You will further develop our MIND-wide approach to quality excellence and zero-defect competence and translate the strategic goals of our Q-Roadmap into concrete initiatives and effective improvements.
  • You control and coordinate strategic quality initiatives and develop effective project and portfolio management that creates transparency, secures priorities and drives the implementation of our Q-Roadmap.
  • You establish a systematic culture of continuous improvement and develop methods, tools and working methods with which our quality organization and our segments sustainably improve their quality performance.
  • You will systematically develop the quality-relevant competencies within the Migros industry and design training, development and qualification programmes for quality, specialist and managerial staff.
  • You build a MIND-wide network of experts and ensure that best practices, lessons learned and successful improvement approaches are shared and scaled across organizations.
  • You actively accompany segments and production sites in change and improvement processes and strengthen an efficient and learning quality culture through coaching, communication and practical support.

Requirements

  • Several years of relevant professional experience in quality management, operational excellence, continuous improvement or organizational development: as well as proven experience in leading complex improvement and transformation initiatives.
  • Completed degree programme (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): in food sciences, quality management, engineering, business administration or a comparable discipline.
  • You bring in-depth experience in quality management, operational excellence or continuous improvement and understand how to translate improvement methods into measurable and sustainable results.
  • You have successfully managed complex improvement or transformation programs and have deep experience in project and portfolio management.
  • You have experience in systematically building up competencies and designing training, development and empowerment programs for different target groups and implementing them effectively.
  • You can win people over for change, moderate complex stakeholder landscapes and effectively lead cross-divisional networks without direct authority.
  • Experience in an industrial production environment, ideally in the food industry and in an organization with multiple locations or business units, is beneficial.
  • You convince as a coach and sparring partner and have distinct moderation, workshop and facilitation skills at different hierarchical levels.
  • You are familiar with modern quality management systems, standards and governance structures and can combine them with continuous improvement and excellence approaches.
