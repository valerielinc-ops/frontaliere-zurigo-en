For our new office in Winterthur, we are looking for committed and motivated police officers who would like to devote themselves to an exciting and varied position. In addition, we work regularly with other police forces and partner organizations, which will expand your network. Become part of an appreciative, dynamic and motivated team that ensures security and order beyond the cantonal borders.
