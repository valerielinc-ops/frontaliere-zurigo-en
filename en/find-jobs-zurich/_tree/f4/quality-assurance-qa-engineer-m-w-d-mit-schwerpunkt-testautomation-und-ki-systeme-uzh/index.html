Central computer science

Job opening by agreement, indefinitely

We are OLAT projects, the DevOps team for the strategic learning platform OLAT of the University of Zurich (UZH). Our interdisciplinary team develops solutions for digital teaching at UZH and we are responsible for the development and maintenance of OLAT. Teachers and learners of all faculties use OLAT in the context of integrated learning as well as in connection with online exams. The OLAT projects team is based in the Multimedia & E-Learning Services (MELS) department of Central Computer Science and works closely with the ELEXA team, which is responsible for the operation and support of OLAT and online exams. To strengthen our team, we are looking for you, an experienced:n and passionate:n Quality Assurance (QA) Engineer (m/f/d), to work in an agile environment to further develop OLAT with innovative ideas and solutions. You are the central expert for software tests and shape how we test.

Tasks

  • Test Management Environment: You build the environment and define and manage structures, test cases and reporting
  • Test strategy: You develop and anchor the strategy with a risk-based approach and clear test definitions
  • Automated E2E tests: You develop and maintain automated tests and integrate them into our build and deployment processes and exchange ideas with our colleague who is responsible for the load tests.
  • Manual testing: You plan and perform exploratory testing, regression testing, and release testing
  • AI-enabled functions: They test our AI system with MCP servers and RAG, including evaluating response quality, robustness and behavior in borderline cases, and developing suitable testing approaches for this still young discipline
  • Bug tracking: You track errors in Jira from the message to the correction and also use logs and monitoring data (e.g. Sentry, Splunk)
  • Collaboration in the team: You work closely with requirements engineering, development, operation and team management, check requirements for testability at an early stage and release the releases from a quality point of view
  • AI as a tool: In the medium term, you can evaluate and introduce AI for testing, for example for test case generation, because we are open to your ideas

Profile

  • Completed training in computer science (uni, FH) or comparable qualification
  • Understanding risk-based testing and the ability to prioritize
  • Independent, structured, solution- and goal-oriented working method
  • Experience in setting up, maintaining and operating (e.g. Qase, Testmo, TestRail, Xray) a test management environment. You know how to structure test cases, test runs and reporting in a meaningful way
  • Practical experience with E2E test automation, ideally with Playwright or a comparable framework and CI/CD pipelines
  • Programming skills in at least one language (e.g. Java, Python or TypeScript/JavaScript)
  • Practical experience with manual testing
  • Experience with tools like Jira and Git
  • Experience in an agile environment (Scrum, Kanban) as well as an agile mindset that you manifest and apply in everyday work
  • The interest in learning established and new technologies and familiarizing yourself with complex problems
  • Critical and analytical thinking
  • Independent, interdisciplinary work and teamwork
  • Communication strength and very good knowledge of German and English

Advantage

  • Experience in higher education or educational environment
  • Methodological test knowledge, e.g. proven by ISTQB certification or equivalent practical experience
  • First experience in testing AI- / LLM-based systems or great curiosity about this topic
  • Experience with container technologies (Docker, Kubernetes)
  • Experience with observability tools (Sentry, Splunk, Grafana)
