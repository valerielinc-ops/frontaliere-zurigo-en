Teamleader in-house logistics — Die Schweizerische Post

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Are you looking for a leadership position where you can fully develop your skills? With you, we promote a positive team culture in which all employees can develop their potential.

You can do that.

  • You lead a team of around 20 employees, promote their development and together with them ensure reliable storage and relocation as well as the supply of working areas with containers.
  • Together with your team, you are responsible for the timely handling of the intercenter connections and ensure smooth transport and handling processes in compliance with safety and quality requirements.
  • As a strong management duo, you create the conditions for efficient and smooth operational processes with your deputy.
  • Together you create the operational planning and design targeted team meetings.
  • You continue to develop your employees based on clear goals and support them as a coach in their daily work.
  • By actively participating in day-to-day business, you support your team and set an example.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed a basic training EFZ and have a Swiss certificate in the field of logistics or management or a comparable further education or are ready to complete it.
  • You have profound experience in the management of employees as well as in logistics or sorting logistics.
  • You are in possession of a valid forklift card and bring the necessary physical resilience for active cooperation in the operative environment.
  • Thanks to your strong organizational ability, you can also keep track of hectic situations, set the right priorities, recognize the needs of your employees and promote open, appreciative and transparent cooperation.
  • The working hours are in the early shift from 06:00-15:00, in the late shift from 14:05-23:00. This also includes occasional weekend assignments on Saturdays or Sundays.
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing as well as a secure handling of MS Office round off your profile.

With you, flexibility becomes everyday life. We promote progressive teamwork and value a balanced working life. This position can also be taken over by two persons in "co-management". You can apply directly as a duo or we support you in the search for a suitable person.
