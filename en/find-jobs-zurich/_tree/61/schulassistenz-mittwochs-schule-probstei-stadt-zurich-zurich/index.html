Full description

We are looking for an appreciative school assistant who supports a boy in the reintegration into the 4th rule class (new school house). You will be accompanied by the experienced class teacher.

Tasks

  • Individual accompaniment and support of a 10-year-old student in everyday school life
  • Support in finding and integrating into the new class community
  • Close cooperation and exchange with the class teacher
  • Time of deployment: Wednesdays, 8.00 – 12.00 (or 8.45 – 12.45, by arrangement)
  • Duration: The position is limited for the time being until the sports holidays 2027.

Profile

  • Confident and positive attitude
  • Appreciative and empathetic personality
  • Enjoyment of working with children and individual accompaniment
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Reliability and responsibility

We offer

We offer you a meaningful and varied activity in which you can accompany a child individually and strengthen him in his school and personal development. You can expect an appreciative, supportive team as well as close cooperation with the teacher and other specialists.

About us

The school district Schwamendingen comprises the entire district 12 of the city of Zurich. School Probstei is a small, green day school located just below the forest edge in Zurich-Schwamendingen. The school comprises two kindergartens, six primary classes and the care area.
