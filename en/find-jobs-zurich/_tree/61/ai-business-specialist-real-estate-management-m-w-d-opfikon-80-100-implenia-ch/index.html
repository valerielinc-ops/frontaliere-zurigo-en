What requirements do you bring with you as AI Business Specialist (Real Estate Management) (m/f/d), Opfikon, 80-100%? • You have a suitable professional and technical background, for example through relevant professional experience, an education, further education or a study in computer science, business informatics, data science, digital business or a comparable field. • You have profound experience in the development and implementation of AI and data solutions and understand how to translate technical requirements into concrete technical solutions. • You have practical experience with modern AI technologies and pro-code approaches, as well as good programming skills, especially in Python and SQL. • Experience in real estate management or In the real estate industry is beneficial. • You are interested in AI, data and digital innovations and follow current developments with curiosity, openness and enthusiasm. • You think connected and entrepreneurial, recognize opportunities and understand how to translate technological possibilities into concrete solutions with a comprehensible business added value. • You act in an advisory and convincing manner, communicate safely at different levels and can inspire different stakeholders for new technologies and solutions. • You work pragmatically, independently and solution-oriented and enjoy working together with the specialist areas to achieve visible and measurable results. • You have very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing.

About Implenia

As a leading Swiss construction and real estate service provider, Implenia develops, realizes and manages living spaces, working environments and infrastructure for future generations in Switzerland and Germany. Implenia also offers tunnelling and related infrastructure projects in other markets. The company combines the know-how of highly qualified consulting, development, planning and execution units under one roof into an integrated, multinationally leading construction and real estate service provider. Headquartered in Opfikon near Zurich, Implenia employs more than 8,000 people (FTE) across Europe and achieved a turnover of CHF 3.5 billion in 2025. More information at implenia.com.
