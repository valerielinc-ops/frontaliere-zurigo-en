Full description

Key responsibilities

  • Active support and further development of a defined corporate customer portfolio in the field of card and T&E solutions
  • Planning and conducting customer visits, consultations and reviews (on-/off-site and remote)
  • Analysis of customer needs, expense and travel processes as well as map usage data to identify potential
  • Advising on corporate card, T&E and additional solutions (e.g. virtual cards, central billing, reporting)
  • Use of CRM, reporting and analytics tools to identify sales opportunities and process them in a targeted manner
  • Coordination of implementations, program setups, limit adjustments and service cases with internal entities
  • active promotion of the use of digital map and T&E tools (portals, apps, self-service)
  • Ensuring compliance with KYC, risk, compliance and card-specific policies
  • Documentation of customer interactions, opportunities and financial statements in the relevant systems
  • contribute market and customer feedback to further develop our map and T&E offering
  • Additional responsibility: Timely assumption of the deputy group management

The team To strengthen our Sales Commercial Cards & Travel & Expense (T&E) team, we are looking for a strong, pragmatic and customer-oriented personality with deep experience in the banking or banking environment. In this role, you manage a defined corporate customer portfolio in the Zurich area, develop card and T&E programs and make a direct contribution to growth, efficiency and customer satisfaction. If you enjoy B2B sales, data-based consulting and modern map/T&E solutions, you are at the right place.

Your skills and experience

  • 3–5 years of relevant experience in card, T&E or corporate banking sales with proven sales and consulting experience
  • prefers professional experience in banking or banking environment
  • Strong sales and consulting expertise in the B2B environment as well as enjoyment of developing long-term customer relationships
  • In-depth understanding of corporate card, T&E and billing solutions or the willingness to familiarize yourself quickly and in-depth
  • Strong analytical skills and safe handling of metrics (volume, use, yield, travel data)
  • Structured, self-responsible and solution-oriented working method with high quality standards
  • High integrity, risk awareness and compliance orientation
  • high load capacity and willingness to perform with a good process understanding
  • very good communication and presentation skills in German (C2) and English (minimum C1); Other languages are beneficial
  • high affinity for digital tools (MS Office, especially PowerPoint and Excel; ideally CRM and specialist systems)

About us UBS Card Center AG, a subsidiary of UBS Switzerland AG, is the leading credit card processor in Switzerland. Well over 3 million Visa and Mastercard credit cards are reliably processed via our systems. With more than 750 dedicated employees at the Glattbrugg and Manno sites, we combine many years of expertise with innovative strength and work every day to make tomorrow’s payments easier, safer and smarter.
