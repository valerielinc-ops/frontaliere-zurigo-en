Full description

The Institute of GP Medicine at the University Hospital Zurich offers the curriculum GP medicine as a 2-year advanced training phase for prospective GPs. The curriculum family medicine allows access to attractive training positions that prepare for later work in primary care.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • 2-year curriculum consisting of three rotations in specialties relevant to GPs (6 months each) and a 6-month practice assistant • Rotations into Emergency Medicine, Psychiatry and Geriatrics (Full Time) • Practical assistance in a basic care practice in the canton of ZH (part-time possible) • Practical preparation for the later basic services • Possibilities for supervised ultrasound Requirements • Swiss doctor’s diploma or EU doctor’s diploma (MEBEKO recognition required) • Career goal as a family physician (FMH General Internal Medicine) • 3 years of experience in general internal medicine, of which at least 2 years inpatient internal medicine incl. A-year • At least 18 months of work experience in Switzerland • Language level German: at least C1 We offer: From July 2027 we will be offering a position for Track 3 in the curriculum family medicine. The rotations take place at the USZ and in cooperation with outpatient medical practices. The focus of emergency rotation is on the fast track including small surgery. A detailed description of the rotations can be found on our homepage.
