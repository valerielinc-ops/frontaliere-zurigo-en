The tumor documentation is part of the Clinical Program (CP) of the Comprehensive Cancer Center Zurich (CCCZ) at USZ. It creates a reliable data basis for quality assurance, certification and the further development of our oncological care. For our team, we are looking for a committed personality who combines tumor documentation with a pronounced IT affinity and participates as an interface between department, data expert and technical partners.

Your main tasks

  • Tumor documentation (about 40%): You document clinical data, especially on thyroid tumors in OnkoStar and ensure their completeness and plausibility.
  • Data & Digitalization (about 30%): You support the tumor center in KPI evaluations, reporting as well as in data, automation and digitalization projects.
  • You take on an interface function between the team Tumor Documentation/QM, our data and IT specialist: inside and – depending on the project – other areas of IT.
  • You take technical requirements from the team, structure them and support them in translating them into technically feasible questions and solutions.
  • You contribute to the further development of data quality, data structures and standardized or automated processes.

Your profile

  • You have medical, scientific, health or medical informatics training or a comparable qualification.
  • You enjoy medical data and bring a basic medical or oncological understanding or are willing to familiarize yourself with the tumor documentation.
  • You work very carefully, structured and reliable and can understand and convey professional requirements.
  • You bring a strong IT affinity, move safely in different systems and enjoy developing data and processes.

Advantage

  • Experience in tumor documentation, quality management or oncology
  • Knowledge of OnkoStar as well as experience with clinical information systems, medical databases or data analysis
  • Interest or initial knowledge in structured clinical data, data models and interfaces, e.g. FHIR or openEHR
  • Experience with Excel or other tools for data preparation and evaluation

Our offer

  • You can expect a versatile task at the interface between oncology, quality management, data and digitalization.
  • You will receive a structured training and work closely with an experienced team of tumor documentation/QM as well as data and digitization specialists at the CCCZ.
  • We offer you room for manoeuvre, flexible working time models and the opportunity for home office within the scope of operational possibilities.
