Full description

We make habitats worth living.

Karl Waechter AG has stood for sanitary and heating installations in the Zurich region for more than 100 years. As part of BKW Building Solutions AG, we support private customers, companies and the public sector from consulting to maintenance with around 50 employees.

Your tasks

  • You lead a small assembly team with foresight, promote open cooperation and ensure clear and step-by-step communication on the construction site.
  • You realize demanding sanitary installations in residential, commercial and industrial buildings in accordance with deadlines, costs and quality.
  • You will coordinate the work with other sectors of building technology and develop optimal technical solutions together.
  • You will participate in construction and coordination meetings, representing the interests of the project competently and solution-oriented.
  • You organize materials, personnel and resources and ensure a smooth construction process.
  • You support the project management with government reports and supplements and keep an eye on the economic success of your construction sites.
  • You actively contribute to our high quality and safety standards.

You bring that with you

  • Completed apprenticeship as a sanitary installer:in EFZ.
  • Some years of experience as a construction manager:r sanitary fitter:in.
  • Delight in leading assembly teams and working with customers, planners and entrepreneurs.
  • Strong understanding of services and customers as well as an independent, structured and solution-oriented working method.
  • Responsibility, organizational talent and entrepreneurial thinking.
  • Friendly, professional and well-groomed appearance.
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing.
  • Category B driving licence.
