Full description

movemi AG operates the largest fitness network in Switzerland with 126 ACTIV FITNESS and 16 fitness parks. You want to create the customer experience of ACTIV FITNESS and Fitnesspark? As Head of Customer Experience, you’ll build a new team and connect digital and physical contact points to an end-to-end experience. You give both brands a clear profile.

Luogo di lavoro: movemi AG, Verwaltungs movemi AG, Thurgauerstrasse 32, 8050 Oerlikon

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You build the Customer Experience team, lead it and take responsibility for its goals and budget. The team includes customer insights, digital products, and CRM and lifecycle.
  • You build a new customer experience landscape, with systems for customer feedback, CRM and personal address, and link them to the processes in the studio.
  • You design the customer journeys of ACTIV FITNESS and seamlessly connect digital contact points with the experience in the store. Improvements are implemented with operations, member care and product development.
  • You sharpen the experience per brand so that our members feel the added value of Fitnesspark in everyday life.
  • You establish a customer feedback program: You measure the recommendation rate (NPS) at the contact points and ensure that feedback generates concrete measures – especially for dissatisfied members.
  • You are responsible for the monthly reporting on the customer experience and the renewal rate for the CXDO and the management.
  • You develop website, webshop and app as customer products with the aim of increasing online deals and solving more concerns in self-service.
  • You design the personalized approach of members throughout their life cycle and coordinate campaigns and contact rules with marketing.
  • You formulate the technical requirements for data and systems and lead the selection of the CRM system, closely coordinated with the Head of Platform & Data.
  • You work in overarching programs of the management and bring the view of the members there.

Requirements

  • At least 10 years of experience: in customer experience, digital or marketing in the branch or subscription business with end customers, of which at least five years in a management function
  • Master's degree (university, university of applied sciences): in business administration, marketing, business psychology or hotel industry; an MBA or further training in customer experience management is desired
  • Experience with personnel, professional and budget responsibility as well as proven success in membership retention, recommendation or online degrees
  • Safe handling of key figures, customer feedback and profitability calculations
  • A good understanding of digital products and their interaction with data and systems
  • The ability to attract management, operations and external partners for joint solutions
  • Willingness to travel at home and abroad, for example to partners and trade fairs
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • English (good knowledge)
  • French (advantageous)

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Expert Discussion: In a specialist discussion, specific knowledge relevant for the execution of the job is requested by the manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, the team. If possible, there is time to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Discussion with case presentation: Specific expertise is examined on the basis of a case or other assessment instruments. After processing, the result of the leader is presented and then discussed together. Usually, the team is also represented at the presentation. As a rule, the conversations are conducted by the superior, without a representative from the HR. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition: After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
