Get your EFZ through training in the technical service. Apprenticeship as a specialist / specialist in operating maintenance EFZ

You will learn this with us

  • Maintenance and repair work in all buildings

  • Indoor greening and gardening in outdoor facilities
  • Completion of work related to occupational safety
  • Technical preparation of seminars

You bring that with you

  • Physical capacity

  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
