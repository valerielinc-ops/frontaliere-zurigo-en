Full description

At PwC, we support leading asset managers, fund initiators and institutional investors throughout the entire fund lifecycle – from classic UCITS structures to alternative investment vehicles. Our aim is not only to implement regulatory and tax requirements, but also to create sustainable, sustainable solutions.

To strengthen our specialized Fund Tax Reporting team, we are looking for a motivated personality who has an interest in taxes, financial products and data-based processes and wants to develop professionally in a collegial, high-performance environment.

  • Learn about the taxation of investment funds and other financial products and their investors in Switzerland.
  • Be responsible for the complex calculation of the taxable income of investment funds.
  • Support our Swiss Investor Fund Tax Reporting Team in advising investment funds in the increasingly growing asset management industry.
  • Be part of an experienced, international team that provides investor fund tax reporting services to a wide range of demanding clients.
  • Work and communicate daily across locations with our worldwide PwC network, national and international customers and their service providers.
  • Actively participate in internal projects for process optimization and the further development of our services in the area of Fund Tax Reporting.
  • Beginners with completed commercial training, ideally in the trust, banking or tax area or a completed business administration degree (FH/HF) preferably with a specialization in accounting or banking/finance.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing.
  • Experienced handling of MS Office and have a particularly good knowledge of Excel.
  • Have a forward-looking, precise and responsible way of working that helps you to maintain high quality standards.
  • Be a flexible and solution-oriented personality, which brings a quick comprehension and a good resilience, as well as a pronounced ability in dealing with numbers.
  • You like to work in a young and dynamic team and bring a safe appearance.
  • Be open to new technologies and get excited about working on digitalization and automation projects.
