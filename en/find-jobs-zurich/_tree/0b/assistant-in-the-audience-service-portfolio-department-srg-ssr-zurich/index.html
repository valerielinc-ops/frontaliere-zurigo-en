Full description

The Audience & Offer Portfolio unites various functions that are of central importance for a modern, audience-oriented and sustainable SRG. The department is responsible for the development and preparation of strategic audience and market knowledge about research and analysis in all regions as well as the nationally guided control, development and quality assurance processes for a strategy- and concession-compliant SRG offer on all vectors.

This is your contribution.

As an assistant: in the co-management Audience & Offer Portfolio, you are an important organizational and coordinative hub within the department. They work closely with the co-management and, if necessary, also support the other nationally active management team. In addition to classical assistance tasks, you independently take on projects of different sizes and complexity. They think along, recognize the need for action and are actively involved in the further development of the department.

  • Independent organisation and coordination of internal and external meetings, travel and events in all language regions
  • Independent and forward-looking agenda and appointment management as well as coordination of different priorities
  • Preparation and preparation of decision bases, presentations and other documents
  • First point of contact for internal and external inquiries as well as professional and addressable communication (emails, correspondence, briefings)
  • Administrative support for personnel entry and exit, in particular coordination of IT accounts and access rights as well as interface to IT
  • Support and holiday representation in the assistance pool of the area Offer
  • Assuming further coordinating tasks, such as topic planning, session preparation and management of the audience intranet site

They inspire us.

  • Commercial apprenticeship and ideally 1–2 years of experience in a comparable position, for example in appointment and event coordination as well as in the preparation of documents and writing of professional texts
  • An independent, forward-looking and reliable way of working. Recognize what’s up, set priorities and take responsibility
  • Enjoying changes, new topics and getting involved in a dynamic environment and actively shaping it
  • A networked and interdisciplinary way of thinking as well as the ability to think beyond one’s own area of responsibility and to recognize connections
  • An open, accessible and communicative personality with pleasure in the exchange and cooperation with different people
  • A high degree of discretion and sensitivity in dealing with confidential information
  • Very good knowledge of German and French (at least B2) and good knowledge of English

So we inspire you.

  • An interdisciplinary agile environment anchored in the different language regions
  • Shaping the future of SRG in a dynamic environment
  • An exciting working environment in a newly oriented department, where new ideas are welcome and you can set your own impulses
  • A lot of personal responsibility and scope for design in the organization and further development of your tasks
  • Close cooperation with the co-management and diverse insights into strategic topics around audience, research and supply development
  • A collaborative environment characterized by openness, mutual support and enjoyment of cooperation
  • A hybrid working model with flexible home office opportunities and attractive, contemporary employment conditions of SRG

Our promise

What does it take to reach the people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expertise, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can contribute and develop. For the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: Welcome to the world of SRG!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, promote creativity and offer a lot of design space for new and high-quality products.
  • Shaping digitalization: Quality journalism is also digital for us. That’s why we explore new possibilities and experiment with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-medial. We design on the pulse of time and on the nerve of the audience.
  • Being human: We meet each other collegially, work independently and shape our working atmosphere individually. Our success is based on the personal strengths as well as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programs. In doing so, we act respectfully and independently; in this way we promote opinion-forming and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
