At the pulse of the economy – and you in the middle of it! At the Commercial Registry Office of the Canton of Zurich, around 80 employees handle more than 70,000 business cases each year. We are the central point of contact for all company registrations in the Canton of Zurich and thus make a decisive contribution to transparency and legal certainty in commercial transactions. Do you enjoy economic issues and corporate law issues? Are you looking for a versatile task in which you can use your legal sensitivity? We look forward to meeting you! These tasks are waiting for you

  • You check commercial register applications and evidence for legal compliance and completeness, including documents on company formations, statutory changes, common share assignments, capital changes, personnel mutations and other types of business.
  • You check company names and control draft registration documents.
  • You provide expert information to our customers as well as to consulting companies in the field of commercial register registrations.
  • You carry out official registration procedures and edit transfers and orders in this regard.
  • You prepare commercial registration applications and registration and rejection orders. You are fit for us when
  • a «CAS Paralegal» or at least equivalent training
  • Professional experience of at least one year in a legal environment
  • you are interested in corporate and commercial law issues
  • a high willingness to perform and are resilient
