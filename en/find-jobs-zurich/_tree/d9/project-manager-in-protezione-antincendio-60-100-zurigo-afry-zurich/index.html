Full description

Become part of our fire protection team. We develop modern fire protection solutions for buildings and infrastructure facilities throughout Switzerland. Our projects range from residential and commercial buildings to complex infrastructure projects. This results in practical, economical and sustainable solutions in close cooperation with internal and external specialist bodies. For this position, we are looking for an experienced person in fire protection who will lead demanding projects professionally and organizationally, advise customers competently and strengthen our team with their know-how. Take on responsibility with us as: SENIOR PROJECT MANAGER FIRE PROTECTION 60-100% - ZURICH

YOUR TASKS

  • Development of fire protection concepts and preparation of evidence in engineering fire protection for buildings and infrastructure structures
  • Advising building owners, architects, specialist planners and executing bodies
  • Coordination and negotiation with authorities as well as support for approval processes
  • Responsibility for quality assurance in fire protection in execution
  • Leadership and technical support of employees in projects
  • Collaboration with in-house specialists from various disciplines
  • Participation in acquisition, customer care and the further development of the fire protection department

YOUR OPPORTUNITIES

  • Challenging and varied projects in building construction and infrastructure
  • High level of personal responsibility and great creative freedom
  • Opportunity to actively contribute your professional experience to projects, customer relationships and the further development of the team
  • Interdisciplinary working environment with experienced specialists
  • Flexible work design with modern infrastructure and home office option
  • Attractive professional and personal development opportunities

YOUR PROFILE

  • Completed training in the field of engineering, architecture or comparable technical basis
  • Several years of experience in structural, technical and organisational fire protection
  • VKF certificate as a fire protection specialist or fire protection expert
  • Very good knowledge of the relevant standards and guidelines
  • Experience in managing and coordinating challenging fire protection projects
  • Confident appearance in dealing with building owners, planners, authorities and other project participants
  • Entrepreneurial thinking as well as enjoyment of responsibility, customer contact and teamwork
  • Very good oral and written German skills
