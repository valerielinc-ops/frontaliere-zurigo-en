Do you want to practice modern family medicine at a high level – without inefficient processes, paper chaos and overcrowded speech? Are you looking for a new practice near Zurich that uses digitalization and AI consistently so that you have more time for medicine and at the same time more time for your private life? Then an exchange is worthwhile – uncomplicated by phone or via WhatsApp. In a highly underserved region near Zurich, a completely new, state-of-the-art family practice with a clear vision is being developed at the beginning of next year: maximum medical quality with maximum efficiency in everyday practice. Practice consistently relies on digital and AI-supported processes: from smart practice software to automated processes and robotics in drug logistics. This results in a much more efficient day-to-day work than in classical GP practices: less administration, fewer empty runs, less organizational burden. An interdisciplinary setting with several family doctors and angiological competence on site is planned. The need in the region is enormous. Already at the start, a large number of patients is expected. The location combines the best of two worlds: working close to nature with a high quality of life – and at the same time the centre of Zurich can be reached in about 20 minutes. The practice is consciously aimed at specialists who want to actively shape modern medicine and have understood that intelligent processes do not mean worse medicine, but create more space for the really important things.
