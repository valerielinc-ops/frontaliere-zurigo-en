Full description

The opportunity

Join a passionate and collaborative team driving innovation in high-voltage technology. You will support exciting GIS (Gas-Insulated switchgear) product development projects and gain hands-on experience in a dynamic, international environment. This internship of at least 6 - 12 months offers the chance to grow your technical and analytical skills while contributing to real-world solutions.

How you'll make an impact

  • Support type tests (high power, high voltage, high current, mechanical, environmental etc.) in GIS development projects
  • Organize, prepare, attend and document test activities
  • Evaluate test results using tools like Excel, LabVIEW or Python
  • Develop and improve test evaluation tools
  • Collaborate in development projects and daily operations
  • Contribute to solving short-term technical challenges
  • Work closely with cross-functional teams
  • Ensure high-quality documentation and reporting

Your background

  • Enrolled Student (Bachelor or Master) or Graduate in Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering or Physics
  • Experience with COMSOL Multiphysics or similar simulation software is advantageous
  • Basic programming skills or willingness to learn (Excel, LabVIEW, Python)
  • Excellent English skills, both written and spoken
  • Team-oriented with strong communication skills
  • Creative and solution-focused mindset
  • Comfortable in a multicultural, multidisciplinary setting

More about us

We are a technology-driven company with a strong focus on innovation, continuous improvement, and sustainable manufacturing. We offer flexible working conditions, dedicated mentorship, and a supportive, inclusive environment where you can grow personally and professionally – empowering you to thrive while balancing life and work.
