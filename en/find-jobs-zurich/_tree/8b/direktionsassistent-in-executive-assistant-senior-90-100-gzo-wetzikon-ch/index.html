Full description

Right hand of the hospital directorate. Own responsibility for contracts and risks. In the middle, while the GZO shapes its future. You coordinate the agenda of our hospital director, prepare decisions of the management and keep the threads of specialist departments and external contacts together. In addition, you further develop the contract management and risk management system and report to the Board of Directors.

Tasks

  • Organise and coordinate appointments and meetings
  • Prepare and follow up meetings, presentations and decision-making bases as well as keep minutes
  • Create and process correspondence
  • Conduct and further develop contract management with contract database, deadline control and signature process
  • Maintain, monitor and further develop RMS risk management system and report to the management and board of directors
  • Act as a hub between internal departments and external contacts
  • Professionally handle confidential information requirements
  • Completed commercial training or comparable qualification as well as a degree as a managerial assistant with a federal law. Specialist card
  • At least 10 years of experience in an assistant role, ideally at management or management level, preferably in the healthcare sector
  • Strong organisational and coordination skills as well as a structured working method
  • Routine and stylish protocol management, even in complex transactions and in committee meetings
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Very good knowledge of Microsoft 365 applications, ideally experience with document management, GRC or contract management systems
  • High discretion and a strong sense of responsibility
  • Safe and professional appearance
  • Independent, reliable, solution-oriented and forward-looking way of working You can look forward to an introduction that deserves this name You will be structured and carefully introduced to your new area of responsibility.
