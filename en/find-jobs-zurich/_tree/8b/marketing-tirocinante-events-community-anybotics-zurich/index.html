Full description

ANYbotics is a fast-growing tech company dedicated to shaping the future of mobile robotics across multiple industries. Join our highly talented and motivated team of more than 190 people and work on cutting-edge robot technology. We are open to internships all year round and post regularly.

We’re always looking for passionate, curious, and motivated interns to join our mission.

This is your chance to collaborate with world-class experts, gain real-world experience, and make meaningful contributions in a fast-paced, interdisciplinary environment.

The Opportunity

As our Marketing Intern for Events & Community, you will gain hands-on experience in how a B2B deep-tech company turns marketing initiatives into real-world pipeline. Working closely with our Marketing Campaign & Operations Specialist and the wider Growth team, you will learn how to coordinate industry events, support our social channels, and assist in managing branded touchpoints. This internship is designed to give you deep insights into campaigns, events, social media, and CRM in a fast-paced environment, providing you with a strong foundation for your future career in marketing while you support real projects under the guidance of experienced professionals.

The Market & Our Technology

ANYbotics builds the autonomous workforce for the global process industry. Founded in 2016 as an ETH Zurich spin-off, we design, manufacture and operate ANYmal, a legged robot for industrial inspection, together with the Data Navigator industrial AI platform. Our robots work at more than 250 industrial sites for 50+ customers across five continents, in oil and gas, chemicals, power, metals and other process industries. About 200 people from around 30 nationalities work at our headquarters in Zurich and our offices in San Francisco and Barcelona.

Your Impact

  • Support the coordination of trade shows and customer events by assisting with logistics, booth materials, and on-site setup under the guidance of our event leads
  • Learn to process event leads into our CRM effectively so Sales can follow up, and help gather event results to support future planning
  • Contribute to ANYbotics' voice in the industrial and robotics community by helping to draft content and support our weekly social calendar
  • Assist in transforming articles, case studies, and webinars into engaging social posts that highlight the people and robots behind ANYbotics
  • Help map our existing content by industry and buyer stage to identify gaps, and participate in testing email journeys to keep our CRM data clean
  • Monitor competitors, press coverage, and industry awards to help keep the marketing team informed
  • Support the sourcing and inventory management of branded materials and merchandise, learning how to balance cost and quality
  • Collaborate on producing the newsletter, refreshing website pages, and organizing a centralized, self-serve library of digital marketing assets
  • Explore and help implement AI agents and automated workflows that assist the team with event reporting and content drafts
  • Review marketing tools and processes alongside the team, helping to document best practices so the whole department can use them

Your Profile

  • Currently enrolled in, or recently graduated from, a Bachelor's or Master's program in marketing, communications, business, or a related field
  • Eligible for an internship in Switzerland
  • Strong organizational skills with the ability to help track multiple tasks, deadlines, and suppliers without dropping any
  • Proven ability to thrive in a fast-paced and dynamic environment, comfortable learning new tasks and adapting to changing priorities
  • Proactive mindset with a desire to learn, take feedback constructively, and support cross-functional projects
  • Strong collaborative skills, eager to work closely with various teams within the Commercial division
  • Fluent written and spoken English
  • Hands-on attitude and willingness to travel to events, including assisting with the physical setup and carrying of booth materials

Bonus Points

  • Experience with LinkedIn and other social platforms, ideally in a professional or academic setting
  • Familiarity with HubSpot, Notion, Canva, or similar tools
  • Enthusiasm for experimenting with modern AI marketing tools
  • Interest in robotics, AI, or industrial technology
