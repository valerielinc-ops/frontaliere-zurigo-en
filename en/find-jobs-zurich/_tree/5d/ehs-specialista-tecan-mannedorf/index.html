Have you ever wondered how your skills and unique background can affect the world of tomorrow? Become part of our EHS/FM team and use the talent and passion that makes you stand out with us.

EHS Specialist / Safety Officer 80-100 %

Männedorf & Nänikon, Zurich

Your contribution

For our locations in Switzerland, we are looking for a committed and competent personality as an EHS specialist / security officer. In this versatile role, you play a central role in the implementation and continuous development of our EHS Environment, Health & Safety organization, EHS Management System (ISO 14001 and 45001) and processes, helping to create a safe, healthy and sustainable workplace.

  • Responsibility or coordination on occupational safety, health protection, hazardous substances, environment and emergency organisation
  • Responsibility for the further development of the EHS management system (ISO 14001 / ISO 45001) in compliance with Group specifications and legal requirements.
  • Ensuring compliance with legal, regulatory and internal requirements in close cooperation with authorities, specialist bodies and service providers
  • Advice, support and training of site management, managers and employees in all ETS areas
  • Implementation and coordination of hazard assessments, risk analysis, audits and safety inspections
  • Analysis of accidents, incidents and near-accidents as well as definition and follow-up of improvement measures
  • Development and monitoring of EHS indicators and management reports

Your profile

We are not just looking for expertise: we are looking for people who can put their passion and talent to work with us.

  • Technical or scientific training with further education as well as qualification as specialist ASGS or Expert ASGS
  • Several years of experience in the field of EHS / occupational safety, ideally in an industrial company
  • In-depth experience in the development, further development and auditing of management systems according to ISO 14001 and ISO 45001
  • Qualifications as fire safety officer, chemical contact person and hazardous goods officer
  • Very good knowledge of Swiss legislation and applications
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Enforcement and at the same time strong advisory and communication skills
  • High personal responsibility and resilience combined with a pragmatic and solution-oriented working method.
  • Negotiable knowledge of German and English
