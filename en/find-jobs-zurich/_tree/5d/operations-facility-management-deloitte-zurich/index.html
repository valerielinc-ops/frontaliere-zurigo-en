Full description

Location : Zurich Work Pattern Percentage : 80-100% Start date : 1 April 2027 About the Role The post-holder will join the Workplace Services & Real Estate (WS&RE) team and report to the Head of WS&RE for Switzerland and Liechtenstein. The Assistant Operations WS&RE will oversee the day-to-day delivery of high-quality soft and hard facilities services at our flagship office in Zurich by managing our key third-party FM contractors on site as well as the catering services provider. The role involves building strong relationships with key office stakeholders, monitoring corrective action plans to address service performance shortfalls and reviewing results against key performance indicators (KPIs), while ensuring facilities services run smoothly at all times. How you can make an impact

  • You oversee day-to-day operations at our Deloitte office in Zurich, including regular quality checks, walkthroughs and analysis of feedback.
  • You manage daily facilities service delivery through our outsourced facility management contract.
  • You manage the catering services provider to make sure the contractually agreed service levels and targets are met, while driving continuous service improvement.
  • You validate the initial approval of quotations and invoices before submitting them to the Head of WS&RE for final approval.
  • You develop and maintain effective partnering relationships with suppliers.
  • You build and maintain strong relationships with key stakeholders across the firm to align service delivery with their expectations and requirements and provide a high-quality customer experience.
  • You provide strategic input and guidance to the Head of WS&RE on the development of the operation and future business needs. What you bring to succeed
  • You are fluent in English and German
  • You have good knowledge of Facility Management or Hospitality, typically from 1-2 years of relevant work experience
  • You have experience with the management of supplier relationships and budgets
  • You are a self-starter and are able to work independently and in teams
  • You have a service-oriented attitude combined with innovative thinking
  • You are business minded, change orientated and proactive, as well as diplomatic, tactful and approachable What sets you apart
  • You are familiar with workplace technologies, workplace applications and data-driven reporting to improve services.
  • You have experience coordinating small to medium-sized office refurbishments, relocations or service improvements.
  • You have experience using supplier KPIs and service-level agreements to address performance gaps.
  • You have experience with space planning and occupancy management to meet changing business needs.
  • You are interested in sustainable operations, ideally with experience in waste reduction, resource efficiency or responsible procurement.
  • You have a relevant qualification in facilities management, or hospitality. Your team Together with our third-party FM partner, our WS&RE team comprises approximately 70 people across Switzerland, including four Deloitte employees who lead operations. We manage approximately 20’000 m² and 1’300 desks across eight locations in Switzerland and Liechtenstein: Basel, Bern, Lausanne, Lugano, Geneva, Vaduz, Vevey and our headquarter in Zurich. In Zurich, we also manage an in-house catering facility with four units: VIP fine dining, a staff restaurant, meeting room catering and an evening bar. Together, these units generate approximately 8’500 sales transactions per month.
