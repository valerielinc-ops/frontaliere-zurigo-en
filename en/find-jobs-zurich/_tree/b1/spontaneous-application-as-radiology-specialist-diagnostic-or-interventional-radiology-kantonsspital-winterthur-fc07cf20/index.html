In the clinic for radiology and nuclear medicine, more than 105,000 examinations and interventions are carried out annually. The Clinic of Radiology and Nuclear Medicine ensures the highest level of expertise and quality of care in all its areas: X-ray and fluoroscopy diagnostics, breast imaging, ultrasound, nuclear medicine, magnetic resonance imaging, computed tomography, pediatric radiology, neuroradiology and interventional radiology. Are you interested in an exciting and versatile job as a radiology specialist? Then we look forward to getting to know you and talking about your perspectives and your possible tasks with us.
