Full description

You are organized and keep the overview even if several things are running at the same time? Then this position could be just right for you!
For our management team of the care pool, we are looking for a reliable person who has the day-to-day business under control and acts as the first point of contact for employees and internal positions.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • As the first point of contact, you will manage our telephone and the central e-mail inbox and ensure that requests are processed quickly and reliably. • You transfer bookings to the service planning system (PEP) and thus support correct planning. • The management team counts on your support for administrative tasks, for example in the registration of new employees. • With your eye for detail, you keep data up-to-date and contribute to efficient processes. • Thanks to your structured working method, everything goes well behind the scenes. Requirements • You bring a commercial education or experience in a comparable administrative function. • MS Office is part of your everyday work, especially Excel you use safely and routinely. • You communicate stylishly in German and easily understand Swiss German. • Self-employment, reliability and a pronounced service orientation distinguish you. • Ideally, you already know the USZ, but even more important to us is your motivation and your interest in the task. We offer: • Your regular working days are Monday and Tuesday from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. The telephone accessibility and the support of the e-mail inbox should be ensured from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. • For a successful start, we accompany you with a structured introduction. During the first two weeks, you work together with the team for two days between Wednesday and Friday. • After your training, there is the possibility to work in the home office on Monday.
