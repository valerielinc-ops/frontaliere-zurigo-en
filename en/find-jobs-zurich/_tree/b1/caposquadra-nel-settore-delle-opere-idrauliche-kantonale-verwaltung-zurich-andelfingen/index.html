The Thur/Rhine operation is a dedicated team of 8 employees who manage around 200 km of river banks. With our work we ensure an ecologically valuable and at the same time flood-safe maintenance of the waters. We connect natural waters and safety for the population and thus actively shape the habitats along Thur and Rhine. Your tasks

  • Coordination of all work in water maintenance
  • Directing work and managing staff
  • Cooperation in mowing and woodworking
  • Administrative tasks (hour recording, documentation of work, etc.) Your profile
  • Completed apprenticeship in forestry/agriculture/horticulture
  • Several years of professional experience as foreman in a similar field
