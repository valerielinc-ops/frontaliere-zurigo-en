Full description

As a diverse real estate service provider, we combine retail know-how with real estate expertise in center and mixed use site management. Thus, we can take over both the technical and the overall management of complex areas as well as shopping centers for our customers.

To complement an existing team in the centre of Regensdorf, we are looking for a competent and committed personality from October or by agreement as

Commercial Manager / Senior Manager (m/f/d)

Regensdorf Centre, 60-100 %

This is what moves you

  • As part of your tasks, you will manage an exciting real estate portfolio of areas, shopping centers and mixed-use properties. You’ll work with the Real Estate Site Manager, the Marketing Director, the Matter Officer and the Technical Manager.
  • You are responsible for drawing up all rental contracts (business, catering, office, parking, etc.)
  • You support the Real Estate Site Manager in negotiations with tenants and contractual partners and negotiate partly independently
  • You create the annual incidental cost accounting and recognize potential for optimization
  • You create reports and budgets including their plausibility checking expertly
  • The billing and further invoicing of services provided also fall within your area of responsibility.
  • In addition, you take over the preparation, budgeting, implementation and monitoring of the owners' finances incl. Corresponding correspondence

This is why you succeed

  • You have a basic commercial education with a further education in the real estate sector (manager: in with professional certificate or dipl.) Property Trustees:in
  • If you have already gained practical experience in the field of commercial / shopping center / real estate management, this is an advantage
  • You have a pronounced numerical flair, have accounting expertise and can demonstrate sound IT knowledge (MS-Office, REM of advantage)
  • With your service-oriented, flexible and resilient nature, you score
  • Communication and negotiation skills distinguish you and are also a real team player

Here are some of our benefits

  • Working hours: We live mobile work with individual (year) working hours and/or part-time work. Up to 40% home office is possible. Work-life balance is a top priority for us, because balanced and relaxed employees are important to us. The purchase of additional holidays is possible.
  • Mobility and food: You will receive a contribution for the mobile phone. For your food, we also make a contribution.
  • Education and training: Promoting employees is an important part of our culture. Benefit from generous support in internal and external education and training. We offer career opportunities in management, specialist and project functions
  • Salär: With us you can expect a market-based remuneration with absolute equal pay for all genders. The pension benefits are attractive and you can choose the right one for you from different models. The accident protection insurance covers the private hospital department.

More information about our benefits can be found on our career page.
