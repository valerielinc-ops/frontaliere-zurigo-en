Full description

We design Zurich from the ground up: liveable and climate-neutral. Bring your expertise and work with us on a future-oriented city. For the assistance of the business unit Transport and Urban Space we are looking for reinforcement as of January 1, 2027 or by agreement.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for the efficient and reliable administrative handling of business within our business unit.
  • They edit and handle all financial transactions in the business area, ensuring high quality and user-friendly processing.
  • They edit population concerns and proposals in consultation with the responsible project managers.
  • They provide support in the preparation and implementation of committees, workshops and events. For individual committees, you manage the office independently in cooperation with the project manager.
  • They think in a networked way, quickly grasp connections and also keep track of different tasks and stakeholders.

Profile

  • several years of professional experience in a demanding administrative function
  • Commercial education is an advantage.
  • Networked and solution-oriented thinking as well as the ability to quickly familiarize yourself with new topics and tasks.
  • Professional interaction with different contacts, strong communication skills and high service orientation.
  • Very good oral and written language skills, stylish German as well as good knowledge of the M365 environment.
  • Structured, independent and responsible personality with a high level of initiative as well as willingness to further develop and gradually assume additional responsibility.

We offer

  • The opportunity to shape the future of the largest Swiss city.
  • A culture that is shaped by respectful, reliable, ambitious and inclusive values and an employer who lives equality, diversity and inclusion.
  • Passionate and committed colleagues whose heart beats for Zurich.
  • A workplace located within walking distance to the HB Zurich.
  • Attractive employment conditions, such as contribution to catering costs, a solid pension fund with an above-average employer share and the opportunity to reconcile work and private life with flexible working hours and home office.
