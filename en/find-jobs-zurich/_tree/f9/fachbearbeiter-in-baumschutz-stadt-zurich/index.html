Full description

A city tree takes decades to grow. It takes an excavator a few minutes to damage it. In order for Zurich to become greener, we are looking for people who will prevent exactly that.

As a specialist tree protection specialist in the South Region, you will accompany construction projects and renovations on public green areas. They assess trees, determine suitable protective measures and ensure that they are implemented professionally. Your place of work is at the Seestrasse 157 base in Zurich.

We are looking for 1. March 2027 or by arrangement a specialist tree protection officer.

  • They establish protection measures for trees and plantations in construction projects and renovations and monitor their implementation
  • They assess trees and create expertise for projects of Grün Stadt Zürich and other service departments
  • You advise the districts and tree care teams of your region on questions about tree protection
  • They carry out practical work on trees together with the tree care group
  • You actively contribute your expertise to working groups and projects on tree conservation
  • You have completed training as a gardener, forest keeper or farmeras well as further training as a tree inspector, arbor care practitioner, arbor care specialist or are ready to complete it
  • They bring several years of professional experience in tree care or tree control as well as in-depth knowledge of woods and tree species
  • You work independently and responsibly and can assess professional situations and make comprehensible recommendations
  • You communicate clearly, respond to different people and can represent your professional assessment convincingly even with different interests
  • They have a category B driving licence and good MS-Office skills. A lift operator test is advantageous

Our cooperation at Grün Stadt Zürich is characterised by appreciation and trust, enjoyment of our work and a view of the whole.

With us you benefit from the employment conditions and social benefits of the city of Zurich: 4 to 6 weeks holidays plus 6 company holidays, the possibility to buy holidays as well as 16 weeks maternity and 4 weeks paternity leave. We also offer flexible retirement models. In addition, there are various training and further education opportunities, a contribution to your lunch check and 600 Fr. mobility contribution per year.

Their gross annual wage is between CHF 84'000 and CHF 98'000 with a 100% pension. Depending on your experience and competencies, your effective wage may differ from this range.

Zurich greener. That is our vision. Grün Stadt Zürich maintains, designs and manages the public green areas. These make up about 40 percent of the city area. With their work, our approximately 620 employees contribute to the high quality of life in the city of Zurich.

Diversity enriches our work. That is why we promote equal opportunities and work for mutual respect and a non-discriminatory workplace.
