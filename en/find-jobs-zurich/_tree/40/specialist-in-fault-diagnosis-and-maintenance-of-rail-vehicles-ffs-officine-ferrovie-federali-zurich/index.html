You can look forward to that.

You have experience in the maintenance of trainsets and want to make a difference in an innovative and established team that makes sense? Then you are exactly right with us. At SBB, you have the chance to contribute your skills and at the same time acquire new skills that will help you to advance professionally. You are not exaggerating when you say that Switzerland stands still without you. As a repair specialist: you make sure that our 62 long-distance double-deck trains are in top condition. Attractive working conditions are important to us, even in the case of irregular working hours. Night and weekend work is paid for by us, which can amount to between 500 and 700 francs per month depending on the use. You will also receive time allowances. This means you have more free time than others without night and weekend assignments. Our other benefits such as child allowances, free 2nd class GA and discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • As a specialist technician, you will carry out maintenance and inspection work on our state-of-the-art FV dosage and thus make an important contribution to ensuring that our customers travel with the highest possible comfort.
  • At our FVD, you will diagnose complex faults in control technology, electrical and electronic units as well as pneumatic and electromechanical systems with the help of technical descriptions, schemes, operating and deployment data, measuring and test devices and experience.
  • You assess the functionality of vehicles, determine repair measures and partly carry them out yourself.
  • You exchange components of different complexity, check them and perform important functional checks on systems and components. Change tasks such as conversions or modifications are also part of your tasks.
  • With the help of modern IT tools, diagnostic tools, software and control systems, you make your contribution so that the rail vehicles are safe and on time.

You bring that with you.

  • For the sometimes physically demanding tasks, you are physically resilient. But do not worry, you can rely on help and support at any time. For us, togetherness and community in the team count.
  • It gives you pleasure to work in a shift company with irregular working hours and weekend assignments.
  • With a completed vocational apprenticeship EFZ as an electrician:in or automatic engineer:in, automotive mechatronic engineer:in, polymechanic:in with an understanding of the electrical field, you are ideally equipped for the new task. Expelled. Experience in the maintenance of multiple units is mandatory for this job.
