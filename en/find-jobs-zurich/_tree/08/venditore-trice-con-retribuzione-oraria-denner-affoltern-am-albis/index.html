Full description

Work location: Denner AG, Filiale 614 Affoltern Albis Jonentalstrasse, Jonentalstrasse 1, 8910 Affoltern am Albis

Get stuck in. Move forward. Make a difference. Lots of warmth and a “hey, can I help you? – team spirit”. And always cross the finish line together. That’s Denner. That’s our store. And soon it could be yours, too. Join the fresh winning team now.

Important information

Career changers and people returning to work are welcome.

Responsibilities

  • Operate and balance the cash register
  • Receive and check fresh produce, as well as all other goods, and stock the shelves in your store
  • Check the best-before dates on products
  • Ensure order and cleanliness in the store and stockroom
  • Correctly implement the store layout guidelines

Requirements

  • Ideally, more than two years of professional experience; experience in retail is an advantage
  • Completed vocational training (EBA); training in retail (food) or a related field is an advantage
  • Willing to work outside the usual “nine to five” hours from time to time—and enjoy your free time when others are working
  • Physically fit; some packages weigh up to 10 kilos
  • Comfortable using a PC, tablet and smartphone—and quick to get the hang of other digital tools (e.g. for inventory management)
  • Enjoy being around people, with plenty of warmth and a positive attitude
  • German (good knowledge)

What we offer

  • Part of the Migros Group: A strong brand rooted in retail under the Migros Group umbrella
  • Healthy working conditions: High standards in occupational safety, ergonomics and hygiene.
  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 5 extra weeks of parental leave (19 weeks) for mothers, at 100% pay. Fathers can enjoy 4 paid weeks with their new baby.
  • Fair wages: 13 monthly salaries and fixed salary increases for everyone instead of bonuses for a few.
  • Mobile phone plan: Spoilt for choice: Get connected with Galaxus Mobile, Wingo or Sunrise from CHF 10.-.
  • Migros Bank: Get better interest rates and fees on your personal, investment or mortgage accounts.
  • Employee benefits: 10 percent off your next big shop (twice a year).
  • Vacation: 6 weeks of vacation for everyone, plus extra days for service anniversaries.
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts on car rentals, hotels, events and many other things.

Contact

Application & contact Fabienne Contini Recruiter, Sales Mittelland

Recruitment process

  • Application screening: We review applications as they come in, shortlist candidates and send the shortlist to the manager for review. We communicate the decision on whether you will continue or be eliminated from the recruitment process by phone or email. Time until response: Up to three weeks
  • Personal video or phone interview: If we receive a large number of applications, we may conduct a personal phone or video interview. This allows us to get to know more candidates in a short time and invite suitable people to the next round. Time until response: Up to one week
  • Trial work: A trial day or half-day gives you the opportunity to get to know your future work environment and team members better. It also gives us the chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Time until response: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & contract issuance: After our verbal job offer, we will verify the key terms of the contract again. Once we have reached an agreement, we will arrange for the contract to be prepared and sent by post. Time until response: Up to three weeks

Note for staffing agencies We only consider direct applications for this position.

Employment rate: 20-40%
