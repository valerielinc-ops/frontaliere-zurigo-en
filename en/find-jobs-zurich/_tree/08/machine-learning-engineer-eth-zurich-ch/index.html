Machine Learning Engineer

100%, Zurich, fixed-term

The Swiss Data Science Center (SDSC) is a national research infrastructure in data science and artificial intelligence (AI) within the ETH domain, with EPFL and ETH Zurich as founding partners. Its mandate is to support academic labs, hospitals, industry and public sector stakeholders, including cantonal and federal administrations, through their entire data science journey, from the collection and management of data to machine learning, AI, and industrialization. With a large multidisciplinary team of professionals across three locations (Lausanne, Zurich, Villigen), the SDSC provides expertise and services across domains such as health and biomedical sciences, energy and sustainability, climate and environment, and large-scale scientific infrastructure.

Project background

You will provide machine learning engineering expertise in the context of several SDSC partnerships in the German speaking part of Switzerland. In this role, you will meet partners (companies, public sector entities, NGOs) to understand their needs and help them define a high-impact project with the SDSC. Together with the SDSC data scientists, you will be responsible for the societal and/or industrial impact of your projects thanks to your machine learning engineering expertise.

Preferred startdate: January 1st

Job description

  • Elaborate solutions that convert data science prototypes into impact in the context of an industry / public sector / NGO collaboration, with particular focus on the engineering side and in view of meeting production requirements
  • Create original solutions with business partners, from solution design to industrialization
  • Share expertise in machine learning engineering (and related fields) with fellow data scientists/ machine learning engineers
  • Presenting to a non-technical audience will also be part of your activities.

Profile

  • Master degree in Computer Science, Mathematics or similar field
  • Good knowledge of machine learning concepts
  • Demonstrated expertise in machine learning engineering, particularly in an industrial context
  • Ability to present to various audiences
  • Open to collaborative work and team spirit
  • Highly appreciated: experience as a full-stack engineer, experience as a (data) architect, experience deploying LLM-based solutions in industrial contexts
  • Proficiency in English; knowledge of German highly appreciated
