Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Canton School at Lee Lecturer(s) for Computer Science 40% The Cantonal School Im Lee Winterthur offers the following maturity profiles for pupils following the 2nd or 3rd class of the secondary school or the lower secondary school in a four-year course:

  • the mathematical-science profile
  • the musical profile
  • the linguistic profile
  • the pedagogical/psychological/philosophical profile Currently about 700 pupils in 34 classes and about 90 teachers belong to the Cantonal School Im Lee. Computer science is offered as a basic and supplementary subject.
  • Your Assignments A full schedule includes 23 lessons weekly. Her teaching assignment comprises 9 lessons in the basic subject of computer science. In addition to their teaching activities, there is at best the opportunity to get involved in special weeks and special projects.
  • You have a completed master's degree in computer science and would like to enter the profession as a high school teacher. Ideally, you already have teaching experience.
