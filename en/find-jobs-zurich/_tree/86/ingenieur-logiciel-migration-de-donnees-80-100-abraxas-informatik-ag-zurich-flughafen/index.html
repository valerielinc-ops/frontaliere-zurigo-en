Full description

We are a motivated software engineering team and are looking for you to further develop our Data Migration Services so that we can connect new customers with our new tax solutions. You will support our team in implementing technical initiatives and work closely with your colleagues. With us, you will work with modern technologies, short decision-making paths, and plenty of room for your own ideas.

Your role

  • You design, optimize, and scale our migration pipelines
  • Complex data structures are your specialty. You analyze dependencies in order to design and implement intelligent mapping logic and automated cleansing strategies
  • You develop automated tests that ensure the integrity and quality of the data throughout the entire process.
  • You prepare the migration and test data and monitor the processing. You bring your findings from error analysis to the team so that solutions can be developed together.
  • You drive the continuous development of our development processes, tools, and best practices.

Your profile

  • Several years of practical experience creating and optimizing complex SQL queries
  • Initiative and analytical thinking to improve data queries and processing times
  • In-depth knowledge of data modeling, data management, and data architecture
  • Ability to quickly familiarize yourself with complex legacy databases
  • Experience analyzing complex data structures and identifying anomalies
  • Good knowledge of SQL databases, particularly PostgreSQL and MS SQL Server
  • Knowledge of Python or experience with Python frameworks is an advantage

Your benefits

  • Up to 60 % working from home possible with a 100 % workload
  • Purchase of vacation days (2 weeks per year)
  • 41-hour week with flexible working hours at a full-time workload
  • At least 25 days of vacation per year
  • Support for continuing education with 100 % financial or time coverage
  • Free SBB Half-Fare Travelcard for all employees

We foster an open first-name culture – so feel free to send us your application directly using the informal form of address.
