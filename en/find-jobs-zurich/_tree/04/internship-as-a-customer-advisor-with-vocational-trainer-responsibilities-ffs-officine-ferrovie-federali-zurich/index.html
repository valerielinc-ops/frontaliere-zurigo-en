SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. You are passionate about vocational training and want to make a difference together with a well-established team of vocational trainers? Do you enjoy working with young people, introducing them to the professional world and accompanying them on their way? Then you are exactly right with us! Become part of our vocational training team, which supervises between 10 and 18 learners. You can move that.

  • As a customer consultant: in a vocational trainer function, you lead learners and together with them and the motivated team of the travel center ensure the advice, sales and support of customers across all channels and along the entire mobility chain.
  • You share responsibility for the agreed financial, productivity and quality goals (e.g. income, costs and customer satisfaction) and thus support the achievement of the goals.
  • You tailor the organization and training of the learners on an ongoing basis and are responsible for the always optimal and economical use of resources.
  • With the right decisions, your drive and your skill, you lead, challenge and develop the learners so that you can make a difference together.
  • You work with the various stakeholders within the SBB and login and ensure the flow of information.

You bring that with you.

  • In daily contact with the learners and our customers, your communicative and winning nature is the key to success.
  • Thanks to your willingness to change and the joy of the digital world, you lead learners with a lot of social skills to sustainable success.
  • You have a 3-year apprenticeship and ideally 2 years of experience as a customer consultant: in public transport.
  • You have completed the training as a vocational trainer:in or are motivated to tackle it.
  • We assume that you have the location language under control (C2) and have knowledge of French (A2) and English at level B1.
