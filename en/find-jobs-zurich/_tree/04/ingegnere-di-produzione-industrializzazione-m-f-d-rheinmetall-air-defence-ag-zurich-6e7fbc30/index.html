Full description

What we are looking for as a certified production technician (m/f/d) industrialization you are the interface between development, production and purchasing. You evaluate components in terms of productionability, cost and quality and contribute significantly to the efficient industrialization of new products. Your aim is to combine technical feasibility with economic efficiency and to convert them into the specification. Tasks

  • contact person of development to manufacturing topics and close cooperation with manufacturing, procurement and quality in the industrialization of components
  • analysis and optimization of individual parts and assemblies in terms of feasibility, economy and process capability (including with methods such as DtC, DfM and Cmk, etc.)
  • release of technical documents such as drawings, specifications and work plans from the manufacturing perspective
  • support of the organization by means of corrective and preventive quality assurance measures such as feasibility and root-cause analyses, red correction, handling of NCR, initial time of production (FAI), lean methods etc.
  • Requirements - completed training as a polymechanic or comparable - completed higher training HF, z.B.
