About the Role

  • Build and maintain data models in dbt on Databricks that turn raw source data into reliable, well-documented data products
  • Translate business questions and requirements into clear data structures, KPIs and shared metric definitions
  • Define and promote best practices for building BI solutions: data model design for reporting, dashboard templets and CI / CD pipelines
  • Enable self-service: give business users certified datasets, clear documentation and guidance so they can build their own analyses on trusted data
  • Set up governance for data consumption, including access rights, certified versus ad hoc content, ownership, and lifecycle management of reports and datasets
  • Work with data engineers, analysts, BI developers and business stakeholders across domains, and coach them on modelling and BI best practices

Your Skills and Experience

  • Educational background in business economics or similar
  • A track record of building data models that feed production reports and dashboards
  • Strong SQL and hands-on dbt experience in production, including incremental models, tests, macros and documentation
  • Solid data modelling skills: dimensional models with fact and dimension tables, and layered architectures from raw data to business-ready tables
  • Familiarity with data mesh concepts and experience building data products
  • Experience with Databricks, including Unity Catalog and performance tuning of queries and tables
  • Knowledge of SAP data and its business logic in areas such as finance, sales or supply chain (a strong plus)
  • Confidence working with business teams to turn their questions into clear metrics and agreed KPI definitions
  • Care for data quality: automated tests, freshness checks and reconciliation of figures against source systems
  • Experience with reporting tools such as Power BI and self-service and natural-language analytics such as Genie
  • Software engineering practices applied to data: Git, code review, CI/CD and some Python for automation
  • Fluency in English
