Full description

movemi AG operates the largest fitness network in Switzerland with 126 ACTIV FITNESS and 16 fitness parks. Do you want to design how gyms will feel and function in the future? In this role you develop the studio concepts of ACTIV FITNESS and Fitnesspark equally. You will accompany ideas that expand our added value, from the first draft to the pilot to the introduction.

Luogo di lavoro: movemi AG, Verwaltungs movemi AG, Thurgauerstrasse 32, 8050 Oerlikon

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You will see trends in fitness, health, leisure and hospitality, watch competition and role models at home and abroad and see what opportunities are offered for our two brands.
  • You develop ideas for new offers that expand our added value in fitness and beyond, and make decisions with clear decision bases.
  • Together with product development and marketing, you will sharpen the differences between ACTIV FITNESS and Fitnesspark – in particular the perceptible added value of Fitnesspark.
  • You plan and lead pilots for new formats, zones and offers. With usage data and customer feedback, you create clear foundations for an introduction or discontinuation decision.
  • From successful pilots you develop specifications for areas, zones and equipment per brand and studio type. You keep the area productivity in mind.
  • You bring the product perspective to new and renewed studios and work closely with Expansion & Construction.
  • You hand over agreed concepts to the responsible teams for devices, content and operations and accompany the introduction in the studios.
  • You develop wellness and recreation concepts together with our Chief Scientific Officer.
  • You measure the success of introduced concepts based on usage, utilization and customer satisfaction and develop them further.

Requirements

  • At least six years of experience: in concept or innovation work in fitness, retail or hotel industry – including pilots and multi-site launches.
  • Completed studies (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): In sports science, interior design or business administration with a focus on shop or space concepts.
  • Feeling for trends, understanding brand and price positioning, and experience with spatial concepts.
  • A structured way of working and the ability to convince construction, operation and marketing with clear basis for decision-making.
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • English (good knowledge)
  • French (advantageous)

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Expert Discussion: In a specialist discussion, specific knowledge relevant for the execution of the job is requested by the manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, the team. If possible, there is time to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Discussion with case presentation: Specific expertise is examined on the basis of a case or other assessment instruments. After processing, the result of the leader is presented and then discussed together. Usually, the team is also represented at the presentation. As a rule, the conversations are conducted by the superior, without a representative from the HR. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition: After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
