Full description

Caritas Switzerland has been operating asylum centres at different locations in the canton of Zurich since March 2024. As a supervisor for the night and weekend service, you are responsible for the personal and age-appropriate care of the unaccompanied minor asylum seekers during the night, weekends and holidays at the MNA-Zentrum an der Aathalstrasse in Zurich-Uster.

Mansioni

  • Introduction, guidance and accompaniment of residents on topics such as nutrition, hygiene, order and cleanliness
  • Co-responsibility for creating a good atmosphere
  • Co-responsibility for compliance with the regulations and safety in the centre
  • Creation of reports and information sharing internally and externally
  • Implementation of cleaning and hygiene concepts
  • Regular tours and presence
  • Participation in employment and animation
  • Participation in monthly team meetings
  • Work in shiftwork with sole responsibility, with focus on weekends

Requisiti

  • Completed training
  • Experience in migration and/or youth work desirable
  • Good knowledge of German language as well as good oral knowledge in English, further foreign language knowledge of advantage
  • Good computer user knowledge (MS-Office)
  • High resilience and resilience and conflict management competence
  • Flexibility, resilience and enforcement
  • self-initiative, self-employment and high level of work organisation
  • to provide temporary work services
  • High sense of responsibility and duty
  • Immigrant Leumund
