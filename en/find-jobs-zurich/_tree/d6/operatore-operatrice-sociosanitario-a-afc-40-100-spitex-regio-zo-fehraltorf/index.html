Full description

Spitex Regio ZO is a progressive (non-profit) organization and provides professional and comprehensive needs-oriented care and support on behalf of the municipalities of Fehraltorf, Russikon and Weisslingen. Our goal is to enable sick, impaired or reconvicted people of all ages to live a self-determined life at home.

We search immediately or by agreement

Health professional EFZ 40% - 100% Do you want to experience meaningful and exciting moments in your learned profession? Then we are the perfect partner for you.

These tasks await you

  • Basic and treatment care in stable client situations
  • Assignments in complex client situations with the support of a nurse
  • Need- and situation-appropriate care services based on care planning, according to competencies and professional application criteria
  • Keeping a meaningful maintenance documentation
  • Needs assessment by means of home economic assessment
  • Need and situational support in the area of living environment and everyday life in simple and complex situations
  • Budget management taking into account the resources of the clients according to economic aspects
  • Support for preservation and promotion, or regaining individual skills with the involvement of our clients’ resources
  • Independent and delegated administrative activities
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation within the organization

  • Resilient, innovative and flexible personality
