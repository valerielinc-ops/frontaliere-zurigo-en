Bahnhofstrasse 3, Postfach 371, Dietlikon / ZHDein Rolle You are responsible for the acquisition of new customers and the expansion of the maintenance portfolio through the recovery and acquisition of assets that are not currently managed by Otis (off-portfolio growth).

Your tasks

  • You identify and analyze attractive market segments, target customers (decision makers) and geographic potential
  • You are responsible for expanding the maintenance business by actively addressing potential new customers (by telephone, email, personal visits, networking and events, in accordance with the provisions of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR))
  • You also use tenders for effective lead generation
  • You advise our customers on the service products and independently conduct contract negotiations until the conclusion of the maintenance contract
  • When you’re not on the phone, you’re out and about with your customers, so you’ll always have your ear on the market and maintain your customer relationships with owners, administrations and real estate companies.
  • Our CRM system is your daily tool, where you maintain all activities as well as contact persons, plant stocks, current service providers, contract terms etc.
  • Close cooperation with our technical teams and (key) account managers

You bring that with you

  • Technical or commercial training with technical understanding
  • Several years of experience in active B2B sales or in new customer acquisition
  • Demonstrable success in building new customer business
  • Strong Hunter mentality with high initiative
  • Safe appearance and strong communication skills
  • Negotiation skills, final strength and entrepreneurial thinking
  • Independent, structured and goal-oriented working method
  • Experience with CRM systems
  • Very good knowledge of German, English skills are advantageous
  • Category B driving licence

Ideally, you will bring the following

  • Experience in the technical service environment
  • Knowledge of the elevator, building, construction or real estate sector
  • Experience in selling service or maintenance contracts

What we offer you

  • A responsible task with a lot of design room
  • Attractive remuneration with performance-related bonus scheme
  • Long-term career prospects in an international company
  • Modern equipment and innovative technologies
  • Company vehicle, also for private use
  • 5 weeks of holidays and an additional 5 days of holiday in the old year week
