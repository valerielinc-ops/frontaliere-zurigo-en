Job description Do you want to become part of a rapidly growing Swiss direct lending fund that is revolutionising SME lending in Switzerland? We offer you the opportunity to actively shape our company!

Who we are

creditworld connects Swiss SMEs with professional investors such as pension funds. SMEs benefit from attractive financing options that go further than traditional banking solutions. Investors gain access to a new asset class with interesting returns, supporting the backbone of the Swiss economy.

Tasks
