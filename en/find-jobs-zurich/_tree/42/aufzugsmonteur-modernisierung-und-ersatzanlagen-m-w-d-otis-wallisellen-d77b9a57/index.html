Bahnhofstrasse 3, Postfach 371, Dietlikon / ZH Basic function You belong to the modernization department, so you revive old plants. You are also responsible for the installation of new lifts in existing buildings. This shall include the following tasks:

Main tasks

  • Carry out the necessary repairs and conversion work on plants in order to give them a longer service life
  • Reception of materials and appropriate preparation of the construction site
  • Assembly of all the mechanical and electrical components of our plants
  • Commissioning of completed plants
  • Transfer to the clientele

Qualifications/Experience/Knowledge

  • You have completed technical training such as electro-installer:in, polymechanic:in, automotive specialist:woman or similar. If you are already familiar with lifts, this is a big advantage.
  • You speak fluent German
  • You like to carry personal responsibility and are not too bad to get your hands dirty
  • You plan and coordinate your day independently and show a friendly attitude
  • We require a category B driving licence

What we offer you

  • It awaits you a task with a lot of freedom and responsibility
  • You will be trained and accompanied by a specialist
  • In addition, you will be equipped with a mobile phone and your own company vehicle, which you can also use privately.
  • We care about your personal development. In concrete terms, this means that we support further training and internal promotion opportunities are open to everyone.
  • 5 weeks holiday
  • Good pension fund
  • Catering allowance
