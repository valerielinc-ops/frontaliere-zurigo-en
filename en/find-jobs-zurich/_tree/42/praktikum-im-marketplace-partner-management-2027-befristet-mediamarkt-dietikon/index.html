Full description

Your mission

From 01.02.2027 to 31.01.2028 you will gain practical experience in the Marketplace Partner Management in the following activities:

  • You implement marketplace and sales strategies and actively contribute to achieving our revenue and growth goals
  • You identify new marketplace sellers at national and European level
  • You build sustainable partnerships and maintain contact with existing and potential partners
  • You organize and conduct sales discussions with future marketplace sellers independently
  • You accompany marketplace partners through the onboarding process and ensure a high-quality implementation
  • You recognize challenges early on and develop suitable solutions
  • You support our partners in using the marketplace back office and relevant tools

Your match with us

  • You are in the bachelor's or master's degree program in economics or would like to gain practical experience in Marketplace Partner Management.
  • First professional experience in sales, purchasing or business development is advantageous
  • You communicate proactively and enjoy working with different teams to achieve common goals
  • You are curious, show your own initiative and convince with a strong hands-on and sales mentality
  • You appear empathetic and recognize the needs of your counterpart quickly and purposefully
  • You organize yourself independently, set clear priorities and keep your goals in mind at all times
  • Confident knowledge of German and English in words and writing, French and/or Italian are advantageous

At MediaMarkt, you are part of a team that achieves more together. We exchange tips, pack together and laugh about small everyday moments – this energy makes our everyday work special. Many colleagues especially appreciate the feeling of moving big things together and having fun. Technology excites us, but the team behind it makes the difference.
