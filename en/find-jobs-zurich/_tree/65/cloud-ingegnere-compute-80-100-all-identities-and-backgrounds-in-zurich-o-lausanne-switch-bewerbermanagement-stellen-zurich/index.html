Full description

Is your team and work interested in building and operating the best research cloud? Then become part of the CURE (Cloud and Undercloud Reliability Engineering) circle, a team of around 10 people who work together collaboratively and agile. The team language is mainly English. Together they develop and operate the research cloud of tomorrow for the Swiss university and research landscape. Their focus is on stable, secure and scalable cloud services, as well as an engineering culture where collaboration, knowledge sharing and continuous improvement are a matter of course. In this role

  • Responsible for the operation and further development of our OpenStack-based cloud platform.
  • Actively shape the technical future of the platform and contribute your ideas to architecture, upgrade and scaling topics.
  • Ensure secure, stable and highly available operations across compute, storage and networking.
  • Automate processes and advance standardization and efficiency in operations.
  • Develop monitoring and observability solutions to identify risks at an early stage and continuously improve service quality.
  • Support in 2nd/3rd level support and solve challenging technical challenges.
  • Actively share your knowledge in the team and contribute to an open engineering culture. You should bring this
  • A university degree in computer science (uni, FH, HF) or comparable qualifications with corresponding professional experience.
  • At least five years of practical experience in the engineering and productive operation of OpenStack as well as experience in securing the platform and technical troubleshooting.
  • Strong technical expertise in OpenStack, solid Linux expertise and good programming skills in Python.
  • You bring experience with infrastructure automation, especially Ansible, as well as Git/GitLab and Docker.
  • Basic knowledge of Kubernetes and an understanding of Monitoring, Reliability Engineering and Service Level Objectives (SLOs) complement your profile
  • Negotiable knowledge of English as well as knowledge of German or the willingness to acquire it independently Not absolutely necessary, but we are happy if you also bring the following:
  • Experience with site reliability engineering and provisioning workflows.
  • Willingness to participate in the further development of OpenStack in the community. This is how we work at Switch
  • At Switch we communicate at eye level, so it is important to us that we start from the first contact.
  • We live collaboration and see lifelong learning as the key to success
  • As a digitalisation and innovation service provider, we are shaping the Swiss university environment of tomorrow
  • Our flexible working hours and hybrid working methods enable a high compatibility of work and everyday life, important details can be found right here
  • With us, bilingualism is a living DNA, switchies switch between German and English in everyday life quite naturally. In order for everyone to achieve a comfortable language level, we offer support and language training
  • Our offices are centrally located and super accessible by public transport.
