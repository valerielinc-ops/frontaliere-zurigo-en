Description

Within a DevOps team in the Document Solutions Engineering area, you will help shape the output processes of Zürcher Kantonalbank and make a direct contribution to future-proof customer correspondence. Together with Business Engineers and IT developers, you will work to ensure that document templates and output processes are professionally specified, implemented and continuously improved across all business areas. In doing so, you will combine broad stakeholder management with the automation and digitization of document-related process steps.

Your tasks

  • Gathering, structuring and specifying requirements for document templates from specialist departments across the entire bank
  • Analyzing and optimizing document processes in the standard output process chain
  • Coordinating, supporting and testing the implementation of requirements with the DevOps team (Business Engineers and IT developers)
  • Advising and empowering specialist departments in the use of the self-service tool for document templates
  • Identifying manual document processes and converting them into automated end-to-end output processes in collaboration with the specialist departments
  • Ensuring the quality and consistency of documents across various template management tools and all channels (e.g. print, email, e-banking)
  • Coordinating stakeholders from various areas of the bank and continuously keeping them informed about progress

Your profile

  • Higher vocational education with a federal diploma or federal HF diploma, or higher vocational education with a federal professional certificate
  • At least 3 years of experience in business engineering and mandatory knowledge of document and/or output management
  • Sound knowledge of process management and agile project methods (SAFe)
  • Experience in the banking sector is a plus
  • You stand out with confident stakeholder management and strong self-management, even when dealing with parallel requirements
  • You impress with initiative, proactive action and a holistic view of end-to-end processes
  • Very good spoken and written German

Our offer

  • A motivated, self-organized and upbeat team that is looking forward to welcoming you
  • Exciting and diverse range of responsibilities with many networking opportunities throughout the bank
  • We give you room for your ideas – together, we shape the bank of tomorrow
  • Your professional development is close to our hearts – we actively support and guide you on your journey
  • Modern workplace in District 5 with excellent public transport connections (Hardbrücke railway station)
  • You can organize your work flexibly in consultation with your team (e.g. mobile working and flexible working hours)
  • Comprehensive package with attractive base salary, variable compensation and a wide range of benefits
