Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 50% - 80% For our innovative and close-to-nature offer "Sandgrueb Animal and Nature-Supported Therapy" in child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy at the Egg site, we are looking for a reliable and responsible personality immediately or by agreement, who will safely chauffeur our young clients from the institution or home to the equestrian complex and back again as Their tasks

  • Safe and punctual driving service throughout the canton of Zurich for children and adolescents to and from the "Sandgrueb" riding facility according to the timetable
  • Accompanying young passengers throughout the boarding and disembarking process and throughout the journey
  • Collaboration with the various professionals (care, social pedagogy, therapy and school) of different locations in the KJPP (inpatient, semi-inpatient and outpatient), parents and management
  • Ensuring vehicle safety such as correct installation of child seats, downhill control, cleanliness in the interior
  • In the case of waiting times in the "Sandgrueb" riding facility, participation in technical or operational activities such as support for specialists in the field of experience pedagogy

Your profile

  • Hold valid category B driving licence
  • Correct, up-to-date special private extract for the legally compliant treatment of minors
  • Educational skill, serenity and empathy in dealing with children and adolescents in demanding situations
  • High de-escalation readiness and psychological resilience in unpredictable behavior
  • Punctuality, reliability and a predictive, safe driving style
  • Good and clear communication skills in German
