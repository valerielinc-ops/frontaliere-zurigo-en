Postdoctoral position at Ingenuity Lab Zurich

100%, Zurich, fixed-term

Engineering the future of medicine. The Ingenuity Lab at ETH Zurich, led by Prof. Inge K. Herrmann, develops next-generation medical materials and technologies at the interface of engineering, materials science and medicine. Our ambition is to fundamentally rethink how technologies interact with biological systems, opening new avenues for precision diagnostics, targeted therapeutics and minimally invasive interventions. We are looking for an exceptionally motivated, creative and scientifically curious doctoral researcher who is excited about addressing unmet clinical needs through engineering innovation.

Project background

We are seeking an outstanding postdoctoral researcher with exceptional intellectual curiosity, scientific creativity and the ambition to explore new scientific frontiers. Our research spans fundamental scientific discovery, engineering innovation and clinical translation, with current activities in:

  • Intelligent and adaptive medical materials
  • Novel sensing principles and spectroscopic technologies
  • Precision therapeutics and minimally invasive surgery
  • Engineering technologies for women's health
  • Materials and technologies for robotic medical interventions

Job description

  • We offer a unique opportunity to jointly shape a research project that builds on your scientific strengths, interests and original ideas, while leveraging the distinctive capabilities of our research environment
  • Embedded in the world-class engineering ecosystem of ETH Zurich and closely connected to the University Hospital Balgrist
  • Our laboratory provides an exceptional setting for exploring unconventional ideas, connecting disciplines and translating fundamental discoveries into transformative medical technologies

Profile

  • A PhD in mechanical or electrical engineering, chemistry, physics or a related discipline
  • An excellent scientific track record demonstrating originality, rigor and intellectual independence
  • Exceptional scientific creativity and the ability to formulate compelling research questions and develop original approaches
  • Strong experimental and analytical capabilities, combined with the ability to turn conceptual ideas into tangible scientific advances
  • Enthusiasm for exploring unfamiliar scientific territory and connecting ideas across disciplines
  • Excellent communication skills and a collaborative approach to research and mentoring

Scientific creativity is one of our most important selection criteria. We particularly value individuals who see possibilities where others see limitations, recognize unexpected connections and are excited by the prospect of creating something genuinely new. We welcome candidates from diverse scientific backgrounds and are particularly interested in complementary expertise that can enrich and expand our research portfolio.

We offer

  • The intellectual freedom and scientific support to develop an original research direction
  • A vibrant, international and highly interdisciplinary research environment
  • Access to cutting-edge research infrastructure, including OR-X (the operating room of the future)
  • Direct interaction with clinicians and opportunities to address unmet medical needs
  • Opportunities to advance discoveries towards clinical translation and entrepreneurship
  • Active support in developing an independent scientific profile, securing competitive fellowships and building an international research network
  • Individual mentoring and career development towards academic or industrial leadership
  • We have a strong trackrecord and highly successful alumni (Forbes 30 under 30, MIT 35 under 35, several spin-off founders)
  • Employment conditions in accordance with ETH Zurich regulations

Curious? So are we.

We look forward to receiving your application, including

  • A curriculum vitae and publication list
  • A motivation letter outlining your scientific interests and ambitions
  • Up to three scientific contributions that best illustrate your originality and individual contribution
  • A brief research vision describing a scientific question, unconventional idea or emerging opportunity that particularly excites you
  • Contact details of two academic references

Further information about The Ingenuity Lab can be found on our Website . Questions regarding the position should be directed to Lisa Cartelli lcartelli@ethz.ch.

Please note that we exclusively accept applications submitted through our online application portal. Applications via email or postal services will not be considered.

Your research vision is an invitation to start a scientific conversation. Together, we will explore how your ideas and expertise can flourish within our research environment and develop into an ambitious and distinctive postdoctoral project.

We would like to point out that the pre-selection is carried out by the responsible recruiters and not by artificial intelligence.
