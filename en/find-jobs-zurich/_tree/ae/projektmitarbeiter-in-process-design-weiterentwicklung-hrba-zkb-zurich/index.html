Full description

80% - 100% | People & Culture | Zurich | Beginners: in The team HR Business Advisors (HRBA) of Zürcher Kantonalbank accompanies and supports executives in all management and HR topics and processes along the entire employee life cycle (e.g. onboarding, performance management, compensation, offboarding). As part of a targeted further development, processes and working methods in the HRBA environment are revised and optimized. The aim is to review, optimize and document processes end-to-end and to use suitable templates and technical support options in a targeted manner in order to support leaders even more effectively in the future. In your role, you actively shape this further development. Your tasks

  • Systematic structuring, documenting and standardizing of existing processes (including roles and responsibilities)
  • Review of processes and documents regarding labour law requirements, internal policies, data protection and comprehensibility
  • Develop templates, checklists and content with uniform formulations that meet the requirements mentioned above
  • Close cooperation with HRBAs and Legal
  • Support in building a structured storage and versioning the processes and templates

Your profile

  • Master's degree, preferably in the field of human resources, labour law, business administration or a comparable degree programme
  • First professional experience or internships in HR administration or in a comparable process-oriented environment
  • Pronounced initiative as well as the willingness to contribute ideas and show optimization possibilities
  • interest and basic understanding of process work (e.g. from study, further education or project experience); ideally first experience with process documentation
  • Basic knowledge of Swiss labour law and data protection or a high willingness to deepen quickly and independently
  • High affinity for structure, rules and accuracy as well as pleasure in bringing order and clarity to processes and documents
  • Very good knowledge of German

Our offer

  • We count on your ideas and offer you space for your impulses in the further development of our process landscape and documentation.
  • Collaborate with dedicated people with a lot of experience who are happy to share their knowledge
  • In-depth insights into the various topics of our successful bank
  • Various opportunities to develop personally and professionally
  • Central and modern workplace
  • Flexible working hours, mobile work and work-life balance are a matter of course for us
