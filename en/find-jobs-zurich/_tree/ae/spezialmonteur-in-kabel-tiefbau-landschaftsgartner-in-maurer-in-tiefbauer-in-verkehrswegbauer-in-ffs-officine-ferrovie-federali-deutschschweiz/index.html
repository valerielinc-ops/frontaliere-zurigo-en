  • You take responsibility for the maintenance and renewal of the cable systems of our infrastructure and thus make an important contribution to the safety, quality and availability of the systems.
  • Maintenance, renewal and renovation work on cable paths and in cable shafts you carry out independently and in a team.
  • You guide and advise external service providers and coordinate materials and deliveries on the construction site.
  • You build and maintain mast foundations reliably.
  • You report completed work and you record your working hours and expenses.
  • As a security manager: in you monitor the security measures according to the security dispository and if you are interested you take on assignments as a security guard: in after appropriate training.

You bring that with you.

  • You bring a high level of responsibility and a high level of safety awareness and enjoy working outdoors in every weather.
  • According to our safety guidelines, you have good health with unrestricted hearing and vision.
  • Ideally, you bring experience as a security guard: or you are ready to complete the training and take on assignments if necessary.
  • You have a Category B driver’s license, advantageously also Category BE, or you are ready to purchase it for material transport and assignments with a trailer.
  • With digital work equipment you are safe and use smartphone, tablet and office applications in everyday life.
  • You have completed basic training with EFZ as a landscape gardener:in, mason:in, deep farmer:in or road builder:in and you communicate safely in German in word and writing.
