Full description

With your craftsmanship talent, you create stylish furniture and refined interior designs. The company focuses on precise working methods and close cooperation in the team. For our client we are looking for activities

  • production of high-quality furniture and interior fittings
  • processing of solid wood veneers and high-quality panel materials
  • Independent execution of demanding carpentry work
  • assembly of exclusive interior fitting projects
  • work according to work plans and technical drawings
  • quality control and detailed processing at a high level
  • Close cooperation with project management and team
