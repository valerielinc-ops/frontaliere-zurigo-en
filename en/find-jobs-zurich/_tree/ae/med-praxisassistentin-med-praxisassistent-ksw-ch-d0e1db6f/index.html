Full description

The Clinic of Cardiology has three cardiac catheter laboratories and a versatile ambulatorium with around 50 dedicated employees. To strengthen the team, we are looking for 1. December 2026 or by appointment a medical practice assistant for our ambulatorium.

Tasks

  • Support for cardiac examinations such as blood sampling, ECGs, ergometries and echocardiographies
  • In addition, you could look after patients for invasive examinations such as stress echocardiography and electrocardioversion.
  • Accompanying apprentices
  • Co-organization and coordination of daily ambulatory operations
  • Reception, scheduling and disposition of our outpatients
  • Processing of telephone requests and correspondence by e-mail

Profile

  • Completed training as medical practice assistant / medical practice assistant EFZ
  • You have several years of professional experience, ideally in the field of cardiology
  • You are a sociable, empathetic person and collaborative cooperation is important to you
  • You have an independent, structured and solution-oriented way of working
  • Through your communicative nature, you succeed in fostering constructive cooperation with the interdisciplinary offices in the hospital.
