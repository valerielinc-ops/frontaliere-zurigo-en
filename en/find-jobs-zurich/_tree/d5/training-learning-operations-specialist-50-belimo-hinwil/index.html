Full description

Introduction

Your chance to help shape training at Belimo.

In this versatile function, you ensure that our training sessions and webinars run smoothly – from planning to moderation and follow-up. At the same time, you supervise our Learning Management System and support our national subsidiaries in dealing with it.

What will you do

  • You will organize training sessions and webinars, moderate in German and English and guide participants safely through the program and Q&A sessions.
  • For our recurring training event in Hinwil, you will help with the organization and moderation – from the invitation to the follow-up.
  • You support participants on site, during and after the trainings and are the contact person.
  • You coordinate internal and external speakers, prepare them and ensure a smooth process.
  • Occasionally, you will guide groups of visitors through our company and give an insight into the world of Belimo.
  • In the Learning Management System you set up training courses, maintain courses, learning paths and documents and create reports for use and participation.
  • You empower those responsible in our national subsidiaries to work with the LMS and support them with questions and technical problems.
  • You collect feedback and reliably and independently perform the administrative tasks around our training offer.

Who you are

  • You have a commercial, technical or educational education. Further training in adult education (e.g. SVEB certificate) is an advantage.
  • You have already gained initial experience in the administration and supervision of a learning management system and enjoy actively shaping and developing digital learning environments.
  • Likewise, you have already organized and accompanied trainings, seminars or events.
  • You feel comfortable in the exchange with different people and like to convey content in a lively and understandable way.
  • It gives you pleasure to learn new digital tools and easily switch between platforms.
  • You take as much care in administrative tasks as you do in the preparation of meetings and moderations.
  • You are interested in technology and building technology. Expertise in heating, ventilation and air conditioning (HLK) is not a requirement.
  • You speak and write fluent German and English and can moderate training in both languages.

We look forward to meeting you.

People at Belimo

Intelligent buildings are created by committed people. We bring talented people together and create an environment where they can succeed. At Belimo, respect, trust and mutual support across departmental boundaries help turn ideas into practical solutions quickly. Together we achieve more than we could ever achieve alone.

About Belimo

Belimo is the world market leader in the development, manufacture and sale of field devices for energy-efficient control of heating, ventilation and air conditioning systems. Flap drives, control valves, sensors and meters form our core business. Founded in 1975, the company has been listed on the Swiss Stock Exchange (SIX) since 1995.
