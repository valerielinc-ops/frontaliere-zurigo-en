About the job

As a key part of Google’s global partner organization, the Partner Investments Strategy and Operations team designs and governs investment and incentive frameworks that reward partner excellence, for healthy profitability and AI transformation of Google Cloud’s partner ecosystem. Working closely with a broad set of stakeholders, this team drives the global strategy for Google Cloud's ten-figure partner investment portfolio with accountability for all segments and all partner types. As a member of the Google Cloud Partner Funds Operations team, you will step into an Individual Contributor (IC) leadership role responsible for the operational execution and compliance oversight of Google Cloud's partner investment fund programs. This includes critical growth engines such as Deal Acceleration Funds (DAF), Partner Services Funds (PSF), Rapid Migration and Modernization Program (RaMP), and the Gemini Enterprise Transformation Fund (GET). In this role, you will require a strong execution background with the ability to maintain rigorous financial integrity and compliance. You will participate in the global implementation of these partner fund programs, serving as a key authority on compliance and high-value fund deployment. Your mission is to build and scale a thriving partner ecosystem while ensuring every dollar of the investment is anchored in a high return on investment (ROI) and strict regulatory governance. Google Cloud accelerates every organization’s ability to digitally transform its business and industry. We deliver enterprise-grade solutions that leverage Google’s cutting-edge technology, and tools that help developers build more sustainably. Customers in more than 200 countries and territories turn to Google Cloud as their trusted partner to enable growth and solve their most critical business problems.

Responsibilities

  • Participate in the regional implementation and administration of DAF, PSF, GET and RaMP partner funding programs.
  • Contribute to the development of a scalable, global review framework to maintain rigorous policy compliance and consistent financial oversight across all operating regions.
  • Serve as the initial point of contact for regional investment requests, ensuring that requests are within the terms of the programs and meeting compliance and value delivery standards.
  • Drive readiness for key strategic programs to regional partner and partner-facing roles, partnering with the global team to provide feedback on existing content.
  • Support the evolution of program policies and long-term strategic direction in alignment with senior leadership objectives, by providing timely feedback from regional partners and field.
