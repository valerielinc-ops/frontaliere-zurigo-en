Are you passionate about the kitchen and would like to become part of a dynamic team at the University Hospital Zurich? We are looking for specialists who provide with creativity and care for the culinary well-being of our patients, employees and guests. Look forward to a modern working environment and become part of our team!

Their main tasks

  • Preparation of the food offer for patients: inside and staff: inside and catering
  • Monitoring of meal preparation
  • Compliance with quality standards, hygiene and occupational safety guidelines
  • Active support in ordering and goods management
  • Contact for our learners
  • Varying working hours from 06.30-18.30

Your profile

  • Completed training as a cook EFZ
  • Operational readiness, high load capacity and flexibility
  • Interest in healthy eating
  • In addition to their cost awareness, they also have good IT knowledge (Word/Excel)
  • Passion for the daily professional challenge and professional pride
  • German in Word and Writing
  • Interest in kitchen ordering and in the associated administrative tasks is advantageous
  • Dietary knowledge or the willingness to develop in this area are desired
  • Motivated, resilient and team capable personality, coupled with high service orientation and a dose of humor

We offer you a varied area of responsibility in a modern working environment. Their further development is important to us. For this we use our in-house offer of training and further education.
