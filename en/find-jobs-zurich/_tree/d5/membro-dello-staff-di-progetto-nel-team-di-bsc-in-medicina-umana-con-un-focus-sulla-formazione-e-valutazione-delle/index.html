Full description

Project member: in the project team BSc Human Medicine with focus on skills training and assessment (m/f/d)

70%-90%, Zurich, permanent

ETH Zurich has been conducting the bachelor’s degree program in human medicine since the autumn semester 2017. This is developed in close cooperation with the university partners for the further master education (University of Basel, University of Zurich and Università della Svizzera italiana). We are looking for a:n project staff member in our alignment team to strengthen the project team at the earliest possible time.

Job description

You are responsible for ensuring the alignment of practical skills and abilities in teaching and exams in our curriculum. Together with lecturers, you will design and coordinate the use of simulation persons in the BSc MED: You will conduct training, develop existing scripts and are responsible for their implementation in the various modules. In the assessment area, you are responsible for the successful introduction of an e-portfolio that makes the competencies acquired in the bachelor’s degree programme visible across all semesters. In addition, you are responsible for the coordination of our Skills Lab MED. This includes the further development of the offers and their integration into the overall curriculum as well as the coordinative and organizational responsibility of the Skills Lab MED. Thus, the quality control and further development of teaching and practical examinations includes a large part of your area of responsibility.

PAKETH involves extensive planning and implementation work, which will also support you. As part of the Teaching Alignment Team, you are also involved in Faculty Development.

Profile

You have basic training in the field of health sciences or a similar field (pharmaceuticals, medicine, care, etc.). In addition, you will have in-depth experience in Health Sciences Education, in particular with regard to training simulation people and planning, implementing and testing practical skills and abilities in medical education. As part of the teaching and examination team, you work closely with our lecturers, so you should enjoy dealing with different actors from research, education and clinics.

They are characterized by an independent, exact and pragmatic way of working. You attach importance to teamwork, while also taking responsibility for your own area of responsibility. Based on your very good social skills, you maintain a service-oriented way of working. They bring excellent communication skills as well as excellent organizational skills. Stylistic German and very good knowledge of English are indispensable for the job. They maintain transparent communication in the team, are capable of criticism and have a “hands-on mentality”. They maintain an overview of several parallel processes. Through your networked thinking, you see possible synergies and can optimize processes.

We offer

This position offers the unique opportunity to help shape a young course of study. You will become part of an interdisciplinary team in the constructive ETH environment, where great emphasis is placed on teamwork and innovative ideas are welcome. Home office and flexible working hours are partly possible. The position can be taken immediately or by agreement.
