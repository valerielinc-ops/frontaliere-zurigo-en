Full description

80% - 100% | IT / Business Engineering | Zurich | Experienced professionals Welcome to the DevOps Team CHAI of the Zürcher Kantonalbank. We are a dynamic and innovative team that works passionately on the development and operation of a modern GenAI platform and implements a wide range of AI use cases for different disciplines. Our mission is to use OpenAI and Azure AI services to create future-oriented solutions that advance IT development and operations and optimize our banking services. Together we accompany and implement exciting AI use cases and thus contribute significantly to the digital transformation of the bank. If you enjoy designing the solution architecture of our GenAI platform in an agile environment while combining modern cloud and integration architectures with the requirements of business, IT security and architecture specifications, you are at the right place. Your tasks

  • Design the overall technical architecture of the GenAI platform, including data acquisition, LLMOps, provision of interfaces and integration into the bank’s existing IT systems
  • Design the implementation of new requirements for the GenAI platform and are responsible for ongoing development and optimization taking into account all components, expenses, operations and company-wide and regulatory requirements
  • Review designs, guide development teams on proven architectural patterns, and decide on technologies and tools to ensure scalability and maintainability
  • Collaboration with business and IT teams, in particular for coordination with system architecture and IT security, to integrate requirements of the departments into the existing architecture
  • Support in the technical planning and coordination of implementations as well as in dissolving dependencies with other teams
  • Observe and assess new technologies, frameworks and tools in the field of artificial intelligence and machine learning

Your profile

  • Several years of professional experience in the architecture and implementation of complex IT or cloud platforms, ideally with a focus on AI or data platforms
  • Strong expertise in system design and integration patterns such as microservices, event-driven architectures and interface architectures
  • In-depth understanding and experience with LLM and LLMOps implementations, including model delivery, pipeline orchestration and operation of a robust GenAI ecosystem
  • In-depth knowledge of cloud platforms, especially Azure, as well as container orchestration such as Kubernetes and the realization of scalable, resilient platform solutions
  • Ability to weigh up on-premises and cloud solutions, especially with regard to data residency in Switzerland and requirements of a highly regulated environment
  • Ability to document and communicate technical issues in a comprehensible manner and to moderate and advance challenging issues with different contact persons
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing

Our offer

  • Motivated, self-organized and established team that looks forward to you
  • Exciting and diverse range of tasks with many network possibilities
  • Great freedom for autonomous decisions and participation
  • Various opportunities to develop personally and professionally
  • Central and modern workplace
  • Flexible working hours, mobile work and work-life balance are a matter of course for us
  • Culture of appreciation and continuous (further) development – personal, technical and professional
  • Comprehensive package with attractive basic salary, variable compensation and versatile benefits
