Institute of Medical Microbiology

Job opening 01. May 2027

The Institute of Medical Microbiology at the University of Zurich is active in the fields of teaching, research and diagnostics.

Tasks

As the first point of contact of our institute, you ensure a friendly and professional welcome for our visitors and reliable support of the telephone exchange.

  • They receive visitors: inside and operate the telephone exchange.
  • They take care of the postal distribution as well as the sending of investigation reports and invoices.
  • They manage the material output, keep an eye on the stockpiles and ensure timely re-orders of sample vessels, office supplies and other consumables.
  • They take on general administrative tasks and thus contribute to a smooth daily routine.

The position includes permanent assignments from Monday to Friday morning as well as full-time on Wednesdays. In addition, we expect your willingness to represent each other in the case of holidays and illness in the reception team.

Profile

They work independently, reliably, service-oriented and think along. They are actively involved in the team and have humor. Even in a lively working environment, you will keep an overview and meet different stakeholders in a friendly and professional manner.

  • They have completed training and several years of professional experience in a similar field of work, preferably in the medical environment.
  • They communicate verbally and in writing very well in German and have good knowledge of English.
  • They master the common MS Office programs and have a quick understanding.
