Full description

Dermatology at the highest level in one of the leading private hospital groups in Switzerland, in the middle of Zurich. In Zurich, we are looking for a specialist in dermatology in the range of 60 – 100% by appointment. In an established clinical group for dermatology as well as aesthetic and plastic surgery, you can expect a dynamic medical team, modern infrastructure and a broad spectrum of dermatology in the outpatient setting. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • They take care of dermatological patients independently in the ambulatory setting
  • They cover the entire spectrum of classical dermatology and also treat aesthetically if interested
  • You write the patient documentation and maintain correspondence with assigning doctors
  • You work in collegial exchange with a medical team of around 30 specialists
  • They actively shape the professional development of dermatology at the site

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist titles in Dermatology and Venerology
  • Completed specialist training
  • Recognition for Switzerland for foreign diplomas. Provided at least three years of work at a recognized Swiss training institution
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

That sets them apart.

  • Self-employed, responsible work with high professional quality standards
  • Strong social and communication skills in dealing with patients, referees and colleagues
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary collaboration in a growing team

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Works in one of the leading private hospital groups for dermatology and aesthetic and plastic surgery in Switzerland
  • Collegial exchange in a medical team of around 30 specialists and around 110 employees
  • Very modern infrastructure
  • Possibility for use at several locations in German-speaking Switzerland
  • Appreciative corporate culture with the aspiration to grow together

Additionally for applicants from the EU

  • Support for MEBEKO recognition of your diploma
  • Assistance in organizing your journey to Switzerland
  • Assistance in finding accommodation through our internal rental department
