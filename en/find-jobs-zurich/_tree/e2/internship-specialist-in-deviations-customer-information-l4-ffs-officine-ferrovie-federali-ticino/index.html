SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. Before, during and after the trip, you will help ensure that the offer published in the timetable is implemented in high quality and that the customer is always well informed. You work closely with long-distance and regional train accompaniment and take on the role of host along the entire travel chain. In the East Region, more than 350,000 people travel every day on more than 3,000 trains. With your commitment, you make a tangible contribution to customer satisfaction. Numerous events and major events also offer your team and you the opportunity to make a major contribution to the Group’s customer orientation goal. Customer information is constantly evolving. Current developments can also mean a change of location. If you enjoy change and cooperation, you can help shape the next steps together with us. You can move that.

  • As a hub in trouble management, you ensure customer-friendly handling of deviations and consistent, accurate customer information at the station, on the train and online.
  • By making direct announcements on trains in the East Region, you inform customers correctly and in good time.
  • With timely information of the counter staff, the appointment of customer representatives: inside and the steering of the supervision of passenger transport you ensure competent information on site.
  • In case of deviations, you develop and decide on alternative travel options so that the travel chain remains reliable.
  • In your hub function, you reconcile customer needs with requirements for quality, safety, environment, efficiency and legal requirements.

You bring that with you.

  • Networked thinking, strong customer orientation and joy in realizing solutions together with the team.
  • Initiative, rapid decision-making, assertiveness, teamwork and high performance.
  • You see irregular working hours, sometimes also on weekends, and/or shift work as beneficial for a flexible design of your free time.
  • Routine in dealing with railway production systems and with Microsoft 365.
  • A commercial or company basic training as well as several years of experience in the operation of public transport with in-depth knowledge of the company processes.
  • Very good knowledge of German at least C1 as well as knowledge of another national language at level A1.
