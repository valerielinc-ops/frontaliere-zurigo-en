Full description

At ABB, we help industries run leaner and cleaner—and every person here makes that happen. You’ll be empowered to lead, supported to grow, and proud of the impact we create together. Join us and help run what runs the world.

This position reports to:

Finance Manager

__

In this role, you will have the opportunity to act as a business partner to the relevant business manager and leads local finance teams to optimize the financial performance within an assigned division / business area. Each day, you will support effective business decisions by monitoring, analyzing, and interpreting financial data to evaluate various business alternatives. You will also showcase your expertise by recommending the most appropriate business solutions.

The work model for the role is: hybrid #LI-hybrid

This role is contributing to the overall success of the PMA Uster location. Main stakeholders are both local and regional management.


You will be mainly accountable for:

  • Site Finance leadership & business partnering Site Finance leadership & business partnering: Lead the finance and controlling function at the manufacturing site and serve as a trusted business partner to the site manager and leadership team.
  • Performance & value creation: budgeting, forecasting, performance monitoring and investment evaluation.
  • Reporting, internal controls & compliance: reliable reporting in line with US GAAP and Swiss requirements, internal controls, and audit readiness, including SOX.
  • People & Finance transformation: standardization and digitalization of finance processes, team development, and collaboration with HR.

• Qualifications for the role:

  • At least five years of experience as a manager in finance, business controlling or a related function, preferably in an industrial or manufacturing environment.
  • Experience in management reporting, budgeting, forecasting, cost accounting and financial planning; familiarity with end-to-end finance processes in a production environment.
  • Knowledge of US GAAP and Swiss statutory accounting requirements.
  • Experience with internal controls and SOX.
  • Confident user of ERP and reporting tools; SAP experience and business analytics platforms
  • Strong analytical, communication and presentation skills, with the ability to translate financial information into clear, actionable insight.
  • Fluent in German and English; able to work effectively across functions and in an international environment.
