Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 80- 100% For our special consultation hour early childhood at the Zurich Ambulatorium in child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement one or more Their tasks

  • Case management with diagnosis and therapy in the age range up to 5 years under regular supervision
  • Psychodiagnostics and therapeutic interventions according to current guidelines
  • Parental counselling and parent groups according to current guidelines
  • Participation in the planning and implementation of further education and lectures
  • Working in an interdisciplinary team under psychological-medical co-direction

Your profile

  • Completed Study of Clinical Psychology (Master or License)
  • Completed or advanced psychotherapeutic training
  • Comprehensive knowledge of psychodiagnostics and psychotherapy with children and adolescents
  • Knowledge of developmental psychology and psychopathology of this age range
  • Team orientation and network strength with good work organization and pleasure in working independently
  • Experience and flexibility in dealing with different nationalities and cultural backgrounds

  • Knowledge that advances: Extensive initial, further and further training offers
