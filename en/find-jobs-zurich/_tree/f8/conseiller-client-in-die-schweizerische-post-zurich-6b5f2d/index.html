Full description

Do you love personal customer contact? Are you happy with the active sales and advice? Together we network Switzerland. We are close to people with you.

You Can Make That Do

  • Be part of the dedicated team Pfannenstiel and bring your flair to the desk and the sales floor in dealing with people.
  • You sell all the postal and non- postal brand products. These include, for example, regional products or different offers in the field of telecommunications.
  • You also actively advise our customers on services from various other sectors such as health insurance, insurance and banks.
  • On the team you have the opportunity to take responsibility in various roles and develop yourself personally.
  • If you have never worked in a post office, we will get you fit! In our 4-month on-the-job training you will be optimally prepared for your new role as a customer consultant:in. During this time you will work with a workload of 80%, after successful completion in the amount you want. For more information on the cross-entry, see the factsheet entry sale and advice at the post office.

You bring this with you

  • Your natural sales flair and your open, friendly way of contact allow you to find the active exchange with our customers.
  • You have a completed basic training in retail trade or sales.
  • As an open and independent personality you can easily find your way around different situations.
  • You are digitally fit and have no trouble applying the usual MS Office programs.
  • For competent advice you need good German skills, further language skills are advantageous.
