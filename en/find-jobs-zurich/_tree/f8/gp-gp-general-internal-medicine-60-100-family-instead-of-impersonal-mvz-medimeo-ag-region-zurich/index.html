Do you want to work independently as a general practitioner, get involved and not have to carry decisions through several hierarchical levels? Are you consciously not looking for a large chain of practice, but a personally run country doctor’s practice with a long-term perspective and direct connection to Zurich? Then I look forward to an uncomplicated exchange by phone, e-mail or WhatsApp. For about 30 years, this established family practice has been firmly anchored in the region east of Zurich. In the course of the retirement of a long-term doctor, a long-term succession solution is now being set up. She expects a classic agricultural practice with a personal character, a loyal patient base and a broad medical spectrum – If interested, including individual children and families. At the same time, you benefit from modern infrastructure with digital X-ray, ultrasound, ECG and your own practice laboratory. The medical team remains consciously manageable in a three-way team. Direct decision-making processes, personal cooperation and the opportunity to contribute your own ideas are part of the concept. Despite the rural environment, Zurich can be reached directly and comfortably in about 25 minutes.

  • Specialist title General Internal Medicine or comparable qualification

Their advantages
