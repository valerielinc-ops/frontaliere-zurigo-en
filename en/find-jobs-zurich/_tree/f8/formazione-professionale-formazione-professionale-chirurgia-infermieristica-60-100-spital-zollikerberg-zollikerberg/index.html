Their tasks

If you want to shape learning, promote people and help shape the care of tomorrow, you can expect a variety of tasks:

  • Actively shape learning: They accompany and promote learners and students individually, oriented towards their educational goals, their level of development and the requirements of the respective education.
  • Creating learning opportunities : They make everyday care a place to learn, design practice-oriented learning sequences and carry out learning days as well as other educational offers.
  • Connect nursing and education: Together with the trainees, you design a professional, competent and holistic care of our patients and combine practical experience with professional learning.
  • Developing the station: As a member of the management team, you contribute educational issues and your perspective to the further development of the station and actively shape the quality of training.
  • Creating education together: You work closely with the nursing team, the vocational training team and the learning and education centre and promote the integration of education into everyday care.

Become part of the Department of Emergency and Acute Medicine at the Zollikerberg Hospital. Under one roof, we combine the areas of internal medicine, surgery, nephrology with dialysis center, the emergency department, the eye center, the medical ambulatorium, the day clinic and other specialized areas. Every year, the Clinic for Internal Medicine looks after more than 2,500 inpatients, while the Clinic for Surgery looks after more than 1,500 inpatients. With more than 6,000 emergency consultations and over 450 patients in the interdisciplinary intensive care unit, we offer a versatile and demanding medical work environment.

On our surgical department with the fields of orthopedics, trauma surgery, gynecology, plastic surgery and visceral surgery, you can expect a varied and demanding field of activity with a wide range of nursing challenges. In a dynamic and interprofessional team, we work in the skill-grade mix and attach great importance to individual, evidence-based and high-quality care of our patients before and after surgery.

In our 360° tour, get a first impression of your new place of work.

So that you can shape your role as a vocational trainee with pleasure and develop professionally, we offer you an environment that actively promotes education:

  • Well-accompanied in your role: A structured introductory concept as well as the accompaniment by the Learning and Education House, the educational responsibility in the department and Piers on the station support you in starting and developing your role.
  • Shaping vocational training together: You benefit from a dedicated network in the department and in the foundation, regular technical days, intervision and professional exchange with other vocational trainees.
  • Grow pedagogically: We actively promote the further development of your pedagogical skills and support you in role- and task-relevant further education.
  • Living education in day-to-day care: You work regularly with trainees and can help shape your assignments within the scope of service planning in coordination with those responsible for planning in order to carry out your educational tasks in a targeted manner.
