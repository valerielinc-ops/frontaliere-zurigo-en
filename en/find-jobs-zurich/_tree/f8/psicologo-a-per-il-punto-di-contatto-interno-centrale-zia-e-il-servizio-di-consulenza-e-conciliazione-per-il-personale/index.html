Central Internal Contact Point (ZIA) and Employee Advice and Mediation Centre (MBS)

Job opening immediately or by agreement, indefinitely

The Central Internal Contact Point (ZIA), which is open to all UZH members, will be rebuilt as part of the further development of university advisory services. The employees of ZIA also work for the Employee Advisory and Conciliation Office (MBS). Both bodies are neutral points of contact. Administratively, ZIA and MBS are assigned to the General Secretariat of UZH.

Tasks

As part of your work as a ZIA employee, you strengthen those seeking advice by:

  • receive and clarify the concerns raised;
  • forward them to the relevant UZH department.

As part of your work as an MBS employee, you advise those seeking advice by:

  • support them in a solution-oriented manner in stress situations at the workplace,
  • assess a conflict and provide competences,
  • assess the escalation level of a conflict and identify any legal issues,
  • motivate, if necessary, for therapeutic support, external coaching or the like,
  • If applicable, also carry out crisis intervention and accompanying measures.

Profile

For this position, we are looking for a psychologist with a license or master’s degree (focus on clinical psychology) as well as a degree in tertiary university or equivalent, federally recognized psychotherapy and/or coaching training. In addition, an education in mediation is desirable resp. There is a willingness to do so. They have professional experience in counselling and conflict resolution contexts, interest in people in their work environment and both sound diagnostic and intervention-oriented skills as well as excellent contact and communication skills (in German and English). They are empathetic and trustworthy, combine a high degree of initiative with good integrative skills (team and networking) and are flexible in terms of working time and method. Very good self-organization and management as well as good analysis and documentation skills round off your profile. Job sharing is possible.
