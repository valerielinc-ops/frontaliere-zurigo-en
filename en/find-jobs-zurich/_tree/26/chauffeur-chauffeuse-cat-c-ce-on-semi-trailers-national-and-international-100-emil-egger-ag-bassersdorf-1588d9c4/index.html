Full description

EMIL EGGER AG is the leading provider of heavy goods logistics in Switzerland. In the areas of crane work, special transports, industrial removals and rental lifts, more than 230 employees are employed. With our comprehensive specialist know-how, we develop individual and innovative customer solutions every day. Do you like to travel on national and international roads, enjoy the transport of machines and appreciate a varied and independent activity? Become part of our team! For our machine transports, we are looking for a reliable and committed personality. Your tasks

  • Carriage of national and international transports, mainly processing machines
  • Driving a modern 4×2 low-trailer tractor with mega-tarpaulin trailer
  • Professional loading and securing of transported goods in compliance with safety regulations
  • Independent and reliable handling of transport orders
  • Occasional transport with other trucks within our various departments
  • Maintenance and maintenance of the assigned vehicle

You bring that with you

  • Valid C/E driving licence and valid CZV certificate
  • Professional experience as a chauffeur, ideally in national and international machine transport
  • Experience in handling semi-trailers
  • Good knowledge of cargo securing
  • Self-employed, reliable and responsible work
  • Friendly and customer-oriented appearance
  • Flexibility regarding working hours and away nights
  • Readiness for isolated transports over the weekend
  • Willingness to take on assignments in other departments if necessary

We offer

  • A permanent job in a successful Swiss family business
  • A responsible and varied activity at home and abroad
  • An own, modern and well-equipped vehicle
  • A collegial working environment with a family-like corporate and du-culture
  • Regular training and training opportunities
  • Attractive employment conditions and good social benefits

Do you want to make a difference with us? Then we look forward to your application and to getting to know you personally!
