Letter and Parcel Delivery Driver — Die Schweizerische Post

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

You purposefully navigate the delivery van or three-wheeled scooter through city traffic – whether at a construction site or during rush hour – you find the fastest route. With you, letters and parcels arrive at the right address intact and on time.

What you can achieve

  • Early in the morning (approx. 06.00), you load the delivery vehicles together with your team and prepare your delivery route.
  • Thanks to you, letters and parcels arrive at the right addresses on time in your assigned area.
  • You answer enquiries about our services in a friendly and competent manner.
  • With your friendliness and reliability, you contribute to customer satisfaction.
  • You use modern work devices – such as a smartphone – to scan the consignments.
  • We also deliver post on Saturdays. Thanks to your team’s great commitment, you can still regularly enjoy Saturdays off with your loved ones.

What you bring

  • You have completed vocational training, preferably in the logistics sector.
  • You enjoy being on the road, hold a category B driver's license and ideally A1 - or are willing to obtain it before starting the position.
  • Teamwork is important to you, and you quickly get your bearings.
  • Happy customers are close to your heart, and you enjoy using modern work equipment every day.
  • Your reliability, as well as your independent and efficient way of working, set you apart, and getting up early is not a problem for you.
  • You are fit for this physically demanding role and enjoy rolling up your sleeves and getting stuck in. Good German skills, both spoken and written, round off your profile.
