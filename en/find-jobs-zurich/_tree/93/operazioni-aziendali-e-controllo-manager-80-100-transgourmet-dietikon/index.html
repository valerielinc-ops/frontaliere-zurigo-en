Full description

Casa del Vino SA stands for the highest consulting and assortment competence in the fascinating world of selected wines. Top-class exclusivities and well-known brands from Spain, France, Italy and all over the world characterize our range of quality products. With the Vinattieri SA winery belonging to the group, we manage our own production plant of the highest quality in Ticino, which is a leader in Switzerland. Our long-term personal relationships with producers, our own logistics, a webshop and Switzerland-wide distribution allow us to act quickly and flexibly on the market. Our customers include restaurants, hotels, resellers and private individuals. The company belongs to Transgourmet Schweiz AG and thus to the international Transgourmet Group.

Tasks

  • You capture the “big picture” including a comprehensive understanding of the processes and proactively introduce and implement improvement proposals taking into account best practice
  • Independent conception and development of various controlling reports including definition of relevant KPIs as well as monthly preparation and further development of the reports in close cooperation with the teams
  • Participation in monthly and annual accounts as well as preparation of the corresponding reporting and support in budgeting
  • You prepare presentations for the management and board of directors and work on various strategic topics
  • As a “super user” and primary contact person of our IT partner, you support your colleagues in dealing with our ERP (VinX) and constantly develop and maintain the system
  • You plan and independently carry out inventories at all locations
  • You support in the preparation of the various revisions (internal and external)

Requirements

  • You have a basic commercial education and several years of experience in a comparable job and / or a completed study including several years of experience
  • You have a high affinity for IT systems and financial/controlling tasks and are ideally familiar with agile working methods
  • You have an analytical and business flair
  • You work independently and are a reliable partner for our team
  • Your high communication ability and safe handling of systems and employees distinguish you
  • A strong knowledge of German is required. Italian and English are beneficial
  • Ideally, partial You are our passion for wine

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
