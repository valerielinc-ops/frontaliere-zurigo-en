The summer of 2026 has shown the great importance of water for agriculture in Switzerland and the conflicts that can arise over the use of resources. Good food, healthy environment

Agroscope is the federal competence centre for research in the agricultural and food industry. The researchers carry out their activities at various locations in Switzerland. The headquarters is Bern-Liebefeld (from 2026: Posieux FR). Agroscope is located at Eidg. Department of Economy, Education and Research WBF.

Additional information

The research group Water Protection and Material Flows conducts research in the field of nutrient management, flows and dynamics in agricultural ecosystems and the environment and links current knowledge with practice and policy advice. The associated Earth observation team develops spatial and non-destructive methods in the context of remote sensing to support these and other tasks of Agroscope in the future.
