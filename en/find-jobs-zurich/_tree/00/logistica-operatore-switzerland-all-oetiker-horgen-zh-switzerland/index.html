Full description

Tasks

  • Recording and processing incoming goods
  • Picking and preparing customer orders, including system entry
  • Packing, staging and recording shipments
  • Supporting general logistics and operational tasks, such as internal mail distribution or external delivery runs
  • Ensuring orderliness, cleanliness and occupational safety in the warehouse area
  • Actively participating in stocktaking

Requirements

  • Completed training as a logistics specialist EFZ or equivalent training
  • Forklift license (walkie stacker, counterbalance forklift and seated reach truck) and category B driver's license mandatory
  • Professional experience in warehouse logistics
  • Good PC user skills; SAP knowledge is an advantage
  • Very good spoken and written German
  • Team-oriented, flexible and resilient personality
  • Independent, conscientious and reliable working style
