You can expect an exciting turntable function in the maintenance of rolling stock in the Zurich region, where you actively shape the material supply and work closely with various specialist areas. We offer you a lot of personal responsibility and creative leeway so that you can promote solutions and make an important contribution to reliable rail operations. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad – also for the family – can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • You ensure that our teams have the required materials and spare parts at all times and can therefore carry out their work safely, efficiently and on schedule.
  • As a material manager: r you keep an overview of stocks and requirements and ensure an economical and reliable material supply.
  • You identify supply risks at an early stage, initiate the right measures and find sustainable solutions for stable material availability.
  • In this role, you work closely with different areas and partners and coordinate the material flow along the entire supply chain.
  • As the first point of contact for material questions, you make an important contribution to a functioning and efficient maintenance.

You bring that with you.

  • You like to analyze complex questions, set priorities and find pragmatic solutions.
  • Working with different stakeholders gives you pleasure. With your communicative and binding nature, you create trust and promote joint success.
  • You bring profound experience from maintenance. Knowledge in the field of rail vehicles is advantageous, but not mandatory.
  • Your basic technical training forms the basis for your broad understanding of technical contexts.
  • You have very good knowledge of German as well as knowledge of a second national language. You can safely handle SAP and MS Office applications.
