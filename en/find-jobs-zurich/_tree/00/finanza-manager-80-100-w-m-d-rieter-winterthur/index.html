Full description

Rieter is the world’s leading system supplier for the processing of natural and man-made fibres into short and long staple yarns and for the production of filament yarns, man-made fibres and nonwovens. With its comprehensive technology portfolio, Rieter covers the entire textile value chain – from fiber to yarn as well as from melt to textured filament, synthetic staple fiber and nonwoven fabric. Headquartered in Winterthur (Switzerland), the company develops and manufactures complex spinning plants, machines, systems and components for efficient and economical production. Rieter’s leading technologies enable a minimal use of resources and thus make a significant contribution to sustainability along the global textile value chain. Rieter has existed for more than 230 years, has 22 production sites in nine countries and employs around 6,400 people worldwide, of which around 9% are in Switzerland. Rieter is listed on the SIX Swiss Exchange under the ticker symbol RIEN.

You have power, are versatile and service- and goal-oriented? Do you like working in a team, are curious and always find innovative ways to reach your goal? Then you fit perfectly with us and can strengthen our team as

As Finance Manager, you support the Head Finance of Rieter AG in the management and further development of the financial function and take over the deputy in defined professional and operational matters. You ensure high-quality financial reporting, compliance with ICS and accounting standards, and advance the standardization of our financial processes. Particularly exciting: You play a key role in shaping the transformation and centralization of financial activities in Switzerland and continue to develop cooperation with our Shared Service Center in India.

Your main tasks and responsibilities

  • Completion and reporting: Review of monthly and annual financial statements and ensure high-quality reporting to the Group.
  • Budget and planning: Responsibility for budget and planning processes, including monthly rolling forecasts.
  • Compliance and controls: Ensuring compliance with IFRS and OR accounting rules, Group Accounting Policies and Internal Control System (ICS) requirements.
  • Process optimization: Identification and leadership of initiatives to optimize, standardize and automate financial processes.
  • Finance Transformation: Support and management of finance transformation projects, especially in the transfer of accounting and reporting tasks to the central financial organization.
  • Shared Service Center: Close cooperation with our Shared Service Center in India as well as continuous optimization of interfaces and processes.
  • Professional management: Professional management, support and further development of finance employees in the areas of accounting, reporting and internal controls.
  • General ledgers and deputies: Participation in selected general ledger processes and securing the necessary deputies in the team.
  • Deputy Head Finance: Representation of Head Finance in defined professional and operational matters.
  • Analysis and decision-making basis: Creation and preparation of business analysis and decision-making basis for management.

Your qualifications

  • University degree in Finance, Accounting, Business Administration or a comparable discipline.
  • Dipl. Auditor (CPA) or an equivalent recognised professional qualification in finance and accounting.
  • At least 5 years of relevant professional experience in accounting, auditing or financial reporting.
  • In-depth knowledge of IFRS, Swiss accounting (OR) and internal control systems.
  • Experience in the optimization of financial processes and in finance transformation projects.
  • Proven project management experience as well as initial leadership experience.
  • Strong analytical skills as well as high review and assessment competence.
  • Very good communication skills and experience in stakeholder management.
  • High flexibility during the month end.
  • Excellent knowledge of German and very good knowledge of English.
  • SAP knowledge is an advantage.
