Full description

With your use, you ensure that the Zurich canal network works reliably and that wastewater can be safely drained. Together with your team, you are on daily missions, cleaning channels and carrying out inspections using modern camera technology. You can expect a varied job that focuses on technical skill, teamwork and security. We are looking for reinforcement at the Werdhölzli location from now on or by agreement.

Ready for a job with meaning?

  • You perform channel inspections using modern camera technology, operate TV probes and other inspection equipment and create precise measurements.
  • You support your team in cleaning the city canal network and private drainage facilities, cleaning street collectors and empty separators as well as pump shafts.
  • You operate dishwashers and vacuum trucks professionally, support in service and maintenance work on the vehicles and ensure safe handling of the work equipment.
  • You secure and signal the work areas, document your assignments in reports and initiate urgent measures if necessary.
  • You take over night and Saturday missions as well as 5 to 6 weeks per year (all are paid extra) and support other groups within the drainage if operational requirements.
  • Completed training (EBA or equivalent, preferably mechanical / artisanal / technical) and driving license Cat. B
  • Rapid comprehension, craftsmanship, good physical constitution for outdoor work
  • Knowledge of German (level B2) and a customer-friendly approach
  • Readiness for night work, special assignments and pique service, where you make sure to arrive at the place of work within 60 minutes
  • Industry experience and PC user knowledge is beneficial
  • Generous mobility contribution
  • Food allowance
  • Equal pay
  • Above-average social benefits
  • Careful training in your work
  • A modern, down-to-earth and future-oriented working environment
  • Maternity leave: 16 weeks paid
  • Paternity leave: 20 paid days
  • Three days of care for your sick child without a holiday deduction
  • Development opportunities through urban education
  • ERZ Sportclub

With around 1,000 employees, ERZ is one of the largest service departments in the city of Zurich and operates in an exciting environment at the pulse of time. We are modern, down-to-earth, open-minded, all per you and want to make a difference. Together we are committed to a clean, innovative and sustainable Zurich.

ERZ stands for diversity and mixed teams.
