Full description

Would you like to participate behind the scenes? Then you're right here. The Belad Herdern is a central part of goods logistics. Every day we supply over 200 locations in the urban area of Zurich in a 7x24 hour operation.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Zurich, Operations Centre Herdern, Pfingstweidstrasse 101, 8005 Zurich

Important information

Deployments Mo.-Sa.. shift start: 05:00 or 06:00, work end 14:30 or 15:30 We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Loading and unloading of trucks
  • Moving goods with the truck
  • Various warehouse work including manual picking

Requirements

  • 1-2 years of professional experience: in logistics
  • Completed basic or further education: an advantage in logistics
  • Passport (cat. B) and own car mandatory, otherwise place of residence ideally in the greater area of the city of Zurich
  • SUVA recognised forklift ticket
  • Team-capable and reliable personality with independent working methods
  • PC knowledge
  • German (good knowledge)

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing.
