Full description

The Cantonal Police of Zurich and its approximately 4,000 employees assume responsibility for security in the canton. Do you want to actively shape our digital future and create the architectural foundations for an innovative, secure and responsible use of artificial intelligence? To strengthen our computer science team in Zurich, we are looking for a committed and competent personality as an AI infrastructure engineer/in 80 - 100% Your responsibilities:

  • Strategic planning, development and operation of a scalable, highly available and secure AI infrastructure (on-premises) and development of hybrid infrastructure solutions in close cooperation with the Office of Informatics (AFI)
  • Planning, deploying and managing computing, storage and network resources including GPU workloads and automating deployment and operational processes (infrastructure-as-code, CI/CD and MLOps)
  • Ensure and optimize the availability, performance, security and cost-effectiveness of the AI infrastructure as well as perform error analysis and support 2nd-level support
  • Developing and further developing infrastructure standards, operational and security concepts and ensuring compliance with requirements in the areas of data protection, information security, compliance and governance
  • Managing complex infrastructure projects with scheduling and financial responsibility as well as professional management of project teams and external service providers
  • Evaluating new technologies in the field of AI infrastructure as well as interdisciplinary cooperation with internal and external stakeholders and cantonal bodies Your profile:
  • Completed university degree (bachelor) in computer science or business informatics
  • Further education in the field of computer science as well as certifications in the field of cloud, container or AI infrastructure are advantageous
  • At least five years of professional experience, including at least two years in the relevant field
  • In-depth knowledge of Linux, Kubernetes, GPU infrastructures, storage and network technologies as well as infrastructure-as-code, CI/CD, IAM and monitoring
  • High level of social competence, strong team skills and an independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • High resilience, operational readiness and self-motivation as well as readiness for selective operations within the framework of the 7×24-hour operating organization Our offer:
  • An innovative and responsible activity at the pulse of technological development
  • Much room for manoeuvre in setting up and further developing the AI landscape of the Cantonal Police of Zurich
  • An exciting, interdisciplinary working environment with high social relevance
  • Attractive training and education opportunities
  • A state-of-the-art workplace in the police and justice centre with staff restaurant and in-house fitness centre
  • Progressive employment conditions and a balanced work-life balance
  • Above-average pension benefits at BVK with an employer share of 60% as well as individually selectable savings options (base, standard, top) Job start: As of 1 January 2027 or by agreement Have we aroused your interest? Then we look forward to your complete online application.
