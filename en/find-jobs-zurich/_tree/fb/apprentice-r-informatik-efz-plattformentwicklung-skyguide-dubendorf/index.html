Description Skyguide is responsible for air traffic control in Switzerland and neighbouring countries. Four values give us orientation: "We team up to succeed", "We drive innovation", "We own what we do" and finally: "We create great customer value". Would you like to learn more? Your mission: Start your apprenticeship with the basic year of apprenticeship at the BBC in Zurich. From the second year of your apprenticeship, you will be personally accompanied and supported by experienced specialist supervisors at Skyguide. During your four-year apprenticeship, you will gain exciting insights into the IT world of aviation and work on a wide range of tasks and projects. You will attend the vocational school in Uster and, if you perform very well at school, you will have the opportunity to complete the vocational baccalaureate. In doing so, we promote an environment in which learning, trying things out and developing further are expressly desired. We see mistakes as an important part of the learning process.

Responsibilities

  • Troubleshooting faults, solving problems and supporting users
  • Planning, installation, documentation, configuration and maintenance of networks, servers and other IT systems
  • Securing the IT infrastructure from hacker attacks and protecting data
  • Installation and configuration of software and hardware
  • Monitor system performance and ensure availability
  • Training of users in the use of IT systems
  • Participation in exciting projects in cooperation with different teams

Requirements

  • Secondary school leaving certificate
  • Motivated, high willingness to learn and fun working together
  • Interest in technical contexts and computer science, initial IT skills are an advantage
  • Teamwork: You give quick, honest feedback with the intention of achieving a constructive result or solution
  • Curiosity: You show curiosity through sincere interest in your surroundings, try new approaches, and ask thoughtful questions to gain a deeper understanding.
  • Fluent in German, good knowledge of English
