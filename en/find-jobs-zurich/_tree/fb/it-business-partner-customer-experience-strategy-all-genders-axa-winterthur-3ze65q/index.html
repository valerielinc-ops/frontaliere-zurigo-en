80-100%, Winterthurt / Smartworking

Create your future – together with us!

You want to work where business strategy and technology come together? You don't just want to discuss data and AI, but also create measurable added value for our customers? Then you're right with us.

As an IT business partner for the Customer Experience & Strategy (CES) department, you are taking overall technological responsibility in the management team of the department. You are shaping the business strategy entrepreneurially, driving the AI and technology transformation of our marketing and implementing the company-wide DTI standards in a binding manner.

As a member of the DTI leadership group "IT Business Circle" and the Customer Excellence Board, you also bring the perspective of the department to the overall level.

Three key issues are at stake: How do we tap into new market potentials with technology, data and AI (e.g. leads, GEO/SEO) and increase conversion, leading to measurable productivity and competitive advantages for the core business? How do we translate the portfolio strategy into an effective portfolio? And how do we ensure that these solutions are based on common platforms, run stable and that the portfolio remains protected from misinvestment, shadow IT and technical debt?

Become part of DTI. We are the technology, data and innovation partner of AXA Switzerland and together with the business we create the basis for unique customer experiences – in line with our company value "Customer First".

In the field of data, technology & innovation, around 600 software and system developers work in Switzerland:inside and develop the technology of tomorrow inhouse. We also collaborate in global teams and networks. We shape and integrate the latest tools, organize hackathons and our ailron product teams run independently and are responsible for their own activities from A to Z.

This is what your role expects you to do, clearly strategically (around 60% strategic, 30% tactical, 10% operative):

  • Business-Tech strategy: You will shape the business strategy in the management team of the department and translate the DTI strategy into a binding technology, data and AI roadmap including AI Lighthouses. Conversely, you will translate the business strategy into DTI priorities
  • Data & AI Value Creation: You identify data and AI potentials, pilot them and scale them industrially into the core processes of the department. To do this, you will work with the business to repair the use and complexity of the "Sweet Case Roadmap", win sponsors and control the value realization
  • Demand, Portfolio & Capacity Management: You are responsible for the requirements and the demand management including cost estimation and the cost portfolio and capacities along the value streams for several years. You can vote on the BAU and Change capacity plans with People Developer, the management team of the department, KGF (customer business field control board) and PPM (project portfolio management)
  • E2E Delivery & PI Governance: Together with Business Owner (BO), you are responsible for sustainable E2E-Delivery (Run & Change, Software & AI, entire stack). You define the annual goals with BO and Product Owner (PO), take off PI targets and promise the achievement of the goal with DTI Alignment
  • Architecture & Platform Governance: You implement the standards, platforms and Golden Paths of CTO and CDO in the department, prevent shadow IT and uncoordinated Legacy, control justified architectural exclusions with dismantling plans and plans for the reduction of technical debts
  • Investment Governance & TCO: You release investment applications technically and financially (DTI-sign-off), plausiblisiert Business case (build, run, licenses), the Burn-Rate and the management team will monitor the lead team early on from imminent budget overruns
  • IT operation & service quality: You design the service operating models, ensure availability, performance and SLA compliance of business-critical applications and are responsible for small applications such as Workplace AI, Robotics and End User, in CES-Ressort
  • Sourcing & Partner: You can strategically design service sourcing and contract negotiations with external service providers with
  • Stakeholder management & escalation: In case of conflict of goals between business requirements and DTI capacity, you manage the expectations of stakeholders. In case of system failures or at risk of business critical milestones, you lead the top escalations

You bring this with you

  • Perennial management or responsibility experience at the interface Business-IT, e.g. as IT business partner, portfolio or delivery manager, ideally in the insurance or financial sector
  • Strategic and entrepreneurial thinking: you understand business models, customer processes and customer experience and translate them into effective technology roadmaps
  • Data & AI competence: A well-founded understanding of data, AI and GenAI, and experience in bringing the pilot's Use case to productive scaled operation
  • Understanding technology and architecture: you are familiar with modern cloud, platform and enterprise architectures, life-cycle management and the conscious handling of technical debts
  • Portfolio & financial expertise: experience with Business Case, TCO considerations, budget and capacity planning, and investment governance
  • Agile Delivery at Scale: You know the scale of the Agile-Frameworks (e.g. SAFe, PIPlan) and the interaction of Business Owner, Product Owner and Product Teams
  • Governance, risk & compliance: knowledge of regulatory requirements (FINMA, DSG) and IT, data and cyber risk management
  • Service & operational understanding: experience with service management, SLAs and crisis or escalation situations
  • Convincing personality: You communicate with a firm and clear mind, except at the management level, remain sovereign in conflicts and inspire others for your ideas
  • You speak a few German and English, French skills are advantageous
  • Completed studies in computer science, business computer science, data science, business administration or a related discipline, or equivalent professional experience We are convinced that satisfied employees will deliver the best results. Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
