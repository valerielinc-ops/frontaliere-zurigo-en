80-100%, Winterthurt / Smart Working

Shape your future – together with us!

You want to work where business strategy and technology come together? Do you not only want to discuss data and AI, but also create measurable added value for our customers? Then you are right with us.

As part of the realignment of the Data, Technology & Innovation (DTI) department, we are strengthening the partnership between business and IT. As an IT Business Partner for the Customer Experience & Strategy (CES) department, you have overall technological responsibility in the department’s management team. You will shape the business strategy entrepreneurially, drive the AI and technology transformation of our marketing and implement the company-wide DTI standards. As a member of the DTI Executive Committee “IT Business Circle” and the Customer Excellence Board, you also bring the perspective of the department to the overall level.

There are three key questions: How do we use technology, data and AI to tap into new market potential (e.g. leads, GEO/SEO) and increase conversion and thus lead to measurable productivity and competitive advantages for the core business? How do we translate the portfolio strategy into an effective portfolio? And how do we ensure that these solutions are based on common platforms, are stable and that the department remains protected from misinvestment, shadow IT and technical debt?

Become part of DTI. We are the technology, data and innovation partner of AXA Switzerland and together with the business we create the basis for unique customer experiences – in line with our corporate value “customer first”.

In the field of data, technology and innovation, around 600 software and system developers work in Switzerland: in-house and develop the technology of tomorrow. We also collaborate in global teams and networks. We shape and integrate the latest tools, organize hackathons and our agile product teams run self-organized and are self-responsible from A to Z.

This is what awaits you Your role is clearly strategic (around 60% strategic, 30% tactical, 10% operational):

  • Business tech strategy: You will help shape the business strategy in the department’s management team and translate the DTI strategy into a binding technology, data and AI roadmap including AI Lighthouses. Conversely, you translate the business strategy into DTI priorities
  • Data & AI Value Creation: You identify data and AI potentials, pilot them and scale them industrially into the core processes of the department. For this, you prioritize the use-case roadmap together with the business according to benefits and complexity, win sponsors and control the value realization
  • Demand, Portfolio & Capacity Management: You are responsible for the requirements and demand management incl. Cost estimation and plans portfolio and capacities multi-annually along the value streams. The BAU and change capacity plans are coordinated with People Developer, the management team of the department, KGF (Customer Business Area Control Board) and PPM (Project Portfolio Management).
  • E2E Delivery & PI Governance: Together with the Business Owner (BO) you are responsible for a sustainable E2E delivery (Run & Change, Software & AI, entire stack). With BO and Product Owner (PO), you define the annual goals, take PI goals and discuss goal achievement for each PI. DTI alignment
  • Architecture & Platform Governance: You enforce the standards, platforms and Golden Paths of CTO and CDO in the department, prevent shadow IT and uncoordinated legacy, manage justified architectural exceptions with dismantling plans and plan capacity for technical debt reduction
  • Investment Governance & TCO: You formally approve investment applications technically and financially (DTI sign-off), plausibility business cases (build, run, licenses), monitoring the burn rate and warning the management team early on of the threat of budget overruns
  • IT operation & service quality: You design the service operating models, ensure availability, performance and SLA compliance of the business-critical applications and are responsible for small applications such as workplace AI, robotics and end user applications in the CES department
  • Sourcing & Partner: You strategically shape service sourcing and contract negotiations with external service providers
  • Stakeholder Management & Escalations: In case of conflicts between business requirements and DTI capacity, you manage the expectations of the stakeholders. In case of system failures or critical business milestones at risk, you lead the top escalations

You bring that with you

  • Several years of management or responsibility experience at the Business–IT interface, e.g. as an IT business partner, portfolio or delivery manager, ideally in the insurance or financial sector
  • Strategic and entrepreneurial thinking: You understand business models, customer processes and customer experience and translate them into effective technology roadmaps
  • Data & AI competence: In-depth understanding of data, AI and GenAI as well as experience in bringing use cases from pilot to productive, scaled operations
  • Understanding technology and architecture: Modern cloud, platform and enterprise architectures, lifecycle management and the conscious handling of technical debt are familiar to you
  • Portfolio & financial expertise: Experience with business cases, TCO considerations, budget and capacity planning and investment governance
  • Agile Delivery at Scale: You know agile scaling frameworks (e.g. SAFe, PI Planning) and the interaction of business owner, product owner and product team
  • Governance, Risk & Compliance: Knowledge of regulatory requirements (FINMA, DSG) as well as IT, data and cyber risk management
  • Service & operational understanding: Experience with service management, SLAs and crisis or escalation situations
  • Convincing personality: You communicate bindingly and clearly up to the management level, remain sovereign even in conflicts and inspire others for your ideas
  • You speak fluent German and English, knowledge of French is an advantage
  • Completed degree in computer science, business informatics, data science, business administration or a related discipline, or equivalent professional experience

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
