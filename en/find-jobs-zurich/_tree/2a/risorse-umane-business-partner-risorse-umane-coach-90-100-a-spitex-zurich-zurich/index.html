Full description

With around 1,500 employees at 22 Spitex locations, Spitex Zurich serves around 10,000 customers and thus provides more than 1 million customers annually. Assignments in the four main business areas Somatic Care, Memory Care, Mental Care and Home & Care as well as in special services. Spitex Zurich operates in the innovative Teamflex operating model: the teams at the locations are assisted to work autonomously. They are each supported by a coach trio consisting of team coach, specialist coach and HR coach. We are a team of ten HR coaches and are looking for someone who wants to participate, thinks independently and loves the professional exchange. And yes – laughter is expressly allowed with us!
