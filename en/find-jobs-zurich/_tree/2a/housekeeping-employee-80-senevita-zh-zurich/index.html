Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,300 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland.

For the Senevita Residenz Nordlicht in Zurich, we are looking for an Employee/in housekeeping 80% Your tasks

  • You ensure a professional cleaning of the public rooms, senior citizens and holiday apartments as well as the care departments.
  • You collect the laundry, sort it and take over the further processing independently.
  • You follow the hygiene rules.
  • You serve and maintain our in-house cleaning equipment.

You bring that with you

  • You are a real team player - One for all and all for one - this is our motto
