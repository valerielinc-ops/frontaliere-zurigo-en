Full description

Platform Engineer Banking Operations 80-100% ◆ Ash or Zurich ◆ m/f/d

Their role

  • They ensure the stable operation and continuous development of our containerized banking platform as well as connected Avaloq and third-party systems (including release and incident management)
  • Expand monitoring and logging with Prometheus, Grafana and the ELK Stack, increasing transparency, availability and operational quality
  • Develop CI/CD pipelines, automated deployments and integration tests with GitLab, Argo CD and Infrastructure as Code
  • They advise development, operations and specialist areas and bring technical requirements into a secure, scalable and operable platform architecture

Your education and experience

  • Completed computer science training or comparable qualification with several years of professional experience in platform or application operations
  • Practice with GitLab, Argo CD, CI/CD, as well as experience with autom. Release and deployment processes and understanding of high availability, scaling, performance and security
  • In-depth experience with OpenShift/Kubernetes and Kafka Event streaming/messaging and rest APIs - as well as monitoring and observability. Avaloq Know How is a Plus

Their way of working

  • They take responsibility, show their own initiative and work in a structured and solution-oriented way, as well as quality-consciously.
  • You bring your position clearly, act cooperatively and achieve sustainable solutions with different stakeholders

Your team

You work in an experienced and dedicated team within IT Application Operations. The team operates at two locations (Eschen and Zurich) and is responsible for central platforms and integration components. We look forward to developing our modern platform together with you.

Your perspective with us

LLB offers you the opportunity to actively shape a technically demanding and varied field of work. They combine operational platform operations with modern automation and observability and thus make a direct contribution to the stability, security and further development of our banking platform. In doing so, you can contribute your broad wealth of experience and continuously develop yourself further. Are you there?
