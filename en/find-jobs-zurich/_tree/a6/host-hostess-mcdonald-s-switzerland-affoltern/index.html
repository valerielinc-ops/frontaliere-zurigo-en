Full description

Company presentation

At McDon's, we think every visit must be a pleasant, friendly and friendly moment. Our team is keen to warmly welcome our customers and make every moment a memorable one. If you love human contact and look for a dynamic, stimulating and many opportunities job, you are meant for us!

Description of the role

As a guest, you are the image of our restaurant. You welcome guests with a big smile, organize original activities and ensure that everyone, and especially the youngest, has a great time. You bring energy, creativity and good humour to the team, making every visit unique.

Main responsibilities

  • Welcome guests and create a friendly and fun atmosphere.
  • Organize birthday parties and creative events for children
  • Organize guided tours of the kitchen for guests
  • Provide support to the team at the counter, Mc-Caribbean or restaurant during peak hours.
  • Support for administrative tasks for the management of the restaurant

qualifications required

  • Personality extraverted and positive
  • I like working with people, especially children.
  • Creative and full of ideas
  • Reliable and motivated team player
  • Good communication skills (local language required, other languages an asset)

Benefits

  • Flexible working hours to promote your work-life balance - A multicultural and supportive team environment - Opportunities for training and career development — many of the managers have started like you!
  • Compensation and working conditions in accordance with the provisions of the collective agreement (L-GAV)
  • 50% discount on our products in our restaurants in Switzerland and Liechtenstein
  • Sunrise subscriptions at reduced prices
  • Access to exclusive discounts via the "Business Benefits" platform
  • Additional holidays are offered to employees over 50 years of age.
  • Free access to the largest online training library
