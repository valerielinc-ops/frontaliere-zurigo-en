Full description

Do you like new challenges and want to get involved in the lively and inspiring environment of the school? Then an exciting task awaits you in a motivated team! Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for a motivated school assistant at the kindergarten level, which will complement our team until the summer holidays 2027.

Tasks

  • Accompaniment and guidance of the children in exercise units, according to the order of the class/promotion teachers at the kindergarten and at the lower level
  • Promoting attention, independence and reliability of students
  • If necessary in consultation with the class team accompaniment for external therapies and school offers
  • Organizational work (e.g. provision of materials, toys, etc.)
  • Participation in school events
  • Working days Wednesday and Thursday each 4 hours in the morning, Friday 4 hours incl. Afternoon

Profile

  • Joy of working with children
  • Solution-oriented basic attitude in daily work
  • Self-initiative and resilience in a dynamic field of work
  • Flexibility in thinking and acting
  • High level of teamwork
  • Ability to work constructively with other professionals in our school team
  • Appropriate knowledge of German
  • Reliability
  • A good dose of humor

We offer

  • Children and a team that is looking forward to you
  • a supportive school management that is at your side
  • a cooperative cooperation in the school team and with the parents
  • a varied and responsible activity
  • A dedicated and humorous team
  • A multicultural and exciting environment
  • a salary according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich
  • Various additional benefits and benefits

About us

The school district Schwamendingen comprises the entire district 12 of the city of Zurich. Schwamendingen is a lively, multicultural residential quarter. With public transport we are easily accessible via Stettbach station and Oerlikon with various tram and bus lines.
