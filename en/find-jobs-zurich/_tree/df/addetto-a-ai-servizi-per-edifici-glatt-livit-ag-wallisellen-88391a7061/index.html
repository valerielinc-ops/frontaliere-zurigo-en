Infrastructure Building Management Wallisellen | Pensum: 100%

The Center Management Glatt takes care of the most successful shopping center in Switzerland with great passion and on behalf of the owner. With a small, powerful team, we ensure all services in the areas of center management and marketing as well as technical, infrastructural and commercial building management around this property. Our customers are the focus with the goal of raising more than 9 million euros. to offer annual visitors a relaxed, varied and unique shopping experience.

Your tasks

  • You maintain, repair, clean and control our properties
  • You carry out general logistics and disposal work
  • You support in parking cleaning and provide winter service, combined with piketting service
  • You control escape and rescue routes and initiate measures
  • You are the contact for customers, tenants and suppliers for individual topics
  • You perform work for third parties and support with event setups and events

Your profile

  • You have a completed education in the field of home maintenance, company maintenance or a comparable qualification
  • You have a B/BE ticket and ideally a truck check or show willingness to purchase it
  • You communicate in German in word and writing
  • You have no problems working at altitude with div. Lifting equipment
  • You are confident and well-groomed and have pleasant manners
  • You are stressful and ready to take on irregular work assignments (early and late shift / Monday to Saturday / occasionally also at night or on Sundays)
  • You can handle the most common MS Office applications
