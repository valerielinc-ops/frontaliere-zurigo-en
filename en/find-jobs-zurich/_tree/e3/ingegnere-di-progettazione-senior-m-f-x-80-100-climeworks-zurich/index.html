Full description

Become part of our Design Engineering Team as a highly experienced Individual Contributor. You independently solve demanding mechanical design tasks, from concepts and CAD models through manufacturing drawings and prototypes to tested hardware, while simultaneously supporting the broader team in well-founded technical decisions.

As a Senior Design Engineer (m/f/x), you are a hands-on member of our Design Engineering Team and design Climeworks' CO2 Collectors, the centerpiece of our Direct-Air-Capture facility. How high-performing these Collectors are, how they are manufactured, and how they can be scaled is critical to the future success of DAC.

Tasks

  • Mechanical Concept & Product Design: You independently turn new concepts into robust physical products: you develop mechanical concepts, create CAD models, design components and complete collector assemblies, create manufacturing drawings, and support the hardware through manufacturing, assembly, and testing.
  • Technical Problem Solving & Risk Reduction: Your experience helps you find the right solutions faster, identify problems early, and reduce risks before they affect manufacturing or testing.
  • CO₂ Collector Design: You develop mechanical systems from requirements and initial concepts through detailed, manufacturing-ready designs. You develop solutions, perform technical calculations, and make the necessary decisions to develop robust industrial hardware.
  • Engineering Based on First Principles: You use your experience and first-principles engineering thinking to identify risks early, quickly evaluate alternatives, and develop simple, robust solutions. You use calculations, tolerance analyses, design reviews, and tests to reduce uncertainty.
  • Design from Concept to Commercialization: You work directly in SolidWorks to create and adapt parts, assemblies, and system layouts. You manage complex interfaces, space constraints, and design changes so that the overall system works together optimally. You create and maintain high-quality 2D manufacturing drawings, including appropriate GD&T specifications, tolerances, materials, surface finishes, and manufacturing requirements.
  • Prototype Manufacturing, Assembly & Testing: You remain closely involved after completing the CAD work. You support the manufacturing, assembly, and testing of prototypes, investigate problems that arise, and feed the insights gained back into the design.
  • Technical Leadership & Design Reviews: You act as an experienced technical point of contact for the team and support the Design Engineering Team leadership with design reviews, engineering standards, and the release of critical designs. You support the broader team with your technical judgment in complex mechanical design decisions, help maintain high engineering standards, and share your experience through day-to-day collaboration, mentoring, and constructive challenge.
  • Interdisciplinary Engineering Collaboration: You work with Manufacturing, Testing, Process Engineering, Procurement, suppliers, and other engineering disciplines to ensure that mechanical designs function as part of the overall product. You involve external design resources when needed.

Requirements

What sets you apart

Your experience makes your engineering work more effective: you identify promising solutions early, uncover weaknesses before they become costly, and deliver designs that are clear, manufacturing-ready, and ready for real-world use.

  • Experience in Mechanical Design: You bring 15+ years of hands-on experience in mechanical design, ideally in the development of complex industrial equipment from conception through manufacturing and assembly to testing.
  • Sheet Metal Design and Manufacturing: You have substantial practical experience designing sheet metal parts, structures, and assemblies for high-volume manufacturing. You are familiar with bending, welding and joining processes, forming, tolerances, and the selection of materials and sheet thickness, and know how design decisions affect tooling, costs, assembly, and quality.
  • CAD and Drawings: You are a SolidWorks expert and continue to work hands-on in CAD yourself. You personally create high-quality manufacturing drawings and are confident working with GD&T and tolerance stacks.
  • System Design and Technical Judgment: You have experience designing components and mechanisms across subsystems and interfaces through to complex assemblies. You understand how decisions at one level affect the entire Collector, and balance structures, airflow, motion, sealing, process interfaces, manufacturability, reliability, and cost to arrive at robust solutions.
  • Manufacturing and DFMA: You have sound knowledge of machined parts, welded structures, materials, and manufacturing processes, and apply DFMA to develop robust, cost-effective designs.
  • Collaboration and Technical Leadership: You work well with engineers, technicians, manufacturing partners, and suppliers. You share your experience, challenge constructively, and help others make better technical decisions.
  • Education: You have a bachelor's or master's degree in mechanical engineering or a comparable degree.
  • Language: You speak fluent English. This is a must-have criterion, as English is our company language.

This position is not for you if…

  • you primarily want to lead people or projects.
  • you primarily work on simulation, conceptual work, or systems engineering without doing your own CAD work.
  • you have no experience with sheet metal, welded structures, or high-volume manufacturing.
