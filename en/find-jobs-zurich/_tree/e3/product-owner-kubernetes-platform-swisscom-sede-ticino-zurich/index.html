Technical Product Owner - Kubernetes Platform

Your future starts here You are responsible for the Kubernetes platform and the services offered on it throughout the entire lifecycle. Together with the team and Product Management, you define the vision and roadmap, prioritize the Product Backlog, and drive the implementation. You do this using clear processes and agile methods. In this way, you continuously develop the platform using DevOps practices and ensure stability, security, and efficiency. Highlights of the position

  • Responsibility for the platform throughout the entire lifecycle from vision to operation and optimization
  • Combination of Product Ownership and active participation in implementation
  • Close coordination with internal stakeholders, the platform's clientele, and other Product Owners
  • Use of modern methods and automation in the CNCF environment, e.g., ArgoCD, Prometheus, Cilium
  • Impact through scope for design and collaboration in a dedicated team

Your Skills – Technical

  • Degree (University/FH/HF) in Computer Science or Business Administration or equivalent degree
  • Experience as a Product Owner, Service Owner, or in a comparable role in a technical environment
  • Practical experience in operating and further developing container-based platforms, ideally with Kubernetes
  • Ability to specifically bundle technical and functional topics and implement them with priority

Your Skills – Personal and Languages

  • Communicative, team-oriented, and reliable, enjoyment of exchange and collaboration
  • Structured, self-reliant working style and initiative
  • You provide on-call duty and reside in Switzerland
  • Very good German and English; other national languages are an advantage

With us, you have the opportunity to work in the office in Zurich and up to three days a week from home. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As an employee of Swisscom, you can look forward to a variety of attractive benefits that will enrich your work experience. These include a pleasant working environment, financial advantages, and exciting opportunities for professional development. Benefits.
