Full description

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a dedicated individual who will help shape our internal Value Assurance process for complex national and international major projects, both operationally and strategically. Value Assurance is a central process at Implenia and thus plays a key role in achieving the group's goal of profitable growth, making it part of corporate risk management. By improving the quality of bids and supporting project execution, revenue is increased and the risk profile is reduced.

Your preferred work location is our headquarters in the Ambassador House in Opfikon, Switzerland. However, other Implenia locations are also possible as work locations. The option to work partially from home is available.

Responsibilities

What tasks can you expect as Senior Project Risk & Value Manager (m/f/d), Opfikon, 80-100%?

  • In this role, you manage the internal approval process for complex major projects and support bidding teams in preparing their proposals.
  • With a focus on efficiency, quality, and impact, you optimize processes – including by reducing errors, increasing hit rates, and improving customer feedback.
  • You conduct independent risk assessments (Second Opinion) and actively contribute to the further development of proposals together with the bidding teams.
  • In addition, you support project teams throughout the entire lifecycle – occasionally on-site – in the successful implementation of complex projects and conduct regular intermediate reviews (Intermediate Reviews / Audits) to specifically accompany relevant projects during the execution phase. In doing so, you report directly to management.
  • You contribute your knowledge to internal development projects, foster exchange between different business areas, and further develop the Value Assurance process as well as digital tools (e.g., Data Analytics, AI), thereby contributing to the continuous improvement of bidding and execution quality.
  • As part of project visits, you support international project teams through knowledge transfer and an independent perspective, thus establishing the connection between the project and management.

Requirements

What qualifications do you bring as Senior Project Risk & Value Manager (m/f/d), Opfikon, 80-100%?

  • You have a completed degree in civil engineering and bring at least 10 years of professional experience in the conception and execution of large and complex projects, ideally also at an international level.
  • Furthermore, you possess a strong understanding of complex contractual relationships as well as commercial contexts and/or project controlling. Experience in risk management is an advantage.
  • You have a basic understanding of Operational Excellence and an interest in topics such as Lean Management and the further development of management systems. Your expertise in applying modern Risk Management methods particularly distinguishes you.
  • In addition, you bring experience in supporting and developing digital applications, for example in the areas of Data Analytics, Artificial Intelligence, or Enhanced Scheduling, as well as in optimizing digital process flows.
  • Your excellent communication skills, strong team orientation, and process-oriented working style distinguish you. You are driven by the desire to network your knowledge with the know-how of others in dialogue with colleagues from various disciplines.
  • Your profile is rounded off by very good French skills.
  • In addition, you need either German or English.
