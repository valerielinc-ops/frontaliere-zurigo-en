Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 70-100% For our ambulatory in the Quadro we are looking for an immediately or by agreement Their tasks

  • Disposition: appointment agreement, waiting list management and dispatch of appointment bids
  • Patient administration and data maintenance: case openings/closures, processing of patient and insurance data, review and management of admission documents and cost language
  • Secretarial work, such as correspondence, administration of office materials
  • Documentation and archiving: maintenance of digital patient documentation, digitization of documents, management of electronic and physical files
  • Front desk services: reception of patients and visitors and reception and verification of patient and insurance data
  • Team and office organisation: representation in the team, training of new employees, updating of work documents, room and equipment management

  • Flowing knowledge of German in word and writing is required. Further language skills are advantageous
