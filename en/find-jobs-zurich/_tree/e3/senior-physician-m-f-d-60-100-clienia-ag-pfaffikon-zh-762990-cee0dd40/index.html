For our center for child, youth and family in Frauenfeld we are looking for a

We are a large child and adolescent psychiatric care clinic with 64 fully stationary places at the Littenheid site and several outpatient locations. Their tasks You are working in a senior position at the ZKJF Frauenfeld. They actively accompany internal hospital processes such as lean management and are interested in leadership, a good exchange with local institutions and the implementation of new therapeutic offers. Your profile They come from Switzerland or are from the EU/EFTA area and enjoy working with children, young people and their families. You are a specialist in child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy, have good integration skills and enjoy working in an interdisciplinary team.

Our offer You will find a varied and responsible job in a committed, multi-professional team. Our clinic has staff rooms and is located in a charming landscape between Lake Constance and the Swiss Pre-Alpines. We are easily accessible from the larger cities of Zurich, Winterthur, St. Gallen and Constance.
