Full description

For our school facilities Freilager and Utogrund in the Zurich-Letzi school district we are looking for 1. December 2026 a motivated, reliable and committed personality as a cleaning employee with a workload of 80%.

The position is divided into two school facilities (50 % Freilager and 30 % Utogrund). You are part of our domestic service teams and contribute significantly to a clean, well-kept and pleasant school environment.

Working hours and holiday cleaning

During school weeks

The work assignments take place in the free camp: Monday to Friday approx. 18:30 - 22:30 In Utogrund: Monday, Wednesday and Friday from 13:30 - 17:30.

During school holidays

The intermediate and basic cleaning usually takes place during the school holiday week. The missions take place during the day, usually between 07.30 and 17.00 clock.

  • Independent and professional cleaning of school, teaching and support rooms at both locations.
  • Implementation of intermediate and basic cleaning during school holidays according to cleaning planning.
  • Ensuring cleaning quality and careful and professional handling of cleaning products, machinery and equipment.
  • Perform final checks in the assigned work area (windows, doors, lighting, water, stores and closing checks) and report damage and hazards.
  • Supporting the domestic service teams in further work and taking over deputies after consultation.
  • Several years of experience in professional cleaning, preferably in school or public buildings, is an advantage.
  • Good knowledge of German for understanding everyday work and for understanding work instructions.
  • A reliable, careful, independent and quality-conscious way of working.
  • Teamwork, flexibility and the willingness to work at two school locations and to take on deputies if necessary.
  • Joy of cleaning work, a friendly appearance, a positive attitude and the physical resilience for the activities involved.

A varied and responsible work at two school locations and a progressive salary and attractive social benefits according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich.

The Zurich-Letzi school district comprises Stadtkreis 9 with the districts Albisrieden, Altstetten and Grünau. In the 16 schools of our rapidly growing school circle, more than 5,000 primary school students are taught and supervised. More than 1,200 employees work for them in our schools in the areas of teaching, care, home service and technology as well as administration. They all work daily for the interests of the students and a smooth school day.
