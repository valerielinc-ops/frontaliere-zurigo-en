Full description

Eraneos is an international strategy, transformation and technology advisory group that helps companies thrive in a dynamic digital world. With first-class experts from business and technology, we empower our customers to set new standards for sustainable transformations – from strategy to implementation. Whether in the design of sustainable organizations, the development of new potential through data and AI or the protection of companies through holistic cybersecurity: We deliver results pragmatically and purposefully. Our team of around 1,300 dedicated employees is represented in offices in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, Portugal, Denmark, Sweden, Austria, the UK, Singapore and the USA and combines global perspectives with strong local roots. Around 450 employees work at the Swiss locations in Zurich, Bern and Lausanne.

This is the job

  • Coordination of interviews and assessments between candidates, hiring managers and partners – even if calendars are full and priorities shift in the short term
  • Reception and support of the candidates: on site so that they feel well looked after at Eraneos from the beginning
  • Clean data and smooth processes in Workday: Record applications, maintain status, send invitations and rejections and prepare reports
  • Support in the organization of career fairs, university events and internal recruiting events as well as participation in job advertisements and LinkedIn contributions
  • Support for the recruiting team in day-to-day business – with their own ideas on how to streamline and accelerate processes

This job is for you if

  • You have a commercial education, a degree program, an HR assistant training or a comparable education.
  • Your first experience in HR, recruiting or an assistant or have gathered coordination function, like in a consultation or agency.
  • Service for you is a matter of course: you answer inquiries quickly, keep track of many parallel appointments and stay friendly when things get hectic.
  • You work carefully and deal discreetly with personal data.
  • You stylishly speak German and good English (min. B2) and write.

As an i-top you bring with you

  • Experience with Workday or another applicant management system
  • Experience in a consulting or agency environment
