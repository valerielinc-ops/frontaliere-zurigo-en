Student Assistant: Qualitative Data Coding and Analysis

20%-30%, Zurich, fixed-term

The Professorship for Learning Sciences and Higher Education is seeking a motivated Student Assistant starting in January 2027. The position involves the systematic analysis of video and audio recordings from a learning experiment. Using an established qualitative coding scheme, you will identify and categorize learners' gestures and verbal explanations of how their gestures represent word meanings. Your work will involve behavioral observation, qualitative data annotation, and structured data extraction.

Depending on your interests, there may also be opportunities to contribute to further data analysis or related research projects in the future.

Our research group is dedicated to investigating human cognition and learning processes. We conduct theory-driven research to better understand how learning environments can be designed to support effective learning.

Project background

We are conducting research on embodied learning, exploring how bodily actions and interactions contribute to learning and cognitive processes. Our research aims to better understand learners' experiences and behaviors across different learning environments. You will work closely with a doctoral researcher and receive training in the relevant research methods.

Job description

  • Apply an established qualitative coding scheme to annotate gestures and verbal explanations in video and audio recordings.
  • Document ambiguous cases and discuss coding discrepancies with the research team.
  • Support inter-rater reliability checks and maintain accurate, confidential research records.

Profile

  • You are enrolled as a student at a university in Zurich, preferably in psychology, linguistics, education, or a related field.
  • You are fluent in English.
  • You have strong attention to detail and a responsible, reliable approach to work.
  • You are available to work approximately 10 -15 hours per week.
  • Previous experience with qualitative research, behavioral observation, video annotation, or data analysis is an advantage.

We offer

  • A workload of up to 15 hours per week
  • Insights into educational research involving VR design and user experience
  • The opportunity to collaborate with an interdisciplinary team and in a friendly and supportive working environment.
