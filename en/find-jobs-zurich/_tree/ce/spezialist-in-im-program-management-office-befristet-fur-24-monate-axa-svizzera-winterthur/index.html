Shape your future – together with us!

Do you also keep track of complex programs?

Do you love to create structure, coordinate topics and smoothly promote collaboration between teams and partners?

If yes, then you are right with us!

For our strategic program for the redesign of claims regulation, we are looking for you as a committed PMO specialist. We offer a dynamic environment in which your organizational talent is in demand and team collaboration comes first. Help us make the future of claims management possible by integrating the new system with our internal and external partners and replacing our legacy system. You can expect a dynamic role with a lot of personal responsibility and direct influence – in which your organizational talent and your proactive way of working make the difference.

In the area of customer operations, we design the individual experiences of our customers in the event of damage and performance. They are our focus and together with them we find the right solution. In this way, our customer-first mentality ensures maximum customer satisfaction.

This is what awaits you

  • As a PMO, you are a central point of contact for organizational and coordination issues in the program. You create transparency about the program progress, risks and dependencies.
  • You are the communicative hub and support a smooth cooperation between our internal teams, the external partner and all stakeholders.
  • Together with the program team, you ensure that our program methods and standards are lived and provide the basis for decision-making for management.
  • You coordinate central risk and issue management, follow agreed measures and escalate as needed.
  • You will organize and moderate central meetings of the program and ensure clear documentation and follow-up of the results.
  • You actively support program communication and help ensure that the participants receive relevant information in a timely manner.

You bring that with you

  • You bring experience in project or program management, ideally you have already successfully acted in a PMO role.
  • You are an organizational talent with a highly structured, proactive and solution-oriented way of working. You think ahead and recognize the need for action early on.
  • You excel in your teamwork, assertiveness and flexibility. In a dynamic environment, you keep a cool head even under high loads and openly approach new challenges. Your own initiative and a positive attitude towards change are a matter of course for you.
  • You are strong in communication, willing to make decisions and act confidently as an interface between different people, disciplines and cultures.
  • Together with the external partner and internal teams, you are ready to find pragmatic solutions to challenges.
  • The safe handling of MS Office (especially Excel, PowerPoint) and PM tools such as Jira or Confluence is a matter of course for you.
  • Fluent German and English skills in word and writing round off your profile.
