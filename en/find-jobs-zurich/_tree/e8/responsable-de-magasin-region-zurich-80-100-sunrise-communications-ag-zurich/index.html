Full description

At Sunrise, we think bigger, go further and create new things. For us, working culture means creating great things together. Here respect and fresh ideas meet real teamwork – every voice counts, every perspective makes us stronger. Our passion drives us to dare new things and to constantly grow. Sounds like you? Then become part of our success story.

Our front-line heroes are the flagship of Sunrise. You score with customized solutions and top service - so every customer contact becomes a long-term connection.

Your challenge

  • You take responsibility for the entire shop – from team leadership to increased sales.
  • You coach and develop your team, recognize talents and promote them specifically.
  • The inviting appearance of your shop is personally important to you.
  • You have the most important figures in mind: sales, stock management, inventory and cash registers you have fully under control.
  • You are not only a team leader, but also a salesperson from body and soul and act as an example with your positive mindset.
  • Customer loyalty is not a foreign word for you – you provide top customer experiences and long-term relationships.
  • You implement the strategy of the region at the forefront and turn your shop into a real success story.

Your kills

  • You have completed vocational training in the retail trade, preferably with further training in sales or management.
  • You are a sales professional and have several years of experience in motivating and developing a team.
  • You know the advantages and characteristics of a dynamic industry and ideally bring knowledge from the telecommunications industry.
  • You want to control your own success: Our variable share of wages motivates you and your shop to achieve maximum performance.
  • Service and customer focus are a matter of course for you, which is why you also promote the service orientation of your employees.
  • You bring fluent German skills, every other language is a plus.

At Sunrise, you work in a dynamic and multinational environment where every voice is heard, perspectives are shared and values are lived. Diversity is not just a program for us, but part of our DNA. Because we celebrate our differences, we achieve great things together.

Sunrise is a partner of Advance Gender Equality in Business, the Swiss LGBTI label and certified with Fair-ON-Pay Advanced for equal pay for work of equal value for all.

Are you ready for the challenge? Then become part of the team and actively shape our future!
