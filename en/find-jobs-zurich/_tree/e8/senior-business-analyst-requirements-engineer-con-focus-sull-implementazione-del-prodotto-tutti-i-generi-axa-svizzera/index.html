80 - 100%, Winterthur / Smart Working

Shape your future – together with us!

The private provision of AXA Switzerland is growing strongly. With our diverse and advanced pension and investment products, we create freedom and security for our customers so that they can live their lives carefree. We support our customers with attractive pension solutions and digital services – from the idea to implementation in our systems.

For our team in Winterthur, which is actively involved in product design and is responsible for mapping and managing new and existing pension products in our systems, we are looking for a:n Business Analyst:in / Requirements Engineer with pleasure in life insurance products.

Are you the technical conscience that permeates complex products and processes and builds the bridge between specialist ideas and IT implementation? Do you want to help shape the future of private provision at AXA?

Then we definitely want to get to know you!

This is what awaits you

  • You are involved in the development of new preventive products right from the start and check the technical feasibility of the concepts
  • You take technical requirements in a structured way, analyze them and create precise specifications for IT implementation
  • As a "Translator:in" between the worlds, you act as the central interface between product management, actuary and our IT developers and ensure that everyone speaks the same language.
  • Together with your team and various stakeholders, you will develop and evaluate solution variants in the areas of product implementation, contract management and contract processes.
  • With your SQL knowledge, you get to the bottom of the data to understand processes, perform analyses and identify sources of error.

You bring that with you

  • You bring several years of experience as a Business Analyst / Requirements Engineer (3-5 years) or in a similar role at the interface between Business and IT, ideally in a regulated environment
  • You use SQL routinely for data analysis, clarifications in the context of requirements and validation of system behavior.
  • Ideally, you already have know-how in the field of private provision (3rd pillar) or are very interested in getting involved here
  • Your affinity for mathematical relationships helps you understand the logic behind insurance products. Knowledge of actuarial is an advantage
  • You enjoy delving into complex issues and don’t give up until you find a clean and viable solution
  • You enjoy working in a team, can inspire different stakeholders and are interested in the people and perspectives in your environment
  • You communicate fluently in German
