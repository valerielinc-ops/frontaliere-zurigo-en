The Hohenegg Private Clinic is a special clinic with 95 beds for psychiatry, psychotherapy and psychosomatics. It is open to the public and is accessible to private, semi-private and general insured persons from Switzerland as well as self-payers. It is housed in a magnificent listed complex, in the middle of nature and high above Lake Zurich. In addition to the inpatient services, we operate a Centre for Mental Health (ZPG) for outpatient and hospital-associated psychiatry at two locations. The sponsorship is a non-profit foundation.
