Institute of Chemistry

Job opening at 1. December 2026 or by agreement

The Institute of Chemistry of the University of Zurich consists of more than 20 research groups and comprises about 300 employees and students who conduct intensive research and teaching in the fields of life sciences, energy research and other chemical fields. For administrative support of a well-established and motivated team in the field of teaching, we are looking for you!

Tasks

  • Management of assistants for the courses: supporting the manager in the allocation of assistants, information to all participants, calculation of hours worked, management and maintenance of the corresponding lists of teaching activities of the assistants
  • Support to the Head of Internships: Support to the Head of Internships in recruiting the tutors, managing and maintaining the GPC mail address, answering requests from the assistants, managing and maintaining databases
  • Support of the department manager teaching: semester planning, ECTS administration (grade inputs etc.) and confirmations, room bookings in SAP-CM, room reservations internally and externally, processing and dispatch of disadvantage compensation, annual support in the qualification interviews teaching for all major and minor subjects
  • E-Learning: OLAT support for the lecture modules of the IfC, support for the lecturers in the technical and organizational implementation of digital learning and teaching offers in the OLAT LMS, ordering and processing of podcasts and video transmissions for lectures

Profile

  • Commercial apprenticeship (EFZ, E-profile)
  • several years of professional experience as a commercial:r clerk:in (experience in the university environment is an advantage)
  • Knowledge of learning platforms and web interfaces of advantage (e.g. OLAT LMS)
  • Knowledge of image editing, graphic design and video editing is advantageous
  • Basic knowledge of HTML/PHP and MySQL databases
  • German as mother tongue and English fluent in word and writing (Advanced Level)
  • Very good IT user knowledge
  • good comprehension and a very independent, efficient and reliable working method
  • High level of teamwork, service orientation, initiative and flexibility
