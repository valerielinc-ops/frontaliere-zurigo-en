Your Team

As an Executive Assistant, you play a key role in enabling our Partners to focus on what matters most. You are a trusted point of contact for internal and external stakeholders and ensure that day-to-day activities run smoothly and efficiently. With a proactive mindset and strong organizational skills, you manage priorities, coordinate meetings and travel, support communication, and contribute to the successful delivery of events and other team initiatives. Your Impact

  • Providing proactive administrative and organizational support to our Partners and act as a trusted point of contact for internal and external stakeholders.
  • Managing complex calendars and inboxes, coordinate priorities, and draft, review and edit correspondence on behalf of Partners.
  • Preparing high-quality PowerPoint presentations and other business documents and coordinate business travel and related expenses.
  • You proactively coordinate internal and external meetings, including preparation, meeting documentation, and follow-up on actions.
  • You plan and coordinate conferences and events with up to 200 participants, ensuring a smooth experience while keeping track of timelines, logistics and budgets.
  • Confidently manage multiple priorities and requests in a dynamic environment, with a strong focus on quality, efficiency, and attention to detail.
  • You embrace digital tools and new ways of working and continuously develop your technical expertise and interpersonal skills through on- and off-the-job learning.

Your Skill Set

  • You have completed a commercial apprenticeship or equivalent qualification and bring at least five years of experience in an Assistant role within a dynamic professional environment.
  • Fluent in German and English, both written and spoken.
  • You are highly proficient in Microsoft Office, particularly Outlook, Word, Excel and PowerPoint, and are confident adopting new digital tools.
  • You are a strong communicator with excellent organizational skills and a service- and solution-oriented mindset.
  • Working independently, take ownership, and enjoy collaborating as part of a team.
  • You stay calm and focused in a fast-paced environment and know how to prioritize effectively when requirements change.
  • You handle sensitive and confidential information with the highest level of discretion and professionalism.
  • Communicate confidently and professionally and know how to build strong relationships across different levels of the organization.
