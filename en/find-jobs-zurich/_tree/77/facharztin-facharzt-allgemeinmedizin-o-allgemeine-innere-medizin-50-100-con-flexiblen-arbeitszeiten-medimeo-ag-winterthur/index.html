Are you looking for a family doctor position where work adapts to your life and not vice versa? In a modernly equipped practice in the garden city of Winterthur, you can expect flexible working hours, at least five weeks of vacation and a mentor for your entry. The Center for Family Medicine covers the entire spectrum of general internal medicine, from acute complaints about chronic diseases to prevention. They work with another medical colleague and an MPA team including learners. A practice coordinator takes over the administration, the accounting is outsourced. Your time stays with the patient: inside. For diagnostics, ultrasound, digital X-ray, ECG and long-term ECG, 24-hour blood pressure measurement, lung function and an own practice laboratory are available. A special plus: The senior doctor works as a tutor, so you can purchase your skills in sonography directly here. Many in the team have been working together for years. As a recognized teaching company for doctors: the practice attaches importance to regular further education. Due to a change and the continued high demand, the team is now being supplemented. Winterthur is the sixth largest city in Switzerland and still does not feel like a big city: Around 40% of the city area is forest, so it is rarely far into the green. The heart of the city is the car-free old town with shops, markets and cafes. The city of Zurich and the international airport can be reached in 20 minutes, and the S-Bahn runs directly to the German border. Families find daycare centers with urbanly subsidized places and urbanly organized care for school children. In addition, there is a rich cultural and museum offering with a total of 17 museums, including the Swiss Science Center Technorama, one of the largest science centers in Europe, the Nature Museum with children's museum and the Bruderhaus wildlife park.
