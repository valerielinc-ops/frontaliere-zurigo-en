Shape your future – together with us!

In our Strategy & Transformation Team, the distribution strategy of AXA CH is developed and concepts are developed and implemented together with the product factory, the technology teams and the various sales channels. Our goal is to create the best customer experience – true to our motto: «We create unique AXA moments». We do not produce paper tigers, no, our ambition is the lion’s share – in the market, today and tomorrow. This is what we are working on: the future of distribution and AXA.

We are looking for a:n motivated:n Distribution Strategy Transformation Specialist to support our super team!

Supported as part of our dynamic and highly motivated team You are the initiative leader in the current implementation of our distribution strategy.

You help us, together with the initiative and product teams, to align the sales of AXA with the future.

Become a part of our distribution. Our area is the window to our customers. Every day, we are committed to meeting customer needs in the best possible way through proactive, progressive and personal consulting solutions – in line with our most important corporate value, “customer first”.

This is what awaits you

  • You understand our business resp. Sales model and our processes and can build bridges between clients, users and the development team
  • You understand the resulting needs and can transform them into actionable requirements
  • You have an agile mindset and work with agile methods
  • In the hectic environment, you retain both nerves and overview and take responsibility for the clarity and correctness of the professional requirements
  • You know when detail and precision are required, and you can deliver them, while always keeping the Big Picture in mind
  • You have a high level of personal responsibility and enjoy driving forward topics with commitment

You bring that with you

  • Successfully completed higher vocational training and / or university of applied sciences in computer science, business informatics, business administration or mathematics
  • Several years of experience in business analysis, project management or requirements engineering or in a comparable function
  • First experience in the agile environment (Scrum / Kanban) desirable
  • Knowledge in robotics, data analytics, process mining, AI or comparable fields desirable
  • You are strong in communication, committed, self-initiated, strong in implementation and solution-oriented
  • Even under stress, you act thoughtfully and stay focused
