At Julius Baer, we celebrate and value the individual qualities you bring, enabling you to be impactful, to be entrepreneurial, to be empowered, and to create value beyond wealth. Let’s shape the future of wealth management together.

We are seeking a highly experienced Front Arena Developer & Platform Specialist to drive the development and support of our trading systems. If you bring eight-plus years of Front Arena expertise in Python, AEL, and ACM, along with a passion for solving complex problems on the trade floor, we want to hear from you.

YOUR CHALLENGE

  • Provide first-class Front User support (Trading, Midoffice, Risk Office)
  • Work on complex problems, experiment with new ideas and continuously learn and improve

YOUR PROFILE

  • Extensive expertise and deep experience in the maintenance and configuration of Front Arena landscapes (must have)
  • Proven proficiency in performance and stability tuning across Front Arena environments (ADS, APS, AMBA, XMBA, TAB, APH, TAPS, MS-SQL) (must have)
  • 8+ years of experience as Front Arena developer in Python, AEL, ACM. ADFL is a plus
  • Competence in structured products issuance, trading and life-cycling
  • Experience working directly with traders on a trade floor
  • Background in Science, Technology, or Engineering
  • Solid and clear communicator with the ability to prioritise effectively and work under pressure
  • Empathetic person with great customer service skills
  • Strong analytical skills and an enthusiastic teamplayer with a hands-on, can-do attitude
  • Fluency in English (verbal/written)
