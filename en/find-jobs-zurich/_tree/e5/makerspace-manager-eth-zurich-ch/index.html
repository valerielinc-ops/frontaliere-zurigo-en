Makerspace Manager: Hochschul-Innovationswerkstatt

80%-100%, Zurich, fixed-term

We inspire and empower students to cultivate a maker and innovator mindset. Through hands-on projects, students gain valuable experience and develop skills needed to tackle future challenges. Our interdisciplinary team supports students from all disciplines in exploring ideas, building prototypes, and bringing projects to life. We provide inspiring facilities, makerspaces, and equipment, along with expert support.

For a new initiative, we’re now looking for a Makerspace Manager to join our team.

Project background

You will help establish and operate "MAKERY - The Makerspace for Education" .

This initiative strengthens hands-on, project-based learning in courses across ETH Zurich by connecting technical infrastructure, educational expertise, and makerspace resources across the university. As a Makerspace Manager, you will play a key role in building the first central hub of the MAKERY ecosystem and shaping how students and teaching staff access facilities, tools, and support for prototyping and making.

You will be based at the new makerspace hub on the ETH Zentrum campus while also contributing to makerspace activities and collaborations across ETH Zurich.

  • We are establishing a new makerspace hub on the ETH Zentrum campus as part of a growing ETH-wide ecosystem.
  • We work closely with students, lecturers, and technical staff from diverse disciplines.
  • We support project-based learning activities throughout the academic year.
  • We take an agile and collaborative approach to developing and improving our services.
  • You will help shape a new ETH-wide initiative with lasting impact on teaching and learning.

Job description

Together, we support lecturers, students and project teams in turning ideas into reality through hands-on making and prototyping. This is an early-career position, ideally suited for candidates with around 1-2 years of professional experience.As a Makerspace Manager, you will be responsible for the following:

  • Operate: You'll operate, maintain, and improve makerspace infrastructure, ensuring equipment, tools, and facilities are safe, reliable and accessible. You'll also support inventory management, material replenishment, and the day-to-day operation of the Makerspace shop.
  • Train: You'll train and certify users, conduct safety introductions and help students and staff develop practical skills.
  • Support: You'll support practical teaching activities, project-based courses, student teams, as well as Bachelor's and Master's thesis projects. You'll help users overcome technical challenges and contribute to the development, assembly and testing of prototypes, mechatronic systems, and experimental setups.
  • Supervise: You'll supervise users in the safe operation of makerspace equipment and help ensure compliance with safety procedures and standards.
  • Build: You'll help establish and continuously develop MAKERY, ensuring smooth and efficient processes, effective use of resources, and the development of new service offerings.

Within the team, we work collaboratively and jointly create an inspiring environment for hands-on learning and innovation.

Profile

We need your energy and expertise to help build and operate MAKERY:

  • You have completed vocational training (EFZ), a Bachelor's degree (from a University of Applied Sciences, ETH Zurich, or an equivalent institution), or another relevant qualification in a technical field.
  • You enjoy working hands-on and have experience building, testing, or operating technical systems. You bring practical skills in areas such as digital fabrication, prototyping, electronics, mechatronics, embedded systems and/or CAD.
  • You enjoy solving technical challenges, working across different technologies and turning ideas into reality.
  • You care about creating a safe, accessible and inspiring makerspace environment where students and staff can build, experiment, and learn.
  • You enjoy supporting people and sharing your knowledge through technical training, user support and hands-on guidance.
  • You have practical experience in a workshop, laboratory, makerspace or engineering environment.
  • You can communicate well in German and English, both at B2 level or higher.

A note: research indicates that women and individuals from marginalized groups tend to apply only when they meet all of the criteria. If you believe you are qualified but do not necessarily meet every criterion, we encourage you to apply.
