Full description

At Julius Baer, we celebrate and value the individual qualities you bring, enabling you to be impactful, to be entrepreneurial, to be empowered, and to create value beyond wealth. Let’s shape the future of wealth management together.

Responsible for ensuring that HR policies, processes and compliance standards are consistently upheld across the organization.

YOUR CHALLENGE

  • You manage and continuously enhance the HR governance framework, including policies, standards, procedures, and controls.
  • You monitor regulatory developments and market practices to ensure HR governance frameworks remain effective and fit for purpose.
  • You oversee policy breach, disciplinary, and consequence management processes, ensuring effective governance, stakeholder coordination, and timely resolution.
  • You partner with HR, Legal, Compliance, Risk, and business stakeholders to ensure regulatory adherence and sound governance outcomes.
  • You coordinate governance meetings, committees, and stakeholder engagements, ensuring timely execution of actions and decisions.
  • You produce executive-quality reporting, analytics, presentations, and decision-support materials for senior leadership and governance forums.
  • You drive excellence, standardisation, and continuous improvement across core HR processes.
  • You support global HR projects and strategic initiatives, contributing governance expertise and implementation oversight.

YOUR PROFILE Personal & Social

  • You are passionate about governance, policy management, and process excellence.
  • You demonstrate strong analytical, conceptual, and problem-solving skills, with an ability to translate complex topics into practical and business-oriented solutions.
  • You possess a structured and independent working style and effectively manage multiple priorities in a dynamic environment.
  • You build trusted relationships and collaborate effectively across functions, seniority levels, and geographies.
  • You communicate clearly and confidently and produce high-quality presentations, reports, and executive materials.
  • You are proactive, solution-oriented, and take ownership for delivering outcomes.
  • You maintain the highest standards of integrity, discretion, and professionalism when handling confidential information.
  • You are a collaborative team player with strong organisational skills and eager to grow

Professional & Technical

  • Bachelor’s degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, Economics, Law, or a related discipline; further education or professional certifications in HR Management, Governance, Risk, or Compliance are advantageous.
  • 3-5 years of professional experience in Human Resources, HR Governance, Legal, Compliance, Risk Management, Business Management, or a related field
  • Solid understanding of HR principles, employment practices, corporate governance, and regulatory requirements.
  • Experience in preparing, analysing, and presenting data, reports, and management information to support effective decision-making.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, particularly Excel and PowerPoint, with the ability to develop high-quality reports, presentations, and executive materials.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills in German and English; additional language skills are an advantage.
  • High degree of integrity, reliability, and professionalism.
