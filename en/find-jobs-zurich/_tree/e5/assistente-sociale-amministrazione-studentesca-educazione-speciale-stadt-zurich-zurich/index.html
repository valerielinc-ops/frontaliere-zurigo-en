Full description

The large student growth and the great heterogeneity are one of the many challenges for our schools. The integration of students with special needs is a concern of parents and society. As a special education specialist, you support the organizational implementation of the integration of children with special education needs in the schools of the Letzi school district. We are looking for a person to complement our team as of February 1, 2027 or by appointment.

  • They shall draw up the special training orders ready for signature in their own competence.
  • They are responsible for monitoring and controlling the resources and costs in the area of special training within the total costs for the management.
  • They organize taxi services/subscriptions for the transport of children and make the appropriate decisions.
  • They write demanding correspondence and take over the protocol management.
  • They keep minutes of parental conversations.
  • Completed commercial training (EFZ), good IT/computer user knowledge.
  • Professional experience, preferably in a public administration and ideally in-depth knowledge of primary education
  • Interest in networking and relationship maintenance with the participating specialist bodies.
  • High degree of independence, care, sense of duty and timeliness (observance of deadlines)
  • Team-oriented, pragmatic and performance-ready personality

You can expect a committed team, a versatile and challenging job in which responsible and independent work is a matter of course, in a collegial and dynamic working atmosphere. Your workplace is located near the Altstetten station.

The Zurich-Letzi school district comprises Stadtkreis 9 with the districts Albisrieden, Altstetten and Grünau. Around 5000 students are taught and supervised in the 16 schools in our rapidly growing school circle. More than 900 employees work for them in the schools.
