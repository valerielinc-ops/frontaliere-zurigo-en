About the role

We are looking to hire a Director of NPI & Lifecycle Governance to join our Global Supply Chain organization.

Reporting to the VP of Supply Chain, this is a global, highly cross-functional role responsible for the end-to-end governance of New Product Introduction (NPI) and product lifecycle transitions, from launch readiness through ramp-up and phase-out. The role connects Operations and Supply Chain with R&D, Regulatory, Quality, Commercial and Finance to ensure that product launches are operationally ready and that risks, dependencies and decisions are addressed at the right time within the Operations team.

This is also an opportunity to help shape and further develop how we manage NPI and lifecycle governance within Operations and Supply Chain globally. The organization and operating model continue to evolve, so we are looking for someone who is comfortable building and improving processes while operating in a dynamic environment.

The role is based in our HQ in Stäfa, Switzerland and requires international collaboration across functions, regions and manufacturing sites (30 % travel expected).

Key responsibilities • Own and continuously improve the NPI phase-gate and launch-readiness framework from an Operations and Supply Chain perspective, including readiness criteria, key milestones and decision points.

  • Ensure operational readiness across manufacturing, suppliers, materials, capacity, demand and supply planning, logistics, service, master data and market deployment.

• Coordinate and challenge cross-functional launch plans, identifying key risks and dependencies and driving timely resolution and decision-making.

  • Drive effective lifecycle transitions, including phase-in and phase-out planning, cannibalisation assumptions, last-time buys, inventory run-down, obsolescence and service/spare-parts considerations.
  • Use data and analysis to challenge assumptions, highlight inconsistencies and provide clear recommendations to senior decision-makers.

• Own and continuously improve the launch scorecard and portfolio readiness view, providing transparency on readiness, critical risks, actions and outstanding decisions across the NPI portfolio. • Align global functions, regions, manufacturing sites and markets around common launch assumptions, priorities and critical dependencies.

  • Drive continuous improvement through post-launch reviews, lessons learned and stronger, more consistent ways of working across the organization.

About you

To succeed in this role, you combine deep expertise in NPI, launch readiness and product lifecycle management with a strong understanding of Operations, Manufacturing and Supply Chain. You bring a structured, analytical and business-oriented mindset, with the ability to challenge assumptions and turn complex operational topics into clear priorities and decisions.

You are comfortable leading through influence in a global, cross-functional environment, creating alignment and accountability across multiple functions without relying on direct authority. You bring strong executive presence while being equally comfortable engaging with operational teams, adapting your communication and translating complex topics to different levels of the organization.

You demonstrate strong ownership and sound judgement and are comfortable building and improving governance in an evolving environment, while keeping active launches and lifecycle transitions moving forward.

Ideally, you bring

  • 8+ years of relevant experience, including direct experience in NPI and complex product launches, combined with exposure to product lifecycle management, Manufacturing, Supply Chain or Operations.
  • Proven experience leading or governing complex product launches and working across multiple functions, regions and/or manufacturing sites.
  • Strong knowledge of phase-gate governance and launch readiness, with an understanding of manufacturing and supplier readiness, demand/supply/capacity planning, lifecycle transitions and inventory risk.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities, with the ability to use data to challenge assumptions, assess risks and support fact-based decision-making.
  • A hands-on, pragmatic approach and the ability to build and improve governance, processes and ways of working in an evolving environment.
  • Strong stakeholder management and influencing skills, with demonstrated ability to create alignment and accountability.
  • Experience operating in a global, matrix organization; experience in medical devices, life sciences or another regulated and complex product environment is an advantage.
  • A university degree in Supply Chain, Engineering, Business Administration, Operations Management or a related field. PMP, PRINCE2, Lean Six Sigma or APICS/ASCM certification is an advantage.
  • Fluent in English. German or additional languages are an advantage.
