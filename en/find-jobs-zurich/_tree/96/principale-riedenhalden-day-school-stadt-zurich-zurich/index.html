About us

We are a large school community in Zurich-Nord with 19 schools. We derive our pedagogical attitude from the concept of New Authority and thus meet the daily challenges in everyday school life together and effectively.

The Riedenhalden School is a lively day school in the middle of Zurich-Affoltern - just a few steps from the S-Bahn. With us, around 10 secondary classes, 10 primaries, 3 kindergartens and 5 support locations learn with each other and from each other. As a long-term QUIMS school, we live diversity not only on paper.
