Responsibilities

  • Lead systems-level safety science and classifier evaluation, anchoring the Data Science team in Zurich as a research leader and manager.
  • Pioneer a systems-thinking approach to platform safety, moving the organization beyond tuning classifiers in isolation to modeling platform safety as a connected ecosystem.
  • Measure classifier and GenAI performance by evolving evaluation frameworks, probability calibration methods, and experimental frameworks governing multimodal classifiers, rankers, and GenAI Agents.
