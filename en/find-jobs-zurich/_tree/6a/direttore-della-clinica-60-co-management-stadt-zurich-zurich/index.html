Full description

We are looking for 1. June 2027 or by arrangement a committed and motivated personality.

  • Professional, personnel and organizational co-management of the school dental clinic Eggbühl
  • Ensuring high-quality, modern and patient-oriented dental care and treatment of children and adolescents
  • Monitoring and optimization of clinical processes, treatment concepts and quality standards
  • Business planning of human and material resources
  • Promoting an appreciative team culture and a constructive, motivating working environment
  • Maintaining and expanding cooperation with internal and external specialist bodies as well as with the University of Zurich
  • Active participation in the strategic development of the school dental service in cooperation with the head of the school dental service and the clinic directors
  • Federal State Examination in Dentistry and Appropriate Professional License (MedReg)
  • Experience in a public administration school dental clinic as well as a continuing education card in pediatric dentistry are beneficial
  • Several years of professional experience in pediatric dentistry
  • Management experience and proven competences in human resources and organisational management
  • Appreciative leadership style with strong communication and social skills
  • Business thinking and a high service orientation
  • Interest in the further development of the School Dental Service
  • Responsible and versatile leadership position
  • Possibility to actively shape the professional, organizational and strategic development of the school dental clinic
  • Committed and competent team with high child dental expertise
  • Exciting professional environment with established contacts to the University of Zurich
  • Pleasant working atmosphere and a modernly equipped clinic
  • Attractive employment conditions of the City of Zurich

The School Health Services are a service department of the City of Zurich School and Sports Department. The medical, psychological and educational specialists form a competence center for prevention and health promotion, advice and support as well as information and public relations work for the benefit of the children and adolescents of the city of Zurich. The School Dental Service is a department of the School Health Services of the City of Zurich School and Sports Department and operates six school dental clinics distributed among the school circles of the city of Zurich.

Information is provided by Dr. med. dent. Oliver Vock, Head of the School Dental Service of the City of Zurich, e-mail: oliver.vock@zuerich.ch
