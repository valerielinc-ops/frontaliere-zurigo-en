Full description

Start: Now Duration: 1 year Pensum: 100%

Eraneos is an international strategy, transformation and technology advisory group that helps companies thrive in a dynamic digital world. With first-class experts from business and technology, we empower our customers to set new standards for sustainable transformations – from strategy to implementation. Whether in the design of sustainable organizations, the development of new potential through data and AI or the protection of companies through holistic cybersecurity: We deliver results pragmatically and purposefully. Our team of around 1,300 dedicated employees is represented in offices in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, Portugal, Denmark, Sweden, Austria, the UK, Singapore and the USA and combines global perspectives with strong local roots. Around 450 employees work at the Swiss locations in Zurich, Bern and Lausanne.

This is the job

  • Creating, auditing and processing customer invoices in accordance with internal guidelines and customer specifications
  • Administrative support of project teams, from kick-off session to project completion
  • Maintenance and updating of customer and project data in the ERP/CRM system
  • Development and maintenance of process documentation as well as participation in process optimization

This job is for you if

  • You have completed a commercial education or similar.
  • You have at least 3 years of experience in the finance/back office or invoicing environment.
  • You are fit in MS 365 (Outlook, Excel, SharePoint, Teams) and ERP systems.
  • You work structured and accurately and keep an overview even under time pressure.
  • You have very good knowledge of German (C1) and English (B2).

As an i-top you bring with you

  • Knowledge of French is beneficial
  • Ideally relevant experience in a consulting or IT company

We are interested in your mindset, your ideas and the impact you want to create. Diversity is a source of growth and inspiration for us. We look forward to every application.

Annalena Raich is your contact person and looks forward to receiving your direct application via our website (CV, diplomas and employment certificates as PDF).
