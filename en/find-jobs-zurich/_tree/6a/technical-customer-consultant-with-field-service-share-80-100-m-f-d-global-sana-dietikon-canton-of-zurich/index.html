Introduction For an internationally active company in the field of industrial plants and solutions, we are looking for a tech-savvy personality with in-depth practical experience at our location in the Zurich region. Ideal is a background as a car, land or truck mechanic or electrician, combined with the willingness to assume organizational responsibility and provide technically competent advice to customers.

Tasks

  • Technical support for customers and service technicians in complex issues
  • Planning and coordination of service missions
  • Preparation and management of service agreements
  • Analysis of disruptions and development of solutions
  • Occasional assignments on site (travel activity about 20–25%)

Profile

  • Completed training as car, agricultural or truck mechanic, electrical installer or equivalent basic technical training
  • Experience in handling machinery, equipment or vehicle technology
  • Knowledge of control and drive technology is advantageous
  • Good knowledge of German and English
  • Organizational talent and pleasure in customer contact

Benefits

  • Varied activity with a technical and organizational share
  • Training opportunities and development prospects
  • Flexible working time models and home office option
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
