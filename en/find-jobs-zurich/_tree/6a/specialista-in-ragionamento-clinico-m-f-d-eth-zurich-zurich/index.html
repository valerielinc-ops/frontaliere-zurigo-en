Specialist: in Clinical Reasoning (m/f/d)

10%, Zurich, temporary

ETH Zurich has been conducting the bachelor’s degree program in human medicine since the autumn semester 2017. This is developed in close cooperation with the university partners for the further master education (University of Basel, University of Zurich and Università della Svizzera italiana). We are looking for a:n specialist in clinical reasoning to strengthen the project team at the earliest possible time.

Job description

They actively participate in the teaching on clinical reasoning, in particular in the module 'From symptom to diagnosis'. You are mainly responsible for organizing teaching content and giving lectures. You are in constant exchange with the other lecturers. Creating and evaluating a performance control is also one of your tasks. Another focus is the participation in the project team of the medical degree program: Vote as a responsible person for the focus clinical reasoning She works closely with the alignment team so that this topic is integrated into the curriculum in a structured manner across the entire curriculum.

Profile

You have a medical education (Dr. med.) with many years of professional experience in the clinical field. You are very familiar with clinical argumentation processes and the corresponding decision-making in your daily work. They have already gained extensive didactic experience and underpinned it with a corresponding additional training (CAS, MAS). They are characterised by innovative, cross-linked thinking; are responsible and problem-solving. In terms of content, you work independently in your specialist area, but at the same time can integrate very well into a team. Excellent knowledge of German and English round off your profile.

We offer

You will become part of an interdisciplinary team in the constructive ETH environment, where emphasis is placed on teamwork, innovative ideas are welcome and various training opportunities are offered. Flexible working hours are possible. The position can be taken immediately.
