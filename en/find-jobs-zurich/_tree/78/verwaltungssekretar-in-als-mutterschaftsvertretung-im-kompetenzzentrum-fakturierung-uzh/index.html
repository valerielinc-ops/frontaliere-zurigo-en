The Institute of Forensic Medicine of the University of Zurich stands for the continuous development from traditional forensic medicine to modern forensics with the sub-disciplines of medicine and imaging, genetics, pharmacology and toxicology, hair analysis and traffic medicine, and constantly strives to create clarity. Just medicine in the service of road safety – this is our motto For our location in Zurich, we are looking for a temporary term immediately or by agreement until 30.09.2027, an:n administrative secretary:in 60% as maternity representation in the Competence Center Invoicing What we do The Department of Transport Medicine deals interdisciplinary with all questions of the transport medical spectrum. Clients are road transport offices, investigative and judicial authorities and courts.

Tasks

  • Preparation of accounts receivable and targeting them
  • Order of files from authorities and offices and control of receipts
  • Case input and preparation/use of case distribution
  • Carefully collecting and maintaining our databases
  • Receiving calls and e-mail requests and providing professional information
  • Physical and electronic mail processing incl. Sending opinions and reports to our working partners
  • Representation for holidays in the competence center Facility Management

In addition,

  • The function offers a lot of variety in administration and cooperation with offices and authorities
  • A modern workplace, structured processes and a helpful team ensure a pleasant working environment
  • Ideas for optimizing processes are welcome and thinking is appreciated
  • The cooperation with various contacts internally and externally as well as our demanding clientele ensures a lively working day

Profile

You have a basic commercial education and several years of experience in a comparable function. They have an independent, efficient and careful working method, quick comprehension, organizational skills and are accustomed to working forward-looking, structured and fast. In addition, you are IT-savvy and have experienced MS-Office and ideally Abacus and IBM Lotus Notes knowledge. You are stylish confident in German, English, Italian and French skills are beneficial, but not a must. Responsibility, resilience and communication skills round off your profile alongside high service orientation, commitment and flexibility. Impeccable manners and a positive charisma distinguish you. We presuppose discretion and a sense of responsibility. Knowledge of medical terminology and the university environment is a plus. Would you like to be part of our collegial team soon?
