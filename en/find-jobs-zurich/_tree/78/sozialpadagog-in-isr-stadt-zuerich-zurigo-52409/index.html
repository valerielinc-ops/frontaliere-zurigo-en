Full description

Become part of our team! In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for an engaged social pedagogue ISR for the individual 1:1 accompaniment of a child of the 1st primary class in the ISR setting (autism spectrum) for the Aussersihl school.

They accompany the child in the school supplementary day-to-day care, create orientation and security and support him to further develop his strengths and independence. A reliable relationship and clear structures form the basis.

The assignment takes place during school time on five days per week in the hoard. Individual missions are planned during school holidays. The workload can be divided by agreement between two specialists.

  • Individual 1:1 accompaniment of a child in school-supplementary day-to-day care
  • Design of a reliable, clearly structured and needs-oriented care framework
  • Accompaniment in social, emotional and challenging everyday situations
  • Support for self-regulation, the development of self-employment and social skills
  • Promoting social participation and integration into the children’s group, taking into account individual needs
  • Accompaniment at meals, in free play as well as leisure and exercise offers
  • Cooperation with the care team, the management of care, the school curative education and other specialists
  • Participation in support planning, professional exchange and cooperation with parents
  • A recognised socio-pedagogical, pedagogical or comparable specialist training (Social Pedagogy FH/HF, Social Work FH, Childhood Pedagogy HF, Child Education HF, a recognised teaching qualification for primary school or a university degree in Social Pedagogy Pedagogy or Educational Sciences). For foreign diplomas, the required Swiss diploma recognition must be attached
  • Experience accompanying children of primary school age, ideally with knowledge of the autism spectrum and neurodivergence
  • Enjoyment of individual relationship work and a good feeling for the needs of children
  • An appreciative, patient and resource-oriented educational attitude
  • The ability to act calmly, clearly and structured even in demanding situations
  • Self-employment, sense of responsibility and a solution-oriented working method
  • Openness for interdisciplinary cooperation and professional reflection
  • A versatile and meaningful activity with the opportunity to support the development of a child immediately and sustainably
  • A dedicated, multi-professional school and care team
  • Professional exchange and cooperation with socio-pedagogical and specialised specialists
  • Space for own ideas and the co-creation of a needs-based everyday care
  • A lively, diverse school environment in the heart of Zurich
  • Progressive employment conditions and remuneration in accordance with the guidelines of the City of Zurich

Zmitzt in the heart of Züri - there(s) are me! Every day at the pulse of education and where our future is growing and learning.

Our Aussersihl Day School is located in the vibrant, culturally diverse heart of Zurich. With us, you can expect a meaningful task in a multi-professional environment in which the individual accompaniment and development of the children are the focus.
