Full description

Planzer Paket stands for reliable and fast parcel deliveries in Switzerland. As part of our team, you actively contribute to further developing our high quality standards and service. We offer you a varied job in a future-oriented company that focuses on sustainable solutions. Become part of our team and shape the future of the package world!

What you move

  • You support our parcel delivery companies in day-to-day operations and ensure that the delivery and pick-up processes run smoothly
  • You coordinate the daily tour and package planning in a forward-looking manner and optimize it continuously
  • During the daily debriefings, you discuss the most important topics, clarify deviations and take care of undelivered packages
  • You have the compliance of our processes under control and initiate the appropriate measures in case of irregularities
  • Administrative tasks are also included: You prepare reports and collect relevant key figures
  • Critically question existing processes, develop new processes and together with your team ensure a productive and ergonomic working environment for our drivers and the coordinators Last-Mile

Why do you succeed?

  • You are an organizational talent and keep an overview even in hectic situations
  • An independent, structured and solution-oriented working method distinguishes you
  • You communicate clearly and service-oriented and openly approach different people
  • Reliability, flexibility and resilience are among your strengths
  • Administrative tasks you do safely and in dealing with digital systems you are well-versed
  • You want to make a difference, actively drive change forward and continue to develop yourself and your environment. Standing still is not an alternative for you

Your benefits

  • Further education and development opportunities: With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • Family culture: You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Free parking: Get ready for parking - with us you can park for free

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
