Full description

Uroviva stands for a modern medical network and includes a special clinic for urology, eleven urological practices and an andrology center. With approximately 70,000 consultations each year in primary, special and emergency care and more than 5,000 operations of all severity levels, Uroviva is an important provider of urological services in Switzerland. For our centrally located andrology center - directly at the Zurich-Stadelhofen station - we are looking for an Senior Medical Assistant in 80-100 %

Their tasks

  • Team management and operational planning
  • Main responsibility for the planning, organization and preparation of office hours incl. Patient reception and care
  • Independent organization of the office hours, specialized in andrology
  • Independent performance of blood sampling, urine analysis and injections
  • Optimisation of processes
  • Performance controlling by ambulant package
  • Reception work such as appointments, reception of patients, operation of the telephone, etc.
  • Management of consumables
  • Responsibility for QM management

Your profile

  • Completed training as MPA EFZ and several years of professional experience
  • Leadership experience, further education as an MPK beneficial
  • Good MS Office skills and foreign language skills
  • Friendly and patient personality as well as high resilience and teamwork
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and action
  • Experience with the operating system Vitodata is advantageous (but not mandatory)

  • Mooring time is paid in time or financially (as desired)
  • Attractive, contemporary social benefits
