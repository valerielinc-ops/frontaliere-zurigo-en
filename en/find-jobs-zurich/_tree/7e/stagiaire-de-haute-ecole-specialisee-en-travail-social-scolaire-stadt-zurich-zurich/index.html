Full description

For the school district Glatttal we are looking for a social work intern for the second semester of the school year 2026/2027 from January 2027 to July 2027.

  • Together with your internship support, you support the children, parents and school staff with social issues.
  • With the guidance of the internship, you will conduct consultations and conflict moderations with individuals or groups.
  • You will work on the development and implementation of prevention and intervention projects and on targeted measures (e.g. on violence, addiction, leisure activities and child protection).
  • Among other things, you apply the methods of systemic solution-oriented discussion and network with various cooperation partners.
  • You are studying social work at a Swiss university of applied sciences
  • Openness and communication skills, appreciative interaction with different partners and people from different cultures
  • Experience in dealing with children and young people
  • Joy of independent work and integrity
  • Motivation, creativity, commitment and determination

Versatile and challenging tasks in a school in the city of Zurich with professional support from experienced school social workers.

With around 2,400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a social Zurich. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.
