Full description

In order to strengthen our motivated and committed medical team, we are looking for a senior physician neonatology immediately or by appointment. The 17-bed neonatology of the KSW takes care of sick newborns and premature babies from the 28th SSW and is permanently integrated in the perinatal center with obstetrics. We work at level NEO 1.1.1 (from 28 SSW/1000 g) and offer a 24/7 transport service with about 200 transports per year. An interdisciplinary, modern environment with modern infrastructure promotes further development and high-quality care. If you enjoy the highly specialized care of premature and newborn children, would like to work interdisciplinary and take on responsibility, we look forward to working with you.

Tasks

  • Responsibility in senior medical function for neonatology and the postpartum/care department
  • Tasks of the General Paediatric Emergency Service covering the full spectrum of paediatrics during periods of service
  • Implementation of ground- and air-bound newborn transports within the framework of Transport Equipe Neonatologie KSW

Profile

  • Specialist title Neonatology or FMH title Pediatric and Adolescent Medicine with experience in Neonatology or Paediatric Intensive Care Medicine
  • Interest in interdisciplinary collaboration and exchange of cross-disciplinary perspectives to jointly develop innovative solutions
  • Organizational talent, team-oriented and communicative personality
