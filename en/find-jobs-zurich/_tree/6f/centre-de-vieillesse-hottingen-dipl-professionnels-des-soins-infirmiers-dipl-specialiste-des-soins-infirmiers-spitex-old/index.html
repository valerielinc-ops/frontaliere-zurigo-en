Welcome to your new place of work.

The Spitex of the old age center Hottingen in the health world Zollikerberg offers you an exciting job in the amount of 50% - 100% as a

Dipl. Nursing professional / Dipl. Care specialist – Spitex Old Age Centre Hottingen We continue to develop and are very happy about strengthening the team.

If you too want to make a difference in people’s lives, these are your tasks:

  • Care, support and advice of our tenants: indoors at home
  • Implementation of needs assessment and case management
  • Ensuring the care process with appropriate documentation
  • Correct collection of performance data
  • Cooperation with relatives, doctors, hospitals, pharmacies and other specialists

Become part of the Old Age Centre Hottingen, which is a company of the health world Zollikerberg / Foundation Diakoniewerk Neumünster – Swiss Nursing School. It offers 150 tenants a modern service living in old age. A medical practice, other medical service providers and a public restaurant complement the range of offers. In total, more than 50 employees work in the areas of care, catering, housekeeping, technical service and administration.
