Full description

What will production look like in five years? At Sonova, there is the opportunity to actively shape the answer.

For our location in Stäfa, we are looking for a future-oriented leader with enthusiasm for automation, innovative technologies and intelligent production processes.

The position focuses on the management and further development of our production organization as well as the transformation of our production to a more automated, efficient and connected production environment.

New perspectives, initiative and the courage to question existing processes are needed. It is not only about developing innovative ideas, but also about translating them into concrete solutions, successfully implementing them and anchoring them sustainably in production.

This is what awaits you

  • Disciplinary management and further development of our production team and promotion of open and constructive cooperation
  • Combination and further development of various production areas into an integrated, flexible and sustainable production organization
  • Development of a long-term vision for our production of the future as well as a clear roadmap with concrete milestones
  • Identification of automation potential and further development of existing processes towards efficient, networked production processes
  • Responsibility for automation projects from initial idea and feasibility testing through validation and industrialization to successful implementation and scale
  • Assessing and introducing new technologies, particularly in robotics, machine vision, AI, smart manufacturing and digital manufacturing solutions
  • Ensuring an efficient production operation with responsibility for quality, safety, delivery reliability, productivity and costs in compliance with regulatory requirements
  • Continuous improvement of our production processes through lean, operational excellence and structured problem solving
  • Close cooperation with engineering, research & development, quality, supply chain and other specialist areas for the implementation of innovative production solutions

You bring that with you

  • Completed degree in engineering, automation engineering, mechatronics, production engineering or a comparable technical qualification
  • Several years of experience in a technical production environment, combined with sound practice in automation, process optimization or industrialization as well as demonstrable success in the implementation of technical improvements
  • Several years of disciplinary management experience and the ability to motivate, develop and lead employees safely even in challenging situations
  • Strategic and future-oriented thinking as well as the ability to translate your own ideas into realistic concepts, clear priorities and concrete milestones
  • Proactive, curious and independent work, coupled with initiative, a sense of responsibility and the joy of breaking new ground
  • Good understanding of industrial production processes and experience with lean manufacturing, continuous improvement and relevant quality standards
  • Experience in medical technology or another regulated industry is beneficial
  • Strong communication and team skills as well as very good German and English skills

We offer this

As one of the world’s leading companies in the field of hearing solutions, headquartered in Switzerland, we attach great importance to an open and inclusive corporate culture in which individual strengths are applied and personal and professional development is promoted.

Flexible working models, diverse training opportunities and individual development perspectives help to combine professional goals with personal interests.

In addition, this position offers the opportunity to actively develop a production organization, to shape the future of our production, to accompany new technologies from idea to productive use and to make a difference in the long term with our own ideas.

Application process

Magdalena Suszek, Talent Acquisition Partner, is looking forward to receiving your application via our online application portal. Please submit your CV and motivation letter. You are also welcome to include references and certificates. For this position, we only consider direct applications.
