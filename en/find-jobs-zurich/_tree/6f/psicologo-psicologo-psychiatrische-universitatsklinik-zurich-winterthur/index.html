The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 15-75% For our Ambulatorium Ponte, an offer in Zurich for children and adolescents seeking protection from the Federal Asylum Centers of the Canton of Zurich, as well as for the consultancy service in an MNA center, we are looking for an Their tasks

  • Implementation of disorder-specific psychotherapeutic treatments in individual and group setting as well as parental counselling if possible
  • Case management with diagnosis and therapy according to the treatment plan
