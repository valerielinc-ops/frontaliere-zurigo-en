About Our Client ##

For an established and successful Swiss company with headquarters in Zurich, we are looking for an experienced, discreet and service-oriented personality as an assistant to the management (m/f/d) 100%.

Job description

  • Proactive support of a management member in all organizational and administrative matters
  • Independent coordination of complex calendars, dates, meetings and business trips at home and abroad
  • Preparation and follow-up of meetings, workshops and business trips
  • Creation, processing and management of correspondence, presentations, reports and decision-making basis
  • Support for strategic and operational projects
  • Maintenance, optimization and further development of administrative processes and filing structures
  • Preparation and control of expense reports as well as support in administrative financial processes
  • Cooperation in internal communication measures and support in the preparation of texts and documents
  • Contact person for internal and external management level stakeholders
  • Reception and support of customers, business partners and guests

The Successful Applicant ##

  • Completed commercial training, further training in the assistance area is advantageous
  • Several years of experience as an executive assistant, personal assistant or management assistant in a demanding corporate environment
  • Experience within a larger, complex corporate structure
  • Confident knowledge of German at native level
  • Very good knowledge of English (at least C1)
  • Good knowledge of French (at least B2)
  • Experienced handling of MS Office as well as affinity for modern digital and AI-based work equipment
  • High organisational strength and a strong sense of quality and responsibility
  • Discreet, loyal and trustworthy personality with sovereign appearance
  • Excellent manners and a high degree of service orientation
