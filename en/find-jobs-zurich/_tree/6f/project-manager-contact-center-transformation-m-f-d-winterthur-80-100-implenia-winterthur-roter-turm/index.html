Full description

As the largest real estate management service provider for demanding residential, office and commercial areas, we offer our customers a comprehensive all-round service package. With our centrally located Customer Value Center (CVC) in Winterthur, we provide tenants, interested parties and suppliers with a central point of contact. Through various channels we ensure a service-oriented and multilingual customer service.

With the “Tenant Service 2030” project, we are further developing our customer service. For this, we are looking for a high-performance personality who drives change and effectively brings together organization, processes and digital solutions as:

Project Manager Contact Center Transformation (m/f/d)

Winterthur, 80-100 %

This is what moves you

  • You lead cross-divisional transformation projects in the “Tenant Service 2030” project from conception to implementation in the company.
  • Together with the specialist areas, you will develop solutions for a customer-oriented, efficient and digital tenant service.
  • You coordinate project teams, internal specialist departments and external partners and control resources, priorities and dependencies.
  • You are responsible for meeting deadlines, costs and quality, making progress, risks and results transparent.
  • You accompany the introduction of new processes and solutions and support employees and managers in the change process.
  • You observe market, technology and service trends on an ongoing basis and derive concrete recommendations for action for the further development of the tenant service.

This is why you succeed

  • You have completed vocational training and higher education in business administration, business informatics, project management or a comparable area.
  • You bring several years of experience in managing complex transformation or digitization projects and use suitable project management methods safely.
  • You understand the interplay between business, processes and IT. Experience in a customer service or contact center organization is beneficial.
  • You think networked, work structured and self-responsible and convince through communication strength and assertiveness.
  • You have experience in accompanying changes and winning different stakeholders for common goals.
  • You have very good knowledge of German and English. Knowledge of French is a plus.

Here are some of our benefits

  • Working hours: We live mobile work with individual (year) working hours and/or part-time work. Up to 40% home office is possible. Work-life balance is a top priority for us, because balanced and relaxed employees are important to us. The purchase of additional holidays is possible.
  • Mobility and food: You will receive a contribution for the mobile phone. For your food, we also make a contribution.
  • Education and training: Promoting employees is an important part of our culture. Benefit from generous support in internal and external education and training. We offer career opportunities in management, specialist and project functions
  • Salär: With us you can expect a market-based remuneration with absolute equal pay for all genders. The pension benefits are attractive and you can choose the right one for you from different models. The accident protection insurance covers the private hospital department.

More information about our benefits can be found on our career page.

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
