Full description

Immediately or by agreement we are looking for a Study Nurse Oncology 40%

Your area of responsibility

  • Recruitment and support of study patients – You are the first contact person and competently accompany the participants through the course of their studies.
  • Contact with doctors and support the study activity
  • Data collection also by means of digital documentation (eCRF)
  • Sample collection and processing, data collection eHealth platform
  • Implemented study protocols in accordance with legislation and standards (SOPs)
