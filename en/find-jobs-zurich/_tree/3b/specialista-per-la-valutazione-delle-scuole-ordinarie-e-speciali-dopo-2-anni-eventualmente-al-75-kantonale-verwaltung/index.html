Canton of Zurich Directorate of Education School Assessment Unit Specialist for the Evaluation of Regular and Special Schools (after 2 years possibly 75%) 60% ASSESSING THE QUALITY OF SCHOOL AND TEACHING HOLISTICALLY The Office for School Assessment in the Canton of Zurich is responsible for reviewing the quality of primary schools. The Office carries out external evaluations of regular and special schools. To complete our team, we are looking for an expert for the evaluation of regular and special schools as of 1 August 2027 or by appointment (60%; after two years with an option of 75%). To your

  • Tasks include planning, conducting and evaluating external school assessments as well as providing feedback and reporting to schools and school authorities. Established work equipment is available to you in alternating teams of two to four people. For this demanding task, we expect a teaching diploma, a university degree or comparable basic training. Additional training and in-depth experience in the areas of school/organizational development or quality management are required. Professional qualifications in special education or special education, in kindergarten or in school management are an advantage. You bring with you in-depth knowledge of elementary schools and the education system and ideally have knowledge and experience in the area of ​​evaluation procedures and methods. Excellent oral and written language skills, excellent organizational and team skills, and good IT skills are also required. Your resilience and willingness to work hard are particularly important: working hours fluctuate within the framework of the annual working hours - in accordance with the rhythm of the school year. We offer you a challenging and meaningful task in an interesting educational environment, the opportunity to use your diverse skills on your own responsibility, and work in a motivated and highly qualified team. You will be carefully prepared for your task in an introductory program lasting several months and by attending the PHZH's CAS School Quality. Would you like to know more?
