Full description

You have an eye for detail, a sense of quality and a sense of enjoyment in making food better and better? GOU (by Hilcona) is an innovative manufacturer for ultra-fresh food in Schlieren ZH, where high-quality day specialties are produced every day with a lot of commitment and craftsmanship. We are looking for an integral and decision-making personality as an employee of quality assurance and product management.

In this versatile function, you ensure compliance with our standards in product safety, quality and legal requirements and bring your expertise into the development of new products. You coordinate projects, evaluate new raw materials and make the exchange with our suppliers. With us you take responsibility and actively shape the quality and further development of our products.

  • Type of contract: indefinite
  • Penalty: 80% - 100%
  • Working time model: flexible
  • Job start: starting immediately
  • Language: German
  • Place of work: Hilcona Gourmet S.A., Ifangstrasse 9 8952 Schlieren

Tasks

  • Taking microbiological samples, carrying out environmental monitoring and the evaluation of sales items in the context of end MHD testing
  • conducting internal process audits, operational visits and hygiene controls
  • training and awareness raising of employees in the areas of quality, hygiene and food safety
  • processing internal error messages and complaints, conducting cause analyses and initiating improvement measures
  • searching and evaluation of new raw materials according to the requirement profile
  • checking and maintaining

Specifications and item master data

  • Support and cooperation in the innovation development team
  • Support for the introduction of new articles

Requirements

  • Completed training in the food industry, such as food technicians HF, ideally with further training in quality assurance or similar
  • High quality of food, its composition and application
  • Experience in the QS/QM of a food processing company
  • Experience in project management and an independent, structured working method
  • Very good German knowledge in word and writing, English and French skills of advantage
  • High level of responsibility, accuracy and communication
  • Versierte Verhandlung mit MS

Office (mainly

Excel), experience with ERP systems of advantage
