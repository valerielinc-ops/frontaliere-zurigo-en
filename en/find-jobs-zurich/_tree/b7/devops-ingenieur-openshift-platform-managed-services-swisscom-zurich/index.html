# DevOps Engineer – OpenShift Platform & Managed Services

Your future starts here

In our agile DevOps team, you operate and develop a modern OpenShift platform for our B2B customers, as well as resilient services running on it. You take responsibility for the setup, lifecycle and reliable operation of this platform and its services. You rely on automation, GitOps and tools from the CNCF ecosystem. With your passion for container technologies and your enthusiasm for collaborating with our customers, you take Swisscom and our B2B services to the next level.

  • Build, operate and continuously develop our Kubernetes platform and the managed services offered on it
  • Ensure high availability, scalability and resilience through robust architecture and automation
  • Use the Swisscom Private Cloud to provide the platform and services
  • Use and manage operators and CNCF tools for logging, monitoring, backup, RBAC, security and other infrastructure components

Your skills

  • Strong hands-on experience in building, operating and further developing production Red Hat OpenShift platforms
  • Very good knowledge of Kubernetes and OpenShift lifecycle management, particularly updates, upgrades and troubleshooting
  • Experience with GitOps and the automation of platform configurations and operational processes, for example with Argo CD and Tekton
  • Experience with monitoring and logging of container-based platforms, for example with Prometheus, Grafana and Loki
  • Red Hat Certified OpenShift Administrator certification is an advantage
  • Additional knowledge of Service Mesh, GitLab or Kafka is an advantage
  • Independent way of working, strong teamwork skills, flexibility and a good understanding of agile and lean ways of working
  • Fluent in german and english
  • Willingness to participate in on-call duty. Your place of residence is in Switzerland

With us, you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in your home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive benefits that will enrich your work experience. These include a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Discover your Benefits.
