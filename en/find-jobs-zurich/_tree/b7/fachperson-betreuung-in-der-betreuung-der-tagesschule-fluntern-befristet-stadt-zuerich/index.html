Full description

The care offer of the day school Fluntern consists of two morning tables, a lunch service and six lunch / evening services. About 300 pupils are supervised in the care, which are allocated in the care according to kindergarten and primary school groups. The care team consists of over 35 caregivers.

The supervision of our school seeks temporary from 1 November 2026 to 31. May 2027 Reinforcement.

Tasks

  • They support the care team at the kindergarten level.
  • They promote and support the children in their development process and their social skills.
  • The implementation of the educational goals of the school is a matter of course for you.
  • They actively shape the care offers with joy of play and urge to move.
  • They perform administrative, operational and domestic tasks.

Profile

  • Training: EFZ specialist care or infant teacher (CH recognition for foreign diplomas is mandatory)
  • Flexibility and resilient personality with great commitment
  • Professional experience in educational work and joy in leading groups at the kindergarten level
  • Readiness for interdisciplinary cooperation in the school team
  • Self-employment, sense of responsibility and good team and communication skills

We offer

A varied, versatile and independent activity, in which you lead the children's group together with the hoard management. A motivated and committed team that looks forward to working together. An exciting environment awaits you, a lot of freedom in the design of day-to-day care and competent support from the management of care. In addition, you can look forward to progressive salary and social benefits according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department.

Your assignment days are divided into the following 4 days: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday

About us

The Zurichberg School District comprises districts 1, 7 and 8 with the districts Altstadt, Fluntern, Hottingen, Hirslanden, Riesbach and Witikon. The district school authority manages 18 school houses. These are affiliated with 43 kindergartens and over 40 care facilities of different sizes. Of the 14 organizational units, 11 are day schools.
