Welcome to your new place of work.

Vera Benz & Monika Stirnimann offer you at 1. January 2027 or by agreement an 80-100% position as

Dipl. Nursing professional / Dipl. Nursing professional / Dipl. Midwife Maternité with heart

If you too want to make a difference in people’s lives, these are your tasks:

  • Care of women who have recently given birth with their infants, patients with high-risk pregnancies and gynecological patients
  • Care for the assigned patients
  • Care and support for students, health professionals and trainees

Become part of the Department of Women's, Child and Obstetric Medicine at the Zollikerberg Hospital. On our postpartum ward we accompany mothers, newborns and their families individually and family-oriented in the first days after birth. The close interprofessional cooperation with obstetrics and neonatology enables comprehensive care of mother and child. The focus is on individual needs and the well-being of families.

About the Zollikerberg hospital #LI-PZ1

We stand for a comprehensive range of services and strong cooperation partners. With a public service mandate and 173 beds, the Zollikerberg Hospital contributes to the optimal medical care of the greater Zurich area. Every year, more than 11,000 inpatients and 75,000 outpatients in all insurance classes can feel comfortable and in good hands. Our broadly based primary care includes internal medicine, gynecology and obstetrics with neonatology, general surgery, an interdisciplinary emergency center and orthopedics with specialized spinal surgery.
