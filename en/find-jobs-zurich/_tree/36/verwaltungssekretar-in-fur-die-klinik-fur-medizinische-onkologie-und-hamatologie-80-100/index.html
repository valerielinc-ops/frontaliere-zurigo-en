Work in an exciting and versatile environment: The Clinic for Medical Oncology and Hematology combines highly specialized patient care, modern diagnostics, teaching and international research under one roof.   • Miscellaneous administrative tasks
  • Deputy within the Secretariat

Your profile

  • Completed training as MPA, medical secretary:in or commercial training with further training as medical and hospital secretary:in
  • Professional experience in a similar function beneficial
  • Independent, team-capable and reliable personality
  • Friendly appearance and efficient, thinking workflow
  • Stylistic German and English, knowledge of Italian is advantageous
  • Dealing with MS-Office
