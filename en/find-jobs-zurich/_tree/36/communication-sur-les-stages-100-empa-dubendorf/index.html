Full description

Materials science and technology are our passion. With our cutting-edge research, Empa’s approximately 1,100 employees make decisive contributions to the benefit of society for a future worth living in. Empa is a research institution of the ETH Domain.

Internship Communication (100%)

The communications department represents Empa on different channels towards internal and external target groups.

Their tasks

As an intern, you will be responsible for the following tasks for three months:

  • You research and write your own editorial contributions for div. Empa’s communication products/channels (webpage, magazine, annual report, social media, etc.). You will also learn to edit texts and to edit and publish image and video material.
  • You will learn and use external and internal communication tasks via Inter- and Intranet as a member of Empa’s editorial team.

Your profile

  • You are currently completing a journalistic training (ZHAW, MAZ, etc.) or studying in the field of (science) communication.
  • They have a good general education and show strong interest in topics with social relevance from the scientific or technical field.
  • They are committed, creative and sociable and ideally have already gained initial experience in the journalistic field.
  • Your own initiative is not a foreign word for you, and you do not shy away from complex contexts.

Our offer

Empa offers an exciting, stimulating and dynamic environment in which innovation, creativity and self-responsibility are important success factors. The internship is limited for three months.

“a” stands for “all” in our job advertisements. We live a culture of inclusion and respect. We welcome all people who have the desire for innovative, sustainable and meaningful activities - only that counts.

We look forward to your complete online application incl. Letter of motivation, CV, certificates, diplomas and contact details of two reference persons. Please submit them exclusively via our application portal. Applications by e-mail or by post will not be considered.
