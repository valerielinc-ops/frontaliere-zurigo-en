Full description

For the research team of the eye clinic at the Triemli site, we are looking for an initiative personality for a limited period of 1 year with an option of extension.

  • Development, testing and use of AI solutions in medicine, in a hospital environment
  • Data management pipelines
  • Design, development and maintenance of artificial intelligence algorithms for ophthalmology research, curation of data sets, creation of APIs and management of cloud and IT resources on site
  • Collection of third-party funds
  • Supporting the research team in studies and scientific publications
  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in computer science or a related field with proven experience in machine learning and high performance computing infrastructures
  • Programming skills in Python, C++ and CUDA
  • Expertise in managing digital storage and infrastructure for high-performance computing, developing APIs and providing cloud services
  • Experience with deep learning frameworks such as: visual transformers, natural language processing, large language models, reinforcement learning and cloud computing
  • Advantages include experience in the implementation of AI tools or with AI applications in the field of med. Imaging (especially ophthalmology)
  • Experience with medical data, image processing, or vascular segmentation is advantageous
  • Cooperative and team-oriented working methods
