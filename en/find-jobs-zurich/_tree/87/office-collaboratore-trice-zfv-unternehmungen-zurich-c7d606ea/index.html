Full description

The catering operation at the University of Zurich Centre has four different outlets with a total of 1‘600 seats. Each day, around 2‘500-3‘000 lunch menus and 400 dinners for delivery are produced. Internal and external catering services are also provided. In keeping with the ZFV philosophy, fresh meals are prepared on site using regional, environmentally responsibly produced and fairly traded ingredients. Are you our new team member who will support us as an office employee as of 1 December 2026 or by agreement? You work from Monday to Friday on early, mid or late shifts. Your working hours are between 07:00 and 20:30. Occasionally, you also work on Saturdays at special events. Your menu of tasks:

  • Washing dishes, cutlery and all kitchen utensils
  • Operating the dishwashing machine and maintaining the machines in the dishwashing area
  • Waste disposal and separation
  • Ensuring cleanliness and order in the work environment
  • Carrying out general tidying and cleaning work Your recipe for success:
  • You have already gained experience in the catering or hotel industry
  • Experience in the dishwashing area is an advantage
  • You work reliably, carefully and efficiently
  • You enjoy pitching in and keep an overview even when the workload is high
  • You are a team player, helpful and open to learning new things
  • You have oral German language skills What we offer you: We create teams that support one another and in which you are encouraged. We make an impact together and focus on everything that promotes well-being. Among other things, we place great importance on pay transparency and equal pay. For this position, with a 100% workload, depending on your experience and qualification, we offer an annual salary of around CHF 52'000 (incl. 13. Monthly salary).
