The MOH study coordination offers an exciting and diverse working environment in which you get to know the entire spectrum of clinical study processes and actively participate. As a central interface for clinical trials, you are in close contact with our medical and nursing team and ensure that processes are clearly structured, transparently communicated and implemented smoothly. At the same time, you will work with internal laboratories, other specialist clinics and external partners such as sponsors and CROs, gaining a comprehensive understanding of the requirements of modern research. In the course of your activity, you will grow into various areas: A central focus is on data management – from the structured collection and maintenance of study data to GCP-compliant documentation of all processes. In addition, you support budget planning with the sponsor, coordinate internal contract processes and accompany patients during their study participation. Study invoicing, logistics management for study medicines and samples and the clear management of all documents are also part of your field of responsibility. This diversity requires communication strength, enjoyment of interdisciplinary collaboration and the ability to switch flexibly and solution-oriented between different priorities. Anyone who wants to take responsibility and actively shape a dynamic research environment will find a task with a lot of development potential here.

Their main tasks

  • Contact person for sponsors and CRO
  • Coordination of cooperation with internal clinics, laboratories, experts and other professional groups
  • Contract management for study-relevant contracts and amendments
  • Examination of budgeting costs and preparation and management of internal agreements
  • Preparation, organisation and implementation of pre-study, initiation, monitoring and close-out visits as well as audits and inspections (sponsor, Swissmedic, internal bodies)
  • Administrative coordination of the study process incl. Planning and process control according to study protocol
  • Data management: Collection, management and documentation of study data (ISF/TMF, digital repository)
  • Ensuring GCP compliant source data documentation
  • Technical support for queries about data points and data quality
  • Contact person:in for administrative patient concerns
  • Management, ordering and delivery of study medicines
  • Ensuring the confidentiality and anonymisation of all data
  • Preparation, processing, storage and dispatch of study-specific laboratory samples
  • Implementation and control of study invoices
  • Participation in internal training, research sessions, meetings and workshops
  • Collaboration in internal and cross-clinical processes for the further development of the study landscape at the USZ

Your profile

  • Vocational training or B.Sc / M.Sc in medical science field
  • Knowledge of medical terminology, physiology and pathophysiology in the hematological/oncological field
  • Experience in conducting clinical trials
  • Proven knowledge of GCP and recognized CH certification
  • Reliable, proactive and solution-oriented way of working, with excellent organizational skills
  • Team-oriented workflow with effective internal and external communication
  • Ability to comply with multiple responsibilities, deadlines and deadlines and to work independently, pre-departurely
  • Safe handling of standard EDP programs
  • High willingness to learn
  • Flowing language skills in German and English
