Full description

You want a job that requires care, reliability and the confidential handling of data? Then you are right with us. In data management, you work with tax-relevant data and are in contact with internal agencies, authorities and taxpayers. To support the tax reference department, we are looking for reinforcement from 1 December 2026 to 31 December 2027. Tasks

  • You check and process tax data (taxation messages) of natural and legal persons and ensure that they are correctly transferred to the system.
  • You also manage the data of all taxpayers as well as the withholding tax offsets for properly taxed persons afterwards.
  • You can clarify questions by telephone or in writing with taxpayers, their representatives, municipal tax offices or internal specialists.
  • In case of ambiguity, you research independently and forward cases correctly if necessary.
  • You support in testing applications and in the further development of processes.
  • Maintaining internal documentation and helping to optimize processes are part of your job. Profile
  • You have completed a commercial education (EFZ).
  • We presuppose very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Other languages are a plus.
  • With MS Office you are familiar and quickly familiar with new applications.
  • You work accurately, quickly and independently – even at high work volumes.
  • You feel comfortable in contact with various contact persons and communicate respectfully and in a situational manner, whether by phone or in writing.
