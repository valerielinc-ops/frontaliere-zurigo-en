Full description

SFS is a nationwide provider of screws, tools, fittings, building ranges, special parts and chemical-technical products throughout Switzerland. We offer our customers professional advice both online and with local sales branches. Numerous employees in the field service support the customers with competent recommendations and solutions. This includes a time and market range. For our team we are looking for an area sales manager in (m/f/d) with sales area Zurich Oberland. Your Tasks:

  • Sales, consultancy and introduction of new products and services
  • Responsibility for turnover, contribution margin and margin in the Eastern Switzerland area
  • Acquisition of new customers and maintenance of existing customer relationships
  • Ensuring customer satisfaction and handling customer complaints
  • Visit planning, reporting and maintenance of customer master data in the CRM
  • Close cooperation with specialists from the Categories and the Key Account Management Your profile:
  • Commercial or technical basic training and ideally a further education as a sales specialist with a federal certificate or a business administration degree (HF/FH)
  • Industry experience from the industrial or metal sector
  • Sales experience in the field
  • Place of residence in the market area
  • Independent and customer-oriented personality
  • Negotiation skills and pleasure in acquiring new customers
  • Good knowledge of MS Office, especially Excel and Teams
  • Very good knowledge of German, English of advantage We offer:
  • Clearly structured training phase with cross-department onboarding program
  • Flexible working hours and home office
  • Six weeks of holidays & additional holiday purchase possible
