Full description

Thanks to its modern infrastructure and high patient frequency, the Clinic for Radio-Oncology at the University Hospital Zurich is one of the leading radio-oncology centres in Switzerland. A scientifically active team, diverse research opportunities and the use of innovative technologies make it possible to treat patients with all oncological diseases according to the latest findings and at the highest medical level. >The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.

Tasks

  • Team-based patient radiooncology treatment with direct cadreal mentoring
  • Independent implementation of the entire treatment spectrum, including indication, irradiation planning and implementation
  • Active participation and participation in clinical-scientific research projects and prospective study protocols
  • Active participation in clinical and scientific training

Requirements

  • They aspire to the specialist in radio-oncology (FMH recognition A) and ideally already have previous experience in radio-oncology
  • Further education and experience in internal medicine or oncological disciplines desirable, but not condition
  • You are interested in research and teaching
  • You are above-average committed and a respectful treatment of patients: inner and employees is a matter of course for you
  • You have a very good knowledge of German

We offer

We offer you an interesting job in a university-innovative environment. A motivated and committed team supports you in all aspects. The internal networking with the other clinics promotes the broad orientation and further education of the employees and allows the optimal care of the patients at the highest clinical-academic level. In addition to participating in external courses (DEGRO, ESTRO) and congresses, we offer excellent training in accordance with the FMH's continuing education programme and demand dedication and commitment from our assistants. With a technological equipment at the highest level consisting of three modern linear accelerators for high-precision radiation therapy, a brachytherapy unit and an MRI linear accelerator as well as two independent MR tomographs, we can offer an internationally highly competitive research environment in addition to training in the most modern radio-oncological treatment areas. This is complemented by a close cooperation with the University of Zurich and the ETH Zurich. If you want to work in a top medical environment where you are challenged and promoted, we look forward to receiving your online application.
