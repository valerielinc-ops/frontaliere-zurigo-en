Full description

Introduction

As a Signal Processing Engineer, you are responsible for the design and verification of digital signal processing concepts and application-oriented algorithms for sensors, actuators and systems. Your primary focus is on mathematical correctness, robustness, performance and documentation, providing a solid and reusable technical basis for implementation. You transform available digital measurement data into reliable estimates, meaningful performance indicators and diagnostic insights. As a central technical contact across development domains, you develop model-based estimation and diagnostic algorithms, derive requirements for transducers and electronics, and verify their performance against theoretical limits and measured data. You complement our simulation and control experts and work closely with the system architecture, sensing, electronics, mechanics, and software teams.

Key Responsibilities

  • Translate application requirements into algorithm concepts, design targets and measurable acceptance criteria early in the development process.
  • Develop and justify filtering, feature extraction, estimation, identification, compensation and post-processing algorithms by deriving requirements together with other technical domains for relevant components in the signal processing chain.
  • Define error budgets and performance limits, accounting for noise, parameter uncertainty, measurement variation, sampling, jitter, quantization and numerical precision.
  • Derive requirements for transducers, analog front ends, excitation, sampling, and timing from the error budget, and agree them with relevant stakeholders.
  • Build MATLAB/Simulink models, support integration into system simulations, and verify using measurement data and automated regression.
  • Define reproducible identification and verification plans with laboratory and domain teams, specifying conditions, reference measurements, data quality and evaluation methods.
  • Document formulations, assumptions, parameters, interfaces, error budgets, validity limits and validation evidence, with traceability to requirements and acceptance criteria; hand over algorithms with traceable documentation, reference models, test vectors, and numerical tolerances, and review the embedded implementations against them.
  • Establish reusable methods and guidelines, coach domain experts, identify capability gaps; select and coordinate with external expertise.

Required Expertise

  • University degree (ETH/Uni/FH) in Electrical Engineering, Cybernetics, Physics, Applied Mathematics or a related discipline.
  • Strong experience in developing digital signal processing/estimation algorithms, data analysis and engineering algorithm development.
  • Solid theoretical knowledge in signals and systems, statistical signal processing, filtering, feature extraction, identification and estimation theory, including observers, matched filtering, time-delay estimation and Kalman filters.
  • Proficiency in MATLAB/Simulink skills for algorithm development, simulation and data analysis including machine learning, optimization, system identification methods and approximate physics models.
  • Strong understanding of dynamics, feedback control and algorithm integration in model-based workflows.
  • Ability to translate specifications and physical relationships into models and algorithms, and identify parameters and uncertainty from data with domain experts; experience with measurement and verification planning, error budgets, requirements-based acceptance criteria, simulation, reproducible test data and automated reference-model regression.
  • Good analytical, problem-solving and documentation skills, with attention to detail; effective communicator, collaborator and coach across disciplines.
  • Fluent in English
  • German is a strong plus.

About Belimo

Belimo is the global market leader in the development, production, and sales of field devices for the energy-efficient control of heating, ventilation and air-conditioning systems. The focus of our core business is on damper actuators, control valves, sensors and meters. Founded in 1975, the company has been listed on the Swiss Exchange (SIX) since 1995.
