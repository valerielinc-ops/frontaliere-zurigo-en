Full description

Your team

Pictet Asset Services provides custody, fund services and trading solutions for external asset managers, fund managers and institutional clients. Our services span the full asset servicing value chain. We offer tailored solutions, including value-adding analytics, trading solutions and digital front-end interfaces, to deliver a high-quality client experience.

As a Client Relationship Officer based in Zurich, you will join our team serving German-speaking Switzerland and act as the first point of contact for operational requests from clients, Business Developers (BDs) and Client Relationship Managers (CRMs). This is an 12 months fixed-term position with the possibility of extension and potential permanent employment.

You will join a service-oriented team that values your motivation, high standards and ability to see connections. Your strong communication and interpersonal skills, together with your ability to work independently, will help you support colleagues and clients. Your organisational skills and thoughtful approach will enable you to keep an overview, even in busy situations. A strong sense of responsibility, a personable manner and a clear client focus will guide your work with the team and our external clients.

Your role

Your responsibilities include the following

  • Act as the main point of contact for clients on operational matters, working closely with the CRM responsible for the relationship.
  • Support the CRM in day-to-day client servicing, preparing meetings and coordinating with internal teams.
  • Present Pictet together with the CRM, online or in person.
  • Provide high-quality operational support to clients on a day-to-day basis.
  • Coordinate client enquiries and follow up on operational matters to ensure timely resolution. Manage day-to-day operations, including payments, orders, transfers and reporting.
  • Handle the operational set-up of new client relationships in collaboration with various operations teams.
  • Work closely with all operational teams and functional divisions across the Group.
  • Ensure that operational documents, including operating memoranda, internal procedures and checklists, are in place and followed.

Your profile

  • Excellent knowledge of asset servicing (custodian banking and investment funds) and operational processes, with client-facing experience and at least five years in a similar role.
  • Knowledge of the legal and regulatory requirements applicable to investment fund products.
  • Sound knowledge of compliance and an awareness of risk management.
  • Expert proficiency in MS Office and knowledge of the Avaloq banking system.
  • Native-level German and a good command of English
  • French is a clear advantage.
  • Proven client-service experience and the ability to build strong relationships through technical expertise and effective solutions.
  • A service-oriented approach with the ability to handle sensitive situations with diplomacy and technical expertise.
  • The ability to build strong internal networks across international teams.
  • Initiative, independence and the ability to make decisions that ensure issues are resolved promptly.

We will not accept any CVs via agencies
