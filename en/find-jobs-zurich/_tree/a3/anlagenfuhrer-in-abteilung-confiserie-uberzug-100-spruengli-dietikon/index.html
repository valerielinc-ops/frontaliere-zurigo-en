Full description

Founded in 1836, the Swiss family business is today one of the most renowned confiseries in Europe with its exquisite range. Sprüngli products stand for the best quality, unique freshness and naturalness. The completed creations bring customers from all over the world to swarm daily.

Plant manager* in the Confiserie overlay department (100%)

100%, Dietikon

Working at Sprüngli

For production in Dietikon, we are looking for a motivated and committed person immediately or by agreement. *n

What you do with us

You support the team in various work steps during the careful production of our products. Your work assignment takes place on weekdays in two shifts: early service from 6 a.m. to about 3 p.m. and from 7 a.m. to about 4 p.m. Due to seasonal conditions, you should also be able to start work at 4 o'clock or work until 10 o'clock. Assignments are also possible on Saturday, depending on the work volume.

  • Setting up, preparing and operating production machines
  • Monitoring the manufacturing process and ensuring smooth operation
  • Coating and decorating high-quality chocolates
  • Compliance with safety, hygiene and quality requirements
  • Responsibility for order, cleanliness and maintenance in your own area
  • Assistance in band work
  • Possible additional task as a practice instructor*

What we want

  • Teaching degree in the food industry (confectioner-confiser EFZ, food technologist EFZ, food practitioner, etc.)
  • Good knowledge of German and good technical understanding
  • Precise and quality-conscious working methods
  • Good manners, teamwork and sense of responsibility
  • Good physical condition and craftsmanship
  • Independent and organized work at a high level
  • Computer knowledge (SAP of advantage)

You can look forward to that.

With us, you can expect a versatile and challenging task in a lively, modern environment. You work in a motivated team with an open you culture and real cohesion.
