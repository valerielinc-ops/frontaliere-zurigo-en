Full description

More than 105,000 examinations and procedures are carried out each year in the Department of Radiology and Nuclear Medicine. The Department of Radiology and Nuclear Medicine guarantees the highest level of professional expertise and quality of care in all its areas: X-ray and fluoroscopy diagnostics, breast imaging, ultrasound, nuclear medicine, magnetic resonance imaging, computed tomography, paediatric radiology, neuroradiology and interventional radiology. Are you interested in an exciting and varied role as a radiology professional? Then we look forward to getting to know you and discussing your prospects and your potential roles with us. We are constantly offering interesting positions for radiology professionals in the various specialist areas.

Tasks

  • You keep an overview of your daily work and skilfully and empathetically connect highly complex, state-of-the-art technology with patients
  • You monitor, provide support, think holistically and take responsibility within the assigned scope of investigation
  • You see working weekend, night and on-call shifts as an opportunity and are willing to take on such shifts from time to time

Profile

  • Completed training as a certified radiology technician (HF)
  • Interest in the field of diagnostic and interventional radiology, as well as a good technical understanding
  • Enthusiasm for interdisciplinary collaboration with patients, doctors and staff
  • You value teamwork, are motivated, reliable, resilient and enjoy working in a diverse company with individual opportunities for advancement
  • Good to very good knowledge of German; other languages are an advantage

Benefits: Work and private life should be compatible. That is why, where operations allow, KSW offers you flexible and flexitime working hours, working from home, generous maternity leave, paternity leave and working hours that can be adapted to different stages of life. Develop yourself professionally and personally: Every year, around 40 interdisciplinary and 50 specialist training courses are available to you at KSW. We also support external further training – in terms of time and/or financially.

  • Sometimes you need support. If you are experiencing personal stress, prolonged illness or difficult situations in your professional or private life, KSW offers you various confidential counselling services.
  • At KSW, we work together and for each other. Appreciation, open and respectful communication, and collaboration across professional groups and departments shape our day-to-day work. You should be able to contribute and develop with your ideas and strengths.

Your future is also taken care of: You benefit from very good social benefits and a pension fund solution with BVK that is well above the statutory minimum. As a KSW employee, you are also very well covered in the event of illness or an accident.
