Full description

In brief

At the Air Force Air Defence Operations Centre, you will manage, monitor and optimize the automated generation of the air situation picture, thereby providing comprehensive support for military airspace surveillance to safeguard air sovereignty.

  • Start date: by agreement
  • Employment type: permanent
  • Reference no.: JRQ$540-20550
  • Place of work: military airfield, 8600 Dübendorf

This is what you can contribute

  • As the responsible quality assurance unit for flight paths, monitor and control the automated generation of the air situation picture
  • Assess ongoing operations, analyze faults and develop the foundations for optimizing the systems
  • Control and configure the radars to ensure optimal radar coverage and take measures to improve the air situation picture
  • Ensure that tasks are carried out as a team in 24-hour / 365-day shift operations and, at times, in underground Air Force facilities
  • Support local specialists and the troops as the subject-matter expert, and assist with system evaluations and in project organizations

What makes you unique

  • Completed technical or electronics training and several years of professional experience
  • Further training in a technical field is desirable
  • Strategic thinking and independent, responsible action
  • Active knowledge of a second official language and good knowledge of English
  • Confident and independent use of digital work tools and applications (MS Office, SAP); competent handling of digital information and forms of communication, as well as a strong awareness of data protection, information security and the secure use of digital technologies
  • Fit and liable for military service and willing to perform periodic military service with the Air Force throughout the entire period of employment

Your commitment to security and freedom The Operations Command (Kdo Op) is responsible for all military operations and deployments of the Swiss Armed Forces. The Operations Command includes, among others, the Air Force, the Army, the four territorial divisions, the SWISSINT Competence Center, the Special Forces Command and the Military Police. Around 40 % of the approx. 2'500 employees work in civilian positions – from engineers through aircraft mechanics to analysts.

The Swiss Armed Forces employ over 9'000 employees in four military and around 200 civilian professions – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also provided for: the Swiss Armed Forces train apprentices in over 30 professions.
