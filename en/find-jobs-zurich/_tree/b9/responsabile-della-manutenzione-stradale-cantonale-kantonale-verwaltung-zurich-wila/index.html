Full description

Do you want to work with a dedicated team to ensure that our cantonal roads remain safe and passable at all times? Then we look forward to you as a manager for our maintenance district 9. Because an efficient road infrastructure does not end with its construction. It is crucial that it remains safe, reliable and functional in the long term. Your tasks

  • You lead the Maintenance District 9 with about 23 specialists and preparatory workers, in personnel, organizational and operational terms
  • You plan, coordinate and monitor operational road maintenance including winter service and piquette operations
  • You ensured road safety and proper maintenance of cantonal roads and their annexes
  • You are responsible for budget and cost control, the reporting and the operating role of the workshop and take over the function as security officer (SiBe)
  • You coordinate cooperation with municipalities, plants, authorities and other external partners Your profile
  • You have training in the construction or craft sector and further training in construction management (e.g. HF) or a comparable qualification
  • You have several years of professional experience in road maintenance or in a comparable technical operating environment
  • You have already gained experience in leadership and coordinate the various tasks routinely
  • You work independently and keep an eye on the costs
  • You represent decisions clearly and comprehensibly, also towards authorities and external partners
  • You lead your team with appreciation and promote your employees in their development
  • You see yourself as a service provider for the population and communities In your district, you will not only manage around 180 km of state roads, but also 80 km of cycle paths and 80 km of hiking trails. So you make sure that everyone is safely on the road in the Tösstal – in the car, on the bike and on foot. Your everyday life takes place between the workshop, the street and the community, with short decision paths and a lot of direct exchange. Careful induction and targeted further training in leadership and specialist topics help you to grow in your role.
