Project Manager at the sus.lab

80%-100%, Zurich, fixed-term

sus.lab, the Sustainability in Business Lab at ETH Zurich, is a think-and-do-tank of 10 people with backgrounds in engineering, science, business, and policy. We help industry move to a net zero and circular economy in practice – bridging the gap between the latest research and industrial application.

Join us as a project manager if you are curious how the latest technologies can help industry reach net zero – and what it takes to make them work in practice.

Start day: As soon as possible, ideally January 2027, and no later than March 2027.

The contract is initially limited to one year, with the intention of a multi-year employment relationship.

Project background

sus.lab is part of the Group for Sustainability and Technology (SusTec) at ETH Zurich’s Department of Management, Technology, and Economics. Further information about sus.lab, our team, and past and current projects can be found here .

Job description

As a Project Manager, you typically work on two projects at a time in small teams. You collaborate with the team and work on dedicated tasks, with own responsibility, growing from individual work packages to overall project steering.

Your tasks can range from techno-economic assessments of recycling routes for automotive materials, industrial remanufacturing, and carbon dioxide removal (CDR) to qualitative studies on options to reduce household food waste, including through digital technologies - and much more.

You will

  • Work on first-of-their-kind projects, both on the implementation side of applied research consortia and directly with industry partners
  • Analyze technologies, business cases, value chains, and policies – e.g., through techno-economic assessments or stakeholder and value chain analyses that reveal dependencies and trade-offs – often in fields that are new to you
  • Prepare, run, and evaluate interviews and workshops with decision-makers from industry, policy, and society, assess the needs and positions of relevant stakeholders and condense their perspectives into our analyses
  • Turn your findings into reports and presentations and present them to our project partners and the wider public
  • Take responsibility for projects: plan and manage your workstreams and help organize meetings and events
  • Help write research and third-party funding proposals

Expect roughly two-thirds desk work (research, analysis, writing) and one-third interaction (interviews, workshops, internal and external meetings).

Profile

This position is ideal for master’s graduates with around two years of relevant work experience. Experience in industry or consulting, e.g., in manufacturing, chemical, electronics, or automotive sector, is particularly welcome – but not a prerequisite. It is NOT a doctoral position.

You need

  • A completed master’s degree in a relevant field (e.g., engineering, natural sciences, business, operations or supply chain management, environmental science, or policy)
  • An excellent command of English (C1 or higher), written and spoken
  • German is highly welcome, French or Italian a plus
  • Residence in Switzerland by your start date and regular presence at our Zurich office; remote work from abroad is not possible

If you hold a PhD or have significantly more than two years of relevant work experience, this role is not the right fit.

You are a great fit if you (no need to tick every box)

  • Have shown your interest in net zero and the circular economy and its practical operationalization beyond the classroom (thesis, job, start-up, volunteering)
  • Have first project management skills and are eager to build them further: you bring structure to complex problems and keep workstreams on track
  • Are comfortable with quantitative analysis – or keen to learn it – e.g., techno-economic assessments, business cases, or simple models
  • Have used qualitative research methods such as interviews or focus groups, e.g., in your master’s thesis
  • Have a strong writing style and communicate complex topics clearly and concisely, both in writing and in person
  • Have some hands-on experience using AI tools for research, analysis and project work, and/or AI applications for net zero & CE
  • Work independently but not alone: you own your tasks, share early drafts, and give and take honest feedback

We offer

  • Responsibility from day one: your own workstreams and direct contact with decision-makers at leading companies, start-ups, and public authorities
  • Guidance from two experienced Co-Leads and your project team members, regular feedback, and a development dialogue twice a year
  • A motivated team and focus on team culture, peer exchanges and joint activities
  • State-of-the-art ETH research combined with hands-on, consulting-style project work, and a network spanning industry, policy, and academia
  • A clear development path, and a strong starting point for what comes after: former colleagues now work in consulting, industry, corporate sustainability, start-ups, and the public sector
  • Flexible hours with up to one home-office day per week
  • ETH Zurich’s employee benefits
