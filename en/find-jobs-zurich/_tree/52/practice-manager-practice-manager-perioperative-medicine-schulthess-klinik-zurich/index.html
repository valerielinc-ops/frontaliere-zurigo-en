Full description

Tasks

  • Personnel and professional management of the secretarial team, consisting of currently 6 employees, including recruitment in cooperation with the Human Resoruces
  • Ensuring a smooth and patient-oriented consultation and secretarial process for the two departments of anesthesia and internal medicine
  • Service and absence planning for the secretarial and medical team, including monthly PEP completion
  • Cooperation with the Chief Physician Anesthesia and the Chief Physician Internal Medicine in organizational and personnel matters
  • Quality assurance and continuous development of the department’s administrative processes and interfaces
  • First contact person for internal and external stakeholders in administrative and organisational matters
  • Organisation and conduct of team meetings

Requirements

  • Basic training as an MPA and/or additional training as a medical secretary with further training as a practice manager or practice coordinator
  • Professional experience in health care, ideally in a comparable function
  • Structured, solution-oriented and independent working method
  • Leader with assertiveness, high social skills and respectful, transparent communication
  • Trustworthy, friendly and service-oriented personality with a high level of service towards patients as well as internal and external stakeholders
  • Sound knowledge of medical terminology and hospital procedures
  • Very good IT user knowledge and a high affinity for digital work processes
  • Very good and stylish knowledge of German as well as good knowledge of English

We offer

  • 40 hourly week
  • Attractive social benefits and numerous benefits
  • Team spirit
  • Cheap food in the staff restaurant
  • ZVV BonusPass / ZVV BonusPass Flex

The Schulthess Clinic in Zurich is one of the leading orthopaedic clinics in Europe with over 1,100 employees. Our central concern is to free people from their pain and restore their mobility. As a specialist clinic, we focus on surgical orthopedics, neurology, rheumatology and sports medicine. Schulthess Clinic is Swiss Olympic Medical Center.
