Full description

In order to be able to manage investments in our water supply facilities in a long-term, comprehensible and risk-oriented manner, the Zurich Water Supply Company is currently establishing a technical and financial basis for decision-making for the production facilities. The aim is to systematically assess the condition, the default risk and the future need for renewal of the plants and to derive sound investment recommendations from this.

Your tasks may include the following activities

  • Conduct literature searches to evaluate various methods and approaches for technical asset management.
  • Examination of the applicability and suitability of identified concepts for the specific context of water supply.
  • Development and further development of models for lifecycle consideration or for decision-making processes using Python (Visual Studio Code) and AzureDevOps.
  • Data evaluation, processing and visual processing of results using Power BI.
  • Active participation in meetings and working groups, including presentation of interim results and associated logging.
  • Documentation of the work steps of your development and results to ensure traceability.
  • Visit water supply facilities to link the theoretical models to the practical infrastructure on site.
  • Completed or almost completed studies (Bachelor/Master) in engineering, natural sciences or mathematics
  • Analytical thinking and a structured way of working
  • Working independently, open-minded and motivated personality
  • Readiness to familiarise yourself with new tools
  • Good oral and written expression in German

...an exciting insight into Switzerland’s largest water supplier and a varied and meaningful activity. You get the opportunity for personal and professional development and accompany various development and change processes. We offer you modern working conditions in the Hardhof (Zurich-Altstetten), in a green environment near Werdinsel with excellent public transport connections. The internship wage is calculated according to your education and the city requirements.

The Zurich water supply supplies the city and numerous municipalities in the region with fresh, clean drinking water around the clock. We fulfill this task in the service of the public with around 300 dedicated employees. Together with us, shape the quality of life of the city and region of Zurich and become part of our team.
