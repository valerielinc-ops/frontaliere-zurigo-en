Full description

Child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy (KJPP), Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK)

Job opening 1.1.2027, temporary

The research project VALPSY, funded by the Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF), aims to validate the German and French versions of the KSADS-COMP. This is a standardized, web-based diagnostic tool for detecting mental disorders in children and adolescents based on the DSM-5 criteria. As part of this international multicentre study, 500 adolescents and their parents will be included from clinical and non-clinical samples to improve psychiatric diagnosis.

We offer

Working in a team with clinicians Together with a research team in Zurich, Lausanne, France and Germany Possibility to gain experience in new diagnostic formats Interesting further and further training opportunities Duration: Temporary: 1.1.2027 - 1.7.2027, with extension option Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich Child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy Neumünsteralle 9 8008 Zurich The Clinic for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Psychotherapy (KJPP) is one of the most traditional institutions for child and adolescent psychiatry and is engaged as a university clinic both clinically and in research, teaching, training and further education.

Tasks

  • Recruitment of children, adolescents and their parents in clinical and non-clinical setting.
  • Communication with the participating families as well as coordination and support throughout the course of the study.
  • Organization of electronic consent and digital data collection through Veeva, REDCap and KSADS-COMP.
  • Checking the completeness of the questionnaires, sending reminders and coordinating the examination dates.
  • Conducting standardized, computer-aided diagnostic interviews with adolescents and their parents, on site or via video conference.
  • Combination of the information of the young people and their parents in the context of a final consensus assessment by means of KSADS-COMP.
  • Study documentation, data management and quality assurance according to study protocol.
  • Active cooperation with national and international research partners.

Profile

  • Master Degree Psychology with emphasis on Clinical Psychology
  • Knowledge of psychopathology of childhood and adolescence
  • Experience working with children and adolescents (internship)
  • Very good communication skills
  • Open and open personality.
