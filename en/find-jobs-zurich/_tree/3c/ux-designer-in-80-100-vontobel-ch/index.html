Full description

Are you ready to shape the digital future and actively improve the experience of our customers? As part of our Digital Client Experience (DCX) team, you will significantly shape how our private customers interact on digital channels. We are passionate about understanding our customers’ needs and creating intuitive UX and UI designs that excite them – whether in e-banking, the digital account opening app or our digital meeting advisory solution. Consulting solution for customer consultants.

If you want to use your know-how and make a difference, we look forward to your application!

What is the content of the role?

  • Together with UI Design, Development and Product Management, you will develop innovative, user-centred UX concepts for our digital channels in private banking.
  • You develop UX concepts from the first ideation to the creation and testing of prototypes and wireframes up to the implementation of the final product.
  • You’ll conduct user research, analyze customer feedback and data to gain deeper insight into our customers’ needs.
  • With your expertise, you establish UX standards and actively bring best practices and new approaches to the team.

What are the requirements?

  • You have completed studies or training in User Experience, Interaction Design, Human-Computer Interaction or a related field.
  • At least 3 years of professional experience in UX design, ideally with a focus on the design of complex applications.
  • Very good knowledge of using Figma to create interactive concepts, prototypes and designs.
  • Practical experience in the use of AI tools for research, ideation, prototyping, testing or design.
  • Proactive, solution-oriented working method and a high degree of initiative.
  • Experience in the financial industry is an advantage.
  • Very good German and English skills are a must.
  • Affinity for Artificial Intelligence (AI) and willingness to work with AI tools.

What do we offer?

  • Top location in Zurich and free food.
  • Friendly, diverse team in an open office.
  • Agile working environment – in our thinking, working methods and mutual trust.
  • Flat hierarchies with cooperation at all levels.
  • Campus atmosphere and a strong team spirit.

We are looking for talented people and not just a CV that meets all the criteria. Even if you don’t bring everything we ask of you, you should apply.

Place of work The position is currently based in Zurich. In 2030, the workplace will be moved to Baar in order to further strengthen cooperation with key stakeholders in the business.
