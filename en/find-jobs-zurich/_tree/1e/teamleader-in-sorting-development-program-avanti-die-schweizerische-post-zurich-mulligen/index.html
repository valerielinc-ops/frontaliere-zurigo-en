Full description

Do you want to take on responsibility, lead employees and feel the pulse of logistics? For our “Avanti” development program in the Zurich-Bullén Briefzentrum, we are looking for committed makers and forward-thinkers who want to take on management tasks with passion and passion. With you, our logistics heart beats faster.

You can do that.

  • In this role, you will get to know all the work areas of the Zurich-Mullen Letter Centre for 12 months.
  • Build your knowledge step-by-step by taking over tasks from incoming goods to sorting and picking to outgoing goods.
  • In addition, you will monitor the correct processes and technical facilities in the control room, get to know the day-to-day operations and experience the sorting center from different perspectives.
  • You get the chance to network internally through your various assignments and to understand the overall processes of a sorting center.
  • Take advantage of the opportunity to gain cross-divisional operational and leadership experience while a member of the management accompanies and promotes you.
  • After successful completion, a wide range of development opportunities open up – whether in team or sub-management. Training is the springboard for your logistics career.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed your basic education and preferably have a further education (professional certificate).
  • Ideally, you have several years of professional experience and want to develop further in the field of leadership.
  • A strong affinity for numbers as well as interest in the logistics industry and organizationally demanding tasks set you apart.
  • You are open to people and change, have a winning, self-confident and solution-oriented personality and like to take on responsibility and leadership tasks.
  • In hectic situations, you keep a cool head and show both physical and psychological resilience.
  • You are ready for a weekly changing 2-shift operation (early shift 06:00-15:00 / late shift 14:05-23:00) including weekend.
  • You communicate verbally and in writing very well in German.
