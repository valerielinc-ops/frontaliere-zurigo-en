Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Secondary School and Vocational Education Office HMS Practitioner/in Enterprise Education 100% RELIABLE, SOLUTIONORIENTED AND FLEXIBEL The Secondary School and Vocational Education Office of the Canton of Zurich is committed to the secondary schools, the vocational colleges, the providers of vocational preparation years and the teaching companies in the interest of youth and business. For the school year 2027/2028 starting on 15 August 2027, an internship is to be filled again in the secondary school and vocational training office. During your internship year, you will get an insight into various tasks of the secondary school and vocational training office. Together with your team, you are responsible for the administrative matters relating to basic in-company training: You collect, control and mutate teaching contract data and provide telephone information to learners, parents, vocational trainers from teaching companies, ÜK providers and vocational schools. In addition, you support post processing, organize events for the team and department, create minutes and presentations. In addition, you work daily in the department of development, where you work in various projects and campaigns and thus get to know the marketing area of the MBA. You will get an insight into other specialist areas in order to experience the cantonal administration. You are in HMS training and are interested in commercial activity in cantonal administration. They have a good knowledge of German in word and writing, are IT-affine and experienced in dealing with Microsoft Office programs. They think networked and act solution-oriented, have a quick apprehension and enjoy working independently as well as in the team. In customer conversations, you score points through your calm and self-confident nature. Furthermore, you convince with a well-kept appearance as well as open, friendly and pleasant manners.
