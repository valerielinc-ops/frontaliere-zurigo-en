OT Zurich: Birgistrasse 7, 8304 Wallisellen, Switzerland Main tasks

  • Assessment of the repair potential of existing elevator installations
  • Starting and technical assessment of necessary on-site repair work
  • Calculation, production and sale of repair quotes
  • Obtaining and coordinating offers from third-party suppliers regarding conditions and dates
  • Technical advice to our customers as well as sales and price negotiations
  • Organisation of internal and external material orders
  • Planning and coordination of repair operations
  • Close cooperation with the service department to increase customer satisfaction and further develop existing customer relationships
  • Support of the service manager in obtaining repair contracts
  • Determination of the required spare parts and calculation of the corresponding repair work

What we expect from you

  • Completed technical training, for example as an electrical installer, electromechanic or in a comparable technical profession and/or sales-oriented education or training
  • Several years of professional experience in the elevator industry, ideally combined with experience in technical sales is an advantage
  • Good knowledge of relevant standards and regulations in the field of lift technology and repairs
  • Strong customer and sales orientation as well as enjoyment of technical advice
  • Safe handling of Microsoft Office
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Category B driving licence

  • Smartphone and business vehicle (car)

  • 5 weeks of holidays and an additional 5 days of holiday in the old year week
