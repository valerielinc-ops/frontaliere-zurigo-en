You want to shape your future career in Switzerland, but restrictions on admission and lack of entry opportunities make it difficult to move from Germany? At the same time, are you looking for a modern dermatological practice in which you can be clinically active right from the start and gain valuable experience? Then this position may offer you exactly the entry you are looking for. For a modern dermatological practice in the Canton of Zurich, we are looking for reinforcement from a specialist in dermatology and venerology. The practice belongs to an established specialist group with several specialized locations and offers the full spectrum of modern dermatology. You benefit from professional structures, a modern infrastructure and an experienced team that supports you in entering the Swiss healthcare system. Especially for candidates from Germany, there is a rare opportunity here: The direct change to the ambulatory care is possible because the company has the necessary training authority.

  • Applications from Germany are expressly welcome. We accompany you through the entire process of recognition and relocation

  • Fixed salary from CHF 192,000 per year plus revenue share

  • Further education opportunities in conservative, operational, aesthetic and laser-based dermatology
  • Long-term perspective in a growing company
