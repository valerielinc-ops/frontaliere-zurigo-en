More about this search for cross-border applicants

Bellinzona, the capital of the canton of Ticino, currently lists around 60 hospital job openings across public and private healthcare providers. Positions span nursing, medical specializations, allied health, administrative and technical roles, reflecting demand driven by an aging population and steady investment in regional healthcare infrastructure. Employers range from the main cantonal hospital to smaller clinics and long-term care facilities serving the Bellinzona district and surrounding valleys. Candidates with Swiss-recognized qualifications, Italian nursing diplomas validated by the Red Cross, or relevant clinical experience typically have the strongest prospects. Cross-border applicants should be aware of G permit quotas, language expectations in Italian, and the practical commute via the A2 and A13 motorways from Como, Varese or the Lugano area.
