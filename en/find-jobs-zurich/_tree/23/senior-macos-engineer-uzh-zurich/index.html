Full description

Central IT

Start date: immediately, permanent position Central IT is the IT competence center of the University of Zurich. The Managed Device Services team is responsible for managed workplaces for Windows, macOS and mobile devices, based on Microsoft Intune. For our rapidly growing Mac fleet, we are looking for an experienced professional to shape and develop the macOS platform. We offer flexible working hours, working from home and the employment conditions of the Canton of Zurich.

Responsibilities

  • Technical responsibility for the macOS platform within the Managed Device Services team, from architecture and operations to further development based on Microsoft Intune
  • Further development of zero-touch deployment with Apple Business Manager and Automated Device Enrollment, including Setup Assistant, the enrollment process and the user onboarding experience
  • Preparation of technical concepts and decision-making documents for management and committees; assessment of new macOS versions and Apple features with regard to operations and security; and representation of the macOS perspective in cross-functional Central IT projects
  • Design and operation of Platform SSO with Entra ID (Secure Enclave), the Microsoft Enterprise SSO Plug-in, and compliance and conditional access scenarios for Macs
  • Hardening of Mac clients based on the Open Intune Baseline for macOS, the macOS Security Compliance Project (mSCP) and the CIS Benchmarks, including FileVault with key escrow, firewall, Gatekeeper and PPPC
  • Introduction and expansion of Declarative Device Management, particularly for controlled distribution of macOS updates and upgrades
  • Operation and further development of Microsoft Defender for Endpoint on macOS in collaboration with the Security team
  • Responsibility for application deployment and patch management on macOS with Munki, Installomator and AutoPkg, supplemented by Intune apps and custom packaging where required by an application
  • Automation of client management processes with shell and Python scripts, the Microsoft Graph API and n8n
  • Handling complex macOS cases as the highest technical escalation level and transferring knowledge to Support and the team, including coaching junior team members and apprentices
  • Preparation of technical concepts, documentation and decision-making documents, as well as assessment of new macOS versions and Apple features with regard to operations

Profile

  • Completed higher education in IT (HF, FH) or a comparable qualification, combined with several years of experience in a senior Apple endpoint management role
  • Extensive, proven experience managing large Mac fleets in a corporate or higher education environment with Microsoft Intune, including architectural decisions, operating at a scale of several hundred to over a thousand devices, and providing technical leadership on macOS topics with the team, Security and management
  • Very good knowledge of Apple Business Manager, Automated Device Enrollment, configuration profiles, Declarative Device Management and macOS security mechanisms (FileVault, SIP, TCC/PPPC, Gatekeeper, notarization)
  • Proven experience designing and implementing Platform SSO with Entra ID, as well as building and operating hardened baselines such as the Open Intune Baseline, mSCP or CIS, including managing exceptions and trade-offs between security and usability
  • Several years of experience building and operating an automated software and patch pipeline on macOS with Munki, Installomator and AutoPkg, as well as packaging and signing PKG installers
  • Very good knowledge of zsh/bash, good knowledge of Python, and experience using the Microsoft Graph API to automate client management processes
  • Experience with Microsoft Defender for Endpoint on macOS, privileged access management on clients (e.g. Admin By Request), tools such as swiftDialog, Nudge or Escrow Buddy, workflow automation with n8n, and an Apple certification (e.g. Apple Certified IT Professional) are an advantage
  • Independent, structured and quality-oriented approach to work, enthusiasm for leading technical topics and sharing knowledge, and excellent communication skills with both specialists and end users
  • Very good German skills and good English skills
