Full description

Enabling new ideas, bringing partners together and jointly promoting net zero: This is what we are committed to in the field of net zero and cooperation. We manage the KlimUp and cooperation programmes and develop new climate protection projects together with companies, organisations and the city administration. Here you have the opportunity to contribute your own ideas, to develop concrete projects out of common interests and to actively shape how the city of Zurich achieves its climate goals.

For this versatile task, we are looking for reinforcement for our team as of January 1, 2027 or by agreement. The job is limited to one year from the start of the job.

  • Together with the relevant actors and partners within and outside the city administration, you will develop projects that contribute to achieving the net-zero goals of the city of Zurich.
  • They participate in the further development of the funding offer cooperations and take over the deputy of the responsible project management.
  • You work in the KlimUp office, check funding applications and coordinate their processing. They help to assess the impact of the program and further develop the offer.
  • University or technical college degree related to the economy, environment, climate, sustainability or a related field of expertise.
  • Several years of experience in managing complex, cross-organisational projects as well as in establishing and implementing cooperations.
  • Knowledge of climate and sustainability, economic understanding and experience in communication and moderation. Knowledge of funding programmes and administrative processes is an advantage.
  • A communicative, negotiating and solution-oriented personality who thinks in a network, quickly learns about new topics and sets priorities independently.

You can expect a versatile task in which you can contribute your own ideas, help shape cooperation and take responsibility for established funding offers. You have room for manoeuvre and work in a dedicated team. To this end, we offer flexible working hours, attractive employment conditions and a wide range of development and further training opportunities.

We are courageously shaping the future. Competent. Together. Effective. For the environment. For the climate. For health.

The Environmental and Health Protection Zurich (UGZ) is committed to a climate-neutral city, a healthy urban environment, a healthy urban nature and effective resource protection.
