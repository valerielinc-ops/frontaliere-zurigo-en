Full description

The Judicial Commission of the Cantonal Council is looking for two secondary commercial judges for the term of office 2025-2031 In this office, you participate in the judicial function in the handling of legal disputes that fall under the jurisdiction of the Commercial Court. The Zurich Commercial Court is usually composed of two members of the Higher Court and three commercial judges to deal with individual legal cases. It shall be chaired by a senior judge. As a commercial judge, you bring your expertise from your full-time job. The Judicial Commission is looking for commercial judges as specialists with specialised knowledge and experience in the following areas:

  • Chamber 1 (Banks and Insurance) : liability law, in particular in the areas of personal injury, medical liability, health law and insurance law.
  • Chamber 10 (different branches): Real estate, in particular the purchase and sale of commercial real estate, as well as brokerage contracts. Commercial judges are elected by the Cantonal Council. Only persons who are entitled to vote in cantonal matters can be elected. According to the resolution of the Intergroup Conference, the retirement age is 70 years. It is a part-time job. The workload varies depending on the annual number of cases and the size of the cases. Meeting allowances shall be paid. Information on the Commercial Court can be found on the Internet at http://www.gerichte-zh.ch/organisation/handelsgericht.html . Further information is provided by Nathalie Malinowski, Secretary of the Judiciary Commission, on phone 043 259 20 67. Interested? Then we look forward to your electronic application with the usual documents as well as a current criminal record and a confirmation of residence (both not older than three months) until 11. December 2026. Judicial Commission of the Cantonal Council
