Put to the Point

In the RobustObst project, you develop new methods for varietal testing in pome and stone fruit at the interface between laboratory and field. The focus is on assessing resistance to disease. You will also develop efficient data management from data collection to publication of results.

Good food, healthy environment

Agroscope is the federal competence centre for research in the agricultural and food industry. The researchers carry out their activities at various locations in Switzerland. The headquarters is Bern-Liebefeld (from 2026: Posieux FR). Agroscope is located at Eidg. Department of Economy, Education and Research WBF.

We offer this

We are committed to the success model Switzerland and work for the benefit of the population. Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring different perspectives. We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Additional information

The research group Extension Obstbau develops cultivation and plant protection strategies for an environmentally friendly and economical production of quality fruit and examines fruit varieties with regard to robustness and cultivation suitability under Swiss conditions. It fulfils an important function as a bridge-builder between basic research and fruit cultivation practice.
