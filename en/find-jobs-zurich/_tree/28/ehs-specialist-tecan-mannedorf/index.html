EHS Specialist / Safety Officer 80-100%

Männedorf & Nänikon, Zurich

Your contribution

For our locations in Switzerland, we are looking for a committed and competent individual as an EHS Specialist / Safety Officer. In this varied role, you will play a central part in implementing and continuously developing our EHS Environment, Health & Safety organization, the EHS management system (ISO 14001 and 45001), and the related processes, helping to create a safe, healthy and sustainable workplace.

  • Responsibility for or coordination of occupational safety, health protection, hazardous substances, the environment and emergency organization
  • Responsibility for the further development of the EHS management system (ISO 14001 / ISO 45001) in compliance with corporate guidelines and legal requirements.
  • Ensuring compliance with legal, regulatory and internal requirements in close cooperation with authorities, specialist bodies and service providers
  • Advising, supporting and training site management, managers and employees in all EHS areas
  • Conducting and coordinating hazard assessments, risk analyses, audits and safety inspections
  • Analysis of accidents, incidents and near misses, as well as defining and following up on improvement measures
  • Development and monitoring of EHS performance indicators and management reports

Your profile

We are not only looking for expertise: we are looking for people who put their passion and talent to use with us.

  • Technical or scientific training with further education, as well as a qualification as an ASGS Specialist or ASGS Expert
  • Several years of experience in EHS / occupational safety, ideally in an industrial company
  • Solid experience in establishing, further developing and auditing management systems in accordance with ISO 14001 and ISO 45001
  • Qualifications as a fire safety officer, chemicals contact person and dangerous goods officer
  • Very good knowledge of Swiss legislation and its application
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working style
  • Assertiveness combined with strong consulting and communication skills
  • A high degree of personal responsibility and resilience combined with a pragmatic and solution-oriented working style.
  • Fluent German and English skills
