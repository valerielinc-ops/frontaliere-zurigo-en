Full description

Doing the right thing. In Switzerland and in around 20 countries on four continents. Engage with us for the vision of a solidarity society, as Caritas Switzerland has been running asylum centres for families, individuals and unaccompanied minors (MNA) at five locations in the Canton of Zurich since March 2024 on behalf of the Cantonal Social Office. For the Department of Health in the MNA area, we are looking for a committed and experienced nurse FH for the combination of cross-location professional management and operational cooperation as a qualified nurse. In this role, you are responsible for the professional development of the health sector and at the same time ensure the age-appropriate medical care and basic health care of minor asylum seekers. We offer you a varied job in a motivated team, modern work equipment and regular working hours from Monday to Friday during the usual office hours. Their tasks

  • Cross-location professional management and support of employees as well as central contact for the KSA
  • Ensuring compliance with cantonal and internal requirements and participating in the strategic development of processes, concepts and working instruments in coordination with other specialist areas
  • Responsibility for personnel and deployment planning, participation in recruitment and other personnel processes
  • Planning and conducting cross-site meetings, case meetings and professional exchange
  • Ensure appropriate use of human and material resources and regular visits to the centres
  • Medical advice, triage, care and intervention for physical and mental health problems of adolescents
  • Organization and coordination of medical care in cooperation with doctors, therapists and external specialists, including vaccinations according to the Swiss vaccination plan
  • Responsibility for health administration and documentation, in particular incoming and exit interviews, tutorials, cost control and medical cost language
  • Preventive health education on nutrition, hygiene, sexuality and addictive substances as well as professional guidance of employees in health issues and medical emergencies

Our requirements

  • Completed training as a qualified nurse FH
  • Advanced training in health or management
  • At least three years of professional experience in the medical environment
  • Experience in migration and care of adults/children and/or MNA
  • Very good knowledge of German and good oral knowledge of English
  • Strong leadership and communication skills
  • Comprehensive knowledge of the relevant requirements of the KSA and Caritas Switzerland in the field of health
  • Ability and resilience to work in a dynamic environment
  • Very good computer user knowledge (especially MS-Office)
  • Self-employment and a high degree of work organisation as well as resilience and flexibility
  • Professional delineation of client cases and personal delineation of workload

The place of work is Zurich. Start of work: by agreement Caritas offers attractive employment conditions and an open working atmosphere at the pulse of social issues. For the transformation towards role-based work, we count on employees with a digital and agile mindset who like to participate in change processes and take responsibility. Information on this position can be provided by Mrs. Rahel Lampart, Head of Team and Department Health, telephone 076 346 04 65. We thank you for your interest and look forward to your application via the online portal. Caritas Switzerland, Adligenswilerstrasse 15, 6002 Lucerne www.caritas.ch
