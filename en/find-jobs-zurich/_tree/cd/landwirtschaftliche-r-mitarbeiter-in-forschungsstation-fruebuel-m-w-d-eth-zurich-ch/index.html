Agricultural:r Employees:in research station Früebüel (m/f/d)

80%-100%, Zurich, temporary

ETH Zurich is one of the world's leading universities for natural sciences and technology. The Institute of Agricultural Sciences in the Department of Environmental Systems Sciences (D-USYS) deals with animal husbandry, plant sciences and economics in the global and Swiss context of agriculture and carries out relevant basic and application-oriented research.

We are looking for a dedicated person (workload 80-100%) with pleasure in versatile agricultural work in a research-oriented environment. This position is to be filled from 01.01.2027 or by agreement. It shall be limited to an initial period of 12 months; there is a possibility that the post will be changed to permanent employment in the short to medium term.

Job description

Its main tasks include animal husbandry, fodder farming, landscape management and machine maintenance on the farm Früebüel. You document your work and your assessments of animal health electronically. They work closely together in a team of agricultural employees as well as the manager and with researchers and develop solutions for challenges in the team. With your skill and attentive eye, you contribute to the optimal care and well-being of the animals as well as to the care and maintenance of the machines and plants.
