  • Are you looking for a representative, fully furnished practice in the Zurich Unterland (20 km to Zurich, 8.5 km to Baden)?

By immediate or by arrangement I rent my bright, fully equipped practice for sublet for 2 to 3 fixed days of the week (days by arrangement)
