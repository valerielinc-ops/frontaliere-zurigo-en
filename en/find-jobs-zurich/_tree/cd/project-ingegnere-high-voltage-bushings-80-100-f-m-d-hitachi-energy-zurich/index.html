Full description

The opportunity

Are you passionate about driving innovation and optimizing technical processes? As a Senior Project Engineer in our Insulation & Components division, you'll play a key role in enhancing the performance and efficiency of high-voltage transformer bushings. This is your chance to work with a global leader in electrical insulation technology, collaborating with cross-functional teams and contributing to cutting-edge industrial solutions. If you thrive in a structured, solution-oriented environment and enjoy leading impactful projects, this role offers a dynamic and rewarding career path.

How you'll make an impact

  • Lead and coordinate internal projects focused on product and process optimization in the high-voltage bushings segment
  • Analyze existing designs and manufacturing processes with a focus on efficiency, quality, and cost
  • Identify areas for improvement and implement corresponding measures
  • Collaborate closely with design, manufacturing, quality, and other departments
  • Prepare technical documentation, monitor progress, and regularly report to management
  • Support the industrialization of new technologies and materials

Your background

  • Completed technical degree (Mechanical Engineering, Mechatronics, Electrical Engineering, or similar) or equivalent qualification
  • Professional experience in managing technical (internal) development or improvement projects
  • Solid mechanical knowledge, ideally in the field of electrical insulators or high-voltage components
  • Experience with Lean methods, Six Sigma, or similar improvement approaches is a plus
  • Structured, solution-oriented working style and strong communication skills
  • Excellent German and English skills, both written and spoken
