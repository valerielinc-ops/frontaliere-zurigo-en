Full description

The Zurich Cantonal Police, with its approximately 4000 employees, assumes responsibility for security in the canton. In order to be able to perform this task reliably, we depend on a state-of-the-art and perfectly functioning technical infrastructure. To ensure smooth and safe operation and to strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed and qualified specialist as an industrial electrician / system technician 100% Your area of responsibility

  • You will be responsible for the professional maintenance and servicing of all electrical and network systems
  • You will carry out installation, inspection and testing tasks and prepare the necessary measurement and test protocols
  • You will be responsible for fault analysis and troubleshooting of electrical installations, building services networks, hardware and fieldbus systems
  • You will operate and monitor network and building management systems
  • You will accompany construction and expansion projects in the field of high- and low-voltage systems (electrical, IT, network) from planning to acceptance
  • You will look after external and maintenance companies and coordinate their assignments
  • You will take over the on-call service for the technical systems, including troubleshooting or calling in third-party contractors.
  • You have completed vocational training as an electrician EFZ or equivalent and have an advantage further training as an electrical safety consultant with a federal. FA
  • You have several years of professional experience and must have the company electrician's licence in accordance with Art. 13 NIV or the necessary requirements
  • You have in-depth knowledge in the areas of electrical installation, building automation, network technology and fieldbus systems (e.g. KNX, Modbus)
  • You are a conscientious, resilient and independent personality
  • The willingness to take over the on-call duty is required, incl. driving licence Cat. B
  • User knowledge of MS Office is required
  • Impeccable reputation: What you can look forward to
  • We offer you a varied and meaningful job in a dynamic environment
  • A centrally located, state-of-the-art workplace in Zurich's Police and Justice Center, including a staff restaurant and access to the in-house fitness center
  • Attractive employment conditions with a secure employer
  • The chance to make an impact in an exciting new challenge and to shape the future together 1.
