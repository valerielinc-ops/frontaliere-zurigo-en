Investment Data Solution Engineer (m/f/d) — Zurich Insurance

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

137466 | 80 - 100% | Oerlikon, Zurich | Hybrid

Are you fascinated by the world of data and its analysis? Do you want to develop innovative solutions that take investment data & analytics to the next level?

At Zurich Invest AG (ZIAG), you will strengthen the Investment Data & Analytics team in the Investment Technology & Innovation division. In this role, you will actively shape the ZIAG data landscape, develop modern data products and BI solutions together with our data engineers and thus contribute to enabling data-driven decisions and sustainably supporting the business success of ZIAG.

You will become part of a dynamic team responsible for managing ZIAG’s investment data throughout its entire lifecycle – from connecting to our source systems and building scalable data pipelines to delivering insights using modern BI solutions.

If you are excited about the development of data products, appreciate the direct exchange with our business units and want to promote the implementation of their data needs in a structured manner, then this is the ideal opportunity for you.

This is what awaits you

  • You are the point of contact for defined ZIAG business units: You take up their data needs and develop customized data and BI solutions to optimally support the business.
  • In collaboration with our Data Engineers in Barcelona, you build high-performance data pipelines and a scalable data platform.
  • You implement and monitor automated data quality checks and monitoring for data loads.
  • You contribute to the continuous development of the ZIAG data landscape and identify new technologies and trends in data, BI and AI.

This sets you apart

  • University degree in a relevant field such as data science, computer science, economics or a related field.
  • First practical experience in the management of data products, ideally in the investment sector.
  • Strong ability to analyse complex relationships, develop viable solutions and implement them in projects.
  • Knowledge of at least one programming language such as Python or R; experience with modern BI tools (e.g. Power BI) and AI tools are beneficial.
  • Strong communication skills in German and English and enjoy working together in a dynamic, interdisciplinary team environment.
