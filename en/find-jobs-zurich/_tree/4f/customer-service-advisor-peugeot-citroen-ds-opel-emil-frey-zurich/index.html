What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family company with over 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessories as well as the provision of financial services. Emil Frey AG Zurich-Nord, the legendary blue garage, shines as a new building. We invite you to be part of it. Together we want to further develop the services around the automobile. With our 21 brands - Abarth, Alfa Romeo, Citroën, Defender Discovery, DS, Fiat, Jaguar, Jeep, Kia, Leapmotor, Lexus, Mitsubishi, Opel, Peugeot, Range Rover, Subaru, Suzuki, Toyota, Volvo and Zeekr - we offer our customers the largest range of brands in and around Zurich. Our goal is to always put our customers at the center of our thinking and actions. Their tasks
