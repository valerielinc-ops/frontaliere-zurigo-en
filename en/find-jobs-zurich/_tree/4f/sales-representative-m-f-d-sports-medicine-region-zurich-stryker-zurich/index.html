Full description

Work Flexibility: Field-based

This is what awaits you

  • Take over the sales activities of our innovative sports medicine products after an intensive training phase.
  • Responsibility for the operational and strategic management of your sales area in the greater Zurich area.
  • Conduct targeted new customer acquisition and support of existing customers with regard to the application of our products.
  • Implement marketing and sales strategies in collaboration with management, based on your market analysis.
  • conduct negotiations with surgical management, chief physicians, clinic administrations and purchasing departments.
  • Development and preparation of demand-oriented customer orders as well as ensuring timely order processing to ensure high customer satisfaction.
  • Advice and training of medical and nursing staff in the application of our products, e.g. in the operating room or in workshops.
  • Attend internal meetings to expand your expertise, as well as trade shows, congresses and business meetings to identify potential new customers and strengthen existing customer relationships.

This is what distinguishes you

Required

  • At least 2 years of sales experience of complex products in need of explanation and/or industry knowledge in medical technology, e.g. as surgical assistance (OTA) or comparable activities within the industry.
  • Communication skills, creativity and flexibility.
  • Entrepreneurial and strategic thinking, solution-orientedness, excellent organizational ability.
  • Possession of a valid car driving licence.
  • Negotiation-proof German and good English skills.

Preferably

  • Completed studies in medical engineering, engineering or economics.
  • Experience in building long-term sustainable customer relationships and generating new business.
  • Experience in the sovereign handling of the C-Suite.
  • Knowledge of the Swiss health and hospital market.

Additional information

  • Travel with your company car in the sales area can be up to 80%, including occasional external overnight stays.
  • Depending on the planning of your workweek, you are the remaining time in the home office.
  • Link to the product portfolio: Sports medicine
