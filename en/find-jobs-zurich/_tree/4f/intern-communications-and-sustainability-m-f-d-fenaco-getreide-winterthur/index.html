The Grain, Oilseeds, Animal Feed business unit of fenaco Genossenschaft is the most competent and capable partner in Switzerland for trading raw products used to produce food and animal feed. At the Winterthur location, we are looking for an intern in communications and sustainability to start immediately or by agreement.

Martin Preisig Head of HR, fenaco GOF +41 58 434 00 29

These tasks will inspire you

  • Planning and implementing internal and external communications (e.g. intranet, employee information, website, newsletter) in coordination with management and the business units
  • Ensuring a consistent, target-group-oriented and professional external presence while complying with CI/CD guidelines
  • Supporting the conceptualisation and creation of communication materials (e.g. presentations, brochures, flyers) in coordination with the business units
  • Coordination with internal and external service providers (Corporate Communications, agencies, graphic designers)
  • Planning and organising events with a focus on the MAXI Event 2027
  • Carrying out data collection for fLG's sustainability reporting

This is the profile we are looking for

  • Independent and proactive personality
  • Communicative and a team player
  • Quick learner
  • Good knowledge of French
  • Willingness to learn how to use various communication channels
  • Bachelor’s degree in communication, marketing or a comparable field of study
  • Knowledge of Swiss agriculture is an advantage
