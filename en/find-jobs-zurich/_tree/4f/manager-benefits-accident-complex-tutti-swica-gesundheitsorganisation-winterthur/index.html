Full description

We are committed to health – because health comes first. This principle is at the heart of our action, including as an employer. We are convinced that a healthy working environment is essential for the best talent to flourish and progress, both professionally and personally. And we are making every effort to make this conviction a reality. Here's how you contribute to health

  • You independently manage complex accident and occupational disease records in the area of compulsory accident insurance LAA
  • This, from the determination of entitlement to benefits up to the payment of insurance benefits (cash and in-kind benefits, including disability pensions, impotence allowances and integrity damages)
  • You make all the necessary clarifications and look for alternative solutions for our customers if needed
  • You are actively, goal-oriented and working in close collaboration with Care Management
  • You maintain close contact with our clients, doctors and other social insurers, particularly disability insurance
  • All your tasks will be done in French

What distinguishes you from others

  • You have commercial or equivalent training with medical knowledge.
  • You are interested in health and insurance
  • You have excellent knowledge of the French language, spoken and written (French is your mother tongue) and you also have good knowledge of German, spoken and written
  • You have an interactive reflection and intelligence with originality and insight
  • You have a flair to get to the bottom of things.
  • You are motivated and flexible. You work in a committed way and have a good sense of humor
  • You have a team spirit.

Why SWICA

  • We believe in benevolence. That is why we place openness, respect and trust at the heart of our professional daily life. We act, take responsibility and focus on solutions. Hand in hand, with our clients. That's why we've been number one customer satisfaction for years.
  • We trust you and encourage your autonomy. With annualised working time, at least five weeks of vacation and unpaid leave opportunities. With part-time models and extended teleworking possibilities when the job allows, to organize your daily life in a flexible way.
  • We estimate and reward your work – with a market-compliant salary, up to 1,500 francs of annual package available for personal projects and a generous contribution to the costs of basic insurance and supplementary insurance, for you and your family.
  • We offer you perspectives. With our generous continuing education program, we support your professional and personal development, in your current functions and for your future ambitions.
  • Because health comes first. With our company health management system and regional offers, we ensure your well-being. And in case of a health problem: 100% maintenance of the net salary.
  • We are thinking in the long run. With three savings plans, a deduction of coordination according to the occupancy rate and benefits well above the legal minimum, your provision is well insured.

Jessica Frauchiger, HR Recrutting, will be pleased to inform you.

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
