In a nutshell

The research area Agroecology and Environment investigates the effects of agriculture on various environmental dimensions: climate, air, soil, biodiversity, nutrient cycles and water. Based on the principles of agroecology and using real data, solutions for sustainable agriculture are developed. Networking between disciplines is essential in order to identify synergies and conflicting goals.

Good food, healthy environment

Agroscope is the federal government's centre of excellence for research in the agriculture and food industry. The researchers carry out their activities at various locations in Switzerland. Its headquarters are in Bern-Liebefeld (from 2026: Posieux FR). Agroscope is based at the Federal Department of Economic Affairs, Education and Research EAER. Additional information

The Climate and Agriculture research group researches options for reducing agricultural greenhouse gas emissions in the areas of soil, fertilisation and carbon sinks. It investigates the opportunities and risks of climate change for crop production and evaluates options for adaptation. In addition, it is responsible for Switzerland's agricultural greenhouse gas inventory.
