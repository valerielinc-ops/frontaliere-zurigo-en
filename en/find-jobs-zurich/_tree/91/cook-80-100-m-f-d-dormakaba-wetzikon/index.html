Full description

Become part of our team Do you love good food, think along and like to tackle yourself? Then become part of our team in the staff restaurant Wetzikon – immediately or by agreement, in the regulated daily service as chef (m/f/d) 80-100%. Your tasks

  • Together with the kitchen team, you are responsible for ensuring a smooth flow in the kitchen
  • Mise en Place and preparation of the various daily dishes in the warm & cold kitchen
  • Menu output during lunch service including checkout service
  • Share responsibility for ordering and stocks
  • At occasional events and evening events you bring your skills

Your profile

  • You are enthusiastic about a varied, seasonal cuisine with international influences and modern food concepts
  • You are curious, like to try new things and actively contribute your creativity to the further development of our offer
  • You plan ahead, set priorities and combine efficient kitchen processes with a high culinary standard
  • You work independently, think along and keep an overview even in lively moments
  • You enjoy direct contact with guests and contribute to a positive restaurant experience with your open nature
  • Fresh products, careful craftsmanship, hygiene and responsible handling of food and equipment are a matter of course for you
  • You have completed training as a chef or several years of practical experience in a kitchen with comparable quality standards
  • You are reliable, communicate openly and appreciate a collegial cooperation at eye level
  • You will certainly communicate in German. English skills are a welcome plus

What we offer

  • Regulated working hours from Monday to Friday between 06.00 and 16.00
  • No room hour – after work time is yours
  • Free on all holidays & weekends
  • A collaborative team that tackles together
  • Structured, personal training, attuned to your experience and strengths
  • Attractive employment conditions and versatile benefits
  • We promote your potential and support you specifically in professional training.
