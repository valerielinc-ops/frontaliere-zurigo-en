Full description

MANDATORY QUALIFICATIONS EU/CH CITIZENSHIP FLUENT IN GERMAN AND ENGLISH LANGUAGE

Your Responsibilities

  • Coordinating and managing room blocks as well as smaller events and meetings
  • Advising and supporting clients throughout the entire planning, execution, and post-event process
  • Preparing proposals, contracts, and event documentation
  • Handling short-notice group, room, and meeting space inquiries
  • Collaborating closely with internal departments to ensure smooth event operations
  • Taking responsibility for the accurate handover of groups and events to the Operations Team
  • Maintaining client relationships and actively promoting additional services and Marriott Bonvoy membership
  • Managing room allocations and event spaces in the relevant systems
  • Preparing invoices and ensuring compliance with internal policies and standards
  • Creating room layouts and event signage
  • Supporting the training and development of interns and apprentices

What You Bring

  • Initial experience in hospitality, food & beverage, event planning, or customer service is an advantage
  • Strong organizational skills and the ability to coordinate multiple tasks simultaneously
  • A customer-focused mindset with a strong service orientation
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, particularly PowerPoint
  • A structured, independent, and solution-oriented working style
  • Team spirit, flexibility, and a strong commitment to quality

What We Offer

  • A dynamic and young team of more than 200 talented professionals who know how to combine hard work with enjoyment
  • Internal training and development opportunities
  • An open and transparent company culture centered around performance, guest focus, and innovation
  • Fair and accurate working time recording
  • Attractive benefits from an international hotel group, including employee rates at over 10,000 hotels worldwide, significantly discounted stays at the Zurich Marriott Hotel, and much more
  • A permanent employment contract with opportunities for autonomy and personal responsibility
  • A diverse and exciting role within a fast-paced yet structured environment, supported by a progressive people-focused culture and the advantages of an international hospitality company.
