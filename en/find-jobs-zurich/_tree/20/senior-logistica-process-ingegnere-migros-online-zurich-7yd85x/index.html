Full description

Step into a pivotal role at Migros Online: take ownership of our end-to-end logistics processes and help optimise the backbone of Switzerland’s leading online supermarket.As the key interface between business, IT, and system & software partners, you drive impactful improvements, unlock efficiencies across the supply chain, and shape the future of scalable, high-performance e-grocery operations.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Online, Migros Online AG - Zurich, Limmatstrasse 152, 8005 Zurich

Key information

Please note that this role can also be based in Ecublens (VD) but requires regular travel to ZH. We welcome all applications, but can only consider applications submitted online. Applications submitted by post will be disposed of for data protection reasons and will not be returned. An application letter or video is required for this position.

Mansioni

  • In close collaboration with all stakeholders, you develop logistics process strategies and concepts using end-to-end approaches and actively contribute to their implementation.
  • You implement measures in a targeted manner, ensuring that existing logistics processes are improved, new processes are optimally implemented, and properly documented for future reference.
  • You analyze and understand the needs of the stakeholders and identify where improvements are necessary based on the available data.
  • You are responsible not only for the processes in day-to-day operations but also fully engage in strategic and cross-departmental projects, such as the development of new fulfillment centers.
  • This role involves the creation of tender documents as well as the analysis and development of business requirements including measurement and evaluation of deliverables against success metrics.
  • You will create, execute and track test cases to ensure thorough process performance and quality.
  • Collaboration with all interest groups is particularly important to you. You work closely with our product, data and IT organisation, customer service and our fulfillment centers.

Requisiti

  • 5+ years of professional experience for a Senior role: Supply Chain Management, Logistics, Process Engineering
  • Completed degree (university / university of applied science / higher technical college): Industrial Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Business Administration or similar - with a focus on Supply Chain Management and/or Logistics
  • English (Business fluent) German (Fluent) French (Good knowledge): Very good oral and written skills in German or French. English skills (at least C1 level) are mandatory.
  • Profound expertise in SCM, logistics, and in manual, semi-automated, + automated warehouse processes with a strong understanding of technology. Min. 5 years of professional experience.
  • Proficiency in process management, process engineering, requirements engineering and continuous improvement.
  • Deep domain expertise in at least one of the following areas: 1. warehouse automation, automated intralogistics, material flow systems, warehouse process design or 2. inbound logistics, supply & inventory planning, demand planning & forecasting
  • Experience in agile project management with an affinity for supply chain and automation projects and a strong hands-on mentality.
  • You find it easy to understand and unite different perspectives, grasp complex interrelationships, and explain them clearly.
  • Understanding of eCommerce, particularly the eGrocery market in Switzerland, is advantageous.
  • Willingness to visit other Migros Online locations on a regular basis (approximately 40% travel).

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We constantly check applications received, create a pre-selection and share it with the line manager for inspection. The decision as to whether the candidate has moved to the next step of the recruitment process or been eliminated is communicated by telephone or e-mail. Replies provided within three weeks
  • Interview with HR & specialist department: The interview is conducted virtually or on-site with the relevant person from HR and/or the specialist department. The main aim is to learn more about the personal and specialist skills of the candidate while giving both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit, both in the company as a whole and within the team. Reply provided: within three weeks
  • Technical interview with case presentation: Specific expertise is assessed by means of a case or other assessment tools. After processing, the result is presented to the line manager and discussed together. Usually, the team is represented at the presentation. Generally speaking, the interviews are conducted by the line manager or a representative from HR. Reply provided: alternative wording: within three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether or not someone fits into the team is an important factor for both the employer and the candidate when deciding whether to make an appointment. We therefore provide an opportunity for everyone to get to know each other in an informal atmosphere.
  • Reference information: When collecting references, we once again enquire about impressions, strengths and weaknesses and ask former line managers or colleagues to verify them. References can be collected after the first or second round and only with your consent. Reply provided: within one week
  • Offer & issuance of the contract (digital): Once our oral job offer has been issued, the key contractual points are checked once again. Once an agreement has been reached, the issuance of the contract is commissioned and sent by e-mail for digital signature. Reply provided: within one week
