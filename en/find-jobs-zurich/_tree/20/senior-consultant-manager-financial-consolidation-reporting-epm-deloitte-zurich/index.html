Full description

Location: Zurich Work Pattern Percentage: 80-100% Start date: Ongoing About the Role CFOs and finance teams are rethinking how they plan, close and report. In our Finance Transformation team in Zurich, you will help them do it by designing and implementing Enterprise Performance Management (EPM) solutions on leading platforms, with a focus on CCH Tagetik and OneStream. Finding the talent that makes Deloitte unique You will be the link between finance and technology: you understand how a group close, a budget cycle or a management reporting pack works, and you know how to turn those processes into a well-designed, scalable EPM solution. Depending on your seniority, you will lead workstreams or entire implementations, manage client relationships and coach junior team members. How you can make an impact

  • Lead the design and implementation of EPM solutions on CCH Tagetik and/or OneStream across the full project lifecycle: requirements, solution design, build, testing, data migration, go-live and hypercare
  • Run workshops with finance stakeholders (CFO office, group controlling, consolidation, FP&A) to capture requirements and translate them into functional and technical designs
  • Design application architecture: dimensions, data models, business rules, workflows, data integration from ERP and source systems (e.g. SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics, Infor M3) and reporting
  • Deliver solutions across EPM processes: financial consolidation and close, budgeting, planning and forecasting, management and statutory reporting, and related areas such as tax reporting
  • Lead and coach project team members, review their deliverables and ensure quality
  • Support EPM software selections, business cases and pre-sales activities, including demos and proposals
  • Contribute to growing our EPM practice: accelerators, methodologies and alliance relationships with software vendors What you bring to succeed
  • 4–7 years of experience in EPM, gained in consulting, at a software vendor or in an in-house finance systems team
  • Hands-on experience designing and implementing at least one of CCH Tagetik or OneStream in several end-to-end projects; experience with both is a strong plus
  • Solid understanding of finance processes behind EPM: consolidation, planning and forecasting, reporting
  • Experience leading workstreams, client workshops and project teams
  • Fluent English What sets you apart
  • Experience with other EPM platforms (e.g. Oracle EPM / HFM, SAP Group Reporting / BPC, Anaplan, Board, Jedox, Pigment)
  • Knowledge of group accounting under IFRS, US GAAP or Swiss GAAP FER (intercompany eliminations, currency translation, minorities)
  • Data integration and scripting skills (SQL, VB.NET for OneStream, Tagetik data processing and business rules)
  • Certifications in CCH Tagetik or OneStream
  • German (strongly preferred); French or Italian is a plus
  • Degree in Finance, Business Administration, Economics, Information Systems or a related field Your team You’ll join a team that brings together deep finance knowledge and technology expertise to deliver complex, high-profile EPM programmes for leading Swiss and international organisations. Your ideas will be valued from day one, and you’ll gain experience across platforms and industries with the support of Deloitte’s global network. Structured training and EPM certifications will help you build a clear career path while a flexible, hybrid working model gives you room to work at your best.
