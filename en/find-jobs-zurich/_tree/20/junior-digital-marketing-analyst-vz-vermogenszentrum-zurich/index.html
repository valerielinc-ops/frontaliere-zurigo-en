Full description

We advise individuals and companies on all matters relating to money, manage assets and develop attractive financial services. As we continue to grow strongly, we are strengthening our marketing team at our headquarters at Paradeplatz in Zurich.

Responsibilities

  • You assist with planning and implementing SEO and SEA measures.
  • You help create reports on the performance of our websites, search engines and campaigns.
  • You will learn how we measure VZ's visibility in AI-based search systems and support reporting.
  • You prepare data and analyses in an understandable way for the marketing team.
  • You contribute your ideas and help further develop our digital channels and content.
  • You contribute to analyses and projects in digital marketing and gradually take on your own responsibilities.

Requirements

  • First experience in digital marketing, for example through an internship, vocational training, studies or personal projects
  • Interest in data, digital trends, search engines and new developments related to AI
  • Enjoyment of working with numbers, a good understanding of interrelationships and an interest in further developing your analytical skills
  • Very good German language skills and a strong affinity for languages, French is a plus
  • Team spirit as well as a structured and reliable way of working

We offer

  • The opportunity to actively help shape current developments in digital marketing.
  • A motivated team that shares its expertise and supports you in your development.
  • Attractive employment conditions as well as opportunities to work from home.
