01 Future employer

An established Swiss company in Zürich is looking for an experienced Workplace Engineer. The position offers the opportunity to provide comprehensive support to regional customers while applying broad technical expertise. The company values solid IT training and offers attractive compensation for qualified professionals.

02 Your role

  • Support and further develop modern workplace environments with Windows 10/11, Microsoft Intune and Microsoft 365
  • Manage and configure end devices, software distribution and client management with Microsoft Endpoint Manager / Configuration Manager (SCCM)
  • Ensure stable and secure operation of clients, mobile devices and peripherals, including troubleshooting at 2nd-level support
  • Implement IT security policies, patch management and device configurations in line with modern standards
  • Contribute to the automation and optimization of workplace processes using PowerShell, Azure Automations and modern cloud technologies

03 Benefits

  • Contribution toward external training
  • Flat hierarchies
  • Flexible working hours
  • Guaranteed hybrid or remote work
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
  • Modern workplace
  • Discounts on public transport

04 Your profile

  • Completed training as a Computer Scientist EFZ in Platform Development or an equivalent IT qualification
  • Strong knowledge of Windows client operating systems, Microsoft 365, Microsoft Intune and Azure Active Directory / Entra ID
  • Experience in client management, software deployment, patch management, and analyzing and resolving technical issues
  • Good knowledge of PowerShell and an interest in automation, cloud solutions and modern workplace technologies
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented approach to work, with a strong customer and service orientation

05 Good to know

  • Flexible working hours model
  • Good employee benefits
  • Diverse IT projects for regional customers in Central Switzerland
  • Work in an established and growing technology environment
  • Short lines of communication and personal customer support
