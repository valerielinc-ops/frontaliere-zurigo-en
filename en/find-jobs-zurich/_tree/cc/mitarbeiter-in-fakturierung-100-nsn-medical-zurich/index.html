Full description

NSN medical services AG is a company of the NSN medical group and provides commercial services for the entire group of companies. The NSN medical Group successfully operates various innovative companies in the healthcare sector in Switzerland, including the Limmat Clinic in Zurich, the Centre for Integrative Oncology at the Zurich, Richterswil and Winterthur sites, and nnarose.ch, which operates in the field of anesthesia throughout Switzerland. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for an engaged person at the Taggenberg (Winterthur) site:

Employee/invoicing 100%

Your responsibilities

  • Timely and complete performance recording, performance control and billing of outpatient cases
  • Application of applicable tariffs, laws and contracts
  • Processing of internal concerns and correspondence with health insurance funds, patients and documentary doctors
  • Processing of rejections and inquiries by insurance companies and patients
  • Active participation in the team and assumption of holiday and absentee representations

Their competences

  • Completed training as medical/practical assistant, dipl. Nurse or specialist Health
  • Experience in health care and tariffs as well as in ambulatory and stationary benefit billing is advantageous
  • Flexible, resilient, responsible personality with positive charisma
  • Independent, exact and structured working method
  • Friendly, service-oriented treatment of patients and partners
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Knowledge of foreign languages is advantageous

Our offer

  • Versatile and varied area of responsibility in a dynamic and interesting company in the healthcare industry
  • Attractive employment package with five weeks holidays
  • Possibility for home office by arrangement
  • Freedoms in the organisation of working time
  • Free coffee and fruits for employees
  • Extensive education and training in the form of working time and/or cost sharing

For further information, Tanya Reinmann, Head of Service Management and Invoicing is at your disposal. We look forward to your online application.
