Specialist Security & Surveillance (100%)

Zurich, Olten | on site | Reference 8399

The Physical Security & Monitoring division makes an important contribution to achieving the objectives of real estate management as well as finance & services. It ensures that the physical security requirements at all Swiss locations are implemented in accordance with the requirements of corporate security. In doing so, it ensures an adequate level of protection for persons, property, buildings and infrastructure. The specialist specialist Security & Monitoring is responsible for the proper execution of the assigned tasks in the operational security area. The function supports the on-duty shift management in ensuring smooth and effective safety operations, implementing the assigned safety measures and ensuring compliance with all relevant processes, instructions and safety requirements in the assigned area of responsibility.

The position is to be filled from 1 April 2027 and has its main place of work in Zurich. A work assignment at the Olten site is required for around 15 weeks per year.

These are your tasks

  • Coverage of specialist tasks as Specialist Security & Surveillance in shift operations (early, late and night shifts, around the clock, 365 days a year) at the locations in Zurich and Olten
  • Compliance with and implementation of physical property protection safety regulations and guidelines
  • Receiving and processing all alarm and fault messages, including the technical piketting service
  • Conducting defined safety tours including reporting and event reporting
  • Initial intervention in medical emergencies within the framework of the occupational health organisation

You bring that with you

  • Sound professional experience in security and security
  • Strong social skills in dealing with internal and external customers
  • Team-oriented working methods and willingness to actively support colleagues
  • High reliability and punctuality
  • English skills are beneficial

• Readiness to work in 24/7 shift operation 365 days a year.
