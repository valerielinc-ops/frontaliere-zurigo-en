Full description

You ensure smooth processes around materials and supplies. The company offers you modern vehicles, short distances and a lot of personal responsibility. On behalf of our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Provision of material for repairs and assembly
  • Loading and unloading of trucks and assembly trailers
  • Responsibility for optimising warehouse processes
  • Control of the delivered material and recording of receipts in the ERP system
  • Implementation of the system-guided inventory including matching and correction of stock differences
  • Transport of materials on construction sites
  • Cooperation with purchasing for orders and delivery dates.

Requirements

  • Completed training in logistics, warehousing, materials management or equivalent, or relevant professional experience
  • Experience with goods management systems and system-managed inventory
  • Valid driving licence category B (mandatory)
  • Technical background, ideally with basic knowledge of locksmith or metalworking
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Willingness to take responsibility for storage processes

Offer

  • Varied tasks with scope for action and independent work
  • Further development opportunities
  • Modern infrastructure and well-equipped assembly vehicles
  • Family working environment, flat hierarchies and fast decision-making processes
  • Free employee parking
  • 40-hour week, free every second Friday
