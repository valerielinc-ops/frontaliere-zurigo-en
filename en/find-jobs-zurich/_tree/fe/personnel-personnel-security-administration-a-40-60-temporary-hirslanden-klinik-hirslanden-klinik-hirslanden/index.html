Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Composition by: 01. January 2027 or by agreement

Type of employment: Temporary until 30. April 2027

Reference number: 75597

As a security and administration clerk, you support the security specialist in all administrative, organizational and security-relevant tasks. You help ensure that our security, access and parking systems are professionally managed and make an important contribution to a safe and smooth clinical operation.

YOUR TASKS

  • You organize and lead the office management for facility management and security and support the specialist security administratively.
  • You create, maintain and manage correspondence, reports, templates and security, emergency and crisis documentation.
  • You manage the access and locking systems (Salto One) including badges, keys and permissions.
  • You manage the parking systems (Digital Parking / Skidata) as well as the mobility management (Urban Connect).
  • You manage the video surveillance system (DSS), process requests in the bus sector and support security-related events.
  • You take over and coordinate training and events

Your profile

  • You have a commercial or comparable education with administrative professional experience.
  • You have very good MS Office knowledge and ideally experience with databases and management systems.
  • You work in a structured, independent and reliable way.
  • You are discreet and responsible.
  • You are service-oriented and have organizational skills.
  • You have stylish knowledge of German in word and writing.
  • Further training in security or facility management is an advantage.

This is what we offer you.

  • A varied and responsible activity in the health environment.
  • Modern equipment and advanced systems.
  • An independent task area with a lot of personal responsibility.
  • Close cooperation with various specialist areas.
  • Attractive employment conditions and training opportunities.

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family responsibilities. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. With a reduced workload, the days off reduce accordingly. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from an equivalent financial compensation. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.
