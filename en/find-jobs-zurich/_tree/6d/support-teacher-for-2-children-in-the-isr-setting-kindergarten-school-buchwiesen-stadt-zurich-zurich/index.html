Full description

The School Buchwiesen is looking for reinforcement as of January 4, 2027!

Basic parameters for the site

  • Accompaniment of two children with special needs (cognitive impairments), who receive integrative training in the classroom
  • Pensum: 12 WL, approx. 43%
  • Working days: Wednesday, Thursday and Friday morning
  • a teaching diploma recognised in Switzerland for kindergarten and primary education
  • a diploma in curative curative education or complete it soon
  • Have experience at best with children with mental impairment
  • look forward to working in the integrated setting together with the class teacher and colleagues of the Pedagogical Team
  • Enjoy teaching with students from different cultures. Classroom management is easy for you
  • enjoy working with other teachers and specialists
  • are a positive, team-capable and resilient support teacher with a high level of social competence
  • a well-organized school that has been professionally run for many years
  • An open-minded, committed team. Accomplished, supportive cooperation in the pedagogical team
  • supplementary offers for teaching (stay in school, studios, assistants)
  • Educational KITS support and school management secretariat on site
  • free use of public transport and cultural offers with class (in the city of Zurich)
  • Workplace well accessible by public transport, lots of greenery in the area

We are a large school community in Zurich-Nord with 18 schools. We derive our pedagogical attitude from the concept of New Authority and thus meet the daily challenges in everyday school life together and effectively.

School time is life time! Under this motto, we design a living space inspiring for the children at the primary school Buchwiesen. Children's participation and participation are important pillars of our coexistence. With free and individualizing learning opportunities, we promote the natural learning pleasure of the children. As a QUIMS school for many years, we have great experience in our multicultural environment. We are a modern, innovative school with 16 primary classes, 6 kindergartens and 3 hoards.
