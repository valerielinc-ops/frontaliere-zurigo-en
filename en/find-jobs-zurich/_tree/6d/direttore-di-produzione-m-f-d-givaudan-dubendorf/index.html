As a production manager, you lead a team of about 6 employees and are responsible for the safe, efficient and timely implementation of production planning, shop floor administration and material supply. You ensure the reliable execution of production processes as well as the continuous improvement of existing, logistics and SAP processes.

You work at the site in Dübendorf and report directly to the Manufacturing Manager Liquids and Spray Dry.

Your tasks

Leadership & Development

  • Leadership, coaching and further development of a team of about 6 employees
  • Ensuring occupational safety, GMP and quality requirements
  • Promoting a performance-oriented and continuously improved work culture

Production planning and order management

  • Ensuring efficient production scheduleing and scheduling
  • Monitoring of production orders from release to completion
  • Coordination of priorities, capacities and resources

SAP Shopfloor Administration

  • Responsibility for the correct execution of all production-relevant SAP processes
  • Ensuring material reservations, production feedback and inventory movements
  • Ensuring high data and process quality

Material flow and logistics

  • Responsibility for shop floor logistics, material supply and transfer orders
  • Ensuring material availability for production
  • Control and optimization of fixed-bin-replenishment processes

Inventory and escalation management

  • Ensuring high stock accuracy
  • Analysis and correction of inventory and process deviations
  • Management and coordination of operational escalations and cross-divisional problem solutions

Your profile

  • Completed degree in supply chain management, or comparable qualification
  • At least 5 years of experience in production, supply chain, lean or logistics
  • Leadership experience in a production environment
  • Very good SAP knowledge
  • Flowing knowledge of German and good English
  • Strong communication, organizational and problem-solving skills
  • Experience in GMP-regulated production facilities is beneficial
