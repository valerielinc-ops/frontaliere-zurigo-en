Head of IT Infrastructure Services (100%)

Männedorf, Zürich

Your contribution

The Head of IT Infrastructure Services is a key IT Leadership Team member, reporting to the Head of IT and responsible for Tecan’s global infrastructure and workplace technology. The role oversees:

  • Cloud & Platform Services: Cloud platforms, platform engineering, automation, DevOps.
  • IT Service Desk: End-user support, service requests, incident management, knowledge management, self-service, employee IT experience.
  • Infrastructure Engineering & Operations: System administration, networking, Microsoft 365, endpoints, workplace devices, VoIP, telephony. The Head of IT Infrastructure Services will take direct leadership responsibility for this team and is therefore expected to bring strong technical understanding and hands-on leadership experience across these infrastructure domains.

This is a leadership and transformation role focused on stabilizing the organization, clarifying direction, strengthening collaboration, and fostering a service-oriented culture. The ideal candidate is an authentic people leader, visible and approachable, who develops talent, sets clear expectations, and guides teams through change.

The role will establish a sustainable operating model, enhance internal capabilities, and define effective collaboration with onshore, nearshore, and strategic partners. It aims to build a resilient, scalable, and future-ready Infrastructure Services organization, advancing service management maturity through AI, automation, and self-service, especially in the IT Service Desk.

The goal is to create a stable, secure, scalable, cost-effective, and service-oriented Infrastructure Services organization that continuously improves the employee IT experience.

Responsibilities

  • People & Organizational Leadership:
  • Lead and develop the three teams, building stability, trust, clear accountability, succession and a shared service culture.
  • Infrastructure Strategy & Engineering:
  • Own the strategy, roadmap and architecture across Azure, AWS, core systems, networks, Microsoft 365, endpoints and telephony.
  • Drive standardization, Infrastructure as Code, DevOps, automation, monitoring and lifecycle management, with clear ownership and service levels.
  • Service Desk, AI & Employee Experience:
  • Own global Service Desk performance; improve employee experience through knowledge management, self-service, AI-enabled support and engineering collaboration.
  • Automate repetitive work, eliminate recurring issues and measure benefits, with security, data protection, governance and human oversight.
  • Operating Model, Partners & Financial Management
  • Define onshore, nearshore and partner delivery; retain critical expertise and governance internally and avoid vendor dependency.
  • Own budgets, sourcing and partner performance through contracts, SLAs, KPIs and business cases; optimize costs while protecting quality, resilience and flexibility.
  • Operational Excellence, Security & Resilience
  • Ensure reliable, secure services through incident, problem, change, continuity and disaster-recovery practices.
  • Embed security, risk, audit and compliance requirements with Cybersecurity; track service KPIs and drive continuous improvement.

Your background

The successful candidate combines deep infrastructure expertise with strong people leadership and transformation capability:

  • 12+ years of relevant IT infrastructure experience, including at least 5 years of leadership experience with responsibility for multidisciplinary infrastructure, engineering and service teams
  • Experience in regulated fields like biotechnology, automotive or aviation is preferred
  • Approachable and people-focused; listens, coaches, develops talent and empowers managers and specialists with clear accountability .
  • Brings deep end-to-end infrastructure expertise across cloud, platforms, networks, Microsoft 365, endpoints and telephony, with particularly strong technical understanding of Infrastructure Engineering & Operations and the credibility to directly lead experienced technical specialists in these areas
  • Unites teams around business services and employee experience, combining strategic thinking with technical and operational depth.
  • Experienced in sourcing and provider management, retaining critical internal capabilities while using external expertise effectively.
  • Uses AI and automation to transform services while sustaining reliable daily operations; communicates confidently with executives, business stakeholders and technical partners.
