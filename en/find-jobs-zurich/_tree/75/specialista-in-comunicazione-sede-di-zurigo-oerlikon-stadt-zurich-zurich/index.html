Full description

Finding good stories, telling complex topics in an understandable way and knowing what works on which channel: If these are your strengths, we look forward to seeing you. Our communication and marketing team demonstrates what makes health centers for old age: from the people who live and work here, from projects that move, and from topics that concern our city. For this we are looking for a specialist in communication as of 1 January 2027 or by agreement

  • They research topics and stories from the health centers for old age and prepare them as a portrait, reportage or interview.
  • They develop content for our online and social media channels and ensure that complex topics are conveyed in a comprehensible, target group-oriented and appealing way – also with pictures and short videos.
  • They accompany internal and external projects and ensure coordinated communication planning with the content team.
  • They process media inquiries and support the maintenance of the intranet.
  • They edit texts, write speeches, editorials and presentations and ensure a uniform appearance of health centers for the age.
  • Style-confident writing and storytelling competence with the ability to convey messages across channels and target groups
  • Secure interview management and know-how for independent production of photo and video material
  • Completed education or studies in communication or journalism, several years of professional experience in corporate communication, ideally with practice in the newsroom
  • Practical experience in media work
  • Knowledge in health care or in the area surrounding the city of Zurich is an advantage
  • Networked, service-oriented thinking and working methods, political sense and digital affinity

A versatile area of responsibility with a lot of room for manoeuvre in a committed team. Different working models support the compatibility of private and professional life.

As part of the city of Zurich, we offer advanced employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

The annual salary for this function is, depending on the experience, between Fr. 103’184. – and Fr. 137’875. – (100%).

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich are one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland and the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

The communication and marketing team is one of the specialist areas at the Zurich-Oerlikon location: these provide central services for the 34 locations of the health centers for old age - from HR and finance to computer science to building management.
