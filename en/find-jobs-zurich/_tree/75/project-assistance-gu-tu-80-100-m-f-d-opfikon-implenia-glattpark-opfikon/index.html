Full description

To strengthen our new GU/TU team in the Zurich region, we are looking for a committed and service-oriented personality as a project assistant: in execution (80-100%). In this versatile function, you support our project and construction managers in all administrative matters and contribute significantly to the smooth running of our construction projects.

Do you want to be part of the team of this exciting project and actively shape our construction projects?

Then apply online now and become part of Implenia!

Tasks

Which tasks await you as project assistant GU/TU 80-100% (m/f/d), Opfikon?

  • As a project assistant, you support our project and construction managers in all administrative and organizational tasks of day-to-day business.
  • You create correspondences, protocols, presentations as well as project-specific evaluations and statistics
  • You participate in tenders, work contracts, orders and administrative processes in procurement and contracting
  • You lead the project filing, coordinate appointments, organize events and ensure a professional flow of information within the team
  • You support in the processing of debtor and creditor invoices as well as in other project-related administrative tasks

Requirements

What requirements do you bring as project assistant GU/TU 80-100% (m/f/d), Opfikon?

  • Commercial basic training (KV) or comparable training
  • First professional experience as an assistant:in or clerk:in a project-oriented environment, ideally in the construction or real estate industry
  • Very good knowledge of Microsoft Office, especially Word and PowerPoint, as well as good knowledge of Excel
  • Confident knowledge of German in words and writing as well as a structured, reliable and independent working method
  • Team-oriented, service-oriented personality with a high level of organizational ability and enjoyment of a dynamic project environment
  • Number affinity and structured working method
  • In addition, you are innovative, open, far-sighted and self-confident, have humor and want to change and improve things

About Implenia

As a leading Swiss construction and real estate service provider, Implenia develops, realizes and manages living spaces, working environments and infrastructure for future generations in Switzerland and Germany. Implenia also offers tunnelling and related infrastructure projects in other markets. The company combines the know-how of highly qualified consulting, development, planning and execution units under one roof into an integrated, multinationally leading construction and real estate service provider. Headquartered in Opfikon near Zurich, Implenia employs more than 8,000 people (FTE) across Europe and achieved a turnover of CHF 3.5 billion in 2025. More information at implenia.com.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
