Full description

The Clienia Private Clinic Littenheid performs a basic care mission for various cantons. With over 253 beds, a day clinic in Frauenfeld and outpatient centers in Frauenfeld, Sirnach, Winterthur, Amriswil and Kreuzlingen, she treats children, adolescents and adults with diseases from the entire diagnostic spectrum of psychiatry and psychotherapy. The clinic is part of the Clienia Group, the largest private provider of psychiatric services in Switzerland.

To complement our team, we are looking for one for our child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy center.

for our outpatient location in Winterthur.

We are a specialized child and adolescent psychiatric outpatient with an interdisciplinary team of doctors, psychologists and social workers. Our goal is to provide holistic, scientifically sound care for children, adolescents and their families in a trusting environment. We are connected to a large child and adolescent psychiatric care clinic with 64 fully stationary places and 5 outpatient locations. There is a full training authorisation (category A).

Their tasks

  • Supervision, training and guidance of assistant doctors
  • Public relations, networking and representation of the department

  • Specialist in child and adolescent psychiatry with clinical experience
