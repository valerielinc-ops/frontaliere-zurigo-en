Ready to shape the future of claims management at AXA?

You are an analytical mind with implementation strength and want to take our claims processes to the next level?

Do you not only want to analyze numbers, but also initiate concrete changes and experience their success directly?

Then become the driving force in our sector "Customer Operation Development & Transformation"!

We are looking for a committed personality who will increase profitability in property insurance with us and actively drive forward the transformation. Use the space to develop your own ideas and achieve a measurable impact.

In the area of customer operations, we design the individual experiences of our customers in the event of damage and performance. They are our focus and together with them we find the right solution. In this way, our customer-first mentality ensures maximum customer satisfaction.

Your field of activity: where you move something

  • Rethinking processes: You analyze and optimize our P&C damage processes end-to-end in order to sustainably increase effectiveness and profitability.
  • Realize potential: You identify and raise potential savings along the entire loss value chain – from fraud detection to recourse to partner management.
  • Projects with impact: You support or lead strategic projects to reduce loss expenses. Your first focus is on optimizing the regression processes, where AI-based solutions are also used.
  • Control: You prepare complex data comprehensibly and translate data-based analyses into concrete recommendations for action.

Your profile: What sets you apart •

  • Your education: You have successfully completed an economics degree or a comparable education.
  • Your experience: Necessary at least 6 years of professional experience in claims management (P&C), in claims controlling, in the data analysis of an insurance company or in consulting (with strong claims / insurance reference).
  • Your strength: With your clarity, you act on organizations, and you have an analytical and structured mindset, coupled with a strong drive to implement – you are a “maker”.
  • Your personality: As a winning and communication-strong personality, you move safely at all hierarchical levels – from operational teams to management.
  • Your mindset: Business transformation is your passion. You have a quick apprehension and like to learn. A healthy dose of pragmatism and humor should not be missing.

Our offer: What you can expect

  • Large design space: With us you should contribute your own ideas and actively shape the damage processes of the future.
  • A central role with high visibility: Your work has a direct impact on the company’s success and enjoys high attention in management.
  • Diversity and development potential: This exciting interface function offers you excellent opportunities for personal and professional development within AXA.
  • A strong team: You work with a diverse team of specialists and experts. Look forward to a dynamic and supportive environment that sees transformation as an opportunity and achieves goals together.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
