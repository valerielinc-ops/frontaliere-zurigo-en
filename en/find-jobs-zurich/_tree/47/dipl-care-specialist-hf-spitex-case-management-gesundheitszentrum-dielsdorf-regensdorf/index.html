Full description

Dipl. Care specialist HF Spitex / case management The Dielsdorf Health Center is a modern geriatric competence center with around 500 employees from 35 nations. Our offer ranges from counselling for older people and their relatives to general and specialized long-term care and care at home. As the largest public Spitex in the Dielsdorf district, we provide ambulatory services for our nine contract municipalities. With our comprehensive range of care services, we enable people to stay in their usual home for as long as possible despite health restrictions. In the future, it is planned to establish a night spitex.

  • Case management, assessments and structured needs assessments
  • Ensuring the care process
  • Advice and support of clients and relatives
  • Assumption of client assignments as part of the reassessment
  • Cooperation with relatives, doctors, hospitals, homes and other specialist centres
  • Pikettdienst every 4 weeks on weekends (detailments and assessments in the home office)
  • Working hours: generally during office hours
  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nursing professional DN II/HF/FH
  • Inter-RAI HC basic course or willingness to complete it
  • Several years of professional experience, ideally in the ambulatory sector (case management or coordination functions)
  • High level of social, methodological and professional competence
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing (min. level B2)
  • Contemporary IT skills (MS Office, Perigon)
  • Self-employed and responsible work
  • Driving licence Cat. B

We offer you a responsible and interesting job in a varied, future-oriented and interprofessional environment. We are in a rural situation with good public transport links. You benefit from our attractive employment conditions, including with mind. 5 weeks holidays, good social benefits, lunch checks, discount at SBB and Reka checks, individual training opportunities, low prices in our restaurants and free parking spaces. Do you live with us care and MORE? Human. Empathetic. Hand in hand. Respectful. We look forward to your online application.

Health Centre Dielsdorf Breitestrasse 11 8157 Dielsdorf Portrait The Dielsdorf Health Centre is a specialized nursing institution with around 430 employees in the Zurich Lower Country and provides care for the 22 political communities of the Dielsdorf district with about 90,000 inhabitants. With more than 200 beds, the inpatient offer of general long-term care is complemented by specialized treatment pathways for rehabilitative acute and transitional care, palliative care, psychiatric geriatrics and the focus on dementia. Spitex Regional is an important part of the care offer. It provides ambulatory services for the municipalities of Regensdorf, Dielsdorf, Niederhasli, Niederglatt and Regensberg.
