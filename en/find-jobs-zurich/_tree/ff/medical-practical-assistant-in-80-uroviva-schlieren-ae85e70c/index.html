Full description

Uroviva stands for a modern medical network and includes a special clinic for urology, nine urological practices and an andrology center. With approximately 70,000 consultations each year in primary, special and emergency care and more than 5,000 operations of all severity levels, Uroviva is an important provider of urological services in Switzerland. For our lively practice, involved in the hospital Limmattal, we are looking for a maternity representation from the 1st. October 2026 limited to 31. May 2027 a* Medical assistant (80%)

Their tasks

  • Planning, organisation and preparation of office hours
  • Care of patients during examinations
  • Independent performance of blood sampling, urine analysis and injections
  • Professional, hygienic and impeccable care of the examination rooms
  • Assistance in cystoscopies and minor ambulatory urological procedures
  • General office work, termination of orders
  • Patient reception

Your profile

  • Completed training as medical/practical assistant EFZ, nurse HF or specialist health EFZ
  • Friendly, reliable and competent personality characterized by a high resilience and great motivation
  • Joy of rain patient contact
  • Maintained appearance
  • Experience with the Axenita operating system is advantageous (but not mandatory)
