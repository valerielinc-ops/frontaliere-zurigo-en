Full description

The opportunity

We are seeking a Technical Trainer to join our team in Zurich. This role offers the opportunity to develop and deliver training programs and materials on our client's products and services for all levels of organizational staff. You will be part of a global team that values diversity and collaboration, driving innovation and excellence. As a Technical Trainer for Gas Insulated Switchgears, you will be responsible for training and certification of our global network of field service engineers. Additionally, you will provide tailored training to external customers worldwide. Being part of the global Gas Insulated Switchgear competence center in Rüti and Oerlikon, Switzerland, you will support the global network in technical aspects as a subject matter expert.

How you'll make an impact

  • Providing technical trainings for our global field service engineers as well as for our customers
  • Manage the entire training project lifecycle, including envisioning (Vision/Scope), planning (Project Plan and Functional Specs), developing (Instructional Design), stabilizing (BETA/Train the Trainer), and deploying (coordination of people, staffing, and other resources & communication)
  • Develop training documentation and use a variety of training mediums such as classes, workshops, Computer Based Training (CBT), and online help
  • Measure training return on investment (ROI) and pilot-test new training techniques, tools, and processes to increase training effectiveness
  • Apply practical knowledge of the job area typically obtained through advanced education and work experience
  • Work independently with general supervision, addressing difficult but typically not complex problems
  • Influence others within the job area through explanation of facts, policies, and practices

Your background

  • Strong experience in the field as field service engineer or supervisor
  • Strong understanding of Gas Insulated Switchgears or similar technical products
  • Experience in developing and delivering technical training programs
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills
  • Ability to work independently and manage multiple projects simultaneously
  • Proficiency in using various training mediums and tools
  • Fluency in English; additional languages are a plus
  • Availability to travel abroad for Customer Site Trainings (Approx. 10% International travel)

Our flexible working practices help you optimize personal and business performance while creating an environment where all employees can develop their skills and grow.
