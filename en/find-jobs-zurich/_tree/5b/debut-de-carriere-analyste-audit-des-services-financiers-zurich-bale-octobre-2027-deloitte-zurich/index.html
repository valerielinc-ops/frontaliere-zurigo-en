Location: Zurich/Basel Work Pattern Percentage: 80-100% Start date: October 2027 You bring the ambition, we provide opportunities. As the world changes, businesses must be ready to respond to the challenges and opportunities that lie ahead. In Deloitte Audit we protect the public interest and build trust in business by delivering leading assurance and living a culture that empowers people to thrive. In close collaboration with our professionals, you conduct audit missions with diverse, major organisations. You work independently while benefiting from the expert advice of experienced colleagues. Your active contribution is pivotal, as you engage in shaping the future of our offerings and nurture your own professional growth. How you can make an impact

  • Gain insight into how financial services organizations operate and the economic and industry challenges they are facing
  • Drive effective and reliable financial and non-financial reporting as a forward-looking auditor with in-depth business process and internal controls understanding
  • Develop expertise in financial services regulations (e.g., AMLO-FINMA) and support organizations in meeting evolving regulatory requirements. Conduct regulatory audits that identify compliance gaps, assess control effectiveness, and drive remediation strategies to ensure adherence to supervisory expectations and industry standards.
  • Develop people early on in your career and build a broad professional network
  • Bring your fresh ideas and pioneer thinking to the forefront early in your career at Deloitte
  • Go a clear, long-term career path and receive first-class training for your professional development, including support to qualify as Swiss certified public accountant (Swiss CPA). ( https://www.expertsuisse.ch/ausbildung ) What you bring to succeed
  • You hold a bachelor’s or master’s degree in Business Administration or Economics (ideally with a specialization in Accounting, Banking, Finance, or Controlling) and preferably have previous internship or work experience
  • You have excellent analytical competencies and are interested in understanding the world of banking and finance
  • You enjoy solving complex challenges in a team and are interested in the financial market, as well as innovation, and transformation (including but not limited to data analytics)
  • You are keen to learn and to complete further education to become a Swiss CPA at EXPERTsuisse
  • You are fluent in German and English, French or Italian are a plus
  • You are curious and courageous Your team Become part of a diverse, multinational and highly motivated team at our Zurich/Basel office. We will help you grow both personally and professionally: through our trainings, mentoring programmes and on-the-job learning, but also by connecting with your colleagues across all business lines. In the audit and assurance team, we believe in a common purpose where we help to protect public interest and trust in business, and we provide independent assurance for organizations to make the right decisions. You bring ambition, we provide opportunities. We support our people to be their authentic selves, and we recognize the collective strength of our diverse backgrounds.
