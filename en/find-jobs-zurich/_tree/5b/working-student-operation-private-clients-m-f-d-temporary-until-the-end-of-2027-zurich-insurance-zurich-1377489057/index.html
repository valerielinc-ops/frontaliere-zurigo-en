Does the idea of gaining practical experience in telephone customer support motivate you in addition to your studies? Would you like to gain insights into the agile development of digital functions at the same time?

In this role, you will strengthen Zurich Invest AG’s Digital Business & Operations PC division at Zurich Switzerland and make an important contribution to our joint success.

You answer technical customer inquiries by phone and support the team in processing the support mailbox. You will also get insights into the agile development of new functions.

This is what awaits you

  • You process the technical customer requests for our customer portal and ensure that all requests are processed in a timely and professional manner.
  • You support the team in the processing of our support mailbox and contribute to the efficient processing of requests
  • You provide written and telephone information about our products to private customers, sales partners and customer consultants of Zurich
  • You mutate and update customer bases and process requests and orders from our end customers
  • You work closely with different teams and support cross-team testing

This sets you apart

  • Matriculation at a technical college or university, ideally in business administration or a similar course of study
  • High customer orientation and a service- and solution-oriented approach
  • Team player mentality as well as an independent, reliable and quality-conscious working style
  • Good MS-Office and Jira user knowledge
  • Stylish-confident German, good knowledge of English and fluent knowledge of French
