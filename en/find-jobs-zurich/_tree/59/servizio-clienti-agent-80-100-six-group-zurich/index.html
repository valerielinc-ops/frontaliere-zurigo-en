Customer Service Agent (80-100%)

Zurich, Olten, up to 40% HomeOffice possible, Reference 8428

Within an established, helpful and well-established team, you can advise bank customers by phone (Inbound) on various topics relating to the use of electronic means of payment such as debit cards, TWINT, Apple Pay, Samsung Pay and Google Pay. As part of our support, flexible working hours from Monday to Sunday in shift operations as well as attractive Fringe Bernards are waiting for you. You work either in our SIX office in Olten or Zurich and you have the opportunity to work up to 40% in the Home Office.

What You Will Do

  • Analysis of potential cards and support in complaints.
  • Technical support for web and app solutions (registration and operation).
  • Execution of debit mastercard, Visa debit, macaroni, VPay and bank card lockouts for Swiss banks on behalf of private customers, banks and third parties (storage services).
  • providing information on payment transactions, products, services and campaigns.
  • 1st level support for the mobile payment app TWINT.

What You Bring

  • Completed training (preferably specialist/woman customer dialogue or basic commercial training) or matura.
  • Experience in the field of call center or customer service in a similar function.
  • Negotiable in German, French, Italian and English.
  • Self-employed, capable of action, flexible and resilient personality with a strong customer and service orientation.
  • Very good PC user knowledge.
