Full description

The Department of Quality Assurance in the Operations Directorate is responsible for the definition, implementation and controlling of product safety requirements for products in the national range of the cooperative retail trade of Migros supermarkets. For a maternity representation we are looking for support.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Supermarket AG, Limmatplatz Zurich, Limmatstrasse 152, 8005 Zurich

Important information

Please note that this is a temporary employment (1.3.27 - 1.3.28).

Mansioni

  • Ensuring product safety from procurement and product launch through sales to after-sale activities for various product groups in the freshness, food and catering sectors
  • Updating and controlling cross-product quality requirements such as instructions and processes
  • Ensure a well-functioning cooperation with the Quality Assurance team of procurement offices in Asia
  • Active management of the early warning system and advice on product-related quality issues

Requirements

  • Experience in a similar position: as well as in-depth knowledge in food quality assurance
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): field of food sciences
  • Good communication skills, strong conviction and argument
  • Decisiveness and assertiveness
  • Responsible personality with openness and motivation to learn new things quickly
  • German (flowing)
  • English (good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Modern infrastructure: Diverse work zones and modern co-working spaces with advanced equipment
  • Central place of work: Central place of work in the city of Zurich (Limmatplatz or near Hardbrücke)
  • Cumulus points: Additional Cumulus points in all Migros branches and various subsidiaries
  • Self-responsibility & freedom: Varied tasks with high self-responsibility
  • Food: Excellent food at reasonable prices in the staff restaurant
  • Healthy work: breastfeeding and rest rooms as well as massage options

  • Submit application documents: For a complete application, we need your CV, certificates and diplomas. A photo in the CV and a letter of motivation are not necessary. We live equal opportunities for all applicants. We have a standardized recruitment process. If this does not work for you, mention it in your application dossier.
  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks

Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Discussion with case presentation: Specific expertise is examined on the basis of a case or other assessment instruments. After processing, the result of the leader is presented and then discussed together. Usually, the team is also represented at the presentation.   • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
