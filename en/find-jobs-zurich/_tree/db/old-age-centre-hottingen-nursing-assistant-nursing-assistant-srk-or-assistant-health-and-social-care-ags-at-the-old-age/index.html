Old age centre Hottingen Nursing assistant / nursing assistant SRK or assistant health and social services (AGS) - at the old age centre Hottingen - Spital Zollikerberg, Zollikerberg (ZH).

Area: Nursing professions.

Welcome to your new place of work.

The Alterzentrum Hottingen offers you an exciting and varied position in the in-house Spitex as

Nursing assistant / nursing assistant SRK or assistant health and social care (AGS) – in the old age center Hottingen

If you too want to make a difference in people’s lives, these are your tasks:

  • Ensuring demanding and individual care and support of tenants in a large team with long-term employees
  • Taking over of domestic activities
  • Support for everyday design
  • Electronic maintenance documentation management
  • Attractive working hours: no shared services, no night watch

To make you feel comfortable with us, many benefits are waiting for you. Just to name a few:

  • Own pension fund with super-compulsory participation of the employer
  • Worldwide private insurance in case of accident
  • Financial support to public transport subscription or bicycle
  • Centrally located and easily accessible by public transport

All amenities for employees can be found here.

This convinces us

  • Experience in care (acute or long-term)
  • Completed course as nursing assistant:in SRC or completed training Assistant:in health and social care
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Independent, friendly and exactly working personality as well as resilient, team-oriented and flexible
  • Practical handling of electronic maintenance documentation
  • Our and your values: appreciation, partnership, commitment, transparency and seeing the whole

More information is available to Uiara Thommen Schröter, Team Leader Spitex at the Old Age Centre Hottingen, T 044 268 77 70.

About the age centre Hottingen #LI-PZ1

The Old Age Centre Hottingen is a company of the Diakoniewerk Neumünster Foundation – Swiss Nursing School. It offers 150 tenants a modern service living in old age. It also includes the restaurant Oase and seminar rooms. In total, more than 50 employees work in the areas of care, catering, housekeeping, technical service and administration.
