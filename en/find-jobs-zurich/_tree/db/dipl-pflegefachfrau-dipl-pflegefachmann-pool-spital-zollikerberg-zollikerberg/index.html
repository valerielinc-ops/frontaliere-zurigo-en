Dipl. Nursing professional / Dipl. Care specialist Pool — Spital Zollikerberg, Zollikerberg (ZH).

Area: Nursing professions.

Welcome to your new place of work.

Our care pool offers you the opportunity for a flexible job with a selectable amount of 10-100% as

Dipl. Nursing professional / Dipl. Care professional pool

If you too want to make a difference in people’s lives, these are your tasks:

  • They work in nursing care teams at all our bed stations and show willingness to engage in changing locations.
  • You work in the skill and grade mix
  • You contribute to the well-being of our patients in a motivated care team
  • They are mainly active in two shifts (early and late service).
  • They ensure the quality of care by applying the respective standards and guidelines
  • You control the care process from entry to exit

Become part of our care pool. You will find an exciting and challenging field of activity and an interdisciplinary activity in the disciplines of surgery, orthopaedics, gynecology, medicine, acute geriatrics and palliative care. Our stations are guided according to the reference care system.

In our 360° tour, get a first impression of your new place of work.

To make you feel comfortable with us, many benefits are waiting for you. Just to name a few:

  • Own pension fund with super-compulsory participation of the employer
  • Ecobonus of up to CHF 650 per year
  • Free use of the fitness room on the site
  • Exclusive discounts on telephony, Internet and TV at Salt and Sunrise
  • Discounted child care (KiTa) on the site
  • Benefits within the pool team:
  • No night services
  • Generous treatment of holiday wishes
  • Family and education-friendly work planning
  • Instruction at all our nursing stations
  • Excellent team culture
  • Possibility of fixed working days and work shifts by arrangement

About the Zollikerberg hospital #LI-PZ1 We stand for a comprehensive range of services and strong cooperation partners. With a public service mandate and 173 beds, the Zollikerberg Hospital contributes to the optimal medical care of the greater Zurich area. Every year, more than 11,000 inpatients and 75,000 outpatients in all insurance classes can feel comfortable and in good hands. Our broadly based primary care includes internal medicine, gynecology and obstetrics with neonatology, general surgery, an interdisciplinary emergency center and orthopedics with specialized spinal surgery.
