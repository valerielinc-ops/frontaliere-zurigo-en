About the job Google's software engineers develop the next-generation technologies that change how billions of users connect, explore, and interact with information and one another. Our products need to handle information at massive scale, and extend well beyond web search. We're looking for engineers who bring fresh ideas from all areas, including information retrieval, distributed computing, large-scale system design, networking and data storage, security, artificial intelligence, natural language processing, UI design and mobile; the list goes on and is growing every day. As a software engineer, you will work on a specific project critical to Google’s needs with opportunities to switch teams and projects as you and our fast-paced business grow and evolve. We need our engineers to be versatile, display leadership qualities and be enthusiastic to take on new problems across the full-stack as we continue to push technology forward.

Join us to scale shopping across Gemini App/Web, Chrome and beyond. Our agentic and conversational shopping features are growing rapidly in usage. We are now expanding this experience across platforms and touchpoints, requiring technical work across quality, triggering, serving, and front-end systems. Our team is positioned at the intersection of conversational search and web browsing, helping users find what they’re shopping for through deep research, side-by-side comparison, and contextual shopping assistance. "The Google Commerce team is driving the shift toward agentic commerce—moving beyond static interactions to build intelligent, adaptive shopping experiences. By bringing Gemini into AI Mode in Search and the Gemini app, we're making shopping even more intuitive. Powered by the Google Shopping Graph's 60B+ listings, our team is helping to establish open industry standards like the Universal Commerce Protocol. We're building shopping experiences across Google, including Search, the Gemini app, YouTube, and Ads, to help people discover and buy products from the brands they love and trust on Google.

Responsibilities

  • Write product or system development code.
  • Participate in, or lead design reviews with peers and stakeholders to decide amongst available technologies.
  • Drive the design, implementation, and launch of high-impact shopping features across Gemini App/Web, Chrome and Android surfaces.
  • Partner with Product Management, UX, and UXR teams to define and scope new features, leveraging data and user behavior analysis.
  • Facilitate cross-site collaboration with Commerce and platform owner teams in Zürich and the US.

  • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent practical experience.
