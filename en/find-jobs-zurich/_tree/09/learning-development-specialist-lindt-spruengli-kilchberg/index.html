About us

We enchant the world with chocolate! Lindt & Sprüngli (Switzerland) AG is a subsidiary of the global Lindt & Sprüngli Group with headquarters and production facilities in Kilchberg on Lake Zurich. In Altendorf we operate our own logistics centre, while the Lindt Cocoa Center in Olten produces cocoa mass for our own use as well as for other European production facilities. Throughout Switzerland, we proudly operate 15 Lindt shops, including iconic locations such as the Jungfraujoch and the Swiss Transport House in Lucerne. More than 1000 employees from more than 50 nations contribute to our success.

Do you want to rethink learning and actively shape the future of digital learning? Then develop learning opportunities with us that really reach our employees, inspire them and create joy in learning.

In technical training, you develop modern learning opportunities with a focus on production: simple, visual, interactive and practical, for people with different professional backgrounds, mother tongues and German skills.

What you do with us

  • Conception and development of trainings, e-learnings and digital learning formats, preferably for production employees
  • Translation of complex subject content into simple, visual and practical learning experiences for employees with different language and German skills
  • Collaboration with production, technology and internal experts
  • Development of modern e-learning with Articulate Rise
  • Use of Synthesia and AI-based tools for video and digital learning content
  • Development according to modern methodological, didactic and adult pedagogical principles
  • Testing of new learning technologies and learning formats
  • Support for learning platform, training standards and competence management

When you fit in with us

  • Education or study in adult education, learning & development, instructional design, media didactics or comparable qualification
  • Several years of experience in the development of training, e-learning or digital learning formats
  • Sound methodological-didactic competence and understanding of adult education
  • Very good practical knowledge of Articulate Rise
  • Experience with Articulate Storyline is a big plus
  • Experience with Synthesia or comparable AI/content creation tools
  • High digital affinity, interest in new technologies and learning formats
  • Ability to prepare complex content simply, comprehensibly and appropriately for target groups
  • Ability to familiarize yourself with new topics even without prior technical knowledge and to develop target group-oriented e-learnings in cooperation with the specialist departments
  • Creativity, initiative and joy in trying out new solutions
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
