In the Facility Services department of ETH Zurich, we create the optimal conditions for teaching, research and technology transfer. We maintain the buildings and ensure the energy and media supply as well as the technical and infrastructural building management of all buildings and rooms used by ETH Zurich.

You like to be among people, keep an overview even in a lively environment and like to help? Then you are exactly right with us.

Campus Hönggerberg is one of the most dynamic places in ETH Zurich. Every day, students, researchers, employees, visitors and suppliers from all over the world move here. Over the next few years, the campus will continue to grow and develop with new buildings and modern infrastructure. As an employee: in the Info + Service Center (80%) you are right in the middle of it and the first point of contact for everyone who needs support, guidance or information. The working days are Tuesday - Friday.

Job description

At the desk of the Info + Service Center (ISC), you are the first point of contact for students, employees, visitors: inside and suppliers: inside at Campus Hönggerberg. With your open and service-oriented nature, you answer questions, provide information and help everyone on the extensive campus quickly find their way around and reach their destination.

As the central hub of facility management, you will meet a wide variety of concerns. You coordinate requests, receive fault messages about the building infrastructure and ensure that they are forwarded to the right places. In addition, you take on administrative tasks in building management, supervise key management and the lost property office and ensure that organizational processes work smoothly.

Thanks to your presence at the counter, you are close to the campus and experience the diversity of a lively university business every day. You work closely with various internal and external stakeholders and make a significant contribution to ensuring that the ETH community and guests feel well looked after at all times. If necessary, you also support your colleagues and take on deputies within the team.

In short: You are the friendly face of Campus Hönggerberg, keep track of many simultaneous concerns and ensure with your commitment that the day-to-day life of the campus runs smoothly.

Profile

  • You have completed a commercial or comparable training and already gained experience in the reception, counter or customer service area.
  • You approach people openly, enjoy the daily exchange and feel at home in the front office.
  • Even if it gets hectic, you keep track, set the right priorities and stay friendly and solution-oriented.
  • Organization is in your blood and you quickly find yourself in new topics thanks to your quick comprehension.
  • Technology does not put you off, but helps you find the right solutions.
  • You enjoy working with others, appreciating the team spirit of a community office and supporting wherever you need it.
  • With MS Office you are safe and communicate stylishly in German as well as in English. Other language skills are a plus.

You are an established, solution-oriented personality who likes to tackle, take responsibility and feel comfortable in a lively environment. With your open nature, you ensure that students, employees and guests on campus feel at all times in good hands.
