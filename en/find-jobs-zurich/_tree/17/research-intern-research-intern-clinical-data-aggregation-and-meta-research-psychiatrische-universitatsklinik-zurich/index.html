Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 40-60% As part of the compulsory module "200-600 internship" in a free internship, with possibility of subsequent master's thesis, we are looking for an immediate or by agreement for our Center for Children and Youth Forensics. Their tasks

  • Literature research and evaluation
  • Establishment and maintenance of a study database
  • Merging and structuring clinical data from different sources
  • Support for categorisation and coding of studies, data entry and statistical analysis
  • Support for the preparation of systematic reviews
  • Collaboration with publications

Your profile

  • Beginning of the Master's programme Psychology
  • Very good English language skills (minor level C1)
  • Self-employed and reliable cooperation
  • Interest in forensic psychiatric issues in children and youth

Our offer

  • Clinic, research and teaching united: working in a university environment with international networking
  • Contributing to innovation: participation in forward-looking research and development projects
  • Together for people: Interdisciplinary cooperation for holistic psychiatric care
  • Strong in the team: appreciative cooperation and mutual support
  • Flexible work: attendance times can be agreed individually (at least 40% availability desired)

Contact

Dr Phil. Nikolaos Boumparis Research assistant nikolaos.boumparis@pukzh.ch will be happy to answer questions. We look forward to your application! Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich Lenggstrasse 31, PO Box 363 8032 Zurich www.pukzh.ch
