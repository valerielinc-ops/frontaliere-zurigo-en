Full description

USZ Campus

immediately or by agreement

In patient admission emergency, you are the first point of contact for patients: inside and their relatives. You ensure a professional admission, clarify insurance relationships and ensure a smooth administrative process. With your service-oriented and empathetic nature, you contribute significantly to a positive first impression at the USZ.

Your main tasks

  • Administrative admission/recording of all emergency patients: inside
  • Preparation and forwarding of the necessary documents and documents for the clinics and invoicing
  • Ensuring the insurance of the patients to be taken in: (written or oral)
  • Debt collection at cash payers
  • Depot calculation during night, weekends and public holidays

Your profile

  • Ideally, you have a commercial education, training in a health profession or experience in the service sector
  • Competence in hospital or social security is an advantage
  • You are adept at dealing with patients: travelers from all over the world and with cultural differences
  • You are communicative, like the direct customer contact, the multi-professional cooperation and know how to deal well with hectic situations
  • You can empathize well and mediatingly with the needs of the patients: inside and relatives
  • You are ready to work in a 24-hour shift operation
  • You have good IT user knowledge
  • Ideally, you will master other languages in addition to German and English.
