Shape your future – together with us!

AXA-ARAG is one of the leading legal expenses insurance companies in Switzerland and is on course for growth. Our goal is to continuously develop the customer experience at the highest level. To this end, we are driving the digitalization and transformation of our business and focusing on innovative solutions. Our corporate culture is based on openness, personal development, agility and cross-divisional collaboration – and that is what defines us.

To strengthen our Data & AI Product Team, we are looking for a GenAI Engineer (all genders). Together we develop AI solutions with measurable added value for our customers, our employees and the company. In this role, you will accompany the entire Agentic AI and GenAI lifecycle: from the analysis of business challenges to solution engineering and modeling to the productive implementation and operation of our applications.

With us you can fully contribute your strengths and ideas. We value critical ideas and innovative contributions from all perspectives.

If you are looking for an exciting challenge in an environment that really appreciates your talents, then you are at the right place!

This is what awaits you

  • You are part of the newly founded product team “Data & AI AXA-ARAG” and actively design innovative AI use cases right from the start.
  • You develop, optimize and operate data and AI applications end-to-end together with our business stakeholders
  • With your maker mentality and curiosity for new concepts and ideas, you bring innovative impulses to the team
  • You will keep up to date with current developments in the field of Agentic AI & GenAI, exchange information in the AXA community and actively share your knowledge with team members and stakeholders.
  • Empower our business stakeholders to think analytically and help them apply AI in their work environment

You bring that with you

  • You have a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Data Science (Business) Informatics or a comparable degree program
  • You have at least 5 years of professional software engineering experience and already have a clear track record of generating measurable value from data and AI applications along with the business
  • You bring in-depth experience in handling the components of our tech stack (GCP, Golang, Python, REST APIs, Microservices, Google ADK, LangChain/LangGraph, Postgres/pgvector, Serverless, Terraform, GitHub Actions)
  • Experience in the use of Agentic AI, Multi-Agent Architectures and Agent2Agent Protocol (A2A) are beneficial in order to automate our claims processes in legal protection insurance in a structured and more efficient way.
  • You have experience with context engineering, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), improvement of retrieval quality, benchmarking and evaluation of AI components
  • You are familiar with the software engineering best practices and enjoy working with cloud infrastructure (Google Cloud Platform) and the architecture of cloud-native applications
  • You speak fluent German

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
