Bioinformatician Spatial Transcriptomics and Single-Cell Analysis

80%-100%, Zurich, fixed-term

The Functional Genomics Center Zurich (FGCZ) is an interdisciplinary research and training center of the University of Zurich and ETH Zurich. We support life science research with state-of-the-art technologies for genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, and metabolomics as well as high-end bioinformatics analysis. The FGCZ bioinformatics group provides raw data processing, high-performance computing, statistical evaluation, interpretation, and integrated analysis of omics data as a service to and in collaboration with research groups in the Zurich area.

The position is available immediately or upon agreement.

Job description

We have expert knowledge in the analysis of single-cell, spatial transcriptomics, and related multi-omics data and work in an open, collaborative environment. With our high-performance computing infrastructure, mature software environment, and established agentic AI support, we provide excellent conditions to work efficiently on exciting data analysis challenges.

  • You will analyze and interpret spatial transcriptomics data (e.g., 10x Genomics Xenium, Visium HD, Atera and Illumina StrataMap) together with researchers.
  • Using established workflows, you will carry out the initial raw data processing and quality assessment in close interaction with our wet lab experts.
  • You will also work with related omics data (scRNA-seq, proteomics, metabolomics datasets) and perform integrative analyses.
  • In addition, you will improve existing workflows and establish new ones.
  • You will follow developments in the field and evaluate new methods.
  • You will collaborate directly with individual research groups focusing on translational research.

Profile

  • PhD degree in bioinformatics or a related field.
  • Proficient in NGS data analysis, with in-depth knowledge of R/Bioconductor and/or Python.
  • Experience with spatial transcriptomics and single-cell data.
  • Experience working in a GNU/Linux environment, ideally including high-performance computing clusters.
  • Good understanding of biological research questions.
  • Highly communicative, team- and service-oriented.
  • Enthusiastic about statistical and bioinformatics data analysis and keen to keep up with the constant developments in the field.
  • Very good command of English (spoken and written).
  • Experience using AI tools for coding and bioinformatics analysis is an asset.

We offer

  • A varied position at the intersection of technology and life science research, with access to the latest spatial and single-cell technologies and a wide range of research projects.
  • Employment conditions follow the regulations of ETH Zurich.
  • You will join an international team in a motivating, friendly, and flexible atmosphere.
