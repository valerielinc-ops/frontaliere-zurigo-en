Full description

Own Internship Recruiting: You take full ownership of recruiting our interns, independently managing the process from job advertisement and screening through interviews, selection and offer coordination. Coordinate Interviews: You keep our broader recruiting processes moving by coordinating candidates, hiring managers and interview schedules. Manage Reference Letters: You coordinate and support the preparation of employment reference letters, ensuring a reliable and timely process. Support HR Operations: You support day-to-day HR activities and help keep our People processes accurate, efficient and employee-focused. Drive HR Projects: You actively contribute to HR initiatives, own defined workstreams and bring in your ideas to improve how we work. Maintain HR Data: You ensure accurate and up-to-date employee and candidate information across our HR systems. Gain Broad HR Exposure: You work closely with different HR functions and gain first-hand experience across a wide range of People topics.

Requirements

Education: You are currently enrolled in or have recently completed a bachelor’s degree or apprenticeship. Communication Skills: You have excellent communication and interpersonal skills, fostering positive relationships with candidates and stakeholders. Adaptability: You thrive in a fast-paced, dynamic environment and adapt quickly to changing priorities. Professionalism: You handle sensitive information with discretion and maintain confidentiality at all times. Languages: You are fluent in English and have good German skills. Availability: You are ideally able to start in February or earlier and commit to a 6‑month internship to gain comprehensive experience in recruitment.
