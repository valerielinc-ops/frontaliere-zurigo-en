Hirslanden stands for first-class, responsible and individual health care that puts people first. From birth to old age as well as from prevention to healing – at any time, physically and digitally – the Hirslanden Group is concentrating on the further development of the “Continuum of Care” together with private and public partners.

Our focus is not only on the well-being of our patients, but also on that of our employees.

YOUR TASKS

  • You will be responsible for the architecture, operation and further development of our Kubernetes and DevOps platform based on SUSE Rancher/RKE2 including the Linux infrastructure.
  • Develop and operate automated CI/CD and GitOps pipelines with Forgejo, Rancher Fleet and Argo CD for reliable software deployments.
  • You automate platform operation with Ansible, AWX and Infrastructure as Code and consistently reduce manual processes.
  • You ensure stable and secure platform operation – from monitoring, updates and lifecycle management to troubleshooting in 2nd-level support and piketting service.
  • You will design the technical architecture, define standards and evaluate new technologies to continuously develop the platform.
  • You work closely with ICT teams as well as application and development teams, supporting them with technical requirements and providing comprehensible documentation.

Your profile

  • You have a completed ICT education (vocational apprenticeship, university or university) or an equivalent qualification.
  • You’ll bring at least five years of experience in System Engineering with Linux, and at least three years of experience in operating prolific Kubernetes platforms and in DevOps, CI/CD and GitOps.
  • You have in-depth knowledge of Kubernetes, SUSE Rancher/RKE2, Git-based DevOps/CI/CD workflows and automation with Ansible/AWX and GitOps with Rancher Fleet or Argo CD. Certifications such as CKA/CKS as well as from SUSE Rancher, Red Hat or in the cloud sector are advantageous.
  • You have experience in the architecture and design of platform solutions as well as in-depth knowledge of Linux, networks, high availability and container security.
  • You work independently, reliably and solution-oriented and like to take responsibility. In addition, you convince with your quality awareness as well as your pronounced customer and service orientation.
  • You communicate safely in German (C2) and have a good knowledge of English (B2); Knowledge of French is an advantage.
