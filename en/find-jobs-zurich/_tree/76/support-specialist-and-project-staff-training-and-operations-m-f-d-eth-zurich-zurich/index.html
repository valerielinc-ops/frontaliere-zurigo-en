Full description

Support specialist:in and project staff:in training and operation (m/f/d)

80%-100%, Zurich, temporary

The Digital Teaching Operations (DL) team of the Academic Services department ensures the smooth operation of campus management systems and forms the central interface between the university administration departments and the computer science services. The Team Support DL is responsible for the professional and technical support of the users: inside, analysis and correction of errors, delivery of database queries, training, advice and documentation on the campus management systems as well as for authorization management.

Project background

As part of the strategic project "Digital Campus", the team is actively involved in the gradual renewal and continuous modernization of our digital system landscape. For the project we are looking for a committed and solution-oriented personality as a support specialist: in temporary employment until the end of the project in September 2029, who enjoys user contact, training and the processes of operational support. In this role, you actively shape the digital transformation of teaching operations and make a decisive contribution to the successful introduction of new processes, systems and working methods.

Job description

Operational support and operation (about 50%)

  • First- and second-level support: Professional and technical support for users: all about the campus management systems via ticket system.
  • Disruption Management: Analyze, reproduce and track errors and document support cases
  • Forwarding tickets to third-level support (IT) and tracking them.
  • Release and testing support: Participation in acceptance and system testing, release preparations and support in intensive operational phases (e.g. Hypercare).
  • Authorization administration: Supervision of authorization management.
  • Data deliveries: creation of data queries, periodic reports and ad hoc evaluations.

Project cooperation Training and operation (about 50 %)

  • Training conception and implementation: conception, preparation and implementation of training and training for users:inside.
  • Documentation and knowledge management: Creation, maintenance and quality assurance of user, process and support documentation, FAQs, glossaries, and guides (e.g. in Confluence).
  • Project Participation and Change Management: Active support in planning, coordinating and tracking digitization projects, accompanying the transition of project results into regular operation. Coordination of tasks, dependencies and administrative project tasks. Documentation and optimization of processes.
  • Requirements and process analysis: Recording recurring support topics and feedback on technical requirements for continuous process and system optimization.

Profile

  • Education and experience: Completed university studies (FH/HF/Uni) or a comparable qualification with relevant professional experience in application support, IT service management or process coordination (ideally in a university or administrative environment).
  • Training and communication competence: demonstrable experience or strong affinity for the preparation of learning content, moderation of training as well as understandable and addressable communication with different stakeholders.
  • Digital Affinity and Tools: Use of common collaboration and support tools (e.g. Jira, Confluence, Microsoft 365, AI assistants).
  • Working method and personality: Analytical, solution-oriented and structured working method with a high level of service awareness and personal responsibility. Ability to capture problems analytically, document them in a structured manner and solve them sustainably. Quick understanding. Joy of direct contact with users: inside and working in agile, interdisciplinary teams.
  • Database knowledge (advantageous): Knowledge of SQL and MS Access for queries, data analysis or handling support cases is a plus.
  • Languages: Very good knowledge of German in word and writing (main working language); Knowledge of English is an advantage.

The close cooperation with various stakeholders and the responsibility for accompanying the introduction and maintenance of new digital infrastructure characterize this body.
