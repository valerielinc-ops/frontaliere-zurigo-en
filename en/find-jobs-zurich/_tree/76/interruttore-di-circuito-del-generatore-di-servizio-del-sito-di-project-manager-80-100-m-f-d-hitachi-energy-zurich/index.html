Full description

The opportunity

Hitachi Energy's High Voltage Products division develops and manufactures Generator Circuit Breaker (GCB) and Gas-Insulated Switchgear (GIS) systems in Zurich-Oerlikon, supporting reliable power transmission worldwide. Our global Service organization provides comprehensive lifecycle support, including commissioning, maintenance, troubleshooting, retrofits, and upgrades. Your expertise matters: you ensure efficient project execution with confidence and precision. With a keen eye for detail and a strong service mindset, you play a key role in coordinating and planning our service activities.

As Project Manager Site Service GCB (f/m/d), you are responsible for the technical and timely execution of commissioning and maintenance activities. You contribute to the continuous improvement of our processes and help achieve high customer satisfaction and successful project outcomes.

How you'll make an impact

  • Coordinate and support the execution of on-site commissioning and maintenance activities worldwide
  • Serve as the primary point of contact for internal and external customers throughout project execution
  • Collaborate with field service personnel to plan installations and maintenance activities, coordinate resources, and track project progress, costs, and reporting
  • Ensure compliance with health, safety, and quality standards, while proactively identifying and mitigating project risks and supporting corrective actions when needed
  • Maintain project documentation, including technical records, test reports, and customer documentation, and support the billing of completed services

Your background

  • Completed technical apprenticeship, vocational training, HF, or comparable qualification; a degree is beneficial but not required
  • Initial professional experience in project coordination, service, installation, commissioning, engineering, or a related technical environment
  • Interest in project management; project management training or certification is an advantage
  • Experience with switchgear, power systems, field service, or industrial equipment is beneficial but not required
  • Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple tasks and priorities
  • Service-oriented mindset and strong communication skills when working with customers and colleagues from different cultural backgrounds
  • Business fluent in English and good German skills
  • Team player with a proactive and hands-on approach
  • Good knowledge of MS Office; SAP experience is an advantage
  • Interest in digital tools and using data to support daily business activities
