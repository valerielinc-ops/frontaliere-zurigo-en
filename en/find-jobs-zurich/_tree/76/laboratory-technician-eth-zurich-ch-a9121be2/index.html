Chemical laboratory:in (m/f/d)

80%-100%, Zurich, temporary

Printed Printing

Become part of an internationally leading research laboratory and work on projects ranging from dating world-famous archaeological finds to climate and environmental research. The Laboratory for Ion Beam Physics (LIP) is part of the Department of Physics at ETH Zurich and is one of the world's leading laboratories in the field of Accelerator Mass Spectrometry (AMS) and ion beam physics applications. Among other things, our accelerators are used for high-precision radiocarbon dating. Our laboratory’s best-known studies include the dating of the Shroud of Turin and the Ötztal glacier mummy (Ötzi). In addition, we develop and build innovative instruments for the ultrasensitive analysis of rare nuclides and collaborate with researchers from archaeology, environmental and climate research, earth sciences and numerous other disciplines.

Job description

  • For radiocarbon dating, you prepare a wide variety of sample materials, clean them and carry them to the measurement
  • The spectrum ranges from valuable archaeological finds and cultural historical documents to challenging scientific samples.
  • Material preparation is carried out according to established standards and requires a high degree of care, precision and responsibility.
  • For the versatile activities you have a modern laboratory infrastructure with all necessary facilities at your disposal.
  • Your future workplace is located on the ETH Campus Hönggerberg (“Science City”) and is excellently accessible by public transport.
