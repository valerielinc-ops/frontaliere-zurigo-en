Full description

PROFILE

  • You have the passion for the realization of innovative and complex signalling and railway automation projects
  • You have a completed, higher technical education (uni, FH, HF) with business education
  • You have at least 5 years of experience in managing complex customer projects in the field of signal technology, or other relevant project experience in the railway industry
  • You know the current developments in the field of automation technology in the railway industry
  • You communicate not only in German, but also in English and/or French (level C1 in word and writing)
  • You enjoy business trips (10%-20% travel activity)

TASKS

  • You have overall responsibility for handling complex customer projects in the area of signalling/rail automation (CBTC, ATO/C-DAS, ESTW)
  • You ensure timely, budget- and contract-compliant project execution
  • You are responsible for customer support and communication with partners, suppliers, authorities and other stakeholders
  • You know the customer contract and use your skills and experience in the negotiations purposefully and in the sense of the overall project
  • You lead and coordinate an interdisciplinary team from all areas along the value chain
  • You coordinate with the suppliers from other Stadler units to promote an integrated solution in the sense of the overall project and Stadler
  • You represent the customer project internally before management and motivate your project employees sustainably
  • Together with the team, you will develop innovative solutions to meet technical and non-technical customer requirements
