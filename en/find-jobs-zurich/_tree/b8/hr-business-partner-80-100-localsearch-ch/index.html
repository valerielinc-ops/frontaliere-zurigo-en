Full description

localsearch is the leading marketing and advertising partner for Swiss SMEs. We make customers successful in the digital world. You like to tackle, take responsibility and not only manage HR, but also shape it? In our 8-member HR team in Zurich, you are the first point of contact for managers and employees – close to the business and with a lot of room for manoeuvre. Our HR organization is in the midst of a transformation, and you actively shape it. We are looking for a hands-on personality with Drive who thinks and implements things.

This is what moves you

  • You are the first point of contact and sparring partner for management, managers and employees – from personnel questions to conflict situations to change processes
  • You accompany personnel and organizational development measures and support managers in further developing their teams
  • You develop HR concepts and processes further, for example in talent, performance and compensation management, and implement them yourself in practice
  • You work data-based: You acquire HR key figures, help shape personnel planning and provide management with sound decision-making bases
  • You lead HR projects and actively participate in change, integration and transformation projects

This is what you bring with you

  • An HR further education (e.g. HR professional with a federal certificate) or a comparable background and several years of experience as HR Business Partner or HR Generalist
  • Strong consulting and coaching competence in dealing with managers at all levels
  • Conceptual strength and implementation power – you are hands-on and also take action
  • Analytical thinking and safe handling of numbers
  • High IT affinity
  • Of course, you use AI tools to work more efficiently
  • An independent, structured and solution-oriented working method, sovereign appearance and persuasion
  • Enjoying a dynamic environment and the further development of a modern HR organization
  • Stylistic German, very good knowledge of English and confident French
  • Italian is a plus

Ready to make a difference? You think strategically, like working close to the business and want to take on responsibility? With us, you can expect a dynamic environment with a lot of creative scope.
