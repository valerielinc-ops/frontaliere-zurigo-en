Automatic service provider (m/f/d) 100% The Selecta Group, headquartered in Switzerland since 1957, is Europe’s leading provider of coffee and innovative food tech solutions in the workplace and in public spaces. Sustainability is an integral part of our business and focuses on the key areas where we can make a positive contribution.
