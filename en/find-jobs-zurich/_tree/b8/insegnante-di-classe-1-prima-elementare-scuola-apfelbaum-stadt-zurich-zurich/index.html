Full description

The day school Apfelbaum is looking for reinforcement as of January 4, 2027.

Key parameters of the body

Pensum: 53% / 14 WL and 0.5 task hours, of which 4 WL TTG, shared class responsibility with an experienced job partner. Schedule: Di (10 a.m. to 12 noon, 1:25 p.m. to 15:30 p.m.), Mi (TTG from 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.), Fr (8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., 13:25 a.m. to 15:00 p.m.)

Main tasks

  • Planning, preparation and implementation of teaching based on the curriculum
  • Follow-up and evaluation of lessons
  • Care of parental contact as well as planning and implementation of special events
  • Collaboration in the college, with the school management, external specialist departments and the guardians (with parental discussions)
  • Teaching diploma for the primary level, recognized in Switzerland
  • Enjoy teaching students from different cultures
  • Strong skills in classroom management
  • Willingness and joy at work in the integrated setting, together with school curative teachers and assistants
  • Positive attitude, teamwork, resilience, high social skills
  • a well-organized school that has been professionally run for many years
  • an open-minded, committed team that maintains a well-established and supportive cooperation in the Pedagogical Team
  • Selective support by class assistance
  • pedagogical KITS support and school management secretariat on site
  • free use of public transport/cultural offers with class within the city of Zurich
  • Workplace about 15 minutes walk from Oerlikon station, lots of greenery in the area

We are a large school community in Zurich-Nord with 19 schools. We derive our pedagogical attitude from the concept of New Authority and thus meet the daily challenges in everyday school life together and effectively.

The day school Apfelbaum is located in a quiet quarter in Zurich-Oerlikon and comprises 17 primary classes and 5 kindergartens. The school was extensively renovated a few years ago and supplemented with a new building and offers a modern infrastructure with a spacious gym and a bright library.
