Liaison Librarian Architecture, Urban Design and Spatial Planning (m/f/d)

80%, Zurich, perpetual

The ETH Library promotes knowledge, supports research and teaching and, as a trustworthy institution, makes the world of yesterday, today and tomorrow easier for you to understand.

For the building library we are looking for a committed personality as Liaison Librarian Architecture, Urban Development and Spatial Planning by 1 February or by agreement.

Job description

Her place of work is the building library on the Hönggerberg campus, one of the world’s most renowned special libraries for the fields of architecture, urban design, spatial planning and civil engineering.

The library offers a wide range of courses and consultations for students, lecturers and researchers. We support teaching and research with a wide range of physical media and digital resources, bring our expertise to joint projects and create an inspiring environment to work and learn.

Co-shaping the library: fields of architecture, urban design and spatial planning

How do students find the right literature for their design? What information do researchers need for their next project? And how can digital tools facilitate research?

You bring people, expertise and information together. Together with us, you will design our literature and information services for architecture, urban design and spatial planning, advise our library clients and develop new services. Your ideas provide important impulses for daily library work as well as for the use of digital technologies.

This is what awaits you

  • A versatile subject area: You select literature, develop our holdings and develop content so that it can be found and used well.
  • Exchange with students: inside and researchers: inside: You support with literature searches and questions about our information and in conversation you find out what our students need and bring these impulses into the further development of our services
  • Library work in a team: You contribute to the daily library operation and create comprehensible, accessible and helpful offers together with your colleagues
  • Contacts within and outside the ETH: You maintain the exchange with departments and specialist communities and make our library offerings known where they support research and teaching
  • Space to try: In projects, you will explore how digital tools and artificial intelligence can support library work, for example in the development, research or transfer of knowledge

Profile

  • Master’s degree in architecture, urban design, spatial planning or a related discipline
  • A library or information science supplementary qualification, ideally an MAS in library and information science, or relevant professional experience in a library or equivalent information institution
  • Knowledge in the selection, development and transmission of literature and information as well as interest in the further development of academic library offerings
  • Enjoy advising people, taking up questions and finding suitable solutions together
  • Curiosity about digital technologies and the willingness to try out new tools and critically examine their use for library work
  • An independent working method and pleasure in contributing your own ideas and developing them together in a team
  • Very good knowledge of German and English

We offer

  • A varied activity and a dynamic working environment in the exciting context of research and teaching
  • Working in a committed and collegial team
  • An attractive workplace at ETH Zurich Hönggerberg
  • Cultural diversity as well as numerous benefits, such as public transport subscriptions and car sharing, a wide range of sports activities offered by the ASVZ, child care and attractive pension services
