137471 | 80 - 100% | Oerlikon, Zurich | Hybrid

Are you motivated by the idea of actively shaping the future of our Corporate Real Estate division and shaping the working world of tomorrow? Does it appeal to you to develop innovative solutions for modern workplaces in a dynamic environment while always keeping the employee experience in focus?

In this role, you will strengthen the Finance division in the area of CRES Operations & Governance at Zurich Switzerland and make an important contribution to our joint success. As an intern in Corporate Real Estate, you will gain a comprehensive insight into the world of Zurich real estate and the processes surrounding our locations. Your assignment at CRES focuses on the team CRE Services SkyKey and the team Real Estate & Construction Priject Management: You get to know the entire breadth of facility management from the perspective of an internal service provider and are actively involved in the conversion and relocation projects in Zurich (e.g. Construction Management Support, PMO). In addition, you prepare decision-making bases for decision-makers step by step – always with the aim of creating comfortable, secure and future-oriented workplaces for our employees.

This is what awaits you

  • You will support our teams in facility management as well as in real estate and construction project management and get a versatile insight into the management and further development of our locations.
  • You participate in smaller and medium-sized projects as well as in renovation and relocation projects and take on independently defined work packages.
  • You support our project managers in construction projects and take on various PMO, coordination and documentation tasks.
  • You analyze and structure information, question existing processes and provide the basis for decision-making for different stakeholders.
  • You work on current topics and initiatives in corporate real estate and actively contribute your ideas to the further development of our working environments and services.

This sets you apart

  • Matriculation at a technical college or university
  • Solution-oriented, independent and service-oriented work with a distinct hands-on mentality
  • Teamwork, self-motivation and pleasure in dealing with people
  • Ability to keep track even under pressure and in unforeseen situations and to complete several tasks simultaneously
  • Sound CAD knowledge is an advantage and enables you to additionally contribute to planning tasks and projects.
  • Very good MS Office skills as well as excellent German language skills (C1), good English skills
  • Other official languages are beneficial
