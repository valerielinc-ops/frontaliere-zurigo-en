Where precision meets system understanding

For our Customer Master Data team, we are looking for a reliable and analytical personality with a strong sense of quality and structure. In this role, you ensure that our customer data is correct, complete and sustainable. You move around the interface between different business areas, analyze complex customer structures and find technical solutions for demanding requirements.

This position is particularly suitable for people who like to work focused and precise, enjoy clear processes and take responsibility for consistently high data quality. The role offers a stable environment with a clearly defined area of responsibility and an important function for corporate success.

Your profile

  • Commercial, technical or comparable basic training
  • Experience in dealing with ERP systems as well as good MS Office knowledge; SAP knowledge (in particular SD and/or FI) is an advantage
  • Enjoy working with data, systems and complex structures
  • High accuracy, patience and endurance as well as a structured approach
  • Very good knowledge of French and German
  • English skills are beneficial
