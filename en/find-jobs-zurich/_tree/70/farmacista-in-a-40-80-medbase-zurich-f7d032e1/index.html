Welcome to the Zur Rose Shop-in-Shop-Pharmacy Limmatplaz

The concept of the Zur Rose Shop-in-Shop pharmacies is unique and customer-oriented. Pharmacies are located directly in selected Migros supermarkets, so customers can easily do their shopping of food and pharmacy products in one place.

We combine classic pharmacy products and personal advice with a modern layout supported by LED displays and iPads to offer our customers a contemporary, pleasant and comfortable shopping experience.

In addition, customers benefit from the opportunity to collect the points of the customer's "Cum" and thus to gain real added value. Shop-in-shop pharmacies are operated by Medbase Apotheken AG, a company of the Medbase Group, which stands for high quality standards and a close connection to the customers' needs.

The everyday life in the Zur Rose shop-in-shop pharmacy is agile and dynamic, with a small team of 6 to 9 people. The pharmacy is located directly in the Migros supermarket in the shopping center on Limmatplatz and offers an ideal working environment in a central location.

The Aktiv Gym, with which a partnership exists, is just 8 minutes' walk away, so employees can benefit from a discounted subscription.

Working in this shop-in-shop pharmacy means being part of an innovative and modern concept that creates real added value for customers with a versatile range of services.
