You can look forward to that.

Your entry takes place in the team: Together we prepare you for your new role. This well-founded training takes place during working hours. At SBB, you develop your talents and discover new ways that enrich you both professionally and personally. As a specialist technician: in industry, you ensure that 10,000 trains and cars are in top condition – so that Switzerland never stands still. Enjoy your free time when others work: Night and weekend work are paid with us, for example with an hourly wage supplement of 16 francs for Sunday work. For night assignments, you get time surcharges of 10 to 30%, which allows you more free time. Further benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits.

Zurich Herdern, 01. February 2027 You can move that.

  • You carry out maintenance and inspection work on rail vehicles so that our customers travel safely and comfortably.
  • You repair components and replace components. You will learn the eye for technical details with us.
  • You test systems with modern software and carry out conversions and functional checks – we introduce you step by step.
  • Your commitment ensures that Switzerland stays on time. Whether with or without experience with rail vehicles – we look forward to your perspective.
  • With us you are constantly evolving. We support you in learning new skills that enrich you in a sustainable way.

You bring that with you.

  • Depending on your individual prior knowledge, you will undergo a three- to six-month, paid internal training, which will optimally prepare you for your task.
  • You like to be physically active and appreciate an environment where you support each other.
  • You have completed a vocational apprenticeship (EFZ) in a field such as electrical, automatic, mechanical, automotive or similar.
  • You have a secure use of digital aids, as well as very good knowledge of German (C1).
