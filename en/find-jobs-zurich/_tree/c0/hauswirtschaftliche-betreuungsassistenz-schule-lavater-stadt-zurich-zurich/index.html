Full description

For our hoard at the school Lavater we are looking for reinforcement on January 1, 2027 or by appointment.

They work 4 days a week (Mo, Di, Do, Fr) over noon in the catering team with our manager*in Grossregenerierküche and other household care assistants and prepare the lunch meal for our young people. They work only in the school weeks and take their holidays in the school-free period. More detailed information on working hours can be discussed during a personal conversation with the supervisor.

Their tasks include

  • Preparation of hot meals, salad buffet, snacks
  • Preparation and provision of food
  • Kitchen and catering areas cleaning services
  • General clean-up tasks
  • Compliance with catering guidelines, safety and hygiene regulations
  • Experience working in kitchens, canteens or gastronomy
  • Joy of preparing meals for children and teenagers
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Careful and organized working methods
  • Interest in working with young people
  • High social skills, resilience and flexibility

A varied and meaningful activity in a living environment. Our employees also benefit from attractive employment conditions. A motivated and open-minded team is looking forward to your application!

The school district Uto is one of seven school districts in the city of Zurich and comprises districts 2 and 3 with the districts Leimbach, Wollishofen, Enge, Friesenberg and Alt-Wiedikon.

The school Lavater is a secondary school with 12 classes from 1st to 3rd secondary level. Since August 2024 we are a day school with a state-of-the-art cafeteria in the renovated catering building of the school.
