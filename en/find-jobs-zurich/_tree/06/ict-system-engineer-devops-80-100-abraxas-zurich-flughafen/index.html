Full description

Our team supports the integration and delivery of our solutions on our own operating platforms or on customer systems, as well as the development teams with technical issues. It assumes an advisory position between developers, customers and the internal specialist teams. Together with your teammates, you ensured a stable, reliable and high-performance operation of the applications. You will process 2nd level inquiries, support the further development and operation of our application platforms and manage smaller sub-projects. For our location at Zurich Airport we are looking for 1. September 2026 or by arrangement a dedicated person as ICT System Engineer DevOps. Your role

  • Operation of our development applications based on VMware or Kubernetes / Rancher
  • Support of our DevOps stacks (Gitlab, Nexus, Harbor, Minio, Dependency Track, Sonarqube, TestRail, Jenkins)
  • Co-responsibility for the operation of the Atlassian Tool Stacks (Jira, Confluence, Crowd) for the entire Abraxas Group
  • Administration of virtual Linux and Windows servers of Abraxas development infrastructure on VMware and HyperV
  • Development of automations (Python, Ansible, Rundeck, Bash)
  • System landscape monitoring by means of CheckMK, performance analyses by means of Prometheus / Grafana
  • 2nd and 3rd level support for the system integration of an established enterprise application for customers throughout Switzerland Your profile
  • At least three years of experience in administration of Linux Enterprise Systems
  • Expertise in the operation of application servers
  • Practical use of databases (PostgreSQL)
  • Experience in scripting automation (Ansible, Python, Bash)
  • Experience in handling and operating Atlassian products
  • Knowledge of the following technologies desired: Kubernetes, Windows Server, CheckMK, Devops topics (CI/CD), NGNIX, Oracle databases
  • The joy of new technologies and the willingness to learn Your Benefits
