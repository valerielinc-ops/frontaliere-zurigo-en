80-100%, Winterthur / Smart Working Temporary period end of 2028

Shape your future – together with us!

As Business Analyst Claims (m/f/d), actively shape the digital future of the insurance industry – in an agile, team-oriented environment that promotes flexibility, personal responsibility and open exchange. Use your analytical strengths, courage to innovate and a clear customer orientation to develop pragmatic solutions that bring immediate measurable benefits to our customers. In the area of customer operations, we design individual damage and performance experiences for our customers. Customers are always our focus. With our customer-first mentality, we ensure maximum customer satisfaction.

In the area of customer operations, we design the individual experiences of our customers in the event of damage and performance. They are our focus and together with them we find the right solution. In this way, our customer-first mentality ensures maximum customer satisfaction.

This is what awaits you

  • Together with experienced colleagues from the product team, you will develop innovative solutions for our customers.
  • You analyze processes, take over requirements engineering and model specialist logic in DMN.
  • You create test cases, carry them out and accompany Enduser during the test phase.
  • You work in an agile, cross-functional team on target and target images and actively drive their implementation with your open mindset.

You bring that with you

  • You are passionate about business analysis and requirements engineering, ideally with experience in the large company environment and are familiar with common modeling and description standards (e.g. BPMN, UML, use case and user story notation).
  • You approach new topics in a structured way, quickly capture complexity and deliver high-quality results.
  • You have a good technical understanding and enjoy working with software developers.
  • Openness to new technologies, especially artificial intelligence and the willingness to use their potential.
  • Certification as a Business Analyst / Requirements Engineer (IREB or equivalent) is beneficial
  • Knowledge of the insurance industry is also beneficial.
  • Experience with CX/UX design and working in agile teams is a plus.
  • Very good German and good English skills round off your profile.
