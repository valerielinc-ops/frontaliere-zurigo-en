Full description

USZ Stettbach

By agreement

In this versatile project role, you are not only “administrator:in”, but also design our HR solutions. We are in an exciting phase: USZ introduces a new HR suite and integrates it into the existing landscape around SAP HCM. At the same time, USZ is converting its IT organization to an agile operating model (SAFe). You will become part of an agile team that drives solutions and actively shapes the technological change in healthcare.

Your tasks

  • Project participation in the introduction and integration of the new HR Suite into the existing HR system landscape of the USZ
  • Requirements Engineering: analyze professional requirements, business processes and HR data and convert them into implementable specifications and specialist concepts
  • Participation in the design and implementation of interfaces between HR Suite, SAP HCM and other systems
  • Planning, implementation and coordination of integration, system and acceptance tests, including error analysis and defect management
  • Participation in ensuring stable application operations during and after project implementation
  • Close cooperation with departments, IT teams, suppliers and external partners
  • Active participation in the agile team: maintenance and refinement of the backlog, participation in PI planning, sprint planning and retrospectives

Your profile

  • Completed training in computer science, business informatics or a comparable qualification
  • Several years of professional experience in IT projects with a focus on business analysis, system integration and data management
  • Experience in the analysis of specialist requirements, business processes and data flows as well as their conversion into specifications
  • Practical experience with middleware or integration platforms (e.g. MuleSoft), data mapping and error analysis
  • Experience in test management (test case creation, test execution and defect management)
  • Understanding HR master data, organizational structures and core HR processes is a plus
  • Experience working in agile teams is an advantage
  • Structured, analytical and independent working method, a high level of initiative as well as a team-oriented, communicative and service-oriented personality with very good knowledge of German in words and writing

Our offer

Become part of one of the most exciting HR digitalization projects at the University Hospital Zurich. You will help shape the introduction of a new HR Suite, combine modern technologies with existing systems and help make HR processes sustainable for several thousand employees. You will have the opportunity to deepen your know-how in the areas of business analysis, system integration, testing and application management and gain additional experience in a complex, modern IT environment. You benefit from a high degree of personal responsibility, short decision-making paths, a collegial agile team and a wide range of internal training and development opportunities.
