Full description

Shape the future of our service organization – with heart, competence and charismatic leadership.

As a leading technology company, Hoval has been inspiring for decades with groundbreaking system solutions for heating and air conditioning technology. To strengthen the Regionalcenter Mitte in Feldmeilen ZH, we are looking for a leader who is passionate, motivates people and leads our regional service organization into the future.

As an innovative person, you are responsible for the dynamic handling of service processes and contribute ideas for the further development of our service processes. Together with your broad-based service team, you will contribute to achieving the goals of the Mitte region and the entire company.

Your tasks – here you take responsibility

  • Operational responsibility for the intensive service area in the Mitte region
  • Personnel management of an approximately 75-strong team of specialists in internal and external service with 6 directly subordinate employees as well as their qualification and development
  • Responsibility for the regional service organisation cost centres
  • Sparring partner of the regional manager and active participation in the regional orientation
  • Implementation of Hoval service strategy in your region
  • Lead and cooperation in national service projects of Hoval Switzerland
  • Regular oral and written communication with private and large customers
  • Ensuring compliance with defined processes within the regional service organisation
  • Interface coordination between sales and service
  • Service controlling by means of relevant key figures
  • Promoting strong cohesion and a collaborative team culture

Your profile – that makes you our ideal match

  • In-depth technical training in building technology/HLKSE, supplemented by professional and business training
  • Proof of performance with several years of management experience in customer care, technical service, disposition, customer service or a similar environment
  • Proven experience in the aftersales area of a technical company with direct customer contact
  • Ability to sustainably develop the organization and lead teams through transformation and change processes
  • Visionary thinking and competent bridging between existing structures and new goals
  • Inspiring, charismatic leadership personality with strong communication, high resilience and assertiveness
  • Structured, solution-oriented working method
  • Very good knowledge of German at native language level, written and oral
  • Secure control of the most common computer applications (Windows, Excel, Word, SAP)

We offer you – an environment that strengthens you

  • An exciting challenge in a successful, sustainably growing company
  • Family, collegial cooperation with real du-culture
  • A performance-oriented environment with plenty of room for design and further development
  • Business cars for private use
  • A professional and structured introductory program

Are you our new, highly motivated team leader and want to become part of the Hoval culture? Then we look forward to your complete online application.
