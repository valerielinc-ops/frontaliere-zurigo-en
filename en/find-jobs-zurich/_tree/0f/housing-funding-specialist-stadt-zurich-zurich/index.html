Full description

The Fachstelle Gemeinnütziges Wohnen is the competence center for charitable housing in the city of Zurich. We are looking for reinforcement as of 1 February 2027 or by agreement.

  • They are responsible for the technical processing of applications in the context of housing promotion and assess projects in the context of the pre-application of low-cost housing according to § 49 b of the Cantonal Planning and Building Act (PBG).
  • They take over the deputy for the application processing of the municipal housing fund and support the responsible specialist with their practical experience in the construction sector in the assessment of complex applications.
  • They examine and assess value-adding investments in accordance with the applicable legal regulations and principles.
  • They support in the examination of the cost rent according to the Zurich model and advise housing development agencies on their application.
  • They participate in the strategic further development of the basics of housing promotion and support the development and introduction of a digitalized application processing.
  • Completed higher vocational training or higher education in construction or real estate, preferably in architecture, construction economics, civil engineering or construction management
  • Proven professional experience in construction cost planning, cost control and assessment of investment costs in new construction and renewal projects
  • High interest in the housing policy context and cheap housing
  • Public administration experience is beneficial
  • Independent and structured working methods, analytical thinking, problem-solving orientation, strong customer and service focus as well as stylish German
  • the opportunity to work at home and at work in the center of Zurich (near Stauffacher)
  • a varied area of responsibility, in a future-oriented environment
  • an appreciative working atmosphere, in a committed team
  • very good employment conditions

As a competence centre for charitable housing in the city of Zurich, the specialist centre for non-profit housing offers attractive subsidies and advises housing agencies professionally and professionally. Various instruments support the creation and maintenance of high-quality and inexpensive housing in the city of Zurich and make an important contribution to achieving the third target. In common with the charitable housing development agencies, the department ensures a good social mix in all quarters and thus makes an important contribution to a livable city of Zurich.
