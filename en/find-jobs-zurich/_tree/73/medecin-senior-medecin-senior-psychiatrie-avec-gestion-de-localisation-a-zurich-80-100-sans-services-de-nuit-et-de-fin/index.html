Sunday night, and you are already thinking about the next ministry? You love your profession, but no longer the way you need to exercise it? Then it’s time for a place where leadership doesn’t mean always being reachable. In Zurich, you manage a location, work psychotherapeutically, and the weekends are yours again. Since autumn 2025, an outpatient specialist clinic for psychiatric psychosomatic treatment has also been represented in Zurich. It is comparable to a psychosomatic day clinic with a connected ambulance in Germany. The concept has proven itself at the other locations for years, the team in Zurich is still young and growing. People are treated with stress secondary diseases, depression, anxiety disorders or chronic pain, with psychotherapy and body therapy in the center.

Their tasks

  • Medical management of the site, in close coordination with the clinic management
  • Professional leadership of the medical team and supervision of the therapists
  • Treatment of own patients, from first examination to case management
  • Quality assurance and further development of the site concept
  • Building a network with assignees, employers and insurance companies

Your Competences

  • Specialist title for psychiatry and psychotherapy. Your German title is recognized through MEBEKO, and Medimeo accompanies you through the procedure
  • Some years of experience as a senior physician, ideally in a psychosomatic clinic, a day clinic or an institute ambulance
  • Joy of leading a team and keeping it together
  • Very good knowledge of German (C1)
  • Desire to participate in a growing company in the long term

Their advantages

  • Leadership responsibility without night and weekend services, five days a week
  • Psychotherapeutically oriented concept instead of pure medication
  • A location that can still carry your handwriting
  • Short decision paths and a direct line to the clinic management
  • 5 weeks holidays and an additional 1 week for further training
  • Living in Zurich: German-speaking, international, with sea and mountains, and with good connections to southern Germany

Your change with Medimeo
