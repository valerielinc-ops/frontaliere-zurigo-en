Full description

Project/team assistant (m/f/d) 80 – 100% Permanent in Zurich, Switzerland Designing instead of just organizing: As a Team Assistant & Sparring Partner: in the leadership team of AXA XL you get involved, question and challen the leadership team, drive processes and structures forward and actively participate in projects. At the same time, you take on challenging organizational tasks such as planning offsites and are in the middle of the action in this role. In this way, you will build up a deep understanding of the structures of AXA XL in Switzerland and develop into an indispensable support for the leadership team! What you'll be doing

Among your most important tasks are

  • Proactive organisation, preparation, coordination and follow-up of internal and external appointments, meetings, events and (pre-) selection of venues and providers
  • Operational project management with active follow-up of tasks, resolutions and measures for timely implementation as well as ad-hoc (project) support of the leadership team in strategic projects, special tasks and organization-wide initiatives.
  • Creation and preparation of, presentations, business correspondence and decision basis for the leadership team
  • Ensuring and continuous optimization of processes and governance structures
  • Support in the conception, planning and organization of internal events as well as offsites of the leadership team
  • Independent monitoring and organization of the postal and e-mail receipt as well as a demanding, dynamic calendar of the country manager, including predictive prioritization of appointments, requests and processes according to urgency (if necessary in consultation)
  • Review and revision of correspondence of inclusive confidential processes – internally and externally
  • Maintenance of the supplier system for country management, including auditing and approval

You will report to the Country Manager Switzerland. What you'll bring For this versatile role, we are looking for a personality who likes to take responsibility, acts forward-looking and keeps an overview at all times in a dynamic environment:

  • High initiative, a strong sense of responsibility and the ability to proactively advance topics
  • Flexibility, agility and serenity in a dynamic, varied environment – with pleasure in keeping track of many parallel topics
  • Strength of enforcement and good stakeholder management
  • High organisational skills, prioritisation skills and the ability to work in a structured way even in the event of changing requirements
  • Experience in supporting executives and coordinating complex appointments, meetings and projects
  • Commercial training, ideally in an assistant or coordination function
  • Very good understanding of organizational structures and experience in dealing with change and transformation processes
  • Discreet and professional handling of confidential information
  • Negotiable knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • Very good knowledge of MS Office applications, especially Outlook, PowerPoint, Word and Excel
