Full description

Responsibility

  • You ensure material and production readiness according to the project and production schedules.
  • You derive the necessary material and production requirements from project plans, parts lists and schedules.
  • You create, plan and schedule production orders in SAP and ensure their correct project allocation and timely implementation.
  • You coordinate and initiate appropriate measures in close cooperation with purchasing, production, engineering, project management and logistics.
  • You ensure the quality and timeliness of the planning and production-relevant data in SAP.
  • You support the implementation of technical changes in current and planned production orders.
  • You recognize optimization potential in demand planning, work preparation and production processing and actively participate in their implementation.

Requirements

  • Several years of experience in demand planning, work preparation, production planning, material disposition or project-related procurement
  • Completed technical or commercial training with a strong technical understanding; further training in logistics, AVOR, production planning, purchasing or supply chain is an advantage
  • Very good understanding of parts lists, production orders, schedules and material flows in an industrial or project-oriented environment
  • Technical understanding of hardware, electronic components and assemblies, ideally with experience in printed circuit boards and electronics
  • Experience in dealing with ERP systems, ideally SAP, as well as in cooperation with purchasing, suppliers, engineering and production
  • Ability to understand technical documentation and material requirements and to identify dependencies between material, technology and production
  • High sense of responsibility, reliability and a structured working method in a project-critical environment
