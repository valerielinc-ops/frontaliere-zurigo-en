Full description

What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family company with over 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessories as well as the provision of financial services. Carmarket is the independent and trustworthy online car marketplace in Switzerland. Developed by the specialist trade, it offers a comprehensive range of immediately available and professionally tested vehicles. As an employee in the field, you are responsible for the sales of the products Carmarket and INSiDE in your sales area. You look after and advise existing customers professionally, gain new customers and develop your area economically. At the same time, you bring market and customer needs into the further development of our products and processes. Sales and territorial responsibility

  • You are responsible for your own sales area on budget, independently supervise it and continuously develop it further.
  • You win targeted new customers and deepen the relationships with existing customers.
  • You distribute the products Carmarket and INSiDE and advise the customers professionally and needs-oriented.
  • You document all activities with active and potential customers as well as contacts consistently in the CRM. Customer loyalty and marketing
  • You implement marketing measures and independently carry out suitable customer loyalty measures.
  • You ensure a reliable flow of information between customers and customer service.
  • You organize and optimize the customer service processes together with the participating bodies. Product development, projects and training
  • You develop the products Carmarket and INSiDE in close cooperation with the product team.
  • You actively shape various projects Carmarket and INSiDE and participate in their implementation.
  • You will conduct product training for Carmarket and INSiDE and teach their application in a practical manner. Your profile
  • You have a completed apprenticeship and ideally training in sales
  • Further education in the field of digital marketing is an advantage.
  • You will bring several years of sales experience in the Swiss automotive industry as well as ideally in-depth knowledge of the online advertising market.
  • You have a good business understanding and a high affinity for digitalization and technical services.
  • You convince with negotiation skills, final strength and a safe and professional appearance.
  • You work team-oriented, flexible, reliable, structured and independent in the field, office and home office.
  • You speak the language of car trading, are well connected in your sales area and have a strong customer orientation.
  • You master the languages of your sales area fluently, these are German and Italian.
  • You are online-savvy and experienced in dealing with the common Microsoft Office applications.
  • You work results- and performance-oriented and want to actively shape the future of Carmarket and INSiDE.
  • You have a valid driver’s license and bring the necessary travel readiness. What we offer you You will find a new challenge with exciting tasks and much more:
  • Numerous discounts (new cars, service work and spare parts, vehicle and health insurance, fuel cards, hotels and much more)
  • An own, attractive pension fund as an independent pension fund
  • A long-term business perspective
  • Individual training and development opportunities
