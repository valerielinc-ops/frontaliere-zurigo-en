Employees: in image digitization (m/f/d)

50%-60%, Zurich, permanent

The ETH Library promotes knowledge, supports research and teaching and, as a trustworthy institution, makes the world of yesterday, today and tomorrow more comprehensible. For the DigiCenter group, we are looking for a:n employee in the field of image digitization as of January 1, 2027 or by agreement.

Job description

The DigiCenter of the ETH Library is responsible for the retrodigitization of holdings of the ETH Library and the ETH. As an employee: in image digitization, you perform the following tasks:

  • Reproduction of image material (e.g. negatives, supervisory templates, slides) on camera installations
  • Performing quality controls according to predefined parameters
  • Testing standards and optimizing processes for orders and projects in consultation with those responsible
  • Assistance in the care of student assistants on the cameras

Profile

  • Training as a photographer: in and in-depth technical expertise in this field
  • Experience with Photoshop and Capture One
  • Knowledge of repro photography
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German and English
  • Communication and team skills as well as skill in the forward-looking planning of tasks
  • Great quality awareness as well as a high concentration and endurance, even for uniform tasks

We offer

  • Working in a committed and collegial team
  • An attractive workplace at ETH Zurich in the center of Zurich
  • Cultural diversity as well as numerous benefits, such as public transport subscriptions and car sharing, a wide range of sports activities offered by the ASVZ, child care and attractive pension services
