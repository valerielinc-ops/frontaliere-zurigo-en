You like contact with people and appreciate a vibrant bistro environment where teamwork and good mood count? In the bistro you can contribute your strengths, take responsibility and actively contribute to the success of the team.

Your main tasks

  • Customer-oriented and professional service of guests
  • Operation of cash registers / billing
  • Acceptance of goods, entry checks and storage
  • Self-assembly and disassembly of goods, professional preparation of food
  • Cleaning work, cooperation in the office
  • Compliance with hygiene standards HACCP

Your profile

  • Training for restoration specialist or hotel specialist EFZ or equivalent training in the food industry
  • Your heart beats for the gastronomy and the provision of services gives you pleasure
  • Careful appearance with good manners, helpfulness and good teamwork are a matter of course for you
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing mandatory as well as PC knowledge
  • Independent and careful work with initiative, communication skills and good physical condition
  • Operations from Monday to Friday between 06.15 and 19.00 as well as on weekends
