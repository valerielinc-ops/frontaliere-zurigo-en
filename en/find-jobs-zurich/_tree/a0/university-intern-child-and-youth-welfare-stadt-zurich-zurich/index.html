Full description

Per 15. On 1 January 2027 or by agreement, we are looking for a committed personality for a period of one year to support the Parenting and Maintenance Department (FEU) and the Child and Youth Assistance Unit.

Tasks

  • They participate in the support of the FEU post manager in project management and in conceptual matters. You take over the project management office in ongoing projects. In this function, you are the contact and coordination point, plan and coordinate meetings, further education and workshops. They prepare sessions and workshops, help shape the content and secure the results. You support the team in administrative and organizational tasks, in particular in room and appointment management, documentation and quality assurance. They participate in other projects, working groups and committees for the further development of the child and youth welfare services and offer corresponding support.

Profile

  • Recently completed university studies
  • Analytical, conceptual and networked thinking
  • Very good knowledge of office applications, including MS teams.
  • Service-oriented, self-responsible and results-oriented working methods as well as the ability to conceptually deal with complex topics
  • Teamwork, high commitment and willingness to learn as well as broad interest in various topics of child and youth welfare
  • First professional experience is beneficial

We offer

You will receive a high degree of independence in dealing with your topics and tasks. The size and complexity of the city of Zurich make it possible to identify trends and developments at an early stage and actively shape them. You have the opportunity to help develop new solutions and models. You will gain insights into an exciting field of activity in an established and motivated team.

About us

With around 2,400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a social Zurich. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.

Every day, the social services in the five social centres and specialist centres are committed to ensuring that people in the city of Zurich can count on effective basic social care.
