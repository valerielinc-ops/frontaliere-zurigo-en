Full description

The Group Technology Assurance Directorate is actively involved in the risk and control management of Migros. We are looking for an engineer who will technically penetrate our central workflow platform, strengthen our independence from the vendor and take security, governance and operations to the next level across all environments.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Cooperative Federation, Limmatstrasse 152, 8005 Zurich

Mansioni

  • Build platform know-how: internally develop a deep understanding of the architecture, data model and security mechanisms of our central workflow platform – and empower us to challenge the vendor at eye level
  • Environments & Deployments: You are responsible for the structured operation across multiple environments (Dev, Qual, Prod) and ensure that changes are migrated through the environments in a controlled manner with processes for testing, release and rollback
  • Integration of environmental systems: You drive the connection of process tools, specialist applications and other data sources via APIs and connectors and ensure that data flows between systems function reliably and securely
  • Security, Governance & Monitoring: You develop security policies for API protection, data flows and permissions across all environments. In addition, you establish monitoring solutions for transparency and coordinate support with the vendor
  • Advise & share knowledge: You advise specialist teams on solution design, conduct architecture reviews, document platform behavior and workarounds and promote knowledge transfer in the team

Requirements

  • Solid professional experience: in the technical handling of vendor-managed SaaS platforms in the enterprise environment and several environments (Dev, Qual, Prod)
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): in computer science, business informatics or comparable training
  • Knowledge of the connection of environmental systems via REST APIs, webhooks and connectors
  • Experience with databases (SQL), complex data models, workflows and business rules
  • Programming knowledge (ideally Python) for automation and monitoring
  • Ability to clearly formulate and enforce technical requirements towards vendors
  • Sovereign communication with departments, stakeholders of Group Technology Assurance and other partners
  • Agile mindset and experience with cloud-based ecosystems and effective use of AI tools to efficiently implement new requirements
  • German (flowing)
  • English (negotiable)
  • French (basic knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Central place of work: Central place of work in the city of Zurich (Limmatplatz or near Hardbrücke)
  • Modern infrastructure: Diverse work zones and modern co-working spaces with advanced equipment
  • Mobile-flexible working model: temporal and spatial flexibility in fulfilling the function (as orientation 40% home office)
  • Social benefits: Attractive employment conditions with very good social benefits
  • Design and decision-making leeway: possibility to independently shape and further develop the field of responsibility
  • Migros Bank: Free account management and preferential interest on the private and investment account

Recruitment process

  • Submit application documents: For a complete application, we need your CV, certificates and diplomas. A photo in the CV and a letter of motivation are not necessary. We live equal opportunities for all applicants. We have a standardized recruitment process. If this does not work for you, mention it in your application dossier.
  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Discussion with case presentation: Specific expertise is examined on the basis of a case or other assessment instruments. After processing, the result of the leader is presented and then discussed together. Usually, the team is also represented at the presentation. As a rule, the conversations are conducted by the superior, without a representative from the HR. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
