We treat active people between the ages of 18 and 70, ambitious amateur athletes and professional athletes, including many tennis players. Our focus is on knees (including cruciate ligament rehab after surgery), shoulder and return-to-sport. We work closely with Swiss Tennis as well as with referring doctors and clinics in Zurich. Your tasks

  • Physiotherapeutic treatment of orthopaedic, traumatological and sports-related complaints
  • Post-operative rehabilitation until return-to-sport
  • Active therapy and MTT in close cooperation with our trainers and kinesiologists
  • Fixed assignment on Saturday (8:00–14:30), supplemented by another half day or day during the week, depending on the workload

Your profile

  • Dipl. Physical therapist: in FH/HF or SRK-approved diploma
  • Experience or strong interest in sports physiotherapy
  • Joy of active therapy and training with patient: inside
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Other languages (English, BKS) are beneficial
  • Independent, reliable and team-oriented working method

What we offer you

  • Modern equipment: radial and focused shock wave, TECAR, cryotherapy, BFR training, dry needling, training surface
  • Young, interdisciplinary team of physiotherapists:inside, trainer:inside and masseur:inside with short distances to doctors:in
  • Professional exchange, internal training and second-opinion culture in the team
  • Family team culture: common running events (starting money takes over the practice), team Christmas dinner and much more.
  • Top reachable: 2 minutes from Zurich Altstetten station

Job opening by arrangement. On around 220 m2, we treat active people of all ages – from amateur athletes to professional athletes – with a focus on knees, shoulders, jaw necks and return-to-sport. Our interdisciplinary team of physiotherapists, trainers, kinesiologists and masseurs works closely with assigning doctors, clinics and Swiss Tennis.
