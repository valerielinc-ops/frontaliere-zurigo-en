Full description

We are looking for a dynamic and committed personality who wants to take on the role of team leader in the «Frankieren Post» team. With you as a team leader: our employees are in the best hands and we continue to make it, every day approx. 5.5 million letters and shipments without address to process.

You can do that.

  • You lead a team of approx. 16 dedicated employees, both technically and in terms of personnel, and ensure an economic deployment of personnel.
  • Together with your deputy, you form a strong leadership duo, you create the short- and long-term assignment planning, lead employee meetings and lead team meetings.
  • You create the framework for good team performance, attach importance to quality and compliance with safety regulations.
  • With your commitment, you lead and develop your team with concrete goals and you act as a contact person and coach.
  • In order to live up to your role model, you actively participate in day-to-day business. The working hours are in late service (14:00 - 23:00) and are provided in a 5-day week from Monday to Friday.

You bring that with you

  • You have a federal certificate in the field of logistics or a comparable education. In addition, you are ready to train yourself in the field of leadership / logistics. Ideally, you will already have initial leadership experience in a similar area.
  • Your leadership experience helps you to quickly gain a foothold in the new function. Organizational flair, flexibility and the ability to keep track of hectic situations set you apart.
  • You put the contribution as a team above your own goals, set the right priorities and communicate objectively and transparently.
  • Very good oral and written German skills as well as advanced MS-Office skills round off your profile.

With you, flexibility becomes everyday life. We promote progressive teamwork and value a balanced working life. This position can also be taken over by two persons in "co-management". You can apply directly as a duo or we support you in the search for a suitable person
