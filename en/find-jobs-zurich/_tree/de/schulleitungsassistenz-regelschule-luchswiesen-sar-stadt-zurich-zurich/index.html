Full description

To complement the school management team Luchswiesen we are looking for 1. March 2027 or by arrangement a school management assistant (SAR), 20% (annual working hours, spread over 2 days).

They work for 39 school weeks in the school secretariat. During the school holidays you have basically free. Excluded from this are the first and last week of the summer holidays in which you also work.

  • Administrative support for school management in organising and ensuring school operation
  • Turntable function for pupils, parents, school staff and administration
  • Telephone operation, provision of information and triage of requests
  • Logging, data and file management
  • Administrative management of the global credit
  • General secretarial tasks
  • Schedule and coordinate school management appointments (MAB, conferences, parent talks, etc.)
  • Commercial education (EFZ) or equivalent qualification
  • Willingness to gradually increase the workload in the coming years according to operational needs
  • Joy of contact with people of all stakeholders and cultures
  • stylish knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Organizational talent with independent and reliable working methods
  • Initiative, flexibility and humor
  • very good user knowledge in information and communication technologies and in data management
  • A diverse and interesting activity
  • Working independently and helping to shape administrative tasks
  • A motivated, helpful and humorous team
  • A good infrastructure
  • progressive employment conditions according to urban specifications

The school district Schwamendingen comprises the entire district 12 of the city of Zurich. Schwamendingen is a lively, multicultural residential quarter. With public transport we are easily accessible via Stettbach station and Oerlikon with various tram and bus lines.

The school Luchswiesen is a QUIMS school in Zurich-Schwamendingen. It currently comprises five kindergartens, sixteen primary classes and the care area. The school Luchswiesen has a long tradition as a student club, which offers the students an integrated full-day care.

We look forward to your application via online portal. Applications by post or e-mail cannot be considered.
