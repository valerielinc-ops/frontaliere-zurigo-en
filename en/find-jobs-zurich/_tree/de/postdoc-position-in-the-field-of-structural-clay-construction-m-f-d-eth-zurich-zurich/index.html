Postdoc position in the field of structural clay construction (m/f/d)

100%, Zurich, temporary

The Chair of Structure Design is located at the interface between engineering disciplines and architecture, with a strong focus on sustainable construction. For the research project "Think Earth" and to supervise several dissertations in the field of constructive clay construction, we are looking for a temporary 2027-2028:n scientific:n employee with experience in experimental research of clay construction.

The vacancy shall be full-time 100 % and temporary. The contract term is initially one year, with the possibility of extension depending on the availability of funds and performance evaluation.

Project background

“Think Earth” Regenerative Building – Innosuisse Flagship Project | ETH Zurich

Job description

  • Active participation in the "Think Earth" project, in particular in subproject 8 on hybrid wooden and clay flooring
  • Active participation in the research of the chair including the writing of publications and presentations
  • Supervision of current and future doctoral theses in the field of structural clay construction
  • Supervision of work of master and bachelor students
  • Participation in the development of the long-term research concept of the chair in the field of clay construction
  • Participation in the development of laboratory infrastructure for regenerative materials in general and clay construction in particular
  • Collaborations with interdisciplinary teams within and outside ETH Züiuch

Profile

  • PhD in Architecture, Civil Engineering, Environmental Engineering or Materials Technology (or similar) with focus on structural clay construction
  • Proven expertise in clay-building techniques and academic clay research
  • Experience in experimental research in one of the above fields
  • Strong organisational, multitasking and prioritisation skills
  • Joy of interdisciplinary cooperation, also with industrial partners
  • Enjoyment of the supervision of doctoral candidates
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • Motivated, independent, tackling, forward-looking, results-oriented

We offer

  • The opportunity to work in a world-leading academic institution with state-of-the-art facilities
  • Access to collaborative projects with academic and industrial partners across Europe
  • A supportive environment that promotes professional growth and development
  • Competitive salary and social benefits according to the standards of ETH Zurich
  • A vibrant workplace in the heart of Zurich with a strong focus on work-life balance
