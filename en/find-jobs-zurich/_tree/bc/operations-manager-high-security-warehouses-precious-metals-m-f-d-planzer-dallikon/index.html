Full description

Whether on the road, sea or in the air – our highly trained employees transport and store valuable goods and valors with the highest safety and precision. In this way, we ensure that valuables arrive safely, on time and as quickly as possible. Become part of our team and shape the future of logistics!

What you move

  • Construction and further development of a new high-security warehouse for precious metals including all operational, control and safety processes.
  • organisation and management of all warehousing operations, from receipt of goods to warehousing and stock management until delivery.
  • Active operational and physical cooperation in day-to-day business, including goods handling, relocation and operation of corresponding handling equipment.
  • Ensure full traceability of all goods movements through digital warehouse management, precise documentation and appropriate control tools.
  • Implementation and monitoring of regular and unannounced inventories, stock voting and consistent clarification of any differences.
  • Responsibility for compliance with security standards, daily controls and structured handover and escalation processes.
  • Recruitment, training, assignment planning and further development of the team with a practical and model-oriented leadership.
  • Coordination and cooperation with value transport partners, insurance companies, audit bodies and customs authorities as well as accompanying audits and inspections.
  • Competent support of customer visits including presentation of the plant and explanation of operational processes.
  • Continuous optimization of operational processes and further development of the warehouse organization as part of the site growth.

Why do you succeed?

  • Professional experience in security, value, banking, warehouse or high-value logistics or in a comparable, strictly controlled environment.
  • Knowledge of high-quality goods, sensitive materials or security-relevant stocks as well as their complete documentation.
  • Experience in operational stock management, inventory management and application of structured control processes.
  • Leadership experience and pleasure in building a team, accompanying it professionally and supporting it through personal cooperation.
  • Pronounced hands-on mentality with the willingness to tackle physically and actively participate in all operational activities.
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working methods as well as experience or interest in building new operating structures and processes.
  • High awareness of safety and responsibility, discretion, reliability and the ability to act consistently and calmly even under load.
  • Safe handling of digital systems and interest in the further development of modern warehouse management and control instruments.
  • Ideally, experience with precious metals, safe and custody processes, transport of value, insurance, audits or customs procedures such as duty-free warehouses, open customs warehouses and passar
  • Appropriate expertise can also be acquired.
  • Confident knowledge of German and English in words and writing
  • Further language skills are advantageous.
  • Professional backgrounds from banking logistics, cash center, safe custody, army, police, aviation, value transport, precious metal processing, watch and jewelry logistics, forwarding, air freight, customs warehouses or regulated cash areas are welcome.

Your benefits

  • Further education and development opportunities: With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • Family culture: You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Modern and ergonomic workplaces: Your health is important to us - our workplaces are modern and ergonomically furnished

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
