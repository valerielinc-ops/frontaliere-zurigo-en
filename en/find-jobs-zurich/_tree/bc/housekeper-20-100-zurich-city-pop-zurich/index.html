Location: City Pop, Militärstrasse 106, 8004 Zürich Employment: 20–100%

ABOUT CITY POP City Pop, part of the Artisa Group, offers fully furnished serviced apartments combining the comfort of home with the convenience of hotel-style services.To strengthen our in-house housekeeping team at our property in Militärstrasse 106, Zurich, we are looking for reliable, motivated, and detail-oriented Housekeepers.

YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Clean and prepare apartments and guest rooms according to City Pop quality standards.
  • Perform departure, stayover, and deep cleaning.
  • Maintain cleanliness in common areas, including corridors, elevators, staircases, laundry rooms, and shared facilities.
  • Change bed linen, replace towels, and replenish guest amenities and supplies.
  • Report damages, maintenance issues, and missing inventory to the Housekeeping Supervisor.
  • Follow hygiene and safety procedures, ensuring a clean and welcoming environment for our guests.

YOUR PROFILE

  • Previous housekeeping experience, preferably in hotels or serviced apartments.
  • Minimum conversational level of English required
  • German is a plus.
  • Strong attention to detail and a high standard of cleanliness.
  • Reliable, punctual, motivated, and able to work independently or as part of a team.
  • Physically fit and comfortable performing daily cleaning tasks.
  • Flexible availability, including weekends and public holidays.

WHAT WE OFFER

  • Flexible employment opportunities from 20% to 100%.
  • A professional and international working environment.
  • A supportive team and structured working procedures.
  • Modern facilities and professional cleaning equipment.
  • The opportunity to be part of a growing in-house housekeeping team.
