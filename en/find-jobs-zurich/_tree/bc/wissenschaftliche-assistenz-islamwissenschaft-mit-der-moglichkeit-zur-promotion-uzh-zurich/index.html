Asia-Orient Institute, Islamic Studies Department

Job opening 1 August 2027, temporary

An assistant position (80%) is to be filled at the Islamic Studies Department of the Asia-Orient Institute of the University of Zurich from 1 August 2027. The Asia-Orient-Institute is the largest centre for research and teaching on Asia and the Muslim world in Switzerland with about 100 employees.

Tasks

It is a scientific qualification body. In addition to participation in teaching (to the extent of 2-4 SWS), independent research with the aim of a doctorate in Islamic Studies is expected. The topic of the dissertation should be suitable for the field of Critical Heritage Studies. In addition, there is the partial assumption of general scientific and administrative tasks at the Islamic Studies Department (e.g. curriculum planning, study consulting or inter/intra-university coordination).

Profile

Completed study of Islamic Studies or a closely related subject (MA, respectively). Master’s degree pending) with a focus on the Arab world, very good knowledge of Arabic. Knowledge of another working language of the SWANA region is welcome. Teaching takes place in German or English. However, the administrative language of the University of Zurich is German. Teaching and administration experience in the university context is desired. Possibility for independent research within the framework of a doctoral project. The term of office shall initially be three years; an extension for a further three years is possible. We offer a varied activity in an academically lively and socially relevant environment. Diversity and inclusion are important to us.
