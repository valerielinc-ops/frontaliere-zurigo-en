Full description

In order to better support the implementation of our strategy, we are looking for you: a person experienced in banking, project management and IT who assumes total responsibility for the project portfolio Risk & Finance and shapes its further development. Take responsibility. Shape the future. Come on.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Bank, Schweizergasse, Schweizergasse 22, 8001 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Control of the budget pot, prioritization and consistency assurance as well as early detection of conflicts, risks, dependencies and synergies – coordinated with finance, risk and business management
  • Project coaching with constructive accompaniment and support of project leaders for problem solving – supplemented by ensuring governance for high-quality project implementation
  • Definition of measurable change goals (OKRs) as well as continuous tracking and reporting of goal achievement in management meetings with action-oriented recommendations
  • Need-based staffing of projects with internal and external project managers as well as their professional management and operational control to ensure project success
  • Collaboration with strategy streams, value streams, IT, legal & compliance, business controlling and overall bank PPM for technical coherence, resource reconciliation and strategic orientation
  • Management of smaller projects and changes in the areas of Risk & Finance

Requirements

  • At least 4 years in a comparable position: Project Management and/or Project Portfolio Management Control
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): Business administration or computer science
  • Strong analytical and forward-looking thinking skills for structured project management and early identification of potential risks and stumbling blocks
  • Ability to easily explain complex content
  • Sovereign handling of numbers and tools
  • Strong communication and joy in pre-engineering and challenging decisions
  • Familiarity with financial and risk issues
  • in German (negotiation confident)
  • French (advantageous)
  • English (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Child care: up to CHF 300 per month per child to external child care
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Migros Bank: accounts, mortgages and financial services with preferential terms
  • Advantages of cadre: One week holidays per year more for cadre members
  • Du culture: Experienced team spirit with transparent communication
  • Flat hierarchies: Permeable hierarchies and cross-level exchange
  • Central workplace: Optimally accessible, centrally located workplace

Contatto

Application and contact Moritz Forrer Team Leader HR Business Partner

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Personality/performance test: Under certain conditions, we use a personality test (BIP, Scales test, etc.) as an additional step to support selection. The results of the test may be discussed personally and form the basis for further selection. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
