Full description

In support of the II. We are looking for a civil chamber by 1. January 2027 or by agreement, temporarily for one year, two court clerks II. ZK (80-100%) As a court clerk, you process cases in family and inheritance law, in rental and other contract law, in debt collection and bankruptcy law as well as cases of care-giving accommodation. In the appeals and complaints transactions assigned to you, you independently file an application. They edit decisions, take part in negotiations occasionally and keep the minutes of the meeting. Your profile

  • Completed legal studies, preferably with a lawyer's patent.
  • Professional experience in the judiciary or legal profession.
  • Very good legal knowledge.
  • Precise linguistic expression, high independence, exact and forward working methods
  • Team-oriented, helpful and positive personality Our offer
  • You will get an in-depth and exciting insight into the judicial activities of a second instance.
