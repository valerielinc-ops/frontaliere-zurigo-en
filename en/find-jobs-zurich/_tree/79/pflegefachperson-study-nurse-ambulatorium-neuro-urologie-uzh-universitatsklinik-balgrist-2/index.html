Tasks

  • Implementation of study-specific and nursing tasks (bubble pressure and other neuro-urological measurements, collection and processing of biological samples, etc.) in the technical-medical field
  • Assistance in and organisation of clinical trials (including at other sites), including preparation of ethics proposals
  • Care of study patients, as well as promotion of cooperative, interprofessional cooperation with clinic, research and administration
  • Collection of study-specific data according to good clinical practice (GCP) with correct patient documentation and database management (e.g. KISIM, REDCap)
  • Continuous development and assurance of research and care quality at the interface between research and care
  • Contact persons for patients, study monitors, sponsors and international collaborators
  • Presentations and presentations of current research projects and results at international conferences in English

Requirements

  • Dipl. Nurse HF/FH or equivalent qualification
  • Several years of professional experience as a study nurse, preferably with Master of Science and CAS Study Nurse
  • Very good knowledge of German and English (minor level B2)
  • High initiative and willingness to become familiar with a specialized and technically demanding field and to continuously develop
  • Flexible, resilient and socially competent personality with patience in dealing with neurological patients
  • Strong sense of responsibility and quality with the highest accuracy and attention to detail in structured data collection and documentation
  • High consensus and decision-making capacity
  • Independent, structured working method, with strong organizational skills and a high technical affinity
  • Experienced user knowledge in common IT systems (e.g. Word, Excel, KIS, research databases)
  • Interest in scientific issues and interdisciplinary cooperation
