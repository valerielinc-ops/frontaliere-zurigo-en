Full description

Uroviva stands for a modern medical network and includes a special clinic for urology, nine urological practices and an andrology center. With approximately 55,000 consultations each year in primary, special and emergency care and more than 5,000 operations of all severity levels, Uroviva is an important provider of urological services in Switzerland. We search for agreement Assistant COO and Practice Management (80-100%)

Their tasks

  • Participation in cross-prax projects
  • Support in the quality management of practices
  • Creation, revision of processes and instructions
  • Introduction and training of work processes, work tools, etc.
  • Control of performance capture and training support
  • Operational planning of jumper services
  • Organisation of assignment events and visits
  • In the event of staff shortages, cooperation as an MPA at various locations

Your profile

  • Training as an MPA or FaGe
  • Good knowledge of Swiss healthcare
  • Very good knowledge of the tariff structures in the Swiss healthcare system
  • Very good PC user knowledge (Axenita, MS Office) is required
  • Willingness to travel to all locations of the practice network
  • Stylistic German orally and in writing
  • Strong social skills and responsibility
  • Organized structured and autonomous working methods, as well as the ability to set priorities and plan workflows efficiently
  • Joy of organizing events and events
