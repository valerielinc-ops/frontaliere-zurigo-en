Expert: in Engineering 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Expert: in Engineering Zurich / Work Smart, from now on or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

A varied working environment in a dynamic team of young and experienced employees awaits you. With our extensive know-how, we ensure that the facilities, buildings and tracks are always in perfect condition so that the maintenance of our fleet runs smoothly. Your commitment contributes significantly to the fact that SBB can transport more than one million people safely and reliably from A to B every day. Your place of work is in Zurich for the maintenance of the facilities of the North-East region. In addition, you have the opportunity to work partly from home or on the road as part of "Work Smart". Our benefits such as child allowances, free 2nd class GA and discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • Among your new tasks is the initial creation of maintenance concepts and maintenance regulations for new plants.
  • You map new plants in SAP EAM as technical objects with the necessary work and maintenance plans and plant documentation. In addition, you continuously optimize the master data quality of the region according to the specifications of data management.
  • We require your support in the Swiss-wide standardization and harmonization of the maintenance of selected plant types (e.g. continuous cleaning plants).
  • You will further develop existing operating and maintenance concepts with regard to quality, availability and costs and coordinate their implementation.
  • Conducting complex analyses (e.g. risk analyses, FMEA) is also one of your tasks. Furthermore, you derive optimization measures from this.
  • The control of the plant portfolio in order to ensure the early initialization of the replacement requirements together with the plant owners is also done by you.

You bring that with you.

  • Several years of professional experience in a technical environment, ideally in the areas of engineering, maintenance or asset management.
  • Your experience in the development and further development of technical concepts and standards brings great added value.
  • Good knowledge of SAP (ideally PM/EAM) as well as a secure use of the common IT tools (e.g. M365) round off your profile.
  • The fact that you have pronounced analytical skills as well as a structured, independent and solution-oriented working method also requires the willingness to travel within Switzerland.
  • For this function, you bring a basic technical training supplemented by a successfully completed further education at the level of Higher Technical School (e.g. technician: in HF) with or a comparable qualification.
  • Very good knowledge of German (minimum B2) in word and writing
  • Knowledge of French is an advantage.
