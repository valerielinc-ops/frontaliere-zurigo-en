Full description

This workplace includes a public self-service restaurant with free-flow area, indoor and outdoor seating, and a cafeteria. The meeting place in Zurich Altstetten creates a welcoming meeting place for employees, travellers and external guests. Our team inspires an average of 610 guests here every day with a wide range – from varied lunch dishes to regular action weeks to banquets, aperitifs and events. Are you our new team member who joined us as a cafeteria employee (60%) on 01 January 2027 or by appointment? You work from Monday to Thursday from 07:00 to 14:00 or from 09:00 to 16:10. On occasions, assignments can also take place until 22:00 or on Fridays. Your task menu:

  • Welcoming and advising guests at the coffee bar and restaurant
  • Preparation of various specialty coffees
  • Sale of hot and cold snacks and baked goods
  • Ensuring an appealing product presentation
  • Creation of the mise en place
  • Participation in catering and special events
  • Completion of general clean-up and cleaning work
  • Implementation of ZFV quality and service standards
  • Compliance and implementation of hygiene and occupational safety guidelines Your recipe for success:
  • Progress is an advantage
  • Practical experience in the catering / hotel industry
  • Good oral knowledge of German
  • Enthusiastic salesperson
  • Passionate Host
  • Winning and charming charisma
  • Enjoyment
  • You convince with your motivation This is what we offer you: We create teams that support each other and in which you are promoted. We work together and focus on everything that is good for you. Among other things, we attach great importance to pay transparency and equal pay. For this position, we offer an annual salary of between CHF 52,000 and CHF 56,000 (incl. 13. Monthly salary). If you would like to share your passion for gastronomy with us, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
