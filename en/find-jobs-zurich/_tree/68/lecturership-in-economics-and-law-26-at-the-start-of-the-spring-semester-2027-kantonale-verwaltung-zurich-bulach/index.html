Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School Zürcher Unterland Lecturership in Economics and Law (26%) at the start of the spring semester 2027 20 - 30% The cantonal school Zürcher Unterland runs a long and a short high school with all six maturity profiles offered in the canton of Zurich as well as an immersive class train (German/English).
