Full description

Eraneos is an international strategy, transformation and technology consulting group, dedicated to empowering organizations to thrive in an ever-changing digital age. By bringing together top-tier experts from business and tech, we help clients to continuously raise the bar in successful transformations, from strategy to execution. Whether we’re designing future-ready organizations, unlocking the potential of data and AI, or securing businesses with cutting-edge cybersecurity, we deliver results pragmatically. Our team of around 1,200 dedicated professionals is based in offices across Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, Denmark, Sweden, Austria, the UK, Singapore, and the USA, and seamlessly blends global perspectives with strong local roots. Around 450 employees work at our Swiss locations in Zurich, Bern and Lausanne.

That’s the job – Your main tasks

  • Data & AI-driven Transformation: Design and deliver Data & AI use cases and solutions for Life Science clients, from use-case identification and business case through to GxP-compliant implementation.
  • Consulting across the Life Science Value Chain: Advise clients on digital and process transformation in R&D and Commercial & Medical Affairs as priority areas, and where relevant in Quality and Manufacturing. Guide the selection and adoption of the right platforms (e.g. Veeva, Salesforce, Medidata or comparable solutions).
  • Organizational Excellence & Transformation: Help clients embed product-centric organizations and new ways of working, and transform into AI-first organizations, from operating model design to organizational change management (OCM) and adoption.
  • Project & Team Leadership: Lead workstreams or projects in multidisciplinary teams, ensuring high-quality delivery and fostering a collaborative, digitally-driven team culture.
  • Client Relationships & Business Development: Build trusted relationships with senior client stakeholders, contribute to proposals and RFPs, and help grow long-term key accounts.

This job is for you, if you bring

  • Strong Consulting Background in Life Science: 3+ years (Consultant) or 5+ years (Senior Consultant) of consulting experience in Life Science, with a strong industry network and a proven track record of delivering client projects.
  • Data & AI Expertise: Proven experience in designing and implementing Data & AI use cases in Life Science, with an understanding of regulatory and GxP requirements for AI.
  • Domain Expertise: Solid knowledge of processes and systems in R&D (Clinical, Regulatory, Pharmacovigilance) and/or Commercial & Medical Affairs, ideally with leading platforms such as Veeva, Salesforce or equivalent; experience in Quality or Manufacturing is a plus.
  • Organizational Transformation Experience: Experience in shaping and embedding new operating models, e.g. product-centric organizations or AI-first ways of working, including change management.
  • Communication & Client Impact: Excellent ability to convey complex ideas clearly to senior stakeholders, and a passion for shaping the Industry within Eraneos and delivering value for clients and patients.

As the icing on the cake, you bring along

  • Certification or hands-on experience with a leading Life Science platform (e.g. Veeva, Salesforce, Medidata or equivalent).
  • Experience in digital strategies, data governance, or master data management
  • Hands-on experience with agile at scale, product operating models or AI adoption programs in a pharma, biotech or MedTech organization.
