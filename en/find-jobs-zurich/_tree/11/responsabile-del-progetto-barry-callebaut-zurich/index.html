About the role

Within Barry Callebaut’s Business Optimization Programs department, we are looking for a Project Manager. In this role you are responsible for driving and managing Barry Callecaut’s strategic projects in a collaborative model across the organization, globally. As the Business Optimization Project Manager, you will be supporting the Program Manager in shaping transformation, ensuring global business alignment and driving change to support our growth agenda.

Key responsibilities include

  • Drive and manage Strategic Business Optimization projects autonomously in alignment with project objectives, supported by the Program Manager
  • Collaborate with a large group of stakeholders on a day-to-day basis and align with Senior Management
  • Play a pivotal role in cross-functional teams to solve challenges and propose solutions
  • Perform data analysis and thoughtfully communicate insights to steer decisions
  • Design the future journey, organization and processes together with your business counterparts to optimize the way we manage our business
  • Define, track and ensure delivery against the Business Case to measure the value generated
  • Develop change plans and drive all aspects of change
  • Build and maintain a good network across the organization

About you

  • University degree: Masters in Economics, Engineering or equivalent
  • Minimum of 5 years of experience in Project Management or/and Change management in a multinational corporate and/or management consultancy environment is a must
  • Positive, proactive, and hands-on working style
  • Proven ability in conceptual thinking, analytical problem solving and solution creation
  • Ability to operate in a multicultural environment
  • Excellent communication (written and oral)
  • Cocoa, Chocolate and/or Commodities business is nice to have
  • Fluent knowledge of English together with other languages spoken in the Barry Callebaut world
