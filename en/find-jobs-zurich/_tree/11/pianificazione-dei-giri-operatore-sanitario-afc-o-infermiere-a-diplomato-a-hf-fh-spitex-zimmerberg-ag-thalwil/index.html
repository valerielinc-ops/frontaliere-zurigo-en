Full description

As a non-profit organization, Spitex Zimmerberg AG is responsible for the care, advice, support and support of people of different age groups who are dependent on external help due to health restrictions. We enable the inhabitants of Adliswil, Thalwil, Kilchberg, Rüschlikon and Langnau am Albis to live a self-determined life in their usual environment. Spitex Zimmerberg AG has around 160 employees, spread over four locations and is one of the largest Spitex organizations in the Canton of Zurich. We are looking for you! You have a flair for organization and want to remain active in care at the same time? Then you are exactly right with us. In this exciting combination, you take on responsibility in route planning and are on the road as a specialist in everyday care. At the following locations we currently have vacancies as tour planning / health care specialist EFZ or nursing specialist HF/FH:

  • 80-90 % at the Thalwil site

Tour planning / Specialist Health EFZ or nurse HF/FH

Your tasks

  • Provision of needs- and situation-appropriate care services for customers: inside
  • Organisation of nursing and domestic assignments and comfort services
  • Telephone contact for all stakeholder groups of Spitex Zimmerberg AG
  • Advice and information to customers: their families
  • Cooperation and first contact with the family doctors: inside, hospitals as well as homes and rehabilitation clinics
  • Recording and data collection of new customers: inside
  • Preparation and planning of assignments for employees
  • Control of the expiration dates of medical orders and planning of needs assessments
  • Independent, self-responsible care and support of customers: inside

Requirement profile

  • Completed training as a health care professional EFZ or as a nurse HF/FH
  • Several years of professional experience in care
  • Strong organizational skills and networked thinking
  • Joy of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • High social and communication skills as well as a professional, customer-oriented appearance
  • Physical and psychological resilience
  • Flexibility in terms of deployment times
  • Good IT user knowledge
  • Category B driving licence

This is what we offer you

  • Holidays : 27 days holidays (from 50 years 32 days)
  • BVG : Flexible choice between the “Mini” and “Maxi” (AXA) plans
  • Diversity: A responsible, varied and independent activity
  • Working environment: A dynamic, growing environment with a motivated and collaborative team
  • Further development: Support for further education and other attractive advantages
  • Occasions : Large employee party twice a year
