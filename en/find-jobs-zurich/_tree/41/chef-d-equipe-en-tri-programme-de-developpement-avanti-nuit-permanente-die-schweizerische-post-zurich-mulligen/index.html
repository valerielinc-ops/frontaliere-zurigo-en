Full description

Do you want to take the next step towards leadership? Does it appeal to you to get to know the processes of a state-of-the-art logistics centre from different perspectives and to take on responsibility? In the 12-month “Avanti” development program, you prepare for a leadership role in logistics and gain valuable experience along the entire value chain. With you, we shape the future of logistics.

You can do that.

  • During the 12-month development program, you will get to know all areas of the Zurich-Mullen Letter Centre and gain a comprehensive understanding of the logistics processes.
  • In addition, you build up your expertise step by step by taking over tasks from incoming goods to sorting and picking up to outgoing goods and developing your skills in a targeted manner.
  • You accompany the operational processes and technical plants in the control room, gain valuable insights into the day-to-day operations and get to know the sorting centre from different perspectives.
  • In addition, you network with different areas, understand the overall context of a modern sorting center and thus lay the foundation for your further development.
  • You gain cross-divisional operational and management experience and are individually accompanied and supported by a member of the management.
  • After successful completion of the program, you take on responsibility in the role of Teamleader:in, which will enable you to advance your logistics career.

You bring that with you

  • You have a completed basic education and ideally have a further education at the level of professional card or a comparable qualification.
  • In addition, you have several years of professional experience and want to develop further in the direction of leadership responsibility.
  • You are interested in the logistics industry, enjoy working with figures and take responsibility in organizationally demanding situations.
  • In addition, you convince through your open, self-confident and solution-oriented personality and actively tackle changes.
  • You also keep an overview in hectic situations, act calmly and bring the necessary physical and psychological resilience for shift operations.
  • You are ready to work in the permanent night shift (working time window 20:00 – 6:00) as well as on Sundays (working time window 18:00 – 5:00). In order to get to know the various processes and processes of the sorting center holistically, you will also complete about 16 weeks of alternate assignments during the 12-month development program Avanti in the early shift (06:00 – 15:00) and late shift (14.05 – 23:00) including weekend.
  • You communicate verbally and in writing very well in German.
