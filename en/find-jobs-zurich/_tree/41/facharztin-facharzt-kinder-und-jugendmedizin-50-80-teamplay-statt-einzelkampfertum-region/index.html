You want to care for children and adolescents without working as a lone fighter? In a modern group practice in Zurich, you can expect an experienced pediatric team, AI-supported relief in everyday practice and plenty of room for your own priorities, with a long-term perspective. This established group practice combines family medicine and paediatric medicine under one roof, caring for families from newborns to adults. The practice accepts new patients without restriction; demand is correspondingly high. Several paediatricians work closely together in paediatrics. The team is international and multilingual, consultations are possible in numerous languages, including German, English, French, Italian and Spanish. Short decision-making paths and targeted investments in modern infrastructure, digital processes and administrative relief create more time for young patients and their families. The practice is located in the agglomeration of Zurich and is very accessible, so that a place of residence in the city can be connected with the place as well as a quieter environment in the green.

Their tasks

  • Independent care of children and adolescents in the ambulatory setting
  • Close cooperation and active professional exchange in the paediatric team
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with the other disciplines of group practice
  • Co-design of modern practical processes and digital solutions in everyday life
  • Introduction of own professional interests and focuses

Your Competences

  • Specialist title of paediatric medicine, recognized in Switzerland or EU equivalent
  • Enjoying teamwork and professional exchange in an international environment
  • Interest in modern outpatient pediatrics and openness to digital solutions
  • Flowing knowledge of German (level C1), other languages beneficial
  • Specialist title from Germany or another EU country? In the case of MEBEKO recognition and vocational training permits, we provide you with advice and financial support.

Their advantages

  • Get started with 50-80% and perspective to expand your workload later
  • A collegial paediatrician team in which no one stands alone
  • Spacious practice rooms and contemporary diagnostics
  • Digital and AI-enabled solutions that significantly relieve you of the burden of administration
  • A lot of room for manoeuvre in a growing practice
  • An attractive, flexible compensation model
  • Very accessible place of work with a long-term perspective in a stable environment
