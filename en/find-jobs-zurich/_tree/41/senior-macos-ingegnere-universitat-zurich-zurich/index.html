Central computer science

Job opening immediately, indefinitely

Central Computer Science is the Computer Science Competence Center of the University of Zurich. The Managed Device Services team is responsible for the managed workstation for Windows, macOS and mobile devices, with Microsoft Intune as the basis. For the fast-growing Mac fleet, we are looking for an experienced personality who shapes the macOS platform professionally and himself. We offer flexible working hours, home office and the employment conditions of the Canton of Zurich.

Tasks

  • Professional responsibility for the macOS platform in the team Managed Device Services, from architecture to operation to further development based on Microsoft Intune
  • Further development of the zero-touch deployment with Apple Business Manager and Automated Device Enrollment, including setup assistant, enrollment process and user onboarding experience
  • Creation of technical concepts and basis for decision-making for management and committees, assessment of new macOS versions and Apple functions with regard to operation and security as well as representation of the macOS view in overarching projects of the Central Computer Science
  • Design and operation of Platform SSO with Entra ID (Secure Enclave), the Microsoft Enterprise SSO plug-in and the compliance and conditional access scenarios for Macs
  • Strengthening of Mac clients based on the Open Intune Baseline for macOS, the macOS Security Compliance Project (mSCP) and the CIS benchmarks, including FileVault with key storage, firewall, gatekeeper and PPPC
  • Introduction and expansion of Declarative Device Management, especially for the controlled distribution of macOS updates and upgrades
  • Operation and development of Microsoft Defender for Endpoint on macOS in collaboration with the security team
  • Responsibility for application deployment and patch management on macOS with Munki, Installomator and AutoPkg, complemented by intune apps and proprietary packaging where an application requires it
  • Automation of client management processes with shell and python scripts, the Microsoft Graph API and n8n
  • Processing complex macOS cases as the top technical escalation level as well as knowledge transfer to support and team, including coaching younger team members and learners
  • Creation of technical concepts, documentation and decision-making basis as well as evaluation of new macOS versions and Apple functions with regard to operation

Profile

  • Completed higher computer science education (HF, FH) or equivalent qualification, combined with several years of experience in a senior role in Apple Endpoint Management
  • Many years of proven experience in the responsibility for large Mac fleets in the corporate or university environment with Microsoft Intune, including architectural decisions, operation on a scale of several hundred to more than a thousand devices and the professional management of macOS topics towards team, security and management
  • Very good knowledge of Apple Business Manager, Automated Device Enrollment, Configuration Profiles, Declarative Device Management and macOS security mechanisms (FileVault, SIP, TCC/PPPC, Gatekeeper, Notarization)
  • Demonstrated experience in the conception and implementation of Platform SSO with Entra ID as well as in the construction and operation of hardened baselines such as the Open Intune Baseline, mSCP or CIS, including handling exceptions and conflicts between security and usability
  • Several years of experience in setting up and operating an automated software and patch pipeline on macOS with Munki, Installomator and AutoPkg as well as in packaging and signing PKG installers
  • Very good knowledge of zsh/bash, good knowledge of Python, experience with the Microsoft Graph API for automating client management processes
  • Benefits include experience with Microsoft Defender for Endpoint on macOS, privileged access management on the client (e.g. Admin By Request), tools such as swiftDialog, Nudge or Escrow Buddy, workflow automation with n8n and an Apple certification (e.g. Apple Certified IT Professional)
  • Independent, structured and quality-oriented working method, pleasure in leading topics professionally and passing on knowledge, as well as strong communication skills towards experts and end users
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English
