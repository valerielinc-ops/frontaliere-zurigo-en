Full description

Key responsibilities Do you have substantial hands-on Swiss civil litigation experience, a proven ability to manage complex mandates independently and the judgment to advise stakeholders in a dynamic and global environment? Are you excited by the opportunity to help build a pioneering in-house litigation function? We are recruiting an experienced Litigation Lawyer at Executive Director level for our hubs in Zürich or Geneva. As a senior member of the team, you will:

  • independently take end-to-end responsibility for civil litigation mandates designated for full in-house handling, from fact-finding and legal assessment through resolution, including developing case strategy, advising internal clients, drafting pleadings and other court submissions, and representing the bank before courts and conciliation authorities
  • lead significant domestic and cross-border civil litigation matters in collaboration with outside counsel, setting the overall strategy and translating legal, financial, regulatory and reputational considerations into clear recommendations; where relevant, you may also be involved in criminal and regulatory matters in a supervisory and strategic capacity alongside outside counsel
  • coordinate business, legal and senior stakeholders across functions, including Finance and Media Relations, and ensure effective management of outside counsel in significant matters, with appropriate quality and cost control
  • act as a trusted point of contact and sounding board for less experienced colleagues, sharing expertise and providing practical guidance
  • help shape a modern, collaborative and AI-enabled litigation practice across Switzerland, working with state-of-the-art legal technology to set new benchmarks for how litigation is handled in a global financial institution, and contribute your litigation expertise to the continuous development of practical, high-quality workflows

The team Global Litigation Switzerland is a specialist unit within UBS Group Legal, with hubs in Zürich, Geneva and Lugano. We are responsible for civil litigation, criminal and regulatory matters across all divisions in booking center Switzerland, often in a cross-border context. You will work autonomously within a multilingual team and collaborate closely with experienced colleagues across UBS on significant litigation matters. The role offers individual responsibility while remaining firmly embedded in a collegial and supportive team environment.

This is a rare opportunity to combine the intellectual challenge and hands-on advocacy of private practice with the strategic influence and broader perspective of a senior in-house role in one of the world’s premier global financial institutions. The position offers exposure to cutting-edge litigation work, genuine autonomy and matter ownership at sustainable intensity, direct strategic impact and access to senior decision-makers, the support of an experienced, collegial team and investment in your continued professional development—without losing the litigation work you value.

Your skills and experience

  • admission to the Swiss bar
  • at least 6 years of post-bar Swiss litigation experience gained in private practice, including substantial hands-on court experience, proven matter leadership and independent responsibility for litigation mandates
  • a strong track record in complex civil litigation, including case strategy, written submissions, negotiations and oral advocacy
  • strong knowledge of Swiss civil procedure and sound judgment in assessing litigation risks
  • native or near-native proficiency in the language of the relevant hub German or French combined with fluent English
  • native or near-native proficiency in Italian is a strong asset
  • clear and credible communication with senior stakeholders, strong commercial awareness, pragmatic decision-making and a collaborative approach
  • enthusiasm for working with AI-enabled tools and an interest in helping develop practical, high-quality litigation workflows
