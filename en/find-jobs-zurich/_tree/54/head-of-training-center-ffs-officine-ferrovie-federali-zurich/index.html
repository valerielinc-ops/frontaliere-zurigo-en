Full description

Chief: in base 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Chief: in base Zurich / Work Smart, 01.02.2027 or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

As boss: at the Zurich base, you take over the personnel and professional leadership and use different management methods appropriate to the situation. You develop your organizational unit in terms of personnel, professional, cultural and business management along the strategy and ambitious goals and ensure high-quality service provision. You are also involved in everyday operations. Your workplace is located in a modern infrastructure that enables contemporary and efficient cooperation. You can move that.

  • In this responsible squad position, you take over the operational leadership for large and special events as well as for patrol service.
  • As base chief: you ensure the implementation of orders from the Chief of Operational Support of the Eastern Region and the operational control center of the transport police in daily front-line operations.
  • The personnel leadership of the group leaders is in your area of responsibility. In addition, you support and coach them in the personnel assessment of employees.
  • You will lead and attend meetings as well as consultation reports SBB internally and at partner organisations.
  • You assume responsibility for handling special police assignments and monitoring the working hours of employees.
  • You are jointly responsible for the creation of operational commands, operational documents, situation assessments, operational devices, files and documentation. You also handle requests from authorities and offices.

You bring that with you.

  • A positive basic attitude, openness to change, enjoyment of leadership as well as a pronounced self- and social competence.
  • High, emphatic communication skills as well as motivational skills in dealing with people.
  • You bring several years of experience as a police officer, several years of management experience and a high degree of identification with the police profession as well as good police tactical and technical knowledge.
  • A quick understanding as well as good IT and administrative knowledge, which you can apply in high quality.
  • Experience as operational manager: in large and special occasions and the motivation to work as a cadre in the order service.
  • You have already attended the professional card as a police officer and the leadership course II or are ready to complete it.

Your rewards and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. Therefore, further and detailed information on the wage system is transparently visible at SBB. This position is in requirement level J, regional allowance level 2.

This is the starting point.

The fact that you want to move Switzerland with us is the greatest motivation for us. Therefore, we refrain from a letter of motivation at this point. If you still want to tell us your motivation, you still have the opportunity to do so in our application tool. Making big is easier with you. This starts with your application.

That's how it goes.
