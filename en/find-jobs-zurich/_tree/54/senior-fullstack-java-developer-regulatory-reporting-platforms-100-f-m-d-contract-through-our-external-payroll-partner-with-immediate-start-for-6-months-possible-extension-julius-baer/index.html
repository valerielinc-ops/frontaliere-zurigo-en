Full description

At Julius Baer, we celebrate and value the individual qualities you bring, enabling you to be impactful, to be entrepreneurial, to be empowered, and to create value beyond wealth. Let’s shape the future of wealth management together.

We are seeking a Senior Full-Stack Java Developer with extensive experience in designing, developing, and maintaining regulatory reporting platforms for the banking sector.

YOUR CHALLENGE

  • Develop and maintain Java-based solutions for regulatory reporting platforms, ensuring scalability, performance, and compliance with banking regulations
  • Design and implement robust ETL processes using technologies such as Spring Boot, Apache Camel, and Talend to support reliable data integration, transformation, and data quality requirements
  • Integrate regulatory reporting platforms with upstream and downstream systems, including Business Rules Engines, REST/SOAP APIs, messaging frameworks, and real-time streaming solutions
  • Work closely with Business Analysts, Regulatory Reporting SMEs, Markets, and cross-functional IT teams to analyse regulatory requirements and translate them into sustainable technical solutions
  • Ensure regulatory compliance and full auditability by maintaining comprehensive documentation of application architecture, business rules, data lineage, data flows, configurations, and code changes
  • Support the delivery of regulatory reporting obligations through rigorous testing, monitoring, troubleshooting, and continuous improvement of reporting processes

YOUR PROFILE

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field
  • Proven track record as a Senior Fullstack Java Developer, with a strong focus on designing and delivering enterprise-grade application
  • Strong proficiency in Java and enterprise application development
  • Hands-on experience with Spring Boot and microservices-based architectures
  • Advanced knowledge of Oracle databases, including SQL and PL/SQL development for high-volume data processing and reporting solutions
  • Experience with modern front-end frameworks such as React or Angular, including JavaScript and TypeScript
  • Solid understanding of containerised and cloud-native environments, incl. Kubernetes (PKS/Tanzu) and virtual machine-based deployments
  • Solid understanding of data integration and ETL processes, with hands-on experience using technologies such as Apache Camel, Talend, or comparable integration frameworks
  • Experience with event-driven architectures and streaming technologies such as Apache Kafka and Avro
  • Familiarity with API design and integration using OpenAPI/Swagger, RESTful services, and service-oriented architectures
  • Proven experience in integrating distributed systems through REST/SOAP APIs, event-driven architectures, and messaging or streaming platforms such as Apache Kafka
  • Ability to create and maintain technical designs and documentation using UML and related modelling techniques
  • Experience with source control, CI/CD, and DevOps practices using tools such as GitLab, SonarQube, and automated deployment pipelines
  • Understanding of software development best practices, including testing, code quality, security, monitoring, and operational support
  • Fluent in English
  • German is a nice-to-have
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a proactive, can-do mindset.
  • Collaborative team player with strong interpersonal, stakeholder management, and cross-cultural communication skills, able to deliver high-quality results under tight deadlines
