Full description

138551 | 80 - 100% | Oerlikon, Zurich | Hybrid

Are you fascinated by risk management and IT? Do you want to actively shape how Zurich implements new life insurance products and technologies such as AI safely and responsibly? Do you enjoy analysing complex relationships, making risks transparent and formulating recommendations that support decisions up to the highest level?

As IT Risk Manager Life, you are part of the Risk Management business unit in the IT Risk Management team and actively participate in the further development of Zurich Switzerland. Together with the team, you will play a central role in monitoring and controlling IT risks. Your focus is on strategic and operational IT risks in the life business area as well as AI. You can expect a dynamic environment that promotes innovation, responsibility and cooperation and in which learning and further development are expressly desired. With your structured thinking and your pragmatic way of working, you develop IT risk management sustainably.

This is what awaits you

  • You are the central contact person for IT risks in the life business area and accompany new products, projects and migrations
  • Together with the business units, you carry out structured risk analyses and check whether the risk assessments are traceable.
  • You make risks transparent and support management in identifying them at an early stage, initiating appropriate measures and consciously using opportunities
  • You ensure that regulatory requirements, internal governance and the defined risk tolerance are adhered to
  • Together with Legal, Compliance and IT, you will develop the governance framework for artificial intelligence. You represent risk management in the AI review panel and bring the risk perspective early on into IT projects and new AI applications so that solutions are implemented safely and responsibly

This sets you apart

  • Completed technical training or corresponding studies, ideally supplemented by further training or certification in IT and risk management, for example CISA, CISM, CRISC or CISSP
  • Several years of professional experience in the insurance industry with a focus on life insurance, IT risk management or audit
  • Knowledge of relevant regulatory requirements and standards such as NIST, ISO 27k or EU AI Act – or the willingness to quickly and thoroughly familiarize yourself with new topics
  • Strong analytical and technical skills, high affinity for data and technology as well as a structured and independent working method. Enjoy working with different disciplines and the ability to convey complex topics in an understandable way
  • Very good German and good English skills in word and writing
