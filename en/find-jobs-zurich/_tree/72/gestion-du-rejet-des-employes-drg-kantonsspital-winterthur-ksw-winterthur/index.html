Full description

To support the DRG rejection management, we are looking for a committed personality for document management. If you have an eye for detail, appreciate routine tasks and always work accurately and reliably, you are a perfect fit for us. We offer you a responsible task at the interface between insurance, nursing, medical profession and management in a well-established, motivated team with an open, error-friendly corporate culture.

Tasks

  • You are the contact point for inquiries from insurance companies and other external agencies, check the facts in-house and initiate the necessary clarifications with the coders, the participating stations and medical teams.
  • You are responsible for sending billing-relevant documents to insurance companies, authorities and internal bodies
  • You support and accompany internal and external coding audits and prepare audit-relevant data and documents

Profile

  • You have a completed medical, nursing or commercial basic education or bring experience in a comparable function. An understanding of medical connections is required
  • You value stable, recurring work processes and keep an overview even in the event of a high workload
  • You distinguish yourself through a quick comprehension and an exact, persistent working method and perform your tasks efficiently, independently, and independently
  • You are IT-affine and quickly find your way around in new applications
