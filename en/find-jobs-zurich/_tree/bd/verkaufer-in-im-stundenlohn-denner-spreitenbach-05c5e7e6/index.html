Tackle. Move forward. Move something. Lots of warmth and a “hey, can I help you? – Team spirit”. And always reach the finish line together. This is Denner. This is our shop. And soon yours too. Switch to the fresh winning team now. Mansioni

  • Operate and bill the cash register
  • Accept fresh products and of course all other goods, check them and fill them on the shelves in your store
  • Check the best-before dates on the products
  • Ensure order and cleanliness in the store and in the warehouse
  • Correctly implement specifications in the store layout
  • Requirements
  • Ideally more than two years of professional experience: in retail is an advantage
  • Completed vocational training (EBA): in retail (food) or a related professional category is an advantage
  • Ready, already to work beyond "nine to five"
  • and to enjoy your free time when others are working
  • Physically fit, some Colis weigh up to 10 kilos
  • Familiar with PC, tablet and smartphone
  • and you quickly make friends with other digital helpers (e.g. for inventory management)
  • Enjoy being around people, a good dose of warmth and a positive attitude
  • German (good knowledge)
