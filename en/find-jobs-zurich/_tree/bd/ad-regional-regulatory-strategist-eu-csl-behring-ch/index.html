Full description

The Role Bringing innovative therapies to patients requires more than strong science. It demands regulatory strategies that navigate complex global requirements while maintaining a clear focus on patient needs. As Associate Director, Regional Regulatory Strategist, you will play a key role in shaping and executing regulatory strategies for CSL's development and commercial products, serving as the regional regulatory expert within Global Regulatory Strategy. Working closely with Global Regulatory Leads and cross-functional colleagues, you will help drive successful regulatory outcomes across major health authorities while contributing to a unified global regulatory voice.

Location: Glattbrugg, Switzerland or Maidenhead, UK (Hybrid, 3 days per week in the office)

Why It Matters This role sits at the intersection of science, regulatory strategy, and the business . You will help guide products through critical regulatory milestones, influence development plans, and engage with EU health authorities to support timely patient access to innovative therapies. By combining regional regulatory expertise with commercial and development insights, you will play an important role in helping CSL deliver medicines to patients while navigating an increasingly complex global regulatory environment.

What You'll Be Responsible For As a key member of the Global Regulatory Strategy organisation, you will provide strategic regulatory leadership across development and marketed products, partnering with global and regional stakeholders to develop effective regional regulatory pathways and achieve positive health authority outcomes.

  • Serve as the regional regulatory lead for assigned products, building productive relationships with health authorities and supporting successful regulatory outcomes.
  • Provide expert guidance on regional regulatory requirements, filing pathways, regulatory risks, and opportunities across development and commercial programmes.
  • Lead regional regulatory activities including submission planning, health authority interactions, core regulatory documentation, and responses to agency questions.
  • Partner closely with Global Regulatory Leads and cross-functional regulatory colleagues to develop and execute integrated global regulatory strategies.
  • Contribute to competitive regulatory intelligence, regulatory policy activities, and the assessment of emerging regulatory trends impacting development programmes.
  • Build strong partnerships across Regulatory Affairs, Development, and Commercial functions while supporting a collaborative, high-performing regulatory environment.

Eperience Required

  • Bachelor’s degree in pharmacy, Biology, Chemistry, Pharmacology, Clinical Medicine, or a related life sciences discipline.
  • Extensive experience within the biotechnology or pharmaceutical industry, including significant regulatory affairs experience supporting development-stage products.
  • Strong knowledge of global drug development, regulatory requirements, and product approval processes in EU and understanding across other key regions such as the US, or Japan.
  • Experience engaging directly with health authorities and managing regulatory interactions for development or marketed products.
  • Demonstrated ability to influence cross-functional teams and drive outcomes within complex matrix organisations.
  • Advanced communication, stakeholder management, and strategic thinking skills, with the ability to balance scientific, regulatory, and commercial considerations.

Travel Occasional international travel may be required to support health authority interactions, cross-functional project meetings, and collaboration with global regulatory and commercial teams.
