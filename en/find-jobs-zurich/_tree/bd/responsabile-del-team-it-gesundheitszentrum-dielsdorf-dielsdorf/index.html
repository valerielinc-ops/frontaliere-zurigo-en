About us

The Dielsdorf Health Center is a modern geriatric competence center with around 500 employees from 35 nations. Our offer ranges from counselling for older people and their relatives to general and specialized long-term care and care at home. As the largest public Spitex in the Dielsdorf district, we provide ambulatory services for our nine contract municipalities.

Tasks

  • Professional and personnel management as well as promotion and further development of the three-member IT team
  • Co-design and implementation of the IT strategy as well as continuous development of the IT landscape, processes and services
  • Planning, management and implementation of IT and digitization projects in close cooperation with the specialist areas and corporate development
  • Ensuring secure, stable and economical operation of the IT infrastructure
  • Active support of the application manager in the operation and further development of our applications as well as deputy of the system engineer and the application manager in the event of absences
  • Responsibility for IT service management, the management of service quality and the need-based prioritization of requirements and support requests
  • Management of external IT service providers as well as responsibility for contract, license and lifecycle management
  • Responsibility for implementing IT security and data protection requirements and ensuring 24/7 emergency management
  • Budget responsibility for ongoing IT operations and monitoring of service quality

Profile

  • Completed basic IT training with further training in business informatics (HF, FH, federal certificate) or a comparable qualification
  • At least five years of relevant professional experience, including at least two years in a managerial capacity
  • In-depth knowledge of IT infrastructure, application management, IT service management and data protection
  • Experience in managing and implementing IT projects
  • Strong leadership skills as well as enjoyment of the promotion and further development of employees
  • Analytical, structured and solution-oriented working method with a high level of initiative and a sense of responsibility
  • High service orientation, communication strength and the ability to prioritize different requirements
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Healthcare experience is beneficial

Offer

You can expect a versatile leadership position with a high level of personal responsibility, exciting digitalization projects and a lot of creative leeway in a meaningful environment. We offer you flexible working time models, the opportunity for home office and support in your professional and personal further education. In addition, you benefit from at least five weeks of holidays, a discounted staff restaurant, free parking, attractive employee discounts and other benefits.
