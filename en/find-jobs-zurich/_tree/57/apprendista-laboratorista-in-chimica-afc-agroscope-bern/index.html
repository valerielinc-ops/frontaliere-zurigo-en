Good food, healthy environment

Agroscope is the federal competence centre for research in the agricultural and food industry. The researchers carry out their activities at various locations in Switzerland. The headquarters is Bern-Liebefeld (from 2026: Posieux FR). Agroscope is located at Eidg. Department of Economy, Education and Research WBF.

We offer this

We are committed to the success model Switzerland and work for the benefit of the population. Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring different perspectives. We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health. We offer seven weeks of holidays per year during the apprenticeship and thus support relaxation alongside work and school. We support our learners individually and thus support their professional development more consistently.

Additional information

The research group Feed Chemistry provides analytical support for Agroscope research projects and official feed control (implementation tasks). The group analyses and develops methods to ensure efficient and up-to-date analysis that meets the needs of agricultural research. The laboratories are accredited to ISO 17025. In addition, apprentices are trained as chemical laboratory technicians.
