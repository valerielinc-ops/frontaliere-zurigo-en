Full description

For our patient admission and quality assurance at the locations Triemli and Waid we are looking for 1. December 2026 or by appointment a motivated personality for a 12-month internship.

  • With your friendly and courteous nature, you are the first contact person for our patients.
  • You collect the data of our patients and clarify the insurance status with the payers.
  • On the way to the station, you accompany the patients and thus ensure the most pleasant entry possible.
  • You create patient records, forms and documents.
  • In quality assurance you work and support the team with statistics.
  • On the phone, you will answer inquiries from patients and relatives as well as internal concerns.
  • Initial experience in the service sector is advantageous, but not a must.
  • Irregular working hours are no problem for you (7-day shifts, no night services).
  • You are familiar with Word, Excel and Outlook. Experience with an ERP is a plus.
  • stylish knowledge of German in word and writing as well as good knowledge of English
  • Other foreign languages are welcome.
  • Contact with people gives you pleasure, and service is yours.
  • Rapid comprehension and an independent and careful working method.

We offer you more than just an internship! You will get an exciting insight into the patient administration of a large hospital. Our city hospital world is characterized by an open du-culture, an uncomplicated, personal togetherness and the necessary touch of humor in everyday life. As a team of over 120 professional groups, we work every day in very different ways for the well-being of our patients. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions and excellent social benefits from the city of Zurich.

With over 34,000 inpatients and around 436,000 outpatients per year, the Stadtspital Zürich is one of the top 10 hospitals in Switzerland. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care - including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Every day, our 4,400 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care.

For more information, Yara Markwalder, Head of Department Patient Admission, telephone, is at your disposal.
