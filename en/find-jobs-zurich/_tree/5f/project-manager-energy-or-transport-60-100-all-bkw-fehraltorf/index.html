Full description

We make habitats worth living.

Eastern Switzerland

Arnold AG is looking for you for project management in transport and energy. You lead versatile infrastructure projects, mainly in the Zurich area. Further application possibilities are offered in interesting large energy projects.

Your field of activity

  • You lead projects with an order volume of around CHF 500,000 to CHF 5 million and are responsible for their execution, deadlines, efficiency and quality.
  • You accompany projects from the customer inquiry and offer preparation through the contract negotiations to acceptance and handover.
  • You coordinate the construction and commissioning and lead a small project team with three to four employees in terms of expertise and, depending on experience, also in terms of personnel.
  • You work closely with customers, engineering, suppliers, construction team and internal departments and ensure clear processes at the interfaces.
  • You recognize new project opportunities, support the acquisition and maintain relationships with existing and potential customers.
  • You contribute to calculations and offers and ensure the complete ordering of your projects.

You bring that with you

  • You have higher technical training in the electrical installation sector or network construction, for example at the level of HF, or a comparable qualification through many years of professional experience.
  • You bring experience in the management and implementation of projects in plant, energy or network construction.
  • You communicate very well in German and act bindingly towards customers, partners and internal stakeholders.
  • You work in a structured, economical and solution-oriented way and also keep track of several parallel projects.
  • You have a category B driver’s license and are willing to travel around 50 percent of your working time within the region.

We do not want requests from personnel service providers.
