Full description

For our area of technology and construction with around 47 employees, we are looking for a committed personality as a specialist engineer for our Team Experts/PL. We offer you a responsible and varied activity in which you can use your experience and expertise in a targeted manner and further develop it. You can expect a modern workplace in a lively environment, a collegial and motivated team and contemporary employment conditions with regular working hours. As part of our interdisciplinary team of experts and project managers, you take on responsibility and actively shape the technical development of our infrastructure.

Tasks

  • Electrical engineering: Planning, design and assessment of power plants in new and conversion projects, including variant studies and tender documents.
  • Coordination and cooperation: coordination of the project objectives with clients and users, coordination of the electrotechnical trades as well as cooperation with internal departments, external specialist planners, authorities and suppliers.
  • Quality, costs and deadlines: auditing planning services and execution quality, monitoring costs and deadlines, early detection of technical risks and ensuring compliance with laws, standards and guidelines.
  • Commissioning and handover: Accompaniment of acceptances, functional tests and commissionings as well as handover to the company with complete technical documentation.
  • Building automation: Technical assessment and coordination of building automation and its interfaces with other building technology.

Profile

  • You have a degree as a dipl. Electrical planning expert HFP, dipl. Electrical technician HF or a completed degree in electrical engineering (FH)
  • You will bring several years of professional experience in electrical planning, preferably in the hospital or health environment (e.g. medical rooms, safety power supply) or in complex buildings.
  • You have in-depth knowledge in the planning and assessment of power plants and their technical interfaces.
  • You work confidently in interdisciplinary teams and have strong communication and coordination skills as well as assertiveness against different stakeholders.
  • You are familiar with the relevant electrotechnical standards and regulations and apply them safely.
  • You have good CAD knowledge; BIM application experience is beneficial. Good MS-Office knowledge is required
  • You are sure to communicate in German in word and writing.
  • Ideally, you will also have further training and practical experience in building automation (GA). Knowledge in the planning, conception and integration of building automation systems is welcome.

You should be able to contribute with your ideas and strengths and develop. Your future is also ensured: You benefit from very good social benefits and a pension fund solution with BVK, which is significantly above the legal minimum.
