Full description

The Human Resources & Development department ensures strategic and operational HR management throughout the entire employee lifecycle. It provides specific services for the entire management in the areas of HR Management, HR Services & Controlling, Education & Development as well as organizational and cultural development. Good decisions require that we know what moves employees. This is where your task starts. You make the experiences and needs of our employees visible, analyze connections and thus create valuable foundations for managers and HR consulting. You combine analytical thinking with a good feeling for people and organizations. Your tasks

  • Conception and realization of our engagement and touchpoint surveys – from planning to evaluation and presentation to management.
  • Analysis of results, recognition of trends and comprehensible and addressable preparation of findings.
  • Further development of survey tools and introduction of new ideas into the Employee Experience.
  • Conceptual development and implementation of the annual process of systematic succession and development planning.
  • Support in the further development of our HR data and digital tools, for example on personnel development, potential and personnel planning.
  • Close cooperation with HR consulting and various stakeholders as well as providing a sound basis for concrete measures.
  • Actively contribute your knowledge to internal and canton-wide projects. You bring that with you
  • Completed university studies, for example in work and organizational psychology, business psychology, business administration or a comparable field of expertise.
  • Interest in people, organizations and data as well as in-depth knowledge of survey methodology and security in dealing with statistical evaluations and analytical questions.
  • Secure handling of Excel and AI applications as well as basic knowledge of programming languages (e.g. Python).
  • Independent and structured working method, experience in project management and enjoyment of trying new things.
  • Ability to adapt to different interlocutors and convey complex results in an understandable and convincing manner. You can look forward to a varied task with a lot of personal responsibility and scope for design. You work on topics that directly affect our employees and can contribute and implement your own ideas. You are part of a dedicated HR team with short paths, an open exchange and a wide range of development opportunities.
