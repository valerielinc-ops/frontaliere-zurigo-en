# IT Consultant Finance

Il tuo futuro inizia qui

You will work in a dynamic team, where you will be responsible for the further development of complex finance applications and processes and take the digital transformation forward for the customer. You will analyze customer requirements, develop innovative solutions and ensure their successful implementation. Thanks to your expertise, you ensure operational readiness and contribute significantly to customer satisfaction.

Highlights of the place

  • Development and optimization of banking modules in the area of finance, innovations and regulations to adapt to customer needs
  • Continuous consulting to improve banking business processes and support with technical and professional changes
  • Integration of new financial modules and applications for digital transformation
  • Responsibility for the end-to-end implementation of customer projects and ensuring customer satisfaction
  • Use of modern technologies for the development and implementation of customized solutions
  • Working in a dynamic and agile team with short decision paths
  • Promoting continuous development through self-responsible action and expertise in the financial sector

Your skills

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in computer science, information technology, a related field or equivalent practical experience
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in banking as a Senior Consultant
  • Experience in AI applications, ML algorithms and complex data modeling
  • Ability to analyze complex issues (architectures/concepts) and independently develop solutions
  • Experience in customer contact, sales or consulting as well as high social competence
  • High customer and service orientation combined with solution-oriented thinking
  • Experience in dealing with agile methods and project management
  • Experience in complex enterprise architectures and system integrations (REST services, JSON, etc.) in the financial sector
  • Tech stack knowledge such as Python, R, PLSQL, LLM, NLP, MCP, Agents, Kubernetes
  • Very good German and English advantage
  • The joy of travelling

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Benefits.
