We are

  • a motivated, dynamic team consisting of 9 physiotherapists: inside,

  • Training rooms and a Pilates/TRX room
  • a small group room and a consultation room.

You bring with you

  • Completed, recognised physiotherapy training with SRK recognition
  • Interest in orthopaedics and sports. You are:
  • motivated, team-oriented and interested in longer-term employment,

  • open and interested in further education and patient care beyond rehabilitation.
