Full description

With a draughtsman apprenticeship at AFRY Schweiz AG, you will set the course for your professional future. We offer you a varied and challenging apprenticeship in the exciting world of an engineering company. Over a period of four years, you will gain interesting insights into various specialist areas and conclude your apprenticeship with a federal certificate of competence at the end. As of August 2027, we have a vacant apprenticeship position at our branch in Winterthur: DRAUGHTSMAN WITH A SPECIALIZATION IN CIVIL ENGINEERING - WINTERTHUR During the apprenticeship, you will learn the following activities, among others:

  • Creation of digital models and plans in civil engineering (including public buildings, residential buildings, roads, track construction, sewerage and utility lines)
  • Creation of digital models and plans in bridge construction (e.g. road and railway bridges for repair and new construction)
  • Field assignments and construction inspections in cooperation with civil engineers
  • Construction site internship of at least 2 weeks

For the apprenticeship, you preferably have the following profile:

  • Completed elementary school (Sek A or good Sek B)
  • Interests / strengths in geometry, mathematics and physics
  • Enjoyment of technical drawing and precise work
  • Good spatial awareness
  • Confident handling of computer applications and interest in digital ways of working

We offer an exciting and varied apprenticeship, in a very easily accessible location, with modern employment conditions in a collegial and helpful team. We look forward to receiving your online application!
