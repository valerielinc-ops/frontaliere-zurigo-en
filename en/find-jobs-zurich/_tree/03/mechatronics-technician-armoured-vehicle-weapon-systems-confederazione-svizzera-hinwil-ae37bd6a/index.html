Your commitment to security and freedom The Logistics Base of the Swiss Armed Forces (LBA) is there for the troops. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss Armed Forces and, with its approximately 3'500 employees and 12'000 militia members, manages the material, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss Armed Forces. Whether in the workshop or office, indoors or outdoors: the LBA offers a wide range of jobs throughout Switzerland. There is something for everyone here.

The Swiss Armed Forces employ over 9'000 people in four military and around 200 civilian professions – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also catered for: the Swiss Armed Forces train apprentices in over 30 professions.
