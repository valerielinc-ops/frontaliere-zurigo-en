Move Switzerland with us. Management Assistant (m/f/d) Zurich, 01.03.2027 or by agreement, 60% Apply now

You can look forward to that.

Customer support and cleaning (KBC) ensures the well-being of our customers in long-distance and regional transport on a daily basis. This includes personal care on board, ticket checks and ensuring cleanliness and quality on the trains through comprehensive cleaning and quality services. For our administration team in the East region, we are looking for an experienced and committed personality as a management assistant. You can expect an appreciative team where cooperation, humor and psychological safety are lived. You get space to contribute your own ideas, improve processes and take on responsibility. You work in a dynamic, customer-focused team and are the hub for the KBC East Region and its partners. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • You will reliably keep the administration and organization of the customer support and cleaning area in the East region (KBC East) running and thus make a visible contribution to smooth processes for internal and external partners.
  • You coordinate appointments, processes and communication within the region and you are the contact person for partner organisations.
  • The learners (merchant service and administration EFZ) accompany and coach you and you as a deputy professional or practical trainer:r ensure the performance goals.
  • You bring in ideas and implement projects that simplify processes and drive the digitalization of internal and external communication.
  • With clear communication and assertiveness, you control requests, prioritize tasks and ensure reliable service quality

You bring that with you.

  • Working in a team, especially with learners, is fun for you and you can identify well with values such as honesty, responsibility, proactivity, freedom and potential development.
  • High social skills and service orientation as well as confidentiality and loyalty distinguish you as a person.
  • You show assertiveness in the matter and you can justify decisions and implement them consistently.
  • You bring a high IT affinity and routinely use common digital tools to digitize and document processes.
  • We require a completed commercial apprenticeship at the EFZ level and you already have profound experience in assistant functions. Ideally, you also have a vocational trainer certificate.
  • On your working days, you are on site, especially on Monday and Friday, so that you are present as a contact person and support the team on site.

Your rewards and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. Therefore, further and detailed information on the wage system is transparently visible at SBB. This job is in requirement level G, Regional Allowance Level 2.
