Institute of Evolutionary Anthropology

Job as per 1. January 2027 or by agreement

At the primate station of the Institute for Evolutionary Anthropology, University of Zurich, a position as animal keeper* is to fill 50% as of 01.01.2027. At our primate station, non-invasive behavioral studies are conducted on socio-cognitive abilities in new world monkeys.

Tasks

  • All aspects of the care of our colony of white bunch and list fumes (feeding, cleaning, enrichment, minor repair work, keeping animal lists on the PC)
  • Assistance in our behavioural observations and experiments

Profile

  • Completed animal care training or equivalent
  • Experience working with New World Apes
  • Craftsmanship desired
  • High degree of responsibility and independence
