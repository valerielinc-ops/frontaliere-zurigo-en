Full description

80% - 100% | Product and channel management | Zurich | Experienced professionals The Client Lifecycle Management team is responsible for product management of customer relationships across all customer segments. You ensure that our customer master data is reliable, complete and optimally usable for the bank throughout the customer lifecycle. In an environment with high dynamics of change, you actively shape the further development of data and process management and bring your expertise to a well-connected specialist team. Your tasks

  • Perform regular measurements and analyses of data quality and produce meaningful reports
  • Derivation, implementation and monitoring of measures to continuously improve data quality (e.g. process adjustments, work instructions, training documents)
  • Accompany and ensure corrective measures for identified data quality issues
  • Act as a central point of contact for technical and system-related concerns about customer master data and the associated client lifecycle processes
  • Accompany and coordinate technical requirements for the further development of data handling in distribution systems
  • Leading and moderating coordination and decision-making bodies in the context of requirements management
  • Support for general inquiries and topics in the area of client lifecycle management

Your profile

  • Completed university or university of applied sciences degree in business administration, business informatics, banking and finance or a comparable subject area
  • Several years of professional experience in data management and/or process management in banking or in a banking-related environment
  • Very good organizational, analytical and conceptual skills as well as high flexibility
  • High affinity for IT systems and digitalization as well as interest in innovative technologies
  • Strong communication skills to work effectively and appropriately with different stakeholders
  • Open-minded, committed personality with a high level of independence, self-drive, teamwork and a solution-oriented working method
  • Very good German skills in word and writing, English skills are an advantage

Our offer

  • Motivated crew with high technical expertise in which cooperation and mutual support are given priority
  • Exciting and diverse range of tasks in the area of client lifecycle management with many interfaces to different areas of the bank
  • Self-determined working methods with a lot of personal responsibility and the opportunity to participate in a dynamic environment
  • Centrally located, modern workplace in the lively district 5 with very good transport connections and the possibility to organize your work flexibly (hybrid)
  • Various opportunities to develop yourself personally and professionally, an employer who takes care of her employees and invests in them
  • Comprehensive package, which in addition to the attractive basic salary and a variable remuneration offers many benefits and perks – from A such as retirement provision to Z such as ZVV bonus pass
