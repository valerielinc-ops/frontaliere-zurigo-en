Full description

To support our team, we are looking for a committed personality as an employee consulting / administration from now on or by agreement.

  • You advise and accompany relatives of the deceased sensitively and competently and organize dignified commemorations, funerals and funerals in close consultation with them.
  • You provide expert information in the call center and advise relatives and grave responsible persons in all questions about the funeral and cemetery system.
  • You will process orders in the back office, funeral provisions and other business cases in our specialist systems. In addition, you are responsible for the administrative handling of grave care orders, from the preparation of offers to invoicing.
  • You manage the reception and are the first point of contact. You provide a professional, appreciative and situational reception.
  • A completed commercial education and experience in the consulting environment.
  • Pronounced service orientation, high empathy and the ability to adapt to the different customer needs and enjoy administrative activities.
  • You value collaboration in a dynamic organization, critically question existing processes and commit to their continuous improvement.
  • You have flexibility, high psychological resilience and openness to new things.
  • Very good communication skills in German and English. Further language skills are advantageous.

We offer you a challenging job in an advanced working environment with the opportunity to actively participate and shape you. You can expect an established and innovative team as well as attractive employment conditions and further development opportunities. Your places of work are in the City House of Zurich and in the crematorium Nordheim.

The annual wage for this job is expected to be between CHF 70,000 and 89,000. For applicants with more or less relevant professional experience, the offered wage may deviate from this range. In the course of the application process, the individual wage is determined on the basis of the municipal wage system. More about this under Wage and Premiums | City of Zurich.

Every day, around 220 employees at the Population Office (BVA) are in personal contact with the people of Zurich. Their needs are paramount in our organization, which includes the civil status office, the registration office, the funeral and cemetery office and the staff areas: weddings, notarizations, removals, changes of address, funerals and much more. We live the vision «s'BVA - for dis Läbe i dim Züri». In the funeral and cemetery office (BFA), we advise relatives on deaths, organize funerals, funerals and memorial ceremonies, transfer the deceased, operate the cremation facilities and the construction in the largest Swiss crematorium and manage graves in 19 municipal cemeteries.

Then we look forward to your online application!
