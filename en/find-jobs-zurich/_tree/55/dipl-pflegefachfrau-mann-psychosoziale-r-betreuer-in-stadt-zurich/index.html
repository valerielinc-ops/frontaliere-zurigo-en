Full description

For the stationary housing integration we are looking for a motivated personality on 01.12.2026 or by appointment.

Tasks

  • They support our 80 sought-after and mentally impaired clients during the day (no night service) in the dorm.
  • They are the reference person for the resident and coordinate the care services.
  • You are responsible for the implementation of the support process, including crisis intervention.
  • They work independently in a cross-professional team 365 days a year.
  • They are responsible for interdisciplinary cooperation with specialists and assigning bodies.

Profile

  • A degree as a nursing specialist HF/FH
  • Experience with addicted or mentally impaired people / acute psychiatry beneficial
  • High social and self-competence in dealing with difficult situations
  • Self-employed and self-reliant working methods and a strong sense of quality and service
  • Enjoying a job in a complex and challenging field

We offer

  • Very good social benefits
  • Promoting education and training opportunities
  • Fair borrowing
  • Safe work at the city of Zurich
  • Meaningful activity on the pulse of time

About us With around 2400 employees, the social department of the city of Zurich is committed to a social Zurich every day. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the quarters.

The stationary housing integration, an offer of the social department of the city of Zurich, is a supervised housing offer for socially and health-affected persons who do not want to live abstinently and are not able to fit in in a regular home.
