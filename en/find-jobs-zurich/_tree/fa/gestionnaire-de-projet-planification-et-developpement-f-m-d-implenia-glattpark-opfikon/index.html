Full description

As a leading Swiss construction and real estate service provider, Implenia develops, realizes and manages living spaces, working environments and infrastructure for future generations in Switzerland and Germany. Implenia also offers tunnelling and related infrastructure projects in other markets. The company combines the know-how of highly qualified consulting, development, planning and execution units under one roof into an integrated, multinationally leading construction and real estate service provider. Headquartered in Opfikon near Zurich, Implenia employs more than 8,000 people (FTE) across Europe and achieved a turnover of CHF 3.5 billion in 2025. More information at implenia.com

For the Zurich Neubau GU/TU region, we are looking for you as a committed and communication-strong overall planning manager (m/f/d). In this role, you will lead projects from the first customer contact to the building permit, thus shaping the decisive early phases of our design build and other types of projects. Your place of work is our headquarters «Connect» in Opfikon.

You move that

  • You lead complex projects in early phases – from initial contact with the building owner to development and building permits
  • You collect, structure and document functional requirements, derive clear project goals from them and define the scope of services
  • You will lead and coordinate interdisciplinary planning and specialist teams
  • You control the planning in terms of functionality, efficiency, eligibility, deadlines, opportunities and risks
  • You coordinate the building permit procedure and ensure a structured transfer to the execution

This is why you succeed

  • Completed training in construction (architecture, civil engineering, construction technology HF/FH or similar) forms the basis for your successful entry
  • You bring several years of experience from early project phases in the TU/GU environment or real estate development and have already successfully managed overall performance competitions
  • You are digitally affine and interested in developing efficient working methods and processes
  • You have experience leading interdisciplinary planning teams and are familiar with government processes
  • You can independently and proactively develop and successfully implement decision-making processes
  • You know the relevant standards, guidelines and procedures in Switzerland
  • Your communication is clear and convincing – in exchange with builders, investors and authorities
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and a structured and solution-oriented way of working distinguish you
  • Very good German skills in word and writing as well as fluent English skills

This is what we offer you

  • A multicultural working environment with the chance to shape a future worth living in
  • State-of-the-art technologies and a contemporary workplace at a sustainable company
  • A company that promotes talent and the training of its employees
  • The security of a financially healthy, broad-based group with strong roots in Switzerland
  • An exciting selection of benefits:
  • 5 weeks holidays and 9 paid bridge days
  • An in-house pension fund with very good coverage and 3 contribution levels
  • Numerous discounts on products and services of well-known providers
