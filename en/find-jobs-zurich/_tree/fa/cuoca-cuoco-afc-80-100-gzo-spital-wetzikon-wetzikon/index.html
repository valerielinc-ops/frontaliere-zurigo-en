The hospital kitchen of the GZO Spital Wetzikon is one of the best in Switzerland: in 2023 it was named the "best hospital kitchen" in the country by public voting at the Viktor Award, which is considered the "Oscar of the healthcare industry". This award is not a snapshot, but an expression of what our 30-strong kitchen team does every day: in the morning, noon and evening, around 130 freshly prepared meals for our patients in up to 37 different diets. In addition, around 300 meals for employees are served at noon as well as the catering for the GZO-affiliated daycare centre and the Bärentswil school lunch table.
