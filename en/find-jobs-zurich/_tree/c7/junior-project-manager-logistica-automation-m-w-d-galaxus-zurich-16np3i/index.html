Full description

In the role of (Junior) Project Manager - Robotics & Automation, you support us in expanding our storage capacities and automating our processes. You take responsibility for projects in the logistics environment and actively shape the future of Digitec Galaxus.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Headquarters, Pfingstweidstrasse 60b, 8005 Zurich

Mansioni

  • Take overall responsibility for small and medium-sized projects in the intralogistics environment
  • As a project manager, you lead project teams of four to five project leaders.
  • In the subprojects you also work yourself and concentrate on extensions of the conveying technology as well as on structural adjustments such as stage construction, electrical installations, sprinkler construction and the like.
  • You work closely with logistics suppliers and specialist planners and ensure that the logistics system and structural measures are optimally coordinated
  • As a committed part of solution finding, you combine analytics and technology for added value research, analysis and calculations in the conception phase, you carry out, prepare the information and present it to the management.
  • You manage internal and external stakeholders, you also take over the resource and scheduling as well as the project controlling

Requirements

  • 1-3 years: Logistics & warehouse automation (robot solutions, shelf and system extensions)
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): industrial engineering, computer science / business informatics, mechanical engineering / mechatronics
  • A completed technical degree (ETH/Uni/FH) with a focus on mechanical engineering, mechatronics, electrical engineering, automation technology, industrial engineering or a similar direction will bring you
  • You have already gained initial experience in the project management of complex technical systems – ideally in industrial automation, intralogistics or production environments
  • Practical experience with robotics, AGV/AMR or material flow engineering projects is a clear advantage
  • High receptiveness and willingness to learn characterize you
  • You approach problems analytically and solve them methodically and structured
  • As a solution-oriented pragmatist and organizational talent, you also personally and actively participate in leading the project to success.
  • Stylish-confident knowledge of German and English in word and writing you bring with you
  • Using creativity and initiative to solve problems and achieve goals
  • Building trust through consistent and reliable actions
  • German (flowing)
  • English (flowing)
