Full description

Quality at all levels is the key to true well-being. As an employee or member of the SWICA team, you understand holistic health as a human service. You work with professionals like you who share this claim. Everyone pulls on the same rope; that's a good feeling. Together, we create solutions that really take things forward. This is how you shape health

  • Personnel and technical management of a team
  • You support and motivate your team to pursue the ambitious goals together with them
  • Ensuring an optimal daily business process by planning, coordinating and controlling the individual processes and KPIs
  • Share responsibility for a continuous improvement process
  • Advice and support of needs-based insurance solutions as well as all concerns on the telephone, by mail and customer portal
  • Recruitment and training of new employees
  • Solution-oriented handling of all customer inquiries and complaints, with the aim of achieving the best customer satisfaction
  • Participation in projects

This sets you apart

  • We require commercial training with experience in the insurance industry
  • First leadership experience is desirable
  • VBV training available or willing to complete it as soon as possible
  • You take customer concerns seriously and offer the best possible solution
  • Dedication, integrity and your customer-oriented thinking round off your profile
  • You recognize the potential of your employees and support and promote their development
  • Very good knowledge of German, other languages are advantageous

Why SWICA

  • We build on humanity. That is why we focus on openness, respect and trust in our everyday work. In doing so, we take on responsibility and think in terms of solutions – together, for our customers. This is also why we have been number 1 in customer satisfaction for many years.
  • We trust you and give you space. With annual working hours, at least 5 weeks holidays and on request also holiday purchase. With part-time models and a generous home office within the scope of the possibilities of the job, you make your everyday life largely flexible.
  • We value and reward your work – with a market-based wage, up to 1,500 francs annual fee for personal wishes and a generous participation in the basic and additional insurance for you and your family.
  • We offer you prospects. With a generous training regulations, we support your professional and personal development – for your role today and for your goals of tomorrow.
  • Because health is everything. With our company health management and region-specific offers, we take care of your well-being. And if it comes otherwise: 100% net wage continued payment.
  • We think long term. With three savings plans, pensum-dependent coordination deduction and benefits well above the legal minimum, you ensure good for your future.
