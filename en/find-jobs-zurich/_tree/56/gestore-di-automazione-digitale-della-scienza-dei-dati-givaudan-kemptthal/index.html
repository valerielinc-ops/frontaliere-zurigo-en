Head of DADS (Digitalization, Automation, Data Science) - A Digital Automation Data Science Manager

Purpose: This role focuses on identifying manual bottlenecks in digital processes within Fragrance S&T and replacing them with automated, data-driven solutions. You will Lead a design data pipelines, deploy predictive models, and leverage modern AI tools to accelerate research and decision-making within S&T and the local Givaudan IT landscape.

You will

  • Lead the digital transformation and evolution at Givaudan in Fragrance S&T
  • Lead digitalisation, automation and data science projects within Fragrance S&T, in collaboration with scientists and IT.
  • Integrate modern AI tools like LLM and predictive models (QSAR, QSOR) into existing and new applications to accelerate decision-making for research eg. designing new molecules, new complex systems and optimising processes for more efficient and effective solutions
  • Scout and implement modeling tools facilitating scale up activities and any other research opportunities
  • Align with corporate IT and collaborators to deploy the solutions to production or other Givaudan infrastructure
  • Configure, deploy and integrate third party tools to facilitate daily research, development and industrialisation tasks
  • Design exploration applications and data pipelines to capture, analyse and visualise data generated across diverse research activities even finding links between CoE’s
  • Provide expertise, guidance and training to scientists (Fragrance S&T community) for data analysis and data visualisation.
  • In close collaboration with IT, follow-up the deployment of solutions directly with end users to address change management, bug-fixing and improvements in user experience.
  • Analyse and further improve existing workflow and processes
  • Collaborate tightly with scientists & labs to identify and replace manual bottlenecks in digital processes with automated workflows across the research organization.
  • Engage the teams in adopting new and efficient ways of working. Keep the S&T organisation up to date with latest best practice in the field

You are

  • Degree (MSc, PhD) in a Life Science discipline (Chemistry, Chemoinformatic, Biology)
  • 10+ years of experience in a research informatics environment. Proven track-record of successfully implementing research informatics projects
  • Strong experience in software development and machine learning with Python, combined with solid expertise in system architecture, SQL/database design, and integration with complex web APIs. Capable of working effectively in Linux environments when needed
  • Understanding of core cheminformatics principles (chemical structure formats, chemical structure searching, fingerprints, descriptors, complex formulations)
  • Understanding of hard chemical data with human responses across psychological, physiological and sensorial, and Ontologies (knowledge graph expertise) is a plus.
  • Willingness to learn new tools and technical skills
  • Strong communication and decision-making skills across diverse stakeholder groups
  • A knowledge of best practice within the current University ecosystem
