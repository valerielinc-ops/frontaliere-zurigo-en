Shape your future – together with us!

At AXA, you can expect a culture in which you can fully develop your strengths. We value critical food for thought and innovative contributions, no matter what hierarchical level they come from. Write your own success story with us now. Know you can.

In the field of data, technology and innovation, around 600 software and system developers work in Switzerland: in-house and develop the technology of tomorrow. We also collaborate in global teams and networks. We shape and integrate the latest tools, organize hackathons and our agile product teams run self-organized and are self-responsible from A to Z.

This is what awaits you

  • You are part of the product team "Genialytics" and share responsibility for the development of the Agentic Offering - a fundamentally new way to create offers in the P&C business. From architecture to productive operation
  • You design, implement and operate robust, LLM-based pipelines and P&C underwriting workflows with the ambition to create measurable value for the business
  • Work closely with data scientists, engineers and business stakeholders to transform insurance requirements into working, reliable AI systems
  • As a lead, you are a sparring partner and technical initiator of the team. You actively share your knowledge – through code reviews, architectural decisions, professional exchange and the establishment of best practices
  • You follow the latest developments in the field of Agentic & Generative AI, evaluate them critically and bring the relevant into the team and to your stakeholders – as a thought leader with ground adherence
  • You consciously move away from your usual paths: you shape something new, question assumptions and have the courage to ask even uncomfortable questions – because this is where the best solutions emerge.

You bring that with you

  • You bring in-depth experience with productive AI systems – from Agentic AI to RAG and Context Engineering. You know the limits of language models from practice and how to deal with them. Solid software engineering fundamentals are a matter of course for you
  • Python and its ecosystems (e.g. LangChain) are part of your everyday life. You build APIs, orchestrate complex workflows and integrate AI components into existing system landscapes. Experience with GCP, MLOps, CI/CD or container technologies round off your profile
  • You think in terms of overall architectures and play a key role in shaping them. Whether latency, cost, security or maintainability – you weigh decisions holistically and know when an agentic approach makes the difference and when a leaner solution is the better choice
  • You understand insurance products and processes – or you burn to understand them. You know: The best AI solutions are not created in a technical vacuum, but where domain knowledge meets engineering. Knowledge in the P&C business, in the offer or in insurance-typical processes is a real plus
  • You act as a thought leader: You set technical impulses, recognize trends early and translate them into concrete solutions for the Agentic Offering. You bring in points of view, question the status quo and inspire your environment – internally and externally
  • You lead by example and attitude. You set the technical direction, get involved yourself and create space for your team’s ideas. Technical leadership does not mean for you to know all the answers – but to find the best one together
  • You work proactively, on your own responsibility and navigate sovereignly through ambiguity. You find your way by tackling, testing hypotheses and learning from mistakes – because you know: iteration is not a detour, but the way.
  • You speak fluent German and English
