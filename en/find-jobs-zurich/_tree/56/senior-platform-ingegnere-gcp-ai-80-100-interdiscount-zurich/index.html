Full description

  • Job opening: immediately or by agreement
  • Field of expertise: Computer Science
  • Location: Interdiscount, Baslerstrasse 52, 8048 Zurich

At Interdiscount, we offer our customers the best shopping experience in Switzerland. Engage in a dynamic and entrepreneurial working environment. Take responsibility, be proactive with us and develop further.

Tasks

  • You are part of a system team and thus an important hub in our agile release train.
  • You will be responsible for the establishment, operation and further development of our GCP cloud infrastructure and will take over DevOps activities for the shop and feature teams in the areas of CI/CD, cloud services, performance and monitoring.
  • Connect GCP Engineering with AI Engineering and develop stable, scalable cloud platforms for data- and AI-based solutions.
  • You analyse faults and error states, initiate appropriate measures for correction and monitor their implementation.

Requirements

  • You have a tertiary education or training (FH, Uni, ETH) in computer science, business informatics or a comparable education.
  • You bring several years of experience in cloud engineering, ideally in complex system landscapes and in the Google cloud environment.
  • You have experience in cloud security and are familiar with security requirements, IAM/authorization concepts and the secure design of cloud platforms and services.
  • You have very good English skills in word and writing as well as good German skills and work structured, analytical and solution-oriented.
