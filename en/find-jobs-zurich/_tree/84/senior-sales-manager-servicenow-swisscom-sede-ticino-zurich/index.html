# Senior Sales Manager ServiceNow

Il tuo futuro inizia

As Senior Sales Manager ServiceNow, you are responsible for the new business of ServiceNow in German-speaking Switzerland. You position our offering as an elite partner – from license to implementation to support and operations – and develop real, recurring business relationships from initial conversations. ServiceNow is your focus, but not your limit: You think in terms of customer experience and also position the breadth of our portfolio with your customers – such as solutions such as Genesys or Salesforce. You will work closely with our key account managers, the delivery team and customer development and develop our ServiceNow business and sales pipeline with a lot of drive.

  • You generate new business for ServiceNow and build your own pipeline – you don’t wait for leads, you create them
  • You lead the entire sales cycle: qualification, solution consulting (together with our consultants and presales), offers, negotiation and conclusion
  • You think in terms of customer experience and position your customers alongside ServiceNow as well as the breadth of our CX portfolio – such as Genesys or Salesforce – and recognize cross-sell potential beyond the individual solution
  • You continue to develop your existing customers and secure renewables at an early stage – together with customer development.
  • You bring great openness and genuine interest in AI
  • You can convincingly position and pitch corresponding solutions to your customers and at the same time use AI profitably in your own sales everyday life.
  • You position Swisscom as a leading ServiceNow elite partner and act as a trusted point of contact
  • You will maintain your network with Key Account Managers and the ServiceNow Manufacturers team to identify and advance opportunities together.

Your skills

  • Several years of experience in B2B solution sales, ideally in the area of ServiceNow, enterprise SaaS or IT services
  • Proven Hunting Mentality: You Can Open Doors, Reveal Needs, and Bring Home Degrees
  • Solid understanding of the ServiceNow platform (ITSM, ITOM, CSM & Co.) – you do not have to configure yourself, but consult at eye level
  • Interested in working your way beyond ServiceNow into the breadth of our CX portfolio (e.g. Genesys, Salesforce) and selling it across solutions
  • Consulting strength, negotiation skills and the ability to translate complex requirements into clear customer benefits
  • Very good German
  • English and French are beneficial
  • Willingness to travel within German-speaking Switzerland

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Benefits.
