Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Vocational Counselling Practical training 2 in the department of foster families during the Bachelor in Social Work for 10 – 12 months, 60 – 80% Good for life – that is our motto. The Office for Youth and Vocational Counselling of the Education Directorate provides, controls and coordinates offers of child and youth welfare and vocational counselling. The Department of Nursing Families in the Central Department Supplementary Assistance for Education offers students an internship within the framework of their bachelor’s degree in social work from February 2027 or after consultation as part of practical training 2 for ten to twelve months. As an intern, you support the team of authorities in their work and are jointly responsible for the acquisition of new foster families for the foster family pool of the AJB, the placement of foster children (including in the context of crisis interventions) in foster families as well as the advice of foster parents, foster children and specialists during the process of getting to know each other. If necessary, the team of authorities will support you in granting nursing permits and in supervising nursing conditions. You also help to further develop the foster care system in the Canton of Zurich. They will also be given the opportunity to develop conceptual foundations for the foster care pool and thus actively contribute to the development of offers and quality in the field of family care in the Canton of Zurich.   • Open way of communicating and approaching people
