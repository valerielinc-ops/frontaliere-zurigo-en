USZ Campus

Starting January 1, 2027 or by agreement, fixed-term for 18 months

🧠The project: During implantation of DBS electrodes in awake patients, we record single neuron activity and EEG while patients perform tasks.

What you'll do

  • Contribute to task design
  • Run intraoperative experiments with patients
  • Analyze spiking activity and its connectivity to EEG

What we're looking for

  • PhD in neuroscience, biomedical engineering, physics or a related field
  • Strong statistics and programming skills
  • Experience with movement disorders and/or other indications for DBS
  • German good enough to run experiments with patients
  • Ability to live and work in Switzerland
