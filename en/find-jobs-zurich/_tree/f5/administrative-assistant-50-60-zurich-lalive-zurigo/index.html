Role description

Our dedicated Support team is looking for an Administrative Assistant, working 50 to 60%. We are seeking a bright, organised and motivated individual with excellent communication skills, strong attention to detail, and a genuine desire to be part of a dynamic international environment.

Reporting to the Office Manager, the Administrative Assistant will primarily support the Lawyers and Legal Assistants with their day-to-day activities, while also providing general administrative support to all members of the Zurich office. This position is ideal for a proactive, flexible and enthusiastic individual who enjoys a varied role in a demanding and stimulating environment.

  • Supporting Legal Assistants and Lawyers with:
  • Filing of submissions with arbitral tribunals and Swiss courts, including document formatting, preparation of electronic and hard-copy documents;
  • Preparing for hearings, particularly before arbitral tribunals
  • Administrative and organisational tasks, including managing calendars, scheduling meetings, making travel arrangements, expense reports, and handling incoming and outgoing correspondence;
  • Managing client files, including physical and electronic filing and archiving.
  • Ad hoc tasks:
  • Covering reception when required, including picking up and bringing post mail to the post office and welcoming visitors and answering telephone calls in German and English;
  • Supporting the organisation of events.

Your profile

What we look for

  • Previous experience in a similar role or within a law firm would be an advantage;
  • Strong interpersonal and communication skills, with a collaborative mindset and team-oriented approach;
  • Flexible, proactive and able to adapt to changing priorities;
  • Meticulous and diligent, with excellent attention to detail and strong organisational skills;
  • Proven ability to work effectively in a fast-paced environment and under tight deadlines;
  • Native German and a good command of English are essential; knowledge of additional languages, particularly French, would be a strong asset.
