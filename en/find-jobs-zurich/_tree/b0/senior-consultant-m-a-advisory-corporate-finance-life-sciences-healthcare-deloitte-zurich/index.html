Full description

Join our M&A Life Science and Healthcare team! Our M&A practice features a dedicated group of highly qualified individuals. All new team members get to work on live engagements and have the opportunity to make a real contribution to transactions as well as deal generation from day one. Our M&A advisors have a long track record of completed deals and are thoroughly trained in bulge bracket investment banks and Big4 corporate finance teams. The Corporate Finance Advisory Team offers our clients financial and strategic advice in the context of M&A transactions. How you can make an impact

  • Be part of landmark transactions in the Swiss marketplace, providing buy- and sell-side advisory services to our discerning financial institutions clientele in Switzerland
  • Interact and work with senior level client staff on demanding projects - both domestically and cross-border, and try your hand at developing financial models (such as valuation or merger models), preparing information memoranda and management presentations to handling the day-to-day client communication
  • Strengthen your deal execution experience: from project management and financial analysis, preparation of transaction documents and client presentations
  • Be part of a global network of M&A specialists and work in a highly professional, diverse, and of course also exciting and fun work environment
  • Develop and broaden your network while working autonomously under the supervision of an engagement manager/director Who we are looking for
  • You are an open and curious person with strong analytical and interpersonal skills as well as international academic or work experiences, and you can cope well in a fast-paced and challenging environment
  • Ideally 2-4 years of prior work experience in the execution and origination of M&A transactions, gained at either an investment bank, a consultancy firm, in private equity/buy-side or another Big4 firm, preferably with the additional advantage of knowing the key Swiss industries sectors, particularly Life sciences and healthcare.
  • Fluent English; additional optional plus: knowledge of German Your team Become part of a diverse, multinational, and highly motivated team of 15+ at our Geneva and Zurich offices.
