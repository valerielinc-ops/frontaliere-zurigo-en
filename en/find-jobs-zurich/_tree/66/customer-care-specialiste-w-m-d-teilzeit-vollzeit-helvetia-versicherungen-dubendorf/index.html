What you do

  • You are the first point of contact for our customers and partners. You answer inquiries quickly, reliably and competently - in writing and by phone

  • You ensure an outstanding customer experience across all communication channels, whether by phone, email, chat or in the digital environment

What you bring to the table

  • You have completed commercial training or a comparable degree
  • You have experience in customer contact in the insurance industry and have successfully completed the VBV certification
  • You score points with a very good knowledge of German
  • French and/or Italian skills
  • You are characterized by strong communication skills, the ability to work in a team, resilience and a positive mindset and always find the best solution for customers and partners with empathy and a focus on solutions
