17.11.2026 Zurich

Become part of our team!

The Data Science & AI unit is responsible for developing data products and AI-based services. We are looking for a motivated, analytically strong and curious personality as Data Scientist (m/f/d). Our offices are in Zurich (The Circle) and in St. Gallen. It is also possible to work in the home office.

What awaits you?

  • Development of data products and AI-based services for our 3.7 million customers
  • Conception, development and operation of data-driven solutions with the right mix of business rules, machine learning and AI
  • Collaboration with departments and IT to solve concrete banking challenges
  • Development of robust data and AI pipelines for reliable and scalable productive operation
  • Evaluation, prototyping and production of innovative AI and Agentic use cases

What do you bring?

  • Successfully completed study or PhD in Data Science, Mathematics, Statistics, Science, Computer Science or comparable training with a quantitative background
  • Good programming skills in Python, R or SQL; Experience with modern data stacks and agentic engineering is an advantage
  • Interest in economic contexts and banking as well as pleasure in developing technical solutions for business challenges
  • Enthusiasm for data engineering, machine learning, AI and the development of intelligent and productive systems
  • High willingness to learn, initiative and an independent and communicative working method
  • Very good knowledge of German is required

We operate at the vital interface between business, data and technology. You can look forward to a strong team and exciting challenges around data science, generative AI and the future of banking. Lukas Seger Head of Data Science & AI Why Raiffeisen?
