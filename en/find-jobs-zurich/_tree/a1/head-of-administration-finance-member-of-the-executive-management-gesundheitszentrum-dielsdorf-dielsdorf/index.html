About us

The Dielsdorf Health Center is a modern geriatric competence center with around 500 employees from 35 nations. The offer ranges from counselling services for older people and their relatives to general and specialized long-term care and care at home. As part of a succession regulation, we are looking for an empathetic and experienced personality as of April 2027.

Their tasks

  • You lead, promote and challenge your team and develop the area of administration and finance including IT professionally, personnel and organizationally
  • They are responsible for finance and accounting, ensure legally compliant financial accounting including the secondary books and the necessary liquidity at all times.
  • They are responsible for internal and external reporting in accordance with the applicable accounting standards and ensure transparent, meaningful management information
  • They are responsible for the cost accounting according to the specifications of the industry association as well as the financial and task plan
  • You plan and control the budget process and ensure its timely implementation in cooperation with the responsible bodies
  • They are responsible for invoicing and debt collection and ensure efficient and reliable processes
  • They represent the area of administration and finance to internal and external contacts and maintain a transparent, addressee-oriented communication policy.
  • As a member of the management team, you participate in strategic and cross-divisional issues and represent your position clearly and firmly even in demanding situations.
  • You will play a key role in the planned replacement of the ERP system and contribute your experience in the digitization, automation and optimization of processes to the project.
  • They ensure the management and further development of IT and together with the responsible specialists ensure a secure, economical and needs-based IT operation

Your profile

  • You are an honest and convincing leader with a qualified track record in management and several years of experience in a comparable financial function.
  • You have a business studies as well as a deepening or further education at university or university level in finance and controlling
  • You have a high IT affinity and use digital opportunities profitably for the company and your employees
  • Ideally, you have practical experience with the introduction or replacement of an ERP system as well as with the automation of finance and administration processes.
  • They are characterized by strong analytical skills, a networked mindset and a good understanding of complex operational contexts
  • You act solution-oriented and strongly implemented and convince with a pragmatic, structured working method
  • They think entrepreneurially and strategically, make responsible decisions and remain steadfast even with different interests
  • They communicate step-by-step, have negotiation skills and maintain a secure relationship with internal and external stakeholders.
  • They bring a high level of social competence and create a performance-enhancing working environment through clarity, reliability and appreciation

What to expect

  • You take on a meaningful, versatile and strategically important leadership role with great creative leeway in a stable and well-connected organization
  • You work closely with a dedicated management team and a competent leadership team
  • They are supported by a professional, committed and interdisciplinary team
  • You get space for entrepreneurial thinking and sustainable further development of structures, processes and culture
  • You benefit from attractive framework conditions, fair compensation and development opportunities
