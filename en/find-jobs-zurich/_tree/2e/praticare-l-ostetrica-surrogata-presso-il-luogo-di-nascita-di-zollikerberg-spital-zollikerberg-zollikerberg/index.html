Practicing surrogate midwife at the Zollikerberg Birthplace

Do you enjoy independent midwife work and would like to promote natural obstetrics?

In the Zollikerberg Birth Center, freelance midwives accompany women and their families holistically and independently during pregnancy, during birth and in postpartum in an extraclinical setting.

What is your everyday work with us?

Accompanying as a free practicing midwife They expectant parents during pregnancy, childbirth in the birth centre and in the postpartum. In the small midwife team, you and your colleagues design the working model individually. They independently lead the birth process of their clients in the birth center. At the birth, a two-thousand baby from the team comes to support. In the first two days of the postpartum, the family will be cared for in the birthplace and, if possible, accompany the families at home.

What can you expect from us?

In the family midwife team we support each other and can learn from each other. Depending on the working model, you can benefit from free times and determine your workload yourself. Thanks to generous birth packages, attractive wage conditions are created. You have the opportunity to offer your own courses or therapies at the Zollikerberg Birthplace.

Internal quality circles: We regularly carry out internal quality circles, which are carried out according to the “characteristics of quality circles” and the “Forum for Quality Circles” of the Swiss Midwifery Association (SHV). As a rule, 2 labeled E-log points can be purchased per quality circle. In addition, training courses are held regularly.

What do you bring?

You are a certified midwife or midwife BSc

You have at least two years of professional experience in obstetrics, ideally in a non-clinical setting

You have a ZSR number or have the opportunity to purchase it

they like to work in a team and enjoy independent midwife work
