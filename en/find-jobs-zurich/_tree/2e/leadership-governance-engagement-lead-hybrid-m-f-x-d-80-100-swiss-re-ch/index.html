About the Role

As a Leadership Governance & Engagement Lead within the CRO Office, you will play a pivotal role in making Swiss Re's Risk Management leadership function perform at its very best. You will be the engine behind effective RM Executive Team (RM ET) governance — ensuring the right issues reach the right people, that decisions are clear and followed through, and that leadership direction is understood and embraced across the organisation. At the same time, you will shape how leadership communicates and engages with the broader Risk Management community, fostering alignment on key messages and enabling a culture of transparency and genuine dialogue. This is a role for someone who is as comfortable in the detail of a decision record as they are crafting a compelling leadership narrative. You will work at the very centre of the function — connecting strategy to execution, and leadership to colleagues.

Key Responsibilities

RM ET Governance & Decision-Making

  • Coordinate RM ET meetings end-to-end — from forward agenda planning to facilitation logistics — ensuring meetings are purposeful, well-prepared, and focused on the topics that matter most
  • Drive decision-readiness by ensuring proposals reaching the RM ET are structured, complete, and actionable, and that decisions made are clearly recorded and owned
  • Maintain accurate and timely decision and action records so that follow-through is tracked, routed to the right owners, and completed with accountability
  • Continuously improve governance effectiveness, with a focus on ensuring that more than 50% of RM ET time is dedicated to strategic topics rather than operational updates

Leadership Alignment & Narrative

  • Foster alignment on RM-wide key messages by developing and maintaining a coherent leadership narrative that reflects strategic priorities and resonates across the function
  • Shape leadership positioning by supporting the GCRO and RM ET in articulating direction clearly, consistently, and compellingly to diverse audiences
  • Develop and maintain leadership notes and engagement priorities that keep the RM ET informed, aligned, and prepared for key moments and milestones

Engagement & Dialogue

  • Enable consistent, coordinated leadership communication across the Risk Management community — including the broader leadership layer — ensuring messages land with clarity and real impact
  • Create channels and moments for meaningful dialogue that bring colleagues into the conversation, build understanding of strategic direction, and generate genuine buy-in
  • Gather and route feedback from across the organisation back into leadership, ensuring that colleague perspectives actively inform decision-making and engagement approaches
  • Measure and improve engagement outcomes, including colleague understanding of and alignment with strategy, particularly within the broader leadership population

About the Team

The CRO Office is part of the RM Operations department at Swiss Re — and it's a team where your work truly matters. As strategic partners and operational catalysts for Risk Management (RM), the RM Operations team plays a central role in enabling effective execution, cross-functional alignment, and transformation across the function.

The CRO Office itself is a dynamic, multifunctional team of experts spanning strategy execution, communications, people management, cost management, project governance, and senior management reporting. We provide focused support to the GCRO, RM Executive Team, and Line Managers, while also serving the broader Risk Management community.

We are architects and change agents — we connect the dots, foster collaboration, ensure operational discipline, and drive excellence. Our team contributes to defining the function's strategy and vision, develops and monitors operational KPIs, leads simplification and agility initiatives, and collaborates closely with partners across the organisation including Communications, HR, FP&A, GDTO, and Group Strategy. If you are looking for a team where your contributions are visible, valued, and impactful — you've found it.

About You

You are a proactive, intellectually curious professional who brings both strategic thinking and hands-on execution to everything you do. You approach challenges with a can-do attitude and a genuine growth mindset — always looking for better ways to work, learn, and deliver impact. You are comfortable navigating ambiguity, building relationships at all levels of an organisation, and translating complex ideas into clear, compelling narratives.
