Full description

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, get involved and grow with us. Become part of our family and shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us.

What you move

  • You are responsible for the regulation and handling of claims cases and take an efficient, structured and solution-oriented approach
  • As a central contact person, you accompany our customers through the entire process and find pragmatic solutions for their concerns
  • The clarification of irregularities in transport shipments as well as the coordination with internal and external bodies are part of your everyday life
  • You keep track of your customers’ shipments and ensure a smooth process of deliveries and collections
  • The maintenance and control of damage and shipment data are also part of your tasks - you ensure high data quality and complete documentation.

Why do you succeed?

  • You have a basic commercial training and ideally bring experience in a comparable function, of advantage in the logistics industry
  • With MS Office, especially Excel and Outlook, you are safe and efficient
  • Your strong customer orientation, your confident appearance and your winning nature make you a valued contact person
  • A structured, independent and reliable way of working is a matter of course for you - even if it gets hectic, you always keep the overview
  • Flexibility is required: You work in the 42.5 hour model in offset working hours between 07:30 and 20:00
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing round off your profile

Your benefits

  • Further education and development opportunities: With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • Family culture: You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Flexible working time models: Early Bird or night owl? Full-time or part-time? We accept your wishes.

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
