Full description

Our rehabilitation clinic Limmattal offers specialized neurological and musculoskeletal rehabilitation including surveillance rehabilitation and accident rehabilitation at a high medical level. Through targeted diagnostics and individually tailored therapy programs, our interdisciplinary team ensures that our patients are optimally looked after on their way back to better health and quality of life. Due to the close cooperation with the Hospital Limmattal and the direct connection to the medical emergency unit, timely acute somatic treatment is always ensured even in the event of emergency complications.

Your tasks

  • Patient-oriented care: Take over the diagnosis, treatment and clarification of inpatients
  • Counselling and guidance as well as auxiliary care: You take over the instruction and guidance for ADL training of patients and their carers
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration: Work independently in an interdisciplinary team with interfaces to the medical profession, nursing and various therapeutic disciplines such as physiotherapy and speech therapy

Your profile

  • Training: Completed training as an occupational therapist or equivalent training with at least one positive SRK PreCheck
  • Experience: You have experience in the treatment of primarily neurological but also orthopaedic patients and want to develop professionally and professionally
  • Personality: You are an enthusiastic and communicative personality with a sense of humor. You convince through a sense of responsibility and high-quality work, can set priorities effectively and do not lose sight of the needs of our patients.

We offer

  • Co-creation: Work in a modern working environment and actively participate
  • Inspiring and collegial working environment: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, car dealers, pharmacies and our membership of Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other perks at the FORTYSEVEN spa.
