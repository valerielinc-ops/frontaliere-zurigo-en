Full description

The Audience & Portfolio Offer department brings together various essential functions for a modern, public-oriented and forward-looking SSR.

The Department is responsible for the development and provision of strategic public and market knowledge through research and analysis in all regions. It also leads, at the national level, the SSR's bid management, development and quality assurance processes, in order to ensure their compliance with the strategy and concession, across all delivery channels.

Your Mission

Within the Audience & Portfolio Offer team, you are committed to the following areas:

  • Direction and direction of interdisciplinary projects for the development of formats and their teams, from the assignment of the mandate to the validation of the format and its transmission for operational implementation, to the ideation, prototyping and user testing phase
  • Ensuring strategic alignment of developed or adapted formats, ensuring that they contribute to defined strategic objectives and that appropriate development processes are engaged (quality assurance)
  • Cross-cutting management and coordination of teams of various professional expertise, without necessarily having a direct hierarchical authority
  • The role of reference interlocutor for certain defined genres, sectors or channels
  • Coordination of interfaces and integration processes, including between different language regions, and collaboration with selection bodies
  • Monitors international market and format trends and actively contributes to the exploration of new areas of activity

Your profile

  • Several years of experience confirmed in format development, media content design or production, in digital, cross media or traditional media fields
  • Very good facilitation and animation skills, as well as demonstrated experience in designing and conducting workshops and sprints based on user-centred approaches (e.g. design methods, prototyping, user testing)
  • Strong storytelling and audiovisual market knowledge for both short and long formats
  • Excellent communication and advisory capabilities, enabling confident management and facilitation of complex interfaces and collaborations
  • Systemic and strategic spirit, as well as a capacity to translate strategic directions into concrete operational measures
  • Very good mastery of French (level C2) and knowledge of German at the B1 level at least

What we offer you

  • A great freedom of creation and experimentation in the development of innovative and forward-looking media formats for the Swiss public
  • A key responsible role in the operational implementation of the national bid design and development process
  • An agile and interdisciplinary working environment rooted in the different linguistic regions
  • The possibility of actively contributing to the future of SSR in a dynamic environment
  • An open and innovative team within the new "Hearing & Portfolio Offer" department, leaving much room for new ideas
  • A hybrid work model, with flexible telework possibilities, as well as advantageous conditions of engagement within the SSR

We invite you to submit your application (motivation letter and CV) by clicking on the "Apply now" button. The deadline for applications is 11 December 2026.

Our promise

Reaching the entire Swiss population with high quality formats requires expertise, enthusiasm and a stimulating and creative environment, in which everyone can invest and evolve. For the best to give the best of themselves, they need to have a source of inspiration: Welcome to the world of SSR!

  • Create Content: Quality journalism is the core of our activity and passion. We enjoy complete freedom of writing, encourage creativity and grant autonomy for innovation and quality.
  • Living digital: In our country, quality journalism can also take a digital form. This is why we are exploring new opportunities and experimenting with new formats and practices: digital, interactive and cross-media. Our content is part of the air of time and responds to public concerns.
  • Human being: We maintain informal relations, work independently and contribute to a good working atmosphere. Our success is based on the strengths of everyone and on the technical skills of all our employees.
  • Make sense: With our program, we reach all of Switzerland. We act independently and respectfully to encourage the formation of opinion and strengthen cultural diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.

Requirements

  • Plusieurs années d'expérience confirmée dans le développement de formats, la conception de contenus médias ou la production, dans les domaines numériques, cross médias ou des médias traditionnels

  • Très bonnes compétences en facilitation et en animation, ainsi qu'une expérience démontrée dans la conception et la conduite d'ateliers et de sprints reposant sur des approches centrées sur les utilisateurs (par exemple : méthodes de design, prototypage, tests utilisateurs)
