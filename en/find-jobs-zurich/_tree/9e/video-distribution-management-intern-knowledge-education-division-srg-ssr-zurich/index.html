Full description

The distribution department ensures that SRF’s offerings are published on the linear radio and TV programs as well as on the digital channels and platforms on which our target groups operate – with the right mix of topics, in the right form, at the right time, on the right scale, with the right brand – supported by digitally oriented marketing.

What are your tasks?

From January 2027, you will be involved in the Distribution Video department for 6 months for the following topics and tasks:

  • Channel-friendly processing of content, selection of suitable channels and platforms as well as effective distribution, e.g. packaging on the YouTube channel of "SRF Wissen"
  • Creation of platform-friendly thumbnails
  • Creating SEO-optimized title, lead and description text
  • Scheduling posts and videos for selected digital channels, for example from “SRF Kids”
  • Interaction with the community in the form of comments on the corresponding channels

What do you bring?

  • Interest in the fields of knowledge and education and documentary formats
  • Creativity and feel for digital storytelling as well as topics on YouTube and social media
  • Secure and careful wording of titles, posting texts and community contributions
  • Knowledge of DaVinci Resolve and Affinity programs or other video editing and graphics programs such as Adobe Premiere Pro and Photoshop

What can you look forward to?

  • A dedicated team that works hand in hand
  • Exciting projects and insights into different areas at SRF
  • A dynamic environment in a learning organization as well as a respectful and trusting working atmosphere
  • Diverse exchange opportunities as well as joint learning with other experienced colleagues:

What we offer

What does it take to reach the people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expert knowledge, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can contribute and develop. For the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: Welcome to the world of SRF!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, promote creativity and offer a lot of design space for new and high-quality products.
  • Shaping digitalization: Quality journalism is also digital for us. That’s why we explore new possibilities and experiment with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-medial. We design on the pulse of time and on the nerve of the audience.
  • Being human: We meet each other collegially, work independently and shape our working atmosphere individually. Our success is based on the personal strengths as well as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programs. In doing so, we act respectfully and independently; in this way we promote opinion-forming and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.

Requirements

  • Interesse für die Themenfelder Wissen und Bildung und dokumentarische Formate

  • Kreativität und Gespür für digitales Storytelling sowie Themen auf YouTube und Social Media

  • Sicheres und sorgfältiges Formulieren von Titeln, Posting-Texten und Community-Beiträgen

  • Kenntnisse der Programme DaVinci Resolve und Affinity oder andere Videoschnitt- und Grafikprogramme wie zum Beispiel Adobe Premiere Pro und Photoshop
