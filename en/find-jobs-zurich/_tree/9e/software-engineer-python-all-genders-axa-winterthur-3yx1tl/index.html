Shape your future – together with us!

Are you looking for an exciting, responsible and extremely versatile challenge? Then you’ve come to the right place at Pricing P&C.

In order to continue to be successful in the dynamic Property & Casualty (P&C) business, we at AXA are continually optimizing our pricing and processes. Our team develops the pricing models from data provision through modeling to embedding in our sales systems. We are looking for reinforcements in our Pricing P&C team for these tasks.

This is what awaits you

  • As a software developer (Python), you work closely with product specialists, actuaries, data scientists and software developers.
  • As part of the “P&C Pricing Engine” product team, you are responsible for providing tariff information & discount decisions in real time for our online & offline sales systems.
  • Together with our pricing specialists, you will continually develop our pricing toolbox. You will take on a leading role in software design.
  • You will be involved with us and all the specialist departments involved in the discussion about the best professional and technical solution for the further development of our products for private and corporate customers.

This is what you bring with you

  • You have a master's degree (ETH, Uni, FH) in computer science, data science, business informatics or comparable training with a focus on software development.
  • You are interested in the data and processes processed by your code and exchange ideas with everyone involved on an equal footing.
  • You have good knowledge of developing, testing and operating (DevOps) backend applications and packages in Python, and you have experience in software design (backend).
  • You meet the requirements of our internal and external stakeholders with expertise and a smile on your face.
  • You speak German fluently.
