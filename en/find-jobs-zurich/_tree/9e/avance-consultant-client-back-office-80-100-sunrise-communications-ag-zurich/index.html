At Sunrise, we think bigger, go further and create new things. For us, working culture means creating great things together. Here respect and fresh ideas meet real teamwork – every voice counts, every perspective makes us stronger. Our passion drives us to dare new things and to constantly grow. Sounds like you? For the 17-member Landlords and Basic Subscription Team, we are looking for a customer consultant at the earliest possible time, who will actively support us in our daily business. If you want to be a driving force in this area and have fun at work, apply now! Your creativity and enthusiasm in solving customer concerns are in demand. Are you ready for that? Your challenge:

  • Advice and answering telephone and written (mail and letter) customer concerns
  • Contract negotiations incl. Preparation of contract quotes
  • Acceptance, recording and final processing of all customer concerns in the sense of customer expectations within the entire customer lifecycle
  • Processing of special and escalation cases
  • Contact and negotiation with migrated customers in order to win them back and to bind them to the company (customer retention)
  • Close cooperation and order processing of key account managers

Your kills

  • Completed education with a strong commercial focus (minor commercial school)
  • Digital world - advanced knowledge of the most common computer applications (Microsoft Word, Excel, Powerpoint, Teams)
  • Strong customer orientation and high social competence in dealing with employees and customers
  • Independent thinking – thanks to your analytical skills, you will be able to quickly grasp a problem, find solutions and make decisions
  • Acting in the sense of our company values
  • Daily communication - excellent written and oral knowledge of German. Other languages nice to have

At Sunrise, you work in a dynamic and multinational environment where every voice is heard, perspectives are shared and values are lived. Diversity is not just a program for us, but part of our DNA. Because we celebrate our differences, we achieve great things together.

Sunrise is a partner of Advance Gender Equality in Business, the Swiss LGBTI label and certified with Fair-ON-Pay Advanced for equal pay for work of equal value for all.
