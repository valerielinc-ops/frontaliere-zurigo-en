Full description

For the Higher School for Rescue Professions (HFRB) we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement a new department management with proven leadership competence.

  • Responsible for the human, strategic, financial and operational management of the Education Offerings Department
  • Conception of the education strategy
  • Contact with the stakeholders and representation of HFRB to the outside
  • Co-shaping the development of our educational institution as part of the divisional management
  • Accompanying team leaders in the implementation and further development of educational formats
  • Leadership competence: Empathic person with the ability to accompany and support employees, with appropriate education and training. Willingness to Change
  • Contextual Thinking: The ability to identify connections and essential elements in the educational landscape, think strategically, create and use synergies
  • Educational management: practical experience in an equivalent capacity, experience at tertiary level B, higher degree in education and excellent knowledge in the education market
  • Communication Capability: Open and transparent mode of communication enabling a benevolent and good relationship with different stakeholders, active network maintenance
  • A versatile, challenging and responsible activity in education
  • A motivated team that is also ready for new paths
  • The opportunity to advance the conception and further development of the Educational Offerings Department
  • Attractive working and employment conditions such as flexible forms of work (e.g. mobile working, job sharing) or targeted education and training
  • A modern and centrally located workplace in Zurich-Nord

Protection & Rescue Zurich (SRZ), as a service department of the Department of Security, unites the professional and militia fire brigades, the medical department and the operational control center (ELZ) for the emergency calls 144 and 118. In addition, civil protection, fire police and a training center with an affiliated Higher School for Rescue Occupations (HFRB) belong to the largest civil rescue organization in Switzerland. Intervention and prevention units protect and save people, animals, property and the environment around the clock in the city of Zurich, at Zurich Airport and in the agglomeration.
