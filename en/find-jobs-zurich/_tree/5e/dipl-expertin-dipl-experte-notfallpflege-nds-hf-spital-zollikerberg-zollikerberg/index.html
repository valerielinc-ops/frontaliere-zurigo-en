Dipl. Expert / Dipl. Expert Emergency Care NDS HF — Spital Zollikerberg, Zollikerberg (ZH).

Area: Nursing professions.

Welcome to your new place of work.

Regula Schlotterbeck, Management Emergency offers you a job with a selectable workload of 50-100 % as

Dipl. Expert / Dipl. Expert Emergency Care NDS HF

Together we make a difference in people’s lives

  • Together, we provide professional initial care for all emergency patients: according to current standards and with a clear focus on their needs
  • We take responsibility for the patient process and work closely together in the interdisciplinary and interprofessional emergency team.
  • We carry out therapeutic and diagnostic measures or support their implementation
  • We manage and coordinate interdisciplinary emergency operations to ensure a safe and efficient patient flow

Become part of the Department of Emergency and Acute Medicine at the Zollikerberg Hospital. At our emergency department, we treat and care for more than 20,000 patients every year. With nine treatment sites and a lively 24-hour operation, a versatile and dynamic working environment awaits you. The emergency department is part of the Clinic for Internal Medicine with continuing education status A and works closely with the various departments of the hospital.

In our 360° tour, get a first impression of your new place of work.

  • Attractive allowances for shift work
  • Financial and time support for training
  • Free use of the fitness room on the Zollikerberg site

  • Resilience, a high sense of responsibility and careful working methods

About the Zollikerberg Hospital We stand for a comprehensive range of services and strong cooperation partners. With a public service mandate and 173 beds, the Zollikerberg Hospital contributes to the optimal medical care of the greater Zurich area. Every year, more than 11,000 inpatients and 75,000 outpatients in all insurance classes can feel comfortable and in good hands. Our broadly based primary care includes internal medicine, gynecology and obstetrics with neonatology, general surgery, an interdisciplinary emergency center and orthopedics with specialized spinal surgery.
