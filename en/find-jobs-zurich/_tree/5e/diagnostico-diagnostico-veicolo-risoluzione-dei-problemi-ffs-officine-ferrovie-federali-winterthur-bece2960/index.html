Diagnostician / vehicle diagnostician troubleshooting 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Diagnostician/vehicle diagnostician troubleshooting Winterthur, October 1st, 2026 or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. An exciting and varied job awaits you as a diagnostician in disorder diagnosis. In this role, you will be significantly involved in ensuring that the trains run and can be put back into operation quickly in the event of disruptions over a continuous five-day week and on a few weekends. In this way, you contribute every day to offering our customers the greatest possible comfort. You work on one of the most modern vehicle fleets (Flirt Evo), which will be put into operation from 2026. You can look forward to a collegial environment in which know-how is shared, as well as interesting opportunities for further development. What counts for us is togetherness and community in the team. We are an attractive employer and offer many benefits such as extra child allowances, generous Sunday allowance, free 2nd class GA and discounted travel abroad

  • As a vehicle diagnostician, you are responsible for diagnosing faults on rolling stock in passenger transport.
  • You enjoy diagnosing faults during operations and during downtimes, as well as working in shifts with irregular working hours and weekend shifts.
  • You provide support in increasing the availability of trains and minimizing disruption times.
  • You will put serviced and repaired vehicles back into operation, ensuring that our customers travel with the highest possible comfort.
  • You support the exchange of knowledge about the fleet and ensure expertise in maintaining the vehicle fleet at the maintenance location.

This is what you bring with you.

  • You have a high degree of independence, initiative and a sense of responsibility. Networked, analytical thinking and actions, as well as your flexibility, characterize you as a person.
  • For this exciting and demanding position, you have completed a 4-year EFZ apprenticeship in electrical engineering. In your career so far, you have been able to gain several years of solid professional experience.
  • We require very good oral and written German skills (C1/C2) for this position.
  • Since all instructions and documentation are in digital form, you have no fear of contact with common modern work tools and IT programs such as MS Office and SAP
  • A category B driving license is an advantage.
