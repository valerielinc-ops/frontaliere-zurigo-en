Full description

80% - 100% | IT / Business Engineering | Zurich | Experienced professionals The Client Lifecycle Management team is responsible for product management of customer relationships across all customer segments. Together we ensure that the regulatory requirements in the area of money laundering and VSB are implemented efficiently, securely and customer-oriented. In an environment with high change dynamics, you actively shape the further development of processes along the entire customer life cycle with us. Your tasks

  • Analyze, evaluate and develop technical solution concepts for regulatory requirements in the area of VSB and money laundering for integration into sales processes and systems
  • Design and prepare the implementation of changes in processes and systems in close cooperation with IT and specialist bodies
  • Professional support of the company on your topics and ensure a smooth use of the solutions in sales
  • Monitor and continuously optimize existing processes, specifications and system support in client lifecycle management
  • Support in other projects that require expertise in regulatory topics and client lifecycle management

Your profile

  • University or university degree in business informatics, banking and finance or a similar subject area
  • At least 8 years of professional experience as a business analyst, ideally with a financial services provider
  • Good knowledge of the VSB, the GwG and the relevant regulatory requirements in Switzerland or the willingness to acquire them quickly
  • Very good organizational, analytical and conceptual skills as well as high flexibility, supplemented by experience in the cooperation or management of projects in an agile environment
  • Independent, responsible and accurate work and a credible, negotiating-strong appearance as well as a high level of communication competence in dealing with different stakeholders
  • Very good German skills in word and writing, English skills are an advantage

Our offer

  • Motivated, team-oriented crew with a high level of expertise looking forward to you
  • Exciting and diverse range of tasks in the area of client lifecycle management with many interfaces to different areas of the bank
  • Self-determined working methods with a lot of personal responsibility and the opportunity to participate in a dynamic environment
  • Centrally located, modern workplace in the lively district 5 with very good transport connections and the possibility to organize your work flexibly (hybrid)
  • Various opportunities to develop yourself personally and professionally, an employer who takes care of her employees and invests in them
  • Promising prospects in a stable, sustainable environment at one of the safest universal banks in the world
  • Comprehensive package, which in addition to the attractive basic salary and a variable remuneration offers many benefits and perks – from A such as retirement provision to Z such as ZVV bonus pass
