24.11.2026 Zurich

Become part of our team!

The Customer Service Center actively supports Raiffeisen Switzerland, Raiffeisen banks and group companies in achieving their service goals by receiving and answering telephone and written end customer inquiries. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a quickly learning, service-oriented and communication-strong personality. We offer you interesting and diverse activities in a fast-paced and varied working environment, modern workplaces and a working model with home office possibilities.

What awaits you?

  • Service-oriented support and advice for private customers of the Raiffeisen Group by telephone
  • Targeted and campaign-oriented sales activities for banking products by telephone
  • Shared responsibility for the targeted further development of the organization
  • Working days from Monday to Friday flexibly distributed throughout the week, but mainly at peak times, supplemented by individual Saturday assignments
  • Training phase: In the first month in a 100% workload

What do you bring?

  • Basic commercial education and experience in the financial sector
  • Experience in the contact center area (inbound and outbound)
  • Affinity for sales and consulting with a strong customer and service orientation
  • Passion and conviction to use your strengths to achieve joint success
  • Excellent knowledge of German in words and writing

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
