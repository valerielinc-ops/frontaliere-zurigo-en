Welcome to your new place of work.

Dr. Tammo Bartnick, Medical Director Oncological Ambulatorium of Oncology Bellevue at the Zollikerberg site You are now working for a 40 -80% job as

Dipl. Nursing professional / Dipl. Nursing specialist oncology (with regulated working hours)

If you too want to make a difference in people’s lives, these are your tasks:

  • Working as a reference nurse in a family care team, coordinating patient care
  • Administration/planning of antitumor therapies/chemotherapies
  • Controlling the care process from entry to exit
  • Complex care and care situations of oncological patients / relatives are a positive challenge for you
  • Active participation in internal projects

Your work is also possible and desirable at both Oncologie Bellevue sites, Zollikerberg and Bellevue (Stadelhofen). This opens up a wide range of all oncological treatment options and expands your knowledge in the treatment of patients with cancer.

The cooperation with specialists in integrative oncology and the specialist departments of the Zollikerberg Hospital with internal medicine (continued education status A) which cares for more than 2,500 inpatient patients, over 6,000 emergencies and more than 450 patients in the interdisciplinary intensive care unit as well as with the interdisciplinary stations with a focus on palliative medicine, acute care and surgery are the basis for the holistic care of our patients. Their cooperation and exchange with these departments is elementary and exciting aspect of their work.

About the Zollikerberg hospital #LI-PZ1

We stand for a comprehensive range of services and strong cooperation partners. Every year, more than 11,000 inpatients and 75,000 outpatients in all insurance classes can feel comfortable and in good hands. Our broadly based primary care includes internal medicine, gynecology and obstetrics with neonatology, general surgery, an interdisciplinary emergency center and orthopedics with specialized spinal surgery.
