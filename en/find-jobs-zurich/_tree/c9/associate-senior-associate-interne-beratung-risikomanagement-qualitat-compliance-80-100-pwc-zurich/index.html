Full description

As a member of our Internal Advisory Risk & Quality team, you play a central role in protecting the company by helping our advisory practice deliver high-quality services to clients across a wide range of industries, in line with relevant regulatory requirements.

  • Examination of contract documents and advice of engagement teams. You also explain the associated risks and independence requirements.
  • You ensure that all services meet internal and external standards.
  • You independently identify potential risks, propose appropriate risk mitigation measures and participate in risk identification and mitigation discussions.
  • Support of practical training and the creation of training documents to train colleagues in the entire advisory practice.
  • Help ensure that internal and external processes and documentation are always up-to-date with regulatory developments.
  • Working closely with internal stakeholders – including Legal, the Independence Office and international R&Q teams – to drive best practices and continuous improvement.
  • Support the annual compliance and quality review process. In addition, you help to ensure the high standards of PwC.
  • Education and/or initial professional experience in finance, audit or law – with an interest in risk management, quality and compliance.
  • Strong analytical skills as well as strong communication and stakeholder management skills.
  • Proactive, detail-oriented and innovative personality with the aim of continuously developing yourself.
  • Flowing in German and English (written and oral); French is a plus.
  • Experience in audit, risk management or compliance is an advantage.

Why PwC?

  • Become part of a diverse, international team that lives cooperation, flexibility and personal development.
  • Actively shape the risk management environment at PwC Advisory and make a tangible contribution to business success.
  • Benefit from ongoing training, mentoring and development opportunities within a global network.
