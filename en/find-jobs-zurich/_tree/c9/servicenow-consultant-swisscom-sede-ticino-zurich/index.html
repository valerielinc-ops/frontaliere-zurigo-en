  • Responsibility for requirements, solution design, architecture issues and coordination with specialist areas
  • Use of modern ServiceNow functions for automation, artificial intelligence and efficient processes
  • Quick entry into ongoing customer projects with a high level of personal responsibility
  • Cooperation with Swisscom B2B customers, development teams, operations and product owners ## Your skills
