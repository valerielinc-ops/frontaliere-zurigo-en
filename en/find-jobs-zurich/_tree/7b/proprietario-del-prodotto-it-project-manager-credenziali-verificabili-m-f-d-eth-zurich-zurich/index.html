Product Owner / IT Project Manager Verifiable Credentials (m/f/d)

80%-100%, Zurich, permanent

At ETH Zurich, more than 20,000 students study at Bachelor's and Master's level or are enrolled as listeners. The teaching operation behind it is handled over a broad application landscape.

For the strategic project "Verifiable Credentials", we are looking for a committed and solution-oriented personality as product owner / IT project management. In this role, you actively shape the digital transformation of teaching operations and make a decisive contribution to the successful introduction of new processes, systems and working methods.

Whether you already have extensive experience or would like to take the next professional step with relevant practical experience: We look forward to your application.

The Academic Services support the central processes around studying and teaching at ETH Zurich. In the field of digital teaching, we are driving the digitization of study-related services.

Project background

Shape the future of digital academic proof at ETH Zurich!

With the E-ID and the federal trust infrastructure (swiyu), a new digital ecosystem is being created in Switzerland. ETH Zurich has been involved from the very beginning. We are introducing Verifiable Credentials (VC): digital academic evidence that is counterfeit and machine-readable and can be verified internationally. The solution is based on open standards, fits European implementations and is developed in cooperation with other Swiss universities.

For the establishment, integration, introduction and further development of our VC solution, we are looking for a committed personality who combines product responsibility, IT project management and a good technical understanding. With us, you actively shape Switzerland’s VC ecosystem.

Job description

In this versatile role, you assume overall responsibility for the introduction, further development and operation of Verifiable Credentials at ETH Zurich. You design the product strategy, control the implementation and together with the experts involved ensure that a sustainable, secure and sustainable solution is created.

Your main tasks include

  • Project management and agile control: You are responsible for planning, coordinating and successfully implementing the project. You control progress, dependencies, risks and resources and ensure transparent basis for decision-making.
  • Product Ownership: You develop the product vision and roadmap further, prioritize the product backlog and translate technical requirements into actionable solutions together with the stakeholders and the project team. You work with agile methods and scrum and use regular feedback for the further development of the product.
  • Stakeholder Management: You coordinate the cooperation between Academic Services, Computer Science Services, Digital Campus, external IT service providers and other internal and external partners. You represent the project in relevant committees and ensure targeted communication.
  • Technical consulting and architecture: You assess technical solution concepts in exchange with the relevant IT specialists, accompany architecture and integration decisions and ensure that requirements for security, interoperability and relevant standards are taken into account.
  • System integration: You accompany the integration of the VC solution into the Digital Campus and the existing system landscape of ETH Zurich.
  • Project governance and finance: You monitor budgets, contracts and project risks, create the basis for decision-making and prepare the reporting for the responsible steering committees.
  • Standardization and networking: You will follow developments around digital identities and educational proofs, in particular swiyu, W3C Verifiable Credentials and EUDI, and represent ETH Zurich in exchange with universities, authorities and other specialist partners.

Profile

  • Good knowledge of project management or product ownership as well as familiarity with roadmap and backlog management and agile methods such as Scrum
  • Good understanding of software architectures, APIs, system integration and IT security
