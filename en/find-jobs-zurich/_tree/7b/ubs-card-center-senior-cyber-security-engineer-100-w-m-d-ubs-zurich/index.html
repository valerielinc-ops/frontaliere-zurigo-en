Full description

The team We are a leading company in the field of credit card processing in Switzerland. To strengthen our IT Security & Risk Team, we are looking for an experienced Cyber Security Engineer (f/m/d) who will take our security tools to the next level together with their team colleagues.

Key responsibilities

  • Design, implementation, and optimization of security controls across EDR, NDR, DLP, email security, and vulnerability management to strengthen the security architecture
  • Application of well-founded technical know-how in cloud technologies, Kubernetes, and Artificial Intelligence (AI) to develop scalable and secure solutions
  • Close collaboration with the SOC to further develop detection and response capabilities and to continuously improve cyber resilience
  • Advising and supporting stakeholders on all cybersecurity issues and acting as a trusted expert
  • Managing the implementation of security measures within the agile delivery framework and achieving results even in complex environments with diverse stakeholders
  • Ongoing analysis of the threat landscape and industry trends in the cyber sector, assessment of the associated risks, and derivation of appropriate adjustments for the security solutions in use

Your skills and experience

  • Completed degree in computer science, IT security, or comparable education
  • Several years of experience in the field of cyber security, ideally in a heavily regulated environment
  • Very good knowledge of modern security technologies (e.g., vulnerability management, network security, endpoint detection, email and cloud security, hardening)
  • Practical experience in Linux engineering with a focus on Linux systems; experience with Windows environments is desirable
  • Pronounced analytical thinking as well as strong communication and persuasion skills
  • Experience with the implementation of PCI DSS requirements as well as certifications such as CISSP, CCSP (Certified Cloud Security Professional), or comparable are a plus
  • High affinity for complex contexts, independent, reliable, and communicative way of working
  • Very good written and spoken German and English language skills

About us UBS Card Center AG, a subsidiary of UBS Switzerland AG, is Switzerland's leading credit card processor. Well over 3 million Visa and Mastercard credit cards are reliably processed via our systems. With more than 750 dedicated employees at the Glattbrugg and Manno locations, we combine many years of expertise with innovative strength and work every day to make the payments of tomorrow simpler, more secure, and smarter.
