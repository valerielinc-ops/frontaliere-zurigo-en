Full description

Do you like to start the day early, love being outdoors, and enjoy putting a smile on people's faces? Then an apprenticeship in delivery (specializing in distribution) is just right for you! During the three-year basic education, you will acquire the tools for a successful entry into the diverse world of logistics.

Your Education

  • Early in the morning, you sort your letters and packages and load your delivery vehicle for the subsequent delivery tour.
  • Afterwards, you will be on the road independently, delivering and picking up shipments of all kinds and taking care of our customers' concerns.
  • Before you start your well-deserved end of work, you complete various follow-up tasks at your training position.
  • During your training, you will be supervised by professionals, introduced to practice, and continuously developed.
  • Additionally, with financial support from the post office, you either obtain a category A1 motorcycle license or a category B motor vehicle license and learn to drive a forklift.
  • With various internal stations, deployment in a warehouse, and customized support measures tailored to your wishes, we complete your broad logistics training.

What you bring with you

We are looking for young people with enthusiasm, joy, and motivation who want to make a difference. You should meet the following criteria:

  • Physical activity and working outdoors will excite you.
  • In addition, you enjoy interacting with people, are reliable, honest, and reserved.
  • You are ready to obtain the learner's license A1 or B by the start of the apprenticeship.
  • You have completed compulsory schooling.
  • At the start of the course, you will have the B2 language level in German.
