Full description

Would you like to start your career in one of Switzerland's best retail programs and manage your own branch within 9-12 months?

Then apply for our Store Manager Trainee Program!, WHAT WE ARE BEEING FOR YOU

You will have the chance to join a retail trade company that wants to be the first choice of candidates as an employer. As a JYSK ambassador you will get the following advantages:

  • A five-week trainee program with interactive training on all the important aspects of JYSK's filial leadership, accompanied by experienced sales and HR experts training you and coaching.
  • An experienced mentor from the organization is available to you and your development needs
  • During the program you will be used as a deputy branch manager and will take responsibility from the beginning
  • Opportunities for own development and ownership
  • Above average revenue bonus for the entire team
  • 20% discount on JYSK and other Lars Larsen Group companies
  • Top pension fund benefits
  • 5 weeks holidays, which increase every 5 years of service
  • Other advantages such as an above-average additional accident insurance, various activities (annual celebration, team events) and great anniversary gifts

WORM IS IT IN OUR TRAINEE PROGRAMME

Our program offers high quality training in leadership, management and branch operations, combined with practical experience and theoretical knowledge. You usually work and learn in close cooperation with other trainees and your mentor.

  • Five modules of one week duration: theoretical knowledge, role playing, group work, team building and discussions in plenary
  • Between the modules you will be trained with an assigned store manager mentor at the workplace and receive coaching
  • After completion of the modules, a celebration awaits you to celebrate the completion of the program

The ultimate goal of the Store Manager Trainee program is to prepare you to run your own branch in the near future. WHAT YOU MITTING SOLTEST

  • You are result-oriented and have a passion for retail
  • You have the ability to remain calm and determined even in stressful situations
  • You feel well taken care of in a dynamic working environment in constant change
  • You like physical work, have a hands-on mind and take the lead with good examples
  • You are flexible and ready to work on Saturdays

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
