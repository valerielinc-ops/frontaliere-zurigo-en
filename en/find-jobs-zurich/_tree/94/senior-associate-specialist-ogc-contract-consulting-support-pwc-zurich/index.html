Your team

In the Office of General Counsel, you support the Contract Consulting Team in a wide range of contractual and legal issues. Together, we ensure that internal inquiries are processed efficiently and that the relevant specialist areas are involved at an early stage. You work at the interface to various teams and support in contract processes, tenders and internal projects. With your structured and reliable way of working, you make an important contribution to smooth processes and professional legal support within PwC. Your impact

  • As the first point of contact, coordinate internal inquiries on contractual and other legal topics and involve the relevant specialists if necessary.
  • Manage more complex inquiries within the OGC Contract Consulting team and ensure their careful review and timely feedback.
  • Coordinate formal bid requests and tenders, bringing together specialist areas such as data protection, risk management and information security.
  • Actively shape internal projects and, with appropriate experience, take responsibility for their coordination and implementation.
  • After your training, support the independent review and commentary on changes to confidentiality agreements and other standard contracts.
  • Maintain contract templates, electronic contract filings and digital platforms and contribute with current content to efficient and reliable processes.

Your skill set

  • Completed commercial or legal training, such as a KV or paralegal training, or a comparable qualification.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing for professional cooperation with different internal contacts: inside.
  • Secure use of MS Office and interest in digital tools, platforms and efficient processes.
  • Structured, independent and reliable working methods, as well as the ability to carefully coordinate several requests and deadlines.
  • Strong team orientation, initiative and enjoyment of working with different specialist areas in a dynamic environment.
  • High degree of professionalism and discretion in dealing with confidential and sensitive legal information as well as a good sense of future requirements.
