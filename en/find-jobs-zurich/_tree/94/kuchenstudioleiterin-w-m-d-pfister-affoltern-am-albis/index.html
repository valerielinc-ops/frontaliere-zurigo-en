We at pfister pursue a vision: We want to move our products into every Swiss home. We are seeking active support for this. There are already around 1,400 people working at pfister today - will you join soon?

Your new tasks

  • Experience leading a team of 2-3 people
  • craft training
  • Quick understanding and solution-oriented action
