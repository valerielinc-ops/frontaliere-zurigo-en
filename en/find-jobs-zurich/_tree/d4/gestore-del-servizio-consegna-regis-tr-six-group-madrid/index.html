Do you want to work in a highly dynamic and regulated financial market environment? Are you passionate about software delivery, governance, and operational excellence? Then our REGIS-TR Service Delivery team would like to hear from you.

In this role, you will act as the bridge between Business, Product, IT and Operations teams, ensuring effective governance, delivery execution, and continuous improvement of the application lifecycle.
