You can look forward to that.

Become part of a motivated team and look forward to a very varied police work at the Genesis Einsatzgruppe in Zurich. Your exciting challenges will include missions throughout Switzerland. As a specialist: in the Einsatzgruppe you will be able to shape and further develop a specialist area. Interested in learning more about our professions? We look forward to seeing you from 22 to 24. 2026 in our SBB Berufswelten Café at Zurich Central Station. All information can be found at sbb.ch/berufsweltencafe. You can move that.

  • As a police officer: in the Genesis task force in Zurich, together with more than 190 employees, you take care of security and order at the stations and in the public transport of various transport companies.
  • You will be used in focal actions to resolve scene formations. With police interventions you contribute to security and order.
  • Your tasks also include personal protection operations and joint operations with cantonal police, city police, military police and the Federal Office for Customs and Border Security.
  • You are also involved in operations to combat pocket and trick theft.
  • You bring the willingness to take over the deputy of the Team Principal Einsatzgruppe in Zurich.

You bring that with you.

  • Since you are in daily contact with our customers, you bring a service-oriented and winning personality. With us, teamwork is the key to success - you are a team player: in and look forward to working together.
  • As a member of the Einsatzgruppe, you are ready to provide regular legal services.
  • You have the Swiss passport Police Officer: in I and bring good police tactical and police technical knowledge. Experience in instruction is beneficial. If you do not yet bring them, you are ready to develop further in this area and complete appropriate training.
  • You have a category B driving licence.
  • You can use your good IT and administrative knowledge regularly.
