Full description

Together with all specialists involved in the care of patients, we are responsible for effective exit management and perform an important task in the recovery process as follow-up and social counselling.

Tasks

  • Professional advice and support for patients and their relatives in the area of psychosocial consequences of illness/accident/pregnancy as well as in social law issues
  • Organising follow-up solutions in the form of rehabilitation, nursing homes, recreational cures, rehab clinics and more
  • Networking with further counselling centres
  • Administrative tasks such as file management, correspondence, etc.
  • Participation in interdisciplinary reports, cooperation with external organisations
  • Independent management of patient dossiers

Profile

  • You are currently studying at ZHAW Soziale Arbeit and are looking for a second internship
  • You are an open, flexible and well-structured person who appreciates working in interdisciplinary teams and working with external advisory services and institutions.
  • You are happy to communicate and are committed to the best possible solutions for patients and their relatives as part of exit management
  • You are used to forwarding work and keep a bold head even in stressful situations
