# Product Management SAP Outsourcing

Il tuo futuro inizia qui

In our Platform & Application Operation team, you take responsibility for the strategic orientation and further development of our SAP Basis Services. You build the bridge between technical depth and strategic product management and transform complex platform capabilities into marketable, secure managed services for our B2B customers. This role is ideal for an experienced product manager with a solid technical background in the SAP base and managed services environment – someone who designs product vision and roadmap as confidently as they translate customer requirements into technical solutions.

  • You will be responsible for the strategy, positioning and further development of our managed services in the area of SAP Basis over the entire product lifecycle – from vision to operation.
  • You define and maintain product vision, roadmap, and features, and control the prioritization of strategic initiatives along business value, customer needs, and security requirements.
  • You support sales and solution architecture in tenders, check the portfolio fit and ensure the technical feasibility of complex customer requirements.
  • You support sales with your technical expertise directly in customer discussions and accompany pre-sales phases for challenging projects.
  • Working closely with DevOps teams, architecture, product owners and other stakeholders, you translate high-level market needs into concrete technical capabilities and user stories.
  • You observe market, technology and customer developments in the SAP environment and derive product initiatives and new features from them.
  • You are responsible for product budget, pricing, cost-effectiveness and portfolio integration and manage product success based on clearly defined KPIs.
  • You promote innovation, automation and operational excellence and thus make a decisive contribution to Swisscom’s strategy.

Your skills

  • You bring experience in product management – ideally for SAP Basis or Managed Services products.
  • You have a solid technical understanding of SAP Basis and managed service operating models – especially in the environment of SAP HANA, S/4HANA, SAP RISE and hybrid and cloud-based SAP landscapes.
  • You know the key requirements for secure, stable and efficient SAP operations and can translate topics such as lifecycle management, availability, performance, automation, monitoring, and backup and recovery into marketable services.
  • You think strategically, act independently and make data-based decisions even in a dynamic, cross-functional environment.
  • You enjoy innovation, customer proximity and close cooperation with technology-related and business stakeholders.
  • You perform confidently and communicate convincingly – both in the technical and business context.

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Benefits.
