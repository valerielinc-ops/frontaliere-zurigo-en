Full description

Eraneos is an international consulting group for strategy, transformation and technology that helps companies succeed in a dynamic digital world. With top-class experts from business and technology, we empower our customers to set new standards for sustainable transformations – from strategy to implementation.

Whether it is in the design of sustainable organisations, the development of new potentials through data and AI or the securing of companies through holistic cybersecurity: We deliver results pragmatically and purposefully. Our team of around 1,200 dedicated employees is represented in offices in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, Denmark, Sweden, Austria, Great Britain, Singapore and the USA and combines global perspectives with strong local roots. Around 450 employees work at the Swiss locations Zurich, Bern and Lausanne.

This is the job - your main tasks

  • Advising our customers in the strategy, conception and implementation of modern solutions in the areas of Agentic AI, Generative AI, Data Science, Data Platforms and Data Management & Governance
  • Active development of existing customer relationships and acquisition of new mandates based on a resilient professional network
  • Co-design and further develop our service portfolio in the area of Data & AI with a clear focus on market relevance, customer benefits and future viability
  • Responsibility in demanding customer projects and active contribution to their successful implementation in close cooperation with specialist departments, technology and management
  • Technical contribution to the expansion of our team and to the further development of our presence in the Data & AI market
  • You use your contacts, acquire new projects and access your long-standing professional network.

This job is for you if

  • at least 7 years of experience in the field of data & AI, ideally in consulting, software development or in a large company's Data & AI team
  • extensive and reliable know-how is brought with you in the Data & AI environment and the relevant topics can be safely classified from the engineering perspective to the C-level communication
  • Projects with high commitment, team orientation and clear implementation requirements are successfully promoted
  • the development and maintenance of resilient customer relationships are just as natural as the development of new customers and strategic partnerships
  • Energy is generated from interaction with people, from entrepreneurial co-design and from the further development of a growing consulting environment
  • Negotiable German and English skills are available at at least C1 level

As an icing on the cake you bring along

  • Certifications in relevant technologies or ecosystems such as Azure, AWS, GCP, Databricks or Snowflake
  • Training in the field of data & AI
  • Experience in positioning and further development of modern data & AI topics in the market
  • a good sense of new customer potential, partnerships and the expansion of existing accounts

Even if not all requirements are fully met, we are very pleased about an application.
