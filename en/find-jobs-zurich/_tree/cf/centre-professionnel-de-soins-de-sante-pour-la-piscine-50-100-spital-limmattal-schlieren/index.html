We look after our residents and their relatives with expertise and a lot of human commitment in often complex situations. The treatment, care and care in high quality therefore require specific knowledge in the field of geriatrics as well as individually adapted support in health, social or mental matters. This is why our multi-professional team works closely together. It consists of doctors:women, nurses, a social worker, activation specialists, therapist:women and pastoral workers. Direct applications are preferred, external recruitment offices are not treated as a priority.

Here they engage

  • in the holistic care and care of residents in the interprofessional team
  • at various care stations in our care center
  • accompanying and promoting apprentices
  • In the guidance and promotion of nursing staff
  • in general assistance in all areas of the station

Support in your everyday work is provided by a competent station management, a kinesthetics trainer and a care expert.

  • have completed vocational training as a health professional
  • have expertise and expertise in geriatrics and palliative care
  • are a motivated and empathetic personality
