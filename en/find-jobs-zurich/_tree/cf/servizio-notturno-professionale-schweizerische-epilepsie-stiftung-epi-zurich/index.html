The Diverse Night

  • Together with your colleagues, you accompany and look after adults with cognitive and psychological impairments and multiple disabilities, with sometimes challenging behaviors and epilepsy during the night.
  • They ensure comprehensive and individual care and care based on the care process during the night
  • They actively participate in the further development of the offer for residents in the nighttime

Their diverse talents

  • Completed nursing training based on EFZ / HF
  • Experience and safety in accompanying and caring for people with disabilities and challenging behaviors
  • Safety in dealing with somatic emergency situations
  • High personal responsibility and structured working methods
  • Empathy, good communication skills and a positive understanding of services
  • Targeted further training on the subject of aggression management and dealing with challenging behavior, a very good internal educational offer and promotion of external further training
  • 5 weeks of vacation, from the 50th. Age 6 weeks
  • Own pension fund with an above-average employer share of 60%
  • Staff apartments and perks in the EPI Park Restaurant, in the pharmacy and with half-tax or general subscription
