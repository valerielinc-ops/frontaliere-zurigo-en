Full description

In the “Account & Service Management” department, you will work on the further development of OIZ’s IT service and product portfolio and help design modern solutions for the city.

  • As the primary point of contact on a tactical level, you look after our customers and maintain relationships through proactive expectation management.
  • You carry out the order clearance independently, taking into account technical, professional and financial conditions.
  • You advise our customers on IT guidelines and services and support them in budget and project planning.
  • You control the cooperation with the customers within the framework of the applicable requirements and ensure the quality of the OIZ services as well as the correct billing.
  • You organize account management meetings and ensure step-by-step communication and professional escalation management.
  • You bring several years of experience in the support of IT customers as well as practice in the management or coordination of IT projects, ideally in the health or hospital environment (e.g. medical informatics, hospital IT, nursing informatics).
  • You are well versed in structured order clearing and can also safely appear and negotiate in demanding customer situations. You communicate at eye level across all hierarchical levels and pursue goals consistently and bindingly.
  • You have a sound understanding of complex IT infrastructures, data center services and the associated operational processes.
  • You have a higher technical or business education (HF/FH/Uni) with a focus on IT or an equivalent proof of experience.
  • A stylish and addressable expression and communication in word and writing (German) rounds off your profile.
  • A diverse and collegial working environment in an agile organization
  • Flexible working time model and above-average social benefits
  • Possibility for home office
  • Targeted training and education opportunities
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave and 4 weeks for co-parents
  • Various perks (mobility contribution, lunch check, fitness, telephony, etc.)
  • Annual health contribution to promote your well-being
  • Accessible jobs

The Organization and Computer Science (OIZ) is the computer science competence center of the city of Zurich. For our customers, we provide high-quality and competitive services in the areas of organization and computer science.
