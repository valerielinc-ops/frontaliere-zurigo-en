This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Responsibility

  • Support for the technical support of the Electronic Key Management System (EKMS) and the associated ICT funds for the benefit of the Swiss Army
  • Creation of concepts, analyses, clarifications and reports as well as development of expertise and cooperation in the use of the EKMS
  • Verify and implement firmware and software changes and maintain the virtual VMware ESXi server infrastructure including backup management and user support
  • Development and maintenance of system documentation for the EKMS as well as for the associated ICT resources and employees in configuration management
  • Assistance in the creation of information security and data protection concepts
  • Contributions to regulations, training and operational needs

Requirements

  • Higher education or higher technical education in a technical field (e.g. computer science or electrical engineering) or equivalent education and training (advantageous field of IT security) and/or several years of professional experience
  • Willingness to familiarize yourself with the topics of ICT topics and cryptology as well as to continuously develop yourself, SAP knowledge desirable
  • Very good knowledge of German and English (B2) in word and writing and good knowledge of French (B1)
  • Analytical, independent and reliable working method with a strong technical understanding
  • Engaged, resilient as well as team- and customer-oriented personality with an independent and responsible working method
  • Swiss nationality (due to function) as well as driving licence category B, readiness for regular piquetting
