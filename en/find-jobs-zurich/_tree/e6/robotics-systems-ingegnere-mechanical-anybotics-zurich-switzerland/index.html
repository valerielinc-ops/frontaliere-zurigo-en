Full description

ANYbotics is a fast-growing tech company dedicated to shaping the future of mobile robotics across multiple industries. Join our highly talented and motivated team of more than 200 people and work on cutting-edge robot technology.

The Opportunity

We are looking for an experienced Robotics Systems Engineer (Mechanical) to join our team of product developers and robot enthusiasts. We build competitive products for our customers, and they depend on tightly integrated, complex mechatronic systems. In this role, you bridge mechanical design and system-level engineering. You own how mechanical subsystems fit into the overall robot architecture, from requirements and interfaces through integration, verification, and field support.

You will work closely with specialists from R&D, electronics, and software development to define, evaluate, integrate, and validate the robotic system across the full product development process. You will also work continuously with our operations team so that system decisions meet product target costs, manufacturability, and quality standards as ONE TEAM. Your work makes our robots more nimble, robust, and user-friendly, and opens new ways to optimize system performance and capabilities. We are passionate about excellent hardware design and system performance at competitive costs.

The Impact

As a Robotics Systems Engineer (Mechanical) , you will play a crucial role in shaping the architecture and design of our future robotic systems. You will turn customer and product needs into system and mechanical requirements, define and manage subsystem interfaces, and drive trade-off decisions on performance, robustness, weight, cost, and serviceability. Your contributions will directly increase the reliability and attractiveness of our products.

With your innovative mindset and industrial experience, you will bring new ideas and methods to how we develop, integrate, and verify our robots. Your hands-on experience with mechatronic systems makes you key to improving and upgrading the robot’s functions. Working alongside our R&D experts, you will strengthen our ability to sustain and continuously improve our product portfolio in the field.

Market & Our Technology

ANYbotics transforms industrial plants in the energy, process, and utility sectors by bringing robotics to a wide range of new applications that were previously out of reach. Our customers are large asset operators and industrial service providers pioneering the use of robotics for inspection and maintenance. Our mobile robot ANYmal uses legs for extreme mobility in complex environments, camera- and LiDAR-based sensing for full autonomy and obstacle avoidance, and AI for high-quality, consistent inspection results. We develop a wide range of custom hardware in-house, including the complete robotic platform, actuators, sensors, inspection payloads, charging systems, and all related mechanical hardware, and integrate it into one reliable system.

Your Contributions

  • Define system and mechanical requirements from customer needs and use cases
  • Shape the system architecture and design integrated mechatronic subsystems
  • Define and manage interfaces between platform, actuators, sensors, payloads, and charging systems
  • Drive trade-offs on performance, robustness, weight, cost, and serviceability
  • Plan and run system integration, verification, and validation
  • Do engineering analyses, including FEA and tolerance stack-ups
  • Monitor robustness, cost, and quality across the product life cycle, including root cause analyses and field inspections
  • Work with R&D, software, and operations on specifications and design reviews
  • Create system and product documentation, from specifications to assembly and user manuals

Your Profile

  • University degree in mechanical engineering, mechatronics, or a related field
  • At least 5 years of industrial experience in systems engineering or mechanical product development
  • Proven experience with requirements engineering, interface management, and system integration of mechatronic products
  • Hands-on experience with verification, validation, and testing
  • Proficient in SolidWorks and PDM environments
  • Experience with FEA, tolerance analysis, and DIN/ISO tolerancing standards
  • Knowledge of design-to-cost and productivity optimization methods
  • Excellent written and spoken English
  • Structured team player who enjoys working across disciplines in a high-tech company

Bonus Points

  • Experience with MBSE or requirements management tools is a plus
  • Experience sizing standard components such as motors, gearboxes, and bearings
  • Experience with explosion-proof design and certification under ATEX and IECEx
  • Knowledge of the design and manufacturing of injection-molded parts
  • Experience with electric drives and control theory
  • Programming skills (e.g. Python) for analysis, testing, or automation

We offer you a very exciting and dynamic work environment, the opportunity to become part of a fast-growing company and an ambitious team that is on a mission to change the industrial inspection market, a chance to leverage your experience and bring in your own ideas, a fair market salary, an attractive employee stock ownership plan, and a job in the city of Zurich.
