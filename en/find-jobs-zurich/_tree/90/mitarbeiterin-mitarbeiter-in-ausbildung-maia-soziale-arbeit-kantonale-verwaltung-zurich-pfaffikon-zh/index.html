Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Career Advice Employee in Education (MAiA) Social work, 60% Good for life – that is our motto. The Office for Youth and Vocational Counselling (AJB) is responsible for child and youth welfare as well as professional counselling. Our work focuses on children, young people and families. We work for them every day with a lot of heart and commitment. The kjz (Child and Youth Assistance Centre) is a polyvalent counselling centre for families, parents, adolescents, children and toddlers and works on the basis of the Children and Youth Assistance Act of the Canton of Zurich. Do you want to work for the good of children and young people? Then, as an employee in training (MAiA) in the field of social work and mandates in the kjz Pfäffikon, you will be able to work on 1 January. February 2027 or by appointment part of our team. Parallel to your education at a university of applied sciences in social work, you will have the opportunity to specialize in child and youth welfare. They are successively incorporated into the task area. Our core business includes:

  • Case management of legal measures on behalf of the child and adult protection authority KESB (assistance and guardianship)
  • Counselling on behalf of parents
  • Clarifications regarding child welfare risks on behalf of the child and adult protection authority KESB or the courts In addition to the core tasks and the cooperation with parents, children and young people, this means in the daily work above all:
  • Filing and drafting of reports and requests to authorities
  • Cooperation with homes, foster families, socio-educational family support, child and adolescent psychiatric services, school psychological services, etc.
  • depending on the mandate also the execution of tasks within the framework of financial administration You have already started with the basic study at a university of applied sciences in social work or bring the admission for the start of studies. We are addressing a motivated, reliable and resilient person with a high level of social competence. They are characterized by an independent working method as well as a good written and oral expressive ability. First professional experience and a minimum age of 25 years are required. We offer you an attractive, meaningful training position in an exciting environment as well as a workplace with modern infrastructure. The employment conditions correspond to the cantonal guidelines. Can we answer any further questions? For further information see Thimothée Ruh, thimothee.ruh@ajb.zh.ch , tel. 043 258 47 93, social worker and practitioner kjz Pfäffikon gladly available. We look forward to your complete application via online tool until the 13th. December 2026 to Raphaela Capeder, Human Resources, office of Hinwil, Meilen, Pfäffikon and Uster districts in Wetzikon. Only applications submitted via our online platform can be processed.

Pensum: 60%
