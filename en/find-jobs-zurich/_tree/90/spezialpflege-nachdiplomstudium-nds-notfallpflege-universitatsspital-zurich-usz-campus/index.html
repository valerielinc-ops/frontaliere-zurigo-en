As a specialist care provider at the University Hospital in Zurich, we are committed to showing commitment with our professional diversity in the top medical environment and always keeping up with the times. We do not achieve success alone, but together! Each one counts and together we bring ourselves to the highest level of care.

Want to be part of our team? With us you can make a difference!

  • With us you treat a variety of emergency patients in an interprofessional and interdisciplinary environment.
  • You will experience an exciting working day with quickly changing situations in one of the largest and most varied emergency departments in Switzerland
  • You apply the combination of human and professional professionalism and develop it further
  • You triage all emergency patients: women according to Emergency Severity Index
  • You are a nurse / nurse specialist HF/FH or have a foreign diploma in nursing recognised by the State Secretariat for Education, Research and Innovation (SBFI). 6 months of experience in acute care
