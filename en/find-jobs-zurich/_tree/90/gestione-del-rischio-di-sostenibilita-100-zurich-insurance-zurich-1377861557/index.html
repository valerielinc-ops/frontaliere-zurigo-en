Full description

Are you interested in sustainability and risk management? Join our Strategic and Operational Risk (SOR) team as a Sustainability Risk Manager and work across sustainability, emerging risks and enterprise risk management. Working closely with the Head of Sustainability Risk, you’ll help manage evolving risk topics while gaining exposure to a broad range of strategic and operational risk areas.

What you will do

  • Work closely with the Head of Sustainability Risk to support the management of sustainability-related risks across the Group, including contributing to risk deep dives, policy governance updates, control reviews, risk reporting and engagement with key stakeholders across the business.
  • Work alongside the Head of Sustainability Risk on the Group’s emerging risk management activities, including the organization of the Emerging and Sustainability Risk Committee, update and monitoring of the Emerging Risk View, policy governance update, risk reporting, and contribution to external industry initiatives such as the CRO Forum.
  • Contribute to broader SOR priorities and assignments beyond sustainability and emerging risks, supporting strategic and operational risk activities as required and collaborating flexibly across the team.

What you bring

  • Bachelor’s Degree in relevant field, such as business administration, economics, finance.
  • 5+ years of relevant experience in sustainability and related fields.
  • Prior experience in functions such as risk management, underwriting, finance, investment management and/or operations is an asset.
  • Proactive, driven, action and results oriented.
  • Exceptional stakeholder management and influential verbal and written communication skills, particularly in English.
