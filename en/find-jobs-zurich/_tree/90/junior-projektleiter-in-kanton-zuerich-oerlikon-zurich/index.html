Full description

In the Zurich notary offices, around 650 employees are committed to the concerns of customers and attach great importance to quality, discretion and customer orientation. All 44 notary offices in the canton of Zurich are also land register and bankruptcy offices and are part of the legal administration in Zurich. The Notarial Inspectorate of the Canton of Zurich performs on behalf of the Higher Court the management in the areas of supervision and administration for the notarial system and provides central services with its employees. The team notarial projects is responsible for the professional handling of project projects, conducts central courses in basic education and is responsible for the content of intranet and website. We are looking for a competent and motivated reinforcement as Junior Project Manager as of January 2027 or by agreement 80 - 100% Your responsibilities

  • Project management in specialist projects (on organizational, personnel, communicative and professional topics) – at the beginning, cooperation, later on under own responsibility.
  • The coordination of working groups as well as evaluations, reporting and research are part of the task profile.
  • Management of the content in the intranet and in the web presence of the notaries, via CMS.
  • Deputy Head of Vocational Education and Training as contact person for vocational and apprentices.
  • Share responsibility for our apprenticeship marketing. What you bring
  • Commercial or equivalent training, with business development. Initial knowledge in project management and IT as well as knowledge in the areas of notary, land register and bankruptcy are clearly beneficial.
  • Experience in internal communication, marketing and vocational training is also a plus.
  • Independence, resilience, organizational skill and teamwork.
  • Conscious, solution-oriented and organized working method.
  • Confident knowledge of German in words and writing. We offer
  • Interesting and versatile work in a collegial team.
  • Attractive employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines.
  • Careful training and further education opportunities. Further information about the notarial inspectorate, the Zurich notarial system and our exciting tasks can be found on our website. If you have any questions, Lukas Häusermann, Head of Notarial Projects, is at your disposal on 044 931 16 62. Please send your complete application documents to bewerbung-np@notariate-zh.ch.
