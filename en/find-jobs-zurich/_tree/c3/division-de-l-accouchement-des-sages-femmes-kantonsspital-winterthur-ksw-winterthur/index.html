Full description

For our birth hall with around 2,200 births per year, we are looking for a committed and experienced midwife with several years of current professional experience. You have in-depth obstetric knowledge, act safely even in complex situations and are happy to take responsibility. Together with our interprofessional team, you will design an evidence-based, individual and family-centered obstetric care and actively contribute your expertise to the further development of our care.

Tasks

  • Independent and responsible care of women and families before, during and after the birth
  • Professional management of physiological births and competent support of midwife-guided births, taking into account the applicable standards and safety criteria
  • Professional care of high-risk pregnancies and births as well as early recognition and assessment of obstetric risks
  • Safe accompaniment of complex birth processes and obstetric emergency situations in in-depth cooperation with the medical team
  • Ensuring evidence-based, individual and family-centred care while safeguarding the self-determination and needs of childbearers
  • Active participation in quality assurance, the further development of obstetric standards and constructive interprofessional cooperation
  • Professional support and accompaniment of students as well as co-creation of a learning-promoting team culture

Profile

  • Completed training as midwife FH/BSc or an equivalent training recognised in Switzerland
  • Several years of current professional experience in the clinical birthing room, preferably in an obstetric ward with comparable birth rates
  • Strong professional security in the independent management of physiological births as well as experience in midwife-guided obstetrics
  • In-depth experience in the care of risk pregnancies and risk births, in the accompaniment of complex birth processes as well as high reliability of action in obstetric emergency situations
  • High personal responsibility, professional judgment and a strong awareness of patient safety and quality
  • Empathetic, respectful and appreciative communication with women, families and loved ones
  • Strong teamwork, flexibility and enjoyment of interprofessional collaboration
