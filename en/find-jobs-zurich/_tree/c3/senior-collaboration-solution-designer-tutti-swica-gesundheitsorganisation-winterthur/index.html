Full description

You translate professional requirements into secure, user-friendly and operable solutions for collaboration and the digital workplace. Your focus is clearly on Microsoft 365 collaboration

  • At the same time, you consider identities, devices, security and operations end-to-end. You combine Solution Design with your own technical implementation and lead solutions from actual recording to introduction.

This is how you shape health

  • You will analyze existing configurations and requirements and develop solution variants and decision bases for collaboration and modern workplace projects
  • You design end-to-end solutions with a focus on Microsoft Teams, SharePoint Online, Exchange Online and OneDrive, and consider Entra ID, Intune, Security, Data Storage, Integration and Operations
  • You are responsible for the solution design and together with engineering, IT security and compliance define standards for authorizations, external collaboration, governance and lifecycle management
  • You evaluate products and external service providers and accompany migrations as well as the replacement of existing systems
  • You technically implement essential parts of your solutions yourself, plan tests and introductions and coordinate major implementations with engineering
  • You document concepts and decisions in a comprehensible manner, present them to the addressee and ensure the clean transfer to the company

This sets you apart

  • Completed training in computer science, ideally supplemented by higher specialist training in computer science or business informatics or comparable further education
  • Several years of experience in Microsoft 365 solution design or engineering with a demonstrably strong focus on collaboration
  • In-depth knowledge of Microsoft Teams, SharePoint Online and Exchange Online as well as good knowledge of OneDrive, Entra ID and Intune
  • Experience in designing solutions across multiple levels: identities, accesses, data storage, security, integration, terminals and operations
  • Practical experience in the administration and configuration of Microsoft 365, both in the admin centers and with PowerShell
  • Experience with product evaluations, migrations as well as the planning, implementation and quality assurance of technical solutions
  • Independent and structured working methods
  • Strong advisory skills
  • Confident knowledge of German as well as good knowledge of English

  • We build on humanity. That is why we focus on openness, respect and trust in our everyday work. We tackle this, take responsibility and think in terms of solutions – together, for our customers. This is also why we have been number 1 in customer satisfaction for many years.
