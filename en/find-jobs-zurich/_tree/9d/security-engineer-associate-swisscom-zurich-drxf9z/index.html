# Security Engineer (Associate)

Il tuo futuro inizia qui

You work on innovative and challenging connectivity and security products in an agile working environment. You will be part of a team of technology and market experts, product owners, cyber security engineers and other agile roles. Our ambitious goal is to bring the best products to the market for our business customers and to support them in their digitalization in the best possible way.

  • Bringing the Best Mobile, Wireline and Security Products to Market
  • Apply innovation and motivation to convince and engage internal stakeholders
  • Customer management from Awareness via purchase conclusion to Up- or Cross-sell
  • You support the new & further development of security products, platforms and services incl. Testing new solutions in laboratory environments. Collaboration in the definition of standards & creation of best practice guides.

Your skills

  • Completed training (EFZ) in computer science and further education (HF/FH) in computer science or cyber security
  • Sound professional and practical knowledge in Network Security
  • Customer understanding and experience in the B2B environment and experience in ICT security (SSE /SASE), Telco, Cloud, Data Security
  • Customer- and team-oriented and acting independently
  • Ready to set priorities and make decisions
  • Conceptually thinking
  • Able to penetrate problems and issues
  • Very good German and English, French and Italian advantages

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Benefits.
