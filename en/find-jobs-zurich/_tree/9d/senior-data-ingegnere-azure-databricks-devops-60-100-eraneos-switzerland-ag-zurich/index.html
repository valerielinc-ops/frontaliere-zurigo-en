Full description

Eraneos is an international strategy, transformation and technology advisory group that helps companies thrive in a dynamic digital world. With first-class experts from business and technology, we empower our customers to set new standards for sustainable transformations – from strategy to implementation. Whether in the design of future-proof organizations, the development of new potential through data and AI or the protection of companies through holistic cybersecurity: We deliver results pragmatically and purposefully. Our team of around 1,300 dedicated employees is represented in offices in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, Portugal, Denmark, Sweden, Austria, the UK, Singapore and the USA and combines global perspectives with strong local roots. Around 450 employees work at the Swiss locations in Zurich, Bern and Lausanne.

This is the job - your main tasks

  • Build data pipelines: You analyze data sources and requirements and implement automated ingestion and transformation processes with Python, PySpark and SQL on Azure Databricks, in batch and streaming.
  • Data modeling: You develop data models, delta tables and data marts and design bronze, silver and gold layers. You ensure the data quality along the entire processing.
  • DevOps and Infrastructure as Code: You automate the provision of code and resources via CI/CD pipelines. Together with Platform Engineering, Terraform builds reproducible environments from development to production.
  • Advice to the customer: You gain a sound understanding of the technical processes, develop technical concepts and architectures and coordinate them directly with specialist areas, business analysts and platform teams.
  • Team and community: You lead less experienced colleagues. For solution concepts, cost estimates and in acquisition, you are a technical expert.

This job is for you if...

  • a university degree (ETH, Uni or FH) in computer science, data science, mathematics, physics, mechanical engineering, electrical engineering or a related discipline forms your professional basis.
  • five years of experience in data engineering and DevOps are behind you and data processing, software development and platform operation are not new territory for you.
  • Python, PySpark and SQL are your tools, and you’ll be familiar with data modeling and Medallion architecture, ideally on Azure Databricks or a similar platform.
  • AI tools are of course part of everyday engineering for you, you are curiously following new developments and constantly questioning how you and your team can work more efficiently.
  • CI/CD and Infrastructure as Code, for example with Terraform, are part of your craft and you need traceable changes, reproducible deployments and maintainable solutions.
  • It is easy for you to understand complex data flows, to solve problems pragmatically and to explain technical relationships in a comprehensible way for specialist areas such as technical teams, ideally with consulting or project experience.
  • Personal responsibility and result orientation distinguish you, you are happy to share your knowledge and actively contribute to the further development of our team.
  • your German and English skills in word and writing are very good (at least C1).

As an i-top you bring with you

  • Experience in the migration of data platforms, in particular in the analysis of existing data models, data volumes and dependencies as well as the definition of migration strategies and migration phases.
  • Experience with dbt, Azure Data Factory, Microsoft Fabric, GitLab, GitHub or Oracle Exadata.
  • Knowledge of MLflow and MLOps, especially in model management and definition of automated model processes.
  • Experience with concepts such as Data Mesh, Data Products or Data Contracts.Certifications for Databricks, Azure, Terraform or similar.

Even if not all requirements are fully met, we are very happy about an application.
