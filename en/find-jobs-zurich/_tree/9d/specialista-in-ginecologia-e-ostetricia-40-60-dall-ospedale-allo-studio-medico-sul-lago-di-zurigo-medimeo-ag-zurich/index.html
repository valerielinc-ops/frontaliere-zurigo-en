You have done your specialist in Germany, have been working in a Swiss hospital for several years and would like to arrive right now? In a modern gynecologist center on Lake Zurich, you build up your own patient base, part-time, with connections to a women’s clinic and in a tax-friendly residential region. The spacious gynecologist center is closely connected to the women’s clinic of a regional hospital and part of a networked care concept for women’s health. It covers the full spectrum of outpatient gynecology and obstetrics. A well-established team, modern infrastructure and short decision-making paths shape everyday life. The practice is located on Lake Zurich, with good connections to Zurich and eastern Switzerland.

Their tasks

  • Independent gynecological and obstetric care of patients, including outpatient examinations and treatments
  • Establishment and long-term support of an own patient base
  • Close cooperation with the practice team and the associated women’s clinic
  • Professional support and further training of assistant doctors: inside
  • Co-design of high-quality care and further development of practice

Your Competences

  • Specialist title in Gynecology and Obstetrics (FMH or equivalent title recognised by MEBEKO)
  • At least three years at a recognised Swiss institution for further education in gynecology and obstetrics
  • Enjoyment of ambulatory work, independent and responsible working methods
  • Very good knowledge of German, other languages are advantageous

Their advantages

  • Start of practice in permanent employment, without the entrepreneurial risk of own practice
  • Part-time workload of 40-60% with room for family and leisure
  • Own patient base and professional connection to a women's clinic with short distances
  • Training of assistant doctor: inside remains part of your everyday life
  • Generous support for internal and external training
  • Salary in Swiss francs, attractive additional benefits and pension provision via the pension fund
