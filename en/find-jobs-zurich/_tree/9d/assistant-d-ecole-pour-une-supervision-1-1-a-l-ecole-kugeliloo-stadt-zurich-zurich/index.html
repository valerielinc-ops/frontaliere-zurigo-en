Full description

In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a school assistant in the supervision of a student in Maienstrasse immediately. Due to temporary spoken resources, the position is to be filled for the time being until the end of the calendar year. A continuation of the post cannot be guaranteed.

Tasks

  • Accompaniment and easier guidance from a student with special needs in day-to-day care
  • Assumption of support measures with a child according to order management care
  • Co-creation of positive social and learning-enhancing relationships with and between students
  • The care days are each on midday/do. from 12:00 to 17:00. In addition to these, Mo./Di./Fr. can be taken from 12:00 to 13:30

Profile

  • Good knowledge of German (level at least B2, level C1 advantageous) and basic PC user knowledge (e.g. Word, Outlook)
  • Experience in dealing with children and young people, patience and resilience
  • Good communication skills, openness to new and surprising situations, safe appearance
  • Good social skills, teamwork and flexibility
  • Confidentiality and discretion against the outside as well as willingness to further develop in pedagogical topics

We offer

  • A varied, versatile and independent activity
  • A multicultural, exciting environment
  • Competent support from the management
  • Progressive salary and social benefits according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich (Horte)

About us

We are a large school community in Zurich-Nord with 19 schools. We derive our pedagogical attitude from the concept of New Authority and thus meet the daily challenges in everyday school life together and effectively.
