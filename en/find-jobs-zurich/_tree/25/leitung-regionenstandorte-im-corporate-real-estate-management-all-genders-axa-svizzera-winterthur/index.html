Shape your future – together with us!

Would you like to actively shape the future with us? And jointly develop innovative solutions and further professionalize our business? If yes - then you are right with us! We offer a working environment in which thinking is required, teamwork is a high priority and flexibility is encouraged. We look forward to you and your participation and are convinced: “Together we make the difference.”

We at Corporate Real Estate Management (CREM) take care of the self-used properties of AXA in Switzerland. The area of responsibility covers all aspects of building management – from the building substance to the building technology and the house maintenance operation to the furnishing and relocation planning. Our headquarters are located in Winterthur, where we manage around 2400 jobs. In addition, there are around 1100 jobs in the regional locations of Zurich, Bern, Lugano, St. Gallen and Lausanne. For these regional locations, we are looking for a new location management.

In the area of customer operations, we design the individual experiences of our customers in the event of damage and performance. They are our focus and together with them we find the right solution. In this way, our customer-first mentality ensures maximum customer satisfaction.

This is what awaits you

  • You lead a team with 6 FTE, which takes care of the operational operation of the buildings in Bern, Lugano, Lausanne, Zurich and St. Gallen. This covers a wide range of topics in the areas of facility management (e.g. house maintenance, reception, cleaning), security (e.g. SIBE, Evak), room management (e.g. removals, parking management, room planning, projects).
  • Today, the team consists of two small teams that will be reunited under you. Bringing the two teams together is an important aspect of your job, especially in the first two years. Together with your team, you analyze the responsibilities, processes and processes and restructure them by simplifying them, standardizing them and further developing their digitalization.
  • You are part of the CREM management team and form the interface to the other specialist areas in CREM (HLKS, electrical, home maintenance, CREM services and workplace management) as well as to the Accounting & Controlling department. You are an input provider so that the needs of the region locations can be incorporated into concepts and decisions. In addition, you implement measures developed by the specialist departments in the regions, taking into account the respective circumstances per location.
  • You are responsible for stakeholder management, which includes internal committees (e.g. employees and managers at the regional locations) as well as external stakeholders (e.g. administrations, tenants) and ensure good communication and cooperation.
  • Together with your team, you are responsible for provider management (e.g. home maintenance, cleaning, reception, post) – you coordinate, negotiate and take on strategic topics and control proactively to ensure ongoing operation.
  • You take responsibility for financial management and contracts in your area, create calculations, SLAs and reporting
  • You will enthusiastically work on the building strategy and implement projects in the regional locations and provide input on the further development of the locations

You bring that with you

  • You have completed your studies in the field of business organization/economics, real estate management, facility management or a similar discipline as well as several years of professional experience
  • You have leadership experience as well as experience in change management and enjoy accompanying a team in change You are flexible, proactive and assertive and convince through strong social skills, in particular empathy, trustworthiness and reliability
  • Strong analytical skills and high digital affinity set you apart. You enjoy working with your team to analyze and further develop the responsibilities, processes and processes.
  • You are happy to travel (Switzerland) and like to travel regularly to the various AXA locations in Switzerland
  • Negotiation skills as well as very good communication and language skills round off your profile – you speak fluent German and can communicate in English and French
