Full description

To strengthen our rapidly growing Data Center team, we are looking for a Health & Safety Supervisor (m/f/d) who is passionate about creating and maintaining a world-class safety culture on complex, high-profile mission-critical projects.

In this challenging role, you will be responsible for supporting and monitoring all health, safety and environmental activities on site, ensuring compliance with Swiss regulations, client requirements and Implenia's high HSE standards. You will work closely with project management, subcontractors and client representatives to ensure that safety remains our highest priority throughout every phase of construction. The role reflects key responsibilities commonly found in leading international data center projects, including site inspections, contractor management, risk assessments, training, incident investigations and emergency preparedness

Responsibilities

What can you except in the role of Health and Safety Manager (m/w/d), Opfikon, 100%? • Lead and promote a proactive safety culture across the Data Center construction site, ensuring compliance with Implenia and client EHS minimum requirement and related national regulations. • Be a strong presence onsite to support and lead contractors to comply with the site EHS standards. • Conduct site inspections, audits, safety walks, and risk assessments, identifying hazards and driving corrective and preventive actions. • Coordinate and monitor subcontractor safety performance, inductions, permits to work, toolbox talks, and EHS documentation. • Review contractors’ documentation and RAMS/WMS to ensure compliance with Implenia standards. • Investigate incidents, near misses, and unsafe conditions, support root cause analyses, and implement effective improvement measures. • Report into the EHS site manager and liaise closely with the Implenia onsite team ensuring clear communication of all EHS related information. • Deliver EHS training, provide expert advice to project stakeholders, and contribute to continuous improvement initiatives across the project. • Provide EHS guidance and support to Implenia construction and package managers. • Update SOPs, and site EHS documentation when required. • Lead the site EHS daily briefings.

Requirements

What qualifications and expierence do you bring to the role of Health and Safety Manager (m/w/d), Opfikon, 100%? • Must hold at least diploma level recognised qualification in Occupational Safety, Health and Welfare at Work, Construction safety management or recognised equivalent diploma. • Several years of EHS experience on large-scale construction projects in pharmaceutical, industrial, infrastructure, or Data Center projects. • Strong knowledge of construction safety management, risk assessment methodologies, contractor management, and permit-to-work systems working at least at General Contractor level. • Confident communicator with the ability to engage professionally with workforce teams, subcontractors, and management while fostering a strong safety culture. • The ability to work independently and as part of a team. • Fluent in English

  • German is a strong advantage. Relevant EHS certifications (e.g., NEBOSH, SUVA, ISO 45001, or equivalent) are highly desirable.
