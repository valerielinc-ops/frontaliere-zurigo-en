# Solution Sales B2B

Il tuo futuro inizia qui

You are the driving force behind sustainable customer relationships and tailor-made offers with real added value. As a solution sales for our large enterprise customers, you recognize business potential at an early stage, develop opportunities in the areas of data center & cloud, network, security and workplace and accompany innovative ideas from the first vision to the successful conclusion. Together with our experts, you will create sustainable business added value, position yourself as a Trusted Advisor and take our customers – as well as Swisscom – to the next level.

  • Development and sale of complex IT solutions with focus on data center & cloud, network, workplace and security
  • Translating customer requirements at management level into economically and technologically convincing offers
  • Building and maintaining strategic customer relationships in the Swiss enterprise market
  • Management of large tenders and tender preparation up to the conclusion of the contract
  • Active cold acquisition and participation in the go-to-market strategy

Your skills

  • Degree (ETH/Uni/FH) in Business Informatics or Computer Science or equivalent
  • Strong sales affinity in the distribution of IT services on the Swiss market
  • Proven and several years of professional experience in the sales of complex IT services (cloud & data center, network, security, workplace) in the B2B environment
  • Negotiable communication with contact persons at management level
  • Enjoy building sustainable customer partnerships
  • Very good German and English
  • French advantage

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Benefits.
