Full description

What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family company with over 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessories as well as the provision of financial services. Emil Frey Digital AG is part of the Emil Frey Group and is a full-service communications service provider for the automotive industry and responsible for digitalization within the Emil Frey Group. To strengthen our Paid Media Team at our location in Zurich Oerlikon, we are looking for a structured, analytical and communicative personality as a Media Planner* in your tasks.

  • You support the development of data-based media strategies for different customers, brands and target groups
  • You create digital media plans with goals, target groups, channels, durations, budgets, performance indicators and measurement basics
  • You analyze market, target group, competition and campaign data and derive relevant insights for planning
  • Coordinate briefings, releases and handovers to internal operations teams and external partners
  • You work closely with strategy, campaign operations, performance management, key account management and external media partners
  • Prepare presentations, status overviews and decision bases for customers and internal stakeholders
  • You observe relevant media, platform and market trends and actively introduce new ideas into customer strategies
  • You bring your first experience or great interest in media, digital media planning, marketing, communication or consulting
  • You have a good understanding of digital channels such as paid social, search, programmatic, display, video or similar platforms
  • You think analytically, work structured and can meaningfully connect data, goals, budgets and target groups
  • You have a reliable, careful and quality-conscious way of working
  • You communicate clearly, professionally and customer-oriented in German
  • English skills are beneficial
  • You are confident in using Excel, PowerPoint and digital tools or ready to get used to them quickly
  • You are curious, willing to learn, team-oriented and want to develop further in the field of media planning What we offer you You will find a new challenge with exciting tasks and much more:
  • Numerous discounts (new and used cars, service work and spare parts, vehicle and health insurance, fuel cards, hotels and much more)
  • An own, attractive pension fund as an independent pension institution
  • A long-term business perspective
  • Individual training and development opportunities
  • At least 25 days of vacation How to contact us We look forward to the full application (e.g. CV, employment certificates, etc.) via the link "Apply now!".
