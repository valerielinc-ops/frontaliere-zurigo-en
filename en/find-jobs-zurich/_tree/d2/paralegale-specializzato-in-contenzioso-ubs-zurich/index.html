Full description

Key responsibilities Are you an organized, detail-oriented legal professional with hands-on experience supporting civil litigation? Do you enjoy working on complex cases in a dynamic and global environment, and are you excited by the opportunity to help build a pioneering in-house litigation function? We are recruiting Litigation Paralegals for our hubs in Zürich and Geneva, also supporting the Lugano hub. You will:

  • support our litigation lawyers throughout the life cycle of civil litigation matters, from fact-finding and collection of evidence through to resolution, including legal research, preparation of chronologies, case summaries and first drafts of correspondence and court documents
  • organize and manage case files, document review and production, exhibits and evidence, and prepare court filings and hearing materials in accordance with procedural requirements
  • monitor and manage procedural deadlines, court dates and case calendars, and maintain accurate matter records and reporting in our case management systems
  • liaise with courts, conciliation authorities, outside counsel and internal stakeholders across functions, and support the coordination and cost monitoring of outside counsel
  • help shape a modern, collaborative and AI-enabled litigation practice across Switzerland, working with state-of-the-art legal technology and contributing to the continuous development of practical, efficient workflows

The team Global Litigation Switzerland is a specialist unit within UBS Group Legal, with hubs in Zürich, Geneva and Lugano. We are responsible for civil litigation, criminal and regulatory matters across all divisions in booking center Switzerland, often in a cross-border context. You will work as part of a multilingual team and collaborate closely with lawyers and colleagues across UBS on significant litigation matters.

Your skills and experience

  • a completed law degree (Bachelor or Master of Law), paralegal qualification or equivalent legal training
  • at least 3 years of experience supporting Swiss civil litigation, gained in a law firm, court or in-house legal department
  • practical knowledge of civil procedure, court processes and filing requirements
  • excellent organizational skills, a high degree of accuracy and the ability to manage multiple deadlines independently
  • native or near-native proficiency in the language of the relevant hub—German or French—combined with fluent English. Native or near-native proficiency in Italian is a strong asset, given the support provided to the Lugano hub.
  • clear communication, discretion in handling confidential information and a collaborative approach
  • enthusiasm for working with AI-enabled tools and legal technology, and an interest in helping develop practical, high-quality litigation workflows
