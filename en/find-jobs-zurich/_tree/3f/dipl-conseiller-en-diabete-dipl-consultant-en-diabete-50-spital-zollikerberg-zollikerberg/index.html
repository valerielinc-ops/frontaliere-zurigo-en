Dipl. Diabetes consultant / Dipl. Diabetes consultant 50%

They want to advise, empower and accompany people. Then look forward to a varied area of responsibility with a lot of room for manoeuvre with us:

  • Independent counselling and support of people with diabetes mellitus in the ambulatory and inpatient area
  • Individual therapy planning and promotion of self-management in close cooperation with patients
  • Care and training in the use of modern diabetes technologies (CGM, insulin pumps, hybrid closed loop systems)
  • Close interprofessional cooperation with doctors, nursing, nutritional advice and other specialist areas
  • Technical advice and training of employees
  • Active participation, further development and expansion of diabetes counselling and diabetological care quality.

Become part of the Department of Emergency and Acute Medicine at the Zollikerberg Hospital. Under one roof, we combine internal medicine, surgery, nephrology with dialysis center, the emergency department, the eye center, the medical ambulatorium, the day clinic and other specialized areas. The Clinic for Internal Medicine looks after more than 2,500 inpatients every year, the Clinic for Surgery over 1,500. With more than 6,000 emergency consultations and over 450 patients in the interdisciplinary intensive care unit, we offer a versatile and demanding medical work environment.
