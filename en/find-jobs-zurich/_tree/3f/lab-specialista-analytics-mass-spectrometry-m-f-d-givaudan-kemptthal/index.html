You will report directly to an experienced chemist and will gain experience in different GC-MS and LC-MS techniques. You will: Measure experiments by GC-MS and LC-MS, analyse the data and report the results Keep track of progress and deadlines Discuss and or explain results within the team and to internal customers Perform method development Work in and contribute to the development of laboratory digitization and automation Work on analytical instruments and perform maintenance Grow your experience in the field of fragrance research You have: Completed professional education in chemistry, biochemistry, or related fields with focus in mass spectrometry including practical experiences with GC-MS or LC-MS, ideally having a bachelor/master in analytical chemistry Experience with Thermo software, e.g. Xcalibur or Compound Discoverer is a plus
