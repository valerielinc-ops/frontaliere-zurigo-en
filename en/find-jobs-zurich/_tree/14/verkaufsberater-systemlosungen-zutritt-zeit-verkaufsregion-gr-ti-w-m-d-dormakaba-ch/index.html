Full description

Become part of our team As a global company that pursues very clear growth goals, we need employees who want to take themselves and the company forward. For our sales team in Switzerland we are looking for you immediately or by agreement as a sales consultant access and time recording in the sales region GR/TI (Graubünden and Ticino). In this versatile function, you manage your sales area independently, advise customers on our access and time recording solutions and develop suitable security concepts. With your commitment, you contribute to sustainable business development.

Your tasks

  • You work on your sales area independently and support existing customers and assigned projects.
  • You gain new clients and advise builders, general contractors, architects, planners and real estate administrations.
  • You develop security concepts, present suitable dormakaba solutions and create professional offers.
  • You will accompany sales opportunities up to technical, commercial and administrative conclusion.
  • You support our specialist partners in advising end customers and maintain long-term customer relationships.
  • You work closely with the sales department, other sales consultants and key account management.
  • You document your sales activities in the CRM, create reports and participate in sales promotions and events.

Your profile

  • You have completed a basic technical training and ideally trained in sales.
  • You bring experience in the system or project business and in the support of existing customers.
  • You know the cooperation with architects, general contractors and builders from practice.
  • You have technical understanding and can convey complex solutions in an understandable way.
  • You convince with customer orientation, negotiating skills and a professional appearance.
  • You work independently, entrepreneurially and structured and pursue your goals consistently.
  • You communicate safely in German and Italian and enjoy working with internal and external partners.

What we offer

  • Best prospects in an internationally successful company with a family corporate culture
  • A motivated and competent team that looks forward to your support
  • Versatile tasks in the distribution of innovative access and time recording solutions
  • Self-responsible work and close cooperation with specialist partners and internal specialists

Who we are For every place that matters. From automatic doors to cloud-based access management, as a global leader in the market for access solutions for schools, banks, airports, hospitals, hotels and many more, we enable safe and sustainable places where people can move seamlessly. With over 160 years of experience and a clear focus on excellence and innovation, we offer exciting opportunities to advance in an environment where you can truly make a difference. At dormakaba, you are encouraged to show initiative, develop your skills and shape your career together with 16,000 experts worldwide who set new standards in the industry every day.
