Full description

Introduction

Create the building technology of tomorrow with us.

Innovative building technology, sustainable solutions and the personal exchange with experts belong together for you? In this position, you bring your technical know-how wherever solutions are created: directly at specialist planners, engineering offices and our partners in Central Switzerland. As part of our sales team in Switzerland, you will accompany challenging projects at an early stage of planning, develop long-term customer relationships and ensure that the right Belimo solutions are taken into account right from the start.

Main responsibilities

  • Competent advice to specialist planners, engineering firms and other partners on technical issues relating to HVAC applications and building automation.
  • Support of construction projects already in the concept and planning phase as well as support in the development of suitable technical solutions.
  • Building and further developing long-term customer relationships and a strong network in Central Switzerland.
  • Implementation of product training, presentations and workshops for customers, partners and educational institutions.
  • Recognizing new projects, market potential and customer needs as well as bringing relevant insights into the further development of our business.
  • Close cooperation with the field service, the technical office and our product specialists.

Required expertise

  • Basic technical training in the field of building technology, HVAC or building automation, ideally supplemented by appropriate further training.
  • Several years of practical experience in building technology as well as a good understanding of technical applications and connections – ideally complemented by experience in the field of district heating.
  • Enjoy consulting and developing technical solutions together with customers and partners.
  • Communication strength and the ability to communicate complex technical relationships in an understandable and practical manner.
  • Independent and structured working method with a high degree of personal responsibility.
  • Very good knowledge of German and driving licence category B.

People at Belimo

Intelligent buildings are created by committed people. We bring talented people together and create an environment where they can succeed. At Belimo, respect, trust and mutual support across departmental boundaries help turn ideas quickly into practical solutions. Together we achieve more than we could ever achieve alone.

About Belimo

Belimo is the world market leader in the development, manufacture and sale of field devices for energy-efficient control of heating, ventilation and air conditioning systems. Flap drives, control valves, sensors and meters form our core business. Founded in 1975, the company has been listed on the Swiss Stock Exchange (SIX) since 1995.
