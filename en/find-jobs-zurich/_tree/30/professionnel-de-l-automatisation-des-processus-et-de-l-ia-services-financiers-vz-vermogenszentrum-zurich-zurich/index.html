Full description

VZ is Switzerland's leading independent financial services provider. We advise private individuals and companies on all matters relating to money, manage assets and offer attractive financial services. As we are growing rapidly, we are looking for additional support for our Corporate Development team at Paradeplatz in Zurich.

Responsibilities

  • You will become a CRM specialist and help shape its further development.
  • You automate and optimize processes with the help of code, AI and ML approaches.
  • You analyze business cases and specify processes, applications and interfaces.
  • You establish standards for development and implementation and monitor compliance.
  • As a subject-matter contact, you train and advise internal developers and configurators.

Requirements

  • Completed or part-time education at a university (of applied sciences).
  • Initial experience with CRM systems is an advantage, ideally with the BSI Customer Suite.
  • Enjoyment of understanding complex business processes and simplifying them in a practical way.
  • Programming skills (e.g. TypeScript, Python, SQL, APIs, automation).
  • Interest in AI, machine learning, RPA and data-driven solutions.
  • Ability to work independently, quick comprehension, a structured approach and a sense of responsibility.
  • Confident manner when dealing with business, IT and internal partners.
  • You can develop quickly and take on responsibility.

What we offer

  • Because we are growing strongly, many doors are open to you. Shape your path together with us.
  • You work in an uncomplicated and open environment.
  • Our satisfaction surveys regularly show that our employees rate VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
