Canton of Zurich Security Directorate Office for Military and Civil Protection Technical staff with flair on IT and network technology 100% Together, around 140 employees at the Office for Military and Civil Protection in Zurich, Birmensdorf and Andelfingen make an important contribution to the security of the population. The Civil Protection Department at the Training Centre in Andelfingen (AZA) is responsible for the training and further education of persons subject to protective service in the Canton of Zurich. The AZA has a versatile training infrastructure for one- or multi-day courses, operational exercises and seminars of all kinds. Our infrastructures are available to civil protection, fire brigades, police, medical services, the army and also civilian organizers.   • You actively participate in exciting digitalization projects of the Office
  • You are the first point of contact for technical questions and help employees and course participants quickly and easily your profile

  • Independent, solution- and customer-oriented working method
