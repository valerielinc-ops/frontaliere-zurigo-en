Full description

Burkhalter Technics AG is your reliable partner for sophisticated electrical engineering in buildings. Our comprehensive services start with consulting, ranging from the planning and execution of installations in large-scale and infrastructure projects to management. We are also a reliable partner for service and maintenance, modern telematics solutions, switchgear and building automation.

To complement our team, we are looking for a KNX service technician immediately or by agreement.

What moves you

  • Independent and early order planning and work preparation (AVOR)
  • Professional handling of service and maintenance contracts in compliance with the quality requirements and the recognized rules of technology
  • Parameterisation, commissioning and troubleshooting of KNX equipment
  • Procurement of materials according to specified guidelines
  • Careful reporting and documentation (service reports, adjustments, protocols)
  • Independent instruction, advice and support of our customers on site
  • Close cooperation with project managers and internal specialist offices

What you bring

  • Basic electrical installer training EFZ completed
  • Demonstrable experience in a similar function (KNX / Service)
  • Readiness for subject-specific training
  • High IT affinity and good knowledge with the common MS-Office programs
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Strong communication skills
  • High customer orientation and service mentality
  • Independent and team-capable working method
  • Flexible, resilient and communication-strong personality

What we offer you

  • Varied and responsible activities in an established team
  • Opportunity for professional and personal development
  • Further education in the areas of low current / CCTV are promoted and supported
  • Advanced 40 hours week
  • Flexibility through annual working hours
  • Opportunity to work part-time
  • Mind. 27 days holidays and 9 public holidays
  • Up to 100 additional free days with our temporary work model B-Flex
  • Above average remuneration
  • Quality work equipment and modern workwear
  • Employee discounts for hotels, leisure events and much more
  • Financial security by intra-corporate pension fund
