Full description

The club school is the meeting point to experience meaningful leisure activities, to discover new worlds and to strengthen body and mind. There is an attractive and wide range of languages, health and creativity to choose from – thanks to the support of the Migros culture percentage. Club School Migros is a brand of Miduca AG, which belongs to the Migros Group.

Luogo di lavoro: Club School Migros, Hofwiesenstrasse 350, 8050 Oerlikon

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Support, course sales and advice at the reception of customers of different cultures and age groups
  • Ensuring the interface with teachers, quality coaches and product management
  • Telephone and written exchanges with assigning agencies such as municipalities or asylum organisations
  • Administrative tasks such as ordering teaching materials, consumables and other required resources
  • Operational assistance in the organisation of events and coordination of infrastructural concerns

Requirements

  • Professional experience: in a comparable function, ideally in the education sector
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): in the commercial sector or in the retail trade
  • English (good knowledge): further knowledge of foreign languages is advantageous
  • Structured, independent and solution-oriented working method with a high level of service orientation
  • Established, communicative and friendly personality with assertiveness
  • Readiness for irregular missions (evening and Saturday services)
  • Serenity and a cool head in hectic situations

Cosa of friamo

  • Part of the Migros Group: As part of the Migros Group, you benefit from stable structures, diverse career opportunities and attractive benefits
  • Central location: Central locations with excellent public transport connections
  • Handy subscription: discounted business conditions for private mobile subscription
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Diverse training offer: Generous offer of free, internal training, free participation in selected courses (national languages and health courses) and financial participation in the total course offer.
  • Self-responsibility & freedom: Varied tasks with a high level of self-responsibility

Contatto

Application and contact Jean-Philippe Weiss Talent Acquisition Coordinator

Recruitment process

  • Submit application documents: For a complete application, we need your CV, certificates and diplomas. A photo in the CV and a letter of motivation are not necessary. We live equal opportunities for all applicants. We have a standardized recruitment process. If this does not work for you, mention it in your application dossier.
  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Delayed video interview: For a large number of suitable applications, a delayed video interview can optionally be conducted. As part of this video interview, the answers to previously recorded questions are recorded with the web or mobile camera. This gives us the opportunity to get to know more candidates within a short time in order to invite suitable people for the next round. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Test work: A trial day or half day gives the opportunity to get to know the future working environment and the team members better. And conversely, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week

Note for personnel service providers For this position, we only consider direct applications.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
