At the earliest possible time we are looking for you as a service technician ‧in elevators – Region Horgen, Rüschlikon and the surrounding area.

Your mission

After careful training, you take over your own service route and are responsible for the independent and high-quality maintenance of our elevator systems.

Your concrete contribution

  • Carrying out maintenance, service operations and repairs in accordance with specified quality and safety standards
  • Ensuring a safe working environment in accordance with all regulations and guidelines
  • Maintaining a professional exchange with customers, contact persons and end users
  • Responsibility for the safety and proper functioning of the allocated installations
  • Proper management of the site environment during work
  • Recognition and reporting of additional repair requirements incl. Coordination with local customers
  • Preparation of precise, timely service reports
  • Participation in Periodic Picetting Service / Disruption Service

Your added value

  • Electrical or mechanical training completed
  • Electrical and control experience
  • Strong craftsmanship and independent working methods
  • Very good (Swiss) knowledge of German (minor level C1)
  • Delight in customer contact and willingness to represent KONE professionally
  • Valid driving licence category B
  • Correct extract from criminal records
  • Residence in Switzerland (Swiss or foreign nationality with valid residence permit)
  • Your place of residence is at most 30 minutes (by car) from the city center of Horgen or Rüschlikon. This enables a balanced work-life balance and facilitates cooperation in the team.

Our offer for your future

  • Working in a globally networked company with international cooperation
  • Innovative, professional and collegial working environment – with a focus on the well-being of employees
  • Various further education opportunities (e.g. KONE Academy in Hanover) and active support for your professional development
  • Market-friendly compensation, a business car with 100% private use (Fr. 150.-/month), food allowance (Fr. 25th-/working day) (without receipt), at least 25 days of holidays as well as other attractive benefits such as a high-performance BVG and participation in exciting company events
  • Fair-Pay-certified: equal wages for women and men – transparent and audited
  • Commitment to sustainability: target CO2 neutrality by 2030

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
