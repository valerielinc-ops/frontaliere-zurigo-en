Full description

Good data creates the basis for better decisions. In the IT Analytics Solutions team, you develop and operate modern data solutions, source connections and data products on our central Azure-based data platform. Together with specialist departments, analysts and architects, you ensure that data can be used reliably, scalably and profitably. This is how you shape health

  • Conception, development and operation of data solutions (data products), source connections and data platform components based on Microsoft Azure, Azure Data Factory, Synapse and Microsoft Fabric
  • Analysis of professional requirements and translation of business issues into sustainable data solutions
  • Design and advancement of data architectures, data models and integration patterns for reporting, analytics and AI/machine learning applications
  • Responsibility for technical and professional data modeling from source to consumable data solution
  • Ensuring data quality, data availability and compliance with governance, data protection and compliance requirements
  • Further develop our platform standards, architecture principles and data governance guidelines
  • Optimization and automation of data product supply chains, deployment processes and operations
  • Close collaboration with data engineers, data analysts, architects and business stakeholders in an agile environment

This sets you apart

  • Higher specialist training in computer science, data engineering or comparable qualification
  • Several years of experience in building data platforms, data warehouses or analytical system landscapes
  • In-depth knowledge of data architecture, data modeling and relational database systems
  • Very good knowledge of SQL, Python and distributed data processing technologies such as Spark
  • Experience with Microsoft Azure, Synapse Analytics, Fabric or comparable cloud platforms
  • Understanding modern data concepts such as data products, data mesh, data governance and self-service analytics
  • Experience in automation and with CI/CD
  • Ability to communicate technical connections in an understandable manner and to accommodate business requirements in a structured manner
  • Experience in requirements engineering and in cooperation with departments and stakeholders
  • Understanding platforms, networks and IT architectures as well as their interaction within an enterprise landscape
  • Structured, analytical and solution-oriented working method with high personal responsibility
  • Negotiable knowledge of German and good knowledge of English
