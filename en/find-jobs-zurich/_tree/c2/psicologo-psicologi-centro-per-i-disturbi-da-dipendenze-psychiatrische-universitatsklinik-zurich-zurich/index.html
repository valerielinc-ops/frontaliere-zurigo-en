Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 80% For our Center for Dependency Diseases, we are looking for the reinforcement of our multi-professional treatment team at the Rheinau site on 1 January. January 2027 or by agreement a psychologist as maternity representation 80% by the end of May 2027. Subsequently, there is the possibility of permanent employment with a percentage of 60%. Their tasks

  • Independent assumption of the inpatient treatment of our patients under medical and psychological direction within a committed, interdisciplinary treatment team
  • Active networking of our centre as well as close cooperation with external specialists and institutions (including practitioners, social services, residential and care facilities, insurance providers, authorities and somatic specialist areas)
  • Conception and implementation of group therapies on different thematic areas of interest and need as well as responsibility for the quality assurance and evaluation of the psychotherapeutic offer of the station
  • Active participation in team reports and case discussions as well as contributing your own technical expertise
  • Participation in the internal psychiatric-psychotherapeutic training programme and, if interested, active participation in it

Your profile

  • Completed degree in psychology (master or equivalent degree)
  • Ongoing or completed psychotherapy training
  • Experience working with mentally ill people as well as within psychiatric-psychotherapeutic institutions
  • Experience in treating people with addictions is beneficial
  • Knowledge and/or training in the field of trauma follow-up disorders is desirable
  • Interest in research and scientific work beneficial

Our offer

  • Knowledge that advances: Extensive initial, further and further training offers
  • Strong in the team: appreciative cooperation and mutual support
  • More time for relaxation: at least 25 days of holiday and possibility for unpaid leave
  • Future provision with added value: 60% employer contribution to the pension fund
  • Sustainable travel: Participation in the ZVV-BonusPass or general subscription
  • Well taken care of in everyday work: discounted food offers or lunch checks
