The Clinic of Cardiology is characterized by a broad range of outpatient and stationary services, in which the interdisciplinary cooperation is lived on equal terms. If you want to work in a top medical environment where you are challenged and promoted, then we look forward to your application We offer you a versatile task in an advanced working environment characterized by high quality, independence, flexibility and a sense of responsibility. You have the opportunity to design your workflows goal-oriented and independently in a dynamic environment. A motivated and committed team supports you actively in your function in all respects.

Your contribution makes the difference

  • Co-responsibility for cost securing in the clinic
  • Depository management Calculation of estimates
  • Ensuring full, timely and correct performance measurement in the stationary and ambulatory areas
  • Overall responsible performance controlling of the clinic
  • Patient management and complaint handling
  • Interface and contact person (by telephone and in writing) for insurers, patients: inside and other third parties
  • Assistance and representation in the Directorate Secretariat
  • Collaboration in projects and process optimization for the further development of the clinic

This sets you apart

  • Completed basic commercial training with first professional experience or ongoing business training (HF/BSc)
  • Preferably with professional experience in a similar function, ideally in the health care or hospital
  • Structured and implementation-oriented working method
  • Resilient, flexible and reliable personality
  • Team-capable and discreet personality with good manners
  • Organizational talent with networked thinking
  • Very good PC and MS Office skills (especially Excel, PowerPoint) are required
  • Experience with SAP, Polypoint PEP and KISIM is of great advantage
  • Very good German and English skills
