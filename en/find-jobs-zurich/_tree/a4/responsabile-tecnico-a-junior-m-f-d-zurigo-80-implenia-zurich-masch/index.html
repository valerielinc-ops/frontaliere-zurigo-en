Full description

As a leading real estate management service provider for demanding residential, office and commercial space, we offer our customers comprehensive services around the property.

Technology, real estate and responsibility are exactly your thing? Then become part of our team and shape the reliable and sustainable operation of our properties with us.

To complement our team in Zurich, we are looking for a motivated and committed personality as

Junior Technical Director (m/f/d)

Zurich, 80 %

This is what moves you

  • You support the technical management in the management of the FM provider and its partner companies in technical and infrastructural building management. For smaller mandates, you take on this task independently
  • You are the contact person for tenants in technical and technical issues and provide a professional solution
  • You are actively involved in exciting construction and renovation projects, support the client trust in the implementation and control minor technical measures independently – from the idea to the conclusion
  • You control and monitor technical and operational contracts, service level agreements and orders and support in the enforcement of warranty work
  • You participate in the planning, organization and monitoring of construction activities and coordinate with internal and external stakeholders
  • You support FM submissions and the implementation of services for the allocated properties
  • You will help plan and monitor budgets for technical and infrastructural building management as well as multiannual planning
  • You review analyses of energy and optimization potential and support economic considerations for sustainability projects
  • You keep an eye on security-relevant topics and participate in the implementation of security and evacuation concepts
  • With your own ideas and their implementation, you actively contribute to improving our processes, standards and services

This is why you succeed

This function is aimed at young professionals with a technical or commercial background who want to develop further in technical building management and have the following profile:

  • You have a basic technical or commercial education. Further education at university level, for example in facility management or real estate management, is an advantage
  • First professional experience in a comparable function, in facility management, in the real estate industry or in the project environment is an advantage
  • You are well versed in dealing with the common Microsoft Office applications and ideally bring your first knowledge of project management
  • You bring your own initiative and work independently, committed and solution-oriented
  • Thanks to your planning and organizational strength, you can keep an overview even in challenging situations
  • You communicate safely, act binding and can assert yourself against different stakeholders
  • You think networked, act entrepreneurially and enjoy understanding technical and infrastructural connections
  • You have stylish knowledge of German
  • Knowledge of English is an advantage.

Here are some of our benefits

Working hours: We live mobile work with individual (year) working hours and/or part-time work. Up to 40% home office is possible. Work-life balance is a top priority for us, because balanced and relaxed employees are important to us. The purchase of additional holidays is possible.

Mobility and food: You will receive a contribution for the mobile phone. For your food, we also make a contribution.

Education and training: Promoting employees is an important part of our culture. Benefit from generous support in internal and external education and training. We offer career opportunities in management, specialist and project functions.

Salär: With us you can expect a market-based remuneration with absolute equal pay for all genders. The pension benefits are attractive and you can choose the right one for you from different models. The accident protection insurance covers the private hospital department.

More information about our benefits can be found on our career page.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
