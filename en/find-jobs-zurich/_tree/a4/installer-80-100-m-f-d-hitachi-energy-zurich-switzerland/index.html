Full description

About the position If you have technical understanding, enjoy working in a team and feel like shaping the future together with us, you are in the right place. As an assembler, you work on the production of our feedthroughs and take on a wide range of assembly and production tasks. With your careful and reliable way of working, you make an important contribution to quality, safety and a smooth production process.

You can do that.

  • Carrying out assembly work on bushings in accordance with orders, regulations, instructions, drawings and measurement sheets
  • Operation of machine tools and chemical processing, assembly and packaging equipment
  • Careful execution of all relevant production and work steps as well as self-responsible work in the team
  • Movement, packaging and sorting of raw materials and finished products
  • Implementation of quality controls and documentation of production and process values and accurate daily production records
  • Compliance with all safety, quality and environmental regulations as well as ensuring order and cleanliness in the workplace
  • Active participation in improvements in the own department as well as situational and independent action in the event of problems
  • Readiness to work in 3-shift operation

You bring that with you

  • Advantageous technical training and basic knowledge of workflows and tools, ideally acquired through appropriate professional experience
  • High safety and quality awareness as well as a careful, reliable and performance-ready working method
  • Team spirit and joy of working with others to achieve common goals
  • Motivation to learn new things and develop professionally and personally
  • Experience in handling drawbar, crane or lifting platform is advantageous
  • Responsibility, flexibility and willingness to work in 3-shift operation

More about us Hitachi Energy is shaping the energy future with innovative technologies for a sustainable and reliable energy supply. With us, you can expect a modern working environment, a strong team and the opportunity to develop professionally and personally.
