Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We advise individuals and companies about money, manage assets and offer attractive financial services. We are very successful and growing strongly.

  • You will complete an external base semester (Bbc) in the first half of the year. From the second semester you will work at our headquarters in the center of Zurich.
  • One or two days a week you attend vocational school. On the other days, you work with experienced colleagues and learn everything you need for everyday work.
  • As part of the IT support team, you support users in dealing with ICT resources.
  • Thanks to your support, PC's, tablets and mobile phone work fine.
  • You integrate peripherals into the network infrastructure and repair disruptions.
  • Complex issues do not cause you a headache, proactively you find solutions for the subject-specific challenges together with the team
  • During your apprenticeship, you will be accompanied by experienced trainers.
  • Good performance in secondary school

  • Generous participation in language stays and language diplomas (English and French)
  • Many events to expand your network
  • Free cold drinks, coffee, tea and fresh fruits
  • Even glacé in summer
  • Very good takeover opportunities after graduation
