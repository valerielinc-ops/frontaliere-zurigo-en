For this versatile and responsible position, we are looking for a committed personality with heart, organizational talent and joy in leadership and cooperation. Here they engage

  • in support of projects of the hospital and medical departments
  • in the administrative management of Central Bed Management
  • during all personnel and qualification interviews and during the recruitment process
  • as contact person for the Clinic Information System (KISIM)
  • Optimization of processes
  • in the management/organisation of MPA training
  • have health education with business or management training or equivalent qualifications
  • have several years of professional and managerial experience in health care or in a comparable organisation
  • have a stylish German in word and writing
  • have communication strength, discretion and a structured and accurate working method
  • enjoy working independently and in contact with internal and external bodies
  • have a high sense of responsibility and a great service orientation
  • have very good knowledge of MS Office applications as well as PEP, teams and KISIM knowledge
  • have a high degree of identification and passion for work, flexible and supportive working methods with the willingness to help out with basic tasks
  • An exciting, central task with a large scope for action within the operational department with a broad network of activities within the hospital

  • Working in a dynamic and future-oriented hospital
