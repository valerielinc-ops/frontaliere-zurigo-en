Full description

As the first point of contact for the concerns of the people of Zurich, you are the voice of ERZ. With your communication strength, you advise our customers competently on our services, answer questions and ensure reliable handling of their concerns. This makes an important contribution to a customer-oriented service for the city of Zurich. We are looking for reinforcement at the Hagenholz site in Zurich Oerlikon from now on.

Ready for a job with meaning?

  • You ensure that the main call number of ERZ Entsorgung + Recycling Zurich can be reached during the fixed office hours (07:00 – 17:00, daily shift 8h 24min).
  • You are responsible for competent information and advice about the services of ERZ as well as the sale of disposal services by telephone and via e-mails.
  • You collect and maintain customer and order data (e.g. bulky goods retrieval, garden bag orders or the like) and carry out written correspondence.
  • You provide invoice information about already created invoices, send invoice copies and forward adjustment orders internally.
  • You manage Züri Wie Neu (ZWN), population reports and forward orders internally.
  • Communicative, team-capable personality with pleasure in contact with customers
  • Client and service-oriented working methods
  • Commercial basic education (level EBA or EFZ), SAP knowledge of advantages
  • Several years of professional experience in a call center, telephone sales or similar activity
  • German language skills Level C2 as well as foreign language skills English, French, Italian or Spanish
  • Generous mobility contribution
  • Food allowance
  • Equal pay
  • Above-average social benefits
  • Careful training in your work
  • A modern, down-to-earth and future-oriented working environment
  • Maternity leave: 16 weeks paid
  • Paternity leave: 20 paid days
  • Three days of care for your sick child without a holiday deduction
  • Development opportunities through urban education
  • ERZ Sportclub

With around 1,000 employees, ERZ is one of the largest service departments in the city of Zurich and operates in an exciting environment at the pulse of time. We are modern, down-to-earth, open-minded, all per you and want to make a difference. Together we are committed to a clean, innovative and sustainable Zurich.

ERZ stands for diversity and mixed teams.
