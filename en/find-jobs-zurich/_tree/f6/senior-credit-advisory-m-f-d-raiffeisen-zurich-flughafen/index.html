Full description

16.11.2026 Zurich, The Circle

Become part of our team!

The Corporate Credit Analysis department forms a competence unit in the management of corporate clients within the Corporate Clients & Trade department. To strengthen our team at the Zurich Airport (The Circle) site, we are looking for a committed, reliable and team-oriented personality immediately or by agreement.

What awaits you?

  • Comprehensive business analysis (including financial analysis, rating preparation and market analysis)
  • Development and assessment of complex financing structures within the framework of, among others, LBO/MBO financing
  • Participation in syndicated loans in the Swiss market and contact for mandated lead arrangers of large Swiss banks
  • Support and representation of key account managers in the support of large multinational Swiss companies (corporate banking)
  • Competent contact for customers, lead banks and internal stakeholders (including legal, credit risk management and various product specialists)

What do you bring?

  • Completed higher education (FH, Uni) in economics
  • Several years of experience in the balance sheet analysis of corporate clients (front or credit risk management)
  • Dealing with different stakeholders
  • Engaged, team-oriented and able to work independently and reliably
  • Negotiation-proof knowledge of German (company language is German)
