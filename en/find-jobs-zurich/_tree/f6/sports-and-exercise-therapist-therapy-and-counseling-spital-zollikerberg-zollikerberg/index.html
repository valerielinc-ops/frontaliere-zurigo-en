Sports and movement therapist / Sports and movement therapist Therapy and counselling – Spital Zollikerberg, Zollikerberg (ZH).

Welcome to your new place of work.

Petar Pavlovic, group leader physiotherapy, and the team offer you by 1. January 2027 or by appointment a job with a percentage of 60% as

Sports and movement therapist / Sports and movement therapist

If you too want to make a difference in people’s lives, these are your tasks:

  • Care of our customers on the training area and in the therapy bath
  • Creating training plans and carrying out training controls and assessments
  • Independent performance of group therapies and sports groups
  • Support of our physiotherapy team in inpatient and outpatient settings such as outpatient pulmonary and cardiac rehabilitation, inpatient acute care medicine and other exciting fields of activity

Become part of the Department of Care Expertise, Therapies and Counselling at the Zollikerberg Hospital. At the Spital Zollikerberg Therapy Center, we bring together various therapeutic disciplines and a wide range of services under one roof. With high-quality care oriented to the individual needs of our patients, we make an important contribution to health care in the region.

In our 360° tour, get a first impression of your new place of work.

To make you feel comfortable with us, many benefits are waiting for you. Just to name a few:

  • Own pension fund with super-compulsory participation of the employer
  • Worldwide private insurance in case of accident
  • Free use of the fitness room on the Zollikerberg site
  • Subsidized crèche solution and a family atmosphere
  • Financial support to public transport subscription or bicycle
  • Modern and well-equipped therapy rooms

  • Completed studies as Bachelor or Master in Sports Sciences / Movement Sciences
  • First professional experience of 1 to 2 years
  • Additional qualification as cardiac therapist / cardiac therapist SCPRS and advantageous further training as pulmonary rehabilitation therapist / pulmonary rehabilitation therapist
  • Willingness to take on evening and weekend services
  • We wish for our team a friendly, committed and reliable personality
  • Our and your values: appreciation, partnership, commitment, transparency and seeing the whole
