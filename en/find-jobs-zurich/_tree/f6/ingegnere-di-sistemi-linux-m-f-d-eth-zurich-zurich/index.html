Linux System Engineer: in (m/f/d)

80%-100%, Zurich, permanent

The IT Service Group (ISGINF) acts as an internal IT service provider of the Department of Computer Science at ETH Zurich, which has established itself as one of the world's leading academic institutions in the field of computer science through excellent research and teaching. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a:n Linux System Engineer:in (80%-100%) immediately or by agreement.

Our nine-member team supervises the department’s own IT infrastructure, which is specifically tailored to the requirements of the professorships and research groups. Together with the researchers, we are continuously developing our systems and applications, relying on the latest technologies.

Job description

You take on a varied activity with a high level of personal responsibility and actively shape innovative solutions. The range of tasks is broad:

  • Work in the server room, including installation and wiring of servers
  • Classic system administration: installation of computers, configuration management, error analysis and updates
  • Development, maintenance and operation of specific solutions
  • Programming tools, interfaces and web applications with the help of AI
  • Operation of our Kubernetes clusters and provision of applications
  • Collaboration with researchers on projects and IT problems
  • Integration of AI into existing and new processes
  • Collaboration on our Ansible-based Linux infrastructure
  • Regular use on the service desk

Your first concrete task will be to take over and rebuild our monitoring system based on Icinga.

Profile

  • Completed training in computer science or in a related field
  • In-depth knowledge of Linux system administration, scripting languages and programming
  • Practical experience in the design and implementation of IT solutions
  • Independent and structured working methods, resilience and a high level of initiative
  • Teamwork and a communicative personality
  • Readiness for continuous training
  • Good knowledge of German and English for dealing with our customers

We offer

Our department is the ideal place for IT specialists: women who want to start in a versatile and experienced team. We work with dedicated system administrators who cover the entire spectrum – from classic server management and cloud solutions to the development of applications and the use of AI for programming. In direct contact with internationally leading researchers, you will get exciting insights into current projects.

We promote your professional and personal development through a wide range of internal and external training opportunities. Flexible working conditions and attractive social benefits facilitate the reconciliation of work and private life. In addition, you benefit from an inspiring working environment on the ETH campus with excellent infrastructure and a lively IT community.

If you enjoy learning new things, taking on responsibility and actively shaping the IT landscape in a world-leading computer science department, we look forward to your application.
