Specialist Operations & Projects (60)

Zurich | Up to 40% working from home possible | Reference 8431

Are you looking for an exciting challenge in a dynamic working environment? As an independent body, SIX Exchange Regulation AG (SER) ensures the independent regulation and supervision of SIX's exchanges. In the Operations & Projects team, you will work as a Specialist Operations & Projects (60% workload) in a small team at the interface between business and IT. As a self-starter with a quick grasp and a structured, goal-oriented approach to work, you independently handle topics in Operations and IT. Thanks to your experience and strong organizational skills combined with systems thinking, you navigate a constantly changing environment with confidence and are not afraid to get hands-on in projects. We offer you the opportunity to play an active role in shaping a dynamic environment and to continue driving standardization/digitalization forward.

Your responsibilities

  • Support and advise employees on Operations and IT matters in day-to-day business
  • Represent the business interests vis-à-vis central IT and other internal departments
  • Gather and document business requirements in close collaboration with the specialist teams and support them through to successful implementation
  • Impact analyses of changes and new developments affecting business processes and applications
  • Identify and implement opportunities for optimization

What you bring

  • Training in business informatics, organization, business administration, or equivalent training
  • 2-3 years of experience in requirements engineering in the financial sector, preferably in a regulated environment
  • Practical experience in an agile delivery model (SAFe, Scrum, Kanban).
  • Quick comprehension, analytical and conceptual thinking, as well as an independent, reliable and precise way of working
  • Fluent German and good English skills
  • Flexibility and willingness to adapt working days to operational needs as required
