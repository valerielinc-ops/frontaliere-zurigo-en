Your mission begins before the siren sounds. SIBEs with firefighting background and design will, get in touch!
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: Fire protection: • Contact person for operational fire protection issues • monitoring compliance with laws, regulations and instructions; Detecting vulnerabilities and proposing measures to address them • Implementation of fire protection control tours in all buildings incl. Documentation • Accompanying fire controls and corrective actions • Administration and implementation of firefighting courses to train staff on fire behaviour • Assumption of further fire protection tasks after consultation with the SiBe fire protection team • Emergency exercise support • Close cooperation with the client representative for fire protection and the technical fire protection specialist Fire brigade: • Cadre member of the company fire brigade • Material warden Requirements • Completed training EGZ or equivalent • Safety Officer:r for fire protection with certificate • Further embodiments in fire protection are advantageous • Several years of professional experience in fire protection and in-depth knowledge of the VKF Directive • Experience with periodic controls is beneficial • Firefighting experience and willingness to participate in the company fire department • Cadre experience in a fire brigade is advantageous • Responsible, negotiating personality with the ability to quickly grasp complex relationships in a networked environment and act as a link between internal and external stakeholders • Strong organisational capacity and a structured and independent working method • Team capable and service-oriented personality with high quality understanding, good communication skills and assertiveness • Good oral and written language in German, English skills are advantageous We offer: With us, you continue to develop professionally. We specifically promote your education and training in the field of fire protection and offer you the opportunity to continuously expand and deepen your knowledge in a demanding hospital environment. At the same time, you can expect a committed team, cross-divisional cooperation, modern working conditions and plenty of space for initiative. You take on a visible and responsible role with a lot of creative leeway, actively contribute your fire department experience and contribute to the further development of fire protection on one of the largest hospital areas in Switzerland. The combination of prevention, training, consulting and fire service makes this function particularly versatile and offers you the opportunity to achieve daily effects.
