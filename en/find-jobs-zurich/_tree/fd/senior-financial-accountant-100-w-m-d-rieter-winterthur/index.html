Full description

Rieter is the world’s leading system supplier for the processing of natural and man-made fibres into short and long staple yarns and for the production of filament yarns, man-made fibres and nonwovens. With its comprehensive technology portfolio, Rieter covers the entire textile value chain – from fiber to yarn as well as from melt to textured filament, synthetic staple fiber and nonwoven fabric. Headquartered in Winterthur (Switzerland), the company develops and manufactures complex spinning plants, machines, systems and components for efficient and economical production. Rieter’s leading technologies enable a minimal use of resources and thus make a significant contribution to sustainability along the global textile value chain. Rieter has existed for more than 230 years, has 22 production sites in nine countries and employs around 6,400 people worldwide, of which around 9% are in Switzerland. Rieter is listed on the SIX Swiss Exchange under the ticker symbol RIEN.

In the central financial organization of Rieter AG in Winterthur, you keep the accounts of selected Swiss companies of the Rieter Group and ensure accurate, timely and compliant financial reporting. You keep the ledger, prepare the monthly and annual accounts, take responsibility for Group reporting according to IFRS and take care of VAT. In addition, you actively shape the unification of processes and systems.

Your main tasks and responsibilities

  • General ledger management: Responsibility for managing, developing and optimising General ledger processes, including account reconciliation and closing activities.
  • Sub-books: Support and ensure efficient operation of sub-books (creditors and debtors).
  • Financial accounts: Active participation and co-responsibility in the preparation of the monthly and annual accounts.
  • Group reporting: Active participation in Group reporting in accordance with IFRS.
  • VAT: Responsibility for the correct and timely preparation of VAT bills and ensuring compliance with all relevant VAT regulations.
  • Project participation: participation in financial and system projects, such as: the unification of processes and accounting systems.
  • Deputy: Assuming deputy duties within the accounting team as required.
  • Working with stakeholders: Working closely with internal and external stakeholders, including controlling, sales and external auditors, to ensure transparent and compliant financial reporting.
  • Compliance and controls: Ensuring compliance with internal guidelines and all relevant legal requirements.

Your qualifications

  • Swiss Financial Accountant or equivalent qualification.
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in accounting.
  • Very good knowledge of MS Excel and SAP (FI/CO).
  • In-depth knowledge of Swiss VAT law as well as practical experience in the preparation and auditing of VAT bills.
  • High availability and flexibility during the end of the month (1st to 7th working day of the month).
  • Deep understanding of business relationships and good analytical skills.
  • Excellent knowledge of German, reliable knowledge of English.
  • Independent, motivated and flexible personality with a high level of personal responsibility.
  • Strong IT affinity and openness to new technologies and processes.
