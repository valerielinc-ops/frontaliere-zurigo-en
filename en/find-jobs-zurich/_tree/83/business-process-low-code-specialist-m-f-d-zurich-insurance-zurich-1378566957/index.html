Full description

138633 | 70 - 100% | Oerlikon, Zurich | Hybrid

Do you want to simplify recurring manual tasks through smart automation? Do you like to develop concrete solutions for everyday work challenges – pragmatically and without waiting for months for budget and prioritization decisions?

In this role, you are part of the Financial Lines division in the team Proposition & Process Management and actively shape the further development of Zurich Switzerland. As a Digital Process & Low-Code Specialist, you recognize inefficient processes together with our specialists and develop fast, tangible solutions. With your process understanding and technical affinity, you actively contribute to the transformation of the Financial Lines area and help the teams to focus on the essentials. You see problems as an opportunity to improve something and are experimental. In addition, you act as a bridge builder between business and IT and like to take things into your own hands.

This is what awaits you

  • You analyze offers and processes and independently develop pragmatic solutions with low-code, especially with the Microsoft Power Platform and Robotic Process Automation
  • Develop AI prompts and integrate them into low-code applications
  • You access and structure required data and support the underwriting financial lines with analysis and reports – for better portfolio management
  • You work closely with departments, IT and data teams to understand requirements, test and develop solutions
  • You help shape the system landscape and support the technical transformation of the business

This sets you apart

  • Completed degree in Business Informatics, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning or a comparable discipline
  • Experience with low-code solutions, ideally with Microsoft Power Apps, Power Automate and Power BI
  • Understand basic data structures and enjoy making data usable
  • Insurance skills are beneficial
  • Analytical, structured and solution-oriented working methods as well as interest in digitalization and new technologies
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Other national languages are beneficial
