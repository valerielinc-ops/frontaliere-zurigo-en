Full description

Together with our customers, we connect the real and the digital world. As one of the leading technology companies, Siemens employs around 6,000 people in Switzerland. With our pioneering solutions in the areas of building technology, energy, mobility, health and industry, we are shaping the future. Do you also want to actively participate in the pulse of time and make the lives of millions of people in Switzerland better? Then you are exactly right with us! Apply as a project engineer today: in low-voltage switchgear and start immediately or after your availability – your team is looking forward to you! Your new tasks

  • You design low-voltage switchgear (SIVACON S8) and develop technically and economically optimal solutions for our customers.
  • You translate customer requirements, specifications and tender documents into project documentation, technical documentation and production documents.
  • You perform technical calculations and dimensioning and support network calculations with SIMARIS Design.
  • You clarify technical interfaces to switching device combinations, power rails, network replacement systems, UPS systems and electrical installations.
  • You work closely with sales, project management, production and suppliers and ensure the timely technical processing of your projects.
  • You accompany projects from order clearance through execution to acceptance and support with technical queries from customers and project partners. Your talents and experiences
  • You have completed a vocational apprenticeship as an electrical installer, automatic technician or a comparable basic electrotechnical education.
  • In addition, you have a further training in electrical engineering or electrical energy technology at the level FH/HF/HFP or are in training.
  • You have experience in the planning or project planning of low-voltage switchgear, energy distribution systems or comparable electrical systems.
  • Experience with engineering and planning tools such as EPLAN, AutoCAD or similar is an advantage.
  • You work independently, structured and quality-conscious and keep an overview of several parallel tasks.
  • You can master German in words and writing. Good knowledge of English and other languages are beneficial. Your advantages
  • 2–3 days/week mobile work is standard
  • Discount public transport subscription and free parking
  • Diverse training opportunities
  • Access to employee stock plans

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
