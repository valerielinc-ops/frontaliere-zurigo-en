Canton of Zurich Economic Directorate Office for Labour Internal Auditor 80 - 100% In the interest of employees and companies, the Office of Labour (AFA) is helping to strengthen Zurich as a location. The AFA bundles labour market policy competences. Around 800 employees work at more than 20 locations. Together with external partners, the Qualification for Job Seekers (QuS) area organises a range of labour market measures (AMM) tailored to the needs of the labour market. These support the reintegration of unemployed people into the labour market. We are looking for competent support for this exciting function from now on or after agreement. Their main tasks include:

  • Ensuring the correct implementation of SECO’s instructions, especially with regard to financial aspects
  • The further development of the financial guidelines of the Canton of Zurich for education and employment measures with a price for measures and the implementation of these at the Offering Institutions (AIN)
  • The preparation of annual statements and documents for the AIN
  • The independent implementation of the audit as well as the organisation and support of the process with external audit, the issue of management letters with the AIN and the further development of the audit processes of AMM
  • Examination of investment applications of AIN as well as the budget review and acceptance of AMM, which are financed according to the action price
  • The establishment and implementation of risk management at QuS in cooperation with the Quality Manager
  • Process responsibility for risk management and continuous development of the same
  • Responsibility for IKS within QuS and as a contact person at QuS regarding IKS AFA Your profile:
  • Bachelor's degree in business administration
  • Several years of professional experience in a comparable position / auditing
  • Good performance and social skills and communication skills
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • High quality awareness both conceptually and hands-on
  • Stylistic German We offer:
  • meaningful, interesting and demanding activity with a high level of personal responsibility
  • well-developed social benefits under cantonal personnel law
  • Contribution to catering costs (lunch check)
  • Place of work only a few minutes walk from Zurich Central Station Have we aroused your interest? For additional information please contact Nicolas Schulthess (Department Manager QuS) Tel. +41 43 257 64 05, nicolas.schulthess@vd.zh.ch . We look forward to your online application. You can find out more about us and our employees at www.zh.ch/afa.

Pensum: 80-100 %
