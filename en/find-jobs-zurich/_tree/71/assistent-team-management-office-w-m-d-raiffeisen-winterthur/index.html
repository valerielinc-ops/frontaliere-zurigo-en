25.11.2026 Winterthur

Become part of our team!

Raiffeisenbank Winterthur stands for strong regional roots and a cooperative philosophy. This makes us an important financial partner in the Winterthur region. We accompany our customers in all walks of life and rely on personal advice, entrepreneurial thinking and real closeness. The Team Management Office is the organizational hub of our bank. We support the management, executives and departments in a wide range of tasks and ensure that everything goes smoothly behind the scenes. Our topics include marketing & communication, events, sponsoring, human resources, leadership and sales support and project work. Together, we ensure that our employees, customers and executives are optimally supported and that our bank acts professionally both internally and externally. As part of a succession plan within our team, we are looking for a committed personality who likes to be organized, networked and involved.

What awaits you?

  • HR assistance around entry and exit, employee events and the maintenance of lists and master data
  • Management and sales support for bank management and departments by creating presentations, reporting and coordinating administrative tasks and deadlines
  • Support for marketing campaigns, communication measures and sponsorship requests
  • Participation in the organisation and implementation of internal and external events
  • Collaboration in cross-sectoral projects

What do you bring?

  • Commercial education or similar training completed
  • Open, helpful and committed personality with organizational skills
  • Independent, reliable and structured working method
  • High service and team orientation
  • Safe handling of Microsoft 365

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
