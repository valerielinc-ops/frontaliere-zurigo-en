Additional information

The research group Fruit Production in the Alpine Region deals with the development of innovative and sustainable production and storage systems for fruit. The main research topics are (i) the breeding of new apricot and pear varieties to improve their resistance to diseases and pests, fruit quality and productivity, (ii) the development of pest and disease control methods to reduce the use of pesticides, and (iii) the determination of the factors influencing fruit quality before and after harvest to reduce the effects of climate change.
