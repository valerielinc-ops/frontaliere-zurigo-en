  • This exciting function includes the independent execution management / construction management of civil engineering and track conversion work, including the project-related management of internal and external employees at construction sites with third parties.
  • You provide technical advice to the project management at early stages and help determine the construction process for the plant parts processed by the specialist engineer. This includes the preparation of the construction phase planning (AVOR 1).
  • As a construction manager: in / specialist construction manager: in you continuously monitor the quality and safety of the work on the construction site and the services in the specialist area of roadway in terms of dates and costs within the framework of the assigned competences and responsibility.
  • You support in the planning, implementation and logging of partial acceptances and take over the task of construction management according to SIA 103.
  • The extent, government reports and business invoices are examined, assessed and adjusted by you, as are the supplements of contracted entrepreneurs.

You bring that with you.

  • Strong communication skills, networked thinking, negotiating skills, resilience are among your strengths. You have a great awareness of security and describe yourself as a team player: with an independent and reliable way of working.
  • You have experience in the railway technology track sector with focus on execution as construction site manager:in, construction manager:in, polisher:in, or have equivalent professional experience.
  • You are flexible for sporadic night and weekend assignments within the framework of ongoing construction sites.
  • A sworn certificate of competence at the level of foremen:in/polisher:in, construction manager:in/construction manager:in(HFP), technician:in (HF) or equivalent training is required. A valid security card SC or SL is an advantage.
  • You have very good knowledge of German (at least C2) and knowledge of a second national language is an advantage. We also require good IT user knowledge.
