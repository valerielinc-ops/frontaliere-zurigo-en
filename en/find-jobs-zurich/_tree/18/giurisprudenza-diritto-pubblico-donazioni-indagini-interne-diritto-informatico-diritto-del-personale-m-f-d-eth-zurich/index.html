Legal Counsel - Public law, donations, internal investigations, IT law, personnel law (m/f/d)

100%, Zurich, perpetual

ETH Zurich stands for excellent teaching, groundbreaking basic research and the application of the results for the benefit of society. Founded in 1855, it is today one of the world's best technical science universities.

The legal service of ETH Zurich advises the school management, the central administration, the departments and other units of ETH Zurich on matters of public law and private law – independently, solution-oriented and with a view to the interests of ETH Zurich as an institution.

In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a legal counsel at the beginning of 2027 or by agreement with:n Legal Counsel with a focus on: public law, donations, internal investigations, IT law, personnel law (m/f/d) with a 100% effort.

Job description

Do you want to be legally broad and make a tangible impact with your work in one of the world's leading universities at the interface of cutting-edge research, teaching, university organization and society? Then this place could be created as for you. As a legal counsel, you independently manage a versatile portfolio of topics, quickly work your way into new areas of law and become a sought-after contact in several areas. Its tasks include in particular the following activities:

  • They process information access requests under the Public Law (BGÖ) – often on topics of high public and media interest – and accompany the associated arbitration and complaint procedures.
  • They examine and negotiate contracts for strategic partnerships and funding of research programmes as well as infrastructure and teaching projects.
  • They advise on substantive and procedural issues within the framework of investigations on suspicion of scientific misconduct and further internal investigations – legally sound and with the necessary sensitivity.
  • They examine and shape regulations, instructions, rules of procedure and contracts, in particular with regard to IT law, and advise on legal issues relating to AI, high-performance computing, data and copyright, hardware and software.
  • They advise executives and HR departments on matters of federal personnel law (BPG, PVO-ETH) and supervise personnel law procedures.

Profile

  • Completed law degree and Swiss attorney’s patent or comparable qualification from Germany (Second State Exam for Law) or Austria (Lawyer’s Exam).
  • Professional experience of several years – if possible partially acquired in Switzerland – e.g. in a law firm, at a court, in public administration or in the private sector, ideally in at least two of the above-mentioned areas of responsibility or in related areas.
  • In-depth knowledge of (federal) administrative law, administrative procedural law (VwVG) and general contract law.
  • interest in IT and AI law
  • Relevant experience (e.g. legal tech, data centers, hardware and software) is a plus.
  • Curiosity and the willingness to familiarize yourself quickly and in-depth with new areas of law and also to look after topics outside your own field of activity.
  • Interested in participating in an internationally established, leading university in the creation of new things and to legally accompany them.
  • Rapid understanding of complex organizational, institutional and political contexts with a view to the interests of the entire organization.
  • Ability to work quickly and pragmatically and yet extremely carefully, to understand complex legal situations and to develop practical solutions.
  • Responsibility, sense of priorities and sovereignty in the parallel handling of several dossiers.
  • Clear, user-friendly communication with different stakeholders, negotiating skills and diplomatic behaviour, even in challenging situations.
  • Committed and collegial personality with a strong team spirit.
  • Excellent knowledge of German (mother tongue or equivalent) and reliable knowledge of English in speech and writing
  • Knowledge of French is beneficial.
