Central computer science

Job opening 1.1.2027

Are you interested in working with us to make the digital future of the University of Zurich accessible? As a lead software engineer (senior) in the area of accessibility, you are actively involved in the technical implementation and further development of digital accessibility at UZH. You develop standards and best practices for accessible software development, support development and project teams in their implementation and at the same time participate in software development projects. You work closely with developers, UX designers, test managers, project managers and other specialists.

Tasks

  • Development of UZH-wide standards and quality assurance for accessible software development
  • Development of standards and best practices for accessible software development
  • Anchoring standards in design patterns, blueprints, templates and development processes
  • Evaluation of tools, technologies and AI-supported approaches regarding accessibility
  • Integration and automation of accessibility checks during development and in CI/CD pipelines

Accessibility Reviews & Audits

  • In cooperation with other accessibility specialists, you will conduct manual, automated and AI-supported accessibility tests
  • Evaluate WCAG compliance applications and document and prioritize findings and actions
  • You will follow the test and examinations.

Advice & knowledge transfer

  • You will advise project and development teams on the accessible implementation according to WCAG and contribute to design and architecture decisions
  • Developers, UX designers and test managers support you through coaching, knowledge transfer as well as technical workshops and training

Software development

  • You work on the development and further development of applications of Central Computer Science, in particular on web applications and system-related scripts
  • You design and implement functions and technical adjustments, analyze errors and further develop existing applications
  • Tests, code reviews, technical documentation and the transfer to the company are also part of your tasks

AI in software development

  • You use AI-enabled tools to support software development and evaluate their use
  • Critical testing of code generated with AI coding assistants is also one of your competencies.

Profile

  • You have a university degree in computer science (FH, university or ETH) or a comparable qualification and proven computer science skills
  • As a software engineer, you have in-depth expertise in digital accessibility, especially in WCAG, WAI-ARIA, semantic HTML and EN 301 549.
  • You have experience in the design, development and testing of accessible applications as well as manual, automated and AI-supported accessibility testing procedures
  • You will bring practical experience with accessibility reviews, audits and screen reader tests as well as AI-supported tools in software development
  • You have expert knowledge of the WCAG standards, in a higher programming language, with relevant accessibility tools and in the use of AI coding assistants
  • Experience with search engines such as Elasticsearch is an advantage
  • You distinguish yourself through consulting and mediation skills, independence and the ability to communicate with non-technical stakeholders
  • You have very good knowledge of German and English (each C1) in word and writing
