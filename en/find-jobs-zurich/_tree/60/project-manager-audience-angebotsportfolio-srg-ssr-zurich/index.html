Full description

The Audience & Offer Portfolio unites various functions that are of central importance for a modern, audience-oriented and sustainable SRG. The department is responsible for the development and preparation of strategic audience and market knowledge about research and analysis in all regions as well as the nationally guided control, development and quality assurance processes for a strategy- and concession-compliant SRG offer on all vectors.

This is your contribution.

In the Audience & Offer Portfolio team, you are committed to the following topics:

  • They are responsible for the supra-regional management, planning and coordination of large content projects until they are successfully transferred to regular operation. In doing so, you ensure that schedules, milestones and delivery objects are adhered to and operate continuous project monitoring including risk and resource management.
  • They work closely with regional teams, departments and interface partners to ensure the sustainable implementation of supra-regional priorities and projects in the SRG offer.
  • They support those responsible for offering services in cooperation across language regions on major priority topics of national importance.
  • They ensure the high-quality and sustainable implementation of strategic specifications, identify optimization potential and, if necessary, initiate suitable measures for achieving goals and improving processes.

They inspire us.

  • You have experience in coordination and/or project management
  • They think in a networked, structured way and move confidently in cross-divisional and cross-language contexts
  • They have a high level of communication skills and are an open, initiative and assertive personality.
  • They enjoy working across language and regional borders and have a high motivation for cooperation in interdisciplinary teams. With different specialists: you communicate at eye level and look for the best solutions together.
  • In-depth understanding of the Swiss media landscape as well as the competence to successfully coordinate and merge the different requirements of the regions (SRF, RTS, RSI, RTR) and genres
  • You have language skills in German at level C2 and in French at least at level B2 – or vice versa: French at level C2 and German at least at level B2

So we inspire you.

  • On a work activity with high social relevance, in which you help shape the future of media public service in Switzerland
  • Responsible hub function with direct insight into the future content and strategic orientation of SRG offerings
  • Interdisciplinary and linguistic-regional environment through close cooperation with managers and specialist managers throughout Switzerland
  • A motivating working environment in the newly oriented “Audience & Offer Portfolio” department with plenty of room for innovation
  • Hybrid working model with flexible home office opportunities and attractive, contemporary employment conditions of SRG

Please submit your application documents (motivation letter and CV) via the “Apply now” button. The application period ends on 7 December 2026.

Our promise

What does it take to reach the people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expertise, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can contribute and develop. For the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: Welcome to the world of SRG!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, promote creativity and offer a lot of design space for new and high-quality products.
  • Shaping digitalization: Quality journalism is also digital for us. That’s why we explore new possibilities and experiment with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-medial. We design on the pulse of time and on the nerve of the audience.
  • Being human: We meet each other collegially, work independently and shape our working atmosphere individually. Our success is based on the personal strengths as well as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programs. In doing so, we act respectfully and independently; in this way we promote opinion-forming and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
