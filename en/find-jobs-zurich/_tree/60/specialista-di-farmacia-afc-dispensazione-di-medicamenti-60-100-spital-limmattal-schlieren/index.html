Full description

From April 2027 or by appointment | No weekend service We are expanding our internal medication delivery office for outpatient and exiting patients. As a central pillar in the hospital Limmattal and in the care center, we ensure effective, safe and economical medication – from the selection of medicines and the distribution in-house via the directional and application process on the stations to the hospital exit. To strengthen our well-established team, we are looking for a committed, communicative personality with pleasure in patient counselling. Direct applications are preferred, external recruitment offices are not treated as a priority. Here they engage

  • Care and expert advice of outpatient and outgoing patients in our medication delivery
  • Management and maintenance of the pharmaceutical warehouse at the delivery point
  • Competent contact person for medical and nursing inquiries
  • Designing and updating work instructions and helping to shape the extended area

Size

  • Completed training as a specialist Pharmacy EFZ (former Pharma Assistant)
  • Ideally, professional experience in official or hospital pharmacy
  • Independent, structured and exact working method – even if it gets hectic
  • Very good IT knowledge and interest in interdisciplinary cooperation

Your new place of work Our top location (Limmattalbahn, 4 S-Bahn, 1 bus line in the immediate vicinity) is complemented by the LIMMI mobility concept:

  • Reduced employee parking
  • Motorcycle parking, lockable bicycle parking
  • With the ZVV Bonus Pass / Z-Bonus Pass we offer an attractive public transport subscription for employees

s'LIMMI: Your partner for life As an important health partner with a comprehensive training mandate, the Hospital Association Limmattal takes over the extended basic medical care of more than 100,000 patients annually, in all phases of life. The LIMMI is broadly specialized and offers high-quality medical care as well as in-depth expert knowledge in numerous surgical, medical and nursing specialist and specialist disciplines. The hospital association has 188 beds in the acute care sector, 126 beds in long-term care, a hospital-bound emergency service as well as nine operating rooms and eight intensive care facilities. More than 1,700 employees from 58 nations provide excellent and diverse services every day.
