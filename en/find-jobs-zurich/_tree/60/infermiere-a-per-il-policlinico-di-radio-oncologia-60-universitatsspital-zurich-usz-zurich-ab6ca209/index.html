We specialize in the outpatient care of patients before, during and after radio-oncological therapies. The individual, competent and comprehensive care of our patients:innen is our focus.

Are you passionate about being part of a highly specialized team and shaping the trend of outpatient medicine?

  • You look after, accompany and monitor our patients from different disciplines with a focus on radiation oncology. Wound counselling and wound care of radiation dermatitis is just as much a part of your tasks as assistance in intracavitary and interstitial brachytherapies.
  • As a nurse / nurse with several years of professional experience, you have already worked in an acute care hospital (ideally in the field of radio-oncological care) or completed an in-depth training in oncological care.
  • You keep an overview at all times, are resilient, set priorities and do not let quickly changing situations upset you.
  • We work from Monday to Friday between 07:15 and 17:15.
  • A dedicated and motivated team of 6 people will gladly work you into the exciting and challenging tasks.
