Full description

In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a social pedagogue at Hort Hürstholz 1 on 1 October 2026 or by agreement.

Tasks

  • They promote and support the children in their development and in their social skills and accompany them authentically, understandingly and appreciatively.
  • You take over the operational management of the morning table in agreement with the management
  • Constructive and appreciative collaboration in the team, at school level and with parenting
  • You pay the 90% fee 5 days per week
  • Administrative and organizational tasks you do routinely and efficiently

Profile

  • degree as a social pedagogue* in FH or HFS; social worker FH or HFS; Teaching diploma for the elementary school level, university degree with a major in social pedagogy, pedagogy or educational sciences (for applications with a foreign diploma / specialist card, a Swiss diploma recognition must be mandatory)
  • Motivated, open, resilient personality with a positive human image
  • Flexibility as well as very good team and communication skills
  • Joy in dealing with people of different cultures and willingness for interdisciplinary cooperation in the school team
  • Professional experience in supplementary family care is beneficial

We offer

  • A varied, versatile and independent activity
  • A multicultural, exciting environment
  • Competent support from the management
  • Progressive salary and social benefits according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich (Horte)

About us

We are a large school community in Zurich-Nord with 19 schools. We derive our pedagogical attitude from the concept of New Authority and thus meet the daily challenges in everyday school life together and effectively.

The lunch / evening hoards Hürstholz 1 and Arnikahof as well as the lunch table Hürstholz 3, belong to the school Hürstholz. In these care centres, up to 170 pupils from kindergarten to middle school age are fed and looked after every day. The opening hours of the lunch and evening hoard are from Monday to Friday from 11.55 to 18.00. The morning table is open from 7.00-8.20.
