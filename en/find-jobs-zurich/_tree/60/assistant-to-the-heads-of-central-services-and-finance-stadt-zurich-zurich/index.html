Full description

For our Finance and Central Services divisions, we are looking for a committed personality as an assistant to the department managers.

  • You are the first point of contact for internal and external stakeholders in the two areas Central Services and Finance.
  • They manage the two departmental secretariats independently in all administrative and organizational matters.
  • They maintain the calendars of the financial and central services divisions.
  • They organise and coordinate meetings, workshops, training sessions and project and working groups. They take over their preparation and follow-up, including the creation of basic principles and presentations as well as protocol management.
  • They work on internal projects and support the specialist teams of the two departments in administrative tasks. In addition, the superuser function for selected specialist applications takes over.
  • Commercial education (KV/Handelsschule) as well as several years of professional experience in a comparable position, further training in project management is advantageous
  • Very good MS-Office knowledge, stylish German as well as experience in protocol management and correspondence
  • Strong organisational, teamwork and assertiveness
  • Independent, structured and reliable working methods, high flexibility and rapid comprehension
  • Willingness to participate occasionally in evening sessions (about 4 - 8 times per year)

An extremely diverse and responsible position with a high degree of freedom of design in a lively, interdisciplinary environment awaits you. As an employee of the SAW, you benefit from attractive employment conditions in accordance with the Staff Law of the City of Zurich as well as targeted training and further education opportunities. In addition to at least four weeks of holidays, you are entitled to six additional company holidays. Your workplace is about 10 minutes. from Zurich Central Station in the heart of District 4.

The Foundation Retirement Apartments of the City of Zurich SAW operates around 40 settlements with more than 2000 affordable apartments for people aged 60 and over. It supports its tenants with everyday, social and nursing services and thus enables them a high quality of life into old age. More about the SAW at www.wohnenab60.ch.
