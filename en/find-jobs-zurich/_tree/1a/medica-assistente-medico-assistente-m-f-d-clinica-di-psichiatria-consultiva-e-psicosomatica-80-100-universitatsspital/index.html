Full description

USZ Campus 1 April 2027 or by agreement

Their main tasks

  • Under medical supervision, you will conduct consiliary examinations and treatments in the somatic clinics of the USZ
  • You participate in the consiliar psychiatric day and weekend service in the USZ including emergency department (no night service, we have a night doctor system)
  • Participation in teaching and research is possible on request. We also support you in obtaining your dissertation.
  • You have the opportunity to produce opinions.

Your profile

  • You are in further education to the specialist title FMH for psychiatry and psychotherapy, psychosomatic medicine, general internal medicine / family medicine or plan a foreign year in another specialty
  • Psychiatric, psychosomatic and/or internistic experience is advantageous, but not a requirement
  • They value interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Your German skills in word and writing are very good

Our offer

We offer you a versatile, interdisciplinary state-of-the-art activity at the interface of mental and somatic diseases and complaints. An appreciative working atmosphere, family-friendly working models and a balanced work-life balance are a matter of course for us. Our clinic holds the continuing education qualification for psychiatry and psychotherapy, category A (3 years) outpatient, for the main title in consultancy and liaison psychiatry (2 years) and for the main title in psychosomatic and psychosocial medicine (SAPPM) (2 years). For this post, dossiers from recruiters cannot be considered. Thank you for your understanding. Our Benefits

Kita on site

In our in-house daycare centers, your children will be cared for lovingly. Long opening hours without company holidays and subsidized care places relieve you in everyday life.

Health

We support your health. In addition to personal consultations and courses of our health management that promote your well-being, we offer you access to over 120 sports with a subsidized annual subscription to the ASVZ.

Flexibility

We promote a balanced work-life balance: Almost half of our employees work part-time, and about 50% of the tasks can be done in the home office – if your working day allows it.

Family friendliness

We help you to reconcile family and work. Mothers are entitled to at least 6 months of maternity leave (of which 16 weeks are paid in full), fathers to at least 2 months of paternity leave (of which 2 weeks are paid in full).

Further education and development

We accompany you on your professional path and support your development with individual career and further education opportunities.

Insurance

We cover 60% of your pension fund contributions and offer attractive special conditions for supplementary insurance.

Mobility

You benefit from a personal ZVV BonusPass at a special price or receive a fixed amount of money for a general subscription (GA) or half-tax PLUS.

Telephony

You and your family members benefit from special conditions for private mobile subscriptions. Other advantages: Details
