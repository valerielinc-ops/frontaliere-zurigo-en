Full description

Shape the technological future of basic IT care for the secondary and vocational schools of the Canton of Zurich. As a Technology Architect, you will be responsible for the technical architecture, develop the cloud landscape further and combine strategic technology decisions with professional leadership and practical implementation. Your tasks:

  • The technical IT architecture for secondary and vocational schools is your responsibility. In doing so, you continuously develop the technological target image.
  • For Microsoft Azure, Entra ID and associated cloud and infrastructure services, you define architecture specifications and ensure compliance with relevant security and technology standards.
  • With the development and control of the technology roadmap, you shape the long-term technological orientation of the education sector.
  • Internal and external specialists lead and coordinate professionally and accompany the implementation of technical solutions together with engineering.
  • In close cooperation with Service Delivery and Operations, you will further develop technical specifications and ensure their practicality in operation.
  • As a contact person for architectural issues, you advise different stakeholders and represent the education sector in overarching technology and strategy projects. Your profile:
  • A higher education in computer science or a comparable field as well as profound experience in the conception and further development of complex IT architectures form your professional basis.
  • With Microsoft Azure and Entra ID, you’ll be intimately familiar with both technologies, particularly in tenant architecture, governance and security.
  • Hybrid IT infrastructures and the architecture of cloud and on-premises solutions are familiar to you from practice.
  • Experience in the technical management of technical specialists as well as in cooperation with engineering and IT operations distinguishes you.
  • Connected thinking, decision-making strength and a cooperative way of working are among your strengths. You also convey complex technical relationships in an understandable and convincing way.
  • Stylistic German and good knowledge of English round off your profile. Certifications such as TOGAF or Microsoft Azure Solutions Architect Expert are beneficial.
