Full description

You are shaping the future of our OpenStack platform. As an experienced Linux engineer, you take on a central role in the design, operation and further development of our infrastructure and actively participate in the expansion of the environment. You get the opportunity to further develop your existing Linux know-how in the direction of cloud and OpenStack technologies. In order to strengthen our team at the Zurich Airport location, we are looking for a committed and competent personality as a Senior Linux Engineer (OpenStack area) immediately or by agreement. Your role

  • Responsibility for the operation, development and automation of our cloud platforms
  • Participation in the design, construction and optimization of our OpenStack environment
  • Planning and implementation of infrastructure and automation solutions with modern tools such as Ansible, OpenTofu or Terraform
  • Ensuring stable and efficient operation as well as continuous optimization of performance, availability and security
  • Analysis and resolution of complex disruptions in 2nd and 3rd level support
  • Close cooperation with infrastructure, network and storage teams
  • Participation in architecture and technology decisions and active design of our future cloud platform
  • Gradual assumption of responsibility in the OpenStack environment and further development into an OpenStack specialist

Your profile

  • Completed degree in computer science or comparable education/training
  • Several years of experience as a Linux System Engineer, Linux Platform Engineer or Linux Administrator in demanding enterprise environments
  • Experience with automation and configuration management tools
  • In-depth knowledge of cloud platforms, virtualization, container technologies and infrastructure as code
  • Understanding networks, storage technologies and highly available infrastructures
  • Analytical, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Very good knowledge of German at C1 level and good knowledge of English
  • Readiness for Picetting

Your benefits

  • Up to 60% home office possible with a 100% workload
  • Holiday purchase (2 weeks per year)
  • 41h week with flexible working hours on a full-time workload
  • Mind. 25 days of holidays per year
  • Support for 100% financial or temporal training
  • Free SBB half-ax subscription for all employees
