Full description

Eraneos is an international strategy, transformation and technology advisory group that helps companies thrive in a dynamic digital world. With first-class experts from business and technology, we empower our customers to set new standards for sustainable transformations – from strategy to implementation. Whether in the design of sustainable organizations, the development of new potential through data and AI or the protection of companies through holistic cybersecurity: We deliver results pragmatically and purposefully. Our team of around 1,300 dedicated employees is represented in offices in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, Portugal, Denmark, Sweden, Austria, the UK, Singapore and the USA and combines global perspectives with strong local roots. Around 450 employees work at the Swiss locations in Zurich, Bern and Lausanne.

This is the job

  • End-to-end responsibility for recruitment processes in selected areas – from briefing with the hiring manager to commitment
  • Not just selecting talent, but actively finding it: Active sourcing via LinkedIn and networks and building talent pools for recurring profiles
  • Hiring managers advise on profiles, market situation and process – quickly, bindingly and with their own opinion
  • A candidate experience that sounds like Eraneos: clear communication, short reaction times and honest feedback, even in the event of cancellations
  • Participation in employer branding and further development of our recruiting processes, for example through data-based reporting and the targeted use of AI tools

This job is for you if

  • You have a completed degree (e.g. business administration, psychology, human resources management) or a commercial education with HR training in your backpack.
  • You have gained your first recruiting experience in a company, a consultancy or a recruitment agency and ideally already managed your own processes.
  • Service is not a phrase for you: concerns of hiring managers and candidates: inside you take seriously, prioritize clean and deliver reliably – even if it has to be fast.
  • You act confidently, in conversation with candidates: as well as with senior managers and partners, and deal discreetly with confidential information.
  • You speak and write German at native language level and English at least at C1 level.

As an i-top you bring with you

  • Experience with Workday or another applicant management system as well as with LinkedIn Recruiter.
  • Experience in recruitment in the consulting, technology or IT environment.
  • Curiosity to try out AI tools in everyday recruiting and use them sensibly.

Not all fulfilled? Apply anyway – we are interested in what you bring and where you want to go.

What is important to us With Eraneos, what counts is what you want to move. Even if you do not fully meet every requirement, we look forward to your application. We are interested in your mindset, your ideas and the impact you want to create. Diversity is a source of growth and inspiration for us. We look forward to every application.

Hüseyin Yalcin is your contact person and looks forward to receiving your direct application via our website (CV, diplomas and employment certificates as PDF). Applications by e-mail or through personnel service providers are not considered.
