Location: Zurich Work Pattern Percentage: 80-100% Start Date: February 2027 About the Role As a Senior Consultant / Manager, you will work with leading banks on end-to-end business transformations, from strategy and target operating model design to process optimisation and technology enablement, with AI embedded throughout. You will take ownership of key areas of our client engagements, turn complex challenges into pragmatic solutions, and help clients translate strategic ambitions into tangible outcomes. Finding the Talent that makes Deloitte unique Are you passionate about the banking industry and motivated to help banks transform their business and operations? Do you enjoy solving complex challenges, working with senior stakeholders and interdisciplinary teams, and using AI to rethink how banking works? Then join our Banking Solutions team! How you can make an impact In your role, you will:

  • Help clients address complex business challenges and drive sustainable growth by translating strategic priorities into actionable solutions and transformation initiatives
  • Advise our clients across a broad range of Banking Solutions domains, including Advisory, Front-to Back Credit & Risk, Banking Technology, and Investment Management Solutions
  • Lead or take ownership of project workstreams, from problem definition and analysis through solution design to implementation
  • Help banks identify, design and implement AI use cases across the value chain, from AI-enabled processes to intelligent operations
  • Work closely with client stakeholders to structure complex problems, develop solutions and effectively communicate recommendations to senior management
  • Contribute to the growth of our Banking Solutions practice by identifying new opportunities, shaping new propositions, and supporting business development activities
  • Collaborate with colleagues across different areas of expertise to bring the right capabilities and perspectives to our clients What you bring to succeed
  • You hold a university degree in banking, finance, business administration, economics, or a similar field of study
  • You have at least 4-6 years of relevant professional experience in consulting and/or the financial services industry, with a strong understanding of banking, asset management and/or wealth management
  • You bring experience in one or more areas relevant to Banking Solutions, such as banking strategy, transformation, operating models, credit and risk, banking technology, investment management or process optimisation
  • You are an analytical and structured thinker who can break down complex problems, develop solutions and translate them into clear recommendations
  • You are fluent in both German and English
  • French or Italian is a plus What sets you apart
  • You are comfortable taking ownership of deliverables and workstreams, managing priorities and proactively identifying and addressing risks
  • You have excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to effectively articulate complex business problems and recommendations to senior client stakeholders
  • You are an energetic team player with a positive, can-do attitude, strong interpersonal skills and a commitment to delivering high-quality work
  • You actively use AI tools in your daily work and understand their potential to transform banking processes, operating models and ways of working
  • You are willing to travel within Switzerland and potentially abroad Your team Join a diverse, international and highly motivated Banking Solutions team. You will work alongside experienced practitioners with expertise spanning banking strategy, business transformation, technology and operations. We will support your continued growth through challenging client engagements, training, mentoring and on-the-job development, while giving you the opportunity to build your network and collaborate with colleagues across business lines and areas of expertise. Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
