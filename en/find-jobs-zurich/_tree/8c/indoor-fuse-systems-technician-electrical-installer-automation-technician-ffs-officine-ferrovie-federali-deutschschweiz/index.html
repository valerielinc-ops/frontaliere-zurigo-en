You can look forward to that.

As a technician: Indoor safety systems, you can independently locate and correct disturbances at our complex systems such as signal boxes and level crossings. You are the one who brings our customers back on track in an instant! Weekly you take over the Picettdienst – and of course there is a fair compensation. In addition, you are responsible for the maintenance, construction and of signalling and control systems. With a well-established team, check not only plants, but the smooth running of Switzerland’s largest transport company. We are looking for support at these locations of German-speaking Switzerland: Lucerne and Sargans. Attractive working conditions are important to us, even in the case of irregular working hours. You can enjoy the free swimming pools and fitness centers during the week and make your free time more flexible. Night and weekend work is also paid for. You will also receive time allowances. This means you have more free time than others without night and weekend assignments. Our other benefits such as child allowances, free 2nd class GA and discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under " Benefits". Further insights into the profession as a security system technician: inside you can get at the professional worlds of SBB or directly here: Technician: inside security systems | SBB You can move that.

  • You independently carry out construction and maintenance activities on the signal box interiors and partly on the exteriors.
  • You actively support the economical guarantee of the reliability, availability and safety of railway infrastructure facilities.
  • During the Picetting Service, you will repair disruptions to the complex facilities to ensure their availability.
  • Depending on the project, you work occasionally at night and rarely on weekends.

You bring that with you.

  • You are a technically affine personality with an independent and flexible way of working.
  • Unrestricted hearing and vision is required in order to be able to recognize important acoustic and visual signals during operations in the track area.
  • You have completed basic electrotechnical training EFZ (automatician: in, electrical installer: in or comparable professions). Ideally, you live close to the respective branch, as the willingness to provide weekly piquetting service is a prerequisite for this job.
  • Readiness to complete the 2 years of part-time internal training for technicians: in indoor security systems. SBB assumes full time and costs for this.
  • Valid driving licence of category B as well as very good knowledge of German in words and writing at at least B2 level are required.
