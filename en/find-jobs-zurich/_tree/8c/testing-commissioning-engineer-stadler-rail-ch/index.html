Full description

EXPERIENCE

  • Diploma in electrical or mechanical engineering (TU/FH) or comparable technical training
  • Several years of practical work in the industrial sector, preferably in the rail sector
  • Experience in commissioning, testing and troubleshooting
  • Experience in the creation, revision and maintenance of technical documentation
  • Experience in requirements management (IBM DNG, PLM or similar)

  • Be a team player, solution oriented and flexible for night work and travel
  • Mastery of English is compulsory, the German or French language is fluent

LIABILITY

  • Coordinate, execute and report system deployments and field tests based on approved on-site test documentation
  • Manage hardware and software configurations in the field, ensuring proper documentation and version control
  • Record and analyze incidents and track mitigation and corrective actions
  • Troubleshooting and solving technical problems, in close collaboration with engineering, project manager and client
  • Plan electrical and mechanical changes and purchase components in coordination with engineering and project manager
  • Conduct or support other field activities (tests, limited electrical and mechanical modifications, etc.), including track work
  • Work closely with customer and installation teams
