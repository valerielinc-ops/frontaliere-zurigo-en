80-100%, Winterthur / Smart Working

Shape your future – together with us!

Would you like to actively shape the future and develop innovative solutions together for our sales partners? If yes – then you have come to the right place!

Our agile product team in AXA Switzerland is responsible for the development and operation of the AXA BrokerPortal. Our mission is to enable brokers to collaborate digitally with AXA in a simple and uncomplicated manner.

As part of our team, you will manage the AXA BrokerPortal and develop innovative solutions tailored to the broker. Another focus is on the support and further development of the EcoHub core processes and the associated interfaces for automated data exchange with our brokers. This includes the operation of the existing DXP (Data Exchange Proxy) interfaces and the implementation of the Standard API Framework (SAF) based on Kafka.

The tasks ahead are demanding, exciting and varied. You can move and shape a lot and get a deep insight into the different areas of AXA. Are you in? Support us on our mission - committed, courageous and open to new things. We look forward to you and your participation and are convinced: “Know you can.”

Around 600 software and system developers work across Switzerland in the area of ​​data, technology and innovation and develop the technology of tomorrow in-house. We also collaborate in global teams and networks. We shape and integrate the latest tools, organize hackathons and our agile product teams are self-organized and take responsibility from A to Z.

This is what awaits you

  • You continuously support and develop the AXA BrokerPortal as well as the central data exchange interfaces (DXP and the modern SAF).
  • You ensure stable operation and professional support for the AXA BrokerPortal and our DXP/SAF interfaces
  • You drive forward technological development and the optimization of working methods
  • You work independently in a team and actively shape the further development of the team
  • You design and conceptualize innovative solutions to improve our processes and offerings
  • You experiment with new approaches and provide prototypes to quickly test and implement ideas

This is what you bring with you

  • You have experience with frontend development: React/Redux, CSS3 and TypeScript
  • You have good knowledge of Java 25 and Springboot. Kotlin, MongoDB and Kafka know-how an advantage
  • You are familiar with CI/CD: Maven, ArgoCD, Helm and OpenShift
  • You are familiar with functional programming and microservices
  • You have a penchant for clean, high-quality solutions
  • Team boundaries are not obstacles for you, but rather an incentive to find out more
  • Very good knowledge of spoken and written German is a prerequisite and good knowledge of English is an advantage
