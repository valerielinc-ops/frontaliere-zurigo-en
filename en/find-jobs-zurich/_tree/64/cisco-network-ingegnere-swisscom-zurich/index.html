# Cisco Network Engineer

Il tuo futuro inizia

Introduction to the role

You work in a team of over 40 system engineers in German-speaking Switzerland. Together, you realize customized ICT solutions with a focus on Enterprise Network and Security for our business customers. Customer orientation and personal responsibility characterize our cooperation. You will advise, design and implement innovative Cisco Enterprise Network solutions.

Highlights of the place

  • Participation in the design and implementation of Cisco Enterprise Network solutions
  • Collaboration in varied customer projects of different sizes
  • Collaboration with experienced network professionals and opportunity for professional development
  • Practical training and self-responsible working in a team
  • Diverse customer environments in the enterprise environment

Your skills

Technical, experience and technology

  • Several years of experience in enterprise networking or ICT infrastructure
  • Very good knowledge of routing and switching as well as experience in the operation and troubleshooting and troubleshooting of networks
  • Interest in SD-WAN and network automation
  • Cisco certification or equivalent knowledge

Personal competences and languages

  • Structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Reliable, team-oriented and communicative personality with a secure appearance
  • Enjoying professional development and new technologies
  • Very good German and good English in word and writing

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. We offer you a flexible working time arrangement that is oriented to your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you benefit from a wide range of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. These include a varied working environment, financial advantages and exciting opportunities for professional development.
