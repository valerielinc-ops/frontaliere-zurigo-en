Chauffeur C / CE (a) Picks from 01.03.2027 At our location in Pfungen, we carry out general cargo and partial cargo transports with a balanced fleet of around 27 vehicles. Our catchment area extends throughout Switzerland with the exception of peripheral regions where we work with our distribution network. Our customers include well-known companies from the region and transport various goods of all kinds. We offer comprehensive storage, picking and packaging services for our customers. To strengthen our proven driver team, we are looking for general cargo, per 1. March 2027 a chauffeur, or a chauffeuse C / CE. Task area

  • Distribution of general cargo
  • Assistance in loading the trucks
  • Ensure daily order and cleanliness in the transhipment warehouse
  • Compliance with the correct handling of the transported goods and an expert loading activity
  • Compare transport goods with transport documents and, if necessary, correct them

Requirement profile

  • CE driving licence
  • Industry knowledge in logistics and road transport
  • Very good knowledge of German (word / writing)
  • Professional appearance and pleasure in customer contact
  • Reliable, flexible, independent, capable of teamwork, forward-looking, quality-conscious
