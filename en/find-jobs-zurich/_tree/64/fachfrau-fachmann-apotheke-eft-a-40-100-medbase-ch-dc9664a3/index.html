Welcome to the Medbase Pharmacy Winterthur Altstadt

Our pharmacy is located a few steps from the main train station, on the edge of the old town of Winterthur. In addition to a wide range of products from conventional medicine, naturopathy and cosmetics, we offer services such as vaccinations, health checks or consultations. Selected cosmetic products and our special offer for mothers and young children, including breastfeeding room, make our everyday life even more diverse. We advise our large regular customers and passers-by competently. We also appreciate the lively interdisciplinary cooperation with doctors. Our pharmacy is also involved in the training of apprentices and pharmacists in the assistant year. At Medbase Apotheke Winterthur Altstadt we care for each other and love the lively exchange with customers.
