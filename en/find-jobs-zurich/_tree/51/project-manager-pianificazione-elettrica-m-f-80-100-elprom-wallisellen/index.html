Job title Project manager electrical planning m/f (80-100%) Description Are you at home in electrical planning, do you like to take on responsibility and keep track of challenging projects? Do you want to contribute your experience, implement your own ideas and make a difference together with a motivated team? Then you are right with us! For our locations in Wallisellen and Pratteln, we are looking for dedicated project managers who enjoy versatile electroengineering projects and want to accompany them from the first idea to the successful implementation. Your tasks

  • You lead versatile electrical engineering projects from conception to successful handover
  • You take overall responsibility for quality, costs and deadlines and ensure professional project implementation
  • You lead your project teams professionally, plan the use of resources and coordinate all project participants
  • You advise and accompany our customers and maintain long-term customer relationships
  • You develop innovative and practical solutions taking into account applicable norms and standards
  • You acquire new projects, create offers and conduct contract negotiations
  • You contribute your experience and ideas and actively shape the further development of our planning processes

You bring that with you

  • Completed training as electrical planner EFZ or electrical installer EFZ
  • Further education such as higher specialist examination HFP, engineer BSc, technician HF or similar
  • Several years of experience in the planning of electrical projects
  • In-depth knowledge of relevant norms, standards and electrical systems
  • Secure use of digital planning and project management tools
  • Commercial understanding and experience in dealing with project budgets, tenders and offers
  • Enthusiasm for modern technologies and developments in electrical engineering
  • Organizational talent, networked thinking and an independent, solution-oriented working method
  • Communication strength, pleasure in managing project teams and direct customer contact

You can look forward to that.

  • 6 Week Holidays & Paid Overtime
  • Attractive pension benefits & supplementary insurance
  • Individual mobility solutions
  • Flexible working hours & modern working environment
  • Support for further education and personal development
  • A long-term function with clear structures
  • Plenty of design freedom, direct cooperation & short decision paths
  • A stable, long-term team with appreciative cooperation

Interested? Do you want to lead exciting projects on your own responsibility, contribute your experience and further develop electrical planning together with us? Then we should get to know each other. Questions about this position will gladly answer Urs Bugmann.
