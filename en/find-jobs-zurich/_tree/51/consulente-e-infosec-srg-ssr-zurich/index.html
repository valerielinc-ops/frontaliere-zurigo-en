Full description

The area of operations assumes the central strategic and steering role in the technological direction and production of SRG SSR.

Within the "Pilote Operations" department, the Information Security team (under the direction of CISO) is responsible for protecting the confidentiality, integrity and availability of all SRG SSR information.

Your Mission

Within the InfoSec Advisory team, you have the following responsibilities:

  • You anchor Security by Design at the SSR by accompanying, from idea to go-live, projects, products and initiatives from all areas of the company (e.g.: Technology, Program, Finance, HR), and ensuring the implementation of security requirements
  • You make risks visible and manageable from the outset, through threat and risk analyses (e.g., threats modelling) and pragmatic recommendations
  • You guarantee cloud architectures and secure solutions by conducting architectural reviews and assuming the role of security architect on major and cross-cutting projects
  • You build confidence in our solutions by delivering the technical safety sign-off, coordinating audits and slopes and monitoring the implementation of the measures
  • You make security reusable by contributing to standards, baselines and security patterns, in connection with RCMP InfoSec (Polices) and Enterprise Architecture
  • You develop cloud security as a skill within the SSR by sharing your knowledge with the team, InfoSec Guild and the Security Champions network

Your profile

  • Several years of experience in information security, ideally with a technical background (engineering or operations)
  • Strong cloud security knowledge (Azure and M365, ideally AWS), e.g. identities and access, landing zones, cloud architectures; application security knowledge and DevSecOps are an asset
  • Ability to popularize complex topics and convince, both with engineers and managers
  • Practical and oriented mind solutions: you secure without blocking
  • Excellent mastery of French or German and very good oral and written knowledge of the other language, to participate actively in the meetings
  • English for documentation
  • Certification such as CCSP, CISSP, CISM or AWS/Azure safety certification is an asset

What we offer you

  • A lot of responsibilities and a lot of flexibility: you are making a lasting contribution to the construction of the new InfoSec Advisory team, its services and its skills
  • Various and exciting challenges in a company undergoing transformation, to support the public service of the SSR in practice
  • Targeted training and certification aligned with your development plan
  • Flexible work models with telework and on-site team days, as well as the conditions of engagement of the SSR CTC

Please submit your application documents (motivation letter and CV) via the "Apply now" button. The deadline for applications ends on December 8, 2026.
