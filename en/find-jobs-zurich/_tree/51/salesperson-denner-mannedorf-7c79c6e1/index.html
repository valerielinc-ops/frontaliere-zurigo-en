Full description

Fresh products that live up to their name. Shelves filled. Orders triggered on time. Area clean. Cash register adds up. Customers satisfied. That's our store. And soon yours too. Join our winning team now. ## Job Responsibilities

  • Operate the cash register and calculate
  • Receive, check and stock fresh products and of course all other goods
  • Check product shelf life dates
  • Ensure order and cleanliness in the branch and warehouse
  • Correctly implement store layout specifications ## Requirements
  • Ideally more than two years of work experience: in retail (food)
  • Completed vocational training (EBA): in retail (food) or a related profession would be ideal
  • Willingness to work beyond 'nine to five'
  • and enjoy leisure time when others work
  • Physically fit, some parcels weigh up to 10 kg
  • Familiar with PC, tablet and smartphone
  • and with other digital helpers (e.g. for inventory management) you adapt quickly
  • Enjoy being around people, a good dose of warmth and a positive attitude
  • German (good knowledge) ## What We Offer
  • Part of the Migros Group: Strong brand anchored in retail under the umbrella of the Migros Group
  • Healthy working: High standards in occupational safety, ergonomics and hygiene.
  • Maternity/paternity leave: 5 weeks longer baby break (19 weeks) for mothers and that at 100% salary. Fathers enjoy 4 paid weeks for the newcomer.
  • Fair wages: 13 monthly salaries and fixed salary increases for all instead of bonuses for a few.
  • Mobile phone subscription: The choice is yours: With Galaxus Mobile, Wingo or Sunrise already from CHF 10.- so or so correctly connected.
  • Migros Bank: Better drive with interest rates and fees for your private, investment or mortgage accounts.
  • Employee offers: 10 percent on the next large purchase (twice a year).
  • Vacation: 6 weeks' vacation for all and additional days for service anniversaries.
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts on car rentals, hotels, events and many other areas. ## Contact ## Application & Contact Gianluca Morciano Recruiter Sales Eastern Switzerland
