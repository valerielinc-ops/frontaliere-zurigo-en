Full description

Shape a leading UK&I Property Facultative franchise. We are looking for an experienced underwriter combining technical depth, commercial judgement and a collaborative mindset to originate business, structure complex solutions and help shape our portfolio and market presence. You will have meaningful underwriting autonomy, supported by a highly experienced and internationally connected team.

About the Role As a Senior Property Facultative Underwriter, you will cover both the domestic UK&I market and, more significantly, international risks placed through the London market. You will be at the heart of our facultative property business, originating, evaluating and underwriting complex risks and facilities with real commercial impact. Based in Zurich and part of our Global Facultative Market Unit, you will combine deep technical expertise with entrepreneurial drive to grow and shape a high-performing portfolio. The role offers meaningful underwriting autonomy and market visibility, with scope to influence portfolio strategy, broker engagement and the continued development of our UK&I franchise.

Key Responsibilities

  • Underwrite simple and complex single risks and facilities, supported by advanced modelling and analytics
  • Originate new business and channels, develop & deepen broker and client relationships
  • Own and develop the portfolio in line with risk appetite, return targets and strategic priorities
  • Translate market and portfolio insights into decisive underwriting actions
  • Leverage and develop the wider team, sharing expertise and collaborating across the organisation

About the Team You will combine the strength, data and global reach of a leading reinsurer with the pace, ownership and direct market access of a specialist facultative team. You will join the Global Fac Market Unit, a highly experienced, internationally connected department with a well-established UK market franchise. We are recognised for our expertise in single-risk and facility underwriting, strong broker and insurer relationships, and high service quality in a fast-paced market. The role sits within a dedicated team of five more underwriters covering UK&I, reporting to the Head of Facultative Property UK&I & MEA. You’ll join a collaborative, intellectually curious team that values technical rigour and commercial creativity.

About You

You are a seasoned Property Fac underwriter combining technical judgement, commercial drive and strong market relationships. You take ownership, collaborate naturally and thrive in a client-facing environment.

We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements

  • Commercial, entrepreneurial mindset with disciplined underwriting judgement
  • Significant property underwriting experience (5 to 10 years) , ideally in the UK market
  • Proven new business origination and broker/client relationship development
  • Strong risk assessment and pricing skills, including catastrophe modelling and analytics for single risks and facilities
  • Collaborative mindset, leveraging team expertise to deliver stronger underwriting outcomes
  • Ability to translate data into clear underwriting actions and strategic insights
  • Excellent written and verbal English

These are additional nice to haves

  • Curiosity, adaptability and a continuous learning mindset
  • Professional e xperience in primary insurance, and /or broking
  • Relevant degree or equivalent professional experience
  • Experience mentoring junior underwriters
