Put to the Point

Are you looking forward to being self-employed in vehicles from the Mercedes Sprinter to the truck on the roads of Switzerland and do you already feel comfortable in traffic today? Then you are exactly right with us! As part of your training, you will get an exciting insight into the varied tasks of the transport sector, you will learn to take responsibility, make decisions and cultivate customer and team contact with your team skills. With us you become a talent in and around our vehicles and get the opportunity to gain your first experience in freight transport and to serve special places and objects of the Swiss army. Do you feel appealed to? We look forward to your application in order to lay the foundation for your professional future together. Further information and portraits of our learners can be found at www.armee.ch/lehre.

Diversity, dynamism, perspectives – teaching professions of the Swiss Army

The logistics base of the Swiss Army (LBA) is there for the force. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss army and manages the material, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss army with its approximately 3,500 employees and 12,000 militia members. Whether in the workshop or in the office, inside or outside: The LBA offers a wide range of workplaces throughout Switzerland. We have something for everyone.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.

We are committed to the integration of people with disabilities.
