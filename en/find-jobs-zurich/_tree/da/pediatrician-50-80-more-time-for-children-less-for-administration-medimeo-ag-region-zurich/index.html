You value outpatient pediatrics, but want more exchange and less paperwork? In an established group practice near Zurich, you work in a collegial pediatrician team, are relieved by digital and AI-supported solutions and determine your workload, today and in the future. This established group practice combines family medicine and paediatric medicine under one roof, accompanying families from newborns to adults. New patients are accepted without restriction, the demand is high and the prospects correspondingly long-term. In the paediatric field, several paediatricians work hand in hand

  • The team is international and multilingual. Short decision-making paths and targeted investments in modern infrastructure and digital processes ensure that you can focus on the essentials: caring for children and their families.

The practice is located in the agglomeration of Zurich and is very accessible, whether from the city or from the surrounding area.

Their tasks

  • Collegial paediatric team with active professional exchange
  • Co-design and further development of modern practical processes
  • Participation in digital and innovative solutions in everyday practice
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation within group practice
  • Opportunity to contribute your own professional interests and priorities

Your Competences

  • Enjoying outpatient pediatrics and working in a team
  • Openness to digital solutions and new ways of working
  • Very good knowledge of German, other languages are advantageous

Their advantages

  • Significant relief from administration thanks to digital and AI-based solutions

  • An attractive, flexible wage model
