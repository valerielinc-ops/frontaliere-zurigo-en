Location: Zurich Switzerland Work Pattern Percentage: 80-100% Advance cutting-edge business areas with your expertise Deloitte Consulting, you support organizations together with our other specialists in technology strategy and transformation to develop strategies and implement systems that create business value and drive performance. Deloitte is a ServiceNow Global Elite Partner and supports over 7,000 organizations worldwide in transforming their operations through the automation of enterprise workflows and integrated AI. Together, you orchestrate IT Service Management, Workflow Automation, and Digital Transformation initiatives with ServiceNow as the central platform – from strategic planning to operational excellence. You shape digital transformations that change organizations – and make a real difference in the process. How you can make an impact You are not just an implementer of requirements – you are a consultant of solutions. In this role:

  • Advise executives and operational teams to solve their most complex IT and business challenges
  • Design ServiceNow solutions that automate processes, increase efficiency, and create real business value
  • Lead transformation projects – from strategy and design through implementation and beyond
  • Learn continuously from experienced partners and develop yourself into a sought-after consulting expert
  • Grow with us: Your expertise in ServiceNow and consulting opens doors to national and international projects and career opportunities Who are we looking for? Must-Have:
  • Is interested to do ServiceNow Consulting and studied Information Systems Management
  • Fluent German and Englisch – You communicate confidently with German-speaking clients but also with international Clients
  • Functional expertise – You understand business requirements and can translate them into ServiceNow “language”
  • Business Analyst mindset – You think in processes, not just features
  • Nice-to-Have (but very welcome):
  • IT understanding – You know IT processes, ITIL fundamentals, or IT Service Management
  • Consulting experience – You have already worked on client projects or in consulting environments
  • 1–3 years of practical experience with ServiceNow (ideally in ITSM, ITOM, CSM, or Workflow modules) or similar automation platforms
  • Certifications – ServiceNow Certified Application Developer or Administrator Your Personality!
  • Motivated to go the extra mile – You see challenges as opportunities, not obstacles
  • Not a 9-to-5 mentality – You're ready to invest in project success
  • Hungry to learn – You're curious, actively develop yourself, and want to build consulting skills
  • Customer-focused – You understand that your work has real impact on business. Customer value is at the centre of what you do!
  • Team player – You enjoy working with colleagues, clients, and cross-functional teams
  • Agile mindset – You always take responsibility for your actions Your team By joining the team of our Zurich office, you will be integrated into a diverse bunch of congenial colleagues right from the start. We take your further development seriously and offer you a comprehensive internal and external training programme. You will also benefit from our expertise in a deep understanding of technology, coupled with excellent communications skills and broader business understanding. Look forward to working together with some of the largest organisations in Switzerland and the world to introduce the change that really makes an impact for their business.
