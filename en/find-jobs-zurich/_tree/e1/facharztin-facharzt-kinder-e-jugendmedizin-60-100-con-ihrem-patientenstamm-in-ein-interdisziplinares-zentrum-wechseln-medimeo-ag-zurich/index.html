Do you already have a paediatric consultation in Switzerland and would like more exchange, less administration and free choice of working model? In a family medicine medical center near Zurich, you bring your patients with you, expand the pediatrics and work hired or independently. This established network of medical centers stands for networked, patient-oriented and interdisciplinary primary care in the agglomeration of Zurich. Several locations form a professional medical network in which the exchange across specialist boundaries is a matter of course. At the site, family medicine, gynecology and pediatrics work under one roof. Families are thus accompanied through various stages of life, from pregnancy through childhood to adulthood. The centre already has a paediatric patient base, accepts new patients and wants to further develop paediatrics in a targeted manner, ideal for continuing and expanding your existing consultation. Modern infrastructure and digital processes provide relief: Medical reports are generated with AI-supported speech recognition, drug management is automated, and the administration takes over a well-established practice organization.

Their tasks

  • Self-employed outpatient care of infants, children and adolescents across the spectrum of outpatient pediatrics
  • Preventive and developmental controls including assessment of physical and psychosocial development as well as vaccination counselling and vaccinations
  • Counselling and long-term support of parents and families
  • Close cooperation with family medicine, gynecology and other disciplines and external partners
  • Co-creation and further development of paediatric services

Your Competences

  • Specialist title of paediatric medicine, acquired or recognized in Switzerland
  • Professional experience as a pediatrician in Switzerland, ideally in outpatient pediatrics
  • Professional authorisation or fulfilment of the relevant conditions
  • Independent, structured and family-oriented work, combined with empathy and communication strength
  • Very good knowledge of German, other languages are advantageous

Their advantages

  • Free choice of model: employment or self-employment with rental
  • The opportunity to bring your patient base with you and further expand it on an existing basis
  • Family medicine under one roof, with short distances to family medicine and gynecology
  • Significant relief thanks to AI-based speech recognition, automated drug management and established administration
  • A lot of room for manoeuvre in expanding the pediatric office hours
  • Professional exchange in a professional network with several medical centers
  • Flexible start date, immediately or by agreement
