You work in the hospital and would like to change to the practice, with scheduled working hours and more time for your patients? In an interdisciplinary medical center in Zurich, you will take on a gynecological consultation with an existing patient base, an attractive fixum and a team that will take over your administration. The independent medical center combines family medicine, gynecology and obstetrics under one roof, a physiotherapy is in the house. The practice relies on long-term care with personal doctor loyalty. The gynecological offer is wide and ranges from prevention, desire to have children and endometriosis to hormone and breast consultation hours to pregnancy care with extended ultrasound. Laboratory, digital X-ray, ultrasound, surgery and pharmacy are available in-house, and experienced medical assistants take over a large part of the administration. Due to a retirement and increasing demand, the team is strengthened. The medical centre is located in the north of the city of Zurich and looks after a multicultural patient clientele.

Their tasks

  • Independent care of patients in the gynecological consultation, from prevention to follow-up care
  • Pregnancy care including ultrasound examinations
  • Advice on contraception, desire to have children, menopause and hormone issues
  • Close cooperation with the gynecologist in the house and the family medical college:innen
  • Co-creation of the gynecological offer, gladly with its own focus

Your Competences

  • Specialist title in Gynecology and Obstetrics
  • At least three years of experience at a further education institution in Switzerland
  • Enjoyment of self-employment and long-term patient engagement
  • Additional qualification in sonography welcome, but no requirement
  • Very good knowledge of German, other languages such as English are desirable

Their advantages

  • Scheduled working hours from Monday to Friday instead of hospital rhythm
  • Pensum between 60 and 100%
  • Fixum from CHF 200,000 plus revenue share, alternatively a purely sales-based model
  • Existing patient base and constantly new patients
  • Permanent employment without entrepreneurial risk, with relief in personnel and accounting
  • Well-equipped practice with laboratory, ultrasound, X-ray, practice surgery and pharmacy
  • Five weeks of vacation, an additional unpaid week is possible
