Full description

In our logistics and in the last mile you work on systems in which software meets physical processes. If you are curious about how orders are processed, how complex storage systems interlock and how a shipment gets from the ramp to the customer and the customer, then we are the team for you!

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Headquarters, Pfingstweidstrasse 60b, 8005 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You are responsible for the technical vision of our existing products, from warehousing in our specially developed ERP to the last mile, which means you are actively involved in shaping the architectural strategy of the department
  • You make sure that our solutions are scalable, maintainable and secure. In addition, you identify and evaluate technical risks, dependencies and possible effects at an early stage.
  • Growing structures can be modernized incrementally without disrupting ongoing operations, thereby reducing technical debt.
  • You always keep an eye on new technologies and trends, evaluate their benefits for our environment and actively bring meaningful innovations to the team
  • Together with product owners, stakeholders and other architects, you translate requirements into clear, feasible solutions
  • At the interfaces to other internal logistics areas, after sales and customer service as well as externally to shipping service providers and authorities, you make stable agreements and keep the dependencies manageable.
  • You communicate in accordance with target groups and recognize when a compromise is more effective than the perfect concept
  • From conception to implementation to operation, you actively work with the development team. You are not only working conceptually, but also implement solutions in code yourself
  • In addition, you coach the team, actively involve them in the development of concepts and visions and support software engineers in their professional development.

Requirements

  • At least three years of experience: In a software architect role
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): In computer science (or sufficiently relevant professional experience)
  • You not only plan complex, distributed systems on the whiteboard, but also implement them successfully – including the connection of external partner systems, ideally in the logistics or shipping environment
  • Excellent knowledge of C# and the .NET Framework, SQL and distributed, high-throughput event-based systems are part of your toolbox – or you have a comparable background
  • Thanks to your pronounced ability to abstract, you recognize the essentials, set clear priorities and do not get lost in the details
  • You are strong in Requirement Engineering: you take technical requirements in a structured way, question them and translate them into technical concepts
  • AI is not a buzzword for you, but an integral part of analysis, conception, implementation and testing
  • The willingness to question “as it has always been done” and propose more efficient, automated or effective solutions
  • The ability to adapt to continuous changes in the working environment
  • German (flowing)
  • English (flowing)
