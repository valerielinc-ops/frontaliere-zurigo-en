Full description

The I. Civil Chamber deals with appeals in family law (marriage law, personal and marital status matters, child matters) and labour law proceedings, complaints against legal opening decisions and appeals in other civil proceedings of the district courts. To strengthen our team, we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement, temporarily for one year a committed and motivated person as a court clerk (100%) You are responsible for independent processing in complaint and appeal proceedings. In doing so, you have complete knowledge of the files, analyze the legal questions that arise, prepare the draft decisions for the judges involved in the decisions (application), make the final editorial team and have the opportunity to independently conduct settlement negotiations. Furthermore, you will be used as a protocol guide in the negotiations (appeal negotiations, settlement negotiations, child hearings, etc.). Your profile

  • Completed legal studies.
  • Lawyer's patent is beneficial.
  • Professional experience in the judiciary or legal profession.
  • Very good references regarding your legal knowledge – preferably in family law – and linguistic skills.
  • They work independently and accurately, are efficient and remain calm despite pendent hills. Our offer
  • Versatile and demanding work.
  • In-depth and instructive insight into the judicial activity of a second instance.
  • Very pleasant working atmosphere in an experienced and motivated team.
  • Modern, central workplace in the middle of the old town of Zurich.
  • Attractive and diverse training offer.
  • Ideal basis for further judicial career, in particular the career step as a district judge. Still questions?
