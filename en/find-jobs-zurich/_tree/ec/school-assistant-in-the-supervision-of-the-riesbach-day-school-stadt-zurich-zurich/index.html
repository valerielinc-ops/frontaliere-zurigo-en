Full description

The Riesbach Day School with the school buildings Mühlebach and Seefeld as well as a school pavilion and six support locations is a committed and innovative primary school in Seefeld, Zurich. Around 370 pupils from kindergarten to middle school are taught and supervised here.

The supervision of our school seeks reinforcement immediately or by agreement.

  • They assist in cleaning up and providing materials (e.g. teaching materials, toys, etc.).
  • You are responsible for simple administrative and organizational work at class/group level (e.g. absence list).
  • If necessary, they support the kitchen team in the housekeeping tasks, including cleaning the kitchen and preparing meals in compliance with the food guidelines as well as the safety and hygiene regulations.
  • They bring with them the willingness to actively cooperate in different teams.
  • They help shape the activities in the lunchtime.
  • Joy of working with students in kindergarten and primary school age
  • Very good knowledge of German (at least level B2)
  • Communication skills with students and adults
  • PC user skills (MS-Office: Word and Outlook)
  • Willingness to actively collaborate in different teams

A challenging and varied job in a motivated team, in which the teaching staff and the support work closely together. We attach importance to a solution- and goal-oriented cooperation as well as the development and development opportunities of the individual employees. Our employees benefit from progressive salary and social benefits according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department

Your days/times are: Monday to Friday from 10:00 to 14:00

The Zurichberg School District comprises districts 1, 7 and 8 with the districts Altstadt, Fluntern, Hottingen, Hirslanden, Riesbach and Witikon. The district school authority manages 18 school buildings. These are connected to 43 kindergartens and over 40 care facilities of different sizes. Of the 14 organizational units, 11 are day schools.
