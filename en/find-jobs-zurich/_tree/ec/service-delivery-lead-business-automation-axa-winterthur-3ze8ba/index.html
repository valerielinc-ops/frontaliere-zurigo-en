Full description

80-100%, Winterthur / Smart Working

  • Shape the «Digital Transformation» of AXA Switzerland with us!
  • As Service Delivery Lead – Business Automation – you are responsible for IT Service Delivery in the Business Automation area. You develop and operate our platforms for Business Process Management, Content Acquisition, Content Management, and Content Delivery. Together with the five agile products, you are a key success factor for the digitalization of AXA Switzerland, in a culture characterized by agile DevOps teams, visionary Product Owners, co-creation in all business areas, and innovation around data, AI, and cloud technologies.
  • This position is responsible for providing the IT platform and, together with the Product Owners, implementing use cases in business process management, document recognition and analysis, as well as their audit-proof storage and content delivery. In collaboration with the Product Teams and Product Owners, you make an important contribution to the further development of technology and processes.
  • You are primarily responsible for the following technical platforms:
  • Input Management (Content Acquisition)
  • Document Management/Archiving (Enterprise Content Management)
  • Output Management (Content Delivery)
  • Central Business Process Management (BPM) and
  • Robotics Automation and the PowerPlatform
  • With your experience and energy, you bring new impulses to our teams and drive AXA’s digitalization further!
  • In the Data, Technology & Innovation area, around 680 software and system developers work nationwide and develop tomorrow's technology in-house. We also collaborate in global teams and networks.
  • We shape and integrate the latest tools, host hackathons, and our agile product teams operate self-organized and are fully responsible from A to Z.

Tasks

  • You work closely with colleagues from all business areas of AXA Switzerland to advance process digitalization and automation. You represent the needs of your service cluster to internal and external stakeholders and support the Product and Service Owners.
  • You define the strategic and operational further development of the technical platforms and the services provided from them together with Product and Service Owners, Architecture, and Stakeholders.
  • You ensure a business- and customer-integrated, efficient, and effective Operating Model as well as the smooth operation of services in high quality.
  • You steer IT providers and are responsible for the Sourcing Strategy for your service cluster.
  • You are responsible for Platform Governance and Oversight: You manage dependencies, drive standardization, monitor availability and performance, and steer technical debt and risks.
  • You live Shared Leadership with the People Developer, Agile Masters, and Product Owners.
  • Together with the Business, you are responsible for content steering and bring in the professional needs of the service cluster. The development of employees is driven by the People Developer in collaboration with you.

Requirements

  • University / College degree in (Business) Informatics or equivalent
  • You have at least five years of professional experience in an international insurance company, a financial service provider, or an online service as a department head
  • You are a professional in handling data, processes, and Artificial Intelligence and achieve the adaptation to our business processes, thereby proactively working on the further development of our platforms
  • You have proven experience in the areas of digital transformation
  • You have a "Know You Can" mindset and live “Thought Leadership”
  • Through your fair negotiation skills, you find sustainable solutions together with internal and external stakeholders
  • Your communication skills are characterized by very good German and English skills
  • You have demonstrably developed complex strategies in the IT environment and accompanied them into production

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
