Full description

Faculty of Law

Job opening immediately or by agreement, indefinitely

As a staff office, the Legal Office supports the Faculty Board and the members of the Faculty of Law in legal matters. It supervises appeal proceedings, examines transactions with legal content and participates in legislation. In addition, it prepares decisions for the Faculty Board and the Faculty Assembly.

Tasks

Responsibility for all administrative tasks in the legal unit RWF. In cooperation with your teammate, you will in particular take on the following tasks:

  • Responsibility for administrative matters of the legal entity
  • Assisting lawyers in clarifying the facts in appeal proceedings
  • Contact person of the legal office for students, assistants and professors
  • Completion of the daily correspondence of the legal entity
  • Processing of applications
  • Management of document storage and archiving of documents in the CMI
  • Support and updating of the website of the legal authority incl. Maintenance of the Law Collection of the Faculty of Law
  • Management of office equipment
  • Compilation of statistics on processed transactions

The workload of 40-50% is spread over at least four half-days. The position offers versatile development potential. Depending on professional competences, interests and suitability, focus areas can be deepened or additional responsibilities can be assumed.

Profile

  • Commercial education with several years of professional experience
  • High willingness to learn and the ability to quickly familiarize yourself with new tasks
  • Experienced handling of MS Office programs
  • Experience in higher education or public administration is beneficial
  • Efficient, independent and careful work with good priorities
  • Flexibility and motivation to participate proactively in a dynamic environment
  • Clear, binding and addressable communication (written and oral)

Applicants who do not yet meet all requirements are welcome – provided they have a strong willingness to learn and the will to develop further. Careful introduction and targeted support are a matter of course for us. They assume a central role with the opportunity to actively shape and shape processes. Good and smooth teamwork is an important prerequisite for this job.
