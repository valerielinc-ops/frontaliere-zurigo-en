Technical Allrounder

Is this you?

  • You have completed vocational training in a skilled trade
  • You have already gained some experience working for a hotel chain or similar businesses
  • You have a good command of German and English, as well as a strong commitment to your work
  • You see yourself as a skilled all-rounder, have a team spirit, and are reliable and trustworthy
  • You enjoy working carefully and in an organized manner, and you’re willing to roll up your sleeves and pitch in
  • Quality is important to you, and you leave nothing to chance
  • You enjoy your work and keep a cool head even during hectic moments
  • You’re dependable: You’re punctual, reliable, and able to handle pressure

Your Responsibilities

  • Monitoring of all technical installations
  • Alarm monitoring
  • On-call service in case of technical malfunctions and for all necessary repairs
  • as far as possible
  • Parking garage monitoring
  • Negotiating with suppliers and service providers and external companies

  • Properly relay information of any kind pertaining to the house to staff, department heads and supervisors
