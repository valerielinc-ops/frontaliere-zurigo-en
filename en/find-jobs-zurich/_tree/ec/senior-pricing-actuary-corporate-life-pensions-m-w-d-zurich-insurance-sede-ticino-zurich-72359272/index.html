Full description

137685 | 80 - 100% | Oerlikon, Zurich | Hybrid

Do you want to play a central role in the further development of our pricing models in the Corporate Life & Pensions business and actively contribute to sustainable profitability?

In this role, at Zurich Switzerland, you will strengthen the Life in Team Collective Life division and make an important contribution to our joint success. As Senior Pricing Actuary Corporate Life & Pensions, you are responsible for the development and application of the collective living tariff and ensure that our solutions are optimally positioned in the field of tension between customer orientation, market dynamics and profitability. You work closely with underwriting, actuarial, product management and sales and bring your expertise strategically and operationally.

This is what awaits you

  • You develop the collective life tariff annually, accompany the internal acceptance process and coordinate the submission to FINMA.
  • You ensure the implementation, the correct implementation and the application of the inventory classification of the collective living tariff in the management system.
  • You actively control portfolio profitability and derive data-based measures for sustainable earnings improvement.
  • You will continuously develop pricing models, tools and processes in close cooperation with Underwriting and Life Performance & Analytics CLP.
  • You position yourself as a competent contact person and sparring partner for sales and management in pricing-relevant issues.

This sets you apart

  • University or technical college degree in a quantitative area
  • Ongoing or completed training for actuary SAV or Readiness to complete them
  • Good programming knowledge, ideally in R or Python, as well as experience in the further development of models and processes
  • Several years of experience in pricing collective life or high motivation to become deeply involved in this field
  • Excellent knowledge of German
