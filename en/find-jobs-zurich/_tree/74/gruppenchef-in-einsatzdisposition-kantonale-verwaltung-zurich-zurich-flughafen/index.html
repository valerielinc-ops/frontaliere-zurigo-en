Full description

The Cantonal Police and its approximately 4,000 employees assume responsibility for security in the Canton. For the airport police control department (FPKOA), which employs around 1000 employees, we are looking for a group manager / in operational disposition 100% for service planning. Your responsibilities:

  • Leading, promoting and developing employees as well as strengths of their professional and personal skills
  • Active participation in the reorientation of deployment disposition and development of efficient and future-oriented processes
  • Coach and accompany employees in change processes as well as promote personal responsibility and an open feedback and team culture
  • Maintaining a constructive cooperation with internal interfaces, the senior management and the responsible offices of Flughafen Zürich AG
  • Processing of specialist shops, participation in projects and working groups as well as further development of the department
  • Support the team if necessary by assuming operational tasks in the deployment disposition Your profile:
  • Proven leadership experience as well as pleasure in the promotion and development of employees
  • Affinity for designing change processes and optimizing workflows, ideally with appropriate practice
  • Enjoying the transfer of knowledge and the promotion of employees as well as good coaching and communication skills
  • Appreciative, binding and solution-oriented leadership style paired with high social skills and assertiveness
  • Organizational skill, analytical thinking skills and decision-making as well as an independent, structured and responsible working method
  • Familiarity with the operational processes at Zurich Airport and the operational disposition of advantages You can look forward to:

  • Attractive employment conditions at a secure employer
