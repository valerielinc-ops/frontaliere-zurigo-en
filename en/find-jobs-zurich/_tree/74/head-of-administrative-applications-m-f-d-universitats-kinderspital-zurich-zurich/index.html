Full description

Your tasks

  • The professional and personnel management of the Administrative Applications team with a focus on ERP and administrative systems is one of your core tasks.
  • You are in the further development of the application landscape in the area of ERP incl. involved in the design of the target architecture and possible successor solutions.
  • Your activities include the analysis, design and specification of requirements in cooperation with specialist areas (finance, HR, pharmacy, patient administration).
  • You will be responsible for planning and coordinating changes, releases, system introductions and upgrades in coordination with specialist areas and ICT.
  • Managing external partners and suppliers as well as ensuring stable and efficient application operations are among your activities.
  • By managing ICT projects in the area of administrative applications, you make a decisive contribution to corporate efficiency and have the opportunity to actively shape the digitization of central administrative processes in a committed environment. Your profile
  • Higher education in medical informatics, computer science or business informatics (HF, FH, university) or comparable qualification
  • Several years of professional experience with ERP systems in the healthcare sector and knowledge of administrative processes in hospitals
  • Good knowledge of healthcare integration standards (e.g. FHIR, HL7)
  • Experience in database queries (SQL) and understanding of data models
  • Experience in application management, requirements engineering and demand management
  • Profound experience as ICT project manager
  • Analytical and structured working method with holistic process understanding
  • High service and customer orientation in dealing with specialist areas
  • Communication strength and social competence in interdisciplinary environments
  • Ability to structure complex issues and implement solutions pragmatically
  • Ideally, management experience and a good understanding of how to successfully manage teams Christian Hammann Division Manager Business Applications / Stv.
