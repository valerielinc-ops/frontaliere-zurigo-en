Full description

The district court of Bülach employs about 90 employees and offers a centrally located work place in the immediate vicinity of the station in Bülach. For our commercial law firm we are looking for 1. August 2026 or by agreement an administrative secretary (female judge) (90-100%) Tasks

  • Pre-challenging and conducting the negotiating agenda in consultation with the competent judges and the party representatives
  • Compilation of proceedings
  • Maintaining and archiving the files
  • Making and sending decisions
  • Time-limits control
  • Together with other law firm employees take over the postal service
  • Interpreting ready to help in telephone service
  • Requirements
  • These varied and challenging tasks require a reliable, service-oriented and flexible personality.
  • You work very precisely and result-oriented and understand it excellently to manage your versatile everyday work independently and structured.
  • Good manners and discretion are a matter of course for you.
  • You have basic commercial training and preferably experience in the public administration or an equivalent professional qualification (e.g. working in a law firm).
  • Very good German knowledge in word and writing as well as good computer-user knowledge are required.
  • If you also keep quiet in hectic situations and keep track of the situation, we are happy to meet you.
  • We offer -
  • We offer you timely employment conditions, good social benefits and training opportunities within the context of the employment conditions of the canton of Zurich as well as a pleasant working environment in a collegial team. Contact
  • For further information please contact the head court clerk, Karin Scharding (tel. 044 863 44 33).
  • Please submit your application online with the usual documents and with your salary expectations until 21 June 2026.
  • An empty criminal record extract is a prerequisite and must be submitted before a job.
