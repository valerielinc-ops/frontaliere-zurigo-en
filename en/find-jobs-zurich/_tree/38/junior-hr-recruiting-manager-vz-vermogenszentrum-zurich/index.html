Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We advise individuals and companies about money, manage assets and offer attractive financial services. The recruiting team contributes significantly to the strong growth. That is why we are looking for reinforcements at our headquarters at Paradeplatz in Zurich.

  • Together with your team, you are responsible for the entire application management of the VZ Group and ensure the optimal filling of the vacancies in your specialist areas.
  • You accompany the line managers through the recruiting process, coordinate appointments, answer inquiries and maintain contact with external partners.
  • You conduct conversations with heart and mind and inspire applicants in your role as the most important contact person.
  • You help define the brand “VZ as an employer” and also represent our values and identity at university fairs and in webinars.
  • You are actively involved in projects and design a contemporary HR so that we can continue to grow strongly and continue to develop as an organization.
  • Completed studies and ideally first professional experience in recruiting or HR environment
  • Strong communication skills, target group-oriented and empathetic as well as a real feeling for people and an open, appreciative approach
  • A structured, reliable and independent working method as well as a high quality standard
  • Initiative, organizational talent and the ability to keep an eye on the overview and priorities even on several parallel topics
  • A dedicated recruiting team that supports and appreciates each other.
  • A team that, according to the largest industry study "best recruiters" belongs to the top 5 percent in Switzerland.
  • We are proud of a company culture that promotes creativity, supports innovative ideas and operates dynamically. We discuss our ideas directly with the CEO of VZ Holding.
  • A permanent workplace at our headquarters in the center of Zurich and the opportunity to work in the home office up to two days a week.
  • Appropriate education and training, because your development is important to us!
  • Anonymous surveys on satisfaction regularly confirm that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
