Full description

The Swiss police corps and the federal agencies responsible for police tasks have set up a public-law corporation «Polizeitechnik und -informatik (PTI) Schweiz» to jointly procure IT systems and technical products and gradually harmonize existing solutions. In order to strengthen our office PTI (13 persons) in Zurich, which is affiliated with the Cantonal Police of Zurich in terms of personnel law, we are looking for a professionally proven personality as a lawyer 80 - 100% tasks

  • Legal support for PTI projects in all project and operational phases, especially with regard to the legal bases of the Confederation and cantons, data protection and IT security (including ISDS, ADV) and the submission and contracting
  • Management or participation in the subprojects Law of PTI projects
  • Writing of model statements on consultations and support for the cantons in consultations of the Confederation on PTI topics
  • Advising and training the PTI committees and specialist groups on legal issues in the field of police technology, information technology and telecommunications.
  • Cooperation with related specialists from the police corps, the KKPKS, the data protection and public representatives of the Confederation and cantons, etc. Profile
  • Completed master's degree in law
  • Affinity to technology, IT and police required
  • Lawyer patent and experience in procurement and data protection
  • Relevant professional experience in related fields of activity for several years
  • Excellent knowledge of German and good knowledge of French (min. B2)
  • Resilient personality, accustomed to deadline pressure with a certain willingness to travel (support of all Swiss police corps)
  • Team-oriented, empathetic and assertive personality
  • Impeccable repute and stewardship: by agreement Have we aroused your interest? Then we look forward to your complete online application.
