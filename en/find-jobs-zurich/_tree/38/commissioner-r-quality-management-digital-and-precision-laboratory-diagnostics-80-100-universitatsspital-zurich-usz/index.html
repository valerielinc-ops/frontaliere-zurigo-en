USZ Campus

immediately or by agreement

In the newly created Institute for Digital and Precision Laboratory Diagnostics, you can expect an innovative and future-oriented working environment. At the interface of highly specialized diagnostics, digitalization and research, you have the opportunity to contribute your ideas and experiences and to actively shape the laboratory medicine of tomorrow. Are you the personality who breaks new ground with us and shapes the future of diagnostics?

Your main tasks

  • Maintenance, monitoring and further development of the quality management system in an ISO 15189 accredited laboratory, management and control of documents
  • Identification of optimization potential and continuous improvement, standardization and harmonization of work processes within the institute
  • Participation in the harmonization of the QM systems of the USZ laboratories, as part of the establishment of the future joint center for laboratory medicine
  • Planning, preparation and monitoring of internal and external audits and support in the implementation of the resulting measures
  • Evaluation of risks in preanalytics, analytics and postanalytics, analysis of errors, deviations and complaints, support in the development of solutions for nonconformities
  • Critical Incident Reporting System (CIRS)
  • Development of quality indicators and quality management reports
  • Support in the management of external quality controls
  • Collaboration with laboratory physicians: inside and biomedical analysts: inside. Training the laboratory team in QM topics and promoting quality awareness

Your profile

  • Completed training as biomed. Analyst: in HF, biomed. Laboratory diagnostician: in FH or natural science university studies with practical experience in a medical diagnostic laboratory
  • experience and/or training in quality management in the regulated environment,
  • You know the requirements for the quality of medical laboratory tests and ideally have experience with the standard ISO 15189
  • Excellent MS Office knowledge
  • Experience with laboratory and hospital computer systems is an advantage
  • You show your own initiative, take responsibility and are ready to develop personally and professionally
  • You convince with very good communication skills
  • You have very good knowledge of German, as well as good knowledge of English in word and writing
