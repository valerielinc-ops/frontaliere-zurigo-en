Full description

The Operations division assumes the central strategic and controlling role for the technological orientation and production of the SRG SSR.

In the “Control Operations” department, the Information Security team (under the direction of the CISO) is responsible for protecting the confidentiality, integrity and availability of all SRG SSR information.

This is your contribution.

In the InfoSec Advisory team, you are committed to the following topics:

  • You will anchor Security by Design in the SRG SSR by accompanying projects, products and initiatives in all areas of the company (e.g. technology, program, finance, HR) from idea to go-live and ensuring the implementation of security requirements
  • Make risks visible and controllable at an early stage by carrying out threat and risk analysis (e.g. threat modelling) and recommending pragmatic measures
  • You ensure secure cloud and solution architectures by examining architectures and taking on the role of a security architect for large, cross-divisional projects
  • You build confidence in our solutions by issuing the technical security sign-off, coordinating audits and pentests and tracking the implementation of the measures
  • You make security reusable by helping to develop standards, baselines and security patterns, coordinated with InfoSec GRC (Policies), Enterprise Architecture
  • You build cloud security as a competence in the SRG SSR by bringing your knowledge to the team, the InfoSec Guild and the Security Champions network

They inspire us.

  • Several years of experience in information security, ideally with a technical background in engineering or operation
  • In-depth expertise in cloud security (Azure and M365, ideally AWS), e.g. identities and accesses, landing zones, cloud architectures
  • Knowledge of Application Security and DevSecOps is an advantage
  • Ability to convey complex security issues in an understandable way and convince engineers and executives
  • Pragmatic, solution-oriented attitude: you secure without blocking
  • Very good knowledge in German or French and good oral and written knowledge of the other language, so that you are also actively involved in meetings
  • English for documentation
  • Certification such as CCSP, CISSP, CISM or an AWS/Azure security certification is an advantage

So we inspire you.

  • A lot of responsibility and creative leeway: They shape the development of the new InfoSec Advisory team, its services and competences in a sustainable way.
  • Diverse, exciting challenges in a company in transformation, with which you effectively support the public service of the SRG SSR
  • Targeted training and certification, tailored to your development plan
  • Flexible working models with home office and joint team days on site as well as employment conditions according to GAV SRG SSR

Please submit your application documents (motivation letter and CV) via the “Apply now” button. The deadline for submitting applications shall be 8. December 2026.
