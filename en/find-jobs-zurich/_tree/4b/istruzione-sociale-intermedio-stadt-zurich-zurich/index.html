Full description

Become part of our team! We are looking for a social pedagogue by 01.01.27 or by agreement 70%-80% (intermediate level)

Tasks

  • Participation in child care and leisure at intermediate level (3/4 + 5/6) in the day school model and holiday care
  • Implementation of socio-pedagogical expertise in day-school operations
  • Promotion and support of children in cooperation with teachers and other professionals of the school team
  • Participation in school development (working groups, etc.) and in school events
  • Implementation and further development of the school’s educational objectives

Profile

  • Completion in Social Education/Social Work FH/HF or equivalent training mandatory
  • Applications with a foreign diploma must be accompanied by a Swiss diploma recognition
  • Advantageous professional experience in supplementary school care
  • Experience in pedagogical and interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Motivated, open, resilient and humorous personality with a positive human image
  • Flexibility as well as very good team and communication skills
  • Joy in dealing with people of different cultures

We offer

  • The opportunity to take over the professional management of an innovative and committed support team
  • a varied, versatile and independent activity

  • A multicultural, exciting environment
  • Modern, generous infrastructure
  • Competent support from the management
  • a contemporary employment according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich
