Hirslanden stands for first-class, responsible and individual healthcare, which places the focus on people. From birth to old age, and from prevention to healing – at any time, physically and digitally – the Hirslanden Group, together with private and public cooperation partners, is focusing on the further development of the "Continuum of Care".

YOUR TASKS

  • Support and further development of SAP applications in the area of patients,
  • Active cooperation and possibility to take over the technical subproject management within our Group-wide strategy project for the standardization of SAP Healthcare (SAP IS-H) system landscape
  • Ensure operational application operation and further development of SAP healthcare and IS-H (patient management and billing, patient / case intake with SAP Fiori) in cooperation with customers
  • Coordination and provision of 2nd-level support and functional tests of the application
  • Systematic deposit of tariff and contractual requirements, as well as the creation of technical concepts and strong cooperation in the management of requirements
  • Co-development of technical concepts in cooperation with the specialist departments

YOUR PROFILE

  • Our job profile is mainly aimed at a technically interested specialist who has worked intensively with SAP IS-H, preferably as a power user, in recent years.
  • Completed commercial or computer science training with several years of experience in the hospital environment or proven experience from the health system with a focus on patients (D.A.), fee management and invoicing
  • Completed or started training as an IT specialist (HF) or FH
  • Several years of experience in SAP healthcare or IS-H or in-depth user experience as a power user of invoice & patient administration and administration.
  • Preferably (project) experience in the 177 and standardization of systems and processes
  • Very good knowledge of German and English and good oral knowledge of French
  • Solution-oriented and cross-process thinking and acting even in complex problems, as well as rapid understanding and high willingness to provide services
