Payment Specialist (80 - 100%)

Zurich, up to 40% Home Office possible, reference 8193

You want to support our team in SIC Operations and make an important contribution to the quality of the Swiss financial centre? Then we are happy to hear from you. Many changes in payment transactions must also be implemented by our participants (banks in Switzerland and throughout Europe). In this function you are the monitoring and start-up center for support requests regarding electronic payment transactions. You work in shift operations (Monday to Friday between 06.30am – 19.30pm) including piket operations (after 1 year) outside office hours.

You also have the opportunity to work in various projects. For this varied and exciting position we offer a hybrid work model (SIX Office & Home Office).

These are your tasks

  • 1st and 2nd level support for Swiss payments (SIC – including instant payments – and euroSIC)
  • Monitoring of payment systems (SIC/euroSIC/SWIFT) and technical connections
  • Management and maintenance of the participant master data
  • Coordination with the Swiss National Bank and the Swiss Euro Clearing Bank
  • Participation in specialist emergency exercises and cooperation in projects
  • Various administrative work

You bring this with you

  • Commercial basic training (commercial apprenticeship, trade school or equivalent training), experience in payment of benefits
  • ready to shift shift (Monday to Friday: between 06.30 – 19.30)
  • After about 1 year: ready for piket service outside office hours including weekend/feetage
  • A high degree of independence and helpfulness
  • Team of the players and joy of customer contact
  • Very good knowledge of MS Office (Words.2, Excel, Visio etc.)
  • Very good German and English skills in word and writing, French skills is a plus
