Full description

The distribution department ensures that SRF’s offerings are published on the linear radio and TV programs, as well as on the digital channels and platforms on which our target groups operate – with the right mix of topics, in the right form, at the right time, on the right scale, with the right brand – supported by digitally oriented marketing.

What are your tasks?

You are engaged from 1. January 2027 or by agreement for 10 months for the following topics:

  • Strategically develop SRF’s social media presence and shape its positioning on all relevant platforms
  • They observe trends, platform developments and best practices and derive recommendations and measures from them.
  • They are responsible for cross-channel governance and ensure that policies, overviews and foundations remain current and effective.
  • You are the contact person for editors, departments, platform partners and the SRG

What do you bring?

  • You have several years of experience in social media management, ideally in a media company
  • You think strategically, work in a structured way and keep an overview even in complex organizations
  • They bring curiosity for digital trends, a good sense of audience needs and a high level of initiative.
  • They know how to bring together different stakeholders, develop viable solutions and jointly advance them.

What can you look forward to?

  • They actively shape the presence of one of the largest media brands in Switzerland on social media and provide impulses for their further development
  • They work at the interface of journalism, audience and platforms and operate in a dynamic digital environment.
  • They network with committed colleagues from editorial, audience and channel management and jointly develop forward-looking solutions
  • You get a lot of creative freedom and the opportunity to shape the digital future of SRF

What we offer

What does it take to reach the people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expert knowledge, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can contribute and develop. For the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: Welcome to the world of SRF!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, promote creativity and offer a lot of design space for new and high-quality products.
  • Shaping digitalization: Quality journalism is also digital for us. That’s why we explore new possibilities and experiment with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-medial. We design on the pulse of time and on the nerve of the audience.
  • Being human: We meet each other collegially, work independently and shape our working atmosphere individually. Our success is based on the personal strengths as well as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programs. In doing so, we act respectfully and independently; in this way we promote opinion-forming and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.

  • Sie verfügen über mehrjährige Erfahrung im Social-Media-Management, idealerweise in einem Medienunternehmen

  • Sie denken strategisch, arbeiten strukturiert und behalten auch in komplexen Organisationen den Überblick

  • Sie bringen Neugier für digitale Trends, ein gutes Gespür für Publikumsbedürfnisse und eine hohe Eigeninitiative mit

  • Sie verstehen es, unterschiedliche Anspruchsgruppen zusammenzubringen, tragfähige Lösungen zu entwickeln und gemeinsam voranzutreiben
