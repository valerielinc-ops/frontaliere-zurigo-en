Full description

NVIDIA is looking for a Startup Partner Manager to build strategic partnerships with AI-native companies, from early-stage startups to unicorns. You will work directly with founders, CTOs and engineering leaders to understand their technology and business ambitions, accelerate adoption of NVIDIA’s platforms, and support their growth. This role combines technical understanding, ecosystem development and ownership of a strategic portfolio.

What you’ll be doing

  • Identify and engage high-potential AI-native companies, assessing their technical differentiation, growth potential and opportunities for collaboration with NVIDIA.
  • Build trusted relationships with founders and technical leaders, understanding their product roadmaps, architectures, workloads and computing requirements as they scale.
  • Drive adoption of NVIDIA hardware, software, libraries and developer platforms, working with Developer Relations, Solutions Architects and product teams to advance technical evaluations, integrations and production deployments.
  • Develop and implement engagement plans with NVIDIA account teams, establishing clear priorities, responsibilities and achievements tailored to each company’s stage and needs.
  • Build relationships with venture capital firms, accelerators and ecosystem partners, employing NVIDIA Startup and the VC Alliance to identify and support promising AI-native companies.
  • Accelerate portfolio growth through relevant infrastructure partnerships, customer introductions and joint go-to-market opportunities, collaborating with NVIDIA’s sales, partner and marketing teams.
  • Translate insights from the portfolio into actionable feedback on emerging technologies, workloads and customer requirements for NVIDIA’s product and business teams.
  • Track outcomes across software adoption, deployed workloads, platform expansion and business opportunities.

What we need to see

  • A technical degree or equivalent experience in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering or a related field.
  • 5+ years' experience demonstrating success in startup partnerships, technical business development, ecosystem development, venture capital or a leadership role at a technology company.
  • Strong understanding of the AI-native ecosystem and the technical and commercial challenges companies face from early development through rapid growth.
  • Working knowledge of AI training and inference, GPU-accelerated computing and modern AI architectures, with the ability to engage credibly with CTOs and engineering leaders.
  • A track record of turning partnerships into measurable outcomes and coordinating technical and commercial teams.
  • Excellent relationship-building, communication and organisational skills, with the judgment and initiative to handle a strategic portfolio independently.

Ways to stand out from the crowd

  • Established relationships with AI-native founders, technical leaders and investors.
  • Experience supporting companies from initial product development through production deployment and international expansion.
  • Familiarity with NVIDIA’s software stack and practical experience supporting platform adoption.
  • Experience building a startup or developing partnerships within an AI infrastructure, cloud or developer-platform business.
  • Strong analytical skills and an evidence-led approach to identifying opportunities and prioritizing engagements.
