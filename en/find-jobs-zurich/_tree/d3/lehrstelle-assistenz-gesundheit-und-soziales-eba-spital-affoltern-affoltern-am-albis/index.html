Full description

As an assistant in Health and Social EBA, you are part of a care team in acute care. You nurture and care for patients in hospital everyday life and accompany them on their way to recovery. For example, you support them in personal care (shower, bath, toilet), help with dressing, shaving or hairdressing. It is important that you can build a trust relationship with the people you care for. As an assistant in health and social EBA in training, you are also responsible for administrative and logistical tasks. Your profile

  • Compulsory schooling completed
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Communion, good manners
  • Patience and respect
  • Empathy and helpfulness
  • Good observation ability, fast reaction ability
  • Careful and accurate working methods
  • A sense of cleanliness and order
  • good physical condition, psychological resilience
  • Flexibility: Willingness to work irregularly Our culture - Your benefits "Bi öis can be no": With us it is family and personal.
