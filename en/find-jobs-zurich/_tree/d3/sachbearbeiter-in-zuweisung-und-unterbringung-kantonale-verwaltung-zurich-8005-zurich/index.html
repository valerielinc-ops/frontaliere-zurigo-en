Canton of Zurich Security Directorate Social Security Officer/in Allocation and Accommodation 100% The Cantonal Social Office with more than 280 employees performs a wide range of steering and operational tasks focusing on social assistance, social insurance, social services and asylum at the interface between the Confederation, other cantons, cities, municipalities and various private organizations. It also runs its own institution, tilia, based in Rheinau. For the department of asylum coordination we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by arrangement temporary for 12 months , an assistant as clerk/in assignment and accommodation, 100%. Their main tasks

  • They ensure the correct processing of mutation reports and case conclusions from the municipalities and cantonal accommodation.
  • They examine and process asylum decisions and ensure that they are forwarded to the authorities responsible for the cases.
  • They carry out stock comparisons with the municipalities.
  • They ensure that the upcoming adaptations to the protection status S are correctly processed in the case management system.
  • They perform special tasks such as quantitative surveys for statistics or develop and document processes.
  • They support the specialists in their daily work and can take on deputies if necessary.
  • They advise the municipality and accommodation on telephone and written inquiries. Your skills and talents
  • You have completed a commercial apprenticeship and have experience in administrative processing, ideally in the area of asylum/migration.
  • You are an administrative and organizational talent that can quickly find its way into changing starting positions and new tasks.
  • They enjoy working independently and are interested in the complex political issues. They have very good oral and written knowledge of German (at least B2). Knowledge of languages spoken by the current refugee groups is an advantage.
  • They have in-depth MS-Office knowledge and actively communicate. They are happy to exchange information with different stakeholders by telephone, e-mail and at the counter. If you recognize challenges as an opportunity and diversity as an enrichment, you will feel comfortable with us. They will be able to contribute and work.
