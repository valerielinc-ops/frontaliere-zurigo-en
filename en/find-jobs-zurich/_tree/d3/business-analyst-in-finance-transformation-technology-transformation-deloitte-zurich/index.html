Location : Zurich Work Pattern Percentage : 100% Start date : ongoing Finding the talent that makes Deloitte unique How you can make an impact

  • Support experienced Business Finance team in designing and implementing planning, budgeting, forecasting, management reporting and financial consolidation processes and solutions.
  • Apply knowledge gained from academic studies or prior internships in finance, accounting, or related fields, with a solid understanding of financial statements such as profit & loss and balance sheet.
  • Demonstrate a strong interest for technology, with the willingness to learn and apply technologies such as SAP, Anaplan, Pigment, Tagetik or OneStream to enhance business finance processes.
  • Contribute to client projects by identifying opportunities to optimise financial planning and reporting workflows, driven by a passion for enhancing business finance operations. What you bring to succeed
  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Finance, Business Administration or a relevant field
  • You are reliable, willing to take on responsibility and happy to work independently.
  • Ability to communicate confidently in German and English. French would be an advantage.
  • Able to conduct analyses, take part in workshops, and prepare documents and presentations.
