Full Stack Software Engineer – Discovery Services (m/f/d)

80%, Zurich, perpetual

The ETH Library promotes knowledge, supports research and teaching and, as a trustworthy institution, makes the world of yesterday, today and tomorrow easier for you to understand. Our team Discovery and API Management in the Digital Business Solutions group develops and supervises the necessary systems.

To 1. March 2027 or by agreement we are looking for a:n Full Stack Software Engineer – Discovery Services (m/f/d) with an 80% effort.

Job description

We are looking for an experienced and committed personality who will assume technical responsibility for two central services in a small team: the search portal «ETH swisscovery» and the Resource Information Bus, the API hub of the ETH Library.

Your tasks

  • Further develop the search portal: You develop ETH-specific components of our search portal with Angular and TypeScript. One focus is the introduction of the new platform generation Primo Next Discovery Experience (NDE) in 2027, with adjustments for the library and research view. You will integrate manufacturer releases, coordinate technical adjustments with the national library network SLSP and manage the existing AngularJS solution until it is replaced in the summer of 2027.
  • Design backend and interfaces: You operate and expand the Resource Information Bus based on Node.js and Express. You develop REST APIs, integrate internal and external data sources and work with the system administration to ensure reliable operation of the application (Linux).
  • Responsible for technical solutions: You analyze requirements, assess their feasibility and plan the implementation together with the product owner and the team management. You advise specialist bodies and represent our technical interests vis-à-vis external partners and system providers.
  • Introducing new technologies: You will participate in projects to further develop digital library services, especially in the field of AI-enabled information retrieval and automation.

Profile

  • University or university degree in computer science, business informatics or an equivalent qualification with corresponding practical experience
  • Several years of experience in full-stack web development with in-depth knowledge of JavaScript/TypeScript, Node.js and REST APIs
  • Experience with modern frontend frameworks, preferably Angular
  • Secure handling of Git-based development workflows and basic Linux skills for supporting Node.js applications
  • Experience in the meaningful and critical use of AI-supported development tools
  • Independent, structured working method, initiative and pleasure in working together in a small team
  • Good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • Knowledge of automated tests, Docker and cloud technologies (Google) and experience with library or discovery systems are beneficial
  • Interest in AI-based applications such as LLMs, RAGs or vector databases

We offer

  • Technical responsibility for central digital services of the ETH Library with a lot of room for manoeuvre and short decision paths
  • A versatile environment at the interface of software development, scientific information and digital transformation with space for new ideas and technologies
  • Flexible annual working hours and the possibility of a partial home office, as well as a workplace in the heart of Zurich
  • Targeted training opportunities
  • Cultural diversity as well as numerous benefits, such as public transport subscriptions and car sharing, a wide range of sports activities offered by the ASVZ, child care and attractive pension services
