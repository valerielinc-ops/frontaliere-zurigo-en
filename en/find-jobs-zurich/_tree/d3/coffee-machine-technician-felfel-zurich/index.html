Full description

IN SHORT

Help keep great coffee flowing at our customers’ workplaces. Join FELFEL as our Coffee Machine Technician (100%) based in Zurich, to install, service and fix Gavetti coffee machines: In the workshop, at customer sites and on the service desk.

YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Rotate with our Gavetti technician team between the workshop (assembly and maintenance), the field (incident response on the road, at customer sites and at events) and ticketing.
  • Install, remove and relocate Gavetti coffee machines at customer sites.
  • Prepare each installation by completing the checklist, flushing water filters and loading the equipment.
  • Set up refurbished coffee machines and check they’re ready for installation.
  • Carry out annual servicing at customer sites.
  • Respond to incidents on site and get machines back up and running quickly.
  • Handle incoming service tickets: diagnose issues, respond quickly and resolve them remotely or on site.
  • Help set up coffee equipment at events.

YOUR PROFILE

  • Hands-on experience servicing coffee machines or comparable electromechanical equipment in the field.
  • A completed vocational qualification in electrical installation, electrical engineering or a comparable technical field.
  • Practical experience diagnosing and resolving electromechanical faults.
  • Nice to have: Experience with Eversys machines or connected devices (IoT), or barista skills.
  • A valid category B driving licence and willingness to travel to customer sites.
  • Flexibility with working hours, including early starts – some customer sites start their day at 6–7am and Gavetti issues need a fast response.
  • A structured, quality-focused approach to working independently and a professional manner with customers.
  • Good to fluent German and strong communication skills in English.
  • Non-smoker - we don't want you to smell like cigarettes, neither at work nor at customer meetings. After all, FELFEL is about good food.
  • Valid work permit or Swiss passport

OUR BENEFITS

  • Lots of delicious food, just a few steps away from your desk - we make your gastronomic dreams come true
  • Best barista coffee and tea for the whole team, thanks to our innovative Gavetti coffee machine
  • 5 weeks holiday per year, as well as the chance to buy an extra week of vacation
  • We care about your future with an above-mandatory pension fund
  • New parents get 16 or 3 weeks, respectively, of fully paid parental leave. We also support the return to work by making a generous financial contribution to childcare until the child reaches kindergarten age.
  • We encourage our employees to use public transport by covering your half-fare travelcard
  • Great team events, such as our well-known FELFEL Summer Outing
  • Stay active and healthy with our weekly sports sessions

ABOUT US

FELFEL is all about good food and coffee in the workplace: we help Swiss employers become masters of hospitality. With our smart fridge ‘FELFEL’ and delicious barista coffee ‘Gavetti’, we enable companies to provide their employees with first-class, ultra-fresh food and drinks throughout the day. Whether hearty and traditional, modern and vegan or high in protein - our weekly changing menu ensures that all employees always have something to be excited about ;). FELFEL is a family business that was founded in 2013. Today, we take care of around 1000 locations in Switzerland, and have 150 employees across our offices in Lausanne, Zurich and New York. www.felfel.ch
