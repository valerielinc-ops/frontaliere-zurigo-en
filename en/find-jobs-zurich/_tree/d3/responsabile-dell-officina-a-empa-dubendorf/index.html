Full description

Materials science and technology are our passion. With our cutting-edge research, Empa’s approximately 1,100 employees make decisive contributions to the benefit of society for a future worth living in. Empa is a research institution of the ETH Domain.

Workshop manager (a)

Technology, organization, responsibility and training – shape the future of our workshop with us. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed and responsible personality who brings her technical expertise, enjoys organizing and enjoys working in an exciting research environment.

Their tasks

  • Management of the workshop, acceptance, planning, coordination and monitoring of production contracts and ensuring professional and timely execution in close cooperation with their deputy
  • Award of production contracts to Empa-Werkstatt SG and external contractors
  • Advising internal customers on manufacturing issues
  • Perspective assumption of responsibility for the training of polymechanics EFZ

Your profile

You have completed training as a polymechanic / EFZ as well as further training as a production specialist / FA or production manager / in industry HFP. They bring in-depth CAM and CNC knowledge, organizational talent, flexibility and resilience. An independent, solution-oriented working method, pronounced service orientation and pleasure in teamwork distinguish you. You are also enthusiastic about training young professionals.

Our offer

We offer you attractive framework conditions, a versatile area of responsibility and space for personal initiative and further development. After a comprehensive introduction, you can fully contribute your expertise in the exciting research environment, take on responsibility and actively shape the development of our workshop. Do you want to use your skills in a versatile, responsible role? Then we look forward to meeting you.
