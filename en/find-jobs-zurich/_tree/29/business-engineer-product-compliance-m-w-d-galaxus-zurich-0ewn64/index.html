Full description

We are looking for a Business Engineer Product Compliance to optimize and further develop our Compliance & Gatekeeping tools. You act as an interface between various stakeholders such as product owners from development, other business engineers, operational compliance or portfolio managers from Switzerland, Germany and Serbia.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Headquarters, Pfingstweidstrasse 60b, 8005 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Business requirements for optimization, innovation and development around product compliance & sustainability are included, questioned and structured into individual projects
  • Evaluations, research and concept analyses on compliance topics are part of your everyday life – from this you derive potential for further development
  • You advance the roadmap and innovation projects for product compliance from the business side and prioritize them together with the Head of Platform and the Product Owners
  • Closely with our data scientists, you can create simple monitoring and controlling for the performance of our compliance tools
  • As an interface between engineering teams and category management, you ensure a smooth exchange in your topic area.
  • Our Compliance & Sustainability Managers train you in the use of new tools, support you with questions and ensure seamless documentation
  • Stakeholder management is in your hands: you involve the relevant stakeholders and convince with clear communication and expertise
  • Beyond your own area of responsibility, you promote the exchange of knowledge among business engineers

Requirements

  • More than five years of relevant professional experience: as a business engineer, product owner, e-commerce category manager, compliance manager or in similar positions
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): in business informatics, business administration or a similar field of study
  • Quick comprehension and very strong analytical skills set you apart, and you work hard with tools like Excel, AI or Tableau. SQL experience is a great advantage
  • You have already successfully managed agile projects, from conception to implementation
  • It is easy for you to abstract complexity, bring different stakeholders and opinions together, win for a common goal and focus on implementing it.
  • Accessible in appearance, efficient and clear in communication
  • Enthusiasm, curiosity and a cooperative way round off your profile
  • Self-responsible, structured and reliable work in an ambitious, energetic team is fun for you – because we are not lone fighters
  • The willingness to question “as it has always been done” and propose more efficient, automated or effective solutions
  • Using creativity and initiative to solve problems and achieve goals
  • German (flowing)
  • English (flowing)
