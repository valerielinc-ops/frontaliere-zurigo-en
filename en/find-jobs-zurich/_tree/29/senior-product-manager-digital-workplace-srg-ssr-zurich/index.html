Full description

The Operations division is responsible for the technical production of all content of the SSR as well as for their technical processing and archiving (TV, radio and multimedia). Within SSR, the PM Digital Workplace (or Head of Digital Workplace) provides employees with a modern, secure and reliable digital work environment. This includes Windows and Apple devices (HW), Microsoft 365 services, collaboration tools and the intranet, as well as process automation and artificial intelligence-based solutions.

This is your contribution.

Within the IT portfolio, as an open and curious personality, you assume responsibility for the strategic development and the successful implementation of all projects relating to the product Digital Workplace:

  • Strategy and implementation responsibility: You define the product vision and the long-term roadmap and ensure the consistent and targeted implementation of complex initiatives in the digital workplace, from the definition of architecture and solutions to the successful company-wide rollout.
  • End-to-end lifecycle: You control the entire product lifecycle actively and data-based: from the demanding evaluation and technical implementation of new solutions to stable and efficient operation, to large-scale replacements and migrations, including the transition to Intune.
  • Professional leadership and orchestration: You take over the professional leadership of the product owners, support the implementation teams in their independence and coordinate the close cooperation with the personnel management operations, the business partners and the central stakeholders across all company units.
  • Financial and supplier management: You are responsible for the product budget, control suppliers, partners and external services in terms of result quality and ensure the achievement of economic goals.
  • Organizational development: They drive the transformation towards a modern, agile and product-oriented organization and anchor sustainable working methods in the implementation.

They inspire us.

  • Proven experience: You have several years of proven practical experience (at least 5 years) in senior product management or overall responsibility for complex IT and digital workplace implementations in large, cross-location organizations. A successful migration to Intune is a real plus.
  • Strong implementation competence and methodology: You have profound experience in agile product and portfolio management (e.g. SAFe, Scrum) and have already successfully proven that you can translate strategic roadmaps into concrete and sustainable solutions.
  • In-depth technical and architectural understanding: You have a broad and solid technical understanding of the entire digital workplace. These include Modern Endpoint Management (Windows/Apple, MDM/MAM, Packaging) and the Microsoft 365 ecosystem (collaboration, intranet, provisioning, automation and self-service solutions, and AI integrations).
  • Innovative power and UX orientation: They combine a consistent focus on a smooth and needs-oriented user experience (UX) with a high level of innovation and a strong sense of technology trends and digital innovations.
  • Control and financial competence: You have proven experience in budget responsibility as well as in the management of costs and product quality on the basis of relevant KPIs and in the preparation of sound business cases and cost-benefit analyses.
  • Leadership and communication: You convince through assertiveness, a secure presence at management level as well as strong moderation skills and manage to successfully balance different stakeholder interests.
  • Languages: Very good knowledge of a national language, supplemented by good knowledge of German or French (at least level B1) and English at least level B1/B2.

Our promise

What does it take to reach the people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expertise, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can contribute and develop. For the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: Welcome to the world of SRG!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, promote creativity and offer a lot of design space for new and high-quality products.
  • Shaping digitalization: Quality journalism is also digital for us. That’s why we explore new possibilities and experiment with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-medial. We design on the pulse of time and on the nerve of the audience.
  • Being human: We meet each other collegially, work independently and shape our working atmosphere individually. Our success is based on the personal strengths as well as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programs. In doing so, we act respectfully and independently; in this way we promote opinion-forming and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
