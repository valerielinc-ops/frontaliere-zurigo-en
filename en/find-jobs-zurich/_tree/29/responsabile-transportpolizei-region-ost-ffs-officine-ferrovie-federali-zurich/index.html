Head: in Transport Police Region East 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Head: in Transport Police Region East Zurich / Work Smart, 01.06.2027 or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

An exciting, versatile and responsible leadership task awaits you in Switzerland’s youngest police corps. Together with a dedicated team, you contribute to public transport safety every day and shape the future of the transport police. You work in a dynamic environment with a wide range of internal and external partners and have the opportunity to play a decisive role in shaping the further development of the transport police force and drive sustainable changes. You can move that.

  • In Transport Police Region East, you lead the region in personnel, professional and financial terms and are responsible for three bases with around 45 employees.
  • You plan, control and coordinate missions and actions in your area of responsibility, lead police forces at major events and are ready to perform regular leadership in the pikett.
  • As a member of the management team, you advise the commander of the transport police in police tactical and strategic matters, actively shape the further development of the transport police and contribute to the successful implementation of the strategy.
  • You create concepts, operational commands and operational documents, carry out situation assessments and develop needs-based operational devices.
  • You will maintain and develop cooperation with internal and external bodies (SBB, police corps, offices and authorities) and represent the interests of the transport police.

You bring that with you.

  • You are a flexible, resilient and assertive leader with high social skills and a strong sense of responsibility.
  • Thanks to your many years of leadership experience, you lead employees goal-oriented and promote a motivating and performance-oriented cooperation with your integrative leadership style.
  • In the police world, you are well connected and follow current developments in security police issues with interest and foresight.
  • You have pronounced planning and organizational skills as well as in-depth police tactical knowledge and bring experience as operational manager: in large and special events.
  • You have a recognized training in the police environment. Ideally, you have already completed the CAS FIP (Guide in Police Operations, SPI) or are ready to complete this training.
  • For cooperation in our national environment, you have very good German skills in word and writing (level C2) and good French skills (at least level B1).

Your rewards and benefits.

We are actively committed to equal pay. This employment takes place in accordance with bond law, taking into account a comparison with the team, the market and the industry.
