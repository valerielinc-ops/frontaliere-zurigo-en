SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. As team leader: in Reisezentrum Altstetten you will lead up to 7 customer consultants and will take over the deputy head of Reisezentrum Altstetten/Enge. You like a lively company, the leadership and development of your employees and seize the opportunity for your next development step. Our 14-member team is looking forward to you.What makes the role exciting: You do not have to bring leadership experience yet. Much more important is your interest in personnel leadership, your motivation and your will to develop yourself.The stage is also possible in co-management. You can move that.

  • As a team leader, you lead customer advisors and together with your motivated team ensure the advice, sales and support of customers across all channels and along the entire mobility chain.
  • You share responsibility for the agreed financial, productivity and quality goals (e.g. income, costs and customer satisfaction) and support the achievement of the goals.
  • You continuously tailor the organization of the travel center to the needs and are responsible for the always optimal and economical personnel deployment.
  • With the right decisions, your drive and your strategic skills, you lead, challenge and develop your team so that you can make a difference.

You bring that with you.

  • Thanks to your willingness to change and the joy of the digital world, you lead your area with a lot of social skills to sustainable success.
  • Ideally, you have already completed a further training in the field of leadership / sales / business administration or are motivated to tackle it.
  • You have a 3-year apprenticeship and some years of experience as a customer consultant: in public transport.
  • In daily contact with our customers, your communicative and winning nature is the key to success. We assume that you have the location language under control (C2) and have knowledge of a second national language as well as English at level B1.
