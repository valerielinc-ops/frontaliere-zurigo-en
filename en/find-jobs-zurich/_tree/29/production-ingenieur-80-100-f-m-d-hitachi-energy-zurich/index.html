The opportunity

At Hitachi Energy, we are advancing a sustainable energy future for all. Join our GIS Development team within the High Voltage Products Business Unit in Zurich-Oerlikon. You will be part of an international R&D team developing next-generation gas-insulated switchgear technologies for high-voltage applications. The team combines experienced specialists and developing engineers. We are now looking for a senior engineer who can strengthen our technical expertise and take ownership of complex engineering challenges. If you enjoy solving complex technical problems, working in an international environment, and driving technology advancements, this role offers excellent opportunities to develop your expertise and influence future product innovations.

How you'll make an impact

  • Deliver R&D activities for high-voltage product development
  • Support the development of innovative eco-gas solutions
  • Lead technical investigations and root cause analyses
  • Drive product improvement and reliability initiatives
  • Plan, support, and evaluate testing and validation activities
  • Collaborate with global laboratories and external suppliers
  • Provide technical support for existing products and applications
  • Share knowledge and contribute to technical excellence across teams

Your background

  • Master's degree or PhD in Electrical Engineering or a related field
  • Minimum five years of industry experience in engineering or R&D
  • Strong technical expertise in electrical engineerin
  • Experience working in an industrial product development environment
  • Proven ability to work with multiple stakeholders
  • Strong communication skills and fluent English
