Full description

In the area of education | development, the Department of Education Management is responsible for the urban education offer with around 500 seminars and 8000 participants every year.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for an experienced personality immediately or by agreement.

  • In cooperation with the specialist responsible bodies, you will assess overall urban education needs, control the further development and prioritization of the education portfolio and develop sound decision-making bases.
  • They assume central process responsibility for the overall city goal agreement and assessment interview and create comprehensible process documentation.
  • In cooperation with the communications department, you will further develop educational marketing and internal educational communication, design target group-oriented communication measures and campaigns to promote the learning culture.
  • They identify and prioritize digitalization potential and accompany the introduction of digital and AI-based solutions.
  • They advise executives and departments, coordinate stakeholders with different interests and lead or accompany cross-divisional projects for the strategic and procedural further development of education management.
  • University of Applied Sciences degree in business administration, educational management or equivalent education
  • Several years of professional experience in the field of education management, digitalization and modern learning media
  • Ability to listen to professionals, identify needs, prioritize and provide the basis for decision-making
  • High willingness to provide services and understanding in dealing with different stakeholder groups, coupled with a solution-, implementation- and process-oriented working method

A demanding, independent and varied activity in a dedicated team in the diverse environment of the city of Zurich.

Employees benefit from attractive employment conditions, flexible forms of work such as mobile working time models with up to 50% home office and a targeted education and training offer. Our workplaces are accessible, centrally located and easily accessible by public transport

  • Equality, diversity and inclusion are lived with us.

As a central service and competence center, the City of Zurich Human Resources Management (HRZ) is responsible for overarching personnel issues for the approximately 50 municipal companies and service departments with over 34,000 employees.
