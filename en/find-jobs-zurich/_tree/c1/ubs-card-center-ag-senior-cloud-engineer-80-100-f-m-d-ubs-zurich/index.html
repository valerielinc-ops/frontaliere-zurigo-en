Full description

Key responsibilities

  • Implementation of cloud infrastructure for SaaS & PaaS usage according to regulatory requirements and industry best practices
  • Shape and implement the hybrid Cloud platform architecture blueprint including identity management, observability and security for Azure and AWS landing zones
  • Establish best practise for automating the provisioning of Cloud resources and workloads including day-2 operations
  • Implement PoCs, maintain technical documentation and support application developers to get to the Cloud

The team As part of the new "Cloud Center of Excellence" team in the IT Ressort of UBS Card Center AG you will shape our journey to the Cloud by defining and implementing technical blueprints.

Your skills and experience

  • University degree in (Business) Informatics or similar - or comparable higher education (University of Applied Sciences)
  • Strong quality and responsibility awareness and excellent communication skills (English, German would be a plus)
  • Hands-on experience in operating Cloud applications in a production environment (Azure/AWS)
  • Familiar with concepts like Landing Zones, GitOps, DevSecOps, Infrastructure as Code, Access Management, Observability (and versed in using corresponding products)
  • Understanding of the high regulatory requirements in the banking/credit card environment (PCI/DSS, Data Residency)
