Full description

80-100 % Winterthur / Smart Working

Shape your future – together with us!

You combine sound cost controlling with enjoyment of data, AI and clear communication? You make complex cost developments understandable, create fact-based decision-making bases and develop controlling together with our stakeholders. Then become part of our Strategy & Controlling sector!

Around 40 people work in our sector, including around 10 in controlling distribution. You enjoy a wide range of topics, enjoy moving in the world of numbers and transformation and are looking for a dynamic, motivated environment? Then we look forward to you.

Become a part of our distribution. Our area is the window to our customers. Every day, we are committed to meeting customer needs in the best possible way through proactive, progressive and personal consulting solutions – in line with our most important corporate value “customer first”.

This is what awaits you

  • You are equally responsible for controlling the administrative costs and the commission costs in the Distribution department. You coordinate budgeting, projections and quarterly financial statements as well as annual and strategic planning, including setting cost targets per line of business.
  • You keep an eye on the cost and resource development (RTD/HC) of the entire department and communicate deviations early and appropriate to the addressee. You also manage the cost center structure and the cost keys of the distribution department.
  • You work closely with the distribution sectors as well as the Finance, Provision and P&C departments, work out the plans with them and develop cost controlling in terms of content and methodology – especially with regard to automation, planning and tools.
  • You are a sparring partner for our stakeholders: you question cost developments, planning assumptions and proposals constructively, identify potential challenges at an early stage with a forward-looking view, identify opportunities for action and work together with them to develop viable solutions. With clear analyses and understandable recommendations, you create a sound basis for decisions.
  • Together with the team, you will identify useful applications for AI and automation in cost controlling, for example in analyses, comments and reporting. You check the benefits and quality of the results, taking into account data protection and internal requirements.

You bring that with you

  • You will bring several years of professional experience in controlling, ideally in cost controlling, as well as strong conceptual, analytical and quantitative skills. Experience in financial accounting is desired.
  • You communicate clearly, convincingly and appropriately. You translate complex financial contexts into understandable statements and concrete possibilities for action, present your analyses confidently and constructively address deviations and different perspectives.
  • You have excellent Microsoft Office skills, especially in Excel, and experience with analytics and reporting tools, such as Power BI or SAP Analytics Cloud. Knowledge of Google Cloud Platform and programming skills are beneficial.
  • You have first practical experience with AI-supported applications and recognize meaningful application possibilities in controlling. You critically question results and want to further develop your knowledge.
  • You are strong in solving problems and convince through holistic, networked thinking and action. You are ready to participate beyond your area of responsibility and to help shape optimal solutions in the sense of OneAXA.
