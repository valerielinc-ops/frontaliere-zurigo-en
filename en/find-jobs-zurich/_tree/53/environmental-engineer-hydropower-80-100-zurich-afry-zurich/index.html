Become part of our HYDRO POWER team. Our area of expertise lies in the planning and implementation of hydroelectric power plants with the best possible preservation of natural habitats and the satisfaction of increasing safety needs.

Take on responsibility with us as

ENVIRONMENTAL ENGINEER HYDROPOWER (80-100%) - ZURICH YOUR TASKS

  • Environmental impact assessments for hydropower plants together with an interdisciplinary team for international projects
  • Investigation and definition of residual water volumes based on ecological and social ranking conditions
  • Hydraulic calculations for residual water studies
  • Collaboration in the design of fish bypasses
  • Water management and hydrological studies
  • Preparation of technical reports and presentations
  • Collaboration for offers

YOUR PROFILE

  • Completed studies as an environmental engineer at ETH or TU with a specialization in hydraulic engineering or hydrology with a very good degree certificate
  • PhD degree is an advantage
  • Fluent in German and English, other language skills (especially French) are an advantage
  • Customer-oriented and team-oriented personality with a high sense of quality and responsibility
  • Willingness and enjoyment of worldwide assignments abroad
