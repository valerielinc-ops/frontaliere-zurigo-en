Full description

We are looking for reinforcement immediately or by agreement.

They work with a motivated team of three hoarding directors, three specialists, a cook and four school assistants three days a week (Mo, Do and Fr) from 10:00 to 13:30 in the midday care for secondary students. The work assignments take place only during school hours, since the lunchtime care is closed during the 13 school holiday weeks. If desired, the days Monday, Tuesday and Thursday might also be possible.

Other tasks

  • Carrying the midday hoard and ensuring day-to-day operations
  • Care of 50-70 secondary students at lunch and in the various leisure activities during lunchtime
  • If necessary, support for care assistants in the school kitchen and in domestic tasks
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with the school team
  • Contacts with parents
  • You have a degree in social work, social pedagogy (FH/HF) or equivalent training at tertiary level in the pedagogical field.
  • As a specialist care you have a professional qualification as a specialist care or infant education*
  • You enjoy working with secondary school youth
  • They are flexible, resilient and keep an overview
  • They enjoy working in a team and appreciate working with teachers and parents
  • You are administratively experienced and quickly integrate into new areas of responsibility
