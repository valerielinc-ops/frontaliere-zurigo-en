Full description

When a mental crisis or illness makes everyday life a challenge, Spitex Zurich supports the affected people. Through targeted advice and support, they are gradually enabled to structure their everyday life as independently as possible and to deal with the effects of their psychological situation. Promoting self-determination and quality of life – that is our goal. We want an open and open personality. In psychiatric care, you make decisions on site, can work flexibly and solution-oriented and have a strong and diverse team behind you.

(a)lle are addressed. Gender, origin, age? We are interested in your qualification and enthusiasm for work. Working days: Monday – Friday

  • Resource and goal-oriented care and care of adults of all ages

  • Responsibility for careful and seamless management of care documentation
