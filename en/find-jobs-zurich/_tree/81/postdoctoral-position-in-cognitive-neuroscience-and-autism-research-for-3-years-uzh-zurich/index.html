Full description

Department of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

Start of employment March 2027 (or by agreement), temporary

The Neurodiversity and Cognition Group of the Department of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Psychotherapy (PUK - University Hospital of Psychiatry Zurich) invites applications for a three-year Postdoctoral Researcher position (80%), funded by the Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF). This postdoctoral position is part of the SNSF Starting Grant “Humour in adolescents on the autism spectrum: a window into cognition and a tool for well-being”, awarded to Prof. Dr. Mirella Manfredi. The successful candidate will contribute to the investigation of humour processing in autistic adolescents, with a particular focus on the development and evaluation of a personalised humour-based intervention aimed at promoting psychological well-being.

Your responsibilities

  • Development, implementation, and evaluation of a personalised humour-based intervention for autistic adolescents
  • Coordination of participant recruitment and experimental data collection
  • Analysis of behavioural, psychological, and neurophysiological data (EEG/ERP)
  • Analysis of facial expressions of amusement using the Facial Action Coding System (FACS)
  • Collaboration with PhD students, clinical psychologists, and other research team members
  • Presentation of research results at international scientific meetings
  • Publication of research results in peer-reviewed journal articles
  • Supervision of Master's theses
  • Assistance in organising scientific workshops

Your profile

  • Successful candidates should be highly motivated and hold a PhD in Cognitive Neuroscience, Experimental Psychology, Cognitive Psychology, Clinical Psychology, or a related field.
  • Candidates should have a strong background in experimental research and quantitative data analysis. Experience with electroencephalography (EEG/ERP) and statistical analysis using MATLAB, R, or Python is highly desirable. Previous experience in autism research, developmental psychology, or psychological interventions will be considered an advantage.
  • The candidate should demonstrate excellent interpersonal and organisational skills and be able to work both independently and collaboratively within an interdisciplinary research team.
  • Excellent knowledge of English and good written and spoken German are required.
