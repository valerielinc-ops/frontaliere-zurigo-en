Full description

Welcome to Zurichberg. With breathtaking views of the city and Lake Zurich, the historic 4* Boutique Hotel Zürichberg is a special place to work. Private and business travellers from all over the world spend the night here in the 66 rooms, stay in the restaurant, meet locals on the terrace and at the bar or meet and celebrate in the event rooms. With special architecture, high-quality equipment and numerous highlights, the hotel delights guests and employees. Our team takes care of unique experiences and unforgettable stays. Are you our new team member, who supports us as boss: de service immediately or by agreement? You work 5 days a week between Monday and Sunday from 06:00 to 15:30, 10:00 to 19:00 or 15:00 to 24:00. We try to do without the room hour if possible. Your area of activity:

  • Management and organisation of the area
  • Responsibility for a visible, attentive and service-oriented presence in the guest room during peak hours
  • Perception of Hosts: Inside Role
  • Acceptance, clarification and implementation of guest needs and wishes
  • Ensuring a high level of guest satisfaction
  • Ensuring service flow and cooperation at the front
  • Planning and implementation of events, banquets and events incl. Preparation and post-processing
  • Preparation of operational planning and participation in the recruitment of new employees
  • Completion of various administrative tasks
  • Implementation and assurance of quality standards and ZFV guidelines (hygiene and occupational safety, service, etc.)
  • The ZFV leadership model "we make others successful" in the everyday life of everyone
  • Promoting a culture of trust, courage, transparency and joy
  • Embody, understand, represent and advance vision and strategy
  • Pursue, prioritize and measure implementation
  • Promoting team building and employee development Your strengths:
  • Completed basic training as a restoration specialist: EFZ or a comparable training in gastronomy
  • Several years of professional experience (at least 5 years) in the gastronomy, ideally with first management experience as boss: de service or in a comparable function
  • Passionate hosts: personality with a strong sense of excellent service and unforgettable guest experiences
  • Empathic, enthusiastic and motivating personality with pleasure in the leadership and development of employees
  • Winning sales talent as well as a strong understanding of quality and services
  • Organizational strength, sense of responsibility and the ability to maintain an overview even in demanding situations
  • Enjoying high-quality food and drinks as well as a strong understanding of pleasure and gastronomy
  • Very good knowledge of German (Swiss or High German) as well as good knowledge of English
