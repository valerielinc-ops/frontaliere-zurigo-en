Full description

80 - 100%, place of work Rümlang

Shape your future – together with us!

To strengthen our back office team, we are looking for an established, flexible personality.

Start your career in an agency at AXA, the No. 1! We support our customers as a competent partner – in every situation in life. Do you too?

What you can expect

  • Deputy of the main agent
  • Assistance in the processing of the private and corporate client portfolio of the main agent
  • Independent management of your own portfolio
  • Actively participate in achieving sales targets
  • Advice and support for customers, mainly on the phone or at customer reception
  • Assisting insurance consultants in arranging customer appointments
  • Pre- and post-processing of customer appointments (sales support)
  • Administrative support for insuranceconsultants
  • Preparation of offers and requests
  • General correspondence

We require from you

  • Commercial training in the insurance industry or many years of experience in insurance is mandatory
  • Passed Intermediary Examination (VBV)
  • Willingness to train regularly
  • Enjoyment of expanding existing customer relationships
  • Reliable, resilient personality combined with an efficient work style
  • High commitment as well as customer and service orientation
  • Persuasive Appearance with pleasure in dealing with people
  • Positive and winning manner, salesy flair
  • Very good knowledge of spoken and written German, knowledge of English and other languages would be an advantage

What we offer

We create framework conditions that give you room to develop and to fully exploit your potential. As the leading Swiss insurance company, we offer ambitious people attractive career opportunities and entrepreneurial freedom.

  • Discount of up to 30% on AXA products
  • 5 weeks holiday
  • Fleet discount/reimbursement of half-fare season ticket/GA (pro rata)
  • Access to the Swibeco platform
  • Room for independent action
  • Innovative structures, processes and modern tools
  • Well-founded, professional education and training opportunities
