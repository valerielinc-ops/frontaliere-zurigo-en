We care about the culinary well-being of our patients and employees. Our catering team offers top service 365 days a year and a rich selection with over 2500 guest menus. For our kitchen, we are looking for an employee or auxiliary cook by appointment.

Tasks

  • Assistance and support of cooks
  • Operation of cooking appliances
  • Service on the feed distribution belt
  • Compliance with hygiene rules
  • Cleaning work throughout the kitchen incl. Boiler, etc.
  • Work between 07.00 and 20.30 during 365 days in the entire kitchen
  • 42 hour week, readiness for weekend services

Profile

  • Experience in gastronomy and cooking, preferably in healthcare
  • Good physical condition
  • Good knowledge of German
