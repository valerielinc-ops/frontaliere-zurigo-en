Full description

High above the city of Zurich, in the Langmatt School House, which is located in the green and yet close to the center, you can expect a well-established, committed team, cheerful, willing students and competent school management.

To support our team, we are looking for an

  • They support a student with special needs in ISR setting in everyday school life.
  • They accompany a kindergarten child Mo 13:30 – 15:30, Di 08:00 – 12:00 and 13:30 – 15:30 and Fr 08:00 – 12:00.
  • They support the child in everyday activities and in learning according to support planning.
  • They take on support measures with individual children, according to the order of the class teacher / school curative teacher and school management.
  • They support the basic pedagogical attitude and are in close exchange with the school curative pedagogue.
  • Training or professional experience in the pedagogical field
  • Experience and joy in dealing with children, patience and resilience
  • Motivated, open, resilient personality with a positive human image
  • Flexibility as well as very good team and communication skills
  • Good knowledge of German, at least level B2 or higher

A varied and independent activity in a motivated and committed team that looks forward to working together. You will receive competent support from the curative pedagogue, the hoard management and the pedagogical school management and progressive salary and social benefits according to the guidelines of the school and sports department of the city of Zurich.

The Zurichberg School District comprises districts 1, 7 and 8 with the districts Altstadt, Fluntern, Hottingen, Hirslanden, Riesbach and Witikon. The district school authority manages 18 school houses. These are connected to 43 kindergartens and over 40 care facilities of different sizes. Of the 14 organizational units, 11 are day schools.
