Full description

In the Zurich notary offices, around 650 employees are committed to the concerns of customers and attach great importance to quality, discretion and customer orientation. All 44 notaries in the canton of Zurich are also land register and bankruptcy offices and belong to the Zurich administration of justice organization. Mobile Equipe Bankruptcy is an overarching bankruptcy unit, which is used in larger and complex bankruptcies. New year, new challenge! As of January 1, 2027 or by agreement, we are looking for a motivated and responsible person to strengthen our team as a specialist expert for bankruptcy (bankruptcy administrator) 80 - 100% Your work content

  • Self-employed conduct of demanding bankruptcy proceedings
  • Guidance and guidance for bankruptcy handlers
  • Negotiations with different stakeholders
  • Support in the training and further education of employees as well as in projects What you bring for this interesting and versatile task
  • Basic commercial education or completed legal or equivalent training
  • Several years of practical professional experience in the field of the Debt Recovery and Bankruptcy Act
  • Passed certificate examination of the specialisation course of the Swiss Association of Professional Examination for Operation and Bankruptcy (SVBBK) in the specialisation bankruptcy or certificate of elective ability as a Zurich notary/notary
  • Strong and assertive communication and negotiation skills as well as skill in written and oral expression
  • Decision-making and resilient personality with pleasure in a responsible and varied task
  • Good IT user knowledge (experience in the application of the software eXpert Bankruptcy)
  • Category B driving licence for field operations What we offer you
  • Exciting, diverse and independent work in a committed and dynamic team
  • Progressive employment conditions and exciting development opportunities
  • Attractive workplace in a central location in the city of Zurich Contact More information about the respective office, the Zurich notary service and our exciting tasks can be found on our website.
