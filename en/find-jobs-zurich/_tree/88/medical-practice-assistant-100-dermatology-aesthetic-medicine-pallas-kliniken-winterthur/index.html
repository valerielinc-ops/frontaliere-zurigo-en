Full description

The Pallas clinics are specialized in ophthalmology and aesthetics. With over 350 employees and 18 locations, we are one of the leading providers in Switzerland.

Our medical staff plays an indispensable role in the customer care process. They act as direct contacts and provide professional and individual support. The focus is always on the well-being of our customers. Through close cooperation with our specialists, we guarantee a holistic supply. Our team is characterized by high commitment, regular further training and the pleasure of the interdisciplinary cooperation with our customers.

To strengthen our MPA team in Winterthur, we are looking for a motivated

Medical practice assistant 60-100% (dermatology, aesthetic medicine)

Responsibility

  • Assistance in medical treatment in the fields of dermatology, veins, aesthetic medicine and plastic surgery
  • Assistance in small-scale surgery and laser therapies
  • Independent implementation of medical prescriptions (e.g. blood sampling, change of dressing, PDT therapy, before-after-photos, etc.)
  • Co-responsibility in the coordination of office hours
  • Check-in and check-out as well as patient care and advice
  • Treatment of patient concerns
  • Responsibility in the appointment and disposition of patients
  • Advice and active sales of care and cosmetic products
  • Delivery of medicines
  • Close cooperation with doctors and beauticians

Requirements

  • Completed medical education
  • Professional experience in a similar activity
  • Good communication skills
  • Willingness and duty to permanently deepen professional knowledge
  • Reliable, independent and service-oriented working methods
  • They are well-groomed and well-groomed.

Benefits

  • Interdisciplinary collaboration in a dynamic and motivated team
  • Careful and thorough training
  • Regulated working hours and five weeks of holidays per year, which can be obtained individually by agreement
  • Employee Discount for Internal Treatments and Advanced Employment Conditions
  • Motivating team spirit as well as regular training and coaching
