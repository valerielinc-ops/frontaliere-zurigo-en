Full description

Become part of our dedicated Customer Care Team with over 70 employees in our modern Customer Care Center in Dübendorf. Support as an intern in the field of client service You join our team during a 12-month internship. You are the first point of contact for our customers and contribute to a first-class customer experience in digital banking. With comprehensive onboarding and targeted training, we prepare you optimally for your tasks – regardless of whether you already have experience in banking or customer service.

Your daily tasks include

  • Telephone support for our customers and processing of inquiries via our ticket system.
  • Support with questions about our products, services and tools as well as the use of our digital platforms and technical concerns.
  • Processing of customer issues in the ticket system, in particular account openings and closures, money and securities transfers, payment orders and other operational topics – including complete documentation and timely processing.
  • Close cooperation with Business Operations, IT, Sales, Legal & Compliance, Marketing and Trading for the efficient solution of customer concerns.
  • Detecting and reporting suspicious activity in accordance with internal policies and processes.
  • You will deepen your knowledge of financial markets, investment products and banking processes and apply it directly in everyday work through practical activities and internal training.
  • You have completed a commercial apprenticeship or are currently completing a commercial apprenticeship that includes a one-year internship.
  • They are interested in the financial markets and are motivated to gain practical experience in the banking industry. No prior knowledge of finance is required.
  • They are characterized by a pronounced analytical thinking ability and an affinity for numbers.
  • They have a pronounced customer and service orientation and enjoy direct customer contact.
  • German is your mother tongue. You also have very good knowledge of English. Knowledge of French and/or Italian is an advantage.
  • They work in a structured way, set the right priorities in a dynamic environment and keep an overview.

Start date: 1 February 2027
