Full description

About the position Are you ready for a new challenge in a technically challenging environment? In this role, you strengthen our assembly team and make a direct contribution to the quality, reliability and on-time production of our gas-insulated high-voltage switchgear. With your craftsmanship and precise working methods, you ensure that our products meet the highest requirements and are continuously improved.

You can do that.

  • Assembly of components and assemblies of gas-insulated high voltage switchgear according to technical documentation
  • Execution of work according to checklists, guidelines and instructions (written & oral)
  • Responsibility for quality, deadlines and correct implementation in your own work area
  • Troubleshooting and active support in resolving deviations
  • Cooperation in the continuous improvement process through ideas and feedback

You bring that with you

  • Completed training as a polymechanic/polytechnician EFZ or equivalent
  • Precise, independent and reliable working method
  • Readiness to work in 2-shift operation
  • Team-oriented, flexible and committed
  • in German Fluent
  • English skills are beneficial

More about us We offer you a modern working environment with flexible working methods in which you can develop professionally and grow personally. You work in a motivated team that values quality, safety and collaboration.
