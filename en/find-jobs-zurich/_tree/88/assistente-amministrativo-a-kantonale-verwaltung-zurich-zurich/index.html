Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School Rämibühl, KRL administrative assistant/in 60 - % The Literargymnasium Rämibühl is a Zurich long-school school with an old- and new-language profile as well as an educational course that leads to a double degree in bilingual schooling (German/English) and International Baccalaureate (IB). The Gymnasium is a place of work and learning for around 770 students and 130 teachers and employees. The traditional high school, founded in 1833, is characterized by its proximity to the University and ETH Zurich, its manageability and a broadly expanded range of free subjects. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for a committed, reliable and quality-conscious personality for our school as an administrative assistant:in (60%) . As an administrative assistant, you support the school management and the management of central services in the areas of finance, school administration and personnel administration and are also a central point of contact in the secretariat. Your working days are two whole days (one of them on Friday), as well as two mornings a week. You only take your holidays during school holidays. Their tasks

  • Secretariat: reception, telephone and e-mail processing.
  • School administration: Supervision of grades 3 and 4 in cooperation with the school management.
  • Finance: independent processing of debtors and creditors.
  • HR administration of the teaching staff.
  • Organization and administration of language and foreign stays as well as the student:inside exchange. Your profile
  • Completed basic commercial education (KV EFZ) or equivalent training.
  • Several years of professional experience in administration, ideally in the secretariat or in financial accounting.
  • Secure knowledge of accounts payable and payable.
  • Very good user knowledge in MS Office.
  • Experience in SAP financial accounting.
  • High quality awareness, precise and careful working method.
  • Strong initiative and independence.
  • flexibility and resilience.
  • High service orientation, social skills and team skills.
  • Discretion and trustworthiness in handling sensitive data.
  • Delight in dealing with young people and adults in a lively school environment.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English. We offer you a versatile and responsible job in a collegiate working environment and progressive employment conditions according to canton law. Your future place of work is in the center of the city of Zurich. Our high school will move into a temporary on the Rämibühl campus in March 2028 and will move to its new location in Seefeld in the summer of 2029. Application Have we aroused your interest?
