Full description

Do you like working with people? You want to start from the 1. March 2027 or by agreement with our education team for the nursing professions?

  • They look after and care for our residents together with the learners and students and accompany and instruct them in everyday clinical life, especially in the areas of tracheostoma care, long-term ventilation and geriatrics.
  • They work closely with the station management, the nurse and the educational team and create a learning-friendly environment.
  • As a professional trainer, you are responsible for planning, coordinating and implementing the training processes of our learners and students.
  • They promote the ability of learners and students to reflect and support them in acquiring the necessary action skills.
  • They participate in the continuous further development of the training structures and ensure a practical and contemporary mediation of the training contents.
  • Sound and reflective professional experience, ideally in the field of specialized care
  • Completed training as a nurse (HF/FH/DNII) with SRC recognition for diplomas from abroad as well as a recognized further education in the pedagogical field (at least SVEB 1) or the willingness to complete it
  • You have initial experience in accompanying learners and students as well as an pedagogical sense
  • Strong leadership skills that enable you to inspire employees for vocational training and develop them together with the management

Look forward to an exciting area of work in a professional and team-oriented working environment with a du-culture throughout the company. The human being is at our center. That is why we support the reconciliation of private and professional life, different working models make it possible. As part of the city of Zurich and due to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, progressive employment conditions and excellent social benefits. The annual salary for this function is, depending on the experience, between 93,838.- and 125,386.-- (100%).

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

The health center for the age of Gehrenholz in Wiedikon in district 3 combines various care and care offers under one roof and is characterized by many years of experience in age medicine. The exceptional architecture and the quiet, green location are further advantages. Here you work together with 250 colleagues.
