Full description

As a specialist care provider at the University Hospital in Zurich, we are committed to showing commitment with our professional diversity in the top medical environment and always keeping up with the times. We do not achieve success alone, but together! Each individual counts and together we bring ourselves to the highest level of professional care.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • End of 0815, we offer complex patient situations: from A like anorexia, H like hyponatremia, P like polytrauma to Z like cell lymphoma, everything is involved • You support the IMC nursing team in everyday life: this includes co-care and coaching in demanding nursing situations; conducts case discussions and training in the care teams and develops, implements and evaluates guidelines and concepts in cooperation with the care expert • Continuous development of a person-centered practice and ensuring the quality of care in close cooperation with the care expert • Our shared governance team offers you the best possible and individual leadership. Presence, communication at eye level and interest in the concerns of employees are characteristics that we exemplify • Ideally, you will bring a Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BScN) or are ready to complete this part-time • With our (voluntary) working time model of the 12-hour shifts on Saturdays and Sundays you will have more weekends in the month for you We offer: Please contact us, we want to get to know you! Let’s find out if we fit together: just load your CV into our eRecruiting tool or send your CV via email so we can easily get in touch with you. Important: If you have completed your nursing education abroad, the SRC recognition procedure must already be completed at the time of application. A positive SRK PreCheck alone is not sufficient.
