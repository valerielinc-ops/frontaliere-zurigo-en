Become part of our team!

Raiffeisenbank Rapperswil-Jona is a successful, innovative and locally oriented bank with headquarters in Rapperswil-Jona SG and offices in Rüti ZH, Hombrechtikon ZH and Egg ZH. We are looking for a committed and service-oriented personality for our modern consulting bank in Hombrechtikon ZH as a customer consultant (m/f/d).

What awaits you?

  • Comprehensive and needs-based advice to customers on the topics of basic products, savings, payments, investment and provision
  • Operation and comprehensive customer service at our consulting desk
  • Recognizing and exploiting the customer potential through active and continuous support of the customer portfolio and forwarding customers to the responsible specialist consultants
  • Cooperation in target group-specific market development measures, in particular in contact campaigns and performance of representative tasks
  • Ensuring the operation of all cash dispensers and the necessary stocks of notes and varieties

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking training or comparable training, ideal for banking graduates
  • Initial experience in the financial performance sector is beneficial
  • Strong customer and sales orientation
  • Independent and goal-oriented working method as well as enjoying intensive customer contact
  • Good communication and social skills
  • Winning, competent and professional appearance
  • Teamwork and flexibility
