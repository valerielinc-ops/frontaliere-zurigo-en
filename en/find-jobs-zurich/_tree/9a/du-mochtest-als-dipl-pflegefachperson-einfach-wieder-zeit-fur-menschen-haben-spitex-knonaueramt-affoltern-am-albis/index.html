“You want to be a dipl.” Do nurses just have time for people again? ☀ It's 7:15 a.m. The sun rises just above the roofs as our Anna, Dipl. HF, your car starts. Her first visit takes her to Ms. Keller, 82, who needs support after a fall. Anna rings, is received with a smile – and with one: “I have already looked forward to you.”

“These moments are it,” says Anna. “Here I’m not just ‘care’, I’m Anna.”

Together we help others to lead a self-determined life in their familiar environment – for this we work in the team at Spitex Knonaueramt and with mutual appreciation.

⌚ Anna takes her time. For wound care, yes – but also for the conversation about the grandchildren, about worries, about small progress. No hectic ringing, no constant change every minute. For this self-responsibility, trust and real relationships.

“I feel like I’m effective,” says Anna. „At Spitex Knonaueramt I see every day what I do.

Portrait Spitex Knonaueramt employs around 160 people who are committed to the needs of its approximately 500 customers on a competent, open, personal and flexible basis every day. The catchment area comprises around 54,000 inhabitants. We offer individual care, care, relief and counselling at home for sick and recovering people of all ages: acute, chronic and mentally ill, people with disabilities, age-related limitations, convalescence, overwork, accident and maternity. We also care for people with serious and/or incurable diseases, accompany and relieve the relatives. Spitex Knonaueramt is a private-law association that provides help and care at home on behalf of 13 municipalities in the district of Affoltern.
