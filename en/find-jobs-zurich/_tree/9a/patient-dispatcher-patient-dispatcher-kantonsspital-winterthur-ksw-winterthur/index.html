Full description

In order to strengthen the central patient disposition in the field of surgical disciplines, we are looking for an open-minded, flexible and resilient personality as a patient dispatcher. Are you looking for a varied everyday work in a well-established team? Then you are exactly right with us! We offer you an interesting and independent activity in a well-organized working environment with long-term perspectives. You can expect a motivated and established team in which cooperation and mutual support are given priority. As an interdisciplinary team, we centrally coordinate the bed resources of the KSW and support the various clinics in their planning. We are in close contact with patients, nurses and doctors every day. Together, we ensure forward-looking planning and smooth running of everyday hospital life.

Tasks

  • Planning of elective entrances and relocations taking into account the available bed capacities as well as the utilization of nursing staff and doctors
  • Planning operations taking into account available human and infrastructural resources
  • Calling patients in writing and by telephone
  • Ensuring a smooth flow of information during entry, exit and relocation
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation with nurses, doctors and other internal and external specialists
  • Working hours are from Monday to Friday between 07.30 and 17.00 and on weekends and public holidays between 07.30 and 15.00

Profile

  • Completed training as a qualified nurse HF/FH and several years of practical professional experience
  • Solution-oriented and proactive way of thinking and acting
  • Secure and convincing appearance and strong communication skills
  • High service orientation, team spirit and enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Enjoying a varied and dynamic working day as well as the ability to keep an overview even in labor-intensive phases
