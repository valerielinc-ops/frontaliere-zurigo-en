20.11.2026 Zurich

Become part of our team!

In the dynamic environment in the middle of the city of Zurich, we are looking for a customer-oriented, experienced personality with pleasure in proactive and holistic support and advice as well as a pronounced flair to inspire customers with heart and competence and to win over for Raiffeisenbank Zurich.

What do you expect?

  • Takeover and expansion of an existing retail portfolio with a focus on real estate financing
  • Development of customized financing solutions for self-used and rented residential properties
  • Holistic support of our customers in the topics of numbers, saving, investing and provision
  • Short decision paths and comprehensive skills for entrepreneurial, customer-oriented action
  • Identification of additional customer needs and targeted integration of internal sales units, in particular financial and estate planning as well as corporate banking

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking training with further education (e.g. Bachelor/HF/Financial Planner) and SAQ certification as individual client advisor
  • Several years and broad experience in private customer consulting with own customer portfolio, in particular real estate financing
  • Strong consulting and final strength with consistent customer focus
  • Entrepreneurship, initiative and digital affinity
  • Anchoring and network in the city of Zurich is an advantage
