  • You independently carry out work in the assembly/disassembly of plants according to the project. Maintenance activities on the SA external systems in operation (switches, signals, track detection systems, train control, ETCS, point heating) are also part of your area of responsibility.
  • Disruption of railway installations within the frame of the pikett organization you carry out independently.

You bring that with you.

  • Valid driving licence of category B as well as very good knowledge of German in words and writing at at least B2 level are required.
