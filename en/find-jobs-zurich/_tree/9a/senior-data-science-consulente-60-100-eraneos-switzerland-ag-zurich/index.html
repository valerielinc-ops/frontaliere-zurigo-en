Eraneos is an international strategy, transformation and technology advisory group that helps companies thrive in a dynamic digital world. With first-class experts from business and technology, we empower our customers to set new standards for sustainable transformations – from strategy to implementation. Our team of around 1,200 dedicated employees is represented in offices in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, Denmark, Sweden, Austria, the UK, Singapore and the USA and combines global perspectives with strong local roots. Around 450 employees work at the Swiss locations in Zurich, Bern and Lausanne.
