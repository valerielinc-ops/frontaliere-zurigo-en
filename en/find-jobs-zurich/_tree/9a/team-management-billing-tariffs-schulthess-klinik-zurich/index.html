Full description

Tasks

  • Professional and personnel management as well as further development of the invoicing team
  • Ensuring correct and timely billing of outpatient and inpatient services (TARDOC, outpatient packages, SwissDRG, physiotherapy tariff, supplementary insurance and self-payers)
  • Responsibility for performance controlling, invoice control and handling of rejections
  • Training and support of stakeholders to ensure correct performance capture and documentation
  • Preparation of evaluations, key figures and financial statements as well as derivation and implementation of appropriate measures
  • Analysis, optimization and digitalization of billing and administration processes
  • Participation in tariff adjustments, system changes and internal hospital projects
  • Contact person for patients in billing matters
  • Coordination and handling of complaints in the billing environment
  • Close cooperation with internal specialist areas, service providers and insurance companies

Requirements

  • Basic commercial training with further training in business administration and in health and Tariffs
  • Several years of experience in billing a hospital and several years of management experience
  • In-depth knowledge of current tariff systems and medical terminology
  • Return-oriented, analytical thinking, digital affinity and joy of process improvement
  • Very good German and good English skills in word and writing, other languages are advantageous
  • Strong communication, strong assertiveness towards insurers and solution-oriented towards patients

We offer

  • Attractive social benefits and numerous benefits
  • Team spirit
  • Training opportunities
  • Cheap food in the staff restaurant
  • 40 hourly week
  • ZVV BonusPass / ZVV BonusPass Flex

The Schulthess Clinic in Zurich is one of the leading orthopaedic clinics in Europe with over 1,100 employees. Our central concern is to free people from their pain and restore their mobility. As a specialist clinic, we focus on surgical orthopedics, neurology, rheumatology and sports medicine. Schulthess Clinic is Swiss Olympic Medical Center.
