You like to take on responsibility and keep an overview even in a dynamic working environment? As shift manager: r AEMP you coordinate and prioritize the processes within your shift, support the employees professionally and take action in the operational operation itself. Together with the team, you ensure a safe, efficient and quality-compliant processing process.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • Organization, coordination and prioritization of workflows within the shift • Professional guidance and support for employees as well as ensuring the defined processes and specifications • Active participation in operational operations and support in all areas of processing • Ensuring a customer- and process-oriented service as well as coordination with internal and external interfaces • Monitoring of processes and facilities as well as early detection and processing of deviations, risks and disruptions • Ensuring and documenting the required routine, process and random checks • Collaboration in projects and process optimizations as well as support of the group management in the further development of the AEMP Requirements • Completed training as medical device technologist/EFZ or equivalent qualification with at least 3 years of professional experience in the AEMP • Further education Specialism II (mandatory) • Secure handling of IT and documentation systems (e.g. batch documentation systems) • Ability to organize and prioritize workflows as well as professional management of employees • High problem-solving capability, resilience and decision-making capability in operational operations • Communication strength and enforcement • Willingness to work in shift operation (3-shift system) as well as on weekends and public holidays • High physical and psychological resilience as well as ability to maintain an overview and act in a structured manner even in demanding situations We offer: We offer you an interesting and varied activity with a lot of creative leeway in your function as shift manager:r. A well-founded familiarization, clear structures and the support of your superiors make it easier for you to get started. In addition, we offer you a wide range of further education opportunities, for example the training for / to medical device technology: in EFZ, in order to further develop your function. Agile AEMP: With our ISO 13485 certified medical device processing unit (AEMP) in the logistics and service centre in Schlieren, USZ has one of the most modern sterile goods supply facilities in Europe.
