Put to the Point

Bring your passion for interdisciplinary cooperation to the National Research Focus CLIM+ and MeteoSwiss. Use your research experience to build new knowledge on extreme weather, circulation changes and climate change.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: #LI-MCH1012
  • Location: Operation Center 1, Frachtstrasse, 8058 Zurich

You can make this contribution

  • Investigate extreme weather events climatologically (inclusion of large-scale flow patterns)
  • Scientific analysis of weather and climate model data
  • Analyze characteristics, variability and trends in pre-industrial, present and future climate
  • Develop data analysis tools, manage data and put scientific results into operation
  • Collaborate with national and international partners, in particular in the National Research Focus "CLIM+ – Climate Extremes and Society: Strengthening Resilience" (CLIM+)
  • Create reports, documentation and scientific publications

This makes them unique.

  • Doctorate in Atmospheric and Climate, Meteorology, Environmental Sciences, Physics or a Related Subject
  • Very good knowledge of the dynamics of extreme weather and climate extremes in the middle latitudes and the influence of circulation and teleconnections
  • Very good knowledge in the evaluation and handling of data sets from numerical weather forecasting, reanalysis and climate models
  • Experience in dealing with modern analysis methods and programming as well as basic knowledge and interest in machine learning
  • Joy of working with project partners and motivation to find solutions together
  • Knowledge of two official languages and very good knowledge of English

MeteoSwiss Good to know

The Federal Office for Meteorology and Climatology (MeteoSchweiz) is the national weather service of Switzerland.
