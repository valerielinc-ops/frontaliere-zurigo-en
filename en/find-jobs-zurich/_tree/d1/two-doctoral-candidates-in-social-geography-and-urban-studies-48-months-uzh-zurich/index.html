Full description

Department of Geography

Start of employment August or September 2027

The Social Geography and Urban Studies research group draws on postcolonial, socio-legal, political-economic and feminist approaches, as well as political and urban theories to trace the transformation of cities and urban societies. In particular, we develop research on urban marginalization, housing precarisation, and the politics of landed property to explore case studies across the global North and South. We are looking for two candidates whose research interests align with the themes outlined above. Specifically, the doctoral projects will contribute to a wider research agenda on the everyday experience of debt and on the politics of landed property, rent, dispossession, and gendered precarity in Mexican cities. Applicants are expected to conduct a qualitative study that examines the legal, urban, and everyday geographies of housing and the social and institutional transformations that shape them.

Your responsibilities

  • Development and implementation of a PhD-research project in the context of the outlined themes under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Hanna Hilbrandt;
  • Participation in the organization and realisation of project workshops and conference sessions;
  • Presentation of research results at conferences and in publications;
  • Collaboration in research, teaching, and administration of the research group.

Your profile

  • Very good MSc. degree in human geography, sociology, urban studies, planning, or a related social science discipline;
  • Knowledge of qualitative methods and a strong interest in empirical research (including qualitative interviews, ethnographic methods, document analysis, etc.); strong analytical skills;
  • Fieldwork experience in Latin America is highly desirable;
  • Excellent written and spoken Spanish and English skills are an essential requirement; joy in academic writing;
  • Ability to work independently and strong skills for collaborative teamwork.

Your benefits

  • Secure funding for four years including for fieldwork costs as well as workshop and conference visits;
  • Diverse networking possibilities, internationally as well as with Swiss research groups;
  • Permanent workplace with high-quality technical equipment in a lively institute and campus environment;
  • Work location with a high quality of life (Zurich);
  • A stimulating and supportive work environment that provides opportunities to develop an academic career.

The salary is commensurate with UZH and SNSF doctoral salary regulations.
