Hirslanden stands for first-class, responsible and individual health care that puts people first. From birth to old age as well as from prevention to healing – at any time, physically and digitally – the Hirslanden Group is concentrating on the further development of the “Continuum of Care” together with private and public partners.

Our focus is not only on the well-being of our patients, but also on that of our employees.

YOUR TASKS

  • Professional and human leadership, development and qualification of a team of strategic buyers with responsibility for different medical specialists and goods groups
  • Overall responsibility for the strategic orientation and further development of the purchase of medical products and medicines as well as for the definition and implementation of group-wide product group, product and supplier strategies
  • Active control and standardization of the product and supplier portfolio with the aim of sustainably combining medical quality, security of supply, efficiency and regulatory requirements
  • Identification, evaluation and consistent realization of savings and value creation potential as well as management of relevant purchasing KPIs and strategic initiatives
  • Conducting complex contract, price and condition negotiations with national and international industrial partners as well as establishing and controlling strategically relevant supplier relationships
  • Close cooperation with clinic directorates, doctors, pharmacies, medical departments, supply chain and other functions of the corporate office
  • Management of cross-cutting projects, bodies and decision-making processes
  • Ensuring legally and regulatoryly compliant procurement taking into account Swiss medicine legislation, the Medical Devices Ordinance and the relevant transparency, compliance and governance requirements

Your profile

  • Completed studies in medical, economic or comparable fields, ideally supplemented by further training in purchasing or supply chain management
  • Several years of profound experience in strategic purchasing with demonstrable responsibility for complex product groups (ideally in the healthcare sector as well as in the medical technology, medical products or pharmaceutical environment)
  • Several years of management experience and the ability to develop employees goal-oriented, transfer responsibility and sustainably develop a high-performance team
  • Strong negotiating and concluding strength, entrepreneurial thinking and a sovereign and binding approach towards medical, commercial and external stakeholders at different hierarchical levels
  • High analytical and conceptual competence, strong affinity for numbers and data as well as the ability to structure complex issues, prepare decisions and implement changes consistently
  • Very good knowledge of German and English as well as a secure handling of SAP, MS Office and data-based control and reporting instruments, knowledge of French is an advantage
