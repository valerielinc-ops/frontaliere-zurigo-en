This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

You will become part of an interdisciplinary team in the area of data and information protection as well as in the area of compliance. The team supports the organization in addressing complex regulatory requirements, particularly those related to data protection and company-wide compliance. In a dynamic and international environment, you will work on a wide range of questions and actively contribute to the further development of processes, guidelines and solutions.

  • Advising internal stakeholders and coordination as well as independent processing of assigned requests, contract negotiations and internal projects.
  • Supervision of central group mailboxes and coordination within the teams.
  • Analysis of legal issues related to data processing, data flows and data transfers, including the development of practical solutions.
  • Organization, coordination and support of various processes within the Technology Risk and Risk Management Team.
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with specialists from other fields such as IT, Human Resources and Cyber Security.
  • Cooperation in the development and further development of policies, guidelines, regulations and processes as well as their communication - especially in the area of data protection.
  • Advice and support in other, evolving legal fields in the field of data protection in close cooperation with experienced professionals.
  • Complementary cooperation and answering legal questions in the area of people and firm compliance.
  • Successfully completed master’s degree in law, preferably in Swiss law.
  • Strong interest in data protection, information and technology law and compliance, ideally complemented by practical experience and/or certification (e.g. CIPP/E) or relevant training.
  • 1-3 years of relevant professional experience in the field of data protection, additional experience in the compliance environment is advantageous.
  • Affinity to current trends in new technologies and artificial intelligence.
  • Analytical, solution-oriented and entrepreneurial way of thinking as well as a team-oriented and flexible way of working.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in words and writing
  • Other foreign languages are beneficial.
  • Long-term perspective with the willingness for continuous professional and personal development.
