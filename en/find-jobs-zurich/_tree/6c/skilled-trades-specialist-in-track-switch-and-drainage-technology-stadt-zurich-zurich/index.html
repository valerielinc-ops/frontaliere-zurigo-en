Full description

Do you like to take part and use your craftsmanship to ensure that our tram network works reliably?

  • You will carry out demanding assembly and insulation work on our track systems. These include tram switches, crossings, rail extension devices and rail lubrication systems. A particular focus is on the installation of the professional drainage and insulation of these plants at the production site.
  • In our track construction workshop you manufacture track components and revise switch changeover systems. In new construction and repair projects, you are directly on the track construction sites and ensure that the insulation of the mechanical infrastructure components is properly assembled and, if necessary, repaired.
  • You operate various special machines and equipment such as crane equipment, welding equipment, core drilling machines and metalworking machines. You also plan the required drainage materials and document your work, material reservations and time feedback in SAP.
  • For technical challenges, you support the team with your expertise and develop solutions in the mentioned categories.
  • You will also work with new employees and temporary staff and will be happy to pass on your knowledge. This will make an important contribution to the safety and availability of our tram network.
  • You have completed vocational training as a sanitary EFZ, pipe network fitter EFZ, maintenance specialist EFZ or equivalent training.
  • You will bring in-depth craftsmanship skills and technical understanding. Knowledge of sanitation and drainage technology is particularly important.
  • You have the driving licence category B.
  • A category R1 forklift pass is an advantage.
  • You enjoy practical work and challenging assembly and maintenance tasks.
  • It is a function without shift work. However, occasional night and weekend assignments on track construction sites are included.
