Full description

Our Private Clients team advises wealthy individuals, entrepreneurs and international families on tax issues with national and cross-border implications. We support our customers in fulfilling their tax obligations, accompany them in complex situations and coordinate solutions with our global PwC network. In doing so, we work closely with specialists from different specialist areas.

You can expect a dynamic, international environment with direct customer contact, exciting development opportunities and an open feedback and coaching culture.

  • Preparation of tax returns for natural persons with national and international circumstances.
  • Review of tax bills as well as tax assessments and constitution of a corresponding recommendation.
  • preparation of legal remedies against tax assessments and clarification or Research in connection with the legal situation.
  • Completion of administrative activities in the family office area.
  • Support of superiors in tax consulting and responsibility for coordination with the international PwC network.
  • Acquire comprehensive technical know-how and soft skills through on-and-off-the-job trainings as well as regular feedback from Kolleg: inside and supervisor and work in a young and dynamic team.
  • Basic commercial training and initial experience in the trust sector or in tax administration.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English.
  • Characterize yourself with a high willingness to learn and have a pronounced flair for numbers and business contexts.
  • Be a highly motivated dynamic and purposeful personality who appreciates working analytically.
