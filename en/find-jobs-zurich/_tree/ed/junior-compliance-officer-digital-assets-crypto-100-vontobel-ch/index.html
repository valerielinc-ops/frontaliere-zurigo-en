Full description

Can you trace a client’s crypto wealth from the first wallet to the fiat off-ramp, and tell whether the story holds up? Join our Client Onboarding & Reviews team in Zurich as our crypto compliance specialist. In that team, you will assess high-risk clients, PEPs and digital-asset relationships. You’ll combine deep on-chain expertise with AI-enabled tools to reach clear, defensible decisions. If you want to shape how a private bank handles crypto wealth, we’d like to hear from you.

What would your role be?

  • Conduct risk-based compliance assessments for onboardings and for periodic, event-driven and ad-hoc reviews. This covers KYC, due diligence, sanctions screening, adverse media and reputational risk, with a focus on PEPs, high-risk clients and crypto-related relationships.
  • Reconstruct and critically assess a client’s full crypto wealth journey, from the original acquisition, through wallets, exchanges and protocols, to the fiat off-ramp.
  • Assess whether exchange statements, wallet evidence, transaction histories, SAFTs, token allocations, mining records and off-ramp documentation genuinely support the client’s claimed source of wealth.
  • Run wallet screening, transaction tracing, attribution and exposure analysis yourself in TRM Labs, Chainalysis or Elliptic, separating meaningful risk from incidental blockchain proximity.
  • Escalate AML concerns to management, and prepare concise, defensible assessments and case presentations for senior management and governance committees.
  • Support the team in day-to-day business, compliance projects, internal controls and quality assurance reviews.

What are we looking for?

  • University degree and compliance-related certifications. A crypto-specific certification (e.g. ACAMS CCAS) is a plus.
  • At least 2 years of AML/Compliance experience in Swiss private banking or at a FINMA-regulated digital asset institution, ideally in client due diligence, source of wealth and negative news screening.
  • At least 5 years of active personal or professional exposure to crypto, including practical use of wallets, exchanges, blockchain transactions and different digital-asset ecosystems.
  • Hands-on experience with TRM Labs, Chainalysis or Elliptic.
  • Deep knowledge of Bitcoin, Ethereum and other major ecosystems. This includes wallet infrastructure, smart contracts, DeFi, bridges, stablecoins, staking, mining, token issuance, vesting and decentralised trading.
  • Practical understanding of crypto-specific financial-crime risks.
  • The ability to turn complex on-chain findings into succinct written and verbal conclusions in English.
  • Fluent in English and German
  • French and Arabic are a plus.
  • Affinity for AI and a willingness to embrace AI-enabled tools and ways of working.

What do we offer?

  • Friendly, diverse team in an open-office space.
  • All the latest technology to help you excel and advance.
  • Agile environment in how we think, the way we work and our trust for each other.
  • Flat hierarchy with collaboration at all levels.
  • Campus feeling and a collaborative spirit.
