Then this job could be just right for you! For my physiotherapy practice in Zurich, I am looking for a dedicated physiotherapist who enjoys manual therapy and is interested in further education and specialization in fascia therapy. Your profile

  • Completed physiotherapy diploma with SRK recognition
  • Good knowledge of German (at least B2) and English
  • Professional experience and pleasure in manual therapy
  • Interest in fascia therapy and readiness for professional training and specialisation
  • Self-employed, responsible and customer-oriented working methods
  • High social competence and pleasure in dealing with people
  • Motivation to actively participate in the professional development of the practice

This is what awaits you · Start with a part-time workload of 40-50%, with the possibility to gradually expand the workload according to your wishes · Working hours can be agreed individually · 5 weeks of vacation · Financial participation in professional development · Participation in the establishment of a competence centre for manual pain treatments · An interesting field of activity with the opportunity to develop and contribute your own professional focuses · Interdisciplinary cooperation with Medical Massage and Craniosacral Therapy, who share the practice with us as subtenants An environment where you are appreciated With us, you will be part of a practice in which professional quality, personal appreciation and a cordial cooperation are given priority. Our specialization in manual therapy and fascia therapy forms an important professional orientation of the practice. At the same time, we work interdisciplinary with medical masseurs and a craniosacral therapist. This creates a versatile therapeutic environment with space for professional exchange, mutual inspiration and personal development. A friendly, collegial and appreciative atmosphere is particularly important to us. Your therapeutic work and commitment are appreciated not only in the team, but also by our clients and the referring doctors. I want to offer you a workplace where you can develop professionally, feel personally comfortable and be perceived as an important part of the whole. Interested? Then I look forward to your contact with CV and the most important information about your previous professional experience. Your employer ReViva Physiotherapie GmbH Reviva Physiotherapie GmbH is a modern, family physiotherapy practice in the heart of Zurich. We specialize in pain therapy, fascia therapy and rehabilitation after injury or surgery. With a high level of professional competence, individual care and an appreciative cooperation, we support our patients on the way to greater mobility and quality of life.
