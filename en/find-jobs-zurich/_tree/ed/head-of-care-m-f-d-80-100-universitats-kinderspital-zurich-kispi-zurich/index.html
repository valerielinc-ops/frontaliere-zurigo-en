Full description

Children's Hospital Zurich Eleonorenstiftung | online since : 17.11.2026

Concentration unit for children and adolescents in the fields of adolescent medicine, psychosomatics, diabetology, general paediatrics and intensive EEG monitoring

Your tasks

  • You take over the professional and personnel management of the bed ward with the focus on adolescent medicine, psychosomatics, diabetology, general pediatrics and intensive EEG monitoring.
  • You ensure effective and family-oriented care as well as the training and further education assignment.
  • You take responsibility for the quality of care and education.
  • You promote the personal responsibility and professional development of employees and represent the values of the Children's Hospital Zurich.
  • You take financial responsibility and argue with key figures.
  • In nursing and multi-professional projects, you take on responsibility and work actively in the interdisciplinary team.

Your profile

  • Diploma in Care HF or FH
  • Sound professional experience in the acute field of paediatrics
  • Proven leadership experience and management training at MAS level
  • Leadership skills
  • Solution-oriented operation and good load capacity
