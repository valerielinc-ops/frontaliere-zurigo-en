Job title

From 1 January 2027 or by agreement

Stronger is a unique combination of competent physiotherapy and personal training. It stands for high-quality therapy, pleasure in exercise and a cool training location. We are looking for a committed personality who leads our physiotherapy and trainer team independently and actively shapes the professional and economic future of our practice.

Coffee, tea, protein shakes, cool people and your own music choice included. ;-

What to expect

  • Responsibility and creative leeway: You take over the overall management of physiotherapy including personnel management, organization and economic development.
  • Variety: Around 80% of your work is devoted to physiotherapy, around 20% to management and administration.
  • A great interdisciplinary team: You will take over an existing, family team of physiotherapists and personal trainers.
  • Versatile patients: From athletes (including personal training) to seniors to domicile therapy, everything is included.
  • Attractive conditions: Five weeks of vacation, an additional week for further training incl. Financial support, personal development opportunities and the opportunity to use the studio for your own training.

What you bring with you

  • An education as a physiotherapist recognised in Switzerland and enjoyment of active therapy.
  • enthusiasm for sport and exercise
  • Experience in sports physiotherapy is a plus.
  • Ideally, leadership experience or the motivation to grow into a leadership role.
  • Organizational talent, initiative and business thinking.
  • Team spirit, good communication skills and very good knowledge of German.
