Full description

This is what awaits you

Would you like to actively shape international insurance programs and ensure that complex processes work reliably? In this versatile specialist function, you coordinate and supervise international liability programs for our corporate customers – from preparation to ongoing implementation.

This is what you do with us

As Multinational Insurance Coordinator, you support our international underwriters and coordinate the administrative handling of international liability insurance programs. You work closely with the relevant departments and our global network partners.

You collect and manage international insurance solutions, coordinate implementation with our network partners and monitor premium flows. You also produce statistics and reports for the departments and management.

In internal projects you bring your expertise and actively work on the further development of our processes.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training in the insurance industry and ideally further education at the level of professional card, insurance diploma or higher technical school
  • Years of experience in administration and support of international insurance programs with a focus on liability insurance. Experience in property insurance is beneficial
  • Enjoy understanding complex topics, working on them in a structured way and finding good solutions together with various stakeholders
  • Independent, reliable and customer-oriented working methods as well as a high level of quality and responsibility
  • Very good German and English skills, French skills are a plus

Working with us

You can expect a committed and collaborative team that supports each other and actively shares knowledge. We maintain an open, solution-oriented cooperation and give you space to take responsibility and help shape improvements.

You can choose your place of work between Bern and Zurich Oerlikon.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
