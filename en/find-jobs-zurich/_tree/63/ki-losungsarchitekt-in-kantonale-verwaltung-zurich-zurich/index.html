Full description

The Cantonal Police of Zurich and its approximately 4,000 employees assume responsibility for security in the canton. Do you want to actively shape our digital future and create the architectural foundations for an innovative, safe and responsible use of artificial intelligence? To strengthen our computer science team in Zurich, we are looking for a committed and competent personality as an AI solution architect - in 80 - 100% Your responsibilities

  • Analyze professional and technical requirements and develop scalable, secure and economical AI solution architectures
  • Developing and further developing architectural foundations as well as defining suitable architecture and operating models for on-premises, cloud and hybrid environments
  • To help shape AI governance and ensure compliance with requirements in the areas of data protection, information security, compliance and responsible AI use
  • Designing data flows and data processing processes as well as defining requirements for the operation, quality assurance and continuous development of AI solutions (MLOps)
  • Advising and supporting AI projects, specialist departments and IT operational services as well as conducting architectural conformity checks
  • Creating and maintaining architectural concepts, technical specifications and architectural diagrams
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with internal and external stakeholders as well as representing the interests of the Cantonal Police of Zurich vis-à-vis cantonal authorities, in particular the organization SynerKI Your profile
  • Completed university degree (bachelor) in computer science, business informatics or an equivalent education
  • Further education in the field of IT architecture is advantageous
  • At least five years of professional experience, including at least two years in the relevant field (AI technologies, data or software architecture)
  • Practical experience with cloud platforms, container solutions, databases, RAGs, APIs and DevOps
  • Strong analytical and conceptual thinking skills as well as an independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • High social skills, strong team and communication skills and enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • High resilience, commitment and self-motivation as well as good knowledge of English
  • Secure appearance and stylish knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Impeccable reputation and our offer
  • An innovative and responsible activity at the pulse of technological development
  • The opportunity to actively shape the development and further development of the AI landscape of the Cantonal Police of Zurich
  • An exciting, interdisciplinary working environment with high social relevance
  • Attractive training and education opportunities
  • A state-of-the-art workplace in the police and justice centre with staff restaurant and in-house fitness centre
  • Progressive employment conditions and a balanced work-life balance
  • Above-average pension benefits at BVK with an employer share of 60% and individually selectable savings options (base, standard, top) Per 1. January 2027 or by appointment Have we aroused your interest? Then we look forward to your complete online application.
