We are looking for a committed personality as a Material Handler (m/f/d) for our location in Dübendorf.

In this varied function, you are an important part of our logistics team and ensure that the material flows function smoothly. You coordinate transports and orders, ensure the supply of materials and at the same time actively participate in operational logistics. You can expect a wide range of tasks – from warehouse management and truck transports to cooperation in various areas.

Your new team is looking forward to meeting you!

Would you also be interested in participating in the company’s own fire brigade? We would be very pleased about that.

Your tasks

  • Organization and disposition of internal and external transport
  • Planning and preparation of storage and removal in high-bay warehouses
  • Active involvement in operational logistics, including forklifts and loading and unloading of trucks
  • Provision of deliveries for road, sea and air freight in compliance with all quality, testing, ADR/IATA and safety regulations
  • Order picking from fully automatic high-bay warehouses
  • Implementation of inventories as well as monitoring and optimization of stockpiles and storage level in SAP
  • Removal of malfunctions in the oxygen-reduced high-bay warehouse
  • Labelling of consignments in compliance with all quality, testing, ADR and safety requirements
  • Participation in continuous improvement measures to further develop the warehouse and distribution divisions
  • Implementation and active application of the 5S philosophy

Your professional profile

  • Completed training as a logistician or a comparable qualification
  • Several years of professional experience in warehouse logistics
  • Ideally, experience in a food business and knowledge of the relevant hygiene regulations
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Further language skills are advantageous
  • SUVA-recognised forklift pass
  • Good IT user knowledge, especially in SAP and MS Office (especially Excel)
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Teamwork, flexibility and enjoyment of varied tasks
