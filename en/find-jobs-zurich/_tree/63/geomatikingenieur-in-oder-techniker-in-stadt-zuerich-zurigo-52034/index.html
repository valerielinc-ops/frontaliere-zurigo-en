Geomatics Engineer or Technician — Stadt Zürich

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

As of February 1, 2027 or by agreement, we are looking for you as a geomatics engineer or technician.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for construction and engineering surveying in the city of Zurich.
  • You are responsible for the creation of building scale calculations and shadow cast calculations and take over their verification.
  • You offer and manage surveying assignments and coordinate work with partners and clients.
  • If necessary, you will work in cross-departmental surveying projects and on the other cadastres of the city of Zurich.

Profile

  • You have completed training as a geomatics engineer FH/ETH or geomatics technician.
  • You bring in-depth expertise, experience in engineering and official surveying.
  • You have very good GIS knowledge and very good knowledge in Microstation, you are welcome experience with rmData / GeoMedia / GEOS Pro.
  • You are a dynamic and curious personality with strong competences in personal responsibility, independence and communication, complemented by technical expertise.
  • You are affinity for handling all tasks through digital processes.

We offer

An attractive activity at the interface between politics, administration and the population awaits you. As a service department of the City of Zurich, we offer advanced employment conditions with excellent social benefits and flexible working hours. We support you in keeping your knowledge up-to-date and offer you a comprehensive training offer for your continuous development. We are a motivated and generationally diverse team and, in addition to a healthy performance orientation, attach importance to equality as well as a good balance between work and family / private life. Your workplace is located at Weberstrasse 5 in Zurich.

About us

Competence and precision in the city of today, for the Zurich of tomorrow.

Geomatics + Surveying (GeoZ) is a service department of the City of Zurich with around 85 employees (including 11 apprentices in four professions). We measure the city in high quality, make the geoinformation available to interested parties and strive for innovative solutions as a forward-looking and reliable service provider.
