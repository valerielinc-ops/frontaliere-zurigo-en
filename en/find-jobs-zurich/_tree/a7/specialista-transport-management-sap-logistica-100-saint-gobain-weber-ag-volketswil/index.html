Full description

To strengthen our SCM Operations & Excellence team, we are looking for an initiative and motivated personality as:

Specialist Transport Management & SAP Logistics, 100%

Their tasks

  • Ensuring the stable operation and further development of SAP Logistics Execution
  • Support and representation in freight billing including cost control
  • Analysis and correction of disruptions in transport and logistics processes
  • Advising and supporting departments and key users on SAP issues
  • Coordination of cooperation with internal logistics teams and external transport service providers
  • Processing of transport complaints and escalations
  • Identification and implementation of process improvements
  • Participation in logistics and SAP projects
  • Support for transport control and bulk material logistics
  • Implementation of system tests and accompanying releases and updates
  • Preparation of key figures, analyses and reporting
  • Documentation and implementation of new solutions and processes
  • Deputy Head of SCM Operations and Excellence during his absence

Your profile

  • Commercial, technical or logistical training
  • Further education in the field of logistics, transport or supply chain management
  • Several years of professional experience in transport logistics or supply chain management
  • In-depth knowledge of SAP as well as experience in dealing with ERP and transport management systems (TMS)
  • Project management experience
  • Very good knowledge of German, French and English is advantageous
  • Secure Stakeholder Management
  • Analytical thinking and high performance and solution competence
  • Resilient, pragmatic personality with a strong hands-on mentality

Their perspectives

  • Versatile area of responsibility in an international and yet family-run company
  • A corporate culture with space for personal responsibility and entrepreneurial ideas
  • An environment in which openness and appreciation are a matter of course
  • Attractive employment conditions with above-average social benefits
  • Support for your personal development
