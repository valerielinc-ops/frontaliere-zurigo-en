SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. An innovative working environment awaits you, in which you can drive major changes and thus make an important contribution to digitalization. Together with your team, you develop solutions for current challenges and implement them in the team. Further education is also not neglected for individual professional development. Further employment conditions and benefits can be found here. You can move that.

  • For passenger transport and the subsidiaries of SBB AG, you will process technical material master data of rail vehicle spare parts in SAP.
  • In your work as a specialist: for material and equipment master data, you open, manage, check and mutate the required vehicle-related spare parts master data.
  • By maintaining and managing the spare parts master data, you ensure that the maintenance as well as the spare parts procurement and management are carried out on time, economically and taking into account all relevant safety requirements.
  • You work closely with the internal and external vehicle and system procurement and support areas and ensure the timely introduction and use of vehicle spare parts with internal and external partners.
  • You also actively participate in procurement projects in the area of material and equipment master data.

You bring that with you.

  • This function requires great independence, a high degree of flexibility, entrepreneurial thinking and action as well as a strong process orientation. You communicate appropriately and sovereignly, even in demanding situations. In addition, you work exactly and with high quality awareness.
  • You are familiar with IT tools such as SAP, Microsoft 365 and other modern tools and methods.
  • You have completed basic technical training, which is advantageously supplemented by further training as a constructor:in. A plus is further training as a process specialist or the willingness to acquire this knowledge in a well-founded further education.
  • You preferably bring proven professional experience in the maintenance of rail vehicles and/or in work preparation (AVOR) in the technical railway sector.
  • For this position, you need very good oral and written knowledge of German (minimum C1) and advantageously also have knowledge of a second national language (F/I).
