You can look forward to that.

As a train driver, you make a big deal: at SBB Passenger Transport, you move around 482 million travelers annually. After completing the paid 15-month second training, the job takes you along the most beautiful railway lines in Switzerland. You bring our passengers to their destination on time and thus ensure a positive travel experience with the SBB. Full of perspectives is also your everyday work with attractive framework conditions for all life situations. Night and weekend work is also paid for with us – for example with 16 francs per hour for Sunday work. For night assignments, you also have time surcharges between 10 and 30 percent. This means that you have more free time than employees without night and weekend assignments. In addition, you can design your workload individually from 60 percent after the training to:to the train driver:in your consultation, with different part-time models available. Interested to learn more about the lateral entry as a train driver in a personal conversation? We look forward to seeing you from 22 to 24. 2026 at Zurich Central Station. All information about the SBB Berufswelten Café can be found at sbb.ch/berufsweltencafe. You can move that.

  • As a train driver, you not only take over the top of the train, but also responsibility for safety and punctuality on the busy rail network.
  • During your journey, you are mindful and respond quickly and correctly to signals, speed controls and unpredictable events.
  • You act independently and are always in contact with operating centers and control centers.
  • In addition to driving and maneuvering the trains, checking the technical condition before and after taking over the vehicle is also part of your tasks.
