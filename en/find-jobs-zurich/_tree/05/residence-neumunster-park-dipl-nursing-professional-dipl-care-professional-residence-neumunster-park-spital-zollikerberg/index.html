Residence Neumünster Park Dipl. Nursing professional / Dipl. Nursing specialist - Residenz Neumünster Park - Spital Zollikerberg, Zollikerberg (ZH).

Area: Nursing professions.

Welcome to your new place of work.

The Spitex of the Residence Neumünster Park in the health world Zollikerberg offers you an exciting job in the amount of 80-100 % as

Dipl. Nursing professional / Dipl. Care professional - Residence Neumünster Park

We continue to develop and are very happy about strengthening the team.

If you too want to make a difference in people’s lives, these are your tasks:

  • Care, support and advice of our tenants at home as our deaconesses in the source
  • Implementation of needs assessment and case management
  • Ensuring the care process with appropriate documentation
  • Correct collection of performance data
  • Cooperation with relatives, doctors, hospitals, homes and other specialist centres

This convinces us

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nursing professional DN II/HF/FH
  • High level of social, methodological and professional competence
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Contemporary IT skills (MS Office, Carecoach)
  • Self-employed and responsible working practices

Particularly convincing and unique are the following characteristics at your new place of work:

  • Short walking distance (without car) and interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation, as everything is under one roof: Spitex, living area of the deaconesses, hospital, family doctor's office
  • Palliative Care: holistic recording of tenants: inside and deaconesses
  • Various vessels for exchange such as specialist groups or (interprofessional) case discussions
  • Regular short-term training for all employees: on topics such as palliative care, aroma care, care documentation and dementia
  • Close cooperation and support by the nursing experts in complex care and care situations
  • Beautiful park with pond to breathe

In our 360° tour, get a first impression of your new place of work.

  • Own pension fund with super-compulsory participation of the employer
  • Ecobonus of up to CHF 650 per year
  • Generous working time allowances
  • Free use of the fitness room on the site
  • Easily accessible by public transport
  • Modern and fair employment conditions
  • Support for further training (financial, working time)

About the Residence Neumünster Park #LI-PZ1

The Residenz Neumünster Park is an operation of the Foundation Diakoniewerk Neumünster – Swiss Nursing School / Health World Zollikerberg, which covers the care and care of the tenants in over 80 apartments on the site, the facility management and the nursery. She is also responsible for the care of the deaconesses in the deaconess sisterhood of Neumünster.
