Full description

Faculty of Medicine

Start date by agreement, permanent position The Faculty of Medicine at the University of Zurich continuously develops its processes and digital systems landscape. To strengthen our IT and digitalisation team, we are looking for an analytical, technically skilled and communicative person to work at the interface between departments and IT. You analyse workflows and systems, actively identify opportunities to increase Digital Productivity, and develop suitable solutions together with the departments. The aim is simpler processes, less manual work, better use of data and more efficient ways of working. You take a technology-agnostic approach: solutions may be created using Microsoft 365, Power Platform and AI, by optimising existing systems such as SAP SLCM, through interfaces and integrations, or by developing custom applications. You implement smaller solutions and prototypes yourself; you design and support larger projects together with developers, central IT or external partners.

Tasks

  • Analyse work and business processes and identify opportunities for efficiency, automation and digitalisation
  • Critically review and simplify existing workflows
  • Translate business requirements into suitable technical solutions
  • Develop and further develop digital solutions, automations and prototypes
  • Use modern AI tools to create new solutions and support development
  • Implement solutions using Microsoft 365, Power Platform, Copilot Studio and other suitable technologies
  • Incorporate SAP SLCM and other existing specialist applications into process and solution concepts
  • Help shape data models, interfaces, integrations and cross-system data flows
  • Contribute to system replacements, migrations and the further development of the application landscape
  • Act as the interface between departments, developers, IT and external partners
  • Support employees in adopting new digital ways of working and using AI

Profile

You have a good technical understanding and can assess business problems from both process and systems perspectives.

Ideally, you have

  • Experience analysing, optimising and digitalising business processes
  • Practical experience designing or developing digital solutions
  • An understanding of data models, interfaces, APIs and integrations
  • Experience with Microsoft 365 and ideally Power Platform, Copilot Studio or Azure
  • SAP knowledge is an advantage
  • Experience with modern AI tools and an interest in new forms of software development
  • An analytical, structured and pragmatic approach to work
  • A high level of initiative and strong communication and consulting skills

A degree in business informatics, computer science, digital business or a related field is an advantage, but not a requirement. We also welcome people with relevant vocational training, further education, or well-founded self-acquired skills and corresponding practical experience. Programming skills, for example in Python, TypeScript, JavaScript or .NET, as well as experience with databases, cloud technologies, SAP or system migrations are an advantage. What matters to us is your technical understanding, your practical problem-solving skills and your ability to identify and implement opportunities for improvement.
