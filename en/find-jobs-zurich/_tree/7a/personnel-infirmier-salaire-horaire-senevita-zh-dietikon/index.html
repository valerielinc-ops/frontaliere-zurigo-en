Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,000 people. Outpatient and stationary offers merge seamlessly. Do you want to use your nursing expertise flexibly and determine your own assignments? For the Senevita Limmatfeld we are looking for motivated carers to strengthen our Care pool teams This position is aimed at nurses who want to provide flexible support – planned, needs-oriented or in the event of short-term failures – while attaching importance to professional framework conditions.
