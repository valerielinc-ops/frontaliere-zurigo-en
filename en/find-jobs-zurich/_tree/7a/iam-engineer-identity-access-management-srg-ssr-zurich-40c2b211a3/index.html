IAM Engineer Identity & Access Management — SRG SSR

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

The Operations sector defines and coordinates SSR's strategic guidelines in the field of technology and production. Safe and reliable management of identity and access guarantees the personnel and other authorised access rights necessary for applications and systems.

Together with the team, it ensures the operation and evolution of our Omada Identity-based cloud IAM solution. Integrate new target systems, automate processes and refine role and authorisation models. It contributes to secure, efficient and traceable management of digital identity and access rights.

Shipments

In Operations, it deals with the following aspects of identity management and access.

  • Manage and monitor the Omada Identity cloud solution and ensure its operation. It identifies abnormalities, analyzes causes and adopts lasting solutions.
  • Design and create connections to new target systems via connectors and APIs, ensuring reliable data exchange.
  • It develops and automates processes along the entire life cycle of identities, especially when recruiting, function changes and exits from the company.
  • In collaboration with industry managers, it improves workflows, role models and authorization processes and approval and recertification of access rights.
  • Plan and manage changes, tests and releases. It keeps the technical documentation up to date in compliance with safety, quality and governance requirements.
  • Work closely with business units, IT and security teams, external partners and suppliers. He participates in the design of technical solutions and shares your knowledge with the team.

Requirements

  • He completed a professional training or university course in computer science, or acquired equivalent qualifications through work experience.
  • It has at least two years of professional experience in managing identity and access (IAM) or in governance and administration of identity (IGA).

The experience with Omada Identity is an advantage.

  • It has experience in integrating target systems through connectors and interfaces, such as REST API, SCIM or LDAP. Knows directory services such as Active Directory and Microsoft Enter ID.
  • It has good scripting skills, such as PowerShell or Python, and experience in process automation and workflows. Knowledge of Infrastructure as Code (IaC) is an advantage.
  • Address problems with method and analysis capabilities, especially when it has to analyze the causes of accidents and identify solutions. He knows role models, the principle of minimum privilege and procedures for the safe assignment of permissions. She likes to work in team and can adapt communication to those in front.
  • Master Italian, German or French at level C1. It also has a good knowledge of English to collaborate with partners and suppliers and consult technical documentation.

What we offer

  • A role of responsibility that allows you to directly contribute to the security of information and ensure the availability of SSR digital work tools.
  • A wide range of autonomy to evolve an IAM solution in the cloud and automate the main processes of identity management and permissions.
  • A varied technological environment in which it cooperates closely with business units, IT staff and external partners.
