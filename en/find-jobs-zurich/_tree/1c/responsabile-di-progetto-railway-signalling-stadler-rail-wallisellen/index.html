Full description

PROFILE

  • Completed university studies in the field of engineering, for example mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, mechatronics, industrial engineering or a comparable discipline.
  • Several years of experience in project management, preferably in the railway or transport industry.
  • Experience in managing interdisciplinary technical projects with responsibility for performance, deadlines, budget, quality and customer satisfaction.
  • Knowledge of relevant standards and regulations in the railway sector, such as TSI CCS and UNISIG, are beneficial.
  • Experience with ETCS onboard systems or track signalling technology is an advantage.
  • Strong communication and problem-solving skills as well as competence in stakeholder management.
  • Structured, proactive and result-oriented working methods and a high degree of personal responsibility.
  • Very good user knowledge in Microsoft Office and Microsoft Project.
  • Very good knowledge of English
  • Knowledge of German and other languages is an advantage.
  • Willingness to travel about 10-25%.

TASKS

  • Technical and commercial management of projects integrating ETCS onboard systems.
  • Responsibility for projects over the entire project life cycle, from handover by sales to the end of the guarantee phase.
  • Ensure the achievement of project goals in terms of deadlines, budget, quality, security and customer satisfaction.
  • Planning, coordination and control of all project activities to ensure successful and timely project implementation.
  • Active stakeholder management as well as building and maintaining trusting relationships with customers, suppliers, internal teams and partners.
  • Proactive identification, evaluation and management of risks, opportunities, changes and project dependencies.
  • Monitoring project performance, including cost, revenue, forecast and profitability.
  • Ensure compliance with contractual obligations and accurate and timely project reporting.
  • Leadership, motivation and further development of interdisciplinary project teams and promotion of effective collaboration and a strong team culture.
  • Continuous development and optimization of project management methods, processes, tools and best practices.
  • Active contribution to improving project execution and business success.
