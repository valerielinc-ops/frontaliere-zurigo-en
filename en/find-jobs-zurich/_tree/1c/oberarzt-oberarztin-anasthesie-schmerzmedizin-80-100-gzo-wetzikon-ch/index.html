Full description

The GZO Spital Wetzikon is an innovative acute care hospital in the Zurich Oberland with 150 beds and around 900 employees. As a company with high customer orientation and appreciative communication, we are committed to our patients every day. Our interdisciplinary operations department performs approximately 4,500 operations annually in four modern operating rooms and one intervention room. Do you like to work interdisciplinary and varied? The Department of Anesthesiology supervises patients in elective or emergency procedures in the specialist areas: visceral surgery, complex bariatric surgery, traumatology of the musculoskeletal system, orthopaedics, hand surgery, urology, ORL as well as a broad gynecological spectrum of surgical gynecology and obstetrics.
