Full description

As a Digital Media Designer, you share responsibility for creative output at Digitec and Galaxus. You work at the interface of design, motion and code and develop campaigns with design and technical precision, as well as interactive and animated applications for various media channels.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Headquarters, Pfingstweidstrasse 60b, 8005 Zurich

Mansioni

  • You design and design digital advertising materials and motion graphics – from moving posters (DOOH) to social media to web-based applications – and shape our brand perception with unconventional content.
  • You implement interactive and data-based formats directly in the code (HTML, CSS, JS) and build animation templates that allow formats to be scaled efficiently and in line with the brand
  • You work in interdisciplinary teams closely with designers, art directors, project managers and actors from different areas of the company and bring your expertise to the projects

Requirements

  • 3-5 years: campaign and brand communication in digital marketing
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): Interactive media, motion design, digital media design
  • In addition to a completed design training (ideally with a focus on digital media), you will bring several years of professional experience in the design field.
  • You have high demands on design and animation, as well as a secure feeling for typography, composition and movement
  • You’ll work routinely and securely with Figma, Adobe AfterEffects, AI-powered tools like Claude Code, and experience with automated and generative design principles.
  • As a team player, you promote an open feedback culture
  • You speak our company language German fluently and complement your linguistic skills with negotiable English. Other languages are welcome but not required
  • Understanding UX/UI design processes, design systems and flexible/adaptive layouts are beneficial, but not mandatory
  • German (flowing)
  • English (flowing)
