Zurich From January 2027 100% Business consultancy services Everyone welcome ID: 772386WD

Your impact

  • Take on a leading professional and organizational role within the Real Estate Advisory Team.
  • Lead project teams in the evaluation of individual properties and real estate portfolios, the creation of portfolio analyses, profitability calculations and property strategies and present the results directly to the customer.
  • As a proven expert, be the contact person for various PwC service lines and provide support with real estate-specific questions in the context of financial due diligence and auditing.
  • Actively drive innovation through data analytics and digitalization and develop new methods and approaches to help shape the PwC standard.
  • Be prepared to represent and further develop the Real Estate Valuation area in a nationally and internationally operating consulting firm.
  • With your commitment and leadership, make a decisive contribution to continuing the success story of PwC in the field of Real Estate Advisory.

Your skill set

  • ETH, university or university of applied sciences degree (bachelor or master) in economics (BWL/VWL), architecture or civil engineering and an affinity for business issues.
  • Several years of work experience in the field of valuation of real estate of institutional investors and project management.
  • Practical, in-depth knowledge with relevant training (MAS in Real Estate, CFA, CAIA or similar).
  • You are well connected in the real estate industry and preferably a member of industry associations (SEK, SIV, RICS).
  • Complete oral and written knowledge of German as well as very good knowledge of English. Knowledge of French is an advantage.
  • Very good analytical skills and strengths in the field of financial modeling, an above-average willingness to perform and motivated to take on new challenges in a dynamic environment.
