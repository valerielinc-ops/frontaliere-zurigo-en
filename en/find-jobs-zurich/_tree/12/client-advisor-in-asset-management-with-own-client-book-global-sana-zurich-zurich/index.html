Job description As a client advisor, look after asset management They provide wealthy private clients and entrepreneurs holistically in all questions relating to asset structuring, asset management and retirement. They bring an existing customer book or a customer portfolio and receive the opportunity at PROGREDO AG to continue and expand it on an independent, professional and FINMA-regulated platform. As a central contact person for your customers, you understand their personal, entrepreneurial and financial situation. Together with our specialists from asset management and financial planning, you will develop individual solutions that go beyond pure investment advice. Your existing customer book forms the basis of your activity. At the same time, we support you with professional structures, investment competence, marketing and sales resources as well as internal specialists in the further development of your customer relationships and in the development of additional customer assets.

Tasks

  • Takeover and long-term support of an existing own customer book
  • Holistic advice to wealthy private clients and entrepreneurs
  • Development of individual investment and asset strategies
  • Asset management, financial planning, retirement and succession consultancy services
  • Systematic further development of existing customer relationships and identification of additional consulting potential
  • Acquisition of new customers through their own network, recommendations and joint sales activities
  • Regular review of customer portfolios and personal strategy discussions
  • Close cooperation with asset management, financial planning and internal and external specialists
  • Ensuring professional, regulatoryly flawless and documented customer support
  • Representation of PROGREDO AG at customer events, network events and professional events

Qualification

  • Several years of professional experience in the service of wealthy private clients or entrepreneurs, ideally at a bank, an independent asset manager, multi-family office or financial service provider
  • Existing and substantial customer book with realistic transmission potential
  • Demonstrable experience in the acquisition, support and further development of demanding customer relationships
  • In-depth knowledge in asset management and structuring of private assets
  • Further education in the financial sector, for example financial advisor IAF, financial planner with federal law. Professional ID, CFA, AZEK or comparable qualification
  • Good understanding of the Swiss financial market and relevant regulatory requirements
  • Strong advisory, communication and relationship skills
  • Entrepreneurial mindset, high level of initiative and pleasure in building long-term customer relationships
  • Independent, structured and responsible working methods
  • High standards of discretion, reliability and personal consulting quality

Benefits

  • An independent and FINMA-regulated platform for the long-term support and development of your customer book
  • Entrepreneurial scope and short decision-making paths
  • Access to professional asset management and sound financial planning
  • Support in customer acquisition, customer development and market development
  • Attractive remuneration model with fixed wages and success-related participation in the managed and newly acquired customer assets
  • Home office opportunity after successful familiarization
  • Modern work equipment and professional internal structures
  • Professional development opportunities
  • Leadership perspective as a team leader or in the management of customer consulting or sales
  • Long-term entrepreneurial perspective with successful development of the customer portfolio

Would you like to continue to serve your customers personally and independently, offer them a wider range of services and further develop your existing customer book on a professional platform? Then we look forward to meeting you and discussing together how you can successfully continue your customer relationships and your entrepreneurial future at PROGREDO AG. PROGREDO is the leader in the field of succession and company handover for the Swiss SME. We support entrepreneurs as a one-stop solution in the handover of their company in all tax, legal and financial matters.
