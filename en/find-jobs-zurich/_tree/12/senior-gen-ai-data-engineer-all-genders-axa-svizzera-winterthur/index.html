80-100%, Winterthur / Smart Working

Shape your future – together with us!

Want to be part of something big? We offer you a great team in a dynamic environment around the hottest topic!

In our “Distribution Strategy, Project & Communication” department, the distribution strategy of AXA CH is realized, concepts are developed and implemented together with the product factory, the technology teams and the various sales channels. Our goal is to create the best customer experience – true to our motto: «We create unique AXA moments».

We are looking for an experienced, technically strong personality who loves responsibility – and takes our productive AI applications to the next level. As part of a team of experts from Data Driven Distribution and the Competence Center Data & Analytics, you will focus on solving complex challenges with the help of GenAI, Agents and more. You can leverage your skills across the entire Data Science Lifecycle, from the problem to the operation of the solution.

You develop AI applications within the distribution and for the entire AXA Switzerland. Help with your analytical skills You keep the strategic orientation on a modern course.

If you are a committed, motivated and initiative personality who wants to actively take responsibility for conception with management and implementation in transversal cooperation, then you are at the right place.

This is what awaits you

  • You are part of a team and bring (gen) AI use cases to success
  • Develop, optimize and operate AI applications in the cloud (GCP) that efficiently use our resources and create measurable value
  • Keep up to date with the latest developments in AI
  • You are in constant and close contact with the engineers of the GenAI Core Framework and the Distribution Strategy Team
  • Use your analytical skills to help shape the Data Driven Distribution strategy
  • You support our business stakeholders in the application of GenAI and analytical topics

You bring that with you

  • Sound data science tech stack, especially Python, SQL knowledge and experience with DevOps (Git-Flow, etc.)
  • At least 3 years of experience implementing AI-based applications on Google Cloud Platform (GCP)
  • Experience in the concepts of Generativer AI
  • RAG
  • LLM frameworks (e.g. LangChain, Vector Stores/DBs)
  • A2A and MCP
  • React Loops
  • Spec Driven Development
