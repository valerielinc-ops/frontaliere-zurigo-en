Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School Rychenberg Administrative Assistant with focus on Personnel 50% The Cantonal School Rychenberg Winterthur is a long school with the profiles old and new languages as well as PPP, a bilingual school course German/English and a specialised school with the profiles Pedagogy and Communication and Information. With around 1300 students and 170 teachers, our school is one of the major schools in the Canton of Zurich. We are looking for an administrative assistant by 1 March 2027 or by agreement with a focus on Personnel 50% Their tasks

  • They take over the personnel administration around our teachers and support the school management and the adjunct in personnel processes.
  • They maintain the personnel data and keep the physical and electronic personnel files.
  • They deal with administrative requests and requests, for example on seniority gifts, leave or individual training.
  • They take over the administrative handling of vicarial missions and the associated compensation.
  • They assist in the running of the hourly continuum.
  • They take on further administrative and organisational tasks. Your profile
  • They have a commercial degree (EFZ) as well as further training in human resources or corresponding professional experience.
  • Experience in personnel administration as well as knowledge in education or public administration are beneficial.
  • You are a service-oriented, empathetic and dutiful personality and work independently, structured and solution-oriented.
  • Discretion and sensitivity in dealing with confidential information are a matter of course for you.
  • They have very good MS Office knowledge and bring interest and openness to digital processes and their further development.
  • They express themselves verbally and in writing and stylishly in German. Our offer
  • A versatile and responsible activity in a lively school business.
  • Personal responsibility and design possibilities in the task area.
  • A collegial working environment and contemporary employment conditions in accordance with cantonal requirements. Your application Have we aroused your interest? We look forward to your application. Please submit your documents exclusively via the online tool by the 15th at the latest. December 2026. For any questions, our adjunct Sabina Peter (sabina.peter@krw.ch or telephone 052 244 04 12) is at your disposal. Further information about our school can be found on our website www.krw.ch.
