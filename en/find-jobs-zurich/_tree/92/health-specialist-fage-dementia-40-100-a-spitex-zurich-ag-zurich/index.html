Full description

Mrs. M. doesn’t know your name anymore, but she smiles when you come in the door. Today it seems uneasy. You're taking Time for a conversation, measure her blood pressure and walk with her for a small round. Later you pull her support stockings Afterwards, you call her daughter. You keep your observations in writing and share them with your team. In our Memory Care teams, you accompany people with dementia over a longer period of time and become a familiar caregiver in their everyday life. You know your customers, their habits and their stories. You create orientation, security and trust and support people to live in their home as long as possible. Professional Health (FaGe) - dementia 40-100% (a) (a)lle are addressed. Gender, origin, age? We are interested in your qualification and enthusiasm for work. YOUR TASKS

  • You accompany and care for people with dementia as a reliable caregiver in their everyday life, you design individual and resource-oriented activities.
  • You observe changes in health status or behavior and document them carefully.
  • You are in contact with relatives and specialists and actively contribute your observations to the team.
  • You recognize changes in the state of health at an early stage, initiate nursing measures and coordinate further care.

You bring with you

  • Completed training as a specialist in health EFZ.
  • Alternatively completed training as a specialist EFZ with degree from 2023 (for degrees before 2023, the H+ Diploma in Medical Engineering is additionally required).
  • Empathetic and respectful attitude towards people with dementia and understanding of their individual needs.
  • Strong observation skills, networked thinking and interest in close cooperation in the customer support network.
  • You convince with your high personal responsibility, your organizational talent and your reliable commitment in day-to-day care.
  • Ideally, you like to travel by e-bike and appreciate the independent mobility between missions.

With us you are more than a FaGe. You become a trusted caregiver for people with dementia and their loved ones. With your accompaniment you give orientation and security. You make a big difference every day. We are delighted on your application
