RegioSpitex Limmattal is an important partner in healthcare With around 135 employees, RegioSpitex provides hospital-external care and support for the inhabitants of Dietikon, Schlieren and Urdorf. To complement our team, we are looking for a Professional / Health Professional (Pensum selectable 40-100%) Your multifaceted tasks

  • You promote self-employment and self-responsibility within the scope of the individual possibilities of customers
  • You carry out the treatment care according to your competences
  • You work with different professional groups
  • You perform administrative tasks
  • You accompany our learners FaGe/AGS in everyday work

Your skills
