Full description

This is what awaits you

At Mobiliar Generalagentur Wil, we value sustainable customer relationships and high service quality. In sales support, you make a significant contribution to the targeted support of our consulting team and the professional support of our customers. For this, we are looking for a committed personality who supports our insurance and pension consultants in day-to-day business and actively contributes to the reliable support of our customers. With your structured working method and your understanding of services, you strengthen your confidence in Mobiliar as the most personal insurance company in Switzerland.

This is what you do with us

You support our insurance consultants in their day-to-day operations and assume administrative and coordinating tasks on your own responsibility.

This includes the creation of correspondence, evaluations and the processing of offers, applications and contract documents. In direct customer contact on the phone or at the counter, you can support our customers competently and reliably.

You answer inquiry and coordinate appointments across all insurance industries. As part of the sales support team, you work closely with colleagues and ensure smooth processes.

This is what you bring with you

  • Basic commercial education and/or professional experience of several years within the insurance industry
  • Training insurance intermediary VBV or willingness to complete it
  • Strong customer orientation and high quality awareness
  • Interest in a versatile field of activity and willingness to learn new things
  • Structured, independent and reliable working method Team orientation, high willingness to help and professional attitude

Working with us

At the general agency Wil you can expect a committed and diverse team. Together, we are strengthening our regional roots and creating consistent 5 star experiences for our customers. We maintain an open, respectful and collaborative exchange and work together in a solution-oriented and responsible manner. You can expect clear structures, short decision paths, a careful introduction and contemporary employment conditions at the Mobiliar.

Your place of work is at our main agency in Ebnat-Kappel and the job is taken by appointment.

In order to perform this function, the Insurance Supervision Act requires the conclusion of an insurance intermediary/in VBV, Profil Allbranche. If this is not available, we are happy to support with tailor-made internal training.

This is what we offer

Participation in success

If we are successful, our employees also benefit. Each year, the distribution of a successful share and its amount is decided.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses. We participate in job-relevant training.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer up to 25% discount on bonuses, discounts on mobile phones and free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
