Full description

What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family company with over 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessories as well as the provision of financial services. Emil Frey IT Solutions AG provides various central ICT services for the Emil Frey Group and manages the server backend systems in two data centers together with the hybrid cloud infrastructure for approx. 10,000 users who work at over 100 locations in Europe. Design a secure, stable and sustainable IT infrastructure for our internationally active group of companies as Head of IT Infrastructure. In this key role, you will lead the infrastructure division with six teams, ensure reliable operations and strategically develop architecture, technologies and services. You combine operational excellence with clear governance, compliance and consistent transformation. Your tasks

  • Professional and human management of the entire infrastructure area with six teams as well as the promotion of active, transparent cooperation
  • Ensuring stable, secure and highly available infrastructure operations and professional service provision
  • Responsibility for infrastructure architecture and informed technology decisions over the entire life cycle
  • Strategic planning and further development of the areas network, endpoint & server, storage and cloud systems
  • Initiation and control of infrastructure, consolidation and transformation projects, for example in the data center and cloud environment
  • Control of external suppliers and partners to ensure reliable, economical service provision
  • Planning and support of the annual audits by the external audit as well as ensuring all IT compliance, operational security and regulatory requirements
  • Control of budget and resource planning including the needs-based prioritization of projects
  • Development, development and monitoring of IT security policies and processes, including the Internal Control System (ICS)
  • Active support of your teams in challenging operational and conceptual issues Your profile
  • Completed studies in computer science, business informatics or a comparable technical education
  • Several years of leadership and management experience in a demanding IT infrastructure organization, ideally with several specialized teams
  • In-depth knowledge in the areas of network, workplace/endpoint, server, storage, data center and cloud infrastructure
  • Demonstrate experience in infrastructure architecture, technology and lifecycle decisions as well as in the implementation of complex transformation projects
  • Secure handling of IT governance, IT compliance, IKS, audit requirements and operational security requirements
  • Experience in managing suppliers and partners as well as in budget, resource and portfolio planning
  • Strong decision-making, prioritization and implementation strength, even in challenging situations
  • Clear, user-friendly communication, professional appearance and constructive handling of different interests and different stakeholders
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, high customer orientation and pleasure in actively and pragmatically driving change
  • Confident in German and English
