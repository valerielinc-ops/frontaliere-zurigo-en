Put to the Point

Are you passionate about technology, exceptional vehicles and careful work? Then you fit perfectly with us! In a collaborative team, you can expect a wide range of tasks, from diagnosis to repair and service work on our vehicles and systems.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20345
  • Place of work: Überlandstrasse 17, 8340 Hinwil

You can make this contribution

  • Perform diagnostic work, fix malfunctions and error messages
  • Carry out service and repair work on passenger cars, light commercial vehicles and trailers and make them available for MFRP
  • Carry out maintenance work in accordance with manufacturer specifications, technical instructions and safety regulations
  • Assistance in preparation for deployment, material provision, material take-back as well as in special orders and operations
  • Record defect reports and order spare parts in the SAP system
  • Caring for learners

This makes you unique

  • Completed vocational training as an automotive mechatronics specialist, specialisation in passenger cars or related professions
  • Cat B driving licence (cat C, CE and SUVA recognized forklift)
  • Good MS-Office and SAP knowledge
  • Independent, conscientious and forward working methods, with good organizational skills
  • Enjoying technical challenges and working on unique vehicles
  • Teamwork and customer-oriented advice
