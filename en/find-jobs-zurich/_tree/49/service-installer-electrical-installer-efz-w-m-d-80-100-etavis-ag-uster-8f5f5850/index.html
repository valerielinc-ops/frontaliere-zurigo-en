About us

As a team of 27 people, we mainly take care of high- and low-voltage installations in the office, industrial and commercial building sectors. We also use our know-how for installations in various areas of the railway industry, in residential conversions and in public buildings such as school buildings. We are also happy to develop projects in cooperation with our customers. The ETAVIS AG branch in Uster is strongly anchored in the region and provides optimal service around the clock.
