Full description

Children's Hospital Zurich Eleonorenstiftung | online since : 16.11.2026

Your tasks

  • You will support our HR team in a wide range of administrative and professional topics and get exciting insights into the entire HR lifecycle.
  • When handling absences as well as illness, accident and maternity cases, you take on important tasks. This includes keeping overviews, maintaining case histories and calculating and controlling daily allowances.
  • In the field of social insurance, you deal with various questions, control insurance bills and ensure careful documentation and reliable data maintenance.
  • You contribute to the monthly wage processing, process personnel mutations and ensure the correct maintenance and control of personnel and wage data through your careful working method.
  • Dealing with various HR systems is also part of your task. You support their application, take care of the data maintenance and contribute to ensuring a high data quality.
  • In addition, you will be involved in a wide range of administrative and organizational tasks in our HR departments. You create evaluations, process invoices and actively support the team in the organization and further development of our HR processes.

Your profile

  • You bring a completed commercial basic education.
  • First professional experience in the HR environment, ideally in the personnel administration, you have in your backpack.
  • Good MS-Office knowledge and a safe handling of Excel are available.
  • A good understanding of numbers as well as an exact, structured and reliable working method characterize you.
  • As a service-oriented personality, you enjoy working in a team and stand out through a discreet and responsible way of working.
  • You enjoy administrative tasks, interest in different HR departments and the motivation to familiarize yourself with new topics.
  • An independent working method, forward-thinking and the ability to keep track of various parallel tasks set you apart. Benefits
  • First experience in wage administration, payroll or in the processing of social security topics.
  • A further training in human resources, for example as an HR assistant or clerk/in human resources.
  • A further training as a clerk/social insurance or interest in completing one.
  • Knowledge of Swiss social security, especially in the areas of sickness, accident and maternity.
  • User knowledge in Hospis, Sunet, EasyLearn or comparable HR systems.
