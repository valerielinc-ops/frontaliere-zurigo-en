With your eye for detail you ensure quality

Correct performance recording is the basis for reliable billing. In the service management of the Hospital Limmattal, you assume a responsible function at the interface between the hospital, the clinic support and the billing.

We are looking for a committed personality who likes to look closely, recognize connections and competently support our clinics with questions about performance recording.

Direct applications are preferred, external recruitment offices are not treated as a priority.

Here they engage

  • They ensure the quality of ambulatory performance recording in the clinics and areas assigned to them
  • They check recorded services for completeness, plausibility and tariff correctness and make necessary corrections independently
  • You process and correct services in the ERP system as well as in the capture tool IBI-care
  • You recognize optimization potential, contribute your ideas and support you in continuously developing processes
  • You work closely with the performance managers and together with invoicing support in the clarification of process and validator errors
  • With your careful and solution-oriented way of working, you make an important contribution to correct, complete and efficient performance recording and billing.
  • have basic knowledge of performance management and are interested in the relationship between performance recording and billing
  • have stable knowledge of the current tariff system
  • bring stable user knowledge in ERP systems, power acquisition tools and Windows
  • work carefully, reliably and quality-consciously and take pleasure in detecting errors and discrepancies and finding solutions
  • Keep an overview even in a varied and demanding working day and are characterized by resilience and flexibility
  • Constructive and interdisciplinary cooperation is a matter of course for you
  • like to work independently, take responsibility for your area of responsibility and actively contribute to the team
  • Reduced employee parking
  • Motorcycle parking, lockable bicycle parking
