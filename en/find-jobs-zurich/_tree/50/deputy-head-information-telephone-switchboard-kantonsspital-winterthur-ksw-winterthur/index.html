Full description

For our organizational unit Information and telephone center with around 12 employees, we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement a committed, service-oriented and organization-strong personality as deputy management. The information and telephone exchange are important first points of contact for our patients, relatives, visitors as well as internal and external stakeholders. We connect callers with hospitalised patients, secretariats, office centres, departments and staff. In addition, we provide information about the hospital, our areas, opening and visiting hours, the car park, appointments and emergency operations. In your role, you support the management in the personnel, organizational and professional management of the area. You take over the deputy in their absence, are an important contact person for the team and actively shape the further development of the organizational unit. At the same time, you regularly work in day-to-day operations.

Tasks

  • Support of the management in the personnel, organizational and professional management of the information and telephone center as well as ensuring regular exchange and close cooperation with the management
  • Assuming the deputy of the management in the event of their absence as well as ensuring a smooth operation and contact person for the employees in case of technical, organizational and personnel concerns
  • Active participation in day-to-day operations in the information and telephone exchange as well as ensuring competent, efficient and service-oriented support for internal and external stakeholders
  • Responsibility for operational and holiday planning in cooperation with the management as well as general administrative and organisational tasks
  • Co-creation and further development of processes and workflows as well as support for the introduction of new processes and the continuous optimization of cooperation
  • Participation in organising and managing team meetings and promoting an open culture of feedback, reflection and learning and supporting team development

Profile

  • Several years of professional experience in the service sector, ideally with direct customer contact. First leadership experience or experience in a coordinating function beneficial
  • Strong organisational skills, a high sense of responsibility and good administrative and commercial skills. Good MS-Office knowledge.
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing as well as good knowledge of at least one other foreign language, preferably English
  • Strong communication, diplomatic and patient personality with a high level of social competence, secure and professional appearance and a well-kept appearance
  • Strong service orientation and pleasure in dealing with different people and concerns. Resilient and solution-oriented personality who keeps an overview even in hectic situations and acts sovereignly
  • High flexibility and readiness for irregular assignments in early, medium and late service between 05.45 and 22.00 o'clock as well as on weekends (no fixed working days)

Benefits: Work and private life should fit together. Therefore, the KSW offers you – where the company makes it possible – flexible and flexible working hours, home office, generous maternity leave, paternity leave and employment levels that can be adapted to different phases of life. Develop yourself further professionally and personally: Every year, around 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses are open to you at KSW. We also support external training – temporally and/or financially.

  • Sometimes it needs support. In the event of personal stress, prolonged illness or difficult experiences in your professional or private life, various confidential discussions are available at KSW.
  • At KSW we work together and for each other. Appreciation, open and respectful communication and cooperation across professional groups and departments shape our everyday work. You should be able to contribute with your ideas and strengths and develop.

Your future is also ensured: You benefit from very good social benefits and a pension fund solution with BVK, which is significantly above the legal minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, you are very well covered as a KSW employee.

  • Your health is important to us. Benefit from discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training and courses as well as discounts in pharmacies and selected health insurance companies.
  • Family and work can be well connected with us. The daycare center La Luna with spacious opening hours is right next to the KSW. In addition, the KSW supports you – depending on income – financially with the crib costs.

There is also something to discover outside of work: Thanks to our cooperation with cultural partners in Winterthur and the surrounding area, you regularly benefit from discounts for concerts and theater performances.
