The LANDI cooperatives support Swiss farmers in the economic development of their farms. Together with the brands Volg, TopShop and AGROLA, the LANDI cooperatives also provide the population in rural Switzerland with food, everyday goods and energy. LANDI Züri Unterland is successfully active in the Zurich Unterland region. For our LANDI stores Höri, Eglisau, Embrach, Bassersdorf and Nürensdorf we are looking for a sales employee from February 2027 to July 2027.
