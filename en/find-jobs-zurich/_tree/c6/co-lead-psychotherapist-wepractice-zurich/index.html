Full description

Good psychotherapy arises where people like to work. We are convinced of this.In this role gestaltest You are a place where therapists can develop and strengthen each other’s back.WePractice is the leading outpatient practice group with the aim of making high-quality psychotherapy accessible to everyone.

Luogo di lavoro: WePractice, Zurich Stauffacher, Lutherstrasse 2, 8004 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Together with a second co-management, you will lead a practice and assume the technical, economic and personnel responsibility for the location.
  • You yourself are therapeutically active and treat your own patients: inside.
  • You develop psychotherapists in further education, provide regular supervision and ensure the treatment quality.
  • You are the bridge between practice team and company: You bring the view of your team and translate decisions from WePractice into everyday practice.
  • You create a culture of trust and openness, actively participate in the team and promote cohesion and a sense of belonging.
  • You ensure good organizational conditions at the location, so that therapists can concentrate on what is at the center of their work: their patients.
  • You bring in ideas, question what already exists and actively shape the further development of WePractice.

Requirements

  • 3 years of professional experience: in psychotherapy
  • Completed basic or further education: Eidg. recognized:r Psychotherapist:in
  • You have a federally recognized title in psychotherapy and meet all additional requirements for OKP approval.
  • You have at least 3 years of professional experience as a psychotherapist: from specialist title.
  • You enjoy leading people and accompanying their development with clarity and trust instead of hierarchy.
  • You take responsibility, stay calm even in challenging situations and find pragmatic solutions.
  • You have stylish knowledge of German in word and writing.
  • First leadership or team responsibility is beneficial, even if you have taken it informally so far.
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Our offices are warm and modern – a place where you and your patients feel comfortable.
  • Individual training: We accompany you with supervisions, case discussions, development discussions and training time / budget.
  • Flat hierarchies: Be part of an appreciative community that supports you and is lived in cooperation at eye level.
  • Design and decision-making leeway: Choose your patient freely and design the therapy approach so that it fits pragmatically and meaningfully to the care.
  • Flexible working hours: Design your working hours as they fit your everyday life and find your own rhythm.
  • Flexible work location / home office: Do therapies or administratives from home and decide for yourself where you are most productive.
