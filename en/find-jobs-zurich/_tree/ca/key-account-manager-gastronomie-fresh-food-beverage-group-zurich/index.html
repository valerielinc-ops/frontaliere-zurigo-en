Key Account Manager Gastronomy — Fresh Food & Beverage Group

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Do you live in French-speaking Switzerland, know the regional gastronomic market and like to develop lasting customer relations? Do you have a sense of business, a strong network and a desire to move things? Then join us and develop with us our market in French-speaking Switzerland!

Luogo di lavoro: Fresh Food & Beverage Group, FFB-Group Josefstrasse Zürich, Josefstrasse 212/216, 8005 Zürich

Important remarks

All applications are welcome, but only online applications can be considered. Applications sent by mail will be eliminated for data protection reasons and will not be returned.

Mansioni

  • Coordinate, develop and implement sales and profitability objectives in French-speaking Switzerland, including in Valais up to Martigny
  • Ensure that sales targets and agreed budgets are met
  • Manage and ensure responsibility for the assortment for gastronomy
  • Observe prices and competition and identify market trends
  • Participate in projects, events, fairs and exhibitions

Required

  • Several years of experience: in a comparable function
  • Completed vocational learning (CFC): in the field of gastronomy or diploma of a higher hotel school
  • Experience in Key Account Management, preferably in food sales
  • Targeted continuing training in sales and/or marketing
  • Excellent negotiating skills, ability to impose and communication ease
  • Strong customer orientation
  • Solution-oriented approach and autonomous working method
  • Network established in food trade
  • French (Very good mastery)
  • German (Very good mastery)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Mobile subscription: Mobile subscriptions at preferential rates with Wingo, Digitec and Sunrise for you and three people from your surroundings.
  • Occupational insurance: Insurance at the Migros Pension Fund, which offers benefits well above the legal minimum; 2/3 are supported by the company.
  • Social benefits: Daily allowances for max. 730 days, with 100% covered salary
  • Points Cumulus: Points x3 on food, points x6 on all other products.
  • Migros Bank: Preferential Interest Rates on Migros Bank Savings Accounts
  • Holidays: 5 weeks of leave for all staff, 6 weeks from 50 years, 7 weeks from 60 years, possibility to acquire additional days of leave.
  • Sport and fitness offers: 70% refund of the cost per course (max. CHF 1000 per course).
  • Migros Club School: 70% refund max. course fees / CHF 1,000 per course without annual limit
  • Tutoement: Hello everybody - and if we were to have a little talk!

Contatto

Candidature and contact David Biedermann Recruitment / HR Business Partner

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of files: We constantly review the applications received, pre-screen and forward them to the manager. The decision to pursue or eliminate the recruitment process is communicated by telephone or e-mail. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Interview with HR and the department: The interview is conducted in distanciel or face-to-face with the HR person responsible and/or the specialized department. It serves primarily to learn more about personal and professional skills and allows both parties to assess cultural compatibility with the company and the team. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Team knowledge: Defining a person's compatibility with the team is an important factor for both the employee and the employer in deciding whether or not to engage. This is why we offer you the opportunity to get to know each other better in an informal setting.
  • Offer and establishment of contract: The main points of the contract are checked again after our verbal job offer. The establishment of the contract is then initiated by mutual agreement before being sent by post. Return time: up to 3 weeks

Grado di occupazione: 100%
