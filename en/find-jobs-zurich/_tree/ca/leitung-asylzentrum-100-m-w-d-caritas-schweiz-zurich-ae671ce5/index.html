Head of Asylum Centre 100% (m/f/d)

Caritas Switzerland has been running asylum centres for families, individuals and unaccompanied minor asylum seekers (MNA) at several locations in the Canton of Zurich on behalf of the Cantonal Social Office (KSA) since March 2024. As a result of a follow-up, we are looking for a dedicated and responsible centre management for an MNA centre.

  • Overall responsibility for personnel planning, personnel management and personnel development

  • Responsibility for the quality of support, order processing and documentation as well as for the implementation of work processes and structural adjustments

  • Designated personnel management experience

  • Strong communication skills and very good knowledge of German
