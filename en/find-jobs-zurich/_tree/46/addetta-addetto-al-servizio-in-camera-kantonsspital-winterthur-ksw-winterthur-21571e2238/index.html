Full description

For the guest service we are immediately looking for a committed and service-oriented personality as support in the room service. Our goal is to offer our patients a professional and needs-oriented service in the hotel industry. The Room Service offers a versatile working environment around patients, their relatives or visitors, in which a high degree of flexibility and adaptability is required.

Tasks

  • A high service orientation as well as a friendly, well-kept appearance
  • Acceptance of meal and drink requests, orders via Orgacard
  • Meals, drinks and room services
  • Entry and exit support
  • Participation in the further development of the guest service area
  • Ensuring professional cooperation with care and other interface partners

Profile

  • Swiss Federal certificate of competence specialist in hospitality-housekeeping, restaurant specialist or training with EFZ in related work
  • Or several years of professional experience in the hospitality industry in health care
  • Networked mindset, structured, working and experienced handling of IT applications
  • Very good knowledge of German (verbal and written mind). Level B2
  • Service-oriented presence
  • Reliability and independent working methods
  • Flexibility, resilience and readiness for irregular assignments, weekend services and public holidays
  • Willingness to engage in different patient situations

Benefits: Work and private life should fit together. Therefore, the KSW offers you – where the company makes it possible – flexible and flexible working hours, home office, generous maternity leave, paternity leave and employment levels that can be adapted to different phases of life. Develop yourself further professionally and personally: Every year, around 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses are open to you at KSW. We also support external training – temporally and/or financially.

  • Sometimes it needs support. In the event of personal stress, prolonged illness or difficult experiences in your professional or private life, various confidential discussions are available at KSW.
  • At KSW we work together and for each other. Appreciation, open and respectful communication and cooperation across professional groups and departments shape our everyday work. You should be able to contribute with your ideas and strengths and develop.

Your future is also ensured: You benefit from very good social benefits and a pension fund solution with BVK, which is significantly above the legal minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, you are very well covered as a KSW employee.
