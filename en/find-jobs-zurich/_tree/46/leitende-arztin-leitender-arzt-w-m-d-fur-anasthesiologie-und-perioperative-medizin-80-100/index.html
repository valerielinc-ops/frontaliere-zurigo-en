Full description

The range of clinical services of the Institute of Anesthesiology and Perioperative Medicine (IFA) includes all anesthesia procedures for outpatient and inpatient surgical and diagnostic procedures, including comprehensive pre- and postoperative care. Further central tasks are the treatment of acute and chronic pain, the shock room care of critically ill patients and the internal clinical resuscitation service. In addition, the institute provides emergency medical specialists for preclinical ground and airborne emergency care as well as the intensive care service in close cooperation with Schutz & Rettung Zürich (SRZ) and the Swiss Rescue Air Guard (REGA). Within the framework of highly specialized medicine, the USZ also has service contracts for all transplantations and for the treatment of severely burnt and multiple injured patients. The institute is a category A1 continuing education institution certified by the Swiss Institute for Medical Education and Training (SIWF).
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.
