Full description

Location: Zurich Work Pattern Percentage: 80–100% About the Role Join our Sales & Service team and help clients make it easier for their people to sell, serve and support customers. As a junior Business Analyst/Consultant, you’ll get close to how a business really works: listening to users, understanding where processes break down and helping turn those insights into practical improvements. You’ll work alongside experienced colleagues on customer-focused technology projects, building your consulting skills while seeing your ideas take shape. How you can make an impact

  • Understand the real need: Speak with client teams, support workshops and document how their sales and service processes work today.
  • Turn insight into clear requirements: Help define user stories, use cases and process improvements that delivery teams can act on.
  • Support implementation: Work with colleagues to configure and deliver customer platforms, and help connect new solutions with existing ways of working.
  • Make sure it works for users: Assist with testing, training materials and feedback so solutions are useful in practice—not just on paper.
  • Improve the customer journey: Spot opportunities to make interactions smoother for customers and simpler for the teams serving them. What you bring to succeed
  • A degree in Business, Engineering, Computer Science, IT or a related field, or equivalent relevant experience.
  • A sound understanding of business processes and a genuine interest in how technology supports sales and service teams.
  • Strong analytical skills: you can ask good questions, organise what you learn and explain it clearly.
  • Confidence working with different people, listening to their perspectives and following through on commitments.
  • Curiosity, attention to detail and a willingness to learn in a hands-on project environment. What sets you apart Experience with a CRM platform such as Salesforce, process mapping, writing user stories or supporting software testing would be valuable, but none is essential. Familiarity with data tools, systems integration or the use of generative AI is also welcome—we’re more interested in your ability to learn and apply new ideas thoughtfully. Your team Our Sales & Service team helps clients make every customer interaction count. We bring together human insight and technology to build experiences that feel personal, useful and easy to navigate, from the first marketing touchpoint through sales and ongoing service. It’s work that connects creative thinking with practical delivery, helping clients strengthen customer relationships and find new opportunities for growth. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
