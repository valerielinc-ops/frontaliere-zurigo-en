Full description

At STRABAG, around 89,000 people are making progress at more than 2,400 locations worldwide. Uniqueness and individual strengths characterize not only our projects, but also each and every one of us. Whether in construction and engineering, road and civil engineering, bridge and tunnel construction, in building materials production, project development or building management - we continue to think about construction: innovation, digitalization and sustainability are central points on our way to building tomorrow. STRABAG is pursuing a clear sustainability strategy. Equal opportunities, diversity and inclusion are just as integral parts of what makes us a company and how we work. Together we implement projects successfully and in partnership and grow in new tasks. Together we create great things. Let's make progress!

Technical:r Controller:in Your contribution with us

  • You take over the technical controlling and project controlling of our construction projects
  • You create and maintain work calculations and support the project teams in economic issues
  • You carry out target-actual comparisons and analyze cost developments and project key figures
  • You create monthly reports, evaluations and decision bases for project management
  • You develop deviation analyses and recalculations and derive optimization potentials
  • You support in subcontracting and supplier procurement as well as in budget planning
  • You will analyze and verify calculation indicators and contribute to the economic control of the projects
  • You contribute to the further development and optimization of existing processes

What matters to us

  • You have completed a degree in civil engineering, industrial engineering or a comparable technical education
  • You have several years of professional experience in technical controlling, project controlling or in a comparable function in the construction industry
  • You have very good user knowledge in RIB iTWO as well as practical experience in working calculation and project management (mandatory)
  • You are safe with calculation and accounting programs and bring a strong commercial understanding
  • You think analytically and work in a structured, independent and responsible way
  • You are characterized by high commitment, teamwork and reliability
  • You convince with very good communication skills and a professional appearance
  • You are ready to travel within Switzerland

Our added value for you You can expect a wide range of construction projects, exciting challenges and a working environment in which you can take on responsibility and contribute your expertise. We offer you attractive employment conditions, modern work equipment and a wide range of development and further education opportunities. Together we create the basis for the sustainable success of our projects and shape the future of construction. :::

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
