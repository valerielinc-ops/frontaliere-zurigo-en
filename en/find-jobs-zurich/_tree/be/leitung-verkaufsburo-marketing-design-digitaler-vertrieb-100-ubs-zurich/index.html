Full description

Key responsibilities The Sales Office within Marketing, Design & Digital Sales at UBS Switzerland plays a central role in translating the bank’s growth ambitions into coordinated commercial action. It strengthens front-to-back sales steering and advances critical sales capabilities across channels in an environment with multiple stakeholders, competing priorities and organizational complexity.

We’re looking for a Head Sales Office to

  • lead the Sales Office, shaping its mandate, operating model, strategic priorities and driving them through execution
  • orchestrate the sales portfolio across Sales Rooms, creating transparency on initiatives, dependencies, capacity and commercial outcomes
  • align marketing, digital sales and capability roadmaps with business-unit sales strategies and growth priorities
  • drive the development and delivery of critical sales capabilities across lead generation, client-advisor tooling, and digital banking
  • lead senior governance forums, navigate differing perspectives, bring stakeholders to decisions and ensure disciplined follow-through
  • influence across organizational boundaries, challenge priorities and investment choices constructively, and secure alignment on roadmaps and funding
  • establish consistent standards, terminology, data logic and ways of working across the front-to-back sales organization

Your Career Comeback We are open to applications from career returners. Find out more about our program on ubs.com/careercomeback .

The team You’ll lead the Sales Office within Marketing, Design & Digital Sales in Zurich, reporting directly to the Head Marketing, Design & Digital Sales. The team works across business units, sales, marketing, product and technology to focus resources where they create the greatest commercial value. Its mandate combines commercial steering with the development of critical sales capabilities across physical, remote and digital channels.

Your skills and experience

  • proven experience leading complex, cross-functional sales, commercial, or transformation teams
  • strong understanding of sales steering, client journeys and the capabilities required to generate and convert sales opportunities across channels
  • a track record of translating strategic priorities into executable roadmaps, and delivering on them in time and in quality
  • sound judgment, personal drive and the confidence to move important topics forward amid ambiguity
  • outstanding stakeholder management skills, with the ability to build pragmatic alignment, influence senior decision-makers and challenge constructively without relying on formal authority
  • a structured and analytical approach, combined with the ambition, resilience and persistence to turn complex agendas into tangible outcomes
  • strong communication and leadership skills, with the ability to simplify complexity and establish clear accountability
  • experience in financial services, digital sales, marketing technology, data or agile product organizations is an advantage
  • you’re curious to explore how AI can improve how we build, deliver and optimize workflows. You do this with sound judgment – validating outputs and aligning with policies, risk standard and ethical use
