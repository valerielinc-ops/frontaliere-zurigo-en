80-100%, Winterthur / Smart Working (60% of working time is expected office presence /40% are flexible)

Shape your future – together with us!

Good software is created where technology meets real customer benefits. In our Claims product teams, you develop solutions that help people in challenging moments. As a senior software engineer, you work in a team that focuses on collaboration, mutual learning and real empowerment.

In the field of data, technology and innovation, around 600 software and system developers work in Switzerland: in-house and develop the technology of tomorrow. We also collaborate in global teams and networks. We shape and integrate the latest tools, organize hackathons and our agile product teams run self-organized and are self-responsible from A to Z.

This is what awaits you

  • Design and implementation of end-to-end solutions in the claims environment – from requirements to productive release
  • Working in a self-organized, agile product team with real personal responsibility over the entire development process
  • Active participation in architectural decisions, code reviews and technical standards
  • Bringing our own ideas and constructively building on the solutions of others – in the sense of our cooperation principles
  • Maintain an open, honest feedback culture and continuously grow together with the team
  • Co-shaping the AI transformation and active use of modern technologies in everyday development
  • Networking in internal communities as well as participation in hackathons and innovation projects

You bring that with you

  • Collaborative, team-oriented working method with pleasure in solving complex challenges together
  • Clear, understandable communication of technical issues to different stakeholders
  • Negotiable knowledge of German and English is required
  • Regular collaboration with stakeholders in English
  • Strong willingness to learn and curiosity – new technologies and changes are seen as opportunities
  • In-depth knowledge of Java as well as common frameworks such as Spring Boot
  • Secure design and implementation of REST APIs and Kafka integrations
  • Know-how in cloud environments (OpenShift, Helm and Argo CD) as well as in CI/CD pipelines, Docker and Kubernetes
  • Secure handling of databases and their connection

This is what we offer you We are convinced: Satisfied employees provide the best services. That’s why we invest in benefits that offer noticeable benefits – beyond the usual.

Flexibility and freedom: Smart Working (flexible working model) Annual working hours, 5 weeks holidays (from 50 years 6 weeks), holiday gift 2 weeks every 5 years, holiday purchase up to 10 additional days/per year

Job and family: family-friendly meeting hours, 22 weeks of maternity leave, 8 weeks of leave for the other parent, crèche subsidies, company family allowances, days off for entering relatives and in challenging, personal situations

Precautions and insurance: Attractive pension fund with far-reaching non-compulsory benefits including choice savings options, up to 30% discount on insurance products

Flexible Benefits: AXA Shareplan (employee participation), CHF 500 annually on the advantage platform Swibeco, discounted lunch or lunch card, Smart Working flat rate CHF 200 per year

Mobility: refund for half-tax, Reka-Pay up to CHF 1’000 per year (20% discount), fleet discount, free e-charging station, AXA rental bicycles

Healthy balance in everyday work: Free check-ups, annual health weeks, a wide range of exercise offers (online/offline), massage offers, rest rooms, employee care, online health portal, various seminars/events around health prevention & mental health

Development: 3 days of individual learning time, free coaching, wide range of learning & development opportunities, financial participation in further education, promotion of internal mobility, talent development offers, seminars for employees 55+ (AXA Horizon), engagement in communities on various topics, participation in innovation projects (Innoday, AXA Hackathon, UnConference), 2 days of volunteering (environment & social affairs)
