Hirslanden stands for first-class, responsible and individual health care that puts people first. From birth to old age as well as from prevention to healing – at any time, physically and digitally – the Hirslanden Group is concentrating on the further development of the “Continuum of Care” together with private and public partners.

As SAP Basis Technical Application Manager, you play a central role in the stable and secure operation as well as in the further development of our SAP system landscape. You combine in-depth technical know-how with operational responsibility and the management of external partners and actively contribute to the optimization of our SAP systems.

YOUR TASKS

  • Together with external service providers, you ensure the stable, secure and highly available operation of our SAP system landscape and actively support operational SAP base operations.
  • You will analyze and repair complex technical failures, monitor system performance and drive continuous optimization of the SAP system landscape.
  • You coordinate external SAP basic and infrastructure providers and ensure compliance with agreed service levels as well as architecture, security and operational standards.
  • You plan and accompany SAP upgrades, releases, support packages, security notes and technical migrations and support technological developments.
  • You will be responsible for incident, change and problem management, develop monitoring and automation concepts and support technical integration with internal and external systems.
  • Together with the responsible partners, you ensure high availability as well as business continuity and disaster recovery and support the SAP Basis Manager in the operational organization and his deputy.

Your profile

  • Completed training or studies in computer science, business informatics or a comparable qualification through professional experience.
  • Several years of profound experience in the technical SAP basic environment and in the operational operation of complex SAP system landscapes.
  • Very good knowledge of SAP base administration, system monitoring, performance analysis and technical troubleshooting as well as experience with upgrades, support packages and migrations.
  • Good knowledge of modern SAP technologies, infrastructure and operational models, ideally also SAP S/4HANA, as well as in the areas of SAP security, high availability and disaster recovery.
  • Experience in the management of external IT service providers as well as in SLA and service management
  • Knowledge of ITIL-based operating processes is an advantage.
  • Analytical, structured and solution-oriented personality with a strong sense of responsibility, strong communication and enjoyment of working with internal and external specialists.
