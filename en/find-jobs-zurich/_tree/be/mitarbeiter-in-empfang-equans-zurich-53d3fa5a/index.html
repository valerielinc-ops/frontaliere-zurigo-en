Full description

We are looking for a service-oriented and motivated personality to strengthen our team in Zurich.

You can move that with us.

As the first point of contact for customers, visitors and employees, you ensure a professional and positive first impression. Your main tasks include:

  • Friendly and competent reception of visitors, customers and suppliers as well as notification of internal contacts
  • Management and distribution of access and visitor media
  • Ensuring a well-maintained and representative reception area
  • Conduct of control and closing circuits
  • Operation and monitoring of the fire alarm system and initiation of necessary measures in the event of occurrences

That's what makes you.

You describe yourself as a service provider: from the innermost conviction with an open and cordial manner. Flexibility and reliability, independence and a quick comprehension are also among your character traits. You also bring the following:

  • Completed basic training, pronounced guest relations and service orientation
  • Several years of professional experience at reception or in a comparable service-oriented function
  • Very good German and fluent English skills in Word
  • Strong communication skills and pleasure in dealing with different stakeholders
  • High customer orientation, reliability and discretion
  • Independent, structured and responsible working methods
  • Good user knowledge of MS-Office products and pleasure in dealing with digital systems

That's what we offer you.

We offer you a responsible and interesting task with plenty of space for initiative in an exciting professional environment. We look forward to your complete application with photo.
