Full description

We are looking for a committed personality as FACHWAU / FACHMANN VEHICLE PURCHASE 100% as of December 2026 or by agreement

  • Independent planning, coordination and on-time handling of vehicle procurement for firefighting organisations in the Canton of Zurich
  • Technical advice and support for fire departments in the procurement of new emergency vehicles
  • Preparation of subsidy undertakings and preparation and coordination of submission-compliant tender documents
  • Preparation and examination of vehicle specifications and loading lists in cooperation with the specialist groups involved
  • Ensuring a complete, comprehensible and up-to-date documentation of ongoing procurement operations
  • Preparation and evaluation of key figures, statistics and decision-making basis
  • Preparation and monitoring of the vehicle procurement budget
  • Representation of the Fire Department in working groups, at trade fairs and at vehicle acceptances and transfers
  • Collaboration in projects as well as active participation in the further development and optimization of firefighting vehicles
  • Completed technical vocational training, preferably in the field of vehicle or commercial vehicle technology, supplemented by commercial or business training
  • Strong technical understanding, especially in the field of vehicle and commercial vehicle technology
  • Experience in project management as well as in the independent coordination and monitoring of complex procurement processes
  • Good knowledge of public procurement law
  • Independent, structured, forward-looking and reliable working method
  • High level of commitment in respect of deadlines, deadlines and agreements
  • Strong communication personality with a proactive, transparent and addressable language
  • Innovative thinking and high cost awareness
  • Good knowledge of fire department, ideally with experience in the higher cadre of a fire department organization
