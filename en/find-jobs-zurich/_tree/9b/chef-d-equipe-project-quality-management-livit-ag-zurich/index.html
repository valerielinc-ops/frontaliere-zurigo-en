Digital Business Zurich | Pensum: 80-100 %

Working on the pulse of digital transformation. Our Digital Business department makes it possible. Together with us, shape the future of real estate management, develop innovative products and processes and move Livit forward together with us. In digital business, you can expect a great, interdisciplinary team, an inspiring working atmosphere and exciting projects in which you can actively participate. Are you excited about innovations, the latest systems, an agile setting and new challenges? Then you are exactly right with Livit. We look forward to your personal contribution.

Your tasks

  • You lead your team in a goal-oriented manner, further develop the employees and ensure a high quality of services.
  • You are responsible for establishing and operating a lean Project & Quality Management with portfolio overview, methods, templates and reporting.
  • You take on independent project management and work operationally in strategic and cross-divisional projects.
  • You control project planning, progress, dependencies, risks and resources, and provide transparency at all levels.
  • You establish pragmatic delivery and collaboration models and promote a balanced combination of agile and classical methods.
  • You are responsible for quality management and develop standards, reviews, test coordination and continuous improvement measures.
  • You work closely with the CIO Office in demand and portfolio management and ensure clear responsibilities between management and implementation.
  • You will empower project leaders and teams in methodology, governance and tooling, and ensure transparent status and risk reporting.

Your profile

  • You have a university degree or a comparable education and training.
  • You bring several years of experience in project or program management as well as in the establishment and operation of a project & quality management.
  • You have successfully managed and implemented complex projects and cross-sectoral projects.
  • You have in-depth knowledge of quality and test management and move confidently in agile, classical and hybrid project environments.
  • You bring leadership experience and understand how to empower and develop employees and teams.
  • You work structured, solution-oriented and with a high quality awareness.
  • You convince through strong communication and moderation skills as well as a professional and partnership-like appearance.
  • You communicate stylishly in German and English and are enthusiastic about new technologies and modern working methods.

For this vacancy, only direct applications are considered and we do not accept dossiers from recruitment agencies.
