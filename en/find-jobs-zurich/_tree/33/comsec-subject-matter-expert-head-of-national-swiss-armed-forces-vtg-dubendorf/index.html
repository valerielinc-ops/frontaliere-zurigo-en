Put to the Point

Are you interested in legal issues and supervisory tasks in a technical area and would like to actively shape requirements? In this role, you assume responsibility in a unique team of specialists and make an important contribution to the security and compliance of the Swiss Army.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-21024
  • Place of work: Überlandstrasse 255, 8600 Dübendorf

You can make this contribution

  • Leading the Swiss COMSEC legislative process and taking responsibility for national legal decrees in the COMSEC area
  • Ensure coherence with national and foreign requirements and assess and document questions of legal application
  • Monitor the COMSEC work processes of the administrative units and industry and advise on the implementation of the specifications
  • Develop and control the certification process of real estate and ICT systems and control the implementation of the requirements
  • Lead the directly subordinated specialist staff, lead working groups and enactment projects as well as define national COMSEC emergency measures and monitor their implementation
  • It is possible to provide piquetting services at regular intervals as well as occasional business trips, also abroad.

This makes them unique.

  • Law degree and at least five years of professional experience, ideally with knowledge of information or cybersecurity and experience in project management
  • Sound skills in the argumentation of complex legal issues as well as negotiating skills and assertiveness
  • Strong strategic, analytical, conceptual and networked thinking, high goal and result orientation and excellent editorial skills
  • Ability to classify different specifications and situations in a larger context and to represent one's own point of view scientifically correctly
  • good, active knowledge of a second official language as well as a reliable knowledge of English
  • Officer training of advantage or readiness to acquire the tools of military staff work
  • Swiss citizen with impeccable reputation

Your commitment to security and freedom The Command Operations (Kdo Op) is responsible for all military operations and operations of the Swiss Army. The Kdo Op includes the Luftwaffe, the Army, the four territorial divisions, the SWISSINT Competence Center, the Special Forces Command and the Military Police. Around 40% of the approximately 2,500 employees are civil employees – from engineers to aircraft mechanics to analysts.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
