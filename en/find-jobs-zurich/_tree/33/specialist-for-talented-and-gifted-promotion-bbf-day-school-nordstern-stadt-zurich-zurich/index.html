Full description

Due to long-term absence from illness, the school Nordstern is looking for a specialist for talented and gifted development (BBF).

From 4 January to 16 January July 2027, the school Nordstern is looking for a deputy.

  • Teaching hours Pullout courses: Tuesday 10 - 3 pm, Thursday 8-12 pm, 1x lunch attendance 45 min.
  • Participation in various school and teaching projects in cooperation with class and support teachers
  • Advice and provision of possible support materials for the school team for use in standard teaching
  • Teaching diploma for the primary level, recognized in Switzerland
  • CAS IBBF is beneficial
  • Existing BBF experience or the willingness to deal intensively with BBF school development
  • Joy, motivation and the ability to promote students (age-mixed) with different interests and strengths
  • Creativity and the initiative to bring new ideas into school life and to give new things form and structure in the sense of the school BBF overall concept
  • a temporary post
  • Already some established structures in the field of self-organized learning (SOL) or in the promotion of gifts and talents (BBF)
  • The support of the school management and the specialist BBF
  • A dedicated, humorous and collegial team
  • A benevolent school culture

The Nordstern Day School at Thurgauerstrasse 55 has established itself as a modern learning and living space since its opening in August 2024. It has a wide range of usable, bright and open spaces, in which the multi-professional team with the children shapes the day school everyday life. Teachers participate 45 minutes at least once a week in the lunchtime and the caregivers support the lessons.

The school places an emphasis on Education for Sustainable Development (ESD): As a FreiDay school, the children are involved in their own projects with the 17 sustainability goals of the UN. Mixed-age classes and a strong participation of students promote self-responsibility and self-efficacy. Portfolio and coaching talks instead of grades, 90-minute teaching blocks, instead of 45-minute subject lessons: We try to do things differently to accommodate the learning of all children.
