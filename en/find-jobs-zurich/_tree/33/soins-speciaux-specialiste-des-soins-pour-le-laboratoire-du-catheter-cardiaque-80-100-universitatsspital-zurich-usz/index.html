By agreement

As a specialist care provider at the University Hospital in Zurich, we are committed to showing commitment with our professional diversity in the top medical environment and always keeping up with the times. We do not achieve success alone, but together! Each one counts and together we bring ourselves to the highest level of care.

Want to be part of our team? With us you can make a difference!

  • From CTO including all intravascular imaging to Impella inserts and all kinds of right heart catheter, also with load and shunt regulations: With us you experience as a nurse / nurse specialist HF / FH the entire range of possible interventions in a cardiac catheter laboratory
  • Patient-oriented accompaniment of cardiological and emergency patients: insides with acute coronary syndrome as well as independent work on hemodynamics (Philips) and sterile assistance in the cardiac catheter laboratory are part of your everyday life
  • We support you with the latest high-tech medicine, which we introduce you to in specific cardiological training courses – we also participate in international workshops and congresses.
  • Your managers meet you with appreciation, cooperation, empathy and optimism and your highly motivated, solution-oriented colleagues also live a positive team spirit.
  • Does everything sound exciting, but you are not yet sure if highly acute emergency situations with pericardium punctures are yours? We will show you how we work on a trial day and introduce you carefully in the first months. We are open to learning from you too!
