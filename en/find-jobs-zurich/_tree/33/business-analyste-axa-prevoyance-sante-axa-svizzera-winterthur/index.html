80-100%, Winterthur / Smart Working

Shape your future – together with us!

As a business analyst in the field of health, you are responsible for the successful implementation of features – from the conception and requirements assessment to the implementation and quality assurance up to the introduction into the company.

We work agile in several product teams and are responsible for digital solutions across all areas of Health – from consulting and conclusion to contract management and services to our digital customer channels and services. Depending on your experience, strengths and needs, you actively shape their further development and operation in these subject areas.

As AXA, we see ourselves as a partner who stands by your side and believes in you. That is why we challenge and promote you and proactively show you possibilities for your future. We encourage you and give you freedom so that you can connect the things that are important to you. Together with your commitment and your individual contribution, this will become a forward-looking partnership.

Are you ready for the multifaceted challenge? Are you committed, courageous and open to new things? We look forward to you and your participation.

AXA is the No. 1 pension partner in Switzerland. With our diverse and advanced products in the areas of health as well as private and occupational care, we create freedom and security for our customers so that they can shape their lives carefree.

This is what awaits you

  • As a member of a product team, you are responsible for the end-to-end implementation of new features. You analyze requirements, develop solution variants, formulate user stories, accompany the implementation and ensure that your features are successfully introduced
  • You bring together professional and technological perspectives and assess solution variants in terms of functionality, quality and cost-effectiveness. You work closely with experts, developers, UX and other stakeholders and carry out user tests if necessary.
  • Together with the product team, you ensure the quality of our solutions and take responsibility for the smooth introduction of new features and releases
  • Together with the Product Owner and the Product Team, you will design the planning and prioritization of ongoing activities, drive your topics independently and ensure close cooperation between all parties involved.
  • You are interested in new technologies and actively deal with how we can further develop our products, processes and working methods

You bring that with you

  • You have a quick apprehension and can quickly find your way around even in a complex environment. You can deal with several topics at the same time, switch between different contexts and keep track of the essentials
  • With your communication-strong, committed and independent personality, you take responsibility for your topics, make well-founded decisions in solution design and organize yourself and your cooperation with the product team in a structured and reliable manner.
  • Ideally, you will bring experience or professional understanding in the health insurance environment and enjoy exploring professional contexts in greater depth.
  • You are curious about technological developments and enjoy getting to know new possibilities – especially around AI and automation – and translating them into concrete solutions
  • An education or training in business informatics or business administration rounds off your profile
  • You have already worked with agile project methods, ideally in a Scrum Team. Experience in dealing with Jira and Confluence is an advantage

This is what we offer you We are convinced: Satisfied employees provide the best services. That’s why we invest in benefits that offer noticeable benefits – beyond the usual.

Flexibility and freedom: Smart Working (flexible working model) Annual working hours, 5 weeks holidays (from 50 years 6 weeks), holiday gift 2 weeks every 5 years, holiday purchase up to 10 additional days/per year

Precautions and insurance: Attractive pension fund with far-reaching non-compulsory benefits including choice savings options, up to 30% discount on insurance products

Flexible Benefits: AXA Shareplan (employee participation), CHF 500 annually on the advantage platform Swibeco, discounted lunch or lunch card, Smart Working flat rate CHF 200 per year

Mobility: refund for half-tax, Reka-Pay up to CHF 1’000 per year (20% discount), fleet discount, free e-charging station, AXA rental bicycles

Healthy balance in everyday work: Free check-ups, annual health weeks, a wide range of exercise offers (online/offline), massage offers, rest rooms, employee care, online health portal, various seminars/events around health prevention & mental health

Development: 3 days of individual learning time, free coaching, wide range of learning & development opportunities, financial participation in further education, promotion of internal mobility, talent development offers, seminars for employees 55+ (AXA Horizon), engagement in communities on various topics, participation in innovation projects (Innoday, AXA Hackathon, UnConference), 2 days of volunteering (environment & social affairs)
