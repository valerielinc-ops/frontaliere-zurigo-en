Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We advise individuals and companies about money, manage assets and offer attractive financial services. As we are growing strongly, we are looking for reinforcements at our headquarters at Paradeplatz in Zurich.

  • Together with the accounting team, you are responsible for complex financial topics (insurance and banking, acquisitions abroad, valuations, etc.) in the VZ Group.
  • You support management in evaluation topics, analyses and evaluations.
  • You are the deputy for international accounting topics according to IFRS.
  • You coordinate deadlines in the closing process, actively participate and take responsibility for closing areas.
  • You process and coordinate requests from the external audit.
  • You develop the basics of company acquisitions and prepare initial accounting in accordance with IFRS.
  • You take on responsibility in various accounting projects of the VZ Group.
  • You are involved in various corporate law topics.
  • An education in finance and accounting or a business administration degree
  • Several years of experience in accounting in an international environment, ideally in the banking or insurance environment
  • Strong communication and presentation skills, including at management level
  • Very good knowledge of IFRS accounting
  • Good English skills for contact with your colleagues in London
  • Affinity for Company Law
  • thirst for knowledge and desire for a dynamic environment by being able to contribute your ideas
  • Reliability and accuracy
  • We offer an exciting area of responsibility. You can deepen your expertise in various business fields: The VZ Group includes financial consultancy, banks, insurance asset managers and brokers.
  • We work closely with our colleagues in Switzerland, Germany and the UK.
  • We are a motivated team that supports each other and implements new projects with enthusiasm and success.
  • The VZ is growing strongly and is proud of its uncomplicated, open culture. For you, this means the opportunity to contribute your own ideas and help develop your specialist area and take on leadership.
  • Also for your further education you are at the right address with us. We support you individually and support you as part of your job profile also in external further education.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
