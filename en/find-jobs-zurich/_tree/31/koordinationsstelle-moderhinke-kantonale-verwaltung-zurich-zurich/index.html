Full description

Canton of Zurich Health Directorate Veterinary Office Coordination Office Moderhinke 100% The Veterinary Office Canton of Zurich is committed to animal welfare and animal health. It helps to ensure animal welfare and the safety of animal food. The Moderhinke is a widespread, painful claw disease that mainly affects sheep. In October 2023, the five-year national programme to combat the Modern Hindge began. Due to an internal change of job, we are looking for reinforcement in our animal disease control team from 1 February 2027 to 31 January 2029 by a project coordinator who will continue the well-running control programme. Are you interested in animal health, epidemiological links and the control of animal diseases? Do you want to get to know the work in a team with official veterinarians? Do you like to work in a structured way, keep track of complex processes and enjoy managing and developing processes independently? You formulate safely and stylishly, can think into questions of the public veterinary service and know how to bring together different stakeholders? Then you are the right person for us! Veterinarians at the beginning of their careers are also welcome. It is possible to familiarize oneself with the official veterinary profession and to complete a corresponding further training. Tasks

  • Planning, organisation and coordination of sampling and rehabilitation of Moderhinke in the Canton of Zurich
  • Other topics in food safety and disease surveillance
  • Independent processing and documentation of cases as well as writing of correspondence, orders and other written documents within the framework of the control programme
  • Monitoring of ordered control measures and ensuring administrative procedures
  • Regular exchange and coordination with animal owners, veterinarians, laboratories and other stakeholders
  • Triage and handling of difficult cases and participation in the clarification of technical and administrative issues
  • Participation in the development, implementation and further development of internal requirements and process flows
  • Performing own sampling and providing technical support and advice to external sampling profile
  • Completed study of veterinary medicine
  • Structured and independent working methods as well as pleasure in handling complex cases
  • Good written expression and security in writing correspondence
  • Interest and understanding of public veterinary procedures and administrative enforcement tasks
  • Affinity to the digital world of work and enjoyment of working with various specialist applications
  • Driving licence Cat. B
  • Good knowledge of German in word and writing Offer
  • Interesting, meaningful and versatile work content
  • Responsible mission profile in the field of animal health with interfaces to animal welfare and primary production
  • Possibility of training as an official veterinarian
  • Motivated young team with helpful colleagues
  • Social benefits and employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines Application Do you feel addressed? Then we look forward to your online application until the 15. December 2026. Information on the task and the challenges can be obtained from Andrea Gál-Stoller, Team Leader Animal Diseases & Veterinary Professions, at 043 259 41 41.Information about our office can be found at www.zh.ch/veta
