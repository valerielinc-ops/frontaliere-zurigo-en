Working at Sprüngli

In our factory in Dietikon, we are looking for a motivated and committed company for the Dietikon Washing Production department. *n

What you do with us

You work at our production site in Dietikon at the dishwashing station and ensure that everything runs smoothly. Your mission will take place from Monday to Friday in two shifts between 06:30 and 18:00. Occasional weekend bets are also possible.

  • Machine cleaning of transport containers, baking plates, moulds and baking and transport rakes
  • Care and maintenance of cleaning machines
  • Ensure that clean containers are correctly delivered to the respective departments

What we want

  • A motivated personality with pleasure in practical work
  • Independent, structured and responsible working methods
  • Experience in the food industry is a plus, but not a must
  • Flexibility, mobility and good physical strength
  • Basic knowledge in German, communication possible in everyday work

You can look forward to that.

  • Varied tasks in a modern working environment with motivated team and culture
  • Attractive employment conditions with 5 weeks holidays and extra day off in the birthday month

  • Free parking and generous shopping discounts
