USZ Campus

by 01.05.2027 or by agreement

People develop. Strengthening skills. Shaping the future. As a Department of Education Care, we play a decisive role in shaping education and training at the USZ. From innovative educational concepts to the support of learners and students to the promotion of the professional development of our employees, we create the basis for excellence in care and therapy.

Their main tasks

  • They are responsible for the 17 initial and further education courses as well as the continuing education program for nursing and MTTB.
  • They align education strategically and further develop topics (quality of training, contacts with all partners: inside, satisfaction of trainees and employees: inside).
  • In addition to the direct pedagogical-didactic dimension of education, you are a very central component with educational responsibility in order to meet the needs of young and skilled workers at the USZ.
  • They actively provide impulses within the framework of HR strategies and processes and locate the education, training and further education accordingly.
  • You will be supported by an experienced education team, which will lead you partly disciplinary and partly professionally.

Your profile

  • They have a Master in Vocational Education and Training (or comparable training) as well as a specialisation or Experience in the field of personnel development.
  • You have leadership experience in complex organizational structures as well as experience in change projects.
  • You are familiar with the Swiss education system in the healthcare sector with the requirements and developments of education and training.
  • Concept development, the ability to communicate them convincingly and to work together with different stakeholders are a matter of course for you.
  • You are a team player and you work closely with the leaders from research, specialist development and the individual areas, with whom you also form the management team of the Management Board of Care and MTTB.
  • If you now also have a professional background in a health profession, ideally in Switzerland, you are our dream cast.

Our offer

The USZ is changing! Be in the change process. We support you with our expertise and offer a unique experience. You will learn a lot, we will learn from you and together we will create something new! They have the opportunity to initiate and implement new developments in education, as well as to contribute and try out ideas. We have numerous lighthouse projects in education, training and further education, which have become the benchmark for other hospitals! For this post, dossiers from recruiters cannot be considered.
