Full description

A career at Lombard Odier means working for a renowned global wealth and asset manager, with a strong focus on sustainable investing. An innovative bank of choice for private and institutional clients, our independently owned Firm is one of the best-capitalised banking groups in the world, managing close to CHF 300 billion and operating from over 25 offices across 4 continents.

With a history spanning over 230 years, Lombard Odier is an investment house providing a comprehensive offering of discretionary and advisory portfolio management, wealth services and custody. We also offer asset management services and investment strategies through Lombard Odier Investment Managers and provide advanced banking technology to other financial institutions.

“Rethink Everything” is our philosophy – it is at the heart of everything we do. We have grown stronger through more than 40 financial crises by rethinking the world around us to provide a fresh investment perspective for our clients.

The External Asset Management (“EAM”) team of Lombard Odier (Europe) SA, based in Luxembourg, is seeking an experienced Relationship Manager to join a dynamic and ambitious team.

Mission

  • Manage, develop, and grow a portfolio of EAM partners.
  • Act as an ambassador of Lombard Odier and single-entry point for sophisticated professional clients, to position the bank as their preferred strategic partner.
  • Ensure the efficient running and servicing of clients' accounts from a day-to-day business perspective, including commercial and operational responsibility.
  • Entirely master and leverage on the Bank's platform, expertise, products and specialists, to promote value added services such as trading, credit, global assets+/global reporting and pro-actively communicate on product and services.
  • Actively participate to marketing and commercial events to promote Lombard Odier’s brand within the EU Wealth Management community.
  • Entirely master and animate commercial meetings and presentations of Lombard Odier in-house IT solutions and platforms (G2/MyLO).
  • Ensure smooth and proper clients’ on-boarding through trainings, documents and support to EAM partners.
  • Ensure the highest standards of risk management, compliance, and regulatory adherence in all activities.

Profile

  • Minimum 5 years of proven experience in managing and developing EAM relationships, within the Luxembourg wealth management industry.
  • Proactive and positive mindset, business development and networking capabilities, very good communication and presentation skills, self-motivation and sense of service, combined with strong execution discipline.
  • Genuine interest in understanding clients' and prospects' business models, challenges, and needs in order to identify growth opportunities.
  • Experience of working in an international and multicultural environment.
  • Strong team player with excellent collaboration skills.
  • Good knowledge of Corporate, Trusts, Insurance, Individual and Joint Personal accounts.
  • Knowledge and understanding of the main asset classes as well as alternative assets.
  • Excellent French and English levels. Other languages would be a strong advantage.
