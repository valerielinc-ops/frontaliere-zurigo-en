Full description

Planzer Paket stands for reliable and fast parcel deliveries in Switzerland. As part of our team, you actively contribute to further developing our high quality standards and service. We offer you a varied job in a future-oriented company that focuses on sustainable solutions. Become part of our team and shape the future of the package world!

What you move

  • With your organizational talent, you keep the agendas of our division managers under control, coordinate appointments in advance and ensure a smooth planning of business trips and branch visits.
  • Whether management meetings, workshops or internal events – you take care of the organization, prepare the necessary documents and make sure that resolutions and penalties are not forgotten.
  • As a communicative hub, you are an important point of contact for internal and external contacts, write stylish correspondence and ensure that information reaches the right recipients.
  • You can skilfully bring figures, facts and information to the point by creating meaningful presentations, reports and decision bases as well as preparing key figures together with controlling.
  • Even in cross-divisional projects, you keep the threads together, coordinate open tasks and support administrative HR topics such as the organization of interviews or training courses.
  • From invoice and expense control to document management to short-term special assignments, you take on a wide range of administrative tasks and ensure a well-organized day-to-day work.

Why do you succeed?

  • Your basic commercial education (EFZ) forms the foundation that you have ideally supplemented with further training as a directorial assistant or a comparable qualification.
  • Thanks to your at least two years of experience in an assistant role, preferably at management level, you know what is important in the professional support of managers.
  • With your stylish German you convince both verbally and in writing, while knowledge in French, Italian or English enrich your profile.
  • In the Microsoft 365 world, you’ll move confidently and be open and curious about new digital tools and AI applications.
  • Self-employment, self-initiative and a strong organizational talent set you apart, so that you keep an overview and set priorities correctly even in hectic situations.
  • Your discreet and loyal nature, your safe presence and your hands-on mentality make you a valued contact at all levels of the hierarchy, with an environment in the logistics or transport industry ideally already familiar.

Your benefits

  • Further education and development opportunities: With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • Family culture: You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Opportunity for home office: We go with the times - enjoy the advantage of home office

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
