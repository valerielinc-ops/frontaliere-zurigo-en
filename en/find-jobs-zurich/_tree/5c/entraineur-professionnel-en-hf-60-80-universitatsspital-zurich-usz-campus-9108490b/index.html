Bring your expertise where it makes the biggest difference: In orthotics at the University Hospital Zurich, you diagnose and treat visual and eye movement disorders interdisciplinary and at the highest medical level – with a focus on early detection, individual therapy and sustainable quality of life. >The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland.
