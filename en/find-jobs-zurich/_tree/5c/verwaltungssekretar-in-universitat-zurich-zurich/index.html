Institute of Legal Medicine

Job start 1 March 2027 or by agreement

On behalf of the criminal investigation authorities, the Institute of Forensic Medicine examines living and deceased people from a forensic point of view. In any case, to provide clarity – that is what we have set ourselves the goal. In order to achieve this goal for the benefit of society, we take up the latest findings in our field and implement them. The IRM-UZH stands for an innovative institute for legal medicine. Become part of it! For the Center for Forensic Hair Analysis we are looking for 1. March 2027 or by arrangement one:n The position is limited to twelve months with permanent employment option What we do The Center for Forensic Hair Analysis is a routine and research laboratory of the Institute of Legal Medicine of the University of Zurich. Our specialty is forensic hair analysis. Our main task is to carry out toxicological analyses of hair samples for potable alcohol, drugs and drugs. to carry out metabolic products as a service in particular for the benefit of the judiciary (prosecutor’s offices as well as court, prison service BVD), police, authorities and (experts) doctors. Our laboratory and the services we offer are accredited according to ISO EN 17025.

Tasks

  • Acceptance, control and labelling of asservates
  • Recording of asservates in the database and allocation of orders by the investigating authorities
  • Preparation and control and cancellation of invoices
  • Independent creation (ready to sign) of reports and opinions for the division manager and the scientific staff: inside using case and laboratory files as well as text modules, templates or layouts
  • Provision of information and transmission of requests
  • Archiving and destruction of case files
  • General secretarial work: correspondence in writing and by telephone with customers and clients
  • Logging at meetings, mailing and ordering office supplies
  • Support for special projects/research
  • Care of the break room
  • With the help of digitalization of processes
  • Working days: Tuesday to Friday, from 7.30h to 16.30h on site, home office is not possible for this function

Profile

For this versatile position, we are looking for a committed, resilient, reliable and service-oriented personality with a commercial education or training as a medical assistant and corresponding professional experience. You like to work independently, carefully and efficiently and are characterized by your quick comprehension and your teamwork ability. In addition, you have excellent, stylish knowledge of German and are very familiar with Office application programs and SAP. Integrity and discretion are a matter of course for you. You also have experience in dealing with digital work processes.
