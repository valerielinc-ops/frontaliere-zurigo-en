Full description

As a business analyst, you form the bridge between specialist areas, digitalization and ICT. You will raise, question and structure professional requirements and develop viable solutions from them. You take on a newly created role and have the opportunity to actively shape the future cooperation between departments and ICT. Your tasks

  • Identify, question and analyze the needs of the departments
  • Design and harmonize business processes and organizational structures
  • Developing and documenting professional, organisational and functional requirements and specifications
  • Creation of technical concepts and technical basis for decision-making
  • Coordination of requirements with the participating specialist bodies in accordance with the applicable governance
  • Validate the developed requirements and solutions with the relevant stakeholders Your profile
  • Tertiary education (FH/uni) in business informatics, business administration or equivalent qualification
  • Experience as a business analyst or in a comparable function with a focus on requirements engineering or process management
  • In-depth knowledge in the analysis and design of business processes as well as in requirements analysis and documentation
  • Good understanding of digitalisation, organisational development and ICT governance
  • Strong analytical and conceptual skills An interesting and diverse task awaits you in an interdisciplinary environment in which you come into contact with a wide range of internal positions.
