Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Vocational Guidance Staff (for one year) Alimony Aid (for one year), 80

  • 100% Good for life – that is our motto. As part of the Education Directorate of the Canton of Zurich, the Office for Youth and Vocational Guidance is responsible for the services in the area of child and youth assistance as well as in career guidance. The alimony aid is part of the Office for Youth and Vocational Guidance and provides collection assistance and the advance payment of maintenance contributions according to the Children's and Youth Aid Act. We are looking for a limited period of one year per immediate or by appointment, with option for an indefinite employment, a committed and motivated person with experience in the alimony aid or in a similar field, who would like to use her broad expertise in the canton of Zurich. Tasks
  • Requirement of maintenance rights of children and spouses
  • Processing of requests for financial benefits
  • Creation of applications with municipalities
  • Implementation of decisions
  • Processing of payments
  • Creation of accounts and reports on the financing communities
  • Representing dependants in the relevant procedures
  • Your profile
  • Completed basic commercial training EFZ with several years of professional experience in the field of alimony or in similar complex material processing (e.g. Execution Office, Social Security, Trust, Accounting, Collection)
  • Completed training to become a specialist in alimony (or the willingness to complete it)
  • Good understanding of numbers and exact and independent working methods
  • Very good communication skills and assertive capacity
  • Interest in legal issues (execution law, family law)
