Your Impact

  • Deliver OECD Pillar Two compliance engagements for multinational clients and contribute to high-quality and consistent service delivery.
  • Coordinate cross-border compliance projects by collaborating with clients, PwC network firms and our Service Delivery Center to achieve successful outcomes.
  • Coordinate the collection, validation and review of financial and tax data required for Pillar Two calculations, tax reporting and regulatory filings.
  • Review Pillar Two calculations, compliance deliverables and supporting documentation to ensure accuracy, quality and adherence to evolving regulatory requirements.
  • Identify opportunities to enhance processes, improve efficiencies and support the implementation of scalable compliance solutions.
  • Build trusted relationships with stakeholders while continuously strengthening technical expertise in global minimum taxation and international tax compliance.

Your Skill Set

  • University degree in Accounting, Finance, Economics, Tax, Law or a related field, or an equivalent professional qualification (e.g. ACCA).
  • 3-6 years of experience in tax reporting, tax accounting, tax compliance, internal controls, international tax coordination or international tax advisory.
  • Foundational knowledge of OECD Pillar Two rules and a strong interest in global minimum tax compliance and reporting requirements.
  • Proven project coordination and stakeholder management skills, ideally gained in international and cross-functional environments.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving and communication skills combined with a high level of accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Fluency in English
  • German or French language skills are considered a strong advantage.
