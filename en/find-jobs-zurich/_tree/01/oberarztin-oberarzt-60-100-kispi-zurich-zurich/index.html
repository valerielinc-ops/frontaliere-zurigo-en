Pediatric nephrology

Your tasks

  • You care for children and adolescents with kidney diseases in the outpatient and inpatient area at Switzerland's largest children's hospital.
  • Together with the multiprofessional nephro team, you will perform all kidney replacement procedures (hemodialysis, peritoneal dialysis, continuous hemofiltration) and care for children and adolescents before/after a kidney transplant.
  • With other pediatric subspecialties, the intensive care unit, transplantation surgery and psychosocial services you maintain a close cooperation.
  • You participate in the paediatric nephrology training program.

Your profile

  • Medical diploma, specialist title of paediatric medicine and adolescent medicine as well as addition in paediatric nephrology
  • Joy of teaching young colleagues, interest in co-creating university teaching in paediatric nephrology
  • If you are interested in scientific activity, we are happy to support
