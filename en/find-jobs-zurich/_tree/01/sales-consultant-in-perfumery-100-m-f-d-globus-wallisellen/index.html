Full description

With great attention to detail, our employees make GLOBUS the leading premium and luxury address in Switzerland. It is people like you who make the difference and bring the “place we love to be” to life together with us.

Here you make the difference

  • You advise our customers personally, attentively and with a sense of beauty, charisma and individuality.
  • You are responsible for the Xerjoff Counter.
  • You are familiar with fragrances, care lines and international brands and will find the right product for every personality.
  • You are actively involved in visual merchandising and design the appearance of the counter in style.
  • You maintain customer relationships, accompany the customer card program and advise with sovereignty, tranquility and real joy of dialogue.

That draws you out

  • You have completed training in the retail trade or perfumery sector as well as several years of experience in consulting-intensive premium sales.
  • You move safely in the beauty world – with a sense of trends, quality and personality.
  • You have a keen eye for details and a real talent to advise people on a type-appropriate and stylish basis.
  • You work structured, responsible and contribute to success on the sales floor.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English.
  • You are flexible and Saturday is a fixed working day.

BENEFITS FOR YOU

Also, look forward to

  • an exceptional working environment in the highest-grossing and most popular shopping centre in Switzerland.
  • an excellent public transport connection – tram and bus stop directly in front of the Glattzentrum.
  • Direct motorway connection (A1) if you come to work by car.
  • Free parking for employees as well as petrol stations and e-gas stations directly in front of the building.
  • a roof terrace for employees to eat in the fresh air of Zmittag or simply to recharge with the sun.
  • Top food options thanks to smooth gastronomy: 14 restaurants and bars offer everything from delicacies, Mediterranean treats, Asian delicacies to takeaways.
  • 100 shops, including pharmacies, a post office and much more – the Glatt, the small shopping town! PS: Thanks to the smooth card you get various perks.
  • attractive social benefits.
  • at least 5 weeks of vacation.
  • the outfit pass with around 50% on 30 clothes per year for you.
  • up to 30% discount on products from the globe for you and your family.
  • a fairytale atmosphere during the extraordinary smooth Christmas season.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
