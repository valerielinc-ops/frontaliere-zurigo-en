Shape your future – together with us!

We are looking for an energetic, flexible personality to strengthen our internal sales team.

Start your career in an agency at AXA, the No. 1! We support our customers as a competent partner – in every situation. You too?

What to expect

  • Advice and support for customers, mainly on the telephone or at customer reception (private customers and SMEs)
  • Supporting insurance advisors in arranging customer appointments
  • Preparation and follow-up of customer appointments (sales support)
  • Administrative support for insurance advisors
  • Preparation of offers and applications
  • General correspondence

This is what you bring with you

  • Commercial training in the insurance industry or many years of experience in the insurance industry is mandatory
  • Passed broker exam is an advantage
  • Enjoy expanding existing customer relationships
  • Reliable, resilient personality combined with a quick way of working
  • High commitment as well as customer and service orientation
  • Convincing appearance with joy in dealing with people
  • Positive and winning manner, salesmanlike flair
  • Good knowledge of spoken and written German, other languages ​​are an advantage

This is what we offer you

We create framework conditions that give you space to develop and fully exploit your potential. As a leading Swiss insurance company, we offer ambitious people attractive career opportunities and entrepreneurial freedom.

  • Discount of up to 30% on AXA products
  • 5 weeks vacation
  • Fleet discount/reimbursement of half-fare travelcard/GA (pro-rata)
  • Access to the Swibeco platform
  • Scope for independent action
  • Innovative structures, processes and modern tools
  • Sound, professional training and further education opportunities
