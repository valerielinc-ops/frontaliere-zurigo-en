Full description

Our Personnel and Organizational Development (POE) department is looking for a humorous, winning and empathetic personality as a human resources / organizational developer, 60 - 70%

  • You are responsible for the implementation of the newly designed talent management program and accompany our employees on their personal development journey
  • You design change processes systematically and advise/accompany the employees involved
  • You will further develop today’s personal diagnostics tools and ensure the implementation of internal aptitude assessments such as individual assessments and potential analyses.
  • You develop personnel development concepts and moderate workshops/seminars on topics such as diversity, values, leadership and thus make an important contribution to strengthening the corporate and leadership culture
  • You advise and coach employees and teams in development and change processes as well as in professional challenges
  • Master in Psychology at the level of University of Applied Sciences (FH) or University with specialization in Work/Organizational Psychology or Business Psychology
  • Further education in the field of change management and coaching training
  • Several years of well-founded professional practice e.g. in the topics of personnel diagnostics, consulting/coaching, change management, diversity and leadership/cultural development
  • Analytical thinking skills, high service orientation as well as openness and pleasure in cooperation in a diverse field of blueness
  • A high degree of responsibility and the opportunity to create new
  • An exciting and unusual activity in a dynamic, collegial working environment of a 24/7 rescue organization
  • Modern employment conditions (sports offers, lunch checks, etc.)
  • Centrally located workplace in Zurich Wiedikon as well as possibility of home office

Protection & Rescue Zurich (SRZ), as a service department of the Department of Security, unites the professional and militia fire brigades, the medical department and the operational control center (ELZ) for the emergency calls 144 and 118. In addition, civil protection, fire police and a training center with an affiliated Higher School for Rescue Occupations (HFRB) belong to the largest civil rescue organization in Switzerland. Intervention and prevention units protect and save people, animals, property and the environment around the clock in the city of Zurich, at Zurich Airport and in agglomeration.
