Full description

With us you shape innovative banking solutions of the future. We are looking for you: a personality who immerses himself in concepts, architectural designs, pipelines and code for DevOps platforms and sees the essentials. As a DecSecOps engineer, stability and security are equally important to you.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Bank, Wallisellen, Industriestrasse 17-19, 8304 Wallisellen

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You will actively work in an agile multidisciplinary team and continuously develop our digital services and platforms. You always keep the customer view in focus.
  • With your DevSecOps mindset, you identify and evaluate the automation potential at the integration level. You will continuously inform yourself about the current developments in the market.
  • You design and create declarative source code for infrastructure, configuration and monitoring, and integrate our security mechanisms into the CI/CD pipelines.
  • You will be responsible for the lifecycle of our DevOps platform, design the architecture and define requirements for project implementation with development and system partners.
  • You’ll be a link between the platform team and the DevSecOps community and you’ll be involved in architecture and governance issues. You advise the departments on technical topics.
  • You support DevSecOps engineers as well as classic security with your focus on "Secure by Design" and "Security Shift-Left".

Requirements

  • 3-5 years of relevant professional experience: Full-Stack Software Engineering
  • Student (Uni/FH/HF): Computer Science
  • Knowledge and practical experience in dealing with Gitlab and container technology / OpenShift, as well as CI / CD pipeline, and / or artifact store and / or secret vault technologies.
  • Knowledge of cloud native development and principles of automated software quality assurance.
  • Knowledge of IT security topics (vulnerability management, network policies, shift-left security).
  • You understand technical connections and can discuss them at eye level with our software development partners.
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • English (negotiable)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central place of work (GABS): Optimally accessible by train (10 minutes from Zurich HB) or car
  • Flexible working hours: annual working hours for all and trusted working hours for many
  • Flexible place of work / Home Office: Individual possibilities for home office
  • Cumulus points: Up to 5 additional points per franc purchase value
  • Du-Culture: Lived team spirit with transparent communication
  • Individual training: Attractive contributions for external training
