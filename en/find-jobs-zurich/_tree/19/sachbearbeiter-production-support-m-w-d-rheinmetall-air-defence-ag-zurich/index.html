Full description

As Production Support (m/f/d), you are responsible for ensuring the ongoing operational support of production contracts in the area of assemblies and final assembly. Specifically, you take on the following tasks:

  • Collection, tracking and correction of order data as well as timely incorporation of changes from ongoing production
  • Processing of post-work orders and Q cases until completion
  • Recording and maintenance of modification confirmations (BTI)
  • Prioritization of pending cases with team leaders, foremen, production managers and AVOR
  • Search for affected orders in ongoing production for change requests
  • Maintaining a transparent case overview as a basis for reporting
  • Participation in the establishment of a structured standard process for BCC, rework and amendments
  • Deputy within the Production Support team if required WHAT YOU SHOULD BUILD
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship in the mechanical sector ideally with further training as process specialist (m/f/d) or comparable
  • Several years of experience in production and work preparation are advantageous
  • Experience in manufacturing technology and reading technical drawings
  • Understanding processes and electronics knowledge is beneficial
  • Experienced handling of common IT systems (Microsoft Office, SAP) as well as good knowledge of dealing with ERP systems
  • Very good German, as well as good English skills in word and writing WHAT WE ARE TO YOU
  • A dynamic working environment with exciting projects
  • Flexible working hours (active and trusted working hours)
  • Very good social benefits and a super-compulsory pension fund
  • Employee benefits of a large corporation, employee share purchase program
  • Promotion of needs-based training
  • Staff restaurant and external catering and shopping facilities nearby
  • Individual internal and external personnel development measures in cooperation with our Rheinmetall Academy
  • Workplace a few minutes walk from the Zurich-Oerlikon train station KONTAKTDETAILS Responsible contact person: Charlene Schnaars For questions about your application, please use the contact form. Please note that dossiers of recruiters are not taken into account.
