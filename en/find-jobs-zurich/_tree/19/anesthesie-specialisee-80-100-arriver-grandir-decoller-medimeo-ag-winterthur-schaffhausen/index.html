Specialist title in the bag, and now? Do you want more responsibility, but do not want to be thrown into the cold water? Here you do not have to lift everything alone from the first day. In a manageable, collegial team, you grow step by step into your new role. Experienced colleagues strengthen your back when it matters, and you help to set the pace. At the same time, you combine this career step with a new beginning in Switzerland, very close to the German border. The position is particularly aimed at younger specialists from Germany who would like to combine the next career step with a move to Switzerland. You bring your experience in general and regional anesthesia, expand your professional competences and gain initial experience at senior medical level. The regionally anchored acute care hospital in northern Switzerland performs around 6,500 anesthesias every year. The broad spectrum includes planned and emergency interventions as well as anesthesiological care in the operating room, in the nursery, in the intensive care and emergency department and in resuscitations. The care is organized via a 24-hour specialist presence system. As part of daytime responsibility as well as shift, night and weekend service, you take on a responsible function. You can always count on the professional support of an experienced team. The location near the German border makes it easier for you to enter Switzerland. If you first want to commute or arrive directly on site, furnished accommodation is available if necessary.

Their tasks

  • Conduct of general and regional anesthesias during planned and emergency interventions, with experienced colleagues as contact persons
  • Anesthesiological care of your patients before, during and after the procedure
  • Gradually assuming more responsibility in clinical everyday life and in the service system, in consultation with the team
  • Collaboration in the operating room, in the bear room, in the intensive care and emergency unit as well as in resuscitations
  • Support for assistant doctors in everyday life
  • Cooperation with operational disciplines, care and other professional groups
  • Bringing your own ideas to the further development of the department

Your Competences

  • Specialist title Anästhesiologie, German or EU-approved
  • Support for recognition in Switzerland by Medimeo
  • First medical experience in general and regional anesthesia
  • Desire to develop professionally and gradually take on more responsibility
  • Medical experience is not required
  • Willingness to participate in shift, night and weekend service
  • Enjoyment of knowledge transfer and cooperation with younger colleagues
  • Collegial personality with pleasure in interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

Their advantages

  • Accompanied entry after the specialist: You grow into more responsibility at your pace, with an experienced team at your back
  • Broad anesthesiological spectrum in a medium-sized acute hospital
  • Manageable department with short distances and personal cooperation
  • Internal and external training as well as individual professional development opportunities
  • Pensum between 80 and 100%, matched to your life situation
  • Furnished accommodation for an uncomplicated start in Switzerland
  • House-owned crèche directly on the hospital area

With Medimeo at your side, you will receive personal and discreet support during the entire application process, in the recognition of your specialist title by the Federal Medical Occupational Commission (MEBEKO), which is necessary for medical activity in Switzerland, as well as for all organisational questions relating to your change.
