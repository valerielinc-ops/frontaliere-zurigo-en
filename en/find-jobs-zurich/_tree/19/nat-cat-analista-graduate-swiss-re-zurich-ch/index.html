Full description

As a Graduate, you will develop a strong natural catastrophe end-to-end modelling foundation while building the technical and interpersonal skills needed to start your career in the reinsurance industry. Through hands-on experience, learning opportunities, and experience beyond the Cat Perils EMEA & Method team, the programme will support your growth into future roles within our organisation. As a Natural Catastrophe Analyst, you will apply your scientific expertise to build, maintain and communicate the next generation of Nat Cat models & Nat Cat underwriting for Europe, Middle East and Africa (EMEA) region.

Start your journey towards a successful career!

We’re looking for curious, motivated graduates with strong people skills who want to grow, learn, and make a difference. If you hold a Bachelor’s, Master’s, or PhD and have up to three years of professional experience, apply to our 18-month Graduate Programme starting 1 September 2027.

You’ll gain hands-on experience in the fast-paced world of reinsurance, rotate into a different local team, and receive tailored training to build your expertise. This is your chance to shape your future, help protect societies from the world’s most threatening hazards and make the world more resilient.

The Graduate Programme will equip you with the skills, foundations and network to pursue a wide range of career opportunities.

About the Role Are you aspiring to use your scientific background to increase society's resilience against natural catastrophes with the help of re/insurance? Do you want to work at the interface of science and commercial re/insurance products? We enable Swiss Re to maintain and expand its leadership in natural catastrophe re/insurance, in close collaboration with underwriters and clients. You will engage in and ultimately lead projects to improve natural catastrophe (Nat Cat) risk assessment and its seamless integration into Swiss Re's underwriting processes by collaborating with a wide range of internal and external teams on natural catastrophe related topics and develop novel insurance solutions to increase resilience against natural catastrophes.

Let us specify the responsibilities

  • Build, maintain and communicate Nat Cat risk views for the EMEA region, primarily extratropical windstorms and severe convective storms, but also wildfires and/or tropical cyclones
  • You advance our priority projects with a business mentality and ask the right questions to make sure we apply our risk view with maximum impact for the company.
  • Acquire and analyse data to quantify the impacts of natural catastrophes on buildings, facilities, and infrastructure.
  • Establish yourself as an expert on natural catastrophe risk and modelling within Swiss Re and especially underwriting teams, effectively communicate risk views internally and externally.
  • Foster a common view and narrative around atmospheric perils in the re/insurance context.

About the Team We are looking forward to welcoming a new team member to the EMEA Cat Perils & Methods team that belongs to a global Cat Perils team, and the Swiss Re's Property & Casualty business unit. The Cat Perils team owns Swiss Re’s global natural catastrophe risk assessment, the respective modelling and geo capabilities. On the natural catastrophe side, we advance innovative probabilistic and proprietary modelling technology for seismic, atmospheric and flood hazards. As part of the global Cat Perils team, you will work alongside a diverse group of about 70 colleagues across three locations in a supportive and collaborative environment where ownership, learning, teamwork, and sharing ideas are encouraged every day.

About You

You demonstrate a high degree of initiative and an interest in interdisciplinary work. Being a fast learner who is eager to take ownership, you approach problems with curiosity and flexibility. With you we win a colleague with excellent social competences who actively contributes to our team spirit.

Requirements

  • Successfully completed university degree in quantitative sciences like meteorology, physics, mathematics, data/computer science, or equivalent.
  • High motivation to become a re/insurance expert and create commercial value from scientific insights. Strong digital literacy. You have experience in programming (e.g. Python, R, Matlab) and have a record in developing numerical models and/or data analysis applications.
  • You have strong presentation skills and excel in verbal and written communication in English and like to explain sophisticated topics to non-experts.

There are some other traits we would see as beneficial

  • Experience in natural hazard assessment, natural catastrophe model development or re/insurance business.
  • Project management experience and stakeholder management skills.
  • We value the knowledge of additional languages as a plus.

Do you bring these traits? Then you might be the addition to our interdisciplinary team we are looking for!
