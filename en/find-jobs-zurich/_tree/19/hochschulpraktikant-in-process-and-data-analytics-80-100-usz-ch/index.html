Full description

USZ Stettbach

as of 1.2.27 or by agreement, limited to 12 months

You work closely with the departments at the interface of business analysis and data science. You turn raw data into scalable insights and intelligent automations and control smaller projects independently or help shape them. In this way, you make a measurable contribution to the efficiency and quality of healthcare processes.

Your main tasks

  • Contribute to data products: For the financial direction, you design dashboards, reports and key performance indicator systems for monitoring and control and improve them continuously (e.g. with Power BI or SAP SAC)
  • Database & Pipelines: You help build an SQL database and help automate data pipelines
  • Identify RPA potentials: You analyze process steps, propose candidates for automation and evaluate them
  • Document processes and knowledge: You create compact process descriptions and how-tos, version them, keep them findable and ensure the transfer to the departments
  • Change Management: You help shape the way of working in the team, introduce SOPs and support change projects in the area
  • Projects: You control smaller projects independently and shape them from analysis to implementation

Your profile

  • Enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's degree (e.g. Economics, Computer Science, Data/Business Analytics or similar)
  • First experience in the hospital environment
  • First practical experience with data analysis tools
  • Structured, curious, strong in communication: you can easily explain complex things
  • Languages: very good German and English in word and writing
  • Nice to have: first touch points with SAP and BI tools such as PowerBI or SAC
