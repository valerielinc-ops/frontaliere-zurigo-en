Full description

We are looking for a dedicated and detail-oriented sales associate with stockroom duties to join our dynamic team at our store in Wädenswil, Switzerland. This is a 50-percent position that combines motivating customer service on the sales floor with essential stockroom duties. If you thrive in a fast-paced retail environment and take pride in maintaining an organized and efficient stockroom operation, we would be very pleased to receive your application. You will play a key role in creating exceptional customer experiences while ensuring that our inventory is well managed and that our store is always stocked with high-quality goods.

Sales floor responsibilities

  • Provide excellent customer service and personalized product recommendations to increase sales and customer satisfaction
  • Process customer transactions accurately and efficiently using point-of-sale systems
  • Acquire and maintain comprehensive product knowledge to effectively communicate product features, benefits, and added value to customers
  • Create visually appealing product displays that meet the store's presentation standards and attract customers' attention
  • Answer customer inquiries and resolve issues professionally, promptly, and empathetically
  • Support the team in ensuring an inviting and well-organized sales environment

Warehouse tasks

  • Receiving, inspecting, and carefully storing incoming goods in accordance with established procedures
  • Maintaining accurate inventory records and conducting regular inventory counts to ensure data integrity
  • Preparing orders for shipment and ensuring the timely delivery of goods to the sales area
  • Organizing and arranging inventory in a logical and accessible manner to optimize efficiency
  • Ensure compliance with safety standards in the warehouse and maintain a clean, orderly work environment
  • Assist with inventory rotation and implement appropriate warehouse procedures to minimize waste and maximize productivity
  • Work with team members to support the overall business operations and goals of the business
