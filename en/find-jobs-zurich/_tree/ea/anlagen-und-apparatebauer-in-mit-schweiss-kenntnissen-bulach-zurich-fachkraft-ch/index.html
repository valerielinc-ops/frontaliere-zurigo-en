Full description

In this function, you produce complex sheet metal and pipe components according to drawing. The company ensures a varied environment and attractive payment. For our client we are looking for one/n activities

  • production of demanding sheet metal and pipe components from technical drawing
  • welding (TIG/WIG) of sheet metal, mainly in chrome-nickel steel
  • grinding of the welded components in visual quality
  • assembly of the individual components to form total plants
  • Requirements
  • Training as a plant and apparatus manufacturer EFZ or welder / locksmith
  • Work experience in the metal industry as well as in the processing of chrome-nickel steel
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Joy of self-responsible, reliable and precise work Offer It awaits you a dynamic environment, a versatile field of activity, a very good hourly wage and the chance of permanent employment.
