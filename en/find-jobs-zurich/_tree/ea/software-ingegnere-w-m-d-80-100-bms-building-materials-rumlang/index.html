Full description

BMS Building Materials Suisse is a wholesaler of building materials for shell and interior construction. Our group of companies includes the renowned specialists Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced experts advise, support and inspire customers from the construction industry as well as private customers at around 110 locations throughout Switzerland. Close to everyday construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Quick, easy and competent.

Rümlang or Buchs (AG), by agreement

Do you not only want to write code, but also develop applications that really make a difference in everyday work? With us, you design data-driven solutions from the first idea to productive use for the entire company and along the entire value chain. You develop your own applications, shape their architecture, work with modern technologies and contribute your ideas directly. You have a lot of creative freedom and see immediately what added value your work creates.

Task area

  • You develop end-to-end features: data model, API and user interface.
  • You design and develop the software architecture of our applications and data pipelines, paying attention to maintainability, simplicity and scalability.
  • You work with our data platform (Databricks, Delta Lake) and turn data into usable features.
  • If necessary, you can integrate AI functions (e.g. agents, MCP servers, LLM APIs) into our applications where they bring real added value.
  • You talk directly to the departments, understand their problems and quickly validate ideas with prototypes, which you develop together with the users.
  • You ensure clean operation, good code quality and compliance with data protection, security and compliance requirements.

Our typical stack

So that you can get an idea of what we are working on

  • Backend: Python, FastAPI, Pydantic
  • Data: PostgreSQL, Databricks, Delta Lake
  • Platform: Azure, Infrastructure as Code (Bicep), CI/CD, modern tooling (uv, ruff, bun)
  • Frontend: React, TypeScript, TanStack
  • AI: Claude, MCP, AI-assisted development with Claude Code

You don’t have to know every tool already. It is important that you can build software clean and learn new things quickly.

Profile

  • You have several years of experience in software development and have brought applications from idea to operation.
  • You are strong in Python and familiar with relational databases and SQL.
  • You have a good sense of software architecture and know how to build systems simply, robustly and expandably.
  • You have experience with Azure and cloud architectures.
  • Frontend experience is beneficial. We use React and TanStack, but we are flexible. AI is good enough today to support you in the frontend.
  • You will develop AI-supported and have practical experience with Claude Code, Skills and MCP.
  • You think business-oriented: you ask first which problem to solve, and then how.
  • You can deal with vague requirements and develop a product step by step together with the stakeholders.
  • You like to work autonomously and take responsibility for what you build.
  • You speak German or French and have a good knowledge of English.
  • Advantage: Experience with Databricks or ML.

You can look forward to that.

  • A lot of design room and responsibility for your own applications
  • Modern technologies and freedom to contribute their own solutions
  • Direct exchange with the departments and short decision-making paths
  • Exciting projects with real added value and visible results
  • Insights into our open source projects on GitHub and in the Fabricks framework

We offer

  • a challenging, versatile and interesting activity in a good working environment, where the emphasis is on active cooperation and at the same time self-employment
  • intensive training is a program, whereby our new colleagues can benefit from product and system training within the company
  • Support our employees in their professional and personal development
  • Home office possible according to regulations and agreement
  • 5 weeks holiday (6 weeks from 50 years of age) and holiday shopping up to max. 10 days, additional benefits such as an excellently financed independent pension fund and discounts on our complete product range as well as external premium benefits

We look forward to your complete application documents in electronic form.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
