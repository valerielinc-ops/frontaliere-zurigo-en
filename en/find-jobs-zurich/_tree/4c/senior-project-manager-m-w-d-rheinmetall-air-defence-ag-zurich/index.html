Full description

Where we are looking for them

  • Management of highly complex, international customer projects as well as self-financed development and sub-projects
  • Professional management of bid teams for the preparation of offers, including conducting customer presentations and contract negotiations
  • Ensuring contract-compliant project implementation within the framework of company goals (focus: processes, costs, deadlines, quality) as well as overall responsibility for project success
  • Responsibility for internal and external contract and claim management
  • Control of the communication flow as a central hub between customers, suppliers and internal departments WHAT YOU SHOULD INVOLVE
  • Successfully completed studies as an engineer (m/f/d) in the field of mechanical engineering or electrical engineering with additional business education – alternatively a business studies program with a sound further education in the engineering disciplines
  • Several years of in-depth experience in end-to-end project management (from the offer phase and negotiation through implementation to the use phase), ideally in the defence sector, machinery or plant engineering
  • A certification according to IPMA Level C is mandatory
  • Qualification according to IPMA Level B is an advantage
  • Entrepreneurially thinking personality with strong negotiating skills, assertiveness as well as high communication and persuasiveness paired with strong team skills
  • Secure and agile appearance as well as targeted communication at all hierarchical levels
  • High resilience, resilience and flexibility for multi-day business trips
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in words and writing as well as a strong intercultural competence; other foreign language skills (e.g. Spanish, Italian) are welcome
  • Military prior knowledge as officer (m/f/d) is beneficial WHAT WE ARE TO YOU
  • A dynamic working environment with exciting projects
  • Flexible working hours (active and trusted working hours)
  • Very good social benefits and a super-compulsory pension fund
  • Employee benefits of a large corporation, employee share purchase program
  • Promotion of needs-based training
  • Staff restaurant and external catering and shopping facilities nearby
  • Individual internal and external personnel development measures in cooperation with our Rheinmetall Academy
  • Workplace a few minutes walk from the Zurich-Oerlikon train station KONTAKTDETAILS Responsible contact: Michael Zmoos For questions about your application, please use the contact form. Please note that dossiers of recruiters are not taken into account.
