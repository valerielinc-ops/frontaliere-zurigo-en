Full description

Place of work: Denner AG, headquarters, Grubenstrasse 10, 8045 Zurich

Do you love food and keep track of the turbulent day-to-day business? As an assistant in purchasing, you are the organizational backbone of our team and ensure that no promotion or listing goes down. And there are endless snacks in our office on top!

Luogo di lavoro: Denner AG, headquarters, Grubenstrasse 10, 8045 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Collect, maintain and control article master data as well as campaign and advertising data in SAP.
  • Administrative support of the Product Manager Food in day-to-day business.
  • Ensure the flow of information with internal contacts such as goods planning, scheduling, marketing and accounting.
  • Independent communication with suppliers on billing and contracts.

Requirements

  • 3-5 years of professional experience: Advantage in a retail or food business; or experience in a comparable position
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): Commercial basic training with further training for clerks* in purchasing an advantage
  • High affinity and user experience in complex ERP systems
  • Open, pleasant and resilient hands-on personality who likes to contribute to a dynamic team
  • Proactive, thoughtful and accurate and efficient working method with a strong service idea
  • in German (negotiation confident)
  • English (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Part of the Migros Group: Strong brand anchored in the retail trade under the Migros Group umbrella
  • Central place of work: In the casual Binz district in Zurich district 3 (2 minutes by foot from Zurich station Binz).
  • Flexible place of work / home office: One to two days a week working at home. Except in IT - we are even more flexible.
  • Mobile subscription: Spoiled for choice: With Galaxus Mobile, Wingo or Sunrise already from CHF 10.- one or the other really connected.
  • Migros Bank: Better ride on interest and fees of your private, investment or mortgage accounts.
  • Personal responsibility & freedom: plenty of room for your ideas and to make a difference together with us.

Contatto

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Test work: A trial day or half day gives the opportunity to get to know the future working environment and the team members better. And conversely, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition: After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
