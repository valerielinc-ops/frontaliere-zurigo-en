Full description

We are looking for a motivated personality for the specialist staff of the Social Urban Life business unit on 1 February 2027 or by agreement, limited to 11 months.

  • They participate in various projects in the field of social urban development and socio-culture.
  • They organise meetings and exchange bodies and take on communication and administration tasks.
  • They participate in the planning and implementation of specialist conferences and events.
  • They research, analyze and prepare the basis for professional developments and reports.
  • You work on the strategic development of the business area.
  • Recently completed university studies (bachelor or master) in a discipline relevant to the position (e.g. social work, social, cultural or humanities, human geography, urban studies or design)
  • Interest in topics of social urban development and socio-culture as well as urban events in the city of Zurich
  • Enjoy working on complex, demanding and interdisciplinary tasks
  • Very good oral and written communication skills as well as good knowledge of common office programs
  • A structured, careful and proactive way of working, as well as flexibility, curiosity and innovative thinking
  • Interesting and varied work
  • Co-creation opportunities
  • Meaningful activity
  • Attractive location in the heart of Zurich
  • Insight into processes within the city administration

With around 2,400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a social Zurich. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.

The Social Urban Life division of Social Services comprises the Youth Cultural House Dynamo, the Debate House Karl der Grosse, the Space Exchange, the Socio-Culture District 3, 4, 5 and the Office for Social Space & Urban Life. The team supports the business unit management in strategic and technical issues and accompanies the departments in the further development of their offers and services. As a small, interdisciplinary team, the staff deals with topics at the interface of socio-culture, urban development and administration.
