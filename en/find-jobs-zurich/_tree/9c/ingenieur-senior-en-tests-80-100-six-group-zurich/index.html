Senior Test Engineer (80-100%)

Zurich, Up to 40% HomeOffice possible, Reference 8324

You like working in a dynamic team and a agile environment

and test automation is your strength? Then become Senior Test Engineer at Terravis and actively shape how we develop smart, scalable solutions.

As a leading digital platform, Terravis sets standards in the domain-wide digital processing of property business and connects the financial industry, notarial and land accounting for secure, efficient and legally compliant property processes and property transactions. As part of our Business Engineering & Testing team, you work closely with internal and external stakeholders to further develop and optimize the range of services around Terravis. We look forward to receiving your application documents.

Tasks

  • You work closely with development and DevOps and share your know-how with the team of the company.
  • Conduct manual tests based on internal requirements / customer requirements, documentation and analysis of test results.
  • Create meaningful Jira tickets as a basis for bugfixing during development and at the same time as a decision-making aid for release management.
  • Execution and documentation of automated tests.
  • Support for re-factoring of existing test machines and the development of a scalable test automation solution.

Requirements

  • Test architecture – You know how to build up a ready-to-use and configurable test automation frameworks.
  • Test data is provided and used independently via interfaces such as SQL servers or Excel, thus creating the basis for reproducible manual and automated testing.
  • Error analysis and reporting – Ability to identify and fix errors in the test run (including logs, screenshots, reports).
  • Create meaningful tickets for bugs, which are found with the functional tests in the test subject.
  • Safe handling of test automations – Safe handling of selenium webdriver and cucbridge.
  • You write time-tested test scenarios and implement the corresponding step definitions across browsers (Chrome, Edge) against our web application.
  • We require well-founded experience in Java and Maven.
  • JavaScript skills are beneficial.
  • Frameworks and tools – experience with jetbrains IntelliJ IDE, JUnit, Selenium, Cucbridge, JDBC, java (wsimport), Smartbear SOAP-UI, GitLab and JIRA.
  • Languages
  • Very good German and English knowledge in word and writing.
