Full description

Are you enthusiastic about the financial world? For our team focused on Swiss private clients we are looking for an engaged Client Associate for our Zurich location who will actively support our team.

Your Tasks

  • You independently manage and support the administration of a client portfolio, always placing the needs of our clients at the forefront.
  • You are the first point of contact for administrative matters related to client relationships, such as cash management, transfers, securities orders and their monitoring, renewals of trust investments, as well as other account activities.
  • You identify potential risks early by carefully monitoring risk and compliance indicators.
  • You support the Relationship Managers on KYC matters, the organization of client meetings, and the preparation of investment proposals.
  • You ensure smooth onboarding of new clients and the timely closure of client relationships.

Your Profile

  • You have experience in a comparable role within the banking industry.
  • You communicate fluently in German and have good English skills as well as strong communication abilities.
  • You are a team‑oriented personality with a strong service orientation and enjoy direct client contact.
  • You have good knowledge of financial products and financial markets.
  • You possess excellent IT user skills, especially in MS Office and other digital tools.
  • You have an affinity for Artificial Intelligence (AI) and are ready to actively use AI‑powered tools and modern working methods.

What We Offer You

  • An attractive workplace in the heart of Zurich with modern work and meeting spaces and free lunch.
  • A collegial and diverse team in an open and dynamic work environment.
  • State‑of‑the‑art technologies that support you in your daily work and professional development.
  • An agile work environment with a culture of trust, personal responsibility and collaboration.
  • Flat hierarchies and straightforward communication at all levels.
  • An inspiring campus atmosphere with strong team spirit and pronounced collaboration.

We are looking for talented people, not just a résumé that meets all requirements. Even if you do not meet every criterion, we look forward to your application if you feel this position is a good fit for you.

Why Vontobel?

At Vontobel you shape better future prospects for our clients, for society, and for yourself. As a family‑owned and publicly listed company with over 2,000 employees at 29 locations worldwide and headquarters in Zurich, we focus on long‑term thinking and sustainable action.

Our diverse team values different perspectives. We are looking for people who are committed, take responsibility and want to work successfully in a dynamic, constantly changing environment. Even if you do not meet all requirements, we explicitly encourage you to apply if you are enthusiastic about the role.
