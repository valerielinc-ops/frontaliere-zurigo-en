Full description

  • Support our customers, system integrators and internal sales and service teams in technical issues related to our robotics portfolio, remotely and on site
  • Advising our customers on the application, programming and integration of our robots
  • Develop and validate suitable automation concepts using various technologies, such as tooling, vision, conveyor tracking and safety
  • Testing the technical feasibility of robotics solutions using simulation software, including clock time and payload analyses
  • Performing proof-of-principle tests with the Stäubli Robotics Suite
  • Supporting sales in technical issues as well as accompanying trade fairs and events as a technical specialist
  • Advising on our entire robot portfolio and the available software solutions
  • Assuming a technical expert role for the specification and application of our robotics portfolio
  • Providing technical know-how within the team and supporting customer training
  • Support our customers with a focus in the DACH region and, if necessary, on site throughout the North East Europe region
  • Technical education or studies, e.g. in electrical engineering, automation engineering, mechatronics, computer science or a similar specialisation
  • Good programming knowledge and experience in robotics or automation
  • Knowledge of relevant technologies such as 3D CAD, 4- and 6-axis robotics, electrical installations and PLC and fieldbus technologies
  • Good technical understanding as well as the ability to analyze technical drawings and work on complex problems in a solution-oriented manner
  • Independent and structured working methods as well as strong communication and team skills
  • Category B driving licence and willingness to travel around 40%
  • Place of residence in the Horgen area or Zurich preferred
  • Interesting activities in an environment full of fascinating technology
  • Fairness, honesty and a humane, respectful approach
  • Both advantages of a medium-sized company and an international group
  • Flexibility time without core time with the possibility of mobile work and company cars that can also be used privately
  • Attractive compensation including 13. Monthly salary with numerous social benefits and other benefits such as taking over the NBU premium, attractive pension benefits and seniority gifts
  • In-house training and promotion opportunities and much more...
