Full description

About the role Modern buildings are traversed both horizontally and vertically by numerous utilities and network services such as cables, pipes and ventilation ducts, resulting in a variety of penetrations in most walls and floors. Sika Schweiz AG offers an extensive range of products for structural fire protection. Our product engineers make an important contribution in all phases of product development. At our location in Zurich-Altstetten, we are looking for a product engineer in fire protection and facade.

In this role, you are responsible for the following tasks

  • Supervision of the product range with regard to technical and application performance
  • Preparation of specifications for the development of new products, management and evaluation of the field test phase and the subsequent market launch in local projects and cooperation in global product developments
  • Advising and schools of sales units on technical issues and complaints
  • Preparation of product data sheets and application guidelines as well as other technical documents
  • Assistance in creating suitable marketing tools
  • Professional guidance of the associated application instructor

Your skills and experience

  • University studies as a civil engineer, economic chemist or materials scientist
  • Several years of professional practice in structural fire protection
  • Stylistic German; good knowledge of English (verbal and written) and French
  • High self-motivation, pleasure in customer contact and of course the necessary bite

Why you should come to us

  • Trust is the most important thing for us. We trust in your abilities and provide you with many design spaces – every day anew.
  • Intensive training in our products, systems and processes will enable you to start successfully.
  • Uncomplicated managers who trust and a familiar atmosphere with a friendly tone make working at Sika special – many of us call it the Sika spirit.

We look forward to your complete application dossier.

We offer competitive remuneration based on local market standards and the specific responsibilities of each job. The remuneration shall be based on the skills, responsibility, education and/or training relevant to the position. We are committed to fair and equitable remuneration schemes in accordance with the applicable laws, the Chemical Tariff Agreement and regulations.

About Sika Sika is a specialty chemical company, global leader in the development and production of systems and products for gluing, sealing, steaming, reinforcing and protecting for the construction and vehicle industries. Sika has a global presence with subsidiaries in 103 countries, produces in over 400 factories and develops innovative technologies for customers around the world, contributing significantly to the sustainable transformation of the construction and vehicle industry. The more than 33,000 employees generated a turnover of CHF 11.2 billion in 2025.
