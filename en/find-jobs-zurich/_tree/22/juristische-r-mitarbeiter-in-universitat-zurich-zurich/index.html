Department of Students

Job opening 1.2.2027 or by agreement, indefinitely

The Students Department is responsible for all centrally organized processes of student administration at UZH such as admission, change of course and study program, collection of semester fees, diploma printing, issuance of UZH Card, holidays and exmatriculation.

Tasks

Together with a second lawyer, you will advise and support the department management and the other staff of the department as well as the study deanates of the faculties in all legal matters concerning admission and the central student administration. In particular, you are responsible for the following tasks:

  • independent and stylish writing of legal documents,
  • replying to inquiries, in particular concerning initial decisions in the field of admissions requiring in-depth legal knowledge;
  • Participation in the revision of legal bases in the area of admission, admission restriction and student administration.

Profile

We are looking for an uncomplicated, empathetic and team capable personality with a university degree in law (lic. iur. or Master of Law). They have very good analytical skills, a quick comprehension and a precise and careful way of working. With your excellent knowledge of German and English, you are able to present complicated issues in writing and orally in German and English. Interest in legal research on foreign education systems on the constitution of objections rounds off your profile. Any knowledge of the national and international education system is advantageous.
