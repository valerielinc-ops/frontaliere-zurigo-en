Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School Stadelhofen Branch Dübendorf ICT system technician/-in 80 - 100% The branch Dübendorf of the Cantonal School Stadelhofen has been running a high school, a short high school and a middle school since the school year 2026/27. In full operation, it offers space for 650 students on Empa’s campus. In the summer of 2027, another building will be opened on the IPZ site in Dübendorf - also for 650 students. For the reliable operation, support and further development of the ICT infrastructure of our branch Dübendorf we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement a dedicated personality as an ICT system technician. You will start your work at Empa and will be used alternately at both locations in Dübendorf after the IPZ site has been commissioned. Their tasks

  • They ensure the reliable operation of the school ICT infrastructure and are the first point of contact for employees, teachers and learners. You provide 1st and 2nd level support and process disruptions and service requests via ServiceNow.
  • They supervise school hardware such as network printers, display monitors and AVS devices and, if necessary, coordinate further support with cantonal offices and external partners. Another focus of her work is in workplace and equipment management. They manage and manage managed devices in Windows and macOS environments, manage the mobile telephony of administrative staff and are responsible for user management, device allocation and device lifecycle management.
  • In the area of network and infrastructure, you act as an interface to external providers. They open tickets at Swisscom, coordinate changes and track network disruptions in the LAN, WLAN and VLAN environment. You also support the operation and monitoring of the building management system.
  • In the Microsoft 365 pane, you maintain user accounts, groups and permissions in Microsoft 365 and Entra ID. They manage SharePoint environments and teams groups and are responsible for entry and exit and mutations of user accounts.
  • In addition, you participate in the creation and maintenance of process and system documentation, work in ICT projects and support tests, pilot operations and acceptance of new systems and solutions. Your profile
  • You have completed training as a computer scientist or equivalent qualification and have experience in ICT support as well as in the operation of workplace and school IT infrastructures.
  • They have a good knowledge of Windows environments and Microsoft 365. Knowledge of macOS environments as well as network and infrastructure topics is an advantage.
  • They work independently, structured and service-oriented and appreciate the direct contact with teachers, learners and employees.
  • Feeling as a communicative and reliable personality You are comfortable in a lively school environment and approach tasks in a solution-oriented manner. An interesting and varied task awaits you in an unusual, dynamic environment of a cantonal middle school. In the context of digital change and the establishment of our branch Dübendorf, you can play a formative role in shaping the ongoing and upcoming changes to our IT infrastructure. Based on your previous professional activities, you can actively contribute your own ideas and experiences for the further development of the school. The Conditions of Employment are governed by the Staff Regulations of the Canton of Zurich. the activity requires a local presence
  • Homeoffice is not intended for this function. Holidays must be paid during school holidays. Application Have we aroused your interest? We look forward to your application. Please submit your documents by 17 December 2026 exclusively via the online tool. Applications outside the online tool are not accepted and deleted immediately. For information, Mr. Enver Kilinc, Head of IT, is at your disposal (+41 43 256 27 06). Further information about our school can be found at Cantonal School Stadelhofen – Filiale Dübendorf.
