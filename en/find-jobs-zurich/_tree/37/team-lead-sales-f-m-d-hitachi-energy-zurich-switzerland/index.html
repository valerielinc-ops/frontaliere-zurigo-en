Full description

The opportunity At Hitachi Energy, we are looking for an experienced people leader with hands-on mentality to take ownership of a globally distributed sales organization supporting our High Voltage business. In this leadership role, you will drive sales effectiveness, strengthen operational excellence, and develop teams across Switzerland, India, and the Czech Republic.

You will play a critical role in ensuring that our sales organization is equipped with the right processes, tools, and capabilities to deliver business growth and exceptional customer support.

How you'll make an impact

  • Lead and develop a team of sales support specialists
  • Provide leadership to sales support organizations in India and the Czech Republic through local team leads
  • Drive workforce and capacity planning across all locations to ensure resources are aligned with business priorities and customer demand
  • Establish transparency on workload, performance, and resource utilization through effective reporting and planning tools
  • Lead sales operations activities, including process optimization, governance, and continuous improvement initiatives
  • Identify and implement opportunities to increase efficiency, collaboration, and service quality across the global organization
  • Partner closely with regional sales teams, business leaders, and support functions to achieve commercial objectives
  • Foster a high-performance, collaborative, and customer-focused culture across geographically dispersed teams
  • Drive the adoption of digital tools and best practices to support scalable growth

Your background

  • Bachelor's degree in business, engineering, economics, or a related field
  • Min. 5-10 years of professional experience in sales, sales operations, commercial excellence, customer support, or a comparable commercial environment
  • Proven leadership experience with direct people management responsibility
  • Experience managing international and cross-functional teams within a matrix organization
  • Proven track record in process improvement, operational excellence, and implementation of business tools
  • Strong analytical skills with the ability to translate data into actionable decisions
  • Hands-on mindset with a pragmatic and solution-oriented approach
  • Technical affinity and the ability to understand complex industrial products and customer requirements
  • Fluency in German and English is mandatory
  • Positive energy, strong communication skills, and the ability to lead with clarity, accountability, and authenticity

More about us Hitachi Energy is a global technology leader advancing a sustainable energy future for all. We serve customers in the utility, industry, and infrastructure sectors with innovative solutions and services across the value chain. Join us and become part of a diverse and collaborative environment where your leadership can make a meaningful impact on our business and our customers worldwide.
