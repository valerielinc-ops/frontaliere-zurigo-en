Full description

As a Graduate, you will develop a strong underwriting foundation while building the technical and interpersonal skills needed to start your career in the reinsurance industry. Through hands-on experience, learning opportunities, and experience across the full underwriting value chain, the programme will support your growth into future roles within our organisation.

As an Underwriter, you will help assess and manage risks ranging from man-made hazards & natural catastrophes to casualty exposures and liability claims. Start your journey towards a successful career!

We’re looking for curious, motivated graduates with strong people skills who want to grow, learn, and make a difference. If you hold a Bachelor’s, Master’s, or PhD and have up to three years of professional experience, apply to our 18-month Graduate Programme starting 1 September 2027.

You’ll gain hands-on experience in the fast-paced world of reinsurance, rotate into a different local team, and receive tailored training to build your expertise. This is your chance to shape your future, help protect societies from the world’s most threatening hazards and make the world more resilient.

The Graduate Programme will equip you with the skills, foundations and network to pursue a wide range of career opportunities. About the Role As a Graduate, you will gain real-world experience at the core of our business while developing the technical and effective communication skills needed for a career in reinsurance. From day one, you will work on meaningful tasks, collaborate with experienced colleagues, and build exposure across different areas of the underwriting value chain.

With support from mentors and a collaborative environment, you will contribute to projects and underwriting activities that help assess risk, drive decisions and support business performance.

Key responsibilities include

  • Supporting underwriting assessments and risk evaluation activities (e.g. supporting risk assessments for large corporate exposures, contributing to pricing and underwriting decisions)
  • Analysing and modelling data and market insights to support informed decision-making (e.g. Evaluate reinsurance treaties in line with company guidelines and standards, make decisions on your assigned treaties and work with Product Underwriters on more complex cases)
  • Working with brokers, clients, and cross-functional teams across the business (e.g. building and nurturing relationships)
  • Being involved in communication and negotiation with brokers and clients
  • Contributing to projects that help you gain a more holistic view of the underwriting value chain.
  • Developing knowledge across underwriting, claims, client management, and data analytics

About the Team P&C Reinsurance is Swiss Re’s largest Business Unit, spanning Property and Casualty Treaty, Specialty, and Facultative lines of business. The team plays a key role in helping clients manage risk through expertise, collaboration, and innovation.

As part of the Graduate Programme, you will join Team M iddle East, focused on Gulf, Egypt & Iraq. You will work alongside a global and diverse group of P&C colleagues across locations in a supportive and collaborative environment where learning, teamwork, and sharing ideas are encouraged every day. We combine reliability and technical expertise with agility, creativity and fresh thinking to build client relationships and grow our portfolio. We manage client portfolios, create tailored reinsurance solutions and partner with clients on strategic discussions, making effective use of Swiss Re’s resources and services. Bring your ideas and energy to a team with entrepreneurial spirit and expertise. Together, we’ll build experience, keep developing and have fun along the way!
