Full description

IT/Informatics Pensum: 50% - 100% Place of work: Level of experience: With professional experience FlexOffice: on site Helvetic Warranty GmbH is an independent Swiss company in the field of embedded insurance and market leader in Switzerland. We offer tailor-made property insurance for the DACH region and are growing strongly in this area. Since 2019 we are a subsidiary of Helvetia Versicherungen. As an intermediary between customers and insurers, we offer B2B2C solutions, from product development to employee training to claims settlement. For more than 10 years, we have been bundling our services centrally in Switzerland, communicating in all national languages and English. What to expect from us

  • A versatile role with real professional responsibility and scope for design.
  • A small, dedicated IT team with short decision paths and direct cooperation.
  • A changing IT landscape in which you can help shape new solutions and develop existing topics sustainably.
  • A working environment where self-employment is expressly desired and you can count on sparring and support at the same time.

What you do

  • You take on the professional responsibility for the further development of our insurance platform in the areas of accounting and reporting as well as for the associated processes, systems and interfaces.
  • You analyze requirements together with the specialist areas, question connections and translate them into complete, comprehensible and implementable specifications for our development partners.
  • You will accompany B2B partner onboarding end-to-end – from clarification of requirements to specification and implementation to testing, go-live and subsequent stabilization.
  • You plan and structure your topics independently, set the right priorities and keep an eye on dependencies, risks and deadlines.
  • You coordinate your topics independently with departments, development partners and other stakeholders and ensure that open points are consistently pursued until the solution.
  • You conduct tests and technical approvals as part of our releases and ensure that new or adapted functions can be used fully and in the required quality productively.
  • Even after the go-live, your topic remains your topic: You support users, analyze problems and improvement possibilities and continuously develop your modules and processes.

What you bring

  • You have experience as a Business Analyst, Requirements Engineer or in a comparable interface function, or you have in-depth accounting/accounting knowledge and want to further develop at the interface between Business and IT.
  • You know requirements engineering methods and enjoy understanding complex technical contexts, preparing requirements in a structured manner and deriving clear, implementable specifications from them.
  • You work independently, think ahead and reliably advance topics with a good sense of priorities, dependencies and quality.
  • You communicate clearly, bindingly and on an equal footing and manage to bring internal and external stakeholders together effectively.
  • You like to take on responsibility throughout the entire lifecycle and have the aspiration not only to implement solutions, but also to develop them sustainably.
  • You have very good knowledge of German and feel safe in English communication.
