Full description

Digitalization is changing healthcare and the way a modern hospital works. The Cantonal Hospital Winterthur (KSW) is strategically realigning its digitalization. Our goal: efficient, secure and economical IT, simpler processes and digital solutions that offer patients, employees and the entire hospital a measurable added value. We are looking for you for this key position: an entrepreneurial and leadership-strong personality who combines strategic foresight with consistent implementation. You take over the leadership of our newly oriented organization Digitalization & IT (DIT) and develop it further in a future-oriented manner. As a member of the Executive Board, you actively shape the further development of the KSW. You combine technology, medicine, care and operational requirements and are responsible for both reliable ICT operations and the successful digital transformation of the hospital. A demanding management task with a great deal of room for manoeuvre, strategic responsibility and a direct impact on the performance of the KSW.

Tasks

  • As a member of the Executive Board, you actively shape the strategic, organizational and economic development of KSW and lead the Digitalization & IT department with overall responsibility.
  • You’ll develop a powerful DIT organization with clear responsibilities, integrated product teams and end-to-end responsibility from requirement to implementation to operation. You strengthen the implementation power and performance of the organization sustainably.
  • You are responsible for the implementation and further development of the digitization strategy 2027-2031 and the ICT strategy of the KSW. Together with the clinical, administrative and operational areas, you will develop digital solutions, simplify processes and drive the targeted use of AI, data and automation. The KISIM ecosystem forms a central basis for clinical digitalization.
  • You are responsible for the management of the DIT portfolio as well as the economic and resource-oriented implementation of the projects. The Clinical Digital Board takes over the professional prioritization of clinical digital projects.
  • You ensure stable, secure and economical ICT operations as well as a sustainable infrastructure and application architecture. You are responsible for operational information security in the area of DIT and ensure effective cooperation with the CISO staff office.
  • You are responsible for budget, investments, cost development and supplier and contract management. By standardizing, automating, cleaning up grown structures and targeted sourcing, you increase profitability and create space for the further development of KSW’s digital services.
  • You develop your leadership teams, create clear structures and commitment and successfully lead your organization through challenging change processes.

Profile

  • You have a university degree in computer science, medical informatics, business informatics or a comparable discipline as well as a further education in leadership and management.
  • You have several years of experience managing a larger IT organization, preferably in the healthcare sector, and understand the requirements of a business-critical operation with high availability. You win over different professional groups for digital changes.
  • You have successfully developed IT organizations and bring experience in transformation, sourcing and the optimization of complex IT structures. Your successes in the further development of organizations and the sustainable implementation of digital changes are demonstrable.
  • You understand the requirements of complex, mission-critical IT environments and the importance of digital solutions for clinical, administrative and operational processes. You can combine different professional and technological perspectives and develop viable solutions.
  • You combine strategic foresight with implementation strength and economic thinking. Complex projects lead you with clear priorities and a consistent view of impact and results.
  • You communicate and negotiate convincingly at the level of management and hospital council. You win over different stakeholders for common goals and accompany them effectively through change.
