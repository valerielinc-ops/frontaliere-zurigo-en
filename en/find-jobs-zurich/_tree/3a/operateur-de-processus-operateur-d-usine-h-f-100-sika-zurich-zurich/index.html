About the role For Sika Manufacturing AG in Zurich-Altstetten, we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement, in our production plant for the production of adhesives and sealants of specialty chemistry a committed and responsible personality as Process operator / plant operator (m/f) 100%

  • Operation and monitoring of production and bottling plants
  • Ensuring quality, safety and timeliness in the production process
  • Capture production data, maintain manufacturing and filling protocols and work with SAP
  • Resolving simple technical failures and performing maintenance, cleaning and maintenance work on assigned facilities and operational infrastructure
  • Active participation in improvements of processes and plants (CIP)

Your skills and experience

  • Completed basic technical or craft training, ideally as a process operator, plant operator or equivalent
  • Experience in an industrial production environment, preferably in the chemical, pharmaceutical, plastics or food industry
  • Readiness for 3- or 4-shift operation
  • Stamp ID for categories R1, R2 and S2 must be available
  • Very good knowledge of German, PC user knowledge
  • Team player with strong safety and quality awareness

Why you should come to us

  • Space for your own ideas, interesting fields of design and taking on responsibility provide a lot of pioneering spirit in the Sika
  • Uncomplicated managers who trust and a family atmosphere with a friendly tone make working at Sika special – many of us call it the Sika spirit.

Do you feel addressed? Then we look forward to your complete application dossier (CV, certificates, diplomas, etc.).

About Sika Sika is a specialty chemical company, global leader in the development and production of systems and products for gluing, sealing, steaming, reinforcing and protecting in construction and industry. Sika has a global presence with subsidiaries in 103 countries, produces in over 400 factories, develops innovative technologies for customers around the world and thus contributes significantly to the sustainable transformation in construction and transport. The more than 33,000 employees generated a turnover of CHF 11.20 billion in 2025.
