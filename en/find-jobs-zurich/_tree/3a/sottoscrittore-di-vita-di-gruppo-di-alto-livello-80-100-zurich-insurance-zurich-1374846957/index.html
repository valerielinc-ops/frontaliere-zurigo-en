Full description

Are you experienced in pricing / underwriting Group Life, Disability, Accident or Health business? Perhaps your background is in Group Insurance, Employee Benefits or Reinsurance Pricing, and you are used to assessing employer, affinity, association or other grouped insurance arrangements, including their claims experience, pricing assumptions, benefit structures, terms, etc.?. (This is a scheme and portfolio underwriting role, not an individual medical underwriting position.)

In this role, you will join Zurich’s global Group Underwriting team, working with underwriting and pricing teams across multiple markets. A large part of the job is hands-on: reviewing complex scheme referrals, analyzing claims experience and exposure data, challenging pricing assumptions, and helping local teams develop practical solutions. The business is varied and global in nature. Cases may be large or technically complex and involve non-standard benefits, limited data, unusual guarantees or market-specific practices. Guided by results orientation and technical excellence, you will bring independent judgement and challenge while working with local teams to help our businesses grow profitably.

What you will do

  • Provide expert advice on complex Group Insurance referrals from markets across Zurich’s global portfolio, assessing pricing, claims experience, exposure data, benefit design, terms, guarantees and reinsurance considerations.
  • Apply scheme underwriting principles across Life, Disability, Accident and Health products, taking account of local market practices and the quality of the available data
  • Work with local teams to challenge proposals where needed and develop practical solutions non-standard business opportunities.
  • Support the development and maintenance of Group underwriting philosophies, authorities, standards and technical guidance
  • Contribute to Technical Underwriting Reviews and other assurance activity, identifying weaknesses and agreeing practical improvements
  • Support selected product, proposition and pricing initiatives, share knowledge and help strengthen underwriting capability across local teams.

What you bring

  • Solid experience (8+ years) in group pricing and/or scheme underwriting within life, disability and health insurance or reinsurance
  • Actuarial training is an advantage
  • Broad exposure to different markets, products and propositions, with the ability to adapt to different cultures and business environments.
  • Deep understanding of group protection products and scheme underwriting principles, including the ability to make confident decisions in imperfect or challenging situations
  • Strong communication and influencing skills, able to explain technical decisions clearly, challenge constructively and work with local teams to find practical solutions.
  • Fluency in English; additional languages are an advantage
