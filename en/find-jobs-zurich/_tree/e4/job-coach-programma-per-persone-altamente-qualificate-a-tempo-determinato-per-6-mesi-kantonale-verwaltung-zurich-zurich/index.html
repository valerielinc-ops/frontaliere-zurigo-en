Canton of Zurich Economic Directorate Office for Labour Job Coach, program for highly qualified (temporary to 6 months) 60% How do we accompany people on the way to the new job? What support does someone need who wants to return to work after a longer break or who dares to take the first step into self-employment? What can we do to help companies and talents come together? In the area of consulting and placement (B&V), we move people and support the labour market. We operate the Regional Employment Centres (RAV) in the Canton of Zurich and support job seekers with personal advice, targeted measures and a strong network, from orientation to a new job. For the Department of Labour Market Measures (AMM) in the Office of Labour (AFA), we are looking for an experienced person for the Temporary Employment for Highly Qualified Persons (PvB HQ) program for January 2027. Your tasks:

  • Coaching of highly qualified job seekers according to our coaching concept (at 60% about 8-10 meetings per week)
  • Promoting the application competence of job seekers in demanding application situations
  • Moderation of effective group coaching
  • Placement of job seekers in suitable project assignments in exchange with assignment companies
  • Professional case management and documentation of coaching processes, incl. Monitoring the achievement of the agreed targets
  • Close cooperation with the personnel consultants of the Regional Employment Services (RAV)
  • Participation in team and department meetings as well as internal development vessels Your profile:
  • Higher vocational degree (FH, HF) and further training in the field of coaching/advice at least at DAS level or in-company mentoring
  • Proven, several years of professional experience as coach of individuals (employed or self-employed)
  • Knowledge and understanding of labour market development
  • Winning, communication-strong and result-oriented personality
  • Seniority in performance
  • Strong customer and service orientation
  • Impeccable German, very good knowledge of English
  • Experience in Out-/Newplacement and Recruitment- Leadership experience desirable
  • Experience in adult education, SVEB1 certification beneficial We offer:
  • Versatile and demanding work with high personal responsibility
  • Careful and sound training
  • Contribution to catering costs (lunch check)
  • Well-developed social benefits according to cantonal personnel law Application Do you appreciate a lively environment with stable and fair framework conditions? Then we should definitely get to know each other – Thomas Artho, Executive Director, thomas.artho@vd.zh.ch, will be happy to provide you with further information. Our staff management is looking forward to your application. You can find out more about us and our employees at www.zh.ch/afa .

Pensum: 60%
