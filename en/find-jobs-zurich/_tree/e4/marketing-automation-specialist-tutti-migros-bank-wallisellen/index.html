Full description

For our sales management team, we are looking for temporary reinforcement in the area of marketing automation. You have experience in BSI CX or a comparable platform and have already successfully implemented numerous campaigns? You are familiar with working with different stakeholders – and are you keen to inspire customers with cleverly designed flows?

  • Safe in stakeholder management and interdisciplinary cooperation

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
