Full description

With the introduction of the day school in August 26, our care offer and the number of children to be cared for have grown - that's why we are expanding our team and creating additional jobs. For our support, we are looking for a committed and thinking Hortleitung at the primary level. They make an assignment of 80% five days a week and are entitled to at least eight weeks of holiday during the school-free period.

Tasks

  • You will take over the professional management of a cluster team and appreciate the close cooperation with the teaching team.
  • They promote and support the children in their development and in their social skills and accompany them authentically, understandingly and appreciatively.
  • Constructive and appreciative collaboration in the team, at school level and with parenting.
  • Administrative and organizational tasks are routine and efficient.
  • Interested in participating in educational working groups.

Profile

  • degree as a social pedagogue* in FH or HFS; or Social worker FH or HFS (for applications with a foreign diploma or professional card, a Swiss diploma recognition is mandatory)
  • Ability to cooperate constructively in the care team as well as in interdisciplinary cooperation in the school team
  • You are a motivated, open and solution-oriented personality with a positive human image
  • Great flexibility as well as strong team and communication skills
  • Professional experience working with children

We offer

  • A responsible, varied and meaningful activity in a lively and appreciative environment.
  • Competent and supportive support by the management support as well as a dedicated and experienced team.
  • A progressive salary and attractive social benefits according to the guidelines of the school and sports department of the city of Zurich.

About us

The Zurich-Letzi school district comprises Stadtkreis 9 with the districts Albisrieden, Altstetten and Grünau. In the 16 schools of our rapidly growing school circle, more than 5,000 primary school students are taught and supervised. More than 1,200 employees work for them in our schools in the areas of teaching, support, home service and technology as well as administration. They all work daily for the interests of the students and a smooth school day.
