Full description

Lateral entry as a shunting specialist: in freight transport 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Lateral entry as a shunting specialist: in freight transport Dietikon, immediately or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. At SBB Cargo you have your finger on the pulse of the Swiss economy. SBB Cargo transports 170,000 tons every day, reducing the burden on the roads by 15,000 truck trips per day and reducing the burden on the environment by 460,000 tons of CO2 every year. An innovative working environment awaits you in which you and your teammates will move SBB Cargo forward. You will also receive varied and paid training in the marshalling yard. There are also various further training opportunities available to you, such as training to become a shunting engine driver. Attractive working conditions are important to us, even with irregular working hours. We pay additional wages for night and weekend work

  • for example, 16 francs per hour for Sunday work. For night shifts you will also receive a time allowance of between 10 and 30%. This means that you have more free time than employees without night and weekend shifts. You can move that.
  • As a shunting specialist, you will help ensure that our customers are served reliably and on time by preparing freight trains for their journey.
  • You take over the cars, carry out safety checks and prepare the train for the next journey through complex shunting movements.
  • You record the necessary data for train routing using personal, modern IT devices (e.g. tablet).
  • By delivering and dropping off the cars at the customer's site in a timely manner, you have direct customer contact on a daily basis. You receive their feedback and, if necessary, forward it to the responsible office. You can find out more about the job description here. This is what you bring with you.
  • You are characterized by attention, concentration and physical resilience so that you can successfully carry out this demanding job in 24-hour shift work. You also enjoy working outdoors in all weathers.
  • Since all instructions and documentation are electronic, you will quickly find your way around using digital tools.
  • You have successfully completed basic training (at least EFZ or an equivalent qualification) and ideally have professional experience in crafts, logistics or a comparable area.
  • You have a good command of German orally and in writing (at least B2).
  • A perfect color sense (no red-green weakness) is a prerequisite for this function, as is a clear criminal and debt collection register.
