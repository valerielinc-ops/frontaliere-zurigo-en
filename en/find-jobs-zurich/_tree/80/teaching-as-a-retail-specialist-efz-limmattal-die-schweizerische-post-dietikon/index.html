Retail Specialist Apprenticeship (EFZ) — Die Schweizerische Post

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Are you enthusiastic about daily contact with people and do you love advising and selling? Then an apprenticeship in retail is exactly right for you! During the three-year basic training, you will acquire the skills and knowledge you need for a successful start in the diverse world of retail at Post. Your training position is located in Zurich or Glattbrugg.

Information about the application process: For this position, in addition to the application dossier, a brief self-assessment of personality and working style is also collected. After submitting your application, we first check whether the basic requirements for the position are met and all necessary information has been provided. If these requirements are met, you will receive a link to the self-assessment by email. The dossier and your self-assessment will be considered jointly for the evaluation.

Your training

  • As the face of Post, you are in daily contact with our customers in the branch, advising them at the counter and in the sales area on the full range of physical and digital products.
  • Accepting letters and parcels, making deposits and withdrawals, selling products and services, or providing needs-based advice – your tasks are varied and diverse.
  • Post is increasingly positioning itself as a service center for partner companies such as insurance companies, banks and health insurance funds – here you have the opportunity to acquire additional industry knowledge.
  • The range of products offered in the branches includes additional products and services relating to topics such as housing, gifting and experiences – you are right in the middle of things when it comes to supporting national and regional campaigns and creating diverse shopping experiences for customers.
  • You will have the opportunity to acquire and deepen your professional competencies in a team and at various branches.
  • In inter-company and internal classes, you connect theory with practical experience.

What you bring

We are looking for young people with enthusiasm, enjoyment and motivation who want to make a difference and are enthusiastic about new things. You should meet the following requirements:

  • You have successfully completed the intermediate level of compulsory school with good results.
  • You have an aptitude for numbers and understand how to handle money.
  • You enjoy interacting with people and can express yourself appropriately in speech and writing, depending on the situation.
  • Your good organizational skills and sense of order set you apart.
  • You are open, reliable, and discreet.
  • You are happy to share your professional knowledge.
