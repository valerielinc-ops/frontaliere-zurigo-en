Full description

Create a better #TomorrowWithUs Want to help shape the future of the railway world together with us? At Siemens Mobility, we are the leading provider of mobility, constantly expanding our innovative portfolio, which includes rail vehicles, rail automation, electrification solutions, complete rail systems and accompanying services. With around 800 specialists in Switzerland and 39 600 employees worldwide, we shape mobility with passion and are one step ahead of our time. For trains to travel reliably. You plan and control the maintenance of our rail vehicles and coordinate the necessary measures with the specialist departments involved. With your technical understanding, organizational talent and eye for deadlines and priorities, you ensure that our vehicles are maintained safely, available and on time. Your responsibilities

  • You plan and coordinate the corrective and preventive maintenance of railway vehicles according to ECM III.
  • You analyze fault messages, prioritize missions and initiate the necessary measures.
  • You coordinate the availability of vehicles, materials, personnel and workshop capacities.
  • You check release documents and ensure the proper restart of the vehicles.
  • You decide about vehicle suspensions or usage restrictions in case of technical defects.
  • You maintain the maintenance documentation in the relevant systems and ensure seamless tracking.
  • You work closely with customers, service teams, workshops and the parent company and regularly take on pikett and occasionally shift services. Your talents and experiences
  • Completed basic technical training, ideally with further training in electrical engineering, mechanical engineering or railway engineering.
  • Experience in preventive and corrective maintenance of railway vehicles desirable
  • Strong analysis and problem-solving skills as well as a structured working method.
  • High customer and service orientation coupled with strong communication skills.
  • Independent, resilient and initiative personality with pleasure in coordination and teamwork.
  • Good MS Office knowledge as well as experience with technical maintenance or ERP systems is an advantage.
  • Negotiable German and English skills
  • French and/or Italian are a plus. You benefit from
  • an open and flexible working culture at one of the leading companies in the field of mobility in Switzerland
  • a 40-hour week on a 100% fee
  • at least 5 weeks of holidays
  • a workplace at the pulse of railway technology
  • Various employee benefits and further education opportunities
  • a hybrid working model: balanced work-life balance and more autonomy Our team and location You want information about our culture, our values, diversity and other important topics?
