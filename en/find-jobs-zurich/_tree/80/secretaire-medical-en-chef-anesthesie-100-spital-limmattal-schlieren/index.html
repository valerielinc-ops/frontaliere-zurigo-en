1 January 2027 In our Institute of Anesthesia & Intensive Care Medicine we look after patients who are being operated on in the Hospital Limmattal, are seriously ill, have suffered an accident or need intensive care. The Institute of Anesthesia and Intensive Care Medicine performs more than 8,800 sedations, general and regional anesthesias every year. In the interdisciplinary intensive care unit, we look after around 1,000 patients per year. The treatment of pain patients and the emergency medical service is also part of our range of services.
