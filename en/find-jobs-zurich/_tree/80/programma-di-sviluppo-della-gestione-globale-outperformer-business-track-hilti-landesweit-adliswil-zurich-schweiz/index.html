Full description

BUILT FOR IMPACT. Designed for Global Leaders.

This program is designed to prepare you for a successful international leadership career. In this dynamic two-year program, you are used in the middle of our customer-oriented business model and take full responsibility from day one.

During the program, you will work with global colleagues, get in touch with senior management and be accompanied by a permanent mentor. You participate in global summits, go through central stations and take on challenging projects with high visibility. This program is more than just work: it is a transformative journey full of learning, growth and valuable experiences that will shape your perspective as a young professional.

Your profile

  • Master’s or MBA degree in business administration, finance or an equivalent field; the programme is aimed at graduates of the years 2024 to 2026
  • fluency in English and German
  • Other languages are beneficial
  • At least 6 months of international experience through studies, internships or initial professional experience
  • Between 6 and 30 months of initial professional experience, for example through internships, scholarships or a first permanent job
  • A global mindset and international mobility – You look forward to diverse cultural experiences and are ready to work and live abroad during your career
  • Enjoy learning, growing and taking on new challenges in a dynamic environment
  • Strong interpersonal and communication skills as well as the ability to work together across teams
  • Initiative, adaptability and a creative, results-oriented approach to solve problems and drive improvement
  • Car driving licence

Imprint

The formal process includes a recorded video interview followed by an online interview with our Talent Acquisition Business Partner. The last step before the Assessment Center on October 28 and 29 in Kaufering, Germany, is a business case with an interview by an experienced business executive. Please attach a letter of motivation to your application and your current grade mirror.

Application deadline: 25 September
