Speculative Application - Wealth Management Assistant Zurich (80-100%) Interested in joining our Wealth Management Team in Zurich, but don't see a suitable role currently available? We're always interested in connecting with talented and motivated Wealth Management Assistants for future opportunities in our Zurich office.

Main responsibilities

  • Contribute to the team success by assisting the relationship managers in opening new relationships and serving clients, in compliance with the bank's internal regulations and guidelines
  • Prepare the necessary documentation for client meetings and business trips
  • Handle and monitor stock exchange orders, forex transactions, fiduciary relationships, loans, payment orders and credit card applications
  • Cash Management
  • Assist in AML clarifications and prepare clients KYC and related updates
  • Provide administrative support to the team
  • Direct interactions with clients by phone, e-mail, and occasionally in meetings
  • Prepare and monitor account openings, closing mutations
  • Maintaining records in compliance with internal and external regulations. For example: Portfolio reviews, AML, KYC, etc.
  • Various administrative tasks:
  • Preparing presentations
  • Travel arrangements
  • Support clients with various requests - banking and non-banking

Requirements

  • Degree/Diploma in a related field or further education
  • Banking Apprenticeship, CYP, Bachelor, Master, CWMA, or similar
  • Previous experience as a Relationship Manager Assistant/Wealth Management Assistant in banking is required
  • Excellent command of English is mandatory (C1) / any other languages are a strong asset
  • Motivated and hardworking personality, highly service- and client-oriented, strong interpersonal and communication skills
  • Good priority setting, ability to cope with pressure and high volumes

When you apply, please include

  • Your preferred workload (80-100%)
  • Your earliest possible start date
  • Your salary expectation for a 100% position

What happens next? We'll review your application and keep your profile in our talent pool for future Wealth Management Assistant opportunities in Zurich. We'll only contact you if a suitable Wealth Management Assistant position becomes available that matches your experience and profile. Your application will be handled confidentially in accordance with our privacy notice.
