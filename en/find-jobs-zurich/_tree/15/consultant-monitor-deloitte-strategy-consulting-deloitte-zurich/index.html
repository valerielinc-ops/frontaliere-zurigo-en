Full description

Location : Zurich Work Pattern Percentage : 80–100% Start date : ASAP About the Role Help clients make strategic decisions about growth, competition and business transformation as a Consultant with Monitor Deloitte in Zurich. You will structure business problems, analyse evidence and turn insights into actionable recommendations with your project team. Work across industries including life sciences and healthcare, financial services, industrial goods, public sector & defence and consumer, while developing your consulting skills through client work, feedback and mentoring. How you can make an impact

  • Help clients make informed strategic decisions through structured problem solving and critical thinking.
  • Take ownership of defined project analyses, turning market and business data into strategic insights.
  • Develop recommendations on topics such as drug launches, organic and inorganic growth, digital transformation and sustainable product portfolio transformations.
  • Use digital and AI tools appropriately to support research and analysis, critically validating their outputs.
  • Collaborate with colleagues and clients to communicate findings and translate insights into practical next steps. What you bring to succeed
  • You approach complex questions with curiosity, structured problem solving and attention to detail.
  • You communicate clearly, collaborate well and adapt to feedback and unfamiliar challenges.
  • You hold a Master’s degree in business, science, engineering or a related discipline.
  • You bring initial experience through internships or professional roles in strategy consulting or a relevant industry, with evidence of analytical work.
  • You are fluent in English, comfortable with digital tools and able to use AI critically and responsibly; you are willing to travel occasionally. What sets you apart
  • German language skills would help you contribute to German-speaking client projects.
  • Experience with programming or data tools such as Python, R, SQL or C#/C++ would strengthen your analytical toolkit.
  • Exposure to life sciences and healthcare, financial services, industrial goods, public sector & defence or consumer markets would add useful industry context. Your team You will join the Monitor Deloitte team in Zurich, working with colleagues from different backgrounds and across Deloitte’s international network. We combine strategy expertise with access to specialists across Deloitte to help clients turn recommendations into action. You can expect collaborative project work, feedback from experienced colleagues and opportunities to learn through mentoring and training. EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
