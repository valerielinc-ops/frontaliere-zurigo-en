The Uitikon Action Center (MZU) is the socio-pedagogical-therapeutic competence center of prison and reintegration (JuWe) for the execution of penalties and measures of young offenders aged 16 to 25. For the central services of the MZU we are looking for a motivated person for a temporary period of 6 months with the prospect of permanent employment. You do that with us.

The Uitikon Action Center (MZU) is one of eight main departments of JuWe and has 58 places for male adolescents and young adults aged 16 to 29. Our work focuses on risk-oriented crime prevention and the development of young people’s personalities. In vocational training, in psychotherapies and in socio-educational housing groups, we rely on clear structures and enforcement openings that are based on risks. As employees of the Uitikon Action Centre, we accompany young people, promote their independent lifestyle and reduce the likelihood of relapse. With around 1400 employees, Prison and Reintegration (JuWe) is the largest office within the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs and is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as for the execution of various types of detention. Our employees from around 40 professions support and promote the reintegration of delinquents and work effectively and progressively for a safe and just society.
