Full description

Put to the Point

With your expertise, as a link between the Army Logistics Center and the military and civilian service providers regarding army vehicles and equipment, consumables, food and personal equipment, you contribute to the smooth flow of materials throughout Switzerland.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20642
  • Place of work: Überlandstrasse 17, 8340 Hinwil

You can make this contribution

  • Responsible for the proper conduct of the work and assigned military personnel and/or assigned civilian personnel in the surrender and take-back of army vehicles and equipment of the force, in compliance with the specified dates
  • The vehicles and the associated material are properly removed, stored and relocated and provided according to the existing regulations and instructions
  • Checking the condition and functionality of the stored vehicles and responsible for returning defective components for repair and inspection during maintenance
  • Instruct and train troops and specialists on the treatment and maintenance of materials in their area of responsibility in the context of the versions of Army systems and equipment
  • Organize warehouse management, material shifts and deployments in cooperation with the transport process

This makes you unique

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as a logistician, or equivalent training
  • Good MS Office and SAP user knowledge for use in warehouse management, inventory management and re-orders
  • Mandatory driving licence of categories B, C, E, F, CE and A beneficial
  • Frequent and conscientious work, as well as organizational skills to support a smooth operation
  • Responsible handling of SAP materials management and pronounced suitability for continuous customer contact and consulting
  • Further training opportunities internally/externally E.g. for a Eidg. Diploma is available

Your commitment to security and freedom The logistics base of the Swiss Army (LBA) is there for the force. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss army and manages the materials, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss army with its approximately 3'500 employees and 12,000 militia members. Whether in the workshop or in the office, inside or outside: The LBA offers a wide range of workplaces throughout Switzerland. We have something for everyone.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
