Full description

PROFILE

  • Currently pursuing a degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Railway Engineering, or a comparable technical field.
  • Basic understanding of electrical systems, circuits, and wiring, with a strong interest in hardware integration and hands-on laboratory work.
  • Initial experience in programming (preferably Python) and familiarity with Windows and/or Linux environments.
  • Basic knowledge of networking technologies (TCP/IP, switches, routers) and confident use of Microsoft Office applications.
  • Proactive and eager-to-learn mindset; experience with Git, DevOps tools, laboratory measurements, testing activities, railway systems, or industrial automation is considered an advantage.

TASKS

  • Support the setup, configuration, maintenance, and troubleshooting of railway signalling test laboratories, including electrical, electronic, and network components.
  • Assist in the preparation, commissioning, and validation of physical and virtual test environments for system, integration, and validation testing.
  • Support test execution, result analysis, and investigation activities, contributing to the continuous improvement of testing processes and tools.
  • Develop and maintain Python-based automation scripts and support engineering activities through data analysis, reporting, and internal tool development.
  • Create and maintain technical documentation, support laboratory organization, and contribute to process improvement and day-to-day engineering operations.
