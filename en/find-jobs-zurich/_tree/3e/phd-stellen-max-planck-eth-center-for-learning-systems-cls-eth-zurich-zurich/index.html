PhD Positions - Max Planck ETH Center for Learning Systems (CLS)

100%, Zurich, fixed-term

In our highly competitive CLS doctoral training program, fellows are co-supervised by one advisor from ETH Zurich and one from the MPI for Intelligent Systems in Stuttgart / Tübingen, the MPI for Informatics in Saarbrücken or the ELLIS Institute Tübingen. Each CLS fellow has a primary location (chosen based on interests and match) and conducts a mandatory 12-month exchange at the other location. All CLS Ph.D. fellows register as graduate students at ETH Zurich and, upon successful completion of their Ph.D. project, will be granted a doctoral degree by ETH. Fellowships are fully funded. The program language is English. Faculty participating in this call (only available as co-advisor for the one-year exchange): Zurich: Andreas Krause, April Wang, Benjamin Grewe, Celestine Mendler-Dünner, Christian Holz, Daniel Razansky, Florian Dörfler, Gunnar Rätsch, Julia Vogt, Konrad Schindler, Marc Pollefeys, Menna El-Assady, Mrinmaya Sachan, Niao He, Quentin Boehler, Robert Katzschmann, Siddhartha Mishra, Siyu Tang, Stelian Coros. We offer

PhD students profit from joint supervision by ETH Professors and MPI Directors, Group Leaders or PIs at the ELLIS Institute Tübingen, ultimately obtaining their doctoral degree from ETH Zurich. With these unique opportunities, CLS offers young academics in the field of learning systems the chance of outstanding collaborations and exchange early in their careers.

The application deadline for all parts of the application is midnight (23:59 CET) on November 2, 2026. Long-listed applicants will be asked to submit a 2-5 minute research video. Short-listed applicants will be invited to the CLS2027 Selection Symposium in Tübingen on March 1-2, 2027.
