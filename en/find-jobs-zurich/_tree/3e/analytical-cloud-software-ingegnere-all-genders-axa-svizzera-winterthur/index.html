Full description

80-100%, Winterthur / Smart Working

Shape your future – together with us!

As a Data Driven Company, AXA Switzerland wants to further promote the transformation process. The Google Cloud as our analytical platform is a central component for the development of analytical use cases. We are looking for a talented software engineer to help our existing Analytics Cloud team continuously improve the platform. The goal is to provide data scientists and developers with a high-quality analytics environment.

Support the Analytics Cloud team in developing new and existing features and stabilizing the platform, ensuring frameworks and documentation are created and advising the development teams in their work on the platform.

In the field of data, technology and innovation, around 600 software and system developers work in Switzerland: in-house and develop the technology of tomorrow. We also collaborate in global teams and networks. We shape and integrate the latest tools, organize hackathons and our agile product teams run self-organized and are self-responsible from A to Z.

This is what awaits you

  • You have a real interest in all tasks in our team to develop creative and innovative solutions together
  • You develop the platform in the sense of DevOps and take care of a continuous improvement
  • You will continuously design, develop and improve our cloud components, services and self-services for use cases and core infrastructure
  • With a healthy eye measure for software quality and in compliance with our security guidelines and CI/CD driven deployment, you keep the quality high
  • You see “technical debt” as a positive challenge
  • You provide support and debugging for developers

You bring that with you

  • You have a university degree (ETH, Uni, FH) in Computer Science, Data Science, Business Informatics or a comparable education
  • You have experience and expertise in DevOps technologies such as Git, GitHub Actions and Docker, as well as creating CI/CD pipelines
  • You have excellent programming skills in Python and are familiar with modern software development practices.
  • You have excellent problem-solving skills, an attention to detail and an urge to automate everything and provide technically good solutions
  • You can work independently on topics and have excellent communication and team skills
  • Very good knowledge of German and English

The following points would also inspire us

  • A data-friendly mindset for our analytics environment
  • Familiarity with the Google Cloud Platform (GCP)
  • Experience with Go, PyCharm, Visual Studio Code, JIRA, Confluence, Terraform, Bash
  • Interest and willingness to learn about IT infrastructure topics such as security and network
  • You are familiar with the use of AI tools (Claude Code, Codex, ...) when performing various tasks

If you have a passion for cloud, DevOps themes and software development and want to play a crucial role in the further development of a modern and future-oriented analytical cloud platform that has already reached an impressive dimension and is constantly growing, then we look forward to your application.
