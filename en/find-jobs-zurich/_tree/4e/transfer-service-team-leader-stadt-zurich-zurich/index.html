Full description

In the area of medical care, we are looking for a committed leader for our department, takes responsibility, moves people and actively shapes everyday operations. For this we fill on 01. April 2027 or by arrangement a vacant position as team leader in transfer service.

  • You lead - 11 employees in the transfer service and are close to the team
  • You give orientation, create commitment and develop people further
  • You make decisions, stand up for them and do not avoid conflicts
  • You ensure that standards are not only on paper, but are lived.
  • You stay in use in the secondary sector yourself, ensure smooth service operations and get involved in projects and the further development of SRZ
  • You take responsibility for recruiting new employees
  • Training as a Dipl. Paramedic HF or transport paramedic FA
  • Several years of professional experience in a Swiss rescue service and proven professional training
  • Leadership experience or leadership training
  • Backbone, empathy and the ability to make clear decisions
  • High psychological, physical and social resilience
  • Driving licence Cat. B121 / C1 / D1 and good repute
  • A demanding leadership task with creative scope
  • Good development and further training opportunities in a 24-hour operation
  • A dedicated team where collaboration, openness and mutual support count
  • Modern equipment and service clothing
  • Contemporary working and employment conditions at a modern workplace in Zurich, you benefit from a mobility contribution and other benefits

Protection & Rescue Zurich (SRZ), as a service department of the Department of Security, unites the professional and militia fire brigades, the medical department and the operational control center (ELZ) for the emergency calls 144 and 118. In addition, civil protection, fire police and a training center with an affiliated Higher School for Rescue Occupations (HFRB) belong to the largest civil rescue organization in Switzerland. Intervention and prevention units protect and save people, animals, property and the environment around the clock in the city of Zurich, at Zurich Airport and in the agglomeration.
