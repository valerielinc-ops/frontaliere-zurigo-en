Full description

For the care team of the eye clinic at the site Triemli we are looking for 1. May 2027 or by arrangement a dedicated personality.

Tasks

  • You will supervise and accompany our inpatient and outpatient patients competently and sensitively.
  • You support with regular and emergency entries and ensure a smooth process.
  • You carry out medically prescribed diagnostic and therapeutic measures independently and professionally.
  • You inform and advise patients in an understandable and addressable manner.
  • If necessary, you will take over i.v. injections with contrast agents in the diagnostics department.
  • You bring flexibility for assignments from Monday to Saturday and support with holiday representatives as well as occasional Saturday and holiday services.

Profile

  • Diploma as a nurse HF and corresponding professional experience
  • Resilience, even in hectic situations
  • Patience and sensitivity
  • Self-employed, precise and efficient working methods
  • Flexibility in employment (holiday representations)
  • Motivated personality that can integrate into a small team
