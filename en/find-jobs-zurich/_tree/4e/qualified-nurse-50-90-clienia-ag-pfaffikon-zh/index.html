Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With more than 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric offer of Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in ambulatory, day-clinical and inpatient settings. The Group employs around 1800 people and achieved a turnover of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For our station B3 with treatment focus trauma follow-up disorders we are looking for an immediate or immediate

The station specializes in the treatment of people with trauma follow-up disorders. The focus is on support for stabilisation and the promotion and use of individual resources. This is based on an evidence-based treatment concept with a focus on DBT trauma. This is complemented by occupational and movement therapy as well as by complementary medical interventions.

Their tasks Professional, resource- and recovery-oriented care and the support of our patients in close coordination with the interdisciplinary team as well as active participation in the management of therapeutic groups.

Your profile For this exciting activity, You have a degree of care at HF/FH level and prefer to already have professional experience in the field of psychiatric care.
