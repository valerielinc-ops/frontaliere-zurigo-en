Full description

Canton of Zurich Health Directorate Veterinary Office Administrative Management 100% The Veterinary Office Canton of Zurich is committed to animal welfare and animal health. It helps to ensure animal welfare and the quality of animal food. In order to strengthen our team "Animal Health and Animal Welfare" we are looking for 1. March 2027 or by agreement a team-capable and communicative personality as administrative clerk / administrative clerk (100%). You perform your assignments in the front desk, where you are responsible for the reception and triage of telephone calls and emails with your teammates. Furthermore, according to the operational plan, you take over the inbox as well as the -shipping or operate the counter. You collect incoming cases electronically and assign them to the relevant specialist areas. To a significant extent, you support the experts in enforcement tasks, approval procedures and defects in the various specialist areas and take over the processing of individual orders. Profile You bring a commercial education, several years of professional practice and a quick apprehension for the wide range of tasks. You are interested in questions about animal welfare and animal health. You have a quick comprehension, are flexible and resilient. You complete your tasks independently, accurately and quickly. You are digitally savvy, quickly get involved in new programs and cleverly use digital possibilities for your daily work. Offer

  • Versatile and challenging tasks
  • Motivated and helpful teammates
  • Training and development opportunities
  • Social benefits and employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines
  • Workplace close to Zurich Central Station Application Do you feel addressed? Then we look forward to your online application until the 6th. December 2026. Further information about the veterinary office can be found at www.zh.ch/veta. For further information, Esther Bressa is at your disposal on 043 259 41 41. She is the head of the animal health and animal welfare office and perhaps your future boss.
