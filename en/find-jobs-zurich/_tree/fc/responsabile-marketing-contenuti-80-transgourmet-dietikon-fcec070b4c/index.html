Full description

Casa del Vino SA stands for the highest consulting and assortment expertise in the fascinating world of selected wines. High-calibre exclusivities and well-known brands from Spain, France, Italy and all over the world distinguish our range of quality products. With the Vinattieri SA winery, which belongs to the group, we manage our own production plant of the highest quality in Ticino, which is leading in Switzerland. Our long-term personal relationships with producers, our own logistics, a webshop and Switzerland-wide distribution allow us to act quickly and flexibly on the market. Our customers include restaurants, hotels, resellers and private individuals. The company belongs to Transgourmet Schweiz AG and thus to the international Transgourmet Group.

Tasks

  • You plan, create and publish content for online and offline channels such as website, newsletter, brochures, magazines and presentations.
  • The support of our social media channels is in your hands – from content planning to community management and monitoring.
  • You ensure that our brand and communication language remains consistent across all platforms.
  • Texts, pictures and videos are prepared according to the target group.
  • In close cooperation with internal departments such as marketing and sales as well as external partners such as agencies and service providers, you coordinate various communication measures.
  • You analyze content and social media activities, create reports for success monitoring and derive optimization measures from them, which you then implement.
  • In first-level support, you process customer inquiries via digital channels such as e-mail, social media and chat and forward requests to the responsible authorities if necessary.
  • Also in the case of webshop disruptions you support internally in first-level support.
  • Maintaining and updating our website content as well as supporting on-page SEO optimization are also part of your tasks.
  • In addition, you take on organizational and administrative tasks around the supervised communication channels.
  • At our events you actively participate.

Requirements

  • Completed training in marketing or communication
  • Experience in content management
  • Experience in supporting social media channels
  • In-depth knowledge in online marketing, especially in writing SEO-optimized texts
  • Stylistic writing in German
  • Enjoyment in the creation of target group-oriented content
  • Structured, reliable and independent working method
  • Strong communication and team skills
  • IT affinity and organizational skill
  • Interest in digital channels and customer interaction
  • Passion for the wine world

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
