Full description

The GZO Spital Wetzikon is an innovative acute care hospital in the Zurich Oberland with 150 beds and around 900 employees. As a company with high customer orientation and appreciative communication, we are committed to our patients every day. The GZO is in a transformation phase. The estate deferral provides the necessary time for the development of a comprehensive restructuring plan. Operation continues normally. Do you want to support us in this extraordinary situation and actively contribute? To complement our team, we are looking for a dedicated MPA.

These are your tasks

  • Independent organisation of emergency practice and ensuring smooth operation
  • Triagulation of all incoming calls via the emergency number
  • Comprehensive care of patients in emergency practice
  • Preparing and helping with wound care and minor septic and aseptic local interventions
  • Preparation, cooperation and follow-up of special diagnostic and therapeutic measures such as various laboratory tests, monitoring, ECG writing etc.
  • Independent performance of laboratory analyses
  • Creation of all common orthoses and dressings
  • Ensuring the sending of patient reports
  • Responsibility for correct performance documentation
  • Completion of general administrative work
  • Economical and correct handling of equipment and materials, medicines, material order The family physician emergency practice is open at the following times: Monday to Friday, 18.00 – 22.00 (MPA 16.30 – 22.30) Sa/So/holiday, 09.30 – 22.00 (in shift operation) You bring that with you
  • You hold the diploma as a medical assistant and bring several years of professional experience
  • Coordination of consultation hours with patients You have a lot of joy
  • You understand how to react agilely to new situations
  • You perform the assigned tasks well and ensure the high quality
  • You like to work on the patient and keep an overview even in hectic situations
  • You are an independent, reliable and open personality, have good MS Office user knowledge
  • You have the German language in style confident
  • Very high flexibility in operational planning and short-term requests for service coverage We offer a very interesting and versatile field of activity in an advanced, dynamic and multi-professional working environment, which is characterized by high quality, respect and appreciation. With us you can develop further and benefit from internal and external training opportunities.
