Full description

24.11.2026 Zurich

Become part of our team!

The mandate of precious metals trading extends from guaranteeing access to the international financial markets to the supply of precious metals to the Raiffeisen Group. Due to a succession arrangement, we are looking for an experienced, agile, curious and forward-looking personality as a precious metals and options trader (m/f/d) to strengthen our trading team at the Zurich Airport location.

What awaits you?

  • Self-responsible management of trading positions in the precious metals sector (spot, forwards and options).
  • Pricing as well as professional support for internal and external customers.
  • Expansion and maintenance of a strong network of banks, brokers and other financial market participants.
  • Analysis of market, economic, monetary and geopolitical trends to derive trade ideas and market assessments.
  • Ensure liquidity management and control and disposition of physical precious metal holdings.
  • Implementation of data analyses as well as cooperation in cross-divisional projects and process optimizations including automation potential.

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking education with higher education in banking, finance or business.
  • Several years of experience in precious metals trading with in-depth knowledge in spot, futures and options trades.
  • In-depth understanding of global financial markets and pricing mechanisms in the precious metals market.
  • Entrepreneurial personality with a high level of initiative and a strong sense of responsibility.
  • Analytical strength and the ability to quickly detect and understand complex issues.
  • Strong communication and presentation skills in German and English
  • Other foreign languages are beneficial. (Company language is German)
