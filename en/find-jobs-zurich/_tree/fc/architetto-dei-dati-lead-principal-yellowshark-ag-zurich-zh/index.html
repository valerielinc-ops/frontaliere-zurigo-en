01 Future employer

Would you like to do more than just manage data and shape the data architecture of a market leader’s central transaction and order management platform? We are looking for a visionary, results-oriented Data Architect for one of the most successful and innovative companies in the German-speaking region.

In this key role, you combine state-of-the-art software architecture with strategic business impact. You build scalable, highly available data landscapes for critical core areas such as Financial Operations, Payment and Fraud Prevention, with a direct impact on millions of customer interactions.

02 Your role

  • Architecture & Vision: You design and take responsibility for the end-to-end data architecture of the central order management platform, ensuring its performance, scalability and resilience.
  • Strategic sparring partner: You work closely with Product Owners, Software Engineers, Data Engineers and business specialists from the finance and fraud prevention teams to translate complex business requirements into elegant data models.
  • Hands-on & mentoring: You support interdisciplinary teams in making architectural decisions, establish best practices for Data Governance and Data Modeling, and translate corporate goals into clear technical roadmaps.
  • Innovation & modernization: You continuously evaluate new technologies, modernize existing data flows (streaming & batch) and drive a no-compromise Data-Driven culture.

04 Your profile

  • Several years of architecture expertise: Extensive experience as a Data Architect, Enterprise Architect, or Lead Data Engineer in a Big Data or high-scale e-commerce environment.
  • Excellent technical domain knowledge: In-depth understanding of modern data modeling (relational, NoSQL, event-driven architectures), cloud data platforms, and enterprise integrations (e.g. event streaming via Kafka, DWH/data lakehouse).
  • Business understanding: Strong affinity for transaction data, financial data streams, and risk management systems.
  • Communication and assertiveness: Ability to clearly explain complex technical concepts to both development teams and management-level stakeholders.
  • Languages: Fluent German and very good English skills for working in an international engineering environment.

05 Things to know

  • Maximum scale: Experience the direct impact of your work on millions of transactions and real-time traffic.
  • A genuine tech culture: No bureaucracy, but flat hierarchies, a high degree of personal responsibility, and an uncompromisingly modern engineering mindset.
  • Maximum flexibility: A modern working model with a significant work-from-home component, flexible working hours, and workloads starting at 80%.
  • Top benefits and top pension provision: An above-average overall package with excellent social benefits, a professional development budget, and attractive employee discounts.
