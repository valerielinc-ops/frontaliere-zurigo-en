Full description

As a result of retirement, we are looking for a committed personality at the Ambulatorium Crossline at Badenerstrasse 435, as of 01.05.2027 or by arrangement.

  • They are responsible for dispensing the medicines in accordance with medical prescriptions and monitor the application and taking of the medicines.
  • They ensure treatment care (wound care, blood draws, etc.) and provide first aid if necessary.
  • You take responsibility for case coordination and caregiver work with our patients.
  • They support our patients in their medical self-care and promote their self-competence with regard to a healthy lifestyle.
  • They monitor the consumer rooms and the treatment rooms and ensure their proper use.
  • Motivated, flexible and resilient personality with several years of professional experience as a Dipl. Nurse HF/FH, at least 25 years old, with experience or affinity for psychiatry or somatics. Willingness to become familiar with treatment care and/or psychiatric care.
  • Experience in the field of addiction is beneficial as well as a strong understanding and sensitivity for people in social border situations.
  • Interested in developing sustainable relationships within the framework of caregiver work and case coordination with our patients. Engaged working with people who use drugs and have psychological and somatic challenges.
  • Flexibility and willingness to work in various services also in the evening and on weekends. Night work is not necessary. Knowledge of foreign languages is an advantage.

A responsible and exciting task in an interdisciplinary and committed team in a dynamic environment. A structured introduction to the task area and an attractive workplace. As an administrative unit of the City of Zurich, we stand for attractive development opportunities, very advanced employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

The Municipal Health Services (SGD) is a service department of the Department of Health and the Environment with around 250 employees, which offers a variety of health services for the city of Zurich. These include the Urban Medical Service (SAD), Zurich in Old Age (ZIA), Public Health (PH), Spitex and Management & Services.

With its focus on social medicine and addiction medicine, the City Medical Service ensures somatic and psychiatric-psychological care for vulnerable and dependency-affected people as well as asylum seekers and offers consulting and counselling services for various institutions.
