The possibility The GIS (gas-insulated switchgear) sector stands for the highest quality, safety and efficiency – and this is where your role comes in. In this role, you take on a central function in the processing and coordination of quality messages in production. You recognize problems at an early stage, analyze them in a structured manner and provide sustainable solutions. You work closely with various internal stakeholders and actively contribute to the further development of our processes.

You can do that.

  • You are the first escalation stage for production and work closely with internal stakeholders.
  • Responsible for creating and coordinating quality messages (QN) in production
  • Ensuring compliance with quality and safety standards
  • Prioritisation of quality messages (Q messages)
  • Close cooperation with internal departments such as production, quality, engineering, planning, project management and purchasing
  • You support the further development of troubleshooting processes for continuous improvement
  • Support for audits and internal reviews

You bring that with you

  • Technical training in electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, quality
  • Experience in troubleshooting, production processes or quality management
  • Analytical thinking, structured working methods and high level of problem-solving competence
  • Strong communication and decision-making skills
  • Negotiating knowledge of German and English
  • Knowledge of SAP, DPS and MES
  • Detailed product knowledge in the field of gas insulated switchgear
