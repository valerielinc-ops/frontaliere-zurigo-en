Full description

Movemi AG operates the largest fitness network in Switzerland with 126 ACTIV FITNESS and 16 fitness parks. Create the gyms of tomorrow with us. For demanding new buildings, major renovations and complex renovations, we are looking for an experienced personality who is responsible for projects from planning to handover to operations.

Luogo di lavoro: movemi AG, Verwaltungs movemi AG, Thurgauerstrasse 32, 8050 Oerlikon

Important information

Switzerland-wide project responsibility We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You will accompany new buildings, major renovations and complex conversions from planning to tendering and awarding to realisation and handover.
  • You support in the coordination of the architects, specialist planners and executing companies.
  • You monitor costs, deadlines, quality and project risks. You actively control assignments and supplements.
  • You ensure the quality requirements.
  • You work closely with expansion, operations, facility management, technology and finance.

Requirements

  • Several years of experience: experience in managing larger and complex construction projects
  • Completed degree programme (uni/ETH, FH, HF): degree programme or higher technical training in architecture, civil engineering, building technology, construction management or equivalent specialisation
  • Very good knowledge of Swiss construction practice and relevant SIA phases.
  • In-depth experience in cost, deadline, quality and supplement management.
  • Experience with technically demanding new buildings, renovations or conversions, ideally in the area of wellness, hotel industry, leisure, sports or bathroom facilities.
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, assertiveness and an independent, structured and solution-oriented way of working.
  • Willingness to travel regularly within Switzerland
  • We enjoy working with interdisciplinary teams and are interested in actively shaping the development of our gyms.
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Part of the Migros Group: Attractive special conditions at Migros Bank, premium discounts at Migros Insurance and discounts at Migros Holidays
  • Healthy work: Modernly furnished workstations with height-adjustable tables
  • Mobile-flexible working model: Flexibility and hybrid working models with home office options
  • Mobile subscription: National flat subscription from CHF 10.– / month (Swisscom /digitec connect)
  • Migros Bank: expense-free private and investment account with preferential interest
  • Employee offers: Fleet discount on numerous car brands and special conditions on car sharing offers and car rentals
  • Work climate: Helpful team members and uncomplicated handling across all hierarchical levels
  • Du-culture: We stand for a respectful coexistence at eye level

If possible, there is time to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
