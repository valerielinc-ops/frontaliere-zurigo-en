Full description

At STRABAG, around 89,000 people are making progress at more than 2,400 locations worldwide. Uniqueness and individual strengths characterize not only our projects, but also each and every one of us. Whether in construction and engineering, road and civil engineering, bridge and tunnel construction, in building materials production, project development or building management - we continue to think about construction: innovation, digitalization and sustainability are central points on our way to building tomorrow. STRABAG is pursuing a clear sustainability strategy. Equal opportunities, diversity and inclusion are just as integral parts of what makes us a company and how we work. As a service and service company within the STRABAG Group, our subsidiary STRABAG BMTI is responsible for the scheduling, rental, maintenance and procurement of all construction machinery and vehicles. Together we implement projects successfully and in partnership and grow in new tasks. Together we create great things. Let's make progress!

Head of Construction Magazine and Deputy Disposition Your contribution to us

  • Organization of the building magazine incl. Ordering and warehouse management
  • Checks on arrival and exit of goods
  • Audit, internal accounting and inventory
  • Machinery contact person for the operational units
  • Technical advice and coordination with construction sites
  • Disposition and operational planning of construction machinery and equipment

What matters to us

  • Completed technical training
  • Proven experience in the construction industry or in the disposition of construction machinery
  • Motivated and committed personality with independent, systematic working methods and technical understanding
  • Solution-oriented and reliable
  • Very good communication skills
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Good and well-founded IT knowledge (MS Office)

Our added value for you You get the opportunity to develop within the team both personally and professionally.
