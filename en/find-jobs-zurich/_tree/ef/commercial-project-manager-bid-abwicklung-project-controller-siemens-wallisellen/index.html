Full description

Create a better #TomorrowWithUs Want to help shape the future of the railway world together with us? At Siemens Mobility, we are the leading provider of mobility, constantly expanding our innovative portfolio, which includes rail vehicles, rail automation, electrification solutions, complete rail systems and accompanying services. With around 800 specialists in Switzerland and 39 600 employees worldwide, we shape mobility with passion and are one step ahead of our time. Do you want to gain deep insights into two exciting business areas of Siemens Mobility and assume commercial responsibility for demanding customer projects? In this role, you work on projects in the area of rail infrastructure and customer services and accompany them from the offer phase to the successful project completion. You combine classic commercial project management with the opportunity to question processes, introduce new ideas and actively shape the digitization and use of AI. We are looking for a curious, entrepreneurial personality who wants to take on responsibility, drive change and develop professionally and personally. If you like to think outside the box, question existing procedures and go the extra mile even in challenging situations, we look forward to getting to know you. Your new role

  • You assume commercial responsibility for customer projects in the areas of rail infrastructure and customer services and accompany them throughout the entire project life cycle.
  • Together with the Bid Manager, you develop offers, support the definition of the business model and are responsible for the commercial aspects of the bid approval process, in particular calculation, risk assessment and pricing.
  • In close cooperation with the technical project manager, you implement customer projects according to the principles of PM@Siemens and are responsible for commercial project management including forecasting, project controlling, invoicing, cash flow planning, foreign currency hedging and risk management.
  • You present offers and projects before management and are the central commercial contact for internal and external stakeholders.
  • You identify potential for optimization, drive the automation and digitalization of processes and implement improvements independently, including through the use of modern AI-based solutions. Your talents and experiences
  • You have a bachelor’s degree in business administration, industrial engineering, finance or a comparable qualification.
  • You have experience in commercial project management, project controlling, finance, bid management or in a comparable commercial function.
  • You have a strong commercial understanding and understand which factors determine the economic success of a project – from the offer phase to the project completion.
  • You work structured and self-responsible, question existing processes and convince through a solution-oriented and entrepreneurial mindset.
  • Knowledge of Siemens processes and tools (e.g. LoA, EPIQ/PALM, ProjectDAsh or ROCCIT) is an advantage.
  • CPM certification is desirable.
  • You speak fluent German and English in word and writing
  • Language skills in French or Italian are beneficial. You benefit from
  • an open and flexible working culture at one of the leading companies in the field of mobility in Switzerland
  • a 40-hour week on a 100% fee
  • at least 5 weeks of holidays
  • a workplace at the pulse of railway technology
  • Various employee benefits and further education opportunities
  • a hybrid working model: balanced work-life balance and more autonomy Our team and location You want information about our culture, our values, diversity and other important topics?
