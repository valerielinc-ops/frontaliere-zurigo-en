Full description

The Operations department is responsible for the technical production of all SSR content, as well as their technical and storage (TV, radio and multimedia). Within the SSR, the PM Digital Workplace (or Head of Digital Workplace) provides collaborators and collaborators with a modern, safe and reliable digital work environment. This includes Windows and Apple (HW), Microsoft 365 services, collaboration tools and intranets, as well as process automation and AI-based solutions.

Shipments

Within the IT portfolio, as an open and curious person, assume responsibility for strategic development and successful implementation of all Digital Workplace projects:

  • Strategy and implementation responsibilities: Define product vision and long-term roadmap, ensuring consistent and targeted implementation of complex initiatives within the Digital Workplace, from the definition of architecture and solutions to successful deployment throughout the company.
  • End-to-end life cycle: Manage the entire product life cycle actively and on the basis of data: from the in-depth evaluation and technical implementation of new solutions, through a stable and efficient exercise, to large-scale replacements and migrations, including the transition to Intune.
  • Functional leadership and orchestration: Take the functional leadership of the Product Owners, accompany the implementation teams to achieve greater autonomy and coordinate close collaboration with the management of the Operations staff, the Business Partners and the central stakeholders of all business units.
  • Financial management and suppliers: You are responsible for the product budget, manage suppliers, partners and external services in a binding manner with respect to the quality of the results and ensure the achievement of economic objectives.
  • Organizational development: Contribute decisively to the transformation towards a modern, agile and product-oriented organization, consolidating sustainable working methods in implementation.

Requirements

  • Proven Experience: You have a multiannual and proven (at least 5 years) experience in Senior Product Management or in the overall responsibility of complex IT and Digital Workplace implementations within large organizations distributed across multiple locations. Successfully conducted a migration to Intune is an important added value.
  • Strong implementation capacity and methodology: You have a solid experience in the agile management of products and portfolios (e.g. SAFe, Scrum) and have already successfully demonstrated the ability to translate strategic roadmaps into concrete, binding and sustainable solutions.
  • Solid technical and architectural understanding: You have a broad and thorough technical knowledge of the entire Digital Workplace. This includes the Modern Endpoint Management (Windows/Apple, MDM/MAM, packaging) and the Microsoft 365 ecosystem (colaboration, intranet, provisioning, automation and self-service solutions, as well as IA integrations).
  • Innovation and orientation capacity UX: Combine a strong focus on a user experience (UX) that is fluid and user-oriented with a strong ability to propose and promote innovations, actively following technological and digital trends.
  • Management and financial skills: You have a proven experience in budget management, cost control and product quality through relevant KPIs, as well as in developing solid business homes and cost-benefit analysis.
  • Leadership and communication skills: You stand out for persuasion, security in management-level interaction and strong moderation skills, which allow you to successfully balance the different interests of stakeholders.
  • Language: Excellent knowledge of a national language, complemented by good knowledge of German or French (at least level B1) and English (at least level B1/B2).

What we offer

  • Management function: A management function with its decision-making skills.
  • Key role of national relevance, with real end-to-end responsibility and a wide margin of manoeuvre in the implementation of a central product at company level.
  • Culture of modern work: Agile and product oriented working methods, lived daily in a dynamic environment in the field of media and technology.
  • Inspiring environment: Collaboration with experienced, interdisciplinary and highly motivated teams.
