Doctoral Researcher in Energy Systems Modelling, Artificial Intelligence and Digitalization

100%, Zurich, fixed-term

The Chair of Energy Systems Analysis (ESA) , within the Department of Mechanical and Process Engineering at ETH Zurich, is recruiting a highly motivated Doctoral Researcher to work broadly in the area of energy systems analysis, artificial intelligence and digitalization. The ESA Chair is headed by Prof. Dr Russell McKenna and generally focuses on the energy system analysis of low-carbon energy systems and technologies, by working at the intersection of engineering and economics to develop and apply energy system models to diverse energy systems and research questions. The Chair holder’s joint appointment as the Head of the Laboratory of Energy Systems Analysis at PSI means a strong collaboration with this group and regular presence is also required.

Project background

This position is linked to the industry-financed project "SyDEF - Systemic Digitalization of Energy Futures” , led and managed by Dr. Fabian Heymann at ESA, Dr. Evangelos Panos (PSI) and Prof. Russell McKenna (ESA and PSI).

In your research, you will develop novel methods to represent digitalization in large energy system models. This involves a) a digitalization module that can link to established energy system models (such as TIMES, MERGEE-ETL or similar), and b) to assess spatially- and temporally varying net-benefits of digital technologies (such as artificial intelligence – robotic and agentic AI), digital practices (e.g. teleworking, platormization, virtualization) and digital infrastructures (such as data centers).

The project will provide urgently needed evidence for more accurate decisions in energy system planning and contribute to the advancing of co-optimizing digital and energy infrastructures.

Job description

  • Your primary responsibility will therefore be to carry out the research related to the SyDEF project, establishing an innovative extension/ module to our suite of cutting-edge energy system models
  • You will also have the opportunity to supervise and work closely with MSc. and BSc. students at the Chair, as well as contributing to our teaching
  • Opportunities for external collaboration with both academic and industry partners at other institutions will be plentiful, and promising collaborations with top external colleagues will be encouraged

Furthermore, a strong involvement is expected in the coordination and management of various other research aspects of the group's activities and projects, potentially including:

  • Organisation of conferences and seminars within and beyond ETH
  • Assisting in other collaborative research projects
  • Participation in teaching and outreach activities is optional but encouraged

Profile

  • Master’s degree in engineering, economics or related modelling and optimization-focused degree programs
  • High motivation, ambition and enthusiasm for energy research, an analytical mindset, attention to detail, creativity and out-of-the-box thinking, self-driven and independent workstyle, with team player skills
  • Academic background and interest in at least one and ideally several: energy system integration, sector coupling, decentralized energy systems, multi-energy systems, energy economics, optimization and machine learning/ data science
  • Strong interpersonal skills and good communication skills in English, both oral and written are fundamental
