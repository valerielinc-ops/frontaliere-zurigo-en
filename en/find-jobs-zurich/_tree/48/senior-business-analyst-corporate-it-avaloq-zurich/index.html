Full description

Founded and headquartered in Switzerland, Avaloq is continuously expanding its global footprint with around 2,500 colleagues in 12 countries, and more than 170 clients in 35 countries. We are an industry-leading provider of wealth management technology and services for financial institutions around the world, including private banks and wealth managers, investment managers, as well as retail and neo banks. Our research led approach and continual innovation is powered by the passion and creativity of our colleagues.

We are always looking for talented people to join us on our mission to orchestrate the financial ecosystem and democratize access to wealth management. Avaloq offers the opportunity to work closely with some of the world’s leading financial institutions as we jointly develop and shape careers. Championing a collaborative, supportive and flexible work environment empowers our colleagues to reach their full potential.

The Corporate IT team provides digital workplace services and solutions across the Avaloq Group. Within our Business Analysis and Application Development team, we work with corporate functions across the organization to improve operational efficiency through simple, standardized and automated business processes. As a Senior Business Analyst, you will act as a trusted advisor to your assigned business areas and as a key interface between business stakeholders and technical teams. You will help identify the underlying business problems behind requests and translate them into pragmatic, sustainable and cost-effective solutions. This is a broad and hands-on Business Analyst role, with responsibilities extending beyond traditional requirements engineering. Depending on the initiative, you may act as a business process consultant, lead an IT workstream, coordinate delivery activities, configure a SaaS application, support testing or drive an initiative from initial assessment through implementation. Your key tasks

  • Build trusted relationships with your assigned business areas, understand their processes and priorities, facilitate workshops, and jointly define and regularly review their digitalization and process improvement roadmap.
  • Analyse and improve end-to-end business processes, identifying opportunities for simplification, standardization, automation, meaningful usage of AI and better user experience.
  • Structure and translate business needs into clear requirements, documenting and validating them through appropriate business process models, business rules, and business object or data models.
  • Assess and recommend solution options based on feasibility, effort, business value and long-term sustainability, leveraging existing capabilities where appropriate and supporting the evaluation of new solutions.
  • Drive initiatives through to implementation, working closely with development teams, architects, vendors and business stakeholders, and contributing hands-on where needed, including functional configuration, testing and delivery coordination.

Qualifiche

  • At least 5 years of experience as a Business Analyst or in a comparable business-facing technology role, with experience in complex software implementation and process improvement initiatives.
  • Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving skills. You look beyond requested solutions to understand the underlying problem, challenge assumptions constructively and use data and evidence to develop well-founded recommendations.
  • A structured and methodical way of working, with solid experience in requirements engineering and the discipline to analyze problems, evaluate alternatives and define clear, consistent and actionable requirements before moving into implementation.
  • Excellent stakeholder management, consulting and communication skills. You are comfortable facilitating workshops, navigating different interests, constructively challenging stakeholders and communicating with both business and technical audiences.
  • A proactive, curious and adaptable mindset. You take ownership, enjoy learning new business domains and technologies, including AI, and are comfortable contributing in different roles depending on what an initiative requires.

It would be a real bonus if you have

  • Practical experience with established business analysis and modelling approaches, such as BPMN, business rule and data modelling, and familiarity with tools such as ADONIS, Jira, Confluence, Microsoft Power Platform or Microsoft 365 Copilot.
  • Experience with corporate business processes and applications, particularly in areas such as Human Resources, Finance, Vendor Management, Legal, Risk Management or Compliance.
  • Experience with SaaS solutions and implementation partners, including hands-on functional configuration of SaaS applications.
