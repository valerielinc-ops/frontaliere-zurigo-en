Full description

ANYbotics is a fast-growing tech company dedicated to shaping the future of mobile robotics across multiple industries. Join our highly talented and motivated team of more than 200 people and work on cutting-edge robot technology.

The Opportunity

As the Engineering Manager - Product Compliance, you will own the compliance strategy and execution for ANYmal X, our Ex-proof legged robot designed for hazardous areas, as it moves into series production and global rollout. Right now, this means preparing for our 2027 market demand by securing ATEX/IECEx, CE, radio, and electrical safety approvals across all launch markets, while navigating the transition to the new Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230, the EU Battery Regulation, and the Cyber Resilience Act. You will work most closely with our R&D engineering teams, notified bodies, and test labs to embed compliance directly into our product development life cycle. This is a unique chance to build scalable compliance processes from the ground up, rather than inheriting bureaucracy, for a product actively used in the world's most demanding oil, gas, and chemical facilities.

The Market & Our Technology

ANYbotics is shaping the future of industrial automation with advanced legged robots that operate in the most demanding and hazardous environments. Our cutting-edge technology performs autonomous inspections in sectors like oil and gas, energy, and chemicals, keeping humans out of harm's way while ensuring facility integrity. ANYmal X is at the forefront of this innovation, bringing explosion-proof robotics into series production. We operate in a highly regulated, rapidly evolving market where rigorous compliance—spanning machinery, radio, battery, explosion protection, and cybersecurity—is not just a legal requirement, but a core competitive advantage that enables our global expansion.

Your Impact

  • Own the product compliance team backlog, priorities, and reviews while dedicating roughly half your time to hands-on deliverables like technical files and risk assessments
  • Ensure ANYmal X is compliant for series production by updating the EU DoC to the Machinery Regulation and maintaining all ATEX/IECEx, radio, and EMC approvals
  • Deliver non-EU market access according to the 2027 commercial plan, driving certification projects for the US (FCC, NRTL) and priority Middle East markets
  • Embed compliance into product development by establishing clear R&D gate checklists and standardizing technical file structures for all new products and major changes
  • Conduct gap analyses and drive execution plans to meet the EU Battery Regulation (EU 2023/1542) and Cyber Resilience Act (EN 18031) requirements

Your Profile

  • Hands-on experience independently writing CE technical files, risk assessments (ISO 12100), and EU Declarations of Conformity for complex electromechanical or electronic products
  • Proven track record of running end-to-end certification projects with test labs and notified bodies, including test planning, witness testing, and closing nonconformities
  • Ability to independently read and apply harmonized standards and confidently defend technical positions with R&D engineers and certifiers
  • Experience leading a small team or project group while continuing to execute a large share of the hands-on technical work
  • Fluency in English and availability to work on-site in a hybrid capacity at our Zurich office

Bonus points

  • Experience with ATEX/IECEx equipment certification (Ex d, Ex p, Ex i) and ISO/IEC 80079-34 QMS
  • Knowledge of lithium-ion battery compliance (IEC 62133-2, UN 38.3, EU Battery Regulation)
  • Experience securing non-EU market access such as FCC, OSHA NRTL/UL, UKCA, ISED, or Middle East country approvals
  • Familiarity with robotics and functional safety standards, including ISO 10218, ISO 13482, ISO 13849, IEC 61508, or ISO 25785-1
  • Hands-on experience with cybersecurity compliance for connected products (EN 18031, CRA)
  • Proficiency in German

We offer you a very exciting and dynamic work environment, the opportunity to become part of a fast-growing company and an ambitious team that is on a mission to change the industrial inspection market, a chance to leverage your experience and bring in your own ideas, a fair market salary, an attractive employee stock ownership plan, and a job in the city of Zurich.
