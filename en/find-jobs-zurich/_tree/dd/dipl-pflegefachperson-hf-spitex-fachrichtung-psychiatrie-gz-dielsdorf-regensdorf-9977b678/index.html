Full description

Dielsdorf Health Center is a modern geriatric competence center with around 500 employees from 35 nations. Our offer ranges from counselling for older people and their relatives to general and specialized long-term care and care at home. As the largest public Spitex in the Dielsdorf district, we provide ambulatory services for our nine contract municipalities. With our comprehensive range of care services, we enable people to stay in their usual home for as long as possible despite health restrictions. In the future, it is planned to establish a night spitex.

Tasks: • Care and support for our clients at home • Ensuring the care process • Consultation services • Responsibility for the professional and organizational coordination of Spitex operations during evening, weekend and public holiday services Requirements • Completed training as a Dipl. Nursing professional DN I/DN II/HF/FH • High level of social, methodological and professional competence • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing • Contemporary IT knowledge (MS Office, Perigon) • Driving licence Cat. B • Self-employed and responsible working practices We offer: We offer you a responsible and interesting job in a varied, future-oriented and interprofessional environment. We are in a rural situation with good public transport links. You benefit from our attractive employment conditions, including with min. 5 weeks holidays, good social benefits, lunch checks, discount at SBB and Reka checks, individual training opportunities, low prices in our restaurants and free parking spaces.
