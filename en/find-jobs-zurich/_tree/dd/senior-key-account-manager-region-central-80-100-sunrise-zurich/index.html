Full description

At Sunrise, we think bigger, go further and create new things. For us, working culture means creating great things together. Here respect and fresh ideas meet real teamwork – every voice counts, every perspective makes us stronger. Our passion drives us to dare new things and to constantly grow. Sounds like you? Then become part of our success story.

As (Senior) Key Account Manager Region Central You strategically develop our most important customers in Aargau, Solothurn and inner Switzerland. One focus is on the active acquisition of new customers in the enterprise segment and the targeted expansion of our market share. You build long-term partnerships, identify growth potential and develop tailor-made solutions. You work closely with internal teams and manage complex sales processes until completion.

Your challenge

  • You increase the customer value of your assigned accounts through targeted up- and cross-selling as well as the active acquisition of new customers
  • Qualify leads, identify customer needs and manage the entire sales process from initial approach to contract conclusion
  • You conduct contract negotiations sovereignly and ensure a smooth implementation of the agreed services
  • You build long-term relationships and resilient networks in the Buying Center and ensure high customer satisfaction through regular, proactive exchange
  • You are responsible for the quality and timeliness of your account plans and forecasts and keep track of customer relationships, projects, offers and pipelines
  • Through proactive lifecycle management, you ensure timely activities for expired contracts and renewals
  • You carry the target and budget responsibility for your customers and prospects and control Your pipeline consistently on result

Your kills

  • Technical or business education and training completed
  • Several years of experience in the ICT industry and in sales to customers in the medium/enterprise segment, ideally in the field of carrier and integration solutions
  • Demonstrable success in new customer acquisition and account management
  • Experience in selling complex solutions and managing longer sales processes
  • Strong hunter mentality, final strength and negotiating certainty at decision-making level
  • High degree of personal responsibility, assertiveness and entrepreneurial thinking
  • Very good knowledge of German, English skills are advantageous

At Sunrise, you work in a dynamic and multinational environment where every voice is heard, perspectives are shared and values are lived. Diversity is not just a program for us, but part of our DNA. Because we celebrate our differences, we achieve great things together.

Sunrise is a partner of Advance Gender Equality in Business, the Swiss LGBTI label and certified with Fair-ON-Pay Advanced for equal pay for work of equal value for all.

Are you ready for the challenge? Then become part of the team and actively shape our future!
