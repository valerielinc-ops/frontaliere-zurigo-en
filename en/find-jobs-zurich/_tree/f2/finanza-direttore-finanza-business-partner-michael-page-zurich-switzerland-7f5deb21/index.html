Full description

About Our Client Our client is a leading international investment and asset management firm with a strong reputation across global wealth management, institutional investment solutions and active asset management. With a culture that combines entrepreneurial thinking, financial discipline and long-term value creation, the organization offers a dynamic and high-performance environment where finance plays a key role in shaping business strategy.The position can be based in either Zurich or London , providing exposure to senior stakeholders across multiple jurisdictions and investment platforms.

Job Description As the Senior Finance Business Partner for the division, you will act as a trusted advisor to senior management while maintaining an independent finance perspective. You will translate strategic initiatives, commercial developments and investment priorities into clear financial recommendations that support informed decision-making.A key component of the role will be leading the budgeting, forecasting and long-term planning processes. You will develop and challenge financial assumptions linked to assets under management, net new money, revenue margins, product profitability, operating expenses and strategic initiatives. Working closely with business leaders and corporate functions, you will ensure that planning outcomes are aligned with both strategic objectives and financial targets.You will own the financial performance dialogue with senior business stakeholders, providing insightful analysis of revenues, margins, costs, headcount development and profitability drivers. Through rigorous analysis and effective communication, you will identify opportunities, highlight risks and propose actions to enhance business performance.The role also encompasses the assessment of strategic projects, investment cases, hiring plans, technology initiatives and business development opportunities. You will evaluate financial returns, risks, implementation considerations and resource requirements, ensuring robust business cases and disciplined capital allocation.In addition, you will contribute to strengthening governance, financial transparency and performance management frameworks while partnering closely with FP&A, Risk, Accounting, Treasury, Tax, HR and other key stakeholders. As a senior member of the finance organization, you will help promote a strong business partnering culture and contribute to the development of best practices across the finance function.

The Successful Applicant The ideal candidate brings significant experience in Finance Business Partnering, FP&A, Controlling or Management Consulting within the Asset Management or Institutional Investment sphere.You possess a deep understanding of investment management economics, including assets under management, fund flows, revenue margins, product profitability and cost structures. You are comfortable operating in complex, international organizations and have a proven ability to influence senior stakeholders through data-driven insight and constructive challenge.A university degree in Finance, Economics or Business Administration is expected, while qualifications such as CFA, CPA, ACA or ACCA will be viewed favourably. You combine strong financial modelling and analytical capabilities with excellent communication skills. Given the international scope of the role, perfect written and spoken English is essential.

What's on Offer

  • A highly visible finance leadership role with direct exposure to senior executives and investment leaders.
  • An entrepreneurial and collaborative culture that values initiative, innovation and ownership.
  • International career development opportunities in either Zurich or London.
  • Competitive compensation and benefits package commensurate with experience.
  • Broad exposure to investment management, institutional clients and corporate strategy.
  • Ideal platform for finance professionals seeking a blend of commercial, strategic and analytical responsibilities.
