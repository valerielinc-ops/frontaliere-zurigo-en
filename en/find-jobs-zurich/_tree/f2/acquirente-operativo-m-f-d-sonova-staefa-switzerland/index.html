Full description

Join Sonova and become part of our local team supporting smooth and reliable production. As an Operational Buyer, you will be responsible for assigned materials and suppliers, ensuring optimal inventory levels and timely availability of materials.

More about the role

  • Manage assigned materials to ensure optimal stock levels and availability for production
  • Create and manage purchase orders and follow up on order confirmations and deliveries
  • Monitor supplier performance and maintain strong day-to-day supplier relationships
  • Maintain purchasing and material master data in SAP
  • Monitor inventory and proactively manage excess or obsolete stock
  • Review and approve invoices and resolve invoice discrepancies
  • Handle quality notifications, returns and related shipping documentation
  • Prepare purchasing, inventory and operational reports
  • Support colleagues during absences and contribute to smooth day-to-day purchasing operations

More about you

  • Commercial or technical vocational education, ideally with further training in Purchasing, Procurement or Supply Chain
  • Previous experience in operational purchasing or supply chain
  • Practical experience with SAP or another ERP system, ideally SAP MM
  • Good understanding of inventory management, replenishment and purchase order processes
  • Good Excel and Microsoft Office skills
  • Strong communication and organizational skills with a proactive, hands-on approach
  • Team-oriented, reliable and comfortable working with internal and external stakeholders
  • Very good German and good English skills

More about what we offer

As one of the world’s leading hearing care providers headquartered in Switzerland, we’re committed to building an inclusive culture. We want to create an environment where you can balance a successful career with your commitments and interests outside of work, through our flexible hybrid working model. We offer a wide range of training opportunities for both your professional and personal development, and there are exceptional growth opportunities with individual development plans.
