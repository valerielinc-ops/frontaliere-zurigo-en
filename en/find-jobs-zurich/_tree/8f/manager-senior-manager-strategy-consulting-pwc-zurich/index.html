Your team

Strategy& is PwC's global strategy consulting business. In Switzerland, we primarily help leading Swiss companies shape their international presence—from headquarters strategy to the transformation of global business units. We develop tailored strategies for leading companies based on clearly differentiating competitive advantages.

Our team is particularly close-knit: from office events at exciting locations to regular team dinners and team-building events—we make sure there is no shortage of fun at work.

Your impact

As a manager in the Strategy & Transformation team, you work with leading national and international organizations across various industries. Together with your team, you develop strategies and concepts across the entire value chain—from business and operating models, through technology and digital strategies, to transformation programs.

You lead (sub-)projects, manage interdisciplinary teams and act as a liaison between clients, partners and internal stakeholders. In doing so, you work closely with colleagues from different consulting areas and draw on the expertise of our global network to develop and implement holistic solutions.

You also take on a mentor role, support the development of your colleagues and actively contribute to an open, collaborative team culture. Through training, you continuously deepen your methodological, leadership and industry expertise.

In projects, you analyze our clients' complex issues, structure problems and develop strategic solutions with lasting added value. Typical topics include:

  • Development of corporate, business unit and digital strategies
  • Redesign of business and operating models
  • Planning and implementation of transformation and change programs
  • Support for M&A initiatives, carve-outs and portfolio strategies
  • Optimization of organizations, processes and value chains
  • Use of data, analytics and new technologies to support decision-making

You are accountable for the results of your projects, ensure quality and adherence to budgets and deadlines, and present results to C-level executives. In addition, you actively support the acquisition of new projects, develop proposals and further expand existing client relationships.

Your skill set

  • Academic background: A successfully completed university degree with above-average results (Master's, MBA or PhD) in business and economics, (business) informatics, (business) engineering, natural or social sciences, statistics/Data Science or a comparable field.
  • Professional experience: Several years of experience at a top strategy consultancy or in positions of responsibility within a company, ideally focused on strategy, transformation, digitalization or Performance Improvement. Experience leading projects and in proposal development and project acquisition.
  • International experience: Experience abroad through study or work and confidence operating in international and intercultural contexts.
  • Communication skills: Very good oral and written communication skills in German and English. Ability to prepare complex content clearly and present it convincingly to decision-making and leadership bodies.
  • Analytical skills: Highly developed analytical skills and a structured approach to work, with the ability to quickly grasp complex matters and turn them into actionable recommendations.
  • Digital aptitude: Proficient in using common tools (e.g. Excel, PowerPoint) and open to modern data analysis and AI tools; knowledge of data processing, statistical software or programming languages is an advantage.
  • Personal profile: High degree of initiative, sense of responsibility and team orientation. Strong client focus and a commitment to creating sustainable impact for clients.
