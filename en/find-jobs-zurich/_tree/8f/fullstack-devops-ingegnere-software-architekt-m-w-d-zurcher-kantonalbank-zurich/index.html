80% - 100% | IT / Business Engineering | Zurich | Experienced professionals

Description

Our 8-person DevOps team Plankton consists of experienced developers and integration specialists. You are responsible for the operation and continuous development of the standard software solutions SIC and CLS PayPlus as well as the internal sanctions filter, which are essential for secure and efficient payment transactions. In doing so, you combine technical know-how with a deep understanding of business processes to provide reliable solutions.

Together, with great commitment, you develop and operate your applications in the area of payment transactions. You integrate purchased software products from renowned manufacturers into the application landscape of Zürcher Kantonalbank using self-developed service layers. Your focus encompasses all of the team's applications throughout the entire product lifecycle, including software architecture tasks. You bring a high willingness to learn, broad IT expertise and experience in agile ways of working such as Kanban, Scrum or SAFe.

Your responsibilities

  • Implementing requirements mainly in the backend (Java, Spring Boot, Spring Batch, Linux) and in the frontend (TypeScript, Angular, Node.js, HTML5, CSS3)
  • Developing software architectures and helping shape the products
  • Taking responsibility for the further development and stable operation of the systems within the team in accordance with the DevOps philosophy
  • Carrying out technical conceptualization, analyses and design of new features in close collaboration with specialist departments and external software partners
  • Introducing current technology and methodology trends and providing impetus within the team
  • Advising the business unit on feasibility as well as developing options and forming opinions

Your profile

  • Completed education in the field of computer science (e.g. university of applied sciences, university, ETH or equivalent training)
  • Several years of professional experience in the development of web applications, and technologies such as Java, Spring Boot, Angular and TypeScript are part of your everyday work
  • Experience in software architecture and the design of distributed systems
  • Good knowledge of Continuous Integration and Delivery (e.g. CloudBees/Jenkins, Maven, Tomcat, Nexus, GitLab)
  • You stand out through your ability to work in a team, your enthusiasm for tackling new topics and your willingness to share your know-how
  • Enjoyment of supporting applications throughout the entire product lifecycle, from conception to operation
  • Very good command of German and an open, transparent communication style; good understanding of Swiss German

Our offer

  • Exciting projects involving applications in payment transactions, using an agile approach (e.g. SAFe)
  • Continuous development – personally, professionally and technically
  • Various events and internal networks offer you the opportunity to network quickly
  • Modern workplace in District 5 with very good transport links (Hardbrücke railway station) and the opportunity to work from home
  • Opportunities for further training, part-time work, mobile and flexible working, as well as unpaid leave
  • Comprehensive overall package which, in addition to the attractive base salary and variable compensation, offers many benefits and discounts – from A for old-age provision to Z for ZVV-Bonuspass
