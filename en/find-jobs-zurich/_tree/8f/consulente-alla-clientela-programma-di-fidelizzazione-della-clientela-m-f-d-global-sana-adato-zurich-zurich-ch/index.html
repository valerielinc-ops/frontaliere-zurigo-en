Tasks

In this function, you answer general inquiries about the customer loyalty program and support the participants as a competent contact person. They accept new registrations and communicate in writing and orally through various channels. This includes emails, chat requests and feedback via social media. With your friendly and solution-oriented manner, you ensure a positive customer experience.

Requirements

For this position, you will have completed basic commercial training and at least one year of professional experience in the office sector. They communicate eloquently and confidently in word and writing and are fluent in Swiss German and French, while English and Italian are beneficial. In addition, you have good knowledge in dealing with the common MS-Office programs. Important are a high service orientation, teamwork and the pleasure of daily customer contact.
