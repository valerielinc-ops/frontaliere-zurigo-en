About the position Hitachi Energy, High Voltage Products, is one of the world’s leading providers of energy transmission and distribution solutions. Our service organization ensures that critical energy infrastructures of our customers around the globe can be operated reliably, safely and efficiently. As a technical buyer, you play a key role at the interface between purchasing, engineering, quality assurance and our global supplier network. Ensure that technically specified materials, components and services meet the required quality, deadline and cost requirements and are available in the long term. With your technical understanding and cross-divisional collaboration, you make an important contribution to product quality, security of supply and operational excellence. This will help Hitachi Energy to successfully provide innovative and sustainable energy solutions.

You can do that.

  • Technical clarification of materials, drawings, specifications and engineering changes in close cooperation with engineering and suppliers
  • Support and coordination of PPAP (Production Part Approval Process) activities, initial sampling and supplier qualifications
  • Conduct of technical assessments of alternative materials, requests for derogation and cases of obsolescence
  • Close cooperation with strategic and operational purchasing on material and supplier-related issues
  • Support for supplier complaints, non-conformity reports (NCR) and root-cause analyses
  • Participation in supplier audits and technical assessments regarding skills, quality standards and process maturity
  • Support in the evaluation and further development of supplier performance together with quality assurance and strategic purchasing
  • Ensuring data quality and completeness of material and supplier master data in the SAP system
  • Technical contact for supplier escalations as well as complex or time-critical procurement topics
  • Support of service organisations and project teams in challenging procurement and supplier issues

Your background

  • Completed bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering, completion of a higher technical school/technical school or basic technical training (e.g. polymechanic, automatist or constructor) with corresponding further training.
  • Ideally, professional experience in technical purchasing or in a comparable supply chain management function
  • Sound understanding of technical drawings, parts lists (BOM) and manufacturing and production processes
  • Experience working with suppliers on technical, quality-relevant and commercial issues
  • Experience with SAP and the relevant material and procurement processes
  • Structured, analytical and solution-oriented working method
  • Good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • High initiative and an independent and result-oriented working method
  • Strong communication skills and enjoyment of cross-sectoral cooperation in an international environment
