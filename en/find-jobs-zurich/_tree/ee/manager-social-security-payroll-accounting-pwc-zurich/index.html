Full description

In the Finance Team of PwC Switzerland, we ensure the quality, transparency and integrity of our financial and payroll processes. Together with colleagues from Payroll, Human Capital and Accounting, we ensure that wage-relevant business transactions are processed, coordinated and reported correctly. In a dynamic environment, you work at the interface of social insurance, payroll and financial accounting and make an important contribution to the reliability of our financial data and the continuous development of our processes.

  • Responsible for the coordination between payroll accounting and financial accounting and ensure the correct accounting of all payroll-relevant transactions.
  • Analyze social security, withholding tax and other pay-related balance sheet accounts and ensure their completeness and accuracy.
  • Identify differences between payroll and financial accounting data and contribute to their sustainable clarification and cleanup.
  • Coordinate the reconciliation of outstanding claims and liabilities and ensure a timely settlement of open positions.
  • Support monthly, quarterly, half-yearly and annual financial statements in all payroll-relevant topics and ensure high data quality.
  • Drive the optimization, standardization and digitalization of payroll and accounting processes together with internal and external stakeholders.
  • Several years of professional experience in payroll accounting, payroll accounting, social insurance or a comparable area of expertise.
  • In-depth knowledge of the Swiss social security system, in particular AHV, ALV, EO, family allowances, BVG and withholding tax.
  • Practical experience in the reconciliation of payroll and social security accounts as well as in the analysis and settlement of differences.
  • Very good understanding of the links between payroll and financial accounting.
  • Experience with SAP S/4HANA and Abacus
  • Knowledge of SAP HCM is an advantage.
  • Excel knowledge and a high affinity for digital processes and systems.
  • Structured, precise and independent working methods combined with a pronounced service and stakeholder orientation.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing.
