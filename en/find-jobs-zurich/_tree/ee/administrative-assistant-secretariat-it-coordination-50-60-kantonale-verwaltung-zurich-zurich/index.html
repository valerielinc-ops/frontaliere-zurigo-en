Full description

You are an organizational talent, enjoy digital solutions and keep an overview even in a lively working environment? Then a varied task awaits you, in which you can contribute both your administrative skills and your flair for IT and digital processes. Tasks

  • You are the first point of contact for the team with questions and problems relating to our specialist applications and take on the role as a super user.
  • You open support tickets, monitor their processing and coordinate the requests with our IT service provider Digisol.
  • You act as a link between our team and Digisol and ensure targeted communication.
  • If necessary, you can provide first level support for the extranet.
  • You take over the deputy for the management of our event management tool and our website.
  • You support the team in the introduction of new processes and systems and actively participate in IT projects
  • Together with your two colleagues, you organize the secretariat and ensure an efficient and professional process of administrative tasks.
  • You lead the business control and ensure a reliable recording and tracking of the business.
  • You process the inbox and mailing, organize the file enclosure and take care of the return and archiving of files.
  • You will carry out administrative work in connection with legal validity investigations, files and case closures.
  • You prepare payments as part of victim support procedures and take over the accounting of general invoices.
  • You take phone calls, forward them and answer simpler requests independently.
  • You’ll organize appointments, sessions, and events and keep the team agenda and log sessions. You bring that with you
  • You have a basic commercial education or a comparable qualification.
  • You will bring experience in secretarial management and administrative support of a team.
  • You have a high IT affinity and are interested in digital applications, systems and processes.
  • You are well-versed in dealing with the common MS Office applications and can quickly find your way around new specialist applications.
  • You work independently, structured, carefully and service-oriented.
  • You are communicative, solution-oriented and enjoy supporting different stakeholders.
  • You also keep track of hectic situations and set the right priorities.
  • You are team-oriented, flexible and happy to join in.
  • Ideally, you will bring experience in the project environment or in cooperation with external IT service providers. You can look forward to that.
  • A versatile and responsible activity with different areas of responsibility.
  • A central role as the organizational and digital hub of our team.
  • The opportunity to contribute your own ideas and actively shape processes.
  • A collegial team that appreciates your support.
  • A varied working environment in which administration, IT and organization go hand in hand.
