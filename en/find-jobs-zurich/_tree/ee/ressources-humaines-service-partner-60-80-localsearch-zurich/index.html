Full description

localsearch is the leading marketing and advertising partner for Swiss SMEs. We make customers successful in the digital world. With you, the threads of the HR administration come together: personnel data is correct, mutations are recorded cleanly and there is a competent answer to questions quickly. Together with two other HR Service Partners: you are the central point of contact for all administrative HR topics and part of our 8-member HR team in Zurich. Our HR organization is in the midst of a transformation, and you actively shape it: with self-responsibility, an eye for the whole and the joy of making things better.

This is what moves you

  • You are responsible for the HR administration throughout the entire employee life cycle – from entry to contract changes and exits.
  • You maintain the personnel master data in Abacus and make sure it is correct and complete
  • You prepare the pay-relevant data monthly and are the contact person for our external payroll partner
  • You answer administrative HR inquiries from employees and managers and are a key user for Abacus
  • You work in HR projects and take on your own sub-projects with the aim of further developing our team
  • You create reports, prepare the data for further use and analyze them independently
  • You are responsible for the social insurance: registration and deregistrations, illness and accident reports and their billing

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed HR training (e.g. HR professional with a federal certificate or HR assistant: in) and several years of experience in HR administration
  • You think along, see where improvements are needed, and tackle them proactively
  • You work independently and actively take responsibility – also for topics outside your core area
  • An exact and independent working method – the dates and deadlines are right for you
  • You are a committed contact person and want to grow on new tasks
  • You like a dynamic environment and are flexible with change
  • Very good user knowledge in Microsoft 365 (especially Word and Excel) as well as active and targeted use of AI tools to increase efficiency and productivity
  • Good knowledge of Swiss social insurance and experience in wage preparation
  • stylish German and good knowledge of English
  • French and Italian are a plus
