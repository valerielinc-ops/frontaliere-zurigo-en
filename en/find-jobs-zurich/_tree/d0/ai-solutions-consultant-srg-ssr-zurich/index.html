Full description

The Operations division assumes the central strategic and controlling role for the technological orientation and production of the SRG SSR. The AI Solutions and Consulting team strengthens the use of AI in production, editorial and support functions and procures and integrates the necessary AI services and tools.

As an AI consultant, you support departments and product teams in identifying, evaluating and implementing effective AI use cases. You will advise on suitable 3rd party tools, procure and integrate them and contribute your expertise in the conception and creation of prompts and agents as well as standardizations in this context. You work in an agile team with other consultants and the product owner.

This is your contribution.

  • Identify, analyze and translate requirements and needs of stakeholders into viable concepts and AI solutions
  • Close cooperation with IT Security and Data & AI Business to cleanly coordinate and align solutions professionally, technically and security-wise
  • Guide AI projects from idea to implementation: plan and control projects, implement and evaluate proofs of concept, evaluate and procure suitable technologies and solutions
  • Design and implementation of AI-supported workflows and implementation of AI agents
  • Assuming professional responsibility for various AI-based services in the support and production functions as well as their continuous development

They inspire us.

  • Extensive experience in the design and implementation of AI solutions, services and agents
  • In-depth technical understanding and practical experience of how AI works, including its capabilities and limitations
  • The ability to clarify technical requirements together with stakeholders, translate them into realistic AI solution approaches and procure suitable technologies, platforms and services
  • Very good knowledge of German and English, knowledge of Italian at least level B1 is an advantage

So we inspire you.

  • Direct impact on how AI is used effectively at SRG SSR, from initial idea to implementation
  • Varied AI topics from dubbing and Speech2Text to chatbots to agents, in close cooperation with editorial teams, production, support functions and IT
  • Great scope for design with a high level of team and personal responsibility
  • Flexible working hours and home office opportunities in a culture of respect, trust and openness

Our promise

What does it take to reach the people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expertise, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can contribute and develop. For the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: Welcome to the world of SRG!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, promote creativity and offer a lot of design space for new and high-quality products.
  • Shaping digitalization: Quality journalism is also digital for us. That’s why we explore new possibilities and experiment with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-medial. We design on the pulse of time and on the nerve of the audience.
  • Being human: We meet each other collegially, work independently and shape our working atmosphere individually. Our success is based on the personal strengths as well as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programs. In doing so, we act respectfully and independently; in this way we promote opinion-forming and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.
