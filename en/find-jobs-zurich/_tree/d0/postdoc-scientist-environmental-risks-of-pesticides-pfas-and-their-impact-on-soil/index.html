Full description

Institut für Evolutionsbiologie und Umweltwissenschaften

Start of employment Winter/Spring 2027, temporary

The research takes place at the Agroecology & Plant Microbiome Interactions Group at the University of Zurich and Agroscope (location Zurich). This is a dynamic group consisting of scientists, technicians, Postdocs, PhD and Master students & guests with app. 25 members. The group focuses on plant-soil-microbe-interactions, mycorrhizal fungi, pesticides, soil biodiversity, the development of sustainable/ecological farming systems, and soil ecological engineering. The university of Zurich and Agroscope have excellent research facilities with well-equipped laboratories, greenhouses, climate chambers and agricultural fields. You will enjoy flexible working hours and good social benefits. Your work will contribute to the further development of a sustainable agriculture and a healthy environment. The project is performed in collaboration with Dr. Thomas Bucheli (Environmental Analytics, Agroscope) and Prof. Dr. Katrin Fenner (Department of Chemistry, University of Zurich) The work builds upon earlier work in the group (Königer et al. 2026, Nature

  • Barmettler et al. 2026, Journal of Hazardous Materials
  • Martin & van der Heijden 2024, New Phytologist
  • Romero et al. 2025, Nature Communications
  • Banerjee et al. 2026, Nature Reviews Microbiology).

Your responsibilities

We recently demonstrated that pesticides are widely distributed in Swiss and European soils. Moreover, further work showed pesticides have a big impact on soil biodiversity and specific groups of soil biota such as arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi (AMF) (Königer et al. 2026

  • Barmettler et al. 2026). This work was based on large Swiss and European networks. Next we need to explore how soil functioning and water quality is affected by pesticides. Also, we need to know, which pesticides (and combinations) have the strongest effects. A further aspect that deserve attention is the presence of long-lived chemicals (e.g. PFAS) in soils and whether they affect soil biodiversity and soil functioning. Building upon this earlier work you will:
  • Coordinate a large sampling network and assess pesticides and PFAS in soil and water samples
  • Co-supervise a PhD student and work in a team of app. 5 persons working on pesticides and PFAS
  • Perform field and greenhouse experiments testing effects of pesticides and PFAS on soil micro-organisms and soil functioning.
  • Use molecular tools (qPCR, metabarcoding) and microscopy to assess how pesticides affect soil life.
  • contribute to data analysis and reporting.
  • Publish results in high-impact, peer-reviewed journals.

Your profile

Doctoral degree (PhD) or equivalent in environmental science, ecology, ecotoxicology, environmental chemistry, soil science, hydrology, biology, or a closely related field
