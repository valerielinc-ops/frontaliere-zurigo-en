Full description

Shape the food safety of the Migros industry across company and location boundaries and consistently develop our prevention and risk management systems. As a central expert, you combine strategic foresight with a high presence in our production plants and make food safety effective where it arises.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Industrie AG, Migros Industrie AG – Zurich, Limmatstrasse 152, 8005 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You develop the MIND-wide strategy for food and product safety and control microbiological, chemical, allergenic and physical risks. Emerging risks are recognized early on and translated into effective prevention strategies.
  • You are responsible for the MIND-wide HACCP system and continuously develop standards, methods and prevention systems. You bring your know-how from existing production processes to the development of new products, technologies and plants.
  • In the event of serious food safety events, you take over the technical leadership and work in the crisis team. You ensure root cause analyses as well as effective corrective and prevention measures and the MIND-wide use of the findings.
  • You develop and control MIND-wide auditing, evaluate the effectiveness of our prevention systems and derive systematic improvements from audits, trends and operational insights.
  • You are regularly present in our production facilities, understand the challenges on site and support the plants hands-on with critical questions, root cause analyses and sustainable improvement measures.
  • You will lead and empower the MIND-wide network of experts in food safety, develop training programmes and ensure the cross-location transfer of expertise, best practices and modern methods.

Requirements

  • At least 8–10 years of relevant professional experience in food safety and quality management within the food industry: ideally combined with several years of leadership experience and responsibility in an industrial production environment.
  • Master’s degree (university, university of applied sciences): in food sciences, food technology, microbiology, biotechnology or a comparable discipline.
  • You have proven expertise in food safety, HACCP, risk-based analysis and preventive quality management as well as in-depth knowledge of GMP, GHP and PRP systems.
  • You will have at least 8–10 years of professional experience in food safety and quality management in the food industry, as well as several years of experience in a line or comparable management role.
  • You are familiar with industrial production processes in practice, understand the operational challenges and can quickly assess risks and develop pragmatic and effective solutions.
  • You have experience in the development and implementation of food safety management systems as well as in auditing, root cause analysis and sustainable action management.
  • You convince through professional authority and can win experts, managers and different organizational units for common goals even without direct authority.
  • Experience with different product categories, several production sites as well as authorities, certification bodies or external specialist organisations is an advantage.
