Full description

Materials science and technology are our passion. With our cutting-edge research, Empa’s approximately 1,100 employees make decisive contributions to the benefit of society for a future worth living in. Empa is a research institution of the ETH Domain.

IT Platform Engineer for Data and Collaboration

So that around 1,000 researchers can work on solutions for the future, we create the necessary digital foundations in IT. As a partner for research, we rely on reliable services, modern technologies and close cooperation across team boundaries. Short decision-making paths and openness to new ideas enable us to actively shape the IT of tomorrow.

In our IT department of about 40 people, you can expect an international, diverse team in which initiative, practical skills and cooperation count. Together, we develop and operate the platforms on which our digital services are built. For our IT team at the Dübendorf location, we are looking for a committed personality immediately or by agreement.

Their tasks

In this versatile role, you shape our central platforms for data and digital collaboration. They combine reliable operation with the further development of our technologies and actively drive automation and standardization.

  • Operating and further developing data platform : You take responsibility for our locally operated Microsoft SQL Server databases, ensure their stable and high-performance operation and continuously develop monitoring, maintenance processes and automation.
  • Microsoft 365 and collaboration: You will further develop our M365 ecosystem with SharePoint Online and Power Platform, shape sustainable governance structures and advise our departments on the implementation of modern collaboration solutions.
  • Drive automation and platform standards: PowerShell or Python, Git and CI/CD pipelines automate recurring tasks, standardize configurations and establish reproducible and reliable deployment processes.

Your profile

They have experience in the operation and further development of IT platforms, think solution-oriented and enjoy improving existing structures and using new technologies.

  • Technical foundation: Good knowledge of relational database systems and experience with Microsoft infrastructures or Microsoft 365. Knowledge of SharePoint Online, Power Platform or Azure is a plus.
  • Joy of automation: Experience with PowerShell or Python as well as Git. Ideally, you have already dealt with Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD or automated deployment processes.
  • Self-employment and team spirit: You work in a structured way, take on responsibility and appreciate cooperation with different specialist areas. Service orientation and pragmatic solutions are important to you.
  • Education and experience: An education in computer science, business informatics or a related field (HF, FH, university, TU) or comparable knowledge from practice and further education.

They do not have to meet all the requirements already. Decisive for us are your technical understanding, your curiosity and your willingness to familiarize yourself with new topics and to continuously develop your skills.

Our offer

  • Design leeway: Bring your ideas and actively develop our IT platforms.
  • Meaningful work: Your work creates the digital basis for research and innovation.
  • Cooperation at eye level: An international team that thrives on open communication, mutual support and uncomplicated cooperation.
  • Personal development: Various possibilities for professional further education and the development of your skills.
  • Attractive framework conditions: Flexible working hours and a fair remuneration according to the wage model of the ETH Domain.

Not quite all the requirements met? We are aware that women and people belonging to a minority in particular often only apply when they meet the requirements profile almost completely. For us, however, motivation, commitment and willingness to learn also count. If you recognize yourself in the position, we look forward to your qualified application, even if you do not meet the requirements 100%.

We look forward to your complete online application incl. Letter of motivation, CV, certificates, diplomas and contact details of two reference persons. Please submit them exclusively via our application portal. Applications by e-mail or by post will not be considered.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
