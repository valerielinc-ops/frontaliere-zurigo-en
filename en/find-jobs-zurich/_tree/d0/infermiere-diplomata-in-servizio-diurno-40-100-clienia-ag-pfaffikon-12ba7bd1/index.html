Description of your tasks Flexibility and enjoyment of variety are central points. Because that’s exactly what working in acute psychiatry means. If this applies to you, here are some of the most important tasks:

  • Reference person work: We treat our patients individually according to their needs and maintain contact with their relatives
  • In addition to individual discussions, there are group offers, which are also led by nursing
  • Our interprofessional collaboration is a huge strength and sets us apart. Active participation and involvement are central components of this collaboration
  • Since we work with permanent night guards, you will be deployed in a two-shift system (early and late shift). If you would still like to spend a few nights, that is certainly possible
  • Requisiti
  • professional competence, service orientation and a lot of humor are the most important qualities you need to have
  • With these qualities we achieve the best treatment results for our patients and the team is happy that you are becoming a part of them
  • If you have experience in nursing but not in the psychiatric environment, it is still possible to join. It is then crucial that you have a lot of motivation and commitment to acquire the specific skills
