Full description

You want to take responsibility for the day from time to time? Are you not ready for Stv.? Here you can prove what you have. Share your knowledge. Collect leadership experience. And of course learn a lot. Denner. This is our shop.

Mansioni

  • You pay special attention to the freshness of fruits, vegetables and bread and say Food Waste with far-sighted orders the fight
  • Fresh products and of course all other goods order, accept, check and fill in the shelves in your store
  • correctly implement specifications in the store layout
  • ensure order and cleanliness in the store and in the warehouse
  • service and bill
  • guide, promote and bring employees to the office as assistants – and be a model for them

Requisiti

  • Ideally more than two years of professional experience: in retail trade (food)
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EBA): in retail trade (food) or related professional genre would be ideal
  • joy in fresh products and ideally already professional experience with it
  • ready to work beyond «nine to five» – and enjoy the free time when others work
  • Physically fit, some Colis are up to 10 kilos heavy
  • happy among people, a good portion of cordiality and a positive basic attitude
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa offriamo

  • Part of the Migros Group: Strong brand anchored in retail under the umbrella of the Migros Group
  • Work healthy: high standards in occupational safety, ergonomics and hygiene.
  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 5 weeks longer baby break (19 weeks) for mothers and at 100% pay. Fathers enjoy the young generation 4 weeks paid.
  • Fair wages: 13 monthly wages and fixed wage increases for all instead of bonuses for few.
  • Mobile phone subscription: The agony of choice: With Galaxus Mobile, Wingo or Sunrise already connected from CHF 10.- or so properly.
  • Migros Bank: Better travel with interest and fees of your private, investment or mortgage dealer.
  • Employee offers: 10 percent on the next major purchase (twice a year).
  • Holiday: 6 weeks holidays for everyone and even more days at service jubilees.
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts for car rentals, hotels, events and many more.
  • Targeted promotion: From dish washer to millionaire? Maybe not exactly, but: Who has bite and talent, comes quickly.
