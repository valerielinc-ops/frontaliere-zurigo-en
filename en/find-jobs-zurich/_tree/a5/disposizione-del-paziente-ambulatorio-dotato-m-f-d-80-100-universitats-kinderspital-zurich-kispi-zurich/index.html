Full description

Children's Hospital Zurich Eleonorenstiftung | online since : 16.11.2026

You keep track, coordinate with foresight and make sure everything is in the right place at the right time.

Your tasks

  • You plan and coordinate outpatient appointments and consultation hours in a forward-looking, reliable manner and taking into account the different needs and framework conditions.
  • You coordinate closely with different specialist areas, recognize dependencies and find pragmatic and creative solutions even for complex requirements.
  • You are a competent and service-oriented contact person for our patients, their parents and internal and external partners. You answer questions and concerns by phone and email and ensure an understandable and binding communication.
  • You keep track of short-term changes and high workloads, set the right priorities and react flexibly to new situations.
  • You are actively involved in the team, support your colleagues and promote open and constructive cooperation.

Your profile

  • Basic medical education (MPA or FaGe) or training in the medical/nursing sector with commercial training
  • Professional experience as a dispatcher in healthcare
  • Able to use IT tools
  • Very good language skills in German and English, other languages are beneficial
  • Organized, structured and service-oriented personality
