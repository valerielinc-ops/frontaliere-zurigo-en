Full description

With great attention to detail, our employees make GLOBUS the leading premium and luxury address in Switzerland. It is people like you who make the difference and bring the place we love to be to life together with us. Here you make the difference

  • With a sense of individual wishes, you advise our customers competently and convincingly.
  • You plan tastings and events and conduct them safely.
  • Also beyond your area of expertise, you are involved in the assortment presentation.
  • You pay attention to legal requirements and a well-kept appearance.
  • In visual merchandising and promotions, you use your eye for details.

That draws you out

  • Experience in sales or in gastronomy – ideally with a focus on wine and spirits – you bring with you.
  • You have an education in the field of wine, gastronomy or retail trade – e.g. as a sommelier/sommelière, wine specialist consultant:in or winemaker:in.
  • You perform with a natural presence and see yourself as a host: in style.
  • Initiative, team spirit and reliability are part of your working style.
  • Very good German, good English skills and commitment from Monday to Saturday you bring with you.

You can rejoice.

Also, look forward to

  • an exceptional working environment in the highest-grossing and most popular shopping centre in Switzerland.
  • an excellent public transport connection – tram and bus stop directly in front of the Glattzentrum.

  • Free parking for employees as well as petrol stations and e-gas stations directly in front of the building.
  • A great roof terrace for employees to eat in the fresh air of Zmittag or simply to recharge with the sun.
  • Top food options thanks to smooth gastronomy: 14 restaurants and bars offer everything from delicacies, Mediterranean treats, Asian delicacies to takeaways.
  • 100 shops, including pharmacies, a post office and much more – the Glatt, the small shopping town! PS: Thanks to the smooth card you get various perks.
  • attractive social benefits.

  • the outfit pass with around 50% on 30 clothes per year for you.

  • a fairy-tale atmosphere during the extraordinary smooth Christmas season.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
