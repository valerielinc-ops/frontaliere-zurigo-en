Sushi chef/cook Two Spice AG — Dietlikon, Dietlikon, Svizzera Two Spice – that’s us! A creative and innovative gastronomy and lifestyle company from Zurich. Immerse yourself in the world of Yooji's and become part of our team that is redefining Japanese cuisine with passion and creativity. Would you like to become part of our team?
