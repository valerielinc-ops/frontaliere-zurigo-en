Full description

Doing the right thing. In Switzerland and in around 20 countries on four continents. Engage with us for the vision of a solidarity society, as Caritas Switzerland has been operating two asylum centres with around 90 unaccompanied minors since March 2024 on behalf of the Cantonal Social Office Zurich. The socio-educational caregivers are responsible for case management in asylum care and in public welfare. To strengthen the reference personnel teams, we are looking for a social worker in an advisory capacity. Their tasks

  • Contact person for socio-educational caregivers on specific issues related to support guidelines and the provision of the relevant basics
  • Further development of social worker case management processes in the MNA area
  • Conception of training, in particular with regard to social assistance law
  • Coaching and support of employees in various functions in processes of social worker case management and punctually in case management issues with assumption of operational tasks after agreement with socio-pedagogical reference person
  • Ensuring the principle of subsidiarity (social insurance, support services, etc.)
  • Active participation in the team and participation in inter- and supervision

Our requirements

  • Diploma in Social Work, Social Education (or comparable education)
  • Knowledge and experience in the application of the Social Assistance Act and/or Asylum Welfare Ordinance (mandatory requirement)
  • Further education in case management and/or on integration topics of advantage
  • Experience in social counselling and enjoyment of complex issues
  • Enjoyment of conceptual work with sporadic client*internal contact
  • Knowledge of integration offers in the Canton of Zurich is an advantage
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German and good oral knowledge of another national language and/or English and/or an asylum language
  • Safe use of digital tools
  • Experience with tutoris is beneficial
  • Intercultural competence, negotiating skills and team spirit
  • Structured, resilient and self-organised working methods

Place of work: Canton of Zurich (Uster and Zurich-Oerlikon) Start of work: immediately Caritas offers attractive employment conditions and an open working environment at the pulse of social issues. For the transformation towards role-based work, we count on employees with a digital and agile mindset who like to participate in change processes and take responsibility. You are welcome to receive information about this position from Mr. Emanuel Zink, Head of Social Education MNA, telephone +41 76 391 45 96. We thank you for your interest and look forward to your application via the online portal. Caritas Switzerland, Adligenswilerstrasse 15, 6002 Lucerne www.caritas.ch
