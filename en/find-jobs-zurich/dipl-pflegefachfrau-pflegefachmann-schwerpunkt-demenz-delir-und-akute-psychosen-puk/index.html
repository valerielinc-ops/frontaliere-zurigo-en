Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich treats and cares for mentally ill people of all ages. The more than 2,500 employees of the 55 different professions make their contribution to the holistic care of patients in interdisciplinary teams, from clarification and therapy to follow-up care. Our work is based on mutual trust and personal responsibility and together we promote an innovative corporate culture. As a university clinic, we are successfully involved in national and international research projects. 50%-100% A new perspective? for patients. And for you. For our acute medical care unit with a focus on dementia, delirium and acute psychosis, we search immediately or by appointment. Their tasks

  • Competent, appreciative care, care and advice for patients aged 65 and over and their relatives
  • Assuming nursing care in the context of caregiver work in close cooperation with an interprofessional team
  • Assuming daily responsibility and ensuring smooth station operations
  • Planning and management of nursing therapy groups to promote activation and therapeutic processes
  • Support for vocational training in the training of learners and students on the station

Your profile

  • Recognized vocational training as a nurse HF/FH (for a foreign diploma with proof of recognition from the Swiss Red Cross)
  • Professional experience in psychiatry and/or gerontology
  • Enjoyment of working with patients and a strong sense of responsibility
  • High flexibility and resilience as well as willingness to actively participate in a constantly evolving team

Our offer

  • Working flexibly: working time models that fit your life
  • Knowledge that advances: Extensive initial, further and further training offers
  • More time for relaxation: at least 25 days of holiday and possibility for unpaid leave
  • Future provision with added value: 60% employer contribution to the pension fund
  • Sustainable travel: Participation in the ZVV-BonusPass or general subscription
  • Focus on health: Various sports offerings, green oases and an internal care team
