Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Composition by: 01. March 2026 or by arrangement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 66368

Klinik Im Park operates a dialysis station with 12 treatment stations, and Klinik Hirslanden has commissioned a dialysis station with 8 treatment stations in autumn 2020. At both locations, dialysis treatments for outpatients of all insurance classes are possible. Inpatients are also dialyzed in the clinic Im Park as well as in the clinic Hirslanden.

For the site Klinik Hirslanden we are looking for 01. March 2026 or by agreement a motivated Dipl. Care specialist dialysis, who actively supports the existing dialysis team.

YOUR TASKS

  • Ensuring patient-oriented care in accordance with quality standards, taking into account the given goals
  • Independent implementation of diagnostic, therapeutic and prophylactic measures prescribed by the doctor
  • Responsible for technically and hygienically perfect shunt punctures as well as continuous monitoring of patients
  • Implementation and care of dialysis of international patients
  • Pictet services on weekends

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training for/for the Dipl. Nursing professional / Dipl. Nursing professional HF / FH or comparable training
  • 2-3 years of professional experience in dialysis with meaningful performance
  • Experience in practical peritoneal dialysis
  • Very good German and English skills in words and writing, other foreign languages are an advantage

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. You work with a modern medical clinic information system and can expect extensive training and development opportunities from us. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family responsibilities. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. Employees who have to move completely for their work receive four additional paid days off with a 100% workload. With a reduced workload, the days off reduce accordingly. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from an equivalent financial compensation. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.

For additional information, Marek Peschel, Head of Dialysis, under T or Janine Moschetto, Team Leader HR Recruitment Partner Platz Zürich, at janine.moschetto@hirslanden.ch are at your disposal. We look forward to your contact.
