Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 100% For our station C2 for affective disorders of the Center for Depression, Anxiety Disorders and Psychotherapy of Adult Psychiatry at the Lenggstrasse site, we are looking for 1. April 2027 or by agreement one or more Their tasks

  • Leading a medical/psychological team and supervising the treatment of inpatients with mental illness, including diagnosis, therapy and exit planning
  • Application of targeted treatment concepts within the focus on affective disorders with cognitive-behavioral and systemic approaches
  • Supervision of treatment by and supervision of assistant doctors and psychologists
  • Participation in teaching and further training of assistant doctors and psychologists
  • Further development of the treatment concept of the station, in cooperation with the multiprofessional team and the care and in coordination with other units in the track Affective disorders and with regard to "Difficult to treat depression"
  • Participation in research and teaching with study recruitment and student teaching
  • Participation in interventional psychiatry treatments with ECT, rTMS and (Es) ketamine treatments

Your profile

  • Presence of a Swiss medical diploma (MEBEKO registered) or MEBEKO recognition of the foreign medical diploma (not registration) plus MEBEKO language entry German
  • Specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy completed or shortly before
  • Leadership experience with high independence and self-organization
  • Experience in Affective Disorders
  • Interest in innovative treatment concepts including interventional psychiatry (e.g. repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation [rTMS], electroconvulsion treatment [EKT], electroencephalogram diagnosis [EEG])
  • German language skills in word and writing at level C2

Our offer

  • Clinic, research and teaching united: working in a university environment with international networking
  • Development with perspective: Diverse career paths in one of the largest psychiatric clinics in Switzerland
  • Shaping the future actively: Participation in change and development processes
  • Sustainable travel: Participation in the ZVV-BonusPass or general subscription
  • Future provision with added value: 60% employer contribution to the pension fund
  • Well taken care of in everyday work: discounted food offers or lunch checks
