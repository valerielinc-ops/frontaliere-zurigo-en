Mission Tasks Are you looking for a job where you don't spend half your life behind a desk? Do you dream of supporting a team every day and achieving goals together? Would you like to be the boss of your own small business without taking on the risk of being a sole proprietor? Then the position as Team Leader in Outdoor/ Winter/ Water/ Rental is exactly right for you. Why?

  • Start date: immediately or by agreement
  • Standort:Letzipark, Zürich Altstetten
  • Workload: 100% 1. You spend 80% of your time on the shop floor in the store Your department is your territory. Whether you spend time with your team, ensure that the merchandising standards are in place, or directly delight our customers. You don't sit on the sidelines. Strategy is important, but we are looking for a hands-on personality for the sales floor. 2. Your success is your team’s success Your job is not to control everything, but to make everything possible. A team member is passionate about their sport and has an innovative idea for the department in the store? Let them do it! Success or failure? It doesn’t matter. The main thing is that you learn together and grow from it. 3. You are in the driver's seat At our company, you lead your department like your own start-up. From precise inventory planning and well-founded KPI analysis to recruiting your talent and commercial strategy. You have a firm hand on the wheel. In Schaffhausen, you shape your business independently and do everything you can not only to achieve your goals, but to exceed them. What a Team Leader does with us: Your mission is simple: The success of your department—both human and economic.
  • Store Life: You coordinate the flow of goods, pitch in and, as “Captain”, are regularly the point of contact for the entire store.
  • People Management: You conduct monthly coaching conversations (INDI), create staffing schedules and seek out the best talent for your team.
  • Strategy: You analyze your metrics and plan the next big thing. Which sports will we promote next season? How will we further develop your team's skills?
  • Business Ownership: P&L and KPI analyses are not abstract theory for you, but your daily tools. You run your department like your own business and make bold decisions for greater profitability. What we offer you: Responsibility from day 1 – we and your team trust you implicitly. Unlimited career opportunities – from Team Leader to Store Manager, Ecosystem Leader. There are no limits here for you and your ambition. 30 days of vacation – so that you have enough time for your own sporting adventures. Profit-sharing & shares – You have the exclusive opportunity to invest up to 25% of your gross salary in company shares and thus participate directly in our growth. Sports & mobility bonus – we get you moving. Team Bonus
  • Together with your team, you have the chance to receive a monthly bonus of up to 10% of your gross salary. A kick-start to family happiness – Because the first moments are priceless, we support you with 16 weeks of maternity leave during your greatest adventure away from the sales floor. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
