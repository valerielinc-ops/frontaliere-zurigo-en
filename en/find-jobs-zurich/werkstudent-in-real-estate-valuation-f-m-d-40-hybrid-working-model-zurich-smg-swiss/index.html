Full description

Your mission

As a working student in the IAZI Valuation Team, you play a decisive role in the successful cooperation with our customers. This position gives you the opportunity to strengthen communication skills and advance your organizational talent while contributing to our overall goals and mission. Become part of our dynamic team, which operates in Zurich, Lausanne and Goa and focuses on creativity, curiosity and collaboration. Together we master challenges, celebrate successes and continuously develop.

Specifically, you will work on the following topics

  • Support of our experts in evaluation mandates
  • Preparation and verification of property data and documentation
  • (Telephone) customer contact for appointment of visits
  • Support for the optimization of ongoing processes

Your tasks continue to evolve as we grow together.

Qualificates

This is what you bring with you

  • Student/in a university or university of applied sciences (from 1st semester) preferably in the fields of architecture, business administration, business administration or real estate
  • Interest in real estate, property valuation and data management
  • A high degree of commitment and initiative
  • Confident oral and written language skills in German and French
  • Italian skills of advantage

We appreciate not only your previous experience, but above all your ability to learn quickly. So do not worry if your professional career does not correspond to the classic pattern.

About us

SMG Swiss Marketplace Group AG is a pioneering network of online marketplaces and an innovative digital company that simplifies people’s lives with future-oriented products.

SMG Swiss Marketplace Group AG offers customers the best tools to help them make important life decisions. Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
