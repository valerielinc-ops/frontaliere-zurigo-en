Full description

Are you an organizational talent and have a passion for efficient processes? In this position, you can use your administrative skills and actively contribute to the implementation of assortment and marketing strategies. You support the Category Field Manager in the development of contemporary ranges in the sense of the strategy: "The best range, simple, at the best price".

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Supermarket AG, Limmatplatz Zurich, Limmatstrasse 152, 8005 Zurich

Mansioni

  • Monitoring and maintaining master data (articles and suppliers) and ensuring high data quality
  • Preparation of price comparisons, calculations as well as inquiries and contract renewals to ensure a sound basis for decision-making and efficient cooperation with suppliers
  • Obtaining standard offers, creating recurring assortment analyses and system capture and control of actions and activities
  • Ensuring business correspondence and efficient cooperation with suppliers, cooperatives, category management, interfaces, etc.
  • Independent processing of inquiries and complaints from the M-Infoline and Storehelp as well as active support in quality planning and assurance

Requirements

  • First years of professional experience: in the same or similar function
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): Commercial or equivalent training
  • In-depth user knowledge in MS-Office (especially Excel) and SAP
  • Independent and communication-strong working method with an eye for detail
  • Experience ideally with focus on assortment management, master data maintenance or commercial processing
  • Open, friendly and reliable personality
  • German (flowing)
  • English (negotiable)
  • French (advantageous)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central place of work: Central place of work in the city of Zurich (Limmatplatz or near Hardbrücke)
  • Modern infrastructure: Diverse work zones and modern co-working spaces with advanced equipment
  • Migros Bank: Free account management and preferential interest on the private and investment account
  • Mobility: cost sharing in public transport (GA, ZVV Bonus Pass) and Mobility CarSharing
  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation in courses of Migros Club School
  • Design and decision-making leeway: Possibility to independently shape and further develop the field of responsibility

Recruitment process

  • Submit application documents: For a complete application, we need your CV, certificates and diplomas. A photo in the CV and a letter of motivation are not necessary. We live equal opportunities for all applicants. We have a standardized recruitment process. If this does not work for you, mention it in your application dossier.
  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Discussion with case presentation: Specific expertise is examined on the basis of a case or other assessment instruments. After processing, the result of the leader is presented and then discussed together. Usually, the team is also represented at the presentation. As a rule, the conversations are conducted by the superior, without a representative from the HR. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week

Grado di occupazione: 80-100 %
