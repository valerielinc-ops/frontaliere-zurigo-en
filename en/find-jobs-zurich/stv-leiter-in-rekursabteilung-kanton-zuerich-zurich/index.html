Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate BI

  • General Secretariat Head of Recourse Department 80
  • 100% The General Secretariat is the head of the Education Director and covers the areas of buildings, education planning, technical and legal services, finance and digital, communication, staff and the Recourse Department. Here the business and projects of the entire Education Directorate are coordinated. The Recourse Department of the General Secretariat of the Education Directorate mainly handles recourses from various legal areas of the Directorate. To strengthen the existing team of eight people, we are looking for a lawyer as a member of the Recourse Department 80-100% by immediately or by appointment. Tasks
  • As a member of the Recourse Department, you support the Head of Recourse Department in technical, organizational and personnel terms and take over the representation in their absence.
  • Together with the team, you will work on recourses against orders in the responsibility of the Education Directorate.
  • These mainly cover the following areas:
  • entrance examinations and school career decisions in the middle school and vocational training field
  • personnel law of cantonal teachers of the elementary schools as well as of vocational and secondary schools
  • training contributions, state contributions and measures under the Kinder- und Jugendheimgesetz
  • In this versatile and responsible function you lead independent recourse procedures, write decision applications and sign in your own competence the recourse decisions submitted to you by the legal employees for examination.
  • You also contribute to the further development of the Rekurs department, in particular in the optimization of internal processes as well as in the preparation and implementation of the digitization of administrative procedures.
  • Requirements
  • You have a legal university degree, ideally with a lawyer's patent or another supplementary qualification.
  • Thanks to your sound professional experience you have very good knowledge of public law and are able to work in new legal areas in a short time and also solve difficult legal issues.
  • The regulations you have written testify to linguistic skill.
  • You understand how to formulate legal texts clearly, precisely and convincingly.
  • You work independently, with a limited degree of care, you have good organizational capacity and an interest in the digitization of administrative procedures.
  • You are distinguished by political sensitivity, decision-making pleasure, resilience and a safe appearance.
  • If you are also happy to take responsibility, work in a team-oriented manner and keep track of the situation even in challenging situations and at the same time do not lose your sense of humour, you are exactly right for us.
  • We offer -
  • We offer you an interesting and varied job with attractive employment conditions.
  • Your work location is located directly at Zurich Central Station. Contact
  • Information about the job is available to Ewa Surdyka, Head of Recourses Department (043-259 20 69), or Lily-Ann Barraud, Head of Recourses Department (043-259 23 27).
  • We look forward to receiving your complete online application.
  • Applications for job placements and applications via e-mail will not be considered for this vacancy.
  • You will receive further information on the Internet at www.bi. zh.ch.
