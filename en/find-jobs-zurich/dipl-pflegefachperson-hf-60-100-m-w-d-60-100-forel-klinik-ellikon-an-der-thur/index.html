Full description

Forel Klinik is one of the leading addiction clinics in Switzerland and specializes in the treatment of people with alcohol or drug dependence. At two locations, more than 180 employees provide support for those affected on their personal path to a better quality of life and self-determination. We offer individually tailored therapy concepts in the inpatient, daily clinical and outpatient setting. Our addiction treatment combines evidence-based methods with a value-oriented attitude – with the aim of enabling sustainable and effective change. As an addiction specialist clinic, it is anchored in our DNA to open up prospects and contribute to the development of people. This principle also shapes our dealings with employees. We are looking for an agreement for our clinic in Ellikon an der Thur. Create with us paths that work. We offer you a nursing job in which relationship design is at the centre – and in which you support people in breaking new ground. At Forel Klinik, you work in an interdisciplinary, professional and humanly demanding environment. Your commitment makes a difference for people in a stressful phase of life.

Their tasks

Our nursing team creates space and time with high commitment for the successful implementation of therapy and support. Also due to the diversity and a good mixture of age and professional background in the care team many things are possible which you may not have been able to get to know so far. With us, care is on an equal footing with all employees involved in the patient process. Care does not take place at our patient bedside, but in interdisciplinary cooperation with other professional groups. We stand for honest and open communication, where every employee can contribute their ideas and skills that serve the recovery process of our patients.

Your profile

They have training at tertiary level (FH, HF), ideally psychiatric care. We are particularly pleased if you have already gained experience in the field of dependency diseases. They are enthusiastic about actively shaping processes. They are reliable, flexible, honest, open, innovative and resilient. Your knowledge of German is very good in word and writing and you like to use IT tools at work.

Our offer

With this position, we offer you responsible tasks in a motivated, modern and well-connected environment. Your professional and personal development is a concern for us, which we support generously and specifically with structured further education and training offers.

The working conditions are contemporary and appealing

  • Reliable service planning in a stable environment
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
  • 60 % employer contribution to the pension fund
  • 100 % employer contribution to KTG insurance

Your place of work: Our stationary clinic in Ellikon an der Thur is idyllic in the countryside and at the same time offers good accessibility from the Zurich, Winterthur and Eastern Switzerland region and the German border area. Further advantages of the place of work are the in-house gastronomy and sufficient free parking. Our offer is aimed at people of all genders and identities. Applications are also welcome in all professional phases.
