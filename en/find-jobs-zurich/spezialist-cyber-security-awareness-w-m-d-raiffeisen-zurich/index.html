Full description

02.11.2026 Zurich St. Gallen

Become part of our team!

The role is responsible for the design, implementation and continuous improvement of awareness measures in the area of information security. A particular focus is on the effective communication of security risks and the development of a sustainable security culture. In order to strengthen the team based in Zurich Airport (The Circle) or St.Gallen as well as the possibility to work partly in the home office, we are looking for a technically experienced and communicative personality as a specialist in cyber security awareness (m/f/d).

What do you expect?

  • Collaboration in the company-wide cyber security awareness strategy and development of modern learning formats (e-learning, workshops, microlearning, campaigns)
  • Planning and implementation of training and phishing simulations (online & on site)
  • Development of a clear security communication strategy, creation of target group-oriented communication and awareness-raising measures as well as internal communication channels
  • Monitoring and reporting the effectiveness of awareness activities
  • Collaboration with IT-Security, HR, Compliance and Communication as well as advising and supporting departments on regulatory requirements

What do you bring?

  • Completed degree in computer science, marketing, communication or economics
  • Good analytical and conceptual competence, good presentation skills and curiosity for new topics
  • Strong communication capability to present complex content simply and in line with the target group
  • Very good knowledge of German is required, further language skills in French and Italian are advantageous

"In this role, you play a key role in shaping Raiffeisen's safety culture." They enable our employees to identify cyber risks at an early stage and to act correctly. Together with various specialist areas, you will develop effective awareness measures and thus make a decisive contribution to strengthening our resilience.
