Full description

As team leader*in grid construction, you coordinate demanding work on our grid and ensure that our grid construction projects are implemented safely, efficiently and on time.

Tasks

  • You coordinate grid construction projects as well as house and construction connections in the low and medium voltage range and ensure a smooth execution.
  • You lead a team of 4 to 8 network electricians and ensure an efficient organization of daily work.
  • You are responsible for the timely execution of tasks and projects, applying the prescribed safety and quality requirements.
  • Resource planning, work preparation, occupational safety and quality control: You are responsible for this.
  • Zurich is missing the tension? No problem for you! You and your team of network electricians will fix the disturbance immediately both at day and at night.

Profile

  • You have a completed vocational education as a network electricianin EFZ or electroinstallatorin EFZ as well as a further education at the Master's examination, HF or FH.
  • Professional experience in grid construction and knowledge in the field of low and medium voltage are mandatory.
  • An independent, structured and responsible working method distinguishes you as well as your joy in working together in the team.
  • As a committed and resilient personality, you are happy to take responsibility and enjoy coordinating and leading staff.
  • You have the B-card and ideally live in a distance that allows you to quickly get up to date (max. 30min from the Zurich H.F.) for operations in Zurich.

We offer

  • More energy for your life: mobile work, flexible work models and part-time opportunities enable you to actively shape your working time and divide it into the way it suits your life best. For example, you can shop for up to 2 weeks extra holidays and work part-time at all levels. Job I/O/-spli-t and unpaid holiday are also possible with us.
  • Sustainable and discounted travel: With the mobility contribution of up to 600 francs per year, you travel cheaply and sustainably – whether you want to cycle through Zurich by bike or explore Switzerland by train.
  • An investment in your future: We not only value paying fair and transparent wages, but also ensure that you have more of your wages. We take over 60% of the savings contributions and offer an over-compulsory interest on the capital saved.
  • A boost to your leisure time: We pay you 100 francs a month on your lunch check card. You also profit from additional Fringe Bernards worth 150 francs a year and attractive mobile subscriptions.
  • Development and further education: With our talent management, we specifically promote your development – through a wide range of internal and external training opportunities as well as exciting development opportunities.
  • Meaningful work: Sustainability and sun-sustainable work are not just words for us: Join us in shaping Zurich and the future. Let energy be released for a good morning together with us.

About us

ewz has been on the road for over 130 years, dynamic and visionary every day. Whether it's sustainable energy production, smart energy solutions or innovative networking technology, develop with us today what is important tomorrow.
