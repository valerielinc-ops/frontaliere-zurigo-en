What you should know about us

Emil Frey Gruppe is not only a Swiss family-owned company with over 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer focus are the supporting pillars of the company. The focus of its activities is on importing automotive brands, operating garages, the logistics of spare and accessory parts, and providing financial services. Emil Frey Digital AG belongs to Emil Frey Gruppe and is a full-service communications provider for the automotive industry, as well as being responsible for digitalization within Emil Frey Gruppe.

We are building the AI division at Emil Frey

Help us build the AI division at Emil Frey Gruppe. As a newly formed team, we shape the AI strategy and the Operating Model, develop governance standards together with Security, Legal, IT and Data, and support the business units in ensuring that AI has a measurable impact on the business. From the business case and risk analysis to the evaluation of partners and technical solutions. We manage the AI portfolio in Switzerland, lead projects, and build AI agents and automated workflows. Internationally, we ensure transparency regarding the initiatives of the country organizations so that synergies can be leveraged across the entire group. As part of the AI team, you will help build the new division, contribute to decisions, take responsibility for your own projects and shape how AI works at Emil Frey. If you bring a hands-on mentality, technical understanding of AI, a structured approach and a pragmatic mindset, we look forward to receiving your application for:

Product Owner AI Portfolio & Delivery

Your responsibilities

  • Operational management of the intake gate: recording, completeness and plausibility checks of incoming AI initiatives (Epic), and sending them back to the business units with specific questions
  • Plans and maintains the CH AI backlog and prioritizes initiatives according to the predefined prioritization logic
  • Implementation & coordination of the risk classification and requirements matrix
  • Obtaining and following up on specialist approvals (CISO, Legal, IT and Enterprise Architect)
  • Creates classifications for the governance recommendation for specific use cases
  • Implementation & coordination of the initial technical assessment in consultation with the Product Manager
  • Takes on project management for a prioritized AI initiative, from analysis through go-live and measuring success
  • Cross-functional stakeholder management with the business units and specialist functions
  • Creates and maintains the foundational materials and documentation (Confluence) and keeps them up to date
  • Conducts market and vendor analyses in the AI field to provide a basis for decisions
  • Implementation & coordination of process improvements in the AI intake and governance process
  • Review & reporting on the success of implemented projects based on the defined success criteria

Your profile

  • Completed degree (ETH, university, university of applied sciences) in business information systems, business administration, or equivalent further education
  • Min. 3 years of professional experience in (agile) project management of complex IT/digital projects, ideally with AI product experience
  • Leadership skills (operational management of a project team)
  • Strong interest in new technologies and continuously stays up to date Ability to design and build AI agents
  • Willingness and ability to build processes and governance from the ground up
  • Experience in data-driven prioritization and backlog management
  • Good interpersonal skills with strong abilities in the areas of: communication, collaboration, organization, and motivation
  • Pragmatic mindset & ability to recognize connections
  • Business and entrepreneurial thinking
  • Independent, structured person with assertiveness
