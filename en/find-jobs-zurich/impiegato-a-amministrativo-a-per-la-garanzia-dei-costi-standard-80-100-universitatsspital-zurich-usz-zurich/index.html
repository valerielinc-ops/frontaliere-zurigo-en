Full description

USZ Stettbach

immediately or by agreement

You secure the costs for our inpatients and ensure a smooth administrative process. You work closely with insurance companies, authorities and internal bodies. You can expect a versatile task in a committed and professional team.

Your main tasks

  • You provide the complete and correct cost guarantee for our inpatients: women with Swiss insurance
  • You apply for, monitor and admonish cost management languages and coordinate their extensions and reconsideration applications with insurance companies and cantons
  • You check and edit error lists and ensure that missing information is added in a timely manner and that the cost guarantees are complete
  • You clarify patient data directly with insurance, resident controls and cantonal authorities
  • You maintain a professional and solution-oriented exchange with patients: their relatives as well as with insurance companies, authorities and employers – by telephone and in writing
  • With your careful and reliable way of working, you contribute to ensuring the administrative and financial requirements for the inpatient treatment of our patients: inside

Your profile

  • You have a basic commercial education and have several years of professional experience in health and/or social security
  • You know the processes of stationary cost protection and ideally have in-depth knowledge of the relevant legal foundations, in particular KVG, UVG and VVG as well as the DRG system.
  • Medical terminology is familiar to you or you are motivated to acquire appropriate knowledge
  • You work independently, structured and carefully and keep an overview even in complex cases and high work volumes
  • You communicate safely and appropriately – both in writing and orally – and act competently and bindingly towards patients: inside, relatives, insurance companies and authorities
  • You have very good IT user knowledge in MS Office and SAP
  • You are an open, resilient and team-oriented personality who likes to take responsibility and actively participate in a committed environment

Our offer

You can expect a versatile and responsible job in a demanding and dynamic environment of a university hospital. You work in a dedicated team where high quality, mutual support and professional interaction are of great importance. Your work makes an important contribution to a smooth administrative handling of the inpatient treatments and thus indirectly supports optimal care for our patients. Look forward to a varied task with different contacts, professional challenge and space for independent work and personal development. For this post, dossiers from recruiters cannot be considered.
