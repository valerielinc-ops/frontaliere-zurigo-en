This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

We are looking for an experienced Test Manager to join our international team. This challenge will require a good ability to work in a team while been self-organized. We are looking for professionals with in-depth experience in quality assurance, who like to work proactively, contribute with ideas to the change process.

Your key tasks

  • Managing the customer's test campaigns (responsibility for the SIT, contact person for the customer in the UAT)
  • Test execution for SIT, issue resolution supporting other Testers
  • Provision of test reporting for the customer and management
  • Monitoring of defect deliveries and coordination of the developer teams
  • Continuous improvement of the status quo and design of a complete and sustainable test approach
  • Planning automation test requirements

Qualifiche

  • Experience with test management in agile development
  • Strong structuring, negotiation and problem-solving and technical skills
  • Experience with banking, Avaloq technologies
  • High level of empathy when working directly with clients and internal teams
  • Hands-on mentality, healthy pragmatism and customer orientation
  • Very good communication and presentation skills with fluency in English
