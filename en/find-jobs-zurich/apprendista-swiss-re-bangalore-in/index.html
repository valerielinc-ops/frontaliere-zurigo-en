About the Team

You will be a part of a highly motivated and experienced team of Property & Casualty (P&C) Reinsurance business operations, handling end-to-end administration of reinsurance contracts. We have a strong link to the other P&C Reinsurance teams and collaborate daily to deliver best service and most value to our clients.

About the Role: Key responsibilities

  • Responsible for efficient and high-quality administrative handling of the reinsurance business of clients.
  • Provide efficient support to client markets, UW and pricing teams.
  • Act as a business-expert for Swiss RE Administrative tools, process and guidelines, maintain the methodology on process.
  • Manage assigned tasks effectively to achieve agreed service/business standards
  • Contact internal business partners to resolve any long pending operational issues
  • Any other ad-hoc/admin related tasks as required from time to time that are deemed appropriate for this role

About you

  • Minimum bachelor's degree in finance, Accounting, Insurance, or related fields.
  • Good written and verbal interpersonal skills in English
  • Proficiency in MS Office tools, especially MS Excel and MS PowerPoint
  • Proactive attitude, self-motivated, team player and ability to learn
  • Flexibility in work timings
