Full description

Due to our continuous growth, we are looking for a customer-oriented and independent personality for the internal sales service Building Automation to strengthen our sales team. In this varied function, you are the central hub between sales, engineering and service. Your future workplace is flexibly located at one of our locations in Switzerland.

You can move that with us.

  • Development of offers in cooperation with sales field service for building automation, KNX and energy management
  • Development of solutions and variants in cooperation with the field service
  • Conduct of technical clarifications
  • Support sales, engineering and service department in organizational and administrative matters
  • Support in procurement discussions with the customer or specialist planner
  • Processing of technical and administrative inquiries of our customers
  • Collection and maintenance of data as well as compilation of the complete offer documentation

That's what makes you.

  • Basic technical training in building, electrical or automation technology supplemented with further training as a technical merchant or HF
  • In-depth professional experience in a similar function in the field of building automation
  • Good user knowledge of MS Office and Salesforce are beneficial
  • Service-oriented, communicative and team-capable personality with rapid comprehension
  • Style-confident knowledge of German in words and writing

That's what we offer you.

  • Multi-faceted work with high personal responsibility
  • Opportunity for hybrid work
  • Co-shaping further growth in building automation
  • Working in a growing and future-oriented market environment around building automation and energy management
  • Development opportunities in sales
  • Modern work equipment
  • Contemporary working time models (40-hour week at 100%)
  • Very attractive social benefits and employment conditions
  • Internal and external training opportunities
