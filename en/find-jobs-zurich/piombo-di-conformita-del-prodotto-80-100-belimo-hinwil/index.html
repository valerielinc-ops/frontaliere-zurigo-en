Full description

Introduction

Take the lead in coordinating product certifications and approvals that enable continued access to global markets. You will manage certification activities, compliance documentation and data, collaborate with external certification partners, and help improve and digitalize compliance processes.

Key Responsibilities

  • Plan, coordinate, and track product certifications, approvals, registrations, and renewals across international markets.
  • Maintain declarations, certificates, test reports, approval records, and other compliance documentation.
  • Manage compliance data and regulatory information in PLM, SAP, and document management systems.
  • Coordinate certification impact assessments and recertification activities following product, software, manufacturing, or supplier changes.
  • Serve as the operational contact for certification bodies, test laboratories, and regulatory authorities.
  • Monitor certification milestones, certificate validity, renewals, and regulatory deliverables to support uninterrupted market access.
  • Prepare compliance evidence for internal and external audits, inspections, and customer requests, and maintain dashboards, KPIs, and status reports.
  • Drive the standardization, optimization, and digitalization of certification and compliance processes.

Required Expertise

  • Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Technical Administration, Quality Management, Business Administration, or a related discipline.
  • Proven experience coordinating product certification or compliance activities across multiple products or international markets.
  • Experience working with certification bodies and external testing laboratories.
  • Strong organizational and project coordination skills, with the ability to manage several certification activities simultaneously.
  • Experience with SAP and document management tools; familiarity with PLM environments is advantageous.
  • Technical affinity and an interest in regulated products; additional training in certification, quality management, or regulatory affairs is a plus.
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management skills.
  • Very good English skills
  • German is an advantage. Reliable, self-organized, and eager to learn.

About Belimo

Belimo is the global market leader in the development, production, and sales of field devices for the energy-efficient control of heating, ventilation and air-conditioning systems. The focus of our core business is on damper actuators, control valves, sensors and meters. Founded in 1975, the company has been listed on the Swiss Exchange (SIX) since 1995.
