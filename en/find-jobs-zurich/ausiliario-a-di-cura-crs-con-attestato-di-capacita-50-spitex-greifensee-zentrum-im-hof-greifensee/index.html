Full description

On behalf of the municipality of Greifensee, the Stiftung Zentrum im Hof provides comprehensive care in the areas of living and care in old age. The modern and brightly designed house is located near the historic town and lake. Connected to the center are three inpatient foster housing groups with 28 residents and 12 age-appropriate apartments. Also affiliated to the foundation is the public Spitex of the municipality of Greifensee. To complement our team in Spitex, we are looking for 1. December or by arrangement an accomplished and reliable Nursing assistant/in SRC with qualification, 50 % which places our clients at the center of their commitment.

Your profile

  • Several years of professional experience in long-term care
  • High social competence and reliability
  • Joy and interest in the care of elderly, acute and chronically ill people
  • Interest in domestic work
  • Readiness for shared services as well as min. 1 weekend per month
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • You have your own car or drive velo

We offer you

  • A varied and demanding task
  • A careful introduction
  • A dedicated, appreciative team
  • A fair remuneration according to cantonal guidelines
  • A beautiful and modern furnished workplace with mind. 5 weeks holiday
  • Training as needed

Do you feel addressed? We would like to meet you.
