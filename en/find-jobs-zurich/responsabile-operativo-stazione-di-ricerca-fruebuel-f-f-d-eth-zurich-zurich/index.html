Operations manager: in the research station Früebüel (m/f/d)

100%, Zurich, temporary

ETH Zurich is one of the world's leading universities for natural sciences and technology. The Institute of Agricultural Sciences in the Department of Environmental Systems Sciences (D-USYS) deals with animal husbandry, plant sciences and economics in the global and Swiss context of agriculture and carries out relevant basic and application-oriented research.

We are looking for a committed person (workload 100%) with pleasure in versatile agricultural work in a committed environment of researchers. This position is to be filled from 01.01.2027 or by agreement. It shall be limited to an initial period of 12 months; there is a possibility that the post will be changed to permanent employment in the short to medium term.

Job description

Its main task is the practical and administrative management of the agricultural operation of the research station Früebüel. They manage agricultural employees, take responsibility for management dates, coordinate the purchase and sale of animals and other agricultural goods and coordinate experiments on the farm together with researchers. In addition, you are involved in the conceptual and operational development of the company. They are instrumental in establishing the research location of the ETH for agricultural livestock and biodiversity in the Zugerberg moorland.

Profile

  • You preferably have the training in agronomy / agricultural sciences (FH, ETH) or are experienced:r farmer:in with master examination (HF) and experience in personnel management
  • They are resilient, flexible, creative, enjoy interdisciplinary projects and are open to new things
  • Physical work outdoors and with agricultural benefits will give you pleasure
  • You work with your own initiative reliably and responsibly

We offer

  • Become part of ETH Zurich, which not only supports your professional development, but also actively contributes to positive changes in society.
  • We are committed to a sustainable and climate-neutral university.
  • You can expect numerous perks, such as public transport subscriptions and car sharing, a wide range of sports offers from the ASVZ, child care and attractive pension services.

Diversity and sustainability are important to us
