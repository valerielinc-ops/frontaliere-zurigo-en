Full description

Personal – competent – close Welcome to the Hospital Uster, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As the central pillar of health care in the Oberland region of Zurich and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you experience the highest level of expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and promoted in your professional development. Spital Uster is a teaching hospital of the Medical Faculty of the University of Zurich. Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of a “regional and digital health hub in Züri East”! Are you looking for an education that is varied and brings new things every day? Then you are exactly right with us!

Exciting challenges await you

  • Preparation of food with modern cooking techniques and tools
  • Dealing with nutritional, economic and ecological principles
  • Cooking of high quality and creative dishes
  • Presenting food in compliance with strict hygiene guidelines

What sets you apart for the job

  • Completed elementary school (secondary school A or B), rapid comprehension, mathematical understanding, as well as good knowledge of German in words and writing.
  • Pronounced sense of taste, pleasure in dealing with food, sense of order and organizational talent, good health (no food or contact allergies), pleasure in teamwork
  • Willingness to work on weekends and public holidays
  • Trial internship, 5 days after consultation, is mandatory

4 reasons to work with us

  • Close accompaniment by the professional and practical trainees who accompany and support you in your learning process
  • A dedicated and happy team
  • Own staff restaurant with benefits
  • Good accessibility with public transport and ZVV-BonusPass

Full online application

  • CV with photo
  • Certificates from 7th school year
  • Testimonials
