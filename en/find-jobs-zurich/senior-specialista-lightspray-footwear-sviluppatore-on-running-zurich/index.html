Your Story:
  • 5+ years of progressive experience in footwear development, with a significant focus on standard inline creation processes.
  • Knowledge of footwear manufacturing processes, including tooling, molding, injecting, welding, lasting, and assembly.
  • You understand factory capabilities, tolerances, and limitations, and know how to design for scalable replication.
  • A natural technical curiosity and a robust, root-cause analysis approach to problem-solving.
  • In-depth, demonstrated knowledge of advanced footwear materials, their properties, and performance characteristics, including experience with advanced testing and analysis.
  • Exceptional communication, negotiation, and influencing skills, with the ability to confidently present and defend concepts to senior management and external partners.
  • A demonstrated ability to work with a high degree of autonomy, navigating ambiguity,y and making independent judgments on complex and significant issues.

Company and context

  • Lead and own the end-to-end creation of SMUs and Special projects within the creation organization.
  • You will manage multiple Lightspray projects independently, from initial strategy and conception through to product sign-off, ensuring alignment with cross-functional teams to ensure company goals.
  • Through the lens of Special Projects, you will leverage your expertise in manufacturing processes, material properties, and Lightspray product construction to develop a product aligned with our strategy.
  • With a strong root-cause analysis mindset, you will tackle challenges in tooling, molding, and assembly, and develop new materials, colors, and new product specifications.
