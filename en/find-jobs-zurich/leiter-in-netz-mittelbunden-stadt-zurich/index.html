Full description

As Head of the Central Grisons Grid, you are responsible for the safe operation of the distribution grid in Central Grisons and further develop your team and our grid infrastructure.

Responsibilities

  • You provide technical and personnel leadership for the Central Grisons Grid division and specifically develop the employees.
  • You ensure the safe, economical and timely operation, maintenance and implementation of the systems in the medium- and low-voltage grid and provide services for third parties.
  • As a delegated asset operator, you are responsible for the safe, economical and timely operation of the grid infrastructure.
  • You represent ewz to authorities, customers and other stakeholders and promote an actively practiced safety culture.
  • You are responsible for the organizational cost center and continuously develop processes and procedures.

Profile

  • You have several years of leadership and professional experience in grid operations or maintenance, as well as higher technical education (HF, FH, university or ETH) in electrical engineering or energy engineering.
  • You have sound knowledge of the operation and maintenance of medium- and low-voltage networks, as well as ideally experience as a plant operator.
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and experience in managing budgets, projects and resources set you apart.
  • Your high leadership, communication and decision-making skills enable you to collaborate effectively with different stakeholders.
  • Your willingness to perform on-call duty rounds off your profile.

We offer

  • More energy for your life: Through mobile working, flexible work models and part-time options, you can actively shape your working hours and arrange them in the way that best suits your life. For example, you can purchase up to 2 additional weeks of vacation and work part-time at all levels. Job sharing/job splitting and unpaid leave are of course also possible with us.
  • Travel sustainably and affordably: With a mobility allowance of up to 600 francs per year, you can travel affordably and sustainably – whether you want to cycle or explore Switzerland by train.
  • An investment in your future: We not only value paying fair and transparent salaries, but also ensure that more of your salary remains with you. We cover 60% of the savings contributions and also offer an above-mandatory interest rate on the accumulated capital.
  • An energy boost for your leisure time: We pay you 100 francs per month as a meal allowance. In addition, you benefit from further fringe benefits worth 150 francs per year and attractive mobile phone plans.
  • Development and continuing education: Through our talent management, we specifically support your development – through a wide range of internal and external continuing education opportunities as well as exciting development opportunities.
  • Meaningful work: Sustainability and meaningful work are not just words for us: Help shape Zürich and the future with us. Together with us, unleash energy for a better tomorrow.

ewz has been dynamic and visionary every day for over 130 years. Whether sustainable energy production, smart energy solution or innovative networking technology: Develop with us today what will be important tomorrow.
