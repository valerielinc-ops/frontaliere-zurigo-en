Full description

Are you looking for a varied and responsible job in an established company in the electrical industry? Does the idea of expanding your special knowledge in complex projects on the job electrify you? Then you are golden with us! Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for a committed person like you – your work makes our customers fans. That's what you do.

  • You carry out high- and low-power installations in commercial, industrial and residential buildings and are responsible for new installations on electrical and telecommunications systems.
  • In addition, you control existing installations and carry out measurements and tests to find and correct possible causes of faults or errors.
  • By the way, you pass on your valuable expertise to our learners. This will make it easier for you to get started.
  • After completing your apprenticeship as an electrical installer: in EFZ or equivalent training, you have preferably already gained several years of professional experience in a comparable function.
  • It gives you great pleasure to put your clientele at the center of your actions and thinking and to do a high-quality work.
  • You feel comfortable in contact with the customer and appreciate a job in which you can work independently and switch flexibly between various tasks. This is what awaits you
  • You are important to us - we offer you an open, appreciative and family environment.
  • We are successful - with us you have a long-term and secure perspective.
  • We take part in our success – with 5 weeks of holidays and bonuses.
  • With us the 4.5 day week applies (Friday afternoon free)
  • Competent managers ensure exciting projects and functioning workflows.
  • With us you have a wide range of training opportunities – we are happy to support you. Still questions?
