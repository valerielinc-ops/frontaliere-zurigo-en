Full description

The EMIL EGGER Group is the leading provider of heavy goods logistics in Switzerland. In the areas of crane work, special transports, industrial removals and rental lifts, more than 230 employees are employed. With the comprehensive special know-how, individual and innovative customer solutions are developed every day. For our location in Bassersdorf, we are looking for a flexible and team-oriented personality to reinforce our team with exceptional transport support. You like to travel, can take on responsibility and do not shy away from challenges? Then come with us! Your tasks

  • Accompanying special and exceptional transport throughout Switzerland – including overnight stays away
  • Function as “police substitute” / Security and rest person in road traffic
  • If necessary, as a chauffeur in heavy goods and special transport
  • Daily new, demanding operational situations with high responsibility

You bring with you

  • ATB training (exceptional transport accompaniment) mandatory
  • Cat C/E driving licence
  • Several years of experience as a chauffeur in special and heavy transport
  • Self-employed, reliable and responsible work
  • High load capacity and flexibility

We offer

  • Exciting tasks in an innovative, dynamic company
  • The ATB accompanying vehicle may be taken home.
  • State-of-the-art vehicles and equipment that make driving fun
  • Fair conditions, attractive social benefits and free parking
  • A friendly, motivated team with a real you culture
  • Flexible admission – by arrangement

Sounds like your new job? Then get in, show what you can and become part of our strong team!
