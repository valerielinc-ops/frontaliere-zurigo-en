Mission Your playing field on time: Decathlon Zurich Become part of our team at the Decathlon Store in Selnau! Directly on our doorstep are the Sihl and the train directly on the Uetliberg – the gateway to the best skiing and winter hiking trails in the city and the direct connection to the Swiss mountains. This is where our customers experience their outdoor adventures. We are looking for temporary reinforcements (70%, until February) to equip locals and tourists with the perfect equipment for snow, mountains and wintry city runs. Your daily action on the sales floor You are not looking for an 08/15 desk job, but want to share your passion for sports? This is what awaits you:

  • In the middle of the action: Your everyday life takes place 100% on the sales floor. You advise beginners: inside and professionals and find the perfect equipment.
  • Taking action: From incoming goods to the attractive presentation of goods ("facing") to the lightning-fast preparation of click & collect orders.
  • Living a circular economy: You not only "sell" but also inspire our sustainable rental and second-life offers. This job is NOTHING for you if...
  • You prefer to sit quietly behind a screen all day.
  • It is difficult for you to approach people openly and actively.
  • You think unpacking pallets and putting up shelves is “not your job.”
  • You would rather have free Saturdays. Profile that you bring with you
  • Athlete DNA & Maker mentality: You are active, courageous and feel like tackling it every day.
  • Customer focus: Excellent manners and a real sense of service.
  • Language competence: Perfect German (dialect understanding is a plus). Good English or French skills are a great bonus. This is what we offer you (at 100% Pensum)
  • Attractive salary: A fixed gross salary between CHF 4,100 and CHF 5,000 (paid out in 13 monthly wages) plus a monthly team bonus (up to 10% of the salary).
  • Decathlon discount: 25% discount on all our products.
  • Your foot in the door: Even as a temporary team member, you gain valuable experience in a fast-growing, global sports brand – who knows what results from it! The application process: Fast & uncomplicated No long letter of motivation necessary! Just send us your CV, answer a few short questions and do our 30-minute Personality Check (AssessFirst). Then we meet directly in the store to see if the chemistry is right. Practical information:
  • Contract type: Temporary until February
  • Job opening: By December or by appointment
  • Location: Zurich Selnau, ZH Ready for the challenge? Then click on "Apply now" and start with us!
