Full description

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, participate and grow with us. Become part of our family and shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us.

Tasks

  • Every day you ensure that all shipments arrive on time with private and corporate customers – reliable and well organized
  • You plan the vehicle utilization cleverly and always keep an eye on ecological and economic aspects
  • You are the point of contact for our drivers and are in constant exchange so that they can concentrate fully on the road
  • You collect and process transport orders and advise our customers with friendliness and a pronounced service idea
  • In addition to scheduling, you also like to do other administrative work and help with exciting projects
  • You promote a close cooperation with superiors and work colleagues, according to the motto "one for all, all for one"

Profile

  • You have completed an apprenticeship and bring experience from the transport or logistics industry
  • Shift work between 04:00 and 22:00? No problem for you, you appreciate the variety
  • Your excellent geography skills make your job easier
  • PC, phone, chat – there you are nimble like a weasel and serve all means of communication with links
  • Your German skills in words and writing are impeccable, other foreign languages are an advantage
  • You work reliably, accurately and quickly adapt to new challenges
  • Even if it gets stressful, you stay cool and keep track.

Benefits

  • With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • With us you start with 5 weeks vacation and benefit from the 5th year of service even from 6 weeks!
  • Do you love cool events? We can celebrate!

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
