24.09.2026 Andelfings

Become part of our team!

To strengthen our team, we are looking for 1. December 2026 or by agreement a committed and customer-oriented personality as corporate client consultant (m/f/d).

What do you expect?

  • Professional and needs-based advice and support for corporate and real estate customers
  • Acquisition and expansion of an existing customer portfolio
  • Independent credit check and development of financing concepts for corporate and real estate customers
  • Active mediation of business opportunities in other areas of our bank

What do you bring?

  • Banking training with experience in banking, in particular in advising corporate and real estate clients
  • Know-how in the area of financing, both in the case of real estate financing (returnables and commercial objects) and in the case of operational financing
  • Negotiation skills and final strength
  • Interest in network building in the Weinland region
