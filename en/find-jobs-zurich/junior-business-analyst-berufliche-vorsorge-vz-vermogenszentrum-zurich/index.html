Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We create transparency in the banking and insurance market and advise our customers comprehensively and neutrally. As we are growing strongly, we are looking for reinforcements at our headquarters in Zurich.

  • You will deal intensively with our operational processes and processes and develop a deep understanding of how our day-to-day business works.
  • You will analyze existing processes, identify potential for improvement and derive optimization and automation approaches from them. You work closely with employees and specialists in operations and process management.
  • You accept optimization requests from the specialist areas, question and structure them and develop clear technical requirements and solutions from them.
  • You act as a link between operations and process management. For larger process and system adjustments, you prepare the requirements from a professional point of view and support them in their implementation. Smaller optimizations are independently and pragmatically implemented.
  • You support the standardization, simplification and digitalization of processes and thus contribute to the continuous development of our area.
  • Completed degree in business informatics, business administration or a comparable field
  • Initial professional experience in the field of business analysis, further development of processes and digital solutions or experience in occupational provision
  • Enjoy operational processes and complex processes
  • Analytical and structured thinking and a sense for simpler, more efficient and digital processes
  • Enjoyment of conceptual work, high quality awareness, initiative and solution-oriented working method
  • A versatile role directly at the interface between operational processes and digitalization in the field of occupational pensions.
  • The opportunity not only to analyze processes, but also to help shape improvements in a concrete way and to immediately see their impact in day-to-day business.
  • Plenty of room for initiative and the opportunity to introduce and implement your own ideas for improvement.
  • An appreciative environment with flat hierarchies and short decision paths
  • Individual promotion and support for further education
  • A dynamic working environment in which you can gradually assume responsibility and further develop your skills in business analysis, process optimization and digitalization.
