Full description

Take responsibility for your own customer portfolio and actively shape processes.

You want to work independently and take responsibility for your own debtor portfolio? In this versatile function, you ensure that our customers pay their invoices on time, make credit decisions within defined competencies and work closely with sales, customers and internal stakeholders. At the same time, you will have the opportunity to contribute your expertise, further develop processes and participate in exciting special topics in the area of Credit & Collection.

Your profile

  • Completed commercial vocational training, further education in finance or accounting.
  • Several years of experience in credit management, collection, debt management, debt collection or finance.
  • Negotiable knowledge of German and good knowledge of French. English and/or Italian skills are beneficial.
  • Good knowledge of SAP S/4HANA and common Microsoft Office applications.
  • High customer and solution orientation as well as a secure presence in dealing with different contacts.
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method with a strong team orientation.
