We are looking for reinforcement immediately.

Tasks

We are looking for a housekeeping assistance for the kitchen of the lower and middle level care of the day school Nordstrasse. The amount is 39 %. You work 4 days (Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday) during the school weeks, during the school holidays (13 weeks) you have free time.

  • cooperation in the kitchen business in the pavilion Nordstrasse
  • Preparation of lunches for 160 children and staff under the direction of the cook
  • Preparation of the Zvieris
  • Creating in dining rooms (lower and middle school children)
  • Cleaning the kitchen, kitchen equipment and furniture
  • Assistance in receiving, controlling and storing food supplies

Profile

  • Practical experience in the field of gastro and/or care at a school
  • Good physical constitution and willingness to wash off and clean the kitchen
  • Mentally resilient
  • You can work well in a large team and make arrangements
  • They speak and understand German well. You can read German and write short emails in German.

We offer

a varied, versatile, independent and responsible activity and a salary according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich.

About us

The day school Nordstrasse is located in Wipkingen and currently comprises three kindergarten classes and 4 multi-year classes at the lower and intermediate levels. Around 230 children attend our school. Through our close cooperation, we create a climate of trust in which development is possible for all children and adults.
