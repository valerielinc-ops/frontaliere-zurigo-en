Full description

Become part of our team Care School Sihlfeld! We are looking for an advisory or specialist assistance immediately or by agreement

Tasks

  • Care of children of all levels at the lunch table and in the offers of the Day School Open Care Offer (OBA) and Individual Learning Time (ILZ)
  • Management of own and cross-group care offers during school holidays
  • Close cooperation with the students, carers and teachers for the day school offers
  • Educational and interdisciplinary cooperation in the school team
  • Administrative and operational tasks
  • Implementation of the educational objectives of the school
  • Working Days and Working Hours: 39% excluding holiday hoarding each Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday 11.30 – 16.00h 50% Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday 11.30 – 16.00h in regular school with approximately 5 weeks of holiday hoarding care

Profile

  • Degree in social pedagogy/social work FH/HF or comparable training for foreign diplomas must be accompanied by Swiss diploma recognition
  • Completion of professional care EFZ
  • Enjoy working with students and in a team
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Motivation, openness, resilience
  • Cooperative, appreciative and respectful leadership style
  • Flexibility as well as very good team and communication skills

We offer

  • A multicultural, exciting environment
  • Competent support from the management
  • a contemporary employment according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich
  • varied, versatile and independent work

Tensions in the heart of Züri - there(s) are me! Every day at the pulse of education and where our future is growing and learning.
