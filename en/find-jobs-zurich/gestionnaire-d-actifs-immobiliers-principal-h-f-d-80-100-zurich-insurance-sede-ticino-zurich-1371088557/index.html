Full description

136602, 80 - 100%, Oerlikon, Zurich, Hybrid

Does the world of real estate fascinate you and want to become part of one of Switzerland's leading asset managers?

Zurich Invest AG offers you the opportunity to work in a dynamic and interdisciplinary team with clear growth ambitions. With a real estate portfolio of around 14 billion Swiss francs and 500 properties held directly in various Swiss investment products, you can contribute your expertise and initiative to shape success together with us.

That's what awaits you

  • You work as a Senior Asset Manager in an interdisciplinary team consisting of portfolio and asset managers as well as building trustees and actively contribute to the joint success
  • You are responsible for the success-oriented and proactive asset management of a portfolio throughout Switzerland including Romandie and implements innovative strategies
  • You take over customer reporting, accompany the reassessments and are responsible for the budgeting and forecasting process to ensure optimal performance
  • You define, identify potential and develop business plans in Immopac that you implement and continuously develop
  • You hire and take care of external service providers such as management, marketers and lawyers to ensure the highest quality and efficiency

That's what makes you feel

  • Degree in architecture, business administration, engineering, finance or law, as well as relevant further education (e.g. IFZ, CUREM)
  • At least 5 years of asset management experience in the management of investment properties, residential, office and retail, all over Switzerland with an institutional investor
  • Expressed analytical skills, experience in customer reporting and evaluation
  • Initiative, independence, decision-making, willingness to perform and assert
  • Excellent German and good, tried and tested French skills
