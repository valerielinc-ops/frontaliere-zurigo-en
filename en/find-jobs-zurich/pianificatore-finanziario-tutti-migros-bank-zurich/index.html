Full description

With us, you can live out your enthusiasm for financial planning every day. You help our customers make better decisions for their pension. We are looking for you: a personality that focuses on individual solutions for our customers.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Bank, Zurich branch - Seidengasse, Seidengasse 12, 8001 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Development of tailor-made financial planning in close cooperation with our regional financial planning specialists (Sales)
  • Support of sales in subject-specific questions including specialist coaching
  • Conduct of specialist presentations at customer events
  • Participation in the development of financial planning services

Requirements

  • two to three years: financial planning
  • Eidg. Diploma: Financial planning
  • Networked thinking and conceptual and analytical skills
  • Initiative, flexible personality with an exact working method
  • Great understanding of individual customer needs
  • Enjoy the development of needs-based comprehensive financial planning for natural persons
  • Tool affinity (especially Allfina)
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • Italian (negotiable)
  • French (advantageous)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Advantages of cadre: One week holidays per year more for cadre members
  • Child care: up to CHF 300 per month per child to external child care
  • Flexible Workplace / Home Office: Individual possibilities for Home Office
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Migros Bank: accounts, mortgages and financial services with preferential terms
  • Du-Culture: Lived team spirit with transparent communication
  • Flat hierarchies: Permeable hierarchies and cross-level exchange

Contatto

Application and contact Moritz Forrer Team Leader HR Business Partner

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Personality/performance test: Under certain conditions, we use a personality test (BIP, Scales test, etc.) as an additional step to support selection. The results of the test may be discussed personally and form the basis for further selection. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Discussion with case presentation: Specific expertise is examined on the basis of a case or other assessment instruments. After processing, the result of the leader is presented and then discussed together. Usually, the team is also represented at the presentation. As a rule, the conversations are conducted by the superior, without a representative from the HR. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
