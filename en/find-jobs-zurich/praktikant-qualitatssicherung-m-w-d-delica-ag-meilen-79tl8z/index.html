Full description

Start your career in quality assurance and ensure that our products meet the highest standards! Support our team in food safety and quality control. Together we create daily moments of enjoyment for our customers. Become part of our innovative team and see how we make great things happen!

Mansioni

  • You will gain insight and cooperation in various activities within quality assurance.
  • You will complete project-based tasks in the areas of food safety, quality control and laboratory.
  • You support the QA departments in day-to-day business, e.g. in carrying out laboratory analyses and monitoring sensory controls as well as resetting patterns.
  • You create and revise concepts, work instructions and training.

Requirements

  • First professional experience beneficial: Preferred in the food industry
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): In the field of food science or a comparable study
  • Basic knowledge of HACCP
  • How to use Microsoft Office
  • Openness, good communication skills and an exact working method
  • in German (negotiation certain)
