Full description

The Swiss family business, founded in 1836, is now one of the most renowned confectioneries in Europe with its exquisite range. The products from Sprüngli stand for the best quality, unique freshness and naturalness. The perfect creations delight customers from all over the world every day.

Baker-confectioner in the pastry department (100%)

100%, Dietikon

Working at Sprüngli

In our factory in Dietikon we are looking for an experienced, motivated and committed person for the pastry department from November 1st, 2026 or by appointment

What you do with us

You work in our bakery in our factory in Dietikon and work as a team to carefully produce our popular yeast and puff pastry products. Your regular position in the bakery is the early shift with work starting at 4 a.m. or 5 a.m. and ending between 1 p.m. and 2 p.m. Thanks to your interest and flexibility, you will also support the entire team in other positions if necessary, for example in the late shift or at the notice post. Saturday assignments are also possible in high season.

Your tasks

  • Handcrafted production of our yeast and puff pastry products
  • Production of our various small breads
  • Production of our seasonal items such as Christmas stollen and Easter products
  • Careful processing of high-quality raw materials according to our proven recipes
  • Ensuring our high quality and hygiene standards
  • Active participation in the varied day-to-day business of the bakery
  • Participation in optimizing our products and manufacturing processes

What we wish for

  • Apprenticeship qualification as a baker-confectioner EFZ or EBA
  • Enjoy the job you have learned and enjoy working in a team
  • An exact and quality-conscious way of working as well as resilience even in hectic situations
  • An open and communicative personality with good physical condition and technical skills
  • Mobility and flexibility
  • Driving license category B
  • PC knowledge
  • Good knowledge of German

You can look forward to that

With us you can expect a diverse and challenging range of tasks in a lively, modern environment. You work in a motivated team with an open “you” culture and real cohesion.

  • 10 minutes paid changing time
  • Work clothing including cleaning
  • An extra day off in your birthday month
  • 5 weeks vacation
  • Free parking space
  • Generous shopping discounts
  • Catering options on site

Attractive night allowances

You benefit from a particularly attractive regulation for operations at night: – 40% wage supplement in cash form between 10 p.m. and 4 a.m – 10% time credit for hours worked between 10 p.m. and 5 a.m

Strong social benefits

– The employer voluntarily covers the full premium for daily sickness benefit insurance – The costs of occupational accident insurance in accordance with the UVG are borne entirely by the employer
