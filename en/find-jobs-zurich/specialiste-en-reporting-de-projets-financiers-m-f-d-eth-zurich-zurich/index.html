Specialist in financial project reporting (m/f/d) 100%, Zurich, permanent print Print Within the Vice President Finance & Controlling, the Financial Third-Party Funding Reporting section acts as a central specialist office for financial reporting to national and international donors. We provide competent support to academic units and central administrative bodies in all questions of financial project reporting. Do you love numbers, think in processes and keep an overview even with complex project reporting? Would you like to actively contribute to ensuring that research projects are carried out in a financially clean, transparent and compliant manner? Then an exciting challenge awaits you here - with a lot of personal responsibility and creative freedom as a specialist in financial project reporting (m/f/d). Job description In this role, you are the central contact person for ETH-internal customers regarding the financial aspects of third-party funded projects. You provide competent advice to professors and project managers and ensure that financial reports are created precisely, on time and in accordance with the rules. One focus of your work is the financial reporting of SNSF research projects. You will create a large number of financial reports semi-automatically, reliably and in high quality within clear deadlines. You work in a structured and precise manner, ensure clean documentation and handle the processes efficiently. Despite the high volume, you maintain an overview, identify discrepancies, carefully carry out the necessary checks and ensure compliance with the applicable ETH regulations as well as the SNSF funding and implementation regulations. You will also prepare financial reports for other national and international funding agencies such as Innosuisse or the European Commission. You actively maintain contact with all departments involved, coordinate processes and ensure smooth and timely billing. You will also be involved in the preparation and implementation of information events and support the continuous development and digitalization of our financial processes - including with innovative approaches, for example through automation or the use of new tools.
