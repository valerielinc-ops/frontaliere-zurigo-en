Full description

Put to the Point

Are you looking for a challenge in the unique ICT environment of the Swiss Army? You play an active role in shaping tomorrow’s airspace surveillance systems and thus make a significant contribution to the digitalization and innovation of the Swiss Army’s system landscape. In the area of air traffic control systems, you can expect a unique and exciting system landscape in a time-critical, highly secure and robust ICT environment.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-19961
  • Location: 8600 Dübendorf

You can make this contribution

  • Share responsibility for the current and future airspace surveillance systems of the Swiss Army
  • Ensure stable, safe and sustainable operation through technical expertise and continuous development
  • Daily responsibility for the operation of the systems, including continuous monitoring, initial analysis and coordination of troubleshooting.
  • Advise and support internal customers in the analysis and resolution of disruptions
  • Company interests represented in projects and support across all phases
  • Pikettdienst (at intervals of about 5 weeks, each during one week)

This makes you unique

  • Higher Technical School (HF) / University of Applied Sciences (FH) in the field of computer science, telematics, electronics or a comparable education with practical experience in computer science
  • Rapid comprehension and pronounced analytical thinking skills
  • Ability to design, develop and implement new solutions combined with deep and networked expertise
  • Team-oriented, helpful and committed personality with a structured and independent working method and high commitment
  • Air traffic control, VoIP or HF experiences desirable

We offer this

  • We are committed to the success model Switzerland and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine the enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, state-of-the-art technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Army and our partners in the Swiss Security Association. The Kdo Cy thus gives the Swiss army the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a safe Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.

Additional information

Home office is limited. Employment with the Swiss Army offers meaning, stability and numerous advantages. Further information can be found under armee.ch/ Vorteile.

Women are still underrepresented in our administrative unit. Your applications are therefore particularly welcome.
