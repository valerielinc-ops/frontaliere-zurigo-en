Full description

Become part of our team

As a global company that pursues very clear growth goals, we need employees who want to advance themselves and the company. For our Rümlang location, we are looking for you immediately or by appointment as a property consultant for access solutions in the East region (m/f). Whether you come from sales, building technology or architecture: what matters is your joy in identifying projects early, building networks and inspiring customers with holistic access solutions.

Your tasks

  • Acquiring new customers and building sustainable customer relationships in your sales area
  • Active processing of new construction projects in the commercial property business
  • Advice and support for architects, general contractors (GU), specialist planners and other decision-makers and influencers
  • Development and placement of access control and locking system solutions in construction projects
  • Development of tenders and technical concepts in collaboration with planners and builders
  • Accompanying projects from the planning phase to awarding
  • Maintaining and expanding a strong network within the construction and planning industry
  • Close collaboration with internal and external sales as well as the project teams

Your profile

  • Entrepreneurial personality with a high level of initiative and a strong customer orientation. Enthusiasm for construction and building technology as well as for innovative access solutions
  • Experience in sales, project acquisition or consulting on construction projects
  • ideally knowledge of access control, locking systems, security technology or related trades
  • Confident appearance towards architects, specialist planners, general contractors and investors
  • Further training in sales, marketing or a technical area (certificate, HF or FH is an advantage)
  • Very good knowledge of German, other languages ​​are an advantage
  • Independent, structured and goal-oriented way of working

What we offer

  • Best prospects in an internationally successful company with a family corporate culture
  • A motivated and competent team that looks forward to your support
  • Exciting and varied projects in the property business
  • High level of personal responsibility and creative freedom in the sales area
  • Modern work equipment and attractive home office options
  • Attractive salary package with performance-oriented components
  • Transparent and clearly defined development opportunities

Who we are For every place that matters. From automatic doors to cloud-based access management, as a global leader in access solutions for schools, banks, airports, hospitals, hotels and more, we enable safe and sustainable places where people can move seamlessly. With over 160 years of experience and a clear focus on excellence and innovation, we offer exciting opportunities for development in an environment where you can make a real difference. At dormakaba you are encouraged to show initiative, develop your skills and shape your career together with 16,000 experts worldwide who set new standards in the industry every day.
