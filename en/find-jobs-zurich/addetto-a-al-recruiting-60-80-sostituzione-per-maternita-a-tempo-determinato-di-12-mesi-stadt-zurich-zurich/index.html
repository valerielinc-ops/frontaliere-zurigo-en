Full description

We are the pulse of Zurich and keep the city in motion every 7.5 minutes. In order for this to work reliably and smoothly, a lot moves behind the scenes of the VBZ. Our colleagues work in over 50 professions: in technology, IT, administration, traffic development, offer planning, infrastructure and many other exciting areas of responsibility. Where we drive, Zurich lives. Come on!

Tasks

  • Finding suitable talents and inspiring for a position is for you the A and O in your job and not a problem, as you have not only implemented this every day since yesterday.
  • You know the recruiting process from A to Z from your daily work. From requirements acceptance to tendering to interviews, selection and conclusion of contracts, you feel safe and keep track of several vacancies.
  • Digital recruiting channels and tools are of course part of everyday life for you. Ideally, you also have experience with the Success Factors ATS.
  • You communicate stylishly in German. Training or further education in HR or recruiting is an advantage, but more important to us are your practical experience and your enthusiasm for recruiting.

Profile

  • You enjoy the exchange, listen well and are really interested in who is behind a CV. You have a good sense of personality, potential and the right fit.
  • Whether with candidates, specialists or executives – you communicate clearly, uncomplicatedly and bindingly and feel comfortable in more demanding conversations.
  • You do not mind even in our dearly named "mass business" the driving service regularly and many interviews (online).
  • Multiple vacancies at the same time? No problem. You set the right priorities, keep an eye on dates and to-dos and get things done reliably.
  • You like to work independently, but you also appreciate the exchange in the team, share your knowledge and get involved if it needs support somewhere.
  • Recruitment is not always predictable. You can quickly rethink, shift priorities and stay relaxed even when things get a bit hectic.
  • You want to develop things further, like to try new things and bring your own personality.

We offer

A lot of appreciation for what you do: The people of Zurich like their VBZ. Working with us is proud. We want you to feel good: We are “Friendly Workspace” certified and do a lot for your health. Discover the whole of Switzerland with the GA – and the family also drives really well thanks to generous discounts.

About us

With our vehicles we travel twice a day around the world and transport over 860,000 passengers. This means that every day, 365 days a year and almost around the clock we make an important contribution to the quality of life in one of the most beautiful cities in the world.
