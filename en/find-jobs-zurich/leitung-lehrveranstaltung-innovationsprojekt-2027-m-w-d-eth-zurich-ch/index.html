Head of Course Innovation Project 2027 (m/f/d)

100%, Zurich, temporary

Professor Mirko Meboldt’s research group deals with innovation and product development in mechanical engineering. The focus is on the combination of new technologies, research, industrial practice and teaching. We see ourselves as a partner of industry and promote the continuous transfer of knowledge through close cooperation with companies as well as the education and training of students and graduates. Our goal is to put new technologies and innovative methods into practice and thus sustainably strengthen the competitiveness of the mechanical engineering industry.

The job is a 100% employment for 13 months and the preferred start is in March 2027.

Project background

The innovation project is a course in the bachelor’s degree program in Mechanical Engineering and teaches about 600 students the basics of agile product development on the basis of a practical mechatronic development project. In 108 teams, students develop a small robot within 14 weeks – from the initial idea through conception and design to the production and testing of a functional system.

Development takes place in two-week sprint cycles. The teams work iteratively, reviewing their solutions through targeted tests and gradually building an understanding of the system to be developed. At the end of the semester, the best teams compete against each other in a public competition.

The innovation project is not only a course, but also a real laboratory for technologies, methods and processes of industrial product development. New approaches, in particular AI-based working methods, are developed, implemented and tested under real conditions together with Professor Meboldt and the project team.

The course is supervised by around 40 specially trained teaching assistants. They coach the teams and take on additional responsibility for areas such as 3D printing, laser cutting and mechatronics.

As leader, you will bear the overall operational responsibility for the innovation project and the 40-strong team. Together with Professor Meboldt, both the implementation of the project and the further development and implementation of new technologies and working methods will be responsible.

The following video gives an impression of the innovation project and the final competition: https://youtu.be/IyoJO5QHZKg?si=R7Eef0s2o5qt-Q1Z.

Profile

  • Initiative and ownership: You work independently, assume responsibility and use the creative leeway to continuously develop processes, methods and the project.
  • Structured and solution-oriented working methods: You also keep track of many parallel tasks, set priorities and reliably advance projects.

Preferred

  • Academic teaching experience (Lecturer:in, Assistant:r, Teaching Assistant)
  • Knowledge of 3D printers (FDM), laser cutters, hand tools or mechatronics (ESP32)
  • Programming skills (Python, Web) as well as experience with Git, Docker and Linux
  • Good English skills

We offer
