Full description

The Migros Club School is the largest provider of adult education in Switzerland. The Migros Ostschweiz cooperative operates 7 Club Schools, where we offer our customers over 600 different courses and programs in a pleasant learning atmosphere.

Responsibilities

  • Your enthusiasm for teaching, commitment, and engaging demeanor inspire participants
  • Comprehensive preparation and follow-up of lessons are also part of your responsibilities
  • You ensure smooth collaboration through regular consultations with the responsible contacts in the Club School administration

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: preferably in hospital or medical practice secretarial work
  • Completed basic or advanced training: as a Medical Practice Assistant (EFZ)
  • You approach your work responsibly and independently
  • You communicate clearly, effectively, and respectfully
  • You stand out for your self-reflection and empathy towards participants
  • Ideally, you hold the SVEB Certificate 1 or an equivalent qualification
  • German (very good knowledge)

What we offer

  • Insurance: Preferential conditions with various insurance companies
  • Cumulus points: Multiple Cumulus points on food and non-food items
  • Migros Bank: Preferential interest rates, free account management, and discounted mortgages
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts on holiday offers and events
  • Sports and fitness offers: Discounts on subscriptions to recreational and fitness facilities
