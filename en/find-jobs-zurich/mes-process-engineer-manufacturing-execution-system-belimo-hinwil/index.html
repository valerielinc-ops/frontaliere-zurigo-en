Original description

Summary

Design the production of tomorrow!

You will advance SAP Digital Manufacturing (MES) globally, optimize processes, connect systems and actively shape innovations in our production. Become part of a team that truly lives digital transformation!

What you will do

  • Definition and development of global MES (Manufacturing Execution System) processes based on the SAP DM (Digital Manufacturing) function
  • Analysis, structuring and translation of global business requirements into scalable MES business processes, concepts and solution models
  • End-to-end implementation and continuous optimization of MES processes including seamless integration of machines, ERP and PLM processes
  • Ensuring stable, high-performance MES operations worldwide, including monitoring, troubleshooting and process monitoring
  • Identification of optimization and automation potential as well as proactive derivation of measures and decision bases
  • Responsibility for project and sub-project management in complex MES implementations and international rollouts
  • Development of training concepts, implementation of global training and workshops, and ensuring knowledge transfer
  • Implementation of targeted system adjustments in close cooperation with IT and partners (customizing & customer-specific developments)

Who you are

  • Completed university or FH studies (business administration, engineering, IT or similar) with solid production and SAP experience
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in a comparable role – ideally in the field of manufacturing, production IT and global industry. Supply chain projects
  • MES expertise, preferably with SAP Digital Manufacturing (DM)
  • Sovereign IT and tool knowledge, especially with SAP DM and SAP MM/PP. Practical experience as a key user, trainer or in application support desirable
  • Confident knowledge of German and English in words and writing and international willingness to travel (about 10%)
  • Analytical, entrepreneurial and networked thinking – with high independence, reliability and implementation strength
  • Communication-strong, resilient personality with assertiveness up to management, team spirit and real enthusiasm to build something new

Who we are

At Belimo, we are proud to make our company a rewarding place to work. Our leaders are value-driven and promote new ideas in their teams around the world. Through our passion, our employees not only create amazing products, but also revolutionize the HVAC industry. Investing in our employees is at the heart of Belimo when it comes to engaging diverse creative talents to build an industry-leading company. The success of our corporate brand is based on the competence and commitment of each individual
