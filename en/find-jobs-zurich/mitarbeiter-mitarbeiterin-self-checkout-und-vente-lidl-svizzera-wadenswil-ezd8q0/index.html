Full description

Is direct contact with customers your passion? Do you want to give your best every day to provide our customers with the best possible shopping experience? Do you want to become part of a team that is needed every day? Then you are in exactly the right place with us. Exciting and diverse career opportunities await you in the dynamic retail environment. Your tasks

  • Operating the regular checkouts
  • Support with scanning and payment at the self-checkout stations
  • Assistance with technical or usage-related questions
  • Approaching and motivating customers to use the self-service options
  • Stocking and maintaining merchandise within the store

Your profile

  • Experience in sales or a similar environment is an advantage
  • Flexibility regarding working hours (MON-SAT between 05:00 and 22:00; early and/or late shift)
  • Enjoyment of direct customer contact and advising customers
  • Good physical fitness
  • Strong communication and problem-solving skills
  • Ability to work under pressure and flexibility, even when customer volumes are high
  • Good German skills; additional languages are an advantage
