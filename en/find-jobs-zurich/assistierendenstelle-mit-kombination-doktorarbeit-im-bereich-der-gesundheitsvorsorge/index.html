Tasks

The activity includes the care of dogs and cats, which are presented in health care and in the special field of clinical infectiology. The precautionary consultation hours are increasingly carried out independently after training; the clinical infectiology consultation hours are carried out under direct supervision by a senior physician (Dipl. ACVIM or Dipl. ECVIM-CA). It also includes participation in the Growing Dog Project (GDP), an internationally oriented longitudinal research project on the health care of young dogs. Within the framework of this project there is the possibility to prepare a clinical doctoral thesis. Participation also includes student clinical training as well as active participation in clinical case discussions, journal clubs and internal training events.

Requirements

A high interest in health care, clinical infectiology and internal issues, dedication, flexibility, team spirit and a very good theoretical knowledge in the internal medicine of small animals are expected. Practical experience in the small animal sector is beneficial. Very good English skills in word and writing are required due to international research activities.

We offer in-depth clinical training with close supervision by proven specialists and the opportunity to actively participate in high-quality clinical research projects. The doctoral thesis includes the planning and implementation of a clinical study, the evaluation and interpretation of the collected data as well as the presentation of the results at national and international congresses and their publication in scientific journals. Participation in the Clinic for Small Animal Medicine also provides an excellent basis for a later application for a residency (ECVIM-CA or ACVIM).
