Full description

Canton of Zurich Directorate of the Judiciary and the Interior Public Prosecutor's Office Communications expert 80 - 100% The Zurich Public Prosecutor’s Office is responsible for prosecuting crimes committed by adults in the Canton of Zurich and thus makes a decisive contribution to security and social coexistence. The Higher Public Prosecutor’s Office leads and supervises the individual public prosecutors’ offices with their approximately 530 employees. In order to strengthen the communications department of the Higher Public Prosecutor's Office, we are looking for a communications expert (80-100%) on January 1, 2027 or by agreement and temporarily for 6 years. In particular, they shall perform the following tasks:

  • Conception and implementation of internal and external project communication for the digital transformation of the public prosecutor’s office as well as the associated changes within the framework of the Federal Act on Platforms for Electronic Communication in the Judiciary (BEKJ)
  • Design and implementation of internal digital communication
  • Development of a modern internal knowledge management – together with the legal service and the department of digital transformation
  • Processing of media inquiries in deputy of the media spokesperson
  • Writing text for internal and external communication channels This profile brings you:
  • Higher education (university or university of applied sciences) with a specialisation in communication, journalism, journalism or a comparable discipline
  • Several years of professional experience in internal or external communication, combined with a corresponding track record in conception and implementation
  • Excellent editorial skills, stylish expression in German and the ability to convey complex issues clearly and appropriately
  • Good understanding of digitalization and a secure handling of new media and digital platforms
  • Team spirit, initiative and a sovereign, convincing appearance as well as an independent and structured working method
  • Interest and willingness to become familiar with issues of criminal law and criminal procedure law and to acquire the necessary expertise "on the job" What awaits you:
  • A varied and responsible task at the interface between the judiciary and the public in a central institution of the rule of law
  • The possibility to actively shape and further develop the internal and external communication of the public prosecutor’s office
  • Plenty of room for manoeuvre and a high level of independence in a dedicated communication team that works closely together beyond its own areas of responsibility
  • An attractive workplace in the police and justice centre in Zurich, just a few minutes from the Hardbrücke station, as well as the opportunity to apply for home office For further information Matthias Baer, Head of Communication (matthias.baer@ji.zh.ch), is at your disposal. We look forward to your full application by 8. November 2026 via our job portal.

Pensum: 80-100 %
