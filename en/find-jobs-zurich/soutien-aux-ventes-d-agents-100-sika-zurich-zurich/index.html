About the role For sales support, we are looking for a dedicated personality who supports all business areas and brands of Sika Schweiz AG in terms of customer master data and pricing. We work carefully, quality-consciously and closely with sales to ensure high data quality.

In this role, you are responsible for the following tasks

  • Customer master data and prices: Ongoing recording and updating in our ERP system
  • Ensuring compliance with service levels and pricing policy according to SLA
  • Conditioning process: participation in the annual process (price letters, net price lists, assortment and customer price lists) in coordination with Marketing and Technical Department
  • Refunds: Active participation in the annual refunds of our key accounts
  • Preparation of training documents and support of sales units as required
  • Active participation in projects related to digitalization and process optimization
  • CRM Salesforce: Support, training, data corrections and participation in system optimization

Your skills and experience

  • Completed basic commercial training with at least 3 years of professional experience
  • Analytical, process-oriented thinking with high IT affinity
  • Experience with ERP SAP and Salesforce benefits
  • Confident knowledge of German, good knowledge of French in words and writing, other languages beneficial
  • Independent, reliable and willing to learn personality with a structured working method and high quality awareness
  • Ability to keep track even in demanding situations and set priorities correctly

Why you should come to us

  • Possibilities for participation in a dynamic and future-oriented environment
  • A motivated team with a lot of know-how, open feedback culture and strong cohesion
  • Team spirit and initiative: We value employees who actively think and drive projects forward
  • Attractive employment conditions and development prospects

We look forward to your complete application dossier.

About Sika Sika is a specialty chemical company, global leader in the development and production of systems and products for gluing, sealing, steaming, reinforcing and protecting in construction and industry. Sika has a global presence with subsidiaries in 103 countries, produces in over 400 factories, develops innovative technologies for customers around the world and thus contributes significantly to the sustainable transformation in construction and transport. The more than 33,000 employees generated a turnover of CHF 11.20 billion in 2025.
