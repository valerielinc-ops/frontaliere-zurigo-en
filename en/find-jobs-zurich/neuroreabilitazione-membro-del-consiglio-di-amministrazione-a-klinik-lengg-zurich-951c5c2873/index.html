Full description

Tasks

  • Overall responsibility for the professional, organizational and personnel management of neurorehabilitation
  • Further development of the Lengg Clinic in cooperation with epileptology in the sense of a competence center for neurology with a high-quality medical-therapeutic offer
  • Promotion and development of the interdisciplinary team
  • Creating an open, team-oriented culture of collaboration
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with other specialist areas and service providers as well as active referral management and close cooperation with referring doctors and institutions.
  • Active participation and implementation of the strategic goals as a member of the management of Klinik Lengg.

Requirements

  • Entrepreneurial, innovative leader with pleasure in interprofessional and interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Specialist title Neurology (FMH) or equivalent
  • Several years of experience as a cadre physician in neurorehabilitation and/or inpatient neurology, including treatment of complex neurological diseases
  • Proven clinical expertise in the areas of movement disorders (especially Parkinson's disease and tremor) and brain stroke treatment (acute phase and rehabilitation)
  • Scientific interest, publications in relevant peer-reviewed journals. Ideally habilitated or shortly before habilitation maturity with interest in the continuation of scientific activity.
  • Networked, solution-oriented and business thinking and acting

Klinik Lengg AG in Zurich is the centre of excellence for epileptology and neurological rehabilitation.

It is our aim to provide patients with neurological diseases with the widest possible rehabilitation and to ensure an accurate diagnosis and sustainable treatment of patients with epilepsy. Around 300 employees are committed to humanely, competently, innovatively and reliably ensuring that our patients regain the greatest possible independence - and thus their quality of life. Become part of one of our interdisciplinary and interprofessional teams.
