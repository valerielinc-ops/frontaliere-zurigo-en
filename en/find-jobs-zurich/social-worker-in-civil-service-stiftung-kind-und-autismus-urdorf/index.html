Full description

  • Social worker in civil service

100 % from October 2026

The Child and Autism Foundation is a competence center for children and adolescents with autism in Urdorf (ZH). The offer includes the special school for 56 children and adolescents, the assisted living with 16 places, the driving service, the advice centre and the early area, the courses and the auxiliary shop. The Child and Autism Foundation employs around 120 people.

We are looking for a civil service provider for at least six months from October 2026 or by appointment.

Your tasks

You are working in a class of our special school. The class team includes the class teacher and educational staff. You also participate in leisure activities and overnight stays. You also make assignments as an assistant in various classes and areas.

We offer you

  • Exciting and versatile areas of responsibility with insight into the special field of autism
  • A careful introduction to work and support during deployment
  • Interesting environment with children and young people
  • Generally regulated working hours from Monday to Friday
  • Your mission is carried out within the framework of the operational agreement with the Central Office for Civil Service (ZIVI)
  • You are a team capable, flexible and resilient personality
  • Motivation to work with special people
  • Enjoyment of practical work
  • Already attended course “Care for people with a disability 2”

Would you like to be part of our team? We look forward to your application. If you have any questions, Daniela Stöckli, Head of Social Pedagogy, is available at the telephone number 044 736 50 70.

We look forward to hearing from you.
