Full description

Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Interior Public Prosecutor's Office Senior Prosecutor 80 - 100% The Zurich-Sihl Public Prosecutor’s Office in Zurich conducts criminal investigations against adults in a sub-area of the city of Zurich. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for a senior prosecutor (80-100%) due to the resignation of the previous post holder. These are your tasks:

  • Management and organization of the office with about 60 employees as well as promotion and evaluation of 8 direct reports
  • Ensuring an optimal age structure of the procedures and ensuring a balanced fall burden through guided allocation to departments
  • Exercise of language and appeal competence, monitoring of key cases and supporting case handlers in complex criminal investigations
  • Active interface between office and management of the senior public prosecutor's office through cooperation in inter-official projects as well as mediation and implementation of strategic projects in the office
  • Several years of successful work in the field of criminal prosecution as well as qualified knowledge in criminal law and criminal procedure law
  • Proven leadership experience and enjoyment of both personnel and organizational tasks
  • Sovereign appearance, high resilience and assertiveness
  • A holistic view and ability for analytical, networked thinking
  • High goal orientation and willingness to make decisions This is what we offer:
  • Responsible cadre function in a socially highly relevant and meaningful area of responsibility
  • Possibility to shape one’s own office professionally and culturally in the sense of the overall organization
  • Employment in the wage class 26 Application We look forward to your application until the 25th. October 2026 through our job portal. For questions, the Chief Prosecutor, Susanne Leu (susanne.c.leu@ji.zh.ch) and the Chief Prosecutor Peter Pellegrini (peter.pellegrini@ji.zh.ch) are at your disposal. Information about the Public Prosecutor’s Office of the Canton of Zurich can be found at www.zh.ch/sta.

Pensum: 80-100 %
