Canton Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School Zimmerberg Physiklaborant/in 50 - 70% The Cantonal School Zimmerberg in Au ZH has been reopened in August 2020. In its coming seventh year of operation, it is expected to have 25 classes with a total of around 600 pupils. It runs a long and a short gymnasium with five maturity profiles (old-tongue, new-language, mathematical-natural science, economic-legal and philosophical-pedagogical-psychological). In the young, manageable cantonal school the personal contact and the individual are in the center. As of 1 January 2027 or by agreement, we are looking for a laboratory ante/laborant for physics to complement the team.
