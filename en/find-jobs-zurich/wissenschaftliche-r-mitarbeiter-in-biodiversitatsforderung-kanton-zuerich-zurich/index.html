Full description

The Department of Nature Conservation is the office responsible for nature conservation and biodiversity in the Canton of Zurich in the Office for Landscape and Nature of the Building Directorate. Employees are committed to the continued existence of valuable habitats and are committed to ensuring that the coexistence of people and nature works in the Zurich canton of business. Your tasks

  • You accompany and lead networking projects in the Canton of Zurich, take over the advice and all administrative tasks.
  • You participate in the development and implementation of the project “Regional Biodiversity and Landscape Quality”.
  • You work in the quality promotion of biodiversity in agriculture.
  • You will work on data engineering questions in a professional context. Your profile
  • You have a completed master's degree preferably in biology or environmental sciences.
  • You will bring in-depth knowledge of ecology and several years of professional experience in the areas of biodiversity and agricultural policy as well as in applied nature conservation.
  • GIS is your strength – from application to data management to organizational tasks.
  • You have experience in project management, work in a structured way and keep track of complex tasks.
  • You communicate clearly, negotiate skillfully and act sovereignly. An exciting and diverse work in an experienced motivated team awaits you. Your contact Martin Graf Stv.
