Full description

We are looking for a dynamic and motivated personality by 1 February 2027 or by agreement.

You can move that with us.

  • You ensure the smooth operation as well as the optimal availability of our modern technical systems (HLKSE).
  • You carry out maintenance, maintenance and repair work on technical plants and facilities independently and professionally.
  • You recognize disruptions at an early stage, analyze their causes and provide a sustainable solution.
  • You are the first contact person for our customers on site and convince with your professional and service-oriented support.
  • You coordinate and monitor external partners and service providers and ensure the quality of the work performed.
  • You carry out regular technical tours and actively contribute to the value preservation of the properties.
  • You support in the planning and implementation of optimizations and smaller projects in the field of building technology.
  • You take on administrative tasks such as reporting, documentation and the maintenance of plant data.
  • You work across departments with internal and external stakeholders and ensure trouble-free building operation.
  • You perform piket service according to assignment planning.

That's what makes you.

  • You have completed technical training in building technology, preferably in the sanitary sector.
  • You bring experience in technical building maintenance, facility management or in a comparable function.
  • You convince with your broad technical understanding and your practical, solution-oriented way of working.
  • You work independently, reliably and like to take responsibility.
  • Customer orientation and service thinking are a matter of course for you.
  • You communicate stylishly in German and perform professionally and friendly. English and other foreign language skills are beneficial.
  • You have a valid category B driving licence.
  • You bring a good reputation.
  • You keep an overview even in hectic situations and are happy to take part.
  • Good IT user knowledge and a safe handling of digital work equipment round off your profile.
  • Participation in the Pikettdienst is a matter of course for you

That's what we offer you.

  • Varied tasks and the opportunity to work independently on site
  • We think and act entrepreneurially. You can take responsibility with us, contribute ideas and get a large creative scope
  • Your health and well-being are our most important asset, which is why we go beyond the legal requirements regarding occupational safety and health protection – also in terms of personal protective equipment.
  • With us you move in an exciting and future-oriented market environment and contribute to a livable, sustainable future
  • We live an open communication with anchored du-culture across all levels
