Full description

Spital Männedorf – Your very special place of work. Whether a small “thank you” or a morning “grüezi” – we focus on real appreciation and humanity. The proximity in our house makes everyday life uncomplicated and allows patient-centered work at the highest level. In an environment where interdisciplinary cooperation at eye level is a matter of course, we offer individual treatments from primary medical care to rehabilitation.

The nature of your activity

  • Self-responsible treatment and care of outpatient patients in the field of oncology
  • Shared management of our tumor boards together with our center partners
  • Consiliary treatment of inpatients and support in the training of colleagues in specialist internist training
  • Cooperation in projects and strategy topics of the clinic and hospital

The basis of your performance

  • Specialist title oncology FMH, or equivalent specialist training recognised in Switzerland
  • Desire to deepen your clinical oncological experience when applying as a senior physician / Several years of clinical oncological experience when applying as a senior physician, ideally in the outpatient sector
  • High patient orientation, strong social and communication skills and team-oriented work
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation in our inter-hospital network
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing (at least level C1)

The advantages of your position

  • A specialized and innovative working environment with state-of-the-art, mostly outpatient treatment methods in an interdisciplinary centre.
  • A strong and committed team (7 doctors, 8 nurses, 9 MPAs) and close cooperation with our network partners for patient-centered treatment.
  • The high level of personal responsibility in the collegial atmosphere, with a lot of personal freedom and active design possibilities.

The reasons for your decision

We offer modern working conditions, up-to-date medical technologies, promote internal training and focus on professional exchange across all disciplines. The quiet location directly on the lake and the proximity to the vibrant city of Zurich round off your workplace. In addition, you can look forward to very special advantages.
