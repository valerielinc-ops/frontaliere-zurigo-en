Full description

BMS Building Materials Suisse is a wholesaler of building materials for shell and interior construction. Our group of companies includes the renowned specialists Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced experts advise, support and inspire customers from the construction industry as well as private customers at around 110 locations throughout Switzerland. Close to everyday construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Quick, easy and competent.

Enrich our Customer Care Team of the Richner brand at our new chassis location at Technoramastrasse 15 in Winterthur (ZH).

On this occasion, we are looking for a motivated personality for the in-house work, which supports the administrative processes. You are the control center and with your structured work and commitment you ensure that everything runs smoothly. If you have a passion for detail, organizational talent and expertise in the sanitary sector, we want to get to know you!

Become part of our team and actively shape the future with us.

Task area

  • Your main tasks include the creation and processing of offers and order releases
  • You are responsible for all telephone requests of our customers (mainly commercial customers from the sanitary industry)
  • From this, you provide information, make clarifications and, if necessary, take and process customer complaints
  • Further administrative tasks such as receiving orders, obtaining offers and tailor-made sketches from suppliers round off the field of activity.
  • You work from Monday to Friday in clearly structured times: 07:15–12:00 and 13:00–17:00

Profile

If your heart beats faster in the task area described above and you bring the following competencies, you are our desired candidate:

  • You bring a completed training in the field of sanitary / building technology / interior design or a commercial training with a technical background resp. Great affinity towards interior design
  • We require good IT knowledge and an experienced handling of the common MS-Office programs
  • Strong communication skills, customer orientation and high quality awareness enhance your profile
  • Your advantages are detailed and team-oriented working methods
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing is required

We offer

  • a challenging, versatile and interesting activity in a good working environment, where the emphasis is on active cooperation and at the same time self-employment
  • intensive training is a program, whereby our new colleagues can benefit from product and system training within the company
  • Support our employees in their professional and personal development
  • Home office possible according to regulations and agreement
  • 5 weeks holiday (6 weeks from 50 years of age) and holiday shopping up to max. 10 days, additional benefits such as an excellently financed independent pension fund and discounts on our complete product range as well as external premium benefits

We look forward to your complete application documents in electronic form.

Want to know more about us? Now visit our career page at jobs.bmsuisse.ch.

We are happy to answer your questions at jobs@bmsuisse.ch.
