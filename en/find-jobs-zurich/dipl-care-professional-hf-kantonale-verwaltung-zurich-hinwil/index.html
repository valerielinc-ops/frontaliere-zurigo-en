Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Prison and Reintegration Dipl. Nursing professional HF 40% With around 1,400 employees, JuWe is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as for carrying out various types of detention. Our employees from around 40 professions support and promote the reintegration of delinquents and work effectively and progressively for a safe and just society. The Medical Service is one of eight main departments of JuWe and as a central organization responsible for the medical-somatic basic care of all detained persons in the institutions of the Canton of Zurich. It ensures medical care from initial examination and treatment to emergency care and accompanies the detained people in everyday life with health issues. As part of the Zurich prison facilities (VEZ), the Bachtel prison center (VZB) specializes in the open execution of prison sentences and, in particular, substitute prison sentences and has 94 detention places for imprisoned men. The focus is on the daily employment of detainees, which creates structure and promotes their development in everyday life.
