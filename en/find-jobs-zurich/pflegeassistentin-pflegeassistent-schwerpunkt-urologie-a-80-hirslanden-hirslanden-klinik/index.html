Location: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Occupation by: from now on or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 74717

Would you like to support our patients in day-to-day care and contribute to professional care? Then you are exactly right with us!

At our urology department, you can expect a versatile day-to-day work in which you can contribute your nursing skills and, together with a committed team, take care of the well-being of our patients.

YOUR TASKS

  • You carry out nursing interventions in consultation with the nursing staff and according to the nursing needs assessment.
  • You support the nurses in the station everyday and contribute to a smooth department operation.
  • You take over patient and material transports and accompany interns during their introduction and coordinate their deployment.
  • Support in documenting care measures and monitoring patients’ vital signs
  • Communication with relatives and other specialist areas in the context of patient care and coordination of appointments

Your profile

  • You have completed training as an assistant / assistant health and social services (AGS) or as a nursing assistant / nursing assistant.
  • Ideally, you will bring professional experience in the acute field, preferably in urology.
  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing.
  • You work reliably, responsibly and service-oriented.
  • You appreciate the cooperation in the interprofessional team and meet our patients with empathy.
  • Teamwork, communication and organisational skills

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. You work with a modern medical clinic information system and can expect extensive training and development opportunities from us. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family responsibilities. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. Employees who have to move completely for their work receive four additional paid days off with a 100% workload. With a reduced workload, the days off reduce accordingly. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from equivalent financial compensation. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.
