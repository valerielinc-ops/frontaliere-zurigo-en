Full description

Personal – competent – close Welcome to the Hospital Uster, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As the central pillar of health care in the Oberland region of Zurich and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you experience the highest level of expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and promoted in your professional development. Spital Uster is a teaching hospital of the Medical Faculty of the University of Zurich. Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of a “regional and digital health hub in Züri East”! B0 is a medical department for general, semi-private and private patients with internistic medical conditions. These include, inter alia, cardiovascular diseases, pneumological diseases, endocrinology, infections, gastrointestinal diseases, autoimmune diseases, oncology. Patients with a chronic condition are also professionally accompanied and cared for.

Exciting challenges await you

  • Your night – your responsibility – your team!
  • you look after patients with versatile medical conditions and ensure that they feel safe, competently cared for and well looked after at night
  • you are responsible for and control the care process, set priorities, recognize changes at an early stage and act appropriately for the situation
  • you coordinate the care in tandem with FaGe closely with the medical service and other professional groups
  • you actively contribute your experience, strengthen an appreciative team culture and accompany FaGe learners and students HF/FH in their practical training

What sets you apart for the job

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nurse HF/FH with experience in the acute hospital
  • open-minded and team-oriented person with a professional way of working
  • You enjoy internal medicine and a wide range of patients
  • you think, keep track and like to be part of a strong team
  • Willingness to like working at night
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

7 Reasons to Work with Us

  • Fixed night service: more planning and no constantly changing shifts
  • a workload of 50-100% that fits your life situation
  • an open, appreciative team and support from our care expertise
  • at least five weeks of vacation plus joker days for your changing time
  • Food even during the night – because night service makes you hungry
  • Central location near Uster station
  • free parking time during your night shift until 07:00
