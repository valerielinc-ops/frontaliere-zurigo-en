Role overview

  • For us, quality means much more than compliance with standards and processes. It is an essential prerequisite for high-performance products, satisfied customers and sustainable corporate success.
  • As Head of Quality (m/f/d), you will assume overall responsibility for our quality organization and consistently develop it further.
  • You combine high quality standards with entrepreneurial thinking, pragmatism and speed.
  • You see quality not just as a control authority, but as a strategic partner of the business - with the aim of identifying risks at an early stage, reducing complexity and continuously improving the performance of the entire organization.
  • You will further develop our quality strategy and, together with management, set clear priorities for its implementation
  • You lead and develop the quality organization and create an environment in which ownership, collaboration and continuous improvement are practiced
  • They make quality measurable: With clear goals, meaningful KPIs and impact-oriented reporting, you create transparency and enable well-founded decisions
  • You question existing processes and structures, identify potential for improvement and consistently promote pragmatic and sustainable solutions
  • You ensure the further development of our quality management system as well as compliance with relevant standards, certifications and regulatory requirements
  • You manage cross-departmental quality and improvement initiatives and ensure that findings result in concrete measures and measurable improvements
  • As a central interface between management, engineering, production, project management and other areas, you bring together different perspectives and create viable solutions
  • You strengthen a quality culture in which quality is not just the responsibility of the quality organization, but rather the joint responsibility of the entire company
  • Completed technical, scientific or economic studies, ideally with a focus on quality management, production, technology or a comparable orientation
  • Several years of relevant professional experience in quality management, preferably in a comparable industrial environment
  • Profound experience in the development and implementation of quality strategies as well as in the further development of management systems and organizational structures
  • Several years of leadership experience as well as proven experience in managing cross-departmental projects and change plans
  • Very good knowledge of relevant quality standards and management systems, especially ISO 9001
  • Knowledge of other relevant standards would be an advantage
  • Strong strategic and entrepreneurial thinking as well as the ability to analyze complex issues in a structured manner and prepare them in a management-friendly manner
  • High communication and persuasive skills as well as a confident demeanor when dealing with management, management and different departments
  • Strong change, implementation and conflict skills as well as a high degree of personal responsibility
  • Very good German and business fluent English skills
  • A dynamic working environment with exciting projects
  • Flexible working hours (flexitime and trust working hours)
  • Very good social benefits and a non-mandatory pension fund
  • Employee benefits of a large corporation, employee share purchase program
  • Promoting needs-based further training
  • Staff restaurant as well as external catering and shopping options nearby
  • Individual internal and external personnel development measures in collaboration with our Rheinmetall Academy
  • Workplace a few minutes' walk from Zurich-Oerlikon train station
