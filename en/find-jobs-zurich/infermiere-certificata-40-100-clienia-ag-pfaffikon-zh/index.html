Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With over 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric services offered by Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in outpatient, day clinic and inpatient settings. The group employs around 1800 people and generated sales of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For our acute wards S2 and T2 we are looking for a

The acute wards S2 and T2 are aimed at people in exceptional psychiatric situations who are in need of immediate help. We offer professional support to these people. We provide help in the acute phase. This includes emergency psychiatric care with careful diagnostics, appropriate treatment and a rapid clarification of the social situation. The goals of the treatment are the reconstruction of everyday skills, the development of possible coping strategies and the stabilization of self-esteem.

The wards have different treatment focuses

S2: Psychosis T2: Affective disorders

Both wards work with the Safewards model and are usually open-ended.

Your tasks Flexibility and enjoyment of variety are central points. Because that is exactly what working in acute psychiatry means.

If this applies to you, here are some of the most important tasks:

  • Caregiver work: We treat our patients individually according to their needs and maintain contact with their relatives
  • In addition to the individual discussions, group offers take place, which are also led by the nursing staff
  • Our interprofessional cooperation is a huge strength and distinguishes us. Active participation and participation are central components of this cooperation
  • Since we work with permanent night watches, you will be deployed in a two-shift system (early and late shift). If you still want to do a few nights, that is certainly possible.

Your profile Professional competence, service orientation and a lot of humor are the most important qualities you need to have. With these qualities, we achieve the best treatment results for our patients and the team is happy that you will become a part of them.

If you have experience in nursing but not in the field of psychiatry, it is still possible to join. It is then crucial that you have a lot of motivation and commitment to acquire the specific skills. As part of our wide range of internal courses and generous participation in further education and training, you can get to know the various therapy methods and then apply them yourself. Of course, you will also be supported by your new colleagues.
