Dispatcher:in production & quality control 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Dispatcher: in production & quality control Zurich Altstetten, December 1st, 2026 or by arrangement, limited until April 2028, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. Are you ready for a new challenge? Our CompanyMail, Transport & Logistics department offers tailor-made logistics and transport services and ensures the smooth transport of all luggage offers and CompanyMail for our customers. For us, no two days are the same. You enjoy working with people and can use your communicative and linguistic skills on a daily basis. Working hours in 2-shift operation are from Monday to Friday between 6:30 a.m. and 11:00 p.m. and on Saturday and Sunday from 7:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. You can move that.

  • In this very varied role, you will ensure incident management on the rail and road network throughout Switzerland and will be responsible for extraordinary incidents such as: B. operational disruptions or delays, suitable solutions for our products.
  • You plan shipments and tours and plan customized group luggage transport.
  • As a dispatcher, you will arrange appointments in German, French, English and sometimes Italian with customers for the collection and delivery of luggage at home and monitor its timely collection and delivery.
  • You also offer technical support to our employees in the event of capacity bottlenecks, misdirected shipments and other irregularities. This is what you bring with you.
  • As a proactive person, you like to take on responsibility and work independently and in a solution-oriented manner. You combine high quality standards with great resilience.
  • You are ready for irregular working hours, including weekend assignments.

Basic commercial training, ideally supplemented by further training in transport scheduling, sets you apart. - Ideally, you have dispatching experience in the areas of transport / train traffic / rolling stock. - You communicate fluently in German (at least C1) and have good knowledge (at least B1) of French and English.
