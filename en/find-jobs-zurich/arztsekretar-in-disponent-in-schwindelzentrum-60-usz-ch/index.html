Tasks

  • Stylish German in word and writing, foreign language skills are advantageous
  • Enjoying working with people and a strong understanding of services

We offer: Besides good employment conditions, we offer an interesting and demanding area of work in a dynamic and multiprof-unified working environment. We guarantee a good, structured training.
