Full description

For the committed leadership and strategic development of our clinic, we are looking for a motivated and professionally experienced leader at the earliest possible time as Chief Physician / Chief Physician Geriatrics. The Cantonal Hospital Winterthur (KSW) is one of the ten largest hospitals in Switzerland and provides medical care in the greater Winterthur area with around 4,000 employees. Our Clinic for Acute Geriatrics has a highly specialized age medicine and covers a broad, future-oriented spectrum: Acute Geriatrics, Memory Clinic, Aging Traumatology and Long Term Care. In the course of the continuous expansion of our age medicine center functions, we are looking for a strong and empathetic personality who wants to contribute their professional expertise and commitment and actively shape the further development of age medicine at the KSW.

Tasks

  • Diagnosis and treatment of patients at the acute-geriatric bed ward and in the KSW’s Retirement Trauma Center.
  • Consultation hours at the Memory Clinic with appropriate previous experience.
  • Implementation of stationary consilies at KSW
  • Treatment of the residents in the Oberi and Adlergarten age centres of the city of Winterthur as part of the medical care service.
  • Deputy head doctor in his absence.
  • Professional support, supervision and promotion of senior physicians and assistant physicians, including teaching and further education.
  • Active participation in the qualitative and strategic development of the medical treatment offer.
  • Assuming overarching organisational tasks.
  • Promote interprofessional and interdisciplinary cooperation.
  • Engagement in teaching, training, quality assurance and network maintenance

Profile

  • Specialist title General Internal Medicine (for foreign diploma with MEBEKO recognition).
  • Main topic: Geriatrics.
  • Several years of experience in a senior or senior function.
  • Responsible and assertive personality with high quality thinking and strong teamwork and creative will.
  • High social skills, strong communication and empathetic treatment of older people and their relatives.
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation as well as the further development of acute medicine
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

Benefits: Work and private life should fit together. Therefore, the KSW offers you – where the company makes it possible – flexible and smooth working hours, home office, generous maternity leave, paternity leave and employment levels that can be adapted to different phases of life. Develop yourself professionally and personally: Every year, around 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses are open to you at KSW. We also support external training – temporally and/or financially.

  • Sometimes it needs support. In the event of personal stress, prolonged illness or difficult experiences in your professional or private life, various confidential discussions are available at KSW.
  • At KSW we work together and for each other. Appreciation, open and respectful communication and cooperation across professional groups and departments shape our everyday work. You should be able to contribute with your ideas and strengths and develop.

Your future is also ensured: You benefit from very good social benefits and a pension fund solution with BVK, which is significantly above the legal minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, you are very well covered as a KSW employee.

  • Your health is important to us. Benefit from discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training and courses as well as discounts in pharmacies and selected health insurance companies.
  • Family and work can be well connected with us. The daycare center La Luna with spacious opening hours is directly next to the KSW. In addition, the KSW supports you – depending on income – financially with the crib costs.

There is also something to discover outside of work: Thanks to our cooperation with cultural partners in Winterthur and the surrounding area, you regularly benefit from discounts for concerts and theatre performances.
