Job description

The perfect teaching for service-oriented all-rounders: women with an interest in information management!

In this training, you are responsible for the following tasks in the library:

  • data management
  • Preparation and provision of information
  • Acquisition, exploitation and maintenance of media
  • Advising customers: insiders on services, media and information services
  • Supporting customers in their research and obtaining information

The training lasts 3 years and takes place mainly in the library in the main building, on campus center. During your training, you will gain insight into another institution in the field of information and documentation through an external internship (e.g. in a public library or a documentation centre).

Profile

You value dealing with customers and are interested in information technologies and electronic media. You inspire your customers with your openness, service awareness and communication skills. Through your analytical and systematic thinking and your sense of order, you always keep an overview.

For this vocational apprenticeship you need a mandatory school degree and good performance in the following school subjects:

  • German
  • English
  • French
  • Spaces, times, society
  • Religion, cultures, ethics
