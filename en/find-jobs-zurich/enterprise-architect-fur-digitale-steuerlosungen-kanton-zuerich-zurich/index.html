Canton of Zurich Financial Directorate Enterprise Architect for digital tax solutions 100% Welcome to the fascinating world of taxes! Together with the municipal tax offices, the cantonal tax office collects taxes for the Confederation, the canton and the municipalities. We support individuals and businesses throughout the tax collection process. Around 800 qualified employees are committed to this at the Zurich Altstetten site. With the project "Joint IT 2032", the cantonal tax office is creating a modern and uniform IT landscape for the taxation of the canton and the 160 municipalities. This requires exceptional minds for the conception and implementation of these important IT transformations. As an enterprise architect with a focus on IT architecture, you will take on tasks of lasting importance for the Canton of Zurich in this newly created position. Our services support more than 2,500 municipal and cantonal employees every day as well as more than one million individuals and companies with the tax declaration. Your tasks

  • In this key position, together with the business architect, you are responsible for the company-wide architecture of the tax office and ensure the alignment of business and IT.
  • In addition to your work in the project "Joint IT 2032", you will take over the conception and implementation of the IT architecture for today's approximately 90 specialist applications that are operated in various data centers.
  • You design our architectural processes, develop concrete target architectures and ensure the long-term continuity of the architecture.
  • You teach your colleagues architectural topics methodically and practically.
  • You communicate with a wide variety of offices within the tax office and at different levels up to the management. Your profile
  • You have several years of experience as a company architect with a focus on IT; ideally using the TOGAF® framework.
  • You have a university degree in computer science, leadership, business administration or similar.
  • You understand how to transform complex issues into practical solutions in a structured and systematic manner.
  • Your own initiative, service orientation and stylish knowledge of German in word and writing round off your profile. Our offer
  • A broad and challenging range of topics – from digitalization to IT security and AI.
  • A stable working environment with long-term and sustainable projects.
  • An outstanding training offer and the time to use it.
  • Flexible working hours and the possibility to increase holidays with flextime balance.
  • An excellent connection to all means of transport. Interested? Then apply now – we are happy to meet you. Please note that we only process applications that are received via our online tool.
