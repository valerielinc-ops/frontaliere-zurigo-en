Full description

Your tasks

  • You will learn about the psychiatric-psychotherapeutic diagnosis and treatment of adults with mental health problems in the daily clinical field.
  • You participate in your daily work in the treatment in the group setting and also attend one-on-one meetings.
  • You will participate in rapports and sessions and continue your education as part of our internal training.
  • During your internship, you will also be supervised by an experienced psychologist from our day clinic team.
  • You are involved in clinical-diagnostic and administrative tasks, including screening diagnostics, reporting tasks and the collection and processing of treatment-relevant data.
  • You have the opportunity to carry out a clinically oriented master’s thesis as part of the day clinic and to collect and evaluate your own clinical data. Your profile
  • You have a completed bachelor's degree in psychology and are enrolled in the master's degree with a clinical focus.
  • You are available for an internship period of three to six months.
  • Your interest is in psychology in the psychiatric-psychotherapeutic context and you are in an interdisciplinary cooperation.
  • If you are also sociable and appreciate the benefits of working independently, we look forward to your application. With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatment and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations.
