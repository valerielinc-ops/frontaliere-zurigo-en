Full description

Does your heart beat for older people? Is the nursing profession your calling? Do you see opportunities to meaningfully contribute your skills and experience to us in order to have a positive impact on the lives of our customers? Then you are a perfect fit for us!

We are looking for someone for the Tertianum Sonnenplatz in Emmenbrücke immediately or by appointment

Dipl. Nursing Specialist HF/FH/DNII 40-100%

Your tasks

  • Holistic care and support for guests in accordance with our care principles and the care process
  • Taking on tasks as a caregiver
  • Responsibility and control of the care process of the assigned guests including the RAI care needs classification
  • Electronic care documentation using CareCoach
  • Planning and accompanying doctor's visits
  • Assumption of day-to-day responsibility with coordination of the day-to-day team
  • Leading case discussions and nursing reports
  • Taking on tasks as a subject person in a specialist topic and thus participating in the group-wide network on care quality

Your profile

  • Completed training to become a qualified nursing specialist HF/FH/DNII or equivalent training recognized by the SRK
  • Professional experience in long-term care an advantage
  • RAI knowledge desired or willingness to attend these courses (paid)
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoy a variety of challenges
  • Good knowledge of spoken and written German
