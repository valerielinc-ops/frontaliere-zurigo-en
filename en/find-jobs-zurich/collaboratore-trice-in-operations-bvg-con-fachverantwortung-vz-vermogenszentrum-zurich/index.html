Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We create transparency in the banking and insurance market and advise our customers comprehensively and neutrally. As we are growing strongly, we are looking for reinforcements for our pension foundations at our headquarters at Paradeplatz in Zurich.

  • You will independently and qualitatively handle the diverse business cases in the area of active insured persons of our collective foundations and ensure correct, efficient and timely processing.
  • You provide insured persons and employers with competent advice on pension issues and support them with complex issues relating to occupational pensions.
  • You process demanding written and telephone inquiries from customers independently and efficiently and thus contribute significantly to a high level of customer satisfaction.
  • You actively participate in projects, contribute your own ideas and support the further development of our services and processes.
  • Depending on your commitment, interest and development, you will gradually assume personal responsibility and position yourself as an internal specialist.
  • Well-founded experience in occupational pension schemes, ideally in the environment of collective foundations
  • Professional further education such as the specialty card social insurance or specialist personnel provision of advantage
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working methods as well as motivation to take on responsibility and actively shape specialist topics
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of French and/or English are advantageous
  • A responsible position with room for manoeuvre and development perspective
  • Varied tasks, a team to count on and a company culture that promotes initiative
  • Exciting topics at the interface of prevention, law, finance and customer advice
  • An appreciative environment with flat hierarchies and short decision paths
  • Individual promotion and support for further education
  • An experienced team that supports each other and wants to make a difference together
