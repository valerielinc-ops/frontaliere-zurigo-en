Full description

Your Area of Responsibility

As a member of the corporate controlling team of a publicly listed, globally operating company, you will assume a broad spectrum of financial and controlling tasks. In this role, you will support the group management and financial managers of our group companies worldwide and contribute with professionalism, quality awareness, and team spirit to effective collaboration.

  • Participation in group consolidation (30 group companies) and preparation of monthly financial statements, reporting and annual financial statements in accordance with Swiss GAAP FER including the management report
  • Support in the area of financial compliance (including non-financial reporting, coordination and further development of internal controls, conducting internal audits)
  • Preparation of ad hoc analyses and participation in projects (e.g., digitalization of our processes, Power BI, taxation, M&A)
  • Maintenance and further development of consolidation software (Cognos)

Your Profile

  • Higher business education (University/University of Applied Sciences, audit or controlling), several years of experience in consolidation and controlling (preferably in industrial environment or in audit) and experience in Swiss GAAP FER
  • Analytical, independent, conceptual and proactive working approach as well as pronounced quality standards
  • Very good IT skills in MS Office, Power BI and SAP; Cognos knowledge is advantageous
  • Confident communication in German and English and ability to prepare and present interconnected relationships in a manner appropriate to the recipient
  • Open, communicative personality with international interest and enthusiasm for challenging financial topics and contacts with group companies
  • Moderate travel for internal audits on site is seen as an enrichment

Why HUBER+SUHNER?

You can expect a varied and responsible position in a publicly listed, globally operating company. In our small, experienced team, professionalism, quality and enjoyment of challenging tasks are highly valued – and your contribution makes the difference.
