Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate – that is what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family business today employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries across the Group, and the trend is rising. The company’s headquarters are located in the Canton of Glarus. In front of a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new, innovative ideas in agile teams. The “Retail” division comprises more than 250 chocolateries in more than 30 countries. Here we offer our chocolate specialties and invite customers to experience fresh chocolate again and again. Do you also want to participate? Then come to us.

This is what awaits you

  • You advise customer-oriented and sell our diverse product range, for example in the selection of gifts
  • You actively respond to our customers, recognize their needs and offer suitable solutions
  • You take care of the cash processes and ensure a precise and correct billing
  • You present the exquisite confiserie and chocolate range in a tasteful and inviting way
  • You also take on production activities as well as the filling and maintenance of sales areas
  • You implement our high quality standards with dedication and care
  • You consistently pay attention to compliance with the hygiene and freshness concept
  • You work hand in hand with your team and contribute to a positive working environment

You bring that with you

  • You bring professional experience in the retail trade and are enthusiastic about sales and direct customer contact
  • You recognize customer needs quickly and advise friendly, individual and convincing
  • You have very good knowledge of German and English
  • French and other languages are beneficial
  • You work responsibly, reliably and quality-oriented and consistently adhere to hygiene and cleanliness standards
  • You are a team player, like to take on responsibility and want to develop in sales and service
  • You are flexible and ready for assignments on weekends and holidays

This is what we offer you

  • An exciting working environment with motivated colleagues
  • A position with a lot of personal responsibility
  • The opportunity to bring in fresh ideas
  • An open and respectful feedback culture
  • Generous discounts on our products
  • Chocolate in the workplace
  • Good accessibility of locations

Sounds interesting? Then apply to us. Tamara Sindin, branch manager, +41 61 263 17 70, is at your disposal for any questions.
