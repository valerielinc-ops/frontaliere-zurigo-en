# IT Consultant & Full Stack Software Engineer

Il tuo futuro inizia

You realize challenging applications in customer projects and use the latest methods and tools. After examining and structuring customer needs, you will find efficient and sustainable solutions to complex problems in the design, development and operation of comprehensive software architectures. The projects bring you together with the dynamic IT Consulting and Software Engineering team to success.

  • You inspire customers for a convincing solution
  • Software Engineering is your passion
  • You take on responsibility in a dynamic team

Your skills

  • Experience in software development and design of extensive applications/software systems
  • Very good know-how as a full stack developer with Java, JavaScript, TypeScript, Angular and the corresponding frameworks (e.g. Spring, JEE, etc.)
  • Conceptually thinking and capable of penetrating facts and problems
  • Self-initiative, committed and very communicative
  • Very good German and English in word and writing

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Benefits.
