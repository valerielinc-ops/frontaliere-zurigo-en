Specialist nursing – Spital Zollikerberg, Zollikerberg (ZH).

Area: Nursing professions.

Welcome to your new place of work.

The interdisciplinary private station offers you an attractive position with a fee of 80-100% as an

Expert / Expert Care

If you too want to make a difference in people’s lives, these are your tasks:

  • You are responsible for the professional care of patients with a focus on the interdisciplinary private unit
  • They ensure professional support and quality assurance on the station in close cooperation with the care expert
  • You are actively integrating specialist topics into everyday station work
  • You share your expertise and pass on your enthusiasm for the profession

Become part of the Department of Emergency and Acute Medicine at the Zollikerberg Hospital. Under one roof, we combine internal medicine, surgery, nephrology with dialysis center, the emergency department, the eye center, the medical ambulatorium, the day clinic and other specialized areas. The Clinic for Internal Medicine looks after more than 2,500 inpatients every year, the Clinic for Surgery over 1,500. With more than 6,000 emergency consultations and over 450 patients in the interdisciplinary intensive care unit, we offer a versatile and demanding medical work environment.

On our private station you can expect an exciting and challenging field of activity in the disciplines of general surgery, orthopaedics, gynecology, urology and plastic surgery. We work according to the reference care system and create an environment in which personal and individual care of our patients is the focus.

In our 360° tour, get a first impression of your new place of work.

  • Green bonus: We offer an eco-bonus of up to CHF 650 per year for environmentally conscious action
  • Focus on further education: We generously support you financially and time-wise in your further education – so that you are always up-to-date
  • Fit and active: Use our fitness room on the Zollikerberg site for free and stay healthy and motivated
  • Career paths without borders: With us you can not only develop in your field, but also change to other areas or specialize – even across several companies
