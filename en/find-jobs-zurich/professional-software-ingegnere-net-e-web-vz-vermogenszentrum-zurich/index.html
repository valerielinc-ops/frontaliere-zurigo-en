Key requirements

  • Eine höhere Informatikausbildung und mindestens fünf Jahre Erfahrung in einer vergleichbaren Rolle

Application process

  • You develop and operate business-critical applications in the banking environment – with the Microsoft stack and modern web technologies.
  • You actively shape the further development and modernization of our systems and bring current technologies into practice.
  • Together with the team, you will design and realize our applications with Angular, .NET and SQL Server on Microsoft Azure.
  • In a self-organized engineering team, you take on end-to-end responsibility for the development and operation of our services and applications according to the DevOps principle.
  • You work in a focused team with short decision paths and a pragmatic, agile development process.
  • A higher computer science education and at least five years of experience in a comparable role
  • Experience in developing modern web applications – from front-end frameworks to REST APIs to databases, ideally with Angular, C#/.NET and SQL Server
  • A strong hands-on mentality and enthusiasm for good software engineering and new technologies
