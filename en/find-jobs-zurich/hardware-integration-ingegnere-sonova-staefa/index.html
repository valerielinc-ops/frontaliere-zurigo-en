Full description

As Hardware Development Engineer , you will contribute to the development of innovative hearing instrument systems, from early concepts and prototypes through hardware integration, testing and product development.

You will develop product-specific hearing system solutions, integrate electronic and electroacoustic subsystems, define transducers and build prototypes to validate new concepts. Working within interdisciplinary agile R&D teams, you will combine expertise in electrical engineering, acoustics, RF and measurement technologies to develop robust, high-quality solutions.

This role offers the opportunity to work hands-on with sophisticated hearing technology while collaborating with diverse experts across R&D and contributing to innovation, knowledge sharing and intellectual property.

More about the role

  • Develop hardware solutions for hearing instrument systems, translating product requirements into robust electronic and electroacoustic designs.
  • Integrate hardware subsystems and define electroacoustic transducers to ensure effective performance of the complete hearing system.
  • Design and build hearing system prototypes, including hands-on activities such as soldering, gluing and working with small electronic and mechanical components.
  • Define and execute hardware measurements and testing, establishing limits, test criteria and quality characteristics for hearing system hardware.
  • Develop software tools for measurement systems to support hardware development, testing and validation activities.
  • Contribute to the R&D development process, including planning, reviews and refinement of product increments within interdisciplinary agile development teams.
  • Collaborate and share knowledge across R&D, actively participating in team activities and technical communities while supporting onboarding and, when required, the hiring process.
  • Contribute to innovation and intellectual property, identifying opportunities for new solutions and supporting the IP process while following relevant development procedures and quality standards.

More about you

  • BSc or MSc in Electrical Engineering or a related technical discipline; additional education or expertise in acoustics or audiology is an advantage.
  • Very good knowledge of electrical engineering, acoustics, measurement technologies and RF engineering, combined with experience in electronic hardware and wireless system design.
  • Practical experience with PCB design, wireless technologies, electronic measurements and hardware prototyping, including soldering and handling small components.
  • Experience developing software for controlling measurement equipment is an advantage, with knowledge of tools and technologies such as MATLAB, Arduino, C# and Python.
  • A structured, system-oriented and quality-conscious approach, combined with creativity, technical curiosity and the ability to develop pragmatic solutions to complex engineering challenges.
  • Strong self-leadership and accountability, with a proactive approach to learning, knowledge sharing, continuous improvement and transparent collaboration.
  • Excellent teamwork and communication skills, with the ability to contribute effectively within distributed, interdisciplinary and culturally diverse development teams.
  • Very good English and German, both written and spoken; experience with project or functional leadership and tools such as Confluence, Microsoft Teams and MS Office is advantageous.

More about what we offer

As one of the world’s leading hearing care providers headquartered in Switzerland, we’re committed to building an inclusive culture. We want to create an environment where you can balance a successful career with your commitments and interests outside of work, through our flexible hybrid working model. We offer a wide range of training opportunities for both your professional and personal development, and there are exceptional growth opportunities with individual development plans.
