Full description

Take responsibility for three teams and actively shape payment transactions. We are looking for you: a leader with an overview, strategic thinking and fresh ideas. Come on – at the bank, which has been putting people first with 1,800 employees since 1958.

Mansioni

  • You will manage the payment transactions department in terms of expertise and personnel and ensure a smooth, high-quality service for internal and external customers in order to achieve the overall banking goals.
  • You’ll continuously optimize payment workflows, improve your team’s performance and take responsibility for the internal control system (ICS) in your area.
  • You are the central contact person for the management in all matters relating to payment transactions and represent the bank in external committees.
  • You optimize key performance indicators, monitor market-driven and regulatory changes and implement them independently or in close coordination with those responsible for operations.
  • You actively contribute your expertise, identify far-reaching automation potential and represent payment transactions in bank-wide projects in order to support strategic goals.

  • Several years of management experience over at least two management levels: Payment transactions
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): Banking
  • You have several years of professional experience in banking with a focus on payments, as well as in-depth knowledge of the relevant processes and processes
  • High methodological competence with regard to problem solving and order structuring as well as experience in the management or cooperation of larger change management projects.
  • Over 4 years of leadership experience over at least two management levels as well as your leadership and communication strength round off your profile.
