Your tasks

  • Together with the job partner, you support infants and toddlers on site in children’s rehab who require intensive inpatient rehabilitation due to the consequences of a congenital or acquired disease or after an accident.
  • You take over the leadership of the game group once a week.
  • The implementation of developmental diagnostic clarifications is part of your everyday work.
  • You are in close contact with the guardians, advise and support them in their parenting situation.
  • You work interdisciplinary together with the specialists of Kinder-Rehab Switzerland and with external specialists. Your profile
  • You have an education in curative education early education or an equivalent education (EDK recognition).
  • You have experience in dealing with impaired or developmentally vulnerable children from birth to kindergarten admission.
  • Your flexibility and your ability to think responsibly and networked are what set you apart. Claudia Westhues School Principal Hospital School and HFE +41 44 762 52 74 Employers Children's Hospital Zurich – Children's Rehab Affoltern at Albis The Children's Hospital Zurich is the largest University Children's Hospital in Switzerland with around 3,000 employees and one of the leading centers for pediatric and adolescent medicine in Europe. But the biggest thing about the "Kispi" are the little ones: up to 100,000 patients are cared for annually. In addition to the entire range of pediatric medicine and pediatric surgery, the Children's Hospital also offers a unique children's rehabilitation in Affoltern on the Albis. Not only state-of-the-art devices and the highest quality in treatment and therapy are important here, but also a good feeling for interpersonal things: In a family environment, the employees accompany children and young people step by step on the way back to a life as independent as possible.
