Behind a host is much more than you see at first glance. Among other things, openness, interest in people, tact and a good feeling for the other person are in demand. Does your heart beat for our many tasks? Then apply! Day services: Various service models between 06.50 and 20.00 / 365 days a year

Your main tasks

  • A warm welcome for our patients at the department and during the initial interview
  • Personal advice on choosing menu and drinks and ordering via iPad
  • Guest friendly and professional service for meals, drinks and other concerns
  • Ordering food and station laundry
  • Miscellaneous hotel work in the patient rooms (making beds, filling up laundry, etc.)
  • Close cooperation with care, kitchen, transport and other interfaces
  • Responsible for compliance with the specified quality and hygiene standards
  • Various administrative tasks

Your profile

  • Completed training (EFZ) and/or several years of professional experience in hospitality or catering
  • Flexible working hours fit for you
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German (Level C1)
  • Dealing with MS-Office is not a problem for you
  • You enjoy working independently, love a dynamic environment and are flexible
  • Standardized procedures are not a problem for you
  • Your heart beats for a courteous service

Our offer

Together we create a well-being among our patients and maintain an open, warm and helpful approach. We achieve success together in the team and we work with enthusiasm. With the patient hospitality industry, a colorful and multicultural team awaits you. We look forward to meeting you. For this post, dossiers from recruiters cannot be considered. Thank you for your understanding. Our Benefits

Kita on site

In our in-house daycare centers, your children will be cared for lovingly. Long opening hours without company holidays and subsidized care places relieve you in everyday life.

Health

We support your health. In addition to personal consultations and courses of our health management that promote your well-being, we offer you access to over 120 sports with a subsidized annual subscription to the ASVZ.

Flexibility

We promote a balanced work-life balance: Almost half of our employees work part-time, and about 50% of the tasks can be done in the home office – if your working day allows it.

Family friendliness

We help you to reconcile family and work. Mothers are entitled to at least 6 months of maternity leave (of which 16 weeks are paid in full), fathers to at least 2 months of paternity leave (of which 2 weeks are paid in full).

Further education and development

We accompany you on your professional path and support your development with individual career and further education opportunities.

Insurance

We cover 60% of your pension fund contributions and offer attractive special conditions for supplementary insurance.

Mobility

You benefit from a personal ZVV BonusPass at a special price or receive a fixed amount of money for a general subscription (GA) or half-tax PLUS.

Telephony

You and your family members benefit from special conditions for private mobile subscriptions.
