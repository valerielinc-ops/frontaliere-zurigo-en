Full description

Are you looking for a responsible job where you can contribute your talent for numbers? Do you like working independently and finding solutions to accounting issues? Do you communicate safely in German and English? Then you are exactly right with us! That's what you do.

  • You support the head of debtors and creditors in all tasks in creditor management
  • In addition, you independently keep several accounts payable and book payment receipts and payments both electronically and manually
  • You enter accounts, i.e. clear and process payment differences, maintain master data of customers and suppliers and reconcile transit accounts.
  • Within the VINCI Energies Switzerland Group and in contact with customers and suppliers, you are also responsible for information, clarifications and general correspondence in German and English. This will make it easier for you to get started.
  • After completing your basic commercial training, you have already gained 2 to 3 years of professional experience in finance and accounting and ideally trained in this area.
  • You use your IT user knowledge, especially MS Office and SAP, as well as your good English skills routinely and with pleasure in daily work.
  • As a resilient, well-organized and service-oriented personality, you like to work independently and find suitable solutions on your own initiative. This is what awaits you
  • You are important to us - we offer you an open, appreciative and family environment.
  • We are successful - with us you have a long-term and secure perspective.
  • We take part in our success – with employee shares and bonuses.
  • Competent managers ensure exciting projects and functioning workflows.
  • With us you have a wide range of training opportunities – we are happy to support you.
  • We work 40 hours a week. Our department, which is part of the Shared Service Center Zurich and supports several ETAVIS companies, is responsible for numerous tasks in finance and accounting and consists of 9 employees: 1 department head, 3 financial accountants as well as 1 team leader and 4 clerks for debtors and creditors.
