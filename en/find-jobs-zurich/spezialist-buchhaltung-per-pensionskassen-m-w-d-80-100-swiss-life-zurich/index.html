Full description

Shape the future. Yours and ours.

Would you like to help people lead a self-determined life? And do you want to use your creative scope to reconcile your professional and personal ambitions? Then we can offer you an exciting prospect: at Swiss Life you will be able to deploy your individual talents and expertise in a stimulating and flexible work environment. What’s more, you will be given a good measure of responsibility and can thus contribute to shaping the future of Swiss Life.

Your area of responsibility

  • You are responsible for the accounting of several company-owned pension funds and also supervise the corresponding secondary books, especially for securities and real estate
  • You prepare the annual accounts independently and prepare the annual accounts including annexes according to Swiss GAAP FER 26
  • For auditors and external stakeholders, you compile the necessary documents and act as contact person during the annual audits.
  • At the annual meetings of the Board of Trustees you present the revised annual accounts
  • In addition, you are actively involved in projects for the further development and optimization of the accounting processes and the accounting software used.
  • You work closely with internal and external stakeholders such as computer science, technical administration, management and audit offices.

Your strengths

  • You have a basic commercial training, ideally supplemented by further training in finance and accounting or in the area of personnel provision
  • Several years of professional experience of at least three to five years in pension fund accounting distinguishes you
  • When dealing with MS Office, especially Excel, you are well-versed. Knowledge of accounting software Abacus are an additional advantage
  • Your way of working is motivated, structured and team-oriented
  • You also convince with your organizational talent, your independence and flexibility as well as the ability to set priorities correctly and meet deadlines reliably.
  • You have very good knowledge of German and Italian in word and writing
  • Good knowledge of French is an advantage

Your benefits We help our employees to shape their professional development and their private life to their best advantage and throughout all phases of their life. Our attractive benefits, such as options for working from home, 25 to 30 days of leave, 3rd pillar premium contributions and a broad range of continued training options contribution to this goal.

No liability will be accepted for spontaneous applications and any fees/costs incurred by them. Thank you for your understanding.
