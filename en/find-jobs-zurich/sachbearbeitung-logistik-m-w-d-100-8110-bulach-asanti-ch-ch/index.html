Administration Logistics (m/f/d) – 100% You bring movement to logistics and keep track of everything as it goes around? Then you've come to the right place!

Your responsibilities

  • You plan and optimise logistical processes.
  • You coordinate the flow of goods from procurement to delivery.
  • You analyze costs and keep inventory under control.
  • You coordinate suppliers, freight forwarders and internal departments.

Your profile

  • Several years of experience in logistics
  • Good IT and ERP skills
  • Analytical thinking and structured way of working
  • Team spirit, communication skills and a good dose of flexibility
