Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With more than 20 locations – including the Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli private clinics as well as the gerontopsychiatric services of Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in outpatient, day-clinic and inpatient settings. The Group employs around 1800 people and generated revenue of CHF 240 Mio. in 2025.

For our child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy department, we are looking for a/an for Ward Z2, with a treatment focus on “therapy programmes”, with immediate effect or by agreement

An interprofessional team ensures comprehensive psychiatric, psychotherapeutic and milieu-therapeutic treatment and care. This includes an individualised range of therapies, close contact with the system of origin and an assessment of the social situation. The aim of treatment is psychological stabilisation and the development of possible coping strategies. The therapy ward offers 10 places for patients. We would be pleased if you would also like to play an active role in the further development of our therapy ward.

Your responsibilities

  • active and attentive support for children and adolescents, as this is exactly what working in child and adolescent psychiatry means to us
  • individual and resource-oriented support for our patients within a milieu-therapeutic treatment setting
  • working with key persons and actively shaping interprofessional collaboration
  • needs- and resource-oriented stay and care planning
  • conduct individual and family sessions and lead therapeutic group sessions
  • organise, actively lead and support the daily routine and leisure activities
  • active contact with the system of origin
  • contributing to the further development of the immediate and broader factors influencing ward routines and treatment processes

Your profile You are flexible, innovative and enjoy variety. You bring professional expertise, enthusiasm for working with young people and plenty of humour combined with empathy. With these qualities, we achieve the best treatment outcomes for our patients, and we are delighted that you will become part of the team.

In addition, we would like you to have

  • a completed qualification as a qualified social pedagogue
  • experience in child and adolescent psychiatry

What we offer

  • Innovative employer with strong interprofessional collaboration
  • very good development opportunities with generous support for continuing education and further training
  • various discounts (e.g. with Reka-Checks, health-promoting offers, selected leisure activities and online shops)
  • 5 weeks of vacation, from 50. Year of age 27 days and from the 60. 32 days of life
  • excellent catering options at very fair prices and much more…

We would be happy to present our employee benefits to you in an interview.

Does this appeal to you? Our department manager Irene Walczewski will be happy to provide you with further information, Tel. +41 44 929 83 73.

We look forward to receiving your application via the application tool.
