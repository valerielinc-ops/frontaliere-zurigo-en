Tasks: In this role, you will build the bridge between research and decision-making: You will conduct evidence-based projects – including literature :14s and meta-analyses – and prepare scientific findings in such a way that they will be used for political design, administration and practice. Your work will thus have immediate social relevance. You are embedded in a dedicated team led by Dr. Doris Hanappi and Prof. Moritz M. Daum. Your tasks

  • Support in the search, coding and evaluation of research literature
  • Cooperation in the preparation of evidence synthesis in the field of education
  • Cooperation in the development of a new evidence tool
  • Support in scientific analyses and reports on evidence tool The evidence tool is a platform available worldwide to help find, merge and use research results from the field of education more easily and quickly for decision-making. For more information, please contact: https://jacobs Foundation.org/Fry/synthesis-ready-ev-site-repository/. Requirements:
  • Completed studies in psychology, sociology, educational sciences, economics or other relevant disciplines of social, educational and behavioural sciences
  • Interest or initial experience in empirical research
  • Interest in carrying out systematic literary reviews
  • Joy in application-oriented work, especially in Switzerland
  • Structured and reliable working methods
  • Native German knowledge, high text security in German and English We offer: Our employees benefit from a wide range of and attractive offers. For more information, see: https://www.uzh.ch/en/explore/work.html
