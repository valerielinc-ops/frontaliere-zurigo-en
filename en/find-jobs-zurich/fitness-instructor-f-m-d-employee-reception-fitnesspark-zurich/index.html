Full description

movemi AG is the leading company in the Swiss fitness industry. For our Fitnesspark, we are looking for motivated support in the fitness field. Become part of a holistic and successful training philosophy: fitness combined with relaxation and nutrition offerings.

Responsibilities

  • You ensure professional care for our customers throughout the entire training area
  • You advise and assist our guests at reception and on the phone
  • You professionally advise prospective customers about our offer and actively sell memberships
  • You handle customer inquiries
  • You serve our guests at reception and at the cash desk

Requirements

  • 1–3 years of experience: in a similar position and in consulting
  • Completed basic or advanced training: as a Fitness Instructor (or PE teacher ESSM, gymnastics or sports teacher with diploma)
  • You bring training or experience in the commercial field and have good IT skills
  • You have a strong service understanding and a winning personality
  • You are motivated and can work well in a team
  • You have a high level of flexibility regarding shift planning (7-day shift operations, evening, weekend, and holiday shifts)
  • You possess a valid BLS-AED certificate
  • German (very good proficiency)
  • English (advantageous)

What We Offer

  • Central work location: Our studios are located in a well-served central location
  • Mobile plan: National flat-rate plan from CHF 10.– / month (Swisscom / digitec connect)
  • Migros Bank: Expense-free private and investment account with preferential interest rates
  • Employee offers: Various discounts in the areas of travel and insurance (Hertz up to 10%, Generali 25%, Axa Insurance up to 15%, etc.)
  • Work climate: Dynamic, motivated company
  • Du culture: We stand for respectful interaction on an equal footing
