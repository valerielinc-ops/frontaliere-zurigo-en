Full description

Your passion meets our mission You will become part of a warm and supportive team in which team spirit and cohesion are given priority.

Your tasks

  • You care for patients in health crisis situations and bring all your empathy and expertise to make vital decisions.
  • You design the care in close cooperation with the parents and help ensure that the families feel well looked after even in crisis situations.
  • You expand your knowledge daily and complete your practical training in our intensive care unit and in various external internships in order to gain a variety of experiences.
  • You will attend the modular theoretical training at the Z-INA in Zurich as well as supplementary courses at the Children's Hospital in Zurich to constantly expand your knowledge.

Your profile

  • You have already obtained your diploma in nursing HF or FH and ideally bring one year of professional experience in pediatrics.
  • You are a team player and appreciate working in a supportive and collaborative environment.
  • You are flexible in your work organization and like to make decisions, even in stressful situations.
  • You enjoy learning and want to continuously develop your skills.

Dipl. Nurses for the post-graduate degree programme HF Intensive Care Paediatrics

Their tasks

Would you like to develop into a highly qualified team member in a practical and fully professional manner? You can attend the modular theoretical training at the Z-INA in Zurich (www.z-ina.ch). You will complete the practical part of the further education at the intensive care unit and in the Skillslab of the Children's Hospital Zurich. Our vocational school teachers and the practice assistants provide you with competent support. During further training, you will be employed at the Children's Hospital in Zurich and will be paid.

They bring with them

We offer

With us you feel the pulse of life and are working in a university hospital with an excellent reputation and international charisma. The well-being of children and their relatives is our priority. Would you also like to contribute to this and become part of our team? You can expect varied and challenging tasks in an interdisciplinary team. We follow and live the latest nursing practice. In addition, we offer a broad internal training offer. Attractive part-time offers and five weeks of holidays round off our offer. We are happy to invite you for an individual insight day or to one of our information events about the continuing education course.

Direct your application Please contact us via our online portal or by post to: Kinderspital Zürich – Eleonorenstiftung, Sonja Auf der Maur, departmental personnel manager, Steinwiesstrasse 75, 8032 Zurich. Visit our homepage www.kispi.uzh.ch.
