Full description

The KSW Emergency Center is one of the largest emergency centers in the region and unites the departments Emergency Adults, Emergency Children and Adolescents, Emergency Practice, Emergency Bed Station and Emergency Reception under one roof. Around 65,000 patients receive interprofessional care every year – quickly, competently and with high quality. For the emergency bed station we are looking for a committed and innovative personality immediately or by agreement – as Stv. department head / Stv. department head care.

Tasks

  • You support and represent the head of department care emergency bed station in all management tasks and are part of a committed, interprofessional team on our interdisciplinary emergency bed station
  • You lead, promote and accompany your directly subordinate employees, take over the service planning and ensure a smooth process in day-to-day business.
  • Part of your workload is active on the emergency bed station over all three shifts; the management and administration tasks you do in regular office hours
  • You act as a role model, strengthen the team culture and bring in new impulses to continuously develop the high quality of care and interprofessional cooperation.

Profile

  • You are Dipl. Nurse and bring several years of experience in the acute hospital
  • You have already gained leadership experience or are motivated to develop into this role – you bring a corresponding leadership education or complete it in a timely manner
  • You have strong social skills, communicative strength and joy in working in a large, diverse and interdisciplinary team.
  • You think along, act forward-looking and contribute with your commitment to a constructive, solution-oriented cooperation
  • You value a dynamic environment where you can take responsibility, shape it and use your expertise on a daily basis.

Benefits: Work and private life should fit together. Therefore, the KSW offers you – where the company makes it possible – flexible and flexible working hours, home office, generous maternity leave, paternity leave and employment levels that can be adapted to different phases of life. Develop yourself further professionally and personally: Every year, around 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses are open to you at KSW. We also support external training – temporally and/or financially.

  • Sometimes it needs support. In the event of personal stress, prolonged illness or difficult experiences in your professional or private life, various confidential discussions are available at KSW.
  • At KSW we work together and for each other. Appreciation, open and respectful communication and cooperation across professional groups and departments shape our everyday work. You should be able to contribute with your ideas and strengths and develop.

Your future is also ensured: You benefit from very good social benefits and a pension fund solution with BVK, which is significantly above the legal minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, you are very well covered as a KSW employee.

  • Your health is important to us. Benefit from discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training and courses as well as discounts in pharmacies and selected health insurance companies.
  • Family and work can be well connected with us. The daycare center La Luna with spacious opening hours is right next to the KSW. In addition, the KSW supports you – depending on income – financially with the crib costs.

There is also something to discover outside of work: Thanks to our cooperation with cultural partners in Winterthur and the surrounding area, you regularly benefit from discounts for concerts and theater performances.
