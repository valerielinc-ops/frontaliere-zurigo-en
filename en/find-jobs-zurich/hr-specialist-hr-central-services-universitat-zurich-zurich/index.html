Full description

Personnel department

Job start 1 January 2027 or by arrangement, indefinitely

The University of Zurich (UZH) is the largest university in Switzerland with over 28,000 students and around 10,000 employees. The Human Resources department offers – in cooperation with the decentralised HR managers – all services and consulting services, processes and systems of an integrated human resources (HR) management for all university units, employees and managers. Our newly created team «HR Zentrale Dienste» supports the responsibilities of university management members in all important HR processes – from employment to retirement – and also takes on more complex HR business. For this purpose, we are looking for committed HR specialists who are inspired by challenges, want to take on responsibility and enjoy actively building the newly created team «HR Zentrale Dienste».

Tasks

  • Advice/support of employees and managers in the assigned university management area regarding general HR topics along the employee life cycle.
  • Administrative handling of HR standard processes within and outside the HR systems incl. organisational and post management.
  • Writing job advertisements, accompanying recruitments and selections as well as onboarding and resignations.
  • Clarify basic questions about wage and social insurance.
  • Administrative handling of time and absence management, in close cooperation with HR specialists: inside the personnel department.
  • Accompaniment of cases outside the HR standard processes with the involvement of HR advice of the personnel department and managers.
  • Assistance of employees and managers in the use of digitally supported HR processes.
  • Participation in HR projects of the assigned university management area and/or the staff department.

Profile

  • Professional examination HR specialist or equivalent (e.g. B.S.c., CAS).
  • Several years of professional experience in HR, preferably at a university.
  • Quality awareness, careful working methods, consulting skills and negotiating skills.
  • Communication Strength: Promote with your communication style You have a constructive and appreciative cooperation with internal and external contacts.
  • Teamwork, positive and motivated attitude.
  • Networked thinking, initiative action, assertiveness and a quick understanding.
  • If you are eligible to lead the team, leadership skills and experience are mandatory
  • Interest and commitment in process optimization and change processes
  • SAP/HR knowledge, preferably users: Inside knowledge SAP SuccessFactors.
  • Very good knowledge of MS-Office programs.
  • Very good knowledge of German as well as good knowledge of English, oral and written.
