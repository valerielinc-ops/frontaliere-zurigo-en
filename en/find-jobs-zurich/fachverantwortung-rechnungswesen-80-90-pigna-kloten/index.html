  • Nearly 280 employees support, support, look after and employ people with disabilities in their living, working and living space. As of January 2027 or by agreement, we are looking for an experienced and committed personality who can help shape our accounting with expertise, personal responsibility and pleasure in numbers.   • Main and secondary books incl. Creditors, creditors, asset and operating accounting and cost accounting (IVSE)

  • Responsibility for VAT settlement by flat-rate tax rates
  • Monitoring and ensuring liquidity
  • Preparation of monthly and annual accounts according to OR and Swiss GAAP FER 21
  • Cooperation in budgeting, controlling and analysis
  • Preparation of meaningful basis for decision-making

  • Mind. 5 years of professional experience in a comparable function, ideally in the NPO, social or health sector
  • Eidg. Financial and accounting certificate or equivalent training
  • Balance sheet and balance sheet assurance according to OR as well as knowledge of Swiss GAAP FER 21
  • Excel skills are excellent
  • Abacus knowledge is beneficial
  • High IT affinity and pleasure in process optimization and digitalization
  • Self-employed, responsible and well organised working practices
  • Precise, structured and reliable approach – even in demanding situations
