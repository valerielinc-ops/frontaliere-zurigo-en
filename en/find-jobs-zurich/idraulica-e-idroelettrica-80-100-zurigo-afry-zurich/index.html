Full description

Become part of our HYDRO POWER team. Our area of expertise lies in the planning and implementation of hydroelectric power plants with the best possible preservation of natural habitats and the satisfaction of increasing safety needs.

Take on responsibility with us as

PROJECT ENGINEER HYDRAULICS AND HYDROPOWER (80-100%) - ZURICH YOUR TASKS

  • Planning of hydropower plants together with an interdisciplinary team
  • Design of hydraulic structures
  • Design of fish bypasses
  • Checking the flood safety of dams
  • Analytical hydraulic calculations
  • Numerical simulations (2D and 3D)
  • Preparation of technical reports and presentations

YOUR PROFILE

  • Completed studies as a civil engineer at ETH or TU with a specialization in hydraulic engineering with a very good degree certificate
  • PhD degree is an advantage
  • Fluent in German and English, other language skills are an advantage
  • Customer-oriented and team-oriented personality with a high sense of quality and responsibility
  • Willingness and enjoyment of worldwide assignments abroad

YOUR OPPORTUNITIES

  • Exciting, challenging and versatile projects with a wide range of tasks at home and abroad
  • Targeted personal development opportunities, e.g. to become a project manager, subject matter expert
  • Flexible and mobile way of working with modern infrastructure in Zurich Altstetten
  • Helping to shape a sustainable future

Become part of the AFRY success story! We offer creative freedom and personal responsibility, a collegial and international working atmosphere, modern employment conditions, flexible working models, above-average social benefits, attractive development opportunities and generous participation in the public transport subscription as well as a Half-Fare travelcard so that you can easily reach our modern office buildings in central locations.
