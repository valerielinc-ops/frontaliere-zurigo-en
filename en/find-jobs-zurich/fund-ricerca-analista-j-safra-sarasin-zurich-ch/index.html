Fund Research Analyst • Zurich, CH • Full-time • Unlimited Your Role As Fund Research Analyst at Bank J. Safra Sarasin you are a team- and performance-oriented, well-organized individual, who has a high interest in financial markets and products, and provide first-class research and advisory service to our discretionary fund managers and our Client Relationship Managers (CRMs).

Your Responsibilities

  • Analyze different equity related to active and passive funds
  • Advise the discretionary portfolio management team within your coverage
  • Advise CRMs on recommended funds
  • Create publications, e.g. recommendation lists
  • Act as a deputy within the fund team and broaden your generalist know-how
  • Support colleagues with fund due diligences

Your Profile

  • University degree in Business Administration, Economics, MBA or completed apprenticeship or similar banking education
  • CFA or CEFA is a plus
  • At least 2–3 years of professional experience in the areas of fund research, fund advisory or portfolio management
  • Experience with Morningstar, Bloomberg, AI
  • Strong analytical and problem solving skills
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills
  • Ability to work under pressure
  • Fluency in English and German

We are successfully operating for more than 180 years across 30 locations worldwide and offer our employees the chance to be a part of our success.
