Aging Psychiatric Acute Station Focus on Dementia, Delirity

The Kilchberg Sanatorium offers you a fulfilling working environment with exciting development opportunities in a dynamic and appreciative team. Bring your strengths and shape the future of the clinic. At Station B3, we work closely together as a lively, motivated and humorous interdisciplinary team.

Their tasks

  • Supporting and assuming activities of daily living in people 65+
  • Holistic, expert care of people 65+ in acute crisis situations
  • Implementation of the care process in the multidisciplinary team
  • Medical and administrative tasks

Your profile

  • Professional qualification nursing assistant SRK
  • Good teamwork
  • Interested in working with elderly people
  • Enjoyment of psychiatric and somatic care
  • Aggression management basic course available or willing to complete it

The Kilchberg Sanatorium is one of the most traditional psychiatric private clinics in Switzerland and looks back on almost 160 years of history. Only 15 minutes from the city centre of Zurich with beautiful views of Lake Zurich and the Alps. As a Listespital, we offer comprehensive psychiatric care for all insured persons and have inpatient and outpatient facilities in Kilchberg and Zurich. Our approximately 650 employees from over 25 professional groups are at the heart of the clinic and are committed to the best possible treatment of our patients. Become part of our team and shape the future of psychiatric care.
