About us

We are a large school community in Zurich-Nord with 19 schools. We derive our pedagogical attitude from the concept of New Authority and thus meet the daily challenges in everyday school life together and effectively. At secondary schools, teachers teach in the teaching sequences of self-organized learning (SOL) level-mixed. The Schulhaus Campus Glattal introduces the “FreiDay” learning format in addition to the SOL for the school year 2026/27. The focus of our school and teaching development lies in the areas of resource orientation and promotion of self-responsible and cooperative learning.

The day school Campus Glattal started operation with 4 secondary classes at the beginning of the 2022/23 school year and grew to 16 secondary classes in the 2024/25 school year. The day school Campus Glattal has its own kitchen and rooms for the lunchtime care of all students. Teachers have the opportunity to work in the midday care at most once a week. We attach great importance to the cooperation of the entire school staff and to a holistic view and support of the young people.
