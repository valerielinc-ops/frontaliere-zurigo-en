About the role

Job Summary The Sales Engineering Specialist supports the sales process by providing technical expertise and helping customers understand how products meet their needs. This role bridges the gap between engineering and sales, ensuring technical accuracy and customer satisfaction. This job profile is intended for professionals who apply advanced knowledge of a job area. Required to manage projects / processes independently with limited supervision. Typically coaches and reviews the work of lower level professionals. Summary of position

As a Technical-Commercial Engineer at Sulzer Ensival Moret France SASU, you will be responsible for the development of sales and technical support of our pumping and agitation solutions with our industrial customers. You will combine your technical expertise with your business skills to identify customers' needs, offer tailored solutions and conclude sales.

Responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain a portfolio of existing customers and explore new customers in France.
  • Analyze the technical needs of customers and propose optimal solutions based on our product range (pumps, agitators, etc.).
  • Develop detailed technical and commercial offers in collaboration with internal teams (engineering, production).
  • Negotiate contracts and terms of sale in accordance with the company's commercial policy.
  • Provide technical monitoring of projects, from the design phase to commissioning, ensuring customer satisfaction.
  • Participate in trade shows, conferences and customer events to promote the products and image of Sulzer Ensival Moret.
  • Conduct technical and commercial presentations to clients.
  • Conduct regular reporting of business activity and sales forecasts.
  • Maintain a technological and competitive watch on the market.

Qualifications

  • Degree in engineering (BAC+5) in mechanics, electromechanical, process engineering or equivalent field.
  • Significant experience (minimum 3-5 years) in a technical-commercial function, ideally in the field of industrial equipment (pumps, valves, agitators) or fluids.
  • Strong technical knowledge in fluid mechanics, hydraulics and/or industrial agitators.
  • Excellent relational, communication and negotiation skills.
  • Ability to work independently and as a team.
  • Mastery of French and technical English (written and oral).
  • Mastery of computer tools (CRM, Office pack).
  • Valid driving licence and availability for frequent travel within the national territory.
  • English required (minimum B1)
