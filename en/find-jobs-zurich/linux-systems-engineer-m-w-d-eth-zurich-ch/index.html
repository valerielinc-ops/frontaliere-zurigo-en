Linux Systems Engineer (m/f/d)

80%-100%, Zurich, permanent

The ID Section Educational IT Services (EduIT) supports contemporary, learning-effective and innovative teaching at ETH with a coordinated portfolio of tools, services and support. The portfolio (e.g. Moodle, EvaSys, EduApp) supports lecturers and students along the entire high-school teaching practice, starting from the learning goals and the design of learning activities, teaching, exams and evaluation.

EduIT is responsible for the service of digital audits at ETH Zurich and is a leader in Switzerland. This includes around 350 examinations on ETH computers with a good 27,000 examinations per year as well as formative tests and intermediate examinations on student devices. EduIT operates two large Moodle instances with tens of thousands of active users and develops open source learning technologies such as Moodle plugins or the Safe Exam Browser (SEB). These tools are used at universities worldwide.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for 1. January 2027 an experienced and dedicated personality as Linux Systems Engineer (m/f/d).

Job description

In this function, you can expect a varied activity with a lot of creative freedom in a well-established team. You are instrumental in ensuring the technical functionality of the systems as well as in developing and successfully implementing technical innovations in the field of teaching/learning technology. In addition, you expand the scalability of our services in the context of increasing student numbers through automation and process development and maintain versatile contacts within the IT services and the ETH.

This versatile position includes the planning, development and operation of the IT systems and services for teaching with the following exciting tasks:

  • Ensure the operation, integration, scaling and optimization of IT systems in the service portfolio of the EduIT section
  • Further development of the IT architecture for the Digital Audit Service
  • Further development of the deployment solution for exam computers (Exam Portal)
  • Maintenance and further development of Moodle maintenance pipelines (Moodle Zero Downtime)
  • Responsibility for the ETH JupyterHub as part of the Computational Competencies Project
  • Collaboration on other innovative projects in the field of teaching technology
  • Main responsibility for monitoring and alerting EduIT systems
  • Close cooperation with IT Operation Managers and service managers
  • Active contribution of own ideas and independent implementation of these

As a Linux Systems Engineer at ETH Zurich, you can expect exciting technical tasks, innovative projects in the field of teaching technology and a varied exchange with various interfaces and customers. Driven by the increasing number of students, we always strive to further automate and professionalize our services.

Within our nine-member team (plus three learners), we attach great importance to a collegial approach. An interesting and versatile position awaits you in an extremely dynamic university environment where you come into contact with different technologies. Nothing stands in the way of rapid integration into the team and the university environment through mutual support, short communication paths and a lived du-culture.

Profile

  • Completed vocational training or university degree in computer science or related field
  • Certifications in the field of Linux (LPIC, RHCE, RHCSA)
  • In-depth knowledge in the following areas:
  • Linux/Unix System Administration (primarily Red Hat)
  • Linux server applications (including web server, DB, proxy)
  • Monitoring (Zabbix, Grafana, Prometheus)
  • PHP-based web applications
  • IT security in web hosting
  • Automation of server deployment and configuration management (Ansible, Semaphore, GitLab CI)
  • Kubernetes and Docker as well as continuous integration and deployment
  • Good knowledge of the following areas:
  • Administration of open source databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB)
  • Scripting and programming (Python, Go)
  • Networks (basics)
  • Experience with Moodle and Software Testing (Playwright, pytest)
  • Certification in the field of Kubernetes (CKA) is an advantage
  • ITIL Foundation certification and experience in the university environment as a plus
