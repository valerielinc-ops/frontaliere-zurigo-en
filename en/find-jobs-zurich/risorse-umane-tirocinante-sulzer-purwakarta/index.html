Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

Job Summary Assist the HR team with end-to-end recruitment activities, including candidate sourcing, resume screening, interview coordination, maintaining recruitment records, job posting administration, and supporting onboarding processes to ensure an efficient hiring experience. Key Responsibilities

  • Assist in sourcing candidates through job portals, social media, and internal databases.
  • Screen resumes and shortlist suitable candidates based on job requirements.
  • Coordinate interview scheduling between candidates and hiring managers.
  • Communicate with candidates regarding interview arrangements and application status.
  • Maintain and update candidate records in the Applicant Tracking System (ATS).
  • Support the recruitment team in posting job advertisements and monitoring applications.
  • Prepare recruitment-related documents, reports, and interview materials.
  • Conduct reference checks and assist with pre-employment documentation.
  • Ensure a positive candidate experience throughout the recruitment process.
  • Provide administrative support for recruitment and onboarding activities.
