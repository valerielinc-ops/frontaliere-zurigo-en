About the role

The Senior Field Service Technician is responsible for installing, configuring, calibrating, and repairing technical products and systems at customer field locations. This role performs a variety of maintenance and technical support tasks and may provide onsite training to customer support personnel. This job profile is intended for experienced support-level staff that generally works autonomously and only requires closer supervision in non-routine situations. Purpose of the Position Provide expert field service, technical support, and supervision for the installation, commissioning, inspection, maintenance, and repair of rotating equipment, with a primary focus on centrifugal pumps. The role ensures the reliable and efficient operation of customer assets by delivering high-quality technical services both at customer sites as well as at the company's Service Centers in Italy and Spain. The successful candidate will act as a technical representative of the company, contributing to customer satisfaction, equipment reliability, and business growth through professional field service and engineering expertise.

Description of Specific Functions

  • Perform the installation, commissioning, and start-up of new centrifugal pumps and related rotating equipment at customer sites across Italy, Spain, Portugal, and worldwide, ensuring compliance with Sulzer standards, customer specifications, and industry best practices.
  • Execute all activities associated with equipment installation and commissioning, including mechanical erection and assembly, foundation and baseplate verification, levelling, laser shaft alignment, pre-startup inspections, operational testing, performance verification, troubleshooting, and collection of operational and performance data.
  • Carry out inspection, maintenance, troubleshooting, overhaul, and repair activities on rotating equipment at customer facilities as well as at Sulzer Service Centers in Italy, Spain, and, when required, other Sulzer service locations worldwide.
  • Utilize specialized diagnostic and maintenance tools and equipment, including laser alignment systems, vibration analysis instruments, precision measuring devices, and a wide range of mechanical and hydraulic hand tools, to ensure safe and accurate execution of field and workshop activities.
  • Prepare comprehensive field service documentation, including daily and monthly service reports, commissioning records, inspection findings, technical recommendations, and corrective action plans, providing clear and timely communication to customers and internal stakeholders.
