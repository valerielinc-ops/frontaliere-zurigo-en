Full description

Our category management at Digitec Galaxus is responsible for the development, purchase and marketing of our product offerings in our online shops. They set up a well-available, broad and up-to-date range with market-oriented prices and always focus on the customer.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Headquarters, Pfingstweidstrasse 60b, 8005 Zurich

Mansioni

  • Partner management and development, including renegotiation of contracts and purchasing conditions
  • Coordination and implementation of discussions/meetings with suppliers regarding qualitative topics
  • Optimization of the profitability of suppliers (purchase prices, purchase costs, RMA costs, etc.)
  • Automation of interfaces to increase efficiency and training of suppliers in internal systems for smooth operations
  • Responsibility for controlling and optimizing the qualitative supplier KPIs in terms of reliability, delivery time, inventory data, billing and delivery note quality
  • Co-responsibility for achieving CM’s revenue, margin and availability targets

Requirements

  • 0-2 years: Category Management / Strategic Procurement in E-Commerce
  • Completed studies (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): Business Administration, Marketing, Commerce, Business Informatics, International Business
  • For this varied and demanding position, you will advantageously bring a higher completed education (Bachelor or Master, ideally in close business disciplines)
  • In order to be able to master your tasks confidently, your solution- and service-oriented, independent and self-responsible working method as well as high organizational, structuring and networking skills will help you.
  • You are analytically strong, well-organized and inspired by your enthusiasm and curiosity.
  • You are familiar with the MS Office applications and a pivot table is not a foreign word for you
  • Negotiable knowledge of German and good knowledge of English in word and writing are mandatory prerequisites for the exercise of this function.
  • A good sense of customer needs, high resilience, motivation, strong curiosity for our products as well as an impeccable reputation are of course available
  • German (flowing)
