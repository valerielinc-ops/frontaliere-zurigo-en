About us

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

The health center for the old Käferberg in Wipkingen in the district 10 combines various care and care offers under one roof and is characterized by many years of experience in old-age medicine. Thanks to the unique location on the Käferberg, residents, patients and guests enjoy a wonderful view of Lake Zurich, the Alps and the rooftops of the city. Here you work together with 400 colleagues.
