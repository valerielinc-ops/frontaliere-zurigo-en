Full description

This is what awaits you

As a CV learner or learner, you are part of the team of the Biel General Agency from your first day. You will get to know the insurance industry first hand and support us in our daily work.

This is what you do with us

The focus of the apprenticeship is your training as a commercial employee in the private insurance industry.

Through various department changes, you get a comprehensive insight into the insurance world and get to know the everyday work step by step thanks to your curious and open-minded nature.

We will not leave you alone. An instructor accompanies you during your training and helps you to get to know the different facets of everyday insurance life.

This is what you bring with you

  • School leaving with good performance
  • Reliable work and willingness to learn new things
  • Open-minded, curious and helpful personality
  • Interest in the Insurance World
  • Good oral and written language skills in German and French

Working with us

A three-year basic education in the insurance industry awaits you with a varied training program and individual support.

The general agency Biel not only distinguishes the optimal location close to the station. No! A diverse team is waiting for you, who can hardly wait to accompany you during your education.

Your place of work is Biel and your apprenticeship starts on 1 August 2027.

This is what we offer

Care

We personally accompany you in your education. During your 3-year apprenticeship, you will be assisted by a vocational trainer.

Training

We will cover your training costs. In addition, we support you with examination simulations and training-specific further education.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate.
