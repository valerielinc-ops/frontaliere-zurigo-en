Full description

For our team Agriculture Agogik we are looking for 1. August or by arrangement you as Employee /-in Agriculture Agogik (80 – 100%) With your fine sense, you accompany people with an impairment in their daily work in the Agriculture & Gardening department. You take their needs holistically and tailor the activities independently, flexibly and creatively to them. With your commitment, you make a significant contribution to ensuring that the people we look after can participate in meaningful activities and participate in life in all its facets. Agriculture & Gardening currently employs around 34 specialists, 32 people at the protected workplace and 30 daycare workers. What matters is you. Every single person is at the center with their individual quality of life. We do everything we can to ensure that our approximately 250 residents, 246 daycareers and 52 employees at protected workplaces in the Wagerenhof can enjoy the highest possible quality of life. For employees of different backgrounds and professionalism, Wagerenhof is an attractive employer and a competent training partner for learners of all levels. For this we give you the responsibility

  • Accompanying, supporting and guiding people at work
  • Co-creation of animal services and daily work with animals
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation in the agricultural team and contact person for the residential groups
  • Keeping of documentation
  • Compliance with quality and safety regulations
  • Contact with internal and, if necessary, external bodies You are suitable for us and for this position, if you also put our residents and our staff in the center of your activities and you bring the following:
  • Work-agogic or socio-educational training or several years of experience working with people with disabilities
  • Joy of working with people with disabilities
  • Joy in the processing of natural materials
  • Several years of experience in agriculture and working with animals
  • A good eye for practical work processes in agriculture
  • Flexible and open for cross-team tasks
  • Good PC user knowledge This makes us the place for your work
  • The appreciative human togetherness
  • Technical assistance available at all times
  • The village character, the park-like structure of the Wagerenhof, as well as the good accessibility with public transport For questions please contact Carol Bolliger, Human Resources, 044 905 13 58.
