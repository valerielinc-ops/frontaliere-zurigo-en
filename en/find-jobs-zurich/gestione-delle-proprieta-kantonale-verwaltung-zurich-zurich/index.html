Full description

Would you like to use your experience in real estate management for an exceptional and versatile portfolio of the Canton of Zurich? In our dedicated property management team, you take on a responsible role in the management of our non-governmental properties. You have the opportunity to contribute your own impulses and to actively shape the digitalization of our management processes. As of January 1, 2027, we will take over a new portfolio. You accompany their takeover from the beginning and have the opportunity to actively contribute your experience and your ideas to the management. Their tasks

  • You are responsible for the management of your real estate portfolio and ensure professional support for the various properties.
  • They coordinate and organize intermediate uses and accompany them from planning to implementation.
  • They create analyses and reporting for ownership and other internal bodies.
  • You accompany structural measures and renovations in your portfolio from the point of view of real estate management.
  • They assess rental rates and determine the corresponding rental conditions.
  • They create billings, for example for the additional costs. Your profile
  • You have a degree as a real estate manager with a federal certificate and have several years of professional experience in real estate management.
  • They like to take on responsibility, work independently and are a reliable contact person for different stakeholders.
  • They are characterized by a high degree of customer orientation and communicate stylishly in German.
  • You are digitally affine and experienced in dealing with MS Office
  • Experience with REM or Garaio REM is beneficial.
  • They bring openness to new topics and enjoy getting involved in a versatile environment. With us, you can expect something different from a classic rental and commercial portfolio: You manage a versatile real estate portfolio, which also includes special properties and uses such as agricultural leases. This makes your task varied and gives you insights into very different facets of the real estate world of the Canton of Zurich. At the same time, you benefit from a strong network within the real estate office and work closely with various specialist areas. In this way, you expand your professional horizon, contribute your experience and get to know the Canton of Zurich from a new perspective.
