Full description

The hospital kitchen of the GZO Spital Wetzikon is one of the best in Switzerland: in 2023 it was named the "best hospital kitchen" in the country by public voting at the Viktor Award, which is considered the "Oscar of the healthcare industry". A key reason for this is the technical precision with which our team prepares up to 37 different diets for our patients without compromising on taste or presentation. As a dietitian/diet chef, you contribute significantly to precisely this expertise and ensure that medically necessary nutrition and culinary enjoyment come together. In addition, there are around 130 freshly prepared meals in the morning, at noon and in the evening, as well as around 300 meals for employees and the meals of the attached daycare centre and the lunch table of the Bärentswil School.
