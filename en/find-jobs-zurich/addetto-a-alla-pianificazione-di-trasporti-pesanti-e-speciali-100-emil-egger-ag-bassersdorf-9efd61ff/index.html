Full description

You don’t just plan transports – you make the seemingly impossible possible. When heavy-duty machines, exceptional transport goods and demanding routes come together, you need someone to keep track. With us you coordinate heavy and special transports from A to Z. You bring customers, drivers, authorities and our special vehicle fleet together and ensure that even demanding transports reach their destination safely and on schedule. The Emil Egger Group is the leading provider of heavy goods logistics in Switzerland. More than 230 employees work in the areas of crane work, special transport, industrial removals and rental lifts. With our comprehensive specialist know-how, we develop individual and innovative solutions for our customers every day. You move that with us

  • You take orders for heavy and special transports, record them and organize their complete handling.
  • You plan and schedule the special vehicles assigned to you and the assigned staff.
  • You organize special permits
  • You are the central contact person for our customers for short-term inquiries and appointment optimization.
  • You also keep track of short-term changes and find pragmatic solutions.
  • You accompany the transports organizationally from planning to successful implementation in cooperation with the project managers.
  • You keep an eye on the reporting and plan the assignments of the employees.
  • You are an organizational professional who always strives for the optimal utilization of your own vehicle fleet.

You bring that with you

  • Professional experience in disposition, ideally in heavy or special transport
  • Technical understanding and interest in special vehicles with their areas of application
  • Independent and solution-oriented working method as well as enjoying taking on responsibility Resilience, organizational talent and a pronounced hands-on mentality
  • Team players and masterminds in the "team" of dispatchers, project managers and chauffeurs
  • Winning handling of appointments and orders by our customers
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Secure handling of MS Office and common IT systems
  • Industry knowledge and an existing network are a plus

This is what awaits you Responsibility instead of routine: You do not coordinate standard transports. Your orders are individual, demanding and always bring new challenges. Technology that inspires: You work with a specialized fleet of vehicles and transport solutions that you don’t see every day. Variety guaranteed: Different transport objects, routes, customers and challenges ensure that no day is like the other. A strong team: You work with experienced professionals who share their know-how and find solutions together.

We also offer you

  • Progressive employment conditions and attractive social benefits
  • Modern infrastructure
  • Free parking
  • Lots of personal responsibility and short decision paths
  • An uncomplicated du-culture and family corporate culture
  • A secure workplace in an established Swiss company

Ready for transports that are not everyday? Apply now and become part of our team. We look forward to seeing you!
