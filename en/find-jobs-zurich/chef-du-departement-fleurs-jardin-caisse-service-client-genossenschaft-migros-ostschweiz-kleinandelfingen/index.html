Full description

Do you have an eye for beauty and are enthusiastic about the world of flowers? 🌸💐 Do you have the skills to bind in the bouquets, are creative and enjoy enthusing our customers with your floral creations? 😊 Then you are exactly right with us! ✅ Let us be «tämä success». 🤝🧡

Mansioni

  • You take over the professional responsibility of the area flowers 🌸 and cashier
  • Sales, cost, inventory and inventory management 📈📦 as well as sales promotion are your core tasks – you always have the overview and control specifically
  • Ordering, incoming goods checks, presentation and disposition 📋🚛✨ are in your hands – you make sure that everything runs perfectly
  • Friendly and competent customer service and advice 😊🛍️ are a matter of course for you and you will advise the customers with your expertise
  • It is fun to make individual bouquets 💐✨ and you take care of the plant care with care and passion 🌱🪴
  • You take care of the returns and deal with complaints

Requirements

  • Professional experience: Advantage in sales
  • Completed basic or further training: in the retail trade, industry flower; as a florist or gardener
  • You bring very good expertise in the field of flowers 🌸
  • Reliability, flexibility and a high level of commitment 💪⏰ distinguish you – you are always there when you are needed
  • With your independent work and your friendly approach 😊🤝 you create a pleasant atmosphere for customers and team
  • You are ready to continue your education 📚🚀 and stay up to date
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Occupational pension provision: Attractive pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Cumulus Points: Multiple Cumulus Points on food and nonfood items
