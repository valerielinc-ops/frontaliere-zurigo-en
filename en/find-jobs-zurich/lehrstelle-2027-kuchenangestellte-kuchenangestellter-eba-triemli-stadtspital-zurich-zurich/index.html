Full description

You like working in the kitchen. You enjoy preparing small appetizers and simple dishes and making them beautifully decorated for guests.

Tasks

  • You prepare simple foods.
  • You ensure that guests receive their food quickly and in good quality.
  • You prepare the ingredients for different dishes.
  • You clean the kitchen equipment and machines.
  • You store the food properly.

Profile

  • Compulsory schooling, middle or lower
  • A sense of cleanliness and order
  • Good sense of taste and smell, good health
  • Resilience and ability to work in a team
  • Willingness to work irregularly

We offer

We offer you more than just an apprenticeship! You can expect a well-founded and varied education in the health sector. You will be supported by us on your way to a successful teaching degree. Our city hospital world is characterized by an open du-culture, an uncomplicated, personal togetherness and the necessary touch of humor in everyday life. As a team of over 120 professional groups, we work every day in very different ways for the well-being of our patients. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions.

About us

With over 36'000 inpatients and around 455,000 outpatients per year, the Stadtspital Zürich ranks among the top 10 Swiss hospitals. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,500 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
