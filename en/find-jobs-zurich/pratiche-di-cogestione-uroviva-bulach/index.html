Full description

Tasks

  • Staff management of non-medical staff in the assigned practices in close coordination with the medical site manager
  • Project management and control in the field of practices
  • Responsible for efficient operational processes
  • Construction of new sites taking into account regulatory requirements
  • Creating and revising organizational manuals, work processes, work tools, work instructions, correspondence templates, etc.
  • Quality management officer of the entire practice network (sicing and further development of a uniform quality standard)
  • Responsible for compliance and control of budget planning
  • Developing the basis for strategic decisions in cooperation with the Operations Director
  • Cooperation as LMPA / MPA (for personnel bottlenecks) in the various locations

Requirements

  • Basic medical education (MPA or similar) and several years of professional and managerial experience of a medical practice
  • Further training as an MPK practical direction or similar further training
  • Commercial and / or business education advantageous
  • Very good knowledge of the tariff structures in the Swiss healthcare system
  • Good knowledge of quality management in healthcare
  • Knowledge of regulatory requirements
  • High willingness to provide services and travel
  • Strong social skills and a high sense of responsibility
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and action
  • Where possible, very good knowledge of Axenita
