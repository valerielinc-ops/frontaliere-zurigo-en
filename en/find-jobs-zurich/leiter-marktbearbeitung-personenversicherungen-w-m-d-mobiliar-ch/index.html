Full description

This is what awaits you: As a market development unit of Mobiliar, the Management of Brokerage Business in Zurich is responsible for the partnership with our management agents. For our market development personal insurance department, we are looking for an experienced manager who specializes in personal insurance. This is what you do with us: As Head: in Market Management Personal insurance you lead a team of seven employees and are responsible for operational market management in accident and medical day allowance insurance. In doing so, you ensure high-quality underwriting in compliance with the applicable underwriting guidelines, develop customer-specific insurance solutions and negotiate with customers and brokers. In addition, you promote the professional education and training of underwriters and actively contribute your know-how to specialist committees and projects. This is what you bring with you:

  • Long-term, practical knowledge of underwriting personal insurance
  • Several years of leadership experience
  • In-depth knowledge of the brokerage market
  • Strategic and integrative thinking as well as enjoyment of personal contact with our internal and external business partners
  • Implementation-strong and curious personality who communicates at eye level with steakholders
  • Further education in the insurance sector (e.g. professional card) and/or business administration
  • native German, good knowledge of French and/or English is advantageous Working with us: The workload is negotiable from 80% and your place of work is Zurich-Oerlikon. We offer you an exciting and varied job in a motivated and committed team in which an open communication culture plays an important role. This is what we offer: Compatibility We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund. Training and development We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously. Advantages Among other things, we offer you up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
