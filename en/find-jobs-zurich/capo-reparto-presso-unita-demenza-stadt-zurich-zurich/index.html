Full description

Do you like working with people? You want to start from the 1. November 2026 or by agreement with our care team for the health center for the Al-ter Käferberg?

Tasks

  • They lead the department in a goal-oriented manner and promote and challenge employees according to professional, social and economic principles.
  • They take responsibility for the holistic care and care of our residents.
  • They are responsible for quality development and ensure compliance with hygiene, care and training regulations.
  • They actively participate in the care and support the team.

Profile

  • Strong capacity for holistic and networked thinking
  • Rounded-off professional experience in the care of people with dementia, as well as the ability to attract employees to common goals and create a climate of trust and commitment
  • Completed training as a nurse (HF/FH/DNII) with SRC recognition for diplomas from abroad
  • Leadership training or willingness to undertake such training
  • Resilience, flexibility, high social skills and good communication skills, written and oral

We offer

Enjoy an exciting and varied area of responsibility in a professional and team-oriented working environment with du culture throughout the company. The human being is at our center. That is why we support the reconciliation of private and professional life.

As part of the city of Zurich and due to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, progressive employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

The annual salary for this function, depending on the experience, is between CHF 103’184.– and CHF 137’875.– (100%).

About us

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

The health center for the old Käferberg in Wipkingen in district 10 combines various care and care offers under one roof and is characterized by many years of experience in age medicine. Thanks to the unique location on the Käferberg, residents, patients and guests enjoy a wonderful view of Lake Zurich, the Alps and the rooftops of the city. Here you work together with 400 colleagues.
