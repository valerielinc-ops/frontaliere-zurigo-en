Full description

Tasks

  • Administrative management and support of real estate properties in the RIMO system
  • Organisation and implementation of housing acceptances and transfers as well as care of tenants
  • Cooperation in locking and access management including management of physical and electronic access authorizations
  • Reception, collection, triage and transmission of messages and orders relating to infrastructure and equipment
  • Support in parking management and support of external partners
  • Recording and coordination of orders in the Planon system
  • General administrative property activities
  • Support for the Finance department in the event of rental losses and heating and ancillary costs accounts

Requirements

  • Completed basic vocational training with EFZ
  • At least 3 years of professional experience, of which ideally 2 years in the relevant field
  • Further training in real estate management is beneficial
  • Stylistic and very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Good MS-Office knowledge
  • Knowledge of RIMO or comparable management software is an advantage
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Strong service orientation and enjoyment of contact with different stakeholders

We offer

  • A lively environment with different professional and stakeholders that you can actively shape with your own ideas
  • A meaningful job at a progressive employer with good working conditions
  • Own pension fund with above average employer share of 60%
  • Extensive internal training offer and support for external training
  • Staff apartments, parking spaces and perks in the EPI Park restaurant, pharmacy and half-tax or general subscription

The Swiss Epilepsy Foundation (EPI) is a multi-sector company. It includes the EPI WohnWerk, the Lengg High School, the donation Dapples, a seminar center with restaurant and the EPI Central Services. In addition, the Swiss Epilepsy Foundation together with another foundation forms the sponsorship of Klinik Lengg AG, each with a clinic for epileptology and neurorehabilitation. Around 1000 employees are committed to these social tasks. The area is located on the outskirts of Zurich, with a fantastic view of the nearby Lake Zurich and the Alps.
