Ready for a new challenge as a carpenter? For our clients in the Canton of Zurich (ZH), we are looking for reliable and experienced carpenters for temporary or permanent positions, starting immediately or by agreement.

02 Your duties

  • As a carpenter, you will work in a varied environment and be responsible for timber structures, erection work and timber framing.
  • You will also be responsible for prefabricated element construction in the workshop.
  • You are comfortable working with machinery and always ensure compliance with SUVA safety regulations.
  • You work on both pitched and flat roofs and are not afraid of heights.
  • Our clients place great value on collegial teamwork and open communication.

04 Your profile

  • Vocational training as a carpenter in Switzerland or an EU country
  • Good knowledge of German
  • 20 to 50 years old
  • Not afraid of heights
  • Good physical condition
  • Independent, flexible and reliable
  • Category B driver's license an advantage
  • Knowledge of occupational safety
