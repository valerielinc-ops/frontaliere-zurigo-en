Full description

At our location in Zurich Central, which focuses exclusively on commercial real estate, we address the individual and complex needs of our customers with specialist expertise and negotiating expertise. As a sparring partner at eye level, we focus especially on office and commercial properties in the larger regional catchment area. With targeted and tailor-made marketing strategies, we pursue the ambition to successfully lease each area and at the same time build sustainable, long-term relationships with owners and commercial tenants. If your heart beats for real estate, you bring a maker mentality and also like to work in a team, then we look forward to your application as a (senior) manager commercial real estate (m/f/d) Zurich Central, 80-100% This is what moves you:

  • You are responsible for the holistic management of a portfolio of commercial properties, including rental, contract and key management and ensuring proper condition
  • Through your expertise and negotiating skills, you lead successful negotiations and create business leases and supplements
  • You coordinate and carry out acceptances and handovers of commercial space and provide for a professional final settlement
  • Communication with owners, tenants, authorities and other stakeholders as well as the organisation of meetings and tenant orientations are your responsibility.
  • You are responsible for obtaining and evaluating offers, awarding contracts to entrepreneurs and controlling and releasing creditor orders and invoices
  • From a professional point of view, you lead the team, empower employees and contribute to the development and implementation of the property strategy.
  • You have a basic commercial education, you are a real estate manager with a federal professional certificate and ideally in possession of the diploma as a real estate trustee
  • You also have several years of professional experience in the real estate industry and in the management of commercial properties.
  • Digital affinity and in-depth PC and software knowledge are skillfully used daily in working with different systems
  • You are team capable, resilient and distinguished by your customer- and service-oriented personality
  • An efficient and goal-oriented working method as well as good communication skills qualify you in dealing with demanding target groups
