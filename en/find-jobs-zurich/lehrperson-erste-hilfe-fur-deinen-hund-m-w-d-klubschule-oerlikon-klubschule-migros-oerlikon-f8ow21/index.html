Full description

An inspiring meeting point to experience meaningful leisure activities! Thanks to the support of the Migros Culture Percentage, the Club School offers an attractive range of language, movement and creativity courses. Join our course participants in developing their talents and achieving their learning goals.

Luogo di lavoro: Club School Migros, Club School Migros Zurich Oerlikon, Hofwiesenstrasse 350, 8050 Oerlikon

Mansioni

  • Preparation, implementation and follow-up of teaching
  • Practice-oriented teaching according to adult education criteria
  • Lesson structure taking into account the different requirements of the course participants
  • Regular agreement with your contact persons of the club school administration
  • Participation in training

Requirements

  • Proven teaching experience in adult education: in the relevant subject area or in a comparable function
  • Completed basic or further training: as a veterinarian or veterinary practice assistant (TPA) with in-depth professional experience in the emergency area or an equivalent veterinary training or dog trainer
  • Customer-oriented, well-groomed and courteous appearance
  • Independent, flexible and reliable personality
  • Provide specialist knowledge varied, participant- and goal-oriented and inspire enthusiasm
  • Enthusiasm for teaching small to medium-sized groups of adults
  • German (good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Part of the Migros Group: As part of the Migros Group, you benefit from stable structures, diverse career opportunities and attractive benefits
  • Central location: Central locations with excellent public transport connections
  • Flexible working hours: Our flexible working hours support a balanced work-life balance
  • Handy subscription: discounted business conditions for private mobile subscription
  • Migros Bank: Private and investment account with preferential interest and expense-free account management
  • Diverse training offer: Generous offer of free, internal training, free participation in selected courses (national languages and health courses) and financial participation in the overall course offer.

Contatto

Application and contact Fabian Waldispühl Quality coach Health

Recruitment process

  • Submit application documents: For a complete application, we need your CV, certificates and diplomas. A photo in the CV and a letter of motivation are not necessary. We live equal opportunities for all applicants. We have a standardized recruitment process. If this does not work for you, mention it in your application dossier.
  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Personal video or telephone conversation: For a large number of applicants, we optionally conduct a personal telephone or video conversation. This gives us the opportunity to get to know more candidates within a short time in order to invite suitable people for the next round. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Expert discussion: In a specialist discussion, specific knowledge relevant for the execution of the job is queried by the manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, the team. If possible, there is time afterwards to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
