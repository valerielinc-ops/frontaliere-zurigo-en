Full description

As of 01. August 2027 or by agreement we are looking for a social worker in training 60-65%

Your responsibilities

  • Planning and organisation of rehabilitation and spa stays
  • Support for returning home (meal service, aids, specialized Spitex, domestic help)
  • Assistance in moving to a retirement and nursing home (long-term or transitional care)
  • Clarification of follow-up solutions for palliative situations
  • Advice to institutions for withdrawal and weaning therapies
  • Advice and information on social insurance: health insurance, accident insurance, AHV/IV, supplementary benefits, helplessness allowance, sickness allowance insurance, unemployment insurance, pension fund
  • Arrangement of contacts with advisory centres, offices and authorities
  • Advice on family issues, addictions, unemployment and triage to specialized institutions

Your profile

  • Current Studies in Social Work BSc
  • Ability to set operational priorities and enjoy interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Interested in organizing and coordinating suitable aftercare solutions for patients
  • Good computer user knowledge (MS Office)

What we offer you

  • Working independently in a motivated and collegial team that supports each other.
  • A place of work with best connections to public transport and low-cost staff parking.
  • Modern infrastructure and a working environment in which you can feel at home – lake views included.
  • Diverse benefits – from great employee discounts to further education offers.
  • A pleasant working atmosphere in an attractive location near Zurich, which has a lot to offer you both professionally and privately. Physical applications cannot be considered or returned.

Benefits

  • 42 hour week (Doctors 50)

  • Daily 20 min paid break
  • Changing time is considered as working time
  • Possibility for Home Office

Ongoing training

  • Internal training for employees
  • Participation in costs and working time for further education and training by employer
  • As an education and training hospital, we offer various internships and apprenticeships
  • Leadership Development Seminars

Rebates and conditions

  • Reduced nutrition advice and massage
  • Annual free flu vaccination
  • Hospital boat for rent
  • Free meals for night workers
  • In Horgen and the surrounding area, various selected companies grant at least 10% discount when presenting the identity card (e.g. Free Zoo Zurich annual tickets, 15% discount in the Alpamare, Brack.ch up to 25% and more).
  • Fleet discounts at amag partners (Audi, VW, Seat, Skoda), BWM and Mini as well as Mercedes and Smart

Extensive insurance

  • See-Spital offers 16 weeks of maternity leave at 100% wage continuation instead of the legally required 14 weeks at 80%
  • Premium for accident supplementary insurance (private) at the expense of the employer

Work-life balance

  • Possibility of part-time and hourly employment (on demand)
  • Flexible working hours (annual working hours)
  • Opportunity for unpaid leave

Occupational health management

  • Team and hospital-wide activities (Summer Festival, Christmas Apéro)

  • External case management
  • Generational management

Other benefits
