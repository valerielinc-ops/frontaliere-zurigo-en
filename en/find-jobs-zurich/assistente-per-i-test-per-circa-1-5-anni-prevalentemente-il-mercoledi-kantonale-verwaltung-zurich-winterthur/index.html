Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Vocational Advice Test Assistance for approximately 1.5 years (mainly Wednesdays) 20% The Office for Youth and Vocational Counselling (AJB) is part of the Education Directorate and is responsible for child and youth welfare, career counselling and scholarship granting. It provides the various consulting and support services centrally and decentrally at various locations in the Canton of Zurich. The biz Winterthur is one of 7 biz in the canton of Zurich and responsible for the city of Winterthur and the districts of Winterthur and Andelfingen. Through a wide range of offers, the biz Winterthur supports both young people and adults in their entire education, career and career. This takes place in the form of individual consultations, diagnostic clarifications, information events, seminars and the use of the information center. As of 1 April 2027 or by agreement, we are looking for a student in the Master's degree in psychology as a test assistant for about 1.5 years (mainly Wednesdays) assignments:

  • Individual testing with individuals or groups
  • Evaluation of the diagnostic procedures and feedback to the professional, study and career advisors on the behavior of the tested (performance, interest, motivation and personality tests, etc.)
  • Test management (test ordering, test PCs, material storage, etc.)
  • Delegate tasks from the person responsible for the diagnosis in the biz Winterthur (e.g. summary of a test, develop worksheet, create documents, etc.) Requirements
  • You are currently completing the final semester in the Bachelor’s degree in Psychology or have recently started your Master’s degree in Psychology
  • You are interested in professional, study and career counselling as well as test diagnostics
  • You have solid knowledge of test design and statistics
  • They show an independent and responsible way of working
  • High level of social skills
  • Reliability
  • Dealing with young people and adults in career choice or career planning gives you pleasure It is very versatile and interesting tasks await you in an active team and lively environment. We offer a careful introduction and employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines. The place of work is just a few minutes' walk from Winterthur Train Station. For additional information, Manuela Hofbauer, Head of biz Winterthur, tel. 043 259 82 30, is at your disposal. Further information can also be found at www.bizwinterthur.zh.ch

Pensum: 20%
