About the role

Job Summary The Lead (Field) Service Technician is responsible for installing, configuring, calibrating, and repairing technical products and systems at customer field locations. This role performs a variety of maintenance and technical support tasks and may provide onsite training to customer support. This job profile is intended for experienced support-level staff that generally works automatically and only require close supervision in non-routine situations. Summary of position

As a Service Technician / Lead (Field) Service Technician, you will be responsible for the installation, maintenance, repair and troubleshooting of our equipment on our customer sites in France. You will also act as a technical reference for the field service team.

Main responsibilities

  • Perform preventive and corrective diagnostics, repairs and maintenance on a range of industrial pumps and agitators.
  • Ensure the commissioning and installation of new equipment among customers.
  • Train customer operators in basic equipment use and maintenance.
  • Write accurate and detailed intervention reports.
  • Manage spare parts stocks required for interventions.
  • Provide first-level technical support to clients and the service team.
  • Identify opportunities for selling services or spare parts during interventions.
  • Participate in continuous improvement of service processes and tools.
  • Act as a technical leader for junior technicians, accompany and train them.
  • Ensure compliance with safety standards and quality procedures.

Qualifications

  • Bac+2 degree (BTS, DUT) in Industrial Maintenance, Electromechanical, or equivalent.
  • Minimum 5 years experience in the maintenance and troubleshooting of industrial equipment, ideally in the pump or rotating machinery sector.
  • Strong skills in mechanics, electricity and hydraulics.
  • Ability to read and interpret technical diagrams and plans.
  • Mastery of diagnostic and measurement tools.
  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Ability to work independently and take initiatives.
  • Valid driving licence and availability for frequent travel on French territory.
  • Master's degree in French (written and oral). Technical English is a plus.
  • Experience in coaching or team leadership would be an asset.
