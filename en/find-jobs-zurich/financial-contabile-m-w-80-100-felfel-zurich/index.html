IN BREAKDOWN

You are passionate about numbers, value precision and want to actively shape the growth of FELFEL? As a Financial Accountant (80-100%, now or by agreement) in Zurich Wiedikon, you will play a key role in our agile finance team. Here you not only shape numbers, but also shape the future of our company together with us.

Your responsibilities

  • Carry out day-to-day operations including main and secondary accounts (debitors, creditors, bank and payment reconciliations) for our Swiss company and support the day-to-day operations of our US company.
  • Settlements to customers and contribute to monthly, annual and consolidated financial statements under Swiss law.
  • Maintain and optimize our ERP system (Abacus / Microsoft Dynamics BC) to ensure its continuous scalability.
  • Act as a contact person for internal and external stakeholders on invoicing and accounting issues.
  • Participate in cross-divisional projects for the continuous development of financial processes.
  • Control communication as an interface to external partners such as ERP support, authorities and suppliers.
  • Assist in the preparation of VAT statements, sales and use tax reports for our US company as well as in the annual tax return.

What you bring

  • 2–4 years of professional experience in finance and accounting in a similar environment.
  • Degree as Eidg. Financial specialist in finance and accounting (or currently in appropriate training).
  • Excel knowledge and experience with ERP systems (Abacus / Microsoft Dynamics BC is an advantage).
  • Passion for numbers, structures and processes in an agile environment.
  • Independent, exact and reliable working method.
  • Strong team spirit and ability to communicate professionally with internal and external partners.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing.
  • Non-smokers: in – we don’t want you to smell like cigarettes, neither at work nor at customer appointments. After all, FELFEL is all about good food.
  • Valid work permit for Switzerland or Swiss passport.

What we offer

  • Lots of delicious food, just a few steps from your desk - we fulfill all your culinary desires...
  • Best barista coffee and tea for the whole team thanks to our innovative Gavetti coffee machine
  • 5 weeks of vacation per year
  • Every year we grant two whole weeks "Work from anywhere" and thus provide for variety in everyday work
  • We finance your half-tax subscription and thus support our employees in the use of public transport
  • Freshly baked parents receive 16 or more from us. 3 weeks of fully paid parental leave. We also support the return to work by making a generous financial contribution to childcare until kindergarten age.
  • Great team events, such as our well-known FELFEL Summer Outings
