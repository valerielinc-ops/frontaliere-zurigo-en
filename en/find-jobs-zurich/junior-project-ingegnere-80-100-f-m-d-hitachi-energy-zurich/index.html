Full description

The opportunity

We are seeking a motivated and detail-oriented Junior Project Engineer to join our high voltage gas-insulated switchgear engineering team. This role involves supporting project planning, design, and implementation activities. The ideal candidate will have a strong foundation in electrical or mechanical engineering, a keen interest in high voltage technology, and a desire to grow within the industry. Join our team!

How you will make an impact

  • Assist in the execution of customer projects and work together with lead engineers during different project phases
  • Support the design of gas-insulated switchgear systems, including single-line diagrams and layout drawings
  • Prepare and maintain project documentation in line with the applicable standards and customer specifications
  • Handle various supporting tasks within the engineering department, for example support in the creation of internal documentation and engineering guidelines
  • Process special material orders from R&D according to the defined process
  • Contribute to continuous learning and improvement through analyzing quality issues and supporting the implementation of improvements
  • Assist in troubleshooting and resolve technical issues to ensure project success
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams in Switzerland and India, for example engineering, project management, sales, supply chain management or production
  • Occasional travel to customer meetings or site visits

Your background

  • Technical qualification in Electrical or Mechanical Engineering
  • Initial experience in high-voltage switchgear engineering is highly desirable
  • Knowledge of SAP and 2D/3D design tools such as Creo or AutoCAD is considered an advantage
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications is required
  • Independent, structured, and proactive working style with the ability to collaborate effectively in an international team environment
  • Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving skills
  • Fluency in German and English; additional languages are an asset
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills with the ability to contribute to technical discussions and coordinate effectively with internal stakeholders, customers, and suppliers

More about us

Our flexible work practices help you optimize personal and business performance while creating an environment where all employees can develop their skills and grow.
