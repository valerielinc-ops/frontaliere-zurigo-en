Aufgaben

  • Contribution to the Institute's post mortem diagnostic service
  • Contribution to the Institute's biopsy and cytology diagnostic service

Anforderungen

  • EU-recognised veterinary degree
  • At least 12 months postgraduate veterinary work (eg. in practice or by working towards a doctoral degree)
  • Interest in pathology and in postgraduate training in this discipline; basic experience in the field is an advantage
  • Optimally scientific publication(s), eg. as part of a completed dissertation (Dr. med. vet., PhD)
  • Very good English and German skills (spoken and written)

We offer

  • Training in all aspects of diagnostic veterinary pathology, including forensic pathology and digital photodocumentation
  • Work in a large diagnostic team and with a broad range of species
  • Qualification for the certifying examination of the European College of Veterinary Pathologists
  • Salary according to the cantonal requirements
