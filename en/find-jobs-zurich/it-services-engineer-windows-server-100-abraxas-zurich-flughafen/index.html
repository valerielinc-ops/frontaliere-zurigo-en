Full description

You have several years of in-depth experience in Windows Server backend services (OS, ADDS, DNS, DHCP, etc.) and would like to deepen and further develop your expertise in the field of automated multi-domain orchestration of Windows Server? In addition, do you feel comfortable in the agile DevOps environment, where you encounter both development and operations tasks? Are you a communicative team player with strong interpersonal skills? Then you are the ideal candidate and should submit your application documents today if possible

To complement the 8-person team, we are looking for a committed person for our location at Zurich Airport, starting immediately or by agreement, as an IT Services Engineer.

Your role

  • Supporting the operation and further development of our Windows server services
  • Incident, change and service request handling (2nd and 3rd level support)
  • Advancing the standardization and optimization of our automation processes
  • Ensuring adherence to compliance, security and architecture requirements
  • Taking on and conscientiously completing work packages in line with planned sprint stories

Your profile

  • Completed degree in computer science or comparable education/further training (FH/HF)
  • Demonstrable several years of experience in the field of Windows Server architectures
  • Knowledge of automation, scripting and modern deployment methods is an advantage
  • Service provider experience is an advantage
  • Strong communication skills and ability to work in a team, as well as excellent spoken and written German (C1 level) and good English skills
  • Enthusiasm for taking on responsibility and contributing strategically
  • Willingness to provide on-call duty and occasional assignments outside office hours (remote)

Your benefits

  • Up to 60% working from home possible with a 100% workload
  • Purchase of additional vacation (2 weeks per year)
  • 41-hour week with flexible working hours for a full-time workload
  • At least 25 days of vacation per year
  • 100% financial or time support for continuing education
  • Free SBB Half Fare Travelcard for all employees

We foster an open first-name culture – so feel free to send us your application directly using the informal form of address.
