Full description

Children's Hospital Zurich Eleonorenstiftung | online since : 06.10.2026

In the IKM, bed planning, surgical programs, personnel deployment and data forecasts are intelligently networked to care for our patients at the right time and place.

Your tasks

  • You implement optimal surgical planning according to strategic and tactical specifications - taking into account all key resources (staff, beds, surgical capacities, equipment).
  • You schedule elective interventions in coordination with the specialist departments.
  • You continuously adjust the planning for short-term changes and prioritize urgent interventions.
  • You are with the patient or Relatives in contact regularly.
  • You control the completeness of relevant patient and planning documents.
  • You actively participate in process optimizations and quality measures.

Your profile

  • Health education (desirable with experience in OPS or anesthesia)
  • Experience in surgical planning
  • Ability for networked thinking and flexible, situational action
  • Resilience, teamwork and strong communication skills
  • Enjoyment of organizational and administrative activities
  • Good user knowledge in a CIS
