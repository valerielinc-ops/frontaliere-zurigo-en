Full description

Education Directorate Vocational School Uster Vocational School teacher mbA for vocational teaching (automatic) (18 weekly lessons) 69% From spring semester 2027 we are looking for a committed and initiative vocational school teacher mbA. You accompany and support our learners in basic vocational training (apprenticeship teaching). With us, you have the chance to develop new training concepts in an innovative and multifunctional learning and working environment and to implement your ideas creatively. Take a look at our website and get an impression. Your tasks

  • You provide our learners with practical expertise in vocational teaching for electronics technicians:inside and automatic technicians:in and accompany them on their way through basic vocational training.
  • You design a varied, practice-oriented and contemporary teaching and establish the reference to professional practice.
  • You promote the professional and social development of our learners and support them individually in their learning process.
  • You will continue to develop your lessons and use modern technologies, digital tools and innovative teaching methods.
  • You are actively involved in the student body and contribute to the further development of teaching and our school. Your profile
  • You have completed a technical degree and have the diploma as a vocational school teacher: in vocational studies in the main profession.
  • You have in-depth expertise and ideally bring practical work experience in your field.
  • It gives you pleasure to inspire young people for professional content and to convey your expertise in a practical way.
  • You are open to new forms of teaching and have a high technological affinity to effectively incorporate modern tools and digital media into your teaching.
  • You accompany our learners with commitment and social competence in their professional and personal development.
  • Your own initiative, team spirit and sense of responsibility characterize you. Our offer
  • A state-of-the-art infrastructure with diverse learning and work opportunities.
  • A motivated, collegial and creative team working together to further develop the educational landscape.
  • The opportunity to contribute your ideas in a dynamic environment and to actively shape the future of vocational training. Become part of our team and shape the educational world of tomorrow. We look forward to meeting you. If you have any questions, Debora Matthey (debora.matthey@bzu.ch ), Head of Human Resources, is happy for you.
