Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 40-100% The department somatic services of the competence center specialist development care of the management nursing, therapies and social work ensures an optimal treatment quality in close cooperation with the various stakeholders from everyday care. True to our motto "Innovation through diversity" and an orientation towards best practice, the specialist development is coordinated and guided by the competence center. We are looking for an immediately or by agreement Their tasks

  • Technical advice on infection prevention issues
  • Planning and implementation of hygiene training and coaching as well as sensitization of employees for hygienic standards and concepts
  • Planning and management of surveillance, hygiene audits and inspections, evaluation of risks and quality data, derivation of improvement measures and their implementation
  • Further development of the hygiene concept and creation and updating of instructions, standards and checklists
  • Collection and analysis of hygiene-relevant key figures, implementation of surveillance measures and derivation of suitable improvement measures, especially in outbreak situations
  • Organization, technical support and support of the hygiene committee as well as for hygiene-related procurement and conversion projects in an interprofessional context

Your profile

  • Diploma as nurse HF/FH or comparable education in the health care sector
  • Completed further training as a specialist expert for infection prevention in health care (SHFV) or willingness to complete it
  • Several years of professional experience in healthcare, ideally with knowledge in infection prevention and hygiene, surveillance and outbreak management
  • Experience with audits, quality development, key figures and training as well as further training in adult education is an advantage
  • Independent, analytical and cooperative work and the ability to transfer professional requirements effectively into practice.
  • Good digital user knowledge and very good German skills in words and writing
  • English skills are beneficial

Our offer

  • Knowledge that advances: Extensive initial, further and further training offers
  • Strong in the team: appreciative cooperation and mutual support
  • Focus on health: Various sports offers, green oases and an in-house care team
  • Sustainable travel: participation in the ZVV-BonusPass or general subscription
  • Well taken care of in everyday work: discounted food offers or lunch checks
  • Future provision with added value: 60% employer contribution to the pension fund
