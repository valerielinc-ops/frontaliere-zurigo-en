Full description

136982 | 80 - 100% | Oerlikon, Zurich | Hybrid

Does the idea of giving people safety and guidance in demanding damage cases motivate you? Do you want to handle complex material and liability damages independently and promote suitable solutions?

In this role, you are part of the Claims business unit in the area of Schaden Sach & Haft at Zurich Switzerland.

You take on the responsibility for handling complex material and liability claims and accompany our customers in challenging situations with clarity, security and empathy. You clarify factual, coverage and liability issues, calculate damages and are a reliable contact person for all parties involved.

This is what awaits you

  • You support and help people in damage cases by relieving them of handling difficult damage cases.
  • You clarify open questions and are responsible for oral and written inquiries from policyholders, injured parties, lawyers, authorities and insurance companies.
  • You have the damage handling under control, including the clarification of factual, coverage and liability issues as well as the implementation of damage calculations.

This sets you apart

  • Successfully completed commercial or equivalent training – ideally supplemented by an insurance certificate or a degree from a Higher School of Insurance (HF).
  • Several years of experience in damage processing.
  • Enjoying IT as well as the ability to quickly and securely familiarize yourself with new systems.
  • Rapid comprehension, a precise working method and pronounced self-organization.
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Other language skills are a plus.

Good to know

Place of work: Oerlikon, Zurich

Job: from 01.01.2027 or by agreement

Travel activity: until none

Hybrid work: up to 40%
