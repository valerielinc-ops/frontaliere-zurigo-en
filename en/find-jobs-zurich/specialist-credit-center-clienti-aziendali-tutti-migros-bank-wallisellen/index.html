Full description

With us, you can assume responsibility from A - Z for the loan applications until the contract comes into force. We are looking for you: A personality with a pronounced flair for numbers, exact working methods and high service orientation. Come on.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Bank, Wallisellen, Industriestrasse 17-19, 8304 Wallisellen

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Recording and processing of loan applications for mortgage, operational and investment financing of corporate clients
  • Balance sheet management: recording and controlling annual accounts, preparing rating and debt capacity
  • Telephone and written communication with corporate customers and authorities, obtaining information of all kinds (e.g. ZEK, management information, Teledata)
  • Accompanying the leasing business until full implementation of the contract
  • Support of corporate client consultants in technical issues

Requirements

  • min. 2 years: credit
  • Due to your experience in the corporate customer segment, you have good expertise in credit management
  • Experience in the processing of loan applications for mortgage, operational and investment financing of corporate clients
  • Exact and forward working methods and enjoyment of administrative activities
  • High service and customer orientation
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ)
  • in German (negotiation certain)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central workplace: Optimally accessible, centrally located workplace
  • Modern infrastructure: future-oriented and ergonomic workplace
  • Flexible place of work / Home Office: Individual possibilities for home office
  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks for mothers and 4 weeks for fathers
  • Child care: up to CHF 300 per month per child to external child care
  • Fair wages: Strong overall package of wages, social security and pensions
  • Social benefits: Optimal coverage for sickness and accident "Private"
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Cumulus points: Up to 5 additional points per franc purchase value
  • Migros Bank: accounts, mortgages and financial services with preferential terms
  • Employee offers: Annual generous well-being credit
  • Leisure and cultural offers: discounts of the M community on concerts, travel, etc.

Contatto

Application and contact Alexander Stauber HR Business Partner

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Personality/performance test: Under certain conditions, we use a personality test (BIP, Scales test, etc.) as an additional step to support selection. The results of the test may be discussed personally and form the basis for further selection. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Expert Discussion: In a specialist discussion, specific knowledge relevant for the execution of the job is requested by the manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, the team. If possible, there is time afterwards to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Reference information: When obtaining references, we ask for impressions, strengths and weaknesses again and have them verified by former supervisors or employees. References can be obtained after the first or second round and are only made with consent. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week

Grado di occupazione: 70 %
