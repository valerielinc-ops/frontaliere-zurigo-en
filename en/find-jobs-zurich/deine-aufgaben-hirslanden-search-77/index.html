Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Type of employment: Permanent

In this varied and responsible role, you can contribute your leadership skills and constantly develop quality in the economy with your team.

YOUR TASKS

  • Coordination of cleaning and quality controls in economics to ensure the highest hygiene standards
  • Ordering and warehouse management for consumables, including orders for medical practices
  • Leadership and development of your team through regular training, coaching and promotion of employees
  • Monitoring medical waste disposal processes and coordination with external waste management
  • Processing of complaints and continuous optimization of work processes in economics
  • Collaboration in the further development of the area of housekeeping and support, as well as in various projects

Your profile

  • Completed training in housekeeping, hotel or building cleaning (EFZ)
  • Completed management training level Divisional management Housekeeping/Hotelry
  • Several years of professional experience in cleaning or facility management, preferably in health care
  • Leadership experience or a clear willingness to lead and develop a team
  • Very good knowledge of MS Office and a good understanding of numbers
  • High social skills and the ability to work in a multicultural environment
  • Customer orientation, flexibility and an independent working style

What we offer

  • An attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich in an experienced, interdisciplinary team
  • Modern working atmosphere and well-founded familiarization
  • Flexible working hours incl. «Leisure Plus» with up to seven weeks of holidays
  • Compensation for changing time with four extra days off at 100% expense
  • Participation in transport costs (ZVV/Z-Pass, SBB coupons)
