Full description

Do you like working with people? You want to start from the 1. November 2026 with our education team for the nursing housing groups of health centers for old age?

Tasks

  • You are a qualified and committed specialist in the care and care of the residents, whenever possible together with the learners/students.
  • As a full-time vocational trainer, you are responsible for planning and implementing all processes relating to our learners and students.
  • Together with a dedicated education team, they are responsible for the quality assurance and continuous improvement of our training conditions.

Profile

  • Sound and reflective professional experience, preferably in the field of care for people with dementia
  • Completed training as a nurse (HF/FH/DNII) with SRC recognition for diplomas from abroad as well as a recognised further education (at least SVEB 1) or the willingness to complete it
  • First experiences in accompanying learners and students as well as pedagogical sense
  • Strong leadership skills that enable you to inspire employees for vocational training and develop them together with the management

We offer

Enjoy an exciting field of work in a professional and team-oriented working environment with a du-culture throughout the company. The human being is at our center. That is why we support the reconciliation of private and professional life, different working models make it possible.

As part of the city of Zurich and due to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, progressive employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

Depending on the experience, the annual salary for this function is between CHF 93'838.- and CHF 125'386.- (100%).

About us

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

The nursing housing groups of health centers for old age are composed of manageable residential communities and specialize in the needs of people with dementia and people with psychiatric conditions. The nursing home groups are located in various quarters of the city of Zurich. Here you work together with between 6-10 colleagues.
