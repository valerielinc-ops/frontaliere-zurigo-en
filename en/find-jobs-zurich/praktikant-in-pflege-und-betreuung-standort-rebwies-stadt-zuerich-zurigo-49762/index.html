About us

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

The Health Center for the Old Rebwies is in a prime location on the sun slope over Zollikon and offers residents a homely home with all amenities. The municipality offers an active village life as well as short distances to the lake promenade, the extensive forests and the city center of Zurich. Here you work together with 70 colleagues.
