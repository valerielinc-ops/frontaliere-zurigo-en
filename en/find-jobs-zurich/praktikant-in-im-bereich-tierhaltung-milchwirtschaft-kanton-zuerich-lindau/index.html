Full description

You will gain insights into the versatile consulting activities in the animal husbandry and dairy industry as well as in the experimental system. Through cooperation with our specialists, but also with agricultural companies, industry organizations and offices, you can complement your professional network with valuable contacts for your further career. You can also apply for this internship as an internship at ETH Zurich. Your tasks

  • You support and accompany our specialists around animal husbandry in projects, consultations as well as the implementation of practical tests (e.g. gal cow feeding, health monitoring calves, volcanoes in rearing piglets, health measures in sheep)
  • You work in interdisciplinary projects with various specialist bodies and organizations and contribute to the creation of important consulting and information materials for farms (e.g. leaflets for prophylactic dairy cattle feeding)
  • You accompany specialist courses and online evenings as well as the Strickhof Milchtag and Schweintag your profile
  • You are in training or a new graduate of the HF, HAFL or ETH Zurich with a focus on livestock farming and already bring practical relevance to agriculture and livestock farming
  • You like to work solution-oriented and independently with a structured working method
  • You are interested in active participation in the optimization of various operational and technical challenges of producing agriculture
