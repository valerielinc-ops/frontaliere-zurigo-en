Full description

With us, you actively shape the future in the field of artificial intelligence! You are significantly involved in the operation and further development of our AI Platform (OnPrem & Cloud) and drive it forward together in the committed (internal / external) team. We are looking for a personality who never loses sight of the essentials when immersed in concepts and code.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Bank, Wallisellen, Industriestrasse 17-19, 8304 Wallisellen

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You are responsible for the operation and further development of the hybrid AI platform (OpenShift & Azure) and ensure stability, scalability, high availability and efficient GPU use.
  • You ensure the smooth operation of the AI operation stack (e.g. MLflow, Dagster, vLLM, Triton).
  • You are responsible for the deployment and serving of LLM and ML models both on the on-prem platform (OpenShift) and via Azure services (e.g. Azure AI Foundry).
  • Operation and optimization of CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure as code. You define deployment and configuration standards and ensure security, compliance and vulnerability standards.
  • You provide a robust data foundation (RDBMS, Object Storage, NoSQL, Backup & Recovery) and accompany migrations from and between On-Prem and Azure.
  • You develop the AI platform into existing observability stacks and ensure stable platform operation (OpenTelemetry, Splunk, Dynatrace, Azure Monitor).

Requirements

  • At least 5 years: AI Engineering / Dev Ops
  • Master's degree (university, university of applied sciences): Computer Science / Computer Science
  • Several years of experience in setting up and operating AI platforms in hybrid environments with a focus on scalability, automation and high availability.
  • Very good knowledge of containerization, orchestration, automation and deployment patterns (e.g. Kubernetes/OpenShift, AKS, Helm, CI/CD).
  • Experience with GPU compute and AI inferencing (e.g. Nvidia GPU Operator, vLLM, Triton) and Azure services (e.g. Azure ML, Azure OpenAI).
  • Knowledge of MLOps and Agentic AI in particular experiment-tracking (e.g. MLflow), orchestration of ML pipelines (e.g. Dagster, Azure ML) and conception and operation of AI-agents.
  • Good knowledge of databases, object storage, data lifecycle, backup & recovery, monitoring as well as security, vulnerability and identity management (e.g. Azure AD).
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • English (very good knowledge)
