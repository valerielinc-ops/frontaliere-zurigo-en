08.10.2026 Valais

Become part of our team!

The Raiffeisenbank Region Glatt is a successful, locally oriented and innovative bank with offices in Wallisellen, Bassersdorf and Effretikon. To strengthen our financial planning team, we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement a dedicated and service-oriented personality as a Senior Financial Planner 80%-100% (m/f/d).

What awaits you?

  • You advise customers on challenging issues relating to retirement, pension provision, property and inheritance law and asset structuring.
  • You develop individual and needs-based solutions taking into account the personal goals and life situations of our customers.
  • You actively shape the future of our financial planning business and contribute your ideas for further development.
  • You support our customer advisors in preventive care issues and use your expertise specifically.
  • You organize and lead customer events, specialist presentations and internal training courses.

What do you bring?

  • You have several years of experience in financial and pension planning.
  • You have the further education as a financial planner with eidg. Professional certificate completed and ideally has further qualifications, for example as CFP®, MAS Financial Consulting or comparable further education.
  • You work independently, structured and goal-oriented and convince through your strong customer and team orientation.
  • You have in-depth knowledge in the areas of pensions, taxes, property and inheritance law as well as in banking and insurance products.
  • You have experience with Allfina/Logismata or comparable financial planning software.

The best financial planning arises where expertise meets personal advice. If you enjoy holistically accompanying customers in the most important financial decisions of their lives and actively shaping the future of our financial planning business, I look forward to meeting you.
