Global Insurance Network Manager — Swiss Re

Full description

About the Role As Global Insurance Network Manager, you will help shape and sustain Swiss Re Corporate Solutions’ partner ecosystem supporting International Programs across markets where Corporate Solutions does not maintain a local insurance presence or license. The role combines strategic partner development, disciplined governance and service oversight to ensure clients receive consistent, compliant and high-quality execution through a strong network of local insurance carrier partners. This position is a key enabler of our international business model and of our ambition to deliver a distinctive client experience globally.

The role has the following key responsibilities

  • Shape, develop and maintain a strong global network of partner insurers that can support International Programs in markets where Corporate Solutions does not operate through an owned entity or local license.
  • Own and deepen senior relationships with network partners, fostering long-term collaboration, mutual accountability and a clear focus on client outcomes, especially when acting as escalation point for the global IP colleague community.
  • Identify, assess and onboard new partners where required, including due diligence, commercial evaluation, contractual implementation and readiness for operational delivery.
  • Steer service performance across the partner network through clear standards, performance monitoring, issue resolution and continuous improvement measures.
  • Anticipate strategic, operational and market developments affecting network partners, and define mitigation or contingency actions to protect continuity, resilience and service quality.
  • Maintain a strong view of local market requirements, including insurance, regulatory, and tax considerations, and ensure relevant insights are reflected in internal guidance, decision-making and network data.
  • Ensure partner interactions and network processes are executed in line with Swiss Re standards, governance expectations, compliance requirements and applicable local regulations.
  • Partner closely with underwriting, operations, legal, compliance, finance, claims as well as third parties (e.g. brokers, as appropriate) and other relevant functions to resolve complex issues and support effective delivery of International Programs. Support sales efforts and advertise Swiss Re Corporate Solutions' network capabilities to potential brokers and clients.
  • Contribute to the evolution of the International Programs value proposition by supporting new partner capabilities, product roll-outs and targeted capability-building initiatives for both partners and internal stakeholders.

About the Team You will join the team responsible for global network and partner management within International Program Operations at Corporate Solutions. Our team plays a central role in enabling Corporate Solutions’ International Programs business. We define network strategy, shape partner propositions and help ensure consistent top-level service delivery to multinational clients around the world. Our success is built on close collaboration across functions, strong partner connectivity, a client-centric mindset and the disciplined use of data, systems and expertise to deliver at scale. Our Network Management function has a business enabler role. We drive our network strategy and our partner propositions centrally and are ultimately responsible for top-level service delivery to international program clients across the globe. Our coordinated customer centric approach, our state-of-the-art systems and tech platforms and our strong team of dedicated specialists located around the globe are key assets for CorSo's continued success in International Program business.

About You

You are an experienced insurance professional with strong commercial judgement, a relationship- and service-oriented mindset, and the ability to navigate complexity across markets, partners and internal stakeholders. You combine strategic thinking with operational discipline and bring a strong sense of ownership, accountability and delivery focus to your work.

  • Strong experience in the primary insurance industry, ideally with exposure to International Programs and functions such as underwriting, distribution, operations, claims, account management or network management.
  • Proven ability to manage external partners, carriers, brokers or clients across multiple markets and to build credible, trusted relationships with senior stakeholders.
  • Solid understanding of the operational, legal, compliance and regulatory dimensions relevant to international insurance business.
  • Good insurance product knowledge, including familiarity with policy wordings across different product lines, and a solid understanding of core reinsurance principles relevant to international insurance business.
  • Demonstrated capability in performance management, including the use of service standards, metrics, governance forums and improvement actions to drive outcomes.
  • Experience in partner onboarding, due diligence, contractual implementation, including reinsurance agreements, and contract negotiation is required; experience in broader business development activities would be an advantage.
  • Strong stakeholder management, communication and influencing skills, including the executive presence to engage credibly with senior management at partner organizations and the ability to communicate clearly and effectively with operational teams.
  • A collaborative, pragmatic and client-focused approach, combined with sound judgement, resilience and a solution-oriented mindset.
  • Strong organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple priorities with attention to detail, quality and timely execution.
  • Fluency in English is required; additional languages are an asset.
  • Willingness to travel internationally as required.
