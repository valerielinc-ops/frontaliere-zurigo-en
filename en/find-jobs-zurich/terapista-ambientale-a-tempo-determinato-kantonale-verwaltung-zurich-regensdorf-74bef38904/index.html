Full description

Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Prison and Reintegration Psychiatric Psychological Service Milieutherapist, temporary 80 - 100% With around 1,400 employees, JuWe is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as for carrying out various types of detention. Our employees from around 40 professions support and promote the reintegration of delinquents and work effectively and progressively for a safe and just society. The Psychiatric Psychological Service (PPD) is one of the eight main departments of JuWe. As one of the largest institutions for forensic psychotherapy and psychiatry in Switzerland, he specializes in the recidivism-preventive treatment of violent and sexual offenders in detention and freedom. The treatment offer ranges from court-ordered measures to therapies of psychiatric primary care. To strengthen our dedicated treatment team in the field of Inpatient Adult Forensics (StEF), we are looking for a motivated milieutherapeutic specialist for the Forensic Psychiatric Department (FPA). The term of office shall be one year. Your tasks

  • Reference work with treatment planning and support of a prosocial and treatment-promoting environment
  • Development and implementation of individual case-related milieutherapeutic treatment measures taking into account the psychological and physical resources of the intervention clients
  • Participation in the securing and further development of reliable executive-organizational and safety-relevant processes at the FPA (e.g. securing hygiene, food, laundry change, sickness reports and postal services)
  • Planning, implementation and evaluation of accompanied therapeutic exits and holidays with your profile
  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nursing professional HF, preferably with a focus on psychiatry
  • Ideally, prison experience, in particular in the areas of care, employment and employment
  • Maturity, resilient and conflictable personality with strong relationship competence
  • Strong team and communication skills as well as enjoyment of trusting and constructive cooperation
  • You are open to an interdisciplinary way of working and ready to learn continuously
  • An impeccable reputation (no entries in the penal and enforcement extract) Our offer
  • Careful and practical training that enables a secure and structured entry into the new area of responsibility
  • Attractive and diverse internal and external training opportunities for professional and personal development
  • Close interdisciplinary exchange in the team, accompanied by regular supervisions and intervisions
  • Clear and reliable working hours in day shift service with good predictability
  • Attractive and fair employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines Your application Take the next step in your professional career with us and send us your application via our online tool.
