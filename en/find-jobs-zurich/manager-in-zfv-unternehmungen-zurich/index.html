Full description

Our modern production kitchen at Kantonsschule Rämibühl in Zürich prepares lunch and dinner fresh every day for the Kantonales Sozialamt. In keeping with the ZFV philosophy, fresh meals are prepared on site using regional, ecologically responsible and fairly traded food. From our kitchen, we supply all locations in the canton with hot meals every day

Are you our new team member who will support us as an operations manager from 14 December 2026 or by agreement? You work 5 days per week between Monday and Sunday, from 07:00 to 16:30. The weekend shifts are covered by the team on a rotating basis. In addition, from August 2027 to February 2028, the role includes catering for Schule LG in the Rämibühl cafeteria.

Your task menu

  • Management and organization of the operation in accordance with ZFV guidelines
  • Ensuring and maintaining employee and client satisfaction
  • Responsibility for visible, attentive and service-oriented collaboration with the various asylum sites
  • Support and advice for orders placed by the individual asylum site managers
  • First point of contact for clients and managers of the asylum sites
  • Ensuring cooking and delivery processes, including working on the front line (e.g. on weekends or covering holiday absences)
  • Planning and further development of an inspiring and varied offering, activities and sales promotion measures
  • Conducting sales and negotiation discussions with the supervisor
  • Planning, organization and implementation of catering services, special events and promotional sales campaigns
  • Preparing the annual budget in cooperation with the supervisor and ensuring compliance with it
  • Handling various administrative tasks in the areas of procurement, accounting, e.g. correct invoicing, People and Culture, etc.
  • Responsible for the daily closing, month-end closing and inventories
  • Ensuring and organizing the school's lunch catering at LG from August 2027 to February 2028
  • Recruitment of new employees in cooperation with the People and Culture department
  • Reviewing operating costs and key figures, including initiating appropriate measures

Your recipe for success

  • Basic training EFZ in gastronomy / hospitality
  • Hotel management school or basic gastronomy seminar G1 / G2 / G3
  • 10 -15 years of professional experience in gastronomy / hospitality and a good understanding of operational processes
  • 5 years of management experience and a passion for developing and motivating employees
  • A strong service and hospitality orientation
  • Organizational talent and an independent, solution-oriented way of working
  • Entrepreneurial and business-minded thinking
  • A confident presence as well as communication and negotiation skills
  • Enjoyment of sales, offer development and looking after clients
  • A flair for numbers and confidence in handling budgets, key figures and administrative tasks
  • Sound IT skills in MS Office and industry-specific systems
  • Flexibility and willingness to also work operationally when required
  • Very good spoken and written German

What we offer you

We create teams that support each other and in which you are encouraged. We make an impact together and focus on everything that does good. Among other things, we place great value on pay transparency and pay equality. For this position, with a 100% workload, depending on your experience and qualifications, we offer an annual salary of around CHF 90'000 (including a 13th month's salary). If you would like to share your passion for gastronomy with us, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
