# Data Governance Counsel

Il tuo futuro inizia

In a dynamic environment, you design the protection of data and secrecy. Legal developments regarding data, secrecy and artificial intelligence (AI) are constantly monitored and implemented pragmatically within the Group. Together with line, departments and management, you control risks, specifications and communication around data use.

  • Track and evaluate new laws on data, secrecy and AI
  • Supports the definition and operational implementation of specifications throughout the Group
  • Control risks and check Group-wide compliance and effectiveness of specifications and training
  • Advises on strategic business, technology decisions and new business models
  • Leads the technical development of tools and processes and shapes communication for data use

Your skills

  • Degree (uni) in Law, Admission to Law of Advantage
  • Several years of experience in a large company or in a law firm
  • Very good knowledge of data and privacy law as well as AI
  • Familiarity with the telecommunications and IT industry
  • You recognize tendencies and connections, work analytically, structured and find pragmatic solutions
  • You communicate clearly, promote exchange and build self-advice skills
  • You keep calm in challenging phases and stay reliable
  • Very good German and English
  • Other national languages are beneficial

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Benefits.
