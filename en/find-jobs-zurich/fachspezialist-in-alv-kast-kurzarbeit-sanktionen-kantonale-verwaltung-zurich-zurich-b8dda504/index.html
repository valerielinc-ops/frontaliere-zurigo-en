Canton of Zurich Economic Directorate Office for Labour Specialist/in ALV / KAST Short-time work & sanctions 80 - 100% You want a task that makes sense, offers responsibility and challenges you professionally? Then you fit us perfectly! To strengthen our team, we are looking for you – a committed personality who likes to take responsibility and enjoy complex cases. The Office of Labour (AFA) strengthens Zurich as a business and work location – with around 800 employees at over 20 locations. In the Department of Unemployment Insurance (ALV / KAST), we ensure that the Federal Act on Compulsory Unemployment Insurance and Insolvency Compensation is implemented uniformly and fairly in the Canton of Zurich. Your tasks:

  • Independent processing of pre-declarations of short-time work as well as requests for waiver of recovery of short-time work allowances
  • Independent handling of bad weather reports
  • independent processing of multiple insurance cases (sanctions & orders regarding entitlement) based on the corresponding legal basis
  • Writing of speechable orders
  • Obtaining the relevant documents and conducting surveys of insured persons and employers
  • Contact with insured persons, employers, unemployment funds, regional employment centres (RAV) and other internal and external bodies
  • Care of the KAE hotline Your profile:
  • completed 3-year commercial basic training or equivalent training
  • at least three years of professional experience in a qualified, materially oriented (social) insurance environment
  • Completed further training in the field of social security
  • Very experienced user knowledge in the MS-Office
  • stilsicher (verbal as in writing) in the German language
  • Adaptive treatment of various stakeholders
  • logical-analytical thinking skills and an efficient way of working We offer:
  • meaningful and interesting activity with a high level of personal responsibility
  • Attractive employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines
  • modern workplace near Zurich Oerlikon station
  • Possibility to HomeOffice
  • well-developed social benefits under cantonal personnel law
  • Contribution to catering costs (lunch check) Do you value a living environment and fair framework conditions? Then we should definitely get to know each other – Mr. Patrick Studer, Head of Team D, telephone 043 259 49 35, will be happy to provide you with further information. We look forward to your application. You can find out more about us and our employees at www.zh.ch/afa .

Pensum: 80-100 %
