For our occupational therapy at the locations Klinik Schlosstal and our day clinic for adolescents we are looking for you occupational therapist / occupational therapist FH / HF 40 - 50% Winterthur Apply now Focus: adult and youth area | Option on pool assignments These are your tasks

  • Treatment of mentally impaired people of different age groups
  • Independent, evidence-based ergotherapeutic treatments in individual and group setting as well as responsibility for the entire therapy process
  • Professional documentation of therapies
  • Active participation in intra- and interdisciplinary cooperation
  • For pool assignments: Planning and agreement of the group contents with the management of ergo- and activation therapy. Independent implementation of ergotherapeutic group offers using your expertise
  • Degree in occupational therapy FH/HF (for foreign diplomas with SRK recognition)
  • Self-employed and flexible working methods
  • Interest in working with people in mental crisis situations
  • Psychiatry experience is beneficial
  • Team-capable, resilient and reflective personality You can expect that
