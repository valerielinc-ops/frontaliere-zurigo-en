Key responsibilities Are you at your best when advising clients and establishing lasting relationships with them? Do you know how to ensure the smooth operation? We are looking for a new colleague or colleague who will take over the following main tasks:

  • Advise and support our clients with professionalism on all issues related to private credit
  • Develop solutions tailored to customer needs and wishes
  • Identify sales opportunities and systematically exploit their potential
  • Use your commercial talent to promote our high-end product line
  • Carefully review credit applications in collaboration with relevant internal services
  • Conduct outbound prospecting campaigns (outbound)
  • Perform various administrative tasks, such as entering and verifying client data.

The desired participation rate is 80 to 100%

The team At BANK-now, you will evolve into a Swiss SME culture, integrated into the UBS group. We are proud of our agile and friendly environment, where almost everyone knows each other. Here, each collaborator contributes directly to our success. We value flat hierarchies and short decision-making circuits. You will join a team of motivated and certified experts.

Your skills and experience

  • Basic commercial training or equivalent training.
  • At least 2 years of professional experience in the banking sector or in a similar financial function (e.g. back office, credit, leasing or financial department).
  • You appreciate the customer advice and are motivated to contribute actively to the development of the agency's portfolio.
  • You have excellent analytical and organizational skills, which allow you to process files accurately and methodically.
  • You are comfortable with a large workload and work reliably and independently, even during periods of high activity.
  • Excellent knowledge of French or German, both oral and written, ideally supplemented by knowledge in Italian or Portuguese.
