Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Center for Education in Health Care Vocational teacher Care HF 60 - 80% The ZAG, Centre for Training in Health Care, offers education and training at various educational levels (primary vocational training, higher vocational training and vocational training). With around 3,500 learners and students and 240 employees, it is one of the largest training centers in Switzerland for professions in the health care sector. We are looking for the beginning of the spring semester as of 1. March 2027 new colleagues for the program Care Higher School of Applied Sciences (Care HF) Their tasks
