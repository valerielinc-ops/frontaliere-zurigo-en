Full description

In this role, you will become part of a team at the heart of our people & culture data landscape. Together we build and operate the systems that make employee data correct, available and usable for the entire company. And because our crew is constantly growing, our platform has to grow with it.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Shop Zurich, Pfingstweidstrasse 60b, 8005 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Full responsibility from design to operation in an agile team: We build it, we ship it, we run it
  • Build and maintain data pipelines that extract, validate and distribute employee data to all dependent systems
  • Further development and maintenance of our data mart on Google BigQuery, which is used company-wide for compliance checks, equal pay analysis, shift planning or workforce insights
  • Development and expansion of our in-house recruiting tools
  • Scaling our systems to keep up with the company’s rapid growth
  • Collaboration with stakeholders from People & Culture, Finance and other development teams so that every detail is right at the end

Requirements

  • First practical experience: In software development
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): Computer science
  • Practical experience with one or more of the following technologies: C#, SQL Server, React, Kubernetes, Dataform or Google BigQuery – no one expects you to be able to do everything, you learn the rest with us
  • A strong sense of responsibility: Quality and impact of your work are important to you
  • Curiosity about the entire tech stack and the desire to constantly learn new technologies
  • The courage to address things early and to seek a conversation across teams instead of programming in a quiet room
  • The ability to adapt to continuous changes in the working environment
  • The willingness to question “as it has always been done” and propose more efficient, automated or effective solutions
  • in German (negotiation confident)
  • English (flowing)
