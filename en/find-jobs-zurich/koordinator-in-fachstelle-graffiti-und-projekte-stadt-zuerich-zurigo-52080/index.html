Full description

For the area Central Services, we are looking for development and projects as of 1. January 2027 or by arrangement a dedicated personality.

Tasks

  • They are responsible for the operation and further development of the municipal graffiti department, assess incoming reports and accompany demanding cases.
  • They advise urban authorities on cleaning, surface protection, prevention and design solutions.
  • They control the contracted external specialist companies and monitor quality, costs, deadlines and reaction times; in the case of recurring damages, you develop suitable measures together with internal and external partners.
  • They coordinate and accompany projects, development projects and organizational issues in the area and develop concepts, decision bases and implementation proposals.
  • They structure new tasks and topics with the participating specialist departments, analyze existing processes, develop practical improvements and prepare or moderate meetings and workshops.

Profile

  • Completion of higher vocational training (HF, Federal Professional Card or similar), preferably in a relevant area such as facility management, real estate, communication, public administration or project management
  • Several years of experience in project coordination in a comparable function
  • Good analytical and conceptual skills as well as strong organisational skills
  • High communication and mediation skills as well as security in stakeholder and network management

About us

On behalf of the City Council, we represent and manage around 1950 properties. In the areas of space and infrastructure, we offer numerous services to the city administration and advise the departments and the city council on strategic issues.
