Original description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School Limmattal Employees/in School Administration 80 - 100% The cantonal school Limmattal is a school of maturity of the Knonauer Amt and Limmattal regions and is located in Urdorf. It comprises a two-year secondary school (7th/8th school year) and the subsequent short high school (9th-12th school year). All maturity profiles and a bilingual matur are offered. Our school is extended by a branch in Affoltern am Albis. That is why we are looking for an employee for the school administration on 1 January 2027 or by agreement. The regular school operation begins in the summer of 2027 at the Cantonal School in Urdorf, where you help set up the school administration. After opening the branch, you will work at the secretariat of the Cantonal School in Affoltern am Albis.

  • Tasks The establishment of the school is in a continuous development process and the task area changes according to project progress. As part of the development work, you are responsible for the following activities:

· Creation and maintenance of project communication, e.g. newsletter and / or website · Taking over subprojects, e.g. documentation of administrative processes for the development · Planning and organization of events within the framework of the project · Support of the project team in various areas After the start of the school operation: · Correspondence and preparation of business for the school management · Accounts payable and debtors / cash management · Processing of the school administration software · Assistance in the organization of school events · Collaboration in the general school administration incl. Telephone and counter service Profile · Completed commercial training, ideally with further education and experience in project management · Qualified professional experience (preferably in education) · Stylistic German, foreign languages advantageous · Very good knowledge of MS Office applications · Sociable and resilient personality, safe appearance even in hectic times · Independent working methods with high quality awareness Offer It awaits you an interesting and varied task in the dynamic environment of a middle school. With your dedicated commitment, you make an important contribution to the establishment of a new branch and contribute to the culture of our school. The training takes place in an existing team. The employment conditions are based on the personnel regulations of the Canton of Zurich Application If you have any questions, the responsible project manager Alexander Nava is at your disposal (alexandre.nava@edu.zh.ch). We look forward to your dossier. You address your application with the usual documents no later than 30. September 2026 electronically to bewerbungen.ksl@edu.zh.ch. Information about the school can be found on our homepage www.kslzh.ch.

Pensum: 80-100 %
