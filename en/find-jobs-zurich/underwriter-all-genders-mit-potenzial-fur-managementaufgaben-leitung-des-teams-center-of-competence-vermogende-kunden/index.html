80-100%, Zurich / Smart Working

Shape your future – together with us!

Do you want to actively shape the future and take responsibility? With us, you can expect a dynamic environment that promotes teamwork, personal responsibility and flexibility. We offer you the opportunity to develop professionally and personally – with a view to deputizing and taking over team management.

High-net-worth private customers are a key target customer segment of AXA. Collaborating with these demanding customers is challenging, at the same time extremely educational and fulfilling both professionally and personally – ideal for sharpening your profile as an underwriter and future leader.

In the Property & Casualty area, we create freedom for our private and corporate customers with innovative products, convincing services and simple digital processes. We offer a wide range of standardised and individual insurance, legal protection and financial security in the area of credit and deposit.

This is what awaits you

  • Underwriting & own customer portfolio: examination and assessment of inquiries for new business as well as support of own portfolio in the area of buildings, household goods, art, collections and valuables for wealthy private customers
  • Policy Creation, Risk Assessment & Portfolio Quality: Creation of policies including tariffing and risk assessment, processing of inquiries from agencies and brokers as well as implementation of restructuring and risk controls to ensure portfolio quality
  • Responsibility for the strategic development of the overall portfolio: Responsibility for the strategic management and further development of the overall portfolio, including the preparation of analyses and reporting, participation in pricing and technical underwriting (development of guidelines for underwriters). Active participation in product development and further development, initiation and implementation of innovation and process optimization projects as well as regular review and update of the go-to-market strategy
  • Hub in the network: Collaboration and provision of information to general agencies, agencies, brokers and claims departments as well as sales support for distribution, including through visits. Regular exchange with the specialty team of AXA XL
  • Step-by-step towards leadership: support in leading the team and step-by-step management tasks with the aim of taking over team leadership

You bring that with you

  • Training: Completed commercial training and further education in the insurance environment (e.g. insurance professional) or comparable, ideally with initial management experience or recognizable leadership potential
  • Practice: Several years of experience in the insurance sector, preferably in the private customer or asset segment
  • Entrepreneurial spirit: High entrepreneurial drive, pronounced hands-on mentality and willingness to take responsibility beyond your own area
  • Organization: Strong organizational and prioritization talent – also on many parallel topics
  • Communication: Ability to inspire and convince people at all hierarchical levels
  • Mindset: Analytical and conceptual strength and willingness to truly permeate questions
  • Languages: Stylistic German and English in word and writing, French is advantageous

This is what we offer you We are convinced: Satisfied employees provide the best services. That’s why we invest in benefits that offer noticeable benefits – beyond the usual.

Flexibility and freedom: Smart Working (flexible working model) Annual working hours, 5 weeks holidays (from 50 years 6 weeks), holiday gift 2 weeks every 5 years, holiday purchase up to 10 additional days/per year

Job and family: family-friendly meeting hours, 22 weeks of maternity leave, 8 weeks of leave for the other parent, crèche subsidies, company family allowances, days off for entering relatives and in challenging, personal situations

Precautions and insurance: Attractive pension fund with far-reaching non-compulsory benefits including choice savings options, up to 30% discount on insurance products

Flexible Benefits: AXA Shareplan (employee participation), CHF 500 annually on the advantage platform Swibeco, discounted lunch or lunch card, Smart Working flat rate CHF 200 per year

Mobility: refund for half-tax, Reka-Pay up to CHF 1’000 per year (20% discount), fleet discount, free e-charging station, AXA rental bicycles

Healthy balance in everyday work: Free check-ups, annual health weeks, a wide range of exercise offers (online/offline), massage offers, rest rooms, employee care, online health portal, various seminars/events around health prevention & mental health

Development: 3 days of individual learning time, free coaching, wide range of learning & development opportunities, financial participation in further education, promotion of internal mobility, talent development offers, seminars for employees 55+ (AXA Horizon), engagement in communities on various topics, participation in innovation projects (Innoday, AXA Hackathon, UnConference), 2 days of volunteering (environment & social affairs)
