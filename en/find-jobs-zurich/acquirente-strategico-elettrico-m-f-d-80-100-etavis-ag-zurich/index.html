We are consistently driving forward the professionalization and digitalization of our purchasing. To strengthen our small purchasing team, we are looking for a dedicated personality who not only administers but actively shapes purchasing. That's what you do.

  • You develop product group and supplier strategies and actively implement them

  • You develop sustainable supplier relationships, supervise framework contracts and develop additional potential together with our partners
  • You identify optimization potential with future-proof technologies and actively implement them
  • You are a sparring partner: in our operating units and accompany the most demanding procurement projects from tendering to contract conclusion
  • You will work with us to shape the purchasing of tomorrow and actively develop our digital procurement processes, eProcurement solutions and data-based purchasing management.
