Apply for a job overview Oberarzttin / Oberarzt Alterpsychiatrie 80-100% Spital Affoltern AG - Affoltern am Albis employees by agreement Would you like to contribute your expertise in the age psychiatry in a committed and interdisciplinary team?

For our specialized department of old-age psychiatry, we are looking for a senior physician (80–100%) by appointment.

In this responsible position, you have the opportunity to actively shape the psychiatric care of older people, to further develop innovative treatment concepts and to lead a multi-professional team.

Their tasks

  • Senior medical director of an elderly psychiatric bed ward with a focus on dementia/delir diseases and related neuropsychiatric disorders
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation with the Centre for Age Medicine
  • Training and supervision of assistant doctors and psychologists
  • Engagement in further education internally and externally
  • Participation in consultancy and liaison services within the hospital
  • Collaboration and networking with referees and institutions Your profile
  • Completed or advanced training as a specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy
  • Emphasis on age psychiatry and psychotherapy beneficial
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing (from level C1)
  • Independent, dedicated, flexible and team-oriented personality with humor
  • Enjoyment of interprofessional and interdisciplinary cooperation as well as the co-creation and further development of the treatment and therapy offer
  • Engaged cooperation in the psychiatric-psychotherapeutic team with openness to different therapeutic directions
  • Evidence-based way of working We offer
  • Working in a well-coordinated, supportive, open and creative team
  • Space for self-responsible, self-initiative work, with options for co-creation
  • Training opportunities in the form of supervision, intervision and hospital-internal and external training
  • We have the FMH training category B in psychiatry and psychotherapy (ambulatory and inpatient).
  • Attractive employment conditions with good insurance services Our culture – Your benefits «Bi öis can mer no»: With us it is family and personal.

As part of the Affoltern hospital team, you will also benefit from attractive employment conditions and numerous Fringe Benefits: Jokertag, shopping benefits and two subscriptions for free admissions to the FORTYSEVEN Wellness Baths are just a few examples of our Fringe Benefits.

The Hospital Affoltern is the largest employer and training company in the Knonau Office.

Around 550 employees work for the well-being of patients every day.
