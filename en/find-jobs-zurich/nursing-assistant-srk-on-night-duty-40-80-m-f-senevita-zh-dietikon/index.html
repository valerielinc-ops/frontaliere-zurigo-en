Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,300 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland.

In the Senevita Limmatfeld in Dietikon ZH, we are looking for an empathetic and motivated personality to complement our care team immediately or by agreement. Nursing assistant / night service SRK 40-80% (m/f) Their tasks

  • Your expertise and experiences are implemented competently and respectfully in primary care
  • You perceive the individual needs of the residents and meet them in the best possible and appreciative way.
  • They support the self-determination and independence of the residents
  • They comply with the hygiene guidelines and also take on domestic activities
  • You carry out the care documentation seamlessly and independently

This is what they bring with them.

  • Completed course as nursing assistant/in SRK
  • Several years of professional experience are beneficial
  • A natural mindfulness for our residents and their needs, even in demanding situations
  • Experience in night service mandatory
  • Your self-evident awareness of being part of the team, where mutual support, respect and cooperation are given priority
  • Your warmth, combined with a pinch of humor, rounded off with a sense of responsibility and open communication skills
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German

We offer them

  • An environment in which the person is at the center and you can give joy and make a difference
  • Extensive training offer of the Senevita Academy, which can be visited free of charge
  • Various perks (food in the in-house catering, tanking, supplementary insurance, fitness, pharmacy, etc.)
  • Good social benefits and at least. 5 weeks holiday
