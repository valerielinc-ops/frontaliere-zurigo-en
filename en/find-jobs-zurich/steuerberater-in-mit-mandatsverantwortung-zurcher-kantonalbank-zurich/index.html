Full description

80% - 100% | Wealth Planning | Zurich | Experienced professionals As a leading bank in the Zurich economic area, we accompany our customers in all important life events related to family protection, retirement planning, inheritance regulation and tax planning. We are convinced that personal support in these topics makes an important difference in the customer relationship. In your role as a tax consultant, you take over the holistic advice on tax issues of natural persons and work closely with our pension and inheritance specialists as well as our colleagues from sales. You can expect an exciting area of responsibility and a versatile environment in which you can take on responsibility right from the start and continue to develop. Your tasks

  • Responsible for the professional preparation of tax declarations of natural persons incl. Review activities as Head of Mandate:in (main job)
  • Advising customers on income and property tax, inheritance and gift tax and property profit tax
  • Representing the interests of our customers vis-à-vis the tax authorities
  • Participation in projects for the further development of our services
  • Supporting colleagues in sales through needs-oriented and comprehensive consulting in establishing and expanding customer relationships

Your profile

  • Completed or ongoing subject-related training (e.g. trustee:in with Federal Professional ID, MAS in Taxation, Bachelor or Master in a relevant field)
  • Several years of professional experience in tax consulting or at a tax authority
  • Very good knowledge in the area of income and property tax as well as ideally in the area of property profit tax
  • You score with empathy, high service orientation and strong communication skills in dealing with customers
  • You convince with flexibility, teamwork and the ability to keep an overview even in demanding situations
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing, every other language is a plus point

Our offer

  • Motivated and established team that looks forward to you
  • Exciting and diverse range of tasks with network possibilities
  • Your career is important to us as an employer and therefore we support and accompany you on your way
  • Central and modern workplace at headquarters with consulting assignments at our branch locations
  • Comprehensive package with variable remuneration as well as versatile benefits and perks – from A such as retirement provision to Z such as ZVV bonus pass
