Canton of Zurich Judicial execution and reintegration Social workers 80

  • 100% JuWe is responsible for the execution of the various types of detention as part of the Directorate of Justice and the Interior of the Canton of Zurich. For our 1,400 employees from around 40 professions, the successful social reintegration of people who have become criminal and the prevention of relapses is of particular importance. The Zurich (VEZ) Department of Enforcement Institutions – one of eight departments of the JuWe – comprises four companies with different types of detention and provides care for about 450 people with over 180 employees. The prison of Affoltern at the Albis with 65 execution places and about 30 employees is specialized in the execution of penalties or residual sentences of no more than 18 months. In addition to the concept of group execution, which aims at increasing the responsibility of the imprisoned persons, a level-based school education (BiSt) is offered. Tasks
