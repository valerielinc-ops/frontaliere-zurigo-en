Main responsibilities

  • Define and execute the roadmap for next-generation Retail AI Ads reporting (including AI search trends and insights, alongside AI-Readiness diagnostics) to give advertisers clarity, trust, and control during the AI Search transition.
  • Collaborate with core SAGE teams (retrieval, bidding, and auction) to eliminate offer delivery gaps and power Shopper-Aware Personalization, leveraging retail signals to match high-intent shoppers with the right merchant catalog.
  • Engage directly with Retail advertisers and Sales/Go-to-Market teams to identify and translate customer pain points into new product innovations.
  • Establish measurement frameworks and rapid experiment pipelines with Engineering and Data Science.
  • Forge partnerships and align joint roadmaps across SAGE, Ad Platform (AP), Search/AIM, Commerce, and GBO/Sales, authoring strategy papers and leading executive reviews.

Key requirements

  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience.
  • 5 years of experience in product management or related technical role.
  • Experience integrating generative AI tools or LLM interfaces into workflows.

Additional details

  • Experience defining success metrics, investigating quantitative data, and evaluating experiments to guide product choices.
  • Proactive, accountable, and outcome-oriented mindset with a track record of building trusted partnerships and aligning joint roadmaps across matrixed teams.
  • Advertisers worldwide use Google Ads to promote their products; publishers use AdSense to serve relevant ads on their website; and business around the world use our products (like Google Shopping, and Google Wallet) to support their online businesses and bring users into their offline stores.
