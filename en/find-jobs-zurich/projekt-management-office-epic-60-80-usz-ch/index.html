Full description

As part of the zKIS/EPIC program, you are actively involved in the introduction of a new clinic information system at the University Hospital Zurich. The project is one of the central digitalization initiatives of the USZ and helps to standardize clinical processes, improve cooperation and sustainably support the everyday work of employees.
A dynamic environment with high impact awaits you, in which you actively participate, take on responsibility and together with an interdisciplinary team realize a future-oriented solution for the USZ.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • You ensure the smooth administrative process in the project and thus support the entire project team • You independently organize the project back office and coordinate the operational project support • You plan and coordinate appointments, room bookings as well as travel and events • You support the organization of training courses as well as other project-related planning and coordination tasks • You create protocols, documentation and documents to support project management • If necessary, you can help set up and coordinate additional back office resources Requirements • You work very structured, reliable and keep an overview even in complex organizational situations • You enjoy organizational and coordinative tasks in a dynamic project environment • You are service-oriented and support teams proactively in everyday project life • You are able to coordinate several tasks at the same time and set priorities correctly. You use tools such as Excel, SharePoint, Teams, Outlook etc. sovereignly. • You communicate clearly and enjoy working with different stakeholders • You will ideally bring experience in administrative, organizational or project support roles • You are flexible, resilient and quickly integrate into new tools and processes We offer: You will be given the opportunity to develop professionally and personally in a complex transformation project and take on responsibility with direct effect on the digital change in everyday hospital life. You can expect a dedicated team working together on a future-oriented solution for the USZ.
