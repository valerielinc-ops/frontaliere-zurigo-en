Full description

For our customer – a dynamic and highly digitalized trust office on the outskirts of the city of Zurich – we are looking for an initiative personality to strengthen the team. Paperless work is standard there, but you are not afraid to pick up a customer who still stores his receipts in the basement? Do you love the variety between national SMEs and international customers? Then you fit perfectly into the team.

This is your daily business

  • Pure diversity: You cover a broad and exciting customer segment from all industries – from innovative restaurateurs to online platforms and service providers to a successful fashionista.
  • International focus: You advise national and international clients and explain Hailey Smith from Birmingham the balance in fluent English.
  • Living digitalization: As experts in the field of digitalization, the team is constantly evolving – and you are actively involved in shaping this path.
  • Self-employment: You lead your mandates in a highly self-responsible, flexible and solution-oriented manner.

This is what you bring with you

  • Well-founded basis: Several years of professional experience in the trust sector is absolutely necessary for this role.
  • Sprachflair: You speak excellent German and your English is so good that you can easily explain complex financial topics.
  • Mentality: You are flexible, ready for constant development and also give full throttle in intensive times with the team.

This is what awaits you

  • Attractive package: A fair annual salary between CHF 80,000 – 120,000 as well as Reka checks / Reka money.
  • Absolute home office freedom: Whether at home, in the mountains or in Ticino on the lake – work where you are most productive.
  • Flexible working hours: Whether you start at 7:00 or 9:00, you decide for yourself. The main thing is that the customers are satisfied. (And yes: On Monday morning, the team also takes a calmer approach).
  • Office Benefits: Free coffee, drinks and private parking right at the office.
  • Top location: The office is located in a charming house on the outskirts of Zurich – including a lounge and grill for joint exchange.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
