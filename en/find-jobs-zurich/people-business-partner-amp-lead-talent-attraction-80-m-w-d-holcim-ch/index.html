Full description

If you want to design the people work holistically, to act close to the teams and to set innovative impulses, you are at the right place!

What to expect

  • Strategic Business Partnering: You advise and accompany executives and employees at our headquarters in Zurich-Oerlikon in all people-related matters and act as a trusting, strategic link to the business.

• Lead Talent Attraction: You are responsible for the strategic orientation of talent acquisition for German-speaking Switzerland and manage the end-to-end recruiting process in close cooperation with our competence center. • Data-driven impulses: You monitor people and recruitment KPIs (e.g. Time to Hire, Diversity, Quality of Hire), derive targeted measures and actively strengthen our employer brand • Team leadership & leadership: You take over the professional and personnel leadership as well as the targeted further development of an employee in the area of talent attraction (employer branding, cooperation with universities) • Close sparring at eye level: You report directly to the Head Talent Central Europe West (CEW), work closely with the Business Partner Team Switzerland and actively promote cross-cutting people projects.

What you bring

  • In-depth training: You have successfully completed a master’s degree in psychology, human resources management, business administration or a comparable discipline.

• Talent background & willingness to change: You bring several years of experience in talent management, strategic talent acquisition or L&D and are eager to successfully contribute this expertise to business partnering or vice versa. • Leadership & consulting competence: You have proven experience in the management of teams and projects and communicate with strong and confident advice at all hierarchical levels up to the management. • Digital & analytical mindset: You use proven and new technologies (AI, Successfactors, SAP, etc.) and have strong analytical skills in dealing with people key figures for data-based decision-making. • Growth Mindset & Mobility: A pronounced “People Value Creation” attitude, empathy, flexibility and networked thinking shape your working style. In addition, you are fluent in German and English and bring regional mobility.

Our offer

  • Appreciation & Team Culture: With us, you are part of a strong and inclusive team that holds together, shares ideas and masters challenges together. A trusting leadership culture ensures that your commitment is seen and appreciated.
  • Flexibility & Work-Life-Balance: We offer flexible working hours, 41 hours, part-time opportunities, 25-30 days holidays, holiday purchase, extended maternity and paternity leave
  • Attractive compensation: We offer our employees an attractive compensation package with many additional benefits and a very good pension fund
  • Employee benefits: From Reka checks to shares at a preferential price to the Swibeco advantage world - we make sure that you feel comfortable and benefit from attractive additional services
