Tierspital Zürich

Job opening 01.08.2026 or by agreement, temporary

In order to meet the growing demands, we are looking for a committed and active person as a veterinarian to strengthen our team at the Clinic for Horse Surgery, as of 01.08.2026 or by agreement. You are interested in working in a motivated and productive team. They enjoy learning new things and appreciate the professional care of patients.

Tasks

Your daily but not everyday tasks include

  • the care of inpatient and outpatient patients, including late, weekend, night and piketting services
  • the preparation and follow-up as well as assistance in smaller and larger diagnostic and therapeutic interventions
  • the maintenance of infrastructure such as stables, facilities and work equipment;

Profile

In addition to the main qualifications, competence in dealing with horses and above-average commitment, your team skills are in the foreground. Additional qualifications that enrich our team (e.g. training as a horsesmith, horsekeeper or similar) are beneficial. For your activity in horse care, the driver's license Cat. B is a prerequisite. We offer them A varied activity in an unusual environment. Training opportunities for internal and external events. A salary according to the cantonal guidelines.
