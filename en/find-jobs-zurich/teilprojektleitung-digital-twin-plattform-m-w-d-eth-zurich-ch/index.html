Full description

Project management Digital Twin Platform (m/f/d) 70%-100%, Zurich, temporary Printed Printing ETH Zurich is developing the future-oriented, digital basis for the planning, construction and operation of its infrastructure and building portfolio in the Digital Twin programme. For this purpose, a Digital Twin platform is created as a central system, data and integration architecture that connects specialist systems, information models and operational data over the entire building life cycle. For this key role, we are looking for a personality as part of the project management Digital Twin Platform. The mission is for the time being limited to 30. April 2029. Project background The Digital Twin programme, which runs until the end of 2028, aims to network and make usable information from planning, construction and operation over the entire life cycle of buildings and infrastructure. The Digital Twin Platform forms the technical basis for the integration of specialist systems, information models and operational data. It deliberately does not pursue a classical data lake or data warehouse approach, but relies on an open and scalable information, integration and metadata architecture based on standardized interfaces, common information structures and as open technologies as possible. Job description You will lead the development and development of the digital twin platform at ETH Zurich and, together with an interdisciplinary core team, develop a future-oriented basis for data- and model-driven infrastructure management. You are responsible for the further development of the platform as a central system, data and integration architecture. You ensure that it remains scalable, expandable and stable in the long term and create the technical conditions for its sustainable operation. Another focus of your work is on the implementation of digital twin use cases. You will integrate specialist systems, information models and meta and operational data and develop solutions that intelligently connect existing and future system landscapes. Together with the departments and the Advanced Data Analytics department, you will create the basis for data-based analyses, visualizations and new digital services. You work confidently at the interface of departments, IT services and external technology partners. You define technical requirements for procurement and implementation projects, accompany their introduction and manage external implementation partners professionally. In addition, you actively shape data and information management. Together in the DT team, we continue to develop data, metadata and governance structures and ensure that information is available in a comprehensible, usable and quality-assured manner in the long term. Profile

  • Sound practice in the design, development and realization of scalable system, data and integration architectures across the entire life cycle
  • Ideally coupled with experience in the field of planning, construction, operation or sustainable development of buildings and infrastructures.
  • Sovereign action at the interface of specialist processes, information models, data and integration architectures as well as modern platform and technology landscapes
  • Deep understanding of how networked systems, data and models create resilient, scalable information and decision-making bases for analytics, automation and future AI applications
  • Willingness to take responsibility, make informed decisions and develop complex projects together with internal and external partners
