Full description

As a leading Swiss online supermarket, we stand for innovation, teamwork and customer proximity. In our new and state-of-the-art logistics centre in Regensdorf, you will ensure a smooth process in the goods receipt, check incoming goods and ensure that quality and processes are adhered to at all times – shape the logistics of tomorrow with us!

Mansioni

  • Incoming goods processing: acceptance, control and professional accounting of receipts of goods in the system.
  • Process assurance: Ensuring a smooth operational incoming goods process.
  • Goods preparation: repackaging and preparation of goods into eligible packages.
  • Material provision & transport: provision and transfer of the goods for storage or further processing.
  • Compliance with standards: monitoring and compliance with safety, quality and hygiene guidelines.

Requirements

  • 2+ years: Logistics
  • Completed Vocational Apprenticeship (EFZ): Logistics
  • Physically active: You like to work in motion and actively engage in everyday work.
  • Flexibility & operational readiness: Readiness for fixed working hours in shift operation (without shift change) and in different temperature zones.
  • Digital competence: enjoyment of working with computer-aided systems and modern workplaces
  • Team orientation: Openness and willingness to support your colleagues in adjacent areas if necessary.
  • Training in logistics: You have completed an apprenticeship as a logistician and ideally already have initial experience in goods receipt.
  • German (good knowledge)
  • English (basic knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks paid maternity leave and 4 weeks paid paternity leave
  • Cumulus Points: Benefit from additional Cumulus Points
  • Sports and fitness offers: We value your health and give you CHF 250 per year when you complete a sports subscription
  • Individual training: We value your development, participate in your growth and offer a budget for it
  • Du-culture: We stand for a respectful coexistence at eye level
  • Personal responsibility & freedom: We value direct communication and involve our employees in decision-making
  • #oneteam: You become part of a team that tackles, is there for each other, lives diversity and pursues common goals with enthusiasm.
