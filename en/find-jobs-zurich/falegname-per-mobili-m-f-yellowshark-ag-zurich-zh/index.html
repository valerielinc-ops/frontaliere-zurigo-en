01 Future employer

Working with wood is your world! We are looking for motivated furniture carpenters for our customers in the Canton of Zurich (ZH) for temporary employment.

02 Your task

  • As a cabinetmaker, you work in a modern workshop and operate the entire range of machines.
  • The production of high-quality kitchens and furniture is part of your daily tasks.
  • You are familiar with veneering partition walls or cabinet fronts and then preparing them for surface treatment.
  • You enjoy working according to plans.
  • Our customers place great value on precise work, a friendly demeanor and respectful interaction with people.

04 Your profile

  • Professional experience as a carpenter in Switzerland or EU countries
  • Knowledge of occupational safety
  • Enthusiasm for and interest in the profession
  • Team player
  • Technical aptitude and knowledge of machinery
  • Precise and efficient work style
  • Good knowledge of German

Only electronic applications can be considered. Applications sent by post will not be returned.
