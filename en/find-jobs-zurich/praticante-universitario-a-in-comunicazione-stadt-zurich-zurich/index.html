Full description

As of January 1, 2027, we are looking for a committed personality for a period of one year to support the communication of the social services of the city of Zurich.

Tasks

  • You prepare topics and content for internal and external communication – target group-oriented, precise and creative.
  • You support in the conception and implementation of events as well as in the design of our offer communication with texts, pictures, videos and content on social media.
  • You take over the management of your own sub-projects – from the idea to the conception to the successful implementation – and actively participate in other projects.
  • You support media work, media monitoring and communication on political projects.

Profile

  • Completed (specialist) university degree in journalism, corporate communication or a comparable degree programme
  • Excellent knowledge of German in words and writing
  • First practical experience in the field of communication is advantageous
  • Enthusiasm for storytelling and the ability to effectively convey content to different audiences
  • High initiative, solution-oriented thinking and a strong service orientation in dealing with internal and external stakeholders

We offer

You work in a small team and are at the same time embedded in a large organization in which we maintain open and transparent communication. You can actively participate in the implementation of new solutions and take on personal responsibility within a team that is looking forward to you and your ideas.

About us

With around 2,400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a social Zurich. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.

Every day, the social services in the five social centres and specialist centres are committed to ensuring that people in the city of Zurich can count on effective basic social care.
