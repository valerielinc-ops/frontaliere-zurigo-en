Full description

80% - 100% | Bank management | Zurich | Experienced professionals Complexity is part of our business. Your goal is to turn it into simple, digital solutions. In the project management & business engineering team of the risk, legal & compliance and overall management sub-portfolio, you will design central control, risk and compliance processes at Zürcher Kantonalbank, combining regulatory requirements, business processes and technology. ServiceNow is one of our strategic platforms, whose use we will continue to expand in the coming years, including in third-party risk management, process management, data protection and data quality management. For this, we are not looking for an administrative role, but a personality that structures complexity, critically questions the existing and develops viable solutions from different needs. Your tasks

  • Assume the role of Product Manager and Business Engineer according to SAFe for selected ServiceNow products
  • Analyzing complex business processes and requirements, critically questioning existing processes as well as deriving technical targets and implementable solution concepts in cooperation with business and IT architecture
  • Structuring divergent stakeholder interests and leading them to viable decisions
  • Designing the Target Operating Model for ServiceNow and further developing roles, responsibilities, governance and collaboration models
  • Assessing new requirements and use cases in terms of business value, feasibility, standardization and strategic fit as well as ensuring a sustainable further development of the platform instead of a collection of individual solutions

Your profile

  • Completed university or university of applied sciences degree, for example in business informatics, banking & finance or business administration, or a comparable education
  • Several years of experience as SAFe Product Manager, Business Engineer, Solution Consultant or in a comparable role at the interface between business and IT
  • Very good knowledge of ServiceNow and its platform logic as well as the ability to classify technical requirements technologically
  • You do not have to develop yourself, but you should understand what is technically behind it
  • Sovereign action at the interface between business, IT and architecture as well as the ability to connect different perspectives
  • You have your strengths in strategic, analytical and networked thinking and are a valuable sparring partner for your team and your stakeholders
  • Very good knowledge of German and English

Our offer

  • Mutual support and team spirit are important to us
  • We offer you space for your ideas and thus design the bank of tomorrow
  • Your career is important to us as an employer and therefore we support and accompany you on your way and support you with many offers
  • Modern workplace in district 5 with very good transport connections (hard bridge station)
  • Comprehensive package, which in addition to the attractive basic salary and a variable remuneration offers many benefits and perks – from A such as retirement provision to Z such as ZVV bonus pass
