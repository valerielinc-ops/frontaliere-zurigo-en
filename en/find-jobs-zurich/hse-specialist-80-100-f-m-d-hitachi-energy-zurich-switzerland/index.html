The opportunity

Are you passionate about protecting people, safeguarding the environment, and driving a strong health and safety culture in a manufacturing environment?

As our HSE Manager for the Generator Circuit Breaker (GCB) factory in Switzerland, you will take full ownership of the Health, Safety, and Environment function and play a key role in shaping a safe, compliant, and sustainable workplace across our operations.
