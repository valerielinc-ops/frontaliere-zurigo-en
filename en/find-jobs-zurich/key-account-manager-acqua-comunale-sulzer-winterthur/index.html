Original description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

We’re looking for a high-impact Key Account Manager to take ownership of one of our most significant long-term utility partnerships – a major regional water provider serving millions of customers across the North West of England.

This is a commercially critical role where you will own the account strategy end-to-end—driving growth across capital projects (pump packages and related equipment) and aftermarket services, while shaping the future of a well-established, high-value relationship. Your main responsibilities:

  • Own the P&L and growth strategy for a utility account, with clear accountability for revenue and margin performance
  • Drive the narrative with the customer—positioning us as a trusted, strategic partner across both capital investment and lifecycle services
  • Lead and coordinate a multi-disciplinary account team, ensuring alignment across sales, service, engineering and delivery
  • Build and deepen senior stakeholder relationships, influencing decision-making at operational and strategic levels
  • Develop and execute a long-term account development plan, aligned to the customer’s AMP cycles and investment priorities
  • Provide accurate forecasting, pipeline management and reporting
  • Act as the voice of the customer internally, ensuring continuous improvement and delivery excellence

What We’re Looking For

  • Whilst this is a remote role, we have a preference for candidates based in the North West of England. Regular customer visits and face-to-face engagement are essential to building relationships, providing effective support, and maintaining a strong presence across the region.
  • Proven experience in a Key Account Management or Strategic Account role within engineering, industrial or utilities environments
  • Strong commercial acumen with experience owning revenue targets and driving growth
  • Background in pumps, rotating equipment, or fluid handling systems is highly desirable. Alternatively, direct experience working closely with a UK -based water utility provider is a strong advantage
  • Demonstrated ability to build and manage high-value, long-term customer relationships
  • Experience leading or influencing cross-functional teams without direct line management
  • Confident communicator, capable of engaging at both technical and executive levels
  • Strategic mindset with the ability to translate customer needs into tangible commercial outcomes
