Do you want to contribute to sustainably protecting the sensitive data of the Zürcher Kantonalbank and thus strengthen the trust of our customers? In our Data Leakage Prevention (DLP) team, you play a central role at the interface between technology, processes and people. You work closely with different areas and help identify and prevent potential data outflows early on. If you like to work analytically and take on responsibility, you are a good fit for us.

Your tasks

  • Supporting the DLP process in day-to-day operations
  • Analyzing DLP notifications and assessing whether instructions have been violated
  • Coordinating security incidents with employees, supervisors and staff
  • Further develop the DLP rules and implement new use cases
  • Creating reports on DLP incidents for internal stakeholders
  • Optimize and automate the DLP system to increase efficiency and quality
  • Completed studies (HF / FH / Uni / ETH) in computer science, business informatics or an equivalent education
  • At least 2 years of experience in information security in a company of comparable size and complexity, ideally in the banking or financial environment
  • Experience in the operation of safety-critical applications, ideally in the field of data leakage prevention
  • Experience in reporting and analyzing data
  • Careful and responsible handling of sensitive information
  • Very good German and English skills
  • Motivated, self-organized and established team that looks forward to you
  • Exciting and diverse range of tasks with many network possibilities
  • Freedom for decision-making and participation in the field of information security
  • Various opportunities to develop personally and professionally
  • Central and modern workplace with the possibility to organize your work flexibly (hybrid)
  • Culture of appreciation and a du-culture with open, constructive cooperation
  • Comprehensive package with attractive basic salary, variable compensation and versatile benefits
