Broad family medicine with its own laboratory and X-ray, a hospital center with numerous disciplines in the same city and Zurich reachable in just under 40 minutes: In the region of Schaffhausen, you take care of your patients: inside responsible and actively shape the offer of a modern medical center - Germany right on your doorstep. The medical center is centrally located in the cantonal capital, with public transport directly on the doorstep. The rooms are modern and bright. A team of two specialists: General Internal Medicine and four MPAs covers the entire spectrum of family medicine. Laboratory and X-ray are in the house, so many findings are still available during the consultation. The team attaches importance to doctors actively shaping the offer. For allocations, the paths are short. The Cantonal Hospital in the same city is a training clinic of the highest category. Zurich can be reached by train in about 38 minutes, the airport in about 40 minutes. The canton of Schaffhausen borders Baden-Württemberg on more than 150 kilometres, and there are direct trains to Stuttgart.

Their tasks

  • You look after your family doctor patients: self-responsible, across the entire spectrum of basic care
  • They use the laboratory and X-ray in-house for rapid diagnostics and assign them specifically to specialists in the region.
  • They further develop the medical offer of the center
  • They represent themselves in the medical team
  • You will expand your expertise with internal and external training.

Your Competences

  • Specialist title General Internal Medicine / Practical physician or an equivalent title, e.g. specialist for general medicine or internal medicine from Germany
  • Design will: You not only want to hold office hours, but also want to further develop processes and offers
  • Communication strength and strong team orientation
  • German language skills at level C1
  • For a foreign diploma you will receive advice and financial support from us for recognition via MEBEKO

Their advantages

  • Visible influence: In a team of around twelve people, your ideas flow directly into the further development of the center
  • Efficient diagnostics: laboratory and X-ray in the house, hospital center and private clinic in the same city
  • training as an investment: budget and paid training days; occupation-specific courses partly during working hours
  • Provision above the law: The employer bears significantly more than the legal minimum share in the pension fund, this strengthens your later pension
  • Location with range: Zurich and airport in about 40 minutes, Germany in sight, direct trains to Stuttgart
