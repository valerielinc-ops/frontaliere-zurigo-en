Full description

Quality at all levels is the key to true well-being. As an employee or member of the SWICA team, you understand holistic health as a human service. You work with professionals like you who share this claim. Everyone pulls on the same rope; that's a good feeling. Together, we create solutions that really bring progress. This is how you shape health

  • Versatile and exciting telephone as well as personal advice for our customers and sales of needs-based insurance solutions
  • Pronounced sales strength and pleasure in the active distribution of insurance solutions with a clear goal and conclusion orientation
  • Independent receipt and processing of customer inquiries with the aim of achieving the best customer satisfaction
  • Working in a dynamic and committed team with clear sales targets
  • Active market development on behalf of the supervisor
  • Creation of offers and customer correspondence in private customer business
  • Implementation of measures for preservation and customer recovery

This sets you apart

  • Completed commercial or industry-related (health industry) training as well as in-depth sales experience, with advantage in the insurance industry
  • VBV training available or willing to complete it as soon as possible
  • Local anchorage and place of residence beneficial
  • Communicative, reliable and resilient personality
  • High initiative, a success-oriented attitude and a high degree of empathy
  • Enjoying customer contact and active sales
  • Strong consulting competence, which is characterized by a high customer and service orientation
  • Appropriate oral and written expression in German, knowledge of Italian is an advantage. Further foreign language skills are welcome
  • Solid MS-Office knowledge

Why SWICA

  • We build on humanity. That is why we focus on openness, respect and trust in our everyday work. We tackle this, take responsibility and think in terms of solutions – together, for our customers. This is also why we have been number 1 in customer satisfaction for many years.
  • We trust you and give you space. With annual working hours, at least 5 weeks holidays and on request also holiday purchase. With part-time models and generous home office within the scope of the possibilities of the job, you make your everyday life largely flexible.
  • We value and reward your work – with a market-based wage, up to 1,500 francs annual fee for personal wishes and a generous participation in the basic and additional insurance for you and your family.
  • We offer you prospects. With a generous training regulations, we support your professional and personal development – for your role today and for your goals of tomorrow.
  • Because health is everything. With our company health management and region-specific offers, we take care of your well-being. And if it comes otherwise: 100% net wage continued payment.
  • We think long term. With three savings plans, pensum-dependent coordination deduction and benefits well above the legal minimum, you ensure good for your future.
