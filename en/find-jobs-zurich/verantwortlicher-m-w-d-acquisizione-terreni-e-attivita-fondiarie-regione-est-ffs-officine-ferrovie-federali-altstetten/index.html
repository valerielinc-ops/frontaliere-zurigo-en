Responsible (m/f/d) Land Acquisition and Land Activities East Region 60-100% | FFS

Move Switzerland with us. Manager (m/f/d) Land Acquisition and Land Activities East Region Altstetten / Work Smart, immediately or on date to be agreed, 60-100% (Possibility of job sharing) Apply here

What we offer you.

The Fund Management (IM-GM) sector has total responsibility for SBB funds and its 100% affiliates. Contribute to the construction of new railway infrastructures, attractive stations and modern business objects, ensuring that the required land and rights are available in good time. A motivated and collaborative team awaits you, who works reliably in dynamic situations and appreciates team spirit and humor. What you can move.

  • You have the total responsibility for the land activities of the railway exercise in the East Region, in particular for the acquisition of land and rights.
  • Together with your team, ensure the proper management from the formal, temporal and financial point of view of land transactions for railway projects.
  • You are responsible for operational, strategic and personnel management as well as costs, deadlines and quality of the unit.
  • Conduct in a competent manner complex negotiations concerning real estate operations and claims for compensation, representing the unit interfunctionally in front of authorities, commissions and third parties.
  • Contribute to the development of the unit by participating in complex and strategic projects, ensuring long-term orientation of land activities.
  • Recognize potential in your team (about 7 people), promote professional development and support employees in achieving their goals.

What you bring with you.

  • Safe presence, strong communication skills and negotiating skills, with which you are able to find sustainable solutions even in challenging conversations.
  • Analytical and conceptual force, as well as strategic thinking skills, which you use to manage complex projects.
  • Experience in the management and promotion of the team; drive your team with empathy, commitment and sense of responsibility.
  • Appreciation for interdisciplinary collaboration, as well as for high reliability and openness.
  • You have a master's degree in law or business economy (SUP/University), a notary license or similar training with relative professional experience; experience in the notary sector, at the land register office or in the real estate sector is particularly appreciated.
  • Excellent knowledge of German level C1 and, ideally, French level B1.

Salary and benefits.

We are actively committed to equal pay. This assumption is governed by the bond code and is based on a comparison with the team, the market and the industry.
