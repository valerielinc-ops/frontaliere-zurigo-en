Full description

Want to take the next career step? Then we have the perfect place to develop you! Become part of ADZ, the leading engineering office for building technology in digitalization. Develop innovative projects with us in the field of heating, ventilation, climate with the latest work equipment.

Your tasks

  • Leading and developing exciting construction projects in the field of heating, ventilation and refrigeration systems
  • Independent implementation of planning services from concept development to planning and implementation
  • As the overall project manager, you are the competent contact person for customers and the planning team in technical and economic matters.
  • As a link to the various trades in construction, you feel safe in dealing with and communicating with the project participants

Your expertise

  • You have basic technical training and further training as a technician: in HF or engineer: in FH Building Technology (HLK)
  • In the project planning and realization of demanding heating and ventilation systems, you can have several years of professional experience
  • You are used to dealing with 3D-based CAD systems
  • Ideally, you have experience as a project manager / overall project manager and would like to further develop in the field of digitalization
  • Communication strength and organisational talent
  • Negotiating knowledge of German
