Full description

Are you fascinated by how claims processes in the field of motor vehicles can be further developed with data? Do you want to derive concrete measures from analyses that measurably improve the damage effort, efficiency and customer satisfaction?

In our Claims Performance and Analytics area in performance management CLAIMS, you are responsible for the performance control of our largest claim line. You work at the interface between claims processing, underwriting, actuarial and analytics and thus support the continuous development of claims processing and the strategic goals of Zurich Switzerland.

Do you have the ability to understandably classify complex data and derive clear, actionable recommendations from it? Do you appreciate the cooperation with different specialist areas and do you want to harm your findings except for area management? This is your next career opportunity.

Find out here why Zurich Switzerland is one of the “Top 3” employers in Switzerland. Become part of it and apply as Claims Performance Manager MF.

What you do

  • You are responsible for performance control in the motor vehicle sector: You develop and monitor KPIs, analyze deviations and derive concrete measures for damage expenditure, efficiency, leakage and customer satisfaction.
  • You analyze large data stocks independently – from data preparation to interpretation – with SQL as well as Python or PySpark and continuously develop data-based analysis approaches.
  • You accompany the annual planning and control process for your line and follow the implementation of the agreed operational and financial measures over the year.
  • You create meaningful reporting for internal stakeholders and group claims, represent the underlying figures and ensure the necessary governance.
  • You are a sparring partner: for the line managers and work closely with claims processing, underwriting, actuarial and analytics to effectively connect department, data and control requirements.

What you bring

  • You have a university degree in economics, statistics, data science, (economics) mathematics or a comparable subject area.
  • You bring several years of experience in non-life insurance, ideally in the property or performance environment, for example in the areas of motor vehicles, property or liability.
  • You work safely with SQL and Python, ideally with PySpark, and have experience with large datasets as well as reporting tools such as Power BI.
  • You can classify complex analyses in an understandable way, formulate clear and actionable recommendations and represent them convincingly to leaders.
  • You work independently, structured and solution-oriented, actively drive forward topics
  • You communicate stylishly in German and English.
