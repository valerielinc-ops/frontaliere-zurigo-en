Full description

Intensive care and anesthesia under one roof. You decide on the mixture. Our interdisciplinary intensive care unit is a category B training facility and provides approximately 700 patients each year with around 710 ventilation days and 530 hemofilter days. Intensive care patients of all specialties are treated, except polytraumata, severe burn injuries and patients undergoing acute transplants. Our interdisciplinary operations department performs approximately 4,500 operations annually in four modern operating rooms and one intervention room. The anesthesia takes care of patients during elective and emergency interventions from the entire surgical spectrum of the house. Both areas belong to the same house, the paths are short and the teams know each other. Therefore, you do not have to choose a specialist area with us:

  • Intensive care only, 50-100 %
  • Intensive care and anesthesia combined, total burden 50-100%, split by arrangement You want to work exclusively in anesthesia or intensive care unit? This is also possible. Become part of our team now as

Duties in the intensive care unit

  • Admission, examination and treatment of intensive care patients of all specialties, except polytraumata, severe burn injuries and patients undergoing acute transplants
  • Regulation, implementation and evaluation of advanced diagnostics and therapy
  • Selection, installation and interpretation of adequate and targeted monitoring
  • Application of current intensive medicine methods such as invasive and non-invasive ventilation, hemofiltration and sonography
  • Assessment of patients with regard to their relocation to the normal station or to suitable external facilities
  • Supervision of the assistant doctors in the intensive care unit
  • Participation in the service system of the intensive care unit in anesthesia, in a combined employment
  • Perioperative anesthesiological care for patients of all ASA classes and from all specialist areas
  • Anesthesiological care during elective and emergency interventions in the entire range of operations of the house
  • Participation in the anesthesia service system
  • Accompaniment and training of assistant doctors and active participation in the internal training programme
  • Exchange with the assigning specialist areas, with documentary doctors and with the assigning family and paediatric practices
  • Documentation and performance recording, including the benchmark data relevant for intensive care
  • You have the specialist title Intensive Medicine and/or the specialist title Anesthesiology or are about to graduate
  • For a foreign diploma, you meet the requirements for federal recognition by MEBEKO
