Full description

Various activities are waiting for you, including painting and wallpapering as well as floor coatings. The company attaches great importance to quality and a pleasant working atmosphere. For our client we are looking for one/n activities

  • All painting and wallpapering work
  • Smaller plaster work in the interior
  • Special floor coatings
  • Jointless wall and floor coverings (e.g. digital printing wallpaper, Naturofloor, AIDO)
  • Facade renovations in the exterior
  • Rift renovations
  • Protective treatments against algae and fungal attack on facades and woodworks
  • Decorative techniques in the modern and classical area
  • Requirements
  • Completed training as a painter or several years of professional experience
  • Self-employed and clean working methods
  • Quality awareness and pleasure in craftsmanship
  • Experience in decorative techniques beneficial
  • Driver's license Cat. B (advantageous)
  • Team-ready, reliable and customer-oriented offer
  • Varied and demanding work
  • Collaboration on special and high-quality projects
  • Family working environment
  • Contemporary employment conditions
  • Development opportunities in the field of special and decorative techniques
