Full description

Shape the future of our dough! Do you love working with quality ingredients and want to be part of a team that creates the basis for delicious pizzas and cakes every day? You are the heart of our production! Whether pizza dough, puff dough or cake dough – thanks to you, our products become a pleasure! Together we create doughs that inspire – in Switzerland and for Switzerland.

Mansioni

  • Operation and monitoring of production facilities for the production of various types of dough
  • Ensuring compliance with recipes and quality requirements
  • Preparation and weighing of raw materials according to the production plan
  • Implementation of quality controls during the production process
  • Resolving minor incidents and supporting the maintenance of the facilities
  • Maintenance and cleaning of production facilities in accordance with hygiene and safety regulations

Requirements

  • Professional experience: experience in food production, ideally as a dough maker or in a similar function
  • Completed apprenticeship (EFZ): baker, cook, food practitioner
  • Technical understanding and experience in operating production facilities
  • High quality and hygiene awareness
  • Physical resilience and readiness for shift work
  • Teamwork, reliability and independent working methods
  • German (good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Employee offers: Employee Points Cumulus, Discount Mobile Subscriptions, Higher Interest Rates Migros Bank, Club School/Fitness contributions and more.
  • Occupational pension provision: Comprehensively secured in the Migros pension fund – with first-class and attractive services.
  • Food: Delicious daily menus at reasonable prices.
  • Holidays: 5 weeks holiday for all, 6 weeks holiday from 50 years of age, 7 weeks holiday from 60 years of age, additional purchase of additional holiday days possible.
  • Education and training: We actively train employees as part of internal programs and, if necessary, support external training with time and money.
  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks paid maternity leave (instead of 14 weeks), 4 weeks paternity leave (instead of 2 weeks).
  • Social benefits: 730 days of sick leave with full net wages.
