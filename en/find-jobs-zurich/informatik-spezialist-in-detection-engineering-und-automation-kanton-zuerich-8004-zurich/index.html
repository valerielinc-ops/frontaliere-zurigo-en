Full description

The Cantonal Police of Zurich and its approximately 4,000 employees assume responsibility for security in the canton. Are you looking for an entry into IT security or are you interested in actively participating in the operation and further development of the Security Operation Center (SOC) and contributing your knowledge and experience? Then you become part of a young, dynamic and motivated IT team and support us actively as a computer science specialist /-in Detection Engineering and Automation 100% tasks

  • Triage and assessment of IT security incidents
  • Active participation in the operation and maintenance of the SOC-IT infrastructure
  • Processing and further development of the central LOG platform and the SIEM, Detection Engineering
  • Devops tasks within the SOC-IT infrastructure (Ansible, Gitlab)
  • Monitoring infrastructure regarding security incident
  • Analysis, detection and resolution of cyberattacks and technical vulnerabilities
  • Continuous development of processes and technical measures to improve cyber defense
  • Maintaining the high level of protection (Hardening/CIS Benchmark/NIST)
  • Project cooperation and advice on security issues Profile
  • Completed Bachelor's degree in Computer Science/Cybersecurity (FH/University/ETH or HF) or equivalent education
  • Several years of practical experience in the technical environment
  • Further education and experience in IT security (e.g. SIEM, SOAR or Pythin/Rest)
  • IT skills in SIEM/Log-Management, Detection as Code (Git/CI-CD), EDR, SOAR, Pythin/REST available
  • Stylistic German in word and writing, good knowledge of English
  • Independent, careful, disciplined and exact working methods
  • High load capacity in exceptional situations
  • Cat B driving licence present
  • Readiness for Pikett service
  • Correct reputation and offer
  • We offer you a versatile and meaningful activity in a dynamic environment
  • A centrally located, state-of-the-art workstation in the Zurich Police and Justice Centre including staff restaurant and access to the in-house fitness centre
  • Attractive employment conditions at a secure employer
  • The opportunity to develop an impact in an exciting new challenge and jointly shape the future Job entry: As of 1 December 2026 or by agreement Still questions?
