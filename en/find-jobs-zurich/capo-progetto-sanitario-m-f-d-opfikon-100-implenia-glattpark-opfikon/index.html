Full description

Do you not only want to accompany sanitation projects, but are responsible for them from the idea to the successful implementation? Then bring your know-how to Planovita in Opfikon as project manager sanitary (m/f/d).

Tasks

What tasks await you as Project Manager Sanitary (m/f/d)?

  • Project planning, devising and execution planning of sanitary facilities
  • Preparation of schemes and submissions and preparation of calculations
  • Project management and support during implementation
  • Coordination of the planning team in the respective project phases
  • Collaboration with building owners, GU/TU, architects and specialist planners as well as managing project meetings
  • Ensuring quality, costs and deadlines across all project phases

Requirements

What requirements do you bring with you as project manager Sanitary (m/f/d)?

  • Training as a technician (m/f/d) HF/TS or several years of professional experience in an adequate position
  • Experience in planning and realizing sanitary projects
  • Good knowledge of MS-Office, as well as CAD (Nova), ideally experience with BIM
  • Self-employed and structured working methods
  • Enjoy working with different project participants
  • Very good knowledge of German

Benefits

What benefits do we offer you as Project Manager Sanitary (m/f/d)?

  • An exciting and challenging activity in a dynamic environment A multicultural working environment with the chance to shape a future worth living in
  • State-of-the-art technologies and a contemporary workplace at a sustainable company
  • A company that promotes talent and the training of its employees
  • The security of a financially healthy, broad-based group with strong roots in Switzerland
  • Flexible working models and modern work equipment with home office

An exciting selection of benefits

  • Up to 40% home office
  • 25 days holidays and 9 paid bridge days (28 days for cadre employees, 30 days holiday for all employees from the age of 50)
  • an in-house pension fund with very good coverage and 3 contribution levels
  • Excellent travel insurance for you and those living in the same household
