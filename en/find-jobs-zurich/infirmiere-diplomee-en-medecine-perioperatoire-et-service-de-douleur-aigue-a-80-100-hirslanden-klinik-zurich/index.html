Place of work: Hirslanden Clinic Hirslanden | Zurich Occupancy as of: October 1, 2026 or by appointment Type of employment: Permanent Reference number: 71260 In Perioperative Medicine at the Institute for Anesthesiology and Intensive Care Medicine at the Hirslanden Clinic in Zurich, you will optimally prepare our patients for the upcoming operation and, if necessary, accompany them postoperatively as part of our acute pain service. To complement our team, we are looking for you as a qualified nurse HF 80-100% for the area of ​​perioperative medicine and acute pain services. YOUR TASKS in Perioperative Medicine

  • You prepare patients optimally for the upcoming operation or anesthesia and coordinate the necessary measures.
  • You organize and coordinate patient documents and clarifications in close collaboration with general practitioners and affiliated doctors.
  • You carry out relevant checks and treatments as part of patient blood management, including iron infusions as prescribed by a doctor.
  • You plan, organize and accompany the anesthesia consultation hours.
  • You take on administrative tasks and are the first point of contact for questions about anesthesia. in the acute pain service
  • you look after patients with acute and chronic pain in the perioperative environment.
  • You plan, conduct and evaluate pain visits independently and support the adjustment of pain therapy.
  • You work closely with anesthetists and attending physicians.
  • You advise and support nurses on pain therapy issues.
  • You conduct training and ensure high-quality pain treatment according to current standards. YOUR PROFILE
  • You are a qualified HF nurse and have at least five years of professional experience in the acute sector.
  • Experience in a monitoring or intensive care unit is an advantage.
  • Ideally, you have further training in pain management or are willing to complete it.
  • You impress with your strong communication skills, independence, assertiveness and ability to work in a team.
  • You have good IT skills as well as very good German and English skills. Our offer

  • Continuous professional training and further education opportunities
