We work in a fascinating field of medical care where every day is full of challenges and interesting experiences. The care of patients after major visceral surgery as well as with complex gastroenterological clinical pictures at university level brings with it a varied and dynamic working environment. From medical-technical interventions to individual care, we are continuously guided by the latest findings from research and practice. Our work is not only our vocation, but also an exciting journey in which we can expand the boundaries of medical possibilities every day and at the same time positively influence the care of our patients.

Your main tasks

  • Care and care of patients: inside with visceral and gastroenterological clinical pictures, focus on liver, bile duct and pancreatic surgery in all layers
  • Dealing with complex, rapidly changing situations
  • Close cooperation in the care team and in tandem with FaGe's
  • Coordination of patient care in the multi-professional team
  • Continuous development of your knowledge and transfer of new findings in the team

What sets you apart?

  • You are a certified nurse or nurse specialist HF / FH or will soon complete your training. You were able to make first experiences in the acute area
  • You are looking for medically complex challenges and appreciate the dynamics of rapidly changing situations
  • You are responsible and appreciate a work environment based on clear guidelines
  • Close cooperation in the team, mutual willingness to help and the exchange of knowledge are important to you

What can we offer you?

  • A dynamic, exciting and demanding working environment in which you can continuously develop your expertise at university level
  • Our team is not only helpful, but also humorous and mixed. We attach great importance to our team spirit and support each other
  • A positive culture of cooperation with mutual support and transfer of knowledge is central to us
  • We recognize and promote the potential of all employees and offer versatile development opportunities in different directions
  • In service planning, we take individual preferences into account and thus promote the compatibility of work and private life
