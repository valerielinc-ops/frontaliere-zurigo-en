Full description

You will be responsible for the safe and efficient operation of multiple operating properties and energy centers and will pass on your energy and expertise to our learners.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for the housework at several company properties and energy centers.
  • As a central contact person on site, you ensure safe and smooth operation.
  • You plan and carry out operation and maintenance work independently.
  • The coordination, monitoring and quality assurance of all external providers for FM services are also part of your tasks.
  • As a vocational trainer* in our six learning specialists, you patiently pass on your expertise and experience.

Profile

  • You have a degree as an expert / employee maintenance EFZ or a basic technical education.
  • You have already gained professional experience in the home maintenance of large company properties over several years.
  • Your approach is independent, reliable, accurate and you act responsibly and solution-oriented.
  • You have an advantage over the Vocational Training Card and already have experience in the training of learners.
  • A flair for technology, flexibility and service orientation distinguishes you as much as organizational strength and computer skills.
  • You have the driving license Cat. B and use company vehicles with care and sense of responsibility.

We offer

  • More energy for your life: With mobile work, flexible working models and part-time opportunities, you can actively shape your working time and divide it into the best fit for your life. For example, you can buy up to 2 weeks of extra holidays and work part-time at all levels. Job sharing/splitting and unpaid holidays are also possible with us.
  • Sustainable and cheaper travel: With the mobility contribution of up to 600 francs a year, you travel cheaply and sustainably – whether you want to cycle through Zurich by bike or explore Switzerland by train.
  • An investment in your future: We not only value paying fair and transparent wages, but also ensure that you have more of your wages left over. We take over 60% of the savings contributions and additionally offer a super-compulsory interest on the capital accumulated.
  • An energy boost for your free time: We pay you 100 francs a month on your lunch-check card. In addition, you benefit annually from further Fringe Benefits worth 150 francs and attractive mobile subscriptions.
  • Development and training: With our talent management, we promote your development in a targeted manner – through a wide range of internal and external training offers as well as exciting development opportunities.
  • Meaningful work: Sustainability and meaningful work are not just words for us: shape Zurich and the future for us. Together with us, release energy for a good morning.

About us

ewz has been dynamic and visionary on the road every day for more than 130 years. Whether sustainable energy production, smart energy solution or innovative networking technology: Develop with us today what is important tomorrow.
