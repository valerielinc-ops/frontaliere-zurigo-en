Full description

Keep our operational areas running and make sure that downtimes do not become a problem in the first place. With us, you take responsibility, drive improvements and bring your expertise where it really has an impact.

What will you do • Rapid and professional correction of faults and plant downtimes during shift operation • Monitoring plant elements and proactive action in accordance with service level agreements • Implementation and coordination of planned maintenance and maintenance work • Creation and optimization of maintenance plans in cooperation with maintenance planning • Ensuring quality standards and continuous improvement by identifying vulnerabilities • Ensuring the safety, availability and reliability of installations • Collaboration in commissionings, conversions, maintenance and cross-departmental projects • Support, training and professional guidance of employees

  • Occasional work at height and thus safe application of PSAgA

Working hours

  • Basic working hours in the shift model between 6 a.m. and 11 p.m. in 2 shifts
  • Readiness for occasional special missions (e.g. Saturday, Sunday and night)

Who you are

• Completed further training as a maintenance specialist (Federal Fa) or comparable qualification • Mind. 5 years of experience in the maintenance of production and logistics facilities • Experience in direct customer contact and strong service orientation

  • Basic PLC knowledge and in-depth knowledge in automation, electrical engineering, mechanics, pneumatics and hydraulics

• Very good German (minimum B2) and good English skills (minimum B1) • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method with high personal responsibility • Rapid comprehension, networked thinking and proactive and innovative action • Team-capable, communicative and flexible personality with high commitment and physical resilience
