Full description

About the role Have you always been interested in the world of investments and finance? Are you good with numbers and detail oriented? If you answered yes to either of these questions, then read on! Take a chance to join the Asset Management department of one of the world's largest reinsurance companies with over 150 years of history. What does the role entail?

  • Confirming and ensuring timely settlement of all investment related payments and security transactions globally
  • Working with internal stakeholders- such as trading desks, treasury, static data, reconciliations, compliance, IT etc. to mitigate operational, financial, and regulatory risk on daily basis
  • Liaising with global market participants (custodian banks, brokers, clearers, system vendors) on daily basis to resolve exceptions and fails
  • Collaborating in initiatives and projects, onboarding of new tasks
  • Challenging status quo and suggesting improvements to enhance processes and tasks
  • In other words, you will have the chance to experience interaction with many external market participants while delivering to various internal clients and build key skills for your future career.

About You

  • Basic knowledge of investments (the more the better, but we are willing to train you)
  • Ability to set priorities and meet deadlines
  • Excellent command of written and spoken English
  • Ability to learn, openness and flexibility for new tasks and challenges
  • Detail orientation and good analytical skills
  • Strong teamwork and "can do" attitude
  • University degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or another related degree is preferred but not necessary
  • Relevant work experience is a plus (suitable for fresh graduates as well)
