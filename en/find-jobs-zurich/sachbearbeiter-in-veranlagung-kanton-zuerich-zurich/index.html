Full description

Canton Zurich Security Directorate Office for Military and Civil Protection Staff 100% The Office for Military and Civil Protection together has around 140 employees at the sites in Zurich, Birmensdorf and Andelfingen, making an important contribution to the security of the population.The Department of Military Replacement Administration (WPEV) of the Canton Zurich is a partner of the federal tax administration as well as the cantonal tax administration and works closely with the military administration and the municipalities of the Canton Zurich.

WPEV is a competent body for the provision of compensation for all Swiss citizens in the canton of Zurich who are not serving in the military or civil service.

Tasks

  • Telephone and written information to the debtors (unfit for service, military service and civil service shifters)
  • Mutation, taxonomy, taxonomy and assessment of the substitutes
  • Registering of the substitutes in the technical application WPEV
  • Processing of the reports from the personnel information system of the army (PISA) and the civil service (ZiviConnect)
  • Responsibility for the various replacement law issues of the civil service (ZIVI)
  • Responsible for the internal logistics areas Postal service, telematics and material procurement requirements -

Completed commercial training (EFZ) and at least two years of professional experience after graduation
