Full description

For our team in Zurich, we are looking for an experienced personality who knows Payroll safely and is independently responsible for the operational HR.

Their tasks

  • You look after employees from entry to exit, create personal documents and process illness and accident cases
  • They are responsible for the monthly payroll of A–Z including withholding tax, social security and end-of-year work, as well as special payouts such as bonuses.
  • You advise employees and managers on HR and labour law issues
  • You take on recruitments and create job descriptions
  • You work together with authorities and insurance companies and prepare HR and payroll evaluations on social security

Your profile

  • HR certificate, further education in payroll/social insurance or comparable qualification
  • Several years of experience in operational HR and in independent contract processing
  • Sound knowledge of Swiss labour and social security law, knowledge of German law of advantage
  • German at native level as well as very good English and French skills
  • Structured, reliable and solution-oriented working method with a high number affinity
  • Knowledge of current payroll and HR programs required

We offer

  • Responsible task with room for manoeuvre and direct reporting line to the CFO
  • Administrative support by a case officer (30%) and the secretariat (20%)
  • Modern office at Paradeplatz, collegial team and flat hierarchies
  • 5 weeks holidays as well as free nights and discounts on furniture and home accessories

Interested? We look forward to your application!
