Full description

To strengthen our care team, we are looking for a specialist care as of 1 November 2026 or by arrangement. Their working days are Monday, Tuesday and Friday. By compensating for the extra hours, eight weeks of holidays are possible per year.

Tasks

  • Provision of leisure activities and offers during the free time, which are adapted to the individual possibilities of the children
  • Care of children during lunchtime and in the various leisure activities
  • Promoting children's strengths and independence
  • Parent contacts and parent talks
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation in the care and school team
  • Organizational and administrative tasks as well as participation in school development

Profile

  • You have several years of experience in pedagogical work with children in lower school and parental work
  • Solution-oriented attitude and humor in daily work
  • Self-initiative and resilience in a dynamic field of work
  • High level of teamwork and flexibility in thinking and acting
  • Ability to work constructively, interdisciplinary with other professionals in our school team

We offer

We offer you

A varied and meaningful activity in a very committed and open school team. We provide you with competent support for complex issues and also offer an interesting and well-paid employment according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich.

About us

The Zurich-Letzi school district comprises Stadtkreis 9 with the districts Albisrieden, Altstetten and Grünau. In the 16 schools of our rapidly growing school circle, more than 5,000 primary school students are taught and supervised. More than 1,200 employees work for them in our schools in the areas of teaching, care, home service and technology as well as administration. They all work daily for the interests of the students and a smooth school day.
