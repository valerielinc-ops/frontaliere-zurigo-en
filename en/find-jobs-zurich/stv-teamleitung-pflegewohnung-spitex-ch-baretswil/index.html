Full description

The nursing home Bäretswil is an operation of Spitex Bärentswil, an innovative, future-oriented company in the Zurich Oberland. In addition to care at home, Spitex Bärentswil offers a care apartment with care and care around the clock. 22 older people find a comfortable, family home here with individual support and security.

To support our care team, we are looking for you, immediately or by appointment. Stv. team management nursing apartment Your tasks

  • Support of team management in day-to-day care and organizational tasks
  • Deputy management of the team in absence
  • Active participation in team leadership, empowerment & support of employees
  • Resource and goal-oriented care of our residents
  • Planning, coordination & controlling of the nursing classification

You bring that with you

  • Nurse HF or equivalent training recognised by the SRC
  • Willingness to take responsibility
  • RAI-NH/interRAI LTCF training or willingness to complete it
  • Warm, communicative, responsible
  • Practical-organizational skill

This is what awaits you

  • A supportive team that holds together
  • Varied responsibility
  • Space for co-creation and active input
  • Team activities
  • Discounted food
  • Free parking

Does that sound like the right job for you? Then we look forward to your application to bewerbung@spitex-baeretswil.ch.
