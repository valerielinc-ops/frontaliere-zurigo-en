Full description

Are you a technical talent, skilled in craftsmanship and motivate yourself to perform varied tasks? Then an education in automation is just right for you! During the challenging four-year basic training, you will acquire the tools to successfully enter the world of automation.

Your education

  • In your training, you will learn a lot about complex systems, electronics and automation.
  • You are the specialist for the maintenance and functionality of the sorting and conveying systems and help to ensure trouble-free operation.
  • Project-oriented, productive and service-related tasks as well as problem analyses and their practical correction shape your area of responsibility.
  • During the two-year basic training, you will learn the basics in the creation, commissioning and maintenance of automated systems and discover further exciting topics at the partner company login Berufsbildung AG in Zurich-Altstetten.
  • During the last two years of apprenticeship you will use your knowledge in a sorting center and consolidate and deepen your knowledge from basic training.

You bring that with you

We are looking for young people with enthusiasm, joy and motivation who want to make a difference. You should fulfill the following points:

  • You have very good knowledge of mathematics and physics, you can think logically and you are strong in solving problems and tricky tasks
  • You have completed compulsory schooling.
  • You have technical understanding, are digitally interested and have high endurance and concentration.
  • You also enjoy working in a team and are reliable and responsible.
