Company and context

  • As a Data Scientist KI, within the KI organization, you will be responsible for the scientific data responsibility along the entire process chain: from evaluating potential use cases to developing Proofs of Concept to implementing successful solutions into operation.
  • Additionally, you will share your knowledge and advise administrative units on data science issues.
  • Do you want to harness the potential of AI for the Canton of Zurich?

  • You will analyze concrete challenges together with administrative units and assess whether and how AI can make a meaningful contribution.

  • You will prepare data, conduct exploratory analyses, and select appropriate models and methods.
  • You will systematically evaluate KI models in terms of quality, robustness, and suitability for the specific use case.
