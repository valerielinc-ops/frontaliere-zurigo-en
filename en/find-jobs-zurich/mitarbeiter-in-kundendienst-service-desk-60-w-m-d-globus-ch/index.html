Full description

With great attention to detail, our employees make GLOBUS the leading premium and luxury address in Switzerland. It is people like you who make the difference and bring the “place we love to be” to life together with us. For our customer service/service desk on the ground floor of the GLOBUS flagship store on Bahnhofstrasse in Zurich, we are looking for a positioned and sympathetic person at the earliest possible time, who can support the existing team in a workload of 60% temporarily until 31.01.2027. Here you make the difference

  • As a host, you ensure that our customers feel welcome and welcomed as guests.
  • You are the focal point for general information.
  • You accept customer complaints and handle returns.
  • You are responsible for handling any Globus Pluscard requests (locking lost cards, creating day passes, credit check, etc.).
  • You work closely with the Pluscard Service and Customer Service National.
  • You process customer orders, customer requests and process online orders (click & reserve).
  • You are responsible for the management of computer checkouts and the management of parking tickets.
  • You control the telephone exchange.
  • You support in the coordination of logistics and delivery of goods (ground floor).

That draws you out

  • You have successfully completed a vocational training (e.g. KV) and / or bring several years of experience in the retail trade / customer service or in the hotel industry.
  • You are a service-oriented personality with a high willingness to provide services.
  • You love and live the role as a host:in and have a pronounced customer orientation.
  • You have a good relationship and a good appearance.
  • You are very reliable and act on your own initiative.
  • You have excellent knowledge of German and fluent knowledge of English
  • Good knowledge of French is a plus, other languages are not a must, but of great advantage for our international guests.

BENEFITS FOR YOU Together with around 1,700 other lovers of detail, you are an important part of our fascinating premium and luxury world. You experience real appreciation, work in a great team and we trust you so that you can use space and make a difference with your skills. If you understand your craft and bring a large portion of passion, then many career doors are open to you. We are happy to support you with internal but also external training and further education.

Also, look forward to

  • an exceptional working environment with flat hierarchies in the heart of Zurich.
  • attractive social benefits.
  • Flexible working time models and at least 5 weeks holidays (PS: You get more free time thanks to the option "unpaid holiday" - from 7 to 180 days).
  • up to 50% discount on products from the globe world for you and your family.
  • Much more...
