Full description

The SQTS Swiss Quality Testing Services is part of the Migros Cooperative Association, which deals with the examination and assessment of foodstuffs and everyday articles. This also includes operational inspections, factory approvals and consultations in the food and consumer goods sector.

Mansioni

  • Contact person for questions about invoices and coordination with internal and external bodies involved
  • Reception of customers and technicians
  • Operation of the telephone exchange and cross-location support of various group mailboxes
  • Various administrative tasks (organization of events, meetings, orders for office supplies, etc.)
  • Maintenance of master data in our system / new customer collection
  • Acquiring and assigning purchase orders (PO’s)

Requirements

  • Several years of experience: in administration, of great advantage in a similar industry
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): in the commercial sector or equivalent training
  • Personality with flexibility and resilience combined with independent work and quick comprehension
  • Friendly and safe appearance as well as pleasure in contact with people
  • Enjoyment with PC systems and good knowledge of MS-Office applications
  • German (flowing)
  • English (good knowledge)
  • French (basic knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Modern infrastructure: Diverse work zones and modern co-working spaces with advanced equipment
  • Self-responsibility & freedom: Varied tasks with a high level of self-responsibility
  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation in courses of Migros Club School
  • Social benefits: Attractive employment conditions with very good social benefits
  • Mobility: cost sharing in public transport and mobility carsharing
  • Occupational pension fund guarantees the financial security of Migros employees in the third stage of life
