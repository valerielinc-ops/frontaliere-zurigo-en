Full description

The Public Prosecutor's Office III of the Canton of Zurich handles qualified economic offences and money laundering procedures and is also responsible for international legal assistance. We are looking for a motivated personality as an auditor / financial analyst for our dedicated team at the Zurich Police and Justice Centre as of 1 April 2027 (80-100%). What to expect from us

  • As an auditor / financial analyst at Public Prosecutor III, you act at the interface between finance and law and play a key role in the investigation of complex economic crimes.
  • You support the prosecutors in conducting criminal investigations and use your in-depth expertise in auditing and accounting to precisely assess and prepare business, financial, tax and accounting issues.
  • We are looking for an active, resilient and results-oriented personality who has a high affinity for legal issues and enjoys working in interdisciplinary teams and challenging issues. How to convince us
  • University degree (uni/FH) in business or finance or higher specialist training (e.g. tax expert, trust expert)
  • Mandatory qualification as Federal Dipl. Auditors
  • Multiannual practice in auditing, trusting or finance (national and international context)
  • Experience in expert work (business expert opinions / court experts) is a plus.
  • High veracity and editorial skill in written expression in German
  • Interest in the use of technical solutions and the methodological further development of sub-
  • Search and analysis concepts
  • Strong analytical and conceptual thinking, creativity, assertiveness, perseverance
  • High social skills and team skills
  • Experienced handling of large amounts of data as well as in-depth user knowledge in modern analysis and review solutions We offer you a complex and interesting area of responsibility in an interdisciplinary environment, contemporary employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines and a pleasant working atmosphere.
