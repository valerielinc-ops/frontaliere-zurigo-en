Full description

For the beginning of the lesson on 1. August 2027 we are looking for you as Learning professional Health / Learning professional Health EFZ As a learning specialist, you nurture and support our residents in their everyday life. You promote their independence and build a trusting relationship. You observe their health status and inform the medical staff if changes occur. With us you get a good education. And you see how everyone here contributes to the people in the Wagerenhof doing well. From day one, you help ensure that the inhabitants have a loving home. What matters is you. Every single person is at the center with their individual quality of life. We do everything we can to ensure that our 250 residents, 246 daycareers and 52 employees at protected workplaces in the Wagerenhof can experience the highest possible quality of life. Wagerenhof is an attractive employer for employees of different backgrounds and professionalism and a competent training partner for learners of all levels.

  • Assist people with disabilities in personal care (shower, bath, toilet), dressing and lining, shaving and hairdressing
  • Residents help with food
  • Promote the independence of the people cared for and build a relationship of trust
  • Supporting people with walking impairment (with a wheelchair or walker)
  • Measure temperature and blood pressure, take pulse
  • Take blood and urine samples and send them to the laboratory
  • Prepare medications and, as directed by the nursing staff, give injections
  • Changing bandages and treating wounds
  • Miscellaneous household tasks
  • Joy in dealing with people with and without impairment
  • Team and communication skills, good manners
  • empathy and helpfulness
  • Interest in medical topics
  • Careful and precise working method
  • Sense of cleanliness and order
  • Good physical condition and psychological stability
  • Flexibility: willingness to work irregularly
  • Higher secondary school A or B

This makes us the place for your education

  • Appreciative, human togetherness
  • qualified and committed vocational trainers
  • Diverse and meaningful tasks
  • Good accessibility with public transport

We look forward to your complete application documents: CV, letter of motivation, school certificates, multicheck and trial evaluations.
