Office Manager at the Chair of AI in Mechanics and Manufacturing (m/f/d)

30%-40%, Zurich, unlimited

The Chair of Artificial Intelligence in Mechanics and Manufacturing is part of the Institute of Virtual Production (IVP) and belongs to the Department of Mechanical and Process Engineering (D-MAVT) at ETH Zurich. It offers an international, dynamic and challenging working environment.

As an office manager, you will proactively support the professor and his team in administrative and organizational matters and take on a central coordinating function in the day-to-day work of the chair. You will work independently, keep track of a wide range of tasks and make a significant contribution to smooth processes in the team. You can expect a varied position with a lot of personal responsibility and creative freedom. The start of the position is on January 1, 2027 or by appointment.

Job Description

Human resources management

  • You will accompany entries and exits and coordinate the associated administrative processes
  • They advise the professor and the staff on a wide range of personnel management issues and coordinate personnel changes
  • In addition, you will supervise academic guests, interns and assistants in administrative matters
  • In personnel matters, you will work closely with internal and external partners

Financial management

  • You will manage the financial resources and budgets of the chair and keep track of ongoing expenses and projects
  • You will coordinate invoices and expense reports, supervise the financial administrative aspects of projects and prepare procurement applications
  • In addition, you will coordinate (international) orders and deliveries

Event management

  • You will plan, organize and coordinate conferences, workshops and other events of the chair and support their smooth implementation

Profile

  • You have completed commercial training or a comparable qualification and ideally already have experience in a university environment
  • You work carefully and in a structured manner, have a keen eye for detail and keep track of things even in complex situations
  • Thanks to your quick comprehension and flexibility, you can quickly adapt to different tasks and requirements
  • You enjoy working in a team and contribute to a positive and collegial working atmosphere with your strong social skills and communication skills. You will identify challenges and potential for optimisation at an early stage and play an active role in finding solutions
  • You are characterized by an independent, responsible and proactive way of working as well as a high degree of commitment
  • You have a good command of German and have a very good command of English. Since communication at the chair is mainly in English, you will also feel confident in an international environment; other language skills are an advantage
  • Experience in the field of occupational safety is an advantage

We offer

  • An activity with impact: Become part of ETH Zurich and an international academic environment that promotes your professional development and contributes to social progress through research and teaching
  • You will be firmly integrated into the team of the chair as well as the admin team of the department. Regular professional and personal exchange as well as open and supportive cooperation offer you a strong network in your everyday work
  • We offer flexible working hours that - as far as operational requirements allow - Creating space for different personal and family needs
  • ETH Zurich offers a wide range of courses and continuing education opportunities
  • Discounts such as public transport subscriptions and car sharing, a wide range of sports activities, the ASVZ, childcare, etc.
  • Attractive pension benefits through the PUBLICA pension fund
