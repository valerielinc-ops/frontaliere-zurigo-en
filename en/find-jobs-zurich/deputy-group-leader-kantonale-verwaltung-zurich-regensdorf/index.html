Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Correctional Services and Reintegration Pöschwies Correctional Facility Deputy Group Management 80 - 100% With around 1,400 employees, Corrections and Reintegration (JuWe) is the largest office within the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs and is responsible for the execution of sentences and measures as well as for the implementation of various types of detention. Our employees from around 40 professions support and promote the reintegration of people who have committed crimes and work effectively and progressively to ensure a safe and fair society. The Pöschwies Correctional Facility (JVA) is one of eight main departments of JuWe and the largest closed facility for men in Switzerland. With around 400 prison places and around 300 employees, it contributes to the protection of the general public in accordance with its legal mandate and at the same time creates the conditions for the rehabilitation of imprisoned people. For our entry group (EG) we are looking for a committed personality as deputy group leader immediately or by appointment. Your tasks

  • You look after, advise and accompany imprisoned people in their everyday lives and are an important contact person for their concerns
  • You will ensure a smooth daily routine as well as compliance with and enforcement of safety and service regulations. You will recognize critical situations early on and react confidently even in demanding situations
  • Administrative tasks such as writing reports and documenting events are also part of your everyday work
  • In the role as deputy.

As a group leader, you take on the areas of responsibility assigned to you and represent the group leader in his or her absence

  • If necessary, you can also contribute your profile to the company fire department
  • You have completed training as a correctional specialist and have several years of professional experience in the correctional system
  • We require you to be willing to continue your training in the area of leadership
  • You maintain an overview even in demanding situations and are characterized by mental and physical resilience
  • Changing working hours including on-call and weekend shifts can be combined with your other commitments
  • You have very good oral and written knowledge of German and ideally have knowledge of other languages
  • You are experienced in using the MS Office palette
  • Your reputation is impeccable (no entries in the criminal or debt collection register) Our offer

  • A variety of internal and external training and further education opportunities support you in your professional and personal development
  • Your workplace is in a well-connected location in Regensdorf
  • Attractive employment conditions in accordance with cantonal guidelines await you.
