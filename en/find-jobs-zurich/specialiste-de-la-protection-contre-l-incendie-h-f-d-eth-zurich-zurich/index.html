Full description

Fire protection specialist (m/f/d)

80%-100%, Zurich, permanent

The Department of Safety, Health and Environment (SGU) advises, supports and trains all organizational units and members of ETH Zurich on questions relating to safety and health at work. It is the point of contact for all centrally organized measures in dealing with hazards and risks for the protection of people, animals, property values, infrastructure and the environment as well as for questions regarding the fulfilment of the corresponding legal requirements. In the event of an incident, it shall maintain an appropriate emergency organisation. Both in everyday life and in the event of an incident, it works closely with the authorities and external forces.

In the field of fire and explosion protection (BEx), we are responsible for the ETH-wide organization and coordination of structural, technical and organizational measures: this includes, for example, the development and updating of internal concepts, structural and technical specifications and leaflets, leadership or participation in integral safety concepts as well as control of their implementation. We also provide information and training for ETH members and contribute our expertise to construction projects. The digital documentation of the quality assurances carried out is also one of our tasks.

In order to be able to fulfil these tasks professionally and effectively, we are looking for a committed, expert and loyal personality at the level of fire protection specialist resp. Fire protection specialist (m/f/d).

Job description

Are you ready to take responsibility for fire and explosion protection in a motivated team in everyday university life? Do you keep the overview in the event of an incident? Then you are exactly right with us!

In this versatile function, you accompany construction projects at ETH Zurich and support the academy, project managers and specialist planners with your in-depth expertise in all fire protection issues. Together with authorities, specialist planners and other stakeholders, you will develop practical fire protection solutions tailored to the needs of ETH Zurich. You test existing and new fire protection concepts, recognize potential for optimization and proactively contribute your suggestions for improvement.

As the central contact person for fire protection topics, you coordinate the cooperation with the various sections within SGU for construction and project enquiries. You review fire documentation such as concepts, fire and fire plans and coordinate their creation and ongoing updates.

Another focus of your activity is quality assurance and control. You organize and carry out internal and external fire police controls, accompany construction acceptances from the point of view of the building owner and ensure compliance with the defined quality and safety requirements. In addition, you take events and ensure the consistent implementation of the applicable fire protection requirements.

In addition, you will advise the academy and the company on questions of explosion protection as well as on the safe handling and proper storage of hazardous substances, in particular combustible liquids and gases. You develop and review concepts, guidelines and internal requirements in your department and thus make a significant contribution to security at ETH Zurich.
