Full description

Are you looking for a task with meaning, technical depth and development opportunities? In our specialized unit for stem and immune cell therapy, you accompany patients in challenging life situations and make a decisive contribution to the success of treatment with your expertise. Look forward to an exciting working environment in which modern therapies, interprofessional cooperation and patient-oriented care are the focus. >The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.

Tasks

  • Care and care of patients: inside with various hemato-oncological diseases, autologous and allogeneic stem cell transplants as well as cellular therapies
  • Application of antitumor therapies and immunotherapies as well as carrying out accompanying care interventions
  • Counselling and support of patients: inside in all phases of the disease and therapy as well as in relation to self-management
  • Participation in learning support in cooperation with the trainer
  • Constructive interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation as an essential part of your work
  • With your professional commitment in all three shifts, you contribute to a high quality of service for our patients:

Requirements

  • Completed training as a qualified nurse HF/FH as well as a willing, independent and resilient personality
  • Joy at work, teamwork skills and a high patient: interior orientation distinguishes you
  • Together with the interdisciplinary team, you take responsibility for the patients and are committed to professional and patient-oriented care.
  • Care adapted to the current life situation and sufficient time for your patient: insides are important to you
  • Good communication skills and a safe handling of complex, fast-changing situations
  • Enjoy sharing your expertise and experience as well as continuously deepening and developing your own professional knowledge

We offer

You work in one of the leading centres for stem cell transplantation and cellular therapies in Switzerland. On our modern station with 16 beds, we look after patients with severe haematological-oncological diseases, including intensive chemotherapy and stem cell transplants according to current standards. You work in a highly specialized environment with innovative therapeutic methods such as CAR-T cell therapy and stem cell transplantation.

We offer you varied tasks, a supportive interprofessional team as well as regular training and further education opportunities. A careful introduction is guaranteed, so that the job is suitable for both experienced specialists and newcomers and offers a wide range of development opportunities.
