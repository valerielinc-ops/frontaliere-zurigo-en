Full description

In the area of administrative and social buildings, we are looking for an experienced project manager for the Projects department.

Tasks

  • You take on the role of project management owner representation in maintenance, repair and new construction projects.
  • They actively advise and represent the tenants and users towards the contracting partners during the entire project and execution phase and coordinate the needs and requirements of the users.
  • They are responsible for the timely procurement of the decision bases of the loan applications.
  • They monitor and control the quality, costs and deadlines through project management and controlling.

Profile

  • Professional with project experience in construction, (e.g. architect, engineer, real estate trustee), with specialisation in business administration and/or project management
  • Several years of practical experience as project manager* in owner representation, preferably: in interdisciplinary projects with professional institutions as tenants or users in the field of office use with modern forms of work such as desk sharing etc.
  • Strong implementation/implementation, sense of pragmatic solutions
  • Winning, convincing and socially competent personality with negotiating skills that remains calm even in difficult situations
  • High degree of personal responsibility, initiative and independence

We offer

You can expect a collegial environment, a motivated team and attractive employment conditions with good social benefits. A structured familiarization is a matter of course for us. The place of work is centrally located, just a few minutes walk from Zurich Central Station. We are open to applications from people with physical impairments.

About us

On behalf of the City Council, we represent and manage around 1900 properties. In the areas of space and infrastructure, we offer numerous services to the city administration and advise the departments and the city council on strategic issues.
