Full description

In the Zurich notary offices, around 650 employees are committed to the concerns of customers and attach great importance to quality, discretion and customer orientation. All 44 notaries in the canton of Zurich are also land register and bankruptcy offices and are part of the legal administration in Zurich. By 01.12.2026 or by agreement a notarial secretary with authorization / notarial assistant / in 100% You work in a motivated and helpful team in a collegial working atmosphere. The preparation and review of contracts in the area of property law (such as purchase, easement and deposit contracts) is just as much a part of your daily work as the advice of customers and the independent certification of contracts. What you bring

  • Completed training businessman/woman EFZ Notariate Switzerland
  • Authorisation for the canton of Zurich
  • Experience in the processing of land register transactions
  • Precise, reliable, independent and forward working methods
  • Excellent knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Professional appearance and confidentiality
  • Service-oriented, friendly and courteous nature We offer you an exciting, versatile and varied activity in a collegial team of around 15 people.
