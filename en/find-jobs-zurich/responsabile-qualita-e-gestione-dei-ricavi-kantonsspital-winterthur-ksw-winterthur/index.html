Full description

In this central leadership function with high visibility, you will lead a versatile department with four direct-subscribed team lines as well as around 45 employees and responsible topics that are crucial for the medical, qualitative and economic development of the hospital. The function is in the area of finance and is part of the area management. Together with your teams, you design the further development of central control and support processes at the interface of medicine, quality, data and economics. Tasks:

  • You are fully responsible for tariffs, performance management (outpatient), medical controlling and coding incl. Return management stationary, quality management incl. Tumor center, certifications and register data as well as the staff quality and performance management.
  • They develop your executives and teams, create orientation and rely on ownership, trust and cooperation. In doing so, you understand taking people along, promoting potentials and supporting them in a targeted manner where leadership and organization are required.
  • They are responsible for the collective bargaining strategy of the hospital and provide sound experience in collective bargaining, fixing procedures, performance contracts and in the further development of collective bargaining and processes.
  • At the same time, you support the clinics and medical areas in achieving their medical and economic goals and actively contribute to issues relating to control, data, processes and salvation.
  • They analyse complex relationships, derive fields of action from them and create transparency and resilient decision bases with sound evaluations and analyses.
  • In addition, the KSW’s leadership advises you on your areas of expertise and actively participate in strategically relevant projects and developments. Furthermore, professional support and further development of quality requirements, certifications and regulatory requirements are part of your responsibility. Profile:
  • Higher education or comparable qualifications
  • Found experience in Swiss collective bargaining, beneficial from the hospital or insurance environment, as well as leadership experience in a comparable function
  • Knowledge of dynamics and challenges of a larger hospital operation, ability to move safely in the field of tension between medicine, quality and economy
  • Impressed leadership qualities as well as responsible, integree and resilient personality
  • experience in dealing with analyses and key figures, ability to quickly identify relationships, to classify complex topics and to convey them to the addressee, to issue relevant orders and to develop solutions together
  • Contact-Friendly, convincing and communication-strong personality with high social competence and strong conceptual understanding Benefits: The KSW provides employees with conditions that enable them to meet their professional goals and personal needs. Flexible, sliding working hours, home office as well as a generous maternity leave and levels of employment adapted to different situations of life – this is all possible at the KSW, provided that it allows operation
  • Every year, the KSW offers 40 advanced and 50 professional training courses. We also support you in external developments in time and/or financial terms
  • In exceptional situations, e.g. longer illness, strong personal burden or difficult experiences in the private as well as in everyday professional life are available to you. The valuable and friendly cooperation within the teams and across the departments is characterized by the working climate at the KSW. As a sign for a strong team idea, the "Du" was introduced as a standard at the KSW. In addition, the KSW supports annual team events financially and organizes a large staff festival for the whole house every four years; KSW employees benefit from very good social benefits. Thus, the pension funds solution with the BVK is far above the statutory minimum. Even in the case of illness or accident, KSW employees are very well secured
  • With discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training options and course offers, discounts in pharmacies and among the most popular health insurance companies. You can benefit from a comprehensive offer for your health
  • Our La Luna daycare centre with generous opening hours is located right next to the KSW. You can also use the income-dependent, financial support provided by the KSW for the crib costs
  • In cooperation with cultural partners in and around Winterthur we regularly offer discounts for concerts and theatre performances. The borrowing of media in the in-house library is also free for employees.
