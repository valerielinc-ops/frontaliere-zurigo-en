137094 | 80 - 100% | Oerlikon, Zurich | Hybrid

Is the idea of establishing a new specialist position motivating you and actively shaping its orientation, cooperation and impact? Do you want to pool expertise, strengthen cross-divisional cooperation and develop effective solutions for the operational teams together with your team? Then we should get to know each other.

As head of Zurich Switzerland, you will strengthen the Claims division in the Claims Life division and make an important contribution to our joint success. You lead the newly created department Performance Life and develop its tasks, cooperation and impact together with the teams in individual and collective life. You are responsible for specialist and operational support in collective life, build up specialist and operational support for individual lives and bring together the previous center-of-competence functions. You ensure that process documentation, case discussions, training, knowledge management, specialist controlling, reporting and projects work together effectively across teams.

This is what awaits you

  • You lead the Service Life department and shape its structure, tasks and cooperation with all areas of Service Life.
  • You bundle the specialist and company support in the collective life and build the specialist and company support in the individual life.
  • You ensure process documentation, professional standards, governance structures and knowledge management and ensure their uniform application.
  • You control case meetings, training courses, specialist controlling and reporting and derive concrete improvements for the teams.
  • You lead cross-divisional projects and strengthen cooperation with internal partners.

This sets you apart

  • Experience in the further development of processes, professional standards and knowledge management
  • Experience in managing employees or in a comparable professional leadership role
  • In-depth experience in social and/or private insurance benefit management
  • Structured, solution-oriented and implementation-strong working method
  • Strong communication and cooperation skills as well as very good knowledge of German

Good to know
