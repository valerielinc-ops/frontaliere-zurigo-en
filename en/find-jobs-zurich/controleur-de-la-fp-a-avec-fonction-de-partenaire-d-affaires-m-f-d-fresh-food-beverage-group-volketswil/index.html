Full description

As an FP&A controller, you actively shape the financial management of the Fresh Food & Beverage Group. You are responsible for controlling, reporting and budgeting, develop systems and processes further and support the business as a strong sparring partner in informed decisions.

Mansioni

  • Responsibility for the monthly CO conclusion (including delimitations, settlement of orders, COPA cycles, etc.).
  • Creation and further development of a user-friendly, driver-based reporting for management (MER, DBR, COGS, Overhead, FTE), including interface to the BI team.
  • Business Partner for specific areas in the overhead, support for financial and strategic issues, preparation and discussion of reporting, support for budgeting.
  • SAP Key User and Process Owner Controlling: Responsible for the maintenance and further development of controlling systems and processes. Optimisation and simplification of cost accounting and value flows.
  • Active role in planning, budget and forecasting processes in SAP / BPC.
  • Ongoing optimization and further development of various financial processes, such as building a forecast concept.
  • Preparation of ad hoc analyses and decision bases for the management and operational decision-making bodies.

Requirements

  • at least 3 years: Controlling (beneficial in a food producing establishment)
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): with specialization in Accounting, Controlling or expert Accounting & Controlling
  • Very sound, field-proven SAP FI/CO knowledge (ideally in S/4HANA).
  • In-depth knowledge in cost accounting and final theses.
  • Excellent analytical and conceptual skills.
  • Strong sense of responsibility, independent working methods and a pronounced goal and solution orientation.
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • French (advantageous)
