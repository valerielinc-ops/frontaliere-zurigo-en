Full description

80% - 100% | IT / Business Engineering | Zurich | Experienced professionals We are the DevOps BI & Calculations team and develop and operate analytical systems and one of the central data warehouses of the Zürcher Kantonalbank. Our task is to provide operational and dispositive data for accounting and controlling, among other things. As a self-organized, agile team of software developers and business engineers, we develop new functionalities based on the SAFe framework within our Agile Release Train. We accompany our applications along the entire product life cycle and are proud of our software made in Kreis 5. If you have a preference for data, quality and modern engineering practices, you are a great fit for us. Your tasks

  • Further development of our systems SAP BW and SAP PaPM as part of the DevOps philosophy
  • Performing technical concepts, analyses and designs of new functions in close cooperation with our specialist colleagues
  • Implementation of requirements in SAP BW and successive assumption of development tasks in SAP PaPM
  • Participation in application operation and ensuring a stable and performant system landscape
  • Advising the department on feasibility as well as variants and opinion formation
  • Analyze and fix software errors in the affected SAP BW and SAP PaPM systems
  • Introducing new technological and methodological impulses and actively developing our engineering practices

Your profile

  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF) in (business) informatics or an equivalent education
  • Several years of practical professional experience in SAP development with a focus on SAP BW
  • First experience in SAP PaPM or great interest in building up this know-how
  • You score with a conceptual, self-responsible and structured working method, fast comprehension and high quality awareness
  • Experience with agile process models is advantageous for you or you want to develop further in this environment
  • Very good knowledge of German and a communicative personality round off your profile

Our offer

  • Motivated, self-organized and established team that looks forward to you
  • Exciting and diverse range of tasks around the SAP BW and SAP PaPM applications with an agile approach
  • Constant further development – personal, professional and technical
  • Various events and internal networks offer you the opportunity to network quickly
  • Modern workplace in the lively district 5 with very good transport connections (hard bridge station) and the possibility to organize your work flexibly (hybrid)
  • Comprehensive package with attractive basic salary, variable compensation and versatile benefits
