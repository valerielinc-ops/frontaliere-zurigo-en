Working at Sprüngli

In our manufactory in Dietikon we are looking for an experienced, motivated and committed person immediately or by agreement. *n

What you do with us

You work in our factory in Dietikon in the Luxembourgerli department and support our team with your commitment to the production of our high-quality creams. From January to August you work between 05.00 and 16.00. During the season from September to December, we operate in two-shift operation, with your operating times from 10 a.m. to about 7 p.m. Due to the seasonal business, it can also occasionally come to stakes on Saturday.

If necessary, you can also support the team in Luxembourgerli production, for example during breaks or during peak loads.

Your tasks

  • Operating the RoboQbo and our cream pasteurizer systems: professional assembly and disassembly, tool and program selection as well as tub, lid and extruder functions.
  • Independent detection and correction of disturbances
  • Timely notification to the technical service, if necessary
  • Professional intermediate and final cleaning according to the cleaning plan
  • Efficient, time-optimized work during production and variety changes
  • Preparation and proper treatment of raw materials: compliance with the cold chain, inventory control, timely organization of TC and thermal goods, work according to the FIFO principle
  • Taking and inputting OPRP samples
  • Control of the masses before emptying and timely preparation based on the daily and weekly schedule
  • Carrying out the MiBi samples and ensuring optimum product quality (weight, optics, etc.) according to the production description
  • Maintaining and controlling production-relevant checklists
  • Ensure order and cleanliness in department and refrigerator
  • The location can vary within the department needs

What we want

  • Experience in food production, ideally in creams, masses
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Independent, careful and quality-conscious working method
  • Technical flair and skill in handling production plants
  • Reliability, teamwork and high flexibility
