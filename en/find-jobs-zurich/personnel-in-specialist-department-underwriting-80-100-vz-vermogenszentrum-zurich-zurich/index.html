Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We create transparency in the banking and insurance market and advise our customers comprehensively and neutrally. As we are growing strongly, we are looking for reinforcements for our insurance team at our headquarters at Paradeplatz in Zurich.

  • You carry out risk assessments in the areas of household contents, private liability, building and motor vehicle insurance.
  • You ensure the entire contract processing.
  • You are responsible for calculations as well as for the creation and tariffing of offers.
  • You make insurance comparisons.
  • You support your teammates with technical questions.
  • You enjoy actively shaping the specialist department.
  • You have completed your commercial apprenticeship with an insurance company or have experience in the private insurance sector.
  • You have excellent knowledge of German and advantageously also have knowledge of French.
  • You want to take more responsibility.
  • You have an independent and proactive way of working.
  • You feel comfortable in a young and dynamic team.
  • The opportunity to help shape and build a newly organized department.
  • We are proud of our uncomplicated, open culture. It gives you the chance to shape and develop a unique company.
  • You are also at the right place with us when it comes to further education: We support our employees individually and also support an external training within the framework of the job profile.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
