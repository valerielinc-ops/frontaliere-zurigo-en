Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,000 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland. For the Senevita Limmatfeld we are looking for an immediately or by agreement Dipl. Nursing specialist HF as residential area management 100% Your tasks

  • Professional, human and organizational management of a care team with around 15 employees
  • Participation in the recruitment of new employees and responsibility for their introduction
  • Ensuring an optimal care and care quality of 16 residents, based on the care concept
  • Planning, coordination and organisation of day-to-day operations
  • Responsible for rostering
  • Ensuring compliance with maintenance processes as well as legal, professional and internal standards and requirements
  • Support and coaching of employees, role model and team motivation
  • Contact person for residents and their relatives
  • Contact person for internal and external bodies and doctors
  • Preparation and assurance of a complete maintenance documentation
  • Cooperation in the further development of care quality and processes
  • Cooperation with other residential management, nursing service management, management and interprofessional teams
  • Participation in management meetings
  • Conducting team meetings, employee meetings and qualifications
  • Approximate division of tasks: 80% bedside care / 20% management and administration tasks

You bring that with you

  • Training as a dipl. Care professional HF/FH
  • Completed leadership training (mandatory)
  • Several years of leadership experience in a comparable function (mandatory), ideally 3–5 years
  • Several years of professional experience in geriatric long-term care
  • Empathetic, structured and quality- and process-oriented leader
  • Knowledge of RAI-LTCF is an advantage
  • Secure handling of electronic maintenance documentation, PEP and common IT applications
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing (at least level B2)
  • High social competence as well as an appreciative and clear communication
  • Self-employed, structured and responsible working practices
  • Economic thinking and action
  • Enjoy the leadership of a team as well as an interprofessional
  • Cooperation with high quality of care
  • Resilience and the ability to maintain an overview even in demanding situations

We offer you

  • A varied activity and flexible working hours
  • The opportunity to work very independently and exchange ideas with an experienced team
  • Extensive training offer of the Senevita Academy, can be visited free of charge
  • Unique culture and various generous employee events
  • Various perks (fuels, supplementary insurance, fitness, pharmacy, etc.)
