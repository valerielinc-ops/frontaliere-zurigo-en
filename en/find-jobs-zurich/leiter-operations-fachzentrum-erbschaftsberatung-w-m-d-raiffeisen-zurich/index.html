Full description

In order to raise the consultations of the specialist centre for inheritance counselling in the context of the entire range of preventive counselling to the next higher level, we are looking for a reliable, innovative and leadership-strong personality who is ready to take action.

We are looking for a head (f/m/d) of the "Operations" sub-area, which, in addition to the digital further development, ensures smooth operational operation of the specialist centre for inheritance consulting. Tasks: • Ensuring smooth operational operation of the centre and ongoing optimisation and standardisation of processes • Answers to technical questions • Ensure efficient cooperation with internal interfaces, in particular IT, preparedness and other specialist areas • Management and cooperation in projects for digitalization, process optimization and further development of the service offering • Personnel management and further development of the Operations team with currently 6 employees (especially junior and assistants) • editing of consulting processes / technical instructions for the officialization of further developments or New services under internal regulations Requirements • Successfully completed basic legal training (e.g. law degree, FH in the field of business and law, notarial training) • Good knowledge of matrimonial property and inheritance law • Leadership experience or the motivation to assume a leadership function with corresponding responsibility • Training in computer science/legal tech is beneficial • Professional experience in the optimization of processes as well as in project and/or change management desired • Entrepreneurial, communication-strong personality with a high level of initiative and a high quality awareness We offer: The work-life balance of our employees is important to us. That’s why we create the conditions for flexible working hours – with offers for mobile work, home office and part-time work.
