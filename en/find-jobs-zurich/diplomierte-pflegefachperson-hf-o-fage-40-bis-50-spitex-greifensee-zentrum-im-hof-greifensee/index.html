Original description

The Foundation Zentrum im Hof ensures comprehensive care for the areas of living and caring for old age on behalf of the municipality of Greifensee. The modern and brightly designed house is located near the historic town of Städtlis and the lake. The centre is connected to three residential care groups with 28 residents and 12 apartments suitable for the age of 18. The public Spitex of the municipality of Greifensee is also affiliated with the foundation.

To complement our team at Spitex, we are looking for an experienced and reliable qualified nurse HF or FAGE 40% to 50%, which puts our clients at the centre of their commitment. Your tasks You care, care and advise clients in their usual environment holistically and maintain, or thus promote their independence. After a careful introduction, you plan the care needs and control and monitor the care process.

Active cooperation with relatives and/or other involved professionals is part of your task as well as close cooperation within the team. Your profile

  • Degree as a specialist in nursing care (FH/HF or FAGE)
  • Self-employed working methods, responsible and committed action
  • Good communication skills and computer skills (Perigon, RAI-HC)
  • Owned a driver's pass, own car (uses are also possible with bike)
  • Re-re-enterers are welcome We offer
  • Careful introduction
  • A dedicated, appreciative team
  • Varied scope of tasks
  • A beautiful and modern workplace
  • Flexible working hours (compatibility of family – work)
  • Minimum five weeks of holidays and good social benefits Do you feel like you are addressed? We would like to meet you. In a personal interview, Mrs. Egli would like to give you an insight into the interesting task.
