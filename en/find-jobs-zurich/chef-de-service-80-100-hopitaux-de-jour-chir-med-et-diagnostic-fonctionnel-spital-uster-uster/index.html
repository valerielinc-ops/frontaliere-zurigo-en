Full description

For our surgical day clinic, medical outpatient department, and functional diagnostics area, we are looking for a leadership personality starting July 1, 2026, or by arrangement. Responsibilities: In this versatile leadership role, you can expect a dynamic work routine with a high degree of responsibility and creative freedom:

  • you will take on personnel and organizational leadership of several departments and, together with your team, ensure that you achieve your goals
  • you will actively participate in daily nursing care, working closely with the team and patients
  • you will contribute to projects, lead them independently, or collaborate in interdisciplinary project groups
  • you will work closely with other professional groups and help shape an open, appreciative, and collegial team culture
  • you will support the development of nursing quality and vocational training, working closely with trainees (students and apprentices) Requirements:
  • completed nursing education with several years of professional experience in acute care, preferably in an outpatient setting
  • you have already gained leadership experience and have completed relevant further training or are willing to do so
  • you enjoy working in a team and value interdisciplinary and interprofessional collaboration
  • you are eager to take on new tasks, communicate clearly and respectfully, and remain resilient and decisive even in challenging situations
  • you think in terms of processes and maintain an overview
  • you have good user knowledge of KISIM and PEP as well as good German language skills, both written and spoken We offer:
  • open and modern work culture in a dynamic environment with room for ideas and creativity
  • family-friendly atmosphere and above-average social benefits, as well as discounts on various brands
  • at least five weeks of vacation and flexible days for shift changes
  • excellent staff restaurant and numerous benefits
  • central location near Uster train station and highway access
  • affordable parking options – with Parkingpay max. CHF 4.50 per day
  • free parking during night shifts (until 07:00)
