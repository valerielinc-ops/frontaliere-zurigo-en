Full description

Override the responsibility for food at the MM Dietikon branch. With your well-founded knowledge of food, you'll advise our customer base. Thanks to your motivated and inspiring way, you contribute significantly to customer satisfaction. That sounds like you? Then apply now!

Mansioni

  • You act as a competent contact person for our customers together with the team and take responsibility for the food sector
  • It gives you pleasure to pass on your expertise and knowledge to new employees and introduce them in the food sector
  • You are responsible for goods orders
  • You take responsibility for efficient goods management, the attractive presentation of the products and ensure that our shelves are always well filled
  • You ensure the high quality of our products including date control
  • If required, you can also support in other store areas

Requisiti

  • 2 years of professional experience: in a similar position
  • Complete professional teaching (EFZ): in sales/detail trade
  • You enter customer-oriented and friendly
  • You like to work independently and reliably
  • You are stress-tolerant and have good communication skills
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa offriamo

  • Social benefits: Fair social benefits
  • Cumulus points: You get additional cumulus points when shopping
  • Migros Bank: preferential interest in private and investment account, free account management, discounted mortgages and more
  • Employee offers: Up to 1,000 CHF per year (depending on your pensum) for your recovery and further development – usable for fitness subscriptions, courses, health offers or a grant for the ÖV subscription.
  • education and training: support for vocational training and training;
  • Social and personal advice: Internal social counselling
