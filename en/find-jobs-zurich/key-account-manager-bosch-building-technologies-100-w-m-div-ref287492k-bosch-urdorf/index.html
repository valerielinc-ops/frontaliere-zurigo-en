Original description

Your tasks As a key account manager for our building technology solutions in Switzerland, you are the face to the customer. You build and develop long-term, strategic partnerships with our customers, sales agents and installation partners. With your deep understanding of the Swiss market and its specific requirements, you position Bosch as a preferred partner for innovative building automation and security technology, primarily in the areas of fire and intrusion alarm systems, access control and video surveillance. Your main tasks include:

  • You identify and analyze the needs of our key customers and develop tailor-made solution strategies.
  • You're begging and expanding your network to decision-makers, planners and relevant actors in the Swiss market.
  • You are responsible for the entire distribution cycle from the acquisition to the supply phase to the conclusion of the contract for complex projects.
  • You analyze market trends and competitive activities to continuously optimize our strategy.
  • You represent Bosch Building Technologies at trade fairs, conferences and customer events.
  • Your profile
  • Education: You have an economic study or comparable qualification, as well as at least 5 years of professional experience in sales or marketing.
  • Experience & know-how: You bring multi-year, successful professional experience in the security or building technology industry. An existing network in the Swiss market for building technology is of great advantage.
  • Personality and work: You are a communication-strong, winning personality with strong negotiation skills and entrepreneurial thinking. Your work is self-sufficient, targeted and you have the pleasure of proactively approaching customers and building relationships.
  • Languages: (Swiss-)German you speak for negotiation. You can also communicate well in English.
