Meet the team

The Sales Planning team within the EMEA Wholesale department plays a key role in driving the business from long-range financial forecasting to monthly sales planning. The team manages in-season performance to ensure targets are met and supports pre-season assortment planning to optimize product and Accounts strategies. Working cross-functionally with Sales, Controlling, Demand Planning, and Operations, the team ensures alignment between commercial goals and operational execution. It is an innovative and constantly evolving function, continuously improving processes and tools to enhance business performance and agility.

Your mission

  • Perform weekly business analyses and generate actionable insights to support the Sales and Management teams in decision-making.
  • Contribute to sales planning activities, including financial forecasting, in-season tracking, and pre-season assortment planning.
  • Enhance and develop analytical tools and files (e.g., sell-out dashboards, forecast roll-ups, invoice trackers, G2N by region/sub-region) to enable more data-driven sales decisions.
  • Support and drive sales-related projects aimed at improving processes, tools, and overall department efficiency.
  • Support on developing AI capabilities within the commercial function.

Your story

  • Demonstrate a proactive and entrepreneurial mindset, taking initiative and ownership of tasks with a “can-do” attitude.
  • You have strong analytical skills and a data-driven approach to support decision-making and identify business opportunities.
  • A natural problem solver, able to navigate ambiguity and find creative, practical solutions.
  • Curious and eager to learn, showing genuine interest in understanding sales processes and business dynamics.
  • You think outside the box and embrace challenges beyond your comfort zone to drive innovation and continuous improvement.
