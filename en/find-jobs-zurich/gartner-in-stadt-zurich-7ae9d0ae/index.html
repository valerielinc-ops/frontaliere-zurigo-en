Tasks

  • They independently carry out demanding care and maintenance work (planting and seeding work, cutting work, plant nutrition, plant protection, plant use) as well as gardening and landscaping work or small changes in the outdoor facilities
  • They participate in maintenance, maintenance and cleaning work on infrastructure, machinery and tools
  • They are responsible for guiding and operating machines and tools
  • They help to guide and accompany learners and/or trained employees with

Profile
