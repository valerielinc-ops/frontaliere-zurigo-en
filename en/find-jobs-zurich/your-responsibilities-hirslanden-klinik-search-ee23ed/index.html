Do you want to contribute your expertise in an interdisciplinary environment and at the same time accompany the next generation of care professionals?

Then you are exactly right with us. In a varied and demanding working day, you take on responsibility, support learners and students in their development and actively shape a professional, patient-oriented care. Together in the team, you ensure a high quality of care and an appreciative learning environment.

YOUR TASKS

  • Advice in specialist care situations
  • High independence and responsibility in clinical decision-making and the measures to be initiated
  • Accompaniment of learners and students of nursing training
  • Implementation of department-internal evaluations and subject-specific short-term training
  • Assumption of additional activities on behalf of the management

For your additional function as a vocational trainer, you will take on the following activities:

  • Accompaniment of learners and students of nursing training
  • Implementation of department-internal evaluations and subject-specific short-term training
  • Competent accompaniment and support of the HF students care as well as the learning specialist health professional as well as pedagogical during their practical training
  • Professional implementation of the educational mission in the department according to the training concept

Your profile

  • Completed training as a dipl. Nursing professional or equivalent SRK recognition in foreign diplomas
  • At least two years of professional experience in acute care
  • Vocational education and training: Vocational trainers in SVEB 1 or willingness to complete them
  • Good knowledge of German (verbal and written)
  • Interest and motivation to accompany development and learning processes among learners and students of the nursing professions
  • Openness, communication, commitment and reliability

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. You work with a modern medical clinic information system and can expect extensive training and development opportunities from us. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family responsibilities. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. Employees who have to move completely for their work receive four additional paid days off with a 100% workload. With a reduced workload, the days off reduce accordingly. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from an equivalent financial compensation. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.

For additional information, Ambra Cotta, Head of Bed Station under T or Janine Moschetto, Team Leader HR Recruitment Partner Platz Zürich at janine.moschetto@hirslanden.ch are at your disposal. We look forward to your contact.
