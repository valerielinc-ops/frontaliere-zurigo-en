Full description

Would you like to help shape the region on the left shore of Lake Zurich and in the Limmat valley with around 50 employees and in close exchange with the municipalities? As a manager/in road region, you are responsible for the construction and operational road maintenance as well as the further development of the transport infrastructure. If you are enthusiastic about a varied area of responsibility at the interface between construction activities, management and politics, you are at the right place. Their tasks

  • They lead the road region with around 50 employees in three maintenance districts and bear overall responsibility for the operation and maintenance of our transport facilities as well as the implementation of road construction projects.
  • As the owner of the state roads, you maintain dialogue with municipalities, authorities and the population and act as the central point of contact.
  • Together with your project managers, you prioritize, control and implement maintenance projects over the entire project cycle.
  • They drive innovation in civil engineering and develop standards for sustainable and economic road maintenance.
  • You are actively involved as a member of the management of the Road Inspectorate. Your profile
  • They are well-versed in dealing with different stakeholders and can skilfully respond to different concerns.
  • Through your strong negotiating skills, you will find good solutions for the parties involved.
  • They have several years of leadership experience and understand how to successfully lead and develop operational teams and project managers.
  • You have completed your studies as a civil engineer or a comparable education as well as in-depth professional experience in civil engineering.
  • They recognise developments in civil engineering at an early stage and actively promote topics such as sustainability, digitalisation or new construction methods.
