Digital Business Zurich | Pensum: 80-100 %

Working on the pulse of digital transformation. Our Digital Business department makes it possible. Together with us, shape the future of real estate management, develop innovative products and processes and move Livit forward together with us. In digital business, you can expect a great, interdisciplinary team, an inspiring working atmosphere and exciting projects in which you can actively participate. Are you excited about innovations, the latest systems, an agile setting and new challenges? Then you are exactly right with Livit. As an experienced leader in digital product management, you take responsibility for the further development of our digital channels and actively shape the digital tenant journey of the future.

Your tasks

  • You lead your team goal-oriented, promote the further development of employees and ensure a high quality of results
  • You are responsible for the digital channels for our tenants, in particular Portal, App, CRM Salesforce and self-service solutions
  • You work operationally and take on tasks in product ownership, conception and implementation control
  • You design the tenant journey with a focus on customer benefit, user-friendliness and satisfaction
  • Prioritize the product roadmap along customer needs, business value and strategic goals
  • You work closely with management, marketing, customer experience and other specialist areas
  • Together with Property Management Solutions and Engineering, you ensure integration into ERP and backend systems
  • Measure usage, customer satisfaction and value proposition against relevant KPIs and drive the use of AI to further develop customer service

Your profile

  • You have a university degree in computer science, business informatics or a comparable education or training with a focus on digital, product management or customer experience
  • You have several years of experience in the management of digital products and services, ideally in the environment of apps, portals or self-service solutions
  • You have deep experience in the CRM environment, experience with Salesforce is an advantage
  • You have experience in managing teams or in the technical management of interdisciplinary product teams and external service providers
  • You have a strong understanding of UX and CX and understand how digital solutions create sustainable customer value
  • You have experience using AI-powered solutions in customer service or digital customer experience
  • You communicate sovereignly between business and IT and convince through moderation strength and a good process understanding
  • You are characterized by customer orientation, self-initiative and a high level of social and self-competence
  • You communicate stylishly in German and English and are enthusiastic about new technologies and digital innovations

For this vacancy, only direct applications are considered and we do not accept dossiers from recruitment agencies.
