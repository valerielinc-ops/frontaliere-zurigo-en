Full description

We are looking for future, ambitious store leaders with mobility in the regions of Zurich, St.Gallen, Zug and Lucerne who want to start with enthusiasm and energy in sports retail! In an intensive incubation phase, you will learn everything about our company, processes, tools and the dynamic world of retail. Then you take over the management of a new branch or take over an existing branch. You live and share our values (vitality, responsibility, authenticity & generosity) and take on the following mission:

  • Education of your dream team: You lead a large and diverse team, promote cooperation, develop talent and create a positive working atmosphere.
  • Strategic Planning: You develop and implement local strategies to further develop the store, increase customer satisfaction and achieve business goals.
  • Excellent customer service: You ensure that our team delivers outstanding customer service, promotes personal shopping and delivers customized solutions for our customers.
  • Operational Efficiency: You optimize store processes, inventory management and resource use to maximize efficiency and profitability.
  • Omnichannel strategy: You develop and implement a seamless omnichannel strategy to provide our customers with an optimal shopping experience across all channels.
  • Local networking: You build strong relationships with sports clubs, local communities and other stakeholders in the region What to expect: With us, you take on responsibility from day one and promote a strong team spirit. With us you will find opportunities for professional development, both nationally and internationally and enjoy a wide range of benefits, profit sharing, 30 days of vacation to ensure a healthy work-life balance and many more. Every day brings new challenges and exciting opportunities for personal and professional development. Profile
  • Your Decathlon passion: You live and embody our values and are passionate about sports.
  • Leadership: You have several years of experience in a management position in the retail sector, ideally in a comparable role with personnel responsibility for a larger team.
  • Strategic Thinking & Intrapreneurship: You have strong business and analytical thinking skills, stand out for your proactivity and are able to make strategic decisions.
  • Customer focus: You have a strong passion for customer service and are committed to exceeding customer expectations.
  • Earnings orientation: You are motivated to achieve goals and have a proven track record in increasing sales and profitability.
  • Communication strength: You communicate clearly and convincingly and are able to build relationships with different stakeholders.
  • Language skills: You speak fluent German (C1) and English (B2). French or other languages are beneficial. If you want to take this challenge, apply now and become part of Decathlon! We look forward to meeting you! Decathlon is committed to creating a diverse environment and is proud to be an employer providing equal opportunities and implementing measures in this context.
