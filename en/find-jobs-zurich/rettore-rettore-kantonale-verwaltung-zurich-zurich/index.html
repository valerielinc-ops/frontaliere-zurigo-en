Canton of Zurich Education Directorate General Vocational School Zurich Rector / Rector 100 % With more than 2000 apprentices, ABZH is one of the major vocational schools in the Canton of Zurich. Located in the immediate vicinity of Zurich Central Station, more than 100 teachers and a professional team in the administration are committed to a high-quality education and training offer. The position of the Rectorate is temporarily filled until the end of this school year and is therefore publicly advertised for a definitive occupation as of 1.9.2027. We are therefore looking for a new rector / rector. You lead the school management and bear the educational, personnel and financial overall responsibility. The school management comprises the three departments „Culinary and Hospitality „Applied Technology, Consulting and Documentation „ Society, Language and Movement. Furthermore, you are subordinated to the “Central Services” division. You will be supported in your work by the departmental management, the administrative management and individual staff offices. Together with your team, you ensure the operation of the school and, in consultation with the competent authorities in the canton, shape the future of the ABZH. You yourself take on a small amount of lessons. You actively maintain contacts outside the school, in particular with economic bodies and professional associations. Your profile

  • Completed studies (Tertiary level A or B)
  • Teaching qualification in the professional field of ABZH
  • Leadership experience in the education and training of a cantonal vocational college
  • Experience in dealing with cantonal administration
  • Knowledge of the Swiss vocational training landscape
  • Good networking with professional associations and bodies
  • Joy and passion for personnel management and development
  • Addressee-friendly communication
  • Strong conceptual skills (process development)
  • Empathetic, long-term thinking leader Our offer The dynamic environment of vocational training offers you a very attractive and diverse management task at one of the leading vocational schools in the Canton of Zurich. In close cooperation with the school committee, your management team and the teachers, you can play a key role in shaping and developing the future of ABZH. We look forward to your application documents at the latest 15. October 2026.Please submit your documents exclusively via the online tool. For further information, please contact Paul Müller, President of the School Commission, email: paul.mueller@abzh.ch.
