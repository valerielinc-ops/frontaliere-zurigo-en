Full description

Ready for a leading role in the distribution area of Wädenswil? With you, we are determined to leverage synergies, drive innovation and build a strong network together that takes our logistics processes to a new level. Shape the future of the post with us!

Are you motivated and would like to further develop the distribution along the entire service delivery process and make a contribution to the post of tomorrow? Then apply now! With you alone or in a powerful duo, our employees are in the best hands!

You can do that.

  • You manage and take responsibility for the Wädenswil distribution area operationally, financially and in terms of personnel.
  • Together with your management team, you are responsible for providing optimal and efficient services in day-to-day business.
  • You use synergies and optimization potential across all functions and ensure efficient and high-quality logistics processes in the distribution area.
  • You will continuously analyse the market and competitive situation of CEP services (courier, express and parcel services) and develop strategies to strengthen the market position and open up new business areas.
  • You drive forward measures and projects for the further development of culture, organization, processes, sustainability and lead or support projects and working groups.
  • As a local contact person for logistics services, you are responsible for communicating with internal and external stakeholders and will profitably expand a strong network in the logistics environment.
  • You work in higher-level national projects in the competence center sorting and delivery as well as in the area of transport.

You bring that with you

  • You bring several years of leadership experience in a larger operational area and have a track record in change and project management.
  • You have completed, higher education in the areas of process or logistics management, leadership and business administration.
  • You have clear visions and ambitious goals and take responsibility for their implementation.
  • You focus on the success of the team, support your team as a leader and coach and act as a role model in values and behavior.
  • With courage and openness, you face new challenges and drive forward the necessary transformation processes and digitalization projects.
  • You communicate very well verbally and in writing in German and have good knowledge of a second national language.

For you, flexibility becomes everyday life. We promote progressive teamwork and value a balanced working life. This position can also be taken over by two people in job sharing. You can apply directly as a duo, or we support you in the search for a suitable person.
