Original description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

As part of Sulzer AG's Water & Industrial business unit, Sulzer Flow Germany GmbH sells products and solutions for the mechanical and biological purification of municipal and industrial wastewater.

As a service technician, you are our face to the customer. On daytime assembly, you investigate and solve problems that arise in customer systems. They are used as part of new assembly, commissioning, conversion work, revisions, maintenance, diagnostics and repairs of pumps, lifting systems and controls.

In this position you are responsible for

  • Testing, maintenance and repair of pumps, lifting systems and controls at the installation site
  • New installation of pump stations, lifting systems and switchgear at the installation site
  • Fault analysis
  • Advising our customers on technical issues

To be successful in this position you should have the following:

  • Completed technical vocational training as a mechatronics engineer (or electronics engineer / industrial mechanic or comparable)
  • Ideally an electrician for specific tasks (otherwise we carry out further training)
  • First professional experience as a service technician is an advantage
  • Basic knowledge of Word and Excel
  • Driving license class B
  • Willingness to travel (approx. 80% day trips)
