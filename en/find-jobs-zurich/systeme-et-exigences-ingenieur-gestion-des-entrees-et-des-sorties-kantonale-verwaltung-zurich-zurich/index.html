Canton of Zurich Office for Computer Science Systems and Requirements Engineer Input & Output Management 80 - 100% The Office for Computer Science (AFI) is the computer science competence center of the Canton of Zurich. It provides comprehensive services in the area of central computer infrastructure and canton-wide applications for directorates, authorities, the administration of justice and independent institutions. In charge of implementing the new Information and Communication Technology (ICT) strategy, the Office of Informatics accompanies and supports the Canton of Zurich in the digital world. Connecting documents, information and processes intelligently – which is exactly what your heart beats for? Then become part of the Office for Computer Science and actively shape the digital administration of the Canton of Zurich. Together we develop solutions that are used by thousands of employees every day and create real added value. Your tasks:

  • Analysis and modeling of business processes in the area of input/output management in scanning / printing
  • Collection, structuring and specification of technical and technical requirements together with the various stakeholders.
  • Implementation and moderation of workshops and interviews for the development of sustainable solutions.
  • Creation and maintenance of use cases, user stories and requirements documentation.
  • Accompaniment of technical implementation as well as planning and coordination of tests.
  • Responsibility in demand, incident and change management as well as close cooperation with internal and external partners.
  • Coordination of external suppliers and promotion of knowledge transfer within the service environment.
  • Further development and optimization of document processes taking into account high quality and security standards.
  • Identification of optimization potential and active participation in the design of modern IT services. Your profile:
  • University degree (FH/uni) in Business Informatics or a comparable subject area.
  • Several years of experience in requirements engineering, business engineering or in a similar role.
  • Enjoy analysing complex requirements and developing viable solutions together with different stakeholders.
  • Experience in test management, project management or document management is an advantage.
  • Analytical, structured and independent working methods and strong communication skills.
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English. What we offer you:
  • We offer you a diverse task at the pulse of the computer science of the Canton of Zurich
  • This interesting task offers You have a high degree of independence and personal responsibility in a motivated team
  • Your work-life balance is important to us, so we support you with home office opportunities and flexible working hours
  • The Canton of Zurich is a solid, fair and reliable employer, with a modern working environment in the heart of Zurich Do you recognize yourself? Then apply now, we look forward to it!
