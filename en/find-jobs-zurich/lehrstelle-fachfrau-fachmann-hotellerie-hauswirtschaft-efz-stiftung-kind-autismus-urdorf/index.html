Full description

Start of teaching: 16. August 2027 You love it when everything is clean and tidy, and you prefer to join in instead of just sitting around? You want a varied education where you see what you have achieved every day? Then this could be your teaching position!

At the Foundation Child and Autism, you can expect a versatile education in a special and meaningful environment. Together with our housekeeping team, you ensure that children, young people and employees feel at home with us. With us, no day is exactly like the other. During your apprenticeship, you will get to know the different areas of the hotel industry. Where possible, external internships are offered in another company.

You

· Get to know professional cleaning and hygiene techniques · Cleans and maintains rooms, facilities and equipment · Work in the laundry and get to know the entire laundry cycle · Learn how to handle food, materials and equipment correctly · Helps with the planning and organization of domestic processes · Help in the kitchen · Takes on more responsibility step by step

Your work makes a difference

Whether cleaning or laundry or with the help of the kitchen: With your work every day, you make children and adolescents with autism feel comfortable in a well-groomed and pleasant environment. You are not alone. Our experienced housekeeping team accompanies you during your training, supports you in your learning and helps you to become more independent step by step.

You

· Completed school education (Second A or B) · Great willingness to learn and a sense of responsibility · Completion and patience as well as interest in dealing with our children · Helpful, sociable and team-oriented · Careful appearance, good manners as well as hygiene awareness and sense of order · Creativity and an eye for detail · A warm and humorous basic attitude · Enjoying versatile challenges and practical work · Good knowledge of German in words and writing · 6 weeks holidays · Regulated working hours from Monday to Friday · Free lunch · CHF 8.- Catering expenses per school day · We pay for your school supplies · Training days · Staffing events

And of course an appreciative team, varied

  • Tasks and a special working environment with children and young people.

Then we want to know you!

· Application letter · CV · School certificates · Interlocking test · trial confirmations or trial reports, if available Apply directly through our website: www.kind-autismus.ch/die-stiftung/jobs/
