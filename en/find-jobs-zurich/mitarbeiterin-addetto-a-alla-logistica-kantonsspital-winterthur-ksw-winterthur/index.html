Full description

For our supply logistics we are looking for an independent, reliable and service-oriented personality as of 01.01.2027 or by agreement, who will actively support our team.

Tasks

  • You are actively involved in our supply logistics, which supplies the entire hospital with consumables
  • As a knowledge provider / in supply logistics, you train employees and support them with technical questions
  • You support the setup and conversion of warehouses and ensure their order, cleanliness and optimal use
  • You check the daily arrivals, book the goods and store them in the right place or transfer them directly
  • In case of delivery deviations, you clarify matters independently with the purchase and other contact persons
  • You accept letters and parcels and ensure their correct distribution throughout the hospital

Profile

  • You have an apprenticeship as a logistician or equivalent training
  • You bring several years of professional experience in logistics, ideally in the hospital environment
  • You work independently, reliably, structured and exact and also like to physically participate
  • You are customer- and service-oriented, work fast and keep an overview even in hectic situations
  • You have MS-Office knowledge and a very good knowledge of German

Benefits: Work and private life should fit together. Therefore, the KSW offers you – where the company makes it possible – flexible and flexible working hours, home office, generous maternity leave, paternity leave and employment levels that can be adapted to different phases of life. Develop yourself further professionally and personally: Every year, around 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses are open to you at KSW. We also support external training – temporally and/or financially.

  • Sometimes it needs support. In the event of personal stress, prolonged illness or difficult experiences in your professional or private life, various confidential discussions are available at KSW.
  • At KSW we work together and for each other. Appreciation, open and respectful communication and cooperation across professional groups and departments shape our everyday work. You should be able to contribute with your ideas and strengths and develop.

Your future is also ensured: You benefit from very good social benefits and a pension fund solution with BVK, which is significantly above the legal minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, you are very well covered as a KSW employee.

  • Your health is important to us. Benefit from discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training and courses as well as discounts in pharmacies and selected health insurance companies.
  • Family and work can be well connected with us. The daycare center La Luna with spacious opening hours is right next to the KSW. In addition, the KSW supports you – depending on income – financially with the crib costs.

There is also something to discover outside of work: Thanks to our cooperation with cultural partners in Winterthur and the surrounding area, you regularly benefit from discounts for concerts and theater performances.
