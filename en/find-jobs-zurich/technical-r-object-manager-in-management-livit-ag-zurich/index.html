Full description

Are you open-minded, ambitious and value a future-oriented way of working on the pulse of the real estate industry? At Livit, you are committed to the concerns of owners:inside and tenants:in with an exemplary customer orientation and thus contribute significantly to a trusting, long-lasting customer relationship and a positive corporate image. Are you ready to tackle the challenges of today and tomorrow and make a difference in the real estate world? Then you are exactly right with Livit. We look forward to your personal contribution.

Tasks

  • You support and advise the management in structural and building-related matters and use condition reports to ensure the documentation for the measures and their budgeting
  • You coordinate, control and monitor external service providers and guarantee the contractual services
  • You ensure the specifications of optimal maintenance and the proper functioning of the technical infrastructure and initiate targeted maintenance measures if necessary.
  • You control the construction and pay attention to costs, deadlines, quality, environment and safety
  • You take over the professional handling of defects, damage and guarantee cases in close cooperation with management and construction management
  • You accompany renovation projects and are the central contact person for management at external construction management or Livit construction
  • You advise on energy and environmental measures and show optimization suggestions and improvement measures

Requirements

  • You have completed a vocational apprenticeship in the construction industry (e.g. draftsman: in or facility management or a comparable technical training)
  • You bring a commercial education or professional experience in the real estate or construction industry
  • You have experience in facility management, building maintenance or technical management
  • You are familiar with the handling of construction projects in the field of construction
  • You have a strong technical understanding of structural damage and warranty cases, know relevant standards and regulations and can derive causes of damage in a comprehensible manner
  • You communicate safely and professionally with internal and external contacts and always act service-oriented
  • You work structured, fast and keep track even under time pressure
  • You are a team player with initiative, willingness to learn and organizational talent

We offer

  • Internal offer of our Livit Academy for specialist and leadership courses
  • Financial and/or time support for external specialist training and access to electronic learning platforms for online language courses
  • individual career planning and development prospects
  • Home / Mobile Office
  • Bonus depending on company success and personal performance
  • At least 5 weeks holiday and purchase of up to 10 additional days holiday (Ferienflex)
  • Above-average pension benefits and premiums for medical day allowance and accident insurance are 100% covered by Livit
  • 14 days paternity leave
