Production Coordinator & Administration (100%)

Two Spice AG — Dietlikon, Dietlikon, Svizzera

Yooji’s Manufaktur, which is part of Two Spice AG, offers a versatile and exquisite range of sushi, sashimi and much more. The production site of the Two Spice Manufaktur is located in Rümlang and employs more than 90 people. Various delicacies are pre-made every day and delivered to Yooji's restaurants and stores several times a day. Specially developed products for our Japanese retail brand ZENBU are also produced in the factory. ZENBU products such as Sushi, Poké, Onigiri and vegan delicacies are produced for Coop and are available directly from Coop throughout Switzerland. Do you want to be part of our team? Now is your chance!

Tasks

  • Support of production management in administrative and organisational matters
  • Administrative work such as orders, quality reports, work instructions and data maintenance
  • Performance and documentation of internal quality controls
  • Telephone contact person for customers
  • Preparation and implementation of internal training
  • Organisation of the production office
  • Supporting production in day-to-day business
  • Ensuring compliance with hygiene and quality standards

Requirements

  • Completed training in the food sector at EFZ level
  • Professional experience in food production
  • Very good knowledge of German, English is an advantage
  • Good MS Office knowledge and IT affinity
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Organizational skills as well as high quality and hygiene awareness
  • Team-capable, resilient and solution-oriented personality
  • Readiness for weekend assignments
