Job Title: Lecturer in Polydesign (3D Design) IKEA Job Description:

We are seeking a qualified and experienced Lecturer in Polydesign (3D Design) to join our team at IKEA.

The position is for a full-time, fixed-term contract from August 1, 2027, to July 31, 2031, with a focus on Styling.

  • Job Title: Lecturer in Polydesign (3D Design) IKEA Job Description:
  • We are seeking a qualified and experienced Lecturer in Polydesign (3D Design) to join our team at IKEA.
  • Key Responsibilities:
  • Develop and deliver educational programs and workshops on polydesign, 3D modeling, and styling for IKEA employees.
  • A bachelor's or master's degree in a relevant field such as design, architecture, or engineering.
  • Significant experience in polydesign, 3D modeling, and styling. A strong understanding of IKEA's design principles and brand identity.

Main responsibilities

  • Key Responsibilities:
  • Develop and deliver educational programs and workshops on polydesign, 3D modeling, and styling for IKEA employees.
  • Collaborate with product development teams to ensure that design and styling principles are applied consistently across our product range.
  • Provide mentorship and guidance to junior designers and stylists, helping them to develop their skills and expertise.
  • Stay up-to-date with the latest trends and developments in polydesign and styling, and incorporate these into our educational programs.
  • Evaluate the effectiveness of our educational programs and make recommendations for improvement.

Key requirements

  • A bachelor's or master's degree in a relevant field such as design, architecture, or engineering.
  • Significant experience in polydesign, 3D modeling, and styling. A strong understanding of IKEA's design principles and brand identity.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to work effectively with diverse teams.
  • Experience in delivering educational programs and workshops.
  • A passion for design and a commitment to continuous learning and development.
