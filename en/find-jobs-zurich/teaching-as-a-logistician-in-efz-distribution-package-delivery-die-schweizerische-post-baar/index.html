Full description

Do you like to start the day early, do you love being in the fresh air, and do you enjoy putting a smile on people’s faces? Then an education in delivery (distribution) is just right for you! During the three-year basic education, you will acquire the tools to successfully enter the diverse world of logistics.

Your education

  • In the early morning you sort your packages and load your yellow delivery vehicle for the following tour.
  • After that, you will be on the road independently, bringing and fetching shipments of all kinds and taking care of the concerns of our customers.
  • Before you start your well-deserved evening, you will do various reworks at your training position.
  • During your training, you will be supervised by professionals, introduced to practice and continuously developed.
  • In addition, with the financial support of Swiss Post, you acquire the driving license for motor cars of category B and learn how to drive a truck.
  • With various internal stations, an assignment in a warehouse, as well as individual support measures according to your wishes, we complete your broad logistics training.

You bring that with you

We are looking for young people with enthusiasm, joy and motivation who want to make a difference. You should fulfill the following points:

  • Working in the fresh air and physical activity inspire you (robustness is a prerequisite).
  • In addition, you enjoy dealing with people, are reliable, honest and secretive.
  • At the beginning of your apprenticeship, you are at least 17 years old.
  • You are ready to obtain the learning license for motor cars of category B until the start of the apprenticeship.
  • You have completed compulsory schooling.
  • At the beginning of the course you have the language level B2 in German.
