Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Career Counselling Vocational, study and career consultants / in health professions 60% Good for life - this is our motto. As part of the Education Directorate of the Canton of Zurich, the Office for Youth and Vocational Counselling is responsible for the offers in the field of child and youth welfare as well as in vocational counselling. Our work focuses on children, young people and families. We work for them every day with a lot of heart and commitment. The biz Oerlikon is a cantonal competence center for professional, study and career advice. We are looking for a 60% professional, study and career consultant as of 1 January 2027 or by agreement.

  • Your responsibilities: Together with a colleague, you form the specialist agency for specialized career counselling for health professions in the biz Oerlikon. In this role, you will advise customers who are already working in the healthcare sector or are interested in entering this professional field. In addition to individual consultations – also on behalf of RAV or IV – you carry out information events for young people. They supervise information services, participate in trade fairs and maintain diverse contacts.
  • Your profile: You are a professional, study and career consultant with completed education and experience in advising adults. Ideally, you will bring experience, knowledge or a healthcare network. They have a high level of performance competence and an independent and efficient way of working with high quality standards. Working in a large organization and with different contacts makes you fun. They work service-oriented, are curious, humorous and strive to find good and practical solutions with customers. Our offer: You can expect a challenging, versatile and independent activity in cooperation with an experienced and motivated colleague.
  • We offer you cantonal employment conditions and a modern workplace within walking distance to the Zurich-Oerlikon station. Your application: Stephan Oetterli, Head of biz Oerlikon, tel. 043 259 96 64, and Julia Stahel, Career Consultant Health Professions, tel. 043 258 78 68, are at your disposal for further information. We look forward to your online application.

Pensum: 60%
