Full description

As a security officer for energy solutions, you design safe working environments, provide proactive advice and support the line with full energy.

Tasks

  • You will advise and support supervisors and project managers in the implementation of ASGS tasks.
  • You conduct regular audits, checks and inspections and ensure that agreed measures are implemented and documented.
  • By means of hazard assessments, also in compliance with the current standards, you keep the employee risk minimal.
  • As a central point of contact for proactive risk consulting, you identify the need for action, develop solutions and drive their implementation.
  • You will coordinate and lead internal training and instructions on occupational safety, safety updates and prevention campaigns.

Profile

  • You have completed technical training with subject-specific further training (specialist for occupational safety and health protection).
  • Several years of professional experience in a comparable position as well as profound knowledge in the field of occupational safety you bring with you.
  • You are well versed in assessing hazards, taking the necessary precautionary measures and conducting audits.
  • Your working method is analytical, structured and solution-oriented and you are familiar with up-to-date working methods and tools.
  • You convince different stakeholders with your communication skills. Knowledge of French is an advantage.

For example, you can buy up to 2 weeks of extra holidays and work part-time at all levels. Job sharing/splitting and unpaid holidays are also possible with us.
  • Sustainable and cheaper travel: With the mobility contribution of up to 600 francs a year, you travel cheaply and sustainably – whether you want to cycle through Zurich or explore Switzerland by train.
  • An investment in your future: We not only value paying fair and transparent wages, but also ensure that you have more of your wages left over. We take over 60% of the savings contributions and additionally offer a super-compulsory interest on the capital accumulated.

  • Development and training: With our talent management, we promote your development in a targeted manner – through a wide range of internal and external training offers as well as exciting development opportunities.
  • Meaningful work: Sustainability and meaningful work are not just words for us: shape Zurich and the future for us. Together with us, release energy for a good morning.
