Your tasks

  • You support the Research Manager in operational and administrative day-to-day business and ensure a smooth process.
  • Administrative HR processes for new and existing team members will accompany you from the preparation of the required documents to recruitments and interviews to entry and onboarding.
  • In administrative financial processes, you get involved – especially in the creation and updating of budget overviews, invoicing and processing as well as accounting in cooperation with the relevant specialist departments.
  • Sessions, workshops, retreats and other events you organize independently and take over their preparation and follow-up.
  • You create agendas and protocols reliably and keep an eye on pendences, dates and deadlines.
  • You coordinate internal and external meetings and appointments carefully and prepare them.
  • In the preparation, compilation and preparation of research, project and other reports, you actively support the team.
  • Project documents and administrative documentation are coordinated and maintained in a structured and conscientious manner.
  • Within the framework of research projects and clinical trials, you will support administrative and regulatory processes, in particular the coordination of study documents, agreements and other relevant regulatory documents.
  • As a central contact person, you coordinate communication with internal and external partners and specialist departments.
  • When setting up and further developing and optimizing administrative processes and structures, you actively contribute your ideas.
  • Team members support you in clarifying processes, responsibilities and required documents and thus contribute to efficient work processes. Your profile
  • Completed higher education in an area relevant to the function, such as administration/management, healthcare or life sciences; a university degree (Bachelor or Master) is an advantage
  • Several years of experience in an administrative or coordinating function, ideally in the university, clinical or scientific environment
  • Knowledge of regulatory requirements and procedures in clinical research as well as experience with study documents and agreements are beneficial or alternatively willing to acquire this knowledge quickly
  • Interest in scientific and medical issues as well as openness and curiosity for research topics
  • Experience in budget/financial administration and project coordination
  • Independent, structured and reliable working methods as well as strong organisational talent
  • Enjoy a dynamic, versatile interface function and the coordination of different tasks
