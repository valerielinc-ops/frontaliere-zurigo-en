The department OST B 1+2 (Cardiology and Cardiac Surgery) is looking for a dedicated and experienced nurse for the function as vocational trainer .

Main responsibilities

  • Development and planning of learning units: Together with the education manager at level 5 or 6, you are responsible for the design, planning and evaluation of practice-oriented learning units
  • Promotion and qualification of learners and students: You support our learners and students through targeted advice, accompany them in their learning process and ensure a high-quality education
  • Conducting interviews and certificates of competence: You conduct interviews for entry, location and internship qualifications and document the learning processes in a structured manner
  • Consulting and supporting the team: You advise and support your colleagues in the care of the apprentices and thus contribute to the professional development of the entire team
  • Quality assurance and further development of education: In close cooperation with those responsible for education, you implement quality assurance measures and actively contribute to the further development of education in the department
  • Professional for educational topics: As a contact person for all questions about education in the department, you ensure the flow of information and actively participate in the discussion of professional and educational policy developments
  • Collaboration in working groups and projects: You participate in various projects and working groups for the further development of the department and the care service
  • Active participation in patient care: You also contribute your professional competence directly to patient care and actively participate in everyday care in order to optimally shape the interaction between training and practice
  • Participation in the LAG: You work actively in the learning and working community (LAG) of the department and support its implementation and further development Requirements
