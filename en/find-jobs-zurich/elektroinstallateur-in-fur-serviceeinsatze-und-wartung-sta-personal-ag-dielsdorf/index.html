Full description

In this task, you independently take over the maintenance and repair of plants in your area. The company offers varied fields of work in an established traditional company. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • You will independently take care of the maintenance and repairs of our facilities in your service area.
  • Regular checks and minor repairs are part of your everyday life.
  • You plan your assignments yourself and make sure that the right material is always at hand.
  • Customers value you as a competent and reliable local contact.
  • From time to time you are also in demand for piquettes – because we count on you.

Requirements

  • Completed technical training, electrical or mechanical.
  • Experience in the field is an advantage, but not a must – the main thing is, you tackle.
  • Independent, reliable and always customer-oriented.

  • Resilient and with a sense of organization.
  • Very good knowledge of German and driving licence category B.

Offer
