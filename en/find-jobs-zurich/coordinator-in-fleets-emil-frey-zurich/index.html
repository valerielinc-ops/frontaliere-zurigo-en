Full description

What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family-owned company with over 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation, and customer orientation are the pillars of the company. The focus of activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessory parts, and the provision of financial services. The central administration of the Emil Frey Group is located at the headquarters in Zurich-Altstetten, which coordinates the overarching activities. The branch management of Emil Frey AG supports and coordinates various aspects of business operations, including sales, customer service, marketing, controlling, organization, etc. For our central retail department, we are looking for a Fleet Coordinator in the fleet management area. Your responsibilities

  • You independently create purchase, full-service leasing offers, and purchase contracts
  • You manage framework agreements and coordinate vehicle returns from large fleets and rental car companies
  • You monitor sales results and volume plans
  • You plan and coordinate smooth vehicle deliveries
  • You carefully record and maintain customer and vehicle master data
  • You actively participate in projects and tenders and support the team at customer events In this varied hub function, you actively support the team in achieving revenue and sales targets in the fleet business. Your profile
  • Commercial or technical basic education with a commercial further education (e.g., sales coordinator) is an advantage

  • Experience in the automotive or leasing market as well as interest in the areas of fleets and commercial vehicles
