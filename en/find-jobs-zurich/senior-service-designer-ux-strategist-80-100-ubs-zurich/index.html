Full description

Key responsibilities As a Senior Service Designer / UX Strategist in the Experience Design Center of Excellence, you will play a key role in shaping end-to-end client experiences across UBS. You will define and orchestrate seamless journeys across touchpoints, channels, people, processes and technology, ensuring consistent, high-quality experiences at scale.

You will work on innovative products, services and AI-powered experiences, helping pioneer the future of human–computer interaction in banking. Beyond hands-on design work, you will lead complex design workstreams, align stakeholders, translate research and business strategy into actionable design direction, and contribute to the maturity of the CoE’s AI experience design practice.

Responsibilities

  • lead strategic service design and UX strategy workstreams across digital and AI-enabled banking journeys, from discovery to delivery support and continuous improvement post-launch.
  • partner with AI platform, business, product, technology, marketing and client need teams to bridge client needs and business strategy, driving scalable and client-centric service experiences.
  • create and evolve end-to-end journey maps, service blueprints and target-state experience concepts to uncover systemic pain points, align stakeholders and prioritize experience improvements.
  • translate qualitative and quantitative research insights into strategic recommendations that inform service design, product direction and prioritization.
  • contribute hands-on across service design, UX/UI design, interaction design, prototyping, conversation design, usability testing and delivery quality assurance.
  • facilitate workshops and strategic alignment sessions using methods such as Design Sprints, Lean UX Canvas, journey mapping, service blueprinting and empathy mapping.
  • represent design in cross-functional stakeholder discussions, helping clarify scope, effort, dependencies, risks, business value and trade-offs.
  • contribute to the evolution of the CoE’s AI experience design practice, including frameworks, principles, reusable patterns, design-system integration and education formats to stay current with market trends and emerging practices in AI and digital experience design.
  • coach and support other designers by role-modelling structured thinking, design quality, collaboration, ownership and stakeholder management.

The team Marketing, Design & Digital Sales is constantly looking for new talents to shape the future of UBS. You’ll be working in the Center of Excellence (CoE) for Experience Design, based in Zurich. In our agile organization, you will collaborate with design, product, channel, marketing, communications, technology and client need teams. You will support the organizational transformation toward a client-centric design organization and help shape client and employee experiences across digital banking, AI-enabled services and emerging interaction models. You will work in a hybrid model, combining time at UBS offices with working from home.

Your skills and experience

  • 7+ years of experience in Service Design, UX Strategy, UX Design, UX Research, Human-Computer Interaction or a related field, with proven delivery ownership and measurable impact.
  • strong experience creating end-to-end journey maps, service blueprints and target-state experience concepts that address user needs and create long-term business value.
  • excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with experience collaborating across cross-functional teams such as Product, Marketing, Sales, Customer Service, Technology and IT.
  • strong facilitation skills, with experience applying methods such as Design Sprints, Lean UX Canvas, empathy mapping, journey mapping and service blueprinting to drive alignment.
  • good understanding of AI-enabled experiences, including conversational interfaces, generative AI interaction patterns, human oversight, trust, transparency and responsible AI.
  • ability to thrive in dynamic, fast-paced and ambiguous environments, effectively planning and delivering design work at different levels of detail while maintaining high-quality outcomes.
  • a proactive, self-driven professional with leadership potential, collaborative mindset and the ability to mentor others, shape design direction and work in global, distributed teams.
  • fluent in English and German, both written and spoken. Additional Swiss national languages, especially French or Italian, are an advantage.
  • degree from a University or University of Applied Sciences in UX Design, Service Design, Human-Computer Interaction, Business Administration, Marketing, Psychology, Communication Design or related field. Relevant professional experience may be considered in lieu of a formal degree.

You’re curious to explore how AI can improve how we build, deliver and optimize workflows. You do this with sound judgment – validating outputs and aligning with policies, risk standard and ethical use.
