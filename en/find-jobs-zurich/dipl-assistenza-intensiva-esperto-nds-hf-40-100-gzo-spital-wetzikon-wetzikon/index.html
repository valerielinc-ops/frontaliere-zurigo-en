Full description

Small IPS, big variety: With us you keep an overview of every case – and still have up to 24 days off a year. In our interdisciplinary intensive care unit with 7 fully monitored beds, we look after patients from all clinics (internal medicine, general surgery, gynecology). We have 5 invasive and 2 non-invasive ventilation sites and offer continuous hemodialysis. Our intensive care unit is recognized by the Swiss Society for Intensive Care Medicine (SGI).

Tasks

  • Comprehensive care and care for critically ill patients based on evidence-based care
  • Monitor patients by telemetry
  • Involving relatives in the care process and supporting and accompanying them in crisis situations
  • Students accompany NDS IPS in day-to-day operations
  • Requirements involved in internal and external projects
  • Diploma Expert/Expert Intensive Care NDS HF or equivalent foreign training (SRC-recognised or recognised in progress) – or the willingness to complete this training
