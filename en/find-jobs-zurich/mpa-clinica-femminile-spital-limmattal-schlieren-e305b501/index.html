As an important focus hospital with a comprehensive training mandate, the Limmattal Hospital takes over the extended basic medical care of more than 96,000 patients every year. It also offers top medicine and in-depth expertise in numerous surgical and medical disciplines. The hospital has 188 beds in the acute care sector, 126 beds in long-term care as well as eight operating rooms and eight intensive care facilities. More than 1,577 employees from 57 nations provide high-quality and diverse services every day. To increase our team, we are looking for a motivated, team-capable and resilient MPA.

Working day at 60% is Wednesday to Friday and at 80% Monday, Wednesday to Friday.

Direct applications are preferred, external recruitment offices are not treated as a priority.

Here they engage

  • in the office organization, patient reception, appointment, triage and processing of all KISIM mailboxes
  • in telephone contact with internal and external bodies
  • in administrative tasks & organizational activities
  • in the laboratory, blood sampling & performing CTG
  • as an assistant in urogynecological investigations and minor diagnostic and therapeutic interventions
  • in the provision of consumables and maintenance of equipment
  • have completed vocational training as a Med. Praxisassistent/in or Dipl. Care professional HF/FH
  • have professional experience in the field of Women's Clinic
  • Flexibility with changes
  • are a team capable, forwarded open and warm personality
  • like to work independently and maintain a good interdisciplinary cooperation with Ärzten and the entire team
  • KISIM experience is beneficial
  • Reduced employee parking
  • Motorcycle parking, lockable bicycle parking
  • With the ZVV Bonus Pass / Z-Bonus Pass we offer an attractive ÖV subscription for employees
