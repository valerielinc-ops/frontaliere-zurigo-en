Stv. Manager/-in gastronomy 80% - 100% | Zurich Do you have questions? Krisztina is at your disposal. Krisztina Révész Head of Gastronomy bewerbung@susenbergklinik.ch 044 268 38 38

Your responsibility

Operational: You coordinate the daily processes in the catering/hotel industry, lead the team, ensure a smooth service and actively participate in the day-to-day business, lunch and evening service. Patients & Service: You take responsibility for the care of guests, ensure the quality of service and contribute to a first-class gastronomy experience. Operational: You ensure cross-divisional cooperation and ensure efficient operations, administrative processes and optimal goods management. Klinik Susenberg is a specialist clinic for acute geriatrics, geriatric and specialized internistic oncological rehabilitation and palliative care at Zurichberg. In order for our patients to be the focus, an organization that works is needed in the background.

This is what you bring with you

Foundation: Completed training as a hotel specialist EFZ, several years of experience in gastronomy, management experience or the willingness to lead a small team, very good knowledge of German. Skills: Self-employed and service-oriented working method, pleasure in contact with people, high social competence, empathetic personality, pronounced service orientation.

We offer

Responsible and varied activity with room for manoeuvre and visible effect. An appreciative, lively environment with short decision paths. Attractive benefits: health promotion and staff catering.
