Full description

We offer you the opportunity to take on a leadership position in a dynamic and highly specialized environment of thoracic surgery and pneumology. On our university bedside ward, you look after patients with complex thoracic surgical and pneumological clinical pictures and actively shape the further development of an evidence-based nursing practice. Participation, interdisciplinary cooperation and a constructive team culture are very important to us.

An exciting workplace awaits you in a committed and open-minded team. As a group leader, you are a member of the shared governance team and are carefully introduced and accompanied into your new area of responsibility by a professionally experienced department leader. The targeted promotion and further development of your leadership skills is a matter of course for us.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • You care for patients with complex thoracic surgical and pulmonary disease patterns (e.g. after pulmonary resections, respiratory insufficiency or oncological therapies) and ensure evidence-based, safe care throughout the entire treatment process • As part of the day-to-day responsibility, you coordinate the processes on the bed station, prioritize resources and act as the central contact person for interdisciplinary professional groups along the patient path. • You support the departmental management in professional and organizational management tasks, assume joint responsibility for personnel planning, support for new employees and the promotion of team development • You are actively involved in ensuring and developing the quality of care, implementing quality standards and getting involved in shared governance bodies or specialist groups • You participate in projects and change processes in the highly specialized environment of thoracic surgery and pneumology and contribute your specialist nursing expertise in innovation and optimization projects.
