Employee: in Research Ethics (m/f/d) 40%-60%, Zurich, temporary Printed Printing The Ethics Committee of ETH Zurich examines and approves in particular research projects with persons and personal data that do not fall within the competence of a cantonal ethics committee. It thus contributes to the promotion of ethically acceptable research and to the strengthening of the ethical sense of responsibility of researchers. The Secretariat of the Human Research and Research Ethics Centre shall assist the Commission in the performance of its task from a technical and administrative point of view. For a temporary representation in the office we are looking for 1. January 2027 an initiative and reliable personality. The term of office shall run until the end of December 2027. Job description After training, you will increasingly perform the following tasks independently:

  • the examination of ethics requests for their formal and substantive completeness;
  • preparing decisions on the basis of the assessments received;
  • correspondence with the applicants;
  • advising researchers, in particular on the competence of the Commission, the assessment procedure and the implementation of conditions in order to remove reservations
  • Support of the Executive Board and exchange with other internal ETH offices
  • the publication of information relevant to the researchers on the web Profile

  • knowledge of research ethics, applied ethics or human research
