Full description

Tasks

  • Conception and implementation of Microsoft technologies (Azure, M365, Windows Server, Hyper-V, Security, Network, Backup, etc.)
  • Operation, development and stabilization of our existing on-premises infrastructure (Windows Server, VMware, Storage, Network, Security, Backup)
  • Responsibility for the architecture and implementation of complex IT infrastructure projects
  • Developing and implementing Azure resources: helping to build and operate an Azure operating platform
  • Ensure that all projects are of high quality, on time and on budget
  • Continuous optimization of our IT processes, architectures and automations
  • Conducting step-by-step presentations and technical workshops
  • Collaboration: Close cooperation with development, DevOps, SecOps, application management and integration of applications and services

Requirements

  • You have completed a computer science education or equivalent professional experience (at least 5 years in cloud engineering or system engineering).
  • Ideally, you have completed a higher education in computer science or have relevant certifications.
  • You bring in-depth technical know-how in Microsoft and cloud technologies – especially in M365, Azure, Windows Server, network technologies and security.
  • You have experience with hybrid IT environments (e.g. VMware, Hyper-V, AD / Azure AD, Hybrid Exchange, VPN, firewalls) work independently, think entrepreneurially and like to take responsibility.
  • You are passionate about innovation, automation and digital transformation.
  • You are sure to communicate in German and English, both verbally and in writing.

We offer

  • Flexible working hours/models
  • 40 hourly week
  • Possibility for home office
  • Attractive social benefits and numerous benefits
  • Team spirit
  • Work-Life Balance / Family Friendliness

The Schulthess Clinic in Zurich is one of the leading orthopaedic clinics in Europe with over 1,100 employees. Our central concern is to free people from their pain and restore their mobility. As a specialist clinic, we focus on surgical orthopedics, neurology, rheumatology and sports medicine. Schulthess Clinic is Swiss Olympic Medical Center.
