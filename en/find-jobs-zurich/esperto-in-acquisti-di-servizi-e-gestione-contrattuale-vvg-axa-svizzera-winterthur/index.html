AXA Healthcare was founded in the summer of 2017 and now has more than 200,000 customers. The provision of health care includes supplementary health insurance as well as innovative services such as billing and billing services. With these services that are unique throughout Switzerland, our customers not only save money, we also take over the administrative effort for them. Saving time or money? At AXA you can do both!

For our up-and-coming business field, we are looking for a:n specialist for our specialist management stationary (VVG), who on the one hand knows how to focus on customer needs, but on the other hand does not lose sight of the business consequences. For this exciting and versatile function, we are looking for a dynamic, resilient and motivated personality who shows initiative and likes to take responsibility.

If you feel comfortable in an environment with a large decision-making and design scope, apply to us! We look forward to seeing you!

AXA is the No. 1 pension partner in Switzerland. With our diverse and advanced products in the areas of health as well as private and occupational care, we create freedom and security for our customers so that they can shape their lives carefree.

This is what awaits you

  • Actively participate in service purchasing: You represent the interests of AXA Health in stationary service purchasing and actively participate in relevant specialist and negotiating committees.
  • Contribute your expertise: You accompany contract and negotiation topics from a professional point of view, analyze existing agreements, create analyses and derive concrete recommendations for action.
  • Ensure quality and compliance: You assess contractual and regulatory issues, in particular in connection with FINMA requirements, and make a significant contribution to ensuring compliant stationary insurance services.
  • Shape the future: You will participate in the technical and strategic development of the stationary service area and bring your insights from service purchasing to our organization.
  • Network: You work closely with internal departments, external partners and relevant stakeholders and ensure an active knowledge transfer between service purchasing and claims.
  • Think outside the box: You take on responsibility in cross-divisional projects, develop new solutions and have the opportunity to continuously develop your area of responsibility.
  • Set standards: You optimize technical guidelines and processes, support the implementation of new contractual requirements and strengthen the quality of our service delivery through your expertise.

You bring that with you

  • Your foundation: You have a commercial or medical basic education with relevant further education in the health insurance sector and have completed a technical or university degree.
  • Your expertise: You know the Swiss healthcare system and the health insurance landscape, especially the inpatient service area. You bring in-depth experience in service purchasing, collective bargaining or contracting.
  • Your analytical view: You understand complex contractual and economic contexts, enjoy working with data and can create well-founded analyses and recommendations for action.
  • Your regulatory understanding: You are interested in regulatory issues and ideally have knowledge of FINMA requirements in the field of supplementary health insurance.
  • Your mindset: You think networked, act independently and understand how to constructively combine different interests. You convince through a structured, solution-oriented working method.
  • Your communication strength: You communicate stylishly in German, represent professional points of view convincingly and move safely in exchange with different stakeholders. A second national language is beneficial.
  • Your personality: You are committed, curious and creative. You want to take responsibility, constantly expand your knowledge and promote new topics together with us.
