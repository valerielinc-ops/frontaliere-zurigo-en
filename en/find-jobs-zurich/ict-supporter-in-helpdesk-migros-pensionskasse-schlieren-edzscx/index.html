Full description

As an ICT Supporter & Helpdesk, you are the first point of contact for technical questions and ensure that our systems function smoothly. With your know-how and your service orientation, you support our employees efficiently and solution-oriented in everyday work.

Mansioni

  • Receiving, analysing and correcting malfunctions/technical problems
  • Supporting and supporting users in the IT sector
  • Installation and maintenance of clients (hardware and software components)
  • Managing Active Directory
  • Evaluations & procurement of suitable systems/products and introduction (hardware and software components)
  • Informatics inventory management

Requirements

  • Several years of experience: in computer science (ideally IT support)
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): computer scientist
  • Good knowledge of current information technologies and the Windows environment
  • Experience in planning and evaluating hardware and software (client/server environment)
  • Service- and solution-oriented personality
  • Networked thinking
  • Good manners
  • Communication and negotiating skills
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • French (basic knowledge)
