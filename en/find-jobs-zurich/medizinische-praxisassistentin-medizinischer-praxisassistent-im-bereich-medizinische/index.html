Full description

You love working in a lively environment, want to be part of a great team and value regular working hours during the week? Then you are right with us! At the medical polyclinic, we treat outpatient patients together, carry out personnel medical checks and offer a consultation for travel medicine.

Tasks

  • As a hub of the clinic, you are responsible for smooth processes and the entire organization of office hours (patient reception, appointment planning, telephone service, mail management)
  • You carry out diagnostic and therapeutic measures from blood sampling to infusions, vaccinations and ECG, 24h BD measurements to assistance with punctures
  • You are a responsible contact person for internal personnel issues. In addition, you are responsible for organizing trustworthy assessments of students of external training institutions
  • We maintain an electronic agenda and medical history (KISIM). General administrative activities are part of your everyday life

Profile

  • You are motivated and capable of teamwork
  • You have completed MPA vocational training with several years of professional experience
  • You have a quick understanding.
  • You are reliable and distinguished by your commitment, flexibility and openness
  • Working on the PC is an ease for you
  • You are a team player with a strong patient orientation and want to actively shape changes
