Full description

You have completed your studies in business administration or law and would like to use your knowledge in practice? Get to know the Cantonal Tax Office at the Talendo Messe in Zurich and get started as a tax commissioner in the private sector. Here, figures, law and people meet: you analyze tax returns, clarify facts and develop a sense of taxation step by step. You don’t have to be a tax expert or a tax expert yet. We train you on the job at full wage and accompany you with an experienced team from day one. Curiosity and joy in understanding complex topics are good prerequisites. The start takes place by agreement and coordinated with our internal training start. Your area of responsibility:

  • Audit and assessment of tax returns of self-employed and self-employed persons
  • Answer tax questions in writing, by telephone or in personal conversation
  • Professional contact person for municipal tax offices
  • Examination of objections and further clarification of open issues
  • Participation in review proceedings
  • Accompaniment of appeal proceedings and representation of the state in corresponding proceedings Your profile:
  • A completed bachelor's degree, ideally in business administration or law, or a higher specialist training in finance and accounting, trust or as a business economist / in HF
  • Interest in tax issues and initial insights into the financial or tax area of advantage
  • Interested in understanding complex issues and making decisions independently
  • A structured, independent working method and joy in taking on responsibility
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing (C2) Your contact: For technical questions, Philipp is at your disposal. Questions about the recruitment process please Julia.
