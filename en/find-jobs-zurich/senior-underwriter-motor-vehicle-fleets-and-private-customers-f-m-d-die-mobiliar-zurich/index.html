Full description

This is what awaits you

As an underwriter of motor vehicle fleets and private customers, you are part of the Management Brokerage Business in Zurich-Oerlikon. Together with the team Market Development Mobility, you assess challenging risks, develop sustainable insurance solutions and actively shape joint success through well-founded underwriting decisions and a partnership with our brokers.

This is what you do with us

In this role, you will independently process inquiries from the brokerage business and make risk-appropriate underwriting and underwriting decisions taking into account risk, profitability and market potential.

You take on the professional responsibility for concluding and providing ongoing support for demanding motor vehicle fleet and private customer transactions. You create offers, restructuring proposals and policies, ensure correct contract management over the entire life cycle and control your portfolio in a targeted manner.

In addition, you coordinate cooperation with brokers and internal specialist departments and build sustainable business relationships with your professional, solution-oriented communication. With your high level of service understanding, you ensure that our partners feel competently accompanied and supported at all times.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed basic training in the insurance company with further training as an insurance specialist with a federal certificate or equivalent qualification
  • Several years of experience in the underwriting of motor vehicle fleet insurance or Retail banking
  • Analytical thinking skills and a strong understanding of economic relationships and profitability
  • Self-employed, structured and responsible working practices
  • Strong communication and negotiating skills as well as enjoyment of working with internal and external partners
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing, other national languages are advantageous

Working with us

In the team Market Development Mobility you can expect a collaborative, solution-oriented and communicative working atmosphere in which cooperation and mutual support are lived. You benefit from modern employment conditions, flexible working time models and the opportunity to develop professionally and personally. You can find out more about our commitment as an employer below.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
