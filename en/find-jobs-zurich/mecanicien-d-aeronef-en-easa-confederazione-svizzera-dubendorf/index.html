Original description

  • Carry out maintenance, testing, maintenance, modifications and fault analysis on mechanical and electromechanical aircraft systems (Line Maintenance +) using English language technical documentation
  • carry out control and servicing work on the aircraft before, between and after the missions (such as towing, refuelling, loading and unloading of cargo and baggage, as well as handing over and taking over the aircraft to and from the crew)
  • Support missions at home and abroad as an accompanying mechanic, from demonstrations with the PC-7 team to SWISSTOPO and LTDB flights (Federal Air Transport Service) to training missions
  • Assume active missions in the Flight Accident Pikett and systematically develop the targeted further development into a function as Flight Accident Pikett Operations Manager
  • Ensure knowledge transfer in technical courses as a technical instructor and assume administrative tasks in the operational competence position
  • Aircraft mechanic/EFZ or similar 4-year basic technical education
  • EASA Part-66 Aircraft Maintenance Licence (category B1) requested
  • Precise, responsible, flexible and team-oriented working methods
  • Enjoyment and motivation in various tasks around aviation
  • Category B (C/E beneficial), good IT skills (MS Office/SAP)
  • Very good knowledge of one or more official languages, knowledge of English (A2)
  • Military basic training beneficial
