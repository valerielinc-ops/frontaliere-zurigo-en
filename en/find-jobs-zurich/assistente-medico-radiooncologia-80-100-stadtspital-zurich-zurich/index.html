Full description

For the clinic Radio-Oncology at the Triemli site, we are looking for a motivated and committed personality immediately or by agreement.

Tasks

  • In this versatile function, you support our team in the treatment and care of our radio-oncological patients.
  • They are happy to support high-quality medical services.
  • With the other internal specialist departments, you maintain a close interdisciplinary exchange.
  • Gathering with great personal commitment You will gain valuable experience on your way to becoming a specialist in Radio-Oncology/Radiotherapy.

Profile

  • Completed non-specialised training and ideally one year of clinical experience in radio-oncology
  • High independence, resilience and pronounced patient and team orientation as well as social skills
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing, foreign languages advantageous

We offer

We offer you more than just a job! We live an open du-culture, which is characterized by an uncomplicated and personal cooperation at eye level, respect and appreciation. As part of our diverse team of over 120 professional groups, you will actively shape a meaningful environment. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions and excellent social benefits from the city of Zurich.

About us

With over 34,000 inpatients and around 436,000 outpatients per year, the Stadtspital Zürich ranks among the top 10 Swiss hospitals. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,400 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
