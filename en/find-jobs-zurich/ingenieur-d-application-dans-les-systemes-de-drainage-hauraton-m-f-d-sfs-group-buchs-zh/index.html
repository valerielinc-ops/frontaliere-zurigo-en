Full description

Drainage solutions have to work on the construction site, which is exactly where your role at SFS with the HAURATON brand comes in. You advise planners/architects, customers and sales teams on technical issues, develop suitable concepts for specific construction projects and accompany projects from tendering to successful implementation. You will contribute your technical know-how at an early stage, identify requirements and ensure that practical solutions emerge from complex challenges.

Tasks

  • Develop technical solutions for drainage projects together with customers and planners
  • Provide technical advice and support to planners, customers and sales teams
  • Manage construction projects from tender to implementation
  • Create hydraulic calculations and object-specific installation plans
  • Recognize new property potential and expand planner networks
  • Act as a technical interface between the market, SFS and HAURATON

Requirements

  • Training in the construction industry with further technical training, e.g. B. Construction foreman, construction manager or technical specialization
  • Practical or project experience in the main construction or ancillary construction industry
  • Experience in technical consulting, project management or field service
  • Ability to communicate technical requirements in an understandable and customer-oriented manner
  • Initiative, organizational talent and enjoyment of responsibility
  • Strong communicator, committed and solution-oriented
  • Business fluent spoken and written German; other languages are welcome

We offer

  • Six weeks holiday & additional holiday purchase possible
  • Annual profit sharing
  • Own healthy pension fund with individual savings plans
  • Internal training courses & support for external training courses
  • Room for personal responsibility and initiative
  • Further benefits: https://join.sfs.com/ch/de/benefits/
