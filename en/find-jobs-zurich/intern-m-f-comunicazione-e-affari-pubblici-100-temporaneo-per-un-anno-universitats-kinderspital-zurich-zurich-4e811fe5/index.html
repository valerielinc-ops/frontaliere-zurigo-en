Your tasks

  • In the day-to-day business, you will help shape topics from everyday hospital life and thus get to know the entire spectrum of corporate communication. You will be accompanied and coached by the team and can benefit from our experience and learn in practice.
  • You take responsibility for your own topics from hospital, research or support areas at an early stage and create concepts, measures and content that you implement for the relevant external and internal channels. You engage independently, bring your creative ideas and implement them.
  • As a focus, you write and edit high-quality texts from everyday hospital life, which you publish on internal and external channels. You bring your joy to journalistic storytelling and tell relevant, target-group-oriented and emotional stories.
  • You create videos and content for social media and support community management.
  • You process media inquiries in close cooperation with our media managers or accompanying media professionals on shooting dates.
  • In addition, you work in strategic projects or produce content for websites or internal channels.
  • You actively work with the team as well as with experts from the hospital, foundation and management. Your profile
  • With a completed bachelor’s degree in communication and journalism or a comparable education, you bring the right foundation.
  • As a career starter, you want to tackle corporate communication and the corporate newsroom, take responsibility and learn.
  • Editorial thinking is yours, and you like to write journalistic texts. In doing so, you succeed in bringing complex issues to the point in an understandable and target group-oriented way.
  • Excellent writing and storytelling craft in German is one of your strengths.
  • A quick comprehension characterizes you and even under load you deliver high-quality work.
