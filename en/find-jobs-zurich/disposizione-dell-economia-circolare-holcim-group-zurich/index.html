Full description

You also keep track of many inquiries, like to organize and enjoy the exchange with customers? Then become part of our team and shape the circular economy of tomorrow with us.

What to expect

  • Organisation and management of material input and output as well as coordination of daily operations
  • Contact person: for internal and external customers in the field of circular economy
  • Planning and coordination of material quantities and processing capacities including troubleshooting
  • Processing of customer requests and performing administrative tasks in various operating systems (e.g. Google, CRM Tool and Sales Force, Logon and SAP)
  • Support for monthly accounts, audits and evaluations
  • Close cooperation with internal interfaces and assumption of further organizational tasks

What you bring

  • Completed commercial vocational training or a comparable qualification, additional business training and sales experience are advantageous
  • Experience in scheduling, circular economy or a comparable industry is beneficial
  • Secure handling of MS Office or Google Workspace and SAP
  • Feel for customer relationships and solution orientation
  • Dynamic and strong personality with self-confidence and assertiveness
  • Structured, independent and solution-oriented working method as well as a good numerical ratio
  • Communication strength, team spirit and friends at customer contact
  • Very good German skills as well as good English & French skills
  • Italian is an advantage

Our offer

  • Onboarding & getting started: Look forward to a team-oriented, open and future-oriented working environment. We accompany you from the beginning with a careful training, practical and structured, so that you can start successfully.
  • Career & Development: We create opportunities. With us, you are at the center as a person with your individual strengths and potential. Anyone who wants to is supported by us and can achieve a lot.
  • Appreciation & Team Culture: With us, you are part of a strong and inclusive team that holds together, shares ideas and masters challenges together. A trusting leadership culture ensures that your commitment is seen and appreciated.
  • Flexibility & Work-Life Balance: We offer flexible working hours, 41 hours, part-time opportunities, 25-30 days holidays, holiday purchases, extended maternity and paternity leave
  • Attractive compensation: We offer our employees an attractive compensation package with many additional benefits and a very good pension fund
  • Employee benefits: From Reka checks to shares at a preferential price to the Swibeco advantage world - we make sure that you feel comfortable and benefit from attractive additional services
