Tasks

  • Active support and development of a defined corporate portfolio in the field of card and T&E solutions
  • Planning and carrying out customer visits, consulting and reviews (on-/off-site and remote)
  • Analysis of customer needs, expenses and travel processes as well as map usage data for identifying potential
  • Advice on company cards, T&E and additional solutions (e.g. virtual cards, central billing, reporting)
  • Use CRM, reporting and analytics tools to identify and target sales opportunities
  • Coordination of implementations, program setups, limit adjustments and service cases with internal bodies
  • Actively promoting the use of digital card and T&E tools (portals, apps, self-service)
  • Ensure compliance with KYC, risk, compliance and card-specific guidelines

Requirements

  • 3–5 years of relevant experience in card, T&E or corporate banking distribution with demonstrable sales and consulting experience
  • prefers professional experience in banking or banking environment

  • a well-founded understanding of business card, T&E and billing solutions or the willingness to work quickly and in depth
  • strong analytical skills and safe handling of key figures (volume, usage, yield, travel data)
  • structured, self-responsible and solution-oriented working methods with high quality standards
  • high integrity, risk awareness and compliance orientation
  • high load capacity and readiness to perform with a good process understanding
  • very good communication and presentation skills in German (C2) and English (at least C1); other languages are of advantage
  • high A-resinity to digital tools (MS Office, especially PowerPoint and Excel; ideally CRM and specialized systems)
