Full description

Key-Ten

  • active management and further development of a defined corporate customer portfolio in the area of card and T&E solutions
  • Planning and conducting customer visits, consultations and reviews (on-/off-site and remote)
  • Analysis of customer needs, expense and travel processes, as well as card usage data to identify opportunities
  • Advising on corporate card, T&E and additional solutions (e.g. virtual cards, centralized billing, reporting)
  • Use of CRM, reporting and analytics tools to identify and proactively pursue sales opportunities
  • Coordination of implementations, program setups, limit adjustments and service cases with internal departments
  • actively promoting the use of digital card and T&E tools (portals, apps, self-service)
  • Ensuring compliance with KYC, risk, compliance and card-specific guidelines
  • Documenting customer interactions, opportunities and deals in the relevant systems
  • Contributing market and customer feedback to the further development of our card and T&E offering

The team

To strengthen our team in the Sales Commercial Cards & Travel & Expense (T&E) division, we are looking for a sales-oriented, pragmatic and customer-focused professional with sound experience in banking or the banking environment. In this role, you will manage a defined corporate customer portfolio in the Zurich area, further develop card and T&E programs, and make a direct contribution to growth, efficiency and customer satisfaction.

If you enjoy B2B sales, data-driven consulting and modern card/T&E solutions, you have come to the right place.

Your Trade and Travel

  • 3–5 years of relevant experience in card, T&E or corporate banking sales, with proven sales and consulting experience
  • Professional experience in banking or the banking environment preferred
  • Strong sales and consulting skills in a B2B environment, as well as an enthusiasm for developing long-term customer relationships
  • Sound understanding of corporate card, T&E and billing solutions, or the willingness to quickly develop an in-depth understanding of them
  • Strong analytical skills and confidence working with key figures (volume, usage, revenue, travel expense data)
  • Structured, self-directed and solution-oriented approach to work with high standards of quality
  • high integrity, risk awareness and a strong focus on compliance
  • high resilience and a strong willingness to perform, with a good understanding of processes
  • very good communication and presentation skills in German (C2) and English (at least C1); additional languages are an advantage
  • strong affinity for digital tools (MS Office, especially PowerPoint and Excel; ideally CRM and specialist systems)

About me

The UBS Card Center AG, a subsidiary of UBS Switzerland AG, is Switzerland's leading credit card processor. Well over 3 million Visa and Mastercard credit cards are reliably processed through our systems. With more than 750 dedicated employees at our locations in Glattbrugg and Manno, we combine many years of expertise with innovative strength and work every day to make the payment traffic of tomorrow simpler, safer and smarter.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
