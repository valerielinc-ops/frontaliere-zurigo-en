Full description

For a growing trust company, we are looking for a committed personality as a clerk at the Zurich Oerlikon site. In a modern environment with digital processes and up-to-date tools like Bexio, you can support exciting SME customers from a wide range of industries. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for you!

This is your job

  • Finance: self-employed management of financial and ancillary accounts for SMEs and participation in interim and annual accounts.
  • Taxes & VAT: Professional preparation of VAT bills and tax returns for natural and legal persons.
  • Payroll & Admin: Management of payroll accounts, assumption of personnel administration and completion of general trust and administration work.
  • Interface: Support of mandate management and direct contact with customers, authorities and insurance companies.

This is what you bring with you

  • Training & experience: Completed basic commercial training (KV) as well as several years of experience in the escrow or accounting sector (further training in escrowing beneficial or in sight).
  • IT & language skills: Very good knowledge of German, good MS Office skills as well as experience with Bexio or similar accounting systems are advantageous.
  • Working method: An independent, exact and reliable working method with pleasure in responsibility and direct customer contact.
  • Mindset: A customer-oriented, professional appearance as well as a team-capable style.

This is what awaits you from the customer

  • Workplace: A modern, digital working environment and a centrally located workplace in Zurich Oerlikon.
  • Flexibility: Flexible working hours and flexible breaks between 60% and 100% for an optimal work-life balance.
  • Culture: A young, motivated team with flat hierarchies, short decision-making paths and direct cooperation with the management.
  • Development: Exciting development opportunities in the trust area as well as active support in your further education.
