Global OPS Continuous Improvement Manager (gn)

We are looking for a Global OPS Continuous Improvement Manager that can be based out of any of the following locations: Raunheim, Wohlen, Bergish Gladbach, Barchfeld, Bingen Brugherio, Liechtenstein, or San Polo di Piave. Global Operations continuously strives to improve profitability, resilience, performance, and organizational effectiveness across the end-to-end value chain. In this role, you are responsible not only for driving and governing savings initiatives across Oerlikon, but also for leading strategic cross-functional transformation programs across Operations, Procurement, Supply Chain, and related business functions.

You ensure transparency, governance, and execution of savings and performance improvement initiatives, while also supporting broader operational transformation topics such as target operating model design, supply chain optimization, risk mitigation, sourcing footprint development, and crisis readiness. The role requires strong collaboration with senior leadership, business functions, regions, and external stakeholders to translate strategic priorities into concrete initiatives, governance mechanisms, and measurable business impact.

Your Responsibilities

  • Lead cost-out and operational improvement projects such as process optimization, footprint initiatives, make-or-buy, BCC, value engineering
  • Support strategic transformation initiatives, including target operating model design, governance clarification, and cross-functional alignment
  • Coordinate supply chain, sourcing, and risk mitigation topics affecting operational performance and business continuity
  • Drive governance, tracking, and reporting of savings initiatives across Oerlikon
  • Collaborate with Finance and business leaders to define savings targets, monitor progress, and escalate gaps where needed
  • Drive crisis readiness and resilience initiatives, including backup planning and simulation activities
  • Prepare executive-ready analyses, updates, and decision proposals for senior management
  • Successful execution of cost-out and transformation programs
  • Improved cross-functional alignment, resilience, and decision-making support for management
  • Transparent governance and reporting of savings and performance initiatives

Your Profile

  • Bachelor’s degree in engineering with a Master’s degree preferred
  • 10+ years of progressive professional experience in Mechanical Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, Continuous Improvement Management, Electrical Engineering, Services Business or similar fields
  • Experience in managing “within the matrix” of an international and intercultural company
  • Track record in transformation environments
  • Proven track record in managing complex projects / programs and demonstrated target achievement and efficiency in multinational industrial companies
  • Experience in mechanical or electrical engineering or Service business

About Oerlikon

Oerlikon is a global leader in surface technologies and advanced materials. With a unique portfolio spanning surface engineering, high-performance materials, coating equipment and components, we make products better by enhancing performance, efficiency and sustainability. Oerlikon serves a wide range of industries, including aerospace, automotive, energy, medical, luxury, semiconductors and tooling. Head-quartered in Pfäffikon, Switzerland, Oerlikon operates in 38 countries with around 10,000 employees, achieving sales of CHF 1.6 billion in 2025.
