Full description

As a partner of the "Züri Velo" bicycle rental system of PubliBike AG, we are looking for temporary support for a year for the network maintenance team as of 01.02.2027 or by appointment.

Tasks

  • They plan tours of battery and compensation logistics independently and drive them forward.
  • They transport batteries with the cargo bike, Kyburz (Kat.M) or the transporter (Kat.B, Automaik/Electronic).
  • They clean stations if necessary and carry out minor repairs of Züri Velos and e-bikes.
  • They are responsible for compliance with the standards for quality, infrastructure and material as well as for occupational safety.
  • They work both during the week, on evenings and on weekends. Working hours under the week three shifts: 8-17 / 13-22 and Saturdays 8-17.

Profile

  • EBA in craft or similar training
  • Time flexibility (including evening shifts and Saturday/Sunday work)
  • Good physical condition
  • Ready to work outside in any weather
  • Driving pass Cat. B mandatory (automatic/electronic)

We offer

  • Temporary employment for 1 year with a view to extension of up to 24 months
  • A varied and challenging activity
  • Attractive employment conditions

About us

With around 2400 employees, the social department of the city of Zurich is committed to a social Zurich every day. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the quarters.

The Work Integration Division of the Social Services Department (SEB) comprises a number of work integration services for adults and young people. The aim of the programmes is the professional and social integration of the participants.
