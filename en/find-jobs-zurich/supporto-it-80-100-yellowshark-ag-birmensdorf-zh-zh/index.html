Future employer

Our client is looking for a service-oriented person to strengthen IT support. In this role, the new employee will serve as an important point of contact for employees in their day-to-day digital work. The company offers a varied range of responsibilities in a collegial team with opportunities for development.

02

Your responsibilities

  • Receiving, classifying and resolving incident reports in the hardware and software areas
  • Supporting users by telephone, via remote maintenance and in person on site
  • Operation and maintenance of the client infrastructure, as well as participation in monitoring
  • Creating and maintaining documentation and user guides
  • Assisting with hardware procurement and inventory management, as well as migration and rollout projects

03

The Commission

  • Support for external training
  • Career and advancement opportunities
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
  • Modern workplace
  • Regular team-building activities and company events
  • Structured and supported onboarding
  • Responsible duties

04

Your profile

  • Completed IT training, for example MCSA, MCSE or a Swiss Federal Diploma of Higher Education in Computer Science
  • Previous experience in 1st-level support is an advantage
  • Knowledge of Windows Server 2012 to 2025 as well as Windows 10/11
  • Basic knowledge of the Microsoft environment and virtualized system environments is an advantage
  • German as a native language, French language skills an advantage, Category B driving licence
