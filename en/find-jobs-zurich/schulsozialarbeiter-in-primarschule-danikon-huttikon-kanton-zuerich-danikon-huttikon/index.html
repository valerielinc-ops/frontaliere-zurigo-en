Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Vocational Guidance School Social Worker/Primary School Dänikon-Hüttikon 50% As part of the cantonal offer in school social work, we offer and lead school social work (SSA) on behalf of schools. By immediate agreement or agreement we are looking for a school social worker/in 50% for the primary school Dänikon-Hüttikon. The primary school Dänikon-Hüttikon is a school with 245 pupils.

The school is characterized by ADL-led classes, young dedicated teachers and a functioning stable day structure. Prevention and participation should be an integral part of the school's culture. The infrastructure is modern. There is a good public transport connection to Zurich, parking spaces available.
