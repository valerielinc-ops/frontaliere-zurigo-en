# System Engineer

Il tuo futuro inizia

You ensure the reliable operation of our Swiss emergency communication control system solution and, together with a motivated team, ensure that our AVANTI software solutions work smoothly around the clock. You work closely with development and project management, implement new ideas and requirements and move in a varied, technical environment with a lot of room for manoeuvre. Depending on the situation, your assignment takes place remotely or directly with our customers on site.

  • You work with modern, modular and technically demanding system landscapes around web technologies, interfaces and infrastructure.
  • You will analyze existing architectures, optimize performance and security and develop sustainable solutions together with the team.
  • You implement infrastructure solutions independently – from conception to installation and configuration.
  • You install, operate and optimize our software solutions and continuously develop your technical know-how regarding system integration and modern IT systems.
  • With growing experience, you can take on additional internal responsibility and develop further.

Your skills

  • Studies in Computer Science (ETH/Uni/FH) or alternatively Computer Science EFZ with several years of professional experience
  • Enjoy analysing complex relationships and developing sustainable solutions
  • Good knowledge of web technologies and software development as well as a solid understanding of Windows client/server architectures and group policies
  • Enjoying technical challenges and complex tasks
  • Strong service orientation and experience in direct contact with customers
  • Very good German (understanding of Swiss German); French and English are beneficial
  • Residence in Switzerland

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Benefits.
