Full description

Put to the Point

Are you looking for ICT challenges with real meaning? In Cyber Command, you will continue to supervise the meteo and command information systems of the Swiss Army – in a highly secure, time-critical environment with a direct impact on operational capabilities.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20248
  • Place of work: Military airfield, 8600 Dübendorf

You can make this contribution

  • Daily responsibility for system monitoring, initial analysis and coordination of troubleshooting of critical infrastructure components
  • Ensure stable, secure and sustainable operation of existing meteorological and leadership information systems and specify and configure infrastructure components
  • Continuously optimize the operation through technical expertise and preventive measures
  • Advise internal customers on the analysis and solution of technical problems
  • Representing operational interests throughout the project lifecycle
  • Readiness for picket service every 5 weeks (one week each)

This makes you unique

  • Completed specialist training (HF/FH or equivalent); academic degrees welcome to Master
  • Several years of practical experience in system operation, ideally with a focus on infrastructure and monitoring
  • Solid knowledge in troubleshooting, escalation management and support of information systems
  • Working independently, analytical thinking skills, communication strength in customer contact
  • English language skills are beneficial for technical documentation

We offer this

  • We are committed to the success model Switzerland and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine the enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, the latest technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Army and our partners in the Swiss Security Association. The Kdo Cy thus gives the Swiss army the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a safe Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.

Additional information

Home office is only possible to a limited extent. In case of piquet, you must be able to reach the location within 60 minutes (even at night and on weekends) Employment with the Swiss Army offers meaning, stability and numerous advantages. Further information can be found under armee.ch/advantages.

Women are still underrepresented in our administrative unit. Your applications are therefore particularly welcome.
