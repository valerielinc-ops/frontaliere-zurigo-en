Head: in Infrastructure Building Management (m/f/d)

100%, Zurich, perpetual

In the Facility Services department of ETH Zurich, we create the optimal conditions for teaching, research and technology transfer. We maintain the buildings and ensure the energy and media supply as well as the technical and infrastructural management of all rooms used by ETH Zurich.

Do you enjoy leading a team, developing processes and ensuring that a building operation functions smoothly, efficiently and customer-oriented? Do you like to work independently and keep an overview even in complex situations? Then we invite you to continue reading – because here you can expect a position with a lot of creative leeway and real effect.

For the Hönggerberg location, we are looking for a:n manager:in IGM (m/f/d) - with responsibility and heart for our buildings in the HC building area.

Job description

As Head of IGM, you will be responsible for the entire infrastructural operation of the HC building area and ensure smooth operation. You ensure that cleaning, maintenance, logistics and other infrastructural services are provided efficiently, economically and sustainably.

With your expertise, you continuously develop existing cleaning and operating processes, control internal employees and external service providers and ensure a high quality of service. You plan and coordinate cleaning and maintenance tasks, monitor the targeted use of personnel and resources and are responsible for the procurement and inventory of materials, equipment and machines.

In addition, you arrange and accompany special and basic cleaning, check the services provided and keep an eye on budgets, contracts and additional and reduced services at all times. In addition, you will take on a wide range of tasks in operational building operations. This includes minor repairs, troubleshooting, opening and closing buildings, optimizing HVAC operating times, and supporting events.

In the area of safety, you play an important role: You monitor fire alarm systems, process alarms, provide first aid if necessary and actively participate in the BMA and EVAK organization.

As a leader, you lead caretakers: inside and cleaning staff, promote their professional and personal development, plan resources forward and create a motivating working environment. In addition, you work in projects, accompany tenders and represent the building area manager in absences in both operational and strategic issues.

In short, you take responsibility for the infrastructural operation of buildings, drive their further development and ensure that processes, services and infrastructure reliably interlink.

Profile

  • Basic craft training and preferably specialist training in facility management as well as several years of experience in infrastructural building management
  • Sound understanding of cleaning, maintenance and building logistics processes
  • Initial leadership experience, a talent for resource planning and team development, the ability to impart knowledge (training, workshops) and a clear, motivational leadership style with high social skills
  • In-depth knowledge of cleaning processes, proven experience in process design, quality management and controlling as well as the ability to reconcile economic and ecological requirements
  • In-depth project management skills, the ability to structure processes and set priorities, and the willingness to take responsibility and make decisions
  • Basic technical understanding of buildings and building technology as well as a structured, solution-oriented approach
  • The ability to quickly identify challenges and solve them pragmatically
  • Communication strength, empathy and professional customer orientation as well as conflict resolution and diplomatic skills
  • Very good German and English skills
