Full description

Psychotherapy at a high professional level and the further development of an interdisciplinary team shape your everyday work in this position. As a cadre practitioner or cadre practitioner psychotherapy, you take on a central leadership role in the Center for Psychotherapy and Psychosomatics with a focus on Schematherapy. With a workload of 40 to 80 %, you actively shape therapeutic processes, lead employees and further develop the station technically and structurally. This position is available from autumn 2026 or by arrangement. What awaits you in this challenging task

  • Medical management of the psychotherapy station in tandem with the station management
  • Professional and human leadership of an interdisciplinary team
  • Further development of modern therapy concepts with a focus on schema therapy
  • Responsibility for the quality of treatment and organizational processes
  • Co-shaping the conceptual and economic development of the station
  • Treatment of patients with depressive diseases, anxiety disorders, dependency disorders and personality disorders

This is what they bring with them.

  • Completed specialist training in psychiatry and psychotherapy
  • Joy of psychotherapy as well as the leadership and development of employees
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

That sets them apart.

  • High professional competence in psychotherapeutic work
  • Leadership and decision-making skills
  • Strong social skills and communication skills Interest in conceptual development
  • Commitment and responsibility

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Responsible governance with scope for design
  • Modern therapeutic concepts with a focus on schematherapy
  • Structured working conditions with regulated working hours
  • Extensive training and further education offer with financial support
  • Supervision, coaching and targeted promotion of your development
  • Possibility to conduct an outpatient private consultation
  • Staff room and childcare on the clinic site
  • Attractive location in a natural environment with good accessibility of the cities of Zurich, Winterthur, St Gallen and Constance

In addition for EU citizens

  • Support for MEBEKO recognition of your diplomas
  • Assistance in organizing travel to Switzerland
  • Support in the search for housing by the internal rental department
