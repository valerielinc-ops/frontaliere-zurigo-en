About the Role Join our team as a Freelance Data Center Technician in Zurich, Switzerland. This is an hourly, on-demand, dispatch-based contract opportunity supporting data center operations and infrastructure projects. Assignments depend on client demand and may range from a few hours to a full day or longer. There are no guaranteed weekly hours, and technicians should be comfortable working independently onsite and responding to assignments as needed. Compensation & Benefits

  • Hourly compensation based on the agreed contractor rate
  • Payment based on actual hours worked
  • Travel allowance may apply to eligible assignments
  • Additional rates may apply for eligible night, weekend, or other scheduled work, subject to the agreed terms

Work Schedule

  • Freelance, hourly, project-based assignments
  • Flexible schedule depending on client requirements and assignment availability
  • Some assignments may require nights, weekends, or short-notice availability
  • No guaranteed minimum number of hours per week

Key Responsibilities

  • Install, rack, stack, and decommission server and network equipment
  • Perform structured cabling, including copper and fiber-optic connections
  • Replace hardware components and perform basic physical troubleshooting
  • Assist with equipment deployments, migrations, and upgrades
  • Troubleshoot physical connectivity, cabling, and hardware issues
  • Maintain accurate documentation, asset records, and ticket updates
  • Follow site procedures, technical instructions, and safety requirements
  • Coordinate with project managers, remote teams, and data center personnel
  • Complete assigned tasks independently and escalate issues when necessary

For technicians with advanced technical experience, assignments may also involve:

  • Advanced Cisco or Juniper routing and switching configuration
  • VLANs, routing protocols, ACLs, and network security
  • Complex network and server troubleshooting, including root cause analysis
  • Data center migrations, infrastructure upgrades, and network reconfiguration
  • Automation, scripting, and advanced server/network configuration
  • Troubleshooting complex connectivity issues across multiple devices and systems

Requirements

2+ years of data center or IT infrastructure experience. Hands-on experience in data center operations, including rack-and-stack, server installation, decommissioning, and structured cabling. Strong cabling skills, including Cat5e, Cat6, Cat6a, single-mode and multimode fiber. Familiarity with server hardware (Dell, HP, Supermicro), networking equipment, and physical troubleshooting. Experience with fiber optics, SFP/QSFP transceivers, or cross-connects is an advantage. Ability to lift 50+ lbs and work in raised-floor environments. Ability to work independently, follow technical instructions, and comply with site safety procedures. Own transportation and ability to work on-site in Zurich and nearby locations as required. Availability for occasional night, weekend, and short-notice assignments. Professional communication skills in English. Legally authorized to work in Switzerland without employer sponsorship. Familiarity with DCIM platforms, ticketing systems, and major colocation providers is preferred. Data center certifications (CDCP, CDCS, or similar) and networking certifications (CCNA, CompTIA Network+) are preferred. Own basic tools, such as screwdrivers, cable testers, label makers, and fiber inspection equipment, as required for assignments.
