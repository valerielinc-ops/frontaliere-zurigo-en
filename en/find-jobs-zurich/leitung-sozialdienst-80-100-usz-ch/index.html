Full description

The University Hospital Zurich is one of the leading hospitals in Switzerland. The combination of care, research and teaching makes it possible to make the knowledge advantage usable for all people. Our more than 10,000 employees meet more than 42,000 inpatients and 850,000 outpatients every year: women with appreciation and humanity. >The social service team consists of the social counselling, rehab center and volunteer service departments. All employees of the social service work together, responsibly and for the benefit of all for the patients: >The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.

Tasks

  • In addition to the social service, they run the rehab center, which coordinates and organizes patient exits in rehabilitation clinics, as well as the volunteer service. You will actively drive forward the strategic and operational development of these areas.
  • They lead the directly subordinate team leaders and promote an appreciative, participatory and development-oriented leadership culture.
  • They ensure an economical and high-quality provision of the service.
  • They coordinate and control operational activities, are supported by team leaders and work closely with our external follow-up organisations.
  • They work in internal and external projects and promote innovative solutions to optimize patient care.
  • They actively represent the social service internally and externally and promote cooperation with partners, specialist groups and associations.
  • They recognize cross-departmental relationships between clinics and professional groups and support interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation.

Requirements

  • Completed studies in social work (Bsc or Msc)
  • Management training at MAS Management/Leadership level
  • Experience in change management
  • Several years of management experience at middle management level, advantageous in the acute hospital
  • High level of leadership and social competence, strategic thinking, binding and clear communication and negotiating skills in interprofessional cooperation
  • Good knowledge and networking in healthcare
  • Strong ability to bring diverse interests together and develop viable solutions in a complex environment

We offer

Expected in our dedicated and dynamically developing department In addition to a varied and demanding task, you have a motivated team.
