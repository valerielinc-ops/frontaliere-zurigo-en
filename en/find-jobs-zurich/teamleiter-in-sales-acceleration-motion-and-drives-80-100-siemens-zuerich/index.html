Do you want to lead a team of experts, bundle technical know-how and inspire customers with first-class and tailor-made automation solutions? As an agile leader, you will exemplify and actively promote cultural change with regard to digitalization and AI, transferring your constant willingness to develop to your team. In addition, you are part of the extended management group of the Swiss Siemens DI Sales Organization and, together with your team, assume responsibility for the business development of a defined part of the Siemens DI overall portfolio. Through close cooperation with the sales channels and targeted and coordinated measures, you contribute significantly to the increase in sales volume and the future-oriented further development of the portfolio. Do you also want to actively shape the future and influence millions of people in Switzerland? Then you are exactly right with us!   • In addition, you already have disciplinary and professional leadership experience in multicultural teams and experience in business responsibility.
  • Industry and business expansion, customer orientation and sales are your passion.
  • You are a person who empowers and motivates your organization to develop its potential to achieve ambitious goals together.
  • You have experience in international cooperation and move confidently in management communication with large customers.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
