Full description

Active and present accompaniment of children and adolescents, this includes for us the work in child and adolescent psychiatry. This includes:

  • the individual and resource-oriented support of our patients within a milieutherapeutic treatment setting
  • the caregiver work and co-creating interprofessional cooperation
  • the need- and resource-oriented stay and care planning
  • the implementation of individual and family discussions and the management of therapeutic group offers
  • the design of the daily structure and leisure activities (actively direct and accompany)
  • the active contact with the origin system You are flexible, innovative and enjoy the variety. You bring expertise, enthusiasm for the children and youth area and a lot of humor coupled with empathy. With these characteristics, we achieve the best treatment results for our patients and we are pleased that you will become part of the team. In addition, we would like to:
