Are you looking for a new challenge? Then help expand the team at Physician Associates and support the medical and nursing teams in the care of patients.

We are looking for one for our orthopedic wards by appointment Tasks: • Professional and organizational leadership and further development of the Physician Associate team • Promote collaboration and further training • Taking over the medically delegated tasks (visits, examinations, prescriptions, discussions, etc.) for inpatients in close cooperation with the doctors in charge of the case •
