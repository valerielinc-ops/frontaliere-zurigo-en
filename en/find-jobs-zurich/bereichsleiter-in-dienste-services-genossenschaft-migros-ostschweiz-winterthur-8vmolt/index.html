Full description

Do you have a feeling for excellent service and are enthusiastic about offering our customers a positive shopping experience at the checkout and customer service? 💳 Do you have leadership experience, are sales-strong 💪 and like to communicate safely with our customers? 😊 Then you are exactly right with us! ✅ Let us be «tämä success». 🤝🧡

Mansioni

  • You take over the professional and personnel management of the area of funds 💼👥 and ensure that everything runs smoothly.
  • You plan the personnel deployment 📅🤝 and make sure that your team is used optimally.
  • The flow of money, administrative tasks and the operation of the telephone exchange 💰📋☎️ are in your hands – you always keep the overview and ensure a smooth process.
  • Friendly and competent customer service and advice 😊🛍️ are a matter of course for you and you will advise the customers with your expertise.
  • You lead and develop the department managers and employees and are responsible for the promotion of young talent.
  • You make sure that the defined processes are adhered to in the appropriate areas
  • You ensure the achievement of sales and cost targets

Requirements

  • Many years of professional experience: in the retail trade
  • Completed basic or further training: in the retail trade
  • You bring very good expertise in the area of cash register / customer service 💼👥 and can draw on valuable leadership experience.
  • Reliability, flexibility and a high level of commitment 💪⏰ distinguish you – you are always there when you are needed.
  • With your independent work and your friendly approach 😊🤝 you create a pleasant atmosphere for customers and team.
  • You are ready to continue your education 📚🚀 and always stay up to date. Ideally, you already have leadership experience that helps you take responsibility and support your team.
  • German (very good knowledge)
