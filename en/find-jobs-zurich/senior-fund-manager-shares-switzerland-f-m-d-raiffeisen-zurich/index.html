Full description

03.08.2026 Zurich

Become part of our team!

Fund management manages Raiffeisen’s active investment funds and is part of the Investment & Provision Center. As of January 2027 or by agreement, we are looking for an analytically strong and dynamic personality as Senior Fund Manager (m/f/d) with experience in the management of global equity funds.

What do you expect?

  • Responsibility for the management and performance of the global equity fund
  • Ongoing market, sector and competition analyses as well as macroeconomic assessments as input for investment decisions
  • Further development and implementation of the investment process in the area of Shares Global
  • Integration of quantitative models to support stock selection, risk monitoring and portfolio optimisation
  • Interaction in a team of specialists in different asset classes, strategists and analysts

What do you bring?

  • Completed studies in economics, finance or a related subject area (ETH, Uni, FH), certifications such as CFA and AZEK are desired
  • Several years of experience managing active global equity funds with a solid track record
  • Profound understanding of stock markets, balance sheet and cash flow analysis and valuation techniques, and experience with derivative financial instruments (stock options)
  • Strong analytical and conceptual skills, structured working methods and high implementation strength, sound programming skills are a plus
  • Collaborative ability as a team player
  • Very good German skills are required, language skills in Italian and French are advantageous
