Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Occupation by September 2027

Type of employment: Temporary

Reference number: 6448

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

Note: Please note that we currently only offer the full-time variant with internships.

YOUR TASKS

  • Completion of the practical part of the training in Diagnostic Radiology, Nuclear Medicine and Radio-Oncology at Hirslanden Clinic; the theoretical part you go through in the Careum Education Center
  • Interface between technology, doctors and patients
  • Connection of support with complex technology and use of current knowledge as well as the latest technologies in the three specialist areas
  • Preparation of patients for the respective situation
  • Care and support of patients during the examination as well as during therapy
  • Documentation of patient data, processing of image data and assessment of their quality

Your profile

  • Completion of three years of vocational training with a federal certificate of competence, a DMS/FMS degree or a high school diploma
  • Passed aptitude procedure at the Careum Education Centre
  • Empathy as well as high social skills in dealing with patients and their relatives as well as in working with employees and doctors
  • Strong technical understanding and interest
  • Willingness to develop expertise and make an important contribution to the success of our clinic

FOLLOWING DOCUMENTS HAVE TO BE INCLUDED

  • Letter of motivation for desired vocational training
  • CV with current photo
  • Proof of the passed admission procedure at the Careum Education Centre
  • Copy of the identity card of the three-year vocational training including grade card, DMS/FMS degree or Matura with corresponding certificate
  • Information from a reference person with telephone number and e-mail address
  • Employment certificates (if any)
  • Copy ID / Residence permit

We offer an interesting learning environment with exciting and complex patient situations. Accompanied by the vocational trainers and training managers, you will gain insight into various specialist areas of highly specialized medicine during your practical assignments. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions and a constant wage payment during the entire training period.

As employees who have to move completely for their work, you will receive four additional paid free days (except specialist and cadre doctors) for a 100% workload. With a reduced workload, the days off reduce accordingly. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from an equivalent financial compensation. These are just a few reasons why the Hirslanden Group is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.

Our benefits for employees

For additional information, please contact Maud de Graaf, Radiology Services Training Manager, T or Alice Nutt, HR recruitment partner, alice.nutt@hirslanden.ch. We look forward to your contact.

Please note that for data protection reasons we cannot consider paper dossiers (will not be returned) nor applications by email. Therefore, please apply exclusively online via this tool.
