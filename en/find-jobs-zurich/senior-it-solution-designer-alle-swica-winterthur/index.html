Full description

You translate professional requirements into secure and operable solutions based on Microsoft 365. You combine solution design with technical implementation and work closely with specialist areas, engineering, IT security and other stakeholders. This is how you shape health

  • You design and develop Modern Workplace solutions around Exchange Online, Teams, SharePoint Online, OneDrive, Intune and Entra ID
  • You will analyze existing configurations and requirements, evaluate solution variants and develop sound decision bases
  • You will accompany product evaluations and migrations and continuously develop existing Modern Workplace Services
  • You define standards for collaboration, access, permissions and data storage and align them with IT security and compliance
  • You implement solutions yourself technically, support projects in planning, testing and implementation and ensure the documented transfer to the company
  • You document solutions comprehensibly, share your knowledge in the team and drive the standardization of Modern Workplace Services

This sets you apart

  • Completed training in computer science, ideally supplemented by higher specialist training in computer science or business informatics or comparable further education
  • Several years of experience in the Microsoft 365 environment, ideally in solution design or engineering
  • In-depth knowledge of Exchange Online, Teams, SharePoint Online and Intune as well as good knowledge of Entra ID
  • Practical experience in the administration and configuration of Microsoft 365, both in the admin centers and with PowerShell
  • Experience in the design, technical implementation and implementation of solutions as well as ideally in migrations and product evaluations
  • Independent and structured working methods as well as the ability to comprehend technical content for different stakeholders
  • Confident knowledge of German (level of mother tongue) as well as good knowledge of English
