Role overview

Tasks

As a medical assistant in the case system, you supervise the psychiatric, drug and psychotherapeutic treatment of people aged 18 and over in our stationary clinic in Ellikon an der Thur. This includes conducting clarification interviews, diagnostics, reporting and careful medical history as well as developing therapy goals and plans, conducting and evaluating psychotherapeutic interventions in individual, couple or family discussions. Site discussions with employers and, if necessary, with other involved [Fv1] third parties or institutions round off the spectrum, in addition, you take over the management of group therapies.

Requirements

Ideally, you already have psychiatric and psychotherapeutic work experience and preferably a completed foreign year, an advanced psychotherapy education is beneficial. You have previous experience in psychiatric, team-oriented and interprofessional action as well as a CH doctor’s diploma or entry in the MEBEKO registry. Very good knowledge of German in word and writing we presuppose.

We offer

With this position, we offer you responsible tasks in a motivated, modern and well-connected environment. Your professional and personal development is a concern for us, which we support generously and specifically with structured further education and training offers. The working conditions are contemporary and appealing:

  • Reliable service planning in a stable environment
  • 42 hours + 4 hours working week
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
  • 60 % employer contribution to the pension fund
  • Our stationary clinic in Ellikon an der Thur is idyllic in the countryside and at the same time offers good accessibility from the Zurich, Winterthur and Eastern Switzerland region and the German border area. Further advantages of the place of work are the in-house gastronomy and sufficient free parking. Our offer is aimed at people of all genders and identities.
