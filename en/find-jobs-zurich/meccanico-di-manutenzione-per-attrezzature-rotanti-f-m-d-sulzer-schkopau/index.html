Original description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society. About the role Sulzer is a global leader in fluid engineering with headquarters in Winterthur, Switzerland. Since 1834, we have been developing innovative solutions for pumping, mixing and separating liquids. With around 13,800 employees and over 180 locations worldwide, we stand for quality, customer proximity and technical excellence. Our services At more than 100 service locations, Sulzer offers comprehensive maintenance, repair and spare parts solutions for pumps, turbines, compressors and other rotating machines – independent of the manufacturer. Our technology-based services ensure maximum efficiency and sustainability throughout the entire product lifecycle – directly at our customers on site. Their tasks

  • Repair, maintenance, testing and commissioning of pumps, compressors, gearboxes, turbines and other rotating machinery
  • Analysis and evaluation of damage cases and troubleshooting
  • Independent disassembly and assembly of equipment and assemblies incl. Slip ring seal and periphery
  • Performance of maintenance work according to drawing or technical specifications
  • Documentation of all work processes, preparation of reports and findings
  • Ensuring the high quality standards of Sulzer
  • Technical advice and support for our customers Your profile
  • Completed training as an industrial mechanic:in or comparable qualification

  • Ideally, experience in sectors such as petrochemicals, chemicals, refineries or energy production
  • Readiness for daily and occasional multi-day assembly
  • Flexibility for short-term assignments and overtime
  • Strong skills in error analysis and troubleshooting
  • High customer and service orientation as well as quality awareness
