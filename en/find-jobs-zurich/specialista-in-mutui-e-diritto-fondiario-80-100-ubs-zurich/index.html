Full description

Key responsibilities Do you have experience in mortgage processing and the Swiss Land Registry? Is efficient, accurate and responsible work exactly your strength? Do you enjoy administrative activities with flexible working hours?

Then apply, for we want to grow and seek reinforcement!

Your tasks

  • Self-employment of approved loan applications
  • Preparation of all necessary credit and security documents
  • Imaging and management of mortgages and collateral (e.g. deposits, policies, guarantees)
  • Preparation of payment promises and timely execution of payments
  • Ensuring all conditions for the release of mortgages and payments
  • Accompaniment and correct termination of business cases under personal responsibility
  • Correspondence with landlords, notaries, banks and insurance companies
  • Dutiful management of potential risks

The team Your new team consists of a dynamic mix of employees with a positive DU culture. Cohesion and a collegial cooperation characterize us. An exciting and extensive field of activity awaits you, in which you can contribute a lot of personal competence, and have the opportunity to work part of your work from home.

Our offices are located directly next to the Zurich-Altstetten station and also in Biel 2 minutes from the station. We process cross-team mortgage products for the whole of Switzerland.

Diversity helps us to grow together. That is why we are committed to promoting and nurturing diversity, equal opportunities and inclusion. This strengthens our business and adds value to our customers.

Your skills and experience

  • Sound knowledge of mortgage processing and/or land registering
  • Great interest in the management of mortgage financing
  • Independent, exact and efficient work with high quality standards
  • Responsible action and professional behaviour
  • Willingness to help and loves to achieve the goals as a team
  • Communication strength and openness
  • Resilience and keep track even in stressful situations
  • Joy to make a difference in the organization and to actively contribute
  • Very good knowledge of German, other national languages and English are advantageous
