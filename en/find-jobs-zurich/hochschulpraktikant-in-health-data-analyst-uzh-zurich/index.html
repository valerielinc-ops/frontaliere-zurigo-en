Clinic for Infectious Diseases and Hospital Hygiene, University Hospital Zurich

Job from 11. January 2027, temporary

The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,000 employees work every day for the well-being of our patients. More than 42,000 inpatients and around 600,000 outpatient consultations per year testify to the great trust in the USZ and its employees. With us you will find an attractive and challenging environment for your knowledge and skills. The Department of Hospital Hygiene of the Clinic of Infectious Diseases and Hospital Hygiene is responsible for the monitoring and prevention of hospital-acquired infections. In the area of health data analytics in hospital hygiene / infection prevention, the development, maintenance and further development of IT systems for monitoring process and outcome indicators is an important and essential area of responsibility. The job is financed by third-party funds and limited to one year as an internship.

Tasks

  • Data management support: Perform SQL queries from the data warehouse, structure and document data
  • Participation in data classification, maintenance and assurance of data quality
  • Support for senior physicians: inside and specialist department for operational data management tasks
  • Exploratory data analysis and visualization using Python or BI tools (e.g. Power BI)
  • Participation in internal team meetings and professional exchange with clinic and IT

Profile

  • Bachelor Degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Natural Sciences or a Related Field
  • Interest in working with medical data and complex data structures
  • Basic knowledge in statistics and machine learning is desirable
  • No professional experience required; initial practical project experience (e.g. through internships or final theses) is beneficial
  • Willingness to work in an interdisciplinary team
  • Analytical thinking, structured and precise working methods
  • Independence, willingness to learn and solution orientation
  • Communication strength and teamwork
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Good IT knowledge (SQL / Python / MS Office)
  • Basic IT knowledge in BI tools (e.g. Power BI, Tableau) / in Git
