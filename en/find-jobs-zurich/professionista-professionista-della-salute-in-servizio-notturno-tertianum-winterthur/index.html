Full description

Tertianum Neuhegi offers people with a need for care and care an attractive living environment and a home to feel good. The residents of our senior center are guaranteed security, care and comprehensive care around the clock. We accompany people in their self-determined lives – regardless of their social background – and are committed to a high quality of our services.

Your heart beats for older people?

Do you not only want to ensure nursing quality with your work, but also significantly enrich the lives of residents? You see care as a task with meaning and responsibility – and would like to use your skills where they really make a difference?

Then you are right with us.

For the Tertianum Neuhegi in Winterthur we are looking for 01.12.2026 or by arrangement a

Professional Health EFZ 80% - 100% on night duty

Their tasks

  • Holistic care and care of the guests in the sense of our care mission statement and the care process
  • Participation in the care process and in the care needs classification with RAI among the assigned guests
  • Electronic care documentation using CareCoach (mobile possible with CareCoach smartphone)
  • Ensuring need-oriented care and care of guests during the night
  • Implementation of regular inspection and safety tours as well as recognition and initiation of appropriate measures in emergency situations
  • Taking over the in-house emergency call and professional cooperation with the medical emergency service
  • Independent organisation and coordination of the night service and ensuring a smooth flow of information to the day service
  • Accompanying, supporting and promoting the well-being of guests at night, taking into account individual needs and habits

Your profile

  • Completed training for/specialist health EFZ
  • Professional experience in long-term care is beneficial
  • RAI knowledge desired
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude
  • Enjoying multiple challenges
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
