Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With more than 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric offer of Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in ambulatory, day-clinical and inpatient settings. The Group employs around 1800 people and achieved a turnover of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For adult psychiatry, we are looking for an

The Center for Adult Psychiatry offers adults between the ages of 18 and 60, support and assistance in crisis situations, difficult phases of life. We work according to current clinical guidelines and findings of evidence-based medicine. The recovery-oriented therapeutic concept of the stations combines disorder-specific therapeutic elements with general crisis intervention techniques and milieutherapeutic action factors. Park A focuses on acceptance and commitment therapy. Park B works with the concept of Safewards and milieutherapteutic elements. Park C crisis intervention works according to the guidelines of the Safewards concept. Park D offers qualified withdrawal treatment and crisis intervention.
