Full description

Nutritional therapy and advice in the area of inpatient and outpatient neurorehabilitation with epileptology with a focus on malnutrition

  • Additional specific questions regarding nutritional therapy for dysphagia, diabetes, gastrointestinal diseases, obesity, neurodegenerative and nephrological diseases
  • Assessment of findings using standardized screening and assessment tools (NRS, BIA, etc.)
  • Responsibility for enteral and parenteral nutrition (tube plans)
  • Ketogenic diet as part of epilepsy treatment
  • Therapeutic eating support for patients in the inpatient rehabilitation area
  • Allergy and intolerance clarifications in collaboration with the diet kitchen
  • Care and support for BSc nutritional counseling students (BFH and FFHS)
  • Independent work and daily exchange with therapists, nurses, doctors and the hotel industry
  • Requirements
  • Degree as a nutritionist FH BSc or MSc
  • Experience in the care of neurological and epileptological clients are an advantage
  • High quality standards and interest in further training
  • Enjoy working with chronically ill and disabled people
  • Strong social skills and the ability to work in a team
  • Networked thinking and acting
  • Initiative and resilience
  • Willingness to perform and solution-oriented way of working
  • Cosa offriamo
  • Klinik Lengg AG in Zurich is the competence center for epileptology and neurological rehabilitation.
  • Our aim is to enable patients with neurological diseases to undergo the greatest possible rehabilitation and to ensure accurate diagnosis and sustainable treatment of patients with epilepsy.
  • Around 300 employees are humanely, competently, innovatively and reliably committed to ensuring that our patients regain the greatest possible independence
  • and thus their quality of life. Contatto
  • Become part of one of our interdisciplinary and interprofessional teams.
