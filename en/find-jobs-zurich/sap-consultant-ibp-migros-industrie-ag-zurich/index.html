Full description

Do you want to actively shape the SAP planning landscape of the Migros industry? In the Business Unit IT we develop and maintain the SAP systems of the Migros industry. For our team, we are looking for a dedicated personality that brings together our SAP IBP planning environment in a dynamic environment with expertise, initiative and team spirit.

Mansioni

  • Analysis and recording of technical requirements in the field of SAP IBP (demand, supply, S&OP)
  • Conception and design of planning processes in SAP IBP
  • Customizing and further development of planning areas, key figures and master data structures
  • Presentation of workshops and advice from stakeholders on planning processes
  • Documentation and communication with stakeholders
  • Planning and accompaniment of IBP introductions, releases and rollouts in the departments

Requisiti

  • Mind. 5 years of professional experience: in the field of SAP IBP Demand, Supply and S&OP environment or in a related area
  • Completed studies (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): in (economic)informatics, business management, supply chain management or comparable training
  • Very good knowledge of SAP IBP configuration (e.g. Planning Areas, Key Figures, Time Profiles, Attributes, Alerts, Analytics)
  • Safe handling of process analysis and design
  • Experience in operation and support of SAP solutions
  • Strong and clear communication with stakeholders
  • German (sales-proof)
  • English (safe for negotiation)
  • French (of advantage)
