Full description

The rapid detection and treatment of cyber attacks is more important because it requires a strong defence architecture, modern Det-Syndon solutions, a reliable and modern security operations center and people who take responsibility. This is where you come in. As a senior cyber security engineer with a focus on incident and threat intelligence, you take a central role in the Cyber Security team of SWICA. Thanks to our work-anywhere regulation, you work flexibly throughout Switzerland, combined with personal exchange at our headquarters in Winterthur.

How to shape health

  • Optimization of processes for the detection, analysis and treatment of security incidents and cyber-scans, as well as implementation of measures for the continuous increase of team-maturity
  • Independent processing and coordination of security incidents (incident handlers) as well as preparation and presentation of management reports and recommendations for management
  • Coordination and management of internal stakeholders and the external SOC and CSIRT partner
  • Implementation of technical analyses, Threat Hunt and Incident Response activities
  • Further development and automation

by SIEM LINES and Runbooks to improve security monitoring together with the SOC provider - development of Cyber Security Playbooks and Pre-Approved Con-Daily Measures - support and implementation of Blue Team exercises to strengthen the WOG security measures

That's what makes you feel

  • Training or studies in computer science or information security
  • Quick understanding and ability to divide complex issues into solutionable work packages
  • At least 3 years of practical experience in the field of cyber security, especially in Incident Response, Incident Handling, Threat Intelligence
  • Well-founded knowledge of modern attack vectors, security frameworks and relevant tools e.g. SIEM / SOAR (MS Sentinel), XDR (MS Defender)
  • Certifications such as GIAC GSOM, GIAC GCIH, CERT CSIH, OSCP or similar are advantageous
  • Scripting and automation skills (e.g.

Python, PowerShell), experience with infrastructure as code or security automation of advantage

  • experience in handling complex cyber security incidents as well as communication to technical and non-technical target groups
  • you are used to working with external partners and leading them
  • structured, analytical and independent working methods
  • pleasure in communicating technical topics in a comprehensible way and taking on professional responsibility
  • Stylish German in word and writing and very good English skills
