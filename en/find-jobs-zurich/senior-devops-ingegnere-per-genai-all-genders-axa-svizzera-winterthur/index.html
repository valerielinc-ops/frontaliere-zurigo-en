Shape your future – together with us!

Do you want to be part of something big? We offer you a great team in a dynamic environment around the hottest topic around!

In our "Distribution Strategy, Project & Communication" department, AXA CH's distribution strategy is implemented, concepts are developed and implemented together with the product factory, the technology teams and the various sales channels. Our goal is to create the best customer experience – true to our motto: "We create unique AXA moments".

We are looking for an experienced, technically strong personality who loves responsibility – and takes our productive AI applications to the next level.

As a Senior DevOps Engineer, you will take full operational responsibility for two productive, heavily used GenAI-based applications – from infrastructure to user support. You not only bring technical depth, but also the seniority to set priorities, design processes and take other team members with you.

Become a part of our distribution. Our division is the window to our customers. Every day, we are committed to meeting customer needs in the best possible way through proactive, progressive and personal consulting solutions – in line with our most important corporate value "Customer first".

What to expect

  • You will optimize the production operations of two GenAI apps with a high daily user count – stability, performance, and availability
  • You plan, take responsibility for and automate the entire deployment process – from the CI/CD pipeline to the production release – with a high degree of automation, maximum reproducibility and minimal risk for ongoing operations
  • You manage the cloud infrastructure on GCP and Azure with Terraform, have OpenShift experience and ensure smooth operation of the applications
  • You automate iteratively – monitoring, alerting, ticket routing, deployments, user communication – with the clear goal of eliminating manual work step by step. What is not yet automated, you can reliably do manually – with an eye for what's next
  • You identify systemic weaknesses, make suggestions for improvement and implement them independently
  • You are a close sparring partner for PO, development teams and business stakeholders and translate technical reality into understandable communication

What you bring to the table

  • Several years of in-depth DevOps experience in production environments with high user loads—you know what's at stake
  • Support mentality with seniority: You take user problems seriously, prioritize confidently and communicate clearly – even under pressure. In doing so, you always have an eye on improvements in the operating process
  • Terraform: You write, structure and manage "Infrastructure as Code" professionally and sustainably
  • Public cloud experience independent of hyperscaler (GCP, Azure or similar): You have a solid understanding of modern cloud concepts and are able to quickly familiarize yourself with different cloud environments.
  • CI/CD: In-depth experience with pipeline tools such as GitHub Actions or comparable solutions
  • Automation affinity: You use the right tools – be it Python, shell scripts or comparable technologies – and eliminate the need for repetitive processes through automation
  • Monitoring & Observability: You will proactively develop and maintain dashboards, alerting and logging setups
  • Committed, self-reliant personality with technical depth and a pragmatic mindset
  • You are fluent in German and English
  • Degree in computer science, engineering or a related discipline – or proven equivalent professional experience
