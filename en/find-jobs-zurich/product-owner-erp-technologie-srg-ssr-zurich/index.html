Full description

The Operations division assumes the central strategic and controlling role for the technological orientation and production of the SRG SSR. The ERP Technology team ensures the stable, secure and efficient operation of the central technical platforms in the SAP environment. It forms the technical basis for the HR, financial and logistics processes and ensures a modern, integrated system landscape.

As a product owner ERP technology, you define and prioritize the further development of these platforms together with the product manager. They lead the specialists from SAP Basis, SAP Security and SAP Development professionally and combine the requirements of the specialist areas with a sustainable technological orientation.

This is your contribution.

In the area of operations, you are involved as a product owner in the ERP technology product team for the following topics:

  • Professional leadership of the team in the areas of SAP Basis, SAP Security and SAP Development as well as ensuring coordinated planning and prioritization of tasks
  • Orientation of the operation and further development of SAP platforms to the product strategy, business requirements and requirements for security, stability and performance
  • Clarification of technical and professional requirements with stakeholders as well as maintenance and prioritization of the product backlog taking into account dependencies, risks and available capacities
  • Joint design and communication of product vision, goals and roadmap with the product manager as well as coordination of implementation with adjacent product teams and external partners
  • Ensure reliable operation together with the team, including handling service requests, incidents, problems and changes, and meeting agreed service levels
  • Coordination of releases, deployments and technical lifecycle measures as well as promotion of standardization, automation and continuous improvement of the SAP system landscape

They inspire us.

  • At least five years, ideally eight years or more professional experience in the SAP technology environment, especially in SAP Basis, SAP Development or SAP Authorizations. You’ll be familiar with SAP S/4HANA and the SAP ecosystem, including adjacent solutions, and experience with on-premise and cloud landscapes
  • Experience in the professional leadership and coordination of technical specialists as well as the ability to provide orientation and set common priorities
  • Experience in the control and further development of products or services as well as in product backlog management and in the prioritization of complex requirements
  • Knowledge of IT service management as well as change, release and deployment coordination
  • Understanding availability, performance, permissions and technical security requirements
  • Strong communication and moderation skills, a customer-focused way of working and an understanding of governance, quality and continuous improvement
  • Very good knowledge of a national language as well as German or French at at least level B1
  • Knowledge of English at least level B1 is an advantage

So we inspire you.

  • Design leeway in the technological development of the SRG SSR central SAP platforms
  • A versatile role with professional leadership and close cooperation with specialists from SAP base, SAP authorizations and SAP development
  • Product-oriented, agile cooperation with the Product Manager, the specialist departments and adjacent product teams
  • The opportunity to actively shape a modern, secure and efficient system landscape for central HR, financial and logistics processes
