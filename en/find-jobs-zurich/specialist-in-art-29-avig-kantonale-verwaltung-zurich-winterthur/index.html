Full description

Canton of Zurich Economic Directorate Office for Labour Specialist/in Art. 29 AVIG 80 - 100% work changes lives - and that is what drives us. Every day, the Office of Labour (AFA) of the Canton of Zurich works to ensure that people regain their jobs, that companies find the right talents and that the labour market in the Canton of Zurich remains strong. With around 900 employees at various locations, we bundle labour market skills and are close to the people, the companies and the realities of working life. You don’t work anywhere with us. You work where your commitment really counts. How do we combine service and efficiency in everyday life? We ensure that insurance benefits from the cantonal unemployment fund are paid out correctly and on time. We are there when it matters: we check claims, calculate unemployment insurance benefits and pay them out. Reliable, competent and with real understanding for the people behind the dossiers. In order to strengthen the specialist team Art. 29 AVIG, which is located in the legal service, we are looking for a committed specialist immediately or by agreement. The place of work is at the headquarters of the unemployment fund Canton of Zurich in Winterthur. Your tasks:

  • Performance testing and calculation of unemployment compensation in case of doubts about entitlements under employment contracts (Art. 29 AVIG)
  • Claiming civil claims against bankruptcy offices and other bodies involved
  • Correspondence with insured persons, employers, bankruptcy offices, legal representatives and other authorities
  • Advising and supporting internal and external stakeholder groups on technical and procedural issues
  • Close cooperation with internal lawyers in the legal assessment and handling of complex issues
  • Ensuring legally compliant, efficient and customer-oriented case processing in compliance with legal requirements
  • Knowledge management: building, maintaining and making available expertise
  • Participate in the further development of performance processes Your profile:
  • completed 3-year commercial basic training or equivalent CV training
  • many years of professional experience in AVIG execution
  • completed further training as a social security specialist or willingness to complete it in a timely manner
  • process-oriented, efficient, decision-making and pronounced figures flair
  • excellent communication skills, good performance skills and didactic experience
  • Strong teamwork
  • safe and networked thinking as well as pronounced endurance in handling complex case constellations
  • Curious for legal issues as well as open to new things We offer:
  • interesting and versatile activity with high personal responsibility
  • Possibility to develop within the organization by means of an established education and training model
  • Attractive employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines
  • Home office opportunity as well as the possibility to work at two different locations (Zurich Oerlikon and Winterthur)
  • Contribution to catering costs (lunch check) Do you value a living environment with stable and fair framework conditions? Then we should definitely get to know each other – Marc Schneider, Head of Department Legal Service & Complaints, telephone 043 258 10 19, will be happy to provide you with further information. We look forward to your application. You can find out more about us and our employees at www.zh.ch/afa.

Pensum: 80-100 %
