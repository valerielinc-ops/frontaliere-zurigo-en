Full description

Working as a police officer at the Cantonal Police Zurich is something of the most interesting and meaningful thing you can do with yourself and your future. Start with your application as a police aspirant / - in 100% tasks Your basic training lasts two years

  • During this time you will receive the full reward. In the first year, you will learn everything essential for your new profession at ZHPS Police School in Zurich. In the second year, you work for the airport police and already take on security-relevant tasks. After two years, you will pass the professional examination for the Swiss federal certificate «Policist/in»; once you have successfully overcome this hurdle, you will be sworn in and accepted into our police corps.
