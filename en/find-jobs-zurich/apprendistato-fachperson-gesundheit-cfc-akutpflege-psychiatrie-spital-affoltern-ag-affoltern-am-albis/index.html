Full description

As a specialist health EFZ you are part of a nursing team in acute care or in a psychiatric department. You nurture and care for patients in hospital everyday life and accompany them on their way to recovery. For example, you support them in personal care (shower, bath, toilet), help with dressing, shaving or hairdressing. It is important that you can build a trust relationship with the people you care for. As a specialist in health EFZ in training, you are also responsible for administrative and logistical tasks. Your profile

  • Compulsory schooling completed
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Communion, good manners
  • Patience and respect
  • Empathy and helpfulness
  • Good observation ability, fast reaction ability
  • Careful and accurate working methods
  • A sense of cleanliness and order
  • good physical condition, psychological resilience
  • Flexibility: willingness to work irregularly
  • Focus on psychiatry: Minimum age 17 years We look forward to your application with the usual documents, multi-check and reports on already completed trial teachings. The Hospital Affoltern as a training company attaches great importance to the professional support of all learners in 7 apprenticeships. In the area of care, two full-time vocational trainers accompany you in addition to practical trainers in the department. In our modern learning workshop, you can practice various tasks with them regularly and practically. At the Hospital Affoltern, committed teams await you in a very familiar environment. This is characterized by high care quality and team culture. Our employees benefit from many advantages and regular employee events (e.g. Student Day). «Bi öis can mer no»: With us it is family and personal. Benefit as part of the hospital team Affoltern You also benefit from attractive employment conditions and numerous fringe benefits: Jokertag, shopping benefits and two subscriptions for free admissions to the FORTYSEVEN Wellness Spa are just a few examples of our employee benefits.
