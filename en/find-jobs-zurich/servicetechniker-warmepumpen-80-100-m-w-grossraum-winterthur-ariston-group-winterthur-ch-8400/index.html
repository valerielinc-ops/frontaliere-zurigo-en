Full description

ELCO offers premium heating solutions with first-class service designed to meet the needs of our customers through the entire life cycle of products and heating systems. The focus is on excellent consulting as well as convincing products and heating systems. ELCO has been a leader in burner, condensing and solar technology for decades and has 1.7 million installed heating systems throughout Europe. Since 1928, ELCO has been a leading European brand offering intelligent solutions and technologies tailored to the needs of private and commercial projects, ensuring maximum energy savings.

Is technology your passion? You like to work independently and want to help shape the energy future with modern heating systems? Then you are exactly right at ELCO.

Become part of our team and shape the energy future with us!

This is what awaits you with us

  • You put modern heating systems such as heat pumps and solar systems into operation
  • You carry out maintenance and repairs independently and reliably repair faults
  • For demanding systems, you take on the technical responsibility and find the right solution
  • You advise our customers competently on the optimal setting and use of their heating systems
  • You work closely with installers and our sales and service teams

You bring that with you

  • A completed technical vocational training EFZ, e.g. as an electrical, heating or sanitary installer, in the car industry or in a similar technical profession
  • Technical flair and interest in modern heating systems and renewable energies
  • Experience in service is a plus, but not a must
  • Enjoyment of direct customer contact
  • An independent, reliable and solution-oriented working method
  • Willingness to participate in the Picetting Service
  • Place of residence in the Winterthur area or in the adjacent region

This is what we offer you

  • Modern service vehicle that you can also use privately
  • Innovative technologies and future-oriented products in the premium sector
  • Comprehensive training and support by experienced colleagues
  • Further education and development opportunities, both professional and personal
  • Attractive social benefits and fair employment conditions
  • A strong team that keeps you together and supports you from day one

Ready for something new? Then we want to get to know you. Apply now and become part of the ELCO service team. We look forward to your application!

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
