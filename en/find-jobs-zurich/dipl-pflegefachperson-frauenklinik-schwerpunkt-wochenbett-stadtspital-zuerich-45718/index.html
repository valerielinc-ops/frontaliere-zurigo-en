Full description

We are looking for a team-oriented personality for our bed station at the Frauenklinik with a focus on postpartum beds at the Triemli site, either immediately or by appointment, for the time being until the end of March 2027.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for the independent, independent and holistic care of women in different life situations and newborns.
  • You take responsibility for the care of women and newborns in obstetric and medical risk and crisis situations.
  • You are responsible for patient-centered documentation of the work and care process as well as for the correct performance recording.
  • You support the team with the introduction of new employees and take on joint responsibility in the care of students and learners
  • You promote a constructive interdisciplinary cooperation with high service quality.
  • You work to improve the quality and development of care.

Profile

  • Training as a Dipl. Nursing professional / Dipl. Care specialist HF/FH or Dipl. Midwife FH/BSc with professional experience on postpartum / Gynecology
  • Self-employed and flexible working methods
  • Strong communication skills, commitment to team development and good interdisciplinary cooperation
  • High level of technical and social competence
  • Willingness to work in shift work

We offer

We offer you more than just a job! Our city hospital world is characterized by an open du-culture, an uncomplicated, personal togetherness and the necessary touch of humor in everyday life. As a team of over 120 professional groups, we work every day in very different ways for the well-being of our patients. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions and excellent social benefits from the city of Zurich.

About us

With over 36'000 inpatients and around 455,000 outpatients per year, the Stadtspital Zürich ranks among the top 10 Swiss hospitals. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,500 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
