Full description

This is what awaits you

Do you like to organize, keep an overview even in lively situations and enjoy working with people? Then you are right with us. In team support, you support our sales and, together with our insurance and pension consultants, help ensure that our customers are personally and reliably looked after.

This is what they do with us

As an important hub in everyday agency life, you work closely with our insurance and pension consultants. They support the sales team in day-to-day business, coordinate internal processes and ensure that issues are dealt with quickly and reliably.

Her versatile tasks include appointment agreements and appointment coordination, organizational and administrative work as well as support in the preparation and follow-up of customer meetings. In addition, our customers at the agency receive you in a friendly and professional manner.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed vocational training, ideally in a commercial or service-oriented environment
  • Enjoy organization, coordination and administrative tasks
  • Strong service orientation and enjoyment of working together in a team
  • Independent, reliable and customer-oriented working method
  • Safe and friendly appearance as well as fluent knowledge of German
  • Other languages are a plus
  • You are also welcome as a lateral entrant. Important to us are your motivation and the willingness to learn new things.

Working with us

With us, you can expect a varied task with a lot of contact with people and a direct insight into sales. In a motivated team, you take on responsibility and are carefully introduced to your new area of responsibility. Insurance skills are beneficial, but not a requirement.

For the exercise of this function, according to the Insurance Supervision Act, the insurance intermediary /-in VBV, profile non-life, is required. If you do not yet bring it, we support you with tailor-made internal training on the way to completion.

This is what we offer

Participation in success

If we are successful, our employees will also benefit. Each year, the distribution of a successful share and its amount is decided.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses. We participate in job-relevant training.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer up to 25% discount on bonuses, discounts on mobile phones and free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
