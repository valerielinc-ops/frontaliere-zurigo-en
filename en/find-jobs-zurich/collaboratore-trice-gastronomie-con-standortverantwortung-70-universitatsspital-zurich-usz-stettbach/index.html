Main responsibilities

  • You take over the operational day-to-day and location responsibility for our catering company at the USZ office in Stettbach and at the same time actively work in the company.
  • As the first contact person on site, you coordinate the daily processes and ensure with your warm and professional manner that our guests feel comfortable.
  • You prepare the daily offer, bake baked goods, manage the self-service area and provide an attractive presentation of our products.
  • The operation of the cash register as well as the execution of the daily accounts are also part of your tasks.
  • You keep track of goods receipts, stocks and orders and carry out the necessary temperature and shelf life controls.
  • Cleaning, cleaning and administrative work are just as much a part of your everyday life as ensuring our quality, hygiene and HACCP standards.
  • Together with the group management, you bring your own ideas, actively shape the location and contribute to its further development. Requirements
  • Completed training as a restoration specialist, hotel specialist EFZ or comparable training in the catering or food industry
  • Several years of professional experience in the catering, community catering or hotel industry, ideally with first experience in a responsible function
  • Your heart beats for the gastronomy and the personal contact with our guests

Company and context

  • With your organizational talent, your hands-on mentality and your pleasure in contacting guests, you ensure that everything goes smoothly on site and our guests feel comfortable.
  • The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland.
  • Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.

Additional details

  • Together with the group management, you bring your own ideas, actively shape the location and contribute to its further development.
  • You are physically fit, flexible and ready for assignments from Monday to Friday within the company opening hours
  • At the same time, you have the special opportunity to help shape the new gastronomic location of the USZ in Stettbach right from the start and to contribute your own ideas. A motivated and committed team supports you in all respects.
