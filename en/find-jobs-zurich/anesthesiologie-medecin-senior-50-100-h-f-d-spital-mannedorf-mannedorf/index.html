Full description

Spital Männedorf – Your very special place of work. Whether a small “thank you” or a morning “Grüezi” – we focus on real appreciation and humanity. The proximity in our house makes everyday life uncomplicated and allows patient-centered work at the highest level. In an environment where interdisciplinary cooperation at eye level is a matter of course, we offer individual treatments from primary medical care to rehabilitation.

The nature of your activity

  • Independent perioperative care of our patients from a broad surgical spectrum
  • Assignments in the shock room, in the bear room, in the acute care service and the anesthesia consultation
  • Caring for our patients in the intensive care unit, with an intensive care physician available in the background at any time

The basis of your performance

  • Specialist title (or shortly before) in anesthesiology (FMH or equivalent)
  • Experience in current regional anesthesia procedures and knowledge in intensive care medicine
  • Initiative, motivated and team-oriented personality
  • Enjoyment of close, cooperative cooperation with all professional groups in the perioperative area

The advantages of your position

  • A challenging activity in a broad spectrum of anesthesiology and intensive care medicine
  • Working in a dynamic team with the opportunity for active participation
  • Very good traffic connection
  • Possibility and promotion of individual development

The reasons for your decision

We offer modern working conditions, up-to-date medical technologies, promote internal training and focus on professional exchange across all disciplines. The quiet location directly on the lake and the proximity to the vibrant city of Zurich round off your workplace. In addition, you can look forward to very special advantages.
