Full description

Location: Hirslanden Clinic in the Park | Zurich

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 74062

Hirslanden Klinik Im Park is a well-established private clinic on the left bank of Lake Zurich with 114 beds and a wide range of specialized and highly specialized medicine. With more than 40 different centers and institutes, it covers almost the entire range of medical services – in a family and appreciative atmosphere.

The SGNOR-certified emergency department under independent medical management plays a central role in the clinic: A good fifth of all inpatients are admitted through it. The number of our emergency patients increased by 8% in the last financial year and is growing again in the current year within the same framework. At the same time, we continue to develop medical quality in a targeted manner.

An important step here is the conversion of our cadre service model to a three-shift model. For this we are strengthening our team and looking for specialists with experience and pleasure in emergency medicine. You can expect a dedicated team in a dynamic, interdisciplinary environment with plenty of room for manoeuvre for the further development of our emergency center.

We are looking for 1. January 2027 or earlier by agreement

YOUR TASKS

  • Independent care of patients in our interdisciplinary emergency center
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with all participating specialist areas and partner doctors
  • Supervision and training of our assistant doctors
  • Cooperation in the preparation of treatment concepts and internal training
  • Participation in in-house Medical Emergency Team deployments

Your profile

  • Specialist title General Internal Medicine or Surgery (or shortly before)
  • Experience and pleasure in interdisciplinary emergency medicine, ideally with an interdisciplinary focus on clinical emergency medicine SGNOR
  • Interested in training assistant doctors
  • Reliable, team-capable personality with high social skills

Our offer

  • Attractive remuneration and versatile opportunities for further training
  • Family working atmosphere and flat hierarchies
  • Independent, interdisciplinary emergency unit with a broad, complex patient spectrum
  • Much room for manoeuvre in the further development of our emergency center
  • Training Centre for Outpatient General Internal Medicine (category IV) and Clinical Emergency Medicine SGNOR (category 3)
  • Close cooperation with specialists from all fields

A motivated, committed and multi-professional team awaits you in an advanced working environment. This is characterized by high quality of care, independence, flexibility and a sense of responsibility. We also offer our employees generous social benefits, sports offers, special insurance conditions and further education opportunities within the framework of our program.

Benefits for employees

For further information, Dr. med. Fabian Hartmann, Medical Director Emergency Department, at T or Emergency Secretariat.impark@hirslanden.ch. We look forward to your contact.
