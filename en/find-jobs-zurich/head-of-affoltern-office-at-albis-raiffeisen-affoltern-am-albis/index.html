25.09.2026 Affoltern on the Albis

Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Kelleramt-Albis, a successful and locally oriented bank with headquarters in Oberlunkhofen and headquarters in Affoltern am Albis, we are looking for a personality as head of the Affoltern am Albis office.

What do you expect?

  • They represent our bank in Affoltern on Albis and the region. You actively maintain your network and are involved in customer events, market development measures and campaigns.
  • You lead the Affoltern am Albis office and lead a team of individual customer consultants.
  • You develop your employees further and promote a performance-oriented and appreciative leadership culture.
  • You look after your own customer portfolio and accompany your customers in the topics of investing, provisioning and financing. Through targeted acquisition of new customers, you expand your customer base.

What do you bring?

  • They are at home in our region and ideally rooted in the Mauliamt.
  • They enjoy proactive customer contact and are willing to represent our bank to the outside world.
  • They are authentic, have good communication skills and a high level of social competence.
  • They have completed banking education as well as higher bank-specific training.
  • You have several years of experience in customer consulting in the areas of investment, provision and financing.
  • You have proven experience in managing employees.

You enjoy contact with people and like to take responsibility? I look forward to your application documents. Manuel Huber Member of the bank management Why Raiffeisen?
