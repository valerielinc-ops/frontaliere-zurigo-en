Original description

Under the motto "As diverse as you", the club school is the inspiring meeting place to experience meaningful leisure activities. It offers an attractive range of language, movement and creativity courses – thanks to the support of the Migros culture percentage. Support our course participants in developing their talents and achieving their learning goals.

Mansioni

  • Preparation, implementation and follow-up of the lessons (mode with certificate and sewing entry-level course)
  • Practice-oriented teaching design according to adult education criteria

Requirements

  • Proven teaching experience in adult education: in the relevant subject area or in a comparable function
  • Completed basic or continuing education: degree at a technical or university in the subject area to be taught (e.g. fashion design) or an equivalent professional qualification with federal recognition (EFZ apparel designer) or self-taught or in training.

  • Independent, flexible and reliable personality
  • Provide specialist knowledge varied, participant- and goal-oriented and inspire enthusiasm
  • Enthusiasm for teaching small to medium-sized groups of adults
  • German (very good knowledge)
