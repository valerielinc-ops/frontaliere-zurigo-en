Project Manager: Civil Engineering Your Contribution with Us

  • Overall management of demanding projects in civil engineering with high technical complexity
  • Technical and personnel leadership of project teams as well as professional management of all internal and external interfaces
  • Ensuring efficient project execution while adhering to cost, schedule, quality and safety requirements
  • Responsibility for proactive contract, risk and change order management
  • Proactive, solution-oriented collaboration with clients, authorities and other stakeholders
  • Strategic resource planning as well as accompanying construction execution until successful handover

What Counts for Us

  • Several years of experience as a project manager in civil engineering
  • Successful management of complex construction projects as lead project manager or experienced site manager with significant investment volume
  • Higher technical education in construction as a civil engineer or an equivalent qualification
  • Strong leadership skills and communication competence in dealing with diverse stakeholders
  • Decisiveness, negotiation skills, assertiveness and a high degree of personal responsibility
  • Fluent German language skills complete your profile
