Original description

Tasks

  • Personal and professional advice and support of our customers: inside
  • Performing haircuts, colorations and stylings at a high professional level
  • Recommendation and sale of high-quality hair care and styling products
  • Support of the management in the organization and coordination of the salon day
  • Co-responsibility for team management, operational planning and the achievement of common goals
  • Coaching and professional development of the Teamkolleg:innen
  • Taking over administrative tasks and accompanying our learners in everyday work

Requirements

  • Completed training as a Coiffeur:in EFZ
  • At least three years of professional experience
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Passion for the Coiffeur craft and joy of dealing with people
  • Willingness to take on responsibility, as well as a good overview also in a lively salon everyday life
  • Reliable, independent and team-oriented working methods
  • Modules 1 and 2 are advantageous, but not a requirement

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Supercompulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business meetings.
