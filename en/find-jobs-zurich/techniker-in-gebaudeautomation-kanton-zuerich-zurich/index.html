With us, you take on the technical and technical responsibility for the building automation of around 200 cantonal objects. As a central point of contact for GA topics, you ensure smooth operation, control the integration of state-of-the-art MSR systems and continuously develop our standards. Take the opportunity to contribute your GA expertise to a manageable, dynamic team and to set sustainable accents in our building infrastructure. Their tasks

  • Support, optimization and further development of building automation systems (automation and management level) across the entire real estate portfolio
  • Technical support for the connection and integration of building automation systems into the higher-level cantonal building management system
  • Ensuring smooth operation and technical support for commissioning, conversion, maintenance and complex failures
  • Technical interface with supplier companies and the Office for Informatics
  • Collaboration with the BNO and security organisation Your profile
  • Completed basic technical training (e.g. automatic technician, electrical installer, building informatician) or a comparable qualification
  • In-depth expertise in building automation as well as experience with common communication protocols (especially BACnet)
  • Proactive and systematic approach to complex tasks as well as independent, efficient and reliable working methods
