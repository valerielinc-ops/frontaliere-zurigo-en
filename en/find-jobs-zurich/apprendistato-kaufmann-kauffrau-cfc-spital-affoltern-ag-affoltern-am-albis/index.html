Full description

As a future logistician EFZ or future logistician EFZ, you work in our small team in the procurement department. You take goods of all kinds, check them and prepare them for storage in the central warehouse or delivery to internal and external customers. You work with medical products, office supplies and many other products of the diverse needs of a hospital. In the field of warehouses, you manage our products in our storage system, in compliance with regulations, e.g. for loading shelves. You check the stocks regularly and help to ensure an optimal flow of goods incoming and outgoings. It is also part of your varied tasks to dispose of packaging and waste in accordance with environmental regulations. During your training at the hospital you will receive Affoltern You have the opportunity to expand the learning goals twice during two months in a well-known partner company in the Knonau Office.
