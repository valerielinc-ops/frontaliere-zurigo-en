Full description

Do you want to contribute your expertise specifically and actively shape the care? In nephrology / angiology at USZ, you play a central role in the further development of care quality and support your team in dealing with complex patient situations. >The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.

Tasks

  • You care for patients: women with complex clinical pictures
  • You actively bring specialist topics into the departmental affairs
  • You impart knowledge and pleasure in the profession with didactic skill
  • Together with the care expertise, you are responsible for the professional support and quality assurance in the department
  • You are for the team members: first contact person for technical questions

Requirements

  • Completed Bachelor's degree as a Dipl. Nursing professional FH
  • At least two years of professional experience in the acute hospital and the willingness to complete in-depth training in nursing
  • You bring very good communication skills and a quick understanding
  • You feel comfortable in quickly changing situations and can set priorities
  • As a cooperative and constructive team member, you take on a role model and together with the management and vocational training are jointly responsible for the department

We offer

The extension of the department offers insight into other departments outside nephrology and the opportunity to actively participate in a growing regular team and to shape a common team culture. Our department specializes in peritoneal dialysis, hemodialysis takes place in another department. Two experienced heads of department and an experienced shared governance team from education and management are looking forward to working with you and supporting you in everyday work. We promote an appreciative and self-determined working environment

We know that you have other priorities besides work. That is why it is important to us that we can tailor our work plans to your wishes in the best possible way. In order to ensure a sound training, you should be able to set up a minimum of 80% for the first 3 months.
