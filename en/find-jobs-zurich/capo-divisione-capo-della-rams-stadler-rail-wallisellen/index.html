PROFILE

  • Completed technical degree (ETH, TU, FH or equivalent qualification)
  • Experience in the field of Functional SafetyKnowledge of railway standards (e.g. EN 50126, EN 50716, EN 50129, TS 50701, ISO 27001, ISO 9001) and/or signalling systems is an advantage
  • Business-fluent German and English
  • Open, communicative, resilient, and flexible personality with an independent, goal-oriented, and forward-looking approach to work
  • Strong motivation to familiarize oneself with new technologies, development processes, and toolsStrong analytical, organizational, and leadership skills

TASKS

As a member of the management team, the position is responsible for leading the Stadler Signalling RAMS Department with 14 employees.

The scope of responsibility includes

  • RAMS Management
  • Validation
  • Verification
  • Information and Cyber Security
  • Safety Assurance
  • Quality Management
  • General homologation and approval topics

The role ensures the continuous development of the team and its competencies.

Responsibilities also include organizing the department, planning and prioritizing work packages, assigning tasks, and monitoring progress to ensure timely and high-quality execution.

The department is represented in leadership meetings and project/order reviews.

The position contributes to strategic decision-making processes as a member of expert groups and cross-functional committees.

Furthermore, the role represents the RAMS Department and Stadler Signalling towards assessors, authorities, customers, at public events, and within the Stadler Group.
