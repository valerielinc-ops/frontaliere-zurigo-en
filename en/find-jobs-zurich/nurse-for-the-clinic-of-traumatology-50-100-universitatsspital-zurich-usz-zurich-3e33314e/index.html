Our heart beats for the treatment of the seriously injured. As a top team in the leading trauma center, we provide top performance every day. A motivated, humorous and helpful team awaits you. In our varied working environment, we look after complex, versatile and challenging patient situations. The introduction is carefully and individually designed. Traumatology specific knowledge is taught to you on the job. Nothing stands in the way of your further development either. A wide internal training offer is available to you and subject-specific training is regularly carried out.

Your main tasks

  • The focus is on the comprehensive care of traumatological (incident surgery) patients
  • you actively shape the patient process and are committed to the further development of the nursing profession
  • You work closely together with your teammates in leadership, education and specialist development
  • You are happy to share your knowledge with our learners and students

Your profile

  • You are already a qualified nurse or are about to complete your studies
  • in a varied field of expertise with rapidly changing patient situations, you really flourish
  • you are reflected, like to develop further and taking on responsibility is a matter of course for you
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation is important to you and you maintain your professional relationships
