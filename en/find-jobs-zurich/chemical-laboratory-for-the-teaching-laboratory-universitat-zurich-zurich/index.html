Institute of Chemistry

Job as per 1. January 2027 or by arrangement, perpetual

The Institute of Chemistry of the University of Zurich consists of more than 20 research groups and comprises about 300 employees and students who conduct intensive research and teaching in the fields of life sciences, energy research and other chemical fields. The institute also has a teaching laboratory, where apprentices are trained as laboratory assistants in the EFZ field of chemistry. In addition, external learners are also trained in regular intercompany courses. In cooperation with the head of the teaching laboratory, you supervise and train the learners.

Tasks

  • Instruction and supervision of learners in the laboratory (practical tasks with a focus on synthesis, analysis, safety, equipment and logistics)
  • Evaluation and discussion of results of practical tasks with the learners
  • Imparting practical theory
  • Correction and review of laboratory journals
  • Compilation, implementation and evaluation of examinations (theoretical and practical examinations)
  • Meetings and instructions individually and in groups
  • Enforcement of regulations in the teaching laboratory (flag laboratory) and control of compliance with laboratory rules (safety)

Profile

  • Completed training as a chemical laboratory:in EFZ + possibly Further education in chemistry
  • Basic course for vocational trainers: desired, familiar with the Swiss education system
  • several years of professional experience
  • solid experience in synthesis and analytical methods
  • First experience in methodology/didactics and vocational education
  • very good German and good English skills in word and writing
  • good knowledge of common software (MS Office range and Chemdraw)
  • independent, goal-oriented, forward-thinking, capable of team and conflict, very reliable
  • role model and motivator:in for young people
  • Joy in dealing with learners is essential
  • Convincing, sovereign appearance and an assertive personality
