Role overview

Balgrist University Hospital is one of the most renowned orthopaedic clinics in Switzerland with an affiliated centre for paraplegia and international recognition.

The radiology team makes specific imaging of the entire musculoskeletal system and supports diagnostics significantly thanks to the latest equipment.

  • Independent performance of radiological examinations rotating on the various modalities: four conventional X-ray machines, one EOS full body scanner, two fluoroscopy, one ultrasound, two mobiles for OPS and IPS, two CTs and five MRIs (Siemens 1.5T and 3T)
