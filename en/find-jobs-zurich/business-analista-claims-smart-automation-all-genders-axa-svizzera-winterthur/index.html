Shape your future – together with us!

Do you want to actively shape the future and develop simple and customer-oriented solutions together? If yes - then you are right with us! We offer an agile working environment in which thinking is required and teamwork and flexibility are of great importance. To achieve our ambitious goals in the field of claims, we are looking for you as a business analyst.

Support us on the way to a digital future - committed, courageous and open to new things. A job with a lot of personal responsibility and direct impact for our customers. We look forward to you and your participation - together we make the difference.

In the area of customer operations, we design the individual experiences of our customers in the event of damage and performance. They are our focus and together with them we find the right solution. In this way, our customer-first mentality ensures maximum customer satisfaction.

This is what awaits you

  • Together with our French implementation partner and internal stakeholders, you will develop the new claim management system of AXA for our customers and employees.
  • You will analyze the processes and technical requirements, take over the requirements engineering and are actively involved in the testing
  • As a member of an agile, cross-functional team with software developers, you will work on target and/or Target images
  • With your open mindset, you actively and responsibly advance larger topics and coordinate their stakeholders

You bring that with you

  • You question the existing, submit proposals for sustainable solutions, be courageous, pragmatic and proactive
  • You are an engaged person who is able to communicate proactively and effectively in the team and with stakeholders
  • You get structured to new topics and can quickly capture complexity
  • You have experience in business analysis or requirements engineering in large organizations and are methodologically strong (IREB certification is advantageous)
  • You enjoy working with software developers and practical experience in agile teams is an advantage
  • You communicate stylishly in German and can communicate well in English
  • Non-life insurance knowledge and financial industry knowledge

This is what we offer you We are convinced: Satisfied employees provide the best services. That’s why we invest in benefits that offer noticeable benefits – beyond the usual.

Flexibility and freedom: Smart Working (flexible working model) Annual working hours, 5 weeks holidays (from 50 years 6 weeks), holiday gift 2 weeks every 5 years, holiday purchase up to 10 additional days/per year

Job and family: family-friendly meeting hours, 22 weeks of maternity leave, 8 weeks of leave for the other parent, crèche subsidies, company family allowances, days off for caring for relatives and in challenging, personal situations

Precautions and insurance: Attractive pension fund with far-reaching non-compulsory benefits including choice savings options, up to 30% discount on insurance products

Flexible Benefits: AXA Shareplan (employee participation), CHF 500 annually on the advantage platform Swibeco, discounted lunch or lunch card, Smart Working flat rate CHF 200 per year

Mobility: refund for half-tax, Reka-Pay up to CHF 1’000 per year (20% discount), fleet discount, free e-charging station, AXA rental bicycles

Healthy balance in everyday work: Free check-ups, annual health weeks, a wide range of exercise offers (online/offline), massage offers, rest rooms, employee care, online health portal, various seminars/events around health prevention & mental health

Development: 3 days of individual learning time, free coaching, wide range of learning & development opportunities, financial participation in further education, promotion of internal mobility, talent development offers, seminars for employees 55+ (AXA Horizon), engagement in communities on various topics, participation in innovation projects (Innoday, AXA Hackathon, UnConference), 2 days of volunteering (environment & social affairs)
