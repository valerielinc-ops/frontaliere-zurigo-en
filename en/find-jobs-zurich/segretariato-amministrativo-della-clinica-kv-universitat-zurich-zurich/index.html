Centre for Dentistry

Job opening immediately or by arrangement, temporary

The Center for Dentistry of the University of Zurich (ZZM) is an internationally recognized clinic, training and research center. In six specialized clinics and one institute, dentists work at the forefront of knowledge and in interdisciplinary teams to achieve the highest quality in patient treatment, teaching and research. In the Clinic for Oral Surgery, patients are treated in the entire field of dentoalveolar outpatient surgery and implantology. Furthermore, a daily mucous membrane consultation session takes place. The clinic provides its services according to modern quality-related guidelines. We are looking for a committed personality for our clinic as a disease representative.

Tasks

  • Contact person for various tasks in day-to-day business
  • Organisation and preparation of meetings
  • Completion of general correspondence and various administrative tasks
  • Processing and forwarding of inquiries by insurance companies, hospitals, etc.
  • Completion and maintenance of reporting incl. Controlling
  • Documentation and archiving of patient information in electronic form (reports, imaging, etc.)

Profile

  • Completed training as a dental assistant:in EFZ;
  • Further training as a dental secretary: in or another training with a commercial background is advantageous
  • Ideally, work experience in a comparable function
  • Strong organisational skills as well as a structured, efficient and independent way of working
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English are advantageous
  • Experienced handling of MS Office, in particular Outlook (term coordination) and Excel (creation and evaluation of lists)
  • Reliable and committed personality with a friendly and professional attitude
  • High resilience, strong social skills and team skills

We offer you interesting, versatile and challenging tasks under cantonal employment conditions. The workplace is located in the heart of Zurich with good connections to public transport. We look forward to your full application dossier.
