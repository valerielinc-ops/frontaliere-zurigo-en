University Library

Job opening 01.06.2027, temporary

The University Library of Zurich (UB) together with the Central Library of Zurich ensures the library coverage of the University of Zurich. Their offer is primarily aimed at the researchers, lecturers and students of UZH. It is also open to the public.

Tasks

During your work, you will get to know all the essential areas of a large scientific library. Accompanying you will complete the MAS program in Library and Information Science from August 2027 to May 2029.

We offer

  • a well-founded practical training in various departments of the library
  • Design possibilities and independent projects in the spectrum of training content
  • Participation in the MAS programme in Library and Information Science (https://www.biw.uzh.ch/en/masbiw.html), 50 % of the tuition fees
  • Additional external internship
  • an interesting and varied task with a workplace in the center of the city of Zurich

Profile

  • University degree (level Bachelor, Master or equivalent)
  • Significant interest in information technologies, digital media and current developments in science and research
  • Good active foreign language skills in English
  • Very good expression in words and writing
  • High team and cooperation ability, service orientation, commitment and flexibility
  • practical experience or internship in an information and documentation facility
  • Experience in scientific project work and/or with scientific work in a university context is advantageous
