Location: Basel, Zurich Work Pattern Percentage: 80-100% Help our clients successfully transform their business through technology Our Deloitte SAP Order to Cash consultants own the capabilities which are required to evaluate, develop and implement a wide range of SAP integrated systems solutions which are required to cover our clients the business for business needs of our clients. They determine clients' business needs, create customized SAP solutions, and smoothly integrate SAP applications with existing IT infrastructure. The SAP team delivers business focussed transformation programmes supported by process design, people solutions and technology. We focus on realizing operational improvements and efficiencies across the business operations. As SAP Global Platinum partner, we closely partner with SAP with the most renowned technology providers globally, Deloitte and combine SAP their tools and functionalities with our proven expertise to offer the best solutions to our clients. How you can make an impact

  • You will support exciting large-scale projects in Switzerland and abroad
  • You will accompany clients from the conceptual design of initial ideas to successful implementation in operations.
  • You will be working on the assumption of sub-project manager tasks within the scope of new implementations
  • You will design and extend SAP standard software solutions
  • You will be working on the development of customer applications and the integration of various software solutions Who we are looking for
  • You have a University degree (Master's, MBA or PhD) and at least 2 year's professional experience in a SAP environment; exposure to S/4HANA is an advantage
  • You have a good understanding of and practical experience in one or more of the SAP Order to Cash domains such as: S/4 HANA Expert Track (Basic Functions, Sales and Shipping) S/4 HANA Service Management S/4 HANA for Advanced ATP Global Trade Management and Compliance (GTS) SPA billing and Revenue Innovation Management (BRIM) OTC Advanced Intercompany SAP Advanced Track and Trace (ATTP) SAP Commerce Cloud Plan To Make domains, such as: SAP S4HANA (Materials Management, Inventory Management) SAP Extended Warehouse Management (EWM) SAP Transportation Management (TM) SAP Production Planning (PP) SAP MES SAP Quality Management (QM) SAP Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) SAP Enterprise Asset Management (EAM) SAP Intelligent Asset Management (IAM) solutions (e.g., ECC, EWM, MDG, MM, PP, GTS, Ariba)
  • You have the skills acting as an SAP enabler and communicator between technical experts and the business
  • You work independently, take the initiative and are happy taking on responsibility. You have excellent technical and analytical skills, are a reliable team player and have experience managing staff
  • You are happy to travel in the area of Switzerland and would also be prepared to work abroad, and you communicate confidently in German and English.
