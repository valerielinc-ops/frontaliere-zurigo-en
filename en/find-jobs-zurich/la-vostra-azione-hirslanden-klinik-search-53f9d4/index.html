Location: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Occupation by: from now on

Type of employment: Temporary

Reference number: 6451

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

ITS TASKS

  • Care of all patients under the supervision of specialists as well as in close exchange with the post-treatment clinicians and attending physicians of all disciplines on our lively interdisciplinary emergency unit with more than 12,000 entries annually

YOUR PROFILE

  • Ability to engage in team-oriented, interdisciplinary collaboration in the interest of optimal patient care
  • One or more years of clinical experience ideally
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Open and pleasant communication style

We are accredited as a training institution of SGAIM for General, Outpatient Internal Medicine, Category IV and SGNOR for Clinical Emergency Medicine, Category 2. It is possible to deepen and apply knowledge in focused emergency sonography. The period of employment is usually 15 months. We attach great importance to your further education so that you are optimally prepared for the future challenges of the medical profession both as a hospital doctor and as a family doctor. In addition, we offer you work in a highly qualified, dynamic team, advanced employment conditions, regular internal and external training, flexible part-time models and good social benefits.

For candidates for the SGNOR Interdisciplinary Focus (ISP) in Clinical Emergency Medicine, there is the possibility of a 1-year post in anesthesia.

As employees who have to move completely for their work, you receive four additional paid free days (except specialists and cadre doctors) for a 100% workload. With a reduced workload, the days off reduce accordingly. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from equivalent financial compensation.

Our benefits for employees

For additional information, KD Dr. med. Abraham Licht, Chief Medical Emergency Center, under T gladly available. We look forward to your contact.

Please note that for data protection reasons we cannot consider paper dossiers (will not be returned) nor applications by email. Please apply exclusively online via this tool.
