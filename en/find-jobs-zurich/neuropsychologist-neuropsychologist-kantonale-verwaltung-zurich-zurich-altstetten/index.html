Full description

Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Prison and Reintegration Psychiatric Psychological Service Neuropsychologist 40 - 80% Prison and Reintegration (JuWe) Zurich is an office of the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs of the Canton of Zurich and employs around 1,400 people from a wide range of professions. JuWe is primarily responsible for executing court sentences and carrying out pre-trial detention. For employees of the Office, the prevention of recidivism and the successful social reintegration of people who have committed crimes is of particular importance. The Psychiatric Psychological Service (PPD) is one of the eight main departments of JuWe. As one of the largest institutions for forensic psychotherapy and psychiatry in Switzerland, he specializes in the recidivism-preventive treatment of violent and sexual offenders in detention and freedom. The treatment offer ranges from court-ordered measures to therapies of psychiatric primary care. The Neuropsychology Service is under development and is developing into a central specialist centre for neuropsychological issues within the PPD. That speaks to you.

  • Independent planning, implementation and documentation of test diagnostics in forensic clients in adolescence and adulthood
  • Preparation of neuropsychological reports
  • Participation in interdisciplinary case discussions and close cooperation, especially with psychotherapeutic and psychiatric colleagues
  • Participation in the further development of concepts, processes and professional standards
  • If interested, take on professional and human management tasks This is you
  • You have a university degree in psychology as well as a Swiss-recognised or equivalent-recognised continuing education in neuropsychology or the specialist title of specialist psychologist / specialist psychologist for neuropsychology FSP
  • You have several years of practical experience in neuropsychological diagnosis and are familiar with the independent planning, implementation, evaluation and documentation of neuropsychological clarifications as well as the preparation of corresponding reports
  • Experience in the diagnosis of ADHD and Autism Spectrum Disorders (ASD) is beneficial. Experience with Neurofeedback is also welcome
  • You work carefully and accurately and have a high quality in written reporting
  • You are interested in neuropsychological issues in a forensic context and enjoy working in an interdisciplinary capacity
  • You want to take on responsibility and help shape the neuropsychology specialist service under construction This is waiting for you
  • A collaborative working environment in which appreciation, openness and genuine interest are lived together
  • A demanding job in an interdisciplinary team that systematically reflects on the course of clarification and treatment and develops it together
  • Various further education and development opportunities in a further education institution for forensic psychiatry and psychotherapy (category A) recognized by the Swiss Institute for Medical Further Education and Training (SIWF)
