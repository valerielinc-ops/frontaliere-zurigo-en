Full description

Herdern is the hub of the Migros Zurich Cooperative. For you, safety comes first and you want to contribute your expertise in construction projects. Then we are sure to offer you a good platform where you can prove your skills.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Zurich, operations headquarters Herdern, Pfingstweidstasse 101, 8005 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Responsibility for the leadership and coordination of a reception team of 6 employees
  • Responsibility for compliance and implementation of structural, technical and organisational fire protection
  • Taking over the 24/7 security stakes to ensure safe operation (about 10 weeks per year)
  • Planning, coordination and management of projects and orders in the area of the logistics platform and the entire operations centre
  • Responsible for cost budgets, service provision, deadline compliance and quality
  • Competent advice and support for different internal and external stakeholders
  • Safety Officer*r Occupational safety and health protection of the department

Requirements

  • 2-3 years of professional experience: as an electrical technician, company specialist or company technician HF
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): As an electrical engineer or other basic technical training
  • Engaged and winning personality with high resilience, strong social skills and team skills
  • Self-responsible and solution-oriented working method combined with networked and cross-divisional thinking
  • Several years of experience in self-responsible management and management of projects
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central location: Working in Zurich West
  • Flexible working hours: reconciling private and professional requirements
  • Childcare: In the in-house crèche "Wirbelschloss"
  • Handy subscription: You can look forward to special conditions for the Handy subscription
  • Occupational pension provision: Excellent pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Social benefits: Fair social benefits
  • Cumulus Points: You get additional Cumulus Points when shopping
  • Migros Bank: Preferred interest on private and investment account, free account management, discounted mortgages and more
  • Leisure and cultural offers: You can benefit from discounted tickets at selected cultural events

Contatto

Application and contact Manuel Frei Recruitment partners

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Discussion with case presentation: Specific expertise is examined on the basis of a case or other assessment instruments. After processing, the result of the leader is presented and then discussed together. Usually, the team is also represented at the presentation. As a rule, the conversations are conducted by the superior, without a representative from the HR. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week

Note for personnel service providers For this position, we only consider direct applications.
