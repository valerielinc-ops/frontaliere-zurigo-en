Full description

##

Are you apprenticed as a retail specialist EFZ / EBA Consumer Electronics? Do you think you're going to eat? Because apply now for Usbildig bi MediaMarkt. Let's go! During your varied training, you will get a comprehensive insight into the processes of retailing in the field of consumer electronics and are always up to date with the latest technology. You clarify the wishes and needs of our customers and inform them about functions and operation of various technical devices. Our vocational trainers are at your side with questions about training and accompany you on the way to a successful teaching degree. Your practical training is complemented by the vocational school and additional intercompany courses. There is also the possibility to complete the training as basic vocational training with professional certificate (EBA).

Are you open, like to approach people and get excited about consumer electronics? Perfect – then we are looking for you!

  • You advise customers competently and find the right solution.
  • Selling is fun – from products to accessories to warranties and insurance.
  • You present products attractively and provide an attractive sales area.
  • Motivation, reliability and team spirit are just as much a part of this for you as a well-groomed appearance.
  • You speak very good German.

Sounds like you? Then apply online for an apprenticeship with us and submit the following documents:

  • Letter of motivation
  • CVC
  • Certificates
  • Multicheck or interlocking test

After your application, a short introduction video follows. Convince us of this, we invite you to a taster day in October.

We are

At MediaMarkt, you are part of a team that achieves more together. We exchange tips, pack together and laugh about small everyday moments – this energy makes our everyday work special. Many colleagues especially appreciate the feeling of moving big things together and having fun. Technology excites us, but the team behind it makes the difference. Want to know more about working at MediaMarkt and what we stand for? Along here!

We believe that you are best when you can be yourself. We value diversity and equal opportunities and therefore welcome all applications!

Your extras

On top to your monthly salary from CHF 800 (gross/full-time); Adjustment of CHF 200 according to apprenticeship year) we still have a lot of benefits for you:

  • Employee discounts: our products are available for you at particularly attractive employee prices
  • Cool deals: e.g. personnel discount on repairs and display protection, product promotions, reduced additional insurance with the health insurance, discounts on corporate benefits (all around fashion, leisure, car, travel, sports, tickets, household, etc.)
  • Participation in success: your commitment pays off – even as a learner you benefit from a success-dependent participation according to our compensation model
  • Employees recruit employees: Get new colleagues on board & secure premium
  • Learning Day: Once a year, our learning event takes place with a varied and exciting program
  • We celebrate you: with small surprises, vouchers at special moments (e.g. birthday), and always great employee parties
  • Cost coverage: We cover the costs for your schoolbooks and pay you CHF 250 one-time to your laptop
  • Holidays: With us you benefit from 6 weeks holidays per year
  • Learn, grow, take off: Whether sales consultant, teamlead or store manager, after your apprenticeship you can really take off with us
