The Sonnweid is considered one of the leading institutions for people with dementia. In our competence center, our 330 employees from different disciplines serve and maintain 174 residents. Home doctors and age psychiatrists ensure medical care. The differentiated housing and care concept is adapted to the stage of the disease. Our semi-acute station offers a temporary home for people with particularly challenging behavior, while the day/night station relieves carers with day and holiday stays. Our mission, which focuses on the needs of dementia, forms the basis for our daily action. With joy and conviction, we make every effort to make the residents feel comfortable and at home in the Sonnweid.
