Your team

Corporate tax is not just about helping companies comply with regulations. We advise nationally or internationally active companies taking into account relevant tax regulations and current developments. In this way, we help our customers to build efficient structures, take into account the tax impact of different business models and avoid tax risks. As part of our International Tax Team, you will advise Swiss and international companies on complex cross-border tax issues and participate in challenging national and international projects. Zurich By agreement 100% Tax, Legal and Workforce Everyone welcome ID: 764969WD

Your impact

  • Advise Swiss and international companies on complex issues of international corporate tax law as well as cross-border structures, expansions and reorganizations.
  • Analyze national and international tax regulations, assess tax implications and develop practical solutions for our customers.
  • Coordinate international projects together with specialists from the tax and legal department and other PwC networks worldwide.
  • Support challenging, often cross-border, business restructuring, takeover and sales projects. Bring your know-how to contribute successfully to the success of the project.
  • Maintain and develop trusting customer relationships through high-quality consulting, entrepreneurial thinking and proactive communication.
  • Deepen your expertise in international tax law and increasingly take responsibility for projects, mandates and the professional development of younger team members.

Your skill set

  • Master's degree in economics, business administration, law or a comparable discipline.
  • Ongoing further development to/from dipl. Tax expert: with planned completion in 2027 or 2028.
  • 2 to 3 years of professional experience in international corporate tax law, ideally in a tax consultancy, lawyer or audit firm.
  • In-depth knowledge of Swiss and international corporate tax law as well as interest in current tax developments.
  • Analytical and solution-oriented thinking with the ability to process complex issues in an understandable and practical manner.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in words and writing
  • Other language skills are a plus.
  • Strong communication and team skills as well as enjoyment of working with customers in an international environment.
  • Engaged, independent and curious personality with a high level of initiative and the desire to take on responsibility.
