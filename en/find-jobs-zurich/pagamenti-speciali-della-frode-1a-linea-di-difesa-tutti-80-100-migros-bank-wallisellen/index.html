Full description

With us, you take responsibility for the detection and prevention of fraud. Share your knowledge with a dedicated team! We are looking for you: A personality with an eye for detail and a sense of justice. With passion and innovative approaches, you strengthen the security of our bank. Come on – at the bank, which has been putting people first with 1,800 employees since 1958.

Mansioni

  • You edit hits in the anti-fraud tool and rejected transactions to identify and prevent potential fraud.
  • You analyze fraud cases by tracking crimes and evaluating log data and creating reports on fraud types and trends.
  • You inform customers in writing and personally about suspicious or fraudulent activities and work together to develop solutions.
  • You perform 1st Line controls to ensure quality assurance and compliance with internal standards.
  • You will continuously optimize and complement processes to increase the efficiency and effectiveness of fraud prevention.

Requirements

  • 3 years: fraud payment transactions / fraud prevention
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): Commercial basic training with further training in the field of compliance or risk management
  • You have in-depth experience in payment transactions, fraud prevention or in a comparable function.
  • You have a strong analytical ability to think and understand complex processes and connections.
  • You have a high IT affinity and are confident in dealing with relevant tools and systems.
  • You are strong in communication, a real team player and master German, French and Italian in word and writing at a very good level.
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • French (very good knowledge)
  • Italian (very good knowledge)
