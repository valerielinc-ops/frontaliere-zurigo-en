Full description

This workplace includes a publicly accessible self-service restaurant with indoor and outdoor seating. The meeting place in Dietikon creates an inviting gathering place for employees and external guests. From Monday to Friday, our team delights an average of 85 guests here with a varied offering – from a small à la carte selection and buffet salads to regular promotional weeks. As the sole chef / cook: (50%) on the late shift, you are responsible for the kitchen and serve up delicious dishes from Monday to Friday. Your workload is 50%, and your working hours are from 16:30 to 21:15. You will start on January 4, 2027, or by agreement, and benefit from free parking or a shuttle from Dietikon station.The position is initially limited to 3 months, but there is an option to extend it. Your menu of tasks:

  • You prepare a varied selection of dishes and ensure smooth operations at the serving lines
  • You prepare your station, ensure the mise en place is ready and plate the meals during service
  • You ensure that supplies are replenished at the serving lines
  • You manage the stock and handle food and materials carefully
  • You ensure cleanliness and order and comply with the ZFV quality standards as well as the hygiene and occupational safety guidelines
  • You operate the cash register and complete the daily closing Your recipe for success:
  • You have completed vocational training as a chef: EFZ
  • 2-4 years of professional experience as a sole chef / cook:
  • You work independently, in a structured and efficient manner, and maintain an overview even during service
  • You impress with your open communication with guests
  • You enjoy working with fresh ingredients and value quality and an appealing presentation of the dishes
  • You work cleanly and reliably and consistently comply with hygiene regulations
  • You are flexible, enjoy lending a hand and actively contribute to day-to-day work
  • You have very good German language skills What we offer you: We create teams that support one another and in which you are encouraged. We work together and focus on everything that is good for us. Among other things, we place great importance on pay transparency and equal pay. For this position, with a 100% workload, depending on your experience and qualifications, we offer an annual salary between CHF 56'000 and CHF 63'000 (incl. 13. Monthly salary).
