Full description

In our governance team at the Triemli site, we are looking for 1. October or by agreement, limited to one year with option of renewal, a proactive, committed and communicative personality.

Tasks

  • You will gain insights into the diverse topics of our governance office and support us in developing our services reliably and service-oriented.
  • You work on varied topics around process management, quality, feedback, risks, environment and document management and support the team in day-to-day business.
  • You take responsibility for your own tasks, participate in ongoing projects and gain practical insights into the various areas of governance.
  • You help to make our business processes clear and professional.
  • You support our process managers in the maintenance, optimization and further development of business processes and participate in the recording and modeling of processes.
  • You work with data and modern tools, prepare information and help build dashboards, such as feedback and patient satisfaction.
  • You help to make developments visible and to create well-founded decision-making bases for different stakeholders.

Profile

  • Ongoing or completed studies (uni, FH, HF)
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Secure use of digital tools (MS Office, SharePoint knowledge is an advantage)
  • Health experience or knowledge is beneficial
  • Strong communication capability
  • Analytical and Structured Thinking
  • Enjoying varied tasks
  • Service orientation and teamwork

We offer

We offer you more than just a job! Our city hospital world is characterized by an open du-culture, an uncomplicated, personal togetherness and the necessary touch of humor in everyday life. As a team of over 120 professional groups, we work every day in very different ways for the well-being of our patients. You will work on the various governance topics in practice, gain deep insights into the management and further development of the city hospital and the Swiss health care system. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions and excellent social benefits from the city of Zurich.

About us

With over 37'000 inpatients and around 450,000 outpatients per year, Zurich City Hospital is one of the top 10 hospitals in Switzerland. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,600 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day. In the management staff / governance department, we provide strategic and operational support to the city hospital in the areas of process management, quality management, feedback management, risk management, environmental management and document management.
