Full description

Where we work, Zurich moves. Join us in the future.

For finance and accounting we are looking for an independent and motivated intern in finance and projects 50 - 100%, temporarily until 31.12.2027.

Tasks

  • You take on tasks in debt management and in account reconciliation.
  • You support the division manager in the analysis of processes and develop optimization proposals.
  • They participate in the preparation of monthly, territorial and annual accounts and the corresponding reports.
  • They independently lead smaller controlling and MIS projects.

Profile

  • Completed bachelor's degree (university/FH) or an initial education with business administration further education
  • Interest in financial topics in the field of sports promotion
  • Joy of learning, networked thinking and good analytical skills
  • Efficient, exact and independent working method

We offer

You have a varied and meaningful activity in a sporting environment. Exciting financial insights into the promotion of grassroots and top sport in the city of Zurich. The possibility to take responsibility and own projects. Flexible and mobile working and a healthy work-life balance.

About us

The Sports Department is a service department of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich. It is the competence centre for sports promotion and makes the sports facilities including indoor and outdoor pools available to the sports-loving population and clubs. The organisation of compulsory swimming lessons and voluntary school sports are also core tasks of the Sports Office. With targeted information, the Sports Office motivates more movement and advises and supports the sports organizations.
