Pricing Actuary (m/w/d) - L&H CE Regional Pricing Team — Swiss Re

Full description

For our Regional Pricing Team Life & Health Continental Europe we are looking for a committed actuary with experience in pricing life and health insurance risks.

If you enjoy combining actuarial expertise with customer contact, strategic issues and commercial aspects while working in an international environment, then we should get to know each other.

About the role

Acquired as Pricing Actuary You play a central role in the calculation of life and health reinsurance business in our European markets with a particular focus on Germany.

You bring high-quality actuarial expertise to cross-functional teams and customer projects and make an important contribution both to the development of new business and to the support and further development of existing portfolios.

You will work closely with colleagues from the fields of underwriting, client management, reporting and product development and develop a deep understanding of the life and health reinsurance market.

Your tasks include in particular

  • Development and review of calculation bases and reinsurance conditions for new and existing business
  • Implementation and interpretation of data analysis on loss development and portfolio performance
  • Ensure profitability and sustainability of existing portfolios in collaboration with reporting and experience studies teams
  • Collaboration with customers and internal stakeholders on pricing and product development initiatives
  • Providing in-depth actuarial expertise in structuring and implementing reinsurance solutions
  • Participation in best practices as well as support for interns and working students in technical issues
  • Self-responsible participation in projects and active participation in team and regional initiatives

About the team

Our Regional Pricing Team is part of Life & Health Continental Europe and responsible for pricing reinsurance business across a wide range of products in various markets, including Germany.

We work in a highly networked and cross-functional environment and cooperate closely with colleagues from the areas of pricing, client management, business management, underwriting, claims and reporting.

We work in an international and diverse team characterized by mutual support and open cooperation. Curiosity, personal initiative and the common aspiration to develop high-quality solutions are our main focus.

The tasks are demanding and varied and offer the opportunity to get to know different markets, products and reinsurance solutions. You can further deepen your actuary expertise, gain new perspectives and develop yourself both professionally and personally.

About you

You are a qualified actuary (DAV, FSA, FIA or comparable qualification) or are in the final phase of your actuary training and have relevant professional experience, ideally in pricing or reinsurance.

In addition, You have the following qualifications

  • Sound understanding of reinsurance structures and/or biometric insurance products
  • very good knowledge of actuarial and statistical modelling methods
  • Experience with R, Python or similar tools is an advantage
  • Strong analytical skills as well as strong problem-solving and decision-making skills
  • Ability to drive forward projects independently and deliver high-quality results even under time pressure
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills as well as the ability to work effectively with internal and external stakeholders
  • Fluent knowledge of German and English
  • Strong customer orientation and the ability to actively support commercial discussions

Ideally, You also about

  • Experience in the German insurance or reinsurance market
  • Experience in professional support or support of trainees or working students
  • Knowledge of another European language
