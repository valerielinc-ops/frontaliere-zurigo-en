Cook 60-80

2 Spice AG — Dietlikon, Dietlikon, Sizzera

Two Spice – that's us! A creative and innovative food and lifestyle company from Zurich. Since 1990 we have been bringing fresh ideas to the table and developing unique concepts and brands that inspire. Our versatile businesses are at home at the central hotspots of different cities and stand for quality, design and pure moments of enjoyment. Welcome to the Rice Up!, which is inspired by the lively ambience of the street food markets in Asia. The bowls are prepared fresh and, if desired, in the Wok right in front of the guests.

Dive into the world of Rice Up! and become part of our team, which redefines Asian cuisine with passion and creativity. Do you want to become part of our team? Then now is your chance!

Tasks

  • Preparation of dishes according to our recipes
  • Ensure a consistently high quality of food
  • Compliance with hygiene and quality standards in the kitchen
  • Team-oriented cooperation with the kitchen team
  • Co-responsibility for orders, goods acceptance and storage management
  • Conduct of cleaning work according to HA 152 guidelines

Requirements

  • Perennial professional experience as chef/cook, preferably in Asian cuisine
  • A quality of system catering and joy in structured processes
  • Self-employed personality with passion for the cooking profession and pleasure of guest contact
  • readiness to shift, weekend and holiday services
  • German knowledge at B1 level in word and writing, other languages are a plus
