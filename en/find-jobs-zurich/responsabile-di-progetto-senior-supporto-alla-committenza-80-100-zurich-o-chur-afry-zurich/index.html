Full description

Become part of our CLIENT SUPPORT team. We manage, develop, design and plan infrastructure projects of all kinds in civil engineering and tunnelling. We accompany these projects from planning to implementation.

Take on responsibility with us as

SENIOR PROJECT MANAGER CLIENT SUPPORT 80-100% - ZURICH or CHUR

YOUR TASKS

  • You will be responsible for the successful completion of infrastructure projects, especially in national road construction
  • You will lead interdisciplinary project teams
  • You will ensure compliance with the cost, quality and deadline targets as well as the sustainability of customer projects
  • Together with the department heads, you will maintain existing and gain new contacts with public and private clients

YOUR OPPORTUNITIES

  • Exciting projects in the transport infrastructure sector
  • Working in interdisciplinary teams
  • Deepening knowledge of building owner support in the field of infrastructure
  • Flexible way of working (mobile working)

YOUR PROFILE

  • You have several years of consulting experience as a builder supporter for cantonal or national infrastructure projects
  • You would like to participate in business and technical aspects of projects, directly with the customers, and are interested in project management activities
  • You are familiar with public projects and approval procedures and have an existing network of contacts with building owners, offices and authorities or are open to getting involved in these topics
  • They are open to new ways of working
  • A very good oral and written expression in German is a matter of course for you, good English skills are an advantage
  • Your appearance is professional, you think in a solution-oriented and entrepreneurial way
  • You convince with your professional competence and open-minded personality
