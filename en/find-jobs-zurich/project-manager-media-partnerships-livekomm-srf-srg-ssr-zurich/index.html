Full description

The distribution department ensures that SRF’s offerings are published on the linear radio and TV programs, as well as on the digital channels and platforms on which our target groups operate – with the right mix of topics, in the right form, at the right time, on the right scale, with the right brand – supported by digitally oriented marketing.

What are your tasks?

Its main tasks consist of

  • They plan and realize target group-oriented events, audience activations and special SRF experiences. You take on project responsibility for more complex projects, including budget, resources and success monitoring, and work closely with internal teams and external partners.
  • They develop media partnerships and event formats in line with the SRG corporate strategy, analyze their impact and contribute their own impulses to strategic projects.
  • Negotiate and implement existing and new media partnerships
  • They coordinate internal and external interfaces, ensure targeted communication with the relevant stakeholders and act as sparring partners:

What do you bring?

  • Qualified education or training in marketing, live communication or sponsorship as well as several years of professional experience in the media, event or entertainment environment
  • In-depth experience in project management, independent and structured working methods and strategic and holistic thinking
  • Negotiation and communication strength as well as ability to communicate convincingly, securely and at eye level in interdisciplinary teams
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss event and media landscape as well as an existing network and language skills in at least one other national language at B2 level

What can you look forward to?

  • Working independently as part of a motivated team with extensive know-how
  • A varied environment with many design possibilities, space for new ideas, creativity and initiative
  • Exciting and versatile projects in an agile and innovative working environment
  • Flexible working hours with the option to work partly in the home office

What we offer

What does it take to reach the people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expert knowledge, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can contribute and develop. For the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: Welcome to the world of SRF!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, promote creativity and offer a lot of design space for new and high-quality products.
  • Shaping digitalization: Quality journalism is also digital for us. That’s why we explore new possibilities and experiment with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-medial. We design on the pulse of time and on the nerve of the audience.
  • Being human: We meet each other collegially, work independently and shape our working atmosphere individually. Our success is based on the personal strengths as well as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programs. In doing so, we act respectfully and independently; in this way we promote opinion-forming and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.

Requirements

  • Qualifizierte Aus- oder Weiterbildung im Bereich Marketing, Live-Kommunikation oder Sponsoring sowie mehrjährige Berufserfahrung im Medien-, Event- oder Unterhaltungsumfeld

  • Fundierte Erfahrung im Projektmanagement, selbstständige und strukturierte Arbeitsweise sowie strategisches und ganzheitliches Denken

  • Verhandlungs- und Kommunikationsstärke sowie Fähigkeit, in interdisziplinären Teams überzeugend, sicher und auf Augenhöhe zu kommunizieren

  • Gute Kenntnisse der Schweizer Veranstaltungs- und Medienlandschaft sowie ein bestehendes Netzwerk und Sprachkenntnisse in mindestens einer weiteren Landessprache auf B2-Niveau
