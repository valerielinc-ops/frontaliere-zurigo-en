Junior Software Engineer Java / Spring

  • 80-100%
  • Permanent employment

Your tasks

  • You are part of our Scrum team (according to SAFe) and participate in the development, maintenance and integration of applications based on Java, Spring, Spring Boot and Vaadin
  • You are responsible for the development, expansion and 3rd level support of selected applications of our own ERP core system with approximately 400 daily users
  • You use the following technologies: Java, SQL, Java-Swing, Vaadin, Angular and various AI tools
  • AI plays an important role for efficiency in your day-to-day development: during development, testing and documentation
  • You use a combination of test, test automation and plausibility checks for effective quality assurance
  • You will work with architects, developers and DevOps from different Scrum teams to design and implement the upcoming, important evolutionary step of the ERP core system.

You bring with you

  • Completion of university or technical school or equivalent education
  • Initial insights and experiences in the development, integration and operation of software in complex business environments (Java, Spring, Git and Maven)
  • Strong interest and possibly already initial experience in agile software development (SAFe, Scrum, Kanban, ...) using AI in coding
  • SQL and database skills (ideally Oracle) are helpful, but not mandatory
  • Knowledge of Vaadin and Web technologies, such as HTML, CSS, WebComponents, Angular is advantageous
  • Experience in using Jira and Confluence
  • Good knowledge of German at least. Level B2 (negotiable)
  • Openness to new things and an innovative mindset set you apart

This is what awaits you

  • Modern jobs and 1/2 of working hours in the home office
  • Flexible working hours and at least five weeks of holidays as well as the possibility of two additional weeks of holiday purchase
  • Very good social benefits, it is provided for you
  • Free tickets to various events
  • Top location in Zurich
