Full description

At our new location «Wohnen» in the most central location on the Zurich Central, tenants not only feel at home, but are also optimally and efficiently cared for by our new division of tasks in the team. The new offices with an urban flair and top connection are part of our new management model (TOM), which thanks to consistent digitalization, clear division of tasks and specialization in the team enables efficient management and puts the customer at the center. If your heart beats for real estate, you are motivated, goal-oriented and also like to work in a team, then we look forward to your application as a real estate manager (m/f/d) Zurich Central, 80-100% This is what moves you:

  • You are responsible for supporting the team in all administrative activities within the Living site to ensure a smooth operation
  • As the first point of contact on site, you take over the customer support and are responsible for the first contact with customers and tenants
  • Communicating with the tenant, home maintenance, craft businesses and offices is one of your tasks to ensure an effective exchange of information.
  • You are responsible for carrying out final statements and custodial settlements as well as for obtaining and assessing offers, awarding contracts to craft businesses and monitoring the work
  • The accounting, processing and forwarding of creditor orders and invoices as well as the debtor control and processing of the dunning system are also your responsibility.
  • You have completed basic commercial training and preferably the property management clerk or the willingness to complete this further training
  • First professional experience as a clerk: in (advantage in the real estate industry or similar) you could already collect
  • You are team capable, resilient and distinguished by your customer- and service-oriented personality
  • An independent and goal-oriented working method as well as good communication skills qualify you in dealing with different groups of people
  • You communicate verbally and in writing fluently in German Here are some of our benefits:

  • Mobility and food: You will receive a contribution for the mobile phone. For your food, we also make a contribution.

The pension benefits are attractive and you can choose the right one for you from different models. Accident protection insurance covers the private hospital department.
