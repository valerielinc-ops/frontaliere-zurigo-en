Full description

Flowers and plants – freshness that inspires! Our range of flowers and plants contributes every day to promoting the well-being of our customers. With much care and passion we take care of the care and presentation of an attractive offer that brings joy of life and natural beauty into everyday life.

Mansioni

  • Implementation of the procurement strategy as well as operational procurement in the assigned product range to ensure optimal product availability and product range design
  • Evaluation, development and monitoring of suppliers to ensure long-term partnerships and sustainable optimisation of supplier performance
  • Enforcement of agreed product quality in close cooperation with quality management to ensure the highest standards and customer satisfaction
  • Conduct analysis of existing and future procurement markets to identify opportunities and enable strategic development of the range
  • Active participation in projects to support the Purchasing Division Manager, with a focus on strategic and operational goal achievement

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: in purchasing
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): in the commercial sector or training in the green sector as well as university of applied sciences degree (FH/HF) or occupational training in the field of purchasing, logistics and marketing
  • Open, friendly and communicative personality with a networked mindset and initiative
  • Robust, assertive, independent and exact working method
  • Strong methodological competence and good analytical skills
  • German (flowing)
  • English (negotiable)
  • French (advantageous)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central place of work: Central place of work in the city of Zurich (Limmatplatz or near Hardbrücke)
  • Modern infrastructure: Diverse work zones and modern co-working spaces with advanced equipment
  • Healthy work: breastfeeding and rest rooms as well as massage options
  • Occupational provision: Above-average benefits and three different savings plans suitable for the individual life situation
  • Migros Bank: Free account management and preferential interest on the private and investment account
  • Sports and fitness offers: Generous financial participation in courses of Migros Club School
