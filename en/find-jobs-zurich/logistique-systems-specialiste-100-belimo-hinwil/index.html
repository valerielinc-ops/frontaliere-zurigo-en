Original description

Our logistics processes are only as strong as the systems behind them. For the operation and further development of our logistics systems, we are looking for a committed personality with technical understanding and enthusiasm for smooth processes.

What will you do

  • Support and further development of the used logistics systems (EWM, SD, MM, PP)
  • First point of contact for system issues and disruptions
  • Maintenance of master data
  • Collaboration in projects and release changes
  • Performing system controlling
  • Preparation and implementation of training documents
  • Identification and implementation of optimization potential in logistics processes
  • Piket inserts

Who you are

  • Basic technical education and training
  • Very good SAP EWM knowledge, also good SAP knowledge (SD, MM, PP)
  • Technical understanding and interest in IT-supported processes
  • Readiness for Pikett service
  • Structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Teamwork and enjoyment of working with different areas
  • High degree of personal responsibility and reliability
  • Good communication skills in German (min. C1), English is advantageous
