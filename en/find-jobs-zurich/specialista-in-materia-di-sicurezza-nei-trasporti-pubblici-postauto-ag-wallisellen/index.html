Full description

Are you interested in the topic of safety? Do you want to support PostAuto in carrying out ticket checks of various public transport companies, mainly in the canton of Zurich (Zurich Verkehrsverbund). Then apply now, because with you every passenger will drive with a valid ticket.

You Can Make That Do

  • Together with your teammates:Inside you are responsible for the professional conduct of ticket checks and the maintenance of safety on many transport companies in the Zurich transport network.
  • In the event of disturbing actions, conflicts or violence during driving passes, you deescalate and intervene if necessary.
  • You perform a wide range of security tasks in the context of ticket controls and provide security and order with your presence.
  • Your competent and friendly advice of our passengers in the vehicles of public transport is asked.
  • You are thus contributing to a significant contribution to the security of revenue and public transport.

You bring this with you

  • You know a completed vocational apprenticeship and have the guide pass cat. B or are ready to complete it.
  • You act independently, have assertive powers and are mentally and physically resilient.
  • Your experience in the security field will make your daily work easier.
  • You appreciate the advantages of irregular working hours including Sundays and holidays and at night and you are flexible.
  • As a good communication personality you can skillfully adjust in conversation. You also have a strong team orientation.
  • Your excellent German skills and good French and/or Italian skills help you to perform this activity optimally. Any other foreign language is of benefit.
