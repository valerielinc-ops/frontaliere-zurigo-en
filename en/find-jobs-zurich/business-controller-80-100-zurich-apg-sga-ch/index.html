Full description

Business controller

  • 80-100%
  • Permanent employment

To strengthen our controlling, we are creating a new function with a focus on data-driven management and further development of the Partner Market. In this versatile role, you will be responsible for establishing professional management reporting, analysing business figures and identifying potential for increasing profitability. You will provide important decision-making bases for management and actively contribute to the optimization of business processes and the further development of the area. An important focus of your work is also the cooperation in tenders and the preparation of well-founded offer and profitability calculations.

Your tasks

  • Expansion and further development of professional management reporting for the Partner Market as well as provision of relevant key figures, analyses and dashboards for business management
  • Support in tendering of publicly owned advertising space and national location partners, in particular in the analysis of tender requirements and in the calculation of tenders
  • Identification of offers with weak demand and preparation of corresponding decision-making basis for product management
  • Further development and optimization of business processes and maintenance of the process landscape in the Partner Market
  • Revenue and concession estimates provided by finance managers for financial statement preparation and financial planning processes (forecast, budgeting)
  • Advising internal stakeholders with meaningful analyses and active contribution to the further development of the Partner Market
  • Administrative activities for the Head of Partner Market (help for presentations for partners, for management and the Board of Directors)

You bring with you

  • Completed studies in business administration (FH, university or comparable education)
  • Interest in legal issues relating to public procurement law and regulatory framework
  • Several years of experience in setting up and operating demanding management reporting with Power BI or comparable business intelligence solutions
  • Very good knowledge of Power BI as well as data analysis and data visualization
  • Good knowledge in the analysis and optimization of business processes
  • Strong analytical skills, high numerical affinity and a good understanding of business relationships
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Strong communication and presentation skills as well as enjoy working with different stakeholders
  • Experience in tendering or submission is an advantage
  • Stylistic German, knowledge of French are an advantage

This is what awaits you

  • Versatile and challenging job - with us you can make a difference
  • Modern jobs and 1/3 of working time in the home office
  • Top location in Zurich
  • Flexible working hours and at least five weeks of holidays as well as the possibility of two additional weeks of holiday purchase
  • Very good social benefits, it is provided for you
  • Free tickets to various events
