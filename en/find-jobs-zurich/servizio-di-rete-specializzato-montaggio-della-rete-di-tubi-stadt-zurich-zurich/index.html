Full description

Did you know that the city of Zurich has a water supply network of around 1,550 km? Our dedicated team ensures fresh, clean Züriwasser every day - and maybe soon with you! Are you from the field of sanitary, heating, metal construction or construction crafts? Are you interested in a job in the network service, but currently no suitable job is advertised or not yet the right time to change? Then we look forward to your spontaneous application for our talent pool.

Tasks

  • Our dedicated team of around 50 pipeline assemblers is responsible for carrying out pipeline construction and repair work on drinking water pipelines and house connections.
  • The focus is on new, extension and maintenance work in the pipeline network; for a reliable water supply of our inhabitants.
  • We do teamwork on impressive piping systems with diameters from 50 mm to over 2 m.
  • After in-depth training, we ensure the supply of pikett services (about 6 times a year, one week each) - even in the event of an incident.

Profile

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship EFZ in the field of sanitary, heating, metal construction or construction crafts
  • Further training as a pipe network fitter* with a federal certificate of advantage or willingness to complete this training (needed for exercise); Acquisition supported by employers
  • Joy of working outdoors and varied assignments
  • Teamwork, reliability, security awareness and commitment
  • Cat B driving licence and good knowledge of German

We offer

We offer you the inclusion in our talent pool and consideration for future suitable vacancies! In the event of a successful selection process, you can expect a structured introduction and support in education and training, a modern infrastructure, attractive urban employment conditions and excellent social benefits. We live an open, respectful work culture with a sense of cooperation and common good.

About us

The Zurich water supply supplies the city and numerous municipalities with fresh, clean drinking water around the clock. We fulfill this task in the service of the public with around 300 dedicated employees.
