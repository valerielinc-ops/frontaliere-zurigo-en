Full description

Are you ready for an apprenticeship as a Health Care Specialist? Get your EFZ through training in care. With us, you start a varied and meaningful apprenticeship with a future, because as a Health Care Specialist, you will be supported from day one in a supportive work environment where you can help people and be accompanied and supported by our experienced trainers.

For the Tertianum Sonnenplatz house in Emmenbrücke, we are looking for you starting August 1, 2026 for the apprenticeship as a Health Care Specialist EFZ.

What you will learn with us

  • Care, support, and accompaniment of guests in everyday and challenging situations
  • Assistance with personal hygiene, getting up, dressing and undressing, eating and drinking
  • Medical procedures such as measuring blood pressure, pulse, and temperature, taking blood, changing dressings, and much more
  • Daily accompaniment of guests with games, crafts, singing, etc.
  • Housekeeping, administrative, and logistical tasks
  • Compliance with and implementation of hygiene and safety regulations
  • Electronic care documentation

What you bring with you

  • Completed secondary education (secondary school A or B) with good school performance
  • Great willingness to learn and sense of responsibility
  • Empathy and patience, as well as interest in dealing with our guests
  • Helpful, sociable, and team-oriented, with a strong sense of care and conscientiousness
  • Willingness to work irregular hours
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude
  • Joy in varied challenges and practical work
  • Good German language skills, both spoken and written

What benefits do we offer you? You are just as important to us as your training is to you! That's why we offer you the following advantages:

  • You get the day off on your birthday
  • Discounted mobile phone contracts with Sunrise
  • Free SBB-Halbtax subscription
  • Every lunch for CHF 5
  • Discounts and offers for fitness, leisure, and well-being
  • Up to 10% discount on your supplementary health insurance
  • And much more…

You start right away with an exciting introduction day, and halfway through your training, we invite you for a whole week to our Summer Camp. Of course, your training completion must be celebrated properly, for which we organize an unforgettable graduation ceremony for all graduates. And perhaps this will be just the beginning of your career with us! We offer numerous opportunities to work directly within our company immediately after completing your apprenticeship.

Further advantages and insights can be found on our website.

Interested? As soon as we have your complete application documents, they will be reviewed by our team. If your dossier convinces us, we look forward to meeting you in person for a first interview. After that, you can already immerse yourself in the Tertianum world during one or more trial days and get an idea of our daily routine.

What do we need for your complete application?

  • Resume
  • School certificates
  • Results of the Stellwerk test and, if available, the Multicheck
