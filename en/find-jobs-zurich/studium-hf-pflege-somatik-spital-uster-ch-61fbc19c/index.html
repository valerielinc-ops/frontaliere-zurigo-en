Full description

Personal – competent – close Welcome to the Hospital Uster, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As the central pillar of health care in the Oberland region of Zurich and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you experience the highest level of expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and promoted in your professional development. Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of a “regional and digital health hub in Züri East”! Field of work: adult somatics (2 or 3 years)

What awaits you with us

  • an exciting and varied education in a modern working environment where no day is the same
  • the opportunity to gain experience in various subject areas and to specialize in your areas of interest
  • Practical learning and insight days that optimally prepare you for your future tasks

And you bring that with you.

  • a completed vocational apprenticeship EFZ, Matura or Fachmittelschule
  • Competence Analysis (Multicheck®) Health HF
  • a high degree of empathy and responsibility in dealing with our patients
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Teamwork, flexibility and openness to new challenges

6 reasons to work with us

  • A team of experienced care professionals who support and promote you
  • Attractive employment conditions for apprentices over 25 years of age
  • various benefits such as generous contribution for school supplies and joker days for changing time
  • Central location near Uster station and motorway connection
  • Cheap parking (with Parkingpay max.) CHF 4.50 per day, discounted apartments next to the hospital
  • Possible employment after diploma

You or you?

Our case is clear. Whether you’re learning, nursing, working as a doctor or CEO, we’re all about you. This du-form signals our family atmosphere in the hospital. We want you to be able to experience this culture before your first day of work. Therefore, we already communicate during the application process and in the interviews in this way. If this sounds likeable for you, then we look forward to your application.
