Tasks

  • Your goal when you lead the Rayon Convenience and Meat? An enjoyable freshness and shopping experience for your customers.

  • Respect is central to you when you lead your team. So you make for a great mood.

  • With sales and leadership in a similar function, you are familiar.

  • You love advising people and sharing your interest in quality products and culinary knowledge.
  • You have a very good grip on German – verbally and in writing.
