Ever wondered how your skills and unique background could impact the world of tomorrow? Join our Product Management team and bring your passion and talent to work.

Associate Product Manager (100%)

Männedorf, Zürich

Your contribution

As an Associate Product Manager, you’ll work closely with our Product Management team to support one of Tecan’s Liquid Handling platforms. This is a great opportunity to learn, grow, and make a real impact—even if you’re just starting out. We’re looking for someone who is eager, honest, and ready to go the extra mile.

Responsibilities

  • Support market research and help gather insights about customer needs
  • Work closely with your team and other departments to achieve shared goals
  • Help promote Tecan products and initiatives
  • Assist in developing strategies and marketing plans for our products
  • Take ownership of assigned tasks and deliver them on time
  • Track product performance and help with pricing analysis
  • Support the product life cycle from idea to launch and beyond
  • Join product development meetings and contribute your perspective

Your background

We value your attitude and potential as much as your experience. If you’re motivated, open to learning, and ready to take on new challenges, we want to hear from you!

  • You have a degree (or are about to graduate) in Chemistry, Biochemistry, Engineering, a technical/scientific field, or similar experience
  • You’re independent, can prioritize your work, and are not afraid to ask questions
  • You’re a team player who fits well with others and communicates honestly
  • You’re a self-starter who is eager to learn
  • You’re open, curious, and want to make a difference
  • You deliver on your commitments
  • You’re comfortable communicating and presenting in English (German or other languages are a plus)
  • Experience in sales, marketing, or the life sciences is helpful but not required
  • Experience with laboratory automation and liquid handling platforms is a plus
  • Ability to travel for work
