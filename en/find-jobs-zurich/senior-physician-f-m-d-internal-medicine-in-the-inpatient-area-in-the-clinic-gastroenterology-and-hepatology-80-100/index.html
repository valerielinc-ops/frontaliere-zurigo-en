Our offer

We offer you a responsible and versatile activity at a university centre with an international reputation and the highest medical standards. On the bed station of the Hospital for Gastroenterology and Hepatology at the University Hospital Zurich, 30 inpatients are usually cared for. For the care of our patients: inside on the Intermediate Care stations and intensive care units we are available for consiliar care. For this task we have provided 2 cadre positions. The Director of Stationary Gastroenterology is Ms. PD Dr. med. Sena Blümel. For the further education of our assistant doctor: inside in general internal medicine we have the further education entitlement of category D. Our inpatient care covers the complete field of gastroenterology and hepatology. Among other things, we provide patients: women with advanced liver diseases up to liver transplantation; with complex pancreato-biliary diseases; with upper and lower gastrointestinal bleeding; with severe forms of chronic inflammatory bowel diseases and motility disorders; as well as patients: for follow-up after complex endoscopic procedures. This results in a close interdisciplinary cooperation with the fields of surgery, radiology, anesthesia and intensive care medicine. They benefit from an inspiring academic environment that actively promotes innovation and development. In addition, we offer attractive working conditions and a wide range of further education.
