Requirements

  • You have overall responsibility for the quality, effectiveness and further development of nursing in the areas of epileptology and neurorehabilitation and ensure patient-oriented, high-quality and cost-effective service provision.
  • You develop and implement a forward-looking nursing strategy and drive key topics such as skill-grade mix, modern working and deployment models, and the digitalisation of nursing processes.
  • Together with your management team, you promote a contemporary leadership and team culture, support the further development of the nursing division through upcoming change and transformation processes, and strengthen interprofessional collaboration with the medical and therapy departments.
  • You consistently further develop Klinik Lengg as an innovative training institution at secondary and tertiary level and are committed to attracting, developing and retaining qualified professionals.
  • As a member of the Executive Management, you actively shape the strategic development of the clinic and represent the interests of nursing at corporate level.

We offer

  • Completed training as a registered nurse at HF/FH level or a Bachelor of Science in Nursing, supplemented by a MAS in Nursing Management / Nursing Leadership or a comparable field.
  • Several years of professional and leadership experience in healthcare, with a proven track record in a comparable management role, ideally at the level of Director of Nursing or CNO.
  • Proven experience in leading change and development processes, as well as a passion for successfully guiding organizations and people through transformations.
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, strong execution skills, and a pronounced understanding of business management interrelationships and the efficient use of resources.
  • Strong social skills, excellent communication and conflict-management skills, as well as the ability to bring together diverse interests constructively.

Description

Klinik Lengg AG in Zurich is the center of excellence for epileptology and neurological rehabilitation.

It is our goal to enable patients with neurological disorders to achieve the most extensive rehabilitation possible and to ensure accurate diagnosis and sustainable treatment for patients with epilepsy. Around 300 employees are committed, in a compassionate, competent, innovative and reliable manner, to ensuring that our patients regain the greatest possible independence - and thus their quality of life. Become part of one of our interdisciplinary and interprofessional teams.
