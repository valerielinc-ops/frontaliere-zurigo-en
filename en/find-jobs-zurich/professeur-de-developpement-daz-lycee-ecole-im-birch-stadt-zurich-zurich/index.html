About us

We are a large school community in Zurich-Nord with 19 schools. We derive our pedagogical attitude from the concept of New Authority and thus meet the daily challenges in everyday school life together and effectively.

The school Im Birch has a modern infrastructure, is located in "Neu-Oerlikon" and can be reached in 5 minutes' walk from Oerlikon train station. It comprises 14 primary classes, 5 kindergartens and 4 nurseries. At the secondary school, teachers teach in the teaching sequences of Self-Organized Learning (SOL) mixed-level team teaching.
