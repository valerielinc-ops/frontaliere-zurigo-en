As Bid & Tendering Specialist you are a key player in translating client requirements and site assessments into detailed, accurate project costings. You are essential in ensuring that tenders for Steam Turbine & Compressor equipment services are competitive, technically precise, and commercially sound. You will be responsible for developing proposals for complex projects, ensuring alignment between technical feasibility, cost efficiency, and commercial success. Role Location: Any Sulzer location. Your main tasks and responsibilities:

  • Ensuring thorough technical scope definition and risk management for complex Steam Turbine & Compressor service projects (e.g. inspections, repairs, parts supply, multi-train solutions, rerates, upgrades and modifications)
  • Prepare end-to-end cost estimation and commercial quotations for Steam Turbine & Compressor projects based on technical specifications
  • Engage and collaborate successfully with clients and external partners (e.g. vendors / subcontractors) as well internal stakeholders
  • Support post-tender activities, ensuring smooth transition from bid to execution, including scope validation and project briefing
  • Contribute to procurement and cost efficiency by working closely with suppliers to secure optimal pricing, lead times and quality for related parts and services
  • Continuously improve cost estimation methods to improve accuracy and efficiency in pricing strategies To succeed in this role, you will need:

  • Familiarity with service and repair supply chains.
