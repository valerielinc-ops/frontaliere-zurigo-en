Ultrasonic tester: in 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. ultrasonic tester:in Zurich / Lucerne, from now on or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

An innovative working environment awaits you, in which you can drive major changes and thus contribute to rail safety. Together with your teammate: inside you develop solutions for current challenges and implement them in the team. Attractive working conditions are important to us. Our benefits such as additional child allowances, free 2nd class GA and discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • Due to railway operation, damage can occur to rails and switches. As an ultrasound tester: with ultrasound test equipment you check the rails and switches for critical damage.
  • You assess and classify error indicators based on the specifications. Used for planning and conducting tests and reporting damage You have the appropriate IT tools.
  • In addition to working on the track You also test drives with the rail test train and then check the results on site.
  • You do this challenging task in the team and support each other. Together, you ensure that the test layer runs smoothly and, if necessary, assume responsibility as safety chief:in.
  • You ensure that our test equipment always runs flawlessly: you maintain them regularly and check their function so that everything works smoothly.

You bring that with you.

  • A reliable, team-capable personality with a strong sense of responsibility and quick comprehension – that’s you.
  • For this position you need a completed 3 to 4 years of professional apprenticeship.
  • You already have experience in non-destructive material testing (e.g. as an ultrasonic tester: with certification at level 1 according to SN EN ISO 9712) or you are interested in familiarizing yourself with the non-destructive testing of rails and switches and achieving certification. We support you in this.
  • You have the ability to interpret ultrasound test results and have a robust physical condition. (Full years of outdoor work, individual night or weekend assignments may be required, high travel activity, operation of test equipment weighing up to 25 kg in the track area). A correct sense of color is necessary.
  • You have very good knowledge of German (C1) and have a category B ticket.
