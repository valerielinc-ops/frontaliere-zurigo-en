Canton of Zurich Security Directorate Social Office University Intern 60 - 80% The Cantonal Social Office (KSA) carries out a variety of controlling and operational tasks in social assistance, social insurance, social institutions and the asylum sector. The Disability Rights Coordination Office in the KSA supports specialists from the cantonal administration and the municipalities in the Canton of Zurich with specialist questions relating to disability rights, particularly with regard to the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities. She also coordinates, among other things, the implementation of the cantonal disability rights action plan. Further information can be found at https://www.zh.ch/de/social/life-with-behinderung/aktionsplan-behindertenrechte.html We are looking for a university intern 60 - 80% for the Disability Rights Coordination Office as of February 1, 2027 or by agreement, for a limited period of 6 or 12 months. The place

  • They deal with questions of the rights of people with disabilities
  • You work on projects, e.g. disability rights action days, support offers for communities
  • You carry out research on current topics
  • You participate in the creation of reports, statements and concepts
  • You will be involved in the development and implementation of information and awareness campaigns or in communication work
  • You support the team with general administrative tasks, e.g. organizing events, accessibility of documents and your profile
  • You are a student pursuing a bachelor's degree in social work and must complete a mandatory internship as part of your studies
  • You are interested in issues of equality for people with disabilities and would like to gain an insight into the work of the Canton of Zurich in the area of disability rights
  • You have completed initial training or a degree and at least three years of professional experience
  • You have strong communication skills, are adept at using MS Office applications and have very good written skills
  • You are a service-oriented, resilient and flexible person with quick comprehension, networked thinking and a structured way of working.
