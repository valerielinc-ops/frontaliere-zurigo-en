About the Role

In this role you will have the following key business responsibilities: Team Leadership

  • Lead and directly manage a team of 2–3 Data Analysts, providing day-to-day guidance, technical oversight, and structured feedback to ensure high-quality and timely delivery.
  • Plan, prioritize, and allocate workload across the team, ensuring effective resource utilization and clear accountability.
  • Review analyses and deliverables to maintain accuracy, consistency, and adherence to agreed standards.
  • Coach and develop team members through hands-on support, knowledge sharing, and continuous performance feedback.
  • Foster a collaborative, solution-oriented team environment focused on delivery excellence and continuous improvement.

Data Analysis

  • Lead the design, mapping, coding, and testing client dataset onboarding to ensure accurate and efficient integration into internal systems.
  • Perform robust data quality controls during ingestion, ensuring data is fit for purpose and minimizes downstream adjustments.
  • Conduct structured data analysis to address business questions, support new initiatives, and generate actionable insights.
  • Identify and evaluate solutions to business challenges, including process improvements, system enhancements, and user enablement where required.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to optimize processes and improve overall data quality and operational efficiency.
  • Continuously enhance BDX data validation frameworks to ensure suitability for Experience Studies, including driving greater standardization of data formats.
  • Strengthen end-to-end validation processes by embedding Experience Studies requirements earlier in the data lifecycle.
  • Act as a key liaison between Experience Studies and Technical Accounting, ensuring alignment between upstream data ingestion and downstream consumption needs.
  • Establish and maintain an effective feedback mechanism with Technical Accounting to support continuous improvement in data validation and quality.
  • Apply understanding of underlying product features to ensure accurate mapping and data interpretation.

About the Team

Are you passionate about transforming data into actionable insights that drive business excellence? Join our innovative Data Management & Insights team, where our mission is to make data accessible and valuable by systematically acquiring, ingesting, and analyzing client data. If you thrive in a collaborative environment and are eager to make a meaningful impact through data-driven solutions, we invite you to explore this exciting opportunity.

About You

Bachelor of Science degree with 3–5 years of experience in data analysis, including hands-on technical expertise

  • Experience leading a small team, aligning team objectives with business priorities, ensuring effective resource utilization, and promoting a culture of accountability and continuous improvement.
  • Strong proficiency in SQL and Python, with practical experience in coding, querying, and data manipulation.
  • Solid experience in data mapping, ETL processes, and end-to-end data integration.
  • Working knowledge of database systems such as SQL Server, MySQL, PostgreSQL, and/or Oracle.
  • Familiarity with OSS (Open-Source Software), DataWorks, MaxCompute, and other Alibaba Cloud products is preferred.
  • Strong strategic, analytical, and conceptual thinking skills, with the ability to translate complex data into meaningful business insights.
  • Effective communication skills, with the ability to clearly and concisely present technical concepts and analytical findings to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
  • Fluency in English and Mandarin Chinese is required.
