Full description

Personal – competent – close Welcome to the Hospital Uster, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As the central pillar of health care in the Oberland region of Zurich and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you experience the highest level of expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and promoted in your professional development. Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of a “regional and digital health hub in Züri East”! Bed Station A0 is an interdisciplinary department with a focus on geriatric acute rehabilitation for patients of all insurance classes. The clinical pictures include, inter alia, cardiovascular diseases, pneumological diseases, endocrinology, infections, gastrointestinal diseases, autoimmune diseases, oncology and hematology, traumas and injuries after falls. As a result of the steady establishment and expansion of our geriatric acute rehabilitation, we are looking for a flexible and committed personality for assignments mainly in late and night service by immediately or by agreement.

Exciting challenges await you

  • you are part of an interdisciplinary treatment team and nurse and supervise, in tandem with a qualified nurse, patients, during their one to two-week stay, according to the principles of activating-therapeutic-accompanying care
  • Medical engineering tasks, as well as administrative activities within the station, you carry out independently
  • You contribute to a learning atmosphere and to the joint achievement of the station goals. You shape an appreciative team culture
  • Education: You help transfer knowledge to learners and interns.

What sets you apart for the job

  • Completed training as a health professional EFZ with experience in acute care hospital
  • Interest in geriatric acute rehabilitation
  • committed, motivated and resilient personality with high social competence
  • You accept changes as challenges and like to actively shape your workspace
  • Willingness to work in all shifts
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

8 reasons to work with us

  • responsible task in an open, curious, colorful and appreciative team
  • Full and part-time employment possible, according to your needs
  • careful familiarization and introduction to the work area by the diplomats or FaGe colleagues
  • at least five weeks holidays and joker days for changing time
  • Excellent staff restaurant and numerous discounts
  • Central location near Uster station and motorway connection
  • ZVV BonusPass or cheap parking - with Parkingpay, max. CHF 4.50
  • free parking time during the night shift (until 7 a.m.)
