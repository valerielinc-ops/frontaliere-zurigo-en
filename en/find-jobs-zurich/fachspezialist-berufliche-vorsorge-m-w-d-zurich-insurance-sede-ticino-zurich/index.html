Full description

137583 | 60 - 100% | Oerlikon, Zurich | Hybrid

Do you want to create direct added value for our insured with your expertise in occupational pension provision and shape our pension fund both professionally and organisationally? Are you enthusiastic about using your expertise in occupational pensions for complex customer concerns, improving processes and helping to shape new solutions?

In this role, you will work in the Pension Fund team, which is organisationally assigned to the HR division, and actively shape the further development of the pension fund of Zurich Switzerland. You take on a central function in the precautionary department of our insured, combine your profound knowledge with a high level of service orientation and contribute to further digitizing and optimizing our processes. In a small, dedicated team, you actively shape the appearance of our pension fund and ensure that our insured are transparent, comprehensible and solution-oriented.

This is what awaits you

  • You accompany our insured persons through all phases of life (entry, exit, home ownership promotion, divorces, etc.) and inform them in an understandable and service-oriented manner.
  • You check, process and accompany disability cases structured and empathetic
  • You ensure a high level of professional quality, participate in controls and continuously develop workflows, checklists and processes
  • As a super user, you optimize our management software, test new functions and form the interface to the IT service provider.
  • You actively participate in the creation of insured information and shipping campaigns, maintain the intranet presence of the pension fund and support the introduction of new digital processes

This sets you apart

  • Successfully completed commercial basic training or matriculum as well as further training for social insurance specialist or specialist for staff provision
  • Several years of sound practical experience in occupational pension schemes
  • High technical understanding and enthusiasm for modern software solutions, digitalized processes and their further development
  • Discreet, integrity and resilient personality with independent, exact and solution-oriented working methods, strong team spirit and strong empathy for our insured
  • German at native language level (C2) as well as fluent knowledge (C1/C2) in French and/or English for the professional support of our multilingual insurance group
