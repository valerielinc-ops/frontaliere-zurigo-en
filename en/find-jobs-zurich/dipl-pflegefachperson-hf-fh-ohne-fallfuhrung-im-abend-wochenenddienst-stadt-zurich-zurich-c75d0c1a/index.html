Full description

Looking for Spitex will be a responsible Dipl immediately or by agreement. Nurse HF without fall guidance.

Tasks

  • During their assignment, they assume evening responsibility for the evening team and the customers of the area.
  • They ensure the qualified and resource-oriented care and support of our customers.
  • They support and guide customers and their relatives, even in complex situations.
  • They perform piquette assignments with telephone responsibility.

Profile

  • Completed training at tertiary level such as Dipl. Nurse FH/HF or equivalent training
  • Joy and interest in dealing with older people
  • Application knowledge in MS-Office and Perigon is beneficial
  • Driving licence Cat B, car advantageous

We offer

Freedom in the design of your work as well as a motivated care team with a fixed customer base. Our employees also benefit from attractive employment conditions in accordance with Staff Law of the City of Zurich as well as targeted training and further education. In addition to at least four weeks holidays, you are entitled to 6 company holidays.

About us

The Foundation for Old-Age Homes of the City of Zurich (SAW) operates 40 settlements in urban areas with more than 2000 affordable apartments for people aged 60 and over. It supports its tenants with everyday, social and nursing services and thus enables them a high quality of life into old age. Its diverse socio-cultural offer supports the development of neighbourly contacts and an active settlement life.
