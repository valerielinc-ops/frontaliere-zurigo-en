Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School Stadelhofen Dübendorf branch Middle school teachers mbA for mathematics 70 - 100% The Dübendorf branch of the Stadelhofen cantonal school has been running a lower secondary school, a short-term high school and a commercial middle school since the 2026/27 school year. We are a young school with a future-oriented educational concept. Our “Dübendorf Model” focuses on activating students, imparting action knowledge and teaching methods that go beyond the classic 45-minute lesson. Our modern rooms on the Empa campus offer an inspiring learning and working environment and create ideal conditions for contemporary teaching.

  • Your tasks At the beginning of the 2027/28 school year we are looking for two middle school teachers with special skills
  • Mathematics tasks ranging from 70-100%. The area of ​​responsibility includes teaching at all levels. As a teacher, you get your students excited about your subject. Together with your colleagues, you will actively shape the implementation of our pedagogical concept. You will also take on responsibilities as a class teacher as well as additional ones
  • Tasks in school development.
  • Your profile You have completed a university master's degree as well as a teaching diploma for baccalaureate schools (including additional vocational education qualification). We are looking for a committed personality who enjoys thinking ahead about lessons and working with our team to create a lively school culture. What we offer You can expect a working environment with a lot of creative freedom, short decision-making processes and an open team that welcomes new ideas. The conditions of employment are based on cantonal requirements. Application Have we aroused your interest? We look forward to your application. Please submit your documents exclusively via the online tool by October 30, 2026. Applications outside of the online tool will not be accepted and will be deleted immediately. The Vice Rector responsible for Mathematics, Mr. Markus Hanhart, will be happy to provide you with any information via email markus.hanhart@edu.zh.ch. Further information about our school can be found at Kantonsschule Stadelhofen - Dübendorf branch.
