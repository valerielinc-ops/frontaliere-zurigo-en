About the role

At Sulzer, we're a global leader in fluid engineering and rotating equipment services, helping customers maximise reliability, improve performance and reduce downtime. With over 190 years of innovation, operations across the globe, and a strong presence in Africa, we support some of the world's most critical industries.

We're looking for an experienced Industry Sales Manager - Oil & Gas to drive growth across South Africa's Oil & Gas sector and strengthen our position as the service partner of choice. Your Main Tasks and Responsibilities

  • Develop and execute the sales strategy for the Oil & Gas industry segment.
  • Drive sustainable growth through new business development and key account management.
  • Build strong relationships with operators, EPC contractors, refineries, petrochemical facilities and maintenance teams.
  • Identify opportunities for maintenance, repair, overhaul, retrofit and field service solutions.
  • Lead commercial negotiations and contract discussions.
  • Collaborate with service centres and operational teams to deliver customer-focused solutions.
  • Maintain an accurate sales pipeline and deliver against revenue and profitability targets.
  • Monitor market trends, competitor activity and emerging opportunities within the Oil & Gas sector.

To Succeed in This Role, You Will Need

  • Proven experience in technical sales, business development or account management within the Oil & Gas industry.
  • Knowledge of rotating equipment, pumps, compressors, turbines, motors, electromechanical equipment or industrial services.
  • Strong commercial acumen with a track record of delivering sales growth.
  • Excellent relationship-building and stakeholder management skills.
  • The ability to influence decision-makers and develop long-term customer partnerships.
  • A results-driven mindset with a passion for identifying and winning new business.
  • A relevant technical or commercial qualification is advantageous.
