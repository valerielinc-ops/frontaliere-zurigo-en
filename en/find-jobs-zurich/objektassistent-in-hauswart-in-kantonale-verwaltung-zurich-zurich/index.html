Our approximately 70 employees in the Facility Services department manage more than 120 central and district administration buildings with a total area of 440,000 m2. For the Zurich High Court and the relocating administrative buildings, we are immediately looking for a person capable of teamwork who, together with her team, ensures smooth operation. Your task

  • Planning, coordinating, monitoring and performing home maintenance work and coordinating external service and cleaning service providers
  • First point of contact for administrative employees, suppliers and craft companies as well as ensuring a high level of service and user orientation
  • Planning and coordination of maintenance, maintenance and repair work
  • Performing general administrative tasks
  • Mutual support and deputy within the team as well as taking over the Pikettdienst Your profile
  • Completed basic technical training with EFZ as well as a Swiss certificate as a housekeeper or maintenance specialist – or the willingness to acquire it in a timely manner
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Category B driving licence and residence in the Canton of Zurich
  • Strong communication and team skills, personal responsibility, networked thinking and acting as well as a high service orientation
  • Good user knowledge in Microsoft 365 This awaits you at the High Court, a motivated and dynamic team that manages a diverse portfolio of prestigious properties in the city of Zurich. Look forward to a varied working environment in which you can contribute your expertise and actively and sustainably shape the further development of the Facility Service.
