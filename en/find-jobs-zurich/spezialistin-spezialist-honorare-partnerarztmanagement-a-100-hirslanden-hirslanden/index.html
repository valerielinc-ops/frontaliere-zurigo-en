Hirslanden stands for high-quality, responsible, self-determined and efficient integrated healthcare. From birth to old age as well as from prevention to healing – at any time, physically and digitally – the Hirslanden Group is concentrating on the further development of the “Continuum of Care” together with private and public partners.

The good of the human being is our focus. That of the patients, but also that of our employees.

YOUR TASKS

  • Central contact point for inquiries about partner doctor fees and clarification of open or audited fee payments
  • Examination of the submitted documents and ensuring uniform and complete processing of partner doctor requests
  • Controlling and monitoring of relevant processes and cases in DPS and SAP
  • Recognition of process deviations as well as elaboration of evaluations and decision basis for process optimization
  • Close cooperation with clinics, partner doctors and the internal departments of the Shared Service Center

Your profile

  • Commercial education, ideally supplemented by further training in health or collective bargaining
  • Several years of professional experience in collective bargaining as well as in the fee examination and billing of ambulatory and stationary services
  • In-depth knowledge of the Swiss healthcare system and experience in complex hospital or clinical structures
  • Secure handling of Microsoft Office and SAP, knowledge of M-KIS is an advantage
  • Analytical and networked thinking, high communication and cooperation skills and an accurate and responsible way of working

Are you ready to take off in an innovative environment?

With us you can fully develop your strengths, your know-how and your skills. On your first working day, we warmly welcome you and make sure that you feel comfortable with us from the beginning.

  • Free employee advice: For your well-being – whether in professional questions, private challenges or simply to gain new perspectives
  • More than 5 weeks of vacation as part of our Leisure Plus program – for the perfect work-life balance

And many more exciting benefits can be found here!
