Psychiatry and psychotherapy and general internal medicine (80-100%)

For the municipal medical service (psychiatry/addiction medicine and somatics) we gladly accept spontaneous applications from assistant doctors.

Tasks

Are you interested in working at the interface of psychiatry, addiction medicine, general internal medicine and social medicine? We are happy to review your application and include you in our applicant pool.

Profile

  • Assistant doctor in further education (psychiatry and psychotherapy or general internal medicine)
  • Interest in addictive and socio-medical issues
  • Sensitivity in dealing with people in stressful life situations
  • Enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation as well as a flexible, committed and resilient personality

We offer

A responsible and exciting task with a high level of independence in a committed, interprofessional team with flexible work organisation. A good introduction to the task area and attractive employment conditions with a 46-hour week (currently in the pilot test 42+4) from Monday to Friday. No night and regular shift services; occasional Saturday speaking hours are possible. A stimulating environment, cooperation at eye level with du-culture and an attractive workplace. Excellent employment conditions such as six additional company holidays, mobility contribution, lunch checks, generous support for your further education and very good social benefits.

About us

The Municipal Health Services (SGD) is a service department of the Department of Health and the Environment with around 250 employees, which offers a variety of health services for the population of Zurich. They include the business units Stadtärztlicher Dienst (SAD), Zürich im Alter (ZiA), Public Health (PH), Spitex, Finance & Services and HR.

The municipal medical service ensures the outpatient and inpatient medical care of people in social border situations. It comprises three outpatient clinics, the public health department and the Zurich Addiction Clinic and combines somatic, psychiatric and addiction medical services. In this way, the SAD contributes significantly to the medical care of vulnerable population groups and to the relief of standard care.

The SAD shall have the following training authorisations

  • General internal medicine (1.5 years, category II)
  • Psychiatry and psychotherapy (category A, 3 years outpatient); Category C, 2 years inpatient and outpatient
  • Consiliar and liaison psychiatry (category A, 2 years SP and 1 additional year for FAT PP ambulant)
  • Psychiatry and psychotherapy of dependency diseases and ability card (category A 2 years SP and 1 additional year for FAT PP inpatient and outpatient)
