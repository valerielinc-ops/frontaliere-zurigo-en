Are you passionate about shaping the future of work through cutting-edge Digital Workplace experiences powered by AI technologies? About the role

Step into a pivotal role within Swiss Re's Digital Workplace team, where you will be at the forefront of continued workplace transformation. As a Lead Workplace Engineer, you will co-lead the strategy, adoption, and delivery of intelligent Digital Workplace capabilities — with a spotlight on Microsoft 365, Workplace AI assistants, agents and automation. Your work will directly shape the modern, best-in-class digital experience for our global workforce, driving measurable business value while ensuring services are delivered in line with security, compliance, regulatory, and governance requirements.

Key Responsibilities

  • Drive platform evolution by identifying, evaluating, and deploying new Microsoft 365 and AI-augmented capabilities, processes, and solutions aligned with business requirements
  • Lead design and implementation of Microsoft 365 capabilities, as well as security, governance and compliance controls, translating internal risk policies into technical measures and operating procedures
  • Deliver transformational initiatives and solutions, such as platform modernisation, migration activities and similar, turning technology into measurable business value
  • Monitor and respond to fast-paced developments in the Digital Workplace space, maintaining active engagement with vendors and internal stakeholders
  • Hands-on implementation with Microsoft 365, Azure workloads and modern scripting and development platforms
  • Leverage data platforms such as MS Power Platform and Palantir Foundry to drive data-informed workplace decisions and visualisations
  • Continuously upskill , improve controls and procedures, and actively share knowledge across teams to foster a culture of learning and innovation

About the Team

We in Digital Workplace (DWP) set out to make it easy for more than 14,000 employees to seamlessly connect with people, ideas, and expertise — within and beyond Swiss Re.

Our team is built around Microsoft 365, Workplace AI solutions, and related services — spanning design, architecture, development, integration, and orchestration across teams and internal partners. We believe the key to success lies in deeply understanding the business needs of our internal clients, identifying where technology can add real value, and focusing on how people will adopt and embrace change.

To support this, we are continuously redefining the way we work. DWP is an early adopter of agile methodologies and agile leadership within Swiss Re — and we're proud of it. We are a diverse, passionate team of experts who genuinely welcome professionals who bring curiosity, drive, and experience to help us deliver world-class solutions. Come join us on our journey!

About You

We are looking for a self-starter with a can-do, hands-on, and end-to-end mentality — someone who is adaptable, curious, values close stakeholder interactions and is ready to challenge colleagues and the status quo in the best possible way. You thrive in an environment where no two days are alike, engaging regularly with new requirements and topics alongside a diverse team of experts. You bring a track record of successful delivery in complex, fast-paced environments and are energised by the opportunity to create real impact.

We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements

  • Expert knowledge of Microsoft 365 digital workplace platform and related services, including security, governance and compliance capabilities
  • Advanced automation and scripting capabilities including experience with modern DevOps technologies and CI/CD methodologies
  • Hand on knowledge with Enterprise AI platforms such as Copilot or ChatGPT Enterprise
  • Good working knowledge of data analytics and visualisation , including hands-on experience with tools such as MS Power Platform and Palantir Foundry
  • Good working knowledge of Azure Cloud, Networking, Identity and Access Management, as well as provisioning, authorisation, and authentication concepts
  • Fluent English with excellent communication skills at all levels of the organisation and beyond

These are additional nice to haves

  • Experience working in a global enterprise environment , preferably within the insurance or financial services sector
  • Familiarity with Regulatory, Governance and Risk Management frameworks and methodologies
  • Experience working in agile SCRUM methodology
