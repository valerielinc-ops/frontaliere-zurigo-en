Full description

Do you like working with people? You would like to work with our team in the hotel industry for the health center for the Alter Klus Park on a temporary basis or by agreement for 6 - 12 months?

Tasks

  • They support our employees in the hotel industry and get to know the tasks and activities of a specialist hotel industry industry with a focus on service.
  • They look after and serve our residents, their guests and external visitors in the restaurant and assist in serving food and drinks.
  • They provide a pleasant atmosphere in the restaurant and carry out general cleaning, cleaning and preparation work in the service area.
  • They carry out various types of cleaning in the residential rooms, public rooms and offices as well as the sanitary facilities and are instructed in the professional and appropriate application of various cleaning devices and machines.
  • They prepare the residential and company laundry.

Profile

  • Joy of contact with elderly people and teamwork
  • An accurate, reliable and responsible way of working
  • Self-employment and commitment
  • Good manners and a well-groomed appearance
  • Knowledge of German in words and writing as well as language understanding in the Swiss dialect

We offer

Enjoy an exciting field of work in a professional and team-oriented working environment with a du-culture throughout the company. The human being is at our center. That is why we support the reconciliation of private and professional life, different working models make it possible.

As part of the city of Zurich and due to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, progressive employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

About us

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

The health center for the Old Klus Park in the Zurich district of Hirslanden in district 7 offers residents a cozy home with all amenities. The apartments with old building charm are located in a magnificent historical building. The surrounding park is very popular in the quarter and an important meeting place. Here you work together with 70 colleagues.
