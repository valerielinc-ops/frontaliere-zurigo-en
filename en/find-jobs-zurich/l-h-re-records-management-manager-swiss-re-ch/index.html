L&H RE Records Management Manager — Swiss Re

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

This newly created role will drive key elements of L&H Re Records Management and represent L&H in the Group Records Management Enhancement Program. The role combines regional accountability with global programme involvement and plays a critical part in embedding the future Records Management Target Operating Model (TOM) within L&H Re. The successful candidate will help transition L&H Re from legacy remediation towards a sustainable, business-integrated Records Management capability.

Key focus areas include

  • Planning, preparing and driving remediation of high-priority Records Management issues in the region and as part of the global team. This includes reducing physical records backlog, reviewing and triaging unindexed boxes, and addressing legacy issues in electronic records.
  • Acting as a gatekeeper between local business teams, Group Records Management, and CRES to ensure efficient coordination, clear accountability, and optimal use of resources.
  • Conducting structured analysis of physical and electronic records data, documenting issues, and tracking remediation and quality improvement initiatives.
  • Actively sharing lessons learned within L&H Re and across Group Records Management and other BUs, ensuring insights are embedded into future TOM design and improvements.
  • Implementing the future Records Management TOM within L&H Re, fully aligned with the Group TOM, and acting as a strong advocate for Records Management across the business.

This role leads initiatives across the L&H Re landscape, covering most of the end-to-end value chain. Success requires understanding the broader strategic context while remaining closely connected to functional and MU stakeholder needs. Sustainable global solutions require strong stakeholder engagement, transparent communication, and decisive execution. About the role

In this role, you will

  • Take a regional lead role within the programme to ensure professional, pragmatic, and end-to-end project execution.
  • Act as regional topic owner for Records Management in L&H Re, ensuring quality assurance activities are conducted and local mandates are clearly defined and executed.
  • Collect and assess regional requirements, connect with local stakeholders, and promote adherence to DRM processes and guidelines.
  • Support the integration of Group deliverables into regional business processes and ensure local adoption and operationalisation.
  • Contribute to planning, issue triage, risk identification, and structured execution across remediation and governance topics.

About the Team

Records Management is a new team as part of L&H Business Operations. We work with stakeholders across the entire L&H value chain, IT, as well as GDTO. Key focus of this new team will be on solving the three following priority tasks for L&H Re:

  • Addressing potential legacy issues on physical and digital records
  • Implement future Records Management TOM design for L&H Re, aligned to the future Group Records Management TOM
  • Actively participate and represent L&H Re in the Group Records Management Enhancement Program

You will report to the Senior L&H Records Management Project Leader.
