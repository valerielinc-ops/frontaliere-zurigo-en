Full description

We are looking for reinforcement by 01.11.2026 or by agreement.

As an employee in one of our primary school support locations, you are responsible for a group of about 60 school children 4 days a week. They work in a team with a hoard management and two specialists and are supported in the catering area and in the care by school assistants. Since we are an apprenticeship company, we also train students at FaBe, who can accompany you in everyday life together with the team. You take your holidays in the school-free time. Precise information on working hours can be discussed during a personal conversation with the management of supervision.

Tasks

  • Participation in school-supplementary care and free time for children
  • Leading the children's group, as well as parental contacts
  • Instruction of FaBe trainees in everyday life and the willingness to assume the vocational training function
  • Domestic and administrative work
  • Intensified cooperation with the teachers in the context of the day school (regular exchange, joint events, participation in the class council, etc.)
  • Collaboration in the holiday camp and on school-free days

Profile

  • Professional experience in the care of children and adolescents (care, crèche, home)
  • Enjoy working in a large team
  • Readiness for interdisciplinary cooperation in the school team
  • Joy of dealing with people of different cultures
  • Good knowledge of German

You have one of the following degrees (all foreign diplomas and professional cards must be accompanied by a Swiss certificate of equivalence):

  • Swiss Federal Certificate of Competence (EFZ) Specialist child care
  • Social work FH/HF, social education FH/HF or equivalent training
  • Kindergärtner/-in, Hortleiter/in
  • Teaching diploma of primary school level (CH)
  • University degree with major in social education, pedagogy or educational science
  • Socio-cultural/animator/

We offer

a varied, versatile and independent activity and a salary according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich.

About us

The Waidhalde school is located in Wipkingen in a beautiful location with great views. It comprises seven midday evening hoards, two morning tables and two midday hoards. In order to enable needs-based care, the students are looked after in their respective class level. In total, about 550 children and young people are looked after in the Waidhalde School House by dedicated nursing directors, specialists and care assistants.
