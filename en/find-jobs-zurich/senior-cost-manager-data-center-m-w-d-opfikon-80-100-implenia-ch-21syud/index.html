Full description

Are you looking for more than the next project? With us, you take responsibility for the cost management of complex construction and infrastructure projects and ensure transparent, predictive and resilient cost management throughout all project phases. With your commercial understanding, you support project management in important decisions and contribute significantly to project success.

Tasks

What tasks await you as Senior Cost Manager Data Center (m/f/d)?

  • Overall responsibility for construction cost planning, budgeting and forecasting of demanding data center projects.
  • Analysis of cost developments, risks and opportunities as well as the development of sound decision-making bases for project management.
  • Management of tendering and awarding processes, including tender evaluation, contract negotiations and award recommendations.
  • Ensure transparent cost controlling by monitoring budgets, commitments, changes and forecasts.
  • Commercial evaluation of addenda and project changes as well as support for change management.
  • Conduct cost, market and benchmark analyses to identify potential for optimisation and savings.
  • Further development of cost management standards, processes and tools as well as technical support and coordination within the project team.

Requirements

Which ones do you bring as Senior Cost Manager Data Center (m/f/d)?

  • Completed studies in civil engineering, industrial engineering, quantity surveying, project management, building technology or a comparable education.
  • Several years of professional experience in cost management, calculation/construction cost planning, quantity surveying or in construction management/project management
  • In-depth knowledge in cost planning, forecasting, tendering, procurement and commercial contracting.
  • Experience in change, claim or contract management as well as in dealing with cost management and reporting tools.
  • Ideally, experience in complex construction, infrastructure, industrial or data center projects.
  • Strong analytical thinking skills, high affinity for numbers and a strong awareness of costs and risks.
  • Independent and structured working methods combined with convincing communication and negotiation skills.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
