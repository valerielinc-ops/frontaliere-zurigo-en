Full description

Spital Männedorf – Your very special place of work. Whether a small “thank you” or a morning “grüezi” – we focus on real appreciation and humanity. The proximity in our house makes everyday life uncomplicated and allows patient-centered work at the highest level. In an environment where interdisciplinary cooperation at eye level is a matter of course, we offer individual treatments from primary medical care to rehabilitation.

The nature of your activity

  • Collaboration (preparation, accompaniment, implementation in medical diagnostics) e.g. residual urine determination with disposable catheters, urodynamics (bubble pressure measurement), preparation and assistance in bladder reflections. Guidance for carrying out a drinking/miction protocol, evaluation thereof.
  • Advice and training in the use of tools to promote and maintain continence (in dealing with vaginal tampons or pessaries, schools of intermittent self-catheterism, adaptation of absorbent material/offer).
  • Instructions for handling lying blistering catheter, discharge systems. Alternation of transurethral permanent catheters or suprapubic fistula catheters.
  • Advice on skin care for incontinence, as well as for peri-/postmenopausal mucous membrane changes in the genital area
  • Documentation/collection of the therapies carried out in the electronic medical record
  • Power measurement
  • Participation in the interdisciplinary pelvic floor board with urology, visceral surgery/gastroenterology and physiotherapy

The basis of your performance

  • Urotherapeut-in with completed training at the Careum Aarau or at the Klinikum l.d.W. in Bremen or
  • APN (advanced practice nurse), care expert for continence promotion or
  • Dipl. Nursing specialist HF/FH or for admission corresponding qualification with mandatory commitment to further training as urotherapeut-in, APN or nursing expert in continence promotion
  • Empathy, social skills
  • Responsibility, reliability, team spirit and flexibility
  • Very good knowledge of German, every other language is advantageous

The advantages of your position

  • Opportunity to actively shape the role and further develop the field of urotherapy
  • Working in a specialized, interdisciplinary environment with close collaboration across different disciplines
  • Professional and personal development through targeted further education and training

The reasons for your decision

We offer modern working conditions, promote internal training and focus on professional exchange across all disciplines. The quiet location directly on the lake and the proximity to the vibrant city of Zurich round off your workplace. In addition, you can look forward to very special advantages.
