Our group is a dynamic, very international working group at the Department of Environmental Systems Sciences at ETH Zurich. In addition to the greenhouse gas exchange between Öcosystems and atmosphere, our group’s research primarily includes the Öcophysiology of plants and the Öcosystem services of agro-ecosystems. The position is limited to 3 years and will be filled no later than December 2026. Project background It is part of PhenoMix: Multidimensional field phenotyping of crop mixtures, a project of ETH Zurich, the Swiss Data Science Center and Agroscope. As part of PhenoMix, we are investigating mixtures of different pea and lentil varieties with wheat, oats and barley grown on an experimental phenotyping platform and a neighboring field. In our work package we collect the microclimate of the stock, plant characteristics and Öcosystem services of pure cultures and mixtures. We use stable isotopes of carbon, nitrogen and water to capture light, nitrogen and water use and to determine the complementarity of mixtures during three growing seasons. Job description
