Developer in digital business EFZ

Zurich | 100% | Reference 8079

Are you interested in technology, digital trends and do not want to program all day only on the computer? Then the apprenticeship as a developer / in digital business EFZ at SIX could be just the right thing for you.

With us, you will analyze processes, work on digital solutions and learn how companies use modern technologies. You’ll talk to different teams, develop ideas, visualize concepts, and help improve digital products and processes.

During your apprenticeship, you will get to know different areas of SIX and change regularly to new teams. So you find out what is special to you and gain versatile practical experience. You will be accompanied by experienced vocational trainers who support and promote you. Step by step, you take on more responsibility and develop both professionally and personally.

This is what awaits you

  • You will work in various projects and gain insights into the development and implementation of digital solutions. In addition, you analyze existing processes and support them in making them more efficient and future-oriented.
  • Every 6 to 12 months you move to a new department and get to know different tasks and exciting topics.
  • In the first year of apprenticeship, you spend the first months in an instructive base year with an external partner.
  • During your apprenticeship, you also attend vocational school one to two days a week. There you will deepen your knowledge in technical subjects as well as in general topics and languages.
  • You are part of a large community of learners. In addition to daily cooperation, you meet regularly for joint activities and events.

You bring that with you

  • Very good final grades of secondary school A or equivalent education
  • Pleasure in dealing with people as well as strong team skills
  • Interest in digital technologies and current trends
  • Talent for presenting and organizing
  • High motivation to learn new things and take on responsibility
