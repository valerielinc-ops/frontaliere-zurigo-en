Full description

Become part of our Civil team (infrastructure and civil engineering rail, roadway and tunnel construction). We manage, develop, design and plan railway, tunnel and infrastructure projects of all kinds. We accompany these projects from planning to implementation. Take on responsibility with us as: INTERN ETHZ / EPFL CIVIL 80 – 100% - ZURICH YOUR TASKS

  • Participation in demanding projects of various sizes in the areas of railway infrastructure construction, civil engineering / railway roadway and tunnel construction
  • Support of the project teams in the daily engineering tasks, such as static and geotechnical calculations, evaluation and presentation of data
  • Assistance with tenders
  • Participation in the preparation of reports
  • Participation in project meetings
  • If required: Interesting, varied activities supporting a construction management team – even for several weeks

YOUR OPPORTUNITIES

  • Gaining professional experience and getting to know our company
  • Applying the learned methods to real problems in projects
  • Training, guidance and support from experienced civil engineers
  • Working with state-of-the-art design applications and methods (BIM, BIM2Field)
  • Strong practical relevance and insight into the varied field of activity as a civil engineer

YOUR PROFILE

  • Bachelor's degree ETHZ / EPFL in Civil Engineering, or equivalent education internationally
  • Candidates shortly before completing their Bachelor's degree will be considered equally
  • Interest in putting the knowledge acquired during the studies into practice
  • Commitment and fun working in a team
  • Very good written and spoken German language skills (level C1)
  • Interest in working for at least 6 months
