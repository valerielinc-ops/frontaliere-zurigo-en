Full description

The Clienia-ICT supports the specialists of the psychiatric and psychotherapeutic practices and clinics of the Clienia Group with professional ICT services.

For ICT we are looking for an agreement

They contribute significantly to the security of ICT infrastructure and, in particular, protect sensitive health and patient data.

Their tasks

  • Evaluate, administer and monitor security components
  • Creating security concepts and decision-making basis
  • Analyze and remedy vulnerabilities
  • Technical implementation of governance requirements
  • Support ICT team on security issues
  • Solve incidents and process service requests
  • Identity & Access Management
  • Evaluating security technology trends
  • accompany BCM/DR planning
  • Maintaining system documentation

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a computer scientist EFZ platform development or completed training of a technical nature with further training in the ICT/Security area
  • Higher education (HF, FH) and/or specific training (e.g. CAS/MAS Information Security, CISSP, CISM, CompTIA Security+) are advantageous
  • You have in-depth knowledge in the core security technologies Firewall (Sophos), PAM (BeyondTrust), CASB, NAC, EDR/XDR, SIEM/SOC (Microsoft Defender), Email Security, Vulnerability Management, Backup & Recovery Security, PKI and Certificate Management
  • They are resilient in crisis situations and are characterized by a high degree of integrity and trustworthiness
  • You think analytically, networked and work team-oriented and independently
  • They show a high willingness to learn and initiative

Our offer

  • Future-oriented working environment and climate in a positive and collegial team
  • Flexible and self-determined design of working hours
  • Mobile work, at home, as well as regularly at our locations
  • Various training opportunities with the support of the Clienia Group
  • Family-friendly and modern employment conditions
