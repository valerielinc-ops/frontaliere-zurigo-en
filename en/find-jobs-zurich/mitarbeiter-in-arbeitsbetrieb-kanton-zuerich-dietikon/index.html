Full description

Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Prisons and Reintegration Zurich Employee/working company 80 - 100% Prison and Reintegration (JuWe) is part of the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs of the Canton of Zurich and employs around 1,400 women and men with a wide range of professional profiles. JuWe is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as the implementation of the various types of detention. For the employees of JuWe, the successful social reintegration of people who have committed crimes and the prevention of recidivism is of particular importance. The Zurich Prisons of Detention (UGZ) – one of eight main departments of JuWe – currently operates seven prisons for pre-trial and security detention. One of these prisons is the Limmattal Prison (GFL), which is located in the district building Dietikon and offers 121 places for detainees together with the Horgen Prison Department (GFH). In addition to a department for adult detainees, the GFL has a strictly separate youth department and a department for crisis intervention.
