Full description

Tasks

  • Creating and/or preparing outpatient and inpatient medical letters, insurance reports, findings, medical record entries and prescriptions according to specifications
  • Processing incoming emails and phone calls
  • Independently handling concerns and inquiries from patients and physicians
  • Requesting external documents
  • Scanning documents
  • Sending reports
  • General administrative tasks such as mail processing, etc.
  • Covering for the executive assistant during her absence, provided the appropriate qualifications are met

Requirements

  • Completed medical training with further administrative training/experience or commercial training with further training in the medical field
  • Professional experience in the same or a similar position
  • Logical, structured thinking, with enjoyment of independent work in an interdisciplinary team and a high quality standard
  • Very good knowledge of German spelling and terminology; knowledge of English and French is an advantage

We offer

  • Salaries according to cantonal guidelines (cost-of-living adjustment as of 2023 of 4.4 %)
  • 5 weeks of vacation, starting from 50. Age year 29 days, from which 60. Age year 31 days
  • Own pension fund with an above-average employer contribution
  • Work according to the principles of Lean Management
  • Flat hierarchies and short decision-making paths
  • Employee discount at our public restaurant, parking options, contribution toward a Half-Fare or General Travelcard, as well as discounts at our pharmacy

Description

Klinik Lengg AG in Zurich is the center of expertise for epileptology and neurological rehabilitation.

It is our aim to enable patients with neurological diseases to achieve the greatest possible rehabilitation and to ensure an accurate diagnosis and sustainable treatment for patients with epilepsy. Around 300 employees are committed, in a compassionate, competent, innovative and reliable manner, to ensuring that our patients regain the greatest possible independence - and thus their quality of life. Become part of one of our interdisciplinary and interprofessional teams.
