Full description

As of 1 February 2027 or by agreement we are looking for a midwife BSc 80-100%

Your responsibilities

  • They independently manage births and look after the parents with their newborns
  • You work closely with the medical service
  • We support a natural obstetrics, also for coeste and twin births
  • We accompany about 800 births per year
  • The midwives work in the rotation system, they make services in the birth room, as well as on the postpartum station
  • They are responsible for the nursing care of inpatient high-risk pregnant women, partly also for the care of gynecological patients
  • For your assigned patients, you assume responsibility and control of the care process
  • They carry out pregnancy checks and advise the couples

Your profile

  • You have completed your studies for the Dipl. Midwife FH and ideally already bring several years of professional experience
  • They are resilient, flexible and bring willingness to take on all shifts
  • Enrich your openness, willingness to learn and commitment You are our team
  • Your strong social skills and communication skills as well as your good PC skills round off your profile

What we offer you

  • Working independently in a motivated and collegial team that supports each other.
  • A place of work with the best connection to public transport, as well as cost-effective staff parking spaces.
  • Modern infrastructure and a working environment in which you can feel at home – lake views included.
  • Diverse benefits – from great employee discounts to further education offers.
  • A pleasant working atmosphere in an attractive location near Zurich, which has a lot to offer you both professionally and privately.

Benefits

  • 42 hour week (Doctors 50)
  • Overtime is usually compensated by free time of the same duration

  • Daily 20 min paid break
  • Changing time is considered as working time
  • Possibility for Home Office

Ongoing training

  • Internal training for employees
  • Participation in costs and working time for further education and training by employer
  • As an education and training hospital, we offer various internships and apprenticeships
  • Leadership Development Seminars

Rebates and conditions

  • Annual free flu vaccination
  • Hospital boat for rent
  • Free meals for night workers
  • In Horgen and the surrounding area, various selected companies grant at least 10% discount when presenting the identity card (e.g. Free Zoo Zurich annual tickets, 15% discount in the Alpamare, Brack.ch up to 25% and more).
  • Fleet discounts at amag partners (Audi, VW, Seat, Skoda), BWM and Mini as well as Mercedes and Smart

Extensive insurance

  • See-Spital offers 16 weeks of maternity leave at 100% wage continuation instead of the legally required 14 weeks at 80%
  • Premium for accident supplementary insurance (private) at the expense of the employer

Work-life balance

  • Possibility of part-time and hourly employment (on demand)
  • Flexible working hours (annual working hours)
  • Opportunity for unpaid leave

Occupational health management
