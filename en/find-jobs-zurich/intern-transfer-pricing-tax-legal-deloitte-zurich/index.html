Location: Zurich Work Pattern Percentage: 80-100% Start date: as soon as possible Our Transfer Pricing Team is looking for you! Our Team offers integrated advisory services that help our clients to optimise and align their business structures with their transfer pricing policies and engage in efficient and compliant tax management throughout their value chain. From day one, you'll receive the opportunity to work in a client-facing role on projects in key industries. You will support our Swiss team in Zurich in providing transfer pricing services to our clients by applying a mix of economics, finance, accounting, (international) tax and legal concepts. How you can make an impact

  • Review, understand and assess the value chain and cross-border operations of multinational companies (e.g. flow of products, services, intellectual property);
  • Participate in the development of concepts for the optimisation of existing business models as well as subsequent implementation of such models on a global basis;
  • Perform complex economic and financial analyses (e.g. valuations, credit rating analyses, benchmarking studies);
  • Prepare transfer pricing documentation, review and design transfer pricing policies and support corporate clients in transfer pricing projects management and implementation. What you bring to succeed
  • You have a bachelor’s and/or master’s degree in economics, accounting, business, finance, or tax law;
  • You have the ability to analyse complex problems and to develop and communicate innovative solutions;
  • You are a reliable and committed team player;
  • You communicate confidently in English and German. Any further languages would be an advantage
  • What sets you apart
  • You are highly motivated and eager to learn;
  • You enjoy working in a fast-paced, international environment and adapt quickly;
  • You bring a positive, can-do attitude and take ownership of your work. Your team Become part of a dynamic, highly motivated and international team in our Zurich office. Our team is diverse, bringing together different backgrounds, perspectives, and experiences.
