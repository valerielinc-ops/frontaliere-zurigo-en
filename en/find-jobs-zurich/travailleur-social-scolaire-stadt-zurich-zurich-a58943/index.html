Full description

For the new overall municipal organizational unit of the school social work since August 2023, which is part of the department of child and youth welfare of the social services, we are looking for the provision of the services of the school social work in the school district Limmattal, in the school buildings Pfingstweid (50%, kindergarten and primary level) and Albisriederplatz (30%, upper level) as of 1. December 2026 or by arrangement a new team member.

Tasks

  • They are the professionals for social and personal topics of the students and their families.
  • They support the school management and the teachers of a school house on social issues.
  • They develop targeted measures and solutions on topics such as child protection, children’s rights, bullying and violence.
  • They provide access to a wide range of support.
  • They shape the living space school.
  • They provide our services in close cooperation with the actors in the living space school.
  • In both school buildings, school sociology has been established for years.

Profile

  • You bring a university degree in social work. A CAS in school social work is beneficial
  • You have experience in school social work or youth and family assistance
  • You enjoy working interdisciplinary, networked and resource-oriented
  • You are an open, outgoing and committed personality and confident in dealing with change

We offer

An interesting and varied work with independent processing of your topics and tasks. In our organizational unit with about 90 school social workers, we maintain open and transparent communication. An attractive training offer as well as internal specialist positions to support in day-to-day business. For more information click here https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/en/bildung/volksschule/support-foerderung/schulsozialarbeit.html

About us

With around 2,400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a social Zurich. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.
