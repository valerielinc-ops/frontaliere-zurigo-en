Full description

Are you interested in technology, apps or digital solutions? Do you want to understand how programs are created that are used by many people every day? Then our “IT-way-up” is just right for you. We offer you:

  • A versatile two-year education with EFZ degree in computer science
  • Personal support by experienced practical trainers
  • A practical education where you learn step by step
  • Exciting projects where you work from the beginning In application development, you ensure that our programs and apps run smoothly and safely by developing creative and high-quality software solutions, fixing errors and continuously introducing improvements. You will learn programming languages such as Cobol, Java, Python and C#, test applications and work closely with other developers. Your education This is your teaching: Your education is a mixture of practice and theory. At the UBS Training Center, you will learn the basics of computer science. In addition, you visit the Vocational School on one to two days a week, where you deepen your IT expertise. In the following months, you will work on exciting IT projects in the international environment. So you see directly how your work creates real digital solutions. At the end of your training, you will receive your Federal Certificate of Ability (EFZ) and will be well prepared for your future. Many apprentices then decide to join UBS. Your education can be the start of a long-term development. Your team During your teaching, you are part of a group of learners with whom you regularly exchange ideas and work on new topics together. You support each other, learn from each other and grow together as a team. This often creates friendships that persist beyond teaching. In your team, you actively shape the future. Together, we integrate artificial intelligence (AI) into our digital solutions and processes to drive contemporary innovation, work more efficiently and make our everyday life easier. You will learn how AI makes our digital products smarter and how you can contribute to further development yourself. Be part of our team and be the change you want to see. Your knowledge and experience You have a degree from a federally recognized maturity school, commercial middle school or a basic vocational education with vocational maturity. But maybe you are already studying and you lack the practical relevance? How you inspire us:
  • You burn for new trends and technologies and teamwork is important to you
  • You are motivated to constantly learn new things and get involved
  • You like to think around the corner and find solutions for every challenge
  • You quickly grasp new connections and can imagine innovative solutions Have you become curious? Then simply apply online here. On this website you will also find further details about teaching and the application process.
