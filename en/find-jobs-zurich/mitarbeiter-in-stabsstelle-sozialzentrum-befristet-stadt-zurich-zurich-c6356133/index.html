Full description

Gaining insight. Take responsibility. To help shape Zurich.

The social services of the City of Zurich are changing: Following the reorganisation of its structure in early 2026, a project will develop the future design of this function. For the temporary re-occupation from January 2027 to December 2027, we are looking for a committed personality who will support the head of social centers Selnau and Albisriederhaus in their versatile management day and get an exciting insight into the work of the city administration.

Tasks

  • You ensure the leadership and project management support for the head of social centers. This includes a wide range of planning and decision-making tasks for the governing bodies.
  • They shall be responsible for the efficient functioning of the Centre Secretariat and shall lead a Secretariat staff member.
  • They assume responsibility for the spatial and infrastructural concerns of the social centre as well as for health and safety issues. You will also process external complaints and work closely with the location-responsible department manager.
  • They design internal communication, ensure an open flow of information to employees and carry out internal training and events.
  • As part of a dedicated and collegial management committee, you participate in the strategic and operational development of the organizational unit, which comprises the social centers Selnau and Albisriederhaus.

Profile

  • A completed university or university of applied sciences degree
  • Experience in staff or assistance functions is an advantage.
  • A pronounced service orientation, a quick understanding as well as resilience and flexibility.
  • initiative and a structured, independent working method.
  • Confident oral and written expression skills in German as well as very good knowledge in MS Office and MS Teams.

We offer

A versatile job in an appreciative environment, the close cooperation with the head of social centers and the professional exchange with your job partner at the Albisriederhaus location. In addition, exciting insights into the city administration and the opportunity to work partly in the home office. And last but not least, your work makes a concrete contribution to the city of Zurich and its population.

About us

In the social services more than 1000 employees shape the social Zurich. We accompany and support people in different situations, promote participation and strengthen social cohesion. Our services include social welfare, child and adult protection, school social work, child and youth welfare and socioculture. We do our work interdisciplinary and throughout the city: in school houses, counselling centres, the five social centres as well as in facilities such as the Youth and Culture House Dynamo, the Debate House Karl der Grosse and the district centres Schütze and Bakeranlage.
