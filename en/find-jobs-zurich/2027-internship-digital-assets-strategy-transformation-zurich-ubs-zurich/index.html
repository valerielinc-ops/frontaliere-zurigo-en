Full description

Your role Are you passionate about digital assets, emerging technologies, strategy execution and organizational transformation? Do you enjoy solving complex business challenges, leveraging AI to drive innovation, and working with senior stakeholders across a global organization?

We're looking for ambitious students to join our 2026 Internship Program within our Group Digital Assets team.

You’ll get to

  • support the execution of strategic and transformational initiatives across Personal & Corporate Banking, Wealth Management, Investment Bank, Asset Management and Group Functions
  • contribute to the development and implementation of UBS's digital assets strategy, operating model and governance framework
  • leverage AI, automation and data analytics tools to improve decision-making, operational efficiency and management insights
  • assist in driving cross-divisional collaboration and coordination across business, technology, operations and control functions
  • analyze market developments, client trends, regulatory developments and competitive dynamics in digital assets, blockchain, tokenization and emerging financial market infrastructures

Your team You'll be working in the Digital Assets Strategy & Transformation team in Zürich. The team is responsible for shaping and driving UBS's digital assets strategy and transformation agenda across the firm. We work closely with senior leadership, business divisions, technology teams and control functions to accelerate the adoption of digital asset capabilities, deliver strategic initiatives, enhance organizational effectiveness and capture business value from emerging technologies.

Your expertise

We’re looking for a candidate who

  • has completed at least 4 semesters of a bachelor's degree, and graduated no longer than 12 months ago (bachelor's or master's degree)
  • has a competitive minimum GPA of 4.5 according to the Swiss grading system
  • has values that align with ours: hard-working, trustworthy, dedicated and collaborative
  • is a strategic thinker with strong communication skills
  • is motivated to work in a business with high demands and tight deadlines
  • can evidence how experimental and responsible use of AI can improve outcomes and efficiency
  • has excellent English skills, German is a plus
  • has a strong interest in digital assets, financial innovation and emerging technologies

Start date: 1 December 2026 or by agreement

We’re looking for someone who’s curious and wants to thrive in a business that never stops moving. We welcome all majors, but it’s important to have an interest in the financial industry. We want to see what makes you unique and discover what you can bring to our team. Perhaps you’ve organized a fundraising event or taken part in team sports? Or stepped out of your comfort zone to learn about a subject you found challenging? Let us know how your achievements match the skills we’re after.

Your program This paid internship will last six months. You’ll work with our experts and learn about the industry, our clients and our firm. You’ll be right at the heart of our business, learning from your colleagues, taking part in day-to-day operations. You'll be given mentors along the way and will have the chance to get to know some of the most senior members of our teams. You’ll have plenty of opportunities to develop new skills and make contacts along the way. If you like challenges, enjoy learning and want to work where no two days are the same, this program is for you.

If you have a successful internship, you may get an offer to return for our Graduate Talent Program after you've completed your studies.
