We make habitats worth living.

Are you motivated to work with technology? Then you are exactly right with us. Become part of a dedicated and motivated team in an innovative company.

Your tasks

  • In your function as an electrical planner: in / Diagram Drawer: in you create together with our building automation specialists the electrical schemes, principle schemes and topologies and are thus the linchpin in the first phase of a project
  • You clear connections and connections with the manufacturers of field devices and are responsible for the cable lists of electrical installers
  • Furthermore, you actively help with the creation of visualizations and function descriptions
  • Another task is the development and further development of macros and templates in CAD

You bring that with you

  • You have completed an apprenticeship as an electrical planner: in EFZ or have completed a basic technical training
  • With electroschemas and CAD (preferably with the eXs E-CAD software) you are very familiar
  • An excellent understanding of complex systems and plants is one of your strengths
  • Your way of working is customer- and quality-oriented and you enjoy working in a team
  • A good communication ability in German rounds off your profile
