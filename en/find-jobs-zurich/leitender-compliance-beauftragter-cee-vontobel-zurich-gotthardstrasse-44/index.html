Full description

We are looking for a talented Senior Compliance Officer to join our Client Lifecycle Compliance Department embedded within the Legal and Compliance Function of Bank Vontobel in Zurich. Vontobel is a Switzerland-based international investment management firm offering investment solutions and advice to private and institutional clients across 27 global locations. As a family-owned business listed on the SIX Swiss Exchange, the bank maintains entrepreneurial independence, social responsibility and a client-focused, investment-led approach.

What would your role be?

  • You will be part of the Onboarding and Review Team covering private clients from Central and Eastern Europe with an increased risk as well as Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs).
  • You will act as a close advisor to the Relationship Managers in matters related to AML and compliance.
  • Provide compliance related training to the first line of defence and act as a trusted stakeholder with other departments of the bank representing compliance.

In this role, your main duties and responsibilities include the following tasks:

  • Independently handling complex client cases and performing quality assurance reviews within the Lifecycle Compliance CEE team.
  • Ensuring Compliance oversight throughout the entire client lifecycle for Central and Eastern European clients, including onboarding, periodic reviews, event-driven reviews and ad-hoc client assessments.
  • Conducting risk-based Compliance assessments for client onboardings, including KYC, due diligence, sanctions screening, adverse media reviews and reputational risk assessments.
  • Performing Compliance assessments related to periodic and event-driven client reviews, with a particular focus on politically exposed persons (PEPs), high-risk clients, sanctions-related matters and other sensitive client relationships.
  • Acting as Subject Matter Expert for Central and Eastern European markets, advising first-line stakeholders, providing second-line expertise and collaborating with relevant control functions on compliance, AML, sanctions, regulatory, reputational risk and client lifecycle-related matters.
  • Independently challenging risk assessments and escalating material compliance concerns where appropriate.
  • Preparing Compliance assessments and case presentations for senior management and governance committees to facilitate decision-making on high-risk and complex client relationships.
  • Monitoring and assessing high-risk and restricted transactions on an ex-ante basis.
  • Conducting internal controls, quality assurance reviews and monitoring activities to ensure adherence to regulatory requirements and internal policies.

What are we looking for?

  • University degree and compliance related certifications.
  • At least 5 years of experience in Swiss private banking covering client onboarding and reviews for Central and Eastern European clients.
  • Strong stakeholder management and collaboration skills.
  • Eloquent and capable of analyzing, summarizing and presenting information succinctly, both in writing and verbally.
  • Fluent in English and German; knowledge of Russian, Ukrainian or other Slavic languages would be an advantage.
  • Affinity for Artificial Intelligence (AI) and a willingness to embrace AI-enabled tools and ways of working.

What do we offer?

  • Top Zurich location with collaboration spaces and free lunch.
  • Friendly, diverse team in an open-office space.
  • All the latest technology to help you excel and advance.
  • Agile environment in how we think, the way we work and our trust for each other.
  • Flat hierarchy with collaboration at all levels.
  • Campus feeling and a collaborative spirit.

We are looking for talented people, not just a CV that ticks all the boxes. Even if you do not have absolutely everything we have asked for, if this role sounds like a good fit for you, we encourage you to apply.
