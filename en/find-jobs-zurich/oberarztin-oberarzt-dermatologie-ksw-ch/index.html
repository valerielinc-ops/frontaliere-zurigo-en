Full description

The Department of Dermatology and Allergology at the Cantonal Hospital Winterthur (KSW) is an established competence centre for dermatological and allergological issues in the region. With a broad range of outpatient and inpatient services, close interdisciplinary cooperation and cooperation with the Dermatological University Hospital Zurich, we offer a versatile and technically demanding working environment. As a recognized FMH training centre for dermatology and venerology (category C), we are also involved in the training of the next generation of doctors. As part of the strategic development and the targeted expansion of our department, we are looking for a senior physician dermatology and venerology immediately or by agreement.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for taking care of patients in our wide range of outpatient dermatological consultation hours and bring your expertise to bear in the diagnosis and treatment of a wide range of diseases.
  • As part of the inpatient consultancy service, you will advise the various clinics and departments of the KSW on complex dermatological issues and maintain a close interdisciplinary cooperation.
  • You are involved in the supervision and further education of our assistant doctors and actively contribute to ensuring a high-quality education
  • You participate in the conceptual and organizational further development of the department and have the opportunity to contribute your own specialist focus and innovative ideas
  • Depending on your interest and qualifications, you can further develop existing special consultation hours, establish new offers and deepen your knowledge in the field of allergology.

Profile

  • You have a CH or EU doctor’s diploma with recognition in Switzerland and have completed the specialist title Dermatology and Venerology
  • A pleasant working atmosphere in the team is important to you and the empathetic treatment of patients is a matter of course for you
  • Furthermore, you bring very good German skills in word and writing (language diploma B2) and have good IT skills

Benefits: Work and private life should fit together. Therefore, the KSW offers you – where the company makes it possible – flexible and flexible working hours, home office, generous maternity leave, paternity leave and employment levels that can be adapted to different phases of life. Develop yourself further professionally and personally: Every year, around 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses are open to you at KSW. We also support external training – temporally and/or financially.

  • Sometimes it needs support. In the event of personal stress, prolonged illness or difficult experiences in your professional or private life, various confidential discussions are available at KSW.
  • At KSW we work together and for each other. Appreciation, open and respectful communication and cooperation across professional groups and departments shape our everyday work. You should be able to contribute with your ideas and strengths and develop. Your future is also ensured: You benefit from very good social benefits and a pension fund solution with BVK, which is significantly above the legal minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, you are very well covered as a KSW employee.
  • Your health is important to us. Benefit from discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training and courses as well as discounts in pharmacies and selected health insurance companies.
  • Family and work can be well connected with us. The daycare center La Luna with spacious opening hours is right next to the KSW. In addition, the KSW – depending on income – financially supports you with the crib costs.

There is also something to discover outside of work: Thanks to our cooperation with cultural partners in Winterthur and the surrounding area, you regularly benefit from discounts for concerts and theatre performances.
