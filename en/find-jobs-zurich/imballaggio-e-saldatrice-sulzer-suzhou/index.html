About the role

Job Summary The role is responsible for assembling, testing, and inspecting products to ensure they meet quality and safety standards. This role supports the manufacturing process by identifying defects, maintaining equipment, and following production protocols. Key Responsibilities

  • Collaborate with team members to meet production goals.
