Shape your future – together with us!

Every success story begins with faith in oneself.

Together with your commitment and your individual contribution, this will become a forward-looking partnership. For you, for us and for our customers. And not just for the moment. But in the long term. Write your own success story with us now. Know you can.

In the field of data, technology and innovation, around 600 software and system developers work in Switzerland: in-house and develop the technology of tomorrow. We also collaborate in global teams and networks.

We shape and integrate the latest tools, organize hackathons and our agile product teams run self-organized and are self-responsible from A to Z.

This is what awaits you

  • You design, control and support transformation and development processes in an agile organization
  • You accompany executives and teams in the optimization of their structures, processes and working methods in the sense of an agile culture
  • You develop strategies for sustainable organizational development, implement them and continuously evaluate their effectiveness
  • You initiate and accompany workshops, training courses and reflection processes to promote an open, innovative corporate culture
  • You will work closely with other experts to exchange best practices and increase the effectiveness of the measures
  • You are the catalyst for AI transformation

You bring that with you

  • Experience in organizational development, team development and transformation, preferably in an agile environment
  • Understanding agile methods as well as knowledge of leadership and cultural development
  • Excellent communication skills, empathy and a solution-oriented, strategic way of working
  • Ability to control complex change processes and adapt to different target groups
  • Independent, structured working method with high self-motivation and team spirit
  • You recognize opportunities, are open to new ways and creative solutions and address them.

This is what we offer you We are convinced: Satisfied employees provide the best services. That’s why we invest in benefits that offer noticeable benefits – beyond the usual.

Flexibility and freedom: Smart Working (flexible working model) Annual working hours, 5 weeks holidays (from 50 years 6 weeks), holiday gift 2 weeks every 5 years, holiday purchase up to 10 additional days/per year

Job and family: family-friendly meeting hours, 22 weeks of maternity leave, 8 weeks of leave for the other parent, crèche subsidies, company family allowances, days off for caring for relatives and in challenging, personal situations

Precautions and insurance: Attractive pension fund with far-reaching non-compulsory benefits including choice savings options, up to 30% discount on insurance products

Flexible Benefits: AXA Shareplan (employee participation), CHF 500 annually on the advantage platform Swibeco, discounted lunch or lunch card, Smart Working flat rate CHF 200 per year

Mobility: refund for half-tax, Reka-Pay up to CHF 1’000 per year (20% discount), fleet discount, free e-charging station, AXA rental bicycles

Healthy balance in everyday work: Free check-ups, annual health weeks, a wide range of exercise offers (online/offline), massage offers, rest rooms, employee care, online health portal, various seminars/events around health prevention & mental health

Development: 3 days of individual learning time, free coaching, wide range of learning & development opportunities, financial participation in further education, promotion of internal mobility, talent development offers, seminars for employees 55+ (AXA Horizon), engagement in communities on various topics, participation in innovation projects (Innoday, AXA Hackathon, UnConference), 2 days of volunteering (environment & social affairs)
