Become part of our team!

Raiffeisenbank Sindberg is a regionally anchored, successful and modern regional bank with around 45 employees at three locations. We stand for closeness, trust and commitment in the region and are looking for you to continue to grow together.

What awaits you?

  • You advise our customers in the areas of numbers, savings, investing and provision.
  • You develop individual solutions and accompany our customers in different life situations.
  • You create positive customer experiences and are the first point of contact at the reception and in the cash area.
  • You recognize potential, communicate our customers with the right specialists and actively expand a customer portfolio team.
  • You contribute your ideas and contribute to the joint success of our team.

What do you bring?

  • You bring experience in banking or in a comparable service-oriented function.
  • You enjoy customer contacts and needs-based advice.
  • You are ideally rooted in the Sindberg region and know the Raiffeisen environment.
  • You convince with your winning nature and pronounced host qualities.
  • You work independently and reliably.
  • You are a team player and actively engage in common goals.

We maintain a family environment

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

We live diversity and equal opportunities

We promote the different perspectives, experiences and competencies in our teams. Because this diversity makes us special and contributes decisively to our success.

We promote personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.

We offer attractive conditions and social benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages in banking products, various purchase discounts at third parties and an above-average retirement provision.
