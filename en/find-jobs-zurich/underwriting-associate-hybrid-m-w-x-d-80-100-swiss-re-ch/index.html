Become part of a global leader in risk management and help make the world more resilient! About the role In this position, you are at the heart of our insurance activities. They support national and international insurance programs and support our underwriters in Germany. With your pronounced detail and organizational skills, you ensure smooth processing of customer documentation and ensure the highest data quality standards. Their tasks

  • Management and supervision of national and multinational insurance programmes in the management and co-insurance business
  • Support for underwriters in day-to-day business
  • Careful processing and organization of incoming customer documents
  • Ensuring data integrity through precise data entry, document creation and storage in our IT systems
  • Regular communication with customers and business partners to clarify requests
  • Close cooperation with various service units within the company
  • Support for financial processes such as technical billing, payment orders and reconciliation of accounts
  • Ensuring data quality through regular checks and inspections
  • Participation in process optimization and continuous improvement initiatives
  • Participation in projects at national and European level
  • Compliance with regulatory requirements of the insurance industry

With around 50 locations worldwide, we offer innovative insurance solutions for large and medium-sized international companies. Our team helps customers reduce their risks while providing a world-class claims service for added security. We promote a flexible working environment that is ideal for curious and adaptable professionals. Your profile You are a detailed professional with insurance experience and feel comfortable in a dynamic, international environment.   • Strong ability to quickly capture and link different processes and process steps
