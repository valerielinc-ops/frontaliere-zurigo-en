Welcome to Medbase Zurich Wiedikon

Our versatile, lively Medical Center (no walk-in practice) is located in the urban district 3, a few minutes walk from the Wiedikon train station with a view of the Üetliberg. Here we look after patients interdisciplinary and quality-oriented. The team, consisting of around 40 employees, consists of specialists and therapists from various specialist areas. The center’s main focus is on the interplay between GP and complementary medicine. At Medbase Zurich Wiedikon we appreciate the respectful and helpful approach in the team. We also ensure a balanced work-life balance. Working part-time is possible in all functions and working hours can be flexible.
