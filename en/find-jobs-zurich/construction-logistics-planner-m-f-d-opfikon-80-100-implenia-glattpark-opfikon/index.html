Full description

Where others see cranes, material flows and driveways, do you recognize a perfectly organized construction site logistics? Then we are looking for you as a construction logistics planner (m/f/d) at our location in Opfikon. What tasks await you as a construction logistics planner (m/f/d)?

  • You will accompany construction logistics projects along the SIA project phases – from the preliminary project through the tendering process to implementation. This includes, in particular, the elaboration of construction logistics concepts, the determination of requirements for storage areas, traffic routes, construction site equipment, construction water and construction electricity, the preparation of logistics schedules and the participation in the preparation of specifications for construction logistics.
  • You are the central contact person for internal and external project partners and actively design project meetings with your expertise
  • Together with the project team, you successfully advance projects and ensure that the project goals are achieved and our quality standards are met.
  • You have an apprenticeship or study in construction and already bring several years of professional practice in work preparation (AVOR), construction logistics, construction site equipment etc.
  • The use of CAD programs and other digital tools you master effortlessly
  • You have a high quality standard, recognize potential for improvement at first glance and attach importance to clean, thoughtful solutions. BCL is part of Implenia As a leading Swiss construction and real estate service provider, Implenia develops, realizes and manages living spaces, working environments and infrastructure for future generations in Switzerland and Germany. Implenia also offers tunnelling and related infrastructure projects in other markets. The company combines the know-how of highly qualified consulting, development, planning and execution units under one roof into an integrated, multinationally leading construction and real estate service provider.
