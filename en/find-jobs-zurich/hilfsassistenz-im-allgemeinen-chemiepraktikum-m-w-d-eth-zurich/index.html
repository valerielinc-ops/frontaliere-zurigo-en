Assistant in the General Chemical Internship (m/f/d)

10%-30%, Zurich, temporary

Printed Printing

The Institute of Pharmaceutical Sciences is looking for assistants (only for ETH-internal master students or bachelor students) for the AC1 internship (General Chemistry, 535-1001-00P) under Dr. Stefan Gruber. The deployment period is from 29.09.2026 to 15.12.2026 Tuesday afternoon or/and Thursday afternoon from 13.00 to 18.00.

Job description

  • Introduction of students to experiments
  • Supporting students in carrying out the experiments
  • Training and control of HCI safety rules
  • Control of students' laboratory notes
  • Maintenance of laboratory infrastructure

Profile

  • They are enrolled at ETH
  • German fluent or English fluent with basic knowledge of German
  • At least in the 3rd year of study, better with passed bachelor exam
  • Basic internship in chemistry in the main subject or completes biology/pharmacy, or a comparable internship abroad
