Full description

You will work across different services that are run on our custody platform, together with product managers, engineers, operations teams and our external technology partners.

  • Off-Exchange Custody
  • clients trade on exchanges while their assets stay in Sygnum custody. You will help onboard new trading venues: mapping the collateral and settlement flows, and writing the operational runbooks and client documentation each launch needs.
  • Transfers
  • deposits, withdrawals, internal transfers and cold-storage movements. You will work on enhancing the existing transfer flows and their compliance screening, and confirm each change behaves as specified before it reaches clients.
  • Wallets
  • the wallet types, whitelisting rules and token and blockchain integrations that underpin every product above. You will run new-asset requests end to end, from the initial assessment to the asset going live.
  • Staking
  • extending the offering to new protocols and assets, from protocol assessment through to reward calculation and reporting. Across all four, this means you will:
  • Shape requirements
  • turn a request into user stories with acceptance criteria and wort on it together with the engineering team
  • Get features live
  • coordinate the release process, brief Client Services and Operations before go-live, and confirm the feature works as specified once deployed
  • Support clients and operations
  • triage requests from Client Services and Operations, find the root cause with engineering, and turn recurring issues into roadmap items.
  • Are you convinced of the disruptive potential of Digital Assets and Decentralized Finance to shape the future of financial services?
  • Do you have a keen interest in the inner workings of blockchain, staking, digital asset wallets and related technologies? Your profile:
  • Strong and demonstrated interest in and knowledge of cryptocurrencies, blockchain and decentralised finance, and a conviction that crypto will (continue to) shape the future of finance
  • Possess or pursuing a Bachelor's degree from a recognised university (preferably in IT or business), with a track record of outstanding academic performance
  • Excellent organisational, analytical and problem-solving skills, with an eye for detail
  • Strong ownership, relentlessly resourceful, enjoys working within an entrepreneurial environment
  • Comfortable communicating in English with both technical and non-technical stakeholders Joining Sygnum means being part of a dynamic, global team that is building a trusted gateway between the traditional and digital asset economies. Working at Sygnum, you will experience our fast-paced, exciting work environment that embraces meritocracy and collaboration and open communication. Alongside our ambitious long-term mission, we also come together for reaching important milestones and annual crypto-industry anniversaries like Bitcoin Pizza Day, and regularly celebrate together at themed company events as part of our journey to shape Future Finance. Sygnum offers a comprehensive package of benefits for all team members. They include:

  • Experienced leadership teams who are masters of scale and facilitate smart growth
  • Colleagues that are friendly, diverse, professional, highly skilled, international, and motivated
  • Flexible/Work at home policies
  • Professional development via Mentoring and Buddy programs If you are passionate about the potential of blockchain to shape Future Finance and your profile is a good fit for this position, please send us your CV today!
