Centre for Dentistry

Job opening immediately or by agreement, indefinitely

The Center for Dentistry of the University of Zurich (ZZM) is an internationally recognized clinic, training and research center. In six specialized clinics and one institute, dentists work at the forefront of knowledge and in interdisciplinary teams to achieve the highest quality in patient treatment, teaching and research.

Tasks

  • Ensuring operational ICT Workplace Support and Service Desk
  • Acceptance, initial triagulation and analysis of support requests and disruptions
  • Processing and solving standard cases in 1st-level support and, where possible, in 2nd-level support
  • Ensuring complete and traceable ticket documentation
  • Proactive communication with users: about the processing status as well as about solutions and workarounds
  • Maintenance and further development of documentation and knowledge bases
  • Function as a single point of contact (SPOC) for client hardware and related concerns
  • Monitoring of service quality and compliance with agreed service levels (SLA)
  • Coordination of technical escalations and disruptions with internal and external bodies
  • Cooperation with UZH’s central computer science, technical application responsibility and external service providers

Profile

  • Completed vocational training in computer science (Informatician EFZ, TS, FH or similar)
  • Experience in ICT support, service management and ICT processes
  • Very good knowledge of Microsoft 365, Windows Client Management and ticketing systems
  • Experience working with external ICT service providers
  • Knowledge of ITIL or service management processes is an advantage
  • Strong service and customer orientation
  • Very good communication and coordination ability
  • Analytical and solution-oriented thinking and working methods
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • High sense of responsibility and a strong quality orientation

We offer

In this versatile and responsible position, you are a central point of contact for ICT-specific concerns and work closely with UZH’s central computer science, technical application responsibility and external service providers. She expects a varied activity with a high level of personal responsibility and diverse interfaces in a demanding university environment. The employment takes place under cantonal conditions.
