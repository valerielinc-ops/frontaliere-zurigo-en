Full description

We make habitats worth living.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for an experienced, independent service technician with pleasure in service work, bathroom renovations, minor renovations and line replacement. You can expect a versatile everyday life from service use to complete bathroom renovation.

Your tasks

  • Independent performance of service, repair and maintenance services in the sanitary sector
  • Design and support of bathroom and wet cell conversions
  • Sanitary work for shell construction and for smaller conversions
  • Replacement and adaptation of cold, hot and sewage pipelines
  • Minor strand and line replacement work in existing properties
  • Recording and assessment of the situation directly at the customer on site
  • Coordination of own work with construction management, customers and other craftsmen
  • Material orders and preparation of required work
  • Independent organisation and handling of smaller orders

You bring that with you

  • Completed training as a sanitary installer EFZ or equivalent training
  • Several years of professional experience in service, renovation or refurbishment are advantageous
  • Experience with bathroom remodeling and working on existing properties
  • Safety in case of bodyshell installations as well as in case of minor line and line replacement work
  • Independent, reliable and solution-oriented working method
  • Enjoying direct contact with customers, administrations and construction management
  • Clean and professional appearance
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Category B driving licence
