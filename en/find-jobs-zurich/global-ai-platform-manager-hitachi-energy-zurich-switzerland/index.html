Full description

The opportunity

Lead the end-to-end operations of Hitachi Energy's enterprise AI Platform, ensuring secure, reliable, scalable, and cost-efficient services for global business use. Partner with cross-functional teams to drive platform evolution, operational excellence, MLOps, AI security, observability, FinOps, and reusable AI capabilities, while leading a multidisciplinary global team of platform operations professionals.

How you will make an impact

  • Lead end-to-end AI Platform Operations, ensuring secure, scalable, and reliable enterprise services.
  • Manage and develop a multidisciplinary team across platform engineering, MLOps, SRE, FinOps, security, and integration support.
  • Own operational excellence, resource planning, workforce development, and budget accountability.
  • Oversee AI platform services, ensuring stability, performance, scalability, and business continuity.
  • Define and govern service catalogs, SLAs, support models, release processes, and operational controls.
  • Drive AI Ops, MLOps, observability, monitoring, incident management, and continuous service improvement.
  • Ensure platform security, identity governance, data protection, compliance, and policy enforcement.
  • Lead FinOps practices, including cost optimization, capacity planning, and spend transparency.
  • Deliver reusable platform capabilities, deployment support, and shared services for development teams.
  • Provide strategic recommendations to leadership on platform investments, priorities, funding, risk posture, and long-term evolution.

Your background

  • 10+ years of experience in AI/ML platforms, technology operations, or enterprise-scale platform management.
  • Proven track record in operating enterprise AI platforms, delivering service reliability, SLA compliance, and operational excellence.
  • Strong expertise in Microsoft AI Services, cloud platforms, MLOps, SRE practices, observability, and platform reliability.
  • Experience with FinOps, cloud cost management, capacity planning, chargeback models, and consumption optimization.
  • Strong knowledge of platform security, identity and access management, and security-by-design principles.
  • Proven experience managing incidents, release governance, root cause analysis, and continuous service improvement.
  • Demonstrated leadership of multidisciplinary technology operations teams in complex global environments.
  • Excellent stakeholder management, communication, and organizational skills, with the ability to drive end-to-end platform operations and governance.
