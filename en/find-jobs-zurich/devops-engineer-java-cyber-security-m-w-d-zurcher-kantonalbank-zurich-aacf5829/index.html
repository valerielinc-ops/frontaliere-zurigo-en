Full description

100% | IT / Business Engineering | Zürich | Experienced professionals

As a well-coordinated DevOps team consisting of full-stack developers, we need your support to further develop Zürcher Kantonalbank's cyber security. Every day, you help ensure that Zürcher Kantonalbank is one of the safest and best-protected universal banks by developing and operating software to identify vulnerabilities and optimizing Identity and Access Management. We consistently develop and operate our solutions according to the DevOps approach and value your contribution throughout the entire lifecycle. Become part of our team and actively help shape the security of tomorrow.

Your tasks

  • Implementing requirements in the backend with Java and in the frontend with Angular and Typescript, both on-premises and in the cloud (Azure)
  • Taking responsibility for the entire software lifecycle from the idea through implementation to operation in production
  • Developing technical concepts and contributing to the further development of the software architecture
  • Analyzing requirements and designing new features in the area of security and Identity Access Management (IAM)
  • Collaborating in a DevOps team and continuously improving the stability, performance, and maintainability of applications

Your profile

  • University degree in computer science or equivalent training or professional experience
  • Several years of professional experience in software development with a focus on full-stack engineering
  • Very good knowledge of Java and solid experience with Angular and Typescript
  • Very good knowledge of database DML/DDL (ideally Postgres and Oracle)
  • Experience with cloud environments (ideally Azure) and modern architectural approaches are a plus
  • Experience in software development with AI is clearly an advantage
  • You enjoy taking responsibility, driving initiatives within the team, and working in a solution-oriented manner
  • You impress us with clear, audience-appropriate communication and a reliable and structured way of working
  • Very good German language skills, both written and spoken
  • English skills are an advantage

What we offer

  • Mutual support and team spirit are highly valued here
  • Exciting and varied range of tasks in the field of security with many networking opportunities
  • Continuous development – personally, professionally and technically
  • Centrally located, easily accessible and attractive workplace with the option to organize your work flexibly (hybrid)
  • Flexible working hours, mobile working including working from home, and the compatibility of work and private life are a matter of course for us
  • Comprehensive overall package that, in addition to an attractive base salary and variable compensation, offers many benefits and discounts – from A for retirement provision to Z for ZVV-Bonuspass
