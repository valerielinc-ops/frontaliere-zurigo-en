Full description

We are looking for a service-oriented, committed and motivated personality.

You can move that with us.

You are responsible for building technology on various mandates in Zurich and contact for our customer in the field of building technology. Your responsibilities include the following activities:

  • Professional responsibility in building technology and maintenance
  • Operating and supervising all house technical systems (HLKSE)
  • Incident analysis and take appropriate corrective action
  • Monitoring and carrying out maintenance/inspections according to maintenance planning
  • Accompanying and monitoring external service providers
  • Advising on ownership in all technical trades
  • Administrative handling of day-to-day operations
  • Repair work of the building infrastructure
  • Planning, organisation and implementation of projects in the field of building technology
  • Optimising the operating costs of buildings with innovative ideas
  • Ensuring customer satisfaction with your service-oriented approach
  • Pictet service

That's what makes you.

You describe yourself as a service provider: in the innermost conviction. Positive feedback on your performance motivates you and spurs you on to further top performances. You also bring the following:

  • Basic technical training in HVAC or electrical systems
  • Several years of professional experience in the technical field of building operations
  • Further training as a maintenance specialist is advantageous
  • Further education as SiBe fire protection is advantageous
  • Strong technical understanding of complex electronic and mechanical installations
  • Experience in the operation of building control systems
  • Ticket Cat. B
  • Good computer user knowledge
  • Teamplayer
  • Maintained appearance

That's what we offer you.

  • Responsible, varied activity
  • Modern working environment and short decision paths
  • Plenty of room for personal initiative and technical and personal development
