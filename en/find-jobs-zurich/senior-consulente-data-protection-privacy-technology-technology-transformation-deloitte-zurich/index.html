Full description

Business Line: Technology & Transformation Location: Zurich Work Pattern Percentage: 80-100% Start date: Ongoing Experience Level: Experienced About the Role Help organisations address complex data protection, privacy and information governance challenges in an increasingly data-driven world. In this role, you will work with clients across industries to design and implement solutions that protect sensitive data and manage data and AI-driven risks. You will collaborate with multidisciplinary teams in Switzerland and globally, gaining hands-on experience across strategy, governance and technology-enabled transformation initiatives. Along the way, you will deepen your expertise in data protection, AI risk management and regulatory compliance while helping clients build confidence in how they use data and AI. How you can make an impact

  • Advise clients on data protection, privacy and AI governance challenges, translating regulatory and business requirements into practical solutions.
  • Design and implement data protection and information governance capabilities, including data classification, retention, encryption and monitoring controls.
  • Assess data and AI risks across the AI lifecycle and help clients establish governance frameworks and responsible AI practices.
  • Collaborate with business, legal and technology stakeholders to deliver transformation initiatives that strengthen compliance and build trust.
  • Contribute to the development of innovative methodologies, tools and client solutions in data protection, privacy and AI governance. What you bring to succeed
  • You enjoy solving complex problems with a structured approach, working collaboratively and translating technical concepts into practical outcomes.
  • You have knowledge of data protection regulations and an interest in applying technology in areas such as data classification, DLP, encryption, retention, AI governance and AI risk management.
  • You have typically 3+ years of experience in consulting, professional services or industry roles related to data protection, privacy, information security or technology risk, or equivalent demonstrated experience.
  • You have a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Business IT or a related field.
  • You communicate effectively in English and German. What sets you apart Experience assessing AI risks or helping teams adopt responsible AI practices would help you turn emerging risks into practical governance measures for clients. Your team We are an entrepreneurial, international team of 60 Cyber professionals across Switzerland, passionate about helping clients address complex challenges with practical solutions. Working across disciplines, we bring different perspectives to data protection, privacy and AI governance projects and support one another’s personal and professional growth through training, mentoring and hands-on client work.
