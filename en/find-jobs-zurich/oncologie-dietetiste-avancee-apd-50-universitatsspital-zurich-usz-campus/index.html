A new role, a lot of creative leeway and the opportunity to bring about sustainable change: As an Advanced Practice Dietitian (APD) Oncology, you shape the nutritional care of tomorrow. Tasks

  • Interprofessional development of patient pathways of oncology
  • Training and qualifications of the interprofessional team on clinical nutrition

Requirements

  • MSc Nutrition and Dietetics
  • Mind. 5 years of professional experience in clinical nutrition
  • Agile, committed and self-employed personality
  • You design the interprofessional work with joy and commitment
  • A high-quality care of the patient: inside is your heart's concern
  • Good performance skills and high communication skills distinguish you

We offer

As an Advanced Practice Dietitian (APD) Oncology, you play a central role in the further development of clinical nutritional therapy at the University Hospital Zurich. You will get the opportunity to actively shape a new function and contribute innovative ideas. In doing so, you work closely with the specialist responsible for clinical nutrition as well as the management of nutritional advice and therapy. You can expect a motivated, interprofessional team that promotes collaboration, knowledge exchange and development. In our cutting-edge medical environment, you will be challenged, supported and can contribute your professional expertise effectively.
