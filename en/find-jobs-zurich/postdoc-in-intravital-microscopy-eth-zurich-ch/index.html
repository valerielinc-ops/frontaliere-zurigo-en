Postdoc in Intravital Microscopy

100%, Zurich, fixed-term

print Drucken

We are seeking a highly motivated postdoctoral researcher to join our group at the Department of Biology, ETH Zurich. The successful candidate will use state-of-the-art intravital microscopy to study macrophage biology and tissue repair in vivo.

Project background

Our lab investigates how tissue-resident immune cells, particularly macrophages, contribute to tissue homeostasis and repair, with a focus on the mesothelium, serosal cavities, and the liver. Using intravital imaging, we track cell behavior and dynamics in real time within living tissue, linking cellular mechanisms to organ-level repair processes. The project has a strong translational angle, motivated by clinical challenges in abdominal surgery such as adhesion formation, and is conducted in close collaboration with clinicians to bridge basic mechanistic insight with surgical practice.

Job description

  • Design and perform intravital microscopy experiments in mouse models
  • Extend intravital imaging approaches to additional organs
  • Investigate macrophage dynamics and/or serosal repair mechanisms
  • Analyze imaging data using quantitative and computational approaches
  • Perform spectral flow cytometry and standard bioinformatics analysis of single-cell and spatial transcriptomics data, correlating findings with imaging data
  • Present findings at lab meetings and international conferences
  • Contribute to manuscript preparation and grant writing
  • Co-supervise MSc/PhD students
