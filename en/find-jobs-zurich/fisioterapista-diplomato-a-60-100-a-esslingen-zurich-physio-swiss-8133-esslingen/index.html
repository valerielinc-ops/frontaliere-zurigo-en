Physical therapist: in FH / HF (60–100%) Location Esslingen ZH | Admission as of early 2027 or by arrangement Are you a physiotherapist: with passion and a desire for an environment in which laughter is just as much part of everyday practice as professional excellence? After our successful start in early 2026, our modern practice in Esslingen continues to grow. With us you do not expect an anonymous practice, but a real feel-good team, plenty of room for your ideas and a wonderful mixture of geriatrics, rehabilitation and sports physiotherapy. What makes your workday with us special

  • Holistic practical work: You treat your patients in bright, spacious individual rooms and use our modern training area for active rehabilitation and MTT.
  • Meaningful geriatrics: You care for residents of the retirement and care center just around the corner – a grateful and humanly enriching task.
  • Sport & activity as a contrast: As a compensation to geriatrics, you accompany active patients: inside and athletes: inside on their way back to top form.
  • Relaxed home visits: For occasional domicile treatments in the area, our in-house car is always available – no stress with the private vehicle.

Why you will feel comfortable with us

  • A team that holds together: We cultivate a warm, open cooperation at eye level. Whether it’s lunch together, a spontaneous coffee chat or regular cross-location events and exchanges with our teams from Volketswil and Winterthur – the “we” is our top priority.
  • Focus on therapy instead of paperwork: 30 minutes of treatment time, 45 minutes for initial findings (with a say!) as well as 30 minutes each day for your reports & administration. Our reception team takes care of the entire scheduling and billing – so your head remains free for your patients: inside.
  • Real development: 5 weeks of holidays plus 1 paid training week per year. We support your professional training generously – financially and organizationally.
  • Modern feel-good atmosphere: A state-of-the-art practice with five treatment rooms, great weight room and lots of light.
  • Best location: Free parking directly in the car park in front of the practice and only 100 meters on foot from the Forchbahn stop Esslingen.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed training as a dipl. Physical therapist: in FH / HF (for a foreign diploma we require the completed SRK recognition).
  • You speak negotiable German, listen to your patient and easily find the right tone.
  • Ideally, you already bring an advanced training in Manual Lymph Drainage (KPE) – or you want to tackle it with us.
  • You have a valid driver's license Kat. B.
  • But above all: You are a real team player, have humor, empathy and enjoy a versatile work with people of all ages.

About us Physiotherapie Esslingen is part of the family-owned Physio Health + Reha GmbH (together with the Physiotherapie Gartenhof in Winterthur and the Physiotherapie Gries in Volketswil). We are three locations with different focuses, but a common spirit: we share our expertise, support each other and meet regularly for collegial exchange and social togetherness. Want to meet us?
