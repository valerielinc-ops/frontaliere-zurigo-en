This is what awaits you

  • You will independently handle complex professional liability cases, in particular from lawyers, notaries, trustees and IT service providers.
  • Your responsibilities also include handling organ liability, cyber and trust damage cases.
  • The activity includes in particular factual clarifications, legal assessment of liability and cover, but also the conduct of negotiations with the claimants and their legal representatives and, where appropriate, the support of processes.
  • In addition, you support underwriting in the risk assessment, work on projects and speak at further education events.

You bring that with you

  • You are an initiative and team-capable personality with assertiveness and negotiating skills, which also enjoys studying files.
  • Thanks to your pronounced analytical thinking skills and your quick comprehension, you will quickly become familiar with complex problems.
  • You have an excellent verbal and written ability to express yourself in German. Good knowledge of French and English (word and writing) is an advantage.
  • You are a lawyer with a Swiss attorney’s patent or dipl. Tax expert and have several years of professional experience (preferably in the legal profession, judiciary or insurance company).
  • An affinity for IT as well as knowledge of liability law, tax law, company law and accounting are classified as complementary skills and round off your profile.
