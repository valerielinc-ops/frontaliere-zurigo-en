Full description

The GZO Spital Wetzikon is an innovative acute hospital in the Zurich Oberland with 150 beds and around 900 employees. As a company with a high level of customer orientation and appreciative communication, we are committed to our patients every day. The GZO is in a transformation phase. The moratorium creates the necessary time to develop a comprehensive restructuring plan. Operations continue as normal. For us, the restructuring plan is not just a necessity, but an opportunity to consciously further develop the GZO. Do you want to actively shape this phase? The logistics and patient transport department ensures competent and service-oriented service for internal and external customers. Become part of our team in November 2026 or by appointment. We are looking for you: a well-positioned, flexible and committed person

Tasks

  • Safe transport of patients within the hospital, with or without aids (e.g. with a bed or wheelchair from department to examination, back to the department or reception)
  • Ordering and providing patients' personal effects from the day clinic to the departments and handing over the effects to the nursing staff
  • Receiving, checking and processing incoming goods
  • Ensuring internal hospital logistics and carrying out transports
  • Processing and coordination of internal orders
  • Ensuring disposal and recycling according to specifications
  • Selective support in related areas and other logistical tasks requirements
  • Good knowledge of spoken and written German as well as understanding of Swiss German
  • Experience in a hospital or nursing environment is an advantage
  • Basic knowledge of hygiene, safety, disposal and recycling regulations
  • Safe handling of transport and logistics aids
  • Physical resilience for transport and lifting activities
  • Good PC user knowledge, experience with ERP systems is an advantage
  • Careful and correct documentation and performance recording
  • High service and patient orientation as well as discretion
  • Empathy when dealing with patients
  • Resilience and calm action in hectic situations
  • Ability to work in a team and work well with nursing and specialist departments
  • Independent, careful and structured way of working
  • Flexibility and willingness to change areas of application What you can look forward to An introduction that deserves the name You will be introduced to your new area of ​​responsibility in a structured and careful manner. Development that continues You benefit from internal and external training opportunities. A hospital where people know each other. Short distances, direct contacts and collaboration in a multi-professional team that is characterized by respect and appreciation. An environment that also looks after you. Various discounts and internal offers are also part of our benefits, as are an excellent staff restaurant, an affiliated daycare center and various events for our employees.
