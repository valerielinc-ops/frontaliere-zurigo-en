Full description

VZ VermögensZentrum is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We advise individuals and companies on all matters relating to money, manage assets and offer attractive financial services. We are very successful and growing strongly. Now we are looking for a committed personality for our team at VZ Operations AG in Zurich.

  • Together with your team, you are responsible for the settlement of stock exchange transactions and securities transfers.
  • You maintain delivery instructions and have contact with counterparties, custodian banks and internal specialist departments.
  • You help with other securities administration tasks, such as entering and registering registered shares in the share register.
  • You take responsibility for a high quality of service and ensure that our processes are processed in a timely and error-free manner.
  • You actively help to further develop and optimize our settlement and securities processes.
  • You participate in exciting projects and support the digitalization of our work processes.
  • You have successfully completed your basic commercial training and have already worked in the back office of a bank for several years.
  • You are interested in securities topics and ideally have experience in the area of settlement.
  • You work structured, process-oriented and fast.
  • You are well organized, set priorities right and feel comfortable in a service-oriented and dynamic environment.
  • You think along and bring your own ideas.
  • You are a team player and like to go the extra mile.
  • A dynamic "you culture" with flat hierarchies
  • A team you can count on and a climate that promotes initiative.
  • The opportunity to shape and further develop the VZ with initiative and commitment.
  • You work in a company that is recognized by BILANZ as a long-standing quality leader in the financial sector.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
