Full description

At STRABAG, around 89,000 people are building progress at more than 2,400 locations worldwide. Uniqueness and individual strengths characterise not only our projects but also each and every one of us. Whether in building and civil engineering, road and underground construction, bridge and tunnel construction, in building materials production, project development or building management – we think construction forward to become the most innovative and sustainable construction technology group in Europe. Equal opportunities, diversity and inclusion are integral parts of what defines us as a company and how we work. Together we implement projects successfully and in partnership and grow with new tasks. Together we create great things. Let's progress!

Purchaser Construction Projects 80-100 % Your contribution with us

  • You procure materials, services and subcontractor services for our construction projects
  • You obtain offers, compare them and conduct price and contract negotiations
  • You work closely with project management, site management and internal specialist departments
  • You maintain and further develop relationships with suppliers and business partners
  • You ensure that materials and services are available on time and on economical terms
  • You contribute your experience to the establishment and further development of our new purchasing team
  • You actively shape processes, standards and ways of working
  • You ensure transparency and quality in our procurement processes

What counts for us

  • You have experience in main or secondary construction trades, in a construction-related industry and/or already in purchasing
  • Possible backgrounds include site manager, foreman, construction clerk, purchaser or a comparable function
  • You understand offers, specifications and commercial contexts and can assess them confidently
  • You are a communicative personality and know how to build sustainable relationships and create trust
  • You conduct negotiations confidently, solution-oriented and with the necessary tact
  • You think economically, act entrepreneurially and keep an overview even with many topics simultaneously
  • You work independently, in a structured and reliable manner
  • You communicate fluently in German. Other languages are welcome

Our added value for you

  • The chance to help build a new structure at the Schlieren site and actively shape the future direction
  • A responsible role with plenty of scope for design and short decision-making paths
  • Collaboration with motivated colleagues and close connection to exciting construction projects
  • Attractive employment conditions as well as targeted training and development opportunities
  • Modern work equipment and flexible solutions for balancing work and private life
  • Health offers, employee discounts, company events and other attractive benefits
