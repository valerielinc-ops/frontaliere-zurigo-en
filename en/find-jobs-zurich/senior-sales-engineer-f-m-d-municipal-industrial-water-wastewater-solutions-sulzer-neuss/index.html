Full description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

In this position, you are the technical interface between customer, sales and internal departments. They accompany the entire sales process from requirements analysis to presentations and implementation and ensure that the solutions are technically sound and tailored to customer requirements. The position is aimed at experienced specialists who work independently and contribute their expertise in a targeted manner. Their tasks

  • Self-responsible distribution of Nordic Water solutions:
  • Filtration systems (especially 4th purification stage/trace substances)
  • MEVA calculations
  • Sludge clearer
  • Development and expansion of a regional customer portfolio (existing customers + new customers)
  • Identification and evaluation of project opportunities in the municipal and industrial environment
  • Technical advice and development of tailor-made solutions
  • Responsibility for the complete offer process incl. Calculation
  • Close cooperation with the sales team for municipal and industrial wastewater solutions
  • Presentation of solutions to customers, planners and decision-makers
  • Support for implementation and clarification of technical issues

Your profile

  • Completed higher education (or equivalent qualification) and at least 3–4 years of relevant professional experience in a comparable role
  • Sound technical background in engineering or a related discipline
  • Experience in structured sales, offer or project processes as well as a good understanding of processes
  • Very good communication and presentation skills
  • Ability to present complex technical content in an understandable and customer-oriented manner
  • Experience in dealing with CRM systems and sales enablement tools
  • Strong problem-solving skills, structured working methods and customer-oriented thinking
  • Willingness to travel up to 80%

What we offer you

  • International cooperation, open corporate culture and a motivated team
  • Contract of indefinite duration
  • Flexible working hours and 30 days of vacation
  • Company cars, whether or not for private use
  • A strong, helpful team with good cohesion
  • Modern work equipment
  • Attractive remuneration and additional benefits, including: perks/employee discounts, JobRad program, company pensions, company special benefits such as family service hotline
