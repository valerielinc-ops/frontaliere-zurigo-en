Full description

Cloud technologies are more than IT infrastructure at Belimo: they enable collaboration, security and efficiency at the global level. In this function, you take responsibility for and actively shape central Microsoft-65, Azure- and Identity-Services.

You work at the interface between operation, security and innovation: you keep critical services running reliably, modernize existing solutions and test new technologies for use at Belimo. You work closely with IT teams, other areas as well as external partners and ensure that our cloud landscape remains efficient, secure and sustainable.

Tasks

  • You take responsibility for defined cloud-Services – from Active Directory/Entra ID to Microsoft 365 to security and Azure topics.
  • Configuration and further development of existing and new services as well as their integration into the system landscape – with a focus on security and usability – are also part of your tasks.
  • You can see where existing solutions can get better and make sure that new technologies arrive at Belimo in a meaningful way.
  • You help to put Belimo's global IT strategy into practice and ensure that it creates concrete improvements for our users.
  • In cross-departmental projects, you support the business and make complex technical topics understandable and usable.
  • You enjoy working with internal teams and external partners – you represent Belimo's interests vis-à-vis service providers.
  • It makes you fun to identify automation potentials so that recurring tasks become easier and more efficient.
  • With complex Service and Incidents, you get involved in 3rd level and help with the team to keep our services stable and reliable.

Requirements

  • You're bringing a completed computer science training.
  • Some years of experience in a similar role in the infrastructure or system environment distinguish you.
  • Good knowledge of Active Directory and Entra ID is a matter of course for you.
  • You also have a solid knowledge of Microsoft teams (telephony and collaboration), Defender, SharePoint and Exchange (hybrid).
  • Knowledge in Endpoint Management is a plus.
  • Knowledge of Azure Cloud Computer and Azure Services is an advantage.
  • You have already participated in IT projects and appreciates the exchange with different stakeholders in order to find good solutions together.
  • You bring very good English skills as well as at least knowledge of German.

About Belimo

  • Belimo is the world leader in the development, manufacture and distribution of field equipment for energy-efficient control of heating, ventilation and air conditioning systems.
  • Valve drives, control valves, sensors and counters form our core business.
