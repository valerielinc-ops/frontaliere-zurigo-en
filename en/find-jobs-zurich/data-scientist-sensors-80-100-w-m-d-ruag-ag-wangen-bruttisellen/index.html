Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organizations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Support the further development of our modeling and analysis software ecosystem through collaborative development, documentation and adherence to proven engineering practices
  • Analysis of measurement and test data from practice, derivation of key performance indicators and preparation of technical documentation and reports for internal use and for customers
  • Development and application of machine learning methods to extract meaningful patterns and insights from radar datasets
  • Participation in the investigation of technical incidents and data anomalies to ensure system reliability and smooth operation
  • Working effectively in highly regulated, air-gapped environments in compliance with strict safety and quality standards

You bring that with you

  • Master’s degree or doctorate in computational science, computer science, physics, applied mathematics or a closely related field
  • Strong expertise in Python for scientific computing and data analysis, with deep experience in relevant libraries such as NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib
  • Expert in mathematical and numerical modeling of complex, dynamic systems, including handling noise and high-dimensional data
  • Practical knowledge of Machine‐Learning‐Frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch)
  • Good familiarity with the Linux ecosystem and initial experience with compiled languages (e.g. C, C++, Rust)
  • Proven ability to access domain-specific knowledge quickly and independently and to translate scientific principles into practical engineering solutions
  • Ability to find a healthy balance between intellectual curiosity and the demands of customers and businesses
  • Enthusiasm for deep immersion in the world of radar data processing
  • Language skills: German mind. C1, English at least. C1, French is a big plus
  • Swiss citizenship is required

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. Over the area

In times of increasing geopolitical tensions and uncertainties, a country’s ability to ensure its security and sovereignty is paramount. Our mission in the Engineering Sensors & Electronic Warfare department at RUAG is to support the Swiss Army in its tasks by enabling a lasting situational awareness.

An essential part of this mission is to provide reliable and robust surveillance and analysis capabilities for military air surveillance radars. For this purpose, we design and develop customised software tools for processing, analysing and visualising the data recorded by these systems.

Our work requires a deep mathematical and physical understanding of the entire signal processing chain – from raw signal acquisition to processing and interpretation.

To support our radar engineering team, we are looking for an experienced computational/data scientist with a rigorous approach.
