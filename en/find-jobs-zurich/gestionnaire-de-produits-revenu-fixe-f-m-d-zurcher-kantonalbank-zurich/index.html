Zürcher Kantonalbank is one of the leading banks for trading fixed income products in Switzerland. As Product Manager Fixed Income, you are at the heartbeat of fixed income trading and shape the development of our trading infrastructure. This includes automated trading systems, access to various trading venues, and complex data analyses. Is your passion fixed income trading, quantitative models, and engineering? Do you want to drive the development of our fixed income platform forward? Are you never satisfied with the status quo and want to actively shape progress? Then our team would be delighted to get to know you!

Your tasks

  • Take on functional responsibility for the trading infrastructure and trading models in the Fixed Income area
  • Provide functional leadership for Quant and IT Engineering, with responsibility for project management and development prioritization, in close collaboration with the Head of Fixed Income Trading & Sales
  • Further develop our pricing infrastructure and apply analytical models for fixed income products
  • Further develop our infrastructure for pricing and trading analytics
  • Work closely with Trading, Sales, and Engineering
  • Be an integral part of the trading team
  • PhD or Master’s degree in a quantitative discipline
  • Relevant experience in implementing analytical models for fixed income products with a focus on bonds
  • Proven programming skills and experience in applying machine/deep learning
  • Experience in engineering and designing front-office systems, especially in the area of fixed income quoting
  • Strong conceptual skills combined with innovative drive and a high level of commitment
  • Experience in leading teams or projects as well as strong stakeholder management skills
  • Excellent English and good German skills
  • A significant contribution to the development and competitiveness of our fixed income trading
  • A position with challenging tasks on a personal, professional, and technical level
  • A competent and performance-oriented team that is looking forward to welcoming a new member
  • A centrally located, well-equipped workplace
  • An employer that cares about its employees and invests in them
