Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With more than 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric offer of Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in ambulatory, day-clinical and inpatient settings. The Group employs around 1800 people and achieved a turnover of CHF 240 million in 2025.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement a committed

We, Clienia Schlössli AG, offer 27 stationary places in our field of child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy. This area also includes a clinic school recognized by the primary school office.

Their tasks

  • Teaching of changing students: inside groups in team teaching or in a deputy function
  • Lessons with individual funding goals in individual and group setting based on funding planning
  • Implementation of school sports preparation, implementation and follow-up of teaching
  • Documentation of teaching in the hospital's internal information system
  • Collaboration in the school team and cooperation with the interprofessional treatment team (medicine, psychology, nursing, social education)

Your profile

  • EDK recognised education and are familiar with the Swiss education system
  • Professional experience in the clinic/special education sector is advantageous
  • They are flexible, innovative and enjoy dealing with young people

Our offer

  • Employment in accordance with the Hospital School Ordinance of the Canton of Zurich (42 h week, salary as upper school canton of Zurich), preparation and follow-up takes place exclusively within working hours
  • Individually applicable holiday days (25 days)
  • Opportunity to participate in the establishment of a new clinic school
  • Innovative employer with a strong interprofessional cooperation
  • Very good development opportunities with generous support in further education and training

For further information please refer to Simone Gehri, Head of School, tel. +41 44 929 81 41 or Irene Walczewski, Head of Department, tel. +41 44 929 83 73.
