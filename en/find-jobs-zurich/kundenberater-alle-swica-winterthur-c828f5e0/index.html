Full description

Advice with heart and expertise makes a real difference. As a customer consultant at SWICA, you actively contribute to the satisfaction of our customers. Your personal development is just as important to us. With the SWICA Market Academy and support in graduating as an insurance intermediary VBV, you will continue to develop – in a committed team where you can grow and your ideas count. This is how you shape health

  • You will advise our customers by telephone and personally and find suitable insurance solutions together
  • You take requests and accompany them step by step until the conclusion
  • You are actively involved in sales and achieve common goals with your team
  • You recognize customer needs and use opportunities in the market
  • You create offers and take over the correspondence in private customer business
  • You help to inspire and win back customers in the long term

This sets you apart

  • A commercial education or experience in customer contact or Sales
  • Experience in the insurance industry is a plus, but not a must
  • German very good and French desirable
  • An open, reliable personality with pleasure in customer contact
  • Interest in sales and advice
  • Motivation to develop yourself (e.g. VBV certification – we support you)

Why SWICA

  • We build on humanity. That is why we focus on openness, respect and trust in our everyday work. We tackle this, take responsibility and think in terms of solutions – together, for our customers. This is also why we have been number 1 in customer satisfaction for many years.
  • We trust you and give you space. With annual working hours, at least 5 weeks of vacation and the possibility of holiday purchase, you take time off when you need it.
  • We appreciate your work and reward it – with fleet discounts at various car manufacturers, an annual lump sum and generous participation in the basic and additional insurance for you and your family.
  • We offer you prospects. With a generous training regulations, we support your professional and personal development – for your role today and for your goals of tomorrow.
  • Because health is everything. With our company health management and region-specific offers, we take care of your well-being. And if it comes otherwise: 100% net wage continued payment.
  • We think long term. With three savings plans, pensum-dependent coordination deduction and benefits well above the legal minimum, you ensure good for your future.
