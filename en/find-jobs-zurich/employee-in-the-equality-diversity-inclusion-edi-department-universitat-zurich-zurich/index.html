Equality, Diversity, Inclusion (EDI)

Job as of 01.01.2027 or by agreement, indefinitely

On behalf of the Vice-Rector and the university management, the EDI department promotes the integration of the topics of equality, diversity and inclusion in all core areas of UZH. In this sense, it develops policies and implements them with the relevant stakeholders, collects data, is responsible for the management of two academic committees, acts as a point of contact for all UZH members and represents the topics inside and outside the university. You have already gained your first professional experience after your studies and are now looking for a new challenge? We offer responsible work at the interface of equal opportunities, organizational development and university administration. You can expect a friendly and goal-oriented team and a workplace at a central location in Zurich.

Tasks

  • Implementation and reporting of the future UZH EDI policy (2027 in force)
  • Implementation of measures, especially in the area of inclusion of people living with disabilities
  • Processing of reports and conducting clarifications on possible discrimination in the university context

Profile

  • MA degree, preferably law
  • Further qualification or readiness for further training in the field of professional conversation, communication skills, etc.
  • 1–3 years of appointment experience relevant to the job
  • Familiarity with current social equality, diversity or inclusion concerns as well as with the Swiss higher education sector
  • Analytical thinking skills as well as the ability to deal with complex questions independently, structured and solution-oriented
  • Stylistic communication with different stakeholders in German and English
  • An integrative personality with high social skills and sensitivity to stressful topics and situations
  • Motivation, teamwork, independence, goal orientation
