Role overview

We are looking for a senior SAP technical transformation leader with deep hands on S/4HANA solution design, integration, data replication and Clean Core experience.

In this role, you will shape and deliver complex SAP transformation programs across multinational ERP landscapes, bridging business, functional and technical teams while guiding global delivery teams from assessment and planning through implementation and stabilization.

Lead S/4HANA technical transformation and upgrade programs: Own technical assessments, readiness checks, impact analysis, retrofit strategy, risk strategy, test planning, cutover support and delivery governance for SAP S/4HANA implementations and upgrades.

Application process

  • Shape complex integration and data replication solutions: Design integration concepts between SAP and non SAP systems, including Central Finance, MDG, BW or BW/4HANA, external finance, tax, planning, reporting and CRM applications, using technologies such as SLT, SAP CPI, SAP PO, MuleSoft, IDoc, RFC, AIF and related middleware.
  • Drive Clean Core and extensibility decisions: Define pragmatic Clean Core approaches for custom code, RICEFW, Fiori, CDS views, AMDP, ABAP developments, SAP BTP extensions, integration patterns and code remediation.
  • Lead finance transformation technology streams: Support S/4HANA Finance and Central Finance programs across Record to Report, Procure to Pay, Project to Delivery, tax, group reporting, central payments, reconciliation and financial close topics.
  • Manage global teams and delivery quality: Lead multidisciplinary teams of consultants, developers, architects, client IT and technology partners, including offshore and nearshore delivery models.
  • Establish code guidelines, review processes, quality gates and performance tuning practices.
  • Bridge stakeholders across business and IT: Align enterprise architects, process owners, functional leads, system owners and program leadership across multiple ERP landscapes and geographies.
  • Create clear decision materials and drive structured issue and escalation management.
  • Bring innovation into practical SAP delivery: Assess and prototype useful applications of SAP BTP, SAP Business AI, Joule, automation, analytics and data services in real transformation contexts.

Company and context

  • A platform to lead technical transformation in large SAP S/4HANA programs with leading organizations.
  • The opportunity to shape integration, Clean Core, Central Finance, SAP BTP and AI enabled SAP outcomes.
  • A collaborative environment with access to SAP, cloud, data, AI and business transformation expertise across PwC.
  • A role with clear client impact, team leadership responsibility, mentoring opportunities, asset development and contribution to market growth.
