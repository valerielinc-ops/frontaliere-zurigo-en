Full description

The food service operation at Toni-Areal is part of Zurich University of Applied Sciences and Zurich University of the Arts at the Toni-Areal campus. The food service operation consists of the Mensa Molki, the Bistro Chez Toni, a cafeteria and the cascade catering operation. In addition, numerous internal and external catering events are carried out. Around 800 lunches are prepared every day. In keeping with the ZFV philosophy, fresh food is cooked on site using regional, ecologically sound and fairly traded ingredients. Are you our new team member who will support us as a buffet employee (70%) with immediate effect or by arrangement? You work flexibly between Friday and Monday in an approximately 70% position. From Friday to Monday, you will work at the coffee bar. On Saturday, coffee deliveries for catering events and returns from Friday must be made before the shift. The position therefore includes a small amount of catering work. Your task menu:

  • Welcoming, serving and advising guests
  • Ensuring a high level of guest satisfaction
  • Operating the cash register
  • Preparing the mise en place
  • Ensuring an appealing presentation of goods
  • Assisting with catering events and special occasions as needed
  • Deliveries and returns for catering events on Saturday morning before the shift at the coffee bar
  • Ensuring cleanliness and order in the work environment
  • Carrying out general tidying and cleaning tasks Your recipe for success:
  • Practical experience in the food service / hotel industry
  • Experience in catering is an advantage
  • Very good spoken German skills
  • Initial experience working at a coffee bar
  • Warm, personable and empathetic
  • Enjoyment of food
  • Hands-on mentality What we offer you: We create teams that support one another and in which you are encouraged. We work together and focus on everything that does us good. Among other things, we place great value on pay transparency and equal pay. For this position, at a 100% workload, depending on your experience and qualifications, we offer an annual salary of around CHF 52'000 (incl. 13. Monthly salary). If you would like to share your passion for gastronomy with us, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
