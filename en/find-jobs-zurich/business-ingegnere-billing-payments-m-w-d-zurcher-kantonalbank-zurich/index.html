Full description

80% - 100% | IT / Business Engineering | Zurich | Experienced professionals

The Projects and Engineering division in Billing & Payments is responsible for the strategic further development and implementation of the digitalization, automation and modernization of our services and solutions. Our focus is on creating sustainable added value for our customers and the bank. As a central unit, we support projects from conception through successful implementation and drive innovative initiatives throughout the Billing and Payments environment. This includes topics such as Instant Payment, SIC, SWIFT, TWINT, eBill and other forward-looking technologies. Become part of our team and help us shape the future of payment transactions by migrating application modules to a modern Application Stack and thereby creating the foundation for future-proof solutions.

Your responsibilities

  • Creating requirements specifications or creating EPICs, Stories and Features, UI prototypes
  • Close collaboration with Product Owners, Release Train Engineers (RTEs) and interdisciplinary teams
  • Participating in the creation of EPIC concepts in close collaboration with Product Management
  • Developing and evaluating solution approaches
  • Preparing analyses and developing solutions, as well as presenting them to specialist and management committees
  • Defining tests for the acceptance of features and expanding regression tests

Your profile

  • University degree in business information systems, business administration or a comparable field, ideally with further training in project or portfolio management
  • At least 5 years of experience in business engineering in an agile environment (e.g. SAFe) and ideally certification in business engineering (e.g. IREB)
  • Convincing communication, presentation and negotiation skills
  • Analytical, strategic and interconnected thinking
  • Familiarity with cross-functional collaboration and complex stakeholder management
  • Very good German language skills

Our offering

  • A range of responsibilities involving exciting topics that you can advance with your knowledge and energy
  • Engaged, highly motivated and competent colleagues who look forward to achieving shared goals with you
  • We support you in reconciling work and private life - whether through part-time work, hybrid working, unpaid leave or flexible working hours
  • Various events and internal networks offer you the opportunity to network quickly
  • As your employer, we care about your career, which is why we foster and accompany you on your journey and support you with many offerings
  • A comprehensive overall package that, in addition to an attractive base salary and variable compensation, offers many benefits and discounts – from A for retirement planning to Z for ZVV-Bonuspass
