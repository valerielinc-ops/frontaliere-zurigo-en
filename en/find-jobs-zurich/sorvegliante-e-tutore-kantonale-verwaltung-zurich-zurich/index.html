As part of the UGZ, Zurich Prison specializes in pre-trial detention for adult men and has 110 detention places. We are looking for committed, motivated and capable personalities with a high sense of responsibility who want to actively participate in the prison in Zurich with their expertise and positive attitude. You do that with us.

  • Care and support of the instructed persons: You ensure an orderly and safe everyday life and stand by the instructed persons in an advisory and leading way
  • Ensure security: You keep an eye on the security devices and cameras and act calmly, purposefully and calmly in special situations
  • Coordination of processes: You organize movements of instructed persons and ensure that the operation runs smoothly
  • Administrative tasks: You document events, create reports and keep all processes transparent and traceable
  • Design and further development: You bring in ideas, show commitment and support the continuous improvement of our operations You bring that with you

Zurich Detention Centers (UGZ) are one of eight main departments of JuWe. They comprise seven companies with about 735 prison places and about 500 employees. They are responsible for provisional arrest, pre-trial detention and security detention, as well as the early execution of penalties and measures until transfer to a prison facility. In this way, they fulfil a central function within the Zurich justice system right from the start. As employees of the Zurich Detention Centers, we provide with expertise and commitment for structured and humane care of admitted persons and help ensure that criminal proceedings are carried out in an orderly manner before a decision is made on guilt or innocence. With around 1400 employees, JuWe is the largest office within the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs and is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as for the execution of various types of detention. Our employees from around 40 professions support and promote the reintegration of delinquents and work effectively and progressively for a safe and just society. We offer...
