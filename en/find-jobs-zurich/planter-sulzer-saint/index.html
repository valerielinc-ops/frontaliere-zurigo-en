Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and innovative development solutions that enable a successful and more sustainable society.

About the role

Job Summary The Machine Operator is responsible for operating and monitoring machinery used in the manufacturing process. This role ensures that machines run efficiently, products meet quality standards, and safety protocols are followed. This job profile is intended for support staff that follows established procedures and works under close supervision. Smaller/easier decisions are made independently. Summary of the post

We are looking for a qualified Planter to join our production team at Sulzer Ensival Moret France SASU. The incumbent will be responsible for the precision machining of metal parts, ensuring compliance with technical specifications and quality standards.

Post responsibilities

  • Read and interpret technical drawings, diagrams and machining instructions.
  • Programming, adjusting and operating traditional, numerically controlled machine tools (turns, milling machines, grinding machines, etc.).
  • Select the appropriate cutting tools, speeds and advances for each operation.
  • Perform self-control of machined parts using measuring instruments (slide foot, micrometer, comparator, etc.) and adjust settings if necessary.
  • Maintain cleanliness and order of workplace and machinery.
  • Participate in the first level maintenance of equipment.
  • Report any defects or defects in production.
  • Respect existing safety procedures and quality standards.

Qualifications required

  • CAP/BEP or Bac Pro in general mechanics, machining, mechanical engineering or equivalent.
  • Significant experience (minimum 2-3 years) as a Usiner on conventional machine tools and/or CNC.
  • Mastering the reading of technical plans.
  • Knowledge of different materials (steel, stainless steel, cast iron, etc.) and their machining properties.
  • Ability to use various measuring instruments accurately.
  • Rigor, precision and attention to detail.
  • Autonomy and team spirit.
  • Compliance with safety instructions.
  • Post in 2*8, night and day shift possibility
  • Work Saturdays, as needed
