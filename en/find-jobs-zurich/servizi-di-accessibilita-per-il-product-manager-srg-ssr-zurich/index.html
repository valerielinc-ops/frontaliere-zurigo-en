Full description

Accessibility Services (AS) enables accessible access to the SRG media and thus makes a concrete contribution to social participation in Switzerland. As a competence center, the department is responsible for the further development and use of subtitle, sign language and audio description services.

In addition, AS is responsible for customer and market dialogue including the national customer center for offers such as Sat-Access, Play+ and Login as well as the exchange with retailers and redistributors, thus ensuring that offers and interfaces are consistently oriented to the needs of the audience and market.

This is your contribution.

As a Product Manager: in the Product Management Accessibility Services team, you actively participate in the further development of our product portfolio in the areas of subtitling, audio description and sign language.

  • You develop the product vision, product strategy and roadmap for products and services in the areas of subtitling, audio description and sign language and prioritize requirements in close coordination with the head of product management as well as relevant internal and external stakeholders
  • They are responsible for the operational management of the product life cycle from the ideation to the introduction to the continuous development of existing products.
  • They analyze user needs, market requirements, technological developments and regulatory framework conditions and derive product requirements and recommendations for action from them.
  • They ensure that product decisions are consistently oriented towards user added value, corporate goals and profitability, define relevant KPIs and derive data-based optimization measures.
  • You support the planning and controlling of product and project budgets, create decision bases and carry out cost-benefit analyses
  • They coordinate the cooperation between departments, operations, technology, product owners as well as internal and external stakeholders and ensure efficient implementation of product requirements
  • They observe market, technology and industry trends and promote a user-centred, agile and data-based way of working in the product environment

They inspire us.

  • Experience in product management or in a comparable function related to product, service or project development
  • Knowledge or interest in methods of agile product management from strategic planning to roadmap design to operational implementation
  • Experience in the analysis of user needs, requirements and key figures as well as the ability to derive concrete measures from them
  • Experience in stakeholder management and cooperation with interdisciplinary teams
  • Strong communication and moderation skills and the ability to combine different interests
  • Analytical, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • High personal responsibility, implementation strength and decision-making pleasure
  • Very good language skills in a national language, plus German or French at least at level B1 and English at level B1
  • Experience in the media environment or in the area of accessibility services is an advantage, but no requirement

So we inspire you.

  • They design products and services with national impact and make a direct contribution to inclusion and accessibility in Switzerland
  • They actively shape the further development of accessibility services in the areas of subtitling, audio description and sign language
  • They work at the exciting interface between media, technology and user needs and are in direct exchange with user groups and associations.
  • They work in interdisciplinary teams and across language regions with specialists, technology and subject experts. You benefit from a flexible and hybrid working model, a high level of personal responsibility and a lot of room for manoeuvre in the further development of the product portfolio.
