Full description

The Altlasten section with its 16 employees is responsible for the investigation, monitoring and rehabilitation of contaminated sites. This ensures that contaminated building waste is rehabilitated as quickly as possible with high urgency, that the resulting contaminated building waste is disposed of in accordance with the law and that the costs are distributed among the polluters. Their tasks

  • Administrative support for investigations, renovations and construction projects on contaminated sites
  • Management of complex enforcement cases
  • Advising the landowners concerned
  • Technical assessment and monitoring of the necessary measures such as investigations and renovations
  • Review of measures Your profile
  • You build on your training as a geologist, environmental scientist or environmental engineer and on your several years of professional experience in the contaminated area
  • Accompanying investigations and, above all, renovation projects as well as working in an interdisciplinary team are demanding and require skill and a structured approach.
  • They have a communicative flair and written fluency for different stakeholders in a sensitive environment.
