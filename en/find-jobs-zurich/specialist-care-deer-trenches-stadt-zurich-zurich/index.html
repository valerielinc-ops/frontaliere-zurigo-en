About us

The Zurichberg School District comprises districts 1, 7 and 8 with the districts Altstadt, Fluntern, Hottingen, Hirslanden, Riesbach and Witikon. The district school authority manages 17 school houses. These include 44 kindergartens and over 40 care facilities of different sizes. Of the 13 organizational units, 9 are day schools.

The school Hirschengraben is located in the heart of Zurich and offers children from kindergarten to primary level a versatile learning and living space. Our supplementary school care is integrated directly into the school and offers children space for play, recreation, shared food and social learning. In our dedicated care team, we accompany and support the children individually and jointly shape a varied and lively day-to-day care. We attach great importance to an appreciative cooperation, a professional pedagogical attitude and an environment in which children and employees feel comfortable.
