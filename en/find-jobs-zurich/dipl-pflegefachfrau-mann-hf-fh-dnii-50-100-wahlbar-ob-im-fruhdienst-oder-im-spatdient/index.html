The ideal entry into 'leadership'...

Your heart beats for older people? Is nursing your vocation? Do you see opportunities to meaningfully apply your skills and experiences with us to have a positive impact on the lives of our customers? Then you are perfect for us!

Are you looking for exactly this position?

For the Tertianum «Etzelblick» in Richterswil, we are looking for immediately or by arrangement a

Deputy Team Leader - Dipl. Nurse HF/FH/DNII 50-100% selectable in the early shift (with the possibility to become a trainer) or in the late shift

Your tasks

  • Holistic care and nursing of guests according to our nursing philosophy and the nursing process
  • Assumption of leadership tasks in agreement with the team leader/LPB
  • Assumption of tasks as a nursing contact person
  • Responsibility and control of the nursing process of the assigned guests including the RAI nursing needs assessment
  • Electronic nursing documentation using CareCoach
  • Planning and accompanying doctor visits
  • Assumption of daily responsibility with coordination of the day team
  • Leadership of case discussions and nursing reports
  • Assumption of tasks as a theme carrier in a specialist topic and thus participation in the company-wide network for nursing quality
  • Accompanying our trainees

Your profile

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nurse HF/FH/DNII or an equivalent training recognized by the CRS
  • Work experience in long-term care preferable
  • RAI knowledge desired or willingness to attend these courses (paid)
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude
  • Joy in varied challenges
  • Good German language skills, both oral and written
  • Experience in the care and support of trainees (SVEB 1 preferable or the willingness to complete it)
