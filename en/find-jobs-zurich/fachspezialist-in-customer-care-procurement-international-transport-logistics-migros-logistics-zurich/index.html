Full description

We deliver! Migros Logistics brings together Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (MVB) and Migros Logistique Romandie SA (MLR) to supply Switzerland every day. With modern technology and highly automated processes, we shape the logistics of tomorrow. Start with us into the future of logistics as a specialist* in Customer Care & Procurement international Transport Logistics at 60%.

Mansioni

  • You support the "Customer Service & Service Purchasing" area on all traffic modalities taking into account operational/customs and foreign trade requirements.
  • Independently, you will process specialist task packages such as transport inquiries, create offers and tenders and carry out calculations and analyses.
  • With your high service orientation, you will supervise and advise specialists and decision-makers of the Migros Group on complex transport subjects.
  • You create presentations and carry out transport training for our customers.
  • Operational reporting, the creation of statistics and the securing of controlling reports are part of your area of responsibility.
  • Demanding clarifications on international framework conditions, customs and foreign trade topics as well as on insurance and transport risks are handled independently.

Requirements

  • Professional experience of several years: In the international logistics/forwarding sector
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): Commercial basic training and a completed further education in the field of logistics or supply chain management (e.g. logistics specialist with federal certificate, CAS Supply Chain/Logistics)
  • You have a solid knowledge of rules of origin, free trade zones and customs regulations.
  • In addition, you bring in-depth experience of customer aspects, negotiations of transport prices on various transport modalities.
  • Self-employment, a sense of responsibility and the joy of actively shaping things are among your strengths.
  • Very good IT knowledge (MS Office, Excel knowledge) is mandatory.
  • Analytical thinking, organizational talent and a solution- and result-oriented working method are among your strengths.
  • As a team player, you enjoy working in a dynamic logistics environment and actively participate in the team.
  • German (flowing)
  • English (very good knowledge)
  • French (good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Central location: Our locations are directly connected to the motorway or public transport
  • Modern infrastructure: Our logistics work with fully automated, state-of-the-art facilities. Office workplaces meet the latest standards
  • Flexible place of work / home office: We promote flexibility, agility and efficiency with our home office options
  • Development opportunities: We offer individual support programs and task hours. For most professions, you can complete a multi-week stage in another company.
  • Education and training: We support our employees through targeted training and further education offers and support them generously
  • Employee offers: We offer our employees many special services and discounts (e.g. fuel, mobile subscriptions)
