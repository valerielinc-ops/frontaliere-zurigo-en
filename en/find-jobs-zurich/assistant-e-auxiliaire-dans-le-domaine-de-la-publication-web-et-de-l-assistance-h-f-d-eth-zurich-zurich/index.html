Assistant: in the field of web publishing and support (m/f/d)

30%, Zurich, temporary

The Department of University Communication at ETH Zurich is looking for a student for a part-time job in the field of web publishing and support. The employment takes place in the hourly wage at a rate of 30%.

Are you interested in online communication and web technologies? Do you work carefully and reliably and do you also enjoy recurring tasks? Would you like to get an insight into the communication work of a university at the same time? Then we look forward to meeting you.

Job description

  • In job sharing with a colleague, you will produce and publish news articles from university communication in the content management system of ETH Zurich as well as on other web platforms. In doing so, you carefully examine the contributions and prepare images for an optimal presentation on the web.
  • They participate in editorial meetings and answer questions about the scheduling, preparation and publication of the contributions.
  • They help the web team update content on the homepage.
  • You also take on administrative tasks within university communication (e.g. contact database, image database update and maintain)

The term of office shall be one year. An extension is possible, provided you are still enrolled in the study.

Profile

  • You are studying, ideally in a subject area related to communication or web technologies.
  • They move safely in the digital world and quickly work their way into new IT tools. Ideally, you already have initial experience with content management systems.
  • They like to work in a team and bring a high level of motivation.
  • They are characterised by reliability and readiness.
  • They have good knowledge of English.
