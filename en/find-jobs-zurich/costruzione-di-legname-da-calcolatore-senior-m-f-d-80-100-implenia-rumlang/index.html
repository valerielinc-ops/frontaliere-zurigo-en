Full description

At Implenia Holzbau, a lot is currently in motion – driven by the growing importance of sustainable construction and our clear focus on modern timber construction. With our many years of experience, high technical competence and a strong interdisciplinary team, we realize demanding timber construction services across all phases – from planning to production to execution.

In order to further develop our organization and shape timber construction in a future-oriented way, we are looking for committed personalities who want to take responsibility and grow together with us – as a calculator (senior, junior) (m/f/d).

Do you have what it takes to turn your communication skills and entrepreneurial thinking into attractive offers? Are analytical, timely and independent work just as important to you as your excellent skills as a team player? Then we are very much looking forward to your commitment for our team in Rümlang!

These tasks await you

  • Responsible preparation of calculations and supplementary offers
  • Independent development of the submission processes until input incl. Coordination of subcontractor requests
  • Development of embodiment variants and economic calculations
  • Preparation and monitoring of contract negotiations
  • Independent acquisition of new orders for element production
  • Preparation and maintenance of calculation key figures based on post-calculations

We want this education and experience from you

  • Apprenticeship as carpenter and polisher training or technician HF
  • Sound knowledge of timber construction
  • Professional experience in the execution of timber construction projects, ideally already as a calculator / builder / project manager
  • Safe handling of Word, Excel and PowerPo

This is what we offer you.

  • Challenging and attractive tasks in larger projects
  • A motivated team
  • Varied challenges
  • Innovative and sustainable working environment
  • Support for employee development (promotion of further education by the Implenia Academy)
  • Implenia-Benefits, including (to name a few):
  • a company-owned pension fund with three contribution levels: Implenia Provision
  • 5 weeks holidays and 9 paid bridge days
  • Travel insurance for you and everyone living in your household
  • Benefits for Implenia employees on products and services

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
