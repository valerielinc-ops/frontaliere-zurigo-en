Full description

You enjoy customer contact and your heart beats for receivables management? Then you are exactly right with us. In the well-established team of our debt collection and loss certificate collection I group, you have the opportunity to participate in the development of processes and IT solutions - an exciting opportunity to contribute your ideas and actively shape your working environment. In the course of a retirement succession, a place with us will be available on January 1, 2027 or by arrangement - perhaps exactly for you.

Tasks

  • You will handle competently, sovereignly and independently the cases assigned to you in our demanding legal environment
  • You initiate and monitor the SchKG procedures
  • They independently agree on payment terms and are responsible for their control and correct account management.
  • They ensure the collection of claims for loss certificates and keep an eye on both the situation of our customers and the interests of the City of Zurich
  • You personally get in touch with our customers and third parties in writing, by telephone and at our counter and process the daily correspondence conscientiously and independently.

Profile

  • Completed commercial or equivalent basic training as well as in-depth debt collection experience and very good knowledge of SchKG
  • Good knowledge of SAP HANA, ASEM and eSchKG applications
  • Willingness to perform, self-confident appearance, open, convincing communication and a solution-oriented approach to our demanding customers
  • Very good written and oral expression in German, we would also be very happy about good language skills in French and Italian, any other language of advantage
  • High IT affinity, willingness to make the most of the opportunities offered by digitalization
  • Team spirit, humor, optimism and a positive human image

We offer

a challenging and varied task area in which you can contribute your strengths. They expect attractive employment conditions with attractive benefits, such as the assumption of 60% of personal savings contributions, a mobility contribution of up to CHF 600 per year, lunch checks or cheap mobile subscriptions for private use. We offer you modern workplaces, easily accessible by public transport, in the administrative center Eggbühl in Zurich-Seebach, the possibility of home office and not forgetting many new colleagues who are already looking forward to you.

About us

The City Court of Zurich with around 60 employees is the largest criminal offence authority in Switzerland. We are responsible for conducting the ordinary criminal proceedings for violations committed on the territory of the city of Zurich. In addition, we manage the loss certificates from almost all areas of the Zurich city administration.
