Full description

Introduction

Join our Electronics Engineering team to play a key role in developing innovative electronics solutions for high-volume industrial products. You will drive the design of integrated power supply systems, leverage advanced simulation tools, and contribute to cutting-edge, data-driven engineering approaches in a collaborative international environment.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design and develop electronics for high-volume industrial products.
  • Create innovative solutions for integrated power supply systems.
  • Use simulation tools to validate concepts, designs, and engineering decisions.
  • Ensure high quality standards throughout the entire product development lifecycle.
  • Support products from concept development through market launch.
  • Drive continuous improvement of engineering methods, tools, and development practices.
  • Contribute to the adoption of modern data-driven and AI-assisted engineering approaches.

Required Expertise

  • Degree in Electrical Engineering (ETH, University, or University of Applied Sciences).
  • Strong expertise in power electronics and analog/digital circuit design.
  • Several years of experience in electronics development within an industrial environment.
  • Excellent communication skills and a collaborative, team-oriented mindset.
  • High level of personal initiative and passion for innovation.
  • Knowledge of systems engineering and control systems is an advantage.
  • Hands-on experience with simulation tools such as LTspice, TINA, and PLECS.
  • Good communication skills in German and English

People at Belimo

Smart buildings start with dedicated people. We bring talented people together and foster an environment to succeed. At Belimo, respect, trust, and support across departments help ideas move quickly into real-world solutions, because we achieve more together than we ever could alone.

About Belimo

Belimo is the global market leader in the development, production, and sales of field devices for the energy-efficient control of heating, ventilation and air-conditioning systems. The focus of our core business is on damper actuators, control valves, sensors and meters. Founded in 1975, the company has been listed on the Swiss Exchange (SIX) since 1995.
