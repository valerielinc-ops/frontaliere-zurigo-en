Full description

Broad internal medicine. Interdisciplinary cooperation. Space for design. The GZO Spital Wetzikon is the acute care hospital for the Zurich Oberland with around 900 employees. Our medical clinic is recognized as a training institution category A and treats more than 4,000 inpatients every year. Twelve specialist areas, neurology with Stroke Unit and emergency and intensive care medicine work closely together. Flat hierarchies, short decision paths and a collegial culture create an environment in which you can take on responsibility and develop professionally. The GZO is currently in a financial restructuring phase as part of a deferred estate. Hospital operations continue to be stable and unrestricted. Together we shape the future of our hospital – with high medical quality and a clear focus on our patients. We are looking for an experienced, committed and communicatively adept acting
