Full description

We are looking for you as a Senior Cloud Network Engineer to join our Cloud Access team. Together with your colleagues, you will be responsible for the further development and operation of Migros' cloud network platform. Your focus is clearly on the cloud sector with the tech stack: Software-Defined Networking (SDN) (Azure/GCP), Core-DNS, Consul, WebProxy, IaC (Terraform/Terragrunt), Observability (Grafana).

Mansioni

  • Design, engineering and operation of the Cloud Landing Zone in Azure and GCP with a clear focus on networking and connectivity as well as scalable and secure platform services
  • Design and implementation of automated cloud network and platform services as well as further development of infrastructure-as-code standards (Terraform / Terragrunt) including their automation
  • Establishment and further development of cloud connectivity (VPC / VNet, routing, DNS, hybrid connectivity, Zero Trust) with a focus on the cloud sector as well as ensuring observability (monitoring, logging, alerting) for platform services
  • Analysis and implementation of requirements from dev teams as well as continuous improvement of the developer experience including troubleshooting and performance optimization of the cloud infrastructure
  • Close cooperation with other platform teams, security, architecture and external partners
  • Active contribution to architecture decisions as well as co-creation of standards and best practices in cloud networking
  • Assumption of 3rd level support and on-call service (OnCall)

Requisiti

  • Several years of experience: as a Cloud Engineer / Platform Engineer / Network Engineer in enterprise environments
  • Completed studies (university/ETH, FH, HF): in computer science or equivalent professional experience
  • Very good knowledge of cloud networking on Azure and/or GCP (VPC/VNet, Routing, DNS, Hybrid Connectivity, Security, Zero Trust)
  • Experience with DevOps principles (CI/CD, automation, GitOps)
  • Experience with Infrastructure as Code (Terraform / Terragrunt) as well as in architecture or design decisions
  • Know-how in the operation of distributed systems as well as in observability (e.g. Grafana, Logging, Metrics)
  • Ability to analyze complex technical problems in a structured way and solve them sustainably
  • "Get things done" mentality and a high level of personal responsibility as well as curiosity and willingness to evaluate and use new technologies and trends in the cloud environment
  • Team player with good communication skills, pragmatic mindset and enjoyment of working together on an equal footing
  • German (business fluent)
  • English (business fluent)
