Location: Hirslanden Clinic in the Park | Zurich

Occupation by appointment

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 22506

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

YOUR TASKS

  • Professional and high-quality care of intensive care patients and care of relatives
  • Independent planning, organisation and execution of the assigned tasks
  • Responsible for the physical and psychological well-being and safety of patients
  • Accompanying and supporting our NDS students in close cooperation with the education team
  • Participation in process optimization as well as in work and/or project groups and management of performance recording

Your profile

  • Completed training as an expert / expert intensive care NDS HF or a similar training with a specialization in intensive care mandatory
  • You like to work independently and have a personality with a high level of technical and social competence who thinks and acts in an interdisciplinary and self-initiative manner.
  • You are open to new things.
  • You are capable of teamwork, flexible and happy to contribute your ideas
  • A high customer orientation is a matter of course for you

You can expect a motivated, committed and multi-professional team in an advanced working environment. This is characterized by high quality of care, independence, flexibility and a sense of responsibility. We also offer our employees generous social benefits, sports offers, special insurance conditions and further education opportunities as part of our program.

As employees who have to move completely for their work, you get four additional paid days off with a 100% workload (except specialist and cadre doctors). With a reduced workload, the days off reduce accordingly. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from an equivalent financial compensation.
