You shape how work works every day. Imagine: Your decisions determine how thousands of employees work, communicate and work together every day. This is your impact in this role. As Teamlead Modern Workplace, you don’t just design IT – you shape the digital working environment of our entire organization. You make work easier, more efficient and more connected. Whether meeting, chat, laptop or collaboration tool – you and your team ensure that everything works reliably and plays together optimally. This is how you shape health

  • Drive the transformation towards cloud, standardization and automation
  • You develop collaboration so that it is really lived – not only technically, but in everyday life.
  • You actively bring future topics such as co-pilot, AI and new forms of work into the organization
  • Ensure that security, compliance and governance requirements are met
  • You build sustainable, strategic partnerships and work with strong partners such as Microsoft and other providers This sets you apart
