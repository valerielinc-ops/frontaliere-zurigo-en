Vocational trainer intensive care unit — Spital Zollikerberg, Zollikerberg (ZH).

Area: Nursing professions.

Welcome to your new place of work.

For our intensive care unit of the Spital Zollikerberg we are looking for you as

Vocational trainer/vocational intensive care unit

If you too want to make a difference in people’s lives, these are your tasks:

  • Professional support as well as targeted promotion and development of the students of the post-graduate intensive care program as well as the students nurses and FaGe learners
  • Mastering complex treatment situations together with students and learners including creation of learning opportunities
  • Active participation in the station’s management team for the further development of training, including the introduction of educational topics
  • General optimization of training and strengthening of the nursing team in support of learners and students
  • Promoting one’s own professional pedagogical competence through professional exchange, networking and active participation in the various organisational networks

Become part of the Department of Medical Services at the Zollikerberg Hospital. In our interdisciplinary intensive care unit, we look after around 500 patients per year. The station complies with the current recommendations and standards of the Swiss Society for Intensive Care Medicine (SGI) and is a recognized training institution for the post-graduate degree program. Expert or Dipl. Expert intensive care NDS. As part of the Service Center Operating Rooms and Intensive Care Medicine (DLZ OP/IS), we work closely with the various departments of the hospital.
