About the role

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a rich tradition of innovation. Become part of our global team to expand your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

Sulzer Chemtech manufactures mass transfer components for the chemical and petrochemical industries. Production employees in the field of “bending, laser and punching machines” are responsible for the production of components for our distributors, floors and other column installations.

Sulzer offers you a variety of opportunities to steer your career in the direction you want, depending on your skills and interests. We invite you to apply when you are ready to take the next step.

Career opportunity: Employee for the production of structured packs (m/f/d) at Sulzer Chemtech in Essen. Tasks and responsibilities:

  • Production of structured packs according to specifications and schedules
  • Workstations: punching and grooves, bending, cutting, assembly, quality control, packaging
  • Work on automatic and semi-automatic production lines with customised production facilities
  • Work in shift mode
  • Implementation of adjustments and adjustments of machines to ensure speed and quality of production output
  • Participation in the implementation of projects at all workplaces in the structured packaging workshop
  • Notification of non-conformities and deviations from production plans to the packaging workshop foreman
  • Contribution to continuous improvement measures in cooperation with the Quality Department
  • Work in accordance with all relevant policies, processes and standard operating procedures
  • Work in accordance with environmental, safety and health guidelines
  • Other production-related tasks and projects by allocation.

Requirements

  • Craft/technical training, workshop training
  • At least 3-5 years of professional experience in these areas
  • Expertise: knowledge of the manufacturing process of cutting, punching, bending and welding sheet metal
  • Affinity for attentive and quality-oriented cooperation with people of different origins and nationalities
  • Negotiating knowledge of German
