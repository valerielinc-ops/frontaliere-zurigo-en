Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We advise individuals and companies about money, manage assets and offer attractive financial services. We are very successful and growing strongly.

  • You will complete an external basic apprenticeship year (Bbc) in the first year of apprenticeship. This allows you to quickly start productive projects. From the second year of apprenticeship, you will work at our headquarters in the center of Zurich.

  • You visit the Sek A in the Canton of Zurich or an equivalent level in another canton, bring good school performance and enjoy people, business and technology.
  • You like to get to the bottom of something and have a flair for numbers.
  • You have quick comprehension and logical thinking.
  • You work carefully and reliably.
  • You bring the openness to discover new things and collaborate with different people.
  • Regular development discussions and individual support
  • Costs for school supplies and a contribution of CHF 1'300 to your school laptop

  • Many events such as the Summer and X-Mas Party or Learning Event
  • Free cold drinks, coffee, tea and fresh fruits
