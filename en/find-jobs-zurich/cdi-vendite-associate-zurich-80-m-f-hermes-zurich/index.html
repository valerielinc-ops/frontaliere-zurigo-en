Original description

Your task Immerse yourself in the extraordinary world of our house and become an important ambassador of our collections. Its mission is to provide each customer with a unique and individual experience, highlighting the richness and diversity of Hermès know-how in our 16 Métiers. You create a unique moment for each customer by attentively and sensitively accompanying them in the discovery of our worlds. They understand how to convey the beauty of the materials, the quality of the workmanship and the story behind each creation.

Their everyday life

  • Welcome every customer warmly and create a welcoming atmosphere that is characterized by listening and goodwill and promotes individual advice.
  • Present our collections in all their diversity, highlighting the individual pieces, materials and craftsmanship.
  • Advised accurately and enthusiastically, proposing harmonious combinations tailored to the needs and lifestyle of each individual customer, reflecting our entire craftsmanship.
  • Increase sales across all categories by integrating a seamless and modern omnichannel approach.
  • Maintain in-depth knowledge of the collections, novelties and trends and receive regular training
  • Working with and supporting the team to ensure a harmonious customer experience and joint success.

The Keys to Your Success

  • Extensive experience in luxury retail with a strong understanding of the requirements of personal customer support.
  • In-depth product knowledge and the ability to move effortlessly between different specialist areas thanks to a strong sense of materials, workmanship and quality.
  • Clear and professional communication in German and English to support a demanding clientele and to bind them to the company.
  • Active listening and adaptability to precisely respond to diverse expectations and adapt the customer experience accordingly.
  • Independence, flexibility and safe use of digital tools, supported by a natural curiosity that promotes continuous development of skills.
  • Team spirit and open-mindedness that contribute to smooth cooperation between the different disciplines.
  • Service orientation and attention to detail, ensuring an impeccable customer experience that meets the standards of the house.

Employeur responsable, nous nous engageons dans l’éthique, les diversités et l’inclusion. Rejoignez l’aventure humaine Hermès !
