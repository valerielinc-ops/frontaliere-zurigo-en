Full description

Are you looking for a role in which you not only develop technical solutions, but also create real added value through deep analysis and moderation? Then join us as an SAP Consultant and help shape the future SAP system landscape with your innovative ideas.

Tasks

  • In this position, you act as an in-depth expert for the SAP S/4HANA system landscape, focusing on the modules MM, PM and PS/PPM. Your main task is not only to understand complex technical requirements, but also to analyze them into the details through sporadic “deep dives” and translate them into robust, user-friendly solutions.
  • You are the interface that transforms technical complexity into comprehensible actions for the department.
  • You consistently look outside the box: you do not consider problems in isolation, but in the overall context of the VBZ processes and coordinate solutions across departments and departments.
  • You coordinate implementation with suppliers and the OIZ, ensure clean test management and ensure that changes not only work technically, but also arrive in everyday work.
  • In addition to project management and the implementation of operational changes, you spend 10% time in 2nd level support to be a reliable contact for demanding questions of the power users.

Profile

  • You have an FH degree in Business Informatics or an equivalent training (NDS/MAS) and bring in-depth practical experience in SAP MM, PM and PS.
  • You have customizing knowledge and experience in agile methods (Scrum, Kanban) and structured project management (Hermes).
  • You have proven experience in project management and the ability to quickly familiarize yourself with new topics and penetrate them deeply.
  • You speak German (C2) and English (B2) to communicate effectively with internal and external stakeholders.
  • You are curious, update your knowledge independently and manage to transfer new technologies such as AI into everyday SAP life.
