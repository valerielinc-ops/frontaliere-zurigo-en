Original description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

Based at our Birmingham Service Centre, you'll work from one of the most advanced facilities of its kind in the UK. Opened in 2021 following a major investment, this 82,000 sq. ft Centre of Excellence has been purpose-built around lean manufacturing principles and is equipped with cutting-edge digital technologies, advanced machining capabilities and state-of-the-art additive manufacturing.

Operating 24/7, the site delivers world-class maintenance, repair and overhaul solutions for pumps, motors, generators, compressors, turbines and other critical rotating equipment across a wide range of industries. What you'll be doing:

  • Operating manual lathes, mills and borers
  • Precision machining of new and refurbished components
  • Mechanical fitting, assembly and repair work
  • Working from engineering drawings and specifications
  • Supporting complex overhaul and refurbishment projects

What we're looking for

  • Strong manual machining skills
  • Mechanical fitting experience is an advantage
  • Ability to work to tight tolerances
  • Pride in producing quality workmanship
  • Metal spraying experience would be a distinct advantage
