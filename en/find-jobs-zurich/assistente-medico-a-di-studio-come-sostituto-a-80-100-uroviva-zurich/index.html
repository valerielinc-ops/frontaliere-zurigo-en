Full description

Uroviva stands for a modern medical network and includes a special clinic for urology, nine urological practices and an andrology center. With approximately 70,000 consultations each year in primary, special and emergency care and more than 5,000 operations of all severity levels, Uroviva is an important provider of urological services in Switzerland. As a result of strong growth, we are looking for you as a reinforcement for our teams. Are you looking for an exciting challenge with a lot of variety and flexibility? Then you are exactly right with us. For our various practice locations we search by agreement we search for a / Medical/practice assistant as springer, 80-100 %

Their tasks

  • They are currently intervening in various practice locations in the cantons of Zurich, Zug and Basel-Land
  • If necessary also in other cantons
  • Planning, organisation and preparation of office hours
  • Reception services such as appointments, telephone triage and patient registration
  • Blood samples, urine analyses, wound and bandage changes and instillations
  • Preparation and assistance of all urological and / or gynecological examinations
  • Professional, hygienic and impeccable care of the examination rooms
  • Miscellaneous administrative work

Your profile

  • Completed training as medical/practice assistant/EFZ or specialist health EFZ
  • Experience as an MPA
  • Reporting experience
  • Good MS Office skills and foreign language skills
  • Friendly, reliable and competent personality who can quickly integrate into different teams
  • Great flexibility and open to innovations
  • High independence and self-initiative in daily work

We offer

Contemporary employment conditions (depending on age 5-6 weeks holidays and a worldwide, private accident insurance) and exciting activities with the opportunity to contribute our own ideas make us an attractive employer. Flat hierarchies and a family, appreciative approach also distinguish us.

  • For this function you will receive a lucrative remuneration
  • You can also look forward to regular working hours without night work.
  • Mooring time is paid in time or financially (as desired)
