Effretikon, Zurich / Schaffhausen Cooperation Permanent employment 40–80% Job: By appointment General physiotherapy Geriatrics Neurology Surgery Orthopaedics / Traumatology Musculoskeleton Contact details Employers Physio Bewegt Effretikon GmbH Märtplatz 19 8307 Effretikon Website Job title What to expect from us

  • You independently treat outpatients and inpatients:
  • You decide independently about the course, structure and adaptation of the therapy
  • You work with multiple, often complex cases
  • AAÜP rehabilitation including functional assessments are part of this
  • You have real time for your patient: inside (no minute clocking)
  • You work closely and uncomplicatedly with doctors, care and ergo

The team and the culture We are a small, family physio team.

What is important to us

  • mutual support instead of competition
  • Open communication without hierarchical thinking
  • Professional exchange at eye level

What we offer you

  • Fair and transparent compensation
  • Flexible employment (40–80%)
  • Working time frame 08:00–17:00 (no evening or weekend work)
  • 5 weeks holiday
  • Support for further education and training (time + money)
  • Modern, bright practice rooms with very good infrastructure
  • Real co-creation in everyday practice
  • Central location with very good public transport connection (Effretikon)

What you should bring

  • Completed training as a physiotherapist: in FH/HF or with SRK recognition
  • Joy of working and thinking independently
  • Structured, careful working method
  • Interested in developing yourself professionally
  • Clear, respectful communication
  • Teamwork
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Interest in Geriatrics
  • Motivational attitude for the elderly and humor

About us Physio Bewegt Effretikon is a modern practice that enables good physiotherapy with time, structure and focus on the essentials. We work evidence-based, holistic and human. It is not the treatment plan that is at the center, but the person. Application – uncomplicated
