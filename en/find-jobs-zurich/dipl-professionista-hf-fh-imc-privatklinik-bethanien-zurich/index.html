Treatment at the highest level, high-class medical care and care, competent care in a more familiar, private atmosphere with hotel comfort, dedication, conscientiousness and absolute discretion – this is what the private clinic Bethanien stands for. Around 340 medical doctors from different disciplines provide high-quality, integrated medical care. Among the most important specialties are orthopaedics, visceral and thoracic surgery, urology, gynecology and birth, gastroenterology, cardiology, pneumology and internal medicine incl. Oncology. In the birth department, around 500 children are born each year. Founded in 1912, the private clinic Bethanien is part of the Swiss Medical Network.
