Full description

You would like to start training as a nurse HF and are looking for a training company? Then you are exactly right with us!

This study is also suitable for lateral beginners. We accompany and support you.

Tasks

  • You will learn everything about care and care in long-term care in an environment that has committed itself to training and young talent development and makes the necessary resources fully available.
  • You will learn and experience with us how exciting, varied and attractive the cooperation between qualified nurses, the medical profession and other medical persons such as physiotherapists and occupational therapists is.
  • You will have the opportunity to participate in insight days in internal areas such as case discussions and medical therapies and to get to know in depth the different focus areas in long-term care at different departments.
  • You will be accompanied by well-qualified vocational trainers during your studies and benefit from the exchange with other students and trainees in your department.

Profile

  • Teamwork and joy in dealing with people
  • Skill in practical work, care and hygiene awareness
  • Networked thinking, responsibility and service orientation
  • A completed vocational apprenticeship with a Federal Certificate of Competence (EFZ), specialised secondary school card or graduate school
  • Ability to communicate professionally and form relationships

We offer

As a student of the Hochschule Pflege (HF) you will become part of the team and get to know the care in all its facets – from long-term care, via dementia, geriatrics, gerontopsychiatry to palliative care and rehabilitative transition care. The full-time study lasts three years, but can be shortened as a trained specialist health EFZ, within two years. In addition, we also offer a part-time study, which can be completed in a 60% period within four years.

We offer you a professional training environment with committed vocational trainers, an open du-culture, team spirit and passion. At the City of Zurich, you benefit from attractive working conditions and comprehensive social benefits. Our houses create a special flair and the ideal environment to develop your talents.

About us

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.
