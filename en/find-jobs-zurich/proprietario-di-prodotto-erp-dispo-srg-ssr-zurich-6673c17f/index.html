Full description

The Operations division assumes the central strategic and controlling role for the technological orientation and production of the SRG SSR. The ERP Dispo team ensures the reliable operation and continuous development of the central Dispo system landscape with VimBiz.

As a Product Owner ERP Dispo, you define and prioritize the further development of the product together with the Product Manager. You combine the needs of the departments with the strategic goals and ensure efficient and reliable operation together with the team.

This is your contribution.

In the area of operations, you are involved as a product owner in the ERP Dispo product team for the following topics:

  • Alignment of VimBiz’s further development and operation with the product strategy, business requirements and user needs
  • Clarification of requirements with departments and other stakeholders as well as translation into implementable, prioritized entries in the product backlog
  • Joint design and clear communication of product vision, goals and roadmap in close cooperation with the Product Manager
  • Technical management of implementation as well as coordination of iteration planning, acceptances, releases and deployments with the team and external partners
  • Ensure efficient handling of service requests, incidents, problems and changes together with the team as well as compliance with the agreed service levels
  • Coordination of interfaces and dependencies to adjacent systems and teams as well as continuous improvement of the product and operational processes

They inspire us.

  • In-depth professional experience in the field of ERP or scheduling systems as well as an understanding of scheduling and resource planning processes
  • Knowledge of vimbiz is beneficial
  • Experience in prioritizing and controlling products or services as well as product backlog management
  • Knowledge in the management of service requests, incidents, problems and changes as well as in release and deployment coordination
  • Strong communication and moderation skills as well as experience working with departments, engineers and external partners
  • Customer-oriented and structured working methods, understanding governance and quality requirements, and motivation to continuously improve throughout the product lifecycle
  • Very good knowledge of a national language as well as German or French at at least level B1
  • Knowledge of English at least level B1 is an advantage

So we inspire you.

  • Design freedom in the further development of VimBiz and the central dispo system landscape of the SRG SSR
  • A versatile task at the interface between IT, disposition and production
  • Product-oriented, agile collaboration with the departments, the product manager and an interdisciplinary team
  • The opportunity to actively shape efficient scheduling processes and the technological development of the SRG SSR

Our promise

What does it take to reach the people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expertise, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can contribute and develop. For the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: Welcome to the world of SRG!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, promote creativity and offer a lot of design space for new and high-quality products.
  • Shaping digitalization: Quality journalism is also digital for us. That’s why we explore new possibilities and experiment with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-medial. We design on the pulse of time and on the nerve of the audience.
  • Being human: We meet each other collegially, work independently and shape our working atmosphere individually. Our success is based on the personal strengths as well as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programs. In doing so, we act respectfully and independently; in this way we promote opinion formation and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.
