Full description

With us, you take on a versatile and responsible task at the interface of HR Payroll and Controlling. You work with modern HR systems, actively contribute your ideas and make an important contribution to data-based decision-making in the company. You can expect a collegial environment, short decision-making paths and attractive employment conditions.

Mansioni

  • Collaboration in the day-to-day operational payroll business and in the processing of wages
  • Responsibility for time management including ensuring data quality
  • Development and further development of HR controlling and reporting (key figures, analyses, plausibility checks and reporting)
  • Contact person for internal and external positions for questions about HR indicators and evaluations
  • Ensuring monthly HR controlling, reporting, ad hoc analyses and high data and process quality
  • Preparation and presentation of relevant key figures for various stakeholders

Requirements

  • In-depth experience and skills: in HR controlling/payroll with implementation and development skills
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): in the commercial sector, HR payroll or financial accounting
  • Very good knowledge of Power BI, SAP SuccessFactors and the motivation to actively shape and develop a new role
  • Personality with a high service orientation as well as pleasure in working with numbers and data
  • Strong analytical mindset and conceptual skills
  • Structured and reliable operation with high quality standards
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • French (advantageous)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central location: Central locations with excellent public transport connections
  • Modern infrastructure: modern, bright office space with ergonomic workstations
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Migros Bank: Private and investment account with preferential interest and expense-free account management
  • Sports and fitness offers: Cost sharing in subscriptions to various leisure and fitness facilities of the Migros Group
