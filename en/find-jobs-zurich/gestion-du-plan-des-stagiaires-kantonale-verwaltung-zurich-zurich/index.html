Canton of Zurich Economic Directorate Office for Mobility Intern/in Plan Administration 60 - 80% The Office for Mobility is the cantonal competence centre for all questions relating to mobility. Whether on two or four wheels, on foot, by train or plane – mobility should be attractive, reliable, affordable and at the same time climate-friendly. Today, we are shaping tomorrow’s mobility offering, taking into account population and economic development, environmental goals and technical innovations. With us, you can expect an exciting working environment, meaningful tasks and committed colleagues. Do you work in a structured way and keep track of large amounts of data? Are you interested in current construction and planning files as well as historical plans of road projects? In the Plan Administration of the Office for Mobility, we carry out the central filing and documentation of the state roads and art buildings in the Canton of Zurich. Research in the files, advice on filing and the management of the stocks are our core tasks. In this way, we create the basis for sound decisions in transport and infrastructure projects. We are looking for you on January 1, 2027 or by appointment as an intern in plan management (limited to 12 months). Your main tasks

  • Participation in the implementation of the digitization of our files (including quality control of metadata)
  • Preparation of files and planning of processes and quality controls as part of the scanning of files by external company
  • Cooperation in the preparation of files to the State Archives
  • Cooperation in general plan management tasks such as customer inquiries, research and information
  • Cooperation in the management of deposit facilities Our requirements
  • You are You are studying at a technical college or university - a technical reference is an advantage but no must
  • Quick comprehension and interest in structural issues and systematic filing
  • Technical understanding and imagination
  • Strong geographical understanding
  • Very good knowledge of Office applications
  • Physical strength and independent working methods
  • Responsibility and confidentiality
  • Teamwork and good written and oral expression Our offer We offer you a versatile area of work in a lively environment and a motivated and well-established team. You can use your experience and competence profitably in this exciting and varied activity. You benefit from our contemporary employment conditions with a workplace directly at Zurich Central Station. Interested in applying? Then we should definitely get to know each other. Mr. Roland Baur, Team Leader Plan Management, telephone 043 259 30 68, will be happy to provide you with information about this interesting task. We look forward to your application!

Pensum: 60-80 %
