Full description

Vehicles are your world. You know how they work and are familiar with spare parts. You make sure that the cars are roadworthy and repair them if necessary.

Tasks

  • You maintain and repair vehicles, i.e. engines, transmissions, brake, exhaust and lighting systems.
  • You test different components of the engine, drive and chassis.
  • You treat carrosserie and chassis against weather and aging and apply new paint.
  • You monitor the spare parts and material storage.

Profile

  • Compulsory schooling completed
  • Passed aptitude test AGVS (Auto Gewerbe Verband Schweiz)
  • Strong technical understanding and skill
  • Exact, careful work
  • Reliability and diligence

We offer

You can expect a well-founded and varied education. Your apprentices support you on your way to a successful teaching degree. You benefit from attractive employment conditions.

About us

If you are interested in commercial vehicles and have technical understanding and are skilled in craftsmanship, an apprenticeship as an automotive specialist EFZ Commercial Vehicles is the right choice. As an automotive specialist EFZ Commercial Vehicles, it is your task to carry out extensive service and control work on various vehicle components and to maintain them. You are responsible for the inspection and maintenance of motors, drives, chassis as well as electrical, electronic, safety and comfort systems. You use computer-assisted measurement and test systems to carry out an accurate diagnosis.
