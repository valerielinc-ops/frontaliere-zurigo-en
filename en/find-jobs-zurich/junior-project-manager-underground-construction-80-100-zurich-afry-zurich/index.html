Become part of our team CIVIL. We manage, develop, design and plan infrastructure projects of all kinds in civil engineering and tunnelling. We accompany these projects from planning to implementation.

Take responsibility with us as

JUNIOR PROJECTOR: IN SUBTAGBAU 80-100% - AUSTRIA YOUR TASKS – TECHNICAL DEEPS. CONCRETE RESPONSIBILITY.

  • Developing technical solutions: elaborating and comparing concepts for challenging underground and infrastructure projects – from the first idea to the ready-for-execution planning
  • Processing project parts independently: taking responsibility for clearly defined work packages and keeping an eye on technical quality, deadlines and effort, with the support of experienced project managers:
  • Coordinate planning: control the creation of plans and models for your work packages and coordinate interfaces with internal and external disciplines
  • Growing into project management: Supporting project management in technical and organisational handling and gradually assuming responsibility for larger project parts

Your chances are growing. MEMBER PROJECTS.

  • Combining technology and project management: deepening expertise in underground mining and at the same time expanding your competences in coordination and project management
  • Use creative leeway: Introducing proprietary solutions into interdisciplinary teams, discussing variants at eye level and actively shaping technical implementations
  • Benefiting from the exchange of experience: Accompaniment by experienced specialists: inside and project managers: inside the project day and support for challenging questions
  • Contributing to your own development: Whether technical deepening, BIM or project management – develop Your priorities continue according to your own interests and strengths
  • Working flexibly: Flexible working hours and the opportunity to work mobile create scope for work and private life

YOUR PROFIL – TECHNIQUED. Already for more responsibility

  • Completed degree in Civil Engineering (ETH, EPFL or FH)
  • First professional experience in project planning or realization of construction projects, ideally in underground or infrastructure construction
  • Motivation to process technically demanding tasks independently and increasingly take responsibility for project parts and their coordination
  • Analyze complex relationships, question solutions and develop technically sound, economical and practical solutions
  • Structured and reliable working methods, clear communication and enjoyment of professional exchange in the team and with customers
  • Interest in digital planning methods such as BIM and in expanding the appropriate professional and methodological skills
  • Very good and stylish knowledge of German (verbal and written) – at native level or at least at level C1
