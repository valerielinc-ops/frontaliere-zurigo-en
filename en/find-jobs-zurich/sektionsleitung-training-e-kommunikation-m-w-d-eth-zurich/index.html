Section Management Training and Communication (m/f/d)

80%-100%, Zurich, permanent

Printed Printing

We at the Department of Safety, Health and Environment (SGU) advise, support and train all organizational units and members of ETH Zurich on questions relating to safety and health at work. We are the contact point for all centrally organized measures in dealing with hazards and risks for the protection of people, animals, property, infrastructure and the environment as well as for questions regarding the fulfilment of the corresponding legal requirements. In the event of an incident, we maintain an appropriate emergency organisation. Both in everyday life and in the event of an incident, we work closely with the authorities and external forces.

SGU informs and trains all ETH members and, if necessary, guests and visitors about safety-relevant topics and thus supports the entire institution in compliance with legal compliance in occupational safety, health protection and occupational environmental protection. The Training and Communication section ensures the target-group-oriented mediation of safety, health and environmental topics, promotes the awareness-raising of ETH members and continuously develops its communication and training offer in the dynamic university environment.

Project background

In order to be able to fulfill these tasks professionally and efficiently, we are looking from 1. September 2026 or by arrangement a dedicated personality as section manager training and communication (m/f/d).

You enjoy and are equally tested in communication (company communication) as well as in training and learning, can combine these two contents under great complexity and convey them in an understandable way? Interdisciplinary questions and the coordination of different stakeholders and target groups are a challenge for you that you are happy to accept? You love to develop existing processes but also your small team and really want to make a difference with your actions? Then you are exactly right with us! We are looking for a personality who, in addition to the focus on communication and training, also has a great interest in security-relevant topics; because in this task you can make a significant contribution to anchoring the security organization and a lived security culture at ETH Zurich.

Are you looking for a position in which you can express your language skills and leadership responsibility in a demanding and visible environment?

That is exactly what we offer you. Because the job requires a high degree of independence, conceptual thinking and coordination skills. You not only have the professional responsibility for the further development of the training and communication offer of the SGU (including quality control), but also lead the section with two employees and one to two KV learners. In addition, you will advise and support the SGU leadership in communication measures and questions. You work together with a large number of internal and external stakeholders and thus ensure addressable communication and adequate information resp. Training on Safety & Security. The function combines strategic concept work with operational implementation and contributes significantly to awareness-raising, compliance and security culture at ETH Zurich.

You ensure an ETH-wide training and qualification management, by

  • Responsible further development of training concept and training portfolio (including legally and organisationally required training)
  • Development and quality assurance of learning opportunities
  • Demonstration and compliance management
