Full description

As a leading Swiss construction and real estate service provider, Implenia develops, realizes and manages living spaces, working environments and infrastructure for future generations in Switzerland and Germany. Implenia also offers tunnelling and related infrastructure projects in other markets. The company combines the know-how of highly qualified consulting, development, planning and execution units under one roof into an integrated, multinationally leading construction and real estate service provider. Headquartered in Opfikon near Zurich, Implenia employs more than 8,000 people (FTE) across Europe and achieved a turnover of CHF 3.5 billion in 2025. More information at implenia.com

For our Zurich conversion department we are looking for you as a polisher: in pollution remediation. Your field of application includes varied construction sites in the Zurich region. You work in a dynamic, uncomplicated team, which convinces with a healthy mixture of experience and fresh wind. You expect a strong team spirit and a lot of personal responsibility.

You move that

  • Management of the construction sites in cooperation with the project manager
  • Professional guidance and instruction of site workers as well as clean and continuous communication with the foremen
  • Plan, prepare and execute:
  • On-site inspections
  • Preparation of the required documents for the authorities and the foremen (e.g. SUVA, UGZ, STEB, cantons, municipality): forms, plans, restructuring concepts
  • Site visit with foremen before the start of the order
  • Check before work starts in the black area (according to EKAS 6503)
  • Final inspection before clearance
  • Assistance in carrying out all rehabilitation work
  • Enforcement of occupational safety
  • Enforcement and compliance with all SUVA guidelines for the protection of employees (EKAS 6503/6508), in particular the “red points list of SUVA”
  • Active cooperation to improve processes and construction site processes, work, use of materials and the like
  • Correct and friendly behavior at the customer / on the construction site
  • Preparation of polishing dimensions
  • Directing
  • Aid for supplementary management
  • Preparation of small quotes

This is why you succeed

  • Ideally, you already have experience in pollutant remediation, or you want to develop specifically in this area.
  • You work structured, organized and exact – even if it gets hectic.
  • With your entrepreneurial mindset and negotiation skills, you are always looking for win-win solutions for the customer and the company.
  • You are resilient, show a high level of commitment and keep an overview even in complex projects.
  • The handling of MS Office is easy for you and you communicate safely in German – other languages are a plus.
  • You are a real team player with a customer-oriented mindset and actively participate in the joint success.

This is what we offer you

  • A multicultural working environment with the chance to shape a future worth living in
  • State-of-the-art technologies and a contemporary workplace at a sustainable company
  • A company that promotes talent and the training of its employees
  • The security of a financially healthy, broad-based group with strong roots in Switzerland
  • An exciting selection of benefits:
  • 5 weeks holidays and 9 paid bridge days
  • an in-house pension fund with very good coverage and 3 contribution levels
  • numerous discounts on well-known suppliers of products and services
