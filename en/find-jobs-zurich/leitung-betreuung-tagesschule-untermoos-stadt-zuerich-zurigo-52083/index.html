Full description

With the introduction of the day school in August 26, our care offer and the number of children to be cared for have grown - that is why we are expanding our management team with an additional co-management care. The 60% position is divided into 40% management and 20% management as of 1 January 2027 with active participation in the supervision.

Tasks

  • Together with your two job partners, you take on operational, pedagogical and personnel responsibility for the care area and actively shape the further development of the Untermoos Day School.
  • They guide and accompany the assigned employees, promote their professional development and ensure an appreciative and constructive cooperation.
  • They take on organizational and administrative tasks and work closely with the school management, the support team and other specialist departments.
  • They work actively on two lunches in the care and support the team in the operative process of the day.
  • From Augsut 27/28 you plan, organize and coordinate as a leisure responsible person of the project «BeFrei» together with the support team.

Profile

  • degree as a social pedagoguein FH or HFS; or Social worker in FH or a recognised leadership training or the willingness to complete such
  • Experience in supplementary school care, pleasure in guiding, accompanying and supporting employees in their development.
  • Team ability both in close cooperation as co-leader of support, as well as as team member in support
  • Resilience, flexibility and calm in challenging situations.
  • Good digital skills, efficient and reliable working methods for common IT applications.

We offer

  • We offer you a responsible, varied and meaningful activity in a lively and appreciative environment.
  • Competent and supportive accompaniment by the co-directors Supervision and the school management
  • A progressive salary and attractive social benefits according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich

About us

The Zurich-Letzi school district comprises Stadtkreis 9 with the districts Albisrieden, Altstetten and Grünau. In the 16 schools of our rapidly growing school circle, more than 5,000 primary school students are taught and supervised. More than 1,200 employees work for them in our schools in the areas of teaching, care, home service and technology as well as administration. They all work daily for the interests of the students and a smooth school day.
