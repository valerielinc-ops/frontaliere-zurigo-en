Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Judicial Enforcement and Reintegration Pöschwie's Senior Specialist in Discharges at Job /I.J., 70% each Description Judicial Enforcement and Reintegration (JuWe) is responsible for the execution of the various types of detentions as part of the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs of the Canton of Zurich.For our 1,400 employees, the successful social reintegration of people who have become illegitimate and the prevention of relapses is of particular importance. The Pöschwies (JVA) prison is one of eight departments of the JuWe. The JVA Pöschwies is the largest closed prison in Switzerland for men with 399 seats. It fulfils a central state task and takes account of the resocialization of the imprisoned persons and the protection of the general public according to the legal mandate. Enforcement relaxation is a central step in the transition back into freedom. They allow imprisoned persons to assume gradual responsibility and test themselves in everyday life outside the institution. This makes an important contribution to socialisation, stabilisation and social integration. The examination of requests for holidays and exits requires a high degree of care, responsibility and judgment. For this demanding technical function, we are looking for two experienced and socially competent personalities who take on the task together in Job /I and convince them with professionalism, resilience and a structured working method.

  • Development of sound and comprehensible opinions on escape, common and relapse risks as a basis for decision-making for the management and instructing authorities
  • Structured, precise and addressable treatment of complex facts
  • Ensure compliance with conditions and conditions of approved reliefs
  • Close cooperation with the social service, the management and external authorities in the context of implementation planning
  • Participation in the further development of technical standards and support in training of accompanying persons in the field of vacation and exit
