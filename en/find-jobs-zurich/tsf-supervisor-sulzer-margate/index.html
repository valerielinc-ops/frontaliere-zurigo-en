Company Overview

Sulzer Rotating Equipment Services (SRES) is an independent provider of services and equipment repairs for the turbomachinery industry. SRES specializes in the repair, overhaul and parts sales of Heavy Industrial Turbines, Aero Gas Turbines, Steam Turbines, Compressors, Pumps, and electromechanical equipment for the Oil & Gas, Petrochemical and Power Generation industries.

Position Summary (TSF) The Production Supervisor is responsible for overseeing daily shop floor operations to ensure work is performed safely, efficiently, and in accordance with approved scopes, procedures, and quality standards. This role leads technicians and shop personnel, manages production schedules, and ensures all work is completed accurately and on time. The Production Supervisor works closely with Operations, Project Management, and Inventory teams to support execution, safety, and continuous improvement initiatives.

Responsibilities

  • Lead and supervise shop employees, ensuring work is performed in compliance with safety policies, SOPs, and quality requirements
  • Manage daily shop scheduling, issue work scopes and packets, and ensure all released work is completed per approved scope
  • Inspect all work performed and review signoffs, documentation, and assembly records for completeness and accuracy
  • Assist in the development and execution of production timelines, supporting delivery commitments and operational priorities
  • Coordinate and manage shop resources, including routers, cranes, hoists, equipment, and general housekeeping
  • Inspect and manage issued parts, request required materials, and ensure parts are allocated to the correct projects
  • Manage employee time, PTO, overtime planning, training needs, and reporting of safety incidents or near misses

Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in operations, engineering, business, or a related field preferred; equivalent experience considered
  • 5+ years of progressive experience in operations management, manufacturing, or industrial production environments
  • Strong leadership, planning, and organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities across departments
  • Proficiency with ERP systems, reporting tools, and Microsoft Office; experience with quality or safety systems preferred
