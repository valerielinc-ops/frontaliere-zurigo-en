Full description

Bräcker AG, the world’s leading supplier of rings and ring travellers for ring spinning machines, is a subsidiary of the Rieter Group. Headquartered in Pfäffikon, Switzerland, Bräcker offers added value to its customers with highly developed industry knowledge, innovative solutions, excellent after-sales services and a global presence. The company’s main products – rings and ring travelers for ring spinning machines – are manufactured in Pfäffikon and Grignasco (Italy). The company’s product range also includes grinding machines for the maintenance of roll covers.

You have power, are versatile and service- and goal-oriented? You enjoy working in a team, are curious and always find innovative ways to reach your goal? Then you fit perfectly with us and can strengthen our team as

Your main tasks and responsibilities

  • Ensure timely customer deliveries
  • Termination of the occupancy of the production machines in cooperation with the department responsible of our production and related rough / detailed planning of the production of our products, incl. Date control of current contracts
  • Procurement of raw materials, components and equipment
  • Preparation and processing of purchase orders, appointment monitoring and controls
  • Contracting to external suppliers with appointment control and all necessary clarifications
  • Close cooperation with production department managers, external suppliers, sales department, our IT and our sister company in the Czech Republic
  • Introduction of new products, master data maintenance as well as appointment monitoring
  • Self-employed storage management of individual product groups
  • Invoice control of suppliers

Your requirement profile

  • Commercial or basic technical training with kaufm. Training
  • In-depth knowledge of production control and planning (PPS) in mass production
  • Sound SAP knowledge module PP and MM
  • Strong enforcement
  • Strong analytical skills and networked thinking
  • Strong communication and organisational skills
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
