Full description

IN BREAKDOWN

You bring in-depth know-how in electrical engineering, electronics or automation, think analytically and like to tackle yourself? As an Electronics Technician (80-100%) from our workshop in Zurich Binz, you ensure that our networked refrigerators and coffee machines run smoothly. In the 12-member technical team, you are the contact point for tricky cases, grows specifically into refrigeration technology and with your knowledge bring the whole team one step further.

Your responsibilities

  • Perform demanding error diagnosis and repairs on our refrigerators and coffee machines, in the workshop and on site with our customers
  • Precisely find the cause of problems, whether it is hardware, configuration or telemetry, and immediately take the right action.
  • As a technical contact, support and advise your colleagues in particularly complex cases
  • Reliably inspect new and repaired devices for their electrical safety
  • Systematically document recurring errors and create or improve work instructions (SOPs)
  • Actively contribute to technical documentation (wire diagrams, risk assessment, NEV/EMV) for our CAREL integration and the POS system
  • Develop your expertise in refrigeration technology and share it with the team

What you bring

  • Completed training as an electrical installer, assembly electrician, electronics technician or automatic technician EFZ or a comparable qualification
  • Min. 5 years of experience in service, repair or troubleshooting of electrical, electronic or electromechanical devices
  • Structured, independent working method and enjoyment of sharing technical knowledge in the team
  • Advantage: Experience in refrigeration technology
  • Prior knowledge in technical documentation, in writing SOPs or in structured root cause analysis
  • Valid Swiss driving licence (category B)
  • Fluent knowledge of German and good English (our team language)
  • Non-smoker – we don’t want you to smell like cigarettes, neither at work nor at customer appointments. After all, FELFEL is about good food.
  • Valid work permit or Swiss passport.

What we offer

  • Lots of delicious food, just a few steps from your desk - we fulfill all your culinary desires...
  • Best barista coffee and tea for the whole team thanks to our innovative Gavetti coffee machine
  • 5 weeks vacation per year, and the possibility to purchase an extra week
  • We think about your future and offer a super-compulsory pension fund solution
  • Freshly baked parents receive 16 or more from us. 3 weeks of fully paid parental leave. We also support the return to work by making a generous financial contribution to childcare until kindergarten age.
  • We finance your half-tax subscription and thus support our employees in the use of public transport
  • Great team events, such as our well-known FELFEL Summer Outings
  • Stay moving and healthy with our weekly sports classes

Does that sound like the perfect job for you? We can’t wait to know more about you! EMPLOY NOW with your complete documents in German or English.
