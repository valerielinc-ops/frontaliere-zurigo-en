Full description

80-100%, Winterthur / Smart Working Fixed-term employment from 1 January 2027 to the end of 2028

Shape your future – together with us!

In the Interaction & Multi-Experience Solutions (IMS) team, we are shaping the digital future of AXA Switzerland. Our interaction solutions support customers and employees on all digital channels, from www.axa.ch to the myAXA customer portal (web and app) to our chatbot Ada.

An equally important component are our form solutions based on the Adobe Experience Manager. In order to create innovative solutions for our customers and strengthen our team, we are looking for you as a talented and motivated AEM Forms Software Engineer with Java expertise.

You can expect an exciting area of responsibility with a lot of creative freedom. Bring your commitment, courage and openness to new things to our collegial team and grow with us with new challenges. We look forward to meeting you: "Know you can."

Around 600 software and system developers work in the Data, Technology & Innovation division throughout Switzerland and develop the technology of tomorrow in-house. In doing so, we also collaborate in global teams and networks.

What to expect

  • You will design and develop sophisticated adaptive forms with AEM Forms and thus play a key role in shaping our digital customer solutions
  • You will be responsible for the AI-supported further development and maintenance of our central AEM platform and connect backend services to ensure a smooth flow of data
  • You will ensure quality through modern development practices and automated processes (CI/CD)
  • You will take responsibility throughout the entire development process, actively contribute to our agile team and share your knowledge with your colleagues

What you bring to the table

  • You have in-depth experience in developing with the Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) stack, especially with AEM Forms, OSGi, Sling and JCR
  • You are well-versed in Java fullstack development and feel at home in both the backend and the frontend. Ideally, you also have knowledge of modern frontend technologies (e.g. React)
  • The use of tools such as Maven, Git as well as CI/CD pipelines and AI tools such as Copilot etc. are a matter of course for you
  • You are characterized by an independent, solution-oriented way of working, very good analytical skills and a passion for solving technical challenges in a team
  • DevOps and agile methods are not foreign concepts to you and working in a self-organized team appeals to you
  • You have a very good knowledge of German. Good English skills are a plus for international cooperation

What we offer you We are convinced that satisfied employees perform at their best. That's why we invest in benefits that offer tangible benefits – beyond the usual.

Flexibility and freedom: Smart Working (flexible working model) annual working hours, 5 weeks of vacation (6 weeks from the age of 50), vacation gift 2 weeks every 5 years, vacation purchase up to 10 additional days/per year

Job and family: Family-friendly meeting times, 22 weeks of maternity leave, 8 weeks of leave for the other parent, crèche subsidies, company family allowances, days off for visiting relatives and in challenging, personal situations

Pension and insurance: Attractive pension fund with extensive extra-mandatory benefits including optional savings options, up to 30% discount on insurance products

Flexible benefits: AXA Shareplan (employee participation), CHF 500 per year on the Swibeco benefits platform, discounted lunch or lunch card, smart working flat rate CHF 200 per year

Mobility: Half-Fare travelcard refund, Reka Pay up to CHF 1,000 per year (20% discount), fleet discount, free e-charging station, AXA rental bikes

Healthy balance in everyday working life: Free check-ups, annual health weeks, a wide range of exercise offers (online/offline), massage offers, relaxation rooms, employee care, online health portal, various seminars/events on health prevention and mental health

Development: 3 days of individual learning time, free coaching, wide range of learning and development opportunities, financial participation in further training, promotion of internal mobility, talent development offers, seminars for employees 55+ (AXA Horizon), involvement in communities on various topics, participation in innovation projects (Innoday, AXA Hackathon, UnConference), 2 days for volunteer assignments (Environment & Social Issues)
