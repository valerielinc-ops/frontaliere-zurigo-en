Full description

You are an experienced, committed personality in the construction industry who wants to shape with drive, vision and real impact? We are looking for active support for our real estate department immediately or by agreement.

Tasks

  • Technical inspection of protective compartment envelopes, armoured doors, emergency exits and ventilation units. Functional check of pressure relief valves and explosion protection valves
  • Checking the operational readiness of the shelter equipment (e.g. dry toilets, beds) and acceptance and approval of shelters created
  • Appointment and coordination with homeowners and property administrations
  • Preparation of defect protocols, legal request for correction periods and various other administrative activities

Profile

  • Completed craftsmanship-technical apprenticeship (EFZ in the construction or secondary sector)
  • Completed certificate course as a shelter controller (BABS/canton) or the willingness to complete it in a timely manner
  • Good IT skills and stylish German in word and writing
  • High social competence in dealing with the population, assertiveness and conflict capability
  • Strong coordination ability, secure appearance and balanced personality, high level of initiative and strong customer orientation

We offer

  • Demanding and versatile work in a smaller team with a long-term perspective
  • Close cooperation and professional exchange with municipal and cantonal authorities
  • Centrally located workplace in Zurich as well as the possibility of mobile office
  • Progressive employment conditions with annual working hours, attractive additional benefits (e.g. lunch checks, mobility contribution) and a solid pension fund
  • A wide range of internal training and further education programs offered by the City of Zurich as well as support for external training and further education

About us

Protection & Rescue Zurich (SRZ), as a service department of the Department of Security, unites the professional and militia fire brigades, the medical department and the operational control center (ELZ) for the emergency calls 144 and 118. In addition, civil protection, fire police and a training center with an affiliated Higher School for Rescue Occupations (HFRB) belong to the largest civil rescue organization in Switzerland. Intervention and prevention units protect and save people, animals, property and the environment around the clock in the city of Zurich, at Zurich Airport and in the agglomeration.
