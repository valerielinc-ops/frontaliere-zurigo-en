  • Providing friendly, prompt, and clean service of food and beverages, as well as attentive and friendly guest service.

  • A permanent position that requires independence and personal responsibility
  • You can expect a versatile role in a very lively and structured environment, progressive and dynamic employee policies, and the benefits of an international hotel group.
