Full description

The Medical Polyclinic is the regional interdisciplinary investigation centre for unclear complex clinical pictures. The treatment of assigned outpatients requires knowledge of the entire spectrum of internal medicine. The focus is on close interdisciplinary cooperation with all disciplines of the KSW and the family doctors of the region. In addition to focuses on inflammatory and autoimmune diseases, we also have special consultation hours such as hypertension, osteology and vaccination consultation hours incl. established travel advice

  • This includes sonography and puncture. In addition, the personnel medical service for the KSW and external institutions is integrated into the medical polyclinic.

Tasks

  • Supervision, supervision and further training of internist assistant doctors
  • conduct an own consultation hour, if necessary Establishment of own special consultation hours
  • Collaboration in the personnel and trust medical service of the KSW and other health institutions (ZHAW, ZAG, age centers city of Winterthur, etc.)
  • Engagement in training events and student courses
  • Participation in various projects, including the establishment of an intensified cooperation with the emergency center and inpatient internal medicine (e.g. post-stationary heart failure consultation)

Profile

  • FMH General Internal Medicine or equivalent training, knowledge of the Swiss health system
  • Clinical experience in outpatient internal medicine
  • Internist cadre experience ambulatory or inpatient
  • Sonography knowledge and experience in punctures
  • interest in the broad spectrum of general internal medicine and interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Joy of internistic detective work
  • High social competence and team and communication skills as well as enjoyment of the education and training of young doctors
