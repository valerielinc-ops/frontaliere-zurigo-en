Full description

As part of the zKIS/EPIC program, you are actively involved in the introduction of a new clinic information system at the University Hospital Zurich. The project is one of the central digitalization initiatives of the USZ and helps to standardize clinical processes, improve cooperation and sustainably support the everyday work of employees.
A dynamic environment with high impact awaits you, in which you actively participate, take on responsibility and together with an interdisciplinary team realize a future-oriented solution for the USZ.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • You take responsibility for the goal achievement of an entire topic within the Epic program • You coordinate all activities in the team as well as cooperation with other teams and interfaces • You plan and control the tasks within your area of responsibility and ensure their implementation • You actively support your team in everyday work, remove obstacles and promote cooperation • You lead your team in the sense of a "primus inter pares" and work through example and commitment • Together with the project management, you ensure that challenges such as resource bottlenecks or failures are solved Requirements • You have professional experience in the hospital environment and/or in Epic and are able to take on professional leadership. This means that you are familiar with at least one major hospital process and can also take on topic leadership in this area. • You have leadership experience or the potential to lead a team and create a structured work environment • You work independently, take responsibility and actively support others in finding solutions (coaching approach) • You are able to manage and accompany complex and demanding project situations in the long term • You communicate clearly, enjoy working in a team and move safely in an interdisciplinary environment • You are resilient, solution-oriented and can keep an overview even in dynamic project phases We offer: You will be given the opportunity to develop professionally and personally in a complex transformation project and take on responsibility with direct effect on the digital change in everyday hospital life. You can expect a dedicated team working together on a future-oriented solution for the USZ.
