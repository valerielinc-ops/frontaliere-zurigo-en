Full description

reCOVER Swiss Olympic Approved Sports Clinic consists of a dynamic and interdisciplinary team of sports doctors, accident surgeons, sports physiotherapists and sports acupuncturists/manual therapists. We are specialized in the treatment of sports injuries and serve many regional sports clubs up to Nationalliga-A teams. Our sports clinic with modern treatment rooms is integrated in the Holmes Place Premium Fitness Club in Seepark Oberrieden, where we use the entire facility directly on the lake. To expand our team, we will be looking for a *n Dipl from 1 November 2026 or by appointment. Sports Physiotherapist - POLICLINIC 80-100%

Requirement profile

  • Dipl. Physical therapist FH or with SRK recognition
  • Advantageous training in sports physiotherapy
  • High intrinsic motivation, fast comprehension and willingness to learn
  • Teamwork and enthusiasm for interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Good knowledge of German (verbal & written) and English (verbal)

Your tasks include

  • Working independently as a sports/physiotherapist in the ambulatory sector
  • Commitment to the further development of quality standards
  • Assignments at regional sports clubs

What do we offer you?

  • Attractive place to work at Holmes Place Premium Fitness Club right on the lake
  • Own therapy rooms and use of the entire fitness facility
  • Modern working conditions and training opportunities
  • Weekly interdisciplinary training and case discussion with experienced sports physiotherapists, sports doctors, accident surgeons and sports acupuncturists
  • Motivated patients with high goals
  • Cooperation with regional sports clubs
