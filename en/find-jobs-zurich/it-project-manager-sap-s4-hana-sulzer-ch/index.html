We are looking for an experienced IT Project Manager SAP S/4HANA to join our Business Transformation Program and play a key role in one of Sulzer's most strategic global initiatives. In this position, you will help shape and drive the design, implementation, and rollout of Sulzer's global SAP S/4HANA template. Working closely with program leadership, business stakeholders, and implementation partners, you will provide expert guidance, governance, and quality assurance to ensure successful project delivery and long-term business value. This is an exciting opportunity for a seasoned SAP transformation professional who enjoys operating at a strategic level while influencing solution design, governance, and delivery across a complex international environment.

  • Provide expert advice and recommendations to program leadership and key stakeholders.
  • Facilitate collaboration between business teams, IT functions, SAP specialists, and implementation partners.

  • Drive continuous improvement by introducing lessons learned and proven practices from large transformation programs.
  • Support knowledge transfer and capability development across the organization.
  • Promote a collaborative One Sulzer culture focused on teamwork, customer value, and continuous improvement.
