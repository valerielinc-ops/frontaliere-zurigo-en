Full description

To strengthen our concrete industry team in German-speaking Switzerland, we are looking for a proactive, customer-oriented individual for the position of:

Technical Sales Consultant, Concrete Industry, 100%

YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Managing and further developing existing customers in the ready-mix concrete, precast concrete and concrete products industries
  • Acquiring new customers and building long-term customer relationships in the Swiss concrete industry
  • Providing technical advice and selling concrete admixtures, fibers, pigments and decorative concrete solutions
  • Developing customer- and project-specific solutions in collaboration with Product Management and Research & Development
  • Conducting product training sessions, customer seminars, trials and presentations
  • Monitoring the market and competition and identifying new business opportunities
  • Supporting product launches and actively contributing to the further development of Concrete - Concrete industry - Businesses in Switzerland

YOUR PROFILE

  • Completed training as a concrete technologist, construction materials tester or civil engineer, or a comparable qualification, with work experience in the concrete industry
  • Several years of experience in the ready-mix concrete, precast concrete and/or concrete products industries
  • A proven track record in technical sales, customer consulting or business development
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss concrete and building materials sector, as well as a strong technical understanding
  • Entrepreneurial, independent and customer-oriented working style with a persuasive presence
  • Very good German language skills; knowledge of French and/or English is an advantage
  • Willingness to travel within Switzerland

YOUR PROSPECTS

  • A position of responsibility with considerable scope for shaping the role in an international company focused on Switzerland
  • Technically sophisticated and innovative products, as well as direct contact with decision-makers in the concrete industry
  • A corporate culture that leaves room for personal responsibility, collaboration and entrepreneurial ideas
  • An environment where openness and appreciation are a matter of course
  • Attractive employment conditions with above-average social benefits, including a company vehicle, as well as ongoing education and training
