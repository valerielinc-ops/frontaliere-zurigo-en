Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With more than 20 locations – including the Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli private clinics, as well as Clienia Bergheim's gerontopsychiatric services – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in outpatient, day-clinic and inpatient settings. The group employs around 1800 people and generated revenue of CHF 240 Mio. in 2025.

For our D0 ward, Psychotherapy Ward for Depression 50+, we are looking for, starting February 1, 2027, or by agreement, a

The D0 ward specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of depressive disorders from the age of 50 onward. The interprofessionally coordinated treatment is based on the CBASP and ACT therapy concepts. The range of therapies is supplemented by complementary medicine services, physical activities and relaxation. The atmosphere on the ward is characterized by a respectful attitude and the careful cultivation of interpersonal relationships.

Your responsibilities

  • individual nursing care and support of patients and contact with relatives as part of key-person work and in consultation with the core team
  • responsibility for nursing development and reviewing the quality of nursing care on the ward
  • Involvement in specialist working groups and projects
  • Ensuring cooperative interdisciplinary collaboration

Your profile For this exciting position, you have a nursing qualification at HF / FH level and professional experience in psychiatric nursing. You are also interested in completing a CAS in geriatric psychiatry. Are you also an open, communicative and engaged person and willing to pursue continuous professional development and pass this knowledge on to the nursing team?

Then you are the person we are looking for!

What we offer

  • An exciting position in a top team that is looking forward to welcoming you
  • Innovative employer with strong interprofessional collaboration
  • Very good development opportunities with generous support for continuing education and training
  • Various discounts (e.g. on Reka-Checks, health-promoting programs, selected leisure activities and online shops)
  • 5 weeks of vacation, starting from 50. Year of age 27 days and from the 60. Age 32 days
  • Excellent dining options at very fair prices
  • ZVV Bonuspass long-service gift from the 5th year of employment

For further information, please contact our ward manager, Melina Rolli, Tel. +41 44 929 86 07, or the Head of Nursing, Melanie Heer, Tel. +41 44 929 83 82.
