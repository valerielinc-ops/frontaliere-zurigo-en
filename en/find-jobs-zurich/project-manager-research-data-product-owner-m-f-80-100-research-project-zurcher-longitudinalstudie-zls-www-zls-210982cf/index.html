Your tasks

  • As project manager / product owner, you take on the professional responsibility for our research database and combine research, data management and technical implementation.
  • You develop data model, requirements, terminologies, mappings and metadata.
  • You ensure the quality, harmonization, validation and longitudinal consistency of the data.
  • You control data mappings – manually and AI-supported – and prepare data requests and exports.
  • The responsibility of data governance, documentation, data protection and data security is also one of your tasks.
  • You test new solutions and take them technically.
  • You prioritize requirements, coordinate development and control the project.
  • You work closely with internal and external partners and support researchers in the use of data.
  • By networking with the Centre for Longitudinal Studies (CLS), you will work closely with them internationally. Your profile
  • Master’s degree in psychology or a related discipline, ideally related to quantitative research, data management or health/social sciences
  • Experience in the management of research, health or survey data, preferably in longitudinal data sets or registers
  • Strong affinity for data, statistics and digital technologies
  • Experience or great interest in data validation, harmonization, terminologies and mappings as well as an understanding of the structured preparation and documentation of research data
  • Ability to clearly formulate technical requirements and, together with technical experts, translate them into practical solutions
