Reference number: 72127

As Head of Medical Documentation, you shape the quality and efficiency of our documentation processes and lead your team with expertise, a sense of responsibility and an eye for continuous development.

YOUR TASKS

  • Professional and personnel management of the medical documentation team
  • Ensuring full and timely filing of patient medical documentation
  • Monitoring of the receipt of reports and coordination of dunning and follow-up of outstanding reports
  • Preparation of reporting, statistics and analyses for management (e.g. center evaluations, minimum case numbers of operators, process status)
  • Continuous optimization of processes as well as further development of evaluations and control instruments (e.g. liquidity-relevant high-cost cases)
  • Contact person for internal departments for questions about medical documentation
  • Collaboration in cross-sectoral projects

Your profile

  • Basic business education or basic medical education with a corresponding continuing education portfolio in business administration. Ideally complemented by experience in health and hospital management.
  • Health economics knowledge of hospital structures and processes as well as relevant regulatory requirements (e.g. SwissDRG, SPLG)
  • Experience with MS Office, SAP and MCC; Knowledge of Access, VBA or TIP HCe is an advantage
  • Analytical, conceptual and solution-oriented working methods as well as strong communication and organisational skills
  • Service-oriented appearance and pleasure in cooperation in an interdisciplinary environment
  • German as a mother tongue or reliable knowledge of German, English and other languages are advantageous
