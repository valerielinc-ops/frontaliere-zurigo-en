Full description

For a modern, fast-growing company in the healthcare industry, we are looking for an analytical and effective personality as a Group Accountant. In this challenging key function, you advance the harmonization of group processes and accompany strategic special projects. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for you!

This is your job

  • Consolidated financial statements: Support in the preparation of the consolidated monthly, quarterly and annual financial statements according to IFRS and HGB with their own areas of responsibility.
  • Process optimization: Analysis, standardization and continuous optimization of operational accounting processes, accounting logics and guidelines within the Group.
  • Interface & Audit: Central contact for auditors as part of preliminary and main audits as well as monitoring of local audit progress.
  • Special projects: Accompaniment and coordination of strategic projects such as the integration of new companies (M&A), system changes or the introduction of new IFRS standards in coordination with stakeholders.

This is what you bring with you

  • Training: Successfully completed business studies with a focus on accounting/accounting or a comparable qualification (e.g. professional card).
  • Expertise: Very good, in-depth knowledge of the accounting standards according to IFRS and ideally HGB.
  • Project skills: Pronounced project management skills as well as the competence to independently advance topics and coordinate them with different specialist areas.
  • Working methods: Analytical-conceptual thinking skills, a structured and self-responsible working method as well as communication strength and assertiveness.
  • Languages: Very good knowledge of German in words and writing.

This is what awaits you from the customer

  • Growth & dynamism: A responsible and demanding activity with excellent development opportunities in an expanding company.
  • Work climate: versatile challenges in exciting, forward-looking projects within a pleasant and highly professional work atmosphere.
  • Design leeway: The opportunity to actively shape and sustainably shape group guidelines and best practices.
