Full description

Introduction

As a Systems Architect, you will shape and lead scalable systems architectures that enable long-term product growth and cross-domain integration. You act as a key technical authority, translating business and system requirements into robust architectural solutions while guiding teams and stakeholders across the organization.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead and coordinate the design of scalable systems architectures across products and projects.
  • Identify architectural needs, define solution options, and drive informed architectural decisions.
  • Act as the main contact for cross-domain architectural challenges (hardware, software, networks).
  • Ensure alignment with architectural principles, standards, and strategic objectives.
  • Translate requirements into architecture artifacts (drivers, constraints, quality attributes).
  • Define and govern standardized interfaces for seamless system integration.
  • Provide technical leadership through mentoring, workshops, and architectural guidance.
  • Document, communicate, and continuously improve architectural decisions and methodologies.

Required Expertise

  • Engineering degree (e.g. Electrical Engineering, Systems Engineering, Mechatronics, Robotics).
  • Several years of experience in systems architecture for embedded devices.
  • Strong expertise in hardware and cross-domain integration.
  • Experience with architectural methods, MBSE tools, and systems engineering practices.
  • Proven ability to lead technical decisions and align interdisciplinary stakeholders.
  • Experience in technical leadership, mentoring, and cross-functional collaboration.
  • Familiarity with architecture governance, standards, and continuous improvement.
  • Excellent English skills, strong communication and presentation abilities, and a holistic mindset.

About Belimo

Belimo is the global market leader in the development, production, and sales of field devices for the energy-efficient control of heating, ventilation and air-conditioning systems. The focus of our core business is on damper actuators, control valves, sensors and meters. Founded in 1975, the company has been listed on the Swiss Exchange (SIX) since 1995.
