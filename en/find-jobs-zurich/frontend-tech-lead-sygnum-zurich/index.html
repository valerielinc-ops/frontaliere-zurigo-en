Original description

At the Portal team, we’re the heart of Sygnum’s client-facing experience, and our work directly impacts how users interact with their digital assets. Our mission is to create state-of-the-art UI/UX solutions that not only meet but exceed client expectations, keeping their needs at the center of everything we do. We collaborate closely with Custody, Trading, Tokenization, and Banking Operations teams to ensure our applications are robust, secure, and cutting-edge. But we’re more than just colleagues, we’re a vibrant community that values creativity, innovation, and fun. We believe a supportive and enjoyable work environment fosters collaboration, trust, and proactive problem-solving. Continuous improvement is in our DNA; we rely on data-driven insights to refine our approaches and deliver excellence. Joining us means being part of a team that’s as committed to quality and user experience as it is to create a workplace you’ll love coming to every day. As a tech lead, you are comfortable wearing two hats: tech and management. From a technical perspective, you will: • Lead a team of software engineers to develop and own state-of-the-art solutions (front and back end). • Constantly enhance quality and security of the applications. • Collaborate with Design to improve our client centric products. • Contribute to writing and reviewing high quality and secured code covered by tests. • Work with SRE engineers and solution architects to improve the availability, resilience, and observability of the applications. From a managerial perspective, you will: • Coach and mentor software engineers in the team. • Improve the efficiency and effectiveness of the team. • Partner with product managers and stakeholders to plan new features and improvements. • Drive blameless retros and post-mortems that help the team learn from mistakes. • Promote a psychologically safe space where everyone feels they can contribute and improve things. • Fluency in JavaScript, TypeScript, React and Nestjs.
