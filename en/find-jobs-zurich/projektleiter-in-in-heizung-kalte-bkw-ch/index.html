Full description

Do you want to accompany challenging projects in the field of heating and cooling systems from the idea to the realization? Then you are exactly right with us. Well located, near the station Stettbach, we offer you a modern workplace so that you can contribute your knowledge.

Your tasks

  • Support of the project team in exciting construction projects in the field of heating and cooling systems
  • Independent implementation of planning services from concept development to planning and implementation
  • Competent contact person for customers and the planning team in technical and economic matters
  • As a link to the various trades in construction, you feel safe in dealing with and communicating with the project participants

Your competence

  • You have basic technical training and further education as a technician: in HF or engineer: in FH Building Technology (HLKK)
  • In the project planning and implementation of demanding heating and cooling systems, you can have several years of professional experience
  • You are used to dealing with 3D-based CAD systems
  • Even in the dynamic building environment, you show that you are a team player and resilient
