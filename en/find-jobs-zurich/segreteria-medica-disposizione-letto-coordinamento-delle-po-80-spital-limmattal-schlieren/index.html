Diversity, responsibility and networking – all in one place. Do you want a varied and meaningful working day? In this exciting function, you combine the activities of a medical assistant or physician secretary with tasks of surgical coordination. Through your work at the interface between patient, team and organization, you make a central contribution to the smooth running of the hospital operation. Thanks to the combination of medical care, administrative tasks and operational planning You not only incorporate your expertise, but also experience new challenges every day in an interdisciplinary environment. If you enjoy working independently, like to keep an overview and are looking for a job with effect, then you are exactly right with us.
