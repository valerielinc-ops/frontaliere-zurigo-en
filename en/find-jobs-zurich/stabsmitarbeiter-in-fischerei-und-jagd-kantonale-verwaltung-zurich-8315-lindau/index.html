Full description

Are you committed to the protection and sustainable use of our fish and wildlife stocks? Does it appeal to you to work on challenging questions at the interface of nature, society and law? Do you want to use your expertise and experience in project management to help develop viable solutions in an exciting and versatile area of responsibility? Then you are in the right place with the fishing and hunting administration of the Canton of Zurich! Your tasks

  • You develop the basis for decision-making in legal and political matters and take over their coordination.
  • You plan, lead and accompany projects, especially in the area of data management systems for fishing and hunting.
  • You coordinate and handle general administrative business and ensure smooth processes.
  • You develop directives, opinions, co-reports and regulations in cooperation with the responsible specialist departments and support the department management in project-related tasks.
  • If appropriate, the assumption of personnel management responsibility is conceivable. Your profile
  • You bring experience in project management and drive ambitious projects forward in a targeted and solution-oriented manner.
  • You have a good understanding of administrative processes, political processes and legal issues, ideally in the public sector.
  • You think strategically and networked, analyze complex issues and prepare them appropriately.
  • You work independently, enjoy taking on responsibility and have strong organizational and coordination skills.
  • You are service-oriented, communicative and capable of teamwork and enjoy interdisciplinary cooperation as well as interest in the topics of fishing, hunting and nature conservation You expect an independent and responsible activity with a lot of room for manoeuvre. You work in a small, motivated team with flat hierarchies and short decision paths. You benefit from an open, uncomplicated work culture and an appreciative cooperation. You can contribute your strengths and ideas and develop professionally and personally.
