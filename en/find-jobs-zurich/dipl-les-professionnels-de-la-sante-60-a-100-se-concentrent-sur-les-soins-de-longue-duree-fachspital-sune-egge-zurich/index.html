Full description

Our specialist hospital for social medicine and addiction diseases Sune-Egge offers inpatient and outpatient treatment for patients with combined somatic, psychiatric and addiction diseases. For them, we have 22 acute care beds and 20 long-term care beds and work closely with public hospitals, doctors, local specialists and addiction support institutions.

  • Competent care: You take over the holistic care and care of our residents.
  • Daily responsibility: You control the care process and keep an overview even on intensive days.
  • Care documentation: You record the care needs precisely (RAI knowledge is a big plus).
  • Reference care: You are a permanent contact person for residents, relatives and doctors.
  • Education: Diploma as nurse HF / DN II / AKP or an equivalent, EDK-approved education.
  • Experience: Sound professional experience in Swiss long-term care.
  • Expertise: Ideally, you are already familiar with the RAI system (no need – we are happy to work with you).
  • Personality: You are an empathetic, team capable and responsible personality with humor.
