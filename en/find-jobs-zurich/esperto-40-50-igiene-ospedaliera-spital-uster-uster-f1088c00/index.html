Exciting challenges await you

  • You are an important contact person for the employees of all teams and disciplines of the hospital for topics of hospital hygiene and infection prevention

  • support the implementation of standard hygiene measures and targeted surveillance and prevention strategies
  • you plan and carry out on-site audits and accompany the derived improvement measures

  • provide support in the event of outbreaks, isolation and crisis management

What sets you apart for the job

  • Certified nurse HF/FH and several years of professional experience in the acute hospital
  • Training as an expert in infection prevention in health care with a federal diploma or the willingness and interest to complete it
  • independent and absolutely reliable working method
  • administrative and IT user knowledge
  • You have communication skills and can respond to different hierarchical levels, cultures and social differences
