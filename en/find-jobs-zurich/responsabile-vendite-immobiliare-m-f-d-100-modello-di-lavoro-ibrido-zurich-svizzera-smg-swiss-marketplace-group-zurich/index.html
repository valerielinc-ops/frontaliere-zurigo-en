Full description

Your mission: As Head of Sales Real Estate, you take overall responsibility for the B2B real estate sales business. You continue to develop our sales organization strategically and operationally, create the conditions for sustainable revenue growth and strengthen our proximity to the market and our customers. With your entrepreneurial thinking, your pronounced sales excellence mentality and leadership skills, you develop a powerful team. You make sure we achieve our sales, customer and growth goals.

You'll be concrete

  • Design, implement and continuously optimise the B2B sales strategy and the Go-to-Market approach for real estate.
  • The sales, ARPA and customer number goals are responsible and market and competition developments translate into concrete sales initiatives.
  • Our customer segmentation and segment specific market processing regularly analyze and sharpen specifically.
  • Field Sales, Key Account Management, Sales Operations and the INA Sales Team lead, strengthen and strengthen the offices.
  • Promote an ambitious, customer-oriented and cross-team sales culture and drive sales excellence through appropriate structures, processes, methods, reporting and incentive models.
  • Control sales consistently via relevant KPIs, bulges and regular review meetings and initiate effective measures in case of deviations.
  • Expand strategic key account management and build sustainable relationships with key customers, potential customers and partners.
  • represent SMG Real Estate at customer events as well as with industry organisations and relevant partners, such as the SMIT.
  • Bringing the market perspective into the cooperation with product, marketing, monetization and customer care and pushing forward central initiatives across the various sectors.
  • As a member of the B2B Leadership Team, prepare relevant sales developments transparently and represent them in the Real Estate Leadership Team.

Skills

You bring this with you

  • Multi-year successful management experience in B2B sales, ideally in a digital, technology-driven environment.
  • Experience in leading and developing sales teams, especially in field sales, inside sales and key account management.
  • Well-founded experience in the development of Go-to-Market strategies as well as in the control of sales, pipeline, and sales indicators.
  • entrepreneurship, a strong focus on results and the ability to set clear priorities.
  • A motivating, binding leadership style and the joy of empowering teams to achieve maximum performance.
  • Analytic and conceptual strength as well as a convincing presence towards customers, partners and internal stakeholders.
  • Understanding digital business models and modern sales approaches; knowledge of product management, OKRs or the way in which the company works is a plus.
  • Negotiable German and very good English skills
  • French is an advantage.
  • willingness to travel regularly within Switzerland.
