Full description

Spital Männedorf – Your very special place of work. Whether a small “thank you” or a morning “grüezi” – we focus on real appreciation and humanity. The proximity in our house makes everyday life uncomplicated and enables interprofessional cooperation at the highest level. In an environment where support goes without saying, your commitment is appreciated and encouraged. The nature of your activity:

  • Coordinating admissions, relocations and bed allocation for regular and emergency admissions
  • Planning of operations taking into account the available surgical resources and the specifications of the medical service
  • Written and oral presentation of patients
  • Contact person for patients before admission (for questions about admission, medical concerns or hospital stay)
  • Ensuring the flow of information concerning entrances, exits and relocations
  • Close cooperation with operators, MPAs, nursing staff and other internal and external bodies
  • Participation in integral capacity management The basis of your performance:
  • Health education or commercial education with professional experience in health care
  • A high degree of reliability, especially in cooperation within the team and in dealing with our customers: inside and patients: inside.
  • Flexibility regarding working days (no fixed working days, assumption of holiday representatives)
  • Independent, committed and transparent working methods as well as high flexibility and resilience in hectic situations
  • Secure and self-confident appearance as well as excellent communication skills (stylish knowledge of German in word and writing)
  • Solution-oriented thinking, diplomatic skills, assertiveness and strong organisational talent
  • IT affinity as well as high service and customer orientation The advantages of your position:
  • Regulated working hours, Monday to Friday, max. until 17:00
  • Train station 3 minutes away
  • Service and holiday planning very flexible The reasons for your decision: We offer modern working conditions, promote internal training and focus on professional exchange across all disciplines. The quiet location directly on the lake and the proximity to the vibrant city of Zurich round off your workplace. In addition, you can look forward to very special advantages.
