Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With more than 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric services of Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in outpatient, day-clinic and inpatient settings. The Group employs around 1800 people and generated revenue of CHF 240 Mio. in 2025.

For our D0 ward, Psychotherapy Ward for Depression 50+, we are looking for, starting February 1, 2027, or by agreement, a

Ward D0 specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of depressive disorders from the age of 50 onwards. Most patients on this ward are between 50 - 65 years of age and require inpatient treatment. The interprofessionally coordinated treatment is based on the therapeutic concepts CBASP and ACT. The range of therapies is supplemented by complementary medical services, physical activities and relaxation. The atmosphere on the ward is characterized by a respectful attitude and the careful cultivation of interpersonal relationships.

Your responsibilities

  • the individual care and support of patients and contact with relatives as part of the primary nursing role and in consultation with the core team
  • Leading and co-leading psychotherapeutic group programs and handovers
  • the coordination and planning of treatment within the interprofessional team
  • supporting patients in the activities of daily living and accompanying them in crisis situations
  • as we work with permanent night staff, you will primarily work a two-shift system (early and late shift)

Your profile For this exciting role, in addition to a qualification as a nursing professional HF/FH, you bring a high level of personal competence and customer orientation and adapt well to changing situations. You should enjoy interprofessional collaboration and recovery-oriented treatment, and they should shape your working day. Ideally, you have experience in caring for people with psychiatric illnesses. If you identify with this and would like to become part of Clienia Schlössli, you are the person we are looking for!

Our offer

  • An exciting position in a top team that is looking forward to welcoming you
  • Innovative employer with strong interprofessional collaboration
  • Appreciative and friendly clinic culture
  • Very good development opportunities with generous support for further training and continuing education
  • Various discounts (e.g. with Reka-Checks, health-promoting offers, selected leisure activities and online shops, ZVV Bonuspass)
  • 5 weeks of vacation, from 50. Year of age 27 days and from the 60. Age 32 days
  • Excellent catering options at very fair prices
  • Long-service gift after just 5 years
