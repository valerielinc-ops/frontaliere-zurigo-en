Full description

Tasks

  • You are responsible for the care process of the patients assigned to you, in the field of epileptology, based on the care models applied in the Lengg Clinic
  • They work together with the health professionals as well as our students, learners and interns.
  • They care for and care for our patients competently in an interprofessional treatment team consisting of therapists, doctors and employees of the hotel industry
  • Accompanying, advising and supporting the relatives of our patients completes the area of responsibility

Requirements

  • You have successfully completed training as a Dipl. Nurse / Nurse specialist HF/FH
  • First professional experience in epileptology and in dealing with severely multiple impaired patients: inside are an advantage
  • They excel as a resilient, communicative and open personality and enjoy working in a team
  • They demonstrate a high level of social competence in dealing with patients and their relatives
  • Good organizational talent and enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation round off their profile

We offer

  • Choose between FIX, FLEX or SUPERFLEX the working model that suits you best. Flex contracts offer you the convenience of a fixed employment contract and additional attractive advantages in the form of flexible work assignments (pilot project from 1 June 2025).
  • Further models with only early service or late service as well as the working model with, without or only night shift have been established with us for a long time.

Klinik Lengg AG in Zurich is the centre of excellence for epileptology and neurological rehabilitation.

It is our aim to provide patients with neurological diseases with the widest possible rehabilitation and to ensure an accurate diagnosis and sustainable treatment of patients with epilepsy. Around 300 employees are committed to humanely, competently, innovatively and reliably ensuring that our patients regain the greatest possible independence - and thus their quality of life. Become part of one of our interdisciplinary and interprofessional teams.
