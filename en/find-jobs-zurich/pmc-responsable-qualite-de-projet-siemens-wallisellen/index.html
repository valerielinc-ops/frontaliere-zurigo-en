Full description

Create a better #TomorrowWithUs Want to help shape the future of the railway world together with us? At Siemens Mobility, we are the leading provider of mobility and are constantly expanding our innovative portfolio, which includes rail vehicles, rail automation, electrification solutions, complete rail systems and accompanying services. With around 800 specialist employees in Switzerland and 39 600 employees worldwide, we shape mobility with passion and are one step ahead of our time. Do you burn for project management, think in a networked way and want to actively develop structures, processes and quality standards? In this role, you act as a central hub for projects, methods and stakeholders – and make a real contribution with your experience. With a lot of design freedom and high visibility You have a direct added value for organization, projects and cooperation. Your new tasks

  • You are the central point of contact for project management topics, coordinate issues from the network and promote the active exchange of experience (e.g. national and international communities)
  • You coach and advise project managers and commercial project managers on complex project situations and the definition of the project setup
  • You will accompany challenging project constellations and support you with strategic and commercial issues incl. Milestone planning
  • You develop and implement process standards and best practices and drive the further development of the integrated management system
  • You analyze projects based on KPIs and quality criteria, derive improvement measures and ensure effective reporting
  • You work on tools, methods and process optimizations (e.g. PALM, PM@Mobility, NC management) and use synergies across interfaces
  • You moderate workshops and training courses and actively participate in quality committees (e.g. RQMS board) and you represent the interests of the Rail Infrastructure Project Execution
  • You have profound and many years of experience incl. Certification in project management (at least IPMA C) in project management, ideally in the (rail) infrastructure sector or plant construction
  • Your heart beats for process and quality topics as well as a deep understanding of project management and complex project landscapes
  • You bring a higher education from the technical environment (bachelor, master) and combine it with meaningful further education in project management and / or the field of quality
  • You work independently, like creative leeway, recognize the need for action at an early stage and proactively advance topics
  • You enjoy developing and implementing processes as well as continuous improvement
  • You are communicative, moderate and moving Secure in a broad stakeholder environment You benefit from
  • an open and flexible working culture at one of the leading companies in the field of mobility in Switzerland
  • a 40-hour week on a 100% fee
  • at least 5 weeks of holidays
  • a workplace at the pulse of railway technology
  • Various employee benefits and training opportunities
  • a hybrid working model: balanced work-life balance and more autonomy Our team and location You want information about our culture, our values, diversity and other important topics?
