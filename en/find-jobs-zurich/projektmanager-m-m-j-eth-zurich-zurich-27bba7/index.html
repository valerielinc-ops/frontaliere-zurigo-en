Industry Relations within the area of Vizepresidium for Knowledge Transfer and Economic Relations (VPWW) supports strategic partnerships between ETH Zurich and industry, strengthens the exchange between science and industry and positions ETH Zurich as a leading innovation partner in the international environment.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a strong, strategic thinking and communicative personality as:

  • Building, maintaining and developing strategic relationships with industrial partners, innovation actors and relevant stakeholders
