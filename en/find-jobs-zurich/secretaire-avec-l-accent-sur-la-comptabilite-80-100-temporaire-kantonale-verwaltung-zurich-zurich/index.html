60 - 80% "Our central task is to protect the personal data and thus the privacy of the residents of the Canton of Zurich." This is what the Data Protection Officer stands for as an independent supervisory authority. It supervises the data processing of the cantonal administration, the municipalities and the other authorities and public institutions in the canton of Zurich. It advises public bodies with its team, carries out controls and informs the population about data protection issues and privacy issues. For our secretariat we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by appointment an accomplished, responsible and team-oriented personality who likes to tackle, think and keep track. As a secretary, you are the central hub of our authority and contact point for internal and external contacts. With your organizational talent, you support the data protection officer and their team in all administrative matters. Their tasks

  • They manage our secretariat independently and ensure that our administrative processes function reliably and efficiently.
  • They perform the various administrative tasks of a modern secretariat. You are the contact person, organizer and coordinator in one person.
  • They participate in projects and take on organizational and administrative tasks in the areas of personnel and finance.
  • They coordinate our internal ordering and manage consumables, furniture and other resources.
  • They maintain the manual of our quality management system and keep address and other data assets up-to-date.
  • They organize internal events and ensure that everything is professionally prepared and works smoothly. Your profile
  • They have completed commercial training and bring several years of professional experience in a comparable position.
  • A sovereign and addressable communication in word and writing as well as very good MS-Office knowledge are among your strengths.
  • You enjoy a central support function and a wide range of tasks around organization, infrastructure, finance and personnel.
  • They think networked, are loyal and discreet and are characterized by an exact and very well-structured way of working. Our offer
