Full description

Location : Zurich Work Pattern Percentage : 80-100% Join Deloitte Switzerland’s Third-Party Advisory & Technology Implementation team and empower organisations through technology‑enabled transformation journeys In a world that is constantly driven by emerging risks and technological advances, organisations need to adapt quickly to remain competitive in the market. The Third Party Advisory and Implementation team assists in identifying, evaluating and mitigating risks associated with external business relationships through advisory, implementation and operate services. We focus on enhancing the maturity of our client’s third party Management (TPM) processes, covering both third party risk management (TPRM) and contract lifecycle management (CLM) activities. This includes setting up Third Party Management frameworks to deploying technologies to manage third party relationships. The Third Party Advisory team drives the growth of TPRM and CLM technology implementations across industries by building strong relationships with leading vendors and combining deep product knowledge with market leading TPRM and CLM strategy and design expertise. How you can make an impact We have a new and exciting opportunity for someone to join our Zurich-based team as a Technology Solution Senior Consultant, to help support the growth of our implementation capability and deliver complex large-scale technology transformation journeys. The role would involve working directly with senior leaders within the team to deliver the implementation of cloud-based SaaS TPRM and/or CLM solutions to clients, including the integration of these solutions with existing client platforms. In addition, the role will provide a great opportunity to develop leading methodologies for delivery of these implementation projects and to support eminence activity with key TPRM and CLM technology vendors. What you’ll do: As a Third Party Advisory and Technology Solution Senior Consultant with experience of large-scale technology implementations, you will have responsibility for:

  • Analysing a client’s TPRM and/or CLM processes, and providing advisory services to enhance their existing ways of working
  • Supporting clients in technology capability assessments, and identifying configuration and integration opportunities for the client’s selected TPRM and/or CLM technology
  • Translating business requirements into technology capabilities, and developing an end-to-end implementation plan based on the TPRM and/or CLM technology selected, including configuration plans, UAT scripts, and integration strategies
  • Supporting the business and technical teams during design, build, test and deployment phases of a TPRM and/or CLM Technology Implementation
  • Configuring and build integration solutions that meet the client’s business requirements
  • Leading delivery of detailed integration architecture including software and infrastructure components - considering functionality, data, security, integration, infrastructure and performance
  • Creating and maintaining technical documentation where required for the implementation Who we are looking for:
  • Technical solution experience, ideally on large scale, complex cloud-based SaaS implementations
  • Specific experience with configuring and implementing market leading TPRM or Contract Management Technologies (e.g. OneTrust, ProcessUnity, ServiceNow, Icertis, Sirion, DocuSign)
  • In-depth understanding of the different integration vendors and technologies, and key differences between these
  • Experience of acting within a Solution Architect role and proven expertise in an IT development background.
  • Knowledge of artificial intelligence (AI) capabilities, with the ability to leverage AI-driven insights within daily tasks
  • Knowledge or experience working in the area of Risk Consulting and/or professional services will be considered an advantage
  • Fluency in English is mandatory
  • German would be considered an advantage What you bring to succeed in the role
  • Highly organised individual with strong attention to detail and able to manage their own workload
  • Strong communication skills, written and verbal, both internally and externally with clients and third parties, and be able to communicate clearly and effectively to all levels
  • Ability to build professional relationships and rapport within the team and its clients
  • Proactive, self-starter & committed individual seeking to add value in the role
  • Strong ability to plan around and manage changing environments and priorities throughout a cloud based Saas implementation project for global organizations across multiple workstreams Your Team & Career Growth : Our team, part of Technology & Transformation, has been one of the fastest-growing teams in Switzerland over the last years. Thus, we can offer unrivalled career growth & learning opportunities as well as an exciting place to work. Successful candidates will get the opportunity to help us shape the future of compliance, develop further our market offering and work closely with the world-top organizations on their third party management transformation journey.
