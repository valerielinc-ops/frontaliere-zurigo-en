Full description

For the Financial Services team of a leading Swiss auditing and consulting firm, we are looking for ambitious, committed and eager personalities to learn to enter auditing in Zurich. On- and off-the-job you get to know the complex world of modern finance and will be accompanied intensively on your way to the next career step. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for you!

This is your job

  • Auditing: Together as a team, you will audit annual and consolidated accounts of a broad portfolio of clients in the banking sector (including banks, investment firms, asset managers) in accordance with national and international accounting standards.
  • Special audits: You are actively involved in supervisory audits, internal audit mandates and a wide range of focus and special audits.
  • Regulatory Advice: Assistance in advising on regulatory issues and complex financial reporting issues.
  • Development: With increasing experience, your responsibility in the mandate grows – always with the clear goal of successfully completing your training as a Federal Certified Auditor.

This is what you bring with you

  • Education: Successfully completed bachelor's or master's degree (preferably economics) with a specialization in Finance & Accounting or a similar orientation.
  • Career goal: You have the clear intention and motivation, the further education to Eidg. Dipl. auditors to graduate.
  • Experience (optional): First professional experience in the financial sector / auditing or theoretical prior knowledge of legal requirements (e.g. banking law, FIDLEG, GwG) are an advantage, but not a must.
  • Skills: Rapid comprehension, high resilience, quality awareness, pronounced IT and digitization affinity as well as stylish German and English.

This is what awaits you from the customer

  • Career & promotion: Full support in external training for the Dipl. auditors, a wide range of internal seminars and structured support programmes for managers.
  • Work model & flexibility: annual working hours with flexible working hours, fair compensation of additional hours, home office options and the possibility for unpaid leave (Sabbatical).
  • Culture: A highly professional, interdisciplinary environment that offers you a holistic insight into renowned companies in the financial sector and understands work-life balance as a lived everyday life.
