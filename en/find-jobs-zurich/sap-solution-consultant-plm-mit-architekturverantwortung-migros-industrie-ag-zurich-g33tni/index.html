Full description

Do you not only want to implement SAP solutions, but actively shape their architecture? In this role, you combine solution consulting with architectural responsibility and develop sustainable solutions for SAP, integrations and PLM together with business and IT.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Industrie AG, Migros Industrie AG – Zurich, Limmatstrasse 152, 8005 Zurich

Mansioni

  • You are responsible for the technical development of our solutions in specification and declaration management.
  • You will analyze the requirements of the departments and translate them into sustainable and practical solutions.
  • You design and implement sustainable solutions around S/4HANA, SAP BTP and connected SaaS systems.
  • You design the system, data and integration architecture and ensure functioning interfaces and data flows.
  • You will evaluate solution and architecture alternatives and work closely with specialist areas as well as solution and enterprise architects.
  • You accompany changes, releases and projects from the idea to the successful implementation.
  • You actively contribute your ideas and shape the further development of our SAP and integration landscape.

Requirements

  • Mind. 5 years of professional experience: as an SAP consultant or solution consultant with architecture responsibility
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): in (business) computer science or comparable training
  • Several years of experience as an SAP consultant with focus on S/4HANA, integrations and SAP BTP.
  • Experience in the analysis of requirements and in solution design.
  • Experience in solution architecture, integration design and architectural decisions.
  • Very good understanding of business processes, interfaces and data flows.
  • Strong and clear communication with stakeholders.
  • Structured, independent and solution-oriented working method.
  • Enjoy connecting business and IT.
  • Knowledge of specification, declaration or PLM environment.
  • Experience with SAP BTP Integration Suite and Cloud Integrations.
  • Experience in an agile environment.
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • English (very good knowledge)

  • Flexible working hours: Flexible working hours, 41-hour week, 5 to 7 weeks of holidays per year.

  • Meals: staff restaurant Guggi on Limmatplatz. Excellent food at reasonable prices.
  • Cumulus points: Additional number of Cumulus points on the purchases made
  • Migros Bank: Private and investment account with preferential interest and expense-free account management
  • Club School Migros: Assumption of course costs from 70% to max. CHF 1000 per year
  • Sports and fitness offers: Contribution to fitness subscriptions: per subscription max. CHF 200 per year

Recruitment process

  • Submit application documents: For a complete application, we need your CV, certificates and diplomas. A photo in the CV and a letter of motivation are not necessary. We live equal opportunities for all applicants. We have a standardized recruitment process. If this does not work for you, mention it in your application dossier.
  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision to advance or withdraw from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Personal video or telephone conversation: For a large number of applicants, we optionally conduct a personal telephone or video conversation. This gives us the opportunity to get to know more candidates within a short time in order to invite suitable people for the next round. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Discussion with case presentation: Specific expertise is examined on the basis of a case or other assessment instruments. After processing, the result of the leader is presented and then discussed together. Usually, the team is also represented at the presentation. As a rule, the conversations are conducted by the superior, without a representative from the HR. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Final discussion before contract issuance: Before the contract is issued, we hold a final discussion in order to clarify open questions of both sides and to regulate contractual details. If all points are clarified, the written offer follows. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
