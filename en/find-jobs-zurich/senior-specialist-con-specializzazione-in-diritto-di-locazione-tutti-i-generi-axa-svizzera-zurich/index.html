Shape your future – together with us!

AXA-ARAG, as one of the leading legal expenses insurance companies in Switzerland, is growing and developing rapidly. Our legal service with around 200 employees is represented at three locations in Switzerland and solves more than 100,000 legal concerns of our customers every year. Our ultimate goal is to continuously improve the customer experience at a high level by breaking new ground, thinking creatively and transforming ourselves through digital transformation.

Our culture is characterised by openness, flexibility and appreciation. With us, your input counts – always. We offer you modern working models and space for your individual development. Do you like to take responsibility and play a key role in your team? Do you want to accompany customers in their legal challenges, pursue innovative paths and grow with digital change?

– Then you are the personality with the right mindset that we are looking for!

This is what awaits you with us

  • Independent case processing: You handle legal cases of private and corporate customers, especially in the area of rental law. Your advice is mainly by phone
  • Use of AI: In your daily work, smart AI tools are available so that you can work efficiently and focus on the essentials
  • Legal advice with a modern twist: you analyze a wide range of issues and solve problems in an uncomplicated, pragmatic and customer-friendly manner
  • Negotiations: You conduct oral and written negotiations with insured persons, counterparties, lawyers and authorities

You bring that with you

  • You have a completed Swiss university degree in law or business law FH
  • You have already gained practical professional experience in the field of rental law and can apply it in practice
  • You are strong in communication and can convey complex issues in an understandable and empathetic way. In addition, you have an unbeatable negotiating skill. Foreign languages (Italian/French) are an advantage
  • The satisfaction of your customers is important to you and you are committed to fulfilling their concerns in the best possible way
  • At the same time, you have a basic economic understanding and understand how to weigh costs and benefits in case processing and interpret case-relevant figures.
  • Studying files for hours is not your style – you work efficiently, solution-oriented and pragmatically
  • You keep calm even in hectic times
  • You are IT-savvy and open to integrating AI tools into your everyday work

This makes your job special Are you looking for more than a classic lawyer job? We offer a collegial culture and a working environment in which thinking is required and your input is of great importance. With a wide range of development opportunities in the sense of a career path, we build a sustainable cooperation that advances you professionally and personally in the long term – for you, for us and for our customers.

This is what we offer you We are convinced: Satisfied employees provide the best services. That’s why we invest in benefits that offer noticeable benefits – beyond the usual.

Flexibility and space: Smart Working (flexible working model) Annual working hours, 5 weeks holidays (from 50 years 6 weeks), holiday gift 2 weeks every 5 years, holiday purchase up to 10 additional days/per year

Job and family: family-friendly meeting hours, 22 weeks of maternity leave, 8 weeks of leave for the other parent, crèche subsidies, company family allowances, days off for caring for relatives and in challenging, personal situations

Precautions and insurance: Attractive pension fund with far-reaching non-compulsory benefits including choice savings options, up to 30% discount on insurance products

Flexible Benefits: AXA Shareplan (employee participation), CHF 500 annually on the advantage platform Swibeco, discounted lunch or lunch card, Smart Working flat rate CHF 200 per year

Mobility: refund for half-tax, Reka-Pay up to CHF 1’000 per year (20% discount), fleet discount, free e-charging station, AXA rental bicycles

Healthy balance in everyday work: Free check-ups, annual health weeks, diverse exercise offers (online/offline), massage offers, rest rooms, employee care, online health portal, various seminars/events around health prevention & mental health

Development: 3 days of individual learning time, free coaching, wide range of learning & development opportunities, financial participation in further education, promotion of internal mobility, talent development offers, seminars for employees 55+ (AXA Horizon), engagement in communities on various topics, participation in innovation projects (Innoday, AXA Hackathon, UnConference), 2 days of volunteering (environment & social affairs)
