Full description

In the spring of 2021, the new residential and care centre Tertianum Giessenturm in Dübendorf opened its doors. The imposing high-rise is located in an urban location and is part of the Giessen area. There are 80 modern apartments for senior citizens and 60 rooms in the care department. Amenities also include a public bistro, physical therapy, massage and a coffee shop.

Your heart beats for older people? Is the nursing profession your calling which for you not only improves the quality of life of others, but also creates a satisfaction and meaningfulness in your own life? Do you see opportunities to bring your skills and experience to bear on us to make a positive impact on our customers’ lives? Then you are right with us!

For the Tertianum Giessenturm in Dübendorf we are looking for 01. December 2026 or by agreement one or

Professional / Health EFZ 60-100% in day service

Their tasks

  • Holistic care and care of the guests in the sense of our care mission statement and the care process
  • Assumption of tasks as a caregiver
  • Participation in the care process and in the care needs classification with RAI among the assigned guests
  • Electronic care documentation using CareCoach (mobile possible with CareCoach smartphone)
  • Planning and support of medical visits
  • Assumption of day-to-day responsibility
  • Assuming tasks as a topic carrier for a subject and thus participating in the group-wide network on care quality

Your profile

  • Completed training as a specialist in health EFZ
  • Professional experience in long-term care is beneficial
  • RAI knowledge desired or readiness
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude
  • Enjoying multiple challenges
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
