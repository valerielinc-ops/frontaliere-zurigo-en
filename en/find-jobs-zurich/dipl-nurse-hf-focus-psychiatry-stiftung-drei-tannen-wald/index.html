Full description

The public Spitex Wald Fischenthal is responsible for the ambulatory health care of the inhabitants of Wald and Fischenthal. Spitex employees care for their clients competently and empathetically in their own home, look after them psychiatrically or support them in everyday life. Spitex Wald Fischenthal is part of the non-profit foundation Drei Tannen, which together with Spitex runs the newly built Rosenthal Care Centre and offers old-age apartments with needs-based service. Dipl. Nurse HF focus psychiatry Your tasks

  • Individual support: You nurture and accompany our clients in their home – with empathy, expertise and personal responsibility.
  • Take responsibility: You manage your cases independently and control the entire care and care process.
  • Holistic support: You support people in life crises, promote their reintegration and work closely with authorities, psychiatrists and other specialist centres.
  • Teamwork & exchange: You are part of a motivated team that works interdisciplinary and supports each other.

What you bring with you

  • Diploma as a nurse HF/FH with a focus on psychiatry (or equivalent training)
  • Also re-entrants* are welcome
  • Good knowledge of German and communication skills
  • Category B driving licence
  • Independent, flexible and solution-oriented working method
  • And very important: a good dose of humor – because laughter can also cure
