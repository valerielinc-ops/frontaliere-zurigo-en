Full description

Do you have a feeling for high-quality meat products and are enthusiastic about the variety of our meat & fish range? Do you have leadership experience, are strong salespeople and like to communicate safely with our customers? Then you are exactly right with us!

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Zurich, MMM City, Löwenstrasse 31-35, 8001 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You act as a competent contact person for our customers together with the team and assume responsibility for the meat and fish sector
  • It gives you pleasure to pass on your expertise and knowledge to new employees and introduce them in the area of meat and fish
  • You take responsibility for the efficient goods management, the attractive presentation of the products and ensure that our shelves are always well filled
  • You ensure the high quality of our products including date control

Requirements

  • 2 years of professional experience: in retail trade / sales in the meat & fish sector
  • Completed initial or further training: in the field of retail trade/butchery or as cook/chef
  • With your independent work and your friendly approach, you create a pleasant atmosphere for customers and team.
  • You are stress-tolerant and have good communication skills
  • You bring very good expertise in the meat & fish sector and can draw on valuable leadership experience
  • Reliability, flexibility and a high level of commitment set you apart – you are there when you are needed
  • You are ready to continue your education and thus always stay up to date.
  • German (good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Healthy work: Health-promoting offers such as massages and health checks
  • Mobility: With the one-time leisure bonus per year of max. CHF 500.– we participate in your public transport subscription
  • Handy subscription: You can look forward to special conditions for the Handy subscription
  • Occupational pension provision: Excellent pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Social benefits: Fair social benefits
  • Cumulus Points: You get additional Cumulus Points when shopping
  • Migros Bank: Preferred interest on private and investment account, free account management, discounted mortgages and more
  • Employee offers: Up to CHF 1,000 per year (depending on your workload) for your recovery and further development – usable for fitness subscriptions, courses, health offers or a subsidy for public transport.
  • Sports and fitness offers: You can get a total free time bonus of max once a year. CHF 1000.– e.g. redeem for an annual subscription to the M-Fitnesscenter
  • education and training: support for professional education and training,

Contatto

Application and contact Mirco Camera Junior Recruitment Partner

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We continuously review applications received, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Expert Discussion: In a specialist discussion, specific knowledge relevant for the execution of the job is requested by the manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, the team. If possible, there is time to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition: After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks

Grado di occupazione: 80-100 %
