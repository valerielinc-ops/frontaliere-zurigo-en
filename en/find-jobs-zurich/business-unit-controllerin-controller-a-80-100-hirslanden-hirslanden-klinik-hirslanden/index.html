Full description

Location: Klinik Hirslanden and Klinik Im Park | Zurich

Composition by: 01. February 2027 or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 75263

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

The Hirslanden Clinic – as the fourth largest Listenspital in the Canton of Zurich – and the Im Park Clinic provide annual care for around 28,000 inpatients. With their broad and partly highly specialized medical portfolio, both clinics provide first-class medical quality at university level and thus continuously achieve top rankings in the top lists of Swiss hospitals.

We are looking for committed support to ensure and further develop the joint controlling of both clinics.

YOUR TASKS

  • Preparation of the monthly accounts, commentary on the monthly reports as well as analysis and interpretation of the cost center and cost carrier calculations of the two clinics and the outpatient operation center
  • Provide meaningful reports, analytics and cockpits to manage business and profitability for the financial manager, performance management and operational management
  • Implementation of the planning and budgeting process within the clinics (OPEX and CAPEX)
  • Delivery of financial and performance statistics according to statutory and group-wide requirements (ITAR K, BfS, PSA et al.), Rekole certifications and audits
  • Support of the clinic in the preparation of business plans and investment calculations, the parameterization of the cost accounting, independent processing of AdHoc inquiries and cooperation in cross-divisional projects as a financial/controlling representative

Your profile

  • Technical/university degree in business administration, preferably with a focus on controlling
  • Several years of experience in controlling (experience in health care beneficial)
  • Strong analytical thinking, user-friendly communication skills and flexibility
  • Reliable, proactive and timely working methods as well as a high degree of independence and teamwork
  • Very good IT knowledge (MS Excel, SAP, BIC3)
  • Very good German skills and good English skills

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family responsibilities. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.
