Full description

Personal – competent – close We at Frauenklinik Züri Ost treat female patients at the two hospitals in Männedorf and Uster. Together, we create a strong, regionally rooted service for gynecology and obstetrics with standardized treatment concepts and the highest standards. Appreciation, helpfulness and humanity are at the heart of what we do. You will find an environment that specifically supports your professional and personal development. Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of being the leading primary care provider with specializations for the regions of the right shore of Lake Zurich and Glattal. We are looking for someone to start immediately or by agreement

Exciting challenges await you

  • you treat and care for female inpatients and outpatients in the delivery room, operating room and outpatient clinic, including emergency patients
  • surgical work with a focus on endoscopy
  • participation in shift work
  • supporting resident physicians in their daily work
  • contributing to the continuing education of resident physicians and nursing staff
  • assuming organizational tasks within Frauenklinik Züri Ost

What qualifies you for the job

  • Specialist title in gynecology and obstetrics
  • Experience as a senior physician is desirable
  • a communicative personality with a high level of commitment and a team-oriented approach to work
  • Enjoyment of teaching colleagues undergoing further training
  • good spoken and written German skills

5 reasons to work with us

  • benefit from the unique opportunity to collaborate across locations in a large catchment area
  • a committed team that works closely together professionally, personally and organizationally
  • an exciting and varied clinically oriented role
  • Proximity to the vibrant city of Zurich
  • Affordable parking options
