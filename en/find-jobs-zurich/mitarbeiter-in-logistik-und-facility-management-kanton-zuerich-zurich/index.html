Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Prison and Reintegration Investigation Prisons Zurich Employees/in Logistics and Facility Management 80 - 100% With around 1,400 employees, Prison and Reintegration (JuWe) is the largest office within the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs and is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as for the execution of various types of detention. Our employees from around 40 professions support and promote the reintegration of delinquents and work effectively and progressively for a safe and just society. The Zurich Detention Centers (UGZ) are one of eight main departments of JuWe. They comprise seven companies with about 735 prison places and about 500 employees. They are responsible for provisional arrest, pre-trial detention and security detention, as well as the premature execution of penalties and measures until transfer to a prison institution. From the outset, they thus fulfil a central function within the Zurich justice system. As part of the UGZ, Zurich West Prison is located in the Zurich Police and Justice Centre and has 241 detention places. It specialises in temporary arrest and pre-trial detention and plays a key role in the reception and first-aid of detained persons.
