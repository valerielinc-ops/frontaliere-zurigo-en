Full description

As an Audit Manager with a focus on Reinsurance and Insurance in Group Internal Audit (GIA), you will leverage your experience across the end-to-end value chain while contributing to a broad range of audit activities. You will regularly engage with senior stakeholders and functional leaders across multiple business units. This role offers the opportunity to address themes with enterprise-wide impact and to help shape and strengthen the risk culture at Swiss Re. Key Responsibilities

  • Work closely with the Audit Director for Re/Insurance audits to engage relevant stakeholders, continuously update risk assessments, and help shape the bi-annual audit plan.
  • Lead and execute a portfolio of audits, taking ownership of select engagements from planning through reporting, while contributing as a key team member on others. Drive high-quality, risk-focused audit delivery by effectively managing timelines, coordinating stakeholders, and ensuring audits are completed on schedule and within budget.
  • Leverage your expertise to contribute to audit planning, enhance re/insurance subject matter knowledge within the team, and strengthen the quality of audit observations and insights.
  • Act as a role model by challenging and coaching audit team members to deliver at a high standard and continuously improve.

About the Team GIA provides independent and objective assurance to support the Board of Directors and Group Executive Committee in protecting Swiss Re’s assets, reputation, and sustainability. GIA’s vision is to advance Swiss Re’s resilience by delivering risk-responsive assurance and providing innovative insights that strengthen the control environment and support the achievement of business objectives.

About You

We are looking for an inspiring and curious professional who is a critical thinker, delivery-oriented, and a strong team player. You communicate effectively in English and are comfortable operating in ambiguous environments. You bring a passion for innovation and transformation. Additional Requirements

  • 10+ years of experience in external and/or internal audit or related roles within the financial services industry
  • Strong knowledge of property & casualty business, including treaty and facultative reinsurance, as well as specialty lines
  • Degree in accounting, actuarial science, business, or another finance-related field
  • Deep understanding of the (re)insurance industry
  • Strong knowledge of risk, governance, and controls, as well as internal audit methodologies, audit project management, and continuous risk assessment frameworks
  • Proven experience managing stakeholders and audit teams in a global, matrixed organization
