Full description

In this key role, you lead an interdisciplinary project team and are responsible for the overall technical integration of strategic, complex and construction-intensive large-scale projects in the food industry of more than 150 MCHF. In particular, new factory buildings, site developments and comprehensive conversion and expansion projects with high structural and technical integration requirements.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Industrie AG, Migros Industrie AG – Zurich, Limmatstrasse 152, 8005 Zurich

Mansioni

  • You have overall responsibility for the technical planning and successful realization of demanding and construction-intensive large-scale projects from about 150 MCHF.
  • You lead and coordinate an interdisciplinary project team and take over the professional leadership of the project leaders.
  • You control the relevant interfaces and ensure that costs, deadlines, quality, resources and risks remain in view at all times.
  • You will create well-founded, risk-based decision-making bases for project clients, the Steering Committee and management.
  • Together with operation, production, engineering, construction, logistics, utilities, automation and IT, you define and coordinate the technical requirements.
  • You develop and evaluate technical and economic solution variants and derive decisions and recommendations for further action.
  • You will accompany tenders, awards and contract negotiations as well as approval and approval procedures.
  • From commissioning through acceptance to documentation and handover to operations, you ensure successful project implementation.

Requirements

  • Demonstrable management of complex and construction-intensive major projects with cost responsibility of over CHF 150 million: in the industrial environment, ideally in the food industry
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): e.g. civil engineering, mechanical engineering, process engineering, electrical engineering or food technology
  • Further education in project management is advantageous.
  • Cost, deadline, risk, quality, contract and claim management are part of your day-to-day project.
  • You understand production processes and the associated supply chains and can evaluate technical and operational connections holistically.
  • You think entrepreneurially, keep track of complex projects and successfully bring together different specialist areas and interests.
  • You convince with negotiating skills, assertiveness and a sovereign handling of demanding situations and different interests.
  • You work independently, structured and solution-oriented and like to take on responsibility.
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • French (good knowledge)
  • English (good knowledge)
