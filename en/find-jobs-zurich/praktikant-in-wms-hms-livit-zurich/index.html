Are you open-minded, motivated and looking for a modern training company on the pulse of the times? Livit offers you just that. Our interns are an important part of the Livit family.   • You are an organizational talent and like to take on responsibility
  • You are interested in HR processes as well as business and personnel-related relationships
  • You are communicative, enjoy working in a team and enjoy dealing with people
  • You have good manners.
  • You enjoy applying new technologies and digital tools
  • You are curious and open to change
  • You learn easily and quickly and work reliably.
