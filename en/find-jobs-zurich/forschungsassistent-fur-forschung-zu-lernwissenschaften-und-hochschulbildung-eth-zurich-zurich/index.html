Research Assistant for Research on Learning Sciences and Higher Education 10%-30%, Zurich, fixed-term print Drucken The Professorship for Learning Sciences and Higher Education at ETH Zurich strives to advance both the fundamental understanding of human cognition and learning and the application of this knowledge in real-world educational contexts, with a particular focus on higher education. The group is looking for a Research Assistant who will support members of the lab in their research activities, including tasks such as data collection, data preparation, literature review, and analysis. Currently, we are looking for someone to contribute to a specific project, as outlined in the task description. However, we are also open to, and would welcome, collaboration beyond this project.
