Your task They feel comfortable not only in the office, but also directly at technical facilities. In this function, you ensure the reliable operation of our HVAC systems, solve technical problems and further develop the infrastructure in a targeted manner.

  • Experience in the preparation of technical concepts, investment applications and profitability considerations
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English in word and writing
