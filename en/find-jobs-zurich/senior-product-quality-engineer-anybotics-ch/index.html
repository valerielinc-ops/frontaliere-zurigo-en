Senior Product Quality Engineer — ANYbotics

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

ANYbotics is a fast-growing tech company dedicated to shaping the future of mobile robotics across multiple industries. Join our highly talented and motivated team of more than 200 people and work on cutting-edge robot technology.

The Opportunity

We are looking for a Product Quality Engineer to join our operations team in Zurich. In this role, you will own the product quality improvement process for our advanced robotic platforms, including ANYmal D and the upcoming ANYmal X. You will play a pivotal role in managing non-conformities, driving root cause analysis, implementing corrective actions, and establishing data-driven quality improvement practices across the company. This is a unique opportunity to work on a highly complex, cutting-edge robotic product in a cross-functional capacity, directly impacting product reliability and scaling our quality systems for global success.

The Market & Our Technology

ANYbotics transitions industrial workplaces into high-efficiency environments through autonomous robotic inspection. Our four-legged robotic platforms, ANYmal and ANYmal X, are engineered to navigate complex, rugged, and hazardous industrial sites, providing high-precision data collection and real-time insights. By automating these challenging tasks, we enable industrial operators in oil & gas, chemicals, power, and mining to increase workforce safety, optimize uptime, and streamline operations.

Your Impact

  • Own and optimize the non-conformity management workflow and quality tools across the organization
  • Ensure proper documentation, prioritization, escalation, and closure of critical quality issues
  • Coordinate cross-functional teams across R&D, industrialization, production, and supply chain to rapidly investigate and solve hardware and software issues
  • Drive structured problem-solving activities using methodologies such as A3, 5W2H, and fault tree analysis
  • Foster and promote a robust, proactive problem-solving culture across engineering and manufacturing teams
  • Define, maintain, and improve product quality KPIs and data dashboards to drive visibility
  • Analyze quality data from production, testing, and field operations to identify trends, recurring issues, and systemic improvement opportunities
  • Report product quality performance and key risks to internal stakeholders to enable data-based design and production decisions
  • Support the monitoring, compliance, and auditing of projects under ISO 9001 and ISO 80079-34 standards
  • Train multidisciplinary teams on quality assurance tools and modern problem-solving methodologies

Your Profile

  • Master’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Industrial Engineering, or a highly related technical field
  • 3 to 7 years of hands-on experience in Product Quality, Systems Quality, or Manufacturing Quality engineering
  • Proven track record of managing non-conformities in a complex, multi-disciplinary technical product environment (such as robotics, automotive, medical devices, or advanced manufacturing)
  • Deep expertise in structured root cause analysis and implementing effective corrective and preventive actions (CAPA)
  • Solid experience building quality indicators, tracking performance metrics, and designing reporting structures or dashboards
  • Exceptional collaborative skills with the ability to bridge gaps and align goals across R&D, operations, and supply chain teams
  • Fluency in English with strong technical communication and documentation skills

Bonus Points

  • Direct experience working with and maintaining ISO 9001 and ISO 80079-34 (explosive atmospheres) quality management systems
  • Experience in a fast-growing hardware tech scale-up environment

We offer you a very exciting and dynamic work environment, the opportunity to become part of a fast-growing company and an ambitious team that is on a mission to change the industrial inspection market, a chance to leverage your experience and bring in your own ideas, a fair market salary, an attractive employee stock ownership plan, and a job in the city of Zurich.
