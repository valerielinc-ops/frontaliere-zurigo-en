Full description

As a member of the Private Relationship Management Team, you are in regular contact with our customers from the HNWI and UHNWI segments. In your role as Sales Support Executive, you support the team and contribute significantly to its overall success. Activity description

  • They act as a first point of contact for HNWI and UHNWI customers and manage daily communications
  • They process ongoing customer correspondence (letters, emails, reports) while ensuring a high level of service and discretion
  • You support the relationship managers in various due diligence tasks
  • You ensure customer satisfaction through the comprehensive support of your team members
  • They coordinate with other departments (e.g. legal, compliance and back office) to ensure smooth business operations
  • Support department leaders in day-to-day monitoring, reporting and validation
  • You plan and coordinate customer appointments, conferences and events together with the relationship managers and specialists
  • They maintain detailed customer profiles
  • Professional experience in the banking industry
  • Completed studies (bachelor) in economics or finance of advantage
  • Knowledge of German and English (mother tongue or very good level), other languages beneficial
  • Previous experience in support in the context of financial services
  • They are open-minded, team-oriented and resilient
  • Structured working methods, strong priority thinking and excellent organisational skills
  • Excellent communication strength and solution-oriented thinking
  • Good knowledge of the Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook) – especially Excel (medium level)
