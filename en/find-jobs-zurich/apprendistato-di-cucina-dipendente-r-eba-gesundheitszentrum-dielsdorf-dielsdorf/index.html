Original description

Every year many young people take the plunge into our professional life as chefs, kitchen employees EBA, health specialists (FaGe), assistants: Health and Social Affairs (AGS), hotel industry specialists EFZ and EBA, maintenance specialists EFZ, ICT specialist EFZ and merchants. The Dielsdorf Health Center is a modern geriatric competence center with around 500 employees from 35 nations. Our offer ranges from counselling for older people and their relatives to general and specialized long-term care and care at home. With more than 200 beds, the inpatient range of general long-term care at the Dielsdorf site is complemented by specialized treatment pathways for rehabilitative acute and transitional care, palliative care, psychiatric geriatrics and the focus on dementia.
