Full description

Behind every analysis is a person who is dependent on a safe transfusion or an innovative cell therapy. With your work in the immune hematology laboratory, you create the basis for vital treatment decisions and give patients safety in crucial moments. >The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.

Tasks

  • Automated blood group determinations including Ak and DAT screening with elution
  • Special investigations (Ak specification, antigen and titre determinations)
  • Provision of blood products for transfusion
  • Cryopreservation of stem cells and lymphocytes for immune cell therapy
  • Taking over late, night and weekend services

Requirements

  • Completed vocational training as biomedical analyst / biomedical analyst HF or biomedical laboratory diagnostician / biomedical laboratory diagnostician FH
  • You are looking forward to familiarization in a highly specialized field
  • You take responsibility, are resilient, motivated, capable of teamwork and a thinking personality
  • You complement your experience with further education and enrich our team with your purposeful, reliable and exact working method

We offer

With us, you can expect a motivated team with strong cohesion, in which you support each other and learn from each other. You will work on challenging cases that go far beyond routine diagnostics and build up an in-depth knowledge of immunohematology. With increasing experience, you will work on complex questions independently and develop continuously. So your work remains exciting, varied and meaningful.
