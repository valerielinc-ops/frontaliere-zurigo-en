Full description

Share your passion for books

Do you love reading? Do you enjoy immersing yourself in incredible worlds of books? And do you love sharing your passion for books with other book enthusiasts? Then apply now for an apprenticeship as a bookseller at Orell Füssli. Because we love books just as much as you do. In addition, Orell Füssli is a place where employees feel comfortable, where people enjoy working well together and achieve more as a team. During your training, you will learn the book trade from the ground up.

We look forward to welcoming you to one of our branches in the Zurich region (Zurich) / Northwestern Switzerland (Basel) on 1 August 2027.

Your main responsibilities

  • Expert advice for customers
  • Recommendation of books and new releases
  • Acceptance and processing of orders
  • Various tasks on the computer and in our merchandise management system
  • Presentation of books and non-book products

What you bring

  • Good secondary school-leaving qualification
  • High service orientation, sociability, attentiveness and curiosity
  • Excellent German skills and enthusiasm for foreign languages
  • Passion for books and reading
  • Passion for media and current affairs

What awaits you

  • Varied training in different departments
  • Collaborative and appreciative corporate culture
  • Generous vacation policy
  • Diverse discount offerings
  • Central workplace with good public transport connections

We are very much looking forward to receiving your online application. Please send it to us including a letter of motivation, your complete school certificates, and a short text about your favorite book. See you soon.
