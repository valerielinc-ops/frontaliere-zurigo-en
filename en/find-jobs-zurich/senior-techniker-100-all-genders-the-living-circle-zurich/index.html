The Living Circle cerca per la sede di Zürich una nuova risorsa. Scopri i dettagli della posizione e candidati online.

Panoramica

  • The Living Circle is the leading, most innovative and probably most diverse hospitality group in Switzerland, redefining luxury: close to nature, regional, historic and authentic. The group, which is family-owned in Switzerland, includes four hotels: the Castello del Sole Beach Resort & Spa in Ascona, the Widder Hotel and the Hotel Storchen in Zurich, as well as Alex Lake Zurich in Thalwil and the private Hideaway Château de Raymontpierre with its own farm in the Jura. Our portfolio is complemented by the restaurant Buech in Herrliberg and the three farms Schlattgut in Herrliberg, the winery Cantina alla Maggia and the Terreni alla Maggia in Ascona.

Cosa ti aspetta

  • Whether in the guest room, in the kitchen or behind the scenes: You ensure that the technology in our hotels works reliably. You tackle yourself, coordinate specialist companies and find a solution even if a disruption occurs in the middle of the guest operation. In addition, our teams will support you with occupational safety and fire protection.
  • Technology and Maintenance – 60%
  • Self-employed repair, maintenance and maintenance work in guest rooms, public areas, kitchens and home equipment
  • Supervision and control of technical facilities as well as rapid and discreet resolution of disruptions in ongoing hotel operations
  • Cooperation in the planning and implementation of preventive maintenance work
  • Coordination and support of external craft and service companies in compliance with safety requirements
  • Cooperation in renovations, renovations and technical projects
  • Participation in the Picetting Service
  • Security Officer (SIBE) – 40%
  • Advice and support for hotel management and managers on occupational safety and health protection
  • Cooperation in the operational security concept and maintenance and updating of security documentation
  • Conduct safety tours, hazard identification and risk assessments together with the superior and external service providers
  • Application for work safety specialist if additional expertise is required

Il tuo profilo’

  • Completed craftsmanship EFZ, for example in electrical, sanitary or building technology
  • Several years of experience in technical maintenance and broad knowledge about building technology
  • Training as safety officer and certificate for fire protection
  • Joy of tackling it yourself and finding a solution even in the event of unexpected problems
  • Independent working methods, good organisational talent and a secure sense of priorities
  • Very good knowledge of German as well as a friendly and discreet approach to guests and employees

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
