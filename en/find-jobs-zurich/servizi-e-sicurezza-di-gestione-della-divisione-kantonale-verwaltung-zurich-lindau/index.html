Full description

We are looking for a committed leader as a member of the Strickhof management by 1 February 2027 or by agreement. If you would like to take responsibility and make a difference with your experience, we look forward to you. Their tasks

  • Responsible for the personnel and professional management of the Facility Services & Security division across all Strickhof locations with around 50 employees
  • Management of the operational areas of catering, accommodation, room scheduling, events, house maintenance and building technology as well as site and ambient maintenance
  • Representation of operators and users in inter-agency projects as well as participation in new construction projects
  • Overall responsibility for operational security (SiBe)
  • Control of quality and safety standards as well as financial responsibility in the business segment Your profile
  • You have a higher specialist training or university degree in Facility Management, Building Technology, Business Administration or equivalent
  • You have good knowledge of technical and infrastructural facility management, ideally with in-depth expertise in at least one of the areas of hospitality, technical FM or infrastructural FM
  • You have several years of leadership experience and understand how to successfully lead and develop operational teams
  • Knowledge of operational safety (SiBe)
  • Demonstrable business skills, in particular in budgeting, cost management, procurement and contract management You expect a versatile and responsible management task with a high degree of independence.
