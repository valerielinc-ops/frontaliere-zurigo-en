Full description

We are seeking a client-centric, service oriented and commercially minded individual to support the continued successful growth of Sygnum's Private and Institutional Client franchise. In your role as Account Manager, you will be in charge of the provisioning of Service Excellence to Sygnum's existing clientele of Private and Institutional Clients. The role combines relationship management, all aspects of client servicing, stakeholder coordination and commercial awareness, making it an excellent opportunity for professionals seeking to build a long-term career in the emerging digital asset wealth management space. You will work in close collaboration with the Business Developers, Product Specialists and Support Functions ensuring clients derive maximum value from Sygnum's digital asset platform, products & services offering, achieving agreed Key Performance Indicators. (e.g. Assets under Custody and incremental revenue growth, Operational Excellence and stakeholder feedback). The role reports to the Head of Client Business based in Singapore. Key Responsibilities: Client Service Excellence & Account Stewardship

  • Act as the primary day-to-day contact for assigned client relationships.
  • Deliver a seamless, responsive and value-added client experience.
  • Manage client enquiries, service requests and transaction-related activities.
  • Execute and coordinate client trades and investment instructions in accordance with established processes.
  • Maintain a strong understanding of client objectives, activity and evolving needs. Client Lifecycle & Governance
  • Coordinate onboarding, account maintenance and lifecycle events.
  • Support KYC reviews, regulatory requirements and documentation activities.
  • Ensure accurate client records and adherence to governance standards.
  • Drive timely resolution of account, operational and control-related matters. Commercial Growth & Client Engagement
  • Support growth in client assets, activity levels and revenue contribution.
  • Identify opportunities to expand product and service utilisation.
  • Partner with Business Developers and Product Specialists on client growth initiatives.
  • Promote relevant platform capabilities, solutions and market opportunities aligned to client needs. Operational Excellence & Stakeholder Management
  • Coordinate internal stakeholders to deliver efficient client outcomes.
  • Oversee the end-to-end execution of client-related processes and requests.
  • Maintain CRM integrity, management information and reporting.
  • Drive process enhancements, service quality and operational efficiency. Risk, Compliance and Professional Conduct
  • Maintain the highest standards of regulatory compliance, professional integrity and risk stewardship, while ensuring adherence to all legal, regulatory, policy and Code of Conduct requirements, as well as applicable industry best practices and supervisory expectations.
