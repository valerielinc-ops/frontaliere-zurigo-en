The Kilchberg Sanatorium is one of the most traditional psychiatric private clinics in Switzerland and looks back on almost 160 years of history. Only 15 minutes from the city centre of Zurich with beautiful views of Lake Zurich and the Alps. As a Listespital, we offer comprehensive psychiatric care for all insured persons and have inpatient and outpatient facilities in Kilchberg and Zurich. Our approximately 650 employees from over 25 professional groups are at the heart of the clinic and are committed to the best possible treatment of our patients.
