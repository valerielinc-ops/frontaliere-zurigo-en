Hirslanden stands for first-class, responsible and individual health care that puts people first. From birth to old age as well as from prevention to healing – at any time, physically and digitally – the Hirslanden Group is concentrating on the further development of the “Continuum of Care” together with private and public partners.

Our focus is not only on the well-being of our patients, but also on that of our employees.

YOUR TASKS

  • Operation, maintenance and further development of our Linux server landscape (Red Hat Enterprise Linux, SUSE Linux Enterprise Server) and the Kubernetes platform based on SUSE Rancher (RKE2)
  • Automation of server and platform operation using Infrastructure as Code (Ansible: Playbooks, roles, collections) and operation of the central automation platform AWX
  • Establishment, operation and monitoring of CI/CD pipelines on our central git platform Forgejo including technical support for application and development teams
  • Ensuring uninterrupted and stable operation incl. Monitoring, 2nd-level support as well as automated updates, upgrades and release changes within the maintenance window
  • Cooperation in security measures (OS hardening, vulnerability remediation) as well as co-service of privileged accounts, certificates and secrets of the data platforms
  • Participation in the Pikett service to ensure platform operation outside regular working hours (7x24)

Your profile

  • Sound basic technical training (Fachschule/FH/Uni/ETH) or equivalent professional experience
  • Advanced knowledge of running Linux servers and Kubernetes in critical 7x24 environments
  • In-depth experience with Ansible and DevOps and automation practices (Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD)
  • Knowledge of platform security, networks (load balancing, DNS, firewalls) and distributed/highly available systems is an advantage
  • ITIL certification as well as technical certifications (cloud, open source) are advantageous
  • Independent, reliable and solution-oriented working method with a high level of initiative, sense of responsibility and service orientation
  • Readiness to work on marginal times and weekends (picquet service)
  • German negotiation-safe (C2), good knowledge of English (B2); Knowledge of French (B1) is an advantage
