Full description

The Kartaus-Münchhalde Day School belongs to the beautiful Zurichberg School District and is located in the Seefeld Quarter. The primary school consists of six kindergarten classes, eight secondary classes and six intermediate classes. The total of 420 students are taught by about 45 teachers. The care area of the day school Kartaus-Münchhalde comprises four care locations for kindergarten and primary school children and one care location for middle school children.

For our location in Kartaus we are looking for 1. November 2026 Support for our team.

Tasks

  • They organize and design the childcare services for children from kindergarten to 6th grade.
  • They perform administrative, operational and domestic tasks.
  • The implementation of the educational goals of the school is a matter of course for you.
  • They provide supplementary school care and children’s free time.
  • They participate in Q groups and participate in meetings, school conferences, school events, etc.

Profile

  • Training: EFZ specialist carer or toddler (for foreign degrees including CH recognition)
  • Flexibility and resilient personality with great commitment
  • Motivated, open and humorous personality with a positive human image
  • Professional experience in pedagogical work and joy in the leadership of especially larger groups
  • Self-employment, sense of responsibility and good team and communication skills

We offer

A varied, versatile and independent activity in which you lead the children's group together with the hoard management and the team. A motivated and committed team that looks forward to working together. An exciting environment awaits you, a lot of freedom in the design of the hoarding day and competent support from the management support. In addition, you can look forward to progressive salary and social benefits according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department.

Your days/times are: 4 days per week (Monday/Tuesday/Thursday/Friday) from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m.

About us

The Zurichberg School District comprises districts 1, 7 and 8 with the districts Altstadt, Fluntern, Hottingen, Hirslanden, Riesbach and Witikon. The district school authority manages 18 school houses. These are connected to 43 kindergartens and over 40 care facilities of different sizes. Of the 14 organizational units, 11 are day schools.
