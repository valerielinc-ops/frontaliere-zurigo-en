PPA Origination Manager — Stadt Zürich

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

In the team Long-Term Trading, you actively shape the securing of our ewz production portfolio on the electricity markets and develop future-oriented marketing strategies together with your teammates.

Tasks

  • You are developing new business opportunities in the European renewable energy markets and shaping PPAs and Corporate PPAs – tailored to our wind, PV and BESS portfolio as well as ewz's asset strategy and growth targets.
  • The tendering and monitoring of direct marketing mandates for our wind, PV and BESS portfolio are your responsibility.
  • You develop and implement innovative marketing solutions – including system services for wind, PV and BESS systems.
  • In cross-sectoral projects around our wind, PV and BESS projects in France, Germany, Norway, Sweden and Switzerland, you take responsibility or actively participate.
  • You coordinate the entire process until the conclusion of the contract, bring the internal stakeholders together and plan and conduct contract negotiations with external partners.

Profile

  • You have a degree in economics, finance, mathematics, computer science or a similar subject and have at least three years of trading experience in the European energy market.
  • Ideally, you will bring a proven track record in the chromonomy and closing of transactions as well as knowledge in the areas of trade, risk management, credit risk, back office or finance.
  • You convince with negotiation skills, entrepreneurial thinking and implementation strength and lead business independently from the chromomination to the successful conclusion.
  • As a communication-strong and well-connected personality, you succeed in building sustainable relationships and creating trust with internal and external partners.
  • You communicate in German in style and have good knowledge of French and English as well as a high IT-A quality. You like to work independently and appreciate the exchange and cooperation in the team.

We offer

  • More energy for your life: mobile work, flexible work models and part-time opportunities enable you to actively shape your working time and divide it into the way it suits your life best. For example, you can shop for up to 2 weeks extra holidays and work part-time at all levels. Job I/O/-spli-t and unpaid holiday are also possible with us.
  • Sustainable and discounted travel: With the mobility contribution of up to 600 francs per year, you travel cheaply and sustainably – whether you want to cycle through Zurich by bike or explore Switzerland by train.
  • An investment in your future: We not only value paying fair and transparent wages, but also ensure that you have more of your wages. We take over 60% of the savings contributions and offer an over-compulsory interest on the capital saved.
  • A boost to your leisure time: We pay you 100 francs a month on your lunch check card. You also profit from additional Fringe Bernards worth 150 francs a year and attractive mobile subscriptions.
  • Development and further education: With our talent management, we specifically promote your development – through a wide range of internal and external training opportunities as well as exciting development opportunities.
  • Meaningful work: Sustainability and sun-sustainable work are not just words for us: Join us in shaping Zurich and the future. Let energy be released for a good morning together with us.

ewz has been on the road for over 130 years, dynamic and visionary every day. Whether it's sustainable energy production, smart energy solutions or innovative networking technology, develop with us today what is important tomorrow.
