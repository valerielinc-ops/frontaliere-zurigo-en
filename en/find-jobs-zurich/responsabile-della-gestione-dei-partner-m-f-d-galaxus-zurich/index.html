Full description

The Partner Development Platform in Category Management is responsible for Partner Management, Partner Integration, Offer Data and Order Management. As an interface to logistics, finance, aftersales and customer service, we are driving the automation and scalability of our order processing.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Headquarters, Pfingstweidstrasse 60b, 8005 Zurich

Mansioni

  • As a business owner, you lead the business engineering team in partner lifecycle management and drive efficient, automated processes around partner master data, integration and portal
  • Together with your team and the Head of Platform, you will develop the partner lifecycle management vision, strategy, goals and measures, which you will coordinate in cooperation with all relevant stakeholders.
  • Together with the Business Engineers, you drive and prioritize innovation projects – always in the optimum of business needs and scalable solutions
  • As the main contact for the partner Lifecycle Management, you represent the team and connect the stakeholders from Category Management and Product Development
  • You define the most important KPIs in the partner lifecycle – from onboarding quality to SLA compliance to purchasing attractiveness – and steer towards corporate goals
  • In business engineering, you can also help yourself, support your team with complex tasks and take on overarching projects.
  • Your team will be coached by you so that all employees can work productively and effectively and your team is well positioned.

  • A pragmatic and analytical personality distinguishes you who loves to implement simple solutions in a complex environment to create the greatest possible value contribution
