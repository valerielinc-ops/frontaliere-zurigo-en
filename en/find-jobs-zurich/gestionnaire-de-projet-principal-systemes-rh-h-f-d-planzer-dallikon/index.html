Full description

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, participate and grow with us. Become part of our family and shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us.

Tasks

  • Complex and cross-divisional HR system projects are your responsibility – from strategic planning to implementation to successful implementation, you manage them with foresight
  • As part of our team of four, you will analyze and prioritize requirements, critically question existing processes and actively and sustainably shape our HR system landscape.
  • Professional system responsibility is in your hands – you are responsible for tests, acceptances and quality assurance and ensure stable and efficient operation
  • Working in day-to-day operations and supporting your teammates in system administration is a matter of course for you
  • Complex support cases are an exciting challenge for you - in 2nd/3rd level support you analyze causes and develop sustainable solutions
  • You pass on your knowledge with enthusiasm – together we make our decentralised personnel managers fit for the future

Profile

  • You have several years of experience in the support and further development of HR systems and know their interaction with HR processes
  • You move confidently between strategy and implementation – both conceptual tasks and operational challenges you master with ease
  • Connected thinking and a strong understanding of HR processes help you to identify connections and develop sustainable solutions
  • With your communication strength, you convince on all levels and bring different stakeholders together successfully
  • You combine strategic thinking with a strong hands-on mentality – you tackle and never lose sight of the big picture.
  • You see change as an opportunity and actively contribute your ideas to the further development of our HR systems and processes
  • A high level of personal responsibility, team spirit and quality awareness distinguish you

Benefits

  • With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • With us you start with 5 weeks vacation and benefit from the 5th year of service even from 6 weeks!
  • We go with the times - enjoy with us the advantage of Homeoffice

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
