Location: Zurich Start: From now on, the amount of money: 60 - 100 % (fixed operating times, spread over several days per week) Duration: 6-12 months with possibility of taking over

About Eraneos Eraneos is an international consulting group for strategy, transformation and technology that helps companies succeed in a dynamic digital world. With top-class experts from business and technology, we empower our customers to set new standards for sustainable transformations – from strategy to implementation.

Whether it is in the design of sustainable organizations, the development of new potentials through data and AI or the protection of companies through holistic cybersecurity: We deliver results pragmatically and purposefully. Our team of around 1,300 dedicated employees is represented in offices in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, Portugal, Denmark, Sweden, Austria, Great Britain, Singapore and the USA and combines global perspectives with strong local roots. Around 450 employees work at the Swiss locations Zurich, Bern and Lausanne.

This is the job – your main tasks

  • Creation, revision and management of legal documents in German, English and occasionally French
  • Participation in projects in digital, contract, IT and data law
  • Support of experienced and experienced lanterns on large projects
  • Implementation of legal clarifications and independent project work
  • Development of guides, templates and internal training
  • Implementation and use of AI-based legal tech tools
  • Various projects relating to legal, business, IT, consulting and project management in a wide range of industries

This job is for you when...

  • You are completing a legal master's degree, ideally with a law & technology degree.
  • You have first practical experience in the legal environment (e.g. law firm, consulting, legal tech).
  • You are enthusiastic about the interface between law, business and technology.
  • You work with a structured, independent and entrepreneurial mindset.
  • You are confident in style in German and English
  • French is a plus.
