Full description

As Brand Communications Manager, you will help shape the brand presence of Galaxus and Digitec and lead campaigns from order clearance to success monitoring. In addition, you take on the role of Marketing Business Engineer: You bundle marketing needs, translate them into technical solutions and drive automation forward.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Headquarters, Pfingstweidstrasse 60b, 8005 Zurich

Mansioni

  • You take responsibility for your campaigns and communication measures – from initiation to production and completion

  • As a marketing business engineer, you represent the marketing perspective over product development, translate requirements into technical solutions and drive automation forward
  • Kick-offs, workshops and project meetings you plan and moderate and ensure transparent flow of information

  • Campaigns based on brand and business goals and derive improvements for upcoming projects
  • You get involved in campaign planning and develop our cooperation and processes
