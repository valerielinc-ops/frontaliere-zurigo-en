Full description

Do you want to drive digital transformation in the SAP environment? In our team, you will develop cloud-based solutions that expand and optimize our ERP landscape. Become part of the Denner SAP CC, work with state-of-the-art tools and design innovative applications that improve our business processes. ## Mansioni

  • Develop modern, scalable applications on SAP BTP and in SAP S/4HANA
  • Design intuitive SAP Fiori applications for an optimal user experience
  • Integrate existing SAP systems with SAP BTP and extend them
  • Accompany innovative cloud extension projects from idea to implementation
  • Optimize processes through the use of AI and other innovative technologies
  • Work closely with architects, DevOps engineers and security experts together in the platform team ## Requisiti
  • Several years of professional experience: with SAP BTP, ABAP (Cloud), Fiori/SAPUI5 and cloud architectures
  • Degree (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): in the field of computer science or equivalent training
  • Experience with programming languages such as ABAP, Python, JavaScript or SQL
  • Knowledge of modern development practices (Git, CI/CD, automated tests)
  • High level of self-motivation, team orientation and willingness to learn
  • German (fluent)
  • English (good knowledge) ## Cosa offriamo
