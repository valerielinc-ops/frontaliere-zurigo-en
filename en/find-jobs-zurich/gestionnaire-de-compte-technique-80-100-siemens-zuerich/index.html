In this hybrid key role, you as a strategic orchestra build the bridge between technical consulting, partner management and strategic marketing. Your new tasks

  • You will support planners from the Swiss tunnel industry, be the central point of contact for solution and distribution partners around program elements and coordinate tailor-made solutions by bringing together the right experts.

  • In doing so, you actively control the distribution, define relevant technical focal points together with the Head of Partner Sales, prepare content in a marketing-friendly manner and coordinate their rollout in the Swiss market.
  • In this context, you identify technological trends and translate them into concrete marketing content, developing stories that help partners and distributors succeed in the market.
  • In addition, you provide the data technology foundation for sales success through precise data analyses and place global content in the Swiss market.
  • Finally, you will design and moderate topic-specific expert workshops as well as curate relevant webinar formats to bring together experts and customers and promote business opportunities. Your talents and experiences
  • Completed technical studies (HTL, FH, University) in the fields of automation technology, electrical engineering, mechatronics or computer science
  • Several years of practical experience in industrial automation or technical sales with a strong affinity for marketing and content definition
