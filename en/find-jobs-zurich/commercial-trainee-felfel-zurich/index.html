Full description

IN BREAKDOWN

Are you communicative, customer-oriented and an organizational talent? Then this could be just right for you! As a commercial trainee (from 1 January or 1 February 2027 for 6 months, 100%) in Zurich Wiedikon, you are right in the middle of exciting customer events – you plan and organize them in the background and ensure a smooth process that inspires our customers.

Your responsibilities

Event Coordination & Administration

  • You coordinate and conduct our launch events for corporate customers. You are responsible for the entire event logistics – from set-up and disassembly to the presentation on site and the high-quality presentation and distribution of our food to the guests.
  • You manage the inventory and materials and ensure a smooth flow.

Commercial Support & Customer Management

  • You work closely with the Commercial Coordinators and support them in daily customer management – from communication to follow-up.
  • You’ll help qualify new leads, identify business opportunities, and independently run lunch bag tours with potential customers.

Product knowledge and customer feedback

  • You will continuously expand your knowledge about our products and partners in order to communicate competently with customers.
  • At the same time, you collect customer feedback at events and summarize it so that we can constantly improve.

All-rounder tasks

  • You work across functions with different departments to get a comprehensive insight into the company.
  • You get to where you’re needed – whether it’s for office tasks or supporting marketing activities.

What you bring

  • You love creating positive experiences for others and enjoy working in a service-oriented environment.
  • Also stay cool under pressure? No problem. You know how to prioritize, work efficiently and still put the customer first.
  • Entrepreneurial thinking is a matter of course for you – you’re curious about how things work and are always looking for ways to grow the company.
  • You are a foodie through and through – so you can tell about our dishes with a lot of passion!
  • A growth mindset makes you up! It is important to you to develop personally and professionally during your internship.
  • Valid category B driving licence (mandatory for the post).
  • Very good German and good English skills in word and writing. Knowledge of French is an advantage.
  • Non-smoker: You do not smell of cigarettes and do not smoke during the day
  • After all, FELFEL is about hearty food
  • Work permit EU/EFTA or Switzerland

What we offer

  • Fine food right at work: Enjoy fresh, high-quality meals every day – just a few steps from your workplace.
  • Barista-Style Coffee & Premium Tea: Start with unlimited barista coffee and premium tea – thanks to our Gavetti machine you are well taken care of.
  • Half-tax subscription is on us: We cover the costs of your Swiss Half-tax subscription and thus support sustainable commuting.
  • Team spirit & space for ideas: You can expect a supportive, dynamic environment in which initiative is appreciated and you can contribute your ideas.
  • Behind the scenes: Get exclusive insights into the various FELFEL teams – from logistics to product development to marketing and IT.
  • Skill Shaping – Learning & Growing: Take part in the monthly learning sessions – with exciting guests from the start-up world, the food industry or directly from the FELFEL team. These sessions promote your personal development and deepen your hospitality know-how.

If this is the right job for you, apply now - we look forward to your application!

About us

FELFEL is all about good food and coffee in the workplace: we help Swiss leaders to be better hosts.

With our smart fridge FELFEL and barista coffee Gavetti, we enable companies to provide their employees with first-class, ultra-fresh food and drinks throughout the day. Whether hearty-traditional, modern-vegan or protein-rich – our weekly changing menu ensures that it always remains exciting for employees ; .

FELFEL is a family business founded in 2013. Today we serve around 1000 locations in Switzerland and have 150 employees in our offices in Lausanne, Zurich and New York.
