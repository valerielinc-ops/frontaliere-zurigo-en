Full description

The emergency centre at KSW is the only emergency in the region open 365 days a year and 24 hours a day. In our emergency center consisting of emergency adults, emergency children & adolescents, emergency practice and emergency bed station, shock room and plaster room, we treat around 64,600 patients annually.

  • The willingness to work shifts is a matter of course for you
  • You also have a high level of social skills, keep an overview even in hectic situations and are interested in participating in the development of the emergency center

Benefits: KSW offers employees conditions that enable them to reconcile their professional goals and personal needs. Flexible, flexible working hours, home office as well as a generous maternity leave and employment levels adapted to different situations – all this is possible at KSW, as long as the company allows it. Every year, the KSW offers 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses. We also support you temporally and/or financially with external training.

In addition, the KSW supports annual team events financially and organizes a large staffing event for the whole house every four years. KSW employees benefit from very good social benefits. Thus, the pension fund solution with BVK is far above the legal minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, KSW employees are very well covered.
