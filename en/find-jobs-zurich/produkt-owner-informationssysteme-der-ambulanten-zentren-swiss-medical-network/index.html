Role overview

The outpatient centers (from the family doctor's office to the medical center) are the first link in integrated care.

The digitization of these centers is a key to optimizing the Swiss healthcare system.

As a product owner, you are the link between the end users and suppliers with regard to contract and product developments and, as the central contact, represent the outpatient centers to the software suppliers.
