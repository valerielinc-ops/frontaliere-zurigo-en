Put to the Point

Would you like to work in a fascinating, dynamic environment at the Luftwaffe? With us, you can combine your knowledge of project management with your interest in operational processes. FLORAKO is the core system for airspace surveillance and operational management. Take your chance to participate in the fascinating system landscape for securing air sovereignty!

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-19131
  • Location: 8600 Dübendorf

You can make this contribution

  • Initialize Air Force projects in the areas of airspace surveillance and air sovereignty, lead to successful completion and accompany them in use
  • Formulate user requirements for our system landscape, implement concepts and implement new requirements and changes
  • Developing Military Basic Papers and Concepts
  • Operational and technical operations
  • Represent the Luftwaffe in project and system committees, at the manufacturer as well as in national and international committees.

This makes them unique.

  • Technical/university degree with 2 - 3 years of functional experience in the field of Information and Communication Technology (ICT)
  • Methodological knowledge in Business Analysis, Requirements Engineering, Project Management (e.g. HERMES) and Basics in Safety Management according to aviation-specific standards
  • Open personality and enjoyment of teamwork and problem solving
  • Active knowledge in two official languages, passive knowledge of a third official language and very good knowledge of English
  • Swiss citizens
  • Officer/in the Air Force requested

Your commitment to security and freedom The Command Operations (Kdo Op) is responsible for all military operations and operations of the Swiss Army. The Kdo Op includes the Luftwaffe, the Army, the four territorial divisions, the SWISSINT Competence Center, the Special Forces Command and the Military Police. Around 40% of the approximately 2,500 employees are civil employees – from engineers to aircraft mechanics to analysts.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
