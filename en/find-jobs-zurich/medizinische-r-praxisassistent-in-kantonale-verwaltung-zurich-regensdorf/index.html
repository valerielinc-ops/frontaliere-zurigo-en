Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Imprisonment and Reintegration Medical / Practical Assistant / 80 - 100% Imprisonment and Reintegration (JuWe), as part of the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs of the Canton of Zurich, is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as the implementation of the various types of detention. For our 1,400 employees from around 40 professions, the successful social reintegration of people who have committed crimes and the prevention of recidivism are of particular importance. The Pöschwies Prison (JVA) is one of eight main departments of JuWe. With 399 seats, the JVA Pöschwies is the largest closed prison in Switzerland for men who have committed crimes. It performs a central state task and, in accordance with the legal mandate, takes into account the rehabilitation of detained persons and the protection of the general public. The Main Department of the Medical Service provides basic medical and somatic care for the detained persons in all prison institutions of the Canton of Zurich. As a central organizational unit, it ensures harmonized and high-quality health care according to the equivalence principle and works closely with the Psychiatric Psychological Service and the JuWe institutions.
