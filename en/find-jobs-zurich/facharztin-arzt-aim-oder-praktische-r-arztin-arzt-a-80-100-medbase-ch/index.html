Welcome to Medbase Kloten

Our modern, lively Medical Center with its own ultrasound and X-ray is centrally located in Kloten and is well connected to public transport. Around 20 employees from the disciplines of General Internal Medicine, Cardiology, Nutritional Counselling and TCM/Acupuncture supervise our international patients interdisciplinary and quality-oriented. After consultation, we also carry out home visits. At Medbase Kloten, we attach great importance to an independent working method with heart. In addition, we ensure a balanced work-life balance. Working part-time is possible in all functions and working hours can be flexible.

As a specialist you offer comprehensive care and ensure a high quality of patient care. At Medbase, you are part of an interdisciplinary team that supports you in your professional development and sets high standards.

  • Self-responsible care and basic care of family doctor patients
  • Ensuring the best possible medical care
  • Collaboration in the team with collegial representation
  • Support for the further development of practical offers
  • Internal and external training for quality improvement

What you bring

  • Completed studies and specialist training for General Internal Medicine or Practical Doctor
  • Commitment to holistic patient care
  • Solution-oriented and team-capable working method
  • Familiarity with electronic medical history
  • Communication Strength and Empathy
  • Good knowledge of German (min. B2)
