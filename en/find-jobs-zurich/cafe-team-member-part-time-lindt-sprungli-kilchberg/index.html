About us

We enchant the world with chocolate! Lindt & Sprüngli (Switzerland) AG is a subsidiary of the global Lindt & Sprüngli Group with its headquarters and production facility in Kilchberg on Lake Zurich. In Altendorf we operate our own logistics center, while the Lindt Cocoa Center in Olten produces cocoa mass for our own needs as well as for other European production facilities. Throughout Switzerland we proudly operate 15 Lindt shops, including iconic locations such as the Jungfraujoch and the Swiss public transport company in Lucerne.

Over 1000 employees from more than 50 nations contribute to our success.
