Full description

At the Office for Urban Development, we are planning the Zurich of tomorrow today. We weave the history of the city with future visions for livable quarters of high urban quality. We are creating the conditions for Zurich to change and grow. High urban and architectural quality is our goal.

The Department of Area Development & Planning South/West consists of a team of spatial planners, architects and geographers who supervise private or urban area developments, in particular with a need for adaptation in use planning. Furthermore, the team is jointly responsible for topics such as the informal participation of broad populations in urban planning or the implementation of the municipal indicative plan of settlement, landscape, public buildings and facilities. For this department we are looking for a committed team member on 1 February 2027 or by agreement.

Tasks

  • They accompany and advise landowners in planning, in particular with regard to possible adjustments of planning instruments (use and special use planning).
  • They lead and accompany urban area developments, spatial planning procedures, test planning and feasibility studies and create planning and urban planning analyses. You work in interdisciplinary teams.
  • They coordinate private and public interests, paying particular attention to informal participation.
  • They are responsible for the development and coordination of design plans, special building regulations, quarter plans and BZO partial revisions.
  • They draft motions to the city and municipal council, opinions, minutes and reports.
  • You support the development of communication concepts, media releases and other means of communication that affect your projects.
  • They write and give presentations and guides.

Profile

  • University or technical college studies in spatial planning, urban planning, geography or architecture combined with practical experience and/or a postgraduate degree
  • Solid expertise in the disciplines of spatial planning and urban planning as well as experience with current spatial planning instruments and procedures
  • Designated project management experience
  • Secure oral and written expression and communication and negotiation skills
  • Strategic and networked thinking
  • Winning, team capable and resilient personality
  • Motivation and willingness to work for the concerns of the city in the field of tension between politics and administration

We offer

You can expect a demanding and versatile job in a dynamic team. We value good cooperation: trust and the recognition of good performance are important to us. We create an environment for new ideas and responsible work. Attractive employment and training conditions as well as a centrally located workplace near the central station are further advantages. With the title "Family AND Occupation", we promote the compatibility of family and work as well as equal opportunities for women and men.
