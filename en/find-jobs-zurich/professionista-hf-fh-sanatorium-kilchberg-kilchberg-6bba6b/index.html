Sanatorium Kilchberg AG | online since 21.08.2026

We are looking for 1. September 2026 or by agreement

Aging Psychiatric Acute Station Focus on Dementia, Delirity

Additional benefit for carers in shift work: 37-hour week The Kilchberg Sanatorium offers you a fulfilling working environment with exciting development opportunities in a dynamic and appreciative team. Bring your strengths and shape the future of the clinic. At Station B3, we work closely together as a lively, motivated and humorous interdisciplinary team.

Their tasks

  • Ensuring patient-oriented professional psychiatric and somatic care
  • Reference work
  • Participation in improving quality and process flows
  • Active participation in the treatment process in interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Active participation in family discussions, in the exchange with homes and in case discussions with the contribution of own expertise

Your profile

  • Completion as a dipl. Nurse HF (recognition at foreign diploma)
  • Joy and interest in working with older people
  • A committed, open-minded personality who not only wants to accompany change, but also actively shape it
  • Training in aggression management or willingness to complete the course
  • Open humorous appearance
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • High flexibility and self-management skills

The Kilchberg Sanatorium is one of the most traditional psychiatric private clinics in Switzerland and looks back on almost 160 years of history. Only 15 minutes from the city centre of Zurich with beautiful views of Lake Zurich and the Alps. As a Listespital, we offer comprehensive psychiatric care for all insured persons and have inpatient and outpatient facilities in Kilchberg and Zurich. Our approximately 650 employees from over 25 professional groups are at the heart of the clinic and are committed to the best possible treatment of our patients. Become part of our team and shape the future of psychiatric care.
