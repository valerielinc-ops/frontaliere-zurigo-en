Full description

We are looking for a school assistant immediately or by agreement, temporarily until 23.04.2027 (option of extension) to strengthen our support team.

Tasks

  • They support the care team in the care and catering area. The team is led by a hoard management.
  • They do the workload of 29% on 5 days per week, from 11:00 to 14:00h and work in a team of 4.
  • In the catering area, you work together with another support assistant.
  • In addition to working in the parental hoard, you have a jumper function for absences in the support team.

Profile

  • Motivated, open, resilient personality with a positive human image
  • Flexibility as well as very good team and communication skills
  • Joy in dealing with people of different cultures
  • Joy in dealing with children (kindergarten to middle school)
  • Enjoyment of designing various leisure activities
  • Willingness to take on the jumper function
  • Readiness for interdisciplinary cooperation in the school team
  • Advantageous professional experience in supplementary family care
  • with advantage professional experience in the domestic sector (cooking)

We offer

  • varied, versatile and independent work
  • A multicultural, exciting environment
  • Competent support from the management
  • Progressive salary and social benefits according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich (Horte)

About us

We are a large school community in Zurich-Nord with 18 schools. We derive our pedagogical attitude from the concept of New Authority and thus meet the daily challenges in everyday school life together and effectively.

The city of Zurich is divided into 7 school circles. The Zurich-Glattal school district comprises the districts of Affoltern, Seebach and Oerlikon and counts around 6,500 school-aged children. Since 2002, all our 18 schools have been run. All hoards are assigned to a school unit. The hoards, which belong to a school unit, are led by a management supervision.

Ettenfeld is part of the Kolbenacker School in Zurich Seebach. In this nursery, up to 30 students are fed and looked after by the kindergarten every day. The nursery is open from Monday to Friday from 11.55 a.m. to 6 p.m.
