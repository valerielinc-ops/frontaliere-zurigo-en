Full description

In the IVF and Andrology Laboratory of the Clinic for Reproductive Endocrinology They are an important contribution to the fulfilment of the desire for children of our patients. In a highly specialized, accredited laboratory, you can expect a varied field of activity, state-of-the-art infrastructure and the opportunity to continuously develop your expertise in reproductive medicine. >The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.

Tasks

  • Egg cell search
  • Implementation of various fertilisation techniques (IVF/ICSI)
  • Cultivation of gametes, prenuclear stages and embryos
  • Implementation of embryo transfers
  • Cryopreservation and thawing of gametes, prenuclear stages and embryos
  • Sperm analysis and processing
  • Cryopreservation of testicular and ovarian tissues
  • Biopsies of Embryos for PGT
  • Cooperation in quality management
  • Responsible for ordering

Requirements

  • You have completed training as a biomedical:r analyst:in HF or a completed biology degree. In addition, you will have practical experience in the IVF and andrology laboratory as well as in-depth theoretical knowledge of assisted reproductive techniques.
  • You are a resilient, team-capable and flexible personality with good to very good knowledge of German. They are accustomed to meeting high standards of work quality and efficiency, taking on responsibility and being committed to a small team. For them, a reliable, precise and well-documented way of working is important. If you are also willing to provide periodic weekend and holiday services (no night services) and are looking for a longer-term job, we look forward to meeting you.

We offer

In the accredited laboratory you can expect a varied, interesting and demanding activity. You have the opportunity to expand your experience through regular internal and external training.
