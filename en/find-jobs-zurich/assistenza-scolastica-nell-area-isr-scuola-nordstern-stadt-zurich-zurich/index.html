Full description

For the accompaniment of a child with special needs, we are looking for care by 1. November 2026 or by arrangement a school assistant.

Tasks

  • They support the primary school child with special needs in supplementary school care. The child is integrated in the 4th grade, in school and care everyday life and is additionally accompanied by a curative pedagogue.
  • They accompany the child over lunch time on 3 days (Mo/Di/Fr) from 12:00 to 13:30 and in the cluster time from 15:00 to 16:00. In between you have a break.
  • Further assignments during the holidays and in class are possible. They would be paid extra.
  • They are interested in interdisciplinary cooperation with the school staff involved in the promotion and the parents.

Profile

  • Further education or professional experience in the pedagogical field desired
  • Very good knowledge of German, at least level C1 or higher
  • Motivated, open, resilient personality with a positive human image
  • Flexibility as well as very good team and communication skills
  • Joy in dealing with people of different cultures and willingness for interdisciplinary cooperation in the school team

We offer

  • A varied, versatile and independent activity
  • A multicultural, exciting environment
  • Competent support from the management
  • Progressive salary and social benefits according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich (Horte)

About us

Nordstern Day School is part of a large school community in Zurich-Nord with 18 schools. Our pedagogical attitude is based on the concept of New Authority – we meet challenges together, clearly and effectively.

The Nordstern Day School focuses on education for sustainable development (ESD). In age-mixed classes and groups and through strong participation, children can take responsibility and experience self-efficacy. The teachers work in the care and the caregivers support the teachers in the teaching, together we create the living space school.
