Full description

At the Institute for Therapies and Rehabilitation, 100 therapists from the fields of physiotherapy, ergotherapy, logopedia and other therapeutic professional groups work in five different specialist teams. In individual and group therapies, stationary and outpatient patients are treated. 17 clinical specialists at the Institute for Therapies and Rehabilitation are active in research and development and the implementation of high quality standards. The Institute also offers training places for up to ten students. As of August 1, 2026 or by agreement, we are looking for a medical masseuse/medical masseuse to 20-40% for our insured patients.
