Full description

Our research group at Wagiareal Schlieren is investigating how the immune system recognizes and combats tumors – with the aim of developing new immunotherapeutic approaches. To strengthen our interdisciplinary team of biologists, doctors and laboratory staff, we are looking for a dedicated person who enjoys experimental work with mouse models and modern analysis methods. Do you want to contribute and further develop your technical skills in a dynamic, research-oriented environment? We look forward to your application. The advertised position is limited in time with extension possibilities.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • Technical and experimental support of scientific staff: • Molecular and protein analysis and genotyping of mice • Immunohistological, flow cytometric (FACS) and functional examination of tissues • Collaboration in experimental animals including care, monitoring and scoring of experimental animals Requirements • Training as a biology laboratory: in or degree in life sciences (MSc) • Sound knowledge of standard analysis techniques (RNA/protein extraction, molecular biological analysis methods, Western blot, immunohistochemistry, cell culture, genotyping, etc.) • Advantageous LTK Module 1 • Joy of learning and introducing new techniques • They communicate easily and reliably We offer: Her place of work is in the Bio-Technopark in Schlieren ZH. We offer a modern and very generous laboratory infrastructure with state-of-the-art equipment. Further information about our laboratory can be found at www.prime-laboratory.com
