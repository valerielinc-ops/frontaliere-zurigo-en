Full description

For more than a century, the Wagerenhof Foundation has offered people with mental impairments a loving home. In this long time, it has constantly grown and evolved. Today, Wagerenhof stands for individual quality of life – as a nursing and Agogic home, competence centre and important social space in Uster. Our motto "What matters is you." determines our actions. We make every effort to ensure that our approximately 250 residents have the highest possible quality of life, regardless of the degree of impairment, the need for care or the age. With its open neighbourhood character, Wagerenhof plays an important role as a bridge between the individual and society and also enables people with multiple impairments to live with the greatest possible self-determination and autonomy. For our HR team we are looking for immediately or by agreement temporarily until 31. July 2027 one/n Internship in Human Resources (80 – 100%) With your willingness to learn as well as your empathetic, sensitive and hands-on nature, you support our professional HR administration. In our 13-member team, you will get comprehensive insights into various human resource tasks. With your solution-oriented way of working, you make an important contribution to the fact that we can offer the people entrusted to us a loving home. The Wagerenhof Foundation (affectionately called “Wagi”) in Uster has been a permanent home for around 250 people with multiple cognitive impairments for more than 100 years and offers over 50 employees who need support accompanied jobs. We are a recognized, non-profit institution with a social mission. With a demand-oriented offer of living and care places as well as various day and leisure offers, we promote the quality of life, participation and self-determination of our residents. The special features of the Wagerenhof include size and Dörfli character, which allows space for encounters. For employees with different careers and professionalism, the Wagerenhof Foundation is an attractive employer with a wide range of development opportunities. For learners, Wagerenhof is a competent training partner.
