Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,300 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland. For the Senevita Alterszentrum Gehren in Erlenbach we are looking for a mindful and team-oriented person as RAI responsible /r 60-100 % Your tasks

  • You take responsibility for the professional use of the instrument RAI NH.
  • You ensure that all codings are correct and traceable, always based on carefully managed maintenance documentation.
  • You recognize optimization potential and implement targeted measures to develop processes efficiently, economically and quality-oriented.
  • You support the teams in a practical manner with questions about the RAI NH.
  • You will conduct internal training and ensure that new employees are safely and competently introduced to the application of the RAI NH.
  • With your commitment, you ensure that the RAI NH ratings are not only technically sound, but also play a key role in our company’s success.
  • You are the contact person for health insurance companies and responsible for the audits.
  • Depending on the employment request, you take on other tasks.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed training in the field of care (tertiary level), several years of professional experience in day-to-day care and a degree as a RAI expert.
  • You think quality-consciously and economically and want to actively shape the maintenance process as well as the company success with your expertise.
  • You work independently, structured and solution-oriented, even in demanding situations.
  • You enjoy sharing your knowledge and accompanying the teams in training, introductions and in everyday life.
  • You are team-oriented, strong in communication and open to an appreciative cooperation at eye level.
  • Dealing with digital tools is easy for you.

We offer you

  • An own office with office hours in the function as RAI supervisor/in
  • A work plan that can do without shared services
  • High degree of flexibility (e.g. workload, service planning) as well as the opportunity to contribute your own idea and develop further
  • Attractive further education offered by the Senevita Academy, which inspires ongoing development
  • An environment where vocational training is written GROSS and brings a breath of fresh air into the house
  • Free drinks and reduced food - heartily prepared by our gastro team
  • Discounts (SBB half-ax, fueling, supplementary insurance, fitness, pharmacy, etc.)
  • Good social benefits and at least. 5 weeks holiday (from 50 years 6 weeks, from 60 years 7 weeks)
  • Exciting activities / events that move employees and residents
  • Very good accessibility (direct motorway connection, location directly at the station Erlenbach)
  • Our commitment: You as a person are important to us!

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
