Your tasks

  • You can expect a varied work in the medical, accredited environment with a focus on molecular genetic studies, especially for leukaemia patients in a committed team with open communication.
  • You work with patient samples (bone marrow / blood / tissue) and isolate gDNA and RNA.
  • Instrumental analytics (qualitative and quantitative PCR analyses, creation of DNA libraries for NGS and aCGH) are performed independently.
  • You are jointly responsible for the operation, maintenance and calibration of laboratory equipment.
  • You are involved in validation and qualification of processes and devices.
  • Your tasks include co-responsibility for carrying out internal and external quality controls.
  • You are responsible for logging and documenting all your work steps and patient data according to the BAG/SRK guidelines.
