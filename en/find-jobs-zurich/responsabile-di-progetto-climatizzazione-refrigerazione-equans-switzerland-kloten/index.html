Full description

To strengthen our team in Zurich or Kloten, we are looking for a committed and entrepreneurial personality as project manager climate / cold. Take over in this versatile function You are responsible for challenging projects from planning to successful handover to our customers.

You can move that with us.

  • Independent management and handling of climate and cooling projects
  • Responsibility for costs, quality, deadlines and resources
  • Technical and commercial project responsibility
  • Leadership and coordination of assembly teams and external partners
  • Support and advice for existing customers as well as building new customer relationships
  • Preparation of offers, supplements and project accounts
  • Participation in construction meetings and coordination with builders, planners and specialist planners
  • Ensuring compliance with quality, safety and environmental standards

That's what makes you.

  • Basic training as refrigeration system assembler EFZ, refrigeration system planner EFZ or similar training in the HVAC environment
  • Further training as project manager, technician HF or comparable qualification is advantageous
  • Several years of experience in the project management of air conditioning and cooling systems
  • Strong technical understanding and commercial flair
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Negotiation skills and high customer orientation

That's what we offer you.

  • Exciting and varied projects in a dynamic environment
  • High personal responsibility and scope for design
  • Modern work equipment and flexible working models
  • Attractive training and development opportunities
  • Collegial team and appreciative corporate culture
  • Progressive employment conditions and attractive social benefits
