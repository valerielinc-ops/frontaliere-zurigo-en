The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 60% (or by agreement) For our innovative and nature-oriented offer "Sandgrueb Animal and Nature-Supported Therapy" in child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy at the Egg location, we are looking for two committed personalities as Their tasks
