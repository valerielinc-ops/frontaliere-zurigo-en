by 1 February 2027 or by agreement

Our polyclinic at USZ Airport “The Circle” performs around 4,800 outpatient procedures every year. The Dermatological Clinic is a reference centre with a worldwide reputation – we treat, clarify, research and much more. No day is the same and if you are interested, your field of application can be extended. One thing is certain: you will find a modern, future-oriented infrastructure and a unique view of the airport.

Are you burning to shape the trend of outpatient medicine?

  • You will receive and accompany patients during the consultation hours in our dermatological polyclinic and assist with surgical procedures. The agreed triage for emergency patients:innen is just as much a part of your tasks as a seamless billing of services and the assumption of administrative work. You work in our association room and assist in our intervention rooms.
  • As a medical practice assistant / medical practice assistant or specialist health / health specialist you have already gained experience in an acute hospital or in a larger practice.
  • You have a high level of communication skills, a strong organisational capacity and a stylish German.
  • Your working hours are from Monday to Friday between about 07:30 and about 17:15 (different services).
  • We are a multicultural and interprofessional team and attach importance to a strong team cohesion. Your personal development is important to us. We are happy to support you through internal and external training.
