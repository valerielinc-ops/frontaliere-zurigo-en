Executive Education of the Faculty of Economics

Job opening immediately or by agreement

The Executive Education of the Faculty of Economics of the University of Zurich offers a wide range of further education for national and international specialists and executives. Our portfolio includes certificate programs, customized company programs and executive MBA programs. We stand for scientifically sound, practical and reflective training, which is conveyed with high quality and great commitment. In the Executive Education of the Faculty of Economics and Business Administration, you can expect an entrepreneurial environment with a lot of room for manoeuvre and personal responsibility. Our offer includes programs in Business Administration, Finance, Real Estate and Computer Science. You can expect a varied and responsible activity in an exciting and socially relevant environment. Modern and flexible working conditions, diverse opportunities for further development and participation in innovative projects offer you the opportunity to contribute your ideas and actively make a difference. Become part of a dynamic and innovative team and shape the future of further education with us!

Tasks

  • You design, develop and implement technical solutions in the areas of business applications, CRM, automation, data and digital platforms.
  • You accompany the management in the conception of the AI strategy.
  • You will take on challenging IT and digitization projects from requirements analysis to technical implementation to introduction and documentation.
  • You develop existing systems, interfaces, web applications as well as reporting and data solutions and ensure their reliable operation.
  • You will analyze complex technical problems, develop practical solutions and support the team in their implementation.
  • You accompany the IT employees as a professional coach, promote the exchange of knowledge and support them in technical decisions and challenges.
  • You will evaluate new technologies and work with the team to develop standards for architecture, development, quality and documentation.
  • You take over the personnel and organizational management of the IT team and ensure good coordination with the program areas, the management as well as internal and external partners.

Profile

  • University Master's degree in Computer Science, Business Informatics or a comparable field
  • Several years of practical experience in the conception, development and implementation of demanding IT solutions
  • In-depth knowledge of Microsoft Power Platform, Dynamics 365 and Azure
  • Good programming skills, ideally in JavaScript and at least one other programming language
  • Good knowledge of the use of AI as well as the ability to use AI applications specifically for the optimization of processes
  • Experience with CRM and business systems, process automation, system integrations and interfaces
  • Knowledge of web technologies, web applications as well as in the operation and further development of digital platforms
  • Experience with data preparation, reporting, web analytics and data-based solutions
  • Ability to independently manage technical projects and accompany solutions from conception to productive implementation
  • Enjoy coaching employees, passing on knowledge and jointly developing viable technical solutions
  • Cooperative role understanding: You give professional orientation, but at the same time actively work on projects and technical solutions
  • Initial experience in team management or the willingness to assume personnel responsibility in addition to the technical role
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
