What we offer you.

A varied and diverse range of activities awaits you, within a team characterized by a high orientation to service and a great commitment. We provide advice to executive directors in a personal, digital and telephone way on all issues relating to conditions of employment (CCL and CO) and the application of directives. As a member of our HR & Personal Policy Management team, you contribute decisively to the development and setting of our business. You are flexible and work regularly in Zurich and Bellinzona as well as in Work Smart. Find benefits such as free AG, travel abroad at discounted prices – even for the family – on our website at the Benefit page. What you can move.

  • As head of HR Consultancy, you can head a team of 10 HR consultants at Zurich Altstetten and Bellinzona. Take professional and high-quality advice for executive directors of Divisions and Central Sectors, mainly in the regions of Zurich, East Switzerland and Ticino. The focus is on staff and labour law issues as well as reorganization.
  • Together with your team, ensure efficient work processes as well as the continuous maintenance and expansion of technical knowledge. Take a high level of counselling, optimal use of resources and constantly develop HR advice and processes.
  • You are close to/from the top and look for a regular exchange with the line. In this way, you quickly recognize needs and developments and positions HR as a competent and solution-oriented partner.
  • Direct HR projects or projects and ensure the HR implementation of organizational reorganisations and changes.
  • As a member of the HR & Personal Policy Management Team, you actively contribute to the further development of HR Consulting. Contribute to a high-quality, nationally uniform advice and further development of your product portfolio.

The one you bring with you.

  • You have strong counselling, communication and social skills and convince both orally and in writing.
  • Your profile includes a university degree in business or psychology and continuing education in Human Resources (e.g. MAS, EMBA, HR or equivalent).
  • It has a multiannual management experience and a solid professional experience in HR consulting, ideally in a large company.
  • You have excellent knowledge of labour law and experience in the context of Change Management and project management.
  • Your native language is German. In addition, you have fluent and fluid knowledge of Italian (level C1). Good knowledge of French is a preferential title. Working throughout Switzerland, you appreciate collaboration in a multilingual environment and you can communicate appropriately to different groups of interlocutors.
