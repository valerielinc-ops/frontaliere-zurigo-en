Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility.

At the Centre for Acute Psychiatric Diseases, we treat people who show the most severe psychiatric conditions. Often accompanied by acute self- or external danger. We are the psychiatric emergency room, intensive care and acute care. We clarify the incidence situations, treat acute crises and stabilize them, so that patients can move or return to an outpatient follow-up setting, to a therapy station or to a social psychiatric institution. The focus is on individual, recovery-oriented, holistic treatment and care. For our care team on our acute care stations we search immediately or by agreement
