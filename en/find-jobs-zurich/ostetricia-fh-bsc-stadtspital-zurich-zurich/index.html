Full description

Join us at the Zurich City Hospital, Triemli location, to create an innovative and family-centered obstetric care system where women and their families feel safe, secure and understood. You can expect a committed interprofessional team that lives its own responsibility, expertise and warmth. Bring your ideas and actively develop our obstetrics. As of January 1, 2027 or by agreement, we are looking for a dedicated personality for our team.

Tasks

  • You care for healthy women and newborns independently, independently and holistically.
  • You contribute to the care of women and children in obstetrical and medical risk and crisis situations and take on joint responsibility.
  • You document the work and care process as well as the performance recording carefully, competently and independently.
  • You support the team with the introduction of new employees, take on joint responsibility for the care of trainees and actively participate in working groups.
  • You promote a constructive interdisciplinary cooperation and are committed to a high quality of service.

Profile

  • Completed training as a qualified midwife FH/BSc with at least two years of professional experience
  • Engaged, resilient and open personality with high social competence
  • Independent and self-responsible working methods as well as enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Interest in midwife-guided obstetrics

We offer

We offer you more than just a job! Our city hospital world is characterized by an open dual culture, an uncomplicated, personal togetherness, respect and appreciation. As a team of over 120 professional groups, we work every day in very different ways for the well-being of our patients. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions and excellent social benefits from the city of Zurich.

About us

With over 36'000 inpatients and around 455,000 outpatients per year, the Stadtspital Zürich is one of the top 10 Swiss hospitals. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire spectrum of basic medical care, including a 24-hour emergency, intensive care and a birth department, where around 3000 children are born each year. Our 4,500 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
