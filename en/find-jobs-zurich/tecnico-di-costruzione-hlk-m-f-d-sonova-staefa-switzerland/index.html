Full description

As HLK (m/f/d) building technician, you ensure that our heating, ventilation, air conditioning and refrigeration systems run smoothly. With your technical expertise and your quick action in the event of disruptions, you contribute directly to the trouble-free operation. With us, you expect a lot of personal responsibility, room for manoeuvre and the chance to further develop your skills in a varied environment – supported by a strong team and short decision-making paths.

More about the role • HVAC (heating, ventilation, air conditioning) • Operation and maintenance of HVAC installations • Update HVAC plans and functional descriptions • Coordination of maintenance • Analysis and correction of disturbances in all buildings • Energy optimisation and implementation of technical improvements • Various media • Management and maintenance of the inventory programme • Coordination of installations • Maintenance, maintenance and repairs

More about you • Heating, ventilation, air conditioning or refrigeration training completed • Several years of experience in the operation, maintenance and troubleshooting of HVAC systems

  • Sound and up-to-date expertise in the entire HVAC area, which can be implemented in a practical manner

• Knowledge of measurement, control and regulation technology (MSRT) and building automation • Completed VDI 6022 hygiene training (category A) • Independent, solution-oriented working method with high operational readiness • Team-capable, communicative and service-oriented • German fluent, English good • Experience in coordination of external service partners and guidance of employees

More about our offer

We offer you

  • An inspiring working environment with a lot of personal responsibility
  • Comprehensive induction and continuous training
  • Attractive development prospects in an international company
  • A dedicated, collegial team and flat hierarchies
