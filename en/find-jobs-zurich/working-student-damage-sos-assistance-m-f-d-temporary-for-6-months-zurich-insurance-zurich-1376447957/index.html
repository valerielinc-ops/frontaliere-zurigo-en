138050 | 40 - 60% | Oerlikon, Zurich | Hybrid

Are you looking for a side job that can be ideally combined with your studies? Does the thought of helping people in emergencies motivate you quickly and competently?

In this role, you will strengthen Zurich Switzerland’s damage division in the area of SOS Schaden Assistance and make an important contribution to our joint success.

SOS & Assistance consists of a multicultural team of passionate and helpful colleagues who advise our insured persons in case of emergency measures. You support us in the receipt and processing of claims cases and get an exciting insight into the claims department within a large company.

This is what awaits you

  • You take care of the receipt of telephone calls in case of damage 24/7h
  • You are in direct contact with customers and partners
  • You coordinate the order of international service partners or towing services after car accidents / breakdowns
  • You take on administrative tasks such as processing damage cases, paying invoices, telephone and written customer inquiries
  • You work on days Monday to Sunday between 06:30 and 22.15 o'clock the assignment times are adjustable depending on the semester load.

This sets you apart

  • Enrolment at a university or university of applied sciences
  • Excellent knowledge of German, good knowledge of Italian, knowledge of French advantageous
  • High customer orientation and a service- and solution-oriented approach
  • Ability and motivation to work under time pressure while keeping calm
  • Flexibility to work Monday to Sunday as well as on holidays

Good to know

Part-time: 40 – 60 %

Place of work: Oerlikon, Zurich

Job: from now on or by agreement

Travel activity: None

Hybrid work: up to 40%
