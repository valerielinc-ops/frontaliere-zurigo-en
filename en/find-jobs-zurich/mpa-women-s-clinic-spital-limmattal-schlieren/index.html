The Limmattal Hospital thrives on interdisciplinary cooperation. The team members of the medical administration support the Ärzteschaft in all administrative matters and work closely together with the care and all other areas. The entire medical administration is under an overall leadership, which further strengthens and simplifies interdisciplinary cooperation. In addition, you are exactly right in the LIMMI if a family atmosphere and a modern workplace are important to you.

Working days at 60%: Monday, Wednesday and Thursday

Direct applications are preferred, external recruitment offices are not treated as a priority.

Here they engage

  • in the organisation and coordination of the office hours of the various medical disciplines (internal medicine, surgery, psychiatry, adipologists and nutritional counselling) of the obesity center
  • for the telephone operation and the processing of the e-mail box of the obesity center, including all KISIM mailboxes and triage processing
  • in blood sampling, injections, caloriemetry and BIA measurement, dressing change and thread removal during consultation hours
  • in appointment management (complex patient appointment organization, Ärzte session planning)
  • in direct patient contact during the consultation hours and patient advice for inquiries and appointments.
  • in the cooperation of scientific studies (coordination of studies)
  • Various administrative work
  • have completed vocational training as a Med. Practical assistant/EFZ
  • have an independent working method and are an organizational talent
  • Enjoy administrative activities
  • are an open, motivated and flexible personality
  • are a team player with high social skills
  • enjoy working with people from a wide variety of medical and nursing disciplines
  • have KISIM experience (advantageous)
  • Freight forward and precise working methods and are not afraid of high work volumes
  • Reduced employee parking
  • Motorcycle parking, lockable bicycle parking
  • With the ZVV Bonus Pass / Z-Bonus Pass we offer an attractive ÖV subscription for employees
