Welcome to the Medbase Zentrale Winterthur

Our modern office building is located just three minutes from Winterthur Train Station, in an urban setting that offers ideal conditions for focused work and creative collaboration. At our headquarters, around 150 specialists from the fields of IT, HR, marketing and finance work hand in hand to jointly develop innovative solutions and achieve our corporate goals. With flexible workplaces and modern meeting pods, we promote an open and efficient working culture. At Medbase, we attach great importance to teamwork and the exchange between departments in order to be successful together. Here, colleagues from our sister companies such as Zur Rose, WePractice or the dental center meet, which further enriches the interdisciplinary exchange.

Your tasks

  • Independent implementation of a correct and timely payroll in compliance with legal, regulatory and internal requirements
  • Responsibility for all pay-relevant mutations along the Employee Life Cycle (entry, withdrawal, contract changes, absences, etc.)
  • Technical handling of social security cases (EO, family allowances, maternity and paternity allowances, sickness allowance, accident insurance)
  • Preparation and control of withholding tax statements and support for audits and annual accounts
  • Advising employees, managers and HR business partners on issues relating to wages, social insurance and time management
  • Ensuring high data quality in HR and payroll systems
  • Cooperation in the further development and digitization of payroll processes as well as in system optimization

You bring that with you

  • Several years of experience in the payroll
  • In-depth knowledge of the Swiss social security and wage system
  • Experience with modern HR and payroll systems, ideally Abacus, Workday or comparable solutions
  • High affinity for digital processes, data quality and system applications
  • Analytical thinking and pleasure in questioning and improving processes
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Strong service orientation and enjoyment of working with different stakeholders
  • Very good knowledge of German, as well as French is advantageous
  • If applicable, basic commercial training and further training in the field of payroll or social security

About us

The Medbase Group operates more than 150 medical, pharmaceutical and dental facilities in Switzerland. In this way, she has greatly expanded her position as a leading provider of outpatient, integrated medicine in recent years. Around 3,200 employees look after patients holistically - from prevention to acute medicine and rehabilitation. More than 480 family and specialist doctors, 340 therapists, 190 pharmacists, around 360 dentists and specialists in orthodontics, implants and dental hygiene work together in a coordinated and interprofessional manner in the group. For companies, Medbase Corporate Health offers specific consulting and holistic projects in occupational health management. In its 12 Sports Medical Centers, Medbase supervises and treats athletes in leisure and elite sports. Medbase also provides junior researchers in the healthcare sector and trains around 340 young women and men.
