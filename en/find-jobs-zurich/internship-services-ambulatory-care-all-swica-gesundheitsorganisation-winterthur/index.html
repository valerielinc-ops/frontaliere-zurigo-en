Full description

We are committed to health – because health is everything. We are committed to this, also as an employer. We are convinced that only in a healthy working world do the best talents feel comfortable and advance: professionally and personally. We give everything for that. In this role, you will implement a concept operationally and work on its further development. In this way, you make an important contribution to quality assurance and further development of the decision-making basis in our services and medicine area. This is how you shape your health

  • Contacting service providers in writing and managing the resulting pending tasks
  • Independent formal review and processing of incoming feedback according to defined criteria
  • First point of contact for questions from service providers
  • Participation in evaluating the results and making recommendations for further development of the project
  • Documentation and preparation of results as a basis for controlling and evaluating the pilot project
  • Ensuring a uniform and comprehensible procedure
  • Organization and coordination of exchange appointments as well as active sharing of relevant findings within the team

This is what sets you apart

  • Ongoing bachelor's degree with a legal, political or economic background
  • Interest in health insurance issues, particularly in the areas of benefits and outpatient care services
  • Experience in writing professional correspondence and in written exchanges with various stakeholders
  • Willingness to learn, openness to new topics and the ability to delve into complex issues
  • Very good knowledge of spoken and written German

Why SWICA

  • We build on humanity. That's why we put openness, respect and trust at the center of our everyday work. We get involved, take responsibility and think of solutions – together, for our customers. This is also why we have been number 1 in customer satisfaction for many years.
  • We trust you and give you space. Annual working hours, at least 5 weeks of vacation, part-time models and a spacious home office within the scope of the position - you can structure your everyday life largely flexibly.
  • We value and reward your work with a market-based wage.
  • Because health is everything. With our company health management and region-specific offers, we take care of your well-being - so that you are healthy and energized.
