Role overview

Tasks

You serve and look after our discerning guests, patients and clinicians in the self-service restaurant and in the bistro. You are responsible for the correct operation of the cash register, including billing. You also manage the hot and cold buffet in the restaurant and carry out various cleaning and filling work. A lively environment with different guest structures awaits you. The service team works in various shifts from 06.30 to 19.30 (without room hour), every Monday to Friday. At the weekend the restoration is closed. In addition, we take over the catering service at congresses and further education events.

Requirements

  • Completed catering training EFZ
  • Friendly, reliable, flexible, helpful and capable of teamwork

We offer

  • Plannability of working hours and shifts
  • Attractive social benefits and numerous benefits
  • Paid or compensable changing time (15 min./day / 6.5 Fridays/year)

The Schulthess Clinic in Zurich is one of the leading orthopaedic clinics in Europe with over 1,100 employees. Our central concern is to free people from their pain and restore their mobility. As a specialist clinic, we focus on surgical orthopedics, neurology, rheumatology and sports medicine. Schulthess Clinic is Swiss Olympic Medical Center.
