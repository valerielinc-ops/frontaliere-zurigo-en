Coding, bytes and clouds are your world? During our two-year IT-Way-up internship, you will get to know our IT in its various areas. You acquire the necessary know-how to start directly after the internship.

We offer you a future-oriented, exciting and varied vocational training – your ideal start in the world of computer science. The Zürcher Kantonalbank is one of the largest training companies in the canton of Zurich and supports more than 420 apprentices every year. Would you like to be part of our Young Talents? We look forward to your application!

Your tasks

  • Support our application development teams in their daily tasks and exciting projects
  • Delving into the secrets of programming
  • Coordinating and discussing assignments with clients to develop, test and hand over programs
  • Developing programs used in the bank (e.g. eBanking) – including web applications
  • Working on future-oriented products and optimal solutions as well as contributing creative ideas and impulses
  • Documenting your orders and recording instructions for future users
  • Learn and deepen your knowledge in the vocational school and in intercompany courses
  • Participation in training and internal events to further develop your personal, social and methodological skills
  • Completion of a federally recognized school of maturity with a grade average of 4.65
  • Solving tricky and complex tasks and questions gives you pleasure
  • Learning new things and moving things give you joy
  • You are characterised by your strong personal responsibility and independence
  • You like to communicate, are open-minded and have fun working with others
  • Required application documents: application/motivation letter, CV, school certificates, optional further documents such as diplomas, etc.
  • Versatile insights into the different areas of our successful bank – your ideal career start
  • Engaged and motivated mentors accompany and support you comprehensively throughout your internship
  • Experienced practical trainers introduce you specifically to the world of computer science
  • Interesting and open teams looking forward to working with you
  • In-depth insight into our exciting "IT Made in Zurich Chreis 5"
  • Our young talents are important to us and therefore we actively promote you
  • Have fun and great colleagues – because without this an education is only half as beautiful
  • Benefit from other benefits such as the ZVV Bonus Pass, support for competitive sports, social events and much more
