Full description

Children are close to your heart. You enjoy making everyday life meaningful with them. You are important to inspire the children for new things and to promote them individually.

Tasks

  • You work in a shelter in an urban school, with children from kindergarten to school leaving.
  • You shape the everyday life of the children with various activities and plan smaller excursions.
  • You encourage the children to creative activities and active leisure activities.
  • You accompany the children in their development and deal with pedagogy.
  • You take on administrative tasks and make contact with the team and parents.

Profile

  • Compulsory schooling completed, middle or upper level
  • Joy in caring and home management tasks
  • Good oral and written German skills, at least language level B2 completed
  • Ability to Work With Others
  • Observation, empathy and resilience

We offer

You will receive a well-founded and varied training. Your professional educators will support you on your way to a successful apprenticeship. You are profiting from attractive employment conditions.

About us

The city of Zurich is divided into 7 school districts. The school district of Schwamendingen comprises the districts of Saatlen, Schwamendingen-Mitte and Hirzbach. All care offers (in the Hort) are assigned to one school and are guided by the management of the local care. There is a vocationally responsible care person per school, who is responsible for the training of the learners. This ad is exclusively for the 3-year apprenticeship.
