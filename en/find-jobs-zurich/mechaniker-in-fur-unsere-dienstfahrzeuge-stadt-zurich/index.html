Full description

We are the pulse of Zurich and keep the city in motion every 7.5 minutes. In order for this to work reliably and smoothly, a lot moves behind the scenes of the VBZ. Our colleagues work in over 50 professions: in technology, IT, administration, traffic development, offer planning, infrastructure and many other exciting areas of responsibility. Where we drive, Zurich lives. Come on!

Tasks

  • In addition to trams and buses, we have many more vehicles behind the scenes - in this exciting and extremely varied activity you have to deal with the whole spectrum
  • Because you are responsible for demanding maintenance and repair work, troubleshooting, diagnosis and commissioning on our diverse company car fleet and on our special vehicles (e.g. track cleaning vehicles, tower cars, vehicles of municipal third-party customers, etc.)
  • This also includes the maintenance, troubleshooting and repairs of passenger information systems on our VBZ trolley and diesel buses.
  • Every now and then you bring vehicles
  • You are also involved in new commissioning of our VBZ buses
  • In addition, you can also use the driver's license Kat. Acquire C and E
  • Your place of work is the Zentralwerkstatt in Zurich Altstetten and the well-established 6-member team is looking forward to your reinforcement!

Profile

  • Completed 4-year vocational apprenticeship as agricultural machinery mechanic or automotive mechatronic engineer or 3-year vocational apprenticeship as automotive specialist, preferably specialisation in heavy motor vehicles
  • Professional experience is desired, but not an absolute must
  • Cat. B and Cat. C and E, or willingness to acquire them
  • You like to learn and are very versatile and have the willingness and interest in the mandatory further training, such as working on trolley and battery buses
  • Good communication skills are required in this activity, because you have to consult and keep up to date in this lively business in the team.
  • Clean and careful work distinguishes your working style
  • You can be relied on and you like to work in a team and yet very independently

We offer

Discover the whole of Switzerland with the free GA – and the family also drives really well thanks to generous discounts. We want you to feel good: We are “Friendly Workspace” certified and do a lot for your health.

About us

With our vehicles we travel twice a day around the world and transport over 860,000 passengers. This means that every day, 365 days a year and almost around the clock we make an important contribution to the quality of life in one of the most beautiful cities in the world.
