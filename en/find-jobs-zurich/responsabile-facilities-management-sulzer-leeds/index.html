Full description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

This is an opportunity to shape and lead a significant transformation in how Facilities Management is delivered across our UK & Ireland operations.

Reporting to the Head of ESH, the Head of Facilities Management will lead the development of a more strategic, consistent and mature approach to FM across a complex, multi-site engineering organisation.

We are bringing together Facilities Management activity across different divisions and legal entities and transitioning towards a first-generation outsourced FM model. This is therefore much more than a traditional FM leadership role. You will help establish the operating model, bring stakeholders together around a common approach and build a strong, collaborative partnership between our business and our chosen FM provider.

Our ambition is to move beyond reactive facilities management and create a function that actively supports statutory compliance, operational performance, asset lifecycle planning, capital investment and the long-term development of our estate.

Success will depend as much on people and relationships as it does on technical FM expertise. You will work with stakeholders across different businesses and locations, some of whom will be working together in this way for the first time. We are looking for an engaging leader who can listen, influence, build trust and bring people with them through change. Key Responsibilities

  • Lead the development and implementation of the UK & Ireland Facilities Management strategy, aligned with wider business objectives.
  • Line Management of a team of two Facilities Managers, working cross-divisionally to manage stakeholders
  • Lead the transition to a first-generation outsourced FM operating model, ensuring the business is effectively prepared for and supported through the change.
  • Establish a strong and collaborative relationship with our outsourced FM partner, creating a true business partnership rather than a transactional client/contractor relationship.
  • Bring together stakeholders from across different divisions, legal entities and locations, creating alignment around common FM standards, priorities, responsibilities and ways of working.
  • Establish clear governance, KPIs and reporting mechanisms that provide visibility of compliance, performance, risk, cost and improvement opportunities.
  • Lead the strategic approach to statutory and regulatory facilities compliance across the estate, ensuring clear ownership, effective assurance and timely management of remedial actions.
  • Develop a multi-year FM and asset lifecycle roadmap, covering planned maintenance, asset condition, compliance, CAPEX requirements, sustainability and workplace improvements.
  • Help the business move from reactive maintenance towards a more planned, risk-based and data-led approach to managing its buildings and assets.
  • Work closely with business and site leaders to understand operational requirements and identify opportunities for estate optimisation, efficiency and future investment.
  • Ensure FM insight informs CAPEX and longer-term business planning, enabling investment decisions to be based on asset condition, lifecycle, compliance risk and operational need.
  • Oversee planned and reactive maintenance programmes across manufacturing, engineering and office environments.
  • Manage FM operational budgets and capital expenditure programmes, ensuring appropriate commercial control and long-term value.
  • Drive continuous improvement across the FM model, using performance data, stakeholder feedback and supplier expertise to continually improve service delivery.
