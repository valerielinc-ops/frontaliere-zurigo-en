The Possibility We are looking for a dynamic and experienced executive for purchasing that strengthens our dedicated service team at Hitachi, Ltd., where speed and quality are of the utmost importance. This allows you to take over the leadership, promotion and further development of our purchasing team and create a performance-oriented and responsible corporate culture. They develop and implement innovative procurement concepts in accordance with our service strategy. In doing so, you place particular emphasis on timely delivery, continuous quality improvement, efficiency improvement in the supply chain and sustainable cost reduction. They work closely with cross-departmental teams such as material planning, engineering, quality and controlling, in order to optimally tune the purchase to the corporate requirements. They control purchase-related risks, including supply chain disruptions, price fluctuations and compliance issues. This brings you with completed studies in the field of supply chain management, business management, engineering or comparable training with relevant professional experience. Very good understanding of purchasing processes, contract law and principles of the supply chain. Impressed analytical skills as well as problem-solving and decision-making.
