If resilience, willingness to change and teamwork are not just keywords for you, but a matter of course, we look forward to getting to know YOU personally. Are you adding to our administration team? A highly motivated, committed, collegial team in the university environment awaits you!

Your main tasks

  • Planning, coordination, recording and confirmation of outpatient appointments for patients.
  • Administrative preparation of office hours
  • Telephone and written contact with patients, assigning doctors, hospitals and internal offices of the USZ
  • Close cooperation with clinic manager, doctors, nursing and other interdisciplinary and external services
  • Responsibility and supervision of an HMS intern (KV)

You bring that with you

  • Commercial or equivalent basic training, ideally with professional experience in the medical field
  • Training as a vocational trainer:in or willing to complete the course internally
  • Reliable, discreet and team capable personality as well as friendly and courteous appearance
  • Strong organisational talent and high resilience
  • Very good knowledge of the common MS-Office products as well as the willingness to familiarize yourself with the clinic-specific software (KISIM).
  • Style-confident German in word and writing, further language skills of advantage
