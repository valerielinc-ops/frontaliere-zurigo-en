Original description

Are you ready to shape the future of how over 17,000 people work, connect, and collaborate every day? If you are passionate about modern endpoint engineering and thrive in a cloud-first, security-conscious environment, this could be the perfect role for you!

About the Role

We're looking for a passionate and skilled Modern Workplace Engineer to join our Digital Workplace team in Madrid. In this role, you'll be a key driver in evolving our endpoint management landscape towards a cloud-native, Intune-first approach, ensuring our 17,000+ employees stay productive, secure, and connected wherever they work. If you love building modern, automated endpoint solutions and have a keen eye for security, we'd love to meet you!

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, deploy and manage modern endpoint solutions using Microsoft Intune, Windows Autopilot and cloud-native management tools across the organisation.
  • Lead the evolution of endpoint management from traditional on-premise approaches towards a cloud-first, Intune-driven architecture.
  • Implement and maintain endpoint security using Microsoft Defender for Endpoint, Conditional Access policies and Zero Trust principles.
  • Automate endpoint provisioning, configuration and compliance using PowerShell scripting and Microsoft Graph API.
  • Manage identity and access across Azure AD / Entra ID, including SSO, MFA and Conditional Access policy management.
  • Support and optimise Microsoft 365 Apps deployment, lifecycle management and end-user experience.
  • Provide expert troubleshooting and support for complex endpoint issues, working collaboratively with the team to diagnose, resolve and document solutions effectively.
  • Collaborate with the agile Endpoints team to develop and integrate new workplace features, products and services.
  • Champion Swiss Re's healthy and transparent risk culture by actively engaging in continuous risk accountability activities.

About the Team

We are a leading organisation committed to enhancing our digital workplace to support over 17,000 employees globally. Our goal is to provide seamless connectivity and collaboration through innovative technology solutions.

The Digital Workplace (DWP) team is a dynamic, forward-thinking group dedicated to enabling seamless connectivity and collaboration for all employees. We embrace agile methodologies and leadership practices to drive innovation and excellence across the organisation. We are a team of technology enthusiasts who are passionate about making a real difference, and we'd love for you to be part of that mission!

About You

You are a modern endpoint enthusiast who is excited about the shift to cloud-native workplace management. You combine deep technical expertise with a security-first mindset and a genuine passion for automation. You apply strong engineering practices in everything you do, from writing clean, maintainable PowerShell scripts to structuring scalable endpoint configurations and approaching problems with a DevOps mindset. You value continuous improvement, peer collaboration, and building solutions that are not only effective today but resilient and adaptable for the future. You thrive in agile environments and take pride in delivering high-quality, well-documented outcomes.

What We Are Looking For

  • 3+ years of hands-on experience with Microsoft Intune as the primary endpoint management tool, including device configuration, compliance policies and application deployment.
  • Proven expertise with Windows Autopilot for zero-touch device provisioning and deployment in enterprise environments.
  • Solid experience with Microsoft Defender for Endpoint and endpoint security management, including threat and vulnerability management and security baselines.
  • Strong proficiency in Azure AD / Entra ID, including Conditional Access policies, SSO, MFA and identity-driven endpoint security.
  • Hands-on PowerShell scripting experience, including automation via Microsoft Graph API for endpoint management tasks.

Additional Nice-to-Haves

  • Experience with SCCM (System Center Configuration Manager), particularly in hybrid environments, and familiarity with transitioning workloads from SCCM to Intune.
  • Experience with co-management (SCCM + Intune) and familiarity with transitioning from on-premise to cloud-native endpoint management.
  • Knowledge of Windows 365 and/or Azure Virtual Desktop environments.
  • Familiarity with Windows Autopatch for automated update management.
  • Experience managing Microsoft 365 Apps lifecycle, including deployment, updates and user experience optimisation.
  • Understanding of Zero Trust architecture principles and their application in endpoint management.
  • Experience with Microsoft Azure DevOps or similar CI/CD tools for endpoint configuration management.
  • University degree or equivalent qualification in engineering or a related field.
