Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Vocational Advice School social worker/primary school Obfelden temporarily 70% Good for life – that is our motto. The Office for Youth and Vocational Counselling Canton of Zurich, office of the districts of Affoltern, Dietikon, Horgen, runs a regional office for school social work. On behalf of schools, she is responsible for the personnel, professional and administrative management of school social workers. In order to ensure a low-threshold school social work offer, the jobs are located in the individual school communities. For the primary school Obfelden we are looking from 1 January 2027 to 28. February 2027 for a representation of a school social worker. Together with your teammate, you advise and accompany young people, parents and teachers in educational and social matters. In the area of child protection and child welfare, you are familiar. In crises and conflicts, you perform interventions in classes and groups. As far as possible, you also do prevention and project work. In addition, you will work with the school team and other specialist departments. For this task, we require a completed education in social work at the university of applied sciences and several years of professional experience in school social work or in the socio-educational field with children and adolescents. They have competences in consulting, prevention, crisis intervention and project work as well as an independent and interdisciplinary working method.

  • We offer you a versatile, interesting and self-employed activity with annual working hours (compensation of school holiday time due to increased workload during school time). You receive support from a professional, collegial team and you can expect lively exchange and networking with other school social workers. The employment conditions correspond to the cantonal guidelines. Martin Graf, Head of Regional Office for School Social Work, Tel. 043 259 92 39. Additional information can be found at www.ajb.zh.ch.
