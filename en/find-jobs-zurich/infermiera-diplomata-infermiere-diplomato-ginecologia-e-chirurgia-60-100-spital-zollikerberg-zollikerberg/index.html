Their activities

  • Our station covers the entire operational spectrum of the Zollikerberg Hospital. From general surgery to highly specialized spinal surgery, plastic surgery and surgical gynecology from which exciting tasks derive
  • In addition to patient-centered care, our nurses need competences in medical technology

Become part of the Department of Emergency and Acute Medicine at the Zollikerberg Hospital. Under one roof, we combine internal medicine, surgery, nephrology with dialysis center, the emergency department, the eye center, the medical ambulatorium, the day clinic and other specialized areas. The Clinic for Internal Medicine looks after more than 2,500 inpatients every year, the Clinic for Surgery over 1,500. With more than 6,000 emergency consultations and over 450 patients in the interdisciplinary intensive care unit, we offer a versatile and demanding medical work environment.

In our surgical team, you can expect a varied field of activity in the disciplines of general surgery, orthopaedics, spinal surgery, gynecology and urology. We work according to the reference care system and are convinced that the best solutions are created where employees contribute their experience, take responsibility and actively shape change. Would you like to shape the future of surgical care together with us?
