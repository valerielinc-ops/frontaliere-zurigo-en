Tasks

  • Independent implementation and organisation of the Institute’s central autoclaving and sterilisation service and maintenance of laboratory glassware
  • Maintenance of laboratory infrastructure and general equipment maintenance, including inspection and maintenance of laboratory equipment and machinery and ordering of laboratory materials
  • Contact person and support for new equipment purchases and replacement purchases
  • Coordination of maintenance, service, calibration and repairs of laboratory equipment
  • Responsible for chemicals and equipment inventory
  • Laboratory safety support (biosafety level 1 and 2) and logistics and quality management assistance (ESF and SAMV concept) and chemical safety support
  • Ensuring operational hygiene in laboratory areas (including disposal logistics and handling of biological and chemical laboratory waste)
  • Support for administrative tasks in laboratory operations as well as maintenance of SOPs and laboratory documentation
  • Assistance in the training of new laboratory staff, including briefings on safety and biosecurity regulations and laboratory tour

Requirements

  • Completed training as a laboratory EFZ or comparable training as well as experience in the academic environment (university research laboratory)
  • Several years of professional experience, ideally in an academic research environment
  • Experience in the operation of autocalves as well as in biological and chemical disposal logistics
  • Knowledge of biosecurity, chemical safety, and BSL2 guidelines is of great advantage
  • Independent, reliable and structured working method with strong organisational skills
  • Communication and teamwork as well as service orientation for dealing with several research groups
  • Good IT User Knowledge (MS Office); Experience with laboratory or inventory management systems
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English
  • Confidentiality and demand for high-quality work as well as enjoyment of practical work in laboratory operations
