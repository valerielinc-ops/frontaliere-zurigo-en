Full description

Create a better #TomorrowWithUs Want to help shape the future of the railway world together with us? At Siemens Mobility, we are the leading provider of mobility and are constantly expanding our innovative portfolio, which includes rail vehicles, rail automation, electrification solutions, complete rail systems and accompanying services. With around 800 specialist employees in Switzerland and 39 600 employees worldwide, we shape mobility with passion and are one step ahead of our time. In the exciting field of railway automation, you actively shape the infrastructure of our railway control systems. You will accompany new solutions from the concept phase through development and piloting to customer introduction. You will work closely with development, product management, project teams and service operations, helping to deliver future-proof, secure and highly available OT infrastructures. Your tasks

  • Development of infrastructure and architecture concepts as well as technical responsibility in development and pilot projects from offer to introduction
  • Planning, design, implementation, testing and commissioning of highly available OT infrastructures and IP networks for the Iltis Traffic Management System (TMS)
  • Establishment, configuration and continuous development of server, virtualization and network infrastructures
  • Analysis of existing OT environments as well as development of optimization and standardization measures
  • Implementation and further development of cybersecurity measures in the OT environment
  • Cooperation in the further development of infrastructure standards, tools and automation solutions
  • Takeover of piketting services (4 to 6 weeks per year) as well as occasional on-site assignments as part of customer projects and commissioning Your talents and skills
  • Completed studies (HF/FH/ETH) or comparable training in computer science, systems engineering or OT
  • Several years of experience in several areas such as networks, virtualization, Windows Server, OT systems or cybersecurity
  • Experience with network technologies, preferably Cisco
  • Enjoying the combination of architecture, engineering, product launch and project work
  • Independent, structured, process-oriented and responsible working methods
  • Readiness for piketting and occasional commissioning operations
  • Very good knowledge of German and very good knowledge of English, other national languages are a plus that characterizes the role you are the technical interface between product management, development, project management and service operations. You ensure that new solutions can not only be developed, but also successfully tested, documented, piloted and introduced to the customer. This will make a decisive contribution to the further development of our railway automation solutions and their successful use in the field. You benefit from
  • an open and flexible working culture at one of the leading companies in the field of mobility in Switzerland
  • a 40-hour week on a 100% fee
  • at least 5 weeks of holidays
  • a workplace at the pulse of railway technology
  • Various employee benefits and training opportunities
  • a hybrid working model: balanced work-life balance and more autonomy Our team and location You want information about our culture, our values, diversity and other important topics? The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
