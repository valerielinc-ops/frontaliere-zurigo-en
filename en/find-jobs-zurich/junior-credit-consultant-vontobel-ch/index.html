Role overview

Real Estate Financing Solutions, based in Zurich, is the Center of Excellence for mortgages and real estate financing and is centrally responsible for this business area across the entire Private Clients Unit.

The mortgage business is an integral part of the bank’s holistic advisory approach for high-net-worth private clients, complementing its existing investment and wealth management offering.

As a Real Estate Financing Solutions Manager, you play a key role in building and further developing the Financing Advisory & Structuring function.
