IKMZ - Institute for Communication Science and Media Research

Job opening 1 March or by arrangement, temporary

At the Institute of Communication Science and Media Research at the University of Zurich (IKMZ), two positions as doctoral candidates are in the research project "Social Media Use and Self-Effects on Adolescents' Physical Activity": To be filled in March 2027 or by agreement. The project is led by Prof. Dr. Thomas Friemel and Dr. Michelle Möri. The focus is on the influence of creating and sharing your own media content on the health behavior of young people (especially in the field of sports and fitness). With the analysis of these “self-effects”, the project extends the classical perspective of media impact research, which has so far focused on reception effects. An important goal of the project is to counter negative effects (e.g. negative body image, excessive training) with appropriate measures. Initially, the positions are limited to four years and are held in the Department of Media Use and Media Impact (www.mediennutzung.ch), which deals with health-relevant aspects of media use and media impact in research and teaching. With its approximately 80 employees, the IKMZ is one of the largest communication science institutes in Europe and provides an excellent infrastructure for research and teaching, an inspiring academic environment and excellent employment conditions. The University of Zurich also offers numerous opportunities for further education and a wide range of interdisciplinary cooperation opportunities.

Tasks

  • Collaboration in the research project and cooperation in other projects in the context of the Department of Media Use and Media Impact
  • Publishing in leading journals and presentations at international conferences
  • Scientific qualification (doctoral degree)
  • Research transfer

The employment takes place in accordance with the specifications of the University of Zurich and the Swiss National Science Fund.

Profile

  • Master in Communication Science or an adjacent research field that is of direct importance for the research project outlined (e.g. psychology)
  • Sound competences in empirical social research (especially quantitative methods)
  • Ability to work both independently and in a team in a challenging research project
  • Very good knowledge of German (due to the data collection at Swiss-German schools) and knowledge of English
