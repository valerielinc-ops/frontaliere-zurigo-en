Full description

The activity includes coordinating workflows and independently solving problems. The company offers a family environment and diverse tasks. To strengthen our customer we are looking for a Activities

  • Coordinate and control workflows and independently develop solutions to problems
  • Employees in the locksmith shop are available as a professional contact person
  • weld independently steel, aluminium and chromium steel according to plan specifications
  • Welding in accordance with ISO EN 1090 and EN 1085
  • Perform quality controls based on QM guidelines and technical drawings

Requirements

  • Completed training as a production mechanic, plant or apparatus manufacturer or in-depth welding experience
  • Knowledge of various welding processes and experience with steel, chromium steel and aluminium
  • ideal welding specialist with VT1+2 visual inspector training
  • Experience in sheet metal processing and technical understanding of drawings
  • good knowledge of German in words and writing as well as characteristics such as teamwork, flexibility, reliability and self-responsible work

Offer

  • Various activities in a family-run company
  • Attractive working conditions and a pleasant working atmosphere
