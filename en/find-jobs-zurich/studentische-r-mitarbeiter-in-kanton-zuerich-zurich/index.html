The Office for Landscape and Nature (ALN) performs a wide range of forward-looking tasks for the Canton of Zurich in the areas of agriculture and forestry, nature and soil protection, sustainable food systems, as well as hunting and fishing. We are looking for immediately or by agreement a student employee who supports our 4-member office lawyer team. Your tasks

  • Independent management of the coordination activities of the ALN to other offices and directorates (e.g. time-limit control, merging of opinions, control, first adjustment)
  • Support of the legal service in various legal matters (e.g. creating legal interpretation regulations, research in literature and jurisprudence)
  • Preparation of co-reports of the ALN and balancing of interests
  • Support within the framework of legislative projects (especially legal clarifications, drafting of reports, evaluation of consultations) and political transactions as well as div. Administrative tasks Your profile
  • Bachelor or Master student in Law
  • Independent, careful and initiative working methods and interest in the field of activity of the ALN
  • Enjoyment of complex legal issues and independent solution finding
  • Structured thinking and editorial flair as well as organizational skills
  • Confident handling of MS Office programs as well as flexibility, teamwork and reliability We offer
