About the role

Job Summary The role is responsible for developing and managing production schedules to ensure timely delivery of products. This role coordinates with procurement, manufacturing, and logistics to optimize resource utilization and meet customer demand. Key Responsibilities

  • Create and maintain production schedules based on demand forecasts.
  • Coordinate with supply chain and manufacturing teams.

  • Analyze capacity and resource constraints.
  • Implement planning tools and systems.
  • Report planning metrics and performance.
  • Support continuous improvement initiatives.

  • Experience with ERP and MRP systems.
  • Analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent organizational and communication abilities.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities.
