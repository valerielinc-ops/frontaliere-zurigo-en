Occupation by: immediately or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 42050

Do you want to contribute your expertise in the operating room and at the same time grow in a specialized, modern environment? Our Plastic Surgery Group offers you the opportunity to become part of a dedicated and interdisciplinary team that lives the highest quality and patient-centered care. With us, you can expect a varied working day with exciting interventions, space for further development and the opportunity to deepen your specialist knowledge. If you want to take on responsibility and value a working environment in which team spirit, professionalism and mutual support are at the heart of everything, then you are at the right place.

YOUR TASKS

  • Instrumentation and service in operations as well as implementation of all pre- and post-operation measures in the specialist group with focus on robotics
  • Technical and professional preparation and operation of the necessary equipment, instruments and sterile goods
  • Monitoring and care of patients in local anesthesia during procedures
  • Documentation of all nursing activities including performance recording
  • Assistance in ensuring a smooth daily routine in the operating room
  • Instrumentation at HSM Surgery

Your profile

  • Training for the specialist surgical technique HF or Dipl. Medical professional FA OP
  • Knowledge of robotics
  • Whether generalist or specialist, with us you will find what suits you.
  • You are a team player and open and respectful communication is just as important to you as it is to us.
  • Your own initiative and commitment are a matter of course for you
  • High motivation and dedication as well as willingness to perform weekend/picquet as well as night service

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, a cool team awaits you with open arms, so that you quickly feel "at home" and like to show you a lot. We offer you individual and tailor-made training as well as flexible working time models that enable you to combine work and family. Re-entrants are also welcome.
