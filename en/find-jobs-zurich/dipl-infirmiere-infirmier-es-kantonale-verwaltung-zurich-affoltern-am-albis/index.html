Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Prison and Reintegration Dipl. Nursing professional HF 80 - 100% With around 1,400 employees, JuWe is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as for the execution of various types of detention. Our employees from around 40 professions support and promote the reintegration of delinquents and work effectively and progressively for a safe and just society. The Medical Service is one of eight main departments of JuWe and as a central organization responsible for the medical-somatic basic care of all detained persons in the institutions of the Canton of Zurich. It ensures medical care from initial examination and treatment to emergency care and accompanies the detained people in everyday life with health issues. As part of the Zurich prisons (VEZ), the prison in Affoltern am Albis (GFA) carries out prison sentences and remaining sentences of up to 18 months and has 65 detention places. With a reintegration-oriented approach, group enforcement, basic education and work opportunities, detainees are prepared for re-entry into society.
