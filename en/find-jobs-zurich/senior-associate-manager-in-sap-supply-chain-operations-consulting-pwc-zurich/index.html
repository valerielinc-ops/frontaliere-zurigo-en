Your impact

  • You work closely with our customers on their supply chain processes, introduce the SAP standard processes and carry out fit to standard workshops. You define the most optimal solution for our customers.
  • Help our customers integrate modern technologies such as IoT or predictive analytics into their S/4HANA solutions.
  • Depending on your role, you lead a small team of consultants in demanding and dynamic SAP transformation programs.
  • You will share your knowledge in the team and expand your skills through targeted training as part of your personal development plan.
  • Become part of a growing, dynamic team that combines SAP innovation with industry expertise and thrive in an environment based on teamwork, trust and mutual support.

Your skill set

  • A completed degree (Bachelor/Master/Diplom) in Business Administration, IT, Engineering, Business Informatics or a digital discipline.
  • At least 2 years of experience in process consulting and implementation of SAP solutions, ideally with deep expertise in SAP supply chain and logistics modules as well as a deep understanding of the associated business processes.
  • Creating functional designs is not a foreign word for you and you bring experience in SAP customizing.
  • An innovative mindset
  • Experience with GenAI and cloud technologies is an advantage.
  • Strong analytical skills, a “can-do” attitude and fluent German and English skills.
  • Flexibility for assignments in other regions of Switzerland and/or abroad as well as a valid work permit.
