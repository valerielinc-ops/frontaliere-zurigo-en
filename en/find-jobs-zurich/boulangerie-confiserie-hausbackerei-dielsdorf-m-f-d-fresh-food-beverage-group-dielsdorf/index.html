Full description

We produce favorite products that inspire – in Switzerland and for Switzerland. Are you an early riser with a passion for baking? Do you love to create something with your hands that makes people happy? Then come to our bakery and become part of a team that works every day with dedication and creativity!

Mansioni

  • You are jointly responsible for the production of our versatile baked goods
  • Monitoring production and carrying out dough, table and oven work
  • You implement the quality and hygiene requirements and are the ambassador of the products to our customers

Requirements

  • Apprentices are also welcome: Bakers
  • Completed Vocational Apprenticeship (EFZ): Bakers
  • Readiness for flexible working hours and Saturday work
  • Joy at work as well as a reliable working method and resilience even in hectic situations
  • German (flowing)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Employee offers: Employee Points Cumulus, Discount Mobile Subscriptions, Higher Interest Rates Migros Bank, Club School/Fitness contributions and more.
  • Occupational pension provision: Comprehensively secured in the Migros pension fund – with first-class and attractive services.
  • Holidays: 5 weeks holiday for all, 6 weeks holiday from 50 years of age, 7 weeks holiday from 60 years of age, additional purchase of additional holiday days possible.
  • Education and training: We actively train employees as part of internal programs and, if necessary, support external training with time and money.
  • Migros Bank: Higher interest rates on personnel investment accounts at Migros Bank.
  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks paid maternity leave (instead of 14 weeks), 4 weeks paternity leave (instead of 2 weeks).
