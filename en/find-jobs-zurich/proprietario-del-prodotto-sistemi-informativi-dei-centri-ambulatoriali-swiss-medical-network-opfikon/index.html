Role overview

The outpatient centres (from the family practice to the medical center) are the first link of integrated care.

The digitisation of these centres is a key to optimizing Swiss healthcare.

As Product Owner, you are the link between end users and suppliers in terms of contract and product development and represent the outpatient centers in relation to the software suppliers as a central contact person.

  • Experience in the health care sector in Switzerland or great will to learn.
