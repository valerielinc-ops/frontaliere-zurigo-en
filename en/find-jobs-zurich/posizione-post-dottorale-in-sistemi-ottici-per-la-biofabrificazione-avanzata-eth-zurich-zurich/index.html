Postdoctoral Position in Optical Systems for Advanced Biofabrication

100%, Zurich, fixed-term

The Tissue Engineering and Biofabrication Laboratory (Prof. Marcy Zenobi-Wong) at ETH Zurich develops light-based 3D bioprinting techniques to engineer functional tissues with multi-scale porosity and topological guidance. Embedded within the Department of Health Sciences and Technology (D-HEST) and the Institute for Biomechanics, our group bridges biomedical engineering, materials science and biology. We are seeking an Optical Systems/Photonics Engineer to design and build light-delivery systems that shape light profiles to control hydrogel architecture.

Job description

  • Design, simulate, and build custom optical setups and beam-shaping trains
  • Characterize how structured light fields interact with photosensitive polymers
  • Collaborate within an interdisciplinary team of bioengineers, chemists, and biologists to translate photonic concepts into engineered scaffolds for regenerative medicine

Profile

  • PhD in Optical Engineering, Photonics, Applied Physics, Electrical Engineering (optics emphasis), or a related discipline
  • Hands-on experience with laser beam shaping, phase modulation
  • Software development for optical modeling and numerical analysis (Python, MATLAB, or equivalent)
  • Strong academic output relative to career stage, evidenced by high-quality publications or working papers
  • Interest in translating optical physics towards products useful in tissue engineering
  • Strong motivation to collaborate with interdisciplinary scientists
  • Excellent written and spoken English

Experience in one or more of the following areas is advantageous:

  • Multi-photon polymerization for optical devices
  • Meta-lens/meta-surfaces
  • Adaptable optics
  • Non-diffractive beam design
