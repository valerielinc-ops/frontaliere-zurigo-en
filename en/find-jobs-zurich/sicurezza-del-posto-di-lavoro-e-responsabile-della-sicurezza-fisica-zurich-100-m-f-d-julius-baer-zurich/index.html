Full description

At Julius Baer, we celebrate and value the individual qualities you bring, enabling you to be impactful, to be entrepreneurial, to be empowered, and to create value beyond wealth. Let’s shape the future of wealth management together.

As Workplace Safety & Physical Security Officer, you assume responsibility for Workplace Safety and health protection within the framework of Julius Baer Switzerland. They ensure compliance with regulatory requirements, further develop existing security standards and promote a sustainable security culture within the bank. In addition, you support the physical security organization with location-related security issues and projects.

Your call

  • Responsibility for workplace safety, health and physical security in Switzerland
  • Ensuring compliance with legal and regulatory requirements, especially as part of the EKAS/ASA model solution
  • Implementation of risk analyses, safety inspections and audits in the area of workplace safety
  • Development and implementation of appropriate risk mitigation and prevention measures
  • Responsibility for emergency, evacuation and alerting concepts as well as their regular review and further development
  • Planning and conducting training, awareness campaigns and emergency exercises
  • Advising management and employees in all matters of occupational safety and physical safety
  • Management of external partners and cooperation with authorities, landlords and specialist bodies
  • Support for the Physical Security Organization in topics such as building security, site protection, security concepts and security-relevant projects
  • Analysis of incidents and derivation of appropriate prevention measures

YOUR PROFILE

  • Several years of professional experience in the field of occupational safety, corporate security or a comparable environment
  • In-depth knowledge of Swiss requirements in the field of occupational safety and health protection
  • Recognised training as a specialist in ASGS or equivalent qualification
  • Practical knowledge in the field of physical security, in particular building security, event protection, security systems and emergency management
  • Experience in carrying out risk analysis, audits and training
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management capabilities
  • Independent, pragmatic and solution-oriented working method
  • Very good German and English skills

Benefits

  • Experience in banking or financial services environment
  • Physical safety and fire safety education or training
  • IVR First Aid Level 3 or equivalent qualification
  • French language skills
