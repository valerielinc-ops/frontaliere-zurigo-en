Full description

For strategic real estate development, we are looking for a dynamic personality who wants to shape the future of the hospital. You work on real estate development and construction projects and ensure that the construction projects are aligned with the hospital’s strategy and the needs of the users. You can gradually familiarize yourself with challenging strategic real estate and construction issues and take on responsibility.

Tasks

  • Cooperation in the strategic development of the real estate portfolio as well as in the development and regular review of the real estate strategy
  • Support in the development of area strategies, master planning and strategic real estate planning
  • Participation in challenging real estate and construction projects – from the first idea to and with feasibility tests until the transfer to execution
  • Support in the development and implementation of a holistic spatial strategy as well as in the development of a strategic spatial management
  • Development of usage concepts and spatial programmes for new buildings and existing properties as well as implementation and preparation of analyses, feasibility studies, test planning and competitions
  • Development of decision bases and presentations for project managers and management as well as coordination and cooperation with internal and external specialist departments and stakeholders

Profile

  • Master's degree (uni/FH) in business administration, architecture or in a similar direction - advantageous additional training in the field of real estate
  • First professional experience in real estate and construction projects – ideally in hospital construction or in construction activities in the public sector
  • Interest in the specific requirements of the health care system and hospital-specific operations
  • Strong conceptual and analytical skills as well as the pleasure of preparing complex issues in a structured and comprehensible way. Solution-oriented, structured and reliable working method with a high willingness to learn and initiative
  • Ability to work independently and at the same time enjoy working together in an interdisciplinary and team-oriented manner. Strength of communication, confident appearance and pleasure in working with different stakeholders
  • Strong interest in strategic real estate development issues as well as the willingness to familiarize themselves with challenging topics and complex organizations

Benefits: Work and private life should fit together. Therefore, the KSW offers you – where the company makes it possible – flexible and flexible working hours, home office, generous maternity leave, paternity leave and employment levels that can be adapted to different phases of life. Develop yourself further professionally and personally: Every year, around 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses are open to you at KSW. We also support external training – temporally and/or financially.

  • Sometimes it needs support. In the event of personal stress, prolonged illness or difficult experiences in your professional or private life, various confidential discussions are available at KSW.
  • At KSW we work together and for each other. Appreciation, open and respectful communication and cooperation across professional groups and departments shape our everyday work. You should be able to contribute with your ideas and strengths and develop. Your future is also ensured: You benefit from very good social benefits and a pension fund solution with BVK, which is significantly above the legal minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, you are very well covered as a KSW employee.
  • Your health is important to us. Benefit from discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training and courses as well as discounts in pharmacies and selected health insurance companies.
  • Family and work can be well connected with us. The daycare center La Luna with spacious opening hours is right next to the KSW. In addition, the KSW – depending on income – financially supports you with the crib costs.

There is also something to discover outside of work: Thanks to our cooperation with cultural partners in Winterthur and the surrounding area, you regularly benefit from discounts for concerts and theatre performances.
