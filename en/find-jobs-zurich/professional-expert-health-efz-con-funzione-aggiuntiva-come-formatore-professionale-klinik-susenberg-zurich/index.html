Full description

Come join us and experience a work environment where appreciation, team spirit and joy in work are at the core! Effective immediately or by arrangement we are looking for reinforcement of a health professional (FAGE) 80 % – 100 % with the additional role as a trainer/educator. The Klinik Susenberg in Zurich is a small specialty clinic with 34 beds in an idyllic location on the Zürichberg. We accompany our patients holistically in acute geriatrics, rehabilitative care and Palliative Care, always with a patient‑centered approach. Klinik Susenberg holds the label “Quality in Palliative Care” from qualitépalliativeⓆ.

Your

  • Tasks with us:
  • You support our nursing team in daily work and perform delegated nursing as well as administrative tasks.
  • You work closely with qualified nursing professionals and contribute to high‑quality patient care.
  • You actively support, accompany and advise our trainees and students in their professional daily life.
  • You ensure a clear and efficient flow of information within the interdisciplinary team.
  • You actively contribute to the collaboration of various professional groups.

Your profile

  • Completed training as a health professional (FAGE) EFZ or equivalent.
  • Experience in acute care and as a trainer/educator is an advantage.
  • You are committed to team‑oriented and interdisciplinary collaboration and meet new challenges openly and positively.
  • Willingness to work shifts and weekends (no night shifts).
  • You are innovative, creative, flexible and resilient.
  • Very good German and PC user skills; KISIM knowledge is an advantage.
  • Career re‑entrants are warmly welcome.

Why you will love it with us

  • Health‑promoting measures as well as targeted further and continuing education opportunities.
  • Modern remuneration and a balanced work‑life balance.
  • Attractive perks for a better quality of life: fine Birchermüesli during breaks, daily fresh menu selection at discounted prices, free drinks and various employee events.
  • A family‑like work environment with close exchange in the interdisciplinary team.
  • Good public transport connection from Zürich HB and contribution to mobility costs.

For further information, Shkendie Doga, Deputy Head of Nursing, phone 044 268 38 69, is happy to assist you. Please send your complete application documents with photo to: bewerbung@susenbergklinik.ch. Only direct applications will be considered.
