Full description

At the Department of Urology at USZ, you work in a highly specialized environment that offers state-of-the-art diagnostics and innovative therapies for the entire urological spectrum – from prostate diseases to minimally invasive, robot-assisted interventions. As a central hub, you ensure a smooth organization of the consultation hours and thus directly contribute to optimal patient care. You are part of an interdisciplinary, dedicated team at one of the leading urological centers in Switzerland. >The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks:

  • Efficient and demanding planning, coordination and scheduling of outpatient appointments (telephone and written appointments)
  • Patient reception and support at the reception
  • Handling of day-to-day business (telephone, post and e-mail)
  • Close cooperation with doctors, hospitals and other interfaces of the Urological Clinic
  • Professional written and oral correspondence with different stakeholders (patients, referees, hospitals and internal interfaces of the USZ)
  • Assumption of other general administrative and organisational tasks
  • Assumption of representation in absence Requirements
  • Completed commercial or medical education or training as medical and / or hospital secretary
  • Several years of professional experience in the field of hospital administration are desired
  • Openness to New Willingness to Continuously Improve Existing Processes
  • Reliable, team-ready, flexible and service-oriented personality who sees it as a challenge to master a lively hospital day in a team
  • Very good IT knowledge (MS-Office, KISIM of advantage)
  • Stylistic German and English in word and writing, further language skills in French and Italian are advantageous We offer: You can expect a versatile and interesting job in an advanced working environment. A motivated team supports you in all respects. If you want to work in a top medical environment where you are challenged and supported, we look forward to receiving your application.
