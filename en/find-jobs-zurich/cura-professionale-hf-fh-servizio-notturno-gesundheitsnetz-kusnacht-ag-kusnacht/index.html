Gesundheitsnetz Küsnacht AG (GNK), with the Tägerhalde and Wangensbach residential and health centers housing around 130 residents, as well as a day care center, Spitex, senior living and counseling services, and a total of around 250 employees, is a nonprofit public limited company wholly owned by the municipality of Küsnacht.

To complement our night-shift team, we are looking for a motivated and resilient person with a warm heart who will competently support our residents throughout the night and, together with the team, contribute to a high quality of care and nursing.

Your new responsibilities

  • Assuming responsibility for the facility as a qualified professional on night shift and serving as a contact person for nursing assistants at night
  • Responsibility for and ensuring professionally competent and individualized care and nursing for residents with varying care needs
  • Calm and professional handling of crisis and emergency situations
  • Responsibility for complete and informative documentation (easyDok)
  • Actively helping shape a positive team culture and collaboration
  • Contributing to quality improvement
  • Diploma as a registered nurse (SRK recognition available)
  • Professional experience in long-term care, geriatrics and/or gerontopsychiatry
  • Empathy and enjoyment of working with elderly people
  • Ability to work in a team, resilience, flexibility and empathy
  • A high degree of independence and sense of responsibility
  • Very good spoken and written German skills as well as good IT user skills
