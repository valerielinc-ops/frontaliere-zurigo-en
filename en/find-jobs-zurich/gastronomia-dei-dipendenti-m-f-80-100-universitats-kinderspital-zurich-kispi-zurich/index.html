Restaurant, Bistro, Cafébar, Occasions, Outlets

Your tasks

  • You ensure a service-oriented catering service of high quality and take care of the debt collection.
  • Various food stations in our restaurant are filled and maintained by you.
  • You prepare meeting and event meals and carry out events.
  • The preparations for the punctual opening / closing of the restaurant are carried out independently.
  • For compliance with a hygienic and appealing presentation of the gastronomy you are responsible with an eye for detail.
  • Your areas of application are the various gastronomy outlets.

Your profile

  • You will bring a completed training in gastronomy or relevant professional experience in a comparable company.
  • You are a service-oriented, independent and communicative personality who has a great willingness and resilience.
  • Your well-groomed appearance is also important to us.
  • Your very good knowledge of German rounds off your profile.
