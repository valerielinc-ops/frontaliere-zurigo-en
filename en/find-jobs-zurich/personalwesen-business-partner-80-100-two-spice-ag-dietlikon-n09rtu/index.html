HR Business Partner 80-100 %

Two Spice AG — Dietlikon, Dietlikon, Svizzera

Two Spice – that’s us! A creative and innovative gastronomy and lifestyle company from Zurich. Since 1990 we bring fresh ideas to the table and develop unique concepts and brands that inspire. Our versatile businesses are at home in the central hotspots of different cities and stand for quality, design and pure pleasure.The catering group now comprises nine different brands with a total of over 27 restaurants and take-out stores. Thanks to more than 30 years of experience and with now around 650 employees, the company is a firm fixture in the Swiss gastro scene. Do you want to be part of our team? Now is your chance!

Tasks

  • Strategic and operational advice for managers in all personnel-relevant issues
  • Supervision of the entire recruitment process – from personnel requirements planning to contract conclusion
  • Assistance of employees and supervisors in development, conflict, return, disciplinary and separation talks
  • Co-design and further development of modern HR processes, instruments and systems
  • Support in personnel and succession planning as well as in talent management processes
  • Planning and implementation of training, workshops and employee events
  • Cooperation in cross-sectoral projects and support for new openings
  • Plannable travel activities (decentralised HR organisation)

Requirements

  • HR training completed (e.g. HR certificate or equivalent qualification)
  • Several years of professional experience as an HR Business Partner, ideally in gastronomy
  • Very good knowledge of Swiss labour law and HR processes
  • High digital and data affinity, secure handling of HR tools
  • Strong consulting competence and sovereign attitude towards managers and employees
  • Proactive, independent and flexible personality with high social competence
