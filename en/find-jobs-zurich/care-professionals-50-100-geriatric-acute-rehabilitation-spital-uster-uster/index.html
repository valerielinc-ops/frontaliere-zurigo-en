Full description

Personal – competent – close Welcome to the Hospital Uster, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As the central pillar of health care in the Oberland region of Zurich and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you experience the highest level of expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and promoted in your professional development. Spital Uster is a teaching hospital of the Medical Faculty of the University of Zurich. Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of a “regional and digital health hub in Züri East”! Bed Station A0 is an interdisciplinary department with a focus on geriatric acute rehabilitation for patients of all insurance classes. The clinical pictures include, inter alia, cardiovascular diseases, pneumological diseases, endocrinology, infections, gastrointestinal diseases, autoimmune diseases, oncology and hematology, traumas and injuries after falls. For our team we are looking for flexible and committed personalities as soon as possible or by agreement.

Exciting challenges await you

  • you are part of an interdisciplinary treatment team and care for and care for patients during their one- to two-week stay, according to the principles of activating-therapeutic-accompanying care
  • you are responsible for and control the care process of your patients entrusted to you and coordinate the care and care in tandem with FaGes, nursing aids and interns
  • you work closely with other professional groups involved in the treatment process and shape an appreciative team culture
  • you work in the practical training of FaGe learners and students HF/FH

What sets you apart for the job

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nurse HF/FH with experience in the acute hospital
  • open-minded and team-oriented person with a professional way of working
  • Interest in geriatric acute rehabilitation
  • Reliable team member who thinks and is motivated
  • Willingness to work in all shifts
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
