Original description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

As our new Rental Warehouse Coordinator, you play a central role in supporting our rental business. They are responsible for the efficient management, storage and coordination of rental facilities and accessories. In doing so, you ensure that rental facilities are available at all times, fully and on time for customer projects. As an important interface between sales, application engineering, service, logistics service providers and external partners, you coordinate the daily warehouse and rental fleet activities. With your structured working method, you contribute to ensuring the smooth running of the rental processes and sustainably supporting customer satisfaction. You will become part of a growing business and play an important role in ensuring the efficient and professional operation of our rental fleet. Your main tasks and responsibilities

  • Management of receipts, storage and dispatch of rental facilities and accessories and ensuring proper storage organisation.
  • monitoring stock accuracy through regular stock controls, inventories and the maintenance of transparent stockpiles.
  • Preparation, picking and packaging of rental facilities for customer projects as well as processing of returns and checking for completeness or damage.
  • Coordination of plant movements between warehouse locations, service centers, project locations and customers, ensuring timely deliveries and returns.
  • Support for logistical processes through the creation of shipping documents, packing lists and proof of delivery as well as coordination with transport service providers.
  • Maintaining and updating relevant data in ERP, warehouse management and rental management systems as well as ensuring high data quality.
  • Support for the further development of warehouse, logistics and fleet management processes in compliance with company policies, safety standards and operational requirements.

What you should bring for this position

  • Completed vocational training in the field of warehouse logistics, logistics, supply chain management or a comparable discipline.
  • Professional experience in the areas of warehousing, logistics, inventory management or ideally in the rental business or a technical or industrial environment.
  • Experience in dealing with ERP systems, warehouse management systems or inventory management software.
  • Structured and reliable operation with high accuracy and a keen eye for detail.
  • Ability to coordinate multiple tasks at the same time, set priorities and maintain an overview even in dynamic situations.
  • Strong team and communication skills as well as a service- and customer-oriented mindset.
  • Practical, solution-oriented working method with a high level of initiative and a sense of responsibility.
  • Valid driving licence class B; a forklift licence is an advantage.
  • Occasional travel to our other rental stations / offices is required. A category C (lorry) driving licence is an advantage.

What we offer you

  • Close cooperation with sales, engineering, service and logistics in an international technical environment
  • Varied activity with responsibility for warehousing, logistics and rental fleet processes
  • Active participation in the optimization of storage and rental plant processes within a growing business area
  • The ability to directly influence customer satisfaction through efficient and reliable processes
  • Development opportunities within an expanding rental business
