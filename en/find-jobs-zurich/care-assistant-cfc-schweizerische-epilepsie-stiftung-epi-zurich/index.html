Original description

Tasks

  • In the highly structured studios of the day care centers, you accompany and support adults with physical and cognitive impairments as well as sometimes highly challenging behaviors in pursuing meaningful and comprehensible activities and enable self-acting action as well as the further development of skills and abilities.
  • They carry out various individual and group activities and thus ensure a varied and meaningful everyday routine.
  • They create and evaluate the agogic development process, are responsible for its implementation and involve all stakeholders closely.
  • Accompaniment of trainees and trainees in the development of their competences round off this exciting area of responsibility

Requirements

  • You have completed a training in social pedagogy or are a specialist care EFZ
  • You can convince us through your profound experience in accompanying people with severe developmental/movement impairments as well as challenging behaviors
  • You are a flexible, resilient, patient personality and enjoy working in a team
  • Organizational talent, a structured way of working and humor round off your talents

We offer

  • A living environment that you can help shape with your own ideas
  • Working hours from Monday to Friday, 7:55 a.m. to 4:55 p.m., 5 weeks of vacation, from 50. Age 6 weeks
  • Thematic support by internal experts
  • Extensive internal training offer and support for external training
  • Own pension fund with above average employer share of 60%
  • Staff apartments, parking spaces and perks in the EPI Park restaurant, pharmacy and half-tax or general subscription

Sometimes quite challenging, but never boring: At EPI WohnWerk, no day is like the other. Because the people we accompany each and everyone have their unique life story. Thanks to the cooperation in the interprofessional team, we are well positioned for our working day. Do you also want to be part of this team?
