Full description

Are you interested in actively shaping modern IT solutions in the financial industry and developing scalable customer communication?

Then join our team and work together with us to further develop our output management platform (Customer Communications Management) as well as our archiving and scanning solutions for the entire Vontobel Group.

We are looking for a committed, practice-oriented personality with an end-to-end understanding that takes responsibility and feels comfortable at the interface between business and IT.

What would your activities be?

  • Responsibility for the expansion, operation and further development of the output management (CCM) platform as well as participation in archiving and scanning solutions.
  • Analysis of requirements together with the departments and implementation into scalable technical solutions.
  • Conception, development, configuration and, in addition, the development and development of the company's development and development team (in cooperation with internal and external partners, e.g. Quadient).
  • Development and operation of end-to-end solutions along the entire document process (rendering, output management, integration).
  • Ensure the stability, performance and availability of the applications responsible.
  • Central interface between business and IT with a focus on consistent process understanding.
  • Active participation in group-wide transformation and platform initiatives.

What are we looking for?

  • Training or studies in computer science and several years of experience in engineering, application management or operation of business-critical systems.
  • Software engineering experience, ideally in output management / CCM environment.
  • Good knowledge of scripting languages (Java) and data formats (XML/JSON) and morphological databases (SQL).
  • Experience in the integration of systems in complex IT landscapes.
  • Knowledge of Quadient In the Tippe Flex Suite, document management, archiving or scanning is an advantage.
  • Understanding end-to-end document processes and back-end integrations.
  • Analytic, solution-oriented and independent working methods with high responsibility.
  • Experience in the teams of the Ailean team and very good knowledge of German and English.

What do we offer?

  • A lot of room for design, flat hierarchies and short decision-making.
  • Modern workplace in Zurich (near Paradeplatz) and flexible hybrid work model.
  • Attractive opportunities for further training and development
  • Open, collaborative team culture ('One Vontobel').
