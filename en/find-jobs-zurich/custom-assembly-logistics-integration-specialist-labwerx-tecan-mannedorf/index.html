Custom Assembly, Logistics & Integration Specialist Labwerx (80-100%)

Männedorf, Zurich

Your contribution

Take responsibility for our logistics and assembly processes! You are at the heart of our production – from incoming goods and carefully checking goods, to booking in SAP, assembling mechanical components, to preparing materials for projects and shipping. With your commitment, you ensure that our projects run smoothly and our teams are optimally equipped.

  • Receiving goods and carefully checking incoming goods
  • Recording incoming goods in SAP
  • Assign and organize materials to the respective projects so that everything is in the right place at the right time
  • Precisely and carefully assemble mechanical components
  • Provide production and project teams with the required materials quickly and reliably
  • Package goods for shipping and pay attention to every detail
  • Support project deliveries through efficient assembly and packaging
  • Perform the annual inventory and keep stock up-to-date
  • Ensure bulk materials are always available to avoid production delays

Your profile

We are not just looking for expertise, but people who want to bring their passion and talent to us.

  • Completed vocational training in logistics, warehouses, production or mechanics
  • Experience in warehouse logistics, goods receipt or assembly
  • Good knowledge of mechanical assembly, tool handling and material management
  • Basic computer skills and willingness to learn new systems (experience with SAP or other ERP systems is beneficial)
  • Physical load capacity for lifting and moving materials
  • Strong team spirit, reliability and a structured, careful working method
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • English skills are beneficial
