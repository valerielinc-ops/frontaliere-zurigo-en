Full description

This is what awaits you

As a senior expert in liability insurance, you will take over the technical management of the national and international liability business in the corporate segment. You control the development and quality of the portfolio, actively shape our products and underwriting guidelines and thus make an important contribution to profitable growth. You will work closely with underwriting, actuarial, product development, sales and other specialist departments.

This is what you do for us

You maintain and manage the national and international liability portfolio and are jointly responsible for the technical results. To do this, you will further develop the underwriting and underwriting guidelines, define the risk appetite and ensure their consistent application.

You examine complex risks that require submission, approve them and participate in underwriting boards for major risks. At the same time, you ensure quality and compliance with underwriting requirements, carry out regular specialist audits and ensure the requirements of the internal control system.

You constantly monitor developments in risks, products and the market, compare our solutions with competing offers and derive appropriate adjustments from this. You will also further develop existing products, build a pipeline for innovations and promote digitalization topics together with the responsible departments.

This is what you bring with you

  • University degree in business administration or mathematics
  • Legal training or relevant specialist knowledge is an advantage
  • Several years of in-depth experience in the underwriting of national and international industrial liability insurance solutions at an expert level
  • Ideally first management experience
  • Great interest in product development, digital solutions and computer science
  • Analytical and networked thinking, assertiveness and a resilient and solution-oriented way of working
  • Strong communication and teamwork skills
  • Very good knowledge of German and English, knowledge of French is an advantage

Work with us

A responsible key role with a lot of creative freedom awaits you. Together with committed colleagues, you will further develop our liability business and ensure needs-based, sustainable insurance solutions for corporate customers.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Further training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible foreign programs. We contribute generously to job-relevant, external further training.

Perks

We offer you, among other things, a premium discount of up to 25%, a half-fare subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with a flat rate and discounts on cultural events.
