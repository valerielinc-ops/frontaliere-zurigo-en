Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School Zürcher Oberland Apprenticeship position specialist/woman company maintenance EFZ (home service) 100% The Cantonal School Zürcher Oberland (KZO) in Wetzikon is a lively school that values an open culture, shared commitment and an inspiring learning environment. In currently 60 classes, she leads a gymnasium secondary level (9th/10th school year) and all Zurich maturity profiles. With around 1300 students, 200 teachers and 40 employees in administration and operations, the KZO is one of the major high schools in the canton. With its diverse services, the Household Service contributes significantly to a functioning school operation and the appearance of the KZO. As of August 2027, an apprenticeship as an expert / woman company maintenance becomes vacant. Your apprenticeship in short:

  • Duration: 3 years
  • Vocational School: 1 day per week
  • Vocational Master's degree is possible to accompany you
  • Completion: Professional / woman company maintenance EFZ (home service)
  • Tasks as learners in the domestic service:
  • You repair damage to buildings, interior and exterior facilities
  • You maintain green spaces and buildings
  • You control the electrical installations, replace lamps, locks and fuses
  • You maintain the house technology and recognize disturbances
  • You help with cleaning the buildings and their surroundings with this you bring:
  • Completed obligatory school time
  • Practical-technical understanding and craftsmanship
  • Reliable and committed
  • Physically resilient
  • Team capable and helpful
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German
  • This is what we offer you:
  • Professional accompaniment and support during the entire training period
  • Contribution to lunch (mensa or meal). Lunch Checks)
  • Cheap mobile subscription More information can be provided by Mr. Agon Behluli, Head of Household Service, telephone 044 933 08 61, e-mail agon.behluli@edu.zh.ch. Did we get your interest? We look forward to your application. Please submit your documents exclusively via the online tool.

Pensum: 100%
