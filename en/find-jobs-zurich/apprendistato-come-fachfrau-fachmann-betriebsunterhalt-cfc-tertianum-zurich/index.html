Full description

Earn your EFZ through an apprenticeship in Technical Services. With us, you will start a varied and meaningful apprenticeship with a future, because as a Facility Maintenance Specialist, you can expect a supportive working environment from day one. During your apprenticeship, you will be accompanied and supported by our experienced vocational trainers.

For the Residenz Im Brühl in Zurich - Höngg, we are looking for you from 1 August 2027 for the

Apprenticeship as a Facility Maintenance Specialist EFZ

What you will learn with us

  • Maintenance and repair work in all buildings
  • Various types of indoor and outdoor cleaning work
  • Inspection and monitoring tasks
  • Indoor greenery and garden maintenance in the outdoor areas
  • Carrying out work related to occupational safety
  • Preparing events
  • Snow removal
  • Maintenance of the swimming pool & mini-golf facility

What you bring

  • Completed schooling (secondary school A or B) with good academic performance
  • Strong willingness to learn and a sense of responsibility
  • Helpful, outgoing and team-oriented, with a strong sense of care and conscientiousness
  • Manual dexterity and technical understanding
  • Physical stamina
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoyment of varied challenges and practical work
  • Good command of German, both spoken and written

What benefits do we offer you?

You are just as important to us as your apprenticeship is to you! That is why we offer you the following benefits:

  • You get the day off on your birthday
  • CHF 500 towards your compulsory school materials
  • Free SBB Half Fare Travelcard
  • Every lunch for CHF 5
  • Discounted mobile phone plans with Sunrise
  • Discounts and offers related to fitness, leisure and your wellbeing
  • Up to 10 % discount on your supplementary health insurance
  • And much more…

You start right away with an exciting introductory day, and halfway through your training, we invite you to our Summer Camp for an entire week. Of course, the completion of your training must be properly celebrated, so we organize an unforgettable graduation ceremony for all graduates. And perhaps that will only be the beginning of your career with us! We offer numerous opportunities to work within our company directly after completing your apprenticeship.

You can find further benefits and insights on our website .

So, interested?

As soon as we have received your complete application documents, our team will review them. If your dossier convinces us, we will be delighted to get to know you personally in an initial interview. After that, you can immediately immerse yourself in the Tertianum world during several trial days and get an impression of everyday life with us.

What do we need for your complete application?

  • CV
  • School reports
  • Results of the Stellwerk test and, if available, the Multicheck
