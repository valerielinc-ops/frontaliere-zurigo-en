Full description

With your electrical engineering expertise, you ensure that streets, paths and public facilities are safely and reliably illuminated at all times.

Tasks

  • You help ensure that streets, parks, pedestrian zones and underpasses are always illuminated.
  • Inspection and maintenance work, as well as lamp replacement and cleaning work, are therefore routine for you.
  • You also ensure smooth lighting during new construction and conversion projects.
  • Always maintaining your balance, you feel confident installing streetlight poles, masts, overhead cable systems and riser cables.
  • Performing occasional special and night assignments is second nature to you.

Profile

  • You have successfully completed electrical engineering training (network electrician, electrical fitter, electrical installer or assembly electrician).
  • Ideally, you have several years of professional experience and knowledge of the city. You are familiar with common Office programs.
  • You have a native-level command of German and hold a category B driver's license, ideally also a category C license.
  • You enjoy working in a team, outdoors and at night. You feel comfortable on ladders and elevated work platforms at great heights.
  • You are committed, work independently and can be relied on.

We offer

  • More energy for your life: Through mobile working, flexible work models and part-time opportunities, you can actively shape your working hours and arrange them to fit your life as well as possible. For example, you can purchase up to 2 additional weeks of vacation and work part-time at all levels. Job sharing/-splitting and unpaid leave are of course also possible with us.
  • Sustainable and discounted travel: With the mobility allowance of up to 600 francs per year, you can travel affordably and sustainably – whether you want to cycle through Zurich or explore Switzerland by train.
  • An investment in your future: We not only value paying fair and transparent salaries, but also ensure that more of your salary remains. We cover 60% of the savings contributions and additionally offer an interest rate above the mandatory rate on the capital saved.
  • An energy boost for your free time: We pay 100 francs into your Lunch-Check card every month. You also benefit each year from additional fringe benefits worth 150 francs and attractive mobile phone plans.
  • Development and continuing education: Through our talent management, we provide targeted support for your development – with a wide range of internal and external training opportunities and exciting development opportunities.
  • Meaningful work: Sustainability and meaningful work are not just words to us: Help shape Zurich and the future with us. Together with us, unleash energy for a better tomorrow.

About us

For over 130 years, ewz has been dynamic and visionary every day. Whether sustainable energy production, smart energy solutions or innovative networking technology: Develop with us today what will matter tomorrow.
