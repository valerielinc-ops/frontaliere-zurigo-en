Full description

Would you like to work behind the scenes? Then you've come to the right place. The Herdern loading department is a central part of our goods logistics. Every day, we supply over 200 locations in the urban area of Zurich in a 7x24 hour operation.

Tasks

  • Loading and unloading of trucks
  • Moving goods with a forklift
  • Various warehouse tasks, including manual order picking

Requirements

  • 1-2 years of professional experience: in logistics
  • Driver's license (cat. B) and own car mandatory, otherwise residence ideally in the greater Zurich area
  • SUVA-recognized forklift license
  • Team-oriented and reliable personality with an independent way of working
  • PC skills
  • No specific education/training required
  • German (good knowledge)

What we offer

  • Central workplace: Working in Zurich West
  • Mobile subscription: You can look forward to special conditions for your mobile subscription
  • Occupational pension: Excellent pension fund benefits with high employer contributions
  • Social benefits: Fair social benefits
  • Catering: Discounts in the staff restaurant
  • Cumulus points: You receive additional Cumulus points when shopping
  • Migros Bank: Preferential interest rates on private and investment accounts, free account management, discounted mortgages, and more
  • Klubschule Migros: Contribution to course costs at the Klubschule
  • Leisure and cultural offers: You benefit from discounted tickets for selected cultural events

Contact Application & Contact: Monika Allenbach rekrutierung@gmz.migros.ch
