Full description

Do you like to work with people? You want to work with the Alter Käferberg health center immediately or by agreement with our nursing team?

Tasks

  • They support, care and care for our residents competently and sensitively on our dementia department.
  • They take on demanding nursing tasks and medical activities.
  • They report observations, they clearly report and they document completely.
  • They help to actively shape the everyday life of our residents and promote their resources.
  • They accompany learners together with the vocational training managers and pass on their knowledge.

Profile

  • Empathic personality with joy in working with older people
  • Team-oriented, responsible and reliable with independent working methods
  • A training as a health professional EFZ with SRK recognition for diplomas from abroad and preferably experience in the field of dementia
  • Good German knowledge in word and writing
  • IT-Aness, ideally EasyDok knowledge

We offer

  • Look forward to an exciting area of responsibility in a professional and team-oriented working environment with a Du culture throughout the company.
  • Man is at the center of our lives.
  • We therefore support the reconciliation of private and professional life, and different work models make it possible.
  • You can actively contribute to the choice of the work possibilty and duty hours.
  • As part of the city of Zurich and due to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, advanced employment conditions and excellent social benefits.
  • The annual salary for this function is between Fr. 70'300.– and Fr. 93'936.– (100%) depending on experience.

About us

The health centres for the age of the city of Zurich are one of the largest Geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive range from a single source.

The health centre for Alter Käferberg in Wipkingen in district 10 combines various care and care services under one roof and is characterized by many years of experience in the field of old age medicine. Thanks to the unique location on Käferberg, the residents, patients and guests enjoy a wonderful view of Lake Zurich, the Alps and the rooftops of the city. Here you work together with 400 colleagues.
