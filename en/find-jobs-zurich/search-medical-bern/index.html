More about this search for cross-border applicants

Bern, the federal capital of Switzerland, hosts a stable healthcare sector anchored by the Inselspital university hospital, numerous outpatient clinics, and a network of cantonal care facilities. The current cluster lists 62 medical vacancies across the canton, spanning roles such as nursing staff, medical assistants, laboratory technicians, physiotherapists, and resident physicians. Demand is particularly consistent in geriatric care and rehabilitation services, reflecting Bern's ageing population. For cross-border candidates, the canton lies outside the Italian-speaking region, so frontalieri commuting from northern Italy would be subject to standard federal permit allocation rather than the bilateral arrangements common in Ticino. Candidates should verify language requirements, as German is typically expected in patient-facing roles, with French useful in bilingual institutions.
