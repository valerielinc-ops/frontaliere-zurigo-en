  • Simona Coiro Head of Nursing Service / Member of Clinic Management Tel: +41 44 762 52 33 Employer Children's Hospital Zurich – Children's rehabilitation Affoltern at Albis The Children's Hospital Zurich is the largest University Children's Hospital in Switzerland with around 3,000 employees and one of the leading centers for pediatric and adolescent medicine in Europe. But the biggest thing about the "Kispi" are the little ones: up to 100,000 patients are cared for annually. In addition to the entire range of pediatric medicine and pediatric surgery, the Children's Hospital also offers a unique children's rehabilitation in Affoltern on the Albis.
