Role overview

What we are looking for as a certified production technician (m/f/d) industrialization you are the interface between development, production and purchasing.

You evaluate components in terms of productionability, cost and quality and contribute significantly to the efficient industrialization of new products.

Your aim is to combine technical feasibility with economic efficiency and to convert them into the specification.
