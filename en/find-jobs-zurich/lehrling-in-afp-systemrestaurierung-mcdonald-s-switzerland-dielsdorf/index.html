Apprenticeship in AFP system restoration

Our goal: to form the succession!

For our restaurant, we are looking for an apprentice employed in AFP system restoration.

AFP learning in system restoration is a 2-year training course for people who want to learn a practical, practical and dynamic environment.

Profile

This training is for you if

  • You've finished your compulsory education.
  • You have basic knowledge in French (oral and written)
  • You like practical and manual work
  • You enjoy working as a team and being in touch with customers.
  • You are reliable, punctual and motivated to learn a profession
  • You will gladly follow clear instructions and processes
  • You are resistant to stress and comfort in a sustained environment
  • Order, cleanliness and hygiene are among your priorities

Your future job

As an apprentice employed in the restoration of AFP system, you will be trained mainly through daily practice in restaurants, with the support of experienced trainers.

You will learn to

  • Prepare products according to quality and hygiene standards
  • Welcoming, serving and advising clients
  • Work effectively as a team in different restaurant positions
  • Respect food safety procedures and rules
  • Maintain order and cleanliness of your workstation

Your training

AFP training lasts 2 years and includes

  • Practical training directly in a restaurant
  • Professional courses to deepen your theoretical knowledge
  • Business-to-business courses to link theory to practice

Your Federal Certificate of Professional Training AFP is a solid first step for your professional future at McDonalds or in the catering sector.

Your outlook for change

At the end of your AFP, you can

  • entering the labour market directly
  • or continue training with a two-year gateway to the CFC in system restoration.

We offer

  • Recognized and Valuing Training
  • A dynamic, structured and caring working environment
  • Professional support and personalized follow-up throughout your learning
  • Experienced and engaged trainers
  • Real prospects for development after your training
