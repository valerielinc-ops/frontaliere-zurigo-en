Employee: in Helpdesk & Disposition 100%

Do you keep track even when many threads converge at the same time? Then you are exactly right with us. For our central helpdesk, we are looking for a committed personality who takes customer concerns professionally, coordinates assignments and keeps track of ongoing orders. In this versatile hub function, you make an important contribution to our service quality and work daily with a wide variety of people and specialist areas.

You can move that with us.

You are the central point of contact for our customers, employees and partners and ensure that issues end up quickly in the right place. As part of our helpdesk team, you will coordinate requests, keep track of ongoing orders and contribute to smooth operations with your organizational talent. The following activities are part of your area of responsibility:

  • You are the first point of contact for incoming requests via phone and helpdesk.
  • You accept disruptions and service requests, analyze them and ensure efficient processing (first-level support).
  • You prioritize tickets according to urgency and coordinate the appropriate measures.
  • You communicate daily with internal and external customers in German, English, French and Italian.
  • You plan and schedule technical and infrastructural missions and service orders.
  • You keep an eye on ongoing orders and make sure that agreed services are provided on time.
  • You support the planning of maintenance and maintenance measures.
  • You take on administrative tasks and actively contribute to the optimization of our processes.

That's what makes you.

You enjoy being in touch with people, coordinating concerns and finding solutions. Even in a dynamic environment, you keep an overview and convince through your pronounced service orientation.

  • Completed commercial or technical-commercial basic training
  • Experience in the helpdesk, service desk, call center or in a similar turntable function
  • Very good MS-Office knowledge and pleasure in working with digital tools
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • Very good knowledge of French for daily customer contact
  • Knowledge of Italian is a plus
  • Strong service and solution orientation
  • Organizational talent with a structured and reliable working method
  • Strong communication personality with pleasure in working with different stakeholders
  • Quick comprehension as well as the ability to set priorities and keep an overview even in hectic situations

That's what we offer you.
