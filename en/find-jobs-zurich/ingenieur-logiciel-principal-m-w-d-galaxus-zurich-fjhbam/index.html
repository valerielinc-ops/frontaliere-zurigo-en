Full description

Be part of one of our dedicated product teams and develop innovative software solutions as a Senior Software Engineer, which show real impact – for our internal processes and for our more than 3 million customers in Switzerland and the EU. With your curiosity, your team spirit and your willingness to learn, you grow together with us and actively shape our platform.

Mansioni

  • As a Senior Software Engineer in Product Development, you are responsible for the design, implementation and continuous development of functions that improve the shopping experience
  • You develop new features together in the team, implement technical innovations across team boundaries and ensure that our software remains user-friendly, performant and scalable
  • You work closely with other engineers as well as with colleagues from the fields of data, design and product management to realize customer-oriented solutions
  • You feel comfortable in an agile, experimental environment and like to introduce your own ideas to continuously improve our platform
  • You take care of the technical infrastructure of your area of responsibility, monitor the systems and ensure a stable and reliable operation

Requisiti

  • At least 5 years of experience: In object-oriented software development (ideally with .NET technologies or C#)
  • Completed study (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): Information technology
  • You already have a lot of experience with one or more technologies from our tech stack: .NET/C#, relational databases (MSSQL, BigQuery), non-relational databases (MongoDB, Redis, Elasticsearch) and Azure
  • Experience in our frontend stack (React, Next, GraphQL) is of advantage, but no need
  • readiness to learn, curiosity and the ability to work on new topics and technologies
  • Holistic thinking and a pronounced sense of sustainable, scalable solutions, combined with a pragmatic hands-on mentality
  • identify potential for improvement and implement changes to increase productivity and effectiveness
  • The ability to adapt to continuous changes in the working environment
  • You communicate safely with different stakeholders - also at management level
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • English (very good knowledge)
