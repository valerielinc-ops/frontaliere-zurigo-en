Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Prison and Reintegration Prison Pöschwies Specialist Production Assembly Workshop 80 - 100% With around 1400 employees, JuWe is the largest office within the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs and responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as for the execution of various types of detention. Our employees from around 40 professions support and promote the reintegration of delinquents and work effectively and progressively for a safe and just society. The Pöschwies Prison (JVA) is one of eight main departments of JuWe and the largest closed facility for men in Switzerland. With around 400 detention places and about 300 employees, it contributes to the protection of the general public in accordance with its legal mandate and at the same time creates the conditions for the rehabilitation of the detainees. As of January 1, 2027 or by agreement, we are looking for reinforcement for our assembly workshop. Together with your experienced professional colleagues, you ensure a structured production day and accompany, supervise and promote the instructed persons at their workplaces. Your tasks

  • The instructed persons accompany, instruct and motivate you in their daily work
  • You plan and coordinate individual employment assignments taking into account the skills and resources of the assigned persons
  • Administrative tasks around production orders are also part of your field of activity
  • Observations on the development and behavior of the instructed persons are recorded in reports and assessments
  • In the company fire brigade you engage as an active member Your profile
  • You have completed basic craft or technical training with a Federal Certificate of Competence (EFZ)
  • You bring professional experience in the craft or technical environment
  • Experience with production facilities is a plus
  • Ideally, you have completed further training as a prison professional as well as additional training in labour agogy.
  • Working with people interests you and you can well imagine combining your expertise with work-related tasks
  • Good knowledge of German in word and writing as well as secure MS-Office skills round off your profile
  • Other languages are a plus
  • You are at least 25 years old and have an impeccable reputation (punitive and enforcement register without entries) Our offer
