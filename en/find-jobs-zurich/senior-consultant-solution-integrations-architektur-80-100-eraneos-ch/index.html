Full description

Eraneos is an international strategy, transformation and technology advisory group that helps companies thrive in a dynamic digital world. With first-class experts from business and technology, we empower our customers to set new standards for sustainable transformations – from strategy to implementation. Whether in the design of sustainable organizations, the development of new potential through data and AI or the protection of companies through holistic cybersecurity: We deliver results pragmatically and purposefully. Our team of around 1,200 dedicated employees is represented in offices in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, Denmark, Sweden, Austria, the UK, Singapore and the USA and combines global perspectives with strong local roots. Around 450 employees work at the Swiss locations in Zurich, Bern and Lausanne. In the area of manufacturing, we help industrial companies modernize their system landscapes – from ERP transformations to OT/IT integration to data-driven production architectures. This is the job - your main tasks

  • Design and further development of integration architectures in industrial system landscapes (ERP, MES, Shopfloor, Cloud).
  • Definition of integration strategies and technological roadmaps for hybrid IT/OT environments.
  • Conception of modern integration patterns such as event-driven architecture, API management and service-oriented architectures in an industrial context.
  • End-to-end solution design for business and production processes with a focus on data flows between ERP, manufacturing execution systems and cloud platforms.
  • Architectural control of transformation projects, e.g. in the context of S/4HANA introductions or MES modernizations.
  • Definition of governance and security principles for interfaces in safety-critical production environments.
  • Evaluation and validation of middleware and integration platforms including technical proof-of-concepts. This job is for you if
  • the integration architecture as a strategic lever for end-to-end processes in industry.
  • you can structure complex IT/OT landscapes and transform them into viable target architectures.
  • Event-driven, API-first and data-driven approaches are practice for you.
  • You have experience in the integration of ERP, MES or production systems.
  • You can document architectural decisions in a comprehensible manner and represent them to technical and professional stakeholders.
  • you feel comfortable in the consulting environment and take responsibility in customer projects.
  • You communicate professionally in German (at least C1) and English. As an i-top you bring with you
  • Experience in the integration of SAP (e.g. S/4HANA), MES systems or shop floor applications.
  • Know-how in dealing with event streaming technologies (e.g. Kafka) in production-related scenarios.
  • Knowledge of industrial communication standards (e.g. OPC UA) or OT-related integration patterns.
  • Experience with cloud integration services (Azure, AWS or GCP) in the manufacturing environment.
  • Expertise in security and identity concepts for hybrid IT/OT architectures.
  • Architecture or cloud certifications (e.g. iSAQB, Azure/AWS Solutions Architect). Even if you do not meet every single requirement, we look forward to your application - for us, your potential, your attitude and your understanding of architecture are crucial. Your next step towards a successful application Jeffrey as your contact person is looking forward to receiving your direct application via our website (CV, diplomas and employment certificates in PDF).
