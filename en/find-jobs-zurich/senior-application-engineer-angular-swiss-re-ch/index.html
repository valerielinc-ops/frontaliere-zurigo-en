Senior Application Engineer - Angular Are you passionate about creating exceptional user experiences through clean, efficient code? Join our innovative team at the forefront of digital transformation in the reinsurance industry. As a Frontend Engineer for SwiftRe®, you'll help shape our cutting-edge platform that provides quick, efficient, and user-friendly solutions for facultative reinsurance business worldwide. About the Role As a Frontend Engineer, you'll be instrumental in developing and enhancing our Angular-based application, collaborating with cross-functional teams to deliver seamless user experiences that drive business value. Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain high-quality Angular components for our SwiftRe® platform
  • Collaborate with Technical Squad Leads on solution design and implementation
  • Create reusable UI components that meet our high standards for quality and performance
  • Implement responsive designs that work across multiple devices and screen sizes
  • Optimize application performance and ensure code quality through testing
  • Participate actively in agile ceremonies and contribute to continuous improvement
  • Work with integrated systems across Swiss Re's value chain
  • Ensure all development meets high security and compliance standards

About the Team You'll join an agile squad working on SwiftRe®, our Azure Cloud-based application with a worldwide user base. Our platform is highly integrated into the full value chain of Swiss Re, including core legacy services, and utilizes state-of-the-art technology stacks. We operate in an agile environment where team empowerment is our highest value, fostering innovation and continuous learning.

About You

We understand that no candidate meets 100% of requirements. If you have experience with Angular development, a genuine curiosity about software engineering, and a collaborative mindset, we'd love to connect with you. We value diverse perspectives and are looking for someone who can contribute to our inclusive team culture.

We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements

  • Strong experience with Angular development (version 19); 10+ Years experience
  • Proficiency in TypeScript and familiarity with Redux patterns and NgRx
  • Experience building reusable UI components and working with component libraries
  • Educational background in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field
  • Excellent communication skills in English, both written and verbal

These are additional nice to haves

  • Experience in UI/UX solution design collaborating with dedicated designers
  • Knowledge of cloud-based applications, particularly in Azure
  • Understanding of security and compliance requirements in financial services
  • Experience with agile development methodologies
  • Knowledge of the reinsurance industry or financial services sector
