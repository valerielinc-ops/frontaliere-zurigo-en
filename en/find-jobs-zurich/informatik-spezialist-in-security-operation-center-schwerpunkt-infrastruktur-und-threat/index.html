Full description

The Cantonal Police of Zurich and its approximately 4,000 employees assume responsibility for security in the canton. Do you have experience in the field of IT security, are you interested in actively participating in the operation and further development of the Security Operation Center (SOC) and contribute your knowledge and experience? Then you become part of a young, dynamic and motivated IT team and support us actively as a computer science specialist / in Security Operation Center (focus on infrastructure and threat management) 100% tasks

  • Triage and assessment of IT security incidents
  • Active participation in the operation and maintenance of the SOC-IT infrastructure
  • Devops tasks within the SOC-IT infrastructure (Ansible, Gitlab)
  • Monitoring infrastructure regarding security incident
  • Analysis, detection and resolution of cyberattacks and technical vulnerabilities
  • Continuous development of processes and technical measures to improve cyber defense
  • Maintaining the high level of protection (Hardening/CIS Benchmark/NIST)
  • Project cooperation and advice on security issues Profile
  • Completed Bachelor's degree in Computer Science/Cybersecurity (FH/University/ETH or HF) or equivalent education
  • Several years of practical experience in the technical environment
  • Further education and experience in IT security (e.g. SANS) is an advantage
  • Stylistic German in word and writing, good knowledge of English
  • Independent, careful, disciplined and exact working methods
  • High load capacity in exceptional situations
  • Driving licence Cat. B
  • Readiness for Pikett service
  • Correct reputation and offer
  • Technically demanding and meaningful area of responsibility
  • Own ideas and solutions are appreciated and promoted
  • Team spirit and appreciation are capitalized
  • Your workplace in the state-of-the-art Zurich police and justice centre is centrally located and offers both a staff restaurant and an in-house fitness centre.
