Full description

The Spitex-Verein Bärentswil is centrally located in the Zurich Oberland and offers ambulatory and inpatient care for the community with around 70 employees. In addition to individual care at home, we operate a care apartment for 22 residents and a care round the clock. Our offer is complemented by meal and driving services. As an organization, we attach great importance to high-quality care and an appreciative cooperation. Employee* in Administration For our organization, we are immediately looking for a dedicated administrative support, which takes over both in the coordination of volunteer work and various administrative activities. The position is suitable for someone with a hands-on mentality, likes to work in a structured way and take responsibility: Their main tasks

  • Coordination of volunteer work – assignment planning, communication, support of volunteers
  • General office organization – filing, document management, appointment overview
  • Correspondence management – e-mail processing, telephone contact, internal communication
  • Data maintenance – updating lists, overviews and operational plans
  • Initial planning – support for preparation, implementation and post-processing

Your profile

  • Commercial education or equivalent qualification
  • Professional experience in administrative activities
  • Strong hands-on mentality and independent working methods
  • Enjoy organization, communication and cooperation with different stakeholders

We offer

  • A versatile task with design scope
  • Collaboration in a committed team in a manageable and appreciative environment.
  • Space for co-creation and active contribution
  • Team activities
  • Free parking
  • Discounted food
