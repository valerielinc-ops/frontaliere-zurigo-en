Full description

Have you known the fitness industry for many years and want to set your own accents as a manager? Do you care about an excellent customer experience? Are you a responsible and motivating leader? Then you are right with us!

Luogo di lavoro: ACTIV FITNESS, Activ Fitness Dietikon, Löwenstrasse 17, 8953 Dietikon

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You support and accompany our customers throughout the entire training process, create training plans and motivate our customers as an excellent service provider.
  • You are responsible for ensuring personnel and operational processes as well as leading and developing your employees and learners
  • Ensure cleanliness and hygiene within the studio
  • You actively acquire future customers, draw up contracts, organize personnel planning and are responsible for various other administrative work
  • You implement the corporate strategy and represent the company against inside and outside
  • You are responsible for budget, goals and quality assurance and work closely with managers

Requirements

  • As well as experience in a similar position: several years of leadership experience
  • Completed basic or further training: Training as a fitness instructor (federal degree or equivalent) with valid BLS/AED certificate
  • You have very good industry knowledge, are well connected and strong in sales
  • You have an above-average understanding of services and have a winning personality
  • You are flexible and like to work in the evening as well as on weekends and public holidays
  • You are loyal and set an example for your employees and our customers
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central location: Our studios are located in a well-developed central location
  • Mobile subscription: National flat subscription from CHF 10.– / month (Swisscom /digitec connect)
  • Migros Bank: expense-free private and investment account with preferential interest
  • Employee offers: Various discounts in the area of holidays and insurance (Hertz up to 10%, Generali 25%, Axa insurance up to 15%, etc.)
  • Du-culture: We stand for a respectful coexistence at eye level

Contatto

Application and contact Beatrice Hardegger-Zobrist

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Expert Discussion: In a specialist discussion, specific knowledge relevant for the execution of the job is requested by the manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, the team. If possible, there is time to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition: After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks

Grado di occupazione: 100 %

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
