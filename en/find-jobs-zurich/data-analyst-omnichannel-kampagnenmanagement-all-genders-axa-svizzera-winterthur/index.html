Shape your future – together with us!

This position is located in the ecosystem of "omnichannel campaign management". We are responsible for the systematic expansion and maintenance of our customer relationships through results-oriented CRM campaigns along the customer journeys. For the further development of this strategically important area, we are looking for a personality who, on the one hand, is very knowledgeable in data technology, statistics and reporting and likes to work as a bridge-builder between business and IT.

We are currently in an exciting transformation phase in which we are migrating our system from SAP marketing to the BSI Customer Suite. To support this transition, your first task will be to revise the existing campaign reporting conceptually, to optimize it and to rebuild it technically. Once the 425 campaigns have been migrated to the BSI Customer Suite by the campaign management team, you will proactively support the team in optimizing individual campaigns, the entire campaign portfolio and channel issues using data-based analytics.

In the area of Customer Experience & Strategy, we are committed to ensuring that our customers are just as enthusiastic about AXA as we are. To achieve this goal, we are constantly developing our products and strategies. We develop innovative, inspiring solutions – and present them simply and comprehensibly to our customers, our employees and our partners as well as media professionals.

This is what awaits you

  • Support and advice to business stakeholders regarding reporting, analysis and data-driven decisions.
  • Independent connection and analysis of data from different sources and domains such as campaigns, customer, damage, sales, etc.
  • Transversal solution development with a large number of contacts from digital sales, marketing strategy, customer management, agile product teams, etc.
  • Exchange of knowledge with various departments and their experts (e.g. Data Scientist)
  • High transparency and clear priorities thanks to agile working methods and ceremonies.

You bring that with you

  • Bachelor or Master in an analytical discipline
  • Several years of experience in data analysis and knowledge of the most common analytics tools such as Power-BI, SAP Analytics Cloud, Looker Studio Pro or others
  • In-depth knowledge of SQL, Python or R and initial experience with the Google Cloud platform are also advantageous
  • A data mindset that focuses on exploration and analytics as well as solution delivery as well as experience with statistical test procedures (A/B testing, significance testing, uplift modeling) – ideally in a marketing or CRM context
  • Dealing with GenAI tools in the analysis environment (e.g. OpenCode, Gemini, co-pilot) as well as understanding of prompt engineering for data analysis issues
  • Convincing stakeholder management with step-by-step communication skills and the ability to generate impact
  • Work experience in the insurance industry and/or experience in the BSI platform – especially with CDP or CX modules – are of great advantage and a strong differentiator.
  • You speak fluent German and bring a good dose of humor
