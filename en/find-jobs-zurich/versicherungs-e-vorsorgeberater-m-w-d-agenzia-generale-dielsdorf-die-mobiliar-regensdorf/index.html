Full description

This is what awaits you

You like to be where people are, build relationships and make a difference in your own territory? Then you are right with us. For our sales area Buchs, we are looking for a committed and entrepreneurial personality who will personally accompany our customers and develop the market together with us.

This is what they do with us

They assume responsibility for the support and further expansion of an existing private and corporate customer portfolio in the Buchs area. As a personal contact person, you accompany your customers in all matters relating to insurance and pension provision.

They recognize needs, create trust and develop individual solutions from our wide range of products. At the same time, you are actively expanding your network in the region, gaining new customers and ensuring that the furniture in Buchs is perceived personally and presently. You get a lot of personal responsibility and the opportunity to actively shape your area.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training, preferably in the commercial sector
  • Professional experience in sales or customer consulting, ideally in the insurance or financial sector
  • Enjoying personal customer contact and actively attracting new customers
  • Strong network and sales orientation
  • Authentic, convincing and binding appearance
  • Self-employed, responsible and entrepreneurial working methods
  • Very good knowledge of German, other languages are a plus
  • Ideally, a personal or professional anchorage in Buchs and the surrounding region

Working with us

With us you do not expect a classic sales outlet, but the opportunity to build and shape your own area in the long term. They work independently and with a lot of creative leeway, but at the same time they are part of a well-established team of our general agency. We support you with a strong brand, professional structures, modern work equipment and targeted professional and personal development.

The Insurance Supervision Act requires for this function the conclusion of insurance intermediary /-in VBV, profile Allbranche. If you do not yet bring it, we support you with our internal training opportunities on the way there.

Job entry takes place immediately or by agreement.

This is what we offer

Participation in success

If we are successful, our employees will also benefit. Each year, the distribution of a successful share and its amount is decided.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses. We participate in job-relevant training.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer up to 25% discount on bonuses, discounts on mobile phones and free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
