Full description

At Julius Baer, we celebrate and value the individual qualities you bring, enabling you to be impactful, to be entrepreneurial, to be empowered, and to create value beyond wealth. Let’s shape the future of wealth management together.

The coach will facilitate interactive, in-person AI workshops for multidisciplinary teams, guide participants through ideation and prototyping, and provide hands-on support with generative AI, data-analysis workflows and automation concepts. Workshop materials, prompts and session outlines will be provided in advance.

Max. 10% employment level; ad hoc based on workshop schedule and mutual availability.

YOUR CHALLENGE

  • Facilitate small groups during interactive AI workshops and enable hands-on, experiential learning
  • Guide participants through brainstorming, solution design and prototyping using accessible AI techniques
  • Support exercises involving generative AI tools, data-analysis workflows and automation concepts
  • Present clearly and professionally to mixed-tenure audiences, including senior management
  • Review the materials, prompts and session outline provided ahead of each session and attend onboarding before the first workshop
  • Occasional support with additional tasks in the field of AI, such as the creation of blueprints for tools and documentation

YOUR PROFILE

  • Currently completing a degree at a university in a technical field
  • Academic background in AI, computer science, data science or a related field
  • Solid understanding of core AI and machine-learning concepts
  • Familiarity with generative AI tools and practical data workflows
  • First experience facilitating hands-on learning and responding dynamically to participant questions
  • Ability to understand and explain practical AI concepts and workflows clearly
  • Approachable and enthusiastic about collaborative learning
  • Clear, confident and professional communication
  • Strong facilitation and interpersonal skills
  • Adaptability and ability to respond dynamically to questions
  • Reliability and preparation for agreed workshop dates
