Activities

  • Independent service and repair work for private and business customers
  • Reception, assessment and execution of service orders
  • Identification of additional repair and refurbishment needs
  • Preparation of offers and cost estimates
  • Customer advice and active sales of suitable solutions
  • Planning and coordination of own operations as well as the operations of a second service installer
  • Technical support and coordination of the service installer
