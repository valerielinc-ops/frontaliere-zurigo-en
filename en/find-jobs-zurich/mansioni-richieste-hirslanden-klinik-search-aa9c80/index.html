Composition by: 01. January 2027 or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 70165

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

Our new start – your chance to participate

We are rebuilding our occupational therapy team and are looking for dedicated occupational therapists who want to actively shape this new start. You can expect to be part of a newly assembled team right from the start and, together with your future colleagues, develop existing structures, shape cooperation and further advance occupational therapy at Hirslanden Clinic.

You have the chance to contribute your experiences, ideas and strengths and to build up a modern and committed occupational therapy together with us.

YOUR TASKS

  • Independent treatment of inpatients: in the internal Stroke Center after an acute neurological event as well as from the fields of neurosurgery, geriatrics, oncology and internal medicine or in the case of deficits in coping with everyday life
  • Planning and implementation of recognised and evidence-based treatment methods in the context of medical diagnosis
  • Result and progress documentation, reporting and performance monitoring
  • Willingness to develop your expertise and make an important contribution to the success of occupational therapy at Hirslanden Clinic
  • Readiness of holiday representation and weekend services

Your profile

  • Completed training as an occupational therapist (SRC recognition for foreign education)
  • Some years of professional experience in the acute neurology field as well as in the intensive care unit are beneficial
  • Experience in neuropsychology desirable
  • Independent, responsible working methods and efficient self-organization
  • Teamwork and joy in jointly developing existing structures, optimizing processes and injecting new impulses
  • Knowledge of foreign languages is advantageous

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. You work with a modern medical clinic information system and can expect extensive training and development opportunities from us. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family responsibilities. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. Employees who have to move completely for their work receive four additional paid days off with a 100% workload. With a reduced workload, the days off reduce accordingly. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from an equivalent financial compensation. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.
