Role overview

The Department of Occupational and Environmental Medicine is jointly supported by the Institute of Epidemiology, Biostatistics and Prevention (EBPI) of the UZH and the Clinic and Polyclinic for Internal Medicine of the USZ.

It deals with the influence of working and environmental conditions on people and their health.

It is a temporary employment for 1 year, with an option of extension, which is 50% at the USZ and UZH.
