With 188 beds in the acute care sector and 126 beds in the long-term care sector, the Limmattal Hospital Association takes over the extended basic medical care of more than 100,000 patients every year. Together, interdisciplinary and with full team strength. Already during patient admission, we lay the foundation for fast, efficient, smooth and holistic care – and this is where you come into play.

Here they engage

  • Reception and administrative care of ambulatory and inpatient patients in patient reception
  • Recording and recording of patient data in the Navision ERP system
  • Examination and investigation of insurance cover for accident insurance and health insurance funds
  • Ensuring correct and complete collection of patient data
  • Contact persons for patients, relatives and external bodies in administrative matters
  • Preparation of Elective Entries
  • Email correspondence and other back office activities
  • Commercial or medical education or equivalent qualification completed
  • Experience in healthcare, especially in the areas of patient admission, administration or secretariat, is beneficial
  • Strong communication skills and a high degree of empathy
  • Structured and independent work, even in stressful situations
  • Good IT knowledge (MS Office is an advantage)
  • Good knowledge of social security
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing, further language skills are advantageous
  • Reduced employee parking
  • Motorcycle parking, lockable bicycle parking
