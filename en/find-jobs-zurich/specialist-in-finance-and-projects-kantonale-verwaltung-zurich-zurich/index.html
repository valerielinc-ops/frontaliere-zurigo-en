Application process

  • Participation in the further development of the internal control system (ICS), financial processes and digitization projects
  • Reporting and further development of directorate-wide risk management
  • Independent management of financially relevant projects You have a university degree in finance or a comparable education (e.g. auditor, expert accounting and controlling) and have several years of practical experience in accounting, controlling and / or auditing.

  • More important to us are your interest in the further development of health care, your pleasure in challenges and your analytical and conceptual skills, which you combine with implementation strength.
