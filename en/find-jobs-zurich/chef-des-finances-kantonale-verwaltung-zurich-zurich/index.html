Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Primary School Office Head of Staff Finance 80 - 100% The primary school office is responsible for the primary school area of the Canton of Zurich. As Head of Finance, you play a key role in the financial management of the primary school office. Her area of responsibility is responsible for the finances of the primary school office, from budget and four-year planning (KEF) to annual accounts and controlling. This includes the organisation of government contributions, especially in the field of special and hospital training. They advise the administration in financial matters, monitor and optimize financially relevant processes and maintain the exchange with other offices within the Education Directorate and the Cantonal Administration. Together with your team, you are the central point of contact for all financial issues, both internally and for our partners in the school field. You combine practical relevance and service orientation with correct and responsible action. As a member of the management team, you represent the primary school office and the education directorate in working groups and projects.

  • You have a university or university of applied sciences degree and several years of management experience. In addition to very good financial knowledge, you have a flair for legal issues and know the public administration. When dealing with different stakeholders, you are well-versed and secure. Analytical thinking, structured and cooperative approach as well as a clear, stylish and binding communication distinguish you, as well as a humorous, uncomplicated nature that ensures acceptance of all participants. Your cooperative management style creates orientation and promotes the personal responsibility of your employees. You encounter new developments openly and actively shape the change. Our offer It awaits you a challenging task at the interface of finance, leadership and administrative development in a collegial team.
