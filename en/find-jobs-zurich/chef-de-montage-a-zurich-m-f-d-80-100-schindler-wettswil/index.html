We are AS Elevators

AS Elevators is a Swiss-wide partner for the assembly, modernization and maintenance of elevators. The diversity and individualization of our products distinguishes us. Be as unique as we are!

Experience the AS Spirit as

Head of Assembly in Zurich (m/f/d) 80-100 %

Through your start at AS Elevators you benefit from an appreciative and family environment. In this way, we promote cohesion among employees through our du-culture and various events. Find out more about us here: Video

What to expect

  • You ensure a high-quality assembly in compliance with all safety regulations
  • You will lead a team of up to 10 installers and coordinate subcontractors on the construction site
  • You control the planning in your region – from rough to detailed planning – and together with the assembly coordination you ensure a predictive and efficient implementation of all ongoing and upcoming projects
  • You will handle customer complaints within ongoing projects in a solution-oriented and professional manner
  • You develop pragmatic solutions for technical and organizational challenges
  • You keep track of assembly progress in terms of quality, cost and deadlines
  • You work closely with sales engineers, project engineers and assembly coordinators
  • You actively participate in construction meetings and competently represent your projects there
  • You delegate tasks specifically and ensure their implementation together with internal partners

What you bring

  • Basic technical training in the electrical or mechanical sector, supplemented by commercial further training (completed or planned)
  • Several years of professional experience – ideally in the assembly or maintenance of elevator installations – as well as initial management experience
  • Good MS Office knowledge, a secure use of digital tools and ideally experience with SAP
  • A structured and solution-oriented way of working as well as the ability to maintain an overview even in demanding situations
  • And above all: You tackle, think and take responsibility

Your many advantages

  • Excellent development opportunities: Personnel development is lived with us. We provide our employees with various national and international talent programs and support them temporarily and financially with training and further education.
  • Work-life balance: 25 to 30 days of vacation, flexible working hours, part-time work and unpaid leave.
  • Healthy working environment: High health & safety standards and company health management including social counselling and health promotion.
  • Own company vehicle, which you can also use privately.
  • Employee benefits: Generous participation in public transport and fitness subscription, fleet discounts, reduced purchase of Reka money, reduced insurance premiums and various benefits.
  • Cultural diversity and inclusion & diversity within the company.
