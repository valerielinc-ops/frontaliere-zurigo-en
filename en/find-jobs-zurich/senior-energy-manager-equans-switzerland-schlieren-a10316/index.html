Senior Energiemanager — Equans Switzerland

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

You want to actively shape the energy transition and create sustainable added value for our customers with information-based solutions? Then you are exactly right for us.

Tasks

  • You continuously monitor, monitor and develop our energy management platform.
  • You analyze, plausiblisate and interpret energy and consumption data and derive optimization potential from it.
  • You ensure high data quality and process meaningful reporting and evaluations.
  • You analyze malfunctions, optimize systems sustainably and support in ensuring smooth operation.
  • You coordinate the interfaces to technical systems such as building automation, measurement systems and other systems.
  • You are responsible for energy management solutions from conception to successful implementation and operation.
  • You identify energy savings potentials at our customers and implement optimization measures in a targeted manner.
  • You accompany energy and sustainability certifications and support our customers in their implementation and compliance.
  • You take over the professional management and coordination of project participants.
  • You lead sub-projects and control external partners.
  • You continue to develop standards and best practice in energy management and certification.

Requirements

  • You have a sound basic electrical training.
  • You have trained yourself as a technician:in HF/FH or engineer:in FH in electrical engineering, energy & environment, automation or a comparable field of expertise.
  • You have several years of experience in energy management.
  • You have practical experience with energy management software (e.g. e3m or comparable solutions).
  • You have proven experience in energy optimization of buildings and technical installations.
  • You know relevant certification systems such as UZV, REida, GRESB or comparable standards.
  • You have a sound knowledge of the analysis and processing of large data volumes.
  • You understand the technical connections between energy, building and system systems (HVAC, measurement concepts and interfaces).
  • Experience in building automation is an advantage.
  • You communicate in German in a style-proof manner
  • French skills are of advantage. Other languages are welcome.

We specialise in comprehensive services in the field of facility management for every phase of the life cycle of commercial properties and areas. Buildings and infrastructures are complex systems. Whether technical operation, strategic planning or infrastructural services – we ensure maximum availability, increase operational safety and improve the comfort of buildings. This way we minimize costs and create optimal conditions for our customers to concentrate on their core business.

As a company of E Quant Switzerland, the leading comprehensive provider of holistic building and energy solutions in Switzerland, we are also driving the transformation and digitalization of our customers forward and working together at the energy transition.
