Full description

In this position, you control metal construction projects from planning to execution. The company attaches great importance to an open working environment and flat hierarchies. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Planning, coordinating and monitoring workshop and assembly operations
  • Support metal construction projects in AVOR, calculation and execution
  • Professional leadership and supporting learners
  • Advise customers and ensure appointments, quality and processes

Requirements

  • Completed training as a metalworker/EFZ
  • Experience in consulting, AVOR, calculation and team leadership
  • Organizational talent and willingness to take responsibility
  • Experience with learners as well as suitable further education is advantageous

Offer

  • Manageable team with short decision paths
