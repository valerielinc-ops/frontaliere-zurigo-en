Fire protection expert:in for preventive fire protection (m/f/d)

80%-100%, Zurich, permanent

Printed Printing

The Department of Safety, Health and Environment (SGU) advises, supports and trains all organizational units and members of ETH Zurich on questions relating to safety and health at work. It is the point of contact for all centrally organized measures in dealing with hazards and risks for the protection of people, animals, property values, infrastructure and the environment as well as for questions regarding compliance with the corresponding legal requirements. In the event of an incident, it shall maintain an appropriate emergency organisation. Both in everyday life and in the event of an incident, it works closely with the authorities and external forces.

In the field of fire and explosion protection (BEx), we are responsible for the ETH-wide organization and coordination of structural, technical and organizational measures: this includes, for example, the development and updating of internal concepts, structural and technical specifications and leaflets, leadership or participation in integral safety concepts as well as control of their implementation. We also provide information and training for ETH members and contribute our expertise to construction projects. The digital documentation of the quality assurances carried out is also one of our tasks.

In order to be able to fulfil these tasks professionally and effectively, we are looking for a committed, expert and loyal personality as a fire protection expert immediately or by agreement: in for preventive fire protection (m/f/d).

Job description

Are you ready to take responsibility for fire and explosion protection in a motivated team in everyday university life? Do you keep the overview in the event of an incident? Then you are exactly right with us:

In this function, you accompany construction projects at ETH Zurich and support the user base, project manager and specialist planner with your expertise in fire protection issues. In cooperation with authorities and planners, you are looking for suitable fire protection solutions for ETH. You review new and existing fire protection concepts and make suggestions for optimization. You also form the interface to the other sections within SGU for construction and project requests. In addition, you will advise the users and the operation on questions regarding the handling and storage of hazardous substances and explosion protection. You develop and review concepts, internal guidelines and coordinate the creation and mutation of fire documentation, such as firefighting and fire protection plans.

Project support & fire protection consultancy

  • Support of construction projects at ETH Zurich as well as advice to users, project managers and specialist planners
  • Development of suitable fire protection solutions in close cooperation with authorities and planners
  • Review of existing and new fire protection concepts incl. Submission of optimisation proposals

Interface and coordination

  • Interface to the sections within SGU for construction and project enquiries
  • Review and commissioning of the mutation of fire documentation (concepts, fire and fire plans, etc.)

Hazardous substances, explosion protection and internal requirements

  • Advising operators and users on explosion protection and the handling and storage of hazardous substances (especially combustible liquids and gases)
  • Development and review of concepts and internal requirements in the department

Profile

Training and expertise

  • Construction or technical degree with professional experience
  • Degree as Eidg. Fire protection expert:in or equivalent
  • Several years of experience in prevention
