Full description

To make Zurich greener, we are looking for people who understand their craft. Professional, organizational and as a leader. We are looking for per

  1. April 2027 or by arrangement a foreman.

As a foreman in the district of Nord 2, you are responsible for the planning and organization of care and maintenance of the green areas in the greater Wipkingen area.

Tasks

  • They lead 5 to 6 employees professionally and carry the personnel management responsibility for them and their teams. They are also jointly responsible for the practical training of our learners
  • They plan and organize demanding maintenance, maintenance, gardening and landscaping works such as planting, seeding and pruning as well as plant nutrition, plant protection and plant use and coordinate their implementation
  • They ensure the professional and natural care of the assigned green areas and are responsible for the quality of the work carried out
  • They ensure that machinery, equipment and tools are used, maintained, maintained and cleaned properly
  • They collect offers, coordinate and accompany external service providers, create reports and take on administrative tasks

Profile

  • You have completed training as a gardenerin EFZ specialisation gardening and landscaping as well as further training as a gardener in FA, green area specialist* or equivalent training
  • They bring experience in the care and maintenance of versatile gardens or public green areas, very good plant knowledge as well as experience in dealing with various machines and equipment with
  • You have leadership experience and bring strong organizational and planning skills
  • You work independently and responsibly, communicate clearly and understand your work as a service for the people and the green in Zurich
  • Good MS-Office knowledge and a category B ticket are required. Category BE is beneficial, but can also be caught up

We offer

Our cooperation at Grün Stadt Zürich is characterised by appreciation and trust, enjoyment of our work and a view of the whole.

With us you benefit from the employment conditions and social benefits of the city of Zurich: 4 to 6 weeks holidays plus 6 company holidays, the possibility to buy holidays as well as 16 weeks maternity and 4 weeks paternity leave. In addition, there are various training and further education opportunities, a contribution to your lunch check and 600 Fr. mobility contribution per year. Flexible retirement is also possible.

Your gross annual wage is between 91'000 Fr. and 108'000 Fr. Depending on your experience and skills, your effective wage may differ from this range.

About us

Zurich greener - that is our vision. Because Grün City of Zurich maintains, designs and manages the public green areas. These make up about 40 percent of the city area. With their work, our 600 employees contribute to the high quality of life in the city of Zurich.

We believe in a diverse and inclusive workforce and are proud to be an equal opportunities employer.
