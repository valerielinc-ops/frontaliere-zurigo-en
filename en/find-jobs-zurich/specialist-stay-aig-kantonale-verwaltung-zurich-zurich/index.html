Full description

Canton of Zurich Security Directorate Migration Office Specialist/in stay/AIG 80 - 100% The Migration Office of the Canton of Zurich operates daily in the exciting political environment of all migration issues and the resulting tasks. It is responsible for the implementation of the Swiss law on aliens and asylum, the Agreement on the free movement of persons between Switzerland and the EU and EFTA states as well as the Schengen and Dublin Agreements. For the Stay/AIG department we are looking for 15. January 2027 or by agreement a specialist 80 - 100%. In particular, you are responsible within the framework of the applicable provisions of the Aliens and Integration Act (AIG) for the independent processing of applications for granting permits under foreigners law as well as for measures ending residence. You check the documents of the applicants, write orders and carry out a variety of correspondence. You appreciate the written contact with the applicants, their legal representatives as well as with the authorities of the Federation, cantons and municipalities in German. For this demanding and versatile task, we require at least a commercial education (Kauffrau/Kaufmann EFZ) and some years of professional experience or a completed Bachelor. You are skilled in written expression and are interested in legal issues as well as in the structured and precise writing of complex decisions. In addition, you convince with decision-making, flexibility, resilience and high commitment. An appreciative and open communication, a sense of collaborative cooperation and a pinch of humor round off your profile. You also have Swiss citizenship. In addition to an exciting activity with a high level of personal responsibility, we guarantee you interesting contacts with the authorities of the Confederation, cantons and municipalities as well as with private stakeholders. You also benefit from modern forms of work, such as flexible working hours and an extended home office. We also promote and support your education and training. Do you feel challenged by this challenging task? Then we look forward to your complete electronic application with photo no later than 28. September 2026. For any questions, team principal Kevin Kottmann will be at your disposal from 15 September 2026 (telephone 043 259 85 50). You can find out more about the Migration Office on our homepage zh.ch/ma.

Pensum: 80-100 %
