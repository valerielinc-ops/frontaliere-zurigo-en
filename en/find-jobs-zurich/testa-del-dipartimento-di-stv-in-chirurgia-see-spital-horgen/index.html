Full description

This is what awaits you

As deputy head of the surgery department, you keep many threads together: You support the department management, accompany your team through everyday life and ensure that everything works well together even with a lot of movement at the front. With your ideas, your experience and your open ear, you make the difference.

  • You set the pace: Together with the department management, you bring structure into action, coordinate the processes and ensure that everything works well together in surgery.
  • You are the joker when it comes down to it: you recognize where support is needed, jump in and ensure that everyday life continues.
  • You build bridges instead of walls: you bring together nurses, doctors and all other professional groups and ensure a cooperation that really works.
  • You plant the care of tomorrow today: you support our trainees and students, pass on your know-how and help them to grow beyond themselves.
  • You show who we are: You represent the See Hospital and the surgery with heart, competence and team spirit.
  • You turn ideas into reality: You take on additional tasks and projects in consultation with the department management and actively shape the further development of the department.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training as a nurse (HF, FH, DNII, AKP) and experience in the acute hospital.
  • First leadership experience or desire to take the next step.
  • You communicate clearly, take action and can assert yourself.
  • You think along, develop further and like to make changes.
  • With MS Office and PC applications, you are per you.
  • Shift and weekend services you master with team spirit.
  • You know the day care and do not let yourself get out of peace even from turbulent days.

What we offer you

  • Working independently in a motivated and collegial team that supports each other.
  • A place of work with the best connection to public transport, as well as cost-effective staff parking spaces.
  • Modern infrastructure and a working environment in which you can feel comfortable – lake views included.
  • Various benefits – from great employee discounts to further education offers.
  • A pleasant working atmosphere in an attractive location near Zurich, which has a lot to offer you both professionally and privately.
  • Planning instead of just being divided: Our team is responsible for service planning.
