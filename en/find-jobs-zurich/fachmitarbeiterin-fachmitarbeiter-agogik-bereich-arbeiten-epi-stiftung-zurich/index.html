Full description

Sometimes quite challenging, but never boring: At EPI WohnWerk, no day is like the other. Because the people we accompany each and everyone have their unique life story. Thanks to the cooperation in the interprofessional team, we are well positioned for our working day. Do you want to be part of this team?

The Multifaceted Day

  • You accompany, support and promote people with cognitive, psychological or physical impairment directly in their daily work.
  • You practically implement the development goals of the clients according to our Agogik concept.
  • You have the warehouse under control: You ensure that everything in logistics (incoming goods, outgoing goods, warehouse management) runs smoothly, the safety rules are adhered to and supports the team in the implementation of the service order.
  • You plan the packaging orders for internal and external customers and ensure that everything is done on time and cleanly on the production line.
  • You pay attention to order, work safety and a gentle handling of the material.
  • You take over the administrative and organizational tasks on the PC and document the development of the clients.

Their diverse talents

  • Completed basic training as a logistics specialist / logistics specialist EFZ.
  • Additional training as a work actor / work actor or social pedagogue / social pedagogue.
  • You bring experience in accompanying people with a disability.
  • You are physically resilient and like to take part in everyday work.
  • You are in possession of a valid forklift (and/or driver's license Cat. B).
  • You are well-versed with the popular PC programs (Outlook, Word, Excel) and ideally have experience with warehouse software.
  • You speak and write good and understandable German.
  • You work accurately, independently and you can absolutely rely on you.
  • You are a team player, enjoy exchanging ideas with others and want to make a difference together with us
  • Schedules 4-6 weeks in advance with regulated free weekends
  • 5 weeks of vacation, from the 50th. Age 6 weeks
  • Annual, binding holiday planning
  • Own pension fund with an above-average employer share of 60%
  • Employee reduction in our restaurant
  • Good accessibility with public transport
  • Staff apartments, parking spaces and discounts in the pharmacy and for half-tax or general subscription
