Full description

RegioSpitex Limmattal is an important partner in healthcare RegioSpitex Limmattal is a modern, future-oriented non-profit organization with around 130 employees, which ensures the hospital external care and care for the inhabitants of Dietikon Schlieren and Urdorf. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a Dipl. Nurse HF / FH (40-100%)

Your versatile tasks are

  • You work in direct care and take over the advice and guidance of customers, relatives and employees in complex care situations
  • You carry out needs assessments with the instrument InterRAI

(Assumption of the costs of InterRAI training by RegioSpitex Limmattal)

  • As a case management, you are responsible for planning and implementing the care process for your customers.
  • You play an active role in cooperation with the supervising, interdisciplinary network and coordinate the corresponding services for your customers

You bring with you

  • You have a degree as a dipl. Nursing specialist HF / FH as well as ideally appropriate professional experience in this area
  • You are an open-minded and flexible personality who likes to approach people
  • You bring good communication skills as well as safe handling of mobile care documentation (smartphone)
  • You think networked, work independently and responsibly

We offer you

  • A versatile and challenging workspace where you can use and develop your expertise and experience
  • A working environment characterized by respect and appreciation
  • A careful introduction to the various home care tasks
  • Attractive employment conditions and modern infrastructure
  • Freely selectable workload and no shared services
  • Generous training and education opportunities
  • All employee benefits
  • Du-culture at all levels
