Full description

Your heart beats for older people and you want to actively shape care quality? With us, you combine professional responsibility with an appreciative leadership and help ensure that our guests can live in a self-determined and safe manner.

Head of care and care

Tertianum Residenz Horgen is located between Zimmerberg and Lake Zurich. We combine living in apartments with in-house Spitex as well as care and care in the nursing living area. The focus is on the individual needs and quality of life of our guests.

Their tasks

  • Responsibility for the quality of care and the implementation of the service mandate as well as the management and further development of care and care processes.
  • Further development of the nursing living area and coordination of processes with the in-house Spitex, so that our guests are well accompanied in the event of changes in nursing needs.
  • Personnel and technical management of subordinates
  • Promote binding and appreciative cooperation.
  • Recruitment, structured introduction and further development of employees in cooperation with the management and HR.
  • Ensure needs-based personnel and deployment planning, taking into account skills and available resources.
  • Ensuring complete care documentation in CareCoach as well as correct care needs and performance recording.
  • monitoring of quality indicators, staffing and budget
  • Derivation of suitable measures and participation in a needs-based occupation in coordination with administration and management.
  • Coordination of entries, transfers and exits in cooperation with the triage, the in-house Spitex and the experts involved.
  • Trusting cooperation with guests, relatives, doctors, hospitals and other partners
  • Accompanying demanding discussions.
  • Implementation and review of care, hygiene and safety standards and promotion of an open learning and error culture.
  • Close cooperation with the management and the areas of administration, housekeeping, catering and technical service.

Your profile

  • Completed training as a dipl. nurse HF/FH/DN II or equivalent training recognised by the SRC.
  • professional and managerial experience in long-term care
  • Experience in outpatient care as well as in dealing with people with dementia is beneficial.
  • At least one to two years of experience as Head of Care and Assistance or at least three to four years of leadership experience as Group or Team Leader.
  • Completed leadership training or willingness to complete a corresponding training.
  • Security in the control of the care process, in quality assurance and in dealing with electronic care documentation.
  • Organizational strength, business understanding and an independent, solution-oriented working method.
  • An empathetic and decision-making leader with team spirit, warmth and humor.
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing as well as clear, addressee-oriented communication.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
