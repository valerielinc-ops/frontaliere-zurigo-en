Each mission is different and requires expertise, flexibility and the ability to find workable solutions on site. This is what awaits you

  • Mobile physiotherapy for patients at home
  • Structured operational and scheduling with clear processes
  • High autonomy in everyday work and self-responsible work

  • Work outside of a classic practice

  • without reapplication process
  • Without administrative effort
  • With secured workplace
  • in a trusted team and working environment

This is how you combine professional stability with personal freedom. You bring that with you

  • Completed vocational training as a physiotherapist
  • Enjoy independent and structured work
  • High social skills and empathy
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • You like to travel (car or bicycle)
  • Good IT user knowledge is beneficial

This is what we offer you

  • Small, uncomplicated team with flat hierarchies
  • Very high flexibility in working hours and assignment planning
  • Own company vehicle (also for private use)
