Full description

Personal – competent – close Welcome to the Hospital Uster, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As the central pillar of health care in the Oberland region of Zurich and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you experience the highest level of expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and promoted in your professional development. Spital Uster is a teaching hospital of the Medical Faculty of the University of Zurich.Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of a "regional and digital health hub in Züri East"! For support in the area of corporate development we are looking for 1. October 2026 or an agreement for 6 to 9 months

Exciting challenges await you

  • Support in the design and implementation of transformation and project portfolio processes
  • Participation in strategic and digital business development projects as well as support in the implementation of defined work packages
  • Participation in building digital skills and promoting understanding of digital developments in the hospital environment
  • Research and preparation of trends, digital solutions and innovations with a focus on future developments in healthcare
  • Preparation of presentations, decision-making and communication documents as well as support during workshops
  • Collaboration in agile and classical project environments and support of the project teams in cooperation
  • Support for the introduction of new projects
  • Organization and preparation of appointments, documentation of meetings, as well as administrative support as required

What sets you apart for the job

  • Completed or ongoing studies (uni/FH/HF) – e.g. in economics, health care, management, social sciences or comparable
  • Interest in transformation, digitalization and the further development of a regional hospital
  • Interest in strategy analyses and their interpretation
  • Pronounced digital affinity and pleasure in understanding new technologies and their applications in the hospital environment
  • Initial experience or interest in agile and classic project management
  • Structured, independent and reliable working methods as well as enjoyment of working with different stakeholders
  • Good communication skills and experience in the target group-oriented preparation of presentations and content
  • Curiosity, initiative and the motivation to actively participate in change processes
  • Enjoy working with different stakeholders and interfaces
  • open, communicative and committed personality with own initiative
  • very good knowledge of German in word and writing

6 reasons to work with us

  • versatile internship in a dynamic hospital environment
  • Direct insight into the company’s development and can contribute your ideas
  • work closely with the management of corporate development, get responsibility and develop skills that will help you advance in the long term.
  • Excellent staff restaurant and numerous discounts
  • Central location near Uster station and motorway connection
  • Cheap parking, max. CHF 4.50 per day
