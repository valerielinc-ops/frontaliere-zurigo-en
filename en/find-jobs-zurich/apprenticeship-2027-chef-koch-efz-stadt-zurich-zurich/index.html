Full description

The kitchen is your world. You enjoy planning fine dishes and preparing all the courses of a menu from starter to dessert. Equipment, cooking, roasting, and baking are your profession.

Tasks

  • You prepare hot and cold foods and sauces.
  • You process the ingredients professionally and set the dishes.
  • You order food and drinks and store them.
  • You check the orders and the quality of the food.
  • You ensure a smooth flow in the kitchen and implement the hygiene and safety regulations.

Profile

  • Compulsory schooling, upper or middle school
  • Good knowledge of German and good performance in mathematics, especially in mental arithmetic and three sets
  • Good sense of taste and smell, organisational talent and resilience
  • Good health, employees in the kitchen are on their feet all day
  • Teamwork and willingness to work irregularly at two different locations

We offer

You can expect a well-founded and varied education. Your apprentices support you on your way to a successful teaching degree. You benefit from attractive employment conditions, such as regulated working hours, no room hours, occasional weekend assignments from the 2nd year of apprenticeship.
