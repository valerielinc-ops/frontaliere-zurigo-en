Head of HSG ETH Student Project House 80%-100%, Zurich, fixed-term print Drucken At the Student Project House (SPH) we inspire and empower students to cultivate a maker and innovator mindset. We believe that by developing their own projects, students learn important competencies for their future. This is why we’ve created an ecosystem to inspire students (Bachelor, Master, and Doctoral students) to get started and explore the real need behind their idea. And to seek diverse views, to work as a team, to think big, to fail, to pivot, and to keep overcoming obstacles they encounter along the way. Our interdisciplinary team of thirty people designs and manages facilities like co-working, ideation spaces as well as a makerspace and lab that gives students access to the tools and equipment they need to prototype. We support project teams with personal coaching, workshops, and a pro-bono expert network from industry. Our first location was established at ETH Zurich. As we scale and expand beyond our original base, we are launching a second location in St.Gallen in collaboration with the University of St.Gallen (HSG) and START Foundation. We are creating a sister organisation in the spirit of SPH in Zurich, with a large focus on building a community of ETH and HSG students who learn from each other through peer-to-peer learning. We are now seeking a Head of HSG ETH SPH to lead this new chapter. Project background You’ll work in one of the most inspiring spaces in St.Gallen, surrounded by whiteboards, ideation equipment, colourful furniture, and creative students. We’re located at the START Campus, 5 minutes away from both St.Gallen station and HSG. We work in the same open space as our project teams and meet with everyone at eye level. The individuals we work with are from a diverse range of backgrounds and study disciplines, which is why we communicate in English. As the projects we support are extracurricular, most of our offers take place in the late afternoon, in the evening, and occasionally at weekends. The projects are very diverse regarding topic, stage, intensity, and goal - some being entrepreneurial and others non-profit. We take an agile approach to conceptualizing and delivering our offer and allocate our capacity flexibly to the most important tasks. Hereby, teamwork is one of our key values.   • Leadership & Team: You will hire and build a great team of motivated people who bring our second location to success, starting with 2-3 FTEs. Your first hires will be Innovation Catalysts who support the development of the location and coach the projects. You foster a strong, value-driven culture aligned with ETH SPH in Zurich.
  • Strategy & Offers: Together with your team and the Board, you will define and execute strategy for the St.Gallen location. You will develop and manage a portfolio of student-centred services for HSG and ETH students, ensuring high quality delivery and efficient processes. You will build on existing ETH SPH structures and learnings rather than start from scratch.
  • Stakeholder Management: You will manage relationships with key stakeholders, including politicians, donors, university leadership, professors, and the Board. Working across ETH, HSG, and the START Foundation, you will secure long-term support beyond the initial two-year pilot phase by making the stakeholders excited about supporting this opportunity.
