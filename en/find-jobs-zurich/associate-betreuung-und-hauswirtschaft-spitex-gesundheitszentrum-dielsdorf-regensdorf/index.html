Full description

Dielsdorf Health Centre is a modern geriatric competence centre with around 500 employees from 35 nations. Our offer ranges from consulting services for older people and their relatives, general and specialized long-term care to care and care at home. As the largest public Spitex in the district of Dielsdorf we provide outpatient services for our nine contract municipalities. With our comprehensive care offer, we allow people to stay in their home as long as possible despite health restrictions. In the future it is planned to establish a night hospital. Tasks:
