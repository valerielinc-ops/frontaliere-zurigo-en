Would you like to re-enter after a family phase or exchange a hospital day for a planned outpatient activity? You take on an already well-utilized office hour and can start with a workload of 60%. The practice, which opened last year, is located in the attractive Lake Zurich region and offers patients comprehensive outpatient care in the areas of gynecology and obstetrics. The gynecological consultation hour is already fully utilized and has a waiting list. There is therefore a reliable demand for the new job holder right from the start. The interdisciplinary medical team works in modern practice facilities and attaches importance to personal medicine, short decision-making paths and collaborative cooperation.

Their tasks

  • You independently look after your patients in the entire spectrum of outpatient gynecology and obstetrics
  • carry out preventive gynecological examinations, investigations and consultations;
  • They accompany patients from the region long-term and build up personal treatment relationships
  • If necessary, they work together with the other medical specialties within the practice
  • You contribute your ideas to the further development of the still young practical structures

Your Competences

  • Specialist title Gynecology and Obstetrics or recognised equivalent qualification
  • Three years of professional experience in a recognised continuing education institution in Switzerland
  • Independent, empathetic and patient-oriented workflow
  • Very good knowledge of German and secure IT application
  • Advanced training in senology or genetic counselling
  • Certificate of pregnancy ultrasound not required

Their advantages

  • Already busy office hours with existing waiting list
  • Starting at 60% with flexible work and holiday planning
  • 40% share in turnover personally generated
  • Suitable for re-entry or change from hospital
  • Modern infrastructure and short decision paths
  • Participation in a young, growing practice
