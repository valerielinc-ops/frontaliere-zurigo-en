Full description

Life is too short for bad software – we are looking for smart minds! Become part of our 70-strong IT team, full of energy, creativity and team spirit.Our team consists of innovative business analysts, crazy programmers, helpful supporters and many other employees. Are you ready to make a difference together? Then apply now!

Tasks

  • You will further develop our COBOL applications and optimize them for more efficient logistics processes in the Transport Management System (TMS)
  • Together with the specialist areas, you will implement adjustments and extensions and thus improve the transport-specific processes
  • Performance optimization and a stable system landscape in the transport area of the Planzer Group are firmly in view
  • Integrate new functions and interfaces seamlessly into the TMS and ensure smooth communication with shipping solutions and middleware
  • Technical documentation, tests and code reviews are also part of your task area
  • When it comes to technological modernization, you get involved – whether it’s migrating legacy code or introducing new technologies
  • You actively participate in specialist meetings and can actively contribute your ideas and perspectives

Profile

  • Analytical thinking, organizational talent and an independent, process-oriented way of working distinguish you
  • You are a team player with a sense of responsibility, initiative and convincing communication skills
  • You approach challenges in a solution-oriented and pragmatic way – methodical thinking is your strength
  • In agile work processes like SCRUM and KANBAN you are at home, tools like DevOps are part of your daily work
  • In databases (DB2 and SQL) you are familiar with and design queries and data structures efficiently
  • Programming with COBOL is in your blood
  • You communicate safely in German in word and writing and English skills are a plus

Benefits

  • With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • With us you start with 5 weeks vacation and benefit from the 5th year of service even from 6 weeks!
  • We go with the times - enjoy with us the advantage of Homeoffice

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
