Full description

To strengthen our research and development team in Volketswil or Winterthur, we are looking for an independent and reliable personality as: Head of Product Safety 80 - 100% (preferably 80%) Responsible in this central position They cover all aspects of product safety - from the raw materials used to our manufactured products and instructions on the internal handling of chemicals. They report directly to the Director R&D and thus to the management. Their tasks

  • Professional management and coordination of all topics related to the product safety of our products and raw materials
  • Early identification and evaluation of regulatory trends and ensuring compliance with the resulting requirements
  • Participation in sustainability and AI issues, in particular in the evaluation of raw materials and ensuring data quality
  • Creation of documents (e.g. safety data sheets, labeling) and responsibility for their accuracy and timeliness
  • Sparring partner for R&D and product management with important inputs on product compliance
  • Contact person for authorities (e.g. FOPH), responsibility for processes and audits in this regard as well as monitoring and coordination of audits
  • Coordination with central product safety offices of the Group and other internal offices (e.g. EHS)
  • Planning and implementation of internal training and other training and information measures
  • Participation in cross-divisional projects as well as in process optimization Your profile
  • Completed studies in natural sciences, preferably chemistry (FH/Uni), or comparable training
  • Several years of experience (> 5 years) in the field of product safety, ideally in the building materials environment
  • Experience in dealing with standards, regulations and laboratory data
  • Affinity to IT and interest in working with and developing AI solutions
  • In-depth knowledge of REACH, CLP, GHS etc. as well as the ability to proactively advance these topics
  • Knowledge of the European Construction Products Regulation is an advantage
  • Good network with relevant stakeholders (authorities, environmental labels, professional associations, etc.)
  • Safe handling of chemical compliance and SDS management systems (ChemGes desirable)
  • Very good knowledge of German and English, French is advantageous
  • Structured, precise and reliable working methods as well as a high sense of responsibility and initiative
  • Flexibility, resilience and solution orientation Their perspectives
  • Exciting tasks in a dynamic company
  • Corporate culture with space for personal responsibility and entrepreneurial ideas
  • A working environment in which openness and appreciation are a matter of course
  • Attractive employment conditions with above-average social benefits
  • Individual introductory programme adapted to the functional requirements
  • Support for your individual professional development "MAKING THE WORLD A BETTER HOME" - that's our purpose.
