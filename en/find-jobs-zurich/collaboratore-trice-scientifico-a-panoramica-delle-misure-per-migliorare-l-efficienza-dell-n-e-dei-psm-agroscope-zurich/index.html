Put to the Point

Nitrogen, phosphorus and plant protection products should be used as efficiently as possible in agriculture. As a scientific expert, you will use literature research to develop an overview of the known agronomic measures for improving resource efficiency and prepare the findings relevant for Swiss agriculture in an understandable way. Together with the Research Area Sustainability Assessment and Agricultural Management (FB NBA), you will then analyse the implementation status of these measures in practice on the basis of surveys of consultancy centres, industry associations and agricultural enterprises. The findings from this project are intended to support a well-founded further development and targeted design of agricultural policy measures. As a scientific employee, you are responsible for the operational management, planning, implementation and evaluation of this project. We are looking for a bright and solution-oriented personality who likes to create well-founded decision-making bases in close coordination with various experts from research, consulting, practice and administration.

Good food, healthy environment

Agroscope is the federal competence centre for research in the agricultural and food industry. The researchers carry out their activities at various locations in Switzerland. The headquarters is Bern-Liebefeld (from 2026: Posieux FR). Agroscope is located at Eidg. Department of Economy, Education and Research WBF.
