Full description

For the emergency sleeping area we are looking for 1. March 2027 for 7 months a motivated person.

Tasks

  • You accept the user’s registrations, clarify the individual needs and inform about the available services.
  • You take responsibility for caring for a group of adults according to generally accepted socio-pedagogical standards.
  • You actively support users in the search for connection solutions and communicate them to medical, psychological and social agencies.
  • You ensure that the basic rules of social behavior are adhered to and you intervene professionally in conflicts and emergency situations.

Profile

  • Study of social work at a technical college (preferably in the 2nd internship)
  • At least 25 years old
  • Resilience, self-confidence and high flexibility
  • Readiness to look after users in the evening (about 20:00/20:30) until the following morning (about 11:00). Between 01:00 and 07:00 you are present during the night shift, retreat to sleep and stand ready when necessary to stand up and support and accompany the users.
  • Independent working methods and strong teamwork
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing as well as good IT user knowledge

We offer

With us, you can expect a varied and demanding job in a socially important and not everyday working environment. You will become part of a dedicated and supportive team that will accompany you during your internship. For night and Sunday work as well as for the attendance time you receive attractive allowances. In addition, we offer you the opportunity to gain valuable practical experience and directly support people in difficult situations.

About us

With around 2,400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a social Zurich. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.

The emergency dormitory offers homeless people from the city of Zurich free of charge a bed for the night, food and a laundry facility. Employees look after and advise users and look for appropriate housing solutions with them. Professionals provide situational care 365 days a year. They mediate to institutions and outpatient medical facilities, they support urgent problems and help with medical emergencies.
