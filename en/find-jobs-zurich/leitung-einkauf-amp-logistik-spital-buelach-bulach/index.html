<b>Bülach Hospital grows - grow with it.</b>

Tasks

  • Overall responsibility for economic, qualitative and needs-based purchasing
  • Development and implementation of the purchasing and storage strategy as well as ongoing optimization of processes
  • Active procurement market research and further development of the product range
  • Public procurement (submissions)
  • Establishment and further development of the supplier portfolio, including negotiations, contract drafting and supplier evaluation
  • Leadership of the team as well as management of the Material Commission and cooperation in cross-divisional projects

Requirements

  • In-depth business education with a focus on purchasing (e.g. buyer with federal law). Professional card or higher vocational training
  • Professional and managerial experience, if possible in healthcare
  • Good knowledge of public procurement
  • Negotiation skills and economic thinking
  • Structured, independent working method
  • Communication strength and strong social skills
  • Good MS Office and ERP user knowledge (advantageous Opale)
