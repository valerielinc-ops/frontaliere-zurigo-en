Full description

The training takes place within SRG SSR and takes place in the SRF regional unit in Zurich.

SRG SSR is the largest media company in Switzerland. With its corporate units SRF, RTS, RSI and RTR, it produces and distributes content in the areas of information, education, culture, entertainment and sport. As a learner, you will become part of a diverse and future-oriented company that connects people across Switzerland and reaches millions of users on different platforms every day.

This internship is part of the basic training to become a businessman (organized at school).

As a graduate of the commercial college, you can complete a one-year internship with us and graduate with the EFZ as a businesswoman with a vocational baccalaureate.

What are your tasks?

You will gain practical experience and engage in the following tasks:

  • Insight into the diverse world of human resources
  • You support the Leadership & Further Education team and take on tasks and projects for young talent & careers.
  • You organize some events for the learners and vocational trainers
  • You work in different HR systems and learn to use them

What are you bringing with you?

  • Organizational, teamwork and communication skills
  • Working responsibly and independently
  • Motivation and reliability
  • Interested in a diverse media world

What can you look forward to?

  • Insights and collaboration into exciting areas of work
  • Relaxed professional trainers who will support you with all your concerns
  • Trained learners, regular team events
  • 6 weeks vacation

How can you apply? Read the application guidelines carefully and send us your application documents using the “Apply now” button.

Our promise

What does it take to reach people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expertise, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can contribute and develop. In order for the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: welcome to the world of SRG!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, encourage creativity and offer plenty of scope for new and high-quality products.
  • Shaping digitalization: For us, quality journalism is also digital. That's why we're exploring new possibilities and experimenting with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-media. We design in tune with the times and the audience's nerves.
  • Be human: We treat each other in a collegial manner, work in a self-determined manner and individually shape our working atmosphere. Our success is based as much on the personal strengths as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programs. We act respectfully and independently
  • In this way, we promote the formation of opinions and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.
