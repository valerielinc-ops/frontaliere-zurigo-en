Full description

You will support our team in the construction, operation and maintenance of medium, low and high voltage systems and ensure that our network remains safe and stable with fast, reliable operations.

Tasks

  • You are involved in the construction, operation and maintenance of network systems in the medium, low and high voltage networks as well as on overhead lines.
  • The pipe network is constantly maintained by you and your teammates.
  • Disturbances in the network? You and your teammates will fix these immediately.
  • Periodic on-call service is a given for you.
  • Your travel time from your home to your place of work is a maximum of 60 minutes.

profile

  • You have completed an apprenticeship as a network electrician EFZ or electrician EFZ.
  • Ideally, you already have some initial professional experience in network construction.
  • You work independently, carefully and precisely and see yourself as a reliable team player. You are also robust, free from dizziness and physically resilient.
  • You have a category B driving license.
  • You have mastered German both orally and in writing.

We offer

  • More energy for your life: Through mobile working, flexible working models and part-time options, you can actively shape your working hours and organize them in the way that best suits your life. For example, you can buy up to 2 weeks of additional vacation and work part-time at all levels. Job sharing/splitting and unpaid vacation are of course also possible with us.
  • Travel sustainably and cheaply: With the mobility contribution of up to 600 francs per year, you can travel cheaply and sustainably - regardless of whether you want to cycle through Zurich or explore Switzerland by train.
  • An investment in your future: We not only value paying fair and transparent wages, but also ensure that you have more left over from your wages. We cover 60% of the savings contributions and also offer extra-mandatory interest on the capital saved.
  • An energy boost for your free time: We pay you 100 francs a month onto your Lunch-Check card. You also benefit from additional Fringe benefits worth 150 francs every year and attractive mobile phone subscriptions.
  • Development and further training: With our talent management, we specifically promote your development – ​​through a wide range of internal and external further training offers as well as exciting development opportunities.
  • Meaningful work: For us, sustainability and meaningful work are not just words: help shape Zurich and the future with us. Release energy for a good tomorrow together with us.
