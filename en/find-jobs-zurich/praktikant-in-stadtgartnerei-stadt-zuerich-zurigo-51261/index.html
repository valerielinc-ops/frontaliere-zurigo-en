Full description

Are you ready for a job where you can make a meaningful difference for people and nature? With immediate effect or by appointment, you have the opportunity to do so, temporarily for one year, as an intern in the Stadtgärtnerei, our Center for Plants and Education.

Tasks

  • They reproduce and cultivate group plants and cut flowers
  • They equip cut flowers for our floristry
  • They nurture the open-air crops
  • You will have the opportunity to research special cultures
  • They support and participate in educational offers such as events, exhibitions or courses

Profile

  • Gardening knowledge of advantage or the will to pursue an academic career. (Environmental sciences, biology with interest in practical implementation)
  • Joy of working with plants
  • Teamwork
  • Category B driving licence is advantageous

We offer

An internship at the City of Zurich provides insights into everyday work, offers the opportunity to get a concrete picture of a profession, gain initial professional experience and opens up prospects for further professional career.

About us

Zurich greener - that is our vision. Because Grün City of Zurich maintains, designs and manages the public green areas. These make up about 40 percent of the city area. With their work, our 580 employees contribute to the high quality of life in the city of Zurich.

We believe in a diverse and inclusive workforce and are proud to be an equal opportunities employer.
