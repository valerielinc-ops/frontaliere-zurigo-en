Moderatorin / Moderator Arena — SRG SSR

Full description

The Information Department produces numerous offers for audio, video and SRF's digital channels. With a wide range of news and background formats as well as classifying discussion and advice programmes on current events in politics, business and society, SRF makes it easier for the audience to form their opinions – informatively, competently and independently.

What are your tasks?

The "Talk Information" department works in multimedia and is responsible, among other things, for the "Arena" programme. It brings Swiss politics to the people and contributes to the formation of opinion.

From January 2027 or by appointment, you will present three editions of the "Arena" every month:

  • As the presenter of the "Arena", you are the face of the programme and, supported by an experienced team, regularly present one of the central discussion programmes in German-speaking Switzerland
  • In cooperation with the Executive Producer, they design the "Arena" in such a way that every Friday there is an engaged and controversial discussion on current topics with top-class guests from politics, business and society
  • They set priorities in terms of content and, together with the producer, are responsible for the dramaturgical elaboration of the debate. They work as a team to develop the programme sequence, look for questions and formulations
  • You deal intensively with political topics, processes and debates, you research and prepare thoroughly for the debates in terms of content. You also actively participate in the selection of topics, research and acquisition of "Arena" guests

What do you bring to the table?

  • Extensive experience in controversial interviews in front of the camera. You conduct talks and interviews confidently and also perform in live situations. Please link your own examples of interviews and moderations in front of the camera in your application documents
  • Very good knowledge of Swiss politics and business as well as important personalities from these fields, high affinity for news
  • Ability and interest to delve beyond the headlines and current affairs into a political topic
  • You are a team player, are extraordinarily resilient and rely on arguments in journalistic dialogue, but can also withstand conflicts

What can you look forward to?

  • As a presenter of the "Arena", you dive deep into a political dossier every week and are enormously close to political events and the decisive players. You penetrate contexts and know a lot that is not public. You are very close to Bern and national politics – and at the same time maintain a spatial and critical distance
  • A well-behaved, very collegial team that is happy to share its professional experience with you
  • Great creative possibilities, room for unconventional ideas and creativity in terms of content, a lot of personal responsibility
  • We will be happy to accept your application as a moderator until 18. October 2026. The interviews will take place on Thursday, 22 and Friday, 23. October. We look forward to seeing you!

What we offer

What does it take to reach people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expert knowledge, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can get involved and develop. In order for the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: Welcome to the world of SRF!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, promote creativity and offer a lot of creative freedom for new and high-quality things.
  • Shaping digitalization: For us, quality journalism is also digital. That's why we explore new possibilities and experiment with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-media. We design at the cutting edge and on the nerve of the audience.
  • Being human: We treat each other in a collegial manner, work in a self-determined manner and help shape our working atmosphere in a very individual way. Our success is based on the personal strengths as well as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programmes. In doing so, we act respectfully and independently; in this way, we promote opinion-forming and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.
