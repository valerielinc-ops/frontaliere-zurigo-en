Full description

Founded in 1836, the Swiss family business is today one of the most renowned confiseries in Europe with its exquisite range. The products from Sprüngli stand for the best quality, unique freshness and naturalness. The completed creations bring customers from all over the world to swarm daily.

Working at Sprüngli

In order to strengthen our teams of various branches in the Zurich area (main station, parade square), we are looking for a service-oriented personality as

What you do with us

  • Advising our national and international customers with excellent service quality
  • Management and presentation of our exquisite Sprüngli products with decorative flair
  • Receiving customer orders and preparing for collection
  • Various management tasks (organization of the daily routine, contact person for customers and employees, etc.)
  • Ordering through MSB5
  • Personnel planning in consultation with the branch manager
  • Preparation of daily and monthly financial statements using TAVS

What we want

  • Completed training in the retail trade (preferably confiserie or food industry)
  • Several years of sales experience
  • First leadership experience
  • Flowing knowledge of German and English
  • Initiative and teamwork
  • Professional appearance and good manners
  • Engaged personality with style, passion and high quality awareness
  • Flexibility in terms of working hours (shift work between 5.45 and 21.30 and 2 to 3 weekend assignments per month)
