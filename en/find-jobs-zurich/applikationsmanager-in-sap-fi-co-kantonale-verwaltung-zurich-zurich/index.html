Role overview

Canton of Zurich Financial Directorate Office for Computer Science Application Manager/-in SAP FI/CO 80 - 100% The Office for Computer Science (AFI) is the computer science competence center of the Canton of Zurich.

It provides comprehensive services in the area of central computer infrastructure and canton-wide applications for directorates, authorities, the administration of justice and independent institutions.

In charge of implementing the new Information and Communication Technology (ICT) strategy, the Office accompanies the canton in the digital world.

Do you not only want to support SAP solutions, but also develop them independently and take responsibility for them from requirements to stable operation?

In our versatile SAP landscape, you take on a comprehensive role with a lot of personal responsibility.

Together with the Business Solutions team and our internal clientele, you will develop viable solutions for the financial processes of the Canton of Zurich.

Your tasks

  • Together with the team, you ensure the stable operation of our SAP-FI/CO solutions and deal with issues and problems in 2nd-level support.
  • You take requirements of stakeholders in the context of operation, service and change requests, question them constructively and translate them into suitable technical solutions.
  • You implement requirements independently and continuously develop the supported applications and processes.
  • You specify development contracts, control internal developers and external partners and check the quality of the implemented solutions.
  • Completed training in business administration, business informatics, computer science or a similar field.

Key requirements

  • Completed training in business administration, business informatics, computer science or a similar field.
