Full description

You like to be with people. You like to accompany our residents in their everyday life so that they can live as independently as possible. You learn to look after and care for people and are supported by your team.

Tasks

  • You help people with health and personal care.
  • You control their vital signs.
  • You serve meals, help with food and mobilization in everyday life.
  • You take on work in the room care, laundry and kitchen.
  • You will perform minor administrative and organizational tasks.

Profile

  • Completed compulsory schooling, Sek B or good performance in Sek C, knowledge of German (minor level B2)
  • Responsibility and careful work
  • empathy and patience
  • Ability to cooperate with others and secrecy
  • Willingness to work irregular hours (taking into account the protection of minors)

We offer

You can expect a well-founded and varied education. Your vocational trainees support you on your way to a successful apprenticeship as AGS. You benefit from attractive employment conditions.

About us

At one of our 34 locations throughout the city of Zurich, you will lay the foundation for your career: Become part of our team and learn from the best. From the beginning you are in the middle of it and not only there. With more than 3,300 employees from more than 100 nations, we are one of the largest healthcare training companies in Switzerland. We currently train 800 apprentices, students and interns in a wide variety of professional groups. We look after and care for people of older age with competence and heart, contribute to their well-being and offer them a comfortable home. With us, the human being is at the center.
