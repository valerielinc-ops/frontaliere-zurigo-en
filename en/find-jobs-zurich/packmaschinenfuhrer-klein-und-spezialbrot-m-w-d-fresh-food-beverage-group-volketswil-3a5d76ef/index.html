Full description

We produce favorite products that inspire - in Switzerland and for Switzerland. Do you have experience in the food sector and technical understanding of machines and packaging systems? Do you enjoy working in a team and it excites you to work in the latest production plant in the Migros industry? Great, then apply now at the most popular bakery in Switzerland.

Mansioni

  • Setting up, controlling, operating and monitoring production and packaging facilities
  • Manufacture of various doughs
  • Quality control of manufactured products
  • Detect problems and ensure troubleshooting
  • Optimizing processes and plants as part of our continuous improvement processes
  • Compliance with safety, hygiene and quality regulations
  • Active participation in production in the early, late and night shift
  • Training of learners in the task area
  • General cleaning work

Requirements

  • Experience as an investment leader: an advantage
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): food technologist, baker, pastry chef or similar (also EBA possible)
  • Clean, accurate and forward working methods
  • Flexibility and physical and mental resilience
  • Reliable, team-capable, responsible and independent personality
  • Advanced technical understanding
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Employee offers: Employee Points Cumulus, Discount Mobile Subscriptions, Higher Interest Rates Migros Bank, Club School/Fitness contributions and more.
  • Holidays: 5 weeks holiday for all, 6 weeks holiday from 50 years of age, 7 weeks holiday from 60 years of age, additional purchase of additional holiday days possible.
  • Occupational pension provision: Comprehensively secured in the Migros pension fund – with first-class and attractive services.
  • Handy subscriptions: discounted mobile subscriptions at Wingo, Digitec and Sunrise for you and if necessary for three other people you know.
  • Social benefits: 730 days of sick leave with full net wages.
