Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Vocational Advice Center Breitenstein Social Worker/in Adult Protection 60 - 80% The Office for Youth and Vocational Counselling of the Education Directorate provides, appoints, controls and coordinates offers of child and youth welfare and vocational counselling. Zentrum Breitenstein is part of the Office for Youth and Vocational Counselling and is supported and co-financed by the municipalities of the district of Andelfingen. Zentrum Breitenstein provides social services in the areas of child and adult protection, personal assistance, advice and prevention for the population of the district of Andelfingen. The offer is aimed at families, young people, children and adults with special needs, social difficulties and/or addiction problems. Our team will be enlarged – together we can make a difference! The Breitenstein Center in Andelfingen is looking for an experienced and committed social worker for adult protection. Together with your team, you provide adult support for 22 municipalities in the district of Andelfingen, which KESB Winterthur-Andelfingen has arranged. The self-determination and personal responsibility of the clients are always in the foreground. The following tasks await you:

  • Management of mandates in accordance with the mandate of KESB, usually with management of income and assets
  • Writing of applications and reports to authorities
  • Representation in personal, legal and financial matters
  • Actively shaping the further development of professional assistance We expect you to:
  • Completed studies Social work FH
  • Professional experience in adult protection or social assistance
  • In-depth knowledge of private law, social security law and social assistance law
  • Empathy towards people in difficult life situations
  • Psychological resilience and flexibility as well as negotiating-safe performance skills What can you expect from us? You can expect a responsible and interesting activity with scope for independent work. Your workplace is located directly at the Andelfingen station and can be reached in about 40 minutes from Zurich HB. In the administrative area, you will be supported by expert clerks. We follow the KOKES recommendations, attach importance to an appreciative working atmosphere and promote the further training of our employees. The collegial exchange as well as intervisions and supervisions help you to focus your consulting content on the essentials and to act safely even in challenging situations. We look forward to your online application. If you have any questions, Karin Fröhlich, Head of Adult Protection (tel. 043 258 46 50) is at your disposal. www.ajb.zh.ch

Pensum: 60-80 %
