Do you want to do a sustainable apprenticeship? You are curious, cosmopolitan, want to make a difference and grow personally at the same time? Want to be on the pulse of time? Then you are exactly right at ETH Zurich, because this is where the future arises.

Job description

The ideal teaching for skilled craftsmen: women with enthusiasm for technology and natural sciences!

With a team of scientists, you work on groundbreaking research projects and help shape the future. With a logical understanding, you immerse yourself in the world of physics-technical topics and, in cooperation with international colleagues, put your ingenious ideas into reality:

  • Conventional materials processing
  • Making CAD designs
  • Developing and understanding electronic circuits and fabricating PCB designs
  • Performing measurements of physical quantities such as temperature, pressure, tension and force
  • Acquisition and evaluation of data and measurement series
  • Manufacture and assembly of prototypes
  • Programming
  • Photography

The training lasts 4 years and takes place mainly on the Hönggerberg campus of the ETH. During the apprenticeship, you will change location according to the rotation principle and get to know the ETH from different angles. You will have an insight into different research groups with different technical focuses (vacuum technology, micro- and nanotechnology, optics, construction, electronics and other exciting focuses).

Profile

If you bring craftsmanship, technology and physics enthusiasm and you enjoy complex questions, then training as a physics lab technician: in EFZ is just right for you!

For this vocational apprenticeship you need a mandatory school degree and good performance in the following school subjects:

  • Natural sciences
  • Mathematics
  • German
  • English
