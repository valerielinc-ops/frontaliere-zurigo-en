Full description

Are you a salesy, bilingual personality with a passion for gastronomy and the food market? Do you know the special features of the Valais market, live there and enjoy long-term customer relationships? Then we offer you the opportunity to strengthen our team and actively expand the market position of Migros Industrie.

Luogo di lavoro: Fresh Food & Beverage Group, FFB-Group Josefstrasse Zurich, Josefstrasse 212/216, 8005 Zurich

Important notes

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post will be disposed of for data protection reasons and will not be returned.

Mansioni

  • Support, advice and further development of the existing customer portfolio in the catering industry in Valais
  • Acquisition of new customers and expansion of market presence in the sales area
  • Planning and carrying out customer visits with the company car as well as building sustainable customer relationships
  • Close cooperation with the regional sales partners of the catering industry in Valais for joint market development and exploitation of potential
  • Responsibility for achieving the defined sales, earnings and growth targets
  • Close cooperation with internal sales, marketing, category management and other internal departments

Props

  • Several years of sales experience: ideally in the foodservice, gastronomy or wholesale environment
  • Completed apprenticeship (EFZ): in the food sector, hospitality, retail trade or similar
  • Strong customer and service orientation
  • Convincing appearance and high finishing strength
  • Good knowledge of the catering industry and an existing network in Valais is an advantage
  • Independent, structured and goal-oriented way of working
  • The place of residence and work is in Valais
  • Good user knowledge of MS Office and CRM systems
  • German (business fluent)
  • French (business fluent)

Cosa offriamo

  • Occupational pensions: Comprehensive protection in the Migros Pension Fund – with first-class and attractive benefits.
  • Du-Kultur: Hoi, hoi zämä, sali - we are on a first-name basis!
  • Fair wages: FFB Group pays its employees a fair wage in line with the market on the basis of our employee remuneration system.
  • Mobile subscription: Discounted mobile plans with Wingo, Digitec and Sunrise for you and, if necessary, for three other people you know.
  • Social benefits: 730 days of daily sickness allowance with full net salary.
  • Cumulus points: 3x points on food, 6x points on all other products.
  • Migros Bank: Higher interest rates on personal investment accounts at Migros Bank.
  • Sports and fitness offers: Reimbursement of CHF 200 per annual fitness subscription.
  • Migros Club School: Reimbursement of 70% of the cost per course (max. CHF 1,000 per course).
  • Holidays: 5 weeks holiday for everyone, 6 weeks holiday from 50. Age year, 7 weeks holiday from 60. Age year, purchase of additional vacation days possible.

Contatto

Application & Contact David Biederman Recruiter / HR Business Partner

Recruitment Process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We review incoming applications on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release it to the manager for viewing. The decision on whether to advance or leave the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or e-mail. Duration until re-registration: Up to three weeks
  • Discussion with HR & specialist department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. The primary aim is to learn more about personnel and professional skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit of the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Trial work: A trial day or half-day gives us the opportunity to get to know the future working environment and the team members better. And vice versa, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Issuance (Digital): After our verbal job offer, the most important contract points will be verified again. After agreement, the contract will be commissioned and sent by e-mail for digital signature. Duration until response: Up to one week

Note for personnel service providers For this position, we only consider direct applications.

Grade of occupancy: 80-100%
