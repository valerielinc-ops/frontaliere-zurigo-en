Laboratory keeper: in (m/f/d) – field of isolators

80%-100%, Zurich, permanent

Working where research shapes the future: At the ETH-Zurich in the EPIC Phenomics Center, we make an important contribution to modern biomedical research. We specialize in the keeping and care of laboratory animals – always under the highest quality, hygiene and animal welfare standards.

A central department of our animal operations are the isolator and transgenic work areas. In these highly standardized environments, animals are cared for under strictly controlled conditions and used for cooperation with various research groups within ETH Zurich.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a dedicated personality who is enthusiastic about this specialized and responsible task.

Start date is 1 March 2027 or subject to availability.

Job description

  • Care, care and care of laboratory animals under insulator conditions
  • Ensuring and consistent compliance with all hygiene, sterility and safety requirements
  • Implementation of cleaning, disinfection and sterilization measures in accordance with applicable guidelines
  • Work according to SOP specifications and regulatory requirements
  • Support in animal experimental activities within the framework of legal provisions
  • Careful documentation of all work processes in the electronic animal house management system (Vivarium)
  • Maintenance, control and functional verification of insulator installations and associated technical facilities
  • Cooperation in weekend services after operational planning

Profile

  • Completed training as a veterinary nurse: in, ideally, the field of research and clinic
  • Experience in dealing with insulators or in sterile working environments
  • High sense of responsibility and a precise, structured and careful working method
  • Teamwork, reliability and flexibility in a dynamic environment
  • Good physical resilience for a practically oriented activity
  • Readiness to work under strict hygienic and safety conditions
  • Good knowledge of German and English in word and writing

We offer

  • Responsible and varied work in a modern research environment at ETH Zurich – with a visible contribution to scientific progress and social impact
  • A comprehensive and structured familiarization by experienced and qualified colleagues:
  • An appreciative, collegial working environment as well as participation in an active, open-minded and multicultural team with currently 16 nationalities
  • Diverse opportunities for professional and personal development, including participation in internal and external training
  • Attractive employment conditions and social benefits according to the guidelines of ETH Zurich
  • Numerous perks, such as public transport subscriptions and car sharing, a broad sports offer of the ASVZ, child care and attractive pension services
