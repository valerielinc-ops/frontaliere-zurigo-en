Technical Lead for IoT Gateway as a Service

Your future starts here At Swisscom IoT, you connect the physical and digital worlds. Our agile team develops and operates products and services that securely connect devices, transform data into valuable insights, and help our customers achieve greater transparency, efficiency, and new business opportunities. We work with a pioneering spirit, close collaboration, and tangible customer impact – with the speed, personal responsibility, and curiosity of a start-up, as well as the strength, trust, and reach of Swisscom. In Zurich, in the Design and Delivery area, as Technical Lead Gateway as a Service, you ensure the secure, reliable, and scalable operation of our managed gateway service through preconfigured industrial hardware, Swisscom IoT Connectivity, and the Gateway Management Portal, provide functional leadership to a team of three specialists, and drive further development. Together with Product Management, Sales, Customer Solutions, suppliers, and operational partners, you work across the entire process from presales and architecture through implementation, lifecycle, support, and escalations, thereby enabling the successful deployment of connected solutions.

  • Functional leadership of three specialists, including skill development, resource planning, and clear objectives
  • Technical responsibility for gateway architectures and the management portal, as well as delivery across the entire process
  • Define quality standards and operational guidelines, maintain documentation, and drive continuous improvement
  • Manage suppliers, represent technical interests, and convince customers in presales and demos
  • Coordinate internal and customer-side inquiries, commitments, costs, and priorities in close collaboration with Product Management

Your skills

  • Bachelor's degree (university/university of applied sciences) in computer science or equivalent qualification, five to eight years of experience in connected solutions, technical delivery, or IoT
  • Very good networking knowledge and practical experience in cybersecurity, secure design and operation of network architectures
  • Cisco CCNA is an advantage
  • Experience with embedded hardware and embedded systems in operational environments, as well as process-oriented quality improvement
  • Experience in customer-oriented roles, collaboration with suppliers, and commercial activities such as quotations and tenders
  • You lead by example, further develop the team and foster an open, learning-oriented culture
  • Analytical and sound in your judgment, you communicate openly, credibly and in a solution-oriented manner – even under pressure
  • Structured, self-directed way of working with clear documentation and ownership of your areas of responsibility
  • Very good German and very good English, French is an advantage

With us, you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or from home. In doing so, you will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a wide range of attractive benefits that will enrich your work experience. These include a pleasant working environment, financial advantages and exciting opportunities for professional development. Benefits.
