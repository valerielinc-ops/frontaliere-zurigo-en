Full description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

Job Summary The Project Assistant is responsible for supporting project teams by performing administrative and coordination tasks, including updating schedules, tracking resources, and assisting with documentation. This job profile is intended for support staff that follows established procedures and works under close supervision. Smaller/easier decisions are made autonomously. Join our Sulzer Turbo Services team, part of Sulzer Services, which is one of the world's most advanced independent providers of repair, overhaul, and maintenance within the turbine industry. From Venlo (Lomm) or on site, we provide our customers worldwide with solid, customer-oriented, and flexible support for their Compressors, Steam and Gas Turbines. Please join our proud and highly skilled international crew as a Field Service Coordinator .

In this role you will be accountable for

  • Preparing, coordinating and supporting execution of Field Service activities in collaboration with project team
  • Analyzing requests from both internal and external stakeholders/customers
  • Updating the manpower plan/project information for the Field Service projects in ERP and tracking costs during the projects
  • Generating purchase requisitions for the Field Service projects
  • Consulting with subcontractors and suppliers with regard to project preparation, delivery times and consumables procurement
  • Managing and maintaining inventory of Field Service tool containers & special tools
  • Organizing all travel related topics for the Field Service team
  • Carrying out cost calculations and close out costings within the scope of concluded agreements

To succeed in this role, you will need

  • A vocational degree as a Technician
  • 5 + years of experience in the field of rotating equipment
  • Very good English language skills
  • A proactive, flexible, and problem-solving attitude
  • Excellent interpersonal and communication skills

What we offer you

Sulzer offers an excellent package of benefits (CAO Metalektro), including:

  • A more than competitive salary
  • A generous number of holidays (40)
  • A solid pension scheme
  • Training opportunities
