Client Consulting – Sales

Pensum: 100% Work Location: Experience Level: Entry Level FlexOffice: On-site

Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, this means more than just processing tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high standards, and bring the courage to break new ground. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand, and your ideas, initiative, and contribution count. This gives you the opportunity to truly make a difference for our clients, your team, and your personal development.

What you achieve

  • During the Sales Excellence Program, you will learn step-by-step everything you need for a successful career as a client advisor.
  • You will advise clients by phone and in person, gaining valuable practical experience in sales.
  • You will acquire new clients and gradually build up your own client base.
  • You will support market development in your region and arrange consultation appointments.
  • You will work together with experienced colleagues and benefit from their knowledge and experience.
  • You will participate in sales and marketing activities and get to know various sales channels.
  • You will complete comprehensive basic training including the VBV certification funded by us and continuously expand your specialist knowledge as well as your consulting and sales skills.

What you bring

  • Completed education (apprenticeship, high school graduation/Matura, or university degree)
  • Joy in dealing with people and enthusiasm for consulting and sales
  • Strong communication skills, empathy, and a confident demeanor
  • Self-initiative, willingness to learn, and the desire to develop personally and professionally
  • Goal-oriented and independent way of working
  • Proficient German language skills in spoken and written
  • Category B driver's license
  • Residence in the region or willingness to build a strong network there
