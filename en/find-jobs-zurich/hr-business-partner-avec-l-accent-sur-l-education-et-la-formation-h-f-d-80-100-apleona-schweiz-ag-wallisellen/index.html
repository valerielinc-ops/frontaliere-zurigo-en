Full description

Your tasks Are you interested as a business partner in shaping the further development of the organization? At Apleona Schweiz AG, we shape the working world of tomorrow – professionally, in partnership and close to our managers and employees. In order to strengthen our HR team, we are looking for a convincing and effective personality immediately or by agreement. In this versatile function, you are responsible for the assigned areas in all personnel matters and act as a single point of contact (SPOC) for managers and employees and support them in all questions relating to leadership, organization and cooperation.

  • Advice / support of managers and employees along the Employee Life Cycle
  • Recruitment of specialists/managers
  • Support as an HR business partner in relation to labour law issues
  • Participation in HR projects and strategy topics
  • Case management incl. Prevention and reintegration measures
  • Responsibility for training strategy and planning
  • Planning and implementation of trainings / workshops incl. Onboarding programmes
  • Design and implementation of personnel development and training programmes
  • Working with pay rounds, staff budgeting and HR key figures for your support areas

Your requirements

  • Completed commercial training with corresponding further training in HR / business administration
  • Several years of professional experience as HR Business Partner in a comparable function
  • In-depth knowledge of Swiss labour law and social insurance
  • HR generalists knowledge
  • Strong communication and conflict resolution skills
  • Pragmatism and hands-on mentality
  • Structured, reliable and independent working method and enjoyment of project work
