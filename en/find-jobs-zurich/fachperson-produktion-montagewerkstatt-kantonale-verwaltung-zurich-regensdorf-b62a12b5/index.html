Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Prison and Reintegration Prison Pöschwies Specialist Production Assembly Workshop 80 - 100% With around 1400 employees, JuWe is the largest office within the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs and responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as for the execution of various types of detention. Our employees from around 40 professions support and promote the reintegration of delinquents and work effectively and progressively for a safe and just society. The Pöschwies Prison (JVA) is one of eight main departments of JuWe and the largest closed facility for men in Switzerland. With around 400 detention places and about 300 employees, it contributes to the protection of the general public in accordance with its legal mandate and at the same time creates the conditions for the rehabilitation of the detainees. As of January 1, 2027 or by agreement, we are looking for reinforcement for our assembly workshop.
