Full description

Do you like working with people? You would like to work immediately or by agreement with our team in the care for the health center for the Alter Dorflinde?

Tasks

  • They look after and care for the residents individually and resource-oriented in the reference person system.
  • They are responsible for the care and care process and assume daily responsibility.
  • You carry out the care and care documentation, plan care measures based on the care process and contribute your expertise and experience in the long-term area.
  • They are involved in family work and actively participate in team meetings, projects and working groups.
  • They shape and develop the area of care and care.

Profile

  • Empathetic personality with a strong human orientation
  • Enthusiasm for self-responsible work, combined with team spirit and commitment
  • Completed training as a nurse (HF/FH/DNII) with SRC recognition for diplomas from abroad
  • Excellent knowledge of German in words and writing as well as excellent communication skills
  • Strong sense of responsibility, the ability to think in a network and high resilience

We offer

Enjoy an exciting field of work in a professional and team-oriented working environment with a du-culture throughout the company. The human being is at our center. That is why we support the reconciliation of private and professional life, different working models make it possible.

As part of the city of Zurich and due to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, progressive employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

Depending on the experience, the annual salary for this function is between Fr. 85’228.– and Fr. 113’882.– (100%).

About us

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

In the dynamic Oerlikon district in district 11, we offer our residents a homely home with all amenities. The versatility of the quarter as well as the good infrastructure and location make Oerlikon an attractive place to live and work. Here you work together with 80 colleagues.
