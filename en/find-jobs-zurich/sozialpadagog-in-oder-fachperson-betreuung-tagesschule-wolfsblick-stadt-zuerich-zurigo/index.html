Full description

At the Wolfsblick Day School, a new place of learning and living has emerged where teaching, care and community are connected.

Supervision of the primary department is part of the day school Wolfsblick in Zurich Affoltern. Over noon, up to 220 children are fed and cared for daily. The care facility is open to primary school students from Monday to Friday from 11.50 a.m. to 6 p.m.

To strengthen our existing team, we are looking for 1. January 2027 or, by agreement, a social pedagogue in the function of a supervisor or a specialist.

Tasks

  • You support the care teams with your expertise in everyday life and keep a cool head even in challenging situations
  • You pay the expense of 80% on 4 - 5 days per week
  • They work with kindergarten and primary school children (up to 6th grade) and accompany them in regular operation, in holiday care and in care on school-free days

Profile

  • degree as a social pedagogue* in FH or HFS; social worker FH or HFS; Teaching diploma for elementary school level, university degree majoring in social pedagogy, pedagogy or educational sciences; EFZ specialist supervision, dipl. toddler teacher, social actor EFZ, social accompanying staff (for applications with a foreign diploma / certificate, a Swiss diploma recognition must be attached)
  • Delight in taking responsibility and helping to shape care
  • Motivation to organize and implement needs-oriented offers
  • Willingness to accompany learner FaBe in day-to-day care (experience as a vocational trainer* of advantage or interest to attend the necessary further education)
  • Readiness for interdisciplinary cooperation in the school team
  • Professional experience in supplementary family care is beneficial

We offer

  • A varied, versatile and independent activity
  • A multicultural, exciting environment
  • Competent support by the management support and team vessels
  • Progressive salary and social benefits according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich (Horte)

About us

We are a large school community in Zurich-Nord with 19 schools. We derive our pedagogical attitude from the concept of New Authority and thus meet the daily challenges in everyday school life together and effectively.

At our school you can expect an open team that supports each other. We have clear binding rules that are supported by everyone. High binding standards regarding respect and consideration for people, materials and the environment are part of our everyday life.
