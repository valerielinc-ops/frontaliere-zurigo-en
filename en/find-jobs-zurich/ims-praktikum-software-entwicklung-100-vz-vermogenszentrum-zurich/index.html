Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We advise individuals and companies about money, manage assets and offer attractive financial services. We offer a versatile practical training in our software engineering team at our headquarters at Paradeplatz in Zurich.

  • Together with your team, you develop challenging business applications based on an agile approach.
  • You will get an insight into an exciting, technical environment.
  • You bring your ideas and work on the implementation of projects.
  • During your internship, you will be accompanied by experienced developers and further develop your skills.
  • You are in the third year of training in a computer science school IMS.
  • You like to program in the Microsoft environment (ASP.net, WPF, entity framework, SQL server).
  • You have a quick comprehension and very good analytical skills.
  • You work carefully and reliably, are a team player, like to learn new things and want to make a difference together with others.
  • Regular development discussions and individual support
  • Many events to expand your network.
  • Free cold drinks, coffee, tea and fresh fruits
  • Even glacé in summer
  • After completion of the internship, there is the possibility of permanent employment.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
