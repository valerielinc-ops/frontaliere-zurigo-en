Historisches Seminar

Start of employment 01.08.2027, temporary

The Chair for Global History (18th to 20th centuries) invites applications for: Department of History, University of Zurich, Switzerland

Your responsibilities

Your responsibilities will include working on your own PhD project, teaching 2 SWS (equivalent to one course a semester), and contributing to the Chair's activities in teaching, research, international collaborations and academic administration.

Your profile

We welcome applications from candidates who possess

  • Research interests in Japanese history and/or the history of the Asia-Pacific region from 1800 to the present
  • Excellent working knowledge of English and Japanese (to research level)
  • Outstanding BA and MA training (including a completed Master's degree in history or a close relevant discipline by the starting date)
  • Commitment to teach at all levels of the Department's BA courses
  • Advanced German communication skills (desirable)
