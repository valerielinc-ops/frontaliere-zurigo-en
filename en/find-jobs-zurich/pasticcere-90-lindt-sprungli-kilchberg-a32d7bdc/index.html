About us

We enchant the world with chocolate! Lindt & Sprüngli (Switzerland) AG is a subsidiary of the global Lindt & Sprüngli Group with headquarters and production facilities in Kilchberg on Lake Zurich. In Altendorf we operate our own logistics centre, while the Lindt Cocoa Center in Olten produces cocoa mass for our own use as well as for other European production facilities. Throughout Switzerland, we proudly operate 15 Lindt shops, including iconic locations such as the Jungfraujoch and the Swiss Transport House in Lucerne. More than 1000 employees from more than 50 nations contribute to our success.

For our Lindt Café in Kilchberg, we are looking for reliable and committed support immediately or by appointment.

What you do with us

  • Production of food in the kitchen and stocking of sales cases in the Centerpiece
  • Active cooperation in the guest area in the café (Center Piece)
  • Assuming a wide range of tasks in the kitchen as well as in service
  • Cooperation in the development of new products

If you suit us

  • Completed basic training as chef/chef EFZ or comparable training
  • Professional experience in a comparable capacity
  • Negotiable German and English, other foreign languages are advantageous
  • Proactive, upstanding and honest personality
  • Resilience, flexibility and openness to change
  • “Host gene” and willingness to go the extra mile
