Your tasks

  • You support hardware lifecycle management from deployment to output to take back laptops, headsets and other IT tools
  • You maintain inventory and device data and help manage our hardware inventory
  • You process hardware assignments and support employees in the installation, commissioning and exchange of their IT work equipment
  • You carry out standardized hardware work and document your activities according to the defined processes and guidelines
  • You prepare hardware and accessories for new employees and support you in the organization and implementation of the Welcome Day
  • You check devices for operational readiness and support them in reuse, repair or professional disposal
  • You create and maintain user manuals, knowledge articles and ICT documentation in Confluence

Your profile

  • You are completing a degree in computer science, business informatics or a comparable discipline
  • You have basic knowledge of hardware, Windows and Microsoft 365 and are interested in ICT support
  • Ideally, you could already gain initial experience with Jira, Confluence or a comparable ticket system
  • You communicate stylishly in German
  • English skills are beneficial
  • You meet employees in a friendly, professional and service-oriented way and enjoy direct exchange
  • You work reliably, carefully and structured and bring a high level of learning and team skills
  • You have a strong affinity for digital technologies, quickly learn about new topics and deal responsibly with devices, data and information
