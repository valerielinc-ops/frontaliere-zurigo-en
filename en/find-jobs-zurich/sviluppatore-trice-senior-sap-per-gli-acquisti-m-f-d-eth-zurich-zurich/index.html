Senior SAP Developer: in Procurement (m/f/d)

80%-100%, Zurich, permanent

To support research and teaching, the IT services of ETH Zurich operate as a service organization in a large and complex IT environment. We offer the members of ETH Zurich high-quality, flexible and scalable computer science services and support them in competition with the best universities in the world.

In the SAP Development group, we develop and operate applications in the SAP back- and front-end together with the SAP Competence Center. The spectrum ranges from back-end solutions for specialized user groups to web applications that are used ETH-wide. We also integrate internal and external applications and partners via integration platforms such as SAP Integration Suite and SAP PI.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for an experienced and committed personality as a Senior SAP Developer: in Procurement (MM) immediately or by agreement.

Job description

In this function, take over You will be responsible for SAP solutions in the areas of material management (MM), procurement and related logistics processes. You control complex requirements from technical clarification to solution architecture and effort estimation to implementation, introduction and sustainable support. You make technical decisions within the applicable architectural and governance requirements and ensure security, maintainability, performance and operational stability.

You develop sophisticated applications and enhancements in the SAP back- and front-end with ABAP and object-oriented ABAP as well as modern technologies such as CDS Views, OData, Fiori, SAPUI5 and RAP. As a technical contact, you will assess solution variants, define target images and development standards and ensure that new solutions take into account the principles of S/4HANA and Clean Core.

During operation, you take responsibility for maintenance, error analysis, performance optimization and 3rd-level support. You document the developed solutions, participate in the definition and further development of development standards and ensure sustainable solution quality through reviews, tests and knowledge transfer.

You will advise departments and the SAP Competence Center at eye level, identify dependencies on SD, FI/CO, HCM and integration solutions and develop cross-divisional solutions. Strengthened through active knowledge transfer, coaching and professional support You join the development team and reduce dependencies on individuals or external partners.

Profile

For this broad and varied area of responsibility, you bring a high degree of independence and judgment, a solution-oriented working method, as well as a university or university of applied sciences degree in computer science, business informatics or a comparable education. In addition:

  • At least 5 years of practical experience in SAP development, especially with ABAP and object-oriented ABAP.
  • Deep understanding of development and business processes in the SAP module MM as well as experience in digital support of procurement and logistics processes.
  • Routine in the entire development cycle from analysis and conception to the introduction and further development of solutions.
  • Experience with SAP WebDynpro ABAP and modern SAP technologies such as CDS Views, OData, Fiori Elements and RAP.
  • Additional knowledge in SD or FI/CO as well as touch points with SAP S/4HANA are a plus.
  • Experience with PunchOut integrations based on OCI and cXML is advantageous.
  • Know-how in the connection of systems via IDoc, web services, REST APIs or comparable interfaces.
  • Structured, solution-oriented working methods as well as the ability to implement complex requirements in an understandable and pragmatic manner.
  • Team spirit, personal responsibility and the willingness to actively contribute and share knowledge.
  • Very good German and English skills for professional cooperation in a versatile environment.

Are you flexible, open and capable of teamwork, show initiative and an efficient working methodology and enjoy helping to shape central SAP solutions at ETH Zurich? Then we would like to meet you.
