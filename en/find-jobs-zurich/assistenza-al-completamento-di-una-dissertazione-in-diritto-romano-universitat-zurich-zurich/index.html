Faculty of Law

  • Chair of Roman Law, Legal Papyrology and Private Law (Chair of Prof. Dr. iur). José Luis Alonso

Job start 1 January 2027 or by agreement

The Chair of Roman Law, Legal Papyrology and Private Law of the University of Zurich by Prof. Dr iur. In his work, José Luis Alonso fully combines Roman private law with ancient legal history and historical comparative law. On January 1, 2027, a position as scientific assistant (60%) with doctoral projects in Roman law must be filled. On the basis of an annual positive evaluation, the employment is limited to a period of 3 years, with an extension option of 1 year.

Tasks

The activity includes support in research and teaching, especially in the field of Roman law, as well as participation in the organization of the chair. In addition to the assistant, you write a dissertation and deepen the necessary knowledge. The working time is devoted in equal parts (30 percent each) to the assistance or scientific activity. You will also participate in seminars, workshops, colloquia and conferences. They are involved in a small, collaborative team with short paths, close supervision and regular exchange about ongoing research projects.

Profile

Formal requirements

  • Completed law degree at the time of entering the job.
  • Readiness for a doctorate in Roman law with enrollment at UZH.
  • Excellent knowledge of German (C2) in word and writing.
  • Knowledge in other scientific languages is advantageous.

Technical requirements

  • Intellectual curiosity and pronounced joy in research and teaching.
  • knowledge in the languages of the legal sources or willingness to acquire appropriate knowledge in a timely manner, in particular Latin.
  • Readiness for academic profile development and further qualification in the field of Roman law as well as other ancient legal traditions.

Personal requirements

  • Independent, precise and forward-looking working method.
  • High self-motivation and teamwork ability.
  • Openness to new, interdisciplinary, questions.
