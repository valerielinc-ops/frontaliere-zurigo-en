Shiftleader Burger King 80-100 (w/m/d)

Marché Restaurants Schweiz AG — Dietlikon, Dietlikon, Svizzera

Marché Restaurants Schweiz AG is one of the 10 Swiss companies with the highest turnover in the field of full-service system gastronomy and is the clear number 1 in motorway gastronomy with the brand "Marché". Since 2014, Marché Restaurants Schweiz AG has been part of the Coop Group as an independent subsidiary. With our brands and concepts – Marché, Zopf & Zöpfli, Burger King, Popeyes and Firehouse Subs – we are not only present on motorways or at Zurich Airport, but also at the largest Swiss railway stations in Zurich, Bern and Basel as well as in some popular shopping centers in German-speaking Switzerland. In other words, we refine the everyday life of our guests, wherever people treat themselves to a food break.100 percent pure beef, open flames for real grill strips, professional high-tech grills from the USA and over 50 years of experience - these are the ingredients for the best grill taste! Each burger is prepared by hand with a lot of passion and fresh ingredients. Are you also a fire and flame for the best grill taste and the finest burger enjoyment? Become part of our Burger King crew!
