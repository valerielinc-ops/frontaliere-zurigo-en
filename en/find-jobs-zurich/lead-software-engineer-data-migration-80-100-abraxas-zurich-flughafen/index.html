Full description

We are a motivated software engineering team and are looking for you to further develop our data migration services so that we can connect new customers to our new tax solutions. You support our team in the implementation of technical projects and work closely with your colleagues. With us you work with modern technologies, short decision paths and a lot of space for your own ideas. Your role

  • You design, optimize and scale our migration routes
  • Complex data structures are your profession. Analyze dependencies to design and implement intelligent mapping logics and automated cleaning strategies
  • Develop automated tests that ensure the integrity and quality of data throughout the process.
  • You prepare the migration and test data and monitor the processing process. You bring your findings from error analysis to the team so that solutions can be worked out together.
  • You drive the continuous development of our development processes, tools and best practices.
  • You control the planning and implementation of the technical connections and keep deadlines safely under control.

Your profile

  • Several years of practical experience in creating and optimizing complex SQL queries
  • Initiative and analytical thinking to improve data queries and processing times
  • In-depth knowledge of data modeling, data management and data architecture
  • Ability to quickly familiarize yourself with complex legacy databases
  • Experience in analyzing complex data structures and identifying anomalies
  • Good knowledge of SQL databases, especially PostgreSQL and MS SQL Server
  • Knowledge of Python or Experience with Python frameworks is beneficial
  • Close cooperation and coordination with other teams and stakeholders
  • Strong communication skills and addressable communication of topics
  • Active support and coaching of team members
  • Secure appearance, persuasion and perseverance in the implementation of solutions

Your Benefits

  • Up to 60% home office possible with a 100% workload
  • Holiday purchase (2 weeks per year)
  • 41h week with flexible working hours on a full-time workload
  • Mind. 25 days of holidays per year
  • Support for 100% financial or temporal training
  • Free SBB half-ax subscription for all employees
