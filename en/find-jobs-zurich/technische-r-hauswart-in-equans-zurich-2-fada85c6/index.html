You are looking for a task in which you take on responsibility, are technically required and can work independently. Then you are right with us!

You can move that with us.

In this function, you ensure the smooth operation of various properties and keep technical systems under control at all times.

Your field of activity includes

  • Supervision and supervision of all building installations (HLKSE)
  • Planning, control and documentation of maintenance
  • Carry out minor repair and maintenance work
  • Administrative handling of day-to-day operations
  • Organisation and implementation of building technology projects
  • Development of optimisation options to reduce operating costs
  • Pictet service

That's what makes you.

You describe yourself as a service provider: in the innermost conviction. Positive feedback on your performance motivates you and spurs you on to further top performances.

  • Basic technical training in HVAC or electrical systems
  • Further training as a maintenance specialist is advantageous
  • Experience in technical building maintenance
  • Organizational and communication talent, constructive work in a team
  • Strong concept of service
  • Driving licence Cat. B
  • Good computer user knowledge

That's what we offer you.
