Full description

Canton of Zurich Security Directorate Social Security Office Study intern/in 70 - 100% With more than 280 employees, the Cantonal Social Office performs a wide range of steering and operational tasks focusing on social assistance, social security, social institutions and asylum at the interface between the Confederation, other cantons, cities, municipalities and various private organizations. It also runs its own facility, the tilia, which has 119 residential and employment spaces, divided into 11 residential groups and 5 studio areas. The locations are Rheinau, Winterthur, Embrach and Freienstein. The tilia supports, accompanies and promotes adults with cognitive and/or psychological impairments. For our residential groups in Rheinau, Freienstein, Embrach and Winterthur we are looking for a study intern/in 70-100% per 01.02.2027 or by appointment, temporarily between 6 -12 months. Their main tasks

  • They get to know the care concept of the respective residential group, work actively in everyday life and promote the practical skills and resources of the residents
  • They support and accompany residents individually in everyday life management, promote their independence and social skills
  • They maintain contacts with field offices
  • You will help shape a positive living group environment, support the achievement of goals, provide appropriate interventions in psychiatric or somatic emergencies and take on administrative and domestic work your skills and talents
  • You are a student in the Bachelor's programme of Social Work
  • You are interested in working with mentally and/or cognitively impaired adults and are ready to deal with the requirements of day-to-day care
  • You are a responsible, resilient and independent personality and bring flexibility and teamwork with what you can expect
  • Careful introduction to work as well as a versatile, demanding field of activity
  • Professional and individual support by a practice trainer
  • Regular team meetings, reports and supervision
  • Interesting Development Opportunities and Cantonal Terms and Conditions of Employment Your Application For more information please see Dieter Pani, Fachdienst Bildung, Tel. 043 258 66 04. Please let us know your preferred location when applying online.
