Tasks

Board management and business preparation

  • Coordination, quality assurance and timely submission of operations of the Directorate Finance for University Management, University Council and other central bodies
  • Ensuring smooth management of committees, including requesting, reviewing and quality assurance of the basis for decision-making

Directorate Support and Administration

  • Organization and coordination of Jour fixes, meetings, workshops and events of the Finance Directorate
  • Ensure efficient management of appointments, documents, releases and pendants and support administrative processes

Quality assurance and further development

  • Support in the preparation and quality assurance of reports, presentations and decision bases
  • Participation in financial and organizational issues as well as in the further development of processes, templates and administrative processes
  • Assistance to the staff for specific contracts and cross-cutting coordination tasks

Requirements

  • Commercial training with several years of professional experience in comparable areas of responsibility
  • Strong organisational skills and a structured, careful and independent working method
  • Very good editorial skills and stylish German in word and writing, English is advantageous
  • Ability to quickly grasp complex relationships and professionally coordinate different stakeholders
  • High service orientation, reliability and discretion
  • Very good knowledge of common office applications and digital collaboration tools

What you can look forward to

  • A responsible and varied activity with a large organizational breadth
  • A dynamic working environment with diverse contacts within central management bodies
  • The opportunity to actively shape central processes and business of the university
  • A collegial and committed team with short decision paths
  • Modern working conditions and flexible forms of work
  • An attractive workplace in the center of Zurich

Why this job is particularly exciting This position offers the rare opportunity to help shape central decision-making and control processes at one of the largest universities in Europe up close. In a dynamic environment with diverse stakeholder groups, you take on an important coordination and hub function, create transparency, ensure commitment and thus make a significant contribution to the ability of the Finance Directorate to act. The function combines organizational skill with a broad insight into the strategic development of the university and offers a varied field of work with a high level of personal responsibility and great impact.
