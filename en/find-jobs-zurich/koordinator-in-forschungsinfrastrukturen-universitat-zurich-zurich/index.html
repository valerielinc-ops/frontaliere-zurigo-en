Tasks: As coordinator: you complement our motivated team in the implementation and further development of measures to promote technology platforms. You act as a bridge-builder between researchers, technology platforms and university decision-making bodies and administrative units. Their tasks include:

  • Monitoring and evaluation of technology platforms
  • Independent management of projects as well as support of the department management in the planning and implementation of measures in the field of technology promotion
  • Moderation of meetings and presentation of concepts and results to various committees
  • Exchange and networking with other university bodies, universities and research funding agencies Requirements

  • Strong analytical skills and the ability to quickly capture complex content and prepare it for target groups
