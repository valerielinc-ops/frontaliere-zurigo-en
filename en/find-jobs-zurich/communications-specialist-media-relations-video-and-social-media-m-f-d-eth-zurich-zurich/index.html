Communication specialist: in media work, video and social media (m/f/d)

80%-100%, Zurich, permanent

Are you interested in science, media and digital communication? Do you like to write, think multimedia and keep an overview in a dynamic environment? For ETH Zurich University Communication, we are looking for a strong and digitally-savvy personality as of January 1, 2027 (or by agreement). Its focus is on media work

  • At the same time, you support the social media team and help to think and develop topics and content across both areas.

Job description

The Department of University Communication (HK) is responsible for the strategic communication of ETH Zurich. As a link between the ETH and the public, we communicate the achievements, concerns and values of the university internally and externally.

In the media team of the HK you are the contact person for journalists:innen. They coordinate media inquiries, accompany interviews and mediate as well as advise experts at ETH Zurich. Apply for selected topics from research, teaching and university They provide communication support, write media releases and other editorial contributions for various channels and organize media conferences. You can also help create media monitoring, participate in the daily newsroom sessions and identify which stories are interesting for media.

For our social media channels you develop and produce posts and videos. From idea and story development to implementation, you prepare complex scientific and institutional content in an understandable, attractive and platform-oriented way. They continue to think about existing formats and develop new narrative forms together with the social media team. They also take on tasks in day service and community management.

Profile

  • Completed studies, preferably in the field of communication or journalism
  • 3- to 5-year professional experience in journalism, university or corporate communication
  • Excellent editorial skills and a strong sense of relevant topics and stories
  • Very good knowledge of the Swiss media landscape and interest in academic, university and socio-political topics
  • Experience with social media in a professional context and a good understanding of how content works on different platforms
  • Safe in the design and independent production of videos – from the idea to rotation and editing to publication
  • Enjoy trying out new digital formats, narratives and forms of presentation
  • Safe and friendly appearance on the phone, online and in direct contact with different stakeholders
  • Familiar with the relevant AI tools
  • They follow current developments and reflect on new opportunities and use them in a targeted manner
  • Independent, structured and reliable working methods as well as strong teamwork
  • Stylistic German and very good English skills

We offer

  • A responsible and varied activity with room for manoeuvre at the interface between media work and social media
  • The opportunity to shape topics from the first idea to implementation on different channels
  • A dedicated, collaborative and helpful team in the exciting context of research and teaching
  • A diverse environment at an internationally renowned university
  • An attractive workplace in the center of Zurich
  • Family-friendly working conditions
  • Attractive offers and discounts as well as opportunities for personal and professional development
