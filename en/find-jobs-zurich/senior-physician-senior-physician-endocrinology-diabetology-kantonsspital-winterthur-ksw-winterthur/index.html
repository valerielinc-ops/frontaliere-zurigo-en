Full description

To strengthen our medical team, we are looking for a senior physician endocrinology & diabetology with a possible perspective on taking on management tasks within the thyroid center. The Cantonal Hospital Winterthur is centrally located, just 30 minutes from Zurich and from the German border. With a catchment area of more than 200,000 inhabitants, it has all the important disciplines and is responsible for the basic care and highly specialized medicine of the Greater Winterthur region. The Department of Endocrinology/Diabetology at the Cantonal Hospital Winterthur comprises an ambulatory with modern infrastructure and a consultancy service with focus on the entire spectrum of hormonal diseases incl. Diabetes mellitus and obesity.

Tasks

  • You take on management tasks, such as tutoring of assistant doctors and organizational activities
