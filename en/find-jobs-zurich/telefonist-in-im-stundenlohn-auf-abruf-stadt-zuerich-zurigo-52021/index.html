Full description

For our team in the telephone exchange, we are looking for an experienced and service-oriented help in the hourly wage by immediate or by agreement. They work in a team of 3 telephone operators from Monday to Friday.

Tasks

  • They are the first point of contact of the city telephone center and competently arrange external customers to the responsible service department, office or contact person.
  • They provide information to internal users, maintain and update the intermediary application "Concierge" and ensure a correct database.
  • They discuss telephone announcements, create statistics and support administrative work around the operation of the telephone exchange.
  • They take over the holiday representation of three employees of the telephone center (about 15 weeks per year in total) and provide flexible missions in the event of illness or other absences.
  • They work on demand from Monday to Friday. Plannable assignments for holiday representatives are already defined in the previous year. Flexibility is required especially for short-term operations in the event of unforeseen failures. Working hours are from 07:30 to 12:00 and from 13:00 to 17:00.

Profile

  • A completed course as a telephone operator or call center agent or equivalent training and several years of professional experience in a comparable job
  • High time flexibility and willingness to jump in spontaneously in the event of short-term failures
  • Good PC knowledge (MS Office) ideally with concierge experience
  • Independent, friendly, reliable, communicative, customer- and team-oriented specialist with good manners, exact working methods and a strong sense of detail
  • Swiss German as a mother tongue, knowledge of English and very good oral knowledge in at least one other national language (all other language skills are advantageous)

About us

On behalf of the City Council, we represent and manage around 1950 properties. In the areas of space and infrastructure, we offer numerous services to the city administration and advise the departments and the city council on strategic issues.
