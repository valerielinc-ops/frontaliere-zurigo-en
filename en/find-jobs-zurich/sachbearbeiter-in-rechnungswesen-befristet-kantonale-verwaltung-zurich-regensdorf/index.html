Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Judicial Enforcement and Reintegration Pöschwies Office of Justice Officer for Accounts, Temporary 80

  • 100% Description Judicial Enforcement and Reintegration (JuWe) is part of the Directorate of Justice and the Interior of the Canton of Zurich responsible for the execution of criminal and measures and the implementation of the various types of detention. For our 1,400 employees from around 40 professions, the successful social reintegration of people who have become guilty of criminal offences and the prevention of relapses are of particular importance. The Judicial Enforcement Institute (JVA) Pöschwies is one of eight main departments of JuWe and with 399 places the largest closed JVA in Switzerland for men who have become guilty of criminal offences. It fulfils a central state task and takes into account the legal mandate of the resocialization of the imprisoned persons and the protection of the general public. Tasks
  • Self-responsible management of client accounts – from bookings and coordination to payment processing – support for a wide range of work in the main book
  • Close cooperation with the cash office and other internal interfaces
  • audit, control and processing of creditor accounts
  • ensuring professional and cooperative cooperation with internal and external work partners
  • taking on representative tasks within the team Finance & controlling
