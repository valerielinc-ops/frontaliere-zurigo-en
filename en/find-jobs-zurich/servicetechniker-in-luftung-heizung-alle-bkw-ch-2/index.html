We make habitats worth living.

Become part of a motivated and diverse team in a dynamic company. Together with you, we shape the future of tomorrow!

Your tasks

  • Timely and economical execution of service orders in the field of ventilation and heating
  • Carrying out maintenance, maintenance and inspection work on existing facilities
  • Independent fault analysis and correction of operational failures
  • Implementation of modernisation, conversion and repair measures
  • Competent advice and support of customers as well as internal contacts
  • Ensuring smooth and professional order processing

You bring that with you

  • You bring a successfully completed apprenticeship as heating installer EFZ (m/f) or similar
  • Ideally, You about a further education as a service technician / in ventilation
  • You are a proven professional with several years of professional experience as a service technician in ventilation and air conditioning technology
  • You work independently, are customer-oriented and appreciate the personal contact with our customers
  • You pay attention to a careful and quality execution of your activity
  • You distinguish yourself through a flexible, reliable and reliable personality and enjoy working in a team
