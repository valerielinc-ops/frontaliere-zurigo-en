Full description

Are you a critical thinker who likes to analyze complex topics and provide insights on risks? Do you enjoy working in a diverse team that employs innovative techniques and tools? Join us at Group Internal Audit and help us to advance Swiss Re's resilience. About the Role As a Senior Auditor with focus on P&C Underwriting in Group Internal Audit (GIA), you will have the opportunity to leverage your underwriting knowledge across the end-to-end value chain while participating in a variety of audit activities. You will engage with senior underwriters and function heads on a regular basis across multiple underwriting lines of business. This role is an opportunity to deal with themes that have direct impact on the execution of Swiss Re strategy.

Ke y Responsibilities

  • Lead / contribute to various audits, using your expertise to deliver technically excellent reports on time.
  • Provide management with insights into improvement opportunities, building on audit findings.
  • Monitor risk landscape, analyze relevant datapoints to inform GIA risk assessment and audit plans
  • Coaching other members of the audit team on technical underwriting topics.
  • Challenging the audit teams to excel by acting as a role model.

About the Team Group Internal Audit is an international team of Auditors with the vision of 'Advancing Swiss Re's Resilience'. We seek to be problem solvers who lead from every seat, challenge the status quo, understand risk, and demonstrate that we are responsive to risk. We apply innovative and risk-focused techniques that ensure that our audit effort is proportionate to risk, and we continuously extend the use of data analytics and automation within our audit processes.

About You

We are looking for an intellectually curious professional with a strong investigative mindset—someone who challenges assumptions, looks beyond the obvious, and analyses core re/insurance processes to identify what could go wrong and where improvements are needed. You combine a delivery-oriented, collaborative approach with critical and creative thinking to strengthen organisational resilience and the control environment. You thrive in ambiguity, ask incisive questions, and communicate clear, persuasive insights that drive meaningful change.

Essentials

  • More than 5 years' experience in the insurance and/or reinsurance industry
  • In-depth understanding of the end-to-end underwriting process in P&C Re/Insurance
  • Practical experience in pricing and underwriting

Nice to Haves

  • Experience with internal or external reviews or audits
  • Experience across multiple lines of business
