Title: Specialist Chargeback Services

For more than 100 banks in Switzerland and Liechtenstein, we provide advice and services in the field of cashless electronic payments. For this fast-growing area we are looking for reinforcement. We offer a flexible working time model (Monday to Friday) with 60% home office option with a workload of 80-100 %

These are your tasks

  • Processing of incoming complaints according to the specifications of the card organizations (Visa, Mastercard, etc.)
  • Ensuring the processing, management, decision-making and, if necessary, forwarding of collection letters (credulous requests) to the specialist departments as well as carrying out fee collections
  • Written advice and response to customer inquiries
  • Active participation to optimize existing services, processes and tools
  • Cooperation in projects as an additional task

You bring that with you

  • Completed training (preferably expert / woman customer dialogue or kaufm). Basic training
  • Several years of experience in a similar function or as a chargeback employee with corresponding proof of performance is advantageous
  • Good to very good PC and MS Office user knowledge
  • Independent, team-capable and resilient personality with a strong focus on customers and services
  • Negotiation security in German, French and English. Italian is a plus!
