Children's Hospital Zurich Eleonorenstiftung | online since : 28.10.2026

Psychosomatic-psychiatric therapy station

Your tasks

  • You care for adolescents with psychosomatic and psychiatric challenges by actively involving their families in the living area.
  • Together with the young people, their parents and our multi-professional team, you design the individual care.
  • You consistently adhere to our high quality standards and contribute to continuous development.
  • You live out your creative vein by creating varied group activities that bring joy to everyone.
  • As an accompanying person you support our prospective nursing students HF/FH on their way.

Your profile

  • You bring a diploma in nursing HF or FH or in social education that underlines your in-depth expertise.
  • With your experience in dealing with adolescents and their families in the milieutherapeutic environment, you are very familiar.
  • You are a team player through and through and shine through your cooperative way of working.
  • Thanks to your flexibility, you always keep track of challenging situations and keep a cool head.
  • Your willingness to take responsibility makes you a valuable support in our team.
