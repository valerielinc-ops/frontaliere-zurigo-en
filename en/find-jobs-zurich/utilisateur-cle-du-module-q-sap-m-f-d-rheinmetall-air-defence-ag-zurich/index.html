Full description

For our quality department in Zurich, we are looking for an SAP Q module key user to strengthen and actively support our teams. They are responsible for the support and data maintenance of the SAP Q module and train and support employees in the quality department. With active participation, you are also responsible for the development and verification of data bases for the continuous improvement and further development of the Q module. Your responsibilities in detail:

  • Analysis, optimization and further development of SAP QM processes
  • Support and implementation of SAP QM projects as well as participation in the transition to S/4HANA
  • Interface management to adjacent modules (e.g. MM, PP, WM)
  • Creation of concepts and implementation of requirements in the system
  • Training and support of users
  • Ensuring the smooth operation of the Q module
  • Ensuring a verified database
  • Daily Business: Master data maintenance, performance recording, implementation of work packages, preparation and review of evaluations and key figures, customer reports, etc. WHAT YOU SHOULD BUILD
  • Completed basic training as well as a completed further education in computer science and initial experience in project management
  • Experience in the industrial sector (mechanical engineering) is advantageous
  • Very good SAP R/3 knowledge is a must. S/4HANA experience is beneficial
  • Experience in process design in the SAP environment
  • Good German and English skills in word and writing (at least level B2 necessary), Italian is advantageous
  • High quality awareness as well as numerical affinity and an exact working method
  • Good analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Resilience Teamwork and good communication skills in word and writing What we offer them
  • A dynamic working environment with exciting projects
  • Flexible working hours (active and trusted working hours)
  • Very good social benefits and a super-compulsory pension fund
  • Employee benefits of a large corporation, employee share purchase program
  • Promotion of needs-based training
  • Staff restaurant and external catering and shopping facilities nearby
  • Individual internal and external personnel development measures in cooperation with our Rheinmetall Academy
  • Workplace a few minutes walk from the Zurich-Oerlikon train station KONTAKTDETAILS Responsible contact: Michael Zmoos For questions about your application, please use the contact form. Please note that dossiers of recruiters are not considered.
