Full description

The GZO Spital Wetzikon is an innovative acute care hospital in the Zurich Oberland with 150 beds and around 900 employees. As a company with high customer orientation and appreciative communication, we are committed to our patients every day. The GZO is in a transformation phase. The estate deferral provides the necessary time for the development of a comprehensive restructuring plan. Operation continues normally. Do you want to support us in this extraordinary situation and actively contribute? If you are interested in being part of an interprofessional, innovative and established team, you are at the right place: We are looking for an open, service-oriented and committed team by 1 July 2026 or by agreement n

Your main tasks

  • Provide professional support based on the care process.
  • Ensure a good quality of care.
  • Target-conscious, patient-oriented planning of the work in close cooperation with a versatile interprofessional team from several specialist areas. Requirements
  • You have a high standard of care quality and are ready to carry new things
  • An open and respectful communication with the stakeholders is a matter of course for you
  • Flexible and economic thinking and action as well as an understanding of interdisciplinary relationships are part of your profile We look forward to committed nurses with professional experience in the Swiss acute care hospital or even after the diploma. Reduced weekly working hours (-10%) at full wage: If you are 100% employed, this means 24 additional non-work days a year (proportionately for part-time). This innovative working time model applies to all nurses at the GZO who regularly work in the three-shift system. We offer a very interesting and versatile field of activity in an advanced, dynamic and multi-professional working environment, which is characterized by high quality, respect and appreciation. With us you can develop further and benefit from internal and external training opportunities. Various discounts and internal offers are just as much part of our benefits as an excellent staff restaurant, kindergarten participation and above-average social benefits.
