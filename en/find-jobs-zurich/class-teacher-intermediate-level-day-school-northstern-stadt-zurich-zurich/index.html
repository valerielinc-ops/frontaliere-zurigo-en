Full description

At an innovative school with a modern day school concept together accompany the children in their holistic development! Due to dismissal after maternity leave, our team is looking for 14. December 2026 support.

Tasks

  • 63% with shared class teacher responsibility, 16 lessons
  • Lessons at a mixed-age middle grade 4.-6. Primary
  • an additional 1x participation in the midday care and 1x task hour
  • Planning, preparation and conduct of standard instruction
  • Cooperation with job partners, with the school management, external specialist departments and the guardians

Profile

  • Have a teaching diploma for primary education recognised in Switzerland
  • Enjoy teaching and working with students from different cultures – inside and outside
  • Motivation to get involved in an interprofessional team and participate in a holistic day school
  • Openness to plan and develop your lessons for age-mixed classes in a team
  • A positive, resilient personality with high social skills and class leadership skills

We offer

  • A modern school with an experienced school management and a dedicated, professional team that welcomes you warmly and with which you can jointly promote school development
  • A very experienced, committed job partner
  • Partly joint lesson planning in the cluster or step team and regular planning and consulting sessions in the interprofessional team (teaching, promotion, supervision)
  • An innovative day school concept with space for your strengths and interests
  • Supplementary offers such as a learning island (KOSMOS) and school assistance
  • Excellent connection to public transport (tram 11 and 10 directly on the doorstep, Oerlikon train station in 5 minutes walking distance)

About us

The Nordstern Day School at Thurgauerstrasse 55 has established itself as a modern learning and living space since its opening in August 2024. It has a wide range of usable, bright and open spaces, in which the multi-professional team with the children shapes the day school everyday life. Teachers participate 45 minutes at least once a week in the midday care and the caregivers support the lessons.

The school places an emphasis on Education for Sustainable Development (ESD): As a FreiDay school, the children are involved in their own projects with the 17 sustainability goals of the UN. Mixed-age classes and a strong participation of students promote self-responsibility and self-efficacy. Portfolio and coaching talks instead of grades, 90-minute teaching blocks, instead of 45-minute subject lessons: We try to do things differently to accommodate the learning of all children.
