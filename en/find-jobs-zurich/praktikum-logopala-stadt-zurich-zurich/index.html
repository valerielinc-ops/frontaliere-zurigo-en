Full description

In various school circles in the city of Zurich, we offer internships in all training semesters.

Tasks

  • Their tasks and responsibilities vary depending on the internship according to the internship guidelines.

Profile

  • You study speech therapy at a recognized German-speaking university / university
  • High team and communication skills
  • Organizational and administrative skills and structured work
  • Happy and conducive treatment of children
  • Enjoy working with people from different cultures

We offer

Insight into the versatile working day of a speech pedagogue at regular and / or special schools. You will be professionally supervised by an experienced internship manager and can benefit from our large network of logopaedic expertise. Depending on the internship requirement, you also have the opportunity to treat more complex disorders, such as children with ISR status, at QUIMS schools or children with ASD or speech flow disorders.

About us

As part of the Special Pedagogy Service Department, the Speech Pedagogy Department of the Primary School of the City of Zurich offers preventive and therapeutic measures for students (SuS) with abnormalities in language development and communication. These offers are available to children and young people of all grades. Our speech pedagogues work either at a standard school with SuS with and without special school status (ISR) or at HPS locations with SuS in separate school setting.
