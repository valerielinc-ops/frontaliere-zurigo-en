You like to work independently, think along and keep an overview in a varied work day? Then you are exactly right with us!

For this function, we are looking for an organized personality who will support our team on Monday, Wednesday and Friday.

YOUR TASKS

  • Maintaining and updating our assignment master data
  • Collaboration in the back office, in particular work in the H-Net
  • Processing of the request input
  • General administrative activities supporting the Radiology Services team

Your profile

  • Completed training in health care (e.g. medical secretary/medical secretary/spital secretary/spital secretary) or in the commercial sector with knowledge of medical terminology
  • Strong understanding of services, strong communication, reliable and resilient
  • Joy of working together in a team and an independent, structured way of working
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Further language skills are advantageous
  • Experienced handling of common IT applications

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. We offer you a modern, pleasant working atmosphere as well as a thorough introduction to the versatile world of radiology. With our working time model «Leisure Plus» you have the opportunity to benefit from a corresponding wage reduction of 7 instead of 5 weeks holidays. If you commute by public transport, we will contribute to your travel costs. You will receive the ZVV or Z-Pass for your commute at a discounted price and enjoy free travel throughout the ZVV area. Z-pass validity area. For longer distances we provide you with an SBB coupon. These and many other benefits make Hirslanden Clinic an attractive employer in the Swiss healthcare sector.
