About us

The school district Waidberg comprises 17 school units with over 6400 pupils and around 1,700 employees. The districts Wipkingen, Höngg, Unterstrass and Oberstrass belong to the lively school circle. The Waidberg stands for a pedagogy at the height of time. Democracy and participation are overarching guidelines of school development.

The school Scherr is located in the lively district of Zurich-Oberstrass and is an established day school in the city of Zurich. 12 primary classes, 4 kindergartens and a versatile care form a common place of learning and living for the children. We live diversity, appreciation and community and create space for development and participation. Open, solution-oriented cooperation and joint learning shape our school culture.
