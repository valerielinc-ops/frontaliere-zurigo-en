The LANDI cooperatives support Swiss farmers in the economic development of their farms. Together with the Volg, TopShop and AGROLA brands, the LANDI cooperatives also supply the population in rural Switzerland with food, everyday necessities and energy. LANDI Zola AG is successfully active in the Zurich Oberland region.We are looking for a motivated, committed and flexible person for our location in Illnau, starting immediately or by agreement, in the role of Sales Back Office and Inventory Accounting Clerk 80-100% (m/f/d).
