NOMIS Foundation ETH Postdoctoral Fellowship

100%, Zurich, fixed-term print Drucken

The NOMIS Foundation–ETH Fellowship Programme supports postdoctoral researchers at ETH Zurich within the Centre for Origin and Prevalence of Life (COPL).

  • NOMIS Foundation ETH Postdoctoral Fellowship

Application process

  • The proposed teaching duties and an estimate of the average load must be specified in the application.
  • The duration of a NOMIS–ETH Fellowship is three years plus one extension year and cannot be prolonged.
  • The fourth year is granted only following a review 18 months after the start of the fellowship and must be funded by the host professor(s).
  • The fellowship is competitive with a limited number of fellowships available each year.
  • For a start in 2027, up to one new NOMIS–ETH Fellow will be selected. Profile
  • At the time of application, applicants must have submitted their PhD thesis.
