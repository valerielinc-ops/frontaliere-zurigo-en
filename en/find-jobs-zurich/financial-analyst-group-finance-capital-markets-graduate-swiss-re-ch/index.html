Full description

Start your journey towards a rewarding career! We’re looking for curious, motivated graduates with strong people skills who want to grow, learn, and make a difference. If you hold a Bachelor’s, Master’s, or PhD and have up to three years of professional experience, apply for our 18-month Graduate Programme starting on 1 September 2027. You’ll gain hands-on experience in the fast-paced world of reinsurance, rotate into different local teams, and receive tailored training to build your expertise. This is your opportunity to shape your career, help protect societies from the world’s most threatening hazards, and contribute to making the world more resilient. By the end of the programme, you’ll have built the skills, foundations and network to pursue a wide range of career opportunities within Swiss Re. This early recruitment cycle is dedicated specifically to Group Strategy and Group Reporting & FP&A Graduate positions . Additional Graduate opportunities across P&C, L&H, Risk Management, Finance & Asset Management functions in Zurich, London, Paris, Milan, and Bratislava will be advertised from November onwards. About the Role Are you interested in understanding how one of the world's leading financial institutions creates value, allocates capital and engages with investors? Are you excited by the intersection of finance, strategy and capital markets? Group FP&A & Investor Relations is looking for a motivated Graduate to join our team.

  • Sitting at the centre of Swiss Re's financial planning, performance management and investor communication, we play a key role in supporting strategic decision-making and shaping how Swiss Re is perceived by the investment community.
  • In this role, you will analyse financial performance, business drivers and market developments, while contributing to materials and insights used by senior management, the Executive Committee and the Board of Directors.
  • You will play an active role in quarterly and annual results, Investor Day and other key investor events, working closely with colleagues across Finance, Strategy, Treasury and the Business Units to gain a deep understanding of how a global financial services company is managed, valued and communicated to investors.

About the Team Group FP&A & Investor Relations sits at the center of Swiss Re's financial and strategic decision-making process. Our work combines financial analysis, corporate strategy, capital management and capital markets communication. As part of a small, high-performing team, you will will work closely with Performance Management, Planning and Group Strategy teams and gain exposure to executive management, business leaders and the investment community. We foster an environment of intellectual curiosity, ownership and continuous learning, where team members are encouraged to challenge ideas, take initiative and contribute from day one.

About You

We are looking for a motivated and ambitious individual with a strong academic background, a keen interest in financial markets and re/insurance, and the determination to develop their skills in a dynamic, fast-paced environment.

  • Master's degree in Finance, Economics, Business Administration or a related field
  • Strong analytical mindset
  • Advanced proficiency in PowerPoint and Excel
  • Excellent written and spoken English
  • German is an advantage
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills
  • Proactive, curious and eager to learn in a fast-paced, international environment
  • High level of integrity and professionalism when handling confidential information

Nice to have

  • Professional experience in consulting, finance, and/or (re)insurance
