Full description

For our HR Business Partner team we are looking for a maternity representation until the end of 2027. There is an option for renewal.

Tasks

  • Sparring partners for managers and employees of various business areas in all personnel-relevant issues
  • Predictive support of personnel and organizational changes thanks to good HR process and business understanding
  • Engagement in HR projects for the continuous development of working methods and services
  • Recruitment of new employees in the assigned areas
  • Ensuring efficient, service-oriented personnel work in cooperation with HR administration and payroll accounting

Requirements

  • Completed basic commercial education with corresponding further education or university degree (FH/University) ideally with business administration or comparable background
  • Completed further training in the field of human resources combined with in-depth knowledge of Swiss labour law
  • At least 3 years of proven professional experience in a comparable role
  • Experience and interest in talent acquisition/employer branding
  • Confident knowledge of German as well as good knowledge of English
  • Pleasure of advice, interest in business and a good look for the essentials
  • Analytical and conceptual skills paired with solution-oriented hands-on mentality
