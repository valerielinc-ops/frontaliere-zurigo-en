Full description

The Tertianum Glasi Residential and Care Centre offers comfortable and safe living for senior citizens in Bülach. The offer includes 62 apartments for senior citizens and 40 single rooms in the nursing department. A public bistro with event space, which is open 7 days a week, completes the comprehensive offer at the Tertianum Glasi. It opened its doors in the summer of 2022. The senior apartments and nursing rooms are part of the new Glasi area, a bustling district with apartments, shops, offices, restaurants and a central meeting place.

We are very proud of our young company and look forward to employees who want to shape the Glasi with us!

If you want to fully develop your potential, you are exactly right with us!

Specialist / Professional Long-term Care Care & Care for Day-Late Service (50-100%)
