Role overview

Key responsibilities

  • Independent preparation, ongoing optimization and further development of internal and external sales documents (e.g. sales presentations, training documents, manuals, factsheets) with focus on impact and target group orientation
  • Leading role in cross-divisional projects and initiatives: active participation and representation of sales requirements, management and coordination with internal and external stakeholders as well as preparation of management presentations and decision-making basis
  • End-to-end coordination, tracking and monitoring of the progress of short-term special activities within the sales unit
  • Identification, conception and implementation of process improvements within the sales unit with focus on efficiency and quality as well as initiation of corresponding measures and success monitoring
  • Close cooperation with product management in planning and implementing measures and product-related sales initiatives
  • Planning, organization and control of internal and external sales actions, including definition of goals, messages and success criteria as well as evaluation and derivation of lessons learned

The team At TopCard, together with us, you will create an outstanding customer experience for the exclusive customers of Swiss private banks. Our focus is on personal support and the targeted expansion of strong partnerships.

Do you want to work in a dedicated team where your ideas are in demand? Here you can actively participate in cross-divisional projects and contribute your strengths. Look forward to an environment where initiative counts and you can develop yourself personally.

Your skills and experience

  • Several years of sales experience with a strong customer and service focus: in-depth knowledge of the sales environment as well as a strong understanding of excellent service and the needs of demanding customers
  • Demonstrable project and process competence: several years of experience in cross-divisional project work, including controlling different stakeholders and prioritizing requirements and ensuring implementation
  • Excellent communication and team skills: confident, user-friendly approach to internal and external partners at different hierarchical levels, ability to clearly position sales interests and enjoy working together in interdisciplinary teams
  • Very structured, analytical and independent working method: Ability to handle complex tasks independently, efficiently and solution-oriented, even under time pressure and with changing priorities
  • Languages: stylish-confident German in word and writing, excellent English skills, French skills are a clear advantage
  • System knowledge: very experienced handling of MS Office, especially PowerPoint (creation of professional management and sales presentations)

About us UBS is a leading global asset manager and the leading universal bank in Switzerland. We also offer diversified solutions in asset management and focused skills in investment banking. Headquartered in Zurich, Switzerland, UBS is present in more than 50 markets worldwide. We know that great work is never done alone. That is why we put cooperation at the heart of everything we do. Together we are more than just ourselves. Want to know more? Visit ubs.com/careers.

Join us UBS Card Center AG, a subsidiary of UBS Switzerland AG, is the leading credit card processor in Switzerland. Well over 3 million Visa and Mastercard credit cards are reliably processed via our systems. With more than 750 dedicated employees at the Glattbrugg and Manno sites, we combine many years of expertise with innovative strength and work every day to make tomorrow’s payments easier, safer and smarter.
