Materials science and technology are our passion. With our cutting-edge research, Empa’s approximately 1,100 employees make decisive contributions to the benefit of society for a future worth living in. Assistant with Drive and number flair 80%-100% (a) The Laboratory of Sustainability Robotics (LSR) is a world-leading group with research focus on the development of novel, biologically inspired flying robots for distributed sensing in air and water and autonomous robotic construction for digital infrastructure systems. The lab is based on a jointly funded Professorship by Empa and the Swiss Federal Institute of Technology in Lausanne (EPFL) and is specialised in robot design, hardware development and multi-modal robot mobility and has an aligned Competence Centre for Sustainability Robotics (CCSR).   • You coordinate the preparation of financial reports and are the contact person for ordering, ordering and accounting

  • They maintain and update databases (publications, conferences, projects, orders, addresses) and coordinate archiving

  • They accompany new employees as well as international (guest) scientists in administrative matters
