Full description

Avaloq is a global leader in wealth management technology and services for financial institutions, including private banks, asset managers, investment managers, and retail and neo-banks. As a company founded in Switzerland with around 2,500 employees in 12 countries and over 170 customers in 35 countries, we are continuously expanding our global presence. Our innovation-driven approach and commitment to research is driven by the passion and creativity of our colleagues. We are always looking for talented professionals to support us in our mission to orchestrate the financial ecosystem and democratize access to wealth management. Avaloq offers the opportunity to work closely with some of the world's leading financial institutions and shape careers together. By fostering a collaborative, supportive and flexible work environment, we create optimal conditions for our employees to reach their full potential.

Are you currently in the 2nd year of your apprenticeship as a computer scientist with a focus on platform development and are looking for a follow-up solution for your 3rd year of apprenticeship? In our internal IT department, we offer a connection solution for continuing and successfully completing your existing training. This offer is specifically aimed at apprentices who have already started their training and are looking for a new training company from the 3rd year of apprenticeship in order to continue their apprenticeship without interruption and bring it to the EFZ degree.

What to expect

  • Admission: Summer 2027 to start your 3rd year of apprenticeship
  • Duration: 2 years until the completion of your existing training
  • Place of employment: Allmendstrasse 140, 8041 Zurich
  • Continuation of your previous school education

With us, you can...

  • continue your training from the 3rd year of apprenticeship and successfully complete the EFZ degree
  • gain practical experience in the internal IT of an internationally active company
  • collaborate and take responsibility in different IT teams
  • collaborate on projects or carry out their own tasks and smaller projects independently
  • get to know and co-supervise the operation of modern IT infrastructures and business applications

What do we stand for

  • You are currently in the 2. Year as a computer scientist (EFZ) specialising in platform development and are looking for a connection solution for your 3. Year.
  • You want to continue and complete your training successfully and set the right priorities accordingly.
  • You can communicate with colleagues in German and English.
  • You have analytical thinking skills and enjoy understanding complex relationships.
  • You are enthusiastic about modern technologies, IT infrastructures and technical innovations.
  • You learn quickly, work carefully and bring patience and perseverance.
  • You like to experiment, try new solutions and learn from your experiences.
  • You are a team player, communicative and reliable and appreciate open collaboration.
  • You take responsibility for your education and bring your own initiative and motivation.

Qualifications

For this connection solution, you should

  • complete ongoing training as a computer scientist EFZ, specialising in platform development
  • be ready to start your 3rd year of apprenticeship at the time of entry
  • be able to document your previous education and academic achievements
  • be motivated to continue and successfully complete your training at Avaloq

Please apply online only. We prefer to have your documents as PDF files in German or English.

Your application should include the following documents

  • Curriculum Vitae (CV)
  • Letter of motivation – please also tell us why you are looking for a new follow-up solution to continue your training
  • Certificates from your previous vocational school and training
  • Upper School Certificates
  • Employment references or internship confirmations (if available)

We look forward to getting to know you and accompanying you on your way to a successful EFZ degree!
