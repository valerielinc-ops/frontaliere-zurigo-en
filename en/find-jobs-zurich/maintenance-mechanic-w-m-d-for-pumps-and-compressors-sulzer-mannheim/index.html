Original description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

Job Description Summary You like to take part yourself, enjoy technology and systematically get to the bottom of causes of disorders? Then you are exactly right with us. In this varied position, you work on pumps, gearboxes, compressors, turbines and other rotating machines. You take over both maintenance and repair work as well as operations directly at our customers on site. Their tasks

  • Maintenance, repair and repair of pumps, gearboxes, compressors, turbines and other rotating equipment
  • Analyse disturbances and damage, identify causes and implement suitable solutions
  • Dismantling, testing and assembly of components up to complete units including mechanical seals and peripherals
  • Carry out service and maintenance work in the workshop and directly at the customer's site
  • Re-commissioning of equipment and machinery after completion of work and checking their function
  • Support customers with technical issues with expertise and practical experience
  • Close cooperation with colleagues to exchange experiences and find the best solution together
  • Ensure that quality and safety standards are met at all times

Your profile

  • Technical training, for example as an industrial mechanic (m/f/d), mechatronics specialist (m/f/d) or in a comparable profession
  • Experience in service, maintenance or repair of rotating machinery
  • Enjoying troubleshooting, root cause analysis and technical challenges
  • Practical and solution-oriented working method
  • Team spirit and willingness to share knowledge and experience with colleagues
  • Customer-oriented appearance and a high quality standard for your own work
  • Readiness for occasional assembly operations
  • Category B driving licence
  • Crane or forklift certificate as well as SCC certification are a plus

What we offer you

  • Key position with responsibility and direct contribution to growth
  • Short decision paths and a great deal of room for manoeuvre
  • Contract of indefinite duration
  • Flexible working hours and 30 days of vacation
  • Modern working environment and equipment
  • Attractive compensation and additional services (e.g. employee discounts, JobRad, company pensions, family service hotline)
  • International cooperation, open corporate culture and a motivated team
