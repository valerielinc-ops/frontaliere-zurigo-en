136515 | 80 - 100% | Oerlikon, Zurich | Hybrid

You want to actively shape governance in individual life and enjoy creating clear structures and analyzing risks? With high quality standards and a sense of responsibility, do you reliably bring your ideas to the ground?

In this role, you are part of the Life Business Unit in the area of governance and actively shape the further development of Zurich Switzerland. Together as a team, you ensure that our life insurance products and processes meet regulatory requirements – while remaining customer-oriented and efficient. In close exchange with colleagues from individual lives, you keep an overview of current developments. With your precise way of working, you create transparency and develop pragmatic processes that really work in everyday life.

This is what awaits you

  • You implement adjustments in the internal control system (ARIS, Polaris) operationally and ensure their effective implementation
  • You carry out key controls independently and document them completely and safely
  • You actively shape our rules in individual life: instructions, manuals and process documentation are thanks Always up-to-date, understandable and easy to find
  • With a clear cockpit, you keep an eye on all ongoing audits, findings and IKS topics in your individual life – and create transparency about their status at all times.
  • You recognize risks and potential for improvement in changes at an early stage and proactively introduce concrete solutions

This sets you apart

  • Completion of a university or university of applied sciences – or a well-founded insurance-specific further education (e.g. VBV, Risk Management)
  • Several years of professional experience in individual life, ideally in customer service, operations, risk management or compliance
  • experience in control and process documentation
  • Knowledge of ARIS or Polaris is a plus
  • Structured, independent working method with high quality standards and clear, addressable communication
  • Very good German and good English skills in word and writing
