Full description

Cloud, hybrid, automation and modernization are more than buzzwords for you? Do you not only want to operate systems, but also actively shape the technological future? Then you are exactly right with us. In this key role, you will continue to develop our hybrid computing landscape and shape the transformation of our server platforms towards the Azure cloud. You combine strategic thinking with pragmatic implementation and bring innovations into a modern enterprise environment. This is how you shape health

  • You plan, develop and operate our hybrid Windows Server platforms in Azure and on-premises environments
  • You are actively involved in the further development of our computing and cloud strategy
  • You accompany the migration of workloads and services to the Azure cloud and bring your know-how to transformation projects
  • You ensure a stable, secure and economical operation of our platforms
  • You drive automation, standardization and the continuous optimization of our operational processes
  • You work closely with architecture, security and application teams
  • You advise internal stakeholders on the introduction and use of modern cloud and hybrid services
  • You evaluate new technologies, carry out proof of concepts and derive innovative solutions from them

This sets you apart

  • Completed degree in computer science/business informatics or vocational training in computer science with corresponding further education
  • Several years of experience in the engineering and operation of Windows server platforms
  • In-depth knowledge of Microsoft Azure and hybrid cloud architecture
  • Certifications in the field of Windows Server, Virtualization and Hybrid Cloud
  • Experience with Windows server platforms, virtualization, hybrid cloud as well as identity, network and security solutions
  • Know-how in infrastructure as code and automation, for example with Terraform, Bicep, PowerShell, Azure Automation or similar technologies
  • You are enthusiastic about new technologies and like to take the lead on topics and move things forward
  • You communicate at eye level and appreciate cooperation with a wide range of stakeholders
  • German at native language level as well as negotiable English

Why SWICA

  • We build on humanity. That is why we focus on openness, respect and trust in our everyday work. In doing so, we take on responsibility and think in terms of solutions – together, for our customers. This is also why we have been number 1 in customer satisfaction for many years.
  • We trust you and give you space. With annual working hours, at least 5 weeks holidays and on request also holiday purchase. With part-time models and a generous home office within the scope of the possibilities of the job, you make your everyday life largely flexible.
  • We value and reward your work – with a market-based wage, up to 1,500 francs annual fee for personal wishes and a generous participation in the basic and additional insurance for you and your family.
  • We offer you prospects. With a generous training regulations, we support your professional and personal development – for your role today and for your goals of tomorrow.
  • Because health is everything. With our company health management and region-specific offers, we take care of your well-being. And if it comes otherwise: 100% net wage continued payment.
  • We think long term. With three savings plans, pensum-dependent coordination deduction and benefits well above the legal minimum, you ensure good for your future.
