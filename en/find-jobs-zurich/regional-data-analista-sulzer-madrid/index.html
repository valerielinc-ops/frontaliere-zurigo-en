Original description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

Join Sulzer’s Business Intelligence & Analytics team and play a key role in accelerating our data transformation across the Services Division. This position sits at the crossroads of business intelligence, analytics, and technology, providing an exciting opportunity to influence how data is leveraged to support strategic and operational decisions across the region. We are looking for a proactive and business-oriented data professional who enjoys turning complex data into actionable insights. The ideal candidate combines strong analytical capabilities with a builder mindset, constantly seeking smarter, faster, and more effective ways of working through technology and innovation. In this highly visible role, you will partner with stakeholders across Sales, Operations, Finance, and Supply Chain, helping teams unlock the full value of data while promoting a culture of data-driven decision making. What you will do:

  • Gather and translate business requirements into clear, decision-enabling dashboards and analyses.
  • Develop, enhance, and maintain business intelligence dashboards, ensuring consistency, usability, and high-quality user experiences.
  • Manage analytics and dashboard development projects from scope definition through delivery and continuous improvement.
  • Build a strong understanding of data sources, structures, and flows across CRM, ERP, and cloud platforms.
  • Ensure dashboards are intuitive, actionable, and accessible to business users with minimal training.
  • Maintain documentation for dashboards, data models, and related processes.
  • Participate in ERP, CRM, and BI governance activities and serve as a regional point of reference for business users.
  • Identify data quality issues and collaborate with stakeholders to implement sustainable improvement solutions.
  • Analyze trends across regions, product lines, and business areas to uncover actionable insights and opportunities.
  • Support stakeholders with ad hoc analyses and deep-dive investigations to address complex business questions.
  • Partner with regional and sub-regional management teams to understand business priorities and enable informed decision-making.
  • Guide and support regional users in adopting and effectively utilizing BI tools and dashboards.
  • Design and deliver training sessions that increase data literacy and user adoption.
  • Support leadership teams with compelling, data-driven narratives and recommendations.
  • Identify opportunities to automate, simplify, and improve existing reporting and analytics processes through emerging technologies and tools.

What you need to succeed

Required

  • 3 to 6 years of experience in a data analytics, business intelligence, or related role.
  • Proven experience developing dashboards that support real business decision-making.
  • Advanced Excel and Power Pivot skills, including complex data models, pivot tables, and data analysis techniques.
  • Hands-on experience with a major BI platform such as Qlik Sense, Power BI, Tableau, or similar.
  • Strong understanding of data structures, data modelling, and working with multiple data sources.
  • Experience partnering with business functions such as Sales, Operations, Finance, or Supply Chain.
  • Experience collaborating with both business stakeholders and technical or IT teams.
  • Excellent analytical and problem-solving abilities.
  • Strong communication and presentation skills, with the ability to explain complex concepts to diverse audiences.
  • Excellent written and spoken English.

Preferred

  • Experience in an industrial, manufacturing, B2B environment, or consultancy.
  • Exposure to CRM systems such as Salesforce.
  • Experience working with ERP systems such as SAP, Microsoft Dynamics 365, or similar.
  • Familiarity with cloud
