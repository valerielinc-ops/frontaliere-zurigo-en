The LANDI cooperatives support Swiss farmers in the economic development of their farms. Together with the brands Volg, TopShop and AGROLA, the LANDI cooperatives also provide the population in rural Switzerland with food, everyday goods and energy. LANDI Surb is successfully active in the region. For the Volg store Steinmaur we are looking for a seller (m/f/d) 60-80% by appointment.

You are looking forward to these tasks

  • Always fresh and friendly advice and service to the clientele
  • Acceptance of goods, making available and making available of the sales assortment
  • Ensuring assortment orders and availability of goods
  • Cash register operation
  • Management of postal services of our integrated postal agency
  • Compliance with order, cleanliness and hygiene

We look forward to this profile

  • Enjoyment of active customer contact
  • completed training or professional experience in sales or a similar environment (preferably food retail)
  • They show initiative and appreciate an independent and versatile activity
  • You are open to learning new things
  • Good knowledge of German

Volg is the specialist for village shops and small spaces in German and French Switzerland. We live close to customers – as a close shopping opportunity as well as in personal contact. The employees are the heart of the shop and we attach great importance to individuality and personality. That’s why our motto is “fresh and cheerful”.

You can look forward to that.
