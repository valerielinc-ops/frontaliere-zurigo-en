Full description

Work with state-of-the-art histological and molecular biological methods in the diagnosis of complex diseases of the nervous system. You can look forward to a versatile field of work, a collegial environment and the opportunity to actively combine research and diagnostics.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • Processing of tissue in the context of diagnostics of the peripheral nervous system (muscle and nerve) • Processing of brainwater samples (liquor) • Acceptance of sample declarations by telephone • Performing various histotechnical techniques incl. Enzyme histochemistry and resin embeddings • Performing molecular biology techniques, including Western blot and real-time quaking-induced conversion (RT-QuIC) • Expression and purification (chromatography) of recombinant protein • Use in biosecurity laboratory stage P3* Requirements • Training as a biomedical:r analyst:in HF, laboratory assistant:in or a completed degree (bachelor or master) in biology, biochemistry or biotechnology • Experience in the mentioned techniques is advantageous • You can easily get into new techniques • You enjoy working in a dynamic and varied environment • Self-employed and self-responsible working methods • Team and communication-capable personality • Good knowledge of German and English (B2 or higher) • Strong quality, duty and service awareness We offer: We offer you a varied activity in a dynamic and international environment. If there is no experience, we will work you into the techniques used. Through its use in research projects, you can further develop your professional skills and gain insight into exciting projects. No weekend or night shifts.
