About the role

Job Summary The HR Specialist is responsible for supporting HR operations and implementing HR programs across the employee lifecycle. This role works independently on routine HR tasks and collaborates with teams to ensure effective service delivery. This job profile is intended for specialist professionals that apply advanced knowledge of a job area. Required to manage projects/processes independently with limited supervision. Typically coaches and reviews the work of lower-level professionals. SUMMARY: The HR Generalist serves as a trusted business partner and provides comprehensive human resources support throughout the employee lifecycle. This position partners with leaders and employees to administer and support employee relations, talent acquisition, compensation, performance management, succession planning, benefits administration, payroll coordination, HRIS management, immigration processes, compliance, employee engagement, and organizational initiatives. The HR Generalist promotes a positive work environment while ensuring compliance with company policies and applicable federal, state, and local employment laws.

ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS

  • Serves as the primary HR contact for assigned business units, employees, and managers regarding HR policies, procedures, benefits, and employment-related matters.
  • Provides guidance and coaching to managers regarding employee relations, attendance, disciplinary actions, investigations, performance concerns, workplace conflicts, and policy interpretation.
  • Conducts and documents workplace investigations involving employee complaints, policy violations, and workplace concerns.
  • Maintains confidentiality of employee information and sensitive company matters.
  • Partners with leaders to support workforce planning, organizational changes, and employee communications.
  • Partners with hiring managers to identify staffing needs and develop recruiting strategies.
  • Coordinates recruiting activities including interviews, background checks, pre-employment screenings, employment verification, offer letters, onboarding, and new hire orientation.
  • Reviews and approves wage increases in accordance with company compensation philosophy, salary structures, and departmental budgets, while ensuring internal equity and external market competitiveness.
  • Recommends compensation adjustments, salary structure modifications, and other compensation-related improvements.
  • Assists with compensation planning, promotional increases, and annual compensation programs.
  • Serves as the primary liaison between assigned business units and Payroll.
  • Reviews, validates, and communicates all weekly payroll changes, including new hires, promotions, transfers, compensation changes, leave activity, employee status changes, and terminations.
  • Assists employees with benefit enrollment, qualifying life events, and benefit-related questions.
  • Serves as liaison between employees and benefit providers to resolve benefit issues.
  • Supports annual Open Enrollment activities and employee benefit communications.
  • Assists with leave administration, ADA accommodations, workers' compensation, and other applicable leave programs.
  • Maintains accurate employee information within the HRIS.
  • Performs periodic audits of employee data, compensation records, payroll transactions, benefits enrollment, personnel files, organizational structures, and HR processes to identify discrepancies and ensure compliance.
  • Supports internal and external audits.
  • Ensures compliance with federal, state, and local employment laws and company policies.
  • Supports the annual performance management process.
  • Assists managers with coaching, corrective actions, and Performance Improvement Plans.
