100%, Place of work Horgen

Shape your future – together with us!

To strengthen our successful team for the Horgen area, we are looking for a customer-oriented personality with strong negotiation skills and a lot of commitment. For career changers and professionals with a local connection, we offer the perfect entry point with first-class training and development opportunities.

Start your career in an agency at AXA, the No. 1! We are at our customers' side as a competent partner – in every situation. Are you too?

What to expect

  • Passionate commitment to AXA's customers
  • Solution-oriented and personal all-round advice (physical or digital) for insurance customers
  • Maintenance and expansion of existing customer relationships
  • Mediation of other AXA products (finance and pension provision, health services).
  • Acquisition of new customers (using modern and digital tools and existing internal AXA processes)

What you bring to the table

  • Completed apprenticeship and professional experience in the service sector or, ideally, in the insurance industry
  • Professionals with a corresponding track record are welcome
  • Anchoring in the region
  • Convincing appearance and good articulation skills
  • Winning and resilient personality combined with a high level of customer and service orientation
  • High independence and flexibility
  • Stylistically confident written and spoken German, other languages are an advantage
  • Enjoyment of modern and contemporary digital tools

What we offer you

We create framework conditions that give you the space to develop and fully exploit your potential. As a leading Swiss insurer, we offer ambitious people attractive career opportunities and entrepreneurial freedom.

  • Discount of up to 30% on AXA products
  • 5 weeks vacation
  • Fleet discount/refund of Half-Fare travelcard/GA travelcard (pro rata)
  • Access to the Swibeco platform
  • Scope for independent action
  • Innovative structures, processes and modern tools
  • Well-founded, professional training and further education opportunities
