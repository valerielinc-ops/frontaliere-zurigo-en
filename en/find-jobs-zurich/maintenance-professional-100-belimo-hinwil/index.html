Full description

Keep our operational areas running and make sure that downtimes do not become a problem in the first place. With us, you take responsibility, drive improvements and bring your expertise where it really has an impact.

What will you do • Rapid and professional correction of faults and plant downtimes during shift operation • Monitoring of plant elements and proactive action in accordance with Service Level Agreements • Implementation and coordination of planned maintenance and maintenance work • Creation and optimization of maintenance plans in cooperation with maintenance planning • Ensuring quality standards and continuous improvement by identifying vulnerabilities • Ensuring the safety, availability and reliability of installations • Collaboration in commissionings, conversions, maintenance and cross-departmental projects • Support, training and professional guidance of employees

  • Occasional work at height and thus safe application of PSAgA

Working hours

  • Basic working hours in the shift model between 6 a.m. and 11 p.m. in 2 shifts
  • Readiness for occasional special missions (e.g. Saturday, Sunday and night)

Who you are

  • Completed technical training in the field of automation, electrical engineering, computer science or comparable and completed further training as a maintenance specialist (Federal School) or comparable qualification
  • Mind. 5 years of experience in the maintenance of production plants and thus skilled in troubleshooting and troubleshooting of PLC controls (Beckhoff of advantage)
  • Good knowledge of industrial networks and experience in ICT infrastructure, Windows systems and network components
  • Experience in 2nd-level support or a comparable technical support function is an advantage
  • Knowledge of mechanics, pneumatics and hydraulics is advantageous
  • Experience in direct customer contact and strong service orientation
  • Very good German (minimum B2) and good English skills (minimum B1)

• Structured, proactive and team-oriented personality with fast comprehension, high personal responsibility and flexibility

Who we are

At Belimo, we are proud to make our company a rewarding place to work. Our leaders are value-driven and promote new ideas in their teams around the world. Through our passion, our employees not only create amazing products, but also revolutionize the HVAC industry. Investing in our employees is at the heart of Belimo when it comes to engaging diverse creative talents to build an industry-leading company. The success of our corporate brand is based on the competence and commitment of each individual.
