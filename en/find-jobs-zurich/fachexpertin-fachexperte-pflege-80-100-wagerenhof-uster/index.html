Original description

Immediately or by agreement we are looking for you as Specialist care as nursing home responsible (80 – 100%) At the center of our activities are the people with a congenital or acquired partly severe and multiple impairment. Through differentiated perception, you recognize their needs and accompany them on the basis of professional care. Together with the operational and the agogic house manager, you form the management team of a house association. The specialists working in the house association are accompanied, supported and empowered by you in everyday life. What matters is you. Every single person is at the center with their individual quality of life. We do everything we can to ensure that our approximately 250 residents, 246 daycareers and 52 employees at protected workplaces in the Wagerenhof can enjoy the highest possible quality of life. Wagerenhof is an attractive employer for employees of different backgrounds and professionalism and a competent training partner for learners of all levels.

For this we give you the responsibility

  • Nursing professional leadership in the house association
  • Professional and conceptual further development of care as a member of the team of nursing home managers (nursing experts)
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Efficient and effective cooperation with the care service
  • Cooperation with external specialists (specialists, therapists and other specialist bodies)

You fit in with us and at this point, if you also put our residents at the center of your activities and you bring the following:

  • Dipl. Nursing professional (FH, HF)
  • Additional training as a nursing expert (preferably in neuro-rehabilitation, geriatrics or palliation)
  • Several years of professional experience in a relevant job environment
  • Good professional and social skills
  • High willingness for interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation

This makes us the place for your work

  • The appreciative, human togetherness
  • The well thought-out overall concept and the technical support available at any time
  • The village character and the park-like structure of the Wagerenhof
