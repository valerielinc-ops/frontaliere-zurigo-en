Original description

Do you have a passion for international insurance and a talent for building relationships across borders? Are you ready to take ownership of a dynamic, globally connected role where your work directly supports large corporate clients around the world? If you thrive in a fast-paced, multicultural environment and love solving complex problems — this could be the perfect next step for you! About the Role At Swiss Re Corporate Solutions, we partner with trusted Network Partners — external insurance companies in countries where we don't have local servicing capabilities — to help us deliver seamless insurance solutions to our global clients. As a Network Partner Analyst, you'll be at the heart of this operation, serving as a key point of contact between our Producing Offices and our global partner network. This is a role with real impact, real variety, and real opportunity to grow in a truly international setting. Key

  • Responsibilities:

Coordinate and manage the end-to-end process of issuing local insurance quotations, policies, and invoices using internal systems for your own portfolio Support team leads in managing regional portfolios, setting priorities, and acting as a team representative in complex program deal teams Onboard, train, and develop team capabilities, supporting team leads in building a high- performing, knowledgeable team Represent the team in global projects and strategic priorities, bringing a solutions-focused mindset to cross-functional initiatives Perform quality checks to ensure all policies are accurate, compliant, and delivered on time to meet and exceed client expectations Monitor and support the collection of payments from local partners, ensuring accurate and timely processing Gather and document market intelligence and partner-specific information that impacts service delivery Drive process improvement by creating and updating clear guidelines and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) to enhance team efficiency Build and maintain trusted relationships with Network Partners and internal stakeholders across multiple geographies About the Team Swiss Re's International Program Services Team is a cornerstone of our global insurance delivery, enabling corporate clients with operations across multiple countries to receive consistent, high-quality coverage wherever they operate. Our team spans Bratislava, Kuala Lumpur, and Mexico City, ensuring proximity to our network partners and seamless service from policy quotation through to premium collection. We are a collaborative, multicultural team that champions curiosity, ownership, and continuous improvement — and we genuinely enjoy working together to solve complex, global challenges.

You take ownership of your responsibilities, build trusted relationships across borders, and approach every challenge with a proactive, solutions-focused attitude. You're equally comfortable working independently and contributing to a team, and you bring genuine curiosity and a commitment to continuous learning to everything you do.

Insurance industry experience in product management, client service, finance, or operations — with a solid understanding of international insurance programs Excellent communication skills in both English and Spanish — written and verbal — with the ability to engage effectively with diverse, multicultural stakeholders Proven ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously, work independently under pressure, and deliver high-quality outputs within tight deadlines

Experience in coordinating or supporting cross-functional or international teams Familiarity with Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) development and process improvement methodologies Strong analytical and lateral thinking skills, with the ability to simplify complex information and communicate it clearly Attention to detail and quality focus, with a track record of delivering accurate, compliant work Experience working in a global or matrix organization and navigating complex stakeholder landscapes Goal-oriented mindset with a demonstrated commitment to professional growth and development Proficiency in additional languages is a plus Our company has a hybrid work model where the expectation is that you will be in the office at least three days per week.
