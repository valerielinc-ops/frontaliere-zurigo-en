Full description

To strengthen our service team in the Central Swiss region, we are looking for you!

As a leading technology company, Hoval has been inspiring for decades with groundbreaking system solutions for heating and air conditioning technology. In order to offer our extensive services to our customers, we need motivated personalities who use their knowledge in a targeted manner. You like to work independently, actively tackle challenges and want to develop professionally? Then we look forward to meeting you.

Your activities

  • Remediation of Hoval oil heaters
  • Maintenance and maintenance services
  • Customer advice on the sale of service contracts and spare parts
  • Pikettdienst, classification in consultation with the team
  • The missions are mainly in the Central Switzerland region from the place of residence

Your profile

  • Completed technical training, for example as a heating installer, fireplace sweeper, automotive mechatronics engineer or agricultural machinery mechanic or similar
  • A high degree of independence in the execution of work
  • Enjoyment of active customer contact
  • Resilience and pleasant manners
  • Enjoyment of a high travel activity

We offer you

  • Responsible and varied activity
  • A collegial and open-minded team
  • Attractive social benefits and a defined benefit system
  • Need-based training and continuous training
  • Modern tools such as business vehicles, laptops, smartphones, etc.

Whether you already have experience in the service environment or come from a technical professional field: At Hoval, exciting challenges, a strong team and attractive development opportunities await you. We look forward to getting to know you and receiving your full online application.
