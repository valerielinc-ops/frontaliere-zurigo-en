Full description

Do you want to combine your experience in nursing with your pleasure in vocational training? On our bedside station Nephrology/Angiology you accompany learners and students in their daily work, impart your knowledge and actively shape the vocational training in the department together with us. At the same time, you stay close to the care and care for patients with complex nephrological diseases.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • In the learning and working community you look after patients with complex nephrology diseases • You actively bring vocational training topics into departmental affairs • You impart knowledge and joy to the profession • Together with the vocational training person of the department, you are responsible for all educational topics Requirements • You are an experienced nurse HF/FH or FaGe • At least two years of professional experience in the acute hospital round off your profile • Very good communication skills and a quick understanding characterize you • In quickly changing situations, you feel comfortable and set the right priorities • Working with learners and students brings you joy • Ideally, you have the educational training SVEB 1 or are ready to complete it • A cooperative and constructive cooperation in the team is a matter of course for you We offer: With the expansion of our department, a growing permanent team is created in which you can actively participate and shape our joint team culture. At the same time, you will gain insights into other disciplines outside of nephrology. Our department specializes in peritoneal dialysis, hemodialysis takes place in another department. In your day-to-day work, you can count on the support of our two experienced department heads as well as the Shared Governance Team from subject, education and management. An appreciative, self-determined working environment and a good compatibility with your private life are important to us. Therefore, we take your wishes into account in the service planning as best as possible. Interested? We look forward to meeting you. :
