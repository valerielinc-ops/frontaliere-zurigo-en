Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School High Promenade secondary school teacher mbA for piano 60 - 70% The cantonal school Hohe Promenade is a long-school school with an old- and modern-language profile (from 2029 onwards additionally with specializations in social sciences and natural sciences), an immersion course German/English and a strong STEM offer. Around 800 students learn at our school, teach and accompany 150 teachers and employees in administration and operations. In attitude and practice, we combine inspired tradition with future-oriented innovation. Committed students, the open-minded college and the latest infrastructure ensure a stimulating environment. More information about our school in the heart of Zurich in the immediate vicinity of the universities can be found at www.kshp.ch . At the beginning of the school year 2027/ 28 or by agreement, 60 to 70 job percentages as a secondary school teacher mbA (with special tasks) are to be filled at our school in the subject piano. There is an internal application. Their tasks

  • They teach the piano in the basic and free subject in the lower and upper high school.
  • They initiate and lead ensembles and participate in interdisciplinary projects.
  • They are involved in the student board and perform special tasks within the school (specialist board, participation in commissions and school development projects, etc.). Your profile
  • Ideally, they have two master’s degrees (pedagogy, performance) or a comparable education.
  • For several years, they have been engaged in teaching children and young people, including in gifted development programmes, using digital opportunities in a targeted manner.
  • They pursue a creative concert activity with broadcast.
  • They are an open, team-ready and committed person who cares about both professional and personal education
  • They want to engage in a diverse and lively school culture. Our offer
  • In our school you welcome committed students, a competent student body for instrumental and singing lessons and an open-minded college.
  • We are a centrally located and modernly equipped school.
  • The employment conditions depend on cantonal specifications. Application Have we aroused your interest? Come and talk to us. We look forward to your application. Please submit your documents no later than 30. October 2026 exclusively via the online tool. Martin Schaub, Rector, Tel. 044 224 64 64, e-mail: martin.schaub@kshp.ch, is available for information.

Pensum: 60-70 %
