About the job As a Product Manager at DeepMind, you will be responsible for shipping AI research breakthroughs into Google’s AI models, products, and open ecosystems. As a Product Manager on Frontier AI within DeepMind, you will be working with AI researchers who are focused on making advances across new model architectures, AGI, and applications like Agents and Robotics. As the Product Manager for AI Ecosystems within Frontier AI, your job will be to guide the strategy and execution across our open-weight models specialized variants (such as the broader Gemmaverse ecosystem) as well as our open interactive training environments, agent harnesses, and privacy-preserving evaluation sandboxes.

You will understand how frontier research breakthroughs can be translated into ecosystems of open AI systems and creating an impactful two-way flywheel between the global open-source community and Google’s core foundation models like Gemini and Gemma. You will help research teams navigate technical, safety, and ecosystem tradeoffs, build collaborations with core model development teams, and partner with Google product areas (such as Cloud, Android, Pixel, and Search) as well as external developers, enterprises, academic researchers, and public-sector institutions to address real-world needs across on-device AI, sovereign and regulated workflows, scientific discovery, and societal benefit. In addition to shipping open-weight model variants, training recipes, and interactive environments, you will build the developer tooling, open-source runtime integrations, and communication strategy so that our open AI systems achieve widespread adoption and set the industry standard. Artificial intelligence will be one of humanity’s most transformative inventions. At Google DeepMind, we are a pioneering AI lab with exceptional interdisciplinary teams focused on advancing AI development to solve complex global challenges and accelerate high-quality product innovation for billions of users. We use our technologies for widespread public benefit and scientific discovery, ensuring safety and ethics are always our highest priority. We are pushing the boundaries across multiple domains. Our global teams offer diverse learning opportunities and varied career pathways for those driven to achieve exceptional results through collective effort.
