Dean of Studies of the Faculty of Mathematics and Science

Job opening immediately or by agreement, indefinitely

The Faculty of Mathematics and Natural Sciences (MNF) is one of the seven faculties of the University of Zurich (UZH). The faculty comprises 14 own institutes and nine institutes jointly managed with other faculties. It occupies a leading position in international research and teaching, which reflects its high social importance. The study offer of the faculty extends over the entire scientific and mathematical subject spectrum. With more than 5000 students and the highest growth rates, the MNF is the second largest faculty in UZH. The Dean of Studies supports both students and lecturers, manages and coordinates all study-relevant processes and serves as an important interface in the field of teaching to the other faculties and central units of the UZH as well as to universities in Germany and abroad. Applications include the processing of admission and other study-related applications as well as objections and recourses.

Tasks

  • Coordination and processing of admission and other study-related applications, in particular complex and legally demanding cases.
  • Processing of objections, appeals and procedures for granting a legal hearing, as well as the preparation of opinions and draft decisions.
  • Professional management of a directly subordinate, distribution of tasks and quality assurance.
  • Collaboration with departments and central offices of UZH as well as coordination with the head of the study department.

Profile

  • Law degree (bachelor or equivalent).
  • professional experience in processing legally demanding applications or administrative procedures
  • Experience in higher education and in professional leadership is beneficial.
  • Legal judgment, independent and careful working methods as well as solution orientation and teamwork.
  • German language skills at level C2 and English language skills at level C1 as well as very good written expression skills.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office; SAP knowledge is an advantage.

We offer them We offer a responsible and diverse activity in a collegial team and an intellectually stimulating professional environment in an attractive workplace on the Irchel campus of the University of Zurich. The employment conditions are based on cantonal personnel law and include contemporary social benefits as well as a wide range of further training opportunities. We attach great importance to a good reconciliation of work and other obligations.
