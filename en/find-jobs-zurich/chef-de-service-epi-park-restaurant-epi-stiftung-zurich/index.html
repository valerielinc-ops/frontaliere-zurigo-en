Full description

The Swiss Epilepsy Foundation (EPI) is a multi-segment company. It comprises the EPI WohnWerk, the Lengg Upper Level School, the Donation of Dapples, a seminar centre with a restaurant and the EPI Central Services. In addition, the Swiss Epilepsy Foundation and another foundation form the sponsorship of the Klinik Lengg AG, with one clinic for epileptology and neurorehabilitation each. About 1000 employees are committed to these social tasks.

The area is located on the outskirts of Zurich, with a fantastic view of the nearby Lake Zurich and the Alps.
