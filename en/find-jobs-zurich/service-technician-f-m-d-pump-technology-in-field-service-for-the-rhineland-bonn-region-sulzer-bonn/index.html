Full description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

As part of the Water & Industrial Business Unit of Sulzer AG, Sulzer Flow Germany GmbH distributes products and solutions for the mechanical and biological cleaning of municipal and industrial wastewater.

As a service technician, you are our face to the customer. On daily Mondays, you investigate and solve any problems that arise in customer facilities. They are used in the context of new assembly, commissioning, conversion measures, inspections, maintenance, findings and repairs of pumps, lifting systems and control systems.

Here you are responsible for

  • Testing, maintenance & repair of pumps, lifting equipment and controls at the installation site
  • New installation of pumping stations, lifting and switchgear at the installation site
  • Disturbance analyses
  • Advising our customers on technical issues

To be successful at this point, you should bring the following:

  • Completed technical vocational training as a mechatronics engineer (or electronics engineer/industrial mechanic or equivalent)
  • Ideally, an electrical specialist for specified activities (otherwise carried out by us as training)
  • First professional experience as a service technician
  • Basic knowledge of Word and Excel
  • Category B driving licence
  • Willingness to travel (about 80% day travel)

At Sulzer, you will become part of a renowned team. We offer you a high degree of personal responsibility as well as excellent future prospects.

  • Contract of indefinite duration

  • 30 days of vacation
  • Intensive familiarisation and training opportunities
  • Possibility of use in the place of residence
  • Top equipped service vans with modern tools
