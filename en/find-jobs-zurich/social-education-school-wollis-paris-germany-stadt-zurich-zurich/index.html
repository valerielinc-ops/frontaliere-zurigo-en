Full description

For the care facility of the school Wollishofen-Im Lee we are looking for reinforcement on 1 October 2026 or by appointment.

Tasks

As a social pedagogue, you work for five days in the middle school and together with the team ensure the care of the nursery. They work in a team with other social educators and specialists and are supported in the catering area and in the care by care assistants. You take your holidays in the school-free time. Precise information on working hours can be discussed during a personal conversation with the management of supervision.

Their tasks include

  • Co-design of school support and ensure day-to-day operations
  • Accompaniment and promotion of children in social and educational matters
  • Cooperation in the pedagogical team, domestic and administrative activities
  • Contacts with parents from different cultural backgrounds

Profile

  • As a social pedagogue you have a degree in social pedagogy / social work (FH / HF), a primary school teaching diploma or an equivalent education
  • In the case of a foreign diploma, a Swiss diploma recognition is mandatory
  • Professional experience in caring for children (hoard, crèche, home)
  • You have very good oral and written knowledge of German
  • You are a flexible, resilient and open personality

We offer

...a varied and meaningful activity in a living environment. Our employees also benefit from the attractive employment conditions of the City of Zurich. A motivated and open-minded team is looking forward to your application!

About us

The school district Uto is one of seven school districts in the city of Zurich and comprises districts 2 and 3 with the districts Leimbach, Wollishofen, Enge, Friesenberg and Alt-Wiedikon. We are a primary school with five kindergarten classes, three 1st, two 2nd, three 3rd grades and two 4th to 6th grades each and a special school class of the Center for Hearing and Language Zurich (ZGSZ) with partial integration (TIK). A cooperation with the ZGSZ also takes place at the kindergarten level.
