Position Summary

Position manages the designers and order related engineer(s) and techs, as well as the technical documentation coordinators in the preparation of submittal documentation and production documentation required to support sales orders and their release through production and to the customer. Technical expert for customer witness testing and product knowledge

Duties and Responsibilities

  • Manage work assignments and workload of personnel.
  • Manage and report on department on metrics.
  • Manage or provide technical support and guidance to other departments.
  • Support operations in planning and scheduling of orders.
  • Correct and provide future mitigation to reduce warranty.
  • Review and provide disposition of Non-Conformance Material Requests (NCMR) assigned to ORE.
  • Review and provide actions for Process Deviation Requests assigned to ORE.
  • Audit Technical Resource Center (TRC) usage and efficiency. supporting ORE.
  • Support and foster ORE harmonization with other ORE departments throughout the organization.
  • Develop and manage processes that improve department efficiency through forms and tools.
  • Prepare and manage department budget.
  • Interview, hire/fire new personnel.
  • Develop performance metrics and conduct performance reviews.
  • Perform other duties and special projects as assigned.
