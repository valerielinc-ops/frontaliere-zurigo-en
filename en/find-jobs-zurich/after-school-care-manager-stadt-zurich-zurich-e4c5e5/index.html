Full description

Become part of our team! We are looking for a curator for the school Im Gut 80% for the primary school (working days: 5 days, Monday-Friday)

Tasks

  • Care of children in the midday and evening hoard
  • Participation in cross-group care during school holidays

  • Administrative and operational tasks
  • Implementation of educational objectives

Profile

  • Degree in social pedagogy/social work FH/HF or an equivalent education mandatory (for applications with a foreign diploma / certificate, a Swiss diploma recognition must be mandatory)
  • Motivated, open, resilient personality with a positive human image
  • Flexibility as well as very good team and communication skills
  • Resilience and high flexibility in cooperation
  • Solution-oriented basic attitude in daily work
  • Interest and willingness to participate in a changing school supplementary care

We offer

  • varied, interesting and independent activity in a versatile, changing field of activity of the school

  • a contemporary employment according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich

About us

Tensions in the heart of Züri - there(s) are me! Every day at the pulse of education and where our future is growing and learning.

The school Im Gut is a day school in the city of Zurich and is located in the middle of a quiet residential quarter in district 3. Around 360 children are taught and cared for here from kindergarten to the sixth primary class. School care is divided into seven care facilities, which are organized in kindergarten and primary level care.
