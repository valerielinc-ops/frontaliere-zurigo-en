Specialist:in Visualization Architecture & XR (m/f/d)

100%, Zurich, temporary

Printed Printing

ETH Zurich is launching a pioneering research project directly related to building practice. The HIL building on Campus Hönggerberg is transformed into a Living Lab. The building in need of renovation will be rebuilt and expanded. Professorships in the Department of Architecture (D-ARCH) as well as Construction, Environment and Geomatics (D-BAUG) will research and plan the renovation of the HIL building in the sense of circular construction in the coming years. The aim is to further develop sustainable design and construction in the existing company and to demonstrate it in an exemplary manner on a real planning and construction project.

We are looking for a creative and tech-savvy personality for the visual translation of our Living Lab HIL project. In this role, you will be responsible for high-end architectural visualizations, the processing of complex research data in interactive dashboards and the development of immersive Extended Reality (XR) applications for mediation and planning. Due to the project, the position has a multi-year, temporary focus. We initially issue a one-year contract with an extension option.

Job description

Taking on this project-related role They focus on the following tasks and responsibilities:

  • High-end visualization: Creation of atmospheric, precise and architecturally demanding renderings, animations and image compositions for the overall project.
  • XR development: Conception, preparation and programming of virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR) scenarios for spatial verification of prototypes and planning statuses.
  • Project dashboards: Visual and interactive preparation of complex project data as well as the development of dashboards for the clear communication of sustainability and research metrics.
  • Data interfaces: Preparation and optimized conversion of 3D data from Rhino, Revit or ArchiCAD for real-time rendering engines and VR platforms.
  • Interface communication: Close cooperation with the digitization specialist as well as the project managers for visual documentation and communication of intermediate statuses.

Profile

To ideally meet the requirements of this demanding position, bring the following profile:

  • Education: Completed degree in architecture, computational design, game design, virtual design or a comparable discipline with a strong focus on architectural representation.
  • Visualization skills: Demonstrate excellence in creating high-end architectural renderings and animations as well as excellent postproduction skills.
  • Real-time & XR: In-depth experience with real-time engines (e.g. Unreal Engine, Unity, RADii) and the development of applications for VR/AR headsets.
  • 3D software: Very good knowledge in Rhino
  • Experience with BIM software (Revit/ArchiCAD) for data transfer is of great advantage.
  • Programming reference: Basic knowledge of Python or visual scripting (grasshopper, etc.) is desired for dashboard creation and XR interactions.
  • Soft skills: stylish, visual and aesthetic sense, structured working methods for large amounts of data, team spirit and enthusiasm for the interface between tech, research and design.
  • Languages: Very good German (min. B2/C1 for voting in the team) and good English skills in word and writing.
