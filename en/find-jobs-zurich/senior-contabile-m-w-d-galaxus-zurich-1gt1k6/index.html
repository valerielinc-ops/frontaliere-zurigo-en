Full description

For our eleven-member team, we are looking for a Senior Accountant from now on or by agreement. Together we are responsible for the financial reporting of Digitec Galaxus AG as well as the international subsidiaries. Our responsibilities also include monthly and annual financial statements, tax returns, daily liquidity planning and group-wide transfer pricing.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Headquarters, Pfingstweidstrasse 60b, 8005 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • The responsibility for central areas of financial accounting lies with you. In addition, you work with monthly and annual accounts
  • You analyze balance sheet and income statement positions, clear deviations down to the transaction level and follow them consistently up to the solution
  • You use automation and AI specifically for voting, deviation analysis, documentation and other recurring work. You pay attention to professional quality, traceability and effective controls
  • You assess demanding business transactions independently and develop suitable booking and process solutions
  • You check data flows between operational systems, interfaces, data warehouse and financial accounting for completeness and accuracy
  • You translate technical requirements into technical requirements and accompanying system changes, interface adjustments and tests
  • You recognize potential for improvement at an early stage and develop accounting processes further across departments
  • Support for internal and external audits is also one of your tasks

Requirements

  • Several years of experience: in financial accounting and financial statement preparation
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): With a focus on finance or accounting or a comparable further education in finance and accounting
  • Sound accounting knowledge
  • Strong IT affinity and a good understanding of ERP systems, data flows and interfaces
  • Knowledge of SQL, BI tools or automation solutions is an advantage
  • Experience with complex, transaction-intensive business models, ideally in retail, e-commerce or technology environments
  • Interest in AI and new technologies as well as pleasure in practically testing and implementing their application possibilities in financial reporting
  • Pronounced analytical and networked thinking as well as the ability to independently structure new and unclear issues and consistently advance them to the solution
  • Taking calculated risks to create value and drive growth
  • Have the courage to try things without planning them down to the smallest detail
  • German (flowing)
  • English (flowing)
