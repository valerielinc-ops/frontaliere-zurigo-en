Full description

Holcim Central Europe West (Switzerland, Italy, southern Germany and Haut-Rhin) is looking forward to exciting legal challenges in a dynamic environment. As Legal Counsel, you become a trusted contact person for our business partners in Switzerland and support the General Counsel directly in groundbreaking projects – from decarbonization to the digital transformation of the legal department. Bring your expertise and move the world of tomorrow with us!

What you're expecting

  • Comprehensive legal advice: You advise and support our Swiss business partners in key areas such as Litigation, M&A, Competition Law, Real Estate and complex commercial contracts.
  • Strategic case management: You conduct court cases and proceedings including strategy development and control external lawyers.
  • Support future projects: You support legal management and negotiation with strategic projects and initiatives, especially with our pioneering industrial decarbonisation projects.
  • Optimise legal infrastructure: You develop, review and revise contracts, standard templates and protocols and assist all departments in general legal matters.
  • Legal Tech & Innovation: You actively drive the digitalization of the legal department of the Central Europe West cluster and develop digital tools for sustainable process optimization.

What you're saying

  • In-depth training: You have successfully completed your university degree in law and have the Swiss attorney's patent (authorization as a lawyer/lawyer in Switzerland).
  • Relevant experience: You will have at least 3 to 5 years of relevant legal experience, which you ideally have gathered in an economic law firm or as an in-house office.
  • Digital A-Hinliness: In addition to the safe handling of MS Office and Google Tools, you have a strong enthusiasm for Legal Tech and the optimization of digital workflows.
  • Strong personality: You are characterized by a solution-oriented, pragmatic approach, high flexibility, strong assertiveness and a true team spirit.
  • Language skills: You communicate in German and English (in word and writing) with confidence in your language and style; good knowledge of French and/or Italian is a welcome advantage.

Our offer

  • Onboarding & Start: Look forward to a team-oriented, open and future-oriented work environment. We accompany you from the beginning with a careful training, practical and structured, so that you can successfully start out.
  • Career & Development: We create opportunities. We focus on you as a person with your individual strengths and potential. If you want, you will be promoted with us and can achieve a lot.
  • Appreciation & Team Culture: With us, you are part of a strong and inclusive team that keeps together, shares ideas and masteres challenges together. A trustful leadership culture ensures that your commitment is seen and appreciated.
  • Flexibility & Work-Life Balance: We offer flexible working time arrangements, 41 hours, part-time opportunities, 25 days holidays, holiday purchase, extended maternity and paternity leave
  • Attractive compensation: We offer our employees an attractive compensation package with many additional benefits and a very good pension fund.
  • Employee benefits: From reka checks to shares at a preferential price to the Swibeco benefit world - we ensure that you feel completely comfortable and profit from attractive additional services.
  • Health and Social Affairs: We are committed to your health and promote it through preventive measures.
