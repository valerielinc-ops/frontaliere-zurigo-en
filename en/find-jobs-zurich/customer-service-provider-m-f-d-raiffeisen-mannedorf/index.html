The Raiffeisenbank rechtser Zürichsee - a successful, modern and regionally strongly anchored bank - is looking for a committed and service-oriented personality as customer advisor (m/f/d) for the Männedorf office.

What awaits you?

  • Support of an own customer portfolio as well as holistic advice for private customers "base" in the areas of numbers, savings, investment and provision
  • Active recognition of customer potential and targeted further development of customer relationships – with the involvement of specialists if necessary
  • Expansion of existing customer relationships and acquisition of new customers
  • Perceiving the host role and ensuring a positive customer experience in the office
  • Competent support of customers in the digital banking world
  • Continuous personal development with prospects within the bank

What do you bring?

  • You have completed banking education
  • You already have experience in consulting and selling basic products and find suitable solutions for your customers
  • Customer orientation is a matter of course for you – you actively respond to needs and create real added value
  • With your communicative, open nature, you convince just as much as with your safe and trustworthy appearance.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
