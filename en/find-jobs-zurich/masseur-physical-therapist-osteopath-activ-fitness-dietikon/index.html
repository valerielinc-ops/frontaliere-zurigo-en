Full description

As part of our new organization, we are looking for a massage therapist who will allow our guests time off. Do you appreciate the combination of consulting and performing professional massages? Then you are right with us!

Luogo di lavoro: ACTIV FITNESS, Activ Fitness Dietikon, Löwenstrasse 17, 8953 Dietikon

Important information

This is a tenancy in our studios, for self-employed masseurs* We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned. An application letter or video is desired for this position.

Mansioni

  • You perform professional massages
  • You advise and supervise our members and non-members
  • You give a relaxed atmosphere
  • You are actively promoting the massage offers
  • You ensure cleanliness and order
  • You independently run your own practice

Requirements

  • 1 to 3 years of experience: As a masseur
  • Completed basic or continuing education: Completed training as a masseur with health insurance recognition
  • You are ready to work in the evening as well as on weekends and holidays
  • You are attentive, resilient and service-oriented
  • You value a well-groomed appearance
  • in German (negotiation certain)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central location: Our studios are located in a well-developed central location
  • Migros Bank: expense-free private and investment account with preferential interest
  • Mobile subscription: National flat subscription from CHF 10.– / month (Swisscom /digitec connect)
  • Employee offers: Various discounts in the area of holidays and insurance (Hertz up to 10%, Generali 25%, Axa insurance up to 15%, etc.)
  • Du-culture: We stand for a respectful coexistence at eye level

Contatto

Application and contact Susanne Hug HR Business Partner

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Expert discussion: In a specialist discussion, specific knowledge relevant for the execution of the job is queried by the manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, the team. If possible, there is time afterwards to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition: After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks

Grado di occupazione: 100 %

Requirements

  • 1 bis 3 Jahre Erfahrung: Als Masseur*in

  • Abgeschlossene Grund- oder Weiterbildung: Abgeschlossene Ausbildung als Masseur*in mit Krankenkassenanerkennung

  • Du bist bereit, abends sowie an Wochenenden und Feiertagen zu arbeiten

  • Du bist aufmerksam, belastbar und dienstleistungsorientiert
