Full description

Are you ready to dive into the exciting world of food production? At Eisberg AG, you have the opportunity to discover your passion for food in a future-proof industry and start your career with the exciting training opportunity as a food technologist/login EFZ with a focus on convenience products.

  • Contract type: apprenticeship
  • Pensum: 100%
  • Working time model: Flexible time
  • Job opening: 01.08.2027
  • Language: German
  • Location: Eisberg AG, Hüttenwiesenstrasse 4 8108 Dällikon

Tasks

  • Explore different production areas and get to know the industrial processing of salads, vegetables and fruits. 🥗
  • Planning and monitoring the entire production process and independently resolving any faults that occur. ⚙️
  • Working with the latest technology and operating large machines. 🏭
  • Ensure the quality of our products – from raw materials 🌱 to the finished product 📦.

Requirements

  • A secondary school degree A or equivalent. 🎓
  • Interest in biology, chemistry and physics – and good grades in these subjects. 🌇
  • Joy in dealing with food and a good technical understanding. 🥗
  • Physical strength and craftsmanship. ✔️
  • Team spirit, commitment and motivation. 🤝
  • Curiosity and motivation to learn new things and develop yourself. 🔪
  • Enthusiasm for fresh produce and salad. 🥗

This is what we offer

  • Food: Cuisine is very important to us: Freshly prepared salads, soups, tea and fruit are available to our employees free of charge. In addition, you can take home products from the personal shelf every day.
  • Mobility: Benefit from convenient connections to public transport, attractive discounts for public transport and sufficient parking – sustainable and worry-free on the road!
  • Discounts & Discounts: You benefit from the Coop Personal Discount Card with various discounts in the retail trade, in specialty shops and supermarkets as well as from other attractive services, for example in the area of insurance.
