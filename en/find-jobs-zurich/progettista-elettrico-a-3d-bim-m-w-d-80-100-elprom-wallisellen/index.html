Job title Electrical planner 3D/BIM m/f (80-100%) Description For our locations in Wallisellen and Pratteln, we are looking for motivated specialists in electrical planning – from electric planners with development potential to experienced project managers with overall responsibility. Your tasks Electrical planning

  • Creation of project, execution, principle and electrical diagrams (2D/3D/BIM)
  • Collaboration in complex and versatile electrical projects
  • Support for feasibility studies & condition analyses
  • Preparation of technical documents & documentation

Project management

  • Overall responsibility for planning, quality, costs & deadlines
  • Technical leadership of project teams & coordination of all stakeholders
  • Customer advice, presentations & support in the acquisition
  • Cooperation in conceptual solutions (business areas, variants, norms & standards)

You bring that with you

  • Training as electrical planner EFZ or electrical installer EFZ
  • Further training or motivation (higher technical examination HFP, engineer BSc, technician HF, or comparable)
  • Experience in the planning or execution of electrical projects
  • Able to deal with ICT and CAD environments
  • Enthusiasm for modern technologies in electrical engineering
  • Team-oriented, networked thinking and pleasure in customer contact

You can look forward to it.

  • 6 Week Holidays & Paid Overtime
  • Attractive pension benefits & supplementary insurance
  • Flexible working hours & modern hybrid working environment
  • Support for further education and personal development
  • Plenty of design freedom, open communication & flat hierarchies
  • Long-term, motivated team & collegial corporate culture

Interested? Would you like to shape the electrical technology of tomorrow with us? Then we look forward to meeting you. Questions about these positions will gladly answer Urs Bugmann.
