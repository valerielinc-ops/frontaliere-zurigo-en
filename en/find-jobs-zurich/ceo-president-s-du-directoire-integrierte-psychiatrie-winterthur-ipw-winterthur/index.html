Our previous job owner has decided to resign from his position for health reasons. For this reason, we are looking for an innovative, energetic and implementation-strong personality as CEO / Chairman of the management 100% The Integrated Psychiatry Winterthur – Zurich Unterland (IPW) advises and accompanies people in crisis situations and with mental illnesses. As a psychiatric and psychotherapeutic competence center for the regions of Winterthur and Zurich Unterland, ipw serves people with a wide range of diseases and in all phases of the disease at 13 locations. ipw is particularly distinguished by individual treatment methods and offers tailored to the individual. In addition, ipw is a recognized educational institution for doctors, nurses and other health and therapy professions. It offers training places for various basic training courses and acts as a teaching hospital for the medical faculty of the University of Zurich. In close cooperation with the management and the hospital council, you shape the corporate and business strategy of ipw, taking into account current health policy developments and regulatory requirements. They are responsible for the creation and implementation of strategies and concepts as well as budget adherence and promote a culture of innovation, collaboration and continuous improvement. Her strategic and entrepreneurial actions contribute significantly to the sustainable medical and economic success of the organization as well as to the positioning of ipw as an attractive employer. As a driving force, you and your leadership team actively promote topics such as innovation, cooperation, digitalization and sustainable development. You act as a reliable, binding and cooperative contact person for internal and external stakeholders. You find an environment with creative leeway stimulating, you use it with foresight and implementation strength.
