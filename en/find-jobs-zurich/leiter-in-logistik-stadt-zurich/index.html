Full description

Due to an imminent retirement, we are looking for a committed successor as Head of Logistics as of 1 January 2027 (or by agreement). In this key position, you lead around 11 directly subordinate employees and control the entire logistics chain. You are responsible for ensuring that all required materials, spare parts and equipment are always in the right place at the right time.

Tasks

  • Warehouse and inventory management and procurement: You manage the central and external warehouse and determine the availability of critical materials and resources. You are responsible for the needs-based procurement of materials and services in accordance with the applicable submission and procurement regulations and ensure a legally secure award.
  • Fleet and transport management: You are responsible for the planning and scheduling of all internal and external transports as well as the management, maintenance and further development of our vehicle fleet.
  • Process optimization and digitalization: You optimize logistics processes, define standards and drive forward the digitalization of logistics processes.
  • Organization and administration: You support the planning of events with regard to infrastructure and logistics, support the Facility Service in the Hardhof with your team and help with budget planning.
  • Occupational safety and environment: You ensure compliance with safety, environmental and health regulations and are responsible for the proper and environmentally compatible disposal of materials and waste. You also ensure the availability of service clothing and personal protective equipment (PPE).

Profile

  • training as a logistician EFZ, merchant EFZ or in a technical profession with in-depth experience in procurement or logistics
  • Further education in the field of logistics, supply chain management, purchasing or business administration
  • Several years of management experience in warehousing, logistics, procurement or materials management
  • You have a flair to actively shape change and optimization processes, drive new solutions and take employees along on this path.
  • Experience with ERP systems such as SAP knowledge is of great advantage
  • Category B driving licence

We offer

An exciting and varied area of responsibility awaits you in a collegial, agile working environment. Our employees benefit from attractive urban employment conditions such as flexible forms of work or targeted education and training. We also have a modern infrastructure and good public transport connections.

About us

The Zurich water supply supplies the city and numerous municipalities with fresh, clean drinking water around the clock. We fulfill this task in the service of the public with around 300 dedicated employees.
