Shape the future of building informatics with us! For our motivated team in Zurich, we are looking for a committed and competent personality as an ICT System Engineer with a focus on modern IT infrastructure solutions.

You can move that with us.

  • Planning, implementation and operation of modern IT infrastructure solutions (client, server, storage, cloud)
  • Creation of solution concepts and technical documentation
  • Support and advice of our customers on site and remotely
  • Operation and further development of our managed services
  • 3rd-level support and piquette as needed
  • Cooperation with internal interfaces (e.g. project management, account management)

That's what makes you.

  • Completed IT vocational training
  • Further education at HF, FH or university level (e.g. computer science, electrical engineering)
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in a service-oriented IT environment with external customers
  • In-depth knowledge of at least two of the following areas: Microsoft Server, Virtualization (VMware, Hyper-V), Microsoft Azure / Office365, Client Engineering or Storage / Backup
  • Experience with complex IT projects
  • Manufacturer certifications (e.g. MCSE, VCP) are beneficial
  • Good knowledge of German and English
  • Independent, team-capable and customer-oriented personality
  • Driving licence Cat. B

That's what we offer you.

  • Exciting and varied tasks in a future-oriented environment
  • The most modern work equipment and infrastructure
  • Flexible working hours and home office options
  • Attractive employment conditions including financially supported training
  • A motivated and collaborative team
