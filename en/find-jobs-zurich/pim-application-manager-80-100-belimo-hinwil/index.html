Full description

Introduction

In this role you are responsible for the technical development and operation of our Centric/Contentserv PIM platform. Together with internal departments, the functional application manager and external 177 partners you ensure a stable operation and develop future-oriented solutions for our product information and publication processes.

Main responsibilities

  • Technical responsibility for our Centric/Contentserv PIM platform in close cooperation with the functional application manager and external 177 partners.
  • Ensure stable operation and take over of 3rd level support by analyzing, debugging and coordinating necessary adjustments in PHP code.
  • Further development of the PIM platform with regard to new releases and modern deployment approaches.
  • Design and implementation of forward-looking, high-performance PIM solutions, including connections to re-systems (e.g. ERP and web environment) and customer exports.
  • Advising internal departments on PIM topics and the related publication processes.
  • To provide training and to promote knowledge building on new software processes and technologies.

Skills required

  • Training as an IT engineer with further training in software development or a comparable qualification.
  • A well-founded knowledge of PHP and modern web-frameworks. Knowledge of other programming languages and stylesheet technologies is an advantage.
  • Experience in the operation of container-based applications as well as with infrastructure-as-code concepts. Knowledge of hyperscaler platforms is an advantage.
  • Good knowledge of modern software development processes, especially DevOps, CI/CD and the development methods of the company.
  • Enjoy the cooperation in a small team of specialists, the exchange with international colleagues as well as the evaluation and implementation of new technologies.
  • High self-initiative, flexibility and a structured and solution-oriented way of working.
  • Very good English language skills in word and writing. German skills are of advantage.

About Belimo

Belimo is the world leader in the development, manufacture and distribution of field equipment for energy-efficient control of heating, ventilation and air conditioning systems. Valve drives, control valves, sensors and counters form our core business. Founded in 1975, Belimo has been listed on the Swiss Stock Exchange (SIX) since 1995.
