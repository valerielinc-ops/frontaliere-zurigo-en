As a member of a dynamic team in the area of Shares & Structured Products, you take on tasks related to the issue and the entire life cycle of our Structured Products. With your responsible interface function, you contribute significantly to the success of the business area. In your daily work, you will be in close contact with our sales and trading teams, specialists from risk monitoring, documentation, legal and IT as well as the back office and our subsidiary Zürcher Kantonalbank Finance (Guernsey) Ltd. You work in a dynamic environment where processes are continuously developed and digital solutions are driven forward. In this way, you make a direct contribution to the competitiveness of our bank in the derivatives business.

Your tasks

  • Responsibility for all activities in the life cycle of structured products (emission, adjustments, corporate actions, expiration process)
  • Identify and implement process improvements to increase efficiency and productivity
  • Collaboration in cross-divisional projects, in particular for the introduction of new products, process optimizations and digitalization projects
  • Support the sales and trading teams in the area of shares and structured products in administrative and operational matters
  • Maintain master data and collect business in front office systems
  • Coordinating and solving ad hoc production problems in collaboration with internal and external stakeholders and IT departments
  • Use and promote digital solutions and automation
  • Completed banking apprenticeship, ideally supplemented by additional training in finance or in training
  • Initial professional experience in securities
  • Sound interest in derivatives and structured products
  • Experience working on projects, ideally in the financial or banking environment
  • Reliable, independent and service-oriented workflow
  • Resilience and teamwork in a dynamic environment round off your profile
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Task area that gives you the chance to gain a broad insight into the bank
  • We count on your ideas and offer you space for your impulses
  • We invest a lot in our employees and offer individual training opportunities
  • Modern workplace in district 5 with very good transport connections (hard bridge station)
  • Various events and internal networks offer you the opportunity to network quickly
  • Comprehensive package, which in addition to the attractive basic salary and the variable compensation offers many additional services and benefits (e.g. sports offers, child care, health insurance, etc.)
