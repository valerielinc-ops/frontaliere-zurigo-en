At our "Wohnen" location in Wallisellen, tenants not only feel at home, but are also looked after optimally and efficiently by our new division of tasks in the team. If your heart beats for real estate, you are motivated, goal-oriented and also like to work in a team, then we look forward to your application as (junior) real estate manager (m/f/d) Wallisellen, 80 - 100% This is what moves you:

  • You take responsibility for managing a portfolio of residential and mixed-use properties

  • An efficient and goal-oriented working method and good communication skills with negotiation skills qualify you in dealing with different and demanding target groups
