Original description

As a reliable contract partner with an excellent market position, we offer motivated franchise entrepreneurs an exciting, future-oriented operating concept at attractive locations for the independent management of a Migrol filling station plant. Are you ready to take the step towards successful self-employment?

Mansioni

  • Independent management of the Migrol Auto Service Station. The operation includes a gas station, a car repair shop, a serviced car wash and a small kiosk
  • You set up your own company and operate the station with your own capital and on your own account in the franchise system
  • Responsible for the operational management of the site
  • Goal-oriented leadership and promotion of your team including independent recruitment
  • Ensuring the implementation of the car wash and workshop concept and compliance with standards
  • Responsible for attracting new customers as well as business results, turnover and costs
  • Responsible for compliance with and enforcement of health, safety and hygiene regulations
  • Achieve or exceed through good cooperation with the headquarters Your agreed budget targets

Requirements

  • Professional experience: management/sales/mobility
  • Completed basic or further education: Completed vocational apprenticeship (technology) and further education in sales or business administration is desired
  • Business know-how and leadership skills
  • Sufficient equity capital consisting of working capital (inventory, stock money, reserve) CHF 175'000.- and bank guarantee of CHF 25,000.-
  • Entrepreneurially thinking personality with a great sales flair
  • Demonstrated experience in managing employees
  • High willingness to provide services and above average
  • High sense of responsibility regarding the course of business, cost development, employees and customers
  • Team-oriented, open and flexible thinking and acting
  • Readiness for continuing education and training
  • Proof of basic automotive training for the workshop sector
  • Experience in the filling station business and in the workshop sector is advantageous
  • in German (negotiation certain)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Successful franchising system: existing stable operation under the Migrol brand, with operating concept for Auto Service and Car Wash, running an independent shop and the Migrol filling station
  • Reliable partnership: permanent training opportunities for you in the field of car wash and safety
  • Experienced services: Competent expert advice and support in achieving our common goals
