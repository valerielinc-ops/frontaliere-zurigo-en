Full description

Have you ever wondered how your skills and unique background can affect the world of tomorrow? Become part of our supply management teams and use the talent and passion that makes you.

Supply Manager (80-100%)

Männedorf, Zurich

Your contribution

As a supply manager at Tecan, you are a central part of our supply management team and ensure that our material supply runs smoothly. You optimize availability, quality and costs, develop our supplier relationships and contribute your ideas for efficient processes. With your commitment, you help us achieve our ambitious departmental and corporate goals – and inspire our customers worldwide.

  • Independent procurement and support of suppliers and product lines within the framework of global and local procurement and quality policy
  • Optimization of material availability, storage handling, quality and costs as well as continuous development of procurement and logistics processes
  • Support in negotiating and concluding contracts with suppliers as well as actively developing supplier relationships
  • Coordination and communication with internal and external interfaces, including processing of non-conformity reports and corrective actions
  • Maintenance and collection of procurement master data in the ERP system (SAP R3/S4) as well as implementation of change requests and article entries
  • Participation in internal projects.

Your profile

We are not just looking for expertise: we are looking for people who can put their passion and talent to work with us.

  • Technical or commercial training completed
  • Ideally, a further development as federal dipl. Buyer:in or similar
  • At least 2–3 years of experience in operational or strategic procurement
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • Experience with SAP R3/S4 and Excel
  • You are familiar with the national and international supplier market
  • You are confident in negotiations, efficient, practical and self-confident
  • Resilience, team spirit, communication strength and social competence distinguish you
  • You are innovative, goal-oriented, reliable and think entrepreneurial
  • Flexibility, willingness to learn and openness to new things round off your profile
