Full description

Do you love bringing cocktail shakers to life and supporting the bar team? We’re looking for an enthusiastic and experienced Bar Shift Leader who will delight our guests with a blend of creativity and excellent service.

Is this you?

  • Completed vocational training or hotel management school, or at least 1 year of practical work experience in the same or a related industry
  • You have a structured approach to organization and enjoy communicating in German and English; handling complex guest correspondence is essential. You creatively address guest issues and implement new ideas with great interest and a keen sense of guest needs.
  • A passion for high-quality beverages and excellent service
  • Creativity and the ability to set new trends
  • Our team is extremely innovative and eager to experiment. If you also enjoy new things and challenges and try out new ideas with curiosity and drive, you’ll be a perfect fit for our team

Your Mission

  • Responsible for ensuring smooth service operations, guest care, and cash register reconciliation during your shift
  • Inspire and motivate the team to perform at their best
  • Enforce and meticulously uphold the hotel’s internal standards for guest care
  • Create innovative and enticing drinks
  • Ensure an unforgettable stay for our guests

Why join us

  • A dynamic and young team of more than 200 bright minds who never forget to have fun at work
  • In-house training
  • An open and transparent corporate culture that centers on the values of a passion for excellence, guest-centricity, and a spirit of innovation.
  • Fair and accurate tracking of working hours
  • Solid benefits from an international hotel chain, such as employee rates at 10,000+ hotels worldwide, and much more. Enjoyment at work
  • Room for creativity and personal development
  • A permanent position that requires independence and personal responsibility
  • You can expect a versatile role in a very lively and structured environment, a progressive and dynamic employee policy, and the benefits of an international hotel group.
  • If you’re ready to turn your passion for drinks and team spirit into an exciting career, then send us your application! We look forward to getting to know you and conquering the world of cocktails together. Cheers!

IMPORTANT Language skills: Fluent English and minimum B2 in German Related Work Experience: 1 year related work experience. Working in Switzerland: B or C permit or EU nationality ONLY
