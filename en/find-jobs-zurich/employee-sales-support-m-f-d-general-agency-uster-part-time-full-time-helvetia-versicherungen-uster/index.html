Customer Consulting – Sales

Pensum: 50 - 100% Workplace: Experience Level: With Professional Experience FlexOffice: On-site

Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, this means much more than simply completing tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high standards, and bring the courage to break new ground. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand, and your ideas, your initiative, and your contribution count. This gives you the opportunity to truly make a difference for our customers, your team, and your personal development.

What you achieve

  • You actively support the field sales force throughout the entire sales process and thus contribute to our shared success.
  • As a central point of contact, you answer professional and technical questions in the areas of Non-Life Private Customers, Non-Life Corporate Customers, Individual Life, Accident, Health, and Occupational Benefits (BVG).
  • You look after our private and corporate customers by phone, in writing, and in person with great competence and commitment.
  • With a feel for individual needs, you develop tailor-made concepts and solutions.
  • Together with the team, you actively shape market development and sales promotion.
  • You accompany our apprentices in their daily professional routine and specifically promote their professional and personal development.

What you bring

  • Commercial insurance training and/or several years of experience with an all-lines insurer.
  • You already hold the VBV certificate (all-lines) or are willing to obtain it.
  • Pleasure in direct customer contact and a pronounced service orientation.
  • Sales-oriented thinking and enthusiasm for the development of individual customer solutions.
  • Efficient, solution-oriented cooperation with the field service and relevant specialist departments.
