Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With over 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric services offered by Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in outpatient, day clinic and inpatient settings. The group employs around 1800 people and generated sales of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For the acute care unit S2 with a focus on psychosis, we are looking for a

The acute care unit S2 is aimed at people in exceptional psychiatric situations who are in need of immediate help. We offer professional support to these people. We provide help in the acute phase. This includes emergency psychiatric care with careful diagnosis, appropriate treatment and a rapid clarification of the social situation. The goals of the treatment are the reconstruction of everyday skills, the development of possible coping strategies and the stabilization of self-esteem. Depending on the focus of treatment, the ward offers various specific therapies and interventions (e.g. voice interview, IPT, etc.). As part of the Recovery model, a peer employee is also an integral part of the treatment team. The ward is usually openly run and implements the Safewards model. We would be happy if you would also participate with your ideas.

Your tasks Flexibility and enjoyment of variety are central points. Because that's exactly what working in acute psychiatry means. If this applies to you, here are some of the most important tasks:

  • Individual care and support for patients at night
  • Leading crisis talks and reports
  • Consultation and planning of treatment with the responsible doctors and therapists
  • For better integration in the team and to ensure processes, it may be that part of the introduction is planned in the day shift

Your profile Professional competence, service orientation and a lot of humor are the most important qualities you need to have. With these qualities, we achieve the best treatment results for our patients and the team is happy that you will become a part of them. If you have experience in nursing, but not in the field of psychiatry, it is still possible to join. It is then crucial that you have a lot of motivation and commitment to acquire the specific skills. As part of our wide range of internal courses and the generous participation in further education and training, you can get to know the various therapy methods and then apply them yourself. Of course, you will also be supported by your new colleagues.
