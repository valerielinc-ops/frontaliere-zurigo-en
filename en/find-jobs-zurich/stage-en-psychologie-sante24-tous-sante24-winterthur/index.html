Full description

santé24 is the telemedicine company of SWICA Health Insurance. For the implementation and further development of the online self-help training offer, we are looking for a committed intern. Since April 2020, santé24 SWICA offers scientifically tested online self-help training. Persons who need psychological help can deal with their complaints in training regardless of time and location and are personally accompanied by psychological coaches through written feedback. Help to expand this innovative offering to meet the need for alternative treatment options for people with mental health conditions.

As an intern, we offer you, among other things

  • Close cooperation in the team of online self-help trainings and exchange with the staff of the psychological-psychiatric consultation
  • Insight into an innovative psychological/psychotherapeutic field of work and practice-oriented application of knowledge from study
  • Comprehensive insight into telemedical triage, counselling and treatment
  • Obligation to attend Monday, Wednesday morning and Friday, otherwise flexible working hours under the guarantee of joint meeting hours and that within two days (excluding weekends and public holidays) feedback on edited lessons can be written
  • Home office according to agreement

This is how you shape health

  • Co-selection and inclusion of SWICA insured persons in various online self-help trainings (onboarding including test psychological diagnosis and telephone anamnesis discussions)
  • Personal support of the participants in the form of semi-structured feedbacks on the edited lessons, chat communication, active contact with non-adherent behavior and completion of the training. Your main task: Writing appreciative, motivating feedback on the individual progress of the participants
  • Cooperation in the further development of the offer

This sets you apart

  • Study of psychology for several semesters (advanced study)
  • Enjoying the written and telephone relationship with the participants of the trainings
  • Affinity and professional curiosity for distance counselling (online therapy, telemedicine)
  • High written language competence, i.e. a stylish, addressee-friendly and impeccable written expression
  • Good communication skills in German and English
  • French, Italian or other languages are beneficial
  • Responsible, team capable personality with high quality and service thinking as well as good time management

Why SWICA

  • We build on humanity. That is why we focus on openness, respect and trust in our everyday work. We tackle this, take responsibility and think in terms of solutions – together, for our customers. This is also why we have been number 1 in customer satisfaction for many years.
  • We trust you and give you space. Annual working hours, at least 5 weeks of holidays, part-time models and generous home office within the scope of the possibilities of the job – you make your everyday life largely flexible.
  • We value and reward your work with a fair market wage.
  • Because health is everything. With our company health management and region-specific offers, we take care of your well-being – so that you are healthy and energized.
  • We think long term. With three savings plans, pensum-dependent coordination deduction and benefits well above the legal minimum, you ensure good for your future.
