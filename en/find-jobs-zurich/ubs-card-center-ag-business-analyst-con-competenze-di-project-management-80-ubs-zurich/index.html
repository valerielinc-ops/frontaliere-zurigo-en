Full description

Key responsibilities

  • You will accompany and (co-)lead strategic and operational projects with a focus on increasing efficiency, production control and process improvements in the Operations department
  • You advance automation and digitalization projects and optimize IT-supported processes together with specialist departments and IT
  • You will use data and reports to support management decisions, develop meaningful analysis and take on a superuser role for reporting tools in the Operations department
  • You work closely with other operations units as an operation interface and support the business needs with data and analysis
  • You identify potential for improvement in processes and systems and implement measures in close cooperation with management to achieve defined goals
  • You plan and control your projects and work packages, manage milestones, risks, dependencies and stakeholder communication and keep track of priorities in a dynamic environment.

The team As an Operations Business Analyst (m/f/d) with project management focus, 80–100% in the Operational Risk & Functional Management group, you will drive the optimization of our operations in the UBS Card Center, from production control to automation to data-based management decisions. You work closely with Operations Management as well as Sales, Product, Risk, Compliance and Technology, contributing directly to the efficiency and quality of our credit card business.

Your skills and experience

Must criteria

  • Completed degree in business informatics, business administration or a comparable technical/quantitative discipline
  • Several years of professional experience as a business analyst or in a similar role with a focus on processes, data and reporting
  • Experience in dealing with databases, data analysis and reporting, and working with data sets to support decisions
  • Good knowledge of Tableau or Power BI as well as SQL as Confluence and Jira
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving thinking: You can structure complex, sometimes still unclear questions and translate them into actionable insights and requirements
  • Collaboration & Working Style: You work effectively with cross-functional teams and stakeholders at all levels, build trusting relationships and stay focused, flexible and constructive in a dynamic environment, even with multiple parallel tasks and priorities
  • Flowing knowledge of German and good knowledge of English in words and writing

May criteria (nice-to-have)

  • Experience in operational environments (e.g. payment transactions, card processes, back office control)
  • Experience in the introduction or further development of production control tools (e.g. capacity planning, monitoring, KPI dashboards)
  • Sound project management experience, ideally in interdisciplinary projects
  • Visionary mindset and passion for innovation and modern technologies – especially for the use of AI and GenAI in the data and workflow context
  • Curious about how AI can help build, deliver, and optimize workflows – combined with healthy judgment, meaning you validate results and ensure they are consistent with policies, risk standards, and principles of ethical use
  • Interested in digitalization, automation and process management as well as working at the interface between business and IT
