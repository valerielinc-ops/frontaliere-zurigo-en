Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We advise individuals and companies about money, manage assets and offer attractive financial services. As we are growing strongly, we are looking for reinforcements at our headquarters at Paradeplatz in Zurich.

  • You carry out the monthly wage processing of our customers incl. administration and billing.
  • You take over the deputy from Head Payroll Services in their absence and support projects.
  • You will assist the team and customers as an expert in questions regarding payroll, social insurance and employment law.
  • You conduct monthly and year-end accounts in all areas of social security and taxes.
  • You take responsibility for new projects and the takeover of customers.
  • You contribute to further development and process optimization, especially through digitalization initiatives.
  • Swiss Federal HR or social security certificate, further education in the field of social security and employment law are advantageous
  • In-depth experience in payroll
  • Desire to develop the area and take responsibility
  • Knowledge thirst and customer orientation
  • Good IT knowledge and initial experience with Abacus
  • Stylistic confident in German and English, good knowledge of French is a plus
  • We are proud of our culture, which focuses on an open exchange: your opinion counts, feedback is desired, and you can participate in the development of our unique company.
  • This position is also open for part-time work, so that there is time for further education, family, sports and projects that are important to you.
  • Surveys on satisfaction regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
