Application process

  • You develop and expand applications in interaction with databases and interfaces.
  • You will learn what is important with understandable code, quality, security and good maintainability
  • You get insights into quality assurance and tests so that applications work reliably and technical requirements can be verified in a traceable manner
  • You will learn about existing systems and older technologies and understand why modern IT not only develops new things, but also keeps existing applications stable and gradually developed.
  • In agile projects, you work together with other trainees and dual students on creative solutions.

  • You can develop with us.
  • Together with you, we shape your development path and prepare you for a wide range of applications in IT.
