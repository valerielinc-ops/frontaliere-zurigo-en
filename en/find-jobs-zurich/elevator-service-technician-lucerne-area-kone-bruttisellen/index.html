KONE (Switzerland) Ltd. is represented by 5 offices and over 400 employees. Our corporate culture is based on the values of #CARE, #COLLABORATION, #CUSTOMER and #COURAGE - values that we live every day to create an inclusive, sustainable and innovative working environment.

We are looking for you as a Service Technician Elevators – Lucerne Area as soon as possible.

Your mission

After careful training, you will take over your own service route and be responsible for the independent and high-quality maintenance of our elevator systems.

Your concrete contribution

  • Carrying out maintenance work, service calls and repairs according to specified quality and safety standards
  • Ensuring a safe working environment in accordance with all regulations and guidelines
  • Maintaining a professional exchange with customers, contact persons and end users
  • Responsibility for the safety and proper functioning of the assigned equipment
  • Proper management of the site environment during work
  • Identification and reporting of additional repair needs, including coordination with customers on site
  • Generate accurate, timely service reports
  • Participation in the periodic on-call service / fault service

Your added value

  • Completed training in the electrical or mechanical field
  • Electrical and control experience
  • Strong manual skills and independent way of working
  • Very good (Swiss) knowledge of German (at least level C1)
  • Enjoyment of customer contact and willingness to represent KONE professionally to the outside world
  • Valid category B driving licence
  • Impeccable criminal record extract
  • Residence in Switzerland (Swiss or foreign citizenship with a valid residence permit)
  • Your place of residence is no more than 30 minutes from Lucerne city centre. This enables a good work-life balance and facilitates team collaboration.

Our offer for your future

  • Working in a globally networked company with international cooperation
  • Innovative, professional and collegial working environment – with a focus on the well-being of employees
  • A wide range of further training opportunities (e.g. KONE Academy in Hanover) and active support of your professional development
  • Market-driven remuneration, a company car with 100% private use (CHF 150/month), meal allowance CHF 25/working day (without receipt), at least 25 days of vacation as well as other attractive benefits such as a high-performance BVG and participation in exciting company events
  • Fair Pay certified: equal pay for women and men – transparent and audited
  • Commitment to sustainability: Goal of CO₂ neutrality by 2030
