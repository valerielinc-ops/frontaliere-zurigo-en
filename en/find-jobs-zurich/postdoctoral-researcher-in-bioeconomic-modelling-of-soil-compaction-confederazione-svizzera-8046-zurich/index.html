Full description

Evaluation and compilation of data on the agronomic and economic impacts of soil compaction, including costs and benefits of prevention measures

  • Develop and implement a bioeconomic model to evaluate strategies to prevent soil compaction
  • Simulate the impact of soil compaction on plant growth and important ecosystem functions
  • Analyze the impact of soil compaction – and its prevention – on farm profitability
  • Conduct cost-benefit analyses of measures for the reduction and prevention of soil compaction
  • Publication of the results in peer-reviewed publications and in presentations at international scientific conferences
  • PhD in soil science, agricultural economics, environmental sciences or a similar field
  • Experience in applying process-based models and programming
  • Knowledge of soil physics, agronomy and agricultural economics
  • Interest in farm management and decision-making processes in agricultural holdings Strong analytical skills, ability to formulate sound hypotheses, safe handling of uncertain or incomplete data, and experience in interdisciplinary research and collaboration Knowledge of two official languages and English
