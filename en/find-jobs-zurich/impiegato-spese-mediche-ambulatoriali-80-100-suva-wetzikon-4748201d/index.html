Full description

You like to be the connecting body, keep an overview and clarify open questions between medicine, insurance and service providers? Then you are right with us. What you do

  • You check outpatient cost languages and medical costs calculations for their medical causality and ensure compliance with the applicable tariff basics.
  • You work closely with our in-house medical specialists as well as with doctors, hospitals and social insurance companies and carry out additional administrative and medical investigations if necessary.
  • You assess and control invoices, release them or reject them in a comprehensible and justified way.
  • You will advise and support our internal specialist offices and service providers on tariff and case-independent questions.
  • You process inquiries from social insurance companies, for example in connection with recoveries. What you bring
  • You have completed paramedical training (EFZ), e.g. as a medical assistant/practice assistant, specialist/health care professional, pharmacist:in or pharmaceutical assistant:in. Otherwise, you will bring a commercial education (EFZ) with experience in the processing of personal insurance (UVG/KVG).
  • You are familiar with tariff systems and enjoy exchanging ideas with different specialist bodies and stakeholders.
  • Several years of professional experience in health or accident insurance is an advantage.
  • You communicate stylishly in German. Good oral French or Italian skills are a plus.
  • You work independently and convince with your assertiveness and your solution-oriented nature.
