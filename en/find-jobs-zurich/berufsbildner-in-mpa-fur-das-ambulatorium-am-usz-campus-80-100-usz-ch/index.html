In our interdisciplinary ambulatory, we combine the fields of cardiology, vascular surgery, immunology, nephrology, traumatology, visceral surgery, plastic surgery and thoracic surgery. We are happy to introduce you to this function and of course also to the various specialist areas. • We are a warm and colourful 40-strong team that appreciates both self-employment and teamwork for our exciting work. Our working hours are between Monday and Friday from 7.30 a.m. to 5.30 p.m.
