You are an organizational talent, keep an overview even in hectic situations and stand for a high level of patient and customer orientation? Do you like working in a team and taking on responsibility? Then you are exactly right with us.

Their main tasks

  • Taking over a competent contact and information point of the dialysis department with a high level of customer orientation
  • Assuming a connecting function between care, logistics, housekeeping, cleaning and medical-therapeutic services
  • Execution of the secretarial tasks according to department- and clinic-specific specifications (coordination and organization of patient appointments, receipt of telephone calls, ordering of laundry, food, medicines and consumables, etc.)
  • Responsibility for the appearance of the department
  • Maintenance and maintenance of various medical devices
  • Storage management incl. Controlling maintenance and repair work
  • Competent support for nurses

Your profile

  • You are an MPA or FAGE with professional experience in an acute hospital
  • They enjoy working together as well as forming and maintaining relationships
  • Your strengths include a safe and well-kept appearance, resilience, flexibility and initiative
  • You keep an overview in a lively hospital operation, know how to deal with hectic situations and communicate calmly and firmly
  • Customer orientation is your priority
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German is a prerequisite
