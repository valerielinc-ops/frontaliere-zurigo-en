Full description

Eraneos is an international strategy, transformation and technology advisory group that helps companies thrive in a dynamic digital world. With first-class experts from business and technology, we empower our customers to set new standards for sustainable transformations – from strategy to implementation. Whether in the design of future-proof organizations, the development of new potential through data and AI or the protection of companies through holistic cybersecurity: We deliver results pragmatically and purposefully. Our team of around 1,300 dedicated employees is represented in offices in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, Portugal, Denmark, Sweden, Austria, the UK, Singapore and the USA and combines global perspectives with strong local roots. Around 450 employees work at the Swiss locations in Zurich, Bern and Lausanne.

This is the job - your main tasks

  • Conception and further development of solution and overall architectures at the levels of data, application and technology architecture
  • Design of cloud and data platform architectures in the public cloud environment, especially based on Microsoft Azure and Microsoft Fabric
  • Design of microservice, integration and event-based architectures as well as interfaces with JSON and XML
  • Documentation of architectures using common methods and standards such as UML, arc42, ArchiMate or comparable templates
  • Consulting and coordination with customers, project teams and management as well as moderation of architectural decisions in agile delivery setups
  • Collaborating with DevOps and engineering teams to deploy container platforms, Kubernetes, Platform-as-a-Service and advanced operational models
  • Consideration of security, data protection and governance requirements as well as support for complex integration projects, in particular in the public or Federal environment

This job is for you if

  • you have completed a computer science degree at a tertiary level, e.g. ETH/Uni, FH, Bachelor, Master or a comparable education, and are also certified as a Certified Professional for Software Architecture, e.g. with iSAQB or a comparable qualification.
  • you have several years of experience as a solution, software or company architect and have already successfully designed and documented complex architectures.
  • you have in-depth knowledge in data analytics, DWH, data mesh and data platforms and are also confident in the field of data science and have already used Spark in practice.
  • you have several years of experience in the public cloud environment, in particular with Microsoft Azure, ideally also with Microsoft Fabric, and additionally have a certification as Azure Solutions Architect Expert or a comparable qualification.
  • you have practical experience with microservices, event-based architectures, interface design and container platforms, especially with Kubernetes, and working with DevOps teams goes without saying.
  • you have experience with agile process models such as SCRUM and/or SAFe and are characterized by strong communication, negotiation and customer-oriented cooperation.
  • you have very good knowledge of German as a primary working language and good knowledge of English and ideally already have experience in integration projects in the federal environment as well as knowledge of cyber security and data protection investigations.

As an i-top you bring with you

  • Ideally a TOGAF certification
  • Certification in HERMES Advanced 2022
