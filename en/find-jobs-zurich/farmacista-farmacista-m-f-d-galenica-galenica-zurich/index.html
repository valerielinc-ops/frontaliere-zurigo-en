Full description

Location: Coop Vitality Letzipark, Shopping Centre Letzipark, Baslerstrasse 50, 8048 Zurich

Do you not only want to advise pharmaceuticals, but also take responsibility and actively shape everyday pharmacy life? At Coop Vitality Letzipark, you combine professional competence with personal customer proximity and at the same time assume an important role in the team. As a deputy to the management, you are actively involved, taking on responsibility and supporting you in shaping everyday pharmacy life together.

You can do that.

  • You represent the management and are an important link to the employees
  • You do everything to ensure that the customers are in good hands – and happy to come back
  • You train the learners and accompany the students and pharmacists in the FPH training
  • Together with the team you provide the services of the pharmacy and expand them as needed
  • Nothing escapes your watchful eyes: You make sure that the quality is right at all times
  • When recruiting new employees, you actively help

You bring that with you

  • Completed pharmacy studies with BAB. You have already completed your FPH, are currently on your way there or would like to take this next step
  • You are happy to take on responsibility and can imagine supporting the management in everyday pharmacy life
  • Working together in a team is important to you and you are committed to a good working environment
  • You work independently, keep an overview and get involved where you need it
  • Enjoy further developing the pharmacy with your ideas
  • German at least level B2

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday
  • Pension fund with advantageous premium distribution - approx. 2/3 are taken over by us
  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and at other top brands incl. Coop Personal Discount Card
  • Expand competences through funded and supported training and internal development opportunities
  • Family friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of wages) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of wages), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Registered shares at preferential prices - not only engage professionally in Galenica, but also invest

Benefits may vary slightly depending on area/place

About us

A powerful joint venture At Coop Vitality, Coop and Galenica work together. Coop Vitality is part of the Galenica network and combines the strength of two partners to offer customers a modern pharmacy experience with a high level of expertise. The network’s pharmacies are usually located in or directly next to larger Coop branches – especially in the German-speaking part of Switzerland. The range includes a wide range of products for health, beauty and disease prevention, complemented by expert advice and pharmaceutical services. At Coop Vitality, you can expect an extraordinary customer orientation. This in the points of sale as well as in the online shop.
