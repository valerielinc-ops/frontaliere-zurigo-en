Full description

More than a job

As part of our operational team, you are largely responsible for ensuring that everything arrives at the right place at the right time and that our customers feel that they are in good hands. With your commitment, you keep supply chains running and find the right solution for every challenge. Whether medicines, toys or important machine parts – thanks to you, products reach people all over the world. This not only brings goods to their destination, but also enables the small and big moments in life. Your contribution to Kühne+Nagel has more impact than you can imagine.

How you get involved

  • End-to-end support for our customers along the entire air freight transport chain
  • Control and coordination of export shipments, taking into account service, quality and deadline specifications
  • Planning and scheduling of transports and ensuring a smooth operation Process
  • Proactive shipment monitoring and transparent communication with customers and partners
  • Creation and review of relevant transport and customs documents.
  • Close collaboration with airlines, trucking companies and internal stakeholders
  • Continuous monitoring of KPIs and implementation of optimization measures

What sets you apart

  • Completed training in freight forwarding and logistics or comparable qualification
  • Professional experience in the area of air freight and dangerous goods certificate is an advantage
  • Skillful use of MS Office
  • Excellent customer and service orientation as well as strong communication skills
  • Organizational talent, team spirit and flexibility
  • Fluent German and good written and spoken English skills

What's in it for you

  • Performance-related compensation package including 13. Monthly salary
  • Extensive training and development opportunities
  • Exclusive discounts at numerous partner companies
  • Employee recruitment program with attractive bonuses
  • Regular team and company events
  • Modernly equipped office space with ideal public transport connections

Who we are

Logistics touches every aspect of our lives - from the goods we consume every day to the healthcare we rely on. With us, your work goes beyond logistics: it enables the small and big moments of life for people all over the world.

As a leading global logistics company with the vision to further develop our world, we offer you a safe and stable environment. This is where your work makes a real difference. Whether it's delivering food, developing sustainable transport solutions or social engagement - your work has more impact than you can imagine.
