Original description

Two Spice – that’s us! A creative and innovative gastronomy and lifestyle company from Zurich. Since 1990 we bring fresh ideas to the table and develop unique concepts and brands that inspire. Our versatile companies are at home at the central hotspots of various cities and stand for quality, design and pure pleasure moments. Welcome to Nooba, inspired by the vibrant streets of Asia and serving a diverse pan Asian cuisine. Our dishes are a fusion of the traditional flavours of Thailand, Japan and India, coupled with urban flair. Immerse yourself in the world of Nooba and become part of a team that redefined the boundaries of Asian cuisine with passion and creativity. Do you want to be part of our team? Now is your chance!
