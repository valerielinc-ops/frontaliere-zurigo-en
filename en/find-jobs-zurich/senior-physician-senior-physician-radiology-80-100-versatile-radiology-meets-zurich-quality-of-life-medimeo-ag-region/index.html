Who says you have to choose between versatile radiology, your career goals, and high quality of life? In the Zurich Oberland you can expect a broad radiology with space for your own focal points, a collegial team and a lake right on your doorstep. Zurich is also just a stone’s throw away! Her future employer is an established acute care hospital in the Zurich Oberland, which ensures comprehensive medical care for the regional population. Radiology carries out around 47'000 examinations annually and covers a broad diagnostic spectrum with CT, MRI, ultrasound, mammography, conventional X-ray and fluoroscopy. You work in a dedicated team with short communication paths and close interdisciplinary exchange. In addition to the broad radiological activity, the department offers you the opportunity to contribute your own professional interests and develop them in a targeted manner. The location is also convincing: the combination of the high quality of life of the Zurich Oberland with the proximity to the city of Zurich. A lake on the doorstep, diverse leisure opportunities and attractive residential areas make the region particularly interesting for doctors who want to combine work and private life well.

Their tasks

  • They independently take over the radiological diagnostics with a versatile examination spectrum, in particular in CT, MRI, ultrasound and conventional X-ray.
  • You contribute your professional interests and have the opportunity to specialise further in musculoskeletal radiology, for example.
  • In regular exchange with the clinical departments, you support diagnostic and therapeutic decisions and participate in interdisciplinary meetings
  • They accompany assistant doctors in their professional development and pass on their knowledge in everyday clinical life.
  • They participate in the radiological emergency service and contribute to the reliable care of patients

Your Competences

  • You have the specialist title radiology, German or EU-approved (we support you in the recognition in Switzerland)
  • You bring knowledge of sectional image diagnostics and interest in continuously expanding your radiology skills
  • They value independent work, like to take on responsibility and attach importance to a careful quality of findings
  • You are a collegial, communicative personality and enjoy working with various medical specialties

Their advantages

  • Variety in everyday work: Around 47'000 examinations annually enable you to perform a versatile job with a wide variety of diagnostic questions
  • Space for your interests: further develop your personal focus and actively shape your professional future
  • Targeted promotion: Your professional development is supported by individual further education and training opportunities
  • City and nature in the immediate vicinity: Zurich is easily accessible, while the Greifensee and the Zurich Oberland offer numerous opportunities for sport, leisure and recreation
  • Appropriate workload: Employee between 80 and 100%, with employment starting from January 2027 or by agreement
