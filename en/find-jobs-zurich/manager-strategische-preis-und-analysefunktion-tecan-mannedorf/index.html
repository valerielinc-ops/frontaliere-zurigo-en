Full description

Ever wondered how your skills and unique background could impact the world of tomorrow?

Tecan is a pioneer and a globally leading company in laboratory automation and a healthcare enabler. The company is improving people’s lives and health by empowering customers to scale healthcare innovation globally. Tecan enables customers to scale their innovation, whether in laboratory workflows for life science research, various applied markets and diagnostics or in product development and manufacturing for the medical market. Tecan delivers this broad range of healthcare products and services directly to end users or with OEM partners.

Are you ready to drive strategic impact on a global scale? As our Strategic Pricing & Analytics Manager, you’ll be at the forefront of our transformation toward value-based pricing, shaping the way we define and measure commercial success. Join our Global Commercial Excellence team and become the champion for pricing innovation and commercial metrics—partnering with Sales, Marketing, Finance, and Product teams to embed best practices and ensure transparency across the organization. If you’re passionate about analytics, collaboration, and making a difference, this is your opportunity to lead change and elevate our commercial performance worldwide!

Manager Strategic Pricing & Analytics 100%

Männedorf, Zurich

Your contribution Join our Global Commercial Excellence team as a Strategic Pricing & Analytics Manager! In this pivotal role you will:

  • Drive with the design, deployment, and continuous improvement of value-based pricing approaches across products, markets, and customer segments.
  • Support new product development and launches with pricing analyses, price recommendations, and business case assessments
  • Partner with Finance and Commercial teams to define a new global pricing policy and governance
  • Build analytical models and methodologies to support value-based price setting, price differentiation, and price positioning
  • Define and standardize metrics, with focus on pricing, such as price realization, discount rate, deal margin, and value capture indicators
  • Design and maintain pricing analytics dashboards, tools, and reporting that enable pricing execution and monitoring
  • Drive and own the global annual price adjustments process and coordinate its implementation

Your background

We’re not just looking for hard skills: We’re looking for people who bring their passion and talent to work. The position requires the following:

Hard / Technical Skills

  • BA / MBA preferably in STEM
  • Min 5 years of experience in pricing management and commercial analytics
  • Strong understanding of pricing and commercial metrics
  • Understanding of value-based pricing principles and value capture mechanisms in B2B environments
  • Demonstrated experience designing dashboards and reports that support pricing and commercial decisions (e.g., in PowerBI or Tableau)
  • Ability to build and interpret pricing models, scenario analyses, and what-if simulations
  • Proficiency in analytical tools such as Excel (advanced), SQL, Python/R, or BI tools (e.g. Power BI, Tableau)
  • Excellent level of English – any additional languages are of added value
  • Passion for life sciences / healthcare topics

Soft Skills

  • Ability to communicate insights clearly and effectively to stakeholders across all levels
  • Strong team player with strong oral & written communication skills
  • Proactive & takes initiative to get things done
  • Can-do mindset
  • Well organized and able to structure fast moving projects
  • Takes accountability for own work and is firmly focused on results
  • Eager to learn, explore new ideas & opportunities on a day-to-day basis
