Full description

For Debattierhaus Karl der Grosse, we are looking for a motivated individual for a 12-month fixed term starting January 1, 2027, or by agreement.

Responsibilities

  • Planning and coordination of formats on various socio-political topics as part of the events team
  • Research into current topics, development of program ideas, and participation in booking speakers and guests
  • Supervision of cooperation partners as well as support in the planning, implementation, and further development of their events
  • Support for those responsible for partnerships and programming with administrative tasks, as well as taking responsibility for subprojects
  • Host at evening events and ensuring a smooth running of events as well as the well-being of guests
  • Participation in the implementation of digital and analogue communication measures as well as editorial work as a liaison with the communications team

Profile

  • A degree from a university of applied sciences or university in social, political or cultural sciences, cultural management or a comparable field (e.g. political science, social work, Popular Cultures, Trends & Identity) – or currently pursuing one
  • A feel for political, social and (socio-)cultural trends and discourses, as well as an interest in networking within the Zurich scene
  • Service-oriented, independent and structured way of working
  • Very good German language skills, spoken and written (approx. C1)
  • Willingness to work evening shifts, which we plan well in advance
  • Interest in and curiosity about working with new and social media

What we offer

  • A varied role with room for your own ideas, responsibility and learning, as well as the opportunity to actively help shape things and try out new things
  • A committed team, open and transparent communication, and a working environment built on trust and collaboration
  • In-depth insight into the programming work of a municipal sociocultural venue and the opportunity to expand your own network
  • Drawing on the City of Zürich's equality plan, at Debattierhaus Karl der Grosse we are committed to a work culture in which all people are treated equally and have equal opportunities regardless of gender, origin, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity or other personal characteristics. Inclusion and diversity are important to us. We therefore particularly encourage people from socially underrepresented groups to apply, including, for example, people with visible and invisible disabilities, people with experience of racism or migration, as well as people with diverse lived realities and backgrounds. We welcome diverse perspectives and skills and, as a matter of course, consider all qualified applications.

About us

With around 2400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zürich works every day for a socially minded Zürich. We support children, young people and adults in various life situations. In doing so, we make an important contribution to social cohesion and a high quality of life in the neighborhoods.

Organizationally, Debattierhaus Karl der Grosse belongs to the Social Services of the City of Zürich and has established itself as a municipal sociocultural meeting place for the population.
