Introduction

Our extended weekend shifts have a day shift from 6.00 to 6.00 p.m. and a night shift from 6.00 to 6.00 p.m.. No shift changes are planned and you work either permanently in the day or night shift on Saturdays and Sundays. The use-free weekends are pre-defined annually by Belimo

Main responsibilities

  • Transport-friendly packaging of the products
  • Adherence to assembly and process processes

Skills required

  • Self-employed and quality-conscious working methods
  • Experience in manufacturing/assembly of advantage
  • Good knowledge of German

About Belimo

Belimo is the world leader in the development, production and distribution of field equipment for energy-efficient control of heating, ventilation and air conditioning systems. Valve drives, control valves, sensors and counters form our core business. Founded in 1975, Belimo has been listed on the Swiss Stock Exchange (SIX) since 1995.
