Full description

As a High Voltage Test Engineer, you take over the management of important on-site tests and ensure compliance with safety and quality standards. You coordinate with customers and internal teams, plan test procedures and create precise technical documentation. This role gives you the chance to take responsibility, travel a lot and represent the company in technically demanding discussions.

You can do that.

  • Conduct of high voltage tests and partial discharge measurements in cooperation with customer representatives
  • Monitoring and reporting of deviations from specifications and ensuring timely correction
  • Ensure on-site work meets safety regulations and quality standards
  • Planning and coordination of test activities, including on-site acceptance of high voltage tests
  • Representation of the company at meetings on site and in technical discussions with customers and internal

Stakeholders

  • Preparation of the necessary documentation such as final reports, minutes and deviation reports

You bring that with you

  • In-depth knowledge of high-voltage technology is absolutely necessary
  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering and/or Electronics
  • Strong sense of responsibility and ability to work independently, structured and organized
  • Fluent in German and English, with excellent communication skills in word and writing
  • Readiness for extensive travel and flexible working hours
  • Decision-making and ability to manage technical clarifications independently
  • Sound understanding of safety standards and ability to intervene in uncertain conditions
  • Requires the development of digital and data literacy as well as the practical application of digital tools and data to support customer interactions and service execution.
