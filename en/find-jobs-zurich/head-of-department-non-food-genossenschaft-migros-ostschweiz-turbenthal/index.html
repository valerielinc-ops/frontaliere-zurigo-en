Full description

Do you have a feeling for high-quality products and are enthusiastic about the variety of our range in the field of non-food, such as dishes, laundry detergents and much more? You are sales-strong 💪 and like to communicate safely with our customers? 😊 Then you are exactly right with us! ✅ Let us be «tämä success». 🤝🧡

Mansioni

  • You take on the professional responsibility for the non-food sector 🍳🎁 and ensure that everything runs smoothly.
  • You take care of sales promotion. 📅🤝 With your organizational talent you ensure a smooth process.
  • Revenue, cost, inventory and inventory management 📈📦 are among your core tasks – you always have the overview and control specifically.
  • Ordering, incoming goods checks, presentation and disposition 📋🚛✨ are in your hands – you make sure that everything runs perfectly.
  • Friendly and competent customer service and advice 😊🛍️ are a matter of course for you and you will advise the customers with your expertise.

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: in the retail trade, household sector
  • Completed basic or further training: in the retail trade, household sector
  • You bring very good expertise in the field of Non Food 🍳🎁.
  • Reliability, flexibility and a high level of commitment 💪⏰ distinguish you – you are always there when you are needed.
  • With your independent work and your friendly approach 😊🤝 you create a pleasant atmosphere for customers and team.
  • You are ready to continue your education 📚🚀 and always stay up to date.
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Occupational pension provision: Attractive pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Cumulus Points: Multiple Cumulus Points on food and nonfood items
