Full description

It's more than a job

When you work in Freight Logistics and Operations at Kuehne+Nagel, you play a key role in optimising processes and ensuring that inventory and shipments move efficiently. But that is not all. Your work also helps ensure that vital medicines reach patients on time and supports other essential deliveries, from wind turbines to semiconductors. At Kuehne+Nagel, our contribution counts in more ways than we imagine.

‎
Join our Sea Logistics team as working student or graduate !

How you create impact

  • Support the accurate and timely processing of customer invoicing and revenue-related activities within Sea Logistics.
  • Review shipment files and financial data to ensure correct cost and revenue allocation.
  • Assist in identifying and resolving billing discrepancies in collaboration with operational teams, customers, and internal stakeholders.
  • Monitor revenue-related KPIs and contribute to maintaining data quality and financial accuracy.
  • Support month-end closing activities, reporting tasks, and revenue assurance processes.
  • Contribute to process improvement initiatives aimed at increasing efficiency, transparency, and profitability.

What we would like you to bring

  • Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's program, or recently graduated in Business Administration, Logistics, Supply Chain Management, Finance, or a related field.
  • Strong interest in logistics, transportation, finance, and commercial processes.
  • Good analytical skills with an affinity for working with numbers and data.
  • Proficiency in MS Office applications, particularly Excel; willingness to learn new systems and digital tools.
  • Detail-oriented, organized, and able to work accurately in a fast-paced environment.
  • Good communication skills and a proactive, solution-oriented mindset.
  • Fluent in English, both written and spoken; additional language skills in French, Italian or German are considered an advantage.

What's in it for you

  • Attractive compensation package
  • Modern office with excellent public transport connections and discounted parking
  • Diverse training and development opportunities within a global market leader
  • Numerous employee discounts in fashion, leisure, electronics, home & more
  • A supportive team environment with regular team and company events

Who we are

Logistics shapes everyday life - from the goods we consume to the healthcare we rely on. At Kuehne+Nagel, your work goes beyond logistics; it enables both ordinary and special moments in the lives of people around the world.

As a global leader with a strong heritage and a vision to move the world forward, we offer a safe, stable environment where your career can make a real difference. Whether we help deliver life-saving medicines, develop sustainable transportation solutions or support our local communities, your career will contribute to more than you can imagine.
