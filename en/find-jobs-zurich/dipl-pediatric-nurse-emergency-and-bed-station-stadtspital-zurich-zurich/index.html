Full description

To strengthen our team, we are looking for 1. June 2026 or by arrangement a dedicated and competent person for a temporary employment with an option for renewal.

Tasks

  • You care for children of all ages with different diseases, in the sense of family-centered care
  • After your introduction, you are ready to provide both emergency services, inpatient pediatrics and substitutes in the children’s ambulatory
  • You are responsible for patient-centered documentation of the work and care process as well as the correct performance recording
  • You work in an interprofessional team as well as in tandem with FaGe's and MPA's
  • You are jointly responsible in the stationary area for the care of the trainees and the introduction of new employees

Profile

  • Degree as a Dipl. Nurse HF/FH, ideally with experience in pediatrics
  • Enjoy working in a lively environment and with children of all ages
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing, further knowledge of foreign languages is an advantage
  • Self-understanding for a solution-oriented, collegial and cross-departmental cooperation
  • Readiness, resilience, teamwork and balance

We offer

We offer you more than just a job! Our city hospital world is characterized by an open du-culture, an uncomplicated, personal togetherness and the necessary touch of humor in everyday life. As a team of over 120 professional groups, we work every day in very different ways for the well-being of our patients. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions and excellent social benefits from the city of Zurich.

About us

With over 36'000 inpatients and around 455,000 outpatients per year, the Stadtspital Zürich ranks among the top 10 Swiss hospitals. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,500 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
