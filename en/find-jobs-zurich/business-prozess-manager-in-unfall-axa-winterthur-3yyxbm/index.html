Shape your future – together with us!

Would you like to actively shape the future with us? And together make end-to-end industry processes more effective, develop innovative solutions and advance corresponding projects?

If yes - then you are right with us!

We offer a working environment in which thinking is required, teamwork is a high priority and flexibility is encouraged. A job with responsibility, design opportunity and business impact. Support us on the way to a successful future - committed, courageous and open to new things.

We look forward to you and your participation. We are convinced: “Together we make the difference.”

In the area of customer operations, we design the individual experiences of our customers in the event of damage and performance. They are our focus and together with them we find the right solution. In this way, our customer-first mentality ensures maximum customer satisfaction.

This is what awaits you

  • Embedded in the Process & Transformation team, you can not only assign terms such as process management, process excellence and process design, but also show us that you are a master at putting them into practice.
  • You design and document the operational damage process end-to-end with a focus on accidents and ensure a correct and efficient process
  • You define relevant process KPIs, monitor the processes and regulations and their optimization with the help of cockpits, carry out quality checks and error analyses, define measures and accompany the business in their implementation
  • You are the central contact partner for the claims process accident vis-à-vis internal and external partners and work closely with the Process Owner and the Center of Competence accident
  • You also have the opportunity to work on various projects as part of our strategy or even lead them

You bring that with you

  • You are implementation-oriented, have a hands-on mentality, distinguish yourself through an open, courageous and committed personality
  • You like to work collaboratively and have a flair for an independent and agile working method
  • You think in a networked way, have analytical skills as well as a healthy curiosity, have an affinity for process optimization and bring methodological knowledge or are ready to acquire it.
  • You are familiar with the business and industry challenges and have extensive experience in claims processing, preferably in the accident sector
  • Your strong communication skills, your proactive stakeholder management and your English skills round off your profile
