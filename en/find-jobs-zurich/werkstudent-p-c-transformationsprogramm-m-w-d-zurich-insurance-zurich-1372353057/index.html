136873 | 40 - 60% | Oerlikon, Zurich | Hybrid

Does the idea of participating in one of Zurich’s most important strategic projects inspire you and gain valuable practical experience in project management? Do you enjoy actively shaping the future of our P&C business in a dynamic team? Then you are exactly right with us!

From November 2026 we are looking for a working student for a period of one year (with possibility of extension) to support in P&C GoDigital, one of Zurich’s most important strategic initiatives. As part of the program, we implement the future products, processes and technologies for our P&C business. To strengthen our Project Management Office team, we are looking for you.

In this role, you will strengthen the Information & Technology division in the P&C Transformation team at Zurich Switzerland and make an important contribution to our joint success.

This is what awaits you

  • You organize and coordinate project meetings and events – from reservations and invitations to the agenda and post-processing. In addition, you maintain the task planning and support in the processing of the project mailbox.
  • You perform on- and off-boarding of project staff, including ordering hardware and access rights.
  • You create and revise project documents in Word and PowerPoint – appealing, comprehensible and adapted to the respective addressees.
  • You are involved in the development of AI-supported assistance solutions with Microsoft Copilot to effectively support project management.

This sets you apart

  • Ongoing studies (bachelor or master) in computer science, business informatics or a comparable field – initial project experience is an advantage
  • Precise, structured work and the ability to deliver on time even under time pressure
  • Good user knowledge in MS Office (especially Excel and PowerPoint) incl. Copilot and SharePoint
  • Knowledge of Confluence, Azure DevOps and Power BI is a plus
  • You are a reliable team player with high commitment, commitment and initiative
  • You have good language skills in German and English.

Good to know

Place of work: Oerlikon, Zurich

Job: from now on or by agreement

Travel activity: None

Hybrid work: up to 40%
