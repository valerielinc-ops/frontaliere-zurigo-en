Full description

In our team, which is responsible for housing developments and individual residential properties in districts 2 & 3, we are looking for committed support for a real estate manager.

Tasks

  • Acceptance and handling of tenant concerns
  • Offer collection and order processing organization including order and invoice processing means SAP
  • Administration for rentals including data maintenance in the SAP system
  • Self-employed rental of car parks, crafts and warehouses
  • Support for heating billing and debt collection

Profile

  • Completed commercial training in the real estate sector or equivalent training with further training as a clerk* in real estate management
  • Professional experience in the real estate industry
  • High customer and service orientation
  • Sociability, high social skills and communication skills
  • Teamwork, independent working methods and good understanding
  • Experienced use of various IT tools such as MS Office programs and an advantage with the SAP system

We offer

a diverse and collegial working environment in a growing organization. Our employees benefit from attractive employment conditions with good social benefits. The centrally located workplace at Hallwylplatz and a wide range of training and further education are further qualities.

About us

Properties for a livable city of Zurich

Properties City of Zurich operates around 10,300 apartments and around 1,600 commercial premises. We rent the apartments according to the principle of cost rent as affordable living space, pay attention to a socially mixed tenant population and contribute with the commercial space to the good supply of the quarters with goods and services. In addition, there are real estate transactions as well as the management of urban land reserves and building rights.
