We are looking for you as Stv. management for physiotherapy:

  • Representation of management in absentia and assumption of management tasks
  • Co-responsibility for the personnel and professional management of the employees
  • Co-responsibility for achieving clinic and departmental goals
  • Share responsibility for compliance with quality standards in physiotherapy
  • Treatment of inpatient and outpatient patients in the fields of orthopaedics, visceral surgery, rheumatology and internal medicine
  • Supervision of group courses (MTT, GLA:D or pulmonary rehabilitation) and fitness clients

With us you will meet 8 motivated graduate physiotherapists who like to expand their knowledge, develop you professionally and share funny or special moments with you. Treatment at the highest level, high-class medical care and care, competent care in a more familiar, private atmosphere with hotel comfort, dedication, conscientiousness and absolute discretion – this is what the private clinic Bethanien stands for.
