Full description

  • The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich treats and cares for mentally ill people of all ages. The more than 2,500 employees of the 55 different professions make their contribution to the holistic care of patients in interdisciplinary teams, from clarification and therapy to follow-up care. Our work is based on mutual trust and personal responsibility and together we promote an innovative corporate culture. As a university clinic, we are successfully involved in national and international research projects. In the Clinic for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Psychotherapy (KJPP) in Männedorf, children aged 5 to 15 years with mental disorders are treated and cared for by an interdisciplinary team in a psychiatric-psychotherapeutic and milieutherapeutic setting. The Center for Child Psychiatry "Brüschhalde" at the Männedorf site has a modern infrastructure with a generously designed outdoor facility. It is located in the recreation area between the Pfannenstiel region and Lake Zurich. The treatment offer for our young patients includes one ward for short interventions and therapies and three psychotherapy wards with different treatment focuses. Eight treatment stations are available on each station. By agreement we are looking for a Dipl. Nursing professional or dipl.
