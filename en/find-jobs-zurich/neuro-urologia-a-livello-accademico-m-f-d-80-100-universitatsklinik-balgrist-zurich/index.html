Full description

Balgrist University Hospital is one of the most renowned orthopaedic clinics in Switzerland with international recognition and an affiliated centre for paraplegia. >Neuro-urology diagnoses and treats damage and diseases of the nerves of sexual organs and urinary tracts. Due to the targeted expansion of our academic neuro-urology, we are looking for a temporarily for two years

Tasks

  • Clinical-urological care of patients with functionally urological and neuro-urological clinical pictures according to the respective level of training
  • Implementation of complex functional urological and neuro-urological diagnostics, in particular video urodynamics
  • Perform minimally invasive surgical procedures in the ambulatory setting
  • Participation in interdisciplinary visits, case meetings and treatment conferences with related disciplines
  • Participation in urological services and emergency support
  • Scientific activity focusing on neuro-urology
  • Collaboration in clinical studies, publications and academic projects with a view to a habilitation

Requirements

  • Specialist title in urology or advanced training
  • Broad clinical interest in the field of urology, in particular functional urology and neuro-urology
  • Basic experience in scientific work as well as solid knowledge of medical statistics
  • Strong interest in clinical and translational research as well as a clinical-academic career
  • Academic experience and ideally first scientific publications
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing at native level or at least level C1 (GER)
  • Very good English skills in word and writing
  • GCP (Good Clinical Practice) certification
  • High self-motivation, structured working methods and strong team spirit

We offer

  • Clearly defined combination of clinical activity and academic development with a clear perspective on scientific further qualification
  • Protected research time and targeted funding of scientific projects
  • Close academic supervision and mentoring as well as structured support for further career development
  • Attractive remuneration and well-founded framework conditions for a further academic career
  • Dynamic, specialized and interdisciplinary networked environment in neuro-urology
