Full description

The Gibeleich Old Age Centre is a public institution for the care and care of the elderly of the city of Opfikon. It offers a total of 76 nursing places, which are spread over the areas of long-term, transitional and dementia care. Since January 2025, the Spitex city of Opfikon has been integrated into the Gibeleich Old Age Centre. She provides services in the Opfikon-Glattbrugg area and provides future-oriented and holistic care and help at home. The Spitex city of Opfikon is in an exciting phase of development and transformation. With modern structures, digital processes and a dedicated management team, we consistently develop our organization. As an experienced person from the Spitex environment who knows our industry-specific processes, you offer our divisional management competent support in financial, administrative and organizational matters. As of October 1, 2026 or by agreement, we are looking for a specialist in finance and administration. Specialist Finance and Administration Spitex 60 - 80%
