  • Move Switzerland with us. Trained shunting specialists cat.Ai40 or A40 marshalling yard Limmattal Spreitenbach, from now on or by appointment, 60-100% Apply now ## You can look forward to this. At SBB Cargo you have your finger on the pulse of the Swiss economy. SBB Cargo transports 185,000 tons every day, reducing the burden on the roads by 15,000 truck journeys every day and reducing the burden on the environment by 460,000 tons of CO2 every year. You will help ensure that our customers are served reliably and on time. An exciting working environment awaits you, in which you and your teammates will move Switzerland forward. Attractive working conditions are important to us. We pay additional wages for night and weekend work
  • e.g. 6 francs per hour for night work. For night shifts you will also receive a time allowance of between 10 and 30%. This means you have more free time than employees without night and weekend shifts. You can develop further internally, for example by completing training to become a shunting specialist or shunting train driver. You can move that.
  • You carry out shunting movements by moving, assembling and dismantling freight trains and wagons.
  • Your daily tasks include coupling and uncoupling locomotives and freight cars as well as carrying out brake tests.
  • You prepare freight trains for travel and ensure that parked vehicles in the track area are secured.
  • In your everyday work, you use various tools such as radios and tablets to complete your tasks efficiently. You can find out more about the job profile here. This is what you bring with you.
  • You are attentive, concentrated and physically resilient in order to successfully complete this demanding work in 24-hour shifts. You also enjoy working outside in any weather.
  • You are already a trained maneuvering specialist in Ai40 or A40.
  • Because of your safety awareness, it is natural for you to work according to regulations.
  • You have good oral German skills (at least level B1).
  • You have a perfect sense of color (no red/green weakness).
  • During the application process, we will require you to provide a current extract from your criminal record and debt collection register (not older than 3 months).
