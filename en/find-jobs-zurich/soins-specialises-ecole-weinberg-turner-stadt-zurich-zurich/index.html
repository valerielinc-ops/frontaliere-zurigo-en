Full description

We are looking for reinforcement immediately or by agreement. (four short assignments over noon: Mo, Di, Do, Fr each 12:00-13:00)

Tasks

She is interested in working with and living with children. The co-creation of the school supplementary care and leisure time of the children, the support of the educational team over lunch in the way supervision or in the organization of lunch in an MST location. Collaboration in a team but also the independent implementation will give you pleasure. You share our basic attitudes within the framework of the “New Authority” and carry them into everyday life with your presence.

Profile

Completed training as a specialist supervision EFZ. You are a committed, upstanding person and may already have experience in caring for children in a school supplementary field. Willingness to work in a team but also self-employment in the agreed area of responsibility. Good oral and written skills in the German language. You like to be part of a team, open to new things and find challenges exciting? If you feel addressed, you are exactly right with us!

We offer

A varied, versatile, independent and responsible work in an open and motivated team, which supports our school motto "zämä läbe - zämä lehre". An appealing working environment, with clear structures and scope for design. In addition to employment in accordance with the guidelines of the City of Zurich (appropriate wage, 4-6 weeks of holidays depending on age and 6 days of compensation), no assignments are expected to be planned during school holidays. At least part of the extra time during school holidays can be compensated (= additional weeks off work).

About us

The day school Weinberg-Turner is located in Zurich Unterstrass and comprises 13 classes, 4 kindergartens and 4 support locations from August 2025. The approximately 400 children entrusted to us are the focus, we are guided by their competences. Treating each other with respect and appreciation is one of our strengths. From a good school atmosphere, we can promote the children according to their abilities.
