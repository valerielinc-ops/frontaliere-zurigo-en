Full description

Store: Manor Winterthur, ZH, Switzerland. Job ID: R3352.

Happiness can be found in the smallest of things. It is our passion to turn everyday routines into more meaningful moments and connect with customers and colleagues. At Rituals, we blend a thriving culture with top talent. Join our fast growing wellbeing brand and nurture an impactful career with us.

  • Make logistics efficient: You accept new products, check the inventory and make sure that everything appears on the sales floor on time and in an orderly manner.
  • Ensure order in the warehouse: You maintain a clean, tidy warehouse where everything has its place so that your team can work safely and efficiently.
  • Collaboration with the sales team: You support the sales consultants in keeping the shelves always full and thus ensure an attractive presentation of goods.
  • Flexibility: You take responsibility for operations by flexibly covering early and late shifts and Saturdays, contributing to the store’s success.
  • You are organized by nature, have a solution-oriented mindset and a strong eye for detail.
  • You are a positive team player who takes responsibility, is familiar with digital devices and enjoys creating clean, efficient spaces.
  • You have relevant experience in retail, logistics or hospitality, would like to work for a luxury brand such as Rituals and have a valid work permit. Residence permit (C, B, L or G permit) or Swiss citizenship.
