Your team

Do you want to actively shape the future of compliance in the Swiss financial market? In our international Compliance & Regulation team in the field of Financial Services Consulting, you combine regulatory expertise with entrepreneurial thinking and visible customer impact. We advise and audit banks, investment firms, insurance companies, asset managers, FinTechs and other financial intermediaries in Switzerland and Liechtenstein. Zurich By agreement 100% Business consultancy services Everyone welcome ID: 766690WD

Your impact

  • Management of demanding consulting and auditing mandates for Swiss and international financial institutions. To do this, you structure work packages, control budgets and timelines and ensure consistently high quality results.
  • Advising customers on current developments in Swiss and Liechtenstein supervisory and financial market law and translating regulatory requirements into pragmatic governance, process and control solutions.
  • Responsible for regulatory audits and reviews in the areas of GwG and terrorist financing, compliance function, sanctions, cross-border, FIDLEG and Suitability, market behavior and other compliance risks. You work closely with the responsible auditors:innen.
  • You will design compliance transformations and target operating models from strategy, risk appetite and risk analysis to organization, roles and responsibilities to instructions, reporting, risk culture and resource planning.
  • You will analyze the effectiveness of KYC and client lifecycle processes, transaction monitoring, screening, periodic reviews, MROS reporting processes and quality assurance and derive concrete, prioritized improvement measures.
  • You support customers with current topics such as the further development of GwG risk analysis and risk tolerance, new transparency and due diligence obligations, digitalized business models, crypto risks as well as data and technology-based compliance.
  • Conducting interviews, walkthroughs, gap and impact analyses as well as risk-based random samples, assessing complex issues and representing results in an addressable manner to compliance functions, management and board members.
  • Building trusting customer relationships, recognizing new consulting needs and participating in offerings, market development and the further development of our services.
  • Leadership and coaching of project teams. You provide clear feedback, promote talent and actively contribute to the professional and cultural development of our team.
  • You will work interdisciplinary with experts from audit, forensics, legal, risk, technology and management consulting as well as with our international PwC network.
  • You position yourself as an expert on the market, develop specialist contributions and thought leadership and have the opportunity to present at customer events and conferences.

Your skill set

  • Availability of a master’s degree in law, business law, business administration or a comparable practical training that prepares you for the application of financial market regulation.
  • At least six years of relevant professional experience are required – ideally in an auditing or consulting firm, in the compliance, risk or internal audit function of a financial institution or at a supervisory authority – and have already managed projects or smaller teams.
  • In-depth experience in several of the following topics is particularly welcome: GwG/GwV-FINMA, KYC and client lifecycle, compliance risk analysis, sanctions, cross-border, FIDLEG/MiFID II, market behavior, corporate governance or FINIG. You have in-depth knowledge of Swiss supervisory and financial market law.
  • You can not only assess regulatory requirements legally, but also translate them into effective processes, controls, role models and actionable recommendations.
  • You convince with analytical strength, secure judgment, high quality orientation and the ability to communicate complex content clearly and appropriately.
  • You act confidently, enjoy working entrepreneurially and take responsibility for results, team and customer relationships.
  • Enjoy sharing knowledge, coaching employees and actively shaping an inclusive, performance-oriented team environment.
  • fluent German and English skills in word and writing
  • French, Italian or other languages are a plus.
  • Ideally, you will have experience with data analytics, digital compliance solutions or technology-supported auditing and consulting approaches. Of course, you are also curious about new technologies.
