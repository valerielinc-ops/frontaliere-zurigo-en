Full description

At Implenia Holzbau, a lot is currently in motion – driven by the growing importance of sustainable construction and our clear focus on modern timber construction. With our many years of experience, high technical competence and a strong interdisciplinary team, we realize demanding timber construction services across all phases – from planning to production to execution.

In order to further develop our organization and shape timber construction in a future-oriented way, we are looking for committed personalities who want to take responsibility and grow together with us – as Bauführer Holzbau (m/f/d).

Do you have what it takes to turn your communication skills and entrepreneurial thinking into attractive offers? Are analytical, timely and independent work just as important to you as your excellent skills as a team player? Then we are very much looking forward to your commitment for our team in Rümlang!

That's what moves you

  • Management of demanding projects in structural engineering timber construction and wood element construction from planning and production to handover.
  • Responsibility for quality and timely project execution within the agreed budgets
  • Optimization of execution solutions in factory planning with regard to material, production and assembly
  • Contact person for clients and coordination of all subcontractors and specialists involved in the project
  • Management of allocated project resources and monitoring of their efficient use
  • Control and monitoring of project execution in compliance with Group guidelines and legal requirements
  • Preparation of production and assembly documents and schedules
  • Conduct of procurement negotiations together with project management and purchasing

This is why you succeed

  • Apprenticeship as carpenter and polishing training or technician HF or wood construction/construction engineer
  • Professional experience as carpenter or polisher
  • Experience in construction of timber construction projects
  • Knowledge of CAD planning
  • Several years of experience in the construction management of timber construction projects (techn. and kaufm). Project management
  • Sound knowledge of Swiss building law
  • Good communication skills and entrepreneurial thinking
  • Good user knowledge of the MS-Office range
  • Professional leadership experience

This is what we offer you

  • A multicultural working environment with the chance to shape a future worth living
  • State-of-the-art technologies and a contemporary workplace at a sustainable company
  • A company that promotes talent and the training of its employees
  • The security of a financially healthy, broad-based group with strong roots in Switzerland
  • Flexible working models and modern tools
  • An exciting selection of benefits:
  • at least 5 weeks holidays and 9 paid bridge days
  • an in-house pension fund with very good coverage and 2 contribution levels
  • numerous discounts on well-known suppliers of products and services
