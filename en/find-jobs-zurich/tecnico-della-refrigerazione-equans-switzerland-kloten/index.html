Full description

We are looking for a dynamic and motivated personality by 1 February 2027 or by agreement.

You can move that with us.

  • You ensure the reliable operation of refrigeration and air conditioning systems through professional maintenance and maintenance work.
  • You analyze faults, repair them independently and ensure the availability of the systems.
  • You support our customers as a competent contact person in all matters relating to refrigeration technology.
  • You carry out conversions, extensions and commissioning of refrigeration and air conditioning systems.
  • You recognize potential for optimization and actively contribute to increasing the efficiency and sustainability of the plants.
  • You coordinate your assignments with customers, suppliers and the internal team.
  • You document your work professionally and ensure compliance with quality and safety standards.
  • You perform picket service at predefined intervals.

That's what makes you.

You describe yourself as a service provider: in the innermost conviction. Positive feedback on your performance motivates you and spurs you on to further top performances. You also bring:

  • You have completed training as a refrigeration system installer: in EFZ.
  • You will bring several years of professional experience in refrigeration and air conditioning technology, preferably in the service sector.
  • You have very good knowledge of German. English and other foreign language skills are beneficial.
  • You have a valid category B driving licence.
  • You bring a good reputation.
  • You work independently, reliably and like to take responsibility.
  • Contact with customers brings you pleasure and you convince through your service-oriented appearance.
  • You think solution-oriented and keep an overview even in demanding situations.
  • Team spirit, professionalism and quality awareness distinguish you.
  • Participation in the pikettdienst is a matter of course for you.

That's what we offer you.

  • Varied tasks and the opportunity to work independently on site
  • We think and act entrepreneurially. You can take responsibility with us, contribute ideas and get a large creative scope
  • Your health and well-being are our most important asset, which is why we go beyond the legal requirements regarding occupational safety and health protection – also in terms of personal protective equipment.
  • With us you move in an exciting and future-oriented market environment and contribute to a livable, sustainable future
  • We live an open communication with anchored Du-culture across all levels
