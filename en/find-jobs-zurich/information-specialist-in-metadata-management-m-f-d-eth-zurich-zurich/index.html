Information specialist: in metadata management (m/f/d)

80%, Zurich, perpetual

The ETH Library promotes knowledge, supports research and teaching and, as a trustworthy institution, makes the world of yesterday, today and tomorrow more comprehensible. For the group Integrated Media Processing, we are looking for an information specialist in the field of metadata management as of 1 January 2027 or by agreement.

Job description

  • Together with the group management, you are responsible for the operational metadata processing within the integrated media processing group. In doing so, you develop and optimize existing processes, implement innovative ideas, coordinate the documentation and ensure compliance with high quality standards.
  • Thanks to your in-depth expertise in formal development according to RDA and in dealing with GND, you will actively contribute your expertise in practice and in relevant specialist committees such as the EG Metadata from SLSP. There you represent the interests of the ETH Library competently and committed.
  • In your role as Functional Expert for Resource Management in Alma, you have in-depth system knowledge. You support the smooth operation and contribute to the continuous optimization of the system.
  • Active participation in the operational tasks of formal development and in the GND editorial team is a matter of course for you. You appreciate the combination of conceptual responsibility and operational activity and bring your know-how to the workday.

Profile

  • You have a degree as an information specialist: in (BSc or MAS) and have in-depth knowledge of the RDA regulations, the MARC21 metadata standard and experience in programming and artificial intelligence
  • You analyze, condense and interpret complex data and information in a targeted manner
  • You understand technical contexts, know organizational processes and processes and convince through a high affinity for digital technologies
  • You enjoy working in a team, communicating to the addressee and have a very good expression in German and English
  • You actively share your knowledge within the organization, promote cross-divisional collaboration and ideally bring experience in lateral leadership.

We offer

  • A varied activity and a dynamic working environment in the exciting context of research and teaching
  • Working in a committed and collegial team
  • An attractive workplace at the ETH Zurich in the center of Zurich with the possibility of home office
  • Cultural diversity and attractive offers and benefits
  • Family-friendly working conditions and active promotion of education and training
  • We live lifelong learning
