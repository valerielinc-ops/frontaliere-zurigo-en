Full description

Founded and headquartered in Switzerland, Avaloq is continuously expanding its global footprint with around 2,500 colleagues in 11 countries, and more than 170 clients in 35 countries. We are an industry-leading provider of wealth management technology and services for financial institutions around the world, including private banks and wealth managers, investment managers, as well as retail and neo banks. Our research led approach and continual innovation is powered by the passion and creativity of our colleagues. We are always looking for talented people to join us on our mission to orchestrate the financial ecosystem and democratize access to wealth management. Avaloq offers the opportunity to work closely with some of the world’s leading financial institutions as we jointly develop and shape careers. Championing a collaborative, supportive and flexible work environment empowers our colleagues to reach their full potential.

As part of Avaloq's Strategy & Corporate Development team, you will help shape the company's strategic direction through rigorous analysis, structured problem-solving, and close collaboration with senior stakeholders. You will contribute to strategy development, transformation initiatives, and corporate development activities while gaining broad exposure across Avaloq's business. This role is ideal for a high-performing professional looking to apply strategic thinking, financial analysis, and execution excellence in a fast-paced fintech environment. Your key tasks

  • Support the development of Avaloq's corporate strategy and corporate development agenda, helping translate strategic priorities into actionable plans
  • Conduct analyses, financial models, business cases, and market assessments to support strategic and operational decision-making
  • Collaborate with sales, delivery, service operations, technology, finance, and other corporate functions to support strategic initiatives
  • Prepare high-quality materials for executive leadership and help structure management discussions

How to apply

Please submit your CV along with a written motivation letter outlining your interest in this role and how your experience aligns with our requirements. Applications without a motivation letter will not be considered.

Qualifiche

  • 2-5 years of experience in top-tier management consulting, corporate strategy, corporate development, private equity, or a comparable strategic role
  • Exposure to banking, fintech, B2B software, or technology-enabled business models
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, including financial modelling and business case development
  • Ability to structure complex topics into clear insights and actionable recommendations
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management skills
  • High ownership, curiosity, and a hands-on mindset
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English
  • German or Italian is a plus

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
