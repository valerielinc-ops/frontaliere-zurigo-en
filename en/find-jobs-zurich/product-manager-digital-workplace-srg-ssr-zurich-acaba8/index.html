Full description

The Operations division is responsible for the technical production of all SRG content as well as its technical handling and archiving (TV, radio and multimedia).

Within SRG SSR, the Digital Workplace provides employees with a modern, secure and reliable digital work environment. It includes Windows and Apple devices, Microsoft 365 services, collaboration and intranet tools, process automation and certain artificial intelligence-based solutions.

This is your contribution.

In the IT sub-portfolio, you are responsible for the product Digital Workplace for the following topics:

  • Definition of product vision and roadmap as well as strategic orientation
  • Continuous and data-driven control of the entire product life cycle: from needs-based evaluation and development to reliable and efficient operation to replacement or migration
  • Professional management of the product owners and close cooperation with the personnel management operations, the business partners operations and the relevant stakeholders in the business
  • Planning and control of financial resources as well as management of external suppliers, components and services
  • Implementation of the transformation into a modern, product-oriented organization

They inspire us.

  • Methodological competence and experience in agile product management, from strategic planning to the roadmap to operational implementation (e.g. SAFe, Scrum)
  • Good professional knowledge and a holistic, strategic understanding of the digital workplace. This includes endpoint management (devices, MDM/MAM, packaging) and Microsoft 365 services (collaboration and communication solutions, intranet, provisioning, automation and self-service solutions, and AI integrations).
  • Clear focus on an end-to-end and user-centric user experience across the entire digital workplace, combined with a strong enthusiasm for digital innovation and technological trends in a rapidly changing environment.
  • Experience in product management including budget management, active control of product cost/quality based on KPIs and cost-benefit analyses
  • Ability to align the product with added value and strategic and operational objectives, taking into account market developments
  • Strong user-friendly communication and moderation skills to manage stakeholder interests, foster interdisciplinary collaboration and empower teams.
  • Very good language skills in a national language, plus German or French at least at level B1 and English at level B1

So we inspire you.

  • Nationally relevant task with end-to-end responsibility for an exciting product
  • Product-oriented, agile working method
  • Collaboration with interdisciplinary, motivated teams

Our promise

What does it take to reach the people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expertise, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can contribute and develop. For the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: Welcome to the world of SRG!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, promote creativity and offer a lot of design space for new and high-quality products.
  • Shaping digitalization: Quality journalism is also digital for us. That’s why we explore new possibilities and experiment with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-medial. We design on the pulse of time and on the nerve of the audience.
  • Being human: We meet each other collegially, work independently and shape our working atmosphere individually. Our success is based on the personal strengths as well as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programs. In doing so, we act respectfully and independently; in this way we promote opinion-forming and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.
