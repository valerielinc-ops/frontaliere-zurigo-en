Original description

The Zurich soils are a livelihood and at the same time under pressure due to diverse usage requirements. In this function, you will strengthen your knowledge of pollutants in the Department of Soil Protection, help to set up enforcement in the areas of PFAS, children's playgrounds and pollutant pollution and then contribute to its implementation. Your tasks

  • You develop and implement implementation principles incl. Data management and maps for chemical soil protection
  • You monitor the development of known and new burdens
  • You assess soil-relevant construction projects and advise private and public authorities
  • You issue requirements for the steering of eroded soil and for the prevention of danger in the case of loaded soil Your profile
  • You have completed FH/uni studies with a scientific orientation or in a related field
  • You have professional experience in chemical soil protection
  • You take responsibility and think customer-oriented
  • You communicate clearly and can communicate complex relationships in an understandable way
  • You work in a structured, forward-looking way and also keep an overview of challenging projects that await you The Department of Soil Protection combines sound expertise with effective, practical implementation.
