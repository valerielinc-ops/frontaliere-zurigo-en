WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR

  • Ensuring stable, highly available and performant database operations
  • Implementation and continuous development of backup, recovery and disaster recovery strategies
  • Identification and resolution of bottlenecks at the CPU, memory, IO, blocking and deadlock levels
  • Further development of database architectures, standards and operational processes
  • Introduction and maintenance of monitoring, security and automation solutions
  • 2nd/3rd level support for database-critical issues

WHAT YOU SHOULD BRING

  • Completed IT training, a degree in information science or equivalent qualification
  • Several years of professional experience in the administration and operation of Microsoft SQL Server environments
  • Excellent knowledge of SQL and a sound understanding of the SQL Server engine
  • Experience in performance tuning, high availability, backup and recovery
  • Know-how in monitoring, automation and scripting in the database environment
  • Structured, analytical and solution-oriented working approach
  • High quality and security orientation as well as strong personal responsibility
  • Good German and English language skills in written and spoken form

  • Employee benefits of a large corporation, employee stock purchase program
  • Promotion of customized ongoing training
  • Employee restaurant and external catering and shopping options nearby
  • Individual internal and external personnel development measures in collaboration with our Rheinmetall-Academy
  • Workplace just a few minutes' walk from Zurich-Oerlikon train station
