What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family-owned company with 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation, and customer orientation are the pillars of the company. The activities focus on the import of automotive brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessory parts, as well as the provision of financial services. The Emil Frey AG, Züri-Oberland Wetzikon is the official sales and service partner of the exclusive brands BMW and MINI and aims to create fascination and joy of driving. Your tasks

  • Execution of diagnostic, repair, and maintenance work on vehicles of the BMW & MINI brands
  • Analysis and resolution of technical and electronic faults with the help of modern diagnostic systems
  • Active participation in daily business
  • Ensuring compliance with quality and safety standards
  • Regular participation in brand-specific training and courses Your profile

  • Analytical, structured, and solution-oriented working style

  • Flexibility, resilience, and sense of duty
