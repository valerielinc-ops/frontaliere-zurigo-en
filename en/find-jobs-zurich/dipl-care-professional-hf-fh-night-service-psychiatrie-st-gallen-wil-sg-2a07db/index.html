Your tasks

  • As a night heroine you ensure that our patients feel safe and well accompanied during the night and can count on reliable care at any time.
  • With heart, humour and professional competence, you design the individual and resource-oriented care of our patients and thus create an appreciative atmosphere during the night.
  • Especially in acute situations, you keep a cool head, react quickly and professionally and act confidently and safely even under demanding conditions.
  • Providing protection, stimulus shielding and stability is just as much a part of your task as actively shaping a calm and safe atmosphere during the night.
  • Your creative ideas are expressly welcome and give you the opportunity to actively participate in the further development of processes and to continuously optimize our care processes. Your profile
  • With a completed training as a nurse HF or FH you bring the necessary professional qualification, whereby a foreign diploma requires an SRK recognition or a positive SRK PreCheck.
  • You are familiar with the dynamics of acute setting and you bring your expertise safely and committedly into the interprofessional cooperation even during the night.
  • Whether in providing protection and stimulus shielding or in helping you cope with your life, you face the night challenges with your heart, mind and the necessary peace of mind.
  • Even in a lively station routine, you keep an overview, react calmly to different situations and meet people at any time with empathy, respect and humor. With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatments and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations. We are on the road digitally and are committed to sustainability, so we look forward to your online application via our homepage. Then we will contact you. During a personal interview we get to know each other and you will learn more about the psychiatry St.Gallen, the advertised position and the challenges. In the second round we would like to get an even better picture of you and find out together with you whether we fit together. You also get the opportunity to get to know your future team. You will receive a telephone confirmation from us if we decide for you after the second interview and ask you if you are still convinced of us. If we agree, we will draw up your contract. In the following days we will send you the contract by post in double. The contract also provides you with relevant information about your employment. Between the acceptance of the job and the first working day, you as a new employee can already access relevant internal information. So you can get a differentiated picture of the company and already prepare for the job. On the first day you can expect an onboarding in your new team and with other colleagues. You set up at the new workplace and get an overview of your next tasks. You also get your individual introduction plan, which helps you to find your way around quickly.
