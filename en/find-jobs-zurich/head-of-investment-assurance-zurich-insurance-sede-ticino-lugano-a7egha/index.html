Role overview

Are you passionate about building something new and successful – helping our customers become more resilient to risks while shaping Zurich’s global risk advisory agenda?

Zurich Resilience Solutions (ZRS) is Zurich’s dedicated commercial risk engineering and risk advisory business, working as a trusted advisor for underwriting, insurance customers and independent risk advisory customers.

We provide customers with services and solutions that help them understand, manage and reduce their risks – from Core Risk Engineering Lines: Property, Casualty, Energy, Construction & Marine, to climate and natural hazards, to cyber, supply chain and other emerging risks.
