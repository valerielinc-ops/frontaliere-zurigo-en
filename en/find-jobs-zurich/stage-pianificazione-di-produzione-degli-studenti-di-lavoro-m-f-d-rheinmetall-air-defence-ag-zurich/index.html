Full description

To support our production planning, we are looking for an intern / working student (m/f/d) with technical understanding, organizational skill and a dose of curiosity to accompany us in day-to-day business.

  • Administrative support for manufacturing orders with subsequent distribution into the respective production areas
  • Revision of the Miscellaneous List
  • Disposition of small materials and shelf-life products (L-parts)
  • Management security stocks Consumables
  • Support in the collection, maintenance and management of processes and data in the process management system
  • Creation of presentations, concept overviews and evaluations
  • Preparation of working documents, such as forms and guidelines as specified
  • Support for smaller projects and special tasks WHAT YOU SHOULD TEACH
  • Studies in the field of industrial engineering, mechanical engineering production engineering or comparable (uni, FH or HF)
  • Sound knowledge and safe handling of MS Office, explicitly Power Point, Excel
  • Technical understanding and interest in production processes
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing WHAT WE ARE TO YOU
  • A dynamic working environment with exciting projects
  • Flexible working hours (active and trusted working hours)
  • Very good social benefits and a super-compulsory pension fund
  • Employee benefits of a large corporation, employee share purchase program
  • Promotion of needs-based training
  • Staff restaurant and external catering and shopping facilities nearby
  • Individual internal and external personnel development measures in cooperation with our Rheinmetall Academy
  • Workspace a few minutes walk from the Zurich-Oerlikon train station KONTAKTDETAILS Responsible contact: Melanie Huellen For questions about your application, please use the contact form. Please note that dossiers of recruiters are not taken into account.
