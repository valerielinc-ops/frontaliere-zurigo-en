Full description

Whether crispy pizzas, the finest pasta creations, traditional fish and meat dishes or homemade dolci, the Molino team spoils its guests in a stylish ambience with the highest quality standards. Would you also like to provide the extra Italianità feeling? Then apply now!

Luogo di lavoro: Pizzeria Ristorante Molino, Pizzeria Ristorante Molino Winterthur, Marktgasse 45, 8400 Winterthur

Mansioni

  • Organisation of the assigned item
  • Preparation of the daily mise-en-place
  • Preparation and distribution of food and desserts in the lunch and evening business
  • Ordering, delivery control, inventory, cleaning and control tasks as well as occupational safety within the framework of the HACCP / KOPAS concept
  • Supporting the chef in menu and offer planning and in compliance with quality and safety standards
  • You are the deputy of the chef during his absence

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience and initial management experience: in gastronomy
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): as a cook
  • Experience with traditional Italian recipes
  • You are a service-oriented, communicative and resilient personality
  • You are a host with body and soul, have good manners and a well-groomed external appearance.
  • You have a quick apprehension, are a team player and used to working independently
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • Italian (advantageous)
