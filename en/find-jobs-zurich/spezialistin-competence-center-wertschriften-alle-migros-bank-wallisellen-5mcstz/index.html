Full description

With us, you are the central specialist who supports internal positions with know-how and masters complex securities topics. We are looking for a personality who takes responsibility with foresight and does not lose sight of the demand for quality. Ready to take the securities administration to the next level? We look forward to seeing you!

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Bank, Wallisellen, Industriestrasse 17-19, 8304 Wallisellen

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You are the first point of contact for internal positions in stock exchange orders, corporate actions and all securities topics.
  • You administer and process the feedback from corporate action events and ensure the correct final report to the specialist departments.
  • You perform mutations of entry prices and position currencies and ensure that our data is always accurate and up-to-date.
  • You apply for Valoren openings and mutations from the external partner and take over the quality control and troubleshooting.
  • Implementation of custodial liquidations, correction of incorrect custodial fees and maintenance of the country restriction list for securities trading
  • assist internal bodies in the application of the core banking system related to securities settlement and ensure smooth operation.

Requirements

  • Mind. 3 years: Securities administration
  • You have in-depth knowledge of securities administration and experience in dealing with the Finnova core banking system
  • Your communication strength and your solution-oriented thinking make you a valued point of contact for internal positions.
  • Networked thinking and the ability to keep track even in hectic situations
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ)
  • in German (negotiation confident)
  • French (very good knowledge)
  • Italian (advantageous)

Cosa of friamo

  • Central place of work (GABS): Optimally accessible by train (10 minutes from Zurich HB) or car
  • Child care: up to CHF 300 per month per child to external child care
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Migros Bank: accounts, mortgages and financial services with preferential terms
  • Employee offers: Annual generous well-being credit

Contatto

Application and contact Sevilay Sesso HR Business Partner

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision to advance or withdraw from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
