For our team Coordination Services & Finance in the Finance Directorate, we are looking for a committed, responsible and solution-oriented personality. In this versatile function, you take on a central role at the interface between income management and the hospitals under supervision and support them in questions relating to performance recording, documentation, billing and cost protection.

Your main tasks

Performance measurement and performance controlling

  • You coordinate questions about performance recording and documentation in the context of invoicing with the hospitals in charge
  • You ensure complete, timely and correct performance recording and effective performance controlling
  • You advise and train the medical profession and nursing on issues of performance recording and the associated processes
  • You will analyze clinically relevant questions and support clinics in the definition and implementation of measures to improve results

Refusals and payers

  • You ensure efficient and traceable processing of rejections and queries for performance recording
  • You clarify critical cases in connection with services and documentation with the guarantors or Cost carriers
  • You support the clinics in tariff and administrative issues related to invoicing

Custodial and cost protection

  • You manage the depots and create and calculate cost estimates
  • You coordinate cooperation on inpatient scheduling and cost assurance in the hospitals under supervision
  • In special cases, you coordinate closely with the social service, especially with regard to therapies, their financing and ethical issues.

Consulting and cooperation with clinics

  • You are the central contact person for the supervised clinics for administrative and performance-related questions
  • You support the clinics with special questions and administrative bottlenecks
  • You work closely with various internal stakeholders and ensure a constructive transfer of information and knowledge

Process optimization and projects

  • You identify optimization potential in the processes around performance recording, documentation and billing and contribute to their implementation
  • You coordinate pilot tests as part of process, instruction or responsibility adjustments by revenue management
  • You lead projects or actively participate in projects

Your profile

  • You have completed a commercial or (para)medical basic education, a further education at tertiary level is advantageous
  • You have in-depth knowledge of the ambulatory and stationary tariff systems of acute somatic service providers, in particular TARDOC/TARMED and SwissDRG
  • You have in-depth knowledge of the Swiss social security system, in particular KVG/KVV
  • You bring at least five years of professional experience with an acute somatic service provider in Switzerland
  • You have a strong understanding of the relationships between performance recording, documentation, invoicing and income management
  • You work analytically, structured, exact and solution-oriented and have a high service orientation
  • You communicate safely and appropriately with different professional groups and can convey complex questions in an understandable way.
  • You have stylish knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • Other national languages are desirable
  • You are a committed, responsible and resilient personality with pleasure in a versatile interface function

Our offer

  • A responsible and varied activity at an important interface between income management and clinics
  • A versatile area of responsibility with direct influence on the quality and further development of the performance processes
  • The opportunity to actively shape processes, identify optimization potential and contribute your own ideas
  • A dynamic and highly professional environment with interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Modern working conditions as well as attractive training and development opportunities
  • A motivated team that values quality, collaboration and a solution-oriented way of working
