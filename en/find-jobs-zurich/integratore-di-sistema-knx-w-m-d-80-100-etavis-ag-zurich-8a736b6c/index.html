Full description

Tasks

  • Using the latest KNX technology, you will independently accompany complex building automation projects in all phases, from configuration through to commissioning and training.
  • You will carry out repair and maintenance work and resolve system faults.
  • You will also support the project management in work preparation and technical matters.

Requirements

  • After completing your apprenticeship as an electrical installer or electrical planner EFZ or an equivalent qualification, you have already gained experience in the fields of KNX or telematics.
  • Initial professional experience with KNX systems or the KNX basic course is required.
  • Very good German skills (oral and written)
  • Category B driving licence
