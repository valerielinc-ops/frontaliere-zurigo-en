Full description

Customer-specific production and repair of light commercial vehicles characterize this field of work. The working environment is characterised by modern infrastructure and a dedicated team. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Production of aluminium chassis, lightweight cases and bridges according to parts lists and CAD specifications
  • Manufacture of special purpose vehicles, trailers and semi-trailers
  • Use of gluing, riveting, screwing and welding techniques in production
  • Assembly of equipment such as lifting platforms, refrigerating appliances and the like
  • Repair services of bodies and chassis
  • Carrying out electrical connection work, in particular on lighting

Requirements

  • Ideally, you will have an education as a car locksmith or car body carrier and enjoy working with light commercial vehicles and aluminum. New entrants with relevant professional experience and completed training are also welcome.
  • They have the driving licence category B, ideally also BE, C1 and trucks.
  • Working independently is just as important to you as an appreciative cooperation in the team. You work efficiently, attach importance to quality “Swiss Made” and appreciate to offer customers real added value with your own products.

Offer

  • Working independently in a motivated team
  • Working on high-quality products
  • Free parking
  • Regular company events
  • Good accessibility with public transport and car as well as modern infrastructure
