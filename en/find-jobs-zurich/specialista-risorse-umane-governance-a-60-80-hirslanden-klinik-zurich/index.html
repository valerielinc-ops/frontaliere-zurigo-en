Hirslanden stands for first-class, responsible and individual health care that puts people first. From birth to old age as well as from prevention to healing – at any time, physically and digitally – the Hirslanden Group is concentrating on the further development of the “Continuum of Care” together with private and public partners.

YOUR TASKS

  • Design: You will continue to develop our group-wide HR governance and ensure standardized, legally compliant and auditable HR processes
  • Optimize: You analyze and design HR processes as well as HR guidelines, regulations and standards with a focus on efficiency, quality, standardization, compliance and user-friendliness
  • You are the central contact person for our HR Business Partners and HR Operations throughout the Hirslanden Group and support them in governance, compliance and process topics
  • Ensure quality: You check HR documents for linguistic quality, consistency and compliance and define binding quality standards
  • Co-shape: You accompany the introduction of new HR standards, guidelines and processes and work in group-wide HR projects

Your profile

  • Completed studies (uni/FH) or a comparable education and training in the field of human resources, business administration, law or a related discipline
  • Several years of professional experience in HR, ideally with a focus on HR processes, HR governance or compliance
  • In-depth knowledge of employment law as well as experience in process management and the optimization of HR processes
  • Analytical, structured and quality-oriented working method with a strong eye for detail
  • Communication strength, assertiveness and enjoyment of working with different stakeholders
  • Very high linguistic security in German as well as good knowledge of English and French (at least B1)
