University intern in various areas 50-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. University intern in various areas Throughout Switzerland / Work Smart, start possible between January 2027 and April 2027, 50-100% Apply now

What you can look forward to. Are you eager for challenges, want to put your knowledge into practice and get a taste of what it's like at SBB? As an SBB university intern, you will work on exciting projects that get your career moving. Among our various areas, we will find the right opportunity and responsible tasks for you. You can complete your university internship both during your studies and afterwards and set your workload flexibly between 50-100%. The start dates can be chosen flexibly between January 1 and April 1, 2027, in consultation with your supervisors. You benefit from a GA Travelcard in class 2. In addition, you enjoy five weeks of vacation per year, with the vacation duration adjusted pro rata according to your employment. With a workload of 100%, your attractive monthly salary during your bachelor's studies is CHF 2'800.-, with a bachelor's degree CHF 3'800.- and with your master's degree CHF 4'800.-. You can find more information under University internships: a multifaceted insight. Are you interested in learning more about our professions? We look forward to welcoming you to our SBB Career Worlds Café at Zurich main station from October 22 to 24, 2026. You can find all the information at sbb.ch/berufsweltencafe. What you can achieve.

  • You will take on challenging tasks in innovative projects or conceptual areas and provide support with administrative tasks.
  • You will gain an in-depth insight into the mobility industry and help move Switzerland forward with us.
  • Through the university intern framework program, you will get to know other university interns and can build a network beyond your area of assignment.
  • From A for "Asset Management" through B for "Rail Operations", F for "Facility Management", H for "Human Resources", I for "IT", M for "Mechanical Engineering", N for "Network Development" to Z for "central functions" – no matter what interests you: we offer corresponding opportunities.

What you bring.

  • You have successfully completed at least four semesters of your university studies (for example, business administration, engineering, IT, natural sciences, humanities, law, etc.) or completed your studies within the last 12 months.
  • You are ambitious, know which areas interest you and want to commit to us for several months.
  • You have strong social skills, enjoy working in a team and want to get involved and contribute your ideas.
  • You speak one of our national languages fluently and have a command of another national language at B1 level.

Your salary and benefits. The salary as a Young Talent is standardized. You can find more information about the specific salary under Students and university graduates .

This is how you get started. That you want to move Switzerland forward with us is our greatest motivation. That is why we are not requiring a cover letter for this position. If you would nevertheless like to tell us about your motivation, you still have the option of doing so in our application tool. Doing great things is easier when we use first names. That starts with your application.
