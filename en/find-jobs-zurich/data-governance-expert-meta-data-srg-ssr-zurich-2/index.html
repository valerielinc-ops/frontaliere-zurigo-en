Full description

Can users at SRG immediately find what data there is, what it means and where it comes from? By establishing company-wide data governance, we create precisely this transparency. In Data Governance, you are responsible for the business glossary and data catalogue of SRG and thus ensure a uniform, comprehensible data language throughout the company.

This is your contribution.

  • Develop, maintain and expand the SRG-wide business glossary and standardize data definitions across domains
  • Define metadata standards and coordinate data lineage documentation across systems and domains
  • You act as the business owner of the data catalogue tool: You prioritize the backlog, promote adoption and continuously improve the user experience
  • Coordinate automatic metadata acquisition in collaboration with IT and Enterprise Data Architect
  • Harmonize metadata across domains in close collaboration with Data Stewards
  • They are involved in the community of practice metadata and take over their operational moderation if necessary

They inspire us.

  • University degree or equivalent education and deep experience in metadata management
  • Good understanding of data architectures, processes and models as well as taxonomy and classification and their practical application
  • Experience with metadata management and data catalog tools as well as basic knowledge in business owner/backlog management
  • knowledge of DAMA-DMBOK knowledge areas, in particular metadata management; CDMP certification is an advantage
  • Strong communication and moderation competence in interdisciplinary and cross-functional settings to advise specialist areas in the use, structuring and quality assurance of data
  • Very good language skills at level C1 in a national language as well as German and French at least at level B1, English at level B1

So we inspire you.

  • Meaningful operational key role in building enterprise-wide data governance with a direct impact on the discoverability and comprehensibility of data at SRG
  • Close cooperation with editorial, business, technology and governance in an interdisciplinary environment
  • Active participation in data governance, data quality and data-driven working methods within the SRG
  • Space for personal responsibility, professional impact and continuous learning
  • Collaboration with an interdisciplinary team across all language regions in Switzerland
  • Hybrid working model with flexible forms of work and home office possibilities

Please submit your application documents (motivation letter and CV) via the “Apply now” button. The application period ends on 6 November 2026.

The position focuses on meta data management. Depending on the profile and organizational needs, it is possible to take on selected tasks in the area of Master Data Management.
