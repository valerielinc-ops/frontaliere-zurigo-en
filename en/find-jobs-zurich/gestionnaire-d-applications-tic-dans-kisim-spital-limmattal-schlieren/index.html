Digitalization plays a central role in the further development of our hospital – and you are right in the middle of it. With your know-how, you ensure the smooth operation of our clinic information system (KISIM), develop it further and are a competent contact person for our employees. Shape the future of digital healthcare with us and become part of our team!

Here they engage

  • Self-responsible support and continuous development of the clinic information system KISIM together with the KISIM application team
  • Development and evaluation of process and requirement definitions in close cooperation with the departments and external partners
  • Monitoring and maintenance of interfaces to other systems
  • Planning and conducting training
  • Support and competent advice for employees on daily application issues
  • Assistance in release management, including test concepts, lifecycle and change management
  • Active participation in the optimization of our internal IT operational processes
  • Completed training in computer science (HF/FH), basic medical training with a high IT affinity or a comparable qualification
  • Years of experience in:
  • Work with clinic information systems, ideally with KISIM
  • Application Operations and Project Management
  • Knowledge of standards such as HL7, DICOM and interface integrations is an advantage
  • Experience in the parameterization of applications
  • They work in a structured, solution-oriented manner and are capable of teamwork – and keep an overview even in complex projects.
  • Reduced employee parking
  • Motorcycle parking, lockable bicycle parking
  • With the ZVV Bonus Pass / Z-Bonus Pass we offer an attractive ÖV subscription for employees
