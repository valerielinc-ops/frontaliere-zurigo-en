You enjoy sharing knowledge and accompanying young people in their development? Then you will find the ideal combination with us: professional care in everyday hospital life and a varied role as a vocational trainer. You motivate, promote and support our learners and students and strengthen them in their professional and personal development.

YOUR TASKS

  • You accompany, promote and support our learners and students in everyday care and actively shape their education
  • You take over the professional as well as customer- and needs-oriented care and care of our patients
  • You implement medical prescriptions and participate in preventive, diagnostic and therapeutic measures
  • You take responsibility in clinical decision-making and independently initiate the necessary measures

Your profile

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nurse HF / FH / DN II / AKP
  • Interest in vocational training and willingness to take the vocational trainer course (if not already available)
  • Joy in passing on knowledge and accompanying young people in their development
  • You are professionally experienced, socially competent and like to take on responsibility in the interdisciplinary treatment team
  • Your open, reliable and team-oriented way distinguishes you
  • Communication at eye level is a matter of course for you

  • Possibility of up to 7 weeks of holidays per year
