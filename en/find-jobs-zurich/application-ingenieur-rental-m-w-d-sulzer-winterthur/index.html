Full description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

Play as our new Application Engineer Rental They play a central role in the further expansion of our rental business in the municipal and industrial wastewater sector. They are responsible for the technical evaluation of customer requirements, the elaboration of offers and the planning and coordination of rental projects. As an important interface between sales, service, workshop, purchasing, customers and external partners, you ensure that technically sound, secure and economical rental solutions are successfully implemented. The position combines technical project planning, sales support and project coordination in a dynamic environment. In doing so, you will help to ensure high customer satisfaction, support the efficient use of rental assets and drive the continuous development of our rental business. You will become part of a dedicated team in a growing business and benefit from the development opportunities offered by the expansion of our rental business. Your main tasks and responsibilities

  • Technical support for sales in the development of rental solutions, including the evaluation of customer requests, the preparation of technical offers, calculations and solution concepts, as well as the selection of suitable pump, piping, electrical and instrumentation components.
  • Planning, preparation and coordination of rental projects in the areas of wastewater extraction, bypass pumping, construction water management, temporary pumping stations and industrial applications, including coordination with customers, suppliers, service partners and internal specialist areas.
  • Preparation and maintenance of technical documentation, test reports, checklists and master data as well as participation in the continuous improvement of processes, standards and fleet management concepts within the rental business.

What you should bring for this position

  • Professional experience in project management, technical service, plant construction, pump technology or in applications of water and wastewater technology, ideally in an industrial or infrastructural environment.
  • Completed technical training with further training as a technician or master or a degree in electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, environmental engineering, process engineering, mechatronics or a comparable discipline.
  • In-depth technical understanding of pump and delivery systems, pipelines and valves, switchgear and control systems, frequency converters (VFDs) as well as sensors and measurement technology.
  • Experience in quotation preparation, calculation or order processing as well as ideally knowledge in dealing with ERP, CRM or quotation management systems.
  • Strong organizational and analytical skills with a structured, independent and solution-oriented way of working.
  • Secure use of Microsoft Office applications and the ability to coordinate multiple projects and priorities simultaneously.
  • High customer orientation, teamwork and a strong sense of responsibility and security.
  • Driving licence class B as well as readiness for project-related business travel within Germany.

What we offer you
