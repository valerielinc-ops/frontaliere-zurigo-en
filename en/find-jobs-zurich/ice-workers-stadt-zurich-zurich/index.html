Full description

We are a day school in the very diverse heart of Zurich! We are looking for an advisory board in a team with a specialist and a school assistant.

Tasks

  • Management of day-to-day operations (pedagogy, household, general organization, administration) of a small-hort of the Aussersihl school
  • Lunch and evening care for children aged 4 to 6 years
  • Responsibility for the care of children with special needs and special school children in close cooperation with the leadership team, SSA, HP and teachers
  • Appropriate parental work
  • Participation in cross-group care during school holidays
  • Participation in school events and in school working groups

Profile

  • Motivated, open, resilient personality with a positive human image
  • Independent and self-organized work
  • Flexibility as well as very good team and communication skills
  • Resilience and high flexibility in cooperation
  • Solution-oriented basic attitude in daily work
  • Interest and willingness to participate in a changing school supplementary care
  • Willingness to perform a workload of 60% on four days of the week (Mo, Di, Do, Fr)
  • degree as social pedagogue FH or HFS; Social worker FH or HFS; director; kindergarten teacher
  • Primary school teacher (CH); university degree with major in social education, pedagogy or educational sciences
  • Child educator or childhood educator HF
  • For applications with a foreign diploma / certificate, a Swiss diploma recognition must be attached

We offer

  • an experienced, committed and humorous care team
  • well organised and proven cross-hort cooperation
  • varied, interesting and independent activity in a versatile, changing field of activity of the school
  • Possibility to develop and contribute your own ideas within the framework of the day school
  • a new, modern infrastructure in a housing estate
  • Competent support from the Management Board and the Management of Grossregenerierküche
  • a contemporary employment according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich
