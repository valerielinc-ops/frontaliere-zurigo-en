Full description

Fixed-term position until July 31, 2027, with the possibility of permanent employment thereafter

For our young Riedtli school, we are looking for an engaged and responsible individual starting February 1, 2027, or by agreement, who enjoys actively helping shape school-enrichment care and leisure activities.

Responsibilities

  • You look after children at primary and kindergarten level as part of school-enrichment care and help shape everyday life at the school.
  • You contribute to the cluster teams and help further develop shared educational approaches and procedures.
  • You support the team of the entire care facility flexibly as a substitute at kindergarten and primary level.
  • Through your presence and attitude, you contribute to implementing the principles of New Authority.

Profile

  • You are an engaged, motivated and curious professional and have completed training as a childcare specialist EFZ or completed a degree in social work, a teaching diploma or comparable pedagogical studies.
  • You identify with our values: Being human – valuing diversity – living community.
  • You set clear parameters, provide guidance and at the same time create room for development.
  • You treat employees and children with appreciation, clarity and reliability and, through your presence, contribute to implementing New Authority.
  • Teamwork – including with various professional groups throughout the entire school – enriches you, and you value professional exchange.
  • You can be deployed flexibly and are willing to competently support different groups and situations.

Important: All foreign diplomas and professional certificates must be accompanied by a Swiss equivalence confirmation.

Do you enjoy being part of a larger whole and are you open to new things at a school that makes a difference? If you feel addressed, you are exactly right with us!

We offer

  • A varied, diverse, independent and responsible job in a lively and inspiring environment.
  • A motivated and humorous school team you can rely on: We support each other, think together and inspire each other with new ideas.
  • A work environment where not only the children are supported, but also our employees. Their professional development is important to us and is specifically supported.

About Us

In the summer of 2025, a new primary school with a full-day program opened in the former Riedtli secondary school building in the Unterstrass district. It comprises two double kindergartens, ten regular classes, and three special education classes. By 2030, the school will grow to twelve regular classes. On each of the four upper floors, two to three regular classes and one multi-grade special education class form a cluster with shared spaces and close collaboration within the entire team. As an inclusive, modern full-day school, we aim to create an attractive learning, living, and working environment where everyone feels comfortable and can develop their potential.
