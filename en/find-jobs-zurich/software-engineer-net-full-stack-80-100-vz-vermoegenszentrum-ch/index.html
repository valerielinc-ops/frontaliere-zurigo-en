Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We advise individuals and companies about money, manage assets and offer attractive financial services. As we are growing strongly, we are looking for reinforcements for our software engineering team at our headquarters at Paradeplatz in Zurich.

  • An inspiring team and a family environment that constantly strives to improve and modernize the existing.
  • The opportunity to actively shape the future of our systems and promote the use of the latest technologies.
  • The opportunity to develop new business applications and expand existing ones as part of the Microsoft Technology stack.

  • Responsibility for the automation and deployment and operation of cloud applications and cloud resources under DevOps.

  • A strong team ability and a “hands-on” mentality.
  • Interest in questions from the financial sector and preferably experience in the regulated environment.
  • Enjoy working with specialist representatives and co-creating professional concepts.
  • Flexible working hours, the possibility to work several days a week in the home office and a modern workplace on site.
  • A collegial working environment with social events, an exciting field of activity and the opportunity to work independently.
  • The opportunity to actively shape and develop a growing company and to contribute your joy of experimentation and learning.
  • Every year you have three days and CHF 1'000 free for further education. We support larger training within the framework of your job profile.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of the VZ family and experience it for yourself!
