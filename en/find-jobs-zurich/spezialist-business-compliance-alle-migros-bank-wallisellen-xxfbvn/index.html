Spezialist Business Compliance (alle) — Migros Bank

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

With us, you can take responsibility for various compliance activities and incorporate your strengths. For a new, interdisciplinary team, we are looking for you: a personality that always keeps an overview. Someone who complements us with a lot of enthusiasm and exact working methods. Come on.

Mansioni

  • You strengthen our team in the recognition and periodic review of business relationships with increased risks (GmeR), including PEP relationships
  • You answer case-specific VSB requests within the bank in connection with the opening and conduct of customer relationships
  • Perform name screenings including in-depth clarifications and corresponding further processing of the identified risks
  • You carry out clarifications both with our customer consultants and directly with the customers
  • You contribute to the optimization of processes at team and higher level

Requirements

  • 5 years: Compliance or comparable
  • Bachelor's degree (university, university of applied sciences): law, economics or comparable
  • You have good knowledge in the practical application of the VSB 20
  • An analytical and solution-oriented approach to the analysis of customer structures and transaction histories is expected, including KYC and consideration of name matching
  • You have experience in writing PEP assessments
  • Experience working with data sets, e.g. for Microsoft Excel-based analyses, as well as in the effective use of AI-based solutions
  • You are a team player and support your colleagues with your positive and motivated nature
  • in German (negotiation confident)
  • French (advantageous)
  • Italian (advantageous)

Cosa of friamo

  • Child care: up to CHF 300 per month per child to external child care
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Migros Bank: accounts, mortgages and financial services with preferential terms
  • Advantages of cadre: One week holidays per year more for cadre members
