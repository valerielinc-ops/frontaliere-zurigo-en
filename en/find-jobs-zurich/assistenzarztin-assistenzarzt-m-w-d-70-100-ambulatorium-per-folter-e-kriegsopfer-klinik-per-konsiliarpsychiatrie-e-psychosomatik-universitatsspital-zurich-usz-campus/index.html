Full description

The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and about 700 trainees are committed to the well-being of our patients every day.

Tasks

  • Under the supervision of the supervising physician, you will conduct dolmetscher-based investigations and treatments in our ambulatory for torture and war victims
  • you will participate in the consiliar syiatric service in the USZ including emergency station
  • participation in teaching and research is possible on request; we will also assist you in obtaining your dissertation
  • you have the opportunity to prepare expert reports

Requirements

  • You have a Swiss doctor's degree or equivalent with Mebeko recognition
  • You are in further education as a specialist title for Psychiatry and Psychotherapy
  • You value the cooperation in an interdisciplinary team
  • You are interested in transcultural and psychotraumatic relationships
  • Your German language skills in word and writing are very good (C1)

We offer you a versatile, interdisciplinary state-of-the-art activity at the interface of mental and somatic diseases and complaints. An appreciative working environment, family-friendly work models and a balanced work-life balance are a matter of course for us.

Our clinic has the qualification for psychiatry and psychotherapy, category A (3 years) outpatient, category C (2 years), for the main title in Consiliar and Liaisonpsychiatrie (2 years) and for the main title in psychosomatic and psychosocial medicine (SAPPM) (2 years).
