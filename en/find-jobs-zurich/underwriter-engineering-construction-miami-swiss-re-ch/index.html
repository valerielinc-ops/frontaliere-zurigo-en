Full description

At Swiss Re Corporate Solutions, we support businesses to thrive by providing innovative risk transfer solutions. As part of our Engineering & Construction (E&C) and Renewable Energy (RE) team, you will contribute to building a resilient and profitable portfolio across Latin America, working closely with experienced underwriters and cross-functional partners. This role is a great opportunity to start and grow your career in underwriting, gaining exposure to complex risks and the renewable energy sector while collaborating across a diverse, international environment. About the Role In this role, you will support the underwriting of Engineering & Construction and Renewable Energy risks across Latin America, contributing to high-quality decision-making and portfolio performance.

You will

  • Support underwriting activities for E&C + RE risks, including risk assessment, pricing and documentation
  • Collaborate with underwriting, operations and claims teams to ensure efficient deal execution
  • Ensure compliance with local regulatory and internal requirements across LatAm markets
  • Maintain accurate underwriting data and documentation in systems, ensuring high data quality
  • Monitor post-bind activities such as premium collection, claims data and portfolio follow-up
  • Contribute to process improvements and operational excellence initiatives

About the Team You will join the CorSo Wholesale Engineering & Construction team covering Latin America, part of the broader Ibero-America, Middle East & Africa division. Our team combines strong technical expertise with a collaborative and client-focused mindset. We work closely with brokers and clients across the region, supporting complex infrastructure and energy projects—including renewable energy—while driving sustainable and profitable growth.

About You

We are looking for a motivated early-career professional who is eager to learn, grow and build a career in underwriting.

You bring

  • A degree in Engineering (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Industrial or related field) or equivalent experience
  • Relevant experience in (re)insurance, underwriting, or a related technical/analytical role (preferred, not mandatory)
  • Strong analytical skills and interest in risk assessment and insurance concepts
  • High attention to detail and ability to manage data, documentation and processes effectively
  • A collaborative mindset and ability to work with diverse, cross-functional teams
  • Effective communication skills in English and Spanish

Why join our team? You’ll be part of a global company with strong local presence, where you can:

  • Learn from experienced underwriters and develop technical expertise
  • Gain exposure to international markets and complex risks
  • Contribute to the growth of renewable energy and infrastructure sectors in LatAm
  • Grow your career in a supportive, collaborative environment

About Swiss Re Swiss Re is one of the world’s leading providers of reinsurance, insurance and other forms of insurance-based risk transfer, working to make the world more resilient. We anticipate and manage a wide variety of risks, from natural catastrophes and climate change to cybercrime. Combining experience with creative thinking and cutting-edge expertise, we create new opportunities and solutions for our clients. This is possible thanks to the collaboration of more than 15,000 employees across the world.
