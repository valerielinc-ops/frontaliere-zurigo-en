Who we are

An established physiotherapy practice in Bülach with a motivated team, a modern infrastructure and a generous training area. We focus on people. -Our patients as well as our employees. Now we are looking for you: a personality who convinces professionally, likes to take on responsibility and wants to shape the future of Physio Neuhof with us.

Your mission with brain, heart and humor

  • You set the direction: You lead our physiotherapy team professionally and organizationally and have an open ear for your employees.
  • You ensure quality: You develop our therapy concepts further and ensure high-quality patient care.
  • You bring movement to the company: You optimize processes, recognize potential for improvement and further develop our training area.
  • You stay with the patient: You also treat yourself and use your professional strengths specifically.
  • You think beyond the treatment table: You bring in ideas, actively shape the further development of the practice and enjoy thinking entrepreneurially.

In short, we are not looking for someone who just wants a title on the office door, but someone who wants to make a difference together with us. You bring that with you

  • A diploma as a physiotherapist in HF/FH with corresponding SRK recognition.
  • Several years of professional experience and ideally experience in a management function.
  • Expertise in physiotherapy, active rehabilitation and training.
  • Joy in leading people, promoting them and achieving goals together.
  • Organizational talent, initiative and a clear, appreciative communication.
  • A healthy dose of humor and the ability to keep track even in hectic moments.
  • Entrepreneurial interest and desire to take on more responsibility in the long term.

You don't have to be able to do everything perfectly. Much more important to us is that you think, shape and fit us.

  • A perspective that goes beyond the employment contract: In the case of a mutual fit, there is explicitly the possibility of a later entrepreneurial participation in the practice.
