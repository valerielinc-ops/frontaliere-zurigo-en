Full description

Do you love structure in complex projects and want to actively shape transformation at Denner? With your PMO know-how, you bring teams together and make progress visible. Come join us in the Denner family!

Mansioni

  • Support program management in planning, controlling and monitoring a complex transformation program
  • Coordinate, prepare and professionally accompany workshops, meetings and steering appointments
  • Create and prepare management reports, status reports and decision bases appropriate to the addressee
  • Capture, maintain, evaluate project, budget and resource data and proactively monitor open tasks
  • Coordinate and support cooperation between project teams, departments and stakeholders

Requirements

  • Several years of experience in project management: in trade sector and/or computer science environment
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): in the commercial or business sector, further training in project management is advantageous
  • Methodological competence in project management (classic / agile, possibly IPMA Level D or equivalent certification)
  • Enjoy supporting and accompanying departments and project teams in coordination
  • Working independently, reliably and in a structured way and keeping an overview at all times
  • Prepare complex topics in a structured and comprehensible way
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • English (good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central place of work: In the casual Binz district in Zurich district 3 (2 minutes by foot from Zurich station Binz).
  • Flexible place of work / home office: One to two days a week working at home. Except in IT - we are even more flexible.
  • Mobile-flexible working model: Take your time - with part-time, unpaid leave (2 weeks / year) or a sabbatical.
  • Modern infrastructure: Freshly renovated with height-adjustable tables everywhere. Who wants to go to the home office? ?
  • Individual further education: internal training and financial support for meaningful external education and training.
  • Personal responsibility & freedom: plenty of room for your ideas and to make a difference together with us.
  • Holidays: 6 weeks holidays for all and even more days for service anniversaries.
