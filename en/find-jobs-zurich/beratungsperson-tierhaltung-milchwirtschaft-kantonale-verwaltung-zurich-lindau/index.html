We are looking for a committed personality for the Lindau site as consulting support in the Animal Farming & Dairy Industry team for a limited period of one year. Their tasks

  • Comprehensive technical advice to livestock holdings (focus on cattle, desirable small ruminants, pig or horse)
  • You actively network theory and practice: in doing so, you organize professional evenings, conferences and courses
  • You contribute the technical expertise for the conception and implementation of new consulting approaches

  • Experience in forage production is desirable
