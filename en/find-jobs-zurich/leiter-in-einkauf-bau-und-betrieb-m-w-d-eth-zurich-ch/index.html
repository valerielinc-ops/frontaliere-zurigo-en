Head of Purchasing Construction and Operation (m/f/d)

80%-100%, Zurich, permanent

Design procurement where research, innovation and social progress emerge.

ETH Zurich is one of the world's leading universities in research and teaching. The department Procurement Services VPIN is responsible for procurement for construction, infrastructure, operation and ICT – economically, legally and sustainably. To expand our purchasing organization, we are looking for a purchasing personality who leads a team and shapes purchasing around the construction and operation of our infrastructure.

Job description

  • You lead and develop your team. You will take over the personnel and professional management of a procurement group with two employees today. An expansion to three to five is planned. They set priorities and promote expertise and personal responsibility.
  • You are responsible for your product groups. This includes construction services, furniture and interior fittings, utilities, operation and maintenance of buildings and other infrastructure-related goods groups. They develop procurement strategies, pool needs and use competition, framework contracts and life cycle costs as a lever for profitability and sustainability.
  • They conduct procurement procedures according to BöB/VöB. This also includes procedures in the area of State Treaty (WTO-GPA). They are responsible for the process from clarification of needs via evaluation to preparation of the contract and accompany the conclusion of the contract. You ensure quality, transparency and legal compliance.
  • They shape contracts and negotiate. They carry out quotation adjustments and negotiations within the permitted limits, draw up contracts for the assigned product groups and develop supplier relationships together with the specialists.
  • They get involved early and make procurement more effective. They clarify requirements with the specialist departments and advise them on the procurement strategy. They drive standardization and digitalization, strengthen internal controls and control with the help of key figures. You work closely with the legal service, other internal stakeholders and the other procurement functions.

Profile

They combine commercial thinking with sound procurement practice and enjoyment of leadership and cooperation.

  • Graduate degree at FH, university or HF level in business administration, engineering, construction, supply chain management or a comparable discipline, supplemented by further training in purchasing or public procurement
  • At least six years of experience in strategic and operational purchasing, including several years in public procurement, ideally including self-managed procedures in the area of state contracts
  • Sound and practical knowledge of Swiss procurement law (BöB/VöB or IVöB)
  • First management experience or several years of project management experience with the will to develop as a leader
  • Experience in procurement strategy, negotiation and contract and supplier management
  • Sovereign appearance and assertiveness: Even with resistance, you remain objective and achieve sustainable solutions
  • Advantage: Experience with construction, building technology, facility management or energy procurement as well as knowledge of KBOB templates and SIA standards. Experienced shopping personalities from other areas are also welcome.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English, French is advantageous
  • Familiarity with digital purchasing and tendering tools
