Project Manager: Civil Engineering Your Contribution with Us

  • Deployment as site manager/project manager in the field of civil engineering
  • Carrying out work preparation and site organisation
  • Observing and directing the construction process as well as site result controls
  • Correct and timely quantity surveying and billing
  • Responsibility for cost, schedule and quality compliance
  • Supervision and coordination of employees
  • Participation and shaping of innovation topics in construction (e.g. BIM)

What Counts for Us

  • Completed basic training in construction with further training to become a certified site manager and/or engineer or equivalent qualification
  • Experience as a site manager in civil engineering
  • Cooperative approach and enjoyment of teamwork
  • Entrepreneurial action with high commitment
  • Proficient in the use of common IT systems
  • Strong sense of responsibility and doer qualities
