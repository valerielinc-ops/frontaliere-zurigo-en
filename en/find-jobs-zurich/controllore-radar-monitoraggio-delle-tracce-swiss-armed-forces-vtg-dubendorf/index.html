Full description

In a nutshell

At the Air Force's Air Defence Operations Centre, you control, monitor and optimize the automated generation of the air picture, thereby providing comprehensive support for military airspace surveillance to safeguard air sovereignty.

  • Start date: by agreement
  • Employment type: permanent
  • Reference no.: JRQ$540-20550
  • Place of work: military airfield, 8600 Dübendorf

This is how you can contribute

  • As the responsible quality assurance unit for flight paths, monitor and control the automated generation of the air picture
  • Assess ongoing operations, analyze malfunctions and develop the basis for optimizing the systems
  • Control and configure the radars to ensure optimal radar coverage and take measures to improve the air picture
  • Ensure that tasks are performed as part of a team in shift work 24 hrs / 365 days and, in some cases, in underground Air Force facilities
  • Support local specialists and the troops as a subject-matter expert and provide support in evaluating systems and in project organizations

What makes you unique

  • Completed technical or electronic training and several years of professional experience
  • Further training in a technical field is desirable
  • Strategic thinking and independent, responsible action
  • Active knowledge of a second official language and good command of English
  • Confident and independent use of digital work tools and applications (MS Office, SAP); competent handling of digital information and forms of communication, as well as a pronounced awareness of data protection, information security and the secure use of digital technologies
  • Fit for military service and liable for military service, and willing to perform periodic military service with the Air Force throughout the entire duration of employment

What we offer

  • Working for Switzerland We are committed to Switzerland's successful model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Diversity in practice Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and contribute different perspectives.
  • Health at the workplace We support and advise our employees on physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom The Operations Command (Kdo Op) is responsible for all military operations and deployments of the Swiss Armed Forces. The Kdo Op includes, among others, the Air Force, the Army, the four territorial divisions, the SWISSINT Competence Center, Special Forces Command and the Military Police. Around 40 % of the approx. 2'500 employees are employed as civilians – from engineers and aircraft mechanics to analysts.

The Swiss Armed Forces employ more than 9'000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations—and do so at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also catered for: the Swiss Armed Forces train apprentices in more than 30 occupations.
