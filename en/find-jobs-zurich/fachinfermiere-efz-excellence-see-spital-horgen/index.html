Full description

As of February 2026 or by agreement we are looking for a specialist health EFZ - Excellence 80-100%

Your responsibilities

  • Competent care and care: They take care of our patients with heart and expertise and work closely with an interprofessional treatment team.
  • Assuming responsibility: You help with the planning, implementation and documentation of care.
  • Collaboration on an equal footing: They exchange ideas and work closely with other professional groups in order to design the best possible treatment plan for our patients.

Your profile

  • Modern infrastructure and a working environment in which you can feel at home – lake views included.
