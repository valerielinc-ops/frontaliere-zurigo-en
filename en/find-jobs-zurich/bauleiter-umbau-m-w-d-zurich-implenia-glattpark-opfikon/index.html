Full description

For our Modernization department in the Zurich region, we are looking for you as a dedicated construction manager (m/f/d). Your location will be the Zurich region.

Tasks

What tasks await you as a construction manager conversion (m/f/d) Zurich?

  • You lead and coordinate renovation projects from tendering to commissioning, taking into account security, deadlines, quality and cost requirements
  • You are the hub on site and the first point of contact for subcontractors, suppliers, planners and authorities
  • You take on the role of contact person for builders, business partners and tenants
  • You have an open and respectful communication culture

Requirements

What requirements do you bring as a construction manager conversion (m/f/d) Zurich?

  • Completed training in the main construction industry with further training for the construction manager, technician or equivalent qualification
  • Several years of experience in the construction management of conversion projects, ideally in dealing with listed or protected objects
  • In-depth building expertise and secure application of scheduling software, preferably Elcosoft Powerproject
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing as well as strong communication and social skills
  • Entrepreneurial, goal-oriented and assertive personality with a high level of initiative, resilience and pleasure in cooperation in interdisciplinary teams

About Implenia

As a leading Swiss construction and real estate service provider, Implenia develops, realizes and manages living spaces, working environments and infrastructure for future generations in Switzerland and Germany. Implenia also offers tunnelling and related infrastructure projects in other markets. The company combines the know-how of highly qualified consulting, development, planning and execution units under one roof into an integrated, multinationally leading construction and real estate service provider. Headquartered in Opfikon near Zurich, Implenia employs more than 8,000 people (FTE) across Europe and achieved a turnover of CHF 3.5 billion in 2025. More information at implenia.com.
