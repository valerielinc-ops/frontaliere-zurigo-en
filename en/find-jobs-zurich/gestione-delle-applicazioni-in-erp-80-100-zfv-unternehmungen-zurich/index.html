Your area of activity

  • You ensure the stable, secure and user-oriented operation of Odoo as a central business application in the catering and goods management environment
  • You take over application support for Odoo, analyze disruptions, coordinate escalations and provide structured handling of recurring problems
  • You work closely with product owners, departments, IT, security and external partners and ensure clear communication between operations and business
  • You take bugs, requirements and suggestions for improvement in a structured manner, support their evaluation and accompany the implementation from an operational point of view
  • You coordinate updates, releases and maintenance windows and ensure testing, documentation and a controlled transfer to the company
  • You support data quality assurance, especially for master data, roles, permissions, workflows and cross-system data flows
  • You are responsible for the operational maintenance of roles, permissions and accesses within the defined security and compliance requirements
  • You create and maintain documentation, instructions and training materials and support users through targeted knowledge transfer
  • You support with license, contract and lifecycle management and provide the basis for a transparent management of costs and operating expenses.
  • Completed vocational training, higher technical school or studies in computer science, business informatics, business administration with IT or comparable qualification
  • Alternatively, several years of practical experience in application management, ERP environment, business application support or IT support
  • Further education in the field of application management, ITIL, ERP, process management or project management is advantageous
  • Experience in the operation and support of business applications, ideally in the ERP or goods management environment
  • Experience in cooperation with departments, IT, external service providers and software partners
  • Experience with testing, release management, documentation and incident management
  • Experience with role/authorisation concepts, master data, interfaces and data quality
  • Advantage: Experience in gastro and/or goods management
  • Experience with Odoo or comparable ERP systems or the willingness to quickly and deeply familiarize yourself with Odoo
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing, French is advantageous
  • Structured, reliable and solution-oriented working method
  • Strong service and user orientation
  • Technical understanding combined with process thinking
  • Joy of support, training and knowledge transfer
  • Clear communication with departments, IT and external partners
  • High quality standards and a sense of responsibility in the operation of critical applications
  • Resilience and tranquility in dealing with disruptions or mission-critical situations Our company: The cooperative ZFV-Unternehmenen (ZFV) creates together with partners inspiring meeting places, inspires with hospitality and authentic experiences.

Founded in 1894 by visionary Zürcher Bürgersfrauen, the ZFV still places special emphasis on innovation and sustainability.

In more than 200 companies throughout Switzerland, ZFV employees take care of the needs of customers and guests.

Activities include restaurants, canteens, staff restaurants, hotels, mobile catering solutions, catering, bakeries and daycare centres.

The cooperative actively shapes society and adopts a stance for the environment, equal opportunities and inclusion and lives hospitality for a society in which we all want to live.
