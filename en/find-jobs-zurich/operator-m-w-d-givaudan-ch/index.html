We are looking for a reliable and quality-conscious personality as Operator(m/f/d) for our Sampling Liquid Compounding department at our Dübendorf site. In this varied function, you produce liquid and powdery aromas with different production technologies, ensuring compliance with recipes, production regulations and quality and food safety requirements.

Your tasks

  • Production of liquid flavours as well as intermediate and final products by means of different technologies according to recipe and production regulations and in compliance with the given time, quantity and quality
  • Production of powdered flavors using various technologies such as spray dry, granulator and blends
  • Ensuring correct storage and booking of raw materials and intermediate and final products
  • Consistent implementation and compliance with food safety guidelines, including CCP points
  • Filling and provision of raw materials
  • Cleaning and cleaning the work area and all equipment used
  • Active and timely information to the competent bodies in the event of production deviations or other irregularities

Working hours are from Monday to Friday in 2-shift operation. The early shift lasts from 06:00 to 14:00, the late shift from 14:00 to 22:00.

Your profile

  • Completed teaching in the food industry or chemistry
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Good English skills are beneficial
  • Experience in occupational hygiene and food safety
  • Good computer user knowledge
  • Careful, reliable and quality-conscious work method
  • Team-oriented and responsible personality
