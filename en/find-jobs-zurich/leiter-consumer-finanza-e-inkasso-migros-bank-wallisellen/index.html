Full description

With us you can make a difference, influence and actively shape the future. We are looking for you: a personality who thinks ahead, inspires with fresh ideas and acts across boundaries. Someone who passionately brings teams and the organization forward. Take responsibility, drive forward projects and develop the bank together with us.

Mansioni

  • You are responsible for the human and professional management of the area Consumer Finance and Debt Collection Management (Teams: Personal Credit, Leasing and Debt Collection Management)
  • You ensure efficient and effective processing and support sales in planning and ensuring goal achievement
  • Together with your management team, you develop our internal processes, ensure the regulatory requirements and drive digitalization with our value streams
  • You represent Migros Bank in the topic of consumer finance and debt collection management against the outside, and within Migros Bank
  • As part of the management team Banking Operations, you optimize centralized processing in the sense of our customers and our strategy
  • You will continue to develop our internal process control with the aim of further improving transparency in terms of quality, efficiency and utilization.

Requirements

  • over 10 years: personal loan and leasing experience and management experience
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): Business administration, marketing, banking or finance
  • You have strong leadership skills (ideally over 2 levels)
  • You bring a high level of social competence and a strong solution orientation
  • High load capacity, flexibility and reliability are not foreign words for you
  • You bring a strong customer orientation as well as convincing communication skills
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • French (advantageous)
  • Italian (advantageous)
  • English (advantageous)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Advantages of cadre: One week holidays per year more for cadre members
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Child care: up to CHF 300 per month per child to external child care
  • Migros Bank: accounts, mortgages and financial services with preferential terms
  • Coaching: Personal coach for executives
