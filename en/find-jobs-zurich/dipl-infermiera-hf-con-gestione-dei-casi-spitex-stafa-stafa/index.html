Full description

Work where others go on holiday! Dipl. Nurse HF with case management Can you imagine working in a small team without a direct leader? We have converted our organizational form to semi-autonomous teams, that is, we are guided by the famous Buurtzorg model, but have adapted it to our needs. In an interdisciplinary team, with case managers, FaGes and housekeeping and support staff at your side, it should be a new form of work that is up-to-date and allows for the necessary flexibility, but also structure and continuity.

You have the following qualifications

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nurse HF or equivalent training
  • Ideally Spitex experience
  • Strong professional and social skills
  • self-employed and responsible working practices
  • valid category B ticket

Your tasks: As a competent care specialist, you take on responsibility in the care and support of our clients at home, including preventive support. You carry out care services independently. As a communicative personality, it is also easy for you to actively cooperate and exchange information with the case leaders, FaGes and the support team.

We offer you

  • a varied, responsible and independent task in an innovative organisation;
  • flexible conditions of employment;
  • 5 weeks of holidays (from 50 years of age 6 weeks);
  • very good social benefits (e.g. pension fund contribution 60:40);
  • A motivating and appreciative working environment.

Job opening: by immediate or agreement Pensum: in agreement Do you have any questions? Call us. Do you feel addressed? We look forward to your application.

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
