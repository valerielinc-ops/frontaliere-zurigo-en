2027 Internship – Tech – Language engineer and terminologist – Zurich — UBS

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Your role Interested in working in finance, specifically in shaping the agentic workflows that underpin the UX localization department? Do you want to work for a firm with a truly global footprint?

We're looking for ambitious students to join our 2027 Internship Program within our Group Technology.

You’ll get to

  • shape, prototype and test the next generation of agents and agentic workflows for language and localization
  • work across the entire agent lifecycle, from experimentation and validation to deployment, improvement and eventual sunsetting
  • explore how we can effectively test and evaluate AI-powered workflows across languages
  • work alongside UX writers, localization professionals, designers, engineers and business stakeholders to find new ways to augment and scale their expertise
  • help evolve our multilingual terminology database and explore how terminology can support both people and AI-powered workflows
  • experiment with emerging technologies and turn new ideas into working prototypes

Your role will focus primarily on language engineering (around 60%) and terminology management (around 30%), and will be complemented by work involving language tools, design tools, and innovation initiatives.

Your team Join the software localization team in Zurich and help shape our world-class multilingual user experience on UBS Digital Banking. We are a team of passionate language experts dedicated to making UBS products and services accessible, intuitive and engaging across languages and markets. Through our work, we help users feel confident and supported, ensuring every interaction is as seamless and meaningful as possible, regardless of the language they choose.

Your expertise

We’re looking for a candidate who

  • has completed at least 4 semesters of a bachelor's degree, and graduated no longer than 12 months ago, preferably in translation or a related field
  • has a competitive minimum GPA of 4.5 according to the Swiss grading system
  • has values that align with ours: hard-working, trustworthy, dedicated and collaborative
  • is a strategic thinker with strong communication skills
  • is motivated to work in a business with high demands and tight deadlines
  • can evidence how experimental and responsible use of AI can improve outcomes and efficiency
  • has excellent English and German skills (native or near-native proficiency). Additional language skills, such as French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese or Chinese are a plus
  • considers themselves a “language person”
  • can critically evaluate content to ensure it is accurate, engaging, and fit for purpose
  • can critically evaluate content to distinguish meaningful information from filler
  • is sensitive to the needs of audiences from diverse cultural and linguistic backgrounds
  • has an interest in multilingual terminology and terminology database management
  • has experience with CAT tools and AI-powered language tools
  • is committed to staying up to date with the latest developments in artificial intelligence and best practices in post-editing and AI-assisted terminology management
  • enjoys working in a dynamic environment where they can actively contribute to shaping and improving our processes
  • enjoys working in an international team
  • is curious, enjoys asking questions and brings forward new and innovative ideas
  • can quickly grasp new and complex subject areas and concepts
  • is eager to learn and open to receiving and implementing feedback
  • has programming skills (a plus)
  • has experience working with AI tools in an IDE (a plus)

Start date: 1 September 2027

Your program This paid internship will last 12-months. You’ll work with our experts and learn about the industry, our clients and our firm. You’ll be right at the heart of our business, learning from your colleagues, taking part in day-to-day operations. You'll be given mentors along the way and will have the chance to get to know some of the most senior members of our teams. You’ll have plenty of opportunities to develop new skills and make contacts along the way. If you like challenges, enjoy learning and want to work where no two days are the same, this program is for you.

If you have a successful internship, you may get an offer to return for our Graduate Talent Program after you've completed your studies.
