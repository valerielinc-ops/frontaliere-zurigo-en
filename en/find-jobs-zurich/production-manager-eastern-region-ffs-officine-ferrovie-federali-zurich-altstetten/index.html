Production Manager: in East Region 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Production Manager: in Region Ost Zurich Altstetten / Work Smart, 01.02.2027 or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

From the Zurich Altstetten location, I-AEP realizes interesting and extensive construction projects in the Zurich and Eastern Switzerland area within a complex transport environment. You are part of the management & coordination and make an important contribution to successful implementation and a positive customer perception. Our team is diverse. You benefit from expertise in construction management, project management, railway operations and portfolio management as well as from a strong network in project planning and execution. We offer you a supportive environment that opens up numerous opportunities for further education and personal development. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • As a production manager, you manage the construction coordination and the optimization of infrastructure projects in your planning area across projects and coordinate them with each other. You develop with your partners the best solutions from the point of view of the overall system.
  • For new projects, you are the gateway on the creator side. With the internal and external partners, you define the production framework conditions (resource availability, logistics concepts, barrier concepts, construction procedures).
  • In your perimeter, you have an eye on the needs and available resources and take action in the event of conflicts. You will also develop decision bases for prioritizations and project changes.
  • You manage the risk of your production risks and ensure the implementation of your concepts.
  • After several years of successful cooperation, this role offers various perspectives in project business or in railway planning (EVU, Infra).

You bring that with you.

  • Through your persuasion and assertiveness, you communicate purposefully and step-by-step with various contacts.
  • It is easy for you to make decisions yourself, prepare and carry out meetings.
  • Experience in construction management / execution project planning in the area of railways or in project management railway construction is mandatory.
  • You have a technical education or study with professional experience and can quickly identify complex situations with your networked thinking.
  • You are flexible and use the possibility of “Work Smart” to work from home or on the road. You are also flexible in working together in changing project teams.
  • You are happy to communicate safely in German (C1) and ideally have additional knowledge in a second national language.

Your reward and benefits.
