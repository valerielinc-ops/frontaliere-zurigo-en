Full description

Welcome to Medbase Zürich Löwenstrasse

In the heart of Zurich, our modern Medical Center provides interdisciplinary and quality-oriented care for patients on an area of around 700 m2. Around 40 employees from the disciplines of general internal medicine, sports medicine, orthopaedic surgery and cardiology, physiotherapy, performance diagnostics, nutritional counselling and osteopathy work together interprofessionally. Our Medical Center is certified by Swiss Olympic in the field of sports medicine. Our flexible working models and deputy solutions enable a good work-life balance.

You can move that.

As a specialist you offer comprehensive care and ensure a high quality of patient care. At Medbase, you are part of an interdisciplinary team that supports you in your professional development and sets high standards.

  • Self-responsible care and basic care of family doctor patients
  • Ensuring the best possible medical care
  • Collaboration in the team with collegial representation
  • Support for the further development of practical offers
  • Internal and external training for quality improvement

What you bring

  • Completed studies and specialist training for PMR, orthopaedic surgery or rheumatology
  • Sports Medicine SEMS completed or near completion (Jan. 2027)
  • Commitment to holistic patient care
  • Solution-oriented and team-capable working method
  • Familiarity with electronic medical history
  • Communication Strength and Empathy
  • Good knowledge of German (min. B2)
