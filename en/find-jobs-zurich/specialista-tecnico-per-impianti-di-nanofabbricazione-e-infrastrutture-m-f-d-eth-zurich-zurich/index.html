Technical:r Specialist: in nanofabrication plants and infrastructure (m/f/d)

100%, Zurich, perpetual

The Binnig and Rohrer Nanotechnology Center (BRNC) at ETH Zurich is a state-of-the-art research infrastructure for nanosciences and nanotechnology. We provide advanced technologies, infrastructure and expertise for research at the highest level. Our operating team supervises more than 1,000 m2 of cleanroom and laboratory space for semiconductor and nanofabrication research and supports forward-looking R&D projects by researchers at ETH Zurich as well as partners from science, industry and the start-up landscape.

In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a practical and service-oriented personality as a specialist for nanofabrication plants and infrastructure. As a central technical contact person for plant maintenance, general cleanroom infrastructure and operational systems, you contribute significantly to the safe, reliable and efficient operation of the BRNC.

This varied position combines technical troubleshooting, maintenance, safety, documentation and digital processes with daily cleanroom operations. It is suitable for a person who likes to understand complex facilities, solve practical challenges systematically and actively participate in the technical development of a versatile research infrastructure.

Job description

  • Technical Support & Troubleshooting: You are the central technical contact for the BRNC operations team. They analyse and correct mechanical, electrical, vacuum, pneumatic and other technical failures to minimise downtime and ensure reliable operation.
  • Plant Responsibility, Maintenance & Installation: You will assume technical responsibility for selected nanofabrication and measurement plants and develop the necessary knowledge for the associated processes. They plan and carry out preventive maintenance, repairs, upgrades and technical optimizations and support the installation and commissioning of new systems in cooperation with suppliers and ETH specialists.
  • Infrastructure: They support the operation, maintenance and continuous improvement of the common cleanroom infrastructure, including vacuum and ultra-pure water systems, technical facilities, laboratory installations and other building-wide systems.
  • Security Strategy & Operation: You act as the central point of contact for security issues within the BRNC and participate in the continuous development of security procedures. They assume operational and administrative tasks in the areas of chemicals, gases, safety facilities and cleanroom operation.
  • Documentation & Digital Processes: You maintain technical documentation, maintenance plans, spare parts warehouses, plant data and operational databases. You also support the development, integration and reliable data backup of digital tools and site-wide workflows.
  • Team support & continuous improvement: You support researchers and colleagues in the safe and reliable operation of the plants and contribute to technical problem solving and training of users. They proactively improve the reliability of plants, maintenance processes and operational processes and assume new responsibilities according to the further development of the research infrastructure.

Profile

  • Completed technical vocational training, advanced training at higher level advantageous, university degree or comparable qualification in electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, materials engineering, chemistry, microtechnology or a related technical field.
  • At least three years of practical professional experience in plant maintenance, technical service, infrastructure operations or an equivalent technical environment. They have good broad mechanical, electrical and general technical knowledge. They take a systematic and practice-oriented approach to troubleshooting, repair and maintenance of complex plants and infrastructure. Experience with cleanroom infrastructure, vacuum systems or experience in semiconductor manufacturing are advantageous.
  • High safety awareness as well as the willingness to assume operational and administrative responsibility in dealing with chemicals, gases and safety facilities.
  • Secure use of modern IT tools and technical databases as well as interest in getting to know and introducing new digital solutions.
  • High initiative, creativity, willingness to learn and solution orientation, combined with a strong sense of responsibility.
  • Strong teamwork and service orientation as well as the motivation to act as a reliable technical contact person and to proactively support colleagues and users of the infrastructure.
  • Openness to new technologies, diverse tasks and changing requirements in a dynamic research environment. Good knowledge of German and English.

We offer

  • Working in a world-class environment: Work at one of the world’s leading universities in science and engineering with state-of-the-art infrastructure in nanofabrication.
  • Meaningful activity: Make a direct contribution to groundbreaking research and development projects in science and industry.
  • Variety & personal responsibility: Take responsibility for facilities, infrastructure and technical processes and actively shape the future development of the research infrastructure.
  • Continuous development: Expand your expertise across different technologies and benefit from attractive training opportunities.
