Full description

Are you someone who thrives in a fast-paced environment, loves solving complex problems, and is passionate about delivering outstanding service? About the Role As a Business Services Associate, you'll be at the heart of our agricultural insurance operations — ensuring that policies are issued accurately, processes run smoothly, and our customers receive the best possible experience. This is a role where your attention to detail, proactive mindset, and collaborative spirit will make a real difference every single day. You'll work across a variety of operational and cross-functional activities, contributing to process improvements, systemic development, and strategic projects — all while building deep expertise in the rural insurance space. Key Responsibilities

  • Issue policies and endorsements accurately and in line with regulatory and compliance standards
  • Manage operational processes and internal controls, ensuring compliance with governance frameworks
  • Handle complex, non-standardized processes, triaging priorities and delivering effective solutions
  • Collaborate cross-functionally to analyze workflows and propose meaningful improvements
  • Represent Operations in projects, meetings, and development programs
  • Support systems development by defining business requirements and participating in testing activities

About the Team The Business Services Agro Team is a group of collaborative, driven professionals united by a shared commitment to operational excellence and customer-centric solutions. The team is constantly evolving — making quick, informed decisions, navigating everyday challenges with simplicity, and taking full ownership in the face of obstacles. We believe in creating a friendly yet challenging environment — one that is focused on delivering results, but equally on enjoying the process and growing together. If you value a culture where people support each other and celebrate progress, you'll feel right at home here.

About You

You are a curious, motivated professional who thrives in dynamic environments. You take initiative, collaborate naturally across teams, and bring a clear, solutions-focused mindset to every challenge. You simplify complexity, communicate with confidence, and always look for smarter ways to get things done.

We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements

  • 1–3 years of experience in insurance, customer service, operations, or underwriting
  • Knowledge of Rural/Agricultural Insurance — familiarity with products and the regulatory environment
  • Proficiency in the Office Package — Excel, Word, and Outlook
  • Experience with Brokers and/or Insurance Companies
  • General knowledge across insurance disciplines — in operations or underwriting
  • Intermediate English

These are additional nice to haves

  • Power BI experience for data visualization and reporting
  • Customer service orientation — commitment to internal and external stakeholder needs
  • Academic background in Business Administration, Accounting, Insurance Management, or Actuarial Science
  • Analytical mindset — ability to assess situations and propose practical improvements
  • Adaptability — comfortable managing shifting priorities with a positive attitude

Our company has a hybrid work model where the expectation is that you will be in the office at least three days per week. Please submit your CV in English.
