Full description

University Hospital Balgrist, Center for Paraplegia, Research Neuro-Urology

Job opening immediately or by arrangement, temporary

Our offer

  • Interesting, versatile tasks in a highly specialized team
  • Active participation in patient care and exciting national and international studies
  • Varied activities in a dynamic environment that combines clinical practice and research.
  • Targeted introduction to the tasks as well as regular internal and external training
  • A supportive and interdisciplinary team.
  • Regular working hours (no night work)
  • Various employee offers

Tasks

  • Implementation of study-specific and nursing tasks (blood sampling, neuro-urological measurements, continuous blood pressure measurements, etc.) in the technical-medical field
  • Assistance in and organisation of clinical trials (including at other sites), including preparation of ethics proposals
  • Care of study patients, as well as promotion of cooperative, interprofessional cooperation with clinic, research and administration
  • Collection of study-specific data according to good clinical practice (GCP) with correct patient documentation (KISIM, REDCap)
  • Continuous development and assurance of research and care quality at the interface between research and care
  • Contact persons for patients, study monitors, sponsors and international collaborators
  • Presentations and presentations of current research projects and results at international conferences in English

Profile

  • Dipl. Care professional HF/FH or equivalent
  • Professional experience as a study nurse, preferably with a Master of Science and CAS Study Nurse
  • Very good knowledge of German and English (B2)
  • Own initiative and willingness to become familiar with a specialized and very technical area and to always develop professionally and personally
  • Flexible and resilient personality with high social skills and patience with neurological patients
  • Responsibility and quality awareness, decision-making capacity, consensus capacity
  • Independent work, flair for technology, good organizational skills, experienced PC user knowledge (Word, Excel, KIS, research databases)
  • Interest in scientific issues and interdisciplinary cooperation
