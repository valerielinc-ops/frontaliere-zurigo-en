Full description

Does your heart beat for older people? Is the nursing profession your calling? Do you see opportunities to meaningfully contribute your skills and experience to us in order to have a positive impact on the lives of our customers? Then you are a perfect fit for us!

We are looking for someone for the house Tertianum Im Vieri in the Zurich region immediately or by appointment

Nursing assistant / nursing assistant SRK 80-100%

Your tasks

  • Supervision and support of assigned guests
  • Support with personal care, exercise and other everyday activities. Offering our guests everyday activities with creative activities, walks, etc.
  • Taking on tasks as a living reference person according to instructions
  • Participation in nursing reports and forwarding of important observations Writing nursing entries in our nursing documentation system CareCoach (mobile possible with CareCoach smartphone)
  • Assistance with logistical and housekeeping activities

Your profile

  • Completed SRK nursing course with completed internship in long-term care
  • Professional experience in long-term care is an advantage
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoy diverse challenges
  • Knowledge of spoken and written German

Why Tertianum? It is important to us that our employees can easily combine their professional and private lives. This includes a wide range of different benefits, e.g.:

  • Internal and external training opportunities
  • Additional day off in the birthday month
  • Daily lunch for CHF 10
  • Discounted mobile and internet subscriptions with Sunrise
  • Discounts on selected supplementary health insurance policies
  • SBB half-fare subscription
  • Various fleet discounts

You can find further benefits on our website.

Are you interested? We offer you an exciting perspective: With us you can use your skills and specialist knowledge in a motivated and flexible team. You take responsibility and help shape the future of Tertianum. Apply now and become part of our team.
