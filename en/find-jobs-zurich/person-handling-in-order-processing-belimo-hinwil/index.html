Original description

Do you keep track even when many threads come together? Then you are exactly right with us. With your organizational talent and your customer orientation, you ensure that our customer orders are processed smoothly and on time.

What will you do

  • You ensure that our customer orders are processed smoothly and on time from incoming orders to delivery.
  • You are the central contact person for our customers and sales companies and find competent solutions for orders, delivery dates and logistics.
  • Together with production, purchasing and logistics You ensure that materials are available and our delivery commitments are met.
  • You also keep track of challenging situations, communicate proactively in the event of scheduling deviations and coordinate suitable solutions.
  • You handle complaints, returns and credits and contribute to a high customer satisfaction.
  • You maintain master data in the SAP/ERP system and ensure high data quality.
  • You analyze key figures such as delivery deadlines and quality and identify potential for improvement.
  • You bring your ideas and actively shape the further development of our processes.

Who you are

  • You have a completed commercial or technical training – ideally supplemented by a corresponding further education.
  • You bring several years of experience in order processing, customer service or supply chain management of a manufacturing company.
  • You work securely with SAP (especially in order processing and master data management) as well as with the MS Office applications.
  • You communicate stylishly in German and well in English – experience in an international environment is a plus.
  • You think analytically, have a good feeling for key figures and are interested in efficient processes.
  • You convince with a high customer orientation, a clear communication and self-initiative.
  • You work in a structured, independent and solution-oriented manner and keep an overview even in a dynamic environment.

Who we are

At Belimo, we are proud to make our company a rewarding place to work. Our leaders are value-driven and promote new ideas in their teams around the world. Through our passion, our employees not only create amazing products, but also revolutionize the HVAC industry. Investing in our employees is at the heart of Belimo when it comes to engaging diverse creative talents to build an industry-leading company. The success of our corporate brand is based on the competence and commitment of each individual.
