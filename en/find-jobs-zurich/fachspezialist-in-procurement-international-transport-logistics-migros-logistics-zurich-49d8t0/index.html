Full description

We deliver! Migros Logistics brings together Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (MVB) and Migros Logistique Romandie SA (MLR) to supply Switzerland every day. Modern technologies and highly automated processes shape the logistics of tomorrow. Start with us into the future of logistics as a specialist in Procurement International Transport Logistics at 80 - 100%.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Logistics, Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (Zurich), Limmatstrasse 152, 8005 Zurich

Mansioni

  • You are responsible for important work in the area of international purchasing of transport services on all transport modalities and independently take on negotiations and analyses.
  • Independently, you carry out international tenders, award them competently and present them to decision-makers. Operational/customs and foreign trade requirements are important aspects.
  • With your high level of service orientation and analytical skills, you supervise and advise experts and decision-makers of the Migros Group on complex transport topics.
  • Operational reporting for Transport Partner negotiations is one of your tasks. You will continuously gain knowledge of the current market conditions and risks and inform internal offices about them.

Requirements

  • Professional experience of several years: In the international logistics/forwarding sector
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): basic commercial education and a completed further education at FH level e.g. in the field of logistics, supply chain management or business administration
  • Self-employment, a sense of responsibility and the motivation to actively shape things are among your strengths.
  • Very good IT user knowledge (MS Office, very good Excel knowledge) is mandatory.
  • In addition, you will be characterized by analytical thinking, organizational talent and a solution- and result-oriented working method.
  • As a team player, you enjoy working in a dynamic logistics environment and actively participate in the team.
  • German (flowing)
  • English (negotiable)
  • French (good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Central location: Our locations are directly connected to the motorway or public transport
  • Modern infrastructure: Our logistics work with fully automated, state-of-the-art facilities. Office workplaces meet the latest standards
  • Employee offers: We offer our employees many special services and discounts (e.g. fuel, mobile subscriptions)
  • Training and further education: We support our employees through targeted training and further education offers and support them generously
  • Development opportunities: We offer individual support programs and task hours. For most professions, you can complete a multi-week stage in another company.
