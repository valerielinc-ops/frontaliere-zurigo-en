Do you want to do a sustainable apprenticeship? You are curious, cosmopolitan, want to make a difference and grow personally at the same time? Want to be on the pulse of time? Then you are exactly right at ETH Zurich, because this is where the future arises.

Job description

An ideal teaching for solution finders: insiders with a pronounced spatial vision and enjoyment of technology and precision!

In this exciting training, you will carry out the following activities:

  • Planning, development and construction of various components, mechanical assemblies and systems for research and teaching
  • Creating three-dimensional design drawings on the computer with CAD programs
  • Preparation of production documents and documentation
  • Vacuum and precision engineering work

The training lasts 4 years, of which 6 months external basic training in the RAU (Regional Training Centre Au).

Profile

You love to develop new things, tinker with problems and are interested in different materials with their physical and mechanical properties. Your good technical understanding, your technical sketching skills, your creativity and your exact and careful working methods will help you find solutions.

For this vocational apprenticeship you need a mandatory school degree and good performance in the following school subjects:

  • Geometry
  • Mathematics
  • Physics
