Full description

Their tasks include

  • Reception of patients of the outpatient consultation as well as from the inpatient area, measurement of vital parameters, 12-channel ECG writing and preparation for studies of cardiology and angiology
  • Independent performance of ergometry
  • Sedation and care of patients during TEE and ECV
  • Preparation and implementation of long-term ECGs, 24h BD measurements and oscillographies (angiological examination)
  • Evaluation of Holter ECGs and 24h-BD
  • Applying compression bandages if required
  • Implementation and follow-up of administrative and logistical work of the ambulatory
  • Ordering and management of food, material and medicine requirements
  • Completed training as a Dipl. Care specialist or FaGe EFZ in acute care
  • Experience in cardiology is beneficial
  • Possibility of using Di, Do and Fr
  • Reliability and loyalty, excellent manners and open and respectful communication with patients, relatives and referees
