Full description

For Döltschi School, we are looking for a temporary relief substitute starting immediately or by agreement.

Responsibilities

  • Opening and closing, as well as supervising the school premises, including the gymnasium
  • Cleaning work in indoor and outdoor areas, assisting with interim and deep cleaning
  • Checking the cleanliness and condition of the equipment and infrastructure
  • Inspection rounds and maintenance work in outdoor areas (removing leaves, clearing snow, spreading salt)
  • Setting up and dismantling tables and chairs before or after events
  • Flexible deployment on special assignments as instructed

Profile

  • Very good German language skills
  • Enjoyment of working with people
  • Cleaning skills and a sense of order and cleanliness
  • Good physical condition
  • Reliability, sense of responsibility, independence and trustworthiness
  • Willingness to take on special assignments

We offer you a professional onboarding process and predictable work assignments. Our employees also benefit from the attractive employment conditions offered by the City of Zurich. A motivated and open-minded team looks forward to receiving your application!
