Full description

We are looking for 1. November 2026 or by arrangement a suitable personality as a social pedagogue, FaBe or school assistant.

The school Kappeli Primar is a QUIMS school (quality in multicultural schools) in Kreis 9, Zurich Altstetten. She attaches great importance to promoting the sense of community and sees the cultural diversity of her students as an enrichment. The school is centrally located near Lindenplatz in the heart of Altstetten. In addition, a children's playground offers both during school and outside a popular meeting place for children to play and stay. Currently, Kappeli Primar School consists of 11 kindergartens and 19 primary classes. We have four nurseries, three primary nurseries and a morning table.

Tasks

  • Ensure child supervision and safety
  • Accompany and support the children in everyday life
  • Encourage children to participate in the various offers and activities
  • Supporting children in conflicts and helping them to find solutions together
  • Perceiving and responding appropriately to children’s needs and behaviour
  • Share relevant observations and feedback with the care team
  • Active and reliable cooperation with the existing support team

They support the care team during three days over the noon (Mo, Di and Do from 11:00 to 14:00) in the child care. We work with a supply structure. This means that in different rooms and outdoor areas, children have different activities and opportunities for free play.

Profile

We look forward to a person with a high sense of responsibility, creativity and a positive human image. Good cooperation skills, resilience and flexibility in thinking and acting are important prerequisites for this work. You have a socio-educational degree (FH, HF) or an equivalent education or a teaching degree as FaBe or without a degree but with work experience with children.

We offer

We offer you an interesting and versatile job at a dynamic school and in an experienced team, support by the management supervision, as well as progressive employment conditions and a remuneration according to the guidelines of the city of Zurich.

About us

The Zurich-Letzi school district comprises Stadtkreis 9 with the districts Albisrieden, Altstetten and Grünau. In the 16 schools of our rapidly growing school circle, more than 5,000 primary school students are taught and supervised. More than 1,200 employees work for them in our schools in the areas of teaching, care, home service and technology as well as administration. They all work daily for the interests of the students and a smooth school day.
