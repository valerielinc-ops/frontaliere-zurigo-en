Full description

The bicycle railways are a central component for a secure, continuous and attractive bicycle network in the Canton of Zurich. You helped shape this project from the beginning. Do you keep track of complex infrastructure projects, like to think strategically and bring people with different perspectives together? Together with municipalities, specialists and other stakeholders, you will develop viable solutions for the mobility of tomorrow. In this function, you control and coordinate the planning and implementation of the various project sections. You create orientation, keep an eye on the overall picture and bring the concerns of the TBA into the cooperation with internal and external partners and stakeholders. You can expect a versatile project portfolio with great creative leeway and high visibility. Your tasks

  • You set up and manage the project organizations for the implementation of the bicycle tracks.
  • You control the cycling projects overridingly and ensure compliance with quality, costs and deadlines.
  • You represent the TBA vis-à-vis municipalities, other cantonal authorities and private third parties.
  • You lead preliminary studies in individual sections and develop decision-making bases. Your profile
  • You have a university degree related to civil engineering or a related subject.
  • You have experience in complex infrastructure projects and are interested in the further development of cycling.
  • You communicate openly, think in a network and develop viable solutions together with different stakeholders.
  • You also keep an eye on the overall picture in challenging situations and take responsibility.
  • You are enthusiastic about sustainable mobility, like to help shape new things and want to promote the bicycle tracks together with others. We are looking forward to you because...
  • you want to actively shape sustainable mobility in the Canton of Zurich.
  • a family-friendly working environment is important to you and you carry the associated mutual support in the team.
  • you value short distances and a direct exchange within the team. Diversity strengthens our teams. We are interested in your career and perspective – even if you do not fully meet all the requirements mentioned.
