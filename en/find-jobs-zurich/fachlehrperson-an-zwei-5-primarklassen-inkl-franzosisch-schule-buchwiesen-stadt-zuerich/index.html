Full description

The School Buchwiesen is looking for reinforcements as of January 4, 2027.

Tasks

Pensum: Specialist teacher at two 5th primary classes: about 54%, 15 WL Lesson days: Tuesday morning, Wednesday morning, Thursday Subjects: F, NMG, MA, BS, MI, MU, RKE (including 6 weekly lessons in French, 2 weekly lessons in team teaching and a weekly lesson in KlaMu with two music teachers)

Profile

  • Teaching diploma for the primary level, recognized in Switzerland
  • Enjoy teaching with students from different cultures
  • Strong skills in classroom management
  • Willingness and motivation to work in an integrated setting, together with curative teachers and assistants
  • Positive attitude, teamwork, resilience, high social skills

We offer

We offer you

  • Many subjects that can be taught identically in both classes
  • Two great and committed job partners
  • a well-organized school that has been professionally run for many years
  • an open-minded, committed team that fosters supportive collaboration in the Pedagogical Team
  • Additional offers for teaching (ateliers, stay in school, assistants)
  • Educational KITS support and school management secretariat on site
  • free use of public transport and cultural offers with class (in the city of Zurich)
  • a workplace well accessible by public transport with lots of greenery in the area

About us

We are a large school community in Zurich-Nord with 19 schools. We derive our pedagogical attitude from the concept of New Authority and thus meet the daily challenges in everyday school life together and effectively.

School time is life time! Under this motto, we design a living space inspiring for the children at the primary school Buchwiesen. Children's participation and participation are important pillars of our coexistence. With free and individualizing learning opportunities, we promote the natural learning pleasure of the children. As a QUIMS school for many years, we have great experience in our multicultural environment. We are a modern, innovative school with 15 primary classes, 2 kindergartens and 4 hoards.
