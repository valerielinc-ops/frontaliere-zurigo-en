  • Nearly 280 employees support, support, look after and employ people with disabilities in their living, working and living space. For this purpose, we offer 148 residential places in various forms of living as well as 175 workplaces in workshops and a service company. In addition, 90 seats are available in the day care center. As of December 1, 2026 or by agreement, we are looking for a dedicated successor for our ICT Support (1st Level) 100% You are the first point of contact for our users, ensure the smooth operation of the workstations and support us in the transition to a modern Microsoft 365 environment. Your responsibilities
  • First point of contact for users, by telephone, via the ticket system and on site
  • Capture, fix and document disruptions and service requests in the 1st level
  • Installation, configuration and maintenance of devices such as PCs, printers and mobile devices
  • Support for users in Microsoft 365 and our specialist applications
  • Maintenance of ICT inventory and documentation
  • Participation in rollouts and in the transition phase to a modern IT environment
  • Cooperation with our external ICT partner
  • Guidance and support for clients working in the ICT sector
  • Completed training as computer science EFZ or comparable experience in ICT support
  • Experience in 1st-level support
  • Good knowledge of Windows clients, Microsoft 365 and the basics of networks
  • Service orientation and the ability to explain IT in an understandable way
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • High social and communication skills
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Agogic background or experience accompanying people in need of support is beneficial
  • Interest in working in a social institution
  • Category B driving licence for operations at our locations in the region

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
