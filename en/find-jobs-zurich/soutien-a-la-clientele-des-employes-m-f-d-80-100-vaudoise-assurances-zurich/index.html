Full description

Vaudoise is one of the leading Swiss insurance companies and an attractive and future-oriented employer. In the spirit of its cooperative identity and values, Vaudoise creates a healthy, inclusive and inspiring working environment in which you can develop. In our general agency in Zurich, we live daily the values of proximity, proactivity, humanity and trustworthiness.

We do not only see ourselves as an insurance provider, but also as a partner of our customers on their way to financial security and well-being. To meet this requirement, we are looking for an employee in customer support who lives these values and supports our customers with commitment and cordiality.

Tasks The following exciting tasks await you

  • Support of our employees in customer support
  • Preparation of offers in the non-life industries in the retail business
  • Processing of claims
  • Handling of the resulting correspondence
  • Operation of the insurance partner and its customers on the telephone

Requirements This distinguishes you

  • Completed training in the insurance industry with experience in the commercial field
  • Good knowledge of the insurance agency channel of advantage
  • Stylish German in word and writing
  • Sales and customer-oriented
  • Self-employed working and flexibility
  • Communicative and open personality

We offer In return, we offer you

  • A pleasant working environment in a cooperative company that has been selected as the best employer on several occasions
  • Numerous social benefits, including flexibility in work organization and various employee discounts
  • A dynamic team
  • The well-being of employees as a first priority

Contact We look forward to receiving your complete application documents electronically (with CV, motivational letters, copy of the work certificates and diplomas).
