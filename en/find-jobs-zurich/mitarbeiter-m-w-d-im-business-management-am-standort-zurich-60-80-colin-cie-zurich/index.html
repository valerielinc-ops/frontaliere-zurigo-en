Original description

Colin&Cie’s independent and value-driven business model creates an environment in which quality, reliability and long-term customer relationships are at the heart of your business and where you can make an important contribution close to the operations. In order to strengthen our business management at the Zurich location, we are looking for a committed personality who enjoys a versatile role in a professional, dynamic environment with short decision-making paths.

This is what we offer you.

  • A modern workplace in Zurich with contemporary social benefits.
  • You will become part of an owner-managed, internationally oriented asset management group.
  • They experience a culture of cooperation and mutual support.
  • In your role, you assume responsibility in a broad field of activity.
  • You can further develop your skills in a professional environment with high independence, short decision-making paths and close exchange with different locations.

Your main tasks with us

  • Participation in regulatory and documentary tasks as well as maintenance and management of master and customer data
  • Support in control, monitoring and quality assurance processes as well as in the implementation of internal requirements
  • Cooperation in securities accounting including coordination of relevant accounts and ensuring high data quality.
  • Support for accounting clarifications, data checks and the clarification of open positions in day-to-day operations.
  • Coordination with landlords, service providers and internal contacts as well as practical support in daily office operations

This is what they bring with them.

  • Completed commercial education or training (banking / financial sector)
  • Experience in the financial services sector, ideally in a similar position
  • Careful, structured and efficient working method with a high degree of initiative
  • Solution-oriented thinking and strong analytical skills
  • High affinity for numbers and quality awareness/orientation
  • Customer and service-oriented thinking, combined with good communication skills
  • Strong team skills and enjoyment of exchange and cooperation in the team
  • Impeccable communication in German in word and writing as well as good knowledge of English
  • Good MS Office skills, especially Excel
  • Basic knowledge of PM1 (expersoft) desirable
