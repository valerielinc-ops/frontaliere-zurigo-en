Your tasks

  • You enjoy programming, parameterizing and commissioning customer-specific building automation controls
  • You create and develop project-specific electrical schematics, principle diagrams, topologies and data point lists
  • You create visualizations according to customer needs
  • You ensure the perfect availability of customer systems and resolve any malfunctions
  • You create rule descriptions based on project specifications. Regulate the systems and optimize them during operation in collaboration with our customers
  • You independently handle smaller projects in the area of building automation This is what you bring with you
  • You are an experienced building automation specialist with at least three years of experience who has completed basic technical training
  • You have in-depth knowledge of programming (Saia, Schneider or similar DDC controls)
  • In addition to experience in the areas of HVAC and MSRL, you enjoy complex systems and Facilities as well as a strong understanding of technical procedures and processes
