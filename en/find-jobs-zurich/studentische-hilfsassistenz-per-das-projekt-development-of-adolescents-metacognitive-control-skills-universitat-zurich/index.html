Full description

Psychological Institute Job as of now (or by agreement, but at the latest in January), temporary As part of the SNSF project "Development of Adolescents' Metacognitive Control", we are investigating how young people assess their own knowledge (metacognitive monitoring) and how they make decisions on this basis in order to specifically control their learning (metacognitive control). For this purpose, we have been collecting data in school classes on a regular basis since 2024 in order to examine the judgments and decisions that young people make when handling learning tasks. The project is now in its final data collection phase. In January and February we will again visit several schools in German-speaking Switzerland to collect data. To support the daily tasks in the research project, we are looking for a student assistant in the amount of 20% from January to June 2027. A temporary increase to up to 40% in January and February 2027 is possible, but the 20% can also be awarded to a further assistant to carry out the data collection at the schools.

Tasks

  • Organization and implementation of data collection at schools in German-speaking Switzerland (classes of the 9th grade)
  • Communication and cooperation with teachers and schools
  • Preparation of test material
  • Preparation and further processing of the collected data
  • Administrative tasks in the project

Profile

  • Current studies in psychology (or in a related subject)
  • Joy in dealing with young people
  • Good communication skills
  • Teamwork, reliability, independent and conscientious working methods
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Time flexibility in January and February

We offer a cooperative working environment with varied tasks. You gain experience in psychological and educational research and learn about innovative research approaches. You will also get interesting insights into the development of adolescents and their metacognition.
