Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Vocational Counselling School social worker/primary school Hausen am Albis 60% Good for life – that is our motto. The Office for Youth and Vocational Counselling Canton of Zurich, office of the districts of Affoltern, Dietikon, Horgen, runs a regional office for school social work. On behalf of schools, she is responsible for the personnel, professional and administrative management of school social workers. In order to ensure a low-threshold school social work offer, the jobs are located in the individual school communities. For the primary school Hausen am Albis we are looking for a school social worker on 1 January 2027 or by agreement. They advise and accompany children, adolescents, parents and teachers in educational and social matters. In the area of child protection and child welfare, you are familiar. In crises and conflicts, you perform interventions in classes and groups. As far as possible, you also do prevention and project work. You will also work with the school team and with other specialists. For this task, we require a completed education in social work at the university of applied sciences level. Some years of professional experience in school social work or in the socio-educational field with children and adolescents are desired. They have competences in consulting, prevention, crisis intervention and project work as well as an independent and interdisciplinary working method.

  • We offer you a versatile, interesting and self-employed activity with annual working hours (compensation of the school holiday time due to increased workload during school time). You will receive support from a professional, collegial team and you can expect lively exchange and networking with other school social workers.The employment conditions correspond to the cantonal guidelines. Martin Graf, Head of Regional Office for School Social Work, Tel. 043 259 92 39. Additional information can be found at www.ajb.zh.ch. Have we aroused your interest? Then we look forward to your complete online application.

Pensum: 60%
