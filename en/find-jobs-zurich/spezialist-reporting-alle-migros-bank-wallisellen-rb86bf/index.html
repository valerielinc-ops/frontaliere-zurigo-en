Full description

With us you can live out your enthusiasm for numbers and analyses. In the area of Closing & Reporting, you contribute to our regulatory and financial reporting and thus make a direct contribution to the transparency of Migros Bank.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Bank, Wallisellen, Industriestrasse 17-19, 8304 Wallisellen

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You create, plausibility and analyze regulatory reporting to FINMA and SNB – including the processing of ad hoc information requirements.
  • You play a key role in external reporting: Annual Report, Sustainability Report and other disclosure and reporting formats.
  • You support the monthly, quarterly and annual accounts in the area of closing & reporting – from closing bookings to votes to analyses of balance sheet and income statement.
  • You take on the professional responsibility for new regulatory and reporting requirements and develop our reporting processes continuously.
  • You will continue to operate and develop the FiRE reporting system and the relevant interfaces to the Finnova core banking system, ensuring high data quality.
  • You ensure the expertise in supervisory and financial reporting in the entire bank and work as a specialist in cross-divisional projects.

Requirements

  • 5 years: Regulatory reporting, financial reproting and/or accounting of a financial institution
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): Business Administration, Banking & Finance or Accounting & Controlling
  • In-depth expertise on regulatory requirements for Swiss banks (e.g. FINMA circulars, own funds and liquidity regulations, SNB statistics).
  • Practice in monthly, quarterly and annual accounts incl. Accounting, reconciliation and analysis of balance sheet and income statement.
  • Strong IT affinity and in-depth user knowledge of the reporting software FiRE as well as good knowledge of the banking software Finnova
  • Knowledge of SQL or BI tools is an advantage.
  • Analytical, structured and careful working methods as well as an interested and team-oriented personality with pleasure in the further development of reporting and closing processes.
  • in German (negotiation confident)
  • French (advantageous)
  • English (flowing)

Cosa of friamo

  • Central place of work (GABS): Optimally accessible by train (10 minutes from Zurich HB) or car
  • Modern infrastructure: future-oriented and ergonomic workplace
  • Child care: up to CHF 300 per month per child to external child care
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Migros Bank: accounts, mortgages and financial services with preferential terms
  • Advantages of cadre: One week holidays per year more for cadre members
  • Du culture: Experienced team spirit with transparent communication
  • Flat hierarchies: Permeable hierarchies and cross-level exchange

Contatto

Application and contact Moritz Forrer Team Leader HR Business Partner

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision to advance or withdraw from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Personality/performance test: Under certain conditions, we use a personality test (BIP, Scales test, etc.) as an additional step to support selection. The results of the test may be discussed personally and form the basis for further selection. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
