About the role One of our strengths is being able to offer our customers integrative and economical system solutions in order to efficiently coordinate interfaces in the construction process. Through our broad product range with well-known and tested products and systems, we cover all areas of civil engineering and civil engineering. Our technical sales consultants make an important contribution to customer loyalty, acquisition and all phases of property support. Would you also like to be a member of a powerful team? For the East sales region (SG/TG/Appenzell/Rhine Valley and Liechtenstein) we are looking for a technical sales consultant concrete and waterproofing as of January 1, 2027.

In this role, you are responsible for the following tasks

  • Advising and selling Sika products to builders, special processors and in concrete production
  • Maintenance of existing and new customer relationships as well as acquisition in the property business in the area of high and high. Civil engineering
  • Preparation of offers and tender documents as well as support of objects during execution
  • Presentation of high-quality Sika construction solutions at information events and customer events
  • Active cooperation with other sales entities

Your skills and experience

  • basic technical training in construction
  • Further training as foremen, polisher or construction manager or construction materials tester
  • Your knowledge in the field of concrete technology is another plus
  • You are an extroverted, sales-oriented personality
  • Negotiation-confident knowledge of German in words and writing, other languages beneficial
  • Very good MS Office skills
  • Salesforce experience is beneficial
  • They noticeably enjoy communication, convince other people and bring a flair for advice and sales
  • You know the market and the customers in the corresponding sales area

Why you should come to us

  • Trust is the most important thing for us. We trust in your skills and provide you with many design spaces – every day anew
  • A motivated team with a lot of know-how and an open feedback culture
  • Uncomplicated leaders who trust, promote and actively support. A family atmosphere makes working at Sika special – many of us call it the Sika spirit
  • Attractive employment conditions and development prospects

We look forward to your complete application dossier (CV, diplomas, certificates, etc.).

We offer competitive remuneration based on local market standards and the specific responsibilities of each job. The remuneration shall be based on the skills, responsibility, education and/or training relevant to the position. We are committed to fair and equitable remuneration schemes in accordance with the applicable laws, the Chemical Tariff Agreement and regulations.

About Sika Sika is a specialty chemical company, global leader in the development and production of systems and products for gluing, sealing, damping, reinforcing and protecting for the construction and vehicle industries. Sika has a global presence with subsidiaries in 103 countries, produces in over 400 factories and develops innovative technologies for customers around the world, contributing significantly to the sustainable transformation of the construction and vehicle industry. The more than 33,000 employees generated a turnover of CHF 11.2 billion in 2025.
