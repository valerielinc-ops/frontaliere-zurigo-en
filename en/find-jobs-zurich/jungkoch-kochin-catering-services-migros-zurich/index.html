Full description

The staff restaurant Topolino offers fresh and seasonal food every day. From a large selection, our guests can cater during the day. To complement our team, we are looking for a service- and front-oriented personality who brings pleasure to the profession.

Mansioni

  • Preparation of the cold and warm food offer
  • Advice and service of our guests as well as the menu output
  • Establishment and maintenance of buffets in the Free Flow (guest area)
  • Active support in the management of goods and in the reception of goods
  • Various Mise en Place Work in the respective work area
  • Compliance with GMZ quality standards and HACCP hygiene guidelines

Requirements

  • At least 1-2 years of professional experience: as cook
  • Completed apprenticeship (EFZ): as cook
  • Flexible operational readiness from Monday to Saturday in different shifts (early and late services, without room hour)
  • Motivated, organized and humorous team player personality
  • Efficient, forward-looking and orderly work
  • Enjoy working with colleagues from various backgrounds and cultures as well as with guests
  • German (very good knowledge)
