Production staff:in 80-100% packaging (night)

Two Spice AG — Dietlikon, Dietlikon, Svizzera

Yooji’s Manufaktur, which is part of Two Spice AG, offers a versatile and exquisite range of sushi, sashimi and much more. The production site of the Two Spice Manufaktur is located in Rümlang and employs more than 90 people. Various delicacies are pre-made every day and delivered to Yooji's restaurants and stores several times a day. Specially developed products for our Japanese retail brand ZENBU are also produced in the factory. ZENBU products such as Sushi, Poké, Onigiri and vegan delicacies are produced for Coop and are available directly from Coop throughout Switzerland. Do you want to be part of our team? Now is your chance!

Tasks

  • Fault-free production, packaging and picking of products according to the communicated daily requirements in compliance with current standards and processes
  • Error-free work preparation
  • Proper picking of various orders
  • Miscellaneous cleaning and cleaning work and compliance with cleaning standards in accordance with applicable cleaning and hygiene standards
  • Infrastructure maintenance and maintenance

Requirements

  • Professional experience in food production, production, packaging or picking
  • Experience in handling food and knowledge of hygiene regulations (HACCP) are beneficial
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Readiness for a permanent night shift as well as for weekend and public holiday missions
  • Physical resilience and willingness to work mainly standing and regularly lifting loads
  • German skills at A2 level, English skills are advantageous
