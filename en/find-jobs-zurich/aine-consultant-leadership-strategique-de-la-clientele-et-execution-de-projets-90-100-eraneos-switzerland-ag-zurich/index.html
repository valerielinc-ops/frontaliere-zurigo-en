Full description

Eraneos is an international strategy, transformation and technology advisory group that helps companies thrive in a dynamic digital world. With first-class experts from business and technology, we empower our customers to set new standards for sustainable transformations – from strategy to implementation. Whether in the design of sustainable organizations, the development of new potential through data and AI or the protection of companies through holistic cybersecurity: We deliver results pragmatically and purposefully. Our team of around 1,200 dedicated employees is represented in offices in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, Denmark, Sweden, Austria, the UK, Singapore and the USA and combines global perspectives with strong local roots. Around 450 of these are active at the Swiss locations in Zurich, Bern and Lausanne. This is the job – your main tasks

  • Close cooperation with senior managers and engagement managers as well as use of targeted sponsorship and mentoring on the way to client lead
  • Active co-creation of the consulting offer by introducing new topics, methods or internal initiatives and thus shaping the further development of Eraneos Switzerland This job is for you if...
  • ... a completed university degree at a renowned university (ETH, HSG, EPFL or similar) at at least master level along with a strong academic track record is your basis
  • ... first relevant experience in consulting, transformation, technology, strategy, public sector or a demanding in-house role is already part of your path
  • ... entrepreneurial thinking, the will to customer responsibility and the ambition to position you as a trusted sparring partner at C-level in the long term, drive you
  • ... high self-motivation and the personal claim to provide top commercial and content services shape your actions – from internal drive instead of external pressure
  • ... very good knowledge of German and English at at least C1 level round off your profile
