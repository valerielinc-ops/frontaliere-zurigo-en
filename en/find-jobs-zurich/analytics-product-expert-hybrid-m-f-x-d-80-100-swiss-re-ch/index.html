Full description

Are you passionate about turning data and AI into decisions that matter? Do you thrive at the intersection of business, analytics, and product development? If so, this is your opportunity to help shape the future of Life & Health Re through innovative analytical products that drive real operational impact.

About the Role

As an Analytics Product Expert, you will be at the heart of a mission to make data and AI work harder for the business. You will bridge the gap between Life & Health Re's operational teams and product delivery, ensuring that analytical products are not just built — but adopted, valued, and embedded into everyday decision-making. From shaping product roadmaps to facilitating stakeholder workshops, your work will directly influence how Swiss Re uses analytics and AI to drive operational excellence.

Key Responsibilities

  • Partner with business stakeholders to deeply understand operational challenges, question existing ways of working, and translate complex business needs into clear product requirements, user journeys, and analytical use cases
  • Support Product Owners in defining and evolving product roadmaps, prioritising enhancements, and shaping new capabilities based on business value, strategic priorities, and user feedback
  • Collaborate cross-functionally with Data Engineering, Data Science, UX, and IT teams to ensure business requirements are clearly understood, accurately scoped, and successfully delivered
  • Drive product adoption through user testing, demonstrations, documentation, and change management activities — ensuring analytical products become genuinely embedded in day-to-day operational decision-making
  • Continuously identify opportunities to enhance business intelligence, improve user experience, and strengthen operational decision support through the smart application of analytics and AI
  • Contribute to strategic analytics products such as L&H Nexus, Operational Health Workbench, and AI-enabled solutions that connect business processes, data, and intelligence

About the Team

The Operational Analytics & Intelligence team sits within Data & Operational Excellence and is a global team responsible for generating end-to-end operational insights and driving decision-making through data, analytics, and AI across Life & Health Re. We play a key role in delivering on the strategic priorities of End-to-end Operational Excellence and Make Data Matter. Our team develops and evolves strategic analytical products that connect business processes, data, and AI to enable measurable business impact — and we're growing.

About You

You are a curious, collaborative, and commercially minded professional who loves making complexity simple. You bring a strong analytical mindset combined with excellent communication skills, and you know how to bring people along on the journey — from initial problem framing through to product adoption. You are energised by working in dynamic, cross-functional environments and are genuinely excited about the potential of AI and analytics to transform how organisations operate.

We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements

  • Business analysis or product analysis experience, ideally within data, analytics, digital products, consulting, or process improvement environments
  • Strong ability to translate complex business problems into structured product requirements, analytical use cases, and user stories that development teams can act on
  • Hands-on experience with analytics platforms such as Power BI or Palantir Foundry, with a solid understanding of how these tools can be applied to solve real business problems
  • Stakeholder engagement and facilitation skills, including experience running workshops and building consensus across diverse, cross-functional teams
  • Understanding of AI and Generative AI concepts, with the ability to identify and articulate where these technologies can add value in an operational context

These are additional nice to haves

  • Experience working in Agile delivery environments, with familiarity with tools such as Jira or Azure DevOps
  • Background in Life & Health (re)insurance or financial services, providing context for the operational processes and data challenges in scope
  • Experience in change management or product adoption initiatives, helping organisations move from tool deployment to genuine behavioural change
  • Ambition to grow into a Product Owner or Product Manager role, with an appetite for taking on increasing ownership over time
  • Comfort working in a global, matrixed organisation and collaborating across time zones and cultures
