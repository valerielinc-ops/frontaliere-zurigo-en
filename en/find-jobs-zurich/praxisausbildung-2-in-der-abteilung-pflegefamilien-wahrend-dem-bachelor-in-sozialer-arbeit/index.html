Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Vocational Counselling Practical training 2 in the department of foster families during the Bachelor in Social Work for 10 – 12 months, 60 – 80% Good for life – that is our motto. The Office for Youth and Vocational Counselling of the Education Directorate provides, controls and coordinates offers of child and youth welfare and vocational counselling. The Department of Nursing Families in the Central Department Supplementary Assistance for Education offers students an internship within the framework of their bachelor’s degree in social work from February 2027 or after consultation as part of practical training 2 for ten to twelve months. As an intern, you support the team of authorities in their work and are jointly responsible for the acquisition of new foster families for the foster family pool of the AJB, the placement of foster children (including in the context of crisis interventions) in foster families as well as the advice of foster parents, foster children and specialists during the process of getting to know each other. If necessary, the team of authorities will support you in granting nursing permits and in supervising nursing conditions. You also help to further develop the foster care system in the Canton of Zurich. They will also be given the opportunity to develop conceptual foundations for the foster care pool and thus actively contribute to the development of offers and quality in the field of family care in the Canton of Zurich. Your profile

  • Currently in the bachelor program Social work at a technical college and would like to complete the second practical training as part of your studies
  • Ideally, first professional experience in child and youth welfare and a high level of social commitment to the concerns of children, in particular foster children and their families
  • Self-employed and committed working methods
  • Open way of communicating and approaching people
  • Discussion experience
  • Quick understanding, independent working methods
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary cooperation and contact with different stakeholders
  • Good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • high degree of self-motivation and the will to help shape the internship We offer them
  • a versatile and responsible task in a meaningful and appreciative working environment
  • a small team that lives collegiality, humor and flexibility
  • Office space in a central location at Exhibition Street 88 in Zurich 8005 and the opportunity to make home office and much more ... Application Have we aroused your interest? Then we look forward to your complete online application.
