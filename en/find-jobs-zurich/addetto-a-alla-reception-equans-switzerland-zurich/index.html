Full description

For a customer location in Zurich, we are looking for a committed and motivated personality for a period of 6 months with the option of renewal.

You can move that with us.

In this role, you are the face of the company and provide a pleasant atmosphere. The following tasks are part of your field of activity:

  • Competent and friendly customer reception
  • Accompanying customers to meeting rooms
  • Rapid and reliable receipt and forwarding of telephones
  • Beverage service including preparation and follow-up
  • Management of meeting rooms
  • Equipment management
  • washing dishes, polishing glasses, mis-en-place preparations and general kitchen work
  • Other administrative tasks

That's what makes you.

  • You are a service provider: from the innermost conviction.
  • You bring at least 3 years of professional experience at the reception in a similar position
  • Ganstronomy/hotel industry training or similar
  • You have very good PC and MS Office user knowledge.
  • German is your mother tongue and you also have good English skills in word and writing
  • You work independently and proactively.
  • You convince by a sympathetic and warm radiance.
  • You are a resilient and flexible personality who enjoys administrative tasks

That's what we offer you.

We offer you a responsible and interesting task with plenty of space for initiative in an exciting professional environment. We look forward to your complete application with photo.
