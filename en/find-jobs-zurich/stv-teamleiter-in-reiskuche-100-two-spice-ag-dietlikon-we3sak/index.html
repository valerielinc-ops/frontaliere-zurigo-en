Stv. team leader: in rice kitchen 100%

Two Spice AG — Dietlikon, Dietlikon, Svizzera

Yooji’s Manufaktur, which is part of Two Spice AG, offers a versatile and exquisite range of sushi, sashimi and much more. The production site of the Two Spice Manufaktur is located in Rümlang and employs more than 90 people. Various delicacies are pre-made every day and delivered to Yooji's restaurants and stores several times a day. Specially developed products for our Japanese retail brand ZENBU are also produced in the factory. ZENBU products such as Sushi, Poké, Onigiri and vegan delicacies are produced for Coop and are available directly from Coop throughout Switzerland. Do you want to be part of our team? Now is your chance!

Tasks

  • Organisation, management and monitoring of rice production
  • Professional management, guidance and operational planning of employees
  • Ensuring compliance with production, quality and hygiene standards
  • Implementation and monitoring of cleaning work according to specifications
  • Detection, reporting and correction of discrepancies in coordination with the production manager

Requirements

  • Completed training in food, production or equivalent qualification
  • Several years of experience in a production plant, ideally in food production
  • Experience in the professional management of employees is advantageous
  • Good knowledge of hygiene, quality assurance and food safety
  • Structured, independent and responsible working methods
  • High quality awareness and assertiveness
  • German skills at B1 level in word and writing
