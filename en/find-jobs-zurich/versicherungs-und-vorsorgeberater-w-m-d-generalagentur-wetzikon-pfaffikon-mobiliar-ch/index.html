Full description

This is what awaits you: In this role, you will offer our customers insurance and pension solutions that are tailored to their respective needs. This is what you achieve with us: The focus of this position is on competent support and the active expansion of your private and corporate customer portfolio. Thanks to your courteous and empathetic nature, you always have an open ear for your customers, who you always support with personal advice and individually tailored solutions from the entire insurance and pension sector. This is what you bring with you:

  • Completed vocational training or studies
  • Several years of career advice, an advantage in insurance and pension advice or in field sales - career changers are also welcome
  • Sales flair
  • Enjoyment of contact with people and acquiring new customers through active work
  • Independent, responsible and customer-oriented way of working
  • Resilient, winning and empathetic personality with an affinity for digital work
  • Driver's license category B
  • Fluent knowledge of German, any other language is a plus Working with us: A diverse and dynamic team is waiting for you at the Wetzikon-Pfäffikon general agency. With 37 employees, including 3 trainees, and our own on-site claims department, we live locally and are a reliable partner for our customers. We are a cooperative - not shareholders, but our customers benefit from our success every year. You get an extensive customer base (private individuals and companies) and, if you haven't already done so, we will train you to become a VBV insurance intermediary. Your place of work is Wetzikon and the workload is 100%. The start of the position takes place by appointment. You can find out more about our commitment as an employer below. This is what we offer: Profit sharing If we are successful, our employees also benefit. Every year a decision is made about the distribution of a profit share and its amount. Further training and development We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as Cicero certifications for high consulting quality and consulting expertise. Discounts We offer, among other things, up to 25% premium discounts, discounts on mobile phones as well as free flat-rate subscriptions and discounts on cultural events.
