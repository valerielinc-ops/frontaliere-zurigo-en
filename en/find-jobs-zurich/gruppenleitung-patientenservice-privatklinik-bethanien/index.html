  • Start date: Immediately or by appointment Company description Treatment at the highest level, high-class medical care and support, competent care in a familiar, private atmosphere with hotel comfort, dedication, conscientiousness and absolute discretion - that's what the Private Clinic Bethanien stands for. Around 340 affiliated doctors from various disciplines ensure high-quality, integrated medical care. The most important specialist areas include orthopedics, visceral and thoracic surgery, urology, gynecology and childbirth, gastroenterology, cardiology, pulmonology and internal medicine including oncology. Around 500 children are born in the maternity ward every year. The Bethanien private clinic, founded in 1912, is part of the Swiss private clinic group Swiss Medical Network.   • Promotion, development and support of employees
  • Participation in projects for continuous process and quality improvement Qualifications
