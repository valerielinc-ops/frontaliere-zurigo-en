Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Secondary and Vocational Education Office Cantonal School Uetikon am See Teaching assignment for Latin 25 - 40% The Cantonal School Uetikon am See (KUE) is a young school in the development phase. She leads a high school secondary level and five of the Zurich maturity profiles (old-language, mathematical-scientific, modern-language, economic-legal and philosophical-pedagogical-psychological). The number of students is around 800, that of teachers and members of the administration about 120. The location and form of the school are unique: The modern premises at the provisional location in the middle of the municipality enable future-oriented and innovative forms of teaching and spur the creation of the "KUE spirit". The team is young and agile, we like to try new things. In six years, KUE will move into its new premises on the site of the former Chemical Factory directly on Lake Zurich.

  • Their tasks At the beginning of the autumn semester 2027/28 we are looking for a lecturer for the subject Latin in the scope of 6 – 9 lessons.
  • We are looking for dedicated teachers with a completed university master's degree in the relevant subject as well as a started teaching diploma for maturity schools or an equivalent diploma for teaching at high school maturity schools according to MAR. Ideally, you could already gain initial teaching experience. Joy in dealing with young people and an educational skill distinguish you. Our offer It is according to the Zurich middle and vocational school teachers ordinance a temporary employment. A long-term development of this position into permanent employment is possible. Application Have we aroused your interest? We look forward to your application. Please submit your documents exclusively via the online tool until 25 October 2026. Further information about our school can be found at www.kuezh.ch. Further information can be provided by the Vice Rector Karin Hunkeler (karin.hunkeler@kuezh.ch), responsible for Latin.

Pensum: 25-40 %
