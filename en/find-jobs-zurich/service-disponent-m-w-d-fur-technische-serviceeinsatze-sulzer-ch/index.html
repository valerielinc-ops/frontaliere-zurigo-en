Full description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

Organizational talent meets customer orientation: As Service Disponent (m/f/d), you coordinate service missions throughout Germany and ensure that our service technicians are at the right place at the right time. As a central interface between customers, service technicians and internal departments, you make a decisive contribution to smooth processes, high service quality and sustainable customer satisfaction. Their tasks

  • Planning and scheduling of service missions throughout Germany.
  • Coordination and control of service technicians taking into account qualifications, availability and customer requirements.
  • Contact person for customers, service technicians and internal departments for all operational topics.
  • Adaptation of operational plans for short-term changes and priority shifts.
  • Ensure full operational documentation as well as close cooperation with the commercial areas in order and invoice processing.
  • Participation in the continuous improvement of service processes and high customer satisfaction.

Your profile

  • Completed commercial or technical vocational training or equivalent qualification.
  • Several years of experience in scheduling, operational planning, service coordination or a comparable environment.
  • Secure handling of ERP, CRM or planning systems, ideally Salesforce.
  • Strong organisational talent as well as a structured and solution-oriented working method.
  • Communication strength, customer orientation and enjoyment of working with different contacts.
  • Very good knowledge of German and basic English.

What we offer you

  • International cooperation, open corporate culture and a motivated team
  • Contract of indefinite duration
  • Flexible working hours and 30 days of vacation
  • Free fruit basket, coffee and water and discounted lunch menu
  • A strong, helpful team with good cohesion
  • Modern work equipment
  • Attractive remuneration and additional benefits, including: perks/employee discounts, JobRad program, company pensions, company special benefits such as family service hotline

Sulzer is an equal opportunity employer. We believe in the strength of a diverse workforce and are committed to offering an inclusive work environment.
