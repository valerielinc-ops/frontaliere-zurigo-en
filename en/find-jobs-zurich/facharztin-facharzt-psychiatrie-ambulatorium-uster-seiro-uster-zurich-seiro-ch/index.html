Full description

Outpatient adult psychiatry with the entire diagnostic and therapeutic spectrum, close to people and without night services. In Uster, Canton of Zurich, we are looking for a senior physician for adult psychiatry and psychotherapy in the range of 40-100% for an outpatient practice with an affiliated day clinic. She expects a broad range of outpatient services as part of the largest Swiss private hospital group for psychiatry and psychotherapy. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • They take over the psychiatric-psychotherapeutic clarification and treatment of adult patients in the outpatient setting
  • They independently bear senior medical case responsibility from diagnosis to therapy planning to implementation
  • You work interdisciplinary with the associated day clinic and the multi-professional team
  • You contribute to the professional and conceptual development of the ambulatory offer
  • They take over the supervision and professional support of assistant doctors*

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist title in psychiatry and psychotherapy
  • Completed specialist training with recognition for Switzerland for foreign diplomas
  • Experience in the outpatient psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment of adult patients
  • Enjoy independent case management and interdisciplinary cooperation

That sets them apart.

  • Patient-centred, trusting basic attitude in psychiatric care
  • Self-employed, responsible work with high professional standards
  • Communication strength and team spirit in a multi-professional environment

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Responsible medical work in the outpatient setting without night and weekend services
  • Broad diagnostic and therapeutic offer for patients of all ages
  • Connection to an established Swiss-wide specialist group for psychiatry and psychotherapy
  • Flexible part-time models from 40 to 100%
  • Collegial, multi-professional working environment with structured training

Additionally for applicants from the EU

  • Support for MEBEKO recognition of your diploma
  • Assistance in organizing your journey to Switzerland
  • Assistance in finding accommodation through our internal rental department
