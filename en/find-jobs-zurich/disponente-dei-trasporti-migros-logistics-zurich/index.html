Full description

We deliver! Migros Logistics brings together Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (MVB) and Migros Logistique Romandie SA (MLR) to supply Switzerland every day. With modern technology and highly automated processes, we shape the logistics of tomorrow. Start with us into the future of logistics as a transport dispatcher, 80 - 100%.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Logistics, Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (Zurich), Limmatstrasse 152, 8005 Zurich

Mansioni

  • Together with our various transport partners, you move between 200 to 300 trucks every day.
  • You plan vehicles and tours and ensure compliance with customer specifications, dates and legal framework conditions.
  • As an important interface between goods recipients, shippers and chauffeurs, you ensure smooth communication and take care of the receipt and recording of customer orders.
  • The assignment of transport orders to our transport partners as well as the notification of deliveries to the goods recipients are also part of your tasks.
  • You plan and award transport orders efficiently and always keep an eye on the quality of transport services.
  • You take on administrative tasks, acquire and maintain data in CADIS and DispoCenter and create the holiday planning in Excel.
  • In various projects, you actively participate and contribute your experience and ideas.

Requirements

  • Initial professional experience: An advantage as a transport dispatcher
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): an advantage in logistics
  • Readiness for shift work (day shift from 06:00 to 19:00) and pikett
  • Readiness for work assignments on all weekdays (MO-SA and regional public holidays)
  • Good understanding and networked thinking
  • Reliable, independent and precise working methods
  • Good IT knowledge (office/disposition software)
  • German (flowing)
  • French (basic knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central location: Our locations are directly connected to the motorway or public transport
  • Modern infrastructure: Our logistics work with fully automated, state-of-the-art facilities. Office workplaces meet the latest standards
  • Training and further education: We support our employees through targeted training and further education offers and support them generously
  • Sports and fitness offers: We support regular, health-promoting and -preserving activities
  • Development opportunities: We offer individual support programs and task hours. For most professions, you can complete a multi-week stage in another company.
