Full description

Faculty of Law

Job as per 1. December 2026 or by agreement

At the Chair of Criminal and Criminal Procedure Law of Prof. Dr. Gunhild Godenzi is from 1. to fill a position as administrative assistant on December 2026 or by agreement. the employment is limited to an initial period of one year

  • An indefinite continuation of employment is possible.

Tasks

  • Administrative support of the chair
  • Processing of the e-mail inbox, maintenance of the calendar and coordination of inquiries and appointments.
  • support for the organisation of courses, examinations and other chair activities
  • Preparation of documents and communication with students, in particular on exam information.
  • Assistance in personnel administration
  • Coordination with internal contact persons and management of administrative processes.
  • Material management and orders, data maintenance and support of the website.
  • processing of invoices, expense and credit card statements
  • Review of the budget and, if necessary, take on other simple financial tasks.

Profile

  • Completed commercial training and several years of experience in administrative tasks, ideally in a law firm or in a comparable position.
  • Very good knowledge of German and secure knowledge of English in word and writing as well as routine handling of MS Office and digital management systems.
  • Solution-oriented, structured, independent and reliable working method, even if several tasks are pending at the same time.
  • High social competence and pleasure in working together in a team.
  • Ideally, work on Mondays or Tuesdays one day on site. The remaining working days can be flexible.
  • Interest in legal topics and cooperation with researchers and students.
