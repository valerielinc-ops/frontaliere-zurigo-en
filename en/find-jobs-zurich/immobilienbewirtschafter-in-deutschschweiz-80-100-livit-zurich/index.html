Management Zurich | Pensum: 80-100 %

Are you open-minded, ambitious and value a future-oriented way of working on the pulse of the real estate industry? At Livit, you are committed to the concerns of owners:inside and tenants:in with an exemplary customer orientation and thus contribute significantly to a trusting, long-lasting customer relationship and a positive corporate image. Are you ready to tackle the challenges of today and tomorrow and make a difference in the real estate world? Then you are exactly right with Livit. We look forward to your personal contribution.

Your tasks

  • You support various management teams with personnel and technical bottlenecks in our offices in German-speaking Switzerland (BE, BS, LU, SG, SO, ZH)
  • You are responsible for the acceptance and transfer of rental properties for first rentals / changes of tenants
  • You are in direct contact with owners, tenants, property managers, craftsmen and authorities
  • You ensure the real estate maintenance according to the management contract
  • You carry out periodic real estate inspections and write condition assessments with corresponding reports
  • You carry out rental contract & rental interest changes as well as indexed rental interest adjustments
  • You monitor the current rental policy and advise homeowners on how to set rental rates

Your profile

  • You have an oath. Professional certificate as a real estate manager or have an adequate further education in the real estate sector
  • You bring several years of professional experience as a clerk / in real estate management or in a junior function
  • Your mother tongue is German and you have very good correspondence skills
  • You are digitally affine and have the ability to actively participate in changes
  • You have an independent and reliable working method and bring travel readiness and flexibility
  • You are customer-oriented and maintain a partnership
  • You think and act entrepreneurially and have pleasant manners
