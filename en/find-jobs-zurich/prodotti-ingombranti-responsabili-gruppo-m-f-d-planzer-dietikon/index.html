Full description

Brand of the Planzer Group: Planzer Paket. Planzer Paket stands for reliable and fast parcel deliveries in Switzerland. As part of our team, you actively contribute to further developing our high quality standards and service. We offer you a varied job in a future-oriented company that focuses on sustainable solutions. Become part of our team and shape the future of the package world! Tasks:

  • You are responsible for the topic of bulky goods throughout the group and develop uniform standards, processes and specifications for all locations
  • Together with the company management on site, you implement your specifications across locations and ensure that they are lived sustainably. As a central interface and first point of contact, you support companies, dispatch and management in all bulky goods issues. By analyzing volumes, throughput times and costs, you identify potential for optimization and actively promote group-wide improvements
  • Investment and infrastructure decisions relating to the handling, storage and transport of bulky goods accompany you from planning to implementation
  • For a sustainable knowledge transfer between the locations, you ensure by exchanging best practices and training the teams on uniform standards Profile:
  • You have in-depth experience in logistics as well as in parcel or general cargo handling, ideally in relation to bulky goods
  • Several years of experience in professional management across several locations distinguishes you
  • With your pronounced process understanding, you think across locations and develop practical solutions that you consistently implement. You analyze figures, volumes and economic relationships with a trained glance and derive informed decisions from them. Thanks to your communication strength and assertiveness, you succeed in creating acceptance and convincing different stakeholders even without direct authority. Willingness to travel within Switzerland you bring as well as very good knowledge of German

Knowledge of French and Italian is beneficial Benefits

  • With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • With us you start with 5 weeks vacation and benefit from the 5th year of service even from 6 weeks!
  • Your health is important to us - our workplaces are modern and ergonomically furnished For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
