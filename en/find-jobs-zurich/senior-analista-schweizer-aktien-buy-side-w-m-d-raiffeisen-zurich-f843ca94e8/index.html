Full description

10.09.2026 Zurich

Become part of our team!

The share analysis is part of the Investment & Provision Center and is responsible for the analysis and recommendations of Swiss shares at Raiffeisen. For the share analysis with Zurich Airport (The Circle) we are looking for a committed, reliable and team-oriented personality as a senior buy-side analyst for Swiss shares (m/f/d).

What do you expect?

  • Independent analysis and evaluation of Swiss companies based on fundamental research methods
  • Coverage of Swiss shares in at least one of the following sectors: industry, finance or consumer goods
  • Creation, further development and maintenance of financial models and company valuations (DCF, multiples, etc.)
  • Development, monitoring and updating of investment recommendations for Swiss shares
  • Presentation and communication of analyses and recommendations (written and oral, internal and external)
  • Conduct company visits and management meetings as well as participation in relevant industry conferences

What do you bring?

  • University degree (Uni/FH) and completed subject-specific further education (CFA or CIIA)
  • Mind. 10 years of professional experience as a stock analyst with a focus on Swiss stocks (buy-side or sell-side)
  • Sound expertise in mind. one of the industrial, financial (banking and insurance) and/or consumer goods sectors
  • Excellent knowledge of business valuation, financial analysis and financial modeling (particularly Excel and Bloomberg)
  • Team-oriented personality with high credibility, professional appearance and strong communication skills
  • High analytical and conceptual competence as well as strong own initiative
  • Very good knowledge of German, French or Italian is advantageous
