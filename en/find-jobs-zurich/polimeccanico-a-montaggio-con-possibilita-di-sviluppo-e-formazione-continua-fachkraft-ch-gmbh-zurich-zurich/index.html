Full description

Complex assemblies and plant modules are precisely assembled and tested. The company attaches importance to structured work and teamwork in an international environment. For our client we are looking for an activity

  • Precise assembly of complex assemblies and plant modules
  • Wire and test components depending on qualification
  • Actively cooperate in product and process optimization
  • Implement error analyses and improvement measures with technology and testing
  • Requirements
  • Completed training as a polymechanic / in EFZ or comparable
  • Several years of experience in the assembly of machines or plants
  • Self-employed, structured and careful working methods
  • Good knowledge of German as well as flexible and team-oriented personality offer
  • 40 hours week with annual working hours
  • At least five weeks holidays
  • Varied
  • Tasks with a high level of personal responsibility - development and further education opportunities in an international environment
