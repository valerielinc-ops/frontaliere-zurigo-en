Looking for new exciting tasks and challenges? You are responsible for ensuring efficient, transparent and cost-optimized administration of the Group insurance portfolio, the management of brokers and the support of the Head of Legal & Insurance. In this versatile function, you are the central point of contact for all administrative and coordinative topics relating to our insurance portfolio: Your area of responsibility

  • You take over the coordination and support of our insurance brokers and ensure the independent administration of the courts billing, information gathering and disclosure. In addition, you support your supervisor with tenders, contract adjustments and offer comparisons.
  • You ensure the insurance administration with regard to complete documentation, maintenance and management of insurance policies and confirmations and support for mutations (new moves, changes and cancellations).
  • You are responsible for auditing and cost control, creating reporting and plausibility offers and contracts.
  • You are the single point of contact for internal stakeholders when it comes to information regarding insurance. This will make it easier for you to get started.
  • You have completed a basic commercial education or comparable - further education in the insurance sector is advantageous (e.g. insurance specialist).
  • You have several years of experience in insurance administration or brokerage.
  • Experience in a larger company with complex structures is an advantage.
  • You are happy to use your excellent knowledge of Excel and your affinity for data and reporting.
  • You combine your independent, structured and reliable working method with your strong service orientation and communication skills in negotiable German - other languages, especially French, are an advantage. This is what awaits you
