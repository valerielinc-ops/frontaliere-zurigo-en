Full description

In the customer restaurant "Münzhof" at UBS headquarters in Zurich, a high-quality catering service is provided for UBS customers and employees. In addition to the management of the 12 VIP lunch rooms, services are also provided for the management floor. In a modern environment, guests are offered fresh and regional gourmet cuisine with a high-quality, discreet service. In addition, various events with up to 200 people are held in the UBS forum. True to the ZFV philosophy, freshly cooked locally with regional, ecologically well-produced and fair trade food. Do you want to run a fine dining restaurant at a 16-point level and at the same time benefit from regular working hours? Then take over the management at UBS Restaurant Münzhof, where you can shape a culinary experience for international guests from Monday to Friday – immediately or by appointment. Your area of activity:

  • Management & organization of the entire operation at 16-point level
  • Visible host role in the guest room – present, attentive, sovereign

  • Reception and implementation of guest wishes for an international, demanding audience
  • Planning and optimization of service processes, including cooperation at the front
  • Planning & implementation of offers, activities, sales promotion and events
  • Organization and implementation of catering, special events and fine dining experiences
  • Communication with the client: in German & English at a professional level
  • Preparation, monitoring and compliance with the annual budget and analysis of key figures
  • Responsibility for administrative processes (purchasing, accounting, people & culture, etc.)
