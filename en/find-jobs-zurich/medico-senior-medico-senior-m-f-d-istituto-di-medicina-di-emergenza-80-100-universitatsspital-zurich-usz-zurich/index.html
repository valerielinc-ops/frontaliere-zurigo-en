Full description

We offer you a versatile, challenging and varied activity in the exciting environment of emergency medicine in an innovative university environment. This includes the training opportunity for ISP clinical emergency medicine SGNOR as well as ultrasound for obtaining SGUM certificates. At USZ, you can consolidate and expand your skills in interdisciplinary care. In addition, due to modern working models, we also offer the possibility of part-time work. A motivated and committed team will actively support you in your function and all matters.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: Their main tasks At the Institute of Emergency Medicine, you provide and support all emergency patients with the most diverse problems in close cooperation with the relevant specialists. Your tasks include patient care, supervision of assistant doctors, participation in shift operations and participation in various projects for the further development of the institute as well as your personal development. Your profile • You have a specialist title in internal medicine (or equivalent), surgery, musculoskeletal traumatology, anesthesiology or intensive care medicine • You are advantageously aiming for the ISP in clinical emergency medicine SGNOR, or already have it • You already have experience in an interdisciplinary emergency department and bring great commitment and a high level of commitment. • You have ideally already gained initial leadership experience • Prerequisite is very good knowledge of German in word and writing (C1 language certificate) • As a communicative personality, you think and act across processes and patient-oriented and interprofessionally. • Your ability to deal with stressful situations in an exemplary manner is an essential prerequisite for being able to work successfully in a lively and demanding environment. Your social skills, your good assertiveness and your reliability round off your profile We offer: We look forward to your application. Together with your and our knowledge, we achieve the best for our patients.
