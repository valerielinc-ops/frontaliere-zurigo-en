Full description

Uroviva stands for a modern medical network and includes a special clinic for urology, nine urological practices and an andrology center. With approximately 70,000 consultations each year in primary, special and emergency care and more than 5,000 operations of all severity levels, Uroviva is an important provider of urological services in Switzerland. For our recovery room team in our special clinic for urology in Bülach we are looking for 1. November or by arrangement a loyal and committed Care professional HF 40 % with proven experience in the recovery room, IMC or IPS is advantageous With us, you can expect short distances, an experienced team and a working environment in which you can rely on each other.

Their tasks

  • Pre- and postoperative care and monitoring of our urological patients, ambulatory and inpatient
  • Close cooperation with anesthesia, surgery, disposition, medical profession and bed station
  • Active co-creation of the AWR day schedule
  • You work early or late from Monday to Friday and flexibly coordinate your services in a team

Your profile

  • Nurse HF, ideally with proven experience in the recovery room, IMC or IPS
  • High level of expertise and a strong concept of service
  • Human and sensitive care for our patients
  • Respectful and solution-oriented attitude
