Welcome to Medbase Zurich Oerlikon

Our modernly furnished Medical Center directly at the Zurich Oerlikon train station offers our patients high-quality all-round care. The range of services includes General Internal Medicine, Gynecology, Dermatology and Cardiology as well as Physiotherapy, Nutritional Counselling and Psychotherapy. Zurich City Hospital also operates a dialysis centre on our premises. We are an established, motivated team and maintain an open and collegial exchange among ourselves. Become part of it and benefit from our benefits and internal training opportunities. What is important to us: an appreciative working atmosphere, active professional exchange and targeted further development. Flexible working models also support a balanced work-life balance.
