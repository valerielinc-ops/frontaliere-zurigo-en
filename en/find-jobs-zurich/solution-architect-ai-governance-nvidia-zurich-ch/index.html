Full description

NVIDIA has been transforming computer graphics, PC gaming, and accelerated computing for more than 25 years. It’s a unique legacy of innovation that’s fueled by great technology—and amazing people. Today, we’re tapping into the unlimited potential of AI to define the next era of computing. An era in which our GPU acts as the brain of computers, robots, and self-driving cars that can understand the world. Doing what’s never been done before takes vision, innovation, and the world’s best talent. As an NVIDIAN, you’ll be immersed in a diverse, supportive environment where everyone is inspired to do their best work. Come join the team and see how you can make a lasting impact on the world. At NVIDIA, we are leading the charge in AI governance and security, crafting solutions that make significant impacts on enterprise environments. As a Senior Solution Architect for AI Governance & Security, you will be part of an exceptionally talented team that is at the forefront of AI innovation. Your role is pivotal in helping our customers navigate the complexities of deploying AI in regulated environments, making this opportunity truly outstanding. What you'll be doing:

  • Drive technical engagements with enterprise customers and partners, ensuring NVIDIA's security and governance solutions are seamlessly coordinated within their AI deployments.
  • Lead discovery sessions, build solution architectures, deliver impactful demonstrations, and craft detailed technical proposals.
  • Build positive relationships with engineering leads, security architects, DevSecOps teams, and compliance officers by providing clear and accurate information about NVIDIA's stack.
  • Translate NVIDIA’s platform capabilities into practical deployment requirements, addressing data residency, access control, audit logging, and regulatory needs.
  • Build and implement NVIDIA-backed deployment architectures tailored to customer requirements, combining components like OpenShell, NeMo Guardrails, NIM inference, and Confidential Computing.
  • Build reusable solution materials including reference architectures, integration guides, and demo environments to aid the broader EMEA field team.
  • Keep informed about EU AI Act regulations and align NVIDIA platform features with compliance inquiries from regulated enterprise purchasers.
  • Represent NVIDIA at technical workshops, developer days, and ecosystem events, providing deep platform insights and gathering valuable feedback for continuous improvement. What we need to see:
  • 3+ years in a customer-facing technical role such as solutions architect, pre-sales engineer, technical account management, or technical consultant.
  • B.Sc. or M.Sc. in Cybersecurity, Computer Science, or a related field, or an equivalent combination of relevant professional expertise and industry-recognized certifications.
  • Hands-on experience with AI and ML platform architecture, including inference pipelines, agent frameworks, and orchestration layers in real deployments.
  • Applied security awareness, with the ability to read and explain vulnerability dashboards and understand sandboxing, guardrails, access control, and audit logging.
  • Experience in enterprise compliance and governance conversations, having navigated concerns raised by legal or security teams and provided constructive answers.
  • Strong communication skills, capable of engaging with both framework engineers and high-level executives in a variety of technical discussions.
  • Proficiency in Python and familiarity with modern deployment tooling, including containers, Kubernetes, and cloud-native patterns. Ways to stand out from the crowd:
  • Experience with NVIDIA AI Enterprise, NIM, or NeMo Guardrails in a deployment context.
  • Background in supporting procurement cycles in regulated European industries such as financial services, healthcare, or public sector.
  • Familiarity with MCP, A2A, or agentic frameworks, enabling effective engagement with partner engineering teams.
