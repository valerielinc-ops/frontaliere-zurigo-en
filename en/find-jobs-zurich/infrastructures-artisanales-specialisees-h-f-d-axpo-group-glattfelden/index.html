Original description

Pensum: 80% or more | Zweidlen, Rheinau

You like to get involved, like working outside and want to make a direct contribution to the energy supply with your work? Then you are exactly right with us. For our power plants in Eglisau and Rheinau we are looking for a committed personality with craftsmanship and technical flair.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • Maintenance and maintenance services of water and water protection structures
  • Maintenance of roads, paths, dams and forest areas
  • Cooperation in repairs, revisions and new installations of power plant plants
  • Ensuring cleanliness and order around buildings and facilities
  • Flood support in shift work
  • Cooperation in further infrastructure and maintenance work

Profile & Requirements

  • Apprenticeship as a forest keeper, maintenance practitioner or company practitioner, specialist in maintenance, landscaper or similar
  • Several years of professional experience in forestry, road or timber construction
  • Technical understanding and interest in power plants
  • Experience in environmental care and cleaning work
  • Cat B mandatory and eligibility for trailers desirable
  • Passport for forklifts, crane and lifts desirable
  • Driving licence Cat A desirable

Reversing your job is worthwhile - We are eagerly waiting for your application documents. Roger Hofer is looking forward to your application.

About the team

Our team of 4 maintenance specialists is responsible for the maintenance and revision of the infrastructure of hydropower plants on the Rhine. You work on exciting and challenging tasks and also make a valuable contribution to future generations. We offer a young, open and collegial working environment in a professional and innovative environment.
