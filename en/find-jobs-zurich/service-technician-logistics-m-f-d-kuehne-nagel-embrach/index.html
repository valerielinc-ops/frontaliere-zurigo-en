Full description

More than a job In the contract logistics team at Kühne+Nagel, you are responsible for warehouse operations, such as order picking. You work precisely and thereby not only contribute to your team’s success, but also ensure smooth day-to-day operations in our warehousing and distribution centers. Through your work, you enable, for example, the storage and delivery of flowers and fresh ingredients for a beautiful ambience and delicious food in your favorite restaurant. Logistics at Kühne+Nagel is more than you can imagine. ‎

As a service and systems technician, you play a key role in our intralogistics: With your technical expertise, you keep our automated systems running reliably, work closely with logistics teams and external technicians, and ensure that our site remains at the highest technical level.

How you contribute

- You carry out maintenance, inspection, and repair work on automated storage and conveyor systems.

- You analyze faults and ensure their swift and lasting resolution.

- You service stacker cranes, shuttles, lifts, roller conveyors, and other intralogistics systems.

- You work with sensors, actuators, light barriers, and scanner technology.

- You support conversions, optimizations, and extensions of the systems.

- You monitor safety-relevant equipment such as emergency-stop circuits and fire protection systems.

- You document your work and coordinate with internal teams and external technicians.

What sets you apart

- Completed technical training, e.g. electrical installer EFZ / electronics technician EFZ, automation technician / industrial electrician, or mechatronics technician

- Good knowledge of electrical engineering, reading circuit diagrams, and measurement methods.

- Basic mechanical knowledge (belts, chains, bearings, guides).

- Experience in maintaining machines or automated systems.

- Independent, reliable, and solution-oriented way of working.

- Enjoyment of hands-on work during ongoing operations.

- Ability to work in a team and clear communication.

- Willingness to work shifts or on-call duty.

- Resilience and the ability to make quick decisions.

  • Proficient German and English skills, both spoken and written

What's in it for you

  • Performance-based compensation: Benefit from an attractive salary package including a 13th-month salary
  • Prospects: Training opportunities for professional and personal development, such as language courses and development programs
  • Discounts: Various discounts at numerous cooperation partners in the areas of fashion, leisure, electronics, home & more.
  • Teamwork: Team and company events

Who we are

Logistics touches every aspect of our lives – from the goods we consume every day to the healthcare we rely on. At our company, your work goes beyond logistics: It enables the small and big moments of life for people all over the world.

As a globally leading logistics company with the vision of advancing our world, we offer you a secure and stable environment. Here, your work makes a real difference. Whether delivering food, developing sustainable transport solutions or engaging with society – your work achieves more than you can imagine.
