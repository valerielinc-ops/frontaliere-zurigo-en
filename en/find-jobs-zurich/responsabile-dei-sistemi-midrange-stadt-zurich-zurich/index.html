Full description

The Infrastructure Managed Systems department creates the basis for digital and innovative city services. As Team Leader* in Midrange Systems, you will take over the professional and human leadership of a motivated team and will be responsible for the operation and further development of our Midrange Systems. Become part of our dedicated team and bring your expertise!

Tasks

  • You lead a high-performance infrastructure team of 13 specialists and shape the future of the digital base of the city of Zurich.
  • You are responsible for the strategic direction and technical development of core infrastructures such as Linux/Open Source solutions, scalable databases (Oracle, PostgreSQL), intelligent automation, robust network services (QIP/DNS) and secure web application firewalls.
  • You control the budget and business value in your field. You plan and monitor the investments and budget in your department efficiently to ensure maximum ROI and resource utilization.
  • You will ensure the uninterrupted operation of critical services, define quality standards and lead strategic lifecycle projects (modernization, migration, scaling) with a clear focus on digital sovereignty, operational excellence and hyperautomation.
  • You are the focal point for internal stakeholders, transforming technical requirements into effective solutions and promoting the digital competence of employees.

Profile

  • IT studies or higher technical training (TS, HF, FH) with a focus on IT or several years of professional experience in the above-mentioned areas of responsibility
  • Several years of experience in the management of employees as well as the ability to motivate them, to promote their potential and to achieve goals together
  • In-depth practical experience in the further development and operation of critical basic infrastructures as well as in-depth knowledge in the relevant specialist areas
  • Self-responsible and proactive working methods as well as active input of ideas for continuous improvement and process optimization
  • Strong communication skills, understandable communication of complex technical contexts and a good feeling for the needs of internal customers
  • Calmness and sovereign action even in demanding situations, combined with perseverance in the implementation of strategic projects

We offer

  • A diverse and collegial working environment in an agile organization
  • Flexible working time model and above-average social benefits
  • Possibility for home office
  • Targeted education and training opportunities
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave and 4 weeks for co-parents
  • 5 weeks of holidays plus 6 company holidays
  • Various perks (mobility contribution, lunch check, fitness, telephony, etc.)
  • Annual health contribution to promote your well-being
  • Accessible jobs

About us

As a competence center for digitalization and IT in the city of Zurich, Organisation und Informatik (OIZ) provides high-quality services for its customers.
