Our team of around 10 people makes an important contribution to the positioning of the Zürcher Kantonalbank and all in line with our three values – responsible, passionate, stimulating. We are a strong team that ensures modern corporate communication in our marketing and communications unit and a newsroom organization. With our integrated organization, we ensure that we are always on the pulse of events and can act together in a forward-looking manner and make clever use of opportunities. We are looking for an experienced specialist with a convincing appearance for this exciting and versatile function.

  • Development of integrated, multimedia communication concepts
  • Developing and writing relevant stories, e.g. for the corporate blog, intranet, guest contribution or similar, adapted to the corporate strategy
  • Creating and publishing posts on social media incl. Monitoring
  • Professional experience (3-5 years) in corporate communications and media relations on the corporate and/or agency side
  • Finding and developing exciting stories as well as their integrated and multimedia implementation does not bother you
  • You score with your consulting and argumentation-savvy way, are a doer and like to design with
  • Your networked thinking skills as well as your distinct conceptual and strategic skills round off your profile
  • Very good editorial skills and knowledge of German
  • Joy in dealing with and using social media
  • Experience in the banking industry and knowledge of the financial world is beneficial
  • Appreciative cooperation as well as learning from and with each other are important in our team
  • We offer you space for your ideas and thus shape our area and the communication of tomorrow
  • Possibility to take responsibility and make an important contribution to the positioning of one of the most successful Swiss banks
  • Your career is important to us as an employer and therefore we actively promote it
  • Central workplace in our headquarters on Bahnhofstrasse with good transport connections
  • Unique overall package with attractive basic salary, variable compensation and numerous benefits
