Full description

Behind the scenes to ensure our store runs smoothly? Welcome to the fresh winning team. Without logistics, nothing works for us. It is the bottleneck, efficiency booster, and heartbeat all in one. Here we need absolute professionals like you. Denner. This is our store.

Responsibilities

  • Manage the order picking team of 20 employees both personally and professionally
  • Ensure smooth processes and contribute to the continuous improvement process (KVP) in daily operations
  • Support employee development, conduct performance reviews, and semi-annual employee qualifications
  • Promote and further develop cross-departmental collaboration within the distribution center
  • Actively support daily operations on the floor, such as order picking, inventory checks, transfers, and openings
  • Participate in site-specific projects

Requirements

  • 10 years of professional experience: in logistics including leadership experience
  • Completed vocational training (EFZ): in logistics or higher technical school with a focus on logistics
  • Excellent communication skills and strong leadership and social competencies
  • High system affinity for standard applications (MS Office; SAP)
  • "Doer qualities" that come into play on the floor, with the people
  • Fluent German in written and spoken form
  • German (business fluent)
  • English (advantageous)
  • Italian (advantageous)

What we offer

  • Migros Bank: Better conditions for interest and fees on your private, investment, or mortgage accounts.
  • Individual training: Internal training and financial support for meaningful external education and training.
  • Vacation: 6 weeks of vacation for everyone and additional days for service anniversaries.
  • Maternity/paternity leave: 5 extra weeks of baby break (19 weeks) for mothers at 100% salary. Fathers enjoy 4 paid weeks.
  • Mobile subscription: The choice is yours: with Galaxus Mobile, Wingo, or Sunrise starting at CHF 10.- always connected.
  • Mobility: Save money with Gowago's all-in-one leasing, car sharing with Mobility, and fuel discounts.

Preselection of applications We continuously review incoming applications, create a preselection, and release it to the responsible person for review. The decision on whether to proceed or exit the recruitment process is communicated by phone or email. Response time: up to three weeks.

Interview with HR & department The interview is conducted with the responsible HR person and/or department, either virtually or on-site. The main focus is to learn more about personal and professional competencies and to assess the cultural fit for the company and team. Response time: up to three weeks.

Personality/performance test Under certain conditions, we use a personality test (BIP, Scales Test, etc.) as an additional step to support the selection process. The test results may be discussed personally and form the basis for further selection. Response time: up to one week.

Trial work A trial day or half-day provides the opportunity to better understand the future work environment and team members. Conversely, it gives us the chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Response time: up to three weeks.

Final interview before contract issuance Before issuing the contract, we conduct a final interview to clarify any open questions from both sides and finalize contractual details. Once all points are clarified, the written offer follows. Response time: up to one week.

Offer & contract issuance After our verbal job offer, the main contract points are verified again. Upon agreement, contract creation is initiated and sent by post. Response time: up to three weeks.
