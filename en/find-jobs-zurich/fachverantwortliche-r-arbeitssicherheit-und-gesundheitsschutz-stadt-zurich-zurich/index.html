Full description

We are the pulse of Zurich and keep the city in motion every 7.5 minutes. In order for this to work reliably and smoothly, a lot moves behind the scenes of the VBZ. Our colleagues work in over 50 professions: in technology, IT, administration, traffic development, offer planning, infrastructure and many other exciting areas of responsibility. Where we drive, Zurich lives. Get on.

Tasks

  • Everyday work takes place where people, vehicles and infrastructure meet. This is where your work starts: You support employees in ensuring that jobs, processes and activities at VBZ are safely designed and comply with legal requirements.
  • You advise and support the employer, the management and the line managers in the fulfilment of their responsibility for safety and health protection in the company.
  • By leading the SiKo Group, you will have a direct impact across all areas of the company. Close cooperation with the Safety Management System (SMS), Security (ZUKO), Occupational Health Management (BGM), environmental managers, fire protection and external security coordination agencies is part of your area of responsibility.
  • In addition, you initiate organization-wide training formats, develop safety standards in a targeted manner and ensure that findings from event investigations, operational tours, audits and the external exchange of experience are consistently transferred into sustainable improvements within the VBZ.

Profile

  • Completed higher technical examination as a safety specialist EKAS or as a safety engineer EKAS.
  • Sound expertise in handling hazardous materials.
  • Experience in training, instruction or auditing of security topics.
  • Understand operational processes in technical or physical working environments.
  • Very good knowledge with MS365, with occupational safety software safely (or similar), with the training software Rise 360 (or similar), with the § software Lexplus (or similar), etc.
  • Safety culture is not only rule-based for you, but teamwork. Rules create clarity, you demand these, explain them understandably and practically.
  • You deal with resistance constructively, objectively and remain solution-oriented in discussions.

We offer

  • A lot of appreciation for what you do: The people of Zurich love their VBZ. Working here is proud.
  • We want you to feel good: We are certified “Friendly Workspace” and are strongly committed to your health.
  • Discover the whole of Switzerland with the free GA – and thanks to generous discounts, the family also drives really well.
  • You can expect a challenging and effective area of responsibility in which you actively shape occupational safety and health protection in an operative environment. Your expertise flows directly into practical solutions, sustainable safety concepts and the further development of standards that make the daily work of thousands of employees noticeably safer.
