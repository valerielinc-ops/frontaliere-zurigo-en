Centre for Dentistry

Job opening immediately or by agreement, indefinitely

The Center for Dentistry of the University of Zurich (ZZM) is an internationally recognized clinic, training and research center. In six specialized clinics and one institute, dentists work at the forefront of knowledge and in interdisciplinary teams to achieve the highest quality in patient treatment, teaching and research.

Tasks

  • Technical responsibility for the stable and secure operation of our central specialist applications and medical IT devices over the entire life cycle
  • Planning, coordination and support of maintenance work, releases, updates and changes in cooperation with internal and external partners
  • Coordination of ICT requirements between departments, central computer science and external suppliers
  • Analysis of complex disruptions, coordination of remediation and ensuring sustainable management
  • Analysis and targeted further development of existing technical and organizational processes
  • Ensuring complete and up-to-date documentation
  • Ensuring compliance with relevant information security, data protection and compliance requirements
  • Close cooperation with professional application responsibility
  • Participation in ICT projects as well as in the introduction and replacement of specialist applications

Profile

  • Completed studies (FH or university) in computer science, business informatics or ICT
  • Several years of experience in technical application management
  • Secure handling of IT service management processes, in particular incident, problem, change and release management
  • In-depth knowledge in the operation and support of business-critical specialist applications as well as in the interaction of applications, interfaces and ICT infrastructure
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft 365 and Windows environments
  • Experience working with system suppliers and external ICT service providers
  • Good understanding of business processes and the ability to translate professional requirements into technical solutions and contexts
  • Strong analytical, conceptual and structured skills
  • Strength of communication and coordination as well as enjoyment of working with different stakeholders
  • Self-employed, reliable and responsible work
  • Strong quality and process understanding

We offer

In this versatile and responsible position, you will work closely with our system suppliers, UZH’s central computer science department and technical application responsibility, forming an important interface between the departments and ICT. With your technical and procedural know-how, you make a significant contribution to the stability, security and further development of our ICT services and support the efficient design of the processes in our clinics and central services. The employment takes place under cantonal conditions.
