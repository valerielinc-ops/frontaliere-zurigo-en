Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We advise individuals and companies about money, manage assets and offer attractive financial services. As we are growing strongly, we are looking for agreement reinforcements at our headquarters at Paradeplatz in Zurich.

  • You contact our customers by phone and make appointments with people who are interested in consulting.
  • You coordinate the appointments with our consultants.
  • You are interested in financial issues and use the know-how you build up and deepen with us effectively in conversation.
  • You are committed and goal-oriented and feel like a young team.
  • You speak accent-free High German.
  • You ideally bring experience in customer contact or in the financial industry.
  • You will be carefully introduced to your tasks, professionally coached and trained for conversation.
  • You work in an uncomplicated and open environment at a central location in Zurich. In this activity, there is no home office possibility.
  • You work at least 17 hours a week with at least one evening shift until 7.30 pm. You can plan your working days independently.
