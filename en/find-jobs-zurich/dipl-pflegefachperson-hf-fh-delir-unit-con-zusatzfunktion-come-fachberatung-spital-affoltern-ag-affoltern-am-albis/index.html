Full description

Do you want to apply and pass on your expertise? Do you already have experience in specialist consulting or are you interested in starting this new job? Then you are exactly right with us! Our specialized delir unit with 11 beds combines acute medical expertise in a manageable setting. The station belongs to the discipline of acute medicine. It has been developed for hospital-needed, elderly people who suffer from dementia/delirium or another condition and at the same time have an acute impairment of physical health. The Delir unit is designed according to the latest findings and needs of our patient group and has recently been modernized. As a nurse with us at the Delir-Unit, you will get the chance to make a big difference in a small team. They advise and support nursing professionals and nursing teams in nursing issues, promote high-quality care according to current standards and involve the station management or the nursing expert APN if necessary.
