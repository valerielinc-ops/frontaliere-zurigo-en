Occupation by: immediately or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 73800

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

YOUR TASKS

  • Responsible for instrumentation and service in outpatient surgical interventions in various specialist areas
  • Ensuring a smooth operating procedure, including preparation and follow-up of operating days
  • Creating and maintaining standards
  • Assumption of tasks in the absence of the surgical management
  • Cooperation in the correct instrument triage and patient storage
  • Care of the associated medical devices

Your profile

  • Training as an operational technician HF
  • Interest in outpatient surgery
  • Self-employment and forwarding
  • High sense of responsibility
  • Engaged, open personality with quick comprehension
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation

What we offer

  • Regulated working hours (excluding pikett, shift and weekend work) in the annual working time model
  • Assignment time planning always in consultation with the employees, even half days possible
  • Family, committed and motivated environment
  • Up to 6 weeks of vacation and unpaid leave possible after consultation
  • Company holidays in summer and over the turn of the year
  • Free parking, good public transport connections

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. You work with a modern medical clinic information system and can expect extensive training and development opportunities from us. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family responsibilities. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from an equivalent financial compensation. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.
