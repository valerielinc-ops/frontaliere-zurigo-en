Tasks

  • Coordination of the Faculty’s controlling, budgeting, forecasting and annual financial statement processes as well as operational implementation for the Dean’s Office
  • Independent development of the financial and resource planning of the Dean's Office as well as consolidation of the planning basics developed by the institutes at faculty level
  • Analysis and commentary on the financial and personnel figures as well as the preparation of user-friendly reporting and decision-making basis for the dean and the responsible governing bodies
  • Preparation and coordination of central financial processes such as development and financial planning and investment applications as well as processing of university controlling requests
  • Further development and optimization of controlling, reporting and planning processes
  • Participation in subject-related projects and representation of the faculty in university working groups and committees
  • Contact person for financially relevant issues within the faculty and central offices of the University of Zurich

Requirements

  • Completed training with further training in finance and accounting, controlling or business administration, e.g. Financial and accounting certificate or a comparable qualification
  • Professional experience in controlling, ideally in a complex organization
  • Experience in financial planning, budgeting, forecasting, reporting and data analysis
  • Strong analytical and conceptual skills, as well as the ability to prepare and communicate complex issues in an addressable manner
  • High independence and initiative as well as a networked and solution-oriented thinking and working method
  • Communication strength and pleasure in working with different stakeholders
  • Experience in the further development of processes and instruments as well as in project management
  • Affinity to digital solutions, data analysis and modern reporting tools
  • Very good German and English skills
  • Experience in higher education or public environment is beneficial
