Full description

Metal constructions are professionally assembled and adapted on site. You are responsible for the organization on construction sites and coordinate the assembly work. For our client we are looking for an activity

  • professionally assemble, adapt and repair metal constructions
  • independently organize construction sites and coordinate assembly work
  • competently support customers on site and professionally represent the operation
  • consistently ensure work safety, quality and clean execution
  • Requirements
  • Training as a metalworker / completed
  • First experience in team management of advantage
  • Craftsmanship as well as independent and forward-looking working methods
  • Good German skills and driver's license Category B Offer
  • Varied projects in a family metal construction company
  • Lots of personal responsibility and versatile assembly assignments
  • Collegial working environment with short decision paths
