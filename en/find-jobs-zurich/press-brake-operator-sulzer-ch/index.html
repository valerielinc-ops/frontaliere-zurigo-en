Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

We are seeking a skilled and motivated Fabricator / Press Brake Operator to join our manufacturing team. This position is primarily responsible for operating NC Press Brake to produce high-quality stainless-steel components according to engineering drawings and specifications. The ideal candidate will also assist with a variety of fabrication tasks, including operating a manual shear, rolling machine, drill press, and metal cutting saws. Key Responsibilities

  • Set up and operate NC press brake equipment to form stainless steel sheet metal components.
  • Read, interpret, and follow engineering drawings, blueprints, work instructions, and specifications.
  • Measure, mark, and lay out cuts and bends on metal materials accurately.
  • Operate a manual shear machine to cut metal to required dimensions.
  • Use hand and power tools, including angle grinders and deburring tools, to finish fabricated parts.
  • Operate fabrication equipment such as:
  • Metal rolling machines
  • Drill presses
  • Large band saws and/or cold saws
  • Other sheet metal fabrication equipment as needed
  • Inspect finished parts to ensure they meet dimensional and quality requirements.
  • Perform basic machine setup, tooling changes, and routine equipment maintenance.
  • Maintain an organized and safe work area.
  • Follow all company safety procedures and wear required PPE.
  • Work collaboratively with supervisors and team members to meet production schedules and quality standards.

Qualifications Required Skills and Experience

  • Previous experience in metal fabrication, sheet metal manufacturing, or press brake operation.
  • Ability to read and interpret blueprints, shop drawings, and work orders.
  • Strong understanding of measurement tools, including tape measures, calipers, squares, and other precision measuring instruments.
  • Experience measuring, marking, and laying out material for fabrication.
  • Ability to safely operate fabrication and metalworking equipment.
  • Experience using hand and power tools, including angle grinders.
  • Basic mechanical aptitude and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience working with stainless steel sheet metal.
  • Experience setting up and operating NC or CNC press brakes.
  • Forklift certification or experience (if applicable).
  • Knowledge of fabrication best practices and quality inspection methods.

Physical Requirements

  • Ability to stand and walk for extended periods throughout the workday.
  • Ability to frequently bend, reach, stoop, and lift materials.
  • Ability to lift and carry up to 50 pounds on a regular basis.
  • Ability to safely work around moving machinery and industrial equipment.
  • Ability to wear required safety equipment, including:
  • Safety glasses
  • Steel-toed footwear
  • Hearing protection
  • Cut-resistant gloves
  • Enhanced PPE as required by the task or work area

Work Environment

  • Manufacturing and fabrication shop environment.
  • Frequent exposure to metal dust, noise, moving equipment, and fabrication processes.
  • Work primarily involves stainless steel sheet metal fabrication.
  • Strict adherence to safety procedures and PPE requirements is essential.
