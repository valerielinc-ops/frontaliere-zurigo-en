Full description

Become part of our Transmission & Distribution team. We have been active in the field of planning systems for the transmission and distribution of electrical energy for decades. The treatment of the associated environmentally relevant aspects rounds off our services. Take on responsibility with us as: JUNIOR PROJECT ENGINEER IN LINE CONSTRUCTION 80-100% - ZURICH

YOUR TASKS

  • Support in the technical processing of projects, e.g. simple calculations, technical specifications, documentation and reports.
  • Collaboration in the planning of pipeline construction projects – from the initial route finding to preparatory project planning tasks to the support of later project phases.
  • Participation in coordination with internal departments and support of customer or authority meetings
  • Assistance in the development of decision-making bases, e.g. through research, technical comparisons or preparation of documents.
  • Support in the preparation of schedule and cost overviews as well as in the maintenance of project-related planning documents.

YOUR OPPORTUNITIES

  • Exciting and challenging projects with a broad and varied range of tasks
  • You will work in a growing organization for which sustainability and future-oriented technologies are important
  • Targeted personal development opportunities
  • Maximum flexibility in working with the option of mobile working / home office
  • Modern office space with the latest infrastructure in a convenient location near Zurich-Altstetten railway station

Qualifications

YOUR PROFILE

  • Completed studies (FH/Uni) in electrical engineering, civil engineering, energy technology or a comparable technical field.
  • First practical insights (e.g. through internships, working student activities or project work) in the field of pipeline construction or energy technology
  • Very good written and spoken German, as well as knowledge of French or Italian is an advantage
  • Basic understanding of the relevant technical standards and legal bases in the field of energy/pipeline construction
  • Initial experience in working with authorities or specialist agencies is helpful, but not mandatory
  • Careful, structured way of working and willingness to take on responsibility step by step.
  • Team-oriented, communicative way of working and enjoyment of working together in a team

Company Description AFRY provides engineering, project management and consulting services that support the energy transition and industrial transformation and strengthen societal resilience. With 18,000 experts worldwide, we combine global reach with local expertise and extensive industry knowledge to make the future of future generations sustainable.
