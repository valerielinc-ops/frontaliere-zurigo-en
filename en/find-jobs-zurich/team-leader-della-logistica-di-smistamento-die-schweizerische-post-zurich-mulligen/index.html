Full description

We are looking for a dynamic and committed personality who wants to take on the role of team leader in the “Sorting Logistics” team. With you as a team leader: our employees are in the best hands and we continue to make it, every day approx. 5.5 million letters and shipments without address to process.

You can do that.

  • You lead a team of about 20 employees and are responsible for the development and communication within the team.
  • As a strong management duo, you create optimal framework conditions for efficient processes with your deputy.
  • Together, you create deployment planning and create productive team meetings.
  • You continue to develop your employees with concrete goals and support them as a coach.
  • You lead by example by actively engaging in day-to-day business and actively supporting your team.
  • The working hours are in the early shift from 06:00-15:00, in the late shift from 14:05-23:00. This also includes occasional weekend assignments.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed a basic training EFZ, continue to have a Swiss certificate in the field of logistics / management or a comparable training or bring the willingness to make up for it.
  • You bring experience in leadership as well as in sorting logistics/logistics.
  • Your organizational skills help you set the right priorities in hectic situations.
  • You recognize the needs of your employees and create an open and transparent working environment.
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German as well as a safe handling of MS-Office round off your profile.

With you, flexibility becomes everyday life. We promote progressive teamwork and value a balanced working life. This position can also be taken over by two persons in "co-management". You can apply directly as a duo or we support you in the search for a suitable person.
