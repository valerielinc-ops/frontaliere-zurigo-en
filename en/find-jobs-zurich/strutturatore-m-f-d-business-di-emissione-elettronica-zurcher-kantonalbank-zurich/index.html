In Structuring for Structured Products – an integral part of ZKB’s trading division – you actively shape ZKB’s electronic issuance business. In a small, experienced team of five specialists, you work closely with trade, sales and other internal positions. Together they ensure that our customers are offered structured financial products efficiently, with high professionalism and first-class service.

Your tasks

  • As part of the Structuring team, you will be responsible for the electronic issue business for structured products as well as its continuous development.
  • You monitor trade deals and configure and optimize pricing.
  • Takes on the main role of contact with platform providers for structured products and other external partners
  • Track developments in the structured product ecosystem and identify trends and opportunities for the electronic emissions business
  • Stimulates initiatives within the organization to further develop the electronic emissions business and related products
  • Engage with internal stakeholders such as Sales, Trading, Risk, Legal Services, Back Office, and IT
  • Lead and coordinate projects and initiatives related to the electronic emissions business internally and externally
  • Find independently and together with other teams solutions for challenges and limitations – also outside your direct area of responsibility
  • Performs statistical analyses, identifies strengths, weaknesses and potentials in the business and implements corresponding optimization measures
  • Completed higher education in economics or finance; alternatively, technical-scientific studies with further economics or comparable experience
  • Several years of experience in the direct environment of issuance of structured products, ideally in structuring or trading
  • In-depth knowledge of products, processes and challenges in the emissions business as well as interest in continuously deepening this knowledge
  • Enjoying the establishment and maintenance of a network in the field of platform emission of structured products; ideally already knowledge of the Swiss platform ecosystem or the willingness to acquire it
  • You score with strong analytical skills and enjoy working with numbers, statistics and evaluations to continuously optimize and develop the business.
  • You convince as a team player with high initiative, curiosity and the willingness to support the organization outside of your direct area of responsibility.
  • Experience in Python or R as well as a basic understanding of databases round off your profile
  • Negotiable knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • Working in a dynamic trading environment with direct market reference
  • An exciting and diverse range of tasks with many network possibilities in the field of structured products
  • Great freedom for independent decisions and participation in the electronic emissions business
  • Extensive opportunities to develop personally and professionally
  • A centrally located and modern workplace
  • Comprehensive package with attractive basic salary, variable compensation and versatile benefits
