Full description

Swiss Quality Testing Services is a versatile laboratory service provider and a competent partner for the testing and assessment of food products and everyday items. Furthermore, the service spectrum includes operational inspections, labeling checks, and consulting services in the food and consumer goods sector.

Tasks

  • Execution of chemical-analytical and sensory tests for the quality and safety assessment of food, packaging, and consumer goods
  • Sample preparation using mechanical-physical methods for independent, timely, and high-quality sample processing
  • Application of the central Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS) for structured, traceable, and efficient documentation of laboratory processes
  • Maintenance, care, operation, and testing of analytical equipment in compliance with ISO 17025 requirements

Requirements

  • Experience: in the food packaging sector is an advantage
  • Completed vocational training (EFZ): as a chemical laboratory technician or comparable education
  • Flexibility, teamwork, and independence as well as an exact and efficient working method
  • Sense of responsibility and reliability in daily laboratory routine
  • High level of care and quality awareness in the execution of tasks
  • Enjoyment in working with PC systems and the Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS)
  • German (fluent)
  • English (good knowledge)

What we offer

  • Modern infrastructure: Diverse work zones and modern co-working spaces with advanced equipment
  • Social benefits: Attractive employment conditions with very good social benefits
  • Sports and fitness offers: Cost participation in subscriptions for over 50 Migros fitness/wellness/aqua parks
  • Migros Bank: Private and investment account with preferential interest rates and fee-free account management
  • Cumulus points: Additional Cumulus points in all Migros branches and various subsidiaries
  • Migros Club School: Generous financial contribution to courses at the Migros Club School
