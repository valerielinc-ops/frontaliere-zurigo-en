Full description

You actively shape the further development of our drainage business unit, connect processes, data and management systems to an effective overall management and create the basis for informed decisions and a successful digital transformation. Together with our departments, you will develop efficient processes, enhance process quality and drive the continuous improvement as well as the establishment, integration and further development of management and control systems in one of the largest urban infrastructure areas.

Ready for a job with meaning? We are now looking for reinforcements at the Werdhölzli site in Zurich Altstetten.

Tasks

  • You develop and take responsibility for the management systems in the areas of quality, environment, asset management, internal control system (ICS), health and records management and ensure their effective anchoring in the drainage business area.
  • You will continuously develop our business processes together with the process owners and recognize optimization and digitalization potential.
  • You will analyze business, process and operational data, further develop key figures including reporting solutions and create the basis for data-based control as well as future automation and AI applications.
  • You develop challenging concepts, risk analyses and decision-making principles and take over the project management or business analysis in digitisation and organisational projects.
  • You work closely with controlling, quality management, IT / digitalization and other partners and ensure a uniform implementation of standards, guidelines and governance.

Profile

  • University degree (FH/uni) in business engineering, business informatics, engineering, business administration, data science or comparable discipline, ideally supplemented by further training in quality or process management
  • Several years of professional experience in business analysis, process management, digitalization or data management as well as experience in managing projects
  • Experience in the analysis and optimization of business processes, in data-based control and in the implementation of digitalization and change projects.
  • Analytical, structured and networked thinking, implementation and solution competence, the ability to communicate complex relationships in an understandable way and the pleasure of actively driving change.
  • Very good knowledge of German (level C2), very good knowledge of the MS-365 environment, digital affinity as well as experience with data modeling, reporting and analysis tools

We are modern, down-to-earth, open-minded, all per you and want to make a difference. Together we are committed to a clean, innovative and sustainable Zurich.
