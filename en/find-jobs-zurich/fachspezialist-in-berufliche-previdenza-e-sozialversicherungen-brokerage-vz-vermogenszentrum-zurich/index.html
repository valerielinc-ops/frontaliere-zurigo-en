Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We advise our corporate clients comprehensively and neutrally and find suitable and affordable insurance solutions for them. As we grow strongly, we are looking for reinforcements for our insurance services team at our headquarters at Paradeplatz in Zurich.

  • You get different offers from the insurance companies and compare the main price and performance differences.
  • You maintain and update the provider database by obtaining relevant information.
  • You manage the administration and documentation for our customers in the corporate customer portal.
  • You will perform various administrative tasks and work on internal projects.
  • A KV degree in the insurance industry
  • Professional experience in occupational provision, UVG, UVGZ and KTG
  • A reliable and accurate way of working
  • Lots of motivation, flexibility and good teamwork
  • The desire to develop professionally and learn something new every day
  • You work in an uncomplicated and open atmosphere.
  • You can quickly take on responsibility and help shape and develop a unique company.
  • We support you individually and support you with external further education within the framework of your job profile.
  • Most of the positions are also open for a part-time stay, so that there is time for further education, family, sports and projects that are important to you.
  • Our surveys on satisfaction regularly confirm that our employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
