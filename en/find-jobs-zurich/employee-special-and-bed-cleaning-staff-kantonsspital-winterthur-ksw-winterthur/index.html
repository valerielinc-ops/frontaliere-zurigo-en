Original description

In the area of housekeeping for special and bed cleaning, the company is looking for a flexible personality for a temporary assignment of 12 months with the possibility of permanent employment. The working hours are from 6 a.m. to 6 p.m. 365 days a year according to the roster.

Tasks

  • Cleaning and provision of patient beds and preparation of bed contents (washing, autoclaving)
  • Basic cleaning services
  • Maintenance periods
  • Exit cleaning and final disinfections after isolation
  • General cleaning work on bed stations
  • Cleaning of general accommodation premises
  • Provision of staff rooms for new tenants
  • Room removals at exits

Profile

  • Several years of professional experience in cleaning or bed cleaning, preferably in health care and/or in the hotel industry
  • Good oral German skills
  • High quality and service awareness and good physical performance
  • High flexibility regarding changing work assignments as well as readiness to work on weekends is mandatory
  • Motivated, honest, team capable and friendly personality with a sense of responsibility
