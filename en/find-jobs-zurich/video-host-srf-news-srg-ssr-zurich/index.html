Video-Host SRF News — SRG SSR

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

The Information department produces numerous offers for SRF audio, video and digital channels. With versatile news and background formats as well as classifying discussion and advisory programmes on current events in politics, business and society, SRF makes it easier for the audience to form opinions – informative, competent and independent.

What are your tasks?

For the social media channels of SRF News we are looking for a strong and authentic personality as a video host. In this function, you prepare current news content for a young audience and convey it in an accessible, contemporary way. As a permanent member of our team, you are regularly present in front of the camera and thus make a significant contribution to fulfilling our public service mission.

  • As a host you are responsible for the presentation of news videos for the Instagram and TikTok channels of SRF News – translate alternately with other hosts They provide current news in line with target groups and are accessible to a young audience
  • You contribute your own topics and stories, research them independently and implement them effectively in your role as a host.
  • In close cooperation with the colleagues of the team, you are responsible for the creation of texts, scripts and/or storyboards as well as the execution of the recordings
  • They take over the independent editing of individual videos and ensure a high quality of design and content

What do you bring?

  • They are enthusiastic about current news and socio-political topics, follow world events closely and can react quickly and safely to current events.
  • They perform authentically, approachably and dynamically in front of the camera and convey even complex news in a comprehensible, precise and with appropriate ease – always with a view to a young audience (18–34 years).
  • You have journalistic experience in news and digital media, are used to working under time pressure and preparing news quickly and high quality
  • First moderation or host experience as well as knowledge of video production and editing are beneficial

What can you look forward to?

  • You are responsible for, shape and realize exciting and diverse content for a young target audience and bring your personal touch to the hosted formats
  • They contribute significantly to fulfilling the public service also for young people and thus make a meaningful contribution to society.
  • You work in the largest multimedia newsroom in Switzerland and are actively involved in everyday news
  • They look forward to working with a young, purposeful and creative team in an environment that is continuously evolving.

In addition to motivation letter and CV, we expect a short video, a maximum of 1 minute, a self-recording, in which you present us with a current news topic

What we offer

What does it take to reach the people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expert knowledge, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can contribute and develop. For the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: Welcome to the world of SRF!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, promote creativity and offer a lot of design space for new and high-quality products.
  • Shaping digitalization: Quality journalism is also digital for us. That’s why we explore new possibilities and experiment with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-medial. We design on the pulse of time and on the nerve of the audience.
  • Being human: We meet each other collegially, work independently and shape our working atmosphere individually. Our success is based on the personal strengths as well as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programs. In doing so, we act respectfully and independently; in this way we promote opinion-forming and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.
