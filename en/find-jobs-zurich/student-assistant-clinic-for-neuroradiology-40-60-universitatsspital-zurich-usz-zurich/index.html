To support our MR centre, we are looking for a:n motivated:n student who enjoys administrative tasks and working in an interdisciplinary environment. Possible operational days can be planned flexibly in consultation with the management.

Your main tasks

  • Telephone communication with internal bodies, patients
  • Reception, care and education at patient reception (explain MRI and CT questionnaires, give changing room instructions, etc.)
  • Regular processing of e-mails (e.g. recording of external assignments)
  • Checking and sending out orders for patients (MRI and CT appointments)
  • Investigations with disposition and radiology specialists in the context of MRI examinations
  • If necessary, assistance in moving patients in need

Your profile

  • Student: in psychology, medicine, health sciences or a related degree program, ideally shortly before completing the bachelor's degree or already in the master's degree program
  • First professional experience in dealing with patients or in a demanding customer contact is advantageous
  • High resilience and joy in dealing with people in sometimes challenging situations
  • Reliable, accurate and independent working methods, sense of responsibility and teamwork
  • Interested in neuroradiological investigations as well as working in an interdisciplinary environment

We offer
