Original description

Are you a detail-oriented finance professional who thrives in a dynamic, fast-paced environment? Do you have a passion for delivering accurate financial insights that drive real business decisions? If so, Swiss Re is looking for someone just like you to join our team in Seoul, South Korea!

As an Accountant within the SRAL Korea Finance team, you will play a pivotal role in ensuring the integrity and accuracy of financial reporting in compliance with local and international standards. This is a great opportunity to be at the heart of Swiss Re's financial operations in Korea, working with cutting-edge frameworks such as IFRS17 and K-ICS, and contributing meaningfully to the broader Global Finance Operations & Transformation (GFO&T) function.

Key Responsibilities

  • Prepare and deliver timely, accurate, and reliable financial reports in compliance with IFRS17 and K-ICS regulatory requirements
  • Monitor and reconcile financial data to ensure completeness and accuracy across all reporting cycles
  • Support the quarter-end and year-end close processes, ensuring all deadlines are met with precision
  • Collaborate closely with internal stakeholders across actuarial, risk, tax, and finance functions to ensure consistent and aligned financial outputs
  • Identify and implement process improvements to enhance efficiency and quality of financial reporting
  • Assist in regulatory submissions and liaise with local regulators and auditors as required
  • Maintain and update accounting policies and procedures in line with evolving local and international standards
  • Contribute to transformation initiatives within the GFO&T framework, supporting the continuous evolution of finance operations

About the Team The SRAL Korea Finance team is part of Swiss Re's Global Finance Operations & Transformation (GFO&T) division. Based in Seoul, our team is responsible for delivering timely, accurate, and reliable financial reporting locally, with a strong focus on Korean regulatory frameworks including IFRS17 and K-ICS. We are a collaborative and forward-thinking group, committed to maintaining the highest standards of financial integrity while embracing innovation and continuous improvement

About You

You are a motivated and meticulous finance professional who takes pride in delivering high-quality work. You bring a collaborative spirit and a proactive mindset, thriving in an environment where accuracy, accountability, and continuous learning are valued. You are comfortable navigating complex regulatory landscapes and enjoy working cross-functionally to solve challenges and drive results. [blank | PDF]

  • IFRS17 expertise: Solid understanding and hands-on experience with IFRS17 financial reporting standards
  • K-ICS knowledge: Familiarity with Korean Insurance Capital Standard (K-ICS) regulatory requirements and reporting
  • Accounting qualifications: Relevant professional accounting qualification (e.g., CPA, ACCA, or equivalent) with demonstrated experience in financial reporting and general ledger accounting
  • Analytical skills: Strong ability to analyse complex financial data, identify discrepancies, and provide accurate, well-reasoned conclusions
  • Regulatory reporting experience: Proven track record of preparing and submitting financial reports in compliance with local and international regulatory requirements [blank | PDF]
