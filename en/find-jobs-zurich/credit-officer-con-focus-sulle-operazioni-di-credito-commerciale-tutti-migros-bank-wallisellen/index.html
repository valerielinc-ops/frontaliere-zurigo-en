Full description

With us you can live out your enthusiasm for numbers and analyses every day. Not only that, but you make us better at what we do. We are looking for you: A personality who can correctly assess risks and move us forward.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Bank, Wallisellen, Industriestrasse 17-19, 8304 Wallisellen

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Examination, endorsement and approval of large and complex commercial credit operations
  • Preparation and presentation of funding applications to higher levels of competence incl. GL and VR
  • Advising on distribution units in corporate banking
  • Examination of loan applications in the field of "structured finance"
  • Reporting activities related to credit monitoring

Requirements

  • 8 years in the financing business of a bank: three years in the loan decision with credit competence
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): Banking & Finance / Business Administration
  • Several years of professional experience in credit analysis of commercial loans
  • Sound expertise in banking regulatory conditions
  • Good analytical skills
  • Good communication and stakeholder management skills
  • Teamplayer
  • in German (negotiation confident)
  • French (basic knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central place of work (GABS): Optimally accessible by train (10 minutes from Zurich HB) or car
  • Child care: up to CHF 300 per month per child to external child care
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Migros Bank: accounts, mortgages and preferential financial services
  • Advantages of cadre: One week holidays per year more for cadre members
  • Du culture: Experienced team spirit with transparent communication
  • Flat hierarchies: Permeable hierarchies and cross-level exchange

Contatto

Application and contact Moritz Forrer Team Leader HR Business Partner

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department (FFB-Group): The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team.
  • Discussion with case presentation: Specific expertise is examined on the basis of a case or other assessment instruments. After processing, the result of the leader is presented and then discussed together. Usually, the team is also represented at the presentation. As a rule, the conversations are conducted by the superior, without a representative from the HR. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
