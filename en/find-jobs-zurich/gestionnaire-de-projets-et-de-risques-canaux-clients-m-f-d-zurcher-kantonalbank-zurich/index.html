The Client Channels division of the Zürcher Kantonalbank is responsible for the design and further development of our digital and physical channels, which serve as central business cards to our customers. Through its activities, the division ensures that our customers can use the products and services of the Zürcher Kantonalbank exactly where and how it is easiest for them – both online and offline.
