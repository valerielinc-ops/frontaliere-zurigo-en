Location: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Occupation by: August 2027

Type of employment: Temporary

Reference number: 72075

Start your education in the largest medical network in Switzerland – Hirslanden Clinic is looking for learners from August 2027

During your education you will learn

  • The preparation of cold, warm and frozen food
  • The procurement, storage and processing of foodstuffs
  • The co-design of the menu
  • The use of various cooking methods
  • The use of modern cooking appliances
  • Compliance with regulations on hygiene, occupational safety and environmental protection
  • Appreciative communication with employees, guests and suppliers
  • Guest and team-oriented thinking

You bring that with you

  • Completed school education (minimum sec A or B)
  • Foreign language skills are an advantage
  • Joy in dealing with food and its preparation
  • Good understanding and skillful hands
  • Strong hygiene awareness and sense of order
  • Good sense of smell and taste
  • Guest and team-oriented thinking
  • Creativity and experimentation
  • High load capacity in hectic situations
  • Willingness to work on weekends and public holidays

The following documents shall be submitted

  • Motivation letter with current CV incl. Photo
  • School certificates from 1st high school
  • Reports of trial days (vocational internships)
  • Provide two reference persons (teacher) with telephone number and e-mail address

Our benefits for employees

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. You can expect extensive training and development opportunities from us. We offer you a modern, pleasant working atmosphere as well as a thorough introduction to the kitchen world. Your working days and times are organized in a shift operation from Monday to Sunday, between 6 a.m. and 9:15 p.m. and without partial service. As a learning cook you wear a uniform workwear with us. The changing time is paid off at a 100% fee in the form of four additional paid days off. If you travel by public transport, we will contribute to your travel costs. This means that you can purchase the ZVV or Z-Pass for your commute at a discounted price and enjoy free travel in your free time throughout the ZVV area or Z-Pass validity area. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.

For additional information, Andreas Schels, Head of Kitchen, is available at T, or Alice Nutt, HR recruitment partner, at alice.nutt@hirslanden.ch. We look forward to your contact.

Please note that we cannot consider paper dossiers (not returned) or applications by e-mail. Therefore, please apply exclusively online via this tool.

Mixed teams work better. We are proud of the experience of more than 10,000 employees from more than 80 nations. Their knowledge and experience are our strength. Every day we promote diversity, equal opportunities and equal pay for equal work. In 2021, the Financial Times recognized us as one of the “Leaders in Diversity” in the healthcare industry in Europe.
