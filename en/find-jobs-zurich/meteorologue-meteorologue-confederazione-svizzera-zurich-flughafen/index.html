Original description

  • Ensure the operational service (24/7) according to the service plan in the weather control center
  • Assume responsibility for weather monitoring, issuance of warnings, product creation and weather advice for specific users according to work plan or instruction
  • Maintain communication with our customers and partner organisations and advise them
  • Write articles, blogs and social media posts and hold briefings via video conference, telephone or on site
  • After initial internal training, assume the function as service manager
  • Take on additional tasks as agreed (e.g. in projects or committees)
  • University degree (Master) with in-depth expertise in meteorology
  • Fulfilment of BIP-M requirements (for details see MeteoSwiss website, Basic Instruction Package for Meteorologists according to WMO No. 1083)
  • enjoyment of working in the team
  • Will and health suitability for work in the tour operation with different working hours (i.e. also early, late, night, weekend and public holiday services)
  • Rapid comprehension and resilience (ability to make quick and well-founded decisions in hectic situations); high integrity, reliability and impeccable reputation
  • perfect written and oral language in German and good active knowledge of a second official language (preferably French) as well as passive knowledge of the third official language
  • Furthermore, good communication and editorial skills in English are also necessary (WMO requirements regarding aviation).
  • Very good skills and interest in communication activities for different target groups on different channels (media, blogs, social networks, briefings)
  • Valuable additional qualifications: reference to aviation, good knowledge of climate and climate change, experience or interest in project management, journalistic experience or affinity with social media
