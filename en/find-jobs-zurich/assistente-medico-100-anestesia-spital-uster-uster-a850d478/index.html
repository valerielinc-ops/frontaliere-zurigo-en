Personal – competent – close Welcome to Uster Hospital, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As a central pillar of healthcare in the Zurich Oberland and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working atmosphere that is characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you will experience the highest level of professional competence every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and supported in your professional development. Become part of our committed community and pursue the vision of a "regional and digital health hub in Zurich East" with us! In our anaesthesia department (Cat. B), 7000 anaesthesia procedures are performed annually; divided into paediatric and adult anaesthesia in the areas of general surgery, traumatology, gynaecology/obstetrics, orthopaedics, urology, hand surgery and ENT. Our regional anaesthesia share is 25%. Our department manages the postoperative pain service together with the pain nurse, is involved in patient care in the interdisciplinary intensive care unit, provides the medical management of the rescue service and is involved in the emergency medical service of the region.
