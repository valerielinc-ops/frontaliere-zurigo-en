Original description

: As a market development unit of Mobiliar, the Management of Brokerage Business in Zurich is responsible for the partnership with professional brokers. For our team in the market development company department, we are looking for a committed personality who enjoys the support and further development of broker relationships and uses their professional know-how in property insurance for our corporate customers.

The position is aimed at specialists from brokerage support or underwriting property insurance who want to take the next career step.

This is what they do with us

In the role as (senior) broker supervisor:in

  • actively build personal broker relationships and maintain them long term.
  • You are the first contact person for our brokers and convince with expertise and personality.
  • actively support strategic market development.
  • Perform independent annual meetings with brokers and create well-founded annual planning.

In the role as underwriter property insurance corporate business

  • Analyze risks of medium and large companies in Switzerland.
  • Develop individual coverage concepts, develop offers and independently draw risks within your competences.
  • calculate premiums, create policies and accompany the dossiers of A-Z.
  • Take part in risk inspections and contribute your technical understanding.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed basic training in the insurance sector as well as further education at the level of specialist card / insurance diploma
  • Experience in brokerage business or in the support of professional brokers
  • Experience and in-depth knowledge of national non-life insurance business
  • Initiative and communicative personality that convinces through negotiation skills and assertiveness
  • Strong customer orientation, high personal responsibility and motivation in personal dealings with our brokers
  • Reliable and solution-oriented working method
  • Mother tongue German, every other language is a plus

Working with us

A motivated and customer-oriented team is waiting for you. Together we attach great importance to a communicative and solution-oriented working atmosphere.

The Insurance Supervision Act requires for the exercise of this function the conclusion of insurance intermediary /-in VBV, profile non-life.
