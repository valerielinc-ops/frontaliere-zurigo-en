Full description

Become part of our Transmission & Distribution team. We have been active in the field of planning systems for the transmission and distribution of electrical energy for decades. The treatment of the associated environmentally relevant aspects rounds off our services. Take on responsibility with us as: JUNIOR CIVIL ENGINEER 80-100% - ZURICH

YOUR TASKS

  • Collaboration in the provision of engineering services in the construction of high-voltage lines (overhead lines and cable lines)
  • Support in the project planning, calculation and tendering of mast foundations, cable pipe blocks, supporting structures and electrical equipment
  • Accompanying participation in construction management for high-voltage overhead lines
  • Assisting with the commissioning of completed plants throughout Switzerland

YOUR OPPORTUNITIES

  • Exciting and challenging projects with a broad and varied range of tasks
  • You will work in a growing organization for which sustainability and future-oriented technologies are important
  • Targeted personal development opportunities
  • Maximum flexibility in working with the option of mobile working / home office
  • Modern office space with the latest infrastructure in a convenient location near Zurich-Altstetten train station

Qualifications

YOUR PROFILE

  • Degree in civil engineering (ETH/FH) or comparable training – career starters are also welcome
  • Initial practical experience in the field of high-voltage overhead lines, e.g. through internships, working student activities or theses, is an advantage
  • Very good written and spoken German, other language skills (French or Italian) are a plus
  • Strong quality awareness and interest in technical contexts
  • Careful, reliable and structured way of working as well as the willingness to take responsibility and learn
  • Team-oriented, communicative and flexible personality with a customer- and cost-conscious mindset

Company Description AFRY provides engineering, project management and consulting services that support the energy transition and industrial transformation and strengthen societal resilience. With 18,000 experts worldwide, we combine global reach with local expertise and extensive industry knowledge to make the future of future generations sustainable.
