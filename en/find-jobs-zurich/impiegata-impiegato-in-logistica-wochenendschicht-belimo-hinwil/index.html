Full description

Make sure our logistics and production run smoothly. In the weekend shift, you take responsibility, bring structure to the warehouse and actively promote efficiency in production and quality.

What will you do

  • Acceptance of goods, control and warehousing incl. System entries
  • Order picking of customer and production orders
  • Production supply and material scaffolding
  • Internal material transport and ensuring material flow
  • Packaging and provision for dispatch
  • Implementation of inventories and inventory controls
  • Compression and optimisation of storage areas
  • Ensuring order, cleanliness and compliance with ESD requirements
  • Cooperation in process improvements as well as ensuring quality, deadlines and efficiency

Work model weekend shift

  • Working hours: Saturday and Sunday 6:00-18:00 (including 2h break per day)
  • Pensum: 58%, advantage: Belimo pays the salary analogous to a 65% pension plus a weekend allowance
  • The training takes place during the week in the early and late shift between 6:00 and 23:00 for about 4-8 weeks.

Who you are

  • Logistician: with federal EFZ or comparable training
  • Mind. 5 years of professional experience as a logistics specialist: in an international industrial, commercial or logistics company
  • Readiness for weekend work
  • German in Word and Writing (min. B2)
  • Forklift identification (including electrode control device)
  • High load capacity under term pressure
  • Teamwork and flexibility
  • Independent, precise and reliable working method
