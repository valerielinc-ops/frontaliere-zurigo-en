Full description

Key responsibilities Please apply via this Link and not via the red button below. Your application runs via another tool and can thus be processed faster and more efficiently.

  • Design and implementation of cloud infrastructure for SaaS and PaaS usage according to regulatory requirements and industry best practices
  • Develop the hybrid cloud platform architecture blueprint (SAD), including network, identity, and security for Azure and AWS landing zones
  • Select and design essential platform services (compute, storage, monitoring, automation) to support SaaS and PaaS cloud workloads
  • Active coordination of roadmaps with the product owner, IT engineers, and internal customers
  • Gather and document relevant cloud architecture principles, standards, and best practice guidelines

The team As part of the new "Cloud Center of Excellence" team in the IT department of UBS Card Center AG, you will shape our journey to the cloud by contributing to a strong cloud governance framework together with cloud security specialists and cloud engineers.

Your skills and experience

  • University degree in (business) informatics or a similar field, or comparable higher education (university of applied sciences)
  • Strong quality and responsibility awareness, and excellent communication skills (German/English)
  • Hands-on experience with cloud architecture and the offerings of major hyperscalers in a production environment, as well as with underlying technologies (virtualization, storage, DevOps)
  • Understanding of the high regulatory requirements in the banking/credit card environment (PCI/DSS, data residency)
