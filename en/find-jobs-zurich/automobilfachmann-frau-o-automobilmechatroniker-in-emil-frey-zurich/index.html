Full description

What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family company with over 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessories as well as the provision of financial services. The Zurich-Altstetten dealership – with its location at the headquarters of the Emil Frey Group – presents the model palettes of eight world brands as well as a large selection of occasions. The company also includes the brand workshops, a carrosserie department and a spare parts warehouse. Their tasks
