Role overview

In cooperation with the current PO, you are responsible for the development of the information systems of our radiology services.

  • In cooperation with the current PO, you are responsible for the development of the information systems of our radiology services.

Application process

  • As the application environment has been recently changed (RIS + PACS), we need to implement innovative solutions to support current and future users.
  • They will be involved in a review of workflows, business processes and integration into the Group’s environment in order to optimize the systems used and increase the value for the company.
  • As the group continues to grow, fast and efficient means are needed to integrate new centres or introduce solutions to partners.
  • They are responsible for implementing our solutions quickly and efficiently in new locations or in locations that are to be integrated into our infrastructure.
  • At the interface between radiologists, TRM and our partners, you understand the challenges of the users and are at the same time familiar with the technical and technological aspects of radiology.
  • We expect innovative proposals from you to further develop our radiology services and make them even more attractive to prescribers and patients.
  • Biomedical engineering training, TRM or training recognised as equivalent.
  • Good knowledge of imaging solutions (PACS/RIS/VNA/...), HL7 and DICOM standards.

Additional details

  • German as a mother tongue, excellent French and good knowledge of English. A multidisciplinary job with interesting projects. A family, personal working atmosphere
  • Development opportunities within the clinic and the Swiss Medical Network Support for individual training wishes
  • An attractive wage with complete and comprehensive social benefits
  • Geneva, Lausanne, Sitten, Freibourg, Solothurn, Basel or Zurich (orders throughout Switzerland)
