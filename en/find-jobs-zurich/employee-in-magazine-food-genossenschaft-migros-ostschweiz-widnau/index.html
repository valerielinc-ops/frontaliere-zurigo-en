Full description

Do you enjoy taking on responsibility and mastering versatile tasks? You are reliable, like to pack and make sure that our shelves are always perfectly filled 🛒 and the goods get into the range on time? 💪 Then you are exactly right with us! ✅ Let us be «tämä success». 🤝🧡

Mansioni

  • You are responsible for loading and unloading the trucks 🚛 and ensure that the goods find their way into the range on time.
  • You make sure that the shelves are always well filled and that the drinks and frozen products are presented perfectly.
  • Provision of PET containers and waste bags.
  • With your commitment, you help ensure that your area remains neat, clean and inviting ✨.

Requirements

  • Professional experience: in warehouse management or retail trade
  • Completed basic or further training: in the retail trade
  • You have a very good physical condition and bring a high commitment 💪🚀.
  • An independent and precise way of working distinguishes you – you perform your tasks with great care and a high degree of personal responsibility. ✅🎯
  • You are very capable of teamwork and convince through your reliability and flexibility. 😆😆
  • With your friendly approach 😊🤝 you create a pleasant atmosphere for customers and team.
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Occupational pension provision: Attractive pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Cumulus Points: Multiple Cumulus Points on food and nonfood items
  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation in self-selected courses
