Full description

Shape the energy future with us: With your feel for data, efficiency and smart solutions, you make energy performance measurable and optimization potential actionable.

Responsibilities

  • You are responsible for end-to-end energy data management in operations, ensure the quality of energy data and continuously develop the associated processes and tools.
  • Energy data reports, plausibility checks and analyses of energy, performance and profitability are part of your responsibilities – in the event of deviations, you initiate suitable measures in a targeted manner.
  • You prepare annual reports for customers and authorities and ensure correct energy data flows and compliance with relevant requirements for concessions and contracts.
  • As a competent point of contact, you advise customers on energy data and billing and support internal employees with energy-related operational optimizations and the development of decarbonization solutions.
  • You actively contribute your expertise to cross-functional projects, workshops and customer events and help shape the further development of energy and data management.

Profile

  • You have a degree in environmental science, energy or engineering (HF/FH/Uni), or equivalent professional experience.
  • You have sound knowledge of energy technology and are confident using MS Office, databases and relevant specialist systems.
  • You think analytically and commercially, recognize connections and develop effective solutions from them.
  • With initiative, a commitment to quality and a desire to shape things, you actively drive improvements and new solutions forward.
  • As a communicative and confident person, you enjoy working in a team and have very good German skills
  • English and French are an advantage.

We offer

  • More energy for your life: Through mobile working, flexible working models and part-time options, you can actively shape your working hours and organize them in the way that best suits your life. For example, you can purchase up to 2 additional weeks of vacation and work part-time at all levels. Job sharing/splitting and unpaid leave are of course also possible with us.
  • Travel sustainably and at a discount: With the mobility contribution of up to 600 francs per year, you can travel affordably and sustainably – whether you want to cycle through Zurich or explore Switzerland by train.
  • An investment in your future: We not only place importance on paying fair and transparent salaries, but also ensure that more of your salary remains in your pocket. We cover 60% of the savings contributions and additionally pay an interest rate above the statutory minimum on the capital saved.
  • An energy boost for your free time: We pay 100 francs into your Lunch-Check card every month. In addition, you benefit each year from further fringe benefits worth 150 francs and attractive mobile phone plans.
  • Development and further training: With our talent management, we specifically support your development through a wide range of internal and external training opportunities as well as exciting development opportunities.
  • Meaningful work: Sustainability and meaningful work are not just words for us: Help shape Zurich and the future with us. Together with us, unleash energy for a better tomorrow.

ewz has been dynamic and visionary every day for over 130 years. Whether sustainable energy production, smart energy solutions or innovative networking technology: Develop with us today what will matter tomorrow.
