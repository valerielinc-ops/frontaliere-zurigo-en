Full description

Become part of our team! We search immediately or by agreement temporarily until 31. July 2027 with the option of extension a dedicated specialist supervising ISR 50% for the accompaniment of students in the ISR setting. They work 4 days a week and accompany pupils with increased need for support in everyday school life. They work closely with teachers, specialists and guardians.

Tasks

  • Care, accompaniment and support of children and adolescents in accordance with the job framework, mission statement
  • Teaching supportive, social and leisure pedagogical activity inward and outward
  • Active participation in day-to-day operations (pedagogy, household, care, general organisation, administration)
  • Appropriate parental work taking into account different cultural, social and linguistic origins
  • Ensuring the companionship school/hoard
  • Participation in support practices
  • Participation in school development, participation in various internal working groups

Profile

  • Degree as a specialist childcare EFZ
  • Good written and oral communication skills in German
  • Accustomed to working independently, safe appearance
  • ISR experience is beneficial
  • Resilience and high flexibility in cooperation
  • Experience in educational work and interdisciplinary cooperation is beneficial

We offer

  • An experienced, committed and humorous team
  • A varied, interesting and independent activity in a versatile, changing field of activity of the school
  • Possibility to develop and contribute your own ideas as part of the day school
  • Professional exchange with teachers, school curative teachers and other specialists
  • A varied everyday life in the middle of the city of Zurich
  • Centrally located and well connected to public transport
