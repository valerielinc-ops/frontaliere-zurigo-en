Full description

An audit at the Meilen District Court allows students to enter the legal system. The audit takes a net year (14 months), which is credited as a practical year within the meaning of the admission conditions for the examination of the lawyer.

Tasks

  • As an auditor, you support the court clerks in the editorial of reasons for decisions.
  • You help to record and keep records.
  • Various other legal and administrative work falls within your remit.
  • Participation in negotiations and participation in the processing of civil and criminal law cases.
  • Insight into different areas of law and different types of proceedings.

Requirements

  • Completed law studies (Mlaw)
  • Very good knowledge of the official language
  • Good reputation (excerpt of criminal record)
  • Ability to act (certificate of action)

We offer

  • Varied everyday work with demanding and exciting legal activity
  • collegial team and pleasant working atmosphere
  • Individual support and support by court clerks and judges
  • Tailor-made training and further training opportunities within the framework of the program of the Zurich Civil and Criminal Courts
  • Modern workplace in walking distance to Lake Zurich
  • Regular promotion to a job as a court clerk if appropriate
  • Part-time employment on request

Contact

The Meilen District Court has to fill auditorates at regular intervals throughout the year. Please submit your application early and indicate when you can take up the position. Besides the planable positions, short-term employment opportunities also arise.

Applications are also accepted before the official graduation ceremony, provided that an early certificate of completion or a sheet of music is included, which shows that all required examination subjects and the Master's thesis have been successfully completed.

Send your application online with the usual documents (CV, Bachelor and Master Diploma with sheet music, criminal record extract, certificate of action, possible work certificates) or send it to the leading court clerk MLaw E. Szczogiel (058 111 21 21).

About us

The Meilen District Court is a first-instance civil and criminal court and employs a good 65 people. Over 4,000 civil and criminal proceedings are settled every year.

Penalty: 50-100%
