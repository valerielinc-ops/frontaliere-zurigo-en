Full description

Vocational trainer:in for polymechanics:inside learning (m/f/d)

80%-100%, Zurich, permanent

ETH Zurich is the study, research and workplace of around 30,000 people from over 120 countries. The Department of Physics (D-PHYS) pursues a diverse research agenda and a broad teaching programme, from research into basic principles of nature to innovative technologies for society. A large part of the approximately 1,500 Bachelor, Master and PhD students, postdocs and professors, learners as well as scientific and service employees are involved in experimental research in one way or another.

ETH Zurich is committed to basic vocational training: a total of around 200 apprentices are trained, about 65 of them in the Department of Physics in four different occupations. These four professions work closely together and support each other in the training of learners.

To strengthen our vocational training team in the technical companies of the Department of Physics, we are looking for 1. February 2027 or by appointment:e Vocational trainers:in for polymechanics:inside learning.

Job description

In this function, you guide and accompany the polymechanics: inner-learners over the entire apprenticeship period, promoting them professionally, methodologically and in their social and self-competence. In addition, you support the head of the apprentice workshop in the organization, operation and further development of the apprentice workshop as well as the vocational training of polymechanics at ETH Zurich. In addition, you take over the deputy of the head of the workshop.

They design and carry out the first two years of basic training in the form of internal courses in our ÜK-exempt training company and continuously develop training content and modules. In addition, you teach the learners of the professions electronics technician:in, physics laboratory:in and constructor:in the practical basics of materials processing within the framework of their basic education.

In addition, you are responsible for carrying out trial apprenticeships and actively participate in the recruitment of future learners. They plan the rotation places for the third and fourth year of apprenticeship, act as contact persons for the vocational trainees on the rotation places and support them in the professional support and promotion of the learners. In addition, you accompany the learners in the preparation for the partial examination. Cooperation with the central vocational training team is another part of your activity. In projects, you take an active role in implementing and developing a modern type of vocational training.

Profile

  • Completed training as a polymechanic:in
  • At least 5 years of professional experience
  • Enjoyment of working with young people and already first experience in learning support
  • Readiness to complete further training SVEB 1, Federal Professional ID trainer:in or an already completed corresponding proof of formal qualifications
  • Secure handling of CNC/CAD systems and MS Office
  • Structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Excellent knowledge of German in words and writing as well as basic knowledge of English
  • Commitment to cross-sectoral cooperation in vocational training, both within the ETH and beyond

We offer

  • Design leeway in the implementation and further development of polymechanics: in training
  • Diverse internal training opportunities through courses as well as support for external training
  • An attractive working environment on Campus Hönggerberg with various additional offers such as discounts, e.g. public transport subscriptions and car sharing, a broad sports offer of the ASVZ, child care and attractive pension services
  • A friendly, dedicated and helpful D-PHYS team
