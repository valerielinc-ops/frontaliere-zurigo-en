Original description

Are you an experienced operations professional with a passion for driving efficiency and leading external partners? Do you enjoy working in a dynamic, international environment where you can influence processes and make a real impact? Then this could be the right opportunity for you. About the Role We are looking for a C&S (Credit and Surety) Outsourcing Expert to join our C&S Operations team in Madrid, reporting to the Global Head of C&S Operations. In this role, you will oversee the outsourced Credit Control (Technical Accounting) activities within the Credit line of business. You will ensure that our external service provider delivers high-quality services in line with agreed SLAs, controls, and business expectations. You will work closely with internal stakeholders, act as a key point of contact for all credit control related matters, and play an important role in strengthening processes, enhancing service delivery, and supporting continuous improvement initiatives within a globally connected organization.

The role includes close collaboration with our external service provider, particularly with the operational team based in Bucharest.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead and oversee the outsourced Technical Accounting team, ensuring delivery against agreed targets, service levels, and quality standards
  • Build and maintain effective relationships with key internal stakeholders and act as a central point of contact for credit control topics
  • Drive process improvements and operational efficiencies across credit control activities, in collaboration with global teams and Operational Excellence
  • Ensure appropriate team structure, resource allocation, and performance monitoring through relevant KPIs, dashboards, and reporting
  • Provide analytical insights and support governance and regulatory reporting, identifying risks and improvement opportunities early
  • Strengthen internal controls and ensure data quality standards are consistently met
  • Manage the relationship and performance of the external service provider, including coordination with internal and external auditors

About You

You are a results-oriented professional with experience in operational environments, ideally within insurance or financial services. You bring a structured and analytical approach, combined with a strong sense of ownership and accountability. You are comfortable working with external partners and stakeholders and are motivated to drive improvements in processes, performance, and service delivery. You demonstrate a collaborative mindset, adaptability, and an interest in working in a global and multicultural environment, while maintaining a strong focus on quality, risk awareness, and client service.

Your profile

  • 5+ years of relevant experience gained within a corporate insurance environment at insurers or within a broking organisation, working in an operational capacity, with proven experience in credit control / technical accounting
  • Experience in third party management, ideally in a corporate insurance or technical accounting context
  • Proven ability to drive process improvements and operational efficiency
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management skills, including the ability to influence and negotiate
  • Fluent English; additional European languages are an advantage
  • Solid technical skills, particularly in MS Office (Excel)
  • High level of integrity, adaptability, and a structured, detail-oriented working style
