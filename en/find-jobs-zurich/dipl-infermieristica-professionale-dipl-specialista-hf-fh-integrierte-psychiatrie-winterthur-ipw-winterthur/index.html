Pflegfachmann HF / FH 80% Winterthur Apply now In the acute care unit for people with dementia, we offer 16 treatment places for people with a mani-fixed dementia diagnosis who are in temporary mental crisis and need a protected framework in the acute psychiatric setting. The interprofessional team treats patients with the close involvement of relatives, assignees and follow-up caregivers. Through medical, psychiatric, nursing, social service and therapeutic measures, an individual treatment plan for each patient is created and targeted exit management is pursued. These are your tasks

  • Design and management of the care process in the caregiver work with the patient, relatives and internal and external specialists
  • Implementation of medical and nursing work
  • Cooperation with relatives within the framework of family-centered care
  • Acting in psychiatric and somatic emergency situations
  • Co-design and implementation of change processes This is what they bring with them.
