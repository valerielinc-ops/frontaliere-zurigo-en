About us

We enchant the world with chocolate! Lindt & Sprüngli (Switzerland) AG is a subsidiary of the global Lindt & Sprüngli Group with its headquarters and production facility in Kilchberg on Lake Zurich. In Altendorf we operate our own logistics center, while the Lindt Cocoa Center in Olten produces cocoa mass for our own needs as well as for other European production facilities. Throughout Switzerland we proudly operate 15 Lindt shops, including iconic locations such as the Jungfraujoch and the Swiss public transport company in Lucerne.

Over 1000 employees from more than 50 nations contribute to our success.

What you do with us

  • You check the quality and quantity of raw materials delivered
  • You produce products according to recipes on a modern system
  • You control your manufactured products on their quality
  • You monitor and control the production process at the plant
  • You carry out cleaning work in the production area
  • You bring in your ideas to further improve our processes

When you fit in with us

  • Good students or very good students
  • Good German skills (at least level B1)
  • Interest in food and its composition
  • Enjoying machines, controls and modern high-tech systems
  • Skills and responsibility
  • Hygiene and cleanliness are a matter of course for you
  • Getting up early is no problem for you – work starts at 06.30

We are all unique!

We recruit free of prejudices and take into account skills and qualifications without affecting personal characteristics such as gender, age, sexual orientation, origin, religion, marital status, impairments, etc.

Well-being Health is important to us. We support you in staying fit with a sports premium, ergonomics consulting, healthy nutrition and other health initiatives.

Development Learning is written GREAT. For personal and professional development, you have various exciting possibilities at your disposal.

Mobility We support you with a monthly mobility premium and e-bikes on site. You can also benefit from the REKA card.

Family friendly environment - properly insured Our social security schemes, as well as maternity leave, go far beyond the mandatory - for your own protection as well as for your loved ones.

Flexibility Flexible working hours, home office and part-time work are possible depending on the function. We also offer you the option to increase the holidays.

Discounts Numerous discounts such as a top staff restaurant, discounts in the Lindt shops or great leisure activities are available.
