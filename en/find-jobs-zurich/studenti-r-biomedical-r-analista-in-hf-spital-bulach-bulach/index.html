<b>Bülach Hospital grows - grows with it.</b>

Tasks

  • Perform blood, urine and tissue analyses to obtain important information about the health status of the patient:
  • Operating modern laboratory equipment and automated systems to deliver accurate and fast results
  • Close cooperation with various professional groups
  • Strict compliance with hygiene regulations and quality standards to ensure the safety of patients: inside and staff
  • documentation of work steps; and
  • Results to provide a reliable basis for diagnosis
  • Identification of pathogens such as bacteria, viruses or fungi
  • Work in various fields such as clinical chemistry, hematology and microbiology
