Put to the Point

Man and machine belong together in aviation. We are looking for a doctor with flair for the combination of medicine, psychology and safety in complex systems. The Aeromedical Institute is a mission-relevant key unit of the Air Force and is civilly accredited as an Aeromedical Center according to EASA and FAA. You can complete further training for the AME with us.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: permanent
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20669
  • Place of work: Bettlistrasse 16, 8600 Dübendorf

You can make this contribution

  • Perform aptitude and fitness assessments for members of military air service as well as for military and civilian specialists
  • Consultation hours including care and follow-up treatment independently
  • Analyze flight medical questions and derive concrete measures from them
  • Impart aero-medical teaching internally and help shape the specialist development
  • Support and document processes of the Aeromedical Center according to EASA and FAA specifications
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration with psychology, flight safety and operations

This makes them unique.

  • Specialist title General internal medicine or occupational medicine
  • Advanced training or in-depth knowledge of aero-medical topics
  • Engaged, team capable and prudent personality
  • Interested in further training as an accredited aeromedic (AME)
  • Good active knowledge of at least one second official language, preferably passive knowledge of a third official language and good knowledge of English

Your commitment to security and freedom The Command Operations (Kdo Op) is responsible for all military operations and operations of the Swiss Army. The Kdo Op includes the Luftwaffe, the Army, the four territorial divisions, the SWISSINT Competence Center, the Special Forces Command and the Military Police. Around 40% of the approximately 2,500 employees are civil employees – from engineers to aircraft mechanics to analysts.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
