Full description

  • The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich treats and cares for mentally ill people of all ages. The more than 2,500 employees of the 55 different professions make their contribution to the holistic care of patients in interdisciplinary teams, from clarification and therapy to follow-up care. Our work is based on mutual trust and personal responsibility and together we promote an innovative corporate culture. As a university clinic, we are successfully involved in national and international research projects. As one of the leading clinics in Switzerland, the Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich employs around 2,000 people. She is responsible for the psychiatric care of the Zurich region, takes on tasks as a university hospital in research and teaching as well as in education and training and is very well connected internationally. The Center for Children’s Youth Forensics (ZKJF) is part of PUK’s Forensic Psychiatry and Psychotherapy (Director: Prof. E. Habermeyer). The ZKJF provides a wide range of services for juvenile justice. This includes outpatient treatment in the enforcement of measures, consiliar psychiatric assignments and youth forensic assessments. In addition, the ZKJF looks after underage prison inmates in the canton of Zurich. Clinical research is also carried out.
