Full description

We are the pulse of Zurich and keep the city in motion every 7.5 minutes. In order for this to work reliably and smoothly, a lot moves behind the scenes of the VBZ. Our colleagues work in more than 50 professions: in technology, IT, administration, traffic development, offer planning, infrastructure and many other exciting areas of responsibility. Where we drive, Zurich lives. Come on!

Tasks

  • In this function, you are responsible for the installation and insulation work on our rails, tram switches, intersections, rail extension devices and rail lubrication systems.
  • In addition, you carry out maintenance work on the insulation for railway equipment, e.g. track construction sites.
  • You will develop challenging, technical and logistical solutions for loading the semi-trailers for the special transport of the track systems to the respective track construction sites.
  • You operate our special machines: various crane systems, truck truck Cat. R1, drawbar, welding equipment, rail bending machine and rail band saw.
  • You communicate with the foreman, polisher or construction manager regarding the execution of the assembly and insulation work.
  • You are ready for sporadic special missions, e.g. on track construction sites in the city in wind and weather, sometimes weekend and night services take place.
  • Furthermore, you collect data for working hours, spare parts, orders and material reservations in SAP.
  • We count on your support in the winter service panel, which takes place rarely and alternately with teammates.

Profile

  • You bring a completed vocational training in the metal industry, and ideally you can also use MAG welding.
  • You are flexible and ready for use, resilient, and physically very fit - we sometimes have irregular working hours.
  • Mandatory driving license category B. If you can operate the Cat. R1 truck and hall cranes, that would be a plus.
  • You enjoy working in a team, being independent, reliable and acting independently.
  • You bring a sense of responsibility and concentration even in sometimes monotonous work.
  • Your German is at least at level B2.
