Original description

Are you passionate about the financial world? For our desk covering CEE, we are looking for a highly motivated and engaged Account Manager.

What would your role be?

  • Take ownership a nd manage a client portfolio, putting the client’s needs in first place.
  • Be responsible for the revenues of the book , managing the volume of work, and demand of the clients .
  • Be the go-to expert when it comes to the administration of the client’s accounts (cash managements, transfers, stock exchange orders and control of executions, fiduciary deposit renews, etc.).
  • Use keen foresight in anticipating new risks through the accurate monitoring of risk and compliance indicators.
  • Guide the relationship managers in KYC related matters, support them in the organization of client meetings, and in the preparation of investment proposals.
  • Ensure the smooth openings and closings of accounts.

What are we looking for?

  • Previous experience in a similar position within the banking industry.
  • Fluency in German and English and effective communication skills. Knowledge of Russian would be an advantage.
  • A strong team player and a strong interest in high -level client service.
  • Good understanding of financial products and markets.
  • Excellent IT skills (software tools, MS office).
  • Affinity for Artificial Intelligence (AI) and a willingness to embrace AI-enabled tools and ways of working.

What do we offer?

  • Top Zurich location with collaboration spaces (including rooftop terraces) and free lunch.
  • Friendly, diverse team in an open-office space with dancing walls.
  • All the latest technology to help you excel and advance.
  • Agile environment in how we think, the way we work and our trust for each other.
  • Flat hierarchy with collaboration on all levels.
  • Campus feeling and collaborative spirit.

We are looking for talented people, not just a CV that ticks all the boxes. Even if you do not have absolutely everything we have asked for, if this role sounds like a good fit for you, we encourage you to apply.

Why Vontobel?

At Vontobel, you are building better futures for our clients and society, as well as for yourself. As a family-owned and publicly listed company with over 2,000 professionals based in 28 locations globally and headquartered in Zurich, we’re committed to long-term thinking and sustainability.

Our diverse team values unique perspectives, and we hire individuals determined to deliver in a dynamic and changing world. Even if you don’t meet all the criteria, we encourage you to apply if you’re enthusiastic about the role.
