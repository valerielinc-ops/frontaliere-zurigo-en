Original description

Movemi AG operates the largest fitness network in Switzerland with 126 ACTIV FITNESS and 16 fitness parks. In our newly created function, you are the central specialist for occupational safety and health protection and make an essential contribution to the further development of movemi.

Mansioni

  • You advise company management, managers and specialist areas in all matters of occupational safety and health protection
  • Develop, maintain and optimize operational security concepts, security systems and relevant AS/GS documentation
  • You plan and accompany hazard investigations, risk assessments, audits and safety tours
  • You manage prevention and safety measures, coordinate their implementation and ensure controlling
  • You organize and conduct training, instructions and instruction in the area of AS/GS
  • You support the locations and the local contact persons AS/GS professionally and methodically
  • You will advise on the selection, provision and safe use of work equipment and personal protective equipment
  • You accompany incident investigations in the event of accidents, near incidents and safety-related incidents and derive improvement measures
  • If necessary, you will coordinate the involvement of other ASA specialists and work together with internal and external specialist departments
  • You represent the company in national specialist groups
  • You define annual goals and control implementation throughout the company
  • You prepare key figures, evaluations and reports and contribute to the further development of the security culture in the company

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: in a comparable position
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): continuing education as an ASA specialist within the meaning of the EKAS Directive 6508 or a comparable qualification in the field of occupational safety / health protection
  • Good knowledge of legal requirements and operational processes in the area of AS/GS
  • Experience in hazard identification, training, audit and incident detection
  • Advisory strength, persuasion and assertiveness
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • High social skills and enjoyment of working with different stakeholders
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • French (advantageous)

Cosa of friamo

  • Central location: Offices and studios in a well-developed, central location
  • Healthy work: Modernly furnished workstations with height-adjustable tables
  • Mobile subscription: National flat subscription from CHF 10.– / month (Swisscom /digitec connect)
  • Migros Bank: expense-free private and investment account with preferential interest
  • Employee offers: fleet discount on numerous car brands and special conditions on car sharing offers and car rentals
  • Leisure and cultural activities: Various employee discounts on current theatre and musical admissions as well as certain Swiss excursion destinations
  • Work climate: Helpful team members and uncomplicated handling across all hierarchical levels
  • Du-culture: We stand for a respectful coexistence at eye level
