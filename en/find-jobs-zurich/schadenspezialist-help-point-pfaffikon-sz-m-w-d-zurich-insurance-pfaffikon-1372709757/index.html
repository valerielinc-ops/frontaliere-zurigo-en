Full description

136871 | 80 - 100% | Pfäffikon, Schwyz

In this role, you are part of the Claims business unit in the Help Point area at Zurich Switzerland.

This is what awaits you

  • Ensuring the service of the Help Point operation in cooperation with the vehicle expert team
  • Direct contact for customers and independent processing of motor vehicle damage
  • Initiation of immediate measures (including expertise) and active solution finding
  • Receiving damage reports on the phone and securing the help point process on site
  • Maintaining personal customer contact as well as regular contact with internal and external partners
  • Flexibility to help at other Help Point locations

This sets you apart

  • Commercial or equivalent training and several years of commercial professional experience, ideally in the insurance industry
  • Very good oral and written expressive skills in German and good language skills in English, every other language is an advantage
  • Flexible, team-capable, resilient and assertive personality
  • Efficient and initiative working method that is characterized by a high level of personal responsibility
  • Communicative person with a strong customer and service orientation as well as a result-oriented and quality-conscious basic attitude

Good to know

Place of work: Pfäffikon, Schwyz

Job: from 01.02.2027 or by arrangement

Travel activity: until none

Hybrid work: up to 40% (by agreement)
