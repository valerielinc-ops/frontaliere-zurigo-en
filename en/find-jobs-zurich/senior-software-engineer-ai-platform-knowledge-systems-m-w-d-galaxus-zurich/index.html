Full description

As a Senior Software Engineer in our AI Enabling Team, you will tackle these problems with real operational data and real users: taking broad ownership of Galaxus Intelligence, our Agentic BI Tool, while building the shared retrieval, tool-access and evaluation platform for AI products across Digitec Galaxus.

Mansioni

  • Define and build the developer experience for AI-assisted engineering and AI-powered products, so teams can move from an idea to production without rebuilding model access, tool integration, evaluation and observability for every use case
  • Build the shared knowledge and tool-access layer that helps people and agents find authoritative internal information through a knowledge graph, semantic data layer, MCP access, and unified search.
  • Take broad technical ownership of Galaxus Intelligence and solve the hard parts of dependable self-service analytics: finding the right data, generating and validating queries, and making answers traceable to their sources
  • Own and evolve the LLM-access and observability layer used across Digitec Galaxus, balancing capability, reliability, latency, data protection and cost across hosted and self-hosted models
  • Create benchmarks, production evaluations and feedback loops that expose regressions and make the quality, usefulness and operational performance of AI systems measurable

Requisiti

  • 3 years: Software Engineering
  • Higher education in computer science, software engineering or a related field, or equivalent practical experience, combined with at least 3 years of experience building and operating production systems
  • Strong software engineering fundamentals, with depth in at least one language such as Python, C#/.NET or TypeScript
  • Hands on experience with LLM applications, agents, tool use, retrieval or evaluation is a strong advantage. Deep expertise in search, data platforms, developer tooling or distributed systems is an equally relevant foundation
  • English is mandatory, while the German is an advantage
  • Proven ability to take an ambiguous problem from its first rough definition to a system running in production, including the judgement to decide what not to build
  • Strong product judgement and clear communication with engineers, analysts and nontechnical stakeholders
  • An entrepreneurial mindset, a habit of questioning established approaches and the courage to make decisions before every detail is known
  • The capacity to adapt to ongoing changes in the work environment
  • Building trust through consistent and reliable actions
  • Completed degree (university / university of applied science / higher technical college)
  • English (Business fluent)
