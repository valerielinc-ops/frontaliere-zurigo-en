  • Job opening: immediately or by agreement
  • Specialty: Selling Food
  • Place of work: Coop, Gewerbestrasse 13, 8132 Egg

Tasks

  • The open counter is our flagship and important point of contact for our customers. You advise and serve them passionately on the buffet.
  • Freshly cut and dressed: You prepare the meat products professionally and attractively and also produce homemade products such as sausages.
  • You manage the goods in self-service and help them sell better.

Requirements

  • You bring joy / flair in customer advice or service, ideally in the food sector, and are interested in meat and fish.

  • Working time: 5 weeks holidays, flexible working time models
  • Further education: More than 600 internal training courses, talent management with diverse development opportunities, training and further education offers of the L-GAV and Coop
  • Sustainability: Commitment to people, animals and nature with over 400 actions
