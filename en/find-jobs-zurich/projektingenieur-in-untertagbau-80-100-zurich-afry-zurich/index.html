Full description

Become part of our team CIVIL. We manage, develop, design and plan infrastructure projects of all kinds in civil engineering and tunnelling. We accompany these projects from planning to implementation.

Take responsibility with us as

PROJECTING EUR: IN SUBTAGBAU 80-100% - AUSTRIA YOUR TASKS – UNDERSTANDING TECHNIK. Develop solutions.

  • Solving technical challenges: developing concepts for challenging underground and infrastructure projects – together with an experienced team that actively supports and is open to new ideas
  • Projecting versatilely: From construction and logistics concepts to static proofs to technical reports and cost estimates in various questions in all project phases
  • Responsible for own work packages with growing experience: take over own tasks and increasingly independently design technical solutions
  • Bringing together planning: coordinating the planning and modeling of your work packages and coordinating solutions with internal and external disciplines
  • Introducing ideas: questioning etch, comparing variants and developing technically convincing and economic solutions

YOUR CHANCEN – MEMBERS. DACULTURE. RECOMMEND.

  • Participate in challenging projects: Active participation in underground mining and infrastructure projects both in Switzerland and internationally and direct contribution of your knowledge in concrete planning tasks
  • Benefit from the knowledge of experienced experts: Support by specialists: inside and project managers: inside the project day, joint discussion of solution approaches and targeted support of your professional and methodical development
  • Growing with your tasks: From the first own work package to the independent processing of larger project parts, gradually taking on more responsibility
  • Understand technical contexts: In interdisciplinary teams, get to know different perspectives and deepen your understanding of complex underground projects
  • Set your own priorities: Whether underground mining, BIM or project planning – individual further development according to your own interests, supported by internal further training and versatile project tasks
  • Working flexibly: Flexible working hours and the opportunity to work mobile create scope for work and private life

YOUR PROFILE – TECHNICAL NEW. ENGAGATED. TEAMORIENTED.

  • Completed degree in Civil Engineering (ETH, EPFL or FH)
  • Whether directly from university or with first professional experience: You want to understand how challenging construction projects work and enjoy analyzing technical issues and developing suitable solutions
  • Networked, solution-oriented and practical thinking as well as structured and reliable working methods
  • Enjoying professional exchange, open communication and contributing your own ideas
  • Interest in digital planning methods such as BIM and in developing professionally and personally
  • Very good and stylish knowledge of German (verbal and written) – at native level or at least at level C1
