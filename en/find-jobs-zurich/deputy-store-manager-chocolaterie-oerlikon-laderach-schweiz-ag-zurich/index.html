Full description

The company is based in the canton of Glarus. Founded in 1962, the family-run company employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries, and is now increasingly active in the group. The company's headquarters are located in the canton of Glarus. In front of the picturesque alpine scenery, we produce our fresh chocolates and develop new, innovative ideas in the teams of the company. The "Retail" division comprises over 250 chocolateries in over 30 countries.

Here we offer our chocolate specialities and invite customers to experience fresh chocolate again and again.

Now we are writing a new chapter: With the reopening of our first outlet in Switzerland, we are creating another place where chocolate lovers can discover the world of Läderach. Do you want to participate in it? Then come to us.

That's what awaits you

  • You support the branch management in the daily management and organization of the branch
  • In the absence of the branch management, you assume operational responsibility for the branch and ensure the smooth running of the branch
  • You offer competent, customer-oriented advice and support in the selection of gifts from our diverse product range
  • You will ensure the attractive presentation of our extensive confiserie and chocolate range, including through creative shop design
  • You guarantee top quality and consistently implement our high standards
  • You are leading the way as a model in sales and actively supporting the team in day-to-day business
  • You are an important contact person for the team and for the customers
  • You contribute to the optimization of workflows and ensure that the store works efficiently and profitably

You bring this with you

  • You have completed a training in retail trade and/or have comparable experience in the handling of food products with
  • You have first leadership experience with, preferably from larger teams
  • Ideally you have first experience in chocolate handling
  • You love a hands-on mentality and are a technical a-sin
  • You are keen on optimization and have entrepreneurial thinking
  • You have an exceptional quality and hygiene awareness
  • You appreciate open and respectful communication and you know German and English safely; knowledge of French and other languages is an additional advantage
  • You are not afraid of flexible working hours and are also fully involved on weekends or holidays

We'll give you that

  • An exciting working environment with motivated colleagues
  • A position with a lot of personal responsibility
  • The possibility of bringing fresh ideas
  • An open and respectful feedback culture
  • Internal and external training
  • Individual career opportunities
  • Attractive sales premium for joint achievement of objectives
  • Generous discounts on our products
  • Chocolate at the workplace
  • Good accessibility of the sites
