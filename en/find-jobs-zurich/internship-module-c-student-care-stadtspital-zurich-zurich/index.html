Full description

We continuously offer internships for students in the Bachelor Care of the Zurich University of Applied Sciences.

Tasks

  • They provide professional care and care for our patients and thus support our teams in various specialist areas.
  • You will gain versatile practical experience based on the theoretically and scientifically learned basics.
  • They actively contribute to constructive and interdisciplinary cooperation.

Profile

  • Ongoing studies in Bachelor Care at the ZHAW
  • Enjoying varied and complex care situations
  • Strong understanding of cross-sectoral contexts and flexibility to operate interdisciplinary
  • Resilience, independence and motivation in work as well as the ability to engage in different care areas / teams
  • High level of service and service competence as well as a strong quality awareness

We offer

After a comprehensive training, you can expect a varied and demanding area of responsibility in a modern hospital operation. They benefit from attractive employment conditions and excellent social benefits from the city of Zurich. After completion of the internship, we offer the possibility of permanent employment in a specialist area. Welcome to our team, we look forward to your application!

About us

With over 34,000 inpatients and around 436,000 outpatients per year, the Stadtspital Zürich ranks among the top 10 Swiss hospitals. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,400 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
