Centre for Dentistry

Job opening 01.12.2026 or by agreement

The Center for Dentistry at the University of Zurich is one of the four dental university centers in Switzerland and comprises five clinics, a research institute and a central administration. The centre employs around 380 people. The Clinic for Orthodontics and Pediatric Dentistry has several main focuses: It serves the education and training of dentists in orthodontics (diagnosis and apparatus treatment of dental and jaw malformations in persons of all ages) or in pediatric dentistry (supplementation of pediatric care for special diagnostics in the oral area, for children with dental accidents, disabilities or serious general diseases). For patients with malformations or other complex problems, it offers specialized care in close cooperation with other clinics of the Center for Dentistry, the University Hospital and the University Children's Hospital. For the senior practice and the patients of the professor in the orthodontics department of our clinic, we are looking for a committed personality.

Tasks

  • Patient administration: patient registration, performance recording, appointment coordination
  • Care and archiving of patient records - digital
  • Ensuring practical hygiene

Profile

You have completed training as a dental assistant EFZ and several years of professional experience. Knowledge of orthodontic treatment procedures is an advantage. They are proactive, courteous and enjoy working in a large team. Our patients, who come from different cultures, look after you thanks to their friendly and patient nature with empathy and a high willingness to provide services. We offer you employment under the usual cantonal conditions in the heart of Zurich.
