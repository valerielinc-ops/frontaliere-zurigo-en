Full description

For our medical/oncological bed ward, we are looking for an established, committed and patient-oriented personality as a nurse / nursing specialist to strengthen our team. The KSW operates a medical bed station with a focus on oncology with 39 beds. The oncological patients receive chemotherapies and remain inpatient. Accompaniment and advice during therapies is very important. In addition to the care of medical patients, we also care for individual anorexia patients according to the concept.

Tasks

  • Holistic care and care of patients
  • Independent organisation of the work area
  • Assumption of tasks in the care of trainees and students
  • Participation in the introduction of new employees
  • Constructive cooperation in your own area and interdisciplinary

Profile

  • You have an education as a Dipl. Nursing professional HF/FH, beneficial with acute experience
  • You are a team player with a strong patient orientation
  • You enjoy working and participating in interprofessional teams

  • You are ready to work in all three shifts
