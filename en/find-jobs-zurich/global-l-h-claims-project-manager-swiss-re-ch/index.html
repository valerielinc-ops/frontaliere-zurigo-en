Original description

Job Title: Global L&H Claims Project Manager Are you looking to play a pivotal role in developing L&H claims-centric solutions that help Swiss Re clients deliver a best-in-class experience to their employees and customers? Are you attracted to diverse challenges that require big picture thinking and hands-on execution to turn vision into reality? The Opportunity The Global L&H Claims Solutions team is seeking a highly motivated project manager to support industry transformation through delivery of L&H claims technology solutions. You will work within a team of claims and technology experts to build, maintain, and implement claims technology solutions globally. As Project Manager, you will have responsibility for the day-to-day running of client implementations and related intra and inter-product build dependencies across the Claims Solutions (PromiseXP) suite and associated project portfolio.

Responsibilities Include

  • Have ownership of and provide oversight for the project portfolio
  • Select the most appropriate project management methodology, tools and techniques per project (waterfall, agile, mixed)
  • Use and maintain project collaboration and communication platform(s)
  • Manage onboarding clients and Swiss Re team members in the selected collaboration and communication platform(s)
  • Evaluate confidentiality need and ensure confidentiality protection is in place
  • Define and document project scope, objectives, milestone level project plans, phasing and desired outcomes
  • Lead and facilitate client and internal meetings, drive stakeholder alignment and effective decision-making while navigating competing priorities, challenging assumptions and ensuring clear ownership and follow-through.
  • Foster decision making and document process and outcomes, ensuring project governance is in place
  • Obtain, record, and document any sign offs in your projects
  • Evaluate and document potential project risks and issues, and mitigation actions in collaboration with project team
  • Track project progress and milestones using appropriate tools
  • Prepare project progress reports for relevant leadership and stakeholders
  • Perform regular assessment of project execution together with project team to identify areas for improvement Arrange and maintain project communication channels (intranet etc)
  • Create and maintain guidance checklists
  • Serve as customer's main point of contact during project delivery and effectively manage client stakeholders to deliver efficiently and on time.

About You

Need to have

  • Demonstrated ability to manage complexity and prioritize effectively
  • Ability to lead projects with cross functional teams
  • Excellent communication, engagement and collaboration skills
  • Strong experience in establishing, managing and delivering technology projects
  • Formal project management qualifications (PMP, PRINCE2, PMI-ACP, CSM/PSM)
  • Relevant experience contributing to and leading teams using Agile, Scrum, or Design Thinking frameworks
  • Experience in waterfall and agile methodologies as well as other project management methodologies and tools
  • Bachelor's degree with a background in business or other relevant discipline
  • Ability to effectively influence and manage key stakeholders based on their unique motivations, cultural norms and communication style

Nice to have

  • Relevant experience leading client deliveries of SaaS products
  • Understand Technology and systems at a high level, particularly to assess the value and level of effort in creating or supporting a technology solution a plus.
  • Understanding of the IT and Operational Frameworks in which insurance or other large, legacy organizations operate, including the constraints faced when making changes to processes or systems
  • Master's degree with a background in business or other relevant discipline
  • Experience in CX/UX design, including journey or process mapping in Figma, Visio, or similar tools
