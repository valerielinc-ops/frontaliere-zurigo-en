Full description

In order to strengthen our growing Data Center team, we are looking for an experienced personality as a construction manager in shell construction, expansion and architecture (CSA). Your contractual place of work is our headquarters “Connect” in Opfikon. However, you will mainly work on one of our data center construction projects in the Zurich region. What tasks await you as construction manager (m/f/d) Structure, expansion and architecture - Data Center?

  • You lead and coordinate all CSA work (Civil, Structural & Architectural) on a large data center project and ensure successful implementation in terms of work safety, quality, costs and deadlines.
  • You lead and coordinate subcontractors, polishers and construction site teams and are responsible for short- and medium-term construction planning using 2-, 4- and 8-week foresight as well as daily construction site coordination
  • You control the central interfaces between the CSA and MEP trades and ensure that workspaces, rooms and systems are handed over to the installation and commissioning teams on schedule.
  • You will monitor construction progress, productivity and resource use, identify risks and bottlenecks at an early stage and initiate appropriate measures to secure the project milestones
  • You ensure compliance with all project specifications, Swiss standards and regulations, SIA standards, fire protection requirements, HSE guidelines and quality management processes Requirements What requirements do you bring as construction manager (m/f/d) shell construction, expansion and architecture - Data Center?
  • Several years of experience in construction with comprehensive responsibility in construction site management as well as proven success in the realization of CSA trades from execution to commissioning to handover
  • In-depth experience on hyperscale data center, mission critical, industrial or comparable complex construction projects as well as a strong understanding of the interfaces between CSA and MEP trades
  • Proven competence in controlling multiple subcontractors simultaneously as well as in short- and medium-term construction schedule planning, scheduling, productivity monitoring and management of dependencies and bottlenecks
  • In-depth technical understanding of execution plans, BIM models, technical specifications and schedules as well as experience in quality controls, defect management and structured project transfers
  • Completed studies or comparable training in civil engineering, construction management, architecture or a related discipline. Knowledge of Swiss building standards (SIA/VKF), experience on large projects and knowledge of Primavera P6 are advantageous
  • Very good German and English skills in word and writing Benefits
