Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We create transparency in the banking and insurance market and advise our customers comprehensively and neutrally. We offer a versatile practical training for our pension foundations at our headquarters at Paradeplatz in Zurich (start August 2027).

  • You will handle the various business cases of our collection foundations together with your team. This includes entry and exit of insured persons, retirements, various mutation cases and the introduction of new customers.
  • You are the contact point for internal and external customers and answer their inquiries promptly and professionally.
  • During your internship, you will be accompanied by experienced instructors.
  • You are in the third year of training in a commercial middle school HMS or business middle school WMS.
  • You enjoy looking after customers and you are interested in preventive care topics.
  • You enjoy working in a team, being communicative and customer-oriented.
  • You have a flair for numbers, work carefully and recognize connections even in more complex situations.
  • You feel comfortable in a dynamic and service-oriented environment, take a structured approach and work reliably and accurately even in hectic times.
  • You have excellent German skills and ideally also French or English skills.
  • Regular development discussions and individual support
  • Many events to expand your network
  • Free cold drinks, coffee, tea and fresh fruits
  • Even glacé in summer
  • After completion of the internship, there is the possibility of permanent employment (also part-time).
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
