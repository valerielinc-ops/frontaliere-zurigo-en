Full description

We are looking for a dynamic and motivated personality immediately or by agreement.

Tasks

In a small team, you manage a shopping center in shift operation from Monday to Saturday (06:30 to 20:30). Your responsibilities include the following activities:

  • Taking over all infrastructural tasks (e.g. environmental work, winter service, outdoor cleaning)
  • General housekeeping work (e.g. change of lamps)
  • Emergency exploration and intervention, BMA operation
  • Carrying out minor repairs
  • Gardening/environment
  • Disposal, provision of containers
  • Shopping cart service
  • Collaboration in the Pikettdienst

Requirements

You describe yourself as a service provider: in the innermost conviction. Positive feedback on your performance motivates you and spurs you on to further top performances. You also bring:

  • Completed basic training (operational maintenance, logistics or similar)
  • Customer-oriented, efficient and independent working method
  • Engaged personality with own initiative
  • Driving licence Cat. B
  • Several years of professional experience desired
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Good computer user knowledge
  • Craftsmanship
  • Technical know-how
  • They are well-groomed and well-groomed.

We specialize in holistic facilities management services for every phase of the business real estate and area lifecycle. Buildings and infrastructures are complex systems. Whether technical operation, strategic planning or infrastructural services – we ensure maximum availability, increase operational safety and improve comfort in buildings. In this way, we minimize costs and create optimal conditions so that our customers can concentrate on their core business.

As a company of Equans Switzerland, the leading full-service provider for holistic building and energy solutions in Switzerland, we are also driving the transformation and digitalization of our customers and working together on the energy transition.
