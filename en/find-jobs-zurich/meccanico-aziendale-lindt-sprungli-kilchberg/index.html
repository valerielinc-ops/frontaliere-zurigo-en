About us

We enchant the world with chocolate! Lindt & Sprüngli (Switzerland) AG is a subsidiary of the global Lindt & Sprüngli Group with headquarters and production facilities in Kilchberg on Lake Zurich. In Altendorf we operate our own logistics centre, while the Lindt Cocoa Center in Olten produces cocoa mass for our own use as well as for other European production facilities. Throughout Switzerland, we proudly operate 15 Lindt shops, including iconic locations such as the Jungfraujoch and the Swiss Transport House in Lucerne. More than 1000 employees from more than 50 nations contribute to our success.

What you do with us

  • Performing troubleshooting as well as revisions to our production facilities
  • General maintenance and disruption service throughout the operation
  • Production of new and spare parts by means of our machine tools
  • Compliance with legal regulations, regulations and standards as well as safety, quality and hygiene regulations
  • Report and maintain the work performed in the maintenance software
  • Active participation in TPM teams
  • Pictet service

When you fit in with us

  • Technical vocational training completed
  • In-depth expertise in maintenance and maintenance
  • Experience in handling conventional cutting machines such as drilling, turning and milling machines
  • Very good knowledge of pneumatics/hydraulics
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Experience using maintenance software and MS-Office range
