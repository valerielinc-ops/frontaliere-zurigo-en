Full description

Introduction

Lead the delivery of complex software development and digital product initiatives in a highly collaborative Agile environment. You will align business goals with engineering execution, ensure predictable and value-driven delivery, and drive continuous improvement across teams, processes, and release practices.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead end-to-end delivery of complex software and digital product projects, balancing scope, timeline, quality, and business value.
  • Coordinate dependencies across development teams, Product Owners, architects, quality, cybersecurity, IT, and business stakeholders.
  • Drive roadmap execution, release planning, and delivery forecasting aligned with strategic priorities.
  • Establish transparency through Agile planning, dashboards, delivery metrics, and reporting.
  • Identify and mitigate risks, dependencies, and delivery obstacles to ensure successful outcomes.
  • Facilitate stakeholder alignment, communication, and decision-making across all organizational levels.
  • Support Agile ways of working and foster continuous improvement through retrospectives and delivery reviews.
  • Coordinate product releases, go-live activities, and post-release follow-up to ensure smooth deployments.

Required Expertise

  • Degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related technical discipline.
  • Several years of experience leading software development, digital product, or technology projects in Agile environments.
  • Strong knowledge of Agile methodologies such as Scrum, Kanban, Lean, or SAFe.
  • Proven experience coordinating cross-functional software teams and managing stakeholder expectations.
  • Solid understanding of software development lifecycle, release management, backlog management, and Agile delivery practices.
  • Hands-on experience with tools such as Jira, Confluence, Azure DevOps, Planview, or similar platforms.
  • Excellent communication, facilitation, and analytical skills with the ability to influence decision-making.
  • Strong English skills; advanced German is highly desirable. Agile certifications are a plus.

People at Belimo

Smart buildings start with dedicated people. We bring talented people together and foster an environment to succeed. At Belimo, respect, trust, and support across departments help ideas move quickly into real-world solutions, because we achieve more together than we ever could alone.

About Belimo

Belimo is the global market leader in the development, production, and sales of field devices for the energy-efficient control of heating, ventilation and air-conditioning systems. The focus of our core business is on damper actuators, control valves, sensors and meters. Founded in 1975, the company has been listed on the Swiss Exchange (SIX) since 1995.
