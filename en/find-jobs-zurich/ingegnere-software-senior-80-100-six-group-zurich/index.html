Senior Software Engineer (80–100%)

Zurich | 40% working from home is possible | Reference 8282

Within the Banking Services division, we develop and operate the eBill and direct debit services used throughout Switzerland. To further develop the eBill platform, we are strengthening our agile development team and are looking for someone with experience in application architecture and a passion for full-stack development with Angular and Java technologies. Together, you will shape a robust, reliable and high-performance platform for Swiss invoicing and payments.

Your responsibilities

  • You will actively help shape the evolutionary transformation of the eBill platform and develop innovative business functionalities for one of Switzerland's leading digital invoicing networks.
  • You will drive the integration of AI capabilities into our product and use modern AI-assisted development tools to sustainably increase quality, efficiency and delivery speed.
  • You will take responsibility for architectural decisions and the further development of a scalable, secure and highly available platform.
  • You will develop and operate business-critical applications using modern Java and web technologies and promote technical excellence through DevOps principles and best practices.
  • You will work closely with Product Management, Architecture and Engineering, actively contribute your experience to the team and support the stable operation of the platform, including on-call duty.

What you bring

  • Solid experience in the development and architecture of modern Java-based web applications, as well as strong conceptual thinking skills.
  • Several years of practical experience with technologies such as Java, Spring Boot, REST APIs and SQL, as well as modern development, CI/CD and version control tools.
  • Experience with agile working methods, DevOps principles and the operation of business-critical applications.
  • Interest in AI-assisted software development and enthusiasm for putting new technologies and innovative approaches to solutions into practice.
  • Very good German and English language skills and the ability to discuss technical topics clearly with different stakeholders.
