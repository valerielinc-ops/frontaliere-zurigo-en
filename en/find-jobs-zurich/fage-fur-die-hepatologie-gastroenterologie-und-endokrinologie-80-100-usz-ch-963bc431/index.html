Full description

We are a department with a focus on hepatology, gastroenterology and endocrinology. We care for patients with inflammatory bowel diseases, metabolic diseases and patients with liver cirrhosis, who are waiting for a transplant. The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and about 700 trainees are committed to the welfare of our patients:inside.
