Full description

The newly created staff department is responsible for coordinating and managing the central staff tasks of the financial administration. So far, the project manager of the financial administration is solely responsible for internal projects. For his support and to strengthen the staff department, we are looking for experienced and committed project assistance immediately or by agreement.

Tasks

  • Support of project management in the planning, control and administration of mainly IT projects (SAP) as well as in the monitoring of dates, milestones and pendants.
  • Coordination and organisation and follow-up of meetings, workshops and information events.
  • Cooperation in project controlling and status reporting.
  • Conducting analyses and research as well as creating presentations, decision-making bases and project documentation.
  • Administrative and organizational support of the department management staff Cooperation in other staff tasks and projects.

Profile

  • Completed basic commercial or equivalent training and some years of professional experience in a comparable capacity
  • Good computer user knowledge, project method HERMES, Microsoft 365
  • Experience in project management primarily of IT projects (SAP) is advantageous
  • Strong organisational and coordination skills as well as an independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Enjoying the day-to-day project and administrative tasks as well as the ability to keep an eye on several projects at the same time
  • Communication strength, service orientation and stylish German in word and writing

We offer

  • the opportunity to work at home and at work in the center of Zurich (near Stauffacher)
  • Extensive training opportunities
  • An appreciative working atmosphere
  • very good employment conditions

About us

The financial administration is a service department of the financial department. The main tasks of the financial administration include the preparation of the overall urban budget and financial planning, the management of the overall urban accounting, the annual accounts, as well as the securing of liquidity, the procurement of funds on the money and capital market, the handling of the overall urban payments, procurement as well as risk and insurance management.
