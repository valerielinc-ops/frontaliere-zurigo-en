Full description

80% - 100% | Banking Operations, Sales and Customer Consulting | Zurich | Experienced Professionals Together with your colleagues, you are passionate about supporting more than 70,000 corporate customers along their most important corporate events. You will accompany SMEs from start-up through the growth phase to company succession and also advise on private concerns of entrepreneurs. You act as a reliable contact person and combine professional competence with proximity to customers. Your workplace is centrally located in the lively district 5 near the station Hardbrücke, alternating with regular use in our headquarters Zurich City. If you enjoy working with corporate customers and want to actively shape the future of our area, then you are a good fit for us. Your tasks

  • Advising founders for a quick and smooth start to self-employment
  • Provision of telephone and personal consultations both for corporate clients (corporate and real estate financing, money transactions, investments, pension provision) and for private concerns of entrepreneurs (real estate financing, investments, pension provision)
  • Examination, application and approval of financing (including mortgages, leasing and loans) and monitoring of credit commitments
  • Active need and potential discovery as well as structured potential recognition and exploitation with the help of our digital consulting platform

Your profile

  • Completed banking or equivalent education with appropriate banking experience
  • Higher education or training (specialist certificate, high school of applied sciences, bachelor’s or comparable degree in business administration or banking and finance)
  • Experience in financing and investment business
  • Enjoying the ideas and challenges of companies and their owners
  • Strong communication skills and experience in customer advice by telephone and in personal conversation
  • Very good knowledge of German and English, further language skills are a plus

Our offer

  • Motivated, self-organized and established team that looks forward to you
  • Our way of working is self-determined – this entails responsibility, offers design possibilities and freedom
  • We invest a lot in our employees and offer individual training opportunities
  • Various events and internal networks offer you the opportunity to network quickly
  • Attractive overall package, which in addition to the attractive basic salary and a variable remuneration offers many benefits and perks – from A such as retirement savings to Z such as zoo tickets
