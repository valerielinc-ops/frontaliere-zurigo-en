Full description

Location: Hirslanden Clinic in the Park | Zurich

Occupation by: Immediately or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 73419

In this responsible role, you ensure the professional preparation of medical devices and flexible endoscopes and thus make an important contribution to patient safety.

YOUR TASKS

  • Competent and professional preparation of medical devices according to recognised quality standards
  • Material and professional preparation of flexible endoscopes including cleaning, disinfection, functional control, care and documentation
  • Ensuring proper sterilisation procedures and carrying out the necessary checks and clearances
  • Compilation and maintenance of instrument screens, sets and individual materials
  • Cleaning, operation and monitoring of AEMP cleaning and disinfection equipment, sterilizers and other technical equipment
  • Co-responsibility for compliance with all relevant standards, laws and guidelines as well as administrative work within the framework of the required documentation
  • Responsibility for the management and storage of sterile goods and for ensuring, promoting and verifying the quality of work

Your profile

  • Completed training as medical device technologist / medical device technologist EFZ (MPT) or as technical sterilization assistant / technical sterilization assistant with specialist knowledge I or II; Several years of professional experience in an AEMP
  • A completed further development in the processing of flexible endoscopes is advantageous.
  • Independent, self-responsible and efficient working methods, a high level of quality awareness and technical understanding
  • Good MS-Office user knowledge
  • Experience with an electronic sterile goods management system (EuroSDS) is an advantage
  • Willingness to work shifts and weekends
  • Good knowledge of German at level B2

You can expect a motivated, committed and multi-professional team in an advanced working environment.
