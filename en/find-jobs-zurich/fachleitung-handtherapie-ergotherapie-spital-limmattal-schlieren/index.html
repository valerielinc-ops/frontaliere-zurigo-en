Full description

  • Specialist management of hand therapy / occupational therapy 80% - 100%, by appointment Apply now Our wide range of services and our therapy team are interdisciplinary and ensure our patients optimal rehabilitation and a smooth return to their familiar surroundings. The overarching goal in the extensive setting of physiotherapy, speech therapy or occupational therapy is to increase the quality of life of our patients and to enable or maintain their ability to act in everyday life, at work and in their leisure time - be it after an operation or because of an acute illness. Here you will be involved:
  • Treatment of patients with hand injuries (conservative and postoperative) as well as splint and aid provision
  • Representation in the treatment of patients in the fields of neurology and geriatrics
  • Implementation of client-centered, everyday-oriented and functional treatments in individual therapies
  • Independent planning, documentation and performance recording in our IT systems
  • Opportunity to be able to help shape the professional development of hand therapy in the Limmattal Hospital in a dynamic team
  • Have completed training as Occupational therapist FH or equivalent training with SRK recognition
  • have experience in the field of hand therapy
  • are interested in the specialist area and in interprofessional collaboration with hand surgery
  • are a team player, flexible and have an independent personality
