Main responsibilities

  • You will take over the end-to-end project management for the evaluation, procurement and implementation of the new Transport Management System (TMS) for our rail transports.
  • You analyze and optimize existing processes together with the specialist departments and develop the future process and system target image.
  • You develop business, process and system requirements and conduct tenders, provider evaluation and proof of concept.
  • You design the order-to-cash processes from commissioning and planning to operational processing to billing, controlling and reporting.
  • Interfaces and system integration are coordinated together with internal teams, partners and external implementation partners – from implementation to tests, training and migration to rollout.

  • Experience in rail or intermodal transport is a plus.
