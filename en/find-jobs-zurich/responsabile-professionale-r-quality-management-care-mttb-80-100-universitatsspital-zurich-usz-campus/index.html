Full description

Is quality more than a standard for you? Then you shape the future of patient care with us. In this versatile role, you will drive innovative projects in the areas of quality management, patient safety and process development. Together in the interprofessional team, you will develop sustainable and evidence-based solutions that improve the quality of care, strengthen clinical processes and create direct added value for our patients. >The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.

Tasks

  • Proven project experience and experience in dealing with different stakeholders, from clinical professionals to patients:
  • Ability to connect, convince and successfully advance change even without direct leadership responsibility
