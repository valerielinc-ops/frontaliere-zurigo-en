Full description

The University Center for Prevention and Sports Medicine is the only university-based Swiss Olympic Medical Center in German-speaking Switzerland. We offer individual examinations, advice and support for people of all ages and regardless of their health or training status. In addition to competitive athletes, we look after amateur and recreational athletes as well as patients with exercise-related complaints. We are a leader in outpatient sports medicine care in Switzerland. Every day we look after more than 300 people with a wide range of sports and exercise medicine issues - from prevention and performance diagnostics to treatment and rehabilitation. Our offer is interprofessional and, in addition to the musculoskeletal focus, includes the areas of “Internal Medicine & Sports” including sports cardiology, “Women & Sports” and “Children & Sports”. The close cooperation between these areas enables holistic sports medicine care at a high clinical and scientific level.

The position offers a versatile and dynamically developing field of activity at the interface of internal medicine, sports cardiology, musculoskeletal medicine as well as child and adolescent medicine - integrated into sports and exercise medicine with a clear focus on competitive sports, prevention and rehabilitation. You will independently assume clinical responsibility and at the same time have a high degree of freedom in the further development of our sports medicine offerings.
You will work in an interdisciplinary medical team from various specialist disciplines and in close collaboration with other professional groups. Mutual support, collegial collaboration and interprofessional treatment approaches characterize our everyday clinical life.

We are looking for someone as of January 1, 2027 or by appointment Tasks: • Independent care of outpatients as well as amateur and top athletes with sports and exercise medicine issues • Implementation and assessment of sports and preventative medicine diagnostics as well as conservative therapy - depending on the professional profile, with a focus on internal medicine, sports cardiology and/or musculoskeletal • Independent implementation of diagnostic procedures, including sonographic diagnostics, according to qualifications and focus of activity • Supporting sports associations and clubs as well as - depending on the area of operation - athletes in everyday training and competitions • Active interdisciplinary and interprofessional collaboration within the center and with adjacent departments • If you are interested, academic work in research and teaching, including participation in our sports medicine research group, publication work and active participation in further training and conferences Requirements: • Specialist title in general internal medicine or physical medicine and rehabilitation (PMR), including MEBEKO recognition for diplomas obtained abroad • Interdisciplinary focus on sports and exercise medicine (SEMS) or equivalent foreign qualification - ideally already available or shortly before completion • Enjoy independent clinical work as well as interdisciplinary and interprofessional collaboration • Interest in independent scientific work and in implementing scientific evidence into clinical practice • Several years of practical professional experience, ideally at least three years, at a recognized Swiss medical institution • Experience in supporting sports associations, sports clubs or athletes • Certificate of competence in sonography of the musculoskeletal system (SGUM or equivalent qualification), particularly with a focus on the musculoskeletal system • Experience in academic work, teaching or publishing We offer: • Direct athlete care - participation in the care of amateur and high-performance athletes in everyday clinical practice, training and competitions. • Clinical independence - wide range of sports medicine activities with the opportunity to independently carry out numerous diagnostic measures, including ultrasound. • Creative scope - active participation in the further development of existing and the creation of new sports medicine offerings. • University environment - work at the only university-based Swiss Olympic Medical Center in German-speaking Switzerland with excellent national and international networks. • Academic development – ​​opportunity for research and teaching as well as further academic qualification up to habilitation, with participation in an established sports medicine research group. • Knowledge transfer & network - support with further training, conferences and professional development. • Modern working environment - interprofessional structures, modern diagnostic options and a high level of digitalization. • At least 26 vacation days per year • Free day off for your birthday • Travel discount of 50% on the ZVV bonus pass or an annual contribution to a general subscription • Enjoy a healthy and balanced lunch at reduced prices in our company staff restaurant • 20% discount at Balgrist Apotheke AG as well • Various shopping discounts Further advantages just a click away: Attractive offer for our employees
