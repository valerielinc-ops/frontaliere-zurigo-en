Full description

Career goal Branch manager*? Then we have the perfect springboard for you. Here you can prove what you got. Share your knowledge. Something moving. Gaining leadership experience. And of course, a lot of learning. Denner. This is our store.

Mansioni

  • You pay special attention to the freshness of fruits, vegetables and bread and say food waste with far-sighted orders the fight
  • Order fresh produce and of course all other goods, accept, check and fill them on the shelves in your store
  • Implement specifications correctly in the store layout
  • Check minimum durability data in product group Freshness
  • As a deputy, guide, promote and advance employees – and be an example to them
  • As a deputy, take on joint responsibility for the figures – in terms of turnover, personnel costs and inventory

Requirements

  • Three or more years of professional experience: in the retail trade as specialist, divisional or rayon management of advantage
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EBA): in the retail trade (food) or related profession would be ideal
  • Joy of fresh products and ideally already professional experience with them
  • Physically fit, some colis are up to 10 pounds heavy
  • Ability to plan and organize tasks and a flair for interpreting key figures
  • Familiar with PC, tablet and smartphone - and with other digital helpers (e.g. for goods management) you quickly make friends
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Part of the Migros Group: Strong brand anchored in the retail trade under the Migros Group umbrella
  • Healthy work: High standards in occupational safety, ergonomics and hygiene.
  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 5 weeks longer baby break (19 weeks) for mothers at 100% wage. Fathers enjoy the offspring 4 paid weeks.
  • Fair wages: 13 monthly wages and fixed wage increases for all instead of bonuses for a few.
  • Mobile subscription: Spoiled for choice: With Galaxus Mobile, Wingo or Sunrise already from CHF 10.- one or the other really connected.
  • Migros Bank: Better off with interest and fees on your private, investment or mortgage accounts.
  • Employee offers: 10 percent on the next major purchase (twice a year).
  • Holidays: 6 weeks holidays for all and even more days for service anniversaries.
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts for car rentals, hotels, events and many more areas.
  • Targeted promotion: From dishwasher to millionaire? Maybe not exactly, but: Who has bite and talent, comes quickly.
