Full description

Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Cultural Internship: in 60% Are you interested in the contents and processes of cantonal cultural promotion and would you like to help shape it yourself? Then you are exactly right with us: From 1 February 2027, or by appointment, the Department of Culture will offer you a one-year internship (60%). With us you will get exciting insights into the funding activities and the versatile tasks in the cultural environment of the Canton of Zurich: from the support of artists and projects to the support of cultural institutions, the development of new funding strategies, the awarding of prizes and the regional promotion of culture – and much more. What to expect

  • Participation in the application assessment from all areas (visual arts, literature, music, dance/theater, extra credit)
  • Administrative activities and support in the organisation of meetings and events (e.g. exhibition of visual arts)
  • Participation in Commission meetings and strategic discussions on the further development of cultural promotion
  • Support in the planning, implementation and evaluation of projects, depending on your strengths and interests
  • Heart for culture and ideally first experience in the field of culture
  • Joy of teamwork, combined with an independent, precise and structured way of working
  • You are studying or have recently completed a degree or have an equivalent education What we offer you
  • Varied and meaningful tasks in a lively cultural environment
  • Motivated team
  • Fair internship wage (between CHF 50,000- CHF 56,000 at 100%)
  • Central workplace in the middle of Zurich – directly at the HB
  • A year full of insights into the cantonal cultural promotion and cultural scene of Zurich – varied, practical and inspiring
  • Together we contribute to a lively cultural scene in Zurich. We look forward to your online application until October 13, 2026. Your questions about the internship answered Tom Hellat (043 259 25 14, tom.hellat@zh.ch), information about the cultural department can be found here. If you have any questions about the recruitment process, Nadja van der Reijden (nadja.vanderreijden@ji.zh.ch) is at your disposal. For this position, we do not consider applications about personnel service companies.

Pensum: 60%
