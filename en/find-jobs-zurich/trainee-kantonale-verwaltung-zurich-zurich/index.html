Role overview

Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Cultural Internship: in 60% Are you interested in the contents and processes of cantonal cultural promotion and would you like to help shape it yourself?

Then you are exactly right with us: From 1 February 2027, or by appointment, the Department of Culture will offer you a one-year internship (60%).

With us you will get exciting insights into the funding activities and the versatile tasks in the cultural environment of the Canton of Zurich: from the support of artists and projects to the support of cultural institutions, the development of new funding strategies, the awarding of prizes and the regional promotion of culture – and much more.   • Support in the planning, implementation and evaluation of projects, depending on your strengths and interests
  • Heart for culture and ideally first experience in the field of culture
