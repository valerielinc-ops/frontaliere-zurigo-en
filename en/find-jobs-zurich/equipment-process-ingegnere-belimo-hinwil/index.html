Full description

You want to make complex production plants work, but to make them better? With us, you combine technical expertise with modern digital technologies and AI and actively shape the future of our manufacturing engineering.

Main responsibilities

  • You take over the technical responsibility for commissioning, verification and qualification of complex production facilities and accompany them until the successful transfer to production.
  • You control demanding FAT, SAT, IQ, OQ and PQ activities and work closely with engineering, production, quality, suppliers and our international locations.
  • You accompany software deployments, system upgrades and transfers and ensure that systems and software work reliably in the respective production environment.
  • In complex technical challenges, you are the central contact person: You conduct root-key analysis and develop sustainable solutions together with the participating teams.
  • You support our production sites in Ramp-ups, technical escalations, modernizations and life-cycle topics.
  • You use your experience from projects and commissioning to continuously develop and work on deployment methods, standards, checklists and processes across locations.
  • You use digital technologies and AI tools to continuously improve troubleshooting, engineering, documentation and knowledge management.
  • As a technical expert:in you promote the exchange of knowledge within the field of manufacturing engineering and bring your expertise in reviews, lessons learned and the further development of our methods.

Skills required

  • You have a technical degree (FH/Uni), e.g. in automation, electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, mechatronics or production technology. This combines with comprehensive relevant practical experience.
  • You have extensive experience with production facilities, especially in the areas of commissioning, verification, qualification, ramp-up and technical lifecycle support.
  • You are a real Hands-on engineer: you like to work directly on systems and systems, get to the bottom of technical problems and also implement solutions yourself in practice.
  • You have high A-resinity on AI topics – ideally also deepened experiences with them.
  • You know very well about automation, system software, interfaces and qualification processes such as FAT/SAT and IQ/OQ/PQ.
  • You deal with complex technical problems in an analytical and structured way and convince yourself of your own initiative, implementation strength and responsibility.
  • You like to work interdisciplinaryally, pass on your knowledge and can convince and support others with your professional competence, even without disciplinary leadership responsibility.
  • You communicate very well in English and are ready to travel nationally and internationally in the event of important project phases or technical challenges.

About Belimo

Belimo is the world leader in the development, manufacture and distribution of field equipment for energy-efficient control of heating, ventilation and air conditioning systems. Valve drives, control valves, sensors and counters form our core business. Founded in 1975, Belimo has been listed on the Swiss Stock Exchange (SIX) since 1995.
