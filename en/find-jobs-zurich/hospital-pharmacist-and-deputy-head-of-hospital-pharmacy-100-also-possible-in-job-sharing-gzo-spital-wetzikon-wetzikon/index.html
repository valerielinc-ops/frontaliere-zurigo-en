Full description

Main Tasks - You will act as the deputy head in the professional and organizational leadership and further development of the hospital pharmacy, build our counter service, contribute clinically (Medication Review), and perhaps open up new logistical perspectives for us. - In addition to daily operations, you will also engage in the ongoing optimization of our medication range, ensuring supply security, as well as maintaining and expanding our QMS. -

  • Your tasks also include patient safety and participating in internal training sessions. You will create and implement application recommendations, provide simple yet competent advice to patients, nursing staff, and physicians, while maintaining interdisciplinary contacts.
  • Requirements
  • You hold a federal or recognized diploma as a pharmacist, ideally with experience in hospital or clinical pharmacy, and you must have a valid professional practice permit.
  • You quickly familiarize yourself with existing processes and take on responsibility. Connected and analytical thinking, as well as an independent, solution-oriented, and pragmatic working style, characterize you.
  • You are a motivated, communicative, reliable, and committed individual who enjoys working in a team and values interdisciplinary collaboration.
  • Your solid IT skills, as well as high social competence and flexibility, complete your profile. Professional Benefits -
