Full description

Personal – competent – close Welcome to the Hospital Uster, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As the central pillar of health care in the Oberland region of Zurich and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you experience the highest level of expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and promoted in your professional development. Spital Uster is a teaching hospital of the Medical Faculty of the University of Zurich. Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of a “regional and digital health hub in Züri East”! Are you looking for a challenging education where you are challenged and can learn a lot? With us at the Hospital Uster, you will find a small, family intensive care unit, where you will quickly become part of the team and grow step by step into the world of intensive care. We offer you a manageable environment with direct exchange, short paths and real learning opportunities – close to the practice, close to the people. Your training will be complemented by a one-year internship in Triemli City Hospital. This gives you additional valuable experience and insight into different ways of working – a strong combination for your professional path. During your entire training period, we accompany you personally and with a lot of commitment – individually, competently and at eye level. Sounds good? Then we look forward to meeting you!
