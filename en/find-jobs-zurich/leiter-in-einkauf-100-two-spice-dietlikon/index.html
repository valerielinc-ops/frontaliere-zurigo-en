Head: in Purchasing 100%

Two Spice AG — Dietlikon, Dietlikon, Svizzera

Two Spice – that’s us! A creative and innovative gastronomy and lifestyle company from Zurich. Since 1990, we have been developing unique concepts and brands that inspire. Our companies at central hotspots stand for quality, design and special moments of enjoyment.In our case, purchasing is anything but one-dimensional. You procure for 30 restaurants and take-out stores, for our own manufacturer, which produces Sushi for the Coop Group, as well as for international trade products from China, Japan and South Korea. Nine brands, 30 locations, our own production and an international retail business: Procurement with us is versatile, dynamic and anything but 08/15.Our purchasing department was re-established a year ago. The foundations are laid, the potential is great. Now we are looking for someone who takes advantage of the opportunities and actively develops purchasing.

Tasks

  • You take on a team of 4 employees, give roles and responsibilities clear contours and develop each individually. Whoever wants to go along will grow with you.
  • You bring structure to processes, optimize processes and drive automation forward.
  • From food and drinks to exclusive commercial products. You decide what to procure locally and what to import, with sourcing markets in Switzerland, Europe, and Asia (focusing on China, Japan, South Korea). Best conditions, best quality, no matter where.
  • You maintain existing relationships, build new ones, and consistently negotiate the best terms. Best quality at the best price is not a coincidence, but your job.
  • You know the rules of the game and make sure that the goods arrive on time and on the right conditions.
  • You are responsible for all purchase and sale items in the system, including checkout configuration and price maintenance. Clean data is the basis for everything we want to automate, and we want to automate a lot.
  • We talk to each other. You work closely with production, product development and other teams.
  • You observe markets, identify trends and risks at an early stage and contribute your recommendations to the strategic development of purchasing.

Requirements

  • Several years of experience in strategic purchasing, with foundations in the food industry or in trade. If you also know how gastronomy ticks, the better.
  • Leadership experience and the understanding that leadership is more than distributing tasks.
  • Strong negotiating strength and commercial strength.
  • Knowledge of import, customs and international sourcing.
  • Secure handling of MS Office and ERP systems.
  • German and English fluent.
  • And most importantly, you don’t want to manage. You want to shape.
