Full description

The SIX staff restaurant offers fresh and seasonal food every day. From a large selection, our guests can cater during the day. To complement our team, we are looking for a service- and front-oriented personality who brings pleasure to the profession.

Luogo di lavoro: Community gastronomy Migros Catering Service, staff restaurant SIX-HTP, Pfingstweidstrasse 110, 8005 Zurich

Important information

Working days from Monday to Friday. We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Preparation and production of food for banquets and events in high quality and large quantities
  • Organization of the Mise en Place and planning of work processes in the team depending on the size of the event
  • Close coordination with the kitchen management and cooperation with service and event management
  • Drafting and issuing the courts according to specifications
  • Co-design of menus and consideration of special wishes and special diets
  • Compliance with GMZ quality standards and the HACCP hygiene directives as well as physical inspection and warehousing management

Requirements

  • 1-2 years of professional experience: in the required area
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): as a chef
  • Motivated, organized and humorous team player personality
  • Efficient, forward-looking and orderly work
  • Enjoy working with colleagues from various backgrounds and cultures as well as with guests
  • Flexible service readiness from Monday to Friday (without room hour)
  • German (very good knowledge)

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Expert Discussion: In a specialist discussion, specific knowledge relevant for the execution of the job is requested by the manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, the team. If possible, there is time to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Test work: A trial day or half day gives the opportunity to get to know the future working environment and the team members better. And vice versa, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
