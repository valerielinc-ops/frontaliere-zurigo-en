Full description

Introduction To strengthen our team, we are looking for an entrepreneurially minded person for the Winterthur plant Maintenance Technician, 80 - 100%

Your tasks

  • Preventive maintenance work on all equipment at the Winterthur plant in accordance with Mobilty Work (maintenance software) planning and allocation
  • Troubleshooting of the systems
  • Defined inspection rounds according to maintenance specifications with feedback in Mobilty Work
  • Execution of maintenance and repair orders according to the specifications and specifications of the maintenance management
  • Collaboration in major maintenance work, occasional extraordinary assignments (e.g. Saturday / cantonal holiday) as well as during winter and summer inspections
  • Recording the faults and downtimes in Mobilty Work
  • Reporting of maintenance material and spare parts consumption and demand
  • Performing the 5S and WCM activities in the area
  • Production support at the plants
  • Cross-plant cooperation Winterthur / Volketswil

Your profile

  • Trained electrician, locksmith, agricultural machinery mechanic, polymechanic,
  • Electrician or similar
  • Further training as a maintenance specialist is an advantage
  • Knowledge of Siematic S7 / Profibus and Profinet is an advantage
  • Pneumatics knowledge
  • Experience from maintenance with heavy machinery as well as experience in the field of conveyor and drive technology
  • Experienced team player with process understanding
  • Openness in dealing with new media/tools
  • Good knowledge of German

Your prospects

  • Versatile area of responsibility in an international yet family-run company
  • A corporate culture with room for personal responsibility and entrepreneurial ideas
  • An environment in which openness and appreciation are a matter of course
  • Attractive employment conditions with above-average social benefits
  • Support for your personal development

More about us "MAKING THE WORLD A BETTER HOME" - that is our purpose. With our broad brand portfolio, we develop, produce and distribute materials and solutions for the construction sector and various industrial markets in a continuous innovation process. As the world's leading manufacturer of building materials for high-quality building envelopes, special products and systems for building and civil engineering, tiling solutions as well as innovative floor and wall constructions, Saint-Gobain Weber AG is committed to the people in their construction and living environment. We are a subsidiary of the French global corporation Saint-Gobain. The group is committed to comfortable, energy-efficient and liveable buildings. In addition to Weber, Saint-Gobain is also represented in Switzerland by companies such as Rigips, ISOVER, Sanitas Troesch and Vetrotech.
