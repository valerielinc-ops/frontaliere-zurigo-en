About the role Are you completing your studies soon and looking for a career start in permanent employment? With us you will get to know the versatile activities of a product engineer. For our professional and well-established team in the Business Support, Technical Department of Sika Schweiz AG, we are looking for a committed, professional and humanly convincing personality as a junior product engineer (80-100%) after agreement in Zurich-Altstetten. In this activity, you are responsible for the following tasks:

  • Support and introduction to various sub-sortia by experienced product engineers
  • Maintenance of a small product range with regard to technical and application performance
  • Preparation of product data sheets, application guidelines and other technical documents
  • Collaboration in various projects (market studies, market development plans)

Your skills and experience

  • A university degree as a civil engineer, economic chemist or materials scientist
  • Professional practice in the construction industry is an advantage
  • You like to approach people, communicate stylishly in German and have good knowledge of English (verbal and written)
  • Your strengths include high self-motivation and initiative, pleasure in customer contact and of course the necessary bite

Why you should come to us

  • Entry to a technical function as a product engineer
  • Intensive training (6-12 Mte.) – Support by experienced product engineers as mentors
  • Trust is the most important thing for us. We trust in your skills and provide you with many design spaces – every day anew
  • Uncomplicated managers who trust and a family atmosphere with a friendly tone - this is what makes working at Sika special - many of us call it the Sika spirit.

Sika is a great employer – especially for employees who rely on trust in cooperation. We look forward to your complete application dossier.

We offer competitive remuneration based on local market standards and the specific responsibilities of each job. The remuneration shall be based on the skills, responsibility, education and/or training relevant to the position. We are committed to fair and equitable remuneration schemes in accordance with applicable laws, the Chemicals Tariff Agreement and regulations.

About Sika Sika is a specialty chemical company, global leader in the development and production of systems and products for gluing, sealing, steaming, reinforcing and protecting in construction and industry. Sika has a global presence with subsidiaries in 103 countries, produces in over 400 factories, develops innovative technologies for customers around the world and thus contributes significantly to the sustainable transformation in construction and transport. The more than 33,000 employees generated a turnover of CHF 11.20 billion in 2025.
