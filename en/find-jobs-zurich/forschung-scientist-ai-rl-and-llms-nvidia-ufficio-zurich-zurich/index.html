  • PhD or equivalent
  • 6+ years of experience in Computer Science, relevant technical field, or similar, completed prior to joining NVIDIA.

  • Proficiency in Python or similar programming languages for developing and debugging.
  • Expertise in analyzing and collecting data from various sources.

Ways to stand out from the crowd

  • Experience in building and analyzing large-scale models for vision, language, speech, or general agents.
  • Established in guiding the development of AI research communities.

  • Outstanding problem-solving skills and the ability to think creatively and collaboratively.
