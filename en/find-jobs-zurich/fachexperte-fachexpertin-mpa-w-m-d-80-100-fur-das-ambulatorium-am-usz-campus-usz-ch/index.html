USZ Campus

1 January 2027 or by agreement

In our interdisciplinary ambulatory, we combine the fields of cardiology, vascular surgery, immunology, nephrology, traumatology, visceral surgery, plastic surgery and thoracic surgery. We work with a state-of-the-art and process-oriented infrastructure in an extremely dynamic and innovative environment.

Do you want to actively contribute your expertise and further expand it?

  • In our versatile ambulatory you work as a medical practice assistant or a health care professional. Our working hours are from Monday to Friday between 07.30 and 17.30.
  • As a specialist / expert, you also work closely with the care expert. You design and evaluate specialist topics and build individual training courses. In everyday work, you support your team with exciting technical questions.
  • Do you have any experience as an expert yet? Then you are still very welcome. Above all, it is important to us that you already have several years of professional experience in a doctor's office or hospital and are interested in the new function. What is also important to us is the willingness to complete the corresponding further training.
  • We live our USZ values: together, responsible and for the benefit of all. Does that also speak to you? Then we are very happy to meet you.
  • As a warm and colourful 40-strong team, we value both self-employment and teamwork in our varied everyday work. With great conviction and enthusiasm, we also train six learners.

Health

We support your health. In addition to personal consultations and courses of our health management that promote your well-being, we offer you access to over 120 sports with a subsidized annual subscription to the ASVZ.

Flexibility

We promote a balanced work-life balance: Almost half of our employees work part-time, and about 50% of the tasks can be done in the home office – if your working day allows it.

Family friendliness

We help you to reconcile family and work. Mothers are entitled to at least 6 months of maternity leave (of which 16 weeks are paid in full), fathers to at least 2 months of paternity leave (of which 2 weeks are paid in full).

Further education and development

We accompany you on your professional path and support your development with individual career and further education opportunities.

Insurance

We cover 60% of your pension fund contributions and offer attractive special conditions for supplementary insurance.

Mobility

Telephony
