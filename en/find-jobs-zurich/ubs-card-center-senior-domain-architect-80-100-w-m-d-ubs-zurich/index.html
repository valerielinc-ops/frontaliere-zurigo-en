Full description

About us The company is a subsidiary of the company, the leading credit card processor in Switzerland. We handle well over 3 million Visa and Mastercard credit cards reliably through our systems. With more than 750 dedicated employees at the Glattbrugg and Manno locations, we combine many years of expertise with innovative power and work daily to make payments of tomorrow easier, safer and smarter.

Description As a Senior Domain Architect at the USIUS Card Center, you combine comprehensive infrastructure know-how with leadership skills. You design the architecture of the IT infrastructure in the map and payment environment, document the architecture landscape and make decisions on the technical lifecycle. At the same time, you take the professional leadership, proactively advance and bring the right people together to drive digital transformation and operational efficiency.

Tasks

  • Development, documentation and further development of the architectural landscape and roadmap for the infrastructure of card processing and payment solutions
  • Establishment and control of a transparent architecture governance including clear decision-making processes
  • proactive driving of infrastructure topics: You manage and coordinate cross-sectoral teams and provide for binding and documented decisions
  • Evaluation of new technologies (e.g. cloud platforms, containers, security solutions) and making sustainable architectural decisions
  • Optimization and redesign of IT infrastructure processes to ensure efficient, stable and secure processes
  • Close collaboration with Business-Stake Bearers, development teams and enterprise/solution architects to translate business needs into robust technology solutions
  • Definition of architectural standards and support for business cases and investment applications for technology initiatives

Requirements

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in computer science, information technology or comparable qualification
  • multi-year experience as an infrastructure architect or in a similar architectural role, ideally in the financial services or payment environment
  • very good understanding of IT infrastructure (networks, platforms, cloud, security) and their interaction in complex environments
  • you like to take responsibility, actively tackle topics, make decisions and target stakeholders to a common goal
  • excellent communication and stakeholder management skills; you are confidently moving at management level, delivering technical content clearly and triggering changes
  • A good knowledge of English and German
