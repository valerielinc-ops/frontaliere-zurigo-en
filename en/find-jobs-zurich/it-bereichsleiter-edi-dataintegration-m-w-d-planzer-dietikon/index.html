Life is too short for bad software – we are looking for smart minds! Become part of our 70-strong IT team full of energy, creativity and team spirit.Our team consists of innovative business analysts, crazy programmers, helpful supporters and many other work-loving employees. Are you ready to make a difference together? Then apply now!

Tasks

  • You assume strategic and operational overall responsibility for the low-code platform Lobster Data World and develop it further
  • Demanding IT projects in the area of data integration are initiated, planned and controlled in compliance with the deadlines, qualitative and financial requirements
  • As a central contact person, you coordinate the specialist departments involved, develop strategic solution concepts and ensure smooth integration of processes and systems.
  • You create project presentations and documentation as well as lead meetings and workshops and work with operational and administrative systems
  • Interdisciplinary project teams and employees lead you professionally and personnel with a cooperative, transparent and goal-oriented leadership style
  • In addition, you promote the development of your employees, coordinate personnel topics with HR and manage the area assigned to you economically and responsibly.

Profile

  • A completed commercial education or an equivalent qualification, for example a degree in computer science or business administration, forms your professional basis
  • Several years of professional experience in supply chain management and computer science bring you as well as ideally experience with the Lobster platform
  • Thanks to your leadership experience, you succeed in leading, promoting and developing employees in an appreciative, clear and goal-oriented manner.
  • A pronounced service concept and a consistent focus on the needs of internal and external customers set you apart
  • Your way of working is goal-oriented, careful and independent. In addition, you convince with pronounced problem capture, structuring and solution competence
  • Communication strength, teamwork, sense of responsibility and entrepreneurial thinking as well as very good German and English skills round off your profile

Benefits

  • You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • With us you start with 5 weeks of holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • We go with the times - enjoy with us the advantage of Homeoffice

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
