Full description

We are looking for a committed person for our consulting team Organic Agriculture, Soil and Biodiversity in Lindau

  • You support and accompany our specialists around the field in projects, consultations, as well as the implementation of practical tests in organic farming
  • You accompany these trials on our organic partner farm from seed to harvest
  • You work in resource projects with several specialist positions and work with your profile when creating important consulting and information materials for farms and specialist courses
  • You are a student or recent graduate of agronomy / agricultural sciences at ETHZ, HAFL or ZHAW or similar courses
  • You are technically broadly interested and open to a diversity of specialist topics and activities from the fields of agriculture and fodder cultivation to plant protection and fertilization to organic farming and digitization
  • You are ready to work hand in hand with our specialists and get to know the most diverse areas of plant cultivation at the Strickhof
  • You have the driver's license Category B That awaits you With us you will gain insights into the diversity of plant growth consultancy and experimentation. By working with specialists, agricultural companies, industry organizations and offices, you can expand your professional network with valuable contacts for your further career. You can also apply for this internship as an internship at ETHZ.
