Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We create transparency in the banking and insurance market and advise our customers comprehensively and neutrally. As we are growing strongly, we are looking for reinforcements at our headquarters at Paradeplatz in Zurich.

  • You will lead the development and implementation of new functions for applications and ensure the support and optimization of existing and new corporate customer applications.
  • You will analyze faults and errors in our applications and coordinate the solution finding with internal and external partners.
  • You will technically support the specialist departments, conduct training courses and drive continuous quality improvement.
  • You create and analyze reports on our applications in order to initiate data-based optimizations and further developments.
  • You coordinate idea management and implement promising innovation and digitalization projects.
  • Completed studies or further education in business informatics, business administration or a related field or shortly before graduation
  • Experience in operation, support, further development of applications and project management, ideally in the financial services environment
  • Enthusiasm for IT topics, knowledge of IT service management and pronounced customer orientation
  • Rapid problem identification, root cause analysis and pragmatic solution finding with internal and external partners
  • Enjoying a dynamic, service-oriented environment, structured working methods, even in hectic times
  • In addition to an exciting and challenging job in an interdisciplinary environment, you have the opportunity to shape the development of the application management of corporate customers through your initiative and commitment.
  • You are also at the right place for your further education: We support you individually and support you with external training within the framework of the job profile.
  • Anonymous surveys on satisfaction regularly confirm that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
