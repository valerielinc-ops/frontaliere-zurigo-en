You describe yourself as a service provider: in the innermost conviction. Positive feedback on your performance motivates you and spurs you on to further top performances. You bring the following requirements:

  • Flexible, sporty active and reliable personality with a high degree of safety thinking

  • Correct reputation (criminal record and eviction not older than 3 months must be attached to the application)
