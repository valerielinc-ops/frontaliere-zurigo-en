Full description

Over two billion people rely on Schindler every day. Our many years of experience and innovative strength make us number one in the Swiss elevator and escalator business. As part of the Schindler family, you design places for everyone more accessible, inclusive and sustainable. We combine innovation, security and inclusion and diversity within the company. Together one floor up as

Hydraulic technician*in elevators Region East (m/f/d/f) 80-100 %

Your day – your plan! Design your 40-hour week according to your needs. We attach great importance to your further development and offer you exactly what you need. Learn more about us in this video

Your new challenges

  • With your know-how in repairs and conversions, you can exchange mechanical, hydraulic and electrical components on hydraulic lift systems.
  • Replacement of control and hydraulic components in the elevator shaft and engine room
  • Your dexterity and technical understanding are fully used in the assembly and commissioning of elevator hydraulics.
  • When it hits, you tackle: You analyze problems, find solutions and get the plants back on track
  • You have an eye for the essentials and carry out condition analyses and inventories on existing elevator systems
  • You are the first contact person for our customers during your work on site
  • Together we continue to optimize! You support the continuous improvement process of the Growing Hydraulic Service Center and the internal processes with your ideas and know-how

Your ideal conditions

  • With your independent and structured working method. You take responsibility and have the claim to deliver clean work.
  • With your solution orientation and your focus on service. You always have the goal of finding the best solutions for our customers.
  • With your commitment and drive to drive things forward and continuously improve.
  • A completed apprenticeship in the field of hydraulics, agricultural machinery mechanics, mechanics, agricultural mechanics or electrical engineering.
  • Several years of professional experience, ideally in the field of hydraulics or elevator technology: you know how to keep track of pressure.
  • Willingness to travel within Switzerland: you love variety and new challenges on site.
  • You are an independent and reliable personality with a high level of initiative
  • Joy in dealing with customers
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Ticket PW (Cat. B)

Our benefits

  • Collaboration on an equal footing: Collaboration in a motivated specialist team of 9 people in a family environment.
  • Solid education and training: Two months of training in preparation for your new function (LiftCamp) as well as additional hydraulic training
  • Remuneration and expenses: Good remuneration with many extras. Catering expenses as well as hotel costs when settling outside the residential area.
  • Work-life balance: 40h week, 25 to 30 days of vacation, flexible working hours, part-time work and unpaid leave
  • Healthy working environment: High health & safety standards and company health management including social counselling and health promotion
  • Modern campus with its own staff restaurant & cafeteria, free car park, very good public transport connection and a park to stay
  • Employee benefits: participation in the public transport subscription, fleet discounts and reduced insurance premiums as well as “Corporate Benefits”
  • Cultural diversity and inclusion & diversity within the company
