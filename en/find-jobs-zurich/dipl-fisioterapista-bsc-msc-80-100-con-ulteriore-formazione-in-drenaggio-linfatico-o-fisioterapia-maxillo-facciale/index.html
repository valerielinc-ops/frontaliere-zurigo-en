BodyLab GmbH is an interdisciplinary joint practice in Zurich Altstetten. Osteopaths and physiotherapists work with us door to door, exchange ideas and discuss patients together. Our offer includes osteopathy/sportosteopathy, physiotherapy/sportophysiotherapy, medical rehabilitation and training, and preventive treatments.

  • Many of us have been there for years. We are proud of this, and we want to carry on this team spirit.

  • The demand for lymphatic drainage and jaw physiotherapy is greater than we can cover today. So your expertise is in demand.

  • · Individual and holistic treatment, care and advice of our patients:in all age groups and athletes:inside, focus musculoskeletal
  • Lymph drainage mainly postoperatively and orthopaedically (without compression bandages) and/or treatment of jaw disease (CMD)
  • · Close cooperation with our osteopath:inside and physiotherapist:inside
  • · Planning and billing of your own patients: inside

  • · Safe knowledge of German (min. B2) and good knowledge of English, other languages are advantageous
  • · Further qualifications such as Dry Needling, sports physiotherapy, active or manual therapy are a plus
  • · Joy in your profession, in your patient: inside and in a team that supports each other
