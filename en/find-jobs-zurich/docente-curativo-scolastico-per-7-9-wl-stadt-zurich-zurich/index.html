Full description

The Balgrist Primary School is located on the city border, on the Wehrenbachtobel in the Riesbach Quarter and belongs to the Zurichberg School District. In total, the Balgrist school unit comprises 8 classes with around 170 students from kindergarten to sixth grade. For four years we have been on the road as a day school with an extensive range of care. We are a dedicated team where teaching, food and leisure work closely together

Our school is looking for reinforcement as of 19 October 2026.

Tasks

  • They support two pupils in the field of ISR age-appropriate and accompany them appreciatively through their everyday school life and take over the IF lessons in the first class.
  • They prepare funding plans, diagnose the individual learning successes and advise the teachers on special educational issues.
  • They support the team as an important team member and contribute to the school in a profitable and motivating way.
  • The workload can be increased with the participation in the day school.

Profile

  • Teaching diploma (EDK recognized) for the elementary school with corresponding additional training as SHP or willingness to complete this part-time in the next 3 years.
  • Enjoyment of working with children and their targeted promotion.
  • They value inclusive and inclusive teaching.

We offer

A challenging task in an open-minded and helpful team, in which the teachers, curative teachers, specialist teachers and the support work closely together. Structured processes in the special education area in the entire school circle are a matter of course for us. We attach importance to a solution- and goal-oriented cooperation as well as the development and development opportunities of the individual employees. We achieve this through a committed school management, a well-organized school secretariat and an established cooperation with the school authority.

About us

The Zurichberg School District comprises districts 1, 7 and 8 with the districts Altstadt, Fluntern, Hottingen, Hirslanden, Riesbach and Witikon. The district school authority manages 18 school houses. These are affiliated with 43 kindergartens and over 40 care facilities of different sizes. Of the 14 organizational units, 11 are day schools.
