Location: Klinik Hirslanden and Klinik Im Park

Occupation by appointment

Reference number: 14386

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

YOUR TASKS

Klinik Hirslanden strengthens its nursing know-how in highly specialized medicine. For the Employee Pool in Zurich, we are looking for motivated and committed employees in the fields of heart medicine, neurosurgery, neurology including stroke, visceral surgery, urology, orthopaedics and oncology.

Are you looking for an independent and demanding task with the opportunity to adapt your roster to your needs? In the employee pool, you determine your service assignments and your monthly workload according to your needs, tailored to the needs of the department. The employment takes place after agreement in the hourly wage. Assignments are possible in the clinic Hirslanden and in the clinic Im Park.

  • Assumption of defined nursing tasks within the framework of the defined competence profile and on delegation of the Dipl. Care professionals
  • Ensuring patient-oriented care in accordance with our quality standards and the care process
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration in the team with our observing physicians and other clinical areas
  • Ensuring the housekeeping, administrative and logistical tasks on the bed department taking into account the hospital's internal requirements and framework conditions

Your profile

  • Completed training for health specialist EFZ (FAGE)
  • Professional experience and/or training in acute care
  • Very good knowledge of German (verbal and written)
  • Enjoy working in different teams
  • High social competence and customer orientation

In addition to an attractive workplace in the Klinik Hirslanden and Klinik In the park, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. You can expect extensive training and development opportunities from us. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family responsibilities. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. As employees who have to move completely for their work, you will receive four additional paid free days (except specialist and cadre doctors) for a 100% workload. With a reduced workload, the days off reduce accordingly. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from an equivalent financial compensation. These are just a few reasons why the Hirslanden Private Hospital Group is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.

For additional information, Eveline Haussener and Tanja Brüggemann, Head of Employees-Pool Square Zurich, under T or Janine Moschetto, Team Leader HR Recruitment Partner Square Zurich, at janine.moschetto@hirslanden.ch are at your disposal. We look forward to your contact.
