Online Marketing Manager (m/w/d) — Migrol AG

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Are you passionate about digital marketing, social media and data-driven campaigns? In this temporary role, you actively shape the online presence of our e-mobility services, develop creative campaigns, bring fresh content to our channels and ensure that our offers reach the right target groups.

Mansioni

  • Planning and conception of all online marketing and communication measures for the business area E-Mobility Services in the B2C and B2B environment
  • Responsibility for the implementation of online marketing measures on web, Migrol app, social media (organic and paid), SEA and other digital channels
  • Creation, planning and optimization of content (picture, text, video) for social media, blog posts and landing pages
  • Further development and optimization of campaigns using GA4 reporting, Google platforms, A/B testing and data-based analysis
  • Responsibility for location platforms such as Uberall and Google My Business Profile
  • Helping with community management
  • Responsibility for the Migrol App and the Migrol Master Platform (MMP)

Requirements

  • Several years of experience, at least 5 years: Marketing/Online Marketing
  • Eidg. Professional ID: Marketing/Communication
  • Several years of experience in online marketing (B2C and ideally also B2B) as well as experience in campaign implementation on various digital channels
  • In-depth knowledge in performance marketing, SEA and social media
  • Strong user experience in Canva, Adobe InDesign and Photoshop
  • Independent and self-responsible personality with a proactive approach
  • Organized, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Strong strength in communication, cooperation and teamwork
  • Analytical and conceptual skills (reading, interpreting and converting data into concrete measures)
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • French (advantageous)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Flexible place of work / home office: possibility to work in the home office on 1 to 2 days per week
  • Fair wages: standardized, gender-neutral and transparent wage model
  • Mobility: discount on fuel (Migrolcard), workshop products, car wash. CHF 200.-/year public transport contribution for car waiver
  • Mobile subscriptions: Highly discounted, private mobile subscriptions. Business mobile on request
  • Occupational pension scheme: Migros pension fund. Breakdown of premiums: Migrol 2/3, employee 1/3
  • Personal responsibility & freedom: Promoting personal responsibility in the professional environment
