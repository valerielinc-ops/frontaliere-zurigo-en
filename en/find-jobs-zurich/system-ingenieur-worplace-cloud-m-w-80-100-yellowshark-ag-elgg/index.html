02 Your responsibilities

  • Engineering: You are responsible for engineering in the areas of Microsoft client infrastructure and Digital Workplace for medium-sized to large customers, ensuring an optimal IT environment.

  • Error analysis and support: You analyze disruptions and error conditions in 3rd level support, initiate appropriate measures to resolve them, and monitor their implementation.

04 Your profile

  • Education: You have a solid IT education in the field of systems engineering.
  • Professional experience: You have been working as a Cloud Engineer for several years, preferably at an IT service provider or in a larger IT department of a company.
  • Client Management Expertise: You possess comprehensive knowledge of operating client management solutions such as Microsoft Intune and Microsoft Endpoint Configuration Manager.
