Role overview

As head of the department, you assume responsibility for a versatile bed station at the Clinic for Cardiology with 26 bed spaces at the University Hospital Zurich.

Here you can expect a challenging and varied spectrum of patients – from complex cardiological diseases and acute clinical pictures to patients with advanced heart failure and LVAD systems .

The Clinic of Cardiology is a nationally and internationally leading centre for the diagnosis, prevention and treatment of cardiovascular diseases.
