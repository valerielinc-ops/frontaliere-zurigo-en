Full description

The Lengg High School (OSSL) is a special school part of the Swiss Epilepsy Foundation. She works on behalf of the primary school office. We offer an interesting job, which is very suitable to start in the area of the separative special school.

Tasks

  • First contact person for students with personal, social or school issues as well as support in individual challenging situations.
  • Professional action in acute conflicts and crises, solution-oriented support for young people and development of perspectives.
  • Planning, implementation and evaluation of preventive projects and workshops on current topics (e.g. bullying, media literacy, social skills) at class level.
  • Close and regular exchange with school management, teachers, guardians as well as external specialist and advisory services, including participation in team and conference meetings.

Requirements

  • Completed training in social work FH (school social work, social pedagogy or social work) with a specialization in children and youth.
  • Ideally, first professional experience in a school context or in open youth work.
  • High social skills, strong empathy and joy in contact with young people at high school.
  • Resilience, assertiveness and serenity in demanding situations as well as a pronounced solution-oriented thinking.
  • Teamwork and enjoyment of an interdisciplinary, appreciative collaboration with teachers and school management.

We offer

  • A versatile, exciting and self-responsible range of tasks at a dynamic high school.
  • An open, supportive and motivated team that fosters a culture of togetherness.
  • The school is in the most beautiful location in the city of Zurich.
  • Space for own ideas, prevention projects and active participation in school life.
  • For a first impression a look at our school newspaper: POINT KOMMA STRICH

The Swiss Epilepsy Foundation (EPI) is a multi-sector company. It includes the EPI WohnWerk, the Lengg High School, the donation Dapples, a seminar center with restaurant and the EPI Central Services. In addition, the Swiss Epilepsy Foundation together with another foundation forms the sponsorship of Klinik Lengg AG, each with a clinic for epileptology and neurorehabilitation. Around 1000 employees are committed to these social tasks. The area is located on the outskirts of Zurich, with a fantastic view of the nearby Lake Zurich and the Alps.
