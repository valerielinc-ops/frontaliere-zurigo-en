Full description

The railway moves us – and we move the railway logistics of tomorrow. Planzer already transports more than 60% of its shipments by rail and handles around 90,000 railway shipments annually in the CDS network. From 2027, we will fundamentally redesign our railway network: a new transport network will be created at more than 20 stations throughout Switzerland. The go-live is early 2029. Do you want to help shape this future project from the beginning? Then come with us!

Tasks

  • You will take over the overall project management for the conception, planning and implementation of the new Swiss-wide rail transport network until the go-live at the beginning of 2029 and beyond.
  • Develop network and train concept: Together with our transport and logistics experts, you can plan train runs, timetables, capacities and production concepts for more than 20 stations with more than 30 CDS stations.
  • You optimize the future railway network in terms of quality, cost, stability and scalability and coordinate the necessary path orders.
  • Design operating concepts: Together with the locations, you develop shunting, handling and operating concepts – from the arrival of the train to the departure.
  • You coordinate the requirements of our CDS companies, RUs, infrastructure partners and other stakeholders and bring them together to form an all-Swiss operating concept.
  • From concept to go-live: You control implementation, migration, deadlines, costs, risks and project progress and ensure the coordination of railway production, road transport, IT and operational processes.

Profile

  • You bring several years of experience in rail freight transport, railway operations, railway production or railway network planning.
  • You know the Swiss railway landscape well and ideally have experience in cooperation with infrastructure operators and railway companies.
  • You have in-depth knowledge in path and scheduling, shunting processes, sidings, terminals or production concepts in rail freight transport.
  • Leading challenging projects is important to you: You have experience managing cross-divisional and cross-company projects and bring different interests to a common result.
  • You think analytically and conceptually, recognize complex relationships and translate them into pragmatic, feasible solutions.
  • Personal responsibility and negotiation skills distinguish you. A university or university of applied sciences degree in business informatics, computer science, logistics, supply chain management, business administration, industrial engineering or a comparable field of expertise is advantageous, but not a requirement. Very good knowledge of German is required
  • Knowledge of French and/or Italian is an advantage.

Benefits

  • With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • With us you start with 5 weeks vacation and benefit from the 5th year of service even from 6 weeks!
  • Early Bird or Night Owl? Full-time or part-time? We accept your wishes.

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
