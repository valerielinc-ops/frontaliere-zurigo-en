Put to the Point

They actively shape our central AWS platform. In our small team, everyone shares development and operations, and we automate consistently. With your enthusiasm for infrastructure as code and modern automation, you are consistently developing our AWS platform. As a valued person of trust, you empower our specialist teams and use your creative leeway to shape the digital future of our IT landscape in a sustainable way.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: #LI-MCH1012
  • Location: Operation Center 1, Frachtstrasse, 8058 Zurich

You can make this contribution

  • Further develop our AWS platform with over 100 AWS accounts with Terraform (Landing Zone, AWS Organizations, Account Vending, SCPs, etc.)
  • Automate recurring work, for example with CI/CD pipelines and Python
  • Support the operation and support of the platform in the team, including on a regular basis
  • As an expert, advise development and specialist teams on architectural issues as well as on the optimization of cost efficiency, the implementation of security requirements, onboarding and migration projects
  • Implement Security and Compliance as Code and Fix Findings

This makes them unique.

  • Completed computer science degree (FH/HF/uni or equivalent) and at least 5 years of professional experience as a Senior AWS Cloud Engineer
  • Analytical thinking and a strong understanding of complex IT architecture landscapes
  • You think like a software engineer, Git, CI/CD, Linux and clean code in Python or another language are a matter of course for you
  • They build infrastructure with code, not clicks. You have experience with Terraform or a comparable tool
  • Very good knowledge of German and a second official language
  • English skills are beneficial
