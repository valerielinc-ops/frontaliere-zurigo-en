Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Prison and Reintegration Prison Pöschwies Assistant / Assistant of Economics & Labour 80 - 100% With around 1400 employees, JuWe is the largest office within the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs and responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as for the execution of various types of detention. Our employees from around 40 professions support and promote the reintegration of delinquents and work effectively and progressively for a safe and just society. The Pöschwies Prison (JVA) is one of eight main departments of JuWe and the largest closed facility for men in Switzerland. With around 400 detention places and around 300 employees, it contributes to the protection of the general public in accordance with its legal mandate and at the same time creates the conditions for the rehabilitation of the detained persons. For the area of Economics & Labour (W&A), we are looking for a motivated, resilient and humorous personality as an assistant or assistant immediately or by agreement. Your tasks

  • In various specialist and project topics you are actively involved and accompany their implementation
  • Ensure the flow of information within the JVA, prepare communication topics appropriate to the addressee and coordinate media requests
  • In security and crisis management, you participate and take on organizational and professional tasks
  • Logging various meetings as well as conducting internal interviews and evaluations are also part of your area of responsibility.
  • Guided tours as well as larger internal and external events are planned, coordinated and set independently around your profile
  • A completed commercial training EFZ and several years of professional experience in a comparable assistant function form your professional basis
  • You have excellent knowledge of German
