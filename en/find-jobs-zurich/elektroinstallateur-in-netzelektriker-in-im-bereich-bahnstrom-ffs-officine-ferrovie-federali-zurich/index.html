You are part of a small, very experienced and collegial team and make an important contribution to the smooth functioning of the SBB railway infrastructure. On our website you will find more exciting information about the benefits. You can move that.

  • You independently carry out the commissioned construction and maintenance work in the area of the control systems of the catenary line as well as other power plants.
  • As a specialist technician: in Bahnstrom you play an important role in troubleshooting the systems.
  • You are assigned to the Zurich and Eastern Switzerland regions and ready to support other regions of Switzerland.
  • In this role, you are responsible for compliance with safety, quality and execution standards.

You bring that with you.

  • A high sense of responsibility and security as well as a high degree of resilience are among your great strengths.
  • You are a technically affine personality with an independent and flexible way of working and enjoy working in a team.
  • Your hearing and vision is good for detecting important acoustic and visual signals during track operations. You have a category B ticket.
  • You have completed basic electrotechnical training EFZ (Electrician: in, grid electrician: in or comparable professions). You communicate in German (verbal and written).
