Do you dream of 30 minutes per patient:in? From a team with a flat hierarchy? In this family-run country doctor’s office, just 20 minutes from Zurich, care quality counts more than case numbers. I'm happy to tell you more! The family practice has been established in its rural region for eight years and continues to grow. For the most part, the team has been working together for a long time, without large hierarchies and with the aspiration to do more than the minimum. Profit maximization is not the priority. Because a doctor leaves the practice at the end of the year, a long-term reinforcement is sought from January 2027, including an option on participation / takeover if interested. The working model is clearly plannable: your colleague (80%) is absent one week per month. This week you are in practice for five days, in the remaining weeks less are possible. The company also scores points with its location: Zurich can be reached in 20 minutes by car and in 30 minutes by train.

Their tasks

  • They take care of patients: inside of all age groups family doctor and with the time that a good medicine needs
  • In the week of absence of your colleague, you take over the full consultation and ensure continuity for the common patients: inside
  • They use on-site diagnostics independently: digital X-ray and sonography
  • Creating an equal team member You are developing the growing practice with

Your Competences

  • Specialist title General Internal Medicine, Practical Doctor / Practical Doctor or an equivalent title recognised in Switzerland (e.g. FA General Medicine or Internal Medicine from Germany)
  • It is important to you to take time for your patients, and you value a faithful, personal patient relationship.
  • Flexibility for a rhythm with one attendance week per month
  • Joy of working together at eye level, without hierarchies and with a real team spirit
  • German language skills at level C1
  • Foreign diploma? In the MEBEKO recognition and the approval steps, we personally accompany you

Their advantages

  • Fair wage: CHF 180,000 at 100%; after training on request change to the turnover share possible
  • Real perspective: In the long term, participation in practice up to takeover is conceivable
  • Plannable workload: 60-100% with a clear monthly rhythm instead of constant ad hoc bets
  • Time instead of time: quality of care before case numbers
  • Team without hierarchy: A well-established, long-standing team that makes decisions together
  • Good infrastructure: Digital X-ray, sonography and around 200 m2 bright practice rooms
  • Country and city: Rural tranquility, Zurich in 20 minutes by car or 30 minutes by train
