Integration coach MNA 80% in Zurich Uster (m/f/d)

Caritas Switzerland has been running asylum centres for families, individuals and unaccompanied minors (MNA) at four locations in the Canton of Zurich since March 2024 on behalf of the Cantonal Social Office (KSA). As an integration coach MNA, you coordinate the social, educational and professional integration of unaccompanied minor asylum seekers at the MNA Center in Uster.

Their tasks

  • Documentation of assessments or location determinations, in particular short assessment IAS "Results of the assessment of potential" for all MNAs
  • Applications for integration measures of the IAZH catalogue
  • Organisation of educational and professional follow-up solutions
  • Documentation of the discussions, planned and implemented measures
  • Quarterly processing of the areas of life vocational and school integration in close cooperation with socio-pedagogical reference person
  • Discussion documentation on integration topics
  • Responsibility Professional integration: clarifications, cooperation with specialist positions and support in the search for apprentices
  • Cooperation with institutions and authorities to discuss integration-specific topics
  • Reporting according to specifications of the Integration Department
  • Preparation of integration-relevant information for the exit phase
  • Exchange with schools, experts (participation in location discussions)
  • Completed training in social work, social pedagogy, vocational, educational or career guidance, pedagogy or equivalent qualification
  • Further education in case management and/or on integration topics of advantage
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss education and training system, in particular of the various educational pathways and follow-up solutions
  • At least five years of professional experience, including at least two in the care sector
  • Joy of working with unaccompanied minor asylum seekers (MNA) and understanding of their special living situation
  • Knowledge and experience in the application of the Social Assistance Act
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German and good oral knowledge of another national language and/or English and/or an asylum language
  • Computer science skills ideally with case guidance software tutoris
  • An unobjectionable special private extract from the criminal record or Willingness to submit it before taking up the job
