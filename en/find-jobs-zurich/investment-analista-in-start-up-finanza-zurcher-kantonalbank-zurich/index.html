At ZKB Start-up Finance, we invest venture capital in innovative start-ups and scale-ups. We help the most promising founders to put their vision into action. Over the past 21 years, we have been able to support more than 320 start-ups, such as GetYourGuide or Climeworks, making us one of the most active venture capital investors in Switzerland.

Do you want to be part of our innovative, dynamic and passionate investment team and contribute your expertise and passion for entrepreneurship and groundbreaking ideas? Do you also have an entrepreneurial and collegial spirit, are curious and appreciate a direct feedback culture? Then we look forward to meeting you.

Your tasks

  • Lead function for our analyst team: Coordination of the tasks of the investment analyst and ensuring efficient cooperation with the investment managers and the team leader
  • Examination of investment opportunities
  • Implementation of due diligence and preparation of investment proposals in close cooperation with our investment managers
  • Support of the start-up finance team in administrative tasks
  • Responsibility and support for an own start-up portfolio
  • Support of start-ups in all investor topics during the investment process
  • Representation of the team at start-up events and networking events
  • University degree at Bachelor's or Master's level
  • First practical experience in the venture / transaction business or at a start-up
  • Entrepreneurial Mindset: You have great interest in new technologies, entrepreneurship and venture capital
  • Maker mentality and ownership: You bring your PS to the ground and take responsibility
  • Communication talent: You have the ability to abstract complex issues and render them comprehensible (e.g. decision-makers)
  • Teamplayer: you are an open-minded personality with analytical skills, an independent, reliable way of working and a quick understanding
  • Flowing German and negotiation-proof English skills
  • Steep learning curve from the start in a task area where you can take on a lot of personal responsibility
  • Participation and networking opportunities in our inspiring start-up ecosystem
  • A team of motivated and committed colleagues who look forward to working with you
  • A modern workplace at the headquarters with very good transport connections and the possibility to organize your work flexibly
  • An employer who invests heavily in her employees and offers attractive training opportunities
  • Attractive overall package, which offers many benefits and perks beyond wages
