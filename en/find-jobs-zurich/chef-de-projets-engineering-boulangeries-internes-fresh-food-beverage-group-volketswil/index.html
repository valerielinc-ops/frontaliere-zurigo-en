Projektleiter Engineering Hausbäckereien (m/w/d) — Fresh Food & Beverage Group

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

You will lead exciting investment projects for Migros in-house bakeries and actively shape standards in infrastructure, construction and manufacturing technology. With your technical know-how, your project management skills and your eye for quality, costs and deadlines, you will make a decisive contribution to the further development of our in-house bakeries.

Mansioni

  • You will develop and define the FFB-HB implementation standards in the areas of infrastructure/construction as well as manufacturing technology and production equipment for home bakeries
  • In cooperation with the heads of production of home bakeries and the heads of sales at Migros, you will be responsible for the professional handling of investment projects, based on the home bakery strategy of the FFB
  • You ensure that the quality specifications in the projects are met in terms of costs, benefits and deadlines
  • You develop solution proposals and preliminary studies according to the defined requirements
  • You will prepare detailed cost estimates, layout drawings and planning of equipment requirements
  • You realize approved projects including order processing
  • You will be responsible for the technical documentation and the structured handover of the projects to the clients.
  • You are responsible for budget preparation and budget implementation in the Migros Home Bakeries division
  • You will provide technical advice and support to the HB mechanics in the maintenance of the home bakery regions

Requisiti

  • Professional experience: Several years of experience in the management of technical projects with, ideally in the field of production, food industry or retail
  • Completed apprenticeship (EFZ): Basic technical training with further training in the field of engineering, construction, production technology or project management
  • You have in-depth knowledge in the areas of infrastructure, construction, manufacturing technology and production equipment
  • You think economically and always keep an eye on costs, benefits, quality and deadlines
  • You have experience in preparing cost estimates, layouts and technical documentation
  • You work independently, in a structured and solution-oriented manner
  • You convince with strong communication skills, coordination skills and a confident appearance
  • You enjoy advising different stakeholder groups and successfully implementing projects
  • German (business fluent)
  • French (very good knowledge)

Cosa offriamo

  • Employee offers: Cumulus employee points, discounted mobile subscriptions, higher interest rates Migros Bank, club school/fitness fees and more.
  • Occupational pensions: Comprehensive protection in the Migros pension fund – with first-class and attractive benefits.
  • Social benefits: 730 days of daily sickness allowance with full net salary.
  • Holidays: 5 weeks of vacation for everyone, 6 weeks of vacation from the age of 50, 7 weeks of vacation from the age of 60, purchase of additional vacation days possible.
  • Mobile-flexible working model: Flexible working hours with the option of working from home.
  • Training and further education: We actively train employees as part of internal programs and, if necessary, support external training with time and cash benefits.
