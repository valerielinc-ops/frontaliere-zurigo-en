Full description

EXPERIENCES

  • Degree in electrical or mechanical engineering (TU/FH) or comparable technical training
  • Several years of practical work in the industrial sector, preferably in the railway sector
  • Experience in commissioning, testing and troubleshooting
  • Experience in creating, reviewing and maintaining technical documentation
  • Experience in requirements management (IBM DNG, PLM or similar)
  • Knowledge of railway signaling systems (CBTC and/or main line interlocking) is an asset highly advantageous
  • Be a team player, solution oriented and flexible for night work and travel
  • English proficiency is required, German or French language fluent RESPONSIBILITIES
  • Develop, maintain and update procedures, test specifications and T&C workflows to ensure consistent execution
  • Coordinate, execute and report system deployments and field tests based on approved on-site test documentation
  • Manage configurations hardware and software in the field, ensuring proper documentation and version control
  • Record and analyze incidents and follow up on mitigation and corrective actions
  • Troubleshoot and resolve technical issues, in close collaboration with engineering, project manager and customer
  • Plan electrical and mechanical modifications and purchase components in coordination with engineering and project manager
  • Perform or support other field activities (testing, limited electrical and mechanical modifications, etc.), including works on the tracks
  • Collaborate closely with the customer and the installation teams
