Original description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

As a cutting machine operator, are you looking for a technical challenge and would you like to use your expertise in a varied job? Would you like to become part of a well-positioned team in which you also have future-oriented perspectives? Then you have come to the right place in our service center at the Schwarze Pumpe location: our experienced team with around 30 employees are part of our service center network in Germany. We offer our customers technically tailored solutions for their areas of application. Our range of services also includes preventive maintenance, servicing and repair of pumps, valves and turbines as well as other rotating machines for both Sulzer and products from other manufacturers.

In this position you are responsible for

As a cutting machine operator, you are responsible for independently machining a wide variety of components on CNC-controlled and conventional cutting machines. In the area of ​​CNC turning technology, you will work on turning and milling machines with Siemens controls. On conventional machines, work is carried out on boring mills and lathes with center widths of up to 4500mm. You can also rely on a wide range of machines and active support from the existing team. Your tasks include, among others:

  • Individual processing of components according to drawings
  • Independent determination of machining values taking material data into account
  • When focusing on CNC turning technology: completely independent creation of CNC programs (cycle control)
  • Independent setup and clamping of the machine or components
  • Operation of the cutting machines
  • Inspection of the manufactured components as part of worker self-inspection
  • Participation in the optimization of machining processes

To be successful in this position you should have the following: You will fit well into our team if, in addition to the necessary technical skills, you also have the right attitude: due to the high demands of our service business, a strong sense of responsibility, a precise and reliable way of working, and flexibility in terms of tasks are particularly important to us. You also have the following qualifications:

  • Successfully completed training as a cutting machine operator or equivalent training/experience
  • Ideally several years of relevant professional experience
  • Crane operator's license is an advantage (otherwise: willingness/suitability for training upon entry)
