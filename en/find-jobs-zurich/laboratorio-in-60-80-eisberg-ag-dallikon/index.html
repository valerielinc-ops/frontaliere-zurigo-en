Full description

You like to work with food, carry out analyses carefully and actively participate in safe, high-quality products – then we look forward to you.

  • Contract type: Perpetual
  • Pensum: 60% - 100%
  • Working time model: Flexible time
  • Job: from now on
  • Language: German
  • Location: Eisberg AG, Dänikerstrasse 31 8108 Dällikon

Tasks

  • Ensuring compliance with all laboratory guidelines and active participation in laboratory operations
  • Carrying out tests on various foods using microbiological and molecular biological methods
  • Maintenance and management of laboratory data in the Limsophy system
  • Manufacture of culture media and incoming goods inspection
  • Execution of general cleaning and hygiene work in the laboratory, including autoclaving, disinfecting and maintenance work, etc.
  • Maintenance, control and monitoring of laboratory equipment and test equipment
  • Management of laboratory stocks

Requirements

  • Completed training as a laboratory assistant in EFZ field of microbiology or comparable qualification
  • Practical experience in the laboratory and good GLP knowledge
  • Good knowledge of MS Office and LIMS
  • Team-capable, open and communicative personality
  • Flexible working hours and readiness for sporadic weekend work
  • Independent, careful and reliable work method
  • Customer and service-oriented thinking and acting

This is what we offer

  • Food: Cuisine is very important to us: Freshly prepared salads, soups, tea and fruit are available to our employees free of charge. In addition, you can take home products from the personal shelf every day.
  • Mobility: Benefit from convenient connections to public transport, attractive discounts for public transport and sufficient parking – sustainable and worry-free on the road!
  • Professional development opportunities: Expand your knowledge through extensive further education offers, high-quality training in the Coop Education Center and a wide range of personal development opportunities such as mentoring and coaching.
  • Discounts & Discounts: You benefit from the Coop Personal Discount Card with various discounts in the retail trade, in specialty shops and supermarkets as well as other attractive services, for example in the area of insurance.
  • Premiums, social benefits, holidays: You benefit from 5 weeks of holidays, from the age of 50 even from 6 weeks. The pension fund contributions are borne 2/3 by the company and 1/3 by the employees. If the employee recommendation is successful, you will receive a premium of CHF 1,000. –
