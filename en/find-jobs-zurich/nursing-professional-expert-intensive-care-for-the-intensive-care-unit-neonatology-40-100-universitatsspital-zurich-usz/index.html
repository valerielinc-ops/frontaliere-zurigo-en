Full description

High tech hits the heart! Become part of our dedicated team in the neonatal intensive care unit - where technical understanding meets human warmth. Together we do great things for the smallest.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • You nurture and care for 1 - 3 premature babies from the 23. SSW or seriously ill newborns - loving, professional and individual. • You accompany children and their families empathetically and family-centered through a challenging time. • You are part of the first hours of a new life: first care in the birth department and accompany newborns in external transports. • You work according to current, evidence-based guidelines and actively shape the further development of care quality. • You are not alone: we work interprofessionally and meet at eye level. Requirements • Completed training as a nurse / nurse specialist. • Ideally, a postgraduate degree in intensive care (NDS) or an NDK neonatology. • Professional experience in pediatrics or neonatology. • Very good knowledge of German at C1 level, other languages are a plus. We offer: • A flexible roster with the ability to work primarily in your preferred shifts. • An individual introduction as well as personal and professional development in the directions of subject, education or management. • Direct access to current research results and work with the latest technology. • An open ear for your ideas and opinions as well as the opportunity to implement them together. • Financial support for external training or courses. • A good work-life balance is important to us, which is why we offer you the following: • short drift times at the beginning of the shift • Days and nights free of service as desired (depending on the amount required) • After training, uncomplicated increase or reduction of your workload • After completion of training: Possibility of additional employment in the nursing pool neonatology - for even more flexibility and exciting assignments • Possibility of unpaid leave • Attractive perks such as ZVV-BonusPass, general subscription or ASVZ annual subscription Do you feel addressed? We are pleased about that! Contact us, we want to get to know you. Let’s find out if we fit together: just upload your dossier to our eRecruiting tool or send your documents to michele.walter@usz.ch so we can get in touch with you.
