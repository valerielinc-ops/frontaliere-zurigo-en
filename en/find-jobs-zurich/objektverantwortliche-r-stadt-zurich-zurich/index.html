Full description

For the Facility Management division, we are looking for an experienced person for the Department of Building Cleaning Administration Buildings immediately or by agreement.

Tasks

  • Personnel and professional management of the subordinate employees in the entrusted large object with regard to work distribution, work execution, quality and safety as well as introduction and training of new employees.
  • Coordination and introduction of sickness, accident and holiday assistance from external companies as well as control functions regarding working hours, absences, work processes, quality, Suva regulations, etc.
  • Review of external or self-cleaning as well as contact person for suppliers.
  • Ordering cleaning materials and equipment in the Service Center Material, material delivery to the cleaning staff.
  • conducting target and assessment interviews.

Profile

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship, basic building cleaning course with additional training in cleaning techniques and products/surfaces
  • Several years of professional experience as well as experience in personnel management
  • Good computer user knowledge
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German
  • Multicultural understanding, teamwork, reliability, resilience and assertiveness

We offer

An exciting and versatile job awaits you in a dynamic working environment where you can contribute your skills. A motivated team looks forward to your support.

About us

On behalf of the City Council, we represent and manage around 1950 properties. In the areas of space and infrastructure, we offer numerous services to the city administration and advise the departments and the city council on strategic issues.
