Become part of our team!

For the operation of the cash center, we are looking for you as a committed and motivated employee forwarding (m/f/d). In our dynamic working environment, we offer you further development opportunities as well as topic-specific training and further education.

What do you expect?

  • Reception and control of banknote and precious metal consignments
  • Checks on entry and exit of consignments
  • Unpacking and unpacking of consignments in accordance with regulations
  • All administrative work related to consignments
  • Assignments in the entire process sequence of a cash center

What do you bring?

  • EFZ logistician or completed vocational apprenticeship
  • Existing forwarding and logistics knowledge
  • Very high reliability, accuracy and sense of duty
  • High load capacity, also physical
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Correct extract from enforcement and criminal records

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
