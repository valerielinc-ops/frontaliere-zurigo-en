For the Department of Visceral and Transplantation Surgery, we are looking for an established personality.

Their main tasks

  • Proactive and independent support of the medical profession in all administrative and organizational matters
  • Organization of meetings and telephone conferences incl. Space Reservations and Catering
  • Organisation of congresses and symposia
  • Writing medical reports off dictation
  • Reporting incl. Controlling
  • Administrative assistance in preparing the office hours
  • Administrative preparation case presentations kidney transplant/live kidney donation
  • Fee and operating service controlling
  • Processing of legal service requests
  • Management of various lists and statistics
  • Introduction of new employees to the KISIM (clinical and information system)
  • General secretarial services incl. Triage
  • Representation of other secretarial staff

Your profile

  • Several years of experience as medical and/or hospital secretary:in
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German and English, as well as advantageous knowledge of French and or Italian
  • Good PC user knowledge
  • Flexibility, resilience and good knowledge of medical terminology
  • Independent, flexible, team capable and reliable personality

Our offer

You can expect a versatile and responsible activity in a modern and dynamic working environment. We attach great importance to an open, collaborative cooperation and a good cooperation in the team. Self-initiative, independence and flexibility are just as much in demand as a high sense of quality and responsibility. A motivated and committed team looks forward to welcoming you and making a difference together with you. For this post, dossiers from recruiters cannot be considered.
