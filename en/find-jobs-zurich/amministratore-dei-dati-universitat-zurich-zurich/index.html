Full description

Psychological Institute

Job opening 01.01.2027 or by arrangement, temporary

With the growing focus of psychological research on large-scale data collection, multimodal data sources, replication studies and open science practices, robust, sustainable data management is becoming a prerequisite. With this position, we want to further develop research data management (FDM) at the Psychological Institute and tailor it precisely to the requirements of modern psychological research. The focus is on processes, standards and support offerings that enable responsible, efficient and sustainable handling of research data throughout the entire research data lifecycle.

Tasks

We are looking for a dedicated personality who combines scientific experience with an interest in research data management and develops viable solutions for psychological research together with researchers and relevant specialist bodies.

Their tasks

  • Analysis of existing initiatives, platforms, tools and offers in the FDM
  • Development and standardization of processes along the Research Data Lifecycle
  • Development of practice-oriented guidelines, templates, workflows and information materials
  • Design of information and training opportunities and participation in implementation
  • Cooperation with relevant specialist bodies and stakeholders as well as clarification of interfaces and responsibilities
  • Advising researchers on FDM issues

Profile

  • Graduate degree at master’s level or higher, preferably in psychology, a related empirical science, data science, information science or a comparable field of expertise
  • Several years of practical experience in empirical research with social, humanities or natural science methods as well as a good understanding of scientific research processes
  • In-depth knowledge of research data management, in particular FAIR principles, open science and good scientific practice
  • Understanding key requirements for the handling of research data, especially in the areas of data protection, information security, ethics and law
  • Ability to analyze complex requirements and translate them into practical processes, standards and guidelines
  • Strong consulting and communication skills as well as enjoyment of working with researchers and various specialist bodies
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working methods as well as strong conceptual skills
  • Very good knowledge of German and English

Advantage

  • Doctorate or comparable in-depth scientific experience
  • Experience in the development and documentation of processes, standards or workflows
  • Knowledge of psychology-specific research software, repositories and collaborative research environments
  • Experience with sensitive research data

What we offer

You will be given the opportunity to actively shape the further development and sustainable anchoring of research data management at the Psychological Institute. The position combines scientific work with conceptual development, consulting, networking and the implementation of concrete solutions for research. You work at the interface between researchers, research support and relevant specialist centres and can contribute your scientific experience directly to the development of contemporary FDM structures and services. The position is linked to a project and limited to four years.
