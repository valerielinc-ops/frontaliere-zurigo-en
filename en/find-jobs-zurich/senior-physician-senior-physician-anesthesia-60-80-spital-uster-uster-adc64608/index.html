Personal – competent – close Welcome to the Hospital Uster, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As the central pillar of health care in the Oberland region of Zurich and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you experience the highest level of expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and promoted in your professional development. Spital Uster is a teaching hospital of the Medical Faculty of the University of Zurich.Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of a "regional and digital health hub in Züri East"! In our anesthesia department (cat. B) we carry out around 7000 anesthesias annually – in child and adult anesthesia. Our broad spectrum includes: general surgery, traumatology, orthopaedics, gynecology/obstetrics, urology, hand surgery, ENT, interventional brochoscopy. Regional anesthesia is an integral part of our clinical everyday life. In addition, our department is responsible for the postoperative pain service, the medical management of the emergency service and the participation in the regional emergency service.
