Full description

Pensum: 60 - 80% | Ottenbach

With energy from biomass into the future! You can actively contribute your knowledge about the biomass, your enthusiasm and your commitment and take responsibility. The biomass plants offer you exciting and challenging tasks as well as the freedom and flexibility to contribute and implement your own ideas.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • Co-responsible for processing the delivered green material according to the technically, economically and qualitatively specified criteria
  • Wheel loading and shifting work
  • Carrying out maintenance, maintenance and repair work
  • Maintaining order and cleaning of plant
  • Readiness to perform picket service

Profile & Requirements

  • Manual, technical or agricultural vocational training completed
  • Driving licence Cat. B
  • Advantage of possession of the truck/wheel loader test
  • Accustomed to acting and working independently and responsibly
  • Maintenance and repair services of machinery and equipment
  • Computer skills
  • Place of residence max. 25 minutes from place of work
