Senior Audit Data Analyst (Hybrid) — Swiss Re

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Do you enjoy using data to uncover insights, strengthen controls and support better business decisions? As a Senior Audit Data Analyst in Group Internal Audit (GIA), you'll combine advanced analytics expertise with agile ways of working to support risk-based auditing and help Swiss Re achieve its vision of resilience. About the role In this role, you will lead and develop data-driven audit approaches by partnering with auditors to understand key risks, controls and business processes. You will identify and acquire the required data, perform analytical assessments, and discuss insights with both audit teams and auditees to support informed decision-making. Beyond delivering analytics solutions, you will contribute to advancing data-driven auditing across GIA by sharing your expertise, coaching junior analysts and auditors, and helping strengthen analytics capabilities within the function. The Audit Analytics Center of Expertise equips GIA with advanced analytics capabilities and a team of specialists dedicated to delivering end-to-end solutions that enhance the effectiveness and efficiency of internal auditing at Swiss Re. About the team Group Internal Audit provides independent and objective assurance to the Board of Directors and Group Executive Committee, helping protect Swiss Re's assets, reputation and long-term sustainability. Through our audits, we assess the effectiveness of internal controls, identify opportunities for improvement, and deliver insights that help strengthen the organization's operations.

About you

Education and experience

  • University degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
  • Minimum 7 years of relevant professional experience.
  • Strong proficiency in SQL and Python.
  • Experience translating business requirements into analytical use cases.
  • Proven ability to manage end-to-end analytics delivery.
  • Experience working in agile environments and applying agile practices.
  • Experience working with Internal Audit or other assurance functions, such as Risk Management or Compliance (preferred).
  • Proven experience developing GenAI assistants and agents, and applying them across the audit lifecycle, including risk assessment, audit scoping, control testing and reporting.
  • Experience applying machine learning techniques to solve business problems (preferred).

Personal skills

  • Critical thinker that sees the "big picture" (e.g. overall themes, trends, goals), generates innovative ideas and solutions to problems and makes recommendations in the face of complexity, conflicting pressures and ambiguous circumstances.
  • Professional communicator, able to gain and maintain a trusted relationship while delivering difficult messages
  • Structured analyst, capable to adopt and use data-driven auditing, including data analytics, to assess risks, scope audits and test controls. Strong attention to detail.
  • Team worker, able to listen to others but also influence in order to see the wider picture and achieve a vision
