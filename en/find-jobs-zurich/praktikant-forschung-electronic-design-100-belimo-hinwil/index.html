Full description

Summary

At Belimo, we are rethinking how HVAC field devices are commissioned and adapted in the field. With nearly 50 years of market leadership, we continuously push the boundaries of embedded systems to enable energy-efficient, reliable, and flexible building automation solutions.

In this internship, you will investigate measurement technologies, system architectures, and embedded implementations for a next-generation electrical measurement platform. The objective is to evaluate concepts, derive requirements, develop prototypes, and validate their technical feasibility. You will work in a multidisciplinary research environment, contributing from concept development through proof of feasibility.

What You'll Do

  • Investigate and evaluate electrical measurement concepts, hardware architectures, and technologies for accurate and cost-effective sensing solutions
  • Design and implement prototype hardware and embedded software solutions
  • Validate measurement performance through laboratory testing and benchmarking
  • Document findings and provide recommendations for future product development

Who You Are

  • You are studying electrical engineering, mechatronics, or a related field
  • You are interested in measurement technology, embedded electronics, and data acquisition systems
  • You have experience with electronic circuits, microcontrollers, and C/C++ programming
  • You enjoy hands-on prototyping, experimentation, and solving complex technical challenges

• Fluency in German or English
