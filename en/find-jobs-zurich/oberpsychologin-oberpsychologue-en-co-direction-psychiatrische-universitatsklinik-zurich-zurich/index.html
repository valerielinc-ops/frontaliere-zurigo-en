Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility.

For our crisis, clarification, emergency and triage centre KANT of child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy on Lenggstrasse we are looking for the day service from 08.00 to 17.00 by

  1. October 2026 or by arrangement one or

Oberpsychologin / Oberpsychologist in co-direction

50%

Their tasks

  • Personnel management of the emergency team consisting of psychologists and assistant doctors
  • Ensuring good cooperation with all interface partners
  • Participation in the design and further development of the emergency centre KANT
  • Taking over the active background service two days a week, preferably second half of the week - supervision and support of employees in the assessment of minors who present themselves in acute crises at the emergency center KANT (4-eyes principle in suicidality)
  • Discussion by telephone of minors, relatives or professionals who report to the KANT emergency centre
  • Quality assurance and monitoring of triagulations and admissions of inpatient minors
  • Timely correction of emergency ambulatory reports, progress reports and certificates of admission
  • Implementation and supervision of opinions on abortion in adolescents
  • Completed degree in psychology (Master/License) and psychotherapy training
  • Experience in clinical work with children and adolescents
  • Co-direction experience is beneficial
  • Ability to cooperate with multidisciplinary interface partners from the private and institutional sector, preferably in the Canton of Zurich
  • Analytical thinking, empathy and assertiveness
  • Resilience, flexibility and humor
  • Very good knowledge of German, other languages are advantageous
  • Together for people: Interdisciplinary cooperation for holistic psychiatric care
  • Strong in the team: appreciative cooperation and mutual support
  • Knowledge that advances: Extensive initial, further and further training offers
  • Combining family and work: access to the company’s own crèche
  • Future provision with added value: 60% employer contribution to the pension fund
  • Sustainable travel: Participation in the ZVV-BonusPass or general subscription
