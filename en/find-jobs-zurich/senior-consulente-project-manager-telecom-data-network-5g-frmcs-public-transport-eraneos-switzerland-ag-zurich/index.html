Full description

Eraneos is an international strategy, transformation and technology advisory group that helps companies thrive in a dynamic digital world. With first-class experts from business and technology, we empower our customers to set new standards for sustainable transformations – from strategy to implementation. Whether in the design of sustainable organizations, the development of new potential through data and AI or the protection of companies through holistic cybersecurity: We deliver results pragmatically and purposefully. Our team of around 1,200 dedicated employees is represented in offices in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, Denmark, Sweden, Austria, the UK, Singapore and the USA and combines global perspectives with strong local roots. Around 450 employees work at the Swiss locations in Zurich, Bern and Lausanne.

Shape the digital future of public transport with us. From the replacement of GSM-R by FRMCS to 5G-based mobile communications to the modernization of IP network infrastructures. Together with our team, you will help leading companies in public transport to digitize, automate and make their infrastructure more sustainable.

This is the job - your main tasks

  • As an experienced project manager, you take on end-to-end responsibility from the first idea to successful implementation.
  • In challenging mandates, you will lead interdisciplinary teams, coordinate internal and external stakeholders and advise our Eraneos Specialist Training Group.
  • With your ICT expertise, you develop studies, strategies and concepts. You advise customers on complex technical and commercial issues. You think holistically, recognize potential for optimization and implement it pragmatically, from the development of technical solutions to the support of procurement and implementations.
  • In short, you ensure that digital infrastructure / mobile communication not only works, but becomes a real enabler for modern, connected and sustainable mobility.

This job is for you if

  • You have a technical degree (FH/University/ETH/EPFL) in engineering, information technology or similar.
  • You mind. brings five years of experience in telecommunications/ICT, ideally in digital consulting, at a service provider or directly at a company in the industry (telecommunications or public transport).
  • You have expertise in the field of mobile communication, especially in the topics 5G / FRMCS (replacement of GSM-R).
  • You have knowledge of data network technologies (IP communication) and understand what is important in their planning and operation or want to familiarize yourself with these topics with enthusiasm.
  • Convince with a collaborative, proactive way of working and have strong communication and leadership skills.
  • You speak and write fluent German and English
  • French is a plus.

As an i-top you bring with you

  • Enjoy designing business development and attracting new customers or projects.
  • Already have a strong personal network in the mentioned industries and it is easy for you to build and maintain long-term customer relationships.
  • Want to participate internationally as well; for mandates in the DACH region.
