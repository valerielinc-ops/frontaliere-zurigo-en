Full description

  • The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich treats and cares for mentally ill people of all ages. The more than 2,500 employees of the 55 different professions make their contribution to the holistic care of patients in interdisciplinary teams, from clarification and therapy to follow-up care. Our work is based on mutual trust and personal responsibility and together we promote an innovative corporate culture. As a university clinic, we are successfully involved in national and international research projects. The Clinic for Forensic Psychiatry is a treatment and assessment center with university connection. At the Center for Stationary Forensic Therapy at the Rheinau site, offenders whose crime is due to a psychiatric illness are treated as part of a court-ordered measure. The treatment approach is based on the principle of recovery and aims at freedom from crime with corresponding resocialization. Our forensic-psychiatric security stations are a unique special offer throughout Switzerland. For the Center for Inpatient Forensic Therapy at the Rheinau site, we are looking for a psychologist / psychologist with additional therapeutic training 80% - 100% Your tasks
  • Case-leading responsible for the implementation of court-ordered, inpatient measures

  • Legal prognostic assessments (risk assessment) in cooperation with the pluridisciplinary team
  • Psychodiagnostic examinations
  • Professional and timely reporting, documentation and correspondence
  • Conduct of group therapies
  • Completed psychology degree with psychopathology of adulthood (university or technical college, master's degree or equivalent)
  • At least 1 year of professional experience from master’s degree
  • Clinical forensic experience desired
  • Ongoing or completed psychotherapeutic additional training, preferably with a cognitive-behavioral focus
  • Team-oriented and interprofessional action as well as good communication skills
  • Analytical thinking, empathy and assertiveness
  • Interest in forensic issues and modern forensic treatment principles
  • An interesting task with a wide range of design possibilities in the interface between justice and psychiatry / psychology
  • Contemporary training opportunities with good university networking
  • Regular supervision and intervision
  • Possibility of further qualification
  • An appreciative working atmosphere
  • Completion of the clinical years (SIWF category C) for admission to work at the expense of the OKP
