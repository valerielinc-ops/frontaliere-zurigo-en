Full description

Very diverse Sometimes quite challenging, but never boring: At EPI WohnWerk, no day is like the other. Because the people we accompany each and everyone have their unique life story. Thanks to the cooperation in the interprofessional team, we are well positioned for our working day. Do you also want to be part of this team?

The Multifaceted Day

  • You are the professional contact person for agogic issues in the area of living and support teams and leaders in a practical way in day-to-day care and team life.
  • You will accompany case discussions, agogic processes and professional reflections and support the teams in complex situations
  • You will develop agogic specialist principles, standards and instruments and support their implementation and further development in everyday living
  • You design practical specialist inputs and further training for employees and contribute to educational offers for clients
  • You promote professional exchange and knowledge transfer and work closely with the agogics experts: women from the fields of work, supported communication and prevention as well as other specialist areas

Their diverse talents

  • You have completed training at tertiary level in the social, healing or special education sector or a related sector with a comparable qualification
  • You bring in-depth agogic expertise and experience in working with people in need of support as well as in specialist advice or practical support
  • You are an empathetic, communication-strong personality with professional serenity that provides peace and orientation even in complex situations.
  • You meet teams and clients at eye level, think in a networked way and look for pragmatic, solution-oriented ways
  • Enjoyment in specialist development, knowledge transfer and cooperation with different professional groups round off your profile
  • A varied field of activity between specialist advice, practice support, education and specialist development
  • A versatile technical function with creative leeway and direct influence on the agogic quality in the EPI WohnWerk
  • A motivated, interdisciplinary team and a lively environment that you can help shape with your own ideas
  • Extensive internal training offer and support for external training
  • You do not meet all criteria 100%, but burn for professional support and agogics? Apply anyway – your attitude and your motivation count for us
