  • Independent physiotherapeutic examination and evidence-based treatment in the domicile
  • Professional, seamless progress documentation
  • Clear, empathetic communication with patients and their relatives
  • Interdisciplinary exchange with medical referees and specialist centres as required
  • Active thinking in the further development and optimization of our mobile processes

No minus hours for outages

  • Full discharge at load. Patient acquisition, scheduling and demand are our responsibility – not yours.
  • No hourly account risk. If free times arise, because there are too few patients or appointments are short-term, you do not experience minus hours.
  • Focus on the essentials: While other domicile providers often pass on load gaps to the hourly account, we deliberately work differently. You focus fully on first-class physiotherapy – we build organization and demand for it.

You bring that with you

  • Completed training as a physiotherapist/FH/HF or an equivalent recognised training (foreign diplomas with SRC recognition or in the ongoing recognition procedure)
  • Professionally clean, structured and self-responsible working method
  • Enjoying the individual care of people in the usual home environment
  • Reliability, communication strength and commitment
  • Willingness to be mobile active in the Zurich City and Agglomeration region
  • Desire and initiative to actively shape the development of our young company right from the start
