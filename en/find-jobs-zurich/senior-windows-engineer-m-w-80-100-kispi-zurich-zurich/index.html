Children's Hospital Zurich Eleonorenstiftung | online since : 28.05.2024

Focus Active Directory / AAD / M365

Your tasks

  • You are responsible for the operation, monitoring and further development of our Windows Server environment.
  • Event, troubleshooting and problem management is one of your tasks.
  • Planning and performing maintenance work does not bother you.
  • You are responsible for the configuration, technical and administrative documentation of new systems.
  • Operating a secure infrastructure is important to you (e.g. SSL certificates, vulnerability management, PKI operation).
  • You maintain close cooperation with our internal customers.
  • You work in projects and also take over partial project management (e.g. migration of the data center into the new Lengg building together with our service providers, M365 Migration).
  • Support of the 1st level support for complex customer problems as well as the 3rd level partners in problem analysis and problem solving are ensured by you.
  • Pictetdienst (every 2 months).
  • The punctual support during apprenticeship rounds off your task area.

Your profile

  • Training as a computer scientist with a focus on systems engineering or equivalent knowledge preferably with an RF degree
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in ICT infrastructure of a larger company
  • Sound Microsoft knowledge (Windows Server OS, SCCM, MS-SQL, M365, Azure, etc.)
  • Professional experience in a hybrid environment (on-prem and cloud)
  • VMWare, Citrix, Backup and Storage Knowledge Are Advantageous
  • Good knowledge of script languages (e.g. Power Shell, Ansible, VB)
  • Knowledge of network technology (TCP/IP, switching / routing); Troubleshooting
  • Willingness to deal with hospital-specific applications
  • Look for customer benefit
  • Structured and reliable working method
  • Communicative team player with creative will
  • Language skills: German (C1), English (B1+)
