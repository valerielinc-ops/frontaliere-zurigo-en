Full description

EXPERIENCE. KNOWLEDGE. FEDERATION.

Working at Henauer Gugler means developing interesting and complex construction projects – in small, highly qualified teams at attractive and modern workplaces in the heart of Zurich. We are a renowned, powerful and successful engineering and planning office with over 100 years of history. With experience, knowledge and passion we shape the world of tomorrow.

Since 2015 we belong to the CSD Ingenieure Group. CSD is a dynamic, ever-growing and multidisciplinary engineering company. Today, it has around 1000 employees from over 80 specialist areas at 30 locations in Switzerland and Europe. The trademark of CSD: Optimal solutions for satisfied customers with a “plus” for quality of life and the environment.

Do you enjoy working on challenging projects in an inspiring and agile environment and attach great importance to quality and efficiency? Then you will be part of our success – we look forward to your application! At our location / To support our team in Zurich we are looking for interns: civil engineering or timber construction (internship period 3-12 months)

Your responsibilities

  • Collaboration in varied projects in the fields of building construction, timber construction or building conservation with the support of experienced colleagues
  • Performance of static calculations
  • Accompanying construction work (construction inspections)

Your profile

  • Studies in Civil Engineering or Wood Engineering (ETH/FH/UNI)
  • Interested in getting to know the craft of structural design in exciting and challenging projects
  • Communicative, team capable personality

Are you interested? Do you want to know what we are doing? Our projects speak for themselves!
