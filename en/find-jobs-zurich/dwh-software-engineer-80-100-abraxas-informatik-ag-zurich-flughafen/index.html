Full description

We are a motivated and dynamic team and are looking for you to support the further development of our proven and new tax products. We need strong support for this. Your main task is the further development and maintenance of our DWH solutions in the tax environment. Your role

  • Design and implement data models and ETL processes for our data warehouse solution under Pentaho
  • Design and implement reports
  • Further development of the technical data warehouse environment
  • Ensuring data quality (e.g. when interface changes to source systems)
  • Analysis and correction of production errors
  • Testing the functionality
  • Planning activities in the team and with other teams (Scrum, SAFe methodology)

Your profile

  • Completed studies or comparable training in computer science
  • Sound experience in the development of ETL processes
  • Practical experience working with Oracle and SQL
  • Interested in developing self-service reporting solutions
  • Knowledge of data modeling (data vault), data management and DWH architectures
  • Accounting skills are a plus
  • Experience with applications and processes in the control area (reference, assessment, register) is a big plus
  • Experience with continuous integration and test-driven development
  • Experience with relevant technologies such as GIT, Docker, Kubernetes and in the integration of software
  • High initiative and willingness to permanently develop yourself and the team technically and methodically
  • Very good German and English skills
  • Fun and joy to work in an agile team

Your Benefits

  • Up to 60% home office possible with a 100% workload
  • Holiday purchase (2 weeks per year)
  • 41h week with flexible working hours on a full-time workload
  • Mind. 25 days of holidays per year
  • Support for 100% financial or temporal training
  • Free SBB half-ax subscription for all employees
