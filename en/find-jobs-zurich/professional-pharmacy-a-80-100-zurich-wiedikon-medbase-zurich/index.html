Welcome to the Medbase Pharmacy Zurich Enge

Our modern, well-frequented pharmacy is located directly at Zurich Enge train station. The Medbase Group is one of the leading companies in ambulatory healthcare. This means services ranging from health promotion to medication and acute treatment to rehabilitation. Health professionals from different disciplines work hand in hand to provide people across Switzerland with integrated and world-class medical care. With logistics services and digital solutions, we support doctors so that they too can fully concentrate on patients.

The Medbase Group includes the brands Medbase Pharmacies, Zur Rose, zahnarztzentrum.ch, WePractice and Bluecare.
