Full description

With us, you will play a technically demanding key role in the sustainable development of Zurich’s infrastructure. You ensure that rainwater is managed sustainably, sewers are planned in advance and urban construction projects are optimally accompanied, i.e. for a long-term value preservation of the drainage network. We are now looking for reinforcements at the Werdhölzli site in Zurich Altstetten.

Ready for a job with meaning?

Tasks

  • You lead challenging canal construction projects in the drainage business area and actively shape the future-oriented rainwater management of the city of Zurich.
  • You will assess canal construction projects, analyze hydraulic relationships and conduct technical negotiations with internal bodies and external partners.
  • You will develop concepts, strategies and decision bases for hydraulic, structural and planning issues using modern analysis and simulation tools.
  • You make an important contribution to the further development of the General Drainage Plan (GEP), supervise hydrodynamic models and coordinate the services of external specialist planners.
  • You work closely with the civil engineering office, other municipal service departments and internal working groups on a project-related and interdisciplinary level.

Profile

  • Civil, cultural or environmental engineer (FH / Uni / ETH)
  • In-depth knowledge of hydraulics, municipal drainage and GEP processing as well as deep experience with hydrodynamic simulation programs and knowledge of databases, ideally scripting languages (e.g. Python)
  • Several years of professional experience in the planning, project planning and realization of municipal civil engineering and/or in urban drainage
  • Confident German in Word and Writing (level C1)
  • Independent and structured working methods, pronounced stakeholder management and enjoyment of technically complex issues

We are modern, down-to-earth, open-minded, all per you and want to make a difference. Together we are committed to a clean, innovative and sustainable Zurich.
