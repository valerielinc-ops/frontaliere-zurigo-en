Tasks

We are looking for active support in the support and further development of the offer and offer an interesting and meaningful activity in an established team and a dynamic work area of the University of Zurich.

  • Supervision and coordination of existing programs of UZH International Summer Schools (UZHISS)
  • Participation in the further development of UZHISS
  • Implementation of the recruitment process as well as communication with international applicants, partner universities and UZH students
  • Marketing and positioning of UZHISS on relevant communication and marketing platforms
  • Planning and implementation of events within the framework of the Social Program
  • Co-responsibility for the financial management of programmes
  • Cooperation in administrative tasks in the area of student mobility and the department

Note on working time

During the recruitment period for UZHISS programmes from March to June, a short response time is essential for success. We therefore expect the willingness to work more than 60% during this time when needed. During the summer schools in July and if necessary. In August, the workload is 100%. There are also fewer labor-intensive months, so that the annual working time is on average 65%.

Requirements

They work independently, structured and precise and appreciate the cooperation in the team. They have a high sense of responsibility and quality and keep an overview even in a variety of tasks and under time pressure. The content and time flexibility of this position suits you. They communicate stylishly with different stakeholders within and outside the UZH. Contact with people from different cultural contexts brings you joy. In addition, you perform safely in front of a larger target audience.

  • Completed university studies (at Bachelor's or Master's level)
  • several years of professional experience in administrative work
  • Experience in marketing is beneficial
  • Excellent knowledge of English, stylish German in word and writing
  • High flexibility
  • Willingness to work 100% during UZHISS
  • Very good MS Office user knowledge
  • Strong digital competence and rapid comprehension in the application of new technologies
  • Knowledge of the university environment, summer schools and UZH
