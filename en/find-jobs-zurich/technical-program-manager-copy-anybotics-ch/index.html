Technical Program Manager — ANYbotics

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

ANYbotics is a fast-growing tech company dedicated to shaping the future of mobile robotics across multiple industries. Join our highly talented and motivated team of more than 200 people and work on cutting-edge robot technology.

The Opportunity

As Technical Program Manager, you will lead the delivery of one of ANYbotics’ most strategically important robot platform programs, bringing engineering, operations and commercial teams together around a clear and credible path to market.

You will carry delivery accountability against the agreed specification, schedule and product-cost targets, providing one clear and credible view of the program. Working closely with Product and Engineering, you will translate agreed product and technical decisions into an executable plan across mechanical and electrical engineering, software, industrialization, supply chain, certification, quality, service and launch readiness.

This is a highly visible, hands-on leadership role. You will combine strong technical understanding with excellent program judgement, moving comfortably between detailed engineering discussions and executive-level decisions. You will bring clarity where information is incomplete, identify emerging risks and create the momentum needed to make difficult trade-offs at the right time.

You will operate as a senior individual contributor, focusing on one major program at a time and leading through influence rather than direct authority. You will work closely with functional leaders and named workstream owners, while having the opportunity to strengthen how ANYbotics runs complex product development and help shape our broader program-management capability as the company grows.

Market & Our Technology

ANYbotics is pioneering autonomous robotic platforms for industrial inspection and operations in challenging environments. From explosion-proof systems in oil and gas to agile robotic platforms for power, utilities, mining, metals and other industrial settings, our technology is built for environments where safety, reliability and uptime are critical.

Our product combines advanced robotics, mechanical and electrical systems, embedded software, autonomy, inspection payloads, analytics and integrations into an end-to-end industrial solution. Bringing a new robot platform to market requires hardware and software development, industrialization, supply chain, certification, quality and customer readiness to progress together.

This creates a distinctive program-management challenge. Long hardware lead times must be balanced with iterative software development, ambitious product goals, demanding certification requirements and complex make-or-buy decisions. Strong technical program leadership is essential to turning this complexity into reliable execution.

About us

ANYbotics is a leading robotics company specialising in advanced autonomous systems. With a successful Series B financing round closed and strong momentum in the market, we are entering an important phase of growth and international expansion.

Our mission is to transform industrial inspection and operations through world-class robotics. We combine deep robotics expertise with real customer deployments in demanding industrial environments. This is an opportunity to join at a stage where the technology is proven, the ambition is high, and the decisions made now will have a major impact on the company’s future.
