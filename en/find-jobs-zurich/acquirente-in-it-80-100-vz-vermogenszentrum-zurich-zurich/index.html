Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We advise individuals and companies on all matters relating to money, manage assets and offer attractive financial services. We are very successful and growing strongly. To strengthen our IT team, we are looking for a solution-oriented and committed personality in Zurich.

  • You are operationally responsible for our diverse IT procurement processes: quotation requests and comparisons, orders, incoming goods inspection, invoice inspection – You are the linchpin of IT shopping!
  • You monitor and maintain IT assets: inventory, update, renewal, decommissioning, reporting – You keep track of hardware, licenses and IT contracts.
  • You support IT management in budgeting.
  • You are actively involved in IT cost management by proactively developing possible cost optimization potentials and preparing them in a structured manner.
  • You process service requests: Especially in the area of software licensing, you are the contact person and ensure that our employees are optimally supported.
  • A successfully completed commercial or computer science education with corresponding further education
  • Professional experience in the field of IT purchasing and IT technical understanding
  • Knowledge of the licensing of Microsoft products and ITIL is an advantage
  • Good communication skills, independent and structured working methods
  • Enforcement and strong negotiating skills
  • Your fluent German and good English skills round off your interesting profile
  • Possibility of co-designing, further developing, monitoring and professionalizing the purchasing processes as well as transferring the existing inventory into a new IT Service Management Platform (ServiceNow).
  • In addition to an exciting and challenging job in an interdisciplinary environment, you have the chance to shape our cloud journey through your initiative and commitment.
  • You are also at the right place for your further education: We support you individually and support you with an external further education within the framework of the job profile.
  • With us, you can freely choose your time so that you have more time for further education, family, sports and projects that are important to you. December 24 and 31 are paid holidays for all employees.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
