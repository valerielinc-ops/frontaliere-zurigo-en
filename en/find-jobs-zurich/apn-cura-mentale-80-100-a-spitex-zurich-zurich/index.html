Full description

Your heart beats for care? Ours too. We work in specialized teams: The Memory Care Team focuses on accompanying people with dementia and their relatives. Through the specialization you benefit from a professional environment with specific knowledge, exchange of experience and targeted support in this demanding field. In the Somatic Care Team, you support customers with a wide range of somatic diseases and benefit from a broad range of nursing care. So you can use your knowledge in many ways and experience varied situations every day.

(a)lle are addressed. Gender, origin, age? We are interested in your qualification and enthusiasm for work.

YOUR TASKS

  • You accompany our customers in their familiar home and support them in making their everyday life as self-determined as possible.
  • You take on nursing and supervising tasks and build trusting relationships with customers and relatives.
  • You work independently and at the same time you are part of a team that supports each other and exchanges expertise.
  • You coordinate closely with nurses as well as other internal and external specialist positions and contribute to a holistic care.
  • You document your assignments digitally and ensure a professional flow of information.
