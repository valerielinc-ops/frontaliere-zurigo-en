To strengthen our team, we are looking for several customer service representatives with many years of experience in the field of construction and real estate financing. On the one hand, this includes interested parties with an existing strong network in this customer segment in the regions of Northwest Switzerland, East/Southeast Switzerland or Bern/Espace.
