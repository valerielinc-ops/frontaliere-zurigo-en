Full description

Shape the digital future of Migros Zurich with us. You can support modern IoT and IT solutions in our stores, ensure stable operations and support roll-outs on site. Together with specialist departments, key users, internal IT teams and external partners, you take responsibility and successfully bring technical solutions into the everyday life of the branches.

Mansioni

  • You ensure the stable operation of our applications and support with faults, analyses and technical issues
  • You maintain, test and optimize data as an important basis for our IoT and digitalization projects
  • You collect data and key figures and thus support project and operational decisions
  • You support the further development of applications, interfaces and processes
  • You will accompany roll-outs in our stores, from technical preparation to hardware coordination to implementation on site
  • You will be involved in piloting, testing, training and technical enabling of key users
  • You can gradually take responsibility for subprojects, features or major change requests, including requirements collection, coordination and testing. Perspectively, you will continue to develop towards IT Service Management
  • You document technical solutions, processes and insights comprehensibly and comprehensibly

Requirements

  • Ideally, first professional experience: in computer science, application management or project environment.
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): in computer science or you have an ongoing/completed degree in computer science, business informatics or a comparable qualification.
  • Analytical thinking, quick comprehension and an independent, solution-oriented working method
  • Enjoying data, applications, technical connections and digital development
  • Readiness for on-site operations in the Zurich area and a category B driving licence
  • Motivation to take on responsibility and develop professionally
  • in German (negotiation certain)
