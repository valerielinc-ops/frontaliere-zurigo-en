Critical requirements

  • Swiss B or C permit or EU citizenship

Purchasing, Receiving & Warehouse Associate (m/f/d) Location: Zurich Workload: 100% Start Date: To be agreed upon

Your Responsibilities

  • Receiving, inspecting, and logging incoming shipments (food, beverages, non-food items)
  • Properly storing goods in the designated basement and storage areas
  • Ensuring correct storage in compliance with hygiene and food safety regulations (FIFO / HACCP)
  • Maintaining organization and order in the storage and basement areas
  • Assisting with inventory counts and stock checks
  • Working closely with the kitchen, service staff, and other departments in the hotel

Your Profile

  • Experience in warehousing, logistics, or the food service industry is a plus
  • Proficiency with Microsoft Office and a willingness to learn purchasing systems
  • Understanding of food hygiene (HACCP knowledge is a plus)
  • Physically fit and reliable
  • Ability to work independently and in a structured manner
  • Good command of German and English
  • Flexibility regarding work hours (including weekends)

  • A friendly work environment and a motivated team
