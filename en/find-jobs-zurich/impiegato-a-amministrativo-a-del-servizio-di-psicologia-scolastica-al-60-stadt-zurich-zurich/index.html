Full description

The School Psychology Service is a counselling service for students, their parents and teachers as well as for school management and school authorities.

As of 01. December 2026 or by agreement, we are looking for a committed and motivated personality for a limited period of one year.

Tasks

  • Correspondence, file management and appointment arrangements for school psychologists
  • Contact with parents, students, teachers and authorities
  • Reception, provision of information and appointment coordination
  • General secretarial tasks

Profile

  • Commercial education
  • Empathy and skill in dealing with children, adolescents and parents of different linguistic and cultural origins
  • Resilience, reliability and teamwork
  • Good PC knowledge (MS-Office) and willingness to familiarize yourself with new programs
  • Independent work, flexibility and organizational talent
  • Stylistic German, knowledge of foreign languages is an advantage

We offer

A versatile and challenging task as an employee in an established team. The employment takes place according to municipal personnel law, it is limited to one year.

About us

The School Health Services are a service department of the City of Zurich School and Sports Department. The medical, psychological and educational specialists form a competence center for prevention and health promotion, advice and support as well as information and public relations work for the benefit of the children and adolescents of the city of Zurich.
