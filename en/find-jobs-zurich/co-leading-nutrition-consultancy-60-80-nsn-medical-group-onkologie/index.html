Full description

The Center for Integrative Oncology

We accompany people with tumor and blood diseases holistically – medically, therapeutically and humanly. Our approach combines modern conventional medicine with complementary therapies and consistently focuses on people. At our locations in Zurich, Winterthur, Uster, Richterswil, Glarus and Oerlikon, doctors, nurses, therapists and administrative staff work closely together – interdisciplinary, respectful and on an equal footing. We create an environment in which quality, time and humanity are not just words, but are lived daily. Do you want a task in which you can contribute your organizational talent and at the same time accompany people in a demanding life situation? Then you are exactly right with us. To strengthen our versatile team in the ZIO Zurich and Lake Zurich in Richterswil, we are looking for an

Co-leading nutrition consultancy 60-80 %

Your daily work

  • You accompany our patients with an individual nutrition therapy, mainly at the Richterswil and Zurich locations
  • You advise our patients in outpatient nutrition advice in various specialist areas competently and practically
  • You design and participate with pleasure in group courses, lectures and workshops and actively contribute your expertise to our interdisciplinary team
  • You take over the co-management for a small team of 3-4 employees with a flat hierarchy and comprehensive training

You bring that with you

  • You have a basic insurance recognised training in nutrition and dietetics (Diplom HF, BSc or MSc)
  • You bring at least 2 years of professional experience in clinical and outpatient nutrition counselling
  • You have knowledge in nutritional therapy of oncological patients, ideally also in enteral and parenteral nutrition at home
  • You are interested in naturopathic topics and integrative therapy approaches
  • You work independently, reliably and flexibly and are ready to work at different locations (mainly Zurich and Richterswil, occasionally Glarus)
  • You enjoy counselling and meet our patients with empathy and commitment
  • You already have leadership experience or wish to take on more responsibility in the future

This is what we offer you

  • A varied and responsible activity in an innovative, holistic medical environment
  • An appreciative and collegial working environment with space for our own ideas and the co-creation of our nutritional therapy
  • Personal and long-term patient support in close interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Regular working hours (excluding shift and weekend work) and 5 weeks of holidays
  • Attractive training opportunities as well as participation in an interdisciplinary team that combines modern conventional medicine with complementary therapies and provides holistically accompanying people
