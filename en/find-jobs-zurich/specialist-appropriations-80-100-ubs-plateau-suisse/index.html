Full description

Key responsibilities Are you passionate about bank loans? You are autonomous, reliable and have a high professional conscience? Do you enjoy working in a dynamic and challenging environment where goals motivate you? If you answered yes to these questions, then join our Credit Services team and be part of a company where your talent will be recognized and valued.

As a credit specialist, you will be responsible for

  • process nationally approved loan applications, playing a key role in the delivery of our clients' projects
  • prepare credit agreements with appropriate security documents, thus ensuring the documentation necessary for our loans
  • capture and manage credits and guarantees (e.g., mortgages, policies and sureties)
  • issue promises of payment and their execution
  • ensure correspondence and dialogue with land registers, notaries, banks and insurance companies, establishing trust relations with our partners
  • maintain professional contacts with clients, Credit Officers and legal services

Homework is enhanced through our commitment to digitizing our processes. Teamwork and commitment are essential to us, and we look forward to seeing how you can contribute to our collective success.

Your skills and experience

  • full commercial training
  • experience in mortgage processing, banking training or knowledge in notarial law and land registry are assets
  • a committed and resilient personality, always ready to meet challenges
  • a capacity for rapid understanding and great autonomy
  • an independent and rigorous working method
  • knowledge in German is a plus

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
