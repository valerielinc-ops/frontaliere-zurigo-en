The Limmattal Hospital thrives on interdisciplinary cooperation. The team members of the medical administration support the Ärzteschaft in all administrative matters and work closely together with the care and all other areas. The entire medical administration is under an overall leadership, which further strengthens and simplifies interdisciplinary cooperation. In addition: you are exactly right in the LIMMI if a family atmosphere and a modern workplace are important to you.

Here we treat our patients within the entire spectrum of internal medicine, including neurological, angiological and rheumatological procedures. Our cadre consists of over 30 medical specialists representing most internist subspecialties. In the fields of gastrointestinal, vascular and tumor diseases, we work in a highly interdisciplinary manner.

Direct applications are preferred, external recruitment offices are not treated as a priority.

Here they engage

  • in the performance of lung function examinations and blood sampling and allergy testing
  • assisting with bronchoscopies and punctures
  • in patient care before, during and after examinations
  • in the instruction of ambulatory and inpatient patients
  • In materials management and performance measurement
  • Workload during the training period likes to be higher (50-60%)
  • Fixed working days Monday and Wednesday
  • Willingness to take over holiday and illness representatives
  • Have a degree as dipl. Nursing professional HF/FH or medical practice assistant
  • ideally already have experience in the field of pneumology and endoscopy
  • Enjoy technical investigations
  • have organisational skills
  • implement a reliable, careful and professional way of working
  • Reduced employee parking
  • Motorcycle parking, lockable bicycle parking
  • With the ZVV Bonus Pass / Z-Bonus Pass we offer an attractive ÖV subscription for employees
