Full description

Introduction

Together with a highly motivated team, you develop a hybrid signal ASIC that forms the heart of every Belimo drive and sensor and is produced millions of times. You take over the technical responsibility for the analog part, which is developed externally by our ASIC supplier.

Main responsibilities

  • Specification and verification of the analogue part of our ASICs
  • Close cooperation with our ASIC supplier and joint solving of technical challenges in the development of the analogue part
  • Technical interface to the internal team for the digital part as well as the system verification team that checks the ASICs within Belimo devices
  • Development and maintenance of PCBs for the development and qualification of ASICs
  • Analog circuit design of new functions

Skills required

  • Master's degree in electrical engineering, ideally with focus on microelectronics or analog electronics
  • Several years of experience in analog circuit design and measurement technology, including the engineering and verification methods of the company.
  • Experience in the field of microelectronics is a big plus
  • Interest in digital circuit design (Verilog or VHDL)
  • Enthusiasm for new technologies and innovative ideas, high self-initiative, strong quality awareness and independent working methods
  • Open, communicative personality with negotiation-proof German and English skills

About Belimo

Belimo is the world leader in the development, manufacture and distribution of field equipment for energy-efficient control of heating, ventilation and air conditioning systems. Valve drives, control valves, sensors and counters form our core business. Founded in 1975, Belimo has been listed on the Swiss Stock Exchange (SIX) since 1995.
