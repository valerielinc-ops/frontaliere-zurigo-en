Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With over 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric services offered by Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in outpatient, day clinic and inpatient settings. The group employs around 1800 people and generated sales of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For our Privé Station D1 for older adults, we are looking for a

Our range of therapies is aimed at older adults aged 65 and over in life crises with depression, anxiety disorders, neurodegenerative diseases from mild to moderate dementia as well as somatic concomitant and underlying diseases. After careful diagnostic clarification, we offer therapeutic services ranging from low-threshold support to psychiatric/psychotherapeutic settings. In addition to occupational therapy and physiotherapy, the range of therapies also includes complementary medical services and psychosocial tasks. Our patients are cared for in close cooperation with the case therapist in the caregiver system. The atmosphere on the ward is characterized by a motivated, humorous interprofessional team with a respectful attitude and the careful cultivation of interpersonal relationships.

Your tasks As a health specialist, you are jointly responsible for the care and support of patients on the ward. You work closely with the reference nurses and take on all work tasks in your area of competence responsibly.

Your profile For this exciting work in an interprofessional team, you have a high level of professional and social competence. Are you a person who enjoys both psychiatric and somatic care? Do you like to actively participate in a team and work independently in your area of expertise? Then we look forward to receiving your complete and creative application.
