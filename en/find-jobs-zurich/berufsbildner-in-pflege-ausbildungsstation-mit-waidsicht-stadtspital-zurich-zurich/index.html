Full description

As part of a pilot project, the Zurich City Hospital has opened the first training station in the Canton of Zurich, which is run by trainees 365 days a year. Young talents receive practical training in which they can assume responsibility with new forms of learning, develop further and work independently under the expert support of the training staff.

We are looking for 1. May 2026 or by agreement at the Waid location a dedicated vocational trainer care for our innovative pilot project. Are you the motivated personality who supports our specialists of tomorrow with enthusiasm for care and training and a strong coaching approach?

Tasks

  • You accompany and coach learners and students in all nursing professions in an innovative learning environment that focuses on self-employment and is based on modern learning methods.
  • You promote the development of trainees through targeted coaching, offer support in the reflection of their learning processes and accompany them in independent decision-making in everyday care.
  • Together with the training team, you will develop new teaching and learning scenarios that combine theory and practice and promote the joy of learning.
  • You are involved in further developing the training concept and taking nursing education to the next level with new approaches. You play a key role in the leadership of trainees and work closely with the leadership, project management and training team.
  • You ensure patient-oriented care and corresponding quality of care.

Profile

  • Training as a Dipl. Nursing professional / Dipl. Nursing professional HF/FH
  • Several years of professional experience in the acute care sector with experience in the training of nursing professions
  • Professional educational qualification equivalent to at least SVEB 1
  • Enthusiasm for new learning methods and coaching
  • Enjoying the promotion of talent in a dynamic, interdisciplinary environment
  • High level of technical, social and organisational competence, ability to reflect and a good feeling for the individual needs of the trainees
  • High motivation, dedication and creative pleasure as well as the willingness to take on an exemplary function
  • Willingness to work primarily in early and late services

We offer

We offer you more than just a job! We live an open du-culture, which is characterized by an uncomplicated and personal cooperation at eye level, respect and appreciation. As part of our diverse team of over 120 professional groups, you will actively shape a meaningful environment. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions and excellent social benefits from the city of Zurich.

About us

The Stadtspital Zürich is one of the top 10 Swiss hospitals with over 36'000 inpatient and around 455,000 outpatients per year. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,500 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
