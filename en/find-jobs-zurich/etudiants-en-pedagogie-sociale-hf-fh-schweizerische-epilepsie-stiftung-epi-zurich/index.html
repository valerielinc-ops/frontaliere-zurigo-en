Full description

Tasks

  • They actively accompany the residents in their living, everyday and leisure activities
  • They support individual, targeted and resource-oriented care and support and participate in their implementation
  • You are involved in the planning, implementation and evaluation of the agogic development process and are actively involved
  • They maintain contact with relatives and external reference persons
  • They exchange ideas interdisciplinary with the team as well as internal specialist and interfaces

Requirements

  • You have a recognised vocational qualification EFZ (at least three years old) or a secondary education qualification
  • You have an internship or professional experience in the socio-educational field
  • Ideally, you have already passed the admission procedure
  • You are a team-oriented personality with high social skills
  • You reflect on your actions and, together with people with impairments and disabilities, shape their living environment with commitment

We offer

  • Wages according to cantonal guidelines
  • 5 weeks holiday, from the 50th. Age 6 weeks
  • Own pension fund with an above-average employer share
  • Very good internal training offers
  • Discounts in our pharmacy
  • Employee discount in our public restaurant

Sometimes quite challenging, but never boring: At EPI WohnWerk, no day is like the other. Because the people we accompany each and everyone have their unique life story. Thanks to the cooperation in the interprofessional team, we are well positioned for our working day. Do you also want to be part of this team?
