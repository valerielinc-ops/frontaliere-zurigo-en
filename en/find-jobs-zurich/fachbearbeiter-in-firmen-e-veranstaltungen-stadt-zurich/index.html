Full description

For the Companies and Events department within the Implementation division, we are looking for a new team member starting in early October 2026 or by agreement.

Responsibilities

  • For the «Work and School» pilot program, you recruit for students in the 2. and 3. Secondary level work placements at training companies in the private sector and at institutions.
  • You organize information events on career choices and upper secondary schools, involving external partners in the process.
  • You coordinate organizational processes related to events and work assignments and ensure smooth execution.
  • You maintain contact and good cooperation with internal stakeholders and external partners.
  • You are familiar with the education landscape and are able to communicate, both in writing and orally, with a wide variety of contact persons in a manner appropriate to the target group.

Profile

  • A completed vocational apprenticeship and at least one continuing-education qualification from a professional examination in sales, HR, communications or business administration are an advantage
  • Sales and organizational talent – tenacity and perseverance in acquisition
  • Organizational talent
  • Quick comprehension, interconnected and logical thinking, good presentation skills, and an independent and conscientious approach to work
  • Affinity for IT-related topics

What we offer

A challenging, varied and meaningful role that you can help shape in an exciting, committed and motivated working environment. Our employees benefit from attractive employment conditions and a centrally located workplace in the heart of Zurich.

About us

With around 2400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich is committed every day to a socially inclusive Zurich. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in various situations in life. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we help ensure a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.

The Career Center of the City of Zurich (LBZ) is a department of the Social Department and, with around 130 employees, is the largest competence center for vocational and career matters in Switzerland. We provide information and advice on career choice, education and continuing education, as well as professional reorientation. Where necessary, we support implementation and enable the financing of education and continuing education through scholarships.
