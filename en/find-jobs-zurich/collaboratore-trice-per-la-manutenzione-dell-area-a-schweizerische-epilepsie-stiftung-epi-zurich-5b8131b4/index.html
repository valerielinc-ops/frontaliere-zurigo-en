Full description

The Multifaceted Day

  • They operate and operate machines and tractors for the care and maintenance of the area.
  • They participate in the care and maintenance of the surrounding gardening and agricultural areas and carry out general cleaning work on the site.
  • They undertake horticultural maintenance and cleaning work on all flat roofs incl. Solar installations in compliance with the necessary safety regulations.
  • They ensure clean and safe transport routes on the site.
  • They take over the snow clearance and slippery ice control on the entire area and provide regular piketting service.
  • They support waste management and contribute to proper disposal.
  • They assist with events and transports, carry out minor repairs on fences, tools and machines and take over the removal of graffiti.
  • They provide varied weekend services in the areas of area care, gardening and animal husbandry.

Their diverse talents

  • You have completed training as a gardener, farmer or have practical experience as a semi-skilled worker at EBA level.
  • You have the further education in the field of fall protection (PSAgA) or are ready to complete it.
  • They have at least five years of professional experience, including at least two years in the relevant field.
  • They have good knowledge of German in words and writing as well as good PC skills.
  • They have a category B driving licence. Experience in handling machinery and tractors and a trailer driving licence are beneficial.
  • They work independently, reliably and responsibly and appreciate the versatile tasks in site care.
  • They are flexible and willing to regularly take on weekend services and piketting services in the winter service.
  • Flexible working hours in 50%, for an optimal balance between work and private life.
  • A varied and versatile activity in the area care.
  • Opportunities to expand your professional skills through further education and professional development

EPI Foundation

The Swiss Epilepsy Foundation (EPI) is a multi-sector company. It includes the EPI WohnWerk, the Lengg High School, the donation Dapples, a seminar center with restaurant and the EPI Central Services. In addition, the Swiss Epilepsy Foundation together with another foundation forms the sponsorship of Klinik Lengg AG, each with a clinic for epileptology and neurorehabilitation. Around 1000 employees are committed to these social tasks. The area is located on the outskirts of Zurich, with a fantastic view of the nearby Lake Zurich and the Alps.
