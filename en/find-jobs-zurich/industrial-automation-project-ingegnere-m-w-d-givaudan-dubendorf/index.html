For our production site in Dübendorf we are looking for an engaged person as Industrial Automation Project Engineer(m/f/d).

In this exciting position, you support the existing engineering team. You will gain your sound expertise in an ongoing major project in the areas of automation technology, electrical engineering, process safety and IT/OT in general directly on site and assume the responsibility of a central part of the project starting from the Detail Design phase.

You work in a production-oriented environment at the Dübendorf site and you are directly rapporting to the site En,gi,nee,ring Manager.

Main tasks & Project Responsibility Automation

MSRL & Automation Engineering: Creation, revision and extension of MSRL lists, function descriptions and source & effect diagrams

Process reliability; execution and post-processing of process risk analyses as well as integration of safety integrity levels (SIL) and performance levels (PL).

Process technology: Dealing with regulatory issues during the selection and commissioning of a wide range of instruments and fittings.

Understanding and extending process flow images / R&Is.

Plant construction: coordination of external trades and technical challenges of solutions in the field of automation and electrical engineering proposed by the technical planners.

Installation & commissioning: Planning and guiding of the activities on site, coordination of the assembly and monitoring/co-ordination/implementation of the commissioning of the process plants.

Electrical

Coordination and support of internal and external trades

Read and interpret electro-schema

Instrumentation & Field Engineering: Asking about the instruments selected by the specialist planner and working out the most optimal solution for each application

IT/OT

Ensuring compliance with global Givaudan IT/OT standards in the ongoing project

Creation and execution of an automation concept taking into account all internal and legal requirements and including all stakeholders' requirements.

Monitoring the commissioning of measuring and control instruments in the process environment.

Your professional profile

Graduated studies (Uni/FH) Subject of automation technology, electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, mechatronics or comparable. Knowledge of process technology or industrial process systems is of benefit.

Experience: At least 5 years of experience as an automation project engineer in industrial plant engineering / project engineer / field engineer (prefers food, chemical or pharmaceutical industry).

Automation & Security: Detection of practice in the creation/extension of MSRL lists, instrument selection and knowledge in the handling of process security, technical data (SIL, performance level).

Software: Ideally experience in handling the Siemens TIA Portal, WinCC, Simatic S7. Good knowledge of Autodesk AutoCAD (2D & P&ID) is an advantage. Secure knowledge MS Office.

Working method: Self-employed, structured and goal-oriented working methods in the execution of parallel project phases.

Languages: German and English in word and writing.

Process technology: Ideally already experience in spray drying.
