Full description

We are committed to health – because health is everything. We are committed to this, also as an employer. We are convinced: Only in a healthy working world do the best talents feel comfortable and move forward: professionally and personally. For that we give everything. This is how you shape health

  • Independent handling of service cases in the area of compulsory accident insurance according to UVG and supplementary insurance according to VVG from A to Z from a single source
  • Corporate client contact in a regional jurisdiction
  • Independent examination of entitlement to benefits and calculation and orientation of daily allowances and healing costs
  • Cooperation with internal network partners (including Care Manager, Legal and Recourse Service)
  • Identification and active initiation of appropriate measures (e.g. assessment by medical network, cooperation with disability insurance)
  • Writing various written correspondence

This sets you apart

  • Basic commercial education and preferably medical knowledge
  • Some years of professional experience in personal insurance (UVG)
  • Flexible, resilient, communicative and customer-oriented personality
  • Very good oral and written expression in German as well as well versed in dealing with the MS-Office range and preferably also in the Syrius
  • Connected thinking, commitment and humor

Why SWICA

  • We build on humanity. That is why we focus on openness, respect and trust in our everyday work. In doing so, we take on responsibility and think in terms of solutions – together, for our customers. This is also why we have been number 1 in customer satisfaction for many years.
  • We trust you and give you space. With annual working hours, at least 5 weeks holidays and on request also holiday purchase. With part-time models and a generous home office within the scope of the possibilities of the job, you make your everyday life largely flexible.
  • We value and reward your work – with a market-based wage, up to 1,500 francs annual fee for personal wishes and a generous participation in the basic and additional insurance for you and your family.
  • We offer you prospects. With a generous training regulations, we support your professional and personal development – for your role today and for your goals of tomorrow.
  • Because health is everything. With our company health management and region-specific offers, we take care of your well-being. And if it comes otherwise: 100% net wage continued payment.
  • We think long term. With three savings plans, pensum-dependent coordination deduction and benefits well above the legal minimum, you ensure good for your future.
