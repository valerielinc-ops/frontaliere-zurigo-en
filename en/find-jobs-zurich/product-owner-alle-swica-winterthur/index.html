Full description

You are passionate about digitalization, think processes end-to-end and want to actively shape the future of our sales and consulting systems? Then you are exactly right with us. In this role, you will be responsible for business-critical applications along the customer journey, drive the automation and optimization of processes and work closely with specialist departments, IT and external partners. With your know-how, you create sustainable added value for employees, customers and our company. This is how you shape health

  • You are responsible for the professional development of sales systems and digital solutions
  • You analyze existing processes and identify automation and optimization potentials
  • You lead internal market projects and work in interdisciplinary project teams
  • You formulate requirements for internal and external implementation partners and accompany their implementation
  • Coordinate releases, prioritize requirements and manage ticket management with external suppliers
  • You test new functions and ensure high quality and stability of the solutions
  • You train and empower users in dealing with the solutions used
  • Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and compliance requirements

This sets you apart

  • Completed studies or a comparable further education in Business Administration/Analysis, Business Informatics or Digital Business Management
  • You are well acquainted with the health insurance industry, ideally you worked in customer consulting yourself
  • Demonstrable experience as a product owner, business analyst or comparable role
  • You have already worked in the implementation of digitalization, automation or process projects
  • Technical understanding and strong ability to analyze processes and develop future-oriented solutions
  • High initiative, ownership mentality and quality awareness
  • Stylistic German in word and writing (level native language) as well as good knowledge of English and/or French

Why SWICA

  • We build on humanity. That is why we focus on openness, respect and trust in our everyday work. We take on responsibility and think in terms of solutions – together, for our customers. This is also why we have been number 1 in customer satisfaction for many years.
  • We trust you and give you space. With annual working hours, at least 5 weeks holidays and on request also holiday purchase. With part-time models and generous home office within the scope of the possibilities of the job, you make your everyday life largely flexible.
  • We value and reward your work – with a market-based wage, up to 1,500 francs annual fee for personal wishes and a generous participation in the basic and additional insurance for you and your family.
  • We offer you prospects. With a generous training regulations, we support your professional and personal development – for your role today and for your goals of tomorrow.
  • Because health is everything. With our company health management and region-specific offers, we take care of your well-being. And if it comes otherwise: 100% net wage continued payment.
  • We think long term. With three savings plans, pensum-dependent coordination deduction and benefits well above the legal minimum, you ensure good for your future.
