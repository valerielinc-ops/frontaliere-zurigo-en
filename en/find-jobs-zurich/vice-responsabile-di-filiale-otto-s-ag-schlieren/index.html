Full description

Do you enjoy daily contact with people, like taking on responsibility and actively contributing? Then OTTO’S is exactly the right place for you. Become part of a successful Swiss company with around 2'600 employees and actively help shape our day-to-day retail operations with your commitment, ideas and personality.

Area of responsibility

  • Varied merchandise presentation: With your creativity, you create attractive, sales-promoting merchandise displays
  • Optimal merchandise planning: Thanks to your strong sense of responsibility, you ensure that products are always available for sale
  • Coordinate the team: Support with personnel management, staff scheduling and work organization
  • Efficient warehouse management: You contribute innovative approaches and thereby promote systematic processes and sustainability
  • Optimize the store appearance: With a trained eye, you ensure an appealing and sales-promoting store environment
  • Deputy store management: In the absence of the store manager, you keep the store on course and demonstrate a “hands-on mentality”

The Commission

  • Family-run, owner-managed company
  • Attractive shopping discounts online and in stores
  • 5 weeks of vacation (6 weeks from the age of 50)
  • Generous loyalty bonuses and a legendary staff party
  • Tickets for various games of FC Luzern, EV-Zug, HC Ambri-Piotta, SC Bern and Lausanne HC
  • Attractive benefits from myOTTO'S partners
  • Weekly working hours of 42 hours, 15 minutes of paid breaks in both the morning and afternoon

Profile

  • Training: Completed training in retail
  • Professional experience: First experience in a leadership position or as a deputy
  • Sales personality: Resilient, service-oriented and with an entrepreneurial flair
  • Team player: Solution-oriented, reliable, helpful and flexible
  • Commitment: Committed and inspiring, responsible and independent
  • Enthusiasm: With your passion for retail, we will go the extra mile together

Area: OTTO’S stores
