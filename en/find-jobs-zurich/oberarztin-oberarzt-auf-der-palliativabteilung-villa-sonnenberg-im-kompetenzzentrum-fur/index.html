Full description

We are a team of palliative medicine practitioners and geriatricians in the hospital Affoltern, who supervise the palliative department as well as the acute care department and various nursing homes. We also provide consultancy services in nearby hospitals. We are looking for you as a senior physician from 1 January 2027 or by agreement to treat patients in inpatient palliative medicine as well as acute geriatric medicine and nursing homes, as well as for possible palliative medical consultation hours and mobile service. Your profile

  • Completed specialist title General Internal Medicine or other completed specialist title with experience in General Internal Medicine
  • You want to obtain the main title of palliative medicine and are interested in geriatrics
  • Enjoyment of working in an interprofessional team as well as a high level of social competence
  • They are flexible and reinforce and enrich the existing team through their expertise
  • Very good language skills in German (verbal/written level C1) Our offer
  • We offer a medical profession in a training hospital with recognition in palliative medicine (A-clinic), geriatrics (A-clinic) and internal medicine (C-clinic)
  • Great independence and competence within the area of responsibility
  • Varied and interesting work in a motivated environment with a family working atmosphere
  • Varied and exciting task area in a dynamic and lively environment
  • Functional infrastructure and attractive employment conditions Our culture – Your benefits «Bi öis can mer no»: With us it is family and personal. As part of the Affoltern hospital team, you will also benefit from attractive employment conditions and numerous Fringe Benefits: Jokertag, shopping benefits and two subscriptions for free admissions to the FORTYSEVEN Wellness Spa are just a few examples of our Fringe Benefits. The Hospital Affoltern is the largest employer and training company in the Knonau Office. Around 550 employees work for the well-being of patients every day. Find out more about us in our portrait.
