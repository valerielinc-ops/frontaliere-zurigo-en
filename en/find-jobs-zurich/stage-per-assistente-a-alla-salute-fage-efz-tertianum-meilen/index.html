Full description

Start with us in a varied and meaningful training! Your future is important to us. Our experienced trainers will accompany and support you throughout your training.

For the Parkresidenz in Meilen, we are looking for you starting August 1, 2027 for the apprenticeship as Healthcare Specialist EFZ.

What you will learn with us

  • Care, support, and accompaniment of guests in everyday and challenging situations
  • Assistance with personal hygiene, getting up, dressing and undressing, eating and drinking
  • Medical procedures such as measuring blood pressure, pulse, and temperature, blood sampling, changing dressings, and much more
  • Daily accompaniment of guests with games, crafts, singing, etc.
  • Housekeeping, administrative, and logistical tasks
  • Compliance with and implementation of hygiene and safety regulations
  • Electronic nursing documentation

What you bring with you

  • Completed secondary education (Sekundarschule A or B) with good school performance
  • Great willingness to learn and sense of responsibility
  • Empathy and patience, as well as interest in dealing with our guests
  • Helpful, sociable, and team-oriented, with a strong sense of care and conscientiousness
  • Willingness to work irregular hours
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoyment of varied challenges and practical work
  • Good German language skills, both spoken and written

Your advantages

We offer a diverse range of different benefits, for example

  • SBB half-fare travelcard
  • Additional free day in the birthday month
  • Discounts on Sunrise
  • Alpamare discounts
  • Fitness discounts
  • Insurance discounts
  • Regular team events
  • Lunch for CHF 5
  • At least 1 hour of learning time per week in the learning space or workshop

Your training starts with an exciting introduction day. In the second year of training, our trainees participate in the Tertianum Summer Camp. The completion of the training is celebrated with a fitting graduation ceremony. After the apprenticeship, numerous opportunities open up to work within our company in various operations and language regions.

Further advantages and insights can be found on our website.

Interested in training with Tertianum? After receiving your complete application documents, we will review them and invite selected candidates for a first interview. Afterwards, you will have the opportunity to get to know the daily work at Tertianum better during one or more trial days.

Your complete application

  • Resume including application photo
  • Letter of motivation
  • School certificates
  • Results of the Stellwerk test and, if available, the Multicheck

Our team looks forward to motivated and interested trainees. Do you recognize yourself? Then we look forward to receiving your complete application via our online application tool.

For questions, Flavia Güdemann, Head of Nursing, is available at 044 925 06 00.
