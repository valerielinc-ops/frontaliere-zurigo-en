Full description

Our customers retire well prepared, invest their money successfully, are optimally secured and save taxes, premiums and fees. This makes us the number one independent financial advisory service. With us, you will get exciting insights during the BEM internship at our office in Zurich.

  • You support different teams and get to know the everyday life of the consultants.
  • During 18 months you will gain insight into asset management, investment advice, tax planning, estate regulation and real estate financing and get to know the independent advice and digital banking services of the VZ.
  • You attend the intercompany courses at the BBZ Banking Advisory Centre in a VZ class for about two days a month and graduate with a diploma recognised throughout Switzerland.
  • You work with experienced consultants who accompany you in customer projects and consultations.
  • Matura, Fachmaturität or EFZ as a merchant with professional maturity in an industry other than a bank
  • Enjoy money topics such as stocks and bonds as well as customer contact
  • Rapid comprehension, flair for numbers, motivation and established personality
  • You will work in different areas and learn from our experienced consultants.
  • A supportive and open environment where everyone looks forward to working with you.
  • After the internship, you have the chance for a permanent job, which is also possible during studies.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which you can fully develop. Become part of it!

Requirements

    • Matura, Fachmaturität oder EFZ als Kauffrau/Kaufmann mit Berufsmaturität in einer anderen Branche als Bank
    • Spass an Geldthemen wie Aktien und Obligationen sowie am Kundenkontakt
