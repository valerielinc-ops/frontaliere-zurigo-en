You can look forward to that.

A varied working environment awaits you in a dynamic team of young and experienced employees. With our extensive know-how, we ensure that the facilities, buildings and tracks are always in perfect condition so that the maintenance of our fleet runs smoothly. Your commitment contributes significantly to the fact that SBB can transport more than one million people safely and reliably from A to B every day. Your place of work is in Zurich for the maintenance of the facilities of the North East region. In addition, you have the opportunity to work partly from home or on the road as part of "Work Smart". Our benefits such as child allowances, free 2nd class GA and discounted travel abroad – also for the family – can be found on our website under Benefits. All information can be found at sbb.ch/berufsweltencafe. You can move that.

  • As an organizational talent, you carry out medium- and short-term maintenance planning in close cooperation with internal and external stakeholders (resource planning, production, third-party companies).
  • You plan preventive maintenance and special orders and prepare them for efficient execution.
  • Managing the master data as well as actively participating in the continuous optimization of work and maintenance plans as well as plant documentation is also one of your tasks.
  • You analyze planning deviations, derive optimization measures with the relevant stakeholders and contribute to the continuous improvement of maintenance planning.
  • Together with resource planning, you ensure efficient planning and control of maintenance services in the region.

You bring that with you.

  • Several years of experience in the preparation of work and the maintenance of plants are required.
  • Good knowledge of SAP (ideally PM/EAM) as well as a secure use of the common IT tools (e.g. M365) round off your profile.
  • You have strong analytical skills as well as a structured, independent and solution-oriented way of working.
  • In order to shine in this function, you have a basic technical training supplemented by a successfully completed higher vocational training (e.g. technician: in HF or comparable).
  • The willingness to travel within the region (especially Basel) does not matter to you.
  • So that you can reliably complete your tasks, we expect the perfect command of the German language, plus French.
