Full description

Service and repair to Porsche of all generations, also air-cooled, plus VW, Audi, Škoda and Seat. Thalwil · 100% · Admission by appointment · Monday to Friday, 7:15 a.m. to 5 p.m. · Feedback within one week Garage Kuhn AG has existed at the same location in Thalwil since 1955. It started as one of the first official Porsche representatives in Switzerland and as a VW company. Today it is an independent Porsche specialist and still a real family business. Porsche is the brand for which our heart beats. The workshop features current Porsches as well as air-cooled 911s, Transaxle classics and the 914, as well as VW, Audi, Škoda and Seat. Their tasks

  • Service and maintenance according to manufacturer specifications, also on hybrid and electric vehicles
  • Repairs of engine, propulsion, chassis and brakes
  • Wheel and tyre service with axle measurement
  • Preparation of vehicles for MFK
  • Work on classics, depending on the order

Workshop equipment

  • Hazet tools and tool wagons, spengler and service tools
  • Diagnostic testers from Bosch, Texa and Launch, manufacturer-specific diagnostics and online portals, including the Porsche service portal PPN
  • Laser measurement and universal parts catalogues

We offer this

  • Four weeks holiday, five weeks for non-smokers
  • High-voltage training up to level 2S (certificates of competence HV 1 and HV 2), further training up to level 3S possible
  • Further education and professional development according to your wishes and skills
  • Targeted training phase with the workshop manager
  • Regular personnel interviews
  • Own employee intranet with instructions, wiki, instructions, absences and appointments
  • From time to time the garage pays Kuhn Znüni or Zmittag
  • Tips from customers may be kept

Conditions

  • Completed basic training as an automotive mechatronics specialist, specialisation in passenger cars, or equivalent training, for example as a car mechanic
  • At least five years of professional experience after graduation
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Category B driving licence

Benefits

  • Experience with Porsche, also with classics, or with VW, Audi, Škoda and Seat
  • English language skills

This is how you work every day

  • They explain a finding to customers in such a way that they understand it without specialist knowledge, for example during vehicle handover or on the phone.
  • They document findings and work carried out in such a way that colleagues can understand the status at any time.
  • If you discover something outside of the assignment, report it and consult the team before you continue working.
  • They handle valuable customer vehicles carefully and keep information about customers and vehicles confidential.
  • They openly address mistakes, including their own.

Framework conditions

  • Overtime is the exception.
  • Saturday missions are rare. They are planned in advance, and you get time compensation for it.
  • We take an ongoing notice period into account.
  • Place of work: Alte Landstrasse 48, 8800 Thalwil
  • We discuss the pay in the first conversation.

Application Apply via the form on this page. CV and certificates are sufficient, as PDF or photo. A letter of motivation is not necessary, but welcome. Your documents go directly to Patrik Hammer, Managing Director, and can only be viewed by the management. He answers questions in advance under 044 723 11 01. Within a week you will receive a personal feedback. After the first interview, we invite you to a trial day in the workshop. You get to know the company and the team, and we see how you work. There are no big projects on this day, it should give both sides an insight.
