Building technician: in HLKKSE (m/f/d) – With responsibility and heart for our buildings

100%, Zurich, perpetual

In the Facility Services department of ETH Zurich, we create the optimal conditions for teaching, research and technology transfer. We maintain the buildings and ensure the energy and media supply as well as the technical and infrastructural management of all rooms used by ETH Zurich.

Are you someone who loves technical challenges and enjoys working where complex building technology meets teaching and research? Do you keep track of dynamic situations and ensure that everything runs reliably? Then this position could be just right for you.

For the Hönggerberg site, we are looking for a:n building technician:in HLKKS (m/f/d) in the building area HP.

Job description

In this versatile and responsible position, you and your team ensure that all HVAC systems are operated without disruption, safely and energy-efficiently. You take over the planning, prioritization and implementation of maintenance and repair measures and thus ensure that all technical systems function reliably at all times.

You coordinate external service providers, accompany their missions, monitor the quality of work and ensure that maintenance contracts are adhered to. The planning, procurement and management of replacement and reserve materials is also your responsibility. With your technical know-how, you advance ongoing operational optimizations: You analyze processes, control and control systems, identify potential for improvement and implement measures on your own initiative.

When commissioning and accepting new and existing plants, you contribute your HLKKSE expertise and ensure their safe and efficient operation. During construction work, you represent the house technical requirements, coordinate interfaces, work closely with project teams and participate in construction meetings, intermediate tests and HLKKS approvals.

In quality management, you pay attention to compliance with internal standards, collect and maintain plant data in GMIS/Planon and actively initiate improvements. In addition, you will take over pikett services such as Tagespikett building area HP and, if necessary, 7/24 Pikett am Hönggerberg. You are also a central person in the area of security: You ensure personal and operational security, supervise security areas, respond to alarms, and support the BMA and EVAK equipment.

In short: You are a pillar of smooth, safe and modern building operations – professionally experienced, responsible and with an eye for the whole.

Profile

  • A completed HLKKSE training as well as further training in maintenance or an equivalent qualification such as ventilation, heating, refrigeration, sanitary service installer:in, commissioning specialist:in or control engineer:in always with very good hydraulic know-how.
  • At least 10 years of experience as a service technician: in, in operation, in maintenance, and in troubleshooting complex technical installations.
  • In-depth expertise, analytical thinking and the ability to assess and specifically improve processes, control strategies and system behavior.
  • Organizational skill, flexibility and experience in dealing with different contact groups – from specialists to external partners.
  • Careful, structured working methods and willingness to systematically maintain and develop operational and plant data.
  • Responsibility, reliability and the ability to act calmly and professionally in security-related situations.
  • High level of teamwork, clear and step-oriented communication, solution-oriented working methods and enjoyment of an academically demanding environment.
  • Good MS-Office and CAFM skills, as well as very good German and English skills
