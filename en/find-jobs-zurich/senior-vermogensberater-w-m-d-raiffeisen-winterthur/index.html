Full description

22.10.2026 Winterthur

Become part of our team!

Success arises where competence meets real proximity. As a regionally anchored and independent cooperative bank, we support our customers sustainably on their financial path with personal advice and modern financial solutions. Our seven-member asset advisory team serves demanding client portfolios in an economically strong and attractive market area and actively shapes the successful further development of our investment and pensions business. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for an experienced personality with a passion for asset management and the aspiration to develop customer relationships sustainably.

What awaits you?

  • You support and develop your own customer portfolio of wealthy private customers and accompany them in all financial situations.
  • You recognize potential in the areas of investing, provisioning and financing and develop needs-based solutions from this.
  • You actively gain new customers, maintain your network and represent the bank at events and customer events.
  • You observe developments in the financial markets and bring your expertise to the consulting and internal knowledge transfer.
  • You work together across all departments and actively shape the further development of our investment and pensions business.

What do you bring?

  • Several years of professional experience in the holistic consulting of wealthy private clients
  • In-depth expertise in investing, pension and financing
  • Banking education/training at tertiary level
  • Strong customer and service orientation as well as pleasure in building long-term customer relationships
  • Winning personality with a high level of initiative, entrepreneurial thinking and a secure appearance
