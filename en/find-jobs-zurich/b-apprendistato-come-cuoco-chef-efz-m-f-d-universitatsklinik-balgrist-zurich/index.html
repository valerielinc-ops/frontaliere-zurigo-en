Full description

Balgrist University Hospital is one of the most renowned orthopaedic clinics in Switzerland with an affiliated centre for paraplegia and international recognition. In our hospital kitchen, menus are prepared every day for around 150 patients and for around 550 – 650 employees.

We are pleased to be able to offer an apprenticeship in our versatile hospital kitchen at the beginning of the apprenticeship in 2027 for a Tasks: • Three-year training in the company with weekly attendance at the vocational school and participation in intercompany courses • Learning to use various modern kitchen appliances and professional working techniques • Quality testing of the available foods for freshness, taste and texture as well as compliance with strict hygiene guidelines • The careful preparation of dishes for our patients, employees and guests Requirements • Completed secondary education level A or B • Joy of processing and preparing food • Good taste, no food or contact allergies, good physical fitness • Strong hygiene awareness and sense of order as well as a well-kept appearance • Good knowledge of German in words and writing (minor level B2) • Team player who remains calm even during high-speed operation and shows initiative in the workflows We offer: • Sound basic training with a high practical relevance, supplemented by theoretical training in the vocational school • Joint development of expertise and competences to achieve the Federal Certificate of Competence (EFZ) • Possibility to complete training in an orthopaedic clinic with an excellent reputation in a versatile large kitchen • Collaborating with motivated team and trainers • Regulated and pleasant working hours • 50% discount on the ZVV bonus pass or an annual contribution to a general subscription Further advantages are just one click away: Attractive offers for our employees
