Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Career Guidance Center Breitenstein Management of adult protection at the Breitenstein Center in Andelfingen 80% The Office for Youth and Career Guidance of the Education Directorate provides, orders, controls and coordinates child and youth welfare and career counseling services. The Breitenstein Center is part of the Office for Youth and Career Guidance and is supported and co-financed by the communities in the Andelfingen district. The Breitenstein Center provides social services in the areas of child and adult protection, personal help, advice and prevention for the population of the Andelfingen district. The offer is aimed at families, young people and children as well as adults with special needs, social difficulties and/or addiction problems. The Breitenstein Center is looking for a head of adult protection 80% as of November 1, 2026 or by appointment. Together with your team, you will provide adult assistance for 22 communities in the Andelfingen district, which the KESB Winterthur-Andelfingen has arranged. In addition, you offer personal help on behalf of individual communities. The client's self-determination and personal responsibility are always in the foreground. Your tasks

  • Technical, organizational and personnel management of the adult protection department with nine specialist employees
  • Responsibility for technical coaching as well as case and performance management
  • Participation in the further development of the organization
  • Collaboration and coordination with internal and external bodies Your profile
  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in social work as well as further training in the area of adult protection and leadership
  • Sound professional experience in managing mandates, knowledge of social security law as well as personal and family law
  • Several years of leadership experience
  • Confident negotiation skills, high facilitation and conflict resolution skills. You can expect a varied job in an interdisciplinary and highly motivated team, attractive further training opportunities and contemporary employment conditions. Your workplace is located right next to Andelfingen train station and can be reached directly from Zurich main train station in 40 minutes. We offer you a good introduction and coaching from your supervisor and the management team. We look forward to your online application. Christoph Willisegger, Head of the Breitenstein Center (043 258 46 21), will be happy to provide you with any information. Further information can also be found at www.zh.ch.

Workload: 80%
