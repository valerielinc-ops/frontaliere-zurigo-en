Role overview

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Career Guidance Intern Youth and Family Counseling kjz Winterthur 80% The Office for Youth and Career Counseling of the Education Directorate provides, orders, controls and coordinates child and youth welfare and career counseling services.

As one of a total of 15 child and youth welfare centers in the canton of Zurich, the kjz Winterthur ensures uniform, regionally and municipally solid service provision in the areas of youth and family counseling, parenting counseling, mothers and fathers counseling as well as child protection.
