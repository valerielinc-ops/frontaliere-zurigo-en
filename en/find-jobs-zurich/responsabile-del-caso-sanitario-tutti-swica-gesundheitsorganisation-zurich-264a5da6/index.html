Full description

We are committed to health – because health is everything. We are committed to this, also as an employer. We are convinced: Only in a healthy working world do the best talents feel comfortable and move forward: professionally and personally. For that we give everything. Do you want to further promote SWICA’s customer satisfaction as a health clerk in the field of paramedicine? Then you are exactly right here! For this important role, we are looking for a detailed and reliable personality who has in-depth knowledge and experience in the field of physiotherapy and is also interested in the fields of occupational therapy and speech therapy. You appreciate the exchange with our insured and thus make a significant contribution to a positive customer journey. In addition to the SWICA benefits, we offer you a modern and safe working environment in a warm and committed team. This is how you shape health

  • Responsibility for the processing of invoices, cost language and service requests in the paramedical environment.
  • Technical examination of performance claims and development of well-founded performance decisions.
  • Close cooperation with insured persons, service providers and internal and external contacts.
  • Coordination and implementation of clarifications in demanding performance cases.
  • Contact person for professional and administrative questions about paramedical services.
  • Co-creation of a service-oriented customer experience through solution-oriented action and high quality standards.

This sets you apart

  • Medical, paramedical or commercial training completed
  • Sound expertise and professional experience in the field of physiotherapy
  • Experience in performance and/or a health or social security card ideally complement your profile
  • Flexible, responsible and resilient personality with an exact and independent working method
  • Impeccable oral and written expression in German as well as good knowledge of English
  • Further language skills are welcome
  • Open personality with communication flair and enjoyment of cross-line collaboration

Why SWICA

  • We build on humanity. That is why we focus on openness, respect and trust in our everyday work. We tackle this, take responsibility and think in terms of solutions – together, for our customers. This is also why we have been number 1 in customer satisfaction for many years.
  • We trust you and give you space. With annual working hours, at least 5 weeks holidays and on request also holiday purchase. With part-time models and generous home office within the scope of the possibilities of the job, you make your everyday life largely flexible.
  • We value and reward your work – with a market-based wage, up to 1,500 francs annual fee for personal wishes and a generous participation in the basic and additional insurance for you and your family.
  • We offer you prospects. With a generous training regulations, we support your professional and personal development – for your role today and for your goals of tomorrow.
  • Because health is everything. With our company health management and region-specific offers, we take care of your well-being. And if it comes otherwise: 100% net wage continued payment.
  • We think long term. With three savings plans, pensum-dependent coordination deduction and benefits well above the legal minimum, you ensure good for your future.
