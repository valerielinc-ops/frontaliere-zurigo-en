Full description

Become part of our WATER & ENVIRONMENT team. We plan and implement wastewater treatment plants with state-of-the-art technologies for the further elimination of nutrients and the removal of organic trace substances. In addition, we minimise energy consumption, use wastewater as a resource and close material cycles for sustainable solutions. Support us in the project planning and implementation of wastewater treatment plants. Plan the sustainable infrastructure facilities of tomorrow with us, which contribute to improving the water quality in rivers and lakes and at the same time inspire our clients with innovative and customer-oriented solutions.

Take on responsibility with us as

PROJECT MANAGER CIVIL ENGINEERING IN THE FIELD OF WATER & WASTEWATER 80-100% - ZURICH Shape the water infrastructure of tomorrow Wastewater treatment plants and urban drainage systems are among the most demanding infrastructure projects: they combine structural and civil engineering, structural engineering, process engineering, mechanical engineering and building technology – often during ongoing operation and under high demands on quality, deadlines and costs. At AFRY, you will play a central role in such projects. You will develop building concepts, coordinate interdisciplinary planning teams and accompany our customers from the initial idea to successful implementation. You don't work alone: In our Water & Environment team, you will meet experienced specialists who share their knowledge, take responsibility and support each other.

YOUR TASKS

  • Planning and implementation of sophisticated building and civil engineering works and developing innovative and economical solutions for operators of wastewater treatment plants and urban drainage systems
  • Take over the overall or sub-project management and control quality, costs, deadlines and risks
  • Coordination of interdisciplinary project teams and lead the interfaces to structural engineering, process engineering, architecture, mechanical engineering, building technology and EI&C
  • We advise and accompany our customers and represent the projects vis-à-vis building owners, authorities, planning parties and executing companies throughout all SIA planning phases

WHAT YOU CAN EXPECT FROM US

  • A well-rehearsed and experienced team
  • Complex projects with technical demands and impact
  • Freedom for initiative and responsibility
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation at eye level
  • Promotion of your individual strengths
  • Flexible and contemporary working models

YOUR PROFILE

  • Degree in civil engineering, architecture or a comparable field
  • Several years of professional experience (at least 5 years) in the planning and implementation of infrastructure, industrial or building construction projects
  • Experience ideally in the field of water and wastewater, in structural engineering, in structural engineering or in specialist construction management
  • Interest in interdisciplinary issues and enjoyment of working with different departments
  • Experience in collaborative project management with BIM and digital planning methods
  • Independent, structured way of working as well as strong communication and teamwork skills
  • Very good oral and written German skills and good English skills. Knowledge of French is an advantage, but not a prerequisite
