Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

Job Summary We are seeking a highly skilled and results-driven Commercial Excellence Expert to lead and support initiatives aimed at improving the efficiency, effectiveness, and profitability of our sales organization. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in transactional sales processes, organizational optimization, and continuous improvement, with a proven track record of delivering cost-saving projects. A Lean Six Sigma Black Belt certification is required. Key Responsibilities:

  • This role requires a Lean Six Sigma Black Belt certification.
  • Analyze and optimize end-to-end sales processes , identifying inefficiencies and areas for improvement.
  • Lead process improvement projects using Lean Six Sigma methodologies to enhance productivity and reduce waste.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams (Sales, Finance, Operations, IT) to implement best practices and standardize activities.
  • Develop and monitor KPIs and performance dashboards to track progress and impact of commercial excellence initiatives.
  • Identify and execute cost-saving opportunities across sales operations, including pricing, order management, customer service, and CRM usage.
  • Support change management and training efforts to ensure adoption of new processes and tools.
  • Drive organizational design improvements to align roles, responsibilities, and workflows with strategic goals.
  • Act as a strategic partner to sales leadership in shaping commercial strategy and operational excellence.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Business, Engineering, Economics, or related field.
  • Lean Six Sigma Black Belt certification is mandatory.
  • Minimum 5 years of experience in commercial excellence , sales operations , or process improvement roles.
  • Strong analytical skills and proficiency in tools such as Excel, Power BI, and CRM systems (e.g., Salesforce).
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
  • Proven ability to lead cross-functional projects and deliver measurable results.
  • Experience in a sales-driven organization with a focus on transactional activities is highly preferred.
