Full description

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, get involved and grow with us. Become part of our family and shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us.

What you move

  • You lead the areas of cleaning, building maintenance and conveyor system and ensure good cooperation in your area of responsibility
  • For the reliable operation of our technical facilities, buildings and infrastructure, you are responsible and ensure a smooth process.
  • Coordinate maintenance, repair and maintenance work in a forward-looking manner and ensure that problems are resolved quickly and pragmatically
  • You are close to the action and also take on yourself. Especially when it gets hectic, you find practical solutions and keep the overview
  • Together with your team, you will continue to develop technology, infrastructure and processes and implement identified optimization potentials.

Why do you succeed?

  • You have completed technical or craft training and ideally bring leadership experience
  • As a natural leader, you motivate your employees, formulate clear expectations and lead at eye level
  • Craftsmanship and technical understanding are among your strengths. You like to do it yourself.
  • You work independently, reliably and solution-oriented and keep track of several challenges
  • A clear, appreciative and binding communication is a matter of course for you. You approach topics proactively and gladly take on responsibility

Your Benefits

  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Family culture: You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • Modern work equipment: We keep up with the times – modern and safe work equipment is available to you
  • Good transport connections: Top transport connections - faster to work, more free time

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
