Full description

Immediately or by agreement we are looking for a senior doctor oncology 80-100%

Your responsibilities

  • Care of predominantly outpatients and co-care of inpatient patients
  • Participation in the tumor board and in interdisciplinary case discussions
  • Participation in internal training for doctors of the clinic as well as in family doctor training

Your profile

  • Specialist title for medical oncology or specialist title hematology desirable
  • Preferred specialization in gastrointestinal or urogenital tumors or other oncological entities
  • High initiative, flexibility, teamwork, social skills and good communication skills
  • Interest in the further expansion of the Oncology Department
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary cooperation and teaching assistant doctors

What we offer you

  • Development opportunity to become a deputy senior doctor
  • Established oncology with three specialists.
  • Certified Breast Centre (Breast Centre Zurich Lake) in cooperation with the Hirslanden Clinic in the Park.
  • Further established collaborations.
  • Possibility of employment in job sharing
  • A place of work with the best connection to public transport, as well as cost-effective staff parking spaces.
  • Diverse benefits – from great employee discounts to further education offers. If you have any questions, you are welcome to contact Dr. Michael Kiessling, Head of Oncology. michael.kiessling@see-spital.ch Physical applications cannot be considered or returned.
