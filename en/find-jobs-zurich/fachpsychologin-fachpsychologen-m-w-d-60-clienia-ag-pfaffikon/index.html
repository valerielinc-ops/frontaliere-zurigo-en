Full description

With Clienia Ambulant, the Clienia Group bundles its psychiatric-psychotherapeutic services for the best possible outpatient and day-to-day clinical care for mentally ill patients of all ages – at 20 locations from Amriswil to Zurich. Interdisciplinary teams of psychotherapists, psychotherapists, nursing specialists and specialists in psychiatry and psychotherapy guarantee comprehensive outpatient treatment methods.

We are looking for 1. December 2026 or by agreement one or more

for our day care clinic in our outpatient psychiatric center in Frauenfeld.

Their tasks They work in our day care clinic, affiliated with our outpatient psychiatry and psychotherapy in our outpatient psychiatric center in Frauenfeld. Under medical supervision, they independently and on a case-by-case basis conduct psychotherapeutic discussions in individual settings, conduct psychotherapeutic group sessions and coordinate the treatment in the interdisciplinary team. They treat and accompany patients in the second half of life from a broad spectrum of diagnosis with a focus on affective diseases, excluding cognitive disorders.

Your profile They enjoy working with older patients (60+) in the daily clinical setting. You are a responsible, reliable and flexible personality. In addition to clinical experience, you bring a specialist title for psychotherapy or are in advanced psychotherapy training. You enjoy working in a multi-professional treatment team and appreciate a collegial and family working atmosphere

Our offer You will find an exciting and responsible job in a committed, multi-professional team with the opportunity to get involved and shape. An extensive further and further training offer as well as supervision support you in your further development. There are regulated working hours (42-hour week), transparent structures and a collegial working atmosphere. We are easily accessible from the larger cities of Zurich, Winterthur, St. Gallen and Constance.

For further information, see Seraina Häfeli-Swallow, Chief Medical Center for Old Age Psychiatry and Privé, Tel. +41 71 929 6282, available.
