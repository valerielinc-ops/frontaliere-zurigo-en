ETH Space: Finance & Controlling and Office Support

80%-100%, Zurich, fixed-term

Please apply until 31 October 2026.

Project background

ETH Space mission is to boost space activities to establish ETH Zurich as the leading university in space research and Earth observation, education and entrepreneurship within Europe and beyond. ETH Space encompasses overall five units: Education, Research, Entrepreneurship, the ETH Swiss GeoLab, and the space mission, LIFE.

ETH Space is led by Prof. Thomas Zurbuchen as its director, and Prof. Richard Kornfeld, as managing director. The team overall consists of about 35 employees. The office is located in Zurich downtown, in one of the central ETH buildings.

The team and finances of ETH Space have grown significantly in recent years, and as ETH Space is transitioning to become an ETH Center, we are looking to add a person for Finance & Controlling and Office Support.

This position is offered as a fixed-term appointment for an initial period of two years, with the possibility of extension. The desired starting date is as soon as possible.

For details see Home - ETH Zurich | Space as well as our LinkedIn channel.

Job description

In this multifaceted role, you will take responsibility for Finance & Internal Controlling as well as support the Office Management of ETH Space, playing a key role in ensuring the efficient and smooth operation of the organizational unit. You will ensure the proper execution of financial and administrative processes, support the leadership team with organizational and operational matters, and act as the central point of contact between internal and external stakeholders - for finance and controlling matters. You will support transparent workflows, reliable administration, and professional support for the ETH Space unit.

Your areas of responsibility include, in particular

Finance & Controlling

  • Budget calculations (annual budget and bi-annual rolling forecasts)
  • Project finances: from budgeting and expenditure to financial reports
  • Procurement of goods and services involving complex ordering structures, including quote comparisons, export control compliance, procurement regulations, tenders, and contract awards
  • Accounting & ETH Space internal controlling
  • Invoice verification and payment
  • Processing employee expense claims
  • Credit card statement reconciliation
  • Performance-based project billing (via SAP)
  • Handling all ETH Zurich internal finance-related inquiries

Office Management Support

  • Administrative support for ETH Space management
  • Preparation of documents and reports
  • Tracking tasks and projects
  • Handling sensitive information confidentially
  • Support office management in managing daily office operations
  • Provide recruitment support (job postings, application coordination)
  • Support new employee onboarding

This position offers a varied and responsible role with a broad spectrum of tasks at the intersection of finance/controlling and office management for ETH Space. You will play a key role in ensuring professional administration, transparent financial processes, and smooth organizational operations within our organization. In doing so, you will work closely with management, staff, and internal and external contacts, contributing significantly to the success of ETH Space.

Profile

  • Education: you completed commercial or administrative vocational training; further training in accounting or controlling, and project or office management is an asset
  • Experience: you have a minimum of 5 years of relevant professional experience. Experience in an academic or research setting is an advantage (incl. prior work at ETH Zurich)
  • Technical Skills :
  • Experience with SAP and proficiency with these systems
  • Advanced user skills in MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) and scheduling tools
  • Very good written and spoken German and English (French an advantage)
  • Competencies & Personality
  • Structured, analytical, flair for numbers and attention to detail
  • Independent approach and strong organizational skills
  • Proactive and reliable personality
  • Solution-oriented and service-oriented working style
  • Enjoy working with complex project structures
  • Strong interpersonal skills and the ability to build bridges across teams

In addition, we are looking for someone who possesses the motivation and drive to define and implement work processes within the ETH Space leadership team.

We offer

  • The great and rare opportunity to help shape the operations and finances of a soon-to-become strategic center of ETH Zurich: ETH Space
  • A dynamic, interdisciplinary and entrepreneurial environment at one of the world’s leading technical universities
  • A workplace in Zurich downtown.
