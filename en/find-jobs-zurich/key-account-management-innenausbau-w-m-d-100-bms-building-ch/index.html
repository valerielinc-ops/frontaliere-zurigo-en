Full description

BMS Building Materials Suisse is a wholesaler of building materials for shell and interior construction. Our group of companies includes the renowned specialists Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced experts advise, support and inspire customers from the construction industry as well as private customers at around 110 locations throughout Switzerland. Close to everyday construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Quick, easy and competent.

Do you have profound experience in key account management, think strategically and understand how to build sustainable customer relationships and develop them in a targeted manner? Then you are exactly right with us! As Key Account Manager for our Richner brand in the Zurich region team, you play a central role in the support and further development of our most important customer relationships and contribute significantly to the long-term success of the company.

As of January 1, 2027 or by agreement, you will assume responsibility for a strategically important customer portfolio, recognize new business potential and expand long-term partnerships. You actively contribute your experience, your network and your entrepreneurial mindset. You can expect a versatile task with creative leeway, personal responsibility and the opportunity to sustainably strengthen the market position of our Richner brand in the Zurich region.

Task area

  • Object processing and tracking in the allocated sales area
  • acquisition of new customers
  • Engineering offices, architects, general contractors and planning offices
  • Shared responsibility for achieving the regions
  • Increasing customer loyalty with appropriate measures
  • Preparation and monitoring of alerts
  • Independent preparation of weekly visit planning as well as documentation of own customer visits in visit reports
  • Assistance and visit of internal and external company events
  • Ambassador for the Richner brand
  • Cooperation with account managers (external service)

Profile

In order to successfully master these versatile challenges, you bring the following competencies:

  • Basic technical training with further training in business administration
  • Several years of experience in interior design or the real estate industry as well as success in a comparable activity
  • Experience in sales including in negotiation situations, good industry knowledge and network in interior design
  • Passion for consulting and sales as well as strong entrepreneurial mindset
  • Independent and effective personality with pleasure in dealing with people
  • High communication strength with persuasion and team-oriented working methods
  • Resilient and resilient personality
  • High willingness to travel
  • Negotiation-proof German, English skills advantageous
  • Very good IT knowledge, especially in the common office range

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
