Full description

We are shaping the care of tomorrow! At Stiftung APW, we are continuing to develop our care services and strengthening collaboration between inpatient care and Spitex. In doing so, we rely on working and project groups and are systematically expanding specialist responsibilities. For this, we are looking for committed individuals who want to take this path with us, contribute their ideas and actively help shape the future of Spitex. Healthcare Specialist EFZ, Spitex To strengthen our Spitex team, we are looking for a committed and reliable Healthcare Specialist EFZ for the outpatient care sector, starting immediately or by agreement. You provide care and support to our clients in their familiar homes and help them manage their daily lives as independently as possible. You perform tasks in basic and treatment care, observe changes in their state of health, and carefully document your visits. In doing so, you work closely with our nursing professionals and the interprofessional team. Do you feel comfortable in a role where you can make a difference?

  • You are a qualified healthcare assistant with an EFZ qualification and work independently and responsibly.
  • You interact with people empathetically, respectfully and on an equal footing.
  • You are flexible, use your initiative and adapt well to changing situations.
  • You value teamwork and actively contribute.
  • You are willing to take on night shifts when required.
  • You hold a Category B driving licence.

Profile

The Spitex service for the lakeside municipalities of Greppen, Vitznau and Weggis supports people in their own homes – individually, professionally and with compassion. Our aim is to enable and facilitate life in familiar surroundings. We provide care, treatment, advice and support where people feel most comfortable: at home. Our service area covers around 7’000 residents. A motivated team of around 30 employees works toward this goal with expertise, compassion and enthusiasm for their work.
