Full description

Company presentation

At McDonald's, we believe that every visit must be a pleasant, friendly and moody moment. Our team is committed to welcoming our customers warmly and making every moment a memorable moment. If you like human contact and are looking for a dynamic, stimulating job with many opportunities, you are meant for us!

Description of role

As a host, you are the image of our restaurant. You welcome guests with a big smile, organize original activities and ensure that everyone, and especially the younger ones, has an excellent time. You bring energy, creativity and good humor to the team, making each visit unique.

Main responsibilities

  • Welcome guests and create a friendly and fun atmosphere.
  • Organize birthday parties and creative events for children
  • Organize guided tours of the kitchen for guests
  • Support the team at the counter, McDrive or restaurant during rush hours.
  • Administrative support for restaurant management

Qualifications required

  • Extraverted and positive personality
  • I like working with people, especially children.
  • Creative and full of ideas
  • Reliable and motivated team player
  • Good communication skills (local language required, other languages an asset)

Benefits

  • Flexible working hours to promote your work-life balance
  • A multicultural and supportive team environment
  • Opportunities for training and career development — many managers have started like you!
  • Compensation and working conditions in accordance with the provisions of the collective agreement (L-GAV)
  • 50% discount on our products in our restaurants in Switzerland and Liechtenstein
  • Sunrise subscriptions at reduced prices
  • Access to exclusive discounts via the Enterprise Benefits platform
  • Additional vacation days are available to employees over 50 years of age.
  • Free access to the largest online training library
