Full description

Here we are

We accompany people with tumor and blood diseases holistically – medically, therapeutically and humanly. Our approach combines modern conventional medicine with complementary therapies and consistently focuses on people. At our locations in Zurich, Winterthur, Uster and Glarus, doctors, nurses, therapists and administrative staff work closely together – interdisciplinary, respectful and on an equal footing. We create an environment where quality, time and humanity are not just words, but are lived daily. Do you want a task in which you can contribute your organizational talent and at the same time accompany people in a demanding life situation? Then you are exactly right with us. To strengthen our team in Zurich, we are looking for you as a committed personality in the field of practice assistant / medical secretariat.

Medical/practice assistant (80–100%) – Zurich (with a perspective of Zurich-Oerlikon)

Your everyday work

  • You are the first contact person for our patients and ensure a trusting atmosphere
  • You organize and coordinate everyday practice efficiently and forward-looking
  • You actively support our doctors in the consultation hours
  • You independently carry out laboratory diagnostics
  • You implement medical orders reliably
  • You take over the telephone management and coordinate appointments professionally
  • You complete administrative tasks and ensure clean documentation

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training as MPA, FaGe or medical secretary
  • Professional experience in the medical environment is beneficial
  • Safe handling of MS Office (practical software knowledge is a plus)
  • Joy in dealing with people and a high level of empathy
  • Structured, independent and responsible functioning
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

Your personality

  • Friendly, empathetic and service-oriented
  • Reliable and well organized
  • Team-ready and flexible
  • Maintained appearance with positive radiation

This is what we offer you

  • A meaningful activity in a future-oriented medical environment
  • An appreciative, committed team with real collaboration at eye level
  • Modern practice rooms and a pleasant working environment
  • Flexible working hours for a good work-life balance
  • The opportunity to actively contribute and shape everyday practice
  • An exciting mix of conventional medicine and complementary therapeutic approaches

Requirements

  • Completed training as an MPA, FaGe, or medical secretary

  • Professional experience in the medical field is an advantage

  • Confident use of MS Office (specific practice software knowledge is a plus)

  • High level of empathy and interpersonal skills

  • Organizational skills, independence, and responsibility

  • Good knowledge of the German language (spoken and written)

  • Friendly, service-oriented, reliable, and well-organized personality

  • Teamwork skills and flexibility
