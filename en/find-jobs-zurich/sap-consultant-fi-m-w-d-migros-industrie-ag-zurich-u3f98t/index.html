Full description

Actively shape the SAP financial landscape of the Migros industry. In this versatile role, you will be responsible for the further development and operation of our SAP FI/CO solutions, work closely with the specialist areas and successfully put innovative ideas into practice in a dynamic industrial environment.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Industrie AG, Migros Industrie AG – Zurich, Limmatstrasse 152, 8005 Zurich

Mansioni

  • You are responsible for the further development and smooth operation of the SAP FICO applications (S/4HANA and R/3) of the Migros industry as well as the relevant interfaces to surrounding systems.
  • You analyse the technical requirements and are in exchange with the various stakeholders and develop, accompany the technical implementation.
  • You will conduct key user training sessions and be in direct contact with our internal customers. In addition, the maintenance and further development of training and process documentation is important to you.
  • You work close to the Shared Service Center Accounting and are therefore networked with a variety of functions of the Migros industry.

Requirements

  • Professional experience: in the manufacturing industry / production with broadly networked SAP systems
  • Higher technical school: in the field of computer science / business informatics
  • In-depth application knowledge in SAP FI (S/4HANA) is required.
  • You gained your experience in finance as a user, key user or SAP specialist in an industrial and / or production company.
  • Comprehensive understanding of economic relationships in the financial sector as well as the associated business processes and specialist areas.
  • High level of teamwork, resilience and communication as well as an independent working method.
  • You already have experience with SAP BTP.
  • It is easy for you to move into agile working methods (SAFe).
  • German (flowing)
  • English (flowing)
  • French (advantageous)
