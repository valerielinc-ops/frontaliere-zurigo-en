Full description

Working on the pulse of digital transformation. Our Digital Business department makes it possible. Together with us, shape the future of real estate management, develop innovative products and processes and move Livit forward together with us. In digital business, you can expect a great, interdisciplinary team, an inspiring working atmosphere and exciting projects in which you can actively participate. Are you excited about innovations, the latest systems, an agile setting and new challenges? Then you are exactly right with Livit. We look forward to your personal contribution.

Tasks

  • You support our BI team from conception and design to development, operation and further development of our BI and reporting solutions
  • You specify, develop and maintain customer reports in close cooperation with internal departments and external partners
  • You ensure the professional and timely provision of electronic interfaces and reports for internal and external recipients
  • You document, maintain and optimize existing and newly developed interfaces, data flows and reporting solutions
  • As part of the replacement of our real estate ERP system, you are actively involved in the migration and development of existing data warehouse and reporting solutions.
  • You will analyze technical requirements and develop sustainable solutions in the field of data warehouse and reporting
  • You use your expertise to continuously improve and develop our business intelligence services

Requirements

  • You have completed vocational training as a computer scientist: in EFZ or a corresponding higher education (HF, FH or university) with a focus on computer science
  • You have several years of experience in database development with MS SQL Server and have profound knowledge in the technologies SSRS, SSIS and in the development of stored procedures
  • Ideally, you have knowledge of PostgreSQL and experience working with different database systems
  • You have practical experience with development tools such as SQL Server Management Studio (SSMS), DBeaver, RedGate Toolbelt, Visual Studio and Confluence and JIRA
  • You have already gained experience in the real estate industry, especially in management or real estate accounting
  • You ideally know the real estate software G-REM from GARAIO REM
  • You communicate stylishly in German and have good knowledge of English in word and writing
  • You distinguish yourself through an independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • You take responsibility, bring your own initiative and enjoy working in a dynamic environment with diverse stakeholders

We offer

  • Internal offer of our Livit Academy for specialist and leadership courses
  • Financial and/or time support for external specialist training and access to electronic learning platforms for online language courses
  • individual career planning and development prospects
  • Home / Mobile Office
  • Bonus depending on company success and personal performance
  • At least 5 weeks holiday and purchase of up to 10 additional days holiday (Ferienflex)
  • Above-average pension benefits and premiums for medical day allowance and accident insurance are 100% covered by Livit
  • 14 days paternity leave
