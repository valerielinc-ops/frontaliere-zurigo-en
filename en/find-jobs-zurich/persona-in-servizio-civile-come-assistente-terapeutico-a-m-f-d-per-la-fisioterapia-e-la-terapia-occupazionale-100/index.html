Full description

USZ Campus by immediate effect or by agreement until the end of February 2027 (for at least 4 months for a first assignment)

What are your main tasks?

  • as part of the individual and group therapy, you supervise and accompany patients in the outpatient as well as inpatient setting under the guidance of a therapist
  • you take over administrative and organizational activities to support the core business in the therapy operation, the tasks are among others: processing of therapy programs, organization of patient transports, provision of materials and their maintenance, support in telephone support and administrative controlling
  • depending on your qualifications, you have the opportunity to use your skills for other tasks, such as process optimizations and further developments

What sets you apart?

  • you are a civil servant (m/f/d)
  • You are a resilient and communicative personality and enjoy working in a team
  • Your reliability, willingness to provide services and high social skills distinguish you
  • you are interested in physiotherapy, occupational therapy and medicine
  • You have an advantage over advanced IT user knowledge

What can we offer you?

Physiotherapy Occupational Therapy USZ with around 140 physiotherapists and occupational therapists stands for the high quality of therapeutic services for patients: inside the clinics and institutes of the USZ. We offer you a diverse and exciting civilian service assignment in the "Physiotherapy Occupational Therapy USZ". You will get a direct insight into the therapies and the interprofessional core business of the University Hospital Zurich. Depending on which therapy team you work in, your area of responsibility in patient care and support varies. You are a member of a therapy team and in close exchange with your colleagues: physiotherapy and occupational therapy. For this post, dossiers from recruiters cannot be considered.
