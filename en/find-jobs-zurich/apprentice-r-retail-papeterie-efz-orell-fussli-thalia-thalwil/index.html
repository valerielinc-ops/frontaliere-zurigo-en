Full description

Make your passion for papetry a profession Since 1904 we have been passionate about paper and office products, books, games and gift ideas. What once began small is now firmly anchored with 13 branches in the region of Zurich Oberland, Lake Zurich, Linthebene and the airport region. During your training, you will get to know the retail trade from the ground up. We look forward to welcoming you to our branch in Thalwil on 1 August 2027.

Your main tasks

  • Competent advice to customers
  • Recommendation of paper and stationery products
  • Receiving orders and handling them
  • Various work on the computer and in our goods management system
  • Presentation of our versatile range

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed secondary school or a good degree
  • High service orientation, sociability, attention and curiosity
  • Appropriate knowledge of German
  • Flair for design and presentation
  • Interest in current affairs and trends

This is what awaits you

  • A versatile and lively working environment
  • A motivated, collaborative team
  • Space for initiative and participation
  • A traditional company with a modern orientation

We look forward to your application via our recruiting tool. Please send us this including a letter of motivation, CV and complete school reports. See you soon.
