Full description

Become part of our team! We are looking for an after-school manager / social pedagogue for 40% on 3 working days (no use in school holidays and school-free days) by immediate or by appointment for the lunchtime care of the secondary school Aemtler B.

Tasks

  • Care and individual support for young people
  • Planning and implementation of projects and leisure activities
  • Close cooperation with a year team/teaching
  • Administrative tasks
  • Cooperation and support during excursions and school events
  • Cooperation in organisational work
  • Implementation of the educational objectives of the school
  • Cooperation and implementation in the development of the day school

Profile

  • Completion in Social Education/Social Work FH/HF or equivalent training mandatory
  • Joy in dealing with young people, humor
  • Experience in pedagogical and interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Responsibility, independence, flexibility
  • Perseverance, high resilience
  • Organizational and improvisational talent
  • Joy in dealing with people of different cultures

We offer

  • Varied, versatile work with many design possibilities
  • Cooperation in the field of care and in the school team
  • 40% employment on three days (Monday, Tuesday, Thursday)
  • Regulated working hours from approximately 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., Monday to 6 p.m.
  • 12 weeks of school holidays, during school holidays the care is closed
  • Receipt of the four weeks holiday and compensation of about eight weeks overtime
  • Conditions of employment according to city guidelines
  • Training opportunities

About us

Tensions in the heart of Züri - there(s) are me! Every day at the pulse of education and where our future grows and learns.

The Zurich-Limmattal school district (city districts 4, 5 and Sihlfeld district 3) is a school district in the middle of the city of Zurich with its 14 schools.

The Aemtler B secondary school has been run as a day school since the summer of 2025 and comprises 11 secondary classes as well as a care facility and employs about 30 teachers and caregivers.
