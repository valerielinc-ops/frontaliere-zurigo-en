As a specialist care provider at the University Hospital Zurich, we are committed to showing commitment with our professional diversity in the top medical environment and always keeping up with the times. We do not achieve success alone, but together! Each individual counts and together we bring ourselves to the highest level of care professionally.

Want to be part of our team? With us you can make a difference!

  • At the forefront: With us you are not only there – you are in the middle of it. In the operating room, you take responsibility, work close to the patient and experience a varied everyday life with meaning.
  • Strong team: Successful interventions are teamwork. You can expect well-established processes, mutual support and an environment in which quality and trust count.
  • Varied surgical routine: From routine to high-tech – our operating areas offer you exciting insights, modern procedures and versatile applications.
  • Your development counts: Whether deepening, further education or development towards leadership – we actively promote your path.
  • Space for you: Flexible work models and modern service planning help you to balance your work and private life.
