Where we are looking for them

  • Independent setup, setup and operation of CNC milling machines (3- and/or 5-axis technology)
  • Creation and optimization of CNC programs directly on the machine (workshop-oriented programming), based on technical drawings
  • Production of precise workpieces and components, both in single-part and small-series production
  • Continuous monitoring of the manufacturing process to ensure smooth operations and dimensional accuracy WHAT YOU SHOULD INVOLVE
  • Successfully completed vocational training as polymechanic (m/f/d) or machine operator (m/f/d)
  • Layer readiness (2-layer)
  • Several years of professional experience in CNC machining technology
  • Ideally, experience in processing small and medium series as well as prototypes
  • CAM experience ideally beneficial with Siemens NX
  • High level of performance and efficient working methods
  • Teamwork and independence
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing what we offer you

  • Flexible working hours (active and trusted working hours)
