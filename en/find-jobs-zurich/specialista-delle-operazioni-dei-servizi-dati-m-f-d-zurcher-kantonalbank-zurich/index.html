Full description

80% - 100% | Banking Operations | Zurich | Experienced professionals In the Securities Lifecycle Management Data Services team, you ensure the quality and availability of central financial market data for the entire bank. Your work on data quality and processes has a direct impact on all of the bank’s portfolios and thus creates visible added value for our customers. In this key role, you combine your data and process know-how with a demanding operational environment and work closely with specialist departments, IT and product management. Your tasks

  • Collaborations with specialist departments, IT, product management and the agile DevOps team at eye level and active participation in cross-divisional projects, specialist committees and working groups financial market data
  • Co-creation of solutions from requirement to concept to implementation as well as technical assessment of projects with regard to feasibility and impact on business
  • Design and optimize end-to-end processes in the operational environment and participate in the implementation of regulatory requirements and internal policies
  • Responsible for the data quality of instrument master data, prices, markets and issuers as well as maintaining and controlling the master data in NeoXam DataHub and Avaloq
  • Performing analysis and reporting tasks (e.g. SQL queries, SAS reports, evaluation of key figures) as well as processing incidents in securities processing and supporting releases and year-end processing
  • Preparation of instructions and training documents, schools of team members, assuming a one-stop-shop for technical issues as well as the possibility to take on deputy tasks of team management with professional responsibility

Your profile

  • Completed higher vocational training with federal diploma or federal diploma. Technical pass/degree HF
  • Several years of experience in the quality management of financial market data
  • High IT affinity and very good knowledge of Excel
  • Knowledge of Avaloq or NeoXam DataHub as well as experience with SQL and SAS are beneficial
  • Strong problem-solving skills and structured, analytical workflow
  • Readiness for shift work (three shifts between Monday and Friday, 07:30 – 17:30), sporadic weekend work and assignments in year-end processing
  • Very good knowledge of German (at least level C1) and good knowledge of English
