Institute of Legal Medicine

Job opening by agreement

Take responsibility. Growing together. Further develop legal medicine. Would you like to contribute and further develop your legal expertise in a collegiate university environment? Do you like to take on responsibility, work scientifically and shape your own specialist focus? Then we look forward to meeting you. At the Institute of Forensic Medicine at the University of Zurich, you can expect the entire spectrum of modern forensic medicine – from clinical-forensic examinations of living persons to legal inspections, to autopsies and state-of-the-art forensic imaging. As a university centre, we combine medical excellence with research and teaching and make an important contribution at the interface of medicine, science and law. We attach great importance to open cooperation, mutual support and an appreciative team culture. At the same time, we create space for personal initiative and personal development. Increasing responsibility opens up a wide range of design possibilities for you – be it in professional development, in establishing your own priorities or in helping to shape our department.

Tasks

Depending on your experience and function, you assume responsibility in different areas. Specialist: doctor They handle forensic medical cases on their own responsibility and have the opportunity to gradually develop into a senior medical function.

Their tasks include

  • Independent processing of legal cases
  • Carrying out autopsies, legal inspections and clinical forensic examinations of living persons
  • Cooperation in forensic on-call service
  • Gradual assumption of supervision of assistant doctors:innen
  • Participation in teaching and research

Senior doctor: doctor You also assume responsibility for the professional and organizational development of our department.

  • Professional and personnel management and supervision of assistant doctors: inside
  • Responsibility for an own professional focus and its further development
  • Co-shaping the strategic development of our department
  • Active participation in university teaching, research and interdisciplinary projects

Profile

As a specialist: doctor

  • Specialist title Legal medicine
  • Joy of self-responsible case handling
  • Interest in university medicine as well as in research and teaching
  • Motivation to take on responsibility and develop professionally and personally
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Category B driving licence

As a senior doctor: doctor

In addition, bring with you

  • Several years of professional experience in forensic medicine
  • Designated expertise in one or more specialised areas
  • Experience in leadership, supervision and further training of assistant doctors: inside
  • Enjoyment in promoting employees and actively developing forensic medicine
