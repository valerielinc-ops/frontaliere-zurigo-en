Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Correctional Services and Reintegration Pöschwies Correctional Facility Foremen/in Korberei 80

100% With around 1,400 employees, Corrections and Reintegration (JuWe) is the largest office within the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs and is responsible for the execution of sentences and measures as well as for the implementation of various types of detention.
