Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We advise individuals and companies on all matters relating to money, manage assets and offer attractive financial services. We are very successful and growing strongly. To strengthen our IT team, we are looking for a solution-oriented and committed personality in Zurich.

  • Leadership of the IT Management Office team of 2 employees in personnel, professional and organizational terms.
  • Together with your team, you take responsibility for purchasing, supplier and service request management of IT.
  • You monitor the life cycle of existing contracts and make tenders in compliance with our compliance requirements.
  • In the role of the ITSM Platform Owner (based on ServiceNow), you drive the further development and actively participate in the implementation.
  • Optimize reporting and ensure that quality and risk management are implemented seamlessly.
  • Completed studies or further education in computer science or business informatics
  • Several years of experience in a comparable role, ideally in the financial services environment
  • In-depth knowledge of ITIL and process design
  • Knowledge of the ITSM of ServiceNow is a plus
  • Strong service orientation and a “hands-on” mentality
  • In addition to an exciting and challenging job in an interdisciplinary environment, you have the chance to help shape the development of a central IT Management Office through your initiative and commitment.
  • You are also at the right place for your further education: We support you individually and support you with an external further education within the framework of the job profile.
  • With us, you can freely choose your time so that you have more time for further education, family, sports and projects that are important to you. December 24 and 31 are paid holidays for all employees.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
