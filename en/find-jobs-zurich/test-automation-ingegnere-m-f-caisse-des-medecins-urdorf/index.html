Postes à pourvoir Test Automation Engineer (m/f) Taux d'occupation 80-100% Début immediately or by appointment Durée unlimited Lieu de travail Urdorf Ärztekasse Genossenschaft provides business process outsourcing and eHealth solutions to health professionals so they get relief from administration and can focus on medical work. In the context of an innovation initiative, Ärztekasse is renewing and extending their products. To support this digitalization step, we are looking for smart and experienced engineers to contribute and shape clever and creative solutions for a meaningful industry. Specifically, we are looking for a test automation engineer to ensure that our products are comprehensively tested before they are handed over to our customers. Main Tasks

  • Plan, design, develop, and execute automated test scenarios. Depending on the project, the automation needs to be done on API level, Web GUI and/or end to end
  • The automated test cases need to be identified based on risk of the according functionality
  • Taking full responsibility for the test of components, including preparation of test cases (manual and automated), preparing test phases, creation of test strategy and test plan
  • The automation shall cover functional and non-functional testing
  • Build up regression test suites which are part of the continuous integration and build process
  • Identify and automate critical performance and security tests
  • Prepare test data for both – function and non-functional tests
  • Collaborate with requirements engineers and business analysts to identify most beneficial test cases

Requirements

  • At least 5 years of experience in software development/ test automation.
  • Experience in testing Java and Web (Angular, REST, SOAP) applications.
  • Strong and proven test automation skills
  • Proven track record in taking overall responsibility for the testing of components
  • Code design affinity. Familiarity with key architecture and design principles.
  • Experience with testing tools and frameworks (like Selenium, Cucumber, Gherkin, Postman, Rest Assured)
  • Deep expertise in Digital Frameworks like Selenium (Web UI), PlayWright, Rest Assured (API), SQL(Databases: Sybase and PostgreSQL).
  • Hands-on experience delivering automation with a focus on continuous integration/continuous deployment. (Git, Bamboo)
  • Hands-on experience with scripting languages (e.g., Python, Java, JavaScript, or Shell scripting).
  • Proven experience with Agile methodologies and exposure to tools like JIRA, Confluence and X-Ray for Test Management.
  • High code quality and good documentation skills.
  • Experience in the Swiss healthcare sector
  • Understanding of security concepts is a plus
  • Self-driven and self-organized
  • Analytical mindset with fast comprehension
  • English is required

What we offer

  • 40 hours per week
  • Highly motivated team
  • Modern office space
  • Good public transport connections
  • 25 days holiday, 30 days holiday from age 50
  • Private accident insurance
