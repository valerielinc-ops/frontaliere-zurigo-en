80% - 100% | IT / Business Engineering | Zurich | Experienced professionals

Welcome to the DevOps Team Appfinity of Zürcher Kantonalbank! We are responsible for operating and continuously developing various in-house-developed software solutions and integrating purchased software products in the bank management portfolio – among others for the areas of Treasury, Accounting and Controlling. In doing so, we actively help shape our application landscape and solution architecture and develop them further step by step.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a Full Stack Developer with knowledge of application integration or a willingness to learn it, who also enjoys contributing to architectural matters and helping shape technical solution designs. Are you a solution-oriented team player with a strong willingness to learn and broad IT expertise, and are you looking for a new challenge? A great team and an exciting and varied working day await you here. We look forward to receiving your application.

Your tasks

  • Work as part of our self-organized and interdisciplinary Scrum Team within the SAFe Framework
  • Implement business and technical requirements in the backend (Java, Spring Boot, Spring Batch) and frontend (TypeScript, Angular, Node.js, HTML5, CSS3)
  • Further develop and maintain the purchased software products and deepen your knowledge of application integration
  • Ensure the continuous further development and stable operation of the systems as part of the DevOps philosophy
  • Develop technical concepts, analyses and designs for new features in close collaboration with internal departments and external software partners
  • Responsible Solution Architect in the DevOps team, responsible for the further development of our application and solution architecture (e.g. integration concepts, interface design, event-driven approaches)
  • Work closely with Solution Architects and other development teams on the development of technical solution designs
  • Bring in new technical and methodological ideas and promote open, transparent communication within the team

Your profile

  • Completed degree in computer science or comparable training in the IT field
  • Several years of professional experience in developing modern web applications, ideally with Java, Spring Boot, Angular and TypeScript
  • Experience as an Application Manager or System Integrator throughout the entire lifecycle of Windows applications (introduction, operation, maintenance) or a willingness to acquire this knowledge
  • Experience in software development with AI
  • Good knowledge of Continuous Integration & Delivery (e.g. CloudBees/Jenkins, Maven, Tomcat, Nexus, GitLab) and experience working with Windows environments
  • Knowledge of Kafka and cloud is a plus
  • You are a team player, enjoy familiarizing yourself with new topics and actively share your know-how within the team
  • You like to take a holistic view of systems – from the application through integration and operations to architecture – and take responsibility throughout the entire product life cycle
  • Very good German language skills (at least C1 level), both spoken and written

Our offer

  • Motivated, self-organized and enthusiastic team that is looking forward to welcoming you
  • Mutual support and team spirit are highly valued here
  • Centrally located, easily accessible and attractive workplace (Hardbrücke railway station) with the opportunity to organize your work flexibly (hybrid)
  • As your employer, we care about your career and therefore promote and support you on your journey with many offerings
  • Comprehensive package that, in addition to an attractive base salary and variable compensation, offers many benefits and discounts – from A for retirement provision to Z for ZVV-Bonuspass
