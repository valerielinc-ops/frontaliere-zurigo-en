Original description

Interested in exploring the world of Compliance? Curious working in a stimulating and challenging environment of a multinational company?

We are looking for a talent with a positive can-do attitude who is able to think "out of the box”.

Apply for this exciting position in Zurich’s Investment Compliance team and support our Next Generation Talent Strategy! Key stakeholders are based in Zurich, Switzerland.

You will be introduced to a broad spectrum of topics while working on several priorities in close collaboration with team members in a truly international environment.

The role provides a great opportunity to collaborate closely with many experts, while being empowered to bring in and present own ideas.

The Investment Compliance team operates a centralized coverage model (with strong regional collaboration), aligning with Group Investment Management’s vertically integrated, global organization.

This position will report directly to the Head Investment Compliance and will interact with various business partners from Group Investment Management and with members of central, regional, and local Compliance teams.

What you will do

As Working Student Investment Compliance, you will

  • Assist in evaluating business activities in Investment Management to assess compliance risks.
  • Assist in advising the business with respect to adoption and implementation of regulations and Group Compliance policies.
  • Support activities to provide assurance to the business that compliance risks are being mitigated and managed and advise and support the business in closing agreed action plans.
  • Assist in executing compliance core processes, such as the annual compliance risk assessment, annual planning, monthly and quarterly reporting.
  • Assist in transformation of processes using artificial intelligence, perform data analytics, create dashboards and other visualizations.

What you bring

As Working Student Investment Compliance, your skills and qualifications will ideally include:

  • Enrolment in a University/ Institute of higher education for the duration of the internship is mandatory
  • Student, ideally nearing completion of a Master in Law, Risk Management, Economics, Business Administration, International Affairs or similar
  • Strong oral and written communication skills
  • Excellent command of MS Office applications. Skills in data analytics, data visualization and application of artificial intelligence are a definite plus
  • Business fluent in English, other languages an advantage
  • Responsible, detail-oriented and ability to work on multiple tasks
  • A team-oriented talent and a strong communicator, able to work in a dynamic and everchanging environment
  • First experience working independently and owning initiatives with cross-functional stakeholders from start to finish
  • You must be located in Switzerland for the duration of the working student contract.
