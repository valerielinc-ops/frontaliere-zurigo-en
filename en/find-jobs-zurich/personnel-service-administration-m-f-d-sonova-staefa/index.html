Sachbearbeiter Service Administration (m/w/d) — Sonova

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

To strengthen our service team, we are looking for you!

You take over the processing and coordination of repair orders, are directly at our customers' side with questions, monitor appointments, record complaints and ensure that repairs, remakes and new custom orders run smoothly. Bring your expertise and actively shape our excellent customer service!

More about the role

  • Processing of repair orders including customer consultation on questions
  • Active appointment control of repairs and ensuring telephone accessibility of the service department
  • Collection of customer complaints
  • Shipping preparation and billing of repairs, remakes and new custom orders
  • Support for administrative tasks, for example when creating hand delivery notes and scanning customer documents
  • Close cooperation with other departments to ensure efficient processes

More about you

Must-have

  • Commercial or technical training
  • Professional experience in customer contact, ideally in a technical environment
  • Very good knowledge of German and solid knowledge of French (at least B1)
  • High reliability and an independent, structured working method
  • Strong customer understanding and service-oriented communication
  • Resilience and flexibility as well as the ability to keep an overview and set priorities correctly, even in hectic situations
  • Teamwork and communication strength
  • Safe handling of Windows and MS Office; SAP knowledge is an advantage

Desirable

  • Experience in hearing aids or in a service organisation
  • Technical understanding and audiology knowledge
  • Knowledge of English and Italian
  • Creativity and willingness to actively contribute your own ideas

More about our offer

As one of the world’s leading audio-acoustics providers with headquarters in Switzerland, we are committed to an inclusive corporate culture. We want to create an environment where you can balance a successful career with your commitments and interests outside of work – thanks to our flexible hybrid work model. We offer a variety of further education opportunities for your professional and personal development as well as excellent growth opportunities through individual development plans.
