Canton of Zurich Financial Directorate Personnel Office Apprenticeship as a businesswoman / Kaufmann EFZ 2027 100% Do you want to become part of the economic engine Switzerland and shape your professional future at the Canton of Zurich? Then you are exactly right with us. Around 300 apprentices complete their training in 25 different professions. There are around 110 apprentices in CV training. The commercial training takes place in the rotation system with an annual change. This gives you the opportunity to get to know different companies, tasks and workplace cultures of the Canton of Zurich. During your training, you will go through various organizational units, such as:

  • Vocational Counselling (biz)
  • Vocational and Technical Colleges / University of Zurich / University of Education
  • Courts / Public Prosecutor's Offices
  • Child and Youth Assistance Centres (kjz)
  • Tax Office, Social Office and Migration Office
  • Road Transport Offices and many more... During your training you will learn a variety of administrative and organizational
  • Tasks know and take over these independently. This includes, among other things, providing telephone information, advising customers at the counter and organizing events. In the intercompany courses of the public administration industry, you acquire the necessary action skills, which you can then further develop in your daily work, projects and events. The
  • Requirements For the three-year basic education as a businesswoman/Kaufmann EFZ, we expect good academic performance at the top level. You are strong in communication and enjoy contact with people. In addition, you have very good oral and written knowledge of German. Openness to new things, willingness to learn and reliability round off your profile. If you have very good academic achievements, you also have the opportunity to complete the vocational maturity in combination with the three-year basic education as a businesswoman / businessman EFZ.
