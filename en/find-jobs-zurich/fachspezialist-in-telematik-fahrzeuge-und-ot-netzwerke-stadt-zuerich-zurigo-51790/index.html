Full description

With our vehicles we travel twice a day around the world and transport over 860,000 passengers. This means that every day, 365 days a year and almost around the clock we make an important contribution to the quality of life in one of the most beautiful cities in the world.

Tasks

Where our line network connects people, you connect the technology behind it. As a specialist in telematics, vehicles & OT networks, you ensure that vehicles, stops, depots and control systems communicate reliably with each other. You will continue to develop the network infrastructure of public transport, put new technologies on track and ensure the long-term operation of our communication systems. Together with internal departments, suppliers and the ZVV, you shape the digital future of mobility in Zurich.

  • As a specialist and technical contact person, you support the department head in the technical management of the competence area Telematics, Vehicles & OT networks.
  • You are responsible for the entire life cycle of the systems and systems assigned to you in the area of IP communication, vehicle telematics, OT networks, sensors and IT systems.
  • You are actively involved in shaping the newly created IP communication platform, where vehicles are networked by the entire ZVV.
  • You support the transport companies in the ZVV with the integration of new systems into the joint IP communication platform and provide second-level support for complex technical issues.
  • You will recognize technology trends, evaluate new products and solutions and develop sustainable concepts for the public transport of tomorrow.
  • You define requirements for hardware, software and network infrastructures and actively participate in requirements engineering for new systems.
  • You ensure the technical integrity of the systems, maintain configurations, test hardware and software releases and take responsibility for their release.
  • You will develop maintenance strategies, maintenance specifications and maintenance planning and ensure that our systems can be operated safely and economically in the long term.

Profile

  • completed vocational apprenticeship as a computer scientist EFZ platform development or a comparable training in the field of computer science, communication or network technology
  • ideally training at the level of HF, MAS, NDS (FH) or as an ICT platform development specialist
  • several years of professional experience in the operation and further development of OT networks, communication systems or industrial IT infrastructures
  • In-depth knowledge of network technologies, administration of IT systems and IT security
  • Experience in the field of vehicle, infrastructure or industrial control systems is an advantage
  • You have a strong system understanding and think networked across hardware, software, networks and operational processes
  • You work in a structured, analytical and solution-oriented manner and keep an overview even in complex technical environments
  • Knowledge of project management methods, ideally according to HERMES, will help you get started
  • Experience in professional management, coordination or management of technical projects is a plus
  • You master German at level C2, English at least at level B1 for technical documentation and cooperation with international suppliers
  • you appreciate the cooperation with different stakeholders and convince through your communicative and pragmatic nature
