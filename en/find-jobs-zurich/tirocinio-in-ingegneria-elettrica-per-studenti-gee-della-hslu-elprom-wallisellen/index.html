Full description

Job title Internship Electroengineering – for GEE students at HSLU Description You are studying Building Electroengineering (GEE) at the Lucerne University of Applied Sciences – Technology & Architecture and would like to apply your knowledge not only in the lecture hall, but also in practice? About us At elprom, you will have the opportunity to actively participate in versatile electroengineering projects during your studies. You get to know our planning processes, take on your own tasks and get insight into the different areas of building technology. Experienced specialists accompany you and give you the opportunity to deepen your theoretical knowledge in practice and develop yourself further. What you do with us

  • You work in the planning and projecting of electrical systems
  • You create and edit project and execution plans as well as electrical schemes
  • You will work on 2D, 3D and BIM models in the project team
  • You support with technical clarifications, calculations and analyses
  • You participate in feasibility studies and the development of concepts
  • You contribute to the creation of technical documentation
  • You get to know the coordination with architects, specialist planners and other project participants
  • You use digital planning and working methods

What we expect

  • Study Building Electroengineering (GEE) at Lucerne University of Applied Sciences – Technology & Architecture
  • Interest in electrical planning, building technology and new technologies
  • Motivation to use what you have learned in concrete projects
  • You work carefully, reliably and with high quality awareness
  • You bring your own ideas, enjoy working in a team and are open with feedback
  • Good knowledge of German

Employment services

  • Insight into versatile and challenging electroengineering projects
  • Practical experience in planning and project planning
  • Personal support by experienced professionals
  • Possibility to take on your own tasks and responsibility
  • Modern working environment with digital planning and BIM methods
  • Flexible working hours and a hybrid working method
  • A collegial team with short decision paths
  • Prospects for the further path at elprom

Interested? You want to supplement your GEE studies with real practical experience and find out what modern electroengineering means in practice? Then we look forward to meeting you. Questions about the internship will gladly answer Urs Bugmann.
