Full description

With Clienia Ambulant, the Clienia Group bundles its psychiatric and psychotherapeutic services for the best possible outpatient and day hospital care for mentally ill patients of all ages – at 20 locations from Amriswil to Zurich. Interdisciplinary teams of psychotherapists, nurses and specialists in psychiatry and psychotherapy guarantee comprehensive outpatient treatment methods.

For the interdisciplinary work in the psychiatric day clinic in Wetzikon, we are looking for a

The Wetzikon Day Clinic offers a modular range of treatments for patients between the ages of 18 and 39, with various psychiatric disorders, including mood disorders (depression, anxiety disorders), disorders of emotion regulation, personality disorders, psychotic illnesses and ADHD.

Your tasks

  • Responsibility for the content and course of occupational therapy treatment
  • Collaboration in the further development of the treatment concept of the day clinic with a focus on reintegration into everyday life, employment and work
  • the independent planning and implementation of topic-specific group therapies as well as individual discussions
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation in a committed team

Your profile For this exciting job, you have a degree as an occupational therapist HF/FH and preferably already have professional experience in a psychiatric setting. You enjoy working in an interprofessional team. You are an action-oriented person who, in addition to enthusiasm, is characterized by a high level of social competence and customer orientation. You are interested and motivated to embed your expertise in the therapeutic principles of the day clinic and to link it to the ward-specific concepts.

Our offer We offer you an interesting and responsible job, good training opportunities and, last but not least, a job in an open and appreciative interprofessional team. We offer professional exchange in the ET/AT team as well as attractive employment conditions in a modern clinic.

Some other highlights

  • innovative employer with strong interprofessional cooperation
  • Very good development opportunities with generous support in further education and training
  • Various discounts (e.g. with Reka checks, health-promoting offers, selected leisure activities and online shops)
  • 5 weeks of vacation, from the 50. 27 days of age and from 60. Age 32 days
  • Good catering options, as well as lunch checks
