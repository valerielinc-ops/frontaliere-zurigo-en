Full description

As a leading technology company, Hoval has been inspiring for decades with innovative system solutions for heating and air conditioning technology.

You have experience in supporting business applications and would like to contribute your knowledge to the service environment? With us, you support our users around SAP and other business applications, develop our service processes together with the specialist areas and carry out training. In addition, you are actively involved in the replacement and introduction of our systems for Field Service Management.

Your tasks

  • Application support: You are the central contact person for our employees in the internal service and our service technicians and support them with questions and concerns about SAP CS and our FSM systems
  • Knowledge transfer: You train our employees in dealing with SAP CS and FSM systems and ensure a practical knowledge transfer
  • Project participation: You are actively involved in projects relating to our business applications, for example in the replacement and implementation of a new field service management system
  • Operational day-to-day business: You support the smooth running of our service processes and take on recurring tasks such as invoicing, controlling service invoices and processing error logs
  • Data preparation: You combine data from different systems, prepare it in Excel and make it available for evaluation and further analysis

Proximity to the business: During visits to our offices, you are in direct contact with employees, conduct training courses and promote the exchange of experiences

We offer you

  • Varied role: You combine application support, day-to-day operations and project cooperation and thus get a versatile insight into our service processes
  • Proximity to the business: You work closely with our internal service and service technicians and directly experience how your work supports the everyday work of our employees
  • SAP S/4HANA transformation: You accompany the transition from SAP R/3 to SAP S/4HANA and gain valuable experience in a company-wide transformation project
  • Exciting system projects: You work on the further development and replacement of our business applications, for example on the introduction of a new field service management system
  • Structured Onboarding: In the first two months you will get to know our day-to-day business and the processes in our offices directly on site. This will give you the necessary practical knowledge for your future role

Your profile

  • Professional experience: You have at least three years of professional experience in a commercial or technical environment
  • Application Support: You have experience supporting users and enjoy helping with questions and problems
  • Business Applications: You have experience in dealing with business applications such as SAP, field service management systems or comparable applications and have a high IT affinity.
  • Excel & Data: You have very good knowledge of Excel and can merge, process and evaluate data from different systems
  • SAP knowledge: Knowledge of SAP CS or another SAP module is an advantage, a corresponding familiarization is guaranteed
  • Project collaboration: You are interested in participating in system and digitalization projects. Initial experience in project work is beneficial
  • Working method: You think networked, work independently and structured and quickly find your way around new systems and processes
  • Provide knowledge: You are happy to explain, understandable and practical and enjoy training employees in dealing with our systems
  • Languages: You have fluent knowledge of German in words and writing. Knowledge of French is beneficial
