Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. With our Mortgage Center, we set standards for the financing of real estate. Within the framework of the two-year PWA training, committed graduate students have the opportunity to complete a versatile practical training at our headquarters at Paradeplatz in Zurich.

  • Your business education starts on 01. March 2027 and lasts 24 months. In the first six months you will visit the Kaderschule Zürich with a focus on business, finance and communication. Afterwards, you will work for 18 months at HypothekenZentrum AG. Upon successful completion, you will receive the diploma Wirtschaftsfachfrau/Wirtschaftsfachmann KSZH. Further information can be found under PWA Economic Program.
  • You will take on various tasks in back office and relationship management and gain insights into various areas of the HZ HypothekenZentrum.
  • You will get to know the background and processes of the back office tasks of a financial service provider and gain valuable knowledge about mortgage and real estate business.
  • You have graduated, are enthusiastic about financial topics and are looking for entry into the banking industry.
  • You are communicative, open and motivated and bring a flair for numbers.
  • Impeccable communication in German in word and writing, very good Swiss German skills are an advantage.
  • Motivation to work in a young, dynamic and service-oriented team.
  • We cover the entire school costs of the Kaderschule Zürich and pay you a fixed wage during the entire two-year internship period.
  • Successful interns are given the opportunity for permanent employment after graduation.
  • A team you can count on and a climate that promotes initiative.
  • A collegial working environment with flat hierarchies.
  • Independent work and the opportunity to contribute and implement your own ideas.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
