Full description

Our customers in the BORS sector (police corps and emergency services organizations) use and operate highly secure, state-of-the-art technologies. Data security and high availability of applications play a central role - around the clock. As an outsourcing partner, we are responsible for the further development and operation of critical IT infrastructures and their applications, and support our customers with innovative solutions. Our experienced team of specialists looks forward to your support!

As an ICT System Engineer, you manage the WAF/IDP infrastructure in the security-critical police environment.

You ensure stable, secure and high-performance operation, support projects and contribute your expertise to the further development of our WAF/IDP systems.

Your role

  • Design, implementation and configuration of Web Application Firewall (WAF) and Identity Provider (IDP) solutions
  • Creation and fine-tuning of Web Application Firewalls and the WAF Rule Engine
  • Integration of Single Sign-On (SSO), Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and federated identities (SAML, OIDC) into the WAM infrastructure
  • Analysis of security logs, identification of attack patterns (false-positive reduction) and support in resolving security incidents
  • Ensuring compliance with service management and change management requirements
  • Responsibility for upgrades, patching and performance optimization of systems in a high-availability environment
  • Advising application owners and developers on securely connecting their services, as well as close collaboration with the SOC (Security Operations Center) and external partners

Your profile

  • Successfully completed education in computer science (HF, FH, Master's degree) or an equivalent qualification, as well as several years of professional experience
  • In-depth experience with market-leading WAF solutions
  • Sound knowledge of access management systems
  • Expert knowledge of HTTP/S, SSL/TLS (certificate management), TCP/IP and modern authentication protocols (OAuth2, OpenID Connect, SAML 2.0)
  • Experience conducting vulnerability analyses and a deep understanding of web technologies (APIs, JSON, REST)
  • Independent, solution-oriented and structured working style
  • Teamwork, customer orientation, commitment and willingness to pursue continuous professional development
  • Ability to remain calm and act in a focused and goal-oriented manner in critical situations and to be available outside regular working hours (e.g. during call-outs or incidents)

  • A great team in which team spirit is highly valued.
  • A diverse working environment with a wide range of state-of-the-art technologies

We cultivate an open, informal culture—so feel free to send us your application using informal language.
