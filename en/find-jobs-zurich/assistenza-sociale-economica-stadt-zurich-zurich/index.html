Full description

For the Economic Assistance specialist unit at Wipkingerplatz Social Centre, we are looking for a motivated individual starting on January 1, 2027, or by agreement, on a fixed-term basis until December 31, 2027.

Tasks

  • You support the specialist unit in preparing and developing technical foundations and in compiling decision-making bases, specialist information, reports and practical tools in the area of financial social assistance.
  • You handle assignments related to a new case management system, thereby supporting citizen-oriented, efficient and correct administrative action.
  • With support from your team colleagues, you advise employees and management personnel on maintaining and developing specialist knowledge and service quality, and on implementing new requirements and guidelines.
  • You support the planning and implementation of projects and participate in working groups and committees.
  • You gain insight into the work and organization of Switzerland's largest social services department and become familiar with processes within an urban administrative context.

Profile

  • You have a master’s degree in social sciences, law, economics, or the humanities.
  • You are interested in approaching tasks and projects on an interdisciplinary basis and developing optimal solutions while taking into account social policy, legal, business administration, and technical perspectives.
  • You possess methodological skills for handling tasks in a structured manner.
  • You enjoy project work and support the planning and implementation of projects.
  • Very good oral and written communication skills as well as very good IT skills.

We offer

  • A versatile and meaningful role in an interdisciplinary team with a high level of expertise. In our organization, we maintain open and transparent communication.

About us

With around 2400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a socially inclusive Zurich. We are here for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different life situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighborhoods.

Every day, the Social Services work in the five social centers to ensure that people in the City of Zurich can count on effective basic social services.
