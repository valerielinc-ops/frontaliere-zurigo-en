Full description

Helvetic Warranty GmbH is an independent Swiss company in the field of embedded insurance and market leader in Switzerland. We offer tailor-made property insurance for the DACH region and are growing strongly in this area. Since 2019 we are a subsidiary of Helvetia Versicherungen. As an intermediary between customers and insurers, we offer B2B2C solutions, from product development to employee training to claims settlement. For more than 10 years, we have been bundling our services centrally in Switzerland, communicating in all national languages and English. We are looking for motivated talents who will shape the future of property insurance in this growth market with us.

Tasks

  • You will oversee topics in the operation and in the further development of our IT landscape and support operational, organizational and technological projects within our IT organization.
  • You work closely with the business, your IT colleagues and external partners and ensure an efficient and transparent flow of information.
  • You recognize connections, prioritize tasks independently and make well-founded decisions to promote topics efficiently.
  • You independently take responsibility for IT initiatives and smaller IT projects and lead them in a structured manner until successful implementation.
  • You think solution-oriented, understand the business context and are actively involved in the development of pragmatic processes and the continuous development of our IT organization.

Requirements

  • You bring a completed degree in business informatics or a comparable education in the IT sector – and you want to contribute your knowledge in a versatile IT environment.
  • You already have profound experience in independently supervising operational IT topics, IT initiatives or projects and successfully implementing them.
  • You understand IT systems and technical contexts very well, work curiously and quickly into new topics and advance tasks with structure, overview and initiative.
  • You approach topics analytically, solution-oriented and with a clear service idea – and convince through personal responsibility, commitment and open, clear communication.
  • You appreciate the cooperation with internal and external partners and move linguistically safely in German and English – verbally and in writing.

We believe in cooperation at eye level, mutual trust and people who want to take responsibility. Different perspectives help us to develop better solutions – for our customers as well as for us as a team.
