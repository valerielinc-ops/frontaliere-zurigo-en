Full description

For the 5 intakes of the city of Zurich we are looking for 01. November 2026 or by agreement reinforcement.

Tasks

  • They perform temporary assignments as representatives of social workers or for general discharge in the 5 intakes of the city of Zurich.
  • They comprehensively clarify the concerns in the context of the economic and personal social assistance of the clients.
  • They provide short-term financial support with the aim of professional and social integration.
  • They are responsible for triage, networking and coordination and initiate immediate measures in the areas of social assistance and crisis intervention.
  • They work with profile teams, specialist bodies, authorities and social insurance companies.
  • You will be administratively supported by administrators, can draw on the knowledge of internal specialist positions and work closely with your colleagues in the team.

Profile

  • University of Applied Sciences degree in Social Work (with deepening Social Work) or in final phase of study
  • Commercial flair and good knowledge of financial topics
  • Joy of working with people and dealing well with conflicting expectations
  • Rapid detection of complex situations and ability, even under pressure qualitatively and quantitatively performs very good work
  • High interest in exchanging ideas in a team, sharing knowledge and experience and efficient cooperation with colleagues as well as with other offices and authorities

We offer

A versatile and exciting job in a team with a high level of expertise. An attractive training offer as well as internal specialist positions to support in day-to-day business. The place of work is located in the 5 social centers of the city of Zurich.

About us

With around 2,400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a social Zurich. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.

In the five social centres, the social services work every day to ensure that people in the city of Zurich can count on effective basic social care.
