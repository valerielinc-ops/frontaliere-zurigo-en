Full description

Precise setup and operation of CNC-controlled machines is one of the core tasks. The company maintains careful process control and promotes your further development. Are you ready for new challenges? For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • CNC turning and milling machines independently set up, operate and program

  • Continuously optimize production processes and create documentation
