Full description

Take over the professional responsibility in the area of cash and administration of the branch at Rigiplatz. With your in-depth knowledge of POS systems, you ensure an excellent shopping experience. Thanks to your motivated and inspiring nature, you contribute significantly to customer satisfaction. Does that sound like you? Then apply now!

Mansioni

  • You act as a competent contact person for our customers together with the team and take responsibility for the cash register and branch administration
  • It gives you pleasure to pass on your expertise and knowledge to new employees and introduce them in the area of cash and branch administration
  • You are responsible for ordering goods
  • You take responsibility for the efficient goods management, the attractive presentation of the products and ensure that our shelves are always well filled
  • You ensure the high quality of our products including date control
  • If necessary, you can also support in other store areas

Requirements

  • 2 years of professional experience: in retail trade/sale
  • Completed basic or further training: in sales/detail trade
  • You are customer-oriented and friendly
  • You like to work independently and reliably
  • You are stress-tolerant and have good communication skills

German (good knowledge)
