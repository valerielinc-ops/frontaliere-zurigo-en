Full description

Personal – competent – close Welcome to the Hospital Uster, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As the central pillar of health care in the Oberland region of Zurich and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you experience the highest level of expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and promoted in your professional development. Spital Uster is a teaching hospital of the Medical Faculty of the University of Zurich.Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of a "regional and digital health hub in Züri East"! The emergency department was reoriented in November 2022 into an interdisciplinary emergency department under its own medical management. We currently treat ca. 26,000 emergency patients from a rapidly growing catchment area of around 200,000 inhabitants. Fundamental changes have already taken place along this path, which are now being expanded and continued. There is currently an interdisciplinary working method of the nursing team and the team of assistant doctors. The interdisciplinary cadre doctors will act as team leaders in a modern 3 shift system (early, medium, late). This leaves time for the structural further development, project work, management tasks and further training of the nursing and assistant doctor team. A structural realignment is also planned. Our emergency department is recognized training center SGAIM Kat IV ambulatory internal medicine, SGNOR Kat II clinical emergency medicine, as well as FA POCUS emergency sonography. The further education in sonography is additionally supported by the in-house radiology.
