Full description

Sygnum's Clients team helps corporate, institutional, and individual clients navigate the breadth of our offerings across Custody, Brokerage, Credit & Lending, Token Products, Asset Management and B2B. Potential clients include private clients, asset managers, financial institutions, foundations, public and private pension funds, hedge funds, etc. We are seeking a dynamic Intern to join our fast-growing Private Banking team at our Zurich office. This is an exciting opportunity to support Sygnum in its mission to become the global partner bank of choice for digital assets. During your 6-month internship, starting in December 2026, you will play an active role in supporting our team with day-to-day operations and assisting in the preparation of client visits and management of client dossiers. Your responsibilities will include:

  • Engaging with end clients through telephone, email, and face-to-face interactions, participating in the entire client life cycle from onboarding to maintaining existing relationships.
  • Providing daily client support, initiating and monitoring client transactions, and executing trading orders.
  • Working on projects and collaborating with various internal departments, gaining exposure to different aspects of our operations.
  • A people person with a strong interest in relationship management and business development.
  • Distinct customer focus, enjoyment of direct customer contact and a strong inner drive to deliver world-class customer service.
  • Excellent communication skills in spoken and written form, both in (Swiss) German and in English.
  • Completed bachelor studies or equivalent.
  • Curiosity and enthusiasm to get to know the world of Digital Assets and gain expertise in our product and service offerings.
  • Previous working experience within banking and/or digital asset space is a plus. Joining Sygnum means being part of a dynamic, global team that is building a trusted gateway between the traditional and digital asset economies. Working at Sygnum, you will experience our fast-paced, exciting work environment that embraces meritocracy and collaboration and open communication. Alongside our ambitious long-term mission, we also come together for reaching important milestones and annual crypto-industry anniversaries like Bitcoin Pizza Day, and regularly celebrate together at themed company events as part of our journey to shape Future Finance. Sygnum offers a comprehensive package of benefits for all team members. They include:
  • Opportunity to be part of a movement shaping the future of our world’s financial system
  • Experienced leadership teams who are masters of scale and facilitate smart growth
  • Colleagues that are friendly, diverse, professional, highly skilled, international, and motivated
  • Flexible/Work at home policies
  • Professional development via Mentoring and Buddy programs If you are passionate about the potential of blockchain to shape Future Finance and your profile is a good fit for this position, please send us your CV today!
