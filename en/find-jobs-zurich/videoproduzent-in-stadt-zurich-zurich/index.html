Full description

The Multimedia Department of the City Police of Zurich is the specialist for audiovisual services and media technology. It plans and implements video and audio productions, supervises events and conference systems and supports employees with professional consulting and support services in the multimedia sector.

Tasks

  • Realization of multimedia content (learning videos, image films, spots, SoMe etc.)
  • Technical support and advice for employees of the city police regarding video technology and image processing
  • Support for multimedia technology

Profile

  • Several years of professional experience as a realiser, editor or video producer in the field of TV or film
  • Routine in the entire production process (from idea, storytelling, organization to export)
  • Very good knowledge of the Adobe product family as well as very good technical understanding
  • Stylistic German in writing and language, good knowledge of English
  • Category B driving licence
  • Readiness for occasional work assignments in the evening and on weekends

We offer

  • Interesting, varied task with a high degree of independence, careful introduction, individual training, in an experienced, motivated team
  • They are carefully and comprehensively introduced to the area of responsibility, can look forward to a modern workplace in Zurich-City
  • Attractive employment conditions such as mobile work, targeted education and training, purchase of additional holidays, etc.
  • Assumption of 60% of PK savings contributions, mobility contribution of up to CHF 600 per year and a maximum of CHF 100 per month in the form of lunch checks (depending on workload)
  • Cheap mobile subscriptions (private use included) as well as other interesting Fringe Benefits

About us

The city police is a service department of the security department of the city of Zurich and, with around 2250 employees, the third largest police corps in Switzerland.
