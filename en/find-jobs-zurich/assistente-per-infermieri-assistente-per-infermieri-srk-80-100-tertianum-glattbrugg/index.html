Full description

The Tertianum Bubenholz in Glattbrugg offers 59 barrier-free senior apartments with à la carte services and 43 single nursing rooms. The Bubenholz is only 200 meters from the town center and offers the best conditions for life, living and care in old age.

We are looking for someone by appointment

Nursing assistant / nursing assistant SRK 80-100%

Your tasks

  • Supervision and accompaniment of the assigned guests
  • Support with personal care, exercise and other everyday activities. Offering our guests everyday activities with creative activities, walks, etc.
  • Taking on tasks as a living reference person according to instructions
  • Participate in care reports and forward important observations Write care entries in our care documentation system CareCoach (mobile possible with CareCoach smartphone)
  • Assisting with logistical and housekeeping activities

Your profile

  • Completed SRK nursing course with completed internship in long-term care
  • Professional experience in long-term care an advantage
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoy a variety of challenges
  • Knowledge of spoken and written German
