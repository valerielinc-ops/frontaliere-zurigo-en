Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

Job Summary The Product Development Engineer is responsible for improving or developing new products, components, equipment, systems, technologies, or processes. This role ensures that research and design methodologies meet scientific and engineering standards and supports the organization in achieving technical, functional, and cost targets. This job profile is intended for early-career professionals who apply broad theoretical knowledge and require the ability to investigate and analyze problems. Solutions are typically discussed/verified with a more senior member. Purpose of the job The Engineering Tools Group has the responsibility to provide the global engineering departments with software solutions supporting engineering processes. To complete our team, we are looking for a motivated and committed NXOpen engineer for development of NX Tools applications.

Main tasks and responsibilities

  1. Programs new and extends existing software in C#.net for generation of 3D models and 2D drawings on the NX Open Interface and follows an existing programming concept.
  2. Analyses and debugs program errors up to final solution.
  3. Documents and demonstrates solutions by developing documentation, code comments and clear code.
  4. Updates job knowledge by studying NX related 3D development tools and programming techniques.
  5. Maintains software by studying user needs, conferring with users, studying systems flow, data usage, and work processes.
  6. Development follows the software development lifecycle.

Accomplishes engineering and organization mission by completing related tasks as needed

Qualifications/Experience and Knowledge Required

  • Bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering with a flair for software development
  • Minimum 5 years of proven experience in mechanical engineering
  • 3 years of experience in developing NXOpen applications using C#
  • Experience in software design, programming, debugging, documentation and testing
  • Exposure to Agile software development methodology and Azure DevOps is beneficial.
  • Experience with 3D and 2D parametric CAD and expression handling
  • Minimum 2 years of experience in surface modelling.
  • Information analysis, structured problem solving, efficient communication and teamwork.
  • Good English skills (written and verbal) required.
  • Experience in Turbo Machinery Engineering or willingness to develop in this domain
