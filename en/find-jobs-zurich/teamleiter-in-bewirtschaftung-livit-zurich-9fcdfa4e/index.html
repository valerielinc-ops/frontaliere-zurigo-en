Full description

Management Zurich | Pensum: 100%

Are you open-minded, ambitious and value a future-oriented way of working on the pulse of the real estate industry? At Livit, you are committed to the concerns of owners and tenants with an exemplary customer orientation and thus contribute significantly to a trusting, long-lasting customer relationship and a positive corporate image. Are you ready to tackle the challenges of today and tomorrow and make a difference in the real estate world? Then you are exactly right with Livit. We look forward to your personal contribution.

Your tasks

  • You are responsible for the goal-oriented leadership of the team, for ensuring quality as well as for personnel planning and promotion of employees
  • You are responsible for market-compliant management and ensuring the maintenance of the real estate portfolio consisting of residential and commercial premises
  • You advise owners and tenants, create and negotiate challenging contracts and control the facility managers
  • You are responsible for ensuring, executing and accompaniing maintenance as well as for periodic assessments of the condition incl. Reporting
  • You develop and control the annual budget incl. Forecast and control the accounting of creditors
  • You issue repair and renovation orders and monitor their execution
  • You monitor the current rental policy and advise homeowners on how to set the rental rate
  • You make lease contract changes, rental rate changes, and indexed rental rate adjustments

Your profile

  • You have an oath. Real estate management or an Eidg. Diploma Immobilientreuhand
  • You have many years of professional experience in the real estate industry, special knowledge in rental law as well as construction and marketing knowledge
  • Your mother tongue is German and you can advantageously communicate verbally and in writing in other languages
  • You have empathy and the ability to perform together as a team
  • You think and act entrepreneurially and have the pleasure of shaping the Livit
  • You live the values of customer orientation, communicate professionally and enjoy dealing with people
  • You have a high level of social and self-competence with the ability to self-control
  • You are digitally affine, have self-learning skills and enjoy sharing your knowledge
