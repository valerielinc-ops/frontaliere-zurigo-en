Full description

In the spring of 2021, the new residential and care centre Tertianum Giessenturm in Dübendorf opened its doors. The imposing high-rise is located in an urban location and is part of the Giessen area. There are 80 modern apartments for senior citizens and 60 rooms in the care department. Amenities also include a public bistro, physical therapy, massage and a coffee shop.

Your heart beats for older people? Is the nursing profession your vocation which for you not only improves the quality of life of others, but also creates a satisfaction and meaningfulness in your own life? Do you see opportunities to use your skills and experience with us to make a positive impact on our customers’ lives? Then you are right with us!

For the Tertianum Giessenturm in Dübendorf we are looking for immediately or by agreement one(s)

Nursing assistant SRK 80%-100 %

Their tasks

  • Assistance and accompaniment of allotted guests
  • Support in personal care, movement and other everyday activities Offering everyday life for our guests with creative activities, walks, etc.
  • Assumption of tasks as a reference person Living according to guidance
  • Participation in nursing reports and forwarding of important observations Nursing entries write in our nursing documentation system CareCoach
  • Assistance in logistical and domestic activities

Your profile

  • Completed SRC nursing course with completed internship in long-term care
  • Professional experience in long-term care
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude
  • Enjoying multiple challenges
  • German skills in word and writing
