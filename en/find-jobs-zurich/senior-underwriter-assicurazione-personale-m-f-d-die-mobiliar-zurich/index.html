Full description

This is what awaits you

As a market development unit of Mobiliar, the Management of Brokerage Business in Zurich is responsible for the partnership with professional brokers. In our department market development personal insurance we are looking for a reinforcement or a senior underwriter personal insurance.

This is what you do with us

As a senior underwriter, you examine risks in the UVG, UVG supplement and collective sickness allowance insurance sectors as part of your competencies. You are responsible for creating the offers and policies and have your own portfolio.

In addition, you develop concepts, conduct negotiations, are responsible for the impeccable, administrative handling and make a direct contribution to the company's success through your high service quality and willingness towards the brokers.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed basic training in insurance, further training at the level of professional card/diploma of insurance and good business knowledge
  • In-depth several years of experience and in-depth knowledge in personal insurance and underwriting
  • Team-oriented, initiative and communicative personality who convinces through negotiating skills and assertiveness
  • Independent, initiative and dynamic working methods with a pronounced customer orientation, high self-responsibility and motivation in personal dealings with our agents
  • Open-minded, reliable and solution-oriented working method
  • native language German, knowledge in other languages is advantageous

Working with us

The workload is negotiable from 80% and your place of work is Zurich Oerlikon. A motivated and customer-oriented team is waiting for you. Together we attach great importance to a communicative and solution-oriented working atmosphere.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
