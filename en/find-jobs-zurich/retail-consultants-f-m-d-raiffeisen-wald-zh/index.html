Full description

At Raiffeisenbank am Ricken, people are at the centre of attention – our customers as well as our employees. We meet them with trust and take their concerns seriously. This is how we create space for partnership-based and individual solutions that offer real added value – today and tomorrow.

We are committed to our region with conviction. And because we think forward, we are working with our employees and our members to create a sustainable future.

To expand our PKI team at our office in Wald ZH, we are looking for an entrepreneurial, committed and customer-oriented personality as a private customer consultant (w/m/d).

Tasks

  • Identifying and exploiting the customer potential through active and continuous support of the personal customer portfolio
  • Ensure competent and holistic advice on all financial issues (financing, investing, pension provision, saving)
  • Development of solutions that meet the needs of the clientele, including individual needs, wishes, goals and living conditions
  • Active expansion of the bank's existing customer portfolio through targeted new customer search
  • Cooperation in target group-specific Mark processing measures such as contact campaigns, customer events and representative tasks
  • To bring in our own ideas and impulses for the further development and co-design of our bank

Requirements

  • Completed banking training and further training in banking or finance (e.g. financial planner with Eidg. FA)
  • Multi-annual experience in private customer consulting
  • Very good knowledge in the areas of financing, investment and provisioning
  • Safe appearance and very good communication skills
  • Team-capable and entrepreneurial personality with a strong customer orientation
  • Solution-oriented, independent and connected thinking

We offer: The cooperative values are our cornerstones: together. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.
