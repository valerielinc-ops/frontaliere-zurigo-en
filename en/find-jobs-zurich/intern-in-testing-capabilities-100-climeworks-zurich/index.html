Original description

  • Measurement instrumentation and controls development : You actively participate in the development of measurement instrumentation and controls development of our direct air capture testing infrastructure.
  • Automation software support : You assist with the development of our automation software to ensure consistency between facilities and to improve efficiency.
  • Process investigation : You assist with investigations into the adsorption / desorption process to enhance our understanding and improve efficiency.
  • Test execution and evaluation : You execute various testing methods and critically evaluate the test data to ensure accurate and reliable results.
  • Feasibility assessment : You assess the feasibility of different measurement methods based on test outcomes and practical applications, providing recommendations for improvements or alternative approaches.

  • Programming skills : You possess sound knowledge of at least one high-level programming language (e.g., Python, C++) and ideally have experience with PLC programming and/or industrial communication protocols.
  • Learning motivation : You desire to expand your knowledge in measurement technology, industrial process controls, and the development of software.
  • Work style : You are reliable, performance-driven, hands-on, and maintain a structured working style. Additionally, you thrive in both fast-paced team environments and when working independently.
  • Language skills : You are fluent in English.
  • Availability : You are available to start on October 1st, 2026, for a 6-month internship.
