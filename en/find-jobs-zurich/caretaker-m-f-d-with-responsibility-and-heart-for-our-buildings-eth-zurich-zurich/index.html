Caretaker:in (m/f/d) – With responsibility and heart for our buildings

100%, Zurich, perpetual

In the Facility Services department of ETH Zurich, we create the optimal conditions for teaching, research and technology transfer. We maintain the buildings and ensure the energy and media supply as well as the technical and infrastructural management of all rooms used by ETH Zurich.

You are skilled in craftsmanship, think in a solution-oriented way and keep an overview even in turbulent moments? Then shape the future of infrastructural building management at ETH Zurich with us!

For the Hönggerberg location, we are looking for a:n janitor:in (m/f/d) in the building area HI.

Job description

In this versatile position, you support the management of infrastructural building management in everyday operations and make a significant contribution to ensuring that our buildings are operated smoothly, efficiently and user-oriented.

You independently carry out repairs, controls and maintenance and are the first point of contact for users and external service providers. You recognize optimization potential and actively contribute your ideas.

Your tasks include minor repair and maintenance work in the areas of painting, carpentry, sanitation and assembly. In addition, you regularly check the building infrastructure, identify defects at an early stage and initiate the necessary measures. You are also responsible for setting up and dismantling events, advising users and supporting them in the operation of multimedia technology.

The implementation of the disposal concept and ensuring a high cleaning quality are also part of your area of responsibility. You ensure this both through your own assignments and through the coordination of external service providers.

As an important interface between technology, service providers and users, you ensure high service quality and customer satisfaction. You accompany events, coordinate missions and take responsibility for a smooth process. In addition, you provide piquetting services and take on tasks in shift operations, such as opening and closing buildings.

You process tickets in the CAFM system, collect offers and coordinate or accompany external specialists in their assignments. In close cooperation with the technical building management, you will support with initial interventions as well as with maintenance work on house technical systems. In addition, you contribute to budgeting and support in the creation and maintenance of the maintenance plan.

You also play a central role in the area of safety: You ensure personal and operational safety, supervise fire alarm systems and security areas, respond to alarms, provide first aid in an emergency and support the BMA and EVAK equipment.

In your function as a practice instructor: in addition, you accompany and support the learning specialist company maintenance in everyday work and ensure a technically sound and practical training.

Profile

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as a specialist / employee EFZ or equivalent training e.g. as a housekeeper / in FA or equivalent further education
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in a comparable function
  • Good knowledge of maintenance (e.g. assembly, carpentry, painting, sanitary work)
  • Good MS-Office and CAFM knowledge as well as experience in dealing with audio, video and presentation technology
  • Very good German and English skills
  • Responsibility, flexibility and an independent and structured working method
  • Communication strength, teamwork and enjoyment of working with different stakeholders
  • Openness to innovation and new technologies
  • Readiness for piquetting services and assignments outside regular working hours
  • Experience and pleasure in the practical support of learners in the profession of specialist company maintenance
