Team We are a vibrant, fast-growing tech company, and we are looking for an enthusiastic, organized, and proactive Office & Internal Events Manager to be the heartbeat of our Zurich office. Our team is spread across key global hubs: Paris, Lyon (our Headquarter) , Zurich , Krakow , and the US . In Zurich, our office is transitioning. Our previous team members are moving on to new adventures, one leaving the company and the other transitioning into an internal communications role. As a result, you will become the central point of contact for our Zurich hub of around 40 to 50 people. This is a crucial role where you will bridge day-to-day office management with internal event planning to foster a strong, connected team culture.

Missions

  1. Office Management & Reception
  • Front-of-House: Welcome and register visitors, ensure they are comfortable, and guide them to their meetings.
  • Daily Operations: Keep the reception, cafeteria, and communal office areas tidy, professional, and fully stocked (supplies, food, and beverages).
  • Communication Hub: Act as the first point of contact for office-related questions. Answer the main phone line for both our Zurich and Berlin offices in German, and English.
  • Logistics & Admin: Handle incoming mail (scanning, distribution, archiving) for both offices, manage deliveries, organize couriers, and handle visitor/employee keys.
  1. Internal Events & Team Support
  • Event Planning: Organize internal and external meetings, workshops, team-building activities, seminars, and company offsites (including scheduling, venue booking, catering, and reminders).
  • Travel Coordination: Help coordinate travel logistics, occasionally utilizing tools like TravelPerk to assist candidates, customers, and team members.
  • Onboarding & Culture: Edit and order business cards for new starters, prepare employee anniversary cards, and gather signatures for time-sensitive documents.

Skills

  • 3–4 years of experience in a similar office management and internal event role.
  • Languages: Native-level German and professional fluency in English are mandatory (both written and spoken).
  • Communication: Strong interpersonal skills with the ability to interact confidently with stakeholders at all levels of the organization.
  • Organization: Highly organized, rigorous, and able to prioritize tasks calmly in a fast-paced environment.
  • Tech Literacy: Comfortable learning and using modern digital tools. Proficiency in Google Workspace (Gmail, Calendar, Docs, Sheets) is required.

Nice-to-Have

  • Experience in the tech industry or a high-growth environment.
  • Fluency in French.
  • Familiarity with tools like Canva, TravelPerk, Spendesk, or NetSuite.

Conditions

  • Contract: full time contract 80% (Monday to Thursday)
  • Location & Remote Work: Fully on-site role in Zurich. No remote work possible.
  • Schedule: Monday to Thursday
