Full description

Actively shape the future of health centers for old age! In our innovation and processes team, you can expect a versatile task with a lot of room for manoeuvre: You can advance exciting projects, develop the internal control system (ICS) and risk management and set new impulses together with us.

Tasks

  • They ensure the operation and further development of the internal control system (ICS), opportunity and risk management as well as business continuity management (BCM), ensure compliance with city regulations and produce meaningful reports for the management.
  • Through targeted optimizations, you increase the impact of these instruments and align them consistently with the strategic priorities of the organization.
  • They lead and accompany strategically relevant, cross-divisional projects and sub-projects, from the management of stakeholder groups to the sustainable anchoring of new solutions and processes.
  • They strengthen project management competence, advise project leaders and executives and promote the exchange of knowledge and experience within the organization.
  • As part of the Innovation and Processes team, you will provide impetus for innovation, digitalisation and quality management and develop viable concepts and decision-making principles.

Profile

  • You are an initiative, self-responsible and implementation-strong personality who likes to take responsibility, actively promotes topics and keeps track of challenging issues.
  • They have in-depth knowledge of the internal control system (ICS) and risk management and have experience in applying and developing these tools
  • You have experience managing cross-divisional projects, being enthusiastic about change and understanding how to bring different people and interests together
  • They have a university degree or higher specialist training, preferably with a business background. Additional training in project management, IKS or risk management is an advantage
  • They think analytically and networked, act pragmatically and solution-oriented and convince through clear communication and understand how to win others for common goals

We offer

An exciting task area in a professional and team-oriented working environment with many design possibilities. The human being is at our center. That is why we support the reconciliation of private and professional life, different working models make it possible.

As part of the city of Zurich and due to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, progressive employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

Depending on the experience, the annual salary for this function is between Fr. 113'388.- and Fr. 151'510.- (100%).

About us

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

The central services division is located in Zurich-Oerlikon and provides various services within the entire service department. These include quality management, computer science (technical IT), finance, HR, marketing, communication, building management, construction development and central purchasing.
