Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,300 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland. For the Senevita Alterszentrum Gehren in Erlenbach we are looking for a residential area manager on 1 November 2026 or by appointment in 80-100 % Your tasks

  • You lead employees professionally and personnel and ensure the care processes of your department (21 residents)
  • You lead by example every day and motivate your team
  • Based on the care concept, you coordinate and organize daily care
  • You ensure the flow of information to the residents, relatives and the care team

You bring that with you

  • An education as a Dipl. Nurse HF / FH or SRK recognized
  • Mind. 2 years of management experience in a similar position, with appropriate training or the willingness to undertake leadership training (leadership or similar)
  • Experience in long-term care and dealing with demented people
  • You think economically and have good RAI knowledge
  • Good computer skills

We offer you

  • High degree of flexibility (e.g. workload, service planning) as well as the opportunity to contribute your own idea and develop further
  • Attractive further education offered by the Senevita Academy, which inspires ongoing development
  • An environment where vocational training is written GROSS and brings a breath of fresh air into the house
  • Free drinks and reduced food - heartily prepared by our gastro team
  • Perks (SBB half-ax, fueling, supplementary insurance, fitness, well-known brands, etc.)
  • Good social benefits and at least. 5 weeks holiday (from 50 years 6 weeks, from 60 years 7 weeks)
  • Very good accessibility (direct motorway connection, location directly at the station Erlenbach)

Our commitment: You as a person are important to us!
