Sanatorium Kilchberg offers you a fulfilling working environment with exciting development opportunities in a dynamic and appreciative team. Contribute your strengths and help shape the future of the clinic.

Your tasks

  • Participation in medical admission and follow-up interviews
  • Conducting follow-up discussions under direct supervision by the person in charge of the case
  • Support with physical examinations
  • Accompaniment at assistant medical services
  • Documentation support
  • Participation in the internal training program
  • Participation in reports You can find more detailed information about the internship here (section "Medical sub-assistants").

Your profile

  • Enrolment at a university (EU/EFTA area) as a student in human medicine (work with us as part of the 5. Elective year / practical year, 6. Academic year)
  • Swiss citizenship or citizenship from the EU/EFTA area
  • German language skills of at least level C1
  • Open, outgoing and interested personality
  • High motivation and willingness to learn
  • Organizational and communication skills
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Ability to work in a teamNote: We do not offer electives, but you can find information about living and working in Switzerland here if required.

The Sanatorium Kilchberg is one of the most traditional private psychiatric clinics in Switzerland and looks back on almost 160 years of history. Only 15 minutes from Zurich city centre with a beautiful view of Lake Zurich and the Alps. As a listed hospital, we offer comprehensive psychiatric care for all insured persons and have inpatient and outpatient facilities in Kilchberg and Zurich. Our approximately 650 employees from over 25 professional groups are the heart of the clinic and are committed to providing the best possible treatment for our patients. Become part of our team and help shape the future of psychiatric care.
