Full description

Your tasks This position is to be filled on 01.12.2026 or 01.01.2027.

  • You support the commercial and technical management of a versatile real estate portfolio and ensure smooth processes in the background.
  • You actively accompany the re-letting – from the coordination and implementation of visits to communication with interested parties.
  • You create rental contracts, attachments and supplements for apartments and ancillary objects and ensure clean contract documentation.
  • You organize and accompany apartment and property acceptances as well as transfers and take over these partly independently.
  • You create tenant final reports and support with further administrative tasks relating to the tenancy.
  • You support debtor and creditor management while keeping track of open processes.
  • You are an important contact person for tenants, craftsmen, suppliers, offices and housekeepers – both in writing and orally.
  • You take on a variety of administrative tasks and relieve the managers in day-to-day business.
  • You are actively involved, think and tackle when it comes to making processes efficient and customer-oriented.

Your requirements

  • Successful graduation of real estate manager: in with federal professional certificate
  • Several years of experience in residential property management
  • Stylistic German in Word and Writing
  • Good IT application knowledge (MS Office programs, GREM, etc.)
  • Charismatic appearance and strong social skills
  • Valid driving licence and own vehicle are required

Look forward to a dynamic team, short decision-making paths and an environment in which cooperation is really lived. Interested? Then apply now with your complete application documents including your salary expectations.
