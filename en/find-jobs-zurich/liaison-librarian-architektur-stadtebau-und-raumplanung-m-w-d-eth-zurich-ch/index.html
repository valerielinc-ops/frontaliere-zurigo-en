Liaison Librarian Architecture, Urban Design and Spatial Planning (m/f/d)

80%, Zurich, perpetual

The ETH Library (www.library.ethz.ch), the Swiss competence center for technical-scientific information, searches for the building library

  1. December 2026 or by appointment one:n Liaison Librarian Architecture, Urbanism and Spatial Planning (d/f/m).

Project background

Your place of work is the building library on the Hönggerberg campus, one of the world’s most renowned special libraries for the fields of architecture, urban design, spatial planning and civil engineering.

The library offers a diverse range of courses and consulting services for students, lecturers and researchers. Our goal is to promote teaching and research, an extensive range of physical media and digital resources, bring our expertise to project collaborations and provide an inspiring work and learning environment.

Job description

We are looking for an independent and motivated personality who supports us in the further development of library specialist services, in project cooperations and in the development of stocks. This job offer is explicitly aimed at people who enjoy actively participating in the application of new digital technologies, especially in the context of business development, research and knowledge management.

Your tasks

  • Professional support as Liaison Librarian in the fields of architecture, urban design and spatial planning including stock development, content development and the needs-oriented, strategic development of the offers
  • Contact person for students, researchers and staff
  • You contribute to a smooth library operation and continuously develop our services together as a team
  • Maintaining and expanding a professional network within and outside ETH Zurich and working closely with departments to strengthen the visibility and benefit of library services in research and teaching
  • Participation in projects to further develop library services with a particular focus on digital innovation and the use of artificial intelligence
  • You are actively involved in designing new offers and bringing your own ideas to the further development of the ETH Library

Profile

  • University academic degree (master) in architecture, urban design, spatial planning or a related discipline
  • Additional library or information science training is an advantage
  • Professional experience in the scientific environment, ideally in a scientific library
  • Strong communication and consulting skills as well as pleasure in establishing and maintaining professional networks
  • Affinity to digital technologies and interest in the potential of artificial intelligence for research, teaching and libraries
  • Very good knowledge of German and English

We offer

  • A varied activity and a dynamic working environment in the exciting context of research and teaching
  • Working in a committed and collegial team
  • An attractive workplace at ETH Zurich Hönggerberg
  • Cultural diversity and attractive offers and benefits
  • Family-friendly working conditions
  • We live lifelong learning
