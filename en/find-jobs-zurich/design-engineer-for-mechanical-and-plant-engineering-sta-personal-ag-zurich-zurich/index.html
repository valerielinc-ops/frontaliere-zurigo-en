Full description

The focus is on the development of 3D designs and 2D drawings in a modern working environment. Collaboration with internal departments and suppliers enables a wide range of technical solutions. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Create 3D designs and 2D drawings for machines and plants
  • Elaborate R&I schemes and dimension components and assemblies
  • Smaller development projects independently plan and implement
  • Coordinate technical solutions with suppliers and internal specialist departments

Requirements

  • Completed training as a constructor or equivalent
  • Several years of experience in machinery or plant engineering
  • Very good 3D CAD knowledge, ideally with Autodesk Inventor
  • Good knowledge of English as well as exact and independent working methods
