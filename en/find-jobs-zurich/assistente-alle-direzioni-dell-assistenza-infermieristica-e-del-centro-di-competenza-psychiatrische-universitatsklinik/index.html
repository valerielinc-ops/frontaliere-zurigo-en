Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 90% The Management of Care, Therapies and Social Work (Directorate PTS) is looking for a service-oriented and committed personality for the management of the Care and Competence Center, from February 2027 or by agreement, at the Lenggstrasse location. Their tasks

  • Support of management Care and competence center in all administrative and organizational matters
  • Organisation of meetings, committees and events incl. Preparation and post-processing
  • Writing of protocols and tracking of the pendant list
  • Management of various storage systems
  • Preparation of various evaluations
  • Cooperation with other administration teams within the PUK

Your profile

  • Completed commercial education and experience in a similar position
  • Strong organisational and communication skills
  • Strong professional, social and self-competence as well as discretion
  • Independent, efficient and service-oriented working method with strong networked thinking and action
  • Sound IT skills
  • Qualification as a vocational trainer and experience in the training of CV learners

Our offer

  • Strong in the team: appreciative cooperation and mutual support
  • Focus on health: Various sports offerings, green oases and an internal care team
  • Future provision with added value: 60% employer contribution to the pension fund
  • Sustainable travel: Participation in the ZVV-BonusPass or general subscription
  • Well supplied in everyday work: Discounted catering offers
  • Combining family and work: access to the company’s own crèche
