Hirslanden stands for first-class, responsible and individual health care that puts people first. From birth to old age as well as from prevention to healing – at any time, physically and digitally – the Hirslanden Group is concentrating on the further development of the “Continuum of Care” together with private and public partners.

In this versatile and responsible function, you will continue to supervise and develop our SAP financial applications and the associated systems together in a team. You ensure a stable, integrated and compliance-compliant operation and thus make a significant contribution to the further development of our financial processes and application landscape.

YOUR TASKS

  • Support, operation and further development of SAP financial applications, in particular SAP FI, CO, AA, PS, IM and RE-FX, as well as the SAP add-ons used
  • Overall responsibility for the application management of the connected systems and other financial applications over their entire life cycle
  • Ensuring stable end-to-end operations including monitoring, interfaces, data flows, permissions and 2nd and 3rd level support
  • Advising the financial and controlling areas as well as processing and coordination of requirements, incidents, changes and functional developments
  • Planning and coordination of releases, updates, tests and deployments
  • Participation in SAP and financial transformation projects as well as assumption of sub-project management including documentation, handover and sustainable support in the company

Your profile

  • Completed ICT training (FH/uni) or equivalent qualification
  • Several years of professional experience as SAP Financial Application Manager or Consultant
  • Experience in application operation as well as in 2nd and 3rd level support
  • Experience with interfaces and server support
  • Structured, independent and solution-oriented working method
  • High service and customer orientation as well as strong quality awareness
  • Very good German and English skills, French is an advantage
