Place of work: Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Composition by: 01. January 2027 or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 74983

Are you enthusiastic about recruiting and are you looking for a versatile healthcare task? Then you are exactly right with us!

As an HR recruitment partner, you accompany managers in attracting qualified employees. You bring people and suitable positions together, develop individual recruitment strategies and ensure a professional application process – from the first contact to the job. You combine your feeling for people with a structured way of working and contribute to attracting the right talent for the Hirslanden clinic.

YOUR TASKS

  • You are responsible for the entire recruitment process for professionals and managers – from the recruitment order to the first working day – in close cooperation with the line and the central HR Service Center.
  • You advise and accompany executives and develop suitable recruitment strategies together with them.
  • You design appealing job advertisements and strengthen our employer image.
  • You control applicant selection, conduct interviews and provide a professional, appreciative candidate experience.
  • You conduct contract negotiations, coordinate the conclusion of the contract and perform the associated administrative tasks.

Your profile

  • Commercial basic training with HR further education or a relevant university degree, for example in work and organizational psychology or business administration
  • At least 1-2 years of experience in recruitment as well as experience in healthcare or clinic environment
  • Strong social skills, service orientation and a secure presence
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented way of working and the ability to set priorities correctly
  • Good MS Office skills; SAP and SuccessFactors knowledge is an advantage
  • Very good oral and written language

You can expect a committed HR team that lives cooperation, initiative and professional exchange. You can contribute your recruiting skills, take on responsibility and develop professionally and personally. In addition, you benefit from attractive training opportunities, progressive employment conditions and comprehensive social benefits. With our leisure plus model, up to seven weeks of holidays are also possible.
