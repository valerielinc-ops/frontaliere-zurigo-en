Full description

Your role Are you passionate about software engineering, Java development, and modern technologies? Are you curious about how electronic trading systems work and excited about the opportunities AI brings to software delivery? We would love to meet you.

In this role, you will

  • contribute to the development and maintenance of our trading connectivity platform, primarily using Java
  • work closely with Software Engineers, Test Engineers, and Product Owners to deliver business requirements
  • participate in backlog refinement, sprint planning, releases, and production support activities
  • assist in troubleshooting and issue analysis with guidance from experienced team members
  • learn and apply AI-assisted engineering practices across the software development lifecycle

Grow your understanding of financial markets, trading workflows, and industry-standard protocols such as FIX and SWIFT

Your team Our team develops and maintains TCP/IP connectivity solutions linking clients, market venues, and internal trading platforms. These interfaces support 24/6 electronic trading across Equities, Fixed Income, ETFs, Derivatives, Structured Products, and more. The platform is used globally across UBS and by external institutional clients. Our team operates from Switzerland, Pune, and Singapore, offering a truly international and collaborative environment. We are also at the forefront of AI-assisted software engineering, continuously exploring how AI can improve the way we design, build, test, and operate software.
