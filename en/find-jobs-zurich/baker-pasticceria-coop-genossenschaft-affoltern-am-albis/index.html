Tasks

  • Thanks to our open bakery, you will also bring your passion to our customers.
  • On site you produce breads, baked goods, patisserie and pastry products. Your craft is in the foreground.
  • With customer wishes, you can incorporate your creativity, advise them and prepare the orders accordingly.

Requirements

  • You have already gained professional experience in a bakery or, ideally, a completed basic education as a baker:in or confectioner:in.
  • You are a friendly and team-oriented personality with a well-groomed demeanor.
  • You appreciate the close customer exchange and are happy to advise for your life.
  • You master German well: verbally and in writing.
  • You consistently implement quality specifications and guidelines.

  • Training: Over 600 internal training courses, talent management with a wide range of development opportunities, support for further education and training offers from the LGAV and Coop
  • Sustainability: Commitment to people, animals and nature with more than 400 actions
