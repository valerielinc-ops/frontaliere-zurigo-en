Main responsibilities

  • Responsibility for the architecture, further development and sustainable design of our SAP EWM solution landscape (Decentralised EWM, SAP S/4HANA)
  • Analysis of business requirements in the areas of logistics, warehouse logistics, production and supply chain management as well as translation into scalable solutions.
  • Hands-on Analysis, conception and implementation of solutions in a highly developed SAP system landscape – with active work in SAP customization and development in ABAP.
  • Definition and assurance of architectural, integration and development standards, as well as the release of solution designs and technical specifications.
  • Management and professional management of external consulting, implementation and development partners.
  • Identification and implementation of process optimizations, automation potentials and support in 2nd and 3rd level support.
  • Active cooperation in international projects, training of key users and close cooperation with internal SAP and system experts.

Skills required

  • Completed studies in business computer science, computer science, business administration or comparable qualifications.
  • Well-founded practical experience in the SAP EWM environment, ideally with decentralized EWM on SAP S/4HANA.
  • Strong experience in architecture, design, configuration of complex EWM solutions.
  • Several years of practical experience in ABAP development, ideally with the help of the software architecture of the ABAP developer/software.
  • Understanding adjacent SAP logistics processes and experience with grown, highly developed SAP system landscapes.
  • Expressed hands-on mentality and willingness to implement solutions in SAP Customizing and ABAP itself.
  • Self-employed, analytical working methods and very good English skills. Very good German skills are a plus.

About Belimo

Belimo is the world leader in the development, production and distribution of field equipment for energy-efficient control of heating, ventilation and air conditioning systems. Valve drives, control valves, sensors and counters form our core business. Founded in 1975, Belimo has been listed on the Swiss Stock Exchange (SIX) since 1995.
