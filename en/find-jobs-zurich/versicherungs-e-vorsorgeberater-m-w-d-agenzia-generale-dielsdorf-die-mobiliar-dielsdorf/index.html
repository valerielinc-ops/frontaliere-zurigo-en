What awaits you

In this position, you are the trusted contact for your customers and build long-term relationships that last.

This is what they do with us

You gain new customers with your personality, your consulting skills and your excellent network. Thanks to your many years of experience, you create trust, build sustainable customer relationships and continuously develop them.

As a competent contact person, you advise your customers holistically in the areas of insurance, provision and assets. You recognize potential, think in a network and accompany your customers over many years with individual solutions.

Through your strong market presence, your good reputation and your high degree of financial strength, you make a significant contribution to the sustainable growth of our general agency.

This is what you bring with you

  • Several years of successful experience in insurance and pension consulting
  • A demonstrable track record in the acquisition and sustainable expansion of customer relationships
  • Excellent networking in the region and the ability to attract new customers through recommendations and personal contacts
  • High level of expertise and enjoyment of holistic and needs-oriented advice
  • A winning, trustworthy personality with strong graduate strength and entrepreneurial thinking
  • Self-employed, responsible and customer-oriented working methods
  • Stylistic German in word and writing
  • Further language skills are advantageous

Working with us

An established market area awaits you with a lot of entrepreneurial room for manoeuvre, short decision paths and a motivated team that supports you professionally.

They benefit from attractive employment conditions, modern work equipment and performance-oriented remuneration. We offer you the opportunity to contribute your experience and your network in a successful environment and to further expand it in the long term.

For the exercise of this function, according to the Insurance Supervision Act, the insurance intermediary/in VBV, Profile Allbranche, is required.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
