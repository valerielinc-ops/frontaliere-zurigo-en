Job description

In our post office, you are the central hub for everything that comes and goes – from letters and packages to the digitization of documents in the scanning center. You ensure that day-to-day operations run smoothly and support us with administrative tasks. The workload is 40% and is divided from Monday to Friday between 05:40 and 09:00.

What awaits you with us? A diverse everyday life in which you...

  • roughly pre-sort the incoming letter mail and assign it to the correct departments or locations,
  • registers parcel shipments in the PostOffice system and allocates them correctly,
  • scan documents on time and provide them digitally,
  • administrative tasks – from statistics and consignment searches to the archiving of documents

Profile

  • Completed basic education (BP/FA) in postal or logistics (advantageous)
  • Several years of experience in post-processing or a comparable function
  • Administrative experience
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Communication strength and ability to communicate knowledge in an understandable way
  • Good MS-Office knowledge
  • Excellent knowledge of German

You are an early riser, also handle heavy shipments easily and appreciate a versatile, varied working environment? Then you are a great fit for us.

You work independently and responsibly, actively participate and contribute to the further development of processes. You live our ETH values: You communicate respectfully, act responsibly and share your knowledge with others.
