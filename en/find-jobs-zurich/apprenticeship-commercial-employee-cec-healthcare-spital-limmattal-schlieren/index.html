Kauffrau/Kaufmann (KV) Health

Your KV-Teaching at the Limmattal Hospital - versatile, practical and future-oriented

Apply now

You don't just want to sit in your office, but also experience how a hospital works? Then you are exactly right for us! During your three-year training as a saleswoman/businessman EFZ you will get to know the diverse commercial areas of a modern hospital business. You work in different departments, take responsibility and gain valuable experience for your professional future. From the patient's (Danish) and financial resources to personnel, purchasing and logistics.

Here you get involved

During your training you will learn about different departments and their tasks. Step by step you will take more responsibility and actively contribute to everyday work.

  • patient reception and settlement including tariff system
  • creditor and debitor accounting
  • main accounting including payment transactions
  • general secretarial and administrative tasks
  • cooperation in logistics, purchasing and central warehouse
  • support in various administrative work
  • getting to know the various processes and areas of a hospital operation

Requirements

You are curious, want to learn new things and enjoy working with people. You also bring:

  • Secondary level A or district school
  • Very good performance in the main subjects
  • interest in commercial activities and health care
  • Good computer skills and pleasure in handling numbers
  • An established, friendly and communicative personality
  • Openness and joy in contact with people
  • High willingness to learn and initiative
  • joy in working with people of different cultures and professional groups
  • Very good oral and written German knowledge

We offer

You will learn about the commercial profession directly in the varied hospital routine. During your apprenticeship you will gain insights into different departments and can apply your knowledge in practice. You will be introduced step by step to new tasks and will be given the opportunity to take on your own responsibility. This will not only help you to know the commercial processes, but also understand how the various areas of a hospital play together and make sure that everything works together.

  • A varied and varied training in different departments
  • Practical insights into the health care system
  • possibility to actively participate and assume responsibility
  • participation in compulsory school materials
  • extensive introductory weeks
  • various changing advantages
