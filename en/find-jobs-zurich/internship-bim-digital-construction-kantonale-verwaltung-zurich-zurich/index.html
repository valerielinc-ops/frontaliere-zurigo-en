Full description

As an intern, would you like to help shape the future of the Building Construction Office in the field of BIM? We are currently creating the necessary foundations at the Building Construction Office to support larger construction projects with the BIM method from 2028 onwards. In this internship, you work at the interface of construction, BIM, data, IT and the organization to ensure that BIM does not only work on paper. If you are enthusiastic about both organizational and methodological topics during this introduction as well as digital tools, then you are in the right place with us. Your tasks

  • Develop BIM use cases (e.g. CO2-Tracking & ReUse) as well as prepare, organize and post-process workshops
  • Support BIM pilot projects organizationally and technically and track their progress
  • Development of basics, standards, templates and design of community formats (e.g. Lunch & Learn)
  • Support data and document management in connection with CDE PIM and participate as a superuser in the technical onboarding of pilot projects
  • Building the PowerBI cockpit for BIM@HBA and working on an AI test case in connection with the cantonal data platform You bring that with you
  • Completed studies in architecture, civil engineering, construction management, digital construction or a related field - motivated lateral entrants with a suitable background are also welcome
  • First knowledge of PowerBI, AI, programming or BIM tools
  • Basic knowledge of specialist data catalogues, IDS and the structured modeling of BIM data are advantageous
  • Basic understanding for construction processes and SIA phases is an advantage
  • Structured and independent working methods
  • Joy of communication, collaboration and new topics You are working on a transformation project with a real impact on the construction and planning industry. You will gain insight into real construction projects, public client processes and current topics around BIM, data management and digitalization. You will work with clients, portfolio managers, architects, engineers and can quickly build up a valuable network as a young professional. And finally, you don't have to be able to do everything. Much more important is that you are curious, like to think and enjoy bringing structure into complex topics. Your contact Erik de Ruiter BIM Responsible HBA +41 43 259 28 61 You work directly with Erik, who leads the introduction of BIM at the Building Construction Office. The cooperation is pragmatic, networked, open and implementation-oriented. Our goal: to build something meaningful together, try new things and have fun at work. Dear personnel service providers!
