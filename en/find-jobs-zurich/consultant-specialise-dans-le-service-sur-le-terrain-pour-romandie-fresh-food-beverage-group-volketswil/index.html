Fachberater*in im Aussendienst für die Romandie — Fresh Food & Beverage Group

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Do you want to contribute significantly to customer satisfaction and smooth running in the Migros stores and convenience stores in the bread and baked goods sector? In our lively environment of the Migros industry in Western Switzerland, a varied activity awaits you! With passion, joy and openness as well as a goal- and result-oriented attitude, you are an ideal fit for us.

Mansioni

  • Planning and conducting customer visits to advise and ensure customer satisfaction
  • Training of staff on the ground in the field of bread and bakery products
  • Active support for new openings and reopenings
  • Conduct of baking training and product presentations
  • Technical support in the area of shop ovens and implementation of new shop ovens in Migros stores including installation and programming

Requirements

  • Professional experience: several years of professional experience in the retail trade and/or in the food industry
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): in the retail trade, as a baker-confectioner, cook or an equivalent education
  • Maintained appearance, independent, flexible and solution-oriented working method with high communication competence and empathy
  • High technical affinity and solid IT knowledge
  • Residence in Romandie (proximity to the customer) and willingness to travel intensively in the car (driving license mandatory)
  • First experience in the field service is desired
  • French (negotiable)
  • German (good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Employee offers: Employee Points Cumulus, Discount Mobile Subscriptions, Higher Interest Rates Migros Bank, Club School/Fitness contributions and more.
  • Occupational pension provision: Comprehensively secured in the Migros pension fund – with first-class and attractive services.
  • Education and training: We actively train employees as part of internal programs and, if necessary, support external training with time and money.
  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks paid maternity leave (instead of 14 weeks), 4 weeks paternity leave (instead of 2 weeks).
  • Social benefits: 730 days of sick leave with full net wages.
  • Holidays: 5 weeks holiday for all, 6 weeks holiday from 50 years of age, 7 weeks holiday from 60 years of age, additional purchase of additional holiday days possible.
