Role overview

Il tuo futuro inizia qui

For the technical operation of the banks Avaloq Core platforms we are looking for a Technical Application Manager.That is why you need to ensure the technical operation of our banks platforms and take on an exciting job in the Swiss financial industry. In this context, you strengthen and support our team as Technical Application Manager with your expertise and know-how to ensure operational stability and high customer satisfaction.

  • You are the central point of contact for customers and partners and impress with your communication skills and expertise
  • You implement effective and lasting solutions in incident and problem management
  • You coordinate cross-company maintenance tasks and projects effectively

Your skills

  • In-depth knowledge of Unix incl. Scripting and Oracle
  • Applications of Job Scheduling (Automic) and Middleware Tools (IBM MQ / RedHat Active MQ)
  • Professional experience in the operation of comprehensive banking platforms and knowledge in Avaloq desirable
  • Good analytical and conceptual skills, coupled with an exact working method
  • Safe and independent appearance, enjoyment of teamwork, as well as excellent communication skills
  • Flowing in German and English, knowledge in other national languages is advantageous

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Benefits.
