Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School Stadelhofen Branch Dübendorf Physics Assistant/-in 80 - 100% The branch Dübendorf of the Cantonal School Stadelhofen has been running a high school, a short high school and a middle school since the school year 2026/27. In full operation, it offers space for 650 students on Empa’s campus. In the summer of 2027, another building will be opened in Dübendorf - also for 650 students. In the school year 2027/28 the physics lessons start. For the development phase and the subsequent school operation at our two locations, we are looking for a committed, self-employed personality as a central member of our department of physics as of April or May 2027. Their tasks

  • Responsibility for the perfect condition of the entire physics collection (devices and experiments) and inventory
  • Support for the establishment of demonstrations and preparation of teaching internships
  • Development and construction of new experimental equipment and storage solutions
  • Repairs and new acquisitions
  • Collaboration in projects and support for mature work
  • Co-determination in the procurement and establishment of the subject-specific infrastructure Your profile
  • Completed vocational training as a physics laboratory EFZ, a completed technical apprenticeship (e.g. in the fields of electromechanics or electronics) or equivalent qualification, preferred a few years of practice in a similar function
  • Broad craftsmanship skills (including basic knowledge of CAD and 3D printing)
  • Good knowledge of German (word and writing) and common IT skills in the MS Office area
  • Independent, reliable and careful working methods and good organisational skills
  • Service-oriented, flexible and capable of teamwork with open and respectful communication
  • We offer an interesting, versatile activity in an unusual, dynamic and meaningful environment of a middle school. With your dedicated commitment, you make an important contribution to the development of a new school and contribute to the culture of our school. The Conditions of Employment are governed by the Staff Regulations of the Canton of Zurich. Application Have we aroused your interest? We look forward to your application. Please submit your documents by 30 October 2026 exclusively via the online tool. Applications outside the online tool are not accepted and deleted immediately. For information, the responsible Vice Rector, Mr. Markus Hanhart, is available exclusively via mail markus.hanhart@edu.zh.ch. Further information about our school can be found at Cantonal School Stadelhofen - Filiale Dübendorf.

Pensum: 80-100 %
