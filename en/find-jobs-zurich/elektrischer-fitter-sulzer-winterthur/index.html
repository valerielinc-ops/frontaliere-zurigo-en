Full description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

Join our team within as an Electrical Fitter in our Laverton North Service Centre.

  • Repair and maintain LV & HV electrical rotating machinery.
  • Assemble and disassemble electrical rotating equipment.
  • Conduct static and dynamic tests on electrical equipment to ensure optimal performance.
  • Rewind electric motors and generators.
  • Perform balancing on motors to keep things running smoothly.
  • Keep the goods flowing with timely ordering when required.
  • Take direction from your Supervisor to ensure seamless day-to-day operations.
  • When required, attend onsite to conduct effective repairs of electrical rotating equipment.

To succeed in this role, you will need

  • CERT III in Electrotechnology or above / equivalent.
  • Experience in repairing and rewinding DC motors, medium to High Voltage electrical motors, and generators.
  • Excellent time management skills to keep things on track.
  • Strong teamwork skills.
  • Dedication to maintaining a safe work environment at all times.

  • Site allowances for field work
  • Technical training and development
