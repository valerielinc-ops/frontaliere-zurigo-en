Full description

Do you enjoy using your analytical thinking to create individual customer solutions in the field of financial and pension planning? Are you used to working as a team player in a dynamic environment with different stakeholders? Do you like working with numbers?

The Center of Competence Financial and Pension Planning develops financial and pension plans for Zurich’s sales. In this role, you support our Zurich pension specialists and create independently and reliably financial and pension planning. A small team is waiting for you, which works together constructively and the willingness to help has an important priority.

Find out here why Zurich Switzerland is one of the "Top 3" employers in Switzerland. Become part of it and apply as a financial planner (m/f/d).

What you do

  • You independently create complex financial and pension planning for sales.
  • You contribute to the further development of financial planning tools and the optimization of processes.
  • You ensure the transfer of knowledge to internal and external partners.
  • You are involved in specialist committees and actively participate in projects.

What you bring

  • Further education as a financial planner with federal law. Professional card as well as practical experience in financial and pension planning, ideally supplemented by sales experience.
  • Independent, structured and responsible working methods.
  • Open, communicative and flexible personality with pleasure in dealing with people.
  • Knowledge of Logismata’s financial planning tools (Allfina Pro and Pension Planner).
  • stylish knowledge of German
  • Knowledge of French and/or Italian is an advantage.
