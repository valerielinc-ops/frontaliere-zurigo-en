Original description

Their tasks

  • Professional, personnel and organizational management as well as further development of the patient administration department with 11 employees
  • Ensuring efficient and smooth processes along all administrative patient processes – from entry to exit
  • Ensuring high data and documentation quality as well as compliance with legal and tariff requirements (KVG, VVG, DRG, TARDOC, TARPSY)
  • Responsibility for correct, complete and timely performance billing
  • Continuous optimization and digitalization of administrative processes
  • Close cooperation with medicine, care, finance as well as external partners and insurance companies
  • Analysis of relevant key figures and derivation of improvement measures
  • Cooperation and management of projects in the field of patient administration and patient management Your profile
  • Basic commercial or business education with further training in hospital administration, health management or business administration
  • Several years of professional experience in patient administration or a comparable administrative environment in the healthcare sector
  • Experience in HR management and team development
  • In-depth knowledge of Swiss accounting, in particular KVG, VVG, TARPSY, TARDOC and DRG
  • Structured and solution-oriented working method with an eye on the essentials
  • Analytical thinking, organizational skills and high service orientation
  • Strength of communication, empathy and enjoyment of interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Experience with Opale (ERP) and/or ines (KIS) is an advantage She expects a varied and demanding management task with a lot of room for manoeuvre and the opportunity to actively shape the further development of our patient administration. Together with a motivated and committed team, you make an important contribution to high-quality patient care. They look forward to an appreciative working environment, short decision-making paths and attractive development and further education opportunities. With us, they find an environment in which self-initiative is welcome, responsibility can be taken and cooperation at eye level is lived. For further information please contact Michael Achermann, CFO Clienia Group, +41 44 929 80 86. We look forward to your online application! The Clienia Group is the largest private hospital group in the field of psychiatry and psychotherapy in German-speaking Switzerland. At various locations, Clienia offers a diverse spectrum of psychiatric, psychotherapeutic and psychosomatic treatment methods for all age groups in the ambulatory, semi-stationary and inpatient setting up to psychiatric long-term care. Clienia AG – the largest private hospital group for psychiatry and psychotherapy in German-speaking Switzerland. Locations: Privatklinik Bellevue (Pfäffikon ZH), Klinik Schlössli (Oetwil am See ZH), Tagesklinik Zürich, Tagesklinik Wetzikon.
