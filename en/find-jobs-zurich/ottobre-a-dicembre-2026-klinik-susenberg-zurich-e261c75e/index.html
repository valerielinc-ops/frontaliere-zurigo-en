Full description

Vacant sub-assistant position We are looking for a sub-assistant at the Susenberg Clinic for the period from October 1st, 2026 to December 27th, 2026. In our traditional special clinic with 34 beds in the most beautiful location on Zurichberg, you can expect a varied task in which empathy, respect and appreciation are essential components of our collaboration. Our service assignments are: acute geriatrics, geriatric rehabilitation, internal medicine-oncological rehabilitation and specialized palliative care. Our sub-assistants appreciate working in a small house with good supervision while learning clinical skills such as taking anamnesis and taking findings, discussing ECGs, laboratory findings and much more. After appropriate training, they are also responsible for their own patients independently and can provide all-round care for their patients under supervision, from the admission prescription to the discharge report. A weekly X-ray report, journal club and case presentations round off the offer. You can find further information about the clinic on our homepage.

  • We offer a 5-day week without night and weekend shifts. Payment is made according to cantonal guidelines.
