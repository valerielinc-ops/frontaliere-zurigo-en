Welcome to Medbase Winterthur Win4

Our modern center Win4 stands for excellent quality in primary care. We accompany our patients on a long-term basis, holistically and at a high professional level – from prevention to continuous care of chronic diseases. In addition to family medicine, we also have specialists from sports medicine, performance diagnostics, orthopaedic surgery and traumatology, physiotherapy and osteopathy working together interprofessionally. In cooperation with the Cantonal Hospital Winterthur, our patients benefit from short distances and networked care. Around 40 employees jointly design high-quality and future-oriented medicine. We attach great importance to a balanced work-life balance – part-time models and flexible working hours are possible with us.
