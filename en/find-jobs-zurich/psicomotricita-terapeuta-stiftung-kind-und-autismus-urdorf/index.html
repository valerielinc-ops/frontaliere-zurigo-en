Full description

30%, from now on or by agreement The Child and Autism Foundation is a competence center for children and adolescents with autism in Urdorf (ZH). The offer includes the special school for 56 children and adolescents, the assisted living with 16 places, the driving service, the advice centre and the early area, the courses and the auxiliary shop. The Child and Autism Foundation employs around 120 people.

We are looking for a psychomotor therapist in the amount of 30% from now on or by agreement. If desired, the workload can be increased in the new school year.
