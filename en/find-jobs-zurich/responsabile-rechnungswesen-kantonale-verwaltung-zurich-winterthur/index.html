Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Juvenile Criminal Justice Head/Accounting 80 - 100% The juvenile justice department of the Canton of Zurich with its approximately 120 employees examines and assesses the crimes committed by young people between the ages of ten and eighteen. The five regional youth advocacy offices conduct criminal investigations and are responsible for implementing the protection measures and penalties under juvenile law. The Oberjugendanwaltschaft carries the overall responsibility for planning, guiding and controlling the juvenile justice system, provides services for the juvenile advocacy offices and is their supervisory body. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for a responsible and committed personality as Head of Accounting 80 – 100%. These tasks are waiting for you:

  • Professional and personnel management of the 2-3 employees of the central accounting (accountable, debtor and client accounting)
  • Ensuring regular, accrual and timely accounting as well as cooperation in interim and annual accounts, budgeting and planning
  • Responsibility for uniform financial processes, their documentation and continuous optimization and further development
  • Contact person for technical issues and close cooperation with internal and external stakeholders in financially relevant topics
  • Collaboration in day-to-day operations if required as well as support and representation in controlling
  • Participation in financial projects, in particular in cooperation with the financial directorate and internal offices
  • Promotion and further development of employees as well as support for operational accounting in the event of personnel bottlenecks This is what distinguishes you:
  • Commercial basic education with further education at the level of Higher School of Applied Sciences (HF) in Finance and Accounting or a Bachelor degree in a comparable subject area
  • Several years of professional experience in finance and accounting, preferably in public administration
  • Leadership experience
  • Very good user knowledge in MS Office and SAP
  • Self-employed, prudent and service-oriented working methods as well as high resilience
  • Loyalty, confidentiality and a sovereign handling of demanding situations and sensitive dossiers We offer you: We offer a meaningful, varied and challenging field of activity. Our employees benefit from the attractive employment conditions of the Canton of Zurich. Application Have we aroused your interest? Then we look forward to your complete online application.
