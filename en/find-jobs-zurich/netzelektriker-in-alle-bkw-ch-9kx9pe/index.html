Full description

We make habitats worth living.

Installations of any size are our specialty. With around 440 employees at 10 locations, we work with the highest level of commitment and constant innovation. Also as a lateral beginner: you are welcome with us. Are you ready to provide secure connections with us?

Your tasks

  • You take over the execution of works in the medium and low voltage network.
  • Independent cable work on low-voltage to medium-voltage lines is part of your everyday work.
  • You are responsible for the planning and organization of construction sites, as well as for power cuts.
  • In addition, you provide assistance in cable construction, mostly in a closed environment.
  • Working on overhead lines, as well as pikettdienst are not among your tasks.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed basic training as a grid electrician:in EFZ or electrical installer:in EFZ or completed another craft apprenticeship with the willingness to complete the certificate course "Introduction to grid construction".
  • It gives you pleasure to work in-house.
  • You are team-oriented, have a high level of safety and risk awareness and can solve problems systematically.
  • Your exact and independent working method complements your strong customer orientation and reliability.
  • You have the driving license of category B and at most BE (trailer).
