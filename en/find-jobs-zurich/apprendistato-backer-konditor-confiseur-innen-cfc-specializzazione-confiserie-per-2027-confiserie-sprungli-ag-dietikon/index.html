Full description

Founded in 1836, the Swiss family business is today one of the most renowned confiseries in Europe with its exquisite range. Sprüngli products stand for the best quality, unique freshness and naturalness. The completed creations bring customers from all over the world to swarm daily.

100%, Dietikon

Working at Sprüngli

As a baker-confectioner you produce with craftsmanship and great care a diverse selection of delicious pastry specialties.

  • Decoration of patisserie and cakes
  • Assistance in the Osterhasen production
  • Manufacture of exclusive cakes and pastry products

  • Sek A or Sek B Degree
