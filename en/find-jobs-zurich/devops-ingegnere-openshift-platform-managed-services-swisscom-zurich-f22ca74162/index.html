# DevOps Engineer – OpenShift Platform & Managed Services

Il tuo futuro inizia

In our agile DevOps team, you will operate and develop a modern OpenShift platform and resilient services for our B2B customers. You take responsibility for the construction, lifecycle and reliable operation of this platform and its services. You rely on automation, GitOps and tools from the CNCF landscape. With your passion for container technologies and your pleasure in working with our customers, you take Swisscom and our B2B services to the next level. Highlights

  • Establishment, operation and continuous development of our Kubernetes platform and the managed services offered on it
  • Ensuring high availability, scalability and reliability through robust architecture and automation
  • Use of the Swisscom Private Cloud to provide the platform and services
  • Use and management of operators and CNCF tools for logging, monitoring, backup, RBAC, security and other infrastructure components

Your skills

  • In-depth practical experience in building, operating and further developing productive Red Hat OpenShift platforms
  • Very good knowledge of Kubernetes and OpenShift lifecycle management, especially updates, upgrades and error analysis
  • Experience with GitOps and the automation of platform configurations and operations, for example with Argo CD and Tekton
  • Experience with monitoring and logging of container-based platforms, for example with Prometheus, Grafana and Loki
  • Certification as a Red Hat Certified OpenShift Administrator is an advantage
  • Additional knowledge in Service Mesh, GitLab or Kafka is an advantage
  • Self-responsible working methods, teamwork, flexibility and a good understanding of agile and lean working methods
  • Willingness to participate in the Pikett service. Your residence is in Switzerland

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Benefits.
