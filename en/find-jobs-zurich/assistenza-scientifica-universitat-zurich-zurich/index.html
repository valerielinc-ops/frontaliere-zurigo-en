Institute of Art History, Chair of Modern and Contemporary Art

Job opening 01.10.2026, temporary

At the Chair of Modern and Contemporary Art, University of Zurich (Prof. Dr. Bärbel Küster), the first October 2026 a doctoral position 60% in art history with focus on postcolonial issues is advertised. The applicant should further qualify with a doctoral project in the field of contemporary art in particular on the spot. The Chair of Modern and Contemporary Art, at Switzerland’s largest Art Historical Institute, is closely connected to institutions in the cultural scene as well as active in international debates in art studies, art theory and museum and exhibitions. Art history in a global, transnational and postcolonial perspective forms a focus in research and teaching.

Tasks

implementation of a doctoral project (research); Conducting courses in the amount of 2 SWS per semester (teaching language is usually German); Student advice and supervision of bachelor theses; participation in the activities of the Chair

  • Support in the organization of events and research activities of the chair as well as the assumption of administrative tasks.

Profile

Excellent degree (M.A.) in Art History, very good language skills in English and French/Spanish. A precisely presented, demanding doctoral project, especially in the field of transcontinental integration history in the 20th/21st century. century, qualifies you to a special degree for this position. The remuneration is based on the cantonal guidelines. The employment is initially limited to three years and is then reduced by max. three years extended. For further qualification with a doctoral project in the field of postcolonial issues in art history, the chair offers support and an international environment. Equal opportunities are an important concern for the University of Zurich, we support diversity-friendly and inclusive cooperation.
