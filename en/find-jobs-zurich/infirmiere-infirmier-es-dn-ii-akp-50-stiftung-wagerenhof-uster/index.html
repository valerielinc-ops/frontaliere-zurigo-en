To complement our team in the care service day we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by arrangement as Nursing professional HF / DN II / AKP (50%) At the heart of what we do is people with disabilities. Through differentiated perception, you recognize the needs of our residents and are ready to respect them as independent personalities with their own will. As a responsible, reliable and resilient personality, you support a large professional nursing service team. The Wagerenhof Foundation (affectionately called “Wagi”) in Uster has been a permanent home for around 250 people with multiple cognitive impairments for more than 100 years and offers over 50 employees who need support accompanied jobs. We are a recognized, non-profit institution with a social mission. With a demand-oriented offer of living and care places as well as various day and leisure offers, we promote the quality of life, participation and self-determination of our residents. The special features of the Wagerenhof include size and Dörfli character, which allows space for encounters. For employees with different careers and professionalism, the Wagerenhof Foundation is an attractive employer with a wide range of development opportunities. For learners, Wagerenhof is a competent training partner.

  • Cooperation in interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation

  • Controls, laboratory activities and blood sampling

You fit in with us and with this position, if you also put our residents (and our supervised employees) at the center of your activities and you bring the following:
