Full description

In the role as Data Scientist in our team Cyberdyne, you design, build and run the ML workflows behind our product data. If you have ever searched, filtered or compared a product on digitec or galaxus, several of our models helped assemble the data you were looking at. We build in Python on BigQuery and other GCP services, and we own our models from the first notebook to production.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Hauptsitz, Pfingstweidstrasse 60b, 8005 Zürich

Key information

We welcome all applications, but can only consider applications submitted online. Applications submitted by post will be disposed of for data protection reasons and will not be returned.

Mansioni

  • Designing, building and running ML workflows to build and improve our product catalog
  • Building evaluation sets and validation logic that catch quality problems before customers do
  • You stay up-to-date with emerging technologies and industry trends (e.g. latest LLM developments) to continuously enhance our solution
  • Improving the tooling our team relies on, so the next workflow is quicker to build than the last

Requisiti

  • Several years of professional experience: In data science or machine learning
  • Completed degree (university / university of applied science / higher technical college): In a data-focused field
  • Strong Python, plus SQL you are comfortable writing against large tables
  • Hands-on experience with an orchestrator such as Airflow or Dagster
  • Practical experience running ML Models over large datasets, including prompt design, cost control and validation of the results
  • Experience with BigQuery or a comparable cloud data warehouse, ideally on GCP
  • A willingness to challenge «how things have always been done» and propose more efficient, automated or effective solutions
  • Using creativity and initiative to solve problems and achieve goals
  • The capacity to adapt to ongoing changes in the work environment
  • German (Advantageous)
