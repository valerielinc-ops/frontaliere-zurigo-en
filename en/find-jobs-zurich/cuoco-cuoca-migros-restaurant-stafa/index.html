Full description

The Migros Restaurant Stäfa offers fresh and seasonal food every day. From a large selection, our guests can cater throughout the day. To complement our team, we are looking for a service- and front-oriented personality who appreciates guest contact and brings pleasure to the profession.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Restaurant, MR Stäfa, Göthestrasse 18, 8712 Stäfa

Mansioni

  • Preparation of the cold and warm food offer
  • Advice and service of our guests as well as the menu output
  • Establishment and maintenance of buffets in the Free Flow (guest area)
  • Active support in the management of goods and in receiving the delivery of goods
  • Various Mise en Place Work in the respective work area
  • Compliance with GMZ quality standards and HACCP hygiene guidelines

Requirements

  • At least 1-2 years of professional experience: in the required area
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): as a chef
  • Flexible operational readiness from Monday to Saturday in different shifts (early and late services, without room hour)
  • Motivated, organized and humorous team player personality
  • Efficient, forward-looking and orderly work
  • German (very good knowledge)

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Expert Discussion: In a specialist discussion, specific knowledge relevant for the execution of the job is requested by the manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, the team. If possible, there is time afterwards to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Test work: A trial day or half day gives the opportunity to get to know the future working environment and the team members better. And vice versa, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
