Original description

137042 | 80 - 100% | Oerlikon, Zurich | Hybrid

You are actively involved in the further development of our claims systems and ensure that claims system processes become simpler, more digital and more efficient. In the Claims Business Development Team, you combine your knowledge from claims management with your IT and Guidewire experience and develop solutions with a direct impact on performance management together with an interdisciplinary team.

Does the idea of actively developing claims system processes at the interface between business and IT motivate you? Do you want to drive digitalization and automation in claims management with Guidewire in an agile environment?

As a Claims Business Analyst at Zurich Switzerland, you will strengthen the Claims business unit in the area of performance management and are part of the Claims Business Development Team. You will analyze and optimize existing system processes, develop IT-related solutions and take responsibility for the reliable operation of our claims systems. You will accompany requirements from the idea to specification and testing to implementation and work closely with interdisciplinary teams.

This is what awaits you

  • You analyze and optimize system processes in claims management and take responsibility for the necessary maintenance work.
  • Together with the agile team, you will develop technical and IT-related solutions for the further development of our claims systems.
  • You specify new system functions and user interfaces and accompany their implementation.
  • You define acceptance criteria, test new functions and ensure a comprehensible implementation of the requirements.
  • You support the development team and ensure a well-founded and structured documentation of requirements and solutions.

This sets you apart

  • Completed training in the insurance or computer science sector.
  • Sound experience in claims management and/or with Guidewire.
  • Strong IT affinity and the ability to quickly grasp complex relationships.
  • Structured, self-responsible and solution-oriented working method.
  • Fluent knowledge of German and English.
