We offer 1-2 apprenticeships as a mediamatist:in EFZ. You can also complete your apprenticeship with us in various school models: with or without vocational training. About teaching In addition to visiting the vocational school BZZ in Horgen, you will spend the first year of apprenticeship in the ZLI basic apprenticeship year (Zürcher Lehrbetriebsverband ICT). This prepares you for your productive use in our teaching company. On the basis of various works, projects and assignments, your professional and work methodological skills are promoted and you receive basic skills. In the second to fourth year of apprenticeship, you rotate with us in various areas of application. This gives you insight into different specialist areas, such as seminar and conference management, video technology, communication or print and design.
