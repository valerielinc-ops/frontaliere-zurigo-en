Customer Administrator

London | 100 | Reference 8190

As a key contributor to our sales operations, you will manage a defined customer territory in close collaboration with a Sales Representative. You will also take ownership of all administrative and support activities related to sales and the implementation of financial information systems for our clients.

What You Will Do

  • Territory Management: Oversee a customer sales territory in partnership with Sales and Account Managers, focusing on financial services products for US customers
  • Client Service: Serve as a primary point of contact for customer inquiries related to products, functionalities, data, pricing, exchange permissions, and issue resolution
  • Contract Administration: Draft, process, and manage customer contracts within the financial customer information system
  • Billing & Financial Oversight: Initiate invoicing processes and monitor dunning procedures and accruals to ensure financial accuracy
  • Project Coordination: Manage product and data entitlements, and plan, coordinate, and track customer and sales-related projects from initiation to completion

What You Bring

  • at least 3 years of experience in a similar role, preferably in a middle or back-office environment
  • strong numerical aptitude and attention to detail
  • customer-focused with a proactive and well-organized approach
  • ability to work independently and collaboratively in a dynamic environment
  • language Proficiency: Excellent verbal and written communication skills
