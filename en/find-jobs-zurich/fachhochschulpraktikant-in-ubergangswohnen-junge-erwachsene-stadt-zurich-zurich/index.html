Full description

For transitional living for young adults we are looking for 1. March 2027 or by arrangement for at least 6 months a motivated team member.

Tasks

  • You will look after and accompany the young adults in our communities individually regarding cleanliness, order, social behavior, hygiene, personal administration, etc.
  • You perceive the office presence with direct informal contact with the young adults.
  • You are in contact with employers, therapists and assignment offices (SOD, AOZ) and maintain an intensive exchange with the social services of the city of Zurich as well as various specialist offices.
  • You gain experience according to individually defined goals in the professional field of social work (forms of conversation, formulation of goals and agreements, triage, networking, dealing with conflicts, contact with case leaders, etc.).
  • You do various administrative work and participate in meetings.
  • You work shifts from Monday to Friday. Working hours vary, usually in the afternoon and evening until a maximum of 10 pm.

Profile

  • Ongoing studies in social work (preferably second internship)
  • Resilience, self-confidence and flexibility
  • Independent working methods and teamwork
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of IT users
  • Minimum age 25 years
  • Motivation and joy in a dynamic environment

We offer

A varied and demanding activity in an unusual working environment. You also benefit from an instructive and broad field of work in social and socio-educational work, in which you can further develop your skills and interests. You will get an overview of the urban facilities and companies, as well as various possibilities of networking. In addition to regular exchange with the social services, the Ambulatorium Badenerstrasse also works closely together. In addition, we offer you a tailor-made promotion of the various fields of competence analogous to your studies during the internship.

About us

With around 2,400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a social Zurich. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.

Transitional living for young adults is aimed at 18- to 24-year-olds who can neither live independently nor fit into a regular home business. The offer is maintained for 365 days and the stay is limited to one year.
