Full description

Actively shape Migros's ServiceNow platform as a strategic core platform: You connect Demand Management, Governance, architecture, and operations. Together with your agile team, you establish binding standards and drive scalable development across teams. Your goal: clear priorities, a stable platform that can be upgraded over the long term, and maximum business value.

Place of work: Migros-Genossenschafts-Bund, Limmatplatz Zürich, Limmatstrasse 152, 8005 Zürich

Responsibilities

  • You work closely with the architect, quality manager, operations lead, and demand team and actively drive the platform's technical and functional development
  • You understand ServiceNow as a strategic platform and can translate demand, architecture, operations, technical development, and organizational governance into an effective operating model
  • You are responsible for the stable operation of the platform and ensure transparency regarding risks, dependencies, opportunities, and improvements
  • You implement binding group-wide standards as well as reusable platform components and foundational services in all areas of Migros that use the core components of the SN platform
  • You are the central point of contact for divisional management and take responsibility for the backlog, prioritization, and communication of core platform topics
  • As Design Authority, you are responsible for the architecture standards for scalable development and integrations, ensuring implementation across teams, maintainability, and long-term upgradeability

Requirements

  • You have several years of practical ServiceNow experience in an agile environment as a platform or product owner, or in an architecture-related leadership role: ideally in a hybrid IT environment with multiple stakeholders and a high diversity of integrations, with proven experience leading teams and projects
  • Completed degree (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): in computer science or business informatics, ideally with further training in Service Now (CTA, CPOA) and agile methods (SAFe, Scrum)
  • You have several years of practical experience with ServiceNow, as well as a sound understanding of enterprise-wide platform technologies and processes (ITIL) and their continuous improvement
  • You are familiar with topics such as Upgradeability, Data Model Discipline, standardization, reusability, controlled extensibility, and the avoidance of uncontrolled Customization
  • You offer a deep understanding of the SN platform architecture (instances, Sub-Prod, releases), integrations with surrounding systems, and operational processes (ServiceNow Best Practices) and their automation
  • You can translate technical complexity into robust prioritization and decision-making frameworks and navigate confidently between architecture, delivery, operations, management, and business functions
  • You bring servant leadership in an agile context, know how to inspire and guide your teams, also enforce priorities, and, as a team player, demonstrate tolerance and a win-win mentality
  • You have broad experience in transformation and change processes in a complex corporate environment and know how to actively shape and successfully implement changes.
  • Your strength lies in your communication and prioritization skills when dealing with different stakeholders, partners and their dependencies, and business vs technology
  • German (negotiation-level proficiency)
  • English (very good command)
  • French (an advantage)

Recruitment process

  • Submitting application documents: For a complete application, we need your CV, certificates and diplomas. A photo in the CV and a cover letter are not necessary. We are committed to equal opportunities for all applicants. We have a standardized recruitment process. If that does not work for you, mention it in your application dossier.
  • Preselection of applications: We review applications received on an ongoing basis, create a preselection and make it available to the manager for review. The decision on whether to proceed or be eliminated from the recruitment process is communicated by phone or email. Time until feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Interview with HR & specialist department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. The primary purpose is to learn more about personal and professional skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit within the company and team. Time until feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Technical interview with case presentation: Specific technical expertise is assessed using a case or other assessment tools. After completing it, the result is presented to the manager and then discussed together. Usually, the team is also represented at the presentation. As a rule, the interviews are conducted by the supervisor, without an HR representative. Time until feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone is a good fit for the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against hiring. We therefore provide an opportunity for everyone to get to know one another in an informal setting.
  • Offer & contract preparation (digital): Following our verbal job offer, the key contractual points are verified once again. After agreement is reached, the contract is commissioned for preparation and sent by email for digital signature. Time until response: Up to one week

Employment percentage: 60-100%
