About the Role

The Cocoa Trader role is part of the Products Trading team based in Zürich. The position is primarily responsible for the external sourcing and subsequent sale of cocoa powder and cake to our European manufacturing sites. In addition, the role supports broader trading activities, including the purchase and sale of cocoa butter and liquor to third-party customers, in line with sales targets and available production capacities.

A key aspect of the role is the monitoring and management of trading exposures and risks arising from intercompany cocoa powder transactions, ensuring effective oversight of the trading book. The position also encompasses responsibilities related to financial transactions, hedging activities, and derivative instruments, contributing to the overall performance and risk management of the trading business.

Key Responsibilities Include

  • Manage the procurement of cocoa powder and cocoa cake to support intercompany supply requirements.
  • Oversee cocoa powder sales and execution, ensuring a reliable and efficient supply to manufacturing sites across Europe.
  • Manage and further develop the supplier portfolio, identifying and evaluating alternative sourcing opportunities.
  • Source cocoa products by gathering market intelligence and supplier offers, providing competitive pricing and contributing to trading strategy development.
  • Monitor and manage risk exposures arising from cocoa powder trading activities and maintain oversight of the trading book.
  • Support cocoa butter and cocoa liquor trading activities, working closely with third-party customers, internal stakeholders, and sales teams.
  • Advise customers and sales teams on market developments, pricing, trading strategies, and business opportunities while maintaining key customer relationships.
  • Execute cocoa futures and foreign exchange transactions in line with trading objectives and risk management guidelines.
  • Resolve quality, logistics, and supply chain issues in collaboration with internal teams, customers, and suppliers.
  • Monitor trade execution and physical product flows while developing a strong understanding of product sourcing processes and related operational requirements.
