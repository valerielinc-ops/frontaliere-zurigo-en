Role overview

Eraneos is an international strategy, transformation and technology consulting group, dedicated to empowering organizations to thrive in an ever-changing digital age. By bringing together top-tier experts from business and tech, we help clients to continuously raise the bar in successful transformations, from strategy to execution. Whether we’re designing future-ready organizations, unlocking the potential of data and AI, or securing businesses with cutting-edge cybersecurity, we deliver results pragmatically. Our team of around 1,300 dedicated professionals is based in offices across Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, Portugal, Denmark, Sweden, Austria, the UK, Singapore, and the USA, and seamlessly blends global perspectives with strong local roots.

M&A forms a central part of our growth strategy as we continue our journey to become a leading AI-first consultancy in Europe. Building on our excellent track record in M&A, we are looking to further strengthen our Corporate M&A team by hiring an M&A Manager. We are seeking a driven and engaging personality who brings strong expertise, takes ownership, and actively contributes to the continued success of the team.

  • Drive M&A initiatives together with the Head of M&A in collaboration with the Executive Management, the business units, and our private equity investor
  • Identify, assess, and execute acquisition opportunities, from target identification and the management of due diligence processes through to signing and closing
  • Hand over the transaction to PMI following completion, ensuring a smooth transition
  • Coordinate internal stakeholders and external partners such as legal and financial advisors, investment banks and other transaction experts
  • Prepare valuations, financial models, deal analyses and decision materials for the Executive Management and Board of Directors
  • Conduct market, company, and competitive analyses
  • A Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Finance, Business Administration, Economics or a related field
  • 4-6 years of relevant experience in Corporate M&A, M&A advisory, investment banking, transaction advisory or private equity
