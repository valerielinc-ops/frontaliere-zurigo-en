Full description

🚀 Nursing offers more than you think – start in a practical, meaningful field with a future!

Do you want to do something meaningful, support people and take on responsibility? Whether you are new to the field or taking the next step as a FaGe EFZ – with us, you will start your training to become a qualified nursing professional HF (Higher Professional School)

💸 Already earn during your training: from CHF 40’300/year

  • 3 years : EFZ, Matura or equivalent
  • 2 years : FaGe EFZ (shortened entry possible)

Your tasks

With us, you will be involved from the very beginning – not just present:

  • Accompany patients from admission to discharge
  • Plan, carry out and document care processes
  • Perform medical-technical tasks
  • Work in an interdisciplinary team with doctors and therapists
  • Provide targeted support to help people return to everyday life (rehabilitation)
  • For beginners: Gradually grow into taking on responsibility
  • For FaGe: Independently manage the entire care process

What you bring

  • Enjoyment of working with people
  • Motivation to take on responsibility and continue developing
  • Independence, team spirit and proactive thinking
  • Observation and reflection skills
  • German language skills at least C1

The following also applies

  • 3-year HF training: EFZ, Matura or equivalent
  • 2-year HF training: FaGe EFZ + passed aptitude assessment (can be started in parallel)

Why us

  • Practical, varied training in the healthcare sector
  • Dedicated team that supports and encourages you
  • Responsibility from day one & genuine opportunities for development
  • Appreciative, collegial working environment
  • Attractive social benefits & perks
  • Support for further training
  • Discounts with partners and access to benefit programs
  • Free admission to the Wellmess-Therme FORTYSEVEN
  • Job shadowing possible at any time

We welcome applications from all motivated individuals – regardless of gender, background or experience.

Ready for your next step?

Then apply now for your training as a qualified nursing professional HF – whether you are starting from scratch or taking the next step as a FaGe.

We look forward to getting to know you!
