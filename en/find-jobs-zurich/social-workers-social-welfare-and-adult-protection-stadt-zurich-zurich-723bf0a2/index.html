Full description

Complex situations. Different perspectives. Developing sustainable solutions together.

With their work in the five social centres, the social services ensure that people in the city of Zurich can count on effective basic social care.

For our dedicated team at the Social Centre Albisriederhaus we are looking for reinforcement as of January 2027 or by agreement.

Tasks

  • They advise and accompany adults in economic welfare and/or in the protection of adults.
  • You take responsibility for your cases, make professional assessments and coordinate the necessary next steps.
  • They work closely with staff, specialist offices and external partners.
  • They move between advice, legal mandate and different interests – and keep an overview.
  • You manage your cases independently and ensure careful and comprehensible documentation.

Profile

  • University of Applied Sciences degree in social work
  • Experience in economic welfare and/or adult protection
  • Commercial flair and good knowledge of financial topics
  • Rapid comprehension and security in dealing with complex situations
  • Sovereignty, clarity and a professional approach to different concerns and expectations

We offer

  • Exciting cases: You work with versatile and challenging cases and can continuously expand your expertise.
  • Strong support: Processing and internal specialist positions relieve you of the burden in everyday administrative life and are at your side with professional questions.
  • In exchange: You work in modern open-plan offices and benefit from the direct, uncomplicated exchange in the team.
  • Development that advances: Attractive internal and external further education and a wide range of opportunities to develop professionally and personally.
  • Workplace with leeway: You are flexible in your everyday work and have the opportunity to do part of your work in the home office.

About us

With around 2,400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a social Zurich. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.
