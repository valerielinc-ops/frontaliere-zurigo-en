Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With more than 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric offer of Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in ambulatory, day-clinical and inpatient settings. The Group employs around 1800 people and achieved a turnover of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For our station Pünt Süd with treatment focus (k) PTSD we are looking for a

Their tasks One of your core tasks is the individual, professional care and care of patients, as a reference person. They use nursing diagnostics as a working instrument, actively shape the therapeutic environment of the station and orient themselves in their way of working on the station concept (mainly DBT-PTSD). The coordinated and multidisciplinary cooperation enables our customers to achieve the best possible treatment success.

Your profile You have completed training for the Dipl. Nurse HF/FH, are interested in the psychiatric field and enjoy and understand in dealing with patients of trauma therapy. Preferably, you already have DBT experience and are interested in redesigning therapy and station concepts.

Our offer You will find an exciting and responsible job in a committed, multi-professional team with the opportunity to get involved and shape. An extensive training and further education offer with financial participation of the clinic as well as internal and external supervision support you in your further development. The conceptual redesign of the station is accompanied by an external DBT PTSD supervisor (Prof. M. Bohus). Our clinic has staff rooms and is located in a charming landscape between Lake Constance and the Swiss Pre-Alpines. We are easily accessible from the larger cities of Zurich, Winterthur, St. Gallen and Constance.

For further information please contact Claire Chimento, station manager Pünt Süd, +41 71 929 66 22.
