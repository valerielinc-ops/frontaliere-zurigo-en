Full description

To strengthen our facade team, we are looking for an initiative and customer-oriented personality as:

Specialist in ventilated curtain wall facade (VCF), 100%

In this role, you will advise on and sell ventilated facade systems from the preliminary project phase to completion.

YOUR TASKS

  • Maintain and expand existing customer relationships through regular visits and targeted professional advice
  • Continuous and sustainable market development
  • Elaboration and development of project-specific facade solutions
  • Preparation of static and building physics basic calculations in the VCF area
  • Preparation of project information for tenders (facade specifications)
  • Technical advice in the VCF area from the planning to the execution phase

YOUR PROFILE

  • Technical basic training in the facade sector
  • Further training in the construction and related industries
  • Experience in preparing tenders in the facade sector is an advantage
  • Flexible personality with a sense of responsibility
  • Solid user knowledge of MS Office, AUTOCAD is an advantage
  • Fluent German skills, French skills are an advantage
  • Independent working style
  • Proactive, structured and careful way of working
  • Customer- and solution-oriented approach
  • Driving licence category B

YOUR PERSPECTIVES

  • Exciting tasks in a dynamic company
  • Company culture with room for personal responsibility and entrepreneurial ideas
  • A working environment where openness and appreciation are a matter of course
  • Attractive employment conditions with above-average social benefits
  • Individual induction programme tailored to the functional requirements
  • Support for your individual professional development
