As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

YOUR TASKS

With us, you take responsibility and accompany our patients with expertise, empathy and commitment.

  • You ensured a holistic, individual and professional care after the care process. You look after the patients entrusted to you before and after surgical procedures in various specialist areas. You carry out medically prescribed diagnostic and therapeutic measures independently and professionally. You recognize emergency situations early, act safely and initiate the necessary measures until the arrival of the doctor. You ensure a smooth process at the day clinic and take on administrative, organizational and logistical tasks. You work closely with an interdisciplinary team and actively contribute your ideas to the further development of our processes.

You can look forward to it.

  • Regular working hours without night and shift work.
  • The day clinic is closed for two weeks in the summer and for one week between Christmas and New Year, so you can plan your holidays early and reliably.

Your profile

You are a committed personality who enjoys patient-oriented care and lives the team idea.

  • You have completed training as a qualified nurse HF/FH or graduated nursing specialist HF/FH or an equivalent education recognised in Switzerland. You work independently, responsibly and keep an overview even in demanding situations. You are characterized by a high level of social competence, empathy and a pronounced service and patient orientation. Organizational talent, flexibility and a structured way of working are among your strengths. You communicate openly, appreciatively and enjoy working interdisciplinary. You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing.

You can expect a motivated, committed and multi-professional team in an advanced working environment. This is characterized by high quality of care, independence, flexibility and a sense of responsibility. We also offer our employees generous social benefits, sports offers, special insurance conditions and further education opportunities as part of our program.
