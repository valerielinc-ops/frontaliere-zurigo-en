Full description

Bits and bytes are your world. You ensure that the IT infrastructure works smoothly in operation and is protected from hackers. You recognize connections lightning fast, like to communicate them and can creatively implement your findings.

Tasks

  • You plan, manage and maintain server systems and services.
  • You plan local networks, select suitable components, install and configure them and monitor ongoing operations.
  • You take care of the security of the systems and services as well as the performance of the network.
  • You ensure that information is protected and can be distributed safely.

Profile

  • Completed compulsory schooling, middle or top level
  • Good knowledge of German and independent working methods
  • Good performance in science subjects
  • Ability to work in a team
  • Strong communication capability

We offer

About us

Organization and Computer Science (OIZ) is the IT competence center of the city of Zurich. In our departments, we build and integrate apps for the residents, companies and various service departments of the city of Zurich. The urban content management system and the intranet are our responsibility. In close cooperation with: 60 service departments and external partners are constantly working on digitalization and progress for the city of Zurich.
