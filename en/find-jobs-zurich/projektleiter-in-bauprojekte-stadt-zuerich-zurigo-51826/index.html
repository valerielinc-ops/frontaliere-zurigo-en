Full description

For the successful expansion and further development of our portfolio, we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement a project manager in portfolio management.

Tasks

  • They lead the portfolio management of SAW and the real estate strategy with implementation and renewal planning.
  • They identify, examine and accompany acquisition and development opportunities to expand and optimize the SAW portfolio.
  • You lead project developments on the client side and are responsible for controlling on the client side.
  • In our diverse projects, they ensure the demands on architectural quality, costs and deadlines.
  • They ensure that legal requirements as well as economic and ecological framework conditions are taken into account in the planning and execution.

Profile

  • Completed degree in architecture ETH/FH or equivalent education
  • Further education in real estate and project development and/or business administration (University, ETH, FH, CUREM)
  • In-depth experience in the real estate industry, with advantage in the portfolio management of a non-profit residential building portfolio (public or non-profit organization)
  • Knowledge of public procurement, the planning and approval processes of the City of Zurich as well as the cantonal and municipal housing promotion Willingness to appropriate them
  • Strong understanding of economic and planning contexts, complex problems and interest in political and social issues

We offer

A versatile and demanding task with a high level of personal responsibility in an agile and committed team. Our employees also benefit from attractive employment conditions in accordance with the personnel law of the City of Zurich as well as targeted training and further education.

About us

The Foundation for Retirement Apartments of the City of Zurich SAW currently operates around 40 settlements with more than 2000 affordable apartments for people aged 60 and over. It supports its tenants with everyday, social and nursing services and thus enables them a high quality of life into old age. By 2035, the SAW plans to put further settlements with around 1000 additional apartments into operation. These should optimally meet the requirements of the target group and create a basis for positive neighbourly cooperation. More about the SAW at www.wohnenab60.ch.
