Full description

(Senior) Underwriter Aerospace (m/f/d) 80 - 100% Permanent in Zurich, Switzerland As an Underwriter, you will be responsible for delivering a profitable ‘Underwriting’ service to your clients. Your key tasks include: underwriting business within the agreed business plan and individual authority; supporting the business planning and forecasting process; monitoring financial exposure within outlined limits; monitoring premium income to ensure it remains within set limits; maintaining awareness of all claims advised while supporting and providing input on settlement of claims; ensuring adequate reinsurance acquired to cover specific risks; building and maintaining relationships with brokers and clients; supporting the design and structure of risks. This is a regulated role under the Central Bank of Ireland’s Fitness and Probity regime.

What you’ll be doing What will your essential responsibilities include?

  • Own and develop a distinctive General Aviation portfolio in Switzerland
  • Assess and price complex, high-profile risks using actuarial tools and market insights, in close cooperation with regional and global functions
  • Analyse submission data and loss runs, and perform market research to determine the degree of risk and make clear underwriting decisions
  • Structure and negotiate deals, define conditions and manage capacities in line with our Underwriting Guidelines and Risk Appetite Strategy
  • Engage with clients and brokers to build trusted relationships and generate business opportunities through site visits or participation at industry events.

You will report to the Underwriting Manager Aerospace, Switzerland.

What you’ll bring

We’re looking for someone who has these abilities and skills

  • Several years of experience as an Aerospace Underwriter with deep knowledge of the (local) general aviation insurance market
  • Knowledge of the Aerospace industry is a significant benefit
  • Passion for anything that flies with the willingness and motivation to go the extra mile to keep our clients airborne
  • Thrives when working in a dynamic environment and can adapt to changes with ease
  • Puts the team's interests before their own and fosters a positive and inclusive team environment
  • Loves to be empowered and feels comfortable taking decisions and managing multiple priorities
  • Fluent in German and English (speaking and writing). French or any other language is a plus.
