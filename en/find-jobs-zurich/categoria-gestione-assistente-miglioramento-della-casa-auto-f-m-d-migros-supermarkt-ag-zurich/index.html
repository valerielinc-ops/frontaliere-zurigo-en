Full description

The Directorate Near / Non Food manages the product range Near Food, clothing, leisure and hardgoods. For you, home improvement and cars are more than just assortments? You want to ensure that our customers are well equipped for every project in their own four walls and every trip? We look forward to your support!

Mansioni

  • Support from the Category Field Manager and Category Manager in the assortment creation taking into account the strategy
  • Monitoring and maintenance of master data and ensuring data quality
  • Cooperation with supplier management in your range area, as well as obtaining offers, article openings, contracting, price mutations, maintenance of promotions
  • Independent processing of inquiries from customers, branches, suppliers and other stakeholders
  • Implementation of packaging designs according to specifications

Requirements

  • First professional experience: in purchasing or marketing
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): commercial basic training or retail apprenticeship with corresponding CV further training
  • Very good MS-Office and SAP knowledge
  • Organizational talent with careful working methods, independent and proactive
  • Affinity and enthusiasm for the range Home improvement and car
  • Solution-oriented and flexible personality with high team spirit and networked thinking
  • German (flowing)
  • English (good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central place of work: Central place of work in the city of Zurich (Limmatplatz or near Hardbrücke)
  • Occupational provision: Above-average benefits and three different savings plans suitable for the individual life situation
  • Cumulus points: Additional Cumulus points in all Migros branches and various subsidiaries
  • Migros Bank: Free account management and preferential interest on the private and investment account
  • Sports and fitness offers: Generous financial participation in courses of Migros Club School
  • Self-responsibility & freedom: Varied tasks with a high level of self-responsibility
