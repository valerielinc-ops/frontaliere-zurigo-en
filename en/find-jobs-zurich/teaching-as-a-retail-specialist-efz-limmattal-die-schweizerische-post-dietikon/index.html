Full description

Are you excited about daily contact with people and do you love to advise and sell? Then an education in the retail trade is just right for you! During the three-year basic education, you will acquire the tools to successfully enter the diverse world of retail at Post. Your apprenticeship is in Schlieren.

Your education

  • As a post office flagship, you are in daily contact with our customers in the store and advise our customers on the entire physical and digital product range at the counter and in the sales area.
  • receiving letters and packages, making deposits and withdrawals, selling products and services or advising on needs – Your tasks are varied and varied.
  • Swiss Post is increasingly positioning itself as a service centre for partner companies such as insurance companies, banks and health insurance companies – here you have the opportunity to acquire further industry knowledge.
  • The range of products and services in the stores includes other products and services related to topics such as living, giving and experiencing – you are right in the middle of supporting national and regional campaigns and creating diverse shopping experiences for customers.
  • You will get the opportunity to acquire and deepen your professional skills in a team and in various branches.
  • In intercompany and internal lessons, you network theory with practical experience.

You bring that with you

We are looking for young people with enthusiasm, joy and motivation who want to make a difference and get excited about new things. You should fulfill the following points:

  • You have successfully completed the middle elementary school level with good performances.
  • You have a flair for numbers and understand the handling of money.
  • Contact with people gives you pleasure, and you can express yourself verbally and in writing in a situational manner.
  • Your good organization and sense of order set you apart.
  • You are open, reliable and secretive.
  • You are happy to pass on your professional knowledge.
