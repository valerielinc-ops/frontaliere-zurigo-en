What you do with us

You work in our food bakery in our factory in Dietikon and make our popular yeast and puff pasta products in the team by careful hand. Your regular post at the food bakery is the early shift with work starting at 4:00 or 5:00 and end of work between 1:00 and 2:00 pm. Thanks to your interest and flexibility, you can also support the entire team in other posts, for example in the late shift or at the post. In the high season, Saturday missions are also possible.

Your tasks

  • Crafting of our yeast and puff pasta products
  • Production of our various small breads
  • Preparation of our seasonal products such as Christmas Stolls and Easter products
  • Careful processing of high-quality raw materials according to our proven recipes
  • Ensuring our high quality and hygiene standards
  • Active participation in the varied day-to-day business of Feinbäckerei
  • Participation in the optimization of our products and manufacturing processes

  • Category B driving licence
