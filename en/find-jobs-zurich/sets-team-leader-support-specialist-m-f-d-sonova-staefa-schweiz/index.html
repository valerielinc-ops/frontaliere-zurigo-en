Full description

Your role with us

In this role, you are the deputy technical leader in the support team of electronics production (m/f/d). Your workday moves directly to our Surface Mount Technology (SMT) lines and in the environment of Automated Optical Inspection (AOI).

You support stable production operations, are an important contact person on the shop floor and actively contribute your technical understanding and experience to the further development of our processes.

More about the role

  • Technical support and coordination of employees (m/f/d) in everyday production as well as deputy team leader in absence
  • Active participation in the ongoing operation of Surface Mount Technology (SMT)
  • Support, operation and protection of Automated Optical Inspection (AOI) including break detachments and jumper function
  • Analysis of incidents and initial corrective measures in close cooperation with technical interfaces
  • Organization of shift transfers and participation in the Tierd Daily Management
  • Active participation in continuous improvement, 5S and Kaizen activities
  • Ensuring a clear flow of information and conducting Gemba tours

More about you

  • Completed recognised vocational training in a technical or industrial environment
  • Several years of professional experience in electronics production, ideally in the environment of Surface Mount Technology (SMT)
  • Familiar with Automated Optical Inspection (AOI) and the associated production processes
  • Strong quality awareness and structured working methods
  • Team-oriented personality (m/f/d) with pleasure in professional responsibility
  • Very good German skills in word and writing, English skills are advantageous

More about what we offer

As one of the world’s leading companies in the field of hearing solutions with headquarters in Switzerland, we attach great importance to an inclusive and appreciative corporate culture.

We offer you

  • Flexible employment opportunities (80-100%)
  • Structured familiarisation and targeted internal and external training
  • Individual development and growth opportunities
  • A modern production environment with short decision paths
  • The opportunity to take responsibility and actively shape processes
