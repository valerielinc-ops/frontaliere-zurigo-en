ServiceNow Platform Architect

Warsaw | Flexible hybrid model | Reference 7831

Do you want to shape how ServiceNow is used across the enterprise to drive transformation?

We are looking for a ServiceNow Platform Architect to join our CIT team in Warsaw. In this role, you will play a key part in shaping and governing the ServiceNow architecture in a greenfield environment, with direct influence on how the platform is designed, built and evolved across the organization.

You will design scalable cross-module solutions and drive standards, governance and best practices across the platform, ensuring a secure, compliant and future-ready setup in a regulated environment. You will work closely with the Platform Owner, Enterprise Architecture, Product Owner and key business stakeholders as part of a structured design authority, helping to build a strong and sustainable platform foundation in a complex enterprise landscape.

This role will also support the transformation from legacy service management and governance tooling into a standardized, scalable and future-ready ServiceNow platform model.

What You Will Do

  • shape and assure the technical architecture of the ServiceNow platform, with a strong focus on stability, performance, security and maintainability
  • ensure the ServiceNow platform architecture aligns with business strategy and supports secure, scalable and future-ready solutions
  • define and maintain the technical architecture roadmap and provide architecture input into platform release planning
  • identify opportunities to maximize value from ServiceNow investments and improve service delivery through automation and standardization
  • define platform and data architecture standards, patterns and documentation, ensuring design consistency and data integrity across the platform
  • guide teams through solution design, technical delivery and environment strategy, ensuring alignment with architectural standards
  • enforce technical governance across the platform through defined standards, architecture reviews and design authority practices
  • design cross-module solutions across ITSM, CMDB/CSDM, IRM/GRC, CSM, HRSD and related platform capabilities, ensuring end-to-end consistency and appropriate integration patterns
  • define and drive the CMDB and service modelling strategy (CSDM) and provide architecture oversight for CMDB health and evolution
  • act as a trusted advisor by translating business requirements into effective ServiceNow solutions and clearly communicating architectural decisions and trade-offs to technical and non-technical stakeholders
  • drive solution direction in close collaboration with the Platform Owner and Enterprise Architecture, ensuring alignment with governance, security and overall platform strategy
  • help ensure that legacy requirements are appropriately challenged and that the target platform evolves through standardization, simplification and scalable design principles

What You Bring

  • bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, IT or a related field, or equivalent practical experience
  • 8+ years of ServiceNow experience, including substantial architecture responsibility in complex enterprise environments
  • strong experience designing ServiceNow solutions in organizations with demanding compliance, audit and governance requirements
  • CSA certification is mandatory
  • additional certifications such as CAD, CIS ITSM or other relevant CIS certifications are strongly preferred
  • strong understanding of ServiceNow implementation strategies, architectural best practices and platform governance
  • certifications such as CIS CSM, CIS GRC, CIS SecOps are preferred
  • CTA or CMA certifications are a strong advantage
  • experience with AI is a strong plus
  • experience with Git is preferred
  • proven ability to design secure and compliant integrations, as well as enterprise-scale workflows
  • experience designing CMDB/CSDM models integrated with BMC Discovery in complex enterprise environments is preferred
  • experience contributing to Design Authority reviews and ensuring alignment with enterprise architecture and platform strategy is a strong plus
  • experience in highly regulated environments is preferred
  • fluent English communication skills, written and spoken
  • German language skills are preferred
