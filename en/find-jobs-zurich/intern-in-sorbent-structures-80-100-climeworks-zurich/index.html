Full description

  • Sorbent Evaluation & Development: You support the evaluation and development of sorbent structures for direct air capture.
  • Material Testing: You work in an integrated team to test novel materials for CO₂ adsorption behavior, stability, and structural integrity.
  • Prototype Development: You develop sample prototypes of novel materials.
  • Independent Work Packages: You work independently on packages to evaluate future technologies, including modeling or lab-based aspects depending on your background.
  • Validation & Documentation: You validate, troubleshoot, and clearly document your work to ensure effective communication within larger projects at Climeworks.

Requirements

  • Education & Experience: You have a bachelor’s degree and/or are working towards a master’s degree in mechanical engineering, process engineering, chemical engineering, materials science, or similar.
  • Availability: You are available for a 6-month internship starting preferably in January 2026 or shortly thereafter.
  • Technical Skills: You enjoy working hands-on in the lab or workshop and have experience with any of the following: characterization of porous materials (BET, Hg porosimetry, TGA), structural analysis (tensile/compression testing, FEM), adsorption characterization (breakthrough testing, DVS), mass transfer modeling, or prototyping in a workshop.
  • Additional Knowledge: Familiarity with CO₂ capture is beneficial but not required.
  • Mindset & Working Style: You are eager to learn new concepts, reliable, motivated, solution-oriented, concise, and have a structured working style.
  • Language Skills: You are fluent in English; knowledge of German or other languages is beneficial but not required.
