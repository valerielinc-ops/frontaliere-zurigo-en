Full description

Immediately or by agreement we are looking for you as

Specialist care (FaBe) for the residential community of sea stars with increased need for care (80%) We have lived here for many years, many of us almost all our lives. In a pavilion in the middle of the Wagerenhof area, we, five residents, are dependent on our employees to accompany us in everyday life. In our everyday life we need clear structures through recurring processes and rituals, as well as a close accompaniment on the relationship level, which gives us security and support. Nevertheless, we can react unexpectedly emotionally and need to set a calm and understanding accompaniment through benevolent boundaries. The Wagerenhof Foundation in Uster has been a permanent home for around 250 people with multiple cognitive impairments for more than 100 years and offers over 50 employees in assisted jobs. We are a recognized, non-profit institution with a social mission. With a demand-oriented offer of living and care places as well as various day and leisure offers, we promote the quality of life, participation and self-determination of our residents. The special features of the Wagerenhof include size and Dörfli character, which allows space for encounters. For employees with different careers and professionalism, the Wagerenhof Foundation is an attractive employer with a wide range of development opportunities. For learners, Wagerenhof is a competent training partner. In these activities you support us together with your teammates:

  • Accompaniment and care of us residents
  • Agogic care and reference work
  • Interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation
  • Assistance in managing the group budget
  • Planning and organisational tasks

You suit us and this place, if you also put us and in the center of your actions and bring the following:

  • Degree as a specialist Supervision (FaBe) EFZ
  • Experience accompanying people with disabilities with challenging behavior
  • Self-employment and flexibility
  • Differentiated perception and reflection
  • Good PC knowledge and good German skills in word and writing

This makes us the place for your work

  • The appreciative, human interaction as part of a well-established team
  • Professional support through an interdisciplinary and professionally acting environment
  • The village character and the park-like structure of the Wagerenhof
