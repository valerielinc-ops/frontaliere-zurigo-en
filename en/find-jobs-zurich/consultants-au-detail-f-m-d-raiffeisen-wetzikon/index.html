20.07.2026 Wetzikon

Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Zürcher Oberland, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Uster, Gossau, Pfäffikon, Wetzikon and in Hinwil, we are looking for a personality as a private customer consultant (m/f/d) with a place of work in Wetzikon.

What awaits you?

  • Comprehensive, needs-based and solution-oriented advice to customers in all credit issues
  • Advising clients on pensions, insurance, retirement and investment issues
  • Development of its own customer book through targeted acquisition of new customers
  • Recognizing and exploiting customer potential through active and continuous customer portfolio support
  • Cooperation in target group-specific market development measures such as contact campaigns, events and representative tasks

What do you bring?

  • Banking or financial education completed
  • Proven professional experience in advising credit and mortgage clients with an affinity for investment and pension issues
  • Strong customer- and sales-oriented thinking and acting
  • Self-employed and responsible work
  • Teamwork, initiative, flexibility
  • Sympathetic and profitable appearance
