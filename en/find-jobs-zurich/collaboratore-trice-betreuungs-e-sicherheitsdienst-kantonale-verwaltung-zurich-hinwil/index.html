Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Justice and Reintegration Enforcement Institutions Zurich Staff Support and Security Service 80

  • 100% Justice and Reintegration (JuWe) is responsible for the execution of sentences and measures as part of the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs of the Canton of Zurich and for the execution of various types of detention. For our 1,400 employees from around 40 professions, the successful social reintegration of people who have become guilty of criminal offences, the prevention of relapses, and the detention and preparation of the departure of persons without valid residence permits are of particular importance. The Bachtel prison centre (VZB) is one of the enforcement facilities of the canton of Zurich (VEZ) – one of seven main departments of the JuWe – and is an institution specialized in the open execution of imprisonment and replacement sentences. To strengthen the support and security service, we are looking for a flexible personality by immediately or by appointment. Tasks
  • Accompanying and caring for detained persons to prevent damage to their detention
  • Maintaining order and security for the detained persons, employees and third parties
  • Maintaining the health of the detained persons and accompanying them to external offices
  • Operation of the security centre and monitoring of the locking systems (inside and outside) as well as operation of the telephone control centre with overview and reliability
  • Reception of visitors and carrying out gift, effects and X-ray checks as well as trustful care of inmates and visitors, including the recognition and management of crisis situations
  • Careful completion of administrative tasks to support smooth operation
  • Interdisciplinary, cross-sectoral cooperation with employees working for the enforcement center Third parties
