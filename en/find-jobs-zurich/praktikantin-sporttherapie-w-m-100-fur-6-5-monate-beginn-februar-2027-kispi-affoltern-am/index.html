Your tasks

  • You train with children and adolescents with motor and cognitive limitations.
  • Your activities include planning and leading different sports groups for different levels of performance in school sports, endurance, wheelchair sports, coordination, swimming, climbing and cycling and much more.
  • You take over the instruction and supervision in medical training therapy.
  • Leading patient-related administration is also one of your tasks.
  • In addition, you work with the other therapy teams as well as with specialists from the fields of medicine, care and school of the center. Your profile
  • Bachelor in Sports Science and/or Movement Science or shortly before graduation
  • Experience in dealing with children
  • Enthusiasm-capable, willing to learn and team-capable personality
  • Self-employed and responsible
