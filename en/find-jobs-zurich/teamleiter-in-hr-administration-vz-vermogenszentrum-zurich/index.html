Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We advise individuals and companies about money, manage assets and offer attractive financial services. As we are growing strongly, we are looking for reinforcements at our headquarters at Paradeplatz in Zurich.

  • Together with the team of six, you are responsible for the entire HR administration of A-Z. You take responsibility for planning and organizing your team’s day-to-day operations.
  • You advise and supervise all employees and supervisors on questions of administrative HR topics.
  • You take responsibility for various control tasks (work contracts, entries, various confirmations, etc.)
  • You support your team in day-to-day operations. This includes the preparation of employment contracts including the contract components, the registration of new employees in the Abacus and the processing of withdrawals.
  • You work on internal projects (such as optimization of HR processes) and create various reports.
  • You have an oath. HR specialist card or comparable training with a specialisation in HR.
  • You are well-versed in personnel administration and have already led a team.
  • You have in-depth office knowledge and ideally already worked with Abacus.
  • You are service-oriented and work independently in a dynamic environment.
  • You like to take responsibility, are eager to know and are characterized by a high resilience.
  • You are a team player and bring a "hands-on" mentality.
  • We work at eye level in an uncomplicated and open environment.
  • You can expect a varied area of responsibility in which you can contribute your ideas as well as a collegial team on which you can count.
  • We support you individually and support you in the context of the job profile also in an external further education.
  • You have the opportunity to help shape and develop a growing company through your own initiative and commitment.
  • Our surveys on satisfaction regularly show that our employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
