Full description

In this exciting position, you will play a central role in the continuous improvement of our electronic production and ensure that lean principles are integrated into everyday production. Your tasks in detail:

  • Development and further development of lean concepts for the electronic manufacturing sectors and ensuring their sustainable implementation
  • Planning and implementation of optimization measures in close cooperation with the participating specialist areas
  • Implementation of lean and 5S audits and derivation and follow-up of appropriate improvement measures
  • Mediation of lean methods and active support of the specialist areas in their practical application
  • Participation in the implementation of new processes and ensuring efficient integration in terms of production, logistics and standardization
  • Analysis of existing production processes as well as identification and realization of optimization potential
  • Close cooperation with internal interfaces and support for cross-cutting improvement initiatives
  • Ensuring and further developing efficient production processes with a focus on productivity, quality and process stability WHAT YOU SHOULD INVOLVE
  • Completed technical training and further education in Lean Management, for example as a Lean Green Belt or with a comparable qualification
  • Demonstrable practical experience in lean management as well as in-depth knowledge of lean methodology and 5S
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing (at least level C1)
  • Good user knowledge in MS Office and experience in dealing with SAP
  • High willingness to perform as well as a strong sense of responsibility and initiative
  • Strong understanding of internal and external customer requirements as well as a consistent focus on their needs
  • Clear and user-friendly communication as well as the ability to convey complex issues in an understandable way
  • Team-oriented personality with a high willingness to change as well as the ability to work together across departments and to represent goals convincingly WHAT WE ARE TO YOU
  • A dynamic working environment with exciting projects
  • Flexible working hours (active and trusted working hours)
  • Very good social benefits and a super-compulsory pension fund
  • Employee benefits of a large corporation, employee share purchase program
  • Promotion of needs-based training
  • Staff restaurant and external catering and shopping facilities nearby
  • Individual internal and external personnel development measures in cooperation with our Rheinmetall Academy
  • Workplace a few minutes walk from the Zurich-Oerlikon train station KONTAKTDETAILS Responsible contact: Michael Zmoos For questions about your application, please use the contact form. Please note that dossiers of recruiters are not considered.
