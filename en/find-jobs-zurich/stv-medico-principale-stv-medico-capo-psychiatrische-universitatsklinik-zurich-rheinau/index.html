Original description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With over 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a universitary clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility.

Forensic Psychiatry and Psychotherapy (FPP) is one of the four PUK clinics that performs outpatient and inpatient treatment and assessment tasks. For our Center for Stationary Forensic Therapy (ZSFT), with 92 places the largest inpatient treatment facility in Switzerland, we are looking for a new treatment facility in January 2027 or, by appointment, a new one.
