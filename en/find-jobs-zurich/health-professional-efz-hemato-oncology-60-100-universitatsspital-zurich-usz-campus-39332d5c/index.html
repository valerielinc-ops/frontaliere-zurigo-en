Full description

Would you like to competently accompany patients in hemato-oncology and internal medicine and work in a dedicated team? You can expect a versatile task with space for professional development and personal commitment
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • Care and care of patients with hematological, oncological and internistic diseases in tandem with a nurse • Accompaniment and counselling of patients and relatives during all phases of the disease and therapy and preparation for everyday life at home • Assumption of administrative, logistics and household tasks in the department • Transfer of knowledge and experience to FaGe learners in everyday work Requirements • Completed training as a health professional EFZ or shortly before successful completion of this training • Responsible personality with good communication skills and pleasure in working together in a team • Positive attitude and pleasure in the demanding and intensive care of patients • Motivation to learn new things and to continuously expand and deepen your own specialist knowledge We offer: Are you looking for a diverse and responsible task and would like to develop professionally? At our interdisciplinary bed department of hemato-oncology, you can expect a varied and demanding working environment with complex patient situations. Even with experience from long-term care, you will find a well-accompanied entry into the acute area with us. During your individual and practical training, you will be supported by a motivated nursing team as well as the management team from subject, leadership and education and promoted in your professional development. An appreciative cooperation and lived teamwork are a matter of course for us. In addition, we take your wishes into account during service planning and offer individual working time models so that work and private life are well compatible.
