Full description

As a leading Swiss construction and real estate service provider, Implenia develops, realizes and manages living spaces, working environments and infrastructure for future generations in Switzerland and Germany. Implenia also offers tunnelling and related infrastructure projects in other markets. The company combines the know-how of highly qualified consulting, development, planning and execution units under one roof into an integrated, multinationally leading construction and real estate service provider. Headquartered in Opfikon near Zurich, Implenia employs more than 8,000 people (FTE) across Europe and achieved a turnover of CHF 3.5 billion in 2025. More information at implenia.com

For our Zurich region, the "Modernisation D-CH" unit within the "Buildings" division, we are looking for you as our new Head of Offering and Cost Planning (m/f/d). Your place of work will be our headquarters «Connect» in Opfikon.

This position gives you the opportunity to play a key role in attracting new projects and actively shape the development of a strategic area at Implenia.

You move that

  • You lead and develop the team for offer management and construction cost planning and actively shape processes, methods and the use of AI.
  • You are responsible for the processing of project developments, overall performance competitions and TU submissions up to the successful submission of a bid.
  • You ensure the cost-effectiveness, plausibility and quality of calculations and are responsible for risk management in the offer phase.
  • You identify potential for optimization and develop innovative and economical solutions for our customers.
  • You work closely with the management, specialists and internal stakeholders and contribute to the strategic development of the area.

This is why you succeed

  • You have completed your studies as an architect: in or civil engineer: in (ETH/FH) and ideally an additional business education (MBA/BWL).
  • You have at least 10 to 15 years of experience in the GU/TU environment and in construction cost planning.
  • You have successfully managed complex large-scale projects and have a robust network in the real estate and construction industry.
  • You convince through entrepreneurial thinking, sound calculation knowledge and a strong understanding of economic connections.
  • You’ll be a high-communication leader with high social skills, empathy, and the ability to inspire teams for shared goals.

This is what we offer you

  • A multicultural working environment with the chance to shape a future worth living in
  • State-of-the-art technologies and a contemporary workplace at a sustainable company
  • A company that promotes talent and the training of its employees
  • The security of a financially healthy, broad-based group with strong roots in Switzerland
  • An exciting selection of benefits:
  • 5 weeks holidays and 9 paid bridge days
  • an in-house pension fund with very good coverage and 3 contribution levels
  • numerous discounts on well-known suppliers of products and services
