Original description

137147, 80 - 100%, Oerlikon, Zurich, Hybrid

Do you want to contribute your reporting and analysis skills to our financial team and actively support the management of our P&C business? Does the idea of creating Business Insights together with various internal partners and driving digital innovation in the financial sector motivate you? Then we should get to know each other.

As a planner and performance reporting analyst, you strengthen the Finance division in the area of Planning & Performance Management – P&C Reporting & Analytics at Zurich Switzerland and make an important contribution to our joint success. You analyze the financial development of our P&C business and create transparency for informed business decisions with meaningful reports, forward-looking reviews and analyses. You work closely with internal partners from Under-Painting, Actuary, Sales, Damage and Finance.

That's what awaits you

  • You periodically start and analyze the degrees and, through presentations and explanations, you create transparency about the results.
  • You and the team work out a multi-year financial plan for our lines of business and segments.
  • You start regular dailies and analyze differences in income statement (P&L) from plan and year-on-year.
  • You support system and process improvements and thus increase the degree of automation and standardization.
  • You support sound business decisions with ad hoc analyses, and help shape projects and initiatives in the financial field.

That's what makes you feel

  • Graduate in higher education or university with a focus on finance, controlling or a similar subject.
  • First relevant experience in controlling, reporting, accounting, under-paper or portfolio management.
  • A strong analytical ability and a structured and goal-oriented approach.
  • Very good MS Office skills, especially Excel and PowerPoint, as well as experience with Power BI.
  • German and English skills.
