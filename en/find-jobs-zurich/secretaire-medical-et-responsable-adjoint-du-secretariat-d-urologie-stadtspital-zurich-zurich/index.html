Full description

For the Clinic of Urology in the Department of Surgery, we are looking for a committed and resilient personality immediately or by agreement.

Tasks

  • You organize and schedule the urological consultation hours across locations.
  • You collect patient data in the central administration system (SAP and KISIM).
  • You send medical reports electronically and control the signature and correction workflow.
  • You take over the telephone service: inform, forward and keep track.
  • You calculate outpatient medical histories and write the office hours correspondence.
  • You do general secretarial work incl. Archiving, mailing and file orders both internally and externally.
  • In the absence of the head of the secretariat, you take over the operational management of the team and ensure a smooth running of day-to-day business.

Profile

  • Completed training as medical secretary, MPA EFZ or FaGe with corresponding further training
  • Several years of experience as medical secretary in the hospital environment
  • Strong quality and service awareness
  • Savvy PC User Knowledge and Routine with Digital Tools
  • Self-employed and reliable working methods
  • Open-minded personality with high social competence
  • Organizational talent with overview and peace

We offer

We offer you more than just a job! Our city hospital world is characterized by an open du-culture, an uncomplicated, personal togetherness and the necessary touch of humor in everyday life. As a team of over 120 professional groups, we work every day in very different ways for the well-being of our patients. About us

With over 36'000 inpatients and around 455,000 outpatients per year, the Stadtspital Zürich ranks among the top 10 Swiss hospitals. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,500 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
