Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School Rychenberg Prorector 80 - 100% At the beginning of the spring semester 2027, a newly created position as vice-rector must be filled. The Cantonal School Rychenberg Winterthur is a long-school with old- and modern-language profiles and the profile PPP, a bilingual school course German / English and a specialised school with the profiles Pedagogy as well as communication and information. With 1250 students, 275 of whom attend FMS, KRW is one of the larger schools in the canton. Further information can be found on our website (www.krw.ch ). Our offer

  • versatile leadership role in pedagogical, personnel, administrative and financial areas with great opportunities for design in a dynamic environment
  • well-functioning, open-minded school management team and teaching staff
  • Living school culture with a diverse offer
  • Conditions of employment according to cantonal specifications Your profile
  • communicative and team-capable personality with a sovereign appearance, well versed in dealing with students, parents, teachers and employees in administration and operations
  • High performance, resilience, organizational talent and leadership ability
  • Knowledge of the teaching offer of the Zurich secondary schools
  • completed university studies, teaching diploma for maturity schools, teaching experience at secondary level II in a subject granted at KRW
  • Certificate as Principal or willingness to acquire one of your tasks
  • Supervision and management of years of FMS and Gymnasium
  • Participation in school management and development, representation of the school towards pupils, parents, authorities and at conferences
  • Supervision and management of some specialist bodies including personnel planning and assessment of teachers
  • Planning and support of projects
  • Commission work
  • Give 10 lessons per week More information is available from our designated Rector Felix Ziegler, felix.ziegler@krw.ch , tel. +41 (0)52 244 04 04. We look forward to receiving your full application from Evelyn Schertler Kaufmann, Mag. phil. MAS, President of the School Commission, until 9. August 2026. Please submit your documents via our online tool.
