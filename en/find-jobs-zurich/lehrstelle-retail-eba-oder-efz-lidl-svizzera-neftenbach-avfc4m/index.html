Full description

Do you already have ideas for the time after finishing school? Start your future with us and take advantage of the development opportunities at Lidl Schweiz. Our stores are at the center of what we do. As a retail apprentice (with a focus on creating shopping experiences), whether EBA or EFZ, you contribute to the success of your Lidl store. Start/end EBA: August 1, 20XX - July 31, 20XX Start/end EFZ: August 1, 20XX - July 31, 20XX Your tasks

  • Operating cash registers and preparing cash reconciliations
  • Ensuring freshness, quality and cleanliness
  • Stocking goods and presenting merchandise
  • Checking incoming goods in the warehouse and supporting your team during inventory counts
  • Supporting the planning and implementation of promotions and seasonal displays
  • Determining stock requirements and ordering goods in the 3rd year of apprenticeship (EFZ)
  • Taking on initial shift management duties in the 3rd year of apprenticeship (EFZ)

Your profile

  • An average to very good lower-secondary school-leaving qualification
  • A foreign language at A1 level for EBA and at least A2 for EFZ
  • Enjoyment of interacting with people
  • Ability to work independently and a quick grasp
  • Teamwork, reliability and a sense of responsibility
  • Willingness to learn and commitment
  • Physical resilience
  • Very good command of German

What we offer

An apprenticeship that opens up many opportunities for you. After successfully completing your apprenticeship and if you are a good personal fit, we will develop you for a management position in the store. In addition, you benefit from the Lidl-GAV and many attractive benefits.
