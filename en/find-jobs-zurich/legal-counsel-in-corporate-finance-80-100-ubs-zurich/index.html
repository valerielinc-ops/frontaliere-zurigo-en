Full description

Key responsibilities Are you a senior lawyer with several years of post-qualification experience and a strong interest in corporate finance transactions? Are you looking for a responsible and varied role at the intersection of law, business and external advisors? Do you enjoy working in a dynamic environment, similar to the transactions team in a commercial law firm? If so, you are the person we are looking for!

What you will do You will become part of an experienced, dynamic and collegial team that is eager to embrace change and values professional exchange. You will:

  • advise on all legal matters regarding the structuring, documentation and execution of complex, domestic or cross-border credit financings, including export finance transactions covered by an export credit risk insurance
  • advise on all legal matters regarding distressed loans and recovery solutions
  • negotiate and draft contractual documents in German and English
  • give clear, precise legal advice to our business partners and other risk functions
  • help shaping the way we work in Group Legal – together with us and with AI by

The team You’ll be working in the Transaction Banking Legal team which, as part of a deal team with other functions within UBS, provides legal support (often together with outside counsel) in structuring, documenting and negotiating complex national and international corporate, leveraged and export financing transactions, as well as recovery matters. We provide our business partners in the Corporate & Institutional Clients units with high-quality, coherent advice and tailor-made solutions.

Your skills and experience

  • a master degree in law and Swiss bar admission, ideally with a postgraduate qualification (LL.M or similar)
  • proven practical experience in a similar position, preferably in the transactions business, either in a commercial law firm or in-house
  • an interest in complex legal issues and business transactions
  • being a great communicator with strong interpersonal skills, able to cooperate with and manage internal business partners and functions as well as external legal counsels
  • able to work independently and under pressure and towards challenging deadlines
  • solution oriented and able to think 'out of the box' when supporting the development of new solutions and products
  • interest and, ideally, work experience in using AI and Legal Tech tools
  • you are fluent in German and English (knowledge of Italian or French is an advantage)
