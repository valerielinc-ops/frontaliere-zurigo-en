Full description

The Swiss Epilepsy Foundation (EPI) is a multi-saving company. It includes the EPI WohnWerk, the EPI Spitalschule, the Lengg High School, the donation Dapples, a seminar center with restaurant and the EPI Central Services. In addition, the Swiss Epilepsie Foundation, together with another foundation, forms the body of the Lengg AG Clinic, a clinic for epilepsy and neurorehabilitation. Around 1000 employees work for these social tasks. The area is located on the outskirts of Zurich, with a fantastic view of Lake Zurich and the Alps. Tasks:

  • Complex and demanding care of all non-medical services (hotel/catering) from our patients from entry to exit
  • Advice, ordering and service of food and beverage selection in the room and in the group rooms according to medical regulations
  • Leading incoming calls and ensuring a consistent high standard
  • Information about our diverse services in the hotel industry
  • Regular working hours distributed by Mo. – So. between 7:00 – 19:30
  • At least two free weekends a month
  • Monthly service planning one month in advance Requirements:
  • Multiannual professional experience in the service area in the hotel industry / gastronomy compellingly, experience in health care of advantage
  • High flexibility and willingness to accept services with commitment on weekends and holidays
  • Very good oral German knowledge (min. level B2)
  • Loadability, communication strength, high quality understanding and a pronounced customer/service orientation We offer: In the Swiss Epilepsy Foundation, you will encounter different professional and claim groups that enrich your work daily. Through your own ideas and contributions, you actively shape your work and thereby make a valuable contribution in a meaningful environment. You can rely on an advanced employer with good working conditions and a healthy pension fund. Floor service From room service to physical well-being to technical equipment and assistance on departure – our hotel industry team takes care that our stationary patients do not need anything.
