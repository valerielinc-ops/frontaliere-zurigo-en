Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate HR-Fachperson Administration 80 - 100% limited to one year with permanent employment option The primary school office is responsible for school organizational tasks and the administration of the teaching staff. The teaching staff department supports the schools in the personnel administration and controls all processes relating to the employment and wages of the cantonal primary school teachers, school management and deputies. For our personnel sector we are looking for 1. November 2026 or by agreement a collegial, flexible and committed personality. Their tasks

  • You are the main contact person for the school communities assigned to you and ensure the administrative HR activities from entry to exit.
  • They examine and process applications, forms and orders for teachers and school leaders as well as vicars.
  • They advise on the telephone, by e-mail or in letter form, among other things, school nurses, school administrations and other administrative offices.
  • They represent colleagues in their absence.
  • You actively support your team during the months of high workload (April to September). This place is made for you if
  • They appreciate working according to process specifications and enjoy detail.
  • You have networked thinking and you are looking for complex issues as a challenge.
  • They are masters/in self-organization and want to work largely independently.
  • You convince us with your skills, your energy and your experience. We are open to applications from people with professional experience in a field management activity, ideally in administration or human resources. This is what we offer you.
  • The first few months you will be intensively trained and accompanied in our complex processes.
  • A collaborative and helpful team, in an appreciative environment.
  • An exciting and varied area of responsibility with an insight into the world of education.
  • An attractive workplace in the immediate vicinity of Zurich Central Station.
  • Conditions of employment according to cantonal guidelines. Curious? We look forward to meeting you. Further information about the position can be provided by our team leader Susanne Kaspar on 043 259 53 10. Further information about the primary education office can be found at www.zh.ch/vsa

Pensum: 80-100 %
