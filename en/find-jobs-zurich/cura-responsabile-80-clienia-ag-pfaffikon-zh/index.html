Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With over 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric services offered by Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in outpatient, day clinic and inpatient settings. The group employs around 1800 people and generated sales of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For our Privé Station D1 for older adults, we are looking for a

The range of therapies is aimed at older adults in life crises with depression, anxiety disorders, neurodegenerative diseases from mild to moderate dementia as well as somatic concomitant and underlying diseases. After careful diagnostic clarification, we offer therapeutic services ranging from low-threshold support to psychiatric/psychotherapeutic settings. In addition to occupational and physiotherapy, the range of therapies also includes complementary medical services and psychosocial tasks. Our patients are cared for in close cooperation with the case therapist in the caregiver system. The atmosphere on the ward is characterized by a respectful attitude and the careful cultivation of interpersonal relationships.

Your tasks

  • Responsibility for the development of nursing and review of the quality of care on the ward
  • the individual care and support of patients and contact with relatives as part of the caregiver work and in consultation with the core team
  • Participation in subject-specific working groups and projects
  • Ensuring cooperative interdisciplinary cooperation
  • the management and co-management of psychotherapeutic group offers and reports
  • Consultation and planning of treatment in the interprofessional team
  • Supporting patients in crisis situations

Since we work with permanent night watches, you will primarily be deployed in a two-shift system (early and late shift).

Your profile For this exciting job, you have a degree in nursing at HF / FH level and professional experience in the field of psychiatric nursing. Ideally, you have a CAS in gerontology, or you are willing to complete it. You would like to work in a special area where customer orientation is lived. Variety and changing situations are a challenge for you and do you enjoy it? In addition, are you an open, honest, communicative and present personality and are you willing to continuously educate yourself and pass on this knowledge to the nursing team? Then ward D1 is the right place for you. We look forward to applicants with joy, humor, their own ideas, a great interest in interprofessional work and cultivated manners.

Do you feel addressed now? Then feel free to apply to us. We look forward to getting to know you.
