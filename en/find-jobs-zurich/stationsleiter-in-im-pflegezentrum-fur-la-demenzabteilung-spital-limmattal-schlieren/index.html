We look after our residents and their relatives with expertise and a lot of human commitment in often complex situations. The treatment, care and care in high quality therefore require specific knowledge in the field of geriatrics as well as individually adapted support in health, social or mental matters. This is why our multi-professional team works closely together. It consists of Ärzt:innen,pflegers, a social worker, activation specialists, therapist:innen and pastoral workers.

Direct applications are preferred, external recruitment offices are not treated as a priority.

Here they engage

  • Organization of care and care of medium to severely needy and demented residents in the context of long-term care (24 places)
  • Planning, control and monitoring of the entire care process (Kisim, RAI-NH)
  • Personnel and professional management as well as advice for the approximately 30 employees
  • Determination of the need for action and development as well as initiation and implementation of corresponding measures at station level taking into account the service mandate
  • Assistance in care as needed (about 50%)
  • have completed vocational training as a Dipl. Care professional HF/FH
  • bring training in management or a few years of experience in a similar function
  • have expertise and expertise in geriatrics, palliative care and in particular dementia
  • Advanced training in dementia is beneficial
  • Reduced employee parking
  • Motorcycle parking, lockable bicycle parking
  • With the ZVV Bonus Pass / Z-Bonus Pass we offer an attractive ÖV subscription for employees
