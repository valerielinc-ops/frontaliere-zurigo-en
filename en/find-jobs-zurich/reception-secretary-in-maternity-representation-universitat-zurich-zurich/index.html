Tasks

  • Reception and care of our patients as the first contact person of the clinic
  • Admission of new patients and maintenance and management of patient data
  • Telephone patient service including appointment agreement, appointment postponements and information provision
  • Organisation and implementation of the call and processing of transfers
  • Management, processing and archiving of medical histories and patient records
  • Processing of general administrative tasks and correspondence
  • Ordering and managing office equipment and supporting smooth clinical operation
  • Ensuring a professional, friendly and patient-oriented reception area

Requirements

You have completed a commercial training EFZ or a training as a dental assistant: in EFZ and ideally bring professional experience in the reception, practice or clinic area. They are characterized by a friendly, empathetic and professional attitude and meet patients with a high service orientation and empathy. Even in hectic situations, you work reliably, structured and keep an overview. They are organizationally strong, flexible and appreciate the cooperation in a committed team. The safe handling of the common Microsoft Office applications is a matter of course for you

  • Knowledge of practical software such as Vitodent is an advantage. Very good knowledge of German in word and writing we presuppose, English or other language skills are welcome. A responsible and discreet handling of sensitive patient data rounds off your profile.
