Full description

This work location includes a cafeteria. The meeting place in Zurich provides a welcoming gathering place for students and external visitors. From Monday to Friday, our team delights an average of 690 guests here with a varied range of offerings – from daily changing menus and a fresh salad buffet to regional and fairly produced dishes. Are you our new team member who will support us as a catering employee (60-80%) immediately or by arrangement? You work 5 days per week between Monday and Saturday, usually from 06:30 to 15:30, 11:00 to 20:00 or from 14:00 to 23:00.Due to the semester breaks, the workload varies from 80-100% in the main semester and 40-60% in the secondary semesters (January, February, April, July, August).

Your task menu

  • Greeting, serving and advising guests
  • Organizing aperitifs, coffee breaks and staffed events
  • Organizing internal / external catering and special events
  • Handling F&B deliveries
  • Assisting with setting up the event or preparing the equipment and premises
  • Ensuring a high level of guest satisfaction
  • Preparing the mise en place
  • Ensuring an appealing presentation of goods
  • Supporting the cafeteria as needed
  • Ensuring cleanliness and order in the work environment
  • Carrying out general tidying and cleaning work

Your recipe for success

  • Experience in the hospitality industry is essential
  • Experience in dealing with guests and customers
  • At least 2 years of professional experience in catering, banquets and events
  • Good spoken German skills (min. B1), any additional language is a plus
  • Organizational skills
  • Connected and forward-thinking mindset
  • Enthusiastic sales personality
  • Passionate host
  • Winning and charming presence
  • A love of good food and drink

This is what we offer you

We create teams that support one another and where you are encouraged. We work together and focus on everything that does you good. Among other things, we place great importance on pay transparency and pay equity. For this position, we offer an annual salary of between CHF 52'000 and CHF 57'000 (including 13th-month salary) for a 100% workload, depending on your experience and qualifications. If you would like to share your passion for gastronomy with us, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
