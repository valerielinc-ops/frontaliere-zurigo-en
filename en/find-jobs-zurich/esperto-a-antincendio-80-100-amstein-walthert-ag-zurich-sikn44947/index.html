Full description

Amstein + Walthert is an engineering company with around 1100 employees:innen. As engineers, planners and consultants, we work on all aspects of the technology of civil engineering and construction over the entire life cycle. Our solutions are innovative, elegant and sustainable. To strengthen our fire protection team in Zurich, we are looking for an experienced and committed personality with an integral and goal-oriented way of working. We offer you a motivated, dynamic team and a modern and generous working environment in the immediate vicinity of the Oerlikon train station. In this versatile function, you contribute significantly to the further development of our area and take on responsibility in exciting projects. These are your tasks

  • For authorities, clients:inside, architect:inside and planner:inside you are the contact person
  • You develop fire protection concepts incl. Plan sketches, concept descriptions, proofs, etc.
  • You carry out the quality control fire protection on the construction site to ensure a professional implementation until commissioning
  • Cooperation in special concepts such as evacuation, smoke extraction, integral safety tests, etc.
  • As a project manager: you have the responsibility regarding quality, costs and deadlines You bring that with you
  • Degree as an engineer:in / technician:in or comparable training
  • Further training as a fire protection expert: required in VKF
  • Several years of experience in the planning and implementation of demanding buildings
  • Practical experience in structural, technical and organizational fire protection
  • Interest in solution-oriented and conceptual work
  • Creativity, commitment, teamwork and good manners
  • High customer awareness
  • Good knowledge in MS-Office, CAD/BIM knowledge is advantageous
  • Safe handling of the German language in word and writing
