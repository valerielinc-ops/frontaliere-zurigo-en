Full description

In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed personality who can play an operational and strategic role in shaping our internal value assurance process for complex national and international major projects. Value Assurance is a central process at Implenia and thus plays a key role in the objective of profitable growth of the Group and thus part of corporate risk management. By increasing the quality of the offer and supporting project implementation, an increase in yield and a reduction in the risk profile is achieved.

Your place of work is preferably our headquarters at Ambassador House in Opfikon, Switzerland. But other locations of Implenia are also possible as a place of work. The possibility to work partly from the home office exists.

Tasks

What tasks await you as Senior Project Risk & Value Manager (m/f/d), Opfikon, 80-100%?

  • In this role, you control the internal approval process of complex large-scale projects and support offer teams in creating their offers.
  • With regard to efficiency, quality and impact, you optimize processes – for example by reducing errors, higher hit rates and better customer feedback.
  • You conduct independent risk assessments (second opinion) and actively participate in the further development of offers together with the offer teams.
  • In addition, you support project teams along the entire life cycle – on-site as well – in the successful implementation of complex projects and carry out regular interim reviews (intermediate reviews / audits) in order to support relevant projects in the execution phase. You report directly to the management.
  • You contribute your knowledge to internal development projects, promote the exchange between different business units and develop the value assurance process as well as digital tools (e.g. data analytics, AI) and thus contribute to the continuous improvement of offer and execution quality.
  • As part of project visits, you support the international project teams through knowledge transfer and an independent view, thus establishing the connection between project and management.

Requirements

What requirements do you bring with you as Senior Project Risk & Value Manager (m/f/d), Opfikon, 80-100%?

  • You have completed your studies as a civil engineer and have at least 10 years of professional experience in the design and implementation of large and complex projects, ideally also on an international level.
  • In addition, you have a strong understanding of complex contractual relationships as well as commercial relationships and/or project controlling. Experience in risk management is an advantage.
  • You have a basic understanding of Operational Excellence and are interested in lean management and the further development of management systems. Your expertise in the application of modern risk management methods sets you apart.
  • In addition, you have experience in the support and further development of digital applications, for example in the areas of data analytics, artificial intelligence or enhanced scheduling, as well as in the optimization of digital process flows.
  • Your very good communication skills, pronounced team orientation and your process-oriented way of working distinguish you. In dialogue with colleagues from different disciplines, networking your knowledge with the know-how of others drives you.
  • Your profile is rounded off by very good knowledge of French.
  • For this you need either German or English.
