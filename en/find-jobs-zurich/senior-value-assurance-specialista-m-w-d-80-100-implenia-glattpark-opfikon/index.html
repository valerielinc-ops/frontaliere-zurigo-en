Role overview

In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed personality who can play an operational and strategic role in shaping our internal value assurance process for complex national and international major projects.

Value Assurance is a central process at Implenia and thus plays a key role in the objective of profitable growth of the Group and thus part of corporate risk management.

By increasing the quality of the offer and supporting project implementation, an increase in yield and a reduction in the risk profile is achieved.

Main responsibilities

  • What tasks await you as a Senior Value Assurance Specialist (m/f/d) 80-100%?
  • In this role, you control the internal approval process of complex large-scale projects and support offer teams in the preparation of their offers.
  • With regard to efficiency, quality and impact, you optimize processes – for example by reducing errors, higher hit rates and better customer feedback.
  • You conduct independent risk assessments (second opinion) and actively participate in the further development of offers together with the offer teams.
  • In addition, you support project teams along the entire life cycle – on-site as well – in the successful implementation of complex projects and carry out regular interim reviews (intermediate reviews / audits) in order to support relevant projects in the execution phase.
  • You report directly to the management.
  • You contribute your knowledge to internal development projects, promote the exchange between different business units and develop the value assurance process as well as digital tools (e.g. data analytics, AI) and thus contribute to the continuous improvement of offer and execution quality.
  • In the context of project visits, you support the international project teams through knowledge transfer and an independent view and thus establish the connection between project and management. Requirements
  • What requirements do you bring as a Senior Value Assurance Specialist (m/f/d) 80-100%?
  • You have completed your studies as a civil engineer and have at least 10 years of professional experience in the design and implementation of large and complex projects, ideally also on an international level.
