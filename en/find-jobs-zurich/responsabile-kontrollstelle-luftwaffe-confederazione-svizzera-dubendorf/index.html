Full description

Professionally manage the control unit for the military flight and jumping service, collect and maintain the corresponding personnel data and approvals accurately and on time and correctly update the civilian license data of the pilots

  • Basic commercial education with further education and in-depth knowledge in finance, accounting and social insurance
  • Ability to think in a networked way and capture complex relationships, a pronounced flair for numbers and good communication skills in words and writing
  • Integrous, dutiful and team capable personality with a strong service orientation
  • Good, active knowledge of a second official language and very good knowledge of English
  • In-depth user knowledge in MS Office (especially MS Excel) and ideally experience with SAP and the accounting software Abacus
