Full description

Are flowers your passion? Join our MM Stäfa branch. There, you will ensure a beautiful ambiance and advise our customers on their flower selection. Our customers also appreciate your great service at the PickMup counter when they pick up their online orders. Interested? Apply now!

Duties

  • You support us with your creativity in the flower and plant department and tie attractive and tasteful bouquets according to customer wishes with great attention to detail
  • You are responsible for various services (e.g., Textil-Fresh, photo booth, PickMup)
  • You provide first-class customer service and support our customers with various concerns, such as returns and complaints
  • You ensure the high quality of our products
  • If necessary, you also support other store areas

Requirements

  • 1-3 years of professional experience: in the flower/plant sector
  • Completed basic or further training: as a florist or in retail
  • You are customer-oriented and friendly
  • You enjoy working independently and reliably
  • You handle customer complaints confidently and communicate effectively
  • You are creative and bring our customers' floral wishes to life
  • German (very good knowledge)

What we offer

  • Healthy working: Health-promoting offers such as massages and health checks
  • Mobility: With the one-time annual leisure bonus of up to CHF 500, we contribute to your public transport subscription
  • Mobile subscription: You can look forward to special conditions for your mobile subscription
  • Occupational pension: Excellent pension fund benefits with high employer contributions
  • Social benefits: Fair social benefits
  • Cumulus points: You receive additional Cumulus points when shopping
  • Migros Bank: Preferential interest rates on private and investment accounts, free account management, discounted mortgages, and more
  • Employee offers: Up to CHF 1,000 per year (depending on your workload) for your relaxation and development – usable for fitness subscriptions, courses, health offers, or a subsidy for a public transport subscription
  • Sports and fitness offers: You can redeem a one-time annual leisure bonus of up to CHF 1,000, e.g., for an annual subscription to the M-Fitness center
  • Education and training: Support for professional education and training
