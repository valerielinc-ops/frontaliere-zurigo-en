Full description

The Operations sector plays a central role in defining technological orientation and managing SSR production. The ERP Dispo team ensures reliable operation and continuous development of the planning and layout systems landscape, with VimBiz as a central solution. As a Product Owner ERP Dispose, define and set priorities for product evolution together with the Product Manager. Connect the needs of specialist sectors to strategic objectives and, together with the team, ensure efficient and reliable exercise.

Shipments

In the Operations sector, as Product Owner of the product team ERP Dispo, you take care of the following topics:

  • Align VimBiz's development and operational management to product strategy, business requirements and needs of people who use it
  • Clarify requirements with specialist sectors and other stakeholders and translate them into activable and prioritized elements of Product Backlog
  • Define jointly and communicate clearly the vision of the product, objectives and roadmap, in close collaboration with the Product Manager
  • Guide functional implementation and coordinate with the team and external partners the planning of iterations, acceptances, releases and deployments
  • Ensure with the team the efficient management of Service Request, Incident, Problem and Change and compliance with agreed service levels
  • Coordinate interfaces and dependencies with connected systems and teams and continuously improve product and operational processes

Requirements

  • Solid professional experience in ERP or disposition systems as well as understanding of resource planning processes and activities
  • VimBiz's knowledge is an advantage
  • Experience in defining priorities and managing products or services as well as in managing Product Backlog
  • Knowledge in the management of Service Request, Incident, Problem and Change and in the coordination of release and deployment
  • Exciting communication and moderation skills as well as experience in collaboration with specialist sectors, engineers and external partners
  • Structured and customer-oriented approach, understanding of governance and quality and motivation requirements to continuously improve the entire product life cycle
  • Excellent knowledge of a national language and knowledge of German or French at least B1 level; knowledge of English at least B1 level is an advantage

What we offer

  • Possibility to actively contribute to the development of VimBiz and the central panorama of SSR systems
  • A diverse role in the interface between computer science, layout and production.
  • Agile and product-oriented collaboration with specialist sectors, Product Manager and an interdisciplinary team
  • The possibility of contributing in person to efficient disposition processes and to the technological evolution of SSR
