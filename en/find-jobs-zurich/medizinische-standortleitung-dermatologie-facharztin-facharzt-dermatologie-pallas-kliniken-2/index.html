Full description

The Pallas clinics are specialized in ophthalmology and aesthetics. With over 350 employees and 17 locations, we are one of the leading providers in Switzerland.

We offer a highly professional environment with attractive employment conditions and a motivated and dynamic team. If you have high commitment, interest in regular training and enjoyment of teamwork, excellent cooperation is already inevitable. Our modern infrastructure in all disciplines is continuously expanded to optimally support your career doctor in aesthetics.

For our medical site management in Winterthur we are looking for a committed and experienced personality as

Medical site management Dermatology Specialist doctor dermatology - 60-100 %

Responsibility

  • Medical overall responsibility for the location. Together with your team, you ensure high-quality, patient-oriented and efficient dermatological care.
  • Professional development of the medical services offered. They recognize relevant developments at an early stage and successfully integrate them into everyday hospital life.
  • Active participation in strategic and operational location development. They combine medical quality with business responsibility.
  • Maintenance and expansion of the referral network as well as sustainable cooperation with established doctors and other partners in the healthcare sector.
  • Coordinated forwarding and close cooperation with the relevant departments on interdisciplinary issues.

Requirements

  • Specialist title dermatology and venerology with proven experience in classical dermatology (conservative and surgical).
  • Leadership experience or clearly identifiable potential and motivation to take on a leadership role.
  • Entrepreneurial, independent working method with a high level of understanding of quality and services.
  • Strong social and communication skills towards patients, employees and internal and external partners.
  • Interest in aesthetic dermatology is beneficial.
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing.

Benefits

  • Leadership responsibility with real scope for design. You shape the development of your location.
  • Significant relief in administrative tasks for the focus on medicine and leadership.
  • Modern infrastructure and an interdisciplinary, cross-location team of specialists.
  • Internal and external training.
  • Market-based, performance-based remuneration.
  • Central location in the immediate vicinity of the station.

Have we aroused your interest? Then we look forward to meeting you personally!
