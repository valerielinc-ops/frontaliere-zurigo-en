Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Composition by: 01. September 2026 or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 72881

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

Do you bring organizational skills and medical understanding? As an employee in patient disposition, you use your organizational talent to ensure that our patients start their treatment in an optimally prepared manner. You support the efficient planning of operations, interventions and beds, work together with various specialist areas and make a significant contribution to smooth clinical operation.

YOUR TASKS

  • Bed planning according to nursing priorities including morning bed coordination on weekends from the home office
  • Administrative support for our patients – from registration to entry
  • Admission of elective treatments incl. Creation of operations in the system
  • Communication with medical doctors, patients and internal/external partners
  • Independent work within an interdisciplinary team

Your profile

  • Training: Specialist Health EFZ, Commercial basic training in the hospital sector, Medical practice assistant EFZ or equivalent training.
  • Experience: Knowledge in the surgical environment, in surgical coordination or in comparable scheduling functions is desirable.
  • Communication and IT skills: stylish knowledge of German in words and writing as well as addressee-oriented, clear communication. Advanced MS Office knowledge.
  • Working methods: Structured, responsible working methods. Process-oriented, networked thinking.
  • Teamwork: Teamplayer spirit, service orientation and resilience even in hectic situations.

What to expect from us – modern & employee-oriented

  • Modern workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich
  • Collegial, interdisciplinary team with a high level of expertise
  • Various training and further education offers
  • Attractive participation in public transport costs (ZVV-/Z-Pass incl.) Leisure use, SBB coupons at longer distances

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. You work with a modern medical clinic information system and can expect extensive training and development opportunities from us. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family responsibilities. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. Employees who have to move completely for their work receive four additional paid days off with a 100% workload. With a reduced workload, the days off reduce accordingly. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from an equivalent financial compensation. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.
