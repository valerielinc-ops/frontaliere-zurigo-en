USZ Campus

by March 2027 or by agreement

Do you want to promote people, accompany changes and at the same time stay close to practice? With us, you take on leadership responsibility in an innovative environment of top university medicine.

Your main tasks

  • As a group leader, you take over the personnel leadership of a team of 4 to 6 radiology specialists and promote the individual development of your employees through annual feedback discussions. You observe the goals according to the strategies and annual goals of the DPM and the Department of Neuroradiology
  • You support the department management in professional and organizational management tasks, take on joint responsibility for personnel planning, the support of new employees and the promotion of team development
  • You are actively involved in ensuring and developing image quality, implementing quality standards and participating in shared governance committees or specialist groups
  • You will actively participate in projects and change processes in the highly specialized field of neuroradiology and contribute your expertise in innovation and optimization projects.
  • You take on joint responsibility for the education, training and further education of subordinate employees and promote their professional development
  • You promote cross-occupational internal and external cooperation as well as constructive communication
  • You work actively in the routine and bring your expertise

Your profile

  • Completed training as radiology specialist HF/FH, preferably with management experience
  • Experience in neuroradiology is advantageous
  • High motivation to continuously develop the department of neuroradiology in cooperation with the department management
  • Strong organisational capacity and sense of responsibility
  • Ability to communicate across all hierarchical levels
  • Familiar in dealing with digital media, good knowledge of German in words and writing

Our offer

The Clinic of Neuroradiology is part of the clinical neurocenter and has a modern apparatus infrastructure. We offer you an exciting and responsible job in a university, top medical environment in which you can actively contribute your knowledge and experience and further develop it. Together with the management team, you are committed to a high-quality imaging service and make an important contribution to the further development of the department. In addition, we offer you the opportunity to participate in scientific studies, attractive employment conditions and various training and further education opportunities.
