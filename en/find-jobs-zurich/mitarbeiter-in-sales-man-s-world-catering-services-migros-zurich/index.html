Full description

You like to win new exhibitors, stay tuned for cancellations and work in a structured way? For Man’s World, we are looking for a strong sales personality who actively addresses new customers, accompanies offers until the end and also tackles administration and event implementation. Your focus is clearly on active sales.

Luogo di lavoro: Catering Services Migros, Hall 550, Birchstrasse 150, 8050 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You research suitable companies and actively win new exhibitors for Man’s World
  • You make contact, recognize the needs of potential partners and present suitable participation opportunities
  • You create offers, conduct negotiations and accompany interested parties until the conclusion
  • You keep contacts, offers, agreements and appointments reliable and ensure a clean administrative process.
  • You support the planning and implementation of the main event and side events – especially in the support of the exhibitors and the implementation on site.
  • You coordinate meetings, briefings and debriefings with internal and external stakeholders.

Requirements

  • At least 2-3 years of professional experience: ideally in B2B acquisition or in the sale of offers in need of explanation
  • Completed basic or further education: in the commercial sector, or in a comparable function
  • Enjoy addressing potential new customers independently and consistently pursue sales opportunities
  • Sales skills, persuasiveness and a secure appearance in conversation with different contacts
  • A structured and precise way of working: You keep track of many contacts, offers and appointments
  • Willingness to reliably take on administrative tasks in addition to sales and to help with operational events
  • A completed commercial basic or further education or a comparable qualification
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • English (very good knowledge)

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
