Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Prisons and Reintegration Detention Centers Zurich Legal / Secretary / Legal Service 80 - 100% With around 1,400 employees, JuWe is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as for carrying out various types of detention. Our employees from around 40 professions support and promote the reintegration of delinquents and work effectively and progressively for a safe and just society. The Zurich Detention Centers (UGZ) are one of JuWe’s eight main departments, comprising seven companies with approximately 735 prison places and approximately 500 employees. They are responsible for provisional arrest, pre-trial detention and security detention, as well as the premature execution of penalties and measures until transfer to a prison institution. In this way, they fulfil a central function within the Zurich justice system.   • Design and implementation of internal training and information events on relevant legal bases for employees
