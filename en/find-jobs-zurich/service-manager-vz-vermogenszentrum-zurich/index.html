Full description

VZ is Switzerland's leading independent financial services provider. We advise private individuals and companies on all matters relating to money, manage assets and offer attractive financial services. As a result, we are very successful and growing strongly. To strengthen our IT team, we are looking for a solution-oriented and committed individual in Zurich.

  • Together with your team colleagues, you take responsibility for procurement as well as IT partner and service request management.
  • You drive the further development of the ITSM platform (based on ServiceNow) and actively participate in its implementation.
  • Together with the stakeholders, you ensure a high level of data quality, consistency and integrity in the CMDB.
  • You manage existing partners, monitor contracts and conduct tenders in compliance with our requirements.
  • You monitor service delivery to our internal customers, optimize reporting and ensure that quality and risk management are implemented seamlessly.
  • Completed degree or further education in computer science or business information systems
  • Several years of experience in a comparable role, ideally in the financial services sector
  • In-depth knowledge of configuring ServiceNow (workflows, forms, service catalog, reports, CMDB, ITSM processes)
  • Sound knowledge of ITIL and process design
  • Strong service orientation and a “hands-on” mentality
  • In addition to an exciting and challenging role in an interdisciplinary environment, you have the opportunity to help shape the development of a central IT Management Office through your initiative and commitment.
  • You are also in the right place with us when it comes to your professional development: We support you individually and also support you with external training as part of your job profile.
  • With us, you can freely choose your workload, leaving more time for further education, family, sports and projects that are important to you. The 24. and 31. December are paid holidays for all employees.
  • Anonymized satisfaction surveys regularly confirm that employees rate VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it too!
