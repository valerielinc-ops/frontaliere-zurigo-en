Full description

Brand of the Planzer Group: Planzer Paket.

Planzer Paket stands for reliable and fast parcel deliveries in Switzerland. As part of our team, you actively contribute to further developing our high quality standards and service. We offer you a varied job in a future-oriented company that focuses on sustainable solutions. Become part of our team and shape the future of the package world!

Tasks

  • As the first contact person, you accompany employees and managers from entry to exit
  • You listen, accompany conversations and find fair solutions even in demanding situations
  • You answer labour law and personnel-related questions competently, clearly and empathetically
  • In BGM and Care Management you coordinate measures and cooperation with our external Care Manager
  • From needs clarification to successful entry, you will help shape the entire recruitment process
  • You have an eye on HR key figures and human resources and support the branch manager in planning
  • In HR projects, you develop processes with regard to quality, efficiency and the needs of our employees.

Profile

  • A basic commercial training and an HR specialist card are part of your backpack
  • Several years of experience as an HR generalist bring you as much pleasure as a dynamic HR everyday life
  • In Swiss employment law, you are saddle-proof and use your knowledge in a solution-oriented manner.
  • As a connecting HR personality, you act close to business, create trust and take responsibility
  • Health, working ability and a respectful cooperation are important to you
  • You like to work with HR indicators and derive targeted measures from them
  • Structured, independent and solution-oriented, you master your everyday life and communicate stylishly in German

Benefits

  • With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • With us you start with 5 weeks of holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Get ready for parking - with us you can park for free

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
