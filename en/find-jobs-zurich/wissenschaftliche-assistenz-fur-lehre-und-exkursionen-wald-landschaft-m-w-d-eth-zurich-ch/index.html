Scientific assistant for teaching and excursions Forest & Landscape (m/f/d) 60%, Zurich, temporary Printed Printing Do you enjoy forest, landscape, teaching and accompanying students on their way through their studies? Do you want to work not only in the lecture hall, but also outside in the forest, on excursions and in practical teaching formats? Then we look for you. The Professorship for Forest Resource Management FORM at the ETH Zurich is looking for a scientific assistant for teaching in the field of forestry and landscape in the degree program Environmental Natural Sciences. The position combines professional depth with a lot of room for manoeuvre, direct contact with students and a varied working day between teaching, organization, supervision and teamwork. About us The FORM Group is a dynamic, interdisciplinary research team at ETH Zurich. We deal with the question of how forests can be sustainably managed, understood and protected today and in the future. For this purpose, we develop new approaches to inventory of forest resources, work with modern data and modeling methods and deal with decisions under uncertainty and risk. In teaching, we are strongly engaged in the Department of Environmental Systems Sciences D-USYS. We offer lectures, internships, field courses and excursions around forest resource management, forest ecology, landscape management and applied decision support. Our teaching is practical, interactive and often where the forest is best understood: outside. The appointment shall take place as soon as possible by agreement. This post is limited to a maximum of 6 years. Job description

  • You support and coordinate courses, internships and excursions in the deepening forest and landscape
  • This includes the preparation and implementation of teaching units, the supervision of students in exercises and field courses as well as the participation in the further development of our teaching formats.
  • An important part of your work is the accompaniment of bachelor and master theses
  • You will help students develop exciting questions, apply clean methods and prepare their results in an understandable way
  • In addition, you support the FORM Group in organizational and scientific aspects of teaching operations
  • No day is quite like the other: sometimes it is about planning an excursion, sometimes it is about supervising an exercise, sometimes it is about exchanging ideas with practice partners, students or colleagues.
  • It is precisely this diversity that makes the job particularly exciting: You build up a broad professional, didactic and organizational competence profile step by step and develop in many areas in a short time – from university teaching to project coordination to communication with different target groups. Profile
  • You have completed a university degree in environmental sciences, forestry sciences, Öcology, geography, biology or a related discipline or are about to graduate
  • You have a great interest in forest, landscape and sustainable resource management
  • You enjoy working with students, enjoy communicating complex content in an understandable way and feel comfortable in an interdisciplinary team
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English are important because our teaching takes place in both German and English
  • Knowledge of the degree program Environmental Sciences, in-depth knowledge of forest ecology and forest management as well as initial didactic experience, for example as teaching assistant, are advantageous
  • We are looking for a person who likes to organize, take responsibility, communicate openly and desire
