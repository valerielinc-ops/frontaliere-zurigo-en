What you do with us

  • You support the establishment and expansion of strategic brokerage, partner and distribution relationships
  • You will analyze market, competition and digitalization trends and derive new business opportunities and partnerships from them.
  • You identify new market segments and develop innovative sales and cooperation models, also outside of the classic insurance distribution
  • You will evaluate and accompany new distribution channels such as embedded insurance, platforms and digital ecosystems
  • You contribute to the further development of digital partner solutions and services.
  • You create business cases, analyses and decision-making bases for evaluating new partnerships
  • You prepare management presentations and decision templates and analyze the performance of partners based on relevant KPIs
  • You identify optimization potential along the partner journey and implement improvements together with internal and external stakeholders

These qualities you bring with you

  • Completed studies or higher education in business administration, marketing, insurance or a comparable specialisation
  • Initial experience in business development, sales, partner management, consulting or the insurance industry
  • Interest in digital business models, innovative sales channels and strategic partnerships
  • Analytical and conceptual thinking and entrepreneurial action
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Strong communication personality with safe appearance and good presentation skills
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • French and/or English are advantageous
