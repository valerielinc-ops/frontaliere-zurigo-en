Full description

Get your EBA through an education in nursing. With us, you start a varied and meaningful apprenticeship with a future, because as an assistant health and social affairs assistant, you can expect a supportive working environment from day one, in which you can help people and are accompanied and supported by our experienced vocational trainers.

For the house Bubenholz in Glattbrugg we are looking for you at 1. August 2027 for the

Apprenticeship as Assistant / Assistant Health and Social EBA

This is what you learn with us

  • Care, care and accompaniment of guests in everyday situations
  • Support for personal care, standing up, dressing, taking off, eating and drinking
  • Daily accompaniment of guests with games, crafts, singing, etc.
  • Domestic, administrative and logistical tasks
  • Compliance and implementation of hygiene and safety rules
  • Electronic maintenance documentation

You bring that with you

  • Completed school education (secondary school B or C)
  • Great willingness to learn and a sense of responsibility
  • Empathy and patience as well as interest in dealing with our guests
  • Helpful, outgoing and team-oriented, with a strong sense of care and conscientiousness
  • Willingness to work irregularly
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude
  • Enjoying versatile challenges and practical work
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

What benefits do we offer you? You are just as important to us as your education! Therefore, we offer you the following advantages:

  • On your birthday you get free
  • CHF 500 to your compulsory school supplies
  • Free SBB half-ax subscription
  • Each lunch for CHF 5
  • Reduced mobile subscriptions at Sunrise
  • Perks and offers around fitness, leisure and your wellbeing
  • Up to 10% discount on your health insurance supplementary insurance
  • Regular team events
  • Mind.2x per month 60 minutes to learn in the learning room
  • And much more...

You start directly with an exciting introductory day and in half of your training we invite you to our summer camp for a whole week. Of course, your qualification must be properly celebrated, for this we organize an unforgettable graduation ceremony for all graduates. And maybe this is just the beginning of your career with us! We offer numerous opportunities to work within our company directly after graduation.

Further advantages and insights can be found on our website.

Then you can dive directly into the Tertianum world on one or more trial days and get a picture of everyday life with us.
