Hirslanden stands for first-class, responsible and individual health care that puts people first. From birth to old age as well as from prevention to healing – at any time, physically and digitally – the Hirslanden Group is concentrating on the further development of the “Continuum of Care” together with private and public partners.

YOUR TASKS

  • Close cooperation with the Head Legal & Company Secretariat as well as taking on own lead topics
  • Advice and support of clinics and group functions in all legal issues, in particular in contract, corporate and health law
  • Preparation, examination and negotiation of contracts with internal and external stakeholders
  • Design and further development of the legal department by working on legal-tech- and AI-based solutions as well as standards and processes
  • Coordination and cooperation with external law firms
  • Support for corporate housekeeping and internal policies
  • Ensuring good corporate governance and compliance
  • Representation of the Hirslanden Group vis-à-vis public authorities and other stakeholders

Your profile

  • Completed university degree in law (lic. iur. / Master of Law), Swiss attorney's patent advantageous
  • several years of professional experience in a multinational company or law firm in the fields of business law, in particular contract law and company law
  • Knowledge of health law or interest in this field
  • Curious, solution-oriented and resilient personality who likes to take responsibility and has excellent communication skills
  • Negotiable in German and English, good knowledge in French is advantageous
  • Structured and precise working methods, rapid comprehension and high social competence in a complex, diverse environment
