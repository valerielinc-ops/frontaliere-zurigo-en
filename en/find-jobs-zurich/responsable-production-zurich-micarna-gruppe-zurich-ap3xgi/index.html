Full description

Become part of a passionate team that produces Swiss culinary culture. With us, you shape processes, support your team, and celebrate shared successes. We give you space for development – both professionally and personally. Appreciation, pride in our products, and growth within the Migros universe await you.

Responsibilities

  • Lead, coach, and develop your team (20-25 employees) to achieve top performance.
  • Manage production and delivery processes, ensuring timely deliveries.
  • Continuously optimize workflows (Lean, Kaizen) to increase efficiency and quality.
  • Ensure compliance with quality, hygiene, and safety standards.
  • Coordinate interfaces with purchasing, engineering, and quality management.

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: in a production environment
  • Completed degree (University/ETH, FH, HF): food industry / FMCG
  • Completed technical or commercial training (e.g. food technology, production technology, HF/FH or equivalent).
  • Several years of professional experience in production or supply chain in the food industry / FMCG, including leadership experience.
  • In-depth knowledge of production control, Lean methods, as well as basic knowledge of QM and GMP/HACCP requirements.
  • Strong leadership skills, communication strength, and team development; pronounced process thinking and solution-oriented action.
  • Willingness to work shifts (3-4 shifts) and corresponding mobility
  • German (very good knowledge)

What we offer

  • Occupational pension: Compared to BVG, Delica pays 7% - 18% more depending on age
  • Meals: Balanced and delicious lunch menu/buffet in the staff restaurant for only CHF 8.-
  • Cumulus points: Double Cumulus points on food and five times on non-food items
  • Sports and fitness offers: Free use of the integrated fitness studios at the locations in Buchs, Birsfelden, and Meilen, plus CHF 200.- reimbursement on annual fitness subscriptions
  • Targeted development: Annual internal talent and development discussions
  • Migros Bank: Migros grants higher interest rates on private and investment accounts for its employees
