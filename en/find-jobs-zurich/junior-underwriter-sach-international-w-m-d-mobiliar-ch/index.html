Full description

This is what you do with us

The focus of your work is on advising and supporting our international corporate customers as well as underwriting in the field of subject matter – you work closely with our senior underwriters.

You support our customers, brokers, key account management and our general agencies in technical issues and help develop individual insurance solutions and international insurance programs. Your area of responsibility also includes the examination, assessment and processing of demanding business cases. You will be in close contact with our cooperation and network partners at home and abroad.

In addition, you are actively involved in the further development and optimization of our underwriting processes and thus shape the cooperation and our processes.

This is what awaits you

In this position, you will become part of our team Underwriting Sach International and deal with the assessment and underwriting of risks for our international corporate clients. Together with your colleagues, you will analyze challenging risks and develop suitable insurance solutions that are tailored to the individual needs of our customers.

This is what you bring with you

  • Higher education (specialised certificate/diploma of insurance or equivalent)
  • Professional experience in the insurance industry with a focus on property insurance and/or underwriting support
  • Business knowledge
  • Know-how and network in the international insurance business as well as pleasure in consulting and sales
  • Customer-oriented, communicative and convincing personality
  • Fluent German and English skills, French is a plus

Working with us

Our experienced underwriting team Sach is waiting for you, in which you benefit from extensive expertise and many years of experience. You can expect a committed, solution-oriented and communicative environment in which cooperation and mutual exchange are given priority.

The position shall be filled on 1 January 2027 or by agreement.

The Insurance Supervision Act requires for the exercise of this function the conclusion of insurance intermediary /-in VBV, profile non-life. If this is not available, we are happy to support with tailor-made internal training.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
