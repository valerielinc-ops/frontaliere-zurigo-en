Full description

Planzer Paket stands for reliable and fast parcel deliveries in Switzerland. As part of our team, you actively contribute to further developing our high quality standards and service. We offer you a varied job in a future-oriented company that focuses on sustainable solutions. Become part of our team and shape the future of the package world!

What you move

  • You take on the responsibility to sort packages safely and efficiently with our sorting system by delivery tours
  • Each package is carefully labeled by you and prepared for the next step
  • When loading the packages into the assigned vehicles, you actively participate
  • Are you ready for more? Exciting additional tasks await you

Why do you succeed?

  • You are fit and like to pack – packages up to 30 kg do not get you out of breath
  • If you already have experience in transport or handling: Jackpot! If not, you are welcome as a newcomer.
  • Your reliable, resilient and flexible nature distinguishes you
  • You can communicate well verbally and in writing in the German language
  • Do you find it easy to work from 01:00-07:00 at night when others are sleeping?

Your Benefits

  • Family culture: You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • Structured familiarization: You will not be thrown into the cold water – we ensure a clear and structured introduction
  • Employee Perks: From discounts to special promotions - benefit all around from various perks
  • Employee Events: Do you love cool events? We can celebrate!

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
