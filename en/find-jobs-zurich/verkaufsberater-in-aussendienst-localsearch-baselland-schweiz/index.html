Full description

Region: Baselland

localsearch is the leading marketing and advertising partner for Swiss SMEs. We make customers successful in the digital world.

To complement our team at renovero, the number 1 tradesman portal in Switzerland, we are looking for you in the field service!

As the engine of our growth strategy, you will bring the digital future to Swiss crafts. We already generate over 65,000 inquiries per year with our craft-focused products, but we want more!

You will convince ambitious companies, for example, how they can win new orders with our AI-supported quotation tool, premium placements in the largest tradesman directory in Switzerland and other fixed-price service offers.

This is what you do specifically Customer advice with impact

  • You identify companies with growth potential and show how renovero packages (presence/starter/professional) increase their visibility fivefold
  • Use case studies to build trust with real successes
  • You analyze regional needs

Skills and requirements

Mindset

  • You are passionate about craftsmanship – whether you are a carpenter, electrician or gardener: you speak their language
  • "Never heard of it before" doesn't exist for you - with your resilience you can crack even skeptical companies

Skills

  • 3+ years of B2B sales experience (SaaS/Crafts is an optional extra
  • In your case, empathy meets negotiating strength
  • Escape from CRM systems? Not with you – you use tools as leverage

Mobility

  • Driving license B with your own vehicle to conquer your sales region

This is what we offer you

  • progressive bonus model
  • Modern technical equipment
  • Contemporary expenses for your own vehicle and flexible working models that fit your life
  • 60% remote – 40% at the customer’s site (no “8-to-5” printing)
  • No micromanagement culture – you determine your sales rhythm
  • As a leading marketing partner for Swiss SMEs, you will help shape the future of our economy

Contact & application

Frédéric Manfrini is looking forward to meeting you! Shape the future of Swiss craftsmanship with us!
