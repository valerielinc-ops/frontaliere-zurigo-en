Full description

You want to make a difference and actively continue writing the success story? In this role, you will shape the strategic development of the nicotine products category, effectively manage portfolio and negotiations and develop a team of four.

Mansioni

  • Responsible for the strategic development of the tobacco and nicotine products category, designing forward-looking concepts and effectively sharpening their positioning.
  • Responsible for the comprehensive analysis of relevant trends, product groups and market and competitive developments.
  • Responsible for and actively controlling the achievement of targets within the budget.
  • Take over the leadership of a team of four, act as a coach and actively promote the further development of employees.
  • Take overall responsibility for negotiations and ensure the sustainable further development of the supplier portfolio.
  • Promote close cooperation with internal interfaces such as marketing, quality management, marketing, sales and logistics.

Requirements

  • min. 5 years of professional experience: proof of performance from a comparable leadership position in category management in the Swiss or European retail trade
  • Bachelor's degree (university, university of applied sciences): in business administration or comparable studies
  • Several years of profound experience in product and category management, in-depth expertise in the product group tobacco and nicotine products as well as experience in the fast-moving consumer goods industry
  • Entrepreneurial and networked overall understanding
  • Strong communication, positive personality with a winning appearance
  • Leadership experience with the ability to lead teams to excellence
  • in German (negotiation confident)
  • English (flowing)
  • French (good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Part of the Migros Group: Strong brand anchored in the retail trade under the Migros Group umbrella
  • Central place of work: In the casual Binz district in Zurich district 3 (2 minutes by foot from Zurich station Binz).
  • Flexible place of work / home office: One to two days a week working at home. Except in IT - we are even more flexible.
  • Mobile-flexible work model: Take your time - with part-time, unpaid leave (2 weeks / year) or a sabbatical.
  • Mobile subscription: Spoiled for choice: With Galaxus Mobile, Wingo or Sunrise already from CHF 10.- one or the other really connected.
  • Migros Bank: Better ride on interest and fees of your private, investment or mortgage accounts.
  • Personal responsibility & freedom: plenty of room for your ideas and to make a difference together with us.
