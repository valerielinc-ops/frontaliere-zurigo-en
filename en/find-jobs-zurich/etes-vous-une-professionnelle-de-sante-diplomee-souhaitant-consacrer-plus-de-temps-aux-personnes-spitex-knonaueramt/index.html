Original description

“You want to be a dipl.” Do nurses just have time for people again? ☀ It's 7:15 a.m. The sun rises just above the roofs as our Anna, Dipl. HF, your car starts. Her first visit takes her to Ms. Keller, 82, who needs support after a fall. Anna rings, is received with a smile – and with one: “I have already looked forward to you.”

“These moments are it,” says Anna. "Here I'm not just 'care', I'm Anna."

Together, we help others to lead a self-determined life in their familiar environment – for this we work in the team at Spitex Knonaueramt and with mutual appreciation.

⌚ Anna takes her time. For wound care, yes – but also for the conversation about the grandchildren, about worries, about small progress. No hectic ringing, no constant change every minute. For this self-responsibility, trust and real relationships.

  • “I feel like I am effective,” says Anna. „At Spitex Knonaueramt I see every day what I do.
  • What Anna appreciates further:
  • 👉 Working independently with support in the team
  • 👉 Flexible operational planning that fits life
  • 👉 Proximity to people – and to their everyday life
  • 👉 Variety instead of station routine

“I go home in the evening and have the feeling: That was a good day.”

  • 🙋 And you?
  • Are you a nurse HF/FH and are not just looking for a job, but sense, closeness
  • And personal responsibility?

Then your path – as with Anna – could lead to Spitex Knonaueramt. ♥ Become part of our team.
