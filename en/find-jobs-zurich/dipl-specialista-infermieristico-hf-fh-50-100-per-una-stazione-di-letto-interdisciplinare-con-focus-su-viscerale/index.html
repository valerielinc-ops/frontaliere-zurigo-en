Full description

Your working environment Our interdisciplinary bed station unites the specialist areas of visceral surgery, throat-nose-ear healing (ENT) and internal medicine. It offers an exciting, varied and demanding working environment with a wide range of patients. Interprofessional exchange with various professional groups is at the heart of high-quality patient care. Their tasks

  • Holistic professional care and care of patients from the mentioned areas of expertise
  • Planning, implementation and evaluation of nursing measures according to the nursing process
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation with doctors, therapists and other specialist areas
  • Accompaniment and guidance of learners and students in the care sector
  • Active participation in the further development of care quality and internal processes
  • Completed training as a Dipl. Care professional HF/FH
  • Professional experience in acute care, ideally with knowledge of visceral, surgery, ENT or internal medicine
  • Responsibility, high social competence and team skills
  • Enjoying a dynamic working environment with changing demands
  • Interest in interdisciplinary cooperation and continuous training
  • A collegial and appreciative working atmosphere in a motivated team
  • Individual working time models (50–100%) for optimal work-life balance
  • Development opportunities within the clinic and the Swiss Medical Network
  • Support for individual training wishes
  • Numerous benefits (see here)
  • Discounts via Swibeco (e.g. at Coop, Zalando, Ikea, etc.)
  • Full coverage of the sickness allowance insurance
  • Various employee actions such as summer party, birthday and Christmas present, etc.
  • Free coffee/tea

Treatment at the highest level, high-class medical care and care, competent care in a more familiar, private atmosphere with hotel comfort, dedication, conscientiousness and absolute discretion – this is what the private clinic Bethanien stands for. Around 340 medical doctors from different disciplines provide high-quality, integrated medical care. Among the most important specialties are orthopaedics, visceral and thoracic surgery, urology, gynecology and birth, gastroenterology, cardiology, pneumology and internal medicine incl. Oncology. In the birth department, around 500 children are born each year. Founded in 1912, the private clinic Bethanien is part of the Swiss Medical Network.
