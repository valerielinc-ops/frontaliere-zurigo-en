Original description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Career Guidance Intern Youth and Family Counseling kjz Winterthur 80% The Office for Youth and Career Counseling of the Education Directorate provides, orders, controls and coordinates child and youth welfare and career counseling services. As one of a total of 15 child and youth welfare centers in the canton of Zurich, the kjz Winterthur ensures uniform, regionally and municipally solid service provision in the areas of youth and family counseling, parenting counseling, mothers and fathers counseling as well as child protection. The kjz Winterthur is the mandate center for the KESB Winterthur in the area of ​​child protection. The kjz Winterthur is offering two students from a Swiss university of applied sciences, majoring in social work, a training position for 6 months for the second internship as of March 1, 2027. As part of the internship you will gain insight into the following activities: • Carrying out civil child protection measures and investigations on behalf of authorities and courts • Clarifying concerns of children, young people, mothers and fathers and introducing supportive measures • Advise and support parents in carrying out their parenting tasks • Writing applications and reports to authorities and courts You have the opportunity to get to know the diverse offerings of the kjz Winterthur and are systematically introduced to our tasks. You can expect a lively, varied job in an institution where work is interdisciplinary and networked with other areas of the institution as a whole. The employment conditions correspond to the cantonal guidelines. Have we piqued your interest? Then we look forward to receiving your online application. Further information can be found at www.ajb.zh.ch Workload: 80%
