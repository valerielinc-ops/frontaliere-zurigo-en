What to expect from us and how to make an "Impact that matters"

  • From day one, you will take on a customer-centric role where you will learn modern auditing techniques and work for various small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs).
  • Specifically, you examine annual accounts and get a 360-degree insight into the financial situation of our clients with your colleagues.
  • Together with your experienced colleagues who will assist you with expert advice, you will expand your skills with each mandate, develop quickly and grow beyond yourself.
  • You have an eye for detail, like to find solutions and enrich our team with your communicative and open-minded nature
  • You have successfully completed a commercial education or are close to completing your studies, ideally in the field of accounting, auditing or controlling
  • A positive attitude, curiosity, willingness to learn
  • Independent work and direct contact with customers
  • You enjoy the close cooperation and communication with the team and the various stakeholders
  • You master German and English fluently Your team Become part of our motivated, international team in our office in Zurich.
