Full description

With the introduction of the day school in the coming school year, our range of care and the number of children to be cared for is growing - that is why we are expanding our team and creating additional jobs. For our care we are looking for immediately or by agreement a committed and thinking Hortleitung or experienced specialist care at the primary level. They work four days a week (Mo, Di, Do and Fr) from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. and accompany the students during their free time.

Tasks

  • Planning and implementation of attractive leisure and care offers during lunchtime
  • They promote and support children of primary school age in their development process and social skills
  • Administrative and domestic tasks

Profile

  • degree as a social pedagogue* in FH or HFS; Social worker FH or HFS (for applications with a foreign diploma / professional card, a Swiss diploma recognition must be attached)
  • As a specialist you have a professional qualification as a specialist
  • Motivated, open, resilient personality with a positive human image
  • Flexibility as well as good team and communication skills

We offer

  • A varied and meaningful activity in a lively environment
  • Competent support from the management
  • Progressive salary and social benefits according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich

About us

The Zurich-Letzi school district comprises Stadtkreis 9 with the districts Albisrieden, Altstetten and Grünau. In the 16 schools of our rapidly growing school circle, more than 5,000 primary school students are taught and supervised. More than 1,200 employees work for them in our schools in the areas of teaching, support, home service and technology as well as administration. They all work daily for the interests of the students and a smooth school day.
