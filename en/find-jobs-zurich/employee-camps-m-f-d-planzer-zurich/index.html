Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, participate and grow with us. Become part of our family and shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us. Tasks: • When loading and unloading trucks, you are the absolute professional and control the goods and pallets • You ensure that the goods land safely and quickly in the right storage space. Chaos? Not with you! • You record all material movements in the system and take care of the processing of delivery notes and loading papers • You always keep the camp in check and are open for additional tasks to ensure a smooth process. Profile: • You are ready to work shifts – whether in the afternoon (14:00 to 23:30) or in the night shift (23:30 to 08:00) • First experiences in the camp and in dealing with the palettrolli you already bring with you • You are the proud owner of a forklift card • You are fit and like to get involved – even when storing goods, you always remain resilient • Oral and written you can safely express yourself in German • You work reliably, accurately and quickly adapt to new challenges • Even if it gets stressful, you stay cool and keep track. Benefits: • With us you start with 5 weeks vacation and benefit from the 5th year of service even from 6 weeks! • With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents • You will spend your first working days in the central introduction, where you will be taught the most important basics • Top transport connections - faster to work, more free time For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
