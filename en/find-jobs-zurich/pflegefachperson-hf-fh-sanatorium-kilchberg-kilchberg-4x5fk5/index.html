Acute station focus on psychosis spectrum

Additional benefit for carers in shift work: 37-hour week The Kilchberg Sanatorium offers you a fulfilling working environment with exciting development opportunities in a dynamic and appreciative team. Bring your strengths and shape the future of the clinic. On station D3 you will find yourself in an exciting environment that lives the interdisciplinary cooperation.

Their tasks

  • Work on an open acute psychiatric station with a focus on psychosis spectrum
  • Individual co-creation of the acute psychiatric setting
  • Support in the implementation of the care process in the multidisciplinary team
  • Active participation in the implementation of new stationary projects
  • Active accompaniment in everyday life and relationship design according to Safewards concept
  • Assumption of reference work or work in the core treatment team

Your profile

  • Completed training as nurse/nurse professional HF or recognition in the case of foreign training
  • High flexibility and self-management skills
  • Open, humorous and warm appearance
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Recovery-oriented attitude
  • In the best case already a further education in aggression management or willingness to take this course

The Kilchberg Sanatorium is one of the most traditional psychiatric private clinics in Switzerland and looks back on almost 160 years of history. Only 15 minutes from the city centre of Zurich with beautiful views of Lake Zurich and the Alps. As a Listespital, we offer comprehensive psychiatric care for all insured persons and have inpatient and outpatient facilities in Kilchberg and Zurich. Our approximately 650 employees from over 25 professional groups are at the heart of the clinic and are committed to the best possible treatment of our patients. Become part of our team and shape the future of psychiatric care.
