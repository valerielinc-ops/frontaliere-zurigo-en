Full description

The FIF supports and advises specialists who accompany people in complex social situations. It promotes interdisciplinary cooperation, networks participating specialist bodies and institutions and supports the clarification of complex case constellations and interfaces. The aim is to develop viable solutions together with the experts involved and to promote coordinated, needs-based support.

For the Department of Interdisciplinary Case Work (FIF), we are looking for 1. December 2026 or by appointment a new team member.

Tasks

  • They advise and support specialists in complex social and psychosocial case constellations, carry out in-depth case analyses and professional research if necessary and jointly develop professional assessments, options for action and solutions.
  • They maintain and develop the existing interdisciplinary network with internal and external specialist departments, authorities and other relevant organizations, open up new network partners if necessary and promote interdisciplinary cooperation.
  • They promote and coordinate cooperation between participating specialists and institutions, organise and moderate case conferences as well as other interdisciplinary exchange formats and support clear clarification of roles and tasks at interfaces.
  • They accompany complex cases within the framework of an agreed monitoring, identify changes in the need for support and advise on further measures if necessary.
  • In justified exceptional cases and in coordination with the case-leading specialists, they take over direct client contact for the professional assessment, orientation or support of the consulting process.
  • They actively participate in the professional development of the specialist centre as well as its offers, processes and working instruments.

Profile

  • You have a recognised degree as a social worker FH, as a qualified nurse HF/FH with in-depth psychiatric expertise or a comparable qualification at tertiary level in the social or health sector with corresponding expertise.
  • They have professional experience in the social, health or psychiatric field and have experience in interdisciplinary cooperation.
  • Experience in networking and cooperation with different specialist bodies and institutions is an advantage.
  • expertise or practical experience in topics such as addiction, delinquency, migration or pregnancy and parenting are beneficial
  • Further specializations are welcome.
  • You have very good oral and written knowledge of German
  • Further language skills are advantageous.
  • They work independently and responsibly, keep an overview even in complex and demanding situations and enjoy interdisciplinary cooperation and a strong communication ability.

We offer

She expects a technically challenging and varied job in an interdisciplinary team with a socially relevant field of responsibility in the city of Zurich. They work during office hours (Mo-Fr). You also have the opportunity to work partially in the home office. In addition, you will receive creative leeway and the opportunity to actively participate in the further development and reorganization of the offer.

About us

With around 2,400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a social Zurich. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.

The Department of Social Protection and Prevention promotes the social integration and health of socially marginalized people with preventive, social and medical measures. The offer of the Meeting Points and Counselling Department is aimed at socially disintegrated adults living in the city of Zurich, who are often confronted with a mental illness and/or addictive addiction. The department is thus aimed at people, on the margins of society, who are also difficult to reach within the social assistance system. This marginalised and often underserved group of people is excluded and therefore faces considerable integration problems.
