Role overview

To strengthen our rapidly growing Data Center team, we are looking for you as a committed personality in the field of Health & Safety Supervision (m/f/d).

With your passion for occupational safety and health protection, you actively contribute to building, developing and sustainably anchoring a world-class safety culture on complex and challenging mission-critical projects.

In this versatile and responsible function, you support and monitor all health, safety and environment (HSE) activities on the construction site.
