Full description

To strengthen our rapidly growing Data Center team, we are looking for you as a committed personality in the field of Health & Safety Supervision (m/f/d). With your passion for occupational safety and health protection, you actively contribute to building, developing and sustainably anchoring a world-class safety culture on complex and demanding mission-critical projects.

In this versatile and responsible function, you support and monitor all health, safety and environment (HSE) activities on the construction site. In doing so, you ensure compliance with Swiss legal requirements, customer requirements and Implenia’s high HSE standards.

You work closely with the project management, our subcontractors and representatives of the building owner and ensure that safety is a top priority in every project phase. Your responsibilities include conducting security inspections, managing and supporting subcontractors, preparing and reviewing risk assessments, organizing and conducting training, investigating incidents, and participating in emergency and crisis preparedness. You make a decisive contribution to the successful and secure running of our international data center projects.

Your contractual place of work is our headquarters “Connect” in Opfikon. However, you will mainly work on one of our data center construction projects in the Zurich region.

Tasks

What tasks await you as Health & Safety Supervisor (m/f/d)?

  • You promote and live a proactive security culture on our data center construction sites and ensure compliance with Implenia HSE standards, customer requirements and legal requirements
  • You are regularly present on site, support our subcontractors in all security issues and ensure that defined HSE standards are consistently implemented.
  • You will conduct safety inspections, audits, inspections and hazard assessments, identify risks early and initiate appropriate corrective and preventive measures
  • You coordinate and monitor the security performance of our subcontractors, check work permits, RAMS/WMS, security documentation and toolbox talks and accompany the corresponding processes
  • You will investigate incidents and near-misses, carry out root cause analyses, lead daily HSE briefings and training, advise project stakeholders and support the continuous development of our safety standards and processes.

Requirements

What requirements do you bring as Health & Safety Supervisor (m/f/d)?

  • Completed education or training in occupational safety, health and safety, construction site safety or equivalent recognised qualification
  • Several years of professional experience in a comparable HSE function on complex large-scale projects, ideally in data center, industrial, infrastructure or pharmaceutical construction
  • In-depth knowledge in the areas of risk management, construction site safety, subcontractor management as well as work release and approval processes on demanding construction sites
  • High social and communication skills combined with a sovereign appearance and the ability to sustainably promote security awareness at all hierarchical levels
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Very good knowledge of English and ideally knowledge of German
  • Recognized HSE certifications such as NEBOSH, SUVA or ISO 45001
