Operational service centre

Job opening by agreement

The Technical Service Department in the Operations Service Center of the University of Zurich is responsible for the maintenance, disruption intervention and modification of the HLKS/E facilities of the University Properties Center, Botanical Garden, Schlieren, Buchs and Oerlikon. We are looking for an experienced and established personality to support our team.

Tasks

  • Operation and maintenance of complex building facilities
  • Interference, maintenance and modification of electrical, heating, ventilation, refrigeration and air conditioning systems and technical equipment for research
  • Control, regulation and monitoring of installations via building control systems
  • Independent implementation or coordination of installations, repairs and optimizations
  • Support and support for technical issues of users
  • Monitoring, maintenance and documentation of facilities and participation in integral tests and mission-critical safety tests
  • Assumption of on-call service after careful training (24/7, about 5-6 times per year)

Profile

  • Completed training as an electrical installer:in EFZ
  • Experience in the operation of energy supply systems and in the creation and implementation of switching programs
  • Knowledge of building and process automation
  • Team-oriented, service-conscious and professional appearance
  • Independent, reliable and conceptual working method
  • Good IT user knowledge (MS Office 365, control systems)
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German, English is advantageous
  • Ideally in possession of a ticket Cat. B
