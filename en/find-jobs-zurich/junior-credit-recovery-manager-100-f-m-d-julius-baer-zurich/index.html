Full description

At Julius Baer, we celebrate and value the individual qualities you bring, enabling you to be impactful, to be entrepreneurial, to be empowered, and to create value beyond wealth. Let’s shape the future of wealth management together.

Team member Impaired Asset Management, responsible for specific Credit Risk aspects of a portfolio of domestic and international private banking clients in financial distress. Assess, analyse and monitor transactions for selected mortgage and/or Lombard clients. Support in client discussions and negotiations.

YOUR CHALLENGE

  • Support the credit risk management of selected defaulted loans with the aim of loss minimisation
  • Assist in conducting credit and collateral analysis; help develop risk-mitigating strategies and perform collateral assessments and provisioning calculations under guidance
  • Shadow and participate in client meetings related to credit recovery loans, observing how the Bank's interests are represented and safeguarded
  • Support senior credit recovery officers and deputise for them as required
  • Collaborate with internal stakeholders to ensure alignment and efficient credit file handling
  • Assist with ad-hoc and recurring reporting, project-related activities, and duties
  • Contribute to credit-related projects and process improvements

YOUR PROFILE

  • Bachelor's degree or completed studies at a university of applied sciences (economics, banking, finance, law), or equivalent education
  • Initial professional experience or internships in recovery management, workout of credit files, or the mortgage or lombard sector (ideally 2-3 years)
  • Basic understanding of legal aspects regarding mortgage and/or lombard business and borrowing structures, with a willingness to deepen this knowledge
  • Effective and efficient working style, including the ability to work under pressure and strong analytical skills
  • Great team player with the ability to work across teams and stakeholders
  • Good IT skills (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
  • Excellent German and English language skills (written and spoken); additional languages (e.g. Italian or French) are an advantage
