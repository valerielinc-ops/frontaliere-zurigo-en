Full description

Canton of Zurich Financial Directorate Personnel Office Apprenticeship as ICT specialist / ICT specialist EFZ 2027 100% Do you want to become part of the economic engine Switzerland and shape your professional future at the Canton of Zurich? Then you are exactly right with us. Around 300 apprentices complete their training in 25 different professions. Around 70 young people in the ICT sector are currently learning from this. Training places for ICT specialists are available, for example, at vocational and cantonal schools, at the Office of Computer Science and at courts. An ICT infrastructure that functions around the clock can no longer be imagined in today's working world. The internal IT departments with their employees and trainees are responsible for the smooth operation. In the three-year apprenticeship to the ICT specialist, you work as a contact person between installation and support. You install hardware and software, expand device configurations and ensure their operation and maintenance. You support users with support requests, instruct them in dealing with ICT tools and create user instructions. In inter-company courses, projects and events, you can expand and deepen your specialist, methodological, social and self-competences. The

  • Requirements For teaching as an ICT specialist we are looking for pupils of the middle level secondary school with good grades in German and English. In addition, you should bring interest and pleasure in dealing with people as well as skill in handling technical devices. Your social skills and an open personality are also very important to us, as you will work a lot in the team and have customer contact every day. If you are interested in a varied and future-oriented education, we look forward to your online application. We are happy to get to know you personally and shape a successful future together with you. Please submit your application with all necessary documents. A complete application includes the following documents:
  • CV
