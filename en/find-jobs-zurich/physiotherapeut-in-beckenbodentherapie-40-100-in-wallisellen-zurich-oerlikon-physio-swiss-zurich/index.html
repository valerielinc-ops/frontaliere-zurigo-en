Our team consists of 9 dedicated physiotherapists who work daily with passion and the latest therapy methods. Due to the increasing demand, we are looking for a physiotherapist with interest or specialization in pelvic floor therapy. With us, you will treat women and men with pelvic floor complaints and benefit from a modern infrastructure including biofeedback technology as well as an interdisciplinary and family working environment. Your tasks

  • Independent care and treatment of patients in the field of pelvic floor therapy
  • Treatment of complaints such as:
  • Urinary and fecal incontinence
  • Complaints during and after pregnancy and childbirth
  • Prostate complaints and rehabilitation after prostate operations
  • Chronic pain in the pelvic and lumbar spine area
  • Implementation of biofeedback-assisted therapy
  • Creation of individual therapy and training programs
  • Active co-creation and further development of our pool floor offering
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with doctors, midwives and other specialists

You bring that with you

  • Completed training as a physiotherapist (BSc/FH or equivalent)
  • SRK recognition or positive pre-check
  • Experience or further education in the field of pelvic floor therapy is advantageous
  • Interest in evidence-based physiotherapy and modern rehabilitation
  • Enjoy independent work and personal patient care
  • Empathetic, open and professional personality

This is what awaits you with us

  • Above average attractive remuneration
  • Flexible working hours and workload of 40-100%
  • Modern practice rooms in the immediate vicinity of the station
  • Own construction and further development of the pelvic floor therapy department
  • Possibility for subsequent professional or managerial responsibility
  • Support for training and specialisations
  • State-of-the-art infrastructure including biofeedback systems, BTL and Milon equipment
  • Family, appreciative team with short decision paths
  • High personal responsibility and a lot of room for manoeuvre
