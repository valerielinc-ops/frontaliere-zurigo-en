Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Technical Vocational School Zurich TBZ Administrative Secretary/in 80% The Technical Vocational School Zurich TBZ is a competence center for basic and further education in the fields of automotive technology, information technology, electrical/electronics, event technology and eye optics. Over 3,600 learners are taught by around 200 teachers. In order to strengthen our administrative team in the electrical/electronics department, we are looking for a motivated and committed personality immediately or by agreement. Their tasks

  • Independent management of the Secretariat in basic education in a team of two
  • Contact person for learners, apprentices, teachers, secondary and vocational training offices and coordination between different stakeholders (turntable function)
  • Complete all administrative work in the secretariat, correspondence by mail, telephone and at the counter
  • Support to department management
  • IT affinity desired (power user function, implementation of new school software programs, internal support)
  • Regular presence on site during school operations (no home office); Preferred working days are Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday Your profile
  • Completed basic commercial training EFZ
  • Several years of professional experience beneficial, ideally in a school secretariat
  • Very good knowledge of Microsoft 365
  • Openness to new school software
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Organizational talent and high service orientation
  • Teamwork, flexibility and resilience
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Joy of working in a dynamic school environment and contact with different stakeholders Our offer
  • Meaningful activity in an important educational environment with versatile and interesting tasks
  • Stable public sector workplace with a long-term perspective
  • Attractive employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines
  • Appreciative work culture with flat hierarchy and open communication
  • Targeted training opportunities and support for professional development
  • Central location in Zurich just a few minutes walk from Zurich Train Station Application Have we aroused your interest? We look forward to your application. Please submit your documents exclusively via the online tool. For information stands Mr. Dominik Steinböck, Co-Department Head Department Electrical/Electronics, tel. +41 44 446 95 95, email dominik.steinboeck@tbz.zh.ch, please contact us. Information about our school can be found at www.tbz.ch.
