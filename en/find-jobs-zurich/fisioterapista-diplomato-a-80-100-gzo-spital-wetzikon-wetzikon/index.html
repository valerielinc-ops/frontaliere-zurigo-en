Full description

(as of 01.02.2027 or by appointment) Small team, broad expertise and a lot of heart for therapy. We are an open-minded, interprofessional team of physiotherapy, occupational therapy and speech therapy. Together we are committed to a high quality of therapy, support each other and do not lose our humor in a demanding everyday life.

Tasks

  • You treat inpatient and outpatient patients from various fields, in particular internal medicine and neurology as well as geriatrics, orthopaedics and surgery.
  • You plan and design the physiotherapeutic treatments independently and orient yourself to the individual needs and therapeutic goals of the patients.
  • Depending on the interest and field of application, it is possible to participate in interdisciplinary outpatient rehabilitation programs.
  • You regularly provide weekend services and also care for patients in the intensive care unit.
  • You maintain a close and constructive cooperation with the medical profession, nursing and other medical specialist areas. Requirements
  • You are an established personality and have a completed education as a Dipl. Physical therapist with SRK recognition
  • Professional experience in the acute hospital is an advantage
  • You are team-oriented, motivated and enjoy interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Ideally, you have experience or training in neurology or lymphology or are motivated to develop in these areas, and you can look forward to an introduction worthy of the name. You will be structured and carefully introduced to your new area of responsibility. Development that continues You benefit from internal and external training opportunities. A hospital where you know each other Short distances, direct contacts and cooperation in a multi-professional team that is characterized by respect and appreciation. Various discounts and internal offers are just as much part of our benefits as an excellent staff restaurant, a connected KiTa and various events for our employees. Further benefits can be found here. The GZO Spital Wetzikon is an innovative acute care hospital in the Zurich Oberland with 150 beds and around 900 employees. As a company with high customer orientation and appreciative communication, we are committed to our patients on a daily basis.
