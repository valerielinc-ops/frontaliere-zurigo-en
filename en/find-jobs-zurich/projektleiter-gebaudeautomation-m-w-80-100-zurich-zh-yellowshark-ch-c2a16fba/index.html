01 Future employer

For our client in the Zurich area, an innovative company that offers solutions for the efficient, sustainable and comfortable control of building systems, we are looking for an experienced project manager in building automation. Are you an experienced, solution-oriented professional who independently manages projects in the field of building automation and leads them to success together with internal and external partners? Then do not hesitate to seize the opportunity to embark on a new career path.

02 Your task

  • Managing and coordinating projects in the field of building automation, from the bidding phase through commissioning and handover
  • Coordinating all trades involved and external partners, including technical coordination and scheduling
  • Developing, optimizing and implementing technical automation concepts while taking energy efficiency and sustainability into account
  • Monitoring project progress, costs, schedules and quality, as well as identifying deviations in good time
  • Supporting and carrying out commissioning, tests and acceptances, including operator training and handover of the documentation

04 Your profile

  • Completed training in electrical engineering, automation engineering, building services engineering or a comparable qualification
  • Experience in project management, ideally in the field of building automation, measurement and control technology or system integration
  • In-depth knowledge of automation systems, bus systems (e.g. BACnet, KNX etc.) and relevant norms & standards
  • Independent, solution-oriented and precise approach to work
  • Teamwork and good communication skills
  • Fluent German, additional languages are an advantage
