Full description

Do you want to actively shape the integration platform of a leading Swiss health insurer? Our Integration Platforms & Solutions team ensures reliable and secure data exchange within SWICA and with external partners. We are looking for an experienced personality with a passion for integration, platform engineering and modern software architectures. In this role, you take over the technical responsibility for our MuleSoft Anypoint platform and actively shape its further development – from stable operating processes to future integration patterns in the AI age. You will work closely with architecture, development teams, security, infrastructure and specialist areas, and bring your technical expertise to strategic integration projects. This is how you shape health

  • Technical ownership of the MuleSoft Anypoint platform including lifecycle management, security and continuous development
  • Further development of our integration architecture towards modern API and AI-based integration solutions
  • Advising and supporting project teams and stakeholders on integration and architecture issues
  • Analysis, concretization and implementation of requirements together with specialist and IT areas
  • Participation in integration projects from conception to production
  • Development of APIs, integration solutions and custom connectors with Java
  • Ensuring stability, performance, observability and operation of the platform
  • Support in 3rd-level support as well as technical error analysis for complex incidents
  • Co-creation of engineering standards, best practices and automation

This sets you apart

  • Higher education in computer science (HF, FH, university or comparable)
  • Several years of experience as a software engineer or integration engineer in complex enterprise environments
  • Experience with API and integration platforms such as MuleSoft Anypoint, Apigee, WSO2, Boomi or similar solutions
  • Very good knowledge of API Design (REST, SOAP, Async/Event-driven), Microservices and Integration Architectures
  • Good programming skills in Java or a comparable object-oriented language
  • Experience in the operation or technical responsibility of platforms or central services
  • Solid understanding of CI/CD, security, observability and modern operating models
  • Good SQL knowledge and experience with relational databases
  • Analytical thinking, high level of initiative and enjoyment of cross-sectoral cooperation
