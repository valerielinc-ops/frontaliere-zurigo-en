Career / Vacancies Professional Health for the Oasis Pensum: 80-100% Admission: Apply by appointment PDF listing People with dementia need people who are happy to be there for them. Sonnweid is considered one of the leading institutions for people with dementia. In our competence center, our 330 employees from various departments look after and care for 174 residents. Family doctors and old-age psychiatrists ensure medical care. The differentiated housing and care concept is adapted to the stage of the disease. Our semi-cut ward offers a temporary home for people with particularly challenging behavior, while the day/night ward relieves carers with day and holiday stays. Our mission statement, which focuses on the needs of people suffering from dementia, forms the basis for our daily actions. With joy and conviction, we do everything we can to make the inhabitants feel at home and at home in Sonnweid. In doing so, we respect each other in our individuality and support each other in challenging situations. more ... Our oasis stations provide space for people with severe dementia and very high need for care. The concept is based on the observation that many people in an advanced disease stage seek more contact with other people.
