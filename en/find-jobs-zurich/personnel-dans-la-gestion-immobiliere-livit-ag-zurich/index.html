Full description

You are open-minded, ambitious and value-added in a future-oriented way of working on the pulse of the real estate industry? At Livit, you are committed to the concerns of owners with an exemplary customer orientation: inside and inside tenants, thus contributing to a trusting, long-lasting customer relationship and a positive company's image. Are you ready to tackle the challenges of today and tomorrow and to make a difference in the real estate world? Then you are exactly right at Livit. We look forward to your personal contribution.

Tasks

  • You support professionals in day-to-day business such as rent changes, housing receipts and ancillary costs statements, as well as in the preparation of final invoices and in the payment system
  • You advise tenants, homekeepers, craftsmen and authorities by telephone and provide information
  • You are working on dismissals
  • You prepare and deliver repair orders
  • You control and control the lenders
  • You are responsible for the general data maintenance and data management of the property.
  • You perform administrative tasks and work on tenant correspondence

  • You have sound commercial experience, benefit from our industry and are ready to learn new things

  • You have high social and self-competence with the ability of self-management
  • You live the values of customer orientation, communicate professionally and enjoy dealing with people
  • You bring a A-resinity to technology and can use new technologies
  • You have self-learning skills and enjoy sharing your knowledge

We offer

  • Individual career planning and development perspectives
