Portrait Spitex Knonaueramt employs around 160 people who are committed to the needs of its approximately 500 customers on a competent, open, personal and flexible basis every day. The catchment area comprises around 54,000 inhabitants. We offer individual care, care, relief and counselling at home for sick and recovering people of all ages: acute, chronic and mentally ill, people with disabilities, age-related limitations, convalescence, overwork, accident and maternity. We also care for people with serious and/or incurable diseases, accompany and relieve the relatives. Spitex Knonaueramt is a private-law association that provides help and care at home on behalf of 13 municipalities in the district of Affoltern.
