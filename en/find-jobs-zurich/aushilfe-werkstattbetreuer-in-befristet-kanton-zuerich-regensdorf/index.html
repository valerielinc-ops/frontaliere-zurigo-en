Full description

Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Prison and reintegration Prison Pöschwies assistant workshop supervisor, temporary 80 - 100% With around 1,400 employees, JuWe is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as for carrying out various types of detention. Our employees from around 40 professions support and promote the reintegration of delinquents and work effectively and progressively for a safe and just society. The Pöschwies Prison (JVA) is one of eight main departments of JuWe. With around 400 detention places, it is the largest closed prison in Switzerland for men who have committed crimes. It fulfils a central state task and contributes to the protection of the general public in accordance with the legal mandate and at the same time creates the conditions for the rehabilitation of the admitted persons. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for you as a resilient personality with strong social skills. Together with your experienced vocational colleagues, you will support and support trained persons in the employment and workplaces in the law enforcement industry. The term of office shall be one year. Your tasks

  • Ensuring order and safety in the workshop and accompaniment of a regulated work facility
  • Guidance and support to instructors in their daily work
  • Responsibility for the care and promotion of a group of instructed persons in the security-oriented enforcement
  • Plan and coordinate employment assignments, taking into account individual skills and resources
  • In regular exchange with the residential group and maintain a close interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Observe developments and behaviors and record them in reports and assessments Your profile
  • Basic craft training (EFZ) or equivalent
  • Professional experience in the artisanal environment and interest in working with people are beneficial
  • Strong social skills and joy in accompanying and promoting people in special life situations
  • Team-oriented, flexible and resilient personality with a positive attitude
  • Good knowledge of German in word and writing as well as secure MS-Office skills
  • Further language skills are advantageous
  • Minimum age 25 years and a good reputation (no entries in the penal and enforcement register) Our offer

  • Food facilities directly on site in the JVA Pöschwies restaurant
  • Regulated working hours in uniformed service
