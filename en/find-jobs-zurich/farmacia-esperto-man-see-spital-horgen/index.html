Full description

Your responsibilities

  • Preparation and direction of medicines: Careful and patient-specific preparation of medicines in compliance with hygienic and quality-relevant standards. This activity must be carried out in the afternoon, but the working days can be arranged flexibly.
  • Workplace organization and documentation: control of medical prescriptions in the clinic information system (CIS), precise regulation of medications and complete documentation of all steps.
  • Communication and cooperation: Close coordination with the pharmacy, the medical service and the nursing staff to clarify any discrepancies and to ensure optimal medical care.
  • Storage and handover: Professional storage of the medication as well as on-time handover to the care, including passing on relevant information about the medication.
  • Quality assurance: conducting regular self-checks, complying with current guidelines and actively participating in internal training for continuous quality improvement.
  • Organizational support: Participation in the optimization of medication stocks on the wards in cooperation with the ward secretary.

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a specialist pharmacy EFZ.
  • Ideally, you will have experience in dealing with medicines as well as knowledge of drug logistics and distribution.
  • A responsible and careful handling of medicines is a matter of course for you.
  • They work in a structured, exact manner and always keep an overview even in a dynamic environment.

What we offer you

  • Working independently in a motivated and collegial team that supports each other.
  • A place of work with the best connection to public transport, as well as cost-effective staff parking spaces.
  • Modern infrastructure and a working environment in which you can feel at home – lake views included.
  • Diverse benefits – from great employee discounts to further education offers.
  • A pleasant working atmosphere in an attractive location near Zurich, which has a lot to offer you both professionally and privately. Physical applications cannot be considered or returned.
