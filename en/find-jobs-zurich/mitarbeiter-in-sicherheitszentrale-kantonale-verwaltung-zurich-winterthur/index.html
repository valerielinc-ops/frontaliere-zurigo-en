Full description

As part of the Zurich Prisons of Detention (UGZ), Winterthur Prison (GFW) assumes central tasks in the area of detention without judgment. The focus is on pre-trial detention and security detention as well as the reception of persons in provisional detention. A total of 101 detention places are available for this purpose. For the security center of the GFW, we are looking for a physically resilient and committed personality immediately or by agreement. The post shall be limited to one year. You do that with us.

  • Monitoring and operation of technical security installations (video surveillance, alarm and access systems)
  • Receiving and handling alarm and malfunction messages and taking the necessary measures
  • Support and coordination of external forces in the event of security incidents
  • Ensuring compliance with security, alert and emergency processes
  • Complete documentation of events, reports and specific occurrences
  • Completion of administrative work in connection with the operation of the security center
  • Completed vocational training, preferably in the security field, technical environment or in a comparable function
  • High attention and a strong sense of responsibility and security
  • Psychological resilience and the ability to act calmly and deliberately even in demanding situations
  • Good IT user knowledge and technical understanding.
  • Style-confident knowledge of German in words and writing, further language skills of advantage
  • Readiness for shift, night and weekend work
  • Team-capable, reliable and discreet personality
  • Judicial experience is beneficial
  • Reliable gum

Zurich Detention Centers (UGZ) are one of eight main departments of JuWe. They comprise seven companies with about 735 prison places and about 500 employees. They are responsible for provisional arrest, pre-trial detention and security detention, as well as the early execution of penalties and measures until transfer to a prison facility. From the outset, they thus fulfil a central function within the Zurich justice system. As employees of the Zurich Detention Centers, we provide structured and human support for detained persons with expertise and commitment and help ensure that criminal proceedings are carried out in an orderly manner before a decision is made on guilt or innocence. With around 1400 employees, JuWe is the largest office within the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs and is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as for the execution of various types of detention. Our employees from around 40 professions support and promote the reintegration of delinquents and work effectively and progressively for a safe and just society. We offer...
