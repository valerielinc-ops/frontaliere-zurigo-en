Do you want to help shape the future of life reinsurance in Germany? Become part of Swiss Re as a (senior) actuary & client manager for L&H in Germany – and join a dedicated, collaborative team that drives innovation and sustainable growth in this unique market environment. About the role In your role as (senior) actuary & client manager/in L&H for Germany, you act as the central interface between Swiss Re and its reinsurance customers. Her strong actuarial expertise forms the basis for a strategically important role in the expansion of Swiss Re’s market presence and in the development of data-based, customer-oriented solutions. The location is Munich and you report to the Head Client Management Germany & Nordics. Main responsibilities

  • They are the central point of contact for Swiss Re’s L&H customers in Germany and ensure seamless communication and high-quality service provision.
  • You build long-term, trusting customer relationships and maintain them by positioning Swiss Re as the technically leading and preferred reinsurance partner in the German market.
  • You will develop and implement growth strategies for existing and potential customers based on actuarial expertise in pricing, product design and portfolio management, driving sustainable business growth and innovation.
  • They are responsible for and manage customer relationships holistically, including the financial performance of portfolios and the evaluation and structuring of new business opportunities from an actuarial and commercial perspective.
  • They establish and maintain a strong professional network in the German insurance market in order to identify and implement opportunities along the strategic pillars of Swiss Re – Core, Solutions and Transactions – especially in complex, actuarial issues.
  • They represent Swiss Re at customer meetings, industry events, conferences and relevant specialist committees and act as credible brand ambassadors with strong technical competence.

About the team You will become part of a dynamic and highly motivated team of professionals who will make every effort to achieve outstanding results and help shape the future of Swiss Re’s business. As an L&H hub in Munich, we are part of a networked continental European L&H team and a global L&H community. The team is extremely diverse and includes various functions relevant to serving our customers, including client management, actuary, underwriting, performance regulation and medicine. The working environment is fast-paced, highly collaborative and intellectually demanding, characterized by analytical excellence, professional exchange and a good dose of pleasure in working together.

About you

You are a dynamic and strategically minded personality with strong actuarial expertise and deep experience in the L&H insurance or reinsurance industry. Her focus is on pricing, portfolio management and the economic evaluation of risks. In direct customer interaction, you feel comfortable building long-term partnerships and leveraging your analytical skills to drive profitable growth and deliver sustainable, measurable results in a fast-paced, collaborative environment.

We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements

  • At least 5 years of experience in the German life insurance and/or reinsurance market
  • Strong actuary background with relevant qualifications (e.g. DAV, actuary DAV or equivalent)
  • Proven ability to translate complex actuarial concepts into business solutions – Experience in pricing and product development for biometric solutions is a plus
  • Sound understanding of HGB accounting and other international regulations (Solvency II, IFRS)
  • Strong analytical skills to understand market patterns and identify emerging trends
  • Experience in client-oriented roles within the insurance or reinsurance sector
  • Fluent and secure communication in German and English.
