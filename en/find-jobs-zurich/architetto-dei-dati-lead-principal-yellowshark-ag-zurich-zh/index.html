Future employer Do you want not only to manage data, but also to shape the data architecture of the central transaction and order management platform of a market leader? For one of the most successful and innovative companies in the German-speaking region, we are looking for a visionary professional with strong execution skills as a Data Architect. In this key role, you will combine state-of-the-art software architecture with strategic business impact. You will build scalable, highly available data landscapes for critical core areas such as Financial Operations, Payment, and Fraud Prevention, with a direct impact on millions of customer interactions.

Your role

  • Architecture & Vision: You design and take responsibility for the end-to-end data architecture in the central order management platform and ensure its performance, scalability, and resilience.
  • Strategic sounding board: You work closely with Product Owners, Software Engineers, Data Engineers, and business specialists from the finance and fraud prevention teams to translate complex business requirements into elegant data models.
  • Hands-on & Mentoring: You support interdisciplinary teams in making architecture decisions, establish best practices for Data Governance and Data Modeling, and translate corporate goals into clear technical roadmaps.
  • Innovation & Modernization: You continuously evaluate new technologies, modernize existing data flows (Streaming & Batch), and drive an uncompromising data-driven culture forward.

Your profile

  • Several years of architecture expertise: Extensive experience as a Data Architect, Enterprise Architect or Lead Data Engineer in a big data or high-scale e-commerce environment.
  • Excellent technical domain knowledge: Deep understanding of modern data modeling (relational, NoSQL, event-driven architectures), cloud data platforms and enterprise integrations (e.g. event streaming via Kafka, DWH/Data Lakehouse).
  • Business understanding: Strong affinity for transactional data, financial data flows and risk management systems.
  • Communication & assertiveness: Ability to communicate complex technical concepts clearly to both development teams and management-level stakeholders.
  • Languages: Fluent German and very good English skills for working in an international engineering environment.

Things to know

  • Maximum scalability: Experience the immediate impact of your work on millions of transactions and real-time traffic.
  • Real tech culture: No bureaucracy, but rather flat hierarchies, a high degree of personal responsibility and an uncompromisingly modern engineering mindset.
  • Maximum flexibility: Contemporary working model with a significant work-from-home component, flexible working hours and employment levels starting at 80 %.
  • Top benefits & top pension provision: Above-average overall package with excellent social benefits, a professional development budget and attractive employee discounts.
