Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Prisons and Reintegration Investigation Prisons Zurich Supervisor/in / Supervisor/in 80 - 100% Prison and Reintegration (JuWe) is responsible as part of the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs of the Canton of Zurich for the execution of penalties and measures as well as the execution of the various types of detention. For our 1,400 employees from around 40 professions, the successful social reintegration of people who have committed crimes and the prevention of recidivism are of particular importance. The Zurich Prisons of Detention (UGZ) – one of eight main departments of JuWe – currently operates seven prisons for pre-trial and security detention. Around 300 employees look after more than 700 detainees. The Horgen prison offers 51 seats and is run as a department of the Limmattal prison. To complement our team in Horgen Prison, we are looking for a committed, cosmopolitan and humorous personality by immediate or by agreement. Your tasks

  • You will accompany, assist and employ detained persons during their pre-trial detention from entry to exit, taking on tasks in care, security and organisation
  • You will meet imprisoned people and visitors professionally, respectfully and in a situational manner and act clearly and de-escalatingly even in demanding situations.
  • You ensure an orderly daily routine, enforce safety and service regulations and manage crisis situations carefully
  • Test at headquarters You ensure the safety of employees, detainees and third parties by operating security, surveillance and communications facilities as well as by triggering the security facility in emergencies
  • You coordinate and supervise the visitation, receive gifts and ensure the necessary controls, including X-ray controls
  • You take on administrative tasks such as reports and documentation and support the administration within the framework of detachment agencies, in particular dossier management, correspondence and the management of detainee accounts Your profile
  • Meet people in a forced context You have a professional attitude, regulated proximity and distance, and the ability to detect crisis situations early and act de-escalating.
  • You are motivated to actively participate in the development and design of prison conditions
  • You are psychologically resilient and flexible for shift, night, weekend and holiday services
  • You have technical understanding and pleasure in dealing with safety-relevant systems
  • Completed commercial training (EFZ) or comparable and several years of professional experience, preferably in a comparable job
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Knowledge of foreign languages is advantageous
  • Able to deal with MS Office
  • Minimum age of 25 years and an impeccable reputation (no relevant entries in the penal or enforcement register) Our offer
  • A demanding, versatile job in a dynamic work environment with a supportive team
  • Careful familiarization as well as diverse internal and external further training possibilities, e.g. B. the part-time training as a specialist for the administration of justice with eidg. Specialist card
  • A centrally located place of work with good connections to public transport
  • Attractive employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines We are pleased about applicants with a lot of initiative, openness, humour and joy in working in a dynamic company.
