As part of the UGZ, Horgen Prison specializes in pre-trial detention for adult men and has 51 detention places. We are looking for committed, motivated and capable personalities with a high sense of responsibility who want to actively participate in the prison Zurich West with their expertise and positive attitude. Zurich Detention Centers (UGZ) are one of eight main departments of JuWe. They comprise seven companies with about 735 prison places and about 500 employees. They are responsible for provisional arrest, pre-trial detention and security detention, as well as the early execution of penalties and measures until transfer to a prison facility. In this way, they fulfil a central function within the Zurich justice system right from the start. As employees of the Zurich Detention Centers, we provide structured and human support for detained persons with expertise and commitment and help ensure that criminal proceedings are carried out in an orderly manner before a decision is made on guilt or innocence. With around 1400 employees, JuWe is the largest office within the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs and is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as for the execution of various types of detention. Our employees from around 40 professions support and promote the reintegration of delinquents and work effectively and progressively for a safe and just society.
