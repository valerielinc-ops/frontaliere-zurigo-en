About us

We enchant the world with chocolate! Lindt & Sprüngli (Switzerland) AG is a subsidiary of the global Lindt & Sprüngli Group with headquarters and production facilities in Kilchberg on Lake Zurich. In Altendorf we operate our own logistics centre, while the Lindt Cocoa Center in Olten produces cocoa mass for our own use as well as for other European production facilities. Throughout Switzerland, we proudly operate 15 Lindt shops, including iconic locations such as the Jungfraujoch and the Swiss Transport House in Lucerne. More than 1000 employees from more than 50 nations contribute to our success.

What you do with us

  • Development, planning and implementation of B2B marketing and customer development projects as well as management of the marketing activity plan (catalogues, brochures, direct mailings, newsletters, LinkedIn and META campaigns, promotions and customer acquisition measures)
  • Further development of the B2B product and service portfolio
  • Analysis of market trends, customer needs and business potential as well as derivation of corresponding measuresPlanning, coordination and management of agencies, printing companies and other external partners and service providers
  • Support and advice for business customers from inquiry and offer to successful order processing
  • Maintenance and active use of CRM and SAP systems

When you fit in with us

  • Basic commercial education and training in marketing, communication or sales
  • Several years of professional experience in project management, marketing and in the support of business customers
  • Experience in the development and implementation of marketing campaigns (online and offline) as well as affinity for direct marketing, print media and customer acquisition
  • Experience in the further development of products, services or marketing concepts
  • Enjoyment of consulting customers as well as experience in offering and coordinating customer projects
  • Strong organisational capacity, secure stakeholder management and a structured, independent working method
  • Entrepreneurial, creative and customer-oriented personality with high implementation strength as well as very good German and English skills
  • French advantage
