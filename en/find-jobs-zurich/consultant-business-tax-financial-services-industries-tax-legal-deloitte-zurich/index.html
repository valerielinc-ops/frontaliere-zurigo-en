Location: Zurich Work Pattern Percentage: 80% - 100% Our tax team is looking for you! Our Tax Team offers integrated tax services that help our customers to successfully manage local and global challenges. Do you want to advise discerning customers on different (operational) tax issues? On an entry level you will be part of our Financial Services Tax (FSI) team and take a vital role within our team of specialists, supporting them in tax advisory and compliance projects for Swiss and international banks, insurance and trust companies as well as asset managers. How you can make an impact

  • Get the opportunity to be involved in activities with client contact and be responsible for cross-divisional projects in various industries from the first day.
  • Get an exciting insight into our various diverse fields of projects such as national and international corporate restructurings, transactions and tax planning projects for banks, insurance companies, asset managers and investment companies as well as funds
  • Develop a clear, long-term career path with internal and external professional training offers as well as the time and financial assistance necessary for the training to become a dipl. Tax expert or study the MAS FH in Swiss and International Taxation / LL.M. Swiss and International Taxation.
  • Be part of an international and interdisciplinary team with plenty of learning on the job opportunities. Who We Are Looking For
  • You have a Master degree, preferably in business administration, economics or law, ideally with tax knowledge
  • You have a positive and pragmatic attitude as well as the willingness to take over responsibility
  • You are interested in investigating complex questions and an attitude that corresponds to our most important values: be curious and bold, respectful and integrative
  • You are interested in obtaining the Certified Swiss Tax Expert qualification/Master in Swiss and International Taxation/LL.M on Swiss and International Taxation
  • You are a native German speaker and communicate confidently in English. Any further languages would be an advantage. Your Team Become part of a diverse, multinational and highly motivated team of 30 employees in our office in Zurich.
