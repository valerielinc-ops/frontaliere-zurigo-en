About the Role In our R&D department in the area of Adhesive Systems we are looking for a motivated and dedicated Project Leader. In this versatile position you are the interface between research, production, quality assurance, marketing and application engineering, fulfilling the following tasks and responsibilities:

  • Independent development of products, technologies and their manufacturing processes as well as internal and external implementation
  • Leading of project teams; defines and calls for work packages, ensures the transfer of know-how and coordination
  • Contact and collaboration with internal and external interface functions
  • Manages the expectations of the customer and stakeholder
  • Technical support of the products and technologies internally and among customers
  • Knows and maintains state of the art technology (e.g. competitive products, specialized literature, congresses, patents)
  • Planning and documentation according to the process guidelines, presentations and vindication of the results/projects internally and externally
  • Patenting of new technologies and initiating proceeding

Your Skills and Experience

  • Education: Bachelor’s degree or higher in Chemistry, Material Science, Chemical Engineering, or a related field
  • Experience: Initial experience in R&D as a Junior Scientist, Product Developer, Project Leader, or similar, with expertise in product or technology development; previous experience in adhesives or related industries with deep market and technology knowledge is a plus
  • Project Management: Strong understanding of project management principles and ability to manage tasks and priorities
  • Problem-Solving: Analytical mindset with a solution-oriented approach
  • Collaboration: Builds and maintains internal networks, fostering teamwork and leveraging global resources
  • Language Skills: Proficient in English
  • Good knowledge in German
  • Technical Skills: Solid IT proficiency

About Sika Sika is a specialty chemicals company with a globally leading position in the development and production of systems and products for bonding, sealing, damping, reinforcing, and protection in the building sector and industrial manufacturing. Sika has subsidiaries in 103 countries around the world and, in over 400 factories, produces innovative technologies for customers worldwide. In doing so, it plays a crucial role in enabling the transformation of the construction and transportation sector toward greater environmental compatibility. With more than 33,000 employees, the company generated sales of CHF 11.20 billion in 2025.
