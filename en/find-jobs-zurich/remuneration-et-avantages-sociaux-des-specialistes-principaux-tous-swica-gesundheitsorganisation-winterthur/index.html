Full description

Would you like to actively shape compensation systems, support data-based decisions and make a noticeable contribution to employer attractiveness? In our HR team, you will take on a central role in compensation and benefits, advise internal stakeholders and continually develop our compensation landscape. This is how you shape your health

  • You are responsible for the preparation, conception and implementation of the annual wage round
  • You support the variable compensation process and the promotion process
  • You carry out market analyzes and benchmarks and further develop our compensation structures and wage bands
  • You analyze salary data, create evaluations and make well-founded recommendations
  • You advise HR colleagues and managers on all Comp&Ben issues
  • You will continuously develop our grading model as well as existing compensation and benefit concepts
  • You ensure professional documentation, governance and knowledge management in the department
  • You identify trends and optimization potential to further strengthen our attractiveness as an employer

This is what sets you apart

  • Bachelor's degree from a technical college or university, preferably in business administration or a related field
  • Several years of experience in the area of compensation & benefits
  • Additional training in the areas of Compensation & Benefits or Human Resources would be an advantage
  • Strong affinity for numbers as well as analytical and conceptual thinking skills
  • High level of advice and service orientation as well as the ability to communicate complex topics in an understandable manner
  • Independent and structured way of working combined with a high level of discretion
  • Your German is business fluent, spoken and written

Why SWICA

  • We build on humanity. That's why we put openness, respect and trust at the center of our everyday work. We get involved, take responsibility and think of solutions – together, for our customers. This is also why we have been number 1 in customer satisfaction for many years.
  • We trust you and give you space. With annual working hours, at least 5 weeks of vacation and, if desired, additional vacation time. With part-time models and a spacious home office within the scope of the position, you can structure your everyday life largely flexibly.
  • We value and reward your work – with a wage in line with the market, up to 1,500 francs an annual allowance for personal wishes and a generous contribution to the basic and supplementary insurance for you and your family.
  • We offer you perspectives. We support your professional and personal development with generous training regulations – for your role today and for your goals tomorrow.
  • Because health is everything. With our company health management and region-specific offers, we take care of your well-being. And if things turn out differently: 100% net salary continued payment.
  • We think long-term. With three savings plans, a workload-dependent coordination deduction and benefits well above the legal minimum, you can take good care of your future.

Veronica Massarelli, HR Recruiting, will be happy to provide you with information.
