Full description

Canton of Zurich Educational Directorate Cantonal School Stadelhofen Branch Dübendorf Company employees / cleaning 60% The Cantonal School Stadelhofen is a cantonal high school with an old- and modern-language, mathematical-scientific and musical profile. In 2026 the school was expanded by a branch in Dübendorf. In full operation, it will provide space for 650 pupils and, in addition to the short gymnasium, also include a commercial middle school and a sub-gymnasium. In the summer of 2027, another building will be opened in Dübendorf – also for 650 students. For the school operation of our new school location in Dübendorf, we are immediately looking for a committed, self-employed personalities as a company employee / cleaning 60%. The regular working hours are from Monday to Friday from 16:00 to 21:00. During school holidays, working hours can be flexibly adjusted due to more extensive maintenance and basic cleaning work. During these periods, the work mainly takes place during the day. Their tasks

  • Independent and thorough cleaning of the allocated areas in the school building
  • Compliance with applicable hygiene and cleaning standards
  • Execution of all work taking into account the occupational safety directives
  • Cooperation in basic, main and intermediate cleaning during school holidays
  • Taking over deputies in the event of absences in the team Your profile
  • You have a good knowledge of German in words and writing and already have experience in cleaning
  • You work independently, reliably and with great care
  • They are physically resilient and actively involved
  • They are flexible and ready for changing working hours
  • They are friendly, well-groomed and respectful.
  • They enjoy working in the school environment and have good access to young people Our offer We offer an interesting, versatile activity in an unusual, dynamic environment of a middle school. With your dedicated commitment, you make an important contribution to the culture of our school. The Conditions of Employment are governed by the Staff Regulations of the Canton of Zurich. Application Have we aroused your interest? We look forward to your application. Please submit your application documents exclusively via the online tool. Applications outside the online tool are not accepted and deleted immediately. For information, Mr. Shkodran Shabani, Head of Facility Management, Tel. 043 256 27 08, is at your disposal. Further information about our school can be found at Cantonal School Stadelhofen - Filiale Dübendorf.

Pensum: 60%
