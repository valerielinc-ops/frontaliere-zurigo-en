Occupation by: immediately or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 46371

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

Are you looking for a new professional challenge in which you do not only your expertise as a dipl. Can specialist use surgical technology, but also pass it on?

Then you are exactly right with us! At Hirslanden Clinic, we offer you the opportunity to deepen your expertise in surgical technology and at the same time exercise a vocational training function.

YOUR TASKS

  • Instrumentation and service during operations as well as implementation of all pre- and follow-up measures of the operations in a specialist group selected by you: specialist group 1: cardiac / vascular surgery, plastic surgery, ORL specialist group 2: trauma, ortho, foot, hand and sports surgery specialist group 3: visceral surgery, urology, gynecology, thoracic / neurosurgery
  • Technical and professional preparation and operation of the necessary equipment, instruments and sterli goods
  • Monitoring and care of patients in local anesthesia during procedures
  • Documentation of all nursing activities including performance recording
  • Assistance in ensuring a smooth daily routine in the operating room
  • Instrumentation at HSM Surgery

As a vocational trainer, you also take on the following activities:

  • Competent support of students both professionally and pedagogically during their practical training
  • Professional implementation of the educational mission in the department according to the training concept
  • Close cooperation and further development in the department with the management and the specialist consultants

Your profile

  • Training for the specialist surgical technique HF or Dipl. Medical professional FA OP
  • Completed SVEB course or willingness to complete it
  • Several years of work experience in acute care
  • You are a team player and open and respectful communication is just as important to you as it is to us.
  • High motivation and dedication as well as willingness to perform weekend/picquet as well as night service

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, a cool team awaits you with open arms, so that you quickly feel "at home" and like to show you a lot. We offer you individual and tailor-made training as well as flexible working time models that enable you to combine work and family. Re-entrants are also welcome.
