Full description

We offer our customers impeccable fitness and wellness facilities and ensure that the operational safety and availability of the technical building facilities, the construction facilities and the water treatment facilities for our fitness parks is implemented economically.

Luogo di lavoro: Fitnesspark, Fitnessparks Winterthur, Zurich Glattpark, Greifensee Milandia, Brunngasse 4, 8400 Winterthur

Important information

You are responsible for the fitness parks Winterthur, Zurich Glattpark, Greifensee Milandia.

Mansioni

  • Maintenance and maintenance of the building facilities, the construction facilities and the water treatment facilities for the fitness parks Zurich Glattpark, Winterthur, Greifensee Milandia
  • Ensuring the operational readiness of fitness parks
  • Maintenance and support of the entire fitness equipment park
  • Storage management and material orders
  • With your strong understanding of services, you contribute to customer loyalty

Requirements

  • Mind. 3 years of professional experience: in a comparable position
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): in the technical field preferably as an electrician / installation electrician / electromechanic / maintenance specialist
  • High willingness to provide services and good communication skills
  • Team-oriented thinking and action, sense of responsibility and independence
  • Flexibility in operational readiness and place of work (evening & weekend assignments, pikettdienst)
  • Holidays can be taken all year round outside the revision hours (July/August), in agreement with the superiors
  • German (very good knowledge)
