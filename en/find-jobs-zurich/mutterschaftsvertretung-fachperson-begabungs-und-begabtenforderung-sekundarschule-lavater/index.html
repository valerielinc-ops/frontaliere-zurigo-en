Full description

For our high school classes at Lavater we are looking for at the latest by 30. November 2026 or by arrangement a deputy for our specialist talent and talent promotion.

Tasks

The gifted and gifted promotion is an important part of our school. Together with the day school, we have established the implementation of the BBF concept. Today, the BBF is firmly anchored in our school day and offers students a wide range of opportunities to discover and develop their individual interests, strengths and talents. The offers are highly appreciated and make an important contribution to a potential-oriented school. Due to a maternity break, we will soon be looking for a committed specialist BBF for the continuation of our offers, at the latest from the end of the year.

Profile

  • Se have an EDK recognised teaching diploma
  • You are a committed, flexible and experienced specialist and support teacher
  • You enjoy working openly with the class and specialist teachers
  • They promote young people goal-oriented, efficient and appreciative
  • You have successfully completed the CAS Talent and Talent Promotion or are currently in training

We offer

  • Close cooperation with teachers
  • Support by and an appreciative cooperation with the school management
  • an own, very well equipped resource room
  • the opportunity to continue established BBF offerings such as pull-out groups at the day school Lavater and to further develop the talent portfolio together with the first year class

About us

The school district Uto is one of seven school districts in the city of Zurich and comprises districts 2 and 3 with the districts Leimbach, Wollishofen, Enge, Friesenberg and Alt-Wiedikon.

The school house Lavater is located in Enge-Zurich. It includes the buildings Schulhaus Lavater and Gym and the Klopstockwiese at the Waffenplatzstrasse tram stop. In total, more than 200 children attend the school Lavater. The children are taught in eleven standard classes by about 30 teachers.
