Digital Business Zurich | Pensum: 80-100 %

Working on the pulse of digital transformation. Our Digital Business department makes it possible. Together with us, shape the future of real estate management, develop innovative products and processes and move Livit forward together with us. In digital business, you can expect a great, interdisciplinary team, an inspiring working atmosphere and exciting projects in which you can actively participate. Are you excited about innovations, the latest systems, an agile setting and new challenges? Then you are exactly right with Livit. We look forward to your personal contribution.

Your tasks

  • You build, implement and continuously develop our ICT risk management processes
  • You pursue legal and regulatory requirements in the area of information security and translate them into concrete measures
  • You lead the IT Security Operations Manager professionally and personnel and promote its further development
  • You are the link to our parent company as well as to external security partners and control the cooperation
  • You conduct assessments and coordinate internal and external audits
  • You will be responsible for IT Continuity Management with Business Impact Analysis, Continuity Planning and Disaster Recovery Tests and will be involved in higher-level Business Continuity Management.
  • You continuously monitor the threat situation and evaluate new risks
  • Alert and report security incidents

Your profile

  • You have completed higher vocational training as a business informatics specialist: or a comparable qualification, ideally supplemented by recognized certifications in the field of information security (e.g. CISM, CISSP or ISO 27001 Lead Auditor)
  • You bring many years of professional experience in IT Risk or Information Security Management and move in this environment independently and confidently
  • You not only know the relevant frameworks (ISO/IEC 27001, CIS, COBIT), but apply them safely and routinely in practice
  • You have proven leadership experience and lead a specialist purposefully and effectively
  • When dealing with internal and external stakeholders, also at the level of parent company and security partners, you act safely and build sustainable relationships
  • You have very good German and good English skills in word and writing
  • You work in a structured, analytical and quality-conscious way and convince through quick comprehension and a strong process understanding.
  • You are a communicative, cooperative personality and like to take on responsibility

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
