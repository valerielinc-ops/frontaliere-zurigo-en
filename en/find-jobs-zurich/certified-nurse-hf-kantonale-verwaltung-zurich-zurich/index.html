Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Imprisonment and Reintegration Medical Service Nursing Specialist HF 80 - 100% Imprisonment and Reintegration (JuWe) is responsible as part of the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs of the Canton of Zurich for the execution of penalties and measures as well as the implementation of the various types of detention. For our 1,400 employees from around 40 professions, the successful social reintegration of people who have committed crimes and the prevention of recidivism are of particular importance. The newly created Main Medical Service Department ensures basic medical and somatic care for the detained persons in all prison institutions of the Canton of Zurich. As a central organizational unit, it ensures harmonized and high-quality health care according to the equivalence principle and works closely with the Psychiatric Psychological Service and the JuWe institutions. As part of the Zurich prison facilities (VEZ), the Centre for Administrative Detention under Foreigners Law (ZAA) has 130 detention places in the immediate vicinity of Zurich Airport. It is responsible for the accommodation of persons without a valid residence status whose alien law procedure must be ensured, taking into account different personal and individual needs of the admitted persons.
