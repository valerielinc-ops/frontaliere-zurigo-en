Full description

Vetsuisse Faculty, University Animal Hospital Zurich, Abbot of KT Dermatology

Job opening 01.02.2027 or by arrangement

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a motivated veterinarian with enthusiasm for dermatology. We offer a specialized internship in a university environment with the opportunity to deepen clinical experience, develop dermatological expertise and actively participate in patient care.

Tasks

  • Independent management and cooperation in the dermatological consultation under professional supervision
  • Care of dermatological patients including diagnosis and therapy
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with specialists from various disciplines
  • Cooperation in the care of inpatients
  • Participation in clinical meetings and training

Profile

  • Completed study of veterinary medicine
  • Basic medical and dermatological knowledge
  • Clinical practice experience is beneficial
  • Good knowledge of German in word and writing (prerequisite)
  • Team spirit, commitment and joy in clinical work
  • Independent, reliable and structured working method

We offer

  • Specialised dermatology internship without rotation into other disciplines
  • Close professional support by experienced specialists
  • State-of-the-art diagnostic and therapeutic options at the University Animal Hospital Zurich
  • Varied and demanding dermatological cases
  • Collegial, interdisciplinary team with diverse development opportunities
