Full description

Your heart beats for older people? Is the nursing profession your vocation which for you not only improves the quality of life of others, but also creates a satisfaction and meaningfulness in your own life? Do you see opportunities to use your skills and experience with us to make a positive impact on our customers’ lives? Then you are right with us!

For the Am Rietpark in the Zurich region we are looking for 01. November 2026 or by arrangement one or

Dipl. Nursing professional HF/FH/DNII 50 -100 %

Their tasks

  • Holistic care and care of guests in the sense of our care mission statement and the care process
  • Carrying out care activities
  • Planning and support of medical visits
  • Electronic care documentation through care coach
  • Taking on responsibilities as a reference person
  • Control of the care process
  • Management of case meetings
  • Assumption of daily responsibility with coordination of the day team
  • Assumption of tasks as a topic carrier, technical authority

Your profile

  • Completed training for/for dipl. HF/FH/DNII nurse or equivalent training recognised by the SRC
  • Professional experience in long-term care and/or in coordinated care assignments
  • RAI knowledge (InterRAI-HC) desired or willingness to attend these courses
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude
  • Enjoying multiple challenges
  • Proper knowledge of German, differentiated verbal and written expressive ability
