Full description

The Zweckverband Pflege und Betreuung Mittleres Tösstal provides the population of the four association municipalities Turbenthal, Wila, Wildberg and Zell with inpatient and ambulatory care, care and residential services according to the service mandate. Spitex Mittleres Tösstal provides professional hospital care and support for the inhabitants of the mentioned communities. We are a progressive and dynamic organization with over 90 employees and integrate interdisciplinary specialist teams, such as acute and long-term care, psychosocial care, palliative care, housekeeping and care as well as Spitex Plus services. In order to strengthen our committed team, we are looking for immediate or by agreement an employee for the meal service in the hourly wage for holiday repayment. Your work assignments are from about 11 a.m. to about 1 p.m. (also on weekends and public holidays).

Their main tasks

  • Delivery of lunch with your own vehicle (mileage expense will be compensated)
  • Personal delivery of meals to our clients at home
  • Removal of the empty goods
  • Forwarding of client feedback to the competent authority

They bring with them

  • Mandatory driving licence Cat. B, preferably with own vehicle
  • Good oral German skills
  • Place of residence in our association communities (Turbenthal, Wila, Wildberg or Zell)
  • Strong and flexible personality with very good manners
  • Willingness to work even on weekends and holidays

We offer

  • An appreciative cooperation in a young and collegial team
  • An innovative corporate culture
  • Various Seasonal Events as Active Health Promotion
