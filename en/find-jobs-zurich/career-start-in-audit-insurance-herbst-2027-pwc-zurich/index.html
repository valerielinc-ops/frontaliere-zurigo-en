Full description

Together we audit group and annual accounts of insurance companies. We deal with balance sheets, income statements, financial information, internal controls and regulatory reports. With our work, we build trust in financial sector reporting and learn from each other on a daily basis. From day one, you work on client mandates, are accompanied by experienced colleagues and get to know the financial sector up close. Step by step you take on more responsibility on your way to / to the dipl. Auditors:

  • Audit financial statements: audit consolidated and annual financial statements, balance sheets, income statements and internal controls, thereby creating confidence in financial reporting.
  • Analyze financial institutions: Analyze financial data and business processes, identify audit risks and develop an understanding of the economic context behind the numbers.
  • Enable regulation: Learn how regulatory requirements shape insurance operations, auditing and reporting.
  • Use data in a targeted manner: Use data analyses, digital testing methods and AI to identify abnormalities and prepare audit processes in a well-founded manner.
  • Assess reports: Depending on your mandate, contribute to regulatory audits or sustainability information reviews and document your findings in a comprehensible way.
  • Build relationships: Collaborate with experienced colleagues and contacts at banks, insurance companies and asset managers up to CFOs and build your professional network.

Why PwC?

At PwC, you don’t just start into a first job. You will learn a recognized profession, work early with customers and gain insights into different companies and management levels. At the same time, you will be targeted on your way to / to dipl. Auditor: accompanied.

Apply now for your career start in auditing and become part of our Community of Solvers.

  • Your professional basis: You are about to graduate or have a Bachelor or Master in Economics, (Business) Informatics, STEM or a comparable field of study.
  • Your interest in the financial sector: You want to build your knowledge in accounting, financial reporting, auditing and the regulatory foundations of the financial sector.
  • Your analytical strength: You work carefully, analyze financial data and want to learn to assess complex issues in a structured way.
  • Your cooperation: You communicate clearly, actively participate in the team and enjoy working with customers and international colleagues.
  • Your development potential: You are willing to learn, open to digital ways of working and ready to take on responsibility. Very good knowledge of German and English round off your profile.

Your perspective: Your path to a diploma Your career start combines practical mandate work in the financial sector with a clearly structured education:

  • Learn the profession: After your onboarding, work directly in exam teams, learn about our exam methodology and audit software and gradually take on your own exam areas.
  • Grow in the Busy Season: From January to April, you work intensively on annual audits and binding customer appointments. In the Financial Services Audit, regulatory reporting deadlines are added. You apply your knowledge directly and develop quickly.
  • Reach your diploma: We accompany you on the way to / to the dipl. Auditors: in and support your education in time and financially. Paid lessons, self-study and exams are associated with your practical work.
