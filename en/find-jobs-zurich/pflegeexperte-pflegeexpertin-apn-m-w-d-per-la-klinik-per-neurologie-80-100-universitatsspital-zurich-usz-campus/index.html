Full description

Our core competence at the University Hospital Zurich is to ensure high-quality patient care with our professional diversity in the top medical environment. >The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.

Tasks

  • Lead and promote specialist development of the departments – with the support of the management team (management, education and specialist)
  • Care and support of patients with complex neurological diseases – with a pronounced patient orientation
  • Interprofessional collaboration in a team with various professional groups to support patients and relatives
  • Development, introduction, implementation and evaluation of technical concepts as well as integration of topic-specific innovations and developments
  • Qualification and coaching of employees in complex care situations – based on several years of professional experience in acute and possibly rehabilitative care
  • Active co-creation as part of the care expert:innen team with co-responsibility for the goal achievement of the area
  • Master of Science in Nursing already existing or degree in preparation
  • Structured familiarization and close cooperation with the long-term care expert of neurology is guaranteed
