Application process

  • As a Research Engineer Mechatronics, you'll help shape future HVAC applications by developing concepts that solve real customer challenges and create lasting value. What You'll Do
  • Lead research and exploration projects from initial hypothesis through to validated concepts, independently defining objectives and managing key stakeholders.
  • Develop innovative mechatronic solutions that address real customer challenges, creating and advancing new product and technology concepts.
  • Design, build, and evaluate mock-ups, prototypes, and functional demonstrators to verify and validate new ideas.
  • Analyze complex technical challenges and derive practical, future-oriented solutions for mechatronic systems, drives, and sensing technologies.
  • Plan, execute, and interpret sophisticated experiments and measurements to generate insights and support decision-making.
  • Challenge existing actuator and sensor concepts and develop sustainable next-generation approaches for HVAC applications.
  • Identify and assess emerging technologies, market developments, and industry trends, translating relevant research findings into future products and applications.

Company and context

  • Collaborate with suppliers, universities, and research partners, support intellectual property activities, and actively promote knowledge sharing across the organization. Who You Are
  • Master's degree in Mechatronics or a related engineering discipline.
  • Several years of experience in the design and development of mechatronic systems.
  • Strong expertise in mechatronics, drive technology, and system-level engineering.
  • Experience working in early-stage concept development and technology exploration.
  • Understanding of sensors, actuators, embedded systems, and control technologies.
  • Familiarity with automation, and emerging mechatronic technologies.
  • Self-motivated, innovative, and able to develop creative solutions for complex challenges.
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management skills.
  • Fluent English, German is a plus. Who We Are
