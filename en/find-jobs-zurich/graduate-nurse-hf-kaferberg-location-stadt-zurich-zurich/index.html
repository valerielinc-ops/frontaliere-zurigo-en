Full description

Do you like to work with people? You want to work with the Alter Käferberg health center immediately or by agreement with our nursing team?

Tasks

  • They assume day responsibility and are responsible for advising and accompanying the residents and relatives during illness and crises.
  • They are responsible for planning, implementing, monitoring and adapting the care process as well as for delegating tasks to employees of other levels of competence.
  • They perform nursing and medical engineering tasks, mainly in complex unstable situations.
  • They are present at doctor's visits and participate in interdisciplinary reports.
  • They contribute to the development of departmental objectives to ensure quality and cooperation.

Profile

  • Flexible work is at the heart of this project: employees design their assignments independently, work in different teams and areas (AÜP/D, dementia and long-term) and plan their own schedule. They say when they work – we say where.
  • Empathic personality with a strong orientation towards people and enthusiasm for self-responsible work, combined with team spirit and commitment
  • Completed training as a nursing specialist (HF/FH/Danii) with SRK recognition for diplomas from abroad
  • Good German knowledge in word and writing as well as excellent communication skills, a prism of humour, a strong sense of responsibility, the ability to think connected and high resilience

We offer

Look forward to an exciting area of work in a professional and team-oriented working environment with a culture of du-culture throughout the company. The person is at the center of our work. That is why we support the compatibility of private and professional life, and different work models make it possible.

As part of the city of Zurich and due to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, advanced employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

The wages of the nursing and therapeutic professions were raised to strengthen these occupational groups. The annual salary for this function lies between Fr. 85,228.– and Fr. 113,882.– (100%) depending on experience.

About us

The health centres for the age of the city of Zurich are one of the largest Geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive range from a single source.

The health centre for Alter Käferberg in Wipkingen in district 10 combines various care and care services under one roof and is characterized by many years of experience in the field of old age medicine. Thanks to the unique location on Käferberg, the residents, patients and guests enjoy a wonderful view of Lake Zurich, the Alps and the rooftops of the city. Here you work together with 400 colleagues.
