Senior Specialist - 3D Footwear Modeller — On Running

Full description

In short On is looking for a 3D Design Modeller to support our product design teams from early ideation phase to final design review. Your vision and expertise as a technical artist will empower designers, accelerate decisions, and shape the evolution of our performance-driven products.

Your mission

  • Leverage 3D Design during exploration and ideation to support creative direction and bring the vision to life.
  • Build comprehensive 3D concepts encompassing both bottom units and upper builds.
  • Produce high-fidelity renders and 3D prints to enhance design review meetings.
  • Prepare clean, optimized, and watertight 3D files for digital simulation and prototyping.
  • Translate input from design and engineering into actionable assets, partnering with designers to streamline the tech packing process.
  • Iterate fast, learn faster, and contribute ideas that improve both product quality and digital workflows.
  • Act as a digital champion - stay ahead of digital trends, mentor design teams, and continuously optimize 3D pipelines.

Your story

  • You hold a Bachelor’s degree in Industrial Design, Mechanical Engineering, Footwear Technology, or a related field (equivalent hands-on industry experience is also highly valued).
  • You bring 5+ years of experience in footwear 3D footwear modelling.
  • You possess mastery in industry-standard footwear 3D tools - with deep expertise in Rhino 3D (including Grasshopper,) alongside Blender (or equivalent.)
  • You have strong poly modeling, subD and nurbs fundamentals and can quickly translate design ideas into refined 3D design models.
  • You have a deep understanding of footwear construction and mold making.
  • You exhibit exceptional spatial awareness, geometric problem-solving skills, and a keen eye for subtle curves, textures, and proportions.
  • You possess excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to manage complex projects and deliver accurate files under critical timelines and deadlines.
  • You are fluent in English, both written and spoken.

Meet the team You will work in the design studio with an international team of designers across different disciplines, designing and developing the most advanced products and technologies. We deliver Innovation and Design by leveraging state of the art manufacturing technologies and design tools. This team envisions and develops the most exciting long-term innovative solutions into high level product concepts across all our product verticals that are driven by Athlete Performance, the Fashion World, consumer insights, Sustainability goals and more.

What we offer

On is a place that is centered around growth and progress. We offer an environment designed to give people the tools to develop holistically – to stay active, to learn, explore and innovate. Our distinctive approach combines a supportive, team-oriented atmosphere, with access to personal self-care for both physical and mental well-being, so each person is led by purpose.
