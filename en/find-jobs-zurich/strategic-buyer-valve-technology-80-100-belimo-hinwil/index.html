Full description

In this role, you proactively shape the procurement of the Innovation Division. You are responsible for the global supplier and technology portfolio, drive cost and performance optimizations and work closely with internal development interfaces. You combine strategic thinking with operational implementation and make a significant contribution to sustainable corporate success.

What will you do

  • Active participation in the development and implementation of the global procurement strategy for our technology portfolio in close cooperation with the Innovation Department
  • Management and cooperation in sourcing and other projects
  • Responsibility for the strategic and operational management of at least one technology (category management)
  • Supplier management including implementation of contract, price and annual negotiations, conclusion of supply and service contracts as well as supplier assessment and development
  • Management and continuous improvement of supplier performance (cost, quality, delivery reliability, risk)
  • Ensuring security of supply and an efficient and demand-oriented logistics process
  • Operational procurement including offer management, order processing and SAP data maintenance
  • Controlling and monitoring key figures at portfolio and supplier level

Who you are

  • Completed mechanical/technical or commercial training, additional training in logistics beneficial
  • Deep technical understanding and in-depth knowledge of logistics chains
  • Several years of experience in operational and strategic procurement, ideally in an international environment
  • Experience in contracting and project management
  • Very good SAP knowledge, especially in the module MM
  • Leader with a high degree of independence, decision-making strength and a strong sense of responsibility
  • Team-oriented, communication-strong and negotiating personality with intercultural competence
  • Flowing knowledge of German and English in words and writing and willingness to travel (about 10%)
