Department of Economics

Job start spring semester

The Institute of Economics at the University of Zurich is a renowned, world-renowned teaching and research institute in the field of economics. With more than 300 members, we conduct research in international teams using an interdisciplinary approach that combines modern economics with political science, psychological, biological and sociological elements.

Tasks

For the event Macroeconomics I at Prof. Andreas Müller in the spring semester we are looking for tutors. Each tutor will hold two tutorial(s) per week of 2 semester hours. The tutorial also includes participation in a kick-off meeting before the start of the semester, six tutorial sessions (every two weeks) and optionally the help of the exam view.

Profile

  • You are currently a student at the University of Zurich.
  • You should have finished the event yourself, Macroeconomics II and Mathematics I/II with excellent to very good performances (or are currently at it).
  • You have a motivating personality and can imagine passing on your knowledge and your own experiences from your studies to the new students.
  • They enjoy being part of a young and dynamic team.

Compensation

  • If you give us a tutorial for the first time: 3 ECTS points according to the Bachelor / Master order (5.3.4 / 4.3.4).
  • Have you already given a tutorial: Choice between payment fee and ECTS points.
