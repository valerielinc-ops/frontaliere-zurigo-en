Full description

You will build our Bidding & Proposal Office Institutions and thus directly influence the expansion of our institutional business. In this key role, you will successfully manage complex public tenders, coordinate interdisciplinary teams, and ensure that we are perceived as a clearly positioned, operationally strong, and reliable education partner.

Tasks

  • Building and further developing the Bidding & Proposal Office Institutions
  • Coordination and management of public tenders and proposal processes
  • Development of bidding strategies as well as assessment of feasibility and profitability
  • Ensuring priorities, resource deployment, and quality standards
  • Coaching and sparring of teams for complex proposal dossiers
  • Establishment and continuous optimization of processes, tools, and best practices

Requirements

  • Several years of experience: in public procurement, ideally in an educational or institutional environment
  • Completed degree (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): in business administration or comparable qualification
  • Experience in public procurement and complex tenders, experience with RFI and RFP processes is desirable
  • Business know-how (calculation, pricing, profitability)
  • Good understanding of the Swiss institutional and educational landscape
  • Team orientation and ability to lead and coordinate in an interdisciplinary manner
  • Willingness to shape, resilience, and joy in building new structures
  • Resilient, active network in all parts of the country
  • German (negotiation-proof)
  • French (fluent)
  • English (fluent)
  • Italian (an advantage)

What we offer

  • Central workplace: Our locations are centrally located
  • Mobile-flexible working model: Hybrid working - temporal and spatial flexibility in fulfilling the function
  • Personal responsibility & freedom: Varied range of tasks with high personal responsibility
  • Maternity leave / Paternity leave: 18 weeks of maternity and 4 weeks of paternity leave
  • Fair wages: Function-oriented and market-compliant remuneration
  • Occupational pension: The Migros pension fund guarantees the financial security of Migros employees in the third phase of life

Recruitment Process Submit application documents: For a complete application, we need your CV, certificates, and diplomas. A photo in the CV and a cover letter are not necessary. We live equal opportunities for all applicants. We have a standardized recruitment process. If this does not work for you, please mention it in your application dossier. Pre-selection of applications: We continuously review incoming applications, create a pre-selection, and release it to the manager for review. The decision on whether to proceed or exit the recruitment process is communicated by phone or e-mail. Time until feedback: Up to three weeks. Interview with HR & Department: The interview is conducted virtually or on-site with the responsible person from HR and/or the department. The primary goal is to learn more about personal and professional skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Time until feedback: Up to three weeks. Technical interview with case presentation: Specific professional knowledge is tested based on a case or other assessment instruments. After processing, the result is presented to the manager and then discussed together. Usually, the team is also represented at the presentation. As a rule, the interviews are conducted by the supervisor, without a representative from HR. Time until feedback: Up to three weeks. Final interview before contract issuance: Before issuing the contract, we conduct a final interview to clarify open questions from both sides and regulate contractual details. If all points are clarified, the written offer follows. Time until feedback: Up to one week. Offer & Contract issuance (Digital): After our verbal job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract creation is commissioned and sent by e-mail for digital signature. Time until feedback: Up to one week.

Note for personnel service providers: For this position, we only consider direct applications.
