Full description

You love food and direct customer contact? Then come to our branch M-Express Altstetten. We place great emphasis on fresh and good quality food and on first-class advice. Then get involved now and join our team for 2 months!

Mansioni

  • You support us actively in all areas and contribute to shaping a positive shopping experience for our customers
  • You take responsibility for efficient goods management, the attractive presentation of the products and ensure that our shelves are always well filled
  • You inspire our customers through friendly and competent advice
  • You ensure the high quality of our products including date control

Requisiti

  • Professional experience: in the field of food or logistics
  • You have fun selling food
  • You enter customer-oriented and friendly
  • You like to work independently and reliably
  • You are flexible and willing to work outside the usual working hours? Enjoy your free time when others work!
  • No training required
  • German (good knowledge)

Cosa offriamo

  • Healthy work: Healthy offers such as massages and health checks
  • Social benefits: Fair social benefits
  • Social and personal advice: Internal social counselling
  • Occupational pension: Excellent pension funds with high employer participation
