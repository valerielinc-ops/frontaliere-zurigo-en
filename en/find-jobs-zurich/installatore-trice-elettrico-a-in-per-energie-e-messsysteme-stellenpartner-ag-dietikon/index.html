Full description

Install and install energy meters and communication systems with care. In a team with modern tools, you will master versatile tasks in a technical environment. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Assembly, installation and commissioning of energy meters and metering systems
  • Establishment and testing of communication and data transmission systems
  • Performance of measurements, functional checks and error analysis
  • Documentation of work and maintenance of technical documentation
  • Independent project assignments throughout Switzerland, including Romandie and Ticino

Requirements

  • Training as an electrical installer:EFZ
  • Experience in electrical installations, ideally in energy or measurement
  • Good knowledge of high and low current installations
  • Good knowledge of German, French and/or Italian is advantageous
  • Category B driving licence and willingness to travel
  • Independent, careful working methods as well as high quality and safety awareness
