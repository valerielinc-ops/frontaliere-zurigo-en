Full description

Eraneos is an international strategy, transformation and technology advisory group that helps companies thrive in a dynamic digital world. With first-class experts from business and technology, we empower our customers to set new standards for sustainable transformations – from strategy to implementation. Whether in the design of future-proof organizations, the development of new potential through data and AI or the protection of companies through holistic cybersecurity: We deliver results pragmatically and purposefully. Our team of around 1,300 dedicated employees is represented in offices in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, Portugal, Denmark, Sweden, Austria, the UK, Singapore and the USA and combines global perspectives with strong local roots. Around 450 employees work at the Swiss locations in Zurich, Bern and Lausanne.

Digital transformation and AI are changing what public administration procures and how it uses public funds effectively. As a Senior Consultant Public Procurement, you support federal agencies, cantons and other public organisations through complex procurement, from strategy to procurement. In doing so, you are actively helping to build up the public procurement advisory service at Eraneos Switzerland.

Your main tasks

  • Management of complex procurement mandates from strategy to requirements collection to procurement
  • Advising on strategic, commercial and legal issues in public procurement
  • Development of procurement and sourcing strategies as well as evaluation of providers and solutions increasingly also in the field of AI
  • Project responsibility at the interface of department, processes, IT and market including control of internal and external participants
  • Further development of the consulting offer, competence building in the team and expansion of long-term customer relationships

This job is for you if

  • You have a university degree and several years of experience in public procurement.
  • You have in-depth knowledge of Swiss procurement law, in particular BöB and IVöB, and can safely manage complex procurement procedures.
  • You will collect specialist requirements from organization, processes and IT in a structured manner and assess providers, solutions and sourcing approaches in a well-founded manner.
  • You manage complex projects in a structured manner and bring different stakeholders together in a targeted manner.
  • You think entrepreneurially, take responsibility and speak German and English at C1 level.

As an i-top you bring with you

  • Project (management) experience with HERMES
  • Experience in procuring IT, digitization or consulting services and with complex contracts
  • Knowledge of French and legal background or training at the interface of procurement and law

What is important to us With Eraneos, what counts is what you want to move. Even if you do not fully meet every requirement, we look forward to your application. We are interested in your mindset, your ideas and the impact you want to create. Diversity is a source of growth and inspiration for us. We look forward to every application.

Annalena is your contact person and looks forward to receiving your direct application via our website (CV, diplomas and employment certificates as PDF).

Applications by e-mail or through personnel service providers are not considered.
