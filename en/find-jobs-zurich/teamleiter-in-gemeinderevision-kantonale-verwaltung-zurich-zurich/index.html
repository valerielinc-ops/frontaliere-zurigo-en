Canton of Zurich Financial Directorate Tax Office Team Leader/in municipal audit 80 - 100% Welcome to the fascinating world of taxes! Together with the municipal tax offices, the cantonal tax office collects taxes for the Confederation, the canton and the municipalities. We support individuals and businesses throughout the tax collection process. Around 800 qualified employees are committed to this at the Zurich Altstetten site. The municipal audit independently and on the basis of the legal requirements checks the approximately 160 municipal tax offices in the canton of Zurich. The focus is on the various areas of state taxes and the interfaces to the cantonal tax office. In this way, we support the cantonal tax office in its supervisory responsibility and make an important contribution to the quality assurance and further development of tax processes in the canton of Zurich. Your tasks:

  • Personnel and organizational leadership as well as targeted further development of a team of 3-5 experienced specialists
  • Specific implementation of own audits at the municipal tax offices
  • Ensure uniform and high-quality audits in the areas of procurement, registers, finances and investments
  • Preparation, maintenance and implementation of risk analyses and the audit and audit plan
  • Ensuring quality, deadline and volume specifications including controlling and reporting
  • Professional advice and support of the municipal tax offices Your profile:
  • Basic commercial training (EFZ) or equivalent degree and training in audit, management or tax areas relevant to the
