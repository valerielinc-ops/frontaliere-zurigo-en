Full description

We are the pulse of Zurich and keep the city in motion every 7.5 minutes. In order for this to work reliably and smoothly, a lot moves behind the scenes of the VBZ. Our colleagues work in more than 50 professions: in technology, IT, administration, traffic development, offer planning, infrastructure and many other exciting areas of responsibility. Where we drive, Zurich lives. Come on!

Tasks

  • Development and implementation of several challenging projects at the assigned locations in SIA phases 2 to 5 as owner representatives or as user representatives
  • Cross-divisional planning, coordination and monitoring of the tasks and needs of users, operators, creators and owners (VBZ), external partners of the City of Zurich, as well as the cantonal and national authorities (BAV)
  • Achieving viable consensus solutions on different objectives and interests of internal and external partners and conducting demanding negotiations
  • Ensuring phase-appropriate integration of facility management requirements and needs into project development and implementation
  • Define project objectives, plan resources, manage risk management and monitor project progress
  • Initiation and supervision of the necessary approval, financing procedures and handling of tenders under public law and the Submission Ordinance of the City of Zurich
  • Periodic project controlling (content, cost, quality, deadlines) with the help of SAP
  • Creation of internal and external decision bases (concepts, plans, key data, presentations, etc.) and documentation

Profile

  • Completed 4-year apprenticeship in the construction industry and/or Matura
  • Degree Architect ETH/FH, Civil Engineer ETH/FH or comparable
  • Safe handling of the relevant SIA standards, in particular SIA 103, 112, 113 and 118
  • In-depth experience with planning and licensing procedures under railway law, in particular with the planning approval procedure (PGV) under railway law as well as cantonal licensing procedures
  • Sound knowledge and practical experience in Swiss construction and planning law
  • Ability to analyze, structure and convert complex and diverse information into clear, comprehensible processes and decision-making basis
  • Experience in planning, controlling and controlling demanding projects in terms of quality, costs, deadlines and content
  • Strong communication skills and a secure approach to different stakeholders

A lot of appreciation for what you do: The people of Zurich like their VBZ. Working with us is proud. We want you to feel good: We are “Friendly Workspace” certified and do a lot for your health. Discover the whole of Switzerland with the GA – and the family also drives really well thanks to generous discounts.

With our vehicles we travel twice a day around the world and transport over 860,000 passengers. This means that every day, 365 days a year and almost around the clock we make an important contribution to the quality of life in one of the most beautiful cities in the world.
