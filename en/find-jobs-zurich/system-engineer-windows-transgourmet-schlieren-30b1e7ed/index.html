Full description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional specialist advice and innovative services. Transgourmet Schweiz AG is part of the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss supply and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retailers).

Tasks

  • You design, implement and operate modern Windows-based platforms and actively develop them further (focus on engineering, with active participation in the company)
  • Together with the team, you ensure stable and secure operation over the entire lifecycle (run/operate & improve): monitoring/alerting, incident/problem management, patch/release management, capacity/performance and availability.
  • You advance standardization, automation and security of system environments (e.g. via IaC/Skripting)
  • You assume engineering responsibility for lifecycle topics: design, build, patch/release management, capacity, performance and availability
  • You analyze disruptions sustainably, perform root-cause analyses and establish lasting improvements (problem management)
  • You work in company-wide projects as a technical lead or specialist and contribute your know-how
  • You advise application managers and internal teams on infrastructure, architecture, security and operational models
  • You maintain system, architecture and operational documentation, standards and runbooks and ensure their availability
  • After training, you take on Pikett services on a defined basis and ensure operational security even outside of office hours
  • Scheduled maintenance windows and changes take place regularly outside office hours and on weekends (as required)

Requirements

  • Completed computer science training (EFZ/HF/FH) or comparable, several years of practical experience as a Windows System Engineer
  • Very good knowledge of the Microsoft ecosystem, e.g. Microsoft 365, Azure, Windows Server & Clients, MS SQL (operational), Active Directory/Entra ID, Exchange (Online/Hybrid), Teams/VoIP
  • Experience with virtualization technologies, ideally VMware (vSphere/ESXi/vCenter)
  • Good knowledge of network technologies and security fundamentals (LAN/WLAN, Cisco, 802.1x, hardening, identity & access concepts)
  • Experience with backup/restore and storage (e.g. Veeam) and monitoring/logging
  • Strong skills in automation & scripting (PowerShell; ideally Git-assisted working method, CI/CD-related methods beneficial)
  • Advantageously, you are familiar with Ansible and source code management with Git
  • Structured, self-responsible working method, high service orientation and good communication
  • User experience in the Atlassian Cloud Suite
  • Very good German
  • Knowledge of French and/or Italian is an advantage
