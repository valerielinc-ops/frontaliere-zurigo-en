At ABB , we help industries run leaner and cleaner—and every person here makes that happen. You’ll be empowered to lead, supported to grow, and proud of the impact we create together. Join us and help run what runs the world.

Vice President, Corporate M&A

We are looking for a Corporate M&A Analyst to join the Corporate Strategy and M&A team in Zurich, Switzerland. The team is involved in a variety of elements related to ABB’s corporate strategy, portfolio development, and the strategic and commercial facets of inorganic transactions, along with other significant initiatives throughout the organization. The team typically works on topics that feature on the agenda of ABB’s executive leadership.

This role reports to the Vice President Corporate M&A and offers a unique opportunity to contribute to ABB’s strategic direction. It is well-suited for early-career professionals who are motivated to grow within a global industrial technology leader.

Your

  • Responsibilities

As a Corporate M&A Analyst, you will support the Corporate Strategy and M&A team in a variety of strategic and operational tasks. You will gain comprehensive insight into the full M&A lifecycle while engaging in collaboration across ABB's diverse business functions.

- Support in implementing group-wide M&A governance model

- Assist in the rollout of a bolt-on deal prioritization tool

- Manage the deal opportunity pipeline and coordinate with Business Area/Division Strategy teams to identify acquisition targets and divestment opportunities

- Track and report on M&A pipeline and deal economics; support implementation of a common platform for opportunity and deal status monitoring

- Help provide visibility on functional M&A resource loading, availability, and allocation

- Support Corporate M&A deal execution efforts and assist in preparing M&A-related presentations for internal and external stakeholders

- Stay informed on M&A market trends, competitor activity, and regulatory developments

  • Qualifications

We are aiming to identify a meticulous and inquisitive candidate who is invested in corporate strategy and M&A.

- Bachelor’s degree in finance, Economics, Accounting, or a related field

- 1–3 years of experience in investment banking, corporate development, or a similar field

- Understanding of financial modeling and valuation techniques

- Proficient analytical, communication, and presentation skills

- Ability to thrive in a demanding, high-stakes environment

- A collaborative attitude and adeptness in engaging effectively with a variety of individuals

Location: Zurich, Switzerland, eligibility for Swiss work permit is a pre-requisite.
