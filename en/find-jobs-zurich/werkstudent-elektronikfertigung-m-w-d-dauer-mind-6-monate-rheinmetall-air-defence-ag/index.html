Full description

In addition to your studies, would you like to gain practical experience in a technologically demanding production environment and actively contribute to the improvement of manufacturing processes? In this versatile function, you support our production team in the analysis and optimization of existing processes, evaluate production data and help to continuously develop our electronics production. Specifically, you expect the following tasks:

  • Documentation of existing production processes in close cooperation with employees and specialist areas
  • Preparation and analysis of production data as well as preparation of meaningful reporting, key figures and overviews
  • Support for ongoing optimization and lean projects in electronics manufacturing
  • Active participation in anchoring the lean idea in the company
  • Creation and updating of process descriptions, working documents and presentations
  • Support in the implementation and follow-up of improvement measures WHAT YOU SHOULD PARTICIPATE
  • Ongoing studies in industrial engineering, electrical engineering, mechanical engineering or a comparable technical discipline
  • Interest in industrial manufacturing, data analysis and continuous process improvement
  • First experience with lean methods is advantageous
  • Very good MS Office skills, especially Excel and PowerPoint
  • Good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • Structured, meticulous and reliable way of working as well as enjoyment of familiarizing yourself with new topics
  • Team-capable, communicative personality with the willingness to actively approach employees in production
  • Self-initiative, sense of responsibility and the ability to tackle new challenges independently and goal-oriented WHAT WE ARE TO YOU
  • A dynamic working environment with exciting projects
  • Flexible working hours (active and trusted working hours)
  • Very good social benefits and a super-compulsory pension fund
  • Employee benefits of a large corporation, employee share purchase program
  • Promotion of needs-based training
  • Staff restaurant and external catering and shopping facilities nearby
  • Individual internal and external personnel development measures in cooperation with our Rheinmetall Academy
  • Workplace a few minutes walk from the Zurich-Oerlikon train station KONTAKTDETAILS Responsible contact: Mr. Michael Zmoos For questions about your application, please use the contact form: https://rheinmetall.com/kontakt-bewerbung We point out that dossiers from recruiters are not considered.
