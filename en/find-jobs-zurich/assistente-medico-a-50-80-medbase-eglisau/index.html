Welcome to Medbase Eglisau

Our Medical Center with its own ultrasound and X-rays is located in the beautiful Zurich lowlands, just a few meters from the Rhine and within walking distance of the train station. Our team, consisting of around 10 employees, is made up of experienced specialists in general internal medicine and MPAs. At Medbase Eglisau we pay attention to a good work-life balance. Working part-time is possible and working hours can be arranged flexibly.

Would you like to gain initial experience in outpatient medicine or specifically deepen your knowledge of primary care? Then you have come to the right place.

Our practice is authorized to provide further training for one year and offers you the opportunity to gain an exciting and varied insight into basic outpatient care.

You can look forward to a wide diagnostic and medical spectrum - from laboratory, LUFU, X-rays and ultrasound to interprofessional collaboration in everyday practice. In addition to structured training, you can expect mentoring from experienced specialists, regular case discussions and internal training.

Tasks

  • Care of patients in general internal medicine under supervision
  • Basic care activities based on managed care principles
  • Independent anamnesis and diagnosis
  • Documentation in the practice management system
  • Collaboration in an interdisciplinary team

Requirements

  • Completed medical studies and interest in further training in the field of general internal medicine
  • Solid knowledge of German (at least B2 level)

We offer

  • Enjoy working independently
  • Openness to teamwork
  • Interest in modern, patient-oriented primary care
  • A helpful, communicative team that places great value on mutual support and positive cooperation

#WeAreMedbase

The Medbase Group is one of the leading companies in outpatient healthcare. This means services from health promotion to medication supply and acute treatment to rehabilitation. Health professionals from various disciplines work hand in hand to offer people throughout Switzerland integrated, first-class all-round medical care. The Medbase Group includes the brands Medbase Pharmacies, Zur Rose, zahnarztzentrum.ch, WePractice and Bluecare.
