Full description

WE LOOK YOU.

For a security job that hardly exists a second time in Switzerland.
The internal security at the University Hospital Zurich is much more than a classic guarding task – it combines a professional alarm centre and operational field service in a highly dynamic hospital environment. Information, alarms and decisions come together at headquarters. In the field service you are present where you are needed.
From prevention and de-escalation to intervention: Your tasks require calm, judgment and presence. Equally important is what you bring humanly. You meet patients, relatives and employees even in exceptional situations with empathy and respect – often people in fear, pain or overwhelming. Anyone who exists here can allow closeness and still maintain distance.
Close cooperation with care, medicine, police and rescue organisations makes this mission unique throughout Switzerland.

Ready for a task that really suits you? Then we want to know you.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: Alarm centre • Capture and triage the incoming alarms and meet the necessary dispositions • Monitoring of building and site safety by means of a camera system • Operation and management of the parking guidance system • Management of the event journal and preparation of mission reports • Coordination of blue light organisations and helicopter landings as well as scheduling in case of incidents and crises (FaoL) Field service • Willingness to help internal and external customers: • Proportionate enforcement of house and traffic regulations on the hospital area as well as ensuring peace and order • Intervention in aggression and threat situations • Preventive control activities in the USZ buildings and on the hospital site • Execution of special orders Requirements • Completed vocational training EFZ, ideally in a technical profession. A federal certificate in the field of security (FSB or FPO) is a great advantage. We are also happy if you already have experience from the security area of a hospital. • We require at least 5 years of proven professional experience in personal / object protection and / or in an alarm center. • Swiss citizenship, a settlement permit C or a residence permit B (EU/EFTA) is also a basic requirement. • German at level C1 with a secure understanding of the Swiss German language. Further language skills are very welcome. • Technical understanding as well as a secure handling of IT systems and MS Office. • Physically and psychologically resilient, responsible and team-oriented personality. • Sovereign occurrence in conflict and stress situations with de-escalating behavior. • Cat. B driving licence and an unobjectionable reputation: Criminal and enforcement register extracts must be submitted at the latest by the trial service. We offer: • Annual planning • We offer up-to-date employment conditions with a transparent, performance-based remuneration according to the cantonal wage table • A collegial team and great self-expectation as well as a high scope for action awaits you • Internal training in the area of security personal/object protection (at least 3 per year, e.g.) Aggression management, personal safety, communication training, interdisciplinary training • Daycare (subsidized care places at four locations) • Health management (courses, consultations, subsidized fitness subscription) Here you can find a little insight into the everyday life of USZ Security: https://www.instagram.com/stories/highlights/17908472707822853/
