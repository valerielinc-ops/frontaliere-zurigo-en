Full description

The Traffic Police (SPA-VP) ensures the traffic police presence, controls traffic flow, ensures the organization and implementation of traffic controls and accompanies VIPs, demonstrations, etc. In this specialized commission we are looking for several employees.

Tasks

  • Traffic police patrolling by motorcycle, patrol car or video vehicle (civilian)
  • Recording and handling of complex traffic accidents
  • Preventive and repressive monitoring of traffic flows and carrying out centre of gravity checks and penalties for specific traffic police offences
  • Reception of missions of the operation centre and reporting or reports to the investigating authorities
  • Accompaniment of exceptional transports, use in demonstrations and events, steering of special vehicles, traffic police escort of important personalities

Profile

  • Completed police training
  • Affinity for vehicles and technology
  • Road traffic law interest and understanding
  • Initiatives, independent work
  • Resilience and readiness for continuous training

We offer

  • Interesting, varied task with a high degree of independence, careful introduction, individual training, in an experienced, motivated team
  • A modern workplace in the immediate vicinity of Zurich Central Station
  • Attractive employment conditions such as targeted education and training, purchase of additional holidays, etc.
  • Assumption of 60% of the PK savings contributions, mobility contribution of up to CHF 600 per year and a maximum of CHF 100 per month in the form of a catering fee (depending on the workload)
  • Cheap mobile subscriptions (private use included) as well as other interesting Fringe Benefits

About us

The City Police is a service department of the Security Department of the City of Zurich and, with around 2,300 employees, the third largest police corps in Switzerland.
