Canton of Zurich Education Directorate EB Zurich ICT administrator/in server infrastructure, initially limited to 2 years 80 - 100% The EB Zurich, Cantonal School for Vocational Education and Training, is an innovative service provider for vocational education and training. It is designed for the Canton of Zurich, companies and organizations educational offers with the aim of preparing working people for the changing demands of the labour market. As of 1 November 2026 or by agreement, we are looking for a dedicated computer scientist for our computer science services. The IT services operate and develop the EB Zurich ICT infrastructure for administration and education. The office combines classic server and system technology with modern cloud, identity and endpoint management tasks. Their tasks

  • They operate and develop servers and platform services further, in particular Windows Server, Microsoft 365
  • You plan and manage the deployment, software and device management with Microsoft Configuration Manager
  • They maintain the directory services (active directory and group policies and maintain the mail services)
  • You are responsible for maintaining the system documentation and the support website
  • They train employees for application supplements or new entries
  • They support education in the development of ICT concepts
  • You are responsible for the entire system technology (including M365, Windows Server, setup and maintenance Identity Management, maintenance IT services and server services)
  • You are involved in the first-level support your profile
  • You have a Bachelor in Computer Science, an EFZ in Systems Engineering or a comparable education and at least 3 years of professional experience in platform development, systems engineering or a similar ICT infrastructure
  • You are familiar with Identity and Access Management, Data Analytics, O365 and Azure Cloud, Datawarehouse, Quality and Interface Management
  • You work independently, but you are also a valuable team player by thinking along and bringing in new ideas.
  • You have a high willingness to learn and constantly adapt your knowledge to current developments
