Software Engineer

APG|SGA is a dynamic service company with a Swiss tradition and focus on Out of Home Media: Digital. Analogue. Mobile. Interactive. We want to inspire people with the best communication solutions in public spaces. On streets and squares, in train stations, airports, shopping centers, in the mountains and in and on means of transport.

  • 80-100%
  • Link Zurich, Giesshübelstrasse 4
  • Permanent employment

Your tasks

  • You are part of our Scrum team (according to SAFe) and participate in the development, maintenance and integration of applications based on Java, Spring, Spring Boot and Vaadin
  • You are responsible for the sustainable conception, technical implementation and operation of our UI framework for our own ERP core system with approximately 400 daily users
  • Ensure quality by performing mutual code reviews and test automation
  • You support the further development of our development process and the implementation of production deployments (Configuration Management, CI & CD) based on Maven, Git, Jenkins and Gradle
  • You will work with architects, developers and DevOps from different Scrum teams to design and implement the upcoming, important evolutionary step of the ERP core system.

You bring with you

  • Completion of university or technical school or equivalent education
  • Many years of experience in the development, integration and operation of software in complex business environments (Java, Spring, Git and Maven)
  • Sound SQL and database knowledge (ideally Oracle)
  • Experience in agile software development (SAFe, Scrum, Kanban, etc.)
  • Experience in the use of AI tools for acceleration or Coding support
  • Ideally you bring experience with Jenkins and Gradle for CI/CD
  • Knowledge of Vaadin and Web technologies, such as HTML, CSS, WebComponents, Angular is advantageous
  • Experience in using Jira and Confluence
  • Openness to new things and an innovative mindset set you apart

This is what awaits you

  • Flexible working hours and at least five weeks of holidays as well as the possibility of two additional weeks of holiday purchase
  • Very good social benefits, it is provided for you
  • Free tickets to various events
