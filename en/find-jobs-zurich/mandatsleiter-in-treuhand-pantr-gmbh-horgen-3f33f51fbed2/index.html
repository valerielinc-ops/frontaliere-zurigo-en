Full description

For our client, we are looking for a strong professional personality as mandate manager: in Treuhand. In this central role, you take over the professional management of mandates and support the management of a mandate portfolio in close cooperation with internal contact persons. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for you!

This is your job

  • Mandate management: Professional management and processing of trust mandates for SMEs, start-ups and individual companies.
  • Finance: management of financial and payroll accounts and preparation of interim and annual accounts.
  • Tax & VAT: Preparation of tax returns for natural and legal persons as well as the preparation of VAT billing and consulting.
  • Consulting & Support: Professional support for business, financial and tax issues.
  • Interface: coordination and cooperation with internal contacts, authorities, auditors and external partners.
  • Innovation: Active participation in the further development of internal processes and digitalization.

This is what you bring with you

  • Training: Completed basic commercial training with further training in the escrow sector (Trustee: in a federal certificate or in training beneficial).
  • Experience: Several years of professional experience in trust and absolutely certain in the preparation of interim and annual accounts according to OR.
  • Expertise: Very good knowledge of accounting, tax law and VAT as well as entrepreneurial thinking.
  • IT skills: Secure handling of common escrow and accounting software – especially Abacus – and MS Office.
  • Working method & language: Independent, structured and reliable working method with high quality standards, pronounced numerical affinity and stylish knowledge of German (other languages are advantageous).

This is what awaits you from the customer

  • Responsibility: A versatile activity with a high degree of independence and a direct mandate.
  • Culture: A collegial, appreciative working environment with short decision-making paths and attractive employment conditions.
  • Future-oriented: Modern working conditions, digitalized processes and a contemporary infrastructure.
  • Development: Professional and personal development opportunities.
