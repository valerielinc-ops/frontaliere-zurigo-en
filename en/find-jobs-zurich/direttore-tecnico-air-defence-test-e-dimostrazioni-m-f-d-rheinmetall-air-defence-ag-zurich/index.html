Full description

What we are looking for you for

Do you have a military background and would like to use your experience in an international high-tech environment in the future? In this key role, you will take on the technical management of demanding testing and demonstration campaigns of state-of-the-art air defense systems with direct customer contact in an international environment.

Tasks

  • Planning, organizing and carrying out tests and demonstrations at the overall system level
  • Development of field testing and demonstration concepts
  • Coordination of all internal and external parties involved and ensuring a smooth process
  • Organization of material, infrastructure and service provision by third-party companies
  • Taking over organizational management including safety responsibility for tests and demonstrations
  • Preparation of operational reports and execution of after action reviews for continuous optimization
  • Technical support for sales through presentations, customer discussions and training
  • Effort determination & cost estimation as well as risk and scheduling planning for testing and demonstration campaigns

Requirements

  • Military training is required
  • Officer career would be an advantage
  • Military knowledge of anti-aircraft defense is required
  • Completed technical apprenticeship with comprehensive further training or technical studies
  • Several years of experience in project management as well as in-depth knowledge of planning complex projects
  • High technical affinity, especially in the areas of electrical engineering and mechanical engineering
  • Very good knowledge of MS Office; SAP knowledge is an advantage
  • Strong leadership experience and organizational skills as well as high resilience and endurance
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented way of working with a high level of customer orientation; intercultural knowledge and skills
  • Very good knowledge of spoken and written German and English as well as didactic skills

We offer

  • A dynamic working environment with exciting projects
  • Flexible working hours (flexitime and trust working hours)
  • Very good social benefits and a non-mandatory pension fund
  • Employee benefits of a large corporation, employee share purchase program
  • Promoting needs-based further training
  • Staff restaurant as well as external catering and shopping options nearby
  • Individual internal and external personnel development measures in collaboration with our Rheinmetall Academy
  • Workplace a few minutes' walk from Zurich-Oerlikon train station

Contact
