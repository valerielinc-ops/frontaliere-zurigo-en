Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School Zürcher Oberland Secondary School Teachers mbA for German 180 - 240% The Cantonal School Zürcher Oberland (KZO) in Wetzikon is a lively school that values an open culture, shared commitment and an inspiring learning environment. In currently 60 classes, she leads a high school secondary level (9th/10th school year) and all Zurich maturity profiles. With around 1300 students, 200 teachers and 40 employees in administration and operations, the KZO is one of the major high schools in the canton. At the beginning of the autumn semester 2027/28 we are looking for secondary school teachers mbA for German (total about 180-240%). According to the Zurich secondary and vocational school teachers ordinance, these are permanent jobs with special tasks (mbA). What we expect:

  • They have a university master's degree in the relevant subject and the teaching diploma for maturity schools or an equivalent diploma for teaching at high school maturity schools according to MAR.
  • They have teaching experience at the high school level and bring solid practical teaching skills.
  • You are motivated to engage in our school as a middle school teacher with special tasks. Among other things, this means: taking over the class teacher office, supervising mature work, participating in commissions, conceptual further development of the subject of German within the framework of WegZH. More information about our school can be found at www.kzo.ch. For further information please refer to Mr. Thomas Stecher, Adjunkt, telephone number 044 933 08 16. We look forward to your online application until August 20, 2026. Please submit your documents exclusively via the online tool.

Pensum: 180-240 %
