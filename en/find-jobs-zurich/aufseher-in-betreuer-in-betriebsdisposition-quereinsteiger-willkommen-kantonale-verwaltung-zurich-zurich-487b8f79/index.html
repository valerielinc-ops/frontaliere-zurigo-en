Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Correctional Services and Reintegration Supervisor / Supervisor - Career Change Welcome 80 - 100% With around 1,400 employees, Corrections and Reintegration (JuWe) is the largest office within the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs and is responsible for the execution of sentences and measures as well as for the implementation of various types of detention. Our employees from around 40 professions support and promote the reintegration of people who have committed crimes and work effectively and progressively to ensure a safe and fair society. The Zurich remand prisons (UGZ) are one of eight main departments of JuWe. They include seven companies with around 735 prison places and around 500 employees. They are responsible for provisional arrest, pre-trial detention and preventive detention as well as early execution of sentences and measures until transfer to a correctional facility. From the very beginning, they have fulfilled a central function within the Zurich justice system. As part of the UGZ, the Zurich prison specializes in pre-trial detention for adult men and has 110 places. As a guard/carer, you accompany prisoners without prejudice during this difficult phase and help ensure that everyday prison life is safe and orderly. If you are characterized by loyalty, respect, consistency and teamwork, you will find your place with us in Zurich Prison. This is how you shape everyday prison life

  • Care and support for prisoners: You ensure an orderly and safe everyday life and provide advice and guidance to the prisoners
  • Ensure safety: You keep an eye on the security devices and cameras and act calmly, deliberately and prudently in special situations
  • Coordination of processes: You organize movements of prisoners and ensure that operations run smoothly
  • Administrative tasks: You document events, create reports and keep all processes transparent and comprehensible
  • Design and further development: You contribute ideas, show commitment and support the continuous improvement of our company. This is what you bring with you
  • Minimum age 25 years and an impeccable reputation (no entries in the criminal or debt enforcement register)
  • Completed training: a federal certificate of proficiency, a (technical) baccalaureate or a comparable qualification
  • Good knowledge of spoken and written German, additional language skills would be an advantage
  • Confident use of MS Office
  • Flexibility for shifts, weekends, nights and special shifts
  • High resilience and resilience, keeping a cool head even in stressful situations You will benefit from this
  • The Zurich prison is centrally located next to Helvetiaplatz, so you have great connections to public transport
