Together we shape the investment solutions of the future! In product management, we develop and implement investment solutions tailored to the needs of our customers. The Projects & Engineering Plants team is involved in the further development of our investment solutions and, as an interface to IT, ensures the effective implementation of the change projects. As a business engineer, you make an important contribution to consolidating our bank’s strong market position in the investment business – especially with regard to the further development of digitalization. Are you looking for an exciting and broad-based job and would like to use your skills in business analysis and take the opportunity to contribute your ideas and bring them to life with motivated colleagues?

  • Developing GUI prototypes taking into account customer journey and user experience approaches
  • Actively accompany your change requirements from creation to introduction - you are a guarantor for the end-to-end implementation and carry out the necessary tests and training measures
  • Interface function in the organization between specialist departments (e.g. product management, sales, legal department, interaction designer) as well as IT with ensuring overarching coordination and communication

  • Strong conceptual and analytical skills as well as methodological competencies, e.g. GUI prototyping, BPMN, UX design, user story writing, use case modeling, etc.
  • Knowledge of agile project implementation (SAFe, SCRUM, Kanban) is an advantage
  • High personal responsibility, flexibility and entrepreneurial thinking and action
  • Pronounced service orientation, creator qualities, willingness to learn and teamwork Winning and secure appearance and eloquent communication in German
  • A challenging and varied job in an innovative and future-oriented company

  • Opportunities to develop yourself personally and professionally and a task area that gives you the chance to take your topics forward on your own responsibility
