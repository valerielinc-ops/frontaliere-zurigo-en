Full description

Tasks

  • Clinical-psychological investigations and psychotherapeutic treatments in outpatients in the area of neuropsychosomatics with adult patients
  • Evaluation and psychological care of inpatients, mostly with epilepsy or dissociative seizures
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation within the clinic
  • Case documentation, reporting and case-related exchange with referees, insurance companies and other specialist bodies
  • Working with young people and young adults
  • Psychotherapeutic treatment of patients with cognitive impairment or supervision for their environment

Requirements

  • Completed psychology studies and completed or advanced psychotherapy training
  • Professional experience with psychosomatic/somatic patients
  • Ability to work independently, high social competence, resilience and teamwork

The Klinik Lengg AG in Zurich is the competence center for epileptology and neurological rehabilitation.

Our goal is to enable patients with neurological diseases to be as well-rehabilitated as possible and to ensure accurate diagnosis and sustainable treatment of patients with epilepsy. Around 300 employees are committed to human, competent, innovative and reliable efforts to ensure that our patients regain the greatest possible independence - and thus their quality of life. Become part of our interdisciplinary and interprof-unciational teams.
