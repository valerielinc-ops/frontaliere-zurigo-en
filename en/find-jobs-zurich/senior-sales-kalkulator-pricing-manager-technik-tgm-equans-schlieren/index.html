Full description

Shape the future of facility management with us As a (Senior) Sales Calculator / Pricing Manager Technik, you will continue to develop the Facility & Property Management division of Equans Switzerland on a national level. You are the central contact person for technical tenders and, together with our specialist areas, develop tailor-made and economical solutions for our customers throughout Switzerland.

You can move that with us.

  • You assume overall responsibility for the professional management of complex tendering and tendering processes from request to contract signing.
  • You analyze tender documents and coordinate the entire tender process as a central interface between sales, operations and specialists: inside.
  • You will develop convincing offer and pricing strategies for technical facility management services.
  • You carry out total and partial calculations in the area of technical building management (TGM), especially for maintenance, operation and maintenance services.
  • You create professional customer and offer presentations and support customer negotiations.
  • You review contracts and coordinate the relevant framework conditions with internal and external stakeholders.
  • You ensure the quality as well as the timely submission of all offer documents.
  • You will continuously develop processes, templates and digital tools and use modern AI solutions.

That's what makes you.

  • Completed studies in Facility Management, Building Technology, Engineering, Real Estate or a comparable field of study.
  • Several years of professional experience in technical facility management or in the calculation of technical services.
  • In-depth knowledge of technical building management (TGM), especially in the areas of maintenance, operation and maintenance.
  • Experience in creating complex calculations and economic analysis.
  • Good knowledge of business contexts and a strong understanding of customer needs.
  • Very good MS Office skills, especially in Excel and PowerPoint.
  • Experience with CRM, CAFM and AI tools is beneficial.
  • Confident knowledge of German and good knowledge of English. Knowledge of French is a plus.

That's what we offer you.

  • Varied tasks and the opportunity to contribute and implement your ideas
  • Diverse and networked areas of activity – Facility and Property Management from A – Z is not just a phrase for us
  • Career opportunities – we enable company, location, trade or transnational change
  • We support our employees and participate in training and further education
  • Working in a future-oriented industry where you can constantly learn and develop
  • Flexible working models and modern working environment
  • Market remuneration

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
