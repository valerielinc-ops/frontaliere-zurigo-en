For our specialized bed station in hepatology, gastroenterology and endocrinology (South E) we are looking for dedicated care professionals with special tasks in education. Are you a qualified nurse with a strong interest in education? Do you want to remain active in direct care and at the same time take on targeted educational responsibility? Then this function in the USZ offers you the ideal introduction to educational work!

This is what awaits you

  • Trainees (FaGe, HF, FH) are accompanied in a targeted way in everyday life, give them feedback and support their integration into the team.
  • At the center of complex clinical situations, you are actively involved in direct care.
  • You plan training moments in close consultation with vocational trainers: inside and thus create a learning-promoting environment with
  • Together with the team, you will develop a practical learning and working community (LAG) – future-oriented and effective
  • The training is based on the Cognitive Apprenticeship Model (CAS), which you carry with you through your participation in learning discussions, reflections or exam situations
  • Your further development towards vocational training is actively supported – for example through method training, internships or SVEB courses of the education team

You bring that with you

  • Diploma as a nurse HF/FH with professional experience in acute care
  • First experience or high interest in accompanying learners
  • Enjoy explaining, guiding and reflecting – with a clear eye for individual development processes
  • Readiness for personal and professional development in the field of education
  • Team spirit, initiative and pedagogical sense

Your advantages with us

  • A multifaceted role with a developmental character: education, care and cooperation at a high level
  • Individual promotion of your career in the field of practical training
  • Cooperation with a dedicated VET team
  • Internal training and targeted accompaniment on your way to the possible role of vocational trainer
  • A workplace with modern infrastructure in the middle of Zurich
