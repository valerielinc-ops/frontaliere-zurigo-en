Full description

As a team for communication and collaboration, we are responsible for the engineering and operation of the urban hybrid communication platform for over 30,000 users. With our services, we serve the entire city administration, which covers all areas of life (police, protection and rescue, hospitals, schools, sports facilities, retirement and nursing centers, social affairs, real estate, 0815 administration and much more) and thus move in a very exciting and dynamic environment. If you want to help us with these automation projects in Python, Django and PowerShell, then you are at the right place. Because to strengthen our team, we are looking from now on or by agreement.

Tasks

  • You support the replacement of an existing client management system.
  • You implement a web-based solution for routing numbers via API.
  • You consolidate different CDR data sources.
  • You are modernizing a module for managing manufacturer tickets.

Profile

  • Computer Science degree (min. last year Bachelor)
  • In-depth knowledge of Python as well as basic knowledge of software development and databases
  • Ready to learn and open to use other scripting languages
  • Motivated personality with stylish communication in German

We offer

  • You can make your working hours flexible, depending on the requirements of your study plan or exam phases.
  • If you want to write a bachelor's or master's thesis in our area, we will find a topic together with us.
  • Your salary depends on the current level of training and the level of requirements of the university internship (for a 100% fee for Bachelor: CHF CHF 2'115.- to 3'172.- / Master: CHF CHF 3'172.- to 4'228.-).
