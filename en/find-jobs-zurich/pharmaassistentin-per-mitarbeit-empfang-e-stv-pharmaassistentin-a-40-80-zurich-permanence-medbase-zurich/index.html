About us

Welcome to Medbase Zürich HP Permanence

In our Medical Center in the middle of Zurich, directly in the main station, we care for patients 365 days a year without prior registration. Our team, consisting of almost 60 employees, consists of specialists in general internal medicine, laboratory staff and MPAs and covers the clinical, diagnostic and therapeutic spectrum of general and internal medicine. About the Medbase Group

#WeAreMedbase

The Medbase Group is one of the leading companies in outpatient healthcare. This means services from health promotion to medical care and acute treatment to rehabilitation.
