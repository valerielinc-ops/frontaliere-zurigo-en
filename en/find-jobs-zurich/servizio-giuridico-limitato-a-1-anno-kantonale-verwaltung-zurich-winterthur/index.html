Original description

Canton of Zurich Economic Directorate Office for Labour Lawyer for 1 year 80 - 100% work changes lives - and that is exactly what drives us. Every day, the Office of Labour (AFA) of the Canton of Zurich works to ensure that people regain their jobs, that companies find the right talents and that the labour market in the Canton of Zurich remains strong. With around 900 employees at various locations, we bundle labour market skills and are close to the people, the companies and the realities of working life. You don’t work anywhere with us. You work where your commitment really counts. How do we combine service and efficiency in everyday life? We ensure that insurance benefits from the cantonal unemployment fund are paid out correctly and on time. We are there when it matters: we check claims, calculate unemployment insurance benefits and pay them out. Reliable, competent and with real understanding for the people behind the dossiers. For the Legal Service & Complaints department at the Unemployment Fund Canton of Zurich, headquartered in Winterthur, we are looking for a lawyer immediately or by agreement. Your tasks:

  • Independent drafting of objection decisions and complaints on the occasion of proceedings before the Social Security and Federal Court
  • Making the necessary legal clarifications
  • Representation of the ALK in labour law proceedings before court (editing of legal documents and personal representation)
  • Advising customers, partners and internal and external bodies
  • Maintaining contact with the SECO supervisory authority (e.g. clarifications regarding AVIG implementation)
  • Collaboration in working groups and process revision Your profile:
  • Law degree (minimum level Bachelor)
  • Legal professional or judicial experience, preferably in the field of social security and labour law
  • Structured, careful and reliable working methods
  • service-oriented and respectful treatment of all stakeholders We offer:
  • Flexible work opportunities at our modern and central locations in Winterthur (headquarters) and Zurich Oerlikon
  • Possibility to HomeOffice
  • versatile and demanding work with a high level of personal responsibility
  • Careful introduction time
  • Contribution to catering costs (lunch check)
  • Well-developed social benefits under cantonal personnel law Do you value a living environment with stable and fair framework conditions? Then we should definitely get to know each other – Marc Schneider, head of department, telephone 043 258 10 19, will be happy to provide you with further information. We look forward to your application. You can find out more about us and our employees at www.zh.ch/afa.

Pensum: 80-100 %
