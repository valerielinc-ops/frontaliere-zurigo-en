Full description

Our Institute for Emergency Medicine (INM) is one of the largest emergency centers in Switzerland at the two locations Triemli and Waid, treating more than 70,000 patients per year. We are therefore looking for a committed personality with enthusiasm for emergency medicine and a strong service mentality.

Tasks

  • In this versatile and exciting function you will learn – supported by the cadre doctors and the emergency care – the emergency medical diagnosis, treatment and care of patients at our interdisciplinary emergency departments at the locations Triemli and Waid.
  • You are happy to provide high-quality medical care and treatment for our emergency patients.
  • With the other internal specialist departments as well as the assigning medical profession, you maintain a close interdisciplinary exchange.
  • With personal commitment, you gain valuable experience on your way to the focus of clinical emergency medicine SGNOR.

Profile

  • Swiss Federal Medical Diploma or this foreign diploma in human medicine recognised by MEBEKO
  • At least two years of clinical experience
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary emergency medicine in an exciting and challenging environment
  • High level of independence, resilience, assertiveness and strong social skills
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Willingness to operate at both locations (Triemli and Waid)

We offer

We offer you more than just a job! Our city hospital world is characterized by an open dual culture, an uncomplicated, personal togetherness, respect and appreciation. Together with the entire team of the institute, you are the visiting card of the city hospital and make a significant contribution to the hospital’s appeal to patients and potential new employees. In addition, you have the opportunity to gain experience at two emergency stations with different orientations during your training period at the Stadtspital Zürich. A competent, committed and collegial team will be happy to accompany you on your way to the emergency medical specialist.

About us

With over 36'000 inpatients and around 455,000 outpatients per year, the Stadtspital Zürich ranks among the top 10 Swiss hospitals. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,500 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
