Full description

For our bariatric consultation hours at the obesity center in Triemli and Europa-Allee, we are looking for a competent person to complement the obesity team immediately or by appointment.

Tasks

  • You work independently and interdisciplinary in the obesity team as part of the obesity consultation.
  • The main task is to supervise drug bariatric therapy and follow-up after bariatric surgery.
  • You are in close contact with your teammates and actively engage in in-clinic training.

Profile

  • Broad training in internal medicine, general medicine with specialist title for general internal medicine
  • Joy and commitment in the care of patients with obesity
  • Communicative, integrative personality with interest in further education functions

We offer

We offer you a very interesting, versatile and challenging area of work in a modern hospital with attractive employment conditions and excellent social benefits in the city of Zurich. You can look forward to regular working hours from Monday to Friday. In addition, we enable you to participate in interdisciplinary reports as well as various further education and training opportunities. We are a GESKES-recognized training institution for the interdisciplinary focus on nutritional medicine.

About us

With over 36'000 inpatients and around 455,000 outpatients per year, the Stadtspital Zürich ranks among the top 10 Swiss hospitals. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,500 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
