Full description

For the new overall urban organizational unit of school social work since August 2023, which is part of the department of child and youth welfare of the social services, we are looking for the provision of the services of school social work in the school district Schwamendingen, in the school house Saatlen on 1st December. December 2026 or by arrangement a new team member.

Tasks

  • They advise and accompany children and parents from different cultures.
  • They support the school management and the school staff of the two school buildings with social issues.
  • They develop targeted measures and solutions on topics such as child protection, bullying, violence and addiction.
  • They initiate internal and cross-school projects.
  • They ensure the flow of information between school, social centre and private institutions.
  • In Saatlen, school social work has been established for years.

Profile

  • You bring a university degree in social work. A CAS in school social work is beneficial
  • You ideally have experience in school social work or in the field of youth and family help
  • You enjoy working interdisciplinary, networked and resource-oriented
  • You are an open, sociable and independent personality and are confident in dealing with changes
  • Saatlen becomes one of the largest educational centers in the city of Zurich and is currently under reconstruction. They are interested in accompanying the children and young people as well as the school team in this development and in shaping it in the area of the SSA.

We offer

An interesting and varied work with independent processing of your topics and tasks. In our organizational unit with about 90 school social workers, we maintain open and transparent communication. An attractive training offer as well as internal specialist positions to support in day-to-day business. For more information click here https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/en/bildung/volksschule/support-foerderung/schulsozialarbeit.html

About us

With around 2,400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a social Zurich. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.
