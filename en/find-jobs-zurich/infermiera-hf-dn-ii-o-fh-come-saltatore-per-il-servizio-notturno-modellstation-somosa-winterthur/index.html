Full description

SOMOSA offers an inpatient 6- to 12-month intensive treatment program for 14- to 18-year-old clients who are in a severe adolescent crisis. The aim of the treatment is to stabilize the clients in their personality to such an extent that they can survive on leaving in the existing supporting or replacing socialization systems.

As a recognized Listespital in the field of child and adolescent psychiatry and as a youth home with a socio-educational mission, we fulfill both provisions through a long-standing, intersystemic cooperation in the fields of social pedagogy, care, psychotherapy and work pedagogy.

We are looking for a dipl. Nurse (HF, DN II or FH) as a jumper:in for night service

By agreement

Your responsibilities

  • Together with your colleagues from the Night Watches, you provide the night rest for our clients in our residential groups. The two residential groups of male clients with 10 rooms each are merged at night, the residential group of female clients with 10 and 4 rooms is located in another building, 5 minutes walk from the main house.
  • Thanks to your professional qualifications and experience, you will be able to convey safety and competently assess delicate situations.
  • If necessary, you act safely and solution-oriented, target the background service in-house or external support.
  • If everything is quiet, you do along with the regular runs and security checks, medication checks and other tasks assigned by the day service.
  • If young people work externally, wake them in the morning and take care of them as far as is necessary.
  • The contact with the treatment team of the day services is maintained by the daily morning and evening reports.

To do so, bring them with you.

  • Nurse HF, DN II or FH with a focus on psychiatry
  • Experience in adolescent psychiatric inpatient and/or dealing with antisocial young men and women
  • Desire and physical ability to work regularly at night
  • PC Knowledge: Word, Excel (pure application)
  • You are at least 28 years old
