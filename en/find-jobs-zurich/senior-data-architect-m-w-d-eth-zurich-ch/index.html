Senior Data Architect (m/f/d)

80%-100%, Zurich, permanent

Shape the data-driven and AI-enabled future of a world-leading university

To support research and teaching, the IT services of ETH Zurich operate as a service organization in a large and complex IT environment. We offer the members of ETH Zurich high-quality, flexible and scalable computer science services and support them in competition with the best universities in the world.

The services of the section ID Applications (APPS) include e-mail, groupware (collaborative software solutions) and the hosting of websites, databases and applications. The portfolio also includes the conception, implementation and further development of various systems (central operational information system, identity & access management, SAP ERP, web content management as well as solutions for teaching development/technology and departments) at ETH Zurich.

ETH Zurich is consistently driving the digital transformation of its data landscape: away from historically grown, punctual integrations – towards a modern, event-driven and governance-strong data platform that also enables the responsible use of artificial intelligence. As a Senior Data Architect, you take on a central, shaping role in this strategic realignment – conceptually as well as hands-on.

Job description

As a Senior Data Architect, you will play a key role in the design and development of our company-wide data landscape. With your strong architectural competence, you are responsible for the conceptual, logical and physical information and data architecture and use canonical data models to create the basis for consistent and sustainable data use across all relevant domains. They actively drive the strategic transformation of our integration architecture and develop it from classic point-to-point interfaces to a modern, event-driven and decentralized data platform according to the principles of event-driven architecture and data mesh.

Thanks to your experience, you will establish sustainable data governance structures and ensure that processes relating to metadata management, data quality, data classification and role and approval concepts are effectively anchored in the organization. At the same time, you create the architectural conditions for the responsible use of business intelligence, analytics and artificial intelligence and enable the development of innovative data products up to machine learning applications.

As an experienced architect, you will design modern persistence and data platform strategies, develop solutions for relational, document-based, graph and vector databases and accompany the development of powerful data lake and lakehouse architectures. You support project teams, ERP managers and specialist areas as a competent contact person and support them in the implementation of modern data pipelines and integration solutions with technologies such as ETL/ELT, Apache NiFi, Kafka or CDC.

In addition, you represent the data architecture on a strategic level in specialist committees, create the necessary architecture documentation and contribute your expertise in evaluations, procurements and tenders. With your foresight, you shape the data-driven further development of our organization sustainably and make a decisive contribution to the digital future.

Profile

  • Completed university studies in computer science, business informatics or comparable qualification as well as several years of experience as a senior data or solution architect.
  • In-depth knowledge of information and data modeling with a focus on relational databases (Oracle), including DDL, SQL and PL/SQL.
  • Experience with heterogeneous persistence architectures (graph, document/JSON, vector) and data warehouse/lakehouse concepts (e.g. spark, databricks or similar).
  • Practice in data pipeline engineering (ETL/ELT, NiFi, Informatica) and event-driven/kafka-based integration patterns (CDC, Schema Registry).
  • Experience in setting up and operating data governance processes and tools (catalogues, classification, metadata).
  • Interest and ideally first experience in the use of AI or generative AI for data insights, data quality or data exploration.
  • Strong communication and stakeholder competence as well as a structured, documentation-intensive working method.
  • Several years of experience in working with demanding stakeholders as well as with decision-makers at various management and hierarchical levels.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing.
