Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Occupation by: March 2027 / September 2027

Type of employment: Temporary

Reference number: 23608

Start your nursing career in an environment that combines quality, humanity and innovation. As a student nurse / student nurse at Klinik Hirslanden, you will have the opportunity to put your theoretical knowledge directly into practice. In a professional and appreciative team, you will be individually accompanied, promoted and gradually introduced to responsible tasks – always with the aim of ensuring excellent and patient-centered care.

YOUR TASKS

  • Responsible for the care and care of patients from hospital entry to exit
  • Control of the entire care process
  • The pivot for patients, relatives and the interdisciplinary team
  • Taking on helping, consulting, coordinative and analytical functions
  • Completion of the practical part at the Hirslanden clinic, the theoretical part at the education provider Careum Zürich or at the ZAG in Winterthur

Your profile

  • Completion of a three-year vocational training course with a federal certificate of competence, a DMS/FMS degree or a high school diploma
  • Empathy and a high level of social competence in dealing with patients and their relatives as well as in working with employees and doctors
  • Customer and service orientation, a strong sense of responsibility and self-employment
  • Willingness to develop your expertise and make an important contribution to the success of our clinic

Our offer

  • Accompanied by your vocational trainee and training manager, you will gain insight into various specialist areas of highly specialized medicine during your practical assignments.
  • You have the opportunity to be employed directly by Klinik Hirslanden for the duration of the training and benefit from our employment conditions as well as our employees.
  • Constant pay during the entire training period at attractive conditions
  • If you are interested in direct employment, we offer you an aptitude internship after the interview, this is part of the aptitude procedure for training HF Care
  • Duration of training: 3 years (abbreviated HF studies of 2 years possible at apprenticeship specialist / health)

Our benefits for employees

For additional information, Mentor Alija, Head of Vocational Education and Training, is available at T / mentor.alija1@hirslanden.ch. We look forward to your contact.
