Full description

Are you ready for a job where you can make a difference for people and nature? To strengthen our team, we are looking for 1. March 2027 or by agreement a dedicated and versatile forest warden foreman for the forest district Nord, which extends from Hönggerberg to Adlisberg.

Tasks

  • You create the weekly planning based on the semester plan and the weather for an efficient and smooth workflow
  • You take over the complete handling of the Christmas tree production
  • They ensure the maintenance of the recreational infrastructure, sports and bike facilities in the forest, nature conservation objects (meadows, Rieter, Weiher, etc.) and the company buildings
  • You take over the personnel and professional management of 3 employees, promote a solution-oriented cooperation in the team and conduct regular team meetings as well as location and assessment discussions
  • They are responsible for the administrative and technical management of the civilian service providers

Profile

  • Completed training as a forest manager (EFZ) and degree as a forest manager foreman (FA) or willingness to complete the forest manager foreman course (FA)
  • Flair for handling machines and equipment
  • Leadership experience as well as joy and willingness to accompany and guide civilian service providers
  • Driving licence category BE
  • Flexibility and independent and service-oriented working methods

We offer

  • A versatile and meaningful area of responsibility and activity
  • Option to buy up to 10 holiday days per year
  • A broad urban training offer and support for external training
  • Catering contributions
  • Above-average social benefits and attractive retirement provision

About us

Zurich greener. That's our vision. Because Grün City of Zurich maintains, designs and manages the public green areas. These make up about 40 percent of the city area. With their work, our 580 employees from over 20 nations contribute to the high quality of life in the city of Zurich. We believe in a diverse and inclusive workforce and are proud to be an equal opportunities employer.
