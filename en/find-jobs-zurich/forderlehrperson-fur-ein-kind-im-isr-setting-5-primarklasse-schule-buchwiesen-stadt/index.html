About us

We are a large school community in Zurich-Nord with 18 schools. We derive our pedagogical attitude from the concept of New Authority and thus meet the daily challenges in everyday school life together and effectively.

School time is life time! Under this motto, we design a living space inspiring for the children at the primary school Buchwiesen. Children's participation and participation are important pillars of our coexistence. With free and individualizing learning opportunities, we promote the natural learning pleasure of the children. As a QUIMS school for many years, we have great experience in our multicultural environment. We are a modern, innovative school with 16 primary classes, 6 kindergartens and 3 hoards.
