Type of employment: Permanent

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

YOUR TASKS

  • Ordering meals by electronic ordering system OrgaCard as well as according to medical guidelines including serving meals at the nursing department
  • Patient support services for entry and exit
  • Presentation of the patient rooms and the infrastructure of the Hirslanden clinic as well as care of the visitors
  • Responsible for cleanliness and order in the patient room and in the own area taking into account Hirslanden quality standards including flower care and laundry care
  • Ensuring the well-being of our demanding patients in close cooperation with the care, kitchen, reception and economy

Your profile

  • Experience in hospitality or gastronomy
  • Good communication skills and high social skills
  • Reliability and responsible work
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing, good knowledge of English
  • Other foreign languages are beneficial

What we offer

  • An attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich in an experienced, interdisciplinary team
  • Extensive training and development opportunities
  • Modern working atmosphere and well-founded familiarization
  • Flexible working hours incl. «Leisure Plus» with up to 7 weeks holidays
  • Compensation for changing time with four extra days off at 100% expense
  • Participation in travel costs (ZVV/Z-Pass, SBB coupons)

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. You work with a modern medical clinic information system and can expect extensive training and development opportunities from us. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family responsibilities. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. Employees who have to move completely for their work receive four additional paid days off with a 100% workload. With a reduced workload, the days off reduce accordingly. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from an equivalent financial compensation. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.
