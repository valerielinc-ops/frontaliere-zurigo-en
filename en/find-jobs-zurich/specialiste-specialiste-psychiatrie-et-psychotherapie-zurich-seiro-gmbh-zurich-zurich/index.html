Full description

Outpatient and daily clinical psychiatry in Zurich, where psychiatric-psychosomatic treatment does not mean assembly line, but real relationship work. In Zurich, we are looking for a specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy in senior medical capacity in the range of 80-100% for a specialized outpatient and day-to-day clinical institution. She expects an interdisciplinary environment with the entire spectrum of psychiatric-psychosomatic diseases. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • They take over the psychiatric assessment and treatment of adult patients with the entire spectrum of mental disorders in the outpatient and daily clinical setting
  • They independently bear senior medical case responsibility from diagnosis to therapy planning to implementation
  • They carry out outpatient crisis interventions
  • They work closely interdisciplinary with the therapeutic team
  • They undertake supervision activities and promote professional exchange
  • They participate in internal training events

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist title for psychiatry and psychotherapy or a diploma recognised in Switzerland
  • Experience in psychiatric and psychosomatic medicine
  • Ideally first leadership experience as well as enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation

That sets them apart.

  • Patient-centered, trusting basic attitude in psychiatric-psychotherapeutic care
  • A sense of responsibility and enjoyment of independent, senior medical case management
  • Communication strength and commitment in an interdisciplinary team

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Responsible medical practice in a specialized outpatient and day-care institution
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with professional exchange and supervision
  • Broad treatment spectrum of psychiatric-psychosomatic pathologies
  • Attractive employment conditions and opportunities for professional and personal development
  • Central, easily accessible location in the city of Zurich

Additionally for applicants from the EU

  • Support for MEBEKO recognition of your diploma
  • Assistance in organizing your journey to Switzerland
  • Assistance in finding accommodation through our internal rental department
