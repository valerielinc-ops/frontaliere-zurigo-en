Gesundheitsnetz Küsnacht AG (GNK) with the Tägerhalde and Wangensbach retirement and health centres with around 130 residents as well as a day care centre, Spitex, housing for the elderly and counselling with a total of around 250 employees is a non-profit public limited company wholly owned by the municipality of Küsnacht.

In this realigned function, you will have the unique opportunity to help build up and sustainably shape the learning and working community (LAG) at Spitex. As a rule, the working hours are from Monday to Friday with a maximum of 6 weekend assignments per year. In the case of temporarily unfilled training positions, the assignment in direct nursing takes place according to the working conditions of the respective area of assignment.

Your new tasks

  • Establishment and further development of the Learning and Working Group (LAG)
  • Independent planning, implementation, documentation and evaluation of learning processes and learning days
  • Professional and individual care and support for our customers and residents
  • Active participation in long-term care during the absence of learners and students (holidays, school days, unfilled training places)
  • Degree as a graduate nurse HF/FH
  • Two years of professional experience, ideally in Spitex and/or long-term care
  • Interest in vocational education and training and the support of learning and development processes
  • Vocational pedagogical training or the willingness to complete it
  • Independent, initiative and responsible way of working
  • Very good written and spoken German skills as well as good IT user skills
