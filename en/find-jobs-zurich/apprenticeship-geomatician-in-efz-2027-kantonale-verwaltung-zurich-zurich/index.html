Role overview

The GIS browser of the Canton of Zurich is a real treasure trove.

Whether it’s maps, population statistics, bicycle infrastructure, flooding or wildlife corridors, you can find out more with the GIS browser.

As a geomatician with a focus on "geoinformatics", you will be trained as a specialist for the acquisition, modeling, tracking, analysis and presentation of spatial information.

  • The GIS browser of the Canton of Zurich is a real treasure trove.
  • Whether it’s maps, population statistics, bicycle infrastructure, flooding or wildlife corridors, you can find out more with the GIS browser.

Application process

  • With state-of-the-art measurement systems, data such as the position or height of an object is recorded and evaluated, managed and analyzed with the help of geoinformation systems and special applications or maps with thematic orientation are created.
  • These data serve as the basis for a wide range of information and planning tasks as well as decisions in technology, business, environment and politics.
  • Geoinformatics is varied and demanding – it is a beautiful but demanding activity.
  • You are currently attending secondary school A and your favorite subjects include mathematics and geometry.
  • You have a preference for technology and working on the computer gives you pleasure.
  • You should also bring good spatial imagination, logical thinking and a quick understanding.
  • You like to work focused and carefully over a longer period of time.
  • You master the German language in word and writing.
