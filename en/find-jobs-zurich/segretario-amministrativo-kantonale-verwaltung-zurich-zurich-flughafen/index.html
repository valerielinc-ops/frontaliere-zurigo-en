Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Implementation and Reintegration Implementation Institutions Canton of Zurich Administrative Secretary/in 80

100% Implementation and Reintegration (JuWe) is responsible as part of the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs of the Canton of Zurich for the execution of penalties and measures as well as the implementation of the various types of detention.

For our 1,300 employees from around 40 professions, the successful social reintegration of people who have committed crimes and the prevention of recidivism are of particular importance.
