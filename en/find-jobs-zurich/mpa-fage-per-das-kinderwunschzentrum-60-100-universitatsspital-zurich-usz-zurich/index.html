At the clinic for reproductive endocrinology, we offer a wide range of services for people with hormonal imbalance and unfulfilled desire to have children. We work with a state-of-the-art and process-oriented infrastructure in an extremely dynamic and innovative environment.

Are you burning to shape the trend of outpatient medicine?

  • You receive and accompany patients during treatment times and assist with various examinations and surgical procedures. Medical engineering tasks are just as much a part of your tasks as a seamless billing of services and the taking over of administrative work. In the accompaniment of our MPA learners, you take on partial tasks.
  • As a medical practice assistant / medical practice assistant, specialist health / health care specialist, nursing specialist / midwife you have already gained experience in an acute hospital or in a larger practice.
  • You have a high customer orientation, enjoy working in the interprofessional team and bring your own initiative. Stylistic German is a prerequisite, every other foreign language is an advantage.
  • We attach great importance to a careful introduction to the field. In addition, you have a broad, internal training offer available.
  • We are an open and motivated team and look forward to an interested colleague. Our working hours are between Monday and Friday from 07:30 to 16:30.
