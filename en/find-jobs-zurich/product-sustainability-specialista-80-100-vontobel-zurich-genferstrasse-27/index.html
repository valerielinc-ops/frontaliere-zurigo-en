Full description

Are you passionate about shaping the future of sustainable investing? Do you enjoy translating complex sustainability requirements into practical investment and product solutions?

Join our Global Products & Solutions team in Zurich and help drive the evolution of sustainable investment capabilities across a diverse range of asset classes and business areas

What would your role be?

  • Drive the development and enhancement of sustainable investment products and solutions.
  • Advise product and investment teams on sustainability integration across asset classes and investment styles.
  • Translate regulatory requirements into practical product and governance frameworks, structures and documents.
  • Establish and maintain sustainability standards, methodologies, data principles, and governance frameworks, ensuring the effective management and use of sustainability-related data.
  • Lead and support sustainability-related projects from concept through implementation and execution.

What are we looking for?

  • Strong product expertise in sustainable investing and sustainability-related topics.
  • Deep understanding of SFDR, MiFID II, EU Taxonomy, AMAS Self-Regulation and further Sustainability related regulations is a plus.
  • Analytical and data-oriented mindset with the ability to structure complex topics effectively.
  • Collaborative personality with excellent stakeholder management skills.
  • Strong communication skills and the ability to influence across functions.
  • University degree in finance, economics, sustainability or a related discipline.
  • Affinity for Artificial Intelligence (AI) and a willingness to embrace AI-enabled tools and ways of working.

What do we offer?

  • Strategic role at the interface of sustainable investing and product development.
  • Exposure to investment, product and ESG experts and senior stakeholders across the organization.
  • Diverse and international working environment with broad collaboration opportunities.
  • Modern office with excellent facilities and collaborative workspaces.
  • Opportunity to shape the future direction of sustainable investment solutions.

Why Vontobel?

At Vontobel, you are building better futures for our clients and society, as well as for yourself. As a family-owned and publicly listed company with over 2,000 professionals based in 28 locations globally and headquartered in Zurich, we’re committed to long-term thinking and sustainability.
