Full description

The school Focus See seeks due to a longer-term staff loss immediately or by arrangement a reliable personality an evening detacher. The employment is temporary.

Tasks

  • They independently carry out cleaning work in the gym, the sanitary and shower facilities and other assigned areas.
  • They open and close the school complex and control doors, windows, stores and premises.
  • They look after the associations during the use hours and are the contact person on site.
  • They control the cleanliness as well as the condition of equipment and infrastructure and carry out simple home service and repair work.
  • You support during the school holidays with basic cleaning work and take on further tasks after consultation with the head of home service and technology.

Profile

  • Experience in domestic service and cleaning
  • Experience in dealing with cleaning machines, in particular scrubber suction and disc machines – an advantage
  • Craftsmanship and good physical condition
  • Self-employed, reliable and responsible work
  • Good oral German skills and willingness to work flexible hours

We offer

  • A varied, versatile and independent activity as well as a salary according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich.

Your working hours

  • Fixed use: Monday morning: 08:00 – 12:00
  • Flexible bets: Monday to Friday evening (seasonal and club-dependent), Monday to Thursday: about 17:00 – 23:00, Friday: about 15:30 – 23:00

Good to know: The exact periods of use depend on the occupation of the school facility by the clubs as well as the operational requirements. Since the club is busier in winter than in summer, your hours vary seasonally.

About us

The School Focus See (SFS) is a recognized special school. The offer includes the special day school and the integrated special training. Around 30 pupils from kindergarten until the end of the compulsory school period attend the day school. In the integrated special training, more than 160 students with visual impairment from the cantons of Zurich and Schaffhausen are supported in standard classes at their place of residence.
