Full description

You can contribute and shape the future of customer advice and the digitalization of banking with us. We're looking for: A forward-thinking person. Someone with whom we can work together as a team to create the future with fresh ideas, innovations, and passion. Come with us.

Tasks

  • Result-oriented first/short advice for private customers in the core areas of account/numbers/savings, pension account, investments, and financing
  • Identifying, treating, and mediating customer potential in all core areas (Cross-Selling/appointment arrangements)
  • Active customer approach in the context of market management (e.g. sales opportunities from campaigns)
  • Various assistance tasks and general administration

Requirements

  • min. 1-2 years: private banking advice
  • Completed vocational training (EFZ): Bank KV
  • First practical experience in sales-oriented advice
  • Specialist knowledge in the areas of account, numbers, savings, cash obligations, and personal loans
  • Basic knowledge in the area of pension, mortgages, investments
  • You can ask structured and connected questions in the advice
  • German (negotiation-secure)

What we offer

  • Central workplace: optimally accessible, centrally located workplace
  • Modern infrastructure: future-oriented and ergonomic workplace
  • Healthy work: sabbaticals as regeneration time after 7 years of employment
  • Professional pension: very attractive pension scheme with above-average employer contribution
  • Cumulus points: up to 5 additional points per franc purchase value
  • Employee offers: annual generous well-being credit
  • Social benefits: optimal coverage in case of illness and accident "private"
  • Fair wages: strong overall package of salary, social security, and pension
  • You-culture: lived team spirit with transparent communication
  • Flat hierarchies: permeable hierarchies and inter-departmental exchange
  • Individual training: attractive contributions for external training
  • Targeted promotion: continuous, individual exchange for professional promotion
