The USZ Personnel Medical Service serves our employees as the central point of contact for occupational health care – directly at the workplace. From admission and exit examinations to vaccination and medical advice to support in the event of psychosocial stresses or accidents: We ensure that health and well-being are not neglected in everyday work. The USZ needs everyday heroes like you!

Are you burning to shape the trend of outpatient medicine?

  • You receive and accompany patients / customers (exclusively employees of the USZ) during the consultation hours and perform various medical technical tasks. You make consultations (especially vaccination consultations) and do administrative work such as performance monitoring, appointments, complaint management, etc.
  • As a medical practice assistant / medical practice assistant or specialist health / health specialist you have already gained experience in an acute hospital or in a larger practice.
  • You have a high customer orientation, enjoy working in a team and bring your own initiative. Stylistic German is a prerequisite, every other foreign language is an advantage.
  • We attach great importance to a careful introduction to the field. In addition, you have a broad, internal training offer at your disposal.
  • We are an open, warm, 3-person team and support ourselves in everyday work. Our working hours are between Monday and Friday during the day.
