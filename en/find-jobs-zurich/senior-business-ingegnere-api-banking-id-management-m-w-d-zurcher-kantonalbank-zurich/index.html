Our Client Channels department designs and develops the physical and digital touchpoints of the Zürcher Kantonalbank, through which millions of interactions take place every day.

As a team, we are responsible for Customer Identity & Access Management (CIAM) and the Open Banking Platform of Zürcher Kantonalbank. We ensure exclusive access to all digital banking services for our customers – simple, secure and seamless.

Your expertise lies in open banking and you are also actively involved in the development and expansion of our CIAM solutions.

Your tasks

  • Continuous monitoring of developments and active advancement of open banking operations to provide our customers and partners with efficient and sustainable access to third parties
  • To help shape the expansion and further development of our CIAM solution
  • Specify requirements, features and user stories as well as accompany the implementation by the developers, including support during tests and rollouts
  • Ensure stable operations, including operational support and compliance with regulatory and security requirements in the Open Banking and CIAM environment
  • Collaboration in interdisciplinary teams as well as analysis of requirements and advice of internal and external partners with active stakeholder management
  • Advanced degree in computer science, economics or an equivalent education with a strong IT focus
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in business engineering with a focus on open finance (consent management, OAuthFlow, API platforms) and experience in process and data analysis
  • Sound methodological competencies in requirements identification, modeling and specification (e.g. UML, BPMN, mock-ups)
  • Experience in coordinating work packages and managing small projects or sub-projects as well as experience in agile setups (e.g. Scrum, SAFe)
  • Knowledge of digital identities, authentication procedures, OAuth and OpenID Connect is an advantage
  • Motivated, inclusive and team-oriented personality with pleasure in cross-sectoral collaboration and a clear view of the essentials
  • Strong analytical skills as well as holistic, targeted thinking and strong consulting competence in dealing with stakeholders
  • Strong ownership mindset with the ability to drive decisions and take responsibility
  • Open culture of cooperation in which mutual support and exchange are a matter of course
  • We support you in reconciling work and private life - whether through part-time, hybrid work, unpaid leave or flexible working hours
  • Your career is important to us as an employer and therefore we support you on your way and support you with many offers
  • Our modern office in the vibrant Kreis 5 in Zurich offers you the ideal environment to work creatively and productively.
  • We offer you a fair and transparent compensation based on clearly defined pay bands
  • Comprehensive package, which in addition to the attractive basic salary and a variable remuneration offers many benefits and perks – from A such as retirement provision to Z such as ZVV bonus pass
