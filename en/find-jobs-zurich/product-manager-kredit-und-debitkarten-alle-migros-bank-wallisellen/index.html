Full description

With us, you can be creative every day, work on new innovations and thus continuously expand and improve our card range. We are looking for you: A personality who, together with the team, actively develops our card portfolio (debit & credit).

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Bank, Wallisellen, Industriestrasse 17-19, 8304 Wallisellen

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Life cycle management of products, services and prices in the debit and credit card sector with a focus on “sustaining innovations” as well as improving customer value and customer experience
  • Support and driving force behind scaling the card portfolio (debit & credit) with a clear focus on profitability
  • Maintain and develop our successful partnerships within and outside the Migros ecosystem as well as rebuild and develop further cooperations
  • Technical analysis of regulatory requirements, conception and implementation of corresponding measures in the card sector as well as tracking and analyzing trends in the card and payment sector
  • Stakeholder management in the card sector as responsible product manager of the agile value stream "Payments" - in cooperation with Legal, Compliance and OpRisk as well as Operations and Marketing
  • Maintenance of the existing card products and their mapping in the various customer channels including answering more complex customer advisor requests

Requirements

  • 5 years: In product management credit and debit cards or in a related field
  • Master's degree (university, university of applied sciences): Business administration, business administration or similar field of study
  • You have extensive expertise in the field of debit and credit cards and the associated economic connections and regulatory requirements
  • Addressable communication with all relevant contact partners up to senior management
  • Very good analytical and conceptual skills coupled with a pragmatic approach
  • You are a flexible, agile and uncomplicated personality
  • You have an independent working method, act proactively and show initiative
  • in German (negotiation confident)
  • English (flowing)

Cosa of friamo

  • Child care: up to CHF 300 per month per child to external child care
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Migros Bank: accounts, mortgages and financial services with preferential terms
  • Employee offers: Annual generous well-being credit

Contatto

Application and contact Doris Liechti, HR Business Partner

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We continuously review applications received, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Discussion with case presentation: Specific expertise is examined on the basis of a case or other assessment instruments. After processing, the result of the leader is presented and then discussed together. Usually, the team is also represented at the presentation. As a rule, the conversations are conducted by the superior, without a representative from the HR. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week

Grado di occupazione: 80-100 %

Requirements

  • 3 Jahre: Im Product Management Kredit- und Debitkarten oder in einem verwandten Bereich

  • Master-Studium (Universität, Fachhochschule): BWL, VWL oder ähnliche Studienrichtung

  • Adressatengerechte Kommunikation mit allen relevanten Ansprechspartnern bis hin zum Senior Management

  • Sehr gute analytische und konzeptionelle Fähigkeiten gepaart mit einer pragmatischen Vorgehensweise

  • Du verfügst über eine selbständige Arbeitsweise, agierst proaktiv und zeigst Eigeninitiative
