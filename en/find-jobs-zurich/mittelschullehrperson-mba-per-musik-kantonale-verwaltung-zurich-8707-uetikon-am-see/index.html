Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Secondary School and Vocational Education Office Secondary School Teacher mbA for Music 50% The Cantonal School Uetikon am See (KUE) is a young school in the development phase. She leads a high school secondary level and five of the Zurich maturity profiles (old-language, mathematical-scientific, modern-language, economic-legal and philosophical-pedagogical-psychological). The number of pupils is almost 800, that of teachers and members of the administration around 120. The location and form of the school are unique: The modern premises at the provisional location in the middle of the municipality enable future-oriented and innovative forms of teaching and spur the creation of the “KUE spirit”.
