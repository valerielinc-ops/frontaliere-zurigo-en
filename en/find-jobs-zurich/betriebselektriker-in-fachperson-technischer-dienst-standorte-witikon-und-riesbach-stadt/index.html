Full description

Do you like working with people? You want to start from the 1. November 2026 with our team of the technical service for the health centers Witikon and Riesbach?

Tasks

  • They carry out a wide range of craft or technical activities, including repairs and repairs. Maintenance of technical equipment and gardening.
  • They provide transport and piquetting services.
  • They assist in compliance with all safety regulations.
  • They participate in the use and control of third-party companies, in material management and in the handling of difficult investigations.
  • You are the contact person for internal employees and residents.

Profile

  • Joy of contact with residents and high service orientation as well as solution orientation, teamwork and reliability
  • Completed apprenticeship as an electrical installer EFZ or equivalent training and several years of professional experience
  • In-depth knowledge of the applicable electrical regulations (e.g. NIN, NIV)
  • Readiness for Picetting Services and Special Operations e.g. on Occasions
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing, IT skills (MS Office, CAFM) and driver’s license category B mandatory

About us

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

The health centers Witikon and Riesbach are run jointly and are located in the middle of Zurich-Witikon and Zurich-Riesbach in districts 7 and 8. The Health Center for Old Witikon combines various care and care services under one roof. The Riesbach Old Age Health Center is fully focused on the treatment, care and care of people with dementia.
