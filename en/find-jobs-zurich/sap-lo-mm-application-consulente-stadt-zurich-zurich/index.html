Full description

We are strengthening our IT team with a high-performance personality who is responsible for the professional and technical development of the SAP modules LO and MM. They ensure that processes are continuously optimized, new requirements are added and implemented in accordance with the city-wide SAP requirements. You are the central interface between IT, the specialist areas, finance and controlling.

Tasks

  • Responsibility for the continuous optimization and further development of SAP LO/MM
  • Analysis of existing business processes as well as identification and implementation of optimization potential
  • Advising and supporting the departments in the efficient use of SAP LO/MM
  • Adopting, prioritizing and coordinating professional requirements in close cooperation with the specialist departments
  • Ensuring compliance with city-wide SAP standards, governance requirements and system architectures
  • Coordination and support of changes, releases, tests and system introductions
  • Creation and maintenance of system and process documentation as well as support of user training

Profile

  • Several years of experience with the SAP modules LO and MM and advantageous knowledge with PM, MDG and RE-FX
  • Willingness to familiarize yourself with the basics of SAP finance and controlling processes (payment transactions, investment management, BPC, SAC planning)
  • Strong understanding of business processes (real estate and financial management) as well as their digitalization and optimization
  • Ability to analyze professional requirements and transform them into sustainable SAP solutions
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method with a high degree of initiative
  • Experience and pleasure in interdisciplinary cooperation as well as high communication and consulting competence

We offer

a diverse and collegial working environment in a growing organization. Our employees benefit from attractive employment conditions such as family-friendly working time models and good social benefits. The centrally located place of work in the administrative centre Werd and a wide range of training and further education are further qualities.

About us

Properties for a livable city of Zurich

With a portfolio of 5.8 billion francs in insurance value, the City of Zurich is one of the major real estate organizations in Switzerland. In addition to the management of around 10,300 apartments and 1,600 commercial premises, we are responsible for the comprehensive ownership representation, the long-term value preservation and the further development of this versatile portfolio. We also manage the municipal land reserves and building rights and are responsible for real estate transactions.
