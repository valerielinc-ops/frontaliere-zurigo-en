Department of Farmed Animals

Job as of 1 October or by arrangement

The Department of Small Animal Reproduction belongs to the Clinic for Reproductive Medicine of the Tierspital of the University of Zurich. She is involved in the teaching, research and service of the Vetsuisse Faculty University of Zurich. In the clinic, diseases of small animals are treated and health measures are taken.

Tasks

  • Clinical activity in the field of small animal reproduction (including participation in night and weekend services, of course after appropriate training)
  • Participation in the clinical training of students
  • Preparation of a dissertation possible

Profile

  • Successfully completed study of veterinary medicine
  • Enjoying clinical activity and working in a team
  • Interest in sound clinical training in small animal reproduction
