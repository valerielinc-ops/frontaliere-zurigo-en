Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We create transparency in the banking and insurance market and advise our customers comprehensively and neutrally. As we are growing strongly, we are looking for reinforcements for our pension foundations at our headquarters at Paradeplatz in Zurich.

  • You are responsible for preparing the provisional financial statements of the affiliated pension funds and are the main contact person.
  • You support your colleagues in the coordination and preparation of the annual reports of the VZ pension foundations.
  • You work directly with the management of the VZ pension foundations and the accounting team, coordinating and monitoring the various work and processes.
  • You check the received data and information and give recommendations for action if necessary.
  • You support the various departments as well as the management of the foundation administration with evaluations and accompany the cross-departmental processes.
  • You will help with the implementation of regulatory or technical adjustments at our collection foundations.
  • You are committed to the further development of our organization, contribute ideas and actively shape your working environment.
  • An education in finance and accounting, auditing or a business administration degree at a (specialist) university
  • Ideally, experience in the field of occupational pensions or in the accounting of pension foundations
  • Accurate and dutiful working methods
  • Project experience and pleasure in coordinating different tasks and stakeholders
  • Good communication, thirst for knowledge, enjoyment of contact with different stakeholders and experience in working with several areas
  • IT affinity and savvy handling of MS Excel
  • Desire to work in a dynamic and service-oriented environment
  • A team to count on, varied tasks and a corporate culture that promotes initiative.
  • With us you can get involved and constantly take on more responsibility.
  • We are a motivated team that supports each other and implements new projects with enthusiasm and success.
  • You can deepen your expertise in the financial management of the various pension foundations: The VZ pension foundations include two investment foundations, two free movement foundations, a 3a foundation and two collective foundations.
  • We maintain an open exchange at eye level and live a flat hierarchy.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
