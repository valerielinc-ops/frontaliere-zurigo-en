Albis Beck has been a traditional company since 1898. With enthusiasm and passion, around 70 employees celebrate the fine craftsmanship at the two production sites Mettmenstetten and Zurich-Höngg every day and pamper customers with the finest delicacies in their own four Albis Beck cafes and other sales outlets. The regional origin of all ingredients as well as high quality standards are our focus.
