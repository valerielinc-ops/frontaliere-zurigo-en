About the Role As an IT Service Engineer II within our Treasury IT and D&T Finance team, you will play a pivotal role in keeping our Collateral Management platforms running smoothly, efficiently, and at the cutting edge. This is a hands-on, high-impact role where you'll work across incident management, change delivery, vendor collaboration, and platform modernisation. You'll have the opportunity to bring your ideas to the table, deepen your expertise, and grow alongside a team that genuinely values innovation, quality, and continuous improvement. Key

You're comfortable navigating ambiguity, managing competing priorities, and communicating confidently with both technical peers and business stakeholders. You care deeply about quality, documentation, testing, and the bigger picture, and you bring a positive, collaborative energy to everything you do.

4–8 years of relevant experience in IT service engineering or a related discipline Strong working knowledge of data modelling and SQL, with the ability to work with complex data structures and queries Solid experience with DevOps practices, including CI/CD pipelines, version control, and automation tooling Hands-on experience with shell scripting, with the ability to write and maintain scripts for automation and operational tasks Bachelor's degree or equivalent in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field
