Full description

<b>Bülach Hospital grows - grow with it.</b>

Tasks

  • Accompanying, promoting and qualifying FaGe learners and students NDS Emergency Care in the versatile environment of the Emergency Center
  • Planning, designing and evaluating competence-oriented learning processes including location interviews, certificates of competence and practical final examinations
  • Support and advise the nursing team in accompanying learners and students as well as ensuring high-quality training
  • Active participation in shared leadership as well as shaping projects and the further development of the emergency center
  • Collaboration in clinical emergency care as Dipl. Expert: in Emergency Care NDS HF

Requirements

  • Diploma as an Expert: in Emergency Care NDS HF or an equivalent education recognised in Switzerland as well as professional experience in emergency care
  • SVEB Certificate instructor/level 1 or willingness to complete it
  • Knowledge of the Swiss education system in health care
  • Enjoyment of accompanying and developing FaGe learners and students NDS Emergency Care as well as strong communication skills and empathy
  • Independent and structured working method, enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation as well as motivation to take responsibility and actively shape the emergency center
