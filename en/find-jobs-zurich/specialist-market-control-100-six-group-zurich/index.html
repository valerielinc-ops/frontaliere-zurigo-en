Specialist Market Control (100%)

Zurich | on site | Reference 8295

Do you want to work in a dynamic environment and actively shape fair trading on the stock market? We are looking for you as Specialist Market Control to ensure proper trading on the SIX Swiss Exchange.

Become part of a team that values transparency, efficiency and fairness in trade.

We are looking for a personality with experience in the financial services environment / stock exchange / trading with self-motivation as well as flexible thinking and action (ongoing trading and late / evening shift). Are you ready for this exciting and varied role? Then we look forward to your application documents!

These are your tasks

  • Management of stock exchange periods and interruption of trading to ensure fair and orderly trading
  • Preparation of Mistrade decisions, including calculation of prices and deviations
  • Adaptation of trading parameters in particular market situations
  • Communication and instruction to participants: inside in special situations
  • Actively monitor stock market operations and provide a telephone help desk.

You bring that with you

  • A completed degree in finance, economics or a comparable field
  • Several years of experience in stock trading or analysis, ideally in Switzerland
  • In-depth knowledge of Swiss financial markets and their regulatory requirements
  • Analytical thinking and the ability to interpret complex market information
  • Team spirit, initiative and a high degree of responsibility
  • Fluent German and English skills, French skills are a plus
  • Readiness for extended working hours until 22:00.
