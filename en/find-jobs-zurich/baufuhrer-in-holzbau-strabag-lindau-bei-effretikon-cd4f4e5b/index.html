Estimator / Purchaser Timber Construction 80-100% Your contribution with us

  • You prepare quotations, determine quantities based on plans and calculate prices for timber construction projects
  • You create work calculations and accompany projects from the tender phase to execution
  • You are responsible for project procurement, obtain offers from suppliers and subcontractors and compare them technically and price-wise
  • You negotiate contracts with subcontractors and material suppliers and conclude them in coordination with project management
  • You maintain calculations and keep master data as well as reference values up to date
  • You coordinate with site management and workshop planning to ensure material and scheduling align
  • You assess opportunities and risks in tenders and incorporate these into the bidding strategy
  • You check invoices from suppliers and subcontractors for compliance with agreed conditions

What counts for us

  • Completed apprenticeship as a carpenter or basic training in the construction industry
  • Further training as foreman, timber construction technician HF or site manager, or equivalent is an advantage
  • Practical experience in calculation, AVOR or procurement of timber and wood-based materials
  • Confident handling of industry software and MS Office
  • Good sense for quantities, materials and prices in timber construction
  • Negotiation skills as well as an independent and responsible working style complete your profile

Our added value for you

  • Modern workplace in a well-coordinated team
  • Exciting, varied timber construction projects with direct influence on their success
  • Attractive employment conditions as well as targeted training and development opportunities
  • Modern work equipment and flexible solutions for balancing work and private life
  • Health offers, employee discounts, company events and other attractive benefits
