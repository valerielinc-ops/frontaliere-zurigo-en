University Animal Hospital Zurich Job opening immediately or by agreement

Tasks

In this versatile and responsible role, you are 40% involved in coordination and 60% as a TPA directly involved in surgical care. You are the central interface between the Clinic for Small Animal Medicine and other areas of the veterinary hospital:

  • Coordination of internal procedures and cooperation with reception, emergency, anesthesia, medicine, intensive care unit, IMC and imaging
  • Organisation and planning of appointments for referred cases
  • Control and release of invoices
  • Transfer management of inpatients
  • Communication with pet owners and referring veterinarians
  • Participation in interdisciplinary reports
  • Introduction of students at the beginning of their clinical rotations
  • Support of the surgical team in patient care, surgical preparation and follow-up care

Profile

  • Completed training as veterinary practice assistant (TPA)
  • Experience in hospital environment or surgery is advantageous
  • Enjoying a lively working day in the university environment
  • Structured, independent and solution-oriented working method
  • High social skills, resilience and team spirit
  • Rapid understanding and assertiveness
  • Service orientation and communication strength
