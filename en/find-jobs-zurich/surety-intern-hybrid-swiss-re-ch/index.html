Original description

Are you looking for an opportunity to learn and to further advance your skills in a great working environment? Do you want to work in a global team, and does the world of finance, insurance and trade fascinate you? Are you attracted by contributing to growing Surety area and transformation of Swiss Re? We are looking for a young professional to join our dynamic team as a Surety Intern! About the Role You will contribute to the support of surety team with developing a monitor intelligence of the risk, apart from to give assistance to our Underwrites to insert information and creating reports. You will have the opportunity to get to know the Surety and Insurance market, interacting with customers and brokers. This is a unique opportunity for you career development, as you will participate in the development of new management tools, in addition to knowledge of Surety products and having contact with the insurance and reinsurance market. About the Team The Surety team reports directly to the Head of Surety Brazil and the Head of Surety Brazil reports directly to the Head of Surety Latam. we are responsible for handling, issuing and maintaining surety bond issues, in addition to providing support for related areas, such as origination, claims and others areas.
