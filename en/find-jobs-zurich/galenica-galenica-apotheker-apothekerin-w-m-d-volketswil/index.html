Full description

Location: Coop Vitality Volketswil, Volkiland, Industriestrasse 1, 8604 Volketswil

Do you want to work part-time and experience a varied pharmacy day? With us you will find exactly the right balance.

You can do that.

  • You support the management and offer it hand in the daily work
  • You give our customers a welcome feeling: Advice on health issues is important to you
  • You train the learners and accompany the students and pharmacists in the FPH training
  • Together with the team you provide the services of the pharmacy and expand them as needed
  • Nothing escapes your watchful eyes: You make sure that the quality is right at all times

You bring that with you

  • Completed pharmacy studies, beneficial already with FPH or BAB
  • Working together in a team is important to you and you are committed to a good working environment
  • Joy of developing the pharmacy with your ideas
  • German at least level B2

In our lively pharmacy, you will find a motivated, medium-sized team with a lot of expertise and team spirit. We offer numerous pharmaceutical services and work in a dynamic environment with varied pharmacy everyday life.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday
  • Pension fund with advantageous premium distribution - approx. 2/3 are taken over by us
  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and at other top brands incl. Coop Personal Discount Card
  • Extend competences through funded and supported training and internal development opportunities
  • Family friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of wages) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of wages), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Registered shares at preferential prices - not only engage professionally in Galenica, but also invest

Benefits may vary slightly depending on area/place

About us

A powerful joint venture At Coop Vitality, Coop and Galenica work together. Coop Vitality is part of the Galenica network and combines the strength of two partners to offer customers a modern pharmacy experience with a high level of expertise. The network’s pharmacies are usually located in or next to larger coop branches – especially in the German-speaking part of Switzerland. The range includes a wide range of products for health, beauty and disease prevention, complemented by expert advice and pharmaceutical services. At Coop Vitality, you can expect an extraordinary customer orientation. This in the points of sale as well as in the online shop.
