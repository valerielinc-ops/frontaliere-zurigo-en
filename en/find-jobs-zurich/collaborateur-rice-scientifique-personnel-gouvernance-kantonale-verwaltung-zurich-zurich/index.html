Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Secondary School and Vocational Education Office Academic / Employees / in Personnel & Governance 80 - 100% FOREIGN AND INTERNETED THINKING The Secondary School and Vocational Education Office (MBA) is involved in shaping the secondary level II in the Canton of Zurich. In the area of finance and personnel schools, we support the cantonal secondary and vocational schools in financial and personnel matters. We focus on trust, personal responsibility, open communication and cooperation. For the further development of our personnel work we are looking for reinforcement from January 2027. The job is limited to four years and offers a lot of space to think and shape. In the Governance Sek II project, you are focusing on implementing the personnel changes to the new school management. This includes permanent employment instead of term of office for school management, job sharing and the delimitation of personnel responsibilities between school commission, school management and education directorate. You accompany the implementation in practice and develop the necessary processes and templates. In addition, you will work on topics such as relief, further education or assessments and develop practical solutions for our schools. You have a university degree, ideally in legal, political or political sciences, and have experience in the public environment or good knowledge of public personnel law. You like to take responsibility, be willing to make decisions and have assertiveness. You think analytically, write clearly and understandably and enjoy structuring and developing complex topics. Experience in project management, especially according to HERMES, is a plus. You work organized, independently, bring your own ideas. You communicate openly and uncomplicatedly and contribute to a good cooperation. A task with social relevance, with a lot of room for manoeuvre and personal responsibility, flexible forms of work with home office and an open official culture awaits you. From January 2027, we will be working on our new, centrally located location at Zeughausstrasse 11 in Zurich. If you have any questions, your future supervisor, beatrice.hasler@mba.zh.ch is at your disposal.

Pensum: 80-100 %
