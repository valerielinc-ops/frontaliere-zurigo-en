Role overview

Enterprise Partner Solutions, part of Microsoft’s Industry & Partner Sales (IPS) organization, is accountable for building and co-selling innovative Microsoft Cloud & AI solutions and services, with our global Enterprise partners. Our Enterprise partner portfolio includes global systems integrators, advisories, high-value management consultancies (SI), software development companies (SDC) - working together to empower people and organizations to achieve more. The SDC team at Microsoft operates at the frontier of what's possible — partnering with organizations across the full innovation spectrum, from early stage startups to established market leaders, to help them build, launch, and scale transformative solutions powered by the Microsoft platform.

As a Partner Development Manager, you'll be at the center of the AI era — partnering with the most disruptive Software Development Companies to accelerate their go-to-market strategies and co-shape the next generation of cloud solutions. This is your opportunity to sharpen your business acumen at the intersection of AI and industry transformation, elevate your commercial craft, and architect bold market strategies that move at the speed of innovation.

You are required to bring your work ethic, enthusiasm, optimism, and customer-centric passion to stimulate growth and transformation within our partner ecosystem. Your challenger mindset, sales management skills, technological and industry knowledge, and superior interpersonal abilities will be leveraged to empower our partners in delivering market-defining solutions to our customers. This role offers you the chance to fast-track your career growth as you engage with intricate partner organizations, establish robust relationships in the C-Suite, and collaborate with multiple stakeholders to boost performance and tackle complex issues. What We Value in This Role

  • Growth mindset — You approach every partner conversation as a learning opportunity, staying curious about technology trends and constantly evolving your strategies alongside the market
  • Accountability with integrity — You own your partners' outcomes — the wins and the misses — and you show up with transparency and honesty in every relationship
  • Challenger spirit — You bring bold ideas to the table, push partners to think bigger, and aren't afraid to reframe the conversation when it unlocks greater opportunity
  • Inclusion and empathy — You build trust across diverse partner organizations, cultures, and leadership styles, creating environments where every partner feels genuinely invested in
  • Collaborative by nature — You thrive in a One Microsoft model, rallying internal stakeholders and breaking down silos to deliver for your partners and customers

Microsoft’s mission is to empower every person and every organization on the planet to achieve more. As employees we come together with a growth mindset, innovate to empower others, and collaborate to realize our shared goals. Each day we build on our values of respect, integrity, and accountability to create a culture of inclusion where everyone can thrive at work and beyond.
