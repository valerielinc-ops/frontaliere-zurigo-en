Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility.

For our Center for Dependency Diseases in Rheinau, we are looking for a

Dipl. Nursing specialist, Center for Dependency Diseases

60-100%

Their tasks

  • Maintenance of all station and shift operations
  • Management of station meetings and nursing services
  • Implementation of measures within the care process
  • Drug management
  • Management and monitoring of entries and exits in a nursing context
  • Approved vocational training in the field of care HF/FH
  • Professional experience in psychiatry
  • Willingness to engage in a dynamic work environment
  • Very good social skills and communication skills
  • Flexibility, resilience, service orientation
  • Development with perspective: Diverse career paths in one of the largest psychiatric clinics in Switzerland
  • Knowledge that advances: Extensive initial, further and further training offers
  • Together for people: Interdisciplinary cooperation for holistic psychiatric care
  • Strong in the team: appreciative cooperation and mutual support
  • Working flexibly: working time models that fit your life
  • More time for relaxation: at least 25 days of holiday and possibility for unpaid leave
