Project manager/sanitary 100% Do you know us? We are a Swiss company with competences in the fields of sanitary heating and refrigeration technology as well as their planning. With our location in Zurich and around 110 employees, we operate throughout the Canton of Zurich. We are part of Burkhalter Group AG, the umbrella organization of traditional companies in building technology and the building envelope in Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein. Are you ready for the next career step? We are looking for one: Project manager/in Sanitary (100%)

Their tasks

  • Planning and execution of projects
  • Accompanying and monitoring construction
  • Work preparation, construction management, intermediate examinations, acceptances
  • Responsibility of purchasing materials
  • Participation in construction meetings with clients, architects and planners
  • Coordinating internal and external processes
  • Responsibility for quality, deadline and costs

Your profile

  • Completed training as a sanitary installer or building engineering planner EFZ
  • Several years of professional experience as a project manager
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing and good manners
  • Independent, reliable and structured working method
  • Good communication skills and responsibility
  • Reliable and team capable personality

We offer you

  • Varied and demanding work in a collegial team
  • Open du-culture with respectful communication and family working environment
  • 4.5-day week for a 40-hour week
  • Regional activity and ambitious projects
  • Advanced working conditions and individual training opportunities
  • Various employee events
