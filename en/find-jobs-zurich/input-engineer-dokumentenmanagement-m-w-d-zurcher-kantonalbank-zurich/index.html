As an input engineer in the field of document management, you are responsible for the smooth, efficient and secure digitization as well as the processing and integration of documents into our system landscape. You bring your flair for the integration of applications and your Java knowledge to further develop our input and document processes. Working closely with IT, departments and external partners, you will design solutions that speed up the entire document lifecycle, increase quality and reduce manual effort.

Your tasks

  • Planning, installing, configuring and maintaining input and document management solutions (Kodak Capture Pro and DocProStar/OCTO)
  • Integrating document management applications into the existing system landscape (interfaces, web services, APIs)
  • Analyzing and resolving technical issues in the input and processing lines and assisting in remediation in surrounding systems
  • Design and develop automation and self-service solutions (e.g. for document import, classification, routing) to accelerate the end-to-end process chain
  • Further development and maintenance of Java-based components in the field of document management and system integration
  • Collaborate with internal agile teams from specialist areas, IT and external partners to develop integrated solutions for customer needs
  • Ensuring the ongoing operation incl. Monitoring input routes and managing allowances, licenses and technical accesses
  • Completed education and/or training in computer science, business informatics or comparable training
  • Relevant experience in the integration of applications and systems (interfaces, web services, APIs) and ideally in the document management environment
  • Good knowledge of Java or great interest in developing yourself in Java development
  • Knowledge of SQL and scripting languages (e.g. Linux Shell; beneficial Python) as well as familiarity with DevOps principles and agile development methods
  • Initial experience with the automation of build and deployment processes (e.g. Jenkins, GitLab CI/CD or similar tools) as well as basic knowledge in cloud and containerization are advantageous
  • You convince with strong analytical skills and a structured, solution-oriented way of working
  • You score with your joy of working together in the team and your clear communication
  • Very good German and good English skills in word and writing
  • Mutual support and team spirit are important to us
  • Exciting and diverse range of tasks with many network possibilities in the field of document and input management
  • We offer you space for your ideas and thus design the bank of tomorrow
  • Your career is important to us as an employer and therefore we support you on your way and support you with many offers
  • Modern workplace in district 5 with very good transport connections (hard bridge station)
  • You can flexibly organize your work in consultation with your team (e.g. mobile work, flexible working hours)
  • We offer a collaborative and appreciative cooperation at eye level with the opportunity to actively shape
  • Comprehensive package with attractive basic salary, variable compensation and versatile benefits
