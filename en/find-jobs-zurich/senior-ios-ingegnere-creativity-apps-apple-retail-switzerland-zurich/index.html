Full description

Summary At Apple, we believe that groundbreaking ideas flourish when exceptional engineering meets innovative vision. We’re building the future of creative expression, aand we need senior engineers who are passionate about crafting elegant, high-performance applications that empower users to bring their most ambitious ideas to life.

The Creativity Apps team needs your help shaping the next generation of creative editing tools. You’ll work on foundational technologies and user experiences that power applications used by millions of artists, designers, musicians, and innovators worldwide. This is a unique opportunity to apply your deep technical expertise to solve complex problems at scale, defining how people interact with powerful creative tools on Apple devices. You’ll contribute to the legacy of world-class creative applications like Final Cut Pro, Logic Pro, GarageBand, Pixelmator, and Photomator.

We care more about how you think than which platform you’ve been thinking on. If you’re a senior engineer from a graphics, systems, media, or ML background who is excited to go deep on Apple platforms — and you’re the kind of person who obsesses over how a feature feels, not just whether it ships — we want to talk to you.

Description As a Senior Software Engineer on our team, you will drive the architectural design, implementation, and evolution of core features for our next-generation creative applications. We’re looking for a problem-solver who excels in a dynamic environment, capable of both deep technical execution and strategic thinking across complex systems. You should be excited by the prospect of rapidly prototyping innovative concepts while simultaneously establishing robust, forward-looking architectural foundations.

This role is for you if you thrive in a dynamic environment and are equally comfortable with deep technical execution and strategic thinking. You have an instinct for the nuances of photography and the challenges faced by visual creators. You are not just writing code; you are shaping the future of how people capture, edit, and share their most cherished visual memories.

Minimum Qualifications

  • 6+ years of professional experience building and shipping production software
  • Demonstrated mastery of software architecture patterns, data structures, and algorithms, especially in high-performance contexts
  • Deep proficiency in at least one modern systems or application language (Swift, C++, Rust, Objective-C, Kotlin, Java, or similar), and a track record of picking up new platforms and languages quickly
  • Strong product sense: a history of shipping features where you owned the user experience, not just the implementation
  • Track record of effective cross-functional collaboration with design, product, and partner engineering teams

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with Apple’s core frameworks — UIKit, SwiftUI, Core Animation, AVFoundation, Core Image, and Metal (or equivalent graphics frameworks on other platforms)
  • Experience integrating machine learning into shipping products — on-device inference, model performance and memory trade-offs, or building product experiences around probabilistic model output
  • Fluency with AI-assisted development tools and an interest in helping the team use them well
  • Extensive experience identifying and resolving performance bottlenecks in client applications, with a focus on UI responsiveness, memory management, power efficiency, and real-time processing
  • Experience developing applications that involve complex media manipulation, rendering, or content creation workflows for professionals or enthusiasts (e.g., video editing, music production, graphic design, or advanced image processing)
  • Demonstrated ability to technically lead projects, mentor engineers, and drive cross-functional initiatives from concept to delivery in a fast-paced environment
