Full description

Your heart beats for older people? Is the culinary profession your calling? Do you see opportunities to contribute your skills and experience to us in a meaningful way in order to have a positive impact on the lives of our customers? Then you are a perfect fit for us!

For our residence Zollikerberg we are looking for a

Sous-Chef 100%

Your tasks

  • Responsible for the professional preparation and presentation of all forms of food according to the menu plan and special requests
  • Jointly responsible for efficient warehouse management
  • Compliance with the HACCP concept and occupational safety guidelines
  • Proper and professional organization of work within the kitchen service
  • Co-responsibility for purchasing goods, budget and controlling management, inventory and quality management
  • Substitute in the absence of the chef
  • Joint responsibility for individual, varied, healthy and target group-oriented catering in the entire catering sector

Your profile

  • Training as a chef EFZ and experience as a sous chef
  • Training as a diet chef is an advantage
  • Many years of experience in the field of hot and cold cuisine, completion/regeneration and food picking
  • Professional and independent working attitude
  • Flair for arranging and decorating food
  • Willingness to work at events and caterings
  • Knowledge of system gastronomy as well as à la carte cuisine
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoyment of a variety of challenges
  • Good knowledge of written and spoken German
