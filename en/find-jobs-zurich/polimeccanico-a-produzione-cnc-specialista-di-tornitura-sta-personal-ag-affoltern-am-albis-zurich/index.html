Full description

The processing of individual pieces and small series takes place independently on CNC lathes. The company offers a diverse working environment with international contacts and a 40-hour week. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Automatically program, set up and operate CNC lathes
  • Manufacture of large parts in individual pieces and small series
  • Carry out quality controls and accurately document results
  • Observing the ongoing machine process and fixing small faults

Requirements

  • Course degree as a polymechanic or polymechanic or equivalent qualification
  • Practice with CNC lathes and Fanuc controls counts as a plus
  • Readiness for operations in 2-shift operation
  • Precise, self-responsible and reliable working method

Offer

  • Wide range of tasks in an international environment
  • 40-hour week
  • At least five weeks of vacation
