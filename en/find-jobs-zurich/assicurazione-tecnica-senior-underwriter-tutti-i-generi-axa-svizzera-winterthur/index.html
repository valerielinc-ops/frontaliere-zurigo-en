Shape your future – together with us!

We are the specialist department for property and technical business insurance and are responsible for the underwriting of large and complex customers. Our technical insurance includes in particular assembly, machine and machine comprehensive insurance as well as solutions in the IT sector.

Are you a technical insurance underwriting professional? Or are you an engineer (e.g. mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, energy or process engineering) and would like to use your know-how in the insurance world? Then you've come to the right place. If you enjoy advice and customer contact, have strong communication and negotiation skills and are not put off by occasional travel within Switzerland, read on - our team of experts looks forward to hearing from you!

This is what awaits you

  • You assess technical risks of medium-sized and large companies domiciled in Switzerland.
  • You calculate and create offers for Swiss customers who sometimes have complex insurance needs.
  • You ensure the consistent application of the risk and underwriting strategy and evaluate risks taking the portfolio structure into account.
  • You support brokers, our own sales, the claims department and the underwriting of the regions with your professional expertise.
  • As a member of the department, you will actively contribute your know-how to the further development of our products and processes in the area of ​​technical insurance and participate in projects.
  • You enjoy passing on your knowledge and take on training and presentation tasks at internal and external conferences and training courses.

This is what you bring with you

  • You have completed a degree in engineering with a technical focus (university e.g. ETH/EPFL, technical college, HF diploma).
  • Ideally, you have several years of experience in underwriting technical insurance (major risks) and know the market environment.
  • Alternatively, you have worked for several years in a technical role in industry (e.g. production, plant engineering, maintenance, project management) and would like to use your know-how in the insurance world - we will provide you with targeted support as you get started in underwriting.
  • You work in a solution-oriented manner and support our partners and customers independently.
  • You think analytically and feel confident in dealing with complex business issues.
  • You will use your very good knowledge of French (C1-C2) for our customers in French-speaking Switzerland and communicate internally, both verbally and in writing, confidently in German
  • Italian is a plus.
