VZ VermögensZentrum is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We advise individuals and companies on all matters relating to money, manage assets and offer attractive financial services. We are very successful and growing strongly. Now we are looking for a committed personality for our team at VZ Operations AG in Zurich.

  • You accompany the implementation of digital processes and/or new functions from specification to configuration and bring them to a successful conclusion.
  • You are close to the specialist areas, clarify complex, technical as well as technical questions and bring structure into demanding requirements.
  • You specify user stories and ensure the implementation (including acceptance and testing).
  • You communicate with specialist areas and technical contacts confidently and on an equal footing, understand their different requirements and create viable solutions for everyone involved through your mediation ability.
  • You work closely with our external and internal partners.
  • Depending on the topic, you take on additional responsibility in the role of project manager.
  • Completed studies in natural sciences, computer science, business informatics or business administration or a similar education.
  • First professional experience in the field of digitalization or as a business analyst.
  • Stakeholder management both internally and with external partners
  • Flair/interest in technological trends and experience in agile project management and business analysis
  • A high level of quality awareness, a structured way of working with clear priorities and a desire for a service-oriented, dynamic environment
  • The opportunity to contribute ideas and drive innovation in a young, growing company.
  • Individual support within the framework of the job profile and support for external further education.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
