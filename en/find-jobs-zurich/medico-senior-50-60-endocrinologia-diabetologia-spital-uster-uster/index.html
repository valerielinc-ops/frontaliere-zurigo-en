Full description

Personal – competent – close Welcome to the Hospital Uster, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As the central pillar of health care in the Oberland region of Zurich and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you experience the highest level of expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and promoted in your professional development. Spital Uster is a teaching hospital of the Medical Faculty of the University of Zurich. Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of a “regional and digital health hub in Züri East”! In addition to a broad general internal medicine, the medical clinic offers an extended basic care in the field of gastroenterology and hepatology, cardiology, pneumology, oncology, hematology, endocrinology, acute geriatrics, angiology, neurology and intensive care medicine. Exciting challenges await you

What sets you apart for the job
