Full description

You look after our small patients and their companions before, during and after the examination. Mansioni

  • You operate modern X-ray equipment in diagnostic radiology (conventional X-ray, computed tomography, magnetic resonance imaging, angiography and fluoroscopy).
  • Planning and billing examinations is part of your area of responsibility.
  • As part of the training, you will complete two external internships (radio oncology and nuclear medicine).
  • Requisiti
  • Completed vocational training EFZ or FMS or secondary school leaving certificate
  • Passed admission procedure from the Careum
  • Interest in children, adolescents and their families
  • Good foundations in physics, chemistry, biology, mathematics and computer science
  • Very good knowledge of German
