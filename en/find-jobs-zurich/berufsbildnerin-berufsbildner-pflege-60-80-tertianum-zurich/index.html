Full description

For our idyllic 'Villa Böcklin' we are looking for an engaged and technically competent Dipl. Nurse HF/FH or Health Care Assistant EFZ who, with great passion, takes over the professional training of our trainees. A high percentage employment, 90-100% is possible with direct assumption of duties in nursing. 60-80% refers exclusively to professional training with regulated working hours. You have the choice... We look forward to you!

We are looking for immediately or by arrangement a

Full-time - Professional Trainer in Nursing 60-80%

Your tasks

  • You create learning situations for the trainees, guide them in the departments, document the learning process, provide feedback and evaluate them orally and in writing.
  • You plan and coordinate the learning accompaniments independently and work with the trainees on a 1:1 basis.
  • You communicate openly and transparently in our education team and nursing teams and take on an important function in the leadership triangle.
  • Carrying out treatment nursing activities that are used as learning situations.
  • Planning and accompanying doctor visits.
  • Electronic nursing documentation using Care-Coach.
  • Taking on tasks as a contact person.
  • Controlling the nursing process - depending on the level of training of the trainees.
  • Leading case discussions - depending on the level of training of the trainees.
  • Taking on the daily responsibility together with the trainees.
  • Taking on tasks as a theme carrier, technical authority.
  • Regulated working hours.

Your profile

  • Completed training as Dipl. Nurse HF/FH/DNII, 'FaGe EFZ' or an equivalent training recognized by the CRS.
  • Professional experience in long-term care preferable.
  • Experience as RAI coordinator preferable.
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude.
  • Joy in diverse challenges.
  • Good German language skills, both oral and written.
  • Joy in working with trainees and students.
  • A recognized training in the pedagogical field (at least professional trainer course).
