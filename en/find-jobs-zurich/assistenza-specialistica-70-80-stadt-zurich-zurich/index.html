Full description

At our three locations in the Sihlfeld School House in Zurich in the Limmattal School District, we are looking for 1. November 2026 or by agreement until 31.07.2027 a specialist care, 70 - 80%.

Tasks

  • Pedagogical accompaniment and promotion of pupils in the school, life practice, hygienic and social area
  • Assistance in the preparation of individual funding plans and implementation of reference work
  • Active use of ASS-specific tools (e.g. UK, Metacom) to support communication and participation
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation in the school and support team as well as participation in meetings and school location discussions
  • Organisation and organisation of childcare time and support for individual and joint leisure activities and school events

Profile

  • Completed training as a specialist (specialist for people with disabilities) or a comparable qualification
  • High commitment to students with special needs as well as the ability to work independently and think in a networked way
  • Motivated, open and resilient personality with a positive human image, humor and joy in intercultural coexistence
  • Very good team and communication skills coupled with great flexibility in everyday work
  • Independent working method with simultaneous willingness to work closely together in a team

We offer

  • Self-employed and varied work in a professional team
  • Exciting interdisciplinary tasks in the special education sector
  • Possibility of active participation in our school
  • Conditions of employment in accordance with the requirements of the City of Zurich
  • Regular working hours

About us

The Heil Pädagogschule der Stadt Zürich is a special day school for 4 to 15-year-old children with a cognitive and/or multiple impairment. We offer the pupils who are taught in separate classes in different school buildings in the city of Zurich a supportive environment and place the focus of the interdisciplinary promotion in their strengths.

Our teams at the various locations in the city of Zurich consist of school curative pedagogues, social pedagogues, specialists care, learners, assistants as well as speech pedagogues, ergo-, physio- and psychomotor therapists.
