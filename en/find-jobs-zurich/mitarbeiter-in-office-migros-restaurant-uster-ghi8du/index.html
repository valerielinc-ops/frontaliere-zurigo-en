Full description

Migros Restaurant Uster offers fresh and seasonal food every day. From a large selection, our guests can cater throughout the day. For our motivated team, we are looking for a service-oriented personality to complement it.

Mansioni

  • Various clean-up and cleaning operations according to the HACCP hygiene guidelines
  • Cleaning and distribution of dishes and cutlery
  • Assistance in receiving delivery of goods
  • Various Mise en Place work as well as help with work in the kitchen
  • Active support for goods management
  • Compliance with GMZ quality standards and guidelines

Requirements

  • 1-2 years of professional experience: in the kitchen sector
  • Professional experience in a similar capacity
  • Flexible operational readiness from Monday to Saturday in different shifts (early and late services)
  • Physically resilient and motivated personality
  • Efficient and orderly functioning
  • Motivated, service-oriented and humorous team player personality
  • No education/training required
  • German (good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Central location: Working in Zurich West
  • Handy subscription: You can look forward to special conditions for the Handy subscription
  • Occupational pension provision: Excellent pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Social benefits: Fair social benefits
  • Food: Discount in the staff restaurant
  • Cumulus Points: You get additional Cumulus Points when shopping
  • Migros Bank: Preferred interest on private and investment account, free account management, discounted mortgages and more
