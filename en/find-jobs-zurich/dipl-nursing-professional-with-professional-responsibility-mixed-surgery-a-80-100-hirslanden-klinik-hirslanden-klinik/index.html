Location: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 74720

Are you looking for a task in which you can combine your professional competence with personal responsibility and freedom of design? At Klinik Hirslanden Zürich you can expect a modern, dynamic working environment in which quality and humanity go hand in hand.

For our surgical station we are looking for a dedicated dipl. Nursing professional / a dipl. Nursing professional with an additional function as professional responsibility.

In a varied and demanding working day, you take on responsibility, support learners and students in their development and actively shape a professional, patient-oriented care. Together in the team, you ensure a high quality of care and an appreciative learning environment.

YOUR SCOPE

  • Need- and customer-oriented care and care of patients according to the care process in cooperation with our partner doctors and an interprofessional team.

YOUR TASKS

  • Holistic and individual care of the patients assigned to you according to the care process
  • Independent implementation of the diagnostic and therapeutic measures prescribed by the doctor with the support of our specialist health staff
  • Identification and control of emergency situations and initiation of adequate measures until the arrival of the doctor
  • Assuming responsibility for correct patient documentation and information sharing in the assigned task area
  • Implementation of department-internal evaluations and subject-specific short-term training
  • Supporting the management in achieving the department-specific annual targets

TRAINING REQUIRED

  • Completed training as a dipl. Nursing professional or equivalent SRK recognition in foreign diplomas

Previous experience

  • Professional experience required in acute hospital

FURTHER SKILLS AND KNOWLEDGE

  • Great flexibility, resilience and high organizational talent
  • Good knowledge of German in word and writing (certificate B2)
  • Good IT user knowledge
  • High social skills and strong empathy
  • Teamwork and ability to guide employees

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. You work with a modern medical clinic information system and can expect extensive training and development opportunities from us. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family responsibilities. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. Employees who have to move completely for their work receive four additional paid days off with a 100% workload. With a reduced workload, the days off reduce accordingly. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from equivalent financial compensation. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.
