Laboratory staff: in the field of materials, corrosion and durability (m/f/d)

80%-100%, Zurich, permanent

Does a versatile laboratory function appeal to you, in which analytical methods, technical support of measurement infrastructure and cooperation with researchers come together? In the research group "Durability of Materials" at the ETH Zurich, you take care of the first By December 2026, or by agreement, modern experiments can be carried out reliably, making an important contribution to groundbreaking research in materials characterisation, corrosion and electrochemistry. Enjoy an international environment, modern and versatile laboratory infrastructure and the opportunity to support students and researchers in their daily work.

Project background

The research group "Durability of Materials" is anchored in the Institute of Building Materials (IfB) of the Department of Construction, Environment and Geomatics (D-BAUG) of ETH Zurich and is therefore at the interface between materials science and civil engineering. The research group focuses on the corrosion of steel in concrete or other media such as water or soil, with particular attention to the sustainability of building materials. We operate a versatile laboratory infrastructure for sample preparation, chemical and electrochemical measurements, microscopy, spectroscopy, anaerobic tests, thermal analysis, as well as non-destructive tests. For more information on the research group: https://ifb.ethz.ch/durability

Job description

In this versatile function, you assume responsibility for laboratory operations and support the research group with your technical know-how.

  • They operate, maintain and calibrate laboratory and measuring instruments and ensure their reliable operational readiness (e.g. glovebox systems).
  • They train and supervise students in selected experimental methods (e.g. in chemical analysis) and promote their independent work.
  • They support researchers in planning and implementing experimental work.
  • They bring their own ideas to the development and construction of measuring equipment, for example using 3D printing technologies.
  • They work closely with the Group Safety Representative and support with inventory and safety management in the laboratory.
  • You will continuously develop your expertise and acquire new analytical methods and measurement methods.
  • They promote open, secure and constructive cooperation in an international research environment.

Profile

  • Completed training as a chemical laboratory:in (at most physics laboratory:in) or a comparable qualification with in-depth experience in the laboratory environment.
  • Experience in chemistry, electrochemistry, corrosion, materials technology or related fields.
  • Knowledge in the operation and maintenance of laboratory and measuring devices, for example gloveboxes, pH measuring devices or electrochemical measuring systems.
  • Interested in building measuring equipment (e.g. 3D printing) and learning new experimental and analytical methods
  • Understand laboratory safety and act responsibly in laboratory operations.
  • Strong organisational and planning skills as well as an independent and structured working method.
  • Enjoy working with students, researchers and technical staff in an intercultural environment.
  • Good knowledge of English for daily cooperation in a predominantly English-speaking research group.
  • Knowledge of German for daily communication in the German-speaking environment of ETH Zurich and other contacts in Switzerland.
  • Communication strength, teamwork and the willingness to actively share knowledge and support others.
