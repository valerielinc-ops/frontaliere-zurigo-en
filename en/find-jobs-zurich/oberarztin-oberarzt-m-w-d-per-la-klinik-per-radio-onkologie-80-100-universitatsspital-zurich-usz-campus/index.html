Full description

USZ Campus by 1.9.2026 or by agreement

Their main tasks

  • Comprehensive care for outpatients and inpatients: insiders in the entire spectrum of radio-oncology – from the indication position in the tumor board to follow-up care
  • They represent radio-oncology in the interdisciplinary tumor boards, in teaching and further education
  • They are actively involved in the introduction of new technologies and radio-oncological methods and evaluate them clinically and scientifically
  • You are responsible for, develop and represent your subspecialization within the USZ, as well as at national and international level

Your profile

  • You are a specialist in radio-oncology (FMH or EU equivalent)
  • You have a strong interest in scientific work and have experience in precision radiation therapy
  • You have oral and written knowledge of German at C1 level
  • They are flexible, capable of teamwork, resilient and value interdisciplinary cooperation
  • You are interested in working in teaching, further and further training as well as in research and quality assurance

What you can look forward to

  • You will get the chance to develop further in a clinically and scientifically internationally recognized institution
  • Benefit from our global network and work actively in national and international professional societies
  • We also offer you exciting clinical and scientific projects and give you the opportunity to lead your own research projects.
  • An attractive location in the heart of the livable city of Zurich completes the overall package
