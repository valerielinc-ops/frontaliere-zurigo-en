Full description

Shape the future of our production site. As one of Switzerland's top employers, you will take on project leadership, establish Operational Excellence as a success factor, develop your team, and implement sustainable improvements with tangible value.

Roles

  • You develop the Operational Excellence strategy for the plant in Meilen and implement it together with the relevant stakeholders within Delica
  • You steer the Lean transformation and embed a sustainable improvement culture at all levels
  • You develop KPI and performance management systems and create transparency for evidence-based decisions
  • You lead cross-departmental transformation and improvement programs including governance and achievement of objectives
  • You lead a team of 4 people and develop competencies in Lean, Operational Excellence, and data-driven work

Requirements

  • Several years of leadership experience: solid practice in Lean and project management and in transformation projects within the process industry.
  • Completed degree (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): in Supply Chain Management, Engineering or comparable, ideally supplemented by Lean Six Sigma or Project Management
  • Persuasive personality with high communication skills and impact on different hierarchical levels
  • Strategic thinking combined with strong implementation and change capability
  • High affinity for metrics, digitization, and performance management
  • German (fluent)
  • English (fluent)

What we offer

  • Employee benefits: Discount on various services at Medbase
  • Migros Bank: Migros offers higher interest rates on private and investment accounts for its employees
  • Cumulus points: 2 times more Cumulus points on food and 5 times on non-food articles
  • Catering: Balanced and fine lunch menu/buffet in the staff restaurant for only CHF 8.-
  • Professional pension: Compared to BVG, Delica contributes 7% - 18% more depending on age
  • Sports and fitness offers: Free use of the integrated fitness studios at Buchs, Birsfelden, and Meilen sites, reimbursement of CHF 200.- on fitness yearly subscriptions
  • Leisure and culture offers: Over 50% discount on subscriptions to Thermalbad Schinznach, Sole Uno, or Erlebnispark Alpamare
  • Migros Club School: Up to 70% reimbursement per course
  • Central workplace: Very good public transport connections and free parking
