Full description

We are looking for a talented Fullstack Developer (senior level) who will strengthen our team and design creative and user-friendly solutions with us. If you are passionate about developing modern and innovative solutions and would like to work in a creative and dynamic environment, we look forward to receiving your application.

Place of work: Migros-Genossenschafts-Bund, Limmatplatz Zürich, Limmatstrasse 152, 8005 Zürich

Responsibilities

  • You capture user requirements, design detailed solutions and, as a senior, are happy to take the lead in implementing, introducing and operating our products
  • Your focus on clean code, scalability and appropriate test coverage during implementation ensures the desired quality and sustainability of our solutions
  • You take responsibility for the entire lifecycle of our software solutions – from development through operation and maintenance to continuous improvement - in close collaboration with the team
  • You handle the acceptance of internal and external solutions, including the associated testing (e.g. code review, checking compliance with guidelines, the technologies used and the application architecture)

Requirements

  • Many years of experience as a Fullstack Software Engineer: in a complex technology environment and/or a large technology company
  • Completed degree (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): in computer science or equivalent knowledge acquired through professional experience and further training in software development
  • You have several years of practical experience in developing scalable backend solutions, ideally with Go, and as a full-stack engineer you also feel at home in frontend development
  • You have strong problem-solving skills, contribute your own ideas, and play a creative role in finding solutions
  • You have a solid command of CI/CD pipelines and Kubernetes
  • You bring additional knowledge of modern frontend frameworks such as React or Next.js
  • You are a communicative person and a true team player
  • You know how to work and succeed in an agile environment, and you are motivated to continuously learn new things
  • It is an advantage if you have initial experience with Shopify and Liquid
  • German (business fluent)
  • English (fluent)

What we offer

  • Modern infrastructure: Various work zones and modern co-working spaces with advanced equipment
  • Catering: Excellent meals at affordable prices in the staff restaurant
  • Occupational pension: Above-average benefits and three different savings plans tailored to individual life circumstances
  • Scope for shaping and decision-making: Opportunity to independently shape and further develop the area of responsibility
  • Leisure and cultural activities: As the main sponsor of races and events, Migros offers employees a limited number of places at half price.
  • Individual professional development: Support for job-related training and continuing education

Recruitment process

  • Submit application documents: For a complete application, we need your CV, certificates and diplomas. A photo in the CV and a cover letter are not necessary. We are committed to equal opportunities for all applicants. We have a standardized recruitment process. If this does not work for you, mention it in your application dossier.
  • Preselection of applications: We review applications received on an ongoing basis, create a preselection and make it available to the manager for review. The decision on whether to proceed or be eliminated from the recruitment process is communicated by phone or email. Time until feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Interview with HR & specialist department: The interview is conducted with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department, virtually or on site. The primary purpose is to learn more about personal and professional competencies and to give both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit within the company and team. Time until feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Technical interview with case presentation: Specific professional knowledge is assessed using a case or other assessment tools. After completing it, the result is presented to the manager and then discussed together. Usually, the team is also represented at the presentation. As a rule, the interviews are conducted by the supervisor, without an HR representative. Time until feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In an informal setting, we therefore provide an opportunity for everyone to get to know one another.
  • Offer & contract issuance (Digital): Following our verbal job offer, the most important contract terms are verified once again. Once an agreement has been reached, the contract is prepared and sent by email for digital signature. Time until feedback: Up to one week

Employment percentage: 60-100%
