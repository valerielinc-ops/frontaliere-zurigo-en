Full description

In order to strengthen our dynamic team in Zurich, we are looking for a committed personality as a project manager immediately or by agreement: in Elektro. In this responsible position, you lead challenging electrical projects, coordinate different stakeholders and ensure successful implementation in terms of quality, deadlines and costs.

You can move that with us.

  • Management of small and medium-sized projects in the high and low current sector
  • Responsible for order preparation and for the timely and professional execution of orders
  • Assuming coordination tasks with other trades and managing subordinates
  • Creation and processing of offers, supplements and dimensions as well as partial and final invoices
  • Acquisition of orders and active support of our demanding customers
  • Responsible for achieving the project goal regarding occupational safety, costs, deadlines and quality
  • Adherence to the specifications and previous life of occupational safety and training of employees

That's what makes you.

  • Basic electrical installer training EFZ
  • Further education as an electrical project manager with eidg. FA or in training for the Federal Dipl. Electrical installers
  • Experienced handling of industry standards such as NIN, NPK and Steiger
  • Willingness to take responsibility and also lead employees
  • Independent, reliable and careful working method
  • Organisational and negotiation skills
  • High level of social, technical and methodological competence
  • German in Word and Writing and Ticket Kat. B is mandatory
  • Good IT knowledge in MS Office as well as an industry application such as Messerli or similar

That's what we offer you.

  • Varied activities
  • Established team
  • Lively and challenging environment
  • Attractive social benefits
  • Awarded the Friendly Work Space Label
  • Internal and external training opportunities
  • With the development of the Electrical Department
