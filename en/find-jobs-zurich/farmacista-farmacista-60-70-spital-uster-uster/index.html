Full description

Personal – competent – close Welcome to the Hospital Uster, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As the central pillar of health care in the Oberland region of Zurich and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you experience the highest level of expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and promoted in your professional development. Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of a “regional and digital health hub in Züri East”! For the hospital pharmacy, the hospital Uster is looking for 1. January 2027 or by appointment a committed personality as an all-rounder, who is happy to participate in the hospital pharmacy as well as at the patient counter for outpatient and emerging inpatient patients.

Exciting challenges await you

  • Care of the patient switch within the hospital pharmacy, central function in ensuring medication safety at the interface between inpatient and outpatient care:

Recipe validation

  • Advice for inpatients of outpatients in hospitals on the safe and correct use of their medicines
  • Only recipes, not OTC. Rather short patient contacts, high frequency, grateful customers
  • Processing of requests for medicinal products
  • Creating guidelines for the use of remedies
  • Development and implementation of further training measures for hospital employees such as nurses, doctors and staff of the hospital pharmacy on pharmaceutical topics
  • Cooperation in the further development and updating of the quality management system
  • Cooperation in projects and further development of hospital pharmacy
  • Participation in day-to-day logistics business if required

What sets you apart for the job

  • Completed pharmacy studies and professional license
  • ideally several years of professional experience in hospital pharmacy
  • Communication skills, social skills and responsibility
  • Joy of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • High load capacity and independence
  • In-depth user knowledge in the MS-Office programs

6 reasons to work with us

  • Competent, motivated team
  • many opportunities to further develop hospital pharmacy
  • Regular working hours
  • Attractive employment conditions
  • Central location near Uster station and motorway connection
  • ZVV BonusPass or cheap parking
