For more than 100 years, GLOBUS has provided unique worlds of inspiration with international brands, exclusive products, sensual shopping experiences and excellent service. With great passion and attention to detail, we inspire our customers again and again. For our high fashion brand BURBERRY we are looking for you as a passionate sales person. As a sales consultant, you are working at a workload of 80-100% at the location in Zurich. Here you make the difference

  • As a host, you make sure that our customers feel welcome and well looked after.
  • Thanks to your first-class service, your fascination for the brand Burberry and your individual fashion and styling consultancy, you will provide unique shopping experiences.
  • With your positive charisma and your passion for Burberry Fashion & Accessories, you contribute decisively to the company’s success.
  • Through your professional and personal advice, you build close relationships with our customers and maintain them in the long term.
  • You will support you in the goods management and implement the warehouse standards as well as the visual merchandising guidelines of GLOBUS.
  • You will continuously improve your fashion know-how by following the latest fashion trends, taking part in training sessions and keeping up to date with new Burberry collections and products. That draws you out
  • Ideally, you have successfully completed vocational training in the retail trade and bring at least three years of sales experience in a luxury or high fashion environment.
  • You are passionate about Brand Burberry and present the current collections in a creative, self-confident and sovereign way.
  • You are dynamic, above average service-oriented and have an open and lively personality with a high affinity and passion for fashion, art and culture.
  • You love the role as a host: in and live an intrinsically motivated customer orientation.
  • You have excellent knowledge of German and very good knowledge of English
  • Other languages are not a must, but of great advantage in dealing with our international guests.
