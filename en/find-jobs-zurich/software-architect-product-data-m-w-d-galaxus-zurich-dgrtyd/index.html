Full description

As part of Team Heisenberg, you are pursuing the ambitious goal of providing our more than ten million active products with the best possible product data and thus improving the shopping experience of our customers. You ensure that features, product images, variants, accessories and other information from different sources are brought together on our products.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Headquarters, Pfingstweidstrasse 60b, 8005 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Designing architecture and tackling it yourself: Together with the development team, you accompany software solutions from conception to implementation to operation, taking on technical and architectural responsibility
  • Continuously develop our software: You will continuously improve the existing codebase and architecture, question established approaches and bring new technologies / automation where they create concrete benefits
  • Combining technical requirements with viable solutions: You question requirements, make complex relationships understandable and find pragmatic solutions together with the participants
  • Think beyond team boundaries: As a technical contact person, you support the Solution Architects and together with the domain architect ensure that your solutions fit into the overall architecture
  • Move the team forward: You share your knowledge, coach software engineers and inspire others to work together. You make decisions together, take responsibility for the outcome and learn from successes and setbacks.

Requirements

  • At least three years of experience: In a software architecture role (ideally in an agile, product-oriented environment)
  • Completed degree programme (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): In Computer Science (or equivalent practical work experience)
  • You have in-depth knowledge of C# and .NET as well as experience with the development and operation of distributed systems – or a comparable technical background
  • As a team player, you listen, take on different perspectives and motivate your colleagues. Joint success is the focus for you
  • Knowledge of data analytics or machine learning is a plus
  • Have the courage to try things without planning them down to the smallest detail
  • The ability to adapt to continuous changes in the working environment
  • German (flowing)
  • English (flowing)
