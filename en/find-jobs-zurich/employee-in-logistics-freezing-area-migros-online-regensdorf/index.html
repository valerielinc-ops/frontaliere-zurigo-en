Full description

As a leading Swiss online supermarket, we bring thousands of orders to our customers every day. In our new, state-of-the-art logistics center in Regensdorf, you ensure that quality, precision and speed are perfectly combined – become part of our "cool" team!

Mansioni

  • Picking: Compiling customer orders from storage containers or shelves into our shipping boxes.
  • System-based work: You work with modern scanner devices and ensure precise processing of pick orders.
  • Quality & process responsibility: Ensure completeness, quality and efficiency in the picking process.
  • Compliance with standards: Monitoring and compliance with safety and hygiene guidelines.

Requisiti

  • 2+ years: Logistics
  • No training required: Logistics
  • Physical health: Good physical condition and resilience for activities in a frozen environment at temperatures up to -21 °C.
  • Flexibility & commitment: Ready for fixed working hours in two-shift operation (no shift change).
  • Team orientation: You like working in a team and support your colleagues in adjacent areas if necessary.
  • Experience in logistics: Ideally you already have first experience in logistics – experience in cooled areas is of benefit.
  • Digital competence: Enjoy working with digital tools and scanner devices.
  • German (good knowledge)
  • English (basic knowledge)

Cosa offiamo

  • Maternity leave / Paternity leave: 18 weeks maternity leave and 4 weeks paternity leave
  • Cum-Stories: Benefit from additional points of the Cum-Stories
  • Sports and fitness: We value your health and give you CHF 250.- per year when you complete a sports subscription
  • Individual training: We appreciate your development, participate in your growth and offer a budget for it
  • #oneteam: You will be part of a team that is packed, lives for each other, and pursues shared goals with enthusiasm.
  • Du culture: We stand for respectful coexistence on an equal footing - personal responsibility & freedom: We value direct communication and involve our employees in decision-making
