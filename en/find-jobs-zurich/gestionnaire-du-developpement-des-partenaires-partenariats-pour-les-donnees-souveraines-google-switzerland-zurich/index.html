Preferred qualifications

  • Experience working with Public Sector/Government industry clients.

  • Ability to coordinate with the regional sales leadership to align field teams and leverage customer relationships and facilitate selling efforts.
  • Ability to optimize onboarding, validation, and operational processes to reduce sales friction and maximize partner velocity.

About the job

The Google Cloud Platform team helps customers transform and build what's next for their business — all with technology built in the cloud. Our products are developed for security, reliability and scalability, running the full stack from infrastructure to applications to devices and hardware. Our teams are dedicated to helping our customers — developers, small and large businesses, educational institutions and government agencies — see the benefits of our technology come to life. As part of an entrepreneurial team in this rapidly growing business, you will play a key role in understanding the needs of our customers and help shape the future of businesses of all sizes use technology to connect with customers, employees and partners.

In this role, you will be the primary lead for the partner ecosystem across Google Cloud's dedicated and data boundary offerings. This role is accountable for driving partner-led customer adoption and pipeline growth in financial services, healthcare and other highly regulated commercial industries leveraging the S3NS and German dedicated cloud, and the global data boundary offering.

Google Cloud accelerates every organization’s ability to digitally transform its business and industry. We deliver enterprise-grade solutions that leverage Google’s cutting-edge technology, and tools that help developers build more sustainably. Customers in more than 200 countries and territories turn to Google Cloud as their trusted partner to enable growth and solve their most critical business problems.

Individual pay is determined by factors including job-related skills, experience, and relevant education or training.

Germany: €140000 - €144000 (EUR) + 66.67% bonus target + equity + benefits

Learn more about benefits at Google.

Responsibilities

  • Work with our Google Cloud dedicated and data boundary partner operators to design, build and roll out an integrated partner program to attract and retain a partner ecosystem.
  • Define and drive the execution of the Go-to-Market (GTM) strategy, including business development activities to drive business growth.
  • Identify, engage,recruit and develop key partners with deep relationships in target commercial sectors to bring qualified net new opportunities and drive consumption.
  • Drive the independent software vendor (ISV) partnership lifecycle, ensuring an ecosystem of ISVs is activated to drive net new business growth and address customer workload needs.
  • Identify and engage strategic systems integrators and advisory partners (e.g., GSIs) to guide regulated industry customers through complex data classification strategies and business case development for Google Dedicated Cloud.
