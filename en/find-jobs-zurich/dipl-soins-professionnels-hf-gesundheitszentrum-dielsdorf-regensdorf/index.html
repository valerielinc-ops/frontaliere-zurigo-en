Dielsdorf Health Center is a modern geriatric competence center with around 500 employees from 35 nations. You benefit from our attractive employment conditions, including with mind. 5 weeks holidays, good social benefits, discounted Reka checks, individual training opportunities, cheap prices in our restaurant and free parking spaces.
