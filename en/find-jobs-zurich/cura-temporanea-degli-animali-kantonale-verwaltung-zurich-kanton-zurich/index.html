Canton of Zurich Health Department Veterinary Office Temporary Animal Care 50% The Canton of Zurich Veterinary Office is committed to animal protection and animal health.

It helps to ensure the welfare of animals and the quality of animal foods.

It helps ward off animal diseases by monitoring animal populations with laboratory tests and combating animal disease outbreaks.

Main responsibilities

  • You will work in an animal shelter in Zurich North.
  • This primarily cares for dogs, cats, rabbits, small mammals, birds and reptiles that are temporarily in the care of the veterinary office.
  • You ensure the well-being of these animals through correct feeding and care and ensure that they are adequately occupied.
  • In addition to direct animal care, there are other tasks related to animal care: administrative work on the computer, cleaning and disinfection of the accommodation, storage management and environmental work at the location.
  • You will also work at the cantonal foundling registration office, where you will check reports from the Swiss Animal Registration Center (STMZ) and initiate the necessary measures.
  • You are flexible and used to working in a structured and independent manner, but also in a team, and processing data using computers.
  • You enjoy taking on responsibility and working in a small, well-coordinated team suits you.
