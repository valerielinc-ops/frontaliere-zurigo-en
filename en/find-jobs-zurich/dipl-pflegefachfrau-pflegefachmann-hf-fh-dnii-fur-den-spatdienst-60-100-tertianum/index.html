Full description

Tertianum Neuhegi offers people with a need for care and support an attractive living environment and a home to feel good at. The residents of our senior center are guaranteed security, support and comprehensive care around the clock. We accompany people in their self-determined lives - regardless of their social background - and are committed to the high quality of our services.

Does your heart beat for older people?

Do you not only want to ensure quality care through your work, but also noticeably enrich the lives of residents? Do you see nursing as a task with meaning and responsibility - and would you like to use your skills where they really make a difference?

Then you have come to the right place.

We are looking for someone for the Tertianum Neuhegi Winterthur immediately or by appointment

Dipl. Nursing Specialist HF/FH/DNII 60 - 100% late shift, with occasional early shifts

Your tasks

  • Holistic care and support for guests in accordance with our nursing principles and the nursing process
  • Carrying out treatment-related nursing activities
  • Planning and accompanying doctor's visits
  • Electronic care documentation using Care Coach
  • Taking on tasks as a caregiver
  • Control of the nursing process
  • Leading case discussions
  • Assumption of late shift responsibilities
  • Taking on tasks as a subject matter representative, technical authority to give instructions

Your profile

  • Completed training to become a qualified nursing specialist HF/FH/DNII or equivalent training recognized by the SRK
  • Professional experience in long-term care an advantage
  • Experience in RAI desired
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoy a variety of challenges
  • Good knowledge of spoken and written German
