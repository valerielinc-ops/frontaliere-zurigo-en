Full description

Welcome to the heart of Zurich, right next to Bahnhofstrasse. Formerly a lively place of the silk trade, the 4*Superior Boutique Hotel Seidenhof is now a modern place of work. Travelers from all over the world stay here in the 78 rooms, stay in the Enja restaurant as well as at the bar or discover the most beautiful places in the neighborhood from here. With modern design and high-quality equipment, the hotel delights and takes up the history of the former silk trade. Our team takes care of unique experiences and unforgettable stays. Are you our new team member who supports us as floor governor:e immediately or by appointment? Your area of activity:

  • Daily room control
  • Ensuring cleanliness and good appearance throughout the hotel together with the Cluster Gouvernante
  • Finding and fulfilling guest needs and wishes
  • Securing the work and operations of housekeeping
  • Control & support of the housekeeping team during cleaning on the floors
  • Room scheduling in cooperation with the Front Office
  • Implementation of the basic annual cleaning of the hotel
  • Ensuring and implementing the “Sorell Standards”, hygiene and occupational safety guidelines
  • Training and management of employees
  • Conduct of briefings and debriefings as well as participation in team and cadre meetings
  • Participation in the recruitment of new employees Your strengths:
  • Basic training as hotel specialist: EFZ or completed hotel school
  • 3 years of professional experience in the hotel industry
  • First leadership experience desirable
  • Good IT knowledge in MS-Office (Word, Excel, etc.)
  • Appropriate oral and written German skills as well as good oral and written English skills
  • User experience in PMS such as Protel, Fidelio or Opera
  • Detailed and service-oriented
  • Ready to learn and resilient
  • Hygiene-conscious
  • Structured and independent With us you will find:
  • And Guest Job
  • Dynamic working environment
  • Free space for ideas and creativity
  • Development margin
  • Corporate culture characterized by a sense of responsibility and trust If you want to share your passion for the hospitality industry with us, we look forward to your complete online application.
