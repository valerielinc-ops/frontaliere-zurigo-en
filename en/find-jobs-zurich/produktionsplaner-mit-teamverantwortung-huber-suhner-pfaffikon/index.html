Full description

Your area of responsibility

Would you like to actively shape production planning, further develop processes and lead a small team at the same time? In this versatile role, you will ensure reliable production planning and contribute to the continuous improvement of processes, systems and ways of working.

  • You will create the production program for the production facilities assigned to you and ensure a high level of delivery readiness
  • You will analyze bottlenecks and derive measures to improve delivery capability, manufacturing costs and throughput times
  • You will optimize processes, key figures and interfaces and actively drive digitalization.
  • You will lead, develop and coach a team of three shop floor planners

Your profile

  • You have experience in production planning in an industrial environment and have several years of management experience
  • You have basic technical training as well as further training in production planning, SCM, business administration or a comparable field
  • You are confident in using SAP as well as common IT tools such as Excel, Power BI, Minitab or Power Automate
  • You have in-depth knowledge of Lean and CIP principles as well as experience in project management
  • You work analytically, in a structured and solution-oriented manner, communicate stylistically in German and can communicate in English

Why HUBER+SUHNER?

  • Responsible area of responsibility and creative freedom for your own ideas
  • Up to 30 days of vacation – for your relaxation
  • Targeted training opportunities – we invest in you
  • Contribution to the Half-Fare travelcard – for your commute to work or spontaneous excursions
  • Strong pension and insurance benefits plus other attractive benefits
