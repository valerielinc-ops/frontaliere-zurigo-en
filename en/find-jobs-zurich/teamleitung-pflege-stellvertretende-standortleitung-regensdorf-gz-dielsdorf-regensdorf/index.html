Immediately or by agreement 8105 Regensdorf Onsite / on site 80% - 100% Permanent

About us

The Dielsdorf Health Center is a modern geriatric competence center with around 500 employees from 35 nations. Our offer ranges from counselling for older people and their relatives to general and specialized long-term care and care at home. At the Regensdorf site, we offer 54 residents a family environment with professional care.

Tasks

  • Ensuring high-quality, needs-oriented care and care as well as residential security
  • Management, promotion and evaluation of employees
  • Responsibility for staff, holiday and service planning as well as the introduction of new employees
  • Review of care quality and work performance as well as implementation of operational requirements and agreed goals
  • Ensuring appreciative cooperation with residents, relatives and internal and external stakeholders
  • Deputy site management of Regensdorf in professional, personnel and organizational matters

Profile

  • Completed training as a dipl. Care specialist HF / dipl. Nursing professional HF
  • At least seven years of professional experience as a dipl. Nursing professional
  • at least three years of leadership experience
  • Management experience is beneficial
  • Enjoyment in the management, promotion and development of employees
  • High level of technical, social and methodological competence
  • Networked thinking, organisational skills and assertiveness
  • Independent, structured and responsible working methods
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary cooperation and helping to shape change processes
  • Style-confident knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Good IT knowledge and secure handling of MS Office
  • Knowledge of Polypoint PEP is an advantage

Offer

A responsible task with room for manoeuvre in a varied, future-oriented and interprofessional environment. You benefit from attractive employment conditions: at least 5 weeks holidays, good social benefits, discounts at SBB and Reka checks, individual training opportunities, discounted food in our restaurants and free parking spaces.
