Master Data Specialist (100%) — Belimo

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

We are looking for a committed personality for our team at the headquarters in Hinwil. With us, you can expect a varied area of responsibility in the area of Master Data Management, in which you take responsibility for production and master data and actively participate in global projects. Look forward to a dynamic environment where your work has a direct impact on our processes and the cooperation in the supply chain.

What will you do

  • Creating, maintaining and managing material master data in the SAP system, including item data, parts lists and work plans in the material plant processes
  • Processing technical changes and global coordination of change cases along the supply chain
  • Performing calculation runs for the individual unit costs in SAP
  • Taxation and global coordination of material extinctions, inventory adjustments and liquidations
  • Monitor the product lifecycle as well as maintain and control the status network at the factory level for all material strains in the customizing centers of the region
  • Collaborating and partially assuming technical coordination in supply chain topics within projects as well as supporting global teams

Who you are

  • You have a bachelor’s degree, preferably with a focus on data, analytics (data science, data analytics, etc.) or business administration
  • You have several years of relevant professional experience in work or production planning as well as in master data maintenance
  • You have experience in project management and can independently coordinate projects
  • You are familiar with SAP, especially in the modules MM and PP
  • You have very good English skills in word and writing
  • You work structured, analytical and accurate, efficient and goal-oriented
  • You are a pleasant, communicative and team-oriented personality

Who we are

At Belimo, we are proud to make our company a rewarding place to work. Our leaders are value-driven and promote new ideas in their teams around the world. Through our passion, our employees not only create amazing products, but also revolutionize the HVAC industry. Investing in our employees is at the heart of Belimo when it comes to engaging diverse creative talents to build an industry-leading company. The success of our corporate brand is based on the competence and commitment of each individual.
