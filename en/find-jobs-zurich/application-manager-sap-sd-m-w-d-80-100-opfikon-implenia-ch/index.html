Full description

Are you looking for a new challenge in the IT sector and would like to contribute your SAP SD expertise in an exciting construction and real estate services environment? To strengthen our Group IT team, we are looking for you as a communication-strong application manager SAP SD (m/f/d). Your main focus is on the support, further development and optimization of our SAP sales processes. At the same time, you are open to getting to know and supervise other sales-related third-party applications in the construction industry and to further develop them together with the business. Your place of work is our headquarters in the Ambassador House in Opfikon, with the possibility to work partly in the home office. Tasks:

  • In this role, you take responsibility for the support, development and lifecycle management of sales applications, with a clear focus on SAP SD.
  • You will analyze requirements from the operational business units, evaluate them technically and professionally and translate them into viable solution concepts.
  • In addition, you create specifications, coordinate tests, accompany releases and ensure a stable application landscape together with internal and external partners.
  • You support the optimization and harmonization of sales processes in the SAP environment, especially in the areas of customer order, billing, interfaces and adjacent processes.
  • As a central interface, you act between business key users, operational business units, Group IT departments and external suppliers, work closely with 1rd-level support and external implementation partners and coordinate the sustainable solution of incidents, changes and minor developments.
  • You actively contribute your SAP SD depth, but at the same time you are ready to familiarize yourself with other third-party applications in the construction industry and take over their technical and technical support.
  • You contribute to application governance and help to further develop our system landscape in a value-added, stable and sustainable way. Requirements
  • You have completed a degree in computer science, business informatics, business administration with IT or a comparable qualification and bring several years of experience in application management, consulting or in the support of SAP SD processes.
  • You have in-depth knowledge of SAP SD, ideally including customizing understanding, process analysis, test coordination and interfaces to adjacent SAP modules.
  • You understand sales processes holistically, take requirements from the business structured and translate them into suitable IT solutions.
  • In addition to SAP SD, you will enjoy familiarizing yourself with other third-party applications, especially applications that support business processes in the construction industry.
  • You work analytically, solution-oriented and structured and keep track of several parallel topics.
  • You communicate securely with business stakeholders, IT colleagues and external partners and see yourself as a connecting interface between the department and IT.
  • You act customer-oriented, take responsibility and have a good sense of stable, pragmatic and sustainable solutions.
