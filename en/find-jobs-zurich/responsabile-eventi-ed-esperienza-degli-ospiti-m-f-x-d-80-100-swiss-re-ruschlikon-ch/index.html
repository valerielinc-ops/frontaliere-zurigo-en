Event & Guest Experience Manager (m/f/x/d; 80-100%) — Swiss Re

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Are you passionate about creating extraordinary experiences? Do you thrive in a dynamic environment where no two days are the same - sometimes you're greeting an international executive, sometimes you're coordinating a smoothly running conference? Then this is your stage.

Job Overview As Event & Guest Experience Manager, you will be responsible for the professional support of our international guests as well as the planning, organisation and implementation of events. You will accompany the entire guest journey process from arrival to departure and at the same time ensure the smooth implementation of events and conference services. As a host and event professional, you are an important business card for the Center of Global Dialogue. With your high level of service orientation, your organizational skills and your commitment, you create first-class experiences for guests, customers and event participants. The role is part of an integrated Event & Guest Experience organisation with the aim of ensuring that employees are flexibly deployed in both areas and represent each other. All employees take on reception as well as conference and event management tasks to ensure maximum flexibility, mutual representation and an outstanding guest experience.

Your tasks Guest Experience & Reception

  • Reception, support and advice for international guests, visitors and event participants in German and English.
  • Ensuring a professional and warm guest experience as the company's first point of contact and being responsible for reserving hotel rooms at the Center of Global Dialogue.
  • Ensuring smooth day-to-day business at the reception as well as support for the visitor management system and visitor registration, check-in and check-out
  • Flexible assumption of reception, reservation and guest service tasks within the framework of a cross-departmental deployment model, as a uniform process with the event tasks.

Conference & Event Management

  • Planning and organization as well as implementation of internal events.
  • Coordination of all relevant event services such as room planning, accommodation, catering, technology and logistics.
  • Processing of the entry gate for our new enquiries
  • Support of guests and seminar participants with administrative matters as well as referral to internal interfaces.

Professional competencies

  • Strong service and customer orientation.
  • Strong organizational skills and high level of personal responsibility.
  • Ability to coordinate multiple tasks at the same time and set priorities correctly.
  • Structured, solution-oriented and independent way of working.
  • Experience with Planet (Protel), Momentus (Ungerboeck), Visitor Registration Tools or comparable systems is an advantage.

About the team Swiss Re's event management team is part of Corporate Real Estate & Services (CRES) Zurich and plans and implements a wide range of events for internal and external clients across the company. The team adheres to Swiss Re's high quality standards and ensures that each event reflects our brand and values. Working closely with various business units and an established network of suppliers, the team operates with precision, creativity and a strong sense of service – making every event an experience to remember.

About you

You are a born host – someone who finds real joy in welcoming others, making them feel valued and well cared for. You bring energy and calm in equal measure, stay calm when things get hectic and find solutions before problems arise. You communicate confidently and cordially across cultures and hierarchies and are proud of the quality of your work. You value variety, adapt quickly and enjoy working in a collegial team where everyone is there for each other.

They are

  • Passionate hosts and create exceptional guest experiences.
  • Service, customer and solution-oriented, at the same time positive, committed and team-oriented.
  • Resilient, flexible and keep track of things even in hectic situations.
  • Communicative, professional and confident in dealing with different stakeholders in German and English.
  • Reliable, quality-conscious, responsible, discreet and loyal.
  • Open-minded to flexible working time models and close cross-team collaboration.
  • Enjoyment of a varied job with changing focuses between reception, hospitality and event management.
