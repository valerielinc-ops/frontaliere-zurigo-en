Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With more than 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric offer of Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in an outpatient, day clinic and inpatient setting. The Group employs around 1800 people and achieved a turnover of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For the area of psychotherapy and psychosomatics (station Pünt Nord) with treatment focus on psychotherapy for young adults we are looking for 1. November 2026 or by agreement

Station Pünt Nord specializes in the inpatient treatment of young adults with borderline disorders, trauma follow-up disorders, ADHD, depression, anxiety and obsessive-compulsive disorders. As a DBT and schema therapy station, the treatment team is trained on the one hand in dialectical behavioral therapy according to M. Linehan and on the other hand in schema therapy according to J. Young.

Their tasks Her core tasks include the individual, professional care and care of patients as a caregiver. They use nursing diagnostics as a working instrument, actively shape the therapeutic environment of the station and orient themselves in their way of working on the station concept. The coordinated and multidisciplinary cooperation enables our patients to achieve the best possible treatment success.

Your profile You have completed training for the Dipl. Nurse HF/FH (including SRK recognition for foreign diplomas), are interested in the psychiatric field and enjoy and understand young adults. As a team-capable, communicative and prudent personality, you are exactly right with us.

Our offer A pleasant, collegial and interdisciplinary working atmosphere awaits you.

  • We offer you an interesting and challenging task in a dynamic, modern environment with advanced employment conditions. It is important to us to carefully train new employees. In order to support you in your professional development, you will be supported by your direct supervisor as well as by specific training and further education.
