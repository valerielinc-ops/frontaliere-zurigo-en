Original description

Accountable for effectively handling allocated portfolio or assigned tasks while ensuring that deliverables are met (quality, turn-around time, and other defined performance indicators). Performs and/or oversees processing work performed by peers for the clients/accounts and/or processes assigned, providing guidance on reinsurance accounting matters as well as authorization and/or escalation of financial transactions and/or payments as per authority matrix, as well as escalate issues to management and/or senior management as per business judgment. Handles internal/external client relationships as per assignments.

  • New hires with little to no prior accounting experience
  • Basic account booking and oversight
  • Focus on learning and understanding portfolio
  • eAdmin (in addition to the above):
  • Basic error resolution
  • Monitor and track automation rates

Essential Responsibilities –describe the major responsibilities of this position in order of importance. Please include an estimate of the % of time spent on each responsibility

  • Exception based handling of accounts with focus on data quality and driving value through active application of client segmentation
  • Technical verification of accounts within the context of our validation framework and contract wording
  • Account Processing per established guidelines/processes which may include escalations to Claims Operations
  • Balance Settlement with External Clients according to reinsurance terms and agreements
  • Cash Flow Management: timely collection of Accounts Receivable, management of Financials within Portfolio assigned in collaboration with RLM Cash Team
  • Ensure that agreed priorities, deadlines and service standards are achieved
  • Achieve target KPIs (Key Performance Indicators)
  • Constantly look to enhance service standards and quality
  • Proactive investigation, analysis and interpretation of processed accounting information with root cause identification
  • Regular reporting to supervisor and internal stakeholders
  • Sharing information with other team members and working cross functionally, as needed
  • Data Quality control and risk management-related activities according to internal guidelines
  • Contact internal and external clients directly (written or verbal) to clarify and resolve operational issues such missing accounting information, incorrect data as identified by validation framework, payment delays, etc.

Requirements - basic criteria for successful completion of the position’s essential functions. Please include educational background and relevant job skills/experiences. List any licenses and or certifications required or preferred.

  • University degree preferably in the area of finance, accounting, economics or administration
  • Language skills: Fluent or Advanced comprehension and use of English (written, spoken, listening)
  • Business correspondence in English with internal and external clients via email, phone calls, and visits as required
  • Proactive exercise of critical thinking analysis and problem solving with root cause identification
  • Proficiency in new workflow technologies (ACORD, B3i, Robotics, etc.)
  • Demonstrate agility and navigate ambiguity to deliver on core expectations through change
  • Ability to work collaboratively in virtual/remote international/global/multicultural team environment
  • MS Office proficiency (Excel, Word, Power Point)
  • Strong analytical skills – detail oriented
  • Team player, willingness to share pertinent business information
