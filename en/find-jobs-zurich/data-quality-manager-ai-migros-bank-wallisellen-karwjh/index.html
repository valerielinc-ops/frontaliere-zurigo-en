Full description

With us, you actively shape the future in the field of data and artificial intelligence. You develop and operate the topic of data quality for our AI use cases. You identify potential to improve data quality and drive data value creation. We are looking for a personality who never loses sight of the essentials when immersed in concepts and codes.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Bank, Wallisellen, Industriestrasse 17-19, 8304 Wallisellen

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You analyze data quality issues, identify specific adjustment options and coordinate the implementation with the specialist areas.
  • Complex database structures and unstructured data formats are your domain and it is easy for you to translate business requests into code (e.g. SQL)
  • You lead projects in the field of data quality in the context of AI use cases and coordinate the implementation with internal and external resources
  • You measure and monitor data quality in applications, knowledge base and data lake

Requirements

  • At least 5 years: Data Quality Management / AI Engineering / Business Analysis
  • Master's degree (university, university of applied sciences): Computer science / computer science / economic informatics
  • Several years of experience in the field of Data Quality and AI
  • Experience in Data Warehousing (Databricks), Data Modeling, SQL ideally in the banking environment
  • Very good knowledge of modern data platform architectures (on-prem & cloud) as well as basic understanding of data protection / compliance.
  • Sound project experience from data and AI projects
  • Good communication and stakeholder management skills
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • English (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central place of work (GABS): Optimally accessible by train (10 minutes from Zurich HB) or car
  • Modern infrastructure: future-oriented and ergonomic workplace
  • Flexible place of work / Home Office: Individual possibilities for home office
  • Flexible working hours: annual working hours for all and trusted working hours for many
  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks for mothers and 4 weeks for fathers
  • Child care: up to CHF 300 per month per child to external child care
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Social benefits: Optimal coverage for sickness and accident "Private"
  • Migros Bank: accounts, mortgages and financial services with preferential terms
  • Employee offers: Annual generous well-being credit

Recruitment process

  • Personality/performance test: Under certain conditions, we use a personality test (BIP, Scales test, etc.) as an additional step to support selection. The results of the test may be discussed personally and form the basis for further selection. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Personal video or telephone conversation: For a large number of applicants, we optionally conduct a personal telephone or video conversation. This gives us the opportunity to get to know more candidates within a short time in order to invite suitable people for the next round. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Discussion with case presentation: Specific expertise is examined on the basis of a case or other assessment instruments. After processing, the result of the leader is presented and then discussed together. Usually, the team is also represented at the presentation. As a rule, the conversations are conducted by the superior, without a representative from the HR. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Reference information: When obtaining references, we ask for impressions, strengths and weaknesses again and have them verified by former supervisors or employees. References can be obtained after the first or second round and are only made with consent. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
