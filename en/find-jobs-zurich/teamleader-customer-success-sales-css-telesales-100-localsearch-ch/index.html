Original description

localsearch is the leading marketing and advertising partner for Swiss SMEs. We make customers successful in the digital world.

We are sales experts, entrepreneurs and digitalisation pioneers. With us, you work in a performance-oriented sales environment where consulting, entrepreneurial thinking and strong degrees are the focus.

Our Customer Success Sales (CSS) team helps Swiss SMEs improve their online visibility and attract new customers. With digital marketing solutions from localsearch, we accompany companies on their way to the digital future.

For the leadership and further development of our team, we are not looking for a pure administrative force, but an experienced leader who inspires people, demands performance and promotes potential, takes responsibility and takes action in everyday operations.

Your mission

You develop a dedicated sales team into a powerful unit and combine clear goals and consistent performance management with real proximity to the people. You are present in day-to-day business, coach on the basis of real customer conversations and create an environment in which employees develop, take responsibility and achieve success together.

You do not wait for ready-made solutions for challenges. You analyze the causes, develop concrete measures and implement them together with your team.

Your responsibility

  • You lead and develop a team of sales agents in telephone B2B sales and are visible, responsive and close to the team in everyday operations.
  • You coach in daily business, regularly listen to customer meetings, provide direct feedback and translate your observations into concrete development goals.
  • You establish clear leadership rhythms: daily control formats, one-on-one meetings, team coachings and performance reviews.
  • You control sales campaigns for cross selling, upselling and new customer acquisition.
  • You monitor sales, productivity, conversion and quality based on defined KPIs, ensure reliable planning and forecasting and initiate targeted measures at an early stage in the event of deviations.
  • You hold regular one-on-one meetings, recognize strengths and areas of development and work out concrete development plans together with your employees.
  • You tackle performance problems early, fairly and consistently, promote personal responsibility and develop service providers and future managers.
  • You will accompany recruitment, onboarding and sustainable integration of new team members.
  • You work closely with marketing, product management, sales and human resources.
  • You ensure high quality of customer conversations and professional sales processes and use Salesforce for control and performance analysis.

Skills and requirements

You are

  • An experienced leader with real pleasure in people and sales. You can demand performance without losing sight of people.
  • Several years and proven experience in the management of performance-oriented sales or contact center teams, ideally in the B2B or SME business, with proven success in sales and performance goals.
  • Strong in coaching, motivation and feedback, with high emotional and social competence. You meet your employees with appreciation and at the same time are clear and consistent.
  • Energetic and operationally present. You get involved yourself and do not lead exclusively on meetings, reporting and key figures.
  • Result-oriented and familiar with the management of sales teams based on clear KPIs, analytical, structured and solution-oriented, capable of acting even in demanding situations.
  • Experience in dealing with top performers, employees with development needs and demanding personnel situations, and can successfully convey change.
  • Digitally affinity, entrepreneurial and interested in marketing solutions for Swiss SMEs.
  • Stylistic in German with a very good understanding of Swiss German. French or Italian are a plus, experience with Salesforce beneficial.

This is what we offer you

  • Remuneration directly related to your success: In addition to your fixed salary, you benefit from a variable share that is measured by the results of your team. How you lead and develop your team is directly reflected in your compensation.
  • Exciting career opportunity: a central role for a market leader in digital marketing for Swiss SMEs, with room for manoeuvre for leadership rhythms, coaching, performance measures and operational processes.
  • Modern office in Zurich:

mainly on site with your team, two home office days per week possible. Personal presence is an important part of this leadership role.

  • Responsibility: for results, leadership, employee development and strong collaboration in a dedicated sales team.
  • Dynamic team: open corporate culture, strong team spirit, clear performance mentality. Humanity and performance orientation are not a contradiction for us.
