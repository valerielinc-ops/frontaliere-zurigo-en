Full description

movemi AG is the leading company in the Swiss fitness industry. For our fitness park we are looking for active support in the area of massage. Become part of a holistic and successful training philosophy: fitness combined with offers for relaxation and nutrition.

Luogo di lavoro: Fitness Park, Fitness Park Regensdorf, Im Zentrum 3, 8105 Regensdorf

Mansioni

  • You perform professional massages
  • You advise and look after our guests
  • You give a relaxed atmosphere
  • You actively promote the massage offers
  • You ensure cleanliness and order

Requirements

  • 1–3 years of experience: As a masseur
  • Basic or continuing education: Completed or ongoing training as a masseur with health insurance recognition
  • You have additional qualifications in Hot Stone, reflex zones or other wellness massages
  • You are ready to work in the evening as well as on weekends and holidays
  • You are attentive, resilient and service-oriented
  • You value a well-groomed appearance
  • in German (negotiation confident)

Cosa of friamo

  • Central location: Our studios are located in a well-developed central location
  • Mobile subscription: National flat subscription from CHF 10.– / month (Swisscom /digitec connect)
  • Migros Bank: expense-free private and investment account with preferential interest
  • Employee offers: Various discounts in the area of holidays and insurance (Hertz up to 10%, Generali 25%, Axa insurance up to 15%, etc.)
  • Du-culture: We stand for a respectful coexistence at eye level

Contatto

Application and contact Laura Swiss HR Business Partner

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We continuously review applications received, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Expert Discussion: In a specialist discussion, specific knowledge relevant for the execution of the job is requested by the manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, the team. If possible, there is time to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition: After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
