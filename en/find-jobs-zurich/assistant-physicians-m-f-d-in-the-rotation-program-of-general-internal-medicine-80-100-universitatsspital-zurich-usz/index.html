Full description

USZ Campus immediately

Their main tasks

  • Care of inpatient and outpatient internist patients under supervision of a cadre doctor
  • Conduct of consultancy services under the supervision of a cadre practitioner
  • Medical reporting and implementation of administrative activities
  • Participation in reports and Journal Club
  • Introduction of newly entering assistant doctors

When working at the Polyclinic on the Circle (ambulatory time):

  • Care of outpatient internist patients in the medical polyclinic: in the general consultation hour, in the special consultation hour for obesity and hypertension, for inflammation and rare disease as well as in the personnel medical service (PAD)

Additional rotation in prison possible

  • Care of outpatient internist patients at the Zurich District Prison (BGZ)
  • Care of outpatient internist patients at Pöschwies prison

Your profile

  • One to two years of experience in general internal medicine in a Swiss hospital. See also https://www.usz.ch/fachfeld/inner-medizin/ueber-us/facharztausbildung/
  • Good qualifications and positive reference, ideally from a hospital of continuing education category A or B
  • Knowledge of German (verbal and written) Minimum C1 according to EU standards as well as recognition of your education and language by MEBEKO https://www.bag.admin.ch/en/geantrag-einliefer-fuer-auslaendische-abluesse-der-medizinal-und-psychologyberufe
  • They have a quick comprehension, are a team player and keep a calm head and overview even in hectic situations.
