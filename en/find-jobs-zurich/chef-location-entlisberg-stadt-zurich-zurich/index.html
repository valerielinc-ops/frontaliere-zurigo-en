Full description

Do you think kitchen further - as a professional production company, as an important part of the quality of life and as a place where people do great things together?

You have several years of leadership experience in a larger catering organization, combine culinary quality with entrepreneurial thinking and want to actively develop a demanding area?

Then you are right with us

You would like to work immediately or by agreement with our team in the catering for the health center for the old Entlisberg?

Tasks

  • They ensure a high-quality, balanced and target-group-oriented catering offer for our residents, employees and guests at several locations in the districts of Wollishofen, Leimbach and Enge.
  • They lead, accompany and develop a team of around 30 employees, learners and interns and promote independent, competent and appreciative cooperation.
  • You take over the technical, personnel and business management of the catering division, are responsible for budget, key figures, resource planning and procurement and develop the division forward-looking and economically.
  • They design all catering processes – from planning to production and logistics to quality assurance – review existing processes, set new impulses and consistently drive the further development of standards and working methods.
  • You organize events and promote a lively host culture together with your team

Profile

  • Several years of management and professional experience as a chef in a larger and demanding catering company, preferably in the health care sector and with cross-location responsibility, ideally supplemented by a further qualification (e.g. chef with a federal diploma) as well as further training in leadership, business administration or change management
  • In-depth knowledge in community gastronomy, large-scale production in the cook & chill process, various diets as well as in hygiene, food safety and quality assurance
  • A strong business understanding and security in dealing with budgets, key figures and resources as well as a structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Modern, clear and appreciative leadership as well as communication strength, decision-making and a convincing appearance
  • Real interest in the people we work for every day

We offer

We support the reconciliation of private and professional life, with attractive working hours. Look forward to a responsible leadership task with creative leeway in a professional and team-oriented working environment with a du-culture throughout the company. In this role, you can actively shape the future of our organization by effectively contributing your experience to change and further development processes.

As part of the city of Zurich and due to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, progressive employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

Depending on the experience, the annual salary for this function is between CHF 103'184.- and CHF 137'875.- (100%).

About us

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

The Health Center for Old Entlisberg in Wollishofen in Kreis 2 combines various care and care services under one roof. Food, host culture and a comfortable atmosphere make an important contribution to the quality of life of our residents. In this role, you work closely with different professional groups and actively participate in the catering in a large house with around 500 colleagues.
