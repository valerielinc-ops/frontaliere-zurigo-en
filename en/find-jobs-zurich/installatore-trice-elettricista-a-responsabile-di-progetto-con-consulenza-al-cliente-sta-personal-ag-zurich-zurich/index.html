Full description

Project management for electrical installations includes planning, execution and monitoring of works. The company offers you an innovative environment with varied tasks and direct customer contact. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Managing and coordinating electrical installation projects including planning and execution
  • Monitoring of assembly work and coordination with the site management
  • Creating and editing offers and supplementary offers
  • Acquisition of new orders as well as advice and support for customers
  • Managing project controlling and ensuring timely delivery to customers

Requirements

  • Completed vocational training as electrical installer EFZ or electrical planner EFZ
  • Several years of practical experience in the realization of building technology projects
  • Swiss Federal Professional Card as electrical project manager with management and expertise
  • Very good knowledge of MS-Office and secure application of digital tools
  • Self-employed, structured working methods, a high sense of responsibility and strong social skills

Offer

  • Modern, innovative working environment with direct customer contact
  • Varied tasks with attractive social benefits
  • Extensive training opportunities for professional and personal development
