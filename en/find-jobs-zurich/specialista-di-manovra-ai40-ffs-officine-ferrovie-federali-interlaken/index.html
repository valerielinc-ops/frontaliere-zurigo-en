Shunting specialist: in Ai40 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Shunting specialist: in Ai40 Zurich, immediately or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. Around 14,000 passenger transport employees ensure that the more than 1.3 million SBB customers we transport every day have easy access to public transport and travel from A to B both safely and on time. As a central professional group, the shunting personnel make a key contribution to this. An innovative working environment awaits you, in which you will move SBB together with your teammates, as well as varied and paid training. You also have various internal training opportunities, such as training to become a shunting engine driver. Attractive working conditions are important to us, even with irregular working hours. We pay additional wages for night and weekend work

  • e.g. 16 francs per hour for Sunday work. For night shifts you will receive additional time surcharges of between 10 and 30%. This means you have more free time than employees without night and weekend shifts. You can move that.
  • As a shunting specialist, you will help ensure that our customers travel reliably and on time by moving, forming and dismantling passenger trains according to the train formation plan.
  • You lead individual shunting movements and reset train compositions.
  • Your responsibility for train preparation includes preheating and air conditioning the trains as well as carrying out train inspections and brake tests.
  • You report any irregularities, special incidents or deviations to the responsible authority.
  • Due to your safety awareness, it is natural for you to work according to regulations. You can find out more about the job profile here. This is what you bring with you.
  • You are attentive, concentrated and physically resilient in order to successfully complete this demanding work in 24-hour shifts. You also enjoy working outside in any weather.
  • You are already a trained shunting specialist in Ai40.
  • Since all instructions and documentation are electronic, you will quickly find your way around using digital tools.
  • You are fluent in German (level C2).
  • You have a perfect sense of color (no red-green weakness).
