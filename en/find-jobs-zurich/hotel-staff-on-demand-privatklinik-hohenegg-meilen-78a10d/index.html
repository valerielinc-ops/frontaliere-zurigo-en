Full description

Hohenegg Private Clinic is a specialist clinic with 95 beds for psychiatry, psychotherapy and psychosomatics. It is open and accessible to private, semi-private and general insured persons from Switzerland as well as self-payers. It is housed in a listed magnificent complex, in the middle of nature and high above Lake Zurich. In addition to inpatient services, we operate a mental health centre (ZPG) for outpatient and hospital-associated psychiatry at two locations. The sponsorship is a charitable foundation. With us, you can contribute yourself and your proven professional skills to a coherent, meaningful working environment. We are looking for well-qualified and socially competent employees who are passionate about their profession. We are looking for committed and reliable support for our cleaning team immediately or by agreement. Their tasks You are responsible for the thorough cleaning and care of our guest rooms so that our guests feel comfortable all around. In addition, you ensure that corridors and common areas remain tidy and inviting. They support our cleaning team and contribute to a harmonious and efficient workflow. Your profile Ideally, they have an education or well-founded professional experience in the cleaning sector. In addition, you like the contact with different people and meet them friendly and courteous. They have a quick understanding, are flexible, helpful, dutiful, work independently and appreciate a dynamic environment. In addition, you can be used flexibly in the period 06:46 to 15:40 and bring the willingness to support our team reliably in the daily process. Their advantages
