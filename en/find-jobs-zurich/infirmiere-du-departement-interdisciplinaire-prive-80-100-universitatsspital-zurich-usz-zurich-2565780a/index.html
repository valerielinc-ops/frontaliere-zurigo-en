immediately or by agreement

You can expect a versatile and responsible work in a modern, interdisciplinary working environment. You support patients from the fields of angiology, cardiac surgery, cardiology, pneumology, thoracic surgery and vascular surgery and bring your nursing competence independently into everyday work. In order for you to find your way around the various areas of expertise, we attach great importance to a careful and structured introduction. You will be accompanied and supported by a motivated and committed team.

Here you are asked

  • Individual care and support on our interdisciplinary private department in tandem with a FaGe
  • Responsibility for the care process and quality of care
  • Telemetry monitoring and early detection of changes
  • Co-responsibility for departmental affairs and interprofessional cooperation
  • Accompaniment and promotion of learners and students
  • Active participation in our processes and team culture. Your ideas are always welcome

This will convince us

  • Completed training as a qualified nurse / qualified nurse HF/FH
  • Acute professional experience
  • Flexible and resilient in a dynamic environment
  • Self-employed and reliable working methods
  • Enjoyment of the care profession and high willingness to provide services

Health

We support your health. In addition to personal consultations and courses of our health management that promote your well-being, we offer you access to over 120 sports with a subsidized annual subscription to the ASVZ.

Flexibility

We promote a balanced work-life balance: Almost half of our employees work part-time, and about 50% of the tasks can be done in the home office – if your working day allows it.

Family friendliness

We help you to reconcile family and work. Mothers are entitled to at least 6 months of maternity leave (of which 16 weeks are paid in full), fathers to at least 2 months of paternity leave (of which 2 weeks are paid in full).

Further education and development

We accompany you on your professional path and support your development with individual career and further education opportunities.

Insurance

We cover 60% of your pension fund contributions and offer attractive special conditions for supplementary insurance.

Mobility

Telephony
