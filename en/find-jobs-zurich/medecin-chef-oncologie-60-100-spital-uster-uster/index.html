Full description

To complement the oncology team, which performs approximately 5,000 consultations and 3,000 chemotherapies annually across the spectrum of solid and hematological neoplasms, we are looking for an immediate or mutually agreed upon

  • Responsibilities
  • you are responsible, together with a chief physician and a senior physician, for the care of oncology patients and ensure the highest quality of care
  • you run an outpatient clinic for solid and, if necessary, hemato-oncological neoplasms
  • you support colleagues in the care of hospitalized oncology patients and work closely with other departments and external partners
  • you participate in the oncology consultation service and, to a lesser extent, in the weekend internal medicine service
  • you train medical assistants and promote a learning atmosphere for the entire medical team
  • Requirements
  • FMH Oncology or equivalent qualification
  • FMH Internal Medicine or equivalent qualification

  • empathetic and communicative personality
  • resilience, high social skills, and enjoyment in teaching assistant physicians and nursing staff
  • What we offer

  • the opportunity to actively contribute to oncology at the hospital and in the region
  • min. 5 weeks of vacation
  • an in-house daycare, excellent catering staff, and attractive benefits with various "brands"
  • an attractive salary and professional development opportunities
  • a central location near Uster station and with access to the highway
