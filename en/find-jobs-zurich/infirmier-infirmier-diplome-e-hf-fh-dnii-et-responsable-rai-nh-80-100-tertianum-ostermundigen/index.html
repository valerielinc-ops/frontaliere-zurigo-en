Full description

Your heart beats for the elderly? Is nursing your calling? Do you see opportunities to meaningfully apply your skills and experiences with us to have a positive impact on the lives of our clients? Then you are perfect for us!

For the Tertianum Lindenhof in Orpund, we are looking for a

Dipl. Nurse HF/FH/DNII as RAI LTCF Responsible 70-100%

Your tasks

  • You are responsible, among other things, for ensuring the correct implementation of the RAI system according to legal and contractual bases
  • You monitor the RAI-LTCF-based care process in our institution and check it randomly
  • You coach and guide all persons involved in the care process with RAI-LTCF appropriately
  • You conduct internal RAI-relevant training
  • You check the correctness and traceability of the MDS codings relevant to the care effort groups based on the nursing documentation
  • You actively participate in quality management in the interpretation of the RAI quality indicators

Your profile

  • Completed training at the tertiary level (HF or FH)
  • Several years of professional experience in long-term care
  • Completion of the RAI LTCF expert course is an advantage (at least supervisor training)
  • In-depth knowledge of RAI-LTCF and interest in actively developing this area in Tertianum Lindenhof
  • Understanding of nursing financing and performance billing
  • Very good IT user skills (MS-Office, RAI-Soft and CareCoach)
  • Enthusiasm for coaching, guiding, and accompanying employees
  • Efficient, solution- and process-oriented way of working
  • An open, appreciative attitude and enjoyment of working in an interdisciplinary team
