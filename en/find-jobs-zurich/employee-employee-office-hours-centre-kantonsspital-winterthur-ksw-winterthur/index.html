This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Our three office hours centres are the central service providers within surgery. In each of 25 examination rooms, patients are looked after by various surgical clinics and other outpatient areas. For the office hours we are looking for 1. November 2026 or by agreement a dedicated, service- and solution-oriented personality. Together with our small, motivated team, you will have the opportunity to contribute your professional and human qualities in a diverse and interesting working environment. You can expect a careful introduction to all tasks and our various processes. We also offer you a modern workplace in a lively hospital environment. We look forward to seeing you!

Tasks

  • Together with your employees in the team, you are responsible for the smooth operation of the office centre.
  • Reception and care of patients of the special and private consultation hours
  • Recording and management of patient data and coordination of preliminary examinations
  • Organisation, replenishment and ordering of medical materials and medicines
  • Administrative support for patients and active participation in process optimization
  • Introduction of new employees

Profile

  • You have an education as a receptionist or a similar professional background
  • Ideally, you already have experience in healthcare
  • A patient-oriented approach is a matter of course for you
  • You are an organizational talent and have an overview at all times
  • You have a reliable and exact working method as well as good IT application knowledge
  • You have very good knowledge of German, other foreign language skills are an advantage
  • It gives you pleasure to rotate in the three office hours centers
  • You are an empathetic, committed and solution-oriented personality

Benefits: Work and private life should fit together. Therefore, the KSW offers you – where the company makes it possible – flexible and smooth working hours, home office, generous maternity leave, paternity leave and employment levels that can be adapted to different phases of life. Develop yourself professionally and personally: Every year, around 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses are open to you at KSW. We also support external training – temporally and/or financially.

  • Sometimes it needs support. In the event of personal stress, prolonged illness or difficult experiences in your professional or private life, various confidential discussions are available at KSW.
  • At KSW we work together and for each other. Appreciation, open and respectful communication and cooperation across professional groups and departments shape our everyday work. You should be able to contribute with your ideas and strengths and develop.

Your future is also ensured: You benefit from very good social benefits and a pension fund solution with BVK, which is significantly above the legal minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, you are very well covered as a KSW employee.

  • Your health is important to us. Benefit from discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training and courses as well as discounts in pharmacies and selected health insurance companies.
  • Family and work can be well connected with us. The daycare center La Luna with spacious opening hours is directly next to the KSW. In addition, the KSW supports you – depending on income – financially with the crib costs.

There is also something to discover outside of work: Thanks to our cooperation with cultural partners in Winterthur and the surrounding area, you regularly benefit from discounts for concerts and theatre performances.

  • Professional field: Administration / ICT / Process design and digitalization
  • Area: Care surgery
  • Pensum: 80-100 %
