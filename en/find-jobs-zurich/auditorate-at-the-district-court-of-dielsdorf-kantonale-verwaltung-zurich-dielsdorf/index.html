Full description

An auditorate at the Dielsdorf District Court allows students to enter the legal system. It takes a net year and is credited as a practical year in accordance with the admission conditions for the examination. Auditorate at the Dielsdorf District Court (100%) Your area of responsibility The Dielsdorf District Court employs nine auditors. As part of the auditorate, you are assigned to all areas of activity for which a district court in the canton of Zurich is responsible. By participating in negotiations, partly independent case handling and the editorial board of decisions, you will gain insight into civil law (e.g. claims including labour disputes, family and rental law), criminal law as well as debt recovery and bankruptcy law. Our ten positions as court clerks and face writers are regularly filled by our own auditors. They thus have the prospect of a longer activity at the Dielsdorf district court and a career in Zurich's justice. Your profile

  • Admission requirements according to § 2 AudVO:
  • Completed legal studies;
  • Very good knowledge of the official language;
  • Good reputation (criminal register extract);
  • Capability of action (certificate of ability to act)
  • Applications are accepted before the official graduation ceremony, provided that the application is accompanied by a confirmation of the university or a sheet of music, which shows that all required examination subjects and the master thesis have been successfully completed. Our offer
  • A varied daily work day with many contacts
  • Highly qualified environment with many young colleagues
  • Access to exclusive training and further education events
  • Planable professional career and unpaid leave for the attorney's examination Send your application including documents (in particular CV, Bachelor's and Master's degree with sheet, criminal record extract, certificate of effectiveness) online.The Dielsdorf District Court regularly fills auditor posts throughout the year. Please indicate in the application when you wish to take up the position. In addition to the planable positions, short-term employment opportunities always arise. For any questions and further information, please contact the leading court clerk Mlaw Nora Michel or her deputy MLaw Manuela Burkard (Tel. 044 854 88 11).Dielsdorf District Court Court The Dielsdorf District Court is a first-instance civil and criminal court with about 40 employees. Approximately 3'500 proceedings are processed per year.
