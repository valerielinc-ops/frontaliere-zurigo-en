Company and context

  • You carry the personnel, technical and organizational overall responsibility for the department and lead the six team leaders directly.
  • You continuously develop the department and set targeted impulses.
  • You ensure the timely and equal assessment of the approximately 300,000 hired or unemployed taxpayers in the area of Zurich South.
  • For economically important or politically sensitive tax assessments, you support your employees and contribute your professional expertise.
  • As a member of the management team of the private sector, you are actively involved in the further development of the sector and the cantonal tax office.
  • You are involved in working groups as well as IT and organizational projects and take on internal and external speaker activities.
  • Together with the municipality department, you maintain contact and cooperation with the municipal tax offices in the southern part of the canton. What do you bring with you
  • You have a higher commercial training or a completed legal or business studies university.
  • You will bring several years of professional experience as well as in-depth knowledge of non-commercial tax law and the various processes in the assessment procedure.
  • You have proven leadership experience and understand how to lead leaders effectively and strengthen their leadership role.

Additional details

  • May 2027 or by arrangement a new management for the Zurich South department with six teams and a total of 53 employees.
  • Together with the municipality department, you maintain contact and cooperation with the municipal tax offices in the southern part of the canton. What do you bring with you
  • Even in challenging situations, you keep an overview and represent the interests of the cantonal tax office sovereignly.
