Training dipl. Nursing professional/woman HF (3 years regular) September 2027 ZAG

100%

Patient-oriented, eager to learn, solution-oriented, empathetic, accurate and flexible - do these values apply to you? Then apply to us – we look forward to you!

The dipl. nurse HF

  • cares for, accompanies and supports acutely ill people of different socio-cultural backgrounds in their individual phase of life
  • is responsible for planning, implementing and evaluating the entire care process
  • carries out health-promoting, life-sustaining, rehabilitative and palliative nursing activities
  • works closely with the intra-/interprofessional team and with relatives

You bring with you

  • Successfully passed aptitude assessment at ZAG, Winterthur
  • Multicheck® Competence Analysis Health HF
  • Higher secondary education, i.e.:
  • Professional qualification with EFZ
  • Specialist secondary school qualification (FMS)
  • Gymnasium maturity
  • Communication and relationship skills
  • Flexibility
  • Analysis and organisational capacity
  • Crosslinking capacity
  • Physical and psychological resilience
  • Learning and performance

The Cantonal Hospital Münsterlingen introduces itself

The cantonal hospitals Frauenfeld & Münsterlingen are part of Spital Thurgau AG, a subsidiary of the thurmed Group. Our acute care hospitals serve around 32,000 inpatients every year and offer a comprehensive range of services in a high-quality infrastructure. Spital Thurgau is one of the largest and most successful hospital groups in Switzerland and has around 4100 employees at various company locations. We offer attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!

During your training, you will be expertly accompanied, supported and supported by motivated education and nursing teams. We attach great importance to a learning environment that takes the best possible account of the networking of theory and practice. For this we offer versatile learning arrangements.
