Laboratory of Neuroepigenetics, Brain Research Institute and Institute of Neuroscience, UZH/ETHZ. The laboratory of Neuroepigenetics is a biology research group at the University Zürich (UZH) and ETH Zürich. It conducts fundamental research on how the brain and body are modified by environmental factors and life experiences and the consequences for physiology, behavior and health. The lab is composed of researchers, doctoral and postdoctoral students, and technical staff working in close collaboration on complementary research projects. The lab provides teaching to undergraduate and graduate students at UZH and ETHZ.
