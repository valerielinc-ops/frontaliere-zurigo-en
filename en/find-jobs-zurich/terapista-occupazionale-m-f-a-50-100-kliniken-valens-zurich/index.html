Far-sightedness for rehab and my career – that is the lifestyle and working feeling at the clinic group Valens. Exchange, progress, interdisciplinary teamwork and good prospects are important here.

Our new Zurich Triemli rehabilitation clinic will open in spring 2027. At the beginning we will operate a station with about 32 beds, rented in the city hospital Zurich Triemli. Our new building is already under construction and will open at the end of 2029 and have about 140 beds. For this exciting operational setup, we are looking for a Tasks: • You accompany and treat our patients with neurological, internistic, oncological and musculoskeletal diseases in everyday rehab – in a modern, interdisciplinary organizational form • You carry out evidence-based ergotherapeutic treatments and assessments independently and professionally • You actively participate in interdisciplinary meetings as well as further education and training • The close and interprofessional cooperation with our committed rehabilitation team is part of your everyday life • You actively shape our internal professional development processes Requirements • You have successfully completed your training or studies as an occupational therapist • You have already been granted SRC recognition (we support international applicants for recognition) • Your way of working is patient-centered and evidence-based • Your strengths include excellent team and communication skills • Flexibility and resilience set you apart in a dynamic working environment • Assignments on weekends and public holidays you will gladly and reliably take over • You have very good knowledge of German; English skills are beneficial • New technologies such as robotics, VR and exergames you meet with openness and interest • The following qualifications are advantageous: • Neurorehabilitation-specific training in concepts of motor learning, task-specific and repetitive training • Experience in the treatment of patients subject to supervision We offer: • Prospective and easy-to-reach workplace – in a collegial environment with clear structures, competent managers and appreciative communication • Real benefits instead of empty words – with paid changing time, 28 days of holidays as well as numerous internal and external discounts and offers. Convince yourself. • Further education that advances you – internal and external training, including through our Rehab Academy, the group-wide training center • Diversity as strength – We rely on thinking, self-confident and critical employees with different experiences, qualifications and perspectives, because we are convinced that this will bring us all forward.
