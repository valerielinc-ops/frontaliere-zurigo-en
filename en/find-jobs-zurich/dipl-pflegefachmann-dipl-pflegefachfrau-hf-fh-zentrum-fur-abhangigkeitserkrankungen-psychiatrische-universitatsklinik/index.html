Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 80-100% For our Center for Dependency Diseases in Zurich, we are looking for a Their tasks

  • Planning and implementation of the care process within the framework of the reference person system
  • Participation in a multimodal, interdisciplinary psychiatric treatment
  • Design and implementation of treatment offers such as group management, day structure, leisure activities
  • Provides all services incl. Night and weekend service
  • Assistance in the training of learners and students in the department

Your profile

  • Completed, recognised vocational training in the field of care HF/FH, (certificate of recognition of the Swiss Red Cross for foreign diplomas)
  • A few years of professional experience are beneficial
  • High level of technical, social and self-competence and a high level of resilience and flexibility
  • High motivation and ability to engage in a dynamic and exciting work environment

Our offer

  • Development with perspective: Diverse career paths in one of the largest psychiatric clinics in Switzerland
  • Knowledge that advances: Extensive initial, further and further training offers
  • Together for people: Interdisciplinary cooperation for holistic psychiatric care
  • Strong in the team: appreciative cooperation and mutual support
  • Working flexibly: working time models that fit your life
  • More time for relaxation: at least 25 days of holiday and possibility for unpaid leave
