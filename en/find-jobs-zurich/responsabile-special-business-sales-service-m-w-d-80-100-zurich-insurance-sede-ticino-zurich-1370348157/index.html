Full description

Are you motivated by the idea of leading a specialized team and developing demanding life insurance, pension and special solutions together with sales?

As Head of Special Business & Sales Service, you are responsible for the professional and strategic development of the division and ensure professional support for our sales partners.

You combine leadership, sales and sound life know-how. With individual offers, special rates and a high quality of service, you help to meet complex customer needs and promote profitable growth.

Find out here why Zurich Switzerland is one of the "Top 3" employers in Switzerland. Become part of it and apply as Head of Special Business & Sales Service.

What you do

  • You will lead the Special Business & Sales Service team professionally and in terms of personnel and continuously develop employees, processes, services and standards.
  • You control and prioritize complex customer and sales inquiries and ensure high service quality and compliance with the agreed service levels.
  • You support sales in large-volume and complex life insurance transactions and develop individual offer and tariff solutions taking into account customer needs and profitability.
  • You are responsible for management tariffs and special offers and coordinate complex solutions with pricing, underwriting, compliance, reinsurance and other specialist areas.
  • You will advise sales partners on demanding customer cases, impart knowledge on products and precautionary solutions and coordinate offering and policy processes up to the conclusion of the contract.

What you bring

  • Several years of leadership experience in the insurance or pension environment as well as strong leadership, coaching and development skills
  • In-depth knowledge of Swiss life insurance and pension products as well as in-depth tariff and pricing know-how in individual life
  • Proven sales experience in advising demanding private customers and intermediaries and a clear focus on customer orientation and profitability
  • Very good knowledge of pension planning and asset succession as well as good knowledge of Swiss tax law and the social security framework
  • Convincing communication and negotiation skills in German and English
  • French is an advantage
