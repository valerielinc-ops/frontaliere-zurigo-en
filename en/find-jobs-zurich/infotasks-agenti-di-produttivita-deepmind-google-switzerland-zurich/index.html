About the job Artificial intelligence will be one of humanity’s most transformative inventions. At DeepMind, we are a pioneering AI lab with exceptional interdisciplinary teams focused on advancing AI development to solve complex global challenges and accelerate high-quality product innovation for billions of users. We use our technologies for widespread public benefit and scientific discovery, ensuring safety and ethics are always our highest priority.

We are pushing the boundaries across multiple domains. Our global teams offer diverse learning opportunities and varied career pathways for those driven to achieve exceptional results through collective effort.

Responsibilities

  • Build and optimize multi-agent hierarchical pipelines and cross-application reasoning agents for productivity agents.
  • Develop, scale, and maintain automated evaluation raters to assess model capabilities under complex, real-world conditions.
  • Maintain secure execution environments (Python Workspace API harnesses, Bash/gws CLI) to evaluate agent trajectories hermetically, and allow for large-scale distillation and training in the environments.
