Full description

Would you like to start your career in one of the best retail programs in Switzerland and run your own branch within 9-12 months? 💪

Then apply for our Store Manager Trainee Program! 🙋 What we offer you

You will get the chance to join a retail company that wants to be the first choice of candidates as an employer. As a JYSK Ambassador you get the following advantages:

  • A five-week trainee program with interactive training on all the important aspects of JYSK branch leadership, accompanied by experienced sales and HR experts who train and coach you.
  • An experienced mentor from the organization is available to you and your development needs
  • During the program, you will be used as deputy branch management and thus assume responsibility from the beginning
  • Possibilities for own development and personal responsibility
  • Above-average sales bonus for the entire team
  • 20% discount at JYSK and other Lars Larsen Group companies
  • Top pension funds services
  • 5 weeks of vacation, which increases every 5 years of service
  • Further advantages such as an above-average accident insurance, various activities (anniversary, team events) and great anniversary gifts

Why is it in our trainee program?

Our program offers high-quality training in leadership, management and branch operations, combined with practical experience and theoretical knowledge. As a rule, you work and learn in close cooperation with other trainees and your mentor.

  • Five modules of one week each: theoretical knowledge, case and role plays, group work, team building and plenary discussions
  • In between modules, you will be trained with an assigned Store Manager Mentor in the workplace and receive coaching.
  • After completing the modules, a celebration awaits you to celebrate the completion of the program

The ultimate goal of the Store Manager Trainee Program is to prepare you to run your own branch in the near future. What you should bring with you

  • Commitment to your and the development of your colleagues
  • You communicate in a motivating and inclusive way and are able to create a positive working environment
  • Ideally, you already have experience in the leadership, motivation and development of a team
  • You are results-oriented and have a passion for retailing
  • You have the ability to stay calm and purposeful even in stressful situations
  • You feel in a dynamic working environment in constant change
  • You like physical work, have a hands-on mentality and lead by example
  • You are flexible and willing to work on Saturdays

Give yourself today.
