Full description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. We are committed to our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive private brands, professional specialist advice and innovative services.

Transgourmet Schweiz AG is a member of the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in Swiss wholesale and pick-up wholesale and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retail).

Tasks

  • You can put your tour together independently using the Touring Boardeau and shake it off in tag ready.
  • You load your vehicle temperature-appropriate and ensure the load professionally.
  • You control the deliveries using the delivery notes – everything must be right.
  • You run out of goods professionally and take back empty goods as well as any returns.
  • You recognize customer needs on the go and forward them internally.
  • You will take care of your vehicle and take on further tasks after consultation.

Requirements

  • You own the guide card Kat. C and have several years of experience as a chauffeur or chauffeur
  • You have experience in the distribution of general goods – ideally with food
  • You know your way around the country and navigate safely
  • You are physically fit and resilient
  • You speak and write well German
  • You work in a structured, reliable way and enjoy customer contact
