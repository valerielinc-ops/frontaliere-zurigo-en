Full description

For our school-based childcare program, we are looking for a childcare specialist to start immediately or by agreement, on a fixed-term basis until 31 July 2027.

Responsibilities

As a childcare specialist, you will support kindergarten- and school-age children at Neubühl Day School during non-teaching hours. Together with the childcare team, you will help shape a varied after-school care routine and support the children in their personal and social development. You will work from Monday to Friday from 11:30 to 14:00, and on Wednesdays, as required, until 18:00. During the school holidays, you will have at least eight weeks of vacation.

Your responsibilities include

  • Helping shape the daily childcare routine and ensuring smooth day-to-day operations
  • Supporting and encouraging children in their personal, social and emotional development
  • Planning and conducting age-appropriate play, creative and leisure activities
  • Respectful collaboration within the childcare team and with teachers and parents
  • Participating in educational working groups and helping shape the further development of the day school
  • Taking on organizational and domestic tasks as part of the after-school care routine

Profile

  • Completed apprenticeship as a childcare specialist or specialist in early childhood education EFZ is mandatory. For applications with a foreign diploma, Swiss diploma recognition must be included.
  • Enjoyment of and interest in supporting children
  • Enjoyment of interacting with people from different cultures, as well as willingness to collaborate across disciplines within the school team
  • Professional experience caring for children
  • Solid German language skills

We offer

  • A varied and meaningful role in a vibrant environment
  • A motivated and open-minded school and leadership team
  • A pleasant working environment
  • Established structures of Tagesschule 2025
  • Attractive employment conditions

About us

The Uto school district is one of 7 school districts in the city of Zürich and encompasses city districts 2 and 3 with the neighborhoods of Leimbach, Wollishofen, Enge, Friesenberg and Alt-Wiedikon. Tagesschule Neubühl is located on the outskirts of Zürich in Zürich-Wollishofen and is a public primary school with an integrated childcare program and courses in the artistic, sports and crafts fields.
