Full description

We make habitats worth living.

Become part of a motivated team within a dynamic company that values family relations. Together we will strengthen our position in energy technology and shape the future of the industry.

Your tasks

  • Creation and development of offers in the field of energy distribution, especially for transformers.
  • Support for tenders as well as technical advice and clarifications for customers.
  • Independent order processing including appointment monitoring and project support.
  • Maintenance and handling of large contracts and public tenders.
  • Maintaining and updating customer and order data in the ERP system.
  • Supporting account management and helping to optimize internal processes and systems.

You bring that with you

  • Basic training in the electrical industry is beneficial, but not mandatory - but interest and technical understanding are indispensable.
  • Ideally, you plan to continue your business education, e.g. as a technical merchant or you already have a degree.
  • Experience in building technology is necessary.
  • Basic knowledge of project management is ideal.
  • German at mother tongue level
  • Knowledge of French and/or English is also welcome.
  • Structured and independent working methods, entrepreneurial thinking and an open, communicative personality with a pronounced service orientation.
  • Interested in further development to sales engineer.

We do not want requests from personnel service providers.
