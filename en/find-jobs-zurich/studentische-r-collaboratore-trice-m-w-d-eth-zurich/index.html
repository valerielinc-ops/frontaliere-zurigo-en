The Department of Teaching and Learning (UTL) promotes high-quality teaching and innovative teaching and learning methods at ETH Zurich. The teaching development group operates the highly academic training and further education offer for the teaching staff. It is responsible for the implementation and continuous development of teaching evaluation and supports the conception of high-quality performance certificates.

The job starts immediately or by agreement. The job is initially limited to one year. Long-term cooperation is sought but cannot be guaranteed. The compensation is based on ETH regulations for student employees in the hourly wage.

Job description

Participation in the teaching development group with the following focuses:

  • Create, clean and transfer to and from Excel lists
  • Quantitative and qualitative evaluation of data sets, evaluation of evaluation results
  • Preparation of presentations (slides)
  • Supervision and management of Moodle courses from a teaching perspective (including appointment monitoring, course communication, participant management and interaction)
  • Providing feedback from a student perspective

Profile

You are a student at a bachelor or master level at a Swiss university, are interested in topics of university teaching and teaching development and will gladly support the group teaching development.

They ideally bring the following knowledge and skills

  • Reliable, efficient, independent and accurate working methods
  • Patience and persistence in routine or time-intensive activities
  • Professional use of MS Office programs (especially Excel) and the Adobe Creative Cloud (especially Photoshop and Acrobat)
  • Affinity to working with AI applications
  • Basis for data evaluation
  • Moodle knowledge or interest in familiarizing yourself with the platform
  • Good understanding of the ETH lecture directory
  • Stylistic and precise in English and German
