Do you want to take on more responsibility in obstetrics and prenatal diagnosis and take the next career step? In a modern women’s clinic in German-speaking Switzerland, you expect a lot of room for manoeuvre, a stable team and the opportunity to actively shape women’s medicine. She expects an established women’s clinic in a modern focus hospital with around 1,000 births each year and a broad range of medical services. The clinic is particularly distinguished by its personnel continuity: The fluctuation is low and the long-standing cooperation is characterised by mutual trust. In this position, you take on the professional and organizational co-responsibility for prenatal diagnosis, obstetrics and the postpartum bed. You will further develop existing structures, contribute your expertise and work closely with paediatrics, neonatology and other specialist areas.

Their tasks

  • Professional and organizational management as well as further development of prenatal diagnosis, obstetrics and postpartum
  • Independent conduct of general and specialized consultation hours for prenatal diagnosis and high-risk pregnancies
  • Care of complex obstetric cases and assumption of the background service of the Frauenklinik
  • Supervision and professional promotion of senior and assistant doctors
  • Leadership and further development of the assistant medical team including recruitment and training
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation with neonatology, paediatrics and the emergency team

Your Competences

  • Specialist title Gynecology and Obstetrics with profound experience in obstetrics, prenatal diagnosis and perinatal medicine
  • Key heading and/or DEGUM II desired
  • Enjoyment in the care of high-risk pregnancies and demanding prenatal diagnosis
  • Leadership experience or motivation to take on additional leadership responsibility
  • Strong teamwork, sense of responsibility and enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation

Their advantages

  • Professional design leeway: Take responsibility and actively develop prenatal diagnostics and obstetrics
  • Attractive clinic size: Around 1,000 births annually offer a versatile obstetric spectrum
  • Sophisticated Medicine: Specialised Prenatal Diagnosis and Care of Risk Pregnancies in a Modern Hospital Environment
  • Interdisciplinary exchange: Close cooperation with neonatology, paediatrics and other disciplines
  • Leadership and the promotion of young talent: Contribute to the medical education and development of your team
