ETH Zurich uses its wide range of competences to make a decisive contribution to the further development of energy research and to promote the transfer of new knowledge into practice.

The Energy Science Center (ESC) of ETH Zurich is an interdisciplinary competence center for the promotion of energy research and teaching. It brings together over fifty professors from nine different departments and promotes cooperation between science, industry and politics.

Project background

The aim of the platform is to provide scientifically sound, technology-neutral and comprehensible information on energy technologies and energy-related issues for politics, the media and the public.

The body shall contribute to speeding up the creation of scientific content for the website. The focus is on the processing of the current state of research as well as the understandable communication of complex scientific findings.

Job description

  • Development of scientifically sound content on the energy technologies and topics defined in the project.
  • Conduct structured literature searches based on high-quality scientific sources (in particular peer-reviewed publications, reviews and meta-analyses).
