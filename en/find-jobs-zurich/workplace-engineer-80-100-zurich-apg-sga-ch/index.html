Workplace Engineer

  • 80-100%
  • Permanent employment

As a workplace engineer, you play a central role in Switzerland-wide out-of-home advertising and in all workplaces at APG|SGA. In the area of digital playout, you ensure that advertising and information reliably appear on displays at train stations, airports, buses, trams and trains throughout Switzerland. In the area of workplaces, you provide a homogeneous, modern and lean client architecture that serves thousands of customers every day. Your work is critical to the operation and development of the digital advertising platforms that millions of people see every day. Your tasks

  • You will supervise and develop the Digital Playout Clients – mainly based on Windows and Windows Embedded. Clients with Linux are also included, but are clearly outnumbered
  • In addition, in close cooperation with specialized service providers, you will supervise and develop workplace clients based on Windows 11 for the entire company.
  • Ensure that the player hardware used is compatible with operating systems and drivers
  • You create and maintain Windows images, configure system services and provide stable functionality.
  • You develop and maintain batch & power shell scripts to automate processes and make them more efficient (intune packaging)
  • You support network integration and work closely with other areas such as infrastructure and service desk
  • You monitor the used clients and hardware, analyze errors and document the system landscape
  • You participate in the 1st and 2nd level support in the area of digital playout
  • You implement projects in the area of digital playout and workplaces

You bring with you

  • You’ll bring very good knowledge of Windows operating systems and in-depth Windows 11 and enterprise client management experience
  • You are familiar with creating images, configuring services and optimizing functionalities (Autopilot, Intune, Entra Join, Compliance and Security Baselines)
  • Experience handling player hardware, processors and driver compatibility
  • Experience in batch and power shell scripting for process automation
  • Basic knowledge of network technologies (e.g. TCP/IP, VLAN, DHCP)
  • Basic Linux skills are a plus, as well as good English skills to keep you safe in a technical environment
  • Willingness to work in an agile environment according to SAFe (Scaled Agile Framework) and actively contribute to the planning, implementation and continuous improvement within the team

This is what awaits you

  • Modern jobs and 1/2 of working hours in the home office
  • Flexible working hours and at least five weeks of holidays as well as the possibility of two additional weeks of holiday purchase
  • Very good social benefits, it is provided for you
  • Free tickets to various events
  • Top location in Zurich
