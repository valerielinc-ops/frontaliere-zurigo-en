Full description

Eraneos is an international strategy, transformation and technology advisory group that helps companies thrive in a dynamic digital world. With first-class experts from business and technology, we empower our customers to set new standards for sustainable transformations – from strategy to implementation. Whether in the design of sustainable organizations, the development of new potential through data and AI or the protection of companies through holistic cybersecurity: We deliver results pragmatically and purposefully. Our team of around 1,300 dedicated employees is represented in offices in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, Portugal, Denmark, Sweden, Austria, the UK, Singapore and the USA and combines global perspectives with strong local roots. Around 450 employees work at the Swiss locations in Zurich, Bern and Lausanne.

This is the job

  • Consultative support for customers in the manufacturing industrial and consumer goods sector in strategic and operational questions for transformation support with a focus on organizational and cultural change
  • Design and implement organizational change, cultural change, learning, communication and workforce transformation initiatives for leading companies
  • Development and implementation of IT strategies, operational models and transformations of IT departments with particular consideration of organizational and cultural impact
  • Managing complex transformation projects to increase efficiency, agility and digitalization with particular attention to the people, structures and culture of the organization
  • Identification and use of new business potential through innovative technologies and data-driven solutions as well as accompanying the associated changes in the organization
  • Contributing to business and technology transformations, including ERP, digital, AI-enabled and operating model transformation programs

This job is for you if

  • You have completed a degree in computer science, business informatics, economics or a comparable field.
  • You have several years of professional experience in organizational and cultural change management – ideally in a management consultancy or in transformation roles on the customer side.
  • You have already accompanied cultural and organizational development processes and know how attitude, behavior and cooperation can be changed sustainably.
  • You have experience in digital transformation projects and a strong understanding of organizational, cultural and technological change.
  • You have already successfully accompanied changes in production, factory or shop floor environments, bringing together the different cultures of management and operational level.
  • You are familiar with common change methods and frameworks and apply them specifically to organizational and cultural issues.
  • You have a reliable knowledge of German and English in words and writing.
