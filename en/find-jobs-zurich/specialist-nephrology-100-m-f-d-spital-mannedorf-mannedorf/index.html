Full description

Spital Männedorf – Your very special place of work. Whether a small “thank you” or a morning “grüezi” – we focus on real appreciation and humanity. The nature of your activity

  • Specialist care for patients in the nephrological ambulatory
  • Consultative care of nephrological patients in the inpatient area
  • Participation in internal service operations
  • Occasional supervision of an internist bed ward as an internist cadre doctor
  • Active participation in their education, training and education
  • Co-responsibility for the organization as well as the strategic and qualitative development of the Faculty of Nephrology, including the optimization of clinical processes and the range of services
  • Active participation in clinical everyday life and in interprofessional teams

The basis of your performance

  • Specialist title for General Internal Medicine and Nephrology, continuing education recognised in Switzerland (MEBEKO)
  • Experience in the entire nephrology with proven expertise in dialysis procedures
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation
  • High patient orientation and strong social and communication skills
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing (at least level C1)

The reasons for your decision

We offer modern working conditions, up-to-date medical technologies, promote internal training and focus on professional exchange across all disciplines. The quiet location directly on the lake and the proximity to the vibrant city of Zurich round off your workplace. In addition, you can look forward to very special advantages.
