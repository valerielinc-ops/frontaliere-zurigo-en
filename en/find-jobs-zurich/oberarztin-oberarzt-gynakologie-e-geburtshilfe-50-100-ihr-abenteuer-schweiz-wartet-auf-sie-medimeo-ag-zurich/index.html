Do you have your specialist title in gynecology and obstetrics in your pocket and play with the thought of daring something new both professionally and personally? Perhaps now is the right time for your adventure Switzerland. Look forward to a versatile senior doctor position with surgical activity, an endoscopic focus and a lot of proximity to obstetrics. At the same time, a workload of between 50 and 100% offers you the opportunity to design your new task to suit your personal life situation. The Frauenklinik offers a broad range of services in gynecology and obstetrics at two locations. The cross-location cooperation creates a versatile clinical environment with close exchange within the medical and interprofessional team. The focus is on personal patient care, collegial cooperation and a working environment in which you can assume responsibility, contribute your operational skills and accompany younger colleagues in their further education. Even outside the hospital, you can expect an attractive living environment with good connections to a large Swiss city.

Their tasks

  • Care of outpatient and inpatient patients in gynecology and obstetrics – in the birth room, surgery, ambulatory and emergency
  • Operational activity focusing on endoscopy
  • Participation in shift work and assuming responsibility in clinical everyday life
  • Accompaniment and further training of assistant doctors
  • Participation in organizational tasks and cross-location cooperation within the Frauenklinik

Your Competences

  • Specialist title in Gynecology and Obstetrics
  • First experience at senior level is advantageous, but not a mandatory requirement
  • Joy of surgical gynecology, obstetrics and teaching
  • Independent, responsible and team-oriented working methods
  • Good knowledge of German as well as openness for a professional and personal restart in Switzerland

Their advantages

  • Flexible workload between 50 and 100% suitable for personal life situation
  • Varied activities in gynecology, obstetrics and surgery with an endoscopic focus
  • Cross-location cooperation in a committed and collegial team
  • Opportunity to get to know the Swiss healthcare system in an established clinical environment
  • Attractive living environment with good connections to a large Swiss city
