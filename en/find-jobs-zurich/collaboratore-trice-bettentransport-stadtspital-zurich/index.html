Full description

For the Waid location we are looking for a motivated and committed personality as of 01.01.2027 or by agreement. You can expect a meaningful task in a versatile hospital operation, in which you make an important contribution to the care of our patients every day with your work. Do you want to ensure quality, safety and a smooth process? Then you are the right person for us.

Tasks

  • You transport patients, materials, furniture and empty beds safely, carefully, quickly and reliably.
  • You clean and prepare beds and wheelchairs and check their functionality.
  • You ensure order and cleanliness and consistently comply with the hygiene and safety regulations.
  • You process transport assignments digitally, coordinate your tasks independently and ensure punctual, smooth processes.
  • You recognize defects, damages and optimization options and report them to your superiors.
  • You meet patients and employees in a friendly and respectful way, support new team members and promote good cooperation.

Profile

  • You have completed compulsory schooling and ideally have an apprenticeship (EBA) or training in cleaning, logistics, housekeeping or a similar field.
  • You bring experience in the service sector and customer contact
  • Experience in the hospital environment is an advantage.
  • You communicate in German at least at level B1 in word and writing and convince through your communication and team skills.
  • You are reliable, punctual and responsible and perform your tasks independently, carefully and conscientiously.
  • You are physically resilient, persistent and willing to learn and also face repetitive and strenuous work.
  • You are flexible in terms of working hours, open to digital tools and new tasks and ready to help out at other locations if necessary.

We offer

We offer you more than just a job! We live an open du-culture, which is characterized by an uncomplicated and personal cooperation at eye level, respect and appreciation. As part of our diverse team of over 120 professional groups, you actively shape a meaningful environment. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions and excellent social benefits from the city of Zurich.

About us

With over 36'000 inpatients and around 455,000 outpatients per year, the Stadtspital Zürich ranks among the top 10 Swiss hospitals. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,500 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
