Full description

We are looking for a service-oriented, motivated and flexible personality to strengthen our team in Zurich in the Prime Tower.

You can move that with us.

You provide the following services in shift operation (24/7) in a competent team:

  • Tactical and active implementation of the predefined security concept in the respective assigned task area
  • Preparation and swift assessment and handling of events, initial interventions, transmission of reports to the competent bodies
  • Maintenance of daily journals and other reporting
  • Operation of various monitoring and control systems
  • Control of access and creation of access authorisations
  • Execution of various administrative tasks

That's what makes you.

You describe yourself as a security service provider out of innermost conviction: and bring the following requirements:

  • Basic technical or commercial training is advantageous
  • Experience in alarm and surveillance centre
  • Team-capable, time-flexible, active and reliable personality with a high degree of security thinking
  • You can make necessary decisions independently and are able to provide help if necessary
  • Independent and exact working and quick understanding
  • Swiss Citizen or Authorization C
  • Good PC user knowledge
  • English skills are beneficial

That's what we offer you.

  • A responsible, varied activity in an exciting area of responsibility
  • Lots of room for own initiative
  • A dynamic, collaborative team
  • A modern working environment with clear processes and high professionalism
