Full description

For our centrally located practice - directly at the Zurich-Stadelhofen station - we are looking for immediately or by agreement Medical assistant (80-100%)

Their tasks

  • Planning, organisation and preparation of office hours
  • Care of patients during examinations
  • Independent performance of blood sampling, urine analysis, injections and instillations
  • Professional, hygienic and impeccable care of the examination rooms
  • Assistance in cystoscopies and minor ambulatory urological procedures
  • Administration of medicines and materials
  • Writing Reports with AI Supporting Software
  • General reception and office work

Your profile

  • Completed training as medical/practical assistant EFZ, nurse HF or specialist health EFZ
  • Professional experience as an MPA is beneficial
  • Friendly, reliable and competent personality characterized by a high resilience and great motivation
  • Joy of rain patient contact
  • Maintained appearance
  • Experience with the operating system Vitodata is advantageous (but not mandatory)

  • Private, worldwide accident insurance

  • Short decision paths, flat hierarchs, possibility to contribute your own ideas
