Where we are looking for them

  • Head of the overall system development of complex air defense systems (System of Systems)
  • Leadership of a team with various sub-project managers and their teams
  • Coordination of the technical area of involved international partner companies, with their own development work, within the framework of international development cooperation
  • Programming in cooperation with the commercial programme manager (m/f/d)
  • Preparation of technical contract documents (product specifications, acceptance concepts) as well as the specifications for partners and suppliers
  • Perform analysis of customer requests and needs
  • Development of overall and subsystem solutions incl. Embedding in higher-level systems and creating the Top Level Design Doc
  • Creation of overall system concepts and implementation of technical presentations and customer discussions WHAT YOU SHOULD BUILD

  • Additional training in business management topics v. Advantage
