Who you are

Help people feel good, make their choices easier and, of course, sell IKEA products. Sounds good? Sounds like you should keep reading.

People work better when they are well. At IKEA, we believe that challenges bring joy when you have fun at work and achieve goals together. We want you to feel comfortable with us and to be able to develop personally and professionally.

How do you see that? See if this suits you:

  • You are interested in furniture and home furnishings.
  • You already have experience in sales or customer service.
  • The daily contact with many different people makes you fun.
  • You are experienced in using the computer and can quickly familiarize yourself with new programs.
  • You work independently and have time management under control.
  • You speak safe German and knowledge of English is an advantage.
  • You are ready to work from Mo-Sa in both early and late shifts.
