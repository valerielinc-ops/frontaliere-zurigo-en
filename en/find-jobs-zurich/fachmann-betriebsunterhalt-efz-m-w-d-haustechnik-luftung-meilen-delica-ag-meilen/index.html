Full description

With us, you keep operations running in the truest sense of the word: from ventilation and waste disposal to the sewage system, you ensure that everything runs smoothly – and you help us build up vocational training in facility maintenance. You will join a team that stands up for each other, celebrates successes, and works with passion to create daily moments of enjoyment for our customers.

Duties

  • You maintain and clean our ventilation systems, replace filters, and ensure that motors and building services run reliably.
  • You manage waste disposal points and the sewage system, cleaning, maintaining, and fixing minor faults while complying with environmental and safety standards.
  • You take care of classic caretaker tasks such as mowing the lawn, removing leaves, winter service, and minor repairs to buildings, doors, gates, and windows.
  • You take over filter and spare parts management, independently procure the necessary parts, and ensure an overview of the entire operation.
  • You actively participate in the development of vocational training in facility maintenance and are motivated to accompany apprentices in the future and pass on your knowledge in a practical way.

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience (approx. 3–5 years): in facility maintenance, building services, or as a caretaker in a similar environment, ideally with ventilation and waste disposal systems.
  • Completed vocational training (EFZ): as a facility maintenance specialist or comparable technical basic training (e.g., electrical/building services/mechanics); ideally with or willingness to undergo vocational trainer training.
  • Mechanical affinity, good technical understanding, confident handling of MS Office, and enjoyment of practical, independent work indoors and outdoors.
  • High social competence: You are communicative and capable of dialogue, enjoy working in a team, and have a passion for sharing knowledge and supporting apprentices in the future.
  • Readiness for on-call duty: approx. 6–7 shifts per year, fault hotline approx. every 1.5 weeks for one day; you are resilient, reliable, and willing to pitch in even in heat, cold, in tight spaces, or at heights.
  • German (negotiation level)
