Security guards: in / Security chief 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Cross-entry security guard: in / security chief Winterthur, from now on or by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

As a safety guard: you ensure that work in the track area is carried out safely with compliance with the safety measures. Your day-to-day work is just as varied as your working hours and the different places you work along the most beautiful railway lines in Switzerland. Attractive working conditions are important to us, even in the case of irregular working hours. Night and weekend work is also paid for with us - e.g. with 16 francs per hour for Sunday work. For night assignments, you also get time surcharges of between 10 and 30%. This means you have more free time than employees without night and weekend assignments. Interested in learning more about our professions? We look forward to seeing you from 22 to 24. 2026 in our SBB Berufswelten Café at Zurich Central Station. All information can be found at sbb.ch/berufsweltencafe. You can move that.

  • Your responsibility is to protect personnel, materials and equipment from the dangers of railway operations and railway power plants.
  • As a security guard: you warn colleagues on the railway construction site about approaching trains.
  • You are responsible for setting up and carrying out a security facility and direct communication by telephone or radio with the competent authorities.
  • You inform those responsible for the construction site about exceptional occurrences in their area of activity.
  • You travel a lot and practice this activity at different locations in your region.

More on the job profile. You bring that with you.

  • For this demanding work, you are ready to work independently, reliably, in a team and open to shifts (early, day, night and weekend assignments).
  • You are physically and psychologically resilient and characterized by a strong security awareness.
  • Ideally, you have completed a basic education (EBA / EFZ) or already have professional experience in the craft, logistical or comparable field. For this purpose, you have completed the training as a security guard:in or security chief:in or are ready to complete it.
  • When dealing with various digital tools (iPad and iPhone) and applications, you are skilful and ready to continue your training and learning.
  • Your fluent German skills round off your profile. Knowledge of a second national language is desirable.
  • A valid ticket of category B (car with manual manual control) and good eyesight and hearing round off your profile.
