Full description

Eraneos is an international strategy, transformation and technology advisory group that helps companies thrive in a dynamic digital world. With first-class experts from business and technology, we empower our customers to set new standards for sustainable transformations – from strategy to implementation. Whether in the design of sustainable organizations, the development of new potential through data and AI or the protection of companies through holistic cybersecurity: We deliver results pragmatically and purposefully. Our team of around 1,300 dedicated employees is represented in offices in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, Portugal, Denmark, Sweden, Austria, the UK, Singapore and the USA and combines global perspectives with strong local roots. Around 450 employees work at the Swiss locations in Zurich, Bern and Lausanne.

This is the job - your main tasks

  • You coordinate and manage demanding bid and tender processes in the public sector and ensure that opportunities become successful projects.
  • You’ll be part of the sales team and help build new customer relationships and develop existing ones.
  • You create, coordinate and take responsibility for offers and quality assurance for offers, contracts, framework contracts and other sales-relevant documents.
  • You keep the threads between consulting, sales, legal and finance together and ensure a smooth process.
  • You make a visible contribution to our growth and to the successful digitalization of Switzerland.

This job is for you if you...

  • We like to take responsibility and enjoy bringing people, information and processes together.
  • bring a degree (bachelor or higher) from a college or university or will achieve it in the coming year.
  • have already gained experience in sales support, bid management, back office or in a comparable function.
  • like to work independently, but appreciates the exchange with different stakeholders.
  • Work in a structured, solution-oriented and reliable manner and keep an overview even in hectic situations.
  • have very good knowledge of German and English.
  • are curious, want to learn new things and are interested in the topics of digitization, data & AI, cybersecurity and transformation.

As an i-top you bring with you

  • experience in public procurement or in public tenders.
  • Knowledge of submissions on the supplier or supplier side.
  • Experience in IT, transformation or consulting projects.
  • A good understanding of tendering processes, deadlines and quality requirements.
  • Enjoy taking responsibility and getting things done reliably.
  • A pronounced service idea and the motivation to be successful together with others.

Even if not all requirements are fully met, we are very happy about your application!
