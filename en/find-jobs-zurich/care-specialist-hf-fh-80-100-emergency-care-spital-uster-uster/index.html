Full description

Personal – competent – close Welcome to the Hospital Uster, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As the central pillar of health care in the Oberland region of Zurich and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you experience the highest level of expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and promoted in your professional development. Spital Uster is a teaching hospital of the Medical Faculty of the University of Zurich. Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of a “regional and digital health hub in Züri East”! At the interdisciplinary emergency department of the Uster Hospital, approximately 25,000 patients of all ages are treated annually. As a SGNOR-certified emergency department, we work in a team at a high quality level and enjoy regular interdisciplinary training. Exciting challenges await you

  • You are responsible for the professional initial assessment in the triage and the medical emergency care and support of our emergency patients including pediatric cases
  • Self-organized, you provide a professional, professional and patient-oriented emergency care incl. Care of patients in the shock room
  • You work at eye level with other professional groups involved in the treatment process and shape an appreciative team culture
  • You form and shape your personal career and share your experience with students and the entire interdisciplinary team

What sets you apart for the job

  • Dipl. Nurse HF/FH with willingness to start the NDS within two years
  • at least 6 months of experience in an acute care unit
  • analytical ability to solidly assess the situation and make decisions and initiate appropriate measures
  • quiet personality as well as high professional and social competence
  • very high customer orientation, diversified conversation – even in stressful situations
  • Resilience, flexibility, good organisational capacity
  • Readiness for shift work
  • Innovation and openness to future-oriented changes
  • Commitment to successful team and care development
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German

8 reasons to work with us

  • working in a very motivated, dynamic family team with good 'team spirit'
  • varied field of activity with daily interdisciplinary short-term training and exciting cases
  • Family atmosphere and very good social benefits
  • at least five weeks holidays and joker days for changing time
  • Excellent staff restaurant and numerous discounts
  • Central location near Uster station and motorway connection
  • ZVV BonusPass or cheap parking, max. CHF 4.50 per day
