Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Leading ICT projects from initialisation through conception and implementation to business handover as well as responsibility for deadlines, costs, risks and quality.
  • Represent the Cantonal Police Aargau in cross-cantonal and national projects as well as coordination with police corps, authorities, specialist offices and other partner organisations.
  • Establishing and formulating professional and technical requirements as well as developing, assessing and implementing suitable solution variants together with specialist areas, ICT and suppliers.
  • Control of project teams and suppliers
  • Coordination and implementation of evaluations and public procurement projects.
  • developing and communicating decision-making bases, concepts and reports for project bodies, police and other stakeholders
  • Preparation of motions by the government and parliament.

What you bring

  • Education at the level of higher education / technical college in computer science, business informatics, electrical engineering or comparable as well as several years of experience in the management of demanding technology projects.
  • experience in the public sector or a similarly regulated organisation
  • Experience in the police environment, civil protection, military or other security-critical environment.
  • Experience in requirements management, in the evaluation and procurement of ICT solutions as well as with the project management methodology HERMES and public procurement procedures.
  • Experience with network architectures, transmission and telecommunication technologies and mission-critical systems, in particular safety-critical communication, emergency call or emergency response systems.
  • Very good communication and expression skills, negotiation skills, assertiveness and a structured, independent working method
  • Cat B driving licence and good repute
  • Knowledge of French is beneficial.

We are committed to the cantonal police Aargau for the safety of the almost 740,000 inhabitants in the canton Aargau.

The Department of Police Engineering ensures the computer and telecommunications operations of the Cantonal Police around the clock, 365 days a year. By adapting new technologies, it strengthens the police today and for the future in their diverse tasks.

The service "ICT Projects" is responsible for the development and further development of the ICT systems and applications of the Cantonal Police Aargau. We work closely with future users and with other police corps. We analyze the needs of users, evaluate and procure suitable systems and technologies and establish them in the organization.

The Cantonal Police Aargau is dependent on reliable and secure ICT, in daily police operations as well as in special and extraordinary situations. As a project manager or ICT project manager, you take responsibility for challenging ICT projects. From the first idea to the conception and procurement to the introduction and transfer to the company.
