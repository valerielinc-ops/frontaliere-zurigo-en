Full description

For our surgical unit with a focus on cardiac surgery and traumatology at the Triemli site, we are looking for 1. September 2026 or by appointment a team-oriented personality who works 80% and in all shifts in the first 3 months and then wants to reduce her workload.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for planning and implementing care based on the care process as well as guidelines and instructions.
  • You are responsible for patient-centered documentation of the work and care process and the correct performance recording.
  • You actively contribute to constructive interdisciplinary cooperation and work with high service quality.
  • You contribute to improving the quality and development of care.
  • You are jointly responsible for supervising trainees and introducing new employees.

Profile

  • Training as a nurse HF/FH
  • Professional experience in a surgical bed ward in a Swiss acute care hospital
  • Independent and self-responsible working methods as well as a strong quality and service awareness
  • Cross-cutting understanding and economic thinking and action
  • Enjoy working in a complex and demanding field

We offer

We offer you more than just a job! Our city hospital world is characterized by an open du-culture, an uncomplicated, personal togetherness and the necessary touch of humor in everyday life. As a team of over 120 professional groups, we work every day in very different ways for the well-being of our patients. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions and excellent social benefits from the city of Zurich.

About us

With over 36'000 inpatients and around 455,000 outpatients per year, the Stadtspital Zürich ranks among the top 10 Swiss hospitals. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,500 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
