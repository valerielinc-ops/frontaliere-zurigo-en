80% - 100% | IT / Business Engineering | Zurich | Experienced professionals

As a well-established DevOps team, consisting of fullstack developers and business engineers, we need your support to further develop the payment traffic of the Zürcher Kantonalbank. Every day you process hundreds of thousands of payments via various channels with a high-performance, self-developed payment application. We consistently develop and operate our solutions according to the DevOps approach and appreciate your contribution across the entire lifecycle. Become part of our team and actively shape the payment traffic of tomorrow.

Your tasks

  • Implementing requirements in the backend with Java and Kotlin as well as in the frontend with Angular and Typescript, both on-premises and in the cloud (Azure)
  • Responsible for the entire software life cycle from the idea to implementation to operation in production
  • Implementation of technical concepts and contributions to the further development of the software architecture
  • Analyze requirements and design of new features in the area of payment transactions
  • Collaborate in a DevOps team and continuously improve the stability, performance and maintainability of applications
  • university degree in computer science or equivalent education or Professional experience
  • Several years of professional experience in software development with focus on fullstack engineering
  • Very good knowledge of Java and Kotlin as well as profound experience with Angular and Typescript
  • Experience with cloud environments (ideally Azure) and modern architecture approaches is a plus
  • You like to take on ownership, set impulses in the team and work solution-oriented
  • You convince with a clear, addressable communication and a reliable, structured working method
  • Very good German skills in word and writing, English skills are an advantage
  • Mutual support and team spirit are important to us
  • Exciting and diverse range of tasks in payment transactions with many network possibilities
  • Constant further development – personal, professional and technical
  • Centrally located, easily accessible and attractive workplace with the possibility to organize your work flexibly (hybrid)
  • Flexible working hours, mobile working and work-life balance are a matter of course for us
  • Comprehensive package, which in addition to the attractive basic salary and a variable remuneration offers many benefits and perks – from A such as retirement provision to Z such as ZVV bonus pass
