Your team

At PwC Pension Consulting, you actively shape the future of occupational pension provision in Switzerland. Together with a specialized team, you will advise companies and pension institutions on strategic and operational issues relating to the BVG and modern pension solutions. As part of our multidisciplinary network, you will work closely with tax, legal, investment, real estate and social insurance professionals to develop holistic and sustainable solutions for our customers. Zurich By agreement 80-100% Tax, Legal and Workforce Everyone welcome ID: 765236WD

Your impact

  • Advise companies and pension institutions on strategic and operational issues relating to occupational pension provision and create sustainable added value for customers.
  • Analyze existing pension solutions and develop individual, sustainable and market-oriented pension concepts.
  • Support your own customers and mandates independently and build long-term, trusting customer relationships.
  • Accompany tenders and evaluations of pension partners and develop well-founded market comparisons, benchmarks and decision-making basis.
  • Analyze pension plans with regard to benefits, financing, costs and risks and derive concrete recommendations for action.
  • Take responsibility for your own projects and lead individual project streams to success independently.
  • Create management presentations, decision bases and customer documents and present analyses and recommendations appropriate to the addressee.
  • Work closely with specialists from the fields of tax, legal, real estate, investment and social insurance and develop holistic solutions for complex customer issues.
  • Observe regulatory and market developments and translate them into concrete recommendations for our customers.

Your skill set

  • In-depth training or further education as well as proven expertise in the field of occupational provision.
  • 3-5 years of professional experience in pension consulting, occupational provision, at a pension fund, insurance or a comparable environment.
  • Very good knowledge of the Swiss BVG and pension system as well as the relevant regulatory framework conditions.
  • Demonstrable experience in advising or supporting companies, pension facilities or demanding customers: inside.
  • Practical knowledge in pension plan analyses, tenders, benchmarks or the evaluation of pension solutions is advantageous.
  • Strong analytical and conceptual skills and enjoy working with numbers and complex questions.
  • Secure appearance, strong communication and the ability to convey complex topics in an understandable way.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Knowledge of French is a plus.
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working methods as well as motivation to take responsibility and actively shape the further development of the area.
