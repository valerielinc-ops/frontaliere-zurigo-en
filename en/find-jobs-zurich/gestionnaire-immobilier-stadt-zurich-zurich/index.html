Full description

In our "Gastronomy / Retail" team, we are looking for an established personality to support our property managers with a portfolio of commercial and sales space.

Tasks

  • Acceptance and handling of tenant concerns
  • Obtaining offers and organizing order and order processing
  • Administrative management of tenant dossiers and data maintenance in the SAP system
  • appointment organization such as meetings, visits
  • Participation in the preparation of heating and operating costs bills
  • Accounting, dunning, collection and turnover reporting
  • Specific tasks under projects

Profile

  • Completed commercial training, preferably with further training as a clerk* in real estate management
  • Several years of professional experience in the real estate industry
  • Customer and service orientation, high social skills and communication skills
  • Teamwork, independent working methods and good understanding
  • Cost-conscious thinking and business understanding
  • Experienced handling of MS Office programs and an advantage with the SAP system

We offer

a diverse and collegial working environment in a growing organization. Our employees benefit from attractive employment conditions and good social benefits. The centrally located workplace at Hallwylplatz and a wide range of training and further education are further qualities.

About us

Properties for a livable city of Zurich

Properties City of Zurich manages around 10,000 apartments and 1200 commercial premises. We rent the apartments according to the principle of cost rent as affordable living space, pay attention to a socially mixed tenant population and contribute with the commercial space to the good supply of the quarters with goods and services. In addition, there are real estate transactions as well as the management of urban land reserves and building rights.
