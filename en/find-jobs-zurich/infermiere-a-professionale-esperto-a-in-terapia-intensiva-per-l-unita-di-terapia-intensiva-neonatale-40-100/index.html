High tech hits the heart! Become part of our dedicated team in the neonatal intensive care unit - where technical understanding meets human warmth. Together we do great things for the smallest.

What to expect from us

  • You nurture and care for 1 - 3 premature babies from the 23. SSW or seriously ill newborns - loving, professional and individual.
  • You accompany children and their families empathetically and family-centered through a challenging time.
  • You are part of the first hours of a new life: first care in the birth department and accompany newborns in external transports.
  • You work according to current, evidence-based guidelines and actively shape the further development of care quality.
  • You are not alone: we work interprofessionally and meet at eye level.

What you bring

  • Completed training as a nurse / nurse specialist.
  • Ideally, a postgraduate degree in intensive care (NDS) or an NDK neonatology.
  • Professional experience in pediatrics or neonatology.
  • Very good knowledge of German at C1 level, other languages are a plus.

What we offer you

  • A flexible roster with the ability to work primarily in your preferred shifts.
  • An individual introduction as well as personal and professional development in the directions of subject, education or management.
  • Direct access to current research results and work with the latest technology.
  • An open ear for your ideas and opinions as well as the opportunity to implement them together.
  • Financial support for external training or courses.
  • A good work-life balance is important to us, which is why we offer you the following:
  • short drift times at the beginning of the shift
  • Days and nights free of service as desired (depending on the amount required)
  • After training, uncomplicated increase or reduction of your workload
  • After completion of training: Possibility of additional employment in the nursing pool neonatology - for even more flexibility and exciting assignments
  • Possibility of unpaid leave
  • Attractive perks such as ZVV-BonusPass, general subscription or ASVZ annual subscription
