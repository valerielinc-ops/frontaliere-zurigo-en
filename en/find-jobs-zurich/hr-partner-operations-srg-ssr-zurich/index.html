Full description

The Human Resources area, managed at the national level, is responsible for and manages the staff affairs of the SSR as a whole. Human Resources supports SSR employees in all personnel matters, offers a modern offer of initial and continuing training, and responds to the overall implementation of modern key processes.

Your Mission

As soon as possible or to be agreed, we are looking for an 80–100% HR Partner.

Within the HR Council team, you support the national area of Operations in its HR issues and current transformation. You have the following responsibilities:

  • You are the reference partner for management and employees on all HR issues and take charge of all HR processes related to your perimeter.
  • You advise and accompany line managers on all personnel matters, including recruitment, remuneration, interviews, development and career management. You also ensure compliance with internal directives, legal requirements and company standards.
  • You bring your expertise on labour law, organisation and operational management issues and are the first point of contact for labour relations issues.
  • You accompany sensitive situations, such as conflicts, absences or disciplinary issues, with tact and professionalism.
  • In close collaboration with line managers, you contribute to the implementation of personnel policy and to the achievement of HR standards.

Your profile

  • An empathic personality, pragmatic and oriented solutions, able to actively accompany employees and executives in times of transformation.
  • Several years of experience in HR consulting, ideally in a complex, dynamic and comparable size environment.
  • Strong knowledge in all areas of human resources, as well as interest in new approaches and changes in the profession.
  • Excellent communication skills and the ability to adapt to different people at all levels of the organization.
  • The willingness to work regularly at the Bern and Zurich sites.
  • An excellent mastery of German and a French level of at least B2

What we offer you

  • A stimulating and dynamic working environment, with many opportunities to contribute and develop.
  • A varied, interesting and demanding position, with real responsibilities and flexibility.
  • A team of experienced and caring colleagues who like to share their knowledge and expertise.
  • A modern working environment, with flexible working hours and the possibility of partially working in telework.

Our promise

Reaching the entire Swiss population with high quality formats requires expertise, enthusiasm and a stimulating and creative environment in which everyone can invest and evolve. For the best to give the best of themselves, they need to have a source of inspiration: Welcome to the world of SSR!

  • Create Content: Quality journalism is the core of our activity and passion. We enjoy complete freedom of writing, encourage creativity and grant autonomy for innovation and quality.
  • Living digital: In our country, quality journalism can also take a digital form. This is why we are exploring new opportunities and experimenting with new formats and practices: digital, interactive and cross-media. Our content is part of the air of time and responds to public concerns.
  • Human being: We maintain informal relations, work independently and contribute to a good working atmosphere. Our success is based on the strengths of everyone and on the technical skills of all our employees.
  • Make sense: With our program, we reach all of Switzerland. We act independently and respectfully to encourage the formation of opinion and strengthen cultural diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.
