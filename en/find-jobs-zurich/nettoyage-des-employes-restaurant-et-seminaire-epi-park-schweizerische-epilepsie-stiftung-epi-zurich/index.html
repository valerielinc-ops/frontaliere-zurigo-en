Full description

The Swiss Epilepsy Foundation (EPI) is a multi-sector company. It includes the EPI WohnWerk, the EPI Hospital School, the Lengg High School, the donation Dapples, a seminar center with restaurant and the EPI Central Services. In addition, the Swiss Epilepsy Foundation together with another foundation forms the sponsorship of Klinik Lengg AG, a clinic for epileptology and neurorehabilitation. Around 1000 employees are committed to these social tasks. The area is located on the outskirts of Zurich, with a fantastic view of Lake Zurich and the Alps.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for cleaning the seminar rooms and the restaurant according to internal guidelines and guidelines
  • You maintain a correct handling of our cleaning machines and equipment and use the existing cleaning and aids correctly
  • You independently forward repair messages to the superior
  • They prepare seminar and banquet rooms according to the respective requirements

Requirements

  • You have completed training in the cleaning or hotel industry, alternatively several years of experience in professional cleaning
  • They are physically fit, very resilient, self-reliant and do not fear heights
  • They convince us with their reliability, flexibility, service orientation and willingness to perform
  • Due to their good knowledge of German, they can easily communicate with various contacts
  • They are interested in learning new things e.g. B. The in-house seminar infrastructure

We offer

  • Monthly service planning with regulated free weekends
  • 5 weeks holiday, from the 50th. Age 6 weeks
  • Own pension fund with above average employer share of 60%
  • Good accessibility by public transport (bus stop in front of the building)
  • Staff apartments, parking spaces and perks in the EPI Park restaurant, pharmacy and half-tax or general subscription

EPI Park and Restarurant EPI Park includes a seminar center and a public restaurant. It offers our patients, employees, clients, seminar participants and visitors a selection of delicious, healthy menus and a salad buffet every day.
