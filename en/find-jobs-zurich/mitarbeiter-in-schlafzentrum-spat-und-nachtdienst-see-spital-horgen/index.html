Your field of activity In the accredited Zurichsee Sleep Center, we accompany patients with sleep and breathing disorders on their way to more life quality and rest in everyday life. To strengthen our small and committed team, we are looking for a responsible person for the late and night shift. Your profile

  • MPA, FaGe, diploma nurse or completed training as a specialist in neurophysiological diagnostics (FND)
  • Enjoyment of work in a medically-technical and patient-oriented environment
  • Interest in sleep medicine and willingness to specialize in a specialized field
  • Autonomous, reliable and structured working style
  • Technical understanding and safe handling of medical devices
  • High social competence and empathetic approach to patients What we offer you
  • Structured and careful introduction to sleep medicine and diagnostic processes
  • Possibility of further training or training as a specialist in neurophysiological diagnostics (FND) with appropriate qualifications
  • Autonomous work in a small, committed and collegial team
  • Modern accredited sleep center with up-to-date infrastructure in an attractive location on Lake Zurich
  • Diverse activity at the interface of medicine, diagnostics and medical technology
  • Care for predominantly self-reliant patients without classical acute care workload
  • Regulated and planable shifts in alternating late and night shift with overlap to the morning shift
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation with pneumology and sleep medicine
  • Attractive employment conditions, further training offers and employee benefits
  • Good reachability with public transport and cost-effective staff parking
