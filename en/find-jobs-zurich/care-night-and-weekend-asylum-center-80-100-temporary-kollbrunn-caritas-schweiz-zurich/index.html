Support night and weekend Asylum center (80-100%) temporary, Kollbrunn

Caritas Switzerland has been operating asylum centres at various locations in the canton of Zurich since March 2024 on a cantonal mission. The centre is located in a rural environment directly at the station of Kollbrunn and accommodates a maximum of 130 families and individuals. For the care of refugees at night and especially at weekends, we are looking for reinforcements of our team.
