Canton of Zurich Financial Directorate Personnel Office Apprenticeship as ICT specialist / ICT specialist EFZ 2027 100% Do you want to become part of the economic engine Switzerland and shape your professional future at the Canton of Zurich? Then you are exactly right with us. Around 300 apprentices complete their training in 25 different professions. Around 70 young people in the ICT sector are currently learning from this. Training places for ICT specialists are available, for example, at vocational and cantonal schools, at the Office of Computer Science and at courts. An ICT infrastructure that functions around the clock is indispensable in today's working world. The internal IT departments with their employees and trainees are responsible for the smooth operation. In your three-year apprenticeship as an ICT specialist, you work as a contact person between installation and support. You install hardware and software, expand device configurations and ensure their operation and maintenance. You support users with support requests, instruct them in dealing with ICT tools and create user instructions. In inter-company courses, projects and events, you can expand and deepen your specialist, methodological, social and self-competences. The

Your social skills and an open personality are also very important to us, as you will work a lot in the team and have daily customer contact.
