Full description

Do you like working with people? You would like to start immediately, temporarily until 31. August 2027, using our team in the hotel industry for the health center for the old Limmat?

Tasks

  • They work in the restaurant as well as in the public bistro and perform all related work and tasks according to the instructions of the superiors and bear the joint responsibility for compliance with the quality standards.
  • You clean the assigned premises independently according to the operational plan, in compliance with economic and environmental aspects.
  • They guarantee and independently carry out a clean, careful and impeccable cleaning, care and preparation of all company and professional linen as well as the private linen of the residents, taking into account internal processes and regulations.
  • They comply with all safety measures as well as with all legal and operational hygiene regulations and cleaning requirements.
  • They run the checkout in the bistro when selling meals and drinks and pay attention to a corresponding ambience in the restaurant.

Profile

  • Joy in contact with residents, passion for work, high quality standards and service orientation
  • Several years of experience in service and cleaning
  • Completed training as a specialist in domestic industry EFZ or equivalent qualification
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German as well as sound computer skills
  • Teamwork, diligence, flexibility and readiness for irregular working hours

We offer

Enjoy an exciting field of work in a professional and team-oriented working environment with a du-culture throughout the company. The human being is at our center. That is why we support the reconciliation of private and professional life, different working models make it possible.

As part of the city of Zurich and due to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, progressive employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

Depending on the experience, the annual salary for this function is between Fr. 64’070.– and Fr. 85’610.– (100%).

About us

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

In the lively district of Zurich-West in district 5, we offer our residents a homely home with all amenities. The health centre is a real bijou thanks to its diverse character, the wide range of offers and the proximity to the central station. Here you work together with 80 colleagues.
