Full description

Magazine articles are our trademark. The “Clippy” team is responsible for the technical solutions and works closely with the editorial team. You lead this cross-functional team of front-end, back-end and ML specialists and translate our product vision into effective solutions with drive and communication power.

Mansioni

  • With the leadership of the team, you take responsibility for a successful cooperation and the further development of the products
  • A significant part of your time you work hands-on, develop scalable features and contribute to architectural decisions to ensure a common technical and professional orientation in the team.
  • Together with your team, you will use behavioral data and machine learning models to go beyond classic rule-based approaches and play content in an automated and personalized way.
  • As a sparring partner for product owners, UX designers, data analysts, data scientists and software architects, you explain technical topics in such a way that they are also understandable outside of engineering.
  • You advance topics within the team and beyond, keep stakeholders informed and make your team’s successes visible. As a link to the Head of Engineering, you bring decisions back to the team
  • The professional and personal development of your team members is your responsibility
  • As a reliable leader, you empower your team to make decisions, take their concerns seriously and create a motivating and positive environment.

Requirements

  • 5+ years: Cross-functional team leadership for editorial content personalization (frontend, backend, ML) in e-commerce
  • Completed studies (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): Software Engineering / Data Science
  • Leadership experience and joy in dealing with different personalities and cultural backgrounds distinguish you
  • Your experience in software engineering or data science helps you securely connect both disciplines. Ideally, you will be familiar with C#/.NET, distributed systems, modern web technologies and machine learning models, Python or DAGs.
  • AI-supported development with Claude Code, Agents and Skills is part of your everyday life – from analysis to testing. You use these tools reflectively and critically check their results
  • Agile principles, guidelines and formalities are familiar to you. You apply them pragmatically and with eye care
  • Your interest is in the product and its further development. You focus on the impact of your work and question the concrete benefits for our customers
  • Positive charisma, natural motivational ability and ability to inspire teams as well as create an environment of psychological security
  • Flowing knowledge of German and English in words and writing
  • The willingness to question “as it has always been done” and propose more efficient, automated or effective solutions
  • Have the courage to try things without planning them down to the smallest detail
  • German (flowing)
  • English (flowing)
