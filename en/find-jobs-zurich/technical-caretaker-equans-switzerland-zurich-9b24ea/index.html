Full description

You are looking for a task in which you take on responsibility, are technically challenged and can work independently. Then you are right with us.

You can move that with us.

In this function, you will ensure the smooth operation of various properties in the Zurich Hardbrücke area and keep technical systems under control at all times.

Your field of activity includes

  • Support and supervision of all building installations (HLKSE)
  • Planning, control and documentation of maintenance
  • Carrying out minor repair and maintenance work
  • Administrative handling of day-to-day operations
  • Organisation and implementation of building technology projects
  • Development of optimisation options to reduce operating costs
  • Pictet service

That's what makes you.

You bring technical know-how and work reliably, structured and efficiently.

This sets you apart

  • Basic technical training in the field of HVAC, specialist / -woman maintenance or electrical
  • Further training as a maintenance specialist is advantageous
  • Experience in technical building maintenance
  • Good organizational skills and clear communication
  • Independent, solution-oriented working method
  • Strong concept of service
  • Driving licence Cat. B
  • Good computer user knowledge

That's what we offer you.

  • Responsible, varied activity
  • Modern working environment and short decision paths
  • Plenty of room for personal initiative and technical and personal development
