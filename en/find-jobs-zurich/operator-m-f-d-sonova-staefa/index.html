Full description

As an operator, you work in the production of our innovative hearing solutions and electronic components. With craftsmanship, a keen eye for quality and the joy of precise work, you and your team can help create products that make a difference for millions of people.

You can expect a modern and varied production environment – from filigree work under the microscope to assembly and quality control to the operation of automated production and testing facilities.

Production experience is required. For us, however, the decisive factor is your motivation, a careful way of working and your willingness to learn new things and develop yourself, as well as your willingness to work in three layers.

More about your tasks

  • Implementation of precise manual work on hearing aids and electronic assemblies, partly under the screen or conventional microscope
  • Sticking, painting, masking and visual quality controls
  • Operation of automated and semi-automated production and testing facilities
  • Packaging of hearing aids, accessories, parts and electronic components
  • Ensuring correct documentation of production orders as well as working and order times
  • Compliance with our quality, safety and labor standards and support for a reliable and efficient production process
  • Active participation in shop floor activities and continuous improvements as well as support for other production areas as needed

More about you

  • Higher secondary education I and II; technical vocational training or further training is advantageous
  • Enjoyment of precise and fine motor work as well as a strong quality and safety awareness
  • Reliable, careful and structured working methods as well as flexibility and willingness to learn
  • Enjoyment of working together in a team and an open and constructive approach to feedback
  • Knowledge of German at least at level B1 in order to be able to safely understand work instructions and production documentation. English is an advantage.
  • Basic PC skills
  • Experience with MS Office, SAP or similar applications is an advantage
  • Experience in a production environment – especially in electronics, medical technology or precision manufacturing – as well as knowledge in soldering, SMD equipment or working under the microscope are advantageous, but not a prerequisite

More about what we offer

As one of the world’s leading providers of hearing solutions, headquartered in Switzerland, we offer you an international and innovative working environment in which quality, collaboration and continuous development are the focus.

With us, you work on high-quality products that help to improve people’s quality of life on a daily basis. You will receive a structured familiarisation and the opportunity to develop your skills in different production areas and build up additional expertise.

We promote a working environment in which people can take responsibility, learn from each other and be successful together.
