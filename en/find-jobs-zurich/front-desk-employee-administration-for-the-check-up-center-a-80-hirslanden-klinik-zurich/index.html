Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Composition by: 01. October 2026 or by arrangement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 72081

In this interesting and versatile activity, you take over the professional care of our national and international patients, who come to us for one- or multi-day check-ups as well as the additional care of top athletes who are connected with us.

As the first contact person for our customers, you represent the Check-Up Center and provide a professional first impression with your friendly appearance and your service-oriented nature.

YOUR TASKS

  • Reception and support of clients
  • Planning and scheduling of check-ups
  • Blood sampling, eye pressure measurements and laboratory activities
  • Power measurement
  • Appointment coordination with specialists and other external bodies

Your profile

  • Commercial training completed
  • Additional medical training beneficial (e.g. MPA, dental assistant)
  • Several years of professional experience in a similar function with focus on administrative activities
  • Good IT skills
  • Very good knowledge of German and English

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. You work with a modern medical clinic information system and can expect extensive training and development opportunities from us. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family responsibilities. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. Employees who have to move completely for their work receive four additional paid days off with a 100% workload. With a reduced workload, the days off reduce accordingly. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from an equivalent financial compensation. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.
