Full description

We are looking for a committed and motivated specialist who, together with the cleaning team, can carry out the cleaning work in the Museum Rietberg. In addition, you are responsible for compliance with and implementation of the hygiene, safety and safety guidelines.

Tasks

  • They organize and coordinate the daily cleaning work and work actively in the daily cleaning.
  • You take over the professional and personnel management of the cleaning team and plan work assignments, holidays and absences.
  • They ensure a high cleaning quality and attach particular importance to careful cleaning in the environment of art objects, exhibition structures and museum facilities.
  • They are responsible for material management in the area of cleaning (orders, storage, consumption control) and the organisation of cleaning textiles (washing, drying, provisioning).
  • They plan and periodically carry out special cleaning in ancillary rooms, warehouses, technical zones and sensitive areas and support cleaning work in the context of exhibition changes or events.

Profile

  • Completed vocational training as a building cleaner* in EFZ or equivalent training
  • Several years of professional experience in cleaning, ideally in a museum or in a public environment
  • Very good knowledge of current cleaning techniques, means and machinery
  • Experience in team management, leadership training is beneficial
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

We offer

It awaits you a versatile and responsible activity with creative leeway in a culturally shaped environment. They work in a central workplace in a culturally historically significant environment. In addition, we offer up-to-date employment conditions in accordance with municipal personnel law, good social benefits and a collegial working atmosphere.

About us

The Museum Rietberg is dedicated to the art of the traditional but also the contemporary cultures of Africa, America, Asia and Oceania as well as their diasporas. With around one hundred employees, it is one of the most important art museums in Switzerland and enjoys international recognition.
