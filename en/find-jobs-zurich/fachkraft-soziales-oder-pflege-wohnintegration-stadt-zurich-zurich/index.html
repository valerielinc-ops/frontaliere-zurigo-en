Full description

Supervised residential integration is aimed at highly disintegrated persons who largely refuse to be looked after and offers furnished apartments and professional care/monitoring around the clock 365 days a year. The residents are addictive and/or mentally ill people whose living and social skills are insufficient to be accommodated in one of the existing residential integration services.

For our team of supervised residential integration at Neufrankengasse 6 in Zurich, we are looking for an experienced professional as of December 2026. The internal job title is Psychosocial Supervisor.

Working hours: shift service (07.00-16.00 / 13.00-22.00) as well as 1-2 weekend and 3-4 pikett services per month (16.15-07.15 incl. overnight stay).

Tasks

  • They monitor the behavior of the clients and their health status.
  • They carry out crisis interventions in medical and psychiatric emergencies and, if necessary, involve third parties (e.g. emergency psychiatrists, police).
  • They demand minimal housing and social skills, enforce house rules and ensure the exchange of information with the participating bodies.

Profile

  • Degree in social work FH/HF or as a nurse FH/HF specialty psychiatry
  • Several years of professional experience in the low-threshold area with addicts and with mentally impaired people
  • Clear, appreciative communication and professional handling of challenging situations
  • assertiveness and high social and self-competence
  • Enjoyment of a demanding field of work as well as readiness for shift, weekend and piquet services

We offer

A field of work for people who stay when other offers end. Challenging, meaningful and supported by a strong team with a 24-hour presence. In addition, there are good employment conditions and opportunities for professional development.

About us

With around 2,400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a social Zurich. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.
