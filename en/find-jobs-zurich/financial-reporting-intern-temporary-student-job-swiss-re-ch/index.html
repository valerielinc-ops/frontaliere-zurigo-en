Original description

Are you interested in understanding how global companies report their financial results? Do you enjoy working with data, learning new technologies, and turning financial analysis into action? About the Role Join our Finance reporting team and gain hands-on experience in statutory reporting and analysing financial performance. You will contribute to strategic projects and work with stakeholders across Swiss Re's global portfolio of legal entities (e.g. service, real estate, investment, financing, and holding entities). This role offers the opportunity to combine finance, technology, and data analytics, while contributing to initiatives with real business impact. Key responsibilities

  • Contribute to the preparation of financial statements and reporting packages for assigned legal entities
  • Analyse financial data while building your understanding of Swiss Re's operations and financial performance
  • Collaborate with colleagues across different countries and functions
  • Support innovation initiatives using automation, data analytics, and AI
  • Present findings and reporting outcomes to management and other stakeholders, including statutory auditors
  • Build practical experience with financial controls , governance frameworks, and regulatory reporting requirements in a global organization

About the Team Our Bratislava-based Non-Technical Carriers Reporting team is responsible for end-to-end financial reporting for a diverse portfolio of Swiss Re entities around the world, with a focus on key hubs across Europe, the US, and Asia.

About you

You are a proactive, self-motivated learner who enjoys solving problems through data-driven insights. You thrive in a collaborative international environment and bring a strong sense of ownership, initiative, and a team-oriented mindset to everything you do.

We are looking for candidates who

  • Are currently pursuing a university degree (3rd to 5th year) in Finance, Accounting, Audit, Data Analytics, or a related field
  • Have an understanding or previous experience with accounting principles and financial statements
  • Enjoy analysing data, solving problems, and thinking critically
  • Are comfortable using Microsoft Office tools , particularly Excel and PowerPoint
  • Communicate effectively and enjoy collaborating with others
  • Have a good command of written and spoken English

Additional qualifications (nice to have)

  • Demonstrated interest in working with data through automation , AI, or modern reporting technologies, such as SAP, Power BI, Palantir Foundry, or similar
  • Knowledge of an additional European language , particularly German

Our company has a hybrid work model where the expectation is that you will be in the office at least 50% of working time. For Slovakia the base salary for this position is 7 EUR / gross / hour. Please note limited benefits apply to student roles.
