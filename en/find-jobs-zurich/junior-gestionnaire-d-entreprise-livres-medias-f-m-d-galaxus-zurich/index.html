Full description

Our category management is at the heart of the range: we develop, buy and market the products in our two online shops. Our goal? A broad, up-to-date offer with prices that convince – always with an eye on our customers.

Mansioni

  • You control the business of your product category: you have revenue, margin and contribution margins under control through clean analysis, smart planning and concrete measures
  • Marketing plans for promotions, daily offers and novelties? Develop and implement yourself

  • You recognize trends and new topics early on – together with Category Portfolio Development you bring a breath of fresh air into the range

  • Subsequent conditions you calculate and check them reliably
  • The business customer department supports you with offers and procurement
