Full description

Amstein + Walthert is an engineering company with around 1100 employees:innen. As engineers, planners and consultants, we work on all aspects of the technology of civil engineering and construction over the entire life cycle. Our solutions are innovative, elegant and sustainable. These are your tasks

  • 3D and BIM modelling of the electrical engineering systems
  • Digital project planning of complex building technology projects in building construction
  • Development of digital solutions in the entire construction planning area
  • Independent conception and planning of subprojects
  • Participation in internal and external project meetings
  • Support of the team in various interesting projects for the public sector, industrial and service enterprises
  • Development / drawing of plans and schemes
  • Close cooperation with other departments through interdisciplinary teams
  • Basic electrical engineering
  • Knowledge of BIM modeling software preferably Autodesk Revit
  • BIM / Digitalization enthusiast with the courage to embark on new paths and actively shape them
  • Knowledge in the construction of data structures and execution of data evaluations
  • Creation of scripts
  • Developing and maintaining databases is familiar to you and interests you
  • Strong networked thinking for system optimization and building project data models
  • Enthusiasm and team player
  • Good professional network in the field of digital planning
  • Safe appearance and skillful handling of customers
  • Independent, reliable personality with pleasure in demanding work
  • Teamwork, social competence and high customer orientation
  • Holistic thinking
  • Complete knowledge of German in words and writing, English is advantageous
