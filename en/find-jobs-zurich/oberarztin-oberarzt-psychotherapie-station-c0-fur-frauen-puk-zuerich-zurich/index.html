Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 100% For our psychotherapy unit for women with a focus on personality and trauma follow-up disorders of the Center for Depression, Anxiety Disorders and Psychotherapy of Adult Psychiatry at the Lenggstrasse site, we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by arrangement, initially limited to 6 months with option of renewal, one or more Their tasks

  • Leading a medical/psychological team and supervising the treatment of inpatients with mental illness, including diagnosis, therapy and exit planning
  • Application of targeted treatment concepts in the context of the focus on personality disorders and trauma follow-up disorders, in particular dialectical-behavioral therapy (DBT) and trauma-specific therapies (especially narrative exposure therapy / DBT-PTSD)
  • Supervision of treatment by and supervision of assistant doctors and psychologists
  • Participation in teaching and further training of assistant doctors and psychologists
  • Further development of the treatment concept of the station in cooperation with the multi-professional team and the care and in coordination with other units with a focus on personality disorders and trauma follow-up disorders in the center

Your profile

  • Presence of a Swiss medical diploma (MEBEKO registered) or MEBEKO recognition of the foreign medical diploma (not registration) plus MEBEKO language entry German
  • Specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy completed or shortly before
  • Leadership experience with high independence and self-organization
  • experience in personality and trauma follow-up disorders
  • Knowledge of specific therapeutic procedures is advantageous
  • Interest in innovative treatment concepts including interventional psychiatry (e.g. repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation [rTMS], electroconvulsion treatment [EKT], electroencephalogram diagnosis [EEG])
  • German language skills in word and writing at level C2

Our offer

  • Clinic, research and teaching united: working in a university environment with international networking
  • Development with perspective: Diverse career paths in one of the largest psychiatric clinics in Switzerland
  • Shaping the future actively: Participation in change and development processes
  • Sustainable travel: Participation in the ZVV-BonusPass or general subscription
  • Future provision with added value: 60% employer contribution to the pension fund
  • Well taken care of in everyday work: discounted food offers or lunch checks
