Your team

Are you looking for an exciting challenge in the field of financial services? Our Risk & Regulation team offers a leading Swiss consulting and auditing firm You have the opportunity to actively participate in the further development of the financial industry. As part of our team, You will conduct regulatory audits with a focus on money laundering prevention and help financial institutions navigate a dynamic, complex environment. Zurich By agreement 100% Advisory services Everyone welcome ID: 742590WD

Your impact

  • Check compliance with the regulatory requirements for combating money laundering and terrorist financing for financial service providers in Switzerland and Liechtenstein.
  • Gain insights into the functioning and processes of different financial services providers and evaluate their effectiveness.
  • Supports us in actively shaping the future of auditing and building trust with our customers and stakeholders.
  • Works on a diversified portfolio of financial clients and is in contact with the Money Laundering Department and Compliance Department.
  • Conduct regulatoryly driven gap and impact analyses and derive concrete recommendations for action from them.
  • Continuously develop your technical know-how and soft skills through on-the-job and off-the-job training.
  • Work in an inspiring team within an international network and receive regular feedback from colleagues and supervisors.

Your skill set

  • You have a master’s degree in law, economics or a comparable education that prepares you for the application of financial market regulation. Initial professional experience in a financial institution, a supervisory authority or in an auditing and consulting firm is an advantage.
  • You are interested in regulatory issues and business contexts and enjoy working independently and customer-oriented in a specialized team.
  • You have a quick apprehension and ideally have already gained initial experience in dealing with customers.
  • You are motivated to train yourself on- and off-the-job and take on new challenges.
  • Dedication and your solution-oriented working style distinguishes you.
  • You have very good knowledge of German and English.
