Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 100% The Management of Nursing, Therapies and Social Work (Directorate PTS) employs around 1,100 people from different professional groups. For the Center for Depression, Anxiety and Psychotherapy of the Clinic for Adult Psychiatry and Psychotherapy in Zurich we are looking for 01. February 2027 or by agreement one or more Their tasks

  • Ensuring care in the Center for Depression, Anxiety and Psychotherapy according to "Best Practice" and "Recovery" approaches
  • Leadership and development of around 80 employees and managers in a university hospital, responsible for personnel, material and training costs budget
  • Collaboration with an interprofessional leadership team and active support of interprofessional treatment teams
  • Responsibility for the implementation of the qualitative and quantitative training mandate in cooperation with the Competence Center Education of the Directorate PTS
  • Further development of care, initiate and implement innovation processes in care and human resources (e.g. practice-relevant career planning, employee campaigns, integration of Advanced Practice Nursing – models in patient care)
  • Collaboration in cross-divisional and cross-clinic projects as well as active networking with internal and external partners

Your profile

  • Engaged and resilient personality with many years of leadership experience and interest in interprofessional cooperation
  • Swiss-recognised education or study in nursing (at least HF) with further education at master level in management, management and leadership areas
  • Pronounced business management, specialized and innovative thinking
  • Self-employed, efficient and service-oriented working methods
  • Very good communication skills as well as oral and written language skills

Our offer

  • Knowledge that advances: Extensive initial, further and further training offers
  • Strong in the team: appreciative cooperation and mutual support
  • Focus on health: Various sports offerings, green oases and an internal care team
  • Future provision with added value: 60% employer contribution to the pension fund
  • Sustainable travel: Participation in the ZVV-BonusPass or general subscription
  • Well supplied in everyday work: Discounted catering offers
