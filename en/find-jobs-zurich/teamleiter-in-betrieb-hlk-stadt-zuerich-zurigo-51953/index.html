Full description

Between Zurich and Geneva, the responsibility for a large HVAC plant portfolio lies with you – including team leadership and the focus on reliable and efficient operation.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for a large HVAC portfolio between Zurich and Geneva.
  • You will lead a team of 6-9 maintenance technicians, coordinate and monitor operations and maintenance work.
  • A reliable supply of our customers is important to you and for customers you are the contact person for inquiries.
  • You ensure compliance with the contracts and the efficiency of the assets within your team.
  • Quality is your top priority – regular reporting is a matter of course and you implement our standards.

Profile

  • You have already successfully led employees, are strong at implementation and have a good knowledge of German and French.
  • You bring several years of experience in the operation and maintenance of heating and cooling systems.
  • The building technical expertise is complemented by business and management-specific competencies.
  • Even in hectic moments you keep the overview.
  • Strategic thinking and experience in economical and efficient management round off your profile.

We offer

  • More energy for your life: With mobile work, flexible working models and part-time opportunities, you can actively shape your working time and divide it into the best fit for your life. For example, you can buy up to 2 weeks of extra holidays and work part-time at all levels. Job sharing/splitting and unpaid holidays are also possible with us.
  • Sustainable and cheaper travel: With the mobility contribution of up to 600 francs a year, you travel cheaply and sustainably – regardless of whether you want to cycle through Zurich by bike or explore Switzerland by train.
  • An investment in your future: We not only value paying fair and transparent wages, but also ensure that you have more of your wages left over. We take over 60% of the savings contributions and additionally offer a super-compulsory interest on the capital accumulated.
  • An energy boost for your free time: We pay you 100 francs a month on your lunch-check card. In addition, you benefit annually from further Fringe Benefits worth 150 francs and attractive mobile subscriptions.
  • Development and training: With our talent management, we actively tackle your development. You benefit from versatile internal and external training opportunities and exciting opportunities to develop further.
  • Meaningful work: Sustainability and meaningful work are not just words for us: shape Zurich and the future for us. Together with us, release energy for a good morning.

About us

ewz has been dynamic and visionary on the road every day for more than 130 years. Whether sustainable energy production, smart energy solution or innovative networking technology: Develop with us today what is important tomorrow.
