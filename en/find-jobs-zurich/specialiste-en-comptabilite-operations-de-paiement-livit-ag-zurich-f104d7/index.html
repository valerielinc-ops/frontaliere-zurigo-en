Full description

Are you open-minded, ambitious and value a future-oriented way of working on the pulse of the real estate industry? With your commitment, we face the challenges of today and tomorrow together. Do you want to participate and make a difference? Then you are exactly right with Livit. We look forward to your personal contribution.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for account management and electronic payments for a defined number of current accounts at banks/Postfinance
  • You book the payment and payment receipts and take over the account reconciliations
  • You are responsible for creating payout files and individual payments
  • You process and check incoming, automatically non-accountable tenant payments for responsible current accounts
  • You support in the preparation of LSV/DD files rental interest collection, in the control of collateral (rental interest deposits) as well as projects for payment transactions and/or finance

Requirements

  • You have a basic commercial education and a further education in accounting
  • You have proven knowledge in accounting and payments, an advantage in the real estate industry
  • You have a good understanding of debt/have, balance sheet/performance statement and effort/yield
  • You can communicate verbally and in writing in German, oral and written knowledge of French and/or Italian is an advantage
  • You are well versed in dealing with the Office applications (Excel, Word and Outlook) and have an advantage knowledge of Mammut LION and REM
  • You have self-learning skills and you enjoy sharing your knowledge with others.

We offer

  • Internal offer of our Livit Academy for specialist and leadership courses
  • Financial and/or time support for external specialist training and access to electronic learning platforms for online language courses
  • Individual career planning and development prospects
  • Home / Mobile Office
  • Bonus depending on company success and personal performance
  • At least 5 weeks holiday and purchase of up to 10 additional days holiday (Ferienflex)
  • Above-average pension benefits and premiums for medical day allowance and accident insurance are 100% covered by Livit
  • 14 days paternity leave

We love real estate and are enthusiastic about preserving the value of our clients’ properties.
