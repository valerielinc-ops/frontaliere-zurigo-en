Centre for Dentistry

Job opening immediately or by agreement

The Center for Dentistry of the University of Zurich (ZZM) is an internationally recognized clinic, training and research center. In six specialized clinics and one institute, dentists work at the forefront of knowledge and in interdisciplinary teams to achieve the highest quality in patient treatment, teaching and research. In the Clinic for Oral Surgery, patients are treated in the entire field of dentoalveolar outpatient surgery and implantology. Furthermore, a daily mucous membrane consultation session takes place. The clinic provides its services according to modern quality-related guidelines. For our team billing we are looking for a committed personality as a disease representative.

Tasks

  • Management and support of the accounting team incl. Personnel planning, task coordination and employee development
  • Ensuring correct, complete and timely billing
  • Examination and processing of services, invoices, cost estimates and cost language
  • Processing patient inquiries and billing complaints
  • Analysis and optimization of existing processes as well as development and implementation of efficient workflows
  • Creation, monitoring and presentation of relevant KPIs and metrics
  • Close cooperation with clinic and team management, reception, treatment, finance/controlling, IT, HR and external partners

Profile

  • In-depth training in finance, accounting or comparable qualifications; min. 6 years of professional experience
  • Leadership experience
  • In-depth knowledge in dental tariff and billing systems as well as in the calculation of cost estimates is advantageous
  • Experience in process design and change management
  • High affinity for numbers and a structured, reliable and solution-oriented working method
  • Enforcement, conflict, negotiating skills and discretion
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • English skills are beneficial
