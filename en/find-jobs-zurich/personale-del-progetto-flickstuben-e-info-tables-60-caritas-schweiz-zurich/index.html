Project staff* in “Flickstuben” and “Info-Tables” (60%)

The educational and integration program "LearningLocal" offers various offers in the field of education and integration.

Our eight patch offices in the Canton of Zurich offer migrant women the opportunity to acquire specialist knowledge in sewing, to get in touch with the population and to gain a first reference for finding work. The offer thus creates important foundations for the social and economic integration of women with a migrant background.

The info-tables are low-threshold contact points for newcomers, where they receive multilingual initial information and find support in orientation in the new environment. For the offers "Flickstuben" and "Info-Tables" we are looking for a project employee (60%) as of January 1, 2027 or by agreement.

  • Operational coordination and support of the eight Flickstubs
  • Recruitment, introduction and support of the approximately 35 volunteers who carry out the Flickstuben on site
  • Ensuring quality and further development of supply
  • Networking with municipalities, parishes and social and specialist bodies
  • Project documentation and reporting
  • Co-operation in raising funds
  • &Ouml;public relations and communication
  • Administrative tasks
  • Coordination and implementation of info-tables
  • Provision and provision of initial multilingual information for newcomers
  • University degree in social work, social pedagogy or a comparable subject
  • In-depth knowledge of the social supply landscape in the Canton of Zurich and in the area of migration
  • Experience in working with and accompanying volunteers
  • Strong flair for planning, organization and coordination
  • Intercultural competence, high social competence and enjoyment of working with people from different backgrounds
  • Further languages as well as knowledge of sewing and patching are advantageous
  • Category B driving licence and willingness to work from Tuesday to Thursday
