Original description

The Opportunity Ready to shape the future of Life & Health reinsurance contracts across Asia Pacific? Join Swiss Re in a regional leadership role with accountability for the APAC Contracts function. Partnering with APAC leadership and the Global Head of L&H Contracts, you'll strengthen commercial discipline and governance while leading a high-performing team. You'll also shape the future of the function by modernizing workflows and accelerating the adoption of GenAI-enabled solutions across the region. About the Role APAC is a diverse and strategically important region within Swiss Re’s L&H reinsurance portfolio. The role leads contract drafting discipline, governance rigor, and in-force contractual risk review within a high-volume, performance-driven underwriting environment. You will serve as a business partner to Swiss Re L&H's APAC business, translating underwriting intent and legal advice into disciplined contract execution that protects long-term value. The role owns deviation governance, negotiation consistency and contractual analysis across the region, while driving modernization through workflow tools and GenAI-enabled practices. While strong legal and drafting expertise is important, this role sits within the L&H Chief Underwriting Officer's organization and partners closely with legal, underwriting, pricing, claims, portfolio management and risk functions. You will lead a regional team of nine Contracts Specialists located across multiple markets in APAC, providing clear direction, building capability, and fostering strong collaboration and consistency across locations. Reporting to the Global Head of L&H Contracts, this role is a member of the L&H Contracts Leadership Team and plays a visible role in shaping the evolution of the Contracts function. About the Life & Health Contracts Function The L&H Contracts function drafts, reviews and advises on reinsurance agreements between Swiss Re and its clients. In close partnership with underwriting, pricing, claims, legal, and risk, the team:

  • Structures and documents traditional reinsurance agreements
  • Ensures contractual terms reflect underwriting intent
  • Provides clear advice on wording implications and risk trade-offs
  • Supports disciplined portfolio management through contracts analysis

Contracts plays a critical role in safeguarding Swiss Re’s risk position while enabling sustainable long-term profitability. Your Mandate

  1. Strengthen Commercial Discipline
  • Ensure contracts reflect underwriting intent and disciplined risk-taking
  • Act as a trusted partner to Client Markets and cross-functional stakeholders (underwriting, pricing, claims, legal, etc), shaping deal structure and contract outcomes
  • Provide clear, decisive recommendations on wording and structural trade-offs, with hands-on involvement in key client accounts
  • Lead commercial interpretation, analysis, and revision of existing agreements
  1. Strengthen Governance & Standardization
  • Ensure consistent application of global contracting standards
  • Own deviation governance, tracking, and transparency across the region
  • Strengthen documentation rigor and decision traceability
  1. Talent Management & Leadership
  • Lead, mentor, and develop a team of Contracts Specialists based across several locations in Asia
  • Define clear roles, accountability, and career pathways for Contracts Counsels
  • Represent APAC within the L&H Contracts Leadership Team
  1. Accelerate Digital Workflow & GenAI Adoption
  • Lead regional adoption of contracting workflow tools and GenAI-enabled practices, embedding responsible and scalable AI use into standard drafting and review workflows

About You

  • 12–15+ years’ experience in Life & Health (re)insurance contracting, including significant drafting and negotiation of (re)insurance agreements; legal qualification preferred
  • Demonstrated commercial judgment and ability to articulate risk trade-offs
  • Proven people leadership experience, with the ability to lead, engage and develop a team of nine professionals working across multiple locations and markets in a regulated, complex reinsurance environment
  • Experience leading multi-location teams in a regulated, complex reinsurance environment
  • A high-accountability leader who drives performance and sets clear standards
  • Decisive and commercially pragmatic, with the confidence to challenge stakeholders constructively and make disciplined risk decisions
  • Committed to developing talent and strengthening capabilities
  • Experience driving workflow modernization and technology-enabled change
