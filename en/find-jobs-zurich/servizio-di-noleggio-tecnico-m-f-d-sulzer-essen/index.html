Original description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

As our new Rental Service Technician, you play a central role in supporting and advancing our rental business. You are responsible for the installation, commissioning, maintenance, repair and dismantling of rental facilities for customers throughout Germany. In addition, you support the maintenance and operational readiness of our rental assets at our rental location. As an important interface between customers, project teams, service partners and internal departments, you ensure that rental facilities are installed, operated and maintained safely, reliably and efficiently. With your technical know-how, you contribute significantly to customer satisfaction and the high availability of our rental fleet. The position combines field operations, technical service missions and workshop work in a dynamic environment. You will make a significant contribution to the growth of our rental business and to the successful implementation of customer projects. Your main tasks and responsibilities

  • Implementation of installations, commissioning, disassembly and technical service operations at customer rental facilities on site as well as support for fault diagnosis and troubleshooting of mechanical and electrical systems.
  • Carry out maintenance, inspection and repair work on pumps, controls, piping systems, metrology, vacuum equipment and other rental equipment to ensure safe and reliable operation.
  • Support in the testing, preparation and preparation of rental facilities for new projects, including documentation of equipment statuses, service requirements and maintenance of service and facility data.
  • Close cooperation with customers, project managers, service partners and internal teams as well as ensuring compliance with quality, safety and environmental standards during all project and service activities.

What you should bring for this position

  • Successfully completed technical vocational training, for example in the field of electrical engineering, mechatronics, industrial mechanics, maintenance or a comparable discipline.
  • Professional experience in technical service, field service, maintenance, repair or installation of industrial plants and equipment.
  • Technical understanding in dealing with pump systems, wastewater applications, electrical controls or comparable industrial systems is advantageous.
  • Ability to read technical drawings, schematics and technical documentation as well as systematic error analysis and problem solving.
  • Strong practical working methods with high personal responsibility, reliability and safety awareness.
  • Good communication and team skills as well as a customer-oriented and professional way of working.
  • Secure handling of changing priorities and the willingness to work flexibly in a project-oriented environment.
  • Driving license class B as well as high willingness to travel within Germany with regular multi-day assignments and overnight stays.

What we offer you

  • Close cooperation with experienced service, project and rental teams in a technically demanding environment.
  • Diverse and varied projects with customers from the municipal and industrial water and wastewater industry.
  • Varied activity with a combination of field service, technical maintenance, customer support and workshop support.
  • Active participation in the growth of a future-oriented business area as well as attractive development and further education opportunities.
