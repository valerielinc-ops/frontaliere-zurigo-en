Full description

What are your tasks? In the Culture team, you will be engaged on a fixed-term basis from September 1, 2026, to November 30, 2026 (or by agreement) for the following topics:

  • You will support the authors of the editorial team in researching and implementing contributions
  • You will assist during filming and in the concept and editing phase
  • You will take minutes of the internal editorial meetings and support the editorial team in administrative processes
  • You will independently seek topics and, if possible, implement them yourself in a contribution. What do you bring with you?
  • You are studying (or have already completed your studies) and have gained some journalistic experience
  • You are interested in cultural and social topics; you enjoy researching and are tech-savvy
  • You have a good command of language, write confidently, and are eager to contribute and implement your own ideas
  • Ideally, you have some experience in producing video contributions (filming, editing, sound design). What can you look forward to?
  • You will gain insight into how cultural background contributions are created in a large media company
  • You will learn about processes and planning and gain valuable know-how in handling texts, images, and videos
  • You will be part of a collegial environment and operate in a creative team
  • You will have the opportunity to bring in your own topics and ideas, research them, and turn them into your own TV stories. For the application process: Oliver Wolf HR Consultant +41 58 135 02 79 The Culture department includes eleven multimedia teams, grouped into four multimedia groups: "Culture", "Society", "Knowledge", and "Fiction". The teams work interdisciplinary, according to the genres and topics defined within the framework of the SRF 2024 strategy for specific formats and content.
