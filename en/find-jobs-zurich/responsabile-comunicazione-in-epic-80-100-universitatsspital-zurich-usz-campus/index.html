Role overview

As part of the zKIS/EPIC program, you will actively shape the central transformation of the University Hospital Zurich.

With the introduction of a new clinic information system, we are not only changing our digital infrastructure, but also clinical processes, collaboration and working methods.

EPIC is thus an essential component on the way to becoming the “hospital of the future” and is intended to sustainably support the everyday work of employees.

Main responsibilities

  • You will be responsible for the operational and conceptual communication of the zKIS/EPIC project and position the project as part of the overall transformation to the "Spital of the Future
  • You develop and control the communication planning for the project in close coordination with corporate communication
  • Ensure that the project is consistently, comprehensibly and appropriately positioned internally and embedded in the overall communication of the USZ
  • You advise the project management on communicative issues and support in the preparation of decisions, appearances and sensitive topics
  • You recognize communication risks and challenges at an early stage and support the development of suitable communication measures
  • You proactively collect information from the project environment, prepare it for the addressee and ensure a continuous flow of information
  • You develop formats for information, participation and mobilization of employees and contribute to the active project community
  • You will ensure that EPIC is understood communicatively as a central component of the “Hospital of the Future” and integrate project communication with the further transformation and strategy topics of the USZ Requirements
  • You have several years of experience (ideally 8-10 years) in corporate, project or transformation communication

Company and context

  • You have experience in complex organizational or major project environments, ideally in healthcare or in highly regulated environments
  • You can develop strategic communication approaches and simultaneously implement them operationally
  • You are able to process complex content in a comprehensible way and convey it in a manner appropriate to the target group
  • You have experience in advising leaders and dealing with sensitive or political issues
  • You recognize communicative risks early on and can develop appropriate measures
  • You work independently, structured and with high initiative in a dynamic environment
  • You have a strong sense of language, impact and storytelling in transformation projects
  • You are open, curious and interested in the further development of the "Spital of the Future We offer:
