Full description

Become part of one of our dedicated product teams and develop innovative software solutions as a Software Engineer that make a real impact – for our internal processes as well as for our more than 3 million customers in Switzerland and the EU. With your curiosity, team spirit, and willingness to learn, you will grow with us and actively shape our platform.

Tasks

  • As a Software Engineer in the Product Development area, you are responsible for the implementation and continuous development of features that improve the shopping experience
  • You develop new features together in the team, implement technical innovations, and ensure that our software remains user-friendly, performant, and scalable
  • You take care of the technical infrastructure of your area of responsibility, monitor the systems, and ensure stable and reliable operation
  • You work closely with other engineers as well as colleagues from your team to realize customer-oriented solutions
  • You feel comfortable in an agile, experimental environment and enjoy contributing your own ideas to continuously improve our platform

Requirements

  • At least 3 years: In object-oriented software development (ideally with .NET technologies or C#)
  • Completed degree (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): In the field of computer science
  • You are already familiar with one or more technologies from our tech stack: .NET/C#, relational databases (MSSQL, BigQuery), non-relational databases (MongoDB, Redis, Elasticsearch) as well as Azure
  • Experience in our frontend stack (React, Next, GraphQL) is an advantage, but not a must
  • Willingness to learn, curiosity, and the ability to familiarize yourself with new topics and technologies
  • You recognize potential for improvement and can implement changes to increase productivity and effectiveness with support
  • The ability to adapt to continuous changes in the work environment
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • English (very good knowledge)

What we offer

  • Values instead of blah-blah: Cooperative, innovative, pirate-like, self-responsible, ambitious – we demand these five values from everyone and live them ourselves.
  • Think for yourself: We encourage everyone to think for themselves and actively participate. And we love it when you think boldly, newly, and differently.
  • Uncomplicated: We avoid the unnecessary and focus on what is important. From mastering challenges to celebrating our successes.
  • Flexibility instead of bending (Office Jobs): In addition to home office options, we enable you to take up to 8 weeks of workation. Furthermore, every position is possible at an 80% workload.
  • Rapid growth: With us, you grow and develop continuously. And you receive EDU points (2,000 CHF for employees in Switzerland, 1,600 EUR for German employees, and 700 EUR for our Serbian employees) for your further development so that you don't stand still.
  • Further benefits Switzerland: Benefit from 25 days of vacation, exclusive employee prices in the shop, mobile, internet, and TV subscriptions at corporate rates, and other advantages.
