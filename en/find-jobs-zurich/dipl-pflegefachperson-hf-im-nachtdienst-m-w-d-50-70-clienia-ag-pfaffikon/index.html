Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With more than 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric offer of Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in an outpatient, day clinic and inpatient setting. The Group employs around 1800 people and achieved a turnover of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For the area of adult psychiatry, the Station Park C, with the treatment focus acute crisis, we are looking for by agreement, a

Station Park C offers adults between the ages of 18 and 60 years, support and assistance in psychological crisis and stress situations. We work according to current clinical guidelines and findings of evidence-based medicine. The recovery-oriented therapeutic concept of the station combines disorder-specific therapeutic elements with established crisis intervention techniques (safewards concept) and milieutherapeutic action factors.

Their tasks Her core tasks include the individual, professional care and care of patients at night. They provide safety on the ward and, in cooperation with on-duty doctors, they can manage crises professionally. They use nursing diagnostics as a working tool, support the implementation of individual care and crisis plans and ensure a calm, stable atmosphere on the station. You work in accordance with the Stations and Safewards concept. The coordinated and multidisciplinary cooperation offers our patients the best possible treatment success.

Your profile You have completed training for the Dipl. Nurse HF/FH, are interested in the psychiatric area. She has empathy and the ability to act calmly and safely in crisis situations. You also have the pleasure of meeting our patients at eye level and understanding for adults from the 18th. Year of life for their specific problem management. If you are a team-capable, communicative and prudent personality, we will gladly accept your application.

What is important to us is the careful, structured training of new employees. To support you in your professional development You are supported by your direct supervisor as well as by specific training and further education. The Clienia Littenheid offers various perks and a flexible billing system for additional services.
