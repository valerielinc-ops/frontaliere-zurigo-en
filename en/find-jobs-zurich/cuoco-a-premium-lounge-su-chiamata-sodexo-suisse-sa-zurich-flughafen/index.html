Full description

Sodexo was founded in 1966 by Pierre Bellon in Marseille and is the world’s leading provider of sustainable company catering and special experiences in all areas of life. With its two divisions Food and Facilities Management Services, Sodexo meets all the challenges of daily life with a double goal: to improve the quality of life of our employees and the people we serve. Growth and social commitment go hand in hand. Sodexo’s motto is “to create a better everyday for everyone to build a better life for all.” In Switzerland, we are the only provider specialising in facility management and catering services. We pursue a holistic approach for our customers, focusing on infrastructure, people and their well-being as well as the environment. Our approximately 650 employees work in the areas of cleaning, catering, corporate catering, logistics, security and reception services, conference services and technical building management. We always strive to make the everyday life of people in front of and behind the scenes better with our actions and thinking.
