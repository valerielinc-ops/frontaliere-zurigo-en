Full description

Ready to use your risk and financial expertise in a dynamic, global environment? Become part of Swiss Re Corporate Solutions and support our European customers in their worldwide guarantee and guarantee needs. About the position As an underwriter Surety based in Frankfurt, you combine analytical knowledge with customer management and work with companies and brokers in Germany, Austria and Switzerland. The role offers the perfect balance between risk underwriting and customer-related tasks in an entrepreneurial environment. Main tasks

  • Perform risk assessments for new and existing customers, in line with existing risk appetite and highest customer satisfaction
  • Responsibility for own customer portfolio and transactions as well as the development of new business opportunities in Europe, especially in Germany, Austria and Switzerland
  • Analysis and assessment of credit and transaction risks in close coordination with internal stakeholders
  • Approval of transactions within their own competences and preparation of structured templates
  • Establishment and support of customers and broker relationships through regular personal discussions
  • Monitoring your own customer portfolio, including internal and external information
  • Market analyses regarding changes in the underwriting, market and legal environment, as well as their risk impact

About the team As part of the Swiss Re Group with a 150-year history, Swiss Re Corporate Solutions is an important partner in solving the risk management and financing needs of companies worldwide. With a focus on a results-oriented underwriting culture, we are looking for a team-oriented personality to reinforce our growing team of one of the leading providers of guarantee and guarantee solutions.

About you

You will gladly bring your analytical skills and commitment to an international team-oriented environment and enjoy working with customers and sales partners. We are looking for candidates who meet the following requirements:

  • At least 3 years of relevant professional experience, preferably in a bank or with a deposit insurer
  • Confident personality with strong communication skills for dealing with customers, business partners and colleagues
  • Ability to work efficiently in a team-oriented environment
  • Analytical decision-making ability and solution orientation
  • Understanding credit risk and financial markets, particularly in relation to debt financing
  • Negotiation-proof German and fluent English skills

In Germany, the basic salary for this position is between EUR 71,000 and EUR 118,000 (full-time employment). The specific salary offer takes into account:

  • Requirements, scope, complexity and responsibility of the position
  • The profile of the applicant, including education/qualifications, expertise, specialisation, skills and experience

In addition to your basic salary, Swiss Re offers an attractive, performance-related variable compensation component that recognises your performance. In addition, you benefit from a variety of global and location-specific services. Eligibility may vary depending on the conditions of Swiss Re and your employment contract. About Swiss Re Corporate Solutions Swiss Re is one of the world’s leading providers of reinsurance, insurance and other forms of insurance-based risk transfer. We anticipate and manage risks, from natural catastrophes and climate change to cybercrime.

Swiss Re Corporate Solutions is the commercial insurance arm of the Swiss Re Group. We offer innovative insurance solutions to large and midsized multinational corporations from our approximately 50 locations worldwide. We help clients mitigate their risk exposure, whilst our industry-leading claims service provides them with additional peace of mind.
