This Position reports to:

Senior Key Account Manager


At the Sevensense office in Zurich, we push the frontiers of Visual SLAM and build the eyes and brains for ABB’s mobile robots. Our real-time tracking solutions provide warehouses and factories with instant visibility into their forklift traffic, utilization, and safety. We’re looking for a passionate Technical Account Manager to join our Marketing & Sales team in Zurich, Switzerland.

Your mission:

  • Manage a sales area with a focus on our forklift tracking solution: identify key players and their needs, and generate leads to grow the sales in your area

  • Proactively identify new business opportunities and grow the customer base

  • Serve as the primary point of contact for all matters specific to your accounts:

    • Build and maintain strong, long-lasting customer relationships

    • Develop sales opportunities by leveraging our success stories

    • Deeply understand the customer needs and derive a compelling value proposition for our Visual SLAM solutions

    • Quote, invoice, forecast, report, and negotiate contracts independently growing revenue and profit

  • Coordinate and influence external and internal stakeholders (technical, managerial, executive) throughout the customer journey to secure high sales volumes and customer satisfaction

Your background:

  • Completed university degree in a technical discipline

  • 3-5 years of work experience in technical sales or project management

  • Analytical attitude with outstanding presentation and communication skills, in particular in situations of conflict and negotiations

  • Willingness to travel: 20-30%

  • Fluency in English
