Full description

Canton of Zurich Economic Directorate Office for Employment Recruitment Agents/in Job Registration Centre (SMZ) temporarily for one year with permanent employment option 80 - 100% work changes lives - and that is exactly what drives us. Every day, the Office of Labour (AFA) of the Canton of Zurich works to ensure that people regain their jobs, that companies find the right talents and that the labour market in the Canton of Zurich remains strong. With around 900 employees at various locations, we bundle labour market skills and are close to the people, the companies and the realities of working life. You don’t work anywhere with us. You work where your commitment really counts. How can employers use the domestic workforce potential? How does professional recruitment work within the framework of the job registration obligation? As a public employment agency in the Canton of Zurich, the Job Registration Centre (SMZ) assumes exactly this task and arranges job-seekers in the RAV for jobs subject to registration. To strengthen the existing team, we are looking for several committed personalities as of December 2026 or by agreement. The place of work is located in the Leonardo Zurich Nord commercial building. Are you a sales-strong, open-minded personality who enjoys advising and supporting demanding customers? Then you are exactly right with us! Your tasks:

  • Examination and publication of job notifications
  • Advice to employers on job registration requirements
  • Offering placement services in accordance with the specifications of the job registration centre and the needs of employers
  • Placement of job seekers in compliance with the statutory deadlines
  • Receiving and processing feedback from employers
  • Customer Relationship Management Employers
  • Data collection, processing and archiving
  • Close cooperation and coordination with the RAV of the Canton of Zurich Your profile:
  • completed apprenticeship EFZ and some years of professional experience in the gastro, technical or construction sector
  • Experience in recruitment and knowledge of the Zurich labour market is advantageous
  • exact working method, confident handling of time pressure and efficiency in administration
  • Organizational skills
  • convincing and adequate communication skills as well as strong sales on the phone
  • stylish German in word and writing, other languages of advantage
  • Reliability and a healthy dose of humor We offer:
  • interesting and demanding job with a high level of personal responsibility
  • Open-minded and dedicated team that guarantees a careful and good introduction
  • Possibility for one day of home office per week
  • Contribution to catering costs (lunch check)
  • Attractive employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines Curious? Ivan Lovrinovic, team leader, telephone 043 258 63 28 will be happy to provide you with further information. We look forward to your application. You can find out more about us and our employees at www.zh.ch/afa .

Pensum: 80-100 %
