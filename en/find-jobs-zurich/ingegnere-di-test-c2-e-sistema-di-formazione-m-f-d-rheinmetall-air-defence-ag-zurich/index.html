WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

  • Definition and creation of precise system verification procedures (Engineering Test Procedures)
  • Independent execution and evaluation of system verification procedures for quality assurance
  • Technical support for test campaigns, including willingness to work abroad if required

WHAT YOU SHOULD BRING

  • Successfully completed master's degree (MSc) or equivalent qualification
  • At least 2 years of demonstrable professional experience in systems or requirements engineering
  • Initial experience as a Test Engineer or in a comparable testing role is an advantage
  • Experience in software engineering is an advantage
  • Business-fluent German skills
  • English proficiency at C2 level

WHAT WE OFFER YOU

  • A dynamic working environment with exciting projects
  • Flexible working hours (flextime and trust-based working hours)
  • Very good social benefits and an occupational pension fund with benefits above the statutory minimum
  • Employee benefits of a large corporation, employee stock purchase program
  • Support for needs-based further training
  • Staff restaurant as well as external catering and shopping options nearby
  • Individual internal and external personnel development measures in cooperation with our Rheinmetall-Academy
  • Workplace a few minutes' walk from Zürich-Oerlikon railway station
