The Inspectorate is attached to the Administrative Commission of the Higher Court and exercises the technical supervision of the Offices of the Canton of Zurich. As of 1 February 2027 or by agreement we are looking for a deputy of the control inspector (80-100%) Your responsibilities

  • Regular inspections of the management and municipal offices, in particular auditing of official business and auditing of accounting.
  • management of and participation in projects.
  • Technical assistance to offices, telephone and written legal information.
  • Co-responsibility for the up-to-date education and training of the officers. Your profile
  • Basic Commercial Education, with Eidg. Professional card: Operation and bankruptcy, equivalent training or legal training (BLaw, MLaw).
  • Several years of professional experience in a management and municipal office in the canton of Zurich.
  • In-depth legal knowledge in debt recovery and related fields.
  • Sociable, reliable and solution-oriented personality with forward-looking working methods.
  • assertiveness, rapid comprehension and sound IT knowledge. Our offer
  • Versatile activity with great scope for action and a high degree of independence.
  • Contemporary employment conditions and diverse training opportunities.
  • A well-established, collegial and established team as well as interesting insights into the civil and criminal courts in Zurich.
