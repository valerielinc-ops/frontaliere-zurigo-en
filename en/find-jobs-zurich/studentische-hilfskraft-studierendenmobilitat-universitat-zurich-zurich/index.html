Global Student Experience

Job start 1 December 2026 or by arrangement, temporary

The Global Student Experience department has the task of providing students at the University of Zurich (UZH) with an international experience. It supports both UZH students (outgoing students) and international students (incoming students) in planning and implementing their stay abroad. At the same time, the department is responsible for developing and providing the basics for student mobility, thus fulfilling an important interface and hub function. The employment is limited to one year with the possibility of a permanent employment.

Tasks

  • Responsible for the administration of an assigned student group in the outgoing team
  • Advising and answering questions from UZH students who are interested in or in an exchange; by e-mail and at the information desk
  • Assistance in the management of student dossiers
  • Requesting and verifying documents
  • Maintenance of data in the database (Mobility Online)
  • Overview of scholarship payments
  • Compilation and control of data and statistics
  • Participation in events for students

Profile

You are studying at the University of Zurich and already have your first professional experience, which you would like to deepen in this part-time position. You have a quick comprehension, work exactly and are characterized by a skilful handling of MS Office and databases. Your German is stylish and you have very good English skills in word and writing. They are flexible, committed and independent. Ideally, you have completed an exchange abroad as part of your studies. We offer a varied activity in an unusual, interesting and socially relevant field. You work in a team with a good working atmosphere and in a dynamic environment. The employment conditions are based on the guidelines of the University of Zurich.
