Your mission

  • Take advantage of the unique opportunity to actively shape our success story and take responsibility for a small market – from sales and earnings to implementing our strategy
  • After initial training, you take over the operational management of a market and independently make important entrepreneurial and personnel decisions on site.
  • You inspire and develop your team, live customer orientation and create a shopping experience that sustainably increases customer satisfaction and NPS.

Your match with us

  • You are familiar with trading and have extensive business knowledge
  • You have already managed a team or a small branch, ideally in the electronics trade
  • You take responsibility towards your team - with respect, appreciation and at eye level
  • It gives you pleasure to achieve goals, develop performance and your team as well as yourself and earn attractive bonuses through good performance.
  • You have good knowledge of German
