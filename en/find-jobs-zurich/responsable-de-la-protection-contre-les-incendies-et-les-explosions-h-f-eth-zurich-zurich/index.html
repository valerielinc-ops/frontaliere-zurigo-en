Position available Leitung Brand- und Explosionsschutz (m/w/d)

  • Type of contract: 100%, Zürich, unbefristet
  • Selection methods: print (Drucken) -- Description of the assignment Abteilung Sicherheit, Gesundheit und Umwelt (SGU) recommends, supports and forms all organizational units and members of ETH Zürich on safety and health at work. It is the point of reference for all centralized measures relating to the management of Gefährdungen and the risks in order to protect people, animals, material goods, infrastructure and environment, as well as for compliance with the relevant regulations. In the Brand- und Explosionssschutz (BEx) sector, we assume responsibility for the organization and coordination of architectural, technical and organizational measures, including:
  • the drafting and updating of internal concepts, technical-architectural requirements and guidelines;
  • management or participation in integral safety concepts;
  • control of the application of such measures;
  • information and training of members of the ETH;
  • the contribution of technical experts in the design field. The digital documentation of the quality activities carried out is one of the responsibilities. -- Reference project Are you ready to take responsibility with a motivated team for safety Brand- und Explosionsschutz in the university? Do you also maintain strategic visibility in the event? Then you're in the right place. Like Leitung BEx, you will strive to ensure security in a leading technical institution worldwide. Combine strategic leadership with technical expertise, bringing your expertise in developing Brand- und Explosionsschutz in research, teaching, administration and construction projects as well as risk management and events. In case of significant events, you will be part of ETH Zürich’s Notfallorganisationisation: you will provide advice and leadership support with your skills and experience. -- Main tasks
  • Take personal, organizational responsibility and in terms of content and expertise within your competence and ensure the proper performance of core activities.
  • As Sicherheitsbeauftragter Brandschutz according to the Weisung GVZ, he coordinates the themes of Brand- und Explosionsschutz across the ETH portfolio, including:
  • planning and execution of internal audits, liberation of evacuation paths, Explosionsschutz, evaluation of events;
  • management and training of internal interventions of first aid «Brand/Evacuation».
  • Responsibility for creating and updating evacuation concepts for all ETH properties and defining related alarm processes.
  • You actively contribute to the whole safety concept design and support resiko management, for example for events.
  • Join ETH project team and provide professional advice to the project management (e.g. Brandmeldeanlagen, safety equipment).
  • Collaboration in the organization and coordination of the management of ETH Zürich events and take an active role in the event.
  • Keep the network inside and outside the ETH for benchmarking, transfer of know-how and exchange of experiences, as well as for the continuous training of your employees.
