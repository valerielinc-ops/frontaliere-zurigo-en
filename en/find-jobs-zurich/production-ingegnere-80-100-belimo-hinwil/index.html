This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

As a production engineer, you take responsibility for our end-of-line test facilities and thus make a decisive contribution to product quality and process stability in series production. You will work closely with our internal teams and external partners to implement practical and effective solutions.

If you want to develop and implement innovative solutions for the continuous improvement of our test and process landscapes, apply now!

What will you do

  • You ensure the availability, stability and performance of our end-of-line test systems and contribute directly to product quality.
  • You industrialize and commission new test facilities - from calibration and verification to stable delivery to series production.
  • You optimize existing plants as well as test and production processes from planning to implementation, including process, product and software changes.
  • You systematically analyze disturbances and deviations, remedy them sustainably and derive targeted improvement measures.
  • You coordinate yourself internally with our maintenance, quality and engineering departments.

• You actively contribute to improving the evaluation and use of test data and support the transfer of knowledge.

Who you are

  • Technical degree or technical training in electronics, mechanics

or comparable

  • Several years of experience in a similar role in a company with a high production volume
  • Experience in optimizing production or testing processes, ideally with Lean or Six Sigma methods
  • Practical experience in the further development of production plants - from the analysis of plant behavior to the definition of changes to the verification and approval for the series
  • Structured and independent working method, interdisciplinary thinking and affinity for metrology
  • Knowledge of Python and SQL for statistical data analysis
  • Flowing German and English skills in word and writing as well as willingness to travel (about 5%)

Who we are

Through our passion, our employees not only create amazing products, but also revolutionize the HVAC industry. Investing in our employees is at the heart of Belimo when it comes to engaging diverse creative talents to build an industry-leading company.
