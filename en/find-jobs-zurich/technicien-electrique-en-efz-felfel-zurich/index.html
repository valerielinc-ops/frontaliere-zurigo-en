Full description

IN BREAKDOWN You bring analytical sharpness, well-founded electric know-how and a real hands-on mentality? As a Senior Maintenance & Reliability Engineer (100%) in Zurich with operations throughout Switzerland, you are the decisive technical authority for our fleet of connected refrigerators and coffee machines. You bring in your technical handwriting, establish clean root-cause analyses and develop our 12-member team of technicians to the next level. ## Your responsibilities

  • As an escalation stage, perform complex fault diagnosis at the component level on our refrigerators and coffee machines.
  • Through in-depth triage and deep understanding of refrigeration circuits, decide precisely between device malfunction, configuration error or telemetry problem.
  • Take over the technical documentation (risk assessment, schematics, NEV/EMV compliance) for our own CAREL integration and the POS system.
  • Continuously test new and repaired units for electrical safety and ensure the highest level of compliance throughout the fleet.
  • Create structured SOPs and establish a sustainable root cause and postmortem process for targeted reduction of recall costs.
  • Coach our 12-member technician team and close skills gaps through targeted mentoring and a dynamic skills matrix.
  • Organize manufacturer training courses (e.g. Eversys) and ensure visible, continuous training progress. ## What you bring
  • Recognised electronics or automatic training (e.g. electronics technician: in EFZ or automatic technician: in EFZ) combined with in-depth knowledge in refrigeration diagnostics (pressures, compressor behaviour, typical fault patterns).
  • Several years of professional experience in fault diagnosis of electromechanical and electronic systems at component level.
  • Demonstrate practice in SOP writing and structured root cause analysis.
  • Good understanding of electrical product safety (NEV/Low Voltage Directive, EMC basics) or the high willingness to acquire this knowledge with the support of our external safety consultant.
  • Independent, responsible person with excellent communication skills at all team levels
  • Great passion both for practical, hands-on work at the workbench and for the precise writing of technical standards.
  • Fluent knowledge of German and very good English.
  • Mandatory: Valid Swiss driving licence (category B).
  • Non-smokers: in – we don’t want you to smell like cigarettes, neither at work nor at customer appointments. After all, FELFEL is all about good food.
  • Valid work permit or Swiss passport. ## What we offer
  • Lots of delicious food, just a few steps from your desk - we fulfill all your culinary desires...
  • Best barista coffee and tea for the whole team thanks to our innovative Gavetti coffee machine
  • 5 weeks vacation per year, and the possibility to purchase an extra week
  • We think about your future and offer a super-compulsory pension fund solution
  • Freshly baked parents receive 16 or more from us. 3 weeks of fully paid parental leave. We also support the return to work by making a generous financial contribution to childcare until kindergarten age.
  • We finance your half-tax subscription and thus support our employees in the use of public transport
  • Great team events, such as our well-known FELFEL Summer Outings
  • Stay Moving and Healthy with Our Weekly Sports Courses Does that sound like the perfect job for you? We can’t wait to know more about you! EMPLOY NOW with your complete documents in German or English.
