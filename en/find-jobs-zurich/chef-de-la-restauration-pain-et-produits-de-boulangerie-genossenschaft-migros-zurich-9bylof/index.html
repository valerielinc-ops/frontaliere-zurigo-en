Take over the management of the food / bread & baked goods department of the Höngg branch. With your well-founded knowledge of food / bread & baked goods you advise our customers first-class. Thanks to your motivated and inspiring way you contribute significantly to customer satisfaction. That sounds like you? Then apply now!

Mansioni
