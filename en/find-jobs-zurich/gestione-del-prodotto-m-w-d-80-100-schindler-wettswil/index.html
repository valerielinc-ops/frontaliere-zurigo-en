We are AS Elevators

AS Elevators is a Swiss-wide partner for the assembly, modernization and maintenance of elevators. The diversity and individualization of our products distinguishes us. Be as unique as we are!

Experience the AS Spirit as

Through your start at AS Elevators you benefit from an appreciative and family environment. In this way, we promote cohesion among employees through our du-culture and various events. Find out more about us here: Video

What to expect

  • Independent implementation of conversions and modernizations of existing elevator installations
  • Responsibility for commissioning your modernized facilities
  • Independent planning and organization of your assigned projects with the support of your manager
  • Direct contact and coordination with local contacts
  • Ensuring a structured, efficient and, above all, safety-conscious way of working
  • Representation of our company through your customer-oriented and friendly appearance

What you bring

  • Completed basic technical training in electronics, electrical engineering or one of them as your passion
  • Enjoying technical challenges and varied tasks
  • Independent, reliable and precise working method with high quality standards
  • You feel safe in the German language when dealing with customers
  • High willingness to learn and own initiative
  • Good physical condition
  • A maker or maker

Your many advantages

  • 2-month training at full wage in our lift camp for your optimal preparation for your new job.
  • Compensation for your lunchtime meal and the cleaning of your work clothes, which of course will be provided by us free of charge.
  • Excellent development opportunities: Personnel development is lived with us. We provide our employees with various national and international talent programs and support them temporarily and financially with training and further education.
  • Work-life balance: 40h week, 25 to 30 days of vacation, flexible working hours, part-time work and unpaid leave.
  • Healthy working environment: High health & safety standards and company health management including social counselling and health promotion.
  • Employee benefits: Generous participation in public transport and fitness subscription, fleet discounts, reduced purchase of Reka money, reduced insurance premiums and various benefits.
