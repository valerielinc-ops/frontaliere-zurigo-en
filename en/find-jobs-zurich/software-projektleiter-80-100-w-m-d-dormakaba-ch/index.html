Full description

dormakaba is one of the top 3 companies in the market for access and security solutions worldwide. We bring more than 100 years of experience and presence in over 60 countries. We offer innovative, comprehensive access control solutions for buildings, facilities and locations as well as solutions for collecting employee and company data. As a global company that pursues very clear growth goals, we need employees who want to take themselves and the company forward. We are looking for you as our software project manager for our Rümlang location.

A job that counts: Your tasks

  • Management of software migrations and updates of the dormakaba exos solution according to order confirmation and service description
  • Overall responsibility for project coordination, resource planning and ensuring the flow of information between internal specialists, customers and IT and security managers
  • Ensuring compliance with IT security and compliance requirements, implementing security-by-design as well as carrying out risk analysis and ensuring secure integration into existing IT infrastructures
  • Establishment and quality assurance of technical documentation
  • Implementation of acceptance with the customer and ensuring customer satisfaction
  • Monitoring of project costs incl. Post-negotiation of order changes
  • Project implementation in the German-speaking Switzerland region

Experience that matters: Your skills

  • Completed FH or HF studies in computer science or electronics, ideally with knowledge in the other discipline
  • 2–4 years of practical experience in software installation and training
  • Experience managing software projects
  • fluent knowledge of German and good knowledge of English
  • Other languages are beneficial

A workplace that counts: Our offer

  • Best opportunities in a global group with the culture of a family business
  • With the public transport well developed work locations, home office possibilities
  • Attractive salary package
  • Transparent and clearly defined further development opportunities
  • Support for militia work/non-profit projects also during working hours
