Full description

The Clinic and Polyclinic for Internal Medicine (KIM) offers an exciting, acute medicine activity with a wide range of complex internistic diseases. >The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.

Tasks

  • Rotating work on the interdisciplinary bed stations and on our Intermediate Care Unit, if suitable and interested also in the Institute of Emergency Medicine
  • Professional and full care of patients: inside and their relatives
  • Supervision and teaching of assistant doctors: women in further education
  • Participation in the Senior Physician Shift Service of Internal Medicine
  • Joy of interdisciplinary cooperation

Requirements

  • Specialist FMH General Internal Medicine; of advantage are additional specialization e.g. intensive care FMH or an equivalent training recognized by MEBEKO
  • Broad and multi-year internistic experience in general internal medicine
  • Owner: in FA Sonography/POCUS or shortly before obtaining
  • Promotion (Dr. med.)
  • Resilient, team capable and communicative personality with pleasure in interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Willingness and interest to participate in university teaching and research projects

We offer

We offer cooperation in an interesting and innovative environment in which you can participate and be supported. Versatile, interdisciplinary activity in a dynamic, clinical and academic environment. You will find an appreciative and pleasant working atmosphere with us.
