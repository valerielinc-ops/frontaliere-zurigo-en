80% - 100% | Corporate development / Strategy / Sustainability | Zurich | Experienced professionals

You want to actively shape the future of the Zürcher Kantonalbank’s Institutionals and Multinationals division and drive change processes effectively. In this role, you work directly with the Chief of Staff in an advanced environment with high visibility and play a key role in shaping strategic initiatives of the business unit. Together with colleagues from the institutional and multinational sectors, you will shape the bank of tomorrow – right in the heart of Zurich.

Your tasks

  • Leading and accompanying central change and transformation projects in the institutional and multinationals business unit
  • Support the Head of Staff and the Head of the Business Unit in complex strategic issues and take over the management of selected projects
  • Perceiving leadership support in strategy, communication and organizational issues, identifying optimization potential and driving implementation until completion
  • Prepare decision-making bases for management and create convincing documents for committees
  • Coordinating and dealing with risk topics in the areas of audit, compliance, operational risk
  • Planning and implementing communication at the level of business unit and planning events and management vessels
  • Identification of process optimization possibilities in the own organization and implementation of the corresponding measures
  • Representing the business unit and its areas in project and steering committees as well as ensuring the interests of institutionals and multinationals
  • Completed studies at the level of technical college or university, ideally in economics
  • Proven background in strategy consulting
  • At least 10 years of experience in the professional management of complex projects and/or a staff function in the banking environment
  • In-depth knowledge in the value chains of trading and investing, financing as well as custody and infrastructure issues are a plus
  • You will score with the ability to bring complex projects to a structured conclusion, think strategically, networked and innovatively and create convincing management presentations (especially in PowerPoint) for the executive committee of the Institutionals and Multinationals business unit and the executive management
  • Very good communication skills in German and English
  • A supervisor who demands a lot, but also promotes and gives freedom in the design of work
  • Exciting and versatile task area with high visibility, in which you drive forward topics on your own responsibility and actively shape the future of the Zürcher Kantonalbank
  • Diverse opportunities to develop yourself personally and professionally – we invest a lot in our employees and offer attractive training opportunities
  • Mutual support and team spirit are important to us
  • You can flexibly organize your work in consultation with your team (e.g. mobile work, flexible working hours)
  • Centrally located and modern workplace in the heart of Zurich
  • Comprehensive package, which in addition to the attractive basic salary and a variable remuneration offers many benefits and perks – from A such as retirement provision to Z such as ZVV bonus pass
