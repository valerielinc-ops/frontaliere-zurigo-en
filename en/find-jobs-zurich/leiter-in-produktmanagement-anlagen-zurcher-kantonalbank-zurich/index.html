Do you want to actively shape the future of investment products – develop new solutions together with your team, drive digitalization forward and proudly ensure the further development and maintenance of our wide range of investment products? With this key function, you take on a central role in a dynamic and future-oriented environment and have the strategic responsibility for a central product range of the Zürcher Kantonalbank. Become part of our product management leadership team for the investment and pensions business and actively shape the future of the investment business of Zürcher Kantonalbank!

Your tasks

  • Responsible for the strategic positioning of the equipment product range throughout the entire product life cycle
  • Further development of the product portfolio along customer needs, market and technology trends, competitive changes and regulatory developments to ensure a sustainable and scalable investment offering
  • Controlling and prioritizing the product and investment portfolio with a focus on customer benefit, growth and profitability
  • Orchestrate cross-divisional cooperation with sales, investment solutions, IT, operations, legal, compliance, risk as well as internal and external partners
  • Representation of the department vis-à-vis senior management as well as strategic internal and external committees and convincing contribution of the product perspective into central decisions
  • Leading and promoting an organization of around 30 employees across multiple management levels and creating a culture of ownership and continuous development
  • Completed studies in Banking & Finance, Business Administration or Economics, supplemented by further education such as CFA or CIIA and a management education
  • Many years of leadership and management experience in a strategically relevant role in the investment or wealth management business, ideally with leadership
  • In-depth understanding of asset management, investment advice and execution-only as well as the underlying products, processes, regulatory requirements and value chains
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and strong ability to combine strategy, customer value, technology, economics and regulatory requirements into a compelling overall picture
  • Excellent conceptual and analytical skills as well as the ability to structure complex issues, set clear priorities and consistently translate them into results
  • Excellent communication skills and sovereign appearance, also towards stakeholders at C-level
  • Authentic, visionary and appreciative leader who inspires, promotes and motivates the team to perform strongly
  • A versatile, exciting and challenging task in a dynamic environment with inspiring colleagues and many networking opportunities
  • An agile and open environment in one of the most successful Swiss banks
  • Comprehensive package, which in addition to the attractive basic salary and a variable compensation offers many benefits and perks
  • Our corporate culture offers you a lot of creative scope for your further development
  • A modern workplace in the booming ZH-West, near the station Hardbrücke
