Intern, Banking Solutions Location : Zurich Work Pattern Percentage : 80-100% Finding the talent that makes Deloitte unique Are you passionate about the banking industry and driven to help banks transform their business operations? Do you enjoy working in interdisciplinary teams to solve complex challenges, with AI as part of your everyday toolkit? Then join our Banking Solutions team! About the Role As an Intern, you will gain hands-on experience from day one, supporting our clients in end-to-end business transformations, from strategy and target operating model design to process optimisation and technology enablement, with AI embedded throughout. How you can make an impact In your role, you will:

  • Build your knowledge across a broad range of Banking Solutions domains, including Advisory, Front-to-Back Credit & Risk, Banking Technology, and Investment Management Solutions
  • Support our clients in addressing key business challenges and driving sustainable growth by translating strategic priorities into actionable solutions
  • Help banks identify, design and implement AI use cases across the value chain, from AI-enabled processes to intelligent operations
  • Contribute to client engagements through research and analysis, solution design, and the preparation of materials for client meetings, workshops and recommendations
  • Work alongside experienced and motivated practitioners from diverse areas of expertise who are committed to sharing their knowledge and supporting your career development Who we are looking for
  • You hold a university degree in banking, finance, business administration, economics, or a similar field of study
  • You have a genuine passion for the banking industry and an understanding of the trends, challenges, and market dynamics shaping its future
  • You are an analytical and insightful thinker, able to structure complex problems and develop creative solutions
  • You are fluent in both German and English
  • French or Italian is a plus
  • You are willing to travel within Switzerland and potentially abroad What sets you apart
  • You are an energetic team player with a positive, can-do attitude and strong interpersonal skills, committed to delivering high-quality work
  • You have excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to effectively articulate business problems and recommendations to our clients
  • You actively use AI tools in your daily work, from research and analysis to solution design, and are curious to explore new ways of working
  • You can work independently and recognise when to seek input or support from others Your team Join a diverse, international, and highly motivated team. We will help you grow both personally and professionally through our training, mentoring programmes, and on-the-job learning, while also connecting you with colleagues across all business lines.
