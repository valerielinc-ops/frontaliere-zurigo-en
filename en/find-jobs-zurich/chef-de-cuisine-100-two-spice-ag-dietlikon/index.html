Two Spice – that’s us! A creative and innovative gastronomy and lifestyle company from Zurich. Since 1990 we bring fresh ideas to the table and develop unique concepts and brands that inspire. Immerse yourself in the world of Yooji’s and become part of our team that is passionately and creatively redefining Japanese cuisine. Do you want to be part of our team? Now is your chance!

Tasks

  • Technical and personnel management of the kitchen team
  • Ensuring an efficient service flow and optimal teamwork
  • Development and planning of the food & beverage offer in close coordination with the management and responsible departments
  • Compliance with and control of operational hygiene and quality standards
  • Calculation of recipes, daily dishes and menus to ensure profitability
  • Organisation of purchase and sale and compliance with the costs of goods
  • Creation of daily specialities, planning and implementation of events and banquets
  • Support, promotion and management of employees

Requirements

  • Completed training in the gastronomy or hotel industry
  • Many years of experience in the kitchen as well as profound leadership experience
  • Strong social skills, assertiveness and empathy in dealing with different personalities
  • Creative and independent personality with passion for the cooking profession and pleasure in guest contact
  • Enthusiasm and the flair for Asian cuisine
  • German language skills at B2 level in word and writing, further language skills are advantageous
