Full description

Numbers are your passion and you also keep track of complex structures? Then you fit perfectly with us!

For the Corporate Service Center in Dübendorf we are looking for an experienced, independent and pragmatic maker personality as soon as possible or by agreement.

Specialist / Specialist Accounting / Analyses 80-100 %

Their tasks

  • Independent preparation of monthly and annual accounts makes OR and Swiss GAAP FER of the management and holding companies
  • Account reconciliation and plausibility of secondary books incl. HR votes
  • Preparation of reporting and analysis
  • Support on tax topics and wage voting
  • Ensuring data quality for all companies in the group
  • Cooperation in ICS controls and process documentation in QM
  • Participation in projects and integrations of new companies
  • Creating reporting and ad hoc analyses

Your profile

  • Completed training as a professional / specialist in finance and accounting or equivalent training
  • Proven several years of professional experience in accounting in a similar function ideally in the service industry / group
  • Good understanding of Swiss GAAP FER and OR accounting
  • Good PC application knowledge (MS Excel and PowerPoint, very good knowledge of SAP ERP FI / module)
  • Very good knowledge of German, as well as good knowledge of French and/or English
