Behind the scenes ensure that our store offers the best and latest tobacco and nicotine products? Without shopping, nothing works for us. That’s why we need professionals like you. Want to make a difference and continue writing the success story together?

Luogo di lavoro: Denner AG, headquarters, Grubenstrasse 10, 8045 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • A part of the range of tobacco products and novel nicotine products, such as snus and e-vapor lead, develop and procure
  • Responsible for a sustainable supplier portfolio and handling contract negotiations
  • Conceptually further develop and implement the product group strategy in relation to the product range
  • You plan, control and optimize all communication and activation measures for your goods group across all communication channels
  • Create market, assortment and action analyses, actively observe the market and competition
  • Close cooperation with other internal interfaces, in particular goods planning, marketing, sales, logistics and finance

  • Strong communication skills and a convincing appearance
  • In-depth experience in product, key account or category management
  • Service-oriented and resilient personality with a high level of customer and market orientation
  • in German (negotiation certain)

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Discussion with case presentation: Specific expertise is examined on the basis of a case or other assessment instruments. After processing, the result of the leader is presented and then discussed together. Usually, the team is also represented at the presentation. As a rule, the conversations are conducted by the superior, without a representative from the HR. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition: After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
