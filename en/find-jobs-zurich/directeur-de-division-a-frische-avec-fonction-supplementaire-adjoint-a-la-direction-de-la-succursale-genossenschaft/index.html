Full description

Do you have a feeling for high-quality food and are enthusiastic about fresh produce? 🍎🧀 Do you have leadership experience, are sales strong 💪 and communicate safely with our customers? 😊 Are you ready to take over the branch management deputy and take responsibility for the branch? 🏪 Then you are exactly right with us! ✅ Let us be «tämä success». 🤝🧡

Mansioni

  • You take on the professional and personnel responsibility for the area Freshness 🥦🍞 as well as for the entire branch in the absence of the branch management
  • You take care of sales promotion and personnel planning. 📅🤝 With your organizational talent you ensure a smooth process.
  • Revenue, cost, inventory and inventory management 📈📦 are among your core tasks – you always have the overview and control specifically.
  • Ordering, incoming goods checks, presentation and disposition 📋🚛✨ are in your hands – you make sure that everything runs perfectly.
  • Friendly and competent customer service and advice 😊🛍️ are a matter of course for you and you will advise the customers with your expertise.

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: in the retail trade, food industry
  • Completed basic or further training: in the retail trade
  • You bring very good expertise in the field of freshness 🥦🧀 and can draw on valuable leadership experience.
  • Reliability, flexibility and a high level of commitment 💪⏰ distinguish you – you are always there when you are needed.
  • With your independent work and your friendly approach 😊🤝 you create a pleasant atmosphere for customers and team.
  • You are ready to continue your education 📚🚀 and always stay up to date. Ideally, you already have leadership experience that helps you take responsibility and support your team.
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Mobile subscription: Highly discounted conditions for private mobile subscription also for family and friends
  • Occupational pension provision: Attractive pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Cumulus Points: Multiple Cumulus Points on food and nonfood items
  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation in self-selected courses
