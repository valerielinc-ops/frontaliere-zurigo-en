Full description

Key responsibilities

  • technical analysis of business requirements from various stakeholders and implementation of the requirements in a comprehensive and modern IT environment
  • preparation of impact analyses and estimates for various projects
  • acting as Subject Matter Expert in the area of Core Card Management for internal and cross-application projects
  • building up knowledge of the existing application landscape and using this knowledge for extensions of the applications
  • providing 3rd level support for the Core Card Management area, further support tasks including on-call duty

The team In our agile Client & Card Management team, you are on the move with enthusiasm as an experienced COBOL developer on the mainframe. You develop and improve robust and scalable components for our core card applications with ease. As a second competence, you have acquired broad knowledge of Linux systems. You are responsible for the further development and maintenance of the core of our application landscape and possess knowledge of the most important business processes in card processing.

Your skills and experience

  • excellent knowledge of the CMM products ACI-Issuer and ACI-Interchange
  • excellent knowledge in programming with COBOL, DB2 (SQL)
  • very good experience with ChangeMan, UrbanCodeDeployment
  • very good knowledge of JCL, Control-M and CICS, VSAM, z/OS
  • very good user knowledge of Confluence Jira
  • expertise in the application of PCI DSS 4.0 standards
  • good knowledge of Java / Spring boot / Maven / Jenkins / Git
  • good analytical skills; you know how to approach and solve problems
  • enjoy independent, proactive work in an agile, motivated and highly competent crew
  • experience in an agile environment, for example as ScrumMaster
  • experience in the financial sector, especially in the card business, is of inestimable advantage
  • language skills in spoken and written English (at least C1) and German (at least B1)

About us UBS Card Center AG with its headquarters in Glattbrugg and an additional office in Manno TI is part of UBS Switzerland AG and employs over 750 people. As the leading credit card processor in Switzerland, we process over 3 million Visa and Mastercard credit cards. In doing so, we stand close by our customers' side - with experience, commitment and vision.

Join us At UBS Card Center we embrace flexible working arrangements and offer different working models such as part-time, job-sharing and hybrid working. Our purpose-driven culture and global infrastructure help us connect and collaborate in agile ways to meet all our business needs. A working environment driven by innovation, digitalization, new mobile payment methods and customer experience awaits you.
