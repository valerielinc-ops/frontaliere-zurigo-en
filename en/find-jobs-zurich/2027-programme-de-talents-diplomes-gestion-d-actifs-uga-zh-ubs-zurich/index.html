Full description

Your role Interested in working in finance, specifically in real asset and infrastructure investing, but not sure where to start? Do you want to work for a firm with a truly global footprint? We're looking for ambitious students to join our 2027 Asset Management Graduate Talent Program.

You’ll get to

  • build expertise in private infrastructure investing and gain hands-on experience across a diverse range of infrastructure strategies.
  • support the investment teams throughout the selection, due diligence and monitoring of infrastructure primary, secondary and co-investment opportunities
  • collaborate cross-functionally with investment, operations, and client service teams to ensure a seamless and high-quality experience throughout the client lifecycle.
  • build an understanding of our industry, culture, and strategy, while learning about how we operate to effectively meet the needs of our clients

Your team You will be working in our UGA Infrastructure team in Zurich. We are part of UBS Asset Management and manage indirect Infrastructure investments on behalf of both institutional and private clients globally.

Your expertise

We’re looking for a candidate who

  • will graduate with a Bachelor or Master’s degree no longer than 18 months prior to starting our program, with less than 12-months of full-time work experience post-graduation
  • is fluent in English and either German, Italian or French (written and spoken)
  • has a structured work style and is confident working autonomously
  • is a strategic thinker with strong communication skills
  • is motivated to work in a business with high demands and tight deadlines
  • has a genuine interest in the infrastructure asset class and its subsectors
  • can evidence how experimental and responsible use of AI can improve outcomes and efficiency

We’re looking for someone who’s curious and wants to thrive in a business that never stops moving. We welcome all majors, but it’s important to have an interest in the financial industry. We want to see what makes you unique and discover what you can bring to our team.

Start date: 1 February 2027

Your program Our Graduate Talent Program is 18 months long and is designed to help you develop the skills you need to grow your career. To start, the program introduces you to our firm, provides professional and technical training and teaches you core skills and knowledge of asset management. And it continues from there: by learning from your colleagues, you’ll have plenty of opportunities to develop new skills and build your network. You’ll have the opportunity to see beyond your job by participating in one, or several, rotations. If you’re ready to work where no two days are the same, this program is for you.

We also provide diversified asset management solutions and focused investment banking capabilities. Headquartered in Zurich, Switzerland, UBS is present in more than 50 markets around the globe.
