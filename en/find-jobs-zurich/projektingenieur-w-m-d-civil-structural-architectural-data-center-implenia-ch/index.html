Full description

To strengthen our growing Data Center team, we are looking for an experienced personality as a project engineer in (m/f/d) Civil, Structural & Architectural. Your contractual place of work is our headquarters “Connect” in Opfikon. However, you will mainly work on one of our data center construction projects in the Zurich region. What tasks await you as a project engineer (m/f/d) Civil, Structural & Architectural - Data Center?

  • You support the Construction Manager in the planning, coordination and implementation of all Civil, Structural & Architectural (CSA) work and ensure a scheduled, quality and safety-related execution
  • You monitor execution readiness by tracking subcontractor plans, technical clearances, materials, permits, access requirements and resources, and identifying and eliminating potential risks and bottlenecks early on
  • You work closely with the teams from planning, BIM, QA/QC, purchasing, logistics and MEP and ensure a smooth construction process as well as an efficient control of the interfaces between CSA and MEP trades.
  • You will conduct site visits, monitor construction progress and productivity, and assist with quality controls, acceptances, defect management (NCR) and step-by-step handovers of CSA areas to MEP teams.
  • You check plans and technical documents, process technical queries (RFIs), complete project documentation and support in the creation of audit documents and the final project transfer requirements What requirements do you bring as a project engineer (m/f/d) Civil, Structural & Architectural - Data Center?
  • Completed degree or equivalent qualification in civil engineering, construction management, architecture or a related technical field
  • 3 to 6 years of professional experience in the construction site organization, construction or project implementation on demanding building construction projects
  • In-depth understanding of CSA construction processes, construction processes and site coordination, as well as the ability to safely interpret execution plans, technical specifications, BIM models and construction requirements
  • Experience in the coordination of subcontractors and interdisciplinary interfaces with the aim of ensuring a safe, efficient and quality-appropriate execution of works in accordance with deadlines and projects
  • Good knowledge of quality management processes in the construction industry, in particular in dealing with technical queries (RFIs), technical approvals, test and inspection plans (ITPs), non-conformance reports (NCRs), quality acceptance and handover processes, supplemented by a structured and careful working method
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • Experience on data center, mission critical, pharmaceutical, semiconductor, healthcare, industrial or comparable complex projects is an advantage. Knowledge of Swiss building standards (SIA/VKF), BIM/Navisworks applications, Primavera P6 and digital QA/QC tools round off the profile benefits
