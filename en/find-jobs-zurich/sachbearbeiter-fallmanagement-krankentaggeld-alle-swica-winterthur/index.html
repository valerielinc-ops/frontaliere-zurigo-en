Full description

Quality at all levels is the key to true well-being. As an employee or member of the SWICA team, you understand holistic health as a human service. You work with professionals like you who share this claim. Everyone pulls on the same rope; that's a good feeling. Together, we create solutions that really take things forward. This is how you shape health

  • Self-employed treatment of sickness benefits
  • Contact person for selected enterprise customers
  • Identification and active initiation of appropriate measures (e.g. obtaining and evaluating medical reports and assessments)
  • Independent verification of the performance requirement and calculation and orientation of daily allowances
  • Coordination with internal and external interfaces such as B Care management, medical network, social insurance etc.
  • Writing individual written correspondence and conducting demanding telephone conversations with insured persons, corporate customers as well as authorities and doctors

This sets you apart

  • Basic commercial education and medical knowledge completed
  • Several years of professional experience in personal insurance, preferably sickness allowance or accident insurance
  • Good handling of work pressure/quantity as well as emotional stress
  • Flexible, communicative and customer-oriented personality with strong networked and cross-divisional thinking
  • Very good oral and written expression in German
  • Enjoy an individual and complex case processing as well as an exact and independent working method

Why SWICA

  • We build on humanity. That is why we focus on openness, respect and trust in our everyday work. We take on responsibility and think in terms of solutions – together, for our customers. This is also why we have been number 1 in customer satisfaction for many years.
  • We trust you and give you space. With annual working hours, at least 5 weeks holidays and on request also holiday purchase. With part-time models and generous home office within the scope of the possibilities of the job, you make your everyday life largely flexible.
  • We value and reward your work – with a market-based wage, up to 1,500 francs annual fee for personal wishes and a generous participation in the basic and additional insurance for you and your family.
  • We offer you prospects. With a generous training regulations, we support your professional and personal development – for your role today and for your goals of tomorrow.
  • Because health is everything. With our company health management and region-specific offers, we take care of your well-being. And if it comes otherwise: 100% net wage continued payment.
  • We think long term. With three savings plans, pensum-dependent coordination deduction and benefits well above the legal minimum, you ensure good for your future.
