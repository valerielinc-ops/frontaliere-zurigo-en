Private Banking Assistant (Russian speaker) (80-100%)

We are seeking a Private Banker Assistant to join our New Markets department in Zurich.

This position is part of the Eastern Countries team . The successful candidate will become part of a dynamic and growing team, working closely with Private Bankers to deliver premium services to our private clients.

YOUR ROLE

  • Support Private Bankers in clients’, administrative and commercial activities, to ensure a service of excellence to a UHNW and HNW demanding clientele.
  • Provide information to clients about their portfolios.
  • Verify, process and place transfer and all market orders in the system as per client instructions, and follow up.
  • Liaise with internal services to ensure timely and accurate execution/resolution of clients’ requests.
  • Manage in an impeccable way account openings and proactively process and follow account openings, monitoring of the accounts, cash management, overdrafts.
  • Proactively deal with all administrative tasks (filing/recording of documentation and KYC, FCRM alerts monitoring, documentation update etc.) in collaboration with Private Bankers.
  • Prepare under the guidance of the Private Bankers, presentation material for client meetings and for business development activities.
  • Deal with clients’ mailing.
  • Support clients with on-line access.

YOUR PROFILE

  • Academic or commercial education in the financial field.
  • Minimum 5 years’ experience in banking, in a similar role.
  • Advanced knowledge of the Microsoft Office suite, in particular Excel and Powerpoint (follow-up of complex tables, creation of presentations and reports, etc.), ideally knowledge of the G2 in-house tool.
  • Perfect command of Russian , as well as English and French/German.
  • Very good knowledge of Private Banking services (Lombard Loans, Portfolio Management, Life Insurance, etc.)
  • Pleasant personality, ability to integrate into an existing team and to work as part of a team, open-mindedness.
  • Commercial spirit, and excellent presentation. Strong resistance to stress, ability to multi-task and prioritise workload. Strong sense of confidentiality.
  • Domicile in Switzerland.
