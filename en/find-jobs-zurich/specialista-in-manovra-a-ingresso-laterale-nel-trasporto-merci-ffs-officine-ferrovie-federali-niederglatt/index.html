Cross-entry shunting specialist: in freight transport 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Cross-entry shunting specialist: in freight transport Niederglatt, 01.02.2027, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

At SBB Cargo, you are on the pulse of the Swiss economy. SBB Cargo transports 170,000 tons a day, relieving road traffic of 15,000 trucks per day and the environment of 460,000 tons of CO2 per year. An innovative working environment awaits you, in which you move the SBB Cargo together with your teammates. In addition, you will receive a varied and paid training in the marshalling station. In addition, various further training opportunities are open to you, such as training as a shunting engine driver. Attractive working conditions are important to us, even for irregular working hours. Night and weekend work is also paid for with us - for example with 16 francs per hour for Sunday work. For night assignments, you also get time surcharges between 10 and 30%. This means that you have more free time than employees without night and weekend assignments. Interested in learning more about our professions? We look forward to seeing you from 22 to 24. 2026 in our SBB Berufswelten Café at Zurich Central Station. All information can be found at sbb.ch/berufsweltencafe. You can move that.

  • As a shunting specialist, you help ensure that our customers are served reliably and on time by preparing freight trains for their journey.
  • You take over the cars, carry out security checks and prepare the train through complex shunting movements for the next journey.
  • You collect the necessary data for the train with personal, modern IT devices (e.g. tablet).
  • Due to the timely delivery and parking of the cars with the customer, you have direct customer contact every day. You receive their feedback and forward it to the responsible body if necessary.

Here you will learn more about the job profile. You bring that with you.

  • Attention, concentration and physical resilience distinguish you so that you can successfully perform this demanding activity in 24-hour shift operation. You also like to work outdoors in any weather.
  • Since all instructions and documentation are electronic, you will quickly find your way around with digital tools.
  • You have successfully completed a basic education (at least EFZ or an equivalent degree) and ideally bring professional experience in the craft, logistical or comparable field.
  • You master German verbally and in writing well (at least B2).
  • An impeccable sense of color (no red-green weakness) is just as much a prerequisite for this function as an entryless penal and enforcement register.
