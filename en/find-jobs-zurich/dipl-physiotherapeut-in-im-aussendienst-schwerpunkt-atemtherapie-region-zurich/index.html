Your sphere of activity – in the field, responsible and diverse: As part of our dedicated team, you look after patients directly on site, in their homes, in clinics, medical practices and nursing facilities. You travel a lot and enjoy the advantages of a varied everyday work in the field service:

  • Individual care and advice for our patients: at home;
  • training and support of medical professionals and doctors:inside;
  • Build and maintain your own network in Eastern Switzerland as well as in the regions of Zurich, Zug, Lucerne and Aarau (primary care physicians, pneumologists: inside, clinics);

  • Acquisition of new customers and strategic expansion of our customer base;
  • Active participation in medical events and training.

Your profile

  • Dipl. in Physiotherapy (SRC recognized);
  • Experience in respiratory physiotherapy, ventilation or pulmonary counselling is beneficial;
  • pleasure in working independently;
  • Structured, self-responsible working methods;
  • Existing network in the medical field (e.g. general medicine, pneumology, sleep medicine) is beneficial;
  • Confident knowledge of German (English and Italian advantageous).
