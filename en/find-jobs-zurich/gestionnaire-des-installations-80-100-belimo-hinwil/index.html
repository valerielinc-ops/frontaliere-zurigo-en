Full description

You want to take on responsibility, actively support your team and contribute your experience in the weekend shift? Then we look forward to a committed personality with the following profile:

What will you do

  • You lead, motivate and develop your assembly team and create a working environment by living cooperation and personal responsibility.
  • You plan and coordinate the assembly as well as the optimal personnel deployment and ensure a smooth, timely process.
  • You take responsibility for quality, deadlines and efficiency and take the right measures if necessary.
  • Acquired as part of our weekend shift team You play an important role in ensuring a smooth production process
  • You will recognize optimization potential and actively drive improvements according to lean principles as well as projects such as 5S, audits or quality initiatives.
  • You ensure that production, logistics and warehouse processes work efficiently and maintain the relevant data in SAP.
  • You lead employee meetings, support personnel decisions together with your manager and HR and promote the further development of your team.
  • You exemplify our safety and quality standards and ensure that they are consistently adhered to in everyday work.

Who you are

  • Technical training or completed apprenticeship
  • Some years of management experience as well as experience in assembly
  • High level of problem-solving competence and strong social competence as well as a goal- and performance-oriented working method
  • Very good knowledge of German (at least level C1)
  • Good PC knowledge (MS Office, SAP)
  • Medical training or willingness to complete it
  • Enjoyment of teamwork, active participation in assembly and logistics tasks
  • High self-motivation and empathy, coupled with a strong quality awareness
  • Willingness to participate in training sessions and department meetings during the week

Who we are

At Belimo, we are proud to make our company a rewarding place to work. Our leaders are value-driven and promote new ideas in their teams around the world. Through our passion, our employees not only create amazing products, but also revolutionize the HVAC industry. Investing in our employees is at the heart of Belimo when it comes to engaging diverse creative talents to build an industry-leading company. The success of our corporate brand is based on the competence and commitment of each individual.
