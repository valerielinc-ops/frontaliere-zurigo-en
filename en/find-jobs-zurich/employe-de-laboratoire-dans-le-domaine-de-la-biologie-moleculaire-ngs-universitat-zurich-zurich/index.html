Tasks

  • Nucleic acid extraction: Self-responsible performance and optimization of DNA extractions from a wide variety of samples.
  • Library Preparation & Quality Control: Preparation of NGS libraries (Illumina and Oxford Nanopore) and quality assessment.
  • Maintenance of sequencing workflows: technical preparation, loading and monitoring of next-generation sequencing platforms as well as support for the establishment of semi-automated laboratory processes.
  • Microbiological & Molecular Biology project work: Active support of various research projects in the laboratory (e.g. cultivation of bacteria, strain management, sample archiving as well as classical PCR and gel electrophoresis).
  • Laboratory organisation: co-responsibility for chemical and consumables management, maintenance of laboratory equipment and documentation.

Requirements

  • Training: Successfully completed training as a biomedical:r analyst:in (HF/FH), biology laboratory:in (EFZ), technical:r assistant:in (BTA/MTA) or a degree (bachelor) in a relevant related life science discipline.
  • Basic laboratory knowledge: In-depth knowledge in everyday laboratory life as well as a safe and routine use of basic laboratory equipment.
  • Experience with NGS & Microbiology: Practical experience with nucleic acid isolation, NGS library preparation or sterile microbiological working techniques are a plus but not a mandatory requirement.
  • Working method: Exact, reliable and structured working method. They enjoy fine motor precision work and bring a high quality awareness.
  • Personality: You are flexible, enjoy working proactively in a dynamic academic environment and appreciate the variety between technical routine and project-based research support. As part of our small team, you bring team spirit and the willingness to represent each other flexibly in everyday laboratory life.
  • Language skills: Good communication skills in German and/or English.
