Full description

Job description

  • Support for the technical processing of projects, e.g. simple calculations, technical specifications, documentation and reports.
  • Collaboration in the planning of substation projects – from the initial preparation of analyses, studies and planning for new and conversion projects of substations to the support of later project phases.
  • Participation in coordination with internal bodies, professional coordination and management of interdisciplinary project teams
  • Preparation in the development of decision-making bases, e.g. through research, technical comparisons or preparation of documents.
  • Support in the preparation of schedules and cost overviews as well as in the maintenance of project-related planning documents.

Qualifications

  • Completed studies (ETH/FH/uni) in electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, energy engineering or a comparable technical discipline.
  • Initial practical insights (e.g. through internships, student work or project work) in the field of high voltage or energy technology
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing, as well as French or Italian knowledge of advantage
  • Basic understanding of the relevant technical standards and legal foundations in the field of energy and the power regulation
  • Initial experience in cooperation in projects or with other specialist bodies in the field of energy transmission and distribution is helpful but not absolutely necessary
  • Careful, structured working methods and willingness to take responsibility step by step.
  • Team-oriented, communicative working method and enjoyment of working together in a team

Business description AFRY offers engineering, project management and consulting services that support the energy transition and industrial transformation and strengthen social resilience. With 18,000 experts worldwide, we combine global reach with local expertise and extensive industry knowledge to shape the future of future generations in a sustainable way. Become part of our Transmission & Distribution team. We have been working for decades in the field of planning equipment for the transmission and distribution of electrical energy. The treatment of the associated environmental aspects rounds off our services. To strengthen our team at the Zurich location, we are looking for a Junior Project Engineer (m/f/d) substations as soon as possible.
