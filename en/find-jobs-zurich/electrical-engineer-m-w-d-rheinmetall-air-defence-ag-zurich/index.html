Full description

WHERE WE LOOK FOR YOU Are you enthusiastic about electronics, enjoy technical challenges and want to actively shape production processes? In this versatile function, you take responsibility for our production facilities and contribute with your expertise to the continuous optimization of our processes. Your tasks in detail:

  • Responsibility for production machinery and ensuring smooth operation
  • Assumption of process responsibility in the area of electronics production
  • Continuous analysis and optimization of existing manufacturing processes
  • Analysis of technical failures and development and implementation of appropriate solutions
  • Evaluation of new production facilities, technologies and manufacturing processes
  • Coordination and support of repair orders as well as independent machine repairs
  • Production and test planning support
  • Active participation in the automation and further development of existing production processes WHAT YOU SHOULD TEACH
  • Completed apprenticeship as an electronics technician EFZ or comparable technical training
  • In-depth practical experience in electronics and electronics manufacturing
  • Very good knowledge of electrical measurement technology as well as reading and interpreting electrical circuit diagrams
  • Good knowledge of electronic components and their application
  • Very good MS Office knowledge and experience in dealing with SAP
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • High willingness to change and enjoy new technical challenges and continuous improvements
  • Independent, solution-oriented working method with strong commitment, sense of responsibility and cross-sectoral thinking WHAT WE ARE TO YOU
  • A dynamic working environment with exciting projects
  • Flexible working hours (active and trusted working hours); Home office, where possible and useful
  • Very good social benefits and a super-compulsory pension fund
  • Employee benefits of a large corporation, employee share purchase program
  • Promotion of needs-based training
  • Staff restaurant and external catering and shopping facilities nearby
  • Individual internal and external personnel development measures in cooperation with our Rheinmetall Academy
  • Workplace a few minutes walk from the Zurich-Oerlikon train station KONTAKTDETAILS Responsible contact: Michael Zmoos For questions about your application, please use the contact form. Please note that dossiers of recruiters are not taken into account.
