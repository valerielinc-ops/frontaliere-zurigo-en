Full description

Want to move instead of standstill? Then you are exactly right with us. For our location in Hinwil, we are looking for dedicated logisticians: women who can help – whether in the warehouse or in production. You bring experience, have the SUVA-approved forklift in your pocket and are ready for use immediately? Perfect!

What will you do

  • Order picking, packaging, entry and removal of goods
  • Loading and unloading of trucks
  • Material supply to production lines
  • General warehouse work
  • Compliance with safety and quality standards

Who you are

  • Experience as a logistician:
  • SUVA-recognised forklift pass
  • Reliable and independent working methods
  • Team spirit, flexibility and physical fitness
  • Good knowledge of German

Who we are

At Belimo, we are proud to make our company a rewarding place to work. Our leaders are value-driven and promote new ideas in their teams around the world. Through our passion, our employees not only create amazing products, but also revolutionize the HVAC industry. Investing in our employees is at the heart of Belimo when it comes to engaging diverse creative talents to build an industry-leading company. The success of our corporate brand is based on the competence and commitment of each individual.
