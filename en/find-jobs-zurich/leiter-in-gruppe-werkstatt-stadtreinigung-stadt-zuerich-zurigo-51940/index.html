Full description

In this key role, you will lead our workshop team at the Wallisellen site, coordinate maintenance and repair work with manufacturers and suppliers and ensure that our fleet is reliable for daily use in the city of Zurich. We are looking for an experienced leader from January 2027 or by appointment.

Ready for a job with meaning?

Tasks

  • You will lead the workshop team of six employees in terms of personnel and expertise, ensure the availability of our fleet, optimize processes, plan the order volume of the workshop and ensure economic utilization as well as our safety and quality standards.
  • You are responsible for the planning, coordination and monitoring of maintenance, repair and maintenance work on municipal vehicles, e.g. sweepers and special equipment, e.g. for winter service and support the mechanics in demanding diagnoses.
  • You are the central contact person for manufacturers, suppliers and external partners, coordinate technical issues, accompanying product developments and test phases, participate in procurement and are in constant exchange with the operative employees in the urban cleaning regions.
  • From November to mid-March, you organize the winter service piket (extra compensation) in three groups and also provide part of the piket service yourself during this time.
  • You take responsibility for the education of our learners and support them in their development.

Profile

  • Training as agricultural machinery mechanic, mechanic or mechatronics technician (EFZ level)
  • Several years of management experience in a workshop environment (leadership training beneficial) as well as experience as a hub in contact with customers as well as in cooperation with manufacturers, suppliers and partners
  • Good knowledge of German (level C1), strong technical understanding and a structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Enjoy taking on leadership responsibility, promoting employees and actively developing processes
  • Category B and C driving licence

We offer

  • Generous mobility contribution
  • Food allowance
  • Equal pay
  • Above-average social benefits
  • Careful training in your work
  • A modern, down-to-earth and future-oriented working environment
  • Maternity leave: 16 weeks paid
  • Paternity leave: 20 paid days
  • Three days of care for your sick child without a holiday deduction
  • Development opportunities through urban education
  • ERZ Sportclub

About us

With around 1,000 employees, ERZ is one of the largest service departments in the city of Zurich and operates in an exciting environment at the pulse of time. We are modern, down-to-earth, open-minded, all per you and want to make a difference. Together we are committed to a clean, innovative and sustainable Zurich.

ERZ stands for diversity and mixed teams.
