Full description

Your responsibilities

At the heart of this function is financial transparency at the interface between innovation and production. With in-depth analyses, the production controller supports production, engineering and research & development in financial decisions and continuous improvement.

  • Advising management teams in production, engineering and research & development on financial and business issues
  • Analysis of production performance, productivity, efficiency and cost structures as well as derivation of concrete recommendations for action
  • Control and analysis of costs, inventories and production deviations as well as accompanying improvement measures
  • Responsibility for budgeting, forecasting, reporting and investment calculations in production and development
  • Creation and further development of management reports, key figures and dashboards for the transparent presentation of performance, opportunities and risks

Your profile

  • Business administration or basic technical training with further training in business administration or controlling (Uni/FH or comparable)
  • Several years of professional experience in controlling an internationally active industrial company or in auditing with a focus on industrial companies
  • Good understanding of product calculation, production processes and investment calculations as well as experience in budgeting, forecasting and reporting
  • High IT affinity and profound knowledge, ideally in SAP S/4, Power BI and MS Office
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Analytical and structured working methods as well as the ability to comprehend complex financial information and provide competent advice to different stakeholders

Why HUBER+SUHNER? A versatile controlling function with a close connection to production, engineering and development. With your analyses, you create transparency, initiate improvements and make an important contribution to the company’s development.
