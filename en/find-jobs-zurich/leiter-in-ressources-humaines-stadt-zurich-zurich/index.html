Full description

As of 1 February 2027, we are looking for a competent person in the area of staff management with interest in the strategic and operational challenges in a politically shaped environment.

Tasks

  • Co-design and further development of the LSZ HR strategy, especially in the topics of corporate culture, employee retention and succession planning
  • Responsibility for personnel and job planning in close cooperation with the management and controlling
  • Consulting and support of the management and the line managers in personnel, organizational and labour law issues
  • Organizational, professional and personnel leadership as well as further development of the HR team consisting of 3 employees
  • Personnel acquisition with focus on cadre and key functions
  • Ensure professional onboarding and offboarding processes as well as continuous optimization and further development of further HR processes and their digitization
  • Implementation of wage-related processes (Lohnrunden, Einzelpreis, Funktionstufenachungen, Verkehrs)

Profile

  • Higher education or higher degree with a focus on human resources, business administration, law or comparable qualifications
  • Multi-annual management experience in a comparable position
  • Experience in organisational development, change processes and strategic HR initiatives
  • Good knowledge of modern HR processes and their standardization and digitalization
  • A well-defined understanding of organisational and business relations
  • Analytic and networked thinking and structured and solution-oriented working methods
  • Enforcement and negotiation strong and convincing personality with strong communication ability
  • Good IT application knowledge, high A-resinity to digital tools and advantage experience with SAP HCM

We offer

a diverse and collegial working environment in a growing organization. Our employees benefit from attractive employment conditions such as family-friendly working time models and good social benefits. The central location of the work place in the Werd administrative center and a wide range of training and further education are further qualities.

About us

Properties for a livable city of Zurich

Property City of Zurich (LSZ) is one of Switzerland's major real estate organisations with its portfolio of 5.8 billion Swiss francs of insurance value. In addition to the management of around 10300 apartments and 1600 commercial premises, we are responsible for the comprehensive ownership representation, long-term value preservation and further development of this versatile portfolio. We also manage the urban land reserves and building rights and are responsible for urban real estate transactions.
