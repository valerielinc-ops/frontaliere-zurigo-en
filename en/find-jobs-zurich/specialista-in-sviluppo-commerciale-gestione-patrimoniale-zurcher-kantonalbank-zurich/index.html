80% - 100% | Asset Management / Portfolio Management | Zurich | Beginners: in, experienced professionals The asset management of Zürcher Kantonalbank is the second largest provider in Switzerland with over CHF 361 billion assets under management (as of 31 August 2026) and 280+ investment experts. We offer all institutional asset management services for financial services providers, companies, foundations and pension funds. In particular, our sustainability and prevention competence is internationally excellent. For the Business Development team in Asset Management, we are looking for a highly motivated and versatile personality with a strong interest in asset management. The team supports the Head of Asset Management in strategic issues and projects and acts as a central point of contact for the various areas within and outside asset management. Do you want to actively participate in the further development? Then you are exactly the right person for this function. We look forward to seeing you. Your tasks

  • Active support of the Head of Business Development and Head of Asset Management in strategic issues and daily business
  • Participation in central strategic initiatives and cross-divisional projects (e.g. growth, efficiency increase, new business models, use of AI in asset management)
  • Support in budget planning as well as close cooperation with financial controlling
  • Independent conception, preparation and further development of management and committee documents as well as presentations at senior management level
  • Conducting market, competitive and profitability analyses to support strategic decisions and business cases
  • Planning, coordination and content preparation of internal stages, workshops and management meetings in asset management
  • Central contact point for specialist areas within asset management as well as coordination of relevant topics with bank-wide stakeholders

Your profile

  • Master’s degree (University/FH) in Business Administration, Economics, Finance or a comparable field of study
  • 2–5 years of professional experience in asset management, strategy consulting or in a comparable function in the financial sector
  • High affinity for asset management and willingness to quickly acquire in-depth know-how on the value chain, products and regulatory environment
  • Strong analytical-conceptual skills as well as high numerical affinity
  • Secure handling of MS Office (especially PowerPoint and Excel) and project experience in the conception or implementation of AI solutions in the financial sector
  • Ability to process complex issues in a structured, addressable and visually appealing manner (management documents, presentations, evaluations)
  • High professionalism in appearance and in cooperation with various stakeholders, incl. Senior management
  • Independent, reliable and precise working methods as well as a strong sense of responsibility and quality
  • Very good communication and expression skills in German and English
  • Other languages (especially French) are beneficial

Our offer

  • Role in a key department influencing the strategic development of Zürcher Kantonalbank’s asset management
  • Close cooperation with Head of Business Development Asset Management and Head of Asset Management
  • High visibility in the senior management of asset management
  • Great room for manoeuvre in the further development of the business development team
  • Broad insight into all areas of asset management as well as close interaction with bank-wide functions
  • Attractive development prospects, targeted training and access to internal and external networks
  • Attractive compensation package with excellent ancillary services and employee benefits
