Full description

As Partner Quality Manager, you are responsible for the master data of our partners and – together with internal and external stakeholders – ensure that they are correctly configured and comply with their SLAs. Your coordinating, quality assurance role gives you a broad overview of processes and interfaces in the company.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Headquarters, Pfingstweidstrasse 60b, 8005 Zurich

Mansioni

  • Partners are recorded and mutated by you along the entire partner lifecycle in our ERP system, ensuring the quality of the master data and contracts.
  • Existing partner configurations and relevant master data in connected partner systems, such as Stripe and Dun & Bradstreet, are maintained and updated.
  • Test orders from new and existing partners will be carried out and check whether the defined requirements for order processing, delivery, packaging and service quality are met.
  • Submitted improvement plans from partners in connection with SLA violations you evaluate and are responsible for the decision to accept or reject them
  • Our internal stakeholders support you with various concerns about partner configuration
  • The company-wide communication to our external partners you accompany and coordinate

Requirements

  • 2+ years: Category Management
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): Business administration, business informatics, quality management
  • Completed basic training and at least two years of professional experience
  • Very good IT user knowledge distinguishes you
  • Experience in Jira and Confluence is of great advantage
  • An independent and exact working method as well as reliability and personal responsibility round off your profile
  • Good assertiveness, negotiating skills and a hands-on mentality are among your strengths
  • With good communication skills and commitment, you convince, and you describe yourself as an organizational talent
  • Safe knowledge of German and English for everyday work
  • Building trust through consistent and reliable actions
  • Treating others as equal partners for the success of Galaxus
  • German (flowing)
  • English (flowing)
