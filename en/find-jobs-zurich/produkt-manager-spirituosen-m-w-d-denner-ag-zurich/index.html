Full description

Behind the scenes ensure that our store offers the best and latest spirits? Without shopping, nothing works for us. That’s why we need professionals like you. Want to make a difference and continue writing the success story together?

Mansioni

  • Procure the range of spirits and carry out tenders at the best possible price/performance ratio.
  • Leading a sustainable supplier portfolio and handling contract negotiations.
  • Conceptually develop and implement the product group strategy in relation to brands and own brands.
  • Manage the existing range, manage the action business and source new suppliers, ranges and products.
  • Create market, assortment and action analyses and actively observe the market and competition.
  • Close cooperation with other internal interfaces, in particular quality management, marketing, sales and logistics.

Requirements

  • 3-5 years of professional experience: in product management in the retail trade or in a comparable position
  • Bachelor's degree (university, university of applied sciences): of advantage in the field of purchasing / marketing
  • Analytical thinking skills and a quick apprehension with a high level of initiative.
  • Strong communication and confident in dealing with a wide range of stakeholders.
  • Service-oriented and resilient personality with a high customer and market orientation.
  • Expertise in purchasing and category management in the field of spirits.
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • French (good knowledge)
  • English (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Part of the Migros Group: Strong brand anchored in the retail trade under the Migros Group umbrella
  • Central place of work: In the casual Binz district in Zurich district 3 (2 minutes by foot from Zurich station Binz).
  • Flexible place of work / home office: One to two days a week working at home. Except in IT - we are even more flexible.
  • Migros Bank: Better off with interest and fees on your private, investment or mortgage accounts.
  • Mobile subscription: Spoiled for choice: With Galaxus Mobile, Wingo or Sunrise already from CHF 10.- one or the other really connected.
  • Individual further education: internal training and financial support for meaningful external education and training.
  • Personal responsibility & freedom: plenty of room for your ideas and to make a difference together with us.
