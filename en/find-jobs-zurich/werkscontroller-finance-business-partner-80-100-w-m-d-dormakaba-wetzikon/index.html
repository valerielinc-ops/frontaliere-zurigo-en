Full description

As a factory controller (Finance Business Partner) – you are responsible for the financial management, planning and analysis of the production plant in Switzerland. You ensure the accuracy and transparency of the financial data, support strategic decisions of the management and actively promote process optimization and the further development of the plant. In this key role, you act as a sparring partner for internal stakeholders on a local, regional and global level.

Open the door to your future: your tasks

  • Responsibility for monthly, quarterly and annual closing processes including ensuring correct, complete and timely financial reports
  • Managing the mid-term planning process, annual budget cycles and rolling forecasts
  • Implementation, maintenance and updating of standard costs in line with operational objectives
  • Analysis of P & V and balance sheet deviations, identification of trends and derivation of measures to increase performance
  • Monitoring relevant KPIs and actively supporting productivity and efficiency initiatives
  • Close cooperation with production, purchasing, supply chain and operations
  • Implementation and monitoring of corporate governance requirements as well as identification and reduction of financial and operational risks
  • Strategic Business Partner Role for Plant Management, Regional Leaders and Global Finance Teams
  • Promote a consistent financial understanding and strengthen financial transparency throughout the company

Hence, we trust you: your capabilities

  • 5+ years of professional experience in finance/controlling, ideally in an industrial or production-related environment
  • Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance or Economics
  • MBA, CPA or comparable additional qualifications are beneficial
  • Very good understanding of production and cost structures
  • Strong analytical skills and high business orientation
  • Secure appearance as a sparring partner at management level
  • Negotiable in German and English

You Can Leave Yourself: A Motivating Work Environment

  • Insights into the financial world of a global corporation with a family culture
  • Participation in exciting financial projects and international exchange
  • A team that supports you and wants to grow together with you
  • Promoting your professional and personal development
  • Attractive salary package, home office option, good public transport connections and a first-class staff restaurant
