Full description

Faculty of Law

Job opening per 1. December 2026 or by arrangement, temporary

The Faculty of Law further develops its examination system with regard to the use of generative AI. The focus is on the question of which certificates of performance can be used to reliably assess the students’ professional and other skills in the future. Existing examination formats and possible adaptations are discussed in a faculty working group.

Tasks

  • Close cooperation with the Dean of Studies and support in the preparation and planning of the project.
  • Review of existing project documents as well as research on AI, legal examinations and experiences of other universities.
  • Development and presentation of own ideas for suitable examination formats
  • Participation in proposals taking into account the applicable study and examination regulations.
  • Substantive preparation and monitoring of the working group as well as documentation of its discussions and results.
  • Taking up and developing ideas and feedback from the working group and sections
  • Development of possible approaches together with the Dean of Studies.
  • participation in concept papers and decision-making bases for the faculty retraite
  • Coordination of open issues with competent bodies.

Profile

  • Completed law studies or a comparable qualification as well as an interest in legal education.
  • Familiarity with the university teaching and examination company as well as with study and examination regulations of UZH or a comparable university
  • Willingness to familiarize yourself with the regulations and teaching offer of the Faculty of Law.
  • Ability to critically assess opportunities and limitations of generative AI for legal examinations.
  • Experience in project work and an independent, structured working method in order to promote tasks independently and to identify the need for coordination at an early stage.
  • Openness to different perspectives and a constructive approach to criticism
  • Ability to organize diverse ideas and prepare them for further discussion.
  • Very good written German, good knowledge of English and pleasure in working with different working groups and specialist bodies.

We offer varied work in a dedicated team and flexible working hours. Diversity and inclusion are important to us. The position includes an own workplace at the Faculty of Law and the advantages of employment at the university. The working time can be divided by agreement with the chair owner. The remuneration is based on cantonal guidelines.
