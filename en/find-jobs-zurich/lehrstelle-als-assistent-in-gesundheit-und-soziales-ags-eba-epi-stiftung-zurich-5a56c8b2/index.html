Full description

Do you want to learn a profession with a heart? At EPI WohnWerk, more than 200 adults live with a disability. You help them live a beautiful life. Every day is different and exciting!

Tasks

  • Support people in everyday life: You work permanently in a residential group. You will help the residents with their daily personal care (e.g. washing, brushing their teeth) and wearing and undressing.
  • Observe health: You learn how people are healthy. For example, you measure the pulse, blood pressure or body weight and tell the team if something changes.
  • Food and drink: You help prepare the meals. You help the residents eat and make sure they drink enough.
  • Order and logistics: You ensure that the rooms and the apartment group are clean and cozy. You help to order and supply materials (such as care items) or food.
  • Free time and companionship: You accompany people in their everyday life – whether during a walk, playing or other group activities.
  • Safety and hygiene: You will learn how to use tools (e.g. wheelchairs) safely and how to keep everything hygienically clean.

Requirements

  • School: You have finished the Sek B or C.
  • Age: You are at least 15 years old when the teaching begins.
  • Joy: You like working with people. If you've ever sniffed, that's great!
  • Personality: You are reliable, nice and enjoy working in a team.
  • Working hours: You know that we also work in the evening or on weekends (shift work).

We offer

  • Teaching: A 2-year training for / assistant: in health and social affairs (EBA).
  • Support: You learn together with other learners in learning workshops.
  • Variety: You do internships in different departments of the foundation.
  • Extras: 5 weeks holidays and discounts (food, pharmacy, bus & train).

Sometimes quite challenging, but never boring: At EPI WohnWerk, no day is like the other. Because the people we accompany each and everyone have their unique life story. Thanks to the cooperation in the interprofessional team, we are well positioned for our working day. Do you want to be part of this team?
