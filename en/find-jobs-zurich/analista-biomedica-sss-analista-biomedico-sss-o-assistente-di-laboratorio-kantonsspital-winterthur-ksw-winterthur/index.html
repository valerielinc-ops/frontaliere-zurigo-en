Full description

For our modernly established, accredited histology laboratory (focus on conventional histology and immunohistochemistry) at the Institute of Pathology, we are looking for a flexible and resilient personality as a biomedical analyst HF / biomedical analyst HF or laboratory assistant / laboratory assistant.

Tasks

  • Your main task is to process tissue samples.
  • You are active in various fields such as sample acceptance and processing, embedding, paraffin cutting, quick cuts, special dyeings and immunohistochemistry
  • Operation and maintenance of various laboratory equipment
  • Assistance with the blank in the distributor
  • Working according to ISO standard 15189

Profile

  • You have completed training as a biomedical analyst / analyst HF
  • Professional experience in histology is beneficial
  • You are used to performing accurately and efficiently under high workload
  • You want to make an important contribution to the diagnosis of diseases, monitoring treatments and prevention
  • You are communicative and enjoy working in a team
