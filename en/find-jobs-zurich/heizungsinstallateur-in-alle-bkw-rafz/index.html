Full description

We make habitats worth living.

Become part of a motivated team in a dynamic company with tradition and family touch. Together we strengthen our position as a first-class company in building technology!

Your tasks

  • Independent implementation of heating renovations in single- and multi-family houses.
  • Replacement of existing oil and gas heating systems with modern heat pump systems.
  • Installation, conversion and commissioning of heating systems.
  • Assembly of heat pumps, storage, piping and heating distributions.
  • Coordination and cooperation with customers, planners and other trades on the construction site.
  • Implementation of service, adaptation and optimisation work on existing plants.
  • Compliance with quality, safety and environmental standards.

You bring that with you

  • Completed training as heating installer EFZ.
  • Professional experience in the field of heating renovations is advantageous.
  • Knowledge of heat pumps or interest in developing yourself in this area.
  • Independent, reliable and customer-oriented working method.
  • Teamwork and responsibility.
  • Category B driving licence.
