The Department of Nature Conservation is the office responsible for nature conservation and biodiversity in the Canton of Zurich in the Office for Landscape and Nature of the Building Directorate. Employees are committed to the continued existence of valuable habitats and are committed to ensuring that the coexistence of people and nature works in the Zurich canton of business.
