Full description

The M-Infoline is the central contact point for inquiries from Migros customers. The top priority is customer satisfaction. With our commitment to the highest quality and performance in customer care, the M-Infoline makes a significant contribution to the company's success.

Responsibilities

  • Competent and service-oriented handling of customer inquiries via telephone, as well as support in written customer communication (e.g., contact form, chat, WhatsApp) in German and French
  • Recording, independently responding to, and finding solutions for customer concerns with specialized departments on various topics related to the Migros world
  • Close collaboration with the marketing department on product-specific topics

Requirements

  • Work experience: in a call center or another service-oriented field
  • Completed vocational training (EFZ): completed vocational training, commercial school, or high school diploma
  • German (fluent) French (fluent): additional foreign language skills are an advantage
  • Willingness to work on Saturdays (1–2 times per month)
  • Excellent oral communication skills in German and French
  • Independent and flexible working style with a passion for efficient work and competent solution finding
  • Resilient and customer-oriented personality with a strong flair for communication

What we offer

  • Modern infrastructure: Diverse work zones and modern co-working spaces with advanced equipment
  • Insurance: Up to 25% discount on various insurances such as car, private liability, household, legal protection, and many more
  • Occupational pension: Above-average benefits and three different savings plans tailored to individual life situations
  • Cumulus points: Additional Cumulus points in all Migros stores and various subsidiaries
  • Club school and leisure facilities: 70% discount on Migros Club School offers, 30% discount for fitness/wellness/aquaparks
  • Healthy working: Nursing and rest rooms as well as massage options
