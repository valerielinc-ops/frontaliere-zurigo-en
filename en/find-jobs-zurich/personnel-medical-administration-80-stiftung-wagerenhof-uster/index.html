Full description

By 1 December 2026 or by agreement we are looking for you as Personnel/in Medical Administration (80%) At the heart of what we do is people with disabilities. As a responsible, communicative and service-oriented personality, you are part of the administration in the field of health and medicine. In doing so, you make a significant contribution to the well-being and safety of the people entrusted to us. The Wagerenhof Foundation (affectionately called “Wagi”) in Uster has been a permanent home for around 250 people with multiple cognitive impairments for more than 100 years and offers over 50 employees who need support accompanied jobs. We are a recognized, non-profit institution with a social mission. With a demand-oriented offer of living and care places as well as various day and leisure offers, we promote the quality of life, participation and self-determination of our residents. The special features of the Wagerenhof include size and Dörfli character, which allows space for encounters. For employees with different careers and professionalism, the Wagerenhof Foundation is an attractive employer with a wide range of development opportunities. For learners, Wagerenhof is a competent training partner.

In these activities you support us

  • General secretarial work
  • Arrange and organize external medical and dental appointments for our residents and appropriate coordination with the various residential groups
  • Obtaining medical records and medical reports
  • Electronic storage of medical resident documents
  • Writing of transfer letters and reports by keywords and submission
  • Processing of incoming mail
  • Management of insurance forms and requests
  • Minutes of meetings

You fit for us and for this place, if you also put the people entrusted to us at the center of your activities and you bring the following:

  • Completed training as MPA, medical secretary or hospital secretary
  • Professional experience in the medical field
  • Saddle Festival in Medical Terminology
  • Good PC user knowledge (familiar with all MS Office programs)
  • Very good knowledge of German (negotiable in word and writing)

This makes us the place for your work
