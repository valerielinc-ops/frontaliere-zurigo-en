Full description

As part of the Zurich Detention Centers (UGZ), Dielsdorf Prison specializes in accommodating women and has 57 detention centers. The focus is on pre-trial and security detention, supplemented by the execution of short and substitute prison sentences. If necessary, women can be admitted together with their infants up to the age of around 18 months. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a resilient and committed personality for a limited period of one year as of January 2027 (with possible transfer to a permanent position). You do that with us.

  • Operation of the security center, mainly in the evening, on weekends and public holidays
  • Ensure safety, opening of doors and handling of alarms
  • Triggering the safety device in emergency situations
  • Coordinating the missions of the supervisors, for example during visits and postponements
  • Implementation of the entry check
  • Receiving and processing call requests of the admitted persons and answering and forwarding requests
  • Maintaining the journal and the necessary documentation
  • Submission of information to instructors
  • Assistance in care and administration tasks You bring that with you
  • You are at least 25 years old and psychologically resilient
  • You meet people empathetically, respectfully and friendly and can build relationships and resolve conflicts professionally
  • You act responsibly, independently, secretly and carefully
  • You are capable of teamwork, communicate clearly, like to learn new things and develop professionally

Zurich Detention Centers (UGZ) are one of eight main departments of JuWe. They comprise seven companies with about 735 prison places and about 500 employees. They are responsible for provisional arrest, pre-trial detention and security detention, as well as the early execution of penalties and measures until transfer to a prison facility. From the outset, they thus fulfil a central function within the Zurich justice system. As employees of the Zurich Detention Centers, we provide structured and human support for detained persons with expertise and commitment and help ensure that criminal proceedings are carried out in an orderly manner before a decision is made on guilt or innocence. With around 1400 employees, JuWe is the largest office within the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs and is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as for the execution of various types of detention. Our employees from around 40 professions support and promote the reintegration of delinquents and work effectively and progressively for a safe and just society. We offer...
