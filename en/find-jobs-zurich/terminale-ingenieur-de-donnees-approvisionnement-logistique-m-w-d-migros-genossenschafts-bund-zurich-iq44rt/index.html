Full description

The Analytics, Reporting, Data & Platfoms division contributes to increasing the value of the Migros Group through the targeted use of data. We use and build a modern data architecture on a state-of-the-art, cloud-based tech stack. As a motivated data engineer, you will help us model and deploy various data sources on the Migros Analytics Data Platform. Mansioni

  • You create automated data pipelines (dbt, SQL, Airflow/CloudComposer), as well as continuous integration and deployment workflows for ETL processes using CI/CD
  • You design and realize data models on the basis of DataVault 2.0, which represent the structure and relationships of the data from the areas of procurement and logistics
  • You take over the professional lead of the team, drive architecture decisions and are in close exchange with the PO
  • You develop and operate scalable, recyclable data products (Data Mesh), which provide business-critical key figures within Migros
