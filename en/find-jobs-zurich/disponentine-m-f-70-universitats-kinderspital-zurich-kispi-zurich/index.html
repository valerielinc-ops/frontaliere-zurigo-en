Children's Hospital Zurich Eleonorenstiftung | online since : 09.09.2026

Your tasks

  • Planning and coordination of consultation appointments and treatments for Center 3 (oncology, immunology, SZT, hematology).
  • Planning of the day clinic in center 3 based on complex specifications and protocols (chemotherapies, infusion appointments, procedures including anesthesia)
  • Planning of the associated bed station (administration of patient and case data, orders, clarifications)
  • Ensure optimal utilization of all center resources involved in close cooperation with the entire treatment team
  • Schedule coordination with various specialist areas
  • The daily exchange with patient parents as well as internal and external bodies – partly in English – is part of this for you.

Your profile

  • Professional experience as a dispatcher in healthcare
  • Very well organized, structured and service-oriented personality
  • IT knowledge in the tools PEP, RAP and PHOENIX is an advantage
  • Very good language skills in German and English, other languages are beneficial
  • Creativity and perseverance are an important part, even if it gets hectic, keep a cool head and keep track.
