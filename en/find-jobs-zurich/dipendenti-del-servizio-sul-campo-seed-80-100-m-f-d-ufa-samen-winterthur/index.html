fenaco is a cooperative with around 11,600 employees and pursues the overarching goal of supporting farmers in the economic development of their companies. It is active in the fields of agriculture, food industry, retail and energy with well-known Swiss brands. In the seed sector, we are the competent partner for farmers, vegetable producers, LANDI and many more with the UFA seed brand. Have you handed over your business to the next generation and would like to continue to actively contribute your knowledge and experience to agriculture? Then we have the right task for you. For the sales regions of Zurich / Zurich Oberland / Thurgau or Zurich / Aargau, we are looking for a committed personality as a sales representative for seeds 80-100% (m/f/d) with immediate effect or by appointment.

Andreas Niederberger HR Business Partner +41 58 433 50 32

These tasks inspire you

  • You will be out and about every day and advise farmers, contractors and LANDI competently on all aspects of seeds.
  • Your area of activity extends over 60,000 hectares of agricultural land.
  • Thanks to your proximity to practice, you will literally hear "the grass grow" and bring your expertise, which you have acquired throughout your life, to the consultation.
  • You will support farmers in choosing the optimal seeds for location, use and yield targets.
  • Together with the LANDI teams, you plan sales activities and customer events.
  • You shape your sales area independently and can make a difference.

We are looking forward to this profile

  • Crop cultivation is your passion.
  • You like to share your practical experience and knowledge with others and are open to new things.
  • You have an agricultural apprenticeship (EFZ) and still have a lot of energy after the farm handover.
  • You are sociable, customer-oriented and speak the language of agriculture. Ideally, you live in the sales area.
  • You work independently, responsibly and appreciate the freedom in the field.

Discover your benefits

  • Training and development: We invest in the development of our employees. A wide range of internal training courses is open to everyone, and we support external training.
  • Attractive vacation policy: Our vacation policy guarantees all employees at least 5 weeks of vacation. From 50. Year of age, it is 6 weeks and from the 60. Age even 7 weeks. In addition, the purchase of additional vacation days is possible.
  • Family time: Mothers receive 100 percent of their insured salary during the 18 weeks of maternity leave. The other parent enjoys 3 weeks of family time at full pay.
  • Insurance benefits: We protect our employees with above-average social and pension benefits and offer special conditions on supplementary insurance with various health insurance companies for their entire family.
  • Benefits and discounts: Our employees benefit from discounts on shopping and refuelling, credits on REKA money and discounted mobile subscriptions for them and their families, among other things.
