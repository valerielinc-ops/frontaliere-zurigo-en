Do you want to contribute your expertise in neurosurgery and at the same time accompany learners on their professional path?

With us, you take on responsibility in everyday care, promote the development of the next generation and become part of a committed team that combines professionalism and humanity.

YOUR TASKS

  • Both competent and individual care and care of our patients taking into account our nursing mission statement as well as independent implementation of the diagnostic and therapeutic measures prescribed by the doctor
  • Identification and control of emergency situations and initiation of adequate measures until the arrival of the doctor
  • Assuming responsibility for the correct patient documentation as well as information sharing in the assigned task area
  • Introduction and support of new employees and learners

In your additional function as a vocational trainer, you take on the following activities:

  • Assuming responsibility for the correct patient documentation as well as information sharing in the assigned task area
  • Competent accompaniment as well as support of the learner health professional and pedagogical during their practical training
  • Professional implementation of the educational mission in the department according to the training concept

Your profile

  • Completed training for / professional health (SRC recognition for foreign degrees)
  • Completed training as a vocational trainer or ready to complete it
  • Mind. 1-2 years of work experience in acute care
