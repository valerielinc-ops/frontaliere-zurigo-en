We at pfister pursue a vision: We want to move our products into every Swiss home. We are seeking active support for this. Help us bring the approximately 100,000 products in our range into the homes of our customers. Whether in one of the 18 branches or at the headquarters in Suhr: You can expect a unique environment, working with attractive products and, last but not least, appreciation and respect for your commitment. There are already around 1,400 people working at pfister today – will you join soon?

Your new tasks

  • During the 4-year training to become a polydesigner in 3D, you will learn the craft of three-dimensional design from trained specialists.
  • You will learn how to implement inspiring and promotional presentations from design, planning and organization to execution
  • For this purpose, countless accessories, materials and lighting possibilities are available, which you get to know well during the apprenticeship period
  • You have a flair for colors and shapes
  • You also enjoy the design of harmonious rooms
  • You like craftsmanship and can use your skills fully in creating and presenting decorative items
