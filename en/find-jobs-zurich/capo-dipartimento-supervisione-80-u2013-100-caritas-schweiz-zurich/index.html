Full description

  • Caritas Zurich is an independent Catholic aid organization. We work for families and disadvantaged people affected by poverty, regardless of nationality or worldview. In this way, we make a significant contribution to their social integration in the Canton of Zurich.

Head of Refugee Services (80%)

Refugees face major challenges after their arrival in Switzerland. Caritas Zürich joins forces to support the integration of refugees; this in addition to the existing legal structures of the canton and in coordination in particular with the reformed regional church. We lead the Refugee Centre on behalf of the Catholic Church in the Canton of Zurich.

As a result of a change of staff, we are looking for 1. October 2026 or, by agreement, a dedicated head of the Refugee Centre (80%).

Their tasks

  • Leadership and further development of the Refugee Centre
  • Cooperation with parishes/church communities and the Communication Office of the Synodal Council
  • Planning and implementation of training and information provision

Stages

  • Networking with local cooperation partners*
  • Internal cooperation with other departments at Caritas Zurich
  • Implementation of the project «zäme da» within the framework of the Integration Agenda Zurich (temporary until 2027, option for continuation) in a co-project management
  • Documentation of activities and reporting to the Refugee Assistance Commission
  • Internal and external communication, cooperation with especially Catholic communication bodies

They have experience and knowledge of transcultural processes. In addition, you are characterized by high social competence, organizational flair and the ability to delegate and coordinate processes. In addition, bring the following:

  • Social work or comparable training with focus on socio-cultural animation, community work, project management
  • High communication competence, possibly. Further education in intercultural communication
  • Willingness to work flexible hours
  • Experience in volunteering and integration (with migrants and/or asylum)
  • Competences in adult education
  • Knowledge of church structures in the Canton of Zurich
  • Basic knowledge of the asylum procedure and the legal basis in the Asylum Act as well as in the Aliens and Integration Act
