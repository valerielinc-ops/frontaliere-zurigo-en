Full description

Doing the right thing. In Switzerland and in around 20 countries on four continents. Engage with us for the vision of a solidarity society, as Caritas Switzerland has been running asylum centres for families, individuals and unaccompanied minors (MNA) at five locations in the Canton of Zurich since March 2024 on behalf of the Cantonal Social Office (KSA). Their tasks

  • Support of social-educational caregivers in establishing/promoting everyday practical skills (e.g. cleaning, homework / learning strategies, handling money / pocket money, balanced nutrition and personal hygiene etc.)
  • Establishment/planning/implementation of daily structure, leisure/employment offers
  • Support for social-educational caregivers in the promotion of young people in dealing with money (budgeting, saving, paying bills), payment of pocket money, contributions in kind
  • Accompanying the MNA to necessary appointments (doctor’s practice, administrative procedures, material purchases, SBB, etc.) in the absence of the social-educational reference person or in agreement with the residential group or centre management
  • Support for social-educational caregivers in crisis interventions and conflict management
  • Support in complying with house rules and maintaining a good residential group atmosphere
  • Management of inventory and consumables: Procurement in consultation with team and/or centre management taking into account the specifications (Leaflet on leisure, Basic Documents KSA)
  • Documentation of incidents/observations in the case management system Tutoris

Our requirements

  • Completed training / further education, e.g. as a specialist care, migration specialist, social support or similar
  • Experience in the field of migration and in caring for people is beneficial
  • Enjoy working with unaccompanied minors asylum seekers (MNA) and interest in different cultural backgrounds
  • Ability to guide, accompany and empower young people in domestic activities
  • Patience, assertiveness and an appreciative attitude in dealing with young people
  • Ability to set clear boundaries and communicate rules in an understandable and consistent manner
  • Willingness to work irregularly, weekends and shifts in accordance with operational requirements
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German
  • Further language skills are advantageous
  • Safe handling of current digital work equipment
  • A perfect special private extract from the criminal record or Willingness to submit it before taking up the job
  • High resilience and resilience

Place of work: Canton of Zurich (Zurich-Oerlikon) Start of work: immediately or by agreement The job is limited to 31. March 2027. Caritas offers attractive employment conditions and an open working environment in a committed team. For the active development and implementation of the digital transformation, we count on employees with a digital and agile mindset who like to participate in change processes.
