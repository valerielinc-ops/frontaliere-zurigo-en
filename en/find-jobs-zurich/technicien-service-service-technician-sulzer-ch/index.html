Full description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and innovative development solutions that enable a successful and more sustainable society.

About the role

Job Summary The Lead (Field) Service Technician is responsible for installing, configuring, calibrating, and repairing technical products and systems at customer field locations. This role performs a variety of maintenance and technical support tasks and may provide onsite training to customer support. This job profile is intended for experienced support-level staff that generally works automatically and only require close supervision in non-routine situations. Summary of the post

As a Service Technician, you will be responsible for the installation, maintenance, troubleshooting and repair of Sulzer Ensival Moret equipment at our customers in France and sometimes abroad, guaranteeing their optimal performance and customer satisfaction.

Post responsibilities

  • Complete installation and commissioning of Sulzer Ensival Moret pumps, agitators and other equipment on customer sites.
  • Conduct preventive and corrective maintenance operations, including fault diagnosis and parts replacement.
  • Provide troubleshooting and repair of complex equipment, identifying the root causes of problems and proposing durable solutions.
  • Write detailed intervention reports (diagnostics, actions, documents used, recommendations).
  • Advise and train customers on the use and maintenance of their equipment.
  • Contribute to the continuous improvement of products and services by bringing back the field information.
  • Meet safety standards and quality procedures of the company and customers.
  • Participate in the technical penalty payment according to established schedules.

Qualifications required

  • BTS or DUT in industrial, mechanical, electromechanical or equivalent maintenance.
  • Minimum 3 years experience as a Service Technician or maintenance of industrial equipment, ideally in the area of pumps or rotating machines.
  • Strong skills in mechanics, hydraulics, and ideally in electricity/automatism.
  • Ability to read and interpret technical diagrams and plans.
  • Mastery of computer tools (Pack Office, GMAO/ERP software).
  • Excellent communication skills and customer relationship.
  • Autonomy, rigor, analysis and sense of responsibility.
  • Permit B valid indispensable.
  • Availability for frequent trips in France and on time internationally.
  • Mastery of French (written and oral) and good basics in technical English.
