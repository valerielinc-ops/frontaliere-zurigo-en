Full description

Are you ready for a job where you can make a difference for people and nature?

For our base Seebach in the district of North 2, we are looking for a group leader for the care and maintenance of the cemetery and parks.

Tasks

  • They organize, coordinate and plan work in the assigned work area
  • They independently carry out demanding care and maintenance work (planting, seeding, cutting and mowing work, plant nutrition, plant protection, plant use). In addition, you ensure the quality in the areas assigned to you, taking into account the natural care goals
  • They participate in maintenance, maintenance and cleaning work on machines, tools and infrastructure and dispose of machines and materials
  • They are responsible for the professional management of a group of about 3-5 employees and help with the training of learners

Profile

  • Completed training as a gardener with several years of experience as a customer gardener in the maintenance of demanding green areas; ideally with professional training (e.g. customer gardener, foremen, cemetery specialist at Jardin Suisse or equivalent) or the willingness to complete it
  • Experience in the professional management of employees
  • Independent and team-capable personality with a service-oriented working method
  • Ticket Cat. B is a mandatory requirement
  • Verified in MS-Office

We offer

As a service department of the City of Zurich, we offer advanced employment conditions with excellent social benefits, support for further training and very good conditions for reconciling work and private life.

Their gross annual wage is between 76'000.00 and 89'000.00 francs (100% expense). Their effective wage may differ from this wage range depending on experience and skills.

About us

Zurich greener - that is our vision. Because Grün City of Zurich maintains, designs and manages the public green areas. These make up about 40 percent of the city area. With their work, our 600 employees contribute to the high quality of life in the city of Zurich.

We believe in a diverse and inclusive workforce and are proud to be an equal opportunities employer.
