Full description

<b>Bülach Hospital grows - grow with it.</b>

Tasks

  • Organisation and preparation of the outpatient urological consultation
  • Assistance during the consultation hours as well as support for urological investigations and interventions
  • Operation of the telephone and provision of information to internal and external bodies
  • Perform blood sampling and ensure pre-analytical processing of urine samples
  • Power measurement according to TarDoc
  • Maintenance of electronic health records, including collection and updating of patient data and case closures
  • Assumption of deputies in the event of absence within the team
  • Implementation and organisation of ordering

Requirements

  • Completed training as a nurse with flair for administrative activities or training as a medical practice assistant / medical practice assistant (MPA) with corresponding professional experience
  • At least 3 years of professional experience
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and script as well as in-depth knowledge of medical terminology
  • Rapid comprehension and an independent, structured and careful working method
  • Strong teamwork and a high sense of responsibility
  • Resilience and flexibility
  • Strong service orientation
