Full description

Do you enjoy working with people and looking for a new challenge?

We are looking for 1. December 2026 an empathetic and experienced leader for our kitchen team in the health center for the Alter Herzogenmühle.

Tasks

  • They bear the overall responsibility for a high-quality, safe and economically managed kitchen operation that functions smoothly seven days a week.
  • You are responsible for the menu design, planning, calculation and implementation of a varied, seasonal offer.
  • They develop a varied culinary offer – from classical and traditional cuisine to full-fledged vegetarian dishes and modern aperitifs – and thus inspire both our residents and the employees and external guests of our public restaurant.
  • You create deployment plans for the 7-member kitchen team (including learners), carry out regular success checks, employee interviews and assessments and promote and challenge your employees to personal responsibility and self-employment.
  • They regularly conduct team meetings, provide training for the kitchen team and train learners.

Profile

  • Team player with comprehensive leadership experience and high reflective ability
  • Enthusiasm for interdisciplinary cooperation, flexibility and high sensitivity for people and situations
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as a cook EFZ and additional training as a head cook with a federal vocational examination or equivalent further education
  • Creativity and passion for fresh, seasonal, full and regional cuisine
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing as well as excellent communication skills

We offer

Enjoy an exciting field of work in a professional and team-oriented working environment with a du-culture throughout the company. The human being is at our center.

As part of the city of Zurich and due to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, progressive employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

Benefit from design and development opportunities in the kitchen and in the gastronomic offer for internal as well as for our various external events.

Depending on experience, the annual salary for this function is between CHF 85'228 and CHF 113'882.– (100%).

About us

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

The Health Center for the Old Herzogenmühle in District 12 in Schwamendingen, the “Gartenstadt” of Zurich, offers 80 residents a homely home with all amenities. Our quarter impresses with the many green areas, parks and local recreation areas that give the district a relaxed and family atmosphere.
