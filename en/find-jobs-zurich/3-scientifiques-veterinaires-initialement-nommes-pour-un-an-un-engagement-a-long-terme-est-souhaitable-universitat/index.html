Aufgaben

An exciting working environment awaits you at the interface of Veterinary Medicine, Human Medicine and future Technologies. As a member of the veterinary team, you will work on various research projects, which include the following responsibilities:

  • Veterinary support, including anesthesia, surgery, pre- and post-operative care for farm animals
  • Contact person for researchers and for handling their requests regarding planning and implementing new projects including writing and managing animal experimental licenses
  • Advising scientists on aspects of animal welfare and 3Rs
  • Active contribution to the smooth running of operations (ordering and organization of consumables and medicines as well as maintenance of the infrastructure)
  • Establishment and maintenance of quality standards (Good Laboratory Practice (GLP))

Anforderungen

  • Degree in veterinary medicine eligible to work in Switzerland (doctorate degree optional, newly Grads welcome)
  • Interest in working with farm animals
  • Great interest in scientific work
  • Flair for novel technologies
  • You are a reliable, precise and well-organized personality with a quick grasp and an independent and careful way of working, and a high level of responsibility
  • You enjoy working independently as well as in a team
  • High level of motivation and flexibility (irregular working schedule), including willingness to work on weekends, public holidays and shift work
  • You are looking to establish yourself long-term in the field and potentially aspire an academic career
