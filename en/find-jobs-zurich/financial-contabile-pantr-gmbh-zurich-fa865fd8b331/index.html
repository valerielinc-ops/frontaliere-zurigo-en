Full description

For one of the leading and most renowned Swiss private banking groups in wealth management, we are looking for an analytically strong and graduate-oriented personality as a financial accountant. In this challenging role, you manage both national and international companies and contribute directly to the integrity and efficiency of global financial operations. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for you!

This is your job

  • Finance & Group: Independent management of the general ledger and financial reporting for the Swiss core bank and related national and international subsidiaries.
  • Financial statements & Reporting: Preparation of monthly financial reports, local statutory financial statements and Group reporting in accordance with IFRS.
  • Compliance & Taxation: Ensure and monitor the internal control system (ICS), prepare VAT bills according to local regulations and implement new internal and external accounting standards.
  • Corporate Activities: Active support in strategic corporate activities such as the creation of new entities, acquisitions or liquidations, as well as carrying out analytical reviews of balance sheet and income statement positions.
  • Interfaces & IT: Cross-divisional cooperation with the tax, controlling, IT and payroll departments as well as active participation in process optimizations and SAP initiatives (R/3, S/4HANA).

This is what you bring with you

  • Education: Completed higher education in finance and accounting (e.g. Federal Law). graduated:r auditor:in / expert:in in accounting and controlling) or a business studies with a focus on finance.
  • Experience: At least 3 years of in-depth professional experience in financial accounting, ideally acquired within the banking sector or financial services industry.
  • Expertise & IT: Well-founded, proven knowledge of international accounting in accordance with IFRS as well as a secure handling of MS Office (SAP R/3 or S/4HANA knowledge are a strong advantage).
  • Languages: Flowing and stylish German and English skills in words and writing for the international environment.
  • Personality: Strong analytical skills, an exact working style and a proactive, team-oriented working style.

This is what awaits you from the customer

  • Environment: A highly professional, international and stable working environment at one of the most respected players in the Swiss financial centre.
  • Culture: A culture characterized by entrepreneurship and self-responsibility, which offers individual scope for design.
  • Perspectives: First-class professional development opportunities at the interface to global finance and IT projects.
