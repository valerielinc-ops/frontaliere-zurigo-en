Network- / Operation Engineer — Stadt Zürich

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

With your know-how, you will keep our telecommunications and network infrastructure running and further develop it.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for the operation and expansion of ewz Telecom's telecommunications platforms (FTTH/business services).
  • You reliably operate, maintain and monitor our network infrastructure and the associated management systems.
  • You will contribute your know-how and actively participate in network projects, rollouts and infrastructure migrations.
  • You provide on-call service in 2nd/3rd level support, analyze and resolve complex network faults and carry out root cause analyses.
  • As an ewz.zürinet expert, you will support the technical conception of new network solutions and work closely with internal IT teams as well as external service providers and manufacturers.

profile

  • You have a degree (University/FH/HF/EFZ) in computer science, telematics, information technology or a related field - alternatively, you can demonstrate equivalent training and relevant professional experience.
  • You have several years of professional experience in network engineering and are very familiar with the operation and maintenance of telecommunications equipment.
  • You are familiar with Nokia, Cisco or comparable network environments and have good knowledge of MPLS, WDM, routing and switching as well as the corresponding protocols.
  • You have experience in network monitoring and troubleshooting and approach challenges analytically and solution-oriented.
  • You are characterized by quick comprehension, accuracy and team spirit. You can also communicate well in English and have a category B driving license.
