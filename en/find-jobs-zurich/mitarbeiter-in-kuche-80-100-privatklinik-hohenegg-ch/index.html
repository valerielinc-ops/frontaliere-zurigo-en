Full description

Hohenegg Private Clinic is a specialist clinic with 95 beds for psychiatry, psychotherapy and psychosomatics. It is open and accessible to private, semi-private and general insured persons from Switzerland as well as self-payers. It is housed in a listed magnificent complex, in the middle of nature and high above Lake Zurich. In addition to inpatient services, we operate a mental health centre (ZPG) for outpatient and hospital-associated psychiatry at two locations. The sponsorship is a charitable foundation.
