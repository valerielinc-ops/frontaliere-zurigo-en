Full description

Canton of Zurich Directorate of Health Veterinary Office Foot Rot Coordination Office 100% The Veterinary Office of the Canton of Zurich is committed to animal welfare and animal health. It helps ensure animal welfare and the safety of foods of animal origin. Foot rot is a widespread, painful hoof disease that primarily affects sheep. In October 2024, the five-year national programme to combat foot rot was launched. Due to an internal staff transfer, we are looking for a project coordinator from 1 February 2027 to 31 January 2029 to continue the well-functioning control programme. Are you accustomed to handling sheep, happy to lend a hand yourself and connected to sheep farming? Are you skilled at communicating with different stakeholder groups, enjoy keeping the reins in hand and are not afraid of challenging discussions? Then you are the person we are looking for! Tasks

  • Planning and coordination of sampling and remediation in the Canton of Zurich
  • Monitoring of control measures and regular exchange with all parties involved
  • Assistance with the design and administration of internal guidelines and processes
  • Independent sampling and advising external sample collectors
  • Triage and processing of cases involving deficiencies
  • Further tasks in the area of crisis preparedness and disease surveillance and processing of cases involving deficiencies Profile
  • Completed vocational training in agriculture or a comparable field
  • Several years of professional experience, ideally also working with sheep
  • Independent, structured way of working
  • Coordination skills and enthusiasm for implementing a new monitoring program
  • Assertiveness and communication skills
  • Category B driver's license
  • Teamwork and flexibility
  • Affinity for the digital working world
  • Good German language skills, spoken and written Offer
  • Interesting, meaningful and varied work content
  • Responsible range of duties in the field of animal health, with interfaces to animal welfare and primary production
  • Opportunity for further training in the field of work
  • Motivated young team with helpful colleagues
  • Social benefits and employment conditions in accordance with cantonal guidelines Application Do you feel addressed?Then we look forward to receiving your online -application by 20. November 2026. Do you have any questions? Ms Andrea Gál- Stoller, Head of the Animal Diseases & Veterinary Professions team, will be happy to provide information about the assignment and the challenges at 043 259 41 41. Information about our office can be found at www.zh.ch/veta
