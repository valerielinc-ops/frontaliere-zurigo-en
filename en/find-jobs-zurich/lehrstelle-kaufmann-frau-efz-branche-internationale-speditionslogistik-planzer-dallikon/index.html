Full description

Brand of the Planzer Group: Helveticor.

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, participate and grow with us. Become part of our family and shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us.

Tasks

  • Planning international transports supports you with regard to punctuality, safety and smooth processes
  • Transports by truck, train, ship or plane are co-organized and accompanied by you
  • In customs matters, insurance and international regulations, you actively participate and find reliable solutions
  • In daily contact with customers, partners and service providers at home and abroad, you show communication strength
  • Orders that have to be processed by air freight, truck or sea freight, as well as documents and shipments, you process and track them in the system
  • Administrative activities are a matter of course for you and you do it in a structured and motivated way.

Profile

  • Curiosity, versatility and joy of variety help you to progress steadily in this profession
  • On the computer you move safely, and even with numbers you handle sovereignly
  • Geographical knowledge will make you fun and capitals will interest you
  • Whether airport or seaport, you are open to discovering new things about it
  • Organizational talent, independence and team spirit are among your strengths
  • With your perseverance, your determination and your ambition, you stay motivated even in demanding situations.
  • You use your linguistic skills skillfully and safely, especially in German orally and in writing.

Benefits

  • Respect, trust and fair treatment count with us – we are with you and accompany you personally on your way.
  • In the first year of apprenticeship you have 8, in the 2nd 7 and in the following years 6 weeks of vacation.
  • During your training time with us, you participate in various events such as, WelcomeDay, specialization seminar, apprentice camp, apprenticeship graduation party, etc.
  • With us, not only the wage is right: you get a fair apprentice wage plus 13. Monthly wages. If you show full commitment at school, in the üK and in the company, you can even secure a performance bonus. We pay for the school material and for the notebook we give you a financial boost. On top of that, there is a half-tax subscription for your entire apprenticeship.
  • After completing your training, there are very good chances of permanent employment.
  • Enjoy delicious menus at discounted prices.

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
