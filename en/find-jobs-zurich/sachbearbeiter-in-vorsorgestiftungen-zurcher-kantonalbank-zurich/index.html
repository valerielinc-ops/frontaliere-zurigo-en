Our team actively shapes the topics of pillar 3a and freedom of movement, handles the different business cases of the Sparen 3 pension foundation and the two freedom of movement foundations of the ZKB and is the point of contact for our customers and sales employees. We focus on the needs of our customers and thus want to participate in the long-term success of our products and services. Our motivated pension team is looking forward to reinforcement by a committed personality who convinces with new ideas and a cooperative way of working.

  • Identification of optimization potential for processes/services and proactive input of ideas for further development
  • Commercial training completed
  • Professional card or further education in the topic of prevention resp. Social security funds

  • You enjoy direct customer contact and the ability to respond to different needs
  • Excellent oral and written knowledge of German
  • Good oral English and French skills (further language skills beneficial)
  • A motivated team with a great spirit that looks forward to working with you and appreciates your ideas
  • Varied activity – supported by contemporary tools
  • Various opportunities to develop personally and professionally
  • A modern workplace in the bustling district 5 with very good transport connections
  • Various events and internal networks that offer you the opportunity to network quickly
  • Unique package with attractive basic salary, variable compensation and versatile benefits
