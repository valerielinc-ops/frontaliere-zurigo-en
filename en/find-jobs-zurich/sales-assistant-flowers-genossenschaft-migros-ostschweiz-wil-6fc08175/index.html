Full description

Do you have an eye for beauty and are enthusiastic about the world of flowers? 🌸💐 Do you have the skills to bind in the bouquets, are creative and take pleasure in inspiring our customers with your floral creations? 😊 Then you are exactly right with us! ✅ Let us be «tämä success». 🤝🧡

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Eastern Switzerland, MM Wil, Obere Bahnhofstr. 5, 9500 Wil

Mansioni

  • You make sure that in the area of flowers 🌸 all shelves are always well filled and the goods are optimally managed, so that customers always find what they are looking for. 🛒
  • Friendly and competent customer service and advice 😊🛍️ are a matter of course for you and you advise the customers with your expertise.
  • You ensure that the products are presented attractively and efficiently arranged to create a pleasant shopping experience 🛍️ and are responsible for the design of the product presentation.
  • You take responsibility for ordering and checking the goods 📦💳, so that there is not too much or too little on the shelf.
  • With your commitment, you help ensure that your area remains neat, clean and inviting ✨.
  • Your creative vein and enthusiasm for flowers allow you to create beautiful arrangements and inspire customers with your ideas.

Requirements

  • Professional experience: in the retail trade, industry flower
  • Completed basic or further training: in the retail trade, industry flower; as a florist or gardener
  • You bring good expertise in the field of flowers 🌸.
  • Reliability, flexibility and a high level of commitment 💪⏰ distinguish you – you are always there when you are needed.
  • With your independent work and your friendly approach 😊🤝 you create a pleasant atmosphere for customers and team.
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Mobile subscription: Highly discounted conditions for private mobile subscription also for family and friends
  • Occupational pension provision: Attractive pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation in self-selected courses
