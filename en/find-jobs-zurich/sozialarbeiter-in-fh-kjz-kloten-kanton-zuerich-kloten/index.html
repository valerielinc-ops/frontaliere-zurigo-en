Canton of Zurich Education Department Office for Youth and Career Guidance Social Worker FH kjz Kloten 60 - 70% Does your heart beat for children and young people and would you like to make a contribution to their well-being and work towards child protection in the Canton of Zurich? Then you've come to the right place! The Office for Youth and Career Guidance (AJB) informs, advises and supports the population of the Canton of Zurich on the topics of family, work and careers. It provides the various services decentrally in various child and youth welfare centers (kjz) and career information centers (biz). The Social Work and Mandates Department fulfills orders across the entire range of statutory and voluntary youth and family welfare. We support children, young people and parents in dealing with and solving their individual questions and problems. To achieve this, we often work in a network of interdisciplinary service providers. For the kjz Kloten we are looking for a social worker from October 1, 2026 or by appointment: FH 60% to 70% permanent with the following tasks:

  • Carrying out civil child protection measures and investigations on behalf of the KESB
  • Advice and support for parents, children and young people on questions about cohabitation or family conflicts
  • Coordinating and initiating supportive measures
  • Networking with other offers in the area of child and youth welfare. For this demanding task we are looking for a committed and resilient personality. You bring with you:
  • a technical college degree in social work or equivalent training
  • Professional experience as a social worker in youth and family welfare as well as mandate management, ideally already experience from a kjz or from the KESB
  • Good knowledge of social insurance
  • a negotiation-skilled, goal-oriented and assertive personality
  • high social skills
  • good written and oral communication skills
  • Systemic thinking skills and enjoyment of administrative tasks We offer you:
  • an interesting, demanding job in a varied and lively working environment
  • a supportive, interdisciplinary team from social work, psychology, health, administration and law
  • Conditions of employment according to cantonal guidelines
  • a centrally located workplace with modern infrastructure
  • the possibility of working partially from home
  • We attach great importance to cooperation, interdisciplinary, professional dialogue and constant further development. For further information, please contact the Head of Social Work and Mandates Department, Anna Gygax 043 259 95 33. We look forward to receiving your online application.

Workload: 60-70%
