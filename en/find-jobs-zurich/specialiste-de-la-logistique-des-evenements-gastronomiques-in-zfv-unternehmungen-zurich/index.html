Full description

Stadium Letzigrund is one of the most modern stadiums in Switzerland. The gastronomy includes several areas, including the outdoor stands and the restaurant Oval. The VIP area at major events includes 11 lodges, 3 business lounges and the Athletics Lounge for almost 800 guests. It hosts the home games of FC Zurich and Grasshopper Club Zurich, the Meeting Weltklasse Zürich as well as the open-air concerts such as those of Robbie Williams, Rolling Stones, Metallica or Pink. In addition, various corporate and private events take place. With a capacity of over 50,000 spectators at concerts, Stadium Letzigrund is the largest venue in Switzerland. We are looking for you for various cool events in the gastronomy at the Stadium Letzigrund in Zurich! Do you want to support us in logistics immediately or by appointment? In this varied task, weekend assignments and evening assignments are planned, as the assignments are based on the events in the stadium (football games, concerts, world class, etc.). In addition, the outside stands are arranged outside, which entails working in the fresh air in any weather. Your area of activity:

  • Preparation of all outside stands required for the event
  • Creation of the Mise en place in the external stands and the storage rooms
  • Acceptance of goods and management of the warehouse
  • Control of external warehouses - coordination of disposal and implementation of inventory
  • Ensuring supplies at the catering stands
  • Collaboration in all areas during the events
  • Ensuring cleanliness and order in all areas of debt
  • Ensuring compliance with and implementation of ZFV quality and service standards as well as the hygiene and occupational safety guidelines Your strengths:
  • Team Player: Inside Personality
  • Professional experience in logistics
  • First experience in the implementation of major events is advantageous
  • IT affinity
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German
  • Administratively versed With us you will find:
  • And a Guest Job
  • Dynamic working environment
  • Free space for ideas and creativity
  • Development margin
  • Corporate culture characterized by a sense of responsibility and trust We create teams that support each other and in which you are promoted. We work together and focus on everything that does good. Among other things, we attach great importance to wage transparency and equal pay. For this position, we offer an annual salary of between CHF 56'000 and CHF 68'000 (including 13th monthly salary), depending on your experience and qualifications. If you would like to share your passion for gastronomy with us, we look forward to your complete online application.
