Full description

Immediately or by agreement we are looking for you as Professional / Specialist Support / FaBe (70 - 100%) You have experience in caring for and caring for people with mental and physical impairments. With your committed, natural and human-oriented nature, you make an important contribution to making our residents feel at home here. What matters is you. Every single person is at the center with their individual quality of life. We make every effort to ensure that our 250 residents, 246 daycare workers and 52 employees at protected workplaces in the Wagerenhof can experience the highest possible quality of life. Wagerenhof is an attractive employer for employees of different backgrounds and professionalism and a competent training partner for learners of all levels.

For this we give you the responsibility

  • Accompanying and caring for persons with severe mental and physical impairments
  • Interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation
  • Assistance in the management of the group budget

You fit in with us and with this position, if you also put our residents and our supervised employees at the center of your activities and you bring the following:

  • Specialist / Professional Support EFZ
  • Sound professional experience in accompanying and caring for severely impaired people
  • Differentiated perception and reflection
  • Good PC knowledge and good German skills in word and writing

This makes us the place for your work

  • Meaningful tasks with a lot of creative scope
  • The appreciative, human interaction as part of a well-established team
  • Professional support through an interdisciplinary and professionally acting environment
  • Good accessibility with public transport
