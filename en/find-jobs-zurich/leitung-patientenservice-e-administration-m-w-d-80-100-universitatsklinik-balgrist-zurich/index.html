Full description

Welcome to Balgrist Tec AG – the leading competence center for orthopaedic aids and medical retailers in Zurich. As a subsidiary of the University Hospital Balgrist, we combine precision craftsmanship with the latest medicine.

We are looking for an organizationally strong and empathetic leader for our committed team. In this versatile key function, you bring reception and administration together into one team and hold the threads of the entire patient service in hand. You lead your team competently, optimize the administrative processes and at the same time actively participate. Tasks: • Personnel team management: You lead your team confidently, maintain a regular flow of information in the team, coordinate the deployment and holiday planning, lead the annual employee meetings, reconcile goals and promote team culture, integration and mutual acceptability. • Professional management: Together with your team, you ensure the competent care of patients in administrative matters. As a central hub, you communicate specifically with the various interfaces and strengthen the professional knowledge transfer in the team. • Active cooperation: You support your team at the reception and in the administration by participating operationally. This includes the entire spectrum of patient administration, from data collection, appointment coordination, preparation of cost estimates and clarifications with the cost carriers (insurances) to billing. • Interdisciplinary cooperation: You maintain a regular exchange with the business units of Balgrist Tec (shoe technology, orthopaedic technology, rehabilitation technology) and the interfaces with the University Hospital Balgrist (e.g. medical secretariats) in order to optimally coordinate the operational processes. • Process & Quality Management: You ensure a smooth overall process in patient service, optimize consistent administrative processes and clear responsibilities and implement measures to increase quality and efficiency. Requirements • Training: Completed basic commercial training, further training in the field of insurance and/or tariff entities is advantageous. • Professional experience: Sound experience in patient care, in direct exchange with payers (insurance) and contact with referees (doctors, clinics). • Leadership: Leadership experience where you lead and develop your team with clarity and appreciation. You see yourself as a role model who actively participates in everyday life, lives values and promotes cooperation at eye level. You make changes constructively. • Macher mentality: You are an organizational talent with a structured way of working, who likes to participate and acts in a solution-oriented manner. • Language & system skills: You communicate fluently in German and have good English skills (other languages are an advantage). You are adept with ERP systems and the common MS Office applications (Word, Excel, PowerPoint). We offer: • Meaningful environment: A responsible position in a renowned, future-oriented company in the university environment. • Variety: The perfect balance between leadership, process design and lively customer contact. • Attractive employment conditions: These include, among other things, a 50% discount on the ZVV bonus pass or an annual contribution to a GA, paid free day in the birthday month, discounted food in the employee restaurant, various shopping discounts and a non-compulsory pension fund solution.
