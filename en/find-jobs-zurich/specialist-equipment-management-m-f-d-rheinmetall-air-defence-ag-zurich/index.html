Full description

In this versatile function, you combine practical work in tool output with the digital management of our equipment and thus make an important contribution to smooth production processes. Your tasks in detail:

  • Ensuring the operational operation of the tool output taking into account economic efficiency, quality and deadlines
  • Creating tool lists, performing gross/net reconciliations and compiling the required tools
  • Measuring tools and coordinating equipment tasks in close cooperation with team leaders
  • Management and maintenance of the equipment range in the Tool Management System
  • Creating and maintaining digital twins of the required resources in the form of 3D models
  • Tool support for machine operators, AVOR and suppliers
  • Optimization of processes and processes as part of the continuous improvement process (CIP)
  • Ensuring compliance with environmental and occupational safety requirements WHAT YOU SHOULD INVOLVE
  • Completed apprenticeship as a polymechanic EFZ or comparable technical training
  • In-depth knowledge in tool and equipment engineering
  • Good user knowledge in NX CAM, Teamcenter and TMS/Zoller
  • Experience in dealing with Siemens NX/MRL is an advantage
  • Good knowledge of MS Office and SAP
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Clear, user-friendly communication and the ability to represent your own point of view convincingly
  • Openness to new challenges as well as an independent, solution- and practice-oriented working method WHAT WE ARE TO YOU
  • A dynamic working environment with exciting projects
  • Flexible working hours (active and trusted working hours)
  • Very good social benefits and a super-compulsory pension fund
  • Employee benefits of a large corporation, employee share purchase program
  • Promotion of needs-based training
  • Staff restaurant and external catering and shopping facilities nearby
  • Individual internal and external personnel development measures in cooperation with our Rheinmetall Academy
  • Workplace a few minutes walk from the Zurich-Oerlikon train station KONTAKTDETAILS Responsible contact: Christoph Badertscher For questions about your application, please use the contact form. Please note that dossiers of recruiters are not considered.
