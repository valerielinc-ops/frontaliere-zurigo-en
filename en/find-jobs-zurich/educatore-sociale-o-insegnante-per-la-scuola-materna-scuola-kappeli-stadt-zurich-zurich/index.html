Full description

The school Kappeli Primar is a QUIMS school (quality in multicultural schools) in district 9, in the district Grünau of Zurich. She attaches great importance to promoting the sense of community and sees the cultural diversity of her students as an enrichment. The school is surrounded by a green park and has its own school garden. In addition, a children's playground offers both during school and outside a popular meeting place for children to play and stay. Currently, Kappeli Primar School consists of 11 kindergartens and 19 primary classes. We have four nurseries, three primary nurseries and a morning table. We are looking for 01. October 2026 or by appointment a suitable personality for our school Kappeli Primar.

Tasks

  • Co-design / Carrying the supplementary school care
  • Interprofessional cooperation
  • Parental work
  • Collaboration in the holiday camp (2-5 weeks)

Profile

  • Educational training HF/FH or teaching diploma
  • Experience leading team members and children’s groups
  • Positive human image and solution-oriented attitude
  • Good and clear communication skills
  • Resilience and flexibility (pleasure to further develop the care offer)
  • Humour

We offer

We offer a versatile, varied activity in an exciting, dynamic, multicultural environment, an open, motivated and committed team, competent support by the school management and the management of care, as well as salaries and social benefits according to the guidelines of the school and sports department of the city of Zurich.

About us

The Zurich-Letzi school district comprises Stadtkreis 9 with the districts Albisrieden, Altstetten and Grünau. In the 16 schools of our rapidly growing school circle, more than 5,000 primary school students are taught and supervised. More than 1,200 employees work for them in our schools in the areas of teaching, care, home service and technology as well as administration. They all work daily for the interests of the students and a smooth school day.
