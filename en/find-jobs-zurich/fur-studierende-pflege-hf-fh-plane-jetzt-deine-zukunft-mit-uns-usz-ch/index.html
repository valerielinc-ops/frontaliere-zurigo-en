Full description

USZ Campus

Are you currently studying to become a nurse HF / FH and want to find out what you could do after graduation? Let's discuss your options at the USZ for the time after graduation.

We will determine your location together based on your interests.

Have you already completed internships in acute somatics or gained relevant professional experience? Then you've come to the right place! But even if you have previously worked in geriatric and long-term care, in a rehabilitation clinic or Spitex, we look forward to an open exchange and will be happy to explore your opportunities at the USZ. Regardless of whether you have already had contact with the USZ, have it or are simply interested: We are available to all nursing students who are about to graduate - internally and externally - for a conversation to plan the next steps.

Let's find out together what we can offer you...

... with minimal effort for you: Simply upload your CV to our eRecruiting tool or send your CV by email so that we can get in touch with you easily. No dossiers from recruiters can be considered for this position. Thank you for your understanding. Our benefits

Daycare center on site

In our in-house daycare centers, your children will be lovingly cared for. Long opening hours without company holidays and subsidized childcare places relieve you in everyday life.

Health

We support your health. In addition to personal consultations and courses from our health management that promote your well-being, we offer you access to over 120 sports with a subsidized annual subscription to the ASVZ.

Flexibility

We promote a good work-life balance: almost half of our employees work part-time, and about 50% of the employees work part-time.

  • Tasks can be completed in the home office – if your everyday work allows it.

Family-friendliness

We help you to reconcile family and career. Mothers are entitled to at least 6 months of maternity leave (of which 16 weeks with full pay), fathers to at least 2 months of paternity leave (of which 2 weeks with full pay).

Training & Development

We accompany you on your professional path and support your development with individual career and further training opportunities.

Insurance

We cover 60% of your pension fund contributions and offer attractive special conditions for supplementary insurance.

Mobility

You benefit from a personal ZVV BonusPass at a special price or receive a fixed amount of money for a GA travelcard or Half-Fare travelcard PLUS.

Telephony

You and your family members benefit from special conditions for private mobile subscriptions.
