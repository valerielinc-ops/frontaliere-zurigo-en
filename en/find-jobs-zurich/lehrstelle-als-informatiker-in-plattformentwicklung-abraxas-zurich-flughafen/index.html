Full description

Want to know how servers, networks and the cloud really work? As a computer scientist: in platform development at Abraxas, you ensure that systems run stable and modern IT infrastructures are created. In your teaching with us, discover how exciting the world behind the scenes of IT is. Your role As a computer scientist: in the field of platform development, you are a specialist: in the development and operation of IT infrastructures. You install and configure server systems, networks and software and automate processes with scripts and tools. You work in the areas of planning, installation, operation and maintenance of computer systems and their applications. During the four apprenticeship years, you will take care of hardware and software – from individual devices to networked system landscapes. This includes detecting, analyzing and correcting disturbances. You also carry out updates and coordinate hardware repairs with external partners if necessary. Your profile

  • Very good logical-abstract thinking
  • Sense of theoretical processes and processes
  • Enjoyment of exact thinking and working methods
  • Interest in solving complex tasks
  • The joy of experimentation
  • Patience and joy in working with computer systems
  • Permanent preparedness
