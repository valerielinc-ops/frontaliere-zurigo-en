Full description

Spital Männedorf – Your very special place of work. Whether a small “thank you” or a morning “grüezi” – we focus on real appreciation and humanity. The proximity in our house makes everyday life uncomplicated and enables interprofessional cooperation at the highest level. In an environment where support goes without saying, your commitment is appreciated and encouraged.

The nature of your activity

  • Professional gastronomic service for patients and visitors
  • Provisional advice, ordering and service of meals taking into account medical guidelines
  • Readiness for shift, weekend and holiday services
  • Accompanying and supporting the additional insured patients during the hospital stay in the hotel sector with check-in and check-out to H-Quality
  • Presentation of patient rooms and infrastructure

The basis of your performance

  • Training as hotel specialist EFZ or restoration specialist EFZ and/or sound professional experience, preferably in the health sector
  • Experience in the operation of an electronic menu selection system is advantageous (e.g. Sanalogic)
  • High willingness to provide services and work independently
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing and solid IT user knowledge (MS-Office)

The reasons for your decision

We offer modern working conditions, promote internal further education and focus on professional exchange across all disciplines. The quiet location directly on the lake and the proximity to the vibrant city of Zurich round off your workplace. In addition, you can look forward to very special advantages.
