Full description

Join our inspired team at our innovative engineering company and become part of the future of living spaces. Benefit from our experience, skills and many employee benefits.

Your tasks

  • Independent preparation and processing of electrical installation plans for demanding construction projects
  • Planning and drawing implementation of high and low current installations
  • Preparation of execution, detail, installation and revision plans
  • Processing and coordination of 3D models as part of the BIM planning methodology
  • Coordination and coordination with internal project managers, specialist planners, architects and other project participants
  • Ensuring a high quality of planning as well as compliance with the applicable standards and project specifications
  • Support of project management in technical and planning issues

Your strengths

  • Completed training as an electrical planner EFZ or comparable electrical training
  • Several years of professional experience in electrical planning, ideally in a Swiss engineering or planning office
  • Very good user knowledge in NOVA and TinLine
  • Practical experience with BIM and model-based 3D planning
  • Demonstrable project experience in the planning and processing of large projects, for example hospitals, industrial plants, administrative buildings or complex commercial buildings
  • In-depth knowledge of Swiss standards and regulations, in particular NIN and SIA
  • Good communication skills and professional appearance
  • High sense of responsibility and reliability
