Full description

Every report to assignees goes through your hands: In the secretariat of cardiology, you ensure that cardiac medicine remains administratively in step. The Clinic of Cardiology provides annual care for around 10,000 outpatient, semi-inpatient and inpatient patients. In addition to ambulatory functional and ischaemic diagnostics, cardiology offers special consultation hours with a focus on rhythmology and heart failure. In addition to the chief physician, the team consists of three senior physicians, two cadre physicians and one assistant physician as well as seven medical-technical assistants and specialist nurses, one health specialist and three medical secretaries.
