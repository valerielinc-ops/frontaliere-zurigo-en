Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Correctional Services and Reintegration Medical Service Springer:in Medical Service (MPA, FaGe or qualified nurse) 40 - 100% Correctional Services and Reintegration (JuWe), as part of the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs of the Canton of Zurich, is responsible for the execution of sentences and measures as well as the implementation of the various types of detention. For our 1,400 employees from around 40 professions, the successful social reintegration of people who have committed crimes and the prevention of relapses are of particular importance. The Medical Service is one of eight main departments at JuWe and is the central organization responsible for the basic medical and somatic care of all incarcerated people in the institutions of the Canton of Zurich. With around 90 employees, it ensures medical care from the initial assessment through treatments to emergency care and supports the incarcerated people in their everyday lives with health issues. In order to strengthen our medical service, we are looking for flexible personalities as jumpers with a fixed workload and working days - for assignments in various prisons in the canton of Zurich.
