These listings concern Ticino. A G permit covers work in Switzerland while keeping a main residence abroad and returning at least weekly. The 20 km border zone is a treaty tax condition, not a general EU/EFTA G-permit requirement. In Ticino, issuance and renewal for adult EU/EFTA nationals cost CHF 75; other categories and procedures have different fees. Apply before starting work and check the expiry shown on the permit.
