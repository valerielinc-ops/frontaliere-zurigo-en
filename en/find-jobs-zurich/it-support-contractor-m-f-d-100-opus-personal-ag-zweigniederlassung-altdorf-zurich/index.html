Back to Overview IT Support Contractor (m/f/d) 100% Job opening Immediately or by agreement Employment level 100% Permanent Place of work/region ZH-Zurich For our customer we are looking for an immediately or by agreement IT Support Contractor (m/f/d)

Task area

In this position, you take on a central role in internal IT support and project support. They ensure smooth IT operations, assist employees with technical issues and contribute to the further development of IT systems.

Their tasks include

  • Support of first- and second-level support for employees
  • Management, setup and staging of workstations and administration of the MS365 / Azure Tenant
  • Responsibility for network infrastructure, including printers and peripherals
  • Support of the product owner / IT manager in various IT projects
  • Participation in the further development of ERP, CRM and eCommerce solutions based on Odoo
  • Conducting employee training following rollouts of new functions
  • Preparation and maintenance of user documentation
  • Optimization of the IT infrastructure and participation in process improvements

Your profile

  • Completed training in IT / Computer Science
  • Strong interest in technology and digital solutions
  • Independent, structured and precise working methods
  • Solution-oriented approach and proactive thinking
  • Safe and friendly approach to stakeholders
