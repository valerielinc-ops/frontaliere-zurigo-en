Full description

The Intermediate Care Unit (IMC) offers an exciting, acute medical activity at the interface between bed ward and intensive care with a focus on complex internalistic diseases and peri-operative care. To strengthen our team, we are looking for motivated assistant doctors. The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees are committed to the welfare of our patients every day.

Tasks

  • Medical care of patients with increased monitoring requirements at the intermediate care unit (IMC)
  • Early detection and assessment of clinical changes and initiation and implementation of appropriate diagnostic and therapeutic measures
  • Coordination of interdisciplinary treatment in close cooperation with the participating disciplines and nursing professionals
  • Active participation in emergency care and the stabilisation of acutely ill patients
  • Commitment to quality, safe and patient-centered care and the continuous development of treatment quality
  • Participation in further training and further training as well as participation in an interprof-essional and dynamic working environment

Requirements

  • Completed medical studies with an Eidgenössische or recognised foreign diploma
  • Pre-experience in a hospital of training category A or B for internal medicine, intensive care medicine/IMC and/or anesthesia
  • Knowledge of German (oral and written) Minimum C1 according to EU standards, as well as recognition of your training and language by MEBEKO https://www.bag.admin.ch/en/applicants-submit-for-auslaendische-abschluesse-der-medizinal-und-psychologieberufe
  • Ability to set priorities even in challenging situations
  • Respectful handling of patients, patients and employees is a matter of course for you

We offer

  • Training in Internal Medicine Category A
  • A varied activity in a universitar-innovative environment
  • In addition to participation in external courses and congresses (SGAIM), we offer comprehensive further training according to the further education program of the SIWF
  • A dedicated and motivated team supports you in all matters
  • Attractive employment conditions and good social benefits
