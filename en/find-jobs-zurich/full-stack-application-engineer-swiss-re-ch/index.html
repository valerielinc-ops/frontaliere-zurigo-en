Full description

Are you passionate about creating innovative software solutions that drive business transformation? Join our dynamic team as an Full Stack Engineer where you'll have the opportunity to work with cutting-edge technologies and contribute to impactful projects that make a difference across our organization! About the Role As a Full Stack Engineer, you'll be at the forefront of developing and maintaining high-quality applications that support business functions. You'll collaborate with cross-functional teams to design, implement, and optimize solutions that meet evolving business needs while adhering to best practices in software development. Key Responsibilities

  • Design and develop robust applications using Angular, .NET, and other modern technologies
  • Collaborate with business analysts, product owners, and other stakeholders to understand requirements and translate them into technical solutions
  • Implement responsive and user-friendly interfaces that enhance user experience
  • Troubleshoot and resolve complex technical issues to ensure optimal application performance
  • Participate in code reviews and provide constructive feedback to maintain code quality
  • Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives for development processes and methodologies
  • Stay current with emerging technologies and industry trends to propose innovative solutions
  • Document code, processes, and technical specifications for knowledge sharing

About the Team The Application Development Services team is responsible for 100+ applications serving multiple domains of the company - from Reinsurance to Finance or HR using agile methodologies. Applications are mostly based on Microsoft tech stack deployed to Microsoft Azure cloud platform. Our team consists of 30+ professionals based in Bratislava, Zurich, Munich and India.

About You

You're a problem-solver at heart with a passion for creating elegant, efficient code. You thrive in collaborative environments and are eager to learn and adapt to new technologies. Your attention to detail and commitment to quality make you an ideal candidate to help us build and maintain critical applications that power our business.

We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements

  • Minimum 5-year experience as a fullstack developer
  • Strong experience with Angular framework and front-end development
  • Proficiency in .NET or equivalent backend development experience
  • Experience working with databases such as MSSQL or PostgreSQL
  • Understanding of software development lifecycle and Scrum methodologies
  • Excellent troubleshooting and problem-solving skills

These are additional nice to haves

  • Experience with cloud platforms (Azure, AWS, etc.)
  • Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and DevOps practices
  • Familiarity with AI solutions including development of custom agents and implementation of AI orchestration frameworks
  • Knowledge of SharePoint development and web parts
  • Previous experience in a similar role within a corporate environment
