Introduction For a renowned company in the automotive industry, we are looking for a service consultant (m/f/d) for a temporary full-time position. In this role, you are the link between the customer and the workshop and ensure that the service processes run smoothly. Tasks

Profile

  • Technical understanding and interest in automotive engineering
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

Benefits
