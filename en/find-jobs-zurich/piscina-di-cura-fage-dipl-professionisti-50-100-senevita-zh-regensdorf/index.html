Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,300 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland.

For Senevita Stockenhof, we are looking for mindful and team-oriented care professionals on an hourly basis. Nursing aid, specialist health, Dipl. Nursing professional Your tasks

  • Professional, individual care and care according to established care guidelines
  • Respectful and loving accompaniment of the inhabitants, focusing on their dignity and self-determination
  • Participation in reports, team meetings and training
  • Ensure full, professional and differentiated care
  • Care documentation in the Care Coach
  • Assuming daily or shift responsibility

You bring that with you

  • Appropriate training or SRC recognition as well as professional experience, beneficial in long-term care
  • You are committed and motivated in the nursing profession and act independently and competently
  • An empathetic accompaniment of the residents is just as obvious to you as an effective work organization and an appreciative cooperation.
  • Ideally, you already have knowledge of RAI/RUG and CareCoach
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German

We offer you

  • Extensive further education offer of the Senevita Academy can be visited free of charge
  • Unique culture and various generous employee events
  • Discounted food and lunch in the in-house gastronomy
  • Various perks (fuels, supplementary insurance, fitness, pharmacy, etc.)
  • After the trial period, Senevita gives you the half-tax subscription
  • Good social benefits and at least. 5 weeks holiday (from 50 years 6 or from 60 years 7 weeks)

Interested? Does the place speak to you? An enthusiastic and open team as well as warm residents are looking forward to you! Please send us your online application.
