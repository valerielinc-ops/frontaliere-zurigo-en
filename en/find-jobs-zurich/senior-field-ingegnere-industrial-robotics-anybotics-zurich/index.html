Application process

  • ANYbotics transforms industrial environments in the energy, process, and utility sectors by introducing robotics to applications previously beyond reach.
  • Our mobile robot, ANYmal, uses legs for extreme mobility in complex environments, camera- and LIDAR-based sensing for full autonomy, and AI for high-quality inspection results.
  • With software developed almost entirely in-house, ANYmal performs autonomous inspection missions for large asset operators and industrial service providers pioneering robotics technology for inspection and maintenance. Your Contributions
  • Lead Deployment Projects: Lead complex, multi-site customer projects from onboarding through to operational autonomy.

Company and context

  • Drive user adoption by navigating organizational dynamics, identifying resistance, and championing the value of robotics within their operational culture.
  • Field Operations Leadership: Coordinate the operational work of field technicians and internal specialists.
  • Set priorities, allocate resources, and resolve on-site hurdles to maintain project momentum and quality.
  • Trusted Advisory: Consult with global industrial clients to integrate robotics into their broader business workflows.
  • Act as the bridge between their operational challenges and our technical teams, ensuring our roadmap aligns with their needs.
  • Value Realization & Training: Lead training and enablement sessions for diverse audiences, from on-the-ground operators to plant management, tailoring your approach to maximize user confidence and system utilization.
  • Strategic Feedback Loop: Translate field insights and recurring operational themes into actionable requirements for our Product and R&D teams, ensuring the voice of the customer drives our innovation.
  • Change Management: Demonstrated ability to manage stakeholder relationships, navigate organizational dynamics, and drive user adoption in industrial environments.
  • Operational Leadership: Strong capability to coordinate teams, manage on-site priorities, and maintain project continuity in high-pressure, international settings.
  • Travel: Enthusiastic about frequent international travel (up to 60%) and comfortable working directly in customer environments.
