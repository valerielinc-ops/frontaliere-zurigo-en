Full description

Education Directorate of Uster Vocational School Substitute for the subject of general education (ABU) 6 lessons (limited assignment) 23% From March 8th to May 30th, 2027, every Friday, we are looking for a committed and proactive teacher for the subject of general education (ABU). You accompany and support our learners in basic vocational training and in their vocational baccalaureate. With us you have the chance to develop new training concepts and creatively implement your ideas in an innovative and multifunctional learning and working environment. Take a look at our website and get an impression. Your tasks

  • You impart knowledge in the subject of ABU to our learners and accompany them on their path to basic vocational training.
  • In our modern learning environment you will develop and implement creative and future-oriented training concepts.
  • You promote the social development of our learners and support them in their professional and personal development.
  • With your enthusiasm for the subject, you inspire learners and motivate them to achieve their full potential. Your profile
  • You are still in the middle of your ABU training or have completed training as a general education teacher.
  • You enjoy exciting young people and inspiring them with new ideas.
  • You are creative and enjoy developing and applying innovative teaching methods and have a high level of technological affinity in order to effectively integrate modern tools and digital media into teaching.
  • Your social skills help you to promote the personal development of the learners and to accompany them individually.
  • You are characterized by initiative, team spirit and a sense of responsibility. Our offer
  • A state-of-the-art infrastructure with a variety of learning and working opportunities.
  • A motivated, collegial and creative team that works together to further develop the educational landscape.
  • The opportunity to contribute your ideas in a dynamic environment and to actively shape the future of vocational training. Your application We look forward to getting to know you. If you have any questions, Debora Matthey ( personal@bzu.ch ), Head of Human Resources, will be happy to help you.
