Rental Management Zurich | Pensum: 100%

Are you open-minded, ambitious and value a future-oriented way of working on the pulse of the real estate industry? In rental management, you are looking for the perfect match and bring residential and commercial properties together with the right tenants. Are you ready to tackle the challenges of today and tomorrow and make a difference in the real estate world? Then you are exactly right with Livit. We look forward to your personal contribution.

Your tasks

  • You are in direct contact with tenants and prospective tenants
  • You agree and carry out visits
  • You are handling the digital re-letting process
  • You proactively advise tenants regarding suitable rental offers
  • You select suitable tenants in cooperation with the management
  • You analyze the vacancy reasons and carry out suitable marketing measures
  • You create and edit object, property photos and 360° video tours
  • You create housing listings and publish them

Your profile

  • You have completed an apprenticeship as a merchant:woman, in customer dialogue or in sales with commercial training
  • You bring professional experience in direct customer contact, ideally in the real estate sector,
  • You have a driving license Kat. B and own vehicle
  • Your mother tongue is German and you can ideally communicate verbally and in writing in other languages
  • You are a sales talent with strong persuasion and a strong service orientation
  • You have a professional and competent appearance combined with winning manners
  • You work independently, accurately, proactively and think and act entrepreneurially

  • At least 5 weeks holiday and purchase of up to 10 additional days holiday (Ferienflex)
  • Above-average pension benefits and premiums for medical day allowance and accident insurance are 100% covered by Livit
