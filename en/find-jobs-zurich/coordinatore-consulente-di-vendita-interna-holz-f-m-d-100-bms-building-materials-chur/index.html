BMS Building Materials Suisse is a wholesaler of building materials for shell construction and interior construction. To our group of companies Included are the renowned specialists Construction Needs, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced experts advise, support and inspire customers from the construction industry with great commitment as well as Private customers at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. Close to everyday construction, we are the local and reliable partner for any building project. Quick, easy and competent.

For our back office in Chur / Wil we are looking for a service-oriented personality as soon as possible or by agreement.
