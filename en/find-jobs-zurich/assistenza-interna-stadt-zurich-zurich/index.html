Full description

In August 2022, Freilager School opened its brand new school building. We started the day school operation. In one of our field stations with kindergarten children we are looking for a care assistant at 17. August or by arrangement.

They work during school hours on Wednesday noon from around 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. and are always free during school holidays.

Tasks

  • Various tasks as part of the children's lunch meal
  • Preparation of meals
  • Portioning and scooping food
  • Cleaning and cleaning work
  • Supporting the educational team in the care of children

Profile

  • They are a joyful, warm, friendly, humorous and reliable personality with enthusiasm, motivation and enthusiasm.
  • They like to take responsibility and face new or unexpected situations with prudence, flexibility and a good dose of humor.
  • They enjoy working with children and feel comfortable in a lively environment.
  • Even at high noise levels and in hectic situations, you keep an overview, work concentrated and maintain your friendliness as well as your positive charisma.
  • An appreciative and respectful treatment of children, parents and employees is a matter of course for you.

We offer

  • Versatile and varied work in an exciting, dynamic and multicultural environment
  • Working in an open, motivated and committed team
  • Attractive employment conditions
  • Salary and social benefits according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich

About us

The Zurich-Letzi school district comprises Stadtkreis 9 with the districts Albisrieden, Altstetten and Grünau. In the 16 schools of our rapidly growing school circle, more than 5,000 primary school students are taught and supervised. More than 1,200 employees work for them in our schools in the areas of teaching, care, home service and technology as well as administration. They all work daily for the interests of the students and a smooth school day.
