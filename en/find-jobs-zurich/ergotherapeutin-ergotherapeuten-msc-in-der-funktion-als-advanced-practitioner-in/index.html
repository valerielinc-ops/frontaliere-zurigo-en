Full description

  • The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich treats and cares for mentally ill people of all ages. The more than 2,500 employees from 55 different professional profiles work in interdisciplinary teams to contribute to the holistic care of patients, from assessment to therapy to aftercare. Our work is based on mutual trust and personal responsibility and together we promote an innovative corporate culture. As a university clinic, we are successfully involved in national and international research projects. In the Directorate of Nursing, Therapies and Social Work, in Zurich, we are looking for an occupational therapist MSc in the function of Advanced Practitioner in Occupational Therapy 100% for the occupational therapy department immediately or by appointment. Your tasks
  • Individual and group occupational therapy for patients in adult and geriatric psychiatry
  • Applying occupational therapy assessments
  • Technically sound and concise documentation in the clinical information system
  • Collaboration in interprofessional treatment teams and the occupational therapy specialist team
  • Helping to shape the professional development of contemporary occupational therapy concepts and offers
  • Ensuring the sustainable implementation of evidence-based interventions
  • Developing and implementing quality assurance measures
  • Promoting competent patient treatment and documentation by occupational therapy employees
  • Establishing vessels and structures for competence development and knowledge transfer
  • Participation in departmental and PUK-internal projects if necessary
  • Completed training in occupational therapy FH, SRK recognition for foreign professional qualifications
  • Master of Science in occupational therapy or in an occupational therapy related science
  • Several years of occupational therapy professional experience in the field of psychiatry
  • Quick comprehension as well as networked and conceptual thinking
  • Structured, process-oriented approach as well as experience in didactics and administrative matters
  • Competence in promoting and supporting the theory-practice transfer
  • Innovative thinking and acting
  • Independent, efficient and service-oriented way of working with a high level of personal responsibility
  • High level of commitment, flexibility as well as the ability to work in a team and resilience
  • Innovative position in the field of university psychiatry
  • Responsible and varied tasks
  • Motivated team, collegial interprofessional collaboration
  • Open and appreciative working atmosphere
  • Flexible working hours taking into account the requirements of the position
  • Conditions of employment in accordance with the cantonal personnel law
  • Attractive social benefits (takeover of 60% of the pension fund contributions)
  • Discounted shopping opportunities and sports activities, participation in the ZVV-BonusPass / general subscription, staff restaurant with subsidized prices, in-house crèche and much more. Please apply exclusively via www.pukzh.ch/karriere-bildung, where you will also receive further information about our clinic. We do not accept applications from recruiters for this position.
