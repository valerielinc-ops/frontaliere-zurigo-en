Foundation Diakoniewerk Neumünster Specialist / Specialist Corporate Communication Middle Office / — Spital Zollikerberg, Zollikerberg (ZH).

Area: administration.

Welcome to your new place of work.

Our Diakoniewerk Neumünster Foundation – Swiss Nursing School has been committed to people and their health for more than 165 years. Humanity, care and respect are our top priority. We accompany people holistically, from birth to the end of life and take time for their physical, psychological, social, cultural and spiritual needs.

As a future-oriented health organization, we develop innovative solutions and stand for excellent basic care. Under the umbrella brand Gesundheitwelt Zollikerberg, we unite the Zollikerberg Hospital, the Hottingen Old Age Centre, the Neumünster Park Residence, the Neumünster Institute and the Neumünster Park Gastronomy. We are one of the leading health organizations and an attractive employer in Zurich and beyond. An overview of our companies can be found directly here via the following link: Gesundheitwelt Zollikerberg .

We offer you an exciting job in an 80-100% amount as

Specialist / Specialist Corporate Communication

Their tasks

  • First contact person for the establishments and areas assigned to you
  • Development and implementation of the corresponding communication planning
  • Self-employment and responsibility for managing communication projects
  • Advising professionals on communication requests

About the Diakoniewerk Neumünster Foundation

Our Diakoniewerk Neumünster Foundation – Swiss nursing school is there for people and their health – for 165 years. Humanity and care are our top priority. We accompany people in every situation from birth to the end of life. We devote ourselves to them holistically, taking time and respecting their physical, psychological, social, cultural and spiritual needs.

We develop future-oriented solutions and train specialist staff. This makes us a leading health organization and a sought-after employer in Zurich and beyond. The foundation includes the Zollikerberg Hospital, the Hottingen Old Age Centre, the Neumünster Park Residence, the Institut Neumünster and the Zollikerberg Gastronomy Centre.
