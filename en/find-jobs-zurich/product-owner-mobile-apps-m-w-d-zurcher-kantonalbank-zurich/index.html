Full description

80% - 100% | Product and channel management | Zurich | Experienced professionals

Our Client Channels team is responsible for further developing the physical and digital touchpoints of Zürcher Kantonalbank. Several million interactions take place on these touchpoints every day. Together with our mobile teams, we continuously develop ZKB Mobile Banking and create the technical and organizational foundation for a secure, stable and compelling digital customer experience.

Our Mobile Banking system team is responsible for the shared app foundation and creates the conditions for multiple Feature Teams to develop and release in parallel while still being coordinated and working on a single codebase. In the role of Product Owner, you will play a central role for ZKB Mobile Banking. You combine product ownership with business-area responsibility and ensure that systems, quality, governance and delivery work together across team boundaries.

Tasks

  • Manage and prioritize the system teams' backlog, focusing on the app foundation, cross-system requirements, enablers and technical development
  • Close collaboration with Business Engineers, developers and architecture to integrate developments from multiple teams into a consistent, stable and scalable Mobile Banking app that meets the growing requirements of Mobile Banking
  • Take responsibility for release and deployment management of the ZKB Mobile Banking App together with Testing and the Feature Teams, and ensure pragmatic, high-quality implementation
  • Assume business-area responsibility for the relevant Mobile Banking systems and ensure compliance with security, risk and governance requirements
  • Assess test coverage, risks and release readiness before releases, and provide functional sign-offs
  • Orchestrate collaboration with internal stakeholders and bring together differing interests and dependencies to reach clear decisions
  • Ensure the app's stability, security and quality, including in critical situations and for the app as a whole rather than by optimizing an individual team

Profile

  • Completed higher education in business, computer science or a comparable field
  • At least 5 years of experience as a Product Owner, Business Analyst or in a similar role in a digital environment involving mobile apps, digital banking or complex platform/system products is a major plus
  • Solid experience in agile product development using methods such as Scrum, ideally complemented by SAFe
  • Good technical understanding of architecture, interfaces, app foundations, release processes and software delivery
  • Strong communication skills with excellent decision-making ability and persuasiveness
  • Analytical, structured and reliable way of working with a strong sense of responsibility
  • An end-to-end perspective across team boundaries and the ability to structure complex dependencies
  • Very good German and English skills

Offer

  • Open culture of collaboration, in which mutual support and exchange are a matter of course
  • We support you in balancing work and private life - whether through part-time work, hybrid working, unpaid leave or flexible working hours
  • Your career is important to us as your employer, which is why we foster and accompany you on your journey and support you with a wide range of offerings
  • Our modern office in Zurich's vibrant Kreis 5 offers you the ideal environment to work creatively and productively
  • We offer you fair and transparent compensation based on clearly defined salary bands
  • Comprehensive overall package, which, in addition to the attractive base salary and variable compensation, offers many benefits and discounts – from A for pension provision to Z for the ZVV-Bonuspass
