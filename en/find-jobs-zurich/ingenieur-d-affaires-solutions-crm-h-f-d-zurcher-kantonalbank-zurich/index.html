The Portfolio & Solution Delivery Sales Platform division is responsible for portfolio management as well as the implementation and operation of the sales applications of the Zürcher Kantonalbank. You support the interaction between sales staff and customers through digital channels and applications. You simplify the everyday life of our sales staff in customer service, sales and customer service, always focusing on users and customers. In the DevOps team “Shenlong” you accompany the further development of a purchased CRM software as a SaaS solution. Are you engaged, versatile and enjoy working in a digital environment where you can tackle complex challenges with creativity and structure?

Then we look forward to getting to know you and taking our sales applications to the next level together with you.

Your tasks

  • Identifying vulnerabilities and potential for improvement in existing processes
  • Developing more efficient and sustainable solutions and implementing them in practice
  • Translate business requirements into clear, technical specifications and elaborate features and stories together with product owner and agile team
  • Creating feature detail concepts and specifications as well as necessary documentation involving relevant stakeholders
  • Accompanying features and stories during implementation and standing up for the intended implementation
  • Independent implementation of adjustments in the configuration in compliance with the specified quality standards
  • Testing releases from a professional point of view in the context of agile team responsibility and assuming 3rd-level support tasks and handling incidents in the DevOps team
  • Accompanying change processes, promoting acceptance and supporting training
  • Higher or higher education degree, in computer science, business informatics or a comparable specialisation
  • At least 5 years of experience as a business engineer, ideally in the CRM environment
  • Strong technical understanding and very good analytical and conceptual skills with high IT affinity
  • Experience in agile projects, ideally with certification in Business Engineering (e.g. IREB)
  • Knowledge of BSI CRM and artificial intelligence are a plus, but not a requirement
  • Enjoy working in interdisciplinary teams and achieving goals together
  • Independent working methods as well as the ability to take on and drive forward tasks on their own initiative and reliably
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • English skills are a plus
  • Motivated DevOps team with a strong team spirit that looks forward to you
  • Exciting and diverse range of tasks with many interfaces in sales and IT
  • Various opportunities to develop personally and professionally
  • Modern workplace in district 5 with very good transport connections (hard bridge station)
  • You can flexibly organize your work in consultation with your team (mobile work, flexible working hours)
  • We offer a collaborative and appreciative cooperation at eye level with the opportunity to actively shape
  • Comprehensive package, which in addition to the attractive basic salary and a variable remuneration offers many benefits and perks – from A such as retirement provision to Z such as ZVV bonus pass
