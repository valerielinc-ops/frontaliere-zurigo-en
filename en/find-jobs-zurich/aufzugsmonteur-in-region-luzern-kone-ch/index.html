At the earliest possible time we are looking for you as an elevator fitter for the Lucerne region.

Your mission

After your training, you install our systems, check them and then put them into operation.

Your concrete contribution

  • Installation and modernisation of lifts
  • Communication with the chief fitter and customers on the construction site
  • Preparation of final acceptance of installed operational facilities
  • Accompanying final acceptance
  • Reporting

Your added value

  • Completed training in the electrical or mechanical sector (electro-installer, car mechanic, polymechanic)
  • Craftsmanship and pleasure in physical work
  • German mother tongue or good level (min. B2)
  • Independent, motivated and reliable personality
  • High initiative and flexibility
  • Valid ticket category B and BE
  • Correct extract from criminal records
  • Residence in Switzerland (Swiss or foreign nationality with valid residence permit)
  • Your place of residence is at most 30 minutes from the city center of Lucerne. This enables a balanced work-life balance and facilitates cooperation in the team.

Our offer for your future

  • Working in a globally networked company with international cooperation
  • Innovative, professional and collegial working environment – with a focus on the well-being of employees
  • Further education opportunities and support for your professional development
  • Market-friendly compensation, a company car with 100% private use without monthly contributions, food allowance Fr. 25- / working day (without receipt), at least 25 holiday days, free life insurance as well as other advantages such as a high-performance BVG provision and participation in exciting company events.
  • Fair Pay certified: equal wages for women and men – transparent and audited
  • Commitment to sustainability: target CO2 neutrality by 2030
