16.10.2026 Zurich St. Gallen

Become part of our team!

The Legal & Compliance division of Raiffeisen Switzerland handles legal issues of all kinds and ensures that the conduct and actions of the Raiffeisen Group comply with internal and external regulations. In order to strengthen the Legal Banking Services team with a place of work at Zurich Airport (The Circle) or St. Gallen as well as the possibility to work partly in the home office, we are looking for a committed and experienced lawyer (m/f/d) with pleasure in the daily consulting work.

What do you expect?

  • Legal advice to Raiffeisen banks throughout Switzerland and various organizational units of Raiffeisen Switzerland and their group companies
  • The focus is on the legal areas of general contract law, company law, property law, employment law, inheritance law, family law and SchKG
  • Participation in internal/external projects and working groups
  • Preparation of opinions and drafting of opinions
  • Preparation, negotiation and examination of contracts
  • Coordination of judicial and administrative proceedings with external lawyers
  • Consultancy within the Raiffeisen Group in selected civil law areas

What do you bring?

  • Swiss attorney’s patent
  • Several years of professional activity as a company lawyer or as a lawyer in a law firm
  • Practical experience in legal advice in the above-mentioned areas of law
  • Entrepreneurial, exact working methods oriented to the needs of Raiffeisen banks/front units
  • Team-oriented, resilient and communication-strong personality
  • High degree of independence, flexibility and rapid comprehension
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of at least one other national language
