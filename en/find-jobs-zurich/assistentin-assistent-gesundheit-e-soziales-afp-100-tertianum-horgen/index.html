Full description

The exclusive Tertianum Residence Horgen with 59 modern apartments and 22 spacious single care rooms as well as an acute geriatric ward with 20 beds offers guests a suitable home in the upper segment. Guests are offered genuine hospitality to maintain an individual lifestyle. In the public restaurant “elegante”, market-fresh and balanced dishes are also served for external guests.

We are looking for someone to join our acute geriatrics team at the Tertianum Residenz Horgen immediately or by appointment

Assistant Health and Social Affairs EBA 60-80%

Your tasks

  • Care and support of assigned patients
  • Support with personal care, exercise and other everyday activities. Organizing and offering our patients everyday activities with creative activities, walks, etc.
  • Taking on tasks as a living reference person according to instructions
  • Participate in nursing reports and forward important observations
  • Assistance and co-organization with logistical and housekeeping activities

Your profile

  • Completed training as an assistant in health and social affairs EBA
  • Professional experience in acute care an advantage
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoy a variety of challenges
  • Knowledge of spoken and written German
