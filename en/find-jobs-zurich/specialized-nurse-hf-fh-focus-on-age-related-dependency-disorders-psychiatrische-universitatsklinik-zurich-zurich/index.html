Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich treats and cares for mentally ill people of all ages. The more than 2,500 employees from 55 different professional profiles work in interdisciplinary teams to contribute to the holistic care of patients, from assessment to therapy to aftercare. Our work is based on mutual trust and personal responsibility and together we promote an innovative corporate culture. As a university clinic, we are successfully involved in national and international research projects. The geriatric psychiatric acute ward G3 with a focus on addictions in old age fulfills its mission within the framework of basic psychiatric care. The therapeutic offer includes a differentiated assessment and treatment, support and therapy tailored to the respective patient, as well as planning discharge management under psychological, biological and social aspects.
