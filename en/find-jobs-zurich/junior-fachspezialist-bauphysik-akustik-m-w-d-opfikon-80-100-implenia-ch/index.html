Full description

Buildings work well when energy, comfort and acoustics work together. Create solutions with us that you may not always see or hear, but whose effect is noticeable.

Tasks

Which tasks await you as a Junior Specialist Building Physics & Acoustics (m/f/d), Opfikon, 80-100%?

  • You work on a wide range of projects in the fields of building physics, building acoustics, room acoustics and noise protection.
  • You support project management in calculations, simulations, measurements and in the creation of concepts, proofs, reports and reports.
  • Together with us, you will assess components and constructions with regard to heat, humidity and sound insulation and develop practical solutions.
  • Construction site inspections, measurements and quality checks are also part of your task.
  • You work together with different disciplines and other project participants and thus get a broad insight into different construction projects.
  • With growing experience, you increasingly take on responsibility as well as your own tasks and smaller projects.

Requirements

What requirements do you bring as a junior specialist building physics & acoustics (m/f/d), Opfikon, 80-100%?

  • You have a degree or in-depth technical training in architecture, civil engineering, building technology, energy and environmental engineering, building physics, acoustics or a comparable field of expertise.
  • Initial experience in building physics or acoustics is an advantage, but not a requirement.
  • Also with a different technical background, you are welcome if you are interested in building physics, energy, sound and buildings.
  • You bring technical understanding, curiosity and willingness to learn and enjoy working your way into different questions.
  • You enjoy working with people from different disciplines and can communicate technical contexts in an understandable way.
  • Even with several projects at the same time, you work in a structured and reliable manner and communicate bottlenecks at an early stage.
  • You communicate in German.
