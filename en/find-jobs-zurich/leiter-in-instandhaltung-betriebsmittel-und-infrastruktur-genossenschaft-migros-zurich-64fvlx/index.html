Full description

Are you looking for independent work in a lively, technically demanding working environment and in a well-established team? Then this position in our Herdern production and logistics centre in the city of Zurich is just right for you.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Zurich, Pfingstweidstrasse 101, 8005 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Leadership, promotion and further development of the two teams (maintenance equipment and infrastructure) consisting of 27 employees and 4 direct reports
  • Overall management and further development of maintenance with focus on organization and processes
  • Further development and implementation of the IHA strategy
  • Responsibility for plant availability
  • Establishing budget, resources and management of costs
  • Identification and advancement of process optimization
  • Succession planning or perspective for taking over the overall management of the technical services

Requirements

  • At least 5-10 years of professional experience: proven leadership experience in the required area
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): in the technical field, preferably engineering
  • Service-oriented and entrepreneurial personality
  • Independent, performance-ready and forward-looking working methods as well as a high degree of personal responsibility
  • Leadership and project management experience in a complex technical environment
  • Experience in the field of automated logistics and production facilities as well as in the life cycle management of technical facilities, warehouse management and strategic maintenance
  • Experience with Lean Management and Operational Exellence
  • Experience in construction project management
  • in German (negotiation certain)

Contatto

Application and contact Bettina Erne Recruitment partners

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Expert Discussion: In a specialist discussion, specific knowledge relevant for the execution of the job is requested by the manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, the team. If possible, there is time afterwards to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week

Grado di occupazione: 80-100 %
