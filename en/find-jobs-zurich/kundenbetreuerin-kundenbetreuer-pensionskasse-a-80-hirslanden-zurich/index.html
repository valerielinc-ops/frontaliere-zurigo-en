Place of work: Hirslanden Corporate Office | Home Office Occupation by appointment Type of employment: Permanent Reference number: 71633 Create with us the professional provision of tomorrow. Hirslanden Pension Fund is an integral part of the Hirslanden Group and serves around 10,000 insured persons. With a balance sheet total of just under CHF 1.8 billion, it is one of the medium-sized, corporate pension funds in Switzerland. Would you like to immerse yourself in the exciting world of the company’s own pension fund or contribute and further develop your existing expertise in the field of occupational pension provision? Then you are exactly right with us. ## YOUR TASKS

  • Processing of mutations (entry and exit, purchases, advance emoluments, divorces, etc.) and benefits cases (age, disability and survivor benefits)
  • Contact point for insured persons in the field of occupational pensions and in other fields of social security
  • Cooperation with the personnel departments of clinics, insured persons and authorities
  • Ongoing process optimization in day-to-day business
  • Project support for the integration of clinics into the pension fund
  • Cooperation in all areas of pension fund administration ##
  • Your profile
  • Completed basic commercial training, ideally supplemented by further training in the field of social insurance or occupational pensions
  • Professional experience in occupational pension schemes (BVG) or in the social insurance sector is advantageous
  • Independent, structured and precise working methods as well as a high sense of responsibility
  • Pronounced service orientation and pleasure in contact with insured persons as well as internal and external stakeholders
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing and good knowledge of French Are you ready to take off in an innovative environment? With us you can fully develop your strengths, your know-how and your skills. On your first working day, we warmly welcome you and make sure that you feel comfortable with us from the beginning.   • More than 5 weeks of vacation as part of our Leisure Plus program – for the perfect work-life balance And many more exciting benefits can be found here!
