Full description

Your area of responsibility

  • Design and further development of devices, equipment and production aids for High Power Charging (HPC) production
  • Direct exchange with steakholders during the entire development process to ensure optimal conditions for the series production of our products
  • Development and evaluation of technical solution concepts, taking into account function, quality, ergonomics and costs
  • Prototyping, testing and validation of new fixtures and devices
  • Creation and maintenance of technical drawings, parts lists and documentation in CATIA/PDM
  • Collaboration in the development of the next generation of production lines: digital, modular, smart
  • Technical support and consulting of the international HPC production sites
  • Collaboration in strategic/tactical development and implementation of digitization and automation programs

Your profile

  • Completed studies (FH/HF) in the field of engineering, process technology, systems engineering or comparable technical training
  • Experience with system integration, digitization and automation required, experience with AI is an advantage
  • Several years of experience in the design and development of apparatus and devices in an industrial environment, experience in the electrotechnical field is an advantage
  • Very good CAD skills (ideally CATIA) as well as confident handling of MS Office
  • Analytical, structured and independent way of working with a strong understanding of technical and physical relationships
  • Communicative personality with manual dexterity, a high level of initiative and innovative spirit
  • Very good knowledge of German and English as well as high social skills in an international environment
  • Willingness to travel internationally

Why HUBER+SUHNER?

  • Participation in international projects and global cooperation
  • Opportunity to help shape the digital and automated production of the future
  • Attractive employment conditions and development opportunities
  • Collegial and modern work culture with a focus on quality and innovation
