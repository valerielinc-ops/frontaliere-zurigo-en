Philosophical Seminary

Job opening 1 August 2028

At the University of Zurich is on the 1. August 2028 to occupy a professorship in Philosophy of Spirit and Language. The future job holder should have an excellent, internationally visible and clearly anchored research profile with proven expertise in both the philosophy of the mind and the philosophy of language and should have relevant teaching experience. Substantial interdisciplinary expertise is also required in at least one of the empirical research fields relevant for the NCCR "Evolving Language", for example in evolutionary linguistic research, biological anthropology, cognitive ethology, etc. Animal communication, developmental psychology, cognitive psychology, cognitive neuroscience, computational linguistics or generative AI. In teaching, theoretical philosophy should be covered broadly, although it is also desirable that certain parts of the history of philosophy can be offered. In addition to innovative research impulses for the subject, cooperation in research and teaching is expected with other professorships at the Philosophical Seminar (especially with its normativity focus), at the Faculty of Philosophy, at other faculties of the UZH, as well as with national and international institutions (especially with the NCCR «Evolving Language»). Prerequisites are a doctorate in philosophy completed at the time of application as well as other excellent scientific publications that show a further focus. Depending on the qualifications of the person to be appointed, the professorship is filled as an ordinariate or extraordinariate. The administrative language of the University of Zurich is German, which is why the acquisition of sufficient knowledge of German within the first three years of employment is expected. The Philosophical Seminar, the University of Zurich and the university location of Zurich offer a stimulating research and teaching environment with a wide range of opportunities for professional exchange and cooperation. The University of Zurich strives for equality and an increase in diversity in research and teaching and explicitly requests corresponding applications. Applications must be submitted online by 23 November 2026. Access and information on online application can be found on the website of the Faculty of Philosophy.
