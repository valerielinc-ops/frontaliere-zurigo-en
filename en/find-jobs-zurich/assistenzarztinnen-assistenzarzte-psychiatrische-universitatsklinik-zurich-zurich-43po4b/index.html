Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility.

For our clinic for child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy, we are constantly looking for committed researchers to strengthen our teams.

Assistant doctors / assistant doctors

50% - 100%

Their tasks

  • Diagnostic investigations and psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment of children, adolescents and their families
  • Assumption of medical responsibility in outpatient, day-care or inpatient setting – depending on the area of application
  • Working in an interprofessional treatment team with psychologists, nurses and other specialists
  • Participation in emergency medical services
  • Participation in the preparation of reports, opinions and, depending on the area of operation, expert opinions
  • Participation in internal training, case meetings and supervision
  • Completed medical degree (for foreign diplomas with recognition by MEBEKO including language entry German)
  • Interest in child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy
  • Clinical experience in child and adolescent psychiatry, psychiatry or pediatrics desired
  • Team-oriented, responsible and independent working methods
  • Good communication skills, analytical thinking, expressive ability and resilience
  • Knowledge that advances: Extensive initial, further and further training offers
  • Clinic, research and teaching united: working in a university environment with international networking
  • Together for people: Interdisciplinary cooperation for holistic psychiatric care
  • Time for quality: maximum working hours of 46 hours including 4 hours of structured training
  • Sustainable travel: participation in the ZVV-BonusPass or general subscription
  • Well taken care of in everyday life: discounted catering offers or lunch checks
