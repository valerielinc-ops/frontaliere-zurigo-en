Full description

You want to actively shape global master data processes and effectively contribute your SAP and MDM expertise? In this role, you take responsibility for our Master Data Governance and develop sustainable solutions with various departments

Main responsibilities

  • You shape and manage the global master data governance of the BELIMO Group in line with the supply chain strategy
  • You continue to support and develop the globally used MDM tools and SAP processes and thereby bring your expertise in SAP customization into focus
  • You ensure global standards and a sustainable high master data quality and keep an eye on connections and effects along the processes
  • You coordinate and responsible for Saharan calculation processes and the calculation of the annual financial statements
  • As a competent contact person you advise and accompany different departments, mediate between different requirements and develop jointly viable solutions
  • With strong stakeholder management and persuasiveness, you can gain understanding of sustainable data structures and gain different stakeholders for common solutions
  • You lead cross-cutting MDM projects, think connected and ensure that sustainable solutions are successfully anchored in the organization

Skills required

  • You have a Bachelor's degree, preferably with a focus on data, analytics, business administration or a similar subject.
  • You bring at least 5 years of relevant professional experience in production planning and master data management and know the requirements of a complex production environment.
  • You have sound SAP expertise including customization as well as very good knowledge of the modules MM, PP, SD and BI and the variant configuration.
  • You have experience in project management and bring know-how in data analysis and big data.
  • With your logical, analytical and networked thinking, you quickly capture complex relationships, keep track of them and work precisely and solution-oriented.
  • You are a communication-strong and convincing personality, who competently advises different stakeholders, brings together different interests and also brings with you persistence even in demanding situations.
  • An efficient and decision-making approach, team orientation as well as very good German and good English skills complete your profile.
