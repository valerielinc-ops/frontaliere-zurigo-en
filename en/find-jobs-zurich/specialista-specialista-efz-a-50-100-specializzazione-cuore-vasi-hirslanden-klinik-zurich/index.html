Location: Hirslanden Clinic in the Park | Zurich

Occupation by: by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 75159

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

YOUR TASKS

  • Assumption of defined nursing tasks within the framework of the defined competence profile and on delegation of the Dipl. Care professionals
  • Ensure patient-oriented care in accordance with our quality standards and the care process
  • Ensuring the housekeeping, administrative and logistical tasks on the bed department taking into account the hospital's internal requirements and framework conditions
  • Cooperation in projects in consultation with the department management
  • Assistance in the introduction and support of new employees and learners

Your profile

  • Training as a specialist / health professional with federal certificate of ability (EFZ)
  • First experience in an acute hospital
  • High organizational and communication skills and customer orientation
  • Resistance and flexibility (shift work)
  • Good knowledge of German (word and writing), every other national language and English is advantageous

A motivated, committed and multi-professional team awaits you in an advanced working environment. This is characterized by high quality of care, independence, flexibility and a sense of responsibility. We also offer our employees generous social benefits, sports offers, special insurance conditions and further education opportunities within the framework of our program.
