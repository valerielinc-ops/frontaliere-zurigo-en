Full description

The corporate development of Migros Supermarket AG plays a central role in the strategic orientation and sustainable further development of our core business. With a dedicated team, we are driving forward projects that shape the future of the supermarket and actively accompany the transformation of the company. Do you want to help shape the success story of Migros Supermarket AG?

Mansioni

  • Complete monitoring of strategic business development projects, from project initiation to project implementation
  • Development of strategic and operational concepts in various areas of the value chain
  • Preparation and presentation of analyses and decision-making basis for corporate development and management
  • Early identification of potential risks and challenges in projects as well as development of proactive solutions while ensuring high conceptual and analytical quality
  • We are looking for several profiles: project management with focus on finance, focus on analyses (e.g. business cases, profitability calculations), project management with focus on processes

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: in corporate development or in management consulting, preferably in retail
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): ideally in business administration or business innovation
  • Experience in finance, focus on analyses (e.g. business cases, economic calculations) OR project management with focus on processes. Retail experience is beneficial
  • Positive hands-on personality with a holistic mindset, strategic perspective and very good analytical and conceptual skills
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and acting with a pragmatic and structured way of working and a strong will to continuously develop yourself and the company
  • Strong social competence as well as the ability to win people of all hierarchical levels for joint solutions and also to keep the big picture in view in long-term processes
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • French (advantageous)
  • Italian (advantageous)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central place of work: Central place of work in the city of Zurich (Limmatplatz or near Hardbrücke)
  • Modern infrastructure: Diverse work zones and modern co-working spaces with advanced equipment
  • Fair wages: functional and market-oriented remuneration - "We pay fair" certified
  • Occupational provision: Above-average benefits and three different savings plans suitable for the individual life situation
  • Self-responsibility & freedom: Varied tasks with a high level of self-responsibility
  • Club school and leisure facilities: 70% discount on Migros club school offers, 30% discount for fitness/wellness/aquaparks
