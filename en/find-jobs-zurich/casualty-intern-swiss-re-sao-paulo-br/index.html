About the Role Support the Casualty Underwriting team through the development of technology-driven solutions, enhancement of controls, and strengthening of data-driven decision-making, including the use of AI and automation tools.

  • Support the development and maintenance of dashboards and reports to monitor portfolio performance (e.g., Power BI, Excel, or similar tools).
  • Assist in the design and enhancement of underwriting controls and operational processes.
  • Support the automation of routines and data workflows to improve efficiency and productivity.
  • Assist in the exploration, implementation, and adoption of AI tools and AI agents to optimize processes, analyses, and reporting.
  • Support system testing, tool validation, and data quality assurance activities related to the Casualty business area.

About the Team

Casualty Underwriting: The team responsible for assessing, structuring, and underwriting liability risks for corporate clients. The team focuses on portfolio profitability, sustainable growth, and underwriting discipline, leveraging data and technology to support decision-making and drive operational efficiency.
