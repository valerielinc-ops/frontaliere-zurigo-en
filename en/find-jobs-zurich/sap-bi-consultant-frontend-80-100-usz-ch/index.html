Full description

To strengthen our reporting team in the Finance Directorate, we are looking for a dedicated personality with a focus on modern front-end solutions, in particular SAP Analytics Cloud. Together with the specialist areas, you will actively develop our BI landscape.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • Design, develop and optimize dashboards, reports and planning applications in SAP Analytics Cloud • Analysis and preparation of company data to support strategic and operational decisions • Collaboration with departments to accommodate and implement reporting and analysis requirements • Development of KPI and key figures models as well as data visualizations • Connection and integration of data sources (e.g. SAP BW, SAP S/4HANA, SAP Datasphere or other non-SAP systems) • Cooperation in the development of modern cloud architectures • Ensuring data quality and continuous improvement of reporting processes • Support for the introduction and further development of business intelligence and data analytics solutions • Conduct of training and workshops for professional users Requirements • Successfully completed study of business informatics, business administration with IT focus, data analytics or comparable qualification • Strong affinity for data, analyses and business relationships • Several years of experience in dealing with SAP Analytics Cloud, ideally in the areas of reporting, planning or predictive analytics • Good knowledge of SAP BI, SAP Datasphere or comparable data warehouse solutions • Understand business processes in finance, controlling, sales or supply chain • Analytical thinking as well as structured and solution-oriented working methods • Communication strength and ability to communicate technical issues in an understandable manner to specialist areas • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English We offer: A versatile and innovative working environment awaits you, in which you actively shape modern business intelligence and analytics solutions. With technologies such as SAP Analytics Cloud, SAP Datasphere and SAP BW, you will develop future-oriented reporting, planning and data solutions that create direct added value for healthcare professionals and decision-makers. You work in a dedicated team with flat hierarchies, short decision-making paths and plenty of room for manoeuvre. You have the opportunity to contribute your own ideas, take on responsibility and actively shape the further development of our BI and analytics landscape. Look forward to a modern work infrastructure, attractive employment conditions and a wide range of training and further education opportunities. A balanced work-life balance is important to us. That is why we offer you flexible working hours and the opportunity to work partly in the home office.
