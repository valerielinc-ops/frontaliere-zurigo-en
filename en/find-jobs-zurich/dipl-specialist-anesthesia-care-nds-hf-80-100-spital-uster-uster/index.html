Full description

Personal – competent – close Welcome to the Hospital Uster, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As the central pillar of health care in the Oberland region of Zurich and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you experience the highest level of expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and promoted in your professional development. Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of a “regional and digital health hub in Züri East”! The Hospital Uster has 5 operating rooms with a focus on orthopaedics and traumatology, visceral surgery, urology, gynecology, obstetrics and ENT. Another important core competence of the anesthesia team is clinical emergency medicine with the care of patients in the shock room and as a resuscitation team for the entire hospital. As an expert in anesthesia care NDS HF, you are an important link in the entire perioperative treatment chain. With the help of your pronounced professional knowledge, your technical skills and your high level of social competence, you independently carry out anesthesia under the delegation of a specialist and are responsible for the continuous perioperative monitoring of patients. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
