  • Complete product development from the idea to the series maturity: concept, circuit design, simulation (e.g. LTSpice), PC layout, prototype construction, testing and commissioning of analog and digital measuring devices, amplifiers and controls for motors and liquid systems.
  • Design and optimization of analog/digital circuits, including signal preparation (filter, amplification, noise suppression) and interfaces (e.g.SPI, I2C, UART).
  • Embedded software development : Programming of microcontrollers (C, C++, Python) for data acquisition, signal processing and control tasks.
  • Integration of sensors and A/D/D/A converters: selection, connection and optimization of signal quality from recording to digital processing. Technical advice and support (20%)
  • Technical advice from internal and external partners in the selection, installation and commissioning of measuring equipment, laboratory equipment and test equipment.
  • Structure and optimization of test and measurement setups, including error analysis and repair at assembly and system level.
  • Training and documentation : preparation of manuals, specifications and project documentation for users and development teams.
  • Support of two photon microscopes: technical maintenance, error analysis and support for use for research and development purposes. Repair & maintenance (15%)
  • Diagnosis and repair of existing installations, prototypes and series equipment, including remote fault diagnosis and spare parts management. Requirements:
  • They are a highly motivated, well-organized and communication-friendly team of players.
  • Technical know-how : Well-founded experience in circuit design (analog/digital) and the PC layout (e.g. KiCad, Altium, OrCAD). Knowledge in signal processing DSP (e.g. filters, FFT) and interface programming (e.g. SPI, I2C, UART). Experience in handling modern measurement and testing technology : Basic laboratory equipment: multimeters, digital oscilloscopes (DSO), function generator, programmable power supplies, electronic loads Special measuring instruments for signal analysis: spectrum analyzer, logic analyzer, THD-meters, EMC test devices.
  • Programming : secure knowledge of C/C++ (Embe Store, Microcontroller) and Python for data analysis and control tasks.
  • Project management : Self-employed planning and control of development projects: from component procurement to documentation.
  • Cooperation : Interdisciplinary coordination with mechanical workshops, software teams and external partners.
  • Training : Continuous examination of new technologies and methods in electronics development.
