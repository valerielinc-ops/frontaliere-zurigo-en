Location: Hirslanden Klinik Beau-Site

Occupation by appointment

Type of employment: Permanent

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

Do you want to take on leadership responsibility and use your expertise in endoscopy? Then a versatile management task awaits you in a family and dynamic environment.

Your advantages with us

  • Long-term and self-organized service planning
  • Family team and appreciative working environment
  • Attractive catering conditions
  • Up to 2 additional holiday weeks with the leisure-plus model
  • Above-average social security benefits
  • Up to 4 additional paid Fridays per year for changing time (at 100% charge)
  • Choosing piquette damage in the form of time or money

All further information on employment conditions and benefits can be found under the following link: Team diagnosis - What we lack is you!

YOUR TASKS

  • Professional, personnel and organizational leadership of the endoscopy team
  • Ensuring smooth day-to-day operations and optimal operational planning in close cooperation with the management of the patient/OPS dispo
  • Creation of process descriptions and SOPs as well as their regular update in Signavio
  • Coordination of validation and maintenance dates
  • Support for investment planning in the area
  • Cooperation in endoscopic examinations as well as care and support of patients
  • Promotion and development of employees as well as training and support of new team members
  • Cooperation in administrative tasks, material management and process optimization
  • Participation in quality-enhancing developments in endoscopy and in cross-sectoral topics

Your profile

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nursing professional HF or Med. Practical assistant/in
  • Professional experience in the acute hospital, ideally in the field of endoscopy
  • First leadership experience or motivation to take on leadership responsibility
  • Leadership training of advantage or willingness to complete it
  • Strong organisational capacity and independent and structured working methods
  • Willingness to accept various services incl. Piket
  • High social skills, communication strength and team orientation
  • Good IT user knowledge and enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation

For additional information, Silke Langlotz, Head of Endoscopy Beau-Site, is available at T or Cindy Auf der Maur, HR recruitment partner, at cindy.aufdermaur@hirslanden.ch. We look forward to your contact.
