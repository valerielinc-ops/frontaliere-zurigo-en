Full description

localsearch is the leading marketing and advertising partner of Swiss SMEs and operates well-known B2C directory platforms. The product portfolio of localsearch helps companies to be found online, to attract new customers and to retain them in the long term. With simple and effective solutions for online marketing, competent advice on site and an attractive price-performance ratio, localsearch contributes to the success of Swiss SMEs in the digital world.

Become part of our success story and help us accompany companies on their digital journey!

This is what we offer you

  • An attractive compensation model

Your success is immediately rewarded – Every successful consultation and every contract agreement increases your income

  • Workplace in the modern office in Zurich : You mainly work on site with your team. One home office day per week is possible.
  • Extensive training

Our experienced coaches support you with structured training, sales know-how and continuous development

  • A dynamic and supportive team

You can expect an open corporate culture with a strong team spirit, mutual support and a clear performance mentality

Your tasks

  • You will have outbound conversations with Swiss SMEs and analyze their online visibility and marketing potential
  • You advise entrepreneurs on equal terms on digital marketing solutions from localsearch
  • You recognize sales opportunities and lead conversations goal-oriented to the conclusion
  • Develop existing customer relationships and identify cross- and upselling potential
  • You gain new SME customers and build sustainable business relationships
  • You work with structured sales campaigns and manage your activities professionally in the Salesforce CRM system

You are

  • Experience in Outbound Sales, Telesales or B2B Sales
  • Convincing and enjoys advising business owners and selling solutions
  • motivated by ambitious goals and strong sales results
  • autonomous, structured and responsible in your way of working
  • digitally affin and interested in marketing solutions for SMEs
  • German at C2 level, Swiss German understanding of advantage
  • Additional French or Italian skills are a plus
  • Experience with CRM systems like Salesforce is an advantage

This role suits you especially well if

  • you understand sales as consulting with clear final responsibility
  • you enjoy working with entrepreneurs and understand their challenges
  • You enjoy getting deals through strong conversations and compelling solutions
