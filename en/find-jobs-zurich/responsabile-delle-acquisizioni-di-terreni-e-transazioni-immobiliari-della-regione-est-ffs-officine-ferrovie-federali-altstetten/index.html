Head of land acquisitions and real estate transactions region East 60-100% - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Head of land acquisitions and real estate transactions region East Altstetten / Work Smart, immediate start or suitable, 60-100% (Job sharing possible) Apply here

What awaits you.

The land management area assumes overall responsibility for the real estate of SBBs and their subsidiaries. You contribute to the development of new railway infrastructure, attractive stations and modern operating objects by ensuring that the necessary land and rights are provided on time. A motivated and collegiate team awaits you, which remains reliable even in dynamic situations and appreciates team spirit and humour. What you can move.

  • You assume overall responsibility for land transactions related to railway operations in the Eastern Region, in particular for land and rights acquisitions.
  • With your team, you ensure that transactions are processed in a formal, temporal and financial manner for railway projects.
  • You are responsible for operational, strategic and personnel direction, as well as costs, timelines and quality of service.
  • Demanding negotiations on land transactions and claims are conducted with confidence, while representing the service to the authorities, commissions and third parties.
  • By participating in complex and strategic projects and securing the long-term direction of land operations, you contribute to the evolution of the service.
  • You identify potentials within your team (about 7 people), promote professional development and support collaborators in achieving their goals.

What you bring.

  • Thanks to your assurance, your sense of communication and your negotiating skills, you can find lasting solutions, even during demanding exchanges.
  • With a spirit of analysis and design and strategic thinking, you put them at the service of conducting complex projects.
  • With proven experience in driving and team development, you implement it with empathy, commitment and sense of responsibility.
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration motivates you and you show great reliability and openness.
  • You have a master's degree in law or business economics (HES/University), notary's degree or similar training with relevant professional experience. An experience at the notariat, at the service of the land register or in real estate is particularly welcome.
  • You master German at level C1 and ideally French at level B1.

Your salary and benefits.

We are actively committed to equal pay. This commitment is in accordance with the Code of Obligations, taking into account a comparison with the team, market and industry.

Here's a good way to start.

The fact that you want to move Switzerland with us is the biggest motivation for us. We therefore waive our request for a cover letter for this position. If you wish, you can write to us in our application tool.
