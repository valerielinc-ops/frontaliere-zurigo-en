Are you looking for a job that brings together precision, state-of-the-art technology and exciting analytics? Then become part of SQTS – Swiss Quality Testing Services. In the area of ​​food contact materials (FCM), we are one of the few laboratories in the world that offer a comprehensive range of analyzes for almost all materials. Mansioni

  • Quality control: You carry out chemical-analytical tests on food, packaging and everyday items and thus ensure compliance with quality and safety requirements
  • Non-target analysis: You analyze samples using GC-QTOF-MS/FID and UPLC-QTOF-MS with screening methods
  • Target analysis: You carry out analyzes on various measuring systems from Agilent, Thermo and AB Sciex in different matrices
  • Equipment responsibility: You maintain, maintain, operate and check analytical equipment in accordance with ISO 17025 and thus ensure smooth, standard-compliant laboratory operations
  • Laboratory management: You work with our laboratory information management system (LIMS) and ensure structured recording, evaluation and documentation of analysis data
