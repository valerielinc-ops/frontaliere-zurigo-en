Full description

As an experienced specialist (m/f/d) for Occupational Safety and Health Protection (ASGS), you actively shape the safety culture in a technologically demanding environment. With your know-how, you ensure that the highest safety standards are adhered to and continuously improved. Their commitment makes a decisive contribution to minimizing risks and ensuring the sustainable protection of our employees. Your responsibilities in detail:

  • Further development of occupational safety incl. Creation of concepts, guidelines and standards as well as development of proposals for continuous improvement of processes and processes
  • Professional advice and support of managers and employees in all aspects of occupational safety and health protection
  • Implementation and monitoring of security policies, procedures and standards as well as regular monitoring of legal requirements and relevant standards to ensure compliance
  • Conduct hazard assessments, safety inspections and audits, and derivation and follow-up of measures
  • Maintenance and further development of the hazardous materials cadastre, management of the SDS and active advice on the safe handling of chemicals
  • Planning, organisation and implementation of training and awareness-raising activities to promote an active safety culture
  • Cooperation with internal and external partners, e.g. authorities and occupational doctors, as well as active participation in safety-related committees, specialist committees and working groups
  • Participation in safety-related projects, in particular in the field of fire protection, conversion projects and digitalisation
  • Preparation of security analyses, reports and KPIs for management as well as structured, regular and traceable documentation of all activities and completed tasks WHAT YOU SHOULD INVOLVE
  • At least federal professional certificate as specialist ASGS or recognised diploma in the field of health & safety, e.g. as safety engineer, SiBe or ASA specialist
  • Several years of experience in a responsible position in the field of occupational safety and hazardous materials, ideally in a technologically demanding industrial environment (e.g. mechanical engineering, defence or automotive industry)
  • In-depth knowledge of relevant legal regulations and standards such as VUV, EKAS and ISO 45001
  • High degree of initiative, analytical thinking and assertiveness
  • Ability to communicate complex issues in an understandable manner and to sensitize different stakeholders to security concerns
  • Experience with digital tools for security monitoring and documentation
  • Willingness to travel occasionally
  • Secure appearance, strong organisational skills and good negotiating skills
  • Very good knowledge of German (C1 in word and writing), English skills of advantage WHAT WE ARE TO YOU
  • A dynamic working environment with exciting projects
  • Flexible working hours (active and trusted working hours); Home office, where possible and useful
  • Very good social benefits and a super-compulsory pension fund
  • Employee benefits of a large corporation, employee share purchase program
  • Promotion of needs-based training
  • Staff restaurant and external catering and shopping facilities nearby
  • Individual internal and external personnel development measures in cooperation with our Rheinmetall Academy
  • Workplace a few minutes walk from the Zurich-Oerlikon train station KONTAKTDETAILS Responsible contact: Michael Zmoos For questions about your application, please use the contact form. Please note that dossiers of recruiters are not taken into account.
