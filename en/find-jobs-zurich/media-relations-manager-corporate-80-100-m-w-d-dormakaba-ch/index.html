Become part of our team

As a global company that pursues very clear growth goals, we need employees who want to take themselves and the company forward. For our location in Rümlang we are looking for you immediately or by agreement as Media Relations Manager (Corporate) (m/f/d) Based at dormakaba’s international headquarters in Rümlang, Switzerland, the Media Relations Manager (Corporate) shapes, strengthens and protects the company’s external reputation through proactive media work as well as professional issue and crisis management. In close cooperation with senior management, the function develops and implements media strategies on company-related, financial, strategic and transformational topics.

Your tasks

  • Responsibility for media work on company-related, strategic, financial and business topics as well as the development and implementation of corresponding media strategies.
  • Management of communication on financial results, acquisitions, partnerships and other significant corporate developments in close coordination with Investor Relations.
  • Build and maintain sustainable relationships with business, financial, trade and industry media as well as proactively identify opportunities to strengthen the visibility and positioning of the company.
  • Creation of media releases, statements, core messages, Q&As, briefing documents and materials for company spokespersons.
  • Develop and anchor corporate messages and strategic narratives, ensuring consistent and coordinated external communication across all communication activities and stakeholders.
  • Positioning of top management and other company spokesmen through interviews, media appearances, specialist contributions and strategic thought leadership activities.
  • Developing and maintaining frameworks, policies and processes for crisis communication and managing communication on sensitive corporate issues and reputational risks, including the preparation of holding statements, media responses and crisis messages.
  • Advising executives and specialists on communication risks, media address, messages and external positioning.
  • Observation of media coverage, reputation trends and emerging topics as well as early identification of risks and opportunities for external positioning.
  • Close cooperation with the second Media Relations Manager to ensure coordinated external positioning as well as mutual support and deputy in case of absences, peak times and important communication events.

Your profile

  • Usually 7–10 years of professional experience in corporate communications, media relations, journalism, public affairs or PR, ideally with proven experience working with journalists in an international environment.
  • Experience in financial communication, M&A, crisis communication or other reputation-sensitive topics.
  • Knowledge of the Swiss media landscape as well as existing contacts with Swiss business, financial or specialist media are a great advantage.
  • Experience in a listed company, an international corporation, a communications consultancy or a media company is of great advantage.
  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in communication, journalism, public relations, business administration, political sciences or a related field.
  • Strong media and stakeholder management skills and the ability to build trust with senior stakeholders and external partners.
  • Very good understanding of corporate communications and corporate reputation and external positioning.
  • Excellent skills in writing, editing and developing compelling messages.
  • Strong business understanding and ability to quickly grasp complex business topics.
  • Strong judgement in dealing with sensitive or confidential issues.
  • Very good project management and organizational skills.
  • Proactive, solution-oriented personality with the ability to work effectively even in dynamic and demanding situations.
  • Very good German and English skills in word and writing are required
  • Bilingual candidates are particularly welcome.
