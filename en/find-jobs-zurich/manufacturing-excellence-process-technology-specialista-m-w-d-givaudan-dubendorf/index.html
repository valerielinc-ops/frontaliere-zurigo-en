As a Manufacturing Excellence & Process Technology Specialist, you are responsible for the continuous improvement, standardization and technical development of the manufacturing processes in the field of Flavor Liquid Compounding. You drive operational excellence initiatives, improve safety, quality, productivity and cost performance, and act as technical contact for production and process issues. You work closely with production, quality, engineering, supply chain, EHS and R&D.

You work at our production site in Dübendorf and report directly to Process Technology Manager.

Your tasks

  • Identification, initiation and implementation of continuous improvement and lean projects
  • Analysis and optimisation of manufacturing, mixing, dosing, transfer and cleaning processes
  • Monitoring and improving KPIs such as OEE, productivity, yield, quality and delivery performance
  • Perform root-cause analyses and define and track sustainable corrective actions
  • Development, implementation and maintenance of standards, SOPs and best practices
  • Support for scale-ups, process validations and product launches
  • Cooperation and management of investment, automation and digitalization projects
  • Support for the interface between liquid compounding and spray drying
  • Ensuring compliance with EHS, quality, GMP, HACCP and compliance requirements
  • Implementation of technical training and transfer of know-how in the production environment
  • Formula Management, New Product Introduction, Internal Complaint Management

Your profile

  • Studies in process engineering, food technology, chemical engineering or similar technical training
  • At least 1 year of professional experience in a production environment in the food, aroma, chemical or process industries
  • Knowledge of liquid compounding and spray drying
  • Experience with Lean Manufacturing, Operational Excellence, Six Sigma or similar methods
  • Knowledge of GMP, HACCP and food safety requirements
  • Analytical, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Strong communication and project management skills
  • High initiative and hands-on mentality in the production environment
  • Very good German and English skills
