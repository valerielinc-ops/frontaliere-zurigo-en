About the role With our competent services, we secure the value of our properties and ensure trouble-free production operations. We are responsible for infrastructural and technical plant and building maintenance at the Zurich and Widen locations. In addition to preventive maintenance work, we are also responsible for repairs and cleanliness in the properties and outdoor areas. To strengthen our team, we are looking for one at the location in Zurich-Altstetten: Building technician / Allrounder Facility Management (m/f/d)

  • Acceptance and correction of plant and structural disturbances
  • Maintenance of technical installations
  • Assistance and support of external companies
  • Troubleshooting and professional documentation in the CAFM
  • Support for conversions and smaller projects

Your skills and experience

  • Completed technical training in HVAC (heating, ventilation, air conditioning, sanitary) electric with NIV15 advantageous
  • Several years of experience in building technology
  • Readiness of Pikettdienst in Zurich and Widen
  • Strong service orientation and solution-oriented working methods
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Category B ticket

Why you should come to us

  • A 40-hour week in 2-shift operation (Monday Friday 05.00 – 20.45)
  • Structured implementation programme
  • Lots of room for design and space for creative ideas
  • Flat hierarchies and lived DU culture
  • A motivated team with a lot of know-how and an open feedback culture
  • Attractive employment conditions and development prospects

Sika is a great employer – especially for employees who appreciate being a linchpin. We welcome your complete application dossier (CV, certificates, diplomas, etc.).

We offer competitive remuneration based on local market standards and the specific responsibilities of each job. The remuneration shall be based on the skills, responsibility, education and/or training relevant to the position. We are committed to fair and equitable remuneration schemes in accordance with applicable laws, the Chemicals Tariff Agreement and regulations.

About Sika Sika is a specialty chemical company, global leader in the development and production of systems and products for gluing, sealing, steaming, reinforcing and protecting in construction and industry. Sika has a global presence with subsidiaries in 103 countries, produces in over 400 factories, develops innovative technologies for customers around the world and thus contributes significantly to the sustainable transformation in construction and transport. The more than 33,000 employees generated a turnover of CHF 11.20 billion in 2025.
