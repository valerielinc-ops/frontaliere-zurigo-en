Place of work: Hirslanden Corporate Office | Home office occupation by: by appointment Type of employment: Permanent Reference number: 68746 Hirslanden stands for first-class, responsible and individual healthcare that puts people at the center. From birth to old age and from prevention to healing – at any time, physically and digitally – the Hirslanden Group, together with private and public cooperation partners, focuses on the further development of the “Continuum of Care”. Our focus is not only on the well-being of our patients, but also on that of our employees. As a traditional Swiss company with international connections to the Mediclinic Group and its divisions, we benefit from global exchange and constant further development. YOUR TASKS

  • Support in the further development of risk management throughout the company by optimizing the framework, internal control system (ICS), processes and tools based on best practices and pragmatic solutions.
  • Coordination and moderation of risk management processes, including definition of risk appetite and key risk indicators (KRIs), risk identification as well as risk and control self-assessments.
  • Analysis of incidents to determine causes, derive insights and recommend effective measures to reduce risks.
  • Creation of appropriate reports and decision-making bases for management and governance committees.
  • Shared responsibility for Business Continuity Management (BCM), including business impact analyses, planning and execution of tests and exercises as well as maintaining business continuity plans.
  • Promoting a risk-aware corporate culture through cross-departmental collaboration, advising departments and conducting training. YOUR PROFILE
  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in business administration, economics or finance, ideally with a focus on risk management, business continuity management or compliance
  • 3–5 years of professional experience in risk management, compliance, operational resilience, auditing or consulting
  • Experience in the healthcare sector is an advantage
  • Strong project and change management skills as well as experience in dealing with Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) processes and tools
  • High level of initiative, entrepreneurial thinking, willingness to perform and strong teamwork skills as well as strong communication and stakeholder management skills in a dynamic environment
  • Good MS Office knowledge, as well as very good knowledge of German and English
  • Knowledge of French is an advantage
