Full description

On our stem and immune cell therapy station, we look after patients with hemato-oncological diseases in different phases of their treatment. As a care expert: in APN you take on the professional leadership and responsibility for the care of these patients and their families and work closely with the shared governance team. >The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.

Tasks

  • The care and advice of patients with complex haemato-oncological clinical pictures and sophisticated therapies (allogeneic and autologous stem cell transplantation, CAR-T cell therapy, bispecific antibodies, chemotherapy) and their relatives
  • The active support of the care team in everyday life. This includes co-care and coaching in demanding care situations
  • The technical guidance of the experts:women on the station
  • Securing and developing the quality of care on the station in cooperation with the Shared Governance Team
  • Active participation in the development, implementation and evaluation of policies and concepts, in compliance with JACIE quality and accreditation standards
  • Active participation in practical development and research projects of the management area
  • Planning and conducting case discussions and training in the care teams
  • Supporting the treatment team in ethical decision-making

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience in hemato-oncological care
  • Completed studies for Master of Science in Nursing (MScN)
  • Experience in the care and care of patients with stem cell transplantation, CAR-T cell therapy beneficial
  • High personal responsibility and a person-centered basic attitude
  • You bring very good communication skills and are happy to pass on your expertise to others

We offer

Look forward to exciting opportunities and challenges in a dynamic special department of the University Hospital Zurich. You will work in a motivated shared governance team, consisting of management, specialist and education, as well as in a committed interprofessional team in the relevant bed departments. Structured training supports you when entering your new function. In addition, you benefit from a close cooperation with the care experts: in NP in the hemato-oncological tumor pathway as well as from a dedicated APN team from various specialist clinics.
