We are looking for the talents of tomorrow!
Raiffeisenbank Zürich Airport is a successful and locally oriented bank that turns its corporate customers into fans. For the development of the corporate customer business, we are looking for a motivated and committed corporate customer consultant.

Our values set us apart:
#I care, #Teamwork rocks, #Stronger together, #Good vibes

Can you identify with our values? Then apply now! Tasks: • You advise our corporate clients in the real estate, commercial and industrial sectors from one-man operations to internationally active SMEs • You independently prepare credit analyses for commercial financing and (partially complex) real estate financing • You will develop individual solutions for the entire range of consulting and services in the corporate customer business • You maintain your customer portfolio and expand customer relationships in the long term (representation in the region and new customer acquisition) • You also do not mind administrative tasks (pre- and post-processing of customer appointments, pendences from your own customer book) and know that these are part of everyday life. Get to know us What we offer you • Working in a committed and established team • A modern, centrally located workplace in a fresh look • An all-round good employment package with many advantages within the Raiffeisen Group Your perspectives • You work in an uncomplicated and open environment that offers development potential. • Our growth course, our promise of outstanding service and our open culture give you the opportunity to shape our company in the long term and develop yourself personally. Requirements • You have completed banking education with further education or university studies (FH, HF, Uni) • You bring several years of experience in SME/corporate customer business and complex property financing (return/business properties) • You move skillfully between the different corporate customers and their needs, and know how to convince them of you and our bank • Your passion and enthusiasm for the corporate customer business inspires you every day • The team has a high priority for you, because only together we are successful We offer: We invite our employees to actively participate in their work and to take on responsibility. This creates varied tasks that create real added value – for everyone who wants to make a difference.
