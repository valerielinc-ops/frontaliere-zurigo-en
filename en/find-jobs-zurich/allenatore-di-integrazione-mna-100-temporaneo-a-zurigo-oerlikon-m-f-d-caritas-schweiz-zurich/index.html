Full description

Doing the right thing. In Switzerland and in around 20 countries on four continents. Engage with us for the vision of a solidarity society, as Caritas Switzerland has been running asylum centres for families, individuals and unaccompanied minors (MNA) at five locations in the Canton of Zurich since March 2024 on behalf of the Cantonal Social Office (KSA). As an integration coach MNA, you coordinate the social, educational and professional integration of unaccompanied minor asylum seekers in the MNA centre. Their tasks

  • Documentation of assessments or location determinations, in particular short assessment IAS "Results of the assessment of potential" for all MNAs
  • Applications for integration measures of the IAZH catalogue
  • Organisation of educational and professional follow-up solutions
  • Documentation of the discussions, planned and implemented measures
  • Quarterly processing of the areas of life vocational and school integration in close cooperation with socio-pedagogical reference person
  • Discussion documentation on integration topics
  • Responsibility Professional integration: clarification, cooperation with specialist positions and support in the search for apprentices
  • Cooperation with institutions and authorities to discuss integration-specific topics
  • Reporting according to specifications of the Integration Department
  • Preparation of integration-relevant information for the exit phase
  • Exchange with schools and experts

Our requirements

  • Completed training in social work, social pedagogy, vocational, educational or career guidance, pedagogy or equivalent qualification
  • Further education in case management and/or on integration topics of advantage
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss educational and vocational training system, in particular the various educational pathways and follow-up solutions
  • At least three years of professional experience, ideally in the social service with focus on case management refugees or in the area of integration
  • Enjoyment of working with unaccompanied minor asylum seekers (MNA) and understanding of their special living situation
  • Knowledge and experience in the application of the Social Assistance Act and the Asylum Assistance Ordinance
  • Good knowledge of the asylum system and relevant requirements in the field of integration
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German, good oral knowledge in English
  • Further knowledge of foreign languages desired
  • Very good computer user knowledge (especially MS office and ideal case management software tutoris)
  • A perfect special private extract from the criminal record or Willingness to submit it before taking up the job

Place of work: Canton of Zurich (Zurich-Oerlikon) Start of work: Immediately or by agreement The job is limited to 28.02.2027.
