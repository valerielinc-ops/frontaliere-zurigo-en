Full description

Children's Hospital Zurich Eleonorenstiftung | online since : 15.09.2026

Your tasks

  • Project planning and management: Creation of project plans (dates, milestones, resources, budget) and monitoring of progress using appropriate methods (e.g. HERMES, Scrum).
  • Requirements management: Collecting, documenting and prioritizing professional and technical requirements together with stakeholders in the field and ICT.
  • Risk and quality management: Identifying project risks, deriving measures and ensuring defined quality standards.
  • Team leadership and coordination: managing the ICT project team, task allocation, facilitating meetings and ensuring effective collaboration.
  • Communication and reporting: Regular exchange and information of all parties involved.

Your profile

  • Completed university studies (uni/FH) in the field of business informatics, business administration, health management or a comparable qualification
  • Several years of professional experience in project management, ideally in a hospital or other healthcare institution
  • Proven project experience in the field of patient data, medical services and ERP systems (e.g. Nexus, SAP or similar solutions)
  • In-depth knowledge and proven expertise in common project management methods – in particular PRINCE2, PMP (PMBOK) and/or HERMES (certification of advantage)
  • Confident use of ServiceNow and Azure DevOps (or comparable PM/ITSM tools)
  • Negotiation-proof German (minimum C1) as well as good English skills in word and writing
  • Structured, solution-oriented and autonomous working method
  • High social skills and assertiveness – even in complex, multi-professional environments
  • Analytical thinking and a sense of critical success factors
  • Communication strength and pleasure in working with clinical and administrative departments
  • Resilience and flexibility in a dynamic hospital everyday life
