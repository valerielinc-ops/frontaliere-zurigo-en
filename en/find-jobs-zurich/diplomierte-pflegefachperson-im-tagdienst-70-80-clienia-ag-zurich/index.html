Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With over 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric services offered by Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in outpatient, day clinic and inpatient settings. The group employs around 1800 people and generated sales of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For our acute care unit S3 with a focus on older adults, we are looking for a

The Acute Care Unit S3 is aimed at people in exceptional psychiatric situations who require intensive, inpatient treatment due to an acute mental illness. Careful psychiatric and somatic clarification as well as the collection of personal life history form the basis for all therapies. The entire treatment program is geared towards coping with the current crisis and reintegration into the old or newly designed conditions and, in addition to recovery-oriented psychosocial support, also includes alternative treatment interventions such as NADA, phytotherapy and much more.

The ward is usually openly run and implements the Safewards model. We would be happy if you would also participate with your ideas.

Your tasks Flexibility and enjoyment of variety are central points. Because that's exactly what working in acute psychiatry means. If this applies to you, here are some of the most important tasks:

  • Caregiver work: We treat our patients individually according to their needs and maintain contact with their relatives
  • In addition to the individual discussions, group offers take place, which are also led by the nursing department
  • Our interprofessional collaboration is a huge strength and sets us apart. Active participation and involvement are central components of this collaboration
  • Since we work with permanent night watches, you will be deployed in a two-shift system (early and late shift). Would you still like to work a few nights? Then we will make that possible too.

Your profile Professional competence, service orientation and a lot of humor are the most important qualities you need to have. With these qualities, we achieve the best treatment results for our patients, and the team is happy that you will become a part of them.

If you have experience in nursing, but not in the field of psychiatry, it is still possible to join. It is then crucial that you have a lot of motivation and commitment to acquire the specific skills. As part of our large internal offer and the generous participation in further education and training, you can get to know the various therapy methods and then apply them yourself. Of course, you will also be supported by your new colleagues.
