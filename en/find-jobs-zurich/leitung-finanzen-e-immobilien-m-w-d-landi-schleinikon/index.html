Advantages - training and further education We invest in the development of our employees. A wide range of internal educational opportunities is open to everyone, and we support external further training. Attractive vacation policy Our vacation policy guarantees all employees at least 5 weeks of vacation. For learners and employees under 20 years of age it is 6 weeks. It is also possible to take unpaid leave. Family leave Mothers receive 100 percent of their insured salary during the 14 to 16 weeks of maternity leave. Insurance benefits We protect our employees with above-average social and pension benefits and offer special conditions on additional insurance from various health insurance companies for their entire family. Benefits and discounts Our employees benefit, among other things, from discounts on the purchase of furniture and new cars, special conditions on private insurance and discounted mobile subscriptions for them and their families. Anniversary gift Every five years we thank our employees for their loyalty and great commitment. Be it with a financial bonus or with additional vacation days.
