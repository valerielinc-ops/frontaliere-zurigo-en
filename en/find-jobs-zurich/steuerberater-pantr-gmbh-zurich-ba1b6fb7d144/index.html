Full description

For one of the leading Swiss consulting companies in the areas of trust, auditing, tax and legal consulting with around 550 employees at eleven locations, we are looking for an experienced personality for the tax team in Zurich. The company advises SMEs, private customers and listed companies on an interdisciplinary basis at the highest quality level. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for you!

This is your job

  • Tax consultancy: Comprehensive support for natural and legal persons in challenging national and international tax law issues.
  • Life cycle accompaniment: Competent advice on entrepreneurial decisions throughout the entire life cycle – from the founding of a company to restructuring and succession.
  • Authority contact & representation: conducting negotiations with tax authorities as well as representing mandates in complex assessment and appeal proceedings.
  • VAT: expert advice and support on issues relating to indirect taxation (VAT).

This is what you bring with you

  • Training: Successfully completed training for/to dipl. Tax expert: in (or a comparable qualification) or in advanced training.
  • Experience: Several years of profound professional experience in national and international tax law and VAT.
  • Personality: Pronounced service and customer orientation, high quality standards, flexibility and an open personality with a strong team spirit.

This is what awaits you from the customer

  • Culture: An appreciative working atmosphere with lived DU culture in an interdisciplinary team of experts.
  • Flexibility: Modern working time models with compensation options and flexible use of the home office.
  • Development & Benefits: Targeted education and training, plenty of room for manoeuvre, modern workplaces in a central location in Zurich and attractive employee benefits.
