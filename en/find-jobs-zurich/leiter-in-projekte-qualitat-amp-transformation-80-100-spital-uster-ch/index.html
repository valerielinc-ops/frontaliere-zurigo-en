Head of Projects, Quality & Transformation (80-100%)

Head of Projects, Quality & Transformation (80-100%) Personal – competent – close Welcome to the Hospital Uster, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As the central pillar of health care in the Oberland region of Zurich and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you experience the highest level of expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and promoted in your professional development. Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of a “regional and digital health hub in Züri East”! We are looking for one by 1 February 2027 or by agreement

Exciting challenges await you

In this key role, you will lead the staff department Projects, Quality & Transformation with three employees, which will report directly to the CEO. You ensure that strategic goals and decisions become concrete results.

  • You control the company-wide project portfolio and multi-project management. You coordinate the available project management resources and ensure professional management and implementation of the prioritized company projects. You manage selected projects yourself.
  • You will lead and develop the department with the functions of quality and process management, portfolio and transformation management and patient service.
  • You drive forward the digital and organizational transformation together with the specialist areas and accompany change projects from conception to sustainable implementation.
  • You actively shape the further development of quality, process and risk management and promote a systematic and patient-oriented culture of improvement.
  • You are responsible for document and contract management and ensure its professional development.
  • You assume the role of data protection coordinator and ensure the coordination of data protection topics in cooperation with the responsible internal and external bodies.
  • You prepare well-founded decision-making bases for the CEO and the management and provide user-friendly reporting for transparency about the progress, impact and challenge of the key corporate projects.

What sets you apart for the job

  • University degree (university/FH), for example in business administration, health management, business informatics or a comparable discipline
  • several years of experience in the management and management of complex, cross-divisional projects and change projects
  • profound experience in multi-project or project portfolio management as well as a secure handling of contemporary project management methods and control instruments
  • Experience leading interdisciplinary teams and the ability to combine diverse expertise into a powerful unit
  • the ability to effectively lead, support and win joint goals for project leaders and interdisciplinary teams even without direct hierarchical authority
  • Good understanding of transformation processes as well as organizational, process, quality and risk management
  • Experience in healthcare or in a comparable complex and regulated organization is beneficial
  • Knowledge of data protection or the willingness to quickly familiarize yourself with this topic
  • Strong analytical and conceptual skills combined with pragmatism, decision-making strength and a clear focus on impact and outcomes
  • Convincing communication and moderation skills, as well as the ability to perform convincingly at different levels of leadership and expertise and achieve viable solutions

7 Reasons to Work with Us

  • You assume a central function at the interface between strategy and implementation
  • You have a lot of room for manoeuvre and work directly with the CEO, the management and the departments
  • You can actively develop the project, quality and transformation capability of the Uster Hospital
  • For company-wide projects, you have a network of experienced specialists from the departments at your disposal alongside your team.
  • An excellent staff restaurant and numerous perks
  • Good accessibility with public transport
  • ZVV BonusPass or cheap parking – with Parkingpay max. CHF 4.50 per day
