Our Data & Storage Platforms division with more than 25 employees is responsible for the further development and operation of the business-critical data and storage infrastructure in close cooperation with our stakeholders. We rely on state-of-the-art technologies such as Oracle Exadata, PostgreSQL and MS SQL to ensure a reliable, innovative and scalable data and storage landscape.

In your role as a Oracle-focused database engineer, you will help develop our entire database landscape and ensure its resilience and performance, actively shaping the future of our data and storage platforms.

Your tasks

  • Responsible for the engineering, operation and further development of our Oracle Exadata platform
  • Responsible for the design and engineering of the central Oracle landscape
  • Ensure performance, stability and availability of Oracle databases, including backup and recovery concepts
  • Further develop the database landscape and plan as well as implement technical lifecycle projects in terms of quality, time and budget
  • Advising and supporting internal stakeholders on architecture, security, performance and operation of database services
  • Regular maintenance outside office hours and on-call duty
  • Completed degree programme (Uni/FH/HF) in computer science or equivalent training with corresponding practical experience
  • Several years of experience in engineering and operating Oracle databases in mission-critical environments
  • In-depth knowledge of Oracle Exadata, including conception, operation and performance optimization
  • Very good know-how in the areas of dataguard, backup and recovery as well as database and SQL performance tuning
  • High affinity for data and platform technologies as well as a good understanding of the requirements of internal stakeholders such as application development or data & analytics
  • Very good German and English skills (at least level B2)
  • Mutual support and team spirit are important to us
  • Various opportunities to develop personally and professionally
  • Central and modern workplace
  • Flexible working hours, mobile work and work-life balance are a matter of course for us
  • We work in a du-culture and promote a supportive and appreciative atmosphere in the team
  • Comprehensive package with attractive basic salary, variable compensation and versatile benefits
