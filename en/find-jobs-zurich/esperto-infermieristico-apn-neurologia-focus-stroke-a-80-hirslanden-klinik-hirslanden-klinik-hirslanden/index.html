Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Occupation by appointment

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 73721

In this newly created APN role, you bring your clinical expertise to the care of neurological patients, especially after Stroke, and you have the opportunity to help shape the APN function in the Stroke Center right from the start. You complement the established interprofessional team of our certified Stroke Center and focus on clinical care, education and the further development of patient advice and support.

YOUR TASKS

  • You bring your clinical expertise to the care of neurological patients, especially stroke patients.
  • You advise and accompany patients and relatives through targeted education and support an individual and sustainable self-management in dealing with the consequences of the stroke together with the team.
  • You are involved in the further development of patient advice and support.
  • You work closely with the interprofessional team and the medical service according to your qualifications and experience.
  • You take over the representation of the APN Delir including the delirvisits

Your profile

  • Completed MSc in Nursing completed, ongoing studies or willingness to complete them in a timely manner
  • Professional experience in the acute hospital, ideally with experience in neurology or Stroke care
  • Neurological or Stroke-specific training or in-depth specialist qualifications in the field of neurology / Stroke are advantageous; A CAS Stroke Care is desirable
  • Enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation as well as the advice and support of patients and relatives
  • Independent, responsible and team-oriented working methods
