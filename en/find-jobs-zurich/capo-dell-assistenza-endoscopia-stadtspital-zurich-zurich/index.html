Full description

For the Department of Gastroenterology / Hepatology at the locations Triemli and Waid, we are looking for a concise fresh personality by 2027 or by appointment.

Tasks

  • You lead the team of the Endoscopy Assistance at two locations in terms of expertise and personnel and ensure a smooth working day.
  • You plan the deployment of employees and coordinate spatial and service planning across both locations.
  • You accompany, train and supervise employees especially in the areas of endoscopy, sedation and hygiene.
  • You are actively involved in endoscopy and assist in various gastroenterological and optionally pneumological investigations and interventions.
  • You ensure high quality in patient care, documentation, hygiene / preparation of endoscopes.
  • You are familiar with quality management and digital work.
  • You work closely with the medical management, disposition and secretariat and contribute your ideas for the further development of endoscopy.

Profile

  • You have a diploma as a nurse/nurse specialist HF
  • You bring professional experience in gastroenterology and experience in endoscopy.
  • You enjoy management tasks and experience in the professional and personnel support of employees.
  • You work independently, organized and reliable and keep an overview even in demanding situations.
  • You communicate in an appreciative way, perform safely and enjoy working interdisciplinary and across locations.
  • You are motivated to pass on your expertise, to develop employees and to continuously shape the quality of our processes.

We offer

We offer you more than just a job! Our city hospital world is characterized by an open dual culture, an uncomplicated, personal togetherness, respect and appreciation. As a team of over 120 professional groups, we work every day in very different ways for the well-being of our patients. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions and excellent social benefits from the city of Zurich.

About us

The Zurich City Hospital is one of the top 10 Swiss hospitals with over 36'000 inpatient patients and around 455,000 outpatients per year. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,500 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
