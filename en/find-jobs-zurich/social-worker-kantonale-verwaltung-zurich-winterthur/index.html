Full description

The Jugendanwaltschaft Winterthur with its approximately 22 employees from the fields of law, social work and administration is responsible for the districts of Andelfingen and Winterthur and leads the criminal investigations and enforcement of criminal sanctions under juvenile law. To complement our team of the Youth Advocacy Office Winterthur we are looking for 1. April 2027 or by appointment a committed and motivated personality. These tasks are waiting for you:

  • They clarify the personal circumstances of delinquent young people and produce reports according to KORJUS.
  • They implement the ordered sanctions (protective measures and penalties) and check their course.
  • In individual cases, you accompany and support young people and their parents.
  • They run tort-specific learning programs with young people.
  • They network with other youth welfare authorities and participate in working groups. This is what distinguishes you:
  • They have a degree from a university of applied sciences or a higher technical school for social work and have completed a subject-specific further education (CAS or similar).
  • They bring at least 2 years of relevant professional experience in social work, ideally in the field of working with people in a forced context.
  • They are interested and willing to work in an interdisciplinary team and constantly develop professionally and methodically.
  • You are a committed and resilient person and show an open and positive attitude towards people.
  • They are accustomed to independent and structured work and are skillful in oral and written expression.
  • You have an affinity for computer science. Have we aroused your interest? Then we look forward to your complete online application.
