Full description

Senior Claims Specialist Property/Construction (m/f/d) 80-100% Permanent in Zurich, Switzerland Our claims department makes the difference. Our experienced claims professionals use their specialized expertise to handle even the most complex claims cases smoothly. How do you make something good even better? By consistently focusing on excellence and creating a culture of continuous improvement. By creating an environment that promotes cooperation, customer orientation and the further development of colleagues. And by building a team of passionate and innovative claims experts, for whom success is an incentive to roll up their sleeves and drive improvement. You work closely with your manager and your claims colleagues to consistently resolve claims. In addition, you are in regular exchange and close cooperation with the Regional Practice Leaders and the underwriting teams. Together, you have the task of achieving the best possible results for our customers by proactively managing, negotiating and concluding claims cases. What you'll be doing

Their main tasks include

  • Independent and proactive processing of complex property damages, in particular property and business interruptions, from loss reporting to completion
  • Management and regulation of major losses in the wholesale business including damage inspections, coverage inspection, reporting and loss negotiations
  • Processing or support in case of damage in the area of construction/energy
  • Responsibility for the assigned customer portfolios and active management of claims with the aim of an efficient and best possible solution for our customers
  • Close cooperation and communication with customers, brokers, underwriting, external service providers and other internal and external stakeholders
  • Active cooperation with our international claims network and other functions within the company to ensure a high level of customer service

You will report to the claims Team Leader Property, Construction, Marine & Specialty. What you'll bring We are looking for someone who has the following knowledge and skills:

  • completed insurance training, comparable specialist training or a degree in engineering, or A comparable qualification
  • Several years of experience in the national or international insurance industry with in-depth expertise in the processing of property damages, in particular Swiss property and business interruptions as well as major losses in the wholesale business
  • Experience in the processing of construction/energy damage is a clear advantage
  • Strong analytical, communicative and negotiating skills as well as a structured and solution-oriented working method
  • High customer and service orientation as well as pleasure in working together in an experienced, international team.
  • Very strong communication skills and excellent negotiation skills in claims settlement
  • Ability to control damage cases independently and to act safely in a dynamic, international and matrix-like organized environment
  • Experience with common claims processing systems and processes is an advantage
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in words and writing
  • Other languages are beneficial
