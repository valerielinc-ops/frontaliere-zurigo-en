Full description

New plants are assembled and commissioned by customers. The work also includes maintenance and repairs directly on site. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Install new systems and put them into operation at the customer
  • Carry out service, maintenance and repairs directly on site
  • Training customers in the operation and application of equipment
  • Build and check controls in your own electrical workshop

  • Experience with PLC controls is advantageous
  • Very good knowledge of German and driving licence category B
  • Flexibility and readiness for regular cooking services
