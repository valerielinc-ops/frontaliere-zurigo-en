Java Backend Engineer

Madrid | Working from home up to 40% | Reference 8115

We're looking for a Java Backend Engineer to join our agile and self-organized engineering team, building innovative solutions for our Repo customers. You'll be part of an exciting greenfield initiative, working with modern technologies, collaborating across multiple teams, and taking ownership of features from design through delivery. If you enjoy solving complex technical challenges, driving technical excellence, and making a real impact in a collaborative environment, we'd love to hear from you.

Apply now with your CV in English. We are looking forward to hear from you.

What You Will Do

  • Design, develop, and maintain scalable backend services and APIs for a greenfield platform using modern Java technologies.
  • Collaborate with product owners, architects, and engineers to deliver high-quality solutions aligned with business needs.
  • Integrate and coordinate with external systems and teams, working with MQ, Elasticsearch, relational databases, APIs, and OpenShift.
  • Contribute to architecture decisions, technical design discussions, and continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Ensure software quality through automated testing, code reviews, and engineering best practices.
  • Create and maintain technical documentation and architecture artifacts to support knowledge sharing and maintainability.

What You Bring

  • 3-5 years of experience developing enterprise applications with Java, Spring Boot, Hibernate/JPA, and the Spring ecosystem with experience building RESTful APIs, microservices, and integrating with external systems and services.
  • Hands-on experience with messaging technologies (MQ and/or Kafka), relational databases, Redis, and Liquibase.
  • Familiarity with Agile/Scrum methodologies, Docker, OpenShift, CI/CD pipelines, and Domain-Driven Design (DDD).
  • Solid testing mindset with experience using JUnit and a commitment to writing reliable, maintainable code.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively across teams.
  • Good level and fluency in both verbal and written English.
