Doctoral Student in Glaciology and Glacial Hazards

100%, Zurich, fixed-term

We are looking for a Doctoral Student to join the team of the Professorship of Glaciology for four years. Our group is affiliated with ETH’s Laboratory of Hydraulics, Hydrology and Glaciology (VAW) as well as the Swiss Federal Institute for Forest, Snow and Landscape Research (WSL).

Project background

The Doctoral Student is offered within the project Stability of cold hanging glaciers under climate warming in the French and Swiss Alps (ICEFAL). ICEFAL is composed of a Swiss-French consortium studying the effects of rising ice temperatures on the stability of cold hanging glaciers and their likelihood to be the source of large ice avalanches. The overarching goal of the project is to provide a data-driven method to create an index of hazard potential for all hanging glaciers in the French and Swiss Alps, with applicability beyond the study region. The index combines geometric and thermal considerations. To achieve this goal, the project combines fieldwork, state-of-the-art thermo-mechanical modeling, and regional-scale geospatial analyses.

Job description

To achieve the project goals, the successful candidate will

  • Create an inventory of hanging glaciers using manual mapping and possibly machine-learning aided approaches
  • Perform field measurements at high-elevation sites in Switzerland
  • The goal is to collect measurements of ice temperatures, which will be complemented with mass balance measurements, firn cores, and analyses of long-term ice-avalanche monitoring data
  • Leverage the collected field data as well as available meteorological data to drive a state-of-the-art thermo-mechanical model (Elmer/Ice) to investigate the past and future evolution of the temperature field of the investigated glaciers and possible repercussions on stability
  • Predict basal temperatures of hanging glaciers from approaches developed at VAW-Glaciology and by project partners, and intersect these with stability criteria to derive a hazard index for hanging glaciers in the study region

The work will be supported by postdoctoral and senior researchers of VAW-Glaciology and is based in Zurich.
