Main responsibilities

  • We are looking for a dedicated person to participate in a research project on the study of skin tissue of dogs with spontaneous and experimentally induced Cushing syndrome in cooperation with the Institute of Animal Pathology of the University of Bern.
  • Skin bioptates are available for this purpose, which are evaluated histologically and by means of modern, AI-supported digital pathology software (Visiopharm).
  • The focus is on the identification of tissue markers that are relevant for the pathogenesis of Cushing syndrome and have an influence on wound healing.
  • After a six-month training phase on site in Bern, some of the evaluations are possible regardless of location.
  • If interested, there is also the possibility of later participation in the endocrinological consultation in Zurich.
  • Previous knowledge of histology is beneficial, but not a condition.
  • Joy in working with the PC, careful laboratory work and scientific work is more important to us. Profile Completed study of veterinary medicine Enjoyment of scientific work
