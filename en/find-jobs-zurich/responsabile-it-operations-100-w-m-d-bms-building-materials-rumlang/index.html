Full description

BMS Building Materials Suisse is a wholesaler of building materials for shell and interior construction. Our group of companies includes the renowned specialists Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced experts advise, support and inspire customers from the construction industry as well as private customers at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. Close to everyday construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Quick, easy and competent.

We are looking for 1. March 2027 a practice-oriented leader for the secure, stable and economical management of our IT infrastructure in Switzerland – with responsibility for people leadership, service ownership, technical governance and transformation to stabilize today’s environment, modernize platforms, strengthen cyber resilience and drive automation.

Sounds like your next challenge? Read on and become part of our team!

Task area

In this key role, you assume overall responsibility for our IT infrastructure and operational services, lead our team across locations and languages and ensure that business and corporate requirements are translated into a clear, feasible roadmap.

  • Manage and continuously improve data center, server, storage, virtualization, network, workplace, identity, collaboration and remote access, including monitoring, patching, backup and documentation.
  • Automate recurring operational tasks and lead modernization and migration decisions for on-premises, cloud and hybrid solutions.
  • Control network and data center infrastructure (LAN/WAN/WLAN, firewalls, VPN, certificates) and operate Citrix and end-user computing platforms in a stable, secure and automated manner.
  • Major incidents lead and maintain tested disaster recovery and business continuity capabilities, including recovery testing and clear IT/OT delineation for connected resources.
  • Implement Group-wide security controls and local remediation (MFA, Privileged Access, Vulnerability Management, Hardening, Data Protection) in cooperation with Group IT and regional security.
  • Manage suppliers, contracts, licenses and budgets, conduct tenders and ensure disciplined ITSM practices with clear stakeholder communication.

Management and working methods

  • Create a culture of ownership, responsiveness and service orientation, with clear roles, standby/representation models and development plans.
  • Conduct effective reviews, 1:1 discussions and governance meetings and further develop the team through coaching and documentation.
  • Connect strategic leadership with hands-on support when incidents or complex disruptions require it.

Profile

  • university degree or higher vocational training in computer science or equivalent
  • Equal years of professional experience welcome.
  • At least 8 years of broad experience in IT infrastructure/operations, including several years leading technical teams in a multi-site organization.
  • Demonstrable responsibility for business critical infrastructure and major incidents
  • In-depth knowledge of high availability, backup/recovery, cyber security and operational risks.
  • Experience in managing external providers, carriers, contracts, licenses, budgets and tenders.
  • Pragmatic automation mentality with experience in scripting, orchestration, DevOps or infrastructure-as-code.
  • fluent English and very good knowledge of German or French (experience in both language regions is advantageous); valid driving licence and willingness to travel and assignments outside office hours.

We offer

  • a challenging, versatile and interesting activity in a good working environment, where the emphasis is on active cooperation and at the same time self-employment
  • intensive training is a program, whereby our new colleagues can benefit from product and system training within the company
  • Support our employees in their professional and personal development
  • Basic wage gross x 13 plus variable wage share
  • 5 weeks holiday (6 weeks from 50 years of age) and holiday shopping up to max. 10 days, additional benefits such as an excellently financed independent pension fund and discounts on our complete product range as well as external premium benefits
