USZ Campus

By agreement

This versatile task combines demanding clinical work in the Stroke Center and on the bed stations with the professional, conceptual further development of the Department of Occupational Therapy in Neurology. Do you like to work according to current evidence, have new ideas for neurological occupational therapy and want to implement them not only on the patient, but in the entire specialist area? Then we have the right place for you.

Your main tasks

Work as an occupational therapist

  • Treatment of inpatients with neurological disorders and neuropsychological impairments in the acute phase and early rehabilitation in the Stroke Center
  • Collaboration in a team with 11 physiotherapists:innen and 6 occupational therapists:innen

Working as a departmental expert

  • Organization and management of the Department of Neurology Occupational Therapy, incl. Tutoring of the experts: inside
  • Continuous subject-specific knowledge generation and assurance as well as further development of the faculty
  • Representation of ergotherapeutic interests in the USZ and public relations
  • Promoting interprofessional cooperation
  • Introducing innovative, evidence-based care concepts

Your profile

  • Occupational therapist: in M.Sc. with several years of professional experience in an acute institution as well as further education and training in the Department of Neurology
  • Safe handling of scientific literature, experience in the application of EBP/EBM
  • Joy of innovation and courage to question the existing and test the new
  • High commitment to teamwork and interprofessional cooperation
  • Interest in lateral leadership and networking in a professional environment

Our offer

As a departmental expert, an experienced and motivated team of 16 occupational therapists awaits you. In the daily routine of the clinic, you also work closely with a committed interprofessional team consisting of 11 physiotherapists and 6 occupational therapists. Both teams look forward to your commitment and skills. We offer you a wide range of opportunities to actively participate and help shape the further development of ergotherapeutic practice. Look forward to a great deal of creative scope for innovations and new ideas, a modern shared governance career model with clear development prospects as well as a lived interprofessional cooperation that promotes professional exchange and joint development.
