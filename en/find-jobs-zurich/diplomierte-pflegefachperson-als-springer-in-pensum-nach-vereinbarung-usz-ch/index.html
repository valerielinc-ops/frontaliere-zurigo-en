immediately or by agreement

Do you want to work in different teams and make your assignments suitable for your everyday life? In the medical field II B you support our bed stations. Together we agree on your specialist areas, your workload and your assignment times.

What are your main tasks?

  • You care for the patient and support the teams in the jointly defined task area.
  • Depending on the agreed application option, you work in internal medicine and rheumatology and/or neurology, neurosurgery and ORL or in both groups of specialist areas.
  • You bring your expertise to various stations and ensure reliable handover and maintenance documentation.

What do you bring?

  • You are a qualified nurse HF/FH or have an equivalent recognised degree and professional experience in one of the mentioned areas of expertise.
  • You work independently, responsibly and patient-oriented.
  • You can quickly find your way around new situations and enjoy working in different teams.

What can you expect from us?

  • Together with us, you choose your technical focus or combine both groups of specialist areas.
  • Half services are also possible by arrangement. Together we arrange your workload and your assignment times to suit your everyday life.
  • An employment in the monthly wage or hourly wage is possible by agreement.
  • A careful introduction to the agreed areas of application as well as further education and training opportunities support you in your work.
