Full description

Faculty of Law UZH, Dean of Studies Job opening immediately or by agreement (late as of 1 December 2026), limited in time The website of the Faculty of Law is the first point of contact for prospective students and students of all levels in all kinds of matters relating to their studies. It also provides orientation for researchers from abroad, faculty members and the wider public. In order to provide people with disabilities with equal access to the study of law and other information and communication services of the faculty, the website must be accessible. In particular, e-accessibility requires the correct formatting of outline levels and lists, meaningful link texts, alternative texts for infographics, etc. We are looking for support for implementation by the end of February 2027. The job is temporary, with approx. 110-170 hours of working time are expected. Remuneration is paid on an hourly basis. Place of work and working hours are flexibly compatible.

Tasks

Under the guidance of the expert project management, they carry out the necessary review and adaptation of the individual websites and documents. This includes:

  • Updating websites in Magnolia CMS
  • Updating Word, PDF and PowerPoint documents
  • Consultation with project management and, if necessary, with other administrative bodies
  • Web research for more complex document structures
  • Exchange with 1–2 other implementation agents for uniform implementation
  • Attendance of the course "CMS: Introduction Magnolia" of the Central Computer Science (1 and 2 December 2026, 6 hours)

Profile

We are looking for an independent and reliable personality that supports us in the realization of accessibility.

  • They work accurately, thoroughly and quickly, think logically and have a high concentration ability and a good eye for detail even in routine work.
  • They have solid basic digital skills, master MS Word (essential: handling format templates) and are motivated to acquire new knowledge and look for solutions themselves.
  • You have mastered the German and English language in writing and can carry out reformulations.
  • The competent administrative authorities contact you independently if you need clarification.
  • Knowledge of how to edit websites (especially HTML and CMS) as well as the mastery of MS PowerPoint are advantageous, but not necessary.
  • Proximity to studies, i.e. content familiarity with organizational processes, is an advantage.
  • Legal knowledge is not necessary.

We offer you an activity with an insight into the structures and working methods of the RWF, in which you have practical experience in the field of digital accessibility. Collect e-accessibility and work effectively for the equality of people with disabilities.
