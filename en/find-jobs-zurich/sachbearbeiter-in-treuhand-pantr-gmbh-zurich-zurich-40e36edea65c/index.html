Full description

For an innovative trust company with around 30 employees at four Swiss locations, we are looking for a committed reinforcement for the team in Zurich. The Zurich team has successfully specialized in supporting primarily Spanish-speaking mandates and offers an international, dynamic environment. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for you!

This is your job

  • Finance: management of financial accounts, accounts receivable, accounts payable and payroll accounts as well as active participation in financial statements and VAT accounts.
  • Mandate support: Assistance in advising national and isolated international customers (small businesses, self-employed, private customers) as well as support in starting companies and business tasks.
  • Taxation: Professional preparation of tax returns for natural and legal persons.
  • Office Management: Maintaining customer relations and taking on general administrative tasks (telephone, e-mail, reception).

This is what you bring with you

  • Training: Commercial degree, ideally already with first experience in the trust area, as well as the willingness to further professional training.
  • Language skills: Very good knowledge of Spanish (ideally at native language level) as well as stylish German (English and Portuguese are a welcome advantage).
  • Working method: A pronounced number flair, an analytical and structured working method combined with reliability, flexibility and high IT affinity.

This is what awaits you from the customer

  • Working model & technology: A modern, digital working world with flexible working models at a central location with excellent transport connections.
  • Culture: A positive working atmosphere in a committed team as well as a careful and sustainable familiarization.
  • Development: Exciting, varied activities and full cost coverage for work-related training (e.g. professional card).
