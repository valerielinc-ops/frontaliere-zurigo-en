Position Overview

Who we are Novelis is driven by its purpose of Shaping a Sustainable World Together.

As a global leader in innovative products and services and the world's largest recycler of aluminum, we partner with customers in the automotive, beverage can, aerospace and specialties industries to deliver solutions that improve the benefits of balanced lightweight aluminum.

We have a coordinated network of technically sophisticated rolling and recycling plants as well as foundries across 29 facilities in 9 countries throughout North America, South America, Europe, and Asia with 12,750 employees worldwide. The company is headquartered in Atlanta, USA.

Our customers include some of the largest and best-known brands in the world, including Coca-Cola, BMW, Jaguar Land Rover, Airbus, Boeing, Nespresso, Samsung, and others. In addition, Novelis is leading the industry with its sustainability and recycling efforts.

Responsibilities & Qualifications

What awaits you

  • You develop the production plan required to meet the service level of our customers.
  • You contribute to process improvement and optimization projects.
  • Your core responsibilities are:
  • Maintain the replenishment strategy at the defined stock level through statistical analysis.
  • Optimize delivery of the service level at the lowest possible costs
  • Close collaboration with internal and external stakeholders across the organization to improve forecast planning, understanding customer/market needs as well as contribute to process improvement and optimization projects
  • Orchestrate metal flow throughout supply chain network incorporating operational targets (i.e. capacity, inventory and cost)
  • Develop and operate tools to process scenario models and determining supply chain results

How you convince us

  • You have a university degree in Supply Chain Management, Operations Management or Industrial Engineering
  • You bring initial experience in Supply Chain and/or Operations management
  • You have strong ability to understand complex problems with advanced analytical skills.
  • You are a collaborative team player and excellent communicator.
  • You are open minded, and solution orientated.
  • Fluent communication skills in English
  • German and/or French would be a plus
  • You have proven knowledge in SAP modules (PDPS, APO DP) and capability to integrate different ERP systems, familiarity with digitalization projects.
  • Experience with new digitization, robotic automation (RPA), visualization tools (e.g. PowerBI/Tableau) and coding (e.g. Python/C++) is a plus
