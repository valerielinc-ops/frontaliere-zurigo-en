You are an organizational talent, maintain an overview even in challenging situations and attach great importance to a pronounced patient and customer orientation. Teamwork brings you joy, and you are happy to take responsibility. Then you are exactly right with us!

Their main tasks

  • Reception of patients: inside and their relatives in the department
  • Competent and communicative contact and information point for the department as well as receiving, processing and forwarding telephone calls
  • Independent performance of administrative and organisational tasks
  • Disposition of department beds in close cooperation with day-to-day responsibility
  • Coordination with other services such as hospitality, cleaning etc.
  • Responsible for ordering and maintaining medicines and consumables in cooperation with supply logistics
  • Registration, coordination and organisation of patient examinations/transports
  • Equipment management and creation / controlling of repair orders
  • Creating various lists, protocols and other documents on delegation
  • Specific assistance in care, e.g. medical and nursing services in the patient's room such as blood sampling, circulatory monitoring on delegation

Your profile

  • Completed training as a health professional with experience in acute care
  • You work independently and prioritized and have a good organizational talent
  • You are a committed, flexible and resilient personality with a high level of social competence and appreciate multi-professional cooperation
  • They take initiative, have assertive power and are resilient
  • You have very good oral and written German skills as well as good knowledge of the MS Office program
