Full description

Our setting is a special feature throughout Switzerland: Through close cooperation with the somatic departments of the University Hospital Zurich, we are able to take in patients with eating disorders who, due to their physical condition, cannot be treated in other psychosomatic and psychiatric clinics. The interdisciplinary team attaches importance to individual, comprehensive and self-sustaining therapy, treatment and care. Inpatient and in the day clinic, we attach great importance to the fact that goals, needs and needs for a positive course of therapy are discussed and implemented in personal conversations.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • are specialized in accompanying, caring for, supporting people with anorexia, bulimia and atypical eating disorders • orient us towards the reference care concept and the individual therapy plan • are involved in the therapeutic setting such as group therapies, one-on-one discussions, eating accompaniments and exposure training • perceive, ask questions, give feedback and keep up with the patient: inside out and through • work closely together in the team and are on an interdisciplinary basis at eye level • Treat each other with interest and appreciation • train RF students and are always learning themselves • Introduce you carefully and thoroughly into the specialty and everyday department Requirements • have completed training as a nurse / nurse specialist HF/FH • brings experience in psychiatry and curiosity about eating disorders • enjoy people and can engage professionally in relationship processes • Think networked, enjoy working independently and enjoy the interdisciplinary exchange • want to adhere to the clear structures of a psychotherapy station and still be flexible with individual agreements • want to work on weekdays in 3-shift operation (start early shift at 7:45) and on weekends in 12-hour shifts in order to have more free time on Saturday and Sunday • are open to new things and want to go along, support, shape We offer: The USZ • offers you very good pay, shift allowances and social benefits • can offer you a night service that corresponds to a work on Pikett on site, but is paid regularly • Support your further development and further education • contribute to your travel costs after the trial period • has favorable staff prices in the USZ restaurants • Gives you many other benefits The ZES (Center for Eating Disorders): Due to the long therapy period from 6 months to one year or more, we have the opportunity to further expand the long-term relationship with our patients even after the inpatient stay in day-to-day clinical care. The self-responsibility of the patients is strongly in the foreground. Did you hear about Kolleg:innen who worked on a ward with a focus on eating disorders and felt like "policeman:innen" because they had to constantly check whether the patients:innen had eaten enough? That will not be the case with us. You are responsible for the pre- and debriefing and we do eating accompaniments and not eating controls. One of our most important concerns is to support patients in restoring their lives to health outside the clinic. If you still doubt whether this job is really suitable for you, we will make you an offer: Just load your CV into our eRecruiting tool and we will find out together whether we can meet your expectations.
