Canton of Zurich State Chancellery Digital Administration Product Owner/in AI Basic Services 80 - 100% Are you ready to actively shape the future of the Canton of Zurich? Together with you, as the Digital Administration department of the State Chancellery, we are driving the digital transformation of the Canton of Zurich forward. Artificial intelligence (AI) offers great potential to support the administration effectively and to provide its services to the population and companies efficiently, securely and appropriately. The use of AI can relieve employees, simplify processes and increase the quality of services. At the same time, clear framework conditions, reliability and responsible handling of data, security and ethical issues are required. In administration and in particular in the Office for Informatics, the Office for Statistics and Data, the Human Resources Office and in the field of digital administration, we therefore promote the safe, responsible and value-adding use of AI. An important component is basic AI services that provide employees with concrete support in everyday work, for example in transcriptions, word processing or chatbot solutions. Are you ready to harness the potential of AI in the Canton of Zurich and thus drive the digital transformation of the Canton of Zurich? In the area of digital administration, we are looking for a product owner/in AI basic services by September or by agreement. Your tasks

  • You are responsible for the AI basic services throughout their entire life cycle at the interface of users, business and technology. Specifically, this means:
  • You assume technical application responsibility, define product goals, requirements, success criteria and control further developments of the AI basic services.
  • You control internal and external development partners professionally and ensure connectivity for specialist applications, new use cases and features.
  • You manage the portfolio of basic AI services throughout the entire life cycle, including synergies, dependencies, continued operation, replacement, decommissioning and regular evaluation of benefits, profitability and alternative solutions.
  • You ensure user-friendly communication, up-to-date documentation, technical support and advice and support the sustainable introduction and anchoring of AI basic services.
  • You lead projects for the development and operational transfer of AI basic services. Your profile
  • You bring a completed bachelor's or master's degree in business informatics, business, data science, artificial intelligence or a comparable degree program.
  • You have at least 5 years of experience in project management and/or product ownership as well as experience in the application of agile methods.
  • You bring a basic understanding of the workings, possibilities and limitations of AI systems.
  • You communicate clearly and confidently between professional requirements, technical possibilities and organizational framework conditions.
  • You assess different interests, dependencies and conflicts of objectives in a structured manner and make transparent prioritization and product decisions.
  • You bring frustration tolerance and remain capable of acting even in the event of uncertainties, conflicts of goals or complex votes. An interesting and diverse task awaits you in an interdisciplinary environment in which you come into contact with a wide range of internal positions. You help shape the digital transformation of administration and contribute to the benefit for the population and the economy, which is the focus of all our activities. We offer contemporary working conditions and a workplace in the immediate vicinity of Zurich Central Station. Are you motivated to get involved in our organization? We look forward to your online application. Pensum: 80-100 %
