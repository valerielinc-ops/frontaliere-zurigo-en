Full description

PROFILE

  • Completed technical training with further education in quality, Environmental and/or Safety management, or comparable qualification
  • Experience in Quality, Environment, Health & Safety, or Integrated Management Systems (ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and/or ISO 45001)
  • Process management expertise with the ability to optimize, analyze performance, and drive continuous improvement through risk-based thinking
  • Auditor qualification is an advantage
  • Strong communicator with a proactive, solution-oriented, and quality-focused mindset
  • Fluent in German and English

TASKS

  • Maintain and continuously improve the Integrated Management System (ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001)
  • Support the continuous improvement of business processes and management systems through process modelling, documentation management, and implementation of digital solutions (e.g., SIGNAVIO and SharePoint)
  • Conduct and support internal and external audits and assessments
  • Monitor compliance and support environmental and occupational safety programs, including chemical compliance activities
  • Assist the QEHS team with evaluations and statistics, including: monitoring and tracking of the open measures and action plans, raw data collection and key figure maintenance
  • Support cross-functional continuous improvement projects and compliance
