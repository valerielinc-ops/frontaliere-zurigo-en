Full description

We are seeking a strategic and forward-thinking Skills Strategy & AI Capability Lead, to design and implement a skills-led approach that supports the growth and transformation priorities of Zurich’s Life, Health & Bank Distribution business. You will translate business strategy and AI-enabled operating models into the technical, functional and leadership capabilities Zurich’s Life, Health & Bank Distribution business needs for the future. You will lead the development of a practical skills framework, strengthen workforce insights and embed skills across talent, learning and workforce processes.

A key part of the role will be designing capability-building approaches for new AI-enabled systems and workflows, helping employees transition from traditional task execution to effective human-AI collaboration.

What You Will Do

  • Lead Life, Health & Bank Distribution skills strategy, framework, taxonomy and implementation roadmap.
  • Translate business priorities and operating model changes into future capability requirements.
  • Identify critical skills gaps and inform workforce planning, hiring, succession, upskilling and reskilling decisions.
  • Shape technical learning for new AI-enabled systems, using simulations, active practice and learning in the flow of work.
  • Build role-based and personalised learning pathways for different technical and proficiency levels.
  • Embed skills across recruitment, performance, learning, succession, career development and internal mobility.
  • Drive adoption by influencing senior leaders and equipping managers and employees to use skills insights effectively.
  • Use Korn Ferry and other workforce data to generate actionable insights and measure capability growth, adoption and business impact.
  • Lead cross-functional delivery across business, HR, Learning & Development, technology, product and data teams.

What You Will Bring

  • Significant experience in skills strategy, strategic workforce planning, organisational development, talent, learning or transformation.
  • Experience designing and implementing skills frameworks or skills-based talent practices in a complex organisation.
  • Strong understanding of how AI, automation and digital technologies are changing work and capability requirements.
  • Expertise in designing practical, role-based learning for technical or operational transformation.
  • A successful track record of leading enterprise change and influencing senior stakeholders.
  • Strong analytical skills and the ability to turn workforce data into clear business recommendations.
  • An agile and pragmatic mindset, with the ability to navigate ambiguity, test approaches, measure outcomes and scale what works.

What Success Looks Like

  • A practical skills framework aligned with Zurich’s Life, Health & Bank Distribution strategic priorities.
  • Clear visibility of current and future capability gaps.
  • Effective learning pathways for new AI-enabled systems and workflows.
  • Skills embedded across workforce and talent decisions.
  • Measurable improvements in proficiency, internal mobility, workforce agility and business outcomes.
  • Leaders and employees confident in building, applying and deploying the capabilities required for the future.
