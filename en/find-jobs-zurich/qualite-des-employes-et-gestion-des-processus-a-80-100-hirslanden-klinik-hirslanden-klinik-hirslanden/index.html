Original description

Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Occupation by appointment

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 73157

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

YOUR TASKS

  • Maintenance and development of the quality management system
  • Planning and management of internal and accompanying external audits (ISO, SSC, AS&GS, Swissmedic)
  • Risk analysis: systematic identification, evaluation and monitoring of risks and derivation of appropriate measures
  • Promote quality and safety culture: Training and awareness of employees for quality standards and normative requirements
  • Processing of occurrence reports and monitoring of measures
  • Moderation of meetings with different stakeholders
  • Process Design & Optimization: Recording, Modeling and Continuous Improvement of Business Processes

Your profile

  • Basic commercial and/or business education (HF/FH/Bachelor)
  • Strength of communication: diplomatic skills, facilitation skills, self-assurance and a solution-oriented approach
  • Affinity for IT applications (e.g. Signavio, Cura, Roxtra, CIRS, Office range)
  • Professional experience in an interface/rod function is advantageous
  • Methodological competence: BPMN 2.0, Lean Management, SWOT analyses and ISO 9001 are advantageous
  • Structured and self-responsible working methods as well as strong teamwork

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. You work with a modern medical clinic information system and can expect extensive training and development opportunities from us. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family responsibilities. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from an equivalent financial compensation. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.
