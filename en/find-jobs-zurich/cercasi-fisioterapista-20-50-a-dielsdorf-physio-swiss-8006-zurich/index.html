Physical therapist sought: 20–50 % in Dielsdorf To complement our team, we are looking for a dedicated physiotherapist for our location in Dielsdorf (30-50%). Zurich, Zurich / Schaffhausen Cooperation Permanent employment 20–50% Job: By appointment General physiotherapy Sports medicine Musculoskeleton Contact details Employers Kinezi Sport Physiotherapy Weinbergstrasse 160 8006 Zurich Website Job title Board and job opening by arrangement. A later increase is possible depending on the development. For our location in Fitness & Racket Park Dielsdorf, we are looking for a dedicated physiotherapist at Kinezi Sport Physiotherapy. We primarily care for patients with musculoskeletal complaints as well as after injuries and operations. Our focus is on individual, active rehabilitation, from everyday life to return to sport. What to expect

  • A small team with personal exchange and short decision-making paths
  • Working independently with space for your therapeutic focuses
  • Treatments at 30-minute intervals
  • An existing patient base and the ability to gradually build your workload
  • Working days by agreement
  • A workplace in the sporting environment of the Fitness & Racket Park

What you bring

  • A Swiss physiotherapy degree or a foreign degree with SRK recognition
  • Interest in musculoskeletal physiotherapy and active rehabilitation
  • An independent, reliable working method and pleasure in personal patient contact
  • Good knowledge of German for treatment and documentation

Further training in sports physiotherapy is welcome, but not a requirement. If you are interested, it is possible to financially support a corresponding further training. New entrants are also cordially invited to apply. Interested? We look forward to your application with a curriculum vitae and a short indication of your desired workload, your possible working days and your early job start. Xavier Delannoy Kinezi Sport Physiotherapy Ruchwiesenstrasse 5, 8157 Dielsdorf 📧 [email protected] www.kinezi.ch Your employer Kinezi Sport Physiotherapy Kinezi Sport Physiotherapy is a modern physiotherapy centre with locations in Zurich (Schaffhauserplatz) and Dielsdorf (Fitness & Racket Park). Our focus is on musculoskeletal and sports physiotherapeutic treatment. We work in an evidence-based, patient-centered and interdisciplinary manner – with the aim of accompanying each patient individually and holistically on the path to recovery. Our philosophy is based on active rehabilitation, functional training and close cooperation with doctors, trainers and health professionals.
