Full description

Your tasks

  • Your ideas make the difference: help shape the BKW Building Solutions Academy and develop our competence center for learning and development.
  • You will recognize strategic skills and development needs and translate them into effective learning and development opportunities.
  • Together with the Academy team, you will develop learning formats that are practical, inspire and enable a sustainable transfer into everyday work.
  • You make sure that our offers are easily accessible, target groups and effective.
  • You help shape development tools and processes such as talent and performance management and develop them further.
  • You drive the digitalization of the learning and development landscape forward and accompany the introduction and further development of an LMS.
  • You work together with a wide range of stakeholders across different departments and contribute to the development of people and organisations together.

You bring that with you

  • Experience in personnel development, adult education or learning & development with corresponding further education.
  • You recognize development needs and understand how to derive effective and practical solutions from them.
  • You like to move between strategy and implementation and bring ideas with the necessary portion of pragmatism to the goal.
  • Working with a wide variety of people makes you happy – from employees on the construction site to management.
  • You take responsibility, work independently and actively advance topics.
  • You value an uncomplicated working environment with little hierarchy, a lot of creative freedom and colleagues: women who do not lose their sense of humor even in demanding situations.
