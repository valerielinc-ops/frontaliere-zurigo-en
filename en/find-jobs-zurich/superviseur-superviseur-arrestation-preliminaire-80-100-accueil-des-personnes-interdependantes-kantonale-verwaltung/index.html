  • Newcomers welcome Corrections and Reintegration (JuWe) is part of the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs of the Canton of Zurich and employs around 1,400 women and men from a variety of professions. The JuWe is primarily responsible for the execution of court judgments and the implementation of pre-trial detention. For JuWe employees, the prevention of relapses and the successful social reintegration of people who have committed criminal offenses are of particular importance. At Zurich West Prison, we look after people in one of the most challenging phases of their lives: immediately after their arrest. As a supervisor / supervisor
  • temporary arrest, you take responsibility for security, order and care and thus make a valuable contribution to society.   • the first point of contact in the Zurich West Prison (GZW): At the GZW we work in shifts 365 days a year. In the Preliminary Arrest Department, you are the first point of contact for detainees after their arrest and ensure that safety, support and structure are guaranteed from the start.
  • You accompany prisoners in particularly stressful situations such as prison shock, suicidality or after personal crises, and organize their daily routine within clearly defined structures
  • You monitor all security-related processes; from admitting new people to cell checks and operating technical systems

  • In crisis situations, you react competently and responsibly and independently take responsibility for several departments during night shifts

  • Written documentation and reporting are just as much a part of your everyday life as communicating the house rules and enforcing applicable regulations. This is what you bring with you
