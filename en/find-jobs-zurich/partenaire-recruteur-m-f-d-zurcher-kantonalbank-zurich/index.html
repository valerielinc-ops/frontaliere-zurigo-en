Responsible, passionate, inspiring – these are the values we stand for at the Zürcher Kantonalbank. Together with around 100 committed and ambitious HR colleagues, we are further developing the future of Human Resources in our bank. Thanks to exciting projects and a growing need for recruitment, especially in the IT sector, we are further expanding our team. This post shall be limited to a maximum of one year.

Your tasks

  • You accompany the entire recruiting life cycle, from tendering to interviews to hiring professionally and goal-oriented
  • You advise your internal clients in partnership on all topics related to talent acquisition and develop effective recruiting strategies
  • For the talents you create an unmistakable and positive candidate experience by accompanying them appreciatively and competently on their way to us
  • In the team, you actively participate in the optimization of our recruiting (instruments, processes) and also have many opportunities to contribute your ideas to interdisciplinary HR projects.
  • Vocational training completed
  • Operational experience in recruiting, ideally in the IT, financial or banking environment
  • Your professional curiosity ensures that you are fit in current recruitment methods and actively deal with upcoming trends and technologies
  • Convincing consulting and communication skills towards different target groups of all hierarchical levels
  • Positive, solution-oriented mindset that reflects the values of our bank, "passionate, stimulating, responsible"
  • Very good oral and written German and good English skills
  • You get deep insights into our bank and get to know our diversity up close
  • An easily accessible, attractive workplace at Hardbrücke station can be combined with home office and remote work
  • You work with open, empathetic and humorous professionals who are looking forward to you
  • As the Bank of Zurich, we ensure that many social, cultural and economic initiatives take place in Zurich - you make a valuable and valued contribution to this.
  • You will become part of our success story in the Swiss financial world and build the bank of tomorrow with us
