Full description

Telemedicine is no longer a vision of the future – it is everyday life. And santé24 is one of the established players in Switzerland. With a large interdisciplinary offer in the areas of prevention, lifestyle (change) and patient support, we complement our basic medical care. At santé24, the online practice of SWICA, you make diagnoses, treat and advise insured persons with real general medical concerns: telephone, structured, with clinical depth. You work in plannable shifts and always know when your shift ends reliably. You are embedded in an interdisciplinary team of healthcare professionals and an interdisciplinary medical team – without your own practice risk, without patient administration on your own account. This is how you shape health

  • Telephone consultations: diagnosis, therapy, triage – according to internal guidelines
  • Prescriptions, UP certificates, physiotherapy prescriptions, laboratory arrangements
  • Digital documentation in the patient information system (always in German)
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with internal specialists from nursing, pharmacy, psychology, nutritional advice, exercise coaching, physiotherapy, etc.
  • Shift operation: 4-, 6- or 8-hour shifts, also on weekends
  • Approximately 2 mandatory days of presence per month at the location, remaining days in the home office (after a four-week training period on site in Winterthur and/or Basel)
  • More exciting information and a video about working as a doctor at santé24 can be found on our career page

This sets you apart

  • Experience in general or internal medicine
  • FMH General Internal Medicine prefers – other specialist titles welcomed with high general medical advice security (e.g. practitioner, orthopaedics, rheumatology, gynecology, paediatric and adolescent medicine, emergency medicine, dermatology)
  • Safe handling and enjoyment of digital work equipment
  • German and English at C1 level; other national languages at C1 level are beneficial

Why SWICA

  • We build on humanity. That is why we focus on openness, respect and trust in our everyday work. We take on responsibility and think in terms of solutions – together, for our customers. This is also why we have been number 1 in customer satisfaction for many years.
  • We trust you and give you space. With annual working hours, at least 5 weeks holidays and on request also holiday purchase. With part-time models and generous home office within the scope of the possibilities of the job, you make your everyday life largely flexible.
  • We value and reward your work – with a market-based wage, up to 1,500 francs annual fee for personal wishes and a generous participation in the basic and additional insurance for you and your family.
  • We offer you prospects. With a generous training regulations, we support your professional and personal development – for your role today and for your goals of tomorrow.
  • Because health is everything. With our company health management and region-specific offers, we take care of your well-being. And if it comes otherwise: 100% net wage continued payment.
  • We think long term. With three savings plans, pensum-dependent coordination deduction and benefits well above the legal minimum, you ensure good for your future.
