Full description

  • The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich treats and cares for mentally ill people of all ages. The more than 2,500 employees from 55 different professional profiles work in interdisciplinary teams to contribute to the holistic care of patients, from assessment to therapy to aftercare. Our work is based on mutual trust and personal responsibility and together we promote an innovative corporate culture. As a university clinic, we are successfully involved in national and international research projects. In child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy (KJPP) in Männedorf, children aged 5 to 15 years with mental disorders are treated and cared for by an interdisciplinary team in two wards and a day clinic in Männedorf in a psychiatric-psychotherapeutic and milieu therapy-oriented setting. The “Brüschhalde” center for child psychiatry at the Männedorf location has a modern infrastructure with a spacious outdoor area. It is located in the local recreation area between the Pfannenstiel region and Lake Zurich. The treatment offering for our young patients includes a ward for short interventions and therapies and three psychotherapy stations with different treatment focuses. There are eight treatment places available on each ward.
