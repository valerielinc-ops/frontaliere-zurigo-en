Location: Hirslanden Clinic in the Park | Zurich

Occupation by: From April 2027

Type of employment: Temporary

Reference number: 65554

The Klinik im Park Zürich stands for high-quality medical care in a personal atmosphere. Embedded in an interprofessional environment and equipped with the latest infrastructure, we offer our patients care at the highest level. Now we are looking for committed personalities who would like to become part of our dynamic team in the intensive care unit as part of the two-year part-time training course for NDS HF.

Are you ready to take responsibility and constantly develop your expertise? We look forward to meeting you!

YOUR TASKS

  • During the two-year professional development, you will be part of our interdisciplinary team in the intensive care unit
  • You will complete the theoretical training at the Zurich Training Center for Intensive, Emergency Anesthesia Care (Z-INA)
  • You will attend two semesters of further education at our cooperation partner, Klinik Hirslanden, in order to obtain a broad spectrum of learning

Your profile

  • Training for Dipl. Nursing specialist or a diploma in health and nursing recognised in Switzerland
  • At least one year of professional experience in a Swiss acute care hospital
  • High motivation and strong commitment to the two-year degree programme
  • Empathy in dealing with intensive care patients and their relatives
  • Willingness to develop your expertise and make an important contribution to the success of our intensive care unit

Theoretical lessons take place at the Z-INA (Zurich Training Center for Intensive Care, Emergency Care and Anesthesia Care). You can expect a motivated, committed and multi-professional team in an advanced working environment – characterized by high care quality, independence, flexibility and a sense of responsibility. Through your interdisciplinary work you have the opportunity to expand your professional network.
