Original description

At ABB, we help industries run leaner and cleaner—and every person here makes that happen. You’ll be empowered to lead, supported to grow, and proud of the impact we create together. Join us and help run what runs the world.

This position reports to:

Strategy Manager

__

Do you enjoy translating data into useful insights that support strategic decision-making? Join our team as an Electrification Business & Market Intelligence Analyst, where you’ll analyze market trends, provide competitor insights, and uncover growth opportunities around the world. You’ll collaborate with the global leadership team to shape strategic initiatives and ensure market transparency.

The work model for the role is: #LI-hybrid or #LI-remote

This role is contributing to Electrification Business Area Strategy, Business & Market Research team

You will be mainly accountable for:

  • Market intelligence processes: Improve existing and define new scalable processes for collecting, validating, and synthesizing market insights from a global sales organization, incl. encouraging adoption and developing reporting frameworks and dashboards for senior leadership

  • Market & Competitor Intelligence: Track and evaluate global market trends, uncovering gaps and opportunities for growth. Deliver compelling analyses to guide management decisions and fuel business growth.

  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Work closely with internal stakeholders and external suppliers to ensure the high-quality delivery and integration of business intelligence into decision-making processes

  • Data-Driven Strategy: Collect and analyze market, competitor, and financial data to provide high-quality insights that support key business decisions and strategic planning.

  • Innovation in Tools & Analytics: System administration of Business Intelligence Tools in Electrification, developing and enhancing business intelligence tools, such as Power BI, and support marketing teams globally by ensuring optimal use of sales tracking systems and data quality.

  • Continuous Improvement: Propose and apply enhancements to business analysis tools so the organization can collect, interpret, and use intelligence more effectively

• Qualifications for the role:

  • 5–7+ years of experience in market intelligence, business insights, or related functions.

  • Demonstrated experience building intelligence and designing processes that scale across large organizations and working effectively in multinational organizations with geographically dispersed teams.

  • Demonstrated skill in stakeholder management, including work alongside commercial leaders across regions and business units

  • Solid analytical capabilities, including experience with data analysis, visualization, and insight generation, with a talent for identifying trends and making strategic recommendations

  • Clear, concise communication and engaging storytelling capabilities

  • Ability to combine strategic thinking with practical execution

  • Proficiency in business intelligence tools like MS Office Suite, Anaplan, Power BI, ThinkCell and other market research platforms

  • A results-focused attitude with a commitment to continuous improvement.

  • Experience with market intelligence, analytics, or business intelligence tools.

  • Knowledge of AI-enabled research, text analytics, or insight-generation tools is a strong plus.

  • For Switzerland location: candidates must bel eligible to work in Switzerland. Visa sponsorship is not offered for this position.

Why You’ll Enjoy Working Here

  • You will join a collaborative team, where you will be able to thrive.

  • Innovative Environment: Work with up-to-date tools and technologies to support meaningful business improvements

  • Global Collaboration: Collaborate with teams from across the globe, ensuring diverse perspectives and a global mindset in everything you do.

  • Professional Growth: Be part of a forward-thinking team that encourages continuous learning and development, with opportunities to grow within the organization.
