Description

Do you want to further develop your legal career in a dynamic financial industry environment? Are you looking for a responsible, varied legal role with direct business relevance and a high learning curve? If so, you are the person we are looking for.

BANK-now is a UBS Group company and is one of the leading providers of credit and leasing solutions in Switzerland. With tailor-made financing products, BANK-now offers a dynamic environment at the interface of financial services, technology and regulation.

We are looking for a Legal Counsel BANK-now to support the team in the following areas:

  • Cooperation in the management of a broad range of legal and regulatory issues for BANK-now
  • Examination, analysis and participation in contracts and support in contract negotiations
  • examination and analysis of civil law, fairness law, competition law and data protection law issues as well as support in the settlement and preparation of civil and criminal proceedings,
  • Support in the implementation of legal and regulatory innovations (especially in consumer credit law)
  • Cooperation with internal committees and projects to ensure corporate governance and regulatory compliance
  • Legal support for BANK-now departments and cooperation with relevant stakeholders within UBS
  • Participation in cross-divisional projects, especially in the context of digitalization and further development of the business model
