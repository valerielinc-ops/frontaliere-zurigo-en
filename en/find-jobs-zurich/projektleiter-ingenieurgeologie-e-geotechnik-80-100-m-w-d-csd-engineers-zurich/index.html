Full description

More than 1000 employees at 30 locations in Switzerland and Europe: The CSD Group is a dynamic, constantly growing engineering company. Inventiveness, competence and multidisciplinarity characterize our teams, optimal solutions for our customers with a plus for the quality of life and the environment are our trademark. Do you enjoy working in a professional and agile environment and attach great importance to quality and efficiency? Become part of our success! We are looking for a specialist in engineering geology and geoengineering as project manager 80-100% (m/f/d).

Your responsibilities

  • Managing and working on projects in the fields of engineering geology and geoengineering in urban areas: from conceptual design to the execution of investigations with reporting to technical construction support
  • The projects in the urban environment offer a wide range of questions in the areas of building soil, excavation, foundations, water management, installations in groundwater, leaching, contaminated excavation, geothermal uses and at best also natural hazards.
  • In case of suitability and interest: familiarisation with other areas of expertise or management tasks

Your profile

  • You have successfully completed your studies as a geologist, earth scientist or geotechnician
  • Conceptual, solution-oriented thinking and reporting are among your strengths
  • They enjoy working out economic solutions and implementing them. Assessments and decisions for operational solution implementation on the construction site are easy for you
  • At least 3 years of professional experience in such a position
  • In German you can express yourself orally and in writing
  • You enjoy working with customers and entrepreneurs as well as in an interdisciplinary team

We offer

  • Exciting tasks in projects in the urban area in the area of tension between structural densification, relocation of infrastructure to the underground, measures to improve the microclimate in the settlement area and the transport infrastructure in the greater Zurich area or beyond
  • Integration into the CSD network of various departments
  • Professional and personal development
  • A dynamic environment and a respectful approach
  • Flexible conditions for optimal work and leisure

We look forward to your application! Offers from recruitment agencies are not considered in this recruitment. CSD will therefore in no way negotiate the payment of claims.
