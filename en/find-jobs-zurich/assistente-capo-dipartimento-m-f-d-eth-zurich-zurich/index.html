Assistant Head of Department (m/f/d)

60%-80%, Zurich, permanent

ETH Zurich is oriented towards the highest international standards in teaching, research and administration. The Campus Services department supports teaching, research and administration in the areas of logistics, mobility and events. It also ensures comprehensive information management and orientation at ETH Zurich and is responsible for the catering and retail services.

For our department management we are looking for 1. 2026 or by appointment an experienced, independent and trustworthy person who supports our Head of Campus Services and the management team in the demanding day-to-day business. In this key role, you hold many threads together, recognize the need for action at an early stage and create added value where support is needed. You are not only thinking along, but ahead.

Job description

You support the Head of Campus Services and the management team in administrative, organizational and coordination tasks and ensure that processes function efficiently and smoothly. You coordinate and organize appointments, workshops, meetings and events, prepare documents and keep an overview of parallel topics.

As a central contact person, you move in a dynamic environment with different stakeholders and sensitive topics. You gain insight into strategic, personnel and organizational issues and contribute significantly to the success of the management team with your discretion, judgment and high level of professionalism.

In addition to classical management assistance, you act as a proactive hub within the department. You recognize potential for optimization, bring your own ideas and take responsibility for the further development of processes and working methods. The organization and follow-up of events, the support of personnel processes from entry to exit as well as the support of new employees during onboarding are just as much a part of your task as the coordination of IT orders and workplace preparations.

In addition, you actively shape internal communication, maintain and develop digital information platforms and ensure that information is prepared and accessible to target groups. You take on coordination tasks in projects and contribute your affinity for digitalization and modern technologies.

Profile

  • Several years of experience in a demanding assistance, coordination or administration function, ideally in the environment of management committees or management teams
  • High level of independence as well as the ability to set priorities on your own responsibility and to keep an overview even in complex situations
  • Strong organisational skills, networked thinking and a look beyond the box
  • Proactive, solution-oriented personality with a high level of initiative and a strong understanding of services
  • Sovereign and absolutely discreet handling of confidential, sensitive and personal information
  • High digital affinity and interest in new technologies, digitalization and artificial intelligence
  • Very good user knowledge in Microsoft 365, SharePoint and modern digital work tools
  • Style-confident knowledge of German as well as very good knowledge of English in word and writing
  • Strength of communication, diplomatic skills and enjoyment of working with a wide range of stakeholders
  • Structured, reliable and quality-conscious working methods as well as a high sense of responsibility
  • Mature personality with strong judgment and professionalism

Those who take responsibility, think and actively shape change will find an environment where ideas are welcome and initiative is appreciated.

In short, you are much more than an administrative support. You are a sparring partner: in, organizational hub and a trustworthy support for the department management and the entire management team.
