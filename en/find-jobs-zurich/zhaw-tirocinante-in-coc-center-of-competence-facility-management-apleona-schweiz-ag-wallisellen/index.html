You want to put theory into practice and gain real insight into the world of facility management? Then you are exactly right at Apleona Schweiz AG. Start now as an intern:in. The main place of work will be the Center of Competence (CoC) department in Wallisellen ZH. The CoC department is commissioned internally by various customer and innovation projects

  • These reflect the full range of services offered by Apleona Switzerland and therefore require a fast understanding, high customer orientation, flexibility and anticipation. The department also includes the departments Quality & Environment and Occupational Safety & Health Protection.
