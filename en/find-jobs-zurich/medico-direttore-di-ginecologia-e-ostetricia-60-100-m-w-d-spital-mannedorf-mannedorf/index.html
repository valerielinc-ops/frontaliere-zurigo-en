Full description

Your very special workplace. The hospitals of Uster and Männedorf have merged their gynecology and obstetrics clinics into the Women's Clinic Züri Ost. The cooperation aims to strengthen medical care in the region and create sustainable framework conditions. With the Women's Clinic Züri Ost, inpatient and outpatient care is ensured for a catchment area of around 300,000 inhabitants in an interconnected care system.

  • Responsibilities
  • Involvement in the management and leadership of the Women's Clinic Züri Ost
  • Active participation in the development of the team at the Women's Clinic Züri Ost to ensure optimal and safe care
  • Treatment and care of inpatients and outpatients in the delivery room, operating theater and outpatient clinic, including emergency patients
  • Participation in the development of subdisciplines such as senology, minimally invasive techniques including robotics or prenatal diagnostics
  • Collaboration with referrers and development of regional partnerships
  • Ensuring the training and supervision of colleagues in training to become specialists in gynecology and obstetrics
  • Requirements
  • Specialist degree in gynecology and obstetrics
  • Knowledge in the fields of urogynaecology, endometriosis and advanced laparoscopic surgery is desirable
  • Leadership personality with solution-oriented thinking, flexibility, team ability and high performance readiness
  • Interest and commitment to the further development of the Women's Clinic Züri Ost
  • Enjoyment in the training of young colleagues Reasons for your decision The Women's Clinic Züri Ost is a cross-site cooperation model that sets a benchmark for modern structures in Switzerland. The position offers the opportunity to influence a clinic in a crucial development phase. For the further development of medical focal points, the institutions provide the best available infrastructure (including two DaVinci systems). Moreover, the region is characterized by an extremely attractive location with high leisure value and excellent quality of life. Questions about living in Switzerland?
