Full description

80% - 100% | Risk | Zurich | Experienced professionals The private banking of the Zürcher Kantonalbank specializes in the support of wealthy private customers at home and abroad, family offices, foundations and external asset managers with tailor-made solutions. In an environment with demanding regulatory requirements, you make a central contribution to a stable and sustainable business model. The Business Risk Management team is responsible for the active management of non-financial risks as part of the 1st Line of Defense of Private Banking. You work closely with sales and specialists and contribute your know-how to risk-relevant decisions and concept developments. Your commitment thus significantly shapes the further development of risk management in private banking. Your tasks

  • Further develop and implement risk management for compliance issues (and other non-financial risks) in private banking
  • Identify, assess and control compliance risks as well as derive and track actions
  • Description, planning, implementation and documentation of BRM mandate controls and ensuring an effective control environment
  • Advising sales and other stakeholders on risk-relevant issues and promoting a strong risk culture
  • Analyze and process risk events, derive sustainable mitigation measures including preparation of learnings and recommendations
  • Representing the Private Banking business unit in selected projects, working groups and committees on compliance issues
  • Close cooperation with compliance, operational risk, process managers, data analytics and other interfaces

Your profile

  • Completed studies at (specialist) college or university level, ideally in business, law or a related field
  • Further training in compliance/non-financial risk management
  • Several years of well-founded practice in dealing with VSB, KYC and GwG topics, preferably in private banking or in an audit firm with a focus in this area
  • Know-how in sales, processes and risks as well as familiarity with regulatory requirements in the banking environment
  • Strong analytical and conceptual skills as well as experience in setting up and optimizing risk and control frameworks
  • High affinity for data and technologies as well as the ability to use them specifically for analyses and decisions in risk management
  • You convince with a structured, self-responsible working method, high quality standards and enjoyment of cross-divisional cooperation
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing; further language skills, especially French and English, are advantageous

Our offer

  • Mutual support and team spirit are important to us
  • Exciting and diverse range of tasks with many network possibilities
  • We offer you space for your ideas and thus design the bank of tomorrow
  • Your career is important to us as an employer and therefore we support and accompany you on your way
  • Central workplace in our headquarters on Bahnhofstrasse with good transport connections
  • Comprehensive package with flexible working hours, mobile work, work-life balance and other benefits
