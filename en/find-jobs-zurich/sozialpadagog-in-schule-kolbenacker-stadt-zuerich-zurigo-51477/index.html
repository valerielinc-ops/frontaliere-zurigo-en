Full description

For the expansion of the team, a social pedagogue is looking for immediately or by agreement in the function of hoard management for lunch / evening hoard Eichrain 1, school Kolbenacker, 60% (later 80% possible).

Tasks

  • You lead the care team as a hoard management in everyday life and keep a cool head even in challenging situations
  • Care of students in the midday / evening hoard Kolbenacker C
  • They plan, organize and lead together with the hoarding team the extracurricular care offer (teaching support social and leisure pedagogical activities)
  • Implementation of the educational objectives of the school Kolbenacker
  • They do the workload at the beginning at 60% 4 days per week, late. As of August 27 at 80% on 5 days and work in a team of 5
  • In the catering area and in the care you will be supported by three care assistants

Profile

  • degree as a social pedagogue FH or HFS; Social worker FH or HFS; University degree with major in social education, pedagogy or educational sciences
  • For applications with a foreign diploma / certificate, a Swiss diploma recognition must be attached
  • Motivated, open, resilient personality with a positive human image
  • Flexibility as well as very good team and communication skills
  • Joy in dealing with people of different cultures and willingness for interdisciplinary cooperation in the school team
  • Professional experience in supplementary family care is beneficial

We offer

  • A varied, versatile and independent activity
  • A multicultural, exciting environment
  • Competent support from the management
  • Cooperative and appreciative cooperation in the school team and in parental cooperation
  • Progressive salary and social benefits according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich (Horte)

About us

The city of Zurich is divided into 7 school circles. The Zurich-Glattal school district in Zurich-Nord comprises the districts of Affoltern, Seebach and Oerlikon and counts around 6,500 school-aged children. Since 2002, all our 18 schools have been run. All hoards are assigned to a school unit and are managed by a management.

The Eichrain 1 lunch / evening hoard belongs to the Kolbenacker school. In this shelter, up to 50 pupils from kindergarten to lower school age are fed and looked after every day. The nursery is open from Monday to Friday from 11.55 a.m. to 6 p.m.
