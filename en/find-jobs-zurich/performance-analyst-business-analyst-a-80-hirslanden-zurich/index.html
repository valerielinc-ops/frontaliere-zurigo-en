Place of work: Hirslanden Clinic Hirslanden | Zurich Occupancy: immediately or by appointment Type of employment: Permanent Reference number: 71246 Are you looking for an exciting challenge in which you can optimally use your specialist knowledge in the healthcare sector and your business management expertise? As a performance analyst in our clinic, you will play a key role in the analysis, optimization and strategic development of our processes and procedures. YOUR TASKS

  • Carrying out comprehensive analyzes and controlling tasks (e.g. Clinic KPIs, capacity management, individual case analyzes) and their systematic further development
  • Participation in the further development and optimization of capacity models to forecast capacity and resource requirements as well as to improve allocation and planning accuracy
  • Taking on administrative tasks in the area of performance management and preparing regular performance reports to support data-based management decisions
  • Monitoring and evaluating planning and operational efficiency and effectiveness
  • Identifying potential for improvement as well as modeling and developing measures to improve performance (performance Improvement)
  • Collaboration with various departments to analyze and optimize the planning patient path in order to increase the efficiency and quality of patient care (process optimization)
  • Support and training of employees with regard to the use of analysis tools and methods to improve data quality and use YOUR PROFILE
  • Business management training ideally with a corresponding training portfolio and experience in health and hospital management as well as controlling knowledge and the willingness to develop this further
  • Several years of professional experience in a comparable function, preferably at specialist expert level
  • Knowledge of the structures and processes in the hospital sector as well as the relevant regulatory requirements (e.g. SwissDRG, SPLG)
  • Familiarity with common analysis tools and software as well as experience in dealing with large amounts of data (e.g. BIC3)
  • Very experienced use of Excel, SAP, MCC
  • Programming knowledge (e.g. Python. You will work with a modern medical clinic information system and can expect extensive further training and development opportunities from us.
