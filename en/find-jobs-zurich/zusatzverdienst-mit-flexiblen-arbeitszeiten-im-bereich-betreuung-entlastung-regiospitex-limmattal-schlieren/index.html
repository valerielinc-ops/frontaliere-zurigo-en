Additional earnings with flexible working hours in the area of care + discharge

  • Walking and companionship

  • Discharge in and around the house (household & gardening)

Your skills

  • Very good German and Swiss German skills in word and writing
  • Joy in dealing with people and good manners
  • Reliable, responsible and independent work
  • Flexible availability, resilience
  • SRK certificate is beneficial
  • Driving license for cars, own car

We offer you

  • A working environment characterized by respect and appreciation
  • Flexible work planning, modern infrastructure, professional management
  • You choose yourself which bets you want to take on and do not have to take on bets that do not correspond to you
  • Du-culture at all levels
