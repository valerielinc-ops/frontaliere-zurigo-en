What you do with us

You work in our Luxembourgerli department in our manufacturer in Dietikon and support the team in the production of our outstanding and popular Luxembourgerli. Your working hours are between 4 a.m. and 4 p.m. and take place between Monday and Friday. Due to the season, operations must be possible on Saturday.

Your tasks

  • Production of our leading product Luxemburgerli in a team
  • Production of various fillings, jelly varieties, creams and protein masses
  • Installation, preparation and operation of production machinery
  • Grinding and rubbing of the various nuts
  • Compliance with and control of safety, hygiene and quality requirements

What we want

  • Multiannual activity in a food processing function
  • A good understanding of machine controls, ideally first experience with PLC, as well as knowledge in the operation of systems
  • An exact, quality-conscious and independent working method as well as resilience even in intense situations
  • Mobility and flexibility
  • with craftsmanship, as well as a good physical condition
  • PC knowledge (MS Office) is an advantage
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Readiness for shift work
