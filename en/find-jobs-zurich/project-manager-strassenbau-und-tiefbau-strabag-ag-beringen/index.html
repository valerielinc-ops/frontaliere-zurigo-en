Full description

At STRABAG, around 89,000 people at more than 2,400 locations worldwide are building progress. Uniqueness and individual strengths characterise not only our projects but also each and every one of us. Whether in building and civil engineering, road and civil engineering, bridge and tunnel construction, building materials production, project development or building management – we think construction forward to become the most innovative and sustainable construction technology group in Europe. Equal opportunities, diversity and inclusion are integral parts of what defines us as a company and how we work. Together we implement projects successfully and in partnership and grow with new tasks. Together we create great things. Let's progress!

Project Manager: Road and Civil Engineering Your Contribution with Us

  • You manage your road and civil engineering projects from work preparation to final billing
  • You lead your site managers and foremen and manage several construction sites in parallel
  • You are responsible for costs, deadlines, quality and safety of your projects
  • You calculate, prepare estimates and negotiate with clients, subcontractors and suppliers
  • You manage change orders, measurements and the NPK billing
  • You coordinate with clients, works and authorities
  • You plan the deployment of personnel, equipment and materials and accompany the execution until handover

What Counts for Us

  • You have several years of experience as a site manager or project manager in road and civil engineering
  • You have independently handled your own projects and managed several construction sites in parallel
  • You are a certified site manager or civil engineer or have an equivalent further education in construction
  • You lead your team and coordinate with clients, planners and site operators
  • Calculation, estimates, NPK billing and change order management are familiar to you
  • You make decisions independently and negotiate confidently
  • You communicate fluently in German

Our Added Value for You

  • Visible projects that you are responsible for from start to finish
  • Company car including insurance and fuel card, also for private use
  • Further training and individual development for your next career step
  • Competitive remuneration and employee discounts
  • Work-life balance
  • Health promotion, sports offers and employee events
  • Free drinks, parking and good transport connections
