Full description

In our R&D group, we develop hearing solutions and services for hearing impaired people and hearing care professionals in the form of hearing instruments, accessories, web services, mobile applications and PC software. From the concept to the deployment phase, you will be engaged in contributing to highly flexible and reliable software and firmware solutions.

As a Software Engineer DSP - Tools & Infrastructure, you will drive the design, implementation and maintenance of SW tools for embedded audio signal processing. These tools enable model-based functional development, and embedded application-level development. They address the needs arising in embedded software development for a multi-processor embedded system dedicated to signal- and neural-network processing.

More about the role • Design and evolve software development tools that enable model-based and embedded functional software development for signal- and neural-network processing • Collaborate with stakeholders to gather, analyze, and refine requirements for design and implementation of software development tools • Actively support the communication and collaboration between the teams that develop the tools and the teams that use the tools • Drive software engineering best practices through design reviews, technical guidelines, and continuous improvement • Contribute to architectural qualities in support of conceptual integrity, modularity, maintainability and extensibility

More about you • University degree (MSc/BSc) or equivalent in computer science, electrical engineering or related technical field • 4+ years of experience in the development, deployment and maintenance of embedded software systems • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills • Proficiency in Python • Experience with Matlab & Simulink • Knowledge of one (or more) of the following technologies: C/C++, Docker, CI/CD (e.g. Jenkins), Bash • Good understanding of signal processing development, knowledge on Neural Network development is a plus • Experience with real-time signal processing systems is a plus • Collaborative mindset with a customer and service-oriented approach • Fluent skills in English

  • German is a plus
