Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With over 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric services offered by Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in outpatient, day clinic and inpatient settings. The group employs around 1800 people and generated sales of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For our psychotherapy ward A0 with a treatment focus on depression, we are looking for a

The ward specialises in the treatment of people with depressive symptoms. The therapeutic work is based on a multidimensional understanding of depression. The psychotherapeutic and pharmacological treatment is based on the latest knowledge and focuses on integrating CBASP and ACT in a modular manner. In addition, complementary medical services are used. The maisonette ward, which is usually open, has 21 treatment places.

Your tasks

  • the individual care and support of patients and contact with relatives as part of the caregiver work and in consultation with the core team
  • Responsibility for the development of nursing and review of the quality of care on the ward
  • Participation in subject-specific working groups and projects
  • Ensuring cooperative interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Leading one-on-one meetings, group offers and reports
  • Consultation and planning of treatment with the responsible doctors and therapists
  • Supporting patients in the activities of daily life and accompanying them in crisis situations

Your profile For this exciting job, you have a degree in nursing at HF / FH level and professional experience in the field of psychiatric nursing. If you appreciate the therapeutic concepts CBASP and ACT and would like to accompany people in inpatient treatment in close interprofessional cooperation, then read on. Are you an open, communicative and present personality who thinks and acts in a recovery-oriented way and are you willing to continuously educate yourself and pass this knowledge on to the nursing team? Then you are the person we are looking for!
