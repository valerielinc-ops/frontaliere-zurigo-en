yellowshark® has been a reliable and sustainable partner in staffing services and career consulting since 2009. Both the candidate and the client are always at the center of attention. This focus on companies and employees results in a dynamic service company that brings interesting candidates together with exciting companies. Our motivated team has extensive knowledge of the market requirements in the specialist areas of commercial administration, IT, engineering and construction. Thanks to innovative technologies, a solid network throughout Switzerland and the strategically important location in Zurich's economic center, yellowshark® operates dynamically, close to the market and sustainably.

To strengthen our construction division, we are looking for an experienced and ambitious personality with a flair for sales, starting immediately.
