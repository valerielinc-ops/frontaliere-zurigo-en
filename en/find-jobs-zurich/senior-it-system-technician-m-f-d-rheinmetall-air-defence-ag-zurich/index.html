Full description

WHERE WE LOOK FOR YOU Are you looking for a challenging task with technical depth, international environment and a lot of personal responsibility? Then you are exactly right with us. As an air defense system technician, you support our customers on site in the country of deployment. Its tasks include:

  • Technical use at the customer
  • Carrying out maintenance and maintenance work on the anti-aircraft system
  • Error analysis and repair directly on site in cooperation with the customer
  • Technical training and support of operators
  • Close cooperation with the location in Switzerland – especially in the reporting of customer needs, system observations and optimization proposals
  • Documentation of technical work and integration into the central support system
  • Using modern technologies such as augmented reality and virtual reality to remotely support and diagnose what you should bring with you
  • Completed technical vocational training as a mechanic or electronics technician (m/f/d) or equivalent
  • Several years of professional experience in the technical environment are advantageous
  • Ideally, military experience in the field of air defense, e.g. as a gunner or radar specialist (m/f/d)
  • Strong analytical skills and enjoyment of working independently in an international environment
  • Very good English skills in word and writing
  • Resilience, flexibility and high commitment for long-term stays abroad
  • Driving license category B WHAT WE ARE ON YOU Before your assignment abroad, you will be comprehensively trained at the Zurich location and prepared for your tasks. You will learn the technical systems in detail, receive training on maintenance processes and be prepared for the requirements of the international mission. In addition, we offer you at our location in Zurich:
  • A dynamic working environment with exciting projects
  • Flexible working hours (active and trusted working hours)
  • Very good social benefits and a super-compulsory pension fund
  • Employee benefits of a large corporation, employee share purchase program
  • Promotion of needs-based training
  • Staff restaurant and external catering and shopping facilities nearby
  • Individual internal and external personnel development measures in cooperation with our Rheinmetall Academy
  • Workplace a few minutes walk from the Zurich-Oerlikon train station KONTAKTDETAILS Responsible contact person: Jascha Köster For questions about your application, please use the contact form. Please note that dossiers of recruiters are not considered.
