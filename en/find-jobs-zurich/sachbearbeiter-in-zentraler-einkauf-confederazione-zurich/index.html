Put to the Point

As a team member of Central Purchasing, you carry out procurements and provide optimal support for the needers together with your colleagues. In addition, you support the team in creating statistics, reports and intranet articles.

  • Entry date: 01.01.2027
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: #LI-MCH1012
  • Location: Operation Center 1, Frachtstrasse, 8058 Zurich

You can make this contribution

  • Operational procurement in the team of the Central Purchasing Office (ZEK)
  • Supporting the assessment of needs and the compilation of the necessary information and documents
  • Ensure reporting and communication for procurement and logistics
  • Advising and supporting stakeholders on procurement issues
  • Prepare specific reports, supplier evaluations and procurement statistics

This makes them unique.

  • Eidg. Professional card as merchant or equivalent training
  • Knowledge of public procurement is an advantage
  • IT affinity (especially SAP knowledge)
  • Teamwork, service orientation, personal responsibility and resilience
  • Very good knowledge of German and good oral and written knowledge of a second official language
