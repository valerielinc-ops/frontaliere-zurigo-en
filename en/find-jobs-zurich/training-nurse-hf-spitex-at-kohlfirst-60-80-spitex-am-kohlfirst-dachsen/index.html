Full description

We are looking for you! Do you want to reorient yourself professionally or are you looking for an education with perspective and responsibility? Do you want a job that makes a difference? Then start your training as a nurse HF at Spitex am Kohlfirst. With us you work where people live – at their home. In the communities of Uhwiesen, Dachsen, Flurlingen and Feuerthalen-Langwiesen, Spitex am Kohlfirst ensures that people at home get the care and care they need – professionally, warmly and reliably. With dedication and passion, we promote young nurses. We invest in the future of care, we invest in you. Training nurse HF Spitex at Kohlfirst, 60%-80 %

We offer you the following training models

  • Full-time study
  • Partial study
  • The employment takes place directly at Spitex am Kohlfirst over the entire duration of the study with a monthly financial remuneration according to cantonal requirements

The training is suitable for

  • Introduction:
  • FaGe graduate: inside
  • People who are looking for a meaningful profession and want a new start

This awaits you with us, you benefit from

  • Individual, flexible solutions for your training path, so the study can also be reconciled with family or previous obligations
  • Practical, close and personal support during your training
  • Meaningful activity with people in their own home
  • Varied work on the road in the region
  • Collaboration in a motivated, warm and committed team

This is what you bring with you

  • Interest in people and care
  • Responsibility, reflectivity and empathy
  • Joy of working independently
  • Willingness to learn new things
  • You have a general education degree (Matura or Fachmittelschule) or a three-year vocational apprenticeship with a federal certificate of ability
  • You have a desire to work in a team
  • Cat. B (own car of advantage)

The application procedure

  • Your application dossier includes: letter of application, letter of motivation, CV, copies of EFZ and further certificates, confirmation of qualification of the training institutions ZAG / Careum / HFP Schaffhausen
  • You will conduct an aptitude interview with our manager and our training managers
  • Perhaps you already have the completed admission procedure in one of the training institutions ZAG / Careum / HFP Schaffhausen
