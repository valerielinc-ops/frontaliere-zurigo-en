Full description

Two Spice – that’s us! A creative and innovative gastronomy and lifestyle company from Zurich. Since 1990 we bring fresh ideas to the table and develop unique concepts and brands that inspire. Our versatile companies are at home at the central hotspots of various cities and stand for quality, design and pure pleasure moments. Welcome to Nooba, inspired by the vibrant streets of Asia and serving a diverse pan Asian cuisine. Our dishes are a fusion of the traditional flavours of Thailand, Japan and India, coupled with urban flair. Immerse yourself in the world of Nooba and become part of a team that redefined the boundaries of Asian cuisine with passion and creativity. Do you want to be part of our team? Now is your chance!

Tasks

  • Operational management and responsibility for the entire operation

  • Development and implementation of measures to achieve business objectives
  • Personnel deployment planning including cost and hour control
  • Planning and implementation of all actions, events and promotional measures

  • Leadership, development and promotion of the team

Requirements

  • Several years of leadership experience in the gastronomy or hotel industry
  • Experience in operational management as well as in the strategic development of a company
  • Strong sense of responsibility and entrepreneurial thinking
  • Strong leadership and decision-making capacity
  • Analytical, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Sovereign and professional approach towards guests, employees and business partners
  • Knowledge of German at C1 level in word and writing is a prerequisite, other languages are a plus
