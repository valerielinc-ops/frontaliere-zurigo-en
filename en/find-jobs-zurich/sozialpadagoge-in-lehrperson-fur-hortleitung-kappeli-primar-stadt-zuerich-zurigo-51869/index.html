Full description

The school Kappeli Primar is a QUIMS school in Kreis 9, Quartier Grünau, with 11 kindergartens, 19 primary classes as well as four kindergarten and three primary level hoards.

As of 1 November 2026 or by agreement we are looking for a social pedagogue or a teacher for the primary level (75 % divided over four working days).

We look forward to a committed and pedagogically savvy personality who, together with our team, creates an appreciative and child-friendly day-care.

Tasks

  • Leadership with an HL (UST children/large team)
  • Participation in supplementary school care
  • Interprofessional cooperation
  • Parental work
  • 5 weeks of work in the summer camp, 8 weeks of holidays in school holidays

Profile

  • Educational training with degree HF or FH
  • Experience in leadership of children’s groups and team leadership
  • Positive human image
  • Communication capability
  • Resilience and flexibility
  • Work experience in supplementary education

We offer

We offer a versatile, varied activity in an exciting, dynamic, multicultural environment, an open, motivated and committed team, competent support by the school management and the management of support, as well as salaries and social benefits according to the guidelines of the school and sports department of the city of Zurich.

About us

The Zurich-Letzi school district comprises Stadtkreis 9 with the districts Albisrieden, Altstetten and Grünau. In the 16 schools of our rapidly growing school circle, more than 5,000 primary school students are taught and supervised. More than 1,200 employees work for them in our schools in the areas of teaching, care, home service and technology as well as administration. They all work daily for the interests of the students and a smooth school day.
