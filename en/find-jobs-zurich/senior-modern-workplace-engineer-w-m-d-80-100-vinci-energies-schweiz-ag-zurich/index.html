Full description

About us

Our Workplace team supports various companies within the Group with suitable workplace solutions. Together, we ensure that our employees receive the digital tools they need for their everyday work. VINCI Energies is part of the VINCI Group, a leading global group for energy, industrial and ICT solutions with over 290,000 employees in more than 120 countries. This means that we offer an international environment, exciting development opportunities and the opportunity to contribute to solutions with a global reach. For this role, we are looking for an experienced person who likes to take on responsibility and proactively develop solutions. You are familiar with modern workplace environments, have a hands-on approach and are confident in interacting with technical issues and exchanging ideas with internal customers. Then you've come to the right place!

Responsibilities

In the position of Senior Modern Workplace Engineer (m/f/d), you will oversee our existing IT infrastructure with a focus on Modern Workplace Management for Switzerland. You will be responsible for the further development and operation of our workplace platforms and work closely with our IT support teams as well as internal stakeholders. Your tasks will include:

  • Workplace management: You will manage our solutions (Microsoft Intune, SCCM) and be the technical point of contact for our 1st level IT supporters. You will manage both our existing SCCM environment and modern cloud management solutions based on Microsoft Intune and Windows Autopilot and accompany the transformation to a modern, cloud-based workplace.
  • Processes and standards: You understand the Group's requirements and implement them in your region. You develop best practices and document your processes to sustainably improve our workplace landscape.
  • Project management: You will contribute to the further development of our solutions by getting involved in national and international project groups.

Requirements

This will make it easier for you to get started

  • Completed IT training (EFZ or higher) as well as more than 3 years of professional experience in Microsoft Workplace (Intune/SCCM) and in the automation of IT processes
  • Structured and target group-oriented communication of technical content as well as experience in the creation and implementation of presentations and reports
  • An independent, structured way of working and the ability to inspire internal teams
  • Willingness to travel as part of occasional business trips (approx. once a quarter within Europe) as well as enjoyment of international cooperation
  • Very good knowledge of German and good English
