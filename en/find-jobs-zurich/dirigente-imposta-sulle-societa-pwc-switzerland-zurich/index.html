Your impact

  • You will advise internationally active Swiss and foreign companies on tax matters and develop tailor-made solutions for their complex problems.
  • You will be part of an inspiring team that oversees challenging projects such as national and international corporate restructuring, transactions, tax planning projects and tax accounting mandates.
  • You develop your own leadership skills in a dynamic team with dedicated employees.
  • You can fully develop in a working environment characterized by team spirit, trust and mutual support.
  • You work on internal and external publications and training courses and support for technical events.
  • You will acquire comprehensive technical know-how and soft skills through on-and-off-the-job training.

Your skill set

  • You have a university degree (uni or FH) in business administration or law and preferably the Swiss tax expert diploma.
  • You have at least 4 years of professional experience in corporate tax law in consulting or industry, possibly also in tax accounting (IFRS or US GAAP).
  • You have very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • Additional language skills are a plus.
  • You take over the leadership of your team and responsibility for your department and projects.
  • You are engaged, flexible and a team- and customer-focused personality with excellent communication skills.
  • You see the larger contexts and are happy to prove your entrepreneurial spirit and your skills in business development.
