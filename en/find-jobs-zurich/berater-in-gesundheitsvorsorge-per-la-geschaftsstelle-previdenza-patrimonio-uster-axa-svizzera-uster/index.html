Full description

100%, Location: Uster

Shape your future – together with us!

For our General Agency Pensions & Assets, we are looking for a strong and motivated personality with strong negotiation skills and in-depth expertise in the health insurance environment or relevant professional experience at a health insurance company to complement our well-rehearsed team. In the "Health" area, which is a top priority of the AXA Group and AXA Switzerland in the long term, we rely exclusively on our own sales and thus on you, because quality is our top priority.

Whether you're an insurance professional or a career changer – start your career in an agency at AXA, the No. 1! We are at our customers' side as a competent partner – in every situation. Are you too?

What to expect

  • Needs-based advice for insurance customers in the field of health care
  • Passionate commitment to our AXA customers
  • Helping to shape our growth and thus become part of our success story: Well over 200,000 customers already place their trust in us in health care
  • Passing on specific expertise to internal colleagues
  • Brokerage of other AXA products (non-life) or services in the area of advance directives and succession planning
  • Acquiring new customers and building external networks

What you bring to the table

  • Completed apprenticeship and professional experience in the insurance industry, an advantage in the field of health insurance
  • Convincing appearance, enjoyment of customer contact and good communication skills
  • Winning and resilient personality combined with a high level of customer and service orientation
  • High degree of independence and flexibility, regional roots
  • Stylistically confident written and spoken German, other languages are an advantage
  • Enjoyment of modern and contemporary digital tools
  • We offer you the diverse development opportunities of the No. 1. - Far beyond the field of health care, we open up perspectives in other topics, specialties, channels and functions. - We promote the internal mobility of our employees

What we offer you

We create framework conditions that give you the space to develop and fully exploit your potential. As a leading Swiss insurer, we offer ambitious people attractive career opportunities and entrepreneurial freedom.

  • Discount of up to 30% on AXA products
  • 5 weeks vacation
  • Fleet discount/refund of Half-Fare travelcard/GA travelcard (pro rata)
  • Access to the Swibeco platform
  • Scope for independent action
  • Innovative structures, processes and modern tools
  • Well-founded, professional training and further education opportunities
