For our general agency Provision & Assets, we are looking for a strong and motivated person with deep expertise in the health insurance environment or relevant professional experience in a health insurance company to complement our experienced team. Whether an insurance professional or a career changer – start your career in an agency at AXA, the No. 1! We stand by our customers as a competent partner – in every situation of life. You too?

  • Need-oriented advice for insurance customers: women in the field of health care

  • Transfer of specific expertise to internal colleagues

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
