More than a job

Our customer care teams are the central interface between customers and internal organizations. Your focus is on excellent customer support and the continuous development of our services, not on operational processing. You ensure that our customers feel optimally cared for at all times and their supply chains are reliably controlled – from proactive communication to strategic development of the cooperation.

Together with your team, you are responsible for an international customer portfolio and ensure that our key customers experience an outstanding service.

How you get in touch

  • Leadership, coaching and further development of a customer care team
  • Responsibility for an international key account portfolio in the area of ocean freight
  • Ensuring proactive, high-quality end-to-end customer support
  • Close cooperation with Sales, Operations and other stakeholders
  • Management of commercial topics including offers, and contracts
  • Implementation of business reviews and management of escalations and continuous development
  • Analysis of customer needs and development of efficient logistics solutions including KPI and performance control

What makes you stand out

  • Multi-year experience in sea freight, ideally in key account environment
  • Well-established know-how in import, export, cross trade
  • Multi-year leadership experience and clear ambitions to develop a team successfully
  • Strong understanding of customer requirements and complex international supply chains
  • Extensive ability to question and advance improvements over and over again
  • High customer orientation combined with entrepreneurial thinking
  • Communication strength and assertiveness in stakeholder management
  • Very good German and English skills

What's in it for you

  • Attractive compensation package including 13th monthly salary and bonus related to our services
  • A responsible key role in an international environment
  • Comprehensive continuing education, from language courses to individual development programmes
  • Discounts for top brands from fashion, leisure, electronics, living & more
  • Team spirit at regular team and company events
  • A state-of-the-art office with optimal public transport connection and parking for employees directly on site

Who we are

Logistics touches every aspect of our lives – from the goods we consume every day to the health care we rely on. Your work goes beyond logistics: it allows the small and large moments of life for people all over the world.

As a global logistics company with a vision of developing our world, we offer you a safe and stable environment. Here, your work makes a real difference. Whether it's the supply of food, the development of sustainable transport solutions or social commitment, your work does more than you can imagine.
