Digital Business Zurich | Pensum: 80-100 %

Working on the pulse of digital transformation. Our Digital Business department makes it possible. Together with us, shape the future of real estate management, develop innovative products and processes and move Livit forward together with us. In digital business, you can expect a great, interdisciplinary team, an inspiring working atmosphere and exciting projects in which you can actively participate. Are you excited about innovations, the latest systems, an agile setting and new challenges? Then you are exactly right with Livit. We look forward to your personal contribution.

Your tasks

  • You will lead the Property Management Solutions, Digital Tenant Services and Business Solutions & AI teams professionally and in personnel
  • You are responsible for the application and product landscape of the business area and continuously develop its roadmaps and value contributions
  • You work closely with the departments and control demand management, prioritization and the realization of business benefits
  • You drive customer-centric digital services and productive AI use cases with measurable added value
  • You promote user-centered solution development according to the principles of user-centered design and ensure a high level of user experience
  • You guarantee the highest quality, security and compliance within the responsible business solutions
  • You control SaaS and supplier relationships, take responsibility for licenses and costs and reduce dependencies on individual providers
  • You work closely with Technology & Enabling Services, leveraging shared platforms, data and services to deliver integrated solutions.

Your profile

  • You have a university degree in business, business informatics, engineering or a comparable education
  • You bring several years of leadership and practical experience in application and product management and develop teams successfully
  • You have strategically built and developed complex product and application landscapes, ideally in the real estate or business software environment
  • You combine strong leadership with a high degree of implementation and change competence
  • You have deep experience in developing user-centric digital solutions and create compelling user experiences
  • You’ll bring a broad understanding of ERP systems, digital platforms, apps, and AI-powered solutions
  • You convince through customer orientation, entrepreneurial thinking as well as a professional and cooperative attitude
  • You communicate stylishly in German and English and are enthusiastic about new technologies and innovations

For this vacancy, only direct applications are considered and we do not accept dossiers from recruitment agencies.
