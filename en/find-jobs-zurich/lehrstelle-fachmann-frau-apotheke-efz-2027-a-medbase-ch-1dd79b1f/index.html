Welcome to the Medbase Pharmacy Wallisellen

Our modern pharmacy is located a few steps from the train station. In addition to a wide range of products from conventional medicine, naturopathy and cosmetics, we offer services such as vaccinations, health checks or consultations. We also have a well-equipped laboratory for manufacturing. As a Medinform certified children’s pharmacy, one of our main focuses is advising families with children. We provide our diverse, international customers and long-standing regular customers with competent and motivated advice. Medbase Apotheke Wallisellen is also involved in the training of apprentices and pharmacists in the assistant year. We appreciate the regular training opportunities, cultivate an appreciative relationship with each other and love the lively exchange with customers.

In your training at Medbase, you will be accompanied and supported by an experienced team. You will learn all the important facets of pharmacy life and develop the skills you need for a successful career.

#WeAreMedbase

The Medbase Group is one of the leading companies in ambulatory healthcare. This means services ranging from health promotion to medication and acute treatment to rehabilitation. Health professionals from various disciplines work hand in hand to provide people across Switzerland with integrated and world-class medical care. With logistics services and digital solutions, we support doctors so that they too can concentrate fully on patients.

The Medbase Group includes the brands Medbase Pharmacies, Zur Rose, Dental Center.ch, WePractice and Bluecare.
