Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Hirslanden | Zurich

Occupation by appointment

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 69005

You want to take responsibility, think along and like to tackle? Then you fit perfectly with our 35-member radiology services team! Here, young, dynamic and committed professionals work hand in hand – reliably and professionally. At the heart of high-quality medicine is always a strong team.

In this versatile function, you work in multi-shift operation from Monday to Friday between 06:30 and 19:00 and rotate within Zurich at five different locations. So if you are looking for a new challenge with a lot of variety and a wide range of tasks as a secretary, then you are at the right place with us.

Your duties

  • Reception and care of our patients and assignees
  • Telephone appointment coordination and planning of all radiological examinations
  • Radiological investigation and preparation
  • General administrative and organisational tasks

Your profile

  • Completed training in health care (e.g. medical secretary/medical secretary/spital secretary/spital secretary) or in the commercial sector with knowledge of medical terminology
  • Enjoying telephone and personal contact with patients and referees
  • Pronounced understanding of services as well as sympathetic, positioned and warm airing
  • Strong communication, resilient and team capable personality
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Further language skills are advantageous
  • Adept handling of common IT applications

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. We offer you a modern, pleasant working atmosphere as well as a thorough introduction to the world of radiology. Your working days and times are organized in a shift operation from Monday to Friday. Thanks to the working time model «Leisure Plus» you have the opportunity to enjoy a percentage wage reduction of 7 instead of 5 weeks holidays. As a secretary / secretary services radiology you wear a work clothes at our reception. Your changing time will be compensated for a 100% fee in the form of two additional paid days off. If you commute by public transport, we will contribute to your travel costs. You will receive the ZVV or Z-Pass for your commute at a discounted price and enjoy free travel throughout the ZVV area. Z-pass validity area. For longer distances we provide you with an SBB coupon. These and many other reasons make Hirslanden Clinic one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.
