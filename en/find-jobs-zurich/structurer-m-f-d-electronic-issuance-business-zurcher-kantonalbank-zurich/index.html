Company and context

  • Several years of experience in the direct environment of issuance of structured products, ideally in structuring or trading
  • In-depth knowledge of products, processes and challenges in the emissions business as well as interest in continuously deepening this knowledge
  • Enjoying the establishment and maintenance of a network in the field of platform emission of structured products; ideally already knowledge of the Swiss platform ecosystem or the willingness to acquire it
  • You score with strong analytical skills and enjoy working with numbers, statistics and evaluations to continuously optimize and develop the business.
