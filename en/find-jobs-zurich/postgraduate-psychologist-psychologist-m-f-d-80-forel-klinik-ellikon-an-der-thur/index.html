Full description

Tasks: The field of activity includes the treatment of alcohol- and drug-dependent patients in individual and group setting and the support in solving their social problems. Supervisions and clinic-internal training events provide an opportunity to deepen daily experience and knowledge. They take over the co-management and later the independent management of group therapies, are used for various representation situations in individual and group setting and will take over case management under supervision. They are introduced to documentation and reporting in order to be able to do this work independently later. The area of activity also includes the diagnostic investigation and the support of the scientific evaluation of the therapy offers. Requirements For this position, we expect a recently completed university degree with a major in psychology or a ZHAW master’s degree with a specialization in clinical psychology. We would like to see a specialist with an interest in interdisciplinary cooperation in a team of doctors, psychologists, design, sports and occupational therapists with the professional goal of the Swiss-approved psychotherapist. We offer: With this position limited to 15 months, we offer you responsible tasks in a motivated, modern and well-connected environment. Your professional and personal development is a concern for us, which we support generously and specifically with structured further education and training offers. In addition, you receive weekly supervision, which can be credited depending on the training institute and status. The working conditions are contemporary and appealing:

  • Reliable service planning in a stable environment
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
  • 60 % employer contribution to the pension fund
  • Our stationary clinic in Ellikon an der Thur is idyllic in the countryside and at the same time offers good accessibility from the Zurich, Winterthur and Eastern Switzerland region and the German border area. Further advantages of the place of work are the in-house gastronomy and sufficient free parking. Our offer is aimed at people of all genders and identities. We look forward to your application. Location: Forel Klinik AG - Ellikon an der Thur Entrance: from now on Forel Klinik — Addiction Medical Clinic in Ellikon an der Thur (Canton of Zurich).
