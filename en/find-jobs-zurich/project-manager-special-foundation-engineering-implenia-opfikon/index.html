Full description

As a leading Swiss construction and real estate service provider, Implenia develops, realizes and manages living spaces, working environments and infrastructure for future generations in Switzerland and Germany. Implenia also offers tunnelling and related infrastructure projects in other markets. The company combines the know-how of highly qualified consulting, development, planning and execution units under one roof into an integrated, multinationally leading construction and real estate service provider. Headquartered in Opfikon near Zurich, Implenia employs more than 8,000 people (FTE) across Europe and achieved a turnover of CHF 3.5 billion in 2025. More information at implenia.com

For our department Special Foundations we are looking for you as our new Senior Project Manager Special Civil Engineering (m/f/d) 80-100%. Your future employment location will be in Opfikon.

You move that.

  • Overall responsibility for planning, controlling and implementing complex special civil engineering projects
  • Management and coordination of internal and external project teams (including construction management, polishers, subcontractors)
  • Ensuring compliance with deadlines, quality and costs
  • Strategic AVOR, resource planning and deployment
  • Contract and supplementary management incl. Negotiations with clients and partners
  • Interface management to other trades and project participants
  • Implementation and further development of project controlling

This is why you succeed

  • Completed training in construction (e.g. civil engineer, dipl.) Construction manager) – ideally with further training in project management
  • Several years of professional experience in project management in special civil engineering
  • Strong leadership skills and experience leading interdisciplinary teams
  • Very good knowledge of MS Office (especially Excel), SAP/R3 and project management tools
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, high personal responsibility and communication strength
  • Resilient, structured and solution-oriented personality with a high level of customer orientation

This is what we offer you

  • Demanding and attractive tasks in sometimes larger projects
  • A motivated team
  • Varied challenges
  • Innovative and sustainable working environment
  • Use of modern technologies
  • Support for employee development (promotion of further education by the Implenia Academy)
  • Implenia-Benefits, including (to name a few):
  • a company-owned pension fund with three contribution levels: Implenia Provision
  • 5 weeks of vacation plus 9 bridge days
  • Travel insurance for you and everyone living in your household
  • Benefits for Implenia employees on products and services
