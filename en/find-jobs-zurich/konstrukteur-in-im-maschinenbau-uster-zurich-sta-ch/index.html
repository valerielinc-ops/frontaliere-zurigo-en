Full description

In an international environment, you design mechanical components and optimize existing assemblies. The company offers you thorough training and flexible working hours. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Adapt existing assemblies and individual parts in 3D CAD
  • Create parts lists and production drawings in SAP
  • Mechanically construct new assemblies and components
  • Developing conversion kits for customer care
  • Technically accompany and implement retrofit orders

Requirements

  • Apprenticeship as a constructor or machine-drawner or machine-drawner
  • Routine in dealing with 3D CAD, ideally Siemens NX
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Practice in the production of mechanical components counts as a plus point
  • Experience with customer care and retrofit jobs would be desirable

Offer
