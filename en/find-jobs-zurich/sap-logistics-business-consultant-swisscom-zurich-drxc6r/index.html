# SAP Logistics Business Consultant

Il tuo futuro inizia

As a Logistics Business Consultant, you are responsible for challenging tasks in SAP transformation and innovation projects for our customers. You are the central contact person from presales to conception and implementation in the field of logistics. You coach internal teams and teams of customers. You work independently and maintain a clear, regular communication. Highlights of the place

  • You will advise our customers on SAP strategy issues and accompany the implementation in the area of logistics
  • You contribute to the sales process and support the sale
  • You work in the conception, planning and implementation of SAP transformation and innovation projects
  • You take over sub-project management or project management and control sub-projects, stakeholders and suppliers

Your skills

  • Several years of experience in SAP strategy and roadmap consulting in logistics (supply chain, procurement, sales and service)
  • Multi-year consulting and customizing know-how in logistics
  • Experience with SAP S/4HANA conversions and common practices; at least one methodology tested in practice
  • (Sub)project management incl. Stakeholder management and workshop moderation as well as coaching of internal teams
  • Industry knowledge in industry, healthcare, public sector and financial services
  • German negotiable, English very good
  • Strong consulting, presentation and communication skills at all organizational levels
  • Team capable, joy of teamwork

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Benefits.
