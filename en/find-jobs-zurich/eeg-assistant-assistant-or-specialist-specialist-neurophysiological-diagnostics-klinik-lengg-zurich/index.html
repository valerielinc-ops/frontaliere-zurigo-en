Full description

Tasks

  • Performance of standard EEG, long-term/video and electroencephalographies (EEG)
  • Patient monitoring and clinical testing for seizures
  • Conducting provocation tests and response time measurements
  • After successful training, you can create the electrodes with our patients and register the EEGs
  • Device/electrode care
  • Administrative tasks
  • Subject-specific training as well as the opportunity to complete the part-time course on "Specialist for Neurophysiological Diagnostics (FND)".
  • Opportunity to participate in the weekend piketting service Requirements
  • Interest and flair in medical-technical tasks
  • Completed medical training (e.g. FaGe, MPA, etc.)
  • Completed training as an FND or prior knowledge of EEG beneficial
  • Open and independent personality with a high level of personal responsibility and teamwork
  • Quick, efficient familiarization with new topics, tasks and processes
  • Good PC application knowledge
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Empathetic and specific behavior towards people of all ages, sometimes with and without severe physical and mental limitations Klinik Lengg AG in Zurich is the centre of excellence for epileptology and neurological rehabilitation. It is our aim to provide patients with neurological diseases with the widest possible rehabilitation and to ensure an accurate diagnosis and sustainable treatment of patients with epilepsy. Around 300 employees are committed to humanely, competently, innovatively and reliably ensuring that our patients regain the greatest possible independence - and thus their quality of life. Become part of one of our interdisciplinary and interprofessional teams.
