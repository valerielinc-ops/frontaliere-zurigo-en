Do you like to move and enjoy dealing with people?
Then become part of our dedicated team and make sure that patients, samples and materials are in the right place at the right time!
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • Transport of patients within the house – in bed, wheelchair or with other suitable means of transport. • carrying out internal courier transports, for example empty beds, laboratory samples, sterile goods and other materials. • Independent work with a mobile device and reliable documentation of orders. • Ensuring friendly, professional and patient-focused customer service. • Consistent compliance with the applicable hygiene and safety regulations. Requirements • Enjoying contact with patients and employees • Reliable, responsible and team-oriented way of working • Willingness to work in a 7×24-hour operation with flexibility in terms of working hours, including weekend and night shifts. • Good physical strength and careful use of transport and materials • Safe handling of digital devices (mobile device) • Good knowledge of German in words and writing We offer: We offer you an exciting and varied environment with versatile tasks. Our patients are always the focus and we are proud to create the best possible starting position for optimal treatment. We are dynamically on the road and are constantly evolving.
