Original description

You organize and carry out maintenance and repair work on machines, plants and technical facilities at the Zurich and St. Gallen locations. You repair problems and carry out unplanned repairs independently in the current production operation. You support machine revisions at our international production sites in Austria and Hungary (about 3 assignments of 2 weeks per year). You work conventionally on lathes, milling, drilling and grinding machines and take on various welding and manual work. You plan, coordinate and implement preventive maintenance measures. You procure spare parts, partly manufacture them yourself and manage the spare parts warehouse. You actively contribute your technical know-how to projects and support them in their implementation. • Requirements Completed training as a machine or polymechanic. In-depth knowledge in assembly technology, pneumatics and conventional production. At least 5 years of professional experience in an industrial environment. Confident knowledge of German in words and writing. Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method. High load capacity, flexibility and enjoyment of varied • Tasks. Readiness for occasional missions abroad. Offer A versatile and responsible activity with a large creative scope. Comprehensive training in an exciting area of work. A dynamic and successful family business with short decision-making paths. Flexible working hours thanks to the flextime model. Centrally located workplace in Zurich with excellent public transport connections. Free parking directly on site. Numerous food options in the immediate vicinity. A collaborative team that values cooperation and mutual support.
