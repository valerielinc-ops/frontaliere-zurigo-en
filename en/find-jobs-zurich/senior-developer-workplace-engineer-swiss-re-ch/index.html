About the Role

As a Senior Developer Workplace Engineer within the Developer Workplace team, you will be a key enabler in our vision to empower every developer at Swiss Re with a seamless, secure, and high-performance workplace. Your role is central to creating a frictionless environment that fosters innovation, accelerates software delivery, and ensures compliance without sacrificing speed. By integrating automation, self-service solutions, and secure-by-design practices, you will help transform Swiss Re into a leading technology-driven reinsurer. Further you will partner with endpoint engineers, developer advocates, and IT Security to standardize developer workstations, automate onboarding and environment setup, and deliver self-service tools that allow engineers to provision their own dev/test environments and access the right packages and privileges from day one.

Developer Background

Strong software development background with extensive experience in programming languages, preferably Java and .NET, along with scripting experience in Shell and PowerShell. Demonstrated ability to design and architect solutions with a

  • strong focus on security.
  • Developer Tooling Ecosystem Knowledge.
  • Deep understanding of the developer tooling ecosystem, including build tools, package managers, IDEs, and artifact repositories.
  • Hands-on experience with tools such as npm, pip, Maven, NuGet, Git, SDK installations, certificates, proxies, and container-based workflows.
  • Experience working in Linux-based environments.
  • Containerization Technology
  • Strong experience with container technologies, including building docker images, running containers, applying containerization best practices and exposure to container-based development is a plus.
  • DevSecOps
  • Proven experience working in a DevSecOps environment, integrating security practices into development and delivery pipelines.
  • Leadership & Stakeholder Management:
  • Demonstrate a strong passion for platform engineering and developer productivity, with the ability to shape long-term technical strategy and influence enterprise-wide engineering practices.
  • Proven ability to build trusted relationships across technical and techno-functional stakeholders, aligning diverse teams around a common vision and driving transformational change.
  • Ability to combine strategic thinking with excellent communication and leadership skills to balance technical excellence, business priorities, and organizational goals.
  • Nice to have:
  • Endpoint Management Tooling: Working knowledge or conceptual understanding of application packaging with Intune, chocolatey and app controls.
