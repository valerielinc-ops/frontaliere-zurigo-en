Full description

Personal – competent – close Welcome to Spital Uster, where modern medicine and a close-knit community complement each other perfectly. As a central pillar of healthcare in the Zurich Oberland and the city of Uster's largest employer, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here, you experience the highest level of professional expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and supported in your professional development. Spital Uster is a teaching hospital of the Faculty of Medicine at the University of Zurich.Become part of our committed community and join us in pursuing the vision of a «regional and digital healthcare hub in Züri Ost»! We are looking for a committed individual as of November 1, 2026, or by agreement, as

The nature of your work

  • Strengthening the focus on maternal-fetal medicine
  • Shared responsibility for organizing and further developing the Frauenklinik Züri Ost
  • Treatment and care of female outpatients and inpatients in the outpatient clinic, delivery room and OPS, as well as participation in the backup on-call service
  • Close collaboration with referring physicians and development of regional partnerships
  • Ensuring the training and supervision of colleagues undergoing further training to become specialists in gynecology and obstetrics

The basis of your performance

  • Specialist qualification in gynecology and obstetrics, ideally with a specialization in maternal-fetal medicine or equivalent qualifications
  • Several years of experience in a senior physician role and in interprofessional work
  • Individual with leadership experience and strong interpersonal and decision-making skills
  • Team-oriented, flexible and resilient individual with a high level of commitment
  • Enthusiasm for training junior colleagues

The reasons for your decision The Frauenklinik Züri Ost is an ambitious, cross-site diagnostic and treatment concept and offers you a responsible role in a future-oriented environment. State-of-the-art equipment is available in the delivery room and obstetric outpatient clinic at both sites. Proven and exceptionally well-coordinated interdisciplinary in-house collaboration with pediatrics ensures comprehensive care. The region also boasts an extremely attractive location with excellent recreational opportunities and quality of life. Your place of work
