Full description

Quality at all levels is the key to true well-being. As an employee or member of the SWICA team, you understand holistic health as a human service. You work with professionals like you who share this claim. Everyone pulls on the same rope; that's a good feeling. Together, we create solutions that really take things forward. As part of our partner management, you are an important hub between SWICA and our service providers. Your experience in healthcare, your organizational talent and your attention to detail help you to keep track of complex situations. If you like to work exactly, enjoy working with different stakeholders and actively shape processes and tools, this job is the right one for you. This is how you shape health

  • Ensuring a high-quality maintenance of our contracts with hospitals, medical networks, observing physicians and other service providers, in cooperation with service purchasing and key account management
  • Coordination of the concerns of hospitals, doctors and other service providers within SWICA
  • Support of our CRM system and the SWICA Expert Finder
  • Evaluations of doctors for the SWICA FAVORIT models
  • Support for the purchase of services in the preparation and follow-up of contract negotiations
  • Participation in organizational tasks and projects in partner management

This sets you apart

  • Commercial or medical basic training with business or similar qualifications
  • Professional experience in the healthcare sector, ideally in a hospital, medical practice or other service provider
  • Enjoy working with different stakeholders and a strong service orientation
  • Very structured, exact and quality-conscious working method with an eye for detail
  • Analytical thinking, organizational skills, and the ability to coordinate multiple topics simultaneously
  • Stylistic German in word and writing
  • Great knowledge of French is a big plus

Why SWICA

  • We build on humanity. That is why we focus on openness, respect and trust in our everyday work. We take on responsibility and think in terms of solutions – together, for our customers. This is also why we have been number 1 in customer satisfaction for many years.
  • We trust you and give you space. With annual working hours, at least 5 weeks holidays and on request also holiday purchase. With part-time models and generous home office within the scope of the possibilities of the job, you make your everyday life largely flexible.
  • We value and reward your work – with a market-based wage, up to 1,500 francs annual fee for personal wishes and a generous participation in the basic and additional insurance for you and your family.
  • We offer you prospects. With a generous training regulations, we support your professional and personal development – for your role today and for your goals of tomorrow.
  • Because health is everything. With our company health management and region-specific offers, we take care of your well-being. And if it comes otherwise: 100% net wage continued payment.
  • We think long term. With three savings plans, pensum-dependent coordination deduction and benefits well above the legal minimum, you ensure good for your future.
