Full description

The emergency centre at KSW is the only emergency in the region open 365 days a year and 24 hours a day. In our emergency center consisting of emergency adults, emergency children & adolescents, emergency practice and emergency bed station, shock room and plaster room, we treat around 64,600 patients annually.
