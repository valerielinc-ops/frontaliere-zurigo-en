Full description

GLOBUS is the leading premium and luxury department store in Switzerland and stands for inspiration, international brands and excellent customer support. Start your 3-year apprenticeship as a retail specialist EFZ with a focus on designing shopping experiences and go through a versatile sales apprenticeship. You will not only gain extensive knowledge in the sale of high-quality products, but also get to know the processes in the back office and logistics center. So you get an overview of the entire retail chain and understand how a luxury department store ticks. Your location is at the GLOBUS Glattzentrum. Be part of a team that values world-class customer advice. The beginning of teaching is 1 August 2027. YOUR TASKS

  • You advise and serve our demanding customers professionally and individually.
  • You will acquire extensive expertise in our industry-specific products.
  • You design the sales area through an attractive presentation of goods.
  • You use various digital tools to optimize the sales process.
  • You support our team in warehouse management and ensure efficient inventory management.
  • You serve the checkouts sovereignly and contribute to a smooth shopping experience.

That's what you bring with you.

  • You are motivated and want to learn constantly.
  • Communication and teamwork skills are your strengths.
  • You are reliable and like to take responsibility.
  • You bring vitality and physical resilience.
  • A well-groomed appearance, impeccable manners and a positive charisma are a matter of course for you.
  • You have very good knowledge of German
  • Other foreign language skills are beneficial.
