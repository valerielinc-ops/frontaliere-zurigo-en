Full description

Your Tasks What makes this task special: With us, you will work independently, responsibly, and embedded in a strong, supportive team. You accompany people not on a minute-by-minute basis, but with genuine presence.

  • Holistic support of people in palliative situations at home, in nursing homes, and consultatively in hospitals
  • Conducting palliative assessments and individual care planning
  • Symptom management, counseling, and emergency planning
  • Support and accompaniment of relatives and loved ones
  • Close collaboration with general practitioners, Spitex, nursing homes, and other professionals
  • Independent execution of medical-technical measures
  • Regular participation in on-call duty to ensure 24/7 care Our
  • Requirements
  • Diploma as a nursing professional HF/FH
  • Further training in Palliative Care, oncology, or comparable experience is an advantage (or willingness to do so)
  • Enjoyment of independent work with high personal responsibility
  • Empathy, calmness, and professional security even in demanding situations
  • Driver's license category B and own vehicle
  • Very good German language skills and secure IT user knowledge Professional Benefits We know: Good Palliative Care requires time, trust, and strong employees. Therefore, we create conditions in which you can perform your work professionally and humanely. Here’s what you can look forward to:
  • Meaningful work with real impact and high independence
  • Experienced, warm, and supportive team
  • Structured introduction and careful onboarding
  • Regular supervision and professional exchange
  • Appreciative leadership culture at eye level With us, you can further develop and benefit from internal and external training opportunities. Various discounts and internal offers are also part of our benefits, as well as an excellent staff restaurant, daycare participation, and above-average social benefits.
