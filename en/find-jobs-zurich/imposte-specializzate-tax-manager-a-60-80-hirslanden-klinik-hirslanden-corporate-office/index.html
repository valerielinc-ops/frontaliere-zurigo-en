Hirslanden stands for first-class, responsible and individual health care that puts people first. From birth to old age as well as from prevention to healing – at any time, physically and digitally – the Hirslanden Group is concentrating on the further development of the “Continuum of Care” together with private and public partners.

YOUR TASKS

  • Central contact person for all VAT inquiries within the Hirslanden Group (excluding VAT declaration)
  • Preparation of tax returns of Hirslanden Group companies as well as various ESTV forms
  • Audit of provisional and definitive tax bills and assessments
  • Review of the tax provisions of all Hirslanden Group companies in the context of annual and half-yearly accounts
  • First contact person for general direct tax issues within the Hirslanden Group and towards our external tax consultants and tax authorities
  • Examination and processing of various tax issues (direct and indirect taxes) as well as participation in the restructuring of internal tax-related processes
  • Preparation of tax memorandums and tax rulings as well as tax reports
  • Administrative support in the preparation of external financial reporting in accordance with International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS)

Your profile

  • Completed studies in the field of economics, law or specialist card finance & accounting (possibly with certificate degree ExpertSuisse "SME tax consultancy" or "VAT" or in training to become a tax expert)
  • First professional experience in taxation
  • Strong analytical skills and a pronounced number flair
  • Structured, precise and timely working methods as well as a high degree of independence and resilience; “Hands-on” mentality
  • SAP-FI system knowledge, Dr. Tax knowledge desired and good Excel knowledge
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Knowledge of French is an advantage
  • Good interpersonal and communication skills
