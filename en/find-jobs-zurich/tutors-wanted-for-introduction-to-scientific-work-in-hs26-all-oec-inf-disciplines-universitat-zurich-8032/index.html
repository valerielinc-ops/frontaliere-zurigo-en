Tasks: In a two-day workshop (in the week before the start of the semester) you will acquire the skills to convey the basics of scientific work and learn what is important when coaching students and organizing groups. You will then take on two practice groups in the bachelor’s lecture “Introduction to Scientific Work”. They introduce the bachelor students to the various topics of scientific work, such as quantitative and qualitative research methods, the use and handling of AI, as well as questions of scientific ethics and philosophy. In addition to leading the exercise groups, acquiring the 6 ECTS points includes correcting the practical exercises, answering students' questions and supporting the lecturers with organizational aspects. Requirements:

  • A flair for leading groups of students in the context of exercises
  • Enthusiasm for scientific work as well as for scientific philosophical and ethical questions
  • Good basic knowledge of quantitative and qualitative research methods
  • A self-responsible, reliable and motivating personality who is happy to pass on their knowledge and their own study experiences to new students
  • Enjoy working in a young team and the willingness to self-reflect Compensation: 6 ECTS points Bachelor students who have completed the assessment level and Those who have successfully completed compulsory modules at the bachelor's level can also apply. Please note that the ECTS can only be used for a master's degree in accordance with the credits in the course catalog and a maximum of 30 ECTS can be used for the master's level.
