Full description

Tasks

  • Office hours organisation and scheduling
  • Reception and care of patients
  • Preparation of medical reports (background speech recognition), recording of their services according to TARDOC and case data maintenance
  • Friendly telephone information and forwarding to the competent authority
  • Blood sampling, injections, infusions
  • Bone density measurements (X-ray required or readiness to obtain it)
  • Instruction of patients for independent drug application

Requirements

  • Training as medical/practice assistant
  • Enjoy the creation of medical reports and safe application of medical terminology
  • High motivation for administrative activities
  • Service-oriented and enjoyment of direct patient contact
  • Very good and stylish knowledge of German, knowledge in English is advantageous
  • To expand our team, we look forward to a friendly, team-capable and independent personality

We offer

  • 40 hourly week
  • Attractive social benefits and numerous benefits
  • Team spirit
  • Training opportunities
  • Cheap food in the staff restaurant

The Schulthess Clinic in Zurich is one of the leading orthopaedic clinics in Europe with over 1,100 employees. Our central concern is to free people from their pain and restore their mobility. As a specialist clinic, we focus on surgical orthopedics, neurology, rheumatology and sports medicine. Schulthess Clinic is Swiss Olympic Medical Center.
