When requests become solutions.

Do you love solving technical questions and inspiring customers with your advice? For our customer service in Zurich-Wollishofen, we are looking for a service-oriented and ready-to-learn personality who can competently advise and inspire our customers in different languages. With us, you can expect a motivated and diverse team that together pursues one goal: to offer our customers a first-class service. What counts for us is not only your previous experience, but above all your willingness to learn, your solution orientation and your desire to make a difference for our customers every day.

Your profile

  • Professional experience in a similar function (customer service)
  • Very good knowledge of German and very good knowledge of Italian or French
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship in the construction or technical area is advantageous, ideally supplemented by further training at level TK, HF or FH
  • Enjoyment of customer contact or advice and support by telephone
  • Ability to prioritize and work in parallel with multiple system interfaces (SAP, OneNote, Sharepoint)
