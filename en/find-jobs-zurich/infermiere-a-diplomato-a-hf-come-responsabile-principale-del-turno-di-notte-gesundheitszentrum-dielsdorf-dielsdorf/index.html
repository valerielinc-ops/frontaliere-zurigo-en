8157 Dielsdorf Onsite / on site 40% - 50% Permanent

The Dielsdorf Health Center is a specialized nursing institution with around 430 employees in the Zurich suburbs and provides care for the 22 political communities of the Dielsdorf district with about 90,000 inhabitants. With more than 200 beds, the inpatient offer of general long-term care is complemented by specialized treatment pathways for rehabilitative acute and transitional care, palliative care, psychiatric geriatrics and the focus on dementia. The Spitex Regional forms an important part of the care offer. It provides ambulatory services for the five municipalities of Dielsdorf, Niederglatt, Niederhasli, Regensberg and Regensdorf.
