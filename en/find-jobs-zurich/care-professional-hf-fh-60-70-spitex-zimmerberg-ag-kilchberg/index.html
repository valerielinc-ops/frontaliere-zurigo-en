Full description

As a non-profit organisation, Spitex Zimmerberg AG is responsible for the care, advice, care and support of people of different age groups who are dependent on external help due to health restrictions. We enable the inhabitants of Adliswil, Thalwil, Kilchberg, Rüschlikon and Langnau am Albis to live a self-determined life in their usual environment. Spitex Zimmerberg AG has around 160 employees, spread over four locations and is one of the largest Spitex organizations in the Canton of Zurich. We are looking for you! To support our teams, we are now or by agreement: Care professional HF/FH 60-70 % Your tasks

  • InterRAI-HC needs assessments
  • Independent, self-responsible care and support of customers
  • Taking over the case management
  • Improving care needs and care costs
  • Creating, adapting and evaluating the maintenance documentation according to interRAI-HC
  • Carry out and document intervention according to care planning
  • Consultation services
  • Ongoing review of quality and effectiveness of care
  • Cooperation with relatives, doctors: inside, hospitals, homes and other specialists

Requirement profile

  • Completed training (HF/FH)
  • Professional, independent working method
  • A broad professional experience
  • Social skills, resilience
  • Flexibility, in particular with regard to operating times
  • Good PC knowledge
  • Ticket Cat. B

This is what we offer you

  • Holidays: 27 days (from 50 years 32 days)
  • BVG: Flexible choice between the “Mini” and “Maxi” (AXA) plans
  • Diversity: A responsible, varied and independent activity
  • Working environment: A dynamic, growing environment with a motivated and collaborative team
  • Further development: Support for further training and other attractive advantages
  • Occasions: Large employee celebration twice a year
