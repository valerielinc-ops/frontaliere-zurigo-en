# Application Manager Syrius Finance

Il tuo futuro inizia

You ensure the stable operation of Adcubum Syrius and optimize financial processes and interfaces. This strengthens quality and customer satisfaction. As an Application Manager, you supervise and develop Adcubum Syrius with a focus on finance. You ensure that in- and ex-collection, premium and service payments as well as booking and coordination processes work reliably. With your insurance expertise, your experience in Syrius parameterization and your technical coordination, you ensure stable operations and comprehensible financial processing.

  • Ensuring the technically correct operation and further development of Adcubum Syrius with focus on the Finance module
  • Monitoring interfaces, jobs and processing processes
  • Analysis and sustainable resolution of technical disturbances and deviations
  • Recording and implementation of customer requirements in Syrius and surrounding systems
  • Management and coordination of changes and releases including testing and productive introduction

Your skills

  • Completed training in business administration, business informatics, computer science or comparable qualification
  • Several years of experience in the health and accident insurance environment with a focus on finance: in/exclusive collection, payment transactions, debtor and creditor processes, booking logics
  • High IT affinity and good understanding of business processes
  • Knowledge in the parameterization of Adcubum Syrius is advantageous
  • Project experience and knowledge in the use of SQL for data queries is advantageous
  • Analytical, structured and solution-oriented working method in a team
  • High personal responsibility and networked thinking
  • Strength of communication and very good knowledge of German in words and writing

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Benefits.
