Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Vocational Advice Social Worker in kjz Rüti, 60 - 80% The Office for Youth and Vocational Counselling (AJB) is responsible for child and youth welfare as well as professional counselling. Our work focuses on children, young people and families. We work for them every day with a lot of heart and commitment. The kjz (Child and Youth Assistance Centre) is a polyvalent counselling centre for families, parents, adolescents, children and toddlers and works on the basis of the Children and Youth Assistance Act of the Canton of Zurich. For the department of social work and mandates in kjz Rüti we are looking for a social worker (60-80%) from 1 February 2027 or by agreement. They advise and accompany children, adolescents, mothers and fathers in the context of child protection measures and on a voluntary basis. In addition, you carry out clarifications in the case of child welfare on behalf of KESB. In fulfilling your tasks, you work closely with authorities, schools, other institutions and specialist bodies. They bring with them:

  • Completed social work training at a technical college or equivalent
  • Experience in youth and family assistance and statutory social work
  • Good knowledge of social security
  • a negotiable, goal-oriented and assertive personality
  • Empathy for people in special life situations
  • systemic thinking and enjoyment of administrative activities We offer you:
  • good introduction and coaching by the department management
  • Supportive, interdisciplinary team, collegial exchange and supervision
  • demanding, meaningful and self-employed work
  • living work that takes place in the middle of the life of a wide variety of population groups
  • fair employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines
  • a centrally located workplace with modern infrastructure next to the Rüti station (1/4 hours from Zurich)
  • the opportunity to work partly in your own four walls Can we answer further questions? For further information please contact Isabelle Ziegler, Head of Social Work and Mandate, at tel. We look forward to receiving your full application via online tool to Mrs. Sandra Zahner, Human Resources, Hinwil, Meilen, Pfäffikon and Uster district office in Wetzikon. Only applications submitted via our online platform can be processed.
