Role overview

Spitex Weinland Mitte is a growing nonprofit organization. So that our customers can continue to live in their own four walls despite impairments, we are committed professionally and with a lot of personal commitment. Our team comprises 40 employees in the fields of somatics, psychiatry and home economics. The supply area comprises the five municipalities of Marthalen, Rheinau, Trüllikon, Truttikon and Benken with around 5600 inhabitants. We are a future-oriented organization – modern, flexible and striving for continuous quality improvement. What we are missing is you! Help - We need you! A plus for us As a specialist in psychiatry, you make care in Spitex Weinland Mitte smarter and are interested in the latest state of psychiatric care.
