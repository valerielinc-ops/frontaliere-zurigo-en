Full description

For our HR department we are looking for 01. February 2027 a specialist staff 100%.

Tasks

  • They are responsible for the entire recruitment process for employees and cadres up to the level of divisional and departmental management.
  • They manage the time and absence management incl. Handling accident and illness cases and taking over the case management in the event of long-term absences.
  • On the HR side, you are responsible for the processes for education management and employment certificates.
  • They maintain organizational management and are responsible for various reports, evaluations and lists.
  • You are the contact person and advise employees and cadres on the topics and processes responsible.

Profile

  • Commercial apprenticeship with additional training in HR (e.g. HR professional / professional with Federal Professional ID)
  • Proven expertise and practical experience in HR, knowledge in the HR of a public administration preferred
  • Proven IT user skills (MS-Office 365 and preferably SAP)
  • Integrous, trustworthy personality with high social and consulting skills, good communication skills and team spirit
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method

We offer

We offer you a varied activity in a small, motivated team. They benefit from attractive employment conditions at the city of Zurich and contemporary infrastructure at a central location.

About us

The technical school Viventa is an educational offer of the city of Zurich. We are a competence center for vocational preparation, integration, special school Viventa15plus as well as parent and adult education.
