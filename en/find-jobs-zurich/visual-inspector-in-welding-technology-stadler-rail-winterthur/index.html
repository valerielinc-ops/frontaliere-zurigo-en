Full description

PROFILE

  • Completed technical vocational training in the manufacture of metal, machinery or vehicles (e.g. plant and apparatus manufacturers, polymechanics, locksmiths, welders or similar)
  • Readiness for shift work in 2-shift operation
  • Certification VT Level 2 according to EN ISO 9712
  • Several years of experience in the visual inspection of welded structures
  • Further test methods (UT, UTPA, VT, PT, MT) are advantageous
  • Experience as a welder is an advantage
  • Good knowledge in reading technical drawings and welding symbols
  • Sound knowledge of relevant standards and quality requirements
  • Safe handling of MS Office and digital test systems
  • High quality awareness and a reliable and structured working method
  • Team capable personality with assertiveness and sense of responsibility
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

TASKS

  • Perform non-destructive tests by visual inspection (VT) on welding assemblies, castings and forged parts
  • Assessment of assemblies according to standards, drawings and customer specifications
  • Documentation and interpretation of test results and preparation of test reports
  • Identification, assessment and reporting of deviations and quality defects
  • Support for root cause analyses and corrective measures
  • Ensuring compliance with quality standards and internal rules
  • Close cooperation with welding supervision, production, quality assurance and engineering
  • Contribution to internal and external audits
