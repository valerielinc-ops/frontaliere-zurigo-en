Head of Fundraising (m/f/d)

80%, Zurich, temporary

The Department of Architecture at ETH Zurich is one of the leading international schools of architecture. We make critical contributions to current topics in architecture, landscape architecture, urban design, and architectural technologies and are strongly networked in academia, business, politics and society.

Project background

In order to develop new sources of financing, we are setting up a fundraising office and are looking for a committed person with expertise and experience in the field of fundraising as of 1.1.2027 or by agreement.

Job description

Your tasks as a fundraising expert include

  • Creation of a business plan for the department’s fundraising, which is oriented towards its strategic goals
  • Gradual establishment of a professional fundraising office (including fundraising processes and CRM systems)
  • Analysis of funding potential as well as building and maintaining long-term relationships with potential funding partners: inside (alumni, business, industry, politics, private)

In your role as a leader: in fundraising

  • Represent your area with departmental management, the ETH Zurich Foundation and other relevant stakeholders
  • Act as a coordination point between potential funding partners: inside and outside the department
  • Are you responsible for fundraising activities that you plan and organize in close cooperation with the field of communication and in active exchange with the department management, institutes, centers and professorships
  • Create fundraising content for the different communication channels
  • Periodic reporting informs the department management about the implemented and planned fundraising activities (including further development proposals)

Profile

  • They have a university degree (preferably in communication, architecture, economics, humanities or social sciences) and are interested in research topics with a particular focus on architecture
  • You are a strategically, analytically and entrepreneurially thinking, communicatively convincing personality. You also bring a strong network in the area of fundraising and communication
  • In addition to excellent communication and presentation skills in German and English, you have high social and intercultural competence, professional appearance and networking skills. They work on their own initiative, structured and goal-oriented, and actively use state-of-the-art (AI) tools to be able to act data- and impact-oriented.
  • Experience in fundraising, marketing and/or communication in the university environment is beneficial

We offer

  • A dynamic, collegial team with an open, appreciative and collegial working culture in a demanding, academic environment.
  • A varied and challenging range of tasks at the interface between the department’s strategic and operational processes, and the opportunity to actively contribute to the design and development of study programmes.
  • They benefit from further education opportunities as well as numerous advantages, including benefits, such as for public transport subscriptions, a versatile sports offer of the ASVZ, various offers for child care and attractive social and pension benefits.
