Role overview

At the University Hospital in Zurich, you will accompany people with oncological diseases in a highly specialized medical environment – in a continuous patient pathway, across specialist boundaries and with a noticeable effect on patients.

In this role, you combine advanced clinical expertise with coordination and interprofessional collaboration.

You work at interfaces and in complex treatment situations with different professions and thus contribute to high-quality patient care. >The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland.

  • At the University Hospital in Zurich, you will accompany people with oncological diseases in a highly specialized medical environment – in a continuous patient pathway, across specialist boundaries and with a noticeable effect on patients.

Main responsibilities

  • You are the first point of contact for patients if they have questions or complaints.
  • You ensure that patients are also reliably accompanied in complex oncological treatment situations.
  • You use your clinical expertise where it makes an immediate difference – in direct care, case management and interprofessional interfaces.
  • You take on responsibility in a highly specialized environment and contribute to coordinated and patient-centered care with your work.
  • You bring your perspective to professional case discussions and roundtables and thus strengthen the quality of cooperation.
  • You will support the further development of the APN care offer in the tumor center and contribute your experience to its further development.
  • You have a Master of Science in Nursing, preferably with a focus on Nurse Practitioner, as well as several years of professional experience, including at least two years in oncology and dermatology, and are distinguished by clinical expertise, ownership and flexibility.
  • You want to build the next tumor pathway in the field of dermatooncology.
