Kitchen Studio Manager (m/f/d)

100%

Affoltern on the Albis

immediately

Share

We at pfister pursue a vision: We want to move our products into every Swiss home. We are seeking active support for this. Help us bring the approximately 100,000 products in our range into the homes of our customers. Whether in one of the 18 branches or at the headquarters in Suhr: You can expect a unique environment, working with attractive products and, last but not least, appreciation and respect for your commitment. There are already around 1,400 people working at pfister today - will you join soon?

Your new tasks

  • Responsibility for the professional and disciplinary management of the kitchen studio team
  • Responsibility for achieving the sales targets in the responsible kitchen studio
  • Responsibility for the attractive presentation of goods in the kitchen studio
  • Competent advice and enthusiasm of our customers in our modern Pfister kitchen studios
  • Inspiration of the customer with our attractive and diverse range
  • Planning and selling dream kitchens to our customers with the help of the planning tool Carat
  • Responsible for the preparation and processing of the offer up to the purchase contract
  • Continuous development of salespeople in the kitchen studio
  • Regular exchange with the managing director and the other managers of the branch
  • Ongoing exchange with the teams in kitchen logistics and after-sales to ensure smooth order processing
  • Joy in consulting-intensive dealings with customers
  • Experience in kitchen sales or assembly of kitchens
  • very good spatial imagination and creativity
  • Experience with digital planning systems (furniture, kitchens, spatial planning) is an advantage
  • Experience leading a team of 2-3 people
  • craft training
  • Quick understanding and solution-oriented action
  • Sovereign and Winning
