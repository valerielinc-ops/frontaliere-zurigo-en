Do you have a heart for the elderly? Is the nursing profession your calling, which not only improves the quality of life of others but also brings satisfaction and meaning to your own life? Do you see opportunities to bring your skills and experiences to us to have a positive impact on the lives of our customers? Then you are in the right place!

For Tertianum Grünegg in the Zürcher Oberland region, we are looking for a

Healthcare Specialist EFZ 40-100%

for day and evening shifts

Your tasks

  • Holistic care and nursing of guests according to our nursing philosophy and the nursing process
  • Taking on tasks as a nursing contact person
  • Participation in the nursing process and in the nursing needs assessment with RAI for the assigned guests
  • Electronic nursing documentation using CareCoach (mobile possible with CareCoach-Smartphone)
  • Planning and accompanying doctor visits
  • Taking on daily responsibility
  • Taking on tasks as a theme bearer of a specialist topic and thus participating in the group-wide network for nursing quality
  • Independent assumption of responsibility for the entire station during the night shift

Your profile

  • Completed training as a healthcare specialist EFZ
  • Work experience in long-term care preferable
  • RAI knowledge desired or willingness to attend these courses (paid)
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude
  • Joy in diverse challenges
  • Good German language skills, both spoken and written
