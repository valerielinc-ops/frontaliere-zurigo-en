Original description

Canton of Zurich Financial Directorate Personnel Office Specialist responsible / basic education KV (co-management) 80 - 100% The Personnel Office of the Canton of Zurich is responsible for all personnel, employment and social security issues of the more than 30,000 employees of the Canton of Zurich. The Human Resources Management (HRM) department includes a large number of tasks and is part of the Human Resources Office. HR consulting, basic vocational training, employer branding and case management are the focus of the work. The vocational basic education team is passionate about recruitment, assignment planning and HR support for the approximately 200 computer and CV learners of the Cantonal Administration. From January 2027 we are looking for active support for our seven-member team. Basic vocational education means shaping the future. Every year, around 35 KV learners start their training at the Canton of Zurich. Behind every apprenticeship there is the opportunity to accompany young people on their way into working life and at the same time to train the specialists of tomorrow. As a specialist responsible for basic education KV (co-director), you and your colleague take on the responsibility for the cantonal KV teaching association. Together with our practical trainees, you ensure that motivated young people become qualified professionals – and thus strengthen the future of the largest employer in the canton of Zurich. How can you get involved?

  • In co-direction, you accompany around 120 KV learners during the entire training period and actively shape their personal and professional development
  • Due to the central HR responsibility for the teaching relationship, you are responsible for the entire Apprentice Life Cycle (including recruitment of around 35 learners annually as well as the rotation planning, support and advice of learners in the association).
  • Supporting the existing and gaining new apprenticeships is just as much a part of your tasks as checking the operational requirements, defining standards and processes and quality management.
  • You actively shape the üK Fachstelle Zürich on behalf of the public administration industry. You organize and coordinate the inter-company courses as well as the qualification process and also contribute your expertise as a speaker in the courses
  • Whether you are a learner, practical trainee or parent – you support, coach and accompany people in challenging situations with empathy, clarity and solution orientation.
  • You work closely with vocational schools, authorities, professional associations and internal stakeholders and represent commercial vocational training within and outside the cantonal administration
  • Further, you organize and moderate training-relevant events (e.g. introductory days, information events, parent evenings, networking events for practical trainees) Who are you?
  • This demanding cross-sectional function requires a combination of HR and leadership experience in the vocational training context.
  • Ideally, you have training in HR/Leadership/Coaching or a similar field and are interested in solutions to complex problems with young people in training
  • You like to work operationally and conceptually, want to engage in vocational training and constantly develop yourself and your area of responsibility
  • With your open and communicative nature, it is easy for you to establish and maintain a trusting relationship with our various contact groups.
  • Between recruitment, coaching, organization and quality management, you work in a structured, independent and never lose focus What do we offer you?
  • You accompany young people on their way into working life and make an important contribution to the promotion of young talent of the largest employer in the Canton of Zurich
  • You can expect a responsible task with a lot of personal responsibility, a committed team and the opportunity to constantly develop and actively shape vocational training.
  • Flexible working hours, contemporary employment conditions and a workplace in the immediate vicinity of Zurich Central Station are part of our attractive offer. What now? We have aroused your interest? Then apply now. We look forward to meeting you. Please note that we only process applications that are received via our online tool. We deliberately waive a letter of motivation. Instead, we look forward to your answers to our questions in the tool – so we get to know your motivation, your experiences and your personality even more specifically.
