Full description

As a supervisor, you are the link between the team and the branch management. You carry responsibility, give orientation and ensure that everyday life in the shop runs smoothly. You live by what you expect from others, arguing clearly and fact-based, always keeping the good of your team and your customers in mind.

Mansioni

  • You support our customers when picking up new goods, returns processing is also part of your area of responsibility
  • You are responsible for recording orders and processing customer orders
  • You support the team in all tasks in the shop, such as filling the warehouse shelves and handling the daily goods transfer
  • As a team player, we expect you to actively support your employees at the front in everyday situations.
  • In the absence of the direct superiors, you are the daily responsible of the branch and assume the necessary responsibility
  • In cases of escalation, you are the first contact person for your employees and work on demanding customer concerns professionally and solution-oriented
  • You are a flexible personality and are not afraid to provide valuable help in exceptional cases with other departments (returns, parcel shipping)

Requirements

  • Completed training in the retail trade as well as in-depth experience in sales are required
  • Flexibility sets you apart and you are ready to take on holiday or sick leave at short notice if necessary.
  • You convince by courteous and professional handling of our customers and communicate fluently in German in word and writing. Further language skills, especially English, are advantageous
