Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Vocational Advice Social Worker in kjz Horgen 80% The Office for Youth and Vocational Counselling Canton of Zurich, office of the districts of Affoltern, Dietikon, Horgen, is a cantonal competence centre that offers professional support in the areas of family/youth and professional counselling. The Kinder- und Jugendhilfezentrum (kjz) is a counselling centre for families, parents, adolescents, children and small children and works on the basis of the Kinder- und Jugendhilfegesetz of the Canton of Zurich. For our kjz team we are looking for a social worker. Belonging, meaningfulness and competence We are an experienced team with a lot of expertise, humour and creativity. We support each other, enjoy working interdisciplinary and are committed to child protection.

  • They advise and accompany children, adolescents and parents within the framework of child protection measures in mandate management as well as on a voluntary basis. In addition, you carry out clarifications in case of suspicion of child welfare. Intensive cooperation with family systems, authorities, schools and other institutions and specialist bodies is important to us. In addition, your inputs and suggestions are required in the further development of our kjz Horgen. Profile You have a degree in social work (FH) and have experience in youth and family help. We are looking for you as a solution-oriented and resilient personality with high social skills, an appreciative basic attitude and enjoyment of teamwork. Administrative activities as well as the creation of reports and file notes are easy for you. Offer
  • We offer you a meaningful, versatile and demanding job in an exciting and interdisciplinary field of work. We will work with you carefully and you can count on support vessels such as coaching, intervision groups and supervision. Because child protection means teamwork and support from the management. Internal training offers are available and we also participate in external training. The employment conditions correspond to the cantonal guidelines. Your workplace is located directly at the train station and at the lake. For further information, Salomé Gossaint, Head of Social Work and Mandates, is available at 043 259 92 10 or salome.gossaint@ajb.zh.ch. Additional information can be found at www.ajb.zh.ch.
