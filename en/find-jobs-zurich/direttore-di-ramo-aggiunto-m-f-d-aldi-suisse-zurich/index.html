About ALDI SUISSE AG

ALDI SUISSE is a Swiss retailer with headquarters in Schwarzenbach (SG). It belongs to the ALDI SÜD group of companies with locations in eleven countries worldwide. Since entering the market in 2005, ALDI SUISSE has been continuously expanding its branch network throughout Switzerland. The company currently operates 247 branches and employs around 4,000 people. The range impresses with top quality at the best price. The ALDI shelf contains high-quality products from Switzerland, a wide range of fresh products and the highest organic standards on the Swiss market. In addition, ALDI SUISSE stands for fairness, both towards its customers and partners as well as towards its employees.
