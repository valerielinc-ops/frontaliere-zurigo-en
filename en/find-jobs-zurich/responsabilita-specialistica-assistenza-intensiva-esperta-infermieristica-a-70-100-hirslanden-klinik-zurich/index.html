Full description

Location: Hirslanden Clinic in the Park | Zurich

Occupation by appointment

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 60557

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

At Klinik Im Park, you will have the opportunity to actively shape the future of intensive care – in a newly created professional role and as a member of the management team.

Our SGI-certified intensive care unit with six beds serves a demanding, interdisciplinary spectrum of patients with a focus on cardiac and visceral surgery. The training of NDS students is also part of our mission.

In your role, you combine around 20% development and concept work with co-operation in shift operations.

YOUR TASKS

  • Development and management of tools to ensure nursing and medical quality (according to SOP & evidence-based standards) as well as development and support of the implementation of nursing concepts
  • Targeted, situation-specific application of evidence-based nursing knowledge and techniques, as well as advising your colleagues on care-specific concerns of intensive care units
  • Case and course support, moderation of case discussions and nursing discussions
  • Active participation in clinical improvement and innovation processes within the framework of project work
  • Active employee in the nursing department of Klinik Im Park
  • Cooperation in the direct care of critically ill patients in shift operations, including monitoring and operation of medical devices (individual service planning possible after consultation)

Your profile

  • Diploma recognised as an expert in intensive care NDS HF or equivalent
  • Bachelor of Science in Nursing FH or equivalent degree; MScN is a plus
  • Several years of experience in an intensive care unit of a Swiss acute care hospital with HSM
  • Strong communication, methodological and social competence
  • Good digital skills
  • Interest in innovation, advancement and clinical process design

In addition to an attractive workplace on the right side of Lake Zurich, an inter- and multi-professional team awaits you. You work with a modern medical clinic information system and can expect extensive training and development opportunities from us. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer flexible working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work and family responsibilities. We pay regionally in line with the market and offer attractive social and non-wage benefits. Employees who have to move completely for their work receive four additional paid days off with a 100% workload. With a reduced workload, the days off reduce accordingly. Employees employed on an hourly basis benefit from an equivalent financial compensation. These and many more are reasons why Klinik Hirslanden is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.
