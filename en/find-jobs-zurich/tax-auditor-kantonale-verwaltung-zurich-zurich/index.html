Full description

Corporate taxation is our passion. We combine business thinking with tax expertise and work on issues that are rarely easy, but all the more exciting. We are communicative, make sound decisions and stay tuned, even if it is demanding. Self-responsible work and mutual support are a matter of course for us. And because not everything is only black or white, we can occasionally «s’foifi grad si laa». Some of our long-term colleagues will soon be retiring. Therefore, we look forward to new tax professionals who complement our team with their expertise, personality and fresh perspectives. Your tasks

  • You prepare book audits for companies and carry them out independently on site.
  • You test companies of different sizes and industries – from small to large, from large international companies to kiosks.
  • In doing so, you analyze annual accounts and clarify tax-relevant issues in a well-founded manner.
  • On this basis, you make the assessment of legal and natural persons.
  • You are in direct exchange with taxpayers, trustees and other stakeholders.
  • Depending on the case, you work with colleagues and actively contribute your expertise. At the same time, you benefit from the exchange with experienced employees. Your profile
  • Ideally, you bring a basic business education and have already gained professional experience in trust, tax, finance or accounting.
  • You work accurately, stick to challenging topics and prevail. You communicate clearly even in delicate situations, argue objectively and remain steadfast.
  • You are curious, enjoy tricky questions in the field of corporate taxation and are happy to develop professionally and personally.
  • You are flexible and are willing to make regular appointments on site with companies as part of your work.
  • You communicate very well in German in word and writing
  • Further language skills, especially English, are a plus.
