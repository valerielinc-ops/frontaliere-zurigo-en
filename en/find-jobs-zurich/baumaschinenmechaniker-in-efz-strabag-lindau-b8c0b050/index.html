Construction Machinery Mechanic EFZ Your contribution with us

  • Overhauls, repairs as well as service and maintenance work on construction machinery and vehicles
  • Repair assignments on construction sites with a well-equipped service vehicle
  • Repair of various small devices
  • General mechanical and welding work

What counts for us

  • Completed apprenticeship EFZ as construction machinery / agricultural machinery or truck mechanic – recent graduates are welcome!
  • Professional experience in the construction machinery / agricultural machinery sector is mandatory
  • Good knowledge of hydraulics, pneumatics, vehicle construction and vehicle electrics as well as welding
  • Driving licence class B
  • Independent, precise and reliable working method
