Role overview

The Institute of GP Medicine at the University Hospital Zurich offers the curriculum GP medicine as a 2-year advanced training phase for prospective GPs.

The curriculum family medicine allows access to attractive training positions that prepare for later work in primary care.

The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland.

  • The Institute of GP Medicine at the University Hospital Zurich offers the curriculum GP medicine as a 2-year advanced training phase for prospective GPs.
  • The curriculum family medicine allows access to attractive training positions that prepare for later work in primary care.
  • 2-year curriculum consisting of three rotations in specialties relevant to GPs (6 months each) and a 6-month practice assistant
  • Rotations into Emergency Medicine, Psychiatry and Geriatrics (Full Time)
  • Swiss doctor’s diploma or EU doctor’s diploma (MEBEKO recognition required)
