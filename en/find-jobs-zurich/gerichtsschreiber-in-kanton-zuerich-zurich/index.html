Full description

Administrative Court of the Canton of Zurich Court Clerk 80 - 100% The Administrative Court is the highest cantonal court in the area of ​​administrative and constitutional law. The 4th department deals with legal remedies in the areas of immigration, personnel, education and civil rights, political rights, the commercial register, information rights, financial equalization and state contributions, hunting, agriculture, fishing, building insurance, civil status, the hospitality industry and culture. We are looking for a 80-100% court reporter to join our team on November 1, 2026 or by appointment.

  • Tasks As a court reporter, you will write judgment applications, participate in the negotiations in an advisory capacity and be responsible for editing the judgments. Profile After completing your law studies at a Swiss university with very good qualifications, you have already gained professional experience and expressed your interest in public law issues with practical or academic work. Ideally, you have a license to practice law and/or a dissertation. Swiss nationality is generally required. You recognize the essentials and can quickly grasp complex issues and present them in an easy-to-understand manner. Your strengths lie in your structured and analytical approach as well as your careful way of working. The interesting and varied work requires the ability to do precise legal work and linguistic fluency in drafting and editing judgments. You will also be happy to familiarize yourself with previously unknown issues and areas of law.

Offer We attach great importance to collegial cooperation, professional exchange as well as the further training and development of our court clerks. The salary is based on the implementation regulations for the Personnel Act (starting wage usually in wage class 19). Application Have we aroused your interest? Then send your application with the usual documents and an extract from your criminal record to the department president, Dr. Tamara Nüssle to bewerbungen@vgrzh.ch. For further information, please contact the chief court clerk, Dr. Sonja Güntert is happy to help (Tel. 043 257 50 00).
