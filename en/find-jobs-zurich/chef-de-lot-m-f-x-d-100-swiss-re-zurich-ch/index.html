Full description

In support of our production team of the cold kitchen of the employee restaurant and the takeaway "Get & Give", which complements our employee restaurant Klubhaus, we are looking for a committed personality as Chef de Partie. In this role, you will assume the following main tasks:

Their main tasks

  • Collaboration in the production of the cold and warm offer for the take away area "Get & Give"
  • Preparation of the varied salad selection for the salad/vegetable buffet in the staff restaurant
  • Introducing ideas for the further development of the Take away offer
  • Ensuring a professional lunch service at the front as well as advising our guests in the Take away area
  • Support for daily ordering
  • Supporting the de-pureting/sauctioning post as required
  • Collaboration at evening events at Campus Mythenquai
  • Compliance with hygiene standards according to food law and internal requirements.
  • Working hours are from Monday – Friday, mainly from 06:00 – 15:00 and evening assignments on occasions
  • Entrance by 1. December 2026 or by agreement
  • Your place of work is Zurich Mythenquai

Swiss Re Gastronomy – Your working environment Corporate Real Estate & Services Zurich (CRES Zurich) is a division of Swiss Re Management Ltd, which is responsible for managing the internal catering and hotel industry. In addition to the joint gastronomy in Zurich, CRES Zurich runs the Guest Restaurant K2 and the Casual Restaurant as well as the conference center with hotel operation Centre for Global Dialogue in Rüschlikon. Under one roof, the Club House on Campus Mythenquai brings together a total of seven efficiently organised gastronomic sub-concepts. On the roof is the dining garden, where Swiss Re Gastronomy grows vegetables, herbs, flowers and salads for immediate use and for durable kiosks and giveaways. They bring with them

  • Successfully completed vocational apprenticeship as chef/chef EFZ
  • At least five years of professional experience, ideally in different restaurants
  • High service orientation and high quality awareness
  • A well-groomed appearance and good manners
  • In addition to having very good knowledge of German You also have good oral English skills

What we offer

  • Varied and well-maintained working environment with different concepts and diverse starting formats
  • Modern, appreciative leadership culture, involvement of employees in decisions
  • Living sustainability, regionality, responsibility towards the environment
  • Internal development opportunities
  • Provided uniform, including cleaning
  • Highly discounted ZVV subscription
  • Free fitness options
  • Above-average pension fund, pension and insurance benefits
