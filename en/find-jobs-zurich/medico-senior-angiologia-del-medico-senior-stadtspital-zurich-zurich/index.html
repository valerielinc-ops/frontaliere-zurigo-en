Full description

In order to expand the Faculty of Angiology, we are looking for a committed and competent personality immediately or by agreement.

Tasks

  • You will lead an independent angiological consultation and engage in consiliar treatment and care of our inpatients with angiological issues.
  • With vascular surgery and interventional radiology in the vascular center, you maintain a close interdisciplinary cooperation.
  • If you are interested, you have the opportunity to independently perform minimally invasive varicose interventions (lasers, sclerotherapy).
  • In addition, you promote a training and further education of our assistant doctors at a high academic level based on constructive feedback and close cooperation.

Profile

  • Swiss or Swiss recognised specialist title for angiology
  • Proven practical experience in the field
  • Communicative and team-capable personality with high social competence
  • Interest and pleasure in interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Development possibility for the function of a senior physician

We offer

We offer you more than just a job! Our city hospital world is characterized by an open dual culture, an uncomplicated, personal togetherness, respect and appreciation. As a team of more than 120 professional groups, we work every day in very different ways for the well-being of our patients. The constructive cooperation with our partners in the vascular center and in the associated departments (cardiology, neurology, nephrology and rheumatology) makes the task very versatile. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions, family-friendly conditions with at least six weeks of holidays and service compensation as well as excellent social benefits from the city of Zurich. In addition, there is an attractive development perspective up to the function of a senior doctor. An established and motivated team is looking forward to working with you!

About us

The Stadtspital Zürich is one of the top 10 Swiss hospitals with over 36'000 inpatient and around 455,000 outpatients per year. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,500 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
