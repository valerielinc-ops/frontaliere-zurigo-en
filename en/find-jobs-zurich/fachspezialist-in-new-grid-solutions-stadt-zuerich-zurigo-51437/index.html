Full description

As a specialist in New Grid Solutions, you are designing the distribution network of the future with data, digitalization and innovative solutions.

Tasks

  • The co-development of ewz smart grid systems as well as the independent analysis of data from different source systems are part of your everyday life
  • You will work on smart grid concepts and grid-side specifications for the efficient integration of consumer, energy generation and storage systems into the distribution network.
  • You create the foundations for smart grid and network solutions and evaluate them technologically, economically and with a view to their use.
  • Innovation projects guide or accompany you with your expertise and prepare insights and results for expert committees in a comprehensible way.
  • Together with partners, you drive data analysis, smart grid topics, the implementation of the UseCAS system and the professional knowledge transfer within the company.

Profile

  • You will also receive a degree in electrical engineering, energy technology or a similar technical field with a connection to the grid.
  • With distribution networks, local generation, storage or electromobility you know your way around and bring your first experience in network planning.
  • You have proven experience in data analysis and in handling suitable analysis tools for assessing complex data; knowledge of Python and Power BI is a plus.
  • Technical connections are holistic and ideally bring a business-related training.
  • You communicate with the addressee, like to work in the team and convince with your own initiative as well as joy in innovation and knowledge transfer.

We offer

  • More energy for your life: mobile work, flexible work models and part-time opportunities enable you to actively shape your working time and divide it into the way it suits your life best. For example, you can shop for up to 2 weeks extra holidays and work part-time at all levels. Job I/O/-spli-t and unpaid holiday are also possible with us.
  • Sustainable and discounted travel: With the mobility contribution of up to 600 francs per year, you travel cheaply and sustainably – whether you want to cycle through Zurich by bike or explore Switzerland by train.
  • An investment in your future: We not only value paying fair and transparent wages, but also ensure that you have more of your wages. We take over 60% of the savings contributions and offer an over-compulsory interest on the capital saved.
  • A boost to your leisure time: We pay you 100 francs a month on your lunch check card. You also profit from additional Fringe Bernards worth 150 francs a year and attractive mobile subscriptions.
  • Development and further education: With our talent management, we specifically promote your development – through a wide range of internal and external training opportunities as well as exciting development opportunities.
  • Meaningful work: Sustainability and sun-sustainable work are not just words for us: Join us in shaping Zurich and the future. Let energy be released for a good morning together with us.
