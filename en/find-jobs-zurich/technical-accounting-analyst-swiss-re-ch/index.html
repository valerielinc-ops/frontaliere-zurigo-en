Full description

Are you passionate about accounting and ready to make an impact at one of the world's leadingreinsurance companies? Join our Operations team in São Paulo and help us deliver exceptionalservice to our clients. About the Role As a Technical Accounting Analyst, you'll be at the core of our reinsurance operations — ensuring financial accuracy, managing complex transactions, and keeping our operational engine running smoothly. Key Responsibilities

  • Book and process accounting transactions accurately and in a timely manner across complex reinsurance portfolios
  • Manage billing and collection cycles, ensuring invoices are issued correctly and payments are tracked and followed up
  • Identify and resolve account and payment discrepancies, working closely with clients and internal teams to reach swift resolutions
  • Interpret complex reinsurance agreements to ensure transactions are processed in line with contractual terms
  • Participate in projects and special tasks, provide training and assistance to team members, and conduct root cause and failure analysis of workflows

About the Team You'll join a motivated Operations team managing Reinsurance portfolios for Brazil & South Cone. We collaborate daily with global peers to deliver fast, effective, and high-quality service. Our continuous improvement mindset keeps us at the forefront of reinsurance operations.

About You

You are a dynamic professional who brings energy and clarity to every challenge. You communicate with confidence, listen actively, and know how to turn complex problems into practical solutions. You thrive in fast-paced environments, adapt quickly to change, and bring a proactive, solution-oriented mindset to your work — making you a valued partner to both clients and colleagues alike.

We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements

  • 1–3 years of experience in insurance, reinsurance, or a related field, with end-to-end reinsurance operations knowledge
  • Strong financial accounting knowledge applied to complex reinsurance transactions
  • Advanced Excel skills and enthusiasm for data analysis; intermediate MS Office proficiency
  • Degree in Accounting, Finance, Actuarial Sciences, Mathematics, Statistics, or Insurance
  • Intermediate English proficiency

These are additional nice to haves

  • Experience with Power BI and R for data insights
  • Spanish proficiency for South Cone client engagement
  • Advanced reinsurance technical accounting expertise in Life & Health
  • Strong ability to prioritize and manage competing deadlines

Our company has a hybrid work model where the expectation is that you will be in the office at least three days per week.
