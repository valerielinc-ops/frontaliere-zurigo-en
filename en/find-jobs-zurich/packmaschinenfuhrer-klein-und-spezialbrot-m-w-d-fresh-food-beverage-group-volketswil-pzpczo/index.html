Full description

We produce favourite products that inspire - in Switzerland and for Switzerland. Do you have experience in the food sector and a technical understanding of machines and packaging systems? Do you enjoy working in a team and are you excited to work in the latest production plant of Migros Industrie? Great, then apply now to the most popular bakery in Switzerland.

Luogo di lavoro: Fresh Food & Beverage Group, headquartered in Volketswil, Erlenwiesenstrasse 9, 8604 Volketswil

Important notes

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post will be disposed of for data protection reasons and will not be returned.

Mansioni

  • Setup, control, operation and monitoring of production and packaging systems
  • Making different doughs
  • Quality control of manufactured products
  • Identify problems and ensure troubleshooting
  • Optimising processes and systems as part of our continuous improvement processes
  • Compliance with safety, hygiene and quality regulations
  • Active participation in production in the early, late and night shifts
  • Training of learners in the area of responsibility
  • General cleaning work

Props

  • Experience as an investment manager: an advantage
  • Completed apprenticeship (EFZ): Food technologist, baker, confectioner, cook or similar (EBA also possible)
  • Clean, accurate and speedy way of working
  • Flexibility and physical and mental resilience
  • Reliable, team-oriented, responsible and independent personality
  • Advanced technical understanding
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa offriamo

  • Mobile subscription: Discounted mobile plans with Wingo, Digitec and Sunrise for you and, if necessary, for three other people you know.
  • Occupational pensions: Comprehensive protection in the Migros Pension Fund – with first-class and attractive benefits.
  • Social benefits: 730 days of daily sickness allowance with full net salary.
  • Employee offers: Cumulus employee points, discounted mobile subscriptions, higher interest rates Migros Bank, club school/fitness fees and more.
  • Holidays: 5 weeks holiday for everyone, 6 weeks holiday from 50. Age year, 7 weeks holiday from 60. Age year, purchase of additional vacation days possible.
  • Du-Kultur: Hoi, hoi zämä, sali - we are on a first-name basis!

Contatto

Application & Contact Carmen Peluso HR Recruiter

Recruitment Process

  • Pre-selection of applications (FFB-Group): We review received applications on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release it to the manager for viewing. The decision on whether to advance or leave the recruitment process is communicated by telephone or e-mail.
  • Discussion with HR & specialist department (FFB Group): The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. The main aim is to learn more about personnel and professional skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit of the company and team.
  • Trial work: A trial day or half-day gives us the opportunity to get to know the future working environment and the team members better. And vice versa, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Final meeting before the contract is issued: Before the contract is issued, we conduct a final meeting to clarify open questions from both sides and to settle contractual details. Once all points have been clarified, the written offer follows. Duration until response: Up to one week
  • Offer & Contract Issuance (Digital): After our verbal job offer, the most important contract points will be verified again. After agreement, the contract will be commissioned and sent by e-mail for digital signature. Duration until response: Up to one week
