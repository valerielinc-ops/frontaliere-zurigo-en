About us

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

Together, we provide holistic care and comprehensive care for the residents in the decentralized care housing groups of the health centers. The nursing housing groups can accommodate groups between eight and 22 people with dementia or gerontopsychiatric disease. In a family atmosphere, comprehensive care and care are offered. The focus is on the individual, everyday and needs-oriented support of the residents and their relatives.
