Role overview

The basic research laboratories of Department of Reproductive Endocrinology of the University Hospital Zürich are located in Schlieren and consists of three research groups that study 1) basic mechanisms of early embryogenesis; 2) molecular and immunological mechanisms underlying endometriosis; and 3) the influence of extracellular vesicles contribute to oocyte and female reproductive health.

We are currently looking for a research technician/biosafety officer to support our research activities and to coordinate biosafety related matters.

More info on the department and our research groups can be found here: https://www.reproendo.uzh.ch/en.html
