Full description

For the contact and contact point barracks we are looking for 1. March 2027 or by arrangement for 6-8 months a motivated person.

Tasks

  • You maintain contact with the interior visitors and support the clients in physical, psychological and social stabilization.
  • You perform motivational work, triage and crisis interventions.
  • You look after the cooperating clients (cooking support, counter service, etc.).
  • You monitor and supervise the consumer rooms and provide emergency medical assistance and basic care as well as secondary prevention (HIV and hepatitis).
  • You work in the shift operation So-Di from 7 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. and Mi-Sa from 10:30 a.m. to 7 p.m.

Profile

  • Ongoing studies at a technical college in social work (preferably 2nd internship and at least 25 years old)
  • Resilience, self-confidence and flexibility
  • Independent working methods and teamwork
  • Readiness for irregular shift services with evening and weekend assignments
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of IT users

We offer

An unusual task in a low-threshold offer with demanding clients. In addition, you can expect an instructive and exciting working environment in which you can further develop your skills.

About us

With around 2,400 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a social Zurich. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.

The contact and contact points (K&A) provide drug-addicted adults with safe consumer spaces and low-threshold access to survival assistance and counselling.
