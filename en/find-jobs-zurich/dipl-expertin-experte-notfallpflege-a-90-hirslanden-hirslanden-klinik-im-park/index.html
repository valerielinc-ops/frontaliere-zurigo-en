Full description

Location: Hirslanden Clinic in the Park | Zurich

Occupation by: from now until 31. December 2027

Type of employment: Temporary

Reference number: 74018

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

Our Emergency Center is available for 365 days 24 hours. The ultimate goal is holistic, competent, safe and forward-looking medical care as well as nursing care for our emergency patients. For this purpose, we have a state-of-the-art infrastructure and state-of-the-art diagnostic and therapeutic options.

YOUR TASKS

  • Comprehensive and individual care of the interdisciplinary emergency patients based on the care process
  • Triage of all entering patients and responsibility for initial assessment
  • Identification and control of emergency situations including initiation of appropriate measures
  • Responsibility for the correct as well as complete documentation and performance recording of patients
  • Assistance in the supervision and support of learners, interns and new employees

Your profile

  • Completed training for/for dipl. Nursing professional
  • At least 2 years of professional experience in an acute hospital and/or experience in an emergency department
  • Very good knowledge of German (verbal and written)
  • Flexible, positioned, motivated and team-oriented personality
  • Professional appearance and respectful manners

Your Advantages

You can expect a modern workplace on the right shore of Lake Zurich and an experienced, inter- and multi-professional team. You will work with a modern medical clinic information system and benefit from a wide range of further education and development opportunities.

Flexible working conditions help you to optimally combine work and private life. In addition, we offer regionally market-oriented remuneration as well as attractive social and wage benefits. Employees who are required to change clothes receive four additional paid days off per year for a 100 percent fee. With a reduced workload, the free days are adjusted accordingly. Employees on an hourly basis receive an equivalent financial compensation.

A motivated, committed and multi-professional team awaits you in an advanced working environment. This is characterized by high quality of care, independence, flexibility and a sense of responsibility. We also offer our employees generous social benefits, sports offers, special insurance conditions and further education opportunities within the framework of our program.

Benefits for employees
