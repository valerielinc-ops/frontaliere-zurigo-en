Full description

For the school Aegerten we are looking for 1. February 2027 or by agreement a successor

Tasks

As Head of Home Service and Technology, they bear the overall responsibility for the maintenance and operation of the Aegerten School and the associated external objects. The school Aegerten is located in district 3 (Alt-Wiedikon) and currently comprises 4 kindergartens, 5 lower and 5 intermediate classes as well as full-day care from 7 a.m. to 6 p.m. The main tasks of the management of domestic service and technology include:

  • Management of all domestic service personnel
  • Recruitment of new employees
  • Administrative and organisational work, including preparation of operational plans and securing deputies
  • Responsible for maintenance, intermediate and basic cleaning (this also includes own cleaning work)
  • Regular inspection of buildings as well as technical equipment and equipment incl. Key management
  • Cooperation with craftsmen and administrative departments
  • Supporting the management team in school development and working closely with the entire school team and other beneficiaries of the school facility

Profile

  • Eidg. Specialist ID Card Housekeeper / Maintenance
  • Completed apprenticeship (preferably in the craft-technical field) or equivalent training
  • Professional experience preferably in the field of domestic service
  • Personnel management experience
  • Very good knowledge of modern information and communication technologies and very good oral and written knowledge of German
  • Resilient and flexible personality with high social, organizational and communication skills
  • Willingness to work closely with different stakeholders

We offer

... a varied and meaningful activity in a lively and multicultural environment. Our employees also benefit from attractive employment conditions according to static guidelines.

About us

The school district Uto is one of seven school districts in the city of Zurich and comprises districts 2 and 3 with the districts Leimbach, Wollishofen, Enge, Friesenberg and Alt-Wiedikon.
