Role overview

The Opportunity At Hitachi Energy, we're accelerating the energy transition by building a more sustainable, flexible and secure energy system.

As our Global Tax Operations Lead , you will have a unique opportunity to develop, lead and strengthen a recently defined global tax function that serves as the operational backbone of our Tax organisation.

In this highly visible leadership role, you will advance, scale and continuously improve a global Tax Operations organisation responsible for delivering compliant, efficient and standardised tax processes across multiple regions.

Company and context

  • How You'll Make an Impact Lead and strengthen the global Tax Operations function, enhancing its operating model, governance framework, and organizational effectiveness.
  • Drive the execution of consistent, accurate and compliant end-to-end transactional tax processes, thrive continuous improvement in tax compliance, data quality, and reporting effectiveness across all regions.
  • Drive large-scale process transitions, integrations and stabilisation activities while maintaining business continuity throughout transformation programmes.

  • Set and monitor KPIs and quality standards to ensure effective and efficient execution of the tax strategy.
