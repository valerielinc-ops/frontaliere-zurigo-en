Full description

Personal – competent – close Welcome to the Hospital Uster, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As the central pillar of health care in the Oberland region of Zurich and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you experience the highest level of expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and promoted in your professional development. Spital Uster is a teaching hospital of the Medical Faculty of the University of Zurich. Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of a “regional and digital health hub in Züri East”! At the interdisciplinary emergency department of the Uster Hospital, approximately 25,000 patients of all ages are treated annually. As a SGNOR-certified emergency department, we work in a team at a high quality level and enjoy regular interdisciplinary training. The theoretical lessons are attended at the higher technical school Z-INA in Zurich. We will accompany you when you complete your postgraduate studies in emergency care.

Exciting challenges await you

  • You will be qualified and practised in professional initial assessment (triage), medical emergency care and support of emergency patients including pediatric cases.
  • You apply the newly learned emergency care measures in highly complex and rapidly changing patient situations and support / accompany the relatives in the emergency situation.
  • You work closely with other professional groups involved in the treatment process and shape an appreciative team culture.
  • You form and shape your career. You will be accompanied and supported by an experienced and reliable vocational training team. The expertise of your colleagues round off your theoretical and practical training.

What makes you stand out for the job

  • Degree as a Dipl. Nurse HF/FH with 6 months of professional experience in an acute hospital
  • high motivation to learn new things, interest in accompanying people and their relatives in emergency situations and rapidly changing care situations
  • Self-responsible working and learning in fast-moving and unpredictable everyday life
  • Flair for devices and modern techniques
  • Applicants with a foreign diploma must already be in the SRC recognition process and have very good oral and written knowledge of German (C1 degree).

6 reasons to study with us

  • solid training support by competent vocational trainers for achieving the learning objectives during the course of studies including regular learning transfer days
  • Assumption of financial training costs
  • close and constructive cooperation in an interprofessional team
  • Family atmosphere and very good social benefits
  • at least five weeks holidays and joker days for changing time
  • Central location near Uster station and motorway connection
  • ZVV BonusPass or cheap parking, max. CHF 4.50 per day
  • Parking time during the night shift (until 07:00)
