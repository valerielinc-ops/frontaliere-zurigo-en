Full description

Portrait The school Affoltern i.E. is a rural school in the Emmental, where students from the villages Affoltern, Weier and Hausmoos are taught. Currently 83 children from kindergarten to 6th grade attend our school.

As a small, family school, it is important to us to accompany and coach the children in the best possible way. Appreciative and benevolent cooperation with all parties is very important to us.

Tasks

At the 1st / 2nd grade mixed class, 24 SuS you as co - class teacher take 16 - 20 lessons. Math, German, NMG, BG, Music / MGS or by agreement Timetable: Mo gT, Di gT, Wednesday morning, Do gT Constructive cooperation with the class team is a prerequisite for successful teaching.

Requirements

Teaching qualification as primary teacher Lesson Experience Cycle 1
