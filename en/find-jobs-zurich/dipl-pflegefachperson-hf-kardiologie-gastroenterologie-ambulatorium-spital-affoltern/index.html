Full description

You would like to use your skills in different areas, like to work independently and appreciate a team in which you support each other? This could be your next challenge! For our Ambulatorium we are looking for a committed Dipl from January 2027. Nurse HF, who enjoys a versatile job and is happy to take on responsibility. With us you can expect exciting insights into cardiology and gastroenterology as well as varied tasks in the ambulatorium. No day is like the other – and that is exactly what makes our work so interesting! Your tasks in cardiology

  • They prepare diagnostic examinations and assist them – for example in rest and exercise ECGs
  • They coordinate appointments and work closely with our doctors and other specialists
  • You will perform administrative tasks and carefully electronically document your work Your tasks in gastroenterology
  • They assist in gastroenterological examinations, especially in gastroscopies and colonoscopies
  • They look after and monitor our ambulatory and inpatient patients before, during and after the examinations
  • They ensure the professional preparation of the equipment and reliably comply with our hygiene requirements
  • You are actively involved in the organization and help ensure that everything runs smoothly in everyday life.
  • They independently prepare infusion therapies, administer and monitor them – for example iron infusions or immunoglobulins
  • They accompany our patients competently and attentively and ensure that they feel well looked after by us. Your profile
  • You have completed training as a dipl. Care professional HF
  • You have several years of professional experience, knowledge of cardiology and/or gastroenterology is a plus
  • They work independently, structured and forward-looking and keep an overview even in lively situations
  • You are helpful, flexible and a real team player
  • They have good IT knowledge and enjoy handling medical equipment and technical equipment
  • You like to take part, think and get involved with your ideas We offer a team that holds together. Tasks that remain exciting. Working hours that fit life.
  • A small, experienced team: We support each other, meet at eye level and value a pleasant working atmosphere
  • Schedulable working hours: With us you work regularly and without weekend or pikett services
  • Diversity instead of routine: You work in different specialist areas and can use your skills broadly and further develop them
  • Responsibility and participation: your experience, your ideas and your initiative are in demand
  • Attractive employment conditions: We offer you progressive framework conditions and attractive further education opportunities Our culture – Your benefits «Bi öis can mer no»: With us it is family and personal. As part of the Affoltern hospital team, you will also benefit from attractive employment conditions and numerous Fringe Benefits: Jokertag, shopping benefits and two free admission subscriptions to the FORTYSEVEN Wellness Bathrooms are just a few examples of our Fringe Benefits. The Hospital Affoltern is the largest employer and training company in the Knonau Office. Around 550 employees work for the well-being of patients every day. Find out more about us in our portrait.
