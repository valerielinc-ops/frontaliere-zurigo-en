Programme Coordinator:in Health Sciences and Technology (m/f/d) 80%-100%, Zurich, perpetual Descrizione The Department of Health Sciences and Technology (D-HEST) of ETH Zurich is looking for support and further development of the programme Health Sciences and Technology (HST) as of 1. December 2026 or by appointment a committed and experienced person as degree programme coordinator:in HST. The post will be filled as a result of retirement. The degree program HST combines health science, natural science and technological perspectives. In this central function, you coordinate the degree program at bachelor and master level, advise students and lecturers and contribute to the quality assurance and further development of teaching. Job Description As a Degree Programme Coordinator: in HST, you take on a key role at the interface of students, lecturers, study direction, study administration, academic services and department. Your main tasks include:

  • Coordination and further development of the degree programme HST at bachelor and master level, including planning and implementation of substantive reforms
  • Support of the study direction in curricular, regulatory and strategic questions, coordination of the teaching offer and financial planning
  • Management of the teaching committee including preparation, logging and follow-up of resolutions
  • Advice of students in individual and partly complex study situations (about 300 students begin each year, a total of about 1200)
  • Advice of lecturers in course-related questions, in particular on teaching offers, examination forms, tutorates and administrative processes
  • Coordination of information and introductory events for prospective students, bachelor, master and exchange students
  • Processing of course-related inquiries and applications, in particular on study planning, subject crediting, grade conferences and master admission
  • HST-specific mobility advice for outgoing and incoming students
  • Analysis and preparation of key figures in the teaching area as well as participation in reporting and quality assurance
  • Representation of the degree programme in relevant ETH committees, projects and networks in the area of teaching
  • Assumption of teaching responsibility in the course You have:
  • A university degree in a field relevant to Health Sciences and Technology
  • Experience in the university environment, ideally in teaching coordination, study consulting or a comparable function
  • A good understanding of academic structures and processes in the field of teaching
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in words and writing
  • Pronounced communication skills and pleasure in working with different stakeholders
  • A structured, pragmatic and solution-oriented working method
  • High service orientation with a clear understanding of roles
  • Very good organizational ability, reliability and self-initiative
  • Resilience, discretion and safe appearance even in demanding consulting situations A doctorate, teaching experience and a didactic training are advantageous, but not absolutely necessary.
  • Cosa offriamo
  • We offer you a versatile and responsible activity in a dynamic academic environment with high personal responsibility and design possibilities. You work on an interdisciplinary degree program that combines health science, natural science and technological perspectives.
