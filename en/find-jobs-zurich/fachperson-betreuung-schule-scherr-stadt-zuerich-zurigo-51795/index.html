Full description

We are looking for reinforcement by 01.10.2026 or by agreement.

Tasks

As a specialist you are responsible for the pedagogical management of morning care from 7:00 to 8:00 a.m. on 5 days per week. They work together with an educational assistance assistant. You take your holidays in the school-free time.

Their tasks include

  • Participation in school-supplementary care and free time for children
  • Leading large groups of children, as well as parental contacts
  • Domestic and administrative work

Profile

  • Apprenticeship certificate as a specialist care or infant educator/in EFZ mandatory
  • For applications with a foreign diploma, a Swiss diploma recognition must be attached.
  • Professional experience in caring for children (hoard, crèche, home)
  • Enjoy working with teachers and carers
  • Flexible, motivated and balanced personality, the joy of dealing with people of different cultures, as well as the willingness for interdisciplinary cooperation in the school team
  • Good knowledge of German

We offer

a varied, versatile, independent and responsible activity and a salary according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich.

About us

The Scherr Day School is located in the Oberstrass Quartier and includes a morning table, lunch/evening services and the Open Restaurant. At the school Scherr up to 250 children are cared for on peak days. Our goal is to bring the children together into a community based on mutual respect, independence and self-responsibility. Our basic pedagogical attitude is based on the concept of the “New Authority” according to Haim Omer.
