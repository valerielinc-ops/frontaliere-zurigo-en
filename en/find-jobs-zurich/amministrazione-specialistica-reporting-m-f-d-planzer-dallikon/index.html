Spezialist Administration & Reporting (m/w/d) — Planzer

Full description

Real professionals are in demand here – not only on the road, but also in logistics and our own workshops. Our state-of-the-art fleet of around 1,800 vehicles deserves regular maintenance and precise repairs. Are you joining us?

What you move

  • You keep administrative everyday life running – from correspondence and appointment management to the maintenance of customer, business and vehicle master data
  • With reports, statistics and evaluations for fleet management, you bring relevant data to the point
  • In projects, you participate and support in the creation and maintenance of documents, forms and lists
  • High data quality is important to you: you work carefully and conscientiously and adhere to defined quality standards reliably
  • You keep processes and workflows clearly and take care of a structured storage with cleanly maintained system and process documentation

Why do you succeed?

  • You have a commercial education or equivalent training in your pocket and ideally already gained several years of experience in an administrative, analytical or data-oriented function.
  • It is up to you to analyze, prepare and interpret data – from this you derive meaningful reporting, key figures and decision-making principles for fleet management.
  • You master Excel very well and even complex formulas, pivot tables, data preparation and evaluations are part of everyday life for you. With larger amounts of data you handle structured and secure
  • Independent, precise and solution-oriented – this is how you work. At the same time, you question existing processes and bring your own initiative to sustainably develop processes, systems and data quality.
  • In exchange with different contacts, you communicate safely and professionally. Good knowledge of French and/or Italian is a plus, as well as experience in the vehicle or leasing industry

Your Benefits

  • Further education and development opportunities: With us you can develop yourself - we promote and challenge talents
  • Family culture: You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Free parking: Get ready for parking - with us you can park for free

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
