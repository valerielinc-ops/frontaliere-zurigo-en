Full description

Do you want to design road infrastructure that is used by thousands of people every day? In this role, you take responsibility for a wide range of repair projects in an exciting catchment area – from Dietikon to the left shore of Lake Zurich. You control your projects independently from planning to implementation, coordinate different participants and ensure that quality, costs and deadlines are right. Your tasks

  • You plan and organize our civil engineering and road repair projects from start to finish.
  • You are responsible for costs, deadlines and quality and keep an eye on your projects throughout all phases.
  • You coordinate planners, companies and other project participants and ensure a smooth project flow.
  • You represent the interests of the canton towards municipalities, authorities, companies and other stakeholders.
  • You ensure compliance with the relevant standards and specifications and ensure comprehensible project documentation. Your profile
  • You have an apprenticeship as a civil engineer (FH/ETH), construction manager or comparable technical training and bring in-depth practical experience in civil engineering or road construction.
  • You organize and plan ahead and lead your projects to success independently.
  • You think economically, set priorities and find viable solutions for different interests.
  • You act safely towards different stakeholders and communicate clearly and appropriately.
  • You work solution-oriented and keep an overview even in demanding project situations. With us, you take on projects whose impact is immediately visible and get the freedom to design them independently. You can expect a versatile project portfolio in a dynamic region and a collegial environment in which you know each other, support and find solutions together. Your experience and ideas are in demand – and you can thus play a concrete role in shaping the street infrastructure of the Canton of Zurich.
