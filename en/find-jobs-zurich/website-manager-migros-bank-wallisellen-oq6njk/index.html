Website-Manager — Migros Bank

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Our website is one of Migros Bank’s most important showcases – and you take it to the next level. You are responsible for the entire lifecycle and the further development of our website and the digital ecosystem. As a designer and initiator, you ensure with strategic foresight and hands-on mentality that our website remains a digital point of contact for customers.

Mansioni

  • You take over the ownership: You are the heart of our website, responsible for its operation and ensure that it is technically stable, up-to-date and perfectly operational at all times.
  • You set impulses: You drive the further development of the website, bring your own ideas, develop a clear roadmap and set priorities that combine operational and strategic goals.
  • You lead through expertise: You are the driving force behind SEO and GEO optimizations, ensuring clear content and ensuring we remain visible online and in AI search results.
  • You are a bridge-builder: You are a sparring partner for business owners and stakeholders, moderate between requirements and make well-founded, prioritized decisions with a view to the big picture.
  • You think economically: You keep an eye on costs and expenses, get offers and make sure that all website projects are implemented economically and efficiently.

Requirements

  • > 5 years in a comparable position: Product Onwer / Business Engineer / Project Manager
  • Bachelor’s degree (university, university of applied sciences): Business Informatics, Mediamatics or Digital Marketing
  • Experience and passion: You have deep experience in the operation and development of complex, sales-oriented websites, as well as interest in digital content, brands & communications.
  • Technical understanding: You are familiar with CMS systems and digital processes and have a good understanding of SEO and GEO basics.
  • Leadership qualities: You are decisive, strong in communication and have a good feeling for our target groups. You take responsibility and lead topics with clarity and conviction.
  • Analytical thinking: Experience with cloud costs, data analytics and metrics is a plus, but not a must. It is important that you think economically and can make data-based decisions.
  • Experience in the CMS Magnolia: Ideally, you already have knowledge in dealing with the Magnolia CMS and enjoy creating, managing and continuously optimizing content efficiently.
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • English (very good knowledge)

  • Design and decision-making leeway: Optimal possibilities for contributing ideas and know-how
