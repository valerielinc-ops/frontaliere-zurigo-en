80% - 100% | Banking Operations, Sales and Customer Consulting | Zurich | Experienced Professionals Together with your colleagues, you are passionate about supporting more than 70,000 corporate customers along their most important corporate events. You will accompany SMEs from start-up through the growth phase to company succession and also advise on private concerns of entrepreneurs. You act as a reliable contact person and combine professional competence with proximity to customers. Your workplace is centrally located in the lively district 5 near the station Hardbrücke, alternating with regular assignments in our branches Oerlikon and Dietikon. If you enjoy working with corporate customers and want to actively shape the future of our area, then you are a good fit for us. Your tasks

  • Advising founders for a quick and smooth start to self-employment

  • Active needs and potential discovery as well as structured potential recognition and exploitation with the help of our digital consulting platform

Your profile

  • Experience in financing and investment business
  • Enjoying the ideas and challenges of companies and their owners
  • Strong communication skills and experience in customer advice by telephone and in personal conversation
  • Very good knowledge of German and English, further language skills are a plus
