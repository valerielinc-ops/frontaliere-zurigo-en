Working at Sprüngli

Are you looking for a new professional challenge in which you can use both your leadership experience and passion and are a structured organizational talent with a flair for data? We look forward to your application. In our manufacturer in Dietikon, we are looking for an experienced, motivated and committed controlling department from now on or by agreement. *n

What you do with us

In our Controlling department, you lead our three-person Controlling team and are responsible for the financial management of the company – with the aim of creating transparency, enabling informed decisions and actively shaping sustainable corporate success.

  • Lead and develop our three-person controlling team
  • Responsible for operational and strategic controlling
  • Create budgets, forecasts and monthly and annual financial statements
  • Analyze key figures and derive sound recommendations for action
  • Advises the management as competent sparring partner
  • Optimizes controlling processes and further develops reporting instruments
  • Actively contributes to the economic development of our company
  • You bring in new ideas and actively act in your role

What we want

  • Completed Commercial Training (EFZ)
  • Minimum 10 years of professional experience in controlling incl. Further education in the controlling field
  • You already have leadership experience and have already completed a leadership training
  • Experience in factory controlling
  • You are strong in dealing with the office programs and can organize your task area independently
  • Very good SAP knowledge (especially S/4 Hana)
  • Affinity for numbers and strong strategic thinking
  • Strong Excel knowledge
  • You appreciate and promote cooperation with other internal bodies
  • Independent, well-structured and committed working method also in intensive phases
  • Open, reliable and communicative personality with quick comprehension
  • Style-confident knowledge of German in writing and language
  • Knowledge in the food industry is an advantage
  • Analytical, entrepreneurial personality with a structured and solution-oriented way of working

You can look forward to it.

A modern and bright workplace awaits you in a traditional atmosphere with a good working atmosphere and in a motivated team. We are happy to take care of the work clothes and their cleaning. We offer you good employment conditions such as 5 weeks holidays, an additional day off in the birthday month, a free parking space and generous shopping discounts. There is the possibility of one home office day per week.
