Become part of our team!

More than 60 apprentices are currently completing their training at Raiffeisen Switzerland, the service and competence center of the Raiffeisen banks. Do you want to be a part of it? At the same time, we guarantee you professional student support throughout your apprenticeship.

What awaits you?

During your teaching

During your apprenticeship, you will immerse yourself in a versatile education, in which you will gain insight into the complex and interesting ICT field of Raiffeisen.

  • As an ICT specialist you install, maintain, configure a wide range of ICT devices and ensure their operation.
  • You support users in the handling and application of ICT devices and help them solve problems.
  • You will run through a wide range of departments in Raiffeisen Switzerland at the modern location at Zurich Airport (The Circle).
