Full description

80% - 100% | IT / Business Engineering | Zurich | Experienced professionals Do you value semantic HTML, scalable front-end architectures and consider accessibility not as an afterthought feature, but as an integral part of modern UI development? Then you are exactly right in our design system team «Tetrizz». Together with the UX/UI team, we are working on the design system of the Zürcher Kantonalbank and are developing the UI toolkit, which is used in numerous web applications at the customer interface. As a team, we are responsible for the internal framework (including Angular implementation) and define the technical foundations for consistent, accessible and reusable user interfaces. Your tasks

  • Further development of the internal frontend framework with HTML, SCSS and JavaScript/TypeScript
  • Design, analysis and implementation of UI components
  • Further development and modernization of the Angular implementation
  • Maintaining the design system in terms of consistency, reusability and scalability in close cooperation with UX/UI
  • Writing technical documentation, guidelines and best practices
  • Supporting development teams on framework application and front-end topics
  • Introducing new technical and methodological impulses around front-end architecture, accessibility and design system engineering

Your profile

  • Compellingly up-to-date in-depth knowledge in Angular and in building component libraries
  • Several years of experience in frontend development with modern web technologies
  • In-depth knowledge of HTML, SCSS/CSS and JavaScript/TypeScript
  • Good knowledge of accessibility and implementation of WCAG guidelines
  • Interest or experience with design systems (multibrand beneficial)
  • A keen eye for UI details and enjoyment of high code quality and consistent user experience
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

Our offer

  • Interesting and varied tasks in a dynamic environment
  • A motivated, self-organized team and #ITmadeinChreis5
  • We offer great freedom for autonomous decisions and participation
  • A culture of appreciation and continuous (further) development – personal, technical and professional
  • Your career is important to us as an employer and therefore we support and accompany you on your way
  • Attractive overall package, which in addition to the attractive basic salary and a variable remuneration offers many benefits and perks – from A such as retirement savings to Z such as zoo tickets
