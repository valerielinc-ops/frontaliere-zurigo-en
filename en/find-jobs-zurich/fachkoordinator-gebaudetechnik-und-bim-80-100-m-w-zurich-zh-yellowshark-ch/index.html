01 Future employer

For our client, an established and innovative company in the field of building services engineering, we are looking for a committed professional as an HVACSE+ Specialist Coordinator.

Our client delivers challenging projects in the field of technical building systems and relies on modern planning methods, digital processes and close interdisciplinary collaboration. Employees have considerable scope to shape their work, can assume responsibility and actively advance their professional development.

02 Your responsibilities

  • You will independently manage and coordinate demanding projects in the field of building services engineering.
  • You will be responsible for the spatial coordination of various technical disciplines and coordinate the relevant interfaces within the project team.
  • You will assume technical responsibility for specialist coordination and contribute to the further development and implementation of modern BIM and planning methods.
  • Depending on your experience and interests, you will also take on the management of medium-sized projects with demanding technical and organizational requirements.
  • You will lead, support and develop project staff and promote constructive collaboration within the team.
  • You can tailor the focus of your role between specialist coordination and project management.
  • You will contribute your expertise to the further development of projects, processes and employees.
  • You will work closely with various specialist departments and project stakeholders and ensure efficient coordination.

04 Your profile

  • You have completed basic training in a field related to HVAC or building services engineering.
  • Ideally, you have completed further education at HF or FH level or obtained a comparable professional certificate.
  • You have practical experience in spatial discipline coordination and in planning building services engineering projects.
  • Knowledge of BIM and digital planning methods is an advantage.
  • Experience with Revit in building services engineering is a plus.
  • You are interested in holistic and integrated building services engineering concepts.
  • You are enthusiastic about digitalization, modern planning methods and the continuous development of your field.
  • You enjoy working independently and taking responsibility for your tasks and projects.
  • You communicate confidently with various project stakeholders and maintain an overview even when handling complex tasks.
  • You are open to change and feel comfortable in a dynamic working environment.
  • You enjoy sharing your knowledge and supporting others in their professional development.
  • Even as a junior, you will have the opportunity to develop specifically into an expert.
