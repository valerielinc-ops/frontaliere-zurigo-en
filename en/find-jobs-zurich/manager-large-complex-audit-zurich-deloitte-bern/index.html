Full description

Location: Basel, Bern, Zurich Work Pattern Percentage: 60-100%

Are you an auditor who wants to make the world a better place by thoroughly examining organizations and their activities, thereby strengthening the trust of investors and stakeholders? Deloitte offers you this opportunity as a Corporate Audit Manager.

What to expect from us

  • We offer you a dynamic, growing environment focused on Swiss and international accounting standards and assurance projects
  • You lead, supervise and manage teams that handle national and international audit and assurance engagements of various sizes
  • You contribute to the acquisition of new engagements and gain valuable experience in the process
  • You have the opportunity to coach junior colleagues and train them through internal training programs
  • You work in multidisciplinary teams and can contribute and further develop your finance knowledge every day

What you bring

  • You hold a Swiss Certified Public Accountant qualification
  • You have four to five years of experience in conducting and leading external audit engagements
  • You have practical experience with the accounting standards Swiss Code of Obligations (OR), Swiss GAAP FER, IFRS or US GAAP
  • You are resilient, enjoy working in a team as well as independently, and are efficient and solution-oriented
  • You communicate confidently and professionally in German and English (any additional language is an advantage) and can build and maintain client relationships

Your team

Become part of our dynamic, international and growing Audit team at our locations in Zurich, Basel or Bern. More than 400 colleagues share your passion for auditing and work with Swiss and international accounting standards and assurance projects.

We also offer you national and international career opportunities as well as a comprehensive internal and external training and development program.

Working at Deloitte

As one of the world's leading consulting and audit firms, Deloitte is passionately committed to shaping the future. In Switzerland, we offer industry-specific services in the areas of Audit & Assurance, Tax & Legal, Strategy, Risk & Transactions Advisory, and Technology & Transformation. With more than 3,000 employees at six locations in Basel, Bern, Geneva, Lausanne, Lugano and Zurich, we serve companies and organizations of all legal forms and sizes in all industries.

At Deloitte, you can make a difference that matters to our employees, our clients and society. We offer an inclusive environment where you can follow your own path and build on what drives you.

You benefit from flexible working conditions, health and parental programs, and build lifelong relationships in an international and dynamic environment.
