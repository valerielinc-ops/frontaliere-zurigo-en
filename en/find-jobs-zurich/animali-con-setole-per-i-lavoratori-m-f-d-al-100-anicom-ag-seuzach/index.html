With around 11,600 employees, fenaco processes, refines and markets agricultural products in its own food and beverage industries. The fenaco Group includes numerous business units and subsidiaries, including Anicom AG, a nationwide animal trading company that markets farm animals for breeding, fattening and slaughtering. For our branch in Winterthur-Ohringen (Seuzach), we are looking for a communicative and entrepreneurial personality as an employee for trade bristle animals with immediate effect or by appointment.

These tasks inspire you

  • Maintaining contacts with the feed industry, the Pig Health Service, LANDI and other agricultural organisations

  • Good MS Office skills

Discover your benefits
