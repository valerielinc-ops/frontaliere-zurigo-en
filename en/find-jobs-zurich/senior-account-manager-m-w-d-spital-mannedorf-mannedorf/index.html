Full description

Spital Männedorf – your very special place of work. Whether it's a small “thank you” or a morning “Grüezi” - our focus is on genuine appreciation and humanity. The proximity in our house makes everyday life uncomplicated and enables patient-centered work at the highest level. In an environment in which interdisciplinary collaboration on equal terms is a given, we offer individual treatments from initial medical care to rehabilitation. The essence of your work:

  • Competent care and support for our patients
  • Ensuring the quality of care by implementing the respective standards
  • Coordination of care in interprofessional collaboration
  • Active participation in diagnostic, therapeutic and nursing measures
  • Close interdisciplinary collaboration with doctors, nurses and other professional groups The basis of your performance:
  • Training as a health specialist EFZ as well as experience in an acute hospital in Switzerland
  • Commitment and initiative in contact with our patients and employees in other areas
  • Independent, responsible and reliable way of working
  • Enjoyment of demanding, complex and varied care situations
  • Flexibility, ability to work in a team and willingness to take on all shifts The advantages of your position: You can expect modern rostering, appreciative leadership and a team that lives true cohesion - even if the ward is not open all year round. A dynamic working model: flexible, humane and more compatible with your own life. During school holidays and public holidays, the ward, with a focus on surgery, is partially closed - for more planning and a better work-life balance. The reasons for your decision: We offer modern working conditions, promote internal training and focus on professional exchange across all disciplines. The quiet location right on the lake and the proximity to the vibrant city of Zurich round off your workplace. You can also look forward to very special benefits. Questions about life in Switzerland?
