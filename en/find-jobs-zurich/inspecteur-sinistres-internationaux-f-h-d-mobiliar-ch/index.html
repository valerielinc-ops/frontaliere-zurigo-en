This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

What awaits you

As a member of XpertCenter SA, a company of the Mobilière Group, you support and advise customers in the settlement of claims in the areas of Motorbusiness, anti-fraud, recourse management, case management and international claims.

What you can achieve with us

You follow a multi-year integration and training programme with the aim of assuming, after its success, the role of international inspector or accident inspector. You gradually deepen your knowledge of the matter of international disasters and you develop, professionally and personally, in this demanding function.

Starting with medium complexity material claims in the field of RC motor vehicles, you are moving towards the autonomous treatment of complex, physical and material claims, in all branches of civil liability as well as claims of branches things like theft, fire, water damage that have a connection with abroad. As part of your business, you ensure the final processing of claims files by performing local, on-site and client visions as required.

With experience, you are dealing with cases such as cases of disability, death or claims of the RC branch companies highly complex in the French language mainly and participate independently in interviews with lawyers, private and social insurers. In addition, you are responsible for the implementation of social and private insurance remedies abroad, supporting the technical services of the Directorate and general agencies and maintaining close contacts with our partners abroad.

What you bring

  • Completed basic training in insurance, a federal patent in private insurance is an asset
  • Professional experience in disaster service and good knowledge of civil liability law
  • Independent and solution-oriented working mode
  • Team-based, flexible and secure negotiating personality
  • French or German mother tongue with excellent knowledge of the other language, both oral and written; any other language is an advantage

Working with us

You will join the international disaster team, made up of collaborators, specialists and accident inspectors. Together, they cultivate a committed, solution-oriented and communication-oriented work environment. Your activity rate is tradable from 80%, and your workplace is, as you wish, in Bern, Zurich-Oerlikon or Nyon. During the first three months, the place of work is fixed in Bern. Next, regular meetings are held between spectators and accident inspectors in Bern.

What we offer

Balance of working life - privacy

We apply flexible work models and allow 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave. We also offer above-average family allowances and a sustainable pension fund.

Continuing training and development

We offer a wide range of in-house courses and allow you to participate in possible programs abroad. We contribute generously to external continuing training related to work.

Benefits

We offer up to 25% discount on premiums, half-rate subscription, discounts on mobile phones, free flat-rate mobile subscriptions and discounts on cultural events.
