Full description

Personally. Doctor Secretary/-in 80% - 100% | Zurich Do you have questions? Rebekah is at your disposal. Rebekka Lückl Management & Services bewerbung@susenbergklinik.ch 044 268 38 38

Your responsibility

Cooperation patient administration: ensuring performance recording and billing/invoicing, preparation of coding, processing of debtors and accounts payable (incl. independent creditors invoice management), handling of administrative activities around our patients. Cooperation at the reception: First point of contact for internal concerns, collection and maintenance of patient data, etc. Klinik Susenberg is a specialist clinic for acute geriatrics, geriatric and specialized internistic oncological rehabilitation and palliative care at Zurichberg. In order for our patients to be the focus, an organization that works is needed in the background.

This is what you bring with you

Mindset: Desire for an all-rounder activity, openly “learning new things”, positive attitude. Foundation: Basic training in the health care system (MPA, DA, FaGe) or commercial training with further training as medical and hospital secretary. Skills: goal-oriented, discreet and independent working method, high service orientation, affinity for numbers, stylish German, networked thinking and a friendly appearance.

We offer

Versatile activity with visible effect. An appreciative, lively environment, short decision-making processes and close cooperation with the operational management of Verwaltungs&Services. Attractive benefits: health promotion and staff catering.
