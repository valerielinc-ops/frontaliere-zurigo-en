We serve a versatile patient clientele from orthopaedics, traumatology, rheumatology and geriatrics. Exercise, training and active rehabilitation are our main focus. Our goal is high-quality physiotherapy in a motivating environment in which both patients and employees feel comfortable. You work in a team of currently 15 physiotherapists and around 10 trainers in the medical training area. Our administration supports you in everyday life so that you can concentrate on therapy. Your tasks

  • Treatment and care of patients, in particular in the fields of orthopaedics, traumatology, rheumatology and geriatrics
  • active rehabilitation involving our large medical training area (MTT)
  • Collaboration with doctors and our interdisciplinary team of physiotherapy and training
  • Co-design of the therapy quality and further development of our offerings

We usually work in the 30-minute treatment rhythm. Per day, you usually treat about 15 patients. For documentation and professional exchange, administration time is available every day.
