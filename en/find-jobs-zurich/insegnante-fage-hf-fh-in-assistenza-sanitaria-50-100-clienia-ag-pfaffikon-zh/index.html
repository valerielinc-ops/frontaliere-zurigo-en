Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With over 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric services offered by Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in outpatient, day clinic and inpatient settings. The group employs around 1800 people and generated sales of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For our wards we are looking for a

The Bergheim is a modern long-term care facility and has been awarded the EFQM Lable Recognised for Excellence 4 Star.

We specialize in the care and support of people who suffer from psychiatric impairments and are of advanced age. The Bergheim belongs to the Clienia Group and has 134 beds, of which 85 beds are operated in protected wards. Around a hundred employees look after the well-being and needs of the residents every day. Creating quality of life together is our focus.
