About us

We enchant the world with chocolate! Lindt & Sprüngli (Switzerland) AG is a subsidiary of the global Lindt & Sprüngli Group with its headquarters and production facility in Kilchberg on Lake Zurich. In Altendorf we operate our own logistics center, while the Lindt Cocoa Center in Olten produces cocoa mass for our own needs as well as for other European production facilities. Throughout Switzerland we proudly operate 15 Lindt shops, including iconic locations such as the Jungfraujoch and the Swiss public transport company in Lucerne.

Over 1000 employees from more than 50 nations contribute to our success.

Tasks

  • Responsibility for process design, customization and implementation in SAP QM module (S/4HANA)
  • Analysis and prioritization of requirements in quality management in cooperation with production, purchasing, logistics and quality assurance
  • Support and further development of SAP QM processes (quality planning, testing planning, quality testing, complaints)
  • Implementation of customization in SAP QM (e.g.

Test plans, characteristics, test-free, measures)

  • Integration of QM processes with production, procurement and logistics processes (PP, MM, EWM)
  • Participation in the integration of external systems and interfaces (e.g. LIMS, IDocs, BAPIs)
  • Coordination and execution of system tests, Go-Live and Hypercare

Requirements

  • You have a degree in computer science, engineering, quality assurance, business information technology or a comparable SAP-related training
  • You have several years of professional experience (3–5 years) in the introduction, support and further development of SAP QM processes
  • You have a sound knowledge of quality planning, testing processes, complaints and blocking inventory management
  • You have experience with S/4HANA and the conversion of classical processes and data structures to S/4 standards (e.g.

Fiori apps, new reporting)

  • You know the integration of QM with MM, PP, SD, EWM and MES systems
  • Knowledge in interface and data integration technologies such as IDoc, BAPI, LIMS coupling are desirable
  • You work analytically, independently and structured, are highly efficient and communication-strong, you also have very good German and good English knowledge in word and writing
