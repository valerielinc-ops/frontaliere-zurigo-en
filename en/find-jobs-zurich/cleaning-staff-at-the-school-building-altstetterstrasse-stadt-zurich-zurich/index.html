Full description

We are looking for the school Altstetterstrasse on 15. September 2026 or by arrangement a motivated cleaning employee.

Tasks

Compliance with urban safety, hygiene and quality guidelines Div. Cleaning work Further tasks according to the order LHT Cooperation with other domestic service staff of the school building and other staff of the school Assistance in basic or intermediate cleaning during school holidays

Profile

  • Several years of experience in cleaning
  • Good knowledge of German
  • A flair for cleanliness and hygiene
  • Teamwork and independence

We offer

  • A versatile and exciting activity
  • A team that supports you
  • Cooperative and appreciative cooperation in the school team
  • Careful training
  • A salary and social benefits according to the guidelines of the city of Zurich

About us

The Zurich-Letzi school district comprises Stadtkreis 9 with the districts Albisrieden, Altstetten and Grünau. In the 16 schools of our rapidly growing school circle, more than 5,000 primary school students are taught and supervised. More than 1,200 employees work for them in our schools in the vocational groups of teaching, care, home service and technology as well as administration. They all work daily for the interests of these children between the ages of 4 and 13 years and a smooth school day.

Interested?
