Center for Preclinical Development

Job start flexible, temporary

The Center for Preclinical Development oversees research projects to advance medical progress in human and veterinary medicine from the academic and industrial environment. The core competencies are working with large animals (pigs and sheep) and rabbits, for the development and testing of new surgical techniques and medical devices. Other areas of focus are the optimisation of imaging techniques for the improvement and early detection of diseases and the development of optimised treatment options.

We offer

  • Attractive and contemporary workplace in the heart of Zurich
  • Remuneration per hour
  • Extension of the remit possible according to prior knowledge and interest
  • Training for the pilot(s) possible

Tasks

In order to ensure optimal care for our animals, we are looking for employees who can take on regular and long-term assignments, ideally on at least two weekends per month. Constant care facilitates organization and allows the animals to get used to familiar people, making handling less stressful and easier. As a member of our team, you are jointly responsible for the optimal care of animals on weekends, public holidays and, if necessary, in the event of illness-related failures or as a holiday representative (on average 3-4 hours per mission). The task includes the following sub-aspects, but can be extended according to the level of experience and interest:

Their tasks

  • Animal care (musting, feeding, drinking)
  • Health status control
  • Optional: Assistance in the medical care of animals (medication, minor interventions (e.g. change of dressing))
  • Optional: Assistance in emergencies at the weekend (assistance in anesthesia in cooperation with veterinary staff)

Profile

  • Practiced handling of large animals (pigs, sheep) and rabbits (or interest in learning this)
  • Interested in an exciting part-time job
  • Flexibility
  • Experience and interest in dealing with and working with large animals
