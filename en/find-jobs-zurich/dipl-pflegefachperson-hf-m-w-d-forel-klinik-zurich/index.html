Full description

Forel Klinik is one of the leading addiction clinics in Switzerland and specializes in the treatment of people with alcohol or drug dependence. At two locations, more than 180 employees provide support for those affected on their personal path to a better quality of life and self-determination. We offer individually tailored therapy concepts in the inpatient, daily clinical and outpatient setting. Our addiction treatment combines evidence-based methods with a value-oriented attitude – with the aim of enabling sustainable and effective change. As an addiction specialist clinic, it is anchored in our DNA to open up prospects and contribute to the development of people. This principle also shapes our dealings with employees. For our day clinic in the city of Zurich we are looking for you at 1. March 2027 or by arrangement. Create with us paths that work. We offer you a nursing job in which relationship design is at the centre – and in which you support people in breaking new ground. At Forel Klinik, you work in an interdisciplinary, professional and humanly demanding environment. Your commitment makes a difference for people in a stressful phase of life.

Their tasks

The nursing team is responsible for the support and implementation in the medical-therapeutic care of patients with a substance dependence who are in the day clinic for semi-inpatient weaning treatment. They ensure targeted treatment oriented to the needs of patients. They lead therapeutic groups and perform case management under medical supervision. You document your services and manage the reporting independently

Your profile

They have several years of professional experience in the field of psychiatry, preferably in the field of addiction or interest in working with people with an addiction disease as well as high social and personality competence as well as resilience and independence. They are capable of teamwork and interested in interdisciplinary cooperation. Furthermore, you have experience in leading groups (experience with or interest in additional therapeutic offers, e.g. mindfulness, yoga, NADA ear acupuncture, etc.).

Our offer

With this position, we offer you responsible tasks in a motivated, modern and well-connected environment. Their professional and personal development is a concern for us, which we support generously and purposefully with structured training and further education offers.

  • Attractive, responsible tasks in a lively, interdisciplinary, innovative environment
  • Regular supervisions, team meetings and a comprehensive interprofessional training offer
  • Active participation and implementation of change processes

The working conditions are contemporary and appealing

  • Reliable service planning without weekend services in a stable environment
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
  • 60 % employer contribution to the pension fund
  • 100 % employer contribution to KTG insurance

A motivated and collaborative team means You are welcome Our day clinic and the ambulatorium in Zurich are centrally located on Sihlquai and are very easily accessible by public transport. The urban location creates a rich working environment. Our offer is aimed at people of all identities and with different life experiences. Further information can be provided by Dr. med. Sarah Müller, senior physician, Tel. 044 277 56 00.
