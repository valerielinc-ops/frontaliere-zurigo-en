You can look forward to this. Take on a leading role in the construction management of complex railway technology projects with a high level of personal responsibility.   • You lead the planning, organization and implementation of complex roadway projects across all construction phases.
  • You ensure the technical feasibility of the railway engineering work and ensure the timely provision of all relevant resources.
  • You also control the construction processes on site, coordinate internal and external partners and immediately initiate corrective measures in the event of deviations.
  • You are responsible for checking the plausibility of the cost estimates, monitoring the cost development during construction and creating the final cost forecasts.
  • You are responsible for training new employees and promoting the transfer of knowledge within the team.
  • You are actively involved in the conceptual development of the departments and represent them in relevant committees. This is what you bring with you.
  • You are experienced in conceptual work and have already been involved in the further development of specialist topics.
  • You are also able to strategically coordinate construction projects, involve internal and external partners and make fact-based decisions.
  • You have in-depth specialist knowledge of railway technology and safety.

You have several years of proven experience in managing demanding construction sites with complex interfaces in railway technology.

  • For this function we require further training or a degree in a relevant technical area (civil engineering, railway technology or comparable further training) or alternatively several years of experience in track construction.
  • You are familiar with the industry-specific IT programs and digital planning tools.
  • You have very good spoken and written knowledge of German (at least C1), knowledge of every other national language is a plus.
