Team leader:in AVU roadway - track construction machines, East region 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Team leader:in AVU roadway - track construction machinery, East Region Zurich, 01.01.2027 or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

Take on an exciting leadership role in a versatile working environment of SBB Infrastructure. Together with your team, you will design the planning and control of track construction machinery deployments in the East Region’s road maintenance and thus make a significant contribution to efficient, economical and reliable road maintenance. You have the opportunity to further develop existing processes, strengthen cross-divisional cooperation and actively participate in digitalization. Flexible forms of work, attractive SBB benefits and a wide range of development opportunities round off our offer. You can move that.

  • You will lead around 7–8 employees in the field of AVU roadway with a focus on track construction machinery (GBM) in terms of personnel and expertise.
  • The overarching planning, coordination and control of track construction machinery deployments in the East region is your responsibility.
  • You ensure stable, efficient and economical production planning as well as optimal utilization of available resources.
  • You recognize bottlenecks and planning deviations at an early stage, initiate suitable measures and ensure the quality of the order planning.
  • The cross-divisional cooperation and the continuous development of planning processes and standards you actively participate.

You bring that with you.

  • Leadership experience is part of your profile, and you understand how to motivate employees and achieve goals together.
  • In-depth knowledge in the planning and coordination of maintenance work, ideally in the area of track and track construction machinery, distinguishes you.
  • You work independently, structured and forward-looking and keep an overview even in demanding situations.
  • With your communicative and solution-oriented personality, you promote cross-divisional cooperation and actively drive change forward.
  • You have basic technical training in the construction, railway or infrastructure sector, supplemented by a higher specialist training (HF) or a comparable qualification.

Your reward and benefits.
