Role overview

As a Data Engineer in the Medical Data Products team, you can ensure that clinical data can be used reliably, safely and efficiently.

  • As a Data Engineer in the Medical Data Products team, you can ensure that clinical data can be used reliably, safely and efficiently.
  • Operation and continuous development of our clinical data platform and data products based on it
  • Development and maintenance of interfaces, data pipelines and integration solutions

Main responsibilities

  • Operation and continuous development of our clinical data platform and data products based on it
  • Development and maintenance of interfaces, data pipelines and integration solutions
  • Detection, integration and harmonizing of new data sources
  • Analysis of data requirements and development and implementation of suitable data solutions with specialist departments and stakeholders
  • Secure data quality, traceability and reliable operating processes
  • Creating and maintaining technical documentation, architectural and operational concepts Requirements:
  • University or Fachhochschule degree in computer science, medical computer science or a similar subject
  • Multi-year experience in data engineering, as well as in the development and operation of information-oriented solutions
  • Well-founded knowledge of SQL, C# with.NET, data modelling and processing large amounts of data with SSIS, Spark, dbt and Airflow as well as the implementation of Power BI Reports
  • Experience with data integration and streaming technologies and the construction of robust data pipelines

Application process
