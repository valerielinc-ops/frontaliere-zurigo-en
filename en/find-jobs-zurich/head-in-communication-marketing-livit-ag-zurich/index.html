Full description

Are you open-minded, ambitious and value a future-oriented way of working on the pulse of the real estate industry? With your commitment, we face the challenges of today and tomorrow together. Do you want to participate and make a difference? Then you are exactly right with Livit. We look forward to your personal contribution.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for the communication and marketing of the company. You lead a committed, small team and ensure continuous development
  • Ensure that internal and external communication across all offline and online channels is consistent with the overall strategy
  • You design relevant content for our various channels, including the website, the intranet, emails, newsletters and social media, etc. (B2B, B2C and B2E)
  • Together with HR, you develop innovative employer branding concepts and create a convincing appearance as an attractive employer
  • You maintain the press and media relations and ensure them in cooperation with the communication managers of Swiss Life Asset Managers. As a press secretary: in the Livit you are also responsible for crisis communication
  • You support the operational areas in the acquisition and business development with convincing communication and marketing measures.

Requirements

  • You have completed your studies at a university of applied sciences with a specialization in communication or marketing
  • You bring experience in a comparable leadership position, ideally in the service industry
  • You have competencies in the strategic and operational support of acquisition and business development activities through target group-oriented communication, effective marketing measures and the professional creation of customer, offer and presentation documents
  • You develop and control internal and external communication and ensure a clear, credible and target-group-oriented communication of corporate strategy and values
  • You are fluent in German and English, knowledge of French is an advantage

We offer

  • Internal offer of our Livit Academy for specialist and leadership courses
  • Financial and/or time support for external specialist training and access to electronic learning platforms for online language courses
  • individual career planning and development prospects
  • Home / Mobile Office
  • Bonus depending on company success and personal performance
  • At least 5 weeks holiday and purchase of up to 10 additional days holiday (Ferienflex)
  • Above-average pension benefits and premiums for medical day allowance and accident insurance are 100% covered by Livit
  • 14 days paternity leave

We love real estate and are enthusiastic about preserving the value of our clients’ properties.

When you work at Livit, you belong to the Livit Family. You are appreciated for what you are and what you do. Because not only your skills and your experience, but also you as a person are important to us. In our working environment you feel comfortable and can develop. On the one hand, this is due to our versatile job profiles and the development opportunities within our company. On the other hand, there is also the distinctive corporate culture that is unparalleled in the real estate world.
