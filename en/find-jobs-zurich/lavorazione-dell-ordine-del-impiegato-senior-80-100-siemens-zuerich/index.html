Apply today as a senior clerk: in order processing 80-100% and start immediately or by agreement – your team is looking forward to you! Your new tasks

  • You assume responsibility for recording and monitoring orders, delivery dates and orders
  • You ensure the appointment control and tracking of SAP orders and ensure timely delivery
  • You take care of the shipping (in Germany and abroad) and prepare the invoice
  • In your hands is the project management in the areas of provision, adherence to deadlines, plant delivery, VAT and customs
  • You will handle repairs and returns and support the sales team and project management
  • You work closely with the goods receipt and exit and take on tasks in accounting (for example, creditors) Your talents and experiences
  • Completed commercial training, ideally supplemented by further training in the field of supply chain management
  • SAP knowledge (SD/MM module) is an advantage
  • Several years of professional experience in the field of order processing or project management
  • You are communicative and capable of teamwork, at the same time customer-oriented and quality-conscious
  • Your way of working is independent and structured
  • You certainly master German in word and writing, other languages such as French or English are a plus your perspective: In this position, you not only expect varied tasks, but also excellent development opportunities.
