Full description

For our diet kitchen, which comprises eleven employees and two apprentices (diet cook / diet chef FA), we are looking for reinforcement. You can expect a varied and demanding task in a lively environment. You benefit from our interesting employment conditions and an attractive location with excellent public transport connections. We look forward to meeting you. Tasks:

  • Nutritional therapy correct and patient-friendly preparation of the various dietary food forms and nutritional therapies
  • Organization of work in the diet kitchen
  • Detailed final inspection of meals from the main and diet kitchen during the food distribution line
  • Evaluation of the entire food and station demand orders and creation of the necessary production lists via computer
  • Assistance in the development of new and the optimization of existing recipes incl. Nutrient calculation
  • Assistance in the training of future diet cooks
  • Collaboration in interdisciplinary projects e.g. with nutritional advice / speech therapy / care / room service Profile:
  • Completed training as dietitian / diet chef FA or EFZ with several years of professional experience, beneficial in the hospitality industry and the community gastronomy
  • Flexible, resilient and open to the new
  • Team capable, working in a young open-minded team
  • Good computer skills (advantageous also with a common menu selection system) Benefits: KSW offers employees conditions that enable them to reconcile their professional goals and personal needs. Flexible, flexible working hours, home office as well as a generous maternity leave and employment levels adapted to different situations – all this is possible at KSW, as long as the company allows it. Every year, the KSW offers 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses. We also support you temporally and/or financially with external training.
  • In extraordinary situations, e.g. B. prolonged illness, severe personal stress or difficult experiences in private life as well as in everyday work, various offers of conversation are available to you.
  • The appreciative and friendly cooperation within the teams and across the departments characterizes the working atmosphere at KSW. As a sign of a strong team spirit, the “you” was introduced as a standard at KSW. In addition, the KSW supports annual team events financially and organizes a large staffing event for the whole house every four years. KSW employees benefit from very good social benefits. Thus, the pension fund solution with BVK is far above the legal minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, KSW employees are very well covered.
  • With discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training opportunities and course offers, perks in pharmacies as well as at the most popular health insurance companies. You can benefit from a comprehensive offer for your health.

  • In cooperation with cultural partners in and around Winterthur, we regularly offer discounts for concerts and theater performances. The borrowing of media in the in-house library is also free of charge for employees.
