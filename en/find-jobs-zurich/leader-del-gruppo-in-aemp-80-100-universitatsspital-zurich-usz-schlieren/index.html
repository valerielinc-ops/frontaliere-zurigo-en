Full description

Our AEMP reliably supplies operating rooms, ambulatories and clinics with sterile medical products. Modern systems as well as the highest quality and hygiene standards ensure a smooth process. As one of the most advanced companies for sterile goods processing in Europe, we are continuously developing our processes. We focus on innovative and sustainable solutions and are committed to the training of specialists. We look forward to applying for a dedicated, friendly and team-oriented personality.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.
