Full description

You want to not only accompany HR, but rebuild and really make a difference? Migros Supermarket AG is currently creating an independent HR – and you have the chance to help shape it right from the start. In close cooperation with employees and managers, you accompany our organization through an exciting transformation phase, strengthen development and shape the leadership of tomorrow.

Mansioni

  • In your area of responsibility, you implement the HR strategy effectively and actively shape the entire employee lifecycle
  • As a sparring partner, you support management and managers in challenging issues and at the same time support employees as a trusted contact person
  • Close cooperation with the Shared Service Center HR and other HR specialist areas is also part of your everyday life – together you ensure efficient and smooth HR processes
  • Change and transformation processes are actively accompanied by you. You also bring your experience to the design and moderation of workshops and team development formats
  • In addition, you will be involved in HR projects and working groups – both internally and within the Migros Group – and contribute to the further development of our HR topics.

Requirements

  • Several years of experience: as an HR Business Partner, preferably in a large company
  • Completed basic or further education: in the field of Human Resources (e.g. HR certificate, MAS, Bachelor/Master)
  • You are well-versed in employment law and HR processes and move safely – even in challenging situations
  • Change is not a buzzword for you, but part of your everyday work: You bring experience in the accompaniment of transformations and implement them effectively
  • A dynamic environment inspires you – you tackle change and development topics proactively and bring them forward with energy
  • With your sense of people and situations, you act sovereignly on all levels and combine clarity with empathy and the necessary assertiveness.
  • German (flowing)
  • French (advantageous)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central place of work: Central place of work in the city of Zurich (Limmatplatz or near Hardbrücke)
  • Modern infrastructure: Diverse work zones and modern co-working spaces with advanced equipment
  • Social benefits: Attractive employment conditions with very good social benefits
  • Design and decision-making leeway: Possibility to independently shape and further develop the field of responsibility
  • Club school and leisure facilities: 70% discount on Migros club school offers, 30% discount for fitness/wellness/aquaparks
  • Self-responsibility & freedom: Varied tasks with a high level of self-responsibility
