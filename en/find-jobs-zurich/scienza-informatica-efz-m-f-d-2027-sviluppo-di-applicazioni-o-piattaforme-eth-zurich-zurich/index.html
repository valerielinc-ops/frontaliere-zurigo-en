Job description

Computer science is possible in the two disciplines "Application Development" and "Platform Development".

Application development

The versatile teaching for curious tinkerers: women with joy in the construction of user-friendly software!

You work actively as part of a team on exciting projects

  • Planning, coding, testing and documenting applications using different programming languages, frameworks and architectures
  • Operation, maintenance and extension of existing applications
  • Planning, developing and maintaining automated deployment workflows to deploy applications on server systems
  • Install, configure and monitor server systems
  • Ensure the security and availability of systems and services
  • Planning and managing projects in the field of application development

Platform development

The versatile teaching for structured problem solvers: women with joy in digital construction!

In this lesson you ensure the smooth operation of the ICT infrastructure:

  • Planning, realizing and automating processes and processes
  • Managing servers and networks
  • Planning and operation of server services and server systems
  • Participation in diverse projects

The training lasts 4 years, including 1 year of basic training in our IT teaching laboratory in Zurich-Oerlikon.

Profile

You are fascinated by computer science, like to think logically abstract and have a spatial imagination. You enjoy finding creative solutions and do not shy away from challenges. Your pronounced ability to concentrate and your systematic and structured way of working take you further. You also enjoy teamwork and customer contact.

  • Mathematics
