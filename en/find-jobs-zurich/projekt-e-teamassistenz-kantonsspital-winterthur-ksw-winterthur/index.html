Full description

With its well 450 employees, the area of operation includes the real estate and construction management, technology, integrale security, supply chain management (including purchasing, logistics and AEMP), catering and housekeeping. In order to complement the "Management Support" department, we are looking for a motivated personality as a project and team assistant by appointment.

Tasks

  • Support for day-to-day management and spital-wide projects
  • Planning and coordination of appointments, meetings and internal events
  • Responsibility for protocol management, document management and storage
  • Prepriorisation and structured forwarding of incoming requests
  • Maintenance and updating of the intranet side of the operation area
  • Preparing the monthly PEP controls and punctual cooperation in project tasks

Profile

  • Completion of commercial training
  • Experience in an administrative, organizational or project-related function
  • Structured and independent operation with high quality standards
  • Secure, address-oriented communication in word and writing
  • Very good knowledge of MS Office; ideally familiar with JIRA, PEP and SharePoint
  • High self-responsibility, organisational talent and the joy of teamwork as well as initial contact points with project management are of advantage

Benefits: The KSW provides employees with conditions that enable them to meet their professional goals and personal needs. Flexible, sliding working hours, home office as well as a generous maternity leave and levels of employment adapted to different situations of life – this is all possible at the KSW if it permits the operation

  • Every year, the KSW offers 40 overtime and 50 professional training courses. We also support you in external developments in time and/or financial terms
  • In exceptional situations, e.g. longer illness, strong personal burden or difficult experiences in the private and in the everyday professional life, you have various conversation offers available. The valuable and friendly cooperation within the teams and across the departments is characterized by the working climate at the KSW. As a sign for a strong team idea, the "Du" was introduced as a standard at the KSW. In addition, the KSW supports annual team events financially and organizes a large staff festival for the whole house every four years; KSW employees benefit from very good social benefits. Thus, the pension funds solution with the BVK is far above the statutory minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, KSW employees are very well secured
  • With discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training options and course offers, discounts in pharmacies and among the most popular health insurance companies. You can benefit from a comprehensive offer for your health
  • Our La Luna daycare centre with generous opening hours is located right next to the KSW. You can also use the income-dependent, financial support provided by the KSW for the crib costs
  • In cooperation with cultural partners in and around Winterthur we regularly offer discounts for concerts and theatre performances. The borrowing of media in the in-house library is also free for employees.
