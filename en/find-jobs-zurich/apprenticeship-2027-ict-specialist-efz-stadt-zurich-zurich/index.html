Full description

You like elderly people and it is important to you that they feel comfortable in their private environment? Then you are right with us: At Spitex SAW you not only work, but bring our customers joy of life directly home.

Tasks

  • You nurture and look after our clients in their homes.
  • You support them in activities of daily life or take them on their behalf.
  • You keep a complete history documentation via smartphone.
  • You also take on domestic assignments.
  • You are mainly working with your caregiver, but you are still part of a team.

Profile

  • Secondary school A or B with good grades
  • Good knowledge of German, at least level C1
  • Completed trial theory as a health specialist
  • Good observation skills, empathy and high social skills
  • Patience and joy in dealing with elderly people
  • Mental and physical resilience

We offer

You can expect a well-founded and varied education. Your apprentices support you on your way to a successful teaching degree. You benefit from attractive employment conditions.

About us

The Foundation for Retirement Apartments of the City of Zurich SAW currently operates 36 settlements with more than 2000 affordable apartments for people aged 60 and over. It supports its tenants with everyday, social and nursing services and thus enables them a high quality of life into old age. By 2035, the SAW plans to put further settlements with around 1000 additional apartments into operation. These should optimally meet the requirements of the target group and create a basis for positive neighbourly cooperation. More about the SAW at www.wohnenab60.ch.
