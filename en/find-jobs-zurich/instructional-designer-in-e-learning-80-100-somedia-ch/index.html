Full description

SOMEDIA LEARNING AG We live e-learning! Our interdisciplinary team supports companies in all aspects of digital learning: from consulting and the didactic conception and implementation of knowledge to training and coaching.

Our projects are as diverse as our customers. And now only you are missing: a creative person who makes knowledge tangible and sustainably anchored.

For Somedia Learning AG in Zurich, we are looking for an Instructional Designer (80-100%) immediately or by agreement. Your job You design and lead digital learning projects. This means:

  • You read into new topics, bring the content into a suitable structure and draw the red thread.
  • You bring knowledge to the point, with precise texts and ideas for the appropriate multimedia implementation.
  • You create learning modules with authoring tools such as Articulate Storyline and Articulate Rise.
  • You keep track of your projects and use time and resources efficiently

Your background You ensure that the knowledge remains in the minds of the target group. You bring the following with you:

  • You have a university or university of applied sciences degree in the field of e-learning, pedagogy, media or communication.
  • You have experience in the digital learning world and in project management.
  • You write correctly and grammatically (German).
  • You are creative and have an eye for design and multimedia.
  • You work independently, reliably and feel comfortable both in a small team and in direct customer contact.

This is what awaits you

  • Projects: Exciting orders for a wide range of Swiss customers.
  • Responsibility: You take on the planning and management of projects as well as their didactic implementation.
  • Team: A motivated, uncomplicated team with a lot of know-how and pleasure in the matter.
  • Working conditions: Flexible working hours with home office and office just a five-minute walk from Altstetten station.
  • Development opportunities: Are you familiar with learning platforms? Perfect! Have you already given training? You could do that with us too! And if you bring additional skills or fresh ideas, we look forward to it – we give you space to bring them in!

Our contact Sounds good? Then we look forward to your application!Please send us your application documents via the application button.
