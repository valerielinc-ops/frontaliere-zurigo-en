Full description

Personal – competent – close Welcome to the Hospital Uster, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As the central pillar of health care in the Oberland region of Zurich and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you experience the highest level of expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and promoted in your professional development. Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of a “regional and digital health hub in Züri East”! Our musculoskeletal centre combines orthopaedics, traumatology and hand surgery. In a specialized, interprofessional team, we accompany our patients holistically – from the consultation session to the surgery to sports medicine and rehabilitation. Our team focuses on cooperative and interdisciplinary cooperation with short communication paths. As a clinical specialist (KFS), you are employed in the medical field. You are responsible for the comprehensive care and management of inpatient and postoperative patients. In close cooperation with the medical team and the nursing and therapeutic services, you coordinate the station sequence and ensure a high quality of care. To complement our team, we are looking for an open-minded and committed personality from January 2027 or by agreement as

Exciting challenges await you

  • Contact person for all professional groups and institutions involved
  • Conduct of visits, examinations and patient and family interviews
  • Preparation of medical prescriptions (medicines, diagnostics, therapy)
  • Preparation of progress and withdrawal reports
  • Coordination of the interdisciplinary treatment process
  • Support for preoperative counselling and postoperative follow-up treatment
  • Participation in quality projects and optimization of supply processes
  • if desired, it is possible to assist with operations and to participate in the consultation
  • Further development of the position in our clinic in close cooperation with the medical director orthopaedics
  • the further development of one’s own role is supported by internal and external training

What sets you apart for the job

  • Dipl. Nursing professional HF/HF with several years of experience in acute hospital, orthopaedics or traumatology
  • CAS clinical specialists or willingness to complete them; or higher degree program (e.g. Master in Science of Nursing)
  • accept changes and innovations as a challenge and look forward to actively shaping them
  • Good English skills are beneficial

8 reasons to work with us

  • Possibility to participate together with the medical and nursing teams in the establishment of the position and in the clinic-wide future
  • Possibility to further develop and specialize in the field of orthopaedics
  • an interesting and versatile task in an innovative company with an appreciative working atmosphere and an open leadership culture
  • above-average support for further education and training
  • min. 5 weeks holiday
  • Excellent staff restaurant
  • Central location near Uster station and motorway connection
  • Cheap parking – with Parkingpay
