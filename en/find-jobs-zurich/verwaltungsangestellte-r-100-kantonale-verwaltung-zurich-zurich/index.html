Role overview

Canton of Zurich Health Directorate Cantonal Therapeutic Control Administration Employees/r 100% 100% The Cantonal Therapeutic Products Control Zurich is the supervisory and licensing authority for the field of medicinal products.

As a cantonal and partly regional competence centre, it controls the production and wholesale of medicines as well as the distribution of remedies.

It grants the necessary authorisations for therapeutic companies and medical and health professionals.
