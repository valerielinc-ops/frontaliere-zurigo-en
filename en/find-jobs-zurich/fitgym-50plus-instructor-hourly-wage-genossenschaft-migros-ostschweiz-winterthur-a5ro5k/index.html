Full description

The Migros Klubschule is the largest provider of adult education in Switzerland. The Migros Ostschweiz cooperative operates 7 Klubschule locations, offering our customers over 600 different courses and programs in a pleasant learning atmosphere.

Responsibilities

  • Your passion for teaching, commitment, and engaging demeanor inspire participants
  • Preparation, execution, and follow-up of Fitgym classes
  • Practical and health-promoting lesson design, taking into account the diverse needs of course participants
  • Ensuring course quality and implementing Klubschule standards

Requirements

  • Professional experience: teaching adults
  • Completed basic or advanced training: in group fitness
  • Independent, flexible, and reliable personality who conveys their knowledge and enthusiasm for movement courses with dedication
  • Engaging demeanor and strong communication skills
  • German (very good proficiency)

What we offer

  • Insurance: Preferential conditions with various insurance companies
  • Cumulus points: Multiple Cumulus points on food and non-food items
  • Migros Klubschule: Generous financial contribution to self-selected courses
