Welcome to Medbase Winterthur Win4

Our modern center Win4 stands for excellent quality in basic care for general practitioners. We accompany our patients in the long term, holistically and at a high professional level – from prevention to continuous care of chronic diseases. In addition to general practitioner medicine, we also work together with specialists from sports medicine, performance diagnostics, orthopaedic surgery and traumatology, physiotherapy and osteopathy interprofessionell.

In cooperation with the Cantonal Hospital Winterthur, our patients benefit from short distances and a networked care. Around 40 employees work together to create a high-quality and future-oriented medicine. We place great importance on a balanced work-life balance – part-time models and flexible working hours are possible with us.
