Full description

Are you passionate about financial analysis and ready to play a crucial role in a global reinsurance leader? Join ourTechnical Accounting team in Bangalore where you'll leverage your analytical expertise to ensure financialaccuracy while collaborating with stakeholders worldwide!

About the Role

As a Senior TA Analyst, you'll be at the heart of our financial operations, ensuring the accuracy of premium andclaim transactions while providing valuable insights through data analysis. You'll work with both internal andexternal stakeholders to resolve complex accounting issues and contribute to the continuous improvement of ourprocesses.

Key Responsibilities Contract Compliance- Validate and analyze premium and claims data against contract wordings/agreementsto ensure compliance Financial Validation- Monitor financial numbers in Technical Accounting systems and identify adversepremium/claim trends from client data Data Analysis- Develop data analysis summaries that aid decision-making across multiple cross-functionalteams Performance Management- Deliver consistently operational commitments and key performance indicators toeffectively manage cash flow Stakeholder Management- Build strong relationships with stakeholders and contribute to strengthening clientrelationships, work effectively with multiple matrixed teams across Geographical Boundaries to implementbest practices. About the Team This position is part of the Global Technical Accounting & Services organization for Life & Health Business, whichhas overall responsibility for Technical Accounting functions worldwide, including Business Solution Centers. Theteam ensures that payments received from clients (premiums) and payments made to clients (claims) reconcileagainst expected amounts. Working within established frameworks while finding innovative solutions is key to theteam's success. The role reports to the Manager Operations Southeast Asia & India.

About You

You're a detail-oriented financial professional with strong analytical skills and excellent communication skills. Youthrive in a collaborative environment and are committed to delivering high-quality results. Your ability to managerelationships with stakeholders and analyze complex financial data makes you an ideal candidate for this role.

We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements

  • Graduate/Postgraduate in Commerce, Accounting, Insurance or equivalent field
  • 3+ years of work experience in banking, insurance, or other financial services industries
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills with excellent client management abilities
  • Proven data analytical skills and experience working with financial data
  • Sound insurance product knowledge and understanding of Insurance/Reinsurance End-to-End Value Chain

These are additional nice to have

  • Experience with SAS and Python
  • Previous experience in technical accounting or similar roles in reinsurance
  • Knowledge of international financial reporting standards
  • Experience working in a global, multicultural environment
  • Problem-solving skills and the ability to work under pressure
  • Our company has a hybrid work model where the expectation is that you will be in the office at least three days per week.
