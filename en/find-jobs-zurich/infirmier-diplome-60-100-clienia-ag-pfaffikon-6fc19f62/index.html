Description of your tasks Flexibility and enjoyment of variety are central points. Because that’s exactly what working in acute psychiatry means. If this applies to you, here are some of the most important tasks:

  • Referent work: We treat our patients individually according to their needs and maintain contact with their relatives.
  • Group offers: In addition to the individual discussions, there are group offers, which are also led by the nursing staff.
  • Interprofessional collaboration: Our interprofessional collaboration is a huge strength and sets us apart. Active participation and involvement are central components of this collaboration.
  • Permanent night guards: Since we work with permanent night guards, you will be deployed in a two-shift system (early and late shift). Would you still like to work a few nights? Then we make that possible too.
  • Requisiti
  • Your profile
  • Professional expertise
  • Service orientation
  • Lots of humor With these characteristics we achieve the best treatment results for our patients and the team is happy that you will become part of them. If you have experience in nursing but not in the psychiatric environment, it is still possible to get started. What is then crucial is that you have a lot of motivation and commitment to acquire the specific skills.
