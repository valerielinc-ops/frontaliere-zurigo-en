Full description

For the newly created position in the business department of the management sector, we are looking for a committed and leadership-strong personality who inspires people, makes clear decisions and actively shapes change.

Tasks

  • Technical and personnel management and further development of the subordinate management team
  • Ensuring professional, customer-oriented and economic management of the assigned real estate portfolio of commercial and parking properties
  • Support and implementation of change and optimization projects
  • Support of the team in the care of demanding tenancy relationships as well as in negotiations with tenants and other internal and external stakeholders
  • Investment and financial planning with budget responsibility for the team portfolio
  • Close cooperation with internal departments and external partners

Profile

  • Completed commercial or technical basic training as well as further education in the real estate sector, ideally as a real estate manager with a federal professional certificate; further development is advantageous
  • Multiple years of professional experience in the management of commercial real estate, solid technical understanding beneficial
  • Many years of experience in entrepreneurial and human leadership
  • Know-how in the optimization and further development of processes and organizations
  • Empathic, communicative and assertive personality with high social, conflict and negotiation skills
  • Entrepreneurial, structured and solution-oriented working method with pronounced cost awareness and business understanding
  • Very good computer science user knowledge, especially MS Office, SAP and Abacus knowledge is an advantage

We offer

a diverse and collegial working environment in a growing organization. Our employees benefit from attractive employment conditions such as flexible working time models and good social benefits. The centrally located workplace at Hallwylplatz and a wide range of training and further education are further qualities.

About us

Properties for a livable city of Zurich

Real Estate City of Zurich (LSZ), with its portfolio of 5.8 billion francs in insurance value, is one of the major real estate organizations in Switzerland. In addition to the management of around 10,300 apartments and 1,600 commercial premises, we are responsible for the comprehensive ownership representation, the long-term value preservation and the further development of this versatile portfolio. In addition, we manage the car parks, the municipal land reserves and building rights and are responsible for municipal real estate transactions.
