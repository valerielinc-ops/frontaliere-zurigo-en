Full description

As (Senior) Legal Counsel, you play a central role within the Implenia Group and assume legal responsibility for selected topics with a focus on Swiss construction and procurement law. You are the first point of contact for our internal stakeholders and support the business units and Group functions with sound legal advice.

Your place of work is our headquarters in the Ambassador House in Opfikon, with the possibility to work partly in the home office.

Tasks: (Senior) Legal Counsel (m/f/d), Opfikon, 80-100 %

  • In this role, you will advise our operational units (especially building construction) comprehensively on legal issues – with a focus on Swiss construction and procurement law.
  • You take over legal risk management in bid procedures, contract negotiations and conclusions (e.g. real estate purchase contracts, work and project development contracts, rental and property contracts).
  • You check, assert and enforce legal claims – or defend them in the interest of Implenia.
  • In addition, you independently conduct contract and settlement negotiations and find pragmatic solutions.
  • You represent Implenia in state courts and authorities and coordinate external law firms.
  • Through internal training, especially in construction and procurement law, you strengthen the legal understanding within the company.

Requirements: (Senior) Legal Counsel (m/f/d), Opfikon, 80-100 %

  • You have successfully completed university studies in Switzerland (lic. iur./MLaw) with a Swiss lawyer patent
  • You will bring 4-6 years of professional experience as a lawyer and/or legal counsel, preferably with experience in building law.
  • Your assertiveness and negotiating skills as well as your strong communication and team competence distinguish you
  • You have a quick understanding of complex issues, work pragmatically and solution-oriented and can communicate legal topics in an understandable way to non-lawyers
  • An independent, fast and careful working method as well as a high degree of commitment and resilience are a matter of course for you
  • You have reliable German and very good English skills, preferably good Italian skills

Benefits: (Senior) Legal Counsel (m/f/d), Opfikon, 80-100 %

  • A multicultural working environment with the chance to shape a future worth living in
  • State-of-the-art technologies and a contemporary workplace at a sustainable company
  • A company that promotes talent and the training of its employees
  • The security of a financially healthy, broad-based group with strong roots in Switzerland
  • Flexible working models and modern work equipment with home office possibility
  • An exciting selection of benefits:
  • 5 weeks holidays and 9 paid bridge days
  • an in-house pension fund with very good coverage and 3 contribution levels
  • numerous discounts on well-known suppliers of products and services

About Implenia

As a leading Swiss construction and real estate service provider, Implenia develops, realizes and manages living spaces, working environments and infrastructure for future generations in Switzerland and Germany. Implenia also offers tunnelling and related infrastructure projects in other markets. The company combines the know-how of highly qualified consulting, development, planning and execution units under one roof into an integrated, multinationally leading construction and real estate service provider. Headquartered in Opfikon near Zurich, Implenia employs more than 8,000 people (FTE) across Europe and achieved a turnover of CHF 3.5 billion in 2025. More information at implenia.com
